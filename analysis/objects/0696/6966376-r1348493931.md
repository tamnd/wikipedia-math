# Associação Brasileira de Estatística

## o1 subject: Associação Brasileira de Estatística

The Brazilian Statistical Association ( (ABE)) is a non-profit organization with the purpose of promoting the development and application of statistical science. It also acts as a debating place for any professional body that has an interest in statistics. Its headquarters is at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics, University of São Paulo.
