# George C. Tiao

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: George C. Tiao

George Ching-Hwuan Tiao (; November 8, 1933 – April 25, 2026) was a Taiwanese-American econometrician and statistician. He was a professor emeritus of economics and statistics at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. He was the founding chair editor of Statistica Sinica. He contributed greatly to the field of Bayesian econometrics.
