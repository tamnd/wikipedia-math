# Six nines in pi

## o1 subject: Six nines in pi

A sequence of six consecutive nines occurs in the decimal representation of the number pi ($\pi$), starting at the 762nd decimal place. It has become famous because of the mathematical coincidence, and because of the idea that one could memorize the digits of $\pi$ up to that point, and then jokingly suggest that $\pi$ is rational.
