# Tara E. Brendle

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Tara E. Brendle

Tara Elise Brendle FRSE is an American mathematician who works in geometric group theory, which involves the intersection of algebra and low-dimensional topology. In particular, she studies mapping class group of surfaces, including braid groups, and their relationship to automorphism groups of free groups and arithmetic groups. She is a professor of mathematics at the University of Glasgow.
