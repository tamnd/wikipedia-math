# Hesse configuration

## o1 subject: Hesse configuration

In geometry, the Hesse configuration is a configuration of 9 points and 12 lines with three points per line and four lines through each point. It can be denoted as (94 123) or configuration matrix $\left [\begin{smallmatrix}9 & 4 \\ 3 & 12 \\ \end{smallmatrix}\right ]$. It is symmetric (point and line transitive) with 432 automorphisms.

### PG(2,3)
The Hesse configuration may in turn be augmented by adding four points, one for each triple of non-intersecting lines, and one line containing the four new points, to form self dual configuration (134) or $\left [\begin{smallmatrix}13 & 4 \\ 4 & 13 \\ \end{smallmatrix}\right ]$.
It is isomorphic to PG(2,3), the set of points and lines of the projective plane over the three-element field.
PG(2,k) has (k3-1)(k3-k)(k3-k2)/(k-1) automorphisms with prime k, here (33-1)(33-3)(33-32)/(2-1)=26⋅24⋅18/2 = 5616 automorphisms, 13× higher than Hesse configuration.
