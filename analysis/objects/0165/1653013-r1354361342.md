# Millionth

## o1 subject: Millionth

One millionth is equal to 0.000 001, or 1 × 10−6 in scientific notation. It is the reciprocal of a million, and can be also written as $\tfrac{1}{1,000,000}$. Units using this fraction can be indicated using the prefix "micro-" from Greek, meaning "small". Numbers of this quantity are expressed in terms of μ (the Greek letter mu).
