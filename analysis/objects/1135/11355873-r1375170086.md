# Johann Christian Martin Bartels

## o1 subject: Johann Christian Martin Bartels

Johann Christian Martin Bartels (12 August 1769 – 20 December 1836) was a German mathematician. He was the tutor of Carl Friedrich Gauss in Braunschweig and the educator of Nikolai Lobachevsky at the University of Kazan in Russia.
