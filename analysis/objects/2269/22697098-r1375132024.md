# Distance of closest approach

## o1 subject: Distance of closest approach

In geometry, the distance of closest approach of two objects is the distance between their centers when they are externally tangent (touching without overlap). The objects may be abstract geometric shapes or physical objects with well-defined boundaries. The distance of closest approach is sometimes referred to as the contact distance.
