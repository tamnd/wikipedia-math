## Properties
Tanaka's formula is the explicit Doob–Meyer decomposition of the submartingale |Bt| into the martingale part (the integral on the right-hand side, which is a Brownian motion), and a continuous increasing process (local time). It can also be seen as the analogue of Itō's lemma for the (nonsmooth) absolute value function $f(x)=|x|$, with $f'(x) = \operatorname{sgn}(x)$ and $f''(x) = 2\delta(x)$; see local time for a formal explanation of the Itō term.
