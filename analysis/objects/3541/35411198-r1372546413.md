# Newton line

## o1 subject: Newton line

In Euclidean geometry, the Newton line is the line that connects the midpoints of the two diagonals in a convex quadrilateral other than a parallelogram.

## o2 section: Properties

## o3 statement/fact: Properties

## Properties
The line segments $\overline{GH}$ and $\overline{IJ}$ that connect the midpoints of opposite sides (the bimedians) of a convex quadrilateral intersect in a point that lies on the Newton line. This point $K$ bisects the line segment $\overline{EF}$ that connects the diagonal midpoints.
By Anne's theorem and its converse, any interior point P on the Newton line of a quadrilateral $ABCD$ has the property that
$[\triangle ABP] + [\triangle CDP] = [\triangle ADP] + [\triangle BCP],$
where $[\triangle ABP]$ denotes the area of triangle $\triangle ABP$.
If the quadrilateral is a tangential quadrilateral, then its incenter also lies on this line.
