# Dmitrii Sintsov

## o1 subject: Dmitrii Sintsov

Dmitrii Matveyevich Sintsov (; 20 November 1867, Vyatka – 28 January 1946, Kharkiv) was a Russian mathematician known for his work in the theory of conic sections and non-holonomic geometry.
