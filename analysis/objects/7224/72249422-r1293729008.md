## o1 subject: Toshmuhammad Sarimsoqov

Toshmuhammad Sarimsoqov (Uzbek Cyrillic: Тошмуҳаммад Алиевич Саримсоқов, , Tashmukhamed Alievich Sarymsakov; 10 September 1915 17 December 1995) was an Uzbek mathematician who served as president of the Academy of Sciences of the Uzbek SSR from 1947 to 1952.
