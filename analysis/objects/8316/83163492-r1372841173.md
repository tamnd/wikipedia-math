# Optimal stable matching

## o1 subject: Optimal stable matching

In the theory of matching markets, an optimal stable matching is a matching that, among all stable matchings, satisfies some criterion of optimality. In the special case in which the matching market is bipartite (e.g. there are men and women, each man should be matched to woman and each woman should be matched to a man), the problem is called optimal stable marriage.
