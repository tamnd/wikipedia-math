# Armenian eternity sign

## o1 subject: Armenian eternity sign

The Armenian eternity sign (⟨֎ ֍⟩, ) or Arevakhach’ (, "Sun Cross") is an ancient Armenian national symbol and a symbol of the national identity of the Armenians. It is one of the most common symbols in Armenian architecture, carved on khachkars and on walls of churches.
