# Julia Rozanska

## o1 subject: Julia Rozanska

Iulia Antonivna Rozhanska (; ; 1901 – 23 April 1967), also spelt Julia Różańska, was a Soviet topologist. After studying under Pavel Aleksandrov, she was an associate professor at Moscow State University. She was a member of the Moscow Mathematical Society. She attended the First International Topological Conference, Moscow, 1935.
