# Ruochuan Liu

## o1 subject: Ruochuan Liu

Ruochuan Liu () is a Chinese mathematician and a Boya Distinguished Professor at Peking University, specialising in number theory and arithmetic geometry. He is best known for his fundamental contributions to p-adic Hodge theory, for which he was awarded the 2024 ICTP Ramanujan Prize.
