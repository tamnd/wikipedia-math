# Anatole Katok

## o2 subject: Anatole Katok

Anatoly Borisovich Katok (; August 9, 1944 – April 30, 2018) was an American mathematician with Russian-Jewish origins. Katok was the director of the Center for Dynamics and Geometry at the Pennsylvania State University.
His field of research was the theory of dynamical systems.
