# Shai Evra

## o1 subject: Shai Evra

Shai Evra () is a mathematician at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem specialising in representation theory. He was awarded the SASTRA Ramanujan Prize in 2020. He researches symmetric spaces of arithmetic groups.
