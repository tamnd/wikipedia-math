# Selberg's 1/4 conjecture

## o1 subject: Selberg's 1/4 conjecture

In mathematics, Selberg's conjecture, also known as Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture, conjectured by , states that the eigenvalues of the Laplace operator on Maass wave forms of congruence subgroups are at least 1/4. Selberg showed that the eigenvalues are at least 3/16. Subsequent works improved the bound, and the best bound currently known is 975/4096≈0.238..., due to .
