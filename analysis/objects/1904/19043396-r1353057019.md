# Markov spectrum

## o1 subject: Markov spectrum

In mathematics, the Markov spectrum, devised by Andrey Markov, is a complicated set of real numbers arising in Markov Diophantine equations and also in the theory of Diophantine approximation.

## o10 statement/theorem: Theorem

Given $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the Hausdorff dimension of $L\cap(-\infty,t)$ is equal to the Hausdorff dimension of $M\cap(-\infty,t)$. Moreover, if d is the function defined as $d(t):=\dim_{H}(M\cap(-\infty,t))$, where dimH denotes the Hausdorff dimension, then d is continuous and maps R onto [0,1].
