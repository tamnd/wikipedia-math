# Cross-sectional study

## o1 subject: Cross-sectional study

In medical research, epidemiology, social science, and biology, a cross-sectional study (also known as a cross-sectional analysis, transverse study, prevalence study) is a type of research design that analyzes data from a population, or a representative subset, at a specific point in time—that is, cross-sectional data.This page does not actually define the term "cross-sectional study".
