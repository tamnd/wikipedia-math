# Kung-Yee Liang

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Kung-Yee Liang

Kung-Yee Liang (; born September 7, 1951) is a Taiwanese biostatistician known for his work on generalized estimating equations, which he introduced together with Scott Zeger in 1986. He is a distinguished chair professor at Feng Chia University and the chairman of OBI Pharma, Inc.
