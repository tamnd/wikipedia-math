# Precalculus

## o1 subject: Precalculus

In mathematics education, precalculus is a course, or a set of courses, that includes algebra and trigonometry at a level that is designed to prepare students for the study of calculus, thus the name precalculus (from ). Schools often distinguish between algebra and trigonometry as two separate parts of the coursework.
