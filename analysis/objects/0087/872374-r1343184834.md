# Parastatistics

## o1 subject: Parastatistics

In quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics, parastatistics is a hypothetical alternative to the established particle statistics models (Bose–Einstein statistics, Fermi–Dirac statistics and Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics). Other alternatives include anyonic statistics and braid statistics, both of these involving lower spacetime dimensions. Herbert S.&nbsp;Green is credited with the creation of parastatistics in 1953. The particles predicted by parastatistics have not been experimentally observed.
