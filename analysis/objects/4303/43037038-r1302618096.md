# Thaine's theorem

## o1 subject: Thaine's theorem

In mathematics, Thaine's theorem is an analogue of Stickelberger's theorem for real abelian fields, introduced by Francisco . Thaine's method has been used to shorten the proof of the Mazur–Wiles theorem , to prove that some Tate–Shafarevich groups are finite, and in the proof of Mihăilescu's theorem .

## Formulation
Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct odd primes with $q$ not dividing $p-1$. Let $G^+$ be the Galois group of $F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_p^+)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, let $E$ be its group of units, let $C$ be the subgroup of cyclotomic units, and let $Cl^+$ be its class group. If $\theta\in\mathbb Z[G^+]$ annihilates $E/CE^q$ then it annihilates $Cl^+/Cl^{+q}$.
