For $\rho <1$, spiral-ring pattern; $\rho = 1,$ regular spiral; $\rho > 1,$ loose spiral. R is the distance of spiral starting point (0, R) to the center. The calculated x and y have to be rotated backward by ($-\theta$) for plotting.
