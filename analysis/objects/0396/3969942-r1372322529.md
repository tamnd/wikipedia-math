# Tatyana van Aardenne-Ehrenfest

## o1 subject: Tatyana van Aardenne-Ehrenfest

Ehrenfest
Pavlovna
Van Aardenne-Ehrenfest
Tatyana Pavlovna van Aardenne-Ehrenfest (Ehrenfest; October 28, 1905 – November 29, 1984) was a Dutch mathematician. She is known for her contributions to De Bruijn sequences, low-discrepancy sequences, and the BEST theorem.
