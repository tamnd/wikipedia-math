# Fibration symmetry

## o1 subject: Fibration symmetry

Fibration symmetry is a mathematical notion of symmetry in networks that overcomes the limitations of classical group-theoretic symmetry through automorphisms. It is based on the theory of graph fibrations and provides a natural framework for understanding robust cluster synchronization and functional organization in complex networks, especially in biological and dynamical systems. Unlike global symmetries defined by automorphism groups, fibration symmetries are local, structural, and robust under perturbations, making them particularly suited to biological networks such as gene regulatory networks and neural circuits.
