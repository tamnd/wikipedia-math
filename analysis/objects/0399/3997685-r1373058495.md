# Stewart Shapiro

## o1 subject: Stewart Shapiro

Stewart Shapiro (; born 1951) was O'Donnell Professor of Philosophy at the Ohio State University until his retirement, and is also distinguished visiting professor at the University of Connecticut. He is a leading figure in the philosophy of mathematics where he defends the abstract variety of structuralism.
