# Benjamin Kagan

## o1 subject: Benjamin Kagan

Veniamin Fyodorovich Kagan (; 9 March 1869 – 8 May 1953) was a Russian and Soviet mathematician and expert in non-Euclidean geometry. He is the maternal grandfather of mathematicians Yakov Sinai and Grigory Barenblatt.
