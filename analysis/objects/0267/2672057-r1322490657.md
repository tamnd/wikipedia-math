# Artin–Schreier theory

## o1 subject: Artin–Schreier theory

In mathematics, Artin–Schreier theory is a branch of Galois theory, specifically a positive characteristic analogue of Kummer theory, for Galois extensions of degree equal to the characteristic p. introduced Artin–Schreier theory for extensions of prime degree p, and generalized it to extensions of prime power degree pn.
