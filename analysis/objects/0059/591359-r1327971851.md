# Dialetheism

## o1 subject: Dialetheism

Dialetheism (; from Greek grc 'twice' and grc 'truth') is the view that there are statements that are both true and false. More precisely, it is the belief that there can be a true statement whose negation is also true. Such statements are called "true contradictions", dialetheia, or nondualisms.
