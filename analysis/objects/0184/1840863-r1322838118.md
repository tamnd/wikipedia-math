# Right circular cylinder

## o1 subject: Right circular cylinder

A right circular cylinder is a cylinder whose generatrices are perpendicular to the bases. Thus, in a right circular cylinder, the generatrix and the height have the same measurements. It is also less often called a cylinder of revolution, because it can be obtained by rotating a rectangle of sides $r$ and $g$ around one of its sides. Fixing $g$ as the side on which the revolution takes place, we obtain that the side $r$, perpendicular to $g$, will be the measure of the radius of the cylinder.
