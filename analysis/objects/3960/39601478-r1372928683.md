# Alexey Stakhov

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Alexey Stakhov

Alexey Petrovich Stakhov ( ; 7 May 1939 – 25 January 2021) was a Ukrainian-Canadian mathematician, inventor and engineer, who has made contributions to the theory of Fibonacci numbers and the "Golden Section" and their applications in computer science and measurement theory and technology. Doctor of computer science (1972), professor (1974). Author of over 500 publications, 14 books and 65 international patents.
