# Source unfolding

## o1 subject: Source unfolding

In computational geometry, the source unfolding of a convex polyhedron is a net obtained by cutting the polyhedron along the cut locus of a point on the surface of the polyhedron. The cut locus of a point $p$ consists of all points on the surface that have two or more shortest geodesics to $p$. For every convex polyhedron, and every choice of the point $p$ on its surface, cutting the polyhedron on the cut locus will produce a result that can be unfolded into a flat plane, producing the source unfolding. The resulting net may, however, cut across some of the faces of the polyhedron rather than only cutting along its edges.
