# Klavdiya Latysheva

## o1 subject: Klavdiya Latysheva

Yakovlevna
Klavdiya Yakovlevna Latysheva (Russian: ; Ukrainian: ; born 14 March 1897, Kyiv, Russian Empire – 11 May 1956, Kyiv, Ukrainian SSR, Soviet Union (now Kyiv, Ukraine)) was a Soviet mathematician known for her contributions to the theory of differential equations, electrodynamics and probability. She was honoured with the Order of Lenin and the Medal "For Valiant Labour in the Great Patriotic War 1941–1945".
