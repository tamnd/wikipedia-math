# Victor Mazurov

## o1 subject: Victor Mazurov

Victor Danilovich Mazurov (; born January 31, 1943) is a Russian mathematician. He is well known for his works in group theory and is the founder of the Novosibirsk school of finite groups. Mazurov is a Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
