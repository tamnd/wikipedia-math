# Álgebra de Baldor

## o1 subject: Álgebra de Baldor

Álgebra, commonly known as Álgebra de Baldor (Spanish: ), is a book by the Cuban mathematician, lawyer, and professor Aurelio Baldor. The first edition was published on 19 June 1941. Baldor's Algebra contains a total of 5,790 exercises, averaging 19 exercises per test. It is considered one of the most comprehensive works on algebra.
