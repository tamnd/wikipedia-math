# Iranian Mathematical Society

## o2 subject: Iranian Mathematical Society

The Iranian Mathematical Society or IMS () is the main mathematical society in Iran. It was officially registered in 1971 by Mehdi Behzad, who was the first president. The current president of the IMS is Omid Ali Shehni Karamzadeh.
