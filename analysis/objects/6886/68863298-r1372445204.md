## o2 subject: John K. Kruschke

John Kendall Kruschke is an American psychologist and statistician known for his work in connectionist models of human learning,
and in Bayesian statistical analysis.
He is Provost Professor Emeritus
in the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences at Indiana University Bloomington.
He won the Troland Research Award from the National Academy of Sciences in 2002.
