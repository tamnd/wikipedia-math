# Grigorii Fikhtengol'ts

## o1 subject: Grigorii Fikhtengol'ts

Grigorii Mikhailovich Fikhtengol'ts (, ; 8 June 1888 – 26 June 1959) was a Soviet mathematician working on real analysis and functional analysis. Fikhtengol'ts was one of the founders of the Leningrad school of real analysis.
