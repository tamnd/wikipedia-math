# Daniel Hershkowitz

## o1 infobox: infobox officeholder

## o2 subject: Daniel Hershkowitz

Daniel Hershkowitz (; born 2 January 1953 in Haifa, Israel) is an Israeli politician, mathematician, and Orthodox rabbi. Since 2018, he has headed the Israel Civil Service Commission. He is professor emeritus of mathematics at the Technion, and is also rabbi of the Ahuza neighborhood in Haifa. He was president of Bar-Ilan University from 2013-17.
