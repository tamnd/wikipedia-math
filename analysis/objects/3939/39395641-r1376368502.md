# Connection (affine bundle)

## o1 subject: Connection

In differential geometry, the classical notion of a connection (mathematics), most commonly encountered in the context of vector bundles, can be generalised to affine bundles. Let $Y \to X$ be an affine bundle modelled over a vector bundle $\overline{Y} \to X$. A connection $\Gamma$ on $Y \to X$ is called an affine connection if, as a section $\Gamma : Y \to J^{1}Y$ of the jet bundle $J^{1}Y \to Y$ of $Y$, it is an affine bundle morphism over $X$.
