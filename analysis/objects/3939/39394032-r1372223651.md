# Fred van der Blij

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Fred van der Blij

Frederik van der Blij (13May 192327January 2018) was a Dutch mathematician. From 1955 until his retirement in 1988 he was professor at the University of Utrecht. His research focused on number theory, among other fields.
