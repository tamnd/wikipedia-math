# Fundamental theorem of Hilbert spaces

## o1 subject: Fundamental theorem of Hilbert spaces

In mathematics, specifically in functional analysis and Hilbert space theory, the fundamental theorem of Hilbert spaces gives a necessary and sufficient condition for a Hausdorff pre-Hilbert space to be a Hilbert space in terms of the canonical isometry of a pre-Hilbert space into its anti-dual.

## Fundamental theorem
Fundamental theorem of Hilbert spaces
Suppose that $(H,B)$ is a Hausdorff pre-Hilbert space where $B:H\times H\to\mathbb{C}$ is a sesquilinear form that is linear in its first coordinate and antilinear in its second coordinate. Then the canonical linear mapping from $H$ into the anti-dual space of $H$ is surjective if and only if $(H,B)$ is a Hilbert space, in which case the canonical map is a surjective isometry of $H$ onto its anti-dual.
