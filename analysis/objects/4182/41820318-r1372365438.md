# Nikolai Günther

## o1 subject: Nikolai Günther

Nikolai Maximovich Günther (; also transliterated as Nicholas M. Gunther, or N. M. Gjunter; December 17 – May 4, 1941) was a Russian mathematician known for his work in potential theory and in integral and partial differential equations: later studies have uncovered his contributions to the theory of Gröbner bases.
