# Dror Bar-Natan

## o1 subject: Dror Bar-Natan

Dror Bar-Natan (; born January 30, 1966) is a Canadian-Israeli mathematician specializing in knot theory, finite type invariants, and Khovanov homology. He is currently a professor at the University of Toronto.
