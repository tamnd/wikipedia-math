# Ferdinand von Lindemann

## o1 subject: Ferdinand von Lindemann

Carl Louis Ferdinand von Lindemann (12 April 1852 – 6 March 1939) was a German mathematician who proved in 1882 that $\pi$ (pi) is a transcendental number, meaning it is not a root of any nonzero polynomial with rational coefficients.
