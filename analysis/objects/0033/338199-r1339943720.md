# Hasse diagram

## o1 subject: Hasse diagram

In order theory, a Hasse diagram (; ) is a type of mathematical diagram used to represent a finite partially ordered set, in the form of a drawing of its transitive reduction. Concretely, for a partially ordered set $(S,\le)$ one represents each element of $S$ as a vertex in the plane and draws a line segment or curve that goes upward from one vertex $x$ to another vertex $y$ whenever $y$ covers $x$ (that is, whenever $x\ne y$, $x\le y$ and there is no $z$ distinct from $x$ and $y$ with $x\le z\le y$). These curves may cross each other but must not touch any vertices other than their endpoints. Such a diagram, with labeled vertices, uniquely determines its partial order.
