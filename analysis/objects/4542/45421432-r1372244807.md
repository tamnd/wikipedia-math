## o2 subject: Alexander Buchstab

Aleksandr Adol'fovich Buchstab (October 4, 1905 – February 27, 1990; , variously transliterated as Bukhstab, Buhštab, or Bukhshtab) was a Soviet mathematician who worked in number theory and was "known for his work in sieve methods". He is the namesake of the Buchstab function, which he wrote about in 1937.
