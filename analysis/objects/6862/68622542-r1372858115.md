# Xiangyu Zhou

## o1 subject: Xiangyu Zhou

Xiangyu Zhou (Zhou Xiangyu, , born March 1965) is a Chinese mathematician, specializing in several complex variables and complex geometry. He is known for his 1998 proof of the "extended future tube conjecture", which was an unsolved problem for almost forty years.
