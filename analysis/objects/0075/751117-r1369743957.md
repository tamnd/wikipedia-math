## o1 subject: Charles-Jean de La Vallée Poussin

Charles-Jean Étienne Gustave Nicolas, baron de La Vallée Poussin (; 14 August 18662 March 1962) was a Belgian mathematician. He is best known for proving the prime number theorem.
