# Commensurability (mathematics)

## o1 subject: Commensurability

In mathematics, two non-zero real numbers a and b are said to be commensurable if their ratio $\tfrac{a}{b}$ is a rational number; otherwise a and b are called incommensurable. (Recall that a rational number is one that is equivalent to the ratio of two integers.) There is a more general notion of commensurability in group theory.
