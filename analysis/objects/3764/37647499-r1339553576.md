# Diamond operator

## o1 subject: Diamond operator

⋄
In number theory, the diamond operators 〈d〉 are operators acting on the space of modular forms for the congruence subgroup Γ1(N), given by the action of a matrix (${}^{a b}_{c \delta}$) in Γ0(N) where δ ≈ d mod N. The diamond operators form an abelian group and commute with the Hecke operators.
