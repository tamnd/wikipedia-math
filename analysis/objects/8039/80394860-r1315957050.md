# Rajaditya (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Rajaditya

Rajaditya ( 1190 CE) was a Jain mathematician, scholar, and poet. In the court of Hoysala king Vishnuvardhana he was known as the earliest author of mathematical works in Kannada. He is remembered for his contributions to medieval Indian mathematics and literature in Karnataka.
