## o1 subject: Dmitry Fuchs

Borisovich
Dmitry Borisovich Fuchs (, born 30 September 1939) is a Soviet and American mathematician, specializing in the representation theory of infinite-dimensional Lie groups and in topology. He was born in Kazan, Soviet Tatarstan.
