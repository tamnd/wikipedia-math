# Hyman Levy

## o1 subject: Hyman Levy

Hyman Levy (28 February 1889 – 27 February 1975) was a Scottish-Jewish mathematician, philosopher, author, political activist, and fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh. For many years he taught mathematics at Imperial College London, eventually retiring as Emeritus Professor.
