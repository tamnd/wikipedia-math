# Tower of fields

## o1 subject: Tower of fields

In mathematics, specifically number theory, a tower of fields is a sequence of field extensions

## o2 section: Examples

## o3 example: Examples

## Examples
- $\mathbb{Q} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is a finite tower of the rational, real and complex numbers.
- The sequence obtained by setting $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}$ and letting
$F_n = F_{n-1}\big(2^{1/2^n}\big)$
for $n\geq 1$ (i.e. $F_n$ is obtained from $F_{n-1}$ by adjoining a $2^n$-th root of $2$) is an infinite tower.
- Similarly, if $p$ is a prime number, the pth cyclotomic tower of $\mathbb{Q}$ is obtained by setting $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}$ and letting $F_n$ be the field obtained by adjoining to $\mathbb{Q}$ the $p^n$-th roots of unity. This tower is of fundamental importance in Iwasawa theory.
- The Golod–Shafarevich theorem shows that there are infinite towers obtained by iterating the Hilbert class field construction to a number field.
