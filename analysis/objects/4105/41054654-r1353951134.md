# Giovanni Carandino

## o1 subject: Giovanni Carandino

Giovanni Carandino, also known as Ioannis Karandinos (), and sometimes as Jean Carantino or John Carandino (1784–1834), was an ethnically Greek mathematician, founder of the Greek mathematics school, and translator into Greek of the major French works on analysis in the early 19th century.
