# William Playfair

## o1 infobox: infobox person

## o2 subject: William Playfair

William Playfair (22 September 1759 – 11 February 1823) was a Scottish engineer and political economist. The founder of graphical methods of statistics, Playfair invented several types of diagrams: in 1786 he introduced the line, area and bar chart of economic data, and in 1801 he published what were likely the first pie chart and circle graph, used to show part-whole relations. Playfair has been reported to have been a secret agent for the British Government, although this has sometimes been a subject of controversy.

### Playfair cycle
The following quotation, known as the "Playfair cycle," has achieved notoriety as it pertains to the "Tytler cycle":
:...wealth and power have never been long permanent in any place.
: ...they travel over the face of the earth,
: something like a caravan of merchants.
: On their arrival, every thing is found green and fresh;
: while they remain all is bustle and abundance,
: and, when gone, all is left trampled down, barren, and bare.
