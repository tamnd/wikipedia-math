# Viktor Nemytskii

## o1 subject: Viktor Nemytskii

Viktor Vladimirovich Nemytskii (; 22 November 1900 – 7 August 1967) was a Soviet mathematician who introduced Nemytskii operators and the Nemytskii plane (Moore plane). He was married to Nina Bari, who was also a mathematician.
