# Cousin problems

## o1 subject: Cousin problems

In mathematics, the Cousin problems are two questions in several complex variables, concerning the existence of meromorphic functions that are specified in terms of local data. They were introduced in special cases by Pierre Cousin (mathematician) in 1895. They are now posed, and solved, for any complex manifold M, in terms of conditions on M.
