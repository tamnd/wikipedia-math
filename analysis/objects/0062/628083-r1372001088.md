# Thomas Digges

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Thomas Digges

Thomas Digges (; – 24 August 1595) was an English mathematician and astronomer. He was the first to expound the Copernican system in English but discarded the notion of a fixed shell of immoveable stars to postulate infinitely many stars at varying distances. He was also first to postulate the "dark night sky paradox".
