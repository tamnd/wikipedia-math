## o2 subject: Stefan Mazurkiewicz

Stefan Mazurkiewicz (Polish: m; 25 September 1888 – 19 June 1945) was a Polish mathematician. He was a representative of the Warsaw School of Mathematics and worked in mathematical analysis, topology, and probability.
