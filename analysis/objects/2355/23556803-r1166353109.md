# Duhem–Margules equation

## o1 subject: Duhem–Margules equation

The Duhem–Margules equation, named for Pierre Duhem and Max Margules, is a thermodynamic statement of the relationship between the two components of a single liquid where the vapour mixture is regarded as an ideal gas:

## o2 section: Derivation

## o3 equation: 1

$n_A \mathrm{d} \mu_A + n_B \mathrm{d} \mu_B = 0$

## o4 equation: 2

$x_A \mathrm{d} \mu_A + x_B \mathrm{d} \mu_B = 0$

## o5 equation: 3

$\mathrm{d} \mu_A = \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_A}{\mathrm{d}x_A} \right)_{T,P}\mathrm{d}x_A$

## o6 equation: 4

$\mathrm{d} \mu_B = \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_B}{\mathrm{d}x_B} \right)_{T,P}\mathrm{d}x_B$

## o7 equation: 5

$x_A \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_A}{\mathrm{d}x_A} \right)_{T,P}\mathrm{d}x_A + x_B \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_B}{\mathrm{d}x_B} \right)_{T,P}\mathrm{d}x_B = 0$

## o8 equation: 6

$x_A \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_A}{\mathrm{d}x_A} \right)_{T,P} = x_B \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_B}{\mathrm{d}x_B} \right)_{T,P}$

## o9 equation: 7

$\frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_A}{\mathrm{d}x_A} = RT \frac{\mathrm{d} \ln P_A}{\mathrm{d}x_A}$

## o10 equation: 8

$\frac{\mathrm{d}\mu_B}{\mathrm{d}x_B} = RT \frac{\mathrm{d} \ln P_B}{\mathrm{d}x_B}$
