# Narayana number

## o1 subject: Narayana number

In combinatorics, the Narayana numbers $\operatorname{N}(n, k), n \in \mathbb{N}^+, 1 \le k \le n$ form a triangular array of natural numbers, called the Narayana triangle, that occur in various counting problems. They are named after Canadian mathematician T. V. Narayana (1930–1987).

## o4 statement: Formula

## Formula
The Narayana numbers can be expressed in terms of binomial coefficients:
$\operatorname{N}(n, k) = \frac{1}{k} {n-1 \choose k-1} {n \choose k-1} = \frac{1}{n} {n \choose k} {n \choose k-1}$
