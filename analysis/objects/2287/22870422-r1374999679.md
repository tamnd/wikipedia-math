# Charles Hermite

## o1 subject: Charles Hermite

Charles Hermite (; 24 December 1822 – 14 January 1901) was a French mathematician who studied analysis, number theory, and algebra. One of his most remarkable achievements was the proof of the transcendence of the number e.
