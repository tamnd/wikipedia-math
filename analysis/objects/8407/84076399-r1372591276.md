# Alex Chui

## o1 subject: Alex Chui

Alex Chui Tsz Fung (; born 2008), is a Hong Kong-born British student who is currently the most decorated International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) contestant since July 2026, with five gold medals and two silver medals. He represented Hong Kong and later represented the United Kingdom. He is the first and only competitor to ever win seven medals consecutively at the IMO.
