# Askold Vinogradov

## o1 subject: Askold Vinogradov

Askold Ivanovich Vinogradov (; 1929 – 31 December 2005) was a Russian mathematician who worked in analytic number theory. The Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem is partially named after him.
