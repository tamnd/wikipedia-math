# Free boundary problem

## o1 subject: Free boundary problem

In mathematics, a free boundary problem (FB problem) is a partial differential equation to be solved for both an unknown function $u$ and an unknown domain $\Omega$. The segment $\Gamma$ of the boundary of $\Omega$ which is not known at the outset of the problem is the free boundary.
