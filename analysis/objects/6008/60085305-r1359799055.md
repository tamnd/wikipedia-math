# Roberto Bachi

## o1 infobox: infobox academic

## o2 subject: Roberto Bachi

Roberto Bachi (; 16 January 1909 – 26 November 1995) was an Italian-Israeli statistician and demographer, and founder of the Israel Central Bureau of Statistics. His research focused on the graphical presentation of statistics and the renewal of Jewish demographic studies in the diaspora.
