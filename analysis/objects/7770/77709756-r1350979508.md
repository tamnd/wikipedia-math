## o2 subject: Estonian Mathematical Society

The Estonian Mathematical Society (Estonian: , EMS) is a mathematical society founded in Estonia in February 1926.
The EMS is based in Tartu, and is a founding member of the European Mathematical Society. It is recognised by the International Mathematical Union.
