# Arc (projective geometry)

## o1 subject: Arc

A (simple) arc in finite projective geometry is a set of points which satisfies, in an intuitive way, a feature of curved figures in continuous geometries. Loosely speaking, they are sets of points that are far from "line-like" in a plane or far from "plane-like" in a three-dimensional space. In this finite setting it is typical to include the number of points in the set in the name, so these simple arcs are called $\boldsymbol{k}$-arcs. An important generalization of $k$-arcs, also referred to as arcs in the literature, is the ($k, d$)-arcs.
