# Nail H. Ibragimov

## o1 subject: Nail H. Ibragimov

Hairullovich
Nail Hairullovich Ibragimov (, January 18, 1939 – November 4, 2018) was a Russian mathematician and mathematical physicist. At his death he was a professor emeritus at the Blekinge Institute of Technology. Ibragimov's research area was differential calculus, group analysis and mathematical physics. He was the author of many books on mathematics and mathematical physics.
