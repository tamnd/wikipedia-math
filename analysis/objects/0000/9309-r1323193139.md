# Extractor (mathematics)

## o1 subject: Extractor

An $(N,M,D,K,\epsilon)$-extractor is a bipartite graph with $N$ nodes on the left and $M$ nodes on the right such that each node on the left has $D$ neighbors (on the right), which has the added property that
for any subset $A$ of the left vertices of size at least $K$, the distribution on right vertices obtained by choosing a random node in $A$ and then following a random edge to get a node x on the right side is $\epsilon$-close to the uniform distribution in terms of total variation distance.
