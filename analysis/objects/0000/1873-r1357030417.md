# Anthemius of Tralles

## o1 subject: Anthemius of Tralles

Anthemius of Tralles (, Medieval Greek: , Anthémios o Trallianós; 474 – 533 x 558) was a Byzantine Greek from Tralles who worked as a geometer and architect in Constantinople, the capital of the Byzantine Empire. With Isidore of Miletus, he designed the Hagia Sophia for Justinian&nbsp;I.
