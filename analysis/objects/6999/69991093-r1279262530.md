## o9 statement/fact: Properties

### Properties
- The center of the Kiepert hyperbola lies on the nine-point circle. The center is the midpoint of the line segment joining the isogonic centers of triangle $ABC$ which are the triangle centers X(13) and X(14) in the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers.
- The image of the Kiepert hyperbola under the isogonal transformation is the Brocard axis of triangle $ABC$ which is the line joining the symmedian point and the circumcenter.
- Let $P$ be a point in the plane of a nonequilateral triangle $ABC$ and let $p$ be the trilinear polar of $P$ with respect to $ABC$. The locus of the points $P$ such that $p$ is perpendicular to the Euler line of $ABC$ is the Kiepert hyperbola.
