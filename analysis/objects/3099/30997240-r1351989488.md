# Ending lamination theorem

## o1 subject: Ending lamination theorem

In hyperbolic geometry, the ending lamination theorem, originally conjectured by as the eleventh problem out of his twenty-four questions, states that hyperbolic 3-manifolds with finitely generated fundamental groups are determined by their topology together with certain "end invariants", which are geodesic laminations on some surfaces in the boundary of the manifold.
