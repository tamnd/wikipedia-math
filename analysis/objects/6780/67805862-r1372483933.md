# Aleksei Markushevich

## o1 subject: Aleksei Markushevich

Aleksei Ivanovich Markushevich (; 2 April, Petrozavodsk – 7 June 1979, Moscow) was a Soviet mathematician, mathematical educator, and historian of mathematics. He is known for the Farrell–Markushevich theorem.
