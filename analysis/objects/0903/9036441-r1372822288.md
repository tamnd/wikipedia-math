# Anatoly Vershik

## o1 subject: Anatoly Vershik

Anatoly Moiseevich Vershik (; 28 December 1933 – 14 February 2024) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician. He is most famous for his joint work with Sergei V. Kerov on representations of infinite symmetric groups and applications to the longest increasing subsequences.
