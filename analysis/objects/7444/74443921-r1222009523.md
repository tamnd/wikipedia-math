# 1971 Indonesian census

## o1 subject: 1971 Indonesian census

The 1971 Indonesian census was the second census of Indonesia as a sovereign state. It enumerated a total population of 119,208,229. Its population density during the 1971 census was 119208229 inhabitants/km2.
