# 1980 Indonesian census

## o1 subject: 1980 Indonesian census

The 1980 Indonesian census was the third census of Indonesia as a sovereign state. It enumerated a total population of 147,490,298. Its population density during the 1980 census was 147490298 inhabitants/km2.
