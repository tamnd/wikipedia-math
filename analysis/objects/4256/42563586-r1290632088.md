# Dioscorides (Stoic)

## o1 subject: Dioscorides

Dioscorides (, fl. 225 BC), sometimes known as Dioscurides, was a Stoic philosopher, the father of Zeno of Tarsus and a pupil of Chrysippus. All other information has been lost.
