# David Dolidze

## o1 subject: David Dolidze

David Egorovich Dolidze (დავით დოლიძე, ) (2 February – 1960) was a Georgian and Soviet mathematician, known from his work in the mathematical theory of fluid motion. In particular he rediscovered an important uniqueness theorem for the classical solutions to the Navier–Stokes equations for an incompressible fluid, previously proved by Emanuele Foà.
