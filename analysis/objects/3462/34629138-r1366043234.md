# Conformal geometric algebra

## o1 subject: Conformal geometric algebra

Conformal geometric algebra (CGA) is the geometric algebra constructed over the resultant space of a map from points in an $n$-dimensional base space $\mathbf{R}^{p,q}$ to null vectors in $\mathbf{R}^{p+1,q+1}$. This allows operations on the base space, including reflections, rotations and translations to be represented using versors of the geometric algebra; and it is found that points, lines, planes, circles and spheres gain particularly natural and computationally amenable representations.
