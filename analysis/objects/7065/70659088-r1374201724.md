# Nina Virchenko

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Nina Virchenko

Nina Opanasivna Virchenko (; born May 5, 1930) is a Ukrainian mathematician, academic, author, and member of the Ukrainian resistance movement. While a student in Kyiv in 1948, she was arrested on charges of Ukrainian nationalism, and was a political prisoner in a gulag in Eastern Siberia for six years.
