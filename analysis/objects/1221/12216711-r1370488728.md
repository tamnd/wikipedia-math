## Example
### The case m = 2
$\begin{aligned}
& {} \quad \binom{2}{0}(w+2)^1(z+0)^0+\binom{2}{1}(w+1)^0(z+1)^1+\binom{2}{2}(w+0)^{-1}(z+2)^2 \\
& = (w+2)+2(z+1)+\frac{(z+2)^2}{w} \\
& = \frac{(z+w+2)^2}{w}.
\end{aligned}$
