## Applications
Equation ((1)), which is numerically approximated with MPDATA, is used in modelling a wide range of transport phenomena across different scales and . MPDATA has documented applications in the following domains:
- numerical weather prediction (NWP) systems:
compressible dynamical core for the COSMO system
nonhydrostatic finite-volume dynamical core for the ECMWF Integrated Forecast System (IFS)
- ocean modelling:
passive tracer advection in terrain-following coordinates in the Regional Ocean Modeling System (ROMS)
- large eddy simulations:
moist atmospheric flows (including representation of cloud and precipitation processes)
multi-phase particle-laden flows (e.g., contrail formation, aerosol-cloud-precipitation interactions)
turbulent magnetohydrodynamics (including Hall magnetohydrodynamics)
simulations of wind-driven sand dune dynamics
simulations of flows past complex structures (e.g. the Pentagon building and the Palace of Culture and Science in Warsaw)
- direct numerical simulations:
multi-phase flows (fog formation)
- solvers for shallow water equations resolving:
double-gyre circulation
wetting–drying areas
- aerosol size-spectrum dynamics:
advection problem in particle size dimension
two-dimensional advection in size-spectral and spatial dimensions
- mathematical finance:
solutions to the Black-Scholes-type equations (a stochastic differential equation transformed into a partial differential equation using Itô's lemma) cast as an advective transport problem for pricing of European and American options
