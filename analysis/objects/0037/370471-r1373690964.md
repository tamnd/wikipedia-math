## o8 subject: Rodrigues

Rodrigues ( ; Kreol rodrig: ) is an autonomous outer island of the Republic of Mauritius, of approximately 108 and about 560 east of the main island of Mauritius. Along with the French overseas department of Réunion, Rodrigues and Mauritius are part of the Mascarene Islands.
