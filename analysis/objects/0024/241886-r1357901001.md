# Secant line

## o1 subject: Secant line

In geometry, a secant is a line that intersects a curve at a minimum of two distinct points.
The word secant comes from the Latin word , meaning "to cut". In the case of a circle, a secant intersects the circle at exactly two points. A chord is the line segment determined by the two points, that is, the interval on the secant whose ends are the two points.
