# Lewis's triviality result

## o1 subject: Lewis's triviality result

In the mathematical theory of probability, Lewis's triviality result (named after David Lewis) is a theorem about the impossibility of systematically equating the conditional probability $P(B\mid A)$ with the probability of a so-called conditional event, $A \rightarrow B$.
