# Q-category

## o1 subject: Q-category

In mathematics, a Q-category or almost quotient category is a category that is a "milder version of a Grothendieck site." A Q-category is a coreflective subcategory. The Q stands for a quotient.

## o3 citation: :0

## o4 section: Definition

## o5 definition: Definition

## Definition
A Q-category is defined by the formula
$$\mathbb{A} : (u^* \dashv u_*) : \bar A \stackrel{\overset{u^*}{\leftarrow}}{\underset{u_*}{\to}} A$$
where $u^*$ is the left adjoint in a pair of adjoint functors and is a full and faithful functor.

## o6 section: Examples

## o7 example: Examples

## Examples
- The category of presheaves over any Q-category is itself a Q-category.
- For any category, one can define the Q-category of cones.
- There is a Q-category of sieves.
