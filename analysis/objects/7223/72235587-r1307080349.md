# Moses Haida

## o1 subject: Moses Haida

Moses ben Joseph Haida (; 17th–18th centuries) was a German mathematician from Hamburg. He was a grandson of Samuel Haida, author of Zikkukin de-nura. He was the author of Sefer ma'aseh ḥoresh ve-ḥoshev, an arithmetic, written at the time of the great fire of Altona (Frankfurt, 1711).
