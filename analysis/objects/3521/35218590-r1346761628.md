# Ludwig Adolf Sohncke

## o1 subject: Ludwig Adolf Sohncke

Ludwig Adolf Sohncke (20 June 1807 in Königsberg (now Kaliningrad), Kingdom of Prussia – 16 January 1853 in Halle, Kingdom of Prussia) was a German mathematician. He was father of the mathematician Leonhard Sohncke.
