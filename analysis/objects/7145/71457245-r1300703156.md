# Yukio Matsumoto

## o1 subject: Yukio Matsumoto

Yukio Matsumoto (, Matsumoto Yukio; born 1944) is a Japanese mathematician, who worked mostly in the field of geometric topology and low-dimensional topology. He was a former professor for mathematics at the University of Tokyo.
