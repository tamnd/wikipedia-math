## Notation
There is no uniformly accepted notation for these groups. Some authors use F in place of Fi (F22, for example).
Fischer's notation for them was M(22), M(23) and M(24)′, which emphasised their close relationship with the three largest
Mathieu groups, M22, M23 and M24.
One particular source of confusion is that Fi24 is sometimes used to refer to the simple group Fi24′, and is sometimes used to refer to the full 3-transposition group (which is twice the size).
