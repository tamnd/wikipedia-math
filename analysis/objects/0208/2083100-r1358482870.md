# Isogonal conjugate

## o1 subject: Isogonal conjugate

In geometry, the isogonal conjugate of a point $P$ with respect to a triangle $\triangle ABC$ is constructed by reflecting the lines $PA$, $PB$, $PC$ about the angle bisectors of $A$, $B$, $C$ respectively. These three reflected lines concur at the isogonal conjugate of $P$. (This definition applies only to points not on a sideline of triangle $\triangle ABC$.) This is a direct result of the trigonometric form of Ceva's theorem.
