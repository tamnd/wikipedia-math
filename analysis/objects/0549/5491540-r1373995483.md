# Lamplighter group

## o1 subject: Lamplighter group

In group theory, the lamplighter group $L$ is the restricted wreath product $\mathbb{Z}_2 \wr \mathbb{Z}$.

## Generalizations
One can also define lamplighter groups $L_n = \mathbb{Z}_n \wr \mathbb{Z}$, with $n \in \mathbb{N}$, so that "lamps" may have more than just the option of "off" and "on."
Higher dimensional versions of these groups of the form $L_n = \mathbb{Z}_n \wr \mathbb{Z}^d$ for a further $d \in \mathbb{N}$ are sometimes also considered.
