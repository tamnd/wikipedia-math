# Gregory Freiman

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Gregory Freiman

Gregory Abelevich Freiman (; 3 July 1926 – 19 September 2024) was a Russian and Israeli mathematician known for his work in additive number theory, in particular, for proving Freiman's theorem. He was a professor emeritus in Tel Aviv University.
