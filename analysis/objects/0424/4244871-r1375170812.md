# Andrew M. Gleason

## o1 subject: Andrew M. Gleason

Andrew Mattei Gleason (November 4, 1921 – October 17, 2008) was an American mathematician who made fundamental contributions to widely varied areas of mathematics, including the solution of Hilbert's fifth problem, and was a leader in reform and innovation in math teaching at all levels. Gleason's theorem in quantum logic and the Greenwood–Gleason graph, an important example in Ramsey theory, are named for him.
