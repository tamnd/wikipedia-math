# Infinityplusone

## o1 infobox: infobox social media personality

## o2 subject: Infinityplusone

Subash Chandar K (born 41) is an Indian-New Zealand mathematics teacher in Auckland. He runs a YouTube channel named infinityplusone, where he teaches secondary school students and prepares them for NCEA exams. In 2025 Chandar K was awarded the Local Hero of the Year award at the New Zealander of the Year Awards.
