# A. W. Faber Model 366

## o1 subject: A. W. Faber Model 366

The A. W. Faber Model 366 was an unusual model of slide rule, manufactured in Germany by the A.&nbsp;W. Faber Company around 1909, with scales that followed a system invented by Johannes Schumacher (1858–1930) that used discrete logarithms to calculate products of integers without approximation.
