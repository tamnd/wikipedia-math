# Order-7 triangular tiling

## o1 subject: Order-7 triangular tiling

In geometry, the order-7 triangular tiling is a regular tiling of the hyperbolic plane with a Schläfli symbol of {3,7}.

## o4 table

| The symmetry group of the order-7 triangular tiling has fundamental domain the (2,3,7) Schwarz triangle, which yields this tiling. | The small cubicuboctahedron is a polyhedral immersion of the Klein quartic, which, like all Hurwitz surfaces, is a quotient of this tiling.
