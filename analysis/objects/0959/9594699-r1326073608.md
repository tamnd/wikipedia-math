# Risk difference

## o1 subject: Risk difference

The risk difference (RD), excess risk, or attributable risk is the difference between the risk of an outcome in the exposed group and the unexposed group. It is computed as $I_e - I_u$, where $I_e$ is the incidence in the exposed group, and $I_u$ is the incidence in the unexposed group. If the risk of an outcome is increased by the exposure, the term absolute risk increase (ARI) is used, and computed as $I_e - I_u$. Equivalently, if the risk of an outcome is decreased by the exposure, the term absolute risk reduction (ARR) is used, and computed as $I_u - I_e$.
