## o1 subject: Daoxing Xia

Xia Daoxing
Daoxing Xia () is a Chinese American mathematician. He is professor emeritus at the Department of Mathematics, Vanderbilt University in the United States. He was elected an academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 1980.
