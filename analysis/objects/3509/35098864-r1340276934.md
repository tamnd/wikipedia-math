# Narasimhan–Seshadri theorem

## o1 subject: Narasimhan–Seshadri theorem

In mathematics, the Narasimhan–Seshadri theorem, proved by , says that a holomorphic vector bundle over a compact Riemann surface is stable if and only if it comes from an irreducible projective unitary representation of the fundamental group.
