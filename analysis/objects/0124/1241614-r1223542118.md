# Atoroidal

## o1 subject: Atoroidal

In mathematics, an atoroidal 3-manifold is one that does not contain an essential torus.
There are two major variations in this terminology: an essential torus may be defined geometrically, as an embedded, non-boundary parallel, incompressible torus, or it may be defined algebraically, as a subgroup $\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}$ of its fundamental group that is not conjugate to a peripheral subgroup (i.e., the image of the map on fundamental group induced by an inclusion of a boundary component).
