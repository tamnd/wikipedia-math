## Variations
An important variation is the truncated moment problem, which studies the properties of measures with fixed first $k$ moments (for a finite $k$). Results on the truncated moment problem have numerous applications to extremal problems, optimisation and limit theorems in probability theory.

## o17 statement/theorem: Theorem (Fréchet-Shohat)

If $\mu$ is a determinate measure (i.e. its moments determine it uniquely), and the measures $\mu_n$ are such that
$$\forall k \geq 0 \quad \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} m_k\left[\mu_n\right]=m_k[\mu],$$
then $\mu_n \rightarrow \mu$ in distribution.

## o18 statement/corollary: Corollary

If a sequence of probability distributions $\nu_n$ satisfy
$$m_{2k}[\nu_n] \to \frac{(2k)!}{2^k k!}; \quad m_{2k+1}[\nu_n] \to 0$$
then $\nu_n$ converges to $N(0, 1)$ in distribution.
