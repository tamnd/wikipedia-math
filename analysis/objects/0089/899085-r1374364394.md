# Cissoid

## o1 subject: Cissoid

In geometry, a cissoid (; grc) is a plane curve generated from two given curves $C_{1}$, $C_{2}$ and a point $O$ (the pole). Let $L$ be a variable line passing through $O$ and intersecting $C_{1}$ at $P_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ at $P_{2}$. Let $P$ be the point on $L$ so that $\overline{OP} = \overline{P_1 P_2}.$ (There are actually two such points but $P$ is chosen so that $P$ is in the same direction from $O$ as $P_{2}$ is from $P_{1}$.) Then the locus of such points $P$ is defined to be the cissoid of the curves $C_{1}$, $C_{2}$ relative to $O$.
