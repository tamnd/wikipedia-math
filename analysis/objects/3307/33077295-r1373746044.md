# Almgren regularity theorem

## o1 subject: Almgren regularity theorem

In geometric measure theory, a field of mathematics, the Almgren regularity theorem, proved by , states that the singular set of a mass-minimizing surface has codimension at least 2. Almgren's proof of this was 955 pages long. Within the proof many new ideas are introduced, such as monotonicity of a frequency function and the use of a center manifold to perform a more intricate blow-up procedure.
