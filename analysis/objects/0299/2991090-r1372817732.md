# Hodgkin–Huxley model

## o1 subject: Hodgkin–Huxley model

The Hodgkin–Huxley model, or conductance-based model, is a mathematical model that shows why and how action potentials in neurons are initiated and propagated. It is a set of nonlinear differential equations that approximates the electrical engineering characteristics of excitable cells such as neurons and muscle cells. It is a continuous-time dynamical system.
