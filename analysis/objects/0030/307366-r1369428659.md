# Vaisheshika

## o1 subject: Vaisheshika

Vaisheshika (IAST: Vaiśeṣika; /vaɪˈʃɛʂɪkə/; ) is one of the six schools of Hindu philosophy from ancient India. In its early stages, Vaiśeṣika was an independent philosophy with its own metaphysics, epistemology, logic, ethics, and soteriology. Over time, the Vaiśeṣika system became similar in its philosophical procedures, ethical conclusions and soteriology to the Nyāya school of Hinduism, but retained its difference in epistemology and metaphysics.
