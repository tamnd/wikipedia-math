# Blumberg theorem

## o1 subject: Blumberg theorem

In mathematics, the Blumberg theorem states that for any real function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ there is a dense subset $D$ of $\mathbb{R}$ such that the restriction of $f$ to $D$ is continuous. It is named after its discoverer, the Russian-American mathematician Henry Blumberg.

## o3 example: Examples

## Examples
For instance, the restriction of the Dirichlet function (the indicator function of the rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$) to $\mathbb{Q}$ is continuous, although the Dirichlet function is nowhere continuous in $\mathbb{R}.$
