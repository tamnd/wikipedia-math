## Definition
The Al-Salam–Carlitz polynomials are given in terms of basic hypergeometric functions by
$U_n^{(a)}(x;q) = (-a)^nq^{n(n-1)/2}{}_2\phi_1(q^{-n}, x^{-1};0;q,qx/a)$
$V_n^{(a)}(x;q) = (-a)^nq^{-n(n-1)/2}{}_2\phi_0(q^{-n}, x;-;q,q^n/a)$
