## Definition
The Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials are given in terms of basic hypergeometric functions and the q-Pochhammer symbol by
$Q_n(x;a,b;q) = \frac{(ab;q)_n}{a^n}{}_3\phi_2(q^{-n}, ae^{i\theta}, ae^{-i\theta}; ab,0; q,q)$
where x = cos(θ).
