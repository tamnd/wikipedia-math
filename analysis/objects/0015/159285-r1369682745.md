# Abel Prize

## o1 infobox: infobox award

## o2 subject: Abel Prize

The Abel Prize ( ; ) is awarded annually by the King of Norway to one or more outstanding mathematicians. It is named after the Norwegian mathematician Niels Henrik Abel (1802–1829) and directly modeled after the Nobel Prizes; as such, it is sometimes considered the Nobel Prize of mathematics. It comes with a commemorative trophy and a monetary award of 7.5 million Norwegian kroner (NOK, about 7500000 in ; increased from 6 million NOK in 2019).
