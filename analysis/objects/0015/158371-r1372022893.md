## o2 subject: John Edensor Littlewood

John Edensor Littlewood (9 June 1885 – 6 September 1977) was a British mathematician. He worked on topics relating to analysis, number theory, and differential equations and had lengthy collaborations with G.&nbsp;H. Hardy, Srinivasa Ramanujan and Mary Cartwright.
