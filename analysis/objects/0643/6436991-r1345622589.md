# Virtual knot

## o1 problem

[Extension of Jones polynomial to general 3-manifolds.] Can the original Jones polynomial, which is defined for 1-links in the 3-sphere (the 3-ball, the 3-space R3), be extended for 1-links in any 3-manifold?

## o2 subject: Virtual knot

In knot theory, a virtual knot is a generalization of knots in 3-dimensional Euclidean space, $\mathbf{R}^{3}$, to knots in thickened surfaces $\Sigma \times [0,1]$ modulo an equivalence relation called stabilization/destabilization.
