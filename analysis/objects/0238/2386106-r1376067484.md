## o3 subject: Jerzy Neyman

Jerzy Spława-Neyman (also known as Jerzy Neyman; ; April 16, 1894 – August 5, 1981) was a Polish mathematician and statistician. He made major contributions to the development of modern statistical theory. Neyman introduced the modern concept of a confidence interval into statistical hypothesis testing and, with Egon Pearson, revised Ronald Fisher's null hypothesis testing. Neyman allocation, an optimal strategy for choosing sample sizes in stratified sampling, is named for him.
