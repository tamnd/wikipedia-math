# Mooney–Rivlin solid

## o1 subject: Mooney–Rivlin solid

In continuum mechanics, a Mooney–Rivlin solid is a hyperelastic material model where the strain energy density function $W\,$ is a linear combination of two invariants of the left Cauchy–Green deformation tensor $\boldsymbol{B}$. The model was proposed by Melvin Mooney in 1940 and expressed in terms of invariants by Ronald Rivlin in 1948.
