# Sigma Zeta

## o1 infobox: infobox fraternity

## o2 subject: Sigma Zeta

Sigma Zeta () is a North American honor society founded in 1925 to recognize undergraduate excellence in the natural sciences, computer science, and mathematics. The society's purpose is to encourage and foster the attainment of knowledge in the natural and computer sciences and mathematics. It has chartered more than eighty chapters in the United States and Canada.
