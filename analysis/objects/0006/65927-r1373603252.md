# Angular velocity

## o1 infobox: infobox physical quantity

## o2 subject: Angular velocity

In kinematics, angular velocity (symbol $\omega$ or $\vec{\omega}$, the lowercase Greek letter omega), also known as the angular frequency vector, is a three-dimensional Euclidean vector that uniquely identifies the plane, direction and angular speed of rotation of a particle rotating in a circle at constant speed in three dimensions.
