# Frederick Bagemihl

## o1 subject: Frederick Bagemihl

Frederick Bagemihl (June 25, 1920 – April 12, 2002) was an American mathematician at University of Wisconsin&ndash;Milwaukee. He was a visiting scholar at the Institute for Advanced Study from 1953 to 1955.
