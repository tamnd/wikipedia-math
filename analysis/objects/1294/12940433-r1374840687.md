# March Boedihardjo

## o1 subject: March Boedihardjo

(沈) Boedihardjo
__NOTOC__
March Tian Boedihardjo () is a Hong Kong mathematician. He is a former child prodigy of ethnic Hokkien descent with ancestry from Anxi, Quanzhou, China. As of 2023, he is an assistant professor of mathematics at Michigan State University.
