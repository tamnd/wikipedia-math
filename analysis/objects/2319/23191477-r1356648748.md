# Eckmann–Hilton duality

## o1 subject: Eckmann–Hilton duality

In the mathematical disciplines of algebraic topology and homotopy theory, Eckmann–Hilton duality in its most basic form, consists of taking a given diagram for a particular concept and reversing the direction of all arrows, much as in category theory with the idea of the opposite category. A significantly deeper form argues that the fact that the dual notion of a limit is a colimit allows us to change the Eilenberg&ndash;Steenrod axioms for homology to give axioms for cohomology. It is named after Beno Eckmann and Peter Hilton.
