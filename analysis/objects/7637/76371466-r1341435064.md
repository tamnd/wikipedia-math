# Heisook Lee

## o1 subject: Heisook Lee

Heisook Lee (, born 1948) is a South Korean mathematician and activist for gender equality in mathematics. She is retired as a professor of mathematics and dean at Ewha Womans University. Her mathematical research has concerned abstract algebra and algebraic coding theory, including work on self-dual codes and bent functions.
