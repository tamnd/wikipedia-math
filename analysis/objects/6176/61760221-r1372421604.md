# Haya Kaspi

## o1 subject: Haya Kaspi

Haya Kaspi (; born 6 October 1948) is an Israeli operations researcher, statistician, and probability theorist. She is a professor emeritus of industrial engineering and management at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.
