# Franco of Liège

## o1 subject: Franco of Liège

Franco of Liège (died ) was an 11th-century mathematician who worked on squaring the circle. He was the chancellor (attested 1057) and later scholaster of Saint Lambert's Cathedral, Liège (before 1066-after 1083).
