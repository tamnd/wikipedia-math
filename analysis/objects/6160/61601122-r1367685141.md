# Nkechi Agwu

## o1 subject: Nkechi Agwu

Nkechi Madonna Adeleine Agwu LL-Q33578_(ibo)-Goodymeraj-Nkechi_Madonna_Adeleine_Agwu.wav (born October 8, 1962) is a mathematics teacher. Agwu is a naturalized American citizen, tenured faculty at the Borough of Manhattan Community College, part of the City University of New York, and was a director of the college's Center for Excellence in Teaching, Learning and Scholarship.
