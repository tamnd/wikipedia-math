## o2 subject: Tomer Schlank

Tomer Moshe Schlank (; born 1982) is an Israeli mathematician and a professor at Harvard University. Previously, he was a professor at University of Chicago and at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He primarily works in homotopy theory, algebraic geometry, and number theory. In 2022 he won the Erdős prize in mathematics and in 2023 he was awarded a European Research Council consolidator grant. He is an editor for the Israel Journal of Mathematics. In 2026 he was awarded the Clay Research Award.
