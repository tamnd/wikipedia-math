# Bibi-binary

## o1 subject: Bibi-binary

The Bibi-binary system for numeric notation (, or abbreviated "") is a hexadecimal numeral system first described in 1968 by singer/mathematician Robert "Boby" Lapointe (1922–1972). At the time, it attracted the attention of André Lichnerowicz, then engaged in studies at the University of Lyon.
