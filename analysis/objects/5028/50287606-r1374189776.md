## o3 subject: Stanisław Świerczkowski

Stanisław (Stash) Świerczkowski (Polish: ś; 16 July 1932 – 30 September 2015) was a Polish mathematician famous for his solutions to two iconic problems posed by Hugo Steinhaus: the three-gap theorem and the non-tetratorus theorem.
