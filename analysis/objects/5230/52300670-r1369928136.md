# Ngaiming Mok

## o1 subject: Ngaiming Mok

Mok Ngaiming
Ngaiming Mok (; born 1956) is a Hong Kong mathematician specializing in complex differential geometry and algebraic geometry. He is currently a professor at the University of Hong Kong.
