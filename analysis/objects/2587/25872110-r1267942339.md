# Capped grope

## o1 subject: Capped grope

In mathematics, a grope is a construction used in 4-dimensional topology, introduced by and named by "because of its multitudinous fingers". Capped gropes were used by as a substitute for Casson handles, that (unlike Casson handles) work better for non-simply-connected 4-manifolds.
