## Representations
The smallest faithful permutation representation of Co1 is on the 98280 pairs {v,–v} of norm 4 vectors.
The double cover Co0 has a 24 dimensional representation; when reduced modulo 2, this becomes a representation of the simple group Co1. The exterior square and symmetric tensor square of the 24 dimensional representation have dimension 276 and 299, respectively; in characteristic not 2, the former is the smallest faithful representation of the simple group Co1.
The centralizer of an involution of type 2B in the monster group is of the form 21+24 ·Co1. Under this subgroup, the 196883 dimensional representation of the monster reduces as follows: the 24x4096=98304 representation of 21+24 ·Co1, the 98280 dimensional representation of 224 ·Co1, and the 299 dimensional representation of Co1.
The Dynkin diagram of the even Lorentzian unimodular lattice II1,25 is isometric to the (affine) Leech lattice Λ, so the group of diagram automorphisms is split extension Λ,Co0 of affine isometries of the Leech lattice.
