# Sandra Mitchell Hedetniemi

## o1 subject: Sandra Mitchell Hedetniemi

Sandra Lee "Sandee" Mitchell Hedetniemi (Mitchell; born July 5, 1949) is an American mathematician and computer scientist, known for her research in graph theory and algorithms on graphs. She is a professor of computer science at Clemson University.
