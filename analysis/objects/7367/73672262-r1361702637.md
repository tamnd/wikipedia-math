## o3 subject: Cheng Hsiao

Cheng Hsiao (; born 28 July 1943) is a Taiwanese-American economist, econometrician, and statistician who is a professor of economics at the University of Southern California. Hsiao is known for his works in time series analysis and panel data analysis.
