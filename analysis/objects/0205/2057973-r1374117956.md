# Free-energy relationship

## o1 subject: Free-energy relationship

A free energy relationship, also known as a Gibbs energy relationship, relates the Gibbs free energy change $\Delta G$ of one series of chemical reactions to the $\Delta G$ of the same or another, related series of reactions. Here, $\Delta G$ represents the difference in Gibbs energy between two states of a reaction.
