# Michael Walker (mathematician)

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Michael Walker

Michael Walker OBE (14 January 1947 – 27 September 2018) was an English mathematician who was instrumental in developing the standards that apply to mobile telecommunications and particularly in respect of the SIM card.
