# Robert Moody

## o1 subject: Robert Moody

Robert Vaughan Moody, (; born November 28, 1941) is a Canadian mathematician. He is the co-discoverer of Kac–Moody algebra, a Lie algebra, usually infinite-dimensional, that can be defined through a generalized root system.
