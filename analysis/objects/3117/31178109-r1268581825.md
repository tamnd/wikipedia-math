# Logarithmically concave sequence

## o1 subject: Logarithmically concave sequence

In mathematics, a sequence $a$ = $(a_{0}, a_{1}, ..., a_{n})$ of nonnegative real numbers is called a logarithmically concave sequence, or a log-concave sequence for short, if $a_{i}^{2} \ge a_{i-1}a_{i+1}$ holds for $0 < i < n$.
