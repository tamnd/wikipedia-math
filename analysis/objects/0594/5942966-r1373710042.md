# Aoibhinn Ní Shúilleabháin

## o1 infobox: infobox academic

## o2 subject: Aoibhinn Ní Shúilleabháin

Aoibhinn Ní Shúilleabháin (; born 25 October 1983) is an Irish academic, teacher, broadcaster and high-profile science communicator. She also won the Rose of Tralee contest in 2005 and toured internationally as the lead singer of an Irish traditional music band. In 2022, she was appointed to chair a national forum on biodiversity loss, presenting its report to Taoiseach Leo Varadkar in April 2023, and presenting on the topic to a committee of the UN General Assembly later that month.
