## o3 subject: Hypercubic honeycomb

In geometry, a hypercubic honeycomb is a family of regular honeycombs (tessellations) in $n$-dimensional spaces with the Schläfli symbols $\{4,3...3,4\}$ and containing the symmetry of Coxeter group $R_{n}$ (or $B^{\sim}_{n-1}$) for $n \ge 3$.
