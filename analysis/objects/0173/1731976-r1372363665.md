# Branko Grünbaum

## o1 subject: Branko Grünbaum

Branko Grünbaum (; 2 October 1929 – 14 September 2018) was a Croatian-born mathematician of Jewish descent and a professor emeritus at the University of Washington in Seattle. He received his Ph.D. in 1957 from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
