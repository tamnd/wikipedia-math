# Arithmetic variety

## o1 subject: Arithmetic variety

In mathematics, an arithmetic variety is the quotient space of a Hermitian symmetric space by an arithmetic subgroup of the associated algebraic Lie group.

## o2 section: Kazhdan's theorem

## o3 statement/theorem: Kazhdan's theorem

## Kazhdan's theorem
Kazhdan's theorem says the following:
Kazhdan's theorem
If X is an arithmetic variety, then, for all automorphisms σ of the complex numbers, σX is also an arithmetic variety.
