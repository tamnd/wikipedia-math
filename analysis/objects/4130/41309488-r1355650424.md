# Katherine Wallman

## o1 subject: Katherine Wallman

Katherine K. Wallman (June 17, 1943January 17, 2024) was an American statistician who served as the chief statistician of the United States from 1992 to 2017. In that role at the U.S. Office of Management and Budget, she provided coordination, guidance, and oversight for the federal statistical system of the United States. She also served as the United States government's representative to international statistical organizations.
