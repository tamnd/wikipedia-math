# Essay d'analyse sur les jeux de hazard

## o1 subject: Essay d'analyse sur les jeux de hazard

fr
(Essay on the Analysis of Games of Chance) is a book on combinatorics and mathematical probability written by Pierre Remond de Montmort published in 1708 with an expanded second edition in 1713. It was the first comprehensive text published on probability theory.
