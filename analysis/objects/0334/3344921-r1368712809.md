# Positive and negative parts

## o1 subject: Positive and negative parts

In mathematics, the positive part of a real or extended real-valued function is defined by the formula
$$f^+(x) = \max(f(x),0) = \begin{cases}
f(x) & \text{ if } f(x) > 0 \\
0 & \text{ otherwise.}
\end{cases}$$
