# Robert Recorde

## o1 subject: Robert Recorde

Robert Recorde (1510 – 1558) was a Welsh physician and mathematician. He invented the equals sign (=) and also introduced the pre-existing plus (+) and minus (−) signs to English speakers in 1557.
