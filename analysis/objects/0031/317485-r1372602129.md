# Laurent Schwartz

## o1 subject: Laurent Schwartz

Laurent-Moïse Schwartz (; 5 March 1915 – 4 July 2002) was a French mathematician who received the Fields Medal in 1950 for pioneering the theory of distributions or generalized functions, giving a well-defined meaning to objects such as the Dirac delta function. For several years he taught at the École polytechnique.
