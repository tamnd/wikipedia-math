# Geoff Smith (mathematician)

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Geoff Smith

Geoffrey Charles Smith, MBE (born 1953) is a British mathematician. He is Honorary Reader in Mathematics at the University of Bath (where he worked in group theory) and currently professor in residence at Wells Cathedral School.
