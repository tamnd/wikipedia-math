# Mikhail Menshikov

## o1 subject: Mikhail Menshikov

Mikhail Vasilyevich Menshikov (; 17 January 1948 – 1 May 2026) was a Russian-British mathematician with publications in areas ranging from probability to combinatorics. He held the post of Professor in the University of Durham until his death in 2026.
