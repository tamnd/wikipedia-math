# James Alexander Shohat

## o1 subject: James Alexander Shohat

James Alexander Shohat ( Jacques Chokhate (or Chokhatte) , 18 November 1886, Brest-Litovsk – 8 October 1944, Philadelphia) was a Russian and American mathematician at the University of Pennsylvania who worked on the moment problem. He studied at the University of Petrograd and married the physicist Nadiascha W. Galli, the couple emigrating from Russia to the United States in 1923.
