# Bing double

## o1 subject: Bing double

In knot theory, a field of mathematics, the Bing double of a knot is a link with two components which follow the pattern of the knot and "hook together". Bing doubles were introduced in by their namesake, the American mathematician R. H. Bing. The Bing double of a slice knot is a slice link, though it is unknown whether the converse is true. The components of a Bing double bound disjoint Seifert surfaces.
