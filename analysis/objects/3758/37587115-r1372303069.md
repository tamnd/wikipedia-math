## o2 subject: Dominique de Caen

Dominique de Caen ( – 2002) was a mathematician, Doctor of Mathematics, and professor of mathematics, who specialized in graph theory, probability theory, and information theory. He is renowned for his research on Turán's extremal problem for hypergraphs.
