## Generalization
For a non-singular hypersurface $H$ of degree $d$ in the projective space $\mathbb{P}^n$ of arithmetic genus $g$ the formula becomes:
$g=\binom{d-1}{n} , \,$
where $\tbinom{d-1}{n}$ is the binomial coefficient.
