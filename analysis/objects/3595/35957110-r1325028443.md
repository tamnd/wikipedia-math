# Mori–Nagata theorem

## o1 subject: Mori–Nagata theorem

In algebra, the Mori–Nagata theorem introduced by and , states the following: let A be a noetherian reduced commutative ring with the total ring of fractions K. Then the integral closure of A in K is a direct product of r Krull domains, where r is the number of minimal prime ideals of A.
