## o2 subject: Bronisław Knaster

Bronisław Knaster (Polish: b; 22 May 1893 – 3 November 1980) was a Polish mathematician and professor at the University of Lviv and the University of Wrocław. His main areas of research included point-set topology and continuum theory.
