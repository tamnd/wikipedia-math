# Nikolai Brashman

## o1 subject: Nikolai Brashman

Nikolai Dmitrievich Brashman (; ; June 14, 1796 – 25 May 1866) was a Russian mathematician of Jewish-Austrian origin. He was a student of Joseph Johann Littrow, and the advisor of Pafnuty Chebyshev and August Davidov.
