# Mean time to recovery

## o1 subject: Mean time to recovery

Mean time to recovery (MTTR), also known as mean time to repair or mean time to resolve, is the average time that a device or system will take to recover from any failure. Examples of such devices range from self-resetting fuses (where the MTTR would be very short, probably seconds), to whole systems which have to be repaired or replaced.
