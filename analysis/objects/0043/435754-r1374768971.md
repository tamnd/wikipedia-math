# Control chart

## o1 subject: Control chart

Control charts (also known as Shewhart charts, after Walter A. Shewhart, or process-behavior charts) are graphical plots used in statistical process control (SPC) to determine whether a manufacturing or business process is in a state of statistical control. They form the basis of modern statistical process control, and are one of the seven basic tools of quality control.
