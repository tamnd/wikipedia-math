# Alois Höfler

## o1 subject: Alois Höfler

Alois Höfler (; April 6, 1853 – February 26, 1922) was an Austrian philosopher and university professor of education. He was seen by the logical positivist Otto Neurath as an important link between Bernard Bolzano's work and the Vienna Circle.
