# Regina Tyshkevich

## o1 subject: Regina Tyshkevich

Regina Iosifovna Tyshkevich (; 20 October 1929 – 17 November 2019) was a Belarusian mathematician, an expert in graph theory, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, professor of the Belarusian State University.
