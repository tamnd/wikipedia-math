# M. T. Naraniengar

## o1 subject: M. T. Naraniengar

Mandyam Tondanur Naraniengar (1871–1940) was an Indian mathematician. He first proved in 1909 the Morley's trisector theorem after it was posed in 1899 by Frank Morley.
