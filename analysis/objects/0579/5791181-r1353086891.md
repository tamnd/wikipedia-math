# HP-28 series

## o1 subject: HP-28 series

The HP-28C and HP-28S were two graphing calculators produced by Hewlett-Packard from 1986 to 1992.
The HP-28C was the first handheld calculator capable of solving equations symbolically.
They were replaced by the HP&nbsp;48 series of calculators, which grew from the menu-driven RPL programming language interface first introduced in these HP-28 series.
