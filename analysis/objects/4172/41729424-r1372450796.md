## o2 subject: Vitalik Buterin

Vitaly Dmitrievich Buterin (; born 31 January 1994), better known as Vitalik Buterin (), is a Russian-Canadian computer programmer best known for co-founding Ethereum. Buterin became involved with cryptocurrency early in its inception, co-founding Bitcoin Magazine in 2011. In 2015, Buterin deployed the Ethereum blockchain with Gavin Wood, Charles Hoskinson, Anthony Di Iorio, and Joseph Lubin.
