## o2 subject: Eitan Tadmor

Eitan Tadmor (; born May 4, 1954) is a distinguished university professor at the University of Maryland, College Park. His work has featured contributions to the theory and computation of partial differential equations with diverse applications to shock wave, kinetic transport, incompressible flows, image processing, and self-organized collective dynamics.
