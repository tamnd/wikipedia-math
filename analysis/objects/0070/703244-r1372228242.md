# Pierre Ossian Bonnet

## o1 subject: Pierre Ossian Bonnet

Pierre Ossian Bonnet (; 22 December 1819 in Montpellier – 22 June 1892 in Paris) was a French mathematician. He made some important contributions to the differential geometry of surfaces, including the Gauss–Bonnet theorem.
