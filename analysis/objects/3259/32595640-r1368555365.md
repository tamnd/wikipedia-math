# Semyon Alesker

## o1 subject: Semyon Alesker

Semyon Alesker (; born 1972) is an Israeli mathematician at Tel Aviv University. For his contributions in convex geometry and integral geometry, in particular his work on valuations, he won the EMS Prize in 2000, and the Erdős Prize in 2004.
