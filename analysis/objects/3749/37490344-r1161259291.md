# Alexandrov theorem

## o1 subject: Alexandrov theorem

In mathematical analysis, the Alexandrov theorem, named after Aleksandr Danilovich Aleksandrov, states that if $U$ is an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ and $f\colon U\to \mathbb{R}^m$ is a convex function, then $f$ has a second derivative almost everywhere.
