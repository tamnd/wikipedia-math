# Equal parallelians point

## o1 subject: Equal parallelians point

In geometry, the equal parallelians point (also called congruent parallelians point) is a special point associated with a plane triangle. It is a triangle center and it is denoted by X(192) in Clark Kimberling's Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers. There is a reference to this point in one of Peter Yff's notebooks, written in 1961.

## Definition
The equal parallelians point of triangle $\triangle ABC$ is a point $P$ in the plane of $\triangle ABC$ such that the three line segments through $P$ parallel to the sidelines of $\triangle ABC$ and having endpoints on these sidelines have equal lengths.

## o9 definition/construction: Construction for the equal parallelians point

## Construction for the equal parallelians point
Let $\triangle A'B'C'$ be the anticomplementary triangle of triangle $\triangle ABC$. Let the internal bisectors of the angles at the vertices $A, B, C$ of $\triangle ABC$ meet the opposite sidelines at $A'', B'', C''$ respectively. Then the lines $A'A'', B'B'', C'C''$ concur at the equal parallelians point of $\triangle ABC$.
As a result, the equal parallelians point is the isotomic conjugate of the incenter of and with respect to the anticomplementary triangle.
