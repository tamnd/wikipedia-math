# Austin moving-knife procedures

## o1 subject: Austin moving-knife procedures

The Austin moving-knife procedures are procedures for equitable division of a cake. To each of n partners, they allocate a piece of the cake which this partner values as exactly $1/n$ of the cake. This is in contrast to proportional division procedures, which give each partner at least $1/n$ of the cake, but may give more to some of the partners.
