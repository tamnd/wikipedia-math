# Hu Hesheng

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Hu Hesheng

Hu Hesheng (; 20 June 1928 – 2 February 2024) was a Chinese mathematician. She served as vice-president of Chinese Mathematical Society, president of the Shanghai Mathematical Society, and was an academician of Chinese Academy of Sciences. She gave the Noether Lecture in 2002.
