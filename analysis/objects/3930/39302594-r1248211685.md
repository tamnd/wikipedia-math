# John Dougall (mathematician)

## o1 subject: John Dougall

Dr. John Dougall FRSE (June 1867 – 24 February 1960) was "one of Scotland's leading mathematicians". Two formulas are named Dougall's formula after him: one for the sum of a 7F6 hypergeometric series, and another for the sum of a bilateral hypergeometric series.
