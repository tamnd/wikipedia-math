# Hurwitz scheme

## o1 subject: Hurwitz scheme

In algebraic geometry, the Hurwitz scheme $\mathcal{H}_{d, g}$ is the scheme parametrizing pairs ($C, \pi: C \to \mathbf{P}^1$) where C is a smooth curve of genus g and $\pi$ has degree d.
