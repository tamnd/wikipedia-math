# Windmill graph

## o1 infobox: infobox graph

## o2 subject: Windmill graph

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the windmill graph $\operatorname{Wd}(k,n)$ is an undirected graph constructed for $k \ge 2$ and $n \ge 2$ by joining $n$ copies of the complete graph $K_{k}$ at a shared universal vertex. That is, it is a 1-clique-sum of these complete graphs.

## o3 citation

## o4 section: Properties

## o5 statement/fact: Properties

## Properties
It has $n(k - 1) + 1$ vertices and $nk(k - 1)/2$ edges, girth 3 (if $k > 2$), radius 1 and diameter 2.
It has vertex connectivity 1 because its central vertex is an articulation point; however, like the complete graphs from which it is formed, it is $(k - 1)$-edge-connected. It is trivially perfect and a block graph.

## o8 statement/generalization: Special cases

## Special cases
By construction, the windmill graph $\operatorname{Wd}(3,n)$ is the friendship graph $F_{n}$, the windmill graph $\operatorname{Wd}(2,n)$ is the star graph $S_{n}$ and the windmill graph $\operatorname{Wd}(3,2)$ is the butterfly graph.
