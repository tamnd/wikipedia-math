# Treks into Intuitive Geometry

## o1 infobox: infobox book

## o2 subject: Treks into Intuitive Geometry

Treks into Intuitive Geometry: The World of Polygons and Polyhedra is a book on geometry, written as a discussion between a teacher and a student in the style of a Socratic dialogue. It was written by Japanese mathematician Jin Akiyama and science writer Kiyoko Matsunaga, and published by Springer-Verlag in 2015 (), with an expanded second edition in 2024 ().
