# Aggregated distribution

## o1 subject: Aggregated distribution

An aggregated distribution, commonly found among predators, parasites, and plants is a highly uneven (skewed) statistical distribution pattern in which they collect or aggregate in regions. These regions may be widely separated, particularly within animal distributions that are influenced by prey or host densities. This distribution makes sampling difficult and invalidates commonly used parametric statistics. A similar pattern is found among predators that search for their prey.
