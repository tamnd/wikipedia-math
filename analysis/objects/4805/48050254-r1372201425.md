## o2 subject: Isabella Bashmakova

Isabella Grigoryevna Bashmakova (; January 3, 1921 – July 17, 2005) was a Russian historian of mathematics. In 2001, she was a recipient of the Alexander Koyré Medal of the International Academy of the History of Science.
