### Examples
If $f$ is the constant function such that $f(x) = 72$ for every $x$ then
$$\begin{aligned}
f'(x) &= 0 \\
\int f(x) \,dx &= 72x + c\\
\lim_{x\rightarrow0}f(x)&=72
\end{aligned}$$
