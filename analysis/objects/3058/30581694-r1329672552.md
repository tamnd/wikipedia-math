# Nadirashvili surface

## o1 subject: Nadirashvili surface

In differential geometry, a Nadirashvili surface is an immersed complete bounded minimal surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with negative Gaussian curvature. The first example of such a surface was constructed by Nikolai Nadirashvili in 1996. This simultaneously answered a question of Hadamard about whether there was an immersed complete bounded surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with negative Gaussian curvature, and a question of Eugenio Calabi and Shing-Tung Yau about whether there was an immersed complete bounded minimal surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
