# HP 33s

## o1 infobox: infobox calculator

## o2 subject: HP 33s

The HP 33s (F2216A) was a scientific calculator marketed by Hewlett-Packard. It was introduced in 2003 as the successor to the HP&nbsp;32SII, and discontinued on the introduction of its successor, the HP&nbsp;35s in 2007.
