# Michel Enock

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Michel Enock

Michel Enock-Levi (1 June 1947 – 5 June 2025) was a French mathematician and a Directeur de recherche au CNRS, credited with the early development of Pontryagin-style dualities for non-commutative topological groups. Enock died in June 2025, at the age of 78.
