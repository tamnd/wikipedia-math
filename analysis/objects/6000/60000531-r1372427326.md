# Andor Kertész (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Andor Kertész

Kertész Andor
Andor Kertész (; 19 February 1929 – 3 April 1974) was a Hungarian mathematician and professor of mathematics at the Lajos Kossuth University (KLTE), Debrecen. He is the father of linguist András Kertész.
