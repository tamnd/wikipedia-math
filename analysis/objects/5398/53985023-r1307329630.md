# Richard Proto

## o1 subject: Richard Proto

Richard C. Proto (February 9, 1940July 27, 2008) was a noted American cryptographer and former Director of Research and Advanced Technology at the United States National Security Agency. He was elected to NSA Hall of Honor in 2013.
