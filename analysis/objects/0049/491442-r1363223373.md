# Prior Analytics

## o1 subject: Prior Analytics

The Prior Analytics (; ) is a work by Aristotle on reasoning, known as syllogistic, composed around 350 BC. Being one of the six extant Aristotelian writings on logic and scientific method, it is part of what later Peripatetics called the Organon.
