# Schiffler point

## o1 subject: Schiffler point

In geometry, the Schiffler point of a triangle is a triangle center, a point defined from the triangle that is equivariant under Euclidean transformations of the triangle. This point was first defined and investigated by Schiffler et al. (1985).

## o2 section: Definition

## o3 definition: Definition

## Definition
A triangle $\triangle ABC$ with the incenter $I$ has its Schiffler point at the point of concurrence of the Euler lines of the four triangles $\triangle BCI, \triangle CAI, \triangle ABI, \triangle ABC$. Schiffler's theorem states that these four lines all meet at a single point.
