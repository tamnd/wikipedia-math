# Vadim G. Vizing

## o1 subject: Vadim G. Vizing

Vadim Georgievich Vizing (, ; 25 March 1937 – 23 August 2017) was a Soviet and Ukrainian mathematician known for his contributions to graph theory, and especially for Vizing's theorem stating that the edges of any simple graph with maximum degree Δ can be colored with at most Δ + 1 colors.
