# Yoav Benjamini

## o1 subject: Yoav Benjamini

Yoav Benjamini (; born 5 January 1949) is an Israeli statistician best known for the development (with Yosef Hochberg) of the false discovery rate (FDR) criterion and the Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) and Benjamini–Yekutieli (BY) procedures for controlling the FDR. He is currently The Nathan and Lily Silver Professor of Applied Statistics at Tel Aviv University.
