# Marcin Śmiglecki

## o1 subject: Marcin Śmiglecki

Marcin Śmiglecki (Latin: Martinus Smiglecius or Leopolitanus, ; 11 November 1564 – 26 or 28 July 1618) was a Polish Jesuit philosopher and logician, known for his erudite scholastic Logica.
