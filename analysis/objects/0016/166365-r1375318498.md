# Vorticity equation

## o1 subject: Vorticity equation

The vorticity equation of fluid dynamics describes the evolution of the vorticity $\mathbf{\omega}$ of a particle of a fluid as it moves with its flow; that is, the local rotation of the fluid (in terms of vector calculus this is the curl of the flow velocity). The first source term on the right hand side represents vortex stretching.
