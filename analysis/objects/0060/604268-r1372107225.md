# Multi-index notation

## o1 subject: Multi-index notation

Multi-index notation is a mathematical notation that simplifies formulas used in multivariable calculus, partial differential equations and the theory of distributions, by generalising the concept of an integer index to an ordered tuple of indices.

## o7 statement/theorem: Multinomial theorem

### Multinomial theorem
$\left( \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right)^k = \sum_{|\alpha|=k} \binom{k}{\alpha} \, x^\alpha.$

## o8 section: Multi-binomial theorem

## o9 statement/theorem: Multi-binomial theorem

### Multi-binomial theorem
$$(x+y)^\alpha = \sum_{\nu \le \alpha} \binom{\alpha}{\nu} \, x^\nu y^{\alpha - \nu}.$$
Note that, since $x + y$ is a vector and $\alpha$ is a multi-index, the expression on the left is short for $(x_{1} + y_{1})^{\alpha_{1}}\cdots(x_{n} + y_{n})^{\alpha_{n}}$.

## o10 section: Leibniz formula

## o11 statement: Leibniz formula

### Leibniz formula
For smooth functions $f$ and $g$,
$$\partial^\alpha(fg) = \sum_{\nu \le \alpha} \binom{\alpha}{\nu} \, \partial^{\nu}f\,\partial^{\alpha-\nu}g.$$
