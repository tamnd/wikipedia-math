# Probabilities and Potential

## o1 subject: Probabilities and Potential

Probabilities and Potential (titled in the original French) is a mathematics book written by Claude Dellacherie and Paul-André Meyer (and Bernard Maisonneuve, for volume 5 only). It was published by Éditions Hermann in five volumes between 1975 and 1992.
