# Vladimir Dmitriev (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Vladimir Dmitriev

Vladimir Ivanovich Dmitriev (; 20 October 1932 – 24 November 2020) was a Russian scientist in the field of mathematical physics, doctor of physical and mathematical sciences (1967), and professor at the MSU Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics (CMC MSU). He served as head of the Laboratory of Mathematical Physics and as deputy dean of the faculty.
