# Ludwig Kraus

## o1 infobox: infobox academic

## o2 subject: Ludwig Kraus

Ludwig Kraus (; 9 April 1857 – 1886) was a Czech mathematician. In 1884, he published a paper giving a partial proof of the result now known as the two-variable case of the Jacobian conjecture more than half a century before Ott-Heinrich Keller proposed the conjecture in its general form in 1939. Kraus's priority went unrecognized until 2026.
