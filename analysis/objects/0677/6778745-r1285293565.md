# Gabriel Judah Lichtenfeld

## o1 subject: Gabriel Judah Lichtenfeld

Gabriel Judah Lichtenfeld (; 1811, Lublin — 22 March 1887, Warsaw) was a Jewish-Polish maskilic mathematician, poet, and author. He wrote for Ha-Shachar, Ha-Tzefirah, Izraelita, and Polish newspapers, mostly on mathematical topics.
