## o2 subject: Hong Wang

Hong Wang (; born 1991) is a Chinese mathematician known for her research in Fourier analysis and geometric measure theory. She was awarded the Fields Medal in 2026 for her contributions to Fourier restriction, Falconer's conjecture, Furstenberg sets in the plane, and the Kakeya conjecture. She is the third woman and the first Chinese woman to win the award.
