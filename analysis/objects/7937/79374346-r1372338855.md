# Sara Shakulova

## o1 subject: Sara Shakulova

Sayyidah Sara Kasimovna Shakulova (; 27 July 1887 — 5 September 1964) was a Russian mathematician and the first female mathematician of Tatar descent.Shakulova taught maths at the Zaraysky Real College in Ryazan Governorate (1915–1916), with a salary of 112-185 rubles, Kazan Urban Female Commercial College (1916–1919), Tatar Aitova School, and L. Khusainova Female School. In 1919–1923, she worked as vice people's commissar for education and head of the Main Office for Professional Technical Education of the Bashkir ASSR. She was a private teacher from 1912 to 1915 and spoke Tatar, Russian and French.
