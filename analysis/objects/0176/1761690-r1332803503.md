## o1 subject: Complex convexity

Complex convexity is a general term in complex geometry.

## o2 section: Definition

## o3 definition: Definition

## Definition
A set $\Omega$ in $\mathbb{C}^n$ is called $\mathbb{C}$-convex if its intersection with any complex line is contractible.
