## o2 subject: Wiesława Nizioł

Wiesława Krystyna Nizioł (pronounced 'v) is a Polish mathematician, director of research at CNRS, based at Institut mathématique de Jussieu. Her research concerns arithmetic geometry, and in particular p-adic Hodge theory, Galois representations, and p-adic cohomology.
