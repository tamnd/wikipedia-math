# Dodecagram

## o1 subject: Dodecagram

In geometry, a dodecagram (el) is a star polygon or compound with 12 vertices. There is one regular dodecagram polygon (with Schläfli symbol $\{12/5\}$ and a turning number of 5). There are also 4 regular compounds $\{12/2\},$ $\{12/3\},$ $\{12/4\},$ and $\{12/6\}.$
