# Jian Ding

## o1 subject: Jian Ding

Jian Ding () is a Chinese mathematician and probability theorist. He is a chair professor at the School of Mathematical Sciences of Peking University, where he also serves as vice dean. In 2023 he was awarded the Loève Prize.
