# Yom Tov Lipman Lipkin

## o1 infobox: infobox person

## o2 subject: Yom Tov Lipman Lipkin

Yom Tov Lipman Lipkin (, ; 1846 – February 21) was a Lithuanian Jewish mathematician and inventor. He was the youngest son of Rabbi Yisroel Salanter, the father of the Musar movement.
