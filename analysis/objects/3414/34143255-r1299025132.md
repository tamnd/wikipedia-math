# Higgs bundle

## o1 subject: Higgs bundle

In mathematics, a Higgs bundle is a pair $(E,\varphi)$ consisting of a holomorphic vector bundle E and a Higgs field $\varphi$, a holomorphic 1-form taking values in the bundle of endomorphisms of E such that $\varphi \wedge \varphi=0$. Such pairs were introduced by , who named the field $\varphi$ after Peter Higgs because of an analogy with Higgs bosons. The term 'Higgs bundle', and the condition $\varphi \wedge \varphi=0$ (which is vacuous in Hitchin's original set-up on Riemann surfaces) was introduced later by Carlos Simpson.
