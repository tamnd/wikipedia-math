# Totative

## o1 subject: Totative

In number theory, a totative of a given positive integer $n$ is an integer $k$ such that $0 < k \le n$ and $k$ is coprime to $n$. Euler's totient function φ(n) counts the number of totatives of n. The totatives under multiplication modulo n form the multiplicative group of integers modulo n.
