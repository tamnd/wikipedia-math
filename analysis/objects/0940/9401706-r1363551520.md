# Quaquaversal tiling

## o1 subject: Quaquaversal tiling

In geometry, the quaquaversal tiling is a nonperiodic tiling of Euclidean 3-space introduced by John Conway and Charles Radin. It is analogous to the pinwheel tiling in 2 dimensions having tile orientations that are dense in $\operatorname{SO}(3)$. The basic solid tiles are 30-60-90 triangular prisms arranged in a pattern such that some copies are rotated by $\pi/3$, and some are rotated by $\pi/2$ in a perpendicular direction.
