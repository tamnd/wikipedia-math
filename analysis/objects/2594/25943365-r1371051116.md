# Serre's conjecture II

## o1 problem

Must the Galois cohomology set of a simply connected, semisimple algebraic group over a perfect field of cohomological dimension at most 2 always equal zero?

## o2 subject: Serre's conjecture II

In mathematics, specifically number theory, Serre's conjecture II is the statement that if G is a simply connected, semisimple algebraic group over a perfect field F of cohomological dimension at most 2, then the Galois cohomology set H1(F, G) is zero. It was proposed by Jean-Pierre Serre in 1962, as an higher-dimension equivalent of his conjecture I (which was later proven).
