These images show two ways to assign the vertices of a hemitesseract to those of a cube.
One way is to assign each pair of tesseract vertices to the one with the smaller integer value, e.g. (0, 15) to 0 and (7, 8) to 7.
Another way is to use the XOR between neighboring digits of the tesseract coordinates. This way (0011, 1100) is assigned to 010.
