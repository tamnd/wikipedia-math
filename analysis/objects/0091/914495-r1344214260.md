# Vinogradov

## o1 subject: Vinogradov

Vinogradov or Vinogradoff () is a surname derived from the Russian word виноград (vinograd, meaning "grape" and виноградник vinogradnik, meaning "vineyard"). Vinogradova () is a feminine version of the same name. It may be a Russian surname originated in clergy, associated with the symbolism of wine as the blood of Christ.
