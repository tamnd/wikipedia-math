# Kenneth W. Wachter

## o1 subject: Kenneth W. Wachter

Kenneth W. Wachter (born January 13, 1947) is an American mathematical demographer and statistician, known for contributions to demographic microsimulation (agent-based modeling), anthropometric history, statistical endocrinology, and evolutionary biodemography. He is Distinguished Professor Emeritus of Demography and Statistics at the University of California, Berkeley. Wachter was elected to the National Academy of Sciences in 1999.
