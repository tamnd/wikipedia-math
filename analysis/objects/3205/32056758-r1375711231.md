# Yair Censor

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Yair Censor

Yair Censor (; born November 29, 1943) is an Israeli mathematician and professor emeritus at the University of Haifa, specializing in computational mathematics and optimization, as well as applications of these fields, in particular to medical imaging and radiation therapy treatment planning.
