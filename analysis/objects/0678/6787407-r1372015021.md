# Yuri Prokhorov

## o1 subject: Yuri Prokhorov

Yuri Vasilyevich Prokhorov (; 15 December 1929 – 16 July 2013) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician, active in the field of probability theory. He was a PhD student of Andrey Kolmogorov at the Moscow State University, where he obtained his PhD in 1956.
