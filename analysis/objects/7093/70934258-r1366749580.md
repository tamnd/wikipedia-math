## Properties
- $W$ is a symmetric matrix.
- $W$ is positive definite.
- The determinant of $W$ is $1$.
- The inverse of $W$ is $W^{-1} =
\begin{bmatrix} 68 & -41 & -17 & 10\\ -41 & 25 & 10 & -6 \\ -17 & 10 & 5 &- 3 \\ 10 & -6 & -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$
- The characteristic polynomial of $W$ is $\lambda^4-35 \lambda^3+146 \lambda^2-100 \lambda+1$.
- The eigenvalues of $W$ are $\quad 0.01015004839789187,\quad 0.8431071498550294,\quad 3.858057455944953,\quad 30.28868534580213$.
- Since $W$ is symmetric, the $L^2$ norm condition number of $W$ is $\kappa_2(W)=\frac{max(\lambda)}{min(\lambda)}=\frac{30.28868534580213}{0.01015004839789187} = 2984.09270167549$.
- The solution of the system of equations $(S1)$ is $x=y=z=u=1$.
- The Cholesky factorisation of $W$ is $W= R^TR$ where $R
=\begin{bmatrix} \sqrt{5} & \frac{7}{\sqrt{5}} & \frac{6}{\sqrt{5}} & \sqrt{5} \\ 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} & -\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \sqrt{2} & \frac{3}{\sqrt{2}} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix}$.
- $W$ has the factorisation $W= LDL^T$ where $L =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \frac{7}{5} & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ \frac{6}{5} & -2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & \frac{3}{2} & 1
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
D=\begin{bmatrix}
5 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{5} & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \frac{1}{2}
\end{bmatrix}$.
- $W$ has the factorisation $W=Z^TZ$ with $Z$ as the integer matrix $Z=
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 3 & 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}$.
