# Chihara–Ismail polynomials

## o1 subject: Chihara–Ismail polynomials

In mathematics, the Chihara–Ismail polynomials are a family of orthogonal polynomials introduced by , generalizing the van Doorn polynomials introduced by van Doorn (1981) and the Karlin–McGregor polynomials. They have a rather unusual measure, which is discrete except for a single limit point at 0 with jump 0, and is non-symmetric, but whose support has an infinite number of both positive and negative points.
