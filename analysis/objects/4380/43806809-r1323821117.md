# Esenin-Volpin's theorem

## o1 subject: Esenin-Volpin's theorem

In mathematics, Esenin-Volpin's theorem states that weight of an infinite compact dyadic space is the supremum of the weights of its points. It was introduced by . It was generalized by Efimov in 1965 and Turzański in 1992.
