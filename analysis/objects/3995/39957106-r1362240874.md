# The Signal and the Noise

## o1 subject: The Signal and the Noise

The Signal and the Noise: Why So Many Predictions Fail – but Some Don't is a 2012 book by Nate Silver detailing the art of using probability and statistics as applied to real-world circumstances. The book includes case studies from baseball, elections, climate change, the 2008 financial crisis, poker and weather forecasting.
