# Timoleon Argyropoulos

## o1 subject: Timoleon Argyropoulos

Timoleon Argyropoulos ( April 12, 1847 - January 16, 1912) was an experimental physicist, mathematician, author, and professor. He was a pioneer in modern Greek education. His field of study was electricity and magnetism. He helped develop experimental physics in modern Greek education, and is called the father of Greek radiology.
