# Arrangement of pseudolines

## o1 subject: Arrangement of pseudolines

An arrangement of pseudolines is a family of curves that share similar topological properties with a line arrangement.
Most commonly, in the study of arrangements of lines, these have the simple property that each crosses every other line exactly once. These can be defined in the projective plane as simple closed curves any two of which meet in a single crossing point. Furthermore, in a simple (or uniform) arrangement, along with all lines being required to cross all others, no 3 pseudolines may cross at the same point.
