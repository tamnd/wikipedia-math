# Alexander Furman

## o1 subject: Alexander Furman

Alexander Furman () is a mathematician at the University of Illinois, Chicago. Furman received his bachelor's degree in mathematics and computer science from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem in 1986, later earning his master's degree and PhD in mathematics in 1989 and 1996, respectively, from the same university.
