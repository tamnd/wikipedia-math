# History of artificial neural networks

## o1 subject: History of artificial neural networks

Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are models created using machine learning to perform a number of tasks. While the computational implementations of ANNs relate to earlier discoveries in mathematics, their creation was inspired by biological neural circuitry. The first implementation of ANNs was the perceptron by Frank Rosenblatt. Despite the frequent claim that little research was conducted on ANNs in the 1970s and 1980s—the AAAI calling this period an "AI winter"—there was ongoing development of ANNs, especially connected to the development of specialized computers for simulating these networks.
