# Jonathan Lazare Alperin

## o1 subject: Jonathan Lazare Alperin

Jonathan Lazare Alperin (; June 2, 1937 – June 22, 2025) was an American mathematician specializing in the area of algebra known as group theory. He is notable for his work in group theory which has been cited over 500 times according to the Mathematical Reviews. The Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem is named after him.
