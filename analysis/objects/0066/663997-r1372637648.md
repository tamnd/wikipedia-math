# Eduard Study

## o1 subject: Eduard Study

Christian Hugo Eduard Study ( ; 23 March 1862, Coburg – 6 January 1930, Bonn) was a German mathematician known for work on invariant theory of ternary forms (1889) and for the study of spherical trigonometry. He is also known for contributions to space geometry, hypercomplex numbers, and criticism of early physical chemistry.
