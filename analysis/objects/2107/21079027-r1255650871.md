# Fedor Nazarov

## o1 subject: Fedor Nazarov

L'vovich
Fedor (Fedya) L'vovich Nazarov (; born 1967) is a Russian mathematician working in the United States. He has done research in mathematical analysis and its applications, in particular in functional analysis and classical analysis (including harmonic analysis, Fourier analysis, and complex analytic functions).
