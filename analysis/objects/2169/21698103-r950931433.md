# Crepant resolution

## o1 subject: Crepant resolution

In algebraic geometry, a crepant resolution of a singularity is a resolution that does not affect the canonical class of the manifold. The term "crepant" was coined by by removing the prefix "dis" from the word "discrepant", to indicate that the resolutions have no discrepancy in the canonical class.
