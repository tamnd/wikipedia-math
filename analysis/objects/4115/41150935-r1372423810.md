# National Statistics Office (Nepal)

## o1 infobox: infobox government agency

## o2 subject: National Statistics Office

The National Statistics Office () formerly known as Central Bureau of Statistics (), is the central agency for the collection, consolidation, processing, analysis, publication and dissemination of statistics in Nepal. The Office is under the Office of the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers. One of its core tasks is to research and publish censuses of Nepal, the most prominent one being the overall population census and Demographics of Nepal.
