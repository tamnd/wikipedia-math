# Tiling with rectangles

## o1 subject: Tiling with rectangles

A tiling with rectangles is a tiling which uses rectangles as its parts. The domino tilings are tilings with rectangles of $1 \times 2$ side
ratio. The tilings with straight polyominoes of shapes such as $1 \times 3$, $1 \times 4$ and
tilings with polyominoes of shapes such as $2 \times 3$ fall also into this category.
