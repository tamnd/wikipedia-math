# Grünbaum–Nash-Williams conjecture

## o1 subject: Grünbaum–Nash-Williams conjecture

Does every 4-vertex-connected toroidal graph have a Hamiltonian cycle?
In graph theory, the Grünbaum–Nash-Williams conjecture states that every 4-vertex-connected toroidal graph has a Hamiltonian cycle. It is a generalization of Tutte's theorem on Hamiltonian cycles, according to which every 4-vertex-connected planar graph has a Hamiltonian cycle. An analogous theorem of Thomas and Yu holds for graphs on the projective plane.
