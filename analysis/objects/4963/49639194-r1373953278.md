# Leaf power

## o1 subject: Leaf power

In the mathematical area of graph theory, a $k$-leaf power of a tree $T$ is a graph $G$ whose vertices are the leaves of $T$ and whose edges connect pairs of leaves whose distance in $T$ is at most $k$. That is, $G$ is an induced subgraph of the graph power $T^k$, induced by the leaves of $T$. For a graph $G$ constructed in this way, $T$ is called a $k$-leaf root of $G$.
