# Convergence proof techniques

## o1 subject: Convergence proof techniques

Convergence proof techniques are canonical patterns of mathematical proofs that sequences or functions converge to a finite limit when the argument tends to infinity.

## o3 section: First principles

## o4 statement: First principles

### First principles
The analytic definition of convergence of $f$ to a limit $f_{\infty}$ is that for all $\epsilon$ there exists a $k_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ such for all $k > k_0$, $\|f(k) - f_{\infty}\| < \epsilon$. The most direct proof technique from this definition is to find such a $k_0$ and prove the required inequality. If the value of $f_{\infty}$ is not known in advance, the techniques below may be useful.
