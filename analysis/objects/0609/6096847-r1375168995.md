# Aleksandr Nekrasov

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Aleksandr Nekrasov

Aleksandr Ivanovich Nekrasov (; 9 December 1883 – 21 May 1957) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician known for his mathematical contributions to hydromechanics and aeromechanics. The Nekrasov integral equation describing surface waves is named for him.
