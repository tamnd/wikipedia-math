# Nicolas Vilant

## o1 subject: Nicolas Vilant

Nicolas Vilant FRSE (1737–1807) was a mathematician from Scotland in the 18th century, known for his textbooks. He was a joint founder of the Royal Society of Edinburgh in 1783.
