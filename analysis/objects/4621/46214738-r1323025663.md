# Voderberg tiling

## o1 subject: Voderberg tiling

Voderberg tiling is a mathematical spiral tiling, invented in 1936 by mathematician Heinz Voderberg (1911–1945). Karl August Reinhardt posed the question, "Is there a tile such that two copies can completely enclose a third copy?" Voderberg, his student, answered in the affirmative with ["On a nonagon as a solution to a problem of Reinhardt"].
