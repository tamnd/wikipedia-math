# Vladimir Abramovich Rokhlin

## o1 subject: Vladimir Abramovich Rokhlin

Abramovich
Vladimir Abramovich Rokhlin (; 23 August 1919 – 3 December 1984) was a Soviet mathematician, who made numerous contributions in algebraic topology, geometry, measure theory, probability theory, ergodic theory and entropy theory.
