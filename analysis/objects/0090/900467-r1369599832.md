# Kazimierz Ajdukiewicz

## o1 subject: Kazimierz Ajdukiewicz

Kazimierz Ajdukiewicz (; 12 December 1890 – 12 April 1963) was a Polish philosopher and logician, a prominent figure in the Lwów–Warsaw school of logic. He taught at the University of Lwów, Adam Mickiewicz University, and the University of Warsaw.
