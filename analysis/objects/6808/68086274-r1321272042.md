## o1 subject: Al-Nijat

Al-Nijat min al-Qarq fi Bahr al-Zalalaat (, salvation from falling into the sea of misguidances) known as Al-Nijat is one of the most famous philosophical works of the Persian philosopher Avicenna (ibn Sīnā, 980-1037). The general theme of the book is philosophy and includes topics in the fields of logic, physics, mathematics and theology.
