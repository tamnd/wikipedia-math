# Mizohata–Takeuchi conjecture

## o1 subject: Mizohata–Takeuchi conjecture

In harmonic analysis, a branch of mathematics, the Mizohata–Takeuchi conjecture proposed a weighted $L^2$ inequality for the Fourier extension operator associated with a smooth hypersurface in Euclidean space. It asserted that the $L^2$ norm of the extension of a function $f$ from the hypersurface to $\mathbb{R}^n$ could be bounded, for any nonnegative weight function, by a constant multiple of the $L^2$ norm of $f$, with the constant depending only on the supremum of the weight over certain tube-shaped regions. The conjecture was disproved in 2025 by Hannah Cairo.
