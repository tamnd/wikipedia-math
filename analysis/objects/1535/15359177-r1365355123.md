# Yuan-Shih Chow

## o1 subject: Yuan-Shih Chow

Yuan-Shih Chow (; 1 September 1924 – 3 March 2022), also known as Y. S. Chow or Zhou Yuanshen, was a Chinese and American mathematician and probabilist. He was a professor of statistics at Columbia University.
