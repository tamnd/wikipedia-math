# Novum Organum

## o1 infobox: infobox book

## o2 subject: Novum Organum

The , fully ("The new Organon, or true directions concerning the interpretation of nature") or ("Part II of The Great Instauration"), is a philosophical work by Francis Bacon, written in Latin and published in 1620. The title is a reference to Aristotle's work Organon, which was his treatise on logic and syllogism. In , Bacon details a new system of logic he believes to be superior to the old ways of syllogism. This is now known as the Baconian method.
