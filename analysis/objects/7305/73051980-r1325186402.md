# Brownian sheet

## o1 subject: Brownian sheet

In mathematics, a Brownian sheet or multiparametric Brownian motion is a multiparametric generalization of the Brownian motion to a Gaussian random field. This means we generalize the "time" parameter $t$ of a Brownian motion $B_t$ from $\mathbb{R}_{+}$ to $\mathbb{R}_{+}^n$.

## o6 statement/fact: Properties

### Properties
From the definition follows
$B(0,t_2,\dots,t_n)=B(t_1,0,\dots,t_n)=\cdots=B(t_1,t_2,\dots,0)=0$
almost surely.

## o7 section: Examples

## o8 example: Examples

### Examples
- $(1,1)$-Brownian sheet is the Brownian motion in $\mathbb{R}^1$.
- $(1,d)$-Brownian sheet is the Brownian motion in $\mathbb{R}^d$.
- $(2,1)$-Brownian sheet is a multiparametric Brownian motion $X_{t,s}$ with index set $(t,s)\in [0,\infty)\times [0,\infty)$.
