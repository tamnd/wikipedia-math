# DFC (cipher)

## o1 infobox: infobox block cipher

## o2 subject: DFC

In cryptography, DFC (Decorrelated Fast Cipher) is a symmetric block cipher which was
created in 1998 by a group of researchers from École Normale Supérieure, CNRS, and France Télécom (including Jacques Stern and Serge Vaudenay) and submitted to the AES competition.
