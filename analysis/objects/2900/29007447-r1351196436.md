# Sergei Konyagin

## o1 subject: Sergei Konyagin

Sergei Vladimirovich Konyagin (; born 25 April 1957) is a Russian mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics at the Moscow State University. His primary research interest is in applying harmonic analysis to number theoretic settings.
