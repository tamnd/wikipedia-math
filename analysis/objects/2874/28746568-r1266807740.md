# Spectral abscissa

## o1 subject: Spectral abscissa

In mathematics, the spectral abscissa of a matrix or a bounded linear operator is the greatest real part of the matrix's spectrum (its set of eigenvalues). It is sometimes denoted $\alpha(A)$. As a transformation $\alpha: \mathrm{M}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, the spectral abscissa maps a square matrix onto its largest real eigenvalue.
