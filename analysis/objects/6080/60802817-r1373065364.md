# Maura Mast

## o1 subject: Maura Mast

Maura B. Mast is an Irish-American mathematician, mathematics educator, and academic administrator who is the current president of Seattle University. She assumed office as the school's 23rd president on September 1, 2026. Her research specialties are in differential geometry and quantitative reasoning. With Ethan D. Bolker, she is the author of the textbook Common Sense Mathematics.
