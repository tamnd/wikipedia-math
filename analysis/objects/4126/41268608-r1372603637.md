# Rose Whelan Sedgewick

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Rose Whelan Sedgewick

Rose Whelan Sedgewick ( – 2000) was an American mathematician. She was the first person to earn a PhD in mathematics from Brown University, in 1929. Her subsequent career in mathematics included assistant professorships at the University of Rochester, the University of Connecticut, Hillyer College, and the University of Maryland.
