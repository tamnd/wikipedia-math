# Virginia Ragsdale

## o1 subject: Virginia Ragsdale

Virginia Ragsdale (December 13, 1870 – June 4, 1945) was a teacher and mathematician specializing in algebraic curves. She is most known as the creator of the Ragsdale conjecture.
