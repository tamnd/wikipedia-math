## o1 subject: 2017 Chilean census

Censo chileno de 2017
The 2017 Chilean Population and Housing Census (), officially the XIX National Population Census and VIII Housing Census (), took place on 19 April 2017, and was organized by the National Statistics Institute.

## Results
In late August 2017, the preliminary results of the census were published, showing a population of 17,574,003 people between 6,499,355 homes (or approximately 2.7 people per home), a 5.6% increase in population since the 2012 census.
