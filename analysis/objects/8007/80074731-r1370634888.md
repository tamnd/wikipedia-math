# Mohammed Abouzaid

## o1 subject: Mohammed Abouzaid

Mohammed Abouzaid (; born 1981) is a Moroccan mathematician working in symplectic topology. He is a professor at Stanford University. He obtained his PhD in 2007 at the University of Chicago under Paul Seidel's supervision.
