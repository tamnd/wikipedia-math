# Rudolph Goclenius

## o1 subject: Rudolph Goclenius

Rudolph Goclenius the Elder (; born Rudolf Gockel or Göckel; 1 March 1547 – 8 June 1628) was a German scholastic philosopher. He is sometimes credited with coining the term psychology in 1590, though the term had been used by Pier Nicola Castellani and Gerhard Synellius 65 years earlier.
