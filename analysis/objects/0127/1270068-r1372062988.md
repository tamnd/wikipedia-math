# Pierre-Louis Lions

## o1 subject: Pierre-Louis Lions

Pierre-Louis Lions (; born 11 August 1956) is a French mathematician. He is known for a number of contributions to the fields of partial differential equations and the calculus of variations. He was a recipient of the 1994 Fields Medal.
