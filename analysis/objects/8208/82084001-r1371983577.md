## o2 subject: Daniel Goroff

Daniel L. Goroff is an American mathematician and academic administrator. He is currently the Vice President and Program Director at the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, where he manages programs centered on economics and the quality of graduate education. He was a technology policy official in the Biden White House.
