# Ioannis Kigalas

## o1 infobox: infobox writer

## o2 subject: Ioannis Kigalas

Ioannis Kigalas (; ; ; c. 1622 – 5 November 1687) was a Greek Cypriot scholar and professor of philosophy and logic who was largely active in Padua and Venice in the 17th-century Italian Renaissance.
