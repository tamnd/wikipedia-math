# Amir Dembo

## o1 subject: Amir Dembo

Amir Dembo (; born October 25, 1958, Haifa) is an Israeli-American mathematician, specializing in probability theory. He was elected a member of the National Academy of Sciences in 2022, and of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2023.
