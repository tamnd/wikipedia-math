## o2 subject: Yakov Eliashberg

Yakov Matveevich Eliashberg (also Yasha Eliashberg; ; born 11 December 1946) is an American mathematician who was born in Leningrad, USSR. He is the Herald L. and Caroline L. Ritch Professor of Mathematics at Stanford University. His research interests are differential topology, symplectic topology, and contact topology. He was awarded many prizes for his work, including the Wolf Prize in 2020 (shared with Simon Donaldson).
