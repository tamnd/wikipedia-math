# Joram Lindenstrauss

## o1 subject: Joram Lindenstrauss

Joram Lindenstrauss (; October 28, 1936 – April 29, 2012) was an Israeli mathematician working in functional analysis. He was a professor of mathematics at the Einstein Institute of Mathematics.
