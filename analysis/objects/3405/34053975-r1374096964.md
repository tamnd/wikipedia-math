# Math Girls

## o1 infobox: infobox book

## o2 subject: Math Girls

is the first in a series of math-themed young adult novels of the same name by Japanese author Hiroshi Yuki. It was published by SoftBank Creative in 2007, followed by Math Girls: Fermat's Last Theorem in 2008, Math Girls: Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems in 2009, Math Girls: Randomized Algorithms in 2011, Math Girls: Galois Theory in 2012, and Math Girls: The Poincaré conjecture in 2018. As of December 2010, the series had sold over 100,000 books in Japan.
