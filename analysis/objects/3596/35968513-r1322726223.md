## o1 subject: Isoperimetric point

## Properties
Let $P$ denote the triangle center X(175) of triangle $\triangle ABC$.
- $P$ lies on the line joining the incenter and the Gergonne point of $\triangle ABC$.
- If $P$ is an isoperimetric point of $\triangle ABC$ in the sense of Veldkamp, then the excircles of triangles $\triangle PBC, \triangle PCA, \triangle PAB$ are pairwise tangent to one another and $P$ is their radical center.
- If $P$ is an isoperimetric point of $\triangle ABC$ in the sense of Veldkamp, then the perimeters of $\triangle PBC, \triangle PCA, \triangle PAB$ are equal to
$$\frac{2 \triangle}{\bigl| 4R + r - (a+b+c) \bigr|}$$
where $\triangle$ is the area, $R$ is the circumradius, $r$ is the inradius, and $a, b, c$ are the sidelengths of $\triangle ABC$.
