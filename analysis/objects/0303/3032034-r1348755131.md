# Josif Shtokalo

## o1 subject: Josif Shtokalo

Josif Zakharovich Shtokalo (; November 16, 1897 – January 5, 1987) was a Ukrainian mathematician. Shtokalo worked mainly in the areas of differential equations, operational calculus and the history of mathematics.
