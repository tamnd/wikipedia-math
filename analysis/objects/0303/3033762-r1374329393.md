## o3 subject: Jan Śleszyński

Ivan Vladislavovich Sleshinsky or Jan Śleszyński () (Polish: ś; 23 July 1854 – 9 March 1931) was a Polish-Russian mathematician. He is regarded a pioneer of the study of logic in Poland.
