# Platon Poretsky

## o1 subject: Platon Poretsky

Platon Sergeevich Poretsky (; 3 October 1846 in Elisavetgrad, Russian Empire – 9 August 1907 in Gorodnyansky Uyezd, Chernigov Governorate, Russian Empire) was a noted Russian Imperial astronomer, mathematician, and logician.
