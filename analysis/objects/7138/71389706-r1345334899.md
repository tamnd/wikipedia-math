# Pseudo-R-squared

## o1 subject: Pseudo-R-squared

In statistics, pseudo-R-squared values are used when the outcome variable is nominal or ordinal such that the coefficient of determination $R$2 cannot be applied as a measure for goodness of fit and when a likelihood function is used to fit a model.
