# Little Astronomy

## o1 subject: Little Astronomy

Little Astronomy ( grc) is a collection of minor works in Ancient Greek mathematics and astronomy dating from the 4th to 2nd century BCE that were probably used as an astronomical curriculum starting around the 2nd century CE. In the astronomy of the medieval Islamic world, with a few additions, the collection became known as the Middle Books ( ar), mathematical preparation for Claudius Ptolemy's Almagest, intended for students who had already studied Euclid's Elements.
