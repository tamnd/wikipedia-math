# Francisco Santos Leal

## o1 subject: Francisco Santos Leal

Santos
Francisco "Paco" Santos Leal (born 28 May 1968) is a Spanish mathematician at the University of Cantabria, known for finding a counterexample to the Hirsch conjecture in polyhedral combinatorics. In 2015 he won the Fulkerson Prize for this research.
