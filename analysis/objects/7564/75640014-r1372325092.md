## o2 subject: Ibrahim Eltayeb

Ibrahim Abdelrazzak Eltayeb () is a Sudanese mathematician and professor of applied mathematics at the University of Nizwa in Oman. He is a member of the African Academy of Sciences, the Royal Astronomical Society, The World Academy of Sciences (TWAS) and the Royal Society of Edinburgh.
