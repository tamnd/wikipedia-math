# Susanna Terracini

## o1 subject: Susanna Terracini

Susanna Terracini (; born 29 April 1963) is an Italian mathematician known for her research on chaos in Hamiltonian dynamical systems, including the n-body problem, reaction–diffusion systems, and the Schrödinger equation.
