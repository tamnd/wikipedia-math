# Rowan Yeats

## o1 subject: Rowan Yeats

Rowan Yeats (born 1980) is a Canadian mathematician and mathematical physicist whose research connects combinatorics to quantum field theory. He holds the Canada Research Chair in Combinatorics in Quantum Field Theory at the University of Waterloo.
