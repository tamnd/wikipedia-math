# On the Sphere and Cylinder

## o1 subject: On the Sphere and Cylinder

On the Sphere and Cylinder (; Perì sphaíras kaì kulíndrou) is a treatise that was published by Archimedes in two volumes . It most notably details how to find the surface area of a sphere and the volume of the contained ball and the analogous values for a cylinder, and was the first to do so.
