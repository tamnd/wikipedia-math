# Signomial

## o1 subject: Signomial

A signomial is a kind of function of one or more independent variables. It is perhaps most easily thought of as an extension of multivariable polynomials—an extension that permits exponents to be arbitrary real numbers (rather than just non-negative integers) while requiring the independent variables to be strictly positive (so that division by zero and other inappropriate arithmetic operations are not encountered).
