# Zorya Shapiro

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Zorya Shapiro

Zorya Yakovlevna Shapiro (; 7 December 1914 – 4 July 2013) was a Soviet mathematician, educator and translator. She is known for her contributions to representation theory and functional analysis in her collaboration with Israel Gelfand, and the Shapiro-Lobatinski condition in elliptical boundary value problems.
