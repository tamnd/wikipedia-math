# Nikolai Andreev

## o1 subject: Nikolai Andreev

Nikolai Nikolayevich Andreev () (born 5 February 1975 in Saratov, Russia) is a Russian mathematician and popularizer of mathematics. He was awarded with the Leelavati Award in 2022.
