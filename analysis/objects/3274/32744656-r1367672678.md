# Femmes et mathématiques

## o1 subject: Femmes et mathématiques

L'association femmes et mathématiques (English: Association of Women and Mathematics) is a voluntary association promoting women in scientific studies, research, and mathematics created in 1987. This organization currently has about 200 members, including university maths professors, maths teachers, sociologists, philosophers and historians that are interested in the "woman question" in scientific domains.
