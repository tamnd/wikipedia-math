# Modus ponens

## o1 statement: Modus ponens

$P$ implies $Q$. $P$ is true. Therefore, $Q$ must also be true.

## o2 subject: Modus ponens

In propositional logic, (; MP), also known as (la), conditional elimination, implication elimination, or affirming the antecedent, is a deductive argument form and rule of inference. It can be summarized as "$P$ implies $Q$. $P$ is true. Therefore, $Q$ must also be true."
