# 60 (number)

## o1 infobox: infobox number

## o2 subject: 60

60 (sixty) (en-us-sixty.ogg) is the natural number following 59 and preceding 61. Being three times 20, it is called threescore in older literature (kopa in Slavic, Schock in Germanic).
