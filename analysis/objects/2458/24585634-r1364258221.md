# Lagrangian system

## o1 subject: Lagrangian system

In mathematics, a Lagrangian system is a pair $(Y, L)$, consisting of a smooth fiber bundle $Y \to X$ and a Lagrangian density $L$, which yields the Euler–Lagrange differential operator acting on sections of $Y \to X$.

## Cohomology and Noether's theorems
Cohomology of the variational bicomplex leads to the so-called
variational formula
$dL=\delta L + d_H \Theta_L,$
where
$d_H\Theta_L=dx^\lambda\wedge d_\lambda\phi, \qquad \phi\in
O^*_\infty(Y)$
is the total differential and $\theta_{L}$ is a Lepage equivalent of $L$. Noether's first theorem and Noether's second theorem are corollaries of this variational formula.
