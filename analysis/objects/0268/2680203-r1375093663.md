# René-François de Sluse

## o1 subject: René-François de Sluse

René-François Walter de Sluse (; also Renatus Franciscus Slusius or Walther de Sluze; 2 July 1622 – 19 March 1685) was a Walloon mathematician and clergyman who was a canon of Saint Lambert's Cathedral, Liège and abbé of Amay.
