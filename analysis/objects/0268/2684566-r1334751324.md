# Drag count

## o1 subject: Drag count

A drag count is a dimensionless unit used by aerospace engineers. 1 drag count is equal to a $C_d$ of 0.0001.

## o5 definition: Definition

## Definition
A drag count $\Delta C_\mathrm d\,$ is defined as:
$\Delta C_\mathrm d = 10^{4} \dfrac{2 F_\mathrm d}{\rho v^2 A}\, ,$
where:
$F_\mathrm d\,$ is the drag force, which is by definition the force component in the direction of the flow velocity,
$\rho\,$ is the mass density of the fluid,
$v\,$ is the speed of the object relative to the fluid, and
$A\,$ is the reference area.
The drag coefficient is used to compare the solutions of different geometries by means of a dimensionless number. A drag count is more user-friendly than the drag coefficient, as the latter is usually much less than 1. A drag count of 200 to 400 is typical for an airplane at cruise. A reduction of one drag count on a subsonic civil transport airplane means about 200 more in payload.
