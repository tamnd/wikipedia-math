# Gunter Malle

## o1 subject: Gunter Malle

Gunter Malle (born 13 May 1960 in Karlsruhe) is a German mathematician, specializing in group theory, representation theory of finite groups, and number theory.
Malle received his doctorate in 1986 from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology under the supervision of Heinrich Matzat with the thesis Exceptional groups of Lie type as Galois groups. He completed his habilitation in 1991 at Heidelberg University and from 1998 was a professor at Kassel University. Since 2005 he has been a professor at the Technische Universität Kaiserslautern.
