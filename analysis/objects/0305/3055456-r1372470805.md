# Jerzy Łoś

## o1 subject: Jerzy Łoś

Jerzy Maria Michał Łoś (; born 22 March 1920 – 1 June 1998) was a Polish mathematician, logician, economist, and philosopher. He studied at the University of Lviv, and during his career held positions at several institutions including the University of Wrocław, the Polish Academy of Sciences and the University of Warsaw.
