# David Kazhdan

## o1 subject: David Kazhdan

David Kazhdan (), born Dmitry Aleksandrovich Kazhdan (; born 20 June 1946), is a Soviet and Israeli mathematician known for work in representation theory. Kazhdan is a 1990 MacArthur Fellow. In 2012, he received the Israel Prize for Mathematics and Computer sciences.
