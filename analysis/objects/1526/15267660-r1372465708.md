# Fanghua Lin

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Fanghua Lin

Fanghua Lin (; born March 11, 1959), also written as Fang-Hua Lin, is a Chinese-born American mathematician. He is currently the Silver Professor at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University. He applies rigorous analysis to nonlinear systems and is a leader in this field. He was elected a member of the National Academy of Sciences in 2025.
