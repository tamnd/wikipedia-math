# Circular sector

## o1 subject: Circular sector

A circular sector, also known as circle sector or disk sector or simply a sector (symbol: ⌔), is the portion of a disk (a closed region bounded by a circle) enclosed by two radii and an arc, with the smaller area being known as the minor sector and the larger being the major sector. In the diagram, $\theta$ is the central angle, $r$ the radius of the circle, and $L$ is the arc length of the minor sector.
