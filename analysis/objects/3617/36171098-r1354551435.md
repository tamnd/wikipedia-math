# 2E6 (mathematics)

## o1 subject: 2E6

In mathematics, 2E6 is a family of Steinberg or twisted Chevalley groups. It is a quasi-split form of E6, depending on a quadratic extension of fields K⊂L. Unfortunately the notation for the group is not standardized, as some authors write it as 2E6(K) (thinking of 2E6 as an algebraic group taking values in K) and some as 2E6(L) (thinking of the group as a subgroup of E6(L) fixed by an outer involution).
