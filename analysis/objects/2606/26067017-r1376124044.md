# Warburg coefficient

## o1 subject: Warburg coefficient

The Warburg coefficient (or Warburg constant; denoted $A_{W}$ or $\sigma$) is the diffusion coefficient of ions in solution, associated to the Warburg element, $Z_{W}$. The Warburg coefficient has units of ${\Omega}/\sqrt{\text{seconds}}={\Omega} s^{-1/2}$
