## o3 subject: Chang Shih-Hsun

Chang Shih-Hsun or Zhang Shixun (; November 16, 1900 - September 10, 1985) was a Chinese mathematician. He was also known by his courtesy name Dingming (), later used as his pseudonym. He was born in Hexi Township, Langzhong, Sichuan, China.
