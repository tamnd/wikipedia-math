# Lantern relation

## o1 subject: Lantern relation

In geometric topology, a branch of mathematics, the lantern relation is a relation that appears between certain Dehn twists in the mapping class group of a surface. The most general version of the relation involves seven Dehn twists. The relation was discovered by Dennis Johnson in 1979.
