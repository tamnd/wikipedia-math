# Semicircle

## o1 infobox: infobox polygon

## o2 subject: Semicircle

In mathematics (and more specifically geometry), a semicircle is a one-dimensional locus of points that forms half of a circle. It is a circular arc that measures 180° (equivalently, $\pi$ radians, or a half-turn). It only has one line of symmetry (reflection symmetry).
