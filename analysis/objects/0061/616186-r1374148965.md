# Ludwig Bieberbach

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Ludwig Bieberbach

Ludwig Georg Elias Moses Bieberbach (; 4 December 1886 – 1 September 1982) was a German mathematician, who achieved early success solving the first part of Hilbert's eighteenth problem; he is best known for formulating the Bieberbach conjecture. He was also a leading representative of National Socialist German mathematics ("Deutsche Mathematik").
