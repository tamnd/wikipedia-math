# Icosagon

## o1 subject: Icosagon

Regular polygon stat table
In geometry, an icosagon or 20-gon is a twenty-sided polygon. The sum of any icosagon's interior angles is 3240 degrees.

## Uses
The Big Wheel on the popular US game show The Price is Right has an icosagonal cross-section.
The Globe, the outdoor theater used by William Shakespeare's acting company, was discovered to have been built on an icosagonal foundation when a partial excavation was done in 1989.
As a golygonal path, the swastika is considered to be an irregular icosagon.
A regular square, pentagon, and icosagon can completely fill a plane vertex.

## Symmetry
The regular icosagon has $\operatorname{Dih}_{20}$ symmetry, order 40. There are 5 subgroup dihedral symmetries: $(\operatorname{Dih}_{10}, \operatorname{Dih}_{5})$, and $(\operatorname{Dih}_{4}, \operatorname{Dih}_{2},\text{ and } \operatorname{Dih}_{1})$, and 6 cyclic group symmetries: $(Z_{20}, Z_{10}, Z_{5})$, and ($Z_{4}, Z_{2}, Z_{1})$.
These 10 symmetries can be seen in 16 distinct symmetries on the icosagon, a larger number because the lines of reflections can either pass through vertices or edges. John Conway labels these by a letter and group order. Full symmetry of the regular form is $\mathbf{r40}$ and no symmetry is labeled $\mathbf{a1}$. The dihedral symmetries are divided depending on whether they pass through vertices ($\mathbf{d}$ for diagonal) or edges ($\mathbf{p}$ for perpendiculars), and $\mathbf{i}$ when reflection lines path through both edges and vertices. Cyclic symmetries in the middle column are labeled as $\mathbf{g}$ for their central gyration orders.
Each subgroup symmetry allows one or more degrees of freedom for irregular forms. Only the $\mathbf{g20}$ subgroup has no degrees of freedom but can be seen as directed edges.
The highest symmetry irregular icosagons are $\mathbf{d20}$, an isogonal icosagon constructed by ten mirrors which can alternate long and short edges, and $\mathbf{p20}$, an isotoxal icosagon, constructed with equal edge lengths, but vertices alternating two different internal angles. These two forms are duals of each other and have half the symmetry order of the regular icosagon.
