## o1 subject: List of prime numbers

This is a list of articles about prime numbers. A prime number (or prime) is a natural number greater than 1 that has no divisors other than 1 and itself. By Euclid's theorem, there is an infinite number of prime numbers. Subsets of the prime numbers may be generated with various formulas for primes.

The Goldbach conjecture verification project reports that it has computed all primes smaller than 4×10${}^{18}$. That means 95,676,260,903,887,607 primes (nearly 10${}^{17}$), but they were not stored. There are known formulae to evaluate the prime-counting function (the number of primes smaller than a given value) faster than computing the primes. This has been used to compute that there are 1,925,320,391,606,803,968,923 primes (roughly 2) smaller than 10${}^{23}$. A different computation found that there are 18,435,599,767,349,200,867,866 primes (roughly 2) smaller than 10${}^{24}$, if the Riemann hypothesis is true.
