# Ammann A1 tilings

## o1 subject: Ammann A1 tilings

In geometry, an Ammann A1 tiling is a tiling from the 6-piece prototile set shown on the right. They were found in 1977 by Robert Ammann. Ammann was inspired by the Robinson tilings, which were found by Robinson in 1971. The A1 tiles are one of five sets of tiles discovered by Ammann and described in Tilings and patterns.
