# Ibn Hamza al-Maghribi

## o1 infobox: infobox scholar

## o2 subject: Ibn Hamza al-Maghribi

Alī ibn Walī Ibn Hamza al-Maghribi (), was a 16th-century Algerian mathematician. He was born between 1554-1575 in Algiers in Ottoman Algeria and died around 1611, during the rule of Murad III.
