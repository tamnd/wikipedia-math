# Elisha Netanyahu

## o1 subject: Elisha Netanyahu

Netanyahu
Elisha Netanyahu ((Mileikowsky);; December 21, 1912 – April 3, 1986) was a Polish-born Israeli mathematician specializing in complex analysis. Over the course of his work at the Technion, he was the Dean of the Faculty of Sciences and established the separate Department of Mathematics. He was the brother of historian Benzion Netanyahu and the uncle of current Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
