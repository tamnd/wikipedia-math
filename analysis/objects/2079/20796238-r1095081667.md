# Diamond graph

## o2 subject: Diamond graph

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the diamond graph is a planar, undirected graph with 4 vertices and 5 edges. It consists of a complete graph $K_4$ minus one edge.

## Algebraic properties
The full automorphism group of the diamond graph is a group of order 4 isomorphic to the Klein four-group, the direct product of the cyclic group $\mathbb{Z} / 2\mathbb{Z}$ with itself.
The characteristic polynomial of the diamond graph is $x(x+1)(x^2-x-4)$. It is the only graph with this characteristic polynomial, making it a graph determined by its spectrum.
