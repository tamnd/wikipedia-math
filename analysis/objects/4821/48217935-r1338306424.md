# Yael Dowker

## o1 subject: Yael Dowker

Yael Naim Dowker (; born Yael Naim; 30 October 1919 – 28 January 2016) was an Israeli-born English mathematician, prominent especially due to her work in the fields of measure theory, ergodic theory and topological dynamics.
