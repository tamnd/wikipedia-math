# HP 38G

## o1 subject: HP 38G

The HP 38G (F1200A, F1892A) is a programmable graphing calculator by Hewlett-Packard (HP). It was introduced in 1995 with a suggested retail price of 80. HP credits a committee of eight high school, community college, and university teachers with assisting in the design of the calculator.
