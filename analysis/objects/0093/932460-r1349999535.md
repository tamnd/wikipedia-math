## Cheng's diameter rigidity theorem
The conclusion of Myers' theorem says that for any $p, q \in M,$ one has $d_g(p,q)\leq\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{k}}$. In 1975, Shiu-Yuen Cheng proved:
Let $(M, g)$ be a complete and smooth Riemannian manifold of dimension $n$. If $k$ is a positive number with $\operatorname{Ric}^{g} \ge (n-1)k$, and if there exists $p$ and $q$ in $M$ with $d_g(p,q)=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{k}}$, then $(M,g)$ is simply-connected and has constant sectional curvature $k$.
