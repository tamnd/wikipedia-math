## Example
The string "Wiki" (01010111 01101001 01101011 01101001 in 8-bit ASCII) can be encrypted with the repeating key 11110011 as follows:
{|
| || 01010111 01101001 01101011 01101001
|-
| $\oplus$ || 11110011 11110011 11110011 11110011
|-
| = || style="border-top: 1px solid black" | 10100100 10011010 10011000 10011010
|}
And conversely, for decryption:
{|
| || 10100100 10011010 10011000 10011010
|-
| $\oplus$ || 11110011 11110011 11110011 11110011
|-
| = || style="border-top: 1px solid black" | 01010111 01101001 01101011 01101001
|}
