# National Statistics and Information Authority

## o1 subject: National Statistics and Information Authority

The National Statistics and Information Authority (NSIA, ; ), formerly known by other names such the Central Statistics Office (; ), is a government agency in Afghanistan that collects and maintains the country's statistical data. It was established in 1972. Abdul Qahir Haji Edris serves as NSIA's General Director.
