# Apeirogonal hosohedron

## o1 subject: Apeirogonal hosohedron

Reg tiling stat table
In geometry, an apeirogonal hosohedron or infinite hosohedron is a tiling of the plane consisting of two vertices at infinity. It may be considered an improper regular tiling of the Euclidean plane, with Schläfli symbol $\{2,\infty\}.$
