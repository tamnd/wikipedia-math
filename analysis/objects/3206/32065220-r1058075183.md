# Springer resolution

## o1 subject: Springer resolution

In mathematics, the Springer resolution is a resolution of the variety of nilpotent elements in a semisimple Lie algebra, or the unipotent elements of a reductive algebraic group, introduced by Tonny Albert Springer in 1969. The fibers of this resolution are called Springer fibers.

## Examples
When G=SL(2), the Lie algebra Springer resolution is T*P1 → n, where n are the nilpotent elements of sl(2). In this example, n are the matrices x with tr(x2)=0, which is a two dimensional conical subvariety of sl(2). n has a unique singular point 0, the fibre above which in the Springer resolution is the zero section P1 .
