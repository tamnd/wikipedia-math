# András Recski

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: András Recski

András Recski (; born 21 April 1948) is a Hungarian mathematician working in matroid theory and combinatorial optimization, and especially in their applications to the theory of electric networks and to rigidity in statics. He is professor emeritus at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME), where he headed the Department of Computer Science and Information Theory from 1990 to 2011, and a Doctor of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences. He was secretary-general of the János Bolyai Mathematical Society from 2006 to 2015.
