# Moncef Ben Salem

## o1 subject: Moncef Ben Salem

Moncef Ben Salem (; February 1, 1953 – March 24, 2015) was a Tunisian politician and university professor. He served as minister of higher education and scientific research under Prime Minister Hamadi Jebali.
