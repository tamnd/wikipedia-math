# Alexander V. Karzanov

## o1 subject: Alexander V. Karzanov

Alexander Viktorovich Karzanov (, born 1947) is a Russian mathematician known for his work in combinatorial optimization. He is the inventor of preflow-push based algorithms for the maximum flow problem, and the co-inventor of the Hopcroft–Karp–Karzanov algorithm for maximum matching in bipartite graphs. In 1977-2018 he was a senior, leading, chief researcher at the Federal Research Center "Computer Science and Control" (Institute for System Analysis) of the Russian Academy of Sciences. Since 2018 he is a chief researcher at Central Institute of Economics and Mathematics (CEMI) of the RAS.
