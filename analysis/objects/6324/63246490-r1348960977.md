# Jack Yung Chang

## o1 subject: Jack Yung Chang

Chang
Jack Yung Chang (; 9 March 1911 – 16 December 1939), courtesy name Junzhi (), was a Chinese historian of mathematics. His most significant work was on calendar systems in Asia.
