# Kosovar Mathematical Society

## o2 subject: Kosovar Mathematical Society

The Kosovar Mathematical Society or KMS (Albanian: , SHMK) is a mathematical society founded in Kosovo in 2008.
The KMS is based in Pristina, and has been a member of the European Mathematical Society since 2012.
