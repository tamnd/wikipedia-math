# S-object

## o1 subject: S-object

In algebraic topology, an $\mathbb{S}$-object (also called a symmetric sequence) is a sequence $\{ X(n) \}$ of objects such that each $X(n)$ comes with an action of the symmetric group $\mathbb{S}_n$.
