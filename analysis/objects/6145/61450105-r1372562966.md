# María del Pilar Díaz

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: María del Pilar Díaz

María del Pilar Díaz is an Argentine statistician. She is a professor of statistics in the Faculty of Economics at the National University of Córdoba (UNC). Pilar Díaz was an instructor in the Department of Statistics and Biostatistics in the School of Nutrition in the Facultad de Ciencias Médicas (Universidad Nacional de Córdoba) at UNC in 1983.
