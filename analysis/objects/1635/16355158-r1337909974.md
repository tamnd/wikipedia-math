# Iwasawa group

## o1 subject: Iwasawa group

__NOTOC__
In mathematics, a group is called an Iwasawa group, M-group or modular group if its lattice of subgroups is modular. Alternatively, a group G is called an Iwasawa group when every subgroup of G is permutable in G . The name is derived from Kenkichi Iwasawa.
