# Homomorphic equivalence

## o1 subject: Homomorphic equivalence

In graph theory, a branch of mathematics, two graphs G and H are called homomorphically equivalent if there exists a graph homomorphism $f\colon G \to H$ and a graph homomorphism $g\colon H \to G$. An example usage of this notion is that any two cores of a graph are homomorphically equivalent.
