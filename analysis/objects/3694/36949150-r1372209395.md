## o2 subject: Gennady Belyi

Gennadii Vladimirovich Belyi (1951–2001, , ) was a Soviet, Ukrainian, and Russian mathematician, known for Belyi's theorem on the representation of algebraic curves as Riemann surfaces and for the Belyi functions arising in that theorem.
