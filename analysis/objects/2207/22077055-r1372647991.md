# Walter Taylor (mathematician)

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Walter Taylor

Walter Taylor (1700 – 23 February 1743/44) was a Trinity College, Cambridge mathematics tutor who coached 83 students in the 1724–1743 period. He later was appointed as the Regius Professor of Greek.
