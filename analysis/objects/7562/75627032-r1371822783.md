# Slavik Vlado Jablan

## o2 subject: Slavik Vlado Jablan

Slavik Vlado Jablan (; 10June 1952 – 26February 2015) was a Serbian mathematician and crystallographer. Jablan is known for his contributions to antisymmetry, knot theory, the theory of symmetry and ornament, and ethnomathematics.
