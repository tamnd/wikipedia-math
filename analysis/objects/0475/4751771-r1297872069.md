# Spherical multipole moments

## o1 subject: Spherical multipole moments

In physics, spherical multipole moments are the coefficients in a series expansion of a potential that varies inversely with the distance $R$ to a source, i.e., as $\tfrac 1 R.$ Examples of such potentials are the electric potential, the magnetic potential and the gravitational potential.
