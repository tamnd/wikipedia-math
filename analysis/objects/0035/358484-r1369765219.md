# Girard Desargues

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Girard Desargues

Girard Desargues (; 21 February 1591September 1661) was a French mathematician and engineer, who is considered one of the founders of projective geometry. Desargues's theorem, the Desargues graph, and the crater Desargues on the Moon are named in his honour.
