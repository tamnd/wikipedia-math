# Hilbert's twelfth problem

## o1 subject: Hilbert's twelfth problem

Hilbert's twelfth problem is the extension of the Kronecker–Weber theorem on abelian extensions of the rational numbers, to any base number field. It is one of the 23 mathematical Hilbert problems and asks for analogues of the roots of unity that generate a whole family of further number fields, analogously to the cyclotomic fields and their subfields. Leopold Kronecker described the complex multiplication issue as his , or "dearest dream of his youth", so the problem is also known as Kronecker's Jugendtraum.
