# International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians

## o1 subject: International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians

The International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians (ICCM) (国际华人数学家大会) is an international non-governmental organization dedicated to bringing together Chinese mathematicians to discuss current research in mathematics as well as recognizing the achievements of Chinese mathematicians and mathematicians of Chinese descent around the world. The Congress was founded in 1998 and has been held every three years since.
