# Xinwen Zhu

## o1 subject: Xinwen Zhu

Xinwen Zhu (; born 1982 in Sichuan) is a Chinese mathematician and professor at Stanford University. His work deals primarily with geometric representation theory and in particular the Langlands program, tying number theory to algebraic geometry and quantum physics.
