# Circumcevian triangle

## o1 subject: Circumcevian triangle

In Euclidean geometry, a circumcevian triangle is a special triangle associated with a reference triangle and a point in the plane of the triangle. It is also associated with the circumcircle of the reference triangle.

## o2 section: Definition

## o3 definition: Definition

## Definition
Let $P$ be a point in the plane of the reference triangle $\triangle ABC$. Let the lines $AP, BP, CP$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $A', B', C'$. The triangle $\triangle A'B'C\boldsymbol{}$ is called the circumcevian triangle of $P$ with reference to $\triangle ABC$.
