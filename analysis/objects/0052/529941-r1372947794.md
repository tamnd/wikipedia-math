# E. M. Wright

## o1 subject: E. M. Wright

Sir Edward Maitland Wright FRSE (13 February 1906 – 2 February 2005) was an English mathematician, best known for co-authoring An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers with G. H. Hardy. He served as the Principal of the University of Aberdeen from 1962 to 1976.
