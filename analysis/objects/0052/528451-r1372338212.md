# Abraham Fraenkel

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Abraham Fraenkel

Abraham Fraenkel (; 17 February 1891 – 15 October 1965) was a German-born Israeli mathematician. He was an early Zionist and the first Dean of Mathematics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He is known for his contributions to axiomatic set theory, especially his additions to Ernst Zermelo's axioms, which resulted in the Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory.
