## o2 subject: Stanislav Smirnov

Stanislav Konstantinovich Smirnov (; born 3 September 1970) is a Russian mathematician currently working as a professor at the University of Geneva. He was awarded the Fields Medal in 2010. His research involves complex analysis, dynamical systems and probability theory.
