# Askey scheme

## o1 subject: Askey scheme

In mathematics, the Askey scheme is a way of organizing orthogonal polynomials of hypergeometric or basic hypergeometric type into a hierarchy. For the classical orthogonal polynomials discussed in , the Askey scheme was first drawn by and by , and has since been extended by and Koekoek, Lesky & Swarttouw (2010) to cover basic orthogonal polynomials.
