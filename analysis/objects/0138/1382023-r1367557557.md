# Quartic interaction

## o1 subject: Quartic interaction

In quantum field theory, a quartic interaction or φ4 theory is a type of self-interaction of a scalar field. Other types of quartic interactions may be found under the topic of four-fermion interactions. A classical free scalar field $\varphi$ satisfies the Klein–Gordon equation. If a scalar field is denoted $\varphi$, a quartic interaction is represented by adding an interaction energy term $({\lambda}/{4!}) \varphi^4$ to the Lagrangian density. The coupling constant $\lambda$ is dimensionless in 4-dimensional spacetime.
