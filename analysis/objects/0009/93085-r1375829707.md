# Johann Heinrich Lambert

## o1 subject: Johann Heinrich Lambert

Johann Heinrich Lambert (; ; 26 or 28 August 1728 – 25 September 1777) was an Alsatian polymath from the Republic of Mulhouse—then an associate of the Swiss Confederacy—who made fundamental contributions to mathematics, physics (particularly optics), philosophy, astronomy, and map projections.
