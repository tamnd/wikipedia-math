# Propositiones ad Acuendos Juvenes

## o1 subject: Propositiones ad Acuendos Juvenes

The medieval Latin manuscript Propositiones ad Acuendos Juvenes () is one of the earliest known collections of recreational mathematics problems. The oldest known copy of the manuscript dates from the late 9th century. The text is attributed to Alcuin of York (died 804.) Some editions of the text contain 53 problems, others 56. It has been translated into English by John Hadley, with annotations by John Hadley and David Singmaster.
