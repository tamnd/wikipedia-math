# Product term

## o1 subject: Product term

In Boolean logic, a product term is a conjunction of literals, where each literal is
either a variable or its negation.

## o2 section: Examples

## o3 example: Examples

## Examples
Examples of product terms include:
$A \wedge B$
$A \wedge (\neg B) \wedge (\neg C)$
$\neg A$
