## o3 subject: A4 polytope

In 4-dimensional geometry, there are 9 uniform polytopes with A4 symmetry. There is one self-dual regular form, the 5-cell with 5 vertices.

## o4 section: Symmetry

## o5 statement/fact: Symmetry

## Symmetry
A4 symmetry, or [3,3,3] is order 120, with Conway quaternion notation +1/60[I×$\overline{I}$].21. Its abstract structure is the symmetric group S5. Three forms with symmetric Coxeter diagrams have extended symmetry, [[3,3,3]] of order 240, and Conway notation ±1/60[I×$\overline{I}$].2, and abstract structure S5×C2.
