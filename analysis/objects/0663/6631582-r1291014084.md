# Nikolay Krasovsky

## o1 subject: Nikolay Krasovsky

Nikolay Nikolayevich Krasovsky (; 7 September 1924 – 4 April 2012, also transliterated Nikolai Krasovskii) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician who worked in the mathematical theory of control, the theory of dynamical systems, and the theory of differential games. He was the author of Krasovskii-LaSalle principle and the chief of the Ural scientific school in mathematical theory of control and the theory of differential games.
