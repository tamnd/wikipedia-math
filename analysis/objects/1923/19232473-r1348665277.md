## o3 subject: 2 21 polytope

In 6-dimensional geometry, the 221 polytope is a uniform 6-polytope, constructed within the symmetry of the E6 group. It was discovered by Thorold Gosset, published in his 1900 paper. He called it a 6-ic semi-regular figure. It is also called the Schläfli polytope.
