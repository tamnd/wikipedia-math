# Anders Johan Lexell

## o1 subject: Anders Johan Lexell

Anders Johan Lexell (24 December 1740 – 11 December) was a Finnish-Swedish astronomer, mathematician, and physicist who spent most of his life in Imperial Russia, where he was known as Andrei Ivanovich Leksel (Андрей Иванович Лексель).
