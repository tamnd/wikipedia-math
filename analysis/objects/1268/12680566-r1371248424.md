## o1 subject: Chow–Liu tree

In probability theory and statistics, a Chow–Liu tree is an efficient method for constructing a second-order product approximation of a joint probability distribution, first described in a paper by . The goals of such a decomposition, as with such Bayesian networks in general, may be either data compression or inference.
