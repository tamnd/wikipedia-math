# Fluent calculus

## o1 subject: Fluent calculus

The fluent calculus is a formalism for expressing dynamical domains in first-order logic. It is a variant of the situation calculus. A binary function symbol $\circ$ is used to concatenate the terms that represent facts that hold in a situation. For example, that the box is on the table in the situation $s$ is represented by the formula $\exists t . s = on(box,table) \circ t$. The frame problem is solved by asserting that the situation after the execution of an action is identical to the one before but for the conditions changed by the action.
