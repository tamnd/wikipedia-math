# Guorong Wang

## o1 subject: Guorong Wang

Guorong Wang (; born 1940) is a Chinese mathematician, working in the area of generalized inverses of matrices. He is a Professor and first Dean of Mathematics & Science College of Shanghai Normal University, Shanghai, China.
