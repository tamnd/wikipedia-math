# Category of manifolds

## o1 subject: Category of manifolds

In mathematics, the category of manifolds, often denoted Manp, is the category whose objects are manifolds of smoothness class Cp and whose morphisms are p-times continuously differentiable maps. This forms a category because the composition of two Cp maps is again continuous and of class Cp.
