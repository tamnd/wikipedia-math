# Basu

## o1 subject: Basu

Basu (, ), also Bose (anglicized), Boshu, Bosu, or Bosh, is a Bengali Kayastha surname originating from the Bengal region of the Indian subcontinent. The name stems from Vāsu (), which means 'Bright one'.
