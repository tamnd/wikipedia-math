# Lyudmila Keldysh

## o1 subject: Lyudmila Keldysh

Vsevolodovna
Lyudmila Vsevolodovna Keldysh (; 12 March 1904 – 16 February 1976) was a Soviet mathematician known for set theory and geometric topology. Her son, Sergei Novikov, was also a mathematician.
