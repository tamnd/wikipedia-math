# Menelaus of Alexandria

## o1 subject: Menelaus of Alexandria

Menelaus of Alexandria (; , Menelaos ho Alexandreus; 100 CE) was a Greek mathematician and astronomer, the first to recognize geodesics on a curved surface as natural analogs of straight lines.
