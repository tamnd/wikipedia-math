# Julian Sochocki

## o1 subject: Julian Sochocki

Julian Karol Sochocki (; ; February 2, 1842 – December 14, 1927) was a Polish-Russian mathematician and a professor at Saint Petersburg University. He is known for his work in complex analysis and number theory. His name is sometimes transliterated from Russian in several different ways (e.g. Sokhotski or Sochotski).
