# List of small groups

## o1 subject: List of small groups

The following list in mathematics contains the finite groups of small order up to group isomorphism.

## o2 section: Counts

## o3 section: Glossary

## o4 entry: Cyclic group

- Zn: the cyclic group of order n (the notation Cn is also used; it is isomorphic to the additive group of Z/nZ)

## o5 entry: Dihedral group

- Dihn: the dihedral group of order 2n (often the notation Dn or D2n is used)

## o6 entry: Klein four-group

- K4: the Klein four-group of order 4, isomorphic to Z2 × Z2 and Dih2

## o7 entry: Symmetric group

- Sn: the symmetric group of degree n, containing the n! permutations of n elements

## o8 entry: Alternating group

- An: the alternating group of degree n, containing the even permutations of n elements, of order 1 for n = 0, 1, and order n!/2 otherwise

## o9 entry: Dicyclic group

- Dicn or Q4n: the dicyclic group of order 4n

## o10 entry: Quaternion group

- Q8: the quaternion group of order 8, also Dic2

## o28 entry: the Heisenberg group modulo p

- Order p3: There are three abelian groups, and two non-abelian groups. One of the non-abelian groups is the semidirect product of a normal cyclic subgroup of order p2 by a cyclic group of order p. The other is the quaternion group for p = 2 and the Heisenberg group modulo p for p > 2.
