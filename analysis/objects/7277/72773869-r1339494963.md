# Madhava's correction term

## o1 subject: Madhava's correction term

Madhava's correction term is a mathematical expression attributed to Madhava of Sangamagrama (c. 1340 – c. 1425), the founder of the Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics, that can be used to give a better approximation to the value of the mathematical constant $\pi$ (pi) than the partial sum approximation obtained by truncating the Madhava–Leibniz infinite series for $\pi$.
