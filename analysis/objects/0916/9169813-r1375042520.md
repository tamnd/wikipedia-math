# François Bruhat

## o1 subject: François Bruhat

François Georges René Bruhat (; 8 April 1929 – 17 July 2007) was a French mathematician who worked on algebraic groups. The Bruhat order of a Weyl group, the Bruhat decomposition, and the Schwartz&ndash;Bruhat functions are named after him.
