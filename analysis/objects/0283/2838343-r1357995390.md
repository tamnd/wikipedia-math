# Calculus on Manifolds (book)

## o1 subject: Calculus on Manifolds

Calculus on Manifolds: A Modern Approach to Classical Theorems of Advanced Calculus (1965) by Michael Spivak is a brief, rigorous, and modern textbook on multivariable calculus, differential forms, and integration on manifolds for advanced undergraduates.

## o3 section: Description

## o4 statement/theorem: Stokes' Theorem for Manifolds with Boundaries.

If $M$ is a compact oriented $k$-dimensional manifold-with-boundary, $\partial M$ is the boundary given the induced orientation, and $\omega$ is a ($k-1$)-form on $M$, then $\int_M d\omega =\int_{\partial M} \omega$.
