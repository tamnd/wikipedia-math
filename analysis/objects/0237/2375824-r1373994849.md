# Dihedral group of order 6

## o1 subject: Dihedral group of order 6

In mathematics, D3 (sometimes alternatively denoted by D6) is the dihedral group of degree 3 and order 6. It is isomorphic to the symmetric group S3. It is also the smallest non-abelian group.
