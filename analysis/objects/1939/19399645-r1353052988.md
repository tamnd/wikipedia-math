# Hans Maass

## o1 subject: Hans Maass

Hans Maass (; June 17, 1911, in Hamburg – April 15, 1992) was a German mathematician who introduced Maass wave forms and Koecher–Maass series and Maass–Selberg relations and who proved most of the Saito–Kurokawa conjecture. Maass was a student of Erich Hecke.
