# Mathematical formulation of the Standard Model

## o1 subject: Mathematical formulation of the Standard Model

The Standard Model of particle physics is a gauge quantum field theory containing the internal symmetries of the unitary product group $\operatorname{SU}(3)\,\times\,\operatorname{SU}(2)\,\times\,U(1)$. The theory is commonly viewed as describing the fundamental set of particles – the leptons, quarks, gauge bosons and the Higgs boson.
