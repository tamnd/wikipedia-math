# Marinus of Neapolis

## o1 subject: Marinus of Neapolis

Marinus (; born c. 440 AD) was a Byzantine Neoplatonic philosopher, mathematician and rhetorician born in Flavia Neapolis (modern Nablus), Palaestina Secunda. He was a student of Proclus in Athens. His surviving works are an introduction to Euclid's Data; a Life of Proclus, and two astronomical texts.
