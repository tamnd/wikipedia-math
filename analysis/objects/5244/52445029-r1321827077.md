The Canadian Census of Agriculture (CEAG) ( (REAG)) is a census conducted every five years by Statistics Canada, alongside the Census of Population, for the purpose of creating, "a comprehensive profile of the physical, economic, social and environmental characteristics of Canada's agriculture industry."
