# Biquadratic field

## o1 subject: Biquadratic field

In mathematics, a biquadratic field is a number field $K$ of a particular kind, which is a Galois extension of degree 4 of the rational number field $\mathbb{Q}$ with Galois group isomorphic to the Klein four-group.
