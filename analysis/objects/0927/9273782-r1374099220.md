# Monad (philosophy)

## o1 subject: Monad

The term monad (grc) is used in some cosmic philosophy and cosmogony to refer to a most basic or original substance. As originally conceived by the Pythagoreans, the Monad is, therefore, the Supreme Being, divinity, or the totality of all things.
