# Bondage number

## o1 subject: Bondage number

In the mathematical field of graph theory, the bondage number of a nonempty graph $G$ is the cardinality of the smallest set of edges whose removal results in a domination number strictly greater than the domination number $\gamma(G)$ of $G$. The bondage number is denoted $b(G)$.

## o10 statement/conjecture: Conjectures

## Conjectures
For the bondage number $b(G)$ and maximum degree $\Delta(G)$ of a graph $G$, is $b(G) \le (3/2)\Delta(G)$ for all graphs?
A conjecture by Fink et al. from 1990 stated that for any nonempty graph $G$,
$b(G) \le \Delta(G) + 1$.
However, this was disproved by Teschner in 1993, who showed that the Cartesian product $K_{3} \square K_{3}$ has $b(K_{3} \square K_{3}) = \Delta(K_{3} \square K_{3}) + 2$. More generally, Hartnell-Rall and Teschner independently showed that $b(K_{n} \square K_{n}) = (3/2)\Delta(K_{n} \square K_{n})$ for $n \ge 3$. They also provided a 4-regular bipartite graph with bondage number 6 as another counterexample.
Teschner then proposed the following weaker conjecture:
For any graph $G$, $b(G) \le (3/2)\Delta(G)$.
This conjecture remains open.
### Planar graphs
For planar graphs, Dunbar et al. conjectured in 1998:
If $G$ is a planar graph, then $b(G) \le \Delta(G) + 1$.
This conjecture has been verified for planar graphs with $\Delta(G) \ge 7$ and for various other special cases, but remains open in general. It is known that $b(G) \le \min(8, \Delta(G) + 2)$ for any connected planar graph $G$.

## o15 statement/generalization: Variations

## Variations
Several variations of the bondage number have been studied based on different types of domination. The total bondage number considers total dominating sets (where every vertex in the dominating set must also be adjacent to another vertex in the dominating set). The Roman bondage number is based on Roman domination, where vertices are assigned values 0, 1, or 2 subject to certain constraints. Further generalizations include the double Roman bondage number, the independent Roman bondage number, and the quasi-total Roman bondage number. Other variations include the k-power bondage number, the cobondage number, and the lower bondage number.
