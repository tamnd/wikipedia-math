## o2 subject: Michiel Hazewinkel

Michiel Hazewinkel (22 June 19434 November 2025) was a Dutch mathematician, and Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at the Centre for Mathematics and Computer Science and the University of Amsterdam, particularly known for his 1978 book Formal groups and applications and as editor of the Encyclopedia of Mathematics.
