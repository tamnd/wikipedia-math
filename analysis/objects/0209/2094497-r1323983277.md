## Examples
For $a = 1$, the polynomials are
$p_0(x)=1;$
$p_1(x)=x;$
$p_2(x)=-2x+x^2;$
$p_3(x)=9x-6x^2+x^3;$
$p_4(x)=-64x +48x^2-12x^3+x^4;$
For $a = 2$, the polynomials are
$p_0(x)=1;$
$p_1(x)=x;$
$p_2(x)=-4x+x^2;$
$p_3(x)=36x-12x^2+x^3;$
$p_4(x)=-512x +192x^2-24x^3+x^4;$
$p_5(x)=10000x-4000x^2+600x^3-40x^4+x^5;$
$p_6(x)=-248832x+103680x^2-17280x^3+1440x^4-60x^5+x^6;$
