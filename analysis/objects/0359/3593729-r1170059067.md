# Abnormal subgroup

## o1 subject: Abnormal subgroup

In mathematics, specifically group theory, an abnormal subgroup is a subgroup H of a group G such that for all x in G, x lies in the subgroup generated by H and Hhairx, where Hhairx denotes the conjugate subgroup xHx−1.
