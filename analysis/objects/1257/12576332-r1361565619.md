# Bolza surface

## o1 subject: Bolza surface

In mathematics, the Bolza surface, alternatively, complex algebraic Bolza curve (introduced by ), is a compact Riemann surface of genus $2$ with the highest possible order of the conformal automorphism group in this genus, namely $GL_2(3)$ of order 48 (the general linear group of $2\times 2$ matrices over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_3$). Its full automorphism group (including reflections) is a semi-direct product $GL_{2}(3)\rtimes\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ of order 96.
