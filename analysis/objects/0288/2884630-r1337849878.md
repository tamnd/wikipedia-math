# William of Sherwood

## o1 subject: William of Sherwood

William of Sherwood or William Sherwood (Latin: Guillielmus de Shireswode; ), with numerous variant spellings, was a medieval English scholastic philosopher, logician, and teacher. Little is known of his life, but he is thought to have studied in Paris, was a master at Oxford in 1252, treasurer of Lincoln from 1254/1258 onwards, and a rector of Aylesbury.
