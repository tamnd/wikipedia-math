# Fyodor Gakhov

## o1 subject: Fyodor Gakhov

Fyodor Dmitriyevich Gakhov (; 19 February 1906, Cherkessk — 30 March 1980, Minsk) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician and a specialist in the field of boundary value problems for analytic functions of a complex variable.
