# James Nwoye Adichie

## o1 infobox: infobox person

## o2 subject: James Nwoye Adichie

Chief James Nwoye Adichie Ig-James Nwoye Adichie.ogg (1 March 1932 – 10 June 2020) was a Nigerian academic who served as a first professor of statistics at the University of Nigeria Nsukka. He was the father of writer Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie.
