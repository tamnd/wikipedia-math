# Lichnerowicz conjecture

## o1 subject: Lichnerowicz conjecture

In mathematics, the Lichnerowicz conjecture is a generalization of a conjecture introduced by . Lichnerowicz's original conjecture was that locally harmonic 4-manifolds are locally symmetric, and was proved by . The Lichnerowicz conjecture usually refers to the generalization that locally harmonic manifolds are flat or rank-1 locally symmetric. It has been proven true for compact manifolds with fundamental groups that are finite groups but counterexamples exist in seven or more dimensions in the non-compact case
