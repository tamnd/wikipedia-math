# Georges Henri Halphen

## o1 subject: Georges Henri Halphen

Georges Henri Halphen (; 30 October 1844 – 23 May 1889) was a French mathematician. He was known for his work in geometry, particularly in enumerative geometry and the singularity theory of algebraic curves, in algebraic geometry. He also worked on invariant theory and projective differential geometry.
