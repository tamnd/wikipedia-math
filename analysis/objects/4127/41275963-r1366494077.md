# Risk aversion (psychology)

## o1 subject: Risk aversion

Risk aversion is the preference for a guaranteed outcome over a gamble with higher or equal expected value. Conversely, rejection of a sure thing in favor of a gamble of lower or equal expected value is known as risk-seeking behavior.
