# Borsuk's conjecture

## o1 problem

What is the lowest $n$ such that not every bounded subset $E$ of the space $\mathbb{R}^n$ can be partitioned into ($n + 1$) sets, each of which has a smaller diameter than $E$?

## o2 subject: Borsuk's conjecture

The Borsuk problem in geometry, for historical reasons incorrectly called Borsuk's conjecture, is a question in discrete geometry. It is named after Karol Borsuk.
