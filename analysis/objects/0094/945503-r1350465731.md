# Hellenic Mathematical Society

## o1 subject: Hellenic Mathematical Society

The Hellenic Mathematical Society (HMS) () is a learned society which promotes the study of mathematics in Greece. It was founded in 1918 in Athens, and publishes the Bulletin of the Hellenic Mathematical Society (Δελτίο της Ελληνικής Μαθηματικής Εταιρίας) among other research and educational publications. It is a member of the European Mathematical Society. The HMS also organizes national math competitions.
