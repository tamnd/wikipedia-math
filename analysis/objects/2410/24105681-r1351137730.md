# Carleson's theorem

## o1 subject: Carleson's theorem

Carleson's theorem is a fundamental result in mathematical analysis establishing the (Lebesgue) pointwise almost everywhere convergence of Fourier series of $L^{2}$ functions, proved by Lennart Carleson. The name is also often used to refer to the extension of the result by Richard Hunt to $L^{p}$ functions for $p \in (1, \infty]$ (also known as the Carleson–Hunt theorem) and the analogous results for pointwise almost everywhere convergence of Fourier integrals, which can be shown to be equivalent by transference methods.
