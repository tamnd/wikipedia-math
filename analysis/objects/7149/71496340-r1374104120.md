# Wang algebra

## o1 subject: Wang algebra

In algebra and network theory, a Wang algebra is a commutative algebra $A$, over a field or (more generally) a commutative unital ring, in which $A$ has two additional properties: (Rule i) For all elements x of $A$, x + x = 0 (universal additive nilpotency of degree 1). (Rule ii) For all elements x of $A$, x⋅x = 0 (universal multiplicative nilpotency of degree 1).
