# Weeks manifold

## o1 subject: Weeks manifold

In mathematics, the Weeks manifold, sometimes called the Fomenko–Matveev–Weeks manifold, is a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold obtained by (5, 2) and (5, 1) Dehn surgeries on the Whitehead link. It has volume approximately equal to 0.942707… () and showed that it has the smallest volume of any closed orientable hyperbolic 3-manifold. The manifold was independently discovered by as well as .

## Symmetries
The Weeks manifold has symmetry group $D_6$, the dihedral group of order 12. Quotients by this group and its subgroups can be used to characterize the manifold as a branched covering based on an orbifold. In particular, the quotient by the order-3 subgroup of the symmetry group has underlying set a 3-sphere and branch set a 52 knot.
