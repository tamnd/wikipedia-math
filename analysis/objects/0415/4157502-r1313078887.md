# Rhombidodecadodecahedron

## o1 subject: Rhombidodecadodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the rhombidodecadodecahedron is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U38. It has 54 faces (30 squares, 12 pentagons and 12 pentagrams), 120 edges and 60 vertices. It is given a Schläfli symbol t0,2$\{\tfrac{5}{2},5\}$, and by the Wythoff construction this polyhedron can also be named a cantellated great dodecahedron.
