# Ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron

## o1 subject: Ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron (or ditrigonary dodecadodecahedron) is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U41. It has 24 faces (12 pentagons and 12 pentagrams), 60 edges, and 20 vertices. It has extended Schläfli symbol b{5,$\tfrac{5}{2}$}, as a blended great dodecahedron, and Coxeter diagram . It has 4 Schwarz triangle equivalent constructions, for example Wythoff symbol 3 | $\tfrac{5}{3}$ 5, and Coxeter diagram .
