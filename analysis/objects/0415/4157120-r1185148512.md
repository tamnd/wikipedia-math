# Nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron

## o1 subject: Nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U17. It has 26 faces (8 triangles and 18 squares), 48 edges, and 24 vertices. It is represented by the Schläfli symbol rr{4,$\tfrac{3}{2}$} and Coxeter-Dynkin diagram of . Its vertex figure is a crossed quadrilateral.
