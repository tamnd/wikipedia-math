# Great retrosnub icosidodecahedron

## o1 subject: Great retrosnub icosidodecahedron

In geometry, the great retrosnub icosidodecahedron or great inverted retrosnub icosidodecahedron is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as $U_{74}$. It has 92 faces (80 triangles and 12 pentagrams), 150 edges, and 60 vertices. It is given a Schläfli symbol $\operatorname{sr}\{\tfrac{3}{2},\tfrac{5}{3}\}.$
