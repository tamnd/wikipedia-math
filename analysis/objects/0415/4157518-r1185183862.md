# Truncated dodecadodecahedron

## o1 subject: Truncated dodecadodecahedron

In geometry, the truncated dodecadodecahedron (or stellatruncated dodecadodecahedron) is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U59. It is given a Schläfli symbol $t_{0,1,2}\{\tfrac{5}{3},5\}.$ It has 54 faces (30 squares, 12 decagons, and 12 decagrams), 180 edges, and 120 vertices. The central region of the polyhedron is connected to the exterior via 20 small triangular holes.
