# Great inverted snub icosidodecahedron

## o1 subject: Great inverted snub icosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the great inverted snub icosidodecahedron (or great vertisnub icosidodecahedron) is a uniform star polyhedron, indexed as U69. It is given a Schläfli symbol $\operatorname{sr}\{\tfrac{5}{3},3\},$ and Coxeter-Dynkin diagram . In the book Polyhedron Models by Magnus Wenninger, the polyhedron is misnamed great snub icosidodecahedron, and vice versa.

### Great inverted pentagonal hexecontahedron
Uniform dual polyhedron stat table
The great inverted pentagonal hexecontahedron (or petaloidal trisicosahedron) is a nonconvex isohedral polyhedron. It is composed of 60 concave pentagonal faces, 150 edges and 92 vertices.
It is the dual of the uniform great inverted snub icosidodecahedron.
