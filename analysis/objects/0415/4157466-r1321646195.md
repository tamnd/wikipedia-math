# Truncated great icosahedron

## o1 subject: Truncated great icosahedron

In geometry, the truncated great icosahedron (or great truncated icosahedron) is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U55. It has 32 faces (12 pentagrams and 20 hexagons), 90 edges, and 60 vertices. It is given a Schläfli symbol $t\{3,\tfrac{5}{2}\}$ or $t_{0,1}\{3,\tfrac{5}{2}\}$ as a truncated great icosahedron.
