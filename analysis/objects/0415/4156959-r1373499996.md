# Great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

## o1 subject: Great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron (or great ditrigonary icosidodecahedron) is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U47. It has 32 faces (20 triangles and 12 pentagons), 60 edges, and 20 vertices. It has 4 Schwarz triangle equivalent constructions, for example Wythoff symbol 3 | 3 $\tfrac{5}{4}$ gives Coxeter diagram = . It has extended Schläfli symbol a{$\tfrac{5}{2}$,3} or c{3,$\tfrac{5}{2}$}, as an altered great stellated dodecahedron or converted great icosahedron.
