# Snub dodecadodecahedron

## o1 subject: Snub dodecadodecahedron

In geometry, the snub dodecadodecahedron is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as $U_{40}$. It has 84 faces (60 triangles, 12 pentagons, and 12 pentagrams), 150 edges, and 60 vertices. It is given a Schläfli symbol $\operatorname{sr}\{\tfrac{5}{2},5\},$ as a snub great dodecahedron.
