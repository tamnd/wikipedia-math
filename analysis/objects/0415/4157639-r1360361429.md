# Nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron

## o1 subject: Nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U67. It has 62 faces (20 triangles, 30 squares and 12 pentagrams), 120 edges, and 60 vertices. It is also called the quasirhombicosidodecahedron. It is given a Schläfli symbol $\operatorname{rr}\{\tfrac{5}{3},3\}.$ Its vertex figure is a crossed quadrilateral.
