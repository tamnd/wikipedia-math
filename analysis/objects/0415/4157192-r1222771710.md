# Great icosicosidodecahedron

## o1 subject: Great icosicosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table
In geometry, the great icosicosidodecahedron (or great icosified icosidodecahedron) is a nonconvex uniform polyhedron, indexed as U48. It has 52 faces (20 triangles, 12 pentagons, and 20 hexagons), 120 edges, and 60 vertices. Its vertex figure is a crossed quadrilateral.
