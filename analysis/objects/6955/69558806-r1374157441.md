# Klein–Kramers equation

## o1 subject: Klein–Kramers equation

In physics and mathematics, the Klein–Kramers equation or sometimes referred as Kramers–Chandrasekhar equation is a partial differential equation that describes the probability density function $f (\mathbf{r}, \mathbf{p}, t)$ of a Brownian particle in phase space $(\mathbf{r}, \mathbf{p})$. It is a special case of the Fokker–Planck equation.
