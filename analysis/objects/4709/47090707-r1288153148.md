# Sylvester graph

## o1 infobox: infobox graph

## o2 subject: Sylvester graph

The Sylvester graph is the unique distance-regular graph
with intersection array $\{5,4,2;1,1,4\}$.
It is a subgraph of the Hoffman–Singleton graph.
