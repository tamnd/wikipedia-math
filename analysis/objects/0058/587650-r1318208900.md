# Pursuit curve

## o1 subject: Pursuit curve

In geometry, a curve of pursuit is a curve constructed by analogy to having a point or points representing pursuers and pursued; the curve of pursuit is the curve traced by the pursuers.

## o2 section: Definition

## o3 definition: Definition

## Definition
With the paths of the pursuer and pursued parameterized in time, the pursued is always on the pursuer's tangent. That is, given $F(t)$, the pursuer (follower), and $L(t)$, the pursued (leader), for every $t$ with $F(t) \ne 0$ there is an $x$ such that
$L(t) = F(t) + xF'\!(t).$
