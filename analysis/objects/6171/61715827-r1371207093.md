## o2 subject: Tanya Khovanova

Tanya Khovanova (, also spelled Tatyana Hovanova; born 25 January 1959) is an American mathematician of Russian origin who became the second female gold medalist at the International Mathematical Olympiads. She is a lecturer in mathematics at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
