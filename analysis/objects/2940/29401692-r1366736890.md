## o3 subject: B8 polytope

In 8-dimensional geometry, there are 255 uniform polytopes with B8 symmetry (to which this article adds for illustration the 8-demicube as an alternation with half symmetry). There are two regular forms, the 8-orthoplex and 8-cube, with 16 and 256 vertices respectively.
