## o2 subject: Boris Feigin

Boris Lvovich Feigin (; ; born 20 November 1953) is a Russian and Israeli mathematician. His research has spanned representation theory, mathematical physics, algebraic geometry, Lie groups and Lie algebras, conformal field theory, homological and homotopical algebra.
