# Gladys Gregory

## o1 subject: Gladys Gregory

Gladys Caroline Gregory (19 May 1899 6 November 1981) was one of two women to share the accolade of first woman actuary in the United Kingdom, the other being Dorothy Spiers, née Davis. Gregory joined Prudential Assurance in 1917, direct from school, and passed the actuarial exams at the Institute of Actuaries in 1923.
