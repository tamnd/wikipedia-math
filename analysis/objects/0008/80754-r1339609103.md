# Division by two

## o1 subject: Division by two

In mathematics, division by two, also called halving, mediation, or dimidiation, is common in formulas and as a step in arithmetical calculations; it is equivalent to multiplication by one half. Starting with an arbitrary number or quantity $x$, its division by two can be written as any of the following equivalent expressions:
$$x \div 2,\ x / 2,\ \frac x 2,\ \tfrac12 x,\ 0.5 x.$$
