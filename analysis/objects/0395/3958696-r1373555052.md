## o2 subject: Tatyana Afanasyeva-Ehrenfest

Tatyana Alexeyevna Afanasyeva-Ehrenfest (Afanasyeva; ; 19 November 1876 – 14 April 1964) was a Russian-Dutch mathematician and physicist who made contributions to the fields of statistical mechanics and statistical thermodynamics with her husband Paul Ehrenfest.
