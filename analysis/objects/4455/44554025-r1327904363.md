# Hiroshi Saito (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Hiroshi Saito

was a Japanese mathematician at the Division of Mathematics and Mathematical Sciences, Graduate School of Science, Kyoto University who worked on automorphic forms. He introduced the base change lifting and the Saito–Kurokawa lift.
