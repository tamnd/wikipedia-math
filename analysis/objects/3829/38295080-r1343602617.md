# Vittorio Francesco Stancari

## o1 subject: Vittorio Francesco Stancari

Vittorio Francesco Stancari (29 July 167828 March 1709) was a professor of mathematics at the University of Bologna who undertook research into the measurement of sounds, and into optics and hydrostatics.
