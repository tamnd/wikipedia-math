# Nets within nets

## o1 subject: Nets within nets

Nets within nets is a modelling method belonging to the family of Petri nets.
This method is distinguished from other sorts of Petri nets by the possibility to provide their tokens with a proper structure, which is based on Petri net modelling again. Hence, a net can contain further net items, being able to move around and fire themselves.
