# Loop theorem

## o1 subject: Loop theorem

In mathematics, in the topology of 3-manifolds, the loop theorem is a generalization of Dehn's lemma. The loop theorem was first proven by Christos Papakyriakopoulos in 1956, along with Dehn's lemma and the Sphere theorem.

## o2 section: Corollary

## o3 statement/corollary: Corollary

## Corollary
One easy corollary of the loop theorem is a following: Let $M$ be a compact orientable irreducible 3-manifold. Then $\partial M$ is incompressible if and only if $\pi_1(F)\to\pi_1(M)$ is injective for each component $F$ of $\partial M$.
