## o3 subject: Zhuo Qun Song

Zhuo Qun Song (; born 1997), also called Alex Song, is a Canadian who was from 2015 to 2026, the most highly decorated International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) contestant, with five gold medals and one bronze medal. He was surpassed by Alex Chui in July 2026.
