# 1930 Dutch East Indies census

## o1 subject: 1930 Dutch East Indies census

The 1930 Dutch East Indies census () was the last census of the Dutch East Indies. The total population was enumerated to be 60,727,233. Its population density in 1930 was 60727233 inhabitants/km2.
