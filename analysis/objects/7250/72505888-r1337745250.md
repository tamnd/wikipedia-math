# Qi-Man Shao

## o1 subject: Qi-Man Shao

Qi-Man Shao (; born 1962) is a Chinese probabilist and statistician mostly known for his contributions to asymptotic theory in probability and statistics. He is currently a Chair Professor of Statistics and Data Science at the Southern University of Science and Technology.
