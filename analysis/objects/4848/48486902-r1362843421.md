# Baliński

## o1 subject: Baliński

Baliński
Baliński (feminine: Balińska) or Balinski is a Polish-language surname. It is a toponymic surname for someone associated with any of various places named Balin. Russian form: Balinsky, Lithuanian: Balinskis.
