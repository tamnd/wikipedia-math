# Sławomir Kołodziej

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Sławomir Kołodziej

Sławomir Kołodziej (Polish: s; born 12 March 1961 in Bielsko-Biała) is a Polish mathematician and Professor at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science of the Jagiellonian University in Kraków. His research interests include complex analysis and theoretical mathematics including Monge–Ampère equation and plurisubharmonic functions.
