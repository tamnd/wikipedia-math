# Shai Haran

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Shai Haran

Shai Haran (; born 1958) is an Israeli mathematician and professor at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology. He is known for his work in p-adic analysis, p-adic quantum mechanics, and non-additive geometry, including the field with one element, in relation to strategies for proving the Riemann Hypothesis.
