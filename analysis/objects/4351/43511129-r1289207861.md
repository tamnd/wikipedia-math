# Darkside communication group

## o1 subject: Darkside communication group

is a publishing group of Japanese Dōjinshi in Kashiwa city. The group is known in Japan for its scientific and Otaku activities. It was established in the 1990s. Their best known work is in 1996. Their monthly magazine won "Best titled book in Japan (日本タイトルだけ大賞)" in 2012.
