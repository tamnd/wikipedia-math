# Euclides Danicus

## o1 infobox: infobox book

## o2 subject: Euclides Danicus

Euclides Danicus (Danish Euclid) is one of three books of mathematics written by Georg Mohr. It was published in 1672 simultaneously in Copenhagen and Amsterdam, in Danish and Dutch respectively. It contains the first proof of the Mohr–Mascheroni theorem, which states that every geometric construction that can be performed using a compass and straightedge can also be done with compass alone.
