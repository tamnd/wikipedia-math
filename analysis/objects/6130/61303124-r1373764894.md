# Nadeschda Gernet

## o1 subject: Nadeschda Gernet

Nikolayevna
Nadeschda Nikolayevna Gernet (; April 18, 1877 – January 1, 1943) was a Russian mathematician, academic, and the second Russian woman to earn a doctorate. Gernet extended the calculus of variations to further functions on the basis developed by her instructor, David Hilbert, and was one of the first to include inequalities in the calculus of variations.
