## o2 subject: Ilya M. Sobol'

Ilya Meyerovich Sobol' (; 15 August 1926 – 9 December 2025) was a Russian mathematician, known for his work on Monte Carlo methods. His research spanned several applications, from nuclear studies to astrophysics, and has contributed significantly to the field of sensitivity analysis.
