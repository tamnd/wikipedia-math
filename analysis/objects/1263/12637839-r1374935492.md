# Chuu-Lian Terng

## o1 subject: Chuu-Lian Terng

Terng Chuu-Lian
Chuu-Lian Terng (; born 1949) is a Taiwanese-American mathematician. Her research areas are differential geometry and integrable systems, with particular interests in completely integrable Hamiltonian partial differential equations and their relations to differential geometry, the geometry and topology of submanifolds in symmetric spaces, and the geometry of isometric actions.
