# Gerhard Frey (mathematician)

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Gerhard Frey

Gerhard Frey (; born 1 June 1944) is a German mathematician, known for his work in number theory. Following an original idea of Yves Hellegouarch, he developed the notion of Frey–Hellegouarch curves, a construction of an elliptic curve from a purported solution to the Fermat equation, that is central to Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem.
