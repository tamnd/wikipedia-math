# Jean Bourgain

## o1 subject: Jean Bourgain

Jean Louis, baron Bourgain (; – 2018) was a Belgian mathematician. He was awarded the Fields Medal in 1994 in recognition of his work on several core topics of mathematical analysis such as the geometry of Banach spaces, harmonic analysis, ergodic theory and nonlinear partial differential equations from mathematical physics.
