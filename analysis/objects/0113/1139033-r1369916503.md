# Pyotr Novikov

## o1 subject: Pyotr Novikov

Sergeyevich
Pyotr Sergeyevich Novikov (; 15 August 1901, Moscow – 9 January 1975, Moscow) was a Soviet mathematician known for his work in group theory. His son, Sergei Novikov, was also a mathematician.
