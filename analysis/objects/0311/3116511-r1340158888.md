# Vlastimil Pták

## o1 subject: Vlastimil Pták

Vlastimil Pták (; 8 November 1925 in Prague – 5 May 1999) was a Czech mathematician. He worked in functional analysis, theoretical numerical analysis, and linear algebra. Notable early work include generalizations of the open mapping theorem.
