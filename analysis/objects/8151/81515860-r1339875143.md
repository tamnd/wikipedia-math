# Ronghui Xu

## o1 subject: Ronghui Xu

Ronghui (Lily) Xu (; born 1969) is a Chinese and American biostatistician whose research interests include random effects models, causal inference, machine learning in biomedical big data, and applications in developmental toxicity including the study of fetal alcohol spectrum disorder and the risks of medicines and vaccines in pregnancy. She is a professor at the University of California, San Diego, affiliated with the university's Wertheim School of Public Health, its Department of Mathematics, and its Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute.
