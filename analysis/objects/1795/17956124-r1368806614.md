# HP-45

## o1 infobox: infobox calculator

## o2 subject: HP-45

The HP-45 is the second scientific pocket calculator introduced by Hewlett-Packard, adding to the features of the HP-35. It was introduced in 1973 with an MSRP of US$395 (US).US Especially noteworthy was its pioneering addition of a shift key that gave other keys alternate functions.
