# Higman group

## o1 subject: Higman group

In mathematics, the Higman group, introduced by , was the first example of an infinite finitely presented group with no nontrivial finite quotients.
The quotient by the maximal proper normal subgroup is a finitely generated infinite simple group. later found some finitely presented infinite groups $G_{n,r}$ that are simple if $n$ is even and have a simple subgroup of index 2 if $n$ is odd, one of which is one of the Thompson groups.
