# Lwów–Warsaw school

## o1 subject: Lwów–Warsaw school

The Lwów–Warsaw School () was an interdisciplinary school (mainly philosophy, logic and psychology) founded by Kazimierz Twardowski in 1895 in Lwów, Austro-Hungary (now Lviv, Ukraine).
