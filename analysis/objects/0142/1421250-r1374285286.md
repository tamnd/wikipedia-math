# Roger Apéry

## o1 infobox: infobox person

## o2 subject: Roger Apéry

Roger Apéry (; 14 November 1916 – 18 December 1994) was a Greek-French mathematician most remembered for Apéry's theorem, which states that $\zeta(3)$ is an irrational number. Here, $\zeta(s)$ denotes the Riemann zeta function.
