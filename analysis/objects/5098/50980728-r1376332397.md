# Wu-Chung Hsiang

## o1 subject: Wu-Chung Hsiang

Hsiang Wu-chung
Wu-Chung Hsiang (; born 12 June 1935) is a Taiwanese mathematician and topologist. He was highly influential in the field of differential topology as a professor at Yale University and then Princeton University, where he was the chairman of the mathematics department from 1982 to 1985. He has been described as one of the most influential topologists of the second half of the 20th century.
