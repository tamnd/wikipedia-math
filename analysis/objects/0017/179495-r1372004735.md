# Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers

## o1 subject: Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers

Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers (; ; 11 October 1758 – 2 March 1840) was a German astronomer. He found a convenient method of calculating the orbit of comets, and in 1802 and 1807, discovered the second and the fourth asteroids Pallas and Vesta.
