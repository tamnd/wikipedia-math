# Nikolay Morozkin

## o1 subject: Nikolay Morozkin

Nikolai Danilovich Morozkin (; born 27 November 1953) is a Soviet and Russian mathematician. Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (1996), Professor (1997), Rector of Bashkir State University (since 2013), Honorary Figure of Higher Education of the Russian Federation (2011).
