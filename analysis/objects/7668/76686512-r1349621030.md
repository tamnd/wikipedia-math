# Romanian Mathematical Society

## o1 subject: Romanian Mathematical Society

The Romanian Society of Mathematical Sciences (, ) is a professional organization of Romanian mathematicians founded in 1910, as a result of the efforts of several Romanian mathematics lovers.
It is recognised by the International Mathematics Union as the official mathematical society of Romania.
