# Cabtaxi number

## o1 subject: Cabtaxi number

In number theory, the $n$-th cabtaxi number, typically denoted $\operatorname{Cabtaxi}(n)$, is defined as the smallest positive integer that can be written as the sum of two positive or negative or 0 cubes in $n$ ways. Such numbers exist for all $n$, which follows from the analogous result for taxicab numbers.
