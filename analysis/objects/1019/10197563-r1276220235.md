# Olga Hahn-Neurath

## o1 subject: Olga Hahn-Neurath

Olga Hahn-Neurath (; 20 July 1882 – 20 July 1937) was an Austrian mathematician and philosopher. She is best known for being a member of the Vienna Circle. She was sister of the mathematician Hans Hahn.
