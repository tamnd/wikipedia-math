# Paul Painlevé

## o1 infobox: infobox officeholder

## o2 subject: Paul Painlevé

Paul Painlevé (; 5 December 1863 – 29 October 1933) was a French mathematician and statesman who served as Prime Minister of the French Third Republic in 1917 and 1925. After working as a professor at the Sorbonne University, he entered politics in 1906.
