# Imbalance conjecture

## o1 subject: Imbalance conjecture

Conjecture: If for all edges $e \in E(G)$ we have $\text{imb}(e) > 0$, then $M_G$ is graphic.
The imbalance conjecture is an open problem in graph theory concerning whether edge imbalance sequences are graphic, first formally stated by Kozerenko and Skochko in 2014.

## o4 section: Definitions

## o5 definition: Definitions

## Definitions
For a simple undirected graph $G$, the imbalance of an edge $e = uv$ is defined as:
$\text{imb}(e) = |\deg(u) - \deg(v)|$
where $\deg(u)$ and $\deg(v)$ denote the degrees of vertices $u$ and $v$ respectively.
The imbalance sequence $M_G$ is the multiset of all edge imbalances in $G$.
A sequence of non-negative integers is called graphic if it is the degree sequence of some graph. Note that the term sometimes allows a multigraph, but here it is defined as the degree sequence of a simple graph.
A graph $G$ is called imbalance graphic if its imbalance sequence $M_G$ is graphic.

## o7 section: Statement of the conjecture

## o8 statement/conjecture: Statement of the conjecture

## Statement of the conjecture
Imbalance Conjecture: If for all edges $e \in E(G)$ we have $\text{imb}(e) > 0$, then $M_G$ is graphic.
In other words, if no edge in a graph connects vertices of equal degree, then the multiset of edge imbalances forms a valid degree sequence for some simple graph.

## o14 statement/conjecture: Related conjectures

## Related conjectures
One related conjecture concerns the mean imbalance of a nonempty graph $G$, defined as:
$m(G) = \frac{I(G)}{|E(G)|}$
Mean Imbalance Conjecture: If $m(G) \geq 2$, then $M_G$ is graphic.
This conjecture was disproved by Kozerenko and Serdiuk in 2023, who showed that for every real number $c > 0$, there exists an imbalance non-graphic graph $G$ with $I(G) \geq c \cdot |E(G)|$.
Bicyclic Graph Conjecture: There is exactly one connected imbalance non-graphic bicyclic graph (shown to exist among graphs with at most 21 vertices).
Block Graph Conjecture: All block graphs are imbalance graphic.
This conjecture has been verified for all block graphs with at most 13 vertices. A weaker version conjectures that all line graphs of trees (which are exactly claw-free block graphs) are imbalance graphic.
