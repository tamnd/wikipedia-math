# Hsiang–Lawson's conjecture

## o1 subject: Hsiang–Lawson's conjecture

In differential geometry, Lawson's conjecture states that the Clifford torus is the only minimally embedded torus in the 3-sphere S3. The conjecture was featured by the Australian Mathematical Society Gazette as part of the Millennium Problems series.
