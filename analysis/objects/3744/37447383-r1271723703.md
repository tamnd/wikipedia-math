# Johann Heinrich Ernesti

## o1 subject: Johann Heinrich Ernesti

Johann Heinrich Ernesti (; ; 12 March 1652 – 16 October 1729) was a German philosopher, Lutheran theologian, Latin classicist and poet. He was rector of the Thomasschule, and Professor of Poetry at Leipzig University. He gained fame through his writings on Cicero.
