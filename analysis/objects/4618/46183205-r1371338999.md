# Y and H transforms

## o1 subject: Y and H transforms

In mathematics, the $Y$ transforms and $\mathbf{H}$ transforms are complementary pairs of integral transforms involving, respectively, the Neumann function (Bessel function of the second kind) $Y_{\nu}$ of order $\nu$ and the Struve function $\mathbf{H}_{\nu}$ of the same order.
