# Hsiao-Lan Kuo

## o1 subject: Hsiao-Lan Kuo

Kuo (Guo)
Hsiao-Lan Kuo (Guo Xiaolan; 1915–2006) was a Chinese-American mathematician and meteorologist. He was a recipient of the Carl-Gustaf Rossby Research Medal.
