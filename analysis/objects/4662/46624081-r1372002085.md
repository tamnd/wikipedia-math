# Tian Ye (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Tian Ye

Tian
Tian Ye or Ye Tian (; born November 1971) is a Chinese mathematician known for his research in number theory and arithmetic geometry. He is a professor of mathematics at the Chinese Academy of Sciences and is the deputy director of its Morningside Center of Mathematics.
