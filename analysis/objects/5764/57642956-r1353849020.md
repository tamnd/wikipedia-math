## o1 infobox: infobox institute

## o2 subject: Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche

The Mathematics Institute of Jussieu–Paris Rive Gauche (, , IMJ-PRG) is a French research institute in fundamental mathematics. It is a "mixed research unit", with three parent organizations: the Centre national de la recherche scientifique, Sorbonne University, and the University of Paris. It is located in Paris, split between two campuses: Jussieu and Paris Rive Gauche.
