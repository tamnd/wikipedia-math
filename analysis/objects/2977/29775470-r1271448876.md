# NemID

## o1 subject: NemID

NemID (EasyID) was a common login solution for Danish Internet banks, government websites, and some other private companies. NemID was managed by the Nets DanID A/S company and came into use on July 1, 2010. During its use, everyone in Denmark who was more than 15 years old and had a CPR-Number was eligible for a NemID, which could be used with their bank as well as public institutions. Anyone over 13 years old was able to use a NemID for internet banking. NemID was scheduled to be phased out on 30 June 2023, and replaced by MitID. It was shut down on 31 October 2023.
