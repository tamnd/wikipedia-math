# Quarter hypercubic honeycomb

## o1 subject: Quarter hypercubic honeycomb

In geometry, the quarter hypercubic honeycomb (or quarter n-cubic honeycomb) is a dimensional infinite series of honeycombs, based on the hypercube honeycomb. It is given a Schläfli symbol q{4,3...3,4} or Coxeter symbol qδ4 representing the regular form with three quarters of the vertices removed and containing the symmetry of Coxeter group ${\tilde{D}}_{n-1}$ for n ≥ 5, with ${\tilde{D}}_4$ = ${\tilde{A}}_4$ and for quarter n-cubic honeycombs ${\tilde{D}}_5$ = ${\tilde{B}}_5$.
