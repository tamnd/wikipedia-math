# Largest known prime number

## o1 subject: Largest known prime number

The largest known prime number is 2136,279,841 − 1, a number that has 41,024,320 digits when written in the decimal system. It was found on October 12, 2024, on a cloud-based virtual machine volunteered by Luke Durant, a researcher from San Jose, California, to the Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search (GIMPS).
