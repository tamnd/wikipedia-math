# Type A Cipher Machine

## o1 subject: Type A Cipher Machine

In the history of cryptography, or , codenamed Red by the United States, was a diplomatic cryptographic machine used by the Japanese Foreign Office before and during World War II. A relatively simple device, it was quickly broken by western cryptographers. The Red cipher was succeeded by the which used some of the same principles. Parallel usage of the two systems assisted in the breaking of the Purple system.
