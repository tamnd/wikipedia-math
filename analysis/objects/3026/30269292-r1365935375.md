# Ten Computational Canons

## o1 subject: Ten Computational Canons

The Ten Computational Canons () was a collection of ten Chinese mathematical works dating from pre-Han dynasty to early Tang dynasty, compiled by the early Tang mathematician Li Chunfeng (602–670) in the 650s, as the official mathematical texts for imperial examinations in mathematics.
