# Boris Kashin

## o1 subject: Boris Kashin

Boris Sergeevich Kashin (; born July 3, 1951, in Moscow) is a Russian mathematician, Academician of the Russian Academy of Sciences (since 2011), Doctor of Sciences, Professor at the MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.
Member of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of the Russian Federation since 2000.
