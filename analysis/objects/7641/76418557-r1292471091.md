# Henry Vuibert

## o1 subject: Henry Vuibert

Désiré-Henry Vuibert (21 August 185727 November 1945) was a French mathematician and publisher of technical books and journals, and founder of the French publishing house Vuibert. He was a publisher of the same class as Louis Hachette and Pierre Larousse, and is said to have begun his company in 1876.
