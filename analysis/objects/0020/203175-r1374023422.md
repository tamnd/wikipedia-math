# Émile Clapeyron

## o1 subject: Émile Clapeyron

Benoît Paul Émile Clapeyron (; 26 January 1799 – 28 January 1864) was a French engineer and physicist, one of the founders of thermodynamics. He is known for developing the ideal gas law.
