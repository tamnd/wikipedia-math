# Gradian

## o1 infobox: infobox unit

## o2 subject: Gradian

In trigonometry, the gradianalso known as the gon (grc), grad, or gradeis a unit of measurement of an angle, defined as one-hundredth of the right angle; in other words, 100 gradians is equal to 90 degrees. It is equivalent to $\tfrac{1}{400}$ of a turn, $\tfrac{9}{10}$ of a degree, or $\tfrac{\pi}{200}$ of a radian. Measuring angles in gradians (gons) is said to employ the centesimal system of angular measurement, initiated as part of metrication and decimalisation efforts.
