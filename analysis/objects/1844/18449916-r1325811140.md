# SC (complexity)

## o1 subject: SC

In computational complexity theory, SC (Steve's Class, named after Stephen Cook) is the complexity class of problems solvable by a deterministic Turing machine in polynomial time (class P) and polylogarithmic space (class PolyL) (that is, O((log n)k) space for some constant k). It may also be called DTISP(poly, polylog), where DTISP stands for deterministic time and space. The definition of SC differs from P ∩ PolyL, since for the former, it is required that a single algorithm runs in both polynomial time and polylogarithmic space; while for the latter, two separate algorithms will suffice: one that runs in polynomial time, and another that runs in polylogarithmic space. It is unknown whether SC and P ∩ PolyL are equivalent.
