# Vadym Adamyan

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Vadym Adamyan

Vadym Movsesovich Adamyan (; ; born 2 December 1938) is an Armenian Ukrainian mathematician and theoretical physicist, professor and head of the Department of Theoretical Physics at Odessa University. He is known for his contributions to operator theory and functional analysis.
