## o2 subject: Greta Panova

Greta Cvetanova Panova (, born 1983 in Sofia, Bulgaria) is a Bulgarian-American mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics and Gabilan Distinguished Professor in Science and Engineering at the University of Southern California in Los Angeles. Her research interests include combinatorics, probability and theoretical computer science.
