# SO(8)

## o1 subject: SO(8)

In mathematics, SO(8) is the special orthogonal group acting on eight-dimensional Euclidean space. It could be either a real or complex simple Lie group of rank 4 and dimension 28.

## o2 section: Spin(8)

## o3 pointer-item: Spin(8)

## Spin(8)
Like all special orthogonal groups SO(n) with n ≥ 2, SO(8) is not simply connected. And like all SO(n) with n > 2, the fundamental group of SO(8) is isomorphic to Z2. The universal cover of SO(8) is the spin group Spin(8).
