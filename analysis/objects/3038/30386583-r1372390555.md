# Olga Holtz

## o1 subject: Olga Holtz

Olga Holtz (; born August 19, 1973) is a Russian mathematician specializing in numerical analysis. She received the Sofia Kovalevskaya Award in 2006 and the European Mathematical Society Prize (2008). Since 2008, she is a member of the Young Academy (Junge Akademie) of Germany.
