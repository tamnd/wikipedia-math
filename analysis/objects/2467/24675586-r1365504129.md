# Homonoia

## o1 subject: Homonoia

Homonoia ( ) is the concept of order and unity, being of one mind together or union of hearts. It was used by the Greeks to create unity in the politics of classical Greece. It saw widespread use when Alexander the Great adopted its principles to govern his Empire.
