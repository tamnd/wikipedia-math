# Radiodrome

## o1 subject: Radiodrome

In geometry, a radiodrome is a specific type of pursuit curve: the path traced by a point that continuously moves toward a target traveling in a straight line at constant speed. The term comes from the Latin (ray or spoke) and the Greek grc (running or racetrack), reflecting the radial nature of the motion.

## o2 section: Mathematical analysis

## o3 equation: 1

$(T_x\ ,\ T_y)\ =\ (A_x\ ,\ A_y+V_t t)~.$

## o4 equation: 2

$\dot x= V_d\ \frac{T_x-x}{\sqrt{(T_x-x)^2+(T_y-y)^2}}$

## o5 equation: 3

$\dot y= V_d\ \frac{T_y-y}{\sqrt{(T_x-x)^2+(T_y-y)^2}} ~.$

## o6 equation: 4

$y'(x)=\frac{T_y-y}{T_x-x}$ .

## o7 equation: 5

$\frac{dT_y}{dx}\ =\ \frac{dT_y}{dt}\ \frac{dt}{dx}\ =\ \frac{V_t}{V_d}\ \sqrt{{y'}^2+1}~ ,$

## o8 equation: 6

$y''=\frac{V_t\ \sqrt{1+{y'}^2}}{V_d(A_x-x)}$

## o9 equation: 7

$\frac{y''}{\sqrt{1+{y'}^2}}=\frac{V_t}{V_d(A_x-x)} ~.$

## o10 equation: 8

$\sinh^{-1}(y')=B-\frac{V_t}{V_d}\ \ln(A_x-x)~,$

## o11 equation: 9

$B=\frac{V_t}{V_d}\ \ln(A_x)+\ln\left(y'(0)+\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}\right) .$

## o12 equation: 10

$y'= \frac{1}{2}\left[\left(y'(0)+\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}\right)\left(1-\frac{x}{A_x}\right)^{-\frac{V_t}{V_d}} +
\left(y'(0)-\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}\right)\left(1-\frac{x}{A_x}\right)^{\frac{V_t}{V_d}}\right]$ .

## o13 equation: 11

$y'(0)= \frac{A_y}{A_x}$ .

## o14 equation: 12

$y= C - \frac{A_x}{2}\left[
\frac{\left(y'(0)+\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}\right)\left(1-\frac{x}{A_x}\right) ^{1 - \frac{V_t}{V_d}} }{1-\frac{V_t}{V_d}} +

\right],$

## o15 equation: 13

\frac{y'(0)+\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}}{1-\frac{V_t}{V_d}} +
\frac{y'(0)-\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}}{1 + \frac{V_t}{V_d}}
\right]$.

## o16 equation: 14

## o18 equation: 16

$C = \frac{A_x}{4}\left(y'(0)-\sqrt{{y'(0)}^2+1}\right) .$
