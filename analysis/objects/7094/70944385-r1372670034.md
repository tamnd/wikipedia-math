# Olena Vaneeva

## o1 subject: Olena Vaneeva

Olena Oleksandrivna Vaneeva (; born 28 June 1982, Dnipro) is a Ukrainian mathematician, corresponding member of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine (since 2024) and leading researcher at the Department of Mathematical Physics of the Institute of Mathematics, National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine. Her major research interest is the group analysis of partial differential equations.
