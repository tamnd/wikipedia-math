# Christian Genest

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Christian Genest

Christian Genest (; born January 11, 1957, in Chicoutimi, Quebec) is a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at McGill University (Montréal, Canada), where he held a Canada Research Chair between 2011 and 2025. He is the author of numerous research papers in multivariate analysis, nonparametric statistics, extreme-value theory, and multiple-criteria decision analysis.
