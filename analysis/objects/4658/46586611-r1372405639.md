# Dmitry Gudkov (mathematician)

## o1 subject: Dmitry Gudkov

Dmitrii Andreevich Gudkov (, also spelled Dmitry;1918–1992) was a Soviet mathematician famous for his work on Hilbert's sixteenth problem and the related Gudkov's conjecture in algebraic geometry. He was a student of Aleksandr Andronov.
