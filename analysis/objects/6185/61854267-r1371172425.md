# George Pirie (mathematician)

## o1 subject: George Pirie

George Pirie (19 July 1843 – 21 August 1904) was a Scottish mathematician, mathematical scientist, and Reverend in the Church of Scotland. He was an expert in the field of dynamics and the approximation of $\pi$.
