# Dmitry Dolgopyat

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Dmitry Dolgopyat

Dmitry Ilyich Dolgopyat (; born November 1, 1972) is a Russian-American mathematician specializing in dynamical systems, a field that studies the time evolution of natural and abstract systems. An internationally acclaimed lecturer, he holds the position of Distinguished University Professor at the University of Maryland, and is a foreign member of the Academia Europaea.
