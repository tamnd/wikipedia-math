# ÉLECTRE

## o1 subject: ÉLECTRE

ÉLECTRE is a family of multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) methods that originated in Europe in the mid-1960s. The acronym ÉLECTRE stands for: ("Elimination and Choice Translating Reality").
