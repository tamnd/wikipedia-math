# Marinus of Tyre

## o1 subject: Marinus of Tyre

Marinus of Tyre (, Marînos ho Týrios; 70–130) was a Greek-speaking Phoenician Roman geographer, cartographer and mathematician, who founded mathematical geography and provided the underpinnings of Claudius Ptolemy's influential Geography.
