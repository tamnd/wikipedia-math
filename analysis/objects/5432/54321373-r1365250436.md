# Étale spectrum

## o1 subject: Étale spectrum

In mathematics, specifically algebraic geometry, the étale spectrum of a commutative ring or of an E∞-ring, denoted by Specét or Spét, is an analog of the prime spectrum Spec of a commutative ring that is obtained by replacing the Zariski topology with the étale topology.
