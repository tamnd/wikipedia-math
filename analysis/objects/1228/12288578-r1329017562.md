# Calderón–Zygmund lemma

## o1 subject: Calderón–Zygmund lemma

In mathematics, the Calderón–Zygmund lemma is a fundamental result in Fourier analysis, harmonic analysis, and singular integrals. It is named for the mathematicians Alberto Calderón and Antoni Zygmund.

## o2 section: Covering lemma

## o3 statement/lemma: Covering lemma

## Covering lemma
Let $f : \mathbf{R}^{d} \to \mathbf{C}$ be integrable and $\alpha$ be a positive constant. Then there exists an open set $\Omega$ such that:
:(1) $\Omega$ is a disjoint union of open cubes, $\Omega = \cup_{k} Q_{k}$, such that for each $Q_{k}$,
::$\alpha\le \frac{1}{m(Q_k)} \int_{Q_k} |f(x)| \, dx \leq 2^d \alpha.$
:(2) $|f(x)| \le \alpha$ almost everywhere in the complement $F$ of $\Omega$.
Here, $m(Q_k)$ denotes the measure of the set $Q_k$.
