# Strömgren sphere

## o1 subject: Strömgren sphere

The Strömgren sphere is an idealized model of ionized hydrogen shells around young OB stars or quasars. Though ideally symmetrical, common examples demonstrate asymmetry as a result of environmental factors like gas inhomogeneity, bow shocks, anisotropic stellar emission, or relativistic effects. The Strömgren sphere is named after Bengt Strömgren, who first described it in 1937. The Rosette Nebula is a prominent example of this type of structure.
