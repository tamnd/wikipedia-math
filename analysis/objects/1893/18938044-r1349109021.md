# Cryptoperiod

## o1 subject: Cryptoperiod

A cryptoperiod is the time span during which a specific cryptographic key is authorized for use. NIST's guidelines typically recommend a long-term cryptoperiod of 1 - 3 years for asymmetric keys
and ≤ 2 years for symmetric keys.
