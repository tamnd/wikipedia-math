# Cynthia Clark

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Cynthia Clark

Cynthia Zang Facer Clark (born April 1, 1942) is an American statistician known for her work improving the quality of data in the federal statistical system, and especially in the National Agricultural Statistics Service. She has also served as the president of the Caucus for Women in Statistics and the Washington Statistical Society. she is executive director of the Council of Professional Associations on Federal Statistics.
