# Adriaan Metius

## o1 subject: Adriaan Metius

Adriaan Adriaanszoon, called Adriaan Metius (9 December 15716 September 1635), was a Dutch geometer and astronomer, born in Alkmaar. The name "Metius" comes from the Dutch word ("measuring"), and therefore means something like "measurer" or "surveyor".
