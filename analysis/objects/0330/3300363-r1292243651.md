# Andreotti–Frankel theorem

## o1 subject: Andreotti–Frankel theorem

In mathematics, the Andreotti–Frankel theorem, introduced by , states that if $V$ is a smooth, complex affine variety of complex dimension $n$ or, more generally, if $V$ is any Stein manifold of dimension $n$, then
$V$ admits a Morse function with critical points of index at most n, and so $V$ is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex of real dimension at most n.
