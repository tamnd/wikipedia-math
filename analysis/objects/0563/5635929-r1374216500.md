# Tonnetz

## o1 subject: Tonnetz

In musical tuning and harmony, the (German for 'tone net') is a conceptual lattice diagram representing tonal space first described by Leonhard Euler in 1739. Various visual representations of the Tonnetz can be used to show traditional harmonic relationships in European classical music.
