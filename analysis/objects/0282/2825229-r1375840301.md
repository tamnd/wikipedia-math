# Nolot

## o1 subject: Nolot

Nolot is a chess test suite with 11 positions from real games. They were compiled by Pierre Nolot () for the French chess magazine Gambisco and posted on the rec.games.chess Usenet group in 1994. They were designed to be particularly hard for chess engines to solve at the time, although modern engines can find a solution near-instantaneously.
