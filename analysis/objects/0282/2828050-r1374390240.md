# Theodosius of Bithynia

## o1 subject: Theodosius of Bithynia

Theodosius of Bithynia ( grc; 2nd–1st century BC) was a Hellenistic astronomer and mathematician from Bithynia who wrote the Spherics, a treatise about spherical geometry, as well as several other books on mathematics and astronomy, of which two survive, On Habitations and On Days and Nights.
