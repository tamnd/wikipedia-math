# Uriel Rothblum

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Uriel Rothblum

Uriel George "Uri" Rothblum (; Tel Aviv, March 16, 1947 – Haifa, March 26, 2012) was an Israeli mathematician and operations researcher. From 1984 until 2012 he held the Alexander Goldberg Chair in Management Science at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology in Haifa, Israel.
