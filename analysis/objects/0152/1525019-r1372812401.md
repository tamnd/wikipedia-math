# Apostolos Doxiadis

## o1 infobox: infobox writer

## o2 subject: Apostolos Doxiadis

Apostolos Doxiadis (; ; born 1953) is a Greek writer. He is best known for his international bestsellers Uncle Petros and Goldbach's Conjecture (2000) and Logicomix (2009). Most of Doxiadis’ writing in recent decades has been fiction and essays, but he has also written for the theatre and composed librettos for musicals. In the past, he had also worked as a director in theatre and film.
