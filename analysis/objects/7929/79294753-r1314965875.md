# Ruth Heller (statistician)

## o1 subject: Ruth Heller

Ruth Heller () is an Israeli statistician and professor at Tel Aviv University. Heller focuses on diverse areas of statistics and biostatistics, including multiple comparisons, selective inference, conformal inference, and study reproducibility.
