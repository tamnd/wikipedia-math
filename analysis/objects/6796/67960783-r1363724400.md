# Sparse polynomial

## o1 subject: Sparse polynomial

In mathematics, a sparse polynomial (also lacunary polynomial or fewnomial) is a polynomial that has far fewer terms than its degree and number of variables would suggest. For example, $x^{10} + 3x^3 + 1$ is a sparse polynomial, as it is a trinomial with a degree of $10$.
