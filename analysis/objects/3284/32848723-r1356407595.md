## o1 subject: Q-Laguerre polynomials

In mathematics, the q-Laguerre polynomials, or generalized Stieltjes–Wigert polynomials P${}^{(\alpha)}_{n}$(x;q) are a family of basic hypergeometric orthogonal polynomials in the basic Askey scheme introduced by . give a detailed list of their properties.

## Definition
The q-Laguerre polynomials are given in terms of basic hypergeometric functions and the q-Pochhammer symbol by
$\displaystyle L_n^{(\alpha)}(x;q) = \frac{(q^{\alpha+1};q)_n}{(q;q)_n} {}_1\phi_1(q^{-n};q^{\alpha+1};q,-q^{n+\alpha+1}x).$
