# Randić index

## o1 subject: Randić index

The Randić index, also known as the connectivity index, of a graph is the sum of bond contributions $1/(d_i d_j)^{1/2}$ where $d_i$ and $d_j$ are
the degrees of the vertices making bond i ~ j.
