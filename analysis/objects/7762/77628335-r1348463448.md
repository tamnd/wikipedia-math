## o2 subject: Finnish Mathematical Society

The Finnish Mathematical Society or FMS (Finnish: , SMY) is a mathematical society founded in Finland in November 1868, making it one of the oldest in the world.
The FMS is based in Helsinki, and is a founding member of the European Mathematical Society. It is recognised by the International Mathematics Union.
