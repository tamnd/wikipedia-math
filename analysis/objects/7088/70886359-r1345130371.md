# Bentsion Fleishman

## o1 subject: Bentsion Fleishman

Bentsion Shimonovich Fleishman (, born 21 November 1923) is a Russian scientist in the field of mathematical statistics, combinatorial analysis and their applications, doctor of physical and mathematical sciences, professor, author of constructive information theory and the theory of potential efficiency.
