## o1 subject: Giovanni Antonio Lecchi

Giovanni Antonio Lecchi or Giannantonio Lecchi (Milan, 17 November 170224 July 1776) was an Italian Jesuit, mathematician, engineer and physicist. He lived and worked with success in Milan rising to a notable level of prominence.
