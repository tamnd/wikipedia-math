# Jane-Ling Wang

## o1 subject: Jane-Ling Wang

Jane-Ling Wang () is a Taiwanese-American statistician who is a distinguished professor of statistics at the University of California, Davis. She is a specialist in dimension reduction, functional data analysis, and aging.
