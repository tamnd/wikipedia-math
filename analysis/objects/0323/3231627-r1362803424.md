# Lagrangian Grassmannian

## o1 subject: Lagrangian Grassmannian

In mathematics, the Lagrangian Grassmannian is the smooth manifold of Lagrangian subspaces of a real symplectic vector space V. Its dimension is $\tfrac{1}{2}$n(n + 1) (where the dimension of V is 2n). It may be identified with the homogeneous space
