# Hilary Shuard

## o1 subject: Hilary Shuard

Hilary Bertha Shuard CBE (14 November 192824 December 1992) was an expert on the teaching of mathematics in primary schools. She was a member of the Cockcroft Committee, and Deputy Principal of Homerton College, Cambridge for twenty years.
