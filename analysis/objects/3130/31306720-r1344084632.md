# Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy

## o1 subject: Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy

The Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy is an endowed professorship established at Harvard College in 1727 by Thomas Hollis. The chair, now part of the Physics Department, is the second oldest at Harvard, and the oldest professorship in science in the United States.
