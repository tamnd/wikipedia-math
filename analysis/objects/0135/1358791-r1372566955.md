# Nicolae Popescu

## o1 subject: Nicolae Popescu

Nicolae Popescu (; 22 September 1937 – 29 July 2010) was a Romanian mathematician and professor at the University of Bucharest. He also held a research position at the Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy, and was elected a corresponding Member of the Romanian Academy in 1997.
