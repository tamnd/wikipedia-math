# Demographics of Asia

## o1 subject: Demographics of Asia

The continent of Asia covers 29.4% of the Earth's land area and has a population of around 4.87 billion (), accounting for about 60% of the world population. The combined population of both China and India is estimated to be over 2.8 billion people .
Asia's population is projected to grow to 5.25 billion by 2055, or about 54% of projected world population at that time. Population growth in Asia was close to 0.55% p.a. , with highly disparate rates. Many Western Asian and South Asian countries have growth rates far above the world average, notably Pakistan at 2% p.a., and India with a 0.6% increase in 2022. On the other hand, East Asian nations are in the middle of a demographic stagnation, with China experiencing a small decrease of –0.06%, after years of rapidly slowing growth.
