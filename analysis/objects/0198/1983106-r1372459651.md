# Franciszek Leja

## o1 subject: Franciszek Leja

Franciszek Leja (Polish: f; 27 January 1885 in Grodzisko Górne near Leżajsk – 11 October 1979 in Kraków, Poland) was a Polish mathematician known for his work in analytic functions and topology. He was one of the leading representatives of the Kraków School of Mathematics.
