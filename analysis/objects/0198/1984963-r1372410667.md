# Gelfond's constant

## o1 subject: Gelfond's constant

In mathematics, the exponential of pi $e^{\pi}$, also called Gelfond's constant, is the real number $e$ raised to the power $\pi$ (i.e., the value of the exponential function at $\pi$).
