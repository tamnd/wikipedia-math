## o2 subject: Stacey Smith?

Stacey R. Smith? (formerly Robert Joseph Smith?, born 28 October 1972) is an Australian-Canadian mathematician known for scholarly research work on the modeling of infectious outbreaks, particularly including the analysis of outbreaks of zombies and Bieber Fever (a phenomenon characterized by extreme fandom for the Canadian singer Justin Bieber). She has also published scholarly books on science fiction.
