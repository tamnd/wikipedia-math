# Chiara Saffirio

## o1 subject: Chiara Saffirio

Chiara Saffirio (born 9 September 1985 in Rome, Italy) is an Italian mathematician working in kinetic theory and classical and quantum systems. She has been Full Professor of Mathematics at University of Freiburg since 2025.
