# Clustered planarity

## o1 subject: Clustered planarity

In graph drawing, a clustered planar graph is a graph together with a hierarchical clustering on its vertices, such that the graph can be drawn together with a collection of simple closed curves surrounding each cluster with no crossings between graph edges or clusters.
