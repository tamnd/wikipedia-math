# Wei-Ming Ni

## o1 infobox: infobox scientist

## o2 subject: Wei-Ming Ni

Wei-Ming Ni (; born 23 December 1950) is a Taiwanese mathematician. He is a Presidential Chair Professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen and a professor emeritus at the University of Minnesota. He was previously the director of the Center for PDE at the East China Normal University.
