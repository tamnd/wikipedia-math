# The Aleph (short story)

## o1 infobox: infobox short story

## o2 subject: The Aleph

"The Aleph" () is a short story by Argentine writer and poet Jorge Luis Borges. First published in September 1945, it was reprinted in the short story collection The Aleph and Other Stories in 1949, and revised by the author in 1974.
