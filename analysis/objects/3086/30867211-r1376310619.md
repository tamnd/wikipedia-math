# Demetra+

## o1 subject: Demetra+

JDemetra+ is a computer program for seasonal adjustments that was developed by the National Bank of Belgium in collaboration with the Deutsche Bundesbank, Insee and Eurostat – European Commission in accordance with the ESS Guidelines on seasonal adjustment. It supports TRAMO&SEATS and X-12-ARIMA methods of adjustment.

## o9 statement/generalization: Extensions

### Extensions
The Demetra+ allows developers to write implementation for:
- time series providers and browsers,
- repository for the definitions of SA processing,
- storage (or further processing) of the results,
- diagnostics on the SA estimations,
- summary (reporting) of a complete SA processing,
- data formatting (drag/drop and copy/paste).
