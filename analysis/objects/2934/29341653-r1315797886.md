# Panconnectivity

## o1 subject: Panconnectivity

In graph theory, a panconnected graph is an undirected graph in which, for every two vertices $s$ and $t$, there exist paths from $s$ to $t$ of every possible length from the distance $d(s,t)$ up to $n - 1$, where $n$ is the number of vertices in the graph. The concept of panconnectivity was introduced in 1975 by Yousef Alavi and James E. Williamson.
