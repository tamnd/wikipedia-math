# Sum of angles of a triangle

## o1 subject: Sum of angles of a triangle

In a Euclidean space, the sum of angles of a triangle equals a straight angle (180 degrees, $\pi$ radians, two right angles, or a half-turn). A triangle has three angles, and has one at each vertex, bounded by a pair of adjacent sides.
