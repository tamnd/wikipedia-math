# Right half-plane

## o1 subject: Right half-plane

In complex analysis, the (open) right half-plane is the set of all points in the complex plane whose real part is strictly positive, that is, the set $\{z \in \mathbb{C}\, :\, \mbox{Re}(z) > 0\}$.
