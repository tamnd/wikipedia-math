# Triebel–Lizorkin space

## o1 subject: Triebel–Lizorkin space

In the mathematical discipline known as functional analysis, a Triebel–Lizorkin space is a generalization of many standard function spaces such as Lp spaces and Sobolev spaces. It is named after Hans Triebel (born February 7th 1936 in Dessau) and Petr Ivanovich Lizorkin.
