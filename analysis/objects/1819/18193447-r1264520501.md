# Kirik the Novgorodian

## o1 subject: Kirik the Novgorodian

Kirik the Novgorodian (; 1110 – after 1158) was a monk of the Antoniev Monastery and later a hieromonk in the entourage of Archbishop Niphont of Novgorod (r. 1130–1156) famous for writing the first mathematical treatise in Eastern Slavdom, the Uchenie o chislakh (, "Teaching on Numbers"); he also wrote entries in the Novgorod First Chronicle in the 1140s and asked some of the 152 questions of Niphont in a theological work known as the Voproshanie Kirika (, "The Questions of Kirik"). He also translated the works of Patriarch Nikephoros I of Constantinople as well as the Pentateuch.
