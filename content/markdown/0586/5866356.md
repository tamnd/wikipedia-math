# Cyril Offord

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyril Offord"}

*Image: Cyril Offord.jpg.* Cyril Offord, c. 1970

**Albert Cyril Offord** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o2] [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (9 June 1906 – 4 June 2000) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was the first professor of mathematics at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) on 9 June 1906 the eldest child of Albert Edwin Offord, a master printer, and his wife Hester Louise, a former opera singer. The family were [Plymouth Brethren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Brethren). He was educated at [Hackney Downs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackney_Downs) Grammar School. He then studied Mathematics at [University College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_London). He then went to [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge) as a postgraduate, working with Prof [John Edensor Littlewood](wikipedia://en/158371).[^o4]

He received two Ph.D.s in mathematics: the first from the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) (under [Bosanquet](wikipedia://en/35221526)) in 1932, the second from [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (under [Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599)) in 1936.

In 1940 he left Cambridge to lecture at [University College, Bangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Bangor). In 1942 he moved to King's College, [Newcastle-upon-Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle-upon-Tyne) (later being named the University of Newcastle). He was created Professor of Mathematics in 1945.

In 1946 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were Sir [Edmund Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Whittaker), [John William Heslop-Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_William_Heslop-Harrison), [Alexander Aitken](wikipedia://en/3348690) and [Alfred Dennis Hobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Dennis_Hobson). He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_London) in 1952.[^o6]

In 1948 he left Newcastle to become Professor of Mathematics at [Birkbeck College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkbeck_College) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) replacing Prof Dienes.[^o7] He left in 1966 to take up a new chair at [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics). He retired in 1973 then becoming a senior research fellow at [Imperial College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College,_London).[^o4]

He died in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford) on 4 June 2000.

## Family {#Family}

In 1945 he married Margaret Yvonne Pickard (generally known as Rita), an English teacher. They had one daughter, Margaret Offord (born 1949).
