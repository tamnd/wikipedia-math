# Miles Reid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miles Reid"}

**Miles Anthony Reid** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (born 30 January 1948) is a mathematician who works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Reid studied the [Cambridge Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_Mathematical_Tripos) at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) and obtained his Ph.D. in 1973 under the supervision of [Peter Swinnerton-Dyer](wikipedia://en/2105540) and [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544).

## Career {#Career}

Reid was a research fellow of [Christ's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_College,_Cambridge) from 1973 to 1978. He became a lecturer at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) in 1978 and was appointed professor there in 1992. He has written two well known books: *Undergraduate Algebraic Geometry* and *Undergraduate Commutative Algebra*.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Reid was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2002. In the same year, he participated as an Invited Speaker in the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). Reid was awarded the [Senior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize) in 2006 for his paper with [Alessio Corti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessio_Corti) and Aleksandr Pukhlikov, "Fano 3-fold hypersurfaces", which made a big advance in the study of 3-dimensional [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808).[^o8] In 2023 he was awarded the [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal) of the Royal Society.[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Reid speaks [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) and has given lectures in Japanese.
