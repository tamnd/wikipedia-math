# Fritz Peter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz Peter"}

**Fritz Peter** (1899–1949) was a German mathematician who helped prove the [Peter–Weyl theorem](wikipedia://en/294474). He was a student of [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544), and later became headmaster of a secondary school (Hawkins 2000, p.502).

:::
