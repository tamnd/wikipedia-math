# Sketch (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sketch"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) theory of [categories](wikipedia://en/53759), a **sketch** is a category *D*, together with a set of [cones](wikipedia://en/7533645) intended to be limits and a set of cocones intended to be colimits. A **model** of the sketch in a category *C* is a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987)

:::

$$M:D\rightarrow C$$

that takes each specified cone to a limit cone in *C* and each specified cocone to a colimit cocone in *C*. Morphisms of models are [natural transformations](wikipedia://en/45022). Sketches are a general way of specifying structures on the objects of a category, forming a category-theoretic analog to the logical concept of a [theory](wikipedia://en/3512103) and its [models](wikipedia://en/19858). They allow multisorted models and models in any category.

Sketches were invented in 1968 by [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108), using a different but equivalent definition. There are still other definitions in the research literature.
