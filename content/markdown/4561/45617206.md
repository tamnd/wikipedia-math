# Ruel Vance Churchill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruel Vance Churchill"}

**Ruel Vance Churchill** (12 December 1899 – 31 October 1987) was an American mathematician and author known for writing three widely used textbooks on [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o2]

:::

Churchill was born in [Akron, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akron,_Indiana) in 1899, and in 1922 he received his undergraduate degree from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). In 1929 he received his PhD from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) under [George Rainich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Yuri_Rainich) with thesis *On the Geometry of the Riemann Tensor*. He spent his entire career as a member of the U. of Michigan mathematics faculty and retired in 1965 as professor emeritus.[^o4] His doctoral students include [Earl D. Rainville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_D._Rainville).

Churchill died in [Ann Arbor, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor,_Michigan) in 1987.

## Books {#Books}

- Complex Variables and Applications, McGraw-Hill, 1st edition 1948, 2nd edition 1960, The 3rd (1974) and later editions were co-authored with James Ward Brown
- Fourier Series and Boundary Value Problems, McGraw-Hill, 1941, 2nd edition 1963[^o6]
- Modern Operational Mathematics in Engineering, McGraw-Hill, 1944[^o7]
- Operational Mathematics, McGraw-Hill, 1958, 2nd edition of the 1944 book but with a new title, 3rd edition 1972
