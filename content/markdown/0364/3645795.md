The **[compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound) of five [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606)**, also known as the chiricosahedron, is one of the five regular polyhedral compounds. This [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) is also a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the regular [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084). It was first described by [Edmund Hess](wikipedia://en/9191575) in 1876.

:::

It can be seen as a faceting of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361).

## As a compound {#As_a_compound}

It can be constructed by arranging five [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) in [rotational icosahedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational_icosahedral_symmetry) (**I**), as colored in the upper right model. It is one of [five regular compounds](wikipedia://en/23901#Regular_compounds) which can be constructed from identical [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905).[^n2]

It shares the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361).

There are two [enantiomorphous](wikipedia://en/1170160) forms (the same figure but having opposite chirality) of this compound polyhedron. Both forms together create the reflection symmetric [compound of ten tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/8010465).

It has a [density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density_%28polyhedra%29) of higher than 1.

## Group theory {#Group_theory}

The compound of five tetrahedra is a geometric illustration of the notion of [orbits and stabilizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29#orbstab), as follows.

The symmetry group of the compound is the (rotational) [icosahedral group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icosahedral_group) *I* of order 60, while the stabilizer of a single chosen tetrahedron is the (rotational) [tetrahedral group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedral_group) *T* of order 12, and the orbit space *I*/*T* (of order 60/12 = 5) is naturally identified with the 5 tetrahedra – the coset *gT* corresponds to which tetrahedron *g* sends the chosen tetrahedron to.

## An unusual dual property {#An_unusual_dual_property}

*Image: Second compound stellation of icosahedron.svg.* Compound of five tetrahedra

This compound is unusual, in that the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) figure is the [enantiomorph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomorph) of the original. If the faces are twisted to the right, then the vertices are twisted to the left. When we [dualise](wikipedia://en/8815), the faces dualise to right-twisted vertices and the vertices dualise to left-twisted faces, giving the chiral twin. Figures with this property are extremely rare.

## In 4-dimensional space {#In_4-dimensional_space}

The compound of five tetrahedra is related to the regular [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821), the 4-simplex [regular 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/18801624), which is also composed of 5 regular tetrahedra. In the 5-cell the tetrahedra are bonded face-to-face such that each triangular face is shared by two tetrahedral cells.

The compound of five tetrahedra occurs embedded in [4-dimensional space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-dimensional_space), inscribed in the 120 dodecahedral cells of the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358). The 120-cell is the [largest and most comprehensive regular 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/717358#Geometry); the regular 5-cell is the smallest and most elemental. The 120-cell contains inscribed within itself instances of every other regular 4-polytope.[^n4] In each of the 120-cell's dodecahedral cells, there are two inscribed instances of the compound of 5 tetrahedra (in other words, one instance of the [compound of ten tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/8010465)). The 5 tetrahedra of each compound of five occur as cells of another regular 4-polytope inscribed within the 120-cell, the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), which has 600 regular tetrahedra as its cells. The 120-cell is a compound of 5 disjoint 600-cells, and each of its dodecahedral cells is a [compound of 5 tetrahedral cells](wikipedia://en/717358#Cell_rotations_of_inscribed_duals), one cell from each of the 5 disjoint 600-cells.
