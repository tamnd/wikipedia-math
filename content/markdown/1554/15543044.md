# Parovicenko space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parovicenko space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Parovicenko space** is a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) similar to the space of non-[isolated points](wikipedia://en/390557) of the [Stone–Čech compactification](wikipedia://en/52897) of the [integers](wikipedia://en/14563).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Parovicenko space is a topological space *X* satisfying the following conditions:

- *X* is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637)
- *X* has no isolated points
- *X* has [weight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_of_a_topological_space) *c*, the [cardinality of the continuum](wikipedia://en/1574901) (this is the smallest cardinality of a [base](wikipedia://en/59220) for the [topology](wikipedia://en/30450)).
- Every two disjoint open [*F*<sub>σ</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-sigma_set) subsets of *X* have [disjoint](wikipedia://en/39359) [closures](wikipedia://en/42309)
- Every [non-empty](wikipedia://en/9566) [*G*<sub>δ</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-delta_set) of *X* has non-empty [interior](wikipedia://en/55610).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The space β**N**\\**N** is a Parovicenko space, where β**N** is the [Stone–Čech compactification](wikipedia://en/52897) of the [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) **N**. (Parovicenko 1963) proved that the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705) implies that every Parovicenko space is isomorphic to β**N**\\**N**. (van Douwen van Mill 1978) showed that if the continuum hypothesis is false then there are other examples of Parovicenko spaces.

:::
