# Dick de Jongh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dick de Jongh"}

de Jongh **Dick Herman Jacobus de Jongh** (born 19 October 1939, [Enschede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enschede))[^o3] is a Dutch [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor emeritus at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). He received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in 1968 from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) under supervision of [Stephen Kleene](wikipedia://en/28805) with a dissertation titled *Investigations on the Intuitionistic Propositional Calculus*.[^o4] De Jongh is mostly known for his work on [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478), [provability logic](wikipedia://en/618119) and [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262). De Jongh is a member of the group collectively publishing under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) [L. T. F. Gamut](wikipedia://en/1091808).[^o5] In

:::

2004, on the occasion of his retirement, the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) at the University of Amsterdam published a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in his honor.[^o6]
