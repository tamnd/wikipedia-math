# Martin Stokhof

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Stokhof"}

**Martin Stokhof** (born 1950, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam)) is a Dutch [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy). Stokhof wrote a joint [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) dissertation with [Jeroen Groenendijk](wikipedia://en/15752422) on the [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107) of questions, under the supervision of [Renate Bartsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Bartsch) and [Johan van Benthem](wikipedia://en/1498176). He was also an important figure in the development of [dynamic semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_semantics) (together with Groenendijk, Veltman and others, following work by [Irene Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Heim) and Kamp). He is also known for his work on [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741).

:::

He is a former director of the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) and a member of the group collectively publishing under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) [L. T. F. Gamut](wikipedia://en/1091808).[^o4]

In 2006 he was elected member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5]
