# Saralees Nadarajah

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Saralees Nadarajah"}

**Saralees Nadarajah** is a British statistician and researcher specialising in distribution theory, [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732), [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), and their applications. He is also a professor at the [Department of Mathematics, University of Manchester](wikipedia://en/10387568) and has worked at the Department of Statistics at the [University of Nebraska-Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) and the [University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Nadarajah was born in [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka) and grew up in [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe). He obtained his B.Sc. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zimbabwe) in [Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harare). Nadarajah earned his M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) from the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield) in the UK, in 1991 and 1994 respectively.[^o4][^o5]

## Career {#Career}

As of April 2025, Nadarajah is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28academic_rank%29) at the [Department of Mathematics, University of Manchester](wikipedia://en/10387568).[^o7] He was previously affiliated with the Department of Statistics, [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) and the [University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida).[^o8][^o9][^o10]

Nadarajah is the founder of Educate Africa project which he started in 2017.[^o11]

## Research {#Research}

Nadarajah specializes in statistical fields including extreme value and [distribution theory](wikipedia://en/36785973), [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773), and reliability.[^o13][^o14] His expertise also extends to [sampling theory](wikipedia://en/160361), statistical software development, and [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) analysis.[^o13] Nadarajah-Haghighi (UNH) class of distribution is named after him.[^o15]
