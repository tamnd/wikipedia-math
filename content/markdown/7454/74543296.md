# Robert I. Jewett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert I. Jewett"}

**Robert Israel "Bob"[^o3] Jewett** (December 14, 1937 – July 30, 2022)[^o4] was an American mathematician and one of the namesakes of [Hales–Jewett theorem](wikipedia://en/640714). He researched harmonic analysis, ergodic theory, differential equations, and combinatorics.[^o5]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

After graduating from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and obtaining his PhD in mathematics from the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), he taught as a professor of mathematics at [Western Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Washington_University) for 40 years until he eventually retired in 2010.[^o5]
