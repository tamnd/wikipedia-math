# Q-RASAR

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-RASAR"}

The **quantitative Read-Across Structure-Activity Relationship (q-RASAR)** concept has been developed by the [DTC Laboratory, Jadavpur University](https://sites.google.com/site/kunalroyindia/home/the-dtc-laboratory?authuser=0) by merging Read-Across and [QSAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QSAR). It is a [statistical modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_modeling) approach that uses the similarity and error-based measures as descriptors in addition to the usual structural and physicochemical descriptors, and it has been shown to enhance the external predictivity of QSAR/QSPR models.[^o2]

:::

The novel quantitative read-across structure-activity relationship (q-RASAR) approach combines the advantages of both QSAR and read-across, thus resulting in enhanced predictivity for the same level of chemical information used. This approach utilizes similarity-based considerations yet can generate simple, interpretable, and transferable models. This approach may be used for any type of structural and [physicochemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicochemical) descriptors and with any modeling algorithms.

[The q-RASAR approach](https://sites.google.com/site/kunalroyindia/home/rasar?authuser=0) has been used by different research groups for different endpoints.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] Among different RASAR descriptors, *RA function*, *Average Similarity* and *gm* (Banerjee-Roy concordance coefficient) have shown high importance in modeling in some studies.[^o6] In 2023, Banerjee-Roy similarity coefficients *sm*1 and *sm2* have also been proposed to identify potential activity cliffs in a data set.[^o7] The q-RASAR approach has the potential in data gap filling in predictive [toxicology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toxicology), [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science), [medicinal chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicinal_chemistry), [food sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_science), [nano-sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanotechnology), [agricultural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_sciences), etc.

A [tutorial presentation](https://www.way2drug.com/dr/bcadd2023/content/presentations/video/BCADD-2023-Roy_Kunal.mp4) on q-RASAR is available. Recently, the q-RASAR framework has been improved by its integration with the [ARKA descriptors in QSAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARKA_descriptors_in_QSAR).[^o8]
