# Monique Combescure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monique Combescure"}

**Monique Combescure** (born 23 April 1950, née Moulin), is a French [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) specializing in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). In 2001, she became director of research at the [Lyon Institute of Nuclear Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_national_de_physique_nucléaire_et_de_physique_des_particules).[^o3][^o4] From 2000 to 2008, she was director of the European Mathematics and Quantum Physics Research Group (GDRE MPhiQ) which aims to promote synergy between [theoretical physicists](wikipedia://en/19594028) and [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) in the field of [quantum physics](wikipedia://en/25202). She received the [Irène-Joliot-Curie Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize) in 2007 and the rank of Officer of the [National Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_national_du_Mérite) in 2011.[^o4]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

In 1970, Combescure joined the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris and decided to devote herself to research in [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028). She defended her thesis at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 1974 on the problem of 3-body quantum scattering under the supervision of [Jean Ginibre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Ginibre). She was then assigned to the theoretical and [high-energy physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics) laboratory at [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay). In 1979 she obtained her doctorate on many-body spectral and scattering problems in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) and [field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). She is a member of the scientific council of the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University), in particular in the third cycle pedagogy commission (theses and authorizations to direct research).[^o4]

She has been elected to many research administration bodies, in particular to the Scientific Council of the Mathematics Physics Particles Universe department of the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS).[^o4]

As the Director of the GDRE MPhiQ [European Research Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Group) titled *Mathematics and Quantum Physics*, she brought together 150 researchers and teacher-researchers working in mathematics or theoretical physics laboratories to explore topical subjects posed by quantum mechanics and field theory.[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

Combescure has received several distinctions.[^o4]

- 1997: Ernest Dechelle Prize from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)
- 2007: [Irène-Joliot-Curie prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize) for woman scientist of the year
- 2008: Chevalier of the [Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour)
- 2011: Officer of the [National Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_national_du_Mérite)
