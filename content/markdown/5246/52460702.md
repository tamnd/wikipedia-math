# Xinwen Zhu

Zhu Xinwen

*Image: Xinwen Zhu (Oberwolfach 2015).jpg.* Zhu in 2015

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xinwen Zhu"}

**Xinwen Zhu** (; born 1982 in [Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan)) is a Chinese mathematician and professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). His work deals primarily with geometric representation theory and in particular the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905), tying [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [quantum physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_physics).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Zhu obtained his A.B. in mathematics from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2004 and his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 2009 under the direction of [Edward Frenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Frenkel).[^o2] He taught at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a Benjamin Peirce Lecturer and at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) as an assistant professor before joining the Caltech faculty in 2014. According to the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "\[Zhu\] studies the geometry and topology of flag varieties of loop groups and applies techniques from the geometric Langlands program to arithmetic geometry."[^o5]

The awards Zhu has received include an [AMS Centennial Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMS_Centennial_Fellowship) in 2013 and a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 2015.[^o6] His research has been published in [Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785) and [Inventiones mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_mathematicae), among other mathematics journals. Zhu, [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911), [Xinyi Yuan](wikipedia://en/52461029) and [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005) are frequent collaborators.[^o7] In 2019 he received the [Morningside Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) jointly with [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005).[^o8] Zhu won the 2020 New Horizons in Mathematics [Breakthrough Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_Prize) "For work in arithmetic algebraic geometry including applications to the theory of Shimura varieties and the Riemann-Hilbert problem for p-adic varieties."
