# Xinyi Yuan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xinyi Yuan"}

Yuan Xinyi

**Xinyi Yuan** (; born 1981) is a Chinese mathematician who is currently a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146).[^o3] In particular, his work focuses on arithmetic intersection theory, [algebraic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_dynamics), [Diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109) and special values of [L-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-functions).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Yuan is from [Macheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macheng), [Huanggang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huanggang), [Hubei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubei) province, and graduated from [Huanggang Middle School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huanggang_Middle_School) in 2000.[^o5] That year, he received a gold medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) while representing [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).[^o6] Yuan obtained his A.B. in mathematics from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2003 and his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2008 under the direction of [Shou-Wu Zhang](wikipedia://en/3582724).[^o7] His article "Big Line Bundles over Arithmetic Varieties," published in [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae), demonstrates a natural sufficient condition for when the [orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29#Orbits_and_stabilizers) under the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) is equidistributed.[^n1]

## Career {#Career}

He spent time at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) before joining the [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) faculty in 2012.[^o10]

Yuan was appointed a [Clay Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Research_Fellow) for a three-year term from 2008 to 2013.[^o11] Together with a number of other collaborators, Yuan was profiled in [Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976) and [Business Insider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Insider) for, among other things, his research on L-functions.[^o12][^o13]

Yuan left UC Berkeley to become a full professor at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2020.[^o14] In 2025, Yuan was award the ICCM Gold medal at the [International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/45275469).[^o15] He is also an invited speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o16]

## Research {#Research}

In his thesis, Yuan generalized the equidistribution theorem of [Lucien Szpiro](wikipedia://en/26992384), [Emmanuel Ullmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Ullmo) and Shou-Wu Zhang to the broader framework of adelic line bundles and in particular to algebraic dynamical systems.[^o18] Together with Shou-Wu Zhang, Yuan proved the [averaged Colmez conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colmez_conjecture) which was later shown to imply the [André–Oort conjecture](wikipedia://en/32262980) for [Siegel modular varieties](wikipedia://en/7721927) by [Jacob Tsimerman](wikipedia://en/48497888).[^o19][^n2]

With Shou-Wu Zhang, Yuan wrote a monograph generalizing the theory of adelic line bundles to quasi-projective varieties.[^o21] Yuan subsequently applied these ideas to reprove the uniform [Bogomolov conjecture](wikipedia://en/18992933) for curves, thereby obtaining a new proof of [Mazur's Conjecture B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazur's_Conjecture_B).[^o22] Later, in collaboration with Jiawei Yu and Shengxuan Zhou, he derived the first explicit uniform estimate for the number of rational points on curves of genus at least two in terms only of the Jacobian rank.[^o23]

Yuan has also made major contributions to Diophantine geometry over function fields through joint work with Junyi Xie. Their results include proofs of the geometric Bogomolov conjecture in arbitrary characteristic [^o24] and the geometric Bombieri-Lang conjecture for ramified covers of abelian varieties.[^o25]
