# Urszula Alicja Foryś

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Urszula Alicja Foryś"}

**Urszula Alicja Foryś** (born 1965 in Warsaw, Poland) is a Polish mathematician specializing in applied mathematics, mathematical biology, and biomedical engineering. She is a professor at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) and is known for her research on mathematical modeling of cancer therapy and biological dynamical systems. Foryś has supervised numerous doctoral students, including [Jan Poleszczuk](wikipedia://en/65373434), and is a laureate of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791)'s Hugo Steinhaus Award (2025). She is also an accomplished powerlifting athlete.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Foryś attended Primary School No. 1 and Jan Śniadecki High School XXX in Warsaw. In 1989 she received a Master of Science degree with honours in applied mathematics from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw)'s Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics (MIM), under the supervision of [Wiesław Szlenk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesław_Szlenk). She completed her Ph.D. in 1996 with the dissertation *Global analysis of the Marczuk model for the immune organism and the immunodeficient organism*, supervised by Szlenk and, following his death, by Andrzej Palczewski.[^o4]

In 2007, she received her habilitation (D.Sc.) in biocybernetics and biomedical engineering from the Nałęcz Institute of Biocybernetics and Biomedical Engineering, [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences), for the dissertation *Mathematical modelling of tumour development taking into account various stages of tumour growth*. On 20 June 2018, the President of the Republic of Poland conferred on her the title of professor of mathematical sciences.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Since 1989 Foryś has worked at the Institute of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics (MIM), [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), where she advanced from junior assistant to full professor (2019). She currently leads the Division of Biomathematics and Game Theory, which she helped establish in 2013.[^o7]

She has held visiting positions at the [China University of Geosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_University_of_Geosciences_%28Wuhan%29) in Wuhan (2011–2014) and has collaborated with numerous research centers, including the [Autonomous University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Barcelona), the Institute for Medical Biomathematics in [Bene Atarot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bene_Atarot), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), and the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) in Scotland. She was also a member of the Cooperation Group "Multiscale Modeling of Tumor Initiation, Growth and Progression" at the Center for Interdisciplinary Research (ZiF) of [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University).[^o8]

In 2019–2020 she carried out research at the Institute for Medical Biomathematics (Israel) under the Bekker Programme of the [Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_National_Agency_for_Academic_Exchange) (NAWA).[^o9]

## Research {#Research}

Foryś's research focuses on applications of finite- and infinite-dimensional dynamical systems to biomedicine, ecology, and social sciences. Her scientific output includes about 100 research papers indexed in [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683) and [zbMATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZbMATH), as well as educational and outreach publications. She is the author of the textbook *Matematyka w biologii* (*Mathematics in Biology*, WNT, 2005),[^o11] and the co-translator (with [Marek Bodnar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marek_Bodnar)) of James D. Murray's *Mathematical Biology I: An Introduction* into Polish (PWN, 2006).[^o12]

She has collaborated with international teams on cancer therapy modeling, including studies on CAR-T cell therapy for glioblastoma[^o13] and hormone-sensitive prostate cancer treatment.[^o14]

Her doctoral students include [Jan Poleszczuk (2014,2015)](wikipedia://en/65373434), Piotr Bajger (2021), and Marcin Choinski(2022).[^o15]

## Professional and community service {#Professional_and_community_service}

Foryś is a founding member of the [European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology](https://www.esmtb.org/) (ESMTB)[^o17] and participated in the first European Conference on Mathematics Applied to Biology and Medicine (L'Alpe d'Huez, 1991). She has served in leadership roles within the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791) (PTM), including as president of its Warsaw Branch (2019–2023) and vice-president (2023–2025). She is also an editor of *Mathematica Applicanda* and organized the exhibition \*On Mathematics and Mathematicians\* in the Senate of Poland (2019).[^o18]

She co-founded and serves as vice-president of the [Polish Women in Mathematics Association](https://pwm.ptm.org.pl/)(PTKwM) since 2016,[^o19] and since 2024 has been a member of the Commission on Mathematical Applications of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences). She also chairs the Scientific Committee of the National Conference on Mathematical Applications in Biology and Medicine (KKZMBM).[^o20]

## Sports career {#Sports_career}

Outside academia, Foryś is an experienced powerlifter, representing TKKF Herkules Warszawa. She is a multiple Polish champion, European Masters II Champion (2015), and World Vice-Champion Masters III (2025).[^o22]
