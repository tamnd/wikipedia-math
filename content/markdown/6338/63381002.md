# David Nadler (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Nadler"}

**David Erie Nadler** (born 1973) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in geometric [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298). He is currently a professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nadler graduated from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematics in 1996. He completed his doctoral studies at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121), earning a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mathematics in 2001. He worked as an instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) for several years before taking a tenure track position at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 2005, where he became a Full Professor in 2011. He moved to his current position at the University of California at Berkeley in 2012.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2007 Nadler was selected as a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow), and in 2013 he became a member of the inaugural class of [Fellows of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822#Fellows).

Nadler delivered the [Arf Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arf_Lecture) in 2012.
