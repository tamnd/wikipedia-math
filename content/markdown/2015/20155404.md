# Carl Theodor Anger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Theodor Anger"}

**Carl Theodor Anger** ([Danzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danzig), 31 July 1803 – Danzig, 25 March 1858) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).[^o2] He was a student of and assistant to [Friedrich Bessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Bessel) at the [Königsberg Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg_Observatory) from 1827 until 1831.[^o3] Thereafter, he was appointed as astronomer by the [Naturforschende Gesellschaft in Danzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danzig_Research_Society).[^o4]

:::

Besides his scientific work, especially that related to [Bessel functions](wikipedia://en/4700), he is also known for his first-hand biographical notes on the life of Bessel.

## Publications {#Publications}

- Anger, C. T. (1847). "Grundzüge der neueren astronomische Beobachtungskunst". Gernhard
- Anger, C. T. (1862). "Populäre Vorträge über Astronomie". Kafemann
