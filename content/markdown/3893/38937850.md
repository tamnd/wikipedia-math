# Chebotarev theorem on roots of unity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chebotarev theorem on roots of unity"}

The **Chebotarev theorem on roots of unity** was originally a conjecture made by [Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091) in the context of [lacunary series](wikipedia://en/8969021).

:::

[Chebotarev](wikipedia://en/3033696) was the first to prove it, in the 1930s. This proof involves tools from [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/457064) and pleased [Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091), who made comments arguing that it "*does meet the requirements of mathematical esthetics*".[^o2] Several proofs have been proposed since,[^o3] and it has even been discovered independently by [Dieudonné](wikipedia://en/1070084).[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $\Omega$ be a matrix with entries $a_{ij} =\omega^{ij},1\leq i,j\leq n$, where $\omega =e^{2\mathrm i\pi / n},n\in \mathbb{N}$. If $n$ is prime then any minor of $\Omega$ is non-zero.

Equivalently, all [submatrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submatrix) of a [DFT matrix](wikipedia://en/1090930) of prime length are invertible.

:::

::: {#o8 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

In [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324),[^o9] the theorem was used by [T. Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) to extend the [uncertainty principle](wikipedia://en/31883#Signal_processing).[^o10]

:::
