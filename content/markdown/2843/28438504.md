# Diana Shelstad

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diana Shelstad"}

**Diana Frost Shelstad** (born August 19, 1947 in [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney))[^o3] is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for her work in [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms). She is a professor at [Rutgers University–Newark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University–Newark).[^o4] She earned her doctorate at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1974 studying real reductive algebraic groups.

:::

## Research {#Research}

Shelstad has been a key player in the development of the theory of [endoscopy](wikipedia://en/20546343) which is part of [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).[^o7] She co-conjectured the [fundamental lemma](wikipedia://en/25431009) with [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986) in 1984. After over 20 years, this conjecture was solved by [Ngô Bảo Châu](wikipedia://en/11133659) in 2009, thus opening up a wealth of consequences.[^o8]

In 1999, Shelstad developed a theory of [twisted endoscopy](wikipedia://en/20546343) with [Robert Kottwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kottwitz). In 2008–9 she completed work on [tempered endoscopy](wikipedia://en/20546343).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
