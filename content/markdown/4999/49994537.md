# Giovanni Lodovico Longo

*Image: Longo\_-\_Discorso\_matematico,\_1588\_-\_119815.jpg.* *Discorso matematico* (1588)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Lodovico Longo"}

**Giovanni Lodovico Longo**, also known as **Gio. Luigi Lungo** ([fl.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floruit) 1588), was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

Born in [Piedmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont), he became a citizen of [Perugia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perugia) and there a member of the Academy of the Senseless (*Accademia degli Insensati*), where he was nicknamed "the Concentrate" (*il Concentrato*).[^o3]
