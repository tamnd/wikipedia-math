# Antonio Ludeña

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Ludeña"}

**Antonio Ludeña** (December 23, 1740 – March 1, 1820) was a [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaniards) [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), and professor at the [University of Camerino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Camerino).[^o3]

:::
