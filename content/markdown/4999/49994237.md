# Giambattista Magistrini

*Image: Magistrini - Facilis et universalis delineatio geometrica umbrarum, 1816 - 618132.tif.* *Facilis, et universalis delineatio geometrica umbrarum*, 1816

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giambattista Magistrini"}

**Giambattista Magistrini** (1777 – 1849) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

From 1804 he was professor of [calculation](wikipedia://en/239121) at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).[^o2]

From 1811 he was a fellow of the [Accademia nazionale delle scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze).[^o3]
