# List of things named after Emmy Noether

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Emmy Noether"}

*Image: Noether.jpg.* Emmy Noether before 1910

[Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) (1882–1935) was a German mathematician who made many important contributions to abstract algebra. This article is dedicated to the things named after her achievements.

:::

## Other {#Other}

### Astronomy {#Astronomy}

*Image: Far side 21.jpg.* Part of the [USGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USGS) far side lunar chart

- The crater [Nöther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nöther_%28crater%29) on the [far side of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_side_of_the_Moon) is named after her.
- The [7001 Noether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7001_Noether) asteroid also is named for her.[^n1][^o24]
