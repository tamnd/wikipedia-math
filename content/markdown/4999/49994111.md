# Angelo Marchetti

*Image: Marchetti\_-\_Natura\_della\_proporzione\_e\_della\_proporzionalita\_con\_nuovo,\_facile\_e\_sicuro\_metodo,\_1695\_-\_4260143.tif.* *La natura della proporzione*, 1695

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Angelo Marchetti"}

**Angelo Marchetti** (1674 – 1753) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [cosmographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmography) from [Pistoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistoia).[^o2]

:::

He was [Alessandro Marchetti](wikipedia://en/12108478)'s son and a member of the [Academy of Arcadia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Academy_of_Arcadia) in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome).[^o3]
