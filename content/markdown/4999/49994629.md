# Vincent Léotaud

*Image: Leotaud\_-\_Examen\_circuli\_quadraturae,\_1654\_-\_857988.jpg.* *Examen circuli quadraturae* (1654)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vincent Léotaud"}

**Vincent Léotaud** (1595–1672) was a French [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

In his work *Examen circuli quadraturae* he affirmed the impossibility of [squaring the circle](wikipedia://en/201359), against the opinion of [Grégoire de Saint-Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grégoire_de_Saint-Vincent).[^o4][^o5]
