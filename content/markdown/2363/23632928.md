# Hosaka plot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hosaka plot"}

A **Hosaka plot** is a [graphical](wikipedia://en/447298) depiction used to evaluate the quality of a compressed image. A Hosaka plot shows [noise levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_noise) at a number of scales.[^o2][^o3] Hosoka plots are named after K. Hosoka, who wrote about them in 1986.[^o4]

:::
