# Twistor correspondence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Twistor correspondence"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **twistor correspondence** (also known as **Penrose–Ward correspondence**) is a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) between [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons) on [complexified](wikipedia://en/755604) [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space) and [holomorphic vector bundles](wikipedia://en/4794668) on [twistor space](wikipedia://en/2688710), which as a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) is $\mathbb{P}^3$, or [complex projective 3-space](wikipedia://en/762977). Twistor space was introduced by [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193), while [Richard Ward](wikipedia://en/5980017) formulated the correspondence between instantons and vector bundles on twistor space.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

There is a bijection between

1. [Gauge equivalence classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_transformation) of anti-self dual Yang–Mills (ASDYM) connections on complexified Minkowski space $M_\mathbb{C} \cong \mathbb{C}^4$ with [gauge group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_group) $\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{C})$ (the complex [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564))
1. [Holomorphic rank n vector bundles](wikipedia://en/4794668) $E$ over projective [twistor space](wikipedia://en/2688710) $\mathcal{PT} \cong \mathbb{P}^3 - \mathbb{P}^1$ which are trivial on each degree one section of $\mathcal{PT} \rightarrow \mathbb{P}^1$.[^o4][^o5]

where $\mathbb{P}^n$ is the [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) of dimension $n$.

:::

::: {#o7 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

### ADHM construction {#ADHM_construction}

On the anti-self dual Yang–Mills side, the solutions, known as [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons), extend to solutions on [compactified](wikipedia://en/6617) Euclidean 4-space. On the twistor side, the vector bundles extend from $\mathcal{PT}$ to $\mathbb{P}^3$, and the reality condition on the ASDYM side corresponds to a reality structure on the algebraic bundles on the twistor side. Holomorphic vector bundles over $\mathbb{P}^3$ have been extensively studied in the field of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), and all relevant bundles can be generated by the [monad construction](wikipedia://en/5165589)[^o9] also known as the **ADHM construction**, hence giving a classification of instantons.

:::
