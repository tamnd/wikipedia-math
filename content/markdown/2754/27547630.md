# Günter Asser

Günter Asser

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Günter Asser"}

**Günter Asser** (26 February 1926, [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) – 23 March 2015) was a professor emeritus of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald).[^o4] He published numerous volumes on [philosophers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902). His own research was in [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414).

:::

In 1954, with his doctoral advisor Karl Schröter, he co-founded the journal *Zeitschrift für Mathematische Logik und Grundlagen der Mathematik*, which later became *Mathematical Logic Quarterly*.[^o5] In 1977, Günter Asser became member of the [German Academy of Sciences at Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin).
