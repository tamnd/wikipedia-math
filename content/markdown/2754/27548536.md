# The Quants

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The Quants"}

***The Quants*** is the debut [New York Times best selling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_best_seller_list) book by [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street) [journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalist) [Scott Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Patterson_%28author%29).[^o3][^o4] It was released on February 2, 2010, by [Crown Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Business). The book describes the world of [quantitative analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_analyst) and the various [hedge funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_fund) that use the technique.[^o5][^o6] Two years later, Patterson published a follow-up book, *Dark Pools: High Speed Traders, AI Bandits and the Threat to the Global Financial System*, an investigative journey into the history of [high-frequency trading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-frequency_trading) and the spread of [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) in today's markets.[^o7][^o8]

:::

## Background {#Background}

Patterson began writing *The Quants* in 2008. He was first exposed to the [quantitative analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_analysis_%28finance%29) [investment strategies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investment_strategies) while covering the [financial industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_industry) for the *[Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_Journal)*.[^o10] As he became more acquainted with the players involved, he found that many of the most successful quants knew each other and carried similar eccentricities.[^o10] Realizing this was a world that the average investor knew little of, Patterson wrote the book to shed light on the strategies, players, and related risks of such trading strategies.[^o10]

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The introduction to *The Quants* describes the real-life, annual, high-stakes [poker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker) match between Wall Street's hedge fund managers, comparing their trading styles to their poker strategies.[^o12] It focuses on, among other things, the 2007 [subprime mortgage crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subprime_mortgage_crisis) and how it helped trigger a sudden and massive unwinding of complex, highly leveraged quantitative strategies. The book also delves into critical short-comings of many quantitative strategies, such as their tendency to lead to crowded trades and their underestimation of the likelihood of chaotic, volatile moves in the markets.[^o13]

The book also delves into the background of the various vanguards of quantitative analysis. It tells the history of *Beat the Market* & *Beat the Dealer* author [Ed Thorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_O._Thorp); [Pete Muller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Muller_%28businessman_and_singer-songwriter%29) from [Morgan Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Stanley)'s hedge fund; [Ken Griffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_C._Griffin) from [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago)'s [Citadel LLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel_LLC); [James Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Harris_Simons) from [Renaissance Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_Technologies); [Clifford S. Asness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cliff_Asness) and [Aaron Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Brown_%28financial_author%29) from [AQR Capital Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AQR_Capital); and [Boaz Weinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boaz_Weinstein) from [Deutsche Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Bank).[^o14][^o15]

## Reception {#Reception}

*The Quants* debuted on *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times)* [bestseller list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bestselling_books).[^o3] [Jon Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Stewart) featured Patterson as a guest on *[The Daily Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Show)* and described the book as "unbelievable."[^o6] Patterson was a guest on [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR), and Ed Thorp, one of the book's main characters, joined Patterson for a live interview.[^o14] *The New York Times* profiled the book, calling it "fascinating and deeply disturbing."[^o4] *The Quants* received additional profiles in [Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_L.P.), [BusinessWeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BusinessWeek), [Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American), [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times), and [Minyanville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minyanville).[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o10][^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15]
