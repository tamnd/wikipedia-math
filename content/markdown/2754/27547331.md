# Evandro Agazzi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Evandro Agazzi"}

**Evandro Agazzi** (born 23 October 1934) is an Italian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and professor at the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa). His fields of interest are [ethics of science and technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics_of_technology), [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), [philosophical anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophical_anthropology), and [systems theory](wikipedia://en/29238).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Agazzi is a graduate of the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan), where he studied physics and the [Catholic University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Milan) where he earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in philosophy in 1957. Agazzi also did postgraduate work at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) and the universities of [Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg) and [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

## Teaching {#Teaching}

Agazzi taught [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at Genoa, and philosophy of science and mathematical logic at the Catholic University of Milan. He then was named professor of the philosophy of science at Genoa in 1970. He was named chair of philosophical anthropology, philosophy of nature and philosophy of science at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) in 1979. He has served as visiting professor at the [University of Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Düsseldorf), the [University of Berne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berne), the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) as well as other institutions of learning.

## Professional associations {#Professional_associations}

Agazzi is currently president of the [International Academy of Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_Philosophy_of_Science), honorary president of the [International Federation of Philosophical Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_Philosophical_Societies) and of the [International Institute of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Institute_of_Philosophy). He previously served as president of the [Italian Society of Logic and Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Society_of_Logic_and_Philosophy_of_Science), the [Italian Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Philosophical_Society), and the [Swiss Society of Logic and Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Society_of_Logic_and_Philosophy_of_Science). He served as treasurer of the [International Council for Philosophy and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Council_for_Philosophy_and_Humanities) of [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO). He also has been a member of the [Italian National Committee for Bioethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_National_Committee_for_Bioethics). He is currently a member of the Editorial Advisory Board of the Journal ["Global Philosophy"](https://link.springer.com/journal/10516/editorial-board).

## Publications {#Publications}

### Editor {#Editor}

#### Journals {#Journals}

Agazzi was the editor of [Epistemologia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemologia), a former Italian journal for the philosophy of science, and of [Nuova Secondaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuova_Secondaria), an Italian journal for high school teachers. He is a consulting editor for several international journals, including [Revue Internationale de Philosophie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revue_Internationale_de_Philosophie), [Zeitschrift für allgemeine Wissenschaftstheorie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeitschrift_für_allgemeine_Wissenschaftstheorie) and [Modern Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Logic).

### Author {#Author}

#### Journal articles {#Journal_articles}

Agazzi has published over seven hundred articles in scholarly journals and essays in volumes of collected works. He is the author of nineteen books, including *[Philosophie, Science, Métaphysique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophie,_Science,_Métaphysique)*, *[Right, Wrong and Science: The Ethical Dimensions of the Techno-Scientific Enterprise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right,_Wrong_and_Science:_The_Ethical_Dimensions_of_the_Techno-Scientific_Enterprise)*
