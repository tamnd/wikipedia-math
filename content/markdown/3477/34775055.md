# Mu Sigma

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Mu Sigma"}

**Mu Sigma** is an American [data analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_analytics) firm providing big data services, [decision sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_sciences) and helping enterprises in data-driven decision making. The company headquartered in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois) partners with over 140 Fortune 500 companies to enhance their analytical and decision-making capabilities. The firm's name is derived from the statistical terms "[Mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_%28letter%29) (μ)" and "[Sigma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma) (σ)" which symbolize the [mean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean) and the [standard deviation](wikipedia://en/27590), respectively, of a [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543).

:::

## History {#History}

Mu Sigma was founded by [Dhiraj Rajaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhiraj_Rajaram), a former strategy consultant at [Booz Allen Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booz_Allen_Hamilton) and [PricewaterhouseCoopers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PricewaterhouseCoopers), in 2004.[^o2] In 2008, Mu Sigma raised its first institutional investment round of \$30 million from FTVentures (now FTV Capital).[^o5] In April 2011, the company raised an additional \$25 million from [Sequoia Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequoia_Capital).[^o6] In December 2011, the company announced a \$108 million round of financing from Sequoia and growth equity investor [General Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Atlantic).[^o7] In February 2013, Mu Sigma received an investment of \$45 million from [MasterCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MasterCard), which placed the company over the \$1 billion (Rs. 5,400 crore) milestone.[^o8]

In early 2016, the company was sued by [Aon Corp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aon_%28company%29) founder [Pat Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pat_Ryan_%28executive%29), who discussed that Mu Sigma lowballed its own growth prospects in order to buy back Ryan's stake in the company.[^o9]

In October 2016, after the divorce of Ambiga Subramanian, who was [CEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CEO) at the time, Dhiraj Rajaram assumed the role.[^o10][^o11]

## Locations {#Locations}

Mu Sigma is headquartered in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois) and has a global delivery centre in [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore). It also has an office in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas).
