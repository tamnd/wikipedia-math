# Anna Mazzucato

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Mazzucato"}

**Anna Laura Mazzucato** is a professor of mathematics, distinguished senior scholar, and associate head of the mathematics department at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University). Her mathematical research involves [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [function spaces](wikipedia://en/340630), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and their applications in [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988) and [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mazzucato earned a master's degree in physics in 1994 from the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan), with a thesis on [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757) under the supervision of [Paolo Cotta-Ramusino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Cotta-Ramusino). However, during her studies she decided that she preferred the mathematics that she was studying to the physics, and took the advice of Cotta-Ramusino to switch to mathematics for her doctoral studies.

She went to the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) for doctoral study, initially planning to work in [quantum cohomology](wikipedia://en/7781359), but switched to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) with [Michael E. Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_E._Taylor) as her doctoral advisor. Her dissertation was *Analysis of the Navier-Stokes and Other Nonlinear Evolution Equations with Initial Data in Besov-Type Spaces*; it studied the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395) and other [nonlinear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52731617).

After postdoctoral research at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (supported by a Liftoff Fellowship from the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655)) and the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088), and a term as Gibbs Instructor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), she became an assistant professor at Pennsylvania State University in 2003. She was promoted to full professor there in 2013.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mazzucato was the winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) for 2011–2012, which she used to fund a research visit to [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). At Cornell, she gave the Michler Lecture on "The Analysis of Incompressible Fluids at High Reynolds Numbers". She was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in the 2021 class of fellows, "for discerning analysis of fundamental problems in partial differential equations and mathematical fluid mechanics including boundary layers, transport, and mixing".
