# Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize"}

The **Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize** is an annual prize in mathematics, awarded by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) to honor outstanding research by a [female mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_women_in_mathematics) who has recently earned [tenure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenure). The prize funds the winner to spend a semester as a visiting faculty member at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), working with the faculty there and presenting a distinguished lecture on their research. It is named after [Ruth I. Michler](wikipedia://en/59776019) (1967–2000), a German-American mathematician born at Cornell, who died in a road accident at the age of 33.

:::

The award was first offered in 2007. Its winners and their lectures have included:

- [Rebecca Goldin](wikipedia://en/50963820) (2007), "The Geometry of Polygons"
- [Irina Mitrea](wikipedia://en/45108074) (2008), "Boundary-Value Problems for Higher-Order Elliptic Operators"
- [Maria Gordina](wikipedia://en/56626954) (2009), "Lie's Third Theorem in Infinite Dimensions"
- [Patricia Hersh](wikipedia://en/48583545) (2010), "Regular CS Complexes, Total Positivity and Bruhat Order"
- [Anna Mazzucato](wikipedia://en/62170805) (2011), "The Analysis of Incompressible Fluids at High Reynolds Numbers"
- [Ling Long](wikipedia://en/56634323) (2012), "Atkin and Swinnerton-Dyer Congruences"
- [Megumi Harada](wikipedia://en/54336690) (2013), "Newton-Okounkov bodies and integrable systems"
- [Sema Salur](wikipedia://en/62246857) (2014), "Manifolds with G2 structure and beyond"
- [Malabika Pramanik](wikipedia://en/54250054) (2015), "Needles, Bushes, Hairbrushes, and Polynomials"
- [Pallavi Dani](wikipedia://en/66646913) (2016), "Large-scale geometry of right-angled Coxeter groups"
- [Julia Gordon](wikipedia://en/60858453) (2017), "Wilkie's theorem and (ineffective) uniform bounds"
- [Julie Bergner](wikipedia://en/59023346) (2018), "2-Segal structures and the Waldhausen S-construction"
- [Anna Skripka](wikipedia://en/70550429) (2019), "Untangling noncommutativity with operator integrals"
- [Shabnam Akhtari](wikipedia://en/70562226) (2021), "Representation of integers by binary forms"
- [Emily E. Witt](wikipedia://en/70543943) (2022), "Local cohomology: An algebraic tool capturing geometric data"
- [Lauren M. Childs](wikipedia://en/76469693) (2023), "Modeling infectious disease dynamics: A case study of malaria immunity"
- [Alexandra Seceleanu](wikipedia://en/76431136) (2024), "Symmetric Ideals"
- [Ling Xiao](wikipedia://en/79653573) (2025), "The boundary value problem with prescribed singularity"
- [Martha E. Precup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_E._Precup) (2026)
