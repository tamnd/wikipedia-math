# Nataliya Kalashnykova

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nataliya Kalashnykova"}

**Nataliya Ivanovna Kalashnykova** is a Soviet and Mexican mathematician specializing in [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033), and especially [bilevel optimization](wikipedia://en/17702796), with applications in modeling [human migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_migration) and in the pricing of [natural gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas) and [toll roads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_road). She is a professor at the [Autonomous University of Nuevo León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Nuevo_León), in the Facultad de Ciencias Físico Matemáticas.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kalashnykova earned a master's degree in mathematical sciences from [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1978. She completed a doctorate there in 1989, through the Siberian Division of the Academy of Sciences of the USSR. Her dissertation, *Control of Accuracy in Bi-Level Iteration Processes*, was supervised by Vladimir Aleksandrovich Bulavsky. She also earned a second master's degree in economics from [Sumy State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumy_State_University) in Ukraine in 1999.

She became a faculty member at the [Altai State Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altai_State_Technical_University), at the [Siberian State University of Telecommunications and Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberian_State_University_of_Telecommunications_and_Informatics) in Novosibirsk, and at Sumy State University, and a postdoctoral researcher at the [Central Economic Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Economic_Mathematical_Institute). She moved to her present position in Mexico at the Autonomous University of Nuevo León in 2001.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kalashnykova is a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Kalashnykova is married to [Vyacheslav Kalashnikov Polishchuk](wikipedia://en/40880697), another former Soviet mathematician in Mexico.

## Books {#Books}

Kalashnykova is a coauthor of books including:

- *Bilevel Programming Problems: Theory, Algorithms and Applications to Energy Networks* (with Stephan Dempe, Vyacheslav Kalashnikov, and Gerardo A. Pérez-Valdés, Springer, 2015)
- *Public Interest and Private Enterprize : New Developments: Theoretical Results and Numerical Algorithms* (with José Guadalupe Flores Muñiz, Viacheslav V. Kalashnikov, and Vladik Kreinovich, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems 138, Springer, 2021)
