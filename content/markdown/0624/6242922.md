# Analysis of rhythmic variance

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Analysis of rhythmic variance"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), **analysis of rhythmic variance** (**ANORVA**) is a method for detecting rhythms in biological [time series](wikipedia://en/406624), published by Peter Celec (Biol Res. 2004, 37(4 Suppl A):777–82). It is a procedure for detecting cyclic variations in biological time series and quantification of their probability. ANORVA is based on the premise that the [variance](wikipedia://en/32344) in groups of data from rhythmic variables is low when a time distance of one period exists between the data entries.

:::
