# John H. Smith (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John H. Smith"}

**John Howard Smith** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and retired professor of mathematics at [Boston College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_College).[^o2] He received his Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1963, under the supervision of [Kenkichi Iwasawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenkichi_Iwasawa).[^o2][^o3] In [voting theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_theory), he is known for the [Smith set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_set), the smallest nonempty set of candidates such that, in every pairwise matchup (two-candidate election/runoff) between a member and a non-member, the member is the winner by majority rule, and for the [Smith criterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_criterion), a property of certain election systems in which the winner is guaranteed to belong to the Smith set.[^o4] He has also made contributions to [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914)[^o5] and [additive number theory](wikipedia://en/4460333).[^o6]
