# Local Fields

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Local Fields"}

***Corps Locaux*** by [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483), originally published in 1962 and translated into English as ***Local Fields*** by [Marvin Jay Greenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvin_Jay_Greenberg) in 1979, is a seminal graduate-level [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) text covering [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375), [ramification](wikipedia://en/384644), [group cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635), and [local class field theory](wikipedia://en/669532). The book's end goal is to present local class field theory from the cohomological point of view.

:::

In this book, a "local field" is defined as a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) [complete](wikipedia://en/7832) with respect to a [discrete valuation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_valuation), but current usage (including later works by Serre) add the condition that the [residue class field](wikipedia://en/10789680) is [finite](wikipedia://en/11615).[^o3]

The Basic Library List Committee recommends this book for acquisition by undergraduate mathematics libraries.[^o4] It has over 3500 citations in [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar), and is often referenced with respect.[^o5][^o6][^o7]

## Contents {#Contents}

1. *Part I, Local Fields (Basic Facts)*: Discrete valuation rings, Dedekind domains, and Completion.
1. *Part II, Ramification*: Discriminant & Different, Ramification Groups, The Norm, and Artin Representation.
1. *Part III, Group Cohomology*: Abelian & Nonabelian Cohomology, Cohomology of Finite Groups, Theorems of Tate and Nakayama, Galois Cohomology, Class Formations, and Computation of Cup Products.
1. *Part IV, Local Class Field Theory*: Brauer Group of a Local Field, Local Class Field Theory, Local Symbols and Existence Theorem, and Ramification.
