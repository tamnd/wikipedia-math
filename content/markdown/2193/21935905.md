# XLfit

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="XLfit"}

**XLfit** is a [Microsoft Excel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Excel) add-in that can perform [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997), [curve fitting](wikipedia://en/555425), and [statistical analysis](wikipedia://en/26685). It is approved by the [UK National Physical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Physical_Laboratory_%28United_Kingdom%29) and the [US National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology)[^o3] XLfit can generate [2D](wikipedia://en/70691232) and [3D](wikipedia://en/3054853) graphs and analyze data sets. XLfit can also analyse the statistical data. It includes over seventy linear and non-linear curve fitting models.[^o4] Predefined categories include:

:::

- [Exponential](wikipedia://en/9678)/[Log](wikipedia://en/164055), [Power Series](wikipedia://en/59958), [Sigmoidal](wikipedia://en/87210), [Hyperbolic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_function), Yield Density, [Linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear), [Polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000), [Dose Response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dose-response_relationship), [Pharmacology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacology), Equilibrium and [Inhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzyme_inhibitor).
- [Levenberg-Marquardt fitting algorithm](wikipedia://en/892446).

A range of [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685) calculations can also be applied to the data from within the [spreadsheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spreadsheet). Example statistics include:

- [*F*-test](wikipedia://en/318976) and [*t*-test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-test)
- [Area under the curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_under_the_curve) / [ROC](wikipedia://en/922505)
- [Confidence intervals](wikipedia://en/280911)
- Error values relating to parameter values or any point on the curve.

Available statistical models include [Spearman's rank correlations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spearman's_rank_correlation), [Student's *t*-test](wikipedia://en/536080), [Mann–Whitney *U* test](wikipedia://en/433164), [least squares](wikipedia://en/82359), and [ANOVA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANOVA), among others. A model editor also allows users to add their own models and statistics to the ones provided.

## Usage {#Usage}

XLfit was validated by The UK National Physical Laboratory[^o6] in 2004; the unit tests for this are provided in the model editor from version 5.4 onwards to allow each version to be easily validated.

XLfit was used by [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) to analyze the battery life of the [Curiosity Mars Lander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curiosity_%28rover%29).[^o7]
