# Mei-Ling Ting Lee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mei-Ling Ting Lee"}

**Mei-Ling Ting Lee** is a [Taiwanese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Americans) biostatistician known for her research on [microarrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microarray). She is a professor of [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) and [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), and the founding editor-in-chief of the journal *Lifetime Data Analysis*. She was president of the International Chinese Statistical Association for 2016.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lee was born in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei), and earned bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) in 1975 and [National Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tsing_Hua_University) in 1977, respectively. She earned her PhD in statistics from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) in 1980, and became a naturalized US citizen.

She has worked on the faculty of [Bridgewater State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgewater_State_University) (1983–1984), [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) (1984–1992), the [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School) (1993–1999), the [Harvard School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_School_of_Public_Health) (2000–2005), [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) (2005–2008), and the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (since 2008). At Ohio State, she chaired the Biostatistics Division of the School of Public Health and became a Distinguished Professor in Biostatistics and Computational Biology.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Lee is an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) since 1995 and a fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) (1998), [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (1999), and [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2005).
