# Clarence Raymond Adams

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clarence Raymond Adams"}

**Clarence Raymond Adams** (April 10, 1898 – October 15, 1965) was an American mathematician who worked on [partial difference equations](wikipedia://en/39147).

:::

He entered [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in the fall of 1915 and graduated in 1918. Adams received his PhD in 1922 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under the direction of [G. D. Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/30871906). On August 17, 1922, he married [Rachel Blodgett](wikipedia://en/64036151), who earned a PhD from Radcliffe College in 1921. As a Sheldon Traveling Fellow of Harvard University, he studied at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) under [Tullio Levi-Civita](wikipedia://en/499180) and at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) under [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238). In 1923 Adams returned to Brown University as an instructor, then became a full professor in 1936 and eventually chair of the mathematics department from 1942 to 1960. In 1965 he retired and died on October 15 of that same year.[^o3][^o4]
