# Lichnerowicz conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lichnerowicz conjecture"}

In mathematics, the **Lichnerowicz conjecture** is a generalization of a conjecture introduced by (txt). Lichnerowicz's original conjecture was that locally harmonic 4-manifolds are locally symmetric, and was proved by (Walker 1949). The Lichnerowicz conjecture usually refers to the generalization that locally harmonic manifolds are flat or rank-1 locally symmetric. It has been proven true for [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) manifolds with [fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/11004) that are [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) (Szabó 1990) but counterexamples exist in seven or more dimensions in the non-compact case (Damek Ricci 1992)

:::
