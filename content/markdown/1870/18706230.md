# Bolyai Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bolyai Prize"}

The **International János Bolyai Prize of Mathematics** is an international prize founded by the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). The prize is named after [János Bolyai](wikipedia://en/98912) and is awarded every five years[^o2] to [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) for [monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph) with important new results in the preceding 10 years.

:::

## Medalists {#Medalists}

- 1905 – France [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740)
- 1910 – Germany [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302)
- 2000 – Israel [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) for his *Cardinal Arithmetic*, Oxford University Press, 1994. [^o4][^o7]
- 2005 – Russia France [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29) for his *Metric Structures for Riemannian and Non-Riemannian Spaces*, Birkhäuser, 1999. [^o7]
- 2010 – Russia Germany [Yuri I. Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_I._Manin) for his *Frobenius Manifolds, Quantum Cohomology, and Moduli Spaces*, American Mathematical Society, 1999. [^o5][^o7]
- 2015 – USA Israel [Barry Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Simon) for his *Orthogonal Polynomials on the Unit Circle*, American Mathematical Society, 2005. [^o6][^o7]
- 2020 – AustraliaAmerica [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) for his *Nonlinear Dispersive Equations: Local and Global Analysis*, American Mathematical Society, 2006. [^o8]
- 2025 – Hungary [János Kollár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/János_Kollár) for his *Families of Varieties of General Type*, Cambridge University Press, 2023. [^o9]
