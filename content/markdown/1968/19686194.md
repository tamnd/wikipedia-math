# Walter Gautschi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Gautschi"}

**Walter Gautschi** (; ; born December 11, 1927) is a Swiss-born American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [writer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writer) and [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of [Computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in [West Lafayette, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Lafayette,_Indiana).[^o3] He is primarily known for his contributions to [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506)[^o4] and has authored over 200 papers in his area and published four books.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gautschi was born December 11, 1927, in [Basel, Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel), to Heinrich Gautschi (1901-1975). His paternal family originally hailed from [Reinach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinach,_Aargau).[^o6] He had one twin brother Werner (1927-1959). He completed a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) on the thesis *Analyse graphischer Integrationsmethoden* advised by [Alexander Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091) and [Andreas Speiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Speiser) (1953).

## Career {#Career}

Since then, he did postdoctoral work as a Janggen-Pöhn Research Fellow at the *Istituto Nazionale per le Applicazioni del Calcolo* in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) (1954) and at the [Harvard Computation Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Computation_Laboratory) (1955). He had positions at the [National Bureau of Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Standards) (1956–59), the [American University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University) in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), the [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Ridge_National_Laboratory) (1959–63) before joining [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) where he has worked from 1963 to 2000 and now being [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus). He has been a [Fulbright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright) Scholar at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (1970) and held visiting appointments at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) (1976), [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory), the [Wright-Patterson Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright-Patterson_Air_Force_Base), [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) (1996-2001), the [University of Padova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padova) (1997), and the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) (2000).[^o9]

As well-known (e.g. Gerhard Wanner, Geneva c. 2011 and the well-known first-hand sources and subsequent reports such as Math. Intelligencer, etc), one of Gautschi's most important contributions on numerical simulation of special functions offered evidence and confidence to [de Branges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Branges)'s tour-de-force attack on the elusive [Bieberbach conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieberbach_conjecture) on the magnitude of coefficients of schlicht functions, which hitherto received only slow, difficult and partial progress by work of Bieberbach, Loewner, Gabaredian and Schiffer.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1960, Gautschi married Erika, who was previously married to his twin brother Werner (1927-1959). Werner was also an academic professor and lecturer and emigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) with his wife in 1956. After his sudden death, Erika returned to Switzerland, while being pregnant with her child to [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel) where she met Walter and married him in 1960.[^o11] They had three daughters;

- Theresa (1961-2018), married Ainsworth, two children; Emily Ainsworth (b. 1994) and Keith (b. 1997), formerly of [Camas, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camas,_Washington).[^o12]
- Doris (b. 1965)[^o13]
- Caroline *Cari* (b. 1969)

Through his predeceased twin brother, he has stepson/nephew, Thomas (b. 1960). Gautschi still resides in [West Lafayette, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Lafayette,_Indiana).
