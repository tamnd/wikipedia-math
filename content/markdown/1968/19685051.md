# Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics"}

The ***Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [SAGE Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAGE_Publications) on behalf of the [American Educational Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Educational_Research_Association) and [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). It covers [statistical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_method) and [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics) in the [educational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_studies) and [behavioral sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_sciences). The journal was established in 1976 as the *Journal of Educational Statistics* and obtained its current name in 1994. The journal's [editors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are Steven Andrew Culpepper ([University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign)) and Gongjun Xu ([University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan)).[^o3]

:::

## Mission statement {#Mission_statement}

The *Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics* (*JEBS*) provides an outlet for papers that are original and useful to those applying statistical approaches to problems and issues in educational or behavioral research. Typical papers will present new methods of analysis. In addition, critical reviews of current practice, tutorial presentations of less well known methods, and novel applications of already-known methods will be published. Papers discussing statistical techniques without specific educational or behavioral interest will have lower priority.[^o5]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics* is abstracted and indexed in, among other databases, [SCOPUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCOPUS) and the [Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.4.[^o7]
