# Octagram

Regular star polygon stat table

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Octagram"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **octagram** is an eight-angled [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303).

:::

The name *octagram* combine a Greek [numeral prefix](wikipedia://en/1705121), *octa-*, with the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) suffix *\-gram*. The *\-gram* suffix derives from γραμμή (*grammḗ*) meaning "line".[^o2]

## Detail {#Detail}

*Image: Octagram lengths.svg.* A regular octagram with each side length equal to 1

In general, an octagram is any self-intersecting [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575) (8-sided [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621)).

The [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) octagram is labeled by the [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {8/3}, which means an 8-sided star, connected by every third point.

## As a quasitruncated square {#As_a_quasitruncated_square}

Deeper truncations of the square can produce isogonal (vertex-transitive) intermediate star polygon forms with equal spaced vertices and two edge lengths. A truncated square is an octagon, t{4}={8}. A quasitruncated square, inverted as {4/3}, is an octagram, t{4/3}={8/3}.[^o8]

The uniform [star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/7087334) [stellated truncated hexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157363), t'{4,3}=t{4/3,3} has octagram faces constructed from the cube in this way. It may be considered for this reason as a three-dimensional analogue of the octagram.

Another three-dimensional version of the octagram is the [nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157120) (quasirhombicuboctahedron), which can be thought of as a quasicantellated (quasiexpanded) cube, t<sub>0,2</sub>{4/3,3}.

## Star polygon compounds {#Star_polygon_compounds}

There are two regular octagrammic star figures (compounds) of the form {8/k}, the first constructed as two squares {8/2}=2{4}, and second as four degenerate [digons](wikipedia://en/5442728), {8/4}=4{2}. There are other isogonal and isotoxal compounds including rectangular and rhombic forms.

::: {#o13 .table cue=table}

| Regular |  | Isogonal |  | Isotoxal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Image: Regular star figure 2(4,1).svg.* a{8}={8/2}=2{4} | *Image: Regular star figure 4(2,1).svg.* {8/4}=4{2} | *Image: Octagram rectangle compound.png.* | *Image: Octagram crossed-rectangle compound.png.* | *Image: Octagram rhombic star.png.* |

:::

{8/2} or 2{4}, like [Coxeter diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) + , can be seen as the 2D equivalent of the 3D [compound of cube and octahedron](wikipedia://en/8010874), + , 4D compound of tesseract and 16-cell, + and 5D [compound of 5-cube and 5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/59209318); that is, the compound of a [n-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-cube) and [cross-polytope](wikipedia://en/716401) in their respective dual positions.

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

*Image: The Ghassulian star.jpg.* The [Ghassulian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghassulian) star

- One of the oldest depictions of several variations of the octagram nested within each other was found in a cave in [Jordan](wikipedia://en/7515964). Dating back 6,000 years and associated with the [Ghassulian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghassulian) culture, it is speculated to have represented some kind of calendar system.[^o20]
- In [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode), the "Eight Spoked Asterisk" symbol [✳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/✳) is U+2733.
- The unicode for [Dingir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dingir), a Sumerian [cuneiform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuneiform) eight-pointed [ideogram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideogram) meaning "sky" and later a [logogram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logogram) for a [deity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deity) is U+1202D 𒀭.

*Image: Jupiter’s Rings And Moons (NIRCam) Commissioning Image (jupiter-hi-res-rings).tiff.* The spikes are specially visible around [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter_%28planet%29)'s moon [Europa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europa_%28moon%29) (on the left) in this [NIRCam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIRCam) image.

*Image: JWST\_diffraction\_spikes.svg.* Edges of the JWST primary mirror segments and spider colour-coded with their corresponding diffraction spikes

- The 8-pointed [diffraction spikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffraction_spike) of the star images from the [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope) are due to the [diffraction](wikipedia://en/8603) caused by the hexagonal shape of the mirror sections and the struts holding the secondary mirror.[^o21]
- Used as a parol or star for the 2010 [ABS-CBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN) Christmas Station ID *Ngayong Pasko Magniningning Ang Pilipino* (This Christmas, the Filipinos will Shine) due to the usage of a [sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Philippines#Symbolism) from the [Philippine flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Philippines), making it also a nationalism and patriotism-themed song aside from being a Christmas song.
