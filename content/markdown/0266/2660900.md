# Robert I. Soare

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert I. Soare"}

*Image: Robert Soare.jpg.* Robert I. Soare

**Robert Irving Soare** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is the Paul Snowden Russell Distinguished Service Professor of Mathematics and Computer Science at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he has been on the faculty since 1967. He proved, together with [Carl Jockusch](wikipedia://en/9859617), the [low basis theorem](wikipedia://en/9767227), and has done other work in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), primarily in the area of [computability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory). His doctoral students at the University of Chicago have included [Barbara Csima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Csima).

:::

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
