# Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society"}

The ***Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*** is a quarterly [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

:::

## Scope {#Scope}

It publishes surveys on contemporary research topics, written at a level accessible to non-experts. It also publishes, by invitation only, book reviews and short *Mathematical Perspectives* articles.[^o4]

## History {#History}

It began as the *Bulletin of the New York Mathematical Society* and underwent a name change when the society became national. The Bulletin's function has changed over the years; its original function was to serve as a research journal for its members.

## Indexing {#Indexing}

The Bulletin is indexed in [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index), [ISI Alerting Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information), CompuMath Citation Index, and [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences.[^o4]
