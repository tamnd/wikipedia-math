# János Bolyai Mathematical Institute

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="János Bolyai Mathematical Institute"}

*Image: János Bólyai Mathematical Institute.jpg.* Front view of the János Bólyai Mathematical Institute

**Bolyai Institute** is the mathematics institute of the Faculty of Sciences of the [University of Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged), named after the Hungarian mathematicians, [Farkas Bolyai](wikipedia://en/1159131), and his son [János Bolyai](wikipedia://en/98912), the co-discoverer of [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610). Its director is László Zádori. Among the former members of the institute are [Frigyes Riesz](wikipedia://en/1073126), [Alfréd Haar](wikipedia://en/491228), Rudolf Ortvay, [Tibor Radó](wikipedia://en/1490393), [Béla Szőkefalvi-Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béla_Szőkefalvi-Nagy), [László Kalmár](wikipedia://en/1988240), [Géza Fodor](wikipedia://en/22763675).

:::
