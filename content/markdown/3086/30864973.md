# German Mathematical Society

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="German Mathematical Society"}

The **German Mathematical Society** (Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung, DMV) is the main professional society of German mathematicians and represents German mathematics within the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) (EMS) and the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU). It was founded in 1890 in [Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremen) with the set theorist [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216) as first president. Founding members included [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216), [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887), [Walther von Dyck](wikipedia://en/752612), [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537), [Carl Runge](wikipedia://en/747636), [Rudolf Sturm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Sturm), [Hermann Schubert](wikipedia://en/7998163), and [Heinrich Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615).[^o3]

:::

The current president of the DMV is Jürg Kramer.[^o4]

## Activities {#Activities}

In honour of its founding president, [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216), the society awards the [Cantor Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_medal). The DMV publishes two scientific journals, the *Jahresbericht der DMV* and *Documenta Mathematica*. It also publishes a quarterly magazine for its membership the *Mitteilungen der DMV*.[^o6] The annual meeting of the DMV is called the *Jahrestagung*; the DMV traditionally meets every four years together with the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society) (ÖMG) and every four years together with the [Gesellschaft für Didaktik der Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Didaktik_der_Mathematik) (GDM). It sometimes organises its meetings jointly with other societies (e.g., 2014 with the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791), PTM, or 2016 with the [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik), GAMM). Twice annually, it organises the [*Gauß Lecture*](wikipedia://en/48440954), a public audience lecture by well-known mathematicians.[^o7]
