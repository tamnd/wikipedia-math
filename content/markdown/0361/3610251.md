# Joe Harris (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joe Harris"}

**Joseph Daniel Harris** (born August 17, 1951) is the Higgins Professor of Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o3] He specializes in the field of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). After earning an AB from [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) he continued at Harvard to study for a PhD under [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414).

:::

## Work {#Work}

During the 1980s, he was on the faculty of [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), moving to Harvard in 1988.[^o3] He served as chair of the department at Harvard from 2002 to 2005. His work is characterized by its classical geometric flavor: he has claimed that nothing he thinks about could not have been imagined by the [Italian geometers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_geometers) of the late 19th and early 20th centuries, and that if he has had greater success than them, it is because he has access to better tools.

Harris is well known for several of his books on algebraic geometry, notable for their informal presentations:

- *Principles of Algebraic Geometry* , with Phillip Griffiths[^o5]
- *Geometry of Algebraic Curves, Vol. 1* , with [Enrico Arbarello](wikipedia://en/28094731), [Maurizio Cornalba](wikipedia://en/62085269), and Phillip Griffiths
- William Fulton, Joe Harris. (1991). "Representation Theory, A First Course". 129. Springer-Verlag. doi:10.1007/978-1-4612-0979-9. isbn:978-0-387-97495-8. mr:1153249, with [William Fulton](wikipedia://en/9642149)
- Harris, Joe. (1992). "Algebraic Geometry, A First Course". Springer Science. isbn:978-0-387-97716-4. <https://www.springer.com/gp/book/9780387977164>
- David Eisenbud, Joe Harris. (2000). "The Geometry of Schemes". 197. Springer-Verlag. isbn:978-0-387-98638-8. mr:1730819, with [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845)
- David Eisenbud, Joseph Harris (2016). (14 April 2016). "3264 and All That: A Second Course in Algebraic Geometry". Cambridge University Press. isbn:978-1107602724
- *Moduli of Curves* , with [Ian Morrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Morrison_%28mathematician%29).[^o10]
- *[Fat Chance: Probability from 0 to 1](wikipedia://en/63709809)*, with [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863) and [Emily Riehl](wikipedia://en/56785970), 2019[^o11]

As of 2018, Harris has supervised 50 PhD students, including [Lucia Caporaso](wikipedia://en/56679890), [Brendan Hassett](wikipedia://en/45374526), [James McKernan](wikipedia://en/25690427), [Rahul Pandharipande](wikipedia://en/8950386), [Zvezdelina Stankova](wikipedia://en/49295094), and [Ravi Vakil](wikipedia://en/5981037).[^o12]
