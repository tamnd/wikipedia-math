# Oskar Schlömilch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oskar Schlömilch"}

**Oskar Xavier Schlömilch** (13 April 1823 – 7 February 1901) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), born in [Weimar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weimar), working in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He took a doctorate at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena) in 1842, and became a professor at [Dresden Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_University_of_Technology) in 1849.

:::

He is now known as the [eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym) of the [Schlömilch function](wikipedia://en/406932),[^o3] a kind of [Bessel function](wikipedia://en/4700). He was also an important textbook writer, and editor of the journal *Zeitschrift für Mathematik und Physik*, of which he was a founder in 1856. He published in 1868 for the first time the [dissection paradox](wikipedia://en/365627), earlier invented by [Sam Loyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Loyd).

In 1862, he was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences).
