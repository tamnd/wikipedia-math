# Michael Thaddeus

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Thaddeus"}

**Michael Thaddeus** (born January 12, 1967) is an American mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o3] He is best known for having been a [whistleblower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whistleblower) in exposing inaccurate data submitted by Columbia University to *[U.S. News & World Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._News_&_World_Report)* (USNWR)'s [Best Colleges Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._News_&_World_Report_Best_Colleges_Ranking) to inflate the university's [college ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_ranking).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Thaddeus was born on January 12, 1967, in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), and grew up in [Morningside Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morningside_Heights). His father, [Patrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Thaddeus), and his mother, Janice, both held doctorates from Columbia. Thaddeus was educated at [St. Hilda's & St. Hugh's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Hilda's_&_St._Hugh's_School) before enrolling at [Hunter College High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College_High_School) in 1980, where he graduated in 1984.[^o7]

Thaddeus matriculated at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where his sister was also a student, with the intent to study biology but switched to mathematics.[^o7] He graduated in 1988 and was awarded a [Rhodes Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_Scholarship) to study at [St John's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Oxford), where he earned his [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (DPhil) under the supervision of [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305).[^o8][^o9] His dissertation, *Algebraic Geometry and the Verlinde Formula*, was published in 1992.[^o10]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

In 1994, Thaddeus was elected a Junior Fellow of the [Harvard Society of Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Society_of_Fellows).[^o12] He was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) and the [Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/401367) from 1994 and 1997.[^o7] In 1998, Thaddeus joined the mathematics faculty of Columbia University.[^o8] He has lectured at multiple institutions including [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College), [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech), and [Creighton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creighton_University), among others.[^o8] He specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o15]

Thaddeus was a member of the School of Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2001.[^o12] From 2017 to 2020, he was the chair of the math department at Columbia University.[^o7]

### Columbia rankings exposé {#Columbia_rankings_exposé}

Thaddeus' exposé, "An Investigation of the Facts Behind Columbia’s U.S. News Ranking,"[^o14] was published in February 2022, identifying discrepancies such as shrunken class sizes and the exaggeration of faculty credentials.[^o15] Due to Thaddeus' analysis, Columbia later dropped from having been ranked 2nd to being ranked 18th, [sparking speculation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_college_and_university_rankings_%28North_America%29) about the integrity of college rankings.[^o16][^o17][^o18]
