# Ram Kishore Saxena

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ram Kishore Saxena"}

**Ram Kishore Saxena** [D.Sc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), [FNASc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India) (born 11 November 1936) is an Indian mathematician and Emeritus professor, UGC [Jai Narain Vyas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Narain_Vyas_University) and former Professor and Head, Department of Mathematics.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Published work {#Published_work}

Saxena has published 356 research papers;[^o6][^o8][^o9] under his supervision many scholars has done PhD and post-doctoral research.[^o3] Saxena has published books.[^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14]
