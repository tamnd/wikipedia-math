# Record value

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Record value"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **record value** or **record statistic** is the largest or smallest value obtained from a sequence of random variables. The theory is closely related to that used in [order statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_statistics).[^o2]

:::

The term was first introduced by K. N. Chandler in 1952.[^o3]
