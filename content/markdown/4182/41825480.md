# Pauli Pylkkö

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pauli Pylkkö"}

**Pauli Pylkkö** is a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finns) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher).[^o2] He was a student of [Jaakko Hintikka](wikipedia://en/1710903), and later a professor and a researcher in both the United States and Finland. Pylkkö has addressed such topics as [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [semiotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiotics), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), and [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science). Pylkkö has published several works focused specifically on the philosophy of [Martin Heidegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Heidegger), and his works have examined the often problematic relationships between language and subjectivity, nationalism, the limits of scientific rationality, and the semiotic and linguistic mechanics of fascism. His work has also aroused interest in theological research.[^o3]

:::
