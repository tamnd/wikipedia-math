# Nikolai Günther

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Günther"}

**Nikolai Maximovich Günther** (Николай Максимович Гюнтер; also transliterated as **Nicholas M. Gunther**,[^o3] or **N. M. Gjunter**;[^o4] December 17 – May 4, 1941) was a Russian mathematician known for his work in [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342) and in [integral](wikipedia://en/474234) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564): later studies have uncovered his contributions to the theory of [Gröbner bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_base).[^o4]

:::

He was an invited speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 at Toronto,[^o5] in 1928 at Bologna,[^o6][^o7] and in 1932 at Zurich.
