# Ulrike Grömping

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrike Grömping"}

**Ulrike Grömping** is a German statistician known for her work on [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997) with variable importance, and for her [R package](wikipedia://en/376707) *relaimpo* for performing linear regression with relative importance. She is Professor for Applied Statistics and Business Mathematics at the [Berliner Hochschule für Technik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Hochschule_für_Technik).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Grömping studied statistics at the [Technical University of Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Dortmund), earning a diploma there in 1991. She completed her Ph.D. in statistics there in 1996. Her dissertation, *Tests for a Monotone Dose-Response Relation in Models with Ordered Categorical Dose with Emphasis on Likelihood Ratio Tests for Linear Inequalities on Normal Means*, was supervised by Siegfried Schach.

After working as a statistician for the [Ford Motor Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Motor_Company) in [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne) from 1997 to 2004, Grömping returned to academia as a professor at the [Technische Fachhochschule Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Fachhochschule_Berlin) (now the Berliner Hochschule für Technik) in 2004.
