# Rational normal scroll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rational normal scroll"}

In mathematics, a **rational normal scroll** is a [ruled surface](wikipedia://en/705158) of degree *n* in [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) of dimension *n* + 1. Here "[rational](wikipedia://en/865939)" means [birational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birational_equivalence) to projective space, "scroll" is an old term for ruled surface, and "normal" refers to [projective normality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_normality) (not [normal schemes](wikipedia://en/2629423)).

:::

A non-degenerate irreducible surface of degree *m* – 1 in **P**<sup>*m*</sup> is either a rational normal scroll or the [Veronese surface](wikipedia://en/1569217).

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

In projective space of dimension *m* + *n* + 1 choose two complementary linear subspaces of dimensions *m* > 0 and *n* > 0. Choose [rational normal curves](wikipedia://en/2380117) in these two linear subspaces, and choose an isomorphism φ between them. Then the rational normal surface consists of all lines joining the points *x* and *φ*(*x*). In the degenerate case when one of *m* or *n* is 0, the rational normal scroll becomes a cone over a rational normal curve. If *m* \< *n* then the rational normal curve of degree *m* is uniquely determined by the rational normal scroll and is called the **directrix** of the scroll.

:::
