# Kentaro Yano (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kentaro Yano"}

**Kentaro Yano** (1 March 1912 in Tokyo, Japan – 25 December 1993) was a mathematician working on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625)[^o4] who introduced the [Bochner–Yano theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochner–Yano_theorem).

:::

He also published a classical book about geometric objects (i.e., sections of [natural fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/54904665)) and [Lie derivatives](wikipedia://en/294437) of these objects.

## Publications {#Publications}

- Les espaces à connexion projective et la géométrie projective des paths, Iasi, 1938
- Geometry of Structural Forms (Japanese), 1947
- Groups of Transformations in Generalized Spaces, Tokyo, Akademeia Press, 1949
- with Salomon Bochner: [Curvature and Betti Numbers](https://books.google.com/books?id=ykEZ5z5-5x8C), Princeton University Press, [Annals of Mathematics Studies](wikipedia://en/83104341), 1953[^o6]
- (1957). "The Theory of Lie Derivatives and its Applications". North-Holland. isbn:978-0-7204-2104-0 [2020 reprint](https://books.google.com/books?id=V97YDwAAQBAJ)
- Differential geometry on complex and almost complex spaces, Macmillan, New York 1965
- Integral formulas in Riemannian Geometry, Marcel Dekker, New York 1970
- with Shigeru Ishihara: Tangent and cotangent bundles: differential geometry, New York, M. Dekker 1973
- with Masahiro Kon: Anti-invariant submanifolds, Marcel Dekker, New York 1976[^o8]
- [Morio Obata](wikipedia://en/80481508) (ed.): [Selected papers of Kentaro Yano](https://books.google.com/books?id=5MV0Yrx4dHYC), North Holland 1982
- with Masahiro Kon: CR Submanifolds of Kählerian and Sasakian Manifolds, Birkhäuser 1983[^o9] [2012 reprint](https://books.google.com/books?id=uK7bBwAAQBAJ)
- with Masahiro Kon: Structures on Manifolds, World Scientific 1984
