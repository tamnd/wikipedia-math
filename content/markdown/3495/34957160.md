# Rademacher–Menchov theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rademacher–Menchov theorem"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), the **Rademacher–Menchov theorem**, introduced by (txt) and (txt), gives a [sufficient condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficient_condition) for a series of [orthogonal functions](wikipedia://en/453041) on an interval to converge [almost everywhere](wikipedia://en/44688).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

If the coefficients *c*<sub>ν</sub> of a series of bounded orthogonal functions on an interval satisfy

$$\sum |c_\nu|^2\log(\nu)^2<\infty$$

then the series converges almost everywhere.

:::
