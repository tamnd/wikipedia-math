# Outline of combinatorics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of combinatorics"}

**[Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170)** is a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) concerning the study of finite or [countable](wikipedia://en/6026) [discrete](wikipedia://en/8492) [structures](wikipedia://en/1809181).
