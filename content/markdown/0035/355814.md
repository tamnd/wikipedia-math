# Outline of discrete mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of discrete mathematics"}

**[Discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492)** is the study of [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [structures](wikipedia://en/1809181) that are fundamentally [discrete](wikipedia://en/56061) rather than [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122). In contrast to [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) that have the property of varying "smoothly", the objects studied in discrete mathematics – such as [integers](wikipedia://en/14563), [graphs](wikipedia://en/325806), and statements in [logic](wikipedia://en/19636)[^o2] – do not vary smoothly in this way, but have distinct, separated values. Discrete mathematics, therefore, excludes topics in "continuous mathematics" such as [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

:::
