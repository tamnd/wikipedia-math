# Orthonormal frame

local coordinates for manifolds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Orthonormal frame"}

In [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) and [relativity theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relativity_theory), an **orthonormal frame** is a tool for studying the structure of a [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) equipped with a metric. If *M* is a manifold equipped with a metric *g*, then an orthonormal frame at a point *P* of *M* is an ordered basis of the [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) at *P* consisting of vectors which are [orthonormal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonormal) with respect to the [bilinear form](wikipedia://en/449477) *g*<sub>*P*</sub>.[^o2]

:::
