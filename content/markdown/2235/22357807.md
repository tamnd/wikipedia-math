# 2000 Panamanian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2000 Panamanian census"}

The **2000 Panamanian census** gathered important [demographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographic) and [economic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic) data for the [Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama). It was the tenth national [census](wikipedia://en/6889) of population and sixth of housing. It was conducted on May 14, 2000, by the Republic of Panama General Directorate of Statistics and Census.[^o2]

:::
