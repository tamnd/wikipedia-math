# Giovanni Giambelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Giambelli"}

*Image: Giambelli Fjeldstad Zurich1932.tif.* Giovanni Giambelli *(left)* and [Jonas Fjeldstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_Fjeldstad) *(right)* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) 1932

**Giovanni Zeno Giambelli** (June 29, 1879 – December 31, 1953) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is best known for [Giambelli's formula](wikipedia://en/22269939).[^o3]
