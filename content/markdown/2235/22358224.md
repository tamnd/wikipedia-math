# Picture (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Picture"}

In [combinatorial mathematics](wikipedia://en/5170), a **picture** is a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) between [skew diagrams](wikipedia://en/683368#Skew_tableaux) satisfying certain properties, introduced by (Zelevinsky 1981) in a generalization of the [Robinson–Schensted correspondence](wikipedia://en/1901026) and the [Littlewood–Richardson rule](wikipedia://en/16004359).

:::
