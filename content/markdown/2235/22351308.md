# Danish Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Danish Mathematical Society"}

The **Danish Mathematical Society** (**Dansk Matematisk Forening**) is a society of [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) founded in 1873 at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), a year after the [French Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France). According to the society website, it has "the purpose of acting for the benefit of mathematics in research and education."

:::

## History {#History}

The society was founded after the idea of [Thorvald N. Thiele](wikipedia://en/701256). The first committee was composed of Thiele, [Hieronymus Georg Zeuthen](wikipedia://en/1732797) and [Julius Petersen](wikipedia://en/76861).

[Bodil Branner](wikipedia://en/45422609) was the first woman to lead the Danish Mathematical Society, which she did from 1998 to 2002.[^o3]

### Presidents {#Presidents}

- [Johan Jensen](wikipedia://en/1430666) (1892–1903)
- [Vilhelm Herman Oluf Madsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilhelm_Herman_Oluf_Madsen) (1903–1910)
- [Niels Nielsen](wikipedia://en/33935937) (1910–1917)
- [Johannes Mollerup](wikipedia://en/49411394) (1917–1926)
- [Harald Bohr](wikipedia://en/638305) (1926–1929, 1937–1951)
- [Børge Jessen](wikipedia://en/20754801) (1954–1958)
- [Werner Fenchel](wikipedia://en/4784468) (1958–1962)
- [Bodil Branner](wikipedia://en/45422609) (1998–2002)
