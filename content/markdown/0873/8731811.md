# Peppercoin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peppercoin"}

**Peppercoin** is a [cryptographic](wikipedia://en/18934432) system for processing [micropayments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micropayment). **Peppercoin Inc.** was a company that offered services based on the peppercoin method.

:::

The peppercoin system was developed by [Silvio Micali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvio_Micali) and [Ron Rivest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Rivest) and first presented at the RSA Conference in 2002[^o2] (although it had not yet been named.) The core idea is to bill one randomly selected transaction a lump sum of money rather than bill each transaction a small amount. It uses "universal aggregation", which means that it aggregates transactions over users, merchants as well as [payment service providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payment_service_provider). The random selection is cryptographically secure—it cannot be influenced by any of the parties. It is claimed to reduce the [transaction cost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transaction_cost) per dollar from 27 cents to "well below 10 cents."[^o3]

Peppercoin, Inc. was a privately held company founded in late 2001 by Micali and Rivest based in [Waltham, MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waltham,_Massachusetts). It has secured about \$15M in venture capital in two rounds of funding.[^o4][^o5] Its services saw modest adoption.[^o6][^o7] Peppercoin collects 5-9% of transaction cost from the merchant.[^o8] Peppercoin, Inc. was bought out in 2007 by Chockstone for an undisclosed amount.[^o5]
