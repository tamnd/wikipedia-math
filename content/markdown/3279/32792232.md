# Theodore William Chaundy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore William Chaundy"}

**Theodore William Chaundy** (19 January 1889 – 14 April 1966) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Burchnall–Chaundy theory](wikipedia://en/32791724).

:::

Chaundy was born to widowed businessman John Chaundy and his second wife Sarah Pates in their shop-cum-home at 49 [Broad Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_Street,_Oxford) in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford). John had eight children, one of whom died as a toddler, with his late first wife and died barely a year after Chaundy was born. The Chaundy home along Broad Street has since been demolished.[^o3]

Chaundy attended [Oxford High School for Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_High_School_for_Boys) and read mathematics at [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford) on a scholarship. In 1912 he became a lecturer at Oxford and later named a Fellow of [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford). He married Hilda Weston Dott (1890–1986) in 1920. They had five children and thirteen grandchildren.[^o3]
