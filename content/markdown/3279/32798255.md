# Erdelyi–Kober operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erdelyi–Kober operator"}

In mathematics, an **Erdélyi–Kober operator** is a [fractional integration operation](wikipedia://en/229939) introduced by (txt) and (txt).

:::

The Erdélyi–Kober fractional integral is given by

$$\frac{x^{-\nu-\alpha+1}}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\int_0^x (t-x)^{\alpha-1}t^{-\alpha-\nu}f(t) dt$$

which generalizes the [Riemann fractional integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_fractional_integral) and the [Weyl integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_integral).
