# Burchnall–Chaundy theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Burchnall–Chaundy theory"}

In mathematics, the **Burchnall–Chaundy theory** of commuting linear [ordinary differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_operator) was introduced by (txt).

:::

One of the main results says that two [commuting](wikipedia://en/7193) [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886) satisfy a non-trivial algebraic relation. The [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) relating the two commuting differential operators is called the **Burchnall–Chaundy polynomial**.
