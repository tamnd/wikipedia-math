# Hopf theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hopf theorem"}

The **Hopf theorem** (named after [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082)) is a statement in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), saying that the [topological degree](wikipedia://en/3094450) is the only [homotopy invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_invariant) of [continuous maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_maps) to [spheres](wikipedia://en/39782).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Formal statement"}

## Formal statement {#Formal_statement}

Let *M* be an *n*-dimensional [compact](wikipedia://en/669475) [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [oriented manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470#Orientability) and $S^n$ the [*n*-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782) and $f,g\colon M\to S^n$ be continuous. Then $\deg(f)=\deg(g)$ if and only if *f* and *g* are [homotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopic).

:::
