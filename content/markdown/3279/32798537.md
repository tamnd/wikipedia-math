# Hermann Kober

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Kober"}

**Hermann Kober** (born 1 February 1888 in [Beuthen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beuthen), Germany (now Bytom, Poland), died 4 October 1973 in Birmingham, England) was a [Jewish-German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish-German) mathematician who introduced [Erdélyi–Kober operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdélyi–Kober_operator). He taught (mathematics and science), up to the early 1960s, at some of the [King Edward VI Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_of_the_Schools_of_King_Edward_VI) schools in Birmingham.

:::
