# Horrocks bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horrocks bundle"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Horrocks bundles** are certain indecomposable rank 3 [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) (locally free sheaves) on 5-dimensional projective space, found by (Horrocks 1978).

:::
