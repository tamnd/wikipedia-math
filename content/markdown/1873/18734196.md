# Ramachandran Balasubramanian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramachandran Balasubramanian"}

*Image: The President, Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam presenting Padma Shri to Prof. Ramachandran Balasubramanian, Director of Institute of Mathematical Science, at investiture ceremony in New Delhi on March 29, 2006.jpg.* The President, Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam presenting Padma Shri to Prof. Ramachandran Balasubramanian, Director of Institute of Mathematical Science, at investiture ceremony in New Delhi on March 29, 2006

**Ramachandran Balasubramanian** (born 15 March 1951) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and was Director of the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/2718996) in [Chennai, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai,_India).[^o3] He is known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), which includes settling the final g(4) case of [Waring's problem](wikipedia://en/33569) in 1986.[^o4][^o5] He is also known for his work in [Cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) which includes his famous work with Koblitz, now commonly called the *Balu-Koblitz Theorem*.[^o6] His work in [Additive Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243381) includes his two page paper on additive complements of squares, hence disproving a long standing conjecture of [Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).[^o7]

:::

His works on moments of Riemann zeta function is highly appreciated and he was a plenary speaker from India at ICM in 2010. He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1980-81.[^o8]

He was a student of [K. Ramachandra](wikipedia://en/32512780), a lifelong collaborator of [Jean-Marc Deshouillers](wikipedia://en/40678456), and co-authored stellar mathematicians like [Ram Murty](wikipedia://en/14988649), [Kumar Murty](wikipedia://en/34926069), [Heath-Brown](wikipedia://en/2984550), [N. Koblitz](wikipedia://en/1587628) and [F. Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015). He was the founder and remains a member of the advisory board of the [Hardy-Ramanujan Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy-Ramanujan_Journal).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

He has received the following awards:

- The [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in 1990.
- The French government's [Ordre National du Mérite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_National_du_Mérite) for "furthering Indo-French cooperation in the field of mathematics" in 2003.[^o10]
- The [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri_Awards_%282000-2009%29#2006), the fourth highest civilian award in India, in 2006.[^o11]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012.[^o12]
- The [Lifetime Achievement Award, 2013](http://www.rediff.com/news/report/pm-honours-four-n-scientists-with-lifetime-achievement-awards/20130115.htm) awarded by Manmohan Singh, the Prime Minister of India.[^o13]
- Fellow of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (1988)[^o14]
