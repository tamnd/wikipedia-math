# Coxeter–James Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Coxeter–James Prize"}

The **Coxeter-James Prize** is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) award given by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) (CMS) to recognize outstanding contributions to mathematics by young mathematicians in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada). First presented in 1978, the prize is named after two renowned Canadian mathematicians, [Donald Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Scott_MacDonald_Coxeter) and [Ralph James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Duncan_James).[^o5]

:::

The prize is awarded annually to a young Canadian mathematician who has made significant contributions to the field of mathematics. It is intended to recognize and encourage young mathematicians in Canada and to promote the development of mathematics in the country.

Recipients of the Coxeter-James Prize are selected by the CMS Research Committee and are typically honored at the Society's annual meeting.[^o3]

The Coxeter-James Prize is one of several awards given by the Canadian Mathematical Society to recognize and encourage excellence in mathematics. Other awards given by the Society include the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069), the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829), and the Blair Spearman Doctoral Prize.[^o3]
