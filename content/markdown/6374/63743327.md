# Monique Jeanblanc

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monique Jeanblanc"}

**Monique Jeanblanc-Picqué** (born 1947) is a French mathematician known for her work in [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680); other topics in her research have included [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). She is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) at the [University of Évry Val d'Essonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Évry_Val_d'Essonne).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jeanblanc was a student at the [Ecole Normale Supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_Paris-Saclay) from 1966 to 1969, when she took a teaching position there. In 1992 she moved to the University of Évry.

## Books {#Books}

Jeanblanc is the author or coauthor of the books including

- *Enlargement of Filtration with Finance in View* (with Anna Aksamit, Springer, 2017)
- *Mathematical Methods for Financial Markets* (with [Marc Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor) and Marc Chesney, Springer, 2009)
- *Marchés financiers en temps continu: valorisation et équilibre* (with Rose-Anne Dana, Economica, 1998), translated into English as *Financial Markets in Continuous Time* (Springer, 2003)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2009, Jeanblanc was made a chevalier in the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour).
