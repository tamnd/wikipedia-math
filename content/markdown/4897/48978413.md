# Arthur Matthews (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Matthews"}

**Arthur Matthews** (1848 – 1911) was a founding professor at [Rhodes University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_University) in [Grahamstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grahamstown), South Africa.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Matthews was born in [Torquay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torquay), England in 1848 to John Redaway and Agnes Mudge Matthews. He studied at [Sidney Sussex College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Sussex_College,_Cambridge) where he obtained the degrees Bachelor of Arts (BA) in 1870 and Master of Arts (MA) in 1873. He later obtained a Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree from the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London).

## Cape Colony {#Cape_Colony}

He came to the [Cape Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Colony) in 1875 as the first lecturer in mathematics and physical science at [St. Andrew's College, Grahamstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Andrew's_College,_Grahamstown), and later became professor and vice-principal of its College Department.[^n1] He built up at a Land Survey Department while at St. Andrew's College and trained many of South Africa's early land surveyors, including four [Surveyors General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveyor_General).[^n2][^n3]

In 1904 Rhodes University College was established and its first four professors appointed from the staff of St. Andrew's College.[^n4] One of these was Matthews, who became professor of mathematics and chairman of the university senate.[^n5]

Matthews retired from Rhodes University in 1910 and died in the [Cape Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Province) in 1911.
