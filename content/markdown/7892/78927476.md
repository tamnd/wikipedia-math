# Joan Lasenby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joan Lasenby"}

**Joan Lasenby** is a British physicist, engineer, and applied mathematician whose research interests include [3D reconstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_reconstruction) and [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture), [geometric algebra](wikipedia://en/12939), [inverse kinematics](wikipedia://en/502323), and the applications of [structured light plethysmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structured_light_plethysmography) in non-invasive medical diagnostics. She is Professor of Image and Signal Analysis in the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) Department of Engineering, and College Lecturer and Director of Studies in Engineering for [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lasenby grew up in [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool), and entered Trinity College, Cambridge in 1978, as one of the first women to enter the college. She read mathematics there, entered graduate study at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University), returned to Cambridge for [Part III of the Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos), and completed a Ph.D., on radio and molecular studies of the galactic centre, through Cambridge's [Cavendish Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavendish_Laboratory).

Before taking her present position, she held a Junior Research Fellowship in [Trinity Hall, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge) from 1987 until 1990, did a year of postdoctoral research at the [Marconi Research Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marconi_Research_Centre), and held a Royal Society University Research Fellowship for research into Applications of Geometric Algebra in Engineering in [Newnham College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge), from 1994 until 2000.

## Books {#Books}

Lasenby has edited two books on [geometric algebra](wikipedia://en/12939):

- *Applications of Geometric Algebra in Computer Science and Engineering* (with Leo Dorst and Chris Doran, Springer, 2002)
- *Guide to Geometric Algebra in Practice* (with Leo Dorst, Springer, 2011)
