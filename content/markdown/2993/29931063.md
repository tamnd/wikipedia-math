# Uniqueness case

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniqueness case"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), the **uniqueness case** is one of the three possibilities for [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519) given by the [trichotomy theorem](wikipedia://en/29598798).

:::

The uniqueness case covers groups *G* of characteristic 2 type with *e*(*G*) ≥ 3 that have an almost strongly *p*-embedded maximal 2-local subgroup for all [primes](wikipedia://en/23666) *p* whose 2-local *p*-rank is sufficiently large (usually at least 3). (txt) [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) that there are no [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) in the uniqueness case.
