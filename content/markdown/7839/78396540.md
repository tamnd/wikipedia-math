# Tanguy Rivoal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tanguy Rivoal"}

**Tanguy Rivoal** (born 1972)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and related fields. He is known for his work on [transcendental numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendental_numbers), [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), and [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558). He currently holds the position of *Directeur de recherche* (Research Director) at the [Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (CNRS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) and is affiliated with the [Université Grenoble Alpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Rivoal obtained his Ph.D. from the [Université de Caen Normandie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Normandy) in 2001 under the supervision of [Francesco Amoroso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Amoroso). His dissertation was titled *Propriétés diophantiennes de la fonction zêta de Riemann aux entiers impairs* (*Diophantine properties of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) at odd integers*).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Rivoal's research focuses on several areas of mathematics, including [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558), [Padé approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padé_approximation), arithmetic [Gevrey series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gevrey_series), values of the [Gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316), [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698), and [E-function](wikipedia://en/7735427). His notable contributions include the proof that there is at least one irrational number among nine numbers ζ(5), ζ(7), ζ(9), ζ(11), ..., ζ(21), where ζ is the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809).[^o8]

Together with [Keith Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Martin_Ball), Rivoal proved that an infinite number of values of ζ at odd integers are linearly independent over $\mathbb{Q}$, for which he was elected an Honorary Fellow of the [Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599)-[Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) Society.[^o9][^o10] They also proved that there exists an odd number *j* such that 1, ζ(3), and ζ(*j*) are linear independent over $\mathbb{Q}$ where *2 \< j \< 170*, a specific case of the more general folklore conjecture stating that $\pi$, ζ(3), ζ(5), ζ(7), ζ(9), ..., are algebraically independent over $\mathbb{Q}$, which is a consequence of [Grothendieck's period conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck's_period_conjecture) for mixed Tate [motives](wikipedia://en/430680).[^o11][^o12][^o13]
