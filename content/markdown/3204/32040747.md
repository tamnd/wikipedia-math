# Affine braid group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Affine braid group"}

In mathematics, an **affine braid group** is a [braid group](wikipedia://en/550138) associated to an affine [Coxeter system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_system). Their [group rings](wikipedia://en/349223) have quotients called [affine Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/10361630). They are subgroups of [double affine braid groups](wikipedia://en/32034473).
