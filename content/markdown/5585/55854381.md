# Robert de Montessus de Ballore

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert de Montessus de Ballore"}

**Robert Fernand Bernard, Viscount de Montessus de Ballore** (20 May 1870, in [Villeurbanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villeurbanne) – 26 January 1937, in [Arcachon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcachon)) was a French mathematician, known for his work on [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199) and [Padé approximants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padé_approximants).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Robert de Montessus was a [viscount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscount), born to a noble family originating in the [Ancien Régime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancien_Régime).

His father Philippe-Georges de Montessus de Ballore (1825–1890) was an officer who was trained at [Saint Cyr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Cyr-l'École) and then resigned from the army to manage a farm in [Charolais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charolais,_France).

Robert had three brothers:

- Fernand de Montessus de Ballore (1851–1923), graduate of l'[École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique), seismologist and the first director of the Seismological Service of Chile;[^o6]
- Jean (1852–1903), a magistrate;
- Henri (1862–1918), professor of physics at Grenoble.

In 1886, Robert obtained his bachelor of science degree. From 1887 to 1889, he attended preparatory classes at l'[École des mines de Saint-Étienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_mines_de_Saint-Étienne). On 8 May 1905, at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), he successfully defended his thesis on continued fractions, written under the supervision of [Paul Appell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Appell).[^o7]

On 29 March 1906, he married Suzanne Montaudon (1884–1983). Their marriage produced four children: Simone (1907–?), Jacques (1909–2003), André (1912–1978) and Geneviève (deceased at birth in 1916).

Robert de Montessus was an editor of the *[Journal de mathématiques pures et appliquées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_de_mathématiques_pures_et_appliquées)* and the author of numerous mathematical publications. He was a member of the *[Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205)* and a member of the *Société des arts, sciences, belles-lettres et d'agriculture de l'Académie de Mâcon*.
