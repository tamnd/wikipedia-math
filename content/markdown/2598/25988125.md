# First-order

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="First-order"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and other [formal sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_sciences), **first-order** or **first order** most often means either:

:::

- "[linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear)" (a polynomial of degree at most one), as in [first-order approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-order_approximation) and other calculus uses, where it is contrasted with "polynomials of higher degree", or
- "without self-reference", as in [first-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983) and other logic uses, where it is contrasted with "allowing some self-reference" (higher-order logic)

In detail, it may refer to:
