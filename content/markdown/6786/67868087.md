# Grigori Tseitin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Grigori Tseitin"}

**Grigori Samuilovitsch Tseitin** (Григорий Самуилович Цейтин, born November 15, 1936, in Leningrad, USSR, deceased August 27, 2022 in Campbell, CA, USA) was a Russian mathematician and computer scientist, who moved to the United States in 1999. He is best known for [Tseitin transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tseytin_transformation) used in [SAT solvers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAT_solver), Tseitin tautologies used in the proof complexity theory, and for his work on [Algol 68](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algol_68).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Tseitin studied mathematics at the Leningrad State University (now [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University)) from 1951 to 1956. He earned his PhD in 1960 under the supervision of Andrei Andreevich Markov. His dissertation was called "Algorithmic Operators on Constructive Complete Separable Metric Spaces".[^o5] In 1968, he received the Russian doctoral degree (corresponding to a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)) from the same university. From 1960 to 2000 Tseitin worked at the Smirnov Research Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics at SPbSU[^o6] and taught classes in computer science at his alma mater.

In 2006, Tseitin was recognized as an [ACM Distinguished Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_distinguished_members_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery).[^o7]
