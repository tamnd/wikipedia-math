# Bruno N Rémillard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bruno N Rémillard"}

**Bruno N Rémillard** (born July 7, 1961) is a Canadian mathematical statistician and an honorary professor at [HEC Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Montréal). He is the 2019 Gold Medalist of the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327)[^o3] and was inducted as a 2019 Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o4] Rémillard was President of the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) in 2022-23.[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rémillard was born in [Saint-Raphaël, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Raphaël,_Quebec), Canada. He received a [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSc) and a [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSc) in mathematics from [Université Laval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Laval) in 1983 and 1985 and two years later a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in probability at [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) under the supervision of [Donald A. Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_A._Dawson). His dissertation was entitled "Large deviations and laws of the iterated logarithm for multidimensional diffusion processes with applications to diffusion processes with random coefficients."[^o7] and earned him the Pierre-Robillard Award from the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) that recognizes the best PhD thesis defended in the relevant fields at a Canadian university in a given year.[^o8] After working as an NSERC postdoctoral fellow at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), Rémillard joined the [Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_du_Québec_à_Trois-Rivières) in 1988. He was promoted to the rank of Associate in 1992 and became full professor in 1996. He settled at [HEC Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Montréal) in 2001.

## Publications {#Publications}

Rémillard has authored over 85 published papers, mostly in high-caliber international journals, in the fields of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), and [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) modelling. He has co-authored with [Christian Genest](wikipedia://en/41992626) nearly 20 papers in [The Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748), [The Annals of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Annals_of_Statistics), [Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_%28journal%29), [Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178), [Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313), [Journal of Multivariate Analysis](wikipedia://en/35997749), etc.[^o10] He is one of the 23 scientists to have published in the four IMS Annals.[^o11] He is the author of a graduate monograph and co-authored of three other books, including two undergraduate textbooks:

- Corina Reischer, Raymond Leblanc, Bruno Rémillard, and Denis Larocque, *Théorie des probabilités: Problèmes et solutions.*, Presses de l'Université du Québec, Montréal, Canada, 2002 [^o12]
- Pierre Duchesne and Bruno Rémillard, *Statistical modeling and analysis for complex data problems*, GERAD 25th Anniversary Series, Springer: New York, 2005 [^o13]
- Pierre Del Moral, Bruno Rémillard, and Sylvain Rubenthaler, *Une introduction aux probabilités*, Éditions Ellipses, 2006 [^o14]
- B. Rémillard, *Statistical methods for financial engineering*, Chapman and Hall/CRC, 2013.[^o15]
