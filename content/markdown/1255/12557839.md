::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="6-cubic honeycomb"}

The **6-cubic honeycomb** or **hexeractic honeycomb** is the only regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 6-space.

:::

It is analogous to the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) of the plane and to the [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) of 3-space.

::: {#o7 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are many different [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) of this [honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honeycomb). The most symmetric form is [regular](wikipedia://en/518693), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3<sup>4</sup>,4}. Another form has two alternating [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462) facets (like a [checkerboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checkerboard)) with Schläfli symbol {4,3<sup>3</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>}. The lowest symmetry [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) has 64 types of facets around each vertex and a prismatic product Schläfli symbol {∞}<sup>(6)</sup>.

:::

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The \[4,3<sup>4</sup>,4\], , Coxeter group generates 127 permutations of uniform tessellations, 71 with unique symmetry and 70 with unique geometry. The [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) 6-cubic honeycomb is geometrically identical to the 6-cubic honeycomb.

The *6-cubic honeycomb* can be [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) into the [6-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12557781), replacing the 6-cubes with [6-demicubes](wikipedia://en/10275945), and the alternated gaps are filled by [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518) facets.

::: {#o10 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Trirectified 6-cubic honeycomb"}

### Trirectified 6-cubic honeycomb {#Trirectified_6-cubic_honeycomb}

A **trirectified 6-cubic honeycomb**, , contains all [birectified 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_6-orthoplex) facets and is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [D<sub>6</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-demicube_honeycomb#D6_lattice). Facets can be identically colored from a doubled ${\tilde{C}}_6$×2, \[4,3<sup>4</sup>,4\] symmetry, alternately colored from ${\tilde{C}}_6$, \[4,3<sup>4</sup>,4\] symmetry, three colors from ${\tilde{B}}_6$, \[4,3<sup>3</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry, and 4 colors from ${\tilde{D}}_6$, \[3<sup>1,1</sup>,3,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry.

:::
