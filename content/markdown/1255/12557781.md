::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="6-demicubic honeycomb"}

The **6-demicubic honeycomb** or **demihexeractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 6-space. It is constructed as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the regular [6-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-cube_honeycomb).

:::

It is composed of two different types of [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29). The [6-cubes](wikipedia://en/10083462) become alternated into [6-demicubes](wikipedia://en/10275945) h{4,3,3,3,3} and the alternated vertices create [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518) {3,3,3,3,4} facets.

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="D6 lattice"}

## D6 lattice {#D6_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the **6-demicubic honeycomb** is the **D<sub>6</sub> lattice**.[^o10] The 60 vertices of the [rectified 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_6-orthoplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the *6-demicubic honeycomb* reflect the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 60 of this lattice.[^o11] The best known is 72, from the [E<sub>6</sub> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E6_lattice) and the [2<sub>22</sub> honeycomb](wikipedia://en/19582093).

The D+ lattice (also called D2) can be constructed by the union of two D<sub>6</sub> lattices. This packing is only a lattice for even dimensions. The kissing number is 2<sup>5</sup>=32 (2<sup>n-1</sup> for n\<8, 240 for n=8, and 2n(n-1) for n>8).[^o12]

∪

The D\* lattice (also called D4 and C2) can be constructed by the union of all four 6-demicubic lattices:[^o13] It is also the 6-dimensional [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic), the union of two [6-cube honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-cube_honeycomb) in dual positions.

∪ ∪ ∪ = ∪ .

The [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) of the D<sub>6</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice is 12 (*2n* for n≥5).[^o14] and its [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) is a [trirectified 6-cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_6-cubic_honeycomb), , containing all [birectified 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_6-orthoplex) [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell), .[^o15]

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are three uniform construction symmetries of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by arrangements of different colors on the 64 [6-demicube](wikipedia://en/10275945) facets around each vertex.
