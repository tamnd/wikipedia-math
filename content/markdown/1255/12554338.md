# Cryptology Research Society of India

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptology Research Society of India"}

**Cryptology Research Society of India (CRSI)** is a scientific organisation that supports research in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) on [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), [data security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_security), and related fields.[^o11][^o3] The organisation was founded in 2001.[^o3] CRSI organises workshops and conferences about cryptology.[^o4]

:::

## Activities {#Activities}

*Image: Asiacrypt06.jpg.* Asiacrypt 2005 organised by CRSI associating with IITM in Taj hotel, Chennai.

CRSI organises several annual workshops and conferences about cryptology.[^o6] More specifically, CRSI organises the annual events [INDOCRYPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INDOCRYPT), an international conference on cryptography,[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10] and the Indian national workshop on cryptology.[^o11][^o12] It also arranged the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043)'s (IACR) workshop on [Fast Software Encryption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_Software_Encryption)[^o13] in 2003 at [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi) and IACR's conference [Asiacrypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiacrypt) in 2013[^o14] and in 2005 at [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai).

In 2024, CRSI hosted ASIACRYPT 2024 in Kolkata from December 9 to 13, followed by the ASK 2024 workshop at TCG CREST, Kolkata, from December 14 to 17.[^o15]

In 2025, CRSI held the International Conference on Cryptology in India (INDOCRYPT 2025) at the [International Institute of Information Technology, Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Institute_of_Information_Technology,_Bhubaneswar) on December 15.[^o16]

## Organizational structure {#Organizational_structure}

The main office of CRSI is located in [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata).[^o18] CRSI was founded by the current general secretary Prof. Bimal Roy, former Director of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418).[^o19] Padmashree R. Balasubramaniam, , is the organization's president.[^o20][^o21]
