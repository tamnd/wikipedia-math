# Balding–Nichols model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Balding–Nichols model"}

In [population genetics](wikipedia://en/219268), the **Balding–Nichols model** is a statistical description of the [allele frequencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allele_frequency) in the components of a sub-divided population.[^o2] With background allele frequency *p* the allele frequencies, in sub-populations separated by [Wright's](wikipedia://en/1700593) *[F<sub>ST</sub>](wikipedia://en/4196163)* *F*, are distributed according to independent draws from

:::

$$B\left(\frac{1-F}{F}p,\frac{1-F}{F}(1-p)\right)$$

where *B* is the [Beta distribution](wikipedia://en/207074). This distribution has mean *p* and variance *Fp*(1 – *p*).[^o3]

The model is due to [David Balding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Balding) and [Richard Nichols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_A._Nichols) and is widely used in the forensic analysis of [DNA profiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_profile) and in population models for [genetic epidemiology](wikipedia://en/16017459).
