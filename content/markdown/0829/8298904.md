# International Centre for Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="International Centre for Mathematical Sciences"}

The **International Centre for Mathematical Sciences** (**ICMS**) is a mathematical research centre based in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh). According to its website, the centre is "designed to bring together mathematicians and practitioners in science, industry and commerce for research workshops and other meetings."

:::

The centre was jointly established in 1990 by the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University), under the supervision of Professor [Elmer Rees](wikipedia://en/157248), with initial support from [Edinburgh District Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_District_Council), the [Scottish Development Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Development_Agency) and the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics).[^o3] Its current operations are primarily funded by grants from the [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) of the UK.

In April 1994 the Centre moved to 14 India Street, Edinburgh, the birthplace of [James Clerk Maxwell](wikipedia://en/28989696) and home of the [James Clerk Maxwell Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Clerk_Maxwell_Foundation). In 2010 it was relocated to 15 South College Street to accommodate larger events. As of 2020, the ICMS is located within the newly established Bayes centre.[^o4][^o5]

The current scientific director (appointed in 2021) is Professor [Minhyong Kim](wikipedia://en/30994594). The ICMS is a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).[^o6]

## Premises {#Premises}

From April 1994, the Centre rented from the James Clerk Maxwell Foundation accommodation at 14, India Street, the birthplace of [James Clerk Maxwell](wikipedia://en/28989696). Increased activity necessitated removal in 2010 to a converted church in South College Street, and then in 2018 to its present location in the nearby [Bayes Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Informatics,_University_of_Edinburgh) of the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh).
