# Heinz-Dieter Ebbinghaus

*Image: Heinz Dieter Ebbinghaus.jpg.* Ebbinghaus in [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover), 1974

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz-Dieter Ebbinghaus"}

**Heinz-Dieter Ebbinghaus** (born 22 February 1939 in [Hemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemer), [Province of Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Westphalia)) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He received his PhD in 1967 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) under [Hans Hermes](wikipedia://en/41139401) and [Dieter Rödding](wikipedia://en/66658943).[^o2]

:::

Ebbinghaus has written various books on [logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), including a seminal work on [Ernst Zermelo](wikipedia://en/178655). His book *Einführung in die mathematische Logik*, joint work with Jörg Flum and Wolfgang Thomas, first appeared in 1978 and became a standard textbook of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) in the German-speaking area. It is currently in its sixth edition (). An English edition of *Mathematical Logic* was published in the [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag) [Undergraduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871375) series in 1984 (), with a second edition in 1994 () and a third edition in 2021 ().
