# Javad Mashreghi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Javad Mashreghi"}

**Javad Mashreghi** is a mathematician and author working in [function space](wikipedia://en/340630) theory, [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He is a professeur titulaire at [Université Laval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Laval) and was the 35th President of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) (2020–2022).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Mashreghi was born in [Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashan), Iran, in 1968. He studied electrical engineering, electronics, (B.Sc., 1991), and pure mathematics (M.Sc., 1993) at the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran). He moved to Canada in 1996 and earned his Ph.D. from the [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in 2001. Since then he lives in the Province of [Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec).

## Service to Canadian mathematical community {#Service_to_Canadian_mathematical_community}

Mashreghi is immensely involved in various aspects of North America's mathematical community, having served on numerous editorial, administrative and selection committees all across Canada and the U.S. ([CMS](wikipedia://en/906055), [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822), [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), CRM, AARMS, [NSERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSERC), [FQRNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FQRNT), NSF). He was the 35th President of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) (2020–2022),[^o5] and is currently the editor-in-chief of the *[Canadian Mathematical Bulletin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Mathematical_Bulletin)* (2020–2025) and *[Concrete Operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_Operators)* (2018–2025).
