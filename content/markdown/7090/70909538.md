# David Guwatudde

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Guwatudde"}

*Image: Guwatudde2- 21Dec18.jpg.* Guwatudde David

**Prof David Guwatudde** is a Ugandan academic and researcher. He is currently a professor of Epidemiology and Biostatistics in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the School of Public Health, [Makerere University College of Health Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makerere_University_College_of_Health_Sciences).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Background and education {#Background_and_education}

He obtained his Msc in statistics from the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton), UK, and his PhD from the [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University), Cleveland, Ohio, USA.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

He is a researcher whose areas of expertise include epidemiology of hypertension, epidemiology of diabetes, evaluation of effectiveness of interventions for the prevention, management and control of high burden diseases, especially non-communicable diseases, and [capacity building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capacity_building) through tertiary training.[^o8] His works have been highly used with 4,361 citations, h-index of 34 and i-10 index of 41.[^o9] Some of his highly cited works include; Injury patterns in rural and urban Uganda,[^o10] The state of hypertension care in 44 low-income and middle-income countries: a cross-sectional study of nationally representative individual-level data from 1· 1 million adults,[^o11] Tuberculosis in household contacts of infectious cases in Kampala, Uganda,[^o12] The burden of hypertension in sub-Saharan Africa: a four-country cross sectional study,[^o13] Citywide trauma experience in [Kampala, Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala,_Uganda): a call for intervention,[^o14] The epidemiology of hypertension in Uganda: findings from the national non-communicable diseases risk factor survey,[^o15] Prevalence factors associated with hypertension in [Rukungiri district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rukungiri_district), Uganda-a community-based study,[^o16] Urban malaria: primary caregivers' knowledge, attitudes, practices and predictors of malaria incidence in a cohort of Ugandan children,[^o17] Health system performance for people with diabetes in 28 low-and middle-income countries: a cross-sectional study of nationally representative surveys,[^o18] Diabetes and pre-diabetes among persons aged 35 to 60 years in eastern Uganda: prevalence and associated factors,[^o19] *Mycobacterium africanum* Subtype II Is Associated with Two Distinct Genotypes and Is a Major Cause of Human Tuberculosis in Kampala, Uganda,[^o20] Prevalence and correlates of diabetes mellitus in Uganda: a population‐based national survey,[^o21] Diabetes diagnosis and care in sub-Saharan Africa: pooled analysis of individual data from 12 countries[^o22] and Looking at non-communicable diseases in Uganda through a local lens: an analysis using locally derived data.[^o23]
