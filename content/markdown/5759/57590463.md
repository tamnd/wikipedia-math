# William Shadish

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Shadish"}

**William Raymond Shadish Jr.** (November 3, 1949 – March 27, 2016) was an American psychologist and statistician who was a distinguished professor and founding faculty member at the [University of California, Merced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Merced). He was known for his work in the field of [behavioral science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_science), especially on the topics of [program evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Program_evaluation), [causal inference](wikipedia://en/37103476), [meta-analysis](wikipedia://en/62329), and the study of [methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology).[^o8]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Shadish was born on November 3, 1949, in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), to William Shadish Sr. and Maryjane Cartmell. Shadish Jr.'s father was a [Korean War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_War) veteran who spent 30 months as a [prisoner of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_war) in [North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea).[^o6] Shadish Jr. was raised in [Redding, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redding,_California),[^o4] and received his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology) from [Santa Clara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_University) in 1972. He went on to attend [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), where he received his master's degree and Ph.D. in Clinical Psychology[^o5] in 1975 and 1978, respectively.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

After teaching at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis) for many years, Shadish joined the faculty of the [University of California, Merced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Merced) (UC-Merced) in 2003. There, he designed the psychology major at the then-brand-new university, which did not have any students or buildings when he first joined its faculty. He also led the process that created the Ph.D. in psychology program at UC-Merced.[^o6] He became the second ever distinguished professor at UC-Merced, and received the Distinguished Research Award from their Academic Senate in 2011.[^o8]

## Affiliations with learned societies {#Affiliations_with_learned_societies}

Shadish was the founding secretary-treasurer of the [Society for Research Synthesis Methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Research_Synthesis_Methodology), later serving as its president from 2013 to 2014. He was also elected president of the [American Evaluation Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Evaluation_Association) in 1996, and of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) in 2014. He was a fellow of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association).[^o6][^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Shadish received the 1994 [Paul F. Lazarsfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_F._Lazarsfeld) Award for [Evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaluation) Theory and the 2000 [Robert Ingle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Ingle) Award from the American Evaluation Association. He also received two Outstanding Research Publication Awards (in 1994 and 1996) from the [American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_Marriage_and_Family_Therapy), the 2002 [Donald T. Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_T._Campbell) Award for Innovations in Methodology from the [Policy Studies Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Policy_Studies_Organization), and the 2009 [Frederick Mosteller](wikipedia://en/6125615) Award for Lifetime Contributions to Systematic Reviews from the [Campbell Collaboration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campbell_Collaboration).[^o12]

## Death {#Death}

Shadish died at his home in [Mariposa, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariposa,_California), on March 27, 2016, of complications from [prostate cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostate_cancer).[^o6]
