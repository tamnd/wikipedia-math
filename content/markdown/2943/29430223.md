::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="B6 polytope"}

In 6-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 64 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821728) with B<sub>6</sub> symmetry. There are two regular forms, the [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518), and [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462) with 12 and 64 vertices respectively. The 6-demicube is added with half the symmetry.

:::

They can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the B<sub>6</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 64 polytopes can be made in the B<sub>6</sub>, B<sub>5</sub>, B<sub>4</sub>, B<sub>3</sub>, B<sub>2</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *\[k+1\]* symmetry, and B<sub>k</sub> has *\[2k\]* symmetry.

These 64 polytopes are each shown in these 8 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position.
