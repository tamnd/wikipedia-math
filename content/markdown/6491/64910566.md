# Michael Handel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Handel"}

**Michael Handel** is an American mathematician known for his work in [Geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164). He is a professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) and a professor of mathematics at the [CUNY Graduate Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUNY_Graduate_Center).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Michael Handel graduated with a B.A. in mathematics from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1971.[^o4] He received his Ph.D. from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 1975 under the supervision of [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424). From 1975 to 1978, he was an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He joined the faculty of [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) as an assistant professor in 1978,[^o6] and was promoted to associate professor in 1983.[^o7] Handel was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1978 to 1979, and again from 1987 to 1988.[^o8] In 1990, he joined the Mathematics Department at Lehman College.

Handel is best known for developing the [Train track map](wikipedia://en/20304533) method in [Geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) in collaboration with [Mladen Bestvina](wikipedia://en/26130054) in 1992.[^o9] Bestvina, Feighn and Handel later proved that the group Out(*F<sub>n</sub>*) satisfies the [Tits alternative](wikipedia://en/12704641), settling a long-standing open problem.[^o10][^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 1984, Handel won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o13]
- In 2014, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14][^o15]
