# Rob Schneiderman (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rob Schneiderman"}

**Robert Roland "Rob" Schneiderman** (born June 21, 1957) is an American jazz pianist who also works as a professor of mathematics at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), where he specializes in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732).[^o4]

:::

## Music career {#Music_career}

Schneiderman's professional jazz career began in [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego) from about age 16, when he played piano for visiting soloists such as [Eddie Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Harris), [Sonny Stitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonny_Stitt), [Harold Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Land), [Charles McPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_McPherson_%28musician%29) and [Peter Sprague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sprague).[^o6] He continued to collaborate intermittently with Harris, until the latter's death in 1996, and with McPherson. In 1982, Schneiderman moved to New York, where he performed and toured with such musicians as [J.J. Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.J._Johnson), [Chet Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chet_Baker), [Art Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Farmer), [Clifford Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Jordan), [James Moody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Moody_%28saxophonist%29) and [Zoot Sims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoot_Sims). A performance fellowship from the [National Endowment for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Endowment_for_the_Arts) in 1987 featured Schneiderman with [George Coleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Coleman), [Jimmy Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Heath), [Claudio Roditi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Roditi), and [Slide Hampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slide_Hampton).[^o7] The collaboration with Slide Hampton resulted in his debut album *[New Outlook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Outlook)*, the first of ten recordings to date as a leader for the [Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Records) music label.[^o9]

His most recent release, entitled *[Tone Twister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tone_Twister)*, is a collaboration with [Brian Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Lynch_%28musician%29) on trumpet and [Ralph Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Moore) on tenor saxophone. The album features [Gerald L. Cannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_L._Cannon) on bass and Pete Van Nostrand on drums. Schneiderman has also played as sidemen for [Billy Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Higgins), [Rufus Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus_Reid), [Brian Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Lynch_%28musician%29), [Ralph Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Moore), [Peter Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Washington), [Lewis Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Nash), [Akira Tana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Tana), [Billy Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Hart), [Gary Smulyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Smulyan) and [Ben Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Riley).

As a jazz educator, he has been in residence at the [Stanford Jazz Workshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Jazz_Workshop).[^o11] He was previously an adjunct professor in the jazz departments of the [William Paterson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Paterson_University) (with [Rufus Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus_Reid)) and [Queens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens_College) (with [Jimmy Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Heath)). He has also been on the faculty of the [Jazzschool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Jazz_Conservatory) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o6]

## Education and academic career {#Education_and_academic_career}

Schneiderman graduated with a B.A. in mathematics from [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York) in 1994. He received his Ph.D. from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2001 under the supervision of [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424). In 2006, Schneiderman became an assistant professor at the Department of Mathematics [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) after stints at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), and at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). From 2013, he has been an associate professor at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College).[^o14] He has also served as chair of the Mathematics Department since 2019.[^o4]

Schneiderman also actively works at the interface of music and mathematics.[^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18]
