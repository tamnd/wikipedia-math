# Tadao Oda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tadao Oda"}

**Tadao Oda** (born 1940, [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto)) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the field of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), especially [toric varieties](wikipedia://en/737087). The field of toric varieties was developed by [Demazure](wikipedia://en/32535595), [Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594), Miyake, Oda and others in the 1970s. He is also known for a book on toric varieties: *Convex Bodies and Algebraic Geometry: An Introduction to the Theory of Toric Varieties.*[^o2]

:::

In 1958 Oda graduated from [Tokai High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokai_High_School) in [Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya), Japan, where [Shigefumi Mori](wikipedia://en/794811) and Hisasi Morikawa also graduated from. He earned his bachelor's degree from [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1962, and five years later earned a Ph.D. under David Mumford from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) with thesis *Abelian varieties over a perfect field and Dieudonné Modules*. After completing his Ph.D., Oda was an associate professor at [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University) and became a professor at [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University) in 1975. He remained at the university for 28 years. He is an emeritus professor at [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University). Oda wrote "Algebraic Geometry, Sendai, 1985" with Hisasi Morikawa, a former professor at [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University).

Tadao has belonged to the [Kiwanis Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiwanis_Club) of Sendai since 1999 and was elected to a three-year term as a trustee of Kiwanis International in 2008.[^o4]
