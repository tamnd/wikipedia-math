# Joris van der Hoeven

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joris van der Hoeven"}

van der Hoeven

**Joris van der Hoeven** (born 1971) is a Dutch mathematician and computer scientist, specializing in [algebraic analysis](wikipedia://en/20846707) and [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805). He is the primary developer of [GNU TeXmacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_TeXmacs).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Joris van der Hoeven received in 1997 his doctorate from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (Paris 7) with thesis *Asymptotique automatique*. He is a [*Directeur de recherche*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_academic_ranks#France) at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) and head of the team *Max Modélisation algébrique* at the Laboratoire d'informatique of the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

His research deals with [transseries](wikipedia://en/58151408) (*i.e.* generalizations of [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012)) with applications to algebraic analysis and asymptotic solutions of [nonlinear differential equations](wikipedia://en/146103#Nonlinear_differential_equations). In addition to transseries' properties as part of [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), he also examines their algorithmic aspects as well as those of classical complex function theory.

He is the main developer of [GNU TeXmacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_TeXmacs) (a free scientific editing platform)[^o7] and Mathemagix (free software, a computer algebra and analysis system).[^o8]

In 2019, van der Hoeven and his coauthor David Harvey announced their discovery of the fastest known [multiplication algorithm](wikipedia://en/57411), allowing the multiplication of $n$-bit [binary numbers](wikipedia://en/238686) in time $O(n\log n)$.[^o9] Their paper was [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) and published in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)* in 2021.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018, he was an Invited Speaker (with [Matthias Aschenbrenner](wikipedia://en/44788496) and [Lou van den Dries](wikipedia://en/55143215)) with the talk *On numbers, germs, and transseries* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).[^o11][^o12] In 2018, the three received the [Karp Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karp_Prize).[^o13] In 2022, he received the N. G. de Bruijn prize[^o14]
