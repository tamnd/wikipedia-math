# Borel subalgebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Borel subalgebra"}

In mathematics, specifically in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **Borel subalgebra** of a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) $\mathfrak{g}$ is a maximal [solvable](wikipedia://en/2966308) subalgebra.[^o2] The notion is named after [Armand Borel](wikipedia://en/365672).

:::

If the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ is the Lie algebra of a [complex Lie group](wikipedia://en/2187847), then a Borel subalgebra is the Lie algebra of a [Borel subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356).

## Borel subalgebra associated to a flag {#Borel_subalgebra_associated_to_a_flag}

Let $\mathfrak g = \mathfrak{gl}(V)$ be the Lie algebra of the endomorphisms of a finite-dimensional vector space *V* over the complex numbers. Then to specify a Borel subalgebra of $\mathfrak g$ amounts to specify a [flag](wikipedia://en/390646) of *V*; given a flag $V = V_0 \supset V_1 \supset \cdots \supset V_n = 0$, the subspace $\mathfrak b = \{ x \in \mathfrak g \mid x(V_i) \subset V_i, 1 \le i \le n \}$ is a Borel subalgebra,[^o4] and conversely, each Borel subalgebra is of that form by [Lie's theorem](wikipedia://en/2975251). Hence, the Borel subalgebras are classified by the [flag variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_variety) of *V*.

::: {#o6 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Primitive element (Lie algebra)"}

## Borel subalgebra relative to a base of a root system {#Borel_subalgebra_relative_to_a_base_of_a_root_system}

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587), $\mathfrak h$ a [Cartan subalgebra](wikipedia://en/1336000) and *R* the [root system](wikipedia://en/277087) associated to them. Choosing a base of *R* gives the notion of positive roots. Then $\mathfrak g$ has the decomposition $\mathfrak g = \mathfrak n^- \oplus \mathfrak h \oplus \mathfrak n^+$ where $\mathfrak n^{\pm} = \sum_{\alpha > 0} \mathfrak{g}_{\pm \alpha}$. Then $\mathfrak b = \mathfrak h \oplus \mathfrak n^+$ is the Borel subalgebra relative to the above setup.[^o7] (It is solvable since the derived algebra $[\mathfrak b, \mathfrak b]$ is nilpotent. It is maximal solvable by a [theorem of Borel–Morozov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theorem_of_Borel–Morozov) on the conjugacy of solvable subalgebras.[^o8])

Given a $\mathfrak g$-module *V*, a **primitive element** of *V* is a (nonzero) vector that (1) is a weight vector for $\mathfrak h$ and that (2) is annihilated by $\mathfrak{n}^+$. It is the same thing as a $\mathfrak b$-weight vector (Proof: if $h \in \mathfrak h$ and $e \in \mathfrak{n}^+$ with $[h, e] = 2e$ and if $\mathfrak{b} \cdot v$ is a line, then $0 = [h, e] \cdot v = 2 e \cdot v$.)

:::
