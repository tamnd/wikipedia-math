# Kiyoshi Igusa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kiyoshi Igusa"}

**Kiyoshi Igusa** (born November 28, 1949) is a Japanese-American mathematician and a professor at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University). He works in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He studied at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where he obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1979, under the direction of [Allen Hatcher](wikipedia://en/1112366) and [Wu-Chung Hsiang](wikipedia://en/50980728).[^o3]

From 1981 to 1983, he was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship), and since 2012 he is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

In 1990, he gave an invited lecture at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Kyoto (Topology Section).[^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Igusa's father, [Jun-Ichi Igusa](wikipedia://en/33588064), was also a mathematician. Igusa is married to [Gordana Todorov](wikipedia://en/67313547),[^o8] with whom he is a frequent collaborator.[^o9]
