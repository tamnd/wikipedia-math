# Sandrine Péché

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sandrine Péché"}

**Sandrine Péché** (born 1977)[^o2] is a French mathematician who works as a professor in the Laboratoire de Probabilités, Statistique et Modélisation of [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University).[^o3] Her research concerns [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and the theory and applications of [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765).

:::

After studying at the [École normale supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Cachan),[^o2] Péché earned a Ph.D. from the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in Switzerland, in 2002, under the supervision of [Gérard Ben Arous](wikipedia://en/48269909). She taught at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Grenoble) before moving to Paris Diderot in 2011.[^o2]

She served as the editor-in-chief of *[Electronic Communications in Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Communications_in_Probability)* from 2015 to 2017.[^o5] She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o6]
