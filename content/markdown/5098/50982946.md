# Zoé Chatzidakis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zoé Chatzidakis"}

**Zoé Maria Chatzidakis** (3 April 1955 – 22 January 2025) was a French mathematician who worked as a director of research at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris, France.[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Chatzidakis was born on 3 April 1955.[^o5] She earned her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1984 from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), under the supervision of [Angus Macintyre](wikipedia://en/11551260), with a dissertation on the model theory of [profinite groups](wikipedia://en/24266).[^o6] She was Senior researcher and team director in [Algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) in the Département de mathématiques et applications de l'[École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure).[^o7][^o8]

Her research concerned [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) and [difference algebra](wikipedia://en/23992517). She was invited to give the [Tarski Lectures](wikipedia://en/54104355) in 2020, though the lectures were postponed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).[^o9]

Chatzidakis died on 22 January 2025 in Paris at the age of 69.[^o5]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

Chatzidakis was the 2013 winner of the [Leconte Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leconte_Prize),[^o11] and was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o12] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Chern Professor for Fall 2020.[^o13]
