# Katrin Wendland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Katrin Wendland"}

*Image: Katrin Wendland.jpg.* Wendland in 2010

**Katrin Wendland** (born 1970)[^o2] is a German [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) who works as a professor at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin).[^o3]

:::

Wendland earned a diploma in mathematics from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1996, and a PhD in physics from the University of Bonn in 2000, under the supervision of [Werner Nahm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Nahm). After being a lecturer and then senior lecturer at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) from 2002 to 2006, she returned to Germany as a professor at the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg), where she held the Chair for Analysis and Geometry. She moved to [Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) in 2011 and then to Trinity College Dublin in 2022.[^o3]

In 2009, Wendland was given the Medal for special merits for Bavaria in a united Europe by the Bavarian government.[^o3] In 2010 she was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065), with a talk entitled "On the geometry of singularities in quantum field theories". In 2012 she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4] She was elected to the [Akademie der Wissenschaften und der Literatur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademie_der_Wissenschaften_und_der_Literatur) in 2013.[^o3] In 2023 she was elected a fellow of Trinity College Dublin.[^o5]
