# Cochran

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cochran"}

**Cochran** is a [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) of [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) (and most likely of Cumbric) origin. The earliest known appearance is in Dumbartonshire (14th cent). The definition is unclear, however, the name may be derived from the extinct [Cumbric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbric) language, which is closely related to the [Welsh language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language).[^o4] At the time of the [British census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_census) of 1881,[^o3] its relative frequency was highest in [Renfrewshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renfrewshire_%28historic%29) (34.3 times the British average), followed by [Wigtownshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigtownshire), [Ayrshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrshire), [Dunbartonshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunbartonshire), [Lanarkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanarkshire), [Buteshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buteshire), [Stirlingshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stirlingshire), [Argyll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argyll), [Kirkcudbrightshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirkcudbrightshire) and [Forfarshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forfarshire). The Cochrans are traditionally mainly a Western [Lowlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Lowlands) family.

:::
