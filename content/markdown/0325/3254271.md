# Faculty of Mathematics, University of Cambridge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Faculty of Mathematics, University of Cambridge"}

*Image: CMS Central Core 01.jpg.* Centre for Mathematical Sciences, Cambridge

The **Faculty of Mathematics** at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) comprises the **Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics** (**DPMMS**) and the **Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics** (**DAMTP**). It is housed in the [Centre for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Cambridge%29) site in West [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge), alongside the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837). Many distinguished mathematicians have been members of the faculty.

## Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics {#Pure_Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Statistics}

The Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics (DPMMS) was created in 1964 under the headship of [Sir William Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430).[^o8] It was housed in a converted warehouse at 16 Mill Lane, adjacent to its sister department DAMTP, until its move around 2000 to the present [Centre for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Cambridge%29) where it occupies Pavilions C, D, and E.

### Statistical Laboratory {#Statistical_Laboratory}

The Statistical Laboratory is a Sub-Department of DPMMS.[^o11] It was created in 1947 with accommodation in a "temporary hut", and was established on 21 March 1953 within the Faculty of Mathematics.[^o8] It moved in 1958 to the basement of the new Chemistry Department in Lensfield Road, and then formed part of the new Department (DPMMS) in Mill Lane on its creation in 1964. It occupies Pavilion D of the [Centre for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Cambridge%29).

## Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics {#Applied_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Physics}

*Image: Centre for mathematical sciences cambridge.jpg.* Department of Applied Mathematics & Theoretical Physics is based at [Centre for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Cambridge%29).

The Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (DAMTP) was founded by [George Batchelor](wikipedia://en/1763397) in 1959, and for many years was situated on Silver Street, in the former office buildings of [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o14] However since the opening of the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in 1991, the Department has been located at the [Centre for Mathematical Sciences (Cambridge)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Cambridge%29). Theoretical Physics (including [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology), [relativity](wikipedia://en/30001), and [high energy physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_energy_physics)) occupies most of Pavilion B, while Applied Mathematics (including [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [solid mechanics](wikipedia://en/458866)) occupies most of Pavilions F, G, and H.
