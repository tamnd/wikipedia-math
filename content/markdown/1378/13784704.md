# Franz Josef Radermacher

*Image: Franz Josef Radermacher, 2016 (cropped).jpg.* Franz Josef Radermacher (2016).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franz Josef Radermacher"}

**Franz Josef Radermacher** (born 20 March 1950) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist). He is Professor of [Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informatics_%28academic_field%29) at [Ulm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulm_University). He is one of the co-founders of the [Global Marshall Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Marshall_Plan) Initiative that suggests a socio-ecological plan to eradicate [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty), increasing global [wealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth) while protecting [natural resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_resources).

:::

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

Radermacher earned a PhD in Mathematics from [RWTH Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen) in 1974. He earned a second PhD in Economics from the [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe) in 1976.[^o3] From 1983 till 1987, Radermacher was Professor for Applied Computer Science at the [University of Passau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Passau). Since 1987 he is Professor for Artificial Intelligence and Databases at the [University of Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ulm). From 1988 till 1992 he was the president of the Society for Mathematics, Economics and Operations Research. Radermacher is an authority in the fields of [globalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Globalization), innovation, [overpopulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_overpopulation) and global [sustainable development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_development).

## Awards {#Awards}

He was awarded the Planetary Consciousness Prize in 2004 by the Club of Budapest.[^o5]
