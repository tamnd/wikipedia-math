# Stanisław Łojasiewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Łojasiewicz"}

**Stanisław Łojasiewicz** (Polish: '; 9 October 1926 – 14 November 2002) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor at the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) and a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) known for his work in [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198) and [theory of distributions](wikipedia://en/51955).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Stanisław Łojasiewicz was born in 1926 in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) to father Stanisław, who served as captain in the [Polish Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Armed_Forces). In 1945, he was admitted to the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University). During his studies he was particularly interested in the theory of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and was influenced by [Tadeusz Ważewski](wikipedia://en/1028319). In 1950, he was awarded a doctorate for his work in this branch of mathematics.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

At the end of the 1950s, Łojasiewicz solved the [problem of distribution division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problem_of_distribution_division) by [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478), introducing the [%C5%81ojasiewicz inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81ojasiewicz_inequality). Its solution opened the road to important results in the new theory of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). The method established by Łojasiewicz led him to advance the theory of [semianalytic sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semianalytic_set), which opened an important chapter in modern analysis.[^o3]

From 1956 to 1960, Łojasiewicz taught at universities in [Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario), [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) and the [Institute for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Studies) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey). In 1962, he became professor at the Jagiellonian University.[^o6] In 1967-68, he worked at the [Institut des Hautes Etudes Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Etudes_Scientifiques) where he produced an elegant proof of the [Malgrange preparation theorem](wikipedia://en/14188480).[^o8]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1970, Łojasiewicz delivered an invited address on the subject of [semianalytic geometry](wikipedia://en/28156210) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Nice, France.[^o8] In 1971, Łojasiewicz became a corresponding member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) and in 1980, its full member.[^o6]

In 1984, Łojasiewicz was awarded the [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122) Medal.[^o10] The following year, he accepted the [Jurzykowski Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurzykowski_Prize). In 1998, he became the recipient of the [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta) (Commander's Cross with Star).[^o11]

## Death and legacy {#Death_and_legacy}

On 14 November 2002, Łojasiewicz died of [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_attack) when returning from a scientific conference in Italy. He was buried at the [Rakowicki Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakowicki_Cemetery) in Kraków.[^o13]

The *Łojasiewicz Lectures*[^o6] are a series of annual lectures in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) given at the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) in honour of Łojasiewicz.
