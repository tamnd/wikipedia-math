# John Smith (anatomist and chemist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Smith"}

**John Smith** (1721–1797)[^o2] was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) physician and academic.[^o6]

:::

Smith was born in [Maybole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maybole), [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland), where his father, William, was a merchant.[^o2][^o3] He studied at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) beginning in 1736, entered [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford) in 1744 with the support of the [Snell Exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snell_Exhibition), and earned a B.A. in 1748 and an M.A. in 1751 from Balliol.[^o2][^o3] He then studied under [Nathan Alcock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Alcock) in [St Mary Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mary_Hall,_Oxford), earning his doctorate in 1757.[^o2][^o4] Alcock left Oxford for Bath in the same year, and Smith took his place.[^o4][^o5] At Oxford, he taught [anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatomy) and [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry).[^o6][^o5] Despite not being a mathematician, he held the [Savilian chair of geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_chair_of_geometry) from 1766 until his death in 1797.[^o6]
