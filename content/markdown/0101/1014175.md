# Michael Barnsley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Barnsley"}

**Michael Fielding Barnsley** (born 1946)[^o2] is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), researcher and an [entrepreneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneur) who has worked on [fractal compression](wikipedia://en/60022); he holds several [patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent) on the technology. He received his Ph.D. in theoretical chemistry from [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1972[^o3] and BA in mathematics from Oxford in 1968.[^o4] In 1987 he founded [Iterated Systems Incorporated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iterated_Systems_Incorporated), and in 1988 he published a book entitled *Fractals Everywhere* and in 2006 *SuperFractals*.

:::

He has also published these scientific papers: "Existence and Uniqueness of Orbital Measures", "Theory and Applications of Fractal Tops", "A Fractal Valued Random Iteration Algorithm and Fractal Hierarchy", "V-variable fractals and superfractals", "Fractal Transformations" and "Ergodic Theory, Fractal Tops and Colour Stealing".

He is also credited for discovering the [collage theorem](wikipedia://en/20967247).

Iterated Systems was initially devoted to [fractal image compression](wikipedia://en/60022) (epitomised by the [Barnsley fern](wikipedia://en/25721564)), and later focused on image archive management and was renamed to MediaBin. It was acquired in 2003 by Interwoven, by which time Barnsley was no longer affiliated with the company.

As of 2005, he is on the faculty of the [Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/14344543) of the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University).[^o5] Barnsley previously held a faculty position at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology).[^o6]

Michael Barnsley is the son of author [Gabriel Fielding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Fielding) (Alan Fielding Barnsley) and a descendant of [Henry Fielding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Fielding).
