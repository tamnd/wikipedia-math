# Edmond Laguerre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edmond Laguerre"}

**Edmond Nicolas Laguerre** (9 April 1834, [Bar-le-Duc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-le-Duc) – 14 August 1886, Bar-le-Duc) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)[^o3] and a member of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences) (1885). His main works were in the areas of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He also investigated [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) (see [Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917)). [Laguerre's method](wikipedia://en/1136614) is a [root-finding algorithm](wikipedia://en/153299) tailored to [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000). He laid the foundations of a geometry of oriented spheres (Laguerre geometry and [Laguerre plane](wikipedia://en/38234611)), including the [Laguerre transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguerre_transformation) or [transformation by reciprocal directions](wikipedia://en/42475403#Transformation_by_reciprocal_directions).

:::
