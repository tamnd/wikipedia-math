# Deane Montgomery

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Deane Montgomery"}

**Deane Montgomery** (September 2, 1909 – March 15, 1992) was an American mathematician specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) who was one of the contributors to the final resolution of [Hilbert's fifth problem](wikipedia://en/152760) in the 1950s. He served as president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) from 1961 to 1962.

:::

Born in the small town of [Weaver, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaver,_Minnesota), he received his [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) from [Hamline University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamline_University) in [St. Paul, MN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Paul,_MN) and his [Master's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) in 1933; his dissertation advisor was Edward Chittenden.[^o3]

In 1941 Montgomery was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship). In 1988, he was awarded the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Lifetime Achievement.

He was a member of the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o4] the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society),[^o5] and of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o6]
