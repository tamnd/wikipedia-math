# Dan Romik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Romik"}

**Dan Romik** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): דן רומיק) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).[^o2] He is known for contributions to probability theory and discrete mathematics.

:::

## Biography and Career {#Biography_and_Career}

Romik received his Ph.D. from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 2002 under the supervision of David Gilat.[^o4] He has worked at the University of California, Davis since 2009. He is an author of 3 books and over 40 papers, including publications in the [Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785) and in the [Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5][^o6] In 2010 he was awarded a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award),[^o7] and he was a Simons Fellow in 2012.[^o8] From 2014 to 2017 he was the chair of the Mathematics Department of the University of California, Davis.[^o9]

Much of Romik's work is in the areas of algebraic and enumerative combinatorics. He was an invited speaker at the [FPSAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FPSAC) 2017 and [AofA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AofA—International_Meeting_on_Combinatorial,_Probabilistic,_and_Asymptotic_Methods_in_the_Analysis_of_Algorithms) 2017 conferences and served as co-chair of the FPSAC 2021 program committee.[^o10][^o11][^o12]

## Work {#Work}

### The sphere packing problem {#The_sphere_packing_problem}

In 2023, Romik published a paper simplifying [Maryna Viazovska](wikipedia://en/50004967)'s solution to the [sphere packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_packing_problem) in dimension 8. Viazovska's original solution relied on computer calculations to verify analytical inequalities that were an essential ingredient in her proof, making the proof a [computer-assisted proof](wikipedia://en/2840305). Romik's paper presents a proof of the same inequalities that does not rely on computer calculations.[^o15]

### The moving sofa problem {#The_moving_sofa_problem}

Romik's research work on the [moving sofa problem](wikipedia://en/11413257) has been featured on the [Numberphile](wikipedia://en/61716514) educational YouTube channel,[^o17] in an article in [Popular Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Mechanics),[^o18] and in several other news publications and websites.[^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23][^o24][^o25]

### Software {#Software}

Romik developed several software packages accompanying his research articles.[^o27] He is the creator of the MadHat software system for mathematical typesetting and publishing.[^o28]
