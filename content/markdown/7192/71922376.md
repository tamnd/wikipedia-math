# Michael Hochman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Hochman"}

**Michael Hochman** (מיכאל הוכמן) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). He is known for his contributions to [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Hochman earned his PhD from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) under supervision of [Benjamin Weiss](wikipedia://en/40423267) in 2007.[^o5] He published his scientific work in such journals as *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*, *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)*, and *[Journal of the European Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_European_Mathematical_Society)*.[^o4]

Hochman was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) in 2018,[^o6] and at the conference Dynamics, Equations and Applications in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) in 2019.[^o7]

In 2015, he received the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) from the [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674).[^o8] In 2018, Hochman was also awarded the [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601).[^o9]
