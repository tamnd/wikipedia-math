# Eugene P. Northrop

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eugene P. Northrop"}

**Eugene P. Northrop** (1908–1969) was an American research [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a math popularizer.

:::

Northrop received his PhD from Yale University in 1934 with thesis advisor [Einar Hille](wikipedia://en/1643924). Northrop held the [William Rainey Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Rainey_Harper) Chair of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago),[^o2] and frequently served in administrative roles and on technical commissions.[^o3] He is most remembered for his 1944 book *Riddles in Mathematics*,[^o4] which was well-received by the mathematical community[^o5] and remains in print as a [Dover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Publications) book (first published in 2014).[^o6]
