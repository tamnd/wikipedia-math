# Regina Nuzzo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regina Nuzzo"}

**Regina Nuzzo** is a professor of statistics at [Gallaudet University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallaudet_University) in [Washington D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), a liberal arts school for deaf and hard-of-hearing students. She also writes articles about the importance of statistical and science communication and is an advocate for people with disabilities in the science and technology field.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Nuzzo graduated from the [University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida) with a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in [industrial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering) and went on to obtain her [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 2004, supervised by [Richard A. Olshen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_A._Olshen).[^o5] Her dissertation was written on the usage of [stochastic models](wikipedia://en/47895) in [biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemistry).

Nuzzo also graduated from the [University of California Santa Cruz's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) science writing program.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Nuzzo has been a faculty member at Gallaudet University since 2006. She has written multiple articles for publication in major magazines, including [*WIRED*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_%28magazine%29) magazine, the [*New York Times*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times), [*Los Angeles Times*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times), as well as [*Reader's Digest*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader's_Digest). In addition to teaching, she gives seminars about statistics, which have been hosted at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington),[^o8][^o9] the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park),[^o10] and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o11]

In 2019, Nuzzo was appointed the Senior Advisor for Statistics Communication and Media Innovation for the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o12]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2014, Nuzzo was awarded the Excellence in Statistical Reporting Award (ESRA) by the American Statistical Association for her article in [*Nature*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_%28journal%29) about statistical [p-values](wikipedia://en/554994).[^o14]
