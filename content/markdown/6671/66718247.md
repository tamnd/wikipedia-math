# Kristina Vušković

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kristina Vušković"}

**Kristina L. Vušković** (Кристина Л. Вушковић, born 6 May 1967) is a Serbian mathematician and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) working in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). She is Professor in Algorithms and Combinatorics in the School of Computing at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds), and a professor of computer science at [Union University (Serbia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_University_%28Serbia%29).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Vušković was born on 6 May 1967 in [Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade). She graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1989, majoring in mathematics and computer science, and completed her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) in 1994. Her dissertation, supervised by [Gérard Cornuéjols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gérard_Cornuéjols), was *Holes in Bipartite Graphs*.

After postdoctoral research as an NSERC Canada International Fellow at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), she became an assistant professor of mathematics at the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky), in 1996. She moved to Leeds in 2000, and was given the chair of algorithms and combinatorics at Leeds in 2011. Since 2007 she has also been a professor of computer science at [Union University (Serbia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_University_%28Serbia%29).

## Research {#Research}

Vušković's research in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) concerns the structure and algorithms of [hereditary classes of graphs](wikipedia://en/13552465). Her results include the recognition of [perfect graphs](wikipedia://en/670531) in [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time); she has also worked in combinatorial algorithms for [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) of perfect graphs.
