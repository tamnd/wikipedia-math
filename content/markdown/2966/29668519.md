# Characteristic 2 type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Characteristic 2 type"}

In [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is said to be of **characteristic 2 type** or **even type** or of **even characteristic** if it resembles a [group of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 2.

:::

In the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445), there is a major division between group of characteristic 2 type, where involutions resemble unipotent elements, and other groups, where involutions resemble semisimple elements.

Groups of characteristic 2 type and rank at least 3 are classified by the [trichotomy theorem](wikipedia://en/29598798).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

A group is said to be of **even characteristic** if

$C_M(O_2(M)) \le O_2(M)$ for all maximal 2-local subgroups *M* that contain a [Sylow 2-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_2-subgroup) of *G*,

where $O_2(M)$ denotes the [2-core](wikipedia://en/893516#The_p-core), the largest normal 2-subgroup (subgroup in which the order of every element is a power of 2) of *M*, which is the intersection of all conjugates of any given Sylow 2-subgroup. If this condition holds for all maximal 2-local subgroups *M* then *G* is said to be of **characteristic 2 type**. (Gorenstein Lyons Solomon 1994, p.55) use a modified version of this called **even type**.

:::
