# George Szekeres Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Szekeres Medal"}

The **George Szekeres Medal** is awarded by the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582) for outstanding research contributions over a fifteen-year period. This award, established in 2001, was given biennially in even-numbered years until 2021 and has since been given annually, for work that has been carried out primarily in Australia.[^o2]

:::

This medal commemorates the work of the late [George Szekeres](wikipedia://en/2563194), [FAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science), for his achievements in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29), and [relativity](wikipedia://en/30001).[^o2]
