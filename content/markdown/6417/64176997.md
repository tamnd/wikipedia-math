# Louis Shapiro (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Shapiro"}

**Louis Welles Shapiro** (born 1941)[^o3] is an American mathematician working in the fields of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [finite group theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_group_theory). He is an emeritus professor at [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University).[^o4]

:::

Shapiro attended [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) for his undergraduate studies and then the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) for graduate school.[^o5] Shapiro is most known for introducing the [Riordan array](wikipedia://en/38457493), named after mathematician [John Riordan](wikipedia://en/11751393),[^o6] and developing the theory around it. He has been an organizer of and speaker at the annual International Conference on Riordan Arrays and Related Topics,[^o7][^o8] which has been held annually beginning 2014.
