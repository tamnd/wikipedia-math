# Ordered algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ordered algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **ordered algebra** is an [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) over the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) $\mathbb{R}$ with unit *e* together with an associated [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order) such that *e* is positive (i.e. *e* ≥ 0), the product of any two positive elements is again positive, and when *A* is considered as a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over $\mathbb{R}$ then it is an [Archimedean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_order) [ordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Let *A* be an ordered algebra with unit *e* and let *C*<sup>\*</sup> denote the [cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_%28mathematics%29) in *A*<sup>\*</sup> (the [algebraic dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_dual) of *A*) of all positive linear forms on *A*. If *f* is a [linear form](wikipedia://en/214137) on *A* such that *f*(*e*) = 1 and *f* generates an [extreme ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_ray) of *C*<sup>\*</sup> then *f* is a multiplicative [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564).[^n1]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Results"}

## Results {#Results}

**Stone's Algebra Theorem**:[^n1] Let *A* be an ordered algebra with unit *e* such that *e* is an [order unit](wikipedia://en/38017102) in *A*, let *A*<sup>\*</sup> denote the [algebraic dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_dual) of *A*, and let *K* be the [$\sigma\left( A^{*}, A \right)$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology)-compact set of all multiplicative positive linear forms satisfying *f*(*e*) = 1. Then under the evaluation map, *A* is isomorphic to a dense subalgebra of $C_{\mathbb{R}}(X)$. If in addition every positive sequence [of type *l*<sup>1</sup>](wikipedia://en/64133229) in *A* is [order summable](wikipedia://en/64133229) then *A* together with the [Minkowski functional](wikipedia://en/3510908) *p*<sub>*e*</sub> is isomorphic to the [Banach algebra](wikipedia://en/4665) $C_{\mathbb{R}}(X)$.

:::
