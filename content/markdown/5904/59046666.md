# MAA Certificate of Merit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MAA Certificate of Merit"}

The **MAA Certificate of Merit** is awarded at irregular intervals by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for special work or service to mathematics or the broader mathematics community.

:::

## Recipients {#Recipients}

The recipients of the MAA Certificate of Merit are:[^o3][^o4]

- 1977: Henry M. Cox
- 1978: [Samuel L. Greitzer](wikipedia://en/715594)
- 1978: [Murray S. Klamkin](wikipedia://en/8881224)
- 1978: Nura D. Turner
- 1983: Hope Daly
- 1986: Raoul Hailpern
- 1988: Walter E. Mientka
- 1994: I. Edward Block
- 2021: [Mary W. Gray](wikipedia://en/17848969)
