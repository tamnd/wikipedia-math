# Ellina Grigorieva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ellina Grigorieva"}

**Ellina Grigorieva** is a Russian mathematician and [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471) known for her books on mathematical [problem solving](wikipedia://en/1467948). She is a professor in the [Texas Woman's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Woman's_University) Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, and an expert on [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and its applications to [the spread of disease](wikipedia://en/66997).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Grigorieva was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), and educated at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).

## Books {#Books}

Grigorieva's problem-solving books include:

- *Methods of Solving Number Theory Problems* (Birkhäuser, 2018)
- *Methods of Solving Sequence and Series Problems* (Birkhäuser, 2016)
- *Methods of Solving Nonstandard Problems* (Birkhäuser, 2015)
- *Methods of Solving Complex Geometry Problems* (Birkhäuser, 2013)
