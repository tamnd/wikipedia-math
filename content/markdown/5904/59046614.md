# Forder Lectureship

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Forder Lectureship"}

The **Forder Lectureship** is awarded by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) to a research mathematician from the United Kingdom who has made an eminent contribution to the field of mathematics and who can also speak effectively at a more popular level. The lectureship is named for Professor [H.G. Forder](wikipedia://en/43469245), formerly of the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), and a benefactor of the London Mathematical Society. The lectureship was funded in 1986 by the London Mathematical Society and the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society), first began in 1987, and is normally awarded every two years. Recipients of the lectureship will give a four- to six-week lecturing tour of most New Zealand universities.[^o3] In alternate years the [Aitken Lectureship](wikipedia://en/75557217) is awarded.[^o5]

:::

- 1989: [Michael F. Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698)
- 1991: [Peter Whittle](wikipedia://en/2600214)
- 1993: [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193)
- 1995: E.G. Rees
- 1997: [Ian Stewart](wikipedia://en/332183)
- 1999: [Michael Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Berry_%28physicist%29)
- 2001: [Tom Körner](wikipedia://en/8920453)

- 2005: [Martin Bridson](wikipedia://en/36378173)
- 2008: [Peter Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Cameron_%28mathematician%29)
- 2010: [Ben Green](wikipedia://en/1927952)
- 2012: [Geoffrey Grimmett](wikipedia://en/22716684)
- 2015: [Endre Süli](wikipedia://en/25003070)
- 2016: [Julia Gog](wikipedia://en/53842028)
- 2020: [Julia Wolf](wikipedia://en/58446958)
- 2023: [Imre Leader](wikipedia://en/3756629)
