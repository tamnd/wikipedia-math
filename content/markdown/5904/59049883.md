# Lotte Hollands

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lotte Hollands"}

*Image: Lotte Hollands Oberwolfach 2025 (cropped).jpg.* Hollands at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2025

**Lotte Hollands** (born 1981) is a Dutch mathematician and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) who studies [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267), [supersymmetric gauge theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetric_gauge_theory), and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305). She is an associate professor and Royal Society Dorothy Hodgkin Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Hollands was born in [Maasbree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maasbree), Netherlands.

## Education {#Education}

Hollands earned her PhD at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 2009. Her dissertation, *Topological Strings and Quantum Curves*, was supervised by [Robbert Dijkgraaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbert_Dijkgraaf). Dijkgraaf and Jim Bryan of the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) jointly supervised Hollands' earlier master's thesis.[^o4]

Hollands did her postdoctoral research with [Sergei Gukov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Gukov) at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).

## Career {#Career}

In 2013, Hollands became a research fellow at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). In 2015, Hollands became an associate professor at the Department of Math at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018 the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) gave her their [Anne Bennett Prize](wikipedia://en/61347778) "in recognition of her outstanding research at the interface between quantum theory and geometry and of her leadership in mathematical outreach activities".
