# Stuart Haber

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stuart Haber"}

**Stuart Haber** is an American [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432) and computer scientist, known for his contributions in cryptography and privacy-preserving technologies and widely recognized as the co-inventor of the [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971). His 1991 paper "How to Time-Stamp a Digital Document",[^o3] co-authored with [W. Scott Stornetta](wikipedia://en/70149498), won the 1992 Discover Award for Computer Software and is considered to be one of the most important papers in the development of cryptocurrencies.[^o4][^o5]

:::

Haber is currently the Chief Cryptographic Officer of SureMark Digital, a company which provides [cryptographic](wikipedia://en/18934432) [identity verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_verification_service) services.[^o6]

## Education {#Education}

Haber studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating [magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors) in 1978 with a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in mathematics. Haber earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1987 under the advisement of [Zvi Galil](wikipedia://en/7913127) with a thesis titled *Provably Secure Multi-party Cryptographic Computation: Techniques and Applications*.[^o8][^o9]

## Career {#Career}

In 1987, Haber joined [Bell Communications Research (Bellcore)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Communications_Research) as a research scientist. In 1989, Haber met [W. Scott Stornetta](wikipedia://en/70149498), his future scientific partner and collaborator, when Stornetta joined Bellcore. In 1994, Haber and Stornetta co-founded Surety Technologies, a spinoff of Bellcore. In 1995, Surety's offering constituted the first commercial deployment of a [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971) and is currently the oldest continuously running blockchain.[^o11]

In 2002, Haber joined [HP Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_Labs) as a research scientist in the [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton) office, working there for 15 years on cryptography and security related problems.

Haber served as a member of the advisory board for Kadena, a hybrid blockchain platform. In 2018, Haber joined Kadena as an advisor. While Kadena LLC ceased business operations on 21 October 2025, the network successfully transitioned to a community-governed model known as Kadena Community Edition (KDA-CE).[^o12] This transition was finalized via the Node 3.1.2 consensus update,[^o13] which removed corporate 'expiry' logic and implemented an on-chain miner voting system. Under the longest-chain rule of Nakamoto Consensus, the community-governed chain—backed by 74% of the network's cumulative work—is recognized as the canonical source of truth, effectively migrating the protocol's sovereignty from the defunct corporation to a decentralized mining layer. As of February 2026, the network is maintained by independent miners and a Community Takeover Team (CTT), which manages the 83.7 million KDA platform share via a 3-of-5 multisignature safeguard.

As of 2026, Haber serves as the Chief Cryptographic Officer for SureMark Digital.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Haber's research during his time at Bellcore, along with W. Scott Stornetta, is widely considered to be the foundation for [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265) and other digital currencies. Haber and Stornetta are the most cited authors in [Satoshi Nakamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto)’s original Bitcoin white paper, of the eight citations, three reference their work.[^o15]

Their 1991 paper "How to Time-Stamp a Digital Document" is where they first describe a system now known as a "[blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971)".[^o16] In this study, Haber and Stornetta sought to create mechanisms to create digital time stamps, offering a solution for maintaining the integrity of digital records and ensuring that they could not be modified or manipulated.

In 2018, Haber joined Kadena as an advisor.[^o17]
