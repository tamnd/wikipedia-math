# Adriana Garroni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adriana Garroni"}

*Image: Adriana Garroni Oberwolfach 2026.jpg.* Garroni at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2026

**Adriana Garroni** (born 1966) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), including the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), and applications to the mathematical modeling of materials including [plasticity](wikipedia://en/240123) and [fracture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fracture). She is a professor in mathematics at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Garroni was born on 22 March 1966 in Rome, and despite having mathematics professor Maria Giovanna Garroni as her mother, she grew up torn between mathematics and the arts.

After earning a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics in 1991 from Sapienza University of Rome, she went to the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) (SISSA) in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste) for graduate study in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) with [Gianni Dal Maso](wikipedia://en/39451526), earning an M.Phil. in 1993 and completing her Ph.D. in 1994.

She returned to Sapienza University as a researcher in 1995, became an associate professor in 1998, and was named full professor in 2017.
