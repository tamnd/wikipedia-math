# Katharina T. Huber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Katharina T. Huber"}

**Katharina Theresia Huber** (born 1965) is a German applied mathematician and mathematical biologist whose research concerns [phylogenetic trees](wikipedia://en/149326), [evolutionary analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylogenetics), their mathematical foundations, and their [mathematical visualization](wikipedia://en/25948250). She is an associate professor in the School of Computing Sciences at the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia) in England, and the school's director of postgraduate research.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Huber completed a doctorate in mathematics at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) in 1997. Her dissertation, *A T-theoretical Approach to Phylogenetic Analysis and Cluster Analysis*, was jointly supervised by [Andreas Dress](wikipedia://en/57220260) and Walter Deuber.

After postdoctoral research at [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University) in New Zealand, Huber became a lecturer in mathematics at [Mid Sweden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid_Sweden_University) in [Sundsvall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundsvall), Sweden in 2000. She moved to the Department of Biometry and Engineering of the [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in Sweden in 2003, and to the School of Computing Sciences at the University of East Anglia in 2004, where she became a senior lecturer in 2012.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Huber is a coauthor of the book *Basic Phylogenetic Combinatorics* (Cambridge University Press, 2012), and a codeveloper of the ape package for evolutionary analysis in [the R statistical programming system](wikipedia://en/376707).
