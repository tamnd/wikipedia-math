# Ban number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ban number"}

In [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446), a **ban number** is a number that does not contain a particular letter when spelled out in English; in other words, the letter is "banned." Ban numbers are not precisely defined, since some [large numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_number) do not follow the standards of number names (such as [googol](wikipedia://en/18994679) and [googolplex](wikipedia://en/12365)).

:::

There are several published sequences of ban numbers:

- The **aban numbers** do not contain the letter [A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A). The first few aban numbers are 1 through 999, 1,000,000 through 1,000,999, 2,000,000 through 2,000,999, ... The word "and" is not counted.
- The **dban numbers** do not contain the letter [D](wikipedia://en/8123). The first few dban numbers are 1 through 99, 1,000,000 through 1,000,099, 2,000,000 through, 2,000,099, etc...
- The **eban numbers** do not contain the letter [E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E). The first few eban numbers are 2, 4, 6, 30, 32, 34, 36, 40, 42, 44, 46, 50, 52, 54, 56, 60, 62, 64, 66, 2000, 2002, 2004, 2006, 2030, 2032, 2034, 2036, 2040, 2042, 2044, 2046, 2050, 2052, 2054, 2056, 2060, 2062, 2064, 2066... . The sequence was coined in 1990 by [Neil Sloane](wikipedia://en/400062). Coincidentally, all the numbers in the sequence are even.
- The **iban numbers** do not contain the letter [I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I). The first few iban numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 40, ... . Since all the -illion numbers contain the letter I, there are exactly 30,275 iban numbers, the largest being 777,777.
- The **nban numbers** do not contain the letter [N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N). The first few nban numbers are 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 30, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 38, 40, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 48, ... . Since "hundred", "thousand", and all the -illion numbers contain the letter N, there are exactly 43 nban numbers, the largest being 88.
- The **oban numbers** do not contain the letter [O](wikipedia://en/22314). The first few oban numbers are 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 23, 25, 26, ... . Since "thousand" and all the -illion numbers contain the letter O, there are exactly 454 oban numbers, the largest being 999. Saying the word "o" for numbers with 0 in the tens place is not counted.
- The **sban numbers** do not contain the letter [S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S). The first few sban numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, ... .
- The **tban numbers** do not contain the letter [T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T). The first few tban numbers are 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 100, 101, 104, 105, 106, 107, 109, 111, 400, 401, 404, 405, 406, ... .
- The **uban numbers** do not contain the letter [U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U). The first few uban numbers are 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, ... .
- The **yban numbers** do not contain the letter [Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y). The first few yban numbers are 1 through 19, 100 through 119, 200 through 219, 300 through 319, ... .

## Basic properties {#Basic_properties}

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Aban numbers"}

### Aban numbers {#Aban_numbers}

- For 1\<N\<10<sup>9</sup>, aban numbers are numbers which the integer part of N/1000 is divisible by 1000.

:::

### Eban numbers {#Eban_numbers}

- Eban numbers are never odd, due to "one", "three", "five", "seven", "nine", "eleven" and the suffix -teen all containing 'e's.

:::
