# Secondary vector bundle structure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Secondary vector bundle structure"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), the **secondary vector bundle structure** refers to the natural [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) structure $(TE, p_{\ast}, TM)$ on the total space *TE* of the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) of a smooth vector bundle $(E, p, M)$, induced by the [push-forward](wikipedia://en/1558510) $p_{\ast} : TE \to TM$ of the original projection map $p : E \to M$. This gives rise to a [double vector bundle](wikipedia://en/45482173) structure $(TE,E,TM,M)$.

:::

In the special case $(E, p, M) = (TM, \pi_{TM}, M)$, where $TE = TTM$ is the [double tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/19254708), the secondary vector bundle $(TTM, (\pi_{TM})_{\ast}, TM)$ is isomorphic to the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) $(TTM, \pi_{TTM}, TM)$ of $TM$ through the [canonical flip](wikipedia://en/19254708).

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction of the secondary vector bundle structure"}

## Construction of the secondary vector bundle structure {#Construction_of_the_secondary_vector_bundle_structure}

Let $(E, p, M)$ be a smooth vector bundle of rank $N$. Then the preimage $(p_{\ast})^{-1}(X) \subset TE$ of any tangent vector $X$ in $TM$ in the push-forward $p_{\ast} : TE \to TM$ of the canonical projection $p : E \to M$ is a smooth [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) of dimension $2N$, and it becomes a vector space with the push-forwards

$$+_*:T(E \times_{\! M} \! E) \to TE, \qquad \lambda_*:TE\to TE$$

of the original addition and scalar multiplication

$$+:E \times_{\! M} \! E \to E, \qquad \lambda:E\to E$$

as its vector space operations. It becomes clear $+_*$ actually defines addition on the fibers of $p_*$ as $T(E \times_{\!M} \! E) = TE \times_{TM} TE$. The triple $(TE, p_{\ast}, TM)$ becomes a smooth vector bundle with these vector space operations on its fibres.

::: {#o5 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

### Proof {#Proof}

Let $(U, \varphi)$ be a local coordinate system on the base manifold $M$ with $\varphi(x) = (x^{1}, ..., x^{n})$ and let

$$\begin{cases}\psi:W \to \varphi(U)\times \mathbf{R}^N \\ \psi \left (v^k e_k|_x \right ) := \left (x^1,\ldots,x^n,v^1,\ldots,v^N \right )\end{cases}$$

be a coordinate system on $W:=p^{-1}(U)\subset E$ adapted to it. Then

$$p_*\left (X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_v + Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_v \right) = X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_{p(v)},$$

so the fiber of the secondary vector bundle structure at $X$ in $T_{x}M$ is of the form

$$p^{-1}_*(X) = \left \{ X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_v + Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_v \ : \ v\in E_x; Y^1,\ldots,Y^N\in\mathbf{R} \right \}.$$

Now it turns out that

$$\chi\left(X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_v + Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_v\right ) = \left (X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_{p(v)}, \left (v^1,\ldots,v^N,Y^1,\ldots,Y^N \right) \right )$$

gives a local trivialization $\chi : TW \to TU \times \mathbf{R}^{2N}$ for $(TE, p_{\ast}, TM)$, and the push-forwards of the original vector space operations read in the adapted coordinates as

$$\left (X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_v + Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_v\right) +_* \left (X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_w + Z^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_w\right) = X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_{v+w} + (Y^\ell+Z^\ell)\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_{v+w}$$

and

$$\lambda_*\left (X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_v + Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_v\right) = X^k\frac{\partial}{\partial x^k}\Bigg|_{\lambda v} + \lambda Y^\ell\frac{\partial}{\partial v^\ell}\Bigg|_{\lambda v},$$

so each fibre $(p_{\ast})^{-1}(X) \subset TE$ is a vector space and the triple $(TE, p_{\ast}, TM)$ is a smooth vector bundle.

:::

## Linearity of connections on vector bundles {#Linearity_of_connections_on_vector_bundles}

The general [Ehresmann connection](wikipedia://en/5731754) $TE = HE \oplus VE$ on a vector bundle $(E, p, M)$ can be characterized in terms of the **connector map**

$$\begin{cases}\kappa:T_vE\to E_{p(v)} \\ \kappa(X):=\operatorname{vl}_v^{-1}(\operatorname{vpr}X) \end{cases}$$

where $\operatorname{vl}_{v} : E \to V_{v}E$ is the [vertical lift](wikipedia://en/276524), and $\operatorname{vpr}_{v} : T_{v}E \to V_{v}E$ is the [vertical projection](wikipedia://en/5731754). The mapping

$$\begin{cases}\nabla:\Gamma(TM)\times\Gamma(E)\to\Gamma(E) \\ \nabla_Xv := \kappa(v_*X) \end{cases}$$

induced by an Ehresmann connection is a [covariant derivative](wikipedia://en/431848) on $\Gamma(E)$ in the sense that

$$\begin{aligned} \nabla_{X+Y}v &= \nabla_X v + \nabla_Y v \\ \nabla_{\lambda X}v &=\lambda \nabla_Xv \\ \nabla_X(v+w) &= \nabla_X v + \nabla_X w \\ \nabla_X(\lambda v) &=\lambda \nabla_Xv \\ \nabla_X(fv) &= X[f]v + f\nabla_Xv \end{aligned}$$

if and only if the connector map is linear with respect to the secondary vector bundle structure $(TE, p_{\ast}, TM)$ on $TE$. Then the connection is called *linear*. Note that the connector map is automatically linear with respect to the tangent bundle structure $(TE, \pi_{TE}, E)$.
