# Fred S. Roberts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fred S. Roberts"}

**Fred Stephen Roberts** (born June 19, 1943)[^o2] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), and a former director of [DIMACS](wikipedia://en/3684858).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Roberts did his undergraduate studies at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College),[^o2] and received his Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1968; his [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) was [Dana Scott](wikipedia://en/161875).[^o4] After holding positions at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), [RAND](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), he joined the Rutgers faculty in 1972.[^o2]

He has been vice president of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) twice, in 1984 and 1986, and has been director of DIMACS since 1996.[^o2]

## Research {#Research}

Roberts' research concerns [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and their applications in modeling problems in the [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science) and [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology).[^o2] Among his contributions to pure mathematics, he is known for introducing the concept of [boxicity](wikipedia://en/11167471), the minimum dimension needed to represent a given [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) as an [intersection graph](wikipedia://en/7726759) of axis-parallel [boxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_%28shape%29).[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

Roberts is the author or co-author of the following books:[^o8]

- *Discrete Mathematical Models, with Applications to Social, Biological and Environmental Problems*, Prentice-Hall, 1976, . Russian translation, Nauka, 1986.
- *Graph Theory and its Applications to the Problems of Society*, CBMS-NSF Regional Conference Series in Applied Mathematics 29, [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376), 1987, .
- *Measurement Theory, with Applications to Decisionmaking, Utility, and the Social Sciences*, Encyclopedia of Mathematics and its Applications 7, Addison-Wesley, 1979, .[^o9] Reprinted by Cambridge University Press, 2009.
- *Applied Combinatorics*, Prentice-Hall, 1984. 2nd edition (with B. Tesman), 2004, . 3rd edition, Chapman & Hall, 2009. Chinese translation, Pearson Education Asia, 2005 and 2007.

He is also the editor of nearly 20 edited volumes.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Roberts received the [ACM SIGACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_SIGACT) Distinguished Service Prize in 1999. In 2001, he won the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Science and Technology Centers Pioneer Award for "pioneering the science and technology center concept".[^o12] In 2003, DIMACS held a Conference on Applications of Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science, in honor of Roberts' 60th birthday.[^o13] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]
