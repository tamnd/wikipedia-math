# Robert G. Bland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert G. Bland"}

**Robert Gary Bland** (born February 25, 1948) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476), a professor of operations research and information engineering at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o2] He was born in New York City.[^o3]

:::

Bland did both his undergraduate and graduate studies at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), earning a bachelor's degree in 1969, M.S. in 1972, and a Ph.D. in 1974 under the supervision of [D. R. Fulkerson](wikipedia://en/239238).[^o2] He began his faculty career at [Binghamton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binghamton_University), but then returned to Cornell in 1978.[^o2]

Bland is known as one of the inventors of [oriented matroids](wikipedia://en/24338041), which he used to define [Bland's rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bland's_rule) for avoiding cycles in the [simplex method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplex_method) for [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730).[^o5][^o6]
