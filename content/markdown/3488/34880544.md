# Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe"}

The **Kavli** **Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe (IPMU)** is an international [research institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute) for physics and mathematics situated in [Kashiwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashiwa), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), near [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo). Its full name is *"Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe, The University of Tokyo Institutes for Advanced Study, the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo), Kashiwa, Japan"*. It is one of 20 [Kavli Institutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavli_Foundation_%28United_States%29#The_Kavli_Institutes).

:::

The main subjects of study at IPMU are [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics), [high energy physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_energy_physics), [astrophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics), [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). The institute addresses five key questions: "How did the universe begin? What is its fate? What is it made of? What are its fundamental laws? Why do we exist?"[^o3]

## History {#History}

The Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe was created on October 1, 2007, by its founding director [Hitoshi Murayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitoshi_Murayama) and the University of Tokyo.[^o5][^o6] It is funded by the [Japanese Ministry of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education,_Culture,_Sports,_Science_and_Technology), as a part of their [World Premier International Research Center Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Premier_International_Research_Center_Initiative). In 2012, the IPMU received an endowment from the [Kavli Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavli_Foundation_%28United_States%29) and was renamed the Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe.[^o7]
