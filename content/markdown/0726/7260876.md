# Ehrling's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ehrling's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Ehrling's lemma**, also known as **[Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500)' lemma**,[^o2] is a result concerning [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989). It is often used in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) to demonstrate the [equivalence](wikipedia://en/990534#Properties) of certain [norms](wikipedia://en/990534) on [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964). It was named after Gunnar Ehrling.[^o3][^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let (*X*, ||⋅||<sub>*X*</sub>), (*Y*, ||⋅||<sub>*Y*</sub>) and (*Z*, ||⋅||<sub>*Z*</sub>) be three Banach spaces. Assume that:

- *X* is [compactly embedded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactly_embedded) in *Y*: i.e. *X* ⊆ *Y* and every ||⋅||<sub>*X*</sub>-[bounded](wikipedia://en/311509) [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) in *X* has a [subsequence](wikipedia://en/236098) that is ||⋅||<sub>*Y*</sub>-[convergent](wikipedia://en/26551602); and
- *Y* is [continuously embedded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuously_embedded) in *Z*: i.e. *Y* ⊆ *Z* and there is a constant *k* so that ||*y*||<sub>*Z*</sub> ≤ *k*||*y*||<sub>*Y*</sub> for every *y* ∈ *Y*.

Then, for every *ε* > 0, there exists a constant *C*(*ε*) such that, for all *x* ∈ *X*,

$$\| x \|_{Y} \leq \varepsilon \| x \|_{X} + C(\varepsilon) \| x \|_{Z}$$

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/corollary cue=named-heading name="Corollary (equivalent norms for Sobolev spaces)"}

## Corollary (equivalent norms for Sobolev spaces) {#Corollary_(equivalent_norms_for_Sobolev_spaces)}

Let Ω ⊂ **R**<sup>*n*</sup> be [open](wikipedia://en/39358) and [bounded](wikipedia://en/48258), and let *k* ∈ **N**. Suppose that the Sobolev space *H*<sup>*k*</sup>(Ω) is compactly embedded in *H*<sup>*k*−1</sup>(Ω). Then the following two norms on *H*<sup>*k*</sup>(Ω) are equivalent:

$$\| \cdot \| : H^{k} (\Omega) \to \mathbf{R}: u \mapsto \| u \| := \sqrt{\sum_{| \alpha | \leq k} \| \mathrm{D}^{\alpha} u \|_{L^{2} (\Omega)}^{2}}$$

and

$$\| \cdot \|' : H^{k} (\Omega) \to \mathbf{R}: u \mapsto \| u \|' := \sqrt{\| u \|_{L^{2} (\Omega)}^{2} + \sum_{| \alpha | = k} \| \mathrm{D}^{\alpha} u \|_{L^{2} (\Omega)}^{2}}.$$

For the subspace of *H*<sup>*k*</sup>(Ω) consisting of those Sobolev functions with [zero trace](wikipedia://en/11199199) (those that are "zero on the boundary" of Ω), the *L*<sup>2</sup> norm of *u* can be left out to yield another equivalent norm.

:::
