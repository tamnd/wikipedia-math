# Kolmogorov Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kolmogorov Prize"}

The **Kolmogorov Prize** is a mathematical prize awarded by the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) for outstanding results in the field of mathematics. It bears the name of the mathematician [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161).[^o2]

:::

The award was established by a Decree of the Presidium of the Russian Academy of Sciences on February 23, 1993.[^o3] As a rule, it is awarded every three years.
