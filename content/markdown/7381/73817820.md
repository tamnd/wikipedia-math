# Oliver Chinganya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oliver Chinganya"}

**Oliver Chinganya** CStatis a Zambian [chartered statistician](wikipedia://en/20579991) (since 1999), [chartered Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartered_Scientist) and a fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664). He currently serves as the director of [African Centre for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Centre_for_Statistics) at [UN Economic Commission for Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Economic_Commission_for_Africa) (UNECA).[^o3] He is also serving as the Vice President of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He was born in [Livingstone, Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livingstone,_Zambia).[^o7] After graduating from secondary school, he joined the [Zambian Statistics Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambian_Statistics_Agency).[^o7] In 1984, he enrolled in a training program sponsored by the [UN Population Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Population_Fund).[^o7] He earned the equivalent of a bachelor's degree from the [Eastern Africa Statistical Training Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Africa_Statistical_Training_Centre) at the [University of Dar es Salaam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dar_es_Salaam).[^o7] He earned an [M.Sc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc) in Statistics from [Southampton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southampton_University) in 1991, and later an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBA) in [Strategic Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Management) from [Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jomo_Kenyatta_University_of_Agriculture_and_Technology) in 2008.[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

His career involved in the field of statistics and statistical development in Africa.[^o9] He served as UNECA's Officer in Charge for the Climate Change, Environment and Natural Resources Management Division, and also set up the Digital Centre of Excellence.[^o10]

Currently, Chinganya serves as the director of the African Centre for Statistics at the UN Economic Commission for Africa.[^o11]

Chinganya emphasized the importance of integrated [civil registration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_registration) and [ID management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ID_management) and also highlighted the significance of civil registration as the foundation for collecting data from birth to death.[^o12]

In 2020, he bemoaned the negative impact of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Africa) on civil registration and vital statistics systems,[^o13] particularly in Africa, leading to the absence of vital data.[^o14]
