# Jing Ning

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jing Ning"}

Ning Jing **Jing Ning** is a Chinese-American professor of biostatistics at the [MD Anderson Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MD_Anderson_Cancer_Center) of the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas). Her research interests include [biomarkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomarker), [semiparametric models](wikipedia://en/4415208) in [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259), inference with [length-biased data](wikipedia://en/12280374), and their applications in modeling the health of cancer patients.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ning studied statistics at the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China), earning a bachelor's degree in 1999 and a master's degree in 2002. She completed a Ph.D. in biostatistics at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in 2007. Her dissertation, *Estimating causal treatment effects for post-randomization marker data with failure event censoring*, was supervised by [Mei-Cheng Wang](wikipedia://en/55679131).

She joined the MD Anderson Cancer Center as a postdoctoral researcher from 2007 to 2009, and was an assistant professor in the [UTHealth School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTHealth_School_of_Public_Health) of the University of Texas from 2009 to 2011 before returning to the MD Anderson Cancer Center as a faculty member.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ning is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), elected to the 2023 class of fellows.
