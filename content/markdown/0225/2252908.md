# Jacques Philippe Marie Binet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Philippe Marie Binet"}

**Jacques Philippe Marie Binet** (; 2 February 1786 – 12 May 1856) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) born in [Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennes); he died in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), in 1856. He made significant contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and the mathematical foundations of [matrix algebra](wikipedia://en/20556859) which would later lead to important contributions by [Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034) and others. In his memoir on the theory of the [conjugate axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_axis) and of the moment of inertia of bodies he enumerated the principle now known as *Binet's theorem*. He is also recognized as the first to describe the rule for [multiplying matrices](wikipedia://en/125280) in 1812, and *Binet's formula* expressing [Fibonacci numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_number) in closed form is named in his honour, although the same result was known to [Abraham de Moivre](wikipedia://en/57327) a century earlier.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Binet graduated from l'[École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 1806, and returned as a teacher in 1807. He advanced in position until 1816 when he became an inspector of studies at l'École. He held this post until 13 November 1830, when he was dismissed by the recently sworn in [King Louis-Philippe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Louis-Philippe) of France, probably because of Binet's strong support of the previous King, [Charles X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_X_of_France). In 1823 Binet succeeded [Delambre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delambre) in the chair of [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) at the *[Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France)*.[^o4] He was made a [Chevalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight) in the [Légion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Légion_d'Honneur) in 1821, and was elected to the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences) in 1843.

## Binet's Fibonacci number formula {#Binet's_Fibonacci_number_formula}

The [Fibonacci sequence](wikipedia://en/10918) is defined by

- $$u_0 = 0$$
- $$u_1 = 1$$
- $$u_n = u_{n-1} + u_{n-2},\text{ for }n > 1$$

Binet's formula provides a [closed-form expression](wikipedia://en/585143) for the $n^\text{th}$ term in this sequence:

$u_n = \frac{(1 + \sqrt{5})^n - (1 - \sqrt{5})^n}{2^n \sqrt{5}}$ [^o7]

Given:

$$\varphi = {(1 + \sqrt{5}) \over 2}$$

a simplified version of Binet's formula is:

$$u_n = \left\lfloor{{\varphi^n \over \sqrt{5}} + \frac 1 2}\right\rfloor.$$
