# Liliana Forzani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Liliana Forzani"}

*Image: LilianaForzani2.jpg.* Forzani in 2017

**Liliana María Forzani** is an Argentine applied mathematician and statistician, a researcher for the Argentine [National Scientific and Technical Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Scientific_and_Technical_Research_Council) (CONICET), a professor at the [National University of the Littoral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_the_Littoral) in [Santa Fe, Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Argentina), and the first woman president of the Mathematical Union of Latin America and Caribbean (UMALCA).

:::

Forzani studied applied mathematics at the National University of the Littoral, earning a degree in 1988. She has two doctorates,, the first in mathematics in 1993 from the [National University of San Luis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_San_Luis), supervised by Hugo Aimar. Her second Ph.D., in 2007, concerned [dimensionality reduction](wikipedia://en/579867) in [statistical regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_regression). It was supervised by [R. Dennis Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Dennis_Cook) at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).

She is a 2008 recipient of the [L'Oreal-UNESCO-Conicet prize for Women in science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards), and the French-Argentine "Scientists Who Count" Award. She has served as Argentina's representative to the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) Committee for Women in Mathematics, and was elected president of the Mathematical Union of Latin America and the Caribbean for the 2021–2024 term.
