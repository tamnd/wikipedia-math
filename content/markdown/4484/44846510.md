# Cobordism ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cobordism ring"}

In mathematics, the **oriented cobordism ring** is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) where elements are oriented [cobordism classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobordism_class)[^o2] of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), the multiplication is given by the Cartesian product of manifolds and the addition is given as the disjoint union of manifolds. The ring is [graded](wikipedia://en/103094) by dimensions of manifolds and is denoted by

:::

$$\Omega^{SO}_* = \oplus_0^\infty \Omega^{SO}_n$$

where $\Omega^{SO}_n$ consists of oriented cobordism classes of manifolds of dimension *n*. One can also define an **unoriented cobordism ring**, denoted by $\Omega^O_*$. If *O* is replaced *U*, then one gets the [complex cobordism ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_cobordism_ring), oriented or unoriented.

In general, one writes $\Omega^B_*$ for the cobordism ring of manifolds with structure *B*.

A theorem of Thom[^o3] says:

$$\Omega^O_n = \pi_{n}(MO)$$

where *MO* is the [Thom spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_spectrum).
