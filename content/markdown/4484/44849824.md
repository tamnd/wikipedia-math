# Atiyah–Bott formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Atiyah–Bott formula"}

Atiyah–Bott fixed-point theorem In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Atiyah–Bott formula** says[^o2] the [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723)

:::

$$\operatorname{H}^*(\operatorname{Bun}_G(X), \mathbb{Q}_l)$$

of the [moduli stack of principal bundles](wikipedia://en/41790574) is a [free](wikipedia://en/494669) [graded-commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/859686) on certain homogeneous generators. The original work of [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) and [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457) concerned the integral cohomology ring of $\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)$.

## See also {#See_also}

- [Borel's theorem](wikipedia://en/43948116), which says that the cohomology ring of a classifying stack is a polynomial ring.
