# Lisa Mantini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lisa Mantini"}

**Lisa Ann Mantini** is an American mathematician and educator.[^o2] In 1995, while at Oklahoma State University, she received an MMA award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics.[^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mantini earned a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), and a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) and PhD from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). All these degrees were in mathematics.[^o4][^o5]

## Teaching {#Teaching}

Mantini taught at [Wellesley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellesley_College) prior to 1985. In 1985, she began to teach at [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University_System).[^o2] Among other awards (see below), in 1995 she received a [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics),[^o7] the highest teaching honor bestowed by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o8][^o9] In 1998, she gave the undergraduate lecture course, "Representations of Finite Symmetry Groups", for the Mentoring Program for Women in Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey.[^o10]

## Mathematical Association of America Governor {#Mathematical_Association_of_America_Governor}

Mantini served the Oklahoma-Arkansas Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) as Governor from 2002 to 2005 and from 2014 to 2017. This made her the first person to serve the Oklahoma-Arkansas Section of the Mathematical Association of America as Governor for two terms.[^o2]

## Notable recognition {#Notable_recognition}

- 1994: Received the [AAUW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_of_University_Women)’s Founder’s Postdoctoral Fellowship[^o2]
- 1994: Was declared one of the Oklahoma-Arkansas Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s "Distinguished College/University Teachers of Mathematics" (one was chosen each year)[^o14]
- 1995: Received a [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics),[^o15] the highest teaching honor bestowed by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o8][^o9]
- 2020: Received a Certificate of Meritorious Service from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o4]
