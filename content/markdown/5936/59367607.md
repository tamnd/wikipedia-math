# Jean Pigeon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Pigeon"}

**Jean Pigeon d'Osangis** (born 1654, [Donzy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donzy), [Nivernais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nivernais); died 1739) was a French physicist and mathematician, noted for the construction of [planispheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planisphere).[^o2][^o3]

:::

He was also a globe maker.[^o4] In the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław)'s map collection, there survives one of only two remaining examples of a 7-cm terrestrial pocket globe that Pigeon published in 1717.[^o5]

He was the father of [Marie Anne Victoire Pigeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Anne_Victoire_Pigeon).

He was a member of the Paris Society of Arts.[^o6][^o7]
