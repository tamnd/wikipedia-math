# Rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombicosidodecahedron"}

Semireg polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombicosidodecahedron** is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847), one of thirteen [convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron) [isogonal](wikipedia://en/581560) nonprismatic solids constructed of two or more types of [regular polygon](wikipedia://en/333306) [faces](wikipedia://en/149926).

:::

It has a total of 62 faces: 20 regular [triangular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular) faces, 30 [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) faces, 12 regular [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) faces, with 60 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), and 120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034).

*Image: Rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a rhombicosidodecahedron

## Names {#Names}

[Johannes Kepler](wikipedia://en/15736) in *[Harmonices Mundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonices_Mundi)* (1618) named this polyhedron a *rhombicosidodecahedron*, being short for *truncated icosidodecahedral rhombus*, with *icosidodecahedral rhombus* being his name for a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025).[^o3][^o4] There are different truncations of a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025) into a [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) rhombicosidodecahedron: Prominently its [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) (left), the one that creates the uniform solid (center), and the rectification of the dual [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) (right), which is the core of the [dual compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_compound).

## Dimensions {#Dimensions}

For a rhombicosidodecahedron with edge length *a*, its surface area and volume are:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \left(30+5\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{25+10\sqrt{5}}\right)a^2 &&\approx 59.305\,982\,844\,9 a^2 \\ V &=\frac{60+29\sqrt{5}}{3}a^3 &&\approx 41.615\,323\,782\,5 a^3 \end{aligned}$$

## Geometric relations {#Geometric_relations}

Cantellating either a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) or [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) produces a rhombicosidodecahedron.[^o7]

*Image: P4-A11-P5.gif.* Cantellation of either a regular dodecahedron or a regular icosahedron creates a rhombicosidodecahedron.

[Expanding](wikipedia://en/6341070) an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) by moving the faces away from the [origin](wikipedia://en/1313432) the right amount, rotating each face so that each triangle vertex continues to touch a pentagon vertex, without changing the size of the faces, and patching the square holes in the result, yields a rhombicosidodecahedron. Therefore, it has the same number of triangles as an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) and the same number of pentagons as a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), with a square for each edge of either.

Alternatively, [expanding](wikipedia://en/6341070) each of five cubes by moving the faces away from the [origin](wikipedia://en/1313432) the right amount and rotating each of the five 72° around so they are equidistant from each other, without changing the orientation or size of the faces, and patching the pentagonal and triangular holes in the result, yields a rhombicosidodecahedron. Therefore, it has the same number of squares as five cubes.

Two clusters of faces of the [bilunabirotunda](wikipedia://en/1170286), the *lunes* (each *lune* featuring two triangles adjacent to opposite sides of one square), can be aligned with a congruent patch of faces on the rhombicosidodecahedron. If two bilunabirotundae are aligned this way on opposite sides of the rhombicosidodecahedron, then a cube can be put between the bilunabirotundae at the very center of the rhombicosidodecahedron.

The rhombicosidodecahedron shares the vertex arrangement with the [small stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157444), and with the uniform compounds of six or twelve [pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/2860528).

The [Zometool](wikipedia://en/75949002) kits for making [geodesic domes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_dome) and other [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470) use slotted balls as connectors. The balls are "expanded" rhombicosidodecahedra, with the squares replaced by rectangles. The expansion is chosen so that the resulting rectangles are [golden rectangles](wikipedia://en/219374).

Twelve of the 92 [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) are derived from the rhombicosidodecahedron, four of them by rotation of one or more [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073): the [gyrate](wikipedia://en/1131111), [parabigyrate](wikipedia://en/1177997), [metabigyrate](wikipedia://en/1178006), and [trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133367). Eight more can be constructed by removing up to three cupolae, sometimes also rotating one or more of the other cupolae.

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a rhombicosidodecahedron with an edge length of 2 centered at the origin are all [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of:

(±1, ±1, ±*φ*<sup>3</sup>),

(±*φ*<sup>2</sup>, ±*φ*, ±2*φ*),

(±(2+*φ*), 0, ±*φ*<sup>2</sup>),

where *φ* = $\tfrac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Therefore, the [circumradius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumradius) of this rhombicosidodecahedron is the common distance of these points from the origin, namely $\sqrt{\varphi^{6}+2}$ = $\sqrt{8\varphi+7}$ for edge length 2. For unit edge length, R must be halved, giving

*R* = $\tfrac{\sqrt{8\varphi+7}}{2}$ = $\tfrac{\sqrt{11+4\sqrt{5}}}{2}$ ≈ 2.233.

## Orthogonal projections {#Orthogonal_projections}

The *rhombicosidodecahedron* has six special [orthogonal projections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_projection), centered, on a vertex, on two types of edges, and three types of faces: triangles, squares, and pentagons. The last two correspond to the A<sub>2</sub> and H<sub>2</sub> [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane).

## Spherical tiling {#Spherical_tiling}

The rhombicosidodecahedron can also be represented as a [spherical tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_tiling), and projected onto the plane via a [stereographic projection](wikipedia://en/143431). This projection is [conformal](wikipedia://en/50627), preserving angles but not areas or lengths. Straight lines on the sphere are projected as circular arcs on the plane.

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This polyhedron is topologically related as a part of a sequence of [cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) polyhedra with vertex figure (3.4.n.4), which continues as tilings of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742). These [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) figures have (\*n32) reflectional [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678).

### Vertex arrangement {#Vertex_arrangement}

The rhombicosidodecahedron shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with three [nonconvex uniform polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedra): the [small stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157444), the [small dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157181) (having the triangular and pentagonal faces in common), and the [small rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157204) (having the square faces in common).

It also shares its vertex arrangement with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [six](wikipedia://en/14848541) or [twelve pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/14848692).

## Rhombicosidodecahedral graph {#Rhombicosidodecahedral_graph}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **rhombicosidodecahedral graph** is the [graph of vertices and edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-skeleton) of the rhombicosidodecahedron, one of the [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847). It has 60 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 120 edges, and is a [quartic graph](wikipedia://en/4669481) [Archimedean graph](wikipedia://en/44969442).[^o29]
