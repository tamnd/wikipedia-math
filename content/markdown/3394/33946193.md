# Edward Bierstone

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Bierstone"}

**Edward Bierstone** (born 1946-12-21) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202), and differential analysis. He is [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received his B.Sc. from the University of Toronto and his Ph.D. at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1972. He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in the summer of 1973.[^o5] He served as the Director of the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) from 2009 to 2013.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bierstone was elected a member of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1992 and, together with [Pierre Milman](wikipedia://en/12011763), received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) in 2005. In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] In 2018, the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o9]
