::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of five tetrahemihexahedra"}

A **compound of five tetrahemihexahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) and a symmetric arrangement of five [tetrahemihexahedra](wikipedia://en/2851834). It could be also called a hemirhombichiricosahedron. It is [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160) with [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) (**I**).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

*Image: 5 thah in icosidodecahedron.png.* Five tetrahemihexahedra in an icosidodecahedron

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). Hence it is a faceting of an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023), shown at left. It shares its edges and triangular faces with the [compound of five octahedra](wikipedia://en/8010546).
