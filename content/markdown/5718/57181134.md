# Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize"}

The **Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize** is a prize in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), first awarded in 1993. It honors mathematician [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer](wikipedia://en/36136840) (1912–1967), a self-taught [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia) mathematician who, despite significant physical disability, was very active in research in classical analysis. This award acknowledges an outstanding mathematical [monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph) of an expository nature, presenting the latest developments in an active area of mathematics research. The annually awarded prize consists of 15,000 as of 2017. The winning monograph is also published in Birkhauser-Verlag's series *Progress in Mathematics*. It is awarded by the Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Foundation.[^o2]

:::
