# Prix Francoeur

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prix Francoeur"}

The **Prix Francoeur**, or Francoeur Prize, was an award granted by the [Institut de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_France), [Academie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences), Fondation Francoeur to authors of works useful to the progress of pure and applied mathematics. Preference was given to young scholars or to geometricians not yet established. It was established in 1882 and has been discontinued.

:::
