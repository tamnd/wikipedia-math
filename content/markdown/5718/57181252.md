# Alice T. Schafer Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alice T. Schafer Prize"}

The **Alice T. Schafer Mathematics Prize** is given annually to an undergraduate woman for excellence in mathematics by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM). The prize, which carries a monetary award, is named for former AWM president and founding member [Alice T. Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_T._Schafer); it was first awarded in 1990.[^o3]

:::
