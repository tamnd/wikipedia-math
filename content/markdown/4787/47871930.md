# Wojciech Samotij

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wojciech Samotij"}

**Wojciech Samotij** (Polish: '; born 1983 in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław)[^o3]) is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) at the School of Mathematical Sciences at the [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). He is known for his work in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [additive number theory](wikipedia://en/4460333), [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o6]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He studied at the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław) where in 2007 he obtained his Master of Science degrees in mathematics and computer science. He received his PhD in 2011 at [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) on the basis of his dissertation titled *Extremal Problems In Pseudo-random Graphs And Asymptotic Enumeration* and written under the supervision of [József Balogh](wikipedia://en/64265306).[^o5]

Between 2010 and 2014, he was a fellow of the [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). Currently, he is an associate professor at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).[^o6] He published his scientific work in such journals as *Random Structures & Algorithms*, *[Journal of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society)*, or *[Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics)*.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

He received the 2013 [Kuratowski Prize](wikipedia://en/78423454) conferred jointly by the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791) to young mathematicians under the age of 30. The same year, he won the 2013 [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics).[^o9] Samotij is also the recipient of the 2016 [George Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize)[^o10] and the 2022 [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize).[^o11] In 2024 he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize for Seminal Contribution to Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leroy_P._Steele_Prize_for_Seminal_Contribution_to_Research) jointly with [József Balogh](wikipedia://en/64265306) and [Robert Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Morris_%28mathematician%29).[^o12]
