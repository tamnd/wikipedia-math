# Karim Adiprasito

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Karim Adiprasito"}

**Karim Alexander Adiprasito** (born in 1988) is a German mathematician at the [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche](wikipedia://en/57642956) who works in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He completed his PhD in 2013 at [Free University Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_Berlin) under the supervision of [Günter M. Ziegler](wikipedia://en/14628647).[^o4] He has held positions at the Hebrew University, and the University of Copenhagen. Since 2022, he is a Directeur de Recherche with the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research), situated at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University). He is of German and Indonesian descent, and bears an Indonesian surname.

:::

He was awarded the 2015 [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics)[^o5] for his work in [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468), in particular on realization spaces of [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) citing "his wide-ranging and deep contributions to discrete geometry using analytic methods particularly for his solution of old problems of Perles and Shephard (going back to Legendre and Steinitz) on [projectively unique polyhedra](wikipedia://en/77720347)."

In joint work with [June Huh](wikipedia://en/54434626) and [Eric Katz](wikipedia://en/54434627), he resolved the [Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron–Rota–Welsh_conjecture) on the [log-concavity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log-concavity) of the [characteristic polynomial of matroids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_polynomial_of_matroids).[^o6] With Huh, he is one of five winners of the 2019 New Horizons Prize for Early-Career Achievement in Mathematics, associated with the [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).[^o7]

Using [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov)'s work on spaces of bounded curvature, in joint work with [Bruno Benedetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Benedetti) he resolved the [Hirsch conjecture](wikipedia://en/10412371) for flag triangulations of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470).[^o8]

In December 2018, he [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) [Peter McMullen](wikipedia://en/40964822)'s [g-conjecture](wikipedia://en/22359636) for simplicial spheres.[^o9][^o10] For his work, he won the 2020 EMS Prize of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).
