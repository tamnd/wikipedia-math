# Dorian M. Goldfeld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dorian M. Goldfeld"}

**Dorian Morris Goldfeld** (born January 21, 1947) is an American mathematician working in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Goldfeld received his B.S. degree in 1967 from Columbia University. His doctoral dissertation, entitled "Some Methods of Averaging in the Analytical Theory of Numbers", was completed under the supervision of [Patrick X. Gallagher](wikipedia://en/59895532) in 1969, also at Columbia. He has held positions at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) ([Miller Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Fellow), 1969–1971), [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) (1971–1972), [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) (1972–1973), [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1973–1974), in Italy (1974–1976), at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) (1976–1982), [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) (1983–1985) and [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1982–1985). Since 1985, he has been a professor at Columbia University.[^o4]

He is a member of the editorial board of *[Acta Arithmetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Arithmetica)* and of *[The Ramanujan Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ramanujan_Journal)*.[^o5][^o6] On January 1, 2018 he became the Editor-in-Chief of the [Journal of Number Theory](wikipedia://en/8422003).[^o7]

He is a co-founder and board member of [Veridify Security](http://www.veridify.com), formerly SecureRF, a corporation that has developed the world's first linear-based security solutions.[^o8]

Goldfeld advised several doctoral students including [M. Ram Murty](wikipedia://en/14988649). In 1986, he brought [Shou-Wu Zhang](wikipedia://en/3582724) to the United States to study at Columbia.[^o10][^o11][^o12]

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

Goldfeld's research interests include various topics in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). In his thesis,[^o14] he proved a version of [Artin's conjecture on primitive roots](wikipedia://en/2085185) on the average without the use of the [Riemann Hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_Hypothesis).

In 1976, Goldfeld provided an ingredient for the effective solution of [Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125)'s [class number problem for imaginary quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_problem_for_imaginary_quadratic_fields).[^o15] Specifically, he proved an effective lower bound for the class number of an imaginary quadratic field assuming the existence of an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) whose [L-function](wikipedia://en/245283) had a zero of order at least 3 at $s=1/2$. (Such a curve was found soon after by [Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863) and [Zagier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagier)). This effective lower bound then allows the determination of all imaginary fields with a given [class number](wikipedia://en/150907) after a finite number of computations.

His work on the [Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](wikipedia://en/435161) includes the proof of an estimate for a partial [Euler product](wikipedia://en/398540) associated to an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225),[^o16] bounds for the order of the [Tate–Shafarevich group](wikipedia://en/25711736).[^o17]

Together with his collaborators, Dorian Goldfeld has introduced the theory of multiple [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258), objects that extend the fundamental Dirichlet series in one variable.[^o18]

He has also made contributions to the understanding of [Siegel zeroes](wikipedia://en/1967197),[^o19] to the [ABC conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_conjecture),[^o20] to [modular forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_forms) on $\operatorname{GL}(n)$,[^o21] and to cryptography (Arithmetica cipher, [Anshel–Anshel–Goldfeld key exchange](wikipedia://en/24708948)).[^o22]

Together with his wife, Dr. [Iris Anshel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_Anshel),[^o23] and father-in-law, Dr. [Michael Anshel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Anshel),[^o24] both mathematicians, Dorian Goldfeld founded the field of [braid group](wikipedia://en/550138) cryptography.[^o25][^o26]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1987 he received the [Frank Nelson Cole Prize in Number Theory](wikipedia://en/1372177), one of the prizes in [Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_Theory), for his solution of [Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125)'s [class number problem for imaginary quadratic fields](wikipedia://en/596282). He has also held the [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) (1977–1979) and in 1985 he received the Vaughan prize. In 1986 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Berkeley. In April 2009 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o28]
