# Penny Haxell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Penny Haxell"}

**Penelope Evelyn Haxell** is a Canadian mathematician who works as a professor in the department of combinatorics and optimization at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). Her research interests include [extremal combinatorics](wikipedia://en/2675917) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Haxell earned a bachelor's degree in 1988 from the University of Waterloo, and completed a doctorate in 1993 from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) under the supervision of [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956).[^o8] Since then, she has worked at the University of Waterloo, where she was promoted to full professor in 2004.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Haxell's research accomplishments include results on the [Szemerédi regularity lemma](wikipedia://en/1962417), [hypergraph](wikipedia://en/232535) generalizations of [Hall's marriage theorem](wikipedia://en/186969) (see [Haxell's matching theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haxell's_matching_theorem)), [fractional](wikipedia://en/6368430) graph [packing problems](wikipedia://en/213003), and [strong coloring](wikipedia://en/690775) of graphs.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Haxell was the 2006 winner of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o8]
