# List of Chinese provincial-level divisions by infant mortality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Chinese provincial-level divisions by infant mortality"}

Sex-based statistics regarding the rate of **infant mortality by administrative divisions in the People's Republic of China** have been collected. Disparity in rates of female [infant mortality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant_mortality) by Chinese administrative divisions has been noted in public health statistics since the [first modern Chinese census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_National_Population_Census_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) in 1982 which showed significantly higher rates of female infant mortality.[^o2] Already abnormal female infant mortality deteriorated further in censuses held in 1989 and 2000.[^o2]

:::

## Census data {#Census_data}

### 2010 {#2010}

The infant mortality rates by administrative division from the [6th National Population Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_National_Population_Census_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) held in 2010 are adjusted upwards according to a methodology by the authors Professors Hong Rongqing and Zeng Xianxin of [Capital University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_University_of_Economics_and_Business) in "Infant Mortality Reported in the 2010 Census: Bias and Adjustment", published in *Population Research* in March 2013.[^o5] The authors estimate that the infant mortality rate from the 6th Census is severely underreported by about 78%.[^o5] Undereporting of the infant mortality rate in the 6th Census is also the conclusion in a note analyzing the 6th Census by the China offices of [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF) and [UNFPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNFPA).[^o6]
