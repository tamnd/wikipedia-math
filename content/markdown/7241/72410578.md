# Martha Takane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martha Takane"}

Takane **Martha Yoko Takane Imay** is a Mexican mathematician whose research topics include [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). She is a professor at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM) and a researcher in the UNAM Institute of Mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Takane studied mathematics at UNAM, earning a bachelor's degree in 1986, master's degree in 1988, and PhD in 1992. Her doctoral dissertation, *Propiedades espectrales de las matrices de Coxeter y las matrices de adyacencia de las algebras hereditarias de tipo salvaje*, was supervised by José Antonio de la Peña.

She has been a faculty member at UNAM since 1991 and a researcher in the Mathematical Institute since 1992. She was a postdoctoral researcher at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) and the [University of Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trondheim), and has taught at the [Autonomous University of Mexico State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Mexico_State) since 2006.

She has also been active in encouraging women in mathematics in Mexico, and co-founded a center for gender studies at UNAM.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Takane won the 1992 Weizmann Prize for the best doctoral thesis in the exact sciences in Mexico. She was elected to the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1998. In 2007 UNAM gave her their Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz prize.
