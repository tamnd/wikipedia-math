# Fuensanta Aroca

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fuensanta Aroca"}

Aroca **Fuensanta Aroca Bisquert** is a Spanish mathematician who works in Mexico as a researcher in the Institute of Mathematics (Oaxaca unit) of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM). Her mathematical research involves the use of [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) to solve [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), and [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041). She has also published research on [screening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screening_%28medicine%29) for [mental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health), and has spoken on discrimination and harassment in mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Aroca was an undergraduate at the [Autonomous University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Madrid), where she graduated in 1992. She completed a Ph.D. in 2000 at the [University of Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valladolid); her dissertation, *Métodos algebraicos en ecuaciones diferenciales de primer orden en el campo complejo*, was supervised by José M. Aroca Hernández Ros.

She has been a researcher at the UNAM Institute of Mathematics (Cuernavaca unit) since 2004.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Aroca was elected to the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2022.
