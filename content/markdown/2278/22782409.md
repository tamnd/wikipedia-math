# Gary Chartrand

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gary Chartrand"}

**Gary Theodore Chartrand** (born 24 August 1936[^o3]) is an American-born [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is known for his [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on introductory graph theory and for the concept of a [highly irregular graph](wikipedia://en/42651881).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gary Chartrand was born in 1936. He was raised in [Sault Ste. Marie, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sault_Ste._Marie,_Michigan) and attended [J. W. Sexton High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._W._Sexton_High_School) located in [Lansing, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lansing,_Michigan). As an undergraduate student, he initially majored in [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering), but switched to mathematics in his junior year, in which he also became a member of the honorary mathematics society [Pi Mu Epsilon](wikipedia://en/1565972).

He earned his B.S. from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), where he majored in mathematics and minored in physical sciences and foreign languages. Michigan State University also awarded him a Master of Science and a PhD for his work in graph theory in 1964. Chartrand became the first doctoral student of Edward Nordhaus, and the first doctoral student at Michigan State University to research graph theory. His dissertation was *Graphs and Their Associated Line-Graphs*. Chartrand worked with [Frank Harary](wikipedia://en/1731731) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), where he spent a year as a Research Associate, and the two have published numerous papers together (along with other authors).

The topic of [highly irregular graphs](wikipedia://en/42651881) was introduced by Chartrand, [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Ortrud Oellermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortrud_Oellermann).[^o5]

Other contributions that Chartrand has made involve [dominating sets](wikipedia://en/1747972), [distance](wikipedia://en/1020021) in graphs, and [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743). During his career at Western Michigan University, he advised 22 doctoral students in their research on aspects of graph theory. Chartrand is currently a professor emeritus of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Western Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Michigan_University).[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

- 1977: *Graphs as Mathematical Models*, Prindle, Weber & Schmidt, reprinted 1985 as *Introductory Graph Theory* .
- 1993: (with [Ortrud R. Oellermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortrud_Oellermann)) *Applied and Algorithmic Graph Theory*, [McGraw Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGraw_Hill) .
- 2008: (with [Ping Zhang](wikipedia://en/57545783)) *Chromatic Graph Theory*, [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press) .
- 2010: (with Linda Lesniak and Ping Zhang) *Graphs & Digraphs*, 5th edition, CRC Press .
- 2010: (with Ping Zhang) *Discrete Mathematics*, Waveland Press.
- 2012: (with Albert D. Polimeni & Ping Zhang) *Mathematical Proofs: A Transition to Advanced Mathematics*, 3rd edition, Pearson.
- 2012: (with Ping Zhang) *A First Course in Graph Theory*, [Dover Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Publications).
- 2015: (with [Arthur T. Benjamin](wikipedia://en/12428320) and Ping Zhang) *The Fascinating World of Graph Theory*, [Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press) .
- 2019: (with Teresa W. Haynes, Michael A. Henning & Ping Zhang) *From Domination to Coloring: Stephen Hedetniemi's Graph Theory and Beyond*, SpringerBriefs in Mathematics.
- 2019: (with Cooroo Egan & Ping Zhang) *How to Label a Graph*, SpringerBriefs in Mathematics .
- 2021: (with Akbar Ali & Ping Zhang) *Irregularity in Graphs*, SpringerBriefs in Mathematics .
