# Royal Dutch Mathematical Society

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Royal Dutch Mathematical Society"}

The **Royal Dutch Mathematical Society** (**Koninklijk Wiskundig Genootschap** in Dutch, abbreviated as **KWG**) was founded in 1778.[^o3] Its goal is to promote the development of mathematics, both from a theoretical and applied point of view.

:::

The society publishes the quarterly journal *[Nieuw Archief voor Wiskunde](wikipedia://en/71957188)*, the magazine *Pythagoras, wiskundetijdschrift voor jongeren*[^o4] for high school children, and the scientific journal *[Indagationes Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indagationes_Mathematicae)*.

Each year the society organizes a winter [symposium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) for high school teachers. Biannually Koninklijk Wiskundig Genootschap organizes the [Dutch Mathematical Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Mathematical_Congress). Once every three years, the society awards the prestigious [Brouwer Medal](wikipedia://en/42578624) to a distinguished mathematician. This medal is named after [L. E. J. Brouwer](wikipedia://en/172896).

- [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology)

- [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University)
