# Johann Makowsky

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Makowsky"}

**Johann (János) A. Makowsky** (born March 12, 1948) is a Hungarian-born naturalised Swiss mathematician who works in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and the logical foundations of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He studied at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 1967–73. He was a student in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) of [Ernst Specker](wikipedia://en/22209158) and Hans Läuchli in mathematical logic, (Diploma in Mathematics and Physics 1971, Dr. math.sc. in 1974), of [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365) ([Topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446)) and [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960) (Algorithmics), and in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) of [Andrzej Mostowski](wikipedia://en/1029314) and [Witek Marek](wikipedia://en/51001800), where he spent 1972 as an exchange student. Makowsky held visiting positions at the Banach Center in Warsaw (Poland), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (USA), [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) (Canada), [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) (Italy), [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) (USA), [Lausanne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne_University) and [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) (Switzerland). He held regular positions at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) and the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) (Haifa, Israel) where he was a full professor.[^o4]

:::

Among his various contributions are:

- In [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), the solution of two open problems in [categoricity theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categoricity) and his study of logics with various [interpolation](wikipedia://en/2056790) and [compactness](wikipedia://en/152207) properties (partially with [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) and Jonathan Stavi).
- In [database theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database_theory), the first [undecidability](wikipedia://en/15631055) result of the consequence problem for [database dependencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database_dependencies) (with [Ashok Chandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashok_K._Chandra) and [Harry Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_R._Lewis)), his work unifying the [entity–relationship model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entity–relationship_model) and the [relational model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relational_database) of databases (with [Victor Markowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_M._Markowitz)), and his work on [Boyce–Codd normal form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyce–Codd_normal_form) (with E.V. Ravve).
- In [logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming), his fundamental studies of [Horn formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horn_formula) and their [complexity](wikipedia://en/7543) (partially with B. Mahr and A. Itai)
- In [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithms), his unifying approach to [tree-width](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree-width) and [clique-width](wikipedia://en/16795502) via model theory, leading to a general theory of [graph polynomials](wikipedia://en/56012962) and their definability in various logical formalisms (partially with I. Averbouch, [Bruno Courcelle](wikipedia://en/43140009), B. Godlin, T. Kotek, U. Rotics and [Boris Zilber](wikipedia://en/47992829)).

Makowsky was a founding member of the [European Association of Computer Science Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EACSL) in 1992, its vice-president (2002–2004) and president (2004–2009), and was a member of EACSL's executive council till 2014.[^o5] During his presidency he established the EACSL Ackermann Award for outstanding PhD theses in computer science logic. In 2008, an event dedicated to Makowsky on his 60th birthday was co-located with the annual meeting of the EACSL.[^o6]

Since 2016, he is a Professor Emeritus at the Faculty of Computer Science at the Technion, and continues his research and teaching and supervising graduate students
