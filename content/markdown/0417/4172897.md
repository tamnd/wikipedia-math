# Franz Rellich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franz Rellich"}

**Franz Rellich** (September 14, 1906 – September 25, 1955) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrians)-[German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made important contributions in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), in particular for the foundations of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) and for the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). The [Rellich–Kondrachov theorem](wikipedia://en/7907151) is named after him.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rellich was born in [Tramin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramin), then in the [County of Tyrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Tyrol). He studied from 1924 to 1929 at the universities of [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) and received his doctor's degree in 1929 under [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238) at [Georg August University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_August_University_of_Göttingen) with the thesis about *"Verallgemeinerung der Riemannschen Integrationsmethode auf Differentialgleichungen n-ter Ordnung in zwei Veränderlichen"* ("Generalization of Riemann's integration method on differential equations of *n*-th order in two variables"). When in 1933 the great mathematical-physical tradition in Göttingen terminated with the *[Machtergreifung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machtergreifung)* of the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism), Rellich, having taken an active position against Nazism, was among those forced to leave. In 1934 he became *[Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)* in [Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg), in 1942 professor in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden), and in 1946 director of the Mathematical Institute in Göttingen, being instrumental in its reconstruction. [Heinz Otto Cordes](wikipedia://en/75312624), [Erhard Heinz](wikipedia://en/31696540), [Konrad Jörgens](wikipedia://en/34870269), and [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316) were among of his doctoral students. His sister Camilla Juliana Anna was the wife of mathematician [Bartel Leendert van der Waerden](wikipedia://en/91390). Rellich died in [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Among Rellich's most important mathematical contributions are his work in the [perturbation theory](wikipedia://en/102140) of [linear operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) on [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932): he studied the dependence of the spectral family $E_\varepsilon(\lambda)$ of a [self-adjoint operator](wikipedia://en/187408) $A_\varepsilon$ on the parameter $\varepsilon$. Although the origins and applications of the problem are in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), Rellich's approach was completely abstract.

Rellich successfully worked on many [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) with [degeneracies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_degeneracy). For instance, he showed that in the elliptic case, the [Monge-Ampère differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monge-Ampere_equation), while not necessarily uniquely soluble, can have at most two solutions.

Particularly relevant to physics was Rellich's mathematical clarification of the outgoing [Sommerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Sommerfeld) conditions.
