# Preliminary reference Earth model

*Image: EarthGravityPREM.jpg.* Earth's Gravity according to PREM. Green curves show hypothetical Earths with density constant (dashed) and decreasing linearly from center to surface (stippled)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Preliminary reference Earth model"}

The **preliminary reference Earth model** (**PREM**) plots the average of [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228)'s properties by depth.[^o2] It includes a table of Earth properties, including [elastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29) properties, [attenuation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anelastic_attenuation_factor), [density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density), [pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure), and [gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_of_Earth).

:::

PREM has been widely used as the basis for [seismic tomography](wikipedia://en/377931) and related global [geophysical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics) models.[^o3] It incorporates [anelastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anelasticity) dispersion and [anisotropy](wikipedia://en/3838003) and therefore it is frequency-dependent and transversely [isotropic](wikipedia://en/14865) for the [upper mantle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_mantle_%28Earth%29).

PREM was developed by [Adam M. Dziewonski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Dziewonski) and [Don L. Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_L._Anderson) in response to guidelines of a "Standard Earth Model Committee" of the [International Association of Geodesy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Geodesy) (IAG) and the [International Association of Seismology and Physics of the Earth's Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Seismology_and_Physics_of_the_Earth’s_Interior) (IASPEI) Other Earth reference models include iasp91[^o4] and ak135.[^o5]
