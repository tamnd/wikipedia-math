# Fujita–Storm equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fujita–Storm equation"}

In [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), **Fujita–Storm equation** is a [nonlinear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear) [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564). It occurs frequently in problems of nonlinear [heat](wikipedia://en/184726) and [mass transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_transfer), [combustion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion_theory) and theory of flows in [porous media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous_medium)[^o2]

:::

$$u_{t}=a (u^{-2} u_x)_x$$
