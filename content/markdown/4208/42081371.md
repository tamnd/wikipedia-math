# Stable ∞-category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stable ∞-category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, a **stable ∞-category** is an [∞-category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-category) such that[^o2]

:::

- (i) It has a [zero object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_object).
- (ii) Every [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) in it admits a [fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_infinity_category) and cofiber.
- (iii) A triangle in it is a [fiber sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_sequence) if and only if it is a [cofiber sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cofiber_sequence).

The [homotopy category](wikipedia://en/2040756) of a stable ∞-category is [triangulated](wikipedia://en/1679700).[^o3] A stable ∞-category admits finite [limits](wikipedia://en/39248) and [colimits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colimit).[^o4]

Examples: the [derived category](wikipedia://en/985897) of an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063) and the ∞-category of [spectra](wikipedia://en/1033697) are both stable.

A **stabilization** of an [∞-category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-category) *C* having finite limits and base point is a functor from the stable ∞-category *S* to *C*. It preserves limits. The objects in the image have the structure of infinite loop spaces; whence, the notion is a generalization of the corresponding notion ([stabilization (topology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilization_%28topology%29)) in classical [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

By definition, the [t-structure](wikipedia://en/1798923) of a stable ∞-category is the t-structure of its homotopy category. Let *C* be a stable ∞-category with a t-structure. Then every filtered object $X(i), i \in \mathbb{Z}$ in *C* gives rise to a [spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/803894) $E^{p, q}_r$, which, under some conditions, converges to $\pi_{p+q} \operatorname{colim} X(i).$[^o5] By the [Dold–Kan correspondence](wikipedia://en/39431504), this generalizes the construction of the [spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/803894) associated to a filtered [chain complex](wikipedia://en/138404) of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974).
