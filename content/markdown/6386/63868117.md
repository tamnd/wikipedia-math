# Anne Canteaut

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anne Canteaut"}

**Anne Canteaut** is a French researcher in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), working at the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413) (INRIA) in Paris. She studies the design and [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of [symmetric-key algorithms](wikipedia://en/53042) and [S-boxes](wikipedia://en/240991).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Canteaut earned a diploma in engineering from [ENSTA Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENSTA_Paris) in 1993.[^o4] She completed her doctorate at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1996, with the dissertation *Attaques de cryptosystèmes à mots de poids faible et construction de fonctions t-résilientes* supervised by Paul Camion.

She is currently the chair of the INRIA Evaluation Committee,[^o6] and of the [FSE](wikipedia://en/87043) steering committee.[^o7] She was the scientific leader of the INRIA team SECRET between 2007 and 2019.

## Cryptographic primitives {#Cryptographic_primitives}

Canteaut has contributed to the design of several new cryptographic primitives:

- [DECIM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DECIM), a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) submitted to the [eSTREAM](wikipedia://en/2287686) project[^o9]
- [SOSEMANUK](wikipedia://en/4546152), a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) selected in the [eSTREAM](wikipedia://en/2287686) portfolio[^o10]
- [Shabal](wikipedia://en/58888784), a [hash function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hash_function) submitted to the [SHA-3 competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHA-3_competition)[^o11]
- [Prince](wikipedia://en/51924657), a lightweight [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594)[^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Canteaut was awarded the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour) in 2019.[^o14] She became a fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043) in 2024.[^o15] She was elected a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2025.[^o16]
