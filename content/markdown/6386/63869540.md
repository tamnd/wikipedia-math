# Dan Margalit (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Margalit"}

**Dan Margalit** (born March 6, 1976) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University). His research fields include [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), with a particular focus on [mapping class groups of surfaces](wikipedia://en/31060586).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Margalit earned his bachelor's degree from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o4] He earned his doctorate from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2003, advised by [Benson Farb](wikipedia://en/32518776). His thesis was titled *Algebra versus Topology in Mapping Class Groups*. Margalit was a postdoctoral scholar at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) from 2003 to 2008. He was a faculty member at [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University) from 2008 to 2010, before going to the Georgia Institute of Technology.[^o4] In 2023, Margalit moved to Vanderbilt University as chair of the Department of Mathematics.[^o6]

Margalit is known for his work in exposition of geometric group theory, particularly his book with [Benson Farb](wikipedia://en/32518776), *A Primer on Mapping Class Groups*.[^o14]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Margalit became a [fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in 2019; he was recognized "for contributions to low-dimensional topology and geometric group theory, exposition, and mentoring."[^o8] Margalit was a 2009 Sloan Research Fellow.[^o9] For 2021 he received the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) of the AMS.[^o10] For 2024 he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition.[^o11]
