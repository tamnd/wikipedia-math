# Bernardo Uribe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernardo Uribe"}

**Bernardo Uribe Jongbloed** (born 1975) is a Colombian mathematician. Uribe's research deals with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [topology](wikipedia://en/38801) with [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) applications.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Uribe graduated from secondary school in [Bogotá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogotá) and then studied from 1994 to 1998 at the [Universidad de Los Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Los_Andes_%28Colombia%29). In 2002 he received his PhD from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison) with thesis *Twisted K-Theory and Orbifold Cohomology of the Symmetric Product* under the supervision of [Alejandro Ádem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Ádem) and [Yongbin Ruan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongbin_Ruan). He was a postdoc at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn. In 2003/04 he was an assistant professor at the University of Michigan. He taught as a professor at the [Universidad Nacional de Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Nacional_de_Colombia) and as a full professor from 2012 to 2014 at Bogotá's Universidad de los Andes. Since 2014 has been a professor at the [Universidad del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Norte,_Colombia) in [Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla). In 2008/09 he was a visiting scholar in [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City). In 2010 he worked with [Wolfgang Lück](wikipedia://en/26197113) at the University of Münster.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2012 he received a [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award), with which he was at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o6]

Uribe received the Mathematics Prize of the [Third World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2012. In 2018 he was an invited speaker with talk *The evenness conjecture in equivariant unitary bordism* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro. From July 2017 to June 2019 he was President of the [Colombian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/76532939).[^o7]
