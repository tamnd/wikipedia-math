# Heiner Zieschang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heiner Zieschang"}

**Heiner Zieschang** (12 November 1936 in [Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiel) – 5 April 2004 in [Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochum)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a professor at [Ruhr University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University) in [Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochum) from 1968 till 2002. He was a [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist). In 1996 he was an honorary doctor of [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse) and in 1997 he was an honorary professor of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).

:::
