In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rectified tesseract**, **rectified 8-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) (4-dimensional [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471)) bounded by 24 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 8 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280), and 16 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606). It has half the vertices of a [runcinated tesseract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_tesseract), with its construction, called a **runcic tesseract**.

:::

It has two uniform constructions, as a *rectified 8-cell* r{4,3,3} and a [cantellated demitesseract](wikipedia://en/1645899#The_D4_.5B31.2C1.2C1.5D_group_family_.28Demitesseract.29), rr{3,3<sup>1,1</sup>}, the second alternating with two types of tetrahedral cells.

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as tC<sub>8</sub>.

::: {#o9 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The rectified tesseract may be constructed from the [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) by [truncating](wikipedia://en/2192366) its vertices at the midpoints of its edges.

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) of the vertices of the rectified tesseract with edge length 2 is given by all permutations of:

$$(0,\ \pm\sqrt{2},\ \pm\sqrt{2},\ \pm\sqrt{2})$$

:::

## Projections {#Projections}

In the cuboctahedron-first parallel projection of the rectified tesseract into 3-dimensional space, the image has the following layout:

- The projection envelope is a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285).
- A cuboctahedron is inscribed in this cube, with its vertices lying at the midpoint of the cube's edges. The cuboctahedron is the image of two of the cuboctahedral cells.
- The remaining 6 cuboctahedral cells are projected to the square faces of the cube.
- The 8 tetrahedral volumes lying at the triangular faces of the central cuboctahedron are the images of the 16 tetrahedral cells, two cells to each image.
