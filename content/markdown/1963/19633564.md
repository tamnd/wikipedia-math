# Mikhail Postnikov

Mikhailovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Postnikov"}

**Mikhail Mikhailovich Postnikov** (Михаи́л Миха́йлович По́стников; 27 October 1927 – 27 May 2004) was a Soviet mathematician, known for his work in [algebraic](wikipedia://en/38801) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Shatura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shatura), near [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow). He received his Ph.D. from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the direction of [Lev Pontryagin](wikipedia://en/582075), and then became a professor at this university. He died in Moscow.
