# Jan Mandel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Mandel"}

**Jan Mandel** is a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat_nauk) from the faculty of mathematics and physics, [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) and was a senior research scientist there. Since 1986, he is professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver). Since 2013, he is senior scientist at the Institute of Computer Science of the [Czech Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

He has worked in the field of [multigrid methods](wikipedia://en/1747763) and [domain decomposition methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_decomposition_method). He developed the [balancing domain decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balancing_domain_decomposition) method and, with coauthors, published the convergence proofs of the [FETI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FETI), [FETI-DP](wikipedia://en/16230368), and [BDDC](wikipedia://en/16229578) methods, and the proof of the equivalence of the [FETI-DP](wikipedia://en/16230368) and the [BDDC](wikipedia://en/16229578) methods. He has been involved in the field of [dynamic data driven application systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_data_driven_application_system) and [data assimilation](wikipedia://en/2458875) with applications in [wildfire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfire_modeling) and [epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemic_model) [modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_simulation). He has contributed to the [WRF-Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WRF-Fire) software.
