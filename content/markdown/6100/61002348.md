# Alik Ismail-Zadeh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alik Ismail-Zadeh"}

**Alik Ismail-Zadeh** is a mathematical geophysicist known for his contribution to computational geodynamics[^o3] and natural hazard[^o4][^o5] studies, pioneering work on data assimilation in geodynamics[^o6] as well as for outstanding service to the Earth and space science community.[^o7] He is Research Professor ([Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)) at the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) in Germany.[^o8]

:::

## Biography and academic career {#Biography_and_academic_career}

Born in Baku, Azerbaijan, Ismail-Zadeh attended the Bulbul specialized secondary music school of the [Baku Academy of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku_Academy_of_Music) and Baku High School #134 (specialized in mathematics) between 1967 and 1978. He graduated from the [Baku State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku_State_University) and the Lomonosov [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1983 before being awarded a Ph.D. degree in 1990 and D. Sc. ([Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)) in 1997 from the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in Moscow, Russia. Ismail-Zadeh has been affiliated with the [Azerbaijan National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_National_Academy_of_Sciences), Institute of Geology and Geophysics (1983-1986, 1990–1992).[^o10] He has been chief scientist, research professor and scientific leader of research group "Computational Geodynamics and Geohazard Modeling" of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) Institute of Earthquake Prediction Theory and Mathematical Geophysics, Moscow (1992–2022).[^o11]

He has been a [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar), lecturer and professor at several universities and academic centers, including the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (UK),[^o12] [University of California at Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Los_Angeles), the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) (Japan),[^o13] [Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_Physique_du_Globe_de_Paris) (France),[^o14] the [University of Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Uppsala) (Sweden),[^o15] and the Abdus Salam [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) in Trieste (Italy).[^o16]

## Research {#Research}

Ismail-Zadeh has conducted research in the fields of [geodynamics](wikipedia://en/9134092), [seismology](wikipedia://en/28935), [sedimentary basins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_basin) and [tectonics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tectonics) (incl. research for [petroleum industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_industry)), [geological hazards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geological_hazard), [disaster risk reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_risk_reduction) analysis, [science diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_diplomacy), and [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History). He is a principal author and co-author of over 150 peer-reviewed papers and several books published by the [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), [Springer Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Nature), and others.[^o18] Scientific results obtained by him and his group are as follows:

**Inverse problems and data assimilation**

- Introduction of data assimilation in models of geodynamics and applications to mantle (plume) dynamics.;[^o19][^o20]
- Reconstruction of sinking [lithosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithosphere) in the southeastern Carpathians;[^o21]
- Reconstruction of the western Pacific and Philippine plate subduction beneath the Japanese islands and implication on the Japan Sea opening;[^o22]
- Reconstruction of flow and temperature inside volcanic lava flow based on surface observations;[^o23]
- Restoration of sedimentary basin evolution complicated by salt tectonics: application to Pricaspian basin of the East European platform.[^o24][^o25]

**Lithosphere dynamics and seismicity**

- Model of the block-and-fault dynamics[^o26][^o27][^o28]
- Quantitative models of a fault network interaction in the Tibet-Himalayan region to explain seismicity and slip rates at major regional faults;[^o29]
- Peculiarities of the Vrancea intermediate-depth seismicity[^o30]

**Seismic and volcanic hazard and risk**

- Deterministic and probabilistic seismic hazard assessments for the Vrancea region (Romania), Tibet-Himalaya, and Shillong Plateau (India).[^o31][^o32][^o33][^o34] Earthquake risk assessment for the Baku city (Azerbaijan);[^o35]
- Volcanic lava hazard evaluation based on lava flow with debris.[^o36]

**Tectonic stress**

- Understanding of tectonic stress localization and its change in the lithospheric slab in terms of style of the slab subduction;[^o37]
- Explanation of coexisting shortening - extension and seismic activity in the Central Apennines by the buoyancy of the lithosphere;[^o38][^o39]
- Tectonic stress localization in the Vrancea earthquake-prone body beneath the south-eastern Carpathians.[^o40][^o41]

**Sedimentary basins**

- Introduction of eclogitization-induced mantle flow mechanism for sedimentary basin evolution;[^o42]
- Quantitative models of the evolution of intracratonic sedimentary basins in North America (Illinois, Michigan and Williston basins),[^o43] East European (Dnieper-Donets, Moscow, Pre-Ural, Timan-Pechora basins),[^o44][^o45] and Siberian ([Tunguska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunguska_Basin) and Viluy basins) platforms;[^o46][^o47]
- Models of geothermal evolution of the Astrakhan Arch of the Pricaspian Basin.[^o48][^o49]

**Numerical methods for geodynamics**

- New numerical methods and algorithms of an enhanced accuracy to study problems of Earth's dynamics;[^o50]
- Numerical methodology for data assimilation in geodynamics (backward advection, variational/adjoint, and quasi-reversibility methods).[^o51]

**Gravitational and thermal instability**

- Theoretical results in problems of Rayleigh-Taylor and Rayleigh-Bernard instabilities of the (geo) structures including analysis of Newtonian, Maxwell, non-Newtonian power law, and perfectly plastic rheologies;[^o52][^o53][^o54]
- Applications to salt diapirism: Models of salt structure evolution in the Pricaspain Basin[^o55]

## Organizational activities {#Organizational_activities}

Alik Ismail-Zadeh is active in promoting science research, science excellency and science for society worldwide. From 2018 to 2021, Ismail-Zadeh served as (inaugural) secretary-general of the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council) (ISC).[^o57] Also, he was (inaugural) chair of the ISC Awards Programme Committee. Currently, he is Vice-Chair of the ISC Fellowship Council[^o58] and Chair of the Scientific Committee of the Integrated Research on Disaster Risk Programme[^o59] jointly co-sponsored by ISC and [United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_for_Disaster_Risk_Reduction) (UNDRR).

Ismail-Zadeh was elected secretary-general of the [International Union of Geodesy and Geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Geodesy_and_Geophysics) (IUGG) in 2007, and served the union for 12 years in this capacity (until 2019).[^o60] He also served the union as chair of the IUGG Commission on Geophysical Risk and Sustainability (2003–2007). He is chair of the IUGG Commission on Mathematical Geophysics (2019–2027).[^o61] He has been a member of the scientific council of the UNESCO East African Institute for Fundamental Research (EAIFR), Kigali, Rwanda, since 2018.[^o62]

He was a founding member and the (inaugural) governing board member of the European Association for the Promotion of Science and Technology ([EuroScience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroScience));[^o63] (inaugural) president of the Natural Hazards Section of the [American Geophysical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Geophysical_Union) (AGU);[^o64] chair of the Committee on Sergei Soloviev Medal on Natural Hazards of the [European Geosciences Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Geosciences_Union) (EGU); chair of the steering committee of GeoUnions grouped under the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council);[^o65] and council member of the [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO) International Geoscience Program.[^o66] He served the [Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Nuclear-Test-Ban_Treaty_Organization) \[CTBTO\],[^o67] the Group on Earth Observations \[GEO\],[^o68] the [United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_for_Disaster_Risk_Reduction) \[UNDRR\][^o69][^o70] and the United Nations [International Year of Planet Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Year_of_Planet_Earth) \[IYPE; 2007-2009\][^o71] in various capacities.

Alik Ismail-Zadeh has been editor or member of several editorial/advisory boards, including *[Surveys in Geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveys_in_Geophysics)*, Springer Nature;[^o72] Special Publications of Geodesy and Geophysics series, Cambridge University Press;[^o73] Computational Seismology and Geodynamics, American Geophysical Union[^o74]
