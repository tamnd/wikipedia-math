# Rolle

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rolle"}

**Rolle** () is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Switzerland) in the [Canton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantons_of_Switzerland) of [Vaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaud) in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland). It was the seat of the district of [Rolle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolle_%28district%29) until 2006, when it became part of the district of [Nyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyon_District). It is located on the northwestern shore of [Lake Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Geneva) (*Lac Léman*) between [Nyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyon) and [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne). Rolle is approximately 30 km northeast of [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) (Genève) in the [La Côte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Côte) wine-growing region, and has views of the high [Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alps).[^o3]

:::

Rolle is also the birthplace of [Frédéric-César de la Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederic-Cesar_de_la_Harpe) (1754–1838), who was the tutor of [Alexander I of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_I_of_Russia) and was largely responsible for the independence of the [Canton of Vaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaud) from the [Bernese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Bern).[^o4]

## History {#History}

*Image: ETH-BIB-Rolle-LBS H1-024455.tif.* Aerial view (1964)

Rolle is first mentioned in 1294 as *Rotuli*.[^o22] In 1295 it was known as Ruello and as Ruelloz, the city, in 1330 after it passed into the hands of Jean De Grailly the 1st, a Knight entrusted to care for the future King Edward the 2nd.

### Prehistoric settlements {#Prehistoric_settlements}

A late [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age) lake side settlement was discovered and partially destroyed in 1835 during construction of the artificial island of *[Île de la Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Île_de_la_Harpe)*. A second settlement from the same period was found in Fleur d'Eau. In 1984, in La Combe, a first to third century AD [Gallo-Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallo-Roman_culture) estate was discovered. The remains of the moat and the ramparts of Rolle Castle were uncovered in 1985. Fragments of a medieval city wall were found in the Champ de verse which may have come from the village of Saint-Nicolas de Ver.[^o22]

### Medieval Rolle {#Medieval_Rolle}

In 1261, the Lords of Mont planned to build a city along the lake that would compete with the [Aubonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aubonne) and [Saint-Prex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Prex). By around 1264, Rolle Castle was built to protect the pier at the lake. However, the planned city was never built by the Mont family. In 1291, the castle was in possession of Count Amadeus V of Savoy, who granted it to several different families as a [fief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fief). In 1295 it passed to the Knight Jean De Grailly the 1st who named it Ruello changed centuries later to Rolle. In the course of the rivalry between the Counts of Savoy and the Lords of Vaud, in 1319 Amadeus V of Savoy finally built a city around the castle. This new city closed a gap in the savoy settlements on the northern shores of Lake Geneva. The layout of the town, a large main street running parallel to the lake shore with a cross street that connects the port with the hinterland, follow the construction patterns of typical [Zähringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Zähringen) towns.[^o22]

The residents of Saint-Nicolas de Ver, possibly an unsuccessful town founded by the Lords de Mont, settled in Rolle. The new city, which was built with a [palisade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palisade), was granted a [city charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_charter) along the lines of the charter of [Moudon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moudon). As compensation, Jean de Mont became a deputy ruler and received a dairy farm and half of the furnaces and mills. Starting in 1366, the citizens became exempt from tolls in Aubonne. The [Duke of Savoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_of_Savoy) granted the right to hold a [weekly market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_town) in 1425. In 1484, he elevated it from a feudal property to a free city.[^o22]

The fief of Le Rosey in the district of Rolle belonged to the lord of [Vufflens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vufflens) in the middle of the 14th Century. It later passed into the possession of the lord of [Avenches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avenches) and the Praroman family.

During the Bernese invasion of 1536, both [Le Rosey Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Rosey_Castle) and Rolle Castle were attacked and burned.[^o22]

### Under Bernese rule {#Under_Bernese_rule}

Under Bernese rule (1536–1798) Rolle was part of the [bailiwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailiwick) of Morges. In 1558, the Bernese merchant Hans Steiger, who was already the lord of Mont-le-Grand, acquired the barony of Rolle. His family retained the property until the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution). The barony included the town of Rolle (except the fief of Les Uttins which belonged until the 18th century to the La Harpe family), Tartegnin, Vinzel, Luins, half of Essertines-sur-Rolle, some homes in Begnins, the region of Vincy, and Saint-Vincent (now in [Gilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilly)), Bursinel and in 1615 they acquired Le Rosey Castle, Dully and Le Vaud. The judicial court was composed of the lord, his deputy, a court clerk, and ten members from Rolle and villages in the district. One of ten members governed the city. In 1740, the town bought itself out from under some taxes and duties.[^o22]

### The end of Bernese rule and modern Rolle {#The_end_of_Bernese_rule_and_modern_Rolle}

Rolle was the birthplace of [Frédéric-César de La Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frédéric-César_de_La_Harpe) and [Amédée de La Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amédée_Emmanuel_Francois_Laharpe), two important figures in the French Revolution and the end of the [Ancien Régime of Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancien_Régime_of_Switzerland). Toward the end of the 18th Century, it was a center of revolutionary fervor. Amédée de La Harpe presided, in Rolle on 15 July 1791, over a revolutionary banquet. Following the [French invasion of Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_invasion_of_Switzerland) in 1798, Rolle became the seat of a district of the same name. In 1799 the [Helvetic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helvetic_Republic) bought the castle from the municipality and used it until 1974 as the seat of government. In 1802, during the [Bourla-papey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourla-papey) uprising, patrician land titles and tax records were burned in the archives at Rolle Castle.[^o22]

## Geography {#Geography}

*Image: Le lac léman -1.jpg.* Lake Geneva and the Alps from Rolle

*Image: Rolle-town.JPG.* Rolle, aerial view

Rolle has an area, , of \2.7 km2. Of this area, \0.81 km2 or 29.6% is used for agricultural purposes, while \0.15 km2 or 5.5% is forested. Of the rest of the land, \1.74 km2 or 63.5% is settled (buildings or roads), \0.02 km2 or 0.7% is either rivers or lakes.[^o12]

Of the built up area, industrial buildings made up 4.0% of the total area while housing and buildings made up 28.5% and transportation infrastructure made up 18.6%. Power and water infrastructure as well as other special developed areas made up 4.4% of the area while parks, green belts and sports fields made up 8.0%. Out of the forested land, 3.3% of the total land area is heavily forested and 2.2% is covered with orchards or small clusters of trees. Of the agricultural land, 17.2% is used for growing crops and 3.3% is pastures, while 9.1% is used for orchards or vine crops. Of the water in the municipality, 0.4% is in lakes and 0.4% is in rivers and streams.[^o12]

The municipality was part of the [Rolle District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolle_District) until it was dissolved on 31 August 2006, and Rolle became part of the new district of Nyon.[^o13]

The municipality is located on Lake Geneva on the Geneva-Lausanne highway.

## Heritage sites of national significance {#Heritage_sites_of_national_significance}

[Rolle Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolle_Castle), the Library of the Community of Rolle and [Île de la Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Île_de_la_Harpe) are listed as Swiss [heritage site of national significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_inventory_of_cultural_property_of_national_and_regional_significance). ## Politics {#Politics}

In the [2007 federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Swiss_federal_election) the most popular party was the [SP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) which received 26.57% of the vote. The next three most popular parties were the [SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People's_Party) (21.74%), the [FDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FDP.The_Liberals) (13.66%) and the [Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Party_of_Switzerland) (12.09%). In the federal election, a total of 982 votes were cast, and the [voter turnout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_turnout) was 42.2%.[^o27]

## Economy {#Economy}

Rolle has gained popularity as a company [tax haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax_haven) in Switzerland. [Yahoo!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo!) moved its European headquarters from UK to Rolle in 2008 and [Ineos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ineos) moved its principal executive offices to Rolle from UK in 2010, for tax reasons. [Cisco Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisco_Systems) also has a decent sized [Research and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_Development) site there.

, Rolle had an unemployment rate of 6.4%. , there were 45 people employed in the [primary economic sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_sector_of_the_economy) and about 7 businesses involved in this sector. 388 people were employed in the [secondary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_sector_of_the_economy) and there were 46 businesses in this sector. 2,327 people were employed in the [tertiary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy), with 275 businesses in this sector.[^o18] There were 1,995 residents of the municipality who were employed in some capacity, of which females made up 44.0% of the workforce.

the total number of [full-time equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full-time_equivalent) jobs was 2,417. The number of jobs in the primary sector was 27, of which 26 were in agriculture and 1 was in fishing or fisheries. The number of jobs in the secondary sector was 367 of which 224 or (61.0%) were in manufacturing and 107 (29.2%) were in construction. The number of jobs in the tertiary sector was 2,023. In the tertiary sector; 966 or 47.8% were in wholesale or retail sales or the repair of motor vehicles, 43 or 2.1% were in the movement and storage of goods, 93 or 4.6% were in a hotel or restaurant, 81 or 4.0% were in the information industry, 33 or 1.6% were the insurance or financial industry, 82 or 4.1% were technical professionals or scientists, 330 or 16.3% were in education and 182 or 9.0% were in health care.[^o29]

, there were 1,314 workers who commuted into the municipality and 1,187 workers who commuted away. The municipality is a net importer of workers, with about 1.1 workers entering the municipality for every one leaving. About 6.1% of the workforce coming into Rolle are coming from outside Switzerland, while 0.3% of the locals commute out of Switzerland for work.[^o30] Of the working population, 15.5% used public transportation to get to work, and 56.3% used a private car.[^o18]

## Education {#Education}

In Rolle about 1,163 or (27.5%) of the population have completed non-mandatory [upper secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Switzerland#Secondary), and 672 or (15.9%) have completed additional higher education (either [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Switzerland) or a *[Fachhochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fachhochschule)*). Of the 672 who completed tertiary schooling, 44.5% were Swiss men, 26.8% were Swiss women, 16.4% were non-Swiss men and 12.4% were non-Swiss women.[^o20]

In the 2009/2010 school year there were a total of 618 students in the Rolle school district. In the Vaud cantonal school system, two years of non-obligatory pre-school are provided by the political districts.[^o33] During the school year, the political district provided pre-school care for a total of 1,249 children of which 563 children (45.1%) received subsidized pre-school care. The canton's [primary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_school) program requires students to attend for four years. There were 334 students in the municipal primary school program. The obligatory lower secondary school program lasts for six years and there were 262 students in those schools. There were also 22 students who were home schooled or attended another non-traditional school.[^o34]

, there were 414 students in Rolle who came from another municipality, while 164 residents attended schools outside the municipality.[^o30]

It is also the location for the main campus of [Institut Le Rosey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Le_Rosey), one of the most prestigious [boarding schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_school) in the world.[^o35] Notable former residents of Rolle, and alumni of Le Rosey, include: [Aga Khan IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aga_Khan_IV), [King Albert II of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_II_of_Belgium), [King Baudouin I of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baudouin_I_of_Belgium), [King Fuad II of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuad_II_of_Egypt), [King Ntare V of Burundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ntare_V_of_Burundi), [Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Reza_Pahlavi), and [Prince Rainier III of Monaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainier_III,_Prince_of_Monaco).[^o36]
