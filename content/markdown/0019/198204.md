# Tetragonal crystal system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tetragonal crystal system"}

*Image: WulfeniteUSGOV.jpg.* An example of tetragonal crystals, [wulfenite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulfenite)

*Image: TP30-CrFe crystalmaker.pdf.* Two different views (top down and from the side) of the unit cell of *tP*30-CrFe (σ-phase [Frank–Kasper structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank–Kasper_phases)) that show its different side lengths, making this structure a member of the tetragonal crystal system.

In [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography), the **tetragonal crystal system** is one of the seven [crystal systems](wikipedia://en/456410). Tetragonal [crystal lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_lattice) result from stretching a cubic lattice along one of its lattice vectors, so that the [cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_%28geometry%29) becomes a rectangular [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) with a square base (*a* by *a*) and height (*c*, which is different from *a*).

:::

## Bravais lattices {#Bravais_lattices}

There are two tetragonal Bravais lattices: the primitive tetragonal and the body-centered tetragonal.

::: {#o3 .table cue=table}

| Bravais lattice | Primitive tetragonal | Body-centered tetragonal |
|---|---|---|
| [Pearson symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearson_symbol) | tP | tI |
| [Unit cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_cell) | *Image: Tetragonal.svg.* | *Image: Body-centered tetragonal.svg.* |

:::

The face-centered tetragonal lattice is equivalent to the body-centered tetragonal lattice with a smaller unit cell,[^o6] while the base-centered tetragonal lattice is equivalent to the primitive tetragonal lattice with a smaller unit cell.[^o7]

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="4 (crystal class)"}
