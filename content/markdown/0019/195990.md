# Polynomial sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polynomial sequence"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **polynomial sequence** is a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) indexed by the nonnegative [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) 0, 1, 2, 3, ..., in which each [index](wikipedia://en/23624339) is equal to the [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652) of the corresponding polynomial. Polynomial sequences are a topic of interest in [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533) and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), as well as [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

:::

## Examples {#Examples}

Some polynomial sequences arise in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713) as the solutions of certain [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753):

- [Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917)
- [Chebyshev polynomials](wikipedia://en/184539)
- [Legendre polynomials](wikipedia://en/100349)
- [Zernike polynomials](wikipedia://en/2025291)
- [Jacobi polynomials](wikipedia://en/30863587)

Others come from [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685):

- [Hermite polynomials](wikipedia://en/195984)

Many are studied in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and combinatorics:

- [Monomials](wikipedia://en/357416)
- [Rising factorials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rising_factorial)
- [Falling factorials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falling_factorial)
- [All-one polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-one_polynomial)
- [Abel polynomials](wikipedia://en/2094497)
- [Bell polynomials](wikipedia://en/572498)
- [Bernoulli polynomials](wikipedia://en/228161)
- [Cyclotomic polynomials](wikipedia://en/171992)
- [Dickson polynomials](wikipedia://en/15640469)
- [Fibonacci polynomials](wikipedia://en/483720)
- [Lagrange polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrange_polynomials)
- [Lucas polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_polynomials)
- [Spread polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spread_polynomials)
- [Touchard polynomials](wikipedia://en/342592)
- [Rook polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rook_polynomials)
