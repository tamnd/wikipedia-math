# Associativity isomorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Associativity isomorphism"}

In mathematics, specifically in the field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), the **associativity isomorphism** implements the notion of [associativity](wikipedia://en/1335) with respect to monoidal products in semi-groupal (or monoidal-without-unit) categories.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A category, $\mathcal{C}$, is called semi-groupal if it comes equipped with a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) $\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}$ such that the pair $(A, B) \mapsto A \otimes B$ for $A, B \in \text{ob}(\mathcal{C})$, as well as a collection of natural [isomorphisms](wikipedia://en/14828) known as the **associativity isomorphisms** (or "associators").[^o4][^o5] These isomorphisms, $a_{X, Y, Z}: X \otimes (Y \otimes Z) \to (X \otimes Y) \otimes Z$, are such that the following "pentagon identity" diagram commutes.

:::

## Applications {#Applications}

### In tensor categories {#In_tensor_categories}

A tensor category,[^o9] or [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604), is a semi-groupal category with an identity object, $I$, such that $I \otimes A \cong A$ and $A \otimes I \cong A$. [modular tensor categories](wikipedia://en/79289899) have many applications in physics, especially in the field of topological quantum field theories.[^o10][^o11]
