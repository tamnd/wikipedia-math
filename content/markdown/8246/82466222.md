# Ramiz Aliguliyev

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Ramiz Aliguliyev"}

**Ramiz Aliguliyev** (Ramiz Məhəmməd oğlu Alıquliyev; March 20, 1961) is an [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan) computer scientist, director of the Institute of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at [Azerbaijan Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Technical_University)[^o5], head of department at the Institute of Information Technology of [Ministry of Science and Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Science_and_Education_%28Azerbaijan%29), doctor of technical sciences, professor, corresponding member of [Azerbaijan National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (ANAS).[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ramiz Aliguliyev was born on March 20, 1961, in [Barda district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barda_District), Azerbaijan SSR.[^o8][^o9]

In 1983, he graduated from the Faculty of Applied Mathematics of the Azerbaijan State University named after S. M. Kirov (currently [Baku State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku_State_University)).[^o9][^o8]

In 1983-1988, he worked at the Institute of Cybernetics of the Azerbaijan SSR Academy of Sciences, and in 1988-2002, at the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics.[^o8]

In 2003, he worked as the head of the sector at the Institute of Information Technology,[^o8][^o6] and since 2004, he has been working as the head of the department.

In 2017, he was elected a corresponding member of ANAS.[^o8]

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

Artificial Intelligence; Data Mining; Big Data Analytics; Natural Language Processing (NLP); Text Mining; E-government Analysis; Scientometrics; Evolutionary and Swarm Intelligence Algorithms.[^o6]

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

In 2000-2002, he completed his doctoral studies at the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics (now the Institute of Mathematics).[^o6]

In 2002, he defended his PhD dissertation entitled “*Investigation of the Qualitative Properties of Solutions to Second-Order Degenerate Elliptic Equations*” for the degree of Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (Doctor of Philosophy) in the specialty 01.01.02 (1211.01) – Differential Equations at the Dissertation Council of the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics (now the Institute of Mathematics).[^o9][^o8]

In 2004–2009, he completed the Doctor of Sciences program at the Institute of Information Technology. In 2010, he defended his doctoral dissertation entitled “*Methods and Algorithms for Intelligent Analysis of Textual Information*” at the Dissertation Council of the Institute of Cybernetics (now the Institute of Mathematics) for the degree of Doctor of Technical Sciences.[^o9][^o8]

In 2019, he was invited to Federation University Australia to conduct joint research.[^o12]

Aliguliyev has supervised 4 PhDs. He currently supervising 12 PhD students.[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2005, he received Diploma of Azerbaijan National Academy of Sciences.[^o9]

In 2013, his article was awarded 1st place for the best scientific work in the "3rd Joint ICT Award Competition" held by the Science Development Foundation under the president of the Republic of Azerbaijan, together with the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology.[^o6]

In 2014, he was awarded the ["Taraggi" medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tereggi_Medal) by the order of the president of the Republic of Azerbaijan.[^o3]

In 2020–2025 Ramiz Aliguliyev has been recognized among the world's top 2% of scientists in the field of artificial intelligence identified by Stanford University.[^o31][^o32][^o33][^o34][^o35][^o36]
