# Homological dimension

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Homological dimension"}

**Homological dimension** may refer to the [global dimension](wikipedia://en/2291739) of a ring. It may also refer to any other concept of dimension that is defined in terms of [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), which includes:

:::

- [Projective dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_dimension) of a module, based on projective resolutions
- [Injective dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_dimension) of a module, based on injective resolutions
- [Weak dimension](wikipedia://en/38263686) of a module, or flat dimension, based on flat resolutions
- [Weak global dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_global_dimension) of a ring, based on the weak dimension of its modules
- [Cohomological dimension](wikipedia://en/9880197) of a group
