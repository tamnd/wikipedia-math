# Snub tetrapentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub tetrapentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub tetrapentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{5,4}.

:::

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual is called an *order-5-4 floret pentagonal tiling*, defined by [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V3.3.4.3.5.

*Image: H2-5-4-floret.svg.*

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

The *snub tetrapentagonal tiling* is fourth in a series of snub polyhedra and tilings with [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) 3.3.4.3.*n*.
