# Truncated tetrapentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated tetrapentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated tetrapentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1,2</sub>{4,5} or tr{4,5}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_tetrapentagonal\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated tetrapentagonal tiling with mirror lines.

There are four small index subgroup constructed from \[5,4\] by mirror removal and alternation. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors.

A radical subgroup is constructed \[5\*,4\], index 10, as \[5<sup>\+</sup>,4\], (5\*2) with gyration points removed, becoming orbifold ([\*22222](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/22222_symmetry)), and its direct subgroup \[5\*,4\]<sup>\+</sup>, index 20, becomes orbifold (22222).
