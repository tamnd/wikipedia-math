# Truncated order-4 pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-4 pentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-4 pentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{5,4}.

:::

## Uniform colorings {#Uniform_colorings}

A half symmetry \[1+,4,5\] = \[5,5\] coloring can be constructed with two colors of decagons. This coloring is called a *truncated pentapentagonal tiling*.

*Image: Uniform tiling 552-t012.png.*

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

There is only one subgroup of \[5,5\], \[5,5\]<sup>\+</sup>, removing all the mirrors. This symmetry can be doubled to [542 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/542_symmetry) by adding a bisecting mirror.
