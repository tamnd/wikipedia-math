# Rhombitetrapentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombitetrapentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombitetrapentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,2</sub>{4,5}.

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Deltoidal tetrapentagonal tiling"}

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual is called the *deltoidal tetrapentagonal tiling* with [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V.4.4.4.5.
