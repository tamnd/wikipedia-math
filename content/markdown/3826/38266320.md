# Tetrapentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrapentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tetrapentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>1</sub>{4,5} or r{4,5}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A half symmetry \[1<sup>\+</sup>,4,5\] = \[5,5\] construction exists, which can be seen as two colors of pentagons. This coloring can be called a *rhombipentapentagonal tiling*.

*Image: Uniform tiling 552-t02.png.*

:::
