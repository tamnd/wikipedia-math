# E-semigroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="E-semigroup"}

In the area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) known as [semigroup theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semigroup_theory), an ***E*-semigroup** is a [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) in which the [idempotents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent) form a [subsemigroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsemigroup).[^o2]

:::

Certain classes of *E*-semigroups have been studied long before the more general class, in particular, a [regular semigroup](wikipedia://en/6797677) that is also an *E*-semigroup is known as an [orthodox semigroup](wikipedia://en/43280816).

Weipoltshammer proved that the notion of [weak inverse](wikipedia://en/22797782) (the existence of which is one way to define [*E*-inversive semigroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-inversive_semigroup)) can also be used to define/characterize *E*-semigroups as follows: a semigroup *S* is an *E*-semigroup if and only if, for all *a* and *b* ∈ *S*, *W*(*ab*) = *W*(*b*)*W*(*a*), where *W*(*a*) ≝ {*x* ∈ *S* | *xax* = *x*} is the set of weak inverses of *a*.[^o2]
