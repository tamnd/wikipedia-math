# Plebanski tensor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Plebanski tensor"}

The **Plebanski tensor** is an [order 4 tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_order) in [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024) constructed from the [trace-free Ricci tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace-free_Ricci_tensor). It was first defined by [Jerzy Plebański](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Plebański) in 1964.[^o2]

:::

Let $S_{ab}$ be the trace-free Ricci tensor:

$$S_{ab}=R_{ab}-\frac{1}{4}Rg_{ab}.$$

Then the Plebanski tensor is defined as

$$P^{ab}{}_{cd}=S^{[a}{}_{[c}S^{b]}{}_{d]}+\delta^{[a}{}_{[c}S^{b]e}S_{d]e}-\frac{1}{6}\delta^{[a}{}_{[c}\delta^{b]}{}_{d]}S^{ef}S_{ef}.$$

The advantage of the Plebanski tensor is that it shares the same symmetries as the [Weyl tensor](wikipedia://en/1026848). It therefore becomes possible to classify different [spacetimes](wikipedia://en/28758) based on additional algebraic symmetries of the Plebanski tensor in a manner analogous to the [Petrov classification](wikipedia://en/2045417).[^o3]
