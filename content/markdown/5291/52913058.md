# Arab Institute for Training, Research and Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arab Institute for Training, Research and Statistics"}

The **Arab Institute for Statistics** (المعهد العربي للتدريب والبحوث والإحصاء), formally the **Arab Institute for Training and Research in Statistics** (**AITRS**) (المعهد العربي للتدريب والبحوث والإحصاء) is an intergovernmental statistical institute serving the National Statistical Offices in all [Arab League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_League) countries. It was established in 1971 by the [United Nations Development Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Development_Programme) (UNDP) under the name "Regional Institute for training and Research in Statistics for the Near East".

:::

## Board of trustees {#Board_of_trustees}

The Board of Trustees is composed of the heads of the national statistical offices in all [Arab League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_League) countries. They meet once per year.
