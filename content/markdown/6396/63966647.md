# Matt Keeling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matt Keeling"}

**Matthew James Keeling** OBE (born June 1970) is a professor in the Mathematics Institute and the School of Life Sciences of the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick). He has been the editor of the journal *[Epidemics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemics_%28journal%29)* since 2007.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Keeling was appointed [Officer of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (OBE) in the [2021 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Birthday_Honours) for services to SAGE during the [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) response.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize) in Mathematics (2005)
- Royal Zoological Society of London, Scientific Medal (2007)
