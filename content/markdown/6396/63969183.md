# Expositiones Mathematicae

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Expositiones Mathematicae"}

***Expositiones Mathematicae*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal). The journal was established in 1983. It has been published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) since 2001. It is published 6 times a year and is edited by Liming Ge.[^o3]

:::
