# Eugène Rouché

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eugène Rouché"}

**Eugène Rouché** (18 August 1832 – 19 August 1910) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He was an alumnus of the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), which he entered in 1852. He went on to become professor of mathematics at the Charlemagne lyceum then at the [École Centrale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale), and admissions examiner at his alma mater. He is best known for [Rouché's theorem](wikipedia://en/1141150) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), which he published in his alma mater's institutional journal in 1862, and for the [Rouché–Capelli theorem](wikipedia://en/28648000) in [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422).

His son, [Jacques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Rouché), was a noted patron of the arts who managed the [Paris Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Opera) for thirty years (1914–1944).
