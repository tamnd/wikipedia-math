# Erhan Çınlar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erhan Çınlar"}

**Erhan Çınlar** (born May 28, 1941,[^o4] [Divriği](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divriği), [Sivas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivas)-[Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey)[^o3]) is a [probabilist](wikipedia://en/23542) and [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He was the Norman J. Sollenberger Professor of the department of [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) and [Financial Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Engineering) (ORFE) at Princeton University.[^o6]

:::

## Academic life {#Academic_life}

He received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and a [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.S.) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [Industrial Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Engineering) and Operations Research, all from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1963, 1964 and 1965, respectively.[^o8] His dissertation, *Analysis of Systems of Queues in Parallel*, was supervised by Ralph L. Disney.

In 1965, he joined the department of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences as an assistant professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). During 1971–1972, he was a visiting professor in the department of Management Science and Engineering at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and became full professor of Operations Research in 1972 at [Northwestern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). He then joined the department of Civil Engineering and [Operations Research](wikipedia://en/43476) as a professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1985.[^o9][^o10]

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

Çınlar is the co-founder (along with [Kai-lai Chung](wikipedia://en/23576590) and Ronald Getoor) of the seminar on [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895),[^o12] an annual conference on probability topics such as [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), superprocesses, [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis), and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).[^o13]
