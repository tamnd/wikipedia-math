::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Excavated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **excavated dodecahedron** is a [star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/7087334) that looks like a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) with concave [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) in place of its faces. Its exterior surface represents the **[Ef<sub>1</sub>g<sub>1</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fifty_Nine_Icosahedra)** stellation of the icosahedron. It appears in [Magnus Wenninger](wikipedia://en/8864685)'s book *Polyhedron Models* as model 28, the *third stellation of icosahedron*.

:::

## Description {#Description}

All 20 vertices and 30 of its 60 edges belong to its [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/7149361) hull. The 30 other internal edges are longer and belong to a [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878). (Each contains one of the 30 edges of the [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/14968) core.) Each face is a [self-intersecting](wikipedia://en/50055439) [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) with alternating long and short edges and 60° angles. The [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) touching a short edge are part of the face. (The smaller one between the long edges is a face of the icosahedral core.)

## Faceting of the dodecahedron {#Faceting_of_the_dodecahedron}

It has the same external form as a certain [facetting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facetting) of the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) having 20 self-intersecting [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) as faces. The non-convex hexagon face can be broken up into four equilateral triangles, three of which are the same size. A true excavated dodecahedron has the three congruent equilateral triangles as true faces of the polyhedron, while the interior equilateral triangle is not present.

The 20 vertices of the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) match the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

The faceting is a [noble polyhedron](wikipedia://en/9352244). With six six-sided faces around each vertex, it is topologically equivalent to a quotient space of the [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/241291) [order-6 hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38387527), {6,6} and is an abstract type {6,6}<sub>6</sub>. It is one of ten [abstract regular polyhedra](wikipedia://en/333299#Abstract_regular_polyhedra) of index two with vertices on one orbit.[^o12][^o13]
