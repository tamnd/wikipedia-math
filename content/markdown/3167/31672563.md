# Stephen Hilbert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Hilbert"}

**Stephen R. Hilbert** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known as co-author of the [Bramble–Hilbert lemma](wikipedia://en/13925681), which he published with [James H. Bramble](wikipedia://en/26993300) in 1970.[^o3] Hilbert's area of specialty is [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o4][^o5] He has been a professor of mathematics at [Ithaca College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_College) since 1968.[^o6][^o7] Additionally, he taught mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) as a [visiting program professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) during the 2003–2004 academic year.[^o8]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hilbert was born in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29).[^o5] As a teenager, he attended [Regis High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regis_High_School_%28New_York_City%29) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City).[^o5] He received his BS in mathematics from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in 1964 and his PhD in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) in 1969.[^o5][^o10] He completed his dissertation, *Numerical Solutions of Elliptic Partial Differential Equations*, with Bramble as his advisor.[^o4][^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Distinguished College Teaching of Mathematics Award – 1994 – Seaway Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o5][^o12]
- Dana Teaching Fellow – 1985[^o5]
- Dana Teaching Fellow – 1982[^o5]
- Outstanding Faculty Award – 1979 – School of Humanities and Sciences, Ithaca College[^o5]
