# James Demmel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="James Demmel"}

**James Weldon Demmel Jr.** (born October 19, 1955) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), the *Dr. Richard Carl Dehmel Distinguished Professor of Mathematics and Computer Science* at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o10]

:::

In 1999, Demmel was elected a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) for contributions to numerical linear algebra and scientific computing.

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh),[^o4] Demmel did his [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) studies at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), graduating in 1975 with a B.S. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o13][^o14] He earned his Ph.D. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) in 1983 from UC Berkeley, under the supervision of [William Kahan](wikipedia://en/303413); his dissertation was entitled *A Numerical Analyst's Jordan Canonical Form*.[^o5] After holding a faculty position at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) for six years, he moved to Berkeley in 1990.[^o13][^o14]

## Academic works {#Academic_works}

Demmel is known for his work on [LAPACK](wikipedia://en/1013768), a [software library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_library) for [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660)[^o13][^o9] and more generally for research in numerical algorithms combining mathematical rigor with high performance implementation.[^o10] Prometheus, a [parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) [multigrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multigrid) [finite element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element) solver written by Demmel, Mark Adams, and Robert Taylor, won the Carl Benz Award at [Supercomputing 1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM/IEEE_Supercomputing_Conference) and the [Gordon Bell Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Bell_Prize) for Adams and his coworkers at Supercomputing 2004.[^o7]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Demmel was elected as a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 1999,[^o9] a fellow of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) in 1999, a fellow of the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) in 2001, a fellow of [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2009, and a member of the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2011.[^o10][^o11] Demmel was one of two scientists honored in 1986 with the [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214).[^o9][^o12] In 1993, Demmel won the [J.H. Wilkinson](wikipedia://en/301380) Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing,[^o10][^o9] and in 2010, he was the winner of the IEEE's [Sidney Fernbach Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Fernbach_Award) "for computational science leadership in creating adaptive, innovative, high-performance linear algebra software".[^o13][^o14] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15] He received the [IEEE Computer Society Charles Babbage Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Parallel_and_Distributed_Processing_Symposium#IEEE_Computer_Society_Charles_Babbage_Award) in 2013.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Demmel is married to [Katherine Yelick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Yelick), who is also an [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow) and professor of electrical engineering and computer science at UC Berkeley,[^o17] and Associate Lab Director for Computing Sciences at [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_National_Laboratory).[^o18]
