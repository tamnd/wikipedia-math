# Christof Geiß

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christof Geiß"}

**Christof Geiß** (born 1964), also called **Geiss Hahn** or **Geiß Hahn**, is a German mathematician.

:::

Geiß studied mathematics at the [University of Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bayreuth), where he received in 1990 his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) with Diplomarbeit *Darstellungsendliche Algebren und multiplikative Basen* and in 1993 his doctorate. His doctoral thesis *Tame distributive algebras and related topics* was written under the supervision of Wolfgang Erich Müller and [José Antonio de la Peña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/José_Antonio_de_la_Peña). Geiß does research and teaches at the [Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Nacional_Autónoma_de_México) (UNAM), where he studied already in 1991/92 and became in 1993 an [Investigador Associado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_rank#Mexico). He is there an [Investigador Titular C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_rank#Mexico).[^o4]

His research deals with [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423) in [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and their categorization, pre-projective algebras, and [quivers](wikipedia://en/355100) in combination with [symmetrizable](wikipedia://en/126474#Symmetrizable_matrix) [Cartan matrices](wikipedia://en/1146433).

In 2018 Geiß was an Invited Speaker with talk *Quivers with relations for symmetrizable Cartan matrices and algebraic Lie Theory* at the [International Congress of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematics).[^o5]
