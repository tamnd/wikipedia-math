# Sébastien Boucksom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sébastien Boucksom"}

**Sébastien Boucksom** (born 26 August 1976 in [Roubaix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roubaix)) is a French mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Boucksom studied at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) from 1996 to 1999, when he qualified with his [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics. He received his doctorate in 2002 from the Institut Fourier of the [Université Grenoble Alpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes) with thesis *Cônes positifs des variétés complexes compactes* under the supervision of [Jean-Pierre Demailly](wikipedia://en/53350424). As a postdoc Boucksom studied with [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305) at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London). From 2003 he did research for the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu of the CNRS and the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) VI. Since 2010 he has been a part-time professor at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and since 2014 a [directeur de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_rank#France) of the CNRS at the Center de Mathématiques [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) of the École Polytechnique.[^o4]

Boucksom's research deals with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), geometry of [*p*-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808), and [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538). Ideas introduced by Boucksom and collaborators [Berman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_J._Berman) and Jonsson using non-Archimedean geometry to study Kähler manifolds have been very influential in the study of [K-stability of Fano varieties](wikipedia://en/70175623).[^o5]

In 2014, the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) awarded him the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602). The laudation cited his work on positive fluxes in compact [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538) with application to characterization of pseudo-effective cones,[^o6] as well as his work on the [Monge-Ampère equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monge-Ampère_equation) with application to the existence of [Kähler-Einstein metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kähler-Einstein_metric) with minimal singularities.[^o7] With [Berman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_J._Berman) and Nyström, he proved a version of the [Fekete problem](wikipedia://en/27621133) in pluripotential theory.[^o8] (An algorithmic version of the Fekete problem is problem number 7 of [Smale's problems](wikipedia://en/8294746).)

In 2018, Boucksom was an Invited Speaker with talk *Variational and non-Archimedean aspects of the Yau-Tian-Donaldson conjecture* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o9]
