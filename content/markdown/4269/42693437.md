# Kevin Costello

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kevin Costello"}

**Kevin Joseph Costello** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) is an Irish mathematician, since 2014 the [Krembil Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krembil_Research_Institute)'s [William Rowan Hamilton](wikipedia://en/39817) chair of theoretical physics at the [Perimeter Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute) in [Waterloo, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterloo,_Ontario), Canada.[^o16][^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Costello was educated at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) where he was awarded a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 2003[^o5] for research on [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291) supervised by [Ian Grojnowski](wikipedia://en/1359175).

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Costello works in the field of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), particularly in the mathematical foundations of [perturbative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perturbative) [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and the applications of [topological](wikipedia://en/443757) and [conformal](wikipedia://en/364774) field theories to other areas of mathematics. In the book *Renormalization and Effective Field Theory*[^o8] he introduced a rigorous mathematical formalism for the [renormalization group](wikipedia://en/291462) flow formalism of [Kenneth Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_G._Wilson) and proved the renormalizability of [Yang–Mills theory](wikipedia://en/672202) in this framework.

More recent work on formalism for quantum field theory uses the idea of a [factorization algebra](wikipedia://en/31824847) to describe the local structure of [quantum observables](wikipedia://en/294248), such as the [operator product expansion](wikipedia://en/1877895) for conformal field theories. Using this language, Costello gave a rigorous construction of the [Witten genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witten_genus) in [elliptic cohomology](wikipedia://en/10295395), using a variant of [Chern–Simons theory](wikipedia://en/391908).[^o9] Along with [Davide Gaiotto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davide_Gaiotto), Kevin Costello was one of two researchers appointed to named chairs by the Perimeter Institute in 2014, funded by a \$4 million investment by the Krembil Foundation. Costello's appointment was praised by [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) recipients [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509) and [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894).[^o3][^o10]

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Costello was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (FRS) in 2018.[^o13] He was awarded the [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize)[^o14][^o15] by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2017.[^o16] In 2020 he was admitted as an honorary member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy).[^o17]
