# Albert Pfluger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert Pfluger"}

*Image: Albert Pfluger.jpg.* Albert Pfluger 1988

**Albert Pfluger** (13 October 1907, [Oensingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oensingen) – 14 September 1993, Zürich) was a Swiss mathematician, specializing in [complex function theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_function_theory).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pfluger, the son of a farmer, attended secondary school in [Stans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stans). He then studied mathematics at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), where in 1935 he received his promotion (Ph.D) under [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya) with a thesis *Über eine Interpretation gewisser Konvergenz- und Fortsetzungseigenschaften Dirichlet'scher Reihen*.[^o5] Afterwards, he became a *Gymnasium* teacher at the canton school in [Zug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zug_%28canton%29) and the canton school in [Solothurn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solothurn). He received his habilitation qualification in 1938 and in 1939 became a professor extraordinarius for applied mathematics and mathematical physics at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg). At ETH Zürich he became a professor extraordinarius in 1940 and in 1943 a professor ordinarius in the professorial chair vacated by George Pólya in 1940. Pfluger retired from ETH Zürich in 1978 as professor emeritus.

He did research on the [value distribution theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_distribution_theory) of [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), [conformal maps](wikipedia://en/50627) and [quasiconformal mapping](wikipedia://en/2749226), as well as [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181). In 1957 Pfluger's monograph *Theorie der Riemannschen Flächen* was published in [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media)'s series *Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften*.[^o7] With Joseph Hersch, he introduced in 1952 a function (now called the Hersch-Pfluger distortion function) useful in estimating the distortion of quasiconformal mappings.[^o8] Entire functions with certain regularity properties were introduced and extensively studied by Pfluger and [Boris Levin](wikipedia://en/49586896); these functions are now referred to as functions of completely regular growth in the sense of Levin and Pfluger.

Pfluger served a two-year term as president of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society) in 1950–1951. In 1973 he was elected a foreign member of the [Finnish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Sciences).

His doctoral students include [Peter Henrici](wikipedia://en/31964476) and [Heinz Rutishauser](wikipedia://en/29245633).

Pfluger was married since 1938 to Maria Jeger.
