# Chelsea Walton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chelsea Walton"}

**Chelsea Walton** is an American mathematician whose research interests include [noncommutative algebra](wikipedia://en/24062516), [noncommutative algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/25493347), [symmetry in quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/39580830), [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714), and [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678). She is a full professor at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) and a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Walton is African-American, originally from [Detroit, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit,_Michigan), and was educated in the [Detroit public schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Public_Schools_Community_District). As a child she made a [letter frequency](wikipedia://en/6767165) table from her children's dictionary, and as a high school student, seeking a way to "do logic puzzles all day and get paid for this", she was already planning a career as a mathematics professor.

She graduated from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) in 2005, and completed her PhD at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 2011. Her dissertation, *On Degenerations and Deformations of Sklyanin Algebras*,[^o4] was jointly supervised by [Toby Stafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Stafford) and [Karen E. Smith](wikipedia://en/41972321), and based in part on her work as a visiting student at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), where Stafford had moved.

Walton did postdoctoral research at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and became a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 2012 to 2015. She came to [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) as Selma Lee Bloch Brown Assistant Professor of Mathematics in 2015 . She moved to the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) in 2018. She joined the faculty at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Walton was named a Sloan Fellow in 2017, becoming the fourth African-American to win a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in mathematics. Walton was also recognized by [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) as a Black History Month 2017 Honoree. In 2018 she won the [André Lichnerowicz Prize in Poisson geometry](wikipedia://en/67093003), the first woman to be awarded this prize. The award citation noted her research on [Sklyanin algebras](wikipedia://en/43177114) in [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry), on the actions of [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714), and on the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of the [Witt algebra](wikipedia://en/1510857). She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.
