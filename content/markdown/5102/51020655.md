# Angelina Cabras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Angelina Cabras"}

**Angelina Cabras** (23 December 1898 – 19 June 1993) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist). She earned degrees in mathematics from the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) in 1924 and in physics from the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari) in 1927. She obtained a position in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) at Cagliari, later moving to the institute of [theoretical mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_mechanics) there. Her research concerned higher dimensional [rigid body dynamics](wikipedia://en/481856), the [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001), and [inductance](wikipedia://en/165146).[^o3][^o4]

:::

She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 1928.[^o5][^o6]

She died in [Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagliari) in 1993.[^o7]
