# Dipendra Prasad

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dipendra Prasad"}

**Dipendra Prasad** (born 1960)[^o3] is an Indian mathematician. He is an Emeritus Fellow of mathematics at the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Bombay). He is a [number theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theorist) known for his work in the areas of [automorphic representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_representations) and the [Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture](wikipedia://en/58884863).[^o4][^o5][^o6] He was the president of Commission for Developing Countries (CDC) of [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (2018–2022)[^o7] and of Indian Math Society (2021–2022).[^o8] Presently, he is the Chairman of the National Board of Higher Mathematics (NBHM) in India.

:::

Two of his siblings, [Gopal Prasad](wikipedia://en/26770296) and [Shrawan Kumar](wikipedia://en/26780647), are also mathematicians.

## Education {#Education}

Prasad obtained his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) from the [St. Xavier's College, Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Xavier's_College,_Mumbai) in 1978 before moving to the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Kanpur) for a [Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) which he completed in 1980.[^o10] From 1980–1985, Prasad worked as a research scholar at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai (TIFR Mumbai).[^o13] He then completed his PhD under the supervision of [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863) at Harvard, in 1989.[^o13]

## Career {#Career}

From 1989–1997, Prasad was a research assistant, fellow, and then reader at TIFR Mumbai.[^o13] From 1992–1993, Prasad was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o13] From 1994–2004, Prasad was an associate professor and then full professor at the [Harish-Chandra Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harish-Chandra_Research_Institute) in [Allahabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allahabad).[^o13] From 2004 until 2019, Prasad was a professor at TIFR Mumbai.[^o13] In 2019, Prasad moved to the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.[^o14]

## Awards {#Awards}

In recognition of his contributions, the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) awarded him the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) for 2002. In 2010 he was awarded the JC Bose of the [Department of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Science_and_Technology_%28India%29), New Delhi.[^o16] He is also a recipient of the Swarna Jayanti Fellowship in 1999.[^o3] He was Invited Speaker on the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 2018 in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) in Section 7 (Lie Groups and Generalizations) with the talk 'Ext-analogues of branching laws'.
