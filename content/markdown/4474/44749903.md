# Elizabeth S. Allman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elizabeth S. Allman"}

**Elizabeth Spencer Allman** (born 1965)[^o3] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She is a professor of mathematics in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alaska_Fairbanks); her research interests range from [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [algebraic statistics](wikipedia://en/17314682) to [biomathematics](wikipedia://en/662088) and [phylogeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylogeny).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Allman earned her Ph.D. in 1995 from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) under the supervision of Murray M. Schacher. Her dissertation, in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), was *Polynomials Without Roots in Division Algebras*.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

With her Fairbanks colleague John A. Rhodes, Allman is the author of a book on [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology) and [mathematical modeling](wikipedia://en/20590), *Mathematical Models in Biology: An Introduction* (Cambridge University Press, 2004).[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012, Allman became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
