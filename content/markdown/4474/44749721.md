# William Abikoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Abikoff"}

**William Abikoff** (born 1944) is an American mathematician. He has been a professor of mathematics at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) since 1981.[^o2]

:::

Abikoff earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1971 from the [New York University Polytechnic School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering) under the supervision of Georges Gustave Weill. In 2012, Abikoff became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

In 1975, Abikoff introduced the idea of an "augmented Teichmüller space", which is obtained as a [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661) along with [regular b-groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_b-group) on its boundary. He showed that every element of the [Teichmüller modular group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_modular_group) can be extended to an automorphism of the corresponding augmented Teichmüller space.[^o6]
