# Igor Lomov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igor Lomov"}

Ломов, Игорь Сергеевич

**Igor Lomov** (Игорь Серге́евич Ло́мов) (born 1956) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3]

:::

He defended the thesis «Mathematical modeling and computer analysis of liquid metal systems» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (2009).[^o4]

Author of 13 books and more than 90 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]
