# Maria Korovina

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Korovina"}

Коровина, Мария Викторовна

**Maria Korovina** (Мари́я Ви́кторовна Коро́вина; born 1962) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).

:::

She defended the thesis «Elliptic problems in spaces with asymptotics and their applications to the construction of self-adjoint extensions of the Laplace operator» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1992).

The author of the monograph «The theory of functional spaces and differential equations» (2007) and more than 70 scientific articles.[^o3][^o4]
