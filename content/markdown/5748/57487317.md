# Mikhail Potapov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Potapov"}

Потапов, Михаил Михайлович (математик)

**Mikhail Potapov** (Михаи́л Миха́йлович Пота́пов) (born 1953) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3]

:::

He defended the thesis «Stable method for solving linear equations with noncompact operators and its applications to control and observation problems» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (2009).[^o4]

Author of 15 books and more than 50 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]
