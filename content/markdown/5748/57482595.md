# Nikolai Kapustin (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Kapustin"}

Капустин, Николай Юрьевич

**Nikolai Yurievich Kapustin** (Никола́й Ю́рьевич Капу́стин; born 3 October 1957) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3]

:::

He defended the thesis "Problems for parabolic-hyperbolic equations and corresponding spectral questions with a parameter at boundary points" for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) in 2012 and is the author of three books and more than 90 scientific articles.[^o4][^o5]
