# Alexander Ilyin (mathematician)

Ильин, Александр Владимирович

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Ilyin"}

**Alexander Ilyin** (Алекса́ндр Влади́мирович Ильи́н, (born January 11, 1973) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC), [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3][^o4] Expert in [robust control](wikipedia://en/3282143) theory.

:::

He defended the thesis «Robust inversion of dynamic systems» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (2009).[^o5]

He has authored four books and more than 90 scientific articles.[^o6][^o7]

He is the son of famed mathematician [Vladimir Ilyin](wikipedia://en/57188648).
