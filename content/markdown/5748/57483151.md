# Viktor Korolev (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Viktor Korolev"}

Королёв, Виктор Юрьевич

**Viktor Korolev** (Ви́ктор Юрьевич Королёв) (born 1954) is a Russian scientist in the field of mathematical statistics, [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3][^o4]

:::

He defended the thesis «Limit distributions of random sequences with independent random indices and some of their applications» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1994).

He has authored 27 books and more than 340 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]
