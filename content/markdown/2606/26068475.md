# Dominique Foata

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dominique Foata"}

**Dominique Foata** (born October 12, 1934) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533). With [Pierre Cartier](wikipedia://en/1470549) and [Marcel-Paul Schützenberger](wikipedia://en/2817337) he pioneered the modern approach to classical combinatorics, that lead, in part, to the current blossoming of [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). His pioneering work on permutation statistics, and his combinatorial approach to special functions, are especially notable.

:::

Foata gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) (1983). Among his honors are the Scientific Prize of the Union des Assurances de Paris (September 1985). With Adalbert Kerber and Volker Strehl he founded the mathematics journal *[Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Séminaire_Lotharingien_de_Combinatoire)*. He is also one of the contributors of the pseudonymous collective [M. Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474). In 1985, Foata received the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602).

He was born in [Damascus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damascus) while it was under [French mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Mandate_for_Syria_and_the_Lebanon).
