# William McFadden Orr

*Image: W M Orr.jpg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William McFadden Orr"}

**William McFadden Orr**, [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o2] (2 May 1866 – 14 August 1934) was a British and Irish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was born in [Comber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comber), [County Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Down) and educated at [Methodist College Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodist_College_Belfast) and [Queen's College, Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Belfast) under [John Purser](wikipedia://en/54277928), before entering [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge) and graduating as [Senior Wrangler](wikipedia://en/19355609) in 1888. He was elected a fellow of his college, and became a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1909.[^o2]

He was appointed professor of mathematics at the [Royal College of Science for Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Science_for_Ireland) in 1892 and professor of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) when the college merged with [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin) in 1926. He retired in 1933 and died in 1934.[^o2]

[Orr–Sommerfeld equation](wikipedia://en/10106425) in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) is named after his work in 1907.[^o4]
