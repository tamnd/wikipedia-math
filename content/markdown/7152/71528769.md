# List of countries by mean age at childbearing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of countries by mean age at childbearing"}

The following list sorts countries and dependent territories by mean age at [childbearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Childbearing). The mean age at childbearing indicates the age of a woman at their childbearing events, if women were subject throughout their lives to the age-specific [fertility rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertility_rates) observed in that given year.[^o2]

:::

In countries with very high fertility rates women can have their first child at a much younger age than the mean age at childbearing.

Note that the "[mean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean)" used is specifically the "[median age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_age)".[^o4]
