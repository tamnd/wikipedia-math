# Adegoke Olubummo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adegoke Olubummo"}

**Adegoke Olubummo** was a Nigerian academic and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for being the first Nigerian professor in mathematics.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Adegoke attended Ifaki Methodist school and finished in 1937. In 1938, he continued to [Wesley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_College,_Ibadan) in Ibadan. He got his a B.A. from [Fourah Bay College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourah_Bay_College) in [Sierra Leone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Leone).[^o5]

In 1952, he earned his M.A. in Mathematics and Ph.D. in 1955 from King's College, [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University).[^o5]

He began lecturing in the mathematics department at the University College, Ibadan in 1955 and became a full professor in 1964. He was the head of the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan) for many years. He became the Dean of the Faculty of Science of [Ondo State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ondo_State_University) after retiring from the University of Ibadan in 1985.[^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Adegoke has three children who are mathematicians. He is the father to the mathematician [Yewande Olubummo](wikipedia://en/61599361).[^o8]
