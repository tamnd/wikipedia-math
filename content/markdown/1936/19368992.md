# Victor Gustave Robin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Gustave Robin"}

**Victor Gustave Robin** (; 17 May 1855 – 1897) was a French [mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical) analyst and [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) who lectured in mathematical physics at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and also worked in the area of [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952).[^o2][^o3][^o4] He is known especially for the [Robin boundary condition](wikipedia://en/9945920). The [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) awarded him the *Prix Francœur* for 1893 and again for 1897 and the [*Prix Poncelet*](wikipedia://en/20842828) for 1895.[^o5][^o6]

:::
