# Quantum invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum invariant"}

In the mathematical field of [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), a **quantum knot invariant** or **quantum invariant** of a [knot](wikipedia://en/366808) or link is a linear sum of [colored Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642#Colored_Jones_polynomial) of [surgery](wikipedia://en/5961115) presentations of the [knot complement](wikipedia://en/1350120).[^o9][^o2][^o3]

:::
