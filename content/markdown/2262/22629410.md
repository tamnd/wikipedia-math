# Michael Makkai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Makkai"}

**Michael Makkai** (Makkai Mihály; 24 June 1939 in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary)) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) of [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) origin, specializing in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636).[^o2] He works in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [algebraic logic](wikipedia://en/7870034), [type theory](wikipedia://en/40282) and the theory of [topoi](wikipedia://en/21391464).

:::

## Career {#Career}

### Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

Makkai was awarded his PhD from the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), Budapest, in 1966, having been supervised by [Rózsa Péter](wikipedia://en/1297277) and [Andrzej Mostowski](wikipedia://en/1029314). He then worked at the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] Between 1974 and 2010, he was professor of mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University), retiring in 2010. He is also an external member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) (1995).

### Work {#Work}

With [Leo Harrington](wikipedia://en/5693354) and [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) he proved the [Vaught conjecture](wikipedia://en/13797188) for ω-stable theories.[^o8]

With Robert Paré he further developed the theory of [Accessible Categories](wikipedia://en/15311782).[^o9]

Makkai has an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) of 1, having published "Some Remarks on Set Theory, X" with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) in 1966.[^o10]
