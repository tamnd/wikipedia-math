# Paul C. Rosenbloom

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul C. Rosenbloom"}

**Paul Charles Rosenbloom** (1920 in [Portsmouth, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth,_Virginia) – 2005)[^o3] was an American mathematician.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rosenbloom studied at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), where as an undergraduate he became a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) in 1941.[^o5] In 1944 he earned his PhD from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) under [Gábor Szegő](wikipedia://en/46065) with thesis *On sequences of polynomials, especially sections of [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)*.[^o6] He was a professor of mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) (around 1951), the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) (middle to end of the 1950s), and [Teachers College, Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teachers_College,_Columbia_University) (from the 1960s to his retirement as professor emeritus). His doctoral students include [Henry Gordon Rice](wikipedia://en/15461434).

Rosenbloom's research includes analysis, special functions, differential equations, logic, and the teaching of mathematics. In the academic year 1959–1960 he was the director of the Minnesota School Mathematics Center.[^o7]

In 1946 he was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow).[^o8] He was at the Institute for Advanced Study for the academic years 1953–1954 and 1971–1972.[^o9]
