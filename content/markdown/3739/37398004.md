# Peter Littelmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Littelmann"}

*Image: Littelmann, Peter.jpg.* Littelmann at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2009

**Peter Littelmann** (born 10 December 1957) is a German mathematician at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) working on algebraic groups and representation theory, who introduced the [Littelmann path model](wikipedia://en/11167326) and used it to solve several conjectures in [standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718) and other areas.

:::

Littelmann was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zürich (1994).
