# Walter Borho

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Borho"}

**Walter Borho** (born 1945) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who works on [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is retired as a university professor at the [University of Wuppertal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wuppertal).[^o2]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Borho was born in 1945 in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg).[^o4] He received his PhD in 1973 from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) under the direction of [Ernst Witt](wikipedia://en/2619911) with a thesis titled *Wesentliche ganze Erweiterungen kommutativer Ringe*.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1986 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o7]
