# Chitikila Musili

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chitikila Musili"}

**Chitikila Musili** was an Indian mathematician at the [University of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hyderabad) who developed [standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718) in collaboration with his PhD supervisor [C. S. Seshadri](wikipedia://en/5633765).

:::
