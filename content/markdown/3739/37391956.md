# Zdeněk Frolík

*Image: Zdenek Frolik.png.* Zdeněk Frolík in 1971

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdeněk Frolík"}

**Zdeněk Frolík** (10 March 1933 – 3 May 1989) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechs) mathematician. His research interests included [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). In particular, his work concerned covering properties of topological spaces, ultrafilters, homogeneity, measures, uniform spaces. He was one of the founders of modern descriptive theory of sets and spaces.[^o2]

:::

Two classes of topological spaces are given Frolík's name: the class P of all spaces $X$ such that $X \times Y$ is [pseudocompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudocompact) for every pseudocompact space $Y$,[^o3] and the class C of all spaces $X$ such that $X \times Y$ is [countably compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countably_compact) for every countably compact space $Y$.[^o4]

Frolík prepared his Ph.D. thesis under the supervision of [Miroslav Katetov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miroslav_Katetov) and [Eduard Čech](wikipedia://en/859830).[^o5]
