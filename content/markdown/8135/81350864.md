# Ruochuan Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruochuan Liu"}

**Ruochuan Liu** () is a Chinese mathematician and a Boya Distinguished Professor at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), specialising in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).[^o3] He is best known for his fundamental contributions to [p-adic Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/26086853), for which he was awarded the 2024 [ICTP Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525).[^o4]

:::

## Education and early life {#Education_and_early_life}

Liu first gained international recognition by winning a gold medal for China at the 1999 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o6] He attended [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), where he completed his bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics. Liu then pursued his doctoral studies at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT), earning his PhD in 2008 under the supervision of [Kiran Sridhara Kedlaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiran_Sridhara_Kedlaya).

## Career {#Career}

After completing his PhD, Liu held a postdoctoral position at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He later returned to China, joining the faculty at the School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University, where he now serves as Vice Dean and a Boya Distinguished Professor.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

Liu's research focuses on the intersection of number theory and arithmetic geometry, with a particular emphasis on p-adic methods. The citation for his Ramanujan Prize noted his "fundamental contributions to p-adic Hodge theory, especially his foundational study of relative p-adic Hodge theory and his remarkable work on rigidity and the Riemann-Hilbert correspondence for p-adic local systems."[^o4] His work applies tools with geometric origins to solve problems in number theory and explores connections between p-adic geometry and algebraic topology.[^o4]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Liu's research has been widely recognized with several awards.

- In 1999, he won a gold medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o6]
- In 2017, he received the National Science Fund Award for Distinguished Young Scholars.[^o3]
- In 2019, he was a recipient of the Tencent Xplorer Prize.[^o11]
- In 2020, he received the China Youth Science and Technology Award.[^o12]
- In 2024, he was awarded the [ICTP Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525) by the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics).[^o4]
