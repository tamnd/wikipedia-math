# Milnor conjecture (knot theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milnor conjecture"}

Milnor conjecture (disambiguation) In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **Milnor conjecture** says that the [slice genus](wikipedia://en/5418032) of the $(p, q)$ [torus knot](wikipedia://en/1165182) is

:::

$$\frac{(p-1)(q-1)}{2}.$$

It is in a similar vein to the [Thom conjecture](wikipedia://en/3846986).

The conjecture (named after [John Milnor](wikipedia://en/61632)) was first proved by [gauge theoretic](wikipedia://en/23912155) methods by [Peter Kronheimer](wikipedia://en/3845647) and [Tomasz Mrowka](wikipedia://en/20652006).[^o2] Jacob Rasmussen later gave a purely [combinatorial proof](wikipedia://en/741875) using [Khovanov homology](wikipedia://en/2639906), by means of the [s-invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-invariant).[^o3]
