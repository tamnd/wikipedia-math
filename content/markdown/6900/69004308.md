# Feuerbach hyperbola

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Feuerbach hyperbola"}

*Image: Feuerbach Hyperbola.svg.* Feuerbach Hyperbola

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Feuerbach hyperbola** is a [rectangular hyperbola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangular_hyperbola) passing through important [triangle centers](wikipedia://en/12401488) such as the [incenter](wikipedia://en/316837), [orthocenter](wikipedia://en/161244), [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_Point), [Nagel point](wikipedia://en/9489914), [mittenpunkt](wikipedia://en/14941140) and [Schiffler point](wikipedia://en/2012094). The center of the hyperbola is the [Feuerbach point](wikipedia://en/547762), the point of tangency of the [incircle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incircle_and_excircles_of_a_triangle) and the [nine-point circle](wikipedia://en/161243).

:::

## Equation {#Equation}

It has the [trilinear equation](wikipedia://en/3837790) $\frac{\cos B - \cos C}{\alpha}+ \frac{\cos C - \cos A}{\beta}+ \frac{\cos A - \cos B}{\gamma} = 0$ (here $A,B,C$ are the angles at the respective vertices and $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$ are the trilinear coordinates).[^o3]

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Like other rectangular hyperbolas, the orthocenter of any three points on the curve lies on the hyperbola. So, the orthocenter of the triangle $ABC$ lies on the curve.

The line $OI$ is tangent to this hyperbola at $I$.

### Isogonal conjugate of OI {#Isogonal_conjugate_of_OI}

The hyperbola is the isogonal conjugate of $OI$, the line joining the circumcenter and the incenter.[^o7] This fact leads to a few interesting properties. Specifically all the points lying on the line $OI$ have their isogonal conjugates lying on the hyperbola. The [Nagel point](wikipedia://en/9489914) lies on the curve since its isogonal conjugate is the point of concurrency of the lines joining the vertices and the opposite [Mixtilinear incircle](wikipedia://en/69072799) touchpoints, also the [in-similitude](wikipedia://en/15685517) of the incircle and the circumcircle. Similarly, the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_Point) lies on the curve since its isogonal conjugate is the [ex-similitude](wikipedia://en/15685517) of the incircle and the circumcircle.

The [pedal circle](wikipedia://en/152983) of any point on the hyperbola passes through the Feuerbach point, the center of the hyperbola.

:::

## Kariya's theorem {#Kariya's_theorem}

*Image: Kariya's theorem.jpg.* Kariya's theorem

Given a triangle $ABC$, let $A_1,B_1,C_1$ be the touchpoints of the incircle $\odot I$ with the sides of the triangle opposite to vertices $A,B,C$ respectively. Let $X,Y,Z$ be points lying on the lines $IA_1, IB_1,IC_1$ such that $IX=IY= IZ$. Then, the lines $AX,BY,CZ$ are concurrent at a point lying on the Feuerbach hyperbola.

The Kariya's theorem has a long history.[^o10] It was proved independently by Auguste Boutin and V. Retali.,[^o11][^o12][^o13] but it became famous only after Taninjirō Kariya's paper.[^o14] Around that time, many generalizations of this result were given. Kariya's theorem can be used for the construction of the Feuerbach hyperbola.

Both [Lemoine's theorem](wikipedia://en/320998) and Kariya's theorem are a special case of [Jacobi's theorem](wikipedia://en/52182665).

### Other rectangular hyperbolas {#Other_rectangular_hyperbolas}
