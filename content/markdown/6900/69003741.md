# Nicolas Bergeron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolas Bergeron"}

**Nicolas Bergeron** (19 December 1975 – 15 February 2024) was a French mathematician who worked in [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris.

:::

## Early career {#Early_career}

Bergeron obtained his PhD at [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) in the year 2000 under the supervision of [Jean-Pierre Otal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Pierre_Otal). His thesis was titled *Cycles géodésiques dans les variétés hyperboliques* ("geodesic cycles in hyperbolic varieties").[^o4]

## Work {#Work}

Bergeron's main interests concerned the geometry and topology of locally symmetric spaces and arithmetic groups, as well as their [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060),[^o6] and their connection to number theory, in particular to Hilbert's 12th problem.[^o7][^o8]

Some of his publications showed an interest in [Oulipo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oulipo), referencing [An Attempt at Exhausting a Place in Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Attempt_at_Exhausting_a_Place_in_Paris) by [Georges Perec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Perec).[^o9] He also wrote a (non-mathematical) article about [Jacques Roubaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Roubaud).[^o10]

Bergeron was latterly a member of the editorial board of the journal [Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publications_Mathématiques_de_l'IHÉS).[^o11]

## Death {#Death}

Bergeron died on 15 February 2024, at the age of 48.[^o13]

## Awards and prizes {#Awards_and_prizes}

- Bergeron won the *Médaille de bronze du CNRS* in 2007[^o24]
- Bergeron was a junior member of [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) from 2010 until 2015[^o25]
- Bergeron was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International congress of mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_congress_of_mathematicians) in Rio with a talk on "Hodge theory and cycle theory of locally symmetric spaces"[^o26]
- Bergeron was a Takagi lecturer in 2018.[^o27]
- Bergeron was a Aisenstadt chair lecturer in Montréal in 2020.[^o28]
- Bergeron received the Prix fondé par l'Etat from French Académie des Sciences in 2023.[^o29]
