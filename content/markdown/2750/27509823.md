# Lester S. Hill

::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Lester S. Hill"}

**Lester S. Hill** (1891–1961) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator) who was interested in applications of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) to [communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications). He received a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) (1911) and a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) (1913) from [Columbia College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_College,_Columbia_University) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (1926). He taught at the [University of Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montana), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine), [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), and [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College). Among his notable contributions was the [Hill cipher](wikipedia://en/1104945). He also developed methods for detecting errors in [telegraphed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegraph) code numbers and wrote two books.
