# Pseudo-intersection

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo-intersection"}

In [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), a **pseudo-intersection** of a family of sets is an [infinite set](wikipedia://en/142679) *S* such that each element of the family contains all but a finite number of elements of *S*. The **pseudo-intersection number**, sometimes denoted by the [fraktur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraktur) letter 𝔭, is the smallest size of a family of infinite subsets of the natural numbers that has the [strong finite intersection property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_finite_intersection_property) but has no pseudo-intersection.

:::
