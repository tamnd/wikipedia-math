# 22 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="22"}

**22** (**twenty-two**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [21](wikipedia://en/209248) and preceding [23](wikipedia://en/362203).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

22 is a [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542), a [Smith number](wikipedia://en/631045), and an [Erdős–Woods number](wikipedia://en/2616413). $\frac{22}{7} = 3.14{\color{red}28}\ldots$ is a commonly used [approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximations_of_π#Miscellaneous_approximations) of the [irrational number](wikipedia://en/20647689) [$\pi$](wikipedia://en/23601), the ratio of the [circumference](wikipedia://en/5916) of a circle to its [diameter](wikipedia://en/8007).

22 can read as "two twos", which is the only [fixed point](wikipedia://en/449738) of [John Conway's](wikipedia://en/15807) [look-and-say function](wikipedia://en/563980).

The number 22 appears prominently within [sporadic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_groups). The [Mathieu group](wikipedia://en/380207) [M<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/15200699) is one of 26 sporadic [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group), defined as the 3-transitive [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027) representation on 22 points.[^o8] There are also 22 [regular complex apeirohedra](wikipedia://en/8581934#Regular_complex_apeirohedra).[^o9]

## In culture and religion {#In_culture_and_religion}

- 22 is a master number in [numerology](wikipedia://en/21137).[^o11]
- There are 22 cards in the [Major Arcana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_Arcana) suit of a traditional [tarot card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarot) deck, though they are often numbered 0-21.

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

- There are 22 [genetically encoded amino acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteinogenic_amino_acid) throughout known life that generate proteins during [RNA translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_%28biology%29).[^o13]
- There are 22 of [yards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yard) in a [chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_%28unit%29).[^o14]
- *[Catch-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catch-22)* (1961), [Joseph Heller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Heller)'s novel, and its 1970 film adaptation gave rise to the expression of logic "[catch-22](wikipedia://en/1172067)".[^o15]
- [22 equal temperament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/22_equal_temperament) is a popular [microtonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microtonal) tuning temperament due to its good approximation of harmonic intervals, especially septimal intervals.
- In [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_%28language%29) jargon, "22" is used as a phrase to warn of the coming of the police (typically *"*22, v'là les flics*!"*, in English: "5-0! Cops!")
- In [Spanish lottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_lottery) and [bingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bingo_%28British_version%29), 22 is nicknamed los dos patitos after its shape.[^o16][^o17]
