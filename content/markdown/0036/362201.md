# 24 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="24"}

**24** (**twenty-four**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [23](wikipedia://en/362203) and preceding [25](wikipedia://en/362204). It is equal to two [dozen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dozen) and one sixth of a [gross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_%28unit%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

24 is the number of [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of four items. Thus it is the order of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) on four symbols, and is the [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) of 4. This is the full symmetry group of the [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521), and also the rotational symmetry group of the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) (or [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635)).[^o4][^o5]

24 is an [even](wikipedia://en/143135) [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), a [highly composite number](wikipedia://en/208732), an [abundant number](wikipedia://en/321831), a [practical number](wikipedia://en/1205310), and a [congruent number](wikipedia://en/2334769). The many ways 24 can be constructed inspired a [children's mathematical game](wikipedia://en/5445272) involving the use of any of the four standard operations on four numbers on a card to get 24.

24 is also part of the only nontrivial solution pair to the [cannonball problem](wikipedia://en/28045698), along with [70](wikipedia://en/343334).[^o6] It is also the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) in [4-dimensional space](wikipedia://en/1364622). This fact can be used in the construction of the 24-dimensional [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919). An [icositetragon](wikipedia://en/24082273) is a [regular polygon](wikipedia://en/333306) with 24 sides. A [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) has 24 two-dimensional [square](wikipedia://en/659939) [faces](wikipedia://en/149926).

The [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), consisting of 24 octahedra and having 24 vertices, is a special polytope that only exists in four dimensions. The vertices of the 24-cell are the [root vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_vector) of the [$D_4$ root system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D4_%28root_system%29):

$$\{\pm e_i \pm e_j : 1\leq i<j\leq 4\}$$

in four-dimensional Euclidean space. In quaternionic form, the same configuration may be identified with the 24 unit [Hurwitz quaternions](wikipedia://en/772241), which form the [binary tetrahedral group](wikipedia://en/10997586).[^o7][^o8][^o9]

The optimal [sphere packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_packing_problem) has been solved in dimension 24, one of the only dimensions where this has been solved (the others being dimensions 1–3, and 8).[^o10]

The number 24 appears prominently in the theory of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) through the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020)[^o11]

$$\eta(\tau)=q^{1/24}\prod_{n>0}(1-q^n),\qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau}.$$

The eta function has weight $1/2$ and transforms with a [multiplier system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplier_system) of order 24. Its 24th power is the [modular discriminant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_discriminant)

$$\Delta(\tau)=\eta(\tau)^{24}=q\prod_{n>0}(1-q^n)^{24},$$

a [cusp form](wikipedia://en/610165) of weight 12 for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$.

The number 24 also occurs in [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869) and finite group theory through the [extended binary Golay code](wikipedia://en/344971), a self-dual binary linear code with parameters $[24,12,8]$. The supports of its codewords of weight 8, called *octads*, form the [Steiner system](wikipedia://en/28016) $S(5,8,24)$, also known as the [Witt design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witt_design). The automorphism group of the extended binary Golay code, equivalently of this Steiner system, is the [Mathieu group $M_{24}$](wikipedia://en/15200706), one of the [sporadic simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_groups). The extended Golay code and the Witt design are also used in standard constructions of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919).[^o12][^o13]

## In religion {#In_religion}

- In Christian [apocalyptic literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalyptic_literature) it represents the complete Church, being the sum of the 12 [tribes of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribes_of_Israel) and the 12 [Apostles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostles_in_the_New_Testament) of the Lamb of God. For example, in *[The Book of Revelation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Revelation)*: "Surrounding the throne were twenty-four other thrones, and seated on them were twenty-four elders. They were dressed in white and had crowns of gold on their heads."[^o15]
- Number of [Tirthankaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirthankaras) in [Jainism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jainism).
- Number of spokes in the [Ashoka Chakra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashoka_Chakra).

## In culture {#In_culture}

- In [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), twenty-four is associated with [homosexuality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homosexuality) as it is the number that stands for the [deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer) in a game known as “[jogo do bicho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jogo_do_bicho)”.
- There are 24 hours in a [day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day).
