# 25 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="25"}

**25** (**twenty-five**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [24](wikipedia://en/362201) and preceding [26](wikipedia://en/362205).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

*Image: 25.svg.* 25 is a square.

It is a [square number](wikipedia://en/213919), being 5<sup>2</sup>. 25 is also the smallest square that is also a sum of two nonzero squares: $25 = 3^2 + 4^2$. In other words, the numbers 3, 4 and 5 form the smallest [Pythagorean triple](wikipedia://en/24172).

## In religion {#In_religion}

In [Ezekiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezekiel)'s vision of a new [temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple): The number twenty-five is of cardinal importance in Ezekiel's Temple Vision (in the Bible, [Ezekiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezekiel) chapters 40–48).[^o5]

## In sports {#In_sports}

In [baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball), the number 25 is typically reserved for the best slugger on the team. Examples include [Mark McGwire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_McGwire), [Barry Bonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Bonds), [Jim Thome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Thome), and [Mark Teixeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Teixeira).

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

**Twenty-five** years of [marriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) is marked in a silver [wedding anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_anniversary).
