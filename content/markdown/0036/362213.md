# 28 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="28"}

**28** (**twenty-eight**) is the natural number following [27](wikipedia://en/369235) and preceding [29](wikipedia://en/362212).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

*Image: Die\_Gartenlaube\_(1887)\_b\_320\_3.jpg.* The number 28 depicted as 28 balls arranged in a triangular pattern with the number of layers of 7

*Image: Square-sum4-28.png.* 28 as the sum of four nonzero squares.

28 is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), a [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670),[^o4] a [harmonic divisor number](wikipedia://en/462698),[^o5] a [centered nonagonal number](wikipedia://en/1216013),[^o6] a [hexagonal number](wikipedia://en/494013),[^o7] a [happy number](wikipedia://en/542994),[^o8] and a [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915).[^o9]

28 also appears in the [Padovan sequence](wikipedia://en/2161709).[^o10]

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), 28 is the number of [bitangents to a general plane quartic curve](wikipedia://en/5204204).

## In science {#In_science}

28 is the fourth [magic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_%28physics%29) in physics.
