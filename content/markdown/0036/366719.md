# Applied probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Applied probability"}

**Applied probability** is the application of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) to [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685) problems and other scientific and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) domains.

:::

## Scope {#Scope}

Much research involving [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) is done under the auspices of **applied probability**. However, while such research is motivated (to some degree) by applied problems, it is usually the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) aspects of the problems that are of most interest to researchers (as is typical of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) in general).

Applied probabilists are particularly concerned with the application of [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and probability more generally, to the natural, applied and social sciences, including [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) (including [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy)), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [information technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology), and [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics).

Another area of interest is in [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering): particularly in areas of [uncertainty](wikipedia://en/63778), [risk management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management), [probabilistic design](wikipedia://en/9499804), and [Quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_assurance).

## History {#History}

Having initially been defined at a symposium of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the later 1950s, the term "applied probability" was popularized by [Maurice Bartlett](wikipedia://en/865429) through the name of a [Methuen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methuen_Publishing) monograph series he edited, *Applied Probability and Statistics*. The area did not have an established outlet until 1964, when the [Journal of Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Applied_Probability) came into existence through the efforts of [Joe Gani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Gani).[^o4]
