# 26 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="26"}

Number 26

**26** (**twenty-six**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [25](wikipedia://en/362204) and preceding [27](wikipedia://en/369235).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

26 is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289). 26 is also a [telephone number](wikipedia://en/35258497), specifically, the number of ways of connecting 5 points with pairwise connections.[^o4]

### Group theory {#Group_theory}

There are 26 [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806).[^o6]

The 26-dimensional Lorentzian [unimodular lattice](wikipedia://en/1648605) [II<sub>25,1</sub>](wikipedia://en/32486086) plays a significant role in [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621) problems and the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445).

## In religion {#In_religion}

- 26 is the [gematric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria) number, being the sum of the Hebrew characters (יהוה) being the name of the god of Israel – [YHWH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetragrammaton) (*Yahweh*). 26 is also the gematric number for GOD with the corresponding substitutions in English (i.e. A=1, B=2, C=3, and so on)
- In the [Tenrikyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenrikyo) religion of Japan, 26 is frequently used, since it marks the lunar calendar date of its founder [Nakayama Miki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakayama_Miki)'s first divine revelation and also her death.
