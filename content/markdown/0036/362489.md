# Samuel Dickstein (mathematician)

*Image: Samuel Dickstein - Album pisarzy polskich (wspolczesnych). Seria 2 1901 (1111568) (cropped).jpg.* Samuel Dickstein

*Image: Grob Samuela Dicksteina-Grave of Samuel Dickstein.JPG.* Grave of Samuel Dickstein at the [Jewish cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okopowa_Street_Jewish_Cemetery) in Warsaw

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel Dickstein"}

**Samuel Dickstein** (12 May 1851 – 28 September 1939) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) of Jewish origin. He was one of the founders of the Jewish party *Zjednoczenie* ("the union"), which advocated the assimilation of Polish Jews.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) and was killed there by a German bomb at the beginning of [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II). All the members of his family were murdered during the [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust).

## Work {#Work}

Dickstein wrote many books on mathematics and founded the journal *Wiadomości Mathematyczne* (*Mathematical News*), now published by the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791). He was a bridge between the times of [Cauchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy) and [Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740) and those of the [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328). He was also thanked by [Alexander Macfarlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Macfarlane) for contributing to the *Bibliography of Quaternions* (1904) published by the [Quaternion Association](wikipedia://en/16416512).[^o4]

He was also one of the personalities who contributed to the foundation of the [Warsaw Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Public_Library) in 1907.
