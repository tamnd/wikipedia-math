# Valery Glivenko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valery Glivenko"}

**Valery Ivanovich Glivenko** (Вале́рий Ива́нович Гливе́нко, Валерій Іванович Гливенко; 2 January 1897 ([Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar)) / 21 December 1896 ([Julian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_calendar)) in [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv) – 15 February 1940 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He worked in the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358), [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711). He taught at the [Moscow Industrial Pedagogical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_Pedagogical_University#Moscow_State_Pedagogical_Institute_%281930–1990%29)[^o3] until his death at age 43.[^o4][^o5] Most of Glivenko's work was published in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language).

:::
