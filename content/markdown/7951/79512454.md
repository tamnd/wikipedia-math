# Infinityplusone

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Infinityplusone"}

**Subash Chandar K** (born 41[^o3]) is an Indian-New Zealand mathematics teacher in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland). He runs a [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) channel named **infinityplusone**, where he teaches secondary school students and prepares them for [NCEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCEA_%28New_Zealand%29) exams. In 2025 Chandar K was awarded the Local Hero of the Year award at the [New Zealander of the Year Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealander_of_the_Year_Awards).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chandar K moved from [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), India, to New Zealand aged 12. His mother was a maths professor in Chennai.[^o3] As of 2025, Chandar K lives in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland) and teaches at [Ormiston Junior College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ormiston_Junior_College) in [Flat Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_Bush).[^o5]

His YouTube channel infinityplusone has 70,700 subscribers as of July 2026, and was named after the amount he loved his wife.[^o5] On the channel he helps students prepare for [NCEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCEA_%28New_Zealand%29) exams by running [live streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_streams) the nights before the exams are run,[^o6] and makes videos which explain how to use equations and calculators.[^o7] The channel was started in 2013[^o3] because Chander K noticed a lack of maths videos designed for children.[^o8] His first live stream was in 2018.[^o3]

In 2018 Chandar K won the National Excellence in Education Awards top honour. In January 2025 Chandar K became one of the 10 finalists of the [Global Teacher Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Teacher_Prize). Chandar K said that after he discovered the award in 2017, he began working towards it.[^o7] In March 2025 Chandar K was awarded the Local Hero of the Year award at the [New Zealander of the Year Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealander_of_the_Year_Awards).[^o5]
