::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Snub 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **snub 24-cell honeycomb**, or **snub icositetrachoric honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) by [snub 24-cells](wikipedia://en/3704756), [16-cells](wikipedia://en/716422), and [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821). It was discovered by [Thorold Gosset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorold_Gosset) with his 1900 paper of semiregular polytopes. It is not semiregular by Gosset's definition of regular facets, but all of its cells ([ridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridge_%28geometry%29)) are regular, either [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) or [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084).

:::

It can be seen as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of a [truncated 24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/31983684), and can be represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) s{3,4,3,3}, s{3<sup>1,1,1,1</sup>}, and 3 other snub constructions.

It is defined by an irregular decachoron [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) (10-celled 4-polytope), faceted by four [snub 24-cells](wikipedia://en/3704756), one [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), and five [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821). The vertex figure can be seen topologically as a modified [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290), where one of the tetrahedra is subdivided at mid-edges into a central octahedron and four corner tetrahedra. Then the four side-facets of the prism, the [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) become [tridiminished icosahedra](wikipedia://en/1123559).

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are five different symmetry constructions of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by different arrangements of colored [snub 24-cell](wikipedia://en/3704756), [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), and [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) facets. In all cases, four snub 24-cells, five [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821), and one [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) meet at each vertex, but the vertex figures have different symmetry generators.
