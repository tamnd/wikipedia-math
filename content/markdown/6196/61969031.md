# Odile Favaron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Odile Favaron"}

**Odile Zink-Favaron** (born May 3, 1938) is a French mathematician known for her research in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), including work on [well-covered graphs](wikipedia://en/32696387), [factor-critical graphs](wikipedia://en/7684634), [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914), [Hamiltonian decomposition](wikipedia://en/24004195), and [dominating sets](wikipedia://en/1747972). She is retired from the [Laboratory for Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratory_for_Computer_Science_%28University_of_Paris-Sud%29) (LRI) at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud).

:::

Favaron earned a doctorate at Paris-Sud University in 1986. Her dissertation, *Stabilité, domination, irrédondance et autres paramètres de graphes* \[*[Independence](wikipedia://en/524501), [domination](wikipedia://en/1747972), irredundance, and other [parameters of graphs](wikipedia://en/1655142)*\], was supervised by Jean-Claude Bermond.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Her father was poet and professor Georges Zink. [Michel Zink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Zink) and [Anne Zink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Zink) are her siblings.
