# Buchsbaum ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Buchsbaum ring"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Buchsbaum rings** are Noetherian [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391) such that every [system of parameters](wikipedia://en/30823935) is a weak sequence. A sequence $(a_1,\cdots,a_r)$ of the maximal ideal $m$ is called a weak sequence if $m\cdot((a_1,\cdots,a_{i-1})\colon a_i)\subset(a_1,\cdots,a_{i-1})$ for all $i$.

:::

They were introduced by (txt) and are named after [David Buchsbaum](wikipedia://en/30781131).

Every [Cohen–Macaulay local ring](wikipedia://en/497407) is a Buchsbaum ring. Every Buchsbaum ring is a [generalized Cohen–Macaulay ring](wikipedia://en/60415782).
