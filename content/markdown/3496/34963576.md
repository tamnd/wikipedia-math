# Enneagram (geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Enneagram"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **enneagram** (🟙 U+1F7D9) is a nine-pointed plane figure. It is sometimes called a **nonagram**, **nonangle, enneangle.**

:::

The word 'enneagram' combines the [numeral prefix](wikipedia://en/1705121) *ennea-* with the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) suffix *\-gram*. The *gram* suffix derives from *γραμμῆ* (*grammē*) meaning a line.[^o3]

## Regular enneagram {#Regular_enneagram}

A **regular enneagram** is a 9-sided [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303). It is constructed using the same points as the regular [enneagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneagon), but the points are connected in fixed steps. Two forms of regular enneagram exist:

- One form connects every second point and is represented by the [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {9/2}.
- The other form connects every fourth point and is represented by the Schläfli symbol {9/4}.

There is also a star figure, {9/3} or 3{3}, made from the regular enneagon points but connected as a compound of three equilateral triangles.[^o5][^o6] (If the triangles are alternately interlaced, this results in a [Brunnian link](wikipedia://en/2492788).) This star figure is sometimes known as the *star of [Goliath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goliath)*, after [{6/2} or 2{3}](wikipedia://en/302441), the star of [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David).[^o7]

The nine-pointed star or enneagram can also symbolize the nine gifts or [fruits of the Holy Spirit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruits_of_the_Holy_Spirit).[^o11]

## In popular culture {#In_popular_culture}

- The [heavy metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band [Slipknot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slipknot_%28band%29) previously used the {9/3} star figure enneagram[^o13] and currently uses the {9/4} polygon as a symbol. The prior figure can be seen on the cover of their album *[All Hope Is Gone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Hope_Is_Gone)*.
- The symbol of Eldia from *[Attack on Titan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Titan)* is an irregular enneagram, representing the nine Titan powers.
