# Lucien Birgé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lucien Birgé"}

*Image: Lucien birge.jpg.* Lucien Birgé, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2009

**Lucien Birgé** (born 1950 in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France)) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lucien Birgé studied from 1970 to 1974 at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He then became an assistant at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6). In 1980 he received his doctorate from the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (Paris 7). The following year he became a professor at the [Paris Nanterre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Nanterre_University). Since 1990 he has been a professor at the University of Pierre and Marie Curie.

Birgé works in the field of mathematical statistics. His research deals with parametric and nonparametric statistics, model selection, adaptation, approximation,[^o3] "dimension and metric entropy",[^o4] and "asymptotic optimality of estimators in infinite-dimensional spaces".[^o5]

In 2005 he received the [Brouwer Medal](wikipedia://en/42578624) of the [Koninklijk Wiskundig Genootschap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koninklijk_Wiskundig_Genootschap) for the *diepte, originaliteit en elegantie van zijn werk op het gebied van de mathematische statistiek* (depth, originality and elegance of his work in the field of mathematical statistics).[^o4][^o5] He is also an Honored Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS).[^o6] In 1993 he gave one of the eight Medallion Lectures of the IMS.[^o7] In 2012 Birgé received the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384).
