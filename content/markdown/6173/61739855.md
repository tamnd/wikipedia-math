# Donna Testerman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Donna Testerman"}

**Donna Marie Testerman** (born 1960) is a mathematician specializing in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924). She is a professor of mathematics at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in Switzerland.

:::

Testerman completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) in 1985. Her dissertation, *Certain Embeddings of Simple Algebraic Groups*, was supervised by [Gary Seitz](wikipedia://en/39300192). As a faculty member at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University), she won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1992.

## Research {#Research}

In 1992, Testerman proved that if *G* is a [semisimple algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_algebraic_group) over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) *k* of characteristic *p* and *p* is a [good prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_prime_%28algebraic_groups%29) for *G*, and *u* is an element of *G* satisfying $u^p = 1$, then there exists a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) *X* of *G* which is isomorphic to the [special linear group](wikipedia://en/144823) $SL_2(k)$ or to the [projective special linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) $PSL_2(k)$.[^o3] Testerman also proved a variant theorem where one may take a surjective [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) $\sigma$ that fixes *u*, and conclude that $\sigma(X)\subseteq X$.[^o3]

In 1993, [Martin W. Liebeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_W._Liebeck), [Jan Saxl](wikipedia://en/63906865), and Testerman classified all simple, closed, connected subgroups *X* of a [simple algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_algebraic_group) *G* over an algebraically closed field *k* in the case where $rank(X)\geq\frac{1}{2}rank(G)$ in terms of a [subsystem subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsystem_subgroup) of *G* in which *X* is a subset.[^o4] In particular, there are only finitely many such [conjugacy classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugacy_classes), except in the case where *X* is [D4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D4_%28root_system%29), *G* is [E7](wikipedia://en/649115), and the characteristic is 2.[^o4] Using similar methods, the three authors were also able to use similar methods to classify subgroups *X* of a finite [group of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) that is [quasisimple](wikipedia://en/1642729) where $rank(X)\geq\frac{1}{2}rank(G)$.[^o4]

## Books {#Books}

Testerman is an author or editor of several books and book-length research monographs in mathematics, including:

- *Irreducible subgroups of exceptional algebraic groups* (1988)
- *$A_1$ subgroups of exceptional algebraic groups* (1999)
- *Centres of centralizers of unipotent elements in simple algebraic groups* (2011)
- *Group Representation Theory* (2007)
- *Linear algebraic groups and finite groups of Lie type* (2011)
