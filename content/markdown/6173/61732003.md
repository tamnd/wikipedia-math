# Ivy Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivy Liu"}

**Ivy I-Ming Liu** is a Taiwanese and New Zealander statistician specializing in [categorical](wikipedia://en/821165) and [ordinal data](wikipedia://en/52904244). She works as an associate professor and as head of the School of Mathematics and Statistics at [Victoria University of Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Wellington) in [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Liu is originally from Taiwan and has a master's degree from [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University). She completed her Ph.D. in 1995 at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida) under the supervision of [Alan Agresti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Agresti).

After returning to Taiwan to work at [National Chung Hsing University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chung_Hsing_University), she came to the [University of Waikato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waikato) in February 1999 for a one-year visiting lectureship, before moving to Victoria University.

She initially chose to work in [categorical data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_data) with the hope that she could collaborate with her husband, then studying [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology). However, he moved to different work before that hope could pan out. More specifically, her research has concerned [differential item functioning](wikipedia://en/9268401), [dimension reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_reduction) for data whose components have mixed types, and multiple response data (survey data in which respondents can provide multiple answers to a question).
