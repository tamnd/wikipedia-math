# Probabilities and Potential

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Probabilities and Potential"}

***Probabilities and Potential*** (titled Probabilités et Potentiel in the original French) is a mathematics book written by [Claude Dellacherie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Dellacherie) and [Paul-André Meyer](wikipedia://en/3653083) (and Bernard Maisonneuve, for volume 5 only). It was published by [Éditions Hermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Éditions_Hermann) in five volumes between 1975 and 1992.

:::

## Background and publication {#Background_and_publication}

In 1966 Meyer published a book on [probabilistic potential theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_potential_theory) called *Probability and Potentials* with publisher Blaisdell. He planned a follow-up book on [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), and published a draft of the book with [Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag) as lecture notes entitled *Processus de Markov* in 1967; however, the publication of [Robert Blumenthal](wikipedia://en/56586935) and [Ronald Getoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Getoor)'s 1968 book *[Markov Processes and Potential Theory](wikipedia://en/80593485)* led Meyer to shelve the plans.

In 1972 Dellacherie published the book *Capacities et Processus Stochastiques* with [Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag). Dellacherie and Meyer's individual books were described by [Joanna Mitro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Mitro) as "cornerstones of the '[general theory of (stochastic) processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_theory_of_processes).'"

The first volume of Dellacherie and Meyer's *Probabilités et Potentiel* was published in French in 1975 by [Éditions Hermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Éditions_Hermann), and was published in English as *Probabilities and Potential* by [North Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Holland_Publishing) in 1978. The second volume was published in French in 1980, and in English as *Probabilities and Potential B* in 1982, translated by J. P. Wilson. The third and fourth French volumes were published in 1983 and 1987, and comprise chapters IX–XI and XII–XVI respectively; the third and final English volume *Probabilities and Potential C* was published in 1988, translated by [James R. Norris](wikipedia://en/22849173), and comprises chapters IX–XIII. The fifth and final French volume, written together with Bernard Maisonneuve, was published in 1992.

## Content {#Content}

*Probabilities and Potential* was intended to be an update and extension of Meyer's 1966 book. Mitro writes that the raison d'être of the book is "the existence of an intimate connection between probability theory and potential theory".

[Marc Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor) summarises the content of each French volume of *Probabilities and Potential* as follows:

- Volume 1 covers integration, [analytic sets](wikipedia://en/634261), and [Gustave Choquet](wikipedia://en/17301193)'s theory of [capacities](wikipedia://en/14368398), and includes an introduction to [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).
- Volume 2 is titled *Martingale theory*, and covers [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), the [Doob-Meyer decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doob-Meyer_decomposition), [semimartingales](wikipedia://en/9653775) (including results like [Girsanov's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girsanov's_theorem) and the [Kunita–Watanabe inequality](wikipedia://en/33744493)), and [stochastic integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_integration) (including [Tanaka's formula](wikipedia://en/9666599)).
- Volume 3 is titled *Discrete potential theory*, and provides an introduction to discrete [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), covering réduite, "new methods in capacity theory", and applications.
- Volume 4 is titled *Potential theory associated with a resolvent; theory of Markov processes*, and covers Markovian [resolvents](wikipedia://en/3378540), [excessive functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excessive_function), the theory of [Feller processes](wikipedia://en/6891551) and Ray processes, [local times](wikipedia://en/5678101), [Kiyosi Itô](wikipedia://en/1329240)'s excursion theory, [Borel right processes](wikipedia://en/12086637), the [carré du champ operator](wikipedia://en/74738076), and [Lévy systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lévy_system).
- Volume 5 is titled *Markov processes (the end)*, and covers excessive measures, time reversal of Markov processes, Kuznetsov measures and Palm measures, [filtrations](wikipedia://en/57854817), [Malliavin Calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malliavin_Calculus), the [Riesz transforms](wikipedia://en/18673900), and [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454).

## Reception {#Reception}

Ronald Getoor, in his 1980 review of the first English volume, wrote that "the current generation of probabilists can only be grateful to Dellacherie and Meyer for their thorough exposition and hope that they do not tire in the task that they have set themselves." In a review of the fourth French volume, Joanna Mitro wrote that "the authors are in complete control of their subject, and the result is masterful. Meyer and Dellacherie...are responsible not only for creating a good deal of new mathematics there, but also for energetically propagating the ideas and techniques among members of the probability community...This current project is a *tour de force*."

In his memorial article on Meyer from 2006, Marc Yor described *Probabilities and Potential* as "a wonderful synthesis of the works of Paul André Meyer, starting from the publication of his book in 1966, and decanting the twenty five volumes of the \[Strasbourg\] Séminaire".
