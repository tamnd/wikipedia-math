# Demographics of Alderney

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Alderney"}

*Image: Guernsey-Alderney.png.* Island of Alderney

The **demography of Alderney** is analysed by the [States of Alderney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Alderney) and produced in Census reports. A Census was lasted held in 2013 however more recently eCensus demographics are compiled annually using various [Bailiwick of Guernsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailiwick_of_Guernsey) databases.[^o2]

:::

## Population {#Population}

As of 2017 the population of the island was estimated via the eCensus to be 1,985 with a seasonal population of over 3,000 during the summer because of tourism.[^o4]

## Gender {#Gender}

Gender disparity on the island is calculated by the Social Security Department and published in the annual eCensus report. Since 2008 Alderney has had a majority female population which has fluctuated between 50.5% and 51.4%.[^o2]
