# Frédérique Oggier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frédérique Oggier"}

**Frédérique Elise Oggier** is a Swiss mathematician and [coding theorist](wikipedia://en/321869) who works as an associate professor of physical and mathematical sciences at [Nanyang Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanyang_Technological_University) in [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore).

:::

## Education {#Education}

After earning bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), Oggier completed her doctorate at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne), in 2005, under the supervision of [Eva Bayer-Fluckiger](wikipedia://en/28654438).

## Books {#Books}

Oggier is the author of:

- *Lattices applied to coding for reliable and secure communications* (with Costa, Campello, Belfiore, and Viterbo, Springer, 2017)
- *An introduction to central simple algebras and their applications to wireless communication* (with Grégory Berhuy, American Mathematical Society, 2013)
- *Coding techniques for repairability in networked distributed storage systems* (with Anwitaman Datta, Now Publishers, 2013)
- *Cyclic division algebras: A tool for space-time coding* (with Jean-Claude Belfiore and Emanuele Viterbo, Now Publishers, 2007)
- *Algebraic number theory and code design for Rayleigh fading channels* (with Emanuele Viterbo, Now Publishers, 2004)
