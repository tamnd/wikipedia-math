# Zonal polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zonal polynomial"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **zonal polynomial** is a multivariate [symmetric](wikipedia://en/1440207) [homogeneous polynomial](wikipedia://en/1698977). The zonal polynomials form a [basis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basis_%28algebra%29) of the space of symmetric polynomials. Zonal polynomials appear in [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) with [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) argument which on the other hand appear in [matrixvariate](wikipedia://en/20556859) [distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_distributions) such as the [Wishart distribution](wikipedia://en/364996) when integrating over [compact Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group). The theory was started in [multivariate statistics](wikipedia://en/19384) in the 1960s and 1970s in a series of papers by [Alan Treleven James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Treleven_James) and his doctoral student [Alan Graham Constantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Graham_Constantine).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

They appear as [zonal spherical functions](wikipedia://en/17708915) of the [Gelfand pairs](wikipedia://en/3018619) $(S_{2n},H_n)$ (here, $H_n$ is the hyperoctahedral group) and $(Gl_n(\mathbb{R}), O_n)$, which means that they describe canonical basis of the double class algebras $\mathbb{C}[H_n \backslash S_{2n} / H_n]$ and $\mathbb{C}[O_d(\mathbb{R})\backslash M_d(\mathbb{R})/O_d(\mathbb{R})]$.

The zonal polynomials are the $\alpha=2$ case of the **C** normalization of the [Jack function](wikipedia://en/8665621).
