# Edward Copson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Copson"}

**Edward Thomas Copson** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (21 August 1901 – 16 February 1980) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed widely to the development of mathematics at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), serving as [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics) amongst other positions.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry), and was a pupil at [King Henry VIII School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Henry_VIII_School,_Coventry), Coventry. He studied at [St John's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Oxford). He was appointed by [E. T. Whittaker](wikipedia://en/1077330) as a lecturer at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), where he was later awarded a DSc.[^o4][^o5]

He married Beatrice, the elder daughter of [E. T. Whittaker](wikipedia://en/1077330),[^o6] and moved to the University of St Andrews where he was [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics), and later dean of science, then Master of the United College. He was instrumental in the construction of the new Mathematics Institute building at the university.

He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1924, his proposers being Sir [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Taylor_Whittaker), [Herbert Stanley Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Stanley_Allen), [Bevan Braithwaite Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bevan_Braithwaite_Baker) and [A. Crichton Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Crichton_Mitchell). He was awarded the [Keith Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Medal) by the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1942 for his research in mathematics.[^o7] He served as the Society's vice president from 1950 to 1953.[^o8]

## Work {#Work}

Copson's primary focus was in [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis), [asymptotic expansions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_expansions), [differential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations) and [integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equations), and applications to problems in theoretical physics. His first book *"The theory of functions of a complex variable"* was published in 1935.[^o10]
