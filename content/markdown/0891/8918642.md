# Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem** characterizes the [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) with [quasidihedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasidihedral) or wreathed[^o2] [Sylow 2-subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup). These are [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) either to three-dimensional [projective special linear groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) or [projective special unitary groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_unitary_group) over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of odd order, depending on a certain congruence, or to the [Mathieu group](wikipedia://en/380207) $M_{11}$. (Alperin Brauer Gorenstein 1970) proved this in the course of 261 pages. The subdivision by 2-fusion is sketched there, given as an exercise in (Gorenstein 1968, Ch. 7), and presented in some detail in (Kwon Lee Cho Park 1980).

:::
