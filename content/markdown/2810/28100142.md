# Mathematical Surveys and Monographs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical Surveys and Monographs"}

***Mathematical Surveys and Monographs*** is a [series of monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monographic_series) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

:::

Each volume in the series gives a survey of the subject along with a brief introduction to recent developments and unsolved problems.

The series has been known as *Mathematical Surveys and Monographs* since 1984. Its [ISSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISSN) is 0885–4653.[^o2] The series was founded in 1943 as 'Mathematical Surveys'.
