# Gavril Farkas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gavril Farkas"}

**Gavril Marius Farkas** (born 1973) is a Hungarian-Romanian-German mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is known for his work on [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260), [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609), and [syzygies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syzygy_%28mathematics%29) of [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808). Farkas is a professor of mathematics at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Farkas was born in [Oradea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oradea), Romania,[^o3] and earned his Bachelor of Science degree in mathematics from [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University) in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca), Romania, in 1995, specializing in geometry and topology. He obtained a Master of Science degree from the Mathematical Research Institute in the Netherlands in 1996 and completed his PhD at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 2000[^o4] with thesis *The Birational Geometry of the Moduli Space of Curves* written under the direction of Gerard van der Geer.

He has held academic positions at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), and the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin)[^o13] before becoming a full professor at Humboldt University in 2007.[^o6]

## Honors {#Honors}

- 2023 Elected member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o8]
- 2022 Elected full member of the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities)[^o9]
- 2019 [ERC Advanced Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) SYZYGY[^o10][^o11]
- 2014 Ad Astra Prize for Excellence in Scientific Research[^o12]
- 2005 [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship)[^o13]
