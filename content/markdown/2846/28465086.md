# Ciprian Foias

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ciprian Foias"}

**Ciprian Ilie Foiaș** (20 July 1933 – 22 March 2020) was a Romanian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was awarded the [Norbert Wiener Prize in Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbert_Wiener_Prize_in_Applied_Mathematics) in 1995, for his contributions in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Reșița](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reșița), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), Foiaș studied mathematics at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). He completed his dissertation in 1957,[^o4] but was not allowed to defend his thesis by the Communist government until 1962[^o5] because his father, a respected physician, had been sent to a [forced labor camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_camp) after the communists came to power.[^o6] He received his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1962 from the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230) (IMAR) under supervision of [Miron Nicolescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miron_Nicolescu).

Foias began teaching at the University of Bucharest in 1954.[^o4] From 1958 to 1978 he was a researcher at IMAR, and from 1966 to 1978 he was a professor in the Faculty of Mathematics at the University of Bucharest.[^o4][^o8] In recognition of his growing international reputation, he was named *[doctor docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent)* in 1968.

Foias was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice) in 1970, and again at the ICM in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) in 1978. Following his lecture in Helsinki, he defected to France, and then taught at the [University of Paris — XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) from 1979 to 1983. He later emigrated to the United States, where he was a professor at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) from 1983 until retirement. Beginning in 2000, he was a teacher and researcher at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), where he was a Distinguished Professor.[^o4]

Together with [Béla Szőkefalvi-Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béla_Szőkefalvi-Nagy), Foias proved the celebrated [commutant lifting theorem](wikipedia://en/9749892). The [Foias constant](wikipedia://en/40221453) is named after him. Foias is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o9] In 1994 he was elected honorary member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy).[^o8][^o10]

He died in [Tempe, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempe,_Arizona) on March 22, 2020.[^o11] That year, the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) established the Ciprian Foias Prize in Operator Theory, which is awarded every three years.[^o12]

## Publications {#Publications}

- with Ion Colojoară: *Theory of generalized spectral operators*. Gordon and Breach, 1968.
- with [Béla Szőkefalvi-Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béla_Szőkefalvi-Nagy): *Harmonic analysis of operators on Hilbert Space*. North Holland 1970 (Translated from the French; first edition: Masson 1967).
- with [Peter Constantin](wikipedia://en/82683896), [Roger Temam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Temam): *Attractors representing turbulent flows*. [Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30441553), vol. 53, 1985.
- with Hari Bercovici, [Carl Pearcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Pearcy): *Dual algebras with applications to invariant subspaces and dilation theory*. CBMS Regional Conf. Ser. in Math., vol. 56, American Mathematical Society, 1985.
- with Peter Constantin: *Navier Stokes Equations*. [University of Chicago Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Press), 1988. .
- with Peter Constantin, Roger Temam, and Basil Nicolaenko: *Integral Manifolds and Inertial Manifolds for Dissipative Partial Differential Equations*. [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), Applied Mathematical Sciences Series, volume 70, 1988.
- with Hitay Özbay, [Allen Tannenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Tannenbaum): *Robust control of infinite dimensional systems*. Springer, 1995.
- with [Roger Temam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Temam), Oscar Manley, and Ricardo Rosa: *Navier Stokes equations and Turbulence*. [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), 2001.
