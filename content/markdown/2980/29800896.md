# Henry Hickman Harte

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Hickman Harte"}

**Henry Hickman Harte** (1790–1848) was an Irish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [clergyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clergyman).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Harte, the son of a solicitor, was born in the county of [Limerick, Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limerick,_Ireland) in 1790.

He entered [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) in 1806, and became a Scholar in 1809. He earned his BA in 1811 (and MA in 1823).[^o3] He served as the [Donegall Lecturer in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegall_Lecturer_in_Mathematics) from 1827 to 1832.[^o4]

In 1831 Harte accepted the college living of [Cappagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cappagh,_County_Tyrone), diocese of Derry, co. Tyrone. He died on Sunday, 5 April 1848, having preached on the same day in his church, where he was also buried.

## Publications {#Publications}

Harte translated [Laplace](wikipedia://en/344783)'s *Système du Monde*.[^o6] He also added 'Mathematical Proofs and Explanatory Remarks,' Dublin, 1830.

He published a translation of [Poisson's](wikipedia://en/44670) *Mécanique, with Notes*, 2 vols. London, 1842, 8vo.[^o7]

He commenced another of Laplace's *Mécanique Céleste*.[^o8]
