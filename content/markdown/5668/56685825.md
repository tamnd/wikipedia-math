# Unscented optimal control

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Unscented optimal control"}

In mathematics, **unscented optimal control** combines the notion of the [unscented transform](wikipedia://en/33452725) with deterministic [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) to address a class of uncertain optimal control problems.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] It is a specific application of tychastic optimal control theory,[^o2][^o13][^o18][^o6] which is a generalization of [Riemmann-Stieltjes](wikipedia://en/330491) optimal control theory,[^o7][^o8] a concept introduced by [Ross](wikipedia://en/36457556) and his coworkers.

:::

## Mathematical description {#Mathematical_description}

Suppose that the initial state $x^0$ of a dynamical system,

$$\dot{x} = f(x, u, t)$$

is an uncertain quantity. Let $\mathrm{X}^i$ be the [sigma points](wikipedia://en/33452725). Then sigma-copies of the dynamical system are given by,

$$\dot\mathrm{X}^i = f(\mathrm{X}^i, u, t)$$

Applying standard deterministic optimal control principles to this ensemble generates an unscented optimal control.[^o10][^o11][^o12] Unscented optimal control is a special case of [tychastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyche) optimal control theory.[^o2][^o13][^o14] According to Aubin[^o14] and Ross,[^o2] tychastic processes differ from [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) in that a tychastic process is conditionally deterministic.

::: {#o16 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Unscented optimal control theory has been applied to UAV guidance,[^o12][^o17] spacecraft attitude control,[^o18] air-traffic control[^o19] and low-thrust trajectory optimization[^o3][^o10]

:::
