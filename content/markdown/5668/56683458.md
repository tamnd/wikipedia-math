# Brennan conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brennan conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), the **Brennan conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) estimating (under specified conditions) the integral powers of the moduli of the [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921) of [conformal maps](wikipedia://en/50627) into the [open unit disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_unit_disk). The conjecture was formulated by James E. Brennan in 1978.[^o2][^o4]

:::

Let $W$ be a [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) [open subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_subset) of $\mathbb{C}$ with at least two boundary points in the [extended complex plane](wikipedia://en/30876799#Extended_complex_numbers). Let $\varphi$ be a conformal map of $W$ onto the open unit disk. The **Brennan conjecture** states that $\int_W |\varphi\ '|^p\, \mathrm{d}x\, \mathrm{d}y < \infty$ whenever $4/3 < p < 4$. Brennan [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) the result when $4/3 < p < p_0$ for some constant $p_0 > 3$.[^o2] Bertilsson proved in 1999 that the result holds when $4/3 < p < 3.422$, but the full result remains open.[^o5][^o6]
