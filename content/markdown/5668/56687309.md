# Catherine Greenhill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catherine Greenhill"}

**Catherine Greenhill** is an Australian pure mathematician known for her research on [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477), [combinatorial enumeration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_enumeration) and [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876).[^o3] She is a professor of mathematics in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Greenhill was born in [Queensland, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensland). She attended [St Margaret's Anglican Girls' School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Margaret's_Anglican_Girls'_School), where she developed an interest in mathematics and science.[^o5] She then completed a Bachelor of Science (Hons) at the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland) in 1991, followed by a Master of Science by research in 1992, supervised by [Anne Penfold Street](wikipedia://en/53945987). She earned her Ph.D. in 1996 at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), under the supervision of [Peter M. Neumann](wikipedia://en/7151472).[^o3] Her dissertation was *From Multisets to Matrix Groups: Some Algorithms Related to the Exterior Square*.

## Career {#Career}

After completing her PhD, she obtained a postdoctoral position at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) where she worked on the analysis of combinatorial [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876) and its [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/6511).[^o3] She then moved back to Australia where she took up an [Australian Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Research_Council) postdoctoral fellowship at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), working with [Nick Wormald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Wormald) on the study of [random regular graphs](wikipedia://en/10151936).[^o3]

In 2003, she joined the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales) (UNSW) and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2014, the first female mathematician to earn such a promotion at UNSW.[^o18] In 2019, she was one of the first three female mathematicians to be promoted to Professor at UNSW.[^o8] In 2023, she was appointed Head of the Pure Mathematics Department at UNSW.[^o3] She is currently the head of the [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) group in the School of Mathematics at UNSW.[^o9] Her main research interests lie in asymptotic, probabilistic and algorithmic combinatorics.[^o9]

She is a former editor-in-chief of the *[Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics),*[^o10] a member of the editorial board for *Random Structures & Algorithms*[^o11] and the *[Australasian Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasian_Journal_of_Combinatorics),*[^o12] and a member of the editorial advisory board for *Special Matrices.[^o13]* She has held positions on the Women in Mathematics Special Interest Group of the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582), the Council of the [Combinatorial Mathematics Society of Australasia](wikipedia://en/53927291), the [Committee for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/14868) and the Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Committee of the School of Mathematics and Statistics at UNSW.[^o13][^o14] In 2025, she became the Mathematical Advisory Committee Chair for the Simon Marais Mathematics Competition[^o3][^o15]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- In 2010, she received the Hall Medal of the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919).[^o17]
- In 2013, she was awarded the UNSW Faculty of Science June Griffith Fellowship for Academic Women in Leadership.[^o18]
- In 2015, she received the Christopher Heyde Medal from the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) for distinguished research in the mathematical sciences.[^o18]
- In 2022, she was elected a Fellow of the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science).[^o19]
- In 2023, she was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_South_Wales).[^o20]
