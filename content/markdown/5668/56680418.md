# Plum Analytics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Plum Analytics"}

**Plum Analytics** was a [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia), [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania)-based [altmetrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altmetrics) company dedicated to measuring the influence of scientific research.

:::

## History {#History}

It was founded in 2011 by Andrea Michalek, who served as President of the company until 2019, and Mike Buschman. Michalek and Buschman met in the product development team at [Serials Solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serials_Solutions), which was subsequently integrated into [ProQuest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProQuest)[^o3].

Plum Analytics was acquired by [EBSCO Information Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_Information_Services) for an undisclosed amount in 2014[^o4], then later sold to [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) in February 2017 [^o5][^o6]. Its metrics were incorporated into Elsevier's existing products, including [Mendeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendeley) and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o7][^o8].

## Product and services {#Product_and_services}

::: {#o11 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="PlumX"}

### PlumX {#PlumX}

Plum Analytics is best known for **PlumX**, which was the company's first product. PlumX is an online tool that provides altmetrics for peer-reviewed journal articles and other scholarly works [^o12] by aggregating information from different sources including Wikipedia, [Mendeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendeley) (for bookmarks and downloads), the [USPTO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Patent_and_Trademark_Office) (for patents), [SSRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Science_Research_Network) (for citation and usage data), [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook) (for shares and [Likes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_like_button)), Lexis-Nexis Metabase (for news) and Overton.io (for government policy citations)[^o13].

In August 2015, Plum Analytics released the "PlumX Suite", which consists of five separate products: Metrics, Dashboards, +Grants, Benchmarks, and Funding Opportunities[^o14]. These were discountined once the company was acquired by Elsever, and PlumX data is now available primarily through badges on Scopus, Mendeley and institutional repositories[^o15].

:::
