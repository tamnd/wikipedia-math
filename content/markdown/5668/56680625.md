# Eva Viehmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eva Viehmann"}

**Eva Viehmann** (born 1980) is a German mathematician who holds a professorial chair in the arithmetic geometry and representation theory research group at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). Before that she was a professor working on [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).

:::

Viehmann studied at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where her 2005 doctoral thesis, *On affine Deligne-Lusztig varieties for $\operatorname{GL}_n$* (supervised by [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548)) won the Felix Hausdorff Memorial Award. She earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2010, and in 2012 was appointed to her professorship at the Technical University of Munich.

Viehmann won the 2012 von Kaven Award in mathematics of the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) for her work on the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905). She was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking in the section on Lie Theory and Generalizations. She was also the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2018. In 2021 she became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).
