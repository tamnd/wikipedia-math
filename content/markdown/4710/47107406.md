# Robert Brown Gardner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Brown Gardner"}

**Robert Brown (Robby) Gardner** ([Tarrytown, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarrytown,_New_York), February 27, 1939 – May 5, 1998) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gardner graduated from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1959,[^o5] earned a master's degree from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1960,[^o6] and completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate_of_philosophy) in 1965 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), under the supervision of [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905).[^o7]

After this, he worked at many places, including the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), and worked as assistant professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) between 1967 and 1970. He joined the faculty of the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 1971 and became a full professor there in 1977.[^o5] He died on May 5, 1998.

## Research {#Research}

Gardner was the author and co-author of three influential books, produced more than fifty papers,[^o9] eighteen masters students and thirteen Ph.D. students.[^o6][^o5] [Robert Bryant](wikipedia://en/24204031), [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University)'s Professor of Mathematics and the president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (2015-2017) was a student of his.[^o7]

His 1991 book, *Exterior Differential Systems*, coauthored with [R. Bryant](wikipedia://en/24204031), [S. S. Chern](wikipedia://en/391905), H. Goldschmidt, and [P. Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414), is the standard reference for the [subject](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_differential_system).[^o3]

He is better known in the United States for his improvements and popularization of the methods of [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274) (most notably, [Cartan's equivalence method](wikipedia://en/1841654), an [algorithmic](wikipedia://en/775) procedure for determining if two geometric shapes are different). The works of Cartan were hard to grasp for most students, and Gardner worked to explain them in more accessible ways.[^o10][^o11]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

In his memory, the UNC Mathematics Department created the Robert Brown Gardner Memorial Fund, devoted to supporting graduate student activities.[^o5]
