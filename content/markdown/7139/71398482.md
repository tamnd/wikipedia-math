# Madhav V. Nori

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Madhav V. Nori"}

**Madhav Vithal Nori** is an Indian mathematician. In 1980 he has received the [INSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) Medal for Young Scientists.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Nori was awarded his PhD in mathematics in 1981 from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai).[^o4] He studies within the fields of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). His areas of interest in research focus on [algebraic cycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_cycles), [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590), [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316), and their interactions. Nori received the [INSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) Medal for Young Scientists in 1980 and is an elected Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences), [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore).

## The fundamental group scheme {#The_fundamental_group_scheme}

Under the direction of [Conjeerveram S. Seshadri](wikipedia://en/5633765) Nori proved the existence of the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612) $\pi_1(X,x)$ during his PhD work, using the theory of [essentially finite vector bundles](wikipedia://en/30035960) that he defined.[^o6][^o7] The fundamental group scheme is also known as Nori fundamental group scheme, taking the name by his creator, and often also denoted as $\pi^N(X,x)$, where $N$ stands for Nori. There is a special family of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) called [Nori-semistable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nori-semistable) vector bundles in Nori's honor as he had the first intuition for their existence and properties.[^o8] His construction has been since then further generalized, for instance a proof of the existence of the fundamental group scheme for schemes defined over Dedekind schemes has been provided by [Marco Antei](wikipedia://en/74584293), Michel Emsalem and Carlo Gasbarri.[^o9][^o10]
