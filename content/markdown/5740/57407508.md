# Vicky Neale

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Vicky Neale"}

**Victoria Ruth Neale** (March 1984 – 3 May 2023)[^o2] was a British mathematician and writer. She was Whitehead Lecturer at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)'s [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850) and Supernumerary Fellow at [Balliol College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College).[^o4][^o5] Her research specialty was [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). The author of the 2017 book *[Closing the Gap: The Quest to Understand Prime Numbers](wikipedia://en/63087914)*,[^o6][^o7] she was interviewed on several [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC) radio programs as a mathematics expert.[^o8][^o9] In addition, she wrote for [*The Conversation*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Conversation_%28website%29) and *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)*.[^o10][^o11] Her other educational and outreach activities included lecturing at the PROMYS Europe high-school program[^o12] and helping to organize the [European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/36583162).[^o13]

:::

Neale was born in 1984.[^o14] She obtained her PhD in 2011 from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). Her thesis work, supervised by [Ben Joseph Green](wikipedia://en/1927952), concerned [Waring's problem](wikipedia://en/33569).[^o4] She then taught at Cambridge while being Director of Studies in mathematics at [Murray Edwards College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Edwards_College,_Cambridge),[^o13][^o16] before moving to Oxford in the summer of 2014.[^o17]

Neale died on 3 May 2023, at the age of 39.[^o18] She had been diagnosed with a rare type of cancer in 2021.[^o19]
