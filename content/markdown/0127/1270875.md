# Random minimum spanning tree

*Image: Random minimum spanning tree.svg.* Random minimum spanning tree on the same graph but with randomized weights.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Random minimum spanning tree"}

In mathematics, a **random minimum spanning tree** may be formed by assigning [independent](wikipedia://en/27593) random weights from some distribution to the edges of an [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph), and then constructing the [minimum spanning tree](wikipedia://en/41795) of the graph.

:::

When the given graph is a [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) on $n$ vertices, and the edge weights have a continuous [distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) whose derivative at zero is $D > 0$, then the expected weight of its random minimum spanning trees is bounded by a constant, rather than growing as a function of $n$. More precisely, this constant tends in the limit (as $n$ goes to infinity) to $\zeta(3)/D$, where $\zeta$ is the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) and $\zeta(3) \approx 1.202$ is [Apéry's constant](wikipedia://en/2216711). For instance, for edge weights that are uniformly distributed on the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324), the derivative is $D = 1$, and the limit is just $\zeta(3)$. For other graphs, the expected weight of the random minimum spanning tree can be calculated as an integral involving the [Tutte polynomial](wikipedia://en/3892303) of the graph.

In contrast to [uniformly random spanning trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_spanning_tree) of complete graphs, for which the typical [diameter](wikipedia://en/2979341) is proportional to the square root of the number of vertices, random minimum spanning trees of complete graphs have typical diameter proportional to the cube root.

Random minimum spanning trees of [grid graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_graph) may be used for [invasion percolation](wikipedia://en/50901898) models of liquid flow through a porous medium, and for [maze generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maze_generation).
