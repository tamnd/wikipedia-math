# Stephen Stigler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Stigler"}

**Stephen Mack Stigler** (born August 10, 1941) is the [Ernest DeWitt Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_DeWitt_Burton) [Distinguished Service Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professors_in_the_United_States#Distinguished_%28research/teaching%29_professor) at the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o3] He has authored several books on the [history of statistics](wikipedia://en/14986442); he is the son of the economist [George Stigler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Stigler).

:::

Stigler is also known for [Stigler's law of eponymy](wikipedia://en/682348) which states that no scientific discovery is named after its original discoverer – whose first formulation he credits to sociologist [Robert K. Merton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_K._Merton).

## Biography {#Biography}

Stigler was born in Minneapolis.[^o5] He received his Ph.D. in 1967 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His dissertation was on [linear functions](wikipedia://en/152111) of [order statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_statistics), and his advisor was [Lucien Le Cam](wikipedia://en/2629104). His research has focused on statistical theory of [robust estimators](wikipedia://en/2885691) and the [history of statistics](wikipedia://en/14986442).

Stigler taught at [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) until 1979 when he joined the University of Chicago. In 2006, he was elected to membership of the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society),[^o6] and is a past president (1994) of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

He received the [Quantrell Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantrell_Award).[^o7]

His father was the economist [George Stigler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Stigler), who was a close friend of [Milton Friedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Friedman).
