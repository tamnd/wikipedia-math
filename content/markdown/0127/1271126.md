# Aida Yasuaki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aida Yasuaki"}

Aida **Aida Yasuaki** also known as **Aida Ammei**, was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period).[^o3]

:::

He made significant contributions to the fields of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and furthered methods for simplifying [continued fractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continued_fractions).

Aida created an original symbol for "equal". This was the first appearance of the notation for equal in East Asia.[^o4]
