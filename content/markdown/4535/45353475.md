# Kenneth Millett

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth Millett"}

**Kenneth C. Millett** (born 1941) is a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara).[^o2] His research concerns [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), and the applications of knot theory to [DNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA) structure;[^o3] his initial is the "M" in the name of the [HOMFLY polynomial](wikipedia://en/1039260).[^o4]

:::

Millett graduated from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1963 with a bachelor's degree in mathematics.[^o2] He earned his Ph.D. in 1967 from the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) under the supervision of Edward R. Fadell. After short-term instructor positions at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) and MIT, he joined the UCSB faculty in 1969 and was promoted to professor in 1979.[^o2]

Millett won the [Carl B. Allendoerfer Award](wikipedia://en/40343255) of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) in 1989 and the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) in 1991 for a paper on knot theory with [W. B. R. Lickorish](wikipedia://en/11177096).[^o6][^o7] He became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 2000.[^o2] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
