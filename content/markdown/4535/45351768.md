# Emma Previato

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Emma Previato"}

**Emma Previato** (November 29, 1952 – June 29, 2022) was an Italian–born American professor of mathematics at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University).[^o3] Her research focused on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Previato received her Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1983 under [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594). She was a faculty member at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University).[^o3] She was the author or co-author of nearly 100 research articles. She served as editor or co-editor of 6 books,[^o3] including *Dictionary of Applied Math for Engineers and Scientists* (2002, [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press)).[^o6]

Previato founded Boston University's chapters of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o9] and of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2003, she received the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) Northeastern Section's Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics for her work in and out of the classroom, especially her mentoring of students.[^o9]

In 2012, Previato became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Previato was born in [Badia Polesine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badia_Polesine) in 1952. She earned undergraduate and master's degrees from the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) before beginning her PhD at Harvard.[^o12]
