::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="A4 polytope"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 9 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/1645899) with A<sub>4</sub> symmetry. There is one self-dual regular form, the [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) with 5 vertices.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A<sub>4</sub> symmetry, or \[3,3,3\] is order 120, with Conway quaternion notation +<sup>1</sup>/<sub>60</sub>\[I×$\overline{I}$\].2<sub>1</sub>. Its abstract structure is the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) S<sub>5</sub>. Three forms with symmetric Coxeter diagrams have extended symmetry, \[\[3,3,3\]\] of order 240, and Conway notation ±<sup>1</sup>/<sub>60</sub>\[I×$\overline{I}$\].2, and abstract structure S<sub>5</sub>×C<sub>2</sub>.

:::

## Visualizations {#Visualizations}

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the A<sub>4</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups. Three [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) [2D projections](wikipedia://en/203052) are given, for the A<sub>4</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, A<sub>2</sub> [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004), showing symmetry order 5,4,3, and doubled on even A<sub>k</sub> orders to 10,4,6 for symmetric Coxeter diagrams.

The 3D picture are drawn as [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595) projections, centered on the cell at pos. 3, with a consistent orientation, and the 5 cells at position 0 are shown solid.

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The coordinates of uniform 4-polytopes with pentachoric symmetry can be generated as permutations of simple integers in 5-space, all in hyperplanes with normal vector (1,1,1,1,1). The A<sub>4</sub> [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) is [palindromic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palindrome), so repeated polytopes exist in pairs of dual configurations. There are 3 symmetric positions, and 6 pairs making the total 15 permutations of one or more rings. All 15 are listed here in order of [binary arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_arithmetic) for clarity of the coordinate generation from the rings in each corresponding Coxeter diagram.

The number of vertices can be deduced here from the [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of the number of coordinates, peaking at 5 [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) for the omnitruncated form with 5 unique coordinate values.
