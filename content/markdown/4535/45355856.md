::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="List of F4 polytopes"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 9 [uniform 4-polytopes](wikipedia://en/1645899) with F<sub>4</sub> symmetry, and one chiral half symmetry, the [snub 24-cell](wikipedia://en/3704756). There is one self-dual regular form, the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) with 24 vertices.

:::

## Visualization {#Visualization}

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the [F<sub>4</sub> Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004#Symmetry_groups_of_regular_polytopes), and other subgroups.

The 3D picture are drawn as [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595) projections, centered on the cell at pos. 3, with a consistent orientation, and the 5 cells at position 0 are shown solid.

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

Vertex coordinates for all 15 forms are given below, including dual configurations from the two regular 24-cells. (The dual configurations are named in bold.) Active rings in the first and second nodes generate points in the first column. Active rings in the third and fourth nodes generate the points in the second column. The sum of each of these points are then permutated by coordinate positions, and sign combinations. This generates all vertex coordinates. Edge lengths are 2.

The only exception is the snub 24-cell, which is generated by *half* of the coordinate permutations, only an even number of coordinate swaps. φ=($\sqrt{5}$+1)/2.
