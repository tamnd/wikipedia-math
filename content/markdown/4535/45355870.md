::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="H4 polytope"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 15 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/1645899) with H<sub>4</sub> symmetry. Two of these, the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) and [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), are [regular](wikipedia://en/518693).

:::

## Visualizations {#Visualizations}

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the H<sub>4</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

The 3D picture are drawn as [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595) projections, centered on the cell at pos. 3, with a consistent orientation, and the 5 cells at position 0 are shown solid.

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The coordinates of uniform polytopes from the H<sub>4</sub> family are complicated. The regular ones can be expressed in terms of the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) *φ* = (1 + $\sqrt{5}$)/2 and *σ* = (3$\sqrt{5}$ + 1)/2. Coxeter expressed them as 5-dimensional coordinates.[^o8]

::: {#o9 .table cue=table}

| *n* | [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) | [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) |
|---|---|---|
| 4D | The 600 vertices of the 120-cell include all [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of (0, 0, ±2, ±2) (±1, ±1, ±1, ±$\sqrt{5}$) (±*φ*<sup>−2</sup>, ±*φ*, ±*φ*, ±*φ*) (±*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, ±*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, ±*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, ±*φ*<sup>2</sup>) and all [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of (0, ±*φ*<sup>−2</sup>, ±1, ±*φ*<sup>2</sup>) (0, ±*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, ±*φ*, ±$\sqrt{5}$) (±*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, ±1, ±*φ*, ±2) | The vertices of a 600-cell centered at the origin of 4-space, with edges of length 1/*φ* (where *φ* = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386)), can be given as follows: 16 vertices of the form $\tfrac{1}{2}$ (±1, ±1, ±1, ±1), and 8 vertices obtained from (0, 0, 0, ±1) by permuting coordinates. The remaining 96 vertices are obtained by taking [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of $\tfrac{1}{2}$ (±*φ*, ±1, ±1/*φ*, 0). |
| 5D | Zero-sum permutation: (30): $\sqrt{5}$ (1, 1, 0, −1, −1) (10): ±(4, −1, −1, −1, −1) (40): ±(*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>, 2, −*σ*) (40): ±(*φ*, *φ*, *φ*, −2, −(*σ*−1)) (120): ±$\sqrt{5}$ (*φ*, 0, 0, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>, −1) (120): ±(2, 2, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{5}$, −*φ*, −3) (240): ±(*φ*<sup>2</sup>, 2*φ*<sup>−1</sup>, *φ*<sup>−2</sup>, −1, −2*φ*) | Zero-sum permutation: (20): $\sqrt{5}$ (1, 0, 0, 0, −1) (40): ±(*φ*<sup>2</sup>, *φ*<sup>−2</sup>, −1, −1, −1) (60): ±(2, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>, *φ*<sup>−1</sup>, −*φ*, −*φ*) |

:::
