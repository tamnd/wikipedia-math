# Ultrahyperbolic equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ultrahyperbolic equation"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), the **ultrahyperbolic equation** is a class of [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) (PDE) [^o2] first described by [R. Courant](wikipedia://en/715238). Specifically non-normal [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/1575825)[^o3] they are used for an unknown [scalar function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_function) $u$ of $2n$ [variables](wikipedia://en/3728109) $x_{1}, ..., x_{n}, y_{1}, ..., y_{n}$ of the form

:::

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x_1^2} + \cdots + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x_n^2} - \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y_1^2} - \cdots - \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y_n^2} = 0.$$

More generally, if $a$ is any [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) in $2n$ variables with [signature](wikipedia://en/463225) $(n, n)$, then any PDE whose [principal part](wikipedia://en/1225337) is $a_{ij}u_{x_ix_j}$ is said to be ultrahyperbolic. Any such equation can be put in the form above by means of a change of variables.[^o4]

The ultrahyperbolic equation has been studied from a number of viewpoints. On the one hand, it resembles the classical [wave equation](wikipedia://en/33691). This has led to a number of developments concerning its [characteristics](wikipedia://en/751933), one of which is due to [Fritz John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_John): the [John equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_equation).

In 2008, Walter Craig and Steven Weinstein proved that under a nonlocal constraint, the initial value problem is [well-posed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-posed) for initial data given on a codimension-one [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101).[^o5] And later, in 2022, a research team at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) extended the conditions for solving ultrahyperbolic wave equations to [complex-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_time_dimensions) (kime), demonstrated space-kime dynamics, and showed data science applications using tensor-based linear modeling of [functional magnetic resonance imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_magnetic_resonance_imaging) data.[^o6][^o7]

The equation has also been studied from the point of view of [symmetric spaces](wikipedia://en/1642877), and [elliptic differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_differential_operator).[^o8] In particular, the ultrahyperbolic equation satisfies an analog of the [mean value theorem](wikipedia://en/19662) for [harmonic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_functions).
