# Curtis L. Meinert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Curtis L. Meinert"}

**Curtis Lynea Meinert** (June 30, 1934 – June 13, 2023) was an American clinical trialist. He was a professor of epidemiology and biostatistics at [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Meinert was born on June 30, 1934, on a farm outside [Sleepy Eye, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleepy_Eye,_Minnesota).[^o4] His parents were Mabel Eleanor Christensen and Arthur August Edward Meinert.[^o4] He was raised in rural [Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota).[^o5] He completed a B.A. in psychology from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1956.[^o4] He earned a doctor of philosophy in statistics at University of Minnesota in 1964.[^o5][^o6] His dissertation was titled *Quantitation of the isotope displacement of immunoassay of insulin*.[^o7] His advisor was [Richard B. McHugh](wikipedia://en/60085534).[^o8]

Meinert was the head of a clinical trial coordinating center at [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland). Meinert researched randomized clinical trials for AIDS, cardiovascular diseases, and asthma.[^o5] He was a professor of epidemiology and biostatistics and the inaugural director of the Center for Clinical Trials at Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health.[^o9]

In 1979, Meinert was elected a fellow of the [American College of Epidemiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_College_of_Epidemiology). In 1995, he was elected [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science). He was a 2001 Fellow of the [American Heart Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Heart_Association). In 2006, he was elected a fellow of the [Society for Clinical Trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Clinical_Trials).[^o9] In 2005, Johns Hopkins University established the professorship, Curtis L. Meinert Professor of Clinical Trials.[^o10]

Meinert married Susan J. Matson on June 22, 1957. They had three daughters. He died on June 13, 2023, at the age of 88.[^o4]
