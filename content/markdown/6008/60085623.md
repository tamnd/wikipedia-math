# James Tonascia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Tonascia"}

**James Tonascia** is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [biostatistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biostatistician). He is the [Curtis L. Meinert](wikipedia://en/60085212) Professor of [Clinical Trials](wikipedia://en/241717) at [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health). He joined the faculty in 1970 and was promoted to full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in 1981.[^o2]

:::
