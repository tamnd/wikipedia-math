# Dugald Macpherson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dugald Macpherson"}

*Image: Dugald Macpherson (cropped).jpg.* Dugald Macpherson

**H. Dugald Macpherson** is a mathematician and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds).[^o2]

:::

He obtained his DPhil from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1983 for his thesis *Enumeration of Orbits of Infinite Permutation Groups* under the supervision of [Peter Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Cameron_%28mathematician%29). In 1997, he was awarded the [Junior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prizes) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3] He continues to research into [permutation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutation_groups) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). He is scientist in charge of the [MODNET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MODNET) team at the University of Leeds.[^o4] He co-authored the book *Notes on Infinite Permutation Groups*.[^o5]
