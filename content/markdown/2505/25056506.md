# Pirmin Stekeler-Weithofer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pirmin Stekeler-Weithofer"}

*Image: Akademientag 2015 Berlin - Pirmin Stekeler-Weithofer 2.JPG.* Pirmin Stekeler-Weithofer (2015)

**Pirmin Stekeler-Weithofer** (born 21 December 1952 in [Meßkirch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meßkirch)) is a German [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and professor of theoretical philosophy at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig). He was the president of the International Ludwig Wittgenstein Society (2006–09) and is now a vice president of this institution.[^o2]

:::

## Philosophy {#Philosophy}

The philosopher studied mathematics and philosophy in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), [Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz), Berlin, [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) and teaches theoretical philosophy at the University of Leipzig.

Stekeler-Weithofer contributes to the [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [action theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_theory_%28philosophy%29), [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and the relationships between classical and analytical philosophy. An important point is the philosophy of Hegel.
