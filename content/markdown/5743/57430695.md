# Fyodor Gakhov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fyodor Gakhov"}

**Fyodor Dmitriyevich Gakhov** (Фёдор Дмитриевич Гахов; 19 February 1906, [Cherkessk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkessk) — 30 March 1980, [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk)) was a Soviet and Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a specialist in the field of boundary value problems for analytic functions of a [complex variable](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fyodor Dmitriyevich Gakhov was born on 19 February 19 1906 in Batalpashinskaya village (present-day [Cherkessk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkessk), [Stavropol Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavropol_Krai)) in the family of a shoemaker. His father died when he was a boy. After graduating from the Circassian Pedagogical College in 1925, he entered [Gorsky Pedagogical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ossetian_State_University) in [Vladikavkaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladikavkaz).[^o4]

In 1928, on the recommendation of Professor L. I. Kreer, he was admitted to [Kazan Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_Federal_University), which he graduated in 1930.[^o4]

In 1934—1937 Fyodor Gakhov taught [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the universities of [Sverdlovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterinburg), and alter enrolled in the graduate school of Kazan University, where his scientific guide was [Boris Gagaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Gagaev). He decided to specialize on the solution of boundary value problems in the theory of analytic functions and the corresponding integral integral equations.[^o4]

In 1937 Gakhov defended his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) thesis "*Linear boundary value problems in the theory of [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478)*". This work was awarded the second prize at the All-Union competition of works of young scientists. From 1937 to 1939 he worked as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the Kazan University, and from 1939 to 1947 he was the head of the Department of Mathematical Analysis of the [North Ossetian Pedagogical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ossetian_State_University).[^o5]

In 1943 he defended his [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) thesis "*Boundary value problems in the theory of analytic functions and singular integral equations*". In 1943 he became a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).[^o4]

In 1947—1953 years Gakhov worked as a professor, and later as head of the Department of Differential Equations of Kazan University. In 1953—1961 he headed the Department of Differential Equations of [Rostov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_State_University).[^o5]

From 1961 until his death Gakhov worked at [Belarusian State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_State_University). There he headed the Department of Mathematical Analysis, then the Department of Theory of Functions and Functional Analysis, and later worked as a professor in the Department of Theory of Functions. In 1962—1963 years he was Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics. In 1966 Gakhov was elected a full member of the Academy of Sciences of the Byelorussian SSR.[^o5]

Fyodor Gakhov died on 30 March 1980 and was buried at the Northern Cemetery of [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk).[^o4]

He was the author of the famous monograph "*Boundary Problems*", where he presented the results on solution of [Riemann-Hilbert problem](wikipedia://en/9710396), which he studied together with a group of students. This work has been translated to [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language)[^o6][^o7] and [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language).[^o8]
