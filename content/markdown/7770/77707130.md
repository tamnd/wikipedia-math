# Filip Rindler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Filip Rindler"}

*Image: Filip Rindler Oberwolfach 2026.jpg.* Rindler at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2026

**Filip Rindler** (born August 15, 1984 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). After studying at [TU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), he finished his doctorate at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 2011 under the supervision of [Jan Kristensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Kristensen). Since 2020 he is a Professor at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick). He works on [the calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), as well as mathematical material science.

:::

In 2018 he was awarded the prestigious [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for "his solutions to fundamental problems on the border between the theory of partial differential equations, calculus of variations and geometric measure theory".[^o2] He received an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Starting Grant in 2017[^o3] and an ERC Consolidator Grant in 2024[^o3]
