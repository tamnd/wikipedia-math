# N. U. Prabhu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="N. U. Prabhu"}

**Narahari Umanath Prabhu** (25 April 1924 – 14 October 2022) was an Indian-American mathematician, known for his contributions to [operation research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_research), in particular [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).[^o4] Although he did not seek a Ph.D., he guided a number of Ph.Ds.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Prabhu, who was from [Mangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangalore) and [Kozhikode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozhikode) got a B.A. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras) (1946), a M.A. in statistics from [University of Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bombay) (1950). Prabhu was the founding head of the department of statistics, [Karnatak University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnatak_University), Dharwar in 1951.[^o6] He obtained a M.Sc. in mathematics from [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) on a thesis entitled *Solution to Some Dam Problems* (1957).

Prabhu lectured at [Gauhati University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauhati_University) (1950–1952), [Karnatak University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnatak_University) (1952–1961), [University of Western Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Australia) (1961–1964), before becoming [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) at [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) (1964–1965) and [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) (1965–1994) where he became [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) (1967) and [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) (1994). Prabhu also had longer research stays at [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) in [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta) (1961), [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) (1970, 1973), [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa) (1973), [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) (1978), [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (1979), and [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) (1984) .

Prabhu was the founding [editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editing) of the journal [Queueing Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queueing_Systems) (1986–1994) and has edited several other journals, as well as published books on *Foundations of Queueing Theory* ([Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag), 1997) and *Stochastic storage processes* (Springer, 1998).

The South Asia Program at Cornell created the [Rabindranath Tagore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabindranath_Tagore) Endowment in Modern Indian Literature, made possible through a gift by Professor Emeritus Narahari Umanath Prabhu and his wife .

Prabhu died on 14 October 2022, at the age of 98.[^o7]
