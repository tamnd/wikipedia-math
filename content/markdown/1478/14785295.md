# David Aldous

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Aldous"}

the English actor

**David John Aldous** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (born 13 July 1952) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his research on [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and its applications. In particular, his research studies topics such as [exchangeability](wikipedia://en/6753623), [weak convergence](wikipedia://en/6811795), [Markov chain mixing times](wikipedia://en/6101938), the continuum random tree and stochastic coalescence. He entered [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge), in 1970 and received his Ph.D. at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1977 under his advisor, D. J. H. Garling. Aldous was on the faculty at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1979 until his retirement in 2018.[^o4][^o5] He is currently an [affiliate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affiliate_professor) at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and lives near Seattle.[^o6]

:::

He was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1980, the [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize) in 1993,[^o7] and was elected a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 1994.[^o8] In 2004, Aldous was elected a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o9] From 2004 to 2010, Aldous was an [Andrew Dickson White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Dickson_White) Professor-at-Large at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o10] He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in 1998 in Berlin[^o11] and a plenary speaker at the ICM in 2010 in Hyderabad.[^o12] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13] He discovered (independently from [Andrei Broder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Broder)) an algorithm for generating a [uniform spanning tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_spanning_tree) of a given graph.
