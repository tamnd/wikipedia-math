This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of two [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651), formed by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) each of the tetrahedra in the [stellated octahedron](wikipedia://en/2601866). It is related to the [cantic cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantic_cube) construction of the truncated tetrahedron, as , which is one of the two dual positions represented in this compound.

:::

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is the same as a convex, but nonuniform [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) having 12 rectangular faces.
