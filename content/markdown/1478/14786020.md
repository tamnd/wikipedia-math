# Ted Harris (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ted Harris"}

**Theodore Edward Harris** (11 January 1919 – 3 November 2005) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his research on [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), including such areas as general state-space [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876) (often now called [Harris chains](wikipedia://en/19485126)), the theory of [branching processes](wikipedia://en/377202) and stochastic models of interacting particle systems such as the [contact process](wikipedia://en/5097569). The [Harris inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_inequality) in [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics) and [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805) is named after him.

:::

He received his Ph.D. at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1947 under advisor [Samuel Wilks](wikipedia://en/8136395). From 1947 until 1966 he worked for the [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation), heading their mathematics department from 1959 to 1965. From 1966 until retirement in 1989 he was Professor of Mathematics and Electrical Engineering at [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).

He was elected to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1988.[^o5]
