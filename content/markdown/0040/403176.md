# Suzuki sporadic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suzuki sporadic group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Suzuki group** *Suz* or *Sz* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

448,345,497,600 = 2<sup>13</sup> · 3<sup>7</sup> · 5<sup>2</sup> · 7 · 11 · 13 ≈ 4.

## History {#History}

*Suz* is one of the 26 Sporadic groups and was discovered by (txt) as a [rank 3 permutation group](wikipedia://en/29763000) on 1782 points with point stabilizer G<sub>2</sub>(4). It is not related to the [Suzuki groups of Lie type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_groups_of_Lie_type). The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 6 and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2.

## Complex Leech lattice {#Complex_Leech_lattice}

The 24-dimensional [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) has a fixed-point-free automorphism of order 3. Identifying this with a complex cube root of 1 makes the Leech lattice into a 12 dimensional lattice over the [Eisenstein integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenstein_integers), called the **complex Leech lattice**. The automorphism group of the complex Leech lattice is the universal cover 6 · Suz of the Suzuki group. This makes the group 6 · Suz · 2 into a maximal subgroup of [Conway's group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway's_group) Co<sub>0</sub> = 2 · Co<sub>1</sub> of automorphisms of the Leech lattice, and shows that it has two complex irreducible representations of dimension 12. The group 6 · Suz acting on the complex Leech lattice is analogous to the group 2 · Co<sub>1</sub> acting on the Leech lattice.

## Suzuki chain {#Suzuki_chain}

The Suzuki chain or Suzuki tower is the following tower of [rank 3 permutation groups](wikipedia://en/29763000) from (Suzuki 1969), each of which is the point stabilizer of the next.

- *G*<sub>2</sub>(2) = *U*(3, 3) · 2 has a rank 3 action on 36 = 1 + 14 + 21 points with point stabilizer PSL(3, 2) · 2
- *J*<sub>2</sub> · 2 has a rank 3 action on 100 = 1 + 36 + 63 points with point stabilizer *G*<sub>2</sub>(2)
- *G*<sub>2</sub>(4) · 2 has a rank 3 action on 416 = 1 + 100 + 315 points with point stabilizer J<sub>2</sub> · 2
- Suz · 2 has a rank 3 action on 1782 = 1 + 416 + 1365 points with point stabilizer G<sub>2</sub>(4) · 2
