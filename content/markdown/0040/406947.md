# Synchrotron function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Synchrotron function"}

*Image: Synchrotron Functions.svg.* First and second Synchrotron functions.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) the **synchrotron functions** are defined as follows (for *x* ≥ 0):[^o2]

:::

- First synchrotron function $$F(x) = x \int_x^\infty K_{\frac{5}{3}}(t)\,dt$$
- Second synchrotron function $$G(x) = x K_{\frac{2}{3}}(x)$$

where *K*<sub>*j*</sub> is the modified [Bessel function](wikipedia://en/4700) of the second kind.

## Use in astrophysics {#Use_in_astrophysics}

In [astrophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics), x is usually a ratio of frequencies, that is, the frequency over a critical frequency (critical frequency is the frequency at which most [synchrotron radiation](wikipedia://en/170808) is radiated). This is needed when calculating the spectra for different types of synchrotron emission. It takes a spectrum of electrons (or any charged particle) generated by a separate process (such as a power law distribution of electrons and positrons from a constant injection spectrum) and converts this to the spectrum of photons generated by the input electrons/positrons.
