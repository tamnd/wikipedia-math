# Unit cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Unit cube"}

*Image: Unitcube.svg.* Unit cube

A **unit cube**, more formally a **cube of side 1**, is a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) whose sides are 1 unit long.[^o2][^o3] The volume of a 3-dimensional unit cube is 1 cubic unit, and its total surface area is 6 square units.[^o4]

:::

## Unit hypercube {#Unit_hypercube}

The term *unit cube* or **unit hypercube** is also used for [hypercubes](wikipedia://en/39783), or "cubes" in [*n*-dimensional spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-dimensional_space), for values of *n* other than 3 and edge length 1.[^o2][^o3]

Sometimes the term "unit cube" refers in specific to the set \[0, 1\]<sup>*n*</sup> of all *n*-tuples of numbers in the interval \[0, 1\].[^o2]

The length of the longest diagonal of a unit hypercube of *n* dimensions is $\sqrt n$, the square root of *n* and the (Euclidean) length of the vector (1,1,1,....1,1) in *n*-dimensional space.[^o3]
