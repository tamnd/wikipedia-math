# Transport integrals

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transport integrals"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics), the **transport integrals** (sometimes **transport functions**) are a family of [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) arising in the theory of [transport phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_phenomena) of solids.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The transport integrals are defined by the integral representation[^o4][^o5]

$$J_n(x) = \int_0^x t^n \frac{e^t}{(e^t - 1)^2}\,dt.$$

Note that

$$\frac{e^t}{(e^t - 1)^2} = \sum_{k=0}^\infty k\,e^{kt}.$$

:::
