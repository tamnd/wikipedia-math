# George A. Elliott

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George A. Elliott"}

**George Arthur Elliott** (born 1945) is a Canadian mathematician specializing in [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras), [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), and [non-commutative geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-commutative_geometry). He is a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics).[^o3] He is best known for his work on [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184), both for initiating their classification[^o4] and highlighting the importance of K-theory in this respect.[^o5]

:::

He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich), 1994.[^o6][^o7]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 1982 Elected as Fellow of [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o9]
- 1996 CRM/Fields Institute Prize[^o10]
- 1996-1998 Killam Research Fellow.[^o11]
- 1998 [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069).[^o12]
- 1999 [John L. Synge Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_L._Synge_Award).[^o13]
- 2012 Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]
- 2019 Fellow of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055)[^o15]
