# Johannes Boersma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Boersma"}

**Johannes Boersma** (5 December 1937 [Marrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrum) – 29 November 2004 [Eindhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven)) was a Dutch mathematician who specialized in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). His PhD advisor at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) was [Adriaan Isak van de Vooren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriaan_Isak_van_de_Vooren).

:::
