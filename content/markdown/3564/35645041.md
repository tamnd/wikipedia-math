# Walter Thom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Thom"}

**Walter Thom** (1770–1824) was a Scottish writer and journalist.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born at [Bervie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bervie), [Kincardineshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kincardineshire), and later moved to [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen), where he established himself as a bookseller. In 1813 he went to Dublin, as editor of the *[Dublin Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Journal)*, assisted by his son [Alexander Thom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Thom_%28almanac_editor%29). He died in there on 16 June 1824.

## Works {#Works}

Thom was the author of a *History of Aberdeen* (Aberdeen, 1811) and of a treatise on *Pedestrianism* (Aberdeen, 1813). He also contributed to *[Brewster's Encyclopædia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewster's_Encyclopædia)*, to [Sir John Sinclair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_John_Sinclair)'s *Statistical Account of Scotland*, and to [William Shaw Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shaw_Mason)'s *Statistical Account of Ireland*.
