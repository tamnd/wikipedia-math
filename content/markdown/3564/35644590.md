# Alexandre Savérien

Alexandre Savérien

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandre Savérien"}

*Image: Savérien - Histoire critique du calcul des infiniment-petits, contenant la métaphysique et la théorie de ce calcul, 1753 - 4685663.tif.* *Histoire critique du calcul des infiniment-petits*, 1753

**Alexandre Julien Savérien** (16 July 1720 – 3 May 1805) was a French mathematician who was also an expert in several other subject areas. He was born in [Arles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arles) and became accomplished in both mathematics and naval engineering at a young age.[^o2] in 1754, he published the *Universal Dictionary of Mathematics and Physics* and *[Histoire des philosophes modernes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Histoire_des_philosophes_modernes)* in 1773, where he named the nine greatest modern philosophers as [Jakob Abbadie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakob_Abbadie), [Erasmus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus), [Thomas Hobbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Hobbes), Nicole, [John Locke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Locke), [Baruch Spinoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baruch_Spinoza), [Nicolas Malebranche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Malebranche), Bayle, Clarke, and Collins.[^o3][^o4] He later became a member of the [Academy of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Lyon).[^o2]

:::
