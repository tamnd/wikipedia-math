# Journal of Statistical Software

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Statistical Software"}

The ***Journal of Statistical Software*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_%28publishing%29) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to [statistical software](wikipedia://en/3932847). The *Journal of Statistical Software* was founded in 1996 by [Jan de Leeuw](wikipedia://en/30963930) of the Department of Statistics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). Its current editors-in-chief are Bettina Grün, Torsten Hothorn, [Rebecca Killick](wikipedia://en/80588406), Nathalie Vialaneix, Achim Zeileis. It is published by the Foundation for Open Access Statistics. The journal charges no author fees or subscription fees.

:::

The journal publishes peer-reviewed articles about statistical software, together with the [source code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_code). It also publishes reviews of statistical software and books (by invitation only). Articles are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_licenses), while the [source codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_code) distributed with articles are licensed under the [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License).

Articles are often about free statistical software and coverage includes packages for the [R programming language](wikipedia://en/376707).

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The *Journal of Statistical Software* is indexed in the [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910) and the [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index). Its 2018 Impact Factor in [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports) is 11.655.[^o5] The journal was named a *Rising Star* by Science Watch[^o6] in 2011.
