# Michael Resnik

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Resnik"}

**Michael David Resnik** (; born March 20, 1938) is a leading contemporary American [philosopher of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Resnik obtained his B.A. in mathematics and philosophy at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1960, and his PhD in Philosophy at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1964. He wrote his thesis on [Frege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frege).[^o7][^o8] He was appointed Associate Professor at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 1967, Professor in 1975, and University Distinguished Professor in 1988.[^o9] He is Professor [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) and currently resides in rural [Chatham County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatham_County,_North_Carolina).
