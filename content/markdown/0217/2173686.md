# Logical holism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Logical holism"}

In [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), **logical holism** is the belief that the world operates in such a way that no part can be known without the whole being known first.

:::

**Theoretical holism** is a theory in [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), that a theory of science can only be understood in its entirety, introduced by [Pierre Duhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Duhem). Different total theories of science are understood by making them [commensurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commensurability_%28philosophy_of_science%29) allowing statements in one theory to be converted to sentences in another.[^o2] [Richard Rorty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Rorty) argued that when two theories are incompatible a process of [hermeneutics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermeneutics) is necessary.[^o2]

**Practical holism** is a concept in the work of [Martin Heidegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Heidegger) than posits it not possible to produce a complete understanding of one's own experience of reality because your mode of existence is embedded in cultural practices, the constraints of the task that you are doing.[^o2]

[Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163) concluded that "[Hegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegel)'s [dialectical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegelian_dialectic) logical holism should be dismissed in favour of the new logic of [propositional analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_analysis)."[^o3] and introduced a form of [logical atomism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_atomism).[^o4]

A unique kind of holism is found in [Chinese philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_philosophy), especially in the writings of the [Tiantai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiantai) school of Buddhism, beginning with the works of [Zhiyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhiyi). The Buddhist studies scholar and philosopher Brook Ziporyn terms it "Omnicentric holism".[^o5]
