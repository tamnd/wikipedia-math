# Small subgroup confinement attack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small subgroup confinement attack"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), a **subgroup confinement attack**, or **small subgroup confinement attack**, on a cryptographic method that operates in a large [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) is where an attacker attempts to compromise the method by forcing a key to be confined to an unexpectedly small [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of the desired [group](wikipedia://en/19447).

:::

Several methods have been found to be vulnerable to subgroup confinement attack, including some forms or applications of [Diffie–Hellman key exchange](wikipedia://en/7903) and [DH-EKE](wikipedia://en/1506894).
