# Excisive triad

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Excisive triad"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of mathematics, an **excisive triad** is a triple $(X; A, B)$ of [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) such that *A*, *B* are subspaces of *X* and *X* is the union of the interior of *A* and the interior of *B*.[^o2] Note *B* is not required to be a [subspace](wikipedia://en/910926) of *A*.

:::
