::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Pentakis snub dodecahedron"}

The **pentakis snub dodecahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with 140 triangular [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 210 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 72 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [chiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiral) [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844).[^o9]

:::

::: {#o11 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It comes from a topological construction from the [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137) with the [kis operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator) applied to the pentagonal faces. In this construction, all the faces are computed to be the same distance from the center. 80 of the triangles are equilateral, and 60 triangles from the pentagons are isosceles. It is a (2,1) [geodesic polyhedron](wikipedia://en/53024913), made of all triangles. The path between the valence-5 vertices is two edges in a row, and then a turn and one more edge.
