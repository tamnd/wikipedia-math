# Krasner's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krasner's lemma"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), more specifically in [*p*-adic analysis](wikipedia://en/291327), **Krasner's lemma** is a basic result relating the [topology](wikipedia://en/30450) of a [complete](wikipedia://en/21156575) [non-archimedean field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-archimedean_field) to its [algebraic extensions](wikipedia://en/2125).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *K* be a complete non-archimedean field and let $\overline{K}$ be a [separable closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_closure) of *K*. Given an element α in $\overline{K}$, denote its [Galois conjugates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_conjugate) by *α*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *α*<sub>*n*</sub>. Krasner's lemma states:[^o4][^o5]

if an element *β* of $\overline{K}$ is such that

$$\left|\alpha-\beta\right|<\left|\alpha-\alpha_i\right|\text{ for }i=2,\dots,n$$

then *K*(*α*) ⊆ *K*(*β*).

:::

::: {#o7 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Krasner's lemma can be used to show that [$\mathfrak{p}$-adic completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completion_%28ring_theory%29) and separable closure of [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358) commute.[^o8] In other words, given $\mathfrak{p}$ a [prime](wikipedia://en/28730822#Places) of a global field *L*, the separable closure of the $\mathfrak{p}$-adic completion of *L* equals the $\overline{\mathfrak{p}}$-adic completion of the separable closure of *L* (where $\overline{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a prime of $\overline{L}$ above $\mathfrak{p}$).

Another application is to proving that **C**<sub>p</sub>—the completion of the algebraic closure of **Q**<sub>p</sub>—is [algebraically closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_closed).[^o9][^o10]

:::

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalization"}

## Generalization {#Generalization}

Krasner's lemma has the following generalization.[^o13] Consider a monic polynomial

$$f^*=\prod_{k=1}^n(X-\alpha_k^*)$$

of degree *n* > 1 with coefficients in a [Henselian field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henselian_field) (*K*, *v*) and roots in the algebraic closure $\overline{K}$. Let *I* and *J* be two disjoint, non-empty sets with union {1,...,*n*}. Moreover, consider a polynomial

$$g=\prod_{i\in I}(X-\alpha_i)$$

with coefficients and roots in $\overline{K}$. Assume

$$\forall i\in I\forall j\in J: v(\alpha_i-\alpha_i^*)>v(\alpha_i^*-\alpha_j^*).$$

Then the coefficients of the polynomials

$$g^*:=\prod_{i\in I}(X-\alpha_i^*),\ h^*:=\prod_{j\in J}(X-\alpha_j^*)$$

are contained in the field extension of *K* generated by the coefficients of *g*. (The original Krasner's lemma corresponds to the situation where *g* has degree 1.)

:::
