# Audrey Terras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Audrey Terras"}

**Audrey Anne Terras** (born September 10, 1942) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works primarily in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). Her research has focused on [quantum chaos](wikipedia://en/313779) and on various types of [zeta functions](wikipedia://en/25809).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Audrey Terras was born September 10, 1942, in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)[^o4] She received a [BS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in mathematics from the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (UMD) in 1964, and [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) degrees from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1966 and 1970 respectively.[^o5] She was married to fellow UMD alumnus [Riho Terras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riho_Terras_%28mathematician%29).[^o6] She stated in a 2008 interview that she chose to study mathematics because "The U.S. government paid me! And not much! It was the time of Sputnik, so we needed to produce more mathematicians, and when I was deciding between Math and History, they weren’t paying me to do history, they were paying me to do math."[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Terras joined the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), as an assistant professor in 1972, and became a full professor there in 1983.[^o9] She retired in 2010,[^o9] and currently holds the title of Professor Emerita.[^o10]

As an undergraduate Terras was inspired by her teacher [Sigekatu Kuroda](wikipedia://en/57830988) to become a [number theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theorist); she was especially interested in the use of [analytic techniques](wikipedia://en/251513) to get algebraic results. Today her research interests are in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) on [symmetric spaces](wikipedia://en/1642877) and [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), [algebraic graph theory](wikipedia://en/1620216), [zeta functions](wikipedia://en/25809) of [graphs](wikipedia://en/325806), arithmetical [quantum chaos](wikipedia://en/313779), and the [Selberg trace formula](wikipedia://en/981694).[^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Terras was elected a [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_AAAS) in 1982.[^o4] She was the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698)- [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) [AWM/MAA Falconer Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM/MAA_Falconer_Lecturer) in 2000, speaking on "Finite Quantum Chaos,"[^o12] and the [AWM](wikipedia://en/9858698)'s [Noether Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_Lecturer) in 2008, speaking on "Fun with Zeta Functions of Graphs".[^o13] In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14] She is part of the 2019 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o15]
