# Grauert–Riemenschneider vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grauert–Riemenschneider vanishing theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Grauert–Riemenschneider vanishing theorem** is an extension of the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/2988093) on the vanishing of higher [cohomology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) of [coherent sheaves](wikipedia://en/603780) on a compact [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742), due to (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Grauert–Riemenschneider conjecture"}

## Grauert–Riemenschneider conjecture {#Grauert–Riemenschneider_conjecture}

The **Grauert–Riemenschneider conjecture** is a conjecture related to the Grauert–Riemenschneider vanishing theorem:

(Grauert Riemenschneider 1970a); Let M be an n-dimensional compact complex manifold. M is [Moishezon](wikipedia://en/3046549) if and only if there exists a smooth Hermitian line bundle L over M whose curvature form which is semi-positive everywhere and positive on an open [dense set](wikipedia://en/23523594).[^o6]

This conjecture was proved by (Siu 1985) using the Riemann–Roch type theorem ([Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/2892412)) and by (Demailly 1985) using Morse theory.

:::
