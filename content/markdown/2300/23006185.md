# Du Bois singularity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Du Bois singularity"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Du Bois singularities** are singularities of [complex varieties](wikipedia://en/39219687) studied by Du Bois.[^o2]

:::

Schwede[^o3] gave the following characterisation of Du Bois singularities. Suppose that $X$ is a [reduced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_scheme) [closed subscheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_subscheme) of a [smooth scheme](wikipedia://en/35033819) $Y$.

Take a log resolution $\pi: Z \to Y$ of $X$ in $Y$ that is an isomorphism outside $X$, and let $E$ be the reduced preimage of $X$ in $Z$. Then $X$ has Du Bois singularities if and only if the induced map $\mathcal{O}_X \to R\pi_{*}\mathcal{O}_E$ is a [quasi-isomorphism](wikipedia://en/3653039).
