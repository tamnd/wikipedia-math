# Bernd Fischer (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernd Fischer"}

**Bernd Fischer** (18 December 1936 – 13 August 2020) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He is best known for his contributions to the classification of finite [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600), and discovered several of the [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806). He introduced [3-transposition groups](wikipedia://en/29399459) and constructed the three [Fischer groups](wikipedia://en/327206), predicted the existence of the [baby monster](wikipedia://en/324799) and [monster groups](wikipedia://en/47422), and described and computed the [character table](wikipedia://en/911520) of the baby monster.

He did his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1963 at the [Johann Wolfgang Goethe University of Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Wolfgang_Goethe_University_of_Frankfurt_am_Main) under the direction of [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114).[^o3][^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Fischer went to Goethe University in Frankfurt to study mathematics under Baer in the early 60s, receiving his PhD in 1963. He later moved to the [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), where he became head of mathematical sciences.

In 1970, he classified the almost-simple groups generated by [3-transpositions](wikipedia://en/29399459). In the process, he discovered three new [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806), which were later called the [Fischer groups](wikipedia://en/327206). His proof that the classification was complete was known but not published in a single narrative until [Aschbacher](wikipedia://en/1652651) published an elementary introduction of 3-transposition groups in 1996.[^o6]

By loosening some of the conditions on his classification, in 1973 he predicted the existence of two larger sporadic simple groups: a {3,4}-transposition group, now known as the [baby monster](wikipedia://en/324799) group, and a {3,4,5,6}-transposition group now known as the [monster](wikipedia://en/47422) group or the [Fischer-Griess Monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fischer-Griess_Monster).[^o7] These would turn out to be the two largest sporadic groups that could exist. [Robert Griess](wikipedia://en/5034215) independently discovered the Monster group and published a description in 1976.[^o8]

Fischer went on to compute the character table for both monsters, in collaboration with Donald Livingstone and Michael Thorne.[^o7][^o9] Leon and Sims first produced a construction of the baby monster in 1977,[^o10] and Griess produced one for the monster in 1980.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Fischer was raised in [Bad Endbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Endbach). He later moved to [North Rhine-Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werther,_North_Rhine-Westphalia), where he died in August, 2020.[^o12]
