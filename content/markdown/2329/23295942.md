# Fjalar Finnäs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fjalar Finnäs"}

**Fjalar Finnäs** (1953–2023)[^o2] was a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) professor of [demographics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics) at the [Åbo Akademi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Åbo_Akademi_University).[^o3] He focused on the demographics of the [Swedish-speaking Finns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish-speaking_Finns).[^o4] His most recent research was in Ethno-Linguistic Exogamy and Divorce.[^o5]

:::

## Researches {#Researches}

- Jan 2014 – Sex composition of children, parental separation, and parity progression[^o7]
- Feb 2014 – Transitions within and from ethno-linguistically mixed and endogamous first unions in Finland
- Feb 2014 – Infant mortality and ethnicity in an indigenous European population[^o8]
- Sep 2015 – The Ethno-linguistic Community and Premature Death
- Aug 2017 – Divorce and parity progression following the death of a child[^o9]
- Mar 2018 – Ethno-Linguistic Exogamy and Divorce
