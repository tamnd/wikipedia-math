# Ralph Greatorex

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ralph Greatorex"}

**Ralph Greatorex** (c. 1625–1675)[^o2] was an English mathematician, mathematical instrument maker, and an apprentice of London clockmaker [Elias Allen](wikipedia://en/22072055).[^o2]

:::

Greatorex is mentioned in [John Aubrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Aubrey)'s [Brief Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brief_Lives) as a great friend of [William Oughtred](wikipedia://en/475249) the mathematician.[^o3] He is also briefly referred to in Aubrey's 'Natural History of Wilts', and in the 'Macclesfield Correspondence'.[^o3] [John Evelyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Evelyn) met Greatorex on 8 May 1656, and saw his 'excellent invention to quench fire.'[^o3] His name appears in '[Pepys's Diary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepys's_Diary).'[^o3] On 10 October 1660, when several engines were shown at work in [St. James's Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._James's_Park), 'above all the rest,' says [Samuel Pepys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Pepys), 'I liked that which Mr. Greatorex brought, which do carry up the water with a great deal of ease.'[^o3]

On 24 October Pepys bought of Greatorex a drawing-pen, 'and he did show me the manner of the lamp-glasses which carry the light a great way, good to read in bed by, and I intend to have one of them. And we looked at his wooden jack in his chimney, that goes with the smoake, which indeed is very pretty.'[^o3] On 9 June and 20 September 1662 and 23 March 1663 ('this day Greatorex brought me a very pretty weather-glasse for heat and cold') Pepys met the inventor; the last entry, 23 May 1663, refers to his varnish, 'which appears every whit as good upon a stick which he hath done, as the Indian.'[^o3]

As well as Pepys, Allen, Oughtred and Evelyn, others that Greatorex worked or corresponded with included [Samuel Hartlib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Hartlib), [Christopher Wren](wikipedia://en/52946), [Robert Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Boyle), [Edward Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Phillips) and [Jonas Moore](wikipedia://en/15685955).[^o2] He also attended meetings of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society), and did horticultural experiments at [Arundel House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arundel_House).[^o2]
