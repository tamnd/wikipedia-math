# Reiko Sakamoto (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reiko Sakamoto"}

**Reiko Sakamoto** (坂本 礼子, born 1939) is a Japanese mathematician affiliated with [Nara Women's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nara_Women's_University). Her teachers have included [Sigeru Mizohata](wikipedia://en/53225329) and Masaya Yamaguchi; her students have included Yoshihiro Shibata. She is known for her research on [mixed boundary conditions](wikipedia://en/2784886) for [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825), for which she won the 1974 Iyanaga Prize of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786), and for her book on hyperbolic [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779).

:::
