# Elena Yanovskaya

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elena Yanovskaya"}

**Elena Yanovskaya** (Russian: Еле́на Бори́совна Яно́вская, born 20 May 1938) is a Soviet and Russian mathematician and economist known for her contributions to [cooperative game theory](wikipedia://en/657958).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Elena Yanovskaya was born in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad) on May 20, 1938.

She studied at the School of Mathematics and Mechanics of the [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University) majoring in probability theory and statistics. After graduation in 1959, she started working as a junior researcher at the [Leningrad Department of Steklov Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Department_of_Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics_of_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), where she worked until 1965. Yanovskaya defended her doctoral thesis ([Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)) in 1964. From 1965 to 1975, Yanovskaya worked at the Leningrad branch of the [Central Economic Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Economic_Mathematical_Institute), where she started as a junior researcher and became the head of the game theory lab. From 1975 to 1990, she worked at the Institute of Socio-Economic Problems of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences). Yanovskaya defended her postdoctoral thesis ([Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences)) in 1980. From 1990 to 2015, she worked as the head of the laboratory of Game Theory and Decision Making of the [St. Petersburg Economics and Mathematics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Economics_and_Mathematics_Institute). Since 2009 she works as a professor at the St. Petersburg campus of the [Higher School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_School_of_Economics).
