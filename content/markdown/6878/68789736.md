# Lenglart's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lenglart's inequality"}

In the mathematical theory of probability, **Lenglart's inequality** was proved by Érik Lenglart in 1977.[^o2] Later slight modifications are also called Lenglart's inequality.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $X$ be a non-negative right-continuous $\mathcal{F}_t$-[adapted process](wikipedia://en/6583420) and let $G$ be a non-negative right-continuous non-decreasing [predictable process](wikipedia://en/33414015) such that $\mathbb{E}[X(\tau)\mid \mathcal{F}_0]\leq \mathbb{E}[G(\tau)\mid \mathcal{F}_0]< \infty$ for any bounded [stopping time](wikipedia://en/1202193) $\tau$. Then $\forall c,d>0, \mathbb{P}\left(\sup_{t\geq 0}X(t)>c\,\Big\vert\mathcal{F}_0\right)\leq \frac{1}{c}\mathbb{E} \left[\sup_{t\geq 0}G(t)\wedge d\,\Big\vert\mathcal{F}_0\right]+\mathbb{P}\left(\sup_{t\geq 0}G(t)\geq d\,\Big\vert\mathcal{F}_0\right).$; $\forall p\in(0,1), \mathbb{E}\left[\left(\sup_{t\geq 0}X(t)\right)^p\Big\vert \mathcal{F}_0 \right]\leq c_p\mathbb{E}\left[\left(\sup_{t\geq 0}G(t)\right)^p\Big\vert \mathcal{F}_0\right], \text{ where } c_p:=\frac{p^{-p}}{1-p}.$

:::
