# Michael McQuillan (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael McQuillan"}

**Michael Liam McQuillan** is a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) studying [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). As of 2019 he is professor at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Michael McQuillan received the doctorate in 1992 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679) ("Division points on semi-Abelian varieties").[^o4]

In 1995, McQuillan proved the [Mordell–Lang conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell–Lang_conjecture).[^o6] In 1996, MacQuillan gave a new proof of a conjecture of [André Bloch](wikipedia://en/11772883) (1926) about holomorphic curves in closed subvarieties of Abelian varieties,[^o7] proved a conjecture of [Shoshichi Kobayashi](wikipedia://en/20695112) (about the Kobayashi-hyperbolicity of generic hypersurfaces of high degree in projective *n*-dimensional space) in the three-dimensional case[^o8] and achieved partial results on a conjecture of [Mark Green](wikipedia://en/56936978) and [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414) (which states that a holomorphic curve on an algebraic surface of general type with $c_{1}^2 > c_2$ cannot be Zariski-dense).[^o9]

From 1996 to 2001 he was a post-doctoral Research Fellow at [All Souls College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Souls_College) of the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)[^o10][^o11] and in 2009 was Professor at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) as well as Advanced Research Fellow of the British [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council). As of 2019 he is professor at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) and an editor of the *European Journal of Mathematics*.[^o12]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2000 McQuillan received the [EMS Prize](wikipedia://en/969470#2000_prizes), which was announced from the European Congress of Mathematics in July 2000, for his work:Michael McQuillan has created the method of dynamic diophantine approximation, which has led to a series of remarkable results in complex geometry of algebraic varieties. Among these results one can mention a new proof of Bloch’s conjecture on holomorphic curves in closed subvarieties of abelian varieties, the proof of the conjecture of Green and Griffiths that a holomorphic curve in a surface of general type cannot be Zariski-dense, and the hyperbolicity of generic hypersurfaces of high degree in projective 3-space (the Kobayashi conjecture).[^o14]

In 2001 he was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o15] In 2002 he was invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) (*Integrating $\partial \bar{\partial}$*).[^o16] In 2001 he received the [Whittaker Prize](wikipedia://en/4579429).[^o17]
