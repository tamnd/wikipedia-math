# Plate appearances per strikeout

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Plate appearances per strikeout"}

In [baseball statistics](wikipedia://en/3797), **plate appearances per strikeout** (**PA/SO**) represents the ratio of a [batter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batting_%28baseball%29)'s [plate appearances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plate_appearance) to their [strikeouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strikeout).

:::

This statistic enables a defensive team to identify hitters in the opposing team's lineup who are more prone to striking out. Such players tend to be more aggressive than the average hitter, when batting, allowing the [pitcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitcher) to approach them with more pitching options, often throwing more [balls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_%28ball%29) out of the [strike zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_zone) in hopes that the batter will swing and miss.

The PA/SO statistic can be calculated by dividing a player's total plate appearances by their total strikeouts. For example, [Reggie Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggie_Jackson) accumulated 2,597 strikeouts and 11,418 plate appearances throughout his 21-year baseball career, recording a PA/SO of 4.39. This means that, on average, Jackson struck out once every 4.39 plate appearances.
