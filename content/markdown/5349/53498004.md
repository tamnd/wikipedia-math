# Li Cai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Li Cai"}

**Li Cai** (; born c. 1980) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [quantitative psychologist](wikipedia://en/3067624). He is a professor of Advanced Quantitative Methodology at the [UCLA Graduate School of Education and Information Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_Graduate_School_of_Education_and_Information_Studies) with a joint appointment in the quantitative area of the [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) Department of Psychology. He is also Director of the [National Center for Research on Evaluation, Standards, and Student Testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Research_on_Evaluation,_Standards,_and_Student_Testing), Managing Partner at [Vector Psychometric Group](https://www.vpgcentral.com/about/people/li-cai/).

:::

He invented the [Metropolis–Hastings](wikipedia://en/56107) [Robbins–Monro](wikipedia://en/8979437#Robbins.E2.80.93Monro_algorithm) algorithm for [inference](wikipedia://en/27577) in [high-dimensional](wikipedia://en/19330658) [latent variable models](wikipedia://en/4237572) that had been [intractable](wikipedia://en/7543#Intractability) with [existing solutions](wikipedia://en/470752). The algorithm was recognized as a [mathematically rigorous](wikipedia://en/655974#Mathematics) breakthrough in the "[curse of dimensionality](wikipedia://en/787776)" and garnered numerous [top-tier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) publications and national awards.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

## Career {#Career}

A native of [Nanjing, China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing) and alumnus of the [Nanjing Foreign Language School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Foreign_Language_School), Cai earned a bachelor's degree with distinction from [Nanjing University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_University) in 2001. He completed his undergraduate studies in three years and, in that time, also won multiple international competitions for [public speaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_speaking).[^o9] After earning a master's degree in [journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalism) at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 2003, he moved to the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), earning a second master's in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) in 2006 and PhD in [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) in 2008.[^o10]

He joined the psychology and education faculty at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2008 and was awarded tenure in 2011. He was appointed a full professor in 2014.

## Research {#Research}

Cai publishes methodology in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) (including [statistical computing](wikipedia://en/15832717)) and [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982), and conducts applied research in [educational measurement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_measurement), [health outcomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outcomes_research), and [developmental psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_psychology). He serves on the editorial boards of various journals in quantitative methods in the social sciences.

## Software {#Software}

Cai develops flexMIRT, a statistical program for [multilevel modeling](wikipedia://en/4521038) and multidimensional [item response theory](wikipedia://en/420159).[^o13] In addition, he is the coauthor (with [David Thissen](wikipedia://en/53411348), his doctoral advisor) of IRTPRO, another program for parametric estimation and test scoring.[^o14]
