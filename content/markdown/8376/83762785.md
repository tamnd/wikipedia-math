# The Geometry of Four-Manifolds

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The Geometry of Four-Manifolds"}

***The Geometry of Four-Manifolds*** is a mathematical [textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) about [Yang–Mills theory](wikipedia://en/672202), or more generally [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155), and its application to the study of the geometry and topology of [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) [4-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1362795) as described by [Donaldson theory](wikipedia://en/7684340). It is considered a standard work for this topic.[^o3] It was written by [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305), who received the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1986 for his work in the field,[^o4] and [Peter Kronheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Kronheimer). It was published on 13 September 1990 with a second revised edition in 1997.

:::

## Content {#Content}

*The Geometry of Four-Manifolds* first describes the foundations of the theories of [4-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1362795) and [connections](wikipedia://en/470632) on [principal bundles](wikipedia://en/310950) as well as [connections](wikipedia://en/391816) on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524).[^o6] Afterwards it explains [Yang–Mills theory](wikipedia://en/672202), which includes [Uhlenbeck's singularity theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uhlenbeck's_singularity_theorem), the [ADHM construction](wikipedia://en/5165589) and the central [Yang–Mills moduli space](wikipedia://en/80813159), on which [Donaldson invariants](wikipedia://en/11256383) are based on.[^o7] A special focus lies on the combination with [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), which includes [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538) and (stable) [holomorphic vector bundles](wikipedia://en/4794668).[^o8]

## Background {#Background}

Simon Donaldson and Peter Kronheimer both obtained their [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPhil) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) under the supervision of [Michael Francis Atiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Francis_Atiyah), in the years 1983 and 1986 respectively. Simon Donaldson was invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) and gave plenar talks about *Gauge theory and topology* as well as *Geometry of Four-Manifolds*.[^o10]

## Reviews {#Reviews}

[Elmer Rees](wikipedia://en/157248) wrote, the textbook is a "polished account of Donaldson's pioneering work; also included are several of the consequent developments in which he has played the leading role". Furthermore, "For someone with a good working knowledge of a range of graduate level courses, this book is self-contained."[^o3]
