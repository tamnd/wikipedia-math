# Alexandr Kostochka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandr Kostochka"}

Vasilyevich

**Alexandr Vasilyevich Kostochka** is a mathematician who works in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is a professor emeritus of mathematics at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) and has been affiliated with the [Sobolev Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobolev_Institute_of_Mathematics) in [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).[^o14][^o15] His research concerns [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743), [graph minors](wikipedia://en/353042), [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568), and [hypergraphs](wikipedia://en/232535). He is known for the Kostochka–Thomason bound on clique minors and for the Borodin–Kostochka conjecture on [chromatic numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number).[^o15][^o24][^o29]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kostochka was raised in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). He trained in the graph-theory school of Alexander Zykov at [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk).[^o20] He defended his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) dissertation on upper bounds for chromatic characteristics of graphs at [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1977 and received his degree in 1978.[^o18][^o16][^o17]

In 1990, he received the higher degree of [Doctor of Physico-Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) for work on extremal combinatorial and probabilistic problems.[^o18] He conducted much of his early career at the Sobolev Institute of Mathematics, part of the [Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberian_Branch_of_the_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), and maintained an affiliation after moving to the United States.[^o15][^o18][^o21] He joined the mathematics faculty of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where he later became professor emeritus, and held an appointment at the university's Center for Advanced Study.[^o14][^o15] According to the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](wikipedia://en/1152126), he has supervised 17 doctoral students.[^o16]

## Research {#Research}

Kostochka has published extensively on problems in graph theory, including graph and [list coloring](wikipedia://en/690669), [equitable coloring](wikipedia://en/21796230), graph and hypergraph packing, [Ramsey-type](wikipedia://en/157175) questions, and [Turán-type](wikipedia://en/360601) extremal problems.[^o15][^o22] He has frequently collaborated with mathematicians such as Oleg Borodin, [Douglas B. West](wikipedia://en/22813653), [Zoltán Füredi](wikipedia://en/22730000), H. A. Kierstead, and [József Balogh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/József_Balogh).[^o22][^o23]

### Graph minors {#Graph_minors}

In the 1980s Kostochka and Andrew Thomason independently determined the order of the largest clique minor forced by the average degree of a graph. They found that every graph of average degree $d$ contains a complete minor of order $\Theta(d/\log d)$, a bound that is tight up to a constant factor.[^o24][^o25] Equivalently, a graph with no $K_{t}$ minor is $O(t\log t)$-[degenerate](wikipedia://en/27545816).[^o26] The result, now commonly called the , gave for several decades the best general lower bound on the size of a clique minor in terms of chromatic number and thus the strongest known partial progress on [Hadwiger's conjecture](wikipedia://en/1657860) for arbitrary graphs.[^o26][^o24] With T. Böhme and Thomason, he later established related bounds on minors in graphs of high chromatic number.[^o27]

### Graph coloring {#Graph_coloring}

In a 1977 paper with Oleg Borodin, Kostochka introduced what became known as the Borodin–Kostochka conjecture: every graph with [maximum degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_degree) $\Delta \ge 9$ whose [clique number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clique_number) satisfies $\omega \le \Delta - 1$ has chromatic number at most $\Delta - 1$.[^o28][^o29] The conjecture, which strengthens [Brooks' theorem](wikipedia://en/21042117), remains open in general; [Bruce Reed](wikipedia://en/37253906) proved it for sufficiently large maximum degree in 1999, and it has been verified for various restricted classes of graphs.[^o29][^o30] Kostochka has also worked on the total chromatic number of multigraphs and on defective and improper colorings of sparse graphs.[^o22]

## Editorial and other activities {#Editorial_and_other_activities}

Kostochka serves on the editorial board of the journal *Diskretnyi Analiz i Issledovanie Operatsii* (published in English translation as the *Journal of Applied and Industrial Mathematics*).[^o19] He edited a volume of *Topics in Graph Theory* dedicated to the ninetieth birthday of the graph theorist Alexander Zykov (1922–2013).[^o14][^o20]
