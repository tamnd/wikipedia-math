# List of Beta Kappa Chi chapters

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Beta Kappa Chi chapters"}

[Beta Kappa Chi](wikipedia://en/44516824) is an American collegiate [honor society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_society) that recognizes academic achievements in the fields of [natural science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was established in 1923 at [Lincoln University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_%28Pennsylvania%29), a [historically Black university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historically_black_university) near [Oxford, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_Pennsylvania).[^o2]

:::

Beta Kappa Chi chapters were originally assigned [Greek letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_letter) names in alphabetical order.[^o3] In 1950, the society abolished Greek letter names and renamed chapters by their host institutions.[^o3]

In the following list of Beta Kappa Chi chapters, inactive institutions are indicated in *italics*.[^o2][^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]
