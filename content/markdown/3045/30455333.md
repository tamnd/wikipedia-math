# Josip Pečarić

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Josip Pečarić"}

**Josip Pečarić** (born 2 September 1948) is a Croatian mathematician and writer. He is a professor of mathematics in the Faculty of Textile Technology at the [University of Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zagreb),[^o3] Croatia, and is a full member of the [Croatian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts).[^o26] He has written and co-authored over 1,200 mathematical publications. He has also published a number of works on history and politics that have been described as comprising [historical negationism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_negationism) or [Holocaust denial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_denial).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Pečarić was born in [Kotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotor), [Montenegro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montenegro) (at the time part of [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia)) on 2 September 1948,[^o26][^o5] where he remained to attend elementary and high school.[^o9] He studied at the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade)'s [Faculty of Electrical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade_Faculty_of_Electrical_Engineering) for his undergraduate and master's degrees, which he completed respectively in 1972 and 1975.[^o5] The supervisor of his master's degree, mathematics professor, Dobrilo Tošić, inspired him to switch fields to mathematics.[^o9]

Pečarić remained at the University of Belgrade, working on his PhD in mathematics from 1975 to 1982.[^o6] He received it under the supervision of Petar Vasić. His dissertation was on *Jensen's and related inequalities*. He began working at the University of Zagreb in 1987.[^o5]

## Mathematics career {#Mathematics_career}

Pečarić is known for his work in the theory of [inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489).[^o9] He has founded several journals, all published by Element in Zagreb: he is currently Editor-in-Chief at *Mathematical Inequalities and Applications*[^o10] and at the *Journal of Mathematical Inequalities*,[^o11] and also founded *Operators and Matrices*.[^o12]

Pečarić has written and co-authored over 1,200 articles on mathematics in journals, books, and conference proceedings.[^o13] He has also coauthored over 20 mathematical books, including 6 that are written in English.

## Political views and historical negationism {#Political_views_and_historical_negationism}

In addition to his mathematical work, Pečarić has published more than 20 books and 40 articles on history and politics.[^o9] This work is from a far-right point of view, and has been criticized as comprising [historical negationism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_negationism) or [Holocaust denial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_denial).[^o15]

For example, Pečarić has advocated for the return of the World War II-era fascist salute *[Za dom spremni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Za_dom_spremni)*.[^o16][^o17] This salute has been called the Croatian equivalent of the German *[Sieg Heil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieg_Heil)*.[^o18] His 2017 book *General Praljak* reinvents the [war criminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_criminal) [Slobodan Praljak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slobodan_Praljak) as a humanist and war hero.[^o19] His books *Serbian Myths about Jasenovac* and *The Jasenovac Lie Revealed*, the latter coauthored with [Stjepan Razum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stjepan_Razum), argued that the [Jasenovac concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasenovac_concentration_camp) was a labor camp with much lower casualties than the commonly accepted figure, and that the bulk of its victims were Croats killed by Yugoslav communist authorities after the war.[^o20][^o21][^o15] This last prompted the [Simon Wiesenthal Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Wiesenthal_Centre) to advocate the Croatian government to ban publications denying the war crimes of the [Ustaša](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ustaša).[^o15][^o22]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Pečarić has received a number of honors and awards. He was awarded the Croatian National Science Award in 1996,[^o24] and received the [Order of Danica Hrvatska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Danica_Hrvatska) in 1999.[^o25] Pečarić was appointed to [full membership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_Croatian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) of the [Croatian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) in 2000.[^o26] In 2008, a conference was held in honor of his 60th birthday and an issue (volume 2 no. 2) of the [Banach Journal of Mathematical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_Journal_of_Mathematical_Analysis) was dedicated to him.[^o27] Another conference was held in 2014 in Pečarić's honor on the occasion of the publication of his 1000th mathematical paper.[^o28]
