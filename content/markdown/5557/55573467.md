# Infinite difference method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite difference method"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **infinite difference methods** are [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods) for solving [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations) by approximating them with [difference equations](wikipedia://en/146806#Relationship_to_difference_equations_narrowly_defined), in which [infinite differences approximate](wikipedia://en/39147#Generalizations) the [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921). In calculus there are two sections, one is differentiation and the other is integration. Integration is the reverse process of differentiation. [^o2]

:::
