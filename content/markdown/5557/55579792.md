# Lynn Kuo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lynn Kuo"}

**Lynn Kuo** (born 1949)[^o2] is a professor emerita of statistics at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) known for her work on statistical decision theory,[^o3][^o4] software reliability,[^o5] and [Bayesian inference in phylogeny](wikipedia://en/7022979). With Ming-Hui Chen and Paul O. Lewis, she is the author of *Bayesian Phylogenetics: Methods, Algorithms, and Applications* (CRC Press, 2014).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kuo did both her undergraduate and graduate studies at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), completing her PhD in 1980. Her dissertation, supervised by [Thomas S. Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_S._Ferguson), was *Computations and Applications of Mixtures of Dirichlet Processes*. She has worked at the [Jet Propulsion Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Propulsion_Lab), the [United States Department of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Agriculture), and the [Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/41998171) before joining the Connecticut faculty.

She was the treasurer of the New England Statistical Society, and has also worked as treasurer for the International Chinese Statistical Association.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Kuo is a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). In 2013, she won the Outstanding Service Award of the International Chinese Statistical Association.
