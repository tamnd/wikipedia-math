# Nancy Temkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nancy Temkin"}

**Nancy R. Temkin** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who works on the [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) of [traumatic brain injury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traumatic_brain_injury). She is a professor of neurological surgery and biostatistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), and serves on the [National Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academies_of_Sciences,_Engineering,_and_Medicine) Committee on Sports-Related Concussions in Youth.

:::

Temkin earned bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics and statistics, respectively, from the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) in 1970 and 1971. She completed her PhD in 1976 from the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo).

She is a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) and a recipient of the Distinguished Service Award of the [American Epilepsy Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Epilepsy_Society).
