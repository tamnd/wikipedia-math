# Diagrammatic Monte Carlo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diagrammatic Monte Carlo"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **diagrammatic Monte Carlo method** is based on stochastic summation of [Feynman diagrams](wikipedia://en/11617) with controllable error bars.[^o2][^o3] It was developed by [Boris Svistunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Svistunov) and [Nikolay Prokof'ev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Prokof'ev). It was proposed as a generic approach to overcome the [numerical sign problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_sign_problem) that precludes simulations of many-body fermionic problems.[^o4] Diagrammatic Monte Carlo works in the thermodynamic limit, and its computational complexity does not scale exponentially with system or cluster volume.[^o5]

:::
