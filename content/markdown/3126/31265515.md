# Meyerhoff manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Meyerhoff manifold"}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), the **Meyerhoff manifold** is the [arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/15239331) obtained by $(5,1)$ [surgery](wikipedia://en/1240699) on the [figure-8](wikipedia://en/59565) [knot complement](wikipedia://en/1350120). It was introduced by (txt) as a possible candidate for the hyperbolic 3-manifold of smallest volume, but the [Weeks manifold](wikipedia://en/4201216) turned out to have slightly smaller volume. It has the second smallest volume

:::

$$V_m = 12\cdot(283)^{3/2}\zeta_k(2)(2\pi)^{-6} = 0.981368\dots$$

of orientable arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifolds, where $\zeta_k$ is the [zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243) of the quartic field of discriminant $-283$. Alternatively,

$$V_m = \Im(\rm{Li}_2(\theta)+\ln|\theta|\ln(1-\theta)) = 0.981368\dots$$

where $\rm{Li}_n$ is the [polylogarithm](wikipedia://en/482471) and $|x|$ is the [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991) of the complex root $\theta$ (with positive imaginary part) of the [quartic](wikipedia://en/308152) $\theta^4+\theta-1=0$.

(txt) showed that this manifold is [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/15239331).
