# Hans Heinrich Bürmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Heinrich Bürmann"}

**Hans Heinrich Bürmann** (died 21 June 1817, in [Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannheim)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and teacher. He ran an "academy of commerce" in Mannheim since 1795 where he taught mathematics.[^o5] He also served as a censor in Mannheim.[^o5] He was nominated Headmaster of the Commerce Academy of the [Grand Duchy of Baden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Baden) in 1811. He did scientific research in the area of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and he contributed to the development of the symbolic language of mathematics. He discovered the generalized form of the [Lagrange inversion theorem](wikipedia://en/94158). He corresponded and published with [Joseph Louis Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Louis_Lagrange) and [Carl Hindenburg](wikipedia://en/545047).

:::

## Iterate function composition notation {#Iterate_function_composition_notation}

The [compositional](wikipedia://en/195947) notation $f^{ n}$ for the $n$-th [iterate](wikipedia://en/1448702) $f^{n} := f\circ f^{n-1} = \overbrace {f\circ f\circ\dotsb\circ f}^{n \text{ times}}$ of function $f$ was originally introduced by Bürmann[^o8][^o6] and later independently suggested by [John Frederick William Herschel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Frederick_William_Herschel) in 1813.[^o7][^o8][^o6]
