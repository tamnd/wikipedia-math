# Gradimir Milovanović

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gradimir Milovanović"}

**Gradimir V. Milovanović** (born 2 January 1948) is a [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). He has published over 450 papers and authored five monographs and more than twenty books in his area.[^o3] He is a full member of the [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) and of other Serbian and international scientific societies.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Born in Zorunovac, in the [Knjaževac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knjaževac) municipality of mideastern [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia), he studied at the [University of Niš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Niš), obtaining a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) (1971), an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (1974), and a [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc.) (1976). His thesis was titled *On Some Functional Inequalities* advised by [Dragoslav Mitrinović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragoslav_Mitrinović).

## Career {#Career}

He served as a member of the faculty of electronic engineering and the department of mathematics at the [University of Niš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Niš), and was promoted to [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in 1986 before serving as the acting Dean of the Faculty of Electronic Engineering from 2002 to 2004. He served as [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the University of Niš from 2004 to 2006, as well as dean of the Faculty of Computer Sciences at the [Megatrend University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megatrend_University) from 2008 to 2011, until he joined the Mathematical Institute[^o7] of the [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) in [Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade) (2011).

He has been a member of the board of the Mathematical Society of Serbia (2003 – 2006), president of the Scientific Council of the Mathematical Institute at [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) in [Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade) (1997 – 2010), [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_president) of the Scientific Society of Serbia since 2002, president of the National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (2006 – 2010), and president of the Scientific Committee for Mathematics, Computer Sciences and Mechanics.
