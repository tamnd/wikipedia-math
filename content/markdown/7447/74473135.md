# Takao Hayashi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takao Hayashi"}

**Takao Hayashi** (born 1949) is a Japanese mathematics educator, historian of mathematics specializing in [Indian mathematics](wikipedia://en/1848052). Hayashi was born in [Niigata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niigata_Prefecture), Japan. He obtained Bachelor of Science degree form [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University), Sendai, Japan in 1974, Master of Arts degree from Tohuku University, Sendai, Japan in 1976 and a postgraduate degree from [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), Japan in 1979. He secured the Doctor of Philosophy degree from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), USA in 1985 under the guidance of [David Pingree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pingree). He was a researcher at [Mehta Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehta_Research_Institute) for Mathematics and Mathematical Physics, [Allahabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allahabad), India during 1982–1983, a lecturer at Kyoto Women's College during 1985–1987. He joined [Doshisha University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doshisha_University), Kyoto as a lecturer in history of science in 1986 and was promoted as professor in 1995. He has also worked in various universities in Japan in different capacities.[^o5]

:::

## Publications {#Publications}

Hayashi has a large number of research publications relation to the history of Indian mathematics. He has also contributed chapters to several encyclopedic publications.[^o3] The books he has published include:[^o5]

- *The [Bakhshali Manuscript](wikipedia://en/4305817): An Ancient Indian Mathematical Treatise*, Egbert Forsten Publishing, 1995
- (jointly with S. R. Sarma, Takanori Kusuba and Michio Yano), *[Gaṇitasārakaumudī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganita_Kaumudi): The Moonlight of the Essence of Mathematics by [Thakkura Pherū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thakkar_Pheru)*, Manohar Publishers and Distributors, 2009
- *[Kuṭṭā̄kāraśiromaṇi](wikipedia://en/49674539) of Devarāja: Sanskrit Text with English Translation*, [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy), 2012
- *Gaṇitamañjarī of [Gaṇeśa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaṇeśa_Daivajna)*, Indian National Science Academy, 2013
- (jointly with [Clemency Montelle](wikipedia://en/65349679), K. Ramasubramanian) *Bhāskara-prabhā*, Springer Singapore, 2018

## Awards/Prizes {#Awards/Prizes}

The awards and prizes conferred on Hayashi include:[^o5]

- The [Salomon Reinach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salomon_Reinach) Foundation Prize, [Institut de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_France) (2001)
- Kuwabara Prize, the History of Mathematics Society of Japan (2005)
- Publication Prize, [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) (2005)
