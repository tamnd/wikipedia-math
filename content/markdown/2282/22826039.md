# Tibor Szele

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tibor Szele"}

*Image: Szele\_Tibor.jpg.* Tibor Szele

**Tibor Szele** (21 June 1918 – 5 April 1955) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384).

:::

Szele was born in [Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debrecen). After graduating at the [Debrecen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debrecen_University), he became a researcher at the [Szeged University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged_University) in 1946, then he went back at the Debrecen University in 1948 where he became full professor in 1952. He worked especially in the theory of [Abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974) and [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424). He generalized [Hajós's theorem](wikipedia://en/12200213). He founded the Hungarian school of algebra. Tibor Szele received the [Kossuth Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kossuth_Prize) in 1952. He died in [Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged).
