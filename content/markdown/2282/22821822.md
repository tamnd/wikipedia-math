# HP 20b

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="HP 20b"}

The **HP 20b Business Consultant** (F2219A, codenamed "Little Euro"[^o15]) is a financial calculator introduced in 2008 by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard). Its function is similar to [HP&nbsp;10bII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP&nbsp;10bII) and includes scientific and statistical functions.

:::

## Hardware specification {#Hardware_specification}

The HP 20b uses a 2 lines display with the first line an 8 character scrolling display and 11 indicators and the second line for 12+3 digit display. It also uses 2 CR2032 batteries, an [Atmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmel) [AT91SAM7L128](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmel_AT91SAM7L128) 30 MHz processor. The processor's serial interface is accessible from under the battery cover to allow the [firmware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firmware) to be updated using a special cable from HP.

## Functions {#Functions}

The HP 20b contains functions similar to the HP 10bII, with financial functions including: [TVM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_value_of_money), [IRR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_rate_of_return), [NPV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_present_value), NUS ("Net Uniform Series"[^o16]), amortization, depreciation, bonds, yield and accrued interest, interest conversion, list-based cashflow analysis, cashflows, break-even analysis.

Math/Statistics functions include: list-base, 1 and 2 variable statistics, mean, standard deviation, population deviation, standard error, forecasting, correlations and covariance, +, -, ×, ÷, %, 1/x, +/-, scientific notation, n!, combinations, permutations, rounding, random numbers, LOG, LN, 10×, PL, square root, trigonometry, probability.[^o17]

For input modes, it supports [RPN](wikipedia://en/1964121#RPN), [Chain](wikipedia://en/1964121#Immediate_execution) and [Algebraic](wikipedia://en/1964121#Infix_notation) input.

It also included several added features include: date calculation, display format and language preferences, memory functions, editing, scroll keys.

## Criticism {#Criticism}

Many users claim that they dislike the model because it lacks the feedback when pressing the keys, and the menus make things complicated.

## Examination approval {#Examination_approval}

The HP 20b is approved by the Hong Kong Examinations and Assessment Authority and can be used in Hong Kong Certificate of Education Examination of 2009 or later.[^o18]

The HP 20b is approved by the Global Association of Risk Professionals (GARP) and can be used for the Financial Risk Manager (FRM) certification exam.[^o19]

## Repurposing {#Repurposing}

Along with the [HP&nbsp;30b](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP&nbsp;30b), the HP 20b calculator is particularly conducive to repurposing, since both the hardware and software are made public by HP. Several projects have been started around reusing this calculator for other purposes. The most advanced is the [WP&nbsp;34S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WP&nbsp;34S), a programmable scientific calculator that combines the functionality of the [HP-42S](wikipedia://en/730770) and [HP-16C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP-16C) and includes many other novel features and functions.[^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23][^o24] A firmware branch with a [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) lock mode is called [WP&nbsp;34C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WP&nbsp;34C).[^o25] The [WP&nbsp;31S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WP&nbsp;31S) is a stripped-down non-programmable version of the WP 34S, designed to be easier to use.
