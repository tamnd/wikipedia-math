# Dianoia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dianoia"}

In [Platonism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonism), ***dianoia*** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek): διάνοια) is the human [cognitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognition) faculty associated with the BC portion of the [analogy of the divided line](wikipedia://en/31068) and related to *[discursive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discursive_reasoning)* thinking about mathematical and technical subjects.[^n1] It stands in contrast to the immediate, cognitive process of *[intuitive](wikipedia://en/154170)* apprehension or *[noesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noesis_%28Platonism%29)*.[^n2]

:::

In Aristotle, *dianoia* is subdivided into theoretical knowledge *([technē](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techne))* and practical knowledge *([phronēsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phronesis))*.[^n3]
