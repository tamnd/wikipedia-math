# Ke Zhao

[Ke (Ko)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ke_%28surname%29)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ke Zhao"}

**Ke Zhao** or **Chao Ko** (, April 12, 1910 – November 8, 2002) was a Chinese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Wenling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenling), [Taizhou, Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taizhou,_Zhejiang).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ke graduated from [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) in 1933 and obtained his doctorate from the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester) under [Louis Mordell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Mordell) in 1937. His main fields of study were [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). Some of his major contributions included his work on [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478), the [Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem](wikipedia://en/296188) and his theorem on [Catalan's conjecture](wikipedia://en/62382).[^o6] In 1955, he was one of the founding members of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4] He was later a professor at [Sichuan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_University)[^o5] and became the president of the university and of the [Chinese Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Mathematical_Society).[^o6]
