# Lévy's continuity theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lévy's continuity theorem"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Lévy’s continuity theorem**, or **Lévy's convergence theorem**,[^o2] named after the French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Paul Lévy](wikipedia://en/556462), connects [convergence in distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergence_in_distribution) of the sequence of random variables with [pointwise convergence](wikipedia://en/340198) of their [characteristic functions](wikipedia://en/3229401). This theorem is the basis for one approach to prove the [central limit theorem](wikipedia://en/39406) and is one of the major theorems concerning characteristic functions.

:::
