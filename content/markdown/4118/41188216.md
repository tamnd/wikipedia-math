# Raymond O. Wells Jr.

*Image: Raymond O. Wells Jr.jpg.* R. O. Wells Jr.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raymond O. Wells Jr."}

**Raymond O'Neil Wells Jr.** (born 1940), "Ronny", is an American mathematician, working in [complex analysis in several variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analysis_in_several_variables)[^o2] as well as [wavelets](wikipedia://en/50903).

:::

Wells received his BA from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 1962 and his Ph.D. in 1965 from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) under the supervision of [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972) (*On the local holomorphic hull of a real submanifold in several complex variables*).[^o3] He was Professor of Mathematics at Rice University, where he served as chairman of the Department of Mathematics. After becoming Professor Emeritus from Rice, he co-founded the [Jacobs University Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobs_University_Bremen). He was Professor of Mathematics and Vice-President of External Affairs.

He has written books on [twistors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twistor_%28mathematics%29), [wavelets](wikipedia://en/50903), and analysis on complex manifolds.

In 1970–71 and 1979–80, he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey.

From 1974 to 1975 he was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) and received the [Humboldt Senior Scientist Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize). He was editor of the [Transactions of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/1388937). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
