# M. Lothaire

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. Lothaire"}

**M. Lothaire** is the pseudonym of a group of mathematicians, many of whom were students of [Marcel-Paul Schützenberger](wikipedia://en/2817337). The name is used as the author of several of their joint books about [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490). The group is named for [Lothair I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothair_I).[^o2]

:::

## Members {#Members}
