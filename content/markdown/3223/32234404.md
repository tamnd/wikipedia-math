# Shuffle algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shuffle algebra"}

In mathematics, a **shuffle algebra** is a [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) with a basis corresponding to words on some set, whose product is given by the **shuffle product** *X* ⧢ *Y* of two words *X*, *Y*: the sum of all ways of interlacing them. The interlacing is given by the [riffle shuffle permutation](wikipedia://en/39949298).

:::

The shuffle algebra on a finite set is the graded dual of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of the [free Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/3174646) on the set.

Over the rational numbers, the shuffle algebra is isomorphic to the [polynomial algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_algebra) in the [Lyndon words](wikipedia://en/3919355).

The shuffle product occurs in generic settings in [non-commutative algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-commutative_algebra); this is because it is able to preserve the relative order of factors being multiplied together - the [riffle shuffle permutation](wikipedia://en/39949298). This can be held in contrast to the [divided power structure](wikipedia://en/8006138), which becomes appropriate when factors are commutative.

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Shuffle product"}

## Shuffle product {#Shuffle_product}

The shuffle product of words of lengths *m* and *n* is a sum over the $\tfrac{(m+n)!}{m!n!}$ ways of interleaving the two words, as shown in the following examples:

*ab* ⧢ *xy* = *abxy* + *axby* + *xaby* + *axyb* + *xayb* + *xyab*

*aaa* ⧢ *aa* = 10*aaaaa*

It may be defined inductively by[^o4]

*u* ⧢ ε = ε ⧢ *u* = *u*

*ua* ⧢ *vb* = (*u* ⧢ *vb*)*a* + (*ua* ⧢ *v*)*b*

where ε is the [empty word](wikipedia://en/835827), *a* and *b* are single elements, and *u* and *v* are arbitrary words.

The shuffle product was introduced by (Eilenberg Mac Lane 1953). The name "shuffle product" refers to the fact that the product can be thought of as a sum over all ways of [riffle shuffling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riffle_shuffle) two words together: this is the [riffle shuffle permutation](wikipedia://en/39949298). The product is [commutative](wikipedia://en/294390) and [associative](wikipedia://en/1335).[^o6]

The shuffle product of two words in some alphabet is often denoted by the **shuffle product symbol** ⧢ ([Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode) character U+29E2 shuffle product, derived from the [Cyrillic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic) letter $\langle \text{ш}\rangle$ [sha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sha_%28Cyrillic%29)).

:::

## Infiltration product {#Infiltration_product}

The closely related **infiltration product** was introduced by (Chen Fox Lyndon 1958). It is defined inductively on words over an alphabet *A* by

*fa* ↑ *ga* = (*f* ↑ *ga*)*a* + (*fa* ↑ *g*)*a* + (*f* ↑ *g*)*a*

*fa* ↑ *gb* = (*f* ↑ *gb*)*a* + (*fa* ↑ *g*)*b*

For example:

*ab* ↑ *ab* = *ab* + 2*aab* + 2*abb* + 4 *aabb* + 2*abab*

*ab* ↑ *ba* = *aba* + *bab* + *abab* + 2*abba* + 2*baab* + *baba*

The infiltration product is also commutative and associative.[^o10]

:::
