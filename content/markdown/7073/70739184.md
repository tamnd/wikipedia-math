# Wei Dongyi

[Wei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wéi_%28surname_韋%29)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wei Dongyi"}

**Wei Dongyi** (; born 1992) is a Chinese mathematician. He is an associate professor and assistant researcher of [mathematical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_science) at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).

:::

He has attracted attention on Chinese social media for his simple living style.[^o3][^o4] In 2021, he was called Peking University's "ugliest teacher" due to his unkempt appearance.[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Wei Dongyi was born in [Lixia, Jinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lixia,_Jinan) in June 8th, 1992. His father Wei Zhongli (韦忠礼, 1963-2016) originally from [Dongyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongyang), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang), and was an alumnus of [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University) with bachelor, master, and doctoral degrees in mathematics; he later served as a professor in [Shandong Jianzhu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Jianzhu_University) and [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University).[^o7] His mother Yu Zhen (俞蓁, 1965-) is also a professor in [Shandong Jianzhu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Jianzhu_University).

Wei entered [Attached Senior School of Shandong Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attached_Senior_School_of_Shandong_Normal_University) in 2007, and during the high school, he won gold medal in [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) representing [Peoples Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoples_Republic_of_China) twice in 2008 and 2009;[^o8] with that grade, we as admitted in [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University). Wei began attending [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2010. He earned his [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in 2014 and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from the university in 2018 under [Tian Gang](wikipedia://en/6716707). His research fields includes [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

In December 2019, Wei completed his post-doctoral work and became an assistant professor in the Mathematics Department of Peking University.[^o9] In November 2025, he became an associate professor of the university.

## Internet fame {#Internet_fame}

Wei Dongyi became viral on the internet from a street interview in May 2021 when he was seen dressed in an unassuming manner, holding a large bottle of drinking water and a bag of [mantou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantou) (steamed buns).[^o11] His appearance has led to netizens dubbing him the "sweeping monk" of Peking University.

A group of doctorate mathematicians had been struggling to build a mathematical model for months and called Wei Dongyi for help. He provided equations to alter their experiment which resulted in the mathematical model obtaining a 96% passing rate. The mathematicians wanted to pay the professor to show their gratitude, but he declined. "It’s unnecessary to pay me for such an easy problem," he said. Wei eventually asked for a recharge of his transport card as a form of compensation.[^o12][^o13]

Wei Dongyi is nicknamed “God Wei” (韦神) by Chinese netizens.
