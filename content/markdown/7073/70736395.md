# Mary C. Meyer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary C. Meyer"}

**Mary C. Meyer** is an American statistician. She is known for both theoretical and computational research in [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369) and [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671), especially for densities with shape constraints such as convexity or monotonicity. She is a professor of statistics at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Meyer obtained her Ph.D. in 1996 from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), under the supervision of [Michael Woodroofe](wikipedia://en/55829743). Her [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) was *Shape-Restricted Inference with Applications to [Nonparametric Regression](wikipedia://en/4536068), Smooth [Nonparametric Function Estimation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric_Estimation), and Density Estimation*. She was a faculty member in statistics at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) before moving to Colorado State University.

While a statistics professor at Colorado State University, Meyer declared that a study of salaries by CSU created salary goals for women faculty that were "[substantially smaller than for men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_pay_gap_in_the_United_States)".[^o4] This led CSU to start studying pay equity in 2015, which in turn led later that year to a quarter of female full professors receiving higher pay.[^o5] She has also led faculty opposition to increases in athletic spending by the university.

## Book {#Book}

Meyer is the author of the textbook *Probability and Mathematical Statistics: Theory, Applications, and Practice in R* (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2019).
