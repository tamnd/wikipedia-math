# Eichler–Shimura congruence relation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eichler–Shimura congruence relation"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **Eichler–Shimura congruence relation** expresses the [local *L*-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_zeta-function) of a [modular curve](wikipedia://en/669552) at a [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) *p* in terms of the [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of [Hecke operators](wikipedia://en/521837). It was introduced by (txt) and generalized by (txt). Roughly speaking, it says that the correspondence on the modular curve inducing the [Hecke operator](wikipedia://en/521837) *T*<sub>*p*</sub> is congruent mod *p* to the sum of the [Frobenius map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_map) *Frob* and its transpose *Ver*. In other words,

:::

*T*<sub>*p*</sub> = *Frob* + *Ver*

as endomorphisms of the Jacobian *J*<sub>0</sub>(*N*)<sub>**F**<sub>*p*</sub></sub> of the modular curve *X*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) over the finite field **F**<sub>*p*</sub>.

The Eichler–Shimura congruence relation and its generalizations to [Shimura varieties](wikipedia://en/10389861) play a pivotal role in the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905), by identifying a part of the [Hasse–Weil zeta function](wikipedia://en/1157842) of a modular curve or a more general modular variety, with the product of [Mellin transforms](wikipedia://en/387297) of weight 2 [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) or a product of analogous automorphic *L*-functions.
