# Johanna G. Nešlehová

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johanna G. Nešlehová"}

**Johanna G. Nešlehová** (born July 26, 1977) is a Czech [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711) who works in Canada at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) as a professor in the department of mathematics and statistics. Her research interests include [copulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copula_%28probability_theory%29), [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732), [multivariate statistics](wikipedia://en/19384), and [operational risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_risk).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nešlehová is originally from [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague), the daughter of painter [Pavel Nešleha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Nešleha) and art historian Mahulena Nešlehová. She studied at the [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University), the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), and the [University of Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oldenburg), earning a degree (vordiplom) from [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) in 1999, a master's degree (diplom) from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) in 2000, and a doctorate from the [University of Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oldenburg) in 2004. Her dissertation, *Dependence of Non-continuous Random Variables*, was supervised by Dietmar Pfeifer.

After working as a postdoctoral researcher and Heinz Hopf Lecturer at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), she joined the McGill University faculty in 2009. Since October 2022 she is also a professor at WU [Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Economics_and_Business).

## Book {#Book}

With Erhard Cramer, Nešlehová is the author of a German-language undergraduate textbook in introductory mathematics, *Vorkurs Mathematik: Arbeitsbuch zum Studienbeginn in Bachelor-Studiengängen* (Springer, 2005; 7th ed., 2018).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Nešlehová was named as an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2011. In 2020 she was named a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

She was the 2019 winner of the CRM-SSC Prize in Statistics "for fundamental contributions to multivariate statistics, and in particular stochastic dependence modeling and extreme-value theory, and for her efforts to promote the sound application of statistics in risk management".

In 2023, Nešlehová was the second statistician (after [Nancy Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Reid) in 1995) to receive the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Nešlehová is married to statistician [Christian Genest](wikipedia://en/41992626). Their son Richard Genest was born in 2014. Johanna became a Canadian citizen in 2015.
