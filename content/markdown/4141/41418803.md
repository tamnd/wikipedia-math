# Equivariant differential form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Equivariant differential form"}

In differential geometry, an **equivariant differential form** on a manifold *M* [acted upon](wikipedia://en/39904936) by a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) *G* is a [polynomial map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_map)

:::

$$\alpha: \mathfrak{g} \to \Omega^*(M)$$

from the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} = \operatorname{Lie}(G)$ to the space of [differential forms](wikipedia://en/221519) on *M* that are equivariant; i.e.,

$$\alpha(\operatorname{Ad}(g)X) = g\alpha(X).$$

In other words, an equivariant differential form is an invariant element of[^o2]

$$\mathbb{C}[\mathfrak{g}] \otimes \Omega^*(M) = \operatorname{Sym}(\mathfrak{g}^*) \otimes \Omega^*(M).$$

For an equivariant differential form $\alpha$, the **equivariant exterior derivative** $d_\mathfrak{g} \alpha$ of $\alpha$ is defined by

$$(d_\mathfrak{g} \alpha)(X) = d(\alpha(X)) - i_{X^\#}(\alpha(X))$$

where *d* is the usual exterior derivative and $i_{X^\#}$ is the [interior product](wikipedia://en/2154963) by the [fundamental vector field](wikipedia://en/3946232) generated by *X*. It is easy to see $d_\mathfrak{g} \circ d_\mathfrak{g} = 0$ (use the fact the Lie derivative of $\alpha(X)$ along $X^\#$ is zero) and one then puts

$$H^*_G(M) = \ker d_\mathfrak{g}/\operatorname{im} d_\mathfrak{g} ,$$

which is called the [equivariant cohomology](wikipedia://en/2976342) of *M* (which coincides with the ordinary equivariant cohomology defined in terms of [Borel construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_construction).) The definition is due to H. Cartan. The notion has an application to the [equivariant index theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_index_theory).

$d_\mathfrak{g}$-closed or $d_\mathfrak{g}$-exact forms are called **equivariantly closed** or **equivariantly exact**.

The integral of an equivariantly closed form may be evaluated from its restriction to the fixed point by means of the [localization formula](wikipedia://en/44300155).
