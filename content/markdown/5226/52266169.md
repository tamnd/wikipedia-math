# Donal O'Shea

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Donal O'Shea"}

**Donal B. O'Shea** is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who is also noted for his bestselling books.[^o5] He served as the fifth president of [New College of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College_of_Florida) in [Sarasota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarasota,_Florida), from July 1, 2012, until June 30, 2021. He was succeeded by [Patricia Okker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Okker) on July 1, 2021.[^o6] Before coming to New College, he served in various roles at [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College), including professor of mathematics, dean of faculty, and vice president for academic affairs.[^o7][^o8]

:::

O'Shea graduated with a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) from [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College),[^o8] and received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario) in 1981; his thesis, titled *On μ-Equivalent Families of Singularities*, was written under the direction of Albert John Coleman.

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

Some of his best known books are:[^o11]

- *The Poincaré Conjecture: In Search of the Shape of the Universe*.[^o12]

The book has consistently received good reviews.[^o13][^o14]

- [David A. Cox](wikipedia://en/41248158), [John Little](wikipedia://en/76433401), and Donal O'Shea: *Using algebraic geometry*, [Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980), vol. 185, Springer-Verlag, 2005.
- [David A. Cox](wikipedia://en/41248158), John Little, and Donal O'Shea: *Ideals, varieties, and algorithms: an introduction to computational algebraic geometry and commutative algebra*, 3rd. edition, Springer Verlag, 2007.
