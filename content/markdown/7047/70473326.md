# National Institute for Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Institute for Mathematical Sciences"}

*Image: 국가수리과학연구소 National Institute for Mathematical Sciences (NIMS) entrance.jpg.* Indoor entrance to NIMS

The **National Institute for Mathematical Sciences** (**NIMS**; ) is a Korean government-funded mathematics research institute. Their work focuses both basic research and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) with industrial and medical applications.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

Founded in 2005,[^o6] NIMS became an affiliated research institute of the [Institute for Basic Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Basic_Science) in 2012.[^o7] After delays for multiple years,[^o8] relocation is planned for late 2023.[^o9] The vision of NIMS is to "attain a global top 10 competitiveness in industrial mathematics research by 2028".[^o10] NIMS celebrated its 20th anniversary in 2025.[^o11]
