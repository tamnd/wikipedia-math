# Kim Hyun-Min

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kim Hyun-Min"}

Kim

**Kim Hyun-Min** (; born March 20, 1966) is a professor in the department of mathematics at [Pusan National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pusan_National_University)[^o5] among other positions at the university.[^o6] He has served as vice president of the Korean Mathematical Society;[^o7] vice president of the Youngnam Mathematical Society;[^o8][^o9] director of the Big Data-based Finance, Fisheries, and Manufacturing Innovation Industrial Mathematics Center;[^o10] and the sixth president of the [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326).[^o11][^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kim majored in mathematics in the College of Natural Sciences at Pusan National University for his bachelor's and master's. He earned a second master's degree and a doctorate in mathematics specializing in numerical analysis and calculation at the [Victoria University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester).[^o13]

## Career {#Career}

His first position was as a post-doc in the department of mathematics at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST). Following that, he taught at Pusan National University and held positions at [Kyungpook National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyungpook_National_University), Samsung Heavy Industries College of Engineering, and the Korean Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics.[^o15]

## Research {#Research}

Kim's research is focused on developing algorithms for solving various types of nonlinear matrix equations.[^o17][^o18][^o19]
