# Ulrich Kohlenbach

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrich Kohlenbach"}

**Ulrich Wilhelm Kohlenbach** (born 27 July 1962 in [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main)) is a German mathematician and professor of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [logic](wikipedia://en/19636) at the [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt). His research interests lie in the field of [proof mining](wikipedia://en/14484306).[^o4]

:::

Kohlenbach was president of the [German Association for Mathematical Logic and for Basic Research in the Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Vereinigung_für_mathematische_Logik_und_für_Grundlagenforschung_der_exakten_Wissenschaften) (DVMLG) from 2008 to 2012 and president of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438) from 2016 to 2018.

## Life {#Life}

He graduated ('Abitur') from [Lessing-Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lessing-Gymnasium,_Frankfurt) (High School) in 1980 and completed his studies of mathematics, philosophy, and [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics) with a [diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) from the [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt). During his studies he received a scholarship from the [Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studienstiftung_des_deutschen_Volkes). At the same university, he received his Ph.D. in 1990 under the supervision of Horst Luckhardt and passed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) ('venia legendi') in mathematics five years later. During the academic year 1996/1997 he was a visiting assistant professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). In 1997, he became an associate professor at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) where he worked until 2004. Kohlenbach is now a full professor at the [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt).[^o6][^o7]

He is married to Gabriele Bahl-Kohlenbach with whom he has a daughter.

In 2011, he received the prestigious Kurt Gödel Research Prize of the [Kurt Gödel Society](wikipedia://en/2129012).[^o8] He was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro. In 2024, he was selected as the first ringbearer of the [Ernst Zermelo](wikipedia://en/178655) Ring.[^o9]
