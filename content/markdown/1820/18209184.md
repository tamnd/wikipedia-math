# ALL (complexity)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ALL"}

In [computability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computability_theory_%28computer_science%29) and [complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543), **ALL** is the class of all [decision problems](wikipedia://en/8336).

:::

## Relations to other classes {#Relations_to_other_classes}

**ALL** contains all of the complex classes of decision problems, including **[RE](wikipedia://en/3106703)** and **[co-RE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-RE)**, and uncountably many languages that are neither **RE** nor **co-RE**. It is the largest complexity class, containing all other complexity classes.
