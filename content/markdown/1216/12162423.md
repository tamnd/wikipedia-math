# Karl Zsigmondy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Zsigmondy"}

**Karl Zsigmondy** (; 27 March 1867 – 14 October 1925) was an [Austro-Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a son of [Adolf Zsigmondy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Zsigmondy) from [Pozsony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pozsony), [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary) (now Bratislava, [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia)) and his mother was [Irma von Szakmáry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irma_von_Szakmáry) of [Martonvásár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martonvásár), [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary).

:::

He studied (1886–1890) and worked (1894–1925) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). After his PhD, in 1890, he studied at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in Paris, but came back to Vienna in 1894. He discovered [Zsigmondy's theorem](wikipedia://en/5767229) in 1892.

He was the brother of the [mountain climber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_climber) [Emil Zsigmondy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Zsigmondy) and the [Nobel Laureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Laureate) chemist [Richard Adolf Zsigmondy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Adolf_Zsigmondy).
