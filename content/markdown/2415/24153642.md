# Jucys–Murphy element

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jucys–Murphy element"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Jucys–Murphy elements** in the [group algebra](wikipedia://en/349223) $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901), named after [Algimantas Adolfas Jucys](wikipedia://en/31618545) and G. E. Murphy, are defined as a sum of [transpositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transposition_%28mathematics%29) by the formula:

:::

$$X_1=0, ~~~ X_k= (1 \; k)+ (2 \; k)+\cdots+(k-1 \; k), ~~~ k=2,\dots,n.$$

They play an important role in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

They generate a commutative subalgebra of $\mathbb{C} [ S_n]$. Moreover, *X*<sub>*n*</sub> commutes with all elements of $\mathbb{C} [S_{n-1}]$.

The vectors constituting the basis of Young's "seminormal representation" are eigenvectors for the action of *X*<sub>*n*</sub>. For any [standard Young tableau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Young_tableau) *U* we have:

$$X_k v_U =c_k(U) v_U, ~~~ k=1,\dots,n,$$

where *c*<sub>*k*</sub>(*U*) is the *content* *b* − *a* of the cell (*a*, *b*) occupied by *k* in the standard Young tableau *U*.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** ([Jucys](wikipedia://en/31618545)): The [center](wikipedia://en/342453) $Z(\mathbb{C} [S_n])$ of the group algebra $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ of the symmetric group is generated by the [symmetric polynomials](wikipedia://en/1440207) in the elements *X<sub>k</sub>*.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** ([Jucys](wikipedia://en/31618545)): Let *t* be a formal variable commuting with everything, then the following identity for polynomials in variable *t* with values in the group algebra $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ holds true:

$$(t+X_1) (t+X_2) \cdots (t+X_n)= \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \sigma t^{\text{number of cycles of }\sigma}.$$

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** ([Okounkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okounkov)–[Vershik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vershik)): The subalgebra of $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ generated by the centers

$$Z(\mathbb{C} [ S_1]), Z(\mathbb{C} [ S_2]), \ldots, Z(\mathbb{C} [ S_{n-1}]), Z(\mathbb{C} [S_n])$$

is exactly the subalgebra generated by the Jucys–Murphy elements *X<sub>k</sub>*.

:::
