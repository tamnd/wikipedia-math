# Leslie Greengard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leslie Greengard"}

**Leslie Frederick Greengard** (born 1957) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).[^o3][^o4] He is co-inventor with [Vladimir Rokhlin Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Rokhlin_Jr.) of the [fast multipole method](wikipedia://en/8280771) (FMM) in 1987, recognized as one of the top-ten [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms) of the 20th century.[^o3][^o5]

:::

Greengard was elected as a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 2006 for work on the development of algorithms and software for fast multipole methods.

## Short biography {#Short_biography}

Leslie Frederick Greengard[^o7] was born in 1957 in [London, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London,_England),[^o8] but grew up in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), and [New Haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Haven). He holds a B.A. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University) (1979), an M.D. from the [Yale School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_School_of_Medicine) (1987), and a Ph.D. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (1987).[^o3][^o4]

From 2006 to 2011, Greengard was director of the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), an independent division of the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) (NYU)[^o4][^o9] and is currently a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at Courant. He is also a professor at [New York University Tandon School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering)[^o10] and the director of the Simons Center for Data Analysis.[^o11]

He formerly served as the Director at the Center for Computational Biology at the [Flatiron Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatiron_Institute). , he has assumed the directorship of the new Center of Computational Mathematics at the Institute.[^o12]

He is the son of neuroscientist [Paul Greengard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Greengard) and the nephew of Irene Kane, later known as [Chris Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Chase), an actress, writer, and journalist.[^o13]
