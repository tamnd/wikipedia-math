# Manfred Einsiedler

*Image: Manfred Einsiedler.jpg.* Einsiedler at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Manfred Einsiedler"}

**Manfred Leopold Einsiedler** is an Austrian mathematician who studies [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986). He was born in Scheibbs, Austria in 1973.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Einsiedler studied mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), where he received his undergraduate degree in 1996 and his PhD in 1999 under [Klaus Schmidt](wikipedia://en/39443367) with thesis *Problems in higher dimensional dynamics*. He was a postdoc in the academic year 2000–2001 at the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia) in Norwich and in the academic year 2001–2002 at [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University). In 2001 he earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the University of Vienna and then became there a [professor extraordinarius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_extraordinarius) (on leave). In the [academic year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_year) 2004–2005 he was a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (as a Clay Research Scholar). At [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) he became in 2006 an [associate Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Professor) and in 2008 a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor). Since 2009 he has been a [professor ordinarius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_ordinarius) at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich).[^o5]

In 2004 he won the Research Prize of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society). In 2008 he was an invited speaker (*Effective equidistribution and spectral gap*) at the [European Mathematical Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Mathematical_Congress) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam). In 2010 he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) (*Application of measure rigidity of diagonal actions*) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad). In 2019 Einsiedler was also an invited speaker at the conference Dynamics, Equations and Applications in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków).[^o6]

Einsiedler works on ergodic theory (especially, dynamical and equidistribution problems on [homogeneous spaces](wikipedia://en/363325)) and its applications to number theory. He has collaborated with [Grigory Margulis](wikipedia://en/1104948) and [Akshay Venkatesh](wikipedia://en/6647964). With [Elon Lindenstrauss](wikipedia://en/28371257) and [Anatole Katok](wikipedia://en/43275327), Einsiedler proved that a conjecture of [John Edensor Littlewood](wikipedia://en/158371) on [diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558) is "almost always" true.[^o7][^o8] ( "Almost always" means in this context that the set of pairs of real numbers for which the conjecture fails has (in particular) [Hausdorff dimension](wikipedia://en/14294) zero.)
