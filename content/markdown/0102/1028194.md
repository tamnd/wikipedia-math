# Warsaw School (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Warsaw School"}

**Warsaw School of Mathematics** is the name given to a group of [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked at [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland), in the two decades between the World Wars, especially in the fields of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology) and [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). They published in the journal *Fundamenta Mathematicae*,[^o2] founded in 1920—one of the world's first specialist [pure-mathematics journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal). It was in this journal, in 1933, that [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)—whose illustrious career would a few years later take him to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley)—published his celebrated theorem on the [undefinability of the notion of truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undefinability_of_the_notion_of_truth).

:::

Notable members of the Warsaw School of Mathematics have included:

- [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122)
- [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075)
- [Edward Marczewski](wikipedia://en/1028149)
- [Bronisław Knaster](wikipedia://en/1003125)
- [Zygmunt Janiszewski](wikipedia://en/362471)
- [Stefan Mazurkiewicz](wikipedia://en/629203)
- [Stanisław Saks](wikipedia://en/362649)
- [Karol Borsuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_Borsuk)
- [Roman Sikorski](wikipedia://en/1983335)
- [Nachman Aronszajn](wikipedia://en/13496987)
- [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501)

Additionally, notable logicians of the [Lwów–Warsaw School of Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów–Warsaw_School_of_Logic), working at Warsaw, have included:

- [Stanisław Leśniewski](wikipedia://en/422271)
- [Adolf Lindenbaum](wikipedia://en/1029330)
- [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)
- [Jan Łukasiewicz](wikipedia://en/305064)
- [Andrzej Mostowski](wikipedia://en/1029314)
- [Helena Rasiowa](wikipedia://en/3482432)

Fourier analysis has been advanced at [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) by:

- [Aleksander Rajchman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksander_Rajchman)
- [Antoni Zygmund](wikipedia://en/362477)
- [Józef Marcinkiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Marcinkiewicz)
- [Otton M. Nikodym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otton_M._Nikodym)
