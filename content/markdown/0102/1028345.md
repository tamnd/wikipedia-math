# Stanisław Zaremba (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Zaremba"}

**Stanisław Zaremba** (3 October 1863 – 23 November 1942) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and engineer.[^o4] His research in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis), particularly on [harmonic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_functions), gained him a wide recognition. He was one of the mathematicians who contributed to the success of the [Polish School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3008223) through his teaching and organizational skills as well as through his research. Apart from his research works, Zaremba wrote many university textbooks and [monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph).

:::

He was a professor of the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) (since 1900), member of [Academy of Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Learning) (since 1903), co-founder and president of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791) (1919), and the first editor of the *[Annales de la Société Polonaise de Mathématique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annales_de_la_Société_Polonaise_de_Mathématique)*.

He should not be confused with his son [Stanisław Krystyn Zaremba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanisław_Krystyn_Zaremba_%28mathematician%29), also a mathematician.

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Zaremba was born on 3 October 1863 in [Romanówka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanivka,_Bereznehuvate_settlement_hromada,_Bashtanka_Raion,_Mykolaiv_Oblast), then part of [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) (present-day [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)), to Hipolit, an engineer,[^o4] and Aleksandra (Kurzańska).[^o6] He was educated at a [grammar school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar_school) in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) and studied at the Institute of Technology of the same city obtaining his diploma in engineering in 1886.[^o4]

The same year he left Saint Petersburg and went to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) to study mathematics. He received his doctoral degree from the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1889[^o4] based on his dissertation *Sur un probleme concernant l'etat calorifique d'un corps solide homogene indefini* written under the supervision of [Gaston Darboux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaston_Darboux) and [Émile Picard](wikipedia://en/42000).[^o7]

During his stay in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), Zaremba established close scientific contacts with [Paul Painlevé](wikipedia://en/402504) and [Édouard Goursat](wikipedia://en/1674255). Another French mathematician, [Jacques Hadamard](wikipedia://en/312675), observed that Zaremba's work enabled overcoming difficulties that mathematicians had been struggling with for years. The generalizations introduced by Zaremba reshaped the foundations of [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342) and other areas of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), becoming the starting point for intensive work by a new generation of mathematicians on [hyperbolic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_equation), the [Dirichlet problem](wikipedia://en/571109), and [harmonic functions](wikipedia://en/55762).[^o2]

He left France in 1900 and joined the faculty at the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) taking over the chair of mathematics. His years in France enabled him to establish a strong bridge between Polish mathematicians and those in France. In 1905, Zaremba was awarded the title of full professor by the Jagiellonian University.[^o8]

In 1919, he was one of the founding members of the Kraków Mathematical Society, transformed the following year into the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o9] According to [Władysław Ślebodziński](wikipedia://en/1983028), Zaremba played a vital role in shaping the Golden Age of mathematics in Poland during the [Interwar period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interwar_period).[^o9] He is also regarded as a leading representative of the [Kraków School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1028214).[^o20]

His notable students included [Stanisław Gołąb](wikipedia://en/4499769), [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122), [Włodzimierz Stożek](wikipedia://en/1821218), and [Witold Wilkosz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witold_Wilkosz).[^o7] Zaremba served as Vice-President of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868), and was a member of the [Polish Academy of Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Learning) (1903)[^o2] as well as a corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (1925).[^o6]

He died on 23 November 1942 in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), during the [German occupation of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Poland_%281939–1945%29).

## Work {#Work}

Zaremba's research was primarily centered on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342). In addition, he made substantial contributions to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) and [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography).[^o11]

Zaremba, together with [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), introduced around 1900 the [direct method in the calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/24499402), a general technique for proving the existence of minimizers of a given [functional](wikipedia://en/1374948) drawing on methods of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).[^o12][^o13]

Another notable result by Zaremba, introduced in 1907, is the [reproducing kernel Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/651196), which pertains to [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) for [harmonic](wikipedia://en/55762) and [biharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/3010562).[^o14][^o8]

In 1910, Zaremba solved the first boundary value problem involving a [mixed boundary condition](wikipedia://en/2784886) for the [Laplace's equation](wikipedia://en/36941).[^o15][^o16]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1908, Zaremba became the first Polish mathematician to have the privilege of giving an invited talk at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome).[^o20] He is among the most frequently invited mathematicians to this conference, having received this honour five times.[^o21]

In 1925, he was awarded the Commander's Cross of the [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta).[^o22] Three years later, he was decorated with the French [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour).[^o23] In 1936, he received the [Gold Cross of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_of_Merit_%28Poland%29).[^o24] Zaremba also received honorary doctorates from [University of Aix-Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University),[^o25] [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University)[^o26] and the [Adam Mickiewicz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University) in Poznań.

In 1982, the [Polish Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Post) issued a 5-[zloty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zloty) commemorative stamp featuring Zaremba as part of the "Polish Mathematicians" series.[^o27]
