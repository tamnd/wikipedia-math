# Andrzej Mostowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Mostowski"}

**Andrzej Mostowski** (; 1 November 1913 – 22 August 1975) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He worked primarily in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) and is perhaps best remembered for the [Mostowski collapse lemma](wikipedia://en/1251604). He was a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) and a representative of the [Warsaw School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1028194).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Lemberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary), Mostowski entered the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1931. He was influenced by [Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075), [Lindenbaum](wikipedia://en/1029330), and [Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920). In 1938, he obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) on the basis of his dissertation *O niezależności definicji skończoności w systemie logiki* (On the Independence of Finitenesss Definitions in a System of Logic).[^o4] It was officially supervised by [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075) but in practice by [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920) who was a young lecturer at that time.

He became an accountant after the German [invasion of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Poland) but continued working in the [Underground Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_education_in_Poland_during_World_War_II). After the [Warsaw Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Uprising) of 1944, the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazis) tried to put him in a concentration camp. With the help of some Polish nurses, he escaped to a hospital, choosing to take bread with him rather than his notebook containing his research. He managed to reconstruct some of this research after the war, however much of it remained lost.

From 1948 to 1949, he spent the academic year at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton), re-establishing his contacts with [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736). He subsequently came back to Warsaw where he served as head of the foundations of mathematics department at the [Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/67042302) of the Polish Academy of Sciences.[^o5]

In 1954 Mostowski was awarded by Knight's Cross of the [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta). In 1960, he became a member of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o8] In 1963, Mostowski was elected a full member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) and in 1973, a member of the [Finnish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5] After the [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he supervised Rasiowa's both master and doctoral theses in logic and the foundations of mathematics. His other notable students included [Andrzej Ehrenfeucht](wikipedia://en/13405819), [Andrzej Grzegorczyk](wikipedia://en/24824350), [Moshé Machover](wikipedia://en/43349930), [Michael Makkai](wikipedia://en/22629410), [Janusz Onyszkiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janusz_Onyszkiewicz), and [Roman Sikorski](wikipedia://en/1983335).[^o4]

His work was largely on [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory) and [undecidability](wikipedia://en/15631055). From 1946 until his death in [Vancouver, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver), Canada, he worked at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). Much of his work, during that time, was on first order [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). He also worked at the [State Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/67042302), which was incorporated into the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1952.[^o6]

His son Tadeusz is also a mathematician working on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o7] With Krzysztof Kurdyka and Adam Parusinski, Tadeusz Mostowski solved [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633)'s [gradient conjecture](wikipedia://en/600373) in 2000.

He died suddenly on 22 August 1975 while on his way to a scientific conference in [Burnaby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnaby), Canada, where he was about to deliver a lecture at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University).[^o8]
