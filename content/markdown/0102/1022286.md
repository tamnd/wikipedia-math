# Derivative algebra (abstract algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Derivative algebra"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **derivative algebra** is an [algebraic structure](wikipedia://en/106364) of the signature

:::

\<*A*, ·, +, ', 0, 1, <sup>D</sup>>

where

\<*A*, ·, +, ', 0, 1>

is a [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) and <sup>D</sup> is a [unary operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unary_operator), the **derivative operator**, satisfying the identities:

1. 0<sup>D</sup> = 0
1. *x*<sup>DD</sup> ≤ *x* + *x*<sup>D</sup>
1. (*x* + *y*)<sup>D</sup> = *x*<sup>D</sup> + *y*<sup>D</sup>.

x<sup>D</sup> is called the **derivative** of x. Derivative algebras provide an algebraic abstraction of the **[derived set](wikipedia://en/1567335)** operator in [topology](wikipedia://en/30450). They also [play the same role](wikipedia://en/990343) for the [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365) *wK4* = *K* + (*p*∧□*p* → □□*p*) that [Boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/3959) play for ordinary [propositional logic](wikipedia://en/18154).
