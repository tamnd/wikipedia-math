# Lwów School of Mathematics

*Image: Lwowscy matematycy1930.jpg.* Lwów School of Mathematics, 1930

*Image: KsiegaSzkocka1.JPG.* Part of the *[Scottish Book](wikipedia://en/4233115)* with [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646)'s and [Stanisław Ulam](wikipedia://en/41531)'s notes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lwów School of Mathematics"}

The **Lwów school of mathematics** (Lwowska szkoła matematyczna) was a group of Polish [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in the [interwar period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interwar_period) in [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic) (since 1945 [Lviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)). The mathematicians often met at the famous [Scottish Café](wikipedia://en/362651)[^o2] to discuss mathematical problems, and published in the journal *[Studia Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studia_Mathematica)*, founded in 1929. The school was renowned for its productivity and its extensive contributions to subjects such as [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Members {#Members}

Notable members of the Lwów school of mathematics included:

- [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646)
- [Feliks Barański](wikipedia://en/6111672)
- [Władysław Orlicz](wikipedia://en/362718)
- [Stanisław Saks](wikipedia://en/362649)
- [Hugo Steinhaus](wikipedia://en/71735)
- [Stanisław Mazur](wikipedia://en/1023162)
- [Stanisław Ulam](wikipedia://en/41531)
- [Józef Schreier](wikipedia://en/56737160)
- [Juliusz Schauder](wikipedia://en/685314)
- [Mark Kac](wikipedia://en/1062425)
- [Antoni Łomnicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Łomnicki)
- [Stefan Kaczmarz](wikipedia://en/1062394)
- [Herman Auerbach](wikipedia://en/1983296)
- [Włodzimierz Stożek](wikipedia://en/1821218)
- [Stanisław Ruziewicz](wikipedia://en/1821290)
- [Eustachy Żyliński](wikipedia://en/73170532)

The biographies and contributions of these mathematicians were documented in 1980 by their contemporary, [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075) in his book *A Half Century of Polish Mathematics: Remembrances and Reflections*.[^o4]

## The end of the school {#The_end_of_the_school}

First the [Soviet annexation of Eastern Galicia and Volhynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_annexation_of_Eastern_Galicia_and_Volhynia) in 1939, then the [German invasion of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Barbarossa) in 1941, caused many of the mathematicians to flee Lwów. Few of them survived [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), but after the war a group including some of the original community carried on their work in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław) in Silesia, after the [post-war Polish population transfers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_population_transfers_%281944–1946%29). A number of the prewar mathematicians, prominent among them [Stanisław Ulam](wikipedia://en/41531), became famous for work done in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).
