# Aleksandr Khinchin

Yakovlevich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksandr Khinchin"}

**Aleksandr Yakovlevich Khinchin** (Алекса́ндр Я́ковлевич Хи́нчин, Alexandre Khintchine; July 19, 1894 – November 18, 1959) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and one of the most significant contributors to the [Soviet school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_school_of_probability) of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

Due to romanization conventions, his name is sometimes written as "Khinchin" and other times as "Khintchine".

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He was born in the village of [Kondrovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondrovo,_Kaluga_Oblast), [Kaluga Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluga_Oblast), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). While studying at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), he became one of the first followers of the famous [Luzin](wikipedia://en/576228) school. Khinchin graduated from the university in 1916 and six years later he became a full professor there, retaining that position until his death.

Khinchin's early works focused on [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). Later he applied methods from the metric theory of functions to problems in probability theory and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He became one of the founders of modern probability theory, discovering the [law of the iterated logarithm](wikipedia://en/496558) in 1924, achieving important results in the field of [limit theorems](wikipedia://en/39406), giving a definition of a [stationary process](wikipedia://en/329898) and laying a foundation for the theory of such processes.

Khinchin made significant contributions to the metric theory of [Diophantine approximations](wikipedia://en/251558) and established an important result for simple real [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199), discovering a property of such numbers that leads to what is now known as [Khinchin's constant](wikipedia://en/435315). He also published several important works on [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics), where he used the methods of probability theory, and on [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773), [queuing theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queuing_theory) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

In 1939 Khinchin was elected as a Correspondent Member of the [Academy of Sciences of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union). He was awarded the [USSR State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) (1941), and the [Order of Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Lenin).[^o5]
