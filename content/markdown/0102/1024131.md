# Jean Bourgain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Bourgain"}

**Jean Louis, baron Bourgain** (; 1954-02-28 – 2018) was a Belgian mathematician. He was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1994 in recognition of his work on several core topics of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) such as the geometry of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [nonlinear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/16678376) from [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bourgain received his PhD from the [Vrije Universiteit Brussel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit_Brussel) in 1977. He was a faculty member at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) and, from 1985 until 1995, professor at [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) at [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette) in France, at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) from 1994 until 2018. He was an editor for the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*. From 2012 to 2014, he was a visiting scholar at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o8]

His research work included several areas of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) such as the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713), and later also [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He proved the uniqueness of the solutions for the initial value problem of the [Korteweg–De Vries equation](wikipedia://en/344116). He formulated what became known as the Bourgain slicing problem in high-dimensional convex geometry. In 1985, he proved Bourgain's embedding theorem in metric dimension reduction, which states that every metric space can be embedded into an $l_p$ space of dimension $O(\log^2 (n))$ with distortion $O(\log(n))$. Together with [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman), he contributed to progress on [Mahler’s conjecture](wikipedia://en/21867246) in 1987. In 2000, he connected the [Kakeya problem](wikipedia://en/588260) to [arithmetic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243381).[^o9][^o10] As a researcher, he was the author or coauthor of more than 500 articles.[^o11]

Together with Ciprian Demeter and [Larry Guth](wikipedia://en/31875947), he proved [Vinogradov's mean-value theorem](wikipedia://en/43822295) in 2015.

Bourgain was diagnosed with [pancreatic cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancreatic_cancer) in late 2014. He died of it on 22 December 2018 at a hospital in Bonheiden, Belgium.[^o12]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Bourgain received several awards during his career, the most notable being the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1994.

In 2009 Bourgain was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o14]

In 2010, he received the [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in Mathematics.[^o15]

In 2012, he and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) received the [Crafoord Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crafoord_Prize) in Mathematics from the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o16]

In 2015, he was made a baron by King [Philippe of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippe_of_Belgium).[^o17]

In 2017, he received the [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).[^o18]

In 2018, he received the [Steele Prize for Lifetime Achievement](wikipedia://en/1252550).[^o19]
