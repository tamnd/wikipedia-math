# Adolf Lindenbaum

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Lindenbaum"}

**Adolf Lindenbaum** (12 June 1904[^o3] – August 1941) was a Polish [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for [Lindenbaum's lemma](wikipedia://en/6625296) and [Lindenbaum–Tarski algebras](wikipedia://en/990343).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born and brought up in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) in a [Polish-Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Poland) family. He earned a Ph.D. in 1928 under [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122) and [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1934. He published works on [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_arithmetic) and [ordinal arithmetic](wikipedia://en/2139226), the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840), the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705), [theory of functions](wikipedia://en/5458932), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). He served as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) from 1935 until the outbreak of [war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) in September 1939. He was [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)'s closest collaborator of the inter-war period. Around the end of October or beginning of November 1935 he married [Janina Hosiasson](wikipedia://en/35637974), a fellow logician of the [Lwow–Warsaw school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwow–Warsaw_school). He and his wife were adherents of [logical empiricism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_empiricism), participated in and contributed to the international [unity of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_of_science) movement, and were members of the original [Vienna Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Circle). Sometime before the middle of August 1941 he and his sister Stefanja were shot to death in [Naujoji Vilnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naujoji_Vilnia) (Nowa Wilejka), 7 km east of [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius), by the occupying German forces or Lithuanian collaborators.[^o5]
