# Kraków School of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kraków School of Mathematics"}

The **Kraków School of Mathematics** (krakowska szkoła matematyczna) was a subgroup of the [Polish School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3008223) represented by mathematicians from the [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) universities—[Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University), and the Mining Academy (now [AGH University of Krakow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGH_University_of_Science_and_Technology))–active during the [interwar period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interwar_period) (1918–1939). Their areas of study were primarily [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), and [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478).

:::

The Kraków School of Differential Equations was founded by [Tadeusz Ważewski](wikipedia://en/1028319), a student of [Stanisław Zaremba](wikipedia://en/1028345), and was internationally appreciated after [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).

The Kraków School of Analytic Functions was founded by [Franciszek Leja](wikipedia://en/1983106). Other notable members included [Kazimierz Żorawski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazimierz_Żorawski), [Władysław Ślebodziński](wikipedia://en/1983028), [Stanisław Gołąb](wikipedia://en/4499769), and [Czesław Olech](wikipedia://en/31633829).
