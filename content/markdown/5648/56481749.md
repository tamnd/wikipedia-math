# Barbara MacCluer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara MacCluer"}

**Barbara Diane MacCluer** is an American mathematician. She is a former professor of mathematics at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) and now a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) there. Her research specialty is in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and [composition operators](wikipedia://en/2702039). She is known for the books she has written on this subject and related areas of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

MacCluer is the daughter of George M. Richards, a research chemist and attorney. She is married to mathematician Thomas Kriete. She graduated from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) in 1975 and earned her Ph.D. there in 1983. Her dissertation, *Holomorphic Self-Maps of the Unit Ball: Iteration and Composition Operators*, was supervised by [Joel Shapiro](wikipedia://en/30792300).

She worked at the University of Virginia from 1983 to 1986, at the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina) from 1986 to 1987 and at the [University of Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Richmond) from 1987 to 1995. She returned to the University of Virginia in 1995.

## Books {#Books}

MacCluer is the author and editor of:

- *Composition Operators on Spaces of Analytic Functions* (CRC, 1995, with Carl C. Cowen)
- *Studies on Composition Operators* (American Mathematical Society, Contemporary Mathematics 213, 1998, edited with F. Jafari, C. Cowen, and A. D. Porter)
- *Elementary Functional Analysis* (Springer, [Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980) 253, 2009)
- *Differential Equations: Techniques, Theory, and Applications* (American Mathematical Society, 2020, with Paul S. Bourdon and Thomas L. Kriete)
