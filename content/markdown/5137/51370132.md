# M. Gweneth Humphreys Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. Gweneth Humphreys Award"}

The **M. Gweneth Humphreys Award** or **Humphreys Award** is a mathematics award established by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in recognition of mathematics educators who have exhibited outstanding mentorship.[^o2] The award is named for [Mabel Gweneth Humphreys](wikipedia://en/56307833) (1911-2006) who earned her Ph.D. at age 23 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1935.[^o3] She taught mathematics to women for her entire career, first at [Mount St. Scholastica College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_St._Scholastica_College), then for several years at [Sophie Newcomb College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Newcomb_College), and finally for over thirty years at [Randolph Macon Woman's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_Macon_Woman's_College). This award, funded by contributions from her former students and colleagues at Randolph-Macon Woman's College, recognizes her commitment to and her influence on undergraduate students of mathematics.[^o2]

:::
