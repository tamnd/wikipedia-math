# Glivenko's theorem (probability theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glivenko's theorem"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Glivenko's theorem** states that if $\varphi_n, n\in \mathbb N$, $\varphi$ are the characteristic functions of some [probability distributions](wikipedia://en/23543) $\mu_n, \mu$ respectively and $\varphi_n \to \varphi$ almost everywhere, then $\mu_n \to \mu$ in the sense of probability distributions.[^o3]

:::
