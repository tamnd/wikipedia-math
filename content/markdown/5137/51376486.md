# Peter Richtarik

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Richtarik"}

**Peter Richtarik** is a [Slovak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovaks) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist)[^o3] working in the area of [big data optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_data_optimization) and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), known for his work on [randomized coordinate descent algorithms](wikipedia://en/34432764), [stochastic gradient descent](wikipedia://en/1180641) and [federated learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federated_learning). He is currently a Professor of Computer Science at the [King Abdullah University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdullah_University_of_Science_and_Technology).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Richtarik earned a master's degree in mathematics from [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University), Slovakia, in 2001, graduating summa cum laude.[^o5] In 2007, he obtained a PhD in operations research from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), advised by Michael Jeremy Todd.[^o6][^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Between 2007 and 2009, he was a postdoctoral scholar in the Center for Operations Research and Econometrics and Department of Mathematical Engineering at [Universite catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universite_catholique_de_Louvain), Belgium, working with [Yurii Nesterov](wikipedia://en/42967707).[^o9][^o10] Between 2009 and 2019, Richtarik was a Lecturer and later Reader in the School of Mathematics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh). He is a Turing Fellow.[^o11] Richtarik founded and organizes a conference series entitled "Optimization and Big Data".[^o12][^o13]

### Academic work {#Academic_work}

Richtarik's early research concerned gradient-type methods, optimization in relative scale, sparse [principal component analysis](wikipedia://en/76340) and algorithms for [optimal design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_design). Since his appointment at Edinburgh, he has been working extensively on building algorithmic foundations of randomized methods in [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411), especially [randomized coordinate descent algorithms](wikipedia://en/34432764) and stochastic gradient descent methods. These methods are well suited for optimization problems described by [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) and have applications in fields such as [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324) and [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904).[^o15][^o16] Richtarik is the co-inventor of an algorithm generalizing the randomized [Kaczmarz method](wikipedia://en/2407650) for solving a [system of linear equations](wikipedia://en/113087), contributed to the invention of [federated learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federated_learning), and co-developed a stochastic variant of the [Newton's method](wikipedia://en/22145).
