# Radoslav Harman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Radoslav Harman"}

**Radoslav Harman** is a Slovak [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the area of [optimal design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_design) of statistical experiments. He is currently a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) at [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 2004, Harman obtained PhD in statistics from [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University), under the supervision of [Andrej Pazman](wikipedia://en/51379895).[^o5] He has published 30 research papers in the field of [optimal design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_design).[^o6]
