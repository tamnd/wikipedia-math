# Compound of two small stellated dodecahedra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of two small stellated dodecahedra"}

*Image: Compound of two small stellated dodecahedra.stl.* 3D model of a compound of two small stellated dodecahedra

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 2 [small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/3029843), in the same arrangement as in the [compound of 2 icosahedra](wikipedia://en/14749958).

:::

It is one of only five polyhedral compounds (along with the [compound of six tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/14810194), the [compound of two great dodecahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_two_great_dodecahedra), the [compound of five great dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14767416), and the [compound of five small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14767417)) which is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) and [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) but not [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive).

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654).
