In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **compound of two great icosahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It is composed of 2 [great icosahedra](wikipedia://en/3029898), in the same arrangement as in the [compound of 2 icosahedra](wikipedia://en/14749958).

:::

The triangles in this compound decompose into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) under action of the symmetry group: 16 of the triangles lie in coplanar pairs in [octahedral](wikipedia://en/80177635) planes, while the other 24 lie in unique planes.

The great icosahedron, as a uniform *retrosnub tetrahedron*

*Image: Retrosnub tetrahedron.png.*

, is similar to these snub-pair compounds: [compound of two icosahedra](wikipedia://en/14749958), [compound of two snub cubes](wikipedia://en/14797458) and [compound of two snub dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14797468).
