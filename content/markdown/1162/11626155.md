# Burt Totaro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Burt Totaro"}

**Burt James Totaro**, [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (b. 1967), is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

## Education and early life {#Education_and_early_life}

Totaro participated in the [Study of Mathematically Precocious Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_of_Mathematically_Precocious_Youth) while in grade school and enrolled at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1980 at the age of thirteen, becoming the youngest freshman in its history.[^o4] He scored a perfect 800 on the math portion and a 690 on the verbal portion of the [SAT-I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAT-I) exam at the age of 12.[^o4] He graduated in 1984 and went on to graduate school at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), receiving his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1989.

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Since 2009, he has been one of three managing editors of the journal *[Compositio Mathematica](wikipedia://en/31882322)*;[^o7] he is also on the editorial boards of [Forum of Mathematics, Pi and Sigma](wikipedia://en/36308727), the [Journal of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), and the [Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2668304). In 2012, he became a Professor in the UCLA Department of Mathematics.[^o8]

Totaro's work is influenced by the [Hodge conjecture](wikipedia://en/244518), and is based on the connections and application of topology to algebraic geometry. His work has applications in a number of diverse areas of mathematics, from [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) to [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and [group cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635).[^o14]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2000, he was elected [Lowndean Professor of Astronomy and Geometry](wikipedia://en/648990) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). In the same year, he was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o13]

In 2009, Totaro was elected [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o14] He was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to algebraic geometry, Lie theory and cohomology and their connections and for service to the profession".[^o15]
