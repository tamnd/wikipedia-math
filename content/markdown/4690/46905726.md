# Yair Minsky

*Image: Minsky yair.jpg.* Yair Minsky, in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) (2004)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yair Minsky"}

**Yair Nathan Minsky** (born in 1962) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerns [three-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774#Three_dimensions), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [holomorphic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_dynamics). He is a professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o2] He is known for having proved [Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190)'s [ending lamination conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ending_lamination_conjecture) along with Jeffrey Brock and [Richard Canary](wikipedia://en/46914909), and for results on the geometry of the [curve complex](wikipedia://en/51024643) obtained in collaboration with [Howard Masur](wikipedia://en/46960501).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Minsky obtained his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1989 under the supervision of [William Paul Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), with the thesis *Harmonic Maps and Hyperbolic Geometry*.

His Ph.D. students include [Jason Behrstock](wikipedia://en/45323078), [Erica Klarreich](wikipedia://en/48706788), Hossein Namazi and [Kasra Rafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasra_Rafi).[^o4]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Minsky received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 1995[^o6][^o7] and was an invited speaker at the 2006 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid.

He was named to the 2021 class of fellows of the American Mathematical Society "for contributions to hyperbolic 3-manifolds, low-dimensional topology, geometric group theory and Teichmuller theory".[^o8] He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2023.[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Minsky is the son of Naftaly Minsky, a professor of computer science at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), and the brother of Yaron Minsky, the CTO of [Jane Street Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Street_Capital).
