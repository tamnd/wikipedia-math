# LLT polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="LLT polynomial"}

In mathematics, an **LLT polynomial** is one of a family of [symmetric functions](wikipedia://en/22510359) introduced as [*q*-analogues](wikipedia://en/1619396) of products of [Schur functions](wikipedia://en/3147062).[^o2]

:::

J. Haglund, [M. Haiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Haiman), and N. Loehr showed how to expand [Macdonald polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_polynomial) in terms of LLT polynomials.[^o3] [Ian Grojnowski](wikipedia://en/1359175) and [Mark Haiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Haiman) proved a positivity conjecture for LLT polynomials that combined with the previous result implies the [Macdonald positivity conjecture](wikipedia://en/10354285#The_Macdonald_positivity_conjecture) for [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285), and extended the definition of LLT polynomials to arbitrary finite root systems.[^o4]
