# Sharadchandra Shankar Shrikhande

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sharadchandra Shankar Shrikhande"}

**Sharadchandra Shankar Shrikhande** (19 October 1917 – 21 April 2020) was an Indian mathematician with notable achievements in [combinatorial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_mathematics). He was notable for his breakthrough work along with [R. C. Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._C._Bose) and [E. T. Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._T._Parker) in their disproof of the famous conjecture made by [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) dated 1782 that there do not exist two mutually [orthogonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal) [latin squares](wikipedia://en/37447) of order 4n + 2 for any n.[^o3] Shrikhande's specialties were combinatorics and [statistical designs](wikipedia://en/9541). The [Shrikhande graph](wikipedia://en/13672415)[^o4] is used in statistical design.

:::

## Life, education and career {#Life,_education_and_career}

He was the fifth of ten siblings. His father worked at a flour mill.[^o6] He completed his B.Sc. from [Government Science College, Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Science,_Nagpur) and went for further studies at the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418).[^o9] He then briefly worked as a lecturer at the [Government Science College, Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Science,_Nagpur).[^o6]

Shrikhande received a Ph.D. in the year 1950 from the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) under the supervision of [Raj Chandra Bose](wikipedia://en/1162266). Shrikhande taught at various universities in the US and in India.[^o7] Shrikhande was a professor of mathematics at [Banaras Hindu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banaras_Hindu_University), Banaras, and the founding head of the department of mathematics, [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) and the founding director of the Center of Advanced Study in Mathematics, Mumbai until he retired in 1978. He was a fellow of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy), the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) and the Institute of Mathematical Statistics, USA.

In 1988, his wife Shakuntala died and he moved to the United States. Shrikhande returned to India in 2009.[^o6] He [turned 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian) in October 2017[^o8] and died in April 2020 at the age of 102.[^o9]

His son Mohan Shrikhande[^o10] is a professor of combinatorial mathematics at [Central Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Michigan_University) in [Mt. Pleasant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pleasant,_Michigan), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan).
