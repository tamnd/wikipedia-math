# Rhode Island statistical areas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhode Island statistical areas"}

The [U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [State of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhode_Island) currently has two [statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_area_%28United_States%29) that have been delineated by the [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget) (OMB). On July 21, 2023, the OMB delineated the [Providence-Warwick, RI-MA Metropolitan Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence-Warwick,_RI-MA_Metropolitan_Statistical_Area) and the [Boston-Worcester-Providence, MA-RI-NH Combined Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston-Worcester-Providence,_MA-RI-NH-CT_Combined_Statistical_Area), which are inclusive of all five of Rhode Island's counties.[^o2]
