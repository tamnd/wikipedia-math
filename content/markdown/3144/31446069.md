# Jean-Pierre Eckmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Eckmann"}

**Jean-Pierre Eckmann** (born 27 January 1944) is a Swiss [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist) in the department of [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva)[^o3] and a pioneer of [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295) and [social network analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_network).[^o4]

:::

Eckmann is the son of mathematician [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365).[^o5] He completed his PhD in 1970 under the supervision of Marcel Guenin at the University of Geneva. He has been a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) since 2001.[^o7] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He is also a member of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o9]

With [Pierre Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Collet_%28physicist%29) and [Oscar Lanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Lanford), Eckmann was the first to find a rigorous mathematical argument for the [universality](wikipedia://en/1752072) of [period-doubling bifurcations](wikipedia://en/4457659) in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), with scaling ratio given by the [Feigenbaum constants](wikipedia://en/79150).[^o10] In a highly cited 1985 review paper with [David Ruelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ruelle),[^o11] he bridged the contributions of mathematicians and physicists to [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986),[^o12] put the varied work on dimension-like notions in these fields on a firm mathematical footing,[^o13] and formulated the Eckmann–Ruelle conjecture on the dimension of hyperbolic ergodic measures, "one of the main problems in the interface of [dimension theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_theory) and dynamical systems".[^o14] A proof of the conjecture was finally published 14 years later, in 1999.[^o15] Eckmann has done additional mathematical work in very diverse fields such as [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

His PhD students have included [Viviane Baladi](wikipedia://en/48221297), [Pierre Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Collet_%28physicist%29), and [Martin Hairer](wikipedia://en/26920158).[^o6]
