# Alexis Bonnet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexis Bonnet"}

**Alexis Bonnet** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [investor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investor). For his research on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) he was awarded the 1996 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize).[^o2] He earned his doctorate from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1992, under supervision of [Henri Berestycki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Berestycki).

:::

Bonnet subsequently joined [Goldman Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldman_Sachs), and was one of the founders of the management company, [Methodology Asset Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology_Asset_Management), in 2005.
