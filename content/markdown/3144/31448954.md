# Bo'az Klartag

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bo'az Klartag"}

**Bo'az Klartag** (בועז קלרטג; born 25 April 1978) is an Israeli mathematician. He currently is a professor at the Weizmann Institute, and prior to that he was a professor at the Department of Pure Mathematics of [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), where he earned his doctorate under the supervision of [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman). Klartag made contributions in [asymptotic geometric analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_geometric_analysis) and won the 2008 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize),[^o4] as well as the 2010 [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize).[^o5] He is an editor of the [Journal d'Analyse Mathématique](wikipedia://en/71721837).[^o6]

:::
