# Ernst Hairer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Hairer"}

**Ernst Hairer** (born 19 June 1949)[^o3] is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) known for his work in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).

:::

His PhD was completed at the [University of Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Innsbruck).

He is the father of the mathematician [Martin Hairer](wikipedia://en/26920158), who won the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 2014.

Hairer is a member of the editorial boards for the journals *[Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation)*[^o5] and *[Journal of Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Scientific_Computing)*.[^o6]

He wrote, with others, *Solving Ordinary Differential Equations* (Hairer, Nørsett, Wanner) and *L'analyse au fil de l'histoire* (Hairer, Wanner).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Hairer holds a doctor honoris causa from the [University of Lund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lund).[^o8][^o9]

In 2003 he was awarded, jointly with [Gerhard Wanner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_Wanner), the [Peter Henrici Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Henrici_Prize) by the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o10]

In 2009 a conference on scientific computing and differential equations was held in honor of his 60th birthday, at the University of Geneva,[^o3] and in the same year the 7th International Conference in Numerical Analysis and Applied Mathematics in [Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crete) was dedicated in his honor.[^o11]

In 2009–2010 he was John-von-Neumann guest professor at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).
