# Boris Trakhtenbrot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Trakhtenbrot"}

**Boris (Boaz) Abramovich Trakhtenbrot** (Борис Авраамович Трахтенброт, בועז טרכטנברוט; 19 February 1921 – 19 September 2016) was a Russian-Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in [logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775), [theory of computation](wikipedia://en/30402), and [cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetics).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Trakhtenbrot was born into a Jewish family in [Brichevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tîrnova,_Dondușeni), northern [Bessarabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessarabia) (now Tîrnova, [Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldova)).[^o4] He studied at the [Moldovan State Pedagogical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Creangă_State_Pedagogical_University) in [Kishinev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chișinău), [Chernivtsi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernivtsi_University), and the [Ukrainian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Ukraine)'s Mathematical Institute, completing a Ph.D. at the latter institution in 1950.

He worked at [Akademgorodok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademgorodok), [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk) during the 1960s and 1970s.[^o5][^o6] In 1964 Trakhtenbrot discovered and proved a fundamental result in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) called the [gap theorem](wikipedia://en/2815277).[^o7] He also discovered and proved the theorem in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), and [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414) now known as [Trakhtenbrot's theorem](wikipedia://en/4232656).[^o8]

After immigrating to [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) in 1981, he became a professor in the Faculty of [Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_Science) at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), and continued as [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professors_in_the_United_States#Professor_emeritus_and_emerita) until his death. He died on 19 September 2016, at the age of 95.
