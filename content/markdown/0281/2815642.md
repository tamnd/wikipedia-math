# Algebra i Logika

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Algebra i Logika"}

***Algebra i Logika*** (English: *Algebra and Logic*) is a peer-reviewed [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) founded in 1962 by [Anatoly Ivanovich Malcev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoly_Ivanovich_Malcev), published by the [Siberian Fund for Algebra and Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberian_Fund_for_Algebra_and_Logic) at [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University).[^o3] An English translation of the journal is published by [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag) as ***Algebra and Logic*** since 1968.[^o4][^o5][^o6] It published papers presented at the meetings of the "Algebra and Logic" seminar at the [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University). The journal is edited by academician [Yury Yershov](wikipedia://en/2606710).

:::

The journal is reviewed cover-to-cover in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*.
