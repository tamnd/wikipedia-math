# AD+

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AD+"}

In [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), **AD<sup>\+</sup>** is an extension, proposed by [W.&nbsp;Hugh Woodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.&nbsp;Hugh_Woodin), to the [axiom of determinacy](wikipedia://en/1002300). The axiom, which is to be understood in the context of [ZF](wikipedia://en/152214) plus DC<sub>**R**</sub> (the [axiom of dependent choice](wikipedia://en/441950) for [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438)), states two things:

:::

1. Every [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of real numbers is [∞-Borel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-Borel).
1. For any [ordinal](wikipedia://en/26547932) *λ* \< [Θ](wikipedia://en/2818849), any *A* ⊆ ω<sup>ω</sup>, and any [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122) *π*: *λ*<sup>ω</sup> → ω<sup>ω</sup>, the [preimage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preimage) *π*<sup>−1</sup>\[A\] is [determined](wikipedia://en/2596030). (Here, *λ*<sup>ω</sup> is to be given the [product topology](wikipedia://en/24910), starting with the [discrete topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_topology) on *λ*.)

The second clause by itself is referred to as *ordinal determinacy*.
