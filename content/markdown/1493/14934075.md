# Volker Oppitz (scientist)

*Image: Volker Oppitz (scientist).jpg.* Volker Oppitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volker Oppitz"}

**Volker Oppitz** (born 6 December 1931 in [Nový Bor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nový_Bor) ([Haida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nový_Bor)), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia)) is a German [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Oppitz graduated in 1950 from the *Deutsche Müllerschule Dippoldiswalde (DMD)*, in 1952 from the School of Engineering [Dippoldiswalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dippoldiswalde) in mechanical and electrical engineering and in 1956 in economics at the [Dresden University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_University_of_Technology). In 1970 he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (*Dr. rer. oec.*) in the field of economics of industrial sectors at the Dresden University of Technology. In 1971 he became Assistant Professor at the Dresden University of Technology in Faculty of Economics. After making his [habilitation treatise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) (*Dr. habil.*) in 1981 at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) he became in 1987 [Associate Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Professor) in the Faculty of Economics of the Dresden University of Technology. In 1990 he was appointed to full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and head of the Department of [Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management), [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) and [Operations management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_management). In 1996 he retired and was still working as [Professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).

Since 1992 Oppitz was [CEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CEO) of *UBI Wirtschaftsberatung GmbH* in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden). Since 2001 he is academic director in sports management at the Europäisches Institut für postgraduale Bildung (EIPOS) at Dresden University of Technology in association with the [Deutscher Olympischer Sportbund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutscher_Olympischer_Sportbund). He lectures on the application of mathematics and statistics in management at EIPOS in Dresden.

In addition to his scientific work Volker Oppitz was between 2002 and 2005 Vice President of the [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_%28soccer%29) [club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_football_clubs_in_Germany) [Dynamo Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamo_Dresden), and since 2005 has been honorary chairman.[^o3] His son, also named [Volker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volker_Oppitz_%28footballer%29), is a player for the club.

## Work {#Work}

Oppitz deals mainly with draft mathematical models and their practical use. He pays special attention to the synthesis of mathematics and economics and practical solutions to the economic problems of corporate practice. Even the most difficult of financial models follow after his scientific explanation of the purpose, in its everyday use to maximize profits and minimize costs. The research activity of Prof. Oppitz includes [company management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management) and [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), management of [planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planning) and [organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization) of economics, [management processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_process) and [engineering management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_Management), [economic modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28economics%29), particularly in [financial/budgetary maths](wikipedia://en/33589680), [risk management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management), and [forecasting methods](wikipedia://en/246074).

Oppitz has published over 200 scientific publications, including more than 20 books and papers, numerous articles in journals, on patents and property rights.
