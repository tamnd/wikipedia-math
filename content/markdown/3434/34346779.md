# Dynamic contagion process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dynamic contagion process"}

In [applied probability](wikipedia://en/366719), a **dynamic contagion process** is a [point process](wikipedia://en/2147685) with stochastic intensity that generalises the [Hawkes process](wikipedia://en/61323423) and [Cox process](wikipedia://en/6196078) with exponentially decaying [shot noise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_noise) intensity.[^o2]

:::
