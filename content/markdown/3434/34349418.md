# Giuseppe Battaglini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giuseppe Battaglini"}

**Giuseppe Battaglini** (11 January 1826 – 29 April 1894) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

He studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the Scuola d'Applicazione di Ponti e Strade (School of Bridges and Roads) of Naples. In 1860 he was appointed professor of *Geometria superiore* at the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II). [Alfredo Capelli](wikipedia://en/29028258) and [Giovanni Frattini](wikipedia://en/8606878) were his [Laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) students.
