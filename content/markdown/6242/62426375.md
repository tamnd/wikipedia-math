# Marcia Groszek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcia Groszek"}

**Marcia Jean Groszek** is an American mathematician whose research concerns [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205), and [recursion theory](wikipedia://en/155414). She is a professor of mathematics at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College).

:::

## Education {#Education}

As a high school student, Groszek felt isolated for her interest in mathematics, but she found a sense of community through her participation in the Hampshire College Summer Mathematics Program, and she went on to earn her bachelor's degree at [Hampshire College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire_College). She completed her Ph.D. in 1981 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). Her dissertation, *Iterated Perfect Set Forcing and Degrees of Constructibility*, was supervised by [Akihiro Kanamori](wikipedia://en/22363114).

## Research {#Research}

With [Theodore Slaman](wikipedia://en/5693320), Groszek showed that (if they exist at all) [non-constructible real numbers](wikipedia://en/343335) must be widespread, in the sense that every [perfect set](wikipedia://en/5075551) contains one of them, and they asked analogous questions of the [non-computable real numbers](wikipedia://en/6206).C With Slaman, she has also shown that the existence of a maximally independent set of [Turing degrees](wikipedia://en/764405), of cardinality less than the [cardinality of the continuum](wikipedia://en/1574901), is independent of [ZFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZFC).A

In the theory of [ordinal definable sets](wikipedia://en/14566906), an unordered pair of sets is said to be a Groszek–Laver pair if the pair is ordinal definable but neither of its two elements is; this concept is named for Groszek and [Richard Laver](wikipedia://en/22280145), who observed the existence of such pairs in certain models of set theory.B

## Service and outreach {#Service_and_outreach}

Groszek was program chair of the 2014 North American annual meeting of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438). Her interest in logic extends to education as well as to research; she has participated in the Association for Symbolic Logic Committee on Logic Education, and in 2011 she was co-organizer of an Association for Symbolic Logic special session on "Logic in the Undergraduate Mathematics Curriculum". With mathematics colleague [Dorothy Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_Wallace) and performance artist [Josh Kornbluth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Kornbluth), Groszek has also helped write and produce a sequence of educational videos about mathematics.
