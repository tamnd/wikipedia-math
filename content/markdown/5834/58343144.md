# Nissan Deliatitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nissan Deliatitz"}

**Nissan ben Avraham Deliatitz** (ניסן בן אברהם דעליאטיץ) was a 19th-century [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) [rabbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He wrote *Keneh Ḥokhmah*, a manual of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) in five parts, published in [Vilna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius) and [Grodno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grodno) in 1829.[^o2] The work received [approbations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haskama) from Rabbi David, the *[av beit din](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Av_beit_din)* of [Novhardok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navahrudak), and Rabbi Avraham Abele ben Avraham Shlomo Poswoler, an eminent scholar who headed the Vilna *[beit din](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_din)*.[^o3]
