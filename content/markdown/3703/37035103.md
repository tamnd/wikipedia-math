# Catalan's minimal surface

*Image: Catalan's Minimal Surface.png.* Catalan's minimal surface.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catalan's minimal surface"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Catalan's minimal surface** is a [minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734) originally studied by [Eugène Charles Catalan](wikipedia://en/639553) in 1855.[^o2]

:::

It has the special property of being the minimal surface that contains a [cycloid](wikipedia://en/41638) as a [geodesic](wikipedia://en/91096). It is also [*swept out*](wikipedia://en/8700856) by a family of [parabolae](wikipedia://en/23231).[^o3]

The surface has the mathematical characteristics exemplified by the following [parametric equation](wikipedia://en/576108):[^o4]

$$\begin{aligned} x(u,v) &= u - \sin(u)\cosh(v)\\ y(u,v) &= 1 - \cos(u)\cosh(v)\\ z(u,v) &= 4 \sin(u/2) \sinh(v/2) \end{aligned}$$
