# Robert Richard Anstice

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Richard Anstice"}

**Robert Richard Anstice** (1813–1853) was an English clergyman and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who wrote two remarkable papers on [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170),[^o4] published the same year he died in the [Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_and_Dublin_Mathematical_Journal). He pioneered the use of [primitive roots](wikipedia://en/186864) in this field, anticipating the work of [Eugen Netto](wikipedia://en/12894892) on [Steiner's triplets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steiner_triple_system).

:::

Anstice studied at [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford),[^o5] where he graduated in 1835, receiving a Master's in 1837. Nothing is known about his life in the next ten years. In 1846, he was ordained priest, and in the following year he became rector of [Wigginton, Hertfordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigginton,_Hertfordshire).[^o6] He died there in 1853, and is buried, under a cast iron tombstone, in the churchyard at [St Michael's Church, Madeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Michael's_Church,_Madeley), Shropshire.[^o2]
