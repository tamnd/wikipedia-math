# Ljubisa D.R. Kocinac

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ljubisa D.R. Kocinac"}

**Ljubisa Dragi Rosanda Kocinac** (born in [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia) in January 1947) is a mathematician and currently a Professor Emeritus at the [University of Niš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Niš), [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He completed his PhD, focused on [cardinal functions](wikipedia://en/14189688), at the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) in 1983, under the supervision of [Đuro Kurepa](wikipedia://en/3130558). Kocinac has published over 160 papers and four books in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and [fields of sets](wikipedia://en/1018676).[^o4][^o5]

He has actively promoted research on [selection principles](wikipedia://en/50398478), as a fruitful collaborator and as an organizer of the first conferences in a series of international workshops titled *Coverings, Selections and Games in Topology*. The fourth of this series, held in [Caserta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caserta), Italy, in June 2012 was dedicated to him on the occasion of his sixty-fifth birthday.[^o5]

His research interests include aspects of topology, especially [selection principles](wikipedia://en/50398478), [topological games](wikipedia://en/15334997) and [coverings](wikipedia://en/317552) of topological spaces, and mathematical analysis. In particular, he introduced star [selection principles](wikipedia://en/50398478). He is also known for his wit. When asked "Would you like some wine?", Kočinac replied "Only if you insist. (Pause) Please insist.".[^o5][^o6]
