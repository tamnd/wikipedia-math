# David Ben-Zvi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Ben-Zvi"}

**David Dror Ben-Zvi** (born 1974) is an American mathematician. He is currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

David Ben-Zvi was born in the San Francisco Bay Area, and grew up in [Rehovot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehovot), Israel, and [Setauket, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setauket,_New_York). He graduated a valedictorian from [Ward Melville High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_Melville_High_School) and was a finalist in the [Westinghouse Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regeneron_Science_Talent_Search). He then graduated *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)* from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).

Ben-Zvi earned his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1999, with a dissertation titled *Spectral Curves, Opers And Integrable Systems* supervised by [Edward Frenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Frenkel). In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- Fellow of the American Mathematical Society, elected in inaugural class, 2012.
- Plenary Lecture, AMS Central Sectional Meeting, Baylor University, 2009.
- [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) Invited Lecture Series, Oxford, 2007.
- [Princeton University Department of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Department_of_Mathematics) Undergraduate Prizes, 1993 Brown Prize, 1994 Covington Prize, 1994 Miller Prize, Barry M. Goldwater Scholarship, 1993-1994.
