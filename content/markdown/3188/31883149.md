# Regev's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regev's theorem"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), **Regev's theorem**, proved by [Amitai Regev](wikipedia://en/25459593),[^n1][^n2] states that the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of two [PI algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PI_algebra) is a PI algebra.

:::
