# Compositio Mathematica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Compositio Mathematica"}

***Compositio Mathematica*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) established by [L.E.J. Brouwer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luitzen_Egbertus_Jan_Brouwer) in 1935.[^o3] It is owned by the Foundation Compositio Mathematica, and since 2004 it has been published on behalf of the Foundation by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in partnership with [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 2-year [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.456 and a 2020 5-year impact factor of 1.696.[^o4]

:::

The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Fabrizio Andreatta, David Holmes, Bruno Klingler, and Éric Vasserot.[^o5]

## Early history {#Early_history}

The journal was established by L. E. J. Brouwer in response to his dismissal from *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)* in 1928.[^o7] An announcement of the new journal was made in a 1934 issue of the *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)*.[^o8] In 1940, the publication of the journal was suspended due to the [German occupation of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Netherlands_%281939–1945%29).[^o7]
