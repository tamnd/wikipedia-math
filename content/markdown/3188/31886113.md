# The Mathematical Magpie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Mathematical Magpie"}

***The Mathematical Magpie*** is an [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology) published in 1962, compiled by [Clifton Fadiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_Fadiman) as a companion volume to his *[Fantasia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/32804892)* (1958).[^o3] The volume contains stories, cartoons, essays, rhymes, music, anecdotes, aphorisms, and other oddments. Authors include [Arthur C. Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_C._Clarke), [Isaac Asimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Asimov), [Mark Twain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Twain), [Lewis Carroll](wikipedia://en/26586083), and many other renowned figures. A revised edition was issued in 1981 and again in 1997. Although out of print, it is recommended for undergraduate mathematics libraries by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) as part of their Basic Library List.[^o4]

:::
