# Thomas Banchoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Banchoff"}

*Image: Thomas Banchoff.jpg.* Thomas Banchoff at Berkeley in 1973

**Thomas Francis Banchoff** (born April 7, 1938) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He is a professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where he has taught since 1967. He is known for his research in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) in three and four dimensions, for his efforts to develop methods of [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics) in the early 1990s, and most recently for his pioneering work in methods of undergraduate education utilizing online resources.

:::

Banchoff graduated from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in 1960, receiving his B.A. in Mathematics, and received his Masters and Ph.D. from [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1962 and 1964, where he was a student of [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905). Before going to Brown he taught at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam).

In 1996 he received the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o3]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4] In addition, he was a president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o5]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- with Stephen Lovett: Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces (2nd edition), A. K. Peters 2010
- with [Terence Gaffney](wikipedia://en/40737298), Clint McCrory: Cusps of Gauss Mappings, Pitman 1982
- with [John Wermer](wikipedia://en/47568103): [Linear Algebra through Geometry](https://books.google.com/books/about/Linear_Algebra_Through_Geometry.html?id=TIWqH-j4PVgC), Springer Verlag 1983
- Beyond the third dimension: geometry, computer graphics, and higher dimensions, Scientific American Library, Freeman 1990
- [*Triple points and surgery of immersed surfaces.*](http://www.ams.org/journals/proc/1974-046-03/S0002-9939-1974-0377897-1/S0002-9939-1974-0377897-1.pdf) Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 46 (1974), 407–413. (concerning the number of triple points of immersed surfaces in $R^3$.)
- *Critical points and curvature for embedded polyhedra.* Journal of Differential Geometry 1 (1967), 245–256. (Theorem of Gauß-Bonnet for Polyhedra)
