# Claire Miller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claire Miller"}

**Claire Miller** (née **Claire Ann Ferguson**) is a Scottish [environmental statistician](wikipedia://en/8919339), a professor of statistics in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow), and a former president of the International Environmetrics Society. Her research focuses on statistical models of [water quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_quality).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Miller was a student at the University of Glasgow, where she received first class honours in mathematics and statistics in 2003, and continued for a Ph.D. in 2007. Her dissertation, *Univariate and multivariate statistical methodologies for lake ecosystem modelling*, was jointly supervised by Adrian Bowman and [Marian Scott](wikipedia://en/56634364).

She has been a member of the academic staff at the University of Glasgow since 2007.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Miller became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2015, and belonged to the [Young Academy of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Academy_of_Scotland) of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) from 2016 to 2021. She served as president of the International Environmetrics Society from 2023 to 2025.

She was the 2018 recipient of the Abdel El-Shaarawi Early Investigator's Award of the International Environmetrics Society, "for outstanding contributions to environmental statistical and quantitative research with a special focus on water quality, including functional data, [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) clustering and dimension reduction for spatiotemporal flow-connected network data". In 2025 she received the Barnett Award of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) for "exceptional contributions to environmental statistics" and "pioneering work in water quality measuring, monitoring and forecasting".
