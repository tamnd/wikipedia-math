# Hester Bijl

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hester Bijl"}

**Hester Bijl** is a Dutch mathematician and since 15 January 2026 the [rector magnificus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_magnificus) of [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology)[^o3]. From 2021 until 2026 she was the [rector magnificus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_magnificus) of [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Bijl received a PhD from the [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology), along with a master's degree from [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).[^o4]

In 1999, she started working the Delft University of Technology in the aerodynamics department.[^o9] In 2013, she became dean of the faculty of [aerospace engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering) at the Delft University of Technology.[^o6] In 2021, Bijl was appointed rector magnificus of Leiden University, succeeding [Carel Stolker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carel_Stolker).[^o6] In 2024, she was reappointed.[^o7][^o10] She is the first female rector of the university.[^o11]

Bijl is on the supervisory board of the [Netherlands Organisation for Applied Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Organisation_for_Applied_Scientific_Research) and on the board of the [Leiden Bio Science Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_Bio_Science_Park).[^o4] She has been a visiting researcher at the [NASA Langley Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley_Research_Center).[^o4]
