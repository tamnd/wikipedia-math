# Muhabbat Sharapova

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Muhabbat Sharapova"}

**Muhabbat Arapovna Sharapova** is a mathematician from [Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan).[^o2][^o3] She has received the title O‘zbekiston Qahramoni ([Hero of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Uzbekistan)).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sharapova has a page on the Uzbekistani government's Republican Center of Spirituality and Enlightenment (Respublika Ma'naviyat va Ma'rifat Markazi).[^o7] According to the page, which provides most of the information for her early life, Sharapova was born in 1949 in [Shahrisabz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrisabz) as one of eight children. Her mother had dreamed of teaching and pushed Sharapova to become a math teacher. In 1971, she graduated from [Samarkand State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarkand_State_University) and began to teach at a school in [Karshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qarshi), which became known for its mathematics.[^o7]

In 2000, Sharapova started teaching mathematics at a specialized boarding school in Karshi, [Kashkadarya region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qashqadaryo_Region).[^o4][^o7] In 2007, she received a national award as an Honored Worker in Public Education.[^o3] In 2016, she was made a Hero of Uzbekistan. According to the Uzbekistani embassy in Ukraine, this award is given to honor "significant achievements before the state and the people."[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Sharapova was recognized as one of the BBC's 100 Women of 2017, which called her "one of the best mathematicians in Uzbekistan."[^o9]
