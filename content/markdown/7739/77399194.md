# Loukas Grafakos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Loukas Grafakos"}

**Loukas Grafakos** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Λουκάς Γραφάκος) is a Greek mathematician working in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). He earned his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), in 1989, under the guidance of [Michael Christ](wikipedia://en/58516365).[^o3] Grafakos is a Curators Distinguished Professor and holds the Mahala and Rose Houchins Distinguished Chair at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri).[^o4]

:::

Grafakos' research interests include [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659), [singular integrals](wikipedia://en/12791220), and [Calderón–Zygmund theory](wikipedia://en/12288578). He is well known for his contributions to the area of multilinear harmonic analysis.[^o5] Grafakos has been supported by the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) and was a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) in Mathematics during the academic year 2022-2023.[^o6]

His monographs, “Classical Fourier Analysis”,[^o7] “Modern Fourier Analysis”,[^o8] and “Fundamentals of Fourier Analysis”,[^o9] are widely used as references and as textbooks and provide comprehensive treatment of the subject.

He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.[^o10]
