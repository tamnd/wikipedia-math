# Yoichi Miyaoka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yoichi Miyaoka"}

**Yoichi Miyaoka** is a mathematician who works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and who proved (independently of [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137)'s work) the [Bogomolov&ndash;Miyaoka&ndash;Yau inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogomolov&ndash;Miyaoka&ndash;Yau_inequality) in an [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae) paper.[^o2]

:::

In 1984, Miyaoka extended the [Bogomolov&ndash;Miyaoka&ndash;Yau inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogomolov&ndash;Miyaoka&ndash;Yau_inequality) to surfaces with quotient singularities, and in 2008 to [orbifold](wikipedia://en/293504) surfaces. Doing so, he obtains sharp bound on the number of quotient singularities on surfaces of general type. Moreover, the inequality for orbifold surfaces gives explicit values for the coefficients of the so-called [Lang-Vojta conjecture](wikipedia://en/34682455) relating the degree of a curve on a surface with its [geometric genus](wikipedia://en/3115807).
