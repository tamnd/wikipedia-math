# Noether inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noether inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Noether inequality**, named after [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337), is a property of [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) minimal [complex surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_surface) that restricts the topological type of the underlying topological [4-manifold](wikipedia://en/1362795). It holds more generally for minimal projective surfaces of general type over an algebraically closed field.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Formulation of the inequality"}

## Formulation of the inequality {#Formulation_of_the_inequality}

Let *X* be a smooth [minimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_model_%28birational_geometry%29) [projective](wikipedia://en/248808#Projective_varieties) [surface of general type](wikipedia://en/2368356) defined over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) (or a smooth minimal compact complex surface of general type) with canonical divisor *K* = −*c*<sub>1</sub>(*X*), and let *p*<sub>g</sub> = *h*<sup>0</sup>(*K*) be the dimension of the space of holomorphic two forms, then

$$p_g \le \frac{1}{2} c_1(X)^2 + 2.$$

For complex surfaces, an alternative formulation expresses this inequality in terms of topological invariants of the underlying real oriented four manifold. Since a surface of general type is a [Kähler](wikipedia://en/390538) surface, the dimension of the maximal positive subspace in intersection form on the second cohomology is given by *b*<sub>\+</sub> = 1 + 2*p*<sub>g</sub>. Moreover, by the [Hirzebruch signature theorem](wikipedia://en/7192444) *c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup> (*X*) = 2*e* + 3*σ*, where *e* = *c*<sub>2</sub>(*X*) is the topological [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) and *σ* = *b*<sub>\+</sub> − *b*<sub>−</sub> is the signature of the [intersection form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_form_%284-manifold%29). Therefore, the Noether inequality can also be expressed as

$$b_+ \le 2 e + 3 \sigma + 5$$

or equivalently using *e* = 2 – 2 *b*<sub>1</sub> + *b*<sub>\+</sub> + *b*<sub>−</sub>

$$b_- + 4 b_1 \le 4b_+ + 9.$$

Combining the Noether inequality with the [Noether formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_formula) 12χ=*c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>+*c*<sub>2</sub> gives

$$5 c_1(X)^2 - c_2(X) + 36 \ge 12q$$

where *q* is the [irregularity of a surface](wikipedia://en/22857258), which leads to a slightly weaker inequality, which is also often called the Noether inequality:

$$5 c_1(X)^2 - c_2(X) + 36 \ge 0 \quad (c_1^2(X)\text{ even})$$

$$5 c_1(X)^2 - c_2(X) + 30 \ge 0 \quad (c_1^2(X)\text{ odd}).$$

Surfaces where equality holds (i.e. on the Noether line) are called [Horikawa surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_general_type#Horikawa_surfaces).

:::

::: {#o5 .proof cue=heading name="Proof sketch"}

## Proof sketch {#Proof_sketch}

It follows from the minimal general type condition that *K*<sup>2</sup> > 0. We may thus assume that *p*<sub>g</sub> > 1, since the inequality is otherwise automatic. In particular, we may assume there is an effective divisor *D* representing *K*. We then have an exact sequence

$$0 \to H^0(\mathcal{O}_X) \to H^0(K) \to H^0( K|_D) \to H^1(\mathcal{O}_X) \to$$

so $p_g - 1 \le h^0(K|_D).$

Assume that *D* is smooth. By the [adjunction formula](wikipedia://en/3118117) *D* has a canonical linebundle $\mathcal{O}_D(2K)$, therefore $K|_D$ is a [special divisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_divisor) and the [Clifford inequality](wikipedia://en/2980541) applies, which gives

$$h^0(K|_D) - 1 \le \frac{1}{2} \deg_D(K) = \frac{1}{2} K^2.$$

In general, essentially the same argument applies using a more general version of the Clifford inequality for local complete intersections with a dualising line bundle and 1-dimensional sections in the trivial line bundle. These conditions are satisfied for the curve *D* by the adjunction formula and the fact that *D* is numerically connected.

:::
