# Geoffrey Grimmett

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Grimmett"}

**Geoffrey Richard Grimmett** (born 20 December 1950) is an English mathematician known for his work on the mathematics of random systems arising in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542)[^o5][^o6][^o7] and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), especially [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805)[^o8] and the [contact process](wikipedia://en/5097569).[^o9] He is the [Professor of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Statistics,_University_of_Cambridge) in the [Statistical Laboratory, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge), and was the [Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_%28college%29) of [Downing College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downing_College,_Cambridge), from 2013 to 2018.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Grimmett was educated at [King Edward's School, Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Edward's_School,_Birmingham) and [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford). He graduated in 1971, and completed his [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1974[^o13] under the supervision of [John Hammersley](wikipedia://en/656531) and [Dominic Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Welsh).

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Grimmett served as the [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) [Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Fellow) at [New College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford), from 1974 to 1976 before moving to the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol).[^o4][^o16] He was appointed [Professor of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Statistics,_University_of_Cambridge) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1992, becoming a fellow of [Churchill College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_College,_Cambridge).[^o17] He was Director of the [Statistical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge) from 1994 to 2000, Head of the [Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Pure_Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Statistics) (DPMMS) from 2002 to 2007, and is a trustee of the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize).[^o18]

Grimmett is particularly recognised for his achievements in the rigorous theory of disordered physical systems.[^o25] Especially influential is his work on and around [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805), the contact model for stochastic spatial epidemics, and the random-cluster model, a class that includes the [Ising](wikipedia://en/292744)/[Potts models](wikipedia://en/1162226) of [ferromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferromagnetism).[^o25] His monograph on percolation is a standard work in a core area of probability, and is widely cited.[^o25] His breadth within probability is emphasized by his important contributions to probabilistic [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and probabilistic [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o25]

In October 2013 he was appointed [Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_%28college%29) of [Downing College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downing_College,_Cambridge), succeeding [Barry Everitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Everitt_%28scientist%29).[^o19] He ended his term as Master on 30 September 2018, being replaced by [Alan Bookbinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Bookbinder).[^o20]

He was appointed Chair of the [Heilbronn Institute for Mathematical Research](wikipedia://en/61922775) in September 2020.[^o21] He was succeeded by [Catherine Hobbs](wikipedia://en/58256072) on September 1 2023.[^o22]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Grimmett was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davidson_Prize) in 1989[^o24] and elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (FRS) in 2014.[^o25]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Grimmett is the son of Benjamin J Grimmett and Patricia W (Lewis) Grimmett.[^o27]

He competed at the [1976 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Summer_Olympics) in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal) as a member of the [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain) Men's [Foil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foil_%28fencing%29) Team, finishing 6th.[^o28]
