# Frank-Olaf Schreyer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank-Olaf Schreyer"}

*Image: Schreyer eisenbud harris.jpg.* [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845) (left), Frank-Olaf Schreyer (middle), [Joseph Daniel Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Daniel_Harris) (right), [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2006

**Frank-Olaf Schreyer** is a German mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and algorithmic algebraic geometry.

:::

Schreyer received in 1983 his PhD from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) with thesis *Syzgies of Curves with Special Pencils* under the supervision of [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845). Schreyer was a professor at [University of Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bayreuth) and is since 2002 a professor at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University).

He is involved in the development of (algorithmic) algebraic geometry advanced by David Eisenbud. Much of Schreyer's research deals with [syzygy theory](wikipedia://en/1280843) and the development of algorithms for the calculation of syzygies.

In 2010 he was an invited speaker (jointly with David Eisenbud) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad).[^o3] In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
