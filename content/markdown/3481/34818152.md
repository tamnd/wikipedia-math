# Federer–Morse theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Federer–Morse theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Federer–Morse theorem**, introduced by (txt), states that if *f* is a [surjective](wikipedia://en/27873) [continuous map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_map) from a [compact metric space](wikipedia://en/6042#Metric_spaces) *X* to a compact metric space *Y*, then there is a [Borel subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_subset) *Z* of *X* such that *f* restricted to *Z* is a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) from *Z* to *Y*.[^o3] Moreover, the inverse of that restriction is a Borel [section](wikipedia://en/10630303) of *f*—it is a [Borel isomorphism](wikipedia://en/33605690).[^o4]

:::
