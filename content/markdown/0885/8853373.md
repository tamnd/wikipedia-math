# Simplicial polytope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simplicial polytope"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **simplicial polytope** is a [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471) whose [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29) are all [simplices](wikipedia://en/39781). It is [topologically dual](wikipedia://en/8815#Topological_duality) to [simple polytopes](wikipedia://en/8853286). Polytopes that are both simple and simplicial are either [simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplices) or two-dimensional [polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygons).

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

In the case of a three-dimensional simplicial polytope, known as the simplicial polyhedron, the polytope contains only triangular faces of any type.[^o4] These polyhedra include [bipyramids](wikipedia://en/4153), [gyroelongated bipyramids](wikipedia://en/5506771), [deltahedra](wikipedia://en/493995) (wherein the faces are equilateral triangles, and [Kleetope](wikipedia://en/31165912) of polyhedra. The simplicial polyhedron corresponds via [Steinitz's theorem](wikipedia://en/19762817) to a [maximal planar graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximal_planar_graph).

For a simplicial tiling, examples are [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) and [Laves tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laves_tiling).

Simplicial [4-polytopes](wikipedia://en/24979) include:

- [convex regular 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_regular_4-polytope)
  - [4-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentachoron), [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447)
- Dual [convex uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/2961):
  - [Disphenoid tetrahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedral_honeycomb)
  - Dual of [cantitruncated cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantitruncated_cubic_honeycomb)
  - Dual of [omnitruncated cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_cubic_honeycomb)
  - Dual of [cantitruncated alternated cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantitruncated_alternated_cubic_honeycomb)

Simplicial higher polytope families:

- [simplex](wikipedia://en/39781)
- [cross-polytope](wikipedia://en/716401) (Orthoplex)
