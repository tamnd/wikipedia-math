# Malliavin derivative

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malliavin derivative"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Malliavin derivative**[^o2] is a notion of [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) in the [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875). Intuitively, it is the notion of derivative appropriate to paths in [classical Wiener space](wikipedia://en/6810363), which are "usually" not differentiable in the usual sense.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $H$ be the [Cameron–Martin space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron–Martin_space), and $C_{0}$ denote [classical Wiener space](wikipedia://en/6810363):

$$H := \{ f \in W^{1,2} ([0, T]; \mathbb{R}^{n}) \;|\; f(0) = 0 \} := \{ \text{paths starting at 0 with first derivative in } L^{2} \};$$

$$C_{0} := C_{0} ([0, T]; \mathbb{R}^{n}) := \{ \text{continuous paths starting at 0} \};$$

By the [Sobolev embedding theorem](wikipedia://en/4665038#Sobolev_embedding_theorem), $H \subset C_0$. Let

$$i : H \to C_{0}$$

denote the [inclusion map](wikipedia://en/492516).

Suppose that $F : C_{0} \to \mathbb{R}$ is [Fréchet differentiable](wikipedia://en/2735727). Then the [Fréchet derivative](wikipedia://en/2735727) is a map

$$\mathrm{D} F : C_{0} \to \mathrm{Lin} (C_{0}; \mathbb{R});$$

i.e., for paths $\sigma \in C_{0}$, $\mathrm{D} F (\sigma)\;$ is an element of $C_{0}^{*}$, the [dual space](wikipedia://en/7988) to $C_{0}\;$. Denote by $\mathrm{D}_{H} F(\sigma)\;$ the [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) [linear map](wikipedia://en/18102) $H \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by

$$\mathrm{D}_{H} F (\sigma) := \mathrm{D} F (\sigma) \circ i : H \to \mathbb{R},$$

sometimes known as the [*H*-derivative](wikipedia://en/6599701). Now define $\nabla_{H} F : C_{0} \to H$ to be the [adjoint](wikipedia://en/667175) of $\mathrm{D}_{H} F\;$ in the sense that

$$\int_0^T \left(\partial_t \nabla_H F(\sigma)\right) \cdot \partial_t h := \langle \nabla_{H} F (\sigma), h \rangle_{H} = \left( \mathrm{D}_{H} F \right) (\sigma) (h) = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{F (\sigma + t i(h)) - F(\sigma)}{t}.$$

Then the **Malliavin derivative** $\mathrm{D}_{t}$ is defined by

$$\left( \mathrm{D}_{t} F \right) (\sigma) := \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left( \left( \nabla_{H} F \right) (\sigma) \right).$$

The [domain](wikipedia://en/50263) of $\mathrm{D}_{t}$ is the set $\mathbf{F}$ of all Fréchet differentiable real-valued functions on $C_{0}\;$; the [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264) is $L^{2} ([0, T]; \mathbb{R}^{n})$.

The **Skorokhod integral** $\delta\;$ is defined to be the [adjoint](wikipedia://en/667175#Adjoint_of_densely_defined_unbounded_operators_between_Hilbert_spaces) of the Malliavin derivative:

$$\delta := \left( \mathrm{D}_{t} \right)^{*} : \operatorname{image} \left( \mathrm{D}_{t} \right) \subseteq L^{2} ([0, T]; \mathbb{R}^{n}) \to \mathbf{F}^{*} = \mathrm{Lin} (\mathbf{F}; \mathbb{R}).$$
