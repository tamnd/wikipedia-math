# Resolution theorem (algebraic K-theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Resolution theorem"}

In [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500), **Quillen's resolution theorem** states that if $\mathcal{A}\subset\mathcal{C}$ is an exact [subcategory](wikipedia://en/310959) where $\mathcal{A}$ is an extension-closed subcategory of $\mathcal{C}$, which is also closed under taking kernels of admissible surjections, and has a finite resolution by objects in $\mathcal{A}$; then the inclusion $\mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ induces a [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) equivalence of their K-theory spectra $K_0(\mathcal{A})\simeq~K_0(\mathcal{C})$.[^o2]

:::
