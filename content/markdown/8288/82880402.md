# Poncelet map

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poncelet map"}

A **poncelet map** is an [algebraic dynamical system](wikipedia://en/9087#Algebraic_dynamical_system) defined by two [conics](wikipedia://en/19008673)[^o2] and the following procedure:

:::

1. Pick a starting point on the outer conic
1. Draw a line from the point that is tangent to the inner conic
1. This line intersects the outer conic at a new point
1. Repeat the process from step 2

Given these are algebraic curves and these are relations of intersections and tangents, the map can be defined purely by algebraic equations and therefore is an algebraic dynamical system.[^o3]

One important property is the [Poncelet's closure theorem](wikipedia://en/2412120).[^o4]
