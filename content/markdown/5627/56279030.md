# Boris Mityagin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Mityagin"}

*Image: Boris Mityagin, 2010 (cropped).jpg.* Boris Mityagin, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2010

**Boris Samuel Mityagin** (Борис Самуилович Митягин, born 12 August 1937, [Voronezh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh))[^o2] is a Russian-American mathematician.

:::

Mityagin received in 1961 his candidate degree (Ph.D.) under [Georgiy Shilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgiy_Shilov) at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and in 1963 his Russian doctorate (higher doctoral degree). He was a researcher at the [Central Economic Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Economic_Mathematical_Institute) of the [Soviet Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1979 he became a professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).

His research deals with functional analysis and mathematical physics (spectra of Schrödinger and Dirac operators).

In 1966 in Moscow he was (with [A. Pełczyński](wikipedia://en/56285756)) an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o4]

In 1960 he received the prize of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251). In 2013 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
