# Ildar Ibragimov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ildar Ibragimov"}

Abdulovich

*Image: Academician Ildar Ibragimov.jpg.* Ildar Abdulovich Ibragimov in 2015

**Ildar Abdulovich Ibragimov** (Ильдар Абдулович Ибрагимов, born 15 July 1932, [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg)) is a Russian mathematician, specializing in probability theory and mathematical statistics.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ibragimov is the son of a father who was an engineer with [Bashkir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkirs) ancestry and a mother who was a physician from a [Tatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars) family with origins in Kazan. Ildar Ibragimov studied at [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University), where he graduated in mathematics in 1956. He received in 1960 his Russian candidate degree (Ph.D.) under [Yuri Linnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Linnik) and in 1967 his Russian doctorate (higher doctoral degree). In 1969 he became a professor of probability at Leningrad State University.[^o5]

He is a senior scientist and director of the laboratory of statistical methods at the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg), a position he has held there since 1972 as the successor to Yuri Linnik. Ibragimov was elected in 1990 a corresponding member and in 1997 a full member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1970 he received the [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize). In 1989 he was the Wald Lecturer of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). In 1966 in Moscow he was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o6]

His doctoral students include [Taivo Arak](wikipedia://en/57962075) and [Boris Tsirelson](wikipedia://en/1661441).[^o4]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- with Linnik: *Independent and stationary sequences of random variables*, Groningen, Wolters-Noordhoff 1971
- with Y. A. Rozanov: *Gaussian Random Processes*, Springer Verlag 1978[^o8]
- with R. Z. Hasminskii: *Statistical estimation, asymptotic theory*, Springer Verlag 1981[^o9]
- as editor with A. Yu. Zaitzev: *Probability theory and mathematical statistics*, Gordon and Breach 1996
- as editor with N. Balakrishnan, V. B. Nevzorov: *Asymptotic methods in probability and statistics with applications*, Birkhäuser 2001
