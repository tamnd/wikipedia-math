::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mirsky"}

- [Alfred Mirsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Mirsky) (1900–1974), American molecular biologist
- [Boris Mirkin-Getzevich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Mirkin-Getzevich) (1892–1955), better known by his pen name Boris Mirsky
- [D. S. Mirsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._S._Mirsky) (1890–1939), Russian historian and critic
- [Eytan Mirsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eytan_Mirsky) (born 1961), American singer-songwriter
- [Jeannette Mirsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeannette_Mirsky) (1903–1987), American writer

- [Mark Jay Mirsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Jay_Mirsky) (born 1939), American novelist
