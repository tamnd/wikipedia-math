# Eisenstein integral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eisenstein integral"}

In mathematical [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), the **Eisenstein integral** is an integral introduced by [Harish-Chandra](wikipedia://en/373190)[^o2] in the representation theory of semisimple [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), analogous to [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458) in the theory of [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146). Harish-Chandra used Eisenstein integrals to decompose the regular representation of a semisimple Lie group into representations induced from parabolic subgroups.[^o3] Trombi gave a survey of Harish-Chandra's work on this.[^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Harish-Chandra[^o7] defined the Eisenstein integral by

$$\displaystyle E(P:\psi:\nu:x) = \int_K\psi(xk)\tau(k^{-1})\exp((i\nu-\rho_P)H_P(xk)) \, dk$$

where:

- *x* is an element of a semisimple group *G*
- *P* = *MAN* is a cuspidal parabolic subgroup of *G*
- ν is an element of the complexification of *a*
- *a* is the Lie algebra of *A* in the [Langlands decomposition](wikipedia://en/11085278) *P* = *MAN*.
- *K* is a maximal compact subgroup of *G*, with *G* = *KP*.
- ψ is a cuspidal function on *M*, satisfying some extra conditions
- τ is a finite-dimensional unitary double representation of *K*
- *H*<sub>*P*</sub>(*x*) = log *a* where *x* = *kman* is the decomposition of *x* in *G* = *KMAN*.

:::
