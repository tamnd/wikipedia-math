# Arthur Rosenthal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Rosenthal"}

*Image: RosenthalArthur Zurich1932.tif.* [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) 1932

**Arthur Rosenthal** (24 February 1887, [Fürth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fürth), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire) – 15 September 1959, [Lafayette, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Indiana)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Rosenthal's mathematical studies started in 1905 in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), under [Ferdinand Lindemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Lindemann) and [Arnold Sommerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Sommerfeld) at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) (LMU Munich) and the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (TUM), as well as at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). After submitting his thesis on [regular polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_polyhedra) in 1909, he was promoted to assistant at the TUM in 1911 and then associate professor at the LMU in 1920. The following year he was appointed associate professor at the [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), with a promotion to full professor in 1930. Between 1932 and 1933, he served as dean in the faculty of mathematics and natural sciences, but was forced from his university position as a result of [Nazi policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_policies) against German Jews. He moved to the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) in 1936 and from there immigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1939. He was appointed lecturer and research fellow at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1940 with a promotion to assistant professor in 1943. In 1946, he became associate professor at the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico) and the following year moved to the [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) as full professor, where he remained until his retirement in 1957. In 1954, he was formally reinstated at Heidelberg University. A scholarship at Purdue University is named in his honor. His doctoral students include [Theodore Chihara](wikipedia://en/32809919).

## Research {#Research}

Rosenthal's mathematical research was in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), in particular the classification of regular polyhedra and [Hilbert's axioms](wikipedia://en/838151). He also made contributions in [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134), including to [Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625)'s theory of measure. With the Swiss mathematician [Michel Plancherel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Plancherel), he made contributions in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).
