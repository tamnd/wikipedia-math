# Marcia Ciol

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcia Ciol"}

**Márcia Aparecida Ciol** is a Brazilian-American [medical statistician](wikipedia://en/11082896) and [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) known for her research on [comorbidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorbidity). She works as a research associate professor in the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington)'s Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, maintains an affiliation with the Center for Brazilian Studies at the [University of Washington Tacoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_Tacoma), and is a past president of the [Caucus for Women in Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucus_for_Women_in_Statistics).

:::

Ciol earned bachelor's and master's degrees in statistics from the [University of Campinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Campinas) in 1979 and 1982 respectively. She earned a second master's degree in biostatistics from the University of Washington in 1987, and completed her Ph.D. at the University of Washington in 1991. Her dissertation, *An Adaptive Case-Cohort Design*, was supervised by Steven Self.
