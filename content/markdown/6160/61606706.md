# Margaret Wiecek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Wiecek"}

**Margaret M. Wiecek** (also known as **Małgorzata M. Więcek**) is a Polish-American [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476) and professor of mathematical sciences at [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University), known for her research in [multi-objective optimization](wikipedia://en/10251864), [Pareto efficiency](wikipedia://en/45468), [robust design](wikipedia://en/442079), and three-dimensional [packing problems](wikipedia://en/213003) in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering).

:::

Wiecek earned her Ph.D. from the [Akademia Górniczo-Hutnicza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGH_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), Poland, in 1984, under the supervision of Henryk Górecki. She joined the Clemson faculty in 1988.

Wiecek was president of the [INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) section on Multiple Criteria Decision Making for 2016–2017. In 2019, the International Society on Multiple Criteria Decision Making gave Wiecek their highest award, the Multiple Criteria Decision Making Gold Medal.
