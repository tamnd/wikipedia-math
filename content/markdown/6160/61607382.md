# Cohomology of a stack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohomology of a stack"}

In algebraic geometry, the **cohomology of a stack** is a generalization of [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231). In a sense, it is a theory that is coarser than the [Chow group of a stack](wikipedia://en/50534018).

:::

The cohomology of a [quotient stack](wikipedia://en/40579087) (e.g., [classifying stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classifying_stack)) can be thought of as an algebraic counterpart of [equivariant cohomology](wikipedia://en/2976342). For example, [Borel's theorem](wikipedia://en/43948116) states that the cohomology ring of a [classifying stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classifying_stack) is a polynomial ring.
