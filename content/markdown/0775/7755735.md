# Odd Aalen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Odd Aalen"}

**Odd Olai Aalen** (born 6 May 1947, in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo))[^o9] is a Norwegian [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) in the Department of Biostatistics at the Institute of Basic Medical Sciences at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo),[^o3] where he is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o4] He is the namesake of the [Nelson–Aalen estimator](wikipedia://en/22788211) and Aalen-Johansen estimator, used in [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259),[^o5] [reliability engineering](wikipedia://en/1724836),[^o6] and [life insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_insurance)[^o7] to estimate the cumulative number of expected events.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Aalen completed his [examen artium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Examen_artium) in 1966 at [Oslo Cathedral School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Cathedral_School) before studying first mathematics and physics and then statistics in which he graduated at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1972.[^o9] He completed a Ph.D. in 1995 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), with the dissertation *Statistical Inference for a Family of Counting Processes* supervised by [Lucien Le Cam](wikipedia://en/2629104).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Aalen is an elected member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o12] He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2007.[^o13]
