# Adjoint filter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adjoint filter"}

In [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324), the **adjoint filter mask** $h^*$ of a [filter mask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_filter) $h$ is reversed in time and the elements are [complex conjugated](wikipedia://en/173918).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

$$(h^*)_k = \overline{h_{-k}}$$

Its name is derived from the fact that the convolution with the adjoint filter is the [adjoint operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_of_an_operator) of the original filter, with respect to the [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) $\ell_2$ of the [sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) in which the inner product is the [Euclidean norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_norm).

$$\langle h*x, y \rangle = \langle x, h^* * y \rangle$$

The [autocorrelation](wikipedia://en/2724) of a signal $x$ can be written as $x^* * x$.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- $${h^*}^* = h$$
- $$(h*g)^* = h^* * g^*$$
- $$(h\leftarrow k)^* = h^* \rightarrow k$$

:::
