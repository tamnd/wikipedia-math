# Franc Hočevar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franc Hočevar"}

**Franz Josef Hočevar**, in Slovenian, **Franc Jože Hočevar** (10 October 1853 in [Metlika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metlika), Slovenia – 19 June 1919 in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), Austria) was an Austrian–Slovenian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and author of mathematical books.

:::

After grammar school in [Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana) Hočevar studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), where he got his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1875. He worked as professor and director of the "Lehrkanzel für Mathematik I" at the Technical University of Graz from 1894 to 1918. He was dean of the "Maschinenbauschule" from 1896 to 1900, 1906 to 1908 and from 1910 to 1913.
