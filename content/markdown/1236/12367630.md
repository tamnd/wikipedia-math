# Charles S. Peskin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles S. Peskin"}

**Charles Samuel Peskin** (born April 15, 1946) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in the mathematical modeling of blood flow in the heart. Such calculations are useful in the design of [artificial heart valves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_heart_valves). From this work has emerged an original computational method for [fluid-structure interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid-structure_interaction) that is now called the “[immersed boundary method](wikipedia://en/17943941)", which allows the coupling between deformable immersed structures and fluid flows to be handled in a computationally tractable way. With his students and colleagues, Peskin also has worked on mathematical models of such systems as the [inner ear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_ear), arterial pulse, [blood clotting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_clotting), [congenital heart disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congenital_heart_disease), light adaptation in the [retina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retina), control of [ovulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovulation) number, control of [plasmid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmid) replication, [molecular dynamics](wikipedia://en/198608), and [molecular motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_motors).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Peskin received an A.B. (1968) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and a Ph.D. (1972) from the [Albert Einstein College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein_College_of_Medicine), [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University) and shortly thereafter joined the faculty of the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He has been a productive educator of applied mathematicians, and has advised more than fifty graduate students as of 2014. Peskin is a [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellow) and a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences), the [Institute of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Medicine) and the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).

In 1969 he married Lucille G. Bisesi. Their son Eric is the manager of High Performance Computing at New York University.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [George David Birkhoff Prize in Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_David_Birkhoff_Prize_in_Applied_Mathematics) from AMS–SIAM, 2003[^o4]
- Invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), 1998[^o12]
- Mayor's Award for Excellence in Science and Technology, 1994
- [Sidney Fernbach Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Fernbach_Award), [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) Computer Society, 1994
- Cray Research Information Technology Leadership Award for Breakthrough Computational Science, 1994
- [Josiah Willard Gibbs Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josiah_Willard_Gibbs_Lecturer), [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 1993
- New York University Margaret and Herman Sokol Faculty Award in the Sciences, 1992
- [James H. Wilkinson Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Wilkinson_Prize_in_Numerical_Analysis_and_Scientific_Computing), [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376), 1985[^o13]
- MacArthur Fellowship, 1983–1988[^o3]

He has also been a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) since 1994,[^o14] a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1995,[^o15] and a member of the [Institute of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Medicine) (now the National Academy of Medicine) since 2000.[^o16] He is also an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o17] and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o18]
