# Maurice Janet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maurice Janet"}

**Maurice Janet** (1888–1983) was a French mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

In 1912, as a student he visited the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).[^o4] He was a professor at the [University of Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen). He was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), in 1932 in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich), and in 1936 in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo).

Named in his honor are [Janet bases](wikipedia://en/41553887), Janet sequences[^o5][^o6] and a related algorithm in the theory of systems of partial differential equations.[^o7] In 1926, he proved results that were later generalized by [John Forbes Nash Jr.](wikipedia://en/102567) in his [embedding theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_embedding_theorem).

In 1948, Janet was the president of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France). He was a close friend of the mathematician [Ernest Vessiot](wikipedia://en/1142911).
