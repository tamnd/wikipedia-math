# Catharina Stroppel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catharina Stroppel"}

*Image: StroppelPrinceton.jpg.* Stroppel in Princeton, 2008

**Catharina Stroppel** (born 1971)[^o2] is a German mathematician whose research concerns [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), and vice-coordinator of the [Hausdorff Center for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221) in Bonn.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Stroppel earned a diploma in mathematics and theology from the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) in 1998.[^o4] She completed her doctorate, also from the University of Freiburg, in 2001, under the supervision of Wolfgang Soergel. After short-term positions at the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester) and [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University), she joined the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) as a research associate in 2004, and was promoted to lecturer in 2005 and reader in 2007. In 2008 she moved to Bonn as a professor.[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2007, the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) awarded Stroppel their [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) "for her contributions to representation theory, in particular in the framework of categorifications and its applications to low-dimensional topology".[^o8] She was an invited speaker at the 2010 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o9] In 2018, she became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o10] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for 2009–2010.[^o11] She was awarded a 2023 [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_Prize) “in recognition of her excellent work in representation theory, especially on the topic of category theory.”[^o12]
