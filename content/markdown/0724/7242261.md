# S. L. Woronowicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="S. L. Woronowicz"}

**Stanisław Lech Woronowicz** (born 22 July 1941) is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_physicist). He is affiliated with the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) and is a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences). Between 1981–1984, he served as dean at the Faculty of Physics of the University of Warsaw.[^o3] Since 1993, he has been a member of the Scientific Council of the Theoretical Physics Center at the Polish Academy of Sciences.

:::

## Research {#Research}

Woronowicz and [Erling Størmer](wikipedia://en/24884274) [classified](wikipedia://en/5168898) [positive maps](wikipedia://en/6083511) in low-dimensional cases,[^o5] which translate to the [Peres–Horodecki criterion](wikipedia://en/2420567) in the context of [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory). He is also known for contributions to [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).

## Awards {#Awards}

Woronowicz was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) in 1983 and in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto) in 1990.[^o7]

In 1993, he received the [Prize of the Foundation for Polish Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_of_the_Foundation_for_Polish_Science) for his work on [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678) and their relations with [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184). He was also awarded [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award) in 2008,[^o8] and [Stefan Banach Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Banach_Medal) of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2009.[^o9]
