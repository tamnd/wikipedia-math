# Darinka Dentcheva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Darinka Dentcheva"}

**Darinka Dentcheva** ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language): Даринка Денчева) is a [Bulgarian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Americans) mathematician, noted for her contributions to [convex analysis](wikipedia://en/3237201), [stochastic programming](wikipedia://en/501898), and [risk-averse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk-averse) optimization.

:::

## Schooling and positions {#Schooling_and_positions}

Dentcheva was born in [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria). She received her MsC and PhD degrees in mathematics from [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) (Germany) in 1981 and 1989, respectively. In 2006 she was granted *[Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)* from [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), for a dissertation on [set-valued analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set-valued_analysis).[^o4]

From 1982 to 1994 Dentcheva was with the [Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Informatics_%28Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences%29), [Bulgarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences), in Sofia (Bulgaria). In 1997–1999 she was a visitor at the Rutgers Center for Operations Research of [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). In 1999–2000 she was a visiting professor at the Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering, [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University). Since 2000 Dentcheva has been with [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevens_Institute_of_Technology), where she holds a position of Professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences. In 2023-2024 she was the Chair of the Faculty Senate.[^o5]

*Image: Dentcheva Stevens Convocation.jpg.* Dentcheva as the Faculty Marshal at the 2023 Convocation Ceremony at the [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevens_Institute_of_Technology).

## Main achievements {#Main_achievements}

Dentcheva developed the theory of Steiner selections of multifunctions,[^o7] the theory of [stochastic dominance](wikipedia://en/3574224) constraints[^o8] (jointly with [Andrzej Ruszczyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Piotr_Ruszczyński)), and contributed to the theory of [unit commitment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_system_simulation) in power systems (with [Werner Römisch](wikipedia://en/68871037)).[^o9] She authored 2 books and more than 70 research papers.[^o10]
