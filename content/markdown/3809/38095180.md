# Ramanujam–Samuel theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramanujam–Samuel theorem"}

In algebraic geometry, the **Ramanujam–Samuel theorem** gives conditions for a [divisor](wikipedia://en/1693055) of a [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) to be principal.

:::

It was introduced independently by (txt) in answer to a question of [Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) and by [C. P. Ramanujam](wikipedia://en/14709171) in an appendix to a paper by (txt), and was generalized by (txt, Theorem 21.14.1).

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Grothendieck's version of the Ramanujam–Samuel theorem (Grothendieck Dieudonné 1967, theorem 21.14.1) is as follows. Suppose that *A* is a local [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) with [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164) *m*, whose [completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completion_%28ring_theory%29) is [integral](wikipedia://en/15462) and [integrally closed](wikipedia://en/21420209), and ρ is a local [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/26411) from *A* to a local Noetherian ring *B* of larger [dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) such that *B* is [formally smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formally_smooth) over *A* and the [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) of *B* is [finite over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_module) that of *A*. Then a [cycle](wikipedia://en/9520954) of [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) 1 in [Spec](wikipedia://en/37291)(*B*) that is principal at the point *mB* is principal.

:::
