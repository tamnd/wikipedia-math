# Kaisa Nyberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kaisa Nyberg"}

**Kaisa Nyberg** is a Finnish [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researcher.

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Nyberg's research includes the theory of perfect nonlinear [S-boxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_box)N (now known as Nyberg S-boxes), [provably secure](wikipedia://en/1182348) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) design (resulting in [KN-Cipher](wikipedia://en/9077186),NK and the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of the [stream ciphers](wikipedia://en/49007) [E0](wikipedia://en/2137226)HN and [SNOW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNOW)B+).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nyberg received her Ph.D. in mathematics in 1980 from the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki). Her dissertation, *On Subspaces of Products of Nuclear Fréchet Spaces*, was in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and was supervised by Edward Leonard Dubinsky.

Nyberg began doing cryptography research for the Finnish Defence Forces in 1987, and moved to [Nokia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia) in 1998. She became professor of cryptology at [Aalto University School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_University_School_of_Science_and_Technology) in 2005, and retired as a professor emerita in 2016.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Nyberg was the 2015 recipient of the Magnus Ehrnrooths Foundation Prize of the Finnish Society of Arts and Letters.
