# Thomas Jakobsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Jakobsen"}

**Thomas Jakobsen** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer), and [computer programmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programmer), formerly an assistant professor at the [Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark) (DTU) and head of research and development at [IO Interactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IO_Interactive). His notable work includes designing the [physics engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_engine) and 3-D pathfinder algorithms for *[Hitman: Codename 47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitman:_Codename_47)*, and the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of a number of [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594).

:::

Jakobsen earned an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in engineering and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics, both from DTU.
