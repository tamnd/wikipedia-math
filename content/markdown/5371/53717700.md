# David Rytz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Rytz"}

**David Rytz von Brugg** (1 April 1801, in Bucheggberg – 25 March 1868, in [Aarau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarau)) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rytz von Brugg was son of a [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) and studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) and [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig). He had teaching positions at various cities, one of them 1835 until 1862 at Aarau, where he was *„Professor der Mathematik an der Gewerbeschule zu Aarau“*.[^o4]

## Merits {#Merits}

Rytz von Brugg is famous for a geometrical method which is known as [Rytz’s axis construction](wikipedia://en/40780131). This classical procedure retrieves the semi-axes of an [Ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277) from any pair of [conjugate diameters](wikipedia://en/24531598). This method is known since 1845, when it was published within a paper by Leopold Moosbrugger.[^o6][^o7]
