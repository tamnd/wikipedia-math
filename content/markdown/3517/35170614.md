# Kähler quotient

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kähler quotient"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), the **Kähler quotient** of a [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538) $X$ by a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) $G$ [acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on $X$ by preserving the Kähler structure and with [moment map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) $\mu : X \to \mathfrak{g}^*$ (with respect to the [Kähler form](wikipedia://en/390538#Complex_viewpoint)) is the [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213)

:::

$$\mu^{-1}(0)/G.$$

If $G$ acts [freely](wikipedia://en/12781#Remarkable_properties_of_actions) and [properly](wikipedia://en/12781#Actions_of_topological_groups), then $\mu^{-1}(0)/G$ is a new Kähler manifold whose [Kähler form](wikipedia://en/390538#Complex_viewpoint) is given by the [symplectic quotient construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map#Symplectic_quotients).[^o2]

By the [Kempf-Ness theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kempf-Ness_theorem), a Kähler quotient by a [compact Lie group](wikipedia://en/750326#Compact_Lie_groups) $G$ is closely related to a [geometric invariant theory](wikipedia://en/1639003) quotient by the [complexification](wikipedia://en/38635109) of $G$.[^o3]
