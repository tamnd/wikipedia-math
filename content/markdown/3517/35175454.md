# Bogomolny equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bogomolny equations"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and especially [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/64324611), the **Bogomolny equation** for [magnetic monopoles](wikipedia://en/175470) is the equation

:::

$$F_A = \star d_A \Phi,$$

where $F_A$ is the [curvature](wikipedia://en/489440) of a [connection](wikipedia://en/308815) $A$ on a [principal $G$-bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) over a [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) $M$, $\Phi$ is a section of the corresponding [adjoint bundle](wikipedia://en/9943932), $d_A$ is the [exterior covariant derivative](wikipedia://en/849738) induced by $A$ on the adjoint bundle, and $\star$ is the [Hodge star operator](wikipedia://en/310914) on $M$. These equations are named after E. B. Bogomolny and were studied extensively by [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) and [Nigel Hitchin](wikipedia://en/1034164).[^o2][^o3]

The equations are a dimensional reduction of the [self-dual Yang–Mills equations](wikipedia://en/35544664#Anti-self-duality_equations) from four dimensions to three dimensions, and correspond to global minima of the appropriate action. If $M$ is closed, there are only trivial (i.e. flat) solutions.
