# Signature cocycle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Signature cocycle"}

In mathematics, the **Meyer signature cocycle**, introduced by (txt). is an integer-valued 2-cocyle on a [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) that describes the signature of a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) whose base and fiber are both [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181).

:::
