# Hyperkähler quotient

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyperkähler quotient"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **hyperkähler quotient** of a [hyperkähler manifold](wikipedia://en/646120) acted on by a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) *G* is the quotient of a fiber of a hyperkähler moment map $M \to \mathfrak{g} \otimes \mathbb{R}^3$ over a *G*-fixed point by the action of *G*. It was introduced by [Nigel Hitchin](wikipedia://en/1034164), Anders Karlhede, [Ulf Lindström](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulf_Lindström), and [Martin Roček](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Roček) in 1987. It is a hyperkähler analogue of the [Kähler quotient](wikipedia://en/35170614).

:::
