# Kervaire semi-characteristic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kervaire semi-characteristic"}

In mathematics, the **Kervaire semi-characteristic**, introduced by (txt), is an invariant of [closed manifolds](wikipedia://en/669475) *M* of dimension $4n+1$ taking values in $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, given by

:::

$$k_F(M) = \sum_{i=0}^{2n} \dim H^{2i}(M,F)\bmod 2$$

where F is a field.

(txt) showed that the Kervaire semi-characteristic of a [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) is given by the [index](wikipedia://en/324752) of a skew-adjoint [elliptic operator](wikipedia://en/550137).

Assuming *M* is [oriented](wikipedia://en/187446), the **Atiyah vanishing theorem** states that if *M* has two linearly independent [vector fields](wikipedia://en/62641), then $k(M) = 0$.[^o4]

The difference $k_\mathbb{Q}(M)-k_{\mathbb{Z}/2}(M)$ is the [de Rham invariant](wikipedia://en/25901631) of $M$.[^o5]
