# Symplectic spinor bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symplectic spinor bundle"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), given a [metaplectic structure](wikipedia://en/32019964) $\pi_{\mathbf P}\colon{\mathbf P}\to M\,$ on a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) $(M, \omega),\,$ the **symplectic spinor bundle** is the [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) bundle $\pi_{\mathbf Q}\colon{\mathbf Q}\to M\,$ associated to the metaplectic structure via the metaplectic representation. The metaplectic representation of the [metaplectic group](wikipedia://en/2981795) — the two-fold covering of the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) — gives rise to an infinite rank [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524); this is the symplectic spinor construction due to [Bertram Kostant](wikipedia://en/3520590).[^o2]

:::

A section of the **symplectic spinor bundle** ${\mathbf Q}\,$ is called a **symplectic spinor field**.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Let $({\mathbf P},F_{\mathbf P})$ be a [metaplectic structure](wikipedia://en/32019964) on a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) $(M, \omega),\,$ that is, an [equivariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant) lift of the [symplectic frame bundle](wikipedia://en/32050867) $\pi_{\mathbf R}\colon{\mathbf R}\to M\,$ with respect to the double covering $\rho\colon {\mathrm {Mp}}(n,{\mathbb R})\to {\mathrm {Sp}}(n,{\mathbb R}).\,$

The **symplectic spinor bundle** ${\mathbf Q}\,$ is defined [^o5] to be the Hilbert space [bundle](wikipedia://en/827658)

$${\mathbf Q}={\mathbf P}\times_{\mathfrak m}L^2({\mathbb R}^n)\,$$

associated to the metaplectic structure ${\mathbf P}$ via the metaplectic representation ${\mathfrak m}\colon {\mathrm {Mp}}(n,{\mathbb R})\to {\mathrm U}(L^2({\mathbb R}^n)),\,$ also called the **Segal–Shale–Weil** [^o6][^o7][^o8] representation of ${\mathrm {Mp}}(n,{\mathbb R}).\,$ Here, the notation ${\mathrm U}({\mathbf W})\,$ denotes the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of [unitary operators](wikipedia://en/225999) acting on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) ${\mathbf W}.\,$

The Segal–Shale–Weil representation [^o9] is an infinite dimensional [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of the metaplectic group ${\mathrm {Mp}}(n,{\mathbb R})$ on the space of all complex valued square [Lebesgue integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_integrable) [square-integrable functions](wikipedia://en/28680558) $L^2({\mathbb R}^n).\,$ Because of the infinite dimension, the Segal–Shale–Weil representation is not so easy to handle.
