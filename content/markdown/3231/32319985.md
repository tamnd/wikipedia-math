# Ichirō Satake

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ichirō Satake"}

**Ichirō Satake** (25 December 1927 – 10 October 2014) was a Japanese mathematician working on [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) who introduced the [Satake isomorphism](wikipedia://en/32319857) and [Satake diagrams](wikipedia://en/20514575). He was considered an iconic figure in the theory of [linear algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/395885) and [symmetric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_spaces).[^o3]

:::

Satake was born in [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo), Japan in 1927, and received his Ph.D. at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1959 under the supervision of [Shokichi Iyanaga](wikipedia://en/18357287). He was a professor at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1968 to 1983. After retirement he returned to Japan, where he spent time at [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University) and [Chuo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuo_University). He died of [respiratory failure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_failure) on 10 October 2014.[^o4]

Although they are often attributed to [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), Satake was the first to introduce [orbifold](wikipedia://en/293504), which he did in the 1950s under the name of *V-manifold*. In (Satake 1956), he gave the modern definition, along with the basic calculus of smooth functions and [differential forms](wikipedia://en/221519). He demonstrated that the [de Rham theorem](wikipedia://en/77429163) and [Poincaré duality](wikipedia://en/540732), along with their proofs, carry over to the orbifold setting. In (Satake 1957), he demonstrated that the standard [tensor calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_calculus) of bundles, connections, and curvature also carries over to orbifolds, along with the [Chern-Gauss-Bonnet theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern-Gauss-Bonnet_theorem) and [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905)'s proof thereof.[^o7]
