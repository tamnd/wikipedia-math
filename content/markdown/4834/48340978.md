# V-ring (ring theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="V-ring"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **V-ring** is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* such that every [simple](wikipedia://en/29430) *R*-[module](wikipedia://en/276410) is [injective](wikipedia://en/473775). The following three conditions are equivalent:[^o2]

:::

1. Every simple left (respectively right) *R*-module is injective.
1. The [radical](wikipedia://en/9379691) of every left (respectively right) *R*-module is zero.
1. Every left (respectively right) [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of *R* is an intersection of [maximal](wikipedia://en/48164) left (respectively right) ideals of *R*.

A [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) is a V-ring [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) it is [Von Neumann regular](wikipedia://en/907524).[^o3]
