# Juan Gutiérrez de Gualda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juan Gutiérrez de Gualda"}

*Image: Juan Gutierrez de Gualda.png.* Front page of the 1564 edition

**Juan Gutiérrez de Gualda** (16th century) was a [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known to be the author of a popular book on arithmetic.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Nothing is known about his life except he was the priest of [Villarejo de Fuentes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarejo_de_Fuentes), [province of Cuenca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Cuenca).[^o4]

Gutiérrez is known by his book on arithmetic which was very successful in his times: *Arte breve y muy provechoso de cuenta castellana y arismética* (Toledo 1539).[^o5] The book was reedited not less than five times in 16th century.[^o6] It consist in a short arithmetic for merchants,[^o7] very basic, which only contains forty pages and practically only explains the four rules and few more.[^o8] The book is an example of the transition from [roman numerals](wikipedia://en/25657) to [arabic numerals](wikipedia://en/1786) because it resolves the problems with arabics (*quenta arismetica*), but it gives his expression in romans (*quenta castellana*).[^o9] His success was in big part due to the fact that [Juan de Yciar](wikipedia://en/48270988) included it as an annex of his book on [calligraphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calligraphy) from 1564.[^o8]
