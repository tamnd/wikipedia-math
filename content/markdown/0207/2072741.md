# Category of small categories

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Category of small categories"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), the **category of small categories**, denoted by **Cat**, is the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) whose objects are all [small categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category) and whose [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are [functors](wikipedia://en/10987) between categories. **Cat** may actually be regarded as a [2-category](wikipedia://en/2740708) with [natural transformations](wikipedia://en/45022) serving as [2-morphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-morphism).

:::

The [initial object](wikipedia://en/59405) of **Cat** is the *empty category* **0**, which is the category of no objects and no morphisms.[^o2] The [terminal object](wikipedia://en/59405) is the *terminal category* or *trivial category* **1** with a single object and morphism.[^o3]

The category **Cat** is itself a [large category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_category), and therefore not an object of itself. In order to avoid problems analogous to [Russell's paradox](wikipedia://en/46095) one cannot form the “category of all categories”. But it is possible to form a [quasicategory](wikipedia://en/2390225) (meaning objects and morphisms merely form a [conglomerate](wikipedia://en/48611088)) of all categories.

## Free category {#Free_category}

The category **Cat** has a [forgetful functor](wikipedia://en/844783) *U* into the [quiver category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quiver_category) **Quiv**:

*U* : **Cat** → **Quiv**

This functor forgets the identity morphisms of a given category, and it forgets morphism compositions. The [left adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_adjoint) of this functor is a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) *F* taking **Quiv** to the corresponding [free categories](wikipedia://en/36769284):

*F* : **Quiv** → **Cat**

## 1-Categorical properties {#1-Categorical_properties}

- **Cat** has [all small limits and colimits](wikipedia://en/62781).
- **Cat** is a [Cartesian closed category](wikipedia://en/237037), with [exponential](wikipedia://en/2237858) $D^C$ given by the [functor category](wikipedia://en/407039) $\mathrm{Fun}(C, D)$.
- **Cat** is *not* locally Cartesian closed.
- **Cat** is [locally finitely presentable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_presentable_category).
