# Heinz-Otto Kreiss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz-Otto Kreiss"}

**Heinz-Otto Kreiss** (14 September 1930 – 16 December 2015) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the fields of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), and what was the new area of [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing) in the early 1960s. Born in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), he earned his Ph.D. at [Kungliga Tekniska Högskolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kungliga_Tekniska_Högskolan) in 1959. Over the course of his long career, Kreiss wrote a number of books in addition to the purely academic journal articles he authored across several disciplines. He was professor at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA). He was also a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences). At the time of his death, Kreiss was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) citizen, living in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm). He died in Stockholm in 2015, aged 85.[^o3]

:::

Kreiss did research on the initial value problem for partial differential equations, numerical treatment of partial differential equations, difference equations, and applications to hydrodynamics and meteorology.

In 1974, he delivered a plenary lecture *Initial Boundary Value Problems for Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equations* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver). In 2002 he won the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) Award in Numerical Analysis and Applied Mathematics.[^o4] In 2003 he was the [John von Neumann Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Lecture) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM). He was elected a member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.

His doctoral students include [Björn Engquist](wikipedia://en/2070192) and [Bertil Gustafsson](wikipedia://en/27171492). His daughter, [Gunilla Kreiss](wikipedia://en/68485669), was a student of Engquist.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- with Jens Lorenz: Initial-boundary value problems and the Navier-Stokes equations, Academic Press 1989, SIAM 2004[^o7]
- with Hedwig Ulmer Busenhart: Time-dependent partial differential equations and their numerical solution, Birkhäuser 2001
- with [Bertil Gustafsson](wikipedia://en/27171492), [Joseph Oliger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Oliger): time-dependent problems and difference methods, Wiley 1995
