# Luis Caffarelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luis Caffarelli"}

**Luis Ángel Caffarelli** (; born December 8, 1948) is an [Argentine-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Americans)[^o5] [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He studies [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and their applications. Caffarelli is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), and the winner of the 2023 [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285).[^o6]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Caffarelli was born and grew up in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires). He obtained his Masters of Science (1968) and Ph.D. (1972) at the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires). His Ph.D. advisor was [Calixto Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calixto_Calderón).[^o8][^o9] He currently holds the Sid Richardson Chair at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) and is core faculty at the [Oden Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oden_Institute_for_Computational_Engineering_and_Sciences).[^o10] He also has been a professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). From 1986 to 1996 he was a professor at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton.

## Research {#Research}

Caffarelli published "The regularity of free boundaries in higher dimensions" in 1977 in *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)*.[^o12] One of his most cited results regards the *Partial regularity of suitable weak solutions of the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395)*; it was obtained in 1982 in collaboration with [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725) and [Robert V. Kohn](wikipedia://en/48477231).[^o13]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 1991, he was elected to the U.S. [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences). He was awarded [honorary doctorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) by the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), Paris, the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), the [Universidad Autónoma de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Autónoma_de_Madrid), and the [Universidad de La Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_de_La_Plata), Argentina. He received the [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) in 1984. He is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o15]

In 2003, [Konex Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konex_Foundation) from Argentina granted him the Diamond [Konex Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konex_Award), one of the most prestigious awards in Argentina, as the most important Scientist of his country in the last decade. In 2005, he received the prestigious [Rolf Schock Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Schock_Prize) of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) "for his important contributions to the theory of nonlinear partial differential equations". He also received the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Lifetime Achievement in Mathematics in 2009.[^o16] In 2012, he was awarded the [Wolf Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/714278) (jointly with [Michael Aschbacher](wikipedia://en/1652651)) and became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o17] In 2017 he gave the [Łojasiewicz Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łojasiewicz_Lecture) (on "Some models of segregation") at the [Jagiellonian University in Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o18]

In 2018, he was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow)[^o19] and he received the [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in Mathematics.[^o20]

In 2023, he was awarded the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) "for his seminal contributions to [regularity theory](wikipedia://en/73361798) for nonlinear partial differential equations including free-boundary problems and the [Monge–Ampère equation](wikipedia://en/4914066)".[^o21][^o22][^o23]

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

Caffarelli has coauthored two books:

1. *Fully Nonlinear Elliptic Equations* by Luis Caffarelli and Xavier Cabré (1995), American Mathematical Society.
1. *A Geometric Approach to Free Boundary Problems* by Luis Caffarelli and Sandro Salsa (2005), American Mathematical Society.
