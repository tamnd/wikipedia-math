# Paul Stäckel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Stäckel"}

*Image: Paul Stäckel.jpeg.* Paul Stäckel.

**Paul Gustav Samuel Stäckel** (20 August 1862, [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) – 12 December 1919, [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), active in the areas of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610). In the area of [prime number theory](wikipedia://en/23666), he used the term *[twin prime](wikipedia://en/41997)* (in its [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) form, "Primzahlzwilling") for the first time.[^o2][^o3]

:::

After passing his *Abitur* in 1880 he studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), but also listened to lectures on philosophy, psychology, education, and history. A year later he qualified for teaching in higher education and then taught at [*Gymnasien*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in Berlin. In 1885 he wrote his doctoral dissertation under [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450) and [Karl Weierstraß](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Weierstraß). In 1891 he completed his *Habilitation* at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle). Later he worked as a professor at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) (*[außerordentlicher Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Außerordentlicher_Professor)* from 1895 to 1897), the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) (*[ordentlicher Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordentlicher_Professor)*, 1897 to 1905), [University of Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hannover) (1905 to 1908), the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) (1908 to 1913), and the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) (1913 to 1919).

Stäckel worked on both mathematics and the history of mathematics. He edited the letters exchanged between [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) and [Wolfgang Bolyai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Bolyai), made contributions to editions of the collected works of Euler and Gauss (for whose works he wrote *Gauss als Geometer*), and edited the *Geometrischen Untersuchungen* by Wolfgang and Johann Bolyai (published in 1913). Additionally he translated works of [Jacob Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64740), [Johann Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/268886), [Augustin Louis Cauchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustin_Louis_Cauchy), [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902), [Joseph-Louis Lagrange](wikipedia://en/87793), [Adrien-Marie Legendre](wikipedia://en/49695), [Carl Gustav Jacobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gustav_Jacobi) from French and Latin into German for the series *[Ostwalds Klassiker der exakten Wissenschaften](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostwalds_Klassiker_der_exakten_Wissenschaften)*.

In 1904 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Heidelberg.[^o4] In 1905 he was the president of the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung). His doctoral students include [Paul Riebesell](wikipedia://en/64408548).
