# Utilization distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Utilization distribution"}

A **utilization distribution** is a [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) giving the probability density that an animal is found at a given point in space. It is estimated from data sampling the location of an individual or individuals in space over a period of time using, for example, [telemetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemetry) or [GPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPS) based methods.

:::

Estimation of utilization distribution was traditionally based on [histograms](wikipedia://en/13266) but newer [nonparametric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric) methods based on [Fourier transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_transformations),[^o2] [kernel density](wikipedia://en/2090057)[^o3] and [local convex hull](wikipedia://en/6385832) methods have been developed.

The typical application for this distribution is estimating the [home range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_range) distribution of animals. According to Lichti & Swihart (2011),[^o4] kernel density methods provided, in many cases, less [biased](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biased_estimator) home-range area estimates compared to convex hull methods.
