# Hans Petersson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Petersson"}

Hans Petersson **Hans Petersson** (24 September 1902 in [Bentschen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bentschen) – 9 November 1984 in [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his research on [modular](wikipedia://en/286000) and [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146). He introduced the [Petersson inner product](wikipedia://en/4028987) and is also known for the [Ramanujan–Petersson conjecture](wikipedia://en/782173).

:::

He received his doctorate in 1925 from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). His thesis advisor was [Erich Hecke](wikipedia://en/1233990).

In a series of papers, Petersson used the [Poincaré series](wikipedia://en/24146591) to give a complete construction of all [meromorphic functions](wikipedia://en/80857) and differentials on a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181).[^o4]
