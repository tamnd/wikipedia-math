# Semi-abelian category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semi-abelian category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **semi-abelian category** is a [pre-abelian category](wikipedia://en/62775) in which the induced [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) $\overline{f}:\operatorname{coim}f\rightarrow\operatorname{im}f$ is a [bimorphism](wikipedia://en/27716891#Monomorphisms_and_epimorphisms), i.e., a [monomorphism](wikipedia://en/59538) and an [epimorphism](wikipedia://en/59539), for every morphism $f$.

:::

The history of the notion is intertwined with that of a [quasi-abelian category](wikipedia://en/51179542), as, for awhile, it was not known whether the two notions are distinct (see [quasi-abelian category#History](wikipedia://en/51179542#History)).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The two properties used in the definition can be characterized by several equivalent conditions.[^o4]

Every semi-abelian category has a [maximal exact structure](wikipedia://en/62775#Maximal_exact_structure).

If a semi-abelian category is not [quasi-abelian](wikipedia://en/51179542), then the class of all kernel-cokernel pairs does not form an [exact structure](wikipedia://en/10987985).

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every [quasiabelian category](wikipedia://en/51179542) is semiabelian. In particular, every [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063) is semi-abelian. Non-quasiabelian examples are the following.

- The [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of (possibly non-[Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637)) [bornological spaces](wikipedia://en/1804457#Bornological_spaces) is semiabelian.[^o7][^o8][^o9]
- Let $Q$ be the [quiver](wikipedia://en/355100)

$$\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & \xrightarrow{} & 2 & \xleftarrow{} & 3 \\ \downarrow{} & & \downarrow{}& & \downarrow{}\\ 4 & \xrightarrow{} & 5 & \xleftarrow{} & 6\\ \end{array}$$

and $K$ be a [field](wikipedia://en/10603). The category of [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/326430) [projective](wikipedia://en/364488) [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) over the [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) $KQ$ is semiabelian.[^o10]

:::

## Left and right semi-abelian categories {#Left_and_right_semi-abelian_categories}

By dividing the two conditions on the induced map in the definition, one can define **left semi-abelian categories** by requiring that $\overline{f}$ is a monomorphism for each morphism $f$. Accordingly, **right semi-abelian categories** are pre-abelian categories such that $\overline{f}$ is an epimorphism for each morphism $f$.[^o12]

If a category is left semi-abelian and [right quasi-abelian](wikipedia://en/51179542#Left_and_right_quasi-abelian_categories), then it is already quasi-abelian. The same holds, if the category is right semi-abelian and left quasi-abelian.[^o13]
