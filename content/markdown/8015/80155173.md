# Peter Kuchment

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Kuchment"}

**Peter Kuchment** (born 1949) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), and [integral geometry](wikipedia://en/349336) with applications to [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging).[^o3]

:::

Peter Kuchment was born in 1949 in [Chernivtsi (formerly Chernovtsy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernivtsi), Soviet Union (now Ukraine). He then studied at a university and worked in Voronezh (Russia), until emigrating to the US in 1989. He holds a M.S. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Voronezh State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh_State_University) (1971), a Ph.D. from [Kharkiv State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VN_Karazin_Kharkiv_National_University), Ukraine (1973), and Doctor of Science from The Institute of Mathematics[^o4] of the Ukrainian Acad. Sci., [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv) (1983) (the latter two degrees in Mathematics and Physics).

## Employment {#Employment}

- 1971–1972: School teacher of mathematics, Voronezh, USSR
- 1972–1989: Faculty (Assistant, Associate, Full Professor and chair) at the Mathematics and Theoretical Mechanics Department of Voronezh Forestry Institute (now [Voronezh State University of Forestry and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh_State_University_of_Forestry_and_Technologies)).
- 1990–2001: Associate and later Full Professor at the Mathematics and Statistics Department of Wichita State University.[^o6]
- 2001–present: Full Professor, later Distinguished Professor (2011), Arthur George and Mary Emolene Owen Chair[^o7] (2025) of the Mathematics Department at the [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University).

## Publications {#Publications}

Kuchment has published 168 peer-reviewed papers.[^o9][^o10] He has four books published and one translated.[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15]

## Advising {#Advising}

Kuchment advised on 21 PhD theses.[^o17]
