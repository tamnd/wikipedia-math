# Laurent Bartholdi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Laurent Bartholdi"}

**Laurent Bartholdi** is a Swiss mathematician working in the areas of [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), [symbolic dynamics](wikipedia://en/1986263), and [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/6511). He is particularly well-known for his contributions to the study of [self-similar groups](wikipedia://en/18707371) and [amenability of groups](wikipedia://en/657430). Currently Bartholdi is a [CNRS Directeur de Recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at Institut Camille Jordan, [Claude Bernard University Lyon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Bernard_University_Lyon_1)[^o2] in France.

:::

Bartholdi received a PhD in Mathematics in 2000 from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), with [Pierre de la Harpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_de_la_Harpe) and [Rostislav Grigorchuk](wikipedia://en/18697527) as co-advisors.[^o3]

In June 2025 Bartholdi delivered the inaugural [Paul Schupp](wikipedia://en/54677806) Distinguished Lecture at the GAGTA 2025 "Groups, Logic and Computation" conference at the [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevens_Institute_of_Technology).[^o4]
