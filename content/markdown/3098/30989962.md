# Riley slice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Riley slice"}

In the mathematical theory of Kleinian groups, the **Riley slice** of [Schottky space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_space) is a family of [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) generated by two parabolic elements. It was studied in detail by (Keen Series 1994) and named after [Robert Riley](wikipedia://en/64550014) by them. Some subtle errors in their paper were corrected by (Komori Series 1998).

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Riley slice consists of the complex numbers ρ such that the two matrices

$$\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\\ \end{pmatrix}, \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ \rho&1\\ \end{pmatrix}$$

generate a Kleinian group *G* with regular set Ω such that Ω/*G* is a 4-times punctured sphere.

The Riley slice is the quotient of the Teichmuller space of a 4-times punctured sphere by a group generated by Dehn twists around a curve, and so is topologically an annulus.

:::
