# Kenneth I. Gross

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth I. Gross"}

**Kenneth Irwin Gross** (14 October 1938 – 10 September 2017) was an American mathematician.

:::

Born in [Malden, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malden,_Massachusetts) in 1938,[^o4] Gross received from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) his bachelor's degree in 1960 and his master's degree in 1962. He received his Ph.D. in 1966 from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) under [Ray Kunze](wikipedia://en/45226885) with thesis *Plancherel Transform of the Nilpotent Part of $G_2$ and Some Applications to the Representation Theory of $G_2$*.[^o5] He was an assistant professor from 1966 to 1968 at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) and an assistant professor from 1968 to 1973 at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College). He became in 1973 an associate professor and eventually a full professor at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina) before resigning in 1981. From 1981 to 1985 he was the chair of the mathematics department of the [University of Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wyoming). In 1988 Gross became a professor at the [University of Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vermont), where he served as chair of the department of mathematics and statistics from 1988 to 1992. On a leave of absence he was for two years (2003–2005) at [Lesley University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_University), where he developed the mathematics program.

Gross has been a visiting professor at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah), the Academia Sinica in Taiwan, [Drexel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drexel_University), [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University), and Australia's [University of Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle_%28Australia%29). He has twice served as a divisional program director for the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation). He was the director of the Vermont Mathematics Initiative.[^o6]

He did research on [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [group representation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_representation_theory), analysis on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and [homogeneous spaces](wikipedia://en/363325), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659), and mathematical applications to physics and [multivariate statistics](wikipedia://en/19384).

In 1979 he received the [Lester Randolph Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Randolph_Ford_Award).[^o7] In 1981 he received the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o8] In 2008 he received a [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o9] In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] He died on 10 September 2017 at the age of 78.[^o11]
