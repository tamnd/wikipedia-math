# Uwe Storch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uwe Storch"}

*Image: Uwe Storch.jpg.* Uwe Storch

**Uwe Storch** (born 12 July 1940, [Leopoldshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staßfurt)– [Lanzarote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanzarote), 17 September 2017) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2] His field of research was [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), and [analytic](wikipedia://en/2202) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), in particular [derivations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivation_%28abstract_algebra%29), [divisor class group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisor_class_group), and [resultants](wikipedia://en/2195020).

:::

Storch studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) and [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). He got his PhD in 1966 under the supervision of [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678) with a [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) on almost (or Q) [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) [rings](wikipedia://en/48404). He did his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1972 in [Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochum) and became a professor at the [University of Osnabrück](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osnabrück_University) in 1974. From 1981 to 2006, when he became emeritus, he was a professor for algebra and geometry at [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum).[^o3]

He was married and had four sons.

## Theorem of Eisenbud–Evans–Storch {#Theorem_of_Eisenbud–Evans–Storch}

The [Theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) of [Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845)-[Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_Evans_%28mathematician%29)-Storch states that every [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) in n-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834) is given geometrically (i.e. up to [radical](wikipedia://en/252009)) by n [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000).

:::
