# R. W. H. T. Hudson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="R. W. H. T. Hudson"}

**Ronald William Henry Turnbull Hudson** (16 July 1876 – 20 September 1904) was a British mathematician.[^o6]

:::

Hudson was born into a family of mathematical talents.[^o3] He was the oldest of four children of William Henry Hoar Hudson, Professor of Mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London),[^o6] and his mother read mathematics at [Newnham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge), Cambridge.[^o4] Both of his sisters became mathematicians.[^o5]

Hudson read mathematics in [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge), beginning in 1895, and became [senior wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_wrangler) in 1898. In the same year he was elected as a Fellow of St John's. He moved to [University College, Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) as a lecturer in 1902, and defended a doctorate (D.Sc.) at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) in 1903.

In 1904, Hudson died in a mountaineering accident in Snowdonia at the age of 28.[^o6]

In 1905, his posthumously-published book *Kummer's Quartic Surface* has gone on to become one of the most foundational texts in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).[^o7]
