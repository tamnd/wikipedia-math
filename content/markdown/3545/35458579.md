# Alexander Braverman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Braverman"}

**Alexander Emmanuilovich Braverman** (Александр Эммануилович Браверман, אלכסנדר ברוורמן; born June 8, 1974) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Jews) mathematician.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Braverman was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow).. He earned in 1993 a BA degree in mathematics from the [University of Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tel_Aviv), where in 1998 he received a Ph.D. (*Kazhdan-Laumon Representations of Finite Chevalley Groups, Character Sheaves and Some Generalization of the Lefschetz-Verdier Trace Formula*) under supervision of [Joseph Bernstein](wikipedia://en/5815383). From 1997 to 1999 he was a [C.L.E. Moore instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_instructor) at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and in 2004 Benjamin Peirce Lecturer at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He was an associate professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) from 2004 to 2009 and then a full professor from 2009 to 2015. He is a full professor at [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) since 2015 and an associate faculty member at [Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics). He was also a visiting scholar at [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1997, 1999), the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI) and the Paris-Nord, the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) in Jerusalem, the [Weizmann Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute), [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) and at the [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES) in Paris.

Braverman specializes in the [geometric Langlands program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_Langlands_program), the intersection of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), which also has applications to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

In 2006 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) (*Spaces of quasi-maps into the flag varieties and their applications*).
