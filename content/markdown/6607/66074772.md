# Ewens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ewens"}

**Ewens** is a surname. Notable people with this surname include:

:::

- Leonard Ewens, Australian politician, [mayor of Walkerville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_the_Town_of_Walkerville)
- [Maurice Ewens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Ewens) (1611–1687), English Jesuit and author
- [Percival Ewens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percival_Ewens), English cricket player
- Sarah Ewens
- [Warren Ewens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Ewens) (born 1937), Australian mathematician
