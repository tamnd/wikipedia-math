# Zdzisław Pawlak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdzisław Pawlak"}

**Zdzislaw I. Pawlak** (10 November 1926 – 7 April 2006) was a Polish mathematician and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). He was affiliated with several organization, including the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [Warsaw School of Information Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_School_of_Information_Technology). He served as the director of the Institute of Computer Science at the [Warsaw University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University_of_Technology) (1989–96). Pawlak was known for his contribution to many branches of [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392). He is credited with introducing the [rough set theory](wikipedia://en/1634778) and also known for his fundamental works on it.[^o3] He also introduced the [Pawlak flow graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawlak_flow_graphs), a graphical framework for reasoning from data.[^o4] He was conferred with [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta) in 1999.[^o5] He was also a full member of [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zdzislaw Pawlak was born on 10 November 1926 in [Łódź](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łódź), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic).[^o5] He graduated from a public elementary school in 1939. In 1946 he passed his Baccalaureate Diploma examination, and in 1947 he began studies at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering of the [Łódź University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łódź_University_of_Technology). Two years later, he moved to the Faculty of Telecommunications at the [Warsaw University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University_of_Technology). He received his M.Sc. degree in Telecommunications in 1951, after which he worked in the Institute of Mathematics of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) until 1957.
