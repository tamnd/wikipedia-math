::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo great rhombicuboctahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pseudo great rhombicuboctahedron** is one of the two [pseudo uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/35753477), the other being the convex [elongated square gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128551) or pseudo rhombicuboctahedron. It has the same vertex figure as the [nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157120) (a uniform polyhedron), but is not a uniform polyhedron (due to not being [isogonal](wikipedia://en/581560)), and has a smaller symmetry group. It can be obtained from the great rhombicuboctahedron by taking a square face and the 8 faces with a common vertex to it (forming a [crossed square cupola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed_square_cupola)) and rotating them by an angle of $\tfrac{\pi}{4}$. It is related to the [nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157120) in the same way that the pseudo rhombicuboctahedron is related to the [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213).

:::
