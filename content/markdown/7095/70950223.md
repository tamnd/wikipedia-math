# Jean Dhombres

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Dhombres"}

**Jean Guy Dhombres** (born 27 August 1942 in Paris) is a French mathematician, historian of mathematics and the mathematical sciences, and biographer of [Lazare Carnot](wikipedia://en/26532764).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dhombres graduated from the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in the class of 1962. He received in 1970 his doctorate in mathematics from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6), which is now part of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University). He was from 1964 to 1971 a [*maître de conférences*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France) at the [École nationale supérieure d'ingénieurs de constructions aéronautiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_nationale_supérieure_d'ingénieurs_de_constructions_aéronautiques) (ENSICA now part of [ISAE-SUPAERO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_supérieur_de_l'aéronautique_et_de_l'espace)) and from 1965 to 1972 an [*attaché de recherche en mathématiques*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France#Temporary_positions) at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). At the [University of Nantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nantes) he was from 1972 to 1978 a [*maître assistant*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France) and from 1980 to 1988 a professor; from 1972 to 1974 he also taught at the [École nationale des ponts et chaussées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_ponts_ParisTech). In the 1970s and early 1980s he held visiting professorships at Bangkok's [Asian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Institute_of_Technology) in 1971–1972, at the [University of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Singapore) in 1973, at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 1975, at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) in 1976–1979, and at the [University of Wuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wuhan) in 1980–1981. During his years in Ottawa he was also scientific advisor to the French Embassy in Canada.[^o5]

At the University of Nantes, Dhombres was director of the *Centre [François Viète](wikipedia://en/310238) d'histoire et philosophie des sciences et des techniques* from 1985 (when it was created) until 1995. He was director of studies in the history of exact science at the [EHESS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EHESS) from 1988 until his retirement. Since 1998 he has held visiting professorships at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) in 1998, at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1999, at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in 1999–2000, and at the [University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mexico) in 2002–2003 and again in 2008. He was in 2004 a visiting member of the U.S. [National Gallery of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Gallery_of_Art)'s Center for Advanced Study in the Visual Arts.[^o5]

Dhombres was from 1983 to 1987 the president of the *Société française d'histoire des sciences*. He was the directing editor of the book series *Un savant, une époque* for the publishing house *Belin éditeur* in Paris. He was the founding editor of the journal *Sciences et Techniques en Perspective* distributed by *Librairie Blanchard* and has been a member of the editorial boards of several journals, including *[Historia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/10841658)* and *[Physis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physis_%28journal%29)*.[^o5]

His mathematical research focuses on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). He has written extensively on many parts of the history of mathematics. Among his other honors, he was made [doctor honoris causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_honoris_causa) by the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in 1997.

He is married with two children and four grandchildren.[^o5]
