# Midpoint theorem (conics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Midpoint theorem"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **midpoint theorem** describes a property of parallel [chords](wikipedia://en/297947) in a [conic](wikipedia://en/19008673). It states that the midpoints of parallel chords in a conic are located on a common line.

:::

The common line or line segment for the midpoints is called the [diameter](wikipedia://en/8007). For a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277) or [hyperbola](wikipedia://en/14052) the diameter goes through its [center](wikipedia://en/4850775#Projective_conics). For a [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231) the diameter is always perpendicular to its [directrix](wikipedia://en/19008673#Eccentricity,_focus_and_directrix) and for a pair of intersecting lines (from a [degenerate conic](wikipedia://en/392586)) the diameter goes through the point of intersection.
