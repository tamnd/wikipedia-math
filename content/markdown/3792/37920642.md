# Lie-\* algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lie-* algebra"}

In mathematics, a **Lie-\* algebra** is a [D-module](wikipedia://en/3440755) with a Lie\* bracket. They were introduced by [Alexander Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613) and [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747),[^o2] and are similar to the **conformal algebras** discussed by Kac (1998)[^o3] and to **vertex Lie algebras**.

:::
