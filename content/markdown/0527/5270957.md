# Louis Charles Karpinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Charles Karpinski"}

**Louis Charles Karpinski** (5 August 1878[^o3] – 25 January 1956[^o4]) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) mathematician.

:::

## Background {#Background}

Louis Charles Karpinski was born on August 5, 1878, in [Rochester, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_New_York). His parents were Henry Hermanagle Karpinski of [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), Poland and Mary Louise Engesser of [Guebwiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guebwiller), France.[^o3][^o6][^o7] He earned his Bachelor of Arts at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1901 and his Ph.D. at [Universität Straßburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Straßburg) in 1903.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

At [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), Karpinski became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) and a university extension lecturer. He taught at [Berea College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea_College) and at the Normal School in [Oswego, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswego,_New_York), now [SUNY Oswego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Oswego). He then accepted a position at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), where he became a full professor of mathematics by 1919. He devoted his attention chiefly to the history and [pedagogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedagogy) of mathematics.

Karpinski served as the president of the [History of Science Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Science_Society) from 1943 to 1944.[^o9]

## Books {#Books}

An authority on the history of science, Karpinski was collaborator on the *Archivo di Storia della Scienza* and author of *The Hindu-Arabic Numerals*[^o11] with [David Eugene Smith](wikipedia://en/3711980) (1911), *Robert of Chester's Latin Translation of the Algebra of Al-Khowarizmi* (1915), and *Unified Mathematics* with [Harry Yandell Benedict](wikipedia://en/60238866) and [John William Calhoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_William_Calhoun) (1913).

- [*The Hindu-Arabic Numerals*](https://archive.org/details/hinduarabicnume02karpgoog) (with David Eugene Smith). Boston: Ginn and Company, 1911.
- [*Robert of Chester's Latin Translation of the Algebra of Al-Khowarizmi*](https://archive.org/details/robertofchesters00khuw), with an Introduction, Critical Notes and an English Version. New York: Macmillan Co., 1915.
- [*Mathematical Pamphlets*](https://books.google.com/books?id=rbvkAAAAMAAJ) (n.p.): (n.p.), 1915.
- [*Unified Mathematics*](https://archive.org/stream/unifiedmathemati00karpiala#page/vi/mode/2up) (with Harry Y. Benedict and John W. Calhoun). Boston: D.C. Heath and Company, 1918 and 1922.
- [*The History of Arithmetic*](https://archive.org/details/historyofarithme0000loui) Chicago: Rand McNally and Co., 1925.
- *Bibliography of the Printed Maps of Michigan, 1804–1880*. Lansing: Michigan Historical Commission, 1931.
- *Historical Atlas of the Great Lakes and Michigan*. Lansing: Michigan Historical Commission, 1931.
- *Bibliography of Mathematical Works Printed in America through 1850*. Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press, 1940.
