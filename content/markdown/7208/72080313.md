# Folgar-Tucker Model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Folgar-Tucker Model"}

The **Folgar-Tucker-Equation (FTE)** is a widespread and commercially applied model to describe the fiber orientation in injection molding simulations of fiber composites.

:::

The equation is based on [Jeffrey's equation](wikipedia://en/53606876) for fibers suspended in melts, but, in addition, accounts for fiber-fiber interactions.[^o2] Tucker and Advani then integrate over an ensemble of fibers and hence obtain an evolution equation for the orientation/alignment tensor[^o3] as a [Field (physics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_%28physics%29). A compact way to express it is[^o4]

$$\frac{dA}{dt}=W\cdot A-A\cdot W+\xi(D\cdot A+ A\cdot D-2A_4:D)+2C\dot{\gamma}(1-3A).$$

The scalar quantities are the shear rate $\dot{\gamma}$, the interaction coefficient C (for an isotropic diffusion) and the parameter accounting for the fibers aspect ratio $\xi$. $A_4$ is a fourth order tensor. Normally, $A_4$ is expressed as a function of A. The detection of the best suited function is known as closure problem. D and W are respectively the symmetric and antisymmetric part of the velocity gradient, while 1 represents the unit tensor. $:$ represents a contraction over two indices.

Thus the Folgar Tucker is a differential equation for the second order [tensor](wikipedia://en/29965) A, namely the orientation tensor. This evolution equation is in the frame of [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918) and is coupled to the velocity field.

Since different closure forms can be inserted, many possible formulations of the equations are possible.[^o5] For most of the closure forms the FTE results in a nonlinear [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309) (though a Lemma to linearize it for some popular closure was introduced [^o6]).

Analytical solutions to some versions of the FTE consists of both exponential, trigonometrical and hyperbolic functions.[^o6]

Numerically the FTE is solved also in commercial software for injection molding simulations. [^o7] [^o8]
