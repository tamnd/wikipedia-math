# Maxwell–Fricke equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maxwell–Fricke equation"}

The **Maxwell–Fricke equation** relates the [resistivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistivity) of [blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood) to [hematocrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hematocrit).[^o2] This relationship has been shown to hold for humans, and a variety on non-human warm-blooded species, including canines.[^o5]

:::

## Equation {#Equation}

The Maxwell–Fricke equation is written as:

$$\frac{\frac{\rho_1}{\rho}-1}{\frac{\rho_1}{\rho}+2} = \varphi\frac{\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}-1}{\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}+2}$$

where *ρ* is the resistivity of blood, *ρ*<sub>1</sub> is the resistivity of plasma, *ρ*<sub>2</sub> is the resistivity of blood cells and *φ* is the hematocrit.
