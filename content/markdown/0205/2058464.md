# Pre-algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pre-algebra"}

*Image: Visual proof of the Pythagorean theorem by area-preserving shearing.gif.* A visual proof of the Pythagorean theorem

**Pre-algebra** is a common name for a [course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Course_%28education%29) taught in [middle school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_school) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in the United States, usually taught in the 6th, 7th, 8th, or 9th grade.[^o2] The main objective of it is to prepare students for the study of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). Usually, Algebra I is taught in the [8th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighth_grade) or [9th grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninth_grade).[^o3]

:::

As an intermediate stage after [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), pre-algebra helps students pass specific conceptual barriers. Students are introduced to the idea that an [equals sign](wikipedia://en/501828), rather than just being the answer to a question as in basic arithmetic, means that two sides are [equivalent](wikipedia://en/90446) and can be manipulated together. They may also learn how numbers, [variables](wikipedia://en/3728109), and words can be used in the same ways.[^o4]

## Subjects {#Subjects}

Subjects taught in a pre-algebra course may include:

- Review of [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) arithmetic
- Types of numbers such as [integers](wikipedia://en/14563), [fractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraction_%28mathematics%29), [decimals](wikipedia://en/8214) and [negative numbers](wikipedia://en/154616)
- [Ratios](wikipedia://en/87837) and [percents](wikipedia://en/64493)
- [Factorization](wikipedia://en/15491) of [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474)
- Properties of operations such as [associativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associativity) and [distributivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributivity)
- Simple (integer) [roots](wikipedia://en/235029) and [powers](wikipedia://en/99491)
- Rules of evaluation of expressions, such as [operator precedence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_precedence) and use of [parentheses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parentheses)
- Basics of [equations](wikipedia://en/9284), including rules for invariant manipulation of equations
- Understanding of variable manipulation
- Manipulation and plotting in the standard [4-quadrant Cartesian coordinate plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-quadrant_Cartesian_coordinate_plane)
- Powers in [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715) (example: 340,000,000 in scientific notation is 3.4 × 10<sup>8</sup>)
- Identifying [Probability](wikipedia://en/22934)
- Solving [Square roots](wikipedia://en/29208)
- [Pythagorean Theorem](wikipedia://en/26513034)[^o6]

Pre-algebra may include subjects from [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), especially to further the understanding of algebra in applications to [perimeter](wikipedia://en/23636), [area](wikipedia://en/1209), and [volume](wikipedia://en/32498).

Pre-algebra may also include subjects from [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) to identify [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and interpret data.

Proficiency in pre-algebra is an indicator of college success. It can also be taught as a remedial course for college students, particularly in [community colleges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_college).[^o7]
