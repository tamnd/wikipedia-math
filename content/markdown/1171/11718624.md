# Trend surface analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trend surface analysis"}

*Image: RSS troposphere stratosphere trend.png.* Worldwide temperature trend analysis

**Trend surface analysis**, also known as **trend surface mapping**, is a mathematical technique used in [archaeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeology) and [environmental sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_science) such as [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology) and [soil science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil_science). The method involves using low-order [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) of spatial coordinates to estimate a regular grid of points from scattered observations such as archeological finds or [soil survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil_survey) results.[^o2]

:::
