# Romberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Romberg"}

**Romberg** is a German surname which may refer to:

:::

- [Andreas Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Romberg) (1767–1821), German composer, violinist
- [Bernhard Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernhard_Romberg) (1767–1841), German cellist and composer
- [Brett Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Romberg) (born 1979), American football player
- [Luci Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luci_Romberg), an American stuntwoman
- [Moritz Heinrich Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moritz_Heinrich_Romberg) (1795–1873), German physician (also see [Romberg's test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romberg's_test))
- [Sigmund Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmund_Romberg) (1887–1951), Hungarian-born American composer
- [Walter Romberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Romberg) (1928–2014), German politician
