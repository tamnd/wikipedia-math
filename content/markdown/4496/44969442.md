# Archimedean graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archimedean graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), an **Archimedean graph** is a [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) that forms the skeleton of one of the [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847). There are 13 Archimedean graphs, and all of them are [regular](wikipedia://en/85821), [polyhedral](wikipedia://en/24656270) (and therefore by necessity also [3-vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) [planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314)), and also [Hamiltonian graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph).[^o2]

:::

Along with the 13, the infinite sets of [prism graphs](wikipedia://en/45012199) and [antiprism graphs](wikipedia://en/45012210) can also be considered Archimedean graphs.[^o3]
