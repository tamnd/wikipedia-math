# David Silvester

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Silvester"}

**David James Silvester** (born 26 December 1958) is a British [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506). He has a Chair in Numerical Analysis and is the former Head of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) in the [Department of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/10387568) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Silvester was born in [Dumfries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumfries), but was educated at [Ysgol Ardudwy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ysgol_Ardudwy) (Wales) and the [University of Manchester Institute of Science and Technology (UMIST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology), from which he gained his B.Sc. in Mathematics (1980) and Ph.D. in Numerical Analysis (1983). His Ph.D. thesis (*An Analysis of Finite Element Approximation for Swirling Flow*) was supervised by Ronald Thatcher. He was appointed lecturer in Mathematics at UMIST in 1984, and later promoted to Senior Lecturer and Reader. In 2003 he was promoted to a personal Chair of Numerical Analysis.[^o7] Silvester held visiting positions in the Computer Science Department at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1991 (as a [Fulbright Senior Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program)) and in 1999, at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) (1994), the [University of the Littoral Opal Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Littoral_Opal_Coast) (2009), and the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) (2019).[^o8]

Silvester is best known for his work on finite element methods, fast iterative solvers for fluid dynamics, and uncertainty quantification.[^o9] He has more than 65 refereed publications[^o3] on topics such as iterative solution of Stokes and Navier–Stokes systems, preconditioning, and error estimation in finite element methods. Silvester's books include *Finite Elements and Fast Iterative Solvers: With Applications in Incompressible Fluid Dynamics*[^o9] and *Essential Partial Differential Equations* (co-authored with David F. Griffiths and [John W. Dold](wikipedia://en/76276064)).[^o10]

Silvester has served as the elected President of the UK and Republic of Ireland section of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (2009–2011). He became a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2023.[^o11] He is on the editorial board of the *[Journal of Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Scientific_Computing)*.[^o7]
