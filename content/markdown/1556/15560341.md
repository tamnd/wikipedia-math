# AREMOS

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AREMOS"}

**AREMOS** is a [data management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_management) and [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) package released by [Global Insight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Insight). It was most popular in the late 1980s and 1990s, when it was used by leading economists.[^o2][^o3] Developed as a DOS application by [Wharton Econometric Forecasting Associates – WEFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_Econometric_Forecasting_Associates_–_WEFA) now [IHS Markit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHS_Markit), it has gone through many iterations. Thomsons' Datastream macroeconomic databases which were accessible with AREMOS, were a key selling point.[^o4]

:::

The U.S. statistical agency [Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Economic_Analysis) ran AREMOS extensively until 2015.[^o5]

Today its use is largely [legacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legacy_system) and has been superseded by newer econometric packages such as [EViews](wikipedia://en/3933682), developed by WEFA successor company, IHS Markit. Although sales of AREMOS to new customers ended in late 2015, certain existing customers were given the option to purchase a perpetual license which doesn't expire until the end of 2036.
