# Xin Sun

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Xin Sun"}

**Xin Sun** () is a Chinese mathematician specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Sun graduated from Peking University in 2011 with a B.S. in mathematics. In 2017, he received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), under the supervision of [Scott Sheffield](wikipedia://en/40341916).[^o3] He was a Junior Fellow of the [Simons Society of Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) from 2017 to 2020, during which time he was a postdoctoral researcher at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o3] From 2020 to 2023 he was a faculty member at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), and he was a visiting researcher at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 2022 to 2023. As of 2023, he is a faculty member at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o3][^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Sun studies random curves and surfaces and their connections with [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774). His work explores, in particular, the relationships between [Schramm–Loewner evolution](wikipedia://en/6706108), Liouville quantum gravity and [Liouville conformal field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liouville_field_theory).[^o7]

With [Nina Holden](wikipedia://en/65366177), Sun proved that uniformly sampled triangulations converge to a Liouville quantum gravity disk under a [conformal embedding](wikipedia://en/50627), establishing joint convergence of distances, boundary lengths and areas.[^o8]

With Pierre Nolin, Wei Qian and Zijie Zhuang, Sun derived an exact expression for the [backbone exponent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percolation_critical_exponents) of critical [site percolation](wikipedia://en/187805) on the triangular lattice. This exponent describes how the probability of two disjoint open paths crossing an annulus decays as the annulus becomes thicker. They proved that it is a [transcendental number](wikipedia://en/30325), whereas all previously known percolation arm exponents were rational.[^o9][^o10]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Sun received the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) New Researcher Award in 2020.[^o12] He received the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 2023, given "for leading work on random planar maps and Liouville quantum gravity",[^o13] and the Frontiers of Science Award in Mathematics in 2024.[^o14] Sun was an invited speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o15]
