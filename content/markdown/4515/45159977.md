# Discrepancy (algebraic geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrepancy"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), given a pair (*X*, *D*) consisting of a [normal variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_variety) *X* and a [$\mathbb{Q}$-divisor](wikipedia://en/1693055#Q-divisors) *D* on *X* (e.g., [canonical divisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_divisor)), the **discrepancy** of the pair (*X*, *D*) measures the degree of the singularity of the pair.

:::
