# NUTS statistical regions of Albania

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Albania"}

The [Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) (NUTS) is a geographical standard used by [Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania) in order to divide its territory into regions at three different levels of specified classes of its population codified by the [Albanian Institute of Statistics](wikipedia://en/31272108) (INSTAT). Albania is a recognised [candidate country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_enlargement_of_the_European_Union) for membership of the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) (EU) and thus part of the classification.[^o2] The three hierarchical levels are known as NUTS-1, NUTS-2 and NUTS-3, moving from larger to smaller territorial divisions.[^o3]

:::
