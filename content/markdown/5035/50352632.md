# Juan José Nieto Roig

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juan José Nieto Roig"}

Nieto

**Juan José Nieto Roig** (born 1958, A Coruña) is a Spanish mathematician, professor of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago_de_Compostela) since 1991 and a Royal Galician Academy of Sciences Fellow. His most influential contributions to date are in the area of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations).

:::

Nieto received his [degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_degree) in mathematics at the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago_de_Compostela) in 1980. He was awarded a [Fulbright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright) scholarship and proceeded to the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas) at Arlington, where he worked with professor [Vangipuram Lakshmikantham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vangipuram_Lakshmikantham). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at the University of Santiago de Compostela in 1983.

Nieto's work may be considered to fall under the ambit of differential equations. His research interests are in [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939), fuzzy equations and epidemiological models. He is one of the most cited mathematicians in the world according to [Web of Knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Knowledge)[^o2] and appears in the Thomson Reuters Highly Cited Researchers list.[^o3] Nieto has also occupied different positions in the University of Santiago de Compostela, such as [Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of Mathematics and Director of the Mathematical Institute. He has also participated as editor in different mathematical journals, being [editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief) of the journal *Nonlinear Analysis: Real World Applications* from 2009 to 2012.[^o4] In 2016, Nieto was admitted as a Fellow of the Royal Galician Academy of Sciences.[^o5]
