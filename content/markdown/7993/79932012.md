# Highway network optimization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Highway network optimization"}

**Highway network optimization** is the problem of configuring highway networks to maximize economic and social utility. Numerous [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) techniques have been brought to bear on the problem, including [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730) and [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

Recent research has included work on treating the highway optimization problem as a dynamic system.[^o5]
