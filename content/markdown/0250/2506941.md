# Paul Zeitz

mathematician Paul A. Zeitz

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Zeitz"}

**Paul Andrew Zeitz** (born July 5, 1958) is a professor of mathematics at the [University of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Francisco) who works primarily in math enrichment.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

In 1974, Paul Zeitz won the [USA Mathematical Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USAMO) (USAMO) and was a member of the first American team to participate in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO). The following year he graduated from [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School).[^o5]

In 1981, he graduated from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) with a Bachelor of Arts in history.[^o6] After graduating he taught high school math for six years in San Francisco and Colorado Springs. He then went to [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley) and obtained a PhD in mathematics in 1992.[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

After graduating from UC Berkeley, Zeitz became a professor at the [University of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Francisco).[^o7]

In 2015, Zeitz co-founded [Proof School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_School), which is a private school for middle and high school grade levels that is focused on math. Proof School teaches mathematics from the perspective that it is a "joyous art form."[^o6][^o9] Zeitz also co-founded the Bay Area Mathematical Olympiad in 1999 and the San Francisco [Math Circle](wikipedia://en/5163815) in 2005.[^o7][^o10][^o11] , he is president of the Organization for Mathematical Engagement & Growth in America (OMEGA).[^o12]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2003, Zeitz received from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) one of the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Awards for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o14]

Zeitz has a lecture series called *Art and Craft of Mathematical Problem Solving* that he made for [The Teaching Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Teaching_Company) on their platform The Great Courses Plus.[^o15]
