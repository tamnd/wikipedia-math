# List of fractals by Hausdorff dimension

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of fractals by Hausdorff dimension"}

According to [Benoit Mandelbrot](wikipedia://en/3999), "A [fractal](wikipedia://en/10913) is by definition a set for which the [Hausdorff-Besicovitch dimension](wikipedia://en/14294) strictly exceeds the [topological dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_dimension)."[^o2] Presented here is a list of fractals, ordered by increasing Hausdorff dimension, to illustrate what it means for a fractal to have a low or a high dimension.

:::
