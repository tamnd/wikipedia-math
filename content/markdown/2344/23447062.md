# Spherical image

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spherical image"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **spherical image** of a [unit-speed curve](wikipedia://en/89246#Length_of_a_curve) is given by taking the curve's [tangent vectors](wikipedia://en/191999) as points, all of which must lie on the [unit sphere](wikipedia://en/27033090). The movement of the spherical image describes the changes in the original curve's direction[^o2] If $\alpha$ is a unit-speed curve, that is $\|\alpha^\prime \| = 1$, and $T$ is the [unit tangent vector field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frenet_frame) along $\alpha$, then the curve $\sigma = T$ is the spherical image of $\alpha$. All points of $\sigma$ must lie on the unit sphere because $\|\sigma\| = \| T\| = 1$.

:::
