# Plücker surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Plücker surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Plücker surface**, studied by (txt), is a [quartic surface](wikipedia://en/20133775) in 3-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) with a double line and 8 nodes.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

For any [quadric line complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadric_line_complex), the lines of the complex in a plane envelop a quadric in the plane. A Plücker surface depends on the choice of a quadric line complex and a line, and consists of points of the quadrics associated to the planes through the chosen line.[^o5]

:::
