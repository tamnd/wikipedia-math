# Robert Tucker (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Tucker"}

**Robert Tucker** (1832–1905) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who was secretary of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for more than 30 years.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Son of a soldier who fought in the [Peninsular War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_War), Tucker studied at [St. John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge), where he was 35th wrangler in 1855.[^n1] He mastered mathematics at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) from 1865 to 1899.

He is known for "Tucker circles", a family of circles [invariant](wikipedia://en/1126638) on parallel displacing.[^o5]

He is also known for editing the *Mathematical Papers* of [William Kingdon Clifford](wikipedia://en/250820) in 1882, and editing the *Book IV* of *[Elements of Dynamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elements_of_Dynamic)* in 1887.[^o6]

Tucker acted as secretary of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) from 1867 to 1902.[^n2]

He was also a collector of mathematician's photographs. His collection, named *Tucker collection* is preserved by the London Mathematical Society at De Morgan house.[^o8]
