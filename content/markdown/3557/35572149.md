# Sigmund Gundelfinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sigmund Gundelfinger"}

**Sigmund Gundelfinger** (14 February 1846 in [Kirchberg an der Jagst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirchberg_an_der_Jagst) – 13 December 1910 in [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans)-[Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish)[^o2] mathematician who introduced the [Gundelfinger quartic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundelfinger_quartic) and proved the completeness of the invariants of a ternary cubic.

:::
