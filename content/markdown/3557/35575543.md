# Ibn al-Durayhim

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ibn al-Durayhim"}

**ʿAlī ibn Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz Ibn Futūḥ ibn Ibrahīm ibn Abū Bakr** (علي بن محمد بن عبد العزيز بن فتوح بن ابراهيم بن أبي بكر; 1312–1359/62 CE)[^o2]**,** known as **Ibn Durayhim al-Mawsilī** (ابن الدريهم الموصلي) was an [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab) [writer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writer), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [cryptologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptologist) and [scribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scribe).[^o4]

:::

## Cryptology {#Cryptology}

Ibn al-Durayhim gave detailed descriptions of eight cipher systems that discussed [substitution ciphers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_ciphers), leading to the earliest suggestion of a "[tableau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographic_tableau)" of the kind that two centuries later became known as the "[Vigenère table](wikipedia://en/157923)".[^o4]

His book entitled *Clear Chapters Goals and Solving Ciphers* (مقاصد الفصول المترجمة عن حل الترجمة) was recently discovered, but has yet to be published. It includes the use of the statistical techniques pioneered by [Al-Kindi](wikipedia://en/272065) and [Ibn 'Adlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_'Adlan).[^o5]

## *The Book on the Usefulness of Animals* {#The_Book_on_the_Usefulness_of_Animals}

In the year 1355 CE, Ibn al-Durayhim compiled the *Book on the Usefulness of Animals* (كتاب منافع الحيوان).[^o4] The work draws largely on the work of [Ibn Bakhtīshūʿ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Bakhtīshūʿ) and [Aristotle](wikipedia://en/308). The manuscript is now housed in the [Escorial Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escorial_Library) (Ar.898).[^o7] The text contains 91 illustrations of various animals.[^o7]
