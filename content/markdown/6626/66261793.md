# AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecture"}

The **AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecture** is an award and lecture series that "highlights significant contributions of women to applied or computational mathematics."[^o6] The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) planned the award[^o2] and lecture series in 2002 and first awarded it in 2003. The lecture is normally given each year at the SIAM Annual Meeting.[^o3] Award winners receive a signed certificate from the AWM and SIAM presidents.[^o4]

:::

The lectures are named after [Sonia Kovalevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonia_Kovalevsky) (1850–1891), a well-known [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) mathematician of the late 19th century.[^o5] [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) regarded Kovalevsky as his most talented student. In 1874, she received her Doctor of Philosophy degree from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) under the supervision of Weierstrass. She was granted *[privatdozentin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)* status and taught at the [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) in 1883; she became an ordinary professor (the equivalent of full professor) at this institution in 1889. She was also an editor of the journal *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)*. Kovalevsky did her important work in the theory of partial differential equations and the rotation of a solid around a fixed point.[^o6]

- 2005 [Ingrid Daubechies](wikipedia://en/1430989), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), “Superfast and (Super)sparse Algorithms”
- 2006 [Irene Fonseca](wikipedia://en/38295848), [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), “New Challenges in the Calculus of Variations”
- 2007 [Lai-Sang Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai-Sang_Young), [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute), “Shear-Induced Chaos”[^o9]
- 2008 [Dianne P. O'Leary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dianne_P._O'Leary), [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland), “A Noisy Adiabatic Theorem: Wilkinson Meets Schrödinger’s Cat”[^o10]
- 2009 [Andrea Bertozzi](wikipedia://en/37498758), [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California), Los Angeles
- 2010 [Suzanne Lenhart](wikipedia://en/46919225), [University of Tennessee at Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee_at_Knoxville), “Mixing it up: Discrete and Continuous Optimal Control for Biological Models”
- 2011 [Susanne C. Brenner](wikipedia://en/40807599), [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University), “A Cautionary Tale in Numerical PDEs”[^o11]
- 2012 [Barbara Keyfitz](wikipedia://en/38347715), [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), “The Role of Characteristics in Conservation Laws”
- 2013 [Margaret Cheney](wikipedia://en/56705936), [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University), “Introduction to Radar Imaging”

- 2015 [Linda J. S. Allen](wikipedia://en/54442688), [Texas Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Tech_University), “Predicting Population Extinction”[^o2]
- 2016 [Lisa J. Fauci](wikipedia://en/47770535), [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University), “Biofluids of Reproduction: Oscillators, Viscoelastic Networks and Sticky Situations”[^o5]

- 2019 [Catherine Sulem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Sulem), [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), “The Dynamics of Ocean Waves”

- 2021 [Vivette Girault](wikipedia://en/60435659), [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre_et_Marie_Curie), "From linear poroelasticity to nonlinear implicit elastic and related models"[^o13]
- 2022 [Anne Greenbaum](wikipedia://en/56288604), [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), "Two of my Favorite Problems”[^o14]
- 2023 [Annalisa Buffa](wikipedia://en/50494257), [Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL), "Simulation of PDEs on Geometries Obtained via Boolean Operations"[^o15]
