# Encyclopedia of Cryptography and Security

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Encyclopedia of Cryptography and Security"}

The ***Encyclopedia of Cryptography and Security*** is a comprehensive work on [Cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) for both [information security](wikipedia://en/15036) professionals and experts in the fields of [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science), [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics), [Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), [Information Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Theory), Data Encryption, etc.[^o3] It consists of 460 articles in alphabetical order and is available electronically and in print. The Encyclopedia has a representative Advisory Board consisting of 18 leading international specialists.

:::

Topics include but are not limited to authentication and identification, copy protection, cryptoanalysis and security, factorization algorithms and [primality tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primality_tests), [cryptographic protocols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographic_protocols), key management, electronic payments and digital certificates, [hash functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hash_function) and MACs, [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) cryptography, [quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/28676005) and [web security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_security).

The style of the articles is of explanatory character and can be used for undergraduate or graduate courses.
