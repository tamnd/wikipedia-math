In 8-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **2<sub>51</sub>** honeycomb is a space-filling [uniform tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb). It is composed of [2<sub>41</sub> polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_2_41_polytope) and [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex) [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) arranged in an [8-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It is the final figure in the [2<sub>k1</sub> family](wikipedia://en/18621102).

:::

::: {#o10 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 9 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 8-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram).

Removing the node on the short branch leaves the [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex).

Removing the node on the end of the 5-length branch leaves the [2<sub>41</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_2_41_polytope).

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [8-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube), 1<sub>51</sub>.

The [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) is the vertex figure of the vertex figure. This makes the [rectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-simplex), 0<sub>51</sub>.
