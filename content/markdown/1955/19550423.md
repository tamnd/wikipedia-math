In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **1<sub>52</sub> honeycomb** is a [uniform tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb) of 8-dimensional Euclidean space. It contains **[1<sub>42</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_1_42_polytope)** and **[1<sub>51</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiocteract)** [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647), in a [birectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_8-simplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It is the final figure in the [1<sub>k2</sub> polytope](wikipedia://en/18621272) family.

:::

::: {#o9 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 9 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 8-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram).

Removing the node on the end of the 2-length branch leaves the [8-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube), 1<sub>51</sub>.

Removing the node on the end of the 5-length branch leaves the [1<sub>42</sub>](wikipedia://en/19091290).

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [birectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_8-simplex), 0<sub>52</sub>.
