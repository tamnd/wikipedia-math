# Honda–Tate theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Honda–Tate theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Honda–Tate theorem** classifies [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties) over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615) up to [isogeny](wikipedia://en/1085176). It states that the isogeny classes of simple abelian varieties over a finite field of order *q* correspond to [algebraic integers](wikipedia://en/98981) all of whose conjugates (given by eigenvalues of the [Frobenius endomorphism](wikipedia://en/1088270) on the first [cohomology group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) or [Tate module](wikipedia://en/3094921)) have absolute value $\sqrt{q}$.

:::

(txt) showed that the map taking an isogeny class to the eigenvalues of the Frobenius is injective, and (txt) showed that this map is surjective, and therefore a bijection.
