# Journal of Econometrics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Econometrics"}

The ***Journal of Econometrics*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) covering [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390). It was established in 1973.[^o3] The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Michael Jansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jansson) ([University of California Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_Berkeley)) and [Aureo de Paula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aureo_de_Paula) ([University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London)).[^o4] According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2023 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 9.9.[^o5]

:::

The journal covers work dealing with estimation and other [methodological aspects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology_of_econometrics) of the application of statistical inference to economic data, as well as papers dealing with the application of econometric techniques to [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics).

Unusually among journals the title of Fellow of Journal of Econometrics is offered to anyone publishing four or more articles in the Journal.[^o6]
