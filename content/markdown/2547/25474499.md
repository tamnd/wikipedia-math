# Trigintaduonion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trigintaduonion"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **trigintaduonions**, also known as the **32-ions**, **32-nions**, **2<sup>5</sup>-nions** form a 32-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/38267) [noncommutative](wikipedia://en/294390) and [nonassociative](wikipedia://en/1335) [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) over the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Names {#Names}

The word *trigintaduonion* is derived from Latin *triginta* 'thirty' + *duo* 'two' + the suffix -*nion*, which is used for [hypercomplex number](wikipedia://en/51438) systems. Other names include *32-ion*, *32-nion*, *2<sup>5</sup>-ion*, and *2<sup>5</sup>-nion*. Trigintaduonions have also been referred to as *pathions* ($\mathbb P$),[^o6][^o7] which Robert P. C. de Marrais had named in reference to the 32 paths of wisdom from the [Kabbalistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabbalah) ([Jewish mystical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_mysticism)) text *[Sefer Yetzirah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sefer_Yetzirah)*. However, this term is not generally accepted as standard terminology.

::: {#o9 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Every trigintaduonion is a [linear combination](wikipedia://en/55632) of the unit trigintaduonions $e_0$, $e_1$, $e_2$, $e_3$, ..., $e_{31}$, which form a [basis](wikipedia://en/18420) of the [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) of trigintaduonions. Every trigintaduonion can be represented in the form

$$x = x_0 e_0 + x_1 e_1 + x_2 e_2 + \cdots + x_{30} e_{30} + x_{31} e_{31}$$

with real coefficients $x_{i}$.

The trigintaduonions can be obtained by applying the [Cayley–Dickson construction](wikipedia://en/175609) to the [sedenions](wikipedia://en/51434).[^o10] Applying the Cayley–Dickson construction to the trigintaduonions yields a 64-dimensional algebra called the *64-ions*, *64-nions*, *sexagintaquatronions*, or *sexagintaquattuornions*.

As a result, the trigintaduonions can also be defined as the following.[^o10]

An algebra of dimension 4 over the [octonions](wikipedia://en/51436) $\mathbb{O}$:

$\sum_{i=0}^{3} a_i \cdot e_i$ where $a_i \in \mathbb{O}$ and $e_i \notin \mathbb{O}$

An algebra of dimension 8 over [quaternions](wikipedia://en/51440) $\mathbb{H}$:

$\sum_{i=0}^{7} a_i \cdot e_i$ where $a_i \in \mathbb{H}$ and $e_i \notin \mathbb{H}$

An algebra of dimension 16 over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) $\mathbb{C}$:

$\sum_{i=0}^{15} a_i \cdot e_i$ where $a_i \in \mathbb{C}$ and $e_i \notin \mathbb{C}$

An algebra of dimension 32 over the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) $\mathbb{R}$:

$\sum_{i=0}^{31} a_i \cdot e_i$ where $a_i \in \mathbb{R}$ and $e_i \notin \mathbb{R}$

$\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}, \mathbb{O}, \mathbb{S}$ are all [subsets](wikipedia://en/27631) of $\mathbb{T}$. This relation can be expressed as:

$$\mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C} \subset \mathbb{H} \subset \mathbb{O} \subset \mathbb{S} \subset \mathbb{T} \subset \cdots$$

:::

## Multiplication {#Multiplication}

::: {#o13 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

### Properties {#Properties}

Like [octonions](wikipedia://en/51436) and [sedenions](wikipedia://en/51434), [multiplication](wikipedia://en/20845) of trigintaduonions is neither [commutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative) nor [associative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associative). However, being products of a Cayley–Dickson construction, trigintaduonions have the property of [power associativity](wikipedia://en/25054), which can be stated as that, for any element $x$ of $\mathbb{T}$, the power $x^n$ is well defined. They are also [flexible](wikipedia://en/36035264), and multiplication is distributive over addition.[^o14] As with the sedenions, the trigintaduonions contain [zero divisors](wikipedia://en/51441) and are thus not a [division algebra](wikipedia://en/26549625). Whereas the sedenions have 84 zero divisors,[^o15] the trigintaduonions have 1,260 zero divisors derived from 147 distinct triples.[^o16] Furthermore, in contrast to the octonions, both algebras do not even have the property of being [alternative](wikipedia://en/3160).

:::

### Geometric representations {#Geometric_representations}

Whereas [octonion](wikipedia://en/51436) unit multiplication patterns can be geometrically represented by [PG(2,2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PG%282,2%29) (also known as the [Fano plane](wikipedia://en/390404)) and [sedenion](wikipedia://en/51434) unit multiplication by [PG(3,2)](wikipedia://en/59836267), trigintaduonion unit multiplication can be geometrically represented by PG(4,2).

## Applications {#Applications}

The trigintaduonions have applications in [quantum physics](wikipedia://en/25202) and other branches of modern physics.[^o19] More recently, the trigintaduonions and other hypercomplex numbers have also been used in research on [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network)[^o24] and [digital signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signal_processing).[^o6]
