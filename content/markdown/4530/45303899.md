# Birge Huisgen-Zimmermann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Birge Huisgen-Zimmermann"}

**Birge Katharina Huisgen-Zimmermann** is a mathematician at [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara) specializing in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Huisgen-Zimmerman was born in Germany. Her father was the chemistry professor [Rolf Huisgen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Huisgen). She received her Ph.D. from [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) in 1974 under the supervision of Friedrich Kasch. Huisgen-Zimmerman received her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) in 1979, and stayed on the faculty at the Technical University of Munich until 1981. She became a researcher at the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft), a faculty member at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa), and a professor with a personal chair at the [University of Passau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Passau), before moving to Santa Barbara in 1987.[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Huisgen-Zimmerman became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
