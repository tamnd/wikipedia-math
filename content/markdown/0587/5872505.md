# Friedrich Schottky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Schottky"}

**Friedrich Hermann Schottky** (24 July 1851 – 12 August 1935) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [elliptic](wikipedia://en/69939), [abelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_function), and [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745) and introduced [Schottky groups](wikipedia://en/5871034) and [Schottky's theorem](wikipedia://en/34544431).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Friedrich Hermann Schottky was born in [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) (now Wrocław, [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)). His father, Dr. Hermann Friedrich Schottky, was an English teacher and his mother, Louise Winkler, was a florist. He attended Maria-Magdalenen-Gymnasium from 1860 to 1870, where his classmates included Max Grube, Heinrich Rosin, and [Eberhard Gothein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eberhard_Gothein).[^o6] From 1870 to 1874 he attended the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau).[^o3]

In 1875 Schottky received his doctorate, studying under [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) and [Hermann von Helmholtz](wikipedia://en/53311) at [Friedrich Wilhelm University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wilhelm_University_of_Berlin).[^o7]

Schottky was a lecturer at the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau) from 1878 to 1882, a professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) from 1882 to 1892, and a professor at [Philipps University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipps_University_of_Marburg) from 1892 to 1902. In 1902, through his friendship with [Ferdinand Georg Frobenius](wikipedia://en/341048), Schottky was able to obtain a professorship at [Friedrich Wilhelm University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wilhelm_University_of_Berlin) where he would remain until his retirement in 1922.[^o8]

Schottky was elected to the Berlin Academy of Sciences in 1903.[^o9]

Schottky died in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) in 1935.[^o3]

Schottky married Henriette Schottky (née Hammer, 1858–1947) and had 5 children, including the physicist [Walter H. Schottky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_H._Schottky) and the botanist [Ernst Max Schottky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Max_Schottky).[^o3]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Schottky's thesis on [conformal mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_mapping) was well regarded by Weierstrass. Published in journal form in 1877, it introduced [automorphic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_functions) and [Schottky groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_groups), to be developed several years later by [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740) and [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887).[^n1][^o3]

Schottky's 1887 paper contributed to the theory of [Poincaré series](wikipedia://en/24146591).[^n2]

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the problem of characterizing when the [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties) of an algebraic curve are [Jacobian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_varieties) is called the [Schottky problem](wikipedia://en/4125909). Initially posed by [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980), Schottky obtained the first results, for the case of genus 4,[^n3] and made subsequent progress with his student [Heinrich Jung](wikipedia://en/37050659).[^n4] The problem was formally solved in 1986 by [Takahiro Shiota](wikipedia://en/32594980)[^o15] but remains an area of active research.[^o16]

In [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [Schottky's theorem](wikipedia://en/34544431) shows the existence of a bound on the value a holomorphic function can take on the unit disk.[^n5]
