# Bernard Helffer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Helffer"}

*Image: Helffer bernard.jpg.* Bernard Helffer, Oberwolfach 2005

**Bernard Helffer** (born 8 January 1949, Paris) is a French mathematician, specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), spectral theory, and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

He is the son of the pianist [Claude Helffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Helffer) and the [musicologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musicology) Mireille Helffer. Helffer studied from 1968 at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and received in 1976 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) his doctorate under Charles Goulaouic with dissertation *Hypoellipticité pour des classes d'opérateurs pseudodifférentiels à caractéristiques multiples*. From 1971 to 1978 he did research at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), from 1978 to 1989 he was a professor at the [University of Nantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nantes), and then he was a professor at the University Paris-Sud (and simultaneously taught for five years at the [École Normale Superieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Superieure)).

His research in mathematical physics deals with statistical mechanics, liquid crystals, superconductivity, semiclassical approximation, and ground-state nodal lines in Laplace operators and Schrödinger operators.

The [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) awarded Helffer in 1991 the *Prix Langevin* and in 2011 the *Prix de l'État*.

Helffer was from 1974 to 1978 the Secretary of the CNRS Mathematics Commission and from 1999 to 2004 a consultant for mathematics at the French Ministry of Education. From 2010 to 2012 he was the President of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205).[^o3]
