# Ingrid Van Keilegom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ingrid Van Keilegom"}

**Ingrid Van Keilegom** (born 24 December 1971 in [Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp)) is a Belgian statistician. She is a professor of statistics at [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven), and a part-time professor at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain). Her research interests include [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259), [non- and semiparametric regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-_and_semiparametric_regression), [quantile regression](wikipedia://en/14692673), [measurement errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_errors), and [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Van Keilegom earned a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in mathematical sciences from [Universitaire Instelling Antwerpen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp) in 1993, a master's degree in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) from [Limburgs Universitair Centrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hasselt) in 1998, and a doctorate in statistics from Limburgs Universitair Centrum in 1998. Her dissertation, *Nonparametric estimation of the conditional distribution in regression with censored data*, was supervised by Noël Veraverbeke.

After working as an assistant professor at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) (1998–1999) and [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) (1999–2000), she returned to Belgium in 2000 with a position at the Université catholique de Louvain. In 2016 she switched her full professorship there to a part-time position, to take another full professorship at KU Leuven.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ingrid Van Keilegom gave the IMS Medallion lecture at the 2023 [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662) in Toronto, Canada. She was awarded an Honorary Doctorate from the [University of A Coruña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_A_Coruña) in Spain in 2022. She became an Elected member of the [Royal Flemish Academy of Belgium for Science and the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Flemish_Academy_of_Belgium_for_Science_and_the_Arts) in 2021. Van Keilegom has also been a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) since 2013, and she was named a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2008 "for contributions to statistical theory and methodology, especially semi- and nonparametric regression, survival analysis, and empirical likelihood methods."
