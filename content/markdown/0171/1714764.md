# Harmonic (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harmonic"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a number of concepts employ the word **harmonic**. The similarity of this terminology to that of [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic) is not accidental: the [equations of motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equations_of_motion) of vibrating strings, drums and columns of air are given by formulas involving [Laplacians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplacian); the solutions to which are given by [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) corresponding to their modes of vibration. Thus, the term "harmonic" is applied when one is considering [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) with [sinusoidal](wikipedia://en/324749) variations, or solutions of [Laplace's equation](wikipedia://en/36941) and related concepts.

:::
