# Hans Robert Müller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Robert Müller"}

**Hans Robert Müller** (26 October 1911 in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz) – 25 March 1999 in [Wolfenbüttel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfenbüttel)) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and director of mathematical institutes at two universities.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

From 1930 to 1935 he studied [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz) and the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology). In 1935 he passed the teaching examination ("Lehramtsprüfung") for mathematics and descriptive geometry. In 1936 he became assistant at the institute of mathematics at the University Graz, obtaining his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1937 in mathematics, the [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1939, and in 1940 he became a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent). In 1941 he was drafted into the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) (reaching the rank of [Unteroffizier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unteroffizier)), became a [prisoner of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_war) and was released in September 1945. In the same year he continued his work as teaching assistant in Graz. He obtained the title [Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent) and subsequently the title [extraordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_professor) in 1950, and was employed as a permanent assistant at the Graz University of Technology. In 1954 he became [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the [Ankara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_University). From 1956 until 1963 he was professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) where he was director of the institute of geometry. In 1963 he became professor at the [Braunschweig University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braunschweig_University_of_Technology) serving as the director of the institute of mathematics, also being the head of the department for natural science from 1963 until 1965, and [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) in 1977.[^o3]

He was mainly concerned with geometrical studies of [kinematics](wikipedia://en/65914), in particular in relation to the [Erlangen program](wikipedia://en/243382) and the works of [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998). He also authored some textbooks on geometry.

Some of his doctoral students were Reinhold Heinemann, Josef Hoschek, Heinz Linder, Klaus Meyer, Werner Pelzer.[^o4]
