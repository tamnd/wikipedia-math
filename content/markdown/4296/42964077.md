# Encompassment ordering

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Encompassment ordering"}

*Image: Encompassment ordering on terms s,t\_svg.svg.* Triangle diagram of two terms *s* ≤ *t* related by the encompassment preorder.

In [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392), in particular in [automated theorem proving](wikipedia://en/2546) and [term rewriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_rewriting), the **containment**,[^o2] or **encompassment**, [preorder](wikipedia://en/23582) (≤) on the set of [terms](wikipedia://en/24885593), is defined by[^o3]

:::

*s* ≤ *t* if a [subterm](wikipedia://en/24885593#Operations_with_terms) of *t* is a [substitution instance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_instance) of *s*.

It is used e.g. in the [Knuth–Bendix completion algorithm](wikipedia://en/614147).

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Encompassment is a [preorder](wikipedia://en/23582), i.e. [reflexive](wikipedia://en/200458) and [transitive](wikipedia://en/200463), but not [anti-symmetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-symmetric_relation),[^o6] nor [total](wikipedia://en/30330)[^o7]
- The [corresponding equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/23582#Constructions), defined by *s* ~ *t* if *s* ≤ *t* ≤ *s*, is [equality modulo renaming](wikipedia://en/24885593#Structural_equality).
- *s* ≤ *t* whenever *s* is a [subterm](wikipedia://en/24885593#Operations_with_terms) of *t*.
- *s* ≤ *t* whenever *t* is a [substitution instance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_instance) of *s*.
- The union of any well-founded [rewrite order](wikipedia://en/42966879) *R*[^o8] with (\<) is [well-founded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-founded), where (\<) denotes the [irreflexive kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreflexive_kernel) of (≤).[^o9] In particular, (\<) itself is well-founded.

:::
