# Yurii Nesterov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yurii Nesterov"}

**Yurii Evgenievich Nesterov** (Russian: Юрий Евгеньевич Нестеров) (born January 25, 1956) is a Russian mathematician, an internationally recognized expert in [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411), especially in the development of efficient [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms) and [numerical optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_optimization) analysis. He is currently a emeritus professor at the [University of Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain) (UCLouvain) and Corvinus University of Budapest, fractional professor at the [Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong,_Shenzhen) (CUHKSZ) and [Shenzhen Loop Area Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenzhen_Loop_Area_Institute).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1977, Yurii Nesterov graduated in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). From 1977 to 1992 he was a researcher at the [Central Economic Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Economic_Mathematical_Institute) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). Since 1993, he has been working at [UCLouvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain), specifically in the Department of Mathematical Engineering from the [Louvain School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louvain_School_of_Engineering), [Center for Operations Research and Econometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Operations_Research_and_Econometrics).

In 2024, Nesterov joined [School of Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Data_Science) of the [Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong,_Shenzhen) and [Shenzhen Loop Area Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenzhen_Loop_Area_Institute) as fractional professor.[^o5]

## Academic work {#Academic_work}

Nesterov is most famous for his work in convex optimization, including his 2004 book, considered a canonical reference on the subject.[^o7] His main novel contribution is an accelerated version of [gradient descent](wikipedia://en/201489) that converges considerably faster than ordinary gradient descent (commonly referred as Nesterov momentum, Nesterov Acceleration or [Nesterov accelerated gradient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nesterov_accelerated_gradient), in short — NAG).[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12] This method, sometimes called "FISTA", was further developed by Beck & Teboulle in their 2009 paper "A Fast Iterative Shrinkage-Thresholding Algorithm for Linear Inverse Problems".[^o13]

His work with [Arkadi Nemirovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkadi_Nemirovski) in their 1994 book[^o14] is the first to point out that the [interior point method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_point_method) can solve [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411) problems, and the first to make a systematic study of [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539) (SDP). Also in this book, they introduced the [self-concordant functions](wikipedia://en/14479711) which are useful in the analysis of [Newton's method](wikipedia://en/22145).[^o15]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2000, Nesterov received the [Dantzig Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_B._Dantzig_Prize).[^o17]

In 2009, Nesterov won the [John von Neumann Theory Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Theory_Prize).[^o18]

In 2016, Nesterov received the [EURO Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURO_Gold_Medal).[^o19]

In 2023, Yurii Nesterov and [Arkadi Nemirovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkadi_Nemirovski) received the WLA Prize in Computer Science or Mathematics, "for their seminal work in convex optimization theory".[^o20]

In 2026, Nesterov was awarded the [Carl Friedrich Gauss Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gauss_Prize) by the IMU at the ICM 2026.[^o21]
