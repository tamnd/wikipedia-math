# Heide Gluesing-Luerssen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heide Gluesing-Luerssen"}

**Heide Gluesing-Luerssen** (born 1961)[^o2] is a German mathematician specializing in [algebraic coding theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_coding_theory). She is currently the Royster Research Professor at [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gluesing-Luerssen earned her doctorate in 1991 from the [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen), and taught in the mathematics department of the [University of Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oldenburg) from 1993 to 2004. While there, she completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2000. She moved to the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) in 2004, and to Kentucky in 2007.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

She is the author of the book *Linear delay-differential systems with commensurate delays: an algebraic approach* (Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1770, Springer-Verlag, 2002).[^o8]
