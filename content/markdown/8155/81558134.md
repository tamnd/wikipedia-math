# Karola Mészáros

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karola Mészáros"}

**Karola Mészáros** is an American mathematician focusing on [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [geometric combinatorics](wikipedia://en/20898181), including the study of [Schur polynomials](wikipedia://en/3147062), [Schubert polynomials](wikipedia://en/31925388), [Ehrhart polynomials](wikipedia://en/316904), [flow polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_polytope), and [chip-firing games](wikipedia://en/54561208). She is a professor of mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mészáros was a student at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT), where she received a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 2005 and completed a Ph.D. in 2010 under the supervision of [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397).

After another year as a lecturer at MIT, she became a postdoctoral researcher and National Science Foundation Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) from 2011 to 2012, before joining Cornell in 2012 as H. C. Wang Assistant Professor of mathematics, a non-tenure-track position. She shifted to a tenure-track assistant professorship in 2014, and was promoted to associate professor in 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mészáros received a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award) in 2019. She was elected to the 2026 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Mészáros is married to Lionel Levine, also a mathematics professor at Cornell.[^o5]
