# Vesselin Dimitrov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vesselin Dimitrov"}

**Vesselin Atanasov Dimitrov** (in Bulgarian: *Веселин Атанасов Димитров*) is a [Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria) mathematician known for his work in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205), [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is currently a professor at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), having previously taught at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).

:::

Dimitrov received the [Oberwolfach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach_Prize) and the [David Goss Prize](wikipedia://en/8422003#David_Goss_prize) in 2022. In 2025, he received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) and the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195), and in 2026, the [Cole Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Nelson_Cole_Prize) in Number Theory.

## Research in mathematics {#Research_in_mathematics}

In 2019 Dimitrov proved the [Schinzel–Zassenhaus conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schinzel–Zassenhaus_conjecture) on algebraic units that are not roots of unity.[^o4][^o13][^o5]

Together with [Ziyang Gao](wikipedia://en/83288827) and [Philipp Habegger](wikipedia://en/67011825), he proved a uniform version of the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture).[^o13][^o6]

In collaboration with [Frank Calegari](wikipedia://en/63299027) and [Yunqing Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056), Dimitrov proved the unbounded denominators conjecture of [Atkin](wikipedia://en/3721430) and [Swinnerton-Dyer](wikipedia://en/2105540): if a modular form $f(\tau)$ is not modular for some [congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694) of the [modular group](wikipedia://en/325019), then the Fourier coefficients of $f(\tau)$ have unbounded denominators.[^o13][^o7]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

In 2005, he won a silver medal in the [International Mathematics Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Olympiad), representing Bulgaria.[^o9]

He earned a PhD from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 2017 under the supervision of [Alexander Goncharov](wikipedia://en/32114133). His thesis is titled "Diophantine approximations by special points and applications to Dynamics and Geometry".

## Awards {#Awards}

Dimitrov's work has been recognized by the following awards:

- 2022: [David Goss Prize](wikipedia://en/8422003#David_Goss_prize) (shared with Ziyang Gao)[^o12]; [Oberwolfach Prize](wikipedia://en/1368669#Oberwolfach_Prize)[^o13]
- 2023: IMI Mathematics Prize (Institute of Mathematics and Informatics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences)[^o14]
- 2025: [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize), "for fundamental contributions to Diophantine geometry and number theory";[^o15] [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195).[^o16]
- 2026: [New Horizons in Mathematics Prize](wikipedia://en/43125933#New_Horizons_in_Mathematics_Prize) (shared with [Yunqing Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056)); [Cole Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Nelson_Cole_Prize) in Number Theory (shared with [Frank Calegari](wikipedia://en/63299027) and Yunqing Tang), for their paper "The unbounded denominators conjecture".[^o17]
