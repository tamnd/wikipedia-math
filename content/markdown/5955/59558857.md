# Stabilization hypothesis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stabilization hypothesis"}

In mathematics, specifically in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **Baez–Dolan stabilization hypothesis**, proposed in (Baez Dolan 1995), states that [suspension](wikipedia://en/1251702) of a [weak *n*-category](wikipedia://en/7349264) has no more essential effect after *n* + 2 times.[^o3] Precisely, it states that the suspension functor $\mathsf{nCat}_k \to \mathsf{nCat}_{k+1}$ is an equivalence for $k \ge n + 2$.[^o4]

:::
