# Wallis's conical edge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wallis's conical edge"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **Wallis's conical edge** is a [ruled surface](wikipedia://en/705158) given by the [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108)

:::

$$x=v\cos u,\quad y=v\sin u,\quad z=c\sqrt{a^2-b^2\cos^2u}$$

where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are constants.

Wallis's conical edge is also a kind of [right conoid](wikipedia://en/23756785). It is named after the English mathematician [John Wallis](wikipedia://en/239290), who was one of the first to use [Cartesian](wikipedia://en/2202) methods to study [conic sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conic_sections).[^o2]
