# Balaban 11-cage

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Balaban 11-cage"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Balaban 11-cage** or **Balaban (3,11)-cage** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 112 vertices and 168 edges named after [Alexandru T. Balaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Balaban).

:::

The Balaban 11-cage is the unique (3,11)-[cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cage_graph). It was discovered by Balaban in 1973.[^o4] The uniqueness was proved by [Brendan McKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_McKay_%28mathematician%29) and [Wendy Myrvold](wikipedia://en/61886633) in 2003.

The Balaban 11-cage is a [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and can be constructed by excision from the [Tutte 12-cage](wikipedia://en/24384368) by removing a small subtree and suppressing the resulting vertices of degree two.[^o6]

It has independence number 52,[^o7] chromatic number 3, chromatic index 3, radius 6, diameter 8 and girth 11. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 3-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Balaban 11-cage is:

$$(x-3) x^{12} (x^2-6)^5 (x^2-2)^{12} (x^3-x^2-4 x+2)^2\cdot$$

$$\cdot(x^3+x^2-6 x-2) (x^4-x^3-6 x^2+4 x+4)^4 \cdot$$

$$\cdot(x^5+x^4-8 x^3-6 x^2+12 x+4)^8.$$

The automorphism group of the Balaban 11-cage is of order 64.[^o6]
