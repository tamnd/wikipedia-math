# Map (graph theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Map"}

*Image: Dodecahedron schlegel.svg.* A map with twelve pentagonal faces

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **map** is a subdivision of a surface such as the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410) into [interior-disjoint](wikipedia://en/55610#Interior-disjoint_shapes) regions, formed by [embedding a graph](wikipedia://en/8149170) onto the surface and forming connected components (faces) of the complement of the graph. That is, it is a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of the surface. A [map graph](wikipedia://en/50947627) is a graph derived from a map by creating a vertex for each face and an edge for each pair of faces that meet at a vertex or edge of the embedded graph.[^o2]

:::
