# Higman–Sims asymptotic formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Higman–Sims asymptotic formula"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/323707), the **Higman–Sims asymptotic formula** gives an asymptotic estimate on number of groups of prime power order.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $p$ be a (fixed) [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666). Define $f(n,p)$ as the number of isomorphism classes of groups of order $p^n$. Then:

$$f(n,p) = p^{\frac{2}{27}n^3 + \mathcal O(n^{8/3})}$$

Here, the [big-O notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big-O_notation) is with respect to $n$, not with respect to $p$ (the constant under the big-O notation may depend on $p$).

:::
