# Cristoforo Alasia de Quesada

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cristoforo Alasia de Quesada"}

**Cristoforo Alasia de Quesada** (1864–1918) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Alasia studied at the universities of Turin (under [Enrico D'Ovidio](wikipedia://en/55588432) and [Giuseppe Peano](wikipedia://en/13043))[^n1] and Rome (under [Luigi Cremona](wikipedia://en/1846002)). In 1893 began his academic career as mathematics professor in different high schools at [Sassari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sassari), [Tempio Pausania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempio_Pausania), [Oristano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oristano), [Ozieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozieri), [Brindisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brindisi) and, finally, at [Albenga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albenga).[^n2]

Alasia was well known at the beginning of 20th century for he was the founder of *Le Matematiche Pure e Applicate*,[^n3] a journal devoted to the improvement of scientific knowledge of mathematics teachers.[^n4]
