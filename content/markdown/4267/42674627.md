In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-3-7 hexagonal honeycomb** or (**6,3,7 honeycomb**) a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {6,3,7}.

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with seven hexagonal tilings existing around each edge and with an [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-3-8 hexagonal honeycomb** or (**6,3,8 honeycomb**) is a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {6,3,8}. It has eight [hexagonal tilings](wikipedia://en/2863719), {6,3}, around each edge. All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with infinitely many hexagonal tilings existing around each vertex in an [order-8 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/38323559) [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644).

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-3-infinite hexagonal honeycomb** or (**6,3,∞ honeycomb**) is a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {6,3,∞}. It has infinitely many [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) {6,3} around each edge. All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with infinitely many hexagonal tilings existing around each vertex in an [infinite-order triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/38758084) [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644).
