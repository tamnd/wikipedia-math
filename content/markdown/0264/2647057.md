# Trinomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trinomial"}

*Image: Pascal\_pyramid\_trinomial.svg.* Layers of [Pascal's pyramid](wikipedia://en/708349) derived from [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) in an upside-down [ternary plot](wikipedia://en/1861608) of the terms in the [expansions of the powers of a trinomial](wikipedia://en/693935)

In [elementary algebra](wikipedia://en/9710), a **trinomial** is a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) consisting of three terms or [monomials](wikipedia://en/357416).[^o2]

:::

## Examples of trinomial expressions {#Examples_of_trinomial_expressions}

1. $3x + 5y + 8z$ with $x, y, z$ variables
1. $3t + 9s^2 + 3y^3$ with $t, s, y$ variables
1. $3ts + 9t + 5s$ with $t, s$ variables
1. $ax^2+bx+c$, the [quadratic polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_polynomial) in standard form with $a,b,c$ variables.[^o4]
1. $A x^a y^b z^c + B t + C s$ with $x, y, z, t, s$ variables, $a, b, c$ nonnegative [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) and $A, B, C$ any constants.
1. $Px^a + Qx^b + Rx^c$ where $x$ is variable and constants $a, b, c$ are nonnegative integers and $P, Q, R$ any constants.

## Trinomial equation {#Trinomial_equation}

A trinomial equation is a [polynomial equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation) involving three terms. An example is the equation $x = q + x^m$ studied by [Johann Heinrich Lambert](wikipedia://en/93085) in the 18th century.[^o6]

### Some notable trinomials {#Some_notable_trinomials}

- The quadratic trinomial in standard form (as from above):

$$ax^2+bx+c$$

- [sum or difference of two cubes](wikipedia://en/82341#Sum/difference_of_two_cubes):

$$a^3 \pm b^3 = (a \pm b)(a^2 \mp ab + b^2)$$

- A special type of trinomial can be [factored](wikipedia://en/82341) in a manner similar to quadratics since it can be viewed as a quadratic in a new variable ($x^{n}$ below). This form is factored as:

$$x^{2n} + rx^n + s = (x^n + a_1)(x^n + a_2),$$

where

$$\begin{aligned} a_1+a_2 &= r\\ a_1 \cdot a_2 &= s. \end{aligned}$$

For instance, the polynomial $x^{2} + 3x + 2$ is an example of this type of trinomial with $n =\,1$. The solution $a_{1} = -2$ and $a_{2} = -1$ of the above system gives the trinomial factorization:

$$x^{2} + 3x + 2 = (x + a_{1})(x + a_{2}) = (x + 2)(x + 1).$$

The same result can be provided by [Ruffini's rule](wikipedia://en/766014#Example), but with a more complex and time-consuming process.
