# Heinrich Martin Weber

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Martin Weber"}

**Heinrich Martin Weber** (5 March 1842, [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Confederation) – 17 May 1913, [Straßburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straßburg), [Alsace-Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alsace-Lorraine), [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire), now [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] Weber's main work was in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is best known for his text *Lehrbuch der Algebra* published in 1895 and much of it is his original research in algebra and number theory. His work *Theorie der algebraischen Functionen einer Veränderlichen* (with [Dedekind](wikipedia://en/23475106)) established an algebraic foundation for [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181), allowing a purely algebraic formulation of the [Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/247261). Weber's research papers were numerous, most of them appearing in *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)* or *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)*. He was the editor of [Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980)'s collected works.

:::

Weber was born in [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg), [Baden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Baden), and entered the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) in 1860. In 1866 he became a privatdozent, and in 1869 he was appointed as extraordinary professor at that school. Weber also taught in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) at the [Federal Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Polytechnic_Institute) (today the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich)), at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg), and at the [Technische Hochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule) in [Charlottenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottenburg) (today [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)). His final post was at the [Kaiser-Wilhelm-Universität Straßburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg), [Alsace-Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alsace-Lorraine), where he died.

In 1893 in Chicago, his paper *Zur Theorie der ganzzahligen algebraischen Gleichungen* was read (but not by him) at the International Mathematical Congress held in connection with the [World's Columbian Exposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World's_Columbian_Exposition).[^o4] In 1895 and in 1904 he was president of the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung). His doctoral students include [Heinrich Brandt](wikipedia://en/600885), [E. V. Huntington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Vermilye_Huntington), [Louis Karpinski](wikipedia://en/5270957), and [Friedrich Levi](wikipedia://en/3752306).
