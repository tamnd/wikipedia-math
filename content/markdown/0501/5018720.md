# HM Government Communications Centre

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="HM Government Communications Centre"}

**His Majesty's Government Communications Centre** (**HMGCC**) is an organisation which provides electronics and software to support the communication needs of the [Government of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_United_Kingdom). Based at [Hanslope Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanslope_Park), near [Milton Keynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Keynes) in [Buckinghamshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckinghamshire), it is closely linked with the [Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign,_Commonwealth_and_Development_Office) and the [British intelligence agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_intelligence_agencies).[^o5]

:::

## History {#History}

HMGCC was founded in 1938, specialising in overseas bespoke wireless communications.[^o7] HMGCC used to have a communications centre at [Signal Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_Hill_%28England%29) near [Gawcott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gawcott), in Buckinghamshire. Stephen Ball was Chief Executive until 2000,[^o8] when Dr John Widdowson took over; Widdowson moved to [GCHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters) in 2005.[^o9] Sarah-Jill Lennard was CEO from 2008 to 2011.[^o10] Juliette Wilcox was CEO from 2019 to 2021.

## Structure {#Structure}

The organisation provides bespoke solutions to fit the needs of the government, its organisations, and specifically its intelligence assets. As of 2024, HMGCC had teams in the following areas:[^o12]

- [Software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering)
- [Manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing)
- [Corporate and support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_services)
- [Project and programme management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_management)
- [Systems engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering)
- [Hardware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_hardware) engineering
