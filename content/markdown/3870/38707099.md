# Truncated order-7 heptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-7 heptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-7 heptagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{7,7}, constructed from one heptagons and two tetrakaidecagons around every vertex.

:::
