# Snub heptaheptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub heptaheptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub heptaheptagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/12126635) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{7,7}, constructed from two regular heptagons and three equilateral triangles around every vertex.

:::
