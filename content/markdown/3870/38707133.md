# Order-4 octagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-4 octagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-4 octagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {8,4}. Its [checkerboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checkerboard) coloring can be called a **octaoctagonal tiling**, and Schläfli symbol of r{8,8}.

:::

## Uniform constructions {#Uniform_constructions}

There are four uniform constructions of this tiling, three of them as constructed by mirror removal from the \[8,8\] [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope). Removing the mirror between the order 2 and 4 points, \[8,8,1<sup>\+</sup>\], gives [\[(8,8,4)\], (\*884)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/884_symmetry) symmetry. Removing two mirrors as \[8,4<sup>\*</sup>\], leaves remaining mirrors [\*4444](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4444_symmetry) symmetry.

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents a hyperbolic [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope) of 8 mirrors meeting as edges of a regular hexagon. This symmetry by [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) is called (\*22222222) or (\*2<sup>8</sup>) with 8 order-2 mirror intersections. In [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502) can be represented as \[8<sup>\*</sup>,4\], removing two of three mirrors (passing through the octagon center) in the \[8,4\] symmetry. Adding a bisecting mirror through 2 vertices of an octagonal fundamental domain defines a trapezohedral [\*4422 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4422_symmetry). Adding 4 bisecting mirrors through the vertices defines [\*444 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/444_symmetry). Adding 4 bisecting mirrors through the edge defines \*4222 symmetry. Adding all 8 bisectors leads to full \*842 symmetry.

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular tilings with [octagonal](wikipedia://en/314575) faces, starting with the [octagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/27990286), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {8,n}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , progressing to infinity.

This tiling is also topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra and tilings with four faces per vertex, starting with the [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,4}, and Coxeter diagram , with n progressing to infinity.
