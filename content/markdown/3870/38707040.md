# Truncated order-7 square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-7 square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-7 square tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{4,7}.

:::
