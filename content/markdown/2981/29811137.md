# Gallup International Association

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gallup International Association"}

The **Gallup International Association** (**GIA**) is an association of [polling](wikipedia://en/277315) organizations based in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich), Switzerland. The Gallup International Association was founded in 1947 in Loxwood Hall, Sussex, UK. [George H. Gallup](wikipedia://en/100077) served as its first President, until his death in 1984.[^o2][^o3] In January 2018, the management headquarters was relocated to [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), Austria.[^o2]

:::

[Gallup, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallup_%28company%29) US and the Gallup International Association (GIA) were involved in a legal dispute over the use of the Gallup name.[^o4][^o5] Gallup International Association or its members are not related to Gallup Inc., headquartered in Washington DC, which is no longer a member of Gallup International Association.

The Board includes Kancho Stoychev (President),[^o6] Michael Nitsche (Executive Vice President),[^o2] Johnny Heald (Vice President),[^o7] [Andrey Milekhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Milekhin) (Vice President)[^o8] and Munqith Dagher (Regional Director, MENA).[^o9] Gallup International has collaborated with [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF),[^o10][^o11] [BBC World Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_World_Service),[^o12][^o13] and [Transparency International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transparency_International) for a couple of its surveys.[^o14] Its data has also been referenced by outlets such as *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)*,[^o15][^o16] *[The Economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Economist)*,[^o17][^o18] [BBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_News),[^o19] [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN),[^o20][^o21] and [Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuters).[^o22][^o23]
