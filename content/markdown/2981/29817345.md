# Spatial bifurcation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spatial bifurcation"}

**Spatial bifurcation** is a form of [bifurcation theory](wikipedia://en/2326042). The classic bifurcation analysis is referred to as an [ordinary differential equation](wikipedia://en/32742753) system, which is independent on the spatial variables. However, most realistic systems are spatially dependent. In order to understand spatial variable system ([partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564)), some scientists try to treat with the spatial variable as time and use the AUTO package[^o2] get a bifurcation results.[^o3][^o4]

:::

The weak nonlinear analysis will not provide substantial insights into the nonlinear problem of [pattern selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_selection). To understand the pattern selection mechanism, the method of spatial dynamics is used,[^o5] which was found to be an effective method exploring the multiplicity of steady state solutions.[^o4][^o6]
