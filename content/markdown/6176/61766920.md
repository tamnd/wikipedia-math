# Joyce Snell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joyce Snell"}

**E. Joyce Snell** (born 1930) is a British [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who taught in the mathematics department at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London). She is known for her work on [residuals](wikipedia://en/461509) and [ordered categorical data](wikipedia://en/52904244), and for her books on statistics.

:::

## Books {#Books}

Snell is the author or editor of:

- *Analysis of Binary Data* (with [David R. Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771), 1969; 2nd ed., Chapman & Hall/CRC, 1989)
- *Applied Statistics: Principles and Examples* (with [David R. Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771), Chapman & Hall/CRC, 1981)
- *Applied Statistics: A Handbook of GENSTAT Analyses* (with H. R. Simpson, 1982)
- *Applied Statistics: A Handbook of BMDP Analyses* (Chapman & Hall/CRC, 1987)
- *Statistical Theory and Modelling: in Honour of Sir David Cox, FRS* (edited with [David Hinkley](wikipedia://en/9539349) and [Nancy Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Reid), Chapman & Hall/CRC, 1991)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Snell was given the Chambers Medal of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1986 "for her outstanding service to the Society", and in particular for her work organizing the 150th anniversary celebration for the society.
