# Claudia Klüppelberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claudia Klüppelberg"}

**Claudia Klüppelberg** (born May 23, 1953) is a German [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711) and applied [probability theorist](wikipedia://en/23542), known for her work in risk assessment and [statistical finance](wikipedia://en/7458786). She is a professor emerita of mathematical statistics at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Klüppelberg completed a doctorate in 1987 at the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim). Her dissertation, *Subexponentielle Verteilungen und Charakterisierungen verwandter Klassen*, was jointly supervised by Horand Störmer and Paul Embrechts.

She earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1993 at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). Then, she became a professor of applied statistics at [Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg_University_Mainz), and moved to the Technical University of Munich in 1997. She retired to become a professor emerita in 2019.

## Books {#Books}

Klüppelberg is the co-author of

- *Modelling Extremal Events: for Insurance and Finance* (with Paul Embrechts and Thomas Mikosch, Springer, 1997)

She is the co-editor of

- *Complex Stochastic Systems* (edited with [Ole Barndorff-Nielsen](wikipedia://en/6328849) and [David R. Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771), Chapman & Hall/CRC, 2001)
- *Risk - A Multidisciplinary Introduction* (edited with Daniel Straub and Isabell M. Welpe, Springer, 2014)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Klüppelberg was awarded the [Order of Merit of the Federal Republic of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Merit_of_the_Federal_Republic_of_Germany) and the Bavarian state order *Pro meritis scientiae et litterarum* in 2001. She is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and was a Medallion Lecturer of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics in 2009.
