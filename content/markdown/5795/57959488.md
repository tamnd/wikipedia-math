# Betül Tanbay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Betül Tanbay"}

**Betül Tanbay** (born 1960) is a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientist) and professor of mathematics at the [Boğaziçi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boğaziçi_University) in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) and the first woman president of the [Turkish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/56457493) between 2010 and 2016.[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Betül Tanbay was born in Istanbul and raised in [Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara) until 1977. She graduated from the [Lycée Janson de Sailly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Janson_de_Sailly), [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) in 1978. She received her Licence en Mathématiques from [Université Louis Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur_University), [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg) in 1982, and her PhD in Mathematics under the supervision of [Robert Solovay](wikipedia://en/3160110) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1989.[^o4]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

She has been a full time member and chairwoman in the Department of Mathematics, at Boğaziçi University, Istanbul. She has been the Vice-Provost for Foreign Affairs and represented her university at the [European University Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_University_Association) between 2004 and 2007.[^o6] She is the founding codirector of the [Istanbul Center for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/39297167) (IMBM). She has been in the scientific boards of research institutes such as IMBM, [Feza Gürsey Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feza_Gürsey_Institute),[^o7] Institut d'Etudes Avancées - Aix Marseille (IMéRA).[^o8] She was the director of a leading doctoral network project of [TÜBİTAK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_and_Technological_Research_Council_of_Turkey) between 2008 and 2012. She has worked as an executive committee member, including presidency, at the Turkish Mathematical Society;[^o9] as delegate, Ethics Committee member, Raising Awareness Committee member,[^o10] Executive Committee member at the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) where she was elected vice-president for the 2019-2022 period;[^o11][^o12] and as delegate, member of the Committee for Women in Mathematics at the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU).[^o13] In 2025 she was appointed as the Chair of the Governing Board of the IMU-IDM (International Day of Mathematics).[^o14]

### Visiting positions {#Visiting_positions}

She has held visiting positions at UC Berkeley and [UC Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), [Université de Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Bordeaux), [Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_mathématiques_de_Jussieu_-_Paris_Rive_Gauche), [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas), [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) and joined the Kadison-Singer Conjecture Workshop at the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005).

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

Her research interests include operator algebras and set theory.
