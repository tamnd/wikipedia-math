# Henry McKean

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Henry McKean"}

**Henry P. McKean, Jr.**[^o4] (December 10, 1930 – April 20, 2024) was an American mathematician at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) in [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He worked in various areas of [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1955 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [William Feller](wikipedia://en/206644).

:::

McKean was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1980. In 2007, he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for his life's work. In 1978, he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) (*Algebraic curves of infinite genus arising in the theory of nonlinear waves*). In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

His doctoral students include [Michael Arbib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Arbib), [Luigi Chierchia](wikipedia://en/62545142), [Donald A. Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_A._Dawson), [Harry Dym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Dym), [Daniel Stroock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Stroock), [Eugene Trubowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Trubowitz), [Victor Moll](wikipedia://en/69850885) and [Pierre van Moerbeke](wikipedia://en/8371350) and Uri Keich.

McKean died on April 20, 2024, aged 93.[^o6]

The American Mathematical Society published a Memorial Tribute to him in its March 2025 issue.[^o7]
