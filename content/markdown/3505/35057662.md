# Ditkin set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ditkin set"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), a **Ditkin set**, introduced by (), is a closed subset of the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) such that a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) *f* vanishing on the [set](wikipedia://en/26691) can be approximated by functions φ<sub>*n*</sub>*f* with φ vanishing in a neighborhood of the set.

:::
