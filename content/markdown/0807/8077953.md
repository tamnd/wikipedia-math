# Riemann–von Mangoldt formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Riemann–von Mangoldt formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Riemann–von Mangoldt formula**, named for [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980) and [Hans Carl Friedrich von Mangoldt](wikipedia://en/1928734), describes the distribution of the zeros of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809).

:::

The formula states that the number *N*(*T*) of zeros of the zeta function with imaginary part greater than 0 and less than or equal to *T* satisfies

$$N(T)=\frac{T}{2\pi}\log{\frac{T}{2\pi}}-\frac{T}{2\pi}+O(\log{T}).$$

The formula was stated by Riemann in his notable paper "[On the Number of Primes Less Than a Given Magnitude](wikipedia://en/536817)" (1859) and was finally proved by Mangoldt in 1905.

Backlund gives an explicit form of the error for all *T* > 2:

$$\left\vert{ N(T) - \left({\frac{T}{2\pi}\log{\frac{T}{2\pi}}-\frac{T}{2\pi} } - \frac{7}{8}\right)}\right\vert < 0.137 \log T + 0.443 \log\log T + 4.350 .$$

This result has since been improved upon, including by E. Hasanalizade, Q. Shen, and P-J. Wong:

$$\left|N(T)-\left(\frac T{2\pi}\log\frac T{2\pi}-\frac T{2\pi}\right)\right|\leq0.1038\log T+0.2573\log\log T+9.3675$$

for *T* ≥ *e*.[^n1]

Under the [Lindelöf](wikipedia://en/1465085) and [Riemann](wikipedia://en/19344125) hypotheses, the error terms can be improved to $o(\log{T})$ and $O(\log{T}/\log{\log{T}})$ respectively.[^n2]

A more precise formula is given by Anatolii A. Karatsuba and M. A. Korolev.[^n3]

Similarly, for any primitive Dirichlet character *χ* modulo *q*, we have

$$N(T,\chi)=\frac{T}{\pi}\log{\frac{qT}{2\pi e}}+O(\log{qT}),$$

where *N(T,χ)* denotes the number of zeros of *L(s,χ)* with imaginary part between *\-T* and *T*.
