# Riemann's existence theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Riemann's existence theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), **Riemann's existence theorem** states that the category of [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) is equivalent to the category of complex [complete algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/60516169).

:::

Sometimes, the theorem also refers to a generalization (a theorem of Grauert–Remmert),[^o2] which says that the category of finite topological coverings of a complex [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) is equivalent to the category of finite étale coverings of the variety.

## Original statement {#Original_statement}

Let $X$ be a compact Riemann surface, $p_1, \cdots, p_s$ distinct points in $X$ and $a_1, \cdots, a_s$ complex numbers. Then there is a [meromorphic function](wikipedia://en/80857) $f$ on $X$ such that $f(p_i) = a_i$ for all $1\leq i\leq s$.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

For now, see SGA 1, Expose XII, Théorème 5.1., or SGA 4, Expose XI. 4.3.

:::

## Consequences {#Consequences}

By definition, if $X$ is a complex algebraic variety, the [étale fundamental group](wikipedia://en/3022906) of $X$ at a geometric point $x$ is the [projective limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_limit)

$$\pi_1^{\textrm{et}}(X, x) = \varprojlim \operatorname{Aut}_X(Y)$$

over all finite Galois coverings $Y$ of $X$. By the existence theorem, we have

$$\operatorname{Aut}_X(Y) = \operatorname{Aut}_{X^{an}}(Y^{an}).$$

Hence, $\pi_1^{\textrm{et}}(X, x)$ is exactly the profinite completion of the usual topological [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) $\pi_1(X^{\textrm{an}}, x)$ of $X$ at $x$.[^o8]
