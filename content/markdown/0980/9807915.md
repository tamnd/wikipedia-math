# Percy John Daniell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Percy John Daniell"}

**Percy John Daniell** (9 January 1889 – 25 May 1946) was a pure and [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Daniell was born in [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valparaíso), [Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile). His family returned to [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) in 1895. Daniell attended [King Edward's School, Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Edward's_School,_Birmingham) and proceeded to [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) (where he was the last [Senior Wrangler](wikipedia://en/19355609) in 1909). At this time Daniell was an applied mathematician/[theoretical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_physicist).

## Mathematical career {#Mathematical_career}

For a year he lectured at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) and then he was appointed to the new [Rice Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas). The Rice Institute had him spend a year at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) studying with [Max Born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Born) and [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302). Daniell was at Rice from 1914 to 1923 when he returned to England to a chair at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield).

In a series of papers published between 1918 and 1928, he developed and expanded a generalized theory of integration and differentiation, which is today known as the [Daniell integral](wikipedia://en/2904201). In the setting of integration, he also worked on results that lead to the [Daniell-Kolmogorov extension theorem](wikipedia://en/6674542) in the theory of stochastic processes, independently of [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161).[^o5] He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1920 at Strasbourg.[^o6]

## Death {#Death}

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) Daniell advised the British [Ministry of Supply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Supply). The strain of work during the war took a heavy toll on his health. He died on 25 May 1946, after having collapsed at his home a few weeks earlier.
