# Robert Wedderburn (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Wedderburn"}

**Robert William Maclagan Wedderburn** (1947– June 1975) was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) statistician who worked at the [Rothamsted Experimental Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rothamsted_Experimental_Station). He was co-developer, with [John Nelder](wikipedia://en/5865261), of the [generalized linear model](wikipedia://en/747122) methodology,[^o2] and then expanded this subject to develop the idea of [quasi-likelihood](wikipedia://en/15214777).[^o3]

:::

Wedderburn was born in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh), where he attended [Fettes College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fettes_College), then studied for a degree and a diploma in statistics at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o5] He died aged 28 of [anaphylactic shock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaphylactic_shock) from an [insect bite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect_bites_and_stings) while on a canal holiday.[^o4]

"His colleagues remember him as someone of engaging diffidence, who would nonetheless hold his own in argument when he was sure he was right (as he usually was)," wrote [John Nelder](wikipedia://en/5865261) in Wedderburn's obituary.[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

Wedderburn published a number of papers.[^o7]

- *[Comparison of 3 measures of species-diversity.](https://doi.org/10.2307/2529585)* R. A. Kempton and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1978. Biometrics. 34 (1), pp. 25-37.
- *[Breed and sex-differences in age of appearance of bovine central incisor teeth.](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0007-1935%2817%2933931-3)* A. H. Andrews and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1977. British Veterinary Journal. 133 (6), pp. 543-547.
- *[Existence and uniqueness of maximum likelihood estimates for certain generalized linear-models.](https://doi.org/10.2307/2335080)* R. W. M. Wedderburn 1976. Biometrika. 63 (1), pp. 27-32.
- *[The effects of biocidal treatments on metabolism in soil .3. The relationship between soil biovolume, measured by optical microscopy, and the flush of decomposition caused by fumigation.](https://doi.org/10.1016/0038-0717%2876%2990003-1)* D. S. Jenkinson, D. S. Powlson and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1976. Soil Biology and Biochemistry. 8 (3), pp. 189-202.
- *[The Woburn Market Garden experiment 1942-1969 I. A history of the experiment, details of the treatments and yields of the crops.](https://doi.org/10.23637/ERADOC-1-33161)* A. E. Johnston and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1975. Rothamsted Experimental Station Report. 2, pp. 79-101.
- *[Langmuir two-surface equation as a model for phosphate adsorption by soils.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2389.1974.tb01121.x)* I. C. R. Holford, R. W. M. Wedderburn and G. E. G. Mattingly 1974. Journal of Soil Science. 25 (2), pp. 242-255.
- *Generalized linear-models specified in terms of constraints.* R. W. M. Wedderburn 1974. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B (Statistical Methodology). 36 (3), pp. 449-454.
- *[Quasi-likelihood functions, generalized linear models, and the Gauss-Newton method.](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/61.3.439)* R. W. M. Wedderburn 1974. Biometrika. 61 (3), pp. 439-447.
- *[Relationship of age to first molar tooth development in a group of cross-bred steers.](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0007-1935%2817%2936332-7)* A. H. Andrews and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1973. British Veterinary Journal. 129 (6), pp. 512-517.
- *[A method for predicting proportions of affected herds from proportions of affected animals.](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022172400062987)* F. B. Leech and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1972. Epidemiology & Infection. 70 (3), pp. 409-414.
- *[Generalized linear models](https://doi.org/10.2307/2344614)* J. A. Nelder and R. W. M. Wedderburn 1972. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series A (Statistics in Society). 135 (3), pp. 370-384.
- *Genstat: a general statistics program.* N. G. Alvey, J. C. Gower, F. B. Lauckner, P. K. Leech, J. A. Nelder, C. E. Rogers, G. J. S. Ross, H. R. Simpson, R. W. M. Wedderburn and G. N. Wilkinson 1970. Rothamsted Experimental Station, Harpenden.
