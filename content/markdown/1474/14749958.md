This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 2 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084). It has [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805) *O<sub>h</sub>*. As a holosnub, it is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) β{3,4} and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) .

:::

The triangles in this compound decompose into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) under action of the symmetry group: 16 of the triangles lie in coplanar pairs in [octahedral](wikipedia://en/80177635) planes, while the other 24 lie in unique planes.

It shares the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a [nonuniform](wikipedia://en/2537223) [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654), having irregular hexagons alternating with long and short edges.

The icosahedron, as a uniform *snub tetrahedron*

*Image: Snub tetrahedron.png.*

, is similar to these snub-pair compounds: [compound of two snub cubes](wikipedia://en/14797458) and [compound of two snub dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14797468).

Together with its convex hull, it represents the icosahedron-first projection of the nonuniform [snub tetrahedral antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_octahedral_prism#Related_polytopes).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the permutations of

(±1, 0, ±τ)

where τ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).
