# Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich formula** describes the [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) of a complete curve with coefficients in an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) or [constructible sheaf](wikipedia://en/26131119), in terms of local data involving the [Swan conductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan_conductor). (txt) and (txt) proved the formula for abelian varieties with tame ramification over curves, and (txt, Exp. X formula 7.2) extended the formula to constructible sheaves over a curve (Raynaud 1965).

:::

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that *F* is a constructible sheaf over a genus *g* smooth projective curve *C*, of rank *n* outside a finite set *X* of points where it has stalk 0. Then

$$\chi(C,F) = n(2-2g) -\sum_{x\in X}(n+Sw_x(F))$$

where *Sw* is the Swan conductor at a point.

:::
