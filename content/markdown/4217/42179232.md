# Test ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Test ideal"}

A **test ideal** is a positive characteristic analog of a [multiplier ideal](wikipedia://en/28300822) in, say, the field of complex numbers.[^o2] Test ideals are used in the study of singularities in algebraic geometry in positive characteristic.[^o3]

:::
