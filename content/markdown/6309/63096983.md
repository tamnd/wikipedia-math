# Gisela Engeln-Müllges

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gisela Engeln-Müllges"}

**Gisela Engeln-Müllges** (1940 – 31 January 2026) was a German mathematician and artist. She was a professor of numerical mathematics at the [Aachen University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FH_Aachen), where she also was a vice rector for research, development, and technology.

:::

## Education and academic career {#Education_and_academic_career}

Engeln-Müllges was born in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) in 1940. After [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Leipzig became part of East Germany (the [German Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Democratic_Republic)), and Engeln-Müllges escaped to the west in 1961. She studied mathematics at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University) beginning in 1961, and completed a doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) in 1971, with a dissertation *Fluchtebenennomogramme zur Darstellung von Funktionensystemen: Ihre Theorie und praktische Verwendbarkeit* concerning [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).

She was a professor at the Aachen University of Applied Sciences from 1982, and was vice rector there from 1992 to 2005. With Frank Uhlig, she was the author of the books *Numerik-Algorithmen mit C* and *Numerik-Algorithmen mit Fortran* (7th ed., 1993, translated into English as *Numerical Algorithms with Fortran* and *Numerical Algorithms with C*, Springer, 1996).

## Art {#Art}

Engeln-Müllges's artworks are abstract, and include both paintings and cast-metal sculptures, based on her many years of work with artist Benno Werth. In 2019, she was one of the selected artists for the London Art Biennale.

## Death {#Death}

Engeln-Müllges died on 31 January 2026, at the age of 85.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Engeln-Müllges was awarded the Federal Cross of Merit in 1992. In 2005, she was given an honorary doctorate by [Nizhny Novgorod State Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod_State_Technical_University).
