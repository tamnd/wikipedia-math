# Rama Cont

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rama Cont"}

**Rama Cont** is the [Statutory Professor of Mathematical Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Finance) at the [University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850).[^o8] [^o9] He is known for contributions to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), stochastic analysis and [mathematical modelling in finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), in particular for his work on pathwise methods in stochastic analysis and mathematical models of [systemic risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_risk).[^o7] He was awarded the [Louis Bachelier Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bachelier_Prize) by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2010.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran) ([Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran)), Cont obtained his undergraduate degree from [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique) (France),[^o9] a master's degree in theoretical physics from [Ecole Normale Superieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Normale_Superieure) and a degree in Chinese Language from [Institut national des langues et civilisations orientales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_national_des_langues_et_civilisations_orientales).[^o11] His doctoral thesis focused on the application of [Lévy processes](wikipedia://en/1025748) in financial modelling.

## Career and achievements {#Career_and_achievements}

Cont started his career as a CNRS researcher in applied mathematics at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique) (France) in 1998 and held academic positions at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique), and [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o11] He was appointed 'Directeur de Recherche CNRS' ([CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) Senior Research Scientist) in 2008 and was chair of mathematical finance at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London)[^o13] from 2012 to 2018. He was elected [Statutory Professor in Mathematical Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Finance) at the [Oxford Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850) and professorial fellow of [St Hugh's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh's_College,_Oxford) in 2018.[^o14][^o15]

Cont's research focuses on probability theory, stochastic analysis and mathematical modelling in finance.[^o16] His mathematical work focuses on pathwise methods in stochastic analysis [^o17] and the Functional Ito calculus.[^o18]

In quantitative finance he is known in particular for his work on models based on jump processes,[^o19] the stochastic modelling of [limit order books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_limit_order_book) as queueing systems [^o20] ,[^o21] [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) methods in finance [^o22] and the mathematical modelling of [systemic risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_risk). [^o24] He was editor in chief of the *Encyclopedia of Quantitative Finance*.[^o25]

Cont has served as advisor to central banks and international organizations such as the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund) and the [Bank for International Settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_for_International_Settlements) on [stress testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_testing) and [systemic risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_risk) monitoring. His work on network models, financial stability and central clearing [^o26] has influenced central banks and regulators .[^o27] He has given numerous media interviews[^o28] [^o29][^o30][^o31] [^o32] on issues related to systemic risk and financial regulation.

## Scientific contributions {#Scientific_contributions}

### Causal functional calculus {#Causal_functional_calculus}

Cont is known in mathematics for his the "Causal functional calculus", a calculus for non-anticipative, or "causal", functionals on the space of paths.[^o35] Cont and collaborators built on the seminal work of German mathematician [Hans Föllmer](wikipedia://en/29997344) [^o36] and [Bruno Dupire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Dupire) to construct a calculus for non-anticipative functionals,[^o37] which includes as a special case the so-called Ito-Föllmer calculus, a pathwise counterpart of Ito's stochastic calculus. [^o38] Subsequent work by Cont and Nicolas Perkowski [^o39] extended the Ito-Föllmer calculus to functions and functionals of more general irregular paths with non-zero p-th order variation.

### Systemic risk modeling {#Systemic_risk_modeling}

Work by Cont and his collaborators on mathematical modeling of systemic risk and financial stability, in particular on network models of financial contagion and the modeling of indirect contagion via 'fire sales', has influenced academic research and policy in this area.[^o27][^o41]

### Central clearing {#Central_clearing}

Cont's research on central clearing in over-the-counter (OTC) markets has influenced risk management practices of [central counterparties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_counterparty_clearing) and regulatory thinking on central clearing.[^o43] Cont has argued that central clearing does not eliminate [counterparty risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterparty_risk) but transforms it into [liquidity risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquidity_risk), therefore risk management and stress testing of central counterparties should focus on [liquidity risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquidity_risk) and liquidity resources, not capital.[^o44]

### Risk measurement and Model risk {#Risk_measurement_and_Model_risk}

Cont introduced a rigorous approach for the assessment of [model risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_risk) [^o46] which has been influential in the design of model risk management frameworks in financial institutions. [^o47][^o48]

Cont, Deguest and Scandolo[^o49] introduced the concept of 'risk measurement procedure', an empirical counterpart of the notion of [risk measure](wikipedia://en/12498127), and defined a robust class of risk measurement procedures known as '[Range Value-at-risk](wikipedia://en/148633)' (RVaR), a robust alternative to Expected shortfall.[^o50]

Cont, Kotlicki and Valderrama define the concept of [Liquidity at risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquidity_at_risk),[^o51] as the amount of liquid assets needed by a financial institution to face liquidity outflows in this scenario.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Cont was awarded the [Louis Bachelier Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bachelier_Prize) by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2010 for his work on mathematical modelling of financial markets.[^o5] He was elected [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM)](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2017 for "contributions to stochastic analysis and mathematical finance".[^o7] He received the Award for Excellence in Interdisciplinary Research (APEX) from the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 2017 for his research on mathematical modelling of systemic risk.[^o6] [^o53] Cont was elected Fellow of the [Academy for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/82295923) in 2026.[^o54]
