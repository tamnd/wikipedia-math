# Erwin Lutwak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Erwin Lutwak"}

**Erwin Lutwak** (born 9 February 1946, [Chernivtsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernivtsi), now [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)), is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Lutwak is [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). His main research interests are [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and its connections with [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He spent the earliest years of his childhood in the Soviet Union, Romania, Israel, Italy, and Venezuela before he settled in Brooklyn when he was ten. He graduated from the Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn, now [New York University Tandon School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering) with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in 1968, a [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in 1972 and with a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1974. Before he became professor at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [NYU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYU), he was a professor at [New York University Tandon School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering). His first position in 1975 was at the Polytechnic Institute of New York (which was created as a result of the merger of the Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn and the NYU School of Engineering).[^o4]

He is a member of the editorial boards of the [Advances in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Mathematics),[^o5] the [Canadian Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Journal_of_Mathematics),[^o6] the [Canadian Mathematical Bulletin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Mathematical_Bulletin),[^o6] and the [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press) Encyclopedia of Mathematics and its Applications.[^o7] He is an Honorary Editor at Advanced Nonlinear Studies (De Gruyter).[^o8]

## Work {#Work}

Erwin Lutwak is known for his Dual Brunn Minkowski Theory,[^o10] his notion of [intersection body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_body) and his contribution to the solution of the [Busemann–Petty problem](wikipedia://en/33294058),[^o11] for proving the long-conjectured upper-semicontinuity of affine surface area,[^o12] his contributions to the L<sub>p</sub> Brunn Minkowski Theory and, in particular, his L<sub>p</sub> [Minkowski problem](wikipedia://en/27966551)[^o13] and its solution in important cases.[^o14]

## Honors {#Honors}

- Lutwak became an Inaugural Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o16] in 2012.
- He received an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) of the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) in 2014.[^o17]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Dr. Lutwak is married to Nancy Lutwak, M.D.. They have one daughter, Hope Lutwak, who graduated with a Bachelor of Science in 2018 from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. The family resides in Manhattan.
