# Mary Ellen Bock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Ellen Bock"}

**Mary Ellen Johnston Bock** is a retired American statistician, now a professor emeritus at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) after becoming the first female full professor of statistics and the first female chair of the department there. She was [president of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2007.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

As an undergraduate at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign), Bock earned a bachelor's degree in the [German language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) in 1967. She switched to mathematics for her graduate studies at the same university, completing her PhD in 1974 under the supervision of Robert B. Ash with a dissertation on *Certain Minimax Estimators of the Mean of a Multivariate Normal Distribution*.[^o5]

As chair of statistics at Purdue from 1995 to 2010, Bock led the department through a period of growth, and took a multidisciplinary approach to the subject that included [computational statistics](wikipedia://en/15832717) as well as application areas including [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878), [statistical finance](wikipedia://en/7458786), and [environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Bock is a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), and of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). She won the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224) in 2013.
