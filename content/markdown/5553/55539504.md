# Bitcoin Gold

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Bitcoin Gold"}

**Bitcoin Gold** (**BTG**) is a [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188) which was created as a [hard fork](wikipedia://en/56222588#Hard_fork) of [bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265).

:::

The stated purpose of the hard fork is to make [mining](wikipedia://en/36662188#Mining) on commonly available graphics cards economically viable. The cryptocurrency has been hit by two [51% hashing attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-spending#51%_attack), once in 2018 and once in 2020.

## History {#History}

Bitcoin Gold [hard forked](wikipedia://en/56222588#Hard_fork) from the bitcoin blockchain on October 24, 2017.[^o6] The stated purpose of the hard fork was to change the [proof of work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_work) [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) so that ASICs ([Application-Specific Integrated Circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application-specific_integrated_circuit)) which are used to mine bitcoin cannot be used to mine the Bitcoin Gold [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971) in the hopes that enabling mining on commonly available graphics cards would allow mining from a home PC.[^o7][^o8]

According to [Mashable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashable) the launch was criticized for being hastily put together and for including a developer pre-mine.[^o8] Shortly after launch, the project's website came under a [distributed denial of service attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_denial_of_service_attack).[^o7]

In May 2018, Bitcoin Gold was hit by a [51% hashing attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-spending#51%_attack) by an unknown actor. This type of attack makes it possible to manipulate the blockchain ledger on which transactions are recorded, and to spend the same digital coins more than once.[^o9] During the attack, 388,000 BTG (worth approximately US\$18 million) was stolen from several cryptocurrency exchanges. Bitcoin Gold was later delisted from Bittrex, after the team refused to help pay some damages.[^o10]

In July 2018, Bitcoin Gold changed mining algorithm to one that requires more memory to further discourage ASIC mining.[^o11]

Bitcoin Gold suffered from 51% attacks again in January 2020.[^o12] In July 2020, the version 0.17.2 was released as an emergency update in order to elude a long attack chain originated a few days before.[^o13]
