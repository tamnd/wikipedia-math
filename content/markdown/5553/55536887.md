# Tamara G. Kolda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tamara G. Kolda"}

**Tamara G. Kolda** is an American [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) and former Distinguished Member of Technical Staff at [Sandia National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandia_National_Laboratories). She is noted for her contributions in [computational science](wikipedia://en/1181008), [multilinear algebra](wikipedia://en/243946), [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253), [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithms), [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033), [parallel computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing), and [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kolda received her bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1992 from the [University of Maryland Baltimore County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland_Baltimore_County) and her PhD in applied mathematics from the [University of Maryland College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland_College_Park) in 1997.[^o7]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Kolda was a Householder Postdoctoral Fellow at [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Ridge_National_Laboratory) from 1997 to 1999 before joining [Sandia National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandia_National_Laboratories).

### Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Kolda received a [Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Early_Career_Award_for_Scientists_and_Engineers) in 2003, best paper prizes at the 2008 IEEE International Conference on Data Mining and the 2013 SIAM International Conference on Data Mining, and has been a distinguished member of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) since 2011.[^o4][^o10] She was elected a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2015.[^o11] She was elected a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) in 2019 for "innovations in algorithms for tensor decompositions, contributions to data science, and community leadership."[^o3] She was elected to the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 2020, for "contributions to the design of scientific software, including tensor decompositions and multilinear algebra".[^o12]
