# Sistema de Información de la Población Objetivo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sistema de Información de la Población Objetivo"}

The **Sistema de Información de la Población Objetivo** (SIPO) is a social registry used by the [Joint Social Welfare Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Social_Welfare_Institute) of Costa Rica to classify the [population of Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Costa_Rica) as to levels of [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty) and [extreme poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_poverty). It seeks to ensure that institutional resources effectively reach the most vulnerable groups of [Costa Rican society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rican_people).

:::

The registry works as an information tool for communities to develop projects, and, more importantly, enables [government institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_institution) to use it to implement programs to benefit target populations. Information in the registry is obtained from the Social Information Sheet ([FIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficha_de_Información_Social)), which contains a set of variables, grouped into 10 sections.
