# Arthur Bakker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Bakker"}

**Arthur Bakker** (born January 3, 1970) is a Dutch mathematics education researcher and associate professor at the [Freudenthal Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freudenthal_Institute), [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University), [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands).[^o3] He is Fellow at the [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen).[^o4]

:::

## Service and Functions {#Service_and_Functions}

He is editor-in-chief of *[Educational Studies in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Studies_in_Mathematics)*.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9] Before, he was associate editor of *[Educational Studies in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Studies_in_Mathematics)* since 2014.[^o10]

With [Celia Hoyles](wikipedia://en/42894729), Phillip Kent, and Richard B. Noss, he is the co-author of *Improving Mathematics at Work: The Need for Techno-Mathematical Literacies* (Routledge, 2010).[^o11]

His main area of expertise is [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), but he has contributed also to a more general boundary-crossing framework,[^o12] and to the development of design research in education as a methodological approach to improve education and education as a design science more generally.[^o13] Other areas of interest include interest development,[^o14] attitudes toward science and mathematics,[^o15] inferentialism,[^o16][^o17][^o18] scaffolding,[^o19][^o20] and embodied design.[^o21][^o22] Bakker is project leader of *The Digital Turn in Epistemology* project funded by NWO.[^o23]

## Career {#Career}

In 2004, Bakker graduated (PhD) on his dissertation titled *Design research in statistics education: On symbolizing and computer tools*[^o25]*,* one of the first dissertations on design research (supervised by Koeno Gravemeijer, Gellof Kanselaar, and Jan de Lange). Alongside this project, he participated as advisor and curriculum author in the [TinkerPlots](wikipedia://en/16939285) project (NSF, ESI-9818946), directed by Cliff Konold (UMass, Amherst).

At the Institute of Education (now [UCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCL_Institute_of_Education)), he was research officer with Phillip Kent, in the TLRP project Technomathematical Literacies in the workplace, codirected by [Celia Hoyles](wikipedia://en/42894729) and Richard Noss (2004–2007).[^o26]

## Honours {#Honours}

In 1989, Bakker received the third prize in the [National Mathematics Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_olympiad) (Netherlands) and also the first prize in the Pythagoras Olympiad.[^o26] Techno-Mathematical Literacies: Bakker co-authored Improving Mathematics at Work: The Need for Techno-Mathematical Literacies (2010), which explores the mathematical skills required in modern workplaces.
