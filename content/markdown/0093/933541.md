# Karoubi envelope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karoubi envelope"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) the **Karoubi envelope** (or **Cauchy completion** or **idempotent completion**) of a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) **C** is a classification of the [idempotents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent) of **C**, by means of an auxiliary category. Taking the Karoubi envelope of a [preadditive category](wikipedia://en/59521) gives a [pseudo-abelian category](wikipedia://en/20956542), hence for additive categories, the construction is sometimes called the pseudo-abelian completion. It is named for the French mathematician [Max Karoubi](wikipedia://en/30942178).

:::

Given a category **C**, an idempotent of **C** is an [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569)

$$e: A \rightarrow A$$

with

$$e\circ e = e.$$

An idempotent *e*: *A* → *A* is said to **split** if there is an object *B* and morphisms *f*: *A* → *B*, *g*: *B* → *A* such that *e* = *g* *f* and 1<sub>*B*</sub> = *f* *g*.

The **Karoubi envelope** of **C**, sometimes written **Split(C)**, is the category whose objects are pairs of the form (*A*, *e*) where *A* is an object of **C** and $e : A \rightarrow A$ is an idempotent of **C**, and whose [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are the triples

$$(e, f, e^{\prime}): (A, e) \rightarrow (A^{\prime}, e^{\prime})$$

where $f: A \rightarrow A^{\prime}$ is a morphism of **C** satisfying $e^{\prime} \circ f = f = f \circ e$ (or equivalently $f=e'\circ f\circ e$).

Composition in **Split(C)** is as in **C**, but the identity morphism on $(A,e)$ in **Split(C)** is $(e,e,e)$, rather than the identity on $A$.

The category **C** embeds fully and faithfully in **Split(C)**. In **Split(C)** every idempotent splits, and **Split(C)** is the universal category with this property. The Karoubi envelope of a category **C** can therefore be considered as the "completion" of **C** which splits idempotents.

The Karoubi envelope of a category **C** can equivalently be defined as the [full subcategory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_subcategory) of $\hat{\mathbf{C}}$ (the [presheaves](wikipedia://en/4840944) over **C**) of retracts of [representable functors](wikipedia://en/844461). The category of presheaves on **C** is equivalent to the category of presheaves on **Split(C)**.

## Automorphisms in the Karoubi envelope {#Automorphisms_in_the_Karoubi_envelope}

An [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) in **Split(C)** is of the form $(e, f, e): (A, e) \rightarrow (A, e)$, with inverse $(e, g, e): (A, e) \rightarrow (A, e)$ satisfying:

$$g \circ f = e = f \circ g$$

$$g \circ f \circ g = g$$

$$f \circ g \circ f = f$$

If the first equation is relaxed to just have $g \circ f = f \circ g$, then *f* is a partial automorphism (with inverse *g*). A (partial) involution in **Split(C)** is a self-inverse (partial) automorphism.

## Examples {#Examples}

- If **C** has products, then given an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) $f: A \rightarrow B$ the mapping $f \times f^{-1}: A \times B \rightarrow B \times A$, composed with the canonical map $\gamma:B \times A \rightarrow A \times B$ of symmetry, is a partial [involution](wikipedia://en/293450).
- If **C** is a [triangulated category](wikipedia://en/1679700), the Karoubi envelope **Split**(**C**) can be endowed with the structure of a triangulated category such that the canonical functor **C** → **Split**(**C**) becomes a [triangulated functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangulated_functor).
- The Karoubi envelope is used in the construction of several categories of [motives](wikipedia://en/430680).
- The Karoubi envelope construction takes semi-adjunctions to [adjunctions](wikipedia://en/53991).[^o6] For this reason the Karoubi envelope is used in the study of models of the [untyped lambda calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untyped_lambda_calculus). The Karoubi envelope of an extensional lambda model (a [monoid](wikipedia://en/19652), considered as a category) is cartesian closed.[^o7][^o8]
- The category of [projective modules](wikipedia://en/364488) over any ring is the Karoubi envelope of its full subcategory of free modules.
- The category of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over any [paracompact space](wikipedia://en/48631) is the Karoubi envelope of its full subcategory of trivial bundles. This is in fact a special case of the previous example by the [Serre–Swan theorem](wikipedia://en/363540) and conversely this theorem can be proved by first proving both these facts, the observation that the [global sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_section) functor is an equivalence between trivial vector bundles over $X$ and free modules over $C(X)$ and then using the [universal property](wikipedia://en/32245) of the Karoubi envelope.
