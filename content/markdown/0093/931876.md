# Pentahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentahedron** (: **pentahedra**) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with five faces or sides. There are no [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) polyhedra with five sides, and there are two distinct topological types. An irregular pentahedron can be a non-[convex](wikipedia://en/1817885) solid: Consider a non-convex (planar) [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) (such as a [dart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dart_%28geometry%29)) as the base of the solid, and any point not in the base plane as the [apex](wikipedia://en/6022079).

## Hosohedron {#Hosohedron}

There is a third topological polyhedral figure with 5 faces, degenerate as a polyhedron: it exists as a spherical tiling of [digon](wikipedia://en/5442728) faces, called a [pentagonal hosohedron](wikipedia://en/2316192) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {2,5}. It has 2 ([antipodal point](wikipedia://en/367577)) vertices, 5 edges, and 5 digonal faces.
