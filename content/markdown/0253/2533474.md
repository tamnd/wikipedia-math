# Padovan polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Padovan polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Padovan polynomials** are a generalization of [Padovan sequence](wikipedia://en/2161709) numbers. These [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) are defined by:[^o2]

:::

$$P_n(x) = \begin{cases} 1, &\mbox{if }n=1\\ 0, &\mbox{if }n=2\\ x, &\mbox{if }n=3\\ xP_{n-2}(x)+P_{n-3}(x),&\mbox{if } n\ge4. \end{cases}$$

The first few Padovan polynomials are:

$$P_1(x)=1 \,$$

$$P_2(x)=0 \,$$

$$P_3(x)=x \,$$

$$P_4(x)=1 \,$$

$$P_5(x)=x^2 \,$$

$$P_6(x)=2x \,$$

$$P_7(x)=x^3+1 \,$$

$$P_8(x)=3x^2 \,$$

$$P_9(x)=x^4+3x \,$$

$$P_{10}(x)=4x^3+1\,$$

$$P_{11}(x)=x^5+6x^2.\,$$

The Padovan numbers are recovered by evaluating the polynomials P<sub>*n*−3</sub>(*x*) at *x* = 1.

Evaluating P<sub>*n*−3</sub>(*x*) at *x* = 2 gives the *n*th [Fibonacci number](wikipedia://en/10918) plus (−1)<sup>*n*</sup>.

The [ordinary generating function](wikipedia://en/160993#Ordinary_generating_function) for the sequence is

$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty P_n(x) t^n = \frac{t}{1 - x t^2 - t^3} .$$
