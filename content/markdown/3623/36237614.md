# Conway group Co1

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conway group Co1"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Conway group** *Co<sub>1</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

4,157,776,806,543,360,000

\= 2<sup>21</sup>3<sup>9</sup>5<sup>4</sup>7<sup>2</sup>111323

≈ 4.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*Co<sub>1</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was discovered by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) in 1968. It is the largest of the three sporadic Conway groups and can be obtained as the quotient of *Co<sub>0</sub>* ([group of automorphisms](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) Λ that fix the origin) by its [center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_of_a_group), which consists of the scalar matrices ±1. It also appears at the top of the automorphism group of the even 26-dimensional unimodular lattice [II<sub>25,1</sub>](wikipedia://en/32486086). Some rather cryptic comments in Witt's collected works suggest that he found the Leech lattice and possibly the order of its automorphism group in unpublished work in 1940.

The [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) is trivial and the [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 2.

## Involutions {#Involutions}

Co<sub>0</sub> has 4 conjugacy classes of involutions; these collapse to 2 in Co<sub>1</sub>, but there are 4-elements in Co<sub>0</sub> that correspond to a third class of involutions in Co<sub>1</sub>.

An image of a dodecad has a centralizer of type 2<sup>11</sup>:M<sub>12</sub>:2, which is contained in a maximal subgroup of type 2<sup>11</sup>:M<sub>24</sub>.

An image of an octad or 16-set has a centralizer of the form 2<sup>1+8</sup>.O${}^{+}_{8}$(2), a maximal subgroup.

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

The smallest faithful permutation representation of Co<sub>1</sub> is on the 98280 pairs {*v*,–*v*} of norm 4 vectors.

The double cover Co<sub>0</sub> has a 24 dimensional representation; when reduced modulo 2, this becomes a representation of the simple group Co<sub>1</sub>. The exterior square and symmetric tensor square of the 24 dimensional representation have dimension 276 and 299, respectively; in characteristic not 2, the former is the smallest faithful representation of the simple group Co<sub>1</sub>.

The centralizer of an involution of type 2B in the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) is of the form 2<sup>1+24 ·</sup>Co<sub>1</sub>. Under this subgroup, the 196883 dimensional representation of the monster reduces as follows: the 24x4096=98304 representation of 2<sup>1+24 ·</sup>Co<sub>1</sub>, the 98280 dimensional representation of 2<sup>24 ·</sup>Co<sub>1</sub>, and the 299 dimensional representation of Co<sub>1</sub>.

The Dynkin diagram of the even Lorentzian [unimodular lattice](wikipedia://en/1648605) [II<sub>1,25</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/II1,25) is isometric to the (affine) Leech lattice Λ, so the group of diagram automorphisms is split extension Λ,Co<sub>0</sub> of affine isometries of the Leech lattice.

:::
