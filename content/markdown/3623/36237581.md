# Conway group Co3

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conway group Co3"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Conway group** *$\mathrm{Co}_3$* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

495,766,656,000

\= 2<sup>10</sup>3<sup>7</sup>5<sup>3</sup>71123

≈ 5.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*$\mathrm{Co}_3$* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was discovered by (txt) as the [group of automorphisms](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) $\Lambda$ fixing a lattice vector of type 3, thus length $\sqrt{6}$. It is thus a subgroup of [$\mathrm{Co}_0$](wikipedia://en/345511). It is isomorphic to a subgroup of $\mathrm{Co}_1$. The direct product $2\times \mathrm{Co}_3$ is maximal in $\mathrm{Co}_0$.

The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) are both [trivial](wikipedia://en/193059).

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

Co<sub>3</sub> acts on a 23-dimensional even lattice with no roots, given by the [orthogonal complement](wikipedia://en/1058833) of a norm 6 vector of the Leech lattice. This gives 23-dimensional representations over any field; over fields of characteristic 2 or 3 this can be reduced to a 22-dimensional faithful representation.

Co<sub>3</sub> has a doubly transitive [permutation representation](wikipedia://en/59487764) on 276 points.

(txt) showed that if a finite group has an absolutely irreducible faithful rational representation of dimension 23 and has no subgroups of index 23 or 24 then it is contained in either $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathrm{Co}_2$ or $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathrm{Co}_3$.

:::

## Maximal subgroups {#Maximal_subgroups}

Some maximal subgroups fix or reflect 2-dimensional sublattices of the Leech lattice. It is usual to define these planes by [**h-k-l triangles**](wikipedia://en/345511#Sublattice_groups): triangles including the origin as a vertex, with edges (differences of vertices) being vectors of types *h*, *k*, and *l*.

## Generalized Monstrous Moonshine {#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine}

In analogy to [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) for the monster *M*, for *Co*<sub>3</sub>, the relevant McKay-Thompson series is $T_{4A}(\tau)$ where one can set the constant term a(0) = 24 (),

$$\begin{aligned}j_{4A}(\tau) &=T_{4A}(\tau)+24\\ &=\Big(\tfrac{\eta^2(2\tau)}{\eta(\tau)\,\eta(4\tau)} \Big)^{24} \\ &=\Big(\big(\tfrac{\eta(\tau)}{\eta(4\tau)}\big)^{4}+4^2 \big(\tfrac{\eta(4\tau)}{\eta(\tau)}\big)^{4}\Big)^2\\ &=\frac{1}{q} + 24+ 276q + 2048q^2 +11202q^3+49152q^4+\dots \end{aligned}$$

and *η*(*τ*) is the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020).
