# Alexander Freeman (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Freeman"}

**Alexander Freeman** (1838–1897) was an English astronomer and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)[^o2] and a noted correspondent of [James Clerk Maxwell](wikipedia://en/28989696) and [Willard Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Gibbs).

:::
