# Foundations of Science

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Foundations of Science"}

***Foundations of Science*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [interdisciplinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinarity) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) focussing on [methodological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology) and [philosophical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) topics concerning the structure and growth of [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science). It is the official journal of the [Association for Foundations of Science, Language and Cognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Foundations_of_Science,_Language_and_Cognition) and is published quarterly by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). The journal was established in 1995. The [editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief) is [Diederik Aerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diederik_Aerts).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [Arts and Humanities Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_and_Humanities_Citation_Index), [Cengage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cengage_Learning), [EBSCO Databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_Industries), [FRANCIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRANCIS), [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar), [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), [PASCAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PASCAL_%28database%29), [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index), [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus), and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).
