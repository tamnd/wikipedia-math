# Ivan Zhegalkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Zhegalkin"}

**Ivan Ivanovich Zhegalkin** (Ива́н Ива́нович Жега́лкин; 3 August 1869, [Mtsensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtsensk) – 28 March 1947, [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)) was a Russian and Soviet [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is best known for his formulation of [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844) as the theory of the ring of integers mod 2, via what are now called [Zhegalkin polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhegalkin_polynomial).

*Image: Могила математика Ивана Жегалкина.JPG.* Ivan Zhegalkin's grave (top)

:::

Zhegalkin was professor of mathematics at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). He helped found the thriving [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) group there, which became the Department of Mathematical Logic established by [Sofya Yanovskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofya_Yanovskaya) in 1959. Reminiscing on his student days, [Nikolai Luzin](wikipedia://en/576228) recalls Zhegalkin as the only professor he was not afraid of.
