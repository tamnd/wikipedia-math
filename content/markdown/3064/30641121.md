# Regression diagnostic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regression diagnostic"}

Regression validation In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **regression diagnostic** is one of a set of procedures available for [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997) that seek to assess the validity of a model in any of a number of different ways.[^o7] This assessment may be an exploration of the model's underlying [statistical assumptions](wikipedia://en/27592), an examination of the structure of the model by considering formulations that have fewer, more or different [explanatory variables](wikipedia://en/437701), or a study of subgroups of observations, looking for those that are either poorly represented by the model ([outliers](wikipedia://en/160951)) or that have a relatively large effect on the regression model's predictions.

:::

A regression diagnostic may take the form of a graphical result, informal quantitative results or a formal [statistical hypothesis test](wikipedia://en/30284),[^o8] each of which provides guidance for further stages of a regression analysis.

## Introduction {#Introduction}

Regression diagnostics have often been developed or were initially proposed in the context of [linear regression](wikipedia://en/48758386) or, more particularly, [ordinary least squares](wikipedia://en/1651906). This means that many formally defined diagnostics are only available for these contexts.

## Assessing model structure {#Assessing_model_structure}

**Adequacy of existing explanatory variables**

- [Partial residual plot](wikipedia://en/18588253)
- [Ramsey RESET test](wikipedia://en/5611588)
- [F test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F_test) for use when there are replicated observations, so that a comparison can be made between the [lack-of-fit sum of squares](wikipedia://en/18883979) and the pure error sum of squares, under the assumption that model errors are [homoscedastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homoscedastic) and have a [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462).

**Adding or dropping explanatory variables**

- [Partial regression plot](wikipedia://en/18597239)
- [Student's t test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student's_t_test) for testing inclusion of a single explanatory variable, or the [F test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F_test) for testing inclusion of a group of variables, both under the assumption that model errors are [homoscedastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homoscedastic) and have a [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462).

**Change of model structure between groups of observations**

- [Structural break test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_break_test)
  - [Chow test](wikipedia://en/2883675)

**Comparing model structures**

- [PRESS statistic](wikipedia://en/32955639)
