# British Polling Council

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="British Polling Council"}

The **British Polling Council** (**BPC**) is an association of [market research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_research) companies whose [opinion polls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polls) are regularly published or broadcast in [media in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_in_the_United_Kingdom). The current President is [Jane Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Green_%28political_scientist%29).[^o3]

:::

The BPC was established in 2004,[^o4] twelve years after the perceived failure of opinion polls to come close to predicting the actual result of the [1992 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_United_Kingdom_general_election). BPC members use a range of fieldwork methods (telephone, door-to-door, and internet) and statistical tools.

## Members {#Members}

### Founding members {#Founding_members}

The following organisations are founding members of the BPC:[^o10][^o11]

- [Ipsos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipsos) (formerly Ipsos MORI)
- ORB International
- [Savanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanta) (formerly ComRes)
- [Verian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verian) (formerly Kantar Public, originally TNS/System 3)
- Walnut Unlimited (formerly [ICM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICM_Research))
- [YouGov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouGov)

NOP (now [GfK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GfK)) was a founding member, but is no longer a member as of 2018.

[Populus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Populus_Ltd) (now Yonder Consulting) applied to be a founding member[^o7] and the Yonder Consulting website claims that it was (e.g.[^o8]), but its name was omitted from the BPC press release listing of founding members.[^o11]
