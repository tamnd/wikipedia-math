# GLIM (software)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="GLIM"}

**GLIM** (an [acronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym) for Generalized Linear Interactive Modelling) is a [statistical software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_software) program for fitting [generalized linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_linear_models) (GLMs). It was developed by the [Royal Statistical Society's](wikipedia://en/552664) Working Party on [Statistical Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_computing) (later renamed the GLIM Working Party),[^o2] chaired initially by [John Nelder](wikipedia://en/5865261).[^o3] It was first released in 1974 with the last major release, GLIM4, in 1993.[^o4] GLIM was distributed by the [Numerical Algorithms Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Algorithms_Group) (NAG).[^o5]

:::

GLIM was notable for being the first package capable of fitting a wide range of [generalized linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_linear_models) in a unified framework, and for encouraging an interactive, iterative approach to statistical modelling.[^o6] GLIM used a [command-line interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command-line_interface) and allowed users to define their own [macros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macro_%28computer_science%29). Many articles in [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) were written about the use of GLIM.[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13] Two GLIM conferences were held in London (1982) and Lancaster (1985) and the [Statistical Modelling Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Modelling_Society), with its annual workshops, grew out of them. GLIM was reviewed in *[The American Statistician](wikipedia://en/16876079)* in 1994, along with other software for fitting generalized linear models.[^o14]

The GLIMPSE system was later developed to provide a [knowledge based](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_base) front-end for GLIM.[^o15]

GLIM is no longer actively developed or distributed.
