# Coxeter–Todd lattice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coxeter–Todd lattice"}

*Image: Orthogonal projection of the Coxeter–Todd lattice onto two dimensions 2.png.* Orthogonal projection of the Coxeter–Todd lattice onto two dimensions

In mathematics, the **Coxeter–Todd lattice** K<sub>12</sub>, discovered by (), is a 12-dimensional even integral [lattice](wikipedia://en/469246) of discriminant 3<sup>6</sup> with no norm-2 vectors. It is the sublattice of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) fixed by a certain automorphism of order 3, and is analogous to the [Barnes&ndash;Wall lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnes&ndash;Wall_lattice). The automorphism group of the Coxeter–Todd lattice has order 2<sup>10</sup>·3<sup>7</sup>·5·7 = 9!·4!·3<sup>2</sup> = 78382080, and there are 756 vectors in this lattice of norm 4 (the shortest nonzero vectors in this lattice).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Coxeter–Todd lattice can be made into a 6-dimensional lattice self dual over the [Eisenstein integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenstein_integers). The automorphism group of this complex lattice has index 2 in the full automorphism group of the Coxeter–Todd lattice and is a [complex reflection group](wikipedia://en/5381096) (number 34 on the list) with structure 6.PSU<sub>4</sub>(**F**<sub>3</sub>).2, called the [Mitchell group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitchell_group).

The [genus](wikipedia://en/36597629) of the Coxeter–Todd lattice was described by (Scharlau Venkov 1995) and has 10 isometry classes: all of them other than the Coxeter–Todd lattice have a root system of maximal rank 12.
