# Roland Speicher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Speicher"}

**Roland Speicher** (born 12 June 1960) is a German mathematician, known for his work on [free probability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_probability_theory). He is a professor at the [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University). After winning the 1979 German national competition [Jugend forscht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jugend_forscht) in the field of mathematics and computer science,[^o3] Speicher studied physics and mathematics at the Universities of Saarbrücken, Freiburg and Heidelberg. He received in 1989 his doctorate from [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) under the supervision of Wilhelm Freiherr von Waldenfels with thesis *Quantenstochastische Prozesse auf der Cuntz-Algebra* (Quantum Stochastic Processes on the Cuntz Algebra). From 2000 to 2010 Speicher was a professor at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario). Since 2010 he is at the University of the Saarland.

:::

His research deals with [free probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_probability_theory) (with application to random matrices, statistical mechanics and operator algebras) and their combinatorial aspects and with [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987).

In 2012, Speicher received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069).[^o5] He also received the Research Excellence Award of the President of Ontario. In 2014, he was an invited speaker with talk *Free Probability and Random Matrices*[^o6] at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).

Speicher is married and has four children.
