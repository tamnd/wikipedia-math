# Alissa Crans

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alissa Crans"}

**Alissa Susan Crans** is an American mathematician specializing in [higher-dimensional algebra](wikipedia://en/19515158). She is a professor of mathematics at [Loyola Marymount University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Marymount_University), and the associate director of [Project NExT](wikipedia://en/14984955), a program of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) to mentor post-doctoral mathematicians, statisticians, and mathematics teachers.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Crans graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors) and [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa) from the [University of Redlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Redlands) in 1999. She earned an M.S. and Ph.D. from the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) in 2000 and 2004 respectively. Her dissertation, *Lie 2-Algebras*, was supervised by [John C. Baez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Baez).

She worked as a lecturer at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College) in 2002, as VIGRE Ross Assistant Professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) from 2005 to 2006, and as Visiting Dickson Instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2008. Meanwhile she started as an assistant professor at [Loyola Marymount University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Marymount_University) in 2004, and was promoted to full professor there in 2016, with another leave to work as Associate Director of Diversity and Education at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) from 2012 to 2014.

She took up her position as associate director of [Project NExT](wikipedia://en/14984955) in 2014. She is also active as a mentor for women in mathematics, and served as member-at-large on the executive committee of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from 2014 to 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

[Diamond Bar High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Bar_High_School) lists Crans among their Distinguished Alumni. In 2011 the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) gave Crans their Henry L. Alder Award[^o5] for Distinguished Teaching by a Beginning College/University Mathematics Faculty Member. In the same year she also won the Merten M. Hasse Prize of the MAA for her paper "Musical Actions of Dihedral Groups". She was an MAA Distinguished Lecturer in 2014, speaking about the [Catalan numbers](wikipedia://en/216488). The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) has included her in the 2020 class of AWM Fellows for "mentoring and supporting women at Loyola Marymount and through EDGE, SMP, SPWM, and Project NExT; for her role in the Pacific Coast Undergraduate Mathematics Conference, recognized as an AMS Program That Makes a Difference".[^o6] She is included in a deck of playing cards featuring notable women mathematicians published by the [Association of Women in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Women_in_Mathematics).[^o7]
