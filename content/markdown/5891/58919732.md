# Stephen Halperin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Halperin"}

**John Stephen Halperin** (born 1 February 1942 in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario))[^o2] is a Canadian mathematician who deals with differential geometry and algebraic topology.

:::

A son of the mathematician [Israel Halperin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Halperin), Stephen Halperin studied at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) with a bachelor's degree in 1966 and a master's degree in 1967. He received in 1970 his PhD from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) under the supervision of [Hsien Chung Wang](wikipedia://en/50742441) with thesis *Real Cohomology and Smooth Transformation Groups*. He then became an assistant professor and in 1979 a full professor at the University of Toronto.

Halperin was a visiting scholar in 1981 at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), in 1986 at the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Nice-Sophia-Antipolis), and in 1995 at the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille).

His research deals with homotopy theory and homology of [loop spaces](wikipedia://en/2174332) with applications in geometry. He wrote a three-volume textbook on differential geometry with Werner H. Greub and Ray Vanstone.

In 1984 Halperin was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada). In 1997 he received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069).

In 1999 he became a full professor at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), where he served as the dean of the College of Computer, Mathematical and Physical Sciences.
