# Albrecht Böttcher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albrecht Böttcher"}

**Albrecht Böttcher** (born 29 December 1954) is a German mathematician. His field of research is [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## History {#History}

Böttcher was born in [Oberwiesenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwiesenthal), Saxony, [GDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDR). Between 1971 and 1973, he was in an elite class for mathematics at the [Chemnitz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemnitz_University_of_Technology). In 1973, he won a silver medal at the 15th [International Math Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Math_Olympiad) in Moscow.[^o3] Böttcher studied mathematics from 1975 to 1979 in [Chemnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemnitz) and then went to [Rostov on Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_on_Don) in 1980 to study and earned a PhD there in 1984. After completing his dissertation, *The finite section method for the Wiener-Hopf integral operator,* he worked as scientist assistance at the university of Chemnitz. Since 1992, the professor for *Harmonic Analysis and Operator theory* at the Chemnitz University of Technology. Since 2020 he is professor emeritus.[^o5]

, he is the author of nine books and about 180 papers.[^o6]
