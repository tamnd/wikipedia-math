# Pontryagin cohomology operation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pontryagin cohomology operation"}

In mathematics, a **Pontryagin cohomology operation** is a [cohomology operation](wikipedia://en/1226719) taking [cohomology classes](wikipedia://en/363530) in *H*<sup>2*n*</sup>(*X*,**Z**/*p*<sup>*r*</sup>**Z**) to *H*<sup>2*pn*</sup>(*X*,**Z**/*p*<sup>*r*+1</sup>**Z**) for some [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*. When *p*=2 these operations were introduced by (txt) and were named **Pontrjagin squares** by (Whitehead 1949) (with the term "**Pontryagin square**" also being used). They were generalized to arbitrary primes by (Thomas 1956).

:::
