# Cyclically reduced word

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclically reduced word"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **cyclically reduced word** is a concept of [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834).

:::

Let $F(X)$ be a [free group](wikipedia://en/59735). Then a [word](wikipedia://en/12792326) in $F(X)$ is said to be *cyclically reduced* [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) every [cyclic permutation](wikipedia://en/449166) of the word is [reduced](wikipedia://en/12792326#Reduced_words).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Every cyclic shift and the inverse of a cyclically reduced word are cyclically reduced again.
- Every word is conjugate to a cyclically reduced word. The cyclically reduced words are minimal-length representatives of the conjugacy classes in the free group. This representative is not uniquely determined, but it is unique up to cyclic shifts (since every cyclic shift is a conjugate element).

:::
