# Peter Donnelly

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Donnelly"}

**Sir Peter James Donnelly** FRS (born 15 May 1959) is an Australian-British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor of Statistical Science at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), and the [CEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Executive_Officer) of [Genomics PLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics_PLC). He is a specialist in [applied probability](wikipedia://en/366719) and has made contributions to [coalescent theory](wikipedia://en/3441831). His research group at Oxford has an international reputation for the development of statistical methodology to analyze genetic data.

:::

## Background, family and education {#Background,_family_and_education}

Peter James Donnelly was born and raised in Brisbane, Queensland, the son of financial adviser, economic theorist and author Austin Donnelly[^o3] and Sheila Donnelly. He is the brother of Sharon Donnelly and of eminent Australian financier, funds manager and company director Melda Donnelly.[^o4] He was educated at [St. Joseph's Christian Brothers College, Gregory Terrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Joseph's_College,_Gregory_Terrace), and later at the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland) and at [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford).

## Professional life {#Professional_life}

When elected to a chair at [Queen Mary College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary,_University_of_London) in 1988 Donnelly was only 29.[^o6] He held a chair at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (1994–96) and was head of the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) from 1996 to 2001. From 2007 to 2018, he was Director, [Wellcome Trust Centre for Human Genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellcome_Trust_Centre_for_Human_Genetics) (WTCHG) in Oxford.[^o7] He is a fellow at [St Anne's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Anne's_College,_Oxford).

Many leading statistical geneticists worked with Donnelly as young researchers including [David Balding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Balding), [Matthew Stephens](wikipedia://en/2950611) and [Jonathan Pritchard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pritchard). One area in which he has a leading reputation is in the interpretation of [DNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA) evidence. He has acted as an [expert witness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expert_witness) on [forensic science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_science) in criminal trials.[^o8]

He is noted for his collaborative work with [biologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biologist). He has been heavily involved in a number of large scale projects, such as the [International HapMap Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_HapMap_Project)[^o9] and the [Wellcome Trust Case Control Consortium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellcome_Trust_Case_Control_Consortium), a [genome-wide association study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome-wide_association_study).[^o10]

In 2015, Donnelly was elected as Chairman of the Royal Society's Machine Learning Working Group.[^o11]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Donnelly was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2006[^o13] and also elected as a Fellow of the [Academy of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Medical_Sciences) in 2008.[^o14]

Other significant awards and honours have included:[^o15]

- 1980 University Medal, [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland)
- 1980-1983 [Rhodes Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_Scholarship)
- 1984-1985 [University of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales) Research Fellowship
- 1988 Elected Ordinary Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute)
- 1990 Special Invited Lecturer, [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)
- 1990-1995 SERC Advanced Fellowship
- 1995 Elected Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)
- 1999 Elected Honorary Fellow, [Institute of Actuaries](wikipedia://en/852871)
- 2000 Bernoulli Lecturer, 5th World Congress of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society)
- 2001 Forum Lecturer, European Meeting of Statisticians
- 2002 Editor's Invited Paper, Statistical Society of Australia
- 2002 Mitchell Prize, of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and ISBA
- 2004 Guy Medal in Silver, [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)
- 2007 Medallion Lecturer, [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).
- 2007 The main paper from the [Wellcome Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellcome_Trust) Case Control Consortium, which Donnelly chaired, won several awards, including *[The Lancet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lancet)*'s Paper of the Year, *[Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American)*'s Research Leader of the Year, the Amadeus Prize, and one of Nature's Editor's Picks for 2007.
- 2009 Awarded the [Weldon Memorial Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weldon_Memorial_Prize)[^o15]
- 2022 Awarded an honorary doctoral degree from the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne)[^o16]

Donnelly was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor) in the [2019 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Birthday_Honours) for services to the understanding of human genetics in disease.
