# Master of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Master of Mathematics"}

A **Master of Mathematics** (or **MMath**) [degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_degree) is a specific advanced [integrated Master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree#Types) for courses in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).

:::

## United Kingdom {#United_Kingdom}

In the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), the MMath is the internationally recognized standard qualification after a four-year course in mathematics at a [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University).[^o3] The MMath programme was set up by most leading universities after the *[Neumann Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neumann_Report)* in 1992.[^o4] It is classed as a level 7 qualification in the [Frameworks of Higher Education Qualifications of UK Degree-Awarding Bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frameworks_of_Higher_Education_Qualifications_of_UK_Degree-Awarding_Bodies). The [UCAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCAS) course codes for the MMath degrees start at *G100* upwards, most courses taking the codes *G101 - G104*.

### Notes {#Notes}

## Canada {#Canada}

In [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), the **MMath** is a [graduate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_degree) offered by the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). The length of the MMath degree program is typically between one and two years, and consists of course work along with a research component. The first Waterloo MMath degrees were awarded in 1967. MMath is the [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) offered by [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science), since it is within the [Faculty of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4320685) at Waterloo.

## India {#India}

In [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), the **M. Math.** is a [graduate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_degree) offered by the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418). The course consists of two years and is done in the [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) and [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata) centres in alternating years. The participants are selected through two screening (one objective and other subjective) tests along with an interview and it is arguably the best graduate course in mathematics offered in India.
