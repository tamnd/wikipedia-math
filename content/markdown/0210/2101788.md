# Krasner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krasner"}

**Krasner** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Gerald Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Krasner) (born 1949), former chairman of Leeds United A.F.C.
  - *[Krasner v McMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasner_v_McMath)*
- [Larry Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Krasner) (born 1961), American lawyer, District Attorney of Philadelphia
- [Lee Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Krasner) (1908–1984), American artist
  - [Pollock-Krasner House and Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollock-Krasner_House_and_Studio)
  - [Pollock-Krasner Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollock-Krasner_Foundation)
- [Lisa Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Krasner), American politician
- [Louis Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Krasner) (1903–1995), Russian-American violinist and teacher
- [Marc Krasner](wikipedia://en/37126879) (1912-1985), Russian-French mathematician known for
  - [Krasner's lemma](wikipedia://en/23007058), in number theory
- [Milton R. Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_R._Krasner), American film cinematographer
- [Naum Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naum_Krasner) (1924–1999), Ukrainian/Russian mathematician and economist
- [Stephen D. Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_D._Krasner) (born 1942), political scientist and author
- [Steven Krasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Krasner) (born 1953), sports writer and author of children's books
