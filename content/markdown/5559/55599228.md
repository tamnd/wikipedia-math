# Jean D. Gibbons

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean D. Gibbons"}

**Jean Dickinson Gibbons** (née Dickinson, born 1938)[^o2] is an American statistician, an expert in [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369) and an author of books on statistics. She was the first chair of the Committee on Women in Statistics of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), and the Jean Dickinson Gibbons Graduate Program in Statistics at [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech) is named for her.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Despite her parents' expectations that she become a nurse or teacher, Gibbons graduated magna cum laude in mathematics in 1958 from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), and continued at Duke for a master's degree, with a master's thesis on *Judgments Concerning Applications of Measures of Central Tendency*. She went on to do graduate study at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), but completed her PhD in 1962 from Virginia Tech. Her dissertation was *The Small-Sample Power of some Nonparametric Tests*.

After teaching at [Mercer University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercer_University) and the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati), she was hired by the [Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1963. She followed her husband to the [University of Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alabama), where she remained despite separating from him and remarrying, until her early retirement in 1995 at age 57.

## Books {#Books}

Gibbons is the author of ten books. They include:

- *Nonparametric Statistical Inference* (McGraw Hill, 1971; 2nd ed., Marcel Dekker, 1985; 4th ed. with Subhabrata Chakraborti, Marcel Dekker, 2003; 5th ed., CRC Press, 2010; 6th ed., CRC Press 2020)
- *Selecting and Ordering Populations: A New Statistical Methodology* (with [Ingram Olkin](wikipedia://en/26702356) and [Milton Sobel](wikipedia://en/62857095), Wiley, 1977; Society for Industrial & Applied Mathematics and Cambridge University Press, 2008)
- *Concepts of Nonparametric Theory* (with John W Pratt, Springer, 1981)
- *Nonparametric Methods for Quantitative Analysis* (American Sciences Press, 1976; 2nd ed., 1985; 3rd ed., 1997)
- *Rank Correlation Methods* (5th ed., with [Maurice Kendall](wikipedia://en/589136), Edward Arnold, 1990, update of a book originally published by Kendall alone in 1948)
- *Nonparametric Statistics: An introduction* (Sage, 1993)
- *Nonparametric Measures of Association* (Sage, 1993)

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Gibbons became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1972, becoming "probably the youngest female ever elected as a fellow".

In 2015, the graduate program in statistics at [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech) was named in her honor.
