# Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences"}

The ***Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences*** is a triannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by [World Scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Scientific) as a [diamond open access journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_open_access_journal) with [article processing charges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_processing_charge) covered by [King Abdulaziz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdulaziz_University). The journal publishes expository papers, mostly invited, in all areas of mathematics, as well as short papers with original research.[^o3] The journal's [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Efim Zelmanov](wikipedia://en/1104849) ([University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego)), S.K. Jain (King Abdulaziz University), and Ahmed Alsaedi (King Abdulaziz University). The journal was established in 2011.[^o4]

:::

The journal should not be confused with the *Bulletin des Sciences Mathématiques* (*Bull. Sci. Math*, 1870 – present).

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in *[MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)*, *[ZbMATH Open](wikipedia://en/1701961)*, [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences, and the [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.485.[^o6] For 2016 the journal's [Mathematical citation quotient (MSQ)](wikipedia://en/1485646#Mathematical_citation_quotient) from [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683) was 0.97.[^o7]
