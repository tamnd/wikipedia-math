# Dianne Cook (statistician)

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Dianne Cook"}

**Dianne Helen Cook** is an Australian statistician, the former editor of the *[Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics](wikipedia://en/31074852)*, and an expert on the [visualization of high-dimensional data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization). She is Professor of Business Analytics in the Department of Econometrics and Business Statistics at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) and professor emeritus of statistics at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University). The [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) status was chosen so that she could continue to supervise graduate students at Iowa State after moving to Australia.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

:::

## Education and early life {#Education_and_early_life}

Dianne Helen Cook grew up in [Wauchope, New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wauchope,_New_South_Wales) as an athletic farm girl, the first woman to play on her local (men's) [cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket) team. She studied statistics at [University of New England (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29), where she earned a [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) and [Dip.Ed.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma_of_Education) in 1982. She received her [MS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in 1990 and her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1993 from [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University); her dissertation, supervised jointly by Andreas Buja and Javier Cabrera, was *Grand Tour and Projection Pursuit*.

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Cook joined the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) faculty in 1993, and remained there until her move to [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) in 2015. At Iowa State, her students have included [Hadley Wickham](wikipedia://en/41916270) and [Yihui Xie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yihui_Xie).

She is one of the developers of [GGobi](wikipedia://en/13543254), and with [Deborah F. Swayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_F._Swayne), she is the author of *Interactive and Dynamic Graphics for Data Analysis: With R and GGobi* (Springer, 2007).

She is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association). She was editor of the *[Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics](wikipedia://en/31074852)* from 2016 to 2018.[^o12]
