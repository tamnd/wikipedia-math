# Elisa T. Lee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elisa T. Lee"}

**Elisa T. Lee** (born May 1, 1939) is a Chinese-American statistician, affiliated with the [University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma_Health_Sciences_Center), where she is Regents Professor Emeritus, George Lynn Cross Research Professor of Biostatistics and Epidemiology, and director of the Center for American Indian Health Research.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Lee was born on May 1, 1939, in Yungsing, China. She earned a bachelor's degree from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) in 1961, a master's degree from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1964, and a PhD from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1973. She is a naturalized U.S. citizen.

She worked for [Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Laboratories) from 1965 to 1971, and then for the [University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_MD_Anderson_Cancer_Center) from 1971 to 1975, before moving to the University of Oklahoma. With John Wang, she is the author of *Statistical Methods for Survival Data Analysis* (Wiley, 1980; 4th ed., 2013).

She became a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1996.
