# Lee-Ann Hayek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lee-Ann Hayek"}

**Lee-Ann Collins Hayek** is the chief [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711) at the [National Museum of Natural History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_Natural_History). Her work has included studying the proportions of metals in Renaissance bronze, and the response of Pandas to vaccines.

:::

With Martin Buzas, she is the author of *Surveying Natural Populations: Quantitative Tools for Assessing Biodiversity* (Cambridge University Press, 1997 & 2010).

She is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664).
