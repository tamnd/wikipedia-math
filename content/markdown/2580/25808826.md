# Sergiu Klainerman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Sergiu Klainerman"}

**Sergiu Klainerman** (born May 13, 1950) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to the study of [hyperbolic differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_differential_equation) and [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024). He is currently the [Eugene Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Higgins) Professor of Mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where he has been teaching since 1987.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in 1950 in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews) family. After attending the [Petru Groza High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tudor_Vianu_National_College_of_Computer_Science),[^o5] he studied mathematics at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) from 1969 to 1974. For graduate studies he went to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), obtaining his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1978.[^o6] His thesis, written under the direction of [Fritz John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_John) and [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725), was titled *Global Existence for Nonlinear Wave Equations*. From 1978 to 1980 Klainerman was a [Miller Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Research_Fellows) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), while from 1980 to 1987 he was a faculty member at New York University's [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), rising in rank to Professor in 1986.[^o6]

Klainerman is a member of the [U.S. National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._National_Academy_of_Sciences) (elected 2005),[^o8] a foreign member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) (elected 2002)[^o9] and a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (elected 1996).[^o10] In 2003, he was awarded the [Romanian Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Merit_%28Romania%29), Commander rank.[^o11] He was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]

He was named a [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellow) in 1991[^o13] and [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) in 1997.[^o14] Klainerman was awarded the [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) by the American Mathematical Society in 1999 "for his contributions to nonlinear hyperbolic equations".[^o2] He is currently a co-Editor-in-Chief of [Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publications_Mathématiques_de_l'IHÉS).[^o15]
