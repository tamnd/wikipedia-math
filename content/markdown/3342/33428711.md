# Analysis Situs (book)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Analysis Situs"}

***Analysis Situs*** is a book by the [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) mathematician [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen), published in 1922. It is based on his 1916 lectures at the Cambridge Colloquium of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). The book, which went into a second edition in 1931, was the first English-language [textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and served for many years as the standard reference for the domain. Its contents were based on the work of [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740) as well as Veblen's own work with his former student and colleague, [James Alexander](wikipedia://en/9848523).[^n1]

:::

Among the many innovations in the book was the first definition of a [topological manifold](wikipedia://en/2119193),[^n2] and systematisations of [Betti number](wikipedia://en/346262), [torsion](wikipedia://en/681186), the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004), and the [topological classification problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_spaces).[^n3]
