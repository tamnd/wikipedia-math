# Onno J. Boxma

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Onno J. Boxma"}

**Onno Johan Boxma** (born 1952) is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and Professor at the [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology), known for several contributions to [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578) and applied [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague), Boxma earned his B.Sc. in Mathematics at [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology) in 1974, and his Ph.D. cum laude in Mathematic from [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) in 1977 on the dissertation entitled "Analysis of Models for Tandem Queues", advised by [Wim Cohen](wikipedia://en/14449263).[^o5][^o6]

Boxma continued at Utrecht as faculty from 1974 to 1985, and was [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research) postdoctoral fellow in 1978–79, before joining the faculty of [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam). There he chaired the performance analysis group until 1998. He was full professor at [University of Tilburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tilburg) from 1987 to 1988. and since 1998 he is as full professor holding the chair of Stochastic Operations Research in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at [Technische Universiteit Eindhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universiteit_Eindhoven), becoming vice dean of the department in 2009.

He was the editor-in-chief of [Queueing Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queueing_Systems_%28journal%29) from 2004 to 2009, and scientific director of [EURANDOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURANDOM)[^o7] from 2005-2010. In 2009 he was awarded an honorary degree by the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) (Israel),[^o8] and received the 2011 [ACM SIGMETRICS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_SIGMETRICS) Achievement Award in June 2011.[^o9] Also, he is honorary professor in [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University), Edinburgh, UK (2008-2010 and 2011-2013).

## Work {#Work}

Boxma's research focuses on the field of applied probability and stochastic operations, particularly of queueing theory and its application to the performance analysis of computer, communication and production systems.
