# Proof mining

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Proof mining"}

In [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478), a branch of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), **proof mining** (or **proof unwinding**) is a research program that studies or analyzes formalized proofs, especially in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), to obtain explicit bounds, [ranges](wikipedia://en/579675) or [rates of convergence](wikipedia://en/999701) from proofs that, when expressed in natural language, appear to be [nonconstructive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconstructive_proof).[^o2] This research has led to improved results in analysis obtained from the analysis of classical proofs.

:::
