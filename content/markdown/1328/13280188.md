# 20,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="20,000"}

**20,000** (**twenty thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) that comes after 19,999 and before 20,001.

:::

## Selected numbers in the range 20001–29999 {#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_20001–29999}

### 20001 to 20999 {#20001_to_20999}

- **20002** = number of surface-points of a tetrahedron with edge-length 100
- **[20067](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20067)** = The smallest uninteresting number[^o6][^o7][^o8]
- **20100** = sum of the first 200 natural numbers (hence the 200th [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915))
- **20160** = 23rd [highly composite number](wikipedia://en/208732); the smallest order belonging to two non-isomorphic [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600): the [alternating group](wikipedia://en/49726) *A*<sub>8</sub> and the [Chevalley group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group) *A*<sub>2</sub>(4)
- **20161** = the largest integer that cannot be expressed as a sum of two [abundant numbers](wikipedia://en/321831)
- **20230** = [pentagonal pyramidal number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_pyramidal_number)
- **20412** = [Leyland number](wikipedia://en/4618420): 9<sup>3</sup> + 3<sup>9</sup>
- **20540** = [square pyramidal number](wikipedia://en/542343)
- **20569** = [tetranacci number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetranacci_number)
- **20593** = [unique prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_prime) in base 12
- **20736** = 144<sup>2</sup> = 12<sup>4</sup>, 10000[<sub>12</sub>](wikipedia://en/8400), [palindromic](wikipedia://en/105956) in [base](wikipedia://en/437052) 15 (6226<sub>15</sub>), also called a dozen great-gross in some [duodecimal](wikipedia://en/8400) nomenclature.
- **20793** = [little Schroeder number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oeis:A0001003)
- **20871** = The number of weeks in exactly 400 years in the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar)
- **20903** = first [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) of form 120*k* + 23 that is *not* a [full reptend prime](wikipedia://en/2673943)
