# 60,000

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="60,000"}

**60,000** (**sixty thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) that follows [59,999](wikipedia://en/13280216#59000_to_59999) and precedes 60,001. It is the value of [$\varphi$](wikipedia://en/53452)([75025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_number)).

:::

## Selected numbers in the range 60,000–69,999 {#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_60,000–69,999}

### 60,001 to 60,999 {#60,001_to_60,999}

- **60,049** = [Leyland number](wikipedia://en/4618420) using 3 & 10 (3<sup>10</sup> + 10<sup>3</sup>)
- **60,101** = smallest [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) with period of reciprocal [100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_%28number%29)

### 61,000 to 61,999 {#61,000_to_61,999}

- **61,871** = approximate number of [planck lengths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_lengths) to amount to one [quectometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quectometer), the smallest [SI unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SI_unit) of length.[^o14]
