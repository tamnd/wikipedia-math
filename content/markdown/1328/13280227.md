# 70,000

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="70,000"}

**70,000** (**seventy thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) that comes after [69,999](wikipedia://en/13280221#69,000_to_69,999) and before 70,001. It is a round number.

:::
