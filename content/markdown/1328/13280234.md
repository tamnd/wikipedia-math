# 90,000

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="90,000"}

**90,000** (**ninety thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [89,999](wikipedia://en/13280231#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_80,000–89,999) and preceding 90,001. It is the sum of the cubes of the first 24 positive integers, and is the square of 300.

:::

::: {#o4 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="99999 (number)"}

## Selected numbers in the range 90,000–99,999 {#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_90,000–99,999}

- **90,625** = the only five-digit [automorphic number](wikipedia://en/234444): 90625<sup>2</sup> = 8212890625[^o5]
- **91,125** = 45<sup>3</sup>
- **91,144** = Fine number
- **92,205** = number of 23-bead necklaces (turning over is allowed) where complements are equivalent
- **93,312** = [Leyland number](wikipedia://en/4618420): 6<sup>6</sup> + 6<sup>6</sup>.[^o8] Also a 3-smooth number.
- **94,249** = [palindromic](wikipedia://en/105956) square: 307<sup>2</sup>
- **94,932** = Leyland number: 7<sup>5</sup> + 5<sup>7</sup>[^o8]
- **95,121** = [Kaprekar number](wikipedia://en/472129): 95121<sup>2</sup> = 9048004641; 90480 + 04641 = 95121[^o9]
- **95,040** = the order of the [Mathieu group](wikipedia://en/380207) M<sub>1</sub><sub>2</sub>
- **95,420** = number of 22-bead binary necklaces with beads of 2 colors where the colors may be swapped but turning over is not allowed
- **96,557** = [Markov number](wikipedia://en/2965726): 5<sup>2</sup> + 6466<sup>2</sup> + 96557<sup>2</sup> = 3 × 5 × 6466 × 96557[^o11]
- **97,336** = 46<sup>3</sup>, the largest 5-digit cube
- **98,304** = 3-[smooth number](wikipedia://en/1035039)
- **98,980** = year in which the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) will begin to differ from the [tropical year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_year) by one-twelfth of a tropical year.[^o12] In other words, it is the year in which the [calendar year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calendar_year) will have about a one [month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Month) offset from the [equinoxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equinoxes).
- **99,066** = largest number whose [square](wikipedia://en/659942) uses all of the [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) [digits](wikipedia://en/280582) once: 99066<sup>2</sup> = 9814072356. It is also strobogrammatic in decimal.
- **99,551** = prime p such that p is k-th prime and p ends in k. [17](wikipedia://en/208288) also has this property.
- **99,719** = The largest five-digit number that is a left-and-right-truncatable prime
- **99,856** = 316<sup>2</sup>, the largest 5-digit square
- **99,991** = largest five-digit [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666)
- **99,999** = [repdigit](wikipedia://en/216551), Kaprekar number: 99999<sup>2</sup> = 9999800001; 99998 + 00001 = 99999[^o9]

### Primes {#Primes}

There are 879 prime numbers between 90000 and 100000.
