# 40,000

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="40,000"}

**40,000** (**forty thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) that comes after 39,999 and before 40,001. It is the square of 200.

:::

## Selected numbers in the range 40001–49999 {#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_40001–49999}

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="40320 (number)"}

### 40001 to 40999 {#40001_to_40999}

- **40320** = smallest [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) (8!) that is not a [highly composite number](wikipedia://en/208732)
- **40425** = [square pyramidal number](wikipedia://en/542343)
- **40585** = largest [factorion](wikipedia://en/13247379)[^o6]
- **40678** = [pentagonal pyramidal number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_pyramidal_number)
- **40755** = the smallest number >1 to be a [Triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915), [Pentagonal number](wikipedia://en/504786), and a [Hexagonal number](wikipedia://en/494013).[^o7]
- **40804** = [palindromic](wikipedia://en/105956) square

:::
