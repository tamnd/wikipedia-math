# Sphenomegacorona

*Image: J88 sphenomegacorona.stl.* 3D model of a sphenomegacorona

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sphenomegacorona"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **sphenomegacorona** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) with 16 equilateral triangles and 2 squares as its faces.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The sphenomegacorona was named by (Johnson 1966) in which he used the prefix *spheno-* referring to a wedge-like complex formed by two adjacent *lunes*—a [square](wikipedia://en/659939) with [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) attached on its opposite sides. The suffix *\-megacorona* refers to a crownlike complex of 12 triangles, contrasted with the smaller triangular complex that makes the [sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1142750). By joining both complexes, the resulting polyhedron has 16 equilateral triangles and 2 squares, making 18 faces. All of its faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306), categorizing the sphenomegacorona as a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034)—a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all of the faces are regular polygons—enumerated as the 88th Johnson solid $J_{88}$. It is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron), meaning it cannot be separated by a plane into two small regular-faced polyhedra.

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) of a sphenomegacorona $A$ is the total of polygonal faces' area—16 equilateral triangles and 2 squares. The [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of a sphenomegacorona is obtained by finding the root of a polynomial, and its decimal expansion—denoted as $\xi$—is given by . With edge length $a$, its surface area and volume can be formulated as:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \left(2+4\sqrt{3}\right)a^2 &\approx 8.928a^2, \\ V &= \xi a^3 &\approx 1.948a^3. \end{aligned}$$

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $k \approx 0.59463$ be the smallest positive root of the [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000)

$$\begin{aligned} & 1680 x^{16}- 4800 x^{15} - 3712 x^{14} + 17216 x^{13} \\ + & 1568 x^{12} - 24576 x^{11} + 2464 x^{10} + 17248 x^9 \\ - & 3384 x^8 - 5584 x^7 + 2000 x^6+ 240 x^5 \\- & 776 x^4+ 304 x^3 + 200 x^2 - 56 x -23 \end{aligned}$$

Then, [Cartesian coordinates](wikipedia://en/7706) of a sphenomegacorona with edge length 2 are given by the union of the orbits of the points

$$\begin{aligned} &\left(0,1,2\sqrt{1-k^2}\right),\,(2k,1,0),\,\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{3-4k^2}}{\sqrt{1-k^2}}+1,\frac{1-2k^2}{\sqrt{1-k^2}}\right), \\ &\left(1,0,-\sqrt{2+4k-4k^2}\right),\,\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{3-4k^2}\left(2k^2-1\right)}{\left(k^2-1\right)\sqrt{1-k^2}}+1,\frac{2k^4-1}{\left(1-k^2\right)^\frac32}\right) \end{aligned}$$

under the action of the [group](wikipedia://en/28186) generated by reflections about the xz-plane and the yz-plane.
