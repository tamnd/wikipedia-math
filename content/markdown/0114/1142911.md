# Ernest Vessiot

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernest Vessiot"}

**Ernest Vessiot** (; 8 March 1865 – 17 October 1952) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), and died in [La Bauche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Bauche), [Savoie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoie), France. He entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1884.

:::

He was Maître de Conférences at [Lille University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in 1892-1893, then moved at Toulouse and Lyon.

After 1910, he was a professor of [analytical mechanics](wikipedia://en/454450) and [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). He presided over entrance examinations at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). As director of [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) until 1935, he overviewed the construction of its new physics, chemistry and geology buildings of 24, Rue Lhomond.

He was elected a member of the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences) in 1943.

Vessiot's work on [Picard–Vessiot theory](wikipedia://en/30247996) dealt with the integrability of [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations).
