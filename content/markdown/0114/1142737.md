# Hebesphenomegacorona

*Image: J89 hebesphenomegacorona.stl.* 3D model of a hebesphenomegacorona

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hebesphenomegacorona"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **hebesphenomegacorona** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) with 18 equilateral triangles and 3 squares as its faces.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The hebesphenomegacorona is named by (Johnson 1966) in which he used the prefix *hebespheno-* referring to a blunt wedge-like complex formed by three adjacent *lunes*—a [square](wikipedia://en/659939) with [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) attached on its opposite sides. The suffix *\-megacorona* refers to a crownlike complex of 12 triangles. By joining both complexes together, the result polyhedron has 18 equilateral triangles and 3 squares, making 21 faces. All of its faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306), categorizing the hebesphenomegacorona as a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034)—a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all of its faces are regular polygons—enumerated as 89th Johnson solid $J_{89}$. It is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron), meaning it cannot be separated by a plane into two small regular-faced polyhedra.

The surface area of a hebesphenomegacorona with edge length $a$ can be determined by adding the area of its faces, 18 equilateral triangles and 3 squares

$$\frac{6 + 9\sqrt{3}}{2}a^2 \approx 10.7942a^2,$$

and its volume is $2.9129a^3$.

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $a \approx 0.21684$ be the second smallest positive root of the [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000)

$$\begin{aligned} &26880x^{10} + 35328x^9 - 25600x^8 - 39680x^7 + 6112x^6 \\ &\quad {}+ 13696x^5 + 2128x^4 - 1808x^3 - 1119x^2 + 494x - 47 \end{aligned}$$

Then, [Cartesian coordinates](wikipedia://en/7706) of a hebesphenomegacorona with edge length 2 are given by the union of the orbits of the points

$$\begin{aligned} &\left(1,1,2\sqrt{1-a^2}\right),\ \left(1+2a,1,0\right),\ \left(0,1+\sqrt{2}\sqrt{\frac{2a-1}{a-1}},-\frac{2a^2+a-1}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}\right),\ \left(1,0,-\sqrt{3-4a^2}\right), \\ &\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{2(3-4a^2)(1-2a)}+\sqrt{1+a}}{2(1-a)\sqrt{1+a}},\frac{(2a-1)\sqrt{3-4a^2}}{2(1-a)}-\frac{\sqrt{2(1-2a)}}{2(1-a)\sqrt{1+a}}\right) \end{aligned}$$

under the action of the [group](wikipedia://en/28186) generated by reflections about the xz-plane and the yz-plane.
