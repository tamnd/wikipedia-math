# Sphenocorona

*Image: J86 sphenocorona.stl.* 3D model of a sphenocorona

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sphenocorona"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **sphenocorona** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) with 12 equilateral triangles and 2 squares as its faces.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The sphenocorona was named by (Johnson 1966) in which he used the prefix *spheno-* referring to a [wedge](wikipedia://en/2951640)-like complex formed by two adjacent *[lunes](wikipedia://en/12935671)*—a [square](wikipedia://en/659939) with [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) attached on its opposite sides. The suffix *\-corona* refers to a crownlike complex of 8 equilateral triangles. By joining both complexes together, the resulting polyhedron has 12 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) and 2 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), making 14 faces. A [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306) is called a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034). The sphenocorona is among them, enumerated as the 86th Johnson solid $J_{86}$. It is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron), meaning it cannot be separated by a plane into two small regular-faced polyhedra.

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) of a sphenocorona with edge length $a$ can be calculated as

$$A=\left(2+3\sqrt{3}\right)a^2\approx7.19615a^2,$$

and its [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) as

$$\left(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{1 + 3 \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}} + \sqrt{13 + 3 \sqrt{6}}}\right)a^3\approx1.51535a^3.$$

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let

$$\begin{aligned} k &= \frac{6 + \sqrt{6} + 2\sqrt{213-57\sqrt{6}}}{30} \\ &\approx 0.85273 \end{aligned}$$

be the smallest positive root of the [quartic polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartic_polynomial) $60x^4 - 48x^3 - 100x^2 + 56x + 23$. Then, [Cartesian coordinates](wikipedia://en/7706) of a sphenocorona with edge length 2 are given by the union of the orbits of the points

$$\left(0,1,2\sqrt{1-k^2}\right),\,(2k,1,0),\left(0,1+\frac{\sqrt{3-4k^2}}{\sqrt{1-k^2}},\frac{1-2k^2}{\sqrt{1-k^2}}\right),\,\left(1,0,-\sqrt{2+4k-4k^2}\right)$$

under the action of the [group](wikipedia://en/28186) generated by reflections about the xz-plane and the yz-plane.

Since the coordinates of the sphenocorona can be expressed using square roots only, it is a [constructible](wikipedia://en/523430) polyhedron.

## Variations {#Variations}

The sphenocorona is also the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [isogonal](wikipedia://en/581560) n-gonal [double antiprismoid](wikipedia://en/2114155#Double_antiprismoids) where n is an odd number greater than one, including the [grand antiprism](wikipedia://en/4058047) with pairs of trapezoid rather than square faces.
