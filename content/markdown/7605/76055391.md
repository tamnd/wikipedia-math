# Sally Thurston

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sally Thurston"}

**Sarah** (**Sally**) **Newcomb Whitney Thurston** (born 1956)[^o2] is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) and [environmental statistician](wikipedia://en/8919339) whose research involves the application of [Bayesian hierarchical modeling](wikipedia://en/42734031) to problems in [environmental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_health), including work on [endocrine disruptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endocrine_disruptors), the effects of [mercury in fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_in_fish) on [prenatal development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prenatal_development), and the health effects of [air pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution). She is a professor of biostatistics and a professor of environmental science in the Department of Biostatistics and Computational Biology at the [University of Rochester Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester_Medical_Center).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Thurston majored in biology at [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College), graduating in 1978. After earning a master's degree in natural resources from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1991, she went to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) for doctoral study in statistics. She earned a second master's degree in 1992 before completing her Ph.D. in 1997. Her doctoral dissertation, *Error Analysis of Food Stamp Microsimulation Models*, was jointly advised by Alan M. Zaslavsky and [Donald Rubin](wikipedia://en/7790659).

She stayed at Harvard as a postdoctoral research fellow (1997–2000) and research associate (2000–2002) before moving to her present position at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 2002. She added a second affiliation in oncology in 2005, and was tenured in 2008.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Thurston is the daughter of mathematician [Hassler Whitney](wikipedia://en/391794). Her mother Mary Barnett Garfield was a granddaughter of [Harry Augustus Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Augustus_Garfield) and great-granddaughter of [James A. Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_A._Garfield).[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Thurston became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2006. She is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), elected in 2019.
