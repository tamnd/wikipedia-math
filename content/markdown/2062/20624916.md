# Jan H. van Schuppen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan H. van Schuppen"}

**Jan Hendrik van Schuppen** (born 6 October 1947) is a Dutch mathematician and Professor at the Department of Mathematics of the [Vrije Universiteit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit), known for his contributions in the field of [systems theory](wikipedia://en/29238), particularly on [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and [system identification](wikipedia://en/552466), on [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), and on a number of related practical applications.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Van Schuppen obtained a PhD at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 1973, where his PhD supervisor was [Pravin Varaiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravin_Varaiya).

Van Schuppen works as a full professor at the Department of Mathematics of the [Free University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit) and as a research leader at the [CWI](wikipedia://en/506063) research institute in Amsterdam. He has been coordinating several European Union funded research networks such as the European Research Network System Identification, for which he has been the Netherlands leader. The lists among the PhD students who worked under van Schuppen's supervision Hendrik (Henk) Nijmeijer, Jan Willem Polderman, Peter Spreij and [Damiano Brigo](wikipedia://en/20519590).

Van Schuppen is Editor in Chief of *[Mathematics of Control, Signals, and Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Control,_Signals,_and_Systems)*, has been Departmental Editor of the *Journal of Discrete Event Dynamic Systems* in 1990–2000, and has been Associate Editor-at-Large of the prestigious and leading journal *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* in 1999–2001.

## Work {#Work}

Van Schuppen's research interest are in the areas of [systems theory](wikipedia://en/29238) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934). These include [system identification](wikipedia://en/552466), and [realization theory](wikipedia://en/10353119), and the area of [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), with control of [discrete-event systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_event_dynamic_system), control of hybrid systems, control and system theory of positive systems, control of stochastic systems, and [adaptive control](wikipedia://en/2020708).

He worked also on the [filtering problem](wikipedia://en/12433418), on dynamic games and team problems, on probability and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and on applications of the theory including control and system theory of biochemical reaction networks, control of communication systems and networks, and control of motorway traffic in a consultancy for the Dutch administration.
