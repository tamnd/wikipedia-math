# Cohomology with compact support

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohomology with compact support"}

In mathematics, **cohomology with compact support** refers to certain [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) theories, usually with some condition requiring that cocycles should have compact support.

:::

## Singular cohomology with compact support {#Singular_cohomology_with_compact_support}

Let $X$ be a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450). Then

$$\displaystyle H_c^\ast(X;R) := \varinjlim_{K\subseteq X \,\text{compact}} H^\ast(X,X\setminus K;R)$$

By definition, this is the cohomology of the sub–[chain complex](wikipedia://en/138404) $C_c^\ast(X;R)$ consisting of all singular [cochains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochain) $\phi: C_i(X;R)\to R$ that have compact support in the sense that there exists some compact $K\subseteq X$ such that $\phi$ vanishes on all chains in $X\setminus K$.

## Functorial definition {#Functorial_definition}

Let $X$ be a topological space and $p:X\to \star$ the map to the point. Using the [direct image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_image) and [direct image with compact support](wikipedia://en/13910217) functors $p_*,p_!:\text{Sh}(X)\to \text{Sh}(\star)=\text{Ab}$, one can define cohomology and cohomology with compact support of a sheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$ as

$$\displaystyle H^i(X,\mathcal{F})\ = \ R^ip_*\mathcal{F},$$

$$\displaystyle H^i_c(X,\mathcal{F})\ = \ R^ip_!\mathcal{F}.$$

Taking for $\mathcal{F}$ the [constant sheaf](wikipedia://en/2783581) with coefficients in a ring $R$ recovers the previous definition.

## de Rham cohomology with compact support for smooth manifolds {#de_Rham_cohomology_with_compact_support_for_smooth_manifolds}

Given a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) *X*, let $\Omega^k_{\mathrm c}(X)$ be the [real vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) of *k*-[forms](wikipedia://en/221519) on *X* with compact support, and *d* be the standard [exterior derivative](wikipedia://en/220782). Then the [de Rham cohomology](wikipedia://en/221530) groups with compact support $H^q_{\mathrm c}(X)$ are the [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) of the [chain complex](wikipedia://en/138404) $(\Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm c}(X),d)$:

$$0 \to \Omega^0_{\mathrm c}(X) \to \Omega^1_{\mathrm c}(X) \to \Omega^2_{\mathrm c}(X) \to \cdots$$

*i.e.*, $H^q_{\mathrm c}(X)$ is the vector space of [closed](wikipedia://en/404181) *q*-forms [modulo](wikipedia://en/898784) that of exact *q*-forms.

Despite their definition as the homology of an ascending complex, the de Rham groups with compact support demonstrate [covariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariance_and_contravariance_of_functors) behavior; for example, given the inclusion mapping *j* for an open set *U* of *X*, extension of forms on *U* to *X* (by defining them to be 0 on *X*–*U*) is a map $j_*: \Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm c}(U) \to \Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm c}(X)$ inducing a map

$$j_*: H^q_{\mathrm c}(U) \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(X).$$

They also demonstrate contravariant behavior with respect to [proper maps](wikipedia://en/1190262) - that is, maps such that the inverse image of every compact set is compact. Let *f*: *Y* → *X* be such a map; then the [pullback](wikipedia://en/494995)

$$f^*: \Omega^q_{\mathrm c}(X) \to \Omega^q_{\mathrm c}(Y) \sum_I g_I \, dx_{i_1} \wedge \ldots \wedge dx_{i_q} \mapsto \sum_I(g_I \circ f) \, d(x_{i_1} \circ f) \wedge \ldots \wedge d(x_{i_q} \circ f)$$

induces a map

$$H^q_{\mathrm c}(X) \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(Y).$$

If *Z* is a submanifold of *X* and *U* = *X*–*Z* is the complementary [open set](wikipedia://en/39358), there is a long exact sequence

$$\cdots \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(U) \overset{j_*}{\longrightarrow} H^q_{\mathrm c}(X) \overset{i^*}{\longrightarrow} H^q_{\mathrm c}(Z) \overset{\delta}{\longrightarrow} H^{q+1}_{\mathrm c}(U) \to \cdots$$

called the long [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215) of cohomology with compact support. It has numerous applications, such as the [Jordan curve theorem](wikipedia://en/267444), which is obtained for *X* = **R**² and *Z* a simple closed curve in *X*.

De Rham cohomology with compact support satisfies a covariant [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](wikipedia://en/378677): if *U* and *V* are open sets covering *X*, then

$$\cdots \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(U \cap V) \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(U)\oplus H^q_{\mathrm c}(V) \to H^q_{\mathrm c}(X) \overset{\delta}{\longrightarrow} H^{q+1}_{\mathrm c}(U\cap V) \to \cdots$$

where all maps are induced by extension by zero is also exact.
