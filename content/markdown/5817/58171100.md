# Frans Oort

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frans Oort"}

**Frans Oort** (born 17 July 1935) is a Dutch mathematician who specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Oort studied from 1952 to 1958 at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University), where he graduated with a thesis on [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225). He received his doctorate in 1961 in Leiden from Willem Titus van Est and [Jaap Murre](wikipedia://en/64164858) with thesis *Reducible and Multiple Algebraic Curves*, but had previously studied under [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and [Aldo Andreotti](wikipedia://en/34935909) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa). Oort was from 1961 at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), where he became a professor in 1967. In 1977, until his retirement in 2000, he was a professor at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University).[^o5]

He was a visiting scholar at several academic institutions, including [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1966/67) and [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) (1972/73). In 2008 he was the Eilenberg Professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o6]

His doctoral students include [Bas Edixhoven](wikipedia://en/69886727), [Michiel Hazewinkel](wikipedia://en/40490058), [Aise Johan de Jong](wikipedia://en/29023548), [Hendrik Lenstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lenstra) and [Joseph Steenbrink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Steenbrink).[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Oort's research deals with, among other topics, [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) and their [moduli](wikipedia://en/64177800). In 1994, he formulated what is now known as the [André–Oort conjecture](wikipedia://en/32262980) (generalizing a conjecture made in 1989 by [Yves André](wikipedia://en/58118010)).[^o8] In 2000 Oort proved a conjecture made by Grothendieck in 1970.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1962, Oort made a short contribution *Multiple algebraic curves* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), but was not an invited speaker. In 2011 he was elected a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o5] In July 2013, he gave a talk at the [International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/45275469) in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei).[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Oort married and later divorced author Marijke Harberts (1936–2020).[^o13]
