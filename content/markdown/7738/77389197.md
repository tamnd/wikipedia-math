# Adam Kanigowski

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adam Kanigowski"}

**Adam Kanigowski** (born 15 April 1989)[^o3] is a Polish mathematician specializing in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986). He is a professor at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kanigowski was born in [Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toruń).[^o3] He earned his master's degree in mathematics from the [Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus_University_in_Toruń) in 2012,[^o3][^o6] and his Ph.D. in 2015 from the [Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences](wikipedia://en/67042302), under the supervision of [Mariusz Lemańczyk](wikipedia://en/54594784) and Joanna Kułaga-Przymus.[^o6][^o7] His dissertation was entitled *Własności ergodyczne gładkich potoków na powierzchniach* (*Ergodic properties of smooth flows on surfaces*) and awarded the [International Stefan Banach Prize](wikipedia://en/2121791#International_Stefan_Banach_Prize) in 2016.[^o7][^o8]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After graduating, Kanigowski joined [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) as an S. Chowla Research Assistant Professor in 2015 and then joined [UMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) as an assistant professor in 2018, where he was promoted to full professor in 2024. Since December 2022, Kanigowski has led a flagship project at [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) that partly supports a research collaboration with UMD.[^o6][^o10]

Kanigowski's research interests include dynamical systems and ergodic theory as well as their interaction with [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). In particular, he is interested in [randomness](wikipedia://en/19196523) and [chaos](wikipedia://en/6295) in smooth dynamical systems, classification problems in abstract ergodic theory, and non-standard ergodic theorems that find application in number theory. Together with collaborators, he solved several longstanding open problems and conjuctures, such as the [Rokhlin](wikipedia://en/7703611) problem, the [Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak) hypothesis, the [Katok](wikipedia://en/43275327) hypothesis and the [Ratner](wikipedia://en/12687622) problem.[^o10]

Kanigowski has published more than 30 papers in premier mathematical journals including the most prestigious ones, such as [Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785), [Journal of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), and [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae).[^o11][^o6] Among his collaborators are [Dmitry Dolgopyat](wikipedia://en/47601930), Bassam Fayad, [Giovanni Forni](wikipedia://en/47291762), [Mariusz Lemańczyk](wikipedia://en/54594784), [Maksym Radziwiłł](wikipedia://en/53780399), [Federico Rodriguez Hertz](wikipedia://en/61753917), and [Corinna Ulcigrai](wikipedia://en/46712569).[^o11]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2015, the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791) gave Kanigowski their Prize for Young Mathematicians (he was awarded for a series of six papers in the field of ergodic theory and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675)).[^o13] He was the 2016 winner of the International Stefan Banach Prize for a doctoral dissertation in the mathematical sciences.[^o8] In 2017 he received the Kazimierz Kuratowski Award from the Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences and the Polish Mathematical Society.[^o14] In March 2024, the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) named Kanigowski a 2024 Simons Fellow in Mathematics,[^o15][^o6] in April he received the Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences Prize for outstanding scientific achievements in mathematics for his "fundamental results in the field of dynamical systems and ergodic theory",[^o16][^o6] and in July he was awarded the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) for "his outstanding contributions to the spectral classification and the mixing properties of slowly chaotic dynamical systems".[^o17][^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Adam Kanigowski has two daughters.[^o19] In June 2024 he finished his first [Ironman triathlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ironman_Triathlon).[^o6][^o19]
