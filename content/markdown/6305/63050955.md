# Alexander Furman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Furman"}

**Alexander Furman** (אלכסנדר פורמן) is a mathematician at the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago), Chicago.[^o3][^o4] Furman received his bachelor's degree in mathematics and computer science from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1986,[^o5][^o6] later earning his master's degree and PhD in mathematics in 1989 and 1996, respectively, from the same university.[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Furman started teaching mathematics in 1996 as an [L. E. Dickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._E._Dickson) Instructor of Mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o5] In 1997, he received a position as a postdoctoral fellow at [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University).[^o5] He has worked at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago) since 1997, serving as an assistant professor from 1999 until 2007 and beginning as a full-time professor in 2007.[^o5] Furman also runs the UIC Math Olympiad Project where he works with high school-age students, discussing and working out mathematical problems.[^o8]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Furman has been awarded multiple [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_Grant)[^o5][^o10] and [Binational Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binational_Science_Foundation) grants.[^o5][^o11] In 2014, Furman was made a [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) Fellow in Mathematics[^o12][^o5] and was awarded the [National Science Foundation CAREER award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award) for his work in teaching through research.[^o13][^o10] The same year, he was an invited speaker for the [International Congress of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematics) hosted in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).[^o13][^o14] For his work in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), and [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), he was made an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Fellow in 2016.[^o15] He has additionally received UIC's University Scholar Award, as well as the LAS Distinguished Professor Award.[^o13][^o16][^o17]
