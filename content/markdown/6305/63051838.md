# Shandelle Henson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Shandelle Henson"}

**Shandelle Marie Henson** (born 1964) is an American mathematician and mathematical biologist known for her work in [population dynamics](wikipedia://en/501466). She is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) at [Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrews_University) in [Berrien Springs, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrien_Springs,_Michigan), and the editor-in-chief of the journal *Natural Resource Modeling*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Henson was an undergraduate at Southern College (now [Southern Adventist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Adventist_University)), and a visiting student at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating from Southern College in 1987 with a bachelor's degree in mathematics, *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors)*, as one of the college's five Southern Scholars for that year. She studied [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), earning a master's degree in 1989, and completed a Ph.D. in 1994 at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee). Her dissertation, *Individual-based Physiologically Structured Population and Community Models*, was on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) in population dynamics, and was supervised by Thomas G. Hallam.

After postdoctoral research as Hanno Rund Visiting Assistant Professor at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona), Henson joined the faculty at the [College of William & Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_William_&_Mary) in 1999, and moved to Andrews University in 2001. There, she was promoted to full professor in 2006, chaired the mathematics department from 2011 to 2016, and added a second affiliation as a professor of ecology in the department of biology in 2016.

## Books {#Books}

Henson is the co-author, with J. M. Cushing, R. F. Costantino, Brian Dennis, and [Robert Desharnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Desharnais), of the book *Chaos in Ecology: Experimental Nonlinear Dynamics* (Academic Press, 2003). She is also the author of a biography of [Sam Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Campbell_%28writer%29), titled *Sam Campbell: Philosopher of the Forest* (Three Lakes Historical Society and TEACH Services, 2001).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2007, Southern Adventist University gave Henson their alumnus of the year award.
