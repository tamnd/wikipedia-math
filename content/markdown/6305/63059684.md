# Katrin Tent

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Katrin Tent"}

**Katrin Tent** is a German mathematician specializing in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the symmetries of groups, algebraic [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), and [Tits buildings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tits_buildings). She is a professor of mathematics and [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tent studied mathematics, linguistics, and computer science at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) from 1982 to 1988, obtaining a PhD in linguistics in 1988. After a year as a postdoctoral fellow at the [Western University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario) in Canada, she earned a diploma in mathematics in 1989 from the University of Kiel. She moved to the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in the United States for doctoral study in mathematics, and completed her PhD there in 1994. Her dissertation, *Classifying totally categorical groups (and others)*, was supervised by Steven A. Buechler.

After working as a visiting researcher at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) and then at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), where she completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2001 with the habilitation thesis *Model theory of groups and BN-pairs*, and after a brief stint as a lecturer at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham), she became a professor of mathematics at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) in 2004. She took her present position as a professor of mathematics and mathematical logic at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) in 2008. She was an invited speaker at the ICM in 2026 in Philadelphia. Since 2020, she is President of the [Deutsche Vereinigung für mathematische Logik und für Grundlagenforschung der exakten Wissenschaften](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Vereinigung_für_mathematische_Logik_und_für_Grundlagenforschung_der_exakten_Wissenschaften).

## Books {#Books}

With Martin Ziegler, Tent is the co-author of a book on [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), *A Course in Model Theory* (Lecture Notes in Logic 40, [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), 2012). She is also the editor of *Tits Buildings and the Model Theory of Groups* ([London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) Lecture Note Series, Vol. 291, Cambridge University Press, 2002) and of *Groups and Analysis: The Legacy of [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544)* (London Mathematical Society Lecture Notes Series, Vol. 354, Cambridge University Press, 2008), and co-editor of *Lectures in Model Theory* (with Franziska Jahnke and Daniel Palacín, Münster Lectures in Mathematics, [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470), 2018).
