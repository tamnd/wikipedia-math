# János Körner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="János Körner"}

**János Körner** is a Hungarian mathematician who works on [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o2]

:::

Körner studied Mathematics at the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) in Budapest with a degree in 1970[^o4] and was then at the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) until 1992. From 1981 to 1983 he was at the [Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs) and in 1987–88 at [Télécom Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Télécom_Paris) (ENST) in Paris.[^o4] He has been a professor at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) since 1993.[^o2]

Over his career, he frequently collaborated with fellow information theorists such as [Rudolf Ahlswede](wikipedia://en/18251788), [Katalin Marton](wikipedia://en/43989003), and [Imre Csiszár](wikipedia://en/13008077). Together with Rudolf Ahlswede and Peter Gács he proved the blowing-up lemma.[^o3] Besides information theory, he also works on [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568).

In 2014 he received the [Claude E. Shannon Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_E._Shannon_Award).[^o4] He served as Associated editor of the [IEEE Transactions on Information Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Transactions_on_Information_Theory) on multiple occasions. He is a member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences.[^o2]

## Books {#Books}

- With Imre Csiszár: Information Theory: Coding Theorems for Discrete Memoryless Systems, Academic Press 1981, 2nd edition Cambridge University Press 2011.
