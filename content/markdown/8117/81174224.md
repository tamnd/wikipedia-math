# American Statistical Association Founders Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="American Statistical Association Founders Award"}

The **American Statistical Association Founders Award** is presented by the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) to members who have rendered distinguished service to the association over a long period of time—often in a variety of leadership roles. Members who have made substantial contributions to the association's objectives and whose influence has lasted beyond their immediate tenure are eligible for the Founders Award. Along with [fellowships](wikipedia://en/51140705), the Founders Award is one of the highest honors presented by the ASA.

:::
