# Straight face test

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Straight face test"}

The **straight face test** (also **laugh test** or **giggle test**) is a test of whether something is legitimate or serious based on whether a given statement or legal argument can be made sincerely, without any compulsion to [laugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laugh).[^o2] The phrase goes back to about 1987.[^o3]

:::
