# Discrete Applied Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrete Applied Mathematics"}

***Discrete Applied Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [algorithmic](wikipedia://en/775) and [applied](wikipedia://en/24295969) areas of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492). It is published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) and the editor-in-chief is [Endre Boros](wikipedia://en/37515463) ([Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University)). The journal was split off from another Elsevier journal, *[Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21988115)*, in 1979,[^o3] with that journal's founder [Peter Ladislaw Hammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Ladislaw_Hammer) as its founding editor-in-chief.[^o4]

:::
