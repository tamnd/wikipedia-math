# Martin Hairer

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Hairer"}

**Sir Martin Hairer** [KBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire) [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (born 14 November 1975) is an Austrian-British mathematician and mathematical physicist working in the field of [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis), in particular [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239). He is Professor of Mathematics at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) and at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London). He previously held appointments at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) and the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o17][^o6][^o8] In 2014 he was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859),[^o9] one of the highest honours a mathematician can achieve.[^o10] In 2020 he won the 2021 [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).[^o11]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hairer was born in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland.[^o5] He attended the Collège Claparède [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) where he received his high school diploma in 1994. He entered a school science competition with sound editing software that was developed into Amadeus,[^o13] and later continued to maintain the software in addition to his academic work; it continued to be widely used .[^o11] He then attended the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), where he obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematics in July 1998, Master of Science in Physics in October 1998 and PhD in Physics under the supervision of [Jean-Pierre Eckmann](wikipedia://en/31446069) in November 2001.[^o2][^o26]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Hairer is active in the field of [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239) in particular, and in stochastic analysis and stochastic dynamics in general.[^o46] He has worked on variants of [Hörmander's theorem](wikipedia://en/13659915), systematisation of the construction of [Lyapunov functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyapunov_functions) for stochastic systems, development of a general theory of [ergodicity](wikipedia://en/5456824) for non-[Markovian systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), [multiscale analysis techniques](wikipedia://en/23923990), [theory of homogenisation](wikipedia://en/23852047), [theory of path sampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_path_sampling) and theory of [rough paths](wikipedia://en/38309455)[^o46] and, in 2014, on his theory of [regularity structures](wikipedia://en/51170495).[^o15]

Under the name HairerSoft, he develops [Macintosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macintosh) software.[^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Hairer holds Austrian and British nationality, and speaks French, German and English; he married fellow mathematician Li Xue-Mei in 2003.[^o5][^o61] His father is [Ernst Hairer](wikipedia://en/31446016), a mathematician at the University of Geneva.[^o62]
