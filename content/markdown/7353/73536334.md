# Lutfullah Muhandis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lutfullah Muhandis"}

**Lutfullah Muhandis** was a 17th century [Mughal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire)-era [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was the second son of the architect [Ustad Ahmad Lahori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ustad_Ahmad_Lahori) and brother of [Ataullah Rashidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ataullah_Rashidi). His book *Diwan-e-Muhandis* discusses the artistic and architectural achievements of his father.[^o2] He was also a well-known mathematician, with a practical treatise on geometrical shapes in his credit.[^o4][^o5] He was also involved in [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture) and completed projects in Mandu and Peshwar as well as several commissions for [Dara Shikoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_Shikoh).[^o6]
