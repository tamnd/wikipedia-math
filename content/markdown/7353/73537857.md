# Jon A. Wellner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jon A. Wellner"}

**Jon August Wellner** (born August 17, 1945) is an American statistician known for his contributions to the fields of statistical inference, [empirical process theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_process_theory), and [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wellner was born in [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon), and grew up in various cities in the US. His father was United States Forest Service scientist [Charles Wellner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Wellner). He attended [Ogden High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogden_High_School) in Ogden, Utah and graduated in 1963.[^o5] He went on to attend the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho) in [Moscow, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Idaho), where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematics and Physics in 1968. After a brief stint in graduate school at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1969–1970 and serving in the [U.S. Army Signal Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Army_Signal_Corps) from 1969 to 1971, he returned to graduate study in statistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and received his Ph.D. in 1975 with [Galen Shorack](wikipedia://en/74602388) as his advisor.[^o6][^o7]

Wellner worked as an assistant professor and associate professor of statistics at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) from 1975 to 1983, after which he moved back to the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington) and became a professor of statistics. He remained at the University of Washington for the rest of his career, retiring in 2020.[^o8]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Wellner was elected a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS) in 1983, a [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 1994, and a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) in 2006; he became an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2010. He was the Noether Distinguished Scholar of the ASA in 2011.[^o10] He gave the Le Cam lecture of the IMS in 2015.[^o11] He served as the executive editor of *[Statistical Science](wikipedia://en/15936018)*. Wellner was the president of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics between 2016 and 2017. In 2010, he was made a [Knight of the Order of the Netherlands Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_of_the_Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion).[^o6][^o7] A conference in honor of Wellner's 65th birthday was held in July of 2010.[^o3]
