# Book on the Measurement of Plane and Spherical Figures

*Image: F. 122r from Kitāb al-mutawassiṭāt.jpg.* A page of the *Book on the Measurement of Plane and Spherical Figures*, [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), New York

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Book on the Measurement of Plane and Spherical Figures"}

The ***Book on the Measurement of Plane and Spherical Figures*** (كتاب معرفة مساحة الأشكال البسيطة والكريّة, ar) was the most important of the works produced by the [Banū Mūsā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banū_Mūsā) (three 9th century Persian brothers who worked in Baghdad).[^n1] A [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) translation by the 12th century Italian [astrologer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrologer) [Gerard of Cremona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_of_Cremona) was made, entitled Liber trium fratrum de geometria and Verba filiorum Moysi filii Sekir. The original work in Arabic was edited by the Persian [polymath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath) [Naṣīr al-Dīn al-Ṭūsī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naṣīr_al-Dīn_al-Ṭūsī) in the 13th century.[^o3] The original work in Arabic is not extant, but its contents are known from later translations.[^n2]

:::

The treatise, which is about [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), was similar to two books by [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844), *On the measurement of the circle* and *On the sphere and the cylinder*.[^o3] It was used extensively in the Middle Ages, and was quoted by authors such as [Thābit ibn Qurra](wikipedia://en/207547), [Ibn al-Haytham](wikipedia://en/1645), [Leonardo Fibonacci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_Fibonacci) (in his Practica geometriae), [Jordanus de Nemore](wikipedia://en/4763255), and [Roger Bacon](wikipedia://en/25879).[^n3] It deals with the geometrical concepts of [area](wikipedia://en/1209) and [volume](wikipedia://en/32498), [angle trisection](wikipedia://en/91111), [construction](wikipedia://en/61229), and [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673).[^n4] It includes [theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theorems) not known to the Greeks.[^n5]

The book was re-published in Latin with an English translation by the American historian [Marshall Clagett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Clagett), who has also summarized how the work influenced [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) during the [Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages).[^n6]
