# Stephen Schanuel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Schanuel"}

**Stephen H. Schanuel** (14 July 1933 – 21 July 2014) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the fields of [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory).[^o3][^o5]

:::

## Life {#Life}

While he was a graduate student at [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), he discovered [Schanuel's lemma](wikipedia://en/15583411), an essential lemma in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410).[^o5] Schanuel received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1963, under the supervision of [Serge Lang](wikipedia://en/872991).[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Shortly thereafter he stated [a conjecture](wikipedia://en/2031353) in the field of [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698), which remains an important open problem to this day.[^o5] Schanuel was a professor emeritus of mathematics at [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo,_The_State_University_of_New_York).[^o3]
