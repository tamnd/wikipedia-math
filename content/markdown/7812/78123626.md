# Peter Todd (programmer)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Todd"}

**Peter Todd** is a Canadian software developer who was an early contributor to the development of [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265).[^o3] Todd has been involved in [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188) since the early 2010s and has worked on blockchain and privacy-focused projects.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Peter Todd was born in Canada. When he was 15 years old he began communicating with [cypherpunks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cypherpunks) like [Adam Back](wikipedia://en/10348688) and [Hal Finney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hal_Finney_%28computer_scientist%29).[^o6] Before his career as a core developer, Peter Todd attended the [Ontario College of Art and Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_College_of_Art_and_Design) in Toronto.[^o6]

In 2019, Todd filed a [defamation suit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_defamation_law) against former [Tor](wikipedia://en/20556944) developer Isis Lovecruft after they publicly accused him of rape and sexual misconduct on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter).[^o7]

## Cryptocurrency involvement {#Cryptocurrency_involvement}

In 2014 Todd stated that [Gavin Andresen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavin_Andresen) had been responsible for code changes that resulted in Bitcoin's rising fees.[^o9] In 2017 Todd was against an increase in the Bitcoin block size in relation to the controversy surrounding the [Bitcoin scalability problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcoin_scalability_problem) and [SegWit](wikipedia://en/54733250).[^o10]

The 2024 [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO) documentary *[Money Electric: The Bitcoin Mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_Electric:_The_Bitcoin_Mystery)* by [Cullen Hoback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cullen_Hoback) named Todd as [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265)'s pseudonymous creator, [Satoshi Nakamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto). Todd has publicly denied being Nakamoto, stating in the film that it was "ludicrous".[^o6][^o3] Hoback's theory includes a range of circumstantial evidence.[^o11][^o12] In the film, Todd denied that he was Nakamoto, saying that it was "ludicrous" and "grasping at straws". Todd criticized Hoback for "[QAnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QAnon) style coincidence-based conspiracy thinking".[^o13][^o12] No concrete evidence has been presented to support the claim, and the theory remains entirely speculative.
