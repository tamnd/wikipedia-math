# Nonabelian cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nonabelian cohomology"}

In mathematics, a **nonabelian cohomology** is any [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) with coefficients in a [nonabelian group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonabelian_group), a [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of nonabelian groups or even in a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450).

:::

If [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) is thought of as the [abelianization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelianization) of [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) (cf. [Hurewicz theorem](wikipedia://en/591587)), then the nonabelian cohomology may be thought of as a dual of [homotopy groups](wikipedia://en/297506).
