# Cocycle category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cocycle category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **cocycle category** of objects *X*, *Y* in a [model category](wikipedia://en/2895304) is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) in which the objects are pairs of maps $X \overset{f}\leftarrow Z \overset{g}\rightarrow Y$ and the [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are obvious [commutative diagrams](wikipedia://en/138484) between them.[^o2] It is denoted by $H(X, Y)$. (It may also be defined using the language of [2-categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicategory).)

:::

One has that if the model category is right proper and is such that [weak equivalences](wikipedia://en/379051) are closed under finite products, then

$$\pi_0 H(X, Y) \to [X, Y], \quad (f, g) \mapsto g \circ f^{-1}$$

is [bijective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijective).
