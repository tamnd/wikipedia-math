# Svetlana Katok

*Image: Svetlana Katok (2009).jpg.* At [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2009

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Svetlana Katok"}

**Svetlana Katok** (born May 1, 1947)[^o2] is a Russian-American mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Katok grew up in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), and earned a master's degree from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1969; however, due to the antisemitic and anti-intelligentsia policies of the time, she was denied admission to the doctoral program there and instead worked for several years in the area of early and secondary mathematical education.[^o3] She immigrated to the US in 1978,[^o3] and earned her doctorate from the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) in 1983 under the supervision of [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557).[^o3] She joined the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) faculty in 1990.[^o3]

Katok founded the Electronic Research Announcements of the American Mathematical Society in 1995; it was renamed in 2007 to the Electronic Research Announcements in Mathematical Sciences, and she remains its managing editor.[^o6]

Katok was an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Council member at large.[^o7]

## Books {#Books}

Katok is the author of:

- *Fuchsian Groups*, Chicago Lectures in Mathematics, [University of Chicago Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Press), 1992.[^o9] Russian edition, [Faktorial Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faktorial_Press), Moscow, 2002.
- *p-adic Analysis Compared with Real*, Student Mathematical Library, vol. 37, American Math. Soc., 2007.[^o10] Russian edition, MCCME Press, Moscow, 2004.

Additionally, she coedited the book *MASS Selecta: Teaching and learning advanced undergraduate mathematics* (American Math. Soc., 2003).[^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Katok was the 2004 [Emmy Noether Lecturer](wikipedia://en/11566873) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o3] In 2012 she and her husband, mathematician [Anatole Katok](wikipedia://en/43275327), both became [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]
