# Julia F. Knight

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia F. Knight"}

*Image: Knight julia.jpg.* Julia F. Knight, 2012

**Julia Frandsen Knight** is an American mathematician, specializing in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) and [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414).[^o2] She is the Charles L. Huisking Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) and director of the graduate program in mathematics there.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Knight did her undergraduate studies at [Utah State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_University), graduating in 1964, and earned her Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1972 under the supervision of [Robert Lawson Vaught](wikipedia://en/3550212).[^o2]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2012, she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o7] and she was elected to be the 30th president of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438).[^o8] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for Fall 2020.[^o9]

In 2014, Knight held the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431), titled *Computable structure theory and formulas of special forms.*
