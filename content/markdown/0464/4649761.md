# Iterated monodromy group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Iterated monodromy group"}

In [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) the **iterated monodromy group** of a [covering map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_map) is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) describing the [monodromy action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monodromy_action) of the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) on all [iterations](wikipedia://en/68833) of the covering. A single covering map between spaces is therefore used to create a tower of coverings, by placing the covering over itself repeatedly. In terms of the [Galois theory of covering spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_theory_of_covering_spaces), this construction on spaces is expected to correspond to a construction on groups. The iterated monodromy group provides this construction, and it is applied to encode the combinatorics and [symbolic dynamics](wikipedia://en/1986263) of the covering, and provide examples of [self-similar groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-similar_group).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The **iterated monodromy group** of *f* is the following [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526):

$$\mathrm{IMG}f := \frac{\pi_1 (X, t)}{\bigcap_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathrm{Ker}\,\digamma^n}$$

where :

- $f:X_1\rightarrow X$ is a [covering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_map) of a [path-connected](wikipedia://en/6233#Path_connectedness) and [locally path-connected](wikipedia://en/6233#Local_connectedness) topological space *X* by its subset $X_1$,
- $\pi_1 (X, t)$ is the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) of *X* and
- $\digamma :\pi_1 (X, t)\rightarrow \mathrm{Sym}\,f^{-1}(t)$ is the [monodromy action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monodromy_group) for *f*.
- $\digamma^n:\pi_1 (X, t)\rightarrow \mathrm{Sym}\,f^{-n}(t)$ is the monodromy action of the $n^\mathrm{th}$ iteration of *f*, $\forall n\in\mathbb{N}_0$.

:::

## Action {#Action}

The iterated monodromy group acts by [automorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphisms) on the [rooted tree](wikipedia://en/48560) of preimages

$$T_f := \bigsqcup_{n\ge 0}f^{-n}(t),$$

where a vertex $z\in f^{-n}(t)$ is connected by an edge with $f(z)\in f^{-(n-1)}(t)$.

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Iterated monodromy groups of rational functions {#Iterated_monodromy_groups_of_rational_functions}

Let :

- *f* be a complex [rational function](wikipedia://en/361210)
- $P_f$ be the union of [forward orbits](wikipedia://en/252250) of its [critical points](wikipedia://en/1951424) (the [post-critical set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-critical_set)).

If $P_f$ is finite (or has a finite set of [accumulation points](wikipedia://en/226505)), then the iterated monodromy group of *f* is the iterated monodromy group of the covering $f:\hat C\setminus f^{-1}(P_f)\rightarrow \hat C\setminus P_f$, where $\hat C$ is the [Riemann sphere](wikipedia://en/30876799).

Iterated monodromy groups of rational functions usually have exotic properties from the point of view of classical group theory. Most of them are infinitely presented, many have [intermediate growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_growth).

#### IMG of polynomials {#IMG_of_polynomials}

The [Basilica group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_group) is the iterated monodromy group of the polynomial $z^2 - 1$
