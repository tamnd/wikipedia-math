# Diminished rhombic dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diminished rhombic dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **diminished rhombic dodecahedron** is a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631) with one or more vertices [removed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishment_%28geometry%29). This article describes diminishing one 4-valence vertex. This diminishment creates one new square face while 4 rhombic faces are reduced to triangles. It has 13 vertices, 24 edges, and 13 faces. It has *C*<sub>4v</sub> symmetry, order 8.

:::

Like the rhombic dodecahedron, the long diagonal of each rhombic face is [$\sqrt{2}$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_root_of_two) times the length of the short diagonal, so that the [acute](wikipedia://en/1196#Types_of_angles) angles on each face measure arccos($\tfrac{1}{3}$), or approximately 70.53°.

## Self-dual {#Self-dual}

Like the dihedral symmetry [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045), and [elongated pyramids](wikipedia://en/36061703), it is [self-dual](wikipedia://en/8815), with the dual geometry inverted across the [axis of symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_of_symmetry). It is one of three self-dual [tridecahedra](wikipedia://en/40116384) with *C*<sub>4v</sub> symmetry.[^o4]

## Space-filling {#Space-filling}

This polyhedron along with the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) is [space-filling](wikipedia://en/6008654), like the [rhombic dodecahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_dodecahedral_honeycomb). Six diminished points come together to form cubic holes.

## Cartesian coordinate {#Cartesian_coordinate}

It has 13 of 14 [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631) are:

8: (±1, ±1, ±1)

1: (2, 0, 0)

2: (0, ±2, 0)

2: (0, 0, ±2)

## Augmented cuboctahedron {#Augmented_cuboctahedron}

The same topological polyhedron with different proportions can be constructed as an **augmented [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280)**, with a square face [augmented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29) by a [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455). This construction requires merging of neighboring coparallel triangular faces into new 60° rhombic faces. This can also be seen as an [asymmetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymmetry) [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of a cuboctahedron. It retains 5 [square](wikipedia://en/659939) faces, and 4 [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285) faces of the cuboctahedron.

The 13 Cartesian coordinate can be positioned as:

1: ( 2, 0, 0)

4: (±1, ±1, 0)

4: (±1, 0, ±1)

4: ( 0, ±1, ±1)

Augmenting two opposite squares will create a [dihedral rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631#Dihedral_rhombic_dodecahedron), doubling the symmetry to *D*<sub>4h</sub> symmetry, order 16. Augmenting pyramids on all six square faces, with merged faces will produce a regular [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), restoring full octahedral symmetry, order 48.
