# 2004 Syrian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2004 Syrian census"}

A [population census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_census) was conducted in [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria) in September [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004), from which the total population was measured as having reached 17.92 million.[^o3] This is the last comprehensive and official census in the country. It was carried out by the Central Bureau of Statistics. It was supposed to conduct a census in 2014, but because of circumstances and sanctions, that did not occur, according to the former director of the Central Bureau of Statistics, Adnan Hmidan.[^o3][^o4]

:::

On 8 November 2004, the Director of the Central Bureau of Statistics, Ibrahim Ali, announced the preliminary results.[^o5]
