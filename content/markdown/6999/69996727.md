# Aretha Teckentrup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aretha Teckentrup"}

**Aretha Leonore Teckentrup** is a UK-based mathematician, known for her research on [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). Her work focuses on [multilevel Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/55634671) for the [numerical solution of partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1634583), [Gaussian processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_processes) and [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571). She is a reader in the mathematics of data science at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Teckentrup was a student in mathematics at the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) beginning in 2005. She earned a master's degree there in 2009, and completed her PhD in 2013. Her dissertation, *Multilevel Monte Carlo methods and Uncertainty Quantification*, was supervised by Robert Scheichl.

After postdoctoral research with [Max Gunzburger](wikipedia://en/10917044) at [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) from 2013 to 2014 and with [Andrew M. Stuart](wikipedia://en/24247900) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) from 2014 to 2016, she became a lecturer at the University of Edinburgh in 2016.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Teckentrup was a second-place winner of the [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214) in 2017. In 2018 she became the inaugural winner of the SIAG/Uncertainty Quantification Early Career Prize of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Uncertainty Quantification. She was one of the 2021 winners of the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).
