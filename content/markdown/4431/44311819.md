# Ore algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ore algebra"}

In [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805), an **Ore algebra** is a special kind of iterated [Ore extension](wikipedia://en/3182658) that can be used to represent linear functional operators, including linear differential and/or recurrence operators.[^o2] The concept is named after [Øystein Ore](wikipedia://en/1643733).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $K$ be a (commutative) field and $A = K[x_1, \ldots, x_s]$ be a commutative polynomial ring (with $A = K$ when $s = 0$). The iterated [skew polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/3182658) $A[\partial_1; \sigma_1, \delta_1] \cdots [\partial_r; \sigma_r, \delta_r]$ is called an **Ore algebra** when the $\sigma_i$ and $\delta_j$ commute for $i \neq j$, and satisfy $\sigma_i(\partial_j) = \partial_j$, $\delta_i(\partial_j) = 0$ for $i > j$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Ore algebras satisfy the [Ore condition](wikipedia://en/3100560), and thus can be embedded in a (skew) field of fractions.

The constraint of commutation in the definition makes Ore algebras have a non-commutative generalization theory of [Gröbner basis](wikipedia://en/357328) for their left ideals.

:::
