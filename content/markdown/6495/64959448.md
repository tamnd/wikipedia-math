# Mark Tomforde

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mark Tomforde"}

**Mark Louis Tomforde** is an American mathematician who works on [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184), topics in the broader area of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). He held academic positions at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) and at the [University of Colorado Colorado Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Colorado_Springs).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Tomforde earned his Ph.D. in mathematics at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) in 2002.

He was an invited speaker at the 2015 Abel Symposium,[^o5] and he is a founding member of the Algebras and Rings in Colorado Springs (ARCS) center.[^o6] Tomforde has researched the related areas of [graph C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/46697691) and [Leavitt path algebras](wikipedia://en/64956453). With Doug Drinen, he is co-creator of the [Drinen–Tomforde desingularization](wikipedia://en/46697691#Desingularization), often simply called desingularization.[^o7] With [Gene Abrams](wikipedia://en/61405069), Tomforde has formulated the Abrams–Tomforde conjectures, and Abrams and Tomforde have verified certain special cases of the conjectures.[^o8] Tomforde studied [ultragraph C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/64958837).[^o9] Tomforde has also been active in organizing several conferences on graph C\*-algebras and their generalizations, with the goal of bringing together analysts and algebraists to share ideas and collaborate.[^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14]

Tomforde created and directed the [Cougars and Houston Area Mathematics Program (CHAMP)](wikipedia://en/64958982),[^o15] which was last active in Spring 2019. CHAMP had previously received an award from [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa)[^o16] and the Award for Mathematics Programs that Make a Difference from the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o17][^o18] In 2019 Tomforde was recognized with the MAA Texas Section's Distinguished College and University Teaching of Mathematics Award. In 2020 he was a recipient of a [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics) from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o19]
