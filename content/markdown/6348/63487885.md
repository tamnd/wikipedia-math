# Jeanette Shakalli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jeanette Shakalli"}

**Jeanette Shakalli** is a [Panamanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panamanians) mathematician best known for her outreach activities in the Republic of Panama. She is currently Executive Director of the Panamanian Foundation for the Promotion of Mathematics (FUNDAPROMAT) a private non-profit foundation established to promote the study of mathematics in Panama.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Shakalli earned a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Chemistry from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).[^o6] In 2012, she received a Ph.D. in Mathematics from [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) under the advisement of [Sarah J. Witherspoon](wikipedia://en/55710076).[^o7][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

From 2012 to 2019 Shakalli worked for [National Secretariat of Science, Technology, and Innovation (SENACYT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretaria_Nacional_de_Ciencia_y_Tecnologia) of Panama.[^o10] In 2016, she created the Program on Math Outreach in Panama which holds several activities a year on [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446).[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14] Since then she has organized over 50 mathematical outreach events in Panama.[^o15]

She is chair of the Program Committee for [Mathematics Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s SIGMAA on Recreational Mathematics program. In 2017, she became the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868)'s (IMU) Committee for Women in Mathematics (CWM) Ambassador for Panama.[^o16][^o17] She is on the board of directors of both the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) Panama Section as secretary[^o18] and the Panamanian Association for the Advancement of Science (APANAC) as admissions director.[^o19]

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2007, Shakalli was awarded the Senior GE Prize for Mathematics Majors at the University of Notre Dame which is given to one who excelled in mathematics in their senior year.[^o21] Shakalli was recognized as [Rotarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_International) of the Year for 2018-2019 by the Rotary Club of Panama.[^o22]
