::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 8-simplexes"}

In eight-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 8-simplex** is a convex [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex).

:::

There are unique 3 degrees of rectifications in regular 8-polytopes. Vertices of the rectified 8-simplex are located at the edge-centers of the 8-simplex. Vertices of the birectified 8-simplex are located in the triangular face centers of the 8-simplex. Vertices of the trirectified 8-simplex are located in the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cell centers of the 8-simplex.

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as S1. It is also called **0<sub>6,1</sub>** for its branching Coxeter-Dynkin diagram, shown as . Acronym: rene (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

The rectified 8-simplex is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [9-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9-demicube), and the edge figure of the uniform [2<sub>61</sub> honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_61_honeycomb).

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of the *rectified 8-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 9-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1,1). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [rectified 9-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_9-orthoplex).
