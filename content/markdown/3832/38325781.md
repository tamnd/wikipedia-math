# S-equivalence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="S-equivalence"}

**S-equivalence** is an [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259) on the families of [semistable](wikipedia://en/18909129) [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) on an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *X* be a [projective curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_curve) over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) *k*. A vector bundle on *X* can be considered as a locally free [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466). Every semistable locally free *E* on *X* admits a Jordan-Hölder [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) with stable [subquotients](wikipedia://en/1639294), i.e.

$$0 = E_0 \subseteq E_1 \subseteq \ldots \subseteq E_n = E$$

where $E_i$ are locally free sheaves on *X* and $E_i/E_{i-1}$ are stable. Although the Jordan-Hölder filtration is not unique, the subquotients are, which means that $gr E = \bigoplus_i E_i/E_{i-1}$ is unique up to isomorphism.

Two semistable locally free sheaves *E* and *F* on *X* are *S*-equivalent if *gr E* ≅ *gr F*.

:::
