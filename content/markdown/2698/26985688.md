# Yan Soibelman

*Image: Yan Soibelman, 2006 (cropped).JPG.* Yan Soibelman 2006

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yan Soibelman"}

**Iakov (Yan) Soibelman** (Russian: Яков Семенович Сойбельман) born 15 April 1956 ([Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR)) is a [Russian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University) (Manhattan, USA), member of the [Kyiv Mathematical Society](http://www.mathsociety.kiev.ua/) ([Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)), founder of Manhattan Mathematical Olympiad.

:::

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

Yan Soibelman is a specialist in theory of [quantum groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_groups), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298). He introduced the notion of quantum Weyl group, studied representation theory of the algebras of functions on compact quantum groups, and meromorphic [braided monoidal categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braided_monoidal_categories). His long term collaboration with [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509) is devoted to various aspects of [homological mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/2164886), a proof of [Deligne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deligne) conjecture about operations on the cohomological Hochschild complex, a direct construction of Calabi-Yau varieties based on [SYZ conjecture](wikipedia://en/17511256) and non-archimedean geometry, and more recently to [Donaldson-Thomas theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donaldson-Thomas_theory). Together with Kontsevich he laid the foundation and developed the theory of motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants. Kontsevich-Soibelman [wall-crossing](wikipedia://en/24270717) formula for Donaldson-Thomas invariants (a.k.a BPS invariants) found important applications in physics.[^o3] They also introduced the notion of Cohomological Hall algebra which has numerous applications in geometric representation theory and quantum physics.[^o4]
