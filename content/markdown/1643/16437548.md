# Zero mode

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zero mode"}

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), a **zero mode** is an [eigenvector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvector) with a vanishing [eigenvalue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue).[^o2]

:::

In various subfields of physics zero modes appear whenever a physical system possesses a certain symmetry. For example, [normal modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_modes) of multidimensional [harmonic oscillator](wikipedia://en/13899) (e.g. a system of beads arranged around the circle, connected with springs) corresponds to elementary vibrational modes of the system. In such a system zero modes typically occur and are related with a rigid rotation around the circle.

The [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) of an operator consists of left zero modes, and the [cokernel](wikipedia://en/279715) consists of the right zero modes.
