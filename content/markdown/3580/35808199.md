# Canonizant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Canonizant"}

In mathematical [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), the **canonizant** or **canonisant** is a covariant of forms related to a canonical form for them.

:::

## Canonizants of a binary form {#Canonizants_of_a_binary_form}

The canonizant of a binary form of degree 2*n* – 1 is a covariant of degree *n* and order *n*, given by the [catalecticant](wikipedia://en/30084691) of the penultimate emanant, which is the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of the *n* by *n* [Hankel matrix](wikipedia://en/377537) with entries *a*<sub>*i*+*j*</sub>*x* + *a*<sub>*i*+*j*+1</sub>*y* for 0 ≤ *i*,*j* \< *n*.
