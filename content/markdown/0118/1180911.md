# Arend Heyting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arend Heyting"}

**Arend Heyting** (; 9 May 1898 – 9 July 1980) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Heyting was a student of [Luitzen Egbertus Jan Brouwer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luitzen_Egbertus_Jan_Brouwer) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), and did much to put [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262) on a footing where it could become part of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636). Heyting gave the first formal development of intuitionistic logic in order to codify Brouwer's way of doing mathematics. The inclusion of Brouwer's name in the [Brouwer–Heyting–Kolmogorov interpretation](wikipedia://en/1925846) is largely honorific, as Brouwer was opposed in principle to the formalisation of certain intuitionistic principles (and went as far as calling Heyting's work a "sterile exercise").[^n1]

In 1942 he became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^n2]

Heyting was born in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), Netherlands, and died in [Lugano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano), Switzerland.
