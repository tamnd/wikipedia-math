# Jacques Charles François Sturm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Charles François Sturm"}

**Jacques Charles François Sturm** (29 September 1803 – 15 December 1855) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who made a significant addition to equation theory with his work, [Sturm's theorem](wikipedia://en/1221919).[^o3]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Sturm was born in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland in 1803. The family of his father, Jean-Henri Sturm, had emigrated from [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg) around 1760—about 50 years before Charles-François's birth. His mother's name was Jeanne-Louise-Henriette Gremay.

In 1818, he started to follow the lectures of the [academy of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva). The death of his father forced Sturm to give lessons to children of the rich in order to support his own family the following year. In 1823, he became tutor to the son of [Madame de Staël](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_de_Staël).

At the end of that year, Sturm stayed in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) for a short time following the family of his student. He resolved, with his school-fellow [Jean-Daniel Colladon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Daniel_Colladon), to try his fortune in Paris, and obtained employment on the *Bulletin universel*.

## Discovery {#Discovery}

In 1829, he discovered the [theorem](wikipedia://en/1221919) that bears his name, and concerns [real-root isolation](wikipedia://en/44012866), that is the determination of the number and the localization of the real [roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_polynomial) of a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000).[^o8]

## Work {#Work}

Sturm benefited from the 1830 revolution, as his Protestant faith ceased to be an obstacle to employment in public high schools. At the end of 1830, he was thus appointed as a professor of Mathématiques Spéciales at the [collège Rollin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_Rollin).

He was chosen a member of the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences) in 1836, filling the seat of [André-Marie Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André-Marie_Ampère). Sturm became *répétiteur* in 1838, and in 1840 professor in the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). The same year, after the death of [Poisson](wikipedia://en/44670), Sturm was appointed as [mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) professor of the Faculté des sciences de Paris. His works, *Cours d'analyse de l'école polytechnique* (1857–1863) and *Cours de mécanique de l'école polytechnique* (1861), were published after his death in Paris,[^o6] and were regularly republished.

He was the co-[eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym) of the [Sturm–Liouville theory](wikipedia://en/490990) with [Joseph Liouville](wikipedia://en/341810).

In 1826, with his colleague [Jean-Daniel Colladon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Daniel_Colladon), Sturm helped make the first experimental determination of the [speed of sound](wikipedia://en/147853) in water.[^o5]

## Death and legacy {#Death_and_legacy}

In 1851 his health began to fail. He was able to return to teaching for a while during his long illness, but died in 1855.[^o5]

The asteroid [31043 Sturm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/31043_Sturm) is named for him.[^o11] Sturm's name is one of [the 72 names](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_72_names_on_the_Eiffel_Tower) engraved at the [Eiffel Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower).

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

- Grand prix de Mathématiques (4 December 1834)
- Member of the academy of Berlin (1835)
- Member of the academy of Saint-Petersburg (1836)
- Officier de la [Légion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Légion_d'Honneur) (1837)
- [Copley Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copley_Medal) of the [Royal Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_London) (1840)
- Member of the [Royal Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) (1840)
