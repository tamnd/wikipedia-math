# Herman Chernoff

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Herman Chernoff"}

**Herman Chernoff** (July 1, 1923 – July 6, 2026) was an American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969), [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and physicist. He was a professor at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), [Stanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Herman Chernoff's parents were Pauline and Max Chernoff, Jewish immigrants from the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). He studied at [Townsend Harris High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townsend_Harris_High_School)[^o5] and earned a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York) in 1943.[^o6] He attended graduate school at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), earning an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) in 1945, and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in applied mathematics in 1948 under the supervision of [Abraham Wald](wikipedia://en/1239430).[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Chernoff became a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1974,[^o9] and was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1980.[^o10] In 1987, he was selected for the [Wilks Memorial Award](wikipedia://en/15966180) by the American Statistical Association,[^o11] and in 2012, he was made an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Chernoff met his future wife, Judith, when they were both graduate students at Brown University in 1945, and married her in 1947.[^o14] She died at the age of 98 on June 9, 2023.[^o15] At the time of her death they were believed to be the oldest couple living in Massachusetts.[^o16]

Chernoff [turned 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian) on July 1, 2023,[^o17] and died in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), Massachusetts, on July 6, 2026, at the age of 103.[^o18]
