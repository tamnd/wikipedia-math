# Anuška Ferligoj

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anuška Ferligoj"}

**Anuška Ferligoj** is a [Slovenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), born August 19, 1947, in [Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana), [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia), whose specialty is [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [network analysis](wikipedia://en/325726). Her specific interests include [multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) (theory and application in social sciences, medicine, etc.), [cluster analysis](wikipedia://en/669675) (constraints, multi-criteria clustering), [social network analysis](wikipedia://en/325726) (blockmodeling, reliability and [validity](wikipedia://en/239140) of network measurement), methodological research of [public opinion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_opinion), analysis of scientific networks. She is a professor emeritus at the [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana), [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ferligoj obtained her B.S. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) in 1971, and her M.S. in [operational research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_research) in 1979 from the [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana). In 1983 she obtained her Ph.D. in [information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_science) from the same university under the supervision of Professor Branislav Ivanović.[^o6][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Since 1972, Ferligoj was employed as [assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_assistant), [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor), [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) and finally from 1994 as full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the Faculty of Social Sciences.[^o8] In 2003–2005, she was the dean of the [Faculty of Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_of_Social_Sciences,_Ljubljana).[^o9] In 1992–2012, she was the head of the Centre for Methodology and Informatics at the Institute of Social Sciences.[^o10] In 2002–2013, she headed the graduate program on statistics, and in 2012–2020 the master program on applied [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685).[^o11] She has been a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) there since 2020.[^o3]

She was a [Fulbright scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_scholar) in 1990—1991[^o12] and visiting professor at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) (1996) and at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) (2009/10).[^o6] She was awarded the title of [Ambassador of Science of the Republic of Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassador_of_Science_of_the_Republic_of_Slovenia) in 1997.[^o13]

## Academic service {#Academic_service}

Ferligoj chaired the Statistical Society of Slovenia in 1982 – 1983.[^o15] She was the president of the Statistical Council of the Republic of Slovenia (2007–2021)[^o16] and a member of the European Statistical Advisory Committee (ESAC) (2011–2015).[^o17]

She was founding editor of the journal Advances in Methodology and Statistics (*[Metodološki zvezki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metodološki_zvezki)*) in 1987 – 2012.[^o18]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Ferligoj twice received awards of Statistical Society of Slovenia – in 1997, and in 2012 (Marijan Blejec award[^o20]).

The book *Generalized Blockmodeling* that she coauthored received Harrison White Outstanding Book Award by Mathematical Sociology Section of the American Sociological Association in 2007.[^o21] Jointly with Vladimir Batagelj, she received Simmel award from the International Network for Social Network Analysis.[^o22]

In 2007 and 2008, students at the Faculty of Social Science chose her as the best professor.[^o8] She received Doctor et Professor Honoris Causa at Eötvös Loránd University in Budapest in 2010,[^o23] and Golden Plaque of University of Ljubljana in 2012.[^o24]

She is an elected member of the European Academy of Sociology[^o25] and [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o26]
