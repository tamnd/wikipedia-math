# Sophie Morel

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Sophie Morel"}

**Sophie Morel** (born 1979)[^o5] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). She is a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) [directrice de recherches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research#Employment) in mathematics at [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon). In 2012 she received one of the ten prizes of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In a 2011 interview, Morel credited a math magazine bought while in 9th grade as well as summer camps for developing her interest in mathematics[^o7] and in a 2012 interview she mentioned being a keen distance runner.[^o8] She studied in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure).[^o15] In 2005 she finished her Ph.D. at the University of Paris-Sud, under the supervision of [Gérard Laumon](wikipedia://en/22565856).[^o8][^o15] Her thesis made progress on the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).[^o8]

After her Ph.D., she was a [Clay Research Fellow](wikipedia://en/7655) between 2005 and 2011. In December 2009 she was appointed as a professor of mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University),[^o9] becoming the first woman in mathematics to be [tenured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenure) there.[^o10] From 2012 to 2020, she was a professor of mathematics in [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where she was also the Henry Burchard Fine Professor in 2015.[^o11] Morel moved to the École Normale supérieure de Lyon as a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) [directrice de recherches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research#Employment) in mathematics in 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010, in the "Number Theory" section.[^o13] In 2012 she received one of the prestigious [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) Prize for young researchers, and in May 2013 she was announced as the winner of the inaugural 2014 [AWM-Microsoft Research Prize in Algebra and Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM-Microsoft_Research_Prize_in_Algebra_and_Number_Theory).[^o14][^o15]
