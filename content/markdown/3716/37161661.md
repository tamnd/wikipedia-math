# Pierre Conner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Conner"}

**Pierre Euclide Conner** (27 June 1932, [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas) – 3 February 2018, [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans), [Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana))[^o2] was an American mathematician, who worked on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562) (especially [cobordism](wikipedia://en/391832) theory).

:::

In 1955 Conner received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [Donald Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_C._Spencer) with thesis *The Green's and Neumann's Problems for Differential Forms on Riemannian Manifolds*. He was a post-doctoral fellow from 1955 to 1957 (and again in 1961–1962) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He was in the 1960s a professor at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), where he collaborated with his colleague [Edwin E. Floyd](wikipedia://en/48509949), leading to the [Conner–Floyd orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conner–Floyd_orientation), and then in the 1970s a professor at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
