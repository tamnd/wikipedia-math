# Indexed search

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Indexed search"}

**Indexed search**,[^o2] also called the **cutpoint method**,[^o3] is an [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) for discrete-distribution [pseudo-random number sampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudo-random_number_sampling), invented by Chen and Asau in 1974.

:::
