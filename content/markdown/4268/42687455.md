# Martin Barner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Barner"}

**Martin Barner** (19 April 1921 – 31 July 2020) was a German mathematician working in the fields of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). Barner received his doctorate in 1950 at the Albert-Ludwigs-University in Freiburg. From 1957, he was professor in Karlsruhe and from 1962 at his alma mater in Freiburg, where he retired in 1989. From 1963 to 1994, he was director of the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) and from 1968 to 1977 he was president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).

:::

He died in July 2020 at the age of 99.[^o3]
