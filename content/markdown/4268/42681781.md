# Principal factor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Principal factor"}

In algebra, the **principal factor** of a $\mathcal{J}$-class *J* of a [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) *S* is equal to *J* if *J* is the [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) of *S*, and to $J \cup \{0\}$ otherwise.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- A principal factor is a [simple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_semigroup), [0-simple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0-simple) or [null semigroup](wikipedia://en/22694038).[^o4]

:::
