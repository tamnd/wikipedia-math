# Strichartz estimate

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Strichartz estimate"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), **Strichartz estimates** are a family of inequalities for linear [dispersive partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/8477417). These inequalities establish size and decay of solutions in [mixed norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_norm) [Lebesgue spaces](wikipedia://en/45194). They were first noted by [Robert Strichartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Strichartz) and arose out of connections to the Fourier restriction problem.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Consider the linear [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) in $\mathbb{R}^d$ with *h* = *m* = 1. Then the solution for initial data $u_0$ is given by $e^{it\Delta/2}u_0$. Let *q* and *r* be real numbers satisfying $2\leq q, r \leq \infty$; $\frac{2}{q}+\frac{d}{r}=\frac{d}{2}$; and $(q,r,d)\neq(2,\infty,2)$.

In this case the homogeneous Strichartz estimates take the form:[^o5]

$$\|e^{it\Delta/2} u_0\|_{L^q_tL^r_x}\leq C_{d,q,r} \|u_0\|_{L^2_x}.$$

Further suppose that $\tilde q, \tilde r$ satisfy the same restrictions as $q, r$ and $\tilde q', \tilde r'$ are their dual exponents, then the dual homogeneous Strichartz estimates take the form:[^o5]

$$\left\| \int_\mathbb{R} e^{-is\Delta/2}F(s)\,ds\right\|_{L^2_x}\leq C_{d,\tilde q, \tilde r}\|F\|_{L^{\tilde q'}_tL^{\tilde r'}_x}.$$

The inhomogeneous Strichartz estimates are:[^o5]

$$\left\| \int_{s<t} e^{i(t-s)\Delta/2}F(s)\,ds\right\|_{L^q_tL^r_x}\leq C_{d, q, r, \tilde q, \tilde r}\|F\|_{L^{\tilde q'}_tL^{\tilde r'}_x}.$$

:::
