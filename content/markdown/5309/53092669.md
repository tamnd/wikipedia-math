# Theodore Gamelin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore Gamelin"}

**Theodore William Gamelin** is an American mathematician. He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o3]

:::

Gamelin was born in 1939. He received his B.S. degree in mathematics from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1960,[^o3] and completed his Ph.D. at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1963. His doctoral advisor was [František Wolf](wikipedia://en/6095350). His doctoral dissertation was titled *The extension problem for restrictions of functions in a subspace of C(X)*.[^o4] He served as [C.L.E. Moore Instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_Instructor) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1963 to 1965, before joining the UCLA faculty.[^o3]

In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
