# George F. Simmons

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George F. Simmons"}

**George Finlay Simmons** (March 3, 1925[^o2] – August 6, 2019)[^o3][^o4] was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [classical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is known as the author of widely used textbooks on university mathematics.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 3 March 1925 in Austin, Texas.

He received his BS degree from [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1946. He received his MS degree from [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1948. After finishing his PhD from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1957, he joined [Colorado College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_College) as a lecturer.[^o6]

From 1957 to his death in 2019, he taught at a number of universities, which include [Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College), the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island), Yale University, the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine), and the University of Chicago.[^o7]

## Quotes {#Quotes}

In the [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) introduction of his book *Precalculus Mathematics in a Nutshell*, Professor George F. Simmons wrote that the [New Math](wikipedia://en/404988) produced students who had "heard of the [commutative law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_law), but did not know the [multiplication table](wikipedia://en/57122)."[^o9]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Some of his books are:

- *Introduction to Topology and Modern Analysis* (1963)[^o11]
- *Differential Equations with Applications and Historical Notes* (1972, 1991, 2016)[^o12]
- *Precalculus Mathematics in a Nutshell* (1981)[^o13]
- *Calculus with Analytic Geometry* (1985, 1996)[^o14]
- *Calculus Gems: Brief Lives and Memorable Mathematics* (1992)[^o15]
