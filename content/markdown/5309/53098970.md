# Doris Cohen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Doris Cohen"}

**Doris Cohen** was a twentieth-century American mathematician and aerodynamicist. She worked at the [Langley Memorial Aeronautical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley_Memorial_Aeronautical_Laboratory) of the [National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Advisory_Committee_for_Aeronautics) (NACA, a precursor to [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)). In 1941, a research report she published made her NACA's first female author.[^o2]

:::

She was married to, and worked with, aerodynamicist [R. T. Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Thomas_Jones_%28engineer%29). With him, she co-founded [Vega Instrument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vega_Instrument) Co., a telescope manufacturing company, in 1957.[^o3]
