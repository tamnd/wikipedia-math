# Sybilla Beckmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sybilla Beckmann"}

**Sybilla Beckmann** is a Josiah Meigs Distinguished Teaching Professor of Mathematics, Emeritus, at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) and a recipient of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) [Louise Hay Award](wikipedia://en/51270001).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sybilla Beckmann received her Sc.B. in Mathematics from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1980[^o6] and her Ph.D. in Mathematics from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) under the supervision of [David Harbater](wikipedia://en/3521313) in 1986. She taught at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) as a J.W. Gibbs Instructor of Mathematics, before becoming a Josiah Meigs Distinguished Teaching Professor of Mathematics at the University of Georgia.[^o5] She retired in 2020.[^o6]

Beckmann's main interests include [mathematical cognition](wikipedia://en/6258906), mathematical education of teachers, and mathematics content for [pre-Kindergarten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-Kindergarten) through Grade 8.[^o7]
