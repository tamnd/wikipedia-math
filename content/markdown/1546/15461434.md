# Henry Gordon Rice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Gordon Rice"}

**Henry Gordon Rice** (July 20, 1920 – April 14, 2003)[^o2][^o3] was an American logician and mathematician best known as the author of [Rice's theorem](wikipedia://en/25852), which he proved in his doctoral dissertation of 1951 at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) with thesis advisor [Paul C. Rosenbloom](wikipedia://en/37395137).[^o4] Rice was also a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Hampshire). After 1960 he was employed by [Computer Sciences Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Sciences_Corporation) in [El Segundo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Segundo,_California).[^o5][^o6]

:::

Rice died on April 14, 2003, in [Davis, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis,_California).[^o7]
