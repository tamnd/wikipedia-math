# Formal science

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Formal science"}

**Formal science** is a [branch of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branches_of_science) studying disciplines concerned with abstract structures described by [formal systems](wikipedia://en/396102).

:::

Whereas the [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science) and [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science) seek to characterize [physical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_systems) and [social systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_system), respectively, using [theoretical](wikipedia://en/30746) and [empirical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical) methods, the formal sciences use language [tools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tools) concerned with characterizing abstract structures described by [formal systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_systems) and the deductions that can be made from them.

The formal sciences aid the natural and social sciences by providing information about the structures used to describe the physical world, and what inferences may be made about them.[^o2]

## Differences from other sciences {#Differences_from_other_sciences}

One reason why mathematics enjoys special esteem, above all other sciences, is that its laws are absolutely certain and indisputable, while those of other sciences are to some extent debatable and in constant danger of being overthrown by newly discovered facts.

Because of their non-empirical nature, formal sciences are construed by outlining a set of [axioms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axioms) and [definitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Definitions) from which other statements ([theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theorems)) are deduced. For this reason, in [Rudolf Carnap](wikipedia://en/57987)'s [logical-positivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_positivism) conception of the [epistemology of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), theories belonging to formal sciences are understood to contain no [synthetic statements](wikipedia://en/6395956), instead containing only analytic statements.[^o7][^o8]
