# Time-use survey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Time-use survey"}

A **time-use survey** is a statistical survey which aims to report data on how, on average, people spend their time.

:::

## Objectives {#Objectives}

The objective of the Time-Use survey is to identify, classify and quantify the main types of activity that people engage in during a definitive time period, e.g. a year, a month, etc. Many surveys are used for calculation of [unpaid work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unpaid_work) done by women as well as men in particular locality.
