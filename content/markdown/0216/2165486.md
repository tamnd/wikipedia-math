# Rosser's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rosser's theorem"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), **Rosser's theorem** states that the $n$th [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) is greater than $n \log n$, where $\log$ is the [natural logarithm](wikipedia://en/21476) function. It was published by [J. Barkley Rosser](wikipedia://en/679224) in 1939.[^o2]

:::

Its full statement is:

Let $p_n$ be the $n$th [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666). Then for $n\geq 1$

$$p_n > n \log n.$$

In 1999, [Pierre Dusart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Dusart) proved a tighter lower bound for $n\geq 2$:[^o3]

$$p_n > n (\log n + \log \log n - 1).$$
