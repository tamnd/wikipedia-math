# Beta Kappa Chi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beta Kappa Chi"}

**Beta Kappa Chi** (**ΒΚΧ**) is an American collegiate [honor society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_society) that recognizes academic achievement in the fields of [natural science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was established in 1923 at [Lincoln University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_%28Pennsylvania%29), a [historically Black university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historically_black_university) near [Oxford, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_Pennsylvania). It has established more than 65 chapters in the United States, and is a member of the [Association of College Honor Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_College_Honor_Societies).

:::

## History {#History}

**Beta Kappa Chi Scientific Society** was founded by science students and faculty at [Lincoln University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_%28Pennsylvania%29) on January 31, 1923.[^o5][^o6][^o7] Its faculty supporters were [Harold Fetter Grimm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Fetter_Grimm), head of the department of biology; Arthur E. James, professor of chemistry' and [Walter Livingstone Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Livingston_Wright), professor of mathematics.[^o8][^o7]

Six of its charter members became medical doctors, one became a dentist, and another became a college president.[^o6][^o7]

The charter members created a constitution and selected the society's keys and symbols.[^o7] Initially, Beta Kappa Chi operated as a local science club.[^o5] Its first guest speaker was [Ernest Everett Just](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Everett_Just), a biologist from [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University).[^o6][^o7]

It became a national organization on May 8, 1926, with the establishment of the Beta chapter at [West Virginia State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_State_College).[^o6][^o5] Beta Kappa Chi Honorary Scientific Society was incorporated in 1929.[^o6] Its purpose was to encourage and advance scientific education, research, and the dissemination of scientific knowledge.[^o10][^o9] Chapters were limited to accredited "grade A" colleges and universities.[^o10]

Next*,* Gamma chapter was established at [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University).[^o5] By 1936, there were eight chapters. The society expanded to other historically black colleges and universities in the United States through 1941.[^o8][^o6] However, the organization almost collapsed in 1941, surviving by the re-establishment of the Delta chapter at [Johnson C. Smith University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_C._Smith_University) by its local science club and by the recruitment activities by members of the Alpha chapter.[^o6][^o11] Gamma chapter was reestablished in 1943.[^o11]

To increase organizational stability, the society added a faculty sponsor for each chapter.[^o6] This helped stabilize all eight of the Beta Kappa Chi chapters and brought the addition of thirteen more chapters.[^o6] A grand chapter was established to oversee the national organization and to support alumni engagement.[^o6]

The society published its first newsletter, *Beta Kappa Chi Scientific Society News Letter*, in May 1943; its name was changed to the current *Beta Kappa Chi Bulletin* with the third issue in May 1944.[^o5]

The society changed its name to Beta Kappa Chi National Honor Society in 1960.[^o8] It was admitted to the [Association of College Honor Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_College_Honor_Societies) in 1961.[^o12][^o9] Nearly every [historically Black college and university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historically_black_colleges_and_universities) in the United States had a chapter of Beta Kappa Chi by 1965.[^o8]

As of 2024, Beta Kappa Chi honor society has 67 active chapters across the United States, and 66,000 members.[^o13] It is a scholastic [honor society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_society) that recognizes academic achievement among students in the fields of [natural science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o4][^o12] It is the oldest mostly Black scientific organization in the United States.[^o9]

## Symbols {#Symbols}

The motto of Beta Kappa Chi is ''Science holds the Golden Key to the Royal Palace of Knowledge''.[^o10][^o15] The meaning of the Greek letters Beta Kappa Chi are only known to the society's members, but represent its motto.[^o10][^o15] Its colors are golden yellow and [royal blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_blue).[^o15][^o4]

The Beta Kappa Chi key designed by charter member John Martyne Howe.[^o7] Its key is shaped like a [benzene ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzene_ring) with the Greek letters ΒΚΧ and a [clover leaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clover) above the Β, a [skull and crossed bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skull_and_crossbones_%28fraternities_and_sports%29) above the Κ, and a circle above the Χ. There is a [retort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retort) below the Β, a [balance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_scale) below the Κ, and scroll with stencils below the Χ.[^o4][^o10][^o15]

The symbols were selected by charter members Horace Bond, W. C. Coles, and Adolphus Gordon.[^o7] The clover leaf symbolizes [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), the scull and crossed bones represent [anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatomy), and the circle stands for [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy).[^o10][^o15] The retort represents chemistry, balance is a symbol of physics, and the scroll with stencils represents mathematics.[^o10][^o15]

Its publication is the *Beta Kappa Chi Bulletin*.[^o12][^o5]

## Membership {#Membership}

Membership is open to undergraduate and graduate students.[^o12] Undergraduates are eligible for membership if they have completed 64 hours of college courses with 17 hours in the sciences, with a B average, and rank in the upper fifth of their class.[^o12] Graduate students must have completed fifteen hours in the sciences with a mix of As and Bs.[^o12]

## Activities {#Activities}

Beta Kappa Chi has an annual national convention that includes research presentations by its members.[^o12] This meeting is held in conjunction with the [National Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Science) in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)[^o18][^o9][^o19] In 2017, it held a joint annual meeting with the [National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Allergy_and_Infectious_Diseases) and the National Institute of Science.[^o20]

## Chapters {#Chapters}

As of 2024, Beta Kappa Chi honor society has 67 active chapters across the United States.[^o13][^o22][^o23] Chapters were assigned Greek letter names in alphabetical order until 1950; at that time Greek letter names were abolished and all chapters were named by their host institution.[^o24]

## Notable members {#Notable_members}

- [Horace Mann Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace_Mann_Bond) (*Alpha*), president of [Fort Valley State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Valley_State_College) and [father](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Father) of [civil-rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_rights_movement) leader [Julian Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Bond)[^o6][^o7][^o9]
- [Ulysses Grant Dailey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_Grant_Dailey) (*Alpha*), surgeon and president of the [National Medical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medical_Association)[^o5]
- [Harold Fetter Grimm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Fetter_Grimm) (Alpha honorary), head of the department of biology and dean for fifty years at [Lincoln University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_%28Pennsylvania%29)[^o11][^o26]
- [Mary Elliott Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Elliott_Hill), one of the earliest African-American women to become a [chemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemist)
- [Robert S. Jason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_S._Jason) (*Alpha*), head of the department of pathology at the [Howard University College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University_College_of_Medicine)[^o6][^o5][^o7]
- [Lynda Marie Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynda_Marie_Jordan), biochemist and associate professor of chemistry at [North Carolina A&T State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_A&T_State_University)
- [Angie Turner King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angie_Turner_King), professor of chemistry and mathematics at [West Virginia State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_State_University)
- [Evelyn Nicol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evelyn_Nicol), [immunologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunology), [microbiologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microbiologist), and the first to isolate the [herpes zoster virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varicella_zoster_virus)
- [E. E. O'Banion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._E._O'Banion), chairman of the chemistry department and director of the natural sciences department at [Prairie View A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie_View_A&M_University) from the 1940s to the 1970s[^o27][^o28][^o19]
- [Joseph Alphonso Pierce](wikipedia://en/63094219), mathematician and statistician
- [Hildrus Poindexter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildrus_Poindexter) (*Alpha*), [bacteriologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacteriologist), pioneer in tropical medicine, and head of the department of preventative medicine, bacteriology, and public health at the [Howard University College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University_College_of_Medicine)[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o9]
- [Johnnie Hines Watts Prothro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnnie_Hines_Watts_Prothro), professor at the [Tuskegee Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuskegee_Institute), [University of Connecticut at Storrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut_at_Storrs), and [Georgia State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_State_University)
- [Carl Glennis Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Glennis_Roberts) (*Alpha*), one of the first African Americans to be elected to the [American College of Surgeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_College_of_Surgeons) and a president of the [National Medical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medical_Association)[^o5]
- [Gladys W. Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gladys_W._Royal), early African-American [biochemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemist)
- [Alberta Jones Seaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Jones_Seaton), professor of biology at [Texas Southern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Southern_University) and the first African-American women awarded a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [zoology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoology)
- [Jerome Singleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerome_Singleton), [Paralympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paralympic) [athlete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_and_field)
- [Frederick D. Stubbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_D._Stubbs) (*Alpha*), [thoracic surgeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoracic_surgeon) in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania)[^o5][^o29][^o30][^o31]
- [Louise Nixon Sutton](wikipedia://en/53733627), first chair of the Department of Physical Sciences and Mathematics at [Elizabeth City State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_City_State_University)
- [Thyrsa Frazier Svager](wikipedia://en/53810333), head of the Department of Mathematics at [Central State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_State_University)
- [Thomas Wyatt Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Wyatt_Turner) (*Alpha honorary*), botanist, head of the department of natural sciences at Howard University, first Black American to receive a Ph.D. in [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), and helped found the [NAACP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAACP) and the [Federated Colored Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federated_Colored_Catholics)[^o11]
- [Levi Watkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_Watkins), heart surgeon who was the first to successfully implant an automatic [defibrillator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defibrillation) in a human patient
- [I. J. K. Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._J._K._Wells) (*Alpha*), state supervisor of Negro schools for [West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia)[^o6]
- [Michael Duryea Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Duryea_Williams), professor at [Clark Atlanta University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Atlanta_University)
- [James Edward Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Edward_Young), professor physics at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology)
