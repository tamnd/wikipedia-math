# Representation of a Lie superalgebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Representation of a Lie superalgebra"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **representation of a Lie superalgebra** is an [action](wikipedia://en/1058218) of [Lie superalgebra](wikipedia://en/297501) *L* on a [**Z**<sub>2</sub>-graded vector space](wikipedia://en/1155734) *V*, such that if *A* and *B* are any two pure elements of *L* and *X* and *Y* are any two pure elements of *V*, then

:::

$$(c_1 A+c_2 B)\cdot X=c_1 A\cdot X + c_2 B\cdot X$$

$$A\cdot (c_1 X + c_2 Y)=c_1 A\cdot X + c_2 A\cdot Y$$

$$(-1)^{A\cdot X}=(-1)^A(-1)^X$$

$$[A,B]\cdot X=A\cdot (B\cdot X)-(-1)^{AB}B\cdot (A\cdot X).$$

Equivalently, a representation of *L* is a **Z**<sub>2</sub>-graded representation of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of *L* which respects the third equation above.

## Unitary representation of a star Lie superalgebra {#Unitary_representation_of_a_star_Lie_superalgebra}

A \* [Lie superalgebra](wikipedia://en/297501) is a complex Lie superalgebra equipped with an [involutive](wikipedia://en/293450) [antilinear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilinear) [map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map) \* such that \* respects the grading and

\[a,b\]\*=\[b\*,a\*\].

A [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of such a Lie algebra is a **Z**<sub>2</sub> [graded](wikipedia://en/1155734) [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) which is a representation of a Lie superalgebra as above together with the requirement that [self-adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-adjoint) elements of the Lie superalgebra are represented by [Hermitian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian) transformations.

This is a major concept in the study of [supersymmetry](wikipedia://en/224636) together with representation of a Lie superalgebra on an algebra. Say A is an [\*-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star-algebra) representation of the Lie superalgebra (together with the additional requirement that \* respects the grading and L\[a\]\*=-(-1)<sup>La</sup>L\*\[a\*\]) and **H** is the unitary rep and also, **H** is a [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of A.

These three reps are all compatible if for pure elements a in A, |ψ> in **H** and L in the Lie superalgebra,

L\[a|ψ>)\]=(L\[a\])|ψ>+(-1)<sup>La</sup>a(L\[|ψ>\]).

Sometimes, the Lie superalgebra is [embedded](wikipedia://en/52119) within A in the sense that there is a homomorphism from the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of the Lie superalgebra to A. In that case, the equation above reduces to

L\[a\]=La-(-1)<sup>La</sup>aL.

This approach avoids working directly with a Lie supergroup, and hence avoids the use of auxiliary [Grassmann numbers](wikipedia://en/3117887).
