# Jørgen Pedersen Gram

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jørgen Pedersen Gram"}

**Jørgen Pedersen Gram** (27 June 1850 – 29 April 1916) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) [actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was born in [Nustrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nustrup), Duchy of [Schleswig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) and died in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen), Denmark.

:::

Important papers of his include *On series expansions determined by the methods of least squares*, and *Investigations of the number of [primes](wikipedia://en/23666) less than a given number*. The mathematical method that bears his name, the [Gram–Schmidt process](wikipedia://en/82361), was first published in the former paper, in 1883.[^o3]

For number theorists his main fame is the series for the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) (the leading function in [Riemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann)'s exact [prime-counting function](wikipedia://en/331826)). Instead of using a series of [logarithmic integrals](wikipedia://en/47137), Gram's function uses logarithm powers and the zeta function of positive integers. It has recently been supplanted by a formula of [Ramanujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan) that uses the [Bernoulli numbers](wikipedia://en/4964) directly instead of the zeta function.

In control theory, the Gramian or [Gram matrix](wikipedia://en/987959) is an important contribution named after him. The [Controllability Gramian](wikipedia://en/5347320) and [Observability Gramian](wikipedia://en/5347359) are both important in the analysis of the stability of [control systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_systems). The Gram matrix is also important in [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning), where it is used to represent the distribution of features in [style transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style_transfer).

Gram was the first mathematician to provide a systematic theory of the development of skew frequency curves, showing that the normal symmetric Gaussian [error curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error_curve) was but one special case of a more general class of frequency curves.[^o4]

[Gram's theorem](wikipedia://en/35215342), the [Gram–Charlier series](wikipedia://en/641132#Gram–Charlier_A_series), and [Gram points](wikipedia://en/1415099#Gram_points) are also named after him.

He died on his way to a meeting of the [Royal Danish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters) after being struck by a cyclist.
