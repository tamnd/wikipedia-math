# Unfoldable cardinal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Unfoldable cardinal"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **unfoldable cardinal** is a certain kind of [large cardinal](wikipedia://en/383644) number.

:::

Formally, a [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173) κ is **λ-unfoldable** if and only if for every [transitive model](wikipedia://en/1118686) *M* of cardinality κ of [ZFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZFC)-minus-[power set](wikipedia://en/23799) such that κ is in *M* and *M* contains all its sequences of length less than κ, there is a non-trivial [elementary embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_embedding) *j* of *M* into a transitive model with the [critical point](wikipedia://en/4888510) of *j* being κ and *j*(κ) ≥ λ.

A cardinal is **unfoldable** if and only if it is an λ-unfoldable for all [ordinals](wikipedia://en/26547932) λ.

A [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173) κ is **strongly λ-unfoldable** if and only if for every [transitive model](wikipedia://en/1118686) *M* of cardinality κ of [ZFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZFC)-minus-[power set](wikipedia://en/23799) such that κ is in *M* and *M* contains all its sequences of length less than κ, there is a non-trivial [elementary embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_embedding) *j* of *M* into a transitive model "N" with the [critical point](wikipedia://en/4888510) of *j* being κ, *j*(κ) ≥ λ, and *V*<sub>λ</sub> is a subset of *N*. [Without loss of generality](wikipedia://en/296000), we can demand also that *N* contains all its sequences of length λ.

Likewise, a cardinal is **strongly unfoldable** if and only if it is strongly λ-unfoldable for all λ.

These properties are essentially weaker versions of [strong](wikipedia://en/248104) and [supercompact](wikipedia://en/248116) cardinals, consistent with [V = L](wikipedia://en/696472). Many theorems related to these cardinals have generalizations to their unfoldable or strongly unfoldable counterparts. For example, the existence of a strongly unfoldable implies the consistency of a slightly weaker version of the [proper forcing axiom](wikipedia://en/6250799).

## Relations between large cardinal properties {#Relations_between_large_cardinal_properties}

Assuming V = L, the least unfoldable cardinal is greater than the least indescribable cardinal.[^o3]<sup>p.14</sup> Assuming a Ramsey cardinal exists, it is less than the least Ramsey cardinal.[^o3]<sup>p.3</sup>

A [Ramsey cardinal](wikipedia://en/326533) is unfoldable and will be strongly unfoldable in L. It may fail to be strongly unfoldable in V, however.

In L, any unfoldable cardinal is strongly unfoldable; thus unfoldable and strongly unfoldable have the same [consistency strength](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consistency_strength).

A cardinal k is κ-strongly unfoldable, and κ-unfoldable, if and only if it is [weakly compact](wikipedia://en/272054). A κ+ω-unfoldable cardinal is [indescribable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_indescribable_cardinal) and preceded by a stationary set of totally indescribable cardinals.
