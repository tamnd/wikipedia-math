# Complex Mexican hat wavelet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Complex Mexican hat wavelet"}

In [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), the **complex Mexican hat wavelet** is a low-oscillation, [complex-valued](wikipedia://en/5826), [wavelet](wikipedia://en/50903) for the [continuous wavelet transform](wikipedia://en/679596). This wavelet is formulated in terms of its [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) as the Hilbert [analytic signal](wikipedia://en/950777) of the conventional [Mexican hat wavelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_hat_wavelet):

:::

$$\hat{\Psi}(\omega) = \begin{cases} 2\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\pi^{-\frac{1}{4}}\omega^2 e^{-\frac{1}{2}\omega^2} & \omega\geq0 \\ 0 & \omega\leq 0. \end{cases}$$

Temporally, this wavelet can be expressed in terms of the [error function](wikipedia://en/177111), as:

$$\Psi(t) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\pi^{-\frac{1}{4}}\left(\sqrt{\pi}\left(1 - t^2\right)e^{-\frac{1}{2}t^2} - \left(\sqrt{2}it + \sqrt{\pi}\operatorname{erf}\left[\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}t\right]\left(1 - t^2\right)e^{-\frac{1}{2}t^2}\right)\right).$$

This wavelet has $O\left(|t|^{-3}\right)$ [asymptotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic) temporal decay in $|\Psi(t)|$, dominated by the [discontinuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discontinuity_%28mathematics%29) of the second [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) of $\hat{\Psi}(\omega)$ at $\omega = 0$.

This wavelet was proposed in 2002 by Addison *et al.*[^o2] for applications requiring high temporal precision [time-frequency analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-frequency_analysis).
