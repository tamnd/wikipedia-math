# Reinhold Remmert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reinhold Remmert"}

*Image: Reinhold Remmert.jpg.* Reinhold Remmert

**Reinhold W. Remmert** (22 June 1930 – 9 March 2016[^o2][^o3]) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Born in [Osnabrück](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osnabrück), [Lower Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Saxony), he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) in [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). He established and developed the theory of [complex-analytic spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex-analytic_space) in joint work with [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031). Until his retirement in 1995, he was a professor for [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) in [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

:::

Remmert wrote two books on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), which contain a huge amount of historical information together with references on important papers in the subject.
