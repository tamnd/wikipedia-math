# Yael Karshon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yael Karshon"}

**Yael Karshon** (יעל קרשון; born 1964) is an Israeli and Canadian mathematician who has been described as "one of Canada's leading experts in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298)". She works as a professor at the [University of Toronto Mississauga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Mississauga) and [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) .

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Karshon took part in the 1982 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), on the Israeli team. She earned her Ph.D. in 1993 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under the supervision of [Shlomo Sternberg](wikipedia://en/5375996).

After working as a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and then earning tenure at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), she moved to the University of Toronto Mississauga in 2002.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Karshon is the author of the monographs *Periodic Hamiltonian flows on four dimensional manifolds* (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society 672, 1999), which completely classified the [Hamiltonian actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_action) of the [circle group](wikipedia://en/508177) on four-dimensional [compact manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_manifold). With [Viktor Ginzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Ginzburg) and [Victor Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636), she also wrote *Moment maps, cobordisms, and Hamiltonian group actions* (Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 98, American Mathematical Society, 2002), which surveys "symplectic geometry in the context of equivariant cobordism".

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Karshon won the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) in 2008.

## Personal {#Personal}

Karshon is from Israel, and lived in the US for ten years, eventually becoming a permanent resident. She took Canadian citizenship in 2011. From her marriage to mathematician [Dror Bar-Natan](wikipedia://en/10363723) she has two sons.
