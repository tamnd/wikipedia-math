# Procrustes transformation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Procrustes transformation"}

A **Procrustes transformation** is a geometric transformation that involves only [translation](wikipedia://en/204682), [rotation](wikipedia://en/39789), [uniform scaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_scaling), or a [combination](wikipedia://en/195947) of these [transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_transformation). Hence, it may change the size, position, and orientation of a geometric object, but not its [shape](wikipedia://en/169191).

:::

The Procrustes transformation is named after the mythical Greek robber [Procrustes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procrustes)[^o2] who made his victims fit his bed either by stretching their limbs or cutting them off.
