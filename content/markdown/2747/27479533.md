# Valentin Afraimovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valentin Afraimovich"}

**Valentin Afraimovich** (Валентин Сендерович Афраймович, 2 April 1945 – 21 February 2018) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians)-[Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made contributions to [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632), qualitative theory of [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations), [bifurcation theory](wikipedia://en/2326042), concept of [attractor](wikipedia://en/191094),[^o2] [strange attractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strange_attractors), [space-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space-time) [chaos](wikipedia://en/6295), [mathematical models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_models) of non-equilibrium media and [biological systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_systems), travelling [waves](wikipedia://en/33516) in lattices, [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity) of [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit) and dimension-like characteristics in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Afraimovich was born on 2 April 1945 in [Kirov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirov,_Kirov_Oblast), [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). He got his PhD ([Kandidat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat)) degree in 1974 at the [Nizhny Novgorod State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod_State_University) under the advice of L. P. Shil'nikov. Also in 1990 he received his [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degree in Mathematics and Physics, at [Saratov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov_State_University) in Russia. After then, he held several academic positions, including:

- 1992-1995 Visiting Principal Research Scientist, [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta)
- 1995-1996 Visiting Professor, [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), [Evanston, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Illinois)
- 1996-1998 Visiting Professor, [National Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tsing_Hua_University), [Hsinchu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsinchu), Taiwan
- 1998–present Professor–researcher, IICO, [Universidad Autónoma de San Luis Potosí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Autónoma_de_San_Luis_Potosí), San Luis Potosí, Mexico

Afraimovich's students include Mark Shereshevsky, Nizhny Novgorod 1990; Todd Ray Young, Atlanta, Georgia, 1995; Antonio Morante, San Luis Potosí (SLP) Mexico, 2002; Salomé Murgia, SLP Mexico, 2003; [Alberto Cordonet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Cordonet), SLP Mexico, 2002; Francisco Ordaz, SLP Mexico, 2004; Leticia Ramirez, SLP Mexico, 2005; Irma Tristan-Lopez, SLP Mexico, 2010; Rosendo Vazquez-Bañuelos, 2013.

He died on 21 February 2018 in [Nizhny Novgorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod), Russia.[^o5]
