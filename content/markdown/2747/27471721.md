# Circle packing in a square

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Circle packing in a square"}

**Circle packing in a square** is a [packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_problem) in [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446) where the aim is to pack $n$ [unit circles](wikipedia://en/27072071) into the smallest possible [square](wikipedia://en/659939). Equivalently, the problem is to arrange $n$ points in a unit square in order to maximize the minimal separation, $d_{n}$, between points.[^o2] To convert between these two formulations of the problem, the square side for unit circles will be $L = 2 + \tfrac{2}{d_{n}}$.

:::

## Solutions {#Solutions}

Solutions (proven optimal for $N \le 30$) have been computed for every $N \le 10,000$.[^o4] Solutions up to $N = 20$ are shown below.[^o4] The obvious square packing is optimal for 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, and 36 circles (the six smallest [square numbers](wikipedia://en/213919)), but ceases to be optimal for larger squares from 49 onwards.[^o4]
