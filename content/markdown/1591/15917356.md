# Horng-Tzer Yau

Yau Horng-Tzer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Horng-Tzer Yau"}

**Horng-Tzer Yau** (; born June 29, 1959) is a Taiwanese-American mathematician who is the Merton Professor of Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Yau was born in 1959 in Taiwan, where he began studying advanced calculus and college algebra in high school.[^o4] He graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.S.) in 1981. As an undergraduate, he studied [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478), and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o5] After graduation, he served two years of military service in the [Republic of China Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_Armed_Forces) from 1981 to 1983.[^o6]

In 1987, Yau earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). His doctoral dissertation, completed under mathematical physicist [Elliott Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Lieb), was titled, "Stability of Coulomb Systems". After receiving his doctorate, Yao completed [postdoctoral research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) under [David Brydges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Brydges) from 1987 to 1988.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

In 1988, Yau joined the mathematics faculty at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He became a full professor at its [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) in 1994. He then moved to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 2003, and then to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2005. He was also a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey, in 2003, and was a distinguished visiting professor there in 2013–14.[^o9]

According to [William C. Kirby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_C._Kirby), dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences at Harvard, "Professor Yau is a leader in the fields of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), ... who has introduced important tools and concepts to study probability, stochastic processes, nonequilibrium statistical physics, and quantum dynamics."[^o10]

Yau is a [2000 MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program).
