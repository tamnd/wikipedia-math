# Malcolm Ludvigsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malcolm Ludvigsen"}

**Malcolm Ludvigsen** (born 14 February 1946) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [plein air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/En_plein_air) painter. He is a former research fellow and visiting lecturer in mathematics at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York) and the author of a book on [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] Many of his paintings depict the beaches of the [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire) coast.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]

:::
