# Henrik Petrini

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Henrik Petrini"}

**Henrik Petrini** (15 April 1863 – 6 October 1957) was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His mathematical contributions are mainly connected with the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), in particular [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342).

:::

He was born in [Falun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falun) and received his PhD in 1890 from [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), where he subsequently held a position as professor. In 1901 he moved to [Växjö](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Växjö), where he worked as a lektor for mathematics and physics at the gymnasium. In 1914 he finally moved to [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm). He is best known for his counterexample of a continuous function for which the [Newton potential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_potential) is not twice differentiable. He was also interested in pedagogical and theological questions and was known to the general public for his radical writings.
