# Housing affordability in anglophone countries

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Housing affordability in anglophone countries"}

The *16th Annual Demographia International Housing Affordability Survey: 2020* analyzed affordability in 7 Anglophone countries and Hong Kong. Among this sample, the housing markets with the least affordable real estate prices are [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong), [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver), and [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney). The top three housing markets with the most affordable real estate prices based on *major* housing markets are [Rochester, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_New_York), [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland), and [Oklahoma City, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City). This survey was performed by Demographia, a global analysis firm.[^o2]

:::

## Methodology {#Methodology}

The prices are based on data from the third quarter of 2019. The housing markets ranked are located in [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), Hong Kong, [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland), [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand), [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore), [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), and the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States). The report also includes special coverage on [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).

The housing markets are ranked on middle income housing affordability, which uses the "[median multiple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_multiple)". This was calculated by taking the median house price divided by the median gross pre-tax household income of the housing market. The median multiple is used widely for analyzing housing markets and is recommended by the [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank) and the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations). It has also been used by the [Joint Center for Housing Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Center_for_Housing_Studies) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

Demographia uses housing affordability ratings to categorize each housing market's median multiple.[^o2]

*Demographia housing affordability ratings[^o2]*

::: {#o4 .table cue=table name="Demographia housing affordability ratings"}

| Housing affordability rating | Median multiple |
|---|---|
| Affordable | \3.0 and under |
| Moderately unaffordable | \3.1 to 4.0 |
| Seriously unaffordable | \4.1 to 5.0 |
| Severely unaffordable | \5.1 and over |

:::
