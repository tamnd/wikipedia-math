# Artin–Zorn theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Artin–Zorn theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Artin–Zorn theorem**, named after [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) and [Max Zorn](wikipedia://en/19740), states that any finite [alternative division ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_division_ring) is necessarily a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615). It was first published in 1930 by Zorn, but in his publication Zorn credited it to Artin.[^o2][^o3]

:::

The Artin–Zorn theorem is a generalization of [Wedderburn's little theorem](wikipedia://en/7132002), which states that finite associative division rings are fields. As a geometric consequence, every finite [Moufang plane](wikipedia://en/14860374) is the classical projective plane over a finite field.[^o4][^o5]
