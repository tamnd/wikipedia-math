# Mitchell's group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mitchell's group"}

In mathematics, **Mitchell's group** is a [complex reflection group](wikipedia://en/5381096) in 6 complex dimensions of order 108 × 9!, introduced by (txt). It has the structure 6.PSU<sub>4</sub>(**F**<sub>3</sub>).2. As a complex reflection group it has 126 reflections of order 2, and its ring of invariants is a [polynomial algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_algebra) with [generators](wikipedia://en/99945) of degrees 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 42. Coxeter gives it group symbol \[1 2 3\]<sup>3</sup> and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) .[^o3]

:::

Mitchell's group is an [index](wikipedia://en/230581) 2 [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [Coxeter–Todd lattice](wikipedia://en/14777789).
