The **compound of six tetrahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It's composed of a symmetric arrangement of 6 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606). It can be constructed by inscribing a [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) within each [cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_%28geometry%29) in the [compound of three cubes](wikipedia://en/14809983), or by [stellating](wikipedia://en/180253) each octahedron in the [compound of three octahedra](wikipedia://en/33808818).

:::

It is one of only five polyhedral compounds (along with the [compound of two great dodecahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_two_great_dodecahedra), the [compound of five great dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14767416), the [compound of two small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14751130), and the [compound of five small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14767417)) which is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) and [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) but not [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive).
