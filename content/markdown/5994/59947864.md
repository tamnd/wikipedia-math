# Samuel Greenhouse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel Greenhouse"}

**Samuel W. Greenhouse** (January 13, 1918 – September 29, 2000) was an American statistician who helped to pioneer the use of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997).[^o3] With [Seymour Geisser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seymour_Geisser), he developed the [Greenhouse–Geisser correction](wikipedia://en/61404733), which is now widely used in the [analysis of variance](wikipedia://en/634) to correct for violations of the assumption of compound symmetry.[^o3]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Greenhouse was one of several founding statisticians at the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) (NIH), where, with [Jerome Cornfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerome_Cornfield), [Jacob Lieberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Lieberman), [Nathan Mantel](wikipedia://en/6184748), and [Marvin Schneiderman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvin_Schneiderman), he co-founded the first [biometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometry) group in the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) in 1948.[^o3][^o5] In 1954, Greenhouse became head of the theoretical statistics and applied mathematics section at the [National Institute of Mental Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Mental_Health). He became chief of the [National Institute of Child Health and Human Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Child_Health_and_Human_Development)'s epidemiology and biometry branch in 1966, and continued to work there until 1974, when he joined the faculty of [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) (GWU). He chaired the GWU department of statistics twice: in 1976–1979 and in 1985–1986. He retired from the GWU faculty in 1988, whereupon he was named an emeritus professor there.[^o3]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Greenhouse was a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664). He was also an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), a fellow of the [American College of Epidemiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_College_of_Epidemiology), and a fellow of the [American Heart Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Heart_Association)'s Council of Epidemiology.[^o5] In 1993 Greenhouse was awarded the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o7]
