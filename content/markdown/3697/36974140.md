# Indigenous bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Indigenous bundle"}

In mathematics, an **indigenous bundle** on a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) is a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) with a [flat connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_connection) associated to some [complex projective structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_projective_structure). Indigenous bundles were introduced by (txt). Indigenous bundles for curves over [p-adic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_field) were introduced by (txt) in his study of [p-adic Teichmüller theory](wikipedia://en/36974047).

:::
