# P-adic Teichmüller theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="P-adic Teichmüller theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), ***p*-adic Teichmüller theory** describes the "uniformization" of [*p*-adic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic) curves and their [moduli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_spaces), generalizing the usual [Teichmüller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_theory) that describes the [uniformization](wikipedia://en/288270) of [Riemann surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_surfaces) and their moduli. It was introduced and developed by (txt).

:::

The first problem is to reformulate the Fuchsian [uniformization](wikipedia://en/288270) of a complex Riemann surface (an isomorphism from the upper half plane to a universal covering space of the surface) in a way that makes sense for *p*-adic curves. The existence of a Fuchsian uniformization is equivalent to the existence of a canonical [indigenous bundle](wikipedia://en/36974140) over the Riemann surface: the unique indigenous bundle that is invariant under complex conjugation and whose [monodromy](wikipedia://en/251250) representation is quasi-Fuchsian. For *p*-adic curves, the analogue of complex conjugation is the [Frobenius endomorphism](wikipedia://en/1088270), and the analogue of the quasi-Fuchsian condition is an integrality condition on the indigenous line bundle. So in *p*-adic Teichmüller theory, the *p*-adic analogue the Fuchsian uniformization of Teichmüller theory, is the study of integral Frobenius invariant indigenous bundles.
