# Foster graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Foster graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Foster graph** is a [bipartite](wikipedia://en/244431) 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 90 vertices and 135 edges.

:::

The Foster graph is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 8, diameter 8 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 10. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) graph. It has [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2 and the upper bound on the [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) is 4.[^o4]

All the [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [distance-regular graphs](wikipedia://en/7768943) are known.[^o5] The Foster graph is one of the 13 such graphs. It is the unique [distance-transitive](wikipedia://en/7768222) graph with [intersection array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_array) {3,2,2,2,2,1,1,1;1,1,1,1,2,2,2,3}.[^o6] It can be constructed as the [incidence graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incidence_graph) of the [partial linear space](wikipedia://en/11197155) which is the unique triple [cover](wikipedia://en/250323) with no 8-gons of the [generalized quadrangle](wikipedia://en/3947006) [*GQ*(2,2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutte-Coxeter_graph). It is named after [R. M. Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._M._Foster), whose *[Foster census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_census)* of [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [symmetric graphs](wikipedia://en/1577896) included this graph.

The [bipartite half](wikipedia://en/39329230) of the Foster graph is a [distance-regular graph](wikipedia://en/7768943) and a [locally linear graph](wikipedia://en/59938063). It is one of a finite number of such graphs with degree six.[^o7]

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Foster graph is a group of order 4320.[^o10] It acts transitively on the vertices, on the edges and on the arcs of the graph. Therefore, the Foster graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896). It has automorphisms that take any vertex to any other vertex and any edge to any other edge. According to the *Foster census*, the Foster graph, referenced as F90A, is the only cubic symmetric graph on 90 vertices.[^o11]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Foster graph is equal to $(x-3) (x-2)^9 (x-1)^{18} x^{10} (x+1)^{18} (x+2)^9 (x+3) (x^2-6)^{12}$.

:::
