# Jeffrey Adams (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey Adams"}

*Image: Jeffrey Adams (mathematician) 1983 (headshot).jpg.* Jeffrey Adams in 1983

**Jeffrey David Adams** (born 1956) is a mathematician at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) who works on [unitary representations](wikipedia://en/292788) of reductive [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and who led the project *[Atlas of Lie Groups and Representations](wikipedia://en/10115601)* that calculated the characters of the representations of [E<sub>8</sub>](wikipedia://en/647994).[^o2][^o3] The project to calculate the representations of E<sub>8</sub> has been compared to the [Human Genome Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Genome_Project) in scope.[^o2][^o3] Together with [Dan Barbasch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Barbasch) and [David Vogan](wikipedia://en/10229758), he co-authored a monograph on a geometric approach to the [Langlands classification](wikipedia://en/10985992) and [Arthur's conjectures](wikipedia://en/20860304) in the real case.

:::

He completed his Ph.D. at Yale University under the supervision of [Gregg Zuckerman](wikipedia://en/10822168) in 1981. In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
