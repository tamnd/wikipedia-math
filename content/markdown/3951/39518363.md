# Giovanni Alberti (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Alberti"}

**Giovanni Alberti** (born March 21, 1965) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is active in the fields of [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694).

:::

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

Alberti has studied at [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) under the guide of [Giuseppe Buttazzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Buttazzo) and [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401); he is professor of mathematics at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). Alberti is mostly known for two remarkable theorems he proved at the beginning of his career, that eventually found applications in various branches of modern [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). The first is a very general [Lusin](wikipedia://en/2257262) type theorem for gradients asserting that every [Borel vector field](wikipedia://en/44055) can be realized as the gradient of a continuously differentiable function outside a [closed subset](wikipedia://en/47279) of a priori prescribed (small) measure.[^o4] The second asserts the rank-one property of the [distributional derivatives](wikipedia://en/1411087) of [functions with bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673), thereby verifying a conjecture of De Giorgi.[^o5] This theorem has found several applications, as for instance in the [Ambrosio](wikipedia://en/25337146)'s proof of an open problem posed by [Di Perna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Di_Perna) and [Lions](wikipedia://en/1270068) concerning the well-posedness of the [continuity equation](wikipedia://en/410793) involving BV vector fields.[^o6] This result is nowadays commonly known as [Alberti's rank-one theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberti's_rank-one_theorem) and its proof rests of a very delicate use of sophisticated tools from [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694); in particular, it makes use of the concept of tangent measure to another measure.[^o7][^o8] Subsequently, Alberti has given contributions to the study of various aspects of Ginzburg-Landau vortices and of the continuity equation.[^o9]

## Recognitions {#Recognitions}

Alberti has been awarded the [Caccioppoli prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) in 2002 and has been an invited speaker at the fourth [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846). He has also been awarded the [Ordine del Cherubino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordine_del_Cherubino) by the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) during the 2024 award ceremony.
