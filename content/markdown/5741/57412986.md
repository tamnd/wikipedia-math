# Radha Kessar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Radha Kessar"}

*Image: Radha Kessar Oberwolfach 2019.jpg.* Kessar at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2019

**Radha Kessar** is an Indian mathematician known for her research in the [representation theory of finite groups](wikipedia://en/874400). She holds the [Fielden Chair in Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/6694084) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), and in 2009 won the [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kessar graduated from [Panjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University) in 1991. She completed her Ph.D. in 1995 from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University); her dissertation, *Blocks And Source Algebras For The Double Covers Of The Symmetric Groups*, was supervised by [Ronald Solomon](wikipedia://en/35255960).

After taking visiting assistant professor positions at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), and working as a Weir Junior Research Fellow at [University College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford), she returned to Ohio State as an assistant professor in 2002. She moved to the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) in 2005, to [City, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City,_University_of_London) in 2012, and then to the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 2022.

## Book {#Book}

With [Michael Aschbacher](wikipedia://en/1652651) and Bob Oliver, she is an author of the book *Fusion Systems in Algebra and Topology* (Cambridge University Press, 2011).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Her 2009 Berwick award was joint with her future City colleague Joseph Chuang, for the research reported in their paper *Symmetric Groups, Wreath Products, Morita Equivalences and Broué's Abelian Defect Conjecture*. She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for 2017-2018.[^o5]
