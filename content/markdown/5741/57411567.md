# Bonnie Gold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bonnie Gold"}

**Bonnie Gold** (born 1948) is an American mathematician, [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636), [philosopher of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), and [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471). She is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emerita) of mathematics at [Monmouth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monmouth_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gold completed her Ph.D. in 1976 at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), under the supervision of [Michael D. Morley](wikipedia://en/2921675).

She was the chair of the mathematics department at [Wabash College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wabash_College) before moving to Monmouth, where she also became department chair.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

The research from Gold's dissertation, *Compact and $\omega$-compact formulas in $L_{\omega_{1},\omega}$*, was later published in the journal *Archiv für Mathematische Logik und Grundlagenforschung*, and concerned [infinitary logic](wikipedia://en/630478).

With Sandra Z. Keith and William A. Marion she co-edited *Assessment Practices in Undergraduate Mathematics*, published by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) in 1999. With Roger A. Simons, Gold is also the editor of another book, *Proof and Other Dilemmas: Mathematics and Philosophy* (MAA, 2008).

Her essay "How your philosophy of mathematics impacts your teaching" was selected for inclusion in *The Best Writing on Mathematics 2012*. In it, she argues that the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) affects the teaching of mathematics even when the teacher's philosophical principles are implicit and unexamined.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012, Gold became the winner of the 22nd [Louise Hay Award](wikipedia://en/51270001) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) for her contributions to [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471). The award citation noted her work in [educational assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_assessment) for undergraduate study in mathematics.
