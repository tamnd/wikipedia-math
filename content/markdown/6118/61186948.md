# Harmonic quadrilateral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harmonic quadrilateral"}

*Image: Harmonic quadrilateral constructions.svg.* Two constructions of a harmonic quadrilateral. Left, the four points where lines through a given point (red) and the vertices of a square (light red) cross the circumcircle of the square. Right, extending a triangle (dark blue) by the point where a [symmedian](wikipedia://en/320997) line (red) crosses its circumcircle.

In [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), a **harmonic quadrilateral** is a [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) whose four vertices lie on a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), and whose pairs of opposite edges have equal products of lengths.

:::

Harmonic quadrilaterals have also been called harmonic quadrangles. They are the images of [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) under [Möbius transformations](wikipedia://en/314493). Every triangle can be extended to a harmonic quadrilateral by adding another vertex, in three ways. The notion of [Brocard points](wikipedia://en/7029586) of triangles can be generalized to these quadrilaterals.

## Definitions and characterizations {#Definitions_and_characterizations}

A harmonic quadrilateral is a [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) that can be inscribed in a circle (a [cyclic quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/194131)) and in which the products of the lengths of opposite sides are equal (an [Apollonius quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/79223479)). Equivalently, it is a quadrilateral that can be obtained as a [Möbius transformation](wikipedia://en/314493) of the vertices of a [square](wikipedia://en/659939), as these transformations preserve both the inscribability of a square and the [cross ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_ratio) of its vertices.[^o3] Four points in the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) define a harmonic quadrilateral when their complex cross ratio is $-1$; this is only possible for points inscribed in a circle, and in this case, it equals the real cross ratio.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

For any point $p$ in the plane, the four lines connecting $p$ to each vertex of the square cut the [circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432) of the square in the four points of a harmonic quadrilateral.[^o3]

Every triangle can be extended to a harmonic quadrilateral in three different ways, by adding a fourth vertex to the triangle, at the point where one of the three [symmedians](wikipedia://en/320997) of the triangle cross its circumcircle. Each symmedian is the line through one vertex of the triangle and through the crossing point of the two [tangent lines to the circumcircle](wikipedia://en/19491492) at the other two vertices.[^o7]

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

The definition of the [Brocard points](wikipedia://en/7029586) of a triangle can be extended to harmonic quadrilaterals. A Brocard point of a polygon has the property that the [line segments](wikipedia://en/22634860) connecting the Brocard to the polygon vertices all form equal angles with the adjacent polygon sides. Each triangle has two Brocard points, one that forms equal angles with the polygon sides adjacent in the clockwise direction from each vertex, and another for the counterclockwise direction. The same property is true for the harmonic quadrilaterals, uniquely among cyclic quadrilaterals.[^o10]
