# Heptahedron

*Image: Chestahedron.png.* A [Chestahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chestahedron), realized with 4 equilateral-triangle and 3 [kite](wikipedia://en/84840) faces, all having the same area,[^o1]

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heptahedron"}

A **heptahedron** (: **heptahedra**) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) having seven sides, or [faces](wikipedia://en/149926).

:::

A heptahedron can take a large number of different basic forms, or topologies. The most familiar are the [hexagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/19659302) and the [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659). Also notable is the [tetrahemihexahedron](wikipedia://en/2851834), which can be seen as a tessellation of the [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325). No heptahedra are [regular](wikipedia://en/333299).

## Topologically distinct heptahedron {#Topologically_distinct_heptahedron}

### Convex {#Convex}

There are 34 topologically distinct *convex* heptahedra, excluding mirror images.[^o5] (Two polyhedra are "topologically distinct" if they have intrinsically different arrangements of faces and vertices, such that it is impossible to distort one into the other simply by changing the lengths of edges or the angles between edges or faces.)
