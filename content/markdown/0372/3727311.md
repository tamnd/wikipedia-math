# Therapeutic effect

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Therapeutic effect"}

**Therapeutic effect** refers to the response(s) after a treatment of any kind, the results of which are judged to be useful or favorable. This is true whether the result was expected, unexpected, or even an [unintended consequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unintended_consequence). An [adverse effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adverse_effect) (including [nocebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nocebo)) is the converse and refers to harmful or undesired response(s). What constitutes a [therapeutic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Therapeutic) effect versus a side effect is a matter of both the nature of the situation and the goals of treatment. No inherent difference separates therapeutic and undesired side effects; both responses are [behavioral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral)/[physiologic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physiologic) changes that occur as a response to the treatment strategy or agent.

:::

## Treatment scope {#Treatment_scope}

To maximize therapeutic effects (desired) and minimize side effects (undesired) requires recognition and quantification of the treatment in multiple dimensions. In the specific case of targeted pharmaceutical interventions, a combination of therapies is often needed to achieve the desired results.

## Pharmacology examples {#Pharmacology_examples}

- A 2015 review found that [aloe vera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloe_vera) exhibits therapeutic [antioxidant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioxidant), [antimicrobial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antimicrobial), immune boosting, [antitumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antitumor), [hypoglycemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypoglycemic), [hypolipidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypolipidemic), [wound healing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wound_healing), and [antidiabetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antidiabetic) effects.
- Also in 2015, a review found that [probiotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probiotics) were beneficial in the treatment of [irritable bowel syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irritable_bowel_syndrome).
- Again in 2015 another review found that [Rituximab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rituximab) was therapeutic in the treatment of [myasthenia gravis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myasthenia_gravis), an [autoimmune disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoimmune_disorder).
- A 2016 review found that the [adjunctive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunctive) use of standardized pharmaceutical-grade nutrients, known as [nutraceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutraceuticals), had a therapeutic effect in patients with [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depression_%28mood%29).
- Also in 2016, a review found that despite limited data [botulinum toxin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botulinum_toxin) type A may be beneficial for the treatment of [trigeminal neuralgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigeminal_neuralgia) [paroxysms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paroxysms) and suggests further study.

## Non-pharmacology examples {#Non-pharmacology_examples}

- A 2014 review found there was a strong therapeutic benefit from [stem cell therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stem_cell_therapy) on organ recovery from injury and that it may also inhibit tumor growth.
- A 2015 review found that [mindfulness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindfulness) based interventions had a therapeutic effect on [stress reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_reduction) for [mental illness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_illness).
- Also in 2015, a review showed that [irradiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irradiation) with [low level laser therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_level_laser_therapy) had the therapeutic effect of increasing [in vitro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_vitro) stem cell proliferation rates.
- A 2017 review showed [aerobic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerobic_exercise) and [resistance exercise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistance_exercise) had a therapeutic effect on the physical and mental well-being of [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer) survivors.
- Also in 2017, a review found that [low-intensity extracorporeal shock wave treatment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extracorporeal_shockwave_therapy) was therapeutic in the treatment of [erectile dysfunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erectile_dysfunction).
