# Paul Vincensini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Vincensini"}

*Image: Georges Reeb, Paul Vincensini, Charles Ehresmann.jpeg.* *Left to right:* [Georges Reeb](wikipedia://en/20126484), Paul Vincensini, and [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108), at a topology conference in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 1949

**Paul Félix Vincensini** (30 April 1896, in [Bastia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastia) – 9 August 1978, in [La Ciotat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Ciotat))[^o2][^o3] was a French mathematician.

:::

In 1927, he wrote his dissertation *Sur trois types de congruences rectilignes* at the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse).[^o4][^o5][^o6] In 1945, working as a Professor at the [University of Besançon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Besançon), he was awarded the [Charles Dupin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dupin) Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences), for his work in higher geometry.[^o7] In 1949, he got the Prix de la Pensée Française. In the same year, he went to Marseille University. He retired in 1967, but still accepted presidency of a symposium of the Florence Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematical Sciences in 1978.[^o3]
