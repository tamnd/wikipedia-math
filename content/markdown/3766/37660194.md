# Aleksei Filippov (mathematician)

(March 2023)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksei Filippov"}

**Aleksei Fedorovich Filippov** (Алексей Фёдорович Филиппов; 29 September 1923 – 10 October 2006) was a Russian mathematician who worked on [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [differential inclusions](wikipedia://en/13725281), [diffraction theory](wikipedia://en/8603) and [numerical methods](wikipedia://en/170089).

:::

Born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1923, Filippov served in the [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) during the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War), then attended [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) ([Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics)). After graduating in 1950, he remained to work at the school. He got his Ph.D. under the supervision of [I. G. Petrovsky](wikipedia://en/4598657), and became a professor in 1978. He taught until his death in 2006.

Filippov showed interest in [continuous loops](wikipedia://en/1189560) in 1950 when he constructed a proof that they divide a plane into interior and exterior parts.[^o4] Known as the [Jordan curve theorem](wikipedia://en/267444), it exemplifies a mathematical proposition easily stated but difficult to prove.

In 1955 Filippov and V. S. Ryaben'kii became interested in [difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation) and wrote *On the Stability of Difference Equations*.[^o5] The work was developed into a [textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) in 1961 which was used in Moscow State University and many other Russian universities for several decades.[^o6] In 1959 he published a paper containing a [lemma](wikipedia://en/18634) about [implicit functions](wikipedia://en/250896) designed for use in [optimal control theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_control_theory) that is named after him (*Filippov's lemma*).[^o7][^o8][^o9]

Filippov made an important contribution in the theory of discontinuous ordinary differential equations with his monograph *Differential Equations with Discontinuous Righthand Sides* (1985).[^o10] Such set-valued dynamical systems arise in [sliding mode control](wikipedia://en/371255), an important class of feedback control systems demonstrating [robust control](wikipedia://en/3282143). Such systems model some mechanical systems with Coulomb friction, and more recently genetic networks.

A. F. Filippov was awarded the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University)'s [Lomonosov Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Award) in 1993.
