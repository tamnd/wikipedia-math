# Section conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Section conjecture"}

In [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265), a branch of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **section conjecture** gives a conjectural description of the splittings of the [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) $\pi_1(X)\to \operatorname{Gal}(k)$, where $X$ is a complete [smooth curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_curve) of genus at least 2 over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) $k$ that is finitely generated over $\mathbb{Q}$, in terms of decomposition groups of rational points of $X$. The conjecture was introduced by (txt) in a 1983 letter to [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226).

:::
