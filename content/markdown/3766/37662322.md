# Brandt matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brandt matrix"}

In mathematics, **Brandt matrices** are [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859), introduced by (txt), that are related to the number of [ideals](wikipedia://en/25977) of given norm in an ideal class of a definite [quaternion algebra](wikipedia://en/2776890) over the [rationals](wikipedia://en/19727024), and that give a representation of the [Hecke algebra](wikipedia://en/55907189).

:::

(Eichler 1955) calculated the traces of the Brandt matrices.

Let *O* be an [order](wikipedia://en/1114171) in a quaternion algebra with [class number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_%28number_theory%29) *H*, and *I*<sub>i</sub>,...,*I*<sub>*H*</sub> invertible left *O*-ideals representing the classes. Fix an integer *m*. Let *e*<sub>*j*</sub> denote the number of units in the right order of *I*<sub>*j*</sub> and let *B*<sub>*ij*</sub> denote the number of α in *I*<sub>*j*</sub><sup>−1</sup>*I*<sub>*i*</sub> with [reduced norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_norm) N(α) equal to *m*N(*I*<sub>*i*</sub>)/N(*I*<sub>*j*</sub>). The Brandt matrix *B*(*m*) is the *H*×*H* matrix with entries *B*<sub>*ij*</sub>. Up to conjugation by a [permutation matrix](wikipedia://en/238849) it is independent of the choice of representatives *I*<sub>*j*</sub>; it is dependent only on the level of the order *O*.
