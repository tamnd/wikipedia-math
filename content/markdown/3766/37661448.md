# Katz–Lang finiteness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Katz–Lang finiteness theorem"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **Katz–Lang finiteness theorem**, proved by (txt), states that if *X* is a [smooth](wikipedia://en/35033819), [geometrically](wikipedia://en/62303294) connected [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) of [finite type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_of_finite_type) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *K* that is finitely generated over the [prime field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_field), and Ker(*X*/*K*) is the kernel of the maps between their [abelianized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelianization) [fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/11004), then Ker(*X*/*K*) is finite if *K* has characteristic 0, and the part of the kernel coprime to *p* is finite if *K* has characteristic *p* > 0.

:::
