# Peter Johnstone (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Johnstone"}

**Peter Tennant Johnstone** (born 28 December 1948) is Professor of the [Foundations of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and a fellow of [St. John's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge).[^o4] He invented or developed a broad range of fundamental ideas in [topos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topos_theory). His thesis, completed at the University of Cambridge in 1974, was entitled "Some Aspects of [Internal Category](wikipedia://en/31813104) Theory in an [Elementary Topos](wikipedia://en/815802)".[^o5]

:::

Peter Johnstone is a choral singer, having sung for over thirty years with the Cambridge University Musical Society and since 2004 with the (London) [Bach Choir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bach_Choir). Following a severe bout of [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) in 2020, he was invited by the Bach Choir's musical director [David Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hill_%28choral_director%29) to provide the text for a new choral work about the pandemic which the Choir commissioned from the composer [Richard Blackford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Blackford); the piece, \`Vision of a Garden', was performed at the Bach Choir's first post-lockdown concert in October 2021 in the Royal Festival Hall, London, and again in July 2023 in King's College Chapel, Cambridge.[^o6]

He is a great-great-great nephew of the Reverend [George Gilfillan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Gilfillan) who was eulogised in [William McGonagall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_McGonagall)'s first poem.[^o7]
