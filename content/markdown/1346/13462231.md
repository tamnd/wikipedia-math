# Clay Mathematics Monographs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clay Mathematics Monographs"}

**Clay Mathematics Monographs** is a series of expositions in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) co-published by [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) and [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655). Each volume in the series offers an exposition of an active area of current research, provided by a group of mathematicians.

:::
