# Map of lattices

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Map of lattices"}

"**26**. A semi-modular lattice is atomic." The concept of a [lattice](wikipedia://en/469304) arises in [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924), a branch of mathematics. The [Hasse diagram](wikipedia://en/338199) below depicts the inclusion relationships among some important subclasses of lattices.

:::

::: {#o3 .proof cue=heading name="Proofs of the relationships in the map"}

## Proofs of the relationships in the map {#Proofs_of_the_relationships_in_the_map}

\1. A [boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) is a [complemented](wikipedia://en/753521) [distributive lattice](wikipedia://en/310883). (def)

**2**. A boolean algebra is a [heyting algebra](wikipedia://en/309343).[^o4]

\3. A boolean algebra is [orthocomplemented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthocomplemented_lattice).[^o5]

\4. A distributive orthocomplemented lattice is [orthomodular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthomodular_lattice#Orthomodular_lattices).

**5**. A boolean algebra is orthomodular. (1,3,4)

**6**. An orthomodular lattice is orthocomplemented. (def)

**7**. An orthocomplemented lattice is complemented. (def)

**8**. A complemented lattice is bounded. (def)

**9**. An [algebraic lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_lattice) is complete. (def)

**10**. A [complete lattice](wikipedia://en/44777) is bounded.

**11**. A heyting algebra is bounded. (def)

**12**. A bounded lattice is a lattice. (def)

**13**. A heyting algebra is [residuated](wikipedia://en/2483542).

**14**. A residuated lattice is a lattice. (def)

**15**. A distributive lattice is modular.[^o6]

\16. A modular complemented lattice is relatively complemented.[^o7]

**17**. A boolean algebra is [relatively complemented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relatively_complemented_lattice). (1,15,16)

**18**. A relatively complemented lattice is a lattice. (def)

**19**. A heyting algebra is distributive.[^o8]

**20**. A [totally ordered set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_ordered_set) is a distributive lattice.

**21**. A [metric lattice](wikipedia://en/71519149) is [modular](wikipedia://en/1089311).[^o9]

**22**. A modular lattice is semi-modular.[^o10]

**23**. A [projective lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_lattice) is modular.[^o11]

**24**. A projective lattice is geometric. (def)

**25**. A [geometric lattice](wikipedia://en/17243191) is semi-modular.[^o12]

**26**. A semi-modular lattice is atomic.[^o13]Content error

**27**. An [atomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_%28order_theory%29) lattice is a lattice. (def)

**28**. A lattice is a semi-lattice. (def)

**29**. A [semi-lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-lattice) is a [partially ordered set](wikipedia://en/23572). (def)
