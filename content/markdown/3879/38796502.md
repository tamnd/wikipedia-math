# Infinite-order apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite-order apeirogonal tiling"}

The **infinite-order apeirogonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_plane). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {∞,∞}, which means it has [countably infinitely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countably_infinite) many [apeirogons](wikipedia://en/5658261) around all its ideal vertices.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents the fundamental domains of \*∞${}^{\infty}$ symmetry.

:::

## Uniform colorings {#Uniform_colorings}

This tiling can also be alternately colored in the \[(∞,∞,∞)\] symmetry from 3 generator positions.
