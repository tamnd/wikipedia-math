# Snub apeiroapeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub apeiroapeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub apeiroapeirogonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{∞,∞}. It has 3 equilateral triangles and 2 [apeirogons](wikipedia://en/5658261) around every vertex, with [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) 3.3.∞.3.∞.

:::
