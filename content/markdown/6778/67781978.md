# Irene Sciriha

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Irene Sciriha"}

**Irene Sciriha Aquilina** is a [Maltese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malta) mathematician specializing in [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914) and [chemical graph theory](wikipedia://en/14222708). A particular topic of her research has been the [singular graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_graph), graphs whose [adjacency matrix](wikipedia://en/244463) is a [singular matrix](wikipedia://en/245528), and the [nut graphs](wikipedia://en/83301535), singular graphs all of whose nontrivial [induced subgraphs](wikipedia://en/1655188) are non-singular. She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malta). She is a Fellow of the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sciriha studied mathematics at the [University of Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malta), earning bachelor's and master's degrees as the only woman studying mathematics or physics there at that time. She completed a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1998 at the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading) in England. Her dissertation, *On some aspects of graph spectra*, was jointly supervised by [Anthony Hilton](wikipedia://en/42746762) and Stanley Fiorini, and she also worked with Nash Williams, David Stirling and Peter Rowlinson.

Sciriha specializes in [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914) and [chemical graph theory](wikipedia://en/14222708). A particular topic of her research has been the [singular graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_graph), graphs whose [adjacency matrix](wikipedia://en/244463) is a [singular matrix](wikipedia://en/245528), and the [nut graphs](wikipedia://en/83301535), singular graphs all of whose nontrivial [induced subgraphs](wikipedia://en/1655188) are non-singular.

She began teaching at the University of Malta in 1971, and is a professor of mathematics there. She was convenor of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) from 2000 to 2001. She was also a representative for Malta on the Helsinki Group of the European Commission.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Sciriha is a Fellow of the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919). One of her students, chemist Martha Borg, won the Turner Prize at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield) for a doctoral dissertation co-advised by Sciriha and Patrick W. Fowler.
