# Carl Jensen Burrau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Jensen Burrau"}

*Image: Carl\_Burrau.jpg.*

**Carl Jensen Burrau** (29 July 1867 – 8 October 1944) was a Danish mathematician who worked on problems relating to physics and astronomy while also working as an actuary.

:::

Burrau was born in [Helsingör](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsingør) (Elsinore), Denmark and was educated at [Copenhagen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). He worked as an astronomy assistant at the university observatory from 1893 to 1898. He is known for his work on a three-body problem, examining the orbits of two equal masses revolving about each other.[^o2][^o3] His collaborations with [Törvald Thiele](wikipedia://en/701256) led to the so-called Thiele–Burrau method. His dissertation of 1895 examines methods of identifying constants from photographs of star positions using Bessel's classic method.[^o4][^o5] He then worked on actuarial mathematics, writing a book on the subject, *Forsikringsstatistikens Grundlag* (1925), and taught applied mathematics at Copenhagen university.[^o6][^o7]
