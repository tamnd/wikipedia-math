# Hal Schenck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hal Schenck"}

**Henry Koewing "Hal" Schenck** is an American mathematician, known for his work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He holds the Rosemary Kopel Brown Eminent Scholars Chair in mathematics at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Schenck attended [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) for his undergraduate degree.[^o5] After receiving his BS degree in 1986, he spent 4 years serving in the [United States Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army), leaving the service as a [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain).[^o5] He then went on to [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) for his graduate work. After an MS in 1994, he completed his PhD in mathematics in 1997.[^o5] His thesis was titled *Homological Methods in the Theory of Splines*, and was advised by [Michael Stillman](wikipedia://en/61705856).

## Career {#Career}

Following completion of his PhD, Schenck held postdoctoral appointments at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University), then at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o5] He moved to [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) in 2001, and was promoted to [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) there.[^o5] In 2007, he moved to the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois), where he was promoted to [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 2012.[^o5] In 2017, he moved to [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University), where he served as chair of the Department of Mathematics.[^o5] He was appointed as the Rosemary Kopel Brown Eminent Scholars Chair in Mathematics at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University) in 2019.[^o8]

Schenck has been (with [Catherine Yan](wikipedia://en/55706943)) one of the editors-in-chief of *[Advances in Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/30498353)* since 2018.[^o9] He was a founding editor (with [Jim Coykendall](wikipedia://en/70468783)) of the [Journal of Commutative Algebra](wikipedia://en/56125679).[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Schenck was elected as a [fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in 2020 for "contributions to research and exposition in applications of algebraic geometry and for service to the profession."[^o12]
