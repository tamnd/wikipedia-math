# Demographics of Andorra

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Andorra"}

This is a [demography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics) of the [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_%28human_biology%29) of [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra), including [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density), [ethnicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group), education level, health of the populace, economic status, religious affiliations and other aspects of the population.

:::

[Spaniards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaniards) form the largest [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity) in Andorra, followed by Andorrans, [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_people), and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people).[^o4]

The national language of the country is [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language), a [Romance language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romance_languages) in the [Western Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Romance) group, spoken by over 9 million people in nearby regions of [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain) and [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). While Catalan is the only official language, [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language) is particularly widespread, and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) and [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) are also commonly spoken due to [immigration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration) and geographic proximity. Most residents of Andorra are [multilingual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism), typically speaking Catalan and their [native language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language), as a significant portion of the population was born outside the country.[^o4]

The vast majority of the population lives in the seven urbanized [valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valley) that form Andorra's political districts, the seven *parròquies* (parishes):

- [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella)
- [Canillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canillo)
- [Encamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encamp)
- [Escaldes-Engordany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaldes-Engordany)
- [La Massana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Massana)
- [Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordino)
- [Sant Julià de Lòria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Julià_de_Lòria)

55% of the population are of foreign nationality, with only 45% holding the Andorran nationality.[^o5]
