# Mikhail Lavrentyev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Lavrentyev"}

**Mikhail Alekseyevich Lavrentyev** (or **Lavrentiev**, Михаи́л Алексе́евич Лавре́нтьев; November 19, 1900 – October 15, 1980) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [hydrodynamicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics).

:::

## Early years {#Early_years}

Lavrentyev was born in [Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan), where his father was an instructor at a college (he later became a professor at [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_University), then [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University)).

He entered [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_University), and, when his family moved to Moscow in 1921, he transferred to the Department of Physics and Mathematics of [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University). He graduated in 1922. He continued his studies in the university in 1923-26 as a graduate student of [Nikolai Luzin](wikipedia://en/576228).

Although Luzin was alleged to plagiarize in science and indulge in anti-Sovietism by some of his students in 1936, Lavrentyev did not participate in the notorious political persecution of his teacher which is known as the [Luzin case](wikipedia://en/576228#The_Luzin_affair_of_1936) or [Luzin affair](wikipedia://en/576228#The_Luzin_affair_of_1936). In fact Luzin was a friend of his father.[^o4]

## Mid career {#Mid_career}

In 1927, Lavrentyev spent half a year in France, collaborating with French mathematicians, and upon returned took up a position with Moscow University. Later he became a member of the staff of the [Steklov Institute](wikipedia://en/984494). His main contributions relate to [conformal mappings](wikipedia://en/50627) and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). [Mstislav Keldysh](wikipedia://en/2769461) was one of his students.

In 1939, [Oleksandr Bogomolets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Bogomolets), the president of the [Ukrainian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Academy_of_Sciences), asked Lavrentyev to become director of the [Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/36753361) at [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv).

One of Lavrentyev's scientific interests was the physics of [explosive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosion) processes, in which he had become involved when doing defense work during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II). A better understanding of the physics of explosions made it possible to use controlled explosions in construction, the best-known example being the construction of the [Medeu Mudflow Control Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medeu_Dam) outside of [Almaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaty) in [Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan).

## In Siberia {#In_Siberia}

Mikhail Lavrentyev was one of the main organizers and the first Chairman of the [Siberian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberia) Division of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (in his time the [Academy of Sciences of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_USSR)) from its founding in 1957 to 1975. The foundation of the Siberia's "Academic Town" [Akademgorodok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademgorodok) (now a district of [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk)) remains his most widely known achievement.

Six months after the decision to found the Siberian Division of the USSR Academy of Sciences Novosibirsk State University was established. The Decree of the [Council of Ministers of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_the_USSR) was signed January 9, 1958.[^o7] From 1959 to 1966 he was a professor at Novosibirsk State University.[^o8]

Lavrentyev was also a founder of the [Institute of Hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavrentyev_Institute_of_Hydrodynamics) of the Siberian Division of the Russian Academy of Sciences which since 1980 has been named after Lavrentyev.[^o9]

Lavrentyev was awarded the honorary title of [Hero of Socialist Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Socialist_Labour), a [Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) and 2 [Stalin Prizes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize), and a [Lomonosov Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Gold_Medal). He was elected a member of several world-renowned academies, and an honorable citizen of [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk).

Mikhail A. Lavrentyev's son, also named Mikhail (Mikhail M. Lavrentyev, 1930-2010), also became a mathematician and was a member of the leadership of Akademgorodok.[^o10]
