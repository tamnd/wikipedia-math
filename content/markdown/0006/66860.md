# List of things named after Gottfried Leibniz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Gottfried Leibniz"}

**[Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281)** (1646–1716) was a German philosopher and mathematician.

:::

In **engineering**, the following concepts are attributed to Leibniz:

::: {#o2 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz wheel"}

- [Leibniz wheel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_wheel), a cylinder used in a class of mechanical calculators

:::

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz calculator"}

- [Leibniz calculator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stepped_reckoner), a digital mechanical calculator based on the Leibniz wheel

:::

In **mathematics**, several results and concepts are named after Leibniz:

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz algebra"}

- [Leibniz algebra](wikipedia://en/5365466), an [algebraic structure](wikipedia://en/106364)

:::

  - [Dual Leibniz algebra](wikipedia://en/36166886)

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Madhava–Leibniz series"}

- [Madhava–Leibniz series](wikipedia://en/26149716)

:::

  - [Leibniz formula for π](wikipedia://en/1301559), an inefficient method for calculating π

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz formula for determinants"}

- [Leibniz formula for determinants](wikipedia://en/5361510), an expression for the determinant of a matrix

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz harmonic triangle"}

- [Leibniz harmonic triangle](wikipedia://en/9845792)

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz integral rule"}

- [Leibniz integral rule](wikipedia://en/2558855), a rule for differentiation under the integral sign

:::

  - [Leibniz–Reynolds transport theorem](wikipedia://en/3488128), a generalization of the Leibniz integral rule

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz's linear differential equation"}

- [Leibniz's linear differential equation](wikipedia://en/32742753#Linear_to_the_nth_order_equations), a first-order, linear, inhomogeneous differential equation

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz's notation"}

- [Leibniz's notation](wikipedia://en/410712), a notation in calculus

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz operator"}

- [Leibniz operator](wikipedia://en/4827691), a concept in abstract logic

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz law"}

- *[Leibniz law](wikipedia://en/35889821)*, see [product rule](wikipedia://en/248988) of calculus

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz rule"}

- [Leibniz rule](wikipedia://en/248988), a formula used to find the derivatives of products of two or more functions

:::

  - [General Leibniz rule](wikipedia://en/1116115), a generalization of the product rule

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz's test"}

- [Leibniz's test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz's_test), also known as Leibniz's rule or Leibniz's criterion

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Newton–Leibniz axiom"}

- [Newton–Leibniz axiom](wikipedia://en/19468696#Second_part)

:::

In **philosophy**, the following concepts are attributed to Leibniz:

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz's gap"}

- [Leibniz's gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz's_gap), a problem in the philosophy of mind

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz's law"}

- [Leibniz's law](wikipedia://en/630398), an ontological principle about objects' properties

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="5149 Leibniz"}

- [5149 Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5149_Leibniz), an asteroid

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Bibliothek"}

:::

- [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize), a German research prize

- [Leibnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibnitz_%28crater%29), a lunar crater

:::

:::

::: {#o28 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz Institute for Science and Mathematics Education at the University of Kiel"}

- [Leibniz Institute for Science and Mathematics Education at the University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_Institute_for_Science_and_Mathematics_Education_at_the_University_of_Kiel), a scientific institute in the field of Education Research

:::

::: {#o29 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz Institute for Solid State and Materials Research"}

- [Leibniz Institute for Solid State and Materials Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_Institute_for_Solid_State_and_Materials_Research), a research institute in the Leibniz Association

:::

::: {#o30 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz Society of North America"}

- [Leibniz Society of North America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_Society_of_North_America), a philosophical society whose purpose is to promote the study of the philosophy of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz

:::

::: {#o31 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz-Keks"}

- [Leibniz-Keks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz-Keks), a German brand of biscuit, although the only connection is that Leibniz lived in Hannover, where the manufacturer is based.[^o32]

:::

::: {#o33 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz–Clarke correspondence"}

- [Leibniz–Clarke correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz–Clarke_correspondence), Leibniz' debate with the English philosopher Samuel Clarke

:::

::: {#o34 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leibniz–Newton calculus controversy"}

- [Leibniz–Newton calculus controversy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz–Newton_calculus_controversy), the debate over whether Leibniz or Isaac Newton invented calculus
