# Alexander Dynin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Dynin"}

**Alexander S. Dynin** (Александр С. Дынин, born 1936) is a Russian mathematician at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) who introduced the [Agranovich–Dynin formula](wikipedia://en/35117697). Since 2009, Dynin claims to have proved the [Yang-Mills Millennium Problem](wikipedia://en/2393975).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
