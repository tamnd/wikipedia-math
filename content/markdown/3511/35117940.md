# Mikhail Agranovich (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Agranovich"}

**Mikhail Semyonovich Agranovich** (Михаил Семёнович Агранович; January 4, 1931, [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) – February 14, 2017[^o2]) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) who introduced the [Agranovich–Dynin formula](wikipedia://en/35117697).

:::
