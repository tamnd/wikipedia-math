# Richard White (mathematician)

the mathematician

*Image: White - Hemisphaerium dissectum, 1648 - 849329 (cropped).jpg.* Title page of the 1648 *Hemisphaerium Dissectum*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard White"}

**Richard White** (Ricardus Albius; 1590 – 1682) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist).

:::

A [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church) from [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex), he studied under [Benedetto Castelli](wikipedia://en/855653) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa) and lived mostly in [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy). His brother, [Thomas White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_White_%28scholar%29) was a Roman Catholic theologian and scholar. He married Catherine Weston, daughter of the [Earl of Portland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Weston,_1st_Earl_of_Portland).[^o2]

In the preface of his book *Hemisphaerium Dissectum*, printed in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) with the [Inquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inquisition)'s permission, he wrote with great admiration of [Galileo Galilei](wikipedia://en/29688374).[^o3] He also had with Galileo a friendly correspondence by letter.[^o4]

White was proposed for election to the [Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in June 1661, and was elected on 26 June 1661. However, he was not re-elected after the second charter in 1663 and is not listed as an original fellow of the Royal Society.[^o5]
