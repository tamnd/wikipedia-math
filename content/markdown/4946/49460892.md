# Onofrio Puglisi

*Image: Puglisi\_-\_Aritmetica,\_1714\_-\_4660310.tif.* *Aritmetica*, title page (1714)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Onofrio Puglisi"}

**Onofrio Puglisi**, also known as **Onofrio Pugliesi Sbernia** (died on 11 January 1679), was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo).[^o2]

:::

He was the first writer of accounting books in southern Italy.[^o3]
