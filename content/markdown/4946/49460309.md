# Jan Vaerman

*Image: Portret van Jan Vaerman.jpg.* Frontispiece from Vaerman's Academia Mathematica of Oeffen-school van de Wis-konst

*Image: Vaerman - Academia Mathematica of Oeffen-school van de Wiskonst, verdeelt in twee boecken, 1719 - 4741367.tif.* *Academia Mathematica of Oeffen-school vande Wis-konst* (1719)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Vaerman"}

**Jan Vaerman** (1653–1731)[^o2] was a [Flemish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_people) mathematician.[^o3][^o4]

:::

He worked as a school teacher first in [Bruges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruges) and then, from 1693 to 1717, in [Tielt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tielt). He wrote about [French grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_grammar), [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261) and [planimetrics](wikipedia://en/1154830).[^o5]
