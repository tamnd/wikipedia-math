# Yehoshua Bar-Hillel

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Yehoshua Bar-Hillel"}

**Yehoshua Bar-Hillel** (יהושע בר-הלל; 8 September 1915 – 25 September 1975) was an Israeli [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [linguist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics). He was a pioneer in the fields of [machine translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_translation) and [formal linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_linguistics).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Born **Oscar Westreich** in Vienna, [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary), he was raised in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin). In 1933 he emigrated to [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) with the [Bnei Akiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bnei_Akiva) youth movement, and briefly joined the [kibbutz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibbutz) [Tirat Zvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirat_Zvi) before settling in Jerusalem and marrying Shulamith.

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), he served in the [Jewish Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Brigade) of the British Army. He fought with the [Haganah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haganah) during the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab–Israeli_War), losing an eye.

Bar-Hillel received his PhD in Philosophy from the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) where he also studied mathematics under [Abraham Fraenkel](wikipedia://en/528451), with whom he eventually coauthored *Foundations of Set Theory* (1958, 1973).

## Career {#Career}

Bar-Hillel was a major disciple of [Rudolf Carnap](wikipedia://en/57987), whose *Logical Syntax of Language* much influenced him. He began a correspondence with Carnap in the 1940s, which led to a 1950 post-doctorate under Carnap at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and to his collaborating on Carnap's 1952 *An Outline of the Theory of Semantic Information*.

Bar-Hillel then took up a position at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), where he was the first academic to work full-time in the field of [machine translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_translation). Bar-Hillel organised the first International Conference on Machine Translation in 1952. Later he expressed doubts that general-purpose fully automatic high-quality machine translation would ever be feasible. He was also a pioneer in the field of [information retrieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_retrieval).

In 1953, Bar-Hillel joined the [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) department at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University), where he taught until his death at age 60. His teachings and writings strongly influenced an entire generation of Israeli philosophers and linguists, including [Asa Kasher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa_Kasher) and [Avishai Margalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avishai_Margalit). In 1953, he founded a pioneering algebraic-computational linguistic group, and in 1961 he contributed to the proof of the [pumping lemma](wikipedia://en/24449) for [context-free languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context-free_languages) (sometimes called the Bar-Hillel lemma). Bar-Hillel helped found the Hebrew University's department of [Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_Science). From 1966 to 1969 Bar-Hillel presided over the Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science of the [International Union of History and Philosophy of Science](wikipedia://en/33072433).

Bar-Hillel's daughter [Maya Bar-Hillel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Bar-Hillel) is a [cognitive psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_psychology) at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University), known for her collaborations with [Amos Tversky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_Tversky) and for her role in critiquing [Bible code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible_code) study. His other daughter, Mira Bar-Hillel, is a freelance journalist who has worked for the London *[Evening Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evening_Standard)*. His granddaughter, [Gili Bar-Hillel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gili_Bar-Hillel), is the Hebrew translator of the [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) series of books.
