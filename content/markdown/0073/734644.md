# List of axioms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of axioms"}

This is a **list of [axioms](wikipedia://en/928)** as that term is understood in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), the word *axiom* is understood differently; see [axiom](wikipedia://en/928) and [self-evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-evidence). Individual axioms are almost always part of a larger [axiomatic system](wikipedia://en/188401).

:::

## ZF (the [Zermelo–Fraenkel axioms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zermelo–Fraenkel_axioms) without the axiom of choice) {#ZF_(the_Zermelo–Fraenkel_axioms_without_the_axiom_of_choice)}

*Together with the axiom of choice (see below), these are the* [de facto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_facto) *standard axioms for contemporary [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) or [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). They can be easily adapted to analogous theories, such as [mereology](wikipedia://en/375380).*
