# Reeh–Schlieder theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reeh–Schlieder theorem"}

The **Reeh–Schlieder theorem** is a result in relativistic [local quantum field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_quantum_field_theory) published by [Helmut Reeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_Reeh) and [Siegfried Schlieder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegfried_Schlieder) in 1961.

:::

The theorem states that the [vacuum state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_state) $\vert \Omega \rangle$ is a [cyclic vector](wikipedia://en/41392084#Definition) for the field algebra $\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})$ corresponding to any open set $\mathcal{O}$ in [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space). That is, any state $\vert \psi \rangle$ can be approximated to arbitrary precision by acting on the vacuum with an operator selected from the local algebra, even for $\vert \psi \rangle$ that contain excitations arbitrarily far away in space. In this sense, states created by applying elements of the local algebra to the vacuum state are not localized to the region $\mathcal{O}$.

For practical purposes, however, local operators still generate quasi-local states. More precisely, the long range effects of the operators of the local algebra will diminish rapidly with distance, as seen by the cluster properties of the [Wightman functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wightman_functions). And with increasing distance, creating a unit vector localized outside the region requires operators of ever increasing [operator norm](wikipedia://en/246722).[^o2]

This theorem is also cited in connection with [quantum entanglement](wikipedia://en/25336). But it is subject to some doubt whether the Reeh–Schlieder theorem can usefully be seen as the [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) analog to [quantum entanglement](wikipedia://en/25336), since the [exponentially-increasing](wikipedia://en/191933) energy needed for long range actions will prohibit any macroscopic effects. However, Benni Reznik showed that vacuum entanglement can be distilled into EPR pairs used in quantum information tasks.[^o3]

It is known that the Reeh–Schlieder property applies not just to the vacuum but in fact to any state with bounded energy.[^o4] If some finite number *N* of space-like separated regions is chosen, the [multipartite entanglement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multipartite_entanglement) can be analyzed in the typical [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) setting of *N* abstract quantum systems, each with a Hilbert space possessing a countable basis, and the corresponding structure has been called *superentanglement*.[^o5]
