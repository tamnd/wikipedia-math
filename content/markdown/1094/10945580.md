# Schwarz integral formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schwarz integral formula"}

In [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Schwarz integral formula**, named after [Hermann Schwarz](wikipedia://en/3165834), allows one to recover a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110), [up to](wikipedia://en/44787) an imaginary constant, from the boundary values of its real part.

:::

## Unit disc {#Unit_disc}

Let *f* be a function holomorphic on the closed unit disc {*z* ∈ **C** | |*z*| ≤ 1}. Then

$$f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \oint_{|\zeta| = 1} \frac{\zeta + z}{\zeta - z} \operatorname{Re}(f(\zeta)) \, \frac{d\zeta}{\zeta}+ i\operatorname{Im}(f(0))$$

for all |*z*| \< 1.

## Upper half-plane {#Upper_half-plane}

Let *f* be a function holomorphic on the closed [upper half-plane](wikipedia://en/287826) {*z* ∈ **C** | Im(*z*) ≥ 0} such that, for some *α* > 0, |*z*<sup>*α*</sup> *f*(*z*)| is bounded on the closed upper half-plane. Then

$$f(z) = \frac{1}{\pi i} \int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{u(\zeta,0)}{\zeta - z} \, d\zeta = \frac{1}{\pi i} \int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{\operatorname{Re}(f)(\zeta)}{\zeta - z} \, d\zeta$$

for all Im(*z*) > 0.

Note that, as compared to the version on the unit disc, this formula does not have an arbitrary constant added to the integral; this is because the additional decay condition makes the conditions for this formula more stringent.

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/corollary cue=named-heading name="Corollary of Poisson integral formula"}

## Corollary of Poisson integral formula {#Corollary_of_Poisson_integral_formula}

The formula follows from [Poisson integral formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_integral_formula) applied to *u*:[^o6][^o7]

$$u(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} u(e^{i\psi}) \operatorname{Re} {e^{i\psi} + z \over e^{i\psi} - z} \, d\psi, \quad |z| < 1.$$

By means of conformal maps, the formula can be generalized to any [simply connected](wikipedia://en/523879) open set.

:::
