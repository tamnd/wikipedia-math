# Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski"}

**Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski** (; 7 October 1926 – 18 September 2015) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Born in [Wilno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilno), [Second Polish Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic) (now Vilnius, [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania)), he was a student of [Hugo Steinhaus](wikipedia://en/71735). At the age of 26 he became professor at [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University). In 1959, he became a professor at the [Wrocław University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University_of_Technology). He was the advisor of 18 PhD theses.[^o4] His main research areas were [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). Several theorems bear his name: the [Ryll-Nardzewski fixed point theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryll-Nardzewski_fixed_point_theorem),[^o5] the [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](wikipedia://en/26628083)[^o6][^o7] in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858#Categoricity), and the [Kuratowski and Ryll-Nardzewski measurable selection theorem](wikipedia://en/46310740).[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12]

He became a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1967.[^o13] He died in 2015 at the age of 88[^o13][^o14] and he is buried in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław) at Grabiszyński Cemetery.[^o15]
