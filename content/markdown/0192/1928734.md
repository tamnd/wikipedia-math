# Hans Carl Friedrich von Mangoldt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Carl Friedrich von Mangoldt"}

**Hans Carl Friedrich von Mangoldt** (18 May 1854 – 27 October 1925) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed to the solution of the [prime number theorem](wikipedia://en/23692).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Mangoldt completed his Doctorate of Philosophy (Ph.D) in 1878 at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), where his supervisors were [Ernst Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117) and [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459). He contributed to the solution of the prime number theorem by providing rigorous proofs of two statements in [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980)'s seminal paper "[On the Number of Primes Less Than a Given Magnitude](wikipedia://en/536817)". Riemann himself had only given partial proofs of these statements. Mangoldt worked as professor at the [RWTH Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen) and was succeeded by [Otto Blumenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Blumenthal).
