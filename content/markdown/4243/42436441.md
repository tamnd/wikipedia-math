# Olli Lehto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olli Lehto"}

*Image: Olli-Lehto-1962.jpg.* Olli Lehto with a computer in 1962.

**Olli Erkki Lehto** (30 May 1925 in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) — 31 December 2020[^o2]) was a Finnish mathematician, specializing in [geometric function theory](wikipedia://en/5458932), and a [chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_%28education%29) of the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki).

:::

Lehto earned his PhD in 1949 from the University of Helsinki under [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna) with thesis *Anwendung orthogonaler Systeme auf gewisse funktionentheoretische Extremal- und Abbildungsprobleme*. At the University of Helsinki, Lehto was from 1961 to 1988 a professor, from 1978 the dean of science, from 1983 the [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academic%29), and from 1988 to 1993 the chancellor.

From 1983 to 1990 he was Secretary of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868). In 1962 he became a member of the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) (Suomalainen Tiedeakatemia). [^o5] In 1968 he was elected member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) and in 1988 he became honorary member of the same society.[^o6] He was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) from 1986.[^o7] In 1975 he was given by the President of Finland the honorary title "Academician of Science" (Tieteen akateemikko). Lehto was the chief organizer of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Helsinki in 1978 and an invited speaker of the ICM in Moscow in 1966 with lecture *Quasiconformal mappings in the plane*. He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

In 2001 Lehto published a biography of his mentor Rolf Nevanlinna.[^o8]
