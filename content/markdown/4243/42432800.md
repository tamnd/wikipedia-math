# Noncommutative symmetric function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncommutative symmetric function"}

In mathematics, the **noncommutative symmetric functions** form a [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) NSymm analogous to the [Hopf algebra of symmetric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopf_algebra_of_symmetric_functions). The Hopf algebra NSymm was introduced by [Israel M. Gelfand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_M._Gelfand), Daniel Krob, [Alain Lascoux](wikipedia://en/32555229), Bernard Leclerc, [Vladimir Retakh](wikipedia://en/54470548), and Jean-Yves Thibon.[^o2] It is noncommutative but cocommutative graded Hopf algebra. It has the Hopf algebra of [symmetric functions](wikipedia://en/22510359) as a quotient, and is a subalgebra of the [Hopf algebra of permutations](wikipedia://en/42350895), and is the graded dual of the Hopf algebra of [quasisymmetric function](wikipedia://en/29661763). Over the rational numbers it is isomorphic as a Hopf algebra to the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of the free Lie algebra on countably many variables.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The underlying algebra of the Hopf algebra of noncommutative symmetric functions is the free ring **Z**⟨*Z*<sub>1</sub>, *Z*<sub>2</sub>,...⟩ generated by non-commuting variables *Z*<sub>1</sub>, *Z*<sub>2</sub>, ...

The coproduct takes *Z*<sub>*n*</sub> to Σ *Z*<sub>*i*</sub> ⊗ *Z*<sub>*n*–*i*</sub>, where *Z*<sub>0</sub> = 1 is the identity.

The counit takes *Z*<sub>*i*</sub> to 0 for *i* > 0 and takes *Z*<sub>0</sub> = 1 to 1.

:::

## Related notions {#Related_notions}

Michiel Hazewinkel showed[^o6] that a [Hasse–Schmidt derivation](wikipedia://en/60921299)

$$D: A \to A [[t]]$$

on a ring *A* is equivalent to an action of NSymm on *A*: the part $D_i : A \to A$ of *D* which picks the coefficient of $t^i$, is the action of the indeterminate *Z*<sub>*i*</sub>.

### Relation to free Lie algebra {#Relation_to_free_Lie_algebra}

The element Σ *Z*<sub>*n*</sub>*t*<sup>*n*</sup> is a [group-like element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group-like_element) of the Hopf algebra of formal [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) over NSymm, so over the rationals its logarithm is primitive. The coefficients of its logarithm generate the free Lie algebra on a countable set of generators over the rationals. Over the rationals this identifies the Hopf algebra NSYmm with the universal enveloping algebra of the free Lie algebra.
