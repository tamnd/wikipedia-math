# Dieter Held

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dieter Held"}

*Image: Dieter\_Held.jpg.* Dieter Held (1999)

**Dieter Held** (born 1936 in Berlin) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2] He is known for discovering the [Held group](wikipedia://en/1110818), one of the 26 sporadic [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Held was a speaker at the 1962 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o5] He earned his Ph.D. in 1964 from [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt), under the supervision of [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114).[^o6] From June 1965 to October 1967 Held first was lecturer at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University) till July 1966 and then lecturer at Monash University, Clayton, Victoria.[^o2] After having resigned from his position at Monash University, he returned to Germany and took up a research fellowship from the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG).[^o2] The discovery of the Held group occurred towards the end of 1968 after he had investigated the properties of an arbitrary finite simple group having a centralizer of an involution isomorphic to that of the centralizer of an involution in the center of a Sylow 2-subgroup of the [Mathieu group](wikipedia://en/380207) M<sub>24</sub> on 24 letters.[^o7][^o8] Shortly afterwards [Graham Higman](wikipedia://en/2600152) and John McKay demonstrated that such a group exists, using a computer. This demonstration has not been published. There is a much later paper by Jörg Hrabe de Angelis showing the existence and uniqueness of the Held group.[^o9]

Up to 2001, Held was professor at the Mathematics Institute of Gutenberg University in Mainz.[^o2]
