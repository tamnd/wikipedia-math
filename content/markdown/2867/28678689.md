::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stericated 6-simplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **stericated 6-simplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728) with 4th order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication)) of the regular [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex).

:::

There are 8 unique sterications for the 6-simplex with permutations of truncations, cantellations, and runcinations.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Small cellated heptapeton (Acronym: scal) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *stericated 6-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 7-space as permutations of (0,0,1,1,1,1,2). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [stericated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stericated_7-orthoplex).

## Related uniform 6-polytopes {#Related_uniform_6-polytopes}

The stericated 6-simplexes are in a set of 35 [uniform 6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821728) based on the \[3,3,3,3,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004), all shown here in A<sub>6</sub> [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052).
