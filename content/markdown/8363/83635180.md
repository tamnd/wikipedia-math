# Igor Prünster

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Igor Prünster"}

**Igor Prünster** is an Italian statistican who studies [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571), in particular Bayesian nonparametrics and Bayesian asymptotics. He is professor of Statistics at [Bocconi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocconi_University). As of 2026, he is the editor-in-chief of the academic journal *[Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/39096238)*. He was the president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2021.

:::

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 2015: Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o4]
- 2018: Fellow of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312)[^o5]
- 2019: Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)[^o6]
