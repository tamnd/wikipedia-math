# Ulrich Stuhler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrich Stuhler"}

**Ulrich Stuhler** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He currently is a professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He is known for his contributions to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905). In 1993, he—along with [Gérard Laumon](wikipedia://en/22565856) and [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548)—proved the [local Langlands conjectures](wikipedia://en/7676971) for the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564) GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*) for positive characteristic [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375) *K*.[^o3]

:::

An alumnus of the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), Stuhler earned his doctorate under supervision of [Martin Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650) in 1970.
