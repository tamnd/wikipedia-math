# Adolph Jensen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adolph Jensen"}

**Adolph Ludvig Otto Jensen** (15 July 1866 – 24 May 1948) was an economist and statistician of international standing, and from 1913 to 1936 the head of the Statistics Department of the [Danish Ministry of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_%28Denmark%29).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Jensen studied Politics at [Århus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Århus_University), 1885–1892, under [Harald Westergaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Westergaard).[^o3] From 1896 to 1936, he worked at the Department of Statistics of the Danish Ministry of Finance, becoming head of the department in 1913.[^o3] He took an active part in international research into statistics, as well as statistical research into economic and social issues.

## Publications {#Publications}

- The chapter on "The Scandinavian Nations" in volume 4 of *[A History of Banking in all the Leading Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_History_of_Banking_in_all_the_Leading_Nations)* (1896; reprinted 1971).
- "The History and Development of Statistics in Denmark", in *The History of Statistics*, collected and edited by John Koren (Published for the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) by the [Macmillan Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macmillan_Company) of New York, 1918), pp. 201–214.[^o6]
- "Report on the Representative Method in Statistics", *[Bulletin de l'Institut International de Statistique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_de_l'Institut_International_de_Statistique)* 22 (1925), 359–380
- "Purposive Selection", *[Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143)* 91:4 (1928), 541–547.
- "Horoscope of the Population of Denmark", *Bulletin de l'Institut International de Statistique*, 25 (1931), 41–49.
- "Migration Statistics of Denmark, Norway and Sweden", in *International Migrations, Volume II: Interpretations*, ed. Walter F. Willcox, National Bureau of Economic Research, 1931.
- *Befolkningsspørgsmaalet i Danmark*, 1939.
- *Tallenes Tale*, 1941.
