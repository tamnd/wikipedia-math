# J. Peter May

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="J. Peter May"}

**Jon Peter May** (born September 16, 1939) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the fields of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), and the foundational aspects of [spectra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_%28homotopy_theory%29). He is known, in particular, for the [May spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/14337630) and for coining the term [operad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operad_theory).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

May received a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) degree from [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College) in 1960 and a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1964. His thesis, written under the direction of [John Moore](wikipedia://en/13433862), was titled *The [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of restricted [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) and of [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714): Application to the [Steenrod algebra](wikipedia://en/2146220)*.

From 1964 to 1967, May taught at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). He has been a faculty member at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) since 1967, and a professor since 1970.

The word "[operad](wikipedia://en/3126358)" was created by May as a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of "operations" and "[monad](wikipedia://en/355140)".[^o5]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012 he became an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] He has advised over 60 doctoral students, including [Mark Behrens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Behrens), [Andrew Blumberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Blumberg), [Dan Isaksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Isaksen), [Frederick Cohen](wikipedia://en/71505731), [Ib Madsen](wikipedia://en/38416618), [Mona Merling](wikipedia://en/79646095), [Emily Riehl](wikipedia://en/56785970), [Michael Shulman](wikipedia://en/45211795), and [Zhouli Xu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhouli_Xu).[^o4] May received the 2026 AMS Elias M. Stein Mentoring Award for his exceptional mentoring.[^o8]
