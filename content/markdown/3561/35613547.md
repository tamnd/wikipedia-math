# Jean Ladrière

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Ladrière"}

Jean Ladrière **Jean Ladrière** (September 7, 1921 – November 26, 2007)[^o2] was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), born in [Nivelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nivelles). He was professor at the [University of Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain) (UCLouvain)[^o3] from 1959 to 1986, where he was chair of the [Higher Institute of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Institute_of_Philosophy) from 1977 to 1985.[^o4]

:::

The overall project animating his work (650 scientific articles, partially assembled into a dozen volumes) was to develop, in his words, "a report that is not a simple confrontation, but a justified report to the both reflected and lived between the [(Christian) faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_faith) and [reason](wikipedia://en/42446)," especially scientific and philosophical reason.

He died in [Ottignies-Louvain-la-Neuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottignies-Louvain-la-Neuve).
