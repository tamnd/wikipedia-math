# Robert Megginson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Megginson"}

**Robert Eugene Megginson** is an American mathematician, the Arthur F. Thurnau Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o3] His research concerns [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989);[^o6] he is the author of the textbook *An Introduction to Banach Space Theory* ([GTM 183](wikipedia://en/7392980), Springer, 1998).[^o4]

:::

Megginson was born in 1948 in [Washington, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_Illinois), of [Oglala Sioux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oglala_Sioux) heritage on his mother's side,[^o6] and grew up in [Sheldon, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon,_Illinois), where his father was mayor.[^o5] He earned a degree in physics from the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) in 1969, and became a software specialist for the [Roper Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roper_Technologies) until 1977, when he returned to graduate school.[^o6] He earned a master's degree in statistics in 1983,[^o7] He completed his Ph.D. in 1984 at the University of Illinois, with a thesis on [normed vector spaces](wikipedia://en/21538) supervised by [Mahlon Marsh Day](wikipedia://en/71432032). This accomplishment made him one of only approximately 12 [Native Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States) to hold a doctorate in mathematics, and he has taken great interest in underrepresented minorities in mathematics.[^o6]

Because his wife was employed nearby in [Decatur, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decatur,_Illinois),[^o5] Megginson took a teaching position in 1983, joining the faculty of [Eastern Illinois University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Illinois_University) as an assistant professor, rather than doing postdoctoral research. He moved to the University of Michigan in 1992, was on leave as the deputy director of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) from 2002 to 2004, and became the Thurnau Professor at Michigan in 2008.[^o7]

Megginson won the U.S. Presidential Award for Excellence in Science, Mathematics, and Engineering Mentoring in 1997.[^o9] The [American Indian Science and Engineering Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Indian_Science_and_Engineering_Society) gave him their Ely S. Parker Award for lifetime service to the Native American community in 1999.[^o7] The [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) elected him as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) in 2009,[^o10] and in the same year the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) gave him their Yueh-Gin Gung and Dr. Charles Y. Hu Award for Distinguished Service, for his work on underrepresented minorities.[^o11] In 2012, Megginson became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]
