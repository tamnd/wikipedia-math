# Tits group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tits group"}

In [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Tits group** <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2)′, named for [Jacques Tits](wikipedia://en/219570) (), is a finite [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

17,971,200 = 2<sup>11</sup> · 3<sup>3</sup> · 5<sup>2</sup> · 13.

This is the only simple group that is a [derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_group) of a [group of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) that is not a group of Lie type in any series from exceptional isomorphisms. It is sometimes considered a 27th [sporadic group](wikipedia://en/324806).

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

The [Ree groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ree_groups) <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2<sup>2*n*+1</sup>) were constructed by (Ree 1961), who showed that they are simple if *n* ≥ 1. The first member <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2) of this series is not simple. It was studied by (txt) who showed that it is [almost simple](wikipedia://en/25027899), its [derived subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_subgroup) <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2)′ of index 2 being a new simple group, now called the Tits group. The group <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2) is a [group of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) and has a [BN pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BN_pair), but the Tits group itself does not have a BN pair. The Tits group is member of the infinite family <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2<sup>2*n*+1</sup>)′ of commutator groups of the Ree groups, and thus by definition not sporadic. But because it is also not strictly a group of Lie type, it is sometimes regarded as a 27th [sporadic group](wikipedia://en/324806).[^o5]

The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) of the Tits group is trivial and its [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2, with the full automorphism group being the group <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2).

The Tits group occurs as a maximal subgroup of the [Fischer group Fi<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/36210170). The group <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2) also occurs as a maximal subgroup of the [Rudvalis group](wikipedia://en/1196760), as the point stabilizer of the [rank-3 permutation action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank-3_permutation_action) on 4060 = 1 + 1755 + 2304 points.

The Tits group is one of the [simple N-groups](wikipedia://en/29504243), and was not included in [John G. Thompson](wikipedia://en/862189)'s first announcement of the classification of simple *N*-groups, as it had not been discovered at the time. It is also one of the [thin finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin_finite_group).

The Tits group was characterized in various ways by (txt) and (Stroth 1980).

## Presentation {#Presentation}

The Tits group can be defined in terms of generators and relations by

$$a^2 = b^3 = (ab)^{13} = [a, b]^5 = [a, bab]^4 = ((ab)^4 ab^{-1})^6 = 1, \,$$

where \[*a*, *b*\] is the [commutator](wikipedia://en/7193) *a*<sup>−1</sup>*b*<sup>−1</sup>*ab*. It has an [outer automorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_automorphism) obtained by sending (*a*, *b*) to (*a*, *b*(*ba*)<sup>5</sup>*b*(*ba*)<sup>5</sup>).
