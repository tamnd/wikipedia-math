# Heinrich Brandt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Brandt"}

*Image: Brandt,Heinrich 1930 Jena.jpg.* Heinrich Brandt, Oct 1930 in Jena

**Heinrich Brandt** (8 November 1886, in [Feudingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feudingen) – 9 October 1954, in [Halle, Saxony-Anhalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle,_Saxony-Anhalt)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was the first to develop the concept of a [groupoid](wikipedia://en/12543).

:::

Brandt studied at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and, from 1910 to 1913, at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). In 1912 he attained his doctorate; he was a student of [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615). From 1913 he was assistant at the [University of Karlsruhe (TH)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe_%28TH%29). He taught [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from 1921 at [RWTH Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen). From 1930 he was the chair for mathematics at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle).

A [Brandt matrix](wikipedia://en/37662322) is a computational way of describing the [Hecke operator](wikipedia://en/521837) action on [theta series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_series) as [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000). The theory was developed in part by Brandt's student [Martin Eichler](wikipedia://en/4224478). It offers an algorithmic approach for machine computation (in that theta series span spaces of modular forms); the theory is now considered by means of [Brandt module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandt_module).
