# Carlo Emilio Bonferroni

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Carlo Emilio Bonferroni"}

**Carlo Emilio Bonferroni** (28 January 1892 – 18 August 1960) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).[^o4] Bonferroni is best known for the [Bonferroni inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonferroni_inequalities) (a generalization of the [union bound](wikipedia://en/344887)), and for the [Bonferroni correction](wikipedia://en/7838811) in statistics, which he did not invent, but which is related to the Bonferroni inequalities.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bonferroni studied piano and conducting in [Turin Conservatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin_Conservatory) and a studied mathematics at [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin)[^o6] under [Giuseppe Peano](wikipedia://en/13043) and [Corrado Segre](wikipedia://en/2422061), where he obtained his [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea). During this time he also studied at [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). During World War I, he was an officer among the engineers. Bonferroni held a position as assistant professor at the [Polytechnic University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Turin), and in 1923 took up the chair of financial mathematics at the Economics Institute of the [University of Bari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bari). In 1933 he transferred to [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence), where he held his chair until his death.

He first discussed the concept of Bonferroni inequalities in his 1935 paper *"Il calcolo delle assicurazioni su gruppi di teste"*.
