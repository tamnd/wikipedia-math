# Sacramental index

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sacramental index"}

The **sacramental index** is a [statistic](wikipedia://en/26703) sometimes used by [Roman Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic) [bishops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop#Catholic,_Eastern_Orthodox,_Oriental_Orthodox,_Lutheran_and_Anglican_churches) as a rough approximation of how active a [parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parish) is, based on the occurrence of [sacraments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrament) or [rites of passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rite_of_passage). The precise formula varies; in the [Archdiocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese) of [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts), the sacramental index for a parish is calculated by adding the total numbers of [baptisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptism) and [funerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funeral), and twice the number of [weddings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_view_of_marriage).[^o3]

:::

The index is used to help determine the number of priests assigned to a parish.
