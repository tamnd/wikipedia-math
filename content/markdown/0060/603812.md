# Message forgery

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Message forgery"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **message forgery** is sending a message so to deceive the recipient about the actual sender's identity. A common example is sending a [spam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-mail_spam) or prank e-mail as if it were originated from an [address other than the one which was really used](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email_spoofing).

:::

## Notable Incidents {#Notable_Incidents}

### 2014 Sony Pictures hack {#2014_Sony_Pictures_hack}

In November 2014, the multinational entertainment studio, [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures), suffered a huge [data leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_breach); over 100 terabytes of confidential company activities were exposed, resulting in well over \$100 million lost.

One of the causes of the hack was through the use of message forgery. The [phishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarus_Group) gained entry by manipulating the recipient to open the malicious attachments contained in the emails that were posing as colleagues of the top-level employees within the company.[^o4]
