# Gradient conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gradient conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **gradient conjecture**, due to [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633) (1989), was proved in 2000 by three Polish mathematicians, Krzysztof Kurdyka ([University of Savoie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Savoie), France), Tadeusz Mostowski ([Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University), Poland) and Adam Parusiński ([University of Angers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Angers), France).

:::

The conjecture states that given a real-valued [analytic function](wikipedia://en/61478) *f* defined on R<sup>*n*</sup> and a [trajectory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trajectory) *x*(*t*) of the [gradient](wikipedia://en/12461) vector field of *f* having a [limit point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_point) *x*<sub>0</sub> ∈ R<sup>*n*</sup>, where *f* has an isolated critical point at *x*<sub>0</sub>, there exists a limit (in the [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) PR<sup>*n*−1</sup>) for the [secant lines](wikipedia://en/241886) from *x*(*t*) to *x*<sub>0</sub>, as *t* tends to zero.

The proof depends on a theorem due to [Stanis%C5%82aw %C5%81ojasiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanis%C5%82aw_%C5%81ojasiewicz).
