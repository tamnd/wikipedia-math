# Wolfgang Gröbner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Gröbner"}

**Wolfgang Gröbner** (11 February 1899 – 20 August 1980) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His name is best known for the [Gröbner basis](wikipedia://en/357328), used for computations in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). However, the theory of Gröbner bases for polynomial rings was developed by his student [Bruno Buchberger](wikipedia://en/1924233) in 1965, who named them for Gröbner. Gröbner is also known for the [Alekseev-Gröbner formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alekseev-Gröbner_formula), which was actually proven by him.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Gröbner was born in [Gossensaß](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gossensaß), which at that time was in part of the [County of Tyrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Tyrol) of the [Austro-Hungarian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_Empire) and is now part of [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).

Gröbner first studied engineering at the [University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology) in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), Austria, but switched to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1929.

## Career {#Career}

He wrote his dissertation *Ein Beitrag zum Problem der Minimalbasen* in 1932 at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna); his advisor was [Philipp Furtwängler](wikipedia://en/895349). After his promotion, he did further studies at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) under [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896), in what is now known as [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). Later he contributed with his students to numerical analysis of ordinary differential equations and the industrial applications of Lie-theoretic methods, including a book on Lie series.[^o5]
