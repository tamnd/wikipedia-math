# Integral Equations and Operator Theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Integral Equations and Operator Theory"}

***Integral Equations and Operator Theory*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published quarterly by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). Established in 1978, it covers [integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equations) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), as well as their applications to [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) and other [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o3]

:::

The founding editor-in-chief of the journal, in 1978, was [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973). Its current editor-in-chief is [Christiane Tretter](wikipedia://en/63227596) ([University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern)).[^o4]
