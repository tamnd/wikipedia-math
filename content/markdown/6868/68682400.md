# Wolfgang Vogel (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Vogel"}

**Wolfgang Vogel** (8 February 1940 – 2 October 1996) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made contributions to [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1968 Vogel became a mathematics student at the [Halle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg). After finishing his degree in 1963 he worked as research scientist at the universities of Halle, [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and [Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innsbruck).[^o5] In 1965 he received his Ph.D. in Halle. He was appointed to the teaching staff ([Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)) in 1968. He was chairman of the department of pure mathematics from 1977 to 1991. In 1993 he was appointed Professor of Pure Mathematics, at [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University). He died of cancer in Palmerston North at the age of 56.[^o5]
