# Christian Robert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Robert"}

**Christian P. Robert** is a French statistician, specializing in [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412) and [Monte Carlo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo) methods.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Christian Robert studied at [ENSAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENSAE_ParisTech) then defended his PhD in 1987 at [Université de Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Rouen).[^o4] He held temporary positions at [Purdue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue) and [Cornell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell) before being an associate professor at [Université Paris 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_6), and then Professor at [Université de Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Rouen). He was also Professor of Statistics at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and director of [Center for Research in Economics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Research_in_Economics_and_Statistics).[^o5] As of 2021, he is professor at [CEREMADE](wikipedia://en/38196034), [Université Paris-Dauphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Dauphine) and a part-time member of the Department of Statistics, [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).

## Works {#Works}

Christian Robert is the author of several textbooks on Bayesian inference and Monte Carlo methods, including:

- Robert, Christian. *The Bayesian choice: from decision-theoretic foundations to computational implementation*. Springer Science & Business Media, 2007.
- Robert, Christian, and [George Casella](wikipedia://en/37543546). *Monte Carlo statistical methods*. Springer Science & Business Media, 2013.
- Marin, Jean-Michel, and Christian P. Robert. *Bayesian essentials with R*. New York: Springer, 2014.

## Service {#Service}

Robert served as the [editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief) of the [Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143), Series B, from 2006 to 2009. He was president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2008. In 2016 he was joint program chair of the [AIStats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIStats) conference.[^o8]
