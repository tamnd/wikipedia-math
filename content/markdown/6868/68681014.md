# Assyr Abdulle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Assyr Abdulle"}

**Assyr Abdulle** (19 January 1971 – 1 September 2021) was a Swiss mathematician.[^o3] He specialized in numerical mathematics.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Abdulle earned a doctorate in mathematics under [Gerhard Wanner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_Wanner) and [Ernst Hairer](wikipedia://en/31446016) at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) with the thesis *Méthodes de Chebyshev basées sur des polynômes orthogonaux*.[^o5] He also earned a degree in violin and music from the [Conservatoire de Musique de Genève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_de_Musique_de_Genève) in 1993. From 2001 to 2002, he was a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and worked at the computational laboratory at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 2002 to 2003. In 2003, he became an assistant professor at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) and an associate professor at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 2007. He then became a full professor at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne).[^o6] At the school, he started the master's degree in [computational science](wikipedia://en/1181008).[^o7] In 2016, he became Director of the Institut Mathicse and was founding Director of the Institut de Mathématiques in 2017.[^o8]

Abdulle was impassioned with modeling and numerical simulations in biology, chemistry, geology, and medicine.[^o9][^o10][^o11] He notably contributed to the development of heterogeneous multi-scale methods. He developed methods for solving multiscale and ergodic stochastic problems.[^o12][^o13] He also invented the Orthogonal Runge-Kutta-Chebyshev,[^o14] which was used to solve stiff differential equations which were then generalized to multiscale stochastic systems.[^o15][^o16]

In 2005, Abdulle won the New Talent Award at the International Conference on Scientific Computation and Differential Equations.[^o17] He received an advanced research fellowship from the [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) in 2007.[^o18] In 2009, he won the [James H. Wilkinson Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Wilkinson_Prize_in_Numerical_Analysis_and_Scientific_Computing),[^o19][^o20] awarded by the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376), for his contributions to applied mathematics.[^o21] He won the Germund Dahlquist Prize in 2013.[^o22][^o23][^o24]

Assyr Abdulle died on 1 September 2021 at the age of 50.[^o3]
