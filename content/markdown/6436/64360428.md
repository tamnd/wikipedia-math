# Martine Queffélec

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martine Queffélec"}

**Martine Queffélec** (née Joublin; born 1949) is a French mathematician associated with the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille) and known for her research on [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199), [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558), [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490), [L-systems](wikipedia://en/46565), and related topics in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Queffélec defended her doctoral dissertation in 1984. By 1987, she was working at the [Université Sorbonne Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Sorbonne_Paris_Nord); she moved to the Lille University of Science and Technology in 1993.

## Books {#Books}

Queffélec is the author of the book *Substitution Dynamical Systems – Spectral Analysis* (Springer, [Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235) 1294, 1987; 2nd ed., 2010). She is the co-author, with Hervé Queffélec, of *Diophantine Approximation and Dirichlet Series* (Harish-Chandra Research Institute Lecture Notes 2, 2013).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2011, the Lille University of Science and Technology hosted a conference "Analyse 2011" in honor of both Martine and Hervé Queffélec.

## Personal life and family {#Personal_life_and_family}

Queffélec's husband, mathematician Hervé Queffélec, is a son of French writer [Henri Queffélec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Queffélec) (1910–1992), and the brother of pianist [Anne Queffélec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Queffélec) and novelist [Yann Queffélec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_Queffélec).
