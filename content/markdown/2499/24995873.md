# Oscillatory integral operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oscillatory integral operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), an **oscillatory integral operator** is an [integral operator](wikipedia://en/2649421) of the form

:::

$$T_\lambda u(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^n}e^{i\lambda S(x, y)} a(x, y) u(y)\,dy, \qquad x\in\mathbb{R}^m, \quad y\in\mathbb{R}^n,$$

where the function *S*(*x*,*y*) is called the [phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_%28waves%29) of the operator and the function *a*(*x*,*y*) is called the [symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbol_of_a_differential_operator) of the operator. *λ* is a parameter. One often considers *S*(*x*,*y*) to be real-valued and smooth, and *a*(*x*,*y*) smooth and [compactly supported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_support). Usually one is interested in the behavior of *T*<sub>*λ*</sub> for large values of *λ*.

Oscillatory integral operators often appear in many fields of mathematics ([analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [integral geometry](wikipedia://en/349336), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)) and in physics. Properties of oscillatory integral operators have been studied by [Elias Stein](wikipedia://en/418073) and his school.[^o2]

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Hörmander's theorem"}

## Hörmander's theorem {#Hörmander's_theorem}

The following bound on the *L*<sup>2</sup> → *L*<sup>2</sup> action of oscillatory integral operators (or [*L*<sup>2</sup> → *L*<sup>2</sup>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L^2) [operator norm](wikipedia://en/246722)) was obtained by [Lars Hörmander](wikipedia://en/500958) in his paper on [Fourier integral operators](wikipedia://en/10138003):[^o5]

Assume that *x,y* ∈ **R**<sup>*n*</sup>, *n* ≥ 1. Let *S*(*x*,*y*) be real-valued and smooth, and let *a*(*x*,*y*) be smooth and [compactly supported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_support). If $\det_{j,k} \frac{\partial^2 S}{\partial x_j \, \partial y_k}(x,y)\ne 0$ everywhere on the support of *a*(*x*,*y*), then there is a constant *C* such that *T*<sub>λ</sub>, which is initially defined on [smooth functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_functions), [extends](wikipedia://en/460637) to a [continuous operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_operator) from *L*<sup>2</sup>(**R**<sup>*n*</sup>) to *L*<sup>2</sup>(**R**<sup>*n*</sup>), with the [norm](wikipedia://en/246722) bounded by $C \lambda^{-n/2}$, for every *λ* ≥ 1:

$$\|T_\lambda\|_{L^2(\mathbf{R}^n)\to L^2(\mathbf{R}^n)}\le C\lambda^{-n/2}.$$

:::
