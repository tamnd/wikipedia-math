# M. Salah Baouendi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. Salah Baouendi"}

**Mohammed Salah Baouendi** (محمد صالح باوندي; October 12, 1937 in [Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis) – December 24, 2011 in [La Jolla, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Jolla,_California)) was a [Tunisian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_people)-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked as a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). His research concerned [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and the theory of [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Baouendi moved from Tunis to France after finishing his [Baccalauréat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baccalauréat) in the high school [Sadiki College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadiki_College), and earned a [licence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) (a French undergraduate degree) in 1961 from the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris).[^o3] In this he had the assistance of a scholarship from the Tunisian government, which however demanded that he return to Tunis afterwards to become a schoolteacher. After the intervention of [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485), Baouendi was allowed to return to France for his graduate studies.[^o5] He completed a doctorate in 1967 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) (then part of the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris)), under the supervision of [Bernard Malgrange](wikipedia://en/9170142), with a dissertation concerning [elliptic operators](wikipedia://en/550137). Schwartz attempted to secure for him a suitable academic position in Tunis, in which he would be allowed to conduct research and collaborate with mathematicians from other countries,[^o5] and Baouendi became an associate professor at [Tunis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis_University) in 1968, but his administrative struggles there were too much, and he left in 1970. After a short stay at the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis), Baouendi moved in 1971 to the USA.[^o3][^o5]

Baouendi's first American faculty position was at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). During his tenure at Purdue, he was promoted to full professor in 1973, became department chair from 1980 to 1987, and also held visiting positions at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). He became a Distinguished Professor at UCSD in 1988 (giving up his Purdue professorship in 1990).[^o7] He was a co-founder of two journals, *Communications in Partial Differential Equations* and *Mathematical Research Letters*.[^o2]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Baouendi was given the Prix d'Aumale of the French Academy of Sciences in 1969. He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 1974. He and his wife, mathematician [Linda Preiss Rothschild](wikipedia://en/44920908), were jointly awarded the [Stefan Bergman Prize](wikipedia://en/9395687#The_Bergman_Prize) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2003.[^o7][^o9] In 2005 he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o2][^o7]
