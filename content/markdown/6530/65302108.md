# Alberto Collino

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Collino"}

*Image: Alberto Collino.png.*

**Alberto Collino** (1947 – 12 September 2020) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for his contributions in the field of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2]

:::

Collino was born in [Verzuolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verzuolo),[^o2] earned a laurea in mathematics in 1970 from the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), and completed a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1974 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3] His dissertation, *The Rational Equivalence Ring of Symmetric Products of Curves*, was supervised by [Arthur Mattuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Mattuck). He spent his professional career at the University of Turin, beginning as an assistant in 1970 and becoming a full professor in 1984.[^o3]

He died in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan).[^o2]

In 2025, a two-volume collection of papers written in memory of Alberto Collino was published by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser) in the series "Progress in Mathematics".[^o5][^o6]
