# Michael Glanzberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Glanzberg"}

**Michael Glanzberg** is an American analytic philosopher specializing in [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language) and [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939) who is currently a professor in philosophy at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o3] He received his PhD in philosophy from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where [Charles Parsons](wikipedia://en/1821365) and [Warren Goldfarb](wikipedia://en/15629024) supervised his dissertation.[^o4] Glanzberg has previously held faculty positions at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis), [Erindale College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Mississauga) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), and [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT).[^o3][^o5] Often working at the intersection of logic and the philosophy of language, Glanzberg is recognized for his work on quantification, paradox, semantics, theories of [truth](wikipedia://en/29920), and the role of context in various linguistic settings. He frequently collaborates with [Jc Beall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jc_Beall).[^o6][^o7][^o8] He serves as Co-Editor-in-Chief for the journal [Linguistics and Philosophy](wikipedia://en/22832531) and edited The Oxford Handbook of Truth (2018).[^o9]

:::
