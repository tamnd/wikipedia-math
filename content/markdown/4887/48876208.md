# Microfit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Microfit"}

**Microfit** is a [statistics package](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_statistical_packages) developed by [Bahram Pesaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahram_Pesaran) and [M. Hashem Pesaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Hashem_Pesaran), and published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press).[^o2] It is designed for econometric modelling with [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) data.[^o3]

:::
