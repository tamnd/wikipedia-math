# Gamma-object

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gamma-object"}

In mathematics, a **Γ-object** of a [pointed category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointed_category) *C* is a [contravariant functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contravariant_functor) from Γ to *C*.

:::

The basic example is [Segal](wikipedia://en/6713316)'s so-called **Γ-space**, which may be thought of as a generalization of [simplicial abelian group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_abelian_group) (or simplicial abelian monoid). More precisely, one can define a Gamma space as an O-monoid object in an infinity-category.[^o2] The notion plays a role in the generalization of [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) that replaces an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) by something [higher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_object).
