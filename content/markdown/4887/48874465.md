# Bidyut Baran Chaudhuri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bidyut Baran Chaudhuri"}

**Bidyut Baran Chaudhuri** (**B. B. Chauduri**) is a senior [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and an emeritus professor of [Techno India University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techno_India_University) in [West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal), India. He is also adjuncted to [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), where he was a professor for about three decades. He was the founding Head of Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Unit (which was established in 1994) of [ISI](wikipedia://en/2238418). Moreover, he was a J.C. Bose Fellow and [Indian National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Academy_of_Engineering)[^o3] Distinguished Professor at [ISI](wikipedia://en/2238418).

:::

He was the vice-president of the Society for Natural Language Technology Research (SNLTR).[^o4]

His primary research contributes to the fields of computer vision, image processing and pattern recognition.[^o5] He is a pioneer of "[Indian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_India) script [OCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_Character_Recognition)".[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Chaudhuri received his BSc (Hons.), BTech and MTech degrees from [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta), India in 1969, 1972 and 1974, respectively and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) Degree from [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Kanpur) in 1980.[^o3] He did his post-doc work during 1981-1982 from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast), U.K, through [Leverhulme Overseas Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverhulme_Trust).

He also worked as a visiting faculty at Tech University, Hannover during 1986-87 as well as at GSF Institute of Radiation Protection (now Leibnitz Institute), Munich in 1990 and 1992.

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Chaudhuri has been elected as a Life Fellow of [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) "for contributions to pattern recognition, especially Indian language script OCR, document processing and natural language processing".[^o5][^o8] He has become a Fellow of [International Association for Pattern Recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAPR) (IAPR) "for contributions to character recognition and speech synthesis in Indian language".[^o9] He is also Fellow of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) (TWAS),[^o10] [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (INSA),[^o11] [Indian National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Academy_of_Engineering) (INAE),[^o3] [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India) (NASI),[^o12] and [Institute of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IETE) (IETE). In 2011, Chaudhuri received the [Om Prakash Bhasin Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Om_Prakash_Bhasin_Award) for his contribution in the field of electronics and information technology.

Chaudhuri's interview on some of his works has been reported in Indian newspaper as well.[^o13][^o14]

He is within world's top 2% scientists and top-10 Indian [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) scientists according to a study conducted by [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o15] He has also been featured as top-10 machine learning researcher from India.[^o16]
