# Vivette Girault

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vivette Girault"}

**Vivette Girault** (born 1943) is a French mathematician, whose research expertise lies in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [finite element methods](wikipedia://en/18233581) and [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924). She has been affiliated with [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).

:::

Girault, was born in [Nice, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice,_France), and attended high school in [Caracas, Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas,_Venezuela), received her bachelor's degree from [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in [Montreal, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal,_Canada). After her undergraduate studies, Girault returned to France to study numerical analysis. She joined the applied mathematics faculty at the Université Paris, which was renamed the [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre_et_Marie_Curie) (UPMC) and is currently known as [Sorbonne Université](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_Université). Girault was named professor emerita at the Sorbonne Université, CNRS, Laboratoire Jacque-Louis Lions, Paris, France.[^o8]

Girault served on the editorial board of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) journal [Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation) from 2006 to 2017.

Girault was selected by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) to be the 2021 [AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM-SIAM_Sonia_Kovalevsky_Lecturer). She will deliver the lecture *"From linear poroelasticity to nonlinear implicit elastic and related models"* and receive the accompanying award at the SIAM annual meeting in [Spokane, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spokane,_Washington) in July 2021.[^o13]
