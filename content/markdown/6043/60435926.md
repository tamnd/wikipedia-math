# Kathryn Leonard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kathryn Leonard"}

**Kathryn Leonard** is an American mathematician and computer scientist. She was President of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) and is now AWM Past-President.[^o3] She is also director of the NSF-funded [Center for Undergraduate Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47702066). She is currently on the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Nominating Committee.[^o4]

:::

Leonard's research focuses on geometric modeling with applications to computer vision, computer graphics, and data science. She has received multiple major grants, including a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards).[^o5]

Leonard and [Misha Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misha_Collins), together with several other collaborators, are authors of "The 2D shape structure dataset", an article on a crowd-sourced database on the structure of shapes.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Leonard received a [Henry L. Alder Award](wikipedia://en/65434512) from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) in 2012.[^o8] She received the AWM Service Award from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) in 2015.[^o9] The Association for Women in Mathematics named her to their 2025 Class of AWM Fellows.[^o10]
