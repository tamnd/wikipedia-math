# Ellen Gethner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ellen Gethner"}

**Ellen Gethner** is a US mathematician and computer scientist specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) who won the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize)[^o3] in 2002 with co-authors [Stan Wagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Wagon) and Brian Wick for their paper *A stroll through the Gaussian Primes*.[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Gethner has two doctorates. She completed her first, a PhD in mathematics from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), in 1992; her dissertation, *Rational Period Functions For The Modular Group And Related Discrete Groups*, was supervised by L. Alayne Parson. She completed a second PhD in computer science from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in 2002, with a dissertation *Computational Aspects of Escher Tilings* supervised by [Nick Pippenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Pippenger) and [David G. Kirkpatrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_G._Kirkpatrick). Gethner is an associate professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver).[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Gethner became interested in connections between geometry and art after a high school lesson using a kaleidoscope to turn a drawing into an [Escher](wikipedia://en/20127)-like [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of the plane. This later inspired some of her research on [wallpaper patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallpaper_pattern) and on converting music into visual patterns.[^o9]
