# Barbara Fantechi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Fantechi"}

**Barbara Fantechi** is an Italian mathematician and Professor at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies). Her research area is [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). She is a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Fantechi received her [Laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1988, with thesis *Secanti di varietà proiettive e applicazioni*.[^o12][^o4][^o5] Her doctoral advisor was Fabrizio [Catanese](wikipedia://en/22843336).

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Her research considers algebraic geometry.[^o7] She has developed the mathematical theories that underpin [algebraic stacks](wikipedia://en/717377). Stacks were first introduced to understand the [moduli space of curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_space_of_curves).[^o8][^o9]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 2018, Fantechi received the Prof. Luigi Tartufari award from the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).[^o11] She was awarded the [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Chancellor's Professorship for 2017-2018, and spent a year at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o12] In 2022, Fantechi was the first woman mathematician to become a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).[^o13][^o14]
