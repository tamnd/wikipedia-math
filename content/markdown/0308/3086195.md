# Seshadri constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Seshadri constant"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Seshadri constant** is an invariant of an [ample line bundle](wikipedia://en/1186896) $L$ at a point on an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808). It was introduced by [Jean-Pierre Demailly](wikipedia://en/53350424) to measure a certain "rate of growth" of the [tensor powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_power) of $L$ in terms of the [jets](wikipedia://en/1521924) of the [sections](wikipedia://en/10630303) of the $L^k$. The object was the study of the [Fujita conjecture](wikipedia://en/3086309). The constant's name stems from its connection to [C. S. Seshadri](wikipedia://en/5633765)'s criterion for ampleness.[^n1]

:::

It is known that [Nagata's conjecture on algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagata's_conjecture_on_algebraic_curves) is equivalent to the assertion that for more than nine general points, the Seshadri constants of the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) are maximal. There is a general conjecture for [algebraic surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_surfaces), the [Nagata–Biran conjecture](wikipedia://en/3086255).

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $X$ be a smooth [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), $L$ an [ample line bundle](wikipedia://en/1186896) on $X$, $x$ a point of $X$, and $\mathcal{C}_x$ the set of all irreducible curves passing through $x$. Define the *Seshadri constant* of $L$ at $x$ by[^n1]

$$\varepsilon(L,x) = \inf_{C \in \mathcal{C}_x} \frac{L \cdot C}{\operatorname{mult}_x(C)},$$

where $L \cdot C$ denotes the [intersection number](wikipedia://en/384327) of $L$ and $C$ and $\operatorname{mult}_x(C)$ measures how many times the curve $C$ passes through $x$.

If $X$ is an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152), then $\varepsilon(L,x)$ is independent of the point $x$ chosen, and we write simply $\varepsilon(L)$.

:::
