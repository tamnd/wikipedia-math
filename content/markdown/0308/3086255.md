# Nagata–Biran conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nagata–Biran conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Nagata–Biran** conjecture, named after [Masayoshi Nagata](wikipedia://en/3086117) and [Paul Biran](wikipedia://en/27273860), is a generalisation of [Nagata's conjecture on curves](wikipedia://en/5140476) to arbitrary polarised surfaces.

:::

The conjecture can be stated as follows: let $X$ be a smooth [algebraic surface](wikipedia://en/648311) and $L$ be an [ample line bundle](wikipedia://en/1186896) on $X$ of degree $d$. Then for sufficiently large $r$, the [Seshadri constant](wikipedia://en/3086195) satisfies

$$\varepsilon(p_1,\ldots,p_r;X,L) = {d \over \sqrt{r}}.$$
