# American Mathematical Association of Two-Year Colleges

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="American Mathematical Association of Two-Year Colleges"}

The **American Mathematical Association of Two-Year Colleges** (**AMATYC**) is an organization dedicated to the improvement of education in the first two years of college [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada). AMATYC hosts an annual conference, summer institutes, workshops and mentoring for teachers in and outside math, and a semiannual [math competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Math_competition). AMATYC publishes one refereed journal, *MathAMATYC Educator,* and issues position statements on matters of mathematics education.

:::

The math competition is held in spring and fall [semester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semester) each year and is limited to problems in [precalculus](wikipedia://en/725821). Only students enrolled in [two-year colleges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_college) are eligible to participate. Only students who haven't received any degree/diploma, including within or outside of the U.S, can enter the competition.

AMATYC was founded in 1974. Its office is at [Southwest Tennessee Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Tennessee_Community_College) in [Memphis, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis,_Tennessee).

AMATYC is divided into eight regions: Central, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, Southwest, and West. A vice president is assigned to each region.
