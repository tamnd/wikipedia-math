## Families of surfaces with members in multiple classes {#Families_of_surfaces_with_members_in_multiple_classes}

::: {#o77 .entry cue=list-entry name="Shimura varieties"}

- Surfaces that are also [Shimura varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimura_varieties):

:::

  - [Hilbert modular surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_modular_surface)
  - [Humbert surfaces](wikipedia://en/20711751)
  - [Picard modular surfaces](wikipedia://en/35016719)
  - [Shioda modular surfaces](wikipedia://en/33530042)

::: {#o78 .entry cue=list-entry name="Elliptic surface"}

- [Elliptic surfaces](wikipedia://en/3073462), surfaces with an elliptic fibration; *quasielliptic surfaces* constitute a modification this idea that occurs in finite characteristic

:::

  - [Raynaud surfaces](wikipedia://en/22712664) and generalized Raynaud surfaces, certain quasielliptic counterexamples to the conclusions of the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/2988093)
- Exceptional surfaces, surfaces whose Picard number achieve the bound set by the central Hodge number *h*<sup>1,1</sup>

::: {#o79 .entry cue=list-entry name="Kähler surfaces"}

- [Kähler surfaces](wikipedia://en/390538), complex surfaces with a Kähler metric; equivalently, surfaces for which the first Betti number *b*<sub>1</sub> is even

:::

::: {#o80 .entry cue=list-entry name="Minimal surfaces"}

- [Minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/15148924), surfaces that can't be obtained from another by blowing up at a point; they have no connection with the minimal surfaces of differential geometry

:::

::: {#o81 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nodal surface"}

- [Nodal surfaces](wikipedia://en/46573101), surfaces whose only singularities are nodes

:::

  - Cayley's nodal cubic, which has 4 nodes
  - Kummer surfaces, quartic surfaces with 16 nodes
  - [Togliatti surface](wikipedia://en/20705037), a certain quintic with 31 nodes
  - [Barth surfaces](wikipedia://en/20746677), referring to a certain sextic with 65 nodes and decic with 345 nodes
  - [Labs surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labs_surface), a certain septic with 99 nodes
  - [Endrass surface](wikipedia://en/35060650), a certain surface of degree 8 with 168 nodes
  - [Sarti surface](wikipedia://en/23341987), a certain surface of degree 12 with 600 nodes

::: {#o82 .entry cue=list-entry name="Orbit space"}

- Quotient surfaces, surfaces that are constructed as the [orbit space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_space) of some other surface by the action of a finite group; examples include Kummer, Godeaux, Hopf, and Inoue surfaces

:::

::: {#o83 .entry cue=list-entry name="Zariski surface"}

- [Zariski surfaces](wikipedia://en/3096979), surfaces in finite characteristic that admit a purely inseparable dominant rational map from the projective plane

:::
