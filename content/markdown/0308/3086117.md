# Masayoshi Nagata

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Masayoshi Nagata"}

**Masayoshi Nagata** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): 永田 雅宜 *Nagata Masayoshi*; February 9, 1927 – August 27, 2008) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in the field of [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He was celebrated for the construction of a number of intricate counterexamples for some seemingly plausible statements in commutative algebra and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), earning him the nickname "Mr. Counterexample".[^o3]

:::

## Work {#Work}

[Nagata's compactification theorem](wikipedia://en/37613671) shows that [algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_varieties) can be embedded in [complete varieties](wikipedia://en/1047627). The [Chevalley–Iwahori–Nagata theorem](wikipedia://en/35215206) describes the quotient of a variety by a [group](wikipedia://en/19447).

In 1959, he introduced a [counterexample](wikipedia://en/1695231#Nagata's_counterexample) to the general case of [Hilbert's fourteenth problem](wikipedia://en/1695231) on [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762). His 1962 book on local rings contains several other counterexamples he found, such as a commutative [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) that is not [catenary](wikipedia://en/10034460), and a commutative Noetherian ring of infinite [dimension](wikipedia://en/56079).

[Nagata's conjecture on curves](wikipedia://en/5140476) concerns the minimum [degree](wikipedia://en/2776558) of a [plane curve](wikipedia://en/971691) specified to have given multiplicities at given points; see also [Seshadri constant](wikipedia://en/3086195). [Nagata's conjecture on automorphisms](wikipedia://en/34996922) concerns the existence of wild [automorphisms](wikipedia://en/1160) of [polynomial algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_algebra) in three variables. Recent work has solved this latter problem in the affirmative.[^o5]
