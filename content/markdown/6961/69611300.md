# William H. Bossert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William H. Bossert"}

**William H. Bossert** (born 1937) is an American mathematician. He is the David B. Arnold, Jr. Professor of Science, Emeritus at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He was the housemaster of [Lowell House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_House) for 23 years.[^o2][^o3] He received his PhD from Harvard in 1963.[^o4]

:::
