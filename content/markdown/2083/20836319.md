# Raoul Bricard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raoul Bricard"}

**Raoul Bricard** (23 March 1870 – 26 November 1943) was a French [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is best known for his work in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), especially [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) and [scissors congruence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scissors_congruence), and [kinematics](wikipedia://en/65914), especially [mechanical linkages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_linkage).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bricard taught geometry at [Ecole Centrale des Arts et Manufactures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Centrale_des_Arts_et_Manufactures). In 1908 he became a professor of applied geometry at the [National Conservatory of Arts and Crafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_National_des_Arts_et_Métiers) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989).[^o4] In 1932 he received the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [Paris Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for his work in geometry.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

In 1896 Bricard published a paper on [Hilbert's third problem](wikipedia://en/152703), even before the problem was stated by [Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302).[^o7] In it he proved that [mirror symmetric](wikipedia://en/1390873) [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) are [scissors congruent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scissors_congruence), and proved a weak version of [Dehn](wikipedia://en/570602)'s criterion.

*Image: Br1-anim.gif.* One of the [Bricard octahedra](wikipedia://en/18605515)

In 1897 Bricard published an important investigation on [flexible polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexible_polyhedra).[^o8] In it he classified all flexible [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), now known as [Bricard octahedra](wikipedia://en/18605515).[^o9] This work was the subject of [Henri Lebesgue's](wikipedia://en/49514) lectures in 1938.[^o10] Later Bricard discovered notable 6-bar linkages.[^o11][^o12]

Bricard also gave one of the first geometric proofs of [Morley's trisector theorem](wikipedia://en/1096323) in 1922.[^o13][^o14]

## Books {#Books}

Bricard authored six books, including a mathematics survey in [Esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto).[^o16] He is listed in *[Encyclopedia of Esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia_of_Esperanto)*.[^o17]

- *Matematika terminaro kaj krestomatio* (in Esperanto), Hachette, Paris, 1905
- [*Géométrie descriptive*](http://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000662067), O. Doin et fils, 1911
- [*Cinématique et mécanismes*](https://books.google.com/books?id=Grs3AAAAMAAJ), A. Colin, 1921
- *Petit traité de perspective*, Vuibert, 1924[^o18]
- [*Leçons de cinématique*](http://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k879032k), Gauthier-Villars et cie., 1926
- [*Le calcul vectoriel*](http://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000469108), A. Colin, 1929
