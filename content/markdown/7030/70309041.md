# Slab (geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Slab"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **slab** is a region between two [parallel lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_lines) in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410),[^o8] or between two parallel [planes in three-dimensional Euclidean space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planes_in_three-dimensional_Euclidean_space) or between two [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862) in [higher dimensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_dimensions).[^o5]

:::

## Set definition {#Set_definition}

A slab can also be defined as a set of points:[^o6]

$$\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid \alpha \le n \cdot x \le \beta \},$$

where $n$ is the normal vector of the planes $n \cdot x = \alpha$ and $n \cdot x = \beta$.

Or, if the slab is centered around the origin:[^o7]

$$\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid |n \cdot x| \le \theta / 2 \},$$

where $\theta = |\alpha - \beta|$ is the thickness of the slab.
