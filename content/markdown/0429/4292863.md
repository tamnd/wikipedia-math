# Adriaan Vlacq

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adriaan Vlacq"}

*Image: Vlacq - Tabulae sinuum, tangentium et secantium et logarithmi sinuum, tangentium et numerorum ab unitate ad 10000, 1670 - 3885877.tif.* *Tabulae*, 1670

*Image: Adriaan Vlacq mark - BEIC.jpg.* Vlacq's mark (from [BEIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblioteca_europea_di_informazione_e_cultura))

**Adriaan Vlacq** (1600–1667) was a Dutch book publisher and author of mathematical tables. Born in [Gouda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gouda,_South_Holland), Vlacq published a table of [logarithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithms) from 1 to 100,000 to 10 decimal places in 1628 in his *[Arithmetica logarithmica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetica_logarithmica)*. This table extended [Henry Briggs'](wikipedia://en/210399) original tables which only covered the values 1-20,000 and 90,001 to 100,000.[^o2] The new table was computed by [Ezechiel de Decker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezechiel_de_Decker) and Vlacq who calculated and added 70,000 further values to complete the tables. This table was further extended by [Jurij Vega](wikipedia://en/40546) in 1794, and by [Alexander John Thompson](wikipedia://en/30059434) in 1952.

:::

A shorter trigonometric table called ***Canon Sinuum*** was included in later works of Vlacq.[^o3]

In 1632, he settled in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) but ten years later with the onset of the [English Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Civil_War), he moved to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and later moved to [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague).

He died at The Hague on 8 April 1667.

The crater [Vlacq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlacq_%28crater%29) on the Moon is named after him.[^o4]
