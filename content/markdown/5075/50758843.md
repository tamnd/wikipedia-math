# Yurii Egorov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yurii Egorov"}

**Yurii** (or **Yuri**) **Vladimirovich Egorov** (Юрий Владимирович Егоров, 11 July 1938 – 6 October 2018) was a Russian-Soviet mathematician who specialised in differential equations.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

*Image: Егоров Юрий Владимирович.png.* Егоров Юрий Владимирович

In 1960 he completed his undergraduate studies at the Mechanics and Mathematics Faculty of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) (MSU). In 1963 from MSU he received his Ph.D. with the thesis "*Некоторые задачи теории оптимального управления в бесконечномерных пространствах*" ("Some Problems of [Optimal Control](wikipedia://en/362565) Theory in Infinite-Dimensional Spaces"). In 1970 from MSU he received his Russian doctorate of sciences ([Doctor Nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_nauk)) with thesis: "*О локальных свойствах псевдодифференциальных операторов главного типа*" ("Local Properties of Pseudodifferential Operators of Principal Type"). He was employed at MSU from 1961 to 1992, and he was a full professor in the Department of Differential Equations of the Mechanics and Mathematics Faculty there from 1973 to 1992. Since 1992 he has been a professor of mathematics at [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University) (Toulouse III).

Egorov's research deals with differential equations and applications in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713), and optimal control theory. In 1970 he was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Nice.[^o3]

Egorov died in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse), France on 6 October 2018, at the age of 80.[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1981 — Lomonosov Memorial Prize (established in 1944) — for his series of publications on "Субэллиптические операторы и их применения к исследованию краевых задач" (Subelliptic operators and their applications to the study of boundary value problems)
- 1988 — [USSR State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) (with several co-authors) — for their series of publications (1958–1985) on "Исследования краевых задач для дифференциальных операторов и их приложения в математической физике" (Research on boundary value problems and their applications in mathematical physics)
- 1998 — Petrovsky Award (jointly with V. A. Kondratiev) for their series of publications on "Исследование спектра эллиптических операторов" (The study of the spectra of elliptic operators)
