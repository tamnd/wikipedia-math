# Quantum groupoid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum groupoid"}

In mathematics, a **quantum groupoid** is any of a number of notions in [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) analogous to the notion of [groupoid](wikipedia://en/12543). In usual geometry, the information of a groupoid can be contained in its [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604) of representations (by a version of [Tannaka–Krein duality](wikipedia://en/2588624)), in its groupoid algebra or in the commutative [Hopf algebroid](wikipedia://en/31530123) of functions on the groupoid. Thus formalisms trying to capture quantum groupoids include certain classes of (autonomous) [monoidal categories](wikipedia://en/330604), [Hopf algebroids](wikipedia://en/31530123) etc.

:::
