# MacCullagh ellipsoid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MacCullagh ellipsoid"}

The **MacCullagh ellipsoid** is defined by the equation:

:::

$$\frac{x^2}{A} + \frac{y^2}{B} + \frac{z^2}{C} = 2 E,$$

where $E$ is the energy and $x,y,z$ are the components of the [angular momentum](wikipedia://en/2839), given in body's principal reference frame, with corresponding [principal moments of inertia](wikipedia://en/157700) $A,B,C$. The construction of such [ellipsoid](wikipedia://en/145381#Applications) was conceived by [James MacCullagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_MacCullagh).[^o2]
