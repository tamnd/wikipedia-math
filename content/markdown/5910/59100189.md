# Judith T. Lessler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Judith T. Lessler"}

**Judith T. Lessler** is an American statistician and expert on [survey methodology](wikipedia://en/272134), particularly on surveys relating to health and [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997).

:::

Lessler was born in [Charlotte, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte,_North_Carolina), and grew up on a farm in [Iredell County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iredell_County,_North_Carolina). She earned her Ph.D. in 1974 at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). Her dissertation, *A Double Sampling Scheme Model for Eliminating Measurement Process Bias and Estimating Measurement Errors in Surveys*, was jointly supervised by Daniel G. Horvitz and [Gary Grove Koch](wikipedia://en/69269904).

She worked as a statistician for [RTI International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTI_International), the [Battelle Memorial Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battelle_Memorial_Institute), and [National Center for Health Statistics](wikipedia://en/1042734), before retiring to operate a consulting business and run an organic farm on the [Alston-DeGraffenried Plantation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alston-DeGraffenried_Plantation) historic site in [Chatham County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatham_County,_North_Carolina).

With William D. Kalsbeek, Lessler is the author of *Nonsampling Error in Surveys* (Wiley, 1992). She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1992. She is also a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).
