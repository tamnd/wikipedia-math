# Patrizia Gianni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patrizia Gianni"}

**Patrizia M. Gianni** (born 1952) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805). She is known for her early research on [Gröbner bases](wikipedia://en/357328) including her discovery of the [FGLM algorithm](wikipedia://en/53596792) for changing monomial orderings in Gröbner bases, and for her development of the components of the [Axiom computer algebra system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_%28computer_algebra_system%29) concerning [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) and [rational functions](wikipedia://en/361210).

:::

Gianni is a professor of algebra in the mathematics department of the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). She earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from the University of Pisa, and has worked for [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research) as well as for the University of Pisa.
