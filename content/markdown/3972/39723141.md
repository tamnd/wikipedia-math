# M. T. Naraniengar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. T. Naraniengar"}

*Image: M. T. Naraniengar.jpg.* M. T. Naraniengar

**Mandyam Tondanur Naraniengar** (1871–1940)[^o2] was an Indian mathematician. He first proved in 1909 the [Morley's trisector theorem](wikipedia://en/1096323) after it was posed in 1899 by [Frank Morley](wikipedia://en/589167).[^o3]

:::

He was the president of [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) from 1930 to 1932[^o4] and the editor of the *Journal of the Indian Mathematical Society* from its founding in 1909 until 1927.[^o2]
