# SDMX

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SDMX"}

**Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange (SDMX)** is a set of technical standards designed to describe statistical data and metadata, normalise their exchange, and improve their efficient sharing across statistical and similar organisations.[^o2] It is published as **[ISO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO) 17369.**[^o3]

:::

## Development {#Development}

The standards were developed by an international initiative that aims at standardising and modernising ("industrialising") the mechanisms and processes for the exchange of statistical data and metadata among international organisations and their member countries.[^o5]

The SDMX sponsoring institutions are the [Bank for International Settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_for_International_Settlements) (BIS), the [European Central Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Central_Bank) (ECB), [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat) (the statistical office of the European Union), the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund) (IMF), the [Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organisation_for_Economic_Co-operation_and_Development) (OECD), the [United Nations Statistics Division](wikipedia://en/2996969) (UNSD), and the [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank).

These organisations are the main players at world and regional levels in the collection of official statistics in a large variety of domains (agriculture statistics, economic and financial statistics, social statistics, environment statistics etc.).

### Version history {#Version_history}

Version 1.0 of the SDMX standard was recognised as an ISO standard in 2005.[^o7] SDMX version 2.1 was released in May 2011,[^o8] and was approved by ISO as International Standard (ISO 17369:2013) in 2013. SDMX version 3.0 was published in September 2021.[^o9]

## Technical standards {#Technical_standards}

SDMX message formats have two basic expressions, SDMX-ML (using [XML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML) syntax) and SDMX-EDI (using [EDIFACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EDIFACT) syntax and based on the [GESMES/TS](wikipedia://en/8263600) statistical message). The standards also include additional specifications (e.g. registry specification, [web services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_services)). The [RDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resource_Description_Framework) Data Cube vocabulary implements the cube model underlying SDMX as Linked Data.[^o11]
