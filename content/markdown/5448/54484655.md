# List of nonlinear ordinary differential equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of nonlinear ordinary differential equations"}

[Differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) are prominent in many scientific areas. [Nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) ones are of particular interest for their commonality in describing real-world systems and how much more difficult they are to solve compared to [linear](wikipedia://en/722503) differential equations. This list presents nonlinear [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) that have been named, sorted by area of interest.

:::
