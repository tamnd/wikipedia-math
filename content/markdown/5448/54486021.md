# Barbara Gertrude Yates

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Gertrude Yates"}

**Barbara Gertrude Yates** (1919–1998) was an Irish mathematician who seems to have been the first woman born and brought up in Ireland to gain a PhD in pure mathematics.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

She was born in January 1919 in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin), to a family with a tradition of excelling in mathematics at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin). Her [Offaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offaly)-born father James Yates (1869–1929) had been a Trinity Scholar in Mathematics prior to his graduation in 1891, and was a school inspector in various parts of Ireland until 1922, when the whole family moved to [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast) following the partition of Ireland. Her older brothers Henry George Yates (1908–1954) and James Garrett Yates (1917–1957) had also been Trinity Scholars in Mathematics, in 1927 and 1936 respectively.[^o5] She herself [received that distinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scholars_of_Trinity_College_Dublin) in 1940, graduating BA in mathematics in 1941.[^o5]

She was on the teaching staff at [Queen's University Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast) 1942–1945, then at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) 1945–1948, following which she moved to [Royal Holloway College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway_College), where she lectured until her retirement at age 65.[^o6] In 1952 she completed her PhD, awarded the following year by the University of Aberdeen, with a thesis entitled "A difference-differential equation". Her advisor was [E. M. Wright](wikipedia://en/529941).[^o7]
