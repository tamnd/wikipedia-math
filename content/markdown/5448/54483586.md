# 1961 United Kingdom census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1961 United Kingdom census"}

The **United Kingdom Census 1961** was a [census](wikipedia://en/6889) of the [United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Northern_Ireland) carried out on 23 April 1961. It was the first to ask about qualifications, migration status, and household tenure. It was also the first to use a computer, an [IBM 705](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_705) at the [Royal Army Pay Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Army_Pay_Corps), [Worthy Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worthy_Down_Camp), [Winchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester).

:::

## Release {#Release}

The census was conducted under the [Census Act 1920](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_Act_1920) which prohibits disclosure. It is expected, however, that it will be released to the public in the year 2062.[^o4]

The 1961 census was the first to use computers to process the results.[^o5] This was the first census to ask where the household head was resident 1 year before the census.[^o5] It was also the last census (since the introduction of the question in 1851) to include the question of county of birth to measure internal migration for the British born population.[^o6]
