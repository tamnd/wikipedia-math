# 1971 United Kingdom census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1971 United Kingdom census"}

The **United Kingdom Census 1971** was a [census](wikipedia://en/6889) of the [United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Northern_Ireland) carried out on 25 April 1971. The census will be released in 2071.[^o3]

:::

A test census for this census year was conducted in 1968.[^o4] According to the preliminary general results of the census, the population of the United Kingdom on 25 April 1971 was as follows:[^o5]

The 1971 census was the first to ask when a foreign born resident arrived to the UK,[^o6] however this was not continued again until 2011. In addition to removed census questions, it was the first to ask residents about their parents' country of birth,[^o7] which was not asked again in any other census following. It was the first census additionally that allowed for the outputting of small area geography statistics in enumeration districts,[^o6] and asked for specific date of birth instead of just the present age of residents to get a more precise age at the date of the census.[^o6]
