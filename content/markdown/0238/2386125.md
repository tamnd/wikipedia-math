# Chris Brand

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Chris Brand"}

**Christopher Richard Brand** (1 June 1943 – 28 May 2017) was a British [psychological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and [psychometric](wikipedia://en/24982) researcher who gained media attention for his controversial statements on [race and intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_and_intelligence) and [paedophilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paedophilia).[^o3]

:::

Brand was a proponent of [IQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IQ) testing and the [general intelligence factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_intelligence_factor), and was "a major influence in the spread of influence of [inspection time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspection_time) as a theoretically interesting correlate of psychometric intelligence," according to [Ian Deary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Deary) and Pauline Smith.[^o4]

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Brand was born in Preston, England on 1 June 1943. He went to [Queen Elizabeth's Grammar School for Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth's_Grammar_School_for_Boys), and was a graduate of [The Queen's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford), and a 1968–1970 Fellow of [Nuffield College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuffield_College), Oxford. He was a lecturer at [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), from 1970 to 1997, teaching in personality, psychopathology and philosophical problems and researching in factorial psychology. In the 1980s he served on the United Kingdom's [Council for National Academic Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_for_National_Academic_Awards). His 1996 book *The g Factor* garnered considerable media attention with its claim that inherited [general intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_intelligence) was like psychological money. Brand wrote that general intelligence is an important factor in determining life outcomes for those with lower scores. He attributed socio-economic differences among people of African descent to differences in general intelligence.

Brand was a Fellow of the [Galton Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galton_Institute). From 2000 to 2004, Brand was a research consultant to the [CRACK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Prevention) program based in [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore), Maryland, which pays drug-addicted mothers \$200 to be sterilised.[^o6] He wrote articles for *[American Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Renaissance_%28magazine%29)* and *[The Occidental Quarterly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Occidental_Quarterly)*. He also wrote a blog, IQ & PC. His review of Buchanan's *Playing with Fire* was published in the journal *[Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_%28journal%29)* in 2011.[^o7]

Brand had three children. He married his third wife in 2001. He died on 28 May 2017.[^o8]

## Published positions {#Published_positions}

### Race and IQ {#Race_and_IQ}

Brand's discussions of [race and intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_and_intelligence) attracted controversy because of his support for the hypothesis that average IQ differences between racial and ethnic groups are at least partly genetic in origin,[^o15] a view that is now considered discredited by mainstream science.[^o11][^o12][^o13]

Brand referred to himself as a "[race realist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_realist)" and has been described as a "[scientific racist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_racism)."[^o14][^o15]

### Race, IQ and women {#Race,_IQ_and_women}

Brand's controversial views generated headlines in April 1996, when he was quoted in the *Independent on Sunday* recommending that "low-IQ girls" be "encouraged to have sex with higher-IQ boys" rather than with their more usual low-IQ companions (which would result in [genetic deterioration](wikipedia://en/1820047)). "There are plenty of intelligent African men for black girls to be having sex with,"[^o17] he said, but added that blacks probably needed to allow [polygamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygamy).[^o18]

Brand also wrote that "women are inclined to deceitful [promiscuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promiscuity)" and that [Sigmund Freud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmund_Freud) was therefore right to ascribe weaker [super-egos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_ego) to women than to men. His 1996 book *[The g Factor: General Intelligence and Its Implications](wikipedia://en/13939966)* led to accusations of [scientific racism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_racism) and [sexism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexism), and his university lectures were protested and closed by the [Anti-Nazi League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Nazi_League) of [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh). Brand's book was subsequently withdrawn by publisher [John Wiley & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons).[^o19] It was then published free on the web by Douance.[^o20]

### Paedophilia {#Paedophilia}

In October 1996 Brand came to the defence of [Nobel laureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize) [Daniel Carleton Gajdusek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Carleton_Gajdusek) who had been charged with [child sex abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_sex_abuse). Brand argued that sex with a consenting partner over the age of 12 was not harmful so long as both partners had an above-average IQ.[^o22][^o23]

The proceedings were initiated in 1996 after the dean of social sciences complained.[^o23] Edinburgh University's Chaplain, a supporter of the Anti-Nazi League, had taken Brand's e-mailed reflections on pederasty to the Scottish press. Edinburgh's Student newspaper's frontpage banner headline was "FIRST IT WAS BLACKS, THEN IT WAS WOMEN, NOW IT'S KIDS".

Brand was fired from his 27-year position at [Edinburgh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_University) a year later in 1997 after hearings.[^o24][^o25] The university said this was for conduct that "brought the university into disrepute".

Brand appealed and sued the university for [unfair dismissal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unfair_dismissal), and received £12,000 (in those days the maximum obtainable from an [employment tribunal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment_tribunal)) in an out-of-court settlement.[^o26] His case became a [cause célèbre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cause_célèbre) among advocates of [academic freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_freedom). [Marek Kohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marek_Kohn) cited the Brand incident in a defence of intellectual freedom on the Internet.[^o27] Others, however, including a former Brand student, considered academic freedom a privilege that carried with it an expectation of "social responsibility".[^o28]

Eric Barendt (University College London), in the chapter "The Chris Brand Case" in his 2010 book *Academic Freedom*, said Brand should have tried harder to get on with his colleagues[^o29] – who Brand replied were "Jew-leftie-commie\[s\]".[^o30]
