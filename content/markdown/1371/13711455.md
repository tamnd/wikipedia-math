# Polish Logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polish Logic"}

***Polish Logic*** is an anthology of papers by several authors—[Kazimierz Ajdukiewicz](wikipedia://en/900467), [Leon Chwistek](wikipedia://en/1029740), [Stanislaw Jaskowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanislaw_Jaskowski), Zbigniew Jordan, [Tadeusz Kotarbinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeusz_Kotarbinski), [Stanisław Leśniewski](wikipedia://en/422271), [Jan Łukasiewicz](wikipedia://en/305064), [Jerzy Słupecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Słupecki), and Mordchaj Wajsberg—published in 1967 and covering the period 1920–1939. The work focuses on the contributions of Polish [logicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), more particularly, [mathematical logicians](wikipedia://en/19636), to [modern logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_logic).[^o2]

:::
