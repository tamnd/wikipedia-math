# Hiroshi Toda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hiroshi Toda"}

**Hiroshi Toda** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who specialized in [stable](wikipedia://en/2946655) and unstable [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831).

:::

He started publishing in 1952. Many of his early papers are concerned with the study of [Whitehead products](wikipedia://en/3129615) and their behaviour under [suspension](wikipedia://en/1251702) and more generally with the (unstable) [homotopy groups of spheres](wikipedia://en/1104697). In a 1957 paper he showed the first non-existence result for the [Hopf invariant](wikipedia://en/4417198) 1 problem. This period of his work culminated in his book *Composition methods in homotopy groups of spheres* (1962). Here he used as important tools the [Toda bracket](wikipedia://en/13687788) (which he calls the *toric construction*) and the [Toda fibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toda_fibration), among others, to compute the first 20 nontrivial homotopy groups for each sphere.

Among his most important contributions to stable homotopy theory was his work on the existence and non-existence of so-called [Toda–Smith complexes](wikipedia://en/47340914). These are [finite complexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CW-complex) which can be characterized as having a particularly simple [ordinary homology](wikipedia://en/431041) (as modules over the [Steenrod algebra](wikipedia://en/2146220)) or, alternatively, by having a particularly simple [BP-homology](wikipedia://en/13698492). They can be used to construct the Greek letter infinite families in the stable homotopy groups of spheres. In his paper *On spectra realizing exterior parts of the Steenrod algebra* (1971), Toda deduced several existence and non-existence results on these complexes. The existence parts are still unsurpassed.

Toda also did important work on the algebraic topology of (exceptional) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).
