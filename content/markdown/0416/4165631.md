# List of films about mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of films about mathematicians"}

This is a list of feature films and documentaries that include [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians), scientists who use math or references to mathematicians.

:::

## About mathematics {#About_mathematics}

Films where mathematics is central to the plot:

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="21"}

- *[21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21_%282008_film%29)* (2008) – A group of current and former [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) students, mostly mathematicians, and an algebra professor devise a [card counting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Card_counting) scheme for success at [Las Vegas Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Strip) [blackjack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackjack) tables.

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Bank"}

- *[The Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bank_%282001_film%29)* (2001) – A mathematician discovers a formula to predict fluctuations in the [stock market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market).

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cube"}

- *[Cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_%281997_film%29)* (1997) – Six people, including math student Leaven, awake in a deathtrap based on mathematical principles.

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Fermat's Room"}

- *[Fermat's Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_Room)* (2007) – Three mathematicians and one inventor are invited to a house under the premise of solving a great enigma and told to use pseudonyms based on famous historical mathematicians. At the house, they are trapped in a room. They must solve puzzles given by the host, who calls himself "Fermat", in order to escape the slowly closing walls of the room.

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Gifted"}

- *[Gifted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gifted_%282017_film%29)* (2017) – Frank Adler ([Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_%28actor%29)) is a single man raising a [child prodigy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_prodigy)—his spirited young niece Mary ([Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace))—in a coastal town in Florida after the death of her mother Diane, a mathematician. Mary's grandmother Evelyn ([Lindsay Duncan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsay_Duncan)) and uncle have different ideas on how to raise her. Mary tells her grandmother she wants to solve the problem her mother was working on, the [Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness](wikipedia://en/6013654) problem.[^o8]

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Good Will Hunting"}

- *[Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting)* (1997) – Janitor and genius Will Hunting ([Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon)) begins to turn his life around with the help of a psychologist ([Robin Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Williams)) and a [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859)-winning professor ([Stellan Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellan_Skarsgård)).

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="I.Q."}

- *[I.Q.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I.Q._%28film%29)* (1994) – [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736) ([Walter Matthau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Matthau)) helps a young man ([Tim Robbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Robbins)) pretend to be a [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) in order to catch the attention of Einstein's niece ([Meg Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Ryan)).

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="An Invisible Sign"}

- *[An Invisible Sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Invisible_Sign)* (2011) – Mona Gray ([Jessica Alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Alba)) gives up everything important to her in life, except mathematics, as part of a "deal with the universe" to help restore her father (a mathematician) to health. Years later, Mona teaches the subject, and does her best to help her students contend with their own personal crises.

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Marguerite's Theorem"}

- *[Marguerite's Theorem](wikipedia://en/75900013)* (2023) – Marguerite ([Ella Rumpf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Rumpf)) is a brilliant doctoral student at the ENS. She has been working on the [Goldbach's conjecture](wikipedia://en/44758) for her thesis and leading a secluded life. However, an error is discovered in her thesis that completely destroys her work. Devastated, she leaves the academia and tries to live with people in the outside world.

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Moebius"}

- *[Moebius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moebius_%281996_film%29)* (1996) – [Topologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist) including a young girl make contributions to the subway system and other facets of reality in Argentina in this math film with a science fiction and surreal feel.

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Moneyball"}

- *[Moneyball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moneyball_%28film%29)* (2011) – Oakland Athletics baseball team's general manager Billy Beane attempts to assemble a competitive team using statistics.

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Oxford Murders"}

- *[The Oxford Murders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oxford_Murders_%28film%29)* (2008) – A Student ([Elijah Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Wood)) finds out about mysterious killings in Oxford and helped by a professor ([John Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hurt)), they reveal the math patterns used by the killer.

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pi"}

- *[Pi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_%28film%29)* (1998) – A mathematician searches for the number that underlies all of nature.

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Proof"}

- *[Proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_%282005_film%29)* (2005) – A former student ([Jake Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Gyllenhaal)) of a recently deceased, brilliant mathematician ([Anthony Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Hopkins)) finds a notebook in his office containing a proof of an important theorem, but the mathematician's daughter ([Gwyneth Paltrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwyneth_Paltrow)) claims it is hers. The ensuing dispute is complicated by signs that she may have inherited her father's mental illness and a burgeoning romance. The movie is based on [the 2001 play](wikipedia://en/166521) by David Auburn.

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Raising Genius"}

- *[Raising Genius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raising_Genius)* (2004) – The film is about a boy ([Justin Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Long)) who locks himself in the bathroom to work out math equations on the shower wall.

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sneakers"}

- *[Sneakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneakers_%281992_film%29)* (1992) – An eclectic team is blackmailed into stealing a mathematician's code-breaking box.

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Travelling Salesman"}

- *[Travelling Salesman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travelling_Salesman_%282012_film%29)* (2012) – The US government hires four mathematicians to solve the most powerful problem ever to plague computer science ([P vs NP problem](wikipedia://en/6115)).

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="X+Y"}

- *[X+Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X+Y)* (2014) – A teenage mathematical prodigy has difficulty understanding people, but finds comfort in numbers.

:::

## Mathematician biographical films {#Mathematician_biographical_films}

Biographical films based on real-life mathematicians:

::: {#o23 .entry cue=list-entry name="Agora"}

- *[Agora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agora_%28film%29)* (2009) – The life of the mathematician, astronomer, and philosopher [Hypatia](wikipedia://en/38375) ([Rachel Weisz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Weisz)), directed by [Alejandro Amenábar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Amenábar).

:::

::: {#o24 .entry cue=list-entry name="A Beautiful Mind"}

- *[A Beautiful Mind](wikipedia://en/3569822)* (2001) – A fictional account based loosely on the life of mathematician [John Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forbes_Nash) ([Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)), who made a breakthrough that wins him the [Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Memorial_Prize_in_Economic_Sciences).

:::

::: {#o25 .entry cue=list-entry name="Breaking the Code"}

- *[Breaking the Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_the_Code_%28film%29)* (1996) – A biographical film based on the life of [Alan Turing](wikipedia://en/1208) adapted from the [1986 play of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_the_Code).

:::

::: {#o26 .entry cue=list-entry name="A Brief History of Time"}

- *[A Brief History of Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Brief_History_of_Time_%28film%29)* (1991) – A biographical documentary film about the physicist [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148), directed by [Errol Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errol_Morris).

:::

::: {#o27 .entry cue=list-entry name="René Descartes"}

- Cartesius (miniseries) (1973) – A miniseries on the life of [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525), directed by [Roberto Rossellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Rossellini).

:::

::: {#o28 .entry cue=list-entry name="Yitang Zhang"}

- *Counting from Infinity: [Yitang Zhang](wikipedia://en/39441844) and the Twin Prime Conjecture* (2015) – A documentary film by [George Paul Csicsery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Paul_Csicsery) about Yitang Zhang, a lecturer at the University of New Hampshire, working in complete isolation and making an important breakthrough towards solving the [twin prime conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_prime_conjecture).

:::

::: {#o29 .entry cue=list-entry name="Enigma"}

- *[Enigma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enigma_%282001_film%29)* (2001) – A story of romantic and psychological intrigue set in [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) during the [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) effort to crack the German [Enigma machine](wikipedia://en/9256).

:::

::: {#o30 .entry cue=list-entry name="Fermat's Last Theorem"}

- *[Fermat's Last Theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_Last_Theorem_%28film%29)* (1997) – A documentary on the mathematician [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027) proving the eponymous theorem, directed by [Simon Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Singh).

:::

::: {#o31 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sun-Yung Alice Chang"}

- *Girls who fell in love with Math* (2017) – Career profiles of mathematicians [Sun-Yung Alice Chang](wikipedia://en/24637925) and [Fan Chung](wikipedia://en/246562).[^o32][^o33]

:::

::: {#o34 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hidden Figures"}

- *[Hidden Figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Figures)* (2016) – African-American mathematicians [Katherine Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Johnson), [Dorothy Vaughan](wikipedia://en/48583314), and [Mary Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Jackson_%28engineer%29) are featured in this film about the early years of the NASA [Project Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Mercury) and [racial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racial_segregation_in_the_United_States) and [sexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_segregation) segregation.

:::

::: {#o35 .entry cue=list-entry name="A Hill on the Dark Side of the Moon"}

- *[A Hill on the Dark Side of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Hill_on_the_Dark_Side_of_the_Moon)* (1983) – A drama film about the professor of mathematics, [Sofya Kovalevskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kovalevskaya).

:::

::: {#o36 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Imitation Game"}

- *[The Imitation Game](wikipedia://en/40281611)* (2014) – British mathematician [Alan Turing](wikipedia://en/1208) ([Benedict Cumberbatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedict_Cumberbatch)), a pioneer in digital computing and artificial intelligence, is tasked with cracking Nazi Germany's Enigma code that would help the Allies win World War II. A new adaptation of the play *Breaking the Code*.

:::

::: {#o37 .entry cue=list-entry name="Infinity"}

- *[Infinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_%281996_film%29)* (1996) – A story about [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize)-winning physicist [Richard Feynman](wikipedia://en/25523) ([Matthew Broderick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Broderick)).

:::

::: {#o38 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Man Who Knew Infinity"}

- *[The Man Who Knew Infinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_Who_Knew_Infinity_%28film%29)* (2015) – The true story of Indian mathematical genius, [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) ([Dev Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dev_Patel)), who develops numerous properties of infinite series as a clerk in India before writing to Cambridge mathematicians who invite him to UK. Directed by Matthew Brown[^o39]

:::

::: {#o40 .entry cue=list-entry name="N Is a Number: A Portrait of Paul Erdős"}

- *[N Is a Number: A Portrait of Paul Erdős](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N_Is_a_Number:_A_Portrait_of_Paul_Erdős)* (1993) – A documentary directed by [George Csicsery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Paul_Csicsery) about the life of Hungarian mathematician [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).

:::

::: {#o41 .entry cue=list-entry name="Ramanujan"}

- *[Ramanujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_%28film%29)* (2014) – A biographical film by [Gnana Rajasekaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnana_Rajasekaran) based on the life of [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717).

:::

::: {#o42 .entry cue=list-entry name="Maryam Mirzakhani"}

- *Secrets of the Surface The Mathematical Vision of [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655)* (2020) – A documentary film by [George Csicsery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Csicsery) about the Fields medalist and Iranian national hero.

:::

::: {#o43 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sofia Kovalevskaya"}

- *[Sofia Kovalevskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kovalevskaya_%28film%29)* (1985) – Epic film in four episodes, based on a true story of mathematician [Sofya Kovalevskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kovalevskaya).

:::

::: {#o44 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Theory of Everything"}

- *[The Theory of Everything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Theory_of_Everything_%282014_film%29)* (2014) – The story of the life and hardships faced by theoretical physicist and mathematician [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148).

:::

::: {#o45 .entry cue=list-entry name="Super 30"}

- *[Super 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_30_%28film%29)* (2019) – A film about the life of mathematician and teacher [Anand Kumar](wikipedia://en/8450166) and its educational program named [Super 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_30).

:::

## With mathematician characters {#With_mathematician_characters}

Films where one or more of the main characters are mathematicians, but that are not otherwise about mathematics:

::: {#o47 .entry cue=list-entry name="It's My Turn"}

- *[It's My Turn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It's_My_Turn_%28film%29)* (1980) – A mathematics professor ([Jill Clayburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jill_Clayburgh)) falls in love with her father's bride's son ([Michael Douglas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Douglas)).

:::

::: {#o48 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Mirror Has Two Faces"}

- *[The Mirror Has Two Faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mirror_Has_Two_Faces)* (1996) – A math professor ([Jeff Bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Bridges)) marries a literature professor ([Barbra Streisand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbra_Streisand)), but they want different things from the relationship.

:::

::: {#o49 .entry cue=list-entry name="Stand and Deliver"}

- *[Stand and Deliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand_and_Deliver)* (1988) – Based on the true story of math teacher [Jaime Escalante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Escalante), who inspired the students at a school in a troubled Hispanic neighborhood to take [Advanced Placement Calculus](wikipedia://en/4234894). The method of [tabular integration](wikipedia://en/147252#Tabular_integration_by_parts) is featured in the film.[^o50]

:::

::: {#o51 .entry cue=list-entry name="Straw Dogs"}

- *[Straw Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straw_Dogs_%281971_film%29)* (1971) – David Sumner ([Dustin Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Hoffman)) is an American mathematical physicist who moves to England, where he and his wife are violently harassed by locals.

:::

::: {#o52 .entry cue=list-entry name="A Summer's Tale"}

- *[A Summer's Tale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Summer's_Tale)* (1996) – A young mathematician vacationing in [Brittany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany).

:::

::: {#o53 .entry cue=list-entry name="Tall Story"}

- *[Tall Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tall_Story)* (1960) – A college physics and mathematics whiz is also the star basketball player, partly because he has devised equations for making baskets.

:::

::: {#o54 .entry cue=list-entry name="A Serious Man"}

- *[A Serious Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Serious_Man)* (2009)

:::

::: {#o55 .entry cue=list-entry name="Incendies"}

- *[Incendies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendies)* (2010) - A graduate student ([Mélissa Désormeaux-Poulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mélissa_Désormeaux-Poulin)), who teaches pure mathematics in Montreal,[^o56] travels to the Middle East in search of her father she thought dead.

:::

## Featuring mathematicians {#Featuring_mathematicians}

Films where one or more of the members of the main cast is a mathematician:

::: {#o58 .entry cue=list-entry name="21 Grams"}

- *[21 Grams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21_Grams)* (2003) – An accident changes many lives, including that of a critically ill mathematics professor ([Sean Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Penn)).

:::

::: {#o59 .entry cue=list-entry name="Antonia's Line"}

- *[Antonia's Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonia's_Line)* (1995) – A genealogical "line" of five generations of women includes a child prodigy, Thérèse, who grows up to be a mathematician.

:::

::: {#o60 .entry cue=list-entry name="Jurassic Park"}

- *[Jurassic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_Park_%28film%29)* (1993) – A mathematician studying [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295) ([Jeff Goldblum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Goldblum)) is among those invited to a [theme park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theme_park) with cloned [dinosaurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaurs), in order to assess its safety.

:::

::: {#o61 .entry cue=list-entry name="The Lost World: Jurassic Park"}

- *[The Lost World: Jurassic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_World:_Jurassic_Park)* (1997) – Mathematician Ian Malcolm ([Jeff Goldblum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Goldblum)) travels to an auxiliary Jurassic Park site to document dinosaurs.

:::
