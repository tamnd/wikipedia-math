# Alfred Klose

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Klose"}

*Image: Alfred-Klose-und-Frau Sommer-1948.jpg.* Alfred Klose and wife, 1948

**Wilhelm Rudolf Alfred Klose** (September 19, 1895 in [Görlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Görlitz) – February 21, 1953 in [Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potsdam))[^o2] was a German applied mathematician and astronomer.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Klose studied at [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau) and [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) from 1916 and was an assistant at the observatory in [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau) from 1917. He received his doctorate in astronomy in 1921 in Breslau under Alexander Wilkens (research on the movement of the planet [189 Phthia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/189_Phthia)). After that he was at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald) and its observatory, where he habilitated in 1922 and became a *[privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)*. In 1923 he habilitated at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). In 1924 he became an associate professor of mechanics and theoretical astronomy at [University of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Latvia) in [Riga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga). In 1929 he became an associate professor of astronomy at [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) and, after [Hilda Geiringer](wikipedia://en/7553047) left in 1933, succeeded her as assistant to [Theodor Vahlen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Vahlen) at the Institute for Applied Mathematics. As an assistant he deputized for Vahlen, who took up a position at the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education) from 1933 and concentrated on his work there, but had succeeded the expelled [Richard von Mises](wikipedia://en/821517) in 1934. In 1937 he became a full professor of applied mathematics and director of the institute. Actually, he had been classified as unsuitable because of his specialization in astronomy. The positive classification by [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186) and Theodor Vahlen played a role in this – Klose was an active National Socialist. The institute went into decline and even in the Second World War no war-related work was done there - instead, Klose and two assistants were drafted. He was the laboratory manager of a test center of the Army Weapons Office for rocket tests in Gottow and at the Kummersdorf firing range near Luckenwalde. On the other hand, one of his assistants, [Karl-Heinz Boseck](wikipedia://en/46187197) (exempt from military service due to illness), had a great influence on the mathematics faculty as a former student leader, member of the SS (he set up a department for numerical computing in the Sachsenhausen concentration camp) and fanatical National Socialist of the Second World War.[^o2][^o4]

In 1939, Klose was also in talks about the successor to [Constantin Caratheodory](wikipedia://en/470625) in Munich (like [Karl Strubecker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Strubecker), among others), which, despite positive reports from Vahlen and Bieberbach (who spoke out in favor of staying in Berlin), resulted in negative assessments by [Oskar Peron](wikipedia://en/1485469) failed. He obtained expert opinions from Ludwig Prandtl and Friedrich Pfeiffer, who were of the opinion that Klose was a particularly unsuspecting ignoramus in the field of applied mathematics.

In 1945/46 he was at the successor company of [GEMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GEMA_%28German_organization%29) in [Köpenick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Köpenick) and from 1946 to 1952 ([Operation Ossawakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Ossawakim)) as one of the German scientists conscripted into service in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). He was sent to Gorodomlija near [Ostashkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostashkov) along with 169 German rocket scientists and engineers, including [Helmut Gröttrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_Gröttrup) and [Fritz Karl Preikschat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Karl_Preikschat). In 1952/53 he was a full professor of applied mathematics at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) and was commissioned to set up an [aeronautical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautical_Engineering) faculty, which only existed there for a short time.[^o5]

[Lothar Collatz](wikipedia://en/1859481) was one of his doctoral students, but he was the last student of [Richard von Mises](wikipedia://en/821517) to complete his work under him and in 1933 he passed the state examination privately with him.

In 1936, Klose was one of the editors of the journal German Mathematics.
