# J-multiplicity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="J-multiplicity"}

In algebra, a **j-multiplicity** is a generalization of a [Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert–Samuel_multiplicity). For *m*-primary ideals, the two notions coincide.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $(R, \mathfrak{m})$ be a [local](wikipedia://en/153391) [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) of [Krull dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) $d > 0$. Then the **j-multiplicity** of an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) *I* is

$$j(I) = j(\operatorname{gr}_I R)$$

where $j(\operatorname{gr}_I R)$ is the normalized coefficient of the degree *d* − 1 term in the Hilbert polynomial $\Gamma_\mathfrak{m}(\operatorname{gr}_I R)$; $\Gamma_\mathfrak{m}$ means the space of sections supported at $\mathfrak{m}$.

:::
