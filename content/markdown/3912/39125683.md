# Complex coordinate space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Complex coordinate space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the [*n*-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2783513) **complex coordinate space** (or **complex *n*-space**) is the set of all ordered *n*-[tuples](wikipedia://en/132729) of [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), also known as *[complex vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_vector)*. The space is denoted $\mathbb{C}^n$, and is the *n*-fold [Cartesian product](wikipedia://en/24104095) of the [complex line](wikipedia://en/7975294) $\mathbb{C}$ with itself. Symbolically,

$$\mathbb{C}^n = \left\{ (z_1,\dots,z_n) \mid z_1, \dots, z_n \in \mathbb{C}\right\}$$

or

$$\mathbb{C}^n = \underbrace{\mathbb{C} \times \mathbb{C} \times \cdots \times \mathbb{C}}_{n}.$$

The variables $z_i$ are the (complex) coordinates on the complex *n*-space. The special case $\mathbb{C}^2$, called the *complex coordinate plane*, is not to be confused with the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628), a graphical representation of the complex line.

:::

Complex coordinate space is a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over the complex numbers, with componentwise addition and [scalar multiplication](wikipedia://en/244373). The real and imaginary parts of the coordinates set up a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) of $\mathbb{C}^n$ with the [2*n*-dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [real coordinate space](wikipedia://en/1482083), $\mathbb R^{2n}$. With the standard [Euclidean topology](wikipedia://en/1482085), $\mathbb{C}^n$ is a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) over the complex numbers.

A function on an open subset of complex *n*-space is holomorphic if it is [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/14110) in each complex coordinate separately. [Several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables) is the study of such holomorphic functions in *n* variables. More generally, the complex *n*-space is the target space for holomorphic coordinate systems on [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742).
