# Mohammed ibn Abdun al-Jabali

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mohammed ibn Abdun al-Jabali"}

**Abu Abd Allah Mohammed ibn Abdun al-Jabali al-Adadi** (محمد بن عبدون الجبلي العذري) (died after 976) was a physician and mathematician from [Al-Andalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Andalus). He is the author of *Risala fi al-Taksir* (Treatise on Measurements), the oldest remaining mathematical text from [Al-Andalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Andalus).[^o2][^o3] He travelled to the learning centers in the East in the years after 958 C.E. He stayed in [Basra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basra) and visited al-Fustat (Old [Cairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo)), Egypt where he was put in charge of the hospital. Ibn Abdun studied the ideas of [Abu Sulayman Sijistani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Sulayman_Sijistani) (d. 990) and according to one source he met him personally in Basra.[^o4] He returned to [Cordoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caliphate_of_Córdoba) in 971 C.E.. He entered the service of the [Caliph al-Mustansir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hakam_II) (died 976) and his son [Hisham II al-Mu'ayad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hisham_II).[^o5] Ibn Abdun was the teacher of [Ibn al-Kattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Kattani).[^o6]

:::
