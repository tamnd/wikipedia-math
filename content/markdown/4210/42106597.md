# Eduardo Barrio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eduardo Barrio"}

**Eduardo Barrio** is an [Argentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_people) [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Since 2005 he is professor of logic at [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) and researcher at [CONICET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONICET). He works in [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888), mainly interested in the notion of *[truth](wikipedia://en/29920)*, especially in semantic paradoxes and the expressive limits of formal languages. He also works on [non-classical logic](wikipedia://en/3192556), [higher-order logic](wikipedia://en/562883), and [unrestricted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range_of_quantification) [quantification](wikipedia://en/43507260). He is also editor-in-chief of the collection "Enciclopedia Lógica", published by [EUDEBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EUDEBA), which includes the first Spanish translation of the [L. T. F. Gamut](wikipedia://en/1091808)'s work *Logic, Language, and Meaning*.[^o4]

He was visiting professor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), CUNY, Oxford University and MCMP. As of 2020, Barrio is currently the director of BA-Logic.[^o5]

He has published three books: *La Verdad Desestructurada*, *Las Lógicas de la Verdad*, (both published by EUDEBA) and *Paradojas, Paradojas y más Paradojas* (published by Collage Pu.). He has several articles published by the best journals in the area: for example, in *Synthese*, *Review of Symbolic Logic*, *Journal of Philosophical Logic, Logic Journal of the IGPL, Studia Logica* and *Analysis*.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14]

## Projects {#Projects}

- Head of the project (with [Hannes Leitgeb](wikipedia://en/40520896) - MCMP - [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich)) "Truth, Paradoxes and Modalities” DAAD-MinCyT, DA/12/01, 2013–2014
- Co-director—along with [Volker Halbach](wikipedia://en/33929531) of a collaborative research group called *Truth, Open-endedness and Inexpressibility*
- Head of the project (with Ole Hjortland - MCMP - LMU Munich) “The Logics of Truth: Operational and Substructural Approaches” (PCB II) CONICET-DFG-MinCyT. 2015–2017
