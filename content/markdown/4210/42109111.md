# Kazimierz Cwojdziński

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kazimierz Cwojdziński"}

**Kazimierz Cwojdziński** (born 8 January 1878 in [Plewiska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plewiska) – died 12 August 1948 in [Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań)) was a Polish mathematician and professor of the School of Engineering in Poznań.[^o2][^o3] Cwojdziński published his works regarding secondary school curriculum and school mathematics in the journals *Wiadomości Matematyczne*, *Muzeum*, *Parametr*, and *Matematyka* as well as the German journal *Archiv der Mathematik und Physik*.[^o2] He was among the first year-group to obtain a doctorate in mathematics from the [Adam Mickiewicz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University).[^o4]

:::

During the Nazi occupation of Poland in [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) Cwojdziński was one of the most active teachers in clandestine schools in [Greater Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Poland).[^o5]
