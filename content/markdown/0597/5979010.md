# Elliptic unit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elliptic unit"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **elliptic units** are certain units of [abelian extensions](wikipedia://en/174448) of [imaginary quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_quadratic_field) constructed using singular values of [modular functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_function), or division values of [elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/69939). They were introduced by Gilles Robert in 1973, and were used by [John Coates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Coates_%28mathematician%29) and [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027) in their work on the [Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](wikipedia://en/435161). Elliptic units are an analogue for imaginary quadratic fields of [cyclotomic units](wikipedia://en/5991396). They form an example of an [Euler system](wikipedia://en/1052176).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A system of elliptic units may be constructed for an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) *E* with [complex multiplication](wikipedia://en/537183) by the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) *R* of an imaginary quadratic field *F*. For simplicity we assume that *F* has [class number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_%28number_theory%29) one. Let **a** be an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of *R* with generator α. For a [Weierstrass model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass's_elliptic_functions) of *E*, define

$$\Theta_{\mathbf{a}}(P) = \alpha^{-12} \Delta_E^{N\mathbf{a} - 1} \prod_{\mathbf{a}P=0, P\ne0} (x-x(P))^{-6} \ .$$

where *P* is a point on *E*, Δ is the discriminant, and *x* is the X-coordinate on the Weierstrass model. The function Θ is independent of the choice of model, and is defined over the field of definition of *E*.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Let **b** be an ideal of *R* coprime to *a* and *Q* an *R*-generator of the **b**-torsion. Then Θ<sub>**a**</sub>(*Q*) is defined over the [ray class field](wikipedia://en/45018204) *K*(**b**), and if **b** is not a prime power then Θ<sub>**a**</sub>(*Q*) is a global unit: if **b** is a power of a prime **p** then Θ<sub>**a**</sub>(*Q*) is a unit away from **p**.

The function Θ<sub>**a**</sub> satisfies a *[distribution relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_relation)* for **b** = (β) coprime to **a**:

$$\prod_{\mathbf{b}Q=0} \Theta_{\mathbf{a}}(P+R) = \Theta_{\mathbf{a}}(\beta P) \ .$$

:::
