# Truncated tetrahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated tetrahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated tetrahexagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are one [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), one [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575), and one [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of tr{6,4}.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are fourteen hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular order-4 hexagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 7 forms with full \[6,4\] symmetry, and 7 with subsymmetry.

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_tetrahexagonal\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated tetrahexagonal tiling with mirror lines in green, red, and blue:

*Image: Tetrahexagonal tiling subgroups.png.* Symmetry diagrams for small index subgroups of \[6,4\], shown in a hexagonal translational cell within a [{6,6} tiling](wikipedia://en/38387527), with a fundamental domain in yellow.

The dual of the tiling represents the fundamental domains of (\*642) [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) symmetry. From \[6,4\] symmetry, there are 15 small index subgroup by mirror removal and [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) operators. Mirrors can be removed if its branch orders are all even, and cuts neighboring branch orders in half. Removing two mirrors leaves a half-order gyration point where the removed mirrors met. In these images unique mirrors are colored red, green, and blue, and alternately colored triangles show the location of gyration points. The \[6<sup>\+</sup>,4<sup>\+</sup>\], (32×) subgroup has narrow lines representing glide reflections. The [subgroup index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgroup_index)-8 group, \[1<sup>\+</sup>,6,1<sup>\+</sup>,4,1<sup>\+</sup>\] (3232) is the [commutator subgroup](wikipedia://en/8847) of \[6,4\].

Larger subgroup constructed as \[6,4\*\], removing the gyration points of \[6,4<sup>\+</sup>\], (3\*22), index 6 becomes ([\*3333](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3333_symmetry)), and \[6\*,4\], removing the gyration points of \[6<sup>\+</sup>,4\], (2\*33), index 12 as ([\*222222](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/222222_symmetry)). Finally their direct subgroups \[6,4\*\]<sup>\+</sup>, \[6\*,4\]<sup>\+</sup>, subgroup indices 12 and 24 respectively, can be given in orbifold notation as (3333) and (222222).
