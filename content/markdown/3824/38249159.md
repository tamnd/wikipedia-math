# Order-6 square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-6 square tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {4,6}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents a hyperbolic [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope) of 4 mirrors meeting as edges of a square, with six squares around every vertex. This symmetry by [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) is called (\*3333) with 4 order-3 mirror intersections. In [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502) can be represented as \[6,4<sup>\*</sup>\], removing two of three mirrors (passing through the square center) in the [\[6,4\] symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/642_symmetry). The \*3333 symmetry can be doubled to [663 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/663_symmetry) by adding a mirror bisecting the fundamental domain.

This bicolored square tiling shows the even/odd reflective fundamental square domains of this symmetry. This bicolored tiling has a [wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) t<sub>1</sub>{(4,4,3)}. A second 6-color symmetry can be constructed from a hexagonal symmetry domain.

### Example artwork {#Example_artwork}

Around 1956, [M.C. Escher](wikipedia://en/20127) explored the concept of representing infinity on a two-dimensional plane. Discussions with Canadian mathematician [H.S.M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328) inspired Escher's interest in hyperbolic tessellations, which are regular tilings of the hyperbolic plane. Escher's wood engravings Circle Limit I–IV demonstrate this concept. The last one *Circle Limit IV (Heaven and Hell)*, (1960) tiles repeating [angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel) and [devils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devil) by (\*3333) symmetry on a hyperbolic plane in a [Poincaré disk](wikipedia://en/30871836) projection.

The artwork seen below has an approximate hyperbolic mirror overlay added to show the square symmetry domains of the order-6 square tiling. If you look closely, you can see one of four angels and devils around each square are drawn as back sides. Without this variation, the art would have a 4-fold [gyration point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyration_point) at the center of each square, giving (4\*3), \[6,4<sup>\+</sup>\] symmetry.[^o6]

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}
