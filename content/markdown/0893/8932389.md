# Kirchhoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kirchhoff"}

**Kirchhoff**, **Kirchoff** or **Kirchhoffer** is a German surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Adolf Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Kirchhoff) (1826–1908), German classical scholar and epigrapher
- [Alfred Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Kirchhoff) (1838–1907), German geographer and naturalist
- [Alphonse Kirchhoffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Kirchhoffer) (1873–1913), French Olympic fencer
- [Bruce K. Kirchoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_K._Kirchoff) (born 1952), American botanist
- [Charles William Henry Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_William_Henry_Kirchhoff) (1853-1916), American editor and metals expert
- [Detlef Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detlef_Kirchhoff) (born 1967), German rower
- [Fritz Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Kirchhoff) (1901–1953), German screenwriter, film producer and director
- [Gustav Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Kirchhoff) (1824–1887), German physicist — Kirchhoff's laws in electricity, spectroscopy, thermochemistry
- [Gottlieb Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottlieb_Kirchhoff) (1764–1833), German chemist
- [Jan Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Kirchhoff) (born 1990), German footballer
- [John Nesbitt Kirchhoffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Nesbitt_Kirchhoffer) (1848–1914), Canadian politician
- [Mary Kirchoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Kirchoff) (born 1959), American fantasy novelist
- [Paul Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Kirchhoff) (1900–1972), German anthropologist and ethnologist of pre-Columbian Mesoamerican cultures
- [Robert Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kirchhoff) (born 1962), Slovak film director
- [Richard A. Kirchhoffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_A._Kirchhoffer) (1890–1977), bishop of the Episcopal Diocese of Indianapolis
- [Ulrich Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulrich_Kirchhoff) (born 1967), German show jumper
- [Walter Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Kirchhoff) (1879–1951), German opera singer
