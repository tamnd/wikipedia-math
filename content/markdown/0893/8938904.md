# Wallace Givens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wallace Givens"}

**James Wallace Givens, Jr.** (December 14, 1910 – March 5, 1993) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a pioneer in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science). He is the eponym of the well-known [Givens rotations](wikipedia://en/680237). Born the son of two teachers in Alberene, [Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia) (a small town near [Charlottesville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottesville)), he obtained his bachelor's degree from their young *[alma mater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alma_mater)*, [Lynchburg College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynchburg_College) in 1928 at the age of 17; his master's degree from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) under Ben Zion Linfield in 1931 (after a one-year fellowship at the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky)); and his doctorate from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1936 under [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen). (Dissertation title: *Tensor Coordinates of Linear Spaces*.)

:::

He was an assistant to Veblen at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) during his doctoral work, and later a professor at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee) in [Knoxville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knoxville,_Tennessee). He also taught at [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University) and [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), and worked early on with [UNIVAC I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNIVAC_I) at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) (NYU) and later with [ORACLE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ORACLE_%28computer%29) at [Oak Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Ridge_National_Laboratory) (both early [vacuum tube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_tube) computers).

In 1963 he was appointed senior scientist at the [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory) near [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), where he was later (1964-1970) director of the Division of Applied Mathematics. From 1968 to 1970 he was fourteenth president of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376). In 1979 he retired as [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professors_in_the_United_States#Professor_emeritus_and_emerita) at Northwestern University. Argonne National Laboratory currently offers a [named fellowship](http://www.anl.gov/postdocs/named-fellowships-luminary-james-wallace-givens) in his honor.
