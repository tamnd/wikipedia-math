# Emmanuel David Tannenbaum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emmanuel David Tannenbaum"}

**Emmanuel David Tannenbaum** (עמנואל דוד טננבאום; June 28, 1978 – May 28, 2012)[^o3] was an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel)/American biophysicist and applied mathematician. He worked as a professor and researcher in the department of chemistry at the [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) and the department of biology at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), specializing in the fields of [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology), [systems biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_biology), and [quantum physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_physics).

:::

Tannenbaum's initial work was in quantum chemistry as part of his [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) doctoral thesis[^o10] where he developed a novel [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) approach to the [EBK quantization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein–Brillouin–Keller_method) of nearly separable Hamiltonians in the quasi-integrable regime. Emmanuel Tannenbaum subsequently devoted his research to studying various problems in [evolutionary dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_dynamics) using [quasispecies](wikipedia://en/25308) models.[^o11] His seminal work centered on the key question of the evolutionary advantages of [sexual reproduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_reproduction). Tannenbaum demonstrated a strong selective advantage for [sexual reproduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_reproduction) with fewer and much less restrictive assumptions than previously considered.[^o4] Closely related to this line of reasoning, was the original work by Tannenbaum and James Sherley on the [immortal strand hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immortal_DNA_strand_hypothesis). Tannenbaum also proposed a pioneering theory of why higher organisms need sleep.[^o5] Towards the end of his life, he proposed a new approach to anti-[stealth technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stealth_technology) based on the theory of [Bose–Einstein condensate](wikipedia://en/4474).[^o6]

Emmanuel Tannenbaum received a number of honors, including the [Robert Karplus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Karplus) Prize in Chemical Physics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), the prestigious [Alon Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alon_Fellowship)[^o7] from the [Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities), and a [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) research fellowship.[^o8] Dr. Tannenbaum is the son of mathematician [Allen Tannenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Tannenbaum) and chemist [Rina Tannenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rina_Tannenbaum). His sister, Sarah Tannenbaum-Dvir, is an oncologist/hematologist.
