# National Statistics and Information Authority

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Statistics and Information Authority"}

The **National Statistics and Information Authority** (**NSIA**, اداره ملی احصائیه و معلومات; د احصایې او معلوماتو ملي اداره), formerly known by other names such the **Central Statistics Office** (ادارۀ مرکزی احصائیه; د احصایې او معلوماتو ملي اداره), is a government agency in [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan) that collects and maintains the country's statistical data.[^o4] It was established in 1972.[^o5] Abdul Qahir Haji Edris serves as NSIA's General Director.[^o6]

:::

NSIA's Civil Registration and Foreign Nationals Registration Department is responsible for registering [citizens of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_of_Afghanistan) in its national database, and providing them with documents such as [national ID cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_identity_card), [birth certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_certificate), [death certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_certificate), [marriage certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_certificate), etc.[^o7] NSIA has branches in all [provinces of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Afghanistan) and in the [United Arab Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates) but its main offices are in [Kabul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabul).[^o8]

NSIA's Civil Registration and Foreign Nationals Registration Department also keeps records of all foreign travelers entering and leaving Afghanistan.[^o9][^o10] The NSIA works closely with the nation's [Ministry of Interior Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_Affairs_%28Afghanistan%29) but is not a part of it.
