# Pauline Barrieu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pauline Barrieu"}

**Pauline Barrieu** is a French [financial statistician](wikipedia://en/7458786), [probability theorist](wikipedia://en/23542), and expert on [financial risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_risk) assessment, [risk transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management), and [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648). She is a professor of statistics in the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Barrieu earned an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) at the [ESSEC Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESSEC_Business_School) in 1997, a [DEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies) in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1998, and a PhD in 2002, simultaneously in finance at [HEC Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Paris) and in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at Pierre and Marie Curie University, supervised by Marc Chesney at HEC Paris and by [Nicole El Karoui](wikipedia://en/26412227) at Pierre and Marie Curie University.

She has been a member of the statistics department at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) since 2002, becoming a professor in 2012 and serving as head of the department for 2016–2019.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2003, Barrieu was one of the winners of the Prix de l'Actuariat, an annual international award for top doctoral dissertations in [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373).

She was the 2018 winner of the [Louis Bachelier Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bachelier_Prize). The award cited her work on "how we address model risk, uncertainty, and risk sharing under uncertainty".
