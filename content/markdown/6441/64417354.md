# Kim Yong-un

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kim Yong-un"}

**Kim Yong-un** (김용운; 6 September 1927 – 30 May 2020) was a South Korean mathematician, philosopher, and critic of civilisations. He was active in various fields of mathematics, philosophy, anthropology, and linguistics, and is considered to have established the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) in Korea. He was a professor of mathematics at [Hanyang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanyang_University).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Kim Yong-un was born in [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo), Japan in 1927.[^o4] He was liberated in the year he entered [Waseda University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waseda_University) and returned to his father's hometown of [Naju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naju), [South Jeolla Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Jeolla_Province) in 1946.[^o5] While working as a math teacher at Mokpo High School and [Gwangju Jeil High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwangju_Jeil_High_School) since 1947, he felt the need to popularize math and later wrote several popular math books.[^o6] After teaching for more than 10 years, he earned a doctorate in science while studying in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).[^o5]

In the late 1970s, he studied the history of mathematics, and since the 1980s, he has analyzed Korea and Japan in a comparative cultural way by combining mathematics and history.[^o6] The original form, which means [collective unconsciousness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_unconscious) of the community, and the locality in which the community is located, were involved in the development of language and history.[^o5] In 1983, he founded the Korean Mathematical History Association and became the first chairman, and in 1994, he participated in the development process of Ungjin Publishing (now Ungjin Thinkbig), a mathematics learning magazine brand of Ungjin Publishing (now Ungjin Thinkbig), along with his younger brother, Professor Kim Yong-guk of Hanyang University. This is also the predecessor of Thinkbig, a learning center brand of Ungjin.[^o7][^o8] He wrote more than 150 books throughout his life.

## Academic background {#Academic_background}

- Bachelor's degree in mining at Waseda University in Japan (dropout)
- Bachelor's degree in mathematics at [Chosun University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chosun_University)[^o10][^o11]
- Graduate School of Orburn University in the U.S. (Bachelor of Science)
- Graduate School of [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta), Canada (Doctor of Science)

## Career {#Career}

- Assistant Professor of [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin_System) (1962–1965)
- Visiting professor at [Kobe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_University) in Japan
- A visiting professor at [Tokyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in Japan
- Guest Professor of the Center for International Cultural Research in Japan
- Professor of Mathematics at Hanyang University (1969–1993)
- President of the Korean Mathematical History Society
- Professor emeritus of mathematics at Hanyang University (1994-current)
- Chairman of the Korea Broadcasting Culture Promotion Agency (2000–2003)
- Director of the Korean Mathematical and Cultural Institute
