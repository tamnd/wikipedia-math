# Michael Eastwood

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Eastwood"}

**Michael G. Eastwood** is a mathematician at the [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide),[^o3] known for his work in [twistor theory](wikipedia://en/260836), conformal differential geometry and invariant differential operators. In 1976[^o4] he received a PhD at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables) under [Robert C. Gunning](wikipedia://en/22647108). He was a member of the twistor research group of [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and he coauthored the monograph *The Penrose Transform: Its Interaction with Representation Theory* with Robert Baston.[^o5] After moving to South Australia in 1985 he was the 1992 recipient of the [Australian Mathematical Society Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Mathematical_Society_Medal)[^o6] and made a [Fellow of the Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science) in 2005.[^o7] In 2012 he was named to the inaugural (2013) class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
