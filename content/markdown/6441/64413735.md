# Christian Reinsch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Reinsch"}

**Christian Reinsch** (1934 – October 8, 2022) was a German mathematician who worked in the area of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).

:::

Reinsch began studying physics in 1953 at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich), graduating in 1958. He received his doctorate in 1961 studying under [Heinz Maier-Leibnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Maier-Leibnitz).

Among other things, he worked in the area of [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and interpolation with [spline functions](wikipedia://en/457680). Several of his works have over two thousand citations each.[^o4] Reinsch died on October 8, 2022.[^o5]
