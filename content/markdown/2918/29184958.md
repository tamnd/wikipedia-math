# Komlós–Major–Tusnády approximation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Komlós–Major–Tusnády approximation"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), the **Komlós–Major–Tusnády approximation** (also known as the **KMT approximation**, the **KMT embedding**, or the **Hungarian embedding**) refers to one of the two **strong embedding** theorems: 1) approximation of [random walk](wikipedia://en/235451) by a standard [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) constructed on the same [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325), and 2) an approximation of the [empirical process](wikipedia://en/3690433) by a [Brownian bridge](wikipedia://en/1843777) constructed on the same [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325). It is named after Hungarian mathematicians [János Komlós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/János_Komlós_%28mathematician%29), [Gábor Tusnády](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gábor_Tusnády), and [Péter Major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Péter_Major), who proved it in 1975.

:::

## Theory {#Theory}

Let $U_1,U_2,\ldots$ be independent [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_distribution_%28continuous%29) (0,1) [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685). Define a uniform [empirical distribution function](wikipedia://en/2690455) as

$$F_{U,n}(t)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbf{1}_{U_i\leq t},\quad t\in [0,1].$$

Define a uniform [empirical process](wikipedia://en/3690433) as

$$\alpha_{U,n}(t)=\sqrt{n}(F_{U,n}(t)-t),\quad t\in [0,1].$$

The [Donsker theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donsker_theorem) (1952) shows that $\alpha_{U,n}(t)$ [converges in law](wikipedia://en/50723) to a [Brownian bridge](wikipedia://en/1843777) $B(t).$ Komlós, Major and Tusnády established a sharp bound for the speed of this weak convergence.

**Theorem** (KMT, 1975) On a suitable [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) for independent uniform (0,1) r.v. $U_1,U_2\ldots$ the empirical process $\{\alpha_{U,n}(t), 0\leq t\leq 1\}$ can be approximated by a sequence of Brownian bridges $\{B_n(t),0\leq t\leq 1\}$ such that

$$P\left\{\sup_{0\leq t\leq 1}|\alpha_{U,n}(t)-B_n(t)|>\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}(a\log n+x)\right\}\leq b e^{-cx}$$

for all positive integers *n* and all $x>0$, where *a*, *b*, and *c* are positive constants.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/corollary cue=named-heading name="Corollary"}

### Corollary {#Corollary}

A corollary of that theorem is that for any real [iid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iid) r.v. $X_1,X_2,\ldots,$ with [cdf](wikipedia://en/5793) $F(t),$ it is possible to construct a probability space where independent sequences of empirical processes $\alpha_{X,n}(t)=\sqrt{n}(F_{X,n}(t)-F(t))$ and [Gaussian processes](wikipedia://en/302944) $G_{F,n}(t)=B_n(F(t))$ exist such that

$\limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{\sqrt{n}}{\ln n} \big\| \alpha_{X,n} - G_{F,n} \big\|_\infty < \infty,$ [almost surely](wikipedia://en/351908).

:::
