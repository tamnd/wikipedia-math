# Themistocles M. Rassias

::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Themistocles M. Rassias"}

**Themistocles M. Rassias** (Θεμιστοκλής Μ. Ρασσιάς; born April 2, 1951) is a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and a professor at the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens) (Εθνικό Μετσόβιο Πολυτεχνείο), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece). He has published more than 300 papers, 10 research books and 45 edited volumes in research [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) as well as 4 [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbooks) in Mathematics (in Greek) for university students. His research work has received more than 21,000 citations according to Google Scholar[^o7] and more than 6,000 citations according to MathSciNet.[^o8] His [h-index](wikipedia://en/3604693) is 52. He serves as a member of the [Editorial Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_Board) of several international mathematical journals.

:::

## Education {#Education}

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) in June 1976. Professor [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294) and Professor [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905) have been his thesis and academic advisors, respectively.

## Research {#Research}

His work extends over several fields of Mathematical Analysis. It includes [Nonlinear Functional Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_Functional_Analysis), [Functional Equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_Equations), [Approximation Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximation_Theory), [Analysis on Manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_on_Manifolds), [Calculus of Variations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_Variations), [Inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489), [Metric Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_Geometry) and their Applications.

He has contributed a number of results in the stability of [minimal submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_submanifold), in the solution of Ulam's Problem for approximate [homomorphisms](wikipedia://en/13564) in [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces), in the theory of [isometric mappings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometric_mapping) in [metric spaces](wikipedia://en/20018) and in [Complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) ([Poincaré's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré's_inequality) and [harmonic mappings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_mapping)).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He has received a number of honors and awards including:

- 1977–1978 and 1978–1979, Membership offer from the [School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics) of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), that he could not accept for family reasons.
- 1980, Research Associate at the Department of Mathematics of [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), invited by [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457).
- 1980, Visiting Research Professor at the Department of Mathematics of the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), invited by F. P. Peterson.
- 1985–1986, 1986–1987, Teacher of the Year.
- 1987, Accademico Ordinario of the Accademia Tiberina, Roma.
- 1989–1990, 1990–1991, 1991–1992, Outstanding Faculty Member, University of La Verne, California (Athens Campus).
- 1991, [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Royal Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Astronomical_Society) of London.
- 2003, A volume entitled *Stability of Functional Equations of Ulam-Hyers-Rassias Type* was dedicated to the 25 years since the publication of Th. M. Rassias' Theorem, edited by S. Czerwik, Hadronic Press Inc., Florida.

- 2008, [Doctor Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Honoris_Causa) (DHC), University of Alba Iulia (Romania).
- 2009, Α special issue of the Journal *Nonlinear Functional Analysis and Applications* (Vol.14, No.5) was dedicated to the 30th Anniversary of Th. M. Rassias' Stability Theorem.

- 2010, [Honorary Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Doctorate), [University of Nis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nis) (Serbia).

- 2012, A volume entitled [*Nonlinear Analysis. Stability, Approximation, and Inequalities. In honor of Themistocles M.
