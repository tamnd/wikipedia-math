# Michele Benzi

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Michele Benzi"}

**Michele Benzi** (born 1962 in [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna)) is an Italian mathematician who works as a full professor in the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa). He is known for his contributions to [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and its applications, especially to the solution of [sparse](wikipedia://en/341015) [linear systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_systems) and the study of [preconditioners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preconditioners).[^o5]

:::

## Previous career {#Previous_career}

He worked as assistant professor at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) from 1993 to 1996, then at Cerfacs in Toulouse from 1996 to 1997, and then at the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory) for three years.

He transferred to the [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University) in Atlanta in 2000, where he held the [endowed chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowed_chair) of [Samuel Candler Dobbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Candler_Dobbs) professor[^o3] starting from 2012 to 2018. Subsequently, he moved back to Italy to the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in Pisa as a full professor.

## Awards {#Awards}

Benzi was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in 2012,[^o5] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2018 "for his contributions in numerical linear algebra, exposition, and service to the profession".[^o8] He was elected as a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2019.[^o9]

As of 2022, he is the [editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief) of the [SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Matrix_Analysis_and_Applications), and he is editor of approximately 20 journals.[^o10] He won a SIAM Outstanding Paper Prize in 2001.[^o11]
