# Langlands classification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Langlands classification"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Langlands classification** is a description of the [irreducible representations](wikipedia://en/385982) of a reductive [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) *G*, suggested by [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986) (1973). There are two slightly different versions of the Langlands classification. One of these describes the irreducible [admissible](wikipedia://en/11055227) (*g*, *K*)-[modules](wikipedia://en/276410), for *g* a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) of a reductive Lie group *G*, with [maximal compact subgroup](wikipedia://en/1496726) *K*, in terms of [tempered representations](wikipedia://en/10995628) of smaller groups. The tempered representations were in turn classified by [Anthony Knapp](wikipedia://en/11072560) and [Gregg Zuckerman](wikipedia://en/10822168). The other version of the Langlands classification divides the irreducible representations into [L-packets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-packet), and classifies the L-packets in terms of certain homomorphisms of the [Weil group](wikipedia://en/6375618) of **R** or **C** into the [Langlands dual group](wikipedia://en/6789133).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Notation"}

## Notation {#Notation}

- *g* is the Lie algebra of a real reductive Lie group *G* in the [Harish-Chandra class](wikipedia://en/11007822).
- *K* is a maximal compact subgroup of *G*, with Lie algebra *k*.
- ω is a [Cartan involution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan_involution) of *G*, fixing *K*.
- *p* is the −1 eigenspace of a Cartan involution of *g*.
- *a* is a maximal abelian subspace of *p*.
- Σ is the [root system](wikipedia://en/277087) of *a* in *g*.
- Δ is a set of [simple roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_root_%28root_system%29) of Σ.

:::

## Classification {#Classification}

The Langlands classification states that the irreducible [admissible representations](wikipedia://en/11055227) of (*g*, *K*) are parameterized by triples

(*F*, σ, λ)

where

- *F* is a subset of Δ
- *Q* is the standard [parabolic subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) of *F*, with [Langlands decomposition](wikipedia://en/11085278) *Q* = *MAN*
- σ is an irreducible tempered representation of the semisimple Lie group *M* (up to isomorphism)
- λ is an element of Hom(*a*<sub>*F*</sub>, **C**) with α(Re(λ)) > 0 for all simple roots α not in *F*.

More precisely, the irreducible admissible representation given by the data above is the irreducible quotient of a parabolically induced representation.

For an example of the Langlands classification, see the [representation theory of SL2(R)](wikipedia://en/11103925).

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Variations"}

## Variations {#Variations}

There are several minor variations of the Langlands classification. For example:

- Instead of taking an irreducible quotient, one can take an irreducible submodule.
- Since tempered representations are in turn given as certain representations induced from discrete series or limit of discrete series representations, one can do both inductions at once and get a Langlands classification parameterized by discrete series or limit of discrete series representations instead of tempered representations. The problem with doing this is that it is tricky to decide when two irreducible representations are the same.

:::
