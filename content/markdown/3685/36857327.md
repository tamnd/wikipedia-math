# Zdzisław Skupień

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdzisław Skupień"}

**Zdzisław Skupień** (27 November 1938 – 1 January 2025) was a Polish mathematician, expert in [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization), [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), academic, and [dr. hab.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) (1982).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

Skupień was born in [Świlcza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Świlcza), Poland.[^o2] In 1964, Skupień introduced the concept of "locally [Hamiltonian graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph)".[^o2][^o6]

In 1976, Skupień introduced the concept of "homogeneously traceable graphs".[^o7]

Skupień authored over 140 publications.[^o2] He died on 1 January 2025, at the age of 86.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1998: [Medal of the Commission for National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_the_Commission_of_National_Education) (Medal Komisji Edukacji Narodowej) [^o2]
- 1988: [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta) (Krzyż Kawalerski Orderu Odrodzenia Polski)[^o2]
- 1983: [Cross of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_of_Merit_%28Poland%29) (Złoty Krzyż Zasługi)[^o2]
