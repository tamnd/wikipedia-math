# Hà Huy Khoái

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hà Huy Khoái"}

**Hà Huy Khoái** (born 24 November 1946, in Ha Tinh) is a Vietnamese mathematician working in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Hà Huy Khoái studied in Vietnam under the "fathers" of Vietnamese mathematics [Lê Văn Thiêm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lê_Văn_Thiêm) and [Hoàng Tụy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoang_Tuy), and in Moscow at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) under [Yuri I. Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_I._Manin).[^o3] He is currently a professor and the director of the Mathematics Institute of [Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Academy_of_Science_and_Technology). He is a senior advisor of the [Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708) Vietnamica journal.[^o4]

His main field of work has been *p*-adic [Nevanlinna theory](wikipedia://en/393135), for example proving part of a non-Archimedean version of [Green's theorem](wikipedia://en/243334) (AMS, 1992, 503-509).[^o5]

## International Mathematical Olympiads {#International_Mathematical_Olympiads}

He has been the Vietnam team leader for several [International Mathematical Olympiads](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o7]
