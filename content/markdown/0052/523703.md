# Myriagon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Myriagon"}

Regular polygon stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **myriagon** or 10000-gon is a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) with 10000 sides. Several philosophers have used the regular myriagon to illustrate issues regarding thought.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Regular myriagon {#Regular_myriagon}

A [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) myriagon is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {10,000} and can be constructed as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) 5000-gon, t{5000}, or a twice-truncated 2500-gon, tt{2500}, or a thrice-truncated 1250-gon, ttt{1250}, or a four-fold-truncated 625-gon, tttt{625}.

The measure of each [internal angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_angle) in a regular myriagon is 179.964°. The [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of a [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) myriagon with sides of length *a* is given by

$$A = 2500a^2 \cot \frac{\pi}{10000}$$

The result differs from the area of its [circumscribed circle](wikipedia://en/161262) by up to 400 [parts per billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parts_per_billion).

Because 10,000 = 2<sup>4</sup> × 5<sup>4</sup>, the number of sides is neither a product of distinct [Fermat primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime) nor a power of two. Thus the regular myriagon is not a [constructible polygon](wikipedia://en/523430). Indeed, it is not even constructible with the use of an angle trisector, as the number of sides is neither a product of distinct [Pierpont primes](wikipedia://en/2595060), nor a product of powers of two and three.

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Symmetries of myriagon.png.* The symmetries of a regular myriagon. Light blue lines show subgroups of index 2. The 5 boxed subgraphs are positionally related by index 5 subgroups.

The *regular myriagon* has Dih<sub>10000</sub> [dihedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_symmetry), order 20000, represented by 10000 lines of reflection. Dih<sub>10000</sub> has 24 dihedral subgroups: (Dih<sub>5000</sub>, Dih<sub>2500</sub>, Dih<sub>1250</sub>, Dih<sub>625</sub>), (Dih<sub>2000</sub>, Dih<sub>1000</sub>, Dih<sub>500</sub>, Dih<sub>250</sub>, Dih<sub>125</sub>), (Dih<sub>400</sub>, Dih<sub>200</sub>, Dih<sub>100</sub>, Dih<sub>50</sub>, Dih<sub>25</sub>), (Dih<sub>80</sub>, Dih<sub>40</sub>, Dih<sub>20</sub>, Dih<sub>10</sub>, Dih<sub>5</sub>), and (Dih<sub>16</sub>, Dih<sub>8</sub>, Dih<sub>4</sub>, Dih<sub>2</sub>, Dih<sub>1</sub>). It also has 25 more [cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327) symmetries as subgroups: (Z<sub>10000</sub>, Z<sub>5000</sub>, Z<sub>2500</sub>, Z<sub>1250</sub>, Z<sub>625</sub>), (Z<sub>2000</sub>, Z<sub>1000</sub>, Z<sub>500</sub>, Z<sub>250</sub>, Z<sub>125</sub>), (Z<sub>400</sub>, Z<sub>200</sub>, Z<sub>100</sub>, Z<sub>50</sub>, Z<sub>25</sub>), (Z<sub>80</sub>, Z<sub>40</sub>, Z<sub>20</sub>, Z<sub>10</sub>), and (Z<sub>16</sub>, Z<sub>8</sub>, Z<sub>4</sub>, Z<sub>2</sub>, Z<sub>1</sub>), with Z<sub>*n*</sub> representing *π*/*n* radian rotational symmetry.

[John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) labels these lower symmetries with a letter and order of the symmetry follows the letter.[^o10] **r20000** represents full symmetry, and **a1** labels no symmetry. He gives **d** (diagonal) with mirror lines through vertices, **p** with mirror lines through edges (perpendicular), **i** with mirror lines through both vertices and edges, and **g** for rotational symmetry.

These lower symmetries allows degrees of freedom in defining irregular myriagons. Only the **g10000** subgroup has no degrees of freedom but can be seen as [directed edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directed_edge).

## Myriagram {#Myriagram}

A myriagram is a 10,000-sided [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303). There are 1999 regular forms given by [Schläfli symbols](wikipedia://en/489394) of the form {10000/*n*}, where *n* is an integer between 2 and 5,000 that is [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to 10,000. There are also 3000 regular [star figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_figure) in the remaining cases.

:::

## In popular culture {#In_popular_culture}

In the novella *[Flatland](wikipedia://en/75214)*, the Chief Circle is assumed to have ten thousand sides, making him not a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), but a myriagon.
