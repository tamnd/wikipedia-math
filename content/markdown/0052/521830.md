# Orthorhombic crystal system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Orthorhombic crystal system"}

In [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography), the **orthorhombic crystal system** is one of the seven [crystal systems](wikipedia://en/456410). Orthorhombic [lattices](wikipedia://en/469246) result from stretching a [cubic lattice](wikipedia://en/666697) along two of its orthogonal pairs by two different factors, resulting in a rectangular [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) with a rectangular [base](wikipedia://en/9579379) (*a* by *b*) and height (*c*), such that *a*, *b*, and *c* are distinct. All three bases intersect at 90° angles, so the three lattice vectors remain mutually [orthogonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal).

:::

## Bravais lattices {#Bravais_lattices}

There are four orthorhombic Bravais lattices: primitive orthorhombic, base-centered orthorhombic, body-centered orthorhombic, and face-centered orthorhombic.

::: {#o3 .table cue=table}

| Bravais lattice | Primitive orthorhombic | Base-centered orthorhombic | Body-centered orthorhombic | Face-centered orthorhombic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Pearson symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearson_symbol) | oP | oS | oI | oF |
| [Unit cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_cell) | *Image: Orthorhombic.svg.* Orthohombic, simple | *Image: Base-centered orthorhombic.svg.* Orthohombic, base-centered | *Image: Orthorhombic-body-centered.svg.* Orthohombic, body-centered | *Image: Orthorhombic-face-centered.svg.* Orthohombic, face-centered |

:::

The [primitive cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_cell) of the base-centered orthorhombic lattice has the shape of a right rhombic prism;[^o6] it can be constructed because the two-dimensional centered rectangular base layer can also be described with primitive rhombic axes. Note that the length $a$ of the primitive cell below equals $\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ of the conventional cell above.

::: {#o8 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="222 (crystal class)"}
