# Cuban prime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cuban prime"}

*Image: visual\_proof\_centered\_hexagonal\_numbers\_sum.svg.* [Proof without words](wikipedia://en/18045517) that the difference between two consecutive cubes is a centered [hexagonal number](wikipedia://en/494013), shown by arranging *n*<sup>3</sup> balls in a cube and viewing them along a [space diagonal](wikipedia://en/1337505) –colors denote horizontal layers and the [dashed lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashed_line) the hexagonal number, respectively.

A **cuban prime** is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) that is also a solution to one of two different specific equations involving differences between third powers of two integers *x* and *y*.

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Centered hexagonal prime"}

## First series {#First_series}

This is the first of these equations:

$p = \frac{x^3 - y^3}{x - y},\ x = y + 1,\ y>0,$[^o4]

i.e. the difference between two successive cubes. The first few cuban primes from this equation are

[7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29), [19](wikipedia://en/208292), [37](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/37_%28number%29), [61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/61_%28number%29), [127](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/127_%28number%29), 271, 331, 397, 547, 631, 919, 1657, 1801, 1951, 2269, 2437, 2791, 3169, 3571, 4219, 4447, 5167, 5419, 6211, 7057, 7351, 8269, 9241, 10267, 11719, 12097, 13267, 13669, 16651, 19441, 19927, 22447, 23497, 24571, 25117, 26227

The formula for a general cuban prime of this kind can be simplified to $3y^2 + 3y + 1$. This is exactly the general form of a [centered hexagonal number](wikipedia://en/497485); that is, all of these cuban primes are centered hexagonal.

the largest known cuban prime has 3,153,105 digits with $y = 3^{3304301} - 1$,[^o5] found by R. Propper and S. Batalov.

:::

## Second series {#Second_series}

The second of these equations is:

$p = \frac{x^3 - y^3}{x - y},\ x = y + 2,\ y>0.$[^o7]

which simplifies to $3y^2 + 6y + 4$. With a substitution $y = n - 1$ it can also be written as $3n^2 + 1, \ n>1$.

The first few cuban primes of this form are:

13, 109, 193, 433, 769, 1201, 1453, 2029, 3469, 3889, 4801, 10093, 12289, 13873, 18253, 20173, 21169, 22189, 28813, 37633, 43201, 47629, 60493, 63949, 65713, 69313

The name "cuban prime" has to do with the role [cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_%28arithmetic%29) (third powers) play in the equations.[^o8]
