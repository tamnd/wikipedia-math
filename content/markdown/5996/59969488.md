# Claude Chabauty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claude Chabauty"}

**Claude Chabauty** (May 4, 1910, in [Oran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oran) – June 2, 1990, in Dieulefit) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He was admitted in 1929 to the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris.[^o3] In 1938 he obtained his doctorate with a thesis on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). Subsequently he was a professor in Strasbourg.[^o4] From 1954 on, and for 22 years, he was the director of the department of pure mathematics at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes).[^o6]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

He worked on [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558) and [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153), where he used both classical and [p-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) analytic methods.[^o6] He introduced the [Chabauty topology](wikipedia://en/7683674) to generalise [Mahler's compactness theorem](wikipedia://en/636427) from Euclidean lattices to more general [discrete subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_subgroup).[^o7]

His 1938 doctoral thesis,[^o8] developing ideas of [Skolem](wikipedia://en/453765),[^o9] is important in algebraic geometry. According to [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019): In his beautiful thesis, Chabauty ..., following ideas of Skolem ..., has shown how the method of *p*-adic completion, with respect to a more or less arbitrary prime *p*, can yield deep results about varieties over an algebraic number-field; there, as already in Skolem's work, the problem concerns the intersection of an algebraic variety and of a multiplicative group; by *p*-adic completion, the latter becomes an algebroid variety defined by linear differential equations.[^o10]
