# Qlik

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Qlik"}

**Qlik** (; formerly known as **Qliktech**) provides a [data integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_integration), analytics, and [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) platform.[^o3] The [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) company was founded in 1993 in [Lund, Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund,_Sweden) and is now based in [King of Prussia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Prussia,_Pennsylvania), United States. Thoma Bravo made the company private in 2016.[^o4]

:::

Qlik Data Integration (QDI) includes tools such as *Qlik Replicate* for data replication, *Qlik Catalog* for data organization, *Qlik Compose* for automation of data lakes and data warehouses, and *Qlik Talend Cloud* for maintenance of data integrity.[^o79][^o5] Qlik's AI program enables AI-powered analytics, natural language capabilities, and visualizations.[^o6] *Qlik Answers* generates answers to questions from [unstructured data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unstructured_data) sources.[^o80] It also includes AutoML for no-code development of predictive models and tools for low-latency data processing.[^o7]

## History {#History}

The company was founded in Lund, Sweden in 1993, by Björn Berg and Staffan Gestrelius. Its Swedish headquarters are still in Lund.[^o9][^o10][^o11] The company's first product, QuikView (now called QlikView), was built to enable users to retrieve detailed data analysis from various systems.[^o12] Måns Hultman became CEO in 2000, and Lars Björk became CFO.[^o11]

The first version of the product came out in 1994, and a patent application was made in 1995.[^o13] *QUIK*, in the original name of the product, stood for Quality, Understanding, Interaction, and Knowledge.[^o11]

QlikView 2 came out in 1996, QlikView 3 in 1997,[^o13] and QlikView 4 in 1999. In 1998, the company received funding from Swedish investors [Handelsbanken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handelsbanken) and Industrifonden[^o11]

The company focused on the area of business intelligence, growing from 35 employees in 1999 to 70 in 2003.[^o14] In 2004, the firm decided to expand internationally and moved the company headquarters to the U.S., while development stayed in Lund.[^o15] That year, it secured \$12.5 million in venture capital funding from equity firms [Accel Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accel_Partners) and [Jerusalem Venture Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Venture_Partners).[^o11]

QlikView 7 was released in 2005, and the single-user desktop tool was replaced with a server-based web tool.[^o16] In 2006, the company had \$44 million in revenue.[^o12]

In 2007, Lars Björk became CEO.[^o17][^o18]

As of April 2011, the company had a [market capitalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_capitalization) over \$2 billion.[^o19] In 2012, [Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes) listed it as one of America's top three fastest-growing tech companies.[^o11]

In 2012, Qlik acquired Expressor Software for metadata management.[^o20]

In 2013, the company opened an office in [Perth, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perth), due to its large consumer base in that country.[^o21][^o22] In August 2015, the company expanded its research and development operations by opening an office in [Ottawa, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa).[^o23]

In 2015 Qlik acquired Vizubi and its NPrinting product.[^o24]

In 2016, private equity firms [Bain Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bain_Capital), [Permira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permira), and [Thoma Bravo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoma_Bravo) submitted offers to buy Qlik.[^o25][^o26] In June, the firm announced that it agreed to sell itself to Thoma Bravo for about \$3 billion.[^o27] In 2017, CEO Lars Bjork transitioned his responsibilities to David Murphy as interim CEO; Mike Capone was named CEO in January 2018.[^o28][^o29]

In 2017, Qlik started working with Philadelphia, Pennsylvania-based [Thomas Jefferson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson_University) and [Jefferson Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Health).[^o30] The two worked together to use data to identify situations where healthcare officials could prescribe fewer opioids, helping to combat the opioid epidemic in Philadelphia.[^o31][^o32][^o33][^o34]

In 2017 Idevio (Geo Analytics) was acquired by Qlik,[^o35] and data catalog company Podium Data[^o36][^o37] in 2018.[^o38] In 2019, the company acquired an AI, natural language bot company, Crunch Data, who it had partnered with for 4 years prior to the acquisition.[^o39] Crunch Data was integrated into its Insight Advisor product.[^o40]

In 2019, the company acquired data integration company Attunity[^o41][^o42] for \$560M which expanded its footprint in the Boston area.

In 2020, the company acquired Knarr Analytic for its real-time collaboration and insight capture capabilities.[^o43] The same year, the company acquired RoxAI for its Ping Alerting and intelligent automation software,[^o44][^o45] and Blendr.io for its data integration platform-as-a-service (iPaaS) to allow for data integration and automation to hundreds of SaaS-based business applications.[^o46][^o47] Qlik was used by the [Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh NHS Foundation Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrightington,_Wigan_and_Leigh_NHS_Foundation_Trust) NHS patient data during the [COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).[^o48]

In 2021, the company introduced Qlik Application Automation, which automates tasks and data workflows between Qlik Cloud and SaaS applications.[^o49][^o50] The same year, the company acquired Salt Lake City-based Big Squid, a leading provider of no-code automated machine learning.[^o51][^o52] The company also went on to acquire NodeGraph, a customizable metadata management solution, to help expand its analytics data pipeline capabilities.[^o53][^o54][^o55]

In 2022, Qlik confidentially filed papers for [IPO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering) with the [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Securities_and_Exchange_Commission) to go public.[^o56]

In 2023, Qlik acquired Talend combining its data integration solutions with Talend’s data transformation, quality, and governance Capabilities.[^o57]

In December 2023, Qlik acquired Mozaic Data, which introduced a Data Product Catalog to streamline data management.[^o58]

In January 2024, Qlik acquired Kyndi, which focused on natural language processing and generative AI to ameliorate the processing of unstructured data.[^o59]

## Products {#Products}

Qlik's Associative Engine lets users do [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) analytics, combining a number of data sources so that associations and connections can be formed across the data.[^o61][^o62] The two main data analytics products,[^o63][^o64] Qlik Sense (current cloud-based software) and QlikView (legacy on-premise product), serve different purposes running on the same engine. QlikView for analyzing static data, while Qlik Sense allows [concatenation](wikipedia://en/64474) of different data sources, allowing drill-down on an individual data record.[^o65]

The Qlik Data Analytics Platform (QDA) offers direct access to Qlik's Associative data engine through open and standard APIs.[^o66] Qlik Data Catalyst is an enterprise data catalog[^o67] management product,[^o68] and Qlik Core is an analytics development platform built around Qlik's Associative engine and company-authored open source libraries.[^o69][^o70]

The Qlik Branch platform and open APIs allow third-party developers to create add-ons that extend Qlik Sense's [visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization) capabilities. Some of the Qlik Sense extension producers include ancoreSoft, [AnyChart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AnyChart), Applybi, ExtendBI, Inphinity, TrueChart, and Vizlib.[^o71]

As in-house add-on products, Qlik GeoAnalytics provides mapping capabilities and built-in support for geoanalytic use cases,[^o72] Qlik DataMarket provides access to a cloud-based service with a variety of external data content that can be used to cross-reference and augment users’ data,[^o73][^o74] and Qlik Connectors are tools that allow QlikView and Qlik Sense to interact with external data sources not accessible through normal [ODBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Database_Connectivity) or [OLE-DB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OLE-DB) data connections[^o75]

The Qlik Data Integration platform, (QDI) includes Qlik Replicate for data replication and real-time data ingestion,[^o76] Qlik Catalog for organizing data,[^o77] and Qlik Compose for data lakes and data warehouse automation.[^o78] Qlik Talend Cloud introduces AI augmented data integration and quality capabilities.[^o79]

Qlik Answers generates answers to questions from unstructured data sources.[^o80]

## Finances {#Finances}

On June 2, 2016, private equity firm Thoma Bravo announced that it would acquire Qlik in an all-cash deal valued at approximately \$3 billion.[^o82] The acquisition was completed in August 2016.[^o83] Thoma Bravo paid \$30.50 per share for Qlik, representing a 40% percent premium to the Company's unaffected 10 day average stock price prior to March 3, 2016 of \$21.83.[^o82]

In January 2022, *[ZDNET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZDNET)* reported that Qlik had confidentially filed papers with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to go public again.[^o84]
