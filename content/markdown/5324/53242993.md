# PyMC

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="PyMC"}

**PyMC** (formerly known as PyMC3) is a [probabilistic programming](wikipedia://en/30431128) library for [Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_%28programming_language%29). It can be used for Bayesian statistical modeling and probabilistic machine learning.

:::

PyMC performs inference based on advanced Markov chain Monte Carlo and/or variational fitting algorithms.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] It is a rewrite from scratch of the previous version of the PyMC software.[^o7] Unlike PyMC2, which had used [Fortran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran) extensions for performing computations, PyMC relies on PyTensor, a Python library that allows defining, optimizing, and efficiently evaluating mathematical expressions involving multi-dimensional arrays. From version 3.8 PyMC relies on [ArviZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArviZ) to handle plotting, diagnostics, and statistical checks. PyMC and [Stan](wikipedia://en/42243853) are the two most popular [probabilistic programming](wikipedia://en/30431128) tools.[^o8] PyMC is an [open source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) project, developed by the community and has been fiscally sponsored by [NumFOCUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NumFOCUS).[^o9]

PyMC has been used to solve inference problems in several scientific domains, including [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy),[^o10][^o11] [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997),[^o12][^o13] molecular biology,[^o14] crystallography,[^o15][^o16] [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry),[^o17] ecology[^o18][^o19] and psychology.[^o20] Previous versions of PyMC were also used widely, for example in climate science,[^o21] public health,[^o22] [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience),[^o23] and parasitology.[^o24][^o25]

After [Theano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theano_%28software%29) announced plans to discontinue development in 2017,[^o26] the PyMC team evaluated [TensorFlow Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TensorFlow) as a computational backend,[^o27] but decided in 2020 to [fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fork_%28software_development%29) Theano under the name Aesara.[^o28] Large parts of the Theano codebase have been refactored and compilation through [JAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_JAX)[^o29] and [Numba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numba) were added. The PyMC team has released the revised computational backend under the name PyTensor and continues the development of PyMC.[^o30]

## Inference engines {#Inference_engines}

PyMC implements non-gradient-based and gradient-based [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) (MCMC) algorithms for Bayesian inference and stochastic, gradient-based [variational Bayesian methods](wikipedia://en/1208480) for approximate Bayesian inference.

- MCMC-based algorithms:
  - No-U-Turn sampler[^o32] (NUTS), a variant of [Hamiltonian Monte Carlo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_Monte_Carlo) and PyMC's default engine for continuous variables
  - [Metropolis–Hastings](wikipedia://en/56107), PyMC's default engine for discrete variables
  - Sequential Monte Carlo for static posteriors
  - Sequential Monte Carlo for [approximate Bayesian computation](wikipedia://en/11864519)
- [Variational inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_inference) algorithms:
  - Black-box Variational Inference[^o33]
