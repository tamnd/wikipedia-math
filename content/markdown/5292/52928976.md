# Abiyun al-Bitriq

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abiyun al-Bitriq"}

**Abiyun al-Bitriq** (ابيون البطريق, ar, [fl.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floruit) 630 CE[^o3]) was a mathematician and a maker of astronomical instruments at the beginnings of Islam.[^o2] He is mentioned in [al-Qifti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qifti)'s *Tarikh al-Hukama* as Anibun (أنبون, ar), and [al-Nadim's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Nadim) *Fihrist*.[^o3] His name is not certain,[^o2] and it was probably *Apion* (Ἀπίων)[^o4] or *Apion [Patrikios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrikios)* (Ἀπίων Πατρίκιος).[^o3] He wrote a book, now lost, titled "*On Operating the Planispherical Astrolabe*" (كتاب العمل بالاسطرلاب المسطح, ar)[^o3][^o2]

:::
