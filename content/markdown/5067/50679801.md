# Vsevolod Romanovsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vsevolod Romanovsky"}

*Image: RomanovskyVI.jpg.* Academician V. I. Romanovsky

**Vsevolod Ivanovich Romanovsky** (Всеволод Иванович Романовский; 4 December 1879, [Verny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaty) – 6 December 1954, [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent)) was a Russian and Soviet mathematician working in [Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic), the founder of the Tashkent school of mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

*Image: Romanovsky student.jpg.* While studying at St. Petersburg University

In 1906 Romanovsky received, under the supervision of [Andrey Markov](wikipedia://en/915), his doctoral degree from [Saint Petersburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University). During 1900–1908 he was a student and then a docent at St. Petersburg University.[^o3][^o4] In 1911–1915 he was a senior lecturer and then professor at the [Imperial University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), in 1915–1918 a professor at the [Imperial University of Warsaw in Rostov-on-Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_University#History), and from 1918 a professor of probability and mathematical statistics at what is now called the [National University of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Uzbekistan) (in [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent)). His doctoral students include [Toshmuhammad Sarimsoqov](wikipedia://en/72249422).

Romanovsky gained an international reputation for his work in mathematical statistics and probability theory. In 1943 he was made an Academician of the [Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic). The Uzbek Academy of Sciences' Romanovsky Institute of Mathematics is named in his honor. Romanovsky was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 in Bologna[^o6] and in 1932 in Zürich.

His body was buried in Tashkent in the Botkin Cemetery.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 1944: [Order of Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Lenin)
- 1948: [Stalin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) of the third class (for the development and introduction of new methods of drawing up the short-term and long-term weather forecasts)
- 16 January 1950: [Order of the Red Banner of Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Red_Banner_of_Labour)[^o8]
- 23 August 2004: [Order of Outstanding Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Outstanding_Merit) – posthumous award by presidential decree of the [Republic of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan)[^o9]
