# Pierre Milman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Milman"}

**Pierre D. Milman** (Пьер Д. Мильман, born 1945[^o2]) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o4]

:::

Milman graduated with a B.A. from the [University of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Moscow) in 1967. He obtained his Ph.D. from the [University of Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tel_Aviv) in 1975 after an interlude of several years as researcher at the Institute of [Chemical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Physics) and then [Solid State Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_State_Physics) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow).[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1997, he was elected Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada). He was awarded a [Killam Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killam_Research_Fellowship) in 2002, and the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) in 2005.[^o4]

## Family {#Family}

Mathematics runs in the Milman family. His father is the mathematician [David Milman](wikipedia://en/5210923), who co-authored the [Krein–Milman theorem](wikipedia://en/3206654). His brother is the mathematician [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman).[^o6]
