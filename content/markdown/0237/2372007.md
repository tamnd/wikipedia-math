# Takeuti's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takeuti's conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Takeuti's conjecture** is the conjecture of [Gaisi Takeuti](wikipedia://en/2593847) that a [sequent formalisation](wikipedia://en/252329) of [second-order logic](wikipedia://en/341428) has [cut-elimination](wikipedia://en/910234) (Takeuti 1953). It was settled positively:

:::

- By [Tait](wikipedia://en/17002765), using a semantic technique for proving cut-elimination, based on work by [Schütte](wikipedia://en/16184365) (Tait 1966);
- Independently by [Prawitz](wikipedia://en/911318) (Prawitz 1968) and Takahashi by a similar technique (Takahashi 1967), although Prawitz's and Takahashi's proofs are not limited to second-order logic, but concern [higher-order logics](wikipedia://en/562883) in general;
- It is a corollary of [Jean-Yves Girard](wikipedia://en/579673)'s syntactic proof of strong normalization for [System F](wikipedia://en/767637).

Takeuti's conjecture is equivalent to the 1-consistency of [second-order arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3542454) in the sense that each of the statements can be derived from each other in the weak system of [primitive recursive arithmetic (PRA)](wikipedia://en/6637022). It is also equivalent to the [strong normalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_normalization) of the Girard/Reynold's [System F](wikipedia://en/767637).
