# Pierre Berthelot

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Berthelot"}

**Pierre Berthelot** ( , ; 27 January 1943 – 7 December 2023)[^o3] was a French mathematician at the [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes). He developed [crystalline cohomology](wikipedia://en/10220067) and [rigid cohomology](wikipedia://en/26002282).

:::
