# Vasili Yakovlevich Zinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vasili Yakovlevich Zinger"}

*Image: ZingerWJ2.jpg.* (unknown date, approximately. 1880–1890)

**Vasili Yakovlevich Zinger** (Василий Яковлевич Цингер; 11 February 1836 – 2 March 1907) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [botanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanist) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). His name is sometimes spelled **Wasili Jakowlewitsch Zinger**.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Zinger was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow). His father was a teacher of mathematics. Zinger graduated in 1859 from the [Imperial Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Moscow_University), where he majored in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He earned a [doctoral degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_degree) in 1867.

## Career {#Career}

Zinger was an active member of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251) and the president of the society (1886–1891).

He also wrote some philosophical essays.

### Zinger as botanist {#Zinger_as_botanist}

Zinger was also a botanist and wrote some works about the [plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plants) of central [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).

*[Zingeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zingeria)* of the [Poaceae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poaceae) was named in honour of Vasily Zinger.[^o5]
