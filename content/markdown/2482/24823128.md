# Vladimir Miklyukov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Miklyukov"}

**Vladimir Michaelovich Miklyukov** (Миклюков, Владимир Михайлович, also spelled Miklioukov or Mikljukov) (8 January 1944 – October 2013) was a Russian educator in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and head of the [Superslow Process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superslow_Process) workgroup based at [Volgograd State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volgograd_State_University).[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1970, as a student of Georgy D. Suvorov at [Donetsk National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donetsk_National_University), he defended his [Ph.D. thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis_or_dissertation) *Theory of Quasiconformal Mappings in Space*.[^o3] In 1981 Miklyukov and his family moved to [Volgograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volgograd). He was transferred to the newly built [Volgograd State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volgograd_State_University) where he became chairman of the Department of [Mathematical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Analysis) and [Theory of Functions](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o7]

His scientific research focused on geometrical analysis. At the same time, he was studying zero [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210) surfaces in [Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9697) and [pseudo-Euclidean spaces](wikipedia://en/10962546), nonlinear elliptic type [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and [quasiregular mappings](wikipedia://en/34304924) of [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652). The main results of that work were related to the following groups of questions:

- The external geometrical structure of zero mean curvature surfaces in Euclidean and pseudo-euclidean spaces; spacelike tubes and bands of zero mean curvature, their stability and instability with respect to small deformations, their life-time, branches, connections between branch points and [Lorentz invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_invariance) characteristics of surfaces;
- Phragmén-Lindelöf type theorems for differential forms; [Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636) type theorems for differential forms with finite or infinite number of different asymptotic tracts; generalizations of Wiman theorem of forms, applications to quasiregular mappings on manifolds; applications of isoperimetric methods to the [Phragmén–Lindelöf principle](wikipedia://en/1377241) for quasiregular mappings on manifolds.

From 1998-2000 Miklyukov was a visiting professor at [Brigham Young University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigham_Young_University).[^o8] In 2009 Miklyukov was named a [Distinguished Scientist of Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_titles_of_the_Russian_Federation).[^o4]
