# Self-dissimilarity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Self-dissimilarity"}

**Self-dissimilarity** is a measure of [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity) defined in a series of papers by [David Wolpert](wikipedia://en/43919399) and [William G. Macready](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_G._Macready).[^o2][^o3] The degrees of self-dissimilarity between the [patterns](wikipedia://en/126706) of a [system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_theory) observed at various [scales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scale_factor) (e.g. the average [matter density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter_density) of a physical body for [volumes](wikipedia://en/32498) at different [orders of magnitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders_of_magnitude)) constitute a complexity "signature" of that system.

:::
