# Regina Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regina Liu"}

**Regina Y. Liu** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). She is a distinguished professor of statistics and chair of the Department of Statistics and Biostatistics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). Her research concerns [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) and [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), including the first formulation of [simplicial depth](wikipedia://en/54727043).PNASAS90

:::

Liu earned her Ph.D. in statistics from Columbia University in 1983, under the supervision of John Raphael Van Ryzin, and joined the Rutgers faculty at that time. She became a distinguished professor at Rutgers in 2001, and department chair in 2005.

Liu became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2005. She is also a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).
