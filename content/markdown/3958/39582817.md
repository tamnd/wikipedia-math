# Maya Stein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maya Stein"}

**Maya Jakobine Stein** is a German mathematician working as a professor at the Department of Mathematical Engineering of the [University of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chile). She is also the vice director and the academic director of the Center for Mathematical Modeling of the University of Chile.[^o2]

:::

After earning a degree in mathematics from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) in 2002,[^o3] she continued for a doctorate in 2005, supervised by [Reinhard Diestel](wikipedia://en/78071340). She then spent three years as a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) with [Yoshiharu Kohayakawa](wikipedia://en/46789502),[^o5] before moving to the University of Chile as an associated researcher in 2008. She obtained an associate professor position in 2016 and was promoted to full professor in 2020.[^o3]

Stein is known for her research in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), in particular in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and her interests include [extremal](wikipedia://en/529568) and [probabilistic](wikipedia://en/173525) combinatorics, [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), as well as structural and algorithmic graph theory and infinite graphs.[^o6][^o7] She has more than 60 publications in these areas.[^o8] She is the vice-chair of the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) activity group for discrete mathematics.[^o9]

## Editorial positions {#Editorial_positions}

- Managing editor for *[Innovations in Graph Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innovations_in_Graph_Theory)*[^o11]
- Editor-in-Chief for *[Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*[^o12]
- Associate editor for *[SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/30666823)*[^o13]
- Associate editor for *Orbita Mathematicae*[^o14]
