# Matrix geometric method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matrix geometric method"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), the **matrix geometric method** is a method for the analysis of [quasi-birth–death processes](wikipedia://en/11219399), [continuous-time Markov chain](wikipedia://en/1125342) whose [transition rate matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_rate_matrix) has a repetitive block structure.[^o2] The method was developed "largely by [Marcel F. Neuts](wikipedia://en/73888681) and his students starting around 1975."[^o3]

:::

## Method description {#Method_description}

The method requires a transition rate matrix with [tridiagonal](wikipedia://en/519218) block structure as follows

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix} B_{00} & B_{01} \\ B_{10} & A_1 & A_2 \\ & A_0 & A_1 & A_2 \\ && A_0 & A_1 & A_2 \\ &&& A_0 & A_1 & A_2 \\ &&&& \ddots & \ddots & \ddots \end{pmatrix}$$

where each of *B*<sub>00</sub>, *B*<sub>01</sub>, *B*<sub>10</sub>, *A*<sub>0</sub>, *A*<sub>1</sub> and *A*<sub>2</sub> are matrices. To compute the stationary distribution *π* writing *π* *Q* = 0 the [balance equations](wikipedia://en/21990785) are considered for sub-vectors *π*<sub>*i*</sub>

$$\begin{aligned} \pi_0 B_{00} + \pi_1 B_{10} &= 0\\ \pi_0 B_{01} + \pi_1 A_1 + \pi_2 A_0 &= 0\\ \pi_1 A_2 + \pi_2 A_1 + \pi_3 A_0 &= 0 \\ & \vdots \\ \pi_{i-1} A_2 + \pi_i A_1 + \pi_{i+1} A_0 &= 0\\ & \vdots \\ \end{aligned}$$

Observe that the relationship

$$\pi_i = \pi_1 R^{i-1}$$

holds where *R* is the Neuts' rate matrix,[^o5] which can be computed numerically. Using this we write

$$\begin{aligned} \begin{pmatrix}\pi_0 & \pi_1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}B_{00} & B_{01} \\ B_{10} & A_1 + RA_0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \end{aligned}$$

which can be solve to find *π*<sub>0</sub> and *π*<sub>1</sub> and therefore iteratively all the *π*<sub>*i*</sub>.

## Computation of *R* {#Computation_of_R}

The matrix *R* can be computed using [cyclic reduction](wikipedia://en/39291274)[^o7] or logarithmic reduction.[^o8][^o9]

## Matrix analytic method {#Matrix_analytic_method}

The matrix analytic method is a more complicated version of the matrix geometric solution method used to analyse models with block [M/G/1](wikipedia://en/33378064) matrices.[^o11] Such models are harder because no relationship like *π*<sub>*i*</sub> = *π*<sub>1</sub> R<sup>*i* – 1</sup> used above holds.[^o12]
