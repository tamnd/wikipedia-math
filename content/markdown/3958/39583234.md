# Étale homotopy type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Étale homotopy type"}

In mathematics, especially in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **étale homotopy type** is an analogue of the [homotopy type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_type) of [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) for [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808).

:::

Roughly speaking, for a variety or [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) *X*, the idea is to consider étale coverings $U \rightarrow X$ and to replace each [connected component](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_component_%28topology%29) of *U* and the higher "intersections", i.e., [fiber products](wikipedia://en/56444110), $U_n := U \times_X U \times_X \dots \times_X U$ (*n*+1 copies of *U*, $n \geq 0$) by a single point. This gives a [simplicial set](wikipedia://en/1027784) which captures some information related to *X* and the [étale topology](wikipedia://en/5707590) of it.

Slightly more precisely, it is in general necessary to work with [étale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etale_morphism) [hypercovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercover) $(U_n)_{n \geq 0}$ instead of the above [simplicial scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_scheme) determined by a usual étale cover. Taking finer and finer hypercoverings (which is technically accomplished by working with the [pro-object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro-object) in simplicial sets determined by taking all hypercoverings), the resulting object is the étale homotopy type of *X*. Similarly to classical topology, it is able to recover much of the usual data related to the étale topology, in particular the [étale fundamental group](wikipedia://en/3022906) of the scheme and the [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) of locally constant étale [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466).

## Example {#Example}

For $Spec(k)$, morally taking $Spec(\bar{k})$ the simplicial complex would be the one computing $BGal(k)$.

:::

## Modern perspective {#Modern_perspective}

The pro-etale site gives a refinement and generates a homotopical object in simplicial profinite sets. This has a [forgetful functor](wikipedia://en/844783) to the [homotopy category](wikipedia://en/2040756) of profinite simplicial sets.
