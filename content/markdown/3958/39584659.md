# Spectral expansion solution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spectral expansion solution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), the **spectral expansion solution method** is a technique for computing the stationary probability distribution of a [continuous-time Markov chain](wikipedia://en/1125342) whose state space is a semi-infinite lattice strip.[^o2] For example, an [M/M/c queue](wikipedia://en/23152853) where service nodes can breakdown and be repaired has a two-dimensional state space where one dimension has a finite limit and the other is unbounded. The stationary distribution vector is expressed directly (not as a transform) in terms of eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a matrix polynomial.[^o3][^o4]

:::
