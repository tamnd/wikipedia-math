# Multiplicative sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Multiplicative sequence"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **multiplicative sequence** or ***m*-sequence** is a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) associated with a formal [group](wikipedia://en/19447) structure. They have application in the [cobordism ring](wikipedia://en/391832) in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *K*<sub>*n*</sub> be polynomials over a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *A* in indeterminates *p*<sub>1</sub>, ... weighted so that *p*<sub>*i*</sub> has weight *i* (with *p*<sub>0</sub> = 1) and all the terms in *K*<sub>*n*</sub> have weight *n* (in particular *K*<sub>*n*</sub> is a polynomial in *p*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *p*<sub>*n*</sub>). The sequence *K*<sub>*n*</sub> is *multiplicative* if the map

$$K: \sum_{n = 0}^\infty q_nz^n\mapsto \sum_{n=0}^\infty K_n(q_1,\cdots, q_n)z^n$$

is an [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) of the multiplicative [monoid](wikipedia://en/19652) $(A[x_1, x_2,\cdots][[z]],\cdot)$, where $q_n\in A[x_1, x_2,\cdots]$.

The [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)

$$K(1+z) = \sum K_n(1,0,\ldots,0) z^n$$

is the *characteristic power series* of the *K*<sub>*n*</sub>. A multiplicative sequence is determined by its characteristic power series *Q*(*z*), and every [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) with constant term 1 gives rise to a multiplicative sequence.

To recover a multiplicative sequence from a characteristic power series *Q*(*z*) we consider the coefficient of *z*<sup>*j*</sup> in the product

$$\prod_{i=1}^m Q(\beta_i z) \$$

for any *m* > *j*. This is symmetric in the *β*<sub>*i*</sub> and homogeneous of weight *j*: so can be expressed as a polynomial *K*<sub>*j*</sub>(*p*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *p*<sub>*j*</sub>) in the [elementary symmetric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_symmetric_function) *p* of the *β*. Then *K*<sub>*j*</sub> defines a multiplicative sequence.

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

As an example, the sequence *K*<sub>*n*</sub> = *p*<sub>*n*</sub> is multiplicative and has characteristic power series 1 + *z*.

Consider the power series

$$Q(z) = \frac{\sqrt z}{\tanh \sqrt z} = 1 - \sum_{k=1}^\infty (-1)^k \frac{2^{2k}}{(2k)!} B_k z^k \$$

where *B*<sub>*k*</sub> is the *k*-th [Bernoulli number](wikipedia://en/4964). The multiplicative sequence with *Q* as characteristic power series is denoted *L*<sub>*j*</sub>(*p*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *p*<sub>*j*</sub>).

The multiplicative sequence with characteristic power series

$$Q(z) = \frac{2\sqrt z}{\sinh 2\sqrt z} \$$

is denoted *A*<sub>*j*</sub>(*p*<sub>1</sub>,...,*p*<sub>*j*</sub>).

The multiplicative sequence with characteristic power series

$$Q(z) = \frac{z}{1-\exp(-z)} = 1 + \frac{x}{2} - \sum_{k=1}^\infty (-1)^k \frac{B_k}{(2k)!} z^{2k} \$$

is denoted *T*<sub>*j*</sub>(*p*<sub>1</sub>,...,*p*<sub>*j*</sub>): these are the *[Todd polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_polynomial)*.

:::

## Genus {#Genus}

The *genus* of a multiplicative sequence is a [ring homomorphism](wikipedia://en/26411), from the [cobordism ring](wikipedia://en/391832) of smooth oriented [compact manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_manifold) to another ring, usually the ring of [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024).

For example, the [Todd genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_genus) is associated to the Todd polynomials with characteristic power series $\frac{z}{1-\exp(-z)}$.
