# Applied Mathematics Letters

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Applied Mathematics Letters"}

***Applied Mathematics Letters*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published monthly by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier). Established in 1988,[^o3] it focuses on short and rapid communications in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o4] Its current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are [Alan Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Tucker) ([Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University)) and Patrick Winkert ([Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)).[^o5]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences[^o7]
- [EBSCO databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_Information_Services)[^o8]
- [Ei Compendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ei_Compendex)[^o8]
- [Inspec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspec)[^o8]
- [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)[^o8]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o7]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o8]
- [zbMATH](wikipedia://en/1701961)[^o8]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2025 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.7.[^o9]
