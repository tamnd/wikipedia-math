# Journal of Mathematical Fluid Mechanics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Mathematical Fluid Mechanics"}

***Journal of Mathematical Fluid Mechanics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published quarterly by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). Established in 1999,[^o3] it covers mathematical theory of [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) with a focus on [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395).[^o4] Its current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Giovanni Paolo Galdi](wikipedia://en/79571079) ([University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh)).[^o5]

:::
