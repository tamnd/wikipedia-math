# Journal of Differential Equations

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Differential Equations"}

***Journal of Differential Equations*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published semi-monthly by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier). Established in 1965,[^o3] it focuses on the theory and the application of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).[^o4] Its current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are [Adrian Constantin](wikipedia://en/66341566) ([University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna)), Kening Lu ([Sichuan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_University)) and [Juncheng Wei](wikipedia://en/17265646) ([Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong)).[^o5]

:::
