# Sy Friedman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sy Friedman"}

**Sy-David Friedman** (born May 23, 1953, in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago)) is an American and [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a (retired) professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and the former director of the Kurt Gödel Research Center for Mathematical Logic. His main research interest lies in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), in particular in [set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatic_Set_Theory) and [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory).

:::

Friedman is the brother of Ilene Friedman and the brother of mathematician [Harvey Friedman](wikipedia://en/3038108).

## Biography {#Biography}

He studied at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) and, from 1970, at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1976 from MIT (his thesis *Recursion on Inadmissible Ordinals* was written under the supervision of [Gerald E. Sacks](wikipedia://en/3050504)).

In 1979, Sy Friedman accepted a position at MIT, and in 1990 he became a full professor there. Since 1999, he has been a professor of mathematical logic at the University of Vienna, and he retired in 2018.[^o4] He is a Fellow of [Collegium Invisibile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collegium_Invisibile).[^o5]
