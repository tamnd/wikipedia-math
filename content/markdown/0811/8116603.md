# COSIC

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="COSIC"}

The **Computer Security and Industrial Cryptography** research group, commonly called **COSIC**, is a [research group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_group) at the Department of Electrical Engineering of [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven), which is headed by [Bart Preneel](wikipedia://en/1520490).[^o3]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Research and expertise in digital security:[^o5]

- Security architectures for information and communication systems
- Cryptographic algorithms and protocols
  - Symmetric key
  - Public key
  - Post-quantum
- Security for embedded systems
- Privacy-preserving systems

Applications:

- Cloud
- Automotive
- Privacy
- Data Protection
- Trusted Systems
- E-payments
- E-documents
- ...

### AES {#AES}

One of the well-known successes is the selection of Rijndael as the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260) (AES), which has millions of users. There are over a thousand applications, such as the protection of US government information.

## Research projects {#Research_projects}

COSIC has participated in over 50 European research projects.[^o8]
