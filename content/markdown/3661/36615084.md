# Brian McGuinness

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brian McGuinness"}

**Brian McGuinness** (22 October 1927 – 23 December 2019) was a British [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [Wittgenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittgenstein) scholar best known for his translation, with [David Pears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pears), of the *[Tractatus](wikipedia://en/30062)*,[^o3] and for his biography of the first half of Wittgenstein's life.

:::

He was christened with the forenames "**Bernard Francis**" but changed his name to "Brian" in his youth.[^o5] He commonly published, and was cited, as **B. F. McGuinness**.

## Biography {#Biography}

He went up to [Balliol College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College) in 1945 where he was tutored by [R. M. Hare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._M._Hare).[^o5] McGuinness was a Fellow and Tutor at [Queen's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Oxford) in [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) from 1953 to 1988, and took a post at the [Netherlands Institute for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Institute_for_Advanced_Study). In 1990, he became a professor at the [University of Siena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Siena), Italy. From 1990 to 1993, he was director of the faculty of philosophy and social sciences of this university.[^o6] During his time at Queen's, he was an invited speaker at the Oxford Socratic Club, speaking with J. D. Mabbott on "The Problem of Free Will" on 14 November 1955.

## Family {#Family}

His son, [Paddy McGuinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_McGuinness_%28intelligence_officer%29), is a former British civil servant who was the Deputy [National Security Adviser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Advisor_%28United_Kingdom%29) for Intelligence.[^o8] His daughter, [Catherine McGuinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_McGuinness_%28English_politician%29), is a lawyer with a senior position in the [City of London Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_Corporation).[^o9]
