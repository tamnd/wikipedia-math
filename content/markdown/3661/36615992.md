# Mariano Giaquinta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mariano Giaquinta"}

**Mariano Giaquinta** (born [Caltagirone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltagirone), 1947), is an Italian mathematician mainly known for his contributions to the fields of [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and regularity theory of [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564). He is currently professor of Mathematics at the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa)[^o3][^o4] and he is the director of [De Giorgi center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Giorgi_center) at [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa).[^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Giaquinta is well known for his basic work in elliptic regularity theory, and especially in the setting of vectorial variational problems. Together with [Enrico Giusti](wikipedia://en/32836383) he has obtained innovative results[^o7][^o8][^o9] on the regularity of minima of variational integrals and related singular sets. The main novelty is in the fact that, for the first time, the regularity of minimizers is obtained using directly the minimality properties without appealing to [Euler–Lagrange equation](wikipedia://en/294995) of the functionals, which in general is not supposed to exist in the cases considered. His work with [Giuseppe Modica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Modica) on the local higher integrability properties of solutions to elliptic systems has had an influence on the development of partial regularity theory.[^o10] Many of these results are summarized in his 1983 book.[^o11]

Giaquinta has been one of the founders, and for many years the managing editor, of the journal "Calculus of Variations and PDE".

## Awards {#Awards}

Giaquinta won the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize) of the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092) in 1979, in 1990 he was awarded with [Humboldt research award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) and in 2006 with the [Amerio Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amerio_Prize). He has been an invited speaker at the 1986 [International congress of mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_congress_of_mathematicians). Giaquinta belongs to the [ISI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information) list of highly cited researchers in Mathematics [^o13] and he is a member of the [German Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).
