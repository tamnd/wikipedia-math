# Frederik Schuh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frederik Schuh"}

*Image: Frederik Schuh.jpg.* Frederik Schuh

**Frederik Schuh** (7 February 1875, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam) – 6 January 1966, [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague)) was a Dutch mathematician.

:::

## Career {#Career}

He completed his PhD in algebraic geometry from Amsterdam University in 1905, where his advisor was [Diederik Johannes Korteweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diederik_Johannes_Korteweg). He taught at the Technische Hoogeschool at Delft (1907–1909 and 1916–1945) and at Groningen (1909–1916).[^o4]

## Works {#Works}

He was the inventor of the [Chomp](wikipedia://en/1306265) game and wrote *The Master Book of Mathematical Recreations* (1943).
