# Wendy Myrvold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wendy Myrvold"}

**Wendy Joanne Myrvold** is a Canadian mathematician and computer scientist known for her work on [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithm), [planarity testing](wikipedia://en/15972636), and algorithms in [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533). She is a professor emeritus of computer science at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria).

:::

Myrvold completed her Ph.D. in 1988 at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). Her dissertation, *The Ally and Adversary Reconstruction Problems*, was supervised by [Charles Colbourn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Colbourn).
