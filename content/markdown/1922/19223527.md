# 14 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="14"}

**14** (**fourteen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [13](wikipedia://en/37231) and preceding [15](wikipedia://en/208252).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

### Properties {#Properties}

14 is the third distinct [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542), being the third of the form $2 \times q$ (where $q$ is a higher prime). More specifically, it is the first member of the second cluster of two discrete semiprimes (14, [15](wikipedia://en/208252)); the next such cluster is ([21](wikipedia://en/209248), [22](wikipedia://en/362193)), members whose sum is the fourteenth prime number, [43](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43_%28number%29).

14 has an [aliquot sum](wikipedia://en/12374274) of [10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_%28number%29), within an [aliquot sequence](wikipedia://en/486266) of two composite numbers (14, [10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_%28number%29), [8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_%28number%29), [7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29), [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), 0) in the prime **7**-aliquot tree.

14 is the third [companion Pell number](wikipedia://en/1355482) and the fourth [Catalan number](wikipedia://en/216488).[^o7][^o8] It is the lowest even $n$ for which the [Euler totient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_totient) $\varphi(x) = n$ has no solution, making it the first even [nontotient](wikipedia://en/748422).[^o9]

According to the [Shapiro inequality](wikipedia://en/5558590), 14 is the least number $n$ such that there exist $x_{1}$, $x_{2}$, $x_{3}$, where:[^o10]

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{x_i}{x_{i+1}+x_{i+2}} < \frac{n}{2},$$

with $x_{n+1} = x_{1}$ and $x_{n+2} = x_{2}.$

A [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) to which it is applied [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) and [complement](wikipedia://en/54347) operations in any possible sequence generates **14** distinct sets.[^o11] This holds even if the reals are replaced by a more general [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450); see [Kuratowski's closure-complement problem](wikipedia://en/10104622).

There are fourteen [even](wikipedia://en/143135) numbers that cannot be expressed as the sum of two odd [composite numbers](wikipedia://en/82289):

$$\{2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 22, 26, 28, 32, 38\}$$

where 14 is the seventh such number.

#### Polygons {#Polygons}

There are fourteen [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) formed by the [sides](wikipedia://en/12158034) and [diagonals](wikipedia://en/349251) of a [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) six-sided [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733). In a [hexagonal lattice](wikipedia://en/2868762), there are fourteen fixed two-dimensional [triangular](wikipedia://en/30654)-celled [polyiamonds](wikipedia://en/185271) with four cells.

There are fourteen [elements](wikipedia://en/23471#Elements) in a [regular heptagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_heptagon) (where there are seven [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) and edges), and the total number of [diagonals](wikipedia://en/349251) between all its vertices.

There are fourteen polygons that can fill a [plane-vertex tiling](wikipedia://en/1548669#Plane-vertex_tilings), where five polygons tile the plane [uniformly](wikipedia://en/1548669), and nine others only tile the plane alongside irregular polygons.[^o16][^o17]

*Image: Klein quartic in 14-gon.svg.* The fundamental domain of the [Klein quartic](wikipedia://en/390976) is a regular hyperbolic 14-sided [tetradecagon](wikipedia://en/6058229), with an area of $8\pi$.

The [Klein quartic](wikipedia://en/390976) is a compact [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) of genus 3 that has the largest possible [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) order of its kind (of order [168](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/168_%28number%29)) whose fundamental domain is a regular hyperbolic 14-sided [tetradecagon](wikipedia://en/6058229), with an area of $8\pi$ by the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss-Bonnet_theorem).

#### Solids {#Solids}

*Image: square-pyramidal-14.png.* 14 is a [square pyramidal number](wikipedia://en/542343).

*Image: stella-octangula-14.png.* 14 is a [stella octangula number](wikipedia://en/34110891).

Several distinguished [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra) in [three dimensions](wikipedia://en/3054853) contain fourteen [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) or [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) as [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647):

- The [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), one of two [quasiregular polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiregular_polyhedra), has 14 faces and is the only [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223) with [radial equilateral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radial_equilateral_symmetry).[^o19]
- The [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631), [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) to the cuboctahedron, contains 14 vertices and is the only [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393) that can [tessellate](wikipedia://en/321671) space.[^o20]
- The [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654) contains 14 faces, is the [permutohedron](wikipedia://en/12745947) of order four, and the only [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847) to tessellate space.
- The [dodecagonal prism](wikipedia://en/3929468), which is the largest [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) that can tessellate space alongside other uniform prisms, has 14 faces.
- The [Szilassi polyhedron](wikipedia://en/7652409) and its dual, the [Császár polyhedron](wikipedia://en/11731171), are the simplest [toroidal polyhedra](wikipedia://en/24804975); they have 14 vertices and 14 triangular faces, respectively.[^o21][^o22]
- [Steffen's polyhedron](wikipedia://en/53375446), the simplest [flexible polyhedron](wikipedia://en/9070022) without self-crossings, has 14 triangular faces.[^o23]

A regular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) [cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29), the simplest [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223) and [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905), is made up of a total of **14** [elements](wikipedia://en/39781#Elements): 4 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 6 vertices, and 4 faces.

- Szilassi's polyhedron and the tetrahedron are the only two known polyhedra where each face shares an edge with each other face, while Császár's polyhedron and the tetrahedron are the only two known polyhedra with a continuous [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) boundary that do not contain any [diagonals](wikipedia://en/349251).
- Two tetrahedra that are joined by a common edge whose four adjacent and opposite faces are replaced with two specific seven-faced *crinkles* will create a new flexible polyhedron, with a total of 14 possible *clashes* where faces can meet.[^o24]<sup>pp.10-11,14</sup> This is the second simplest known triangular flexible polyhedron, after Steffen's polyhedron.[^o24]<sup>p.16</sup> If three tetrahedra are joined at two separate opposing edges and made into a single flexible polyhedron, called a *2-dof flexible polyhedron*, each hinge will only have a total range of motion of 14 degrees.[^o24]<sup>p.139</sup>

14 is also the root (non-unitary) trivial [stella octangula number](wikipedia://en/34110891), where two [self-dual tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) are represented through [figurate numbers](wikipedia://en/325260), while also being the first non-trivial [square pyramidal number](wikipedia://en/542343) (after [5](wikipedia://en/6412297)); the simplest of the ninety-two [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) is the [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455) $J_{1}.$ There are a total of fourteen [semi-regular polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-regular_polytope), when the [pseudorhombicuboctahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudorhombicuboctahedron) is included as a non-[vertex transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_transitive) [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847) (a lower class of polyhedra that follow the five Platonic solids).[^o27][^o28]

Fourteen possible [Bravais lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bravais_lattices) exist that fill three-dimensional space.

#### G<sub>2</sub> {#G2}

The [exceptional Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/6573043) **[G<sub>2</sub>](wikipedia://en/292864)** is the simplest of five such algebras, with a minimal [faithful representation](wikipedia://en/2617872) in fourteen dimensions. It is the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [octonions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octonions) $\mathbb {O}$, and holds a compact form [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to the [zero divisors](wikipedia://en/51441) with entries of [unit norm](wikipedia://en/990534) in the [sedenions](wikipedia://en/51434), $\mathbb {S}$.[^o31][^o32]

### Riemann zeta function {#Riemann_zeta_function}

The [floor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floor_function) of the [imaginary part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_part) of the first non-trivial zero in the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) is $14$, in equivalence with its [nearest integer](wikipedia://en/169945) value, from an approximation of $14.1347251417\ldots$[^o37]

## In religion and mythology {#In_religion_and_mythology}

### Christianity {#Christianity}

There is a fourteen-point silver star marking the traditional spot of Jesus’ [birth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nativity_of_Jesus) in the [Basilica of the Nativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Nativity) in [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem). According to the [genealogy of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genealogy_of_Jesus) in the [Gospel of Matthew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gospel_of_Matthew), “...there were fourteen generations in all from [Abraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham) to [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David), fourteen generations from David to the [exile to Babylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylonian_captivity), and fourteen from the exile to the [Messiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ)” (Matthew 1:17).

### Islam {#Islam}

In Islam, 14 has a special significance because of [the Fourteen Infallibles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fourteen_Infallibles) who are especially revered and important in [Twelver Shi'ism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelver_Shi'ism). They are all considered to be infallible by Twelvers alongside [the Prophets of Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prophets_and_messengers_in_Islam), however these fourteen are said to have a greater significance and closeness to [God.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allah)

These fourteen include:

1. [Prophet Muhammad (SAWA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad)
1. His daughter, [Lady Fatima (SA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatima)
1. Her husband, [Imam Ali (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali)
1. His son, [Imam Hasan (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasan_ibn_Ali)
1. His brother, [Imam Husayn (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husayn_ibn_Ali)
1. His son, [Imam Ali al-Sajjad (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_al-Sajjad)
1. His son, [Imam Muhammad al-Baqir (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_al-Baqir)
1. His son, [Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ja'far_al-Sadiq)
1. His son, [Imam Musa al-Kazim (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musa_al-Kazim)
1. His son, [Imam Ali al-Rida (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Reza)
1. His son, [Imam Muhammad al-Jawad (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_al-Jawad)
1. His son, [Imam Ali al-Hadi (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_al-Hadi)
1. His son, [Imam Hasan al-Askari (AS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasan_al-Askari)
1. His son, [Imam Muhammad al-Mahdi (AJTFS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_al-Mahdi)
