# Alexander Molev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Molev"}

**Alexander Ivanovich Molev** (Алекса́ндр Ива́нович Мо́лев) (born 1961) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)-[Australian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia) mathematician.

:::

He completed his Ph.D. in 1986 under the supervision of [Alexandre Kirillov](wikipedia://en/3772515) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). He was awarded the [Australian Mathematical Society Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Mathematical_Society_Medal) in 2001 and became a Fellow of the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) in 2019.

Amongst other things, he has worked on [Yangians](wikipedia://en/15272567) and [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944).

He is currently a Professor in the [School of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_School_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics), [Faculty of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Faculty_of_Science), [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney).
