# Renata Mansini

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Renata Mansini"}

**Renata Mansini** (born 22 August 1968) is an Italian [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969), economist, and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476) known for her research on problems in [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) including [portfolio optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portfolio_optimization) and [vehicle routing](wikipedia://en/7799668). She is a professor of operations research at the [University of Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brescia).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mansini earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in economics and business from the [University of Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brescia) in 1991–1992, winning a prize from the Associazione Italiana di Studio del Lavoro for the best thesis in applied mathematics. She completed a doctorate in 1996–1997 at the [University of Bergamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergamo), with the dissertation *Modelli di programmazione lineare mista intera per problemi finanziari: analisi, algoritmi e risultati computazionali* \[*mixed integer linear programming models for financial problems: analysis, algorithms, and computational results*\].

## Book {#Book}

Mansini is the co-author, with Włodzimierz Ogryczak and [M. Grazia Speranza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Grazia_Speranza), of the book *Linear and Mixed Integer Programming for Portfolio Optimization* (EURO Advanced Tutorials on Operational Research, Springer, 2015).
