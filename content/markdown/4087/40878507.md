# Fluid limit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fluid limit"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), a discipline within the mathematical [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), a **fluid limit**, **fluid approximation** or **fluid analysis** of a stochastic model is a deterministic real-valued process which approximates the evolution of a given stochastic process, usually subject to some scaling or limiting criteria.

:::

Fluid limits were first introduced by Thomas G. Kurtz publishing a [law of large numbers](wikipedia://en/157055) and [central limit theorem](wikipedia://en/39406) for [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876).[^o2][^o3] It is known that a queueing network can be stable, but have an unstable fluid limit.[^o4]
