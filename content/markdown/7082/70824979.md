# 1941 Romanian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1941 Romanian census"}

The **1941 Romanian census** (Recensământul General al României din 1941) was conducted on 6 April 1941 in all territories still remaining in the [Kingdom of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Romania), following the loss of land to [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) ([Northern Transylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Transylvania)), [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria) ([Southern Dobruja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Dobruja)), and the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) ([Bessarabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessarabia), [Hertsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertsa_region), and [Northern Bukovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Bukovina)).[^o3] After the [Axis invasion of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Barbarossa), Romania retook control of its lands that the Soviet Union had invaded, in which it conducted the census separately in the autumn of 1941. Later, Romania also annexed the [Transnistria Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnistria_Governorate), finishing the census by conducting it there in December 1941.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

The census-taking process was overseen by Friedrich Burgdörfer, the chairman of the Bavarian Statistical Office of [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany). After a six-day trip across multi-ethnic Romanian regions, he reported to [Sabin Manuilă](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabin_Manuilă) and [Ion Antonescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Antonescu) (then the [leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conducător) of Romania), praising the methods of the census and predicting that it would offer an accurate count.[^o7] Hungarian statistician Varga E. Árpád has stated that the data, specifically with reference to ethnic [Hungarians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians) in [Southern Transylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Transylvania), is quite correct.[^o8] Despite this, several other Hungarian ethnographers and demographers continue to dispute the numbers found by the census.[^o9] The provisional results of the census were first publicly released in 1944 by the [National Institute of Statistics](wikipedia://en/8718156).[^o7]

Notably, it was the first Romanian census to include ethnic origin as a separate category.[^o9] The census, on Manuilă's direction, also included a special section cataloguing all [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews)-owned property, a summary of which was sent to the [German Main Security Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS-Reichssicherheitshauptamt).[^o10]
