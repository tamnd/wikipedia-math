# Index of a Lie algebra

Algebras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Index of a Lie algebra"}

In algebra, let **g** be a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) **K**. Let further $\xi\in\mathfrak{g}^*$ be a [one-form](wikipedia://en/314692) on **g**. The stabilizer **g**<sub>*ξ*</sub> of *ξ* is the Lie subalgebra of elements of **g** that annihilate *ξ* in the [coadjoint representation](wikipedia://en/3226011). The **index of the Lie algebra** is

:::

$$\operatorname{ind}\mathfrak{g}:=\min\limits_{\xi\in\mathfrak{g}^*} \dim\mathfrak{g}_\xi.$$

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Reductive Lie algebras {#Reductive_Lie_algebras}

If **g** is [reductive](wikipedia://en/863720) then the index of **g** is also the rank of **g**, because the [adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_representation_of_a_Lie_algebra) and coadjoint representation are isomorphic and rk **g** is the minimal dimension of a stabilizer of an element in **g**. This is actually the dimension of the stabilizer of any regular element in **g**.

### Frobenius Lie algebra {#Frobenius_Lie_algebra}

If ind **g** = 0, then **g** is called *Frobenius Lie algebra*. This is equivalent to the fact that the [Kirillov form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirillov_form) $K_\xi\colon \mathfrak{g\otimes g}\to \mathbb{K}:(X,Y)\mapsto \xi([X,Y])$ is non-singular for some *ξ* in **g**<sup>\*</sup>. Another equivalent condition when **g** is the Lie algebra of an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) *G*, is that **g** is Frobenius if and only if *G* has an open orbit in **g**<sup>\*</sup> under the coadjoint representation.

### Lie algebra of an algebraic group {#Lie_algebra_of_an_algebraic_group}

If **g** is the Lie algebra of an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) *G*, then the index of **g** is the [transcendence degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendence_degree) of the field of rational functions on **g**<sup>\*</sup> that are invariant under the (co)adjoint action of *G*.[^o7]
