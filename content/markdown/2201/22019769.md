# Levitzky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Levitzky"}

**Levitzky** is a surname descending from the name [Lev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev_%28given_name%29), [East Slavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Slavic_languages) form of [Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_%28given_name%29). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Dmitry Levitzky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Levitzky) (1735–1822), painter
- [Károly Levitzky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Károly_Levitzky) (1885–1978), rower
- [Steven Levitsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Levitsky) (1968–present), political scientist
