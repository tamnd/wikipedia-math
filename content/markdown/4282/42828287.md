In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **tetrahedral-square tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) and [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram), , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::
