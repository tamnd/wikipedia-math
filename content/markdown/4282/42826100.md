# Kleinian integer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kleinian integer"}

In [mathematical cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_cryptography), a **Kleinian integer** is a [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) of the form $m+n\frac{1+\sqrt{-7}}{2}$, with *m* and *n* [rational integers](wikipedia://en/14563). They are named after [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887).

:::

The Kleinian integers form a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) called the **Kleinian ring**, which is the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) in the imaginary [quadratic field](wikipedia://en/454781) $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$. This ring is a [unique factorization domain](wikipedia://en/60546).
