# Thompson uniqueness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thompson uniqueness theorem"}

In mathematical [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) theory, Thompson's original **uniqueness theorem** (Feit Thompson 1963, theorems 24.5 and 25.2) states that in a minimal [simple](wikipedia://en/47600) finite [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of odd [order](wikipedia://en/493527) there is a unique maximal [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) containing a given [elementary abelian subgroup](wikipedia://en/4843202) of [rank](wikipedia://en/18720088) 3. (Bender 1970) gave a shorter proof of the uniqueness theorem.

:::
