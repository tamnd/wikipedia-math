# Mathematics Magazine

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematics Magazine"}

***Mathematics Magazine*** is a [refereed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) bimonthly publication of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). Its intended audience is teachers of collegiate mathematics, especially at the junior/senior level, and their students. It is explicitly a journal of pedagogy rather than research. Rather than articles in the terse "theorem-proof" style of [research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) journals, it seeks articles which provide a context for the mathematics they deliver, with examples, applications, illustrations, and historical background.[^o4] Paid circulation in 2008 was 9,500 and total circulation was 10,000.[^o5]

:::

*Mathematics Magazine* is a continuation of *Mathematics News Letter* (1926–1934) and *National Mathematics Magazine* (1934–1945).[^o6] [Doris Schattschneider](wikipedia://en/39957951) became the first female editor of *Mathematics Magazine* in 1981. [^o7][^o8]

The MAA gives the [Carl B. Allendoerfer](wikipedia://en/3466784) [Awards](wikipedia://en/40343255) annually "for articles of expository excellence" published in *Mathematics Magazine*.[^o9]
