# Roland Fraïssé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Fraïssé"}

**Roland Fraïssé** (; 12 March 1920 – 30 March 2008[^o3]) was a French [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Fraïssé received his doctoral degree from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1953. In his thesis,[^o5][^o6] Fraïssé used the [back-and-forth method](wikipedia://en/2033586) to determine whether two [model-theoretic](wikipedia://en/19858) [structures](wikipedia://en/4055928) were [elementarily equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementarily_equivalent). This method of determining elementary equivalence was later formulated as the [Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game](wikipedia://en/1040475).

Fraïssé worked primarily in [relation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_relations). Another of his important works was the [Fraïssé construction](wikipedia://en/62592127) of a Fraïssé limit of finite structures.

He also formulated [Fraïssé's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraïssé's_conjecture) on order embeddings, and introduced the notion of compensor in the [theory of posets](wikipedia://en/23572).[^o7]

Most of his career was spent as Professor at the [University of Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Provence) in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille), France.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Sur quelques classifications des systèmes de relations*, thesis, University of Paris, 1953; published in *Publications Scientifiques de l'Université d'Alger*, series A **1** (1954), 35–182.
- *Cours de logique mathématique*, Paris: Gauthier-Villars Éditeur, 1967; second edition, 3 vols., 1971–1975; tr. into English and ed. by David Louvish as *Course of Mathematical Logic*, 2 vols., Dordrecht: Reidel, 1973–1974.
- *Theory of relations*, tr. into English by P. Clote, Amsterdam: North-Holland, 1986; rev. ed. 2000.
