# Mathematika

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematika"}

***Mathematika*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) journal that publishes both pure and applied mathematical articles. The journal was founded by [Harold Davenport](wikipedia://en/991111) in the 1950s. The journal is published by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), on behalf of the journal's owner [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London).

:::

## Indexing and abstracting {#Indexing_and_abstracting}

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2023 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.8.[^o4] The journal is indexed in the following [bibliographic databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliographic_database):[^o5]

- [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)
- [Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science)
- [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)
