# Normal-exponential-gamma distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normal-exponential-gamma distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **normal-exponential-gamma distribution** (sometimes called the NEG distribution) is a three-parameter family of continuous [probability distributions](wikipedia://en/23543).[^o2] It has a [location parameter](wikipedia://en/17516) $\mu$, [scale parameter](wikipedia://en/769503) $\theta$ and a [shape parameter](wikipedia://en/9210865) $k$ .

:::

## Probability density function {#Probability_density_function}

The [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) (pdf) of the normal-exponential-gamma distribution is proportional to

$$f(x;\mu, k,\theta) \propto \exp{\left(\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{4\theta^2}\right)}D_{-2k-1}\left(\frac{|x-\mu|}{\theta}\right),$$

where *D* is a [parabolic cylinder function](wikipedia://en/1762360).

As for the [Laplace distribution](wikipedia://en/1106213), the pdf of the NEG distribution can be expressed as a [mixture](wikipedia://en/28897820) of [normal distributions](wikipedia://en/21462),

$$f(x;\mu, k,\theta)=\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty\ \mathrm{N}(x| \mu, \sigma^2)\mathrm{Exp}(\sigma^2|\psi)\mathrm{Gamma}(\psi|k, 1/\theta^2) \, d\sigma^2 \, d\psi,$$

where, in this notation, the distribution-names should be interpreted as meaning the density functions of those distributions.

Within this [scale mixture](wikipedia://en/28897820), the scale's *mixing distribution* (an [exponential](wikipedia://en/45906) with a [gamma](wikipedia://en/207079)-distributed rate) actually is a [Lomax distribution](wikipedia://en/32253318).

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The distribution has heavy tails and a sharp peak at $\mu$ and, because of this, it has applications in [variable selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_selection).
