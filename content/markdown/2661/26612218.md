# Lehmer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lehmer"}

**Lehmer** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Derrick Norman Lehmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Norman_Lehmer) (1867–1938), number theorist who produced tables of prime factors and mechanical devices like [Lehmer sieves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehmer_sieve)
- [Derrick Henry Lehmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Henry_Lehmer) (1905–1991), number theorist known for [Lucas–Lehmer test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas–Lehmer_test), son of D. N. Lehmer and husband of Emma Lehmer
- [Emma Lehmer](wikipedia://en/7682439) (1906–2007), Russian mathematician, known for her work on reciprocity laws, wife of D. H. Lehmer
- [Kat Lehmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Lehmer), American film director, writer, actor and artist
