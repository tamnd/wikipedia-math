# Ahmed I. Zayed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ahmed I. Zayed"}

**Ahmed I. Zayed** (born August 14, 1948) is an [Egyptian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Egyptian_Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) focusing on [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and its applications. He is a professor at [DePaul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DePaul_University) of the Department of Mathematical Sciences in the DePaul College of Science and Health.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zayed earned bachelor's and master's degrees from [Cairo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_University) in 1970 and 1974. He completed a PhD at the [University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Milwaukee) in 1979.[^o3] His dissertation, *Generalized Functions and Boundary Value Problems*, was jointly supervised by Gilbert G. Walter.

He was on the faculty of [California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Polytechnic_State_University,_San_Luis_Obispo), joining the university as an assistant professor in 1980 and subsequently promoted to associate and full professor. He moved to the [University of Central Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Florida) in 1990, and to his current position as professor at DePaul in 2001.[^o3]

## Books {#Books}

Zayed is the author of books including:

- *Advances in Shannon's Sampling Theory* (CRC Press, 1993)[^o6]
- *Handbook of Function and Generalized Function Transformations* (CRC Press, 1996)[^o7]
- *Fractional Integral Transforms: Theory and Applications* (CRC Press, 2024)

His [edited volumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edited_volume) include:

- *Sampling, Wavelets, and Tomography* (with John J. Benedetto, Birkhäuser, 2004)
- *Multiscale Signal Analysis and Modeling* (with Xiaoping Shen, Springer, 2013)
