# Stretching field

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stretching field"}

In [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), **stretching fields** provide the local deformation of an infinitesimal circular fluid element over a finite time interval ∆*t*. The logarithm of the stretching (after first dividing by ∆*t*) gives the finite-time [Lyapunov exponent](wikipedia://en/166441) λ for separation of nearby fluid elements at each point in a flow. For periodic two-dimensional flows, stretching fields have been shown to be closely related to the mixing of a passive scalar concentration field. Until recently, however, the extension of these ideas to systems that are non-periodic or weakly turbulent has been possible only in numerical simulations.

:::
