# Edgar H. Brown

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edgar H. Brown"}

**Edgar Henry Brown, Jr.** (December 27, 1926 – December 22, 2021)[^o3] was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), and for many years a professor at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University).[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Brown was born in [Oak Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Park,_Illinois), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois).[^o3] He completed his bachelor's degree in mathematics at the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) in 1949. He completed his master's degree in mathematics at [Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_University) in 1951.

## Career {#Career}

He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1954. His doctoral supervisor was [George W. Whitehead](wikipedia://en/13641389), and his doctoral dissertation was on *Finite Computability of the Homotopy Groups of Finite Groups*.

In 1962–63, he visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey),[^o8] and in 1964 he received the [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship).[^o9] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1974[^o10] and a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.

He died on December 22, 2021, just before his 95th birthday, as a Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at Brandeis University.[^o11]

## Contributions to mathematics {#Contributions_to_mathematics}

He made numerous contributions to mathematics, including:

- [Brown's representability theorem](wikipedia://en/1073746)
- [Brown–Peterson cohomology](wikipedia://en/13698492)
- [Brown–Gitler spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown–Gitler_spectrum)

His publications include:
