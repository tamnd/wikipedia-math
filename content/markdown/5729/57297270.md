# Autumn Kent

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Autumn Kent"}

**Autumn Kent** is an American mathematician specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). She is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and Vilas Associate at the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).[^o3][^o4][^o5] She is a [transgender woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender_woman) and a promoter of [trans rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_rights).[^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kent received her B.A. from the [University of North Carolina at Asheville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Asheville) in 1999.[^o13] Originally, she had planned to become a high-school English teacher, but she switched to mathematics, majoring in it and in literature.[^o10] She earned her Ph.D. under the advising of [Cameron McAllan Gordon](wikipedia://en/4695115) at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in 2006; her dissertation was entitled *Geometry and Algebra of Hyperbolic 3-manifolds*. After a four-year position as the Tamarkin Assistant Professor of Mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University),[^o10] she joined the faculty of University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2010. She became associate professor in 2016, and a full professor in 2020.[^o13][^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Kent has published over 20 papers in various journals, mostly regarding [low dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). Many of these were published under her former name, or [deadname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadname), before she came out as transgender.[^o6][^o8][^o13]

Kent served on the Policy and Advocacy Committee for the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from February 2019 through January 2022.[^o14] In addition, she served on the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Panel Discussion Promoting Inclusion in [STEM](wikipedia://en/3437663) at the 2019 [Joint Mathematics Meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Mathematics_Meeting).[^o15][^o16] With [Harrison Bray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Bray), she organized the LG&TBQ+ conference at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) to foster collaboration between [LGBTQ+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT) mathematicians working in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) with funding from Kent's [NSF Career Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards).[^o20][^o17] She gave the Spectra Lavender Lecture at the 2022 Joint Mathematics Meeting.[^o18]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1999, Kent won the Frank Gerth Dissertation Award.[^o10] In 2014, she was awarded an [NSF Career Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) to study moduli of [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181).[^o20] In 2015 and 2016, she was a von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o21] She was named as a Vilas Associate at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) for 2018 and 2019.[^o4][^o22] In 2019, she received a [Simons Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o23][^o24][^o25][^o26]

She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows.[^o27]
