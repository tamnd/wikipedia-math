# Alan Gaius Ramsay McIntosh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alan Gaius Ramsay McIntosh"}

**Alan Gaius Ramsay McIntosh** (17 January 1942 – 8 August 2016) was an Australian mathematician who dealt with analysis ([harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564)). He was a professor at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University) in Canberra.[^o3]

:::

McIntosh was born in Sydney, New South Wales on 17 January 1952.[^o4] He studied at the [University of New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29) with a bachelor's degree in 1962 (as a student he also received the University Medal ) and PhD in 1966 with [Frantisek Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frantisek_Wolf) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), for his thesis "Representation of Accretive Bilinear Forms in Hilbert Space by Maximal Accretive Operator ". At Berkeley, he was also a student of [Tosio Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosio_Kato). As a post-doctoral student, he was at the Institute for Advanced Study and from 1967 he taught at [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University), moving to the Australian National University in 1999.[^o4] In 2014 he became emeritus.

McIntosh was involved in solving the [Calderon conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calderon_conjecture) in the theory of [singular integral operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_integral_operator).

In 2002, he solved with [Pascal Auscher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Auscher), [Michael T. Lacey](wikipedia://en/6508239), [Philipp Tchamitchian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Tchamitchian) and [Steve Hofmann](wikipedia://en/7717092) the open [Kato root problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kato_root_problem) for [elliptic differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_differential_operator).

He also worked on [singular integral operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_integral_operator), [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) of partial differential equations with applications (such as [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory) of the [Maxwell equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_equations) in irregular areas), [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) and [functional calculus](wikipedia://en/965348) of operators in [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), analysis with [Clifford algebras](wikipedia://en/45305), barriers for the [heat kernel equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_kernel_equation) and functional calculus for [elliptic partial differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_partial_differential_operator).

In 1986 he became a fellow of the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science), whose [Hannan Medal](wikipedia://en/23265859) he received in 2015.[^o5] In 2002 he received the [Moyal Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moyal_Medal) from Macquarie University.[^o6]
