# István Fáry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="István Fáry"}

**István Fáry** (30 June 1922 – 2 November 1984) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people)-born [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).[^o3] He proved [Fáry's theorem](wikipedia://en/6805386) that every [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314) has a [straight-line embedding](wikipedia://en/12015517) in 1948, and the [Fáry–Milnor theorem](wikipedia://en/3133127) lower-bounding the curvature of a nontrivial [knot](wikipedia://en/366808) in 1949.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fáry was born June 30, 1922, in [Gyula, Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyula,_Hungary). After studying for a master's degree at the [University of Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Budapest), he moved to the [University of Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged), where he earned a Ph.D. in 1947. He then studied at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) before taking a faculty position at the [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal) in 1955. He moved to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1958 and became a full professor in 1962. He died on November 2, 1984, in [El Cerrito, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cerrito,_California).[^o3]
