# Gennadii Rubinstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gennadii Rubinstein"}

**Gennadii Shlemovich Rubinstein** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Геннадий Шлемович Рубинштейн) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] His research focused on [mathematical programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_programming) and [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476). His name is associated to the **[Kantorovich](wikipedia://en/194660)–Rubinstein metric**, also commonly known as the **[Wasserstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Vaseršteĭn) distance**, used in optimal transport.

:::

- Alternate form of the first name: Gennady.
- Alternate forms of the last name: Rubinšteĭn, Rubinshtein.

## Doctorate {#Doctorate}

Gennadii Rubinstein got his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [St. Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_State_University) in 1956, under the supervision of [Leonid V. Kantorovich](wikipedia://en/194660).
