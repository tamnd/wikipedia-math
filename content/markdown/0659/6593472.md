# Structure theorem for Gaussian measures

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Structure theorem for Gaussian measures"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **structure theorem for Gaussian measures** shows that the [abstract Wiener space](wikipedia://en/6592746) construction is essentially the only way to obtain a [strictly positive](wikipedia://en/6607051) [Gaussian measure](wikipedia://en/6572909) on a [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989). It was proved in the 1970s by [Kallianpur](wikipedia://en/39349487)–Satô–Stefan and [Dudley](wikipedia://en/3824977)–[Feldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Feldman)–[le Cam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucien_le_Cam).

:::

There is the earlier result due to H. Satô (1969) [^o2] which proves that "any Gaussian measure on a separable Banach space is an [abstract Wiener measure](wikipedia://en/6592746) in the sense of [L. Gross](wikipedia://en/64592988)". The result by Dudley et al. generalizes this result to the setting of Gaussian measures on a general [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752).

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let *γ* be a strictly positive Gaussian measure on a separable Banach space (*E*, || ||). Then there exists a [separable Hilbert space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_Hilbert_space) (*H*, ⟨ , ⟩) and a map *i* : *H* → *E* such that *i* : *H* → *E* is an abstract Wiener space with *γ* = *i*<sub>∗</sub>(*γ*<sup>*H*</sup>), where *γ*<sup>*H*</sup> is the [canonical](wikipedia://en/515096) Gaussian [cylinder set measure](wikipedia://en/6571387) on *H*.

:::
