# H-derivative

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="H-derivative"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the ***H*-derivative** is a notion of [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) in the study of [abstract Wiener spaces](wikipedia://en/6592746) and the [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $i : H \to E$ be an abstract Wiener space, and suppose that $F : E \to \mathbb{R}$ is [differentiable](wikipedia://en/2735727). Then the [Fréchet derivative](wikipedia://en/2735727) is a map

$$\mathrm{D} F : E \to \mathrm{Lin} (E; \mathbb{R});$$

i.e., for $x \in E$, $\mathrm{D} F (x)$ is an element of $E^{*}$, the [dual space](wikipedia://en/7988) to $E$.

Therefore, define the **$H$-derivative** $\mathrm{D}_{H} F$ at $x \in E$ by

$$\mathrm{D}_{H} F (x) := \mathrm{D} F (x) \circ i : H \to \mathbb{R},$$

a [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) [linear map](wikipedia://en/18102) on $H$.

Define the **$H$-gradient** $\nabla_{H} F : E \to H$ by

$$\langle \nabla_{H} F (x), h \rangle_{H} = \left( \mathrm{D}_{H} F \right) (x) (h) = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{F (x + t i(h)) - F(x)}{t}.$$

That is, if $j : E^{*} \to H$ denotes the [adjoint](wikipedia://en/667175) of $i : H \to E$, we have $\nabla_{H} F (x) := j \left( \mathrm{D} F (x) \right)$.

:::
