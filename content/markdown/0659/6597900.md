# Identity theorem for Riemann surfaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Identity theorem for Riemann surfaces"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **identity theorem for [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181)** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) that states that a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) is completely determined by its values on any subset of its domain that has a [limit point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_point).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $X$ and $Y$ be Riemann surfaces, let $X$ be connected, and let $f, g : X \to Y$ be holomorphic. Suppose that $f|_{A} = g|_{A}$ for some subset $A \subseteq X$ that has a limit point, where $f|_{A} : A \to Y$ denotes the [restriction](wikipedia://en/1589032) of $f$ to $A$. Then $f = g$ (on the whole of $X$).

:::
