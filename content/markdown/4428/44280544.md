# William B. Gragg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William B. Gragg"}

**William B. Gragg** (2 November 1936 – 25 December 2016) ended his career as an emeritus professor in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School). He has made fundamental contributions in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), particularly the areas of [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and [numerical methods for ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/272829).

:::

He received his PhD at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) in 1964 under the direction of [Peter Henrici](wikipedia://en/31964476). His dissertation work resulted in the [Gragg Extrapolation](wikipedia://en/20221609) method[^o3] for the numerical solution of [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations) (sometimes also called the [Bulirsch–Stoer algorithm](wikipedia://en/20221609)).

Gragg is also well known for his work on the [QR algorithm](wikipedia://en/594072) for [unitary](wikipedia://en/129222) [Hessenberg matrices](wikipedia://en/519204), on updating the [QR factorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_factorization),[^o4] superfast solution of [Toeplitz systems](wikipedia://en/151569),[^o5] parallel algorithms for solving eigenvalue problems,[^o6][^o7] as well as his exposition on the [Pade table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pade_table) and its relation to a large number of algorithms in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o8]
