# Inverted bell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Inverted bell"}

The **inverted bell** is a [metaphorical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphor) name for a [geometric shape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_shape) that resembles a [bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_%28instrument%29) upside-down.

:::

## By context {#By_context}

In [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture), the term is applied to describe the shape of the [capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_%28architecture%29) of [Corinthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinthian_order) [columns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Column).

The inverted bell is used in shape classification in [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery),[^o3] often featured in [archaeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeology) as well as in modern times.

In statistics, a [bimodial distribution](wikipedia://en/1068378) is sometimes called an [inverted bell curve](wikipedia://en/33641902).
