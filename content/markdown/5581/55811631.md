# Paul Mansion

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Mansion"}

**Paul Mansion** (3 June 1844 – 16 April 1919) was a Belgian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), editor of the journal *[Mathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathesis_%28journal%29)*.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Mansion was the ninth of ten brothers. His father died when he was only a baby and he was brought up by his mother and his older brothers. He studied at [Huy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huy) school and high school.[^n1] In 1862 he entered in the *École Normale des Sciences*, attached to the [University of Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ghent), where he graduated in 1865. From this time till 1867 he taught mathematics in the artillery academy in [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent), while he was working in his doctoral thesis. He was awarded PhD in 1867.[^n2]

In 1867, after the death of his professor Mathias Schaar, he was appointed to the chair of calculus at the university of Ghent.[^n3] He remained there until he was appointed to the chair of probability in 1892.[^n4] Also, from 1884, he taught the history of mathematics.

In 1874, with [Eugène Catalan](wikipedia://en/639553), he founded the journal *Nouvelle Correspondence Mathématique*, and in 1880, with [Joseph Neuberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Neuberg), he founded the journal *[Mathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathesis_%28journal%29)*.[^n5]

The works of Mansion, deal mainly with [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610),[^n6] [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220),[^n7] and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). He published 349 works in very different journals.[^n8]
