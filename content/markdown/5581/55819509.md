# Juan de Ortega (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juan de Ortega"}

*Image: Ortega, Juan de – Tratado subtilissimo de aritmetica y de geometria, 1515 – BEIC 104004.jpg.* Frontispiece of Ortega's *Tratado*

**Juan de Ortega** (born [Palencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palencia), Spain, c. 1480; died c. 1568),[^o2] was a Spanish mathematician. He wrote some of the earliest works on commercial arithmetic, and discovered an improved method for calculating [square roots](wikipedia://en/29208).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Very little is known of Ortega's life.[^o4] He was a member of the [Dominican Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Order) in [Aragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Aragon),[^o4] and he taught [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) in Spain and Italy.[^o2]

## Mathematical contributions {#Mathematical_contributions}

For his work on arithmetic Ortega drew on that of [Boethius](wikipedia://en/195684) and of 13th-14th century mathematicians.[^o2]

A widely known[^o6] publication among Ortega's works was *Tratado subtilissimo de Aritmetica y de Geometria* ("Most refined treatise on arithmetic and geometry") (Barcelona, 1512). The work was published in Spain, France and Italy, and translated into several languages.[^o4] The *Tratado* was innovative[^o6] in focusing on the practical, in particular commercial, application of arithmetical and geometrical techniques.[^o4] In later editions[^o7] this work also introduces a novel [approximation method](wikipedia://en/21506) for calculating [square roots](wikipedia://en/29208),[^o8] which appears to be largely based on the [Pell equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pell_equation) and thereby the best available technique,[^o9] even though no general solution of this equation is known to have been found until much later.[^o2]

Another textbook by Ortega was *Cursus quattuor mathematicarum artium liberalium* ("Course of four mathematical arts") (Paris, 1516).[^o2]

Another textbook by Ortega was "Conpusicion de la arte de la arismetica y juntamente de geometría"[^o10]
