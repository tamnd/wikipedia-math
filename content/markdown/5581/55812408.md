# Heinrich Schotten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Schotten"}

**Heinrich Georg Leonhard Schotten** (13 July 1856, in [Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg) – 18 February 1939, in Berlin) was a German mathematician and mathematical pedagogue,[^o2] known for his work on reforms in the teaching of geometry.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Schotten was a Gymnasium student in Marburg[^o5] and in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) (St. Nikolai) and studied from 1876 to 1882 in Leipzig, Breslau, Berlin and Marburg with teaching qualification via state examination in Marburg in 1882 and with doctorate in 1883.[^o6] His dissertation has the title *Über einige bemerkenswerte Gattungen der Hypocycloiden*. After completing his doctorate, he spent a probationary year in [Kassel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassel) as a Gymnasium teacher. He was employed as a Gymnasium teacher in [Bad Hersfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Hersfeld), [Schmalkalden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmalkalden) and Kassel (Königliches Friedrichs-Gymnasium). In 1896 he became rector of the Oberrealschule in Halle. On his initiative a new building was completed in 1908. In 1921 he retired. In 1938, because of illness, Schotten as a widower went to live with his children in Berlin.

He was an editor for the *Unterrichtsblätter für Mathematik und Naturwissenschaften* and was a member of the German National Teaching Commission for Mathematics, which at that time was mainly founded on [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887)'s initiative as part of an international exchange on methods of teaching mathematics. Schotten was also part of mathematical evening circle of professors from Leipzig and Halle, including [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216) und [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989). Schotten corresponded with Felix Klein.

In 1894 Schotten was elected a member of the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o7] In 1904 he was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Heidelberg.[^o8]
