# Beneš method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beneš method"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), a discipline within the mathematical [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), **Beneš approach**[^o2] or **Beneš method**[^o3] is a result for an exact or good approximation to the [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) of queue length. It was introduced by [Václav E. Beneš](wikipedia://en/12676927) in 1963.[^o4]

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

The method introduces a quantity referred to as the "virtual waiting time" to define the remaining workload in the queue at any time. This process is a step function which jumps upward with new arrivals to the system and otherwise is linear with negative gradient.[^o6] By giving a relation for the distribution of unfinished work in terms of the excess work, the difference between arrivals and potential service capacity, it turns a time-dependent virtual waiting time problem into "an integral that, in principle, can be solved."[^o7]
