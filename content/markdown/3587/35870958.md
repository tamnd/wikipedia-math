# Order-2 apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-2 apeirogonal tiling"}

Reg tiling stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **order-2 apeirogonal tiling**, **apeirogonal dihedron**, or **infinite dihedron**[^o2] is a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (gap-free filling with repeated shapes) of the [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) consisting of two [apeirogons](wikipedia://en/5658261#Hyperbolic_geometry). It may be considered an improper [regular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_tiling) of the [Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9417) plane, with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{\infty, 2\}.$ Two apeirogons joined along all their [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) can completely fill the entire plane, as an apeirogon is infinite in size and has an [interior angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_angle) of 180°, which is half of a full 360°.

:::

## Related tilings and polyhedra {#Related_tilings_and_polyhedra}

Similarly to the [uniform polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polyhedra) and the [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/12126635), eight uniform tilings may be based from the regular apeirogonal tiling. The [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) and [cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) forms are duplicated, and as two times infinity is also infinity, the [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) and [omnitruncated](wikipedia://en/7083690) forms are also duplicated, therefore reducing the number of unique forms to four: the apeirogonal tiling, the apeirogonal hosohedron, the [apeirogonal prism](wikipedia://en/18119040), and the [apeirogonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/18119626).
