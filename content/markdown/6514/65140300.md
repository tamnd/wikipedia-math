# Differential effects

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Differential effects"}

**Differential effects** play a special role in certain [observational studies](wikipedia://en/6326483) in which treatments are not assigned to subjects at random, where differing outcomes may reflect biased assignments rather than effects caused by the treatments.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

For two treatments, differential effects is the effect of applying one treatment in lieu of the other.[^o4] Differential effects are not immune to differential biases, whose possible consequences are examined by [sensitivity analysis](wikipedia://en/620083).

:::

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Methods"}

## Methods {#Methods}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904), causality is often tested via [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997). Several methods can be used to distinguish actual differential effects from [spurious correlations](wikipedia://en/484778). First, the balancing score (namely propensity score) matching method can be implemented for controlling the covariate balance.[^o7] Second, the [difference-in-differences](wikipedia://en/3404894) (DID) method with a parallel trend assumption (2 groups would show a parallel trend if neither of them experienced the treatment effect) is a useful method to reduce the impact of extraneous factors and selection bias.[^o8] The differential effect of treatments (DET) was explored using several examples and models.

In the [biomedicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedicine) area, differential effects of early [hippocampal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippocampus) pathology[^o9] were investigated on [episodic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episodic_memory) and [semantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_memory) memory. The differential effects of [apolipoproteins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apolipoprotein) E3 and E4 were also examined on neuronal growth in vitro.[^o10]

:::
