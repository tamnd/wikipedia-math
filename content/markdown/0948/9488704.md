# David Naccache

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Naccache"}

**David Naccache** is a cryptographer, currently a professor at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) and a member of its Computer Laboratory. He was previously a professor at [Panthéon-Assas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panthéon-Assas_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1995 from the [École nationale supérieure des télécommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_nationale_supérieure_des_télécommunications). Naccache's most notable work is in [public-key cryptography](wikipedia://en/24222), including the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of [digital signature](wikipedia://en/59644) schemes. Together with [Jacques Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Stern_%28cryptographer%29) he designed the similarly named but very distinct [Naccache-Stern cryptosystem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naccache-Stern_cryptosystem) and [Naccache-Stern knapsack cryptosystem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naccache-Stern_knapsack_cryptosystem).

In 2004 David Naccache and [Claire Whelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Whelan), then employed by [Gemplus International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemplus_International), used [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing) techniques to uncover [redacted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redaction) information from the declassified 6 August 2001 [President's Daily Brief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Daily_Brief) *[Bin Ladin Determined To Strike in US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bin_Ladin_Determined_To_Strike_in_US)*. They also demonstrated how the same process could be applied to other redacted documents.[^o4]

Naccache is also a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor) and [researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Researcher) at the [Information Security Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Security_Group) of [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London).[^o5]

In 2021, two epidemiologists denounced David Naccache to the management of the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29). They accused him of having produced a purported fraudulent report in November 2018 for Genevrier Laboratories in exchange for a large payment (more than €250,000) in defence of a delisted drug, [Chondrosulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condrosulf).[^o6]. The lawsuit has been declined by a judgment of the Cour supérieure du Québec on August 28, 2024 that "warns \[the plaintiffs\] against diffmatory tourism" with "justice costs in favor of the defendants \[who are David Naccache, the ex-minister Noëlle Lenoir and the CEO of Laboratoires Genévrier Dominique Vacher\]".

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2020 Naccache was listed as a Fellow of the IACR, the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043), "for significant contributions to applied cryptography in industry and academia, and for the service to the IACR."<https://www.iacr.org/fellows/2020/naccache.html>
