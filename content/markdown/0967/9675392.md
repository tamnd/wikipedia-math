# Paul van Oorschot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul van Oorschot"}

**Paul C. van Oorschot** is a cryptographer and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researcher, currently a professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) in Ottawa, Ontario, where he held a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in authentication and computer security over the period 2002-2023. He is a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) (FRSC). He is best known as a co-author of the *Handbook of Applied Cryptography* (), together with [Alfred Menezes](wikipedia://en/4356319) and [Scott Vanstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Vanstone). He is also the author of *Computer Security and the Internet: Tools and Jewels from Malware to Bitcoin* (). Van Oorschot was awarded the 2000 [J.W. Graham Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.W._Graham_Medal) in Computing Innovation.[^o6] He also helped organize the first [Selected Areas in Cryptography](wikipedia://en/9545951) (SAC) workshop in 1994.

:::

Van Oorschot received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1988 from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). He was recognized (2016) as a [Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery)[^o2] for "contributions to applied cryptography, authentication and computer security."[^o3] He is also a Fellow of the IEEE (2019). His most recent book is *Computer Security and the Internet: Tools and Jewels from Malware to Bitcoin* (2nd edition, 2021; Springer International).
