# Howard Heys

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Heys"}

**Howard M. Heys** is a cryptographer, a retired professor of [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_engineering) at [Memorial University of Newfoundland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_University_of_Newfoundland). His research includes the design and analysis of [stream](wikipedia://en/49007) and [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) and efficient hardware implementations of them; he participated in the design of [CAST-256](wikipedia://en/612143) and has published [cryptanalyses](wikipedia://en/5715) of such block ciphers as [RC5](wikipedia://en/102600) and [CIKS-1](wikipedia://en/8745236). He has served twice as co-chair of the [Selected Areas in Cryptography](wikipedia://en/9545951) workshop: with [Carlisle Adams](wikipedia://en/9212118) in 1999, and with [Kaisa Nyberg](wikipedia://en/9413796) in 2002.[^o2]

:::

Heys received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1994 from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston). He lives in [St. John's, Newfoundland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's,_Newfoundland) with his wife and two children.
