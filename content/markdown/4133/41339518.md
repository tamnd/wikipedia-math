# Francesco Mainardi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Mainardi"}

**Francesco Mainardi** (born December 29, 1942) is an Italian [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Mainardi was born in [Lugo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugo), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), on December 29, 1942. He graduated from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) in 1966 with a degree in [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028).

## Career {#Career}

Mainardi became a professor of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) at Bologna. From 1971 to 1973, he was a lecturer at the [Marche Polytechnic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marche_Polytechnic_University) on [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586). He teaches [non-Gaussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Gaussianity) [stochastics](wikipedia://en/292222) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) among other science-related subjects. He has offered courses on [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939).[^o6] From January 24–28, 2000, he worked for the Center for Mathematical Physics and Stochastic at the [University of Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aarhus), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark).[^o7]

## Publications {#Publications}

In 1982, he was the editor of *Wave Propagation in Viscoelastic Media,* which was published by Pitman Publishing in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London). In September 1996, he and A. Carpinteri authored *Scaling Laws and Fractality in Continuum Mechanics*. A year later. Mainard and Carpinteri co-edited *Fractals and Fractional Calculus in Continuum Mechanics*. He went on hiatus until 2010 when the [Imperial College Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_Press) issued *Fractional Calculus and Waves in Linear Viscoelasticity* in London . Then in 2022 [World Scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Scientific) (Singapore) has published the second (revised and enlarged) edition of this book. In 2011, he co-authored (with S. Rogosin), *The Legacy of A.Ya. Khintchine's Work in Probability Theory*.[^o6].For Springer Monographs in Mathematics [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Publishing) he has published the book "Mittag-Leffler Functions, Related Topics and Applications" with R. Gorenflo, A.A. Kilbas and S. Rogosin in two editions, 2014 and 2020.

## Works abroad {#Works_abroad}

He gave lectures abroad at institutions such as the [Indian Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science) of [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore), International Centre of Mathematical Sciences of [Palai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palai) in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin). He worked at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) and at the Institute of Oceanography of [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego). He was a lecturer at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney) and then worked at the [University of KwaZulu-Natal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_KwaZulu-Natal) of [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa). In [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), worked at the [University of Campinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Campinas) in applied mathematics.[^o6]
