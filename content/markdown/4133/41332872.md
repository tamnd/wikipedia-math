# Yuri Luchko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Luchko"}

**Yuri Luchko** is a German professor of mathematics at the [Berlin University of Applied Sciences and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Hochschule_für_Technik). His 90 works were [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) and appeared in such journals as the *[Fractional Calculus and Applied Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_Calculus_and_Applied_Analysis)* and *[Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications](wikipedia://en/54943204)*, among others.[^o2]

:::
