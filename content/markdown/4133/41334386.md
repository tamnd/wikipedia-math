# Rudolf Gorenflo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Gorenflo"}

**Rudolf Gorenflo** (31 July 1930 – 20 October 2017)[^o2] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gorenflo was born on July 31, 1930, in [Friedrichstal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrichstal), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). From 1950 to 1956 he attended [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) from which he received his diploma in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). From 1957 to 1961 he became a scientific assistant there and for a year later worked at Standard Electric Lorenz Company. From 1962 to 1970 he worked at the [Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Plasma_Physics), at [Garching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garching) not too far away from [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich). He was a resident in mathematics at the [Technical University in Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_in_Aachen) in 1970 and a year later became a professor there.[^o4]

In 1972 he was invited as a guest professor to the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) and only by October 1973 became a full-time professor at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin). In 1995 he became a professor at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) and by October 1998 returned to Free University as [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus). During his life he collaborated with scientists from [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), former [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and [Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam).[^o4]

## Academic work {#Academic_work}

As of 2013, he had published over 250 [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) articles, one of which has over 1800 citations. His works have appeared in such journals as the [Journal of Vibration and Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Vibration_and_Control) and various [Journal of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Physics_%28disambiguation%29) journals.[^o6]
