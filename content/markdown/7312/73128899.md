# Thomas Kappeler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Kappeler"}

**Thomas Kappeler** (12 February 1953 – 30 May 2022) was a Swiss mathematician and professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o2][^o3] Kappeler's main research was in [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) in infinite dimensions.

:::

Kappeler co-founded *the Zurich Graduate School in Mathematics*, a joint doctoral program of the Mathematics departments of [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and University of Zurich.[^o4] He also actively supported young kids with talent in mathematics. He was the co-leader of the children's math club *Junior Euler Society*.[^o5]

## Life {#Life}

Kappeler studied mathematics at ETH Zurich, where he did his Ph.D. in 1981 under the supervision of [Corneliu Constantinescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corneliu_Constantinescu). He was a visiting professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) and [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). He was a professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) from 1990 till 1996.[^o8] After that he became a mathematics professor at University of Zurich.[^o9]

Kappeler published more than 150 research articles.[^o11] He also published two books on partial differential equations.
