# Variational vector field

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Variational vector field"}

In the [mathematical fields](wikipedia://en/18831) of the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **variational vector field** is a certain type of [vector field](wikipedia://en/62641) defined on the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) of a [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) which gives rise to variations along a vector field in the manifold itself.

:::

Specifically, let *X* be a vector field on *M*. Then *X* generates a [one-parameter group](wikipedia://en/754851) of [local diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/1728483) *Fl*<sub>X</sub><sup>t</sup>, the [flow](wikipedia://en/2620168) along *X*. The [differential](wikipedia://en/1558510) of *Fl*<sub>X</sub><sup>t</sup> gives, for each *t*, a mapping

$$d\mathrm{Fl}_X^t : TM \to TM$$

where *TM* denotes the tangent bundle of *M*. This is a one-parameter group of local diffeomorphisms of the tangent bundle. The variational vector field of *X*, denoted by *T*(*X*) is the tangent to the flow of *d Fl*<sub>X</sub><sup>t</sup>.
