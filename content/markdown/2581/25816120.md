# Michele Cipolla

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michele Cipolla"}

*Image: Michele Cipolla.jpg.*

**Michele Cipolla** (28 October 1880, [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo) – 7 September 1947, Palermo) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), mainly specializing in [number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_Theory).

:::

He was a professor of Algebraic Analysis at the [University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania) and, later, the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo). He developed (among other things) a theory for sequences of sets and [Cipolla's algorithm](wikipedia://en/25766973) for finding square roots modulo a prime number. He also solved the problem of binomial congruence.
