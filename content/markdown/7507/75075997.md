# 267 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="267"}

**267** (**two hundred \[and\] sixty-seven**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [266](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/266_%28number%29) and preceding [268](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/268_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

- 267 is an odd [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) with two prime factors.[^o4]
- 267 is the number of [planar partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planar_partitions) of the number 12. Planar partitions are the number of ways in which the given number can be organized as split in an array.[^o5]
- 267 is the sum of perfect cubes in two different ways. It is the sum of 1<sup>3</sup>+2<sup>3</sup>+2<sup>3</sup>+5<sup>3</sup>+5<sup>3</sup> and 2<sup>3</sup>+2<sup>3</sup>+2<sup>3</sup>+3<sup>3</sup>+6<sup>3</sup>
- 267 is the largest number that cannot be expressed as the sum of distinct [hexagonal numbers](wikipedia://en/494013).
