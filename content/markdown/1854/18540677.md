# Guy Nason

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guy Nason"}

**Guy Philip Nason** (born 28 August 1966)[^o3] is a British statistician, and professor of Statistics at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London).

:::

Nason received his BSc from the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) in 1988, a diploma in Mathematical Statistics from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1989, and a PhD in Statistics from the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) in 1992. He served as a Council member of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) (2004–08), and was Vice-President (Academic Affairs) 2016–2020. He is a member of the [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPSRC) Strategic Advisory Team for Mathematics. He was an [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPSRC) Advanced Research Fellow during 2000–5 and was awarded the [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in bronze by the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) (RSS) in 2001.[^o4][^o5] He took over the post as head of mathematics at Bristol from [Stephen Wiggins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Wiggins) in 2008.

Nason is best known for his work in the area of [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis), especially [wavelet](wikipedia://en/50903) approaches.[^o6][^o7][^o8] He has served as the Secretary of the RSS Research Section (2002–04), associate editor for the Journal of the RSS, Series B, Computational Statistics and Statistica Sinica and is currently an associate editor for *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178)*.[^o9]
