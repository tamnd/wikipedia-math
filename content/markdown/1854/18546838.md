# Washek Pfeffer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Washek Pfeffer"}

**Washek F. Pfeffer** (November 14, 1936 – January 3, 2021) was a Czech-born American mathematician and Emeritus Professor at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis). Pfeffer was one of the world's pre-eminent authorities on real [integration](wikipedia://en/15532) and has authored several books on the topic of integration, and numerous papers on these topics and others related to many areas of [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory). Pfeffer gave his name to the [Pfeffer integral](wikipedia://en/18547070), which extends a [Riemann-type construction](wikipedia://en/26390) for the integral of a measurable function both to higher-dimensional domains and, in the case of one dimension, to a superset of the [Lebesgue integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_integrable) functions.
