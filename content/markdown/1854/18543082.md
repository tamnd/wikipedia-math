# Hermite number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermite number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Hermite numbers** are values of [Hermite polynomials](wikipedia://en/195984) at zero argument. Typically they are defined for physicists' Hermite polynomials.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

The numbers *H*<sub>n</sub> = *H*<sub>n</sub>(0), where *H*<sub>n</sub>(*x*) is a [Hermite polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_polynomial) of order *n*, may be called Hermite numbers.[^o4]

The first Hermite numbers are:

$$H_0 = 1\,$$

$$H_1 = 0\,$$

$$H_2 = -2\,$$

$$H_3 = 0\,$$

$$H_4 = +12\,$$

$$H_5 = 0\,$$

$$H_6 = -120\,$$

$$H_7 = 0\,$$

$$H_8 = +1680\,$$

$$H_9 =0\,$$

$$H_{10} = -30240\,$$

:::

## Recursion relations {#Recursion_relations}

Are obtained from [recursion relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_relation) of Hermitian polynomials for *x* = 0:

$$H_{n} = -2(n-1)H_{n-2}.\,\!$$

Since *H*<sub>0</sub> = 1 and *H*<sub>1</sub> = 0 one can construct a closed formula for *H*<sub>n</sub>:

$$H_n = \begin{cases} 0, & \mbox{if }n\mbox{ is odd} \\ (-1)^{n/2} 2^{n/2} (n-1)!! , & \mbox{if }n\mbox{ is even} \end{cases}$$

where (*n* − 1)!! = 1 × 3 × ... × (*n* − 1).

## Usage {#Usage}

From the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993) of Hermitian polynomials it follows that

$$\exp (-t^2 + 2tx) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty H_n (x) \frac {t^n}{n!}\,\!$$

Reference [^o4] gives a [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012):

$$H_n (x) = (H+2x)^n\,\!$$

where formally the *n*-th power of *H*, *H*<sup>*n*</sup>, is the *n*-th Hermite number, *H*<sub>*n*</sub>. (See [Umbral calculus](wikipedia://en/214124).)
