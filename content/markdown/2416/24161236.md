# Great stellapentakis dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great stellapentakis dodecahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great stellapentakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great stellapentakis dodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great stellapentakis dodecahedron** (or **great astropentakis dodecahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the dual of the [truncated great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157466). It has 60 intersecting triangular faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(-\frac{7}{36}-\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{5})\approx 138.891\,114\,686\,59^{\circ}$ and two of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{12}\sqrt{5})\approx 20.554\,442\,656\,71^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-80+9\sqrt{5}}{109})\approx 123.320\,065\,258\,47^{\circ}$. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.
