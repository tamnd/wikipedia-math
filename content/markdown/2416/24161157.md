# Small stellapentakis dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small stellapentakis dodecahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small stellapentakis dodecahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [truncated great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157430). It has 60 intersecting triangular faces.

:::

*Image: Small stellapentakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small stellapentakis dodecahedron

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have two acute angles of $\arccos(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{5}\sqrt{5})\approx 18.699\,407\,085\,149^{\circ}$ and one obtuse angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{10}-\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{5})\approx 142.601\,185\,829\,70^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-24-5\sqrt{5}}{41})\approx 149.099\,125\,827\,35^{\circ}$. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.
