# SMath Studio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SMath Studio"}

**SMath Studio** is a [freeware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeware) ([free of charge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_of_charge) for personal use,[^o7] but not [libre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_software)), closed-source, mathematical notebook program similar to [Mathcad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathcad). It is available for Windows, Linux, iOS, Android, [Universal Windows Platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Windows_Platform), and on some handhelds.

:::

Among its capabilities are:

- Solving [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309);
- Graphing functions in two or three dimensions;
- [Symbolic calculations](wikipedia://en/23659805), including solving [systems of equations](wikipedia://en/249617);
- [Matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) operations, including [determinants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Determinants);
- Finding roots of [polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomials) and functions;
- Symbolic and [numeric differentiation](wikipedia://en/1254566) of functions;
- [Numeric integration](wikipedia://en/170089);
- Simple multiline looped programs;
- [User-defined functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User-defined_function);
- [Units of measurement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_of_measurement).

SMath has been applied in the fields of physics[^o8][^o9][^o10] and thermal engineering[^o11].
