# Noncommutative measure and integration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncommutative measure and integration"}

**Noncommutative measure and integration** refers to the theory of [weights, states, and traces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebras#Weights.2C_states.2C_and_traces) on [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185) (Takesaki 1979 v. 2 p. 141).

:::
