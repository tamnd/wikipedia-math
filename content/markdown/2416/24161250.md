# Great deltoidal hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great deltoidal hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great deltoidal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great deltoidal hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great deltoidal hexecontahedron** (or **great sagittal ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157639). It is visually identical to the [great rhombidodecacron](wikipedia://en/24368340). It has 60 intersecting [cross quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_quadrilateral) faces, 120 edges, and 62 vertices. Its faces are darts. Part of each dart lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

It is also called a *great strombic hexecontahedron.*

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The darts have two angles of $\arccos(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{5}\sqrt{5})\approx 18.699\,407\,085\,15^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{10}\sqrt{5})\approx 91.512\,394\,720\,74^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(-\frac{1}{8}-\frac{9}{40}\sqrt{5})\approx 231.088\,791\,108\,96^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-19+8\sqrt{5}}{41})\approx 91.553\,403\,672\,16^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{21+3\sqrt{5}}{22}\approx 1.259\,463\,815\,11$.
