# Werner Müller (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Werner Müller"}

*Image: Werner müller.jpg.* Müller at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

**Werner Müller** (born 7 September 1949) is a German mathematician. His research focuses on [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Werner Müller grew up in the [German Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany) (East Germany). He studied mathematics at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in East Berlin. In 1977 he completed his PhD under the supervision of Herbert Kurke. In his thesis, *Analytische Torsion Riemannscher Mannigfaltigkeiten,* he solved, at the same time as but independently of [Jeff Cheeger](wikipedia://en/7343761), the Ray–Singer conjecture on the equality between [analytic torsion](wikipedia://en/14388042) and [Reidemeister torsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reidemeister_torsion). Thereafter he moved to the Karl-Weierstraß-Institut für Mathematik of the [Academy of Sciences of the GDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin). After the reunion of Germany he spent some time at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn. Since 1994 he is professor at the Mathematics Institute of the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o3] He is the successor on the chair of [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495). He has supervised 12 doctoral students, including [Maryna Viazovska](wikipedia://en/50004967).

Together with [Jeff Cheeger](wikipedia://en/7343761), he has been awarded the Max-Planck-Forschungspreis in 1991 .[^o4] The [Cheeger–Müller theorem](wikipedia://en/14388042#Cheeger–Müller_theorem) on the [analytic torsion](wikipedia://en/14388042) of [Riemannian manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_manifolds) is named after them.[^o5][^o6]
