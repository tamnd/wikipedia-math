# David Ríos Insua

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Ríos Insua"}

**David Ríos Insua** (born June 21, 1964 in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid)) is a [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and son and disciple of [Sixto Ríos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixto_Ríos), the "father of Spanish statistics." He is currently also the youngest Fellow of the [Spanish Royal Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Royal_Academy_of_Sciences) (de la Real Academia de Ciencias Exactas, Físicas y Naturales, RAC),[^o2] which he joined in 2008.[^o3][^o4] He received a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Computational Sciences at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds). He is Full Professor of the Statistics and Operations Research Department at [Rey Juan Carlos University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rey_Juan_Carlos_University) (URJC),[^o5] and he has been Vice-dean of New Technologies and International Relationships at URJC (2002–2009). He has worked in fields such as [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571) in neuronal networks, MCMC methods in decision analysis, Bayesian robustness or adversarial risk analysis. He has also worked in applied areas such as Electronic Democracy,[^o6] reservoirs management, counterterrorism model and many others. He is married and has two daughters.

:::

## Career {#Career}

- He obtained his master's degree in Mathematics at [Universidad Complutense de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Complutense_de_Madrid) (UCM, 1987), with Extraordinary and National Awards.
- He did his PhD studies at Manchester and Leeds where he obtained his PhD degree in 1990.
- He has been a lecturer and researcher at [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Politécnica_de_Madrid) (UPM), [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), [Université Paris-Dauphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Dauphine), [International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Institute_for_Applied_Systems_Analysis) (IIASA), Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche-Istituto di Matematica Applicata e Tecnologie Informatiche (CNR-IMATI),[^o8] Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute (SAMSI), where he was director of the Risk Analysis, Extreme Events and Decision Theory program.[^o9]
- He was director of the Towards Electronic Democracy (TED) program de la European Science Foundation (ESF).[^o10]
- He is current codirector of the Algorithmic Decision Theory (ALGODEC) program[^o11] cofunded by the European Cooperation in Science and Technology (COST) and the European Science Foundation (ESF).
- He has supervised 15 PhD theses.
- He has supervised over 40 cofunded projects.

## Awards {#Awards}

- Extraordinary and National master's degree Award.
- UPM Award for young researchers.
- Ramiro Melendreras Award of Sociedad de Estadística e Investigación Operativa (SEIO) for young researchers.[^o13]
- Peccei Award from International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis (IIASA) for young researchers.
- WirsboURJC Research Award.
- [Everis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everis) Award from Capital Semilla.
- SRA Award for best Decision Analysis paper.

## Publications {#Publications}

- He is the author of 15 books or monographs, including *Sensitivity Analysis in Multiobjective Decision Making* (Springer), *Statistical Decision Theory* (Kendall's),[^o15] *Robust Bayesian Analysis* (Springer),[^o16] *E-Democracy: A GDN perspective* (Springer), and *Bayesian Analysis of Stochastic Processes* (Wiley).
- He is or has been associated editor of journals such as the *Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics*,[^o17] *Group Decision and Negotiation*,[^o18] *Journal of Multicriteria Decision Analysis*,[^o19] *Serie A: Matemáticas de la Revista de la Real Academia de Ciencias Exactas, Físicas y Naturales* ([RACSAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RACSAM)),[^o20] *International Journal of Public Information Systems*.[^o21] *International Transactions in Operational Research*.[^o22] and *Decision Analysis*.[^o23]
