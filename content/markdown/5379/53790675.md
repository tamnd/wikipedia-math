# Giuseppe Bruno (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giuseppe Bruno"}

**Giuseppe Bruno** (1828–1893) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) in the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Bruno has born in a very poor family, but he won a stipend to study in the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), where he graduated in philosophy in 1846. The following years he was professor at secondary level, while he studied to graduate in engineering (1850) and to doctorate in mathematics (1851).[^o4]

In 1852 he was appointed substitute professor of mathematics in the university of Turin; he combined this work with his private and public teaching in lower levels.[^o5] In 1862 he was appointed professor of geometry in the university; in 1881 he became president of the Sciences Faculty.[^o6]

Bruno wrote twenty one-papers, all but two in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), but he is mostly known by his teaching.[^o7] Among his students are [Giuseppe Peano](wikipedia://en/13043) and [Corrado Segre](wikipedia://en/2422061).
