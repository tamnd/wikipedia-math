# Center (ring theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Center"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **center of a ring** $R$ is the [subring](wikipedia://en/24873145) consisting of the elements $x$ such that $xy = yx$ for all elements $y$ in $R$. It is a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) and is denoted as $Z(R)$; 'Z' stands for the German word Zentrum, meaning "center".

:::

If $R$ is a ring, then $R$ is an [associative algebra](wikipedia://en/2112) over its center. Conversely, if $R$ is an associative algebra over a commutative subring $S$, then $S$ is a subring of the center of $R$, and if $S$ happens to be the center of $R$, then the algebra $R$ is called a **central algebra**.

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The center of a commutative ring $R$ is $R$ itself.
- The center of a [skew-field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skew-field) is a [field](wikipedia://en/10603).
- The center of the (full) [matrix ring](wikipedia://en/487541) with entries in a commutative ring $R$ consists of $R$-scalar multiples of the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718).[^o4]
- Let $F$ be a [field extension](wikipedia://en/11634) of a field $k$, and $R$ an algebra over $k$. Then $\operatorname{Z}(R \otimes_k F) = \operatorname{Z}(R) \otimes_k F$.
- The center of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) plays an important role in the [representation theory of Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_theory_of_Lie_algebras). For example, a [Casimir element](wikipedia://en/513672) is an element of such a center that is used to analyze [Lie algebra representations](wikipedia://en/318712). See also: *[Harish-Chandra isomorphism](wikipedia://en/5352569)*.
- The center of a [simple algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_algebra) is a field.

:::
