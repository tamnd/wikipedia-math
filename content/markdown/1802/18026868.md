# Detlef Gromoll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Detlef Gromoll"}

**Detlef Gromoll** (13 May 1938 – 31 May 2008) was a mathematician who worked in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gromoll was born in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) in 1938,[^o4] and was a classically trained violinist. After living and attending school in [Rosdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosdorf) and graduating from high school in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), he obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1964. Following sojourns at several universities, he joined the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook) in 1969.

He married Suzan L. Lemay on 29 December 1971, and they had three children together: Hans Christian (also a mathematician), Heidi, and Stefan, a physicist and cofounder of Scientific Media.[^o4] He died in 2008 at age 70.
