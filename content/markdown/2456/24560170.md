# Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity** of a [coherent sheaf](wikipedia://en/603780) *F* over [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) $\mathbf{P}^n$ is the smallest integer *r* such that it is **r-regular**, meaning that

:::

$$H^i(\mathbf{P}^n, F(r-i))=0$$

whenever $i>0$. The regularity of a [subscheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subscheme) is defined to be the regularity of its sheaf of ideals. The regularity controls when the [Hilbert function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_function) of the sheaf becomes a polynomial; more precisely dim $H^0(\mathbf{P}^n, F(m))$ is a polynomial in *m* when *m* is at least the regularity. The concept of *r*-regularity was introduced by (txt, lecture 14), who attributed the following results to (txt):

- An *r*-regular sheaf is *s*-regular for any $s\ge r$.
- If a coherent sheaf is *r*-regular then $F(r)$ is [generated by its global sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generated_by_its_global_sections).

## Graded modules {#Graded_modules}

A related idea exists in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). Suppose $R= k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ is a [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *k* and *M* is a [finitely generated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_module) [graded *R*-module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graded_algebra#Graded_module). Suppose *M* has a [minimal graded free resolution](wikipedia://en/19745659#Graded_modules_and_algebras)

$$\cdots\rightarrow F_j \rightarrow\cdots\rightarrow F_0\rightarrow M\rightarrow 0$$

and let $b_j$ be the maximum of the degrees of the generators of $F_j$. If *r* is an integer such that $b_j - j \le r$ for all *j*, then *M* is said to be *r*-regular. The regularity of *M* is the smallest such *r*.

These two notions of regularity coincide when *F* is a coherent sheaf such that $\operatorname{Ass}(F)$ contains no closed points. Then the graded module

$$M=\bigoplus_{d \in \mathbb{Z}} H^0(\mathbf{P}^n,F(d))$$

is finitely generated and has the same regularity as *F*.
