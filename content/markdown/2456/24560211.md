# Heinz Hopf Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz Hopf Prize"}

The **Heinz Hopf Prize** is awarded every two years at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). The prize honours *outstanding scientific work in the field of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210)*. It is named after the German mathematician [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082) (1894–1971), Professor of Mathematics at ETH from 1931 to 1965. The prize is awarded on the occasion of the Heinz Hopf Lectures that are given at ETH by the laureate.[^o2]

:::

The prize was awarded for the first time in October 2009.
