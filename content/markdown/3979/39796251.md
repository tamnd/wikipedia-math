# Augustin Sesmat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Augustin Sesmat"}

**Augustin Sesmat** (1885-4-7 [Dieulouard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieulouard) -- 1957-12-12)[^o2] was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He was professor of history and [criticism of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_science) at the [Institut Catholique de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Catholique_de_Paris) in the 1930s.[^o3] He was probably the first person to discover the [logical hexagon](wikipedia://en/29594530), thus solving a problem posed by Aristotle.

:::
