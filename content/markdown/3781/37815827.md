# Astrostatistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Astrostatistics"}

**Astrostatistics** is a discipline which spans [astrophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics), [statistical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_analysis) and [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253).[^o2] It is used to process the vast amount of data produced by [automated scanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_scanning) of the cosmos, to characterize complex datasets, and to link [astronomical data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_data) to [astrophysical theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_astrophysics). Many branches of statistics are involved in astronomical analysis including [nonparametrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametrics), [multivariate regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_regression) and [multivariate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_classification), [time series analysis](wikipedia://en/406624), and especially [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571). The field is closely related to [astroinformatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astroinformatics).

:::
