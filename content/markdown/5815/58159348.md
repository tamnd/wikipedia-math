# Wilhelm Winter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Winter"}

*Image: Wilhelm Winter, 2012.jpg.* Wilhelm Winter, 2012.

**Wilhelm Winter** (born 1968) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) (and particularly [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184)).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Winter received in 1996 his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) from the [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) and in 2000 his doctorate ([Promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29)) from the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) with thesis advisor [Joachim Cuntz](wikipedia://en/38543091) and thesis *Covering Dimension for Nuclear C\*-Algebras*. At the University of Münster he was a research assistant from 2001 to 2007 and habilitated there in 2006 in Münster. In Fall 2002 he was a visiting assistant professor at [Texas A & M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A_&_M_University). From 2007 to 2011 he was at [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham), first as a lecturer and later as a reader. Winter is a professor of mathematics at the University of Münster since 2011.[^o4]

In 2010 he received with [Andrew Toms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Toms) the G. de B. Robinson Award.[^o5] In 2018 Winter was an invited speaker with talk *Structure of nuclear C\*-algebras: From quasidiagonality to classification, and back again* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o6]
