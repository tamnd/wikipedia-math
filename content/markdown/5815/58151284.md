# Antonio Auffinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Auffinger"}

**Antonio Auffinger** is an American Brazilian mathematician. He works in the area of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Auffinger completed his doctorate at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) in 2011; his dissertation was supervised by [Gerard Ben Arous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Ben_Arous) and was awarded the Francisco Aranda-Ordaz Prize by the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability).

He was [Leonard Eugene Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247) instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) before moving in 2014 to [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), where he is a professor of mathematics.

## Book {#Book}

He co-authored a book on [first passage percolation](wikipedia://en/44370787) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Auffinger won a [National Science Foundation CAREER award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) in 2016. In 2017, he was awarded a gold medal prize by the International Consortium of Chinese Mathematicians for his proof of the uniqueness of the Parisi measure in [spin glasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_glasses).

He was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to probability theory and mathematical physics and, in particular, to the study of spin glasses and [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805)".
