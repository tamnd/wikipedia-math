# Matilde Lalín

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matilde Lalín"}

**Matilde Noemí Lalín** is an Argentine-Canadian mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and known for her work on [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283), [Mahler measure](wikipedia://en/1088425), and their connections. She is a professor of mathematics at the [Université de Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Montréal).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lalín is originally from [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires), and is a [dual citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_citizen) of Argentina and Canada. As a high school student, she represented Argentina twice in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), in 1993 and 1995, earning a silver medal in 1995.

She earned a [licenciatura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciatura) in 1999 from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires). After starting graduate study at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and spending a term as a visiting student at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), she completed her doctorate in 2005 at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). Her dissertation, *Some Relations of Mahler Measure with Hyperbolic Volumes and Special Values of L-Functions*, was supervised there by Fernando Rodriguez-Villegas.

She became a postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques), [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960), and [Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/34978739), before obtaining a tenure-track faculty position in 2007 as an assistant professor of mathematics at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta). She moved to the Université de Montréal in 2010, earned tenure as an associate professor there in 2012, and was promoted to full professor in 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lalín is the 2022 winner of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055), "for her outstanding contributions to research in Number Theory and related areas". She was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to number theory, including the study of L-functions, and for service to the mathematical community". In 2024, she will become a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), "For her ongoing contributions to the AWM, most notably her leadership role in the Women in Numbers Network and considerable contributions to its growth; her service to the International Mathematics Union Committee for Women; and for her ardent efforts towards making conferences more welcoming and accessible for researchers by actively advocating for childcare resources."[^o4]
