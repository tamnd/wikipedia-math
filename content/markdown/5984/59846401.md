# Yifeng Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yifeng Liu"}

Liu Yifeng

**Yifeng Liu** (; born July 19, 1985) is a Chinese professor of mathematics at [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Liu graduated from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in 2007 and earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2012 under [Shou-Wu Zhang](wikipedia://en/3582724). He was a C.L.E. Moore Instructor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) from 2012 to 2015 and an assistant professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) from 2015 to 2018 before being appointed an associate professor at Yale University.[^o6][^o8] Liu returned to China in 2021 to join [Zheijiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheijiang_University) became a full professor of mathematics.[^o3]

Liu has made important contributions to arithmetic geometry and number theory. His contributions span a wide spectrum of topics such as arithmetic [theta lifts](wikipedia://en/2995611) and derivatives of [L-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-functions), the [Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture](wikipedia://en/58884863) and its arithmetic counterpart, the [Beilinson–Bloch–Kato conjecture](wikipedia://en/25305882), the [geometric Langlands program](wikipedia://en/10500178), the p-adic [Waldspurger theorem](wikipedia://en/55941264), and the study of [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) on [Artin stacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artin_stack).[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

He received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2017.[^o6]

He was awarded the 2018 [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) for his contributions to the field of mathematics. He shared the prize with [Jack Thorne](wikipedia://en/59209985).[^o7][^o8]
