# J-line

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="J-line"}

In the study of the arithmetic of [elliptic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_curves), the ***j*-line** over a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* is the coarse [moduli scheme](wikipedia://en/3043915) attached to the moduli problem sending a ring $R$ to the set of isomorphism classes of elliptic curves over $R$. Since elliptic curves over the complex numbers are isomorphic (over an algebraic closure) if and only if their $j$-invariants agree, the affine space $\mathbb{A}^1_j$ parameterizing [j-invariants](wikipedia://en/451988) of elliptic curves yields a [coarse moduli space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coarse_moduli_space). However, this fails to be a [fine moduli space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine_moduli_space) due to the presence of elliptic curves with automorphisms, necessitating the construction of the [Moduli stack of elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/49905706).

:::

This is related to the [congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694) $\Gamma(1)$ in the following way:[^o2]

$$M([\Gamma(1)]) = \mathrm{Spec}(R[j])$$

Here the *j*-invariant is normalized such that $j=0$ has [complex multiplication](wikipedia://en/537183) by $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_3]$, and $j=1728$ has complex multiplication by $\mathbb{Z}[i]$.

The *j*-line can be seen as giving a coordinatization of the [classical modular curve](wikipedia://en/5249663) of level 1, $X_0(1)$, which is isomorphic to the [complex](wikipedia://en/762977) [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578) $\mathbb{P}^1_{/\mathbb{C}}$.[^o3]
