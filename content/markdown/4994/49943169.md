# Ralph McKenzie

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ralph McKenzie"}

**Ralph Nelson Whitfield McKenzie** (born October 20, 1941) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065), and [universal algebraist](wikipedia://en/45200).[^o4] He received his doctorate from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) in 1967.[^o5]

:::

He is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- McKenzie with David Hobby: The structure of finite algebras, AMS 1988
- McKenzie with Ralph Freese: Commutator Theory for Congruence Modular Varieties, London Math. Society Lecture Notes, Cambridge University Press 1987
