# Worldly cardinal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Worldly cardinal"}

In mathematical [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), a **worldly cardinal** is a [cardinal](wikipedia://en/6173) κ such that the [rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_%28set_theory%29) *V*<sub>κ</sub> is a [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28model_theory%29) of [Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory](wikipedia://en/152214).[^o2] A [strong limit cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_limit_cardinal) κ is worldly if and only if for every natural *n*, there are unboundedly many ordinals θ \< κ such that *V*<sub>θ</sub> ≺<sub>Σ<sub>n</sub></sub> *V*<sub>κ</sub>.

:::

## Relationship to inaccessible cardinals {#Relationship_to_inaccessible_cardinals}

By [Zermelo's categoricity theorem](wikipedia://en/76697957), every [inaccessible cardinal](wikipedia://en/248076) is worldly. By Shepherdson's theorem, inaccessibility is equivalent to the stronger statement that (*V*<sub>κ</sub>, *V*<sub>κ+1</sub>) is a model of second order Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory.[^o4] Being worldly and being inaccessible are not equivalent; in fact, the smallest worldly cardinal has countable cofinality and therefore is a [singular cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cardinal).[^o5]

The following are in strictly increasing order, where $I$ is the least inaccessible cardinal:

- The least worldly κ.
- The least worldly κ and λ (κ\<λ, and same below) with *V*<sub>κ</sub> and *V*<sub>λ</sub> satisfying the same theory.
- The least worldly κ that is a limit of worldly cardinals (equivalently, a limit of κ worldly cardinals).
- The least worldly κ and λ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> ≺<sub>Σ<sub>2</sub></sub> *V*<sub>λ</sub> (this is higher than even a κ-fold iteration of the above item).
- The least worldly κ and λ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> ≺ *V*<sub>λ</sub>.
- The least worldly κ of cofinality ω<sub>1</sub> (corresponds to the extension of the above item to a chain of length ω<sub>1</sub>).
- The least worldly κ of cofinality ω<sub>2</sub> (and so on).
- The least κ>ω with *V*<sub>κ</sub> satisfying replacement for the language augmented with the (*V*<sub>κ</sub>,∈) satisfaction relation.
- The least κ inaccessible in *L*<sub>κ</sub>(*V*<sub>κ</sub>); equivalently, the least κ>ω with *V*<sub>κ</sub> satisfying replacement for formulas in *V*<sub>κ</sub> in the [infinitary logic](wikipedia://en/630478) *L*<sub>∞,ω</sub>.
- The least κ with a transitive model M⊂*V*<sub>κ+1</sub> extending *V*<sub>κ</sub> satisfying [Morse–Kelley set theory](wikipedia://en/2693655).
- (not a worldly cardinal) The least κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> having the same Σ<sub>2</sub> theory as *V*<sub>$I$</sub>.
- The least κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> and *V*<sub>$I$</sub> having the same theory.
- The least κ with *L*<sub>κ</sub>(*V*<sub>κ</sub>) and *L*<sub>$I$</sub>(*V*<sub>$I$</sub>) having the same theory.
- (not a worldly cardinal) The least κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> and *V*<sub>$I$</sub> having the same Σ<sub>2</sub> theory with real parameters.
- (not a worldly cardinal) The least κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> ≺<sub>Σ<sub>2</sub></sub> *V*<sub>$I$</sub>.
- The least κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> ≺ *V*<sub>$I$</sub>.
- The least infinite κ with *V*<sub>κ</sub> and *V*<sub>$I$</sub> satisfying the same *L*<sub>∞,ω</sub> statements that are in *V*<sub>κ</sub>.
- The least κ with a transitive model M⊂*V*<sub>κ+1</sub> extending *V*<sub>κ</sub> and satisfying the same sentences with parameters in *V*<sub>κ</sub> as *V*<sub>$I+1$</sub> does.
- The least inaccessible cardinal $I$.
