# B-coloring

*Image: Graph b coloring example.svg.* Example of B-coloring of [Shrikhande graph](wikipedia://en/13672415) with 6 colors: highlighted nodes have neighbors in each other colors. Since each node is adjacent to another 6, a 7-color B-coloring may be possible.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="B-coloring"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **b-coloring** of a [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) is a [coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) of the vertices where each color class contains a vertex that has a neighbor in all other color classes.

:::

The **b-chromatic number** of a *G* graph is the largest b(G) positive integer that the *G* graph has a b-coloring with b(G) number of colors.

Victor Campos, Carlos Lima and Ana Silva[^o2] used the relation between b-coloring and a graph's smallest [cycle](wikipedia://en/168609) to partly prove the [Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture](wikipedia://en/691803).
