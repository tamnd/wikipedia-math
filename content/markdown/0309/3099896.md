# Gonality of an algebraic curve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gonality of an algebraic curve"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **gonality** of an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) *C* is defined as the lowest degree of a nonconstant [rational map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_map) from *C* to the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578). In more algebraic terms, if *C* is defined over the [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *K* and *K*(*C*) denotes the [function field](wikipedia://en/2783843) of *C*, then the gonality is the minimum value taken by the degrees of [field extensions](wikipedia://en/11634)

:::

*K*(*C*)/*K*(*f*)

of the function field over its [subfields](wikipedia://en/11634) generated by single functions *f*.

If *K* is algebraically closed, then the gonality is 1 precisely for curves of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) 0. The gonality is 2 for curves of genus 1 ([elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225)) and for [hyperelliptic curves](wikipedia://en/499361) (this includes all curves of genus 2). For genus *g* ≥ 3 it is no longer the case that the genus determines the gonality. The gonality of a curve of genus *g* is less than or equal to the [floor function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floor_function) of

(*g* + 3)/2.

**Trigonal curves** are those with gonality 3, and this case gave rise to the name in general. Trigonal curves include the [Picard curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picard_curve), of genus three and given by an equation

*y*<sup>3</sup> = *Q*(*x*)

where *Q* is of degree 4.

The **gonality conjecture**, of M. Green and R. Lazarsfeld, predicts that the gonality of the algebraic curve *C* can be calculated by [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) means, from a [minimal resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_resolution_%28algebra%29) of an [invertible sheaf](wikipedia://en/581859) of high degree. In many cases the gonality is two more than the [Clifford index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_index). The **Green–Lazarsfeld conjecture** is an exact formula in terms of the [graded Betti numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graded_Betti_number) for a degree *d* embedding in *r* dimensions, for *d* large with respect to the genus. Writing *b*(*C*), with respect to a given such embedding of *C* and the minimal free resolution for its [homogeneous coordinate ring](wikipedia://en/23833909), for the minimum index *i* for which β<sub>*i*, *i* + 1</sub> is zero, then the conjectured formula for the gonality is

*r* + 1 − *b*(*C*).

According to the 1900 ICM talk of [Federico Amodeo](wikipedia://en/48171871), the notion (but not the terminology) originated in Section V of [Riemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann)'s *Theory of Abelian Functions.* Amodeo used the term "gonalità" as early as 1893.
