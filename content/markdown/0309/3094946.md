# Tate twist

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tate twist"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Tate twist**,[^o2][^o3] named after [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933), is an operation on [Galois modules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module).

:::

For example, if *K* is a [field](wikipedia://en/10603), *G<sub>K</sub>* is its [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781), and ρ : *G<sub>K</sub>* → Aut<sub>**Q**<sub>*p*</sub></sub>(*V*) is a [representation](wikipedia://en/12695) of *G<sub>K</sub>* on a finite-dimensional [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) *V* over the field **Q**<sub>*p*</sub> of [*p*-adic numbers](wikipedia://en/51423), then the Tate twist of *V*, denoted *V*(1), is the representation on the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/43079145) *V*⊗**Q**<sub>*p*</sub>(1), where **Q**<sub>*p*</sub>(1) is the [*p*-adic cyclotomic character](wikipedia://en/18846136) (i.e. the [Tate module](wikipedia://en/3094921) of the group of [roots of unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roots_of_unity) in the [separable closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_closure) *K<sup>s</sup>* of *K*). More generally, if *m* is a [positive integer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_integer), the ***m*th Tate twist of *V***, denoted *V*(*m*), is the tensor product of *V* with the *m*-fold tensor product of **Q**<sub>*p*</sub>(1). Denoting by **Q**<sub>*p*</sub>(−1) the [dual representation](wikipedia://en/1442471) of **Q**<sub>*p*</sub>(1), the *−m*th Tate twist of *V* can be defined as

$$V\otimes\mathbf{Q}_p(-1)^{\otimes m}.$$
