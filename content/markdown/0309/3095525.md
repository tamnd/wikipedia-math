# Noether's theorem on rationality for surfaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noether's theorem on rationality for surfaces"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Noether's theorem on rationality for surfaces** is a classical result of [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337) on complex [algebraic surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311), giving a criterion for a [rational surface](wikipedia://en/2380148). Let *S* be an algebraic surface that is [non-singular](wikipedia://en/253260#Singularities) and projective. Suppose there is a morphism φ from *S* to the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578), with *general fibre* also a projective line. Then the theorem states that *S* is rational.[^o2]
