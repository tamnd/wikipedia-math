# Seymour Lipschutz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Seymour Lipschutz"}

**Seymour Saul Lipschutz** (born 1931 died March 2018) was an author of technical books on [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), including a collection of [Schaum's Outlines](wikipedia://en/9597816).[^o3]

:::

Lipschutz received his Ph.D. in 1960 from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)'s [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences). He received his BA and MA degrees in Mathematics at [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College). He was a mathematics professor at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University), and before that on the faculty at the [Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_Brooklyn).[^o3]
