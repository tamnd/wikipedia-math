# David Vogan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Vogan"}

**David Alexander Vogan Jr.** (born September 8, 1954) is a mathematician at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) who works on [unitary representations](wikipedia://en/292788) of [simple Lie groups](wikipedia://en/292831).

:::

While studying at [the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Chicago), he became a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) in 1972.[^o4] He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from M.I.T. in 1976, under the supervision of [Bertram Kostant](wikipedia://en/3520590). In his thesis, he introduced the notion of lowest K type in the course of obtaining an algebraic classification of irreducible [Harish Chandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harish_Chandra) modules. He is currently one of the participants in the [Atlas of Lie Groups and Representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_of_Lie_Groups_and_Representations).

Vogan was elected to the American Academy of Arts and Sciences in 1996.[^o6] He served as head of the Department of Mathematics at MIT from 1999 to 2004.[^o7] In 2012 he became Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He was president of the AMS in 2013–2014.[^o9] He was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2013.[^o10] He was the [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185) Chair of Mathematics at MIT until his retirement in 2020, and is currently the Norbert Wiener Emeritus Professor of Mathematics.[^o11]

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Representations of real reductive Lie groups.* Birkhäuser, 1981[^o13]
- *Unitary representations of reductive Lie groups.* [Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press), 1987 [^o14]
- with [Paul Sally](wikipedia://en/2789475) (ed.): *Representation theory and harmonic analysis on semisimple Lie groups.* American Mathematical Society, 1989
- with Jeffrey Adams & Dan Barbasch (ed.): *The Langlands Classification and Irreducible Characters for Real Reductive Groups.* Birkhäuser, 1992
- with [Anthony W. Knapp](wikipedia://en/11072560): [*Cohomological Induction and Unitary Representations.*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Cohomological_Induction_and_Unitary_Repr.html?id=-dL9HXRV8IgC) Princeton University Press, 1995
- with [Joseph A. Wolf](wikipedia://en/41199481) and Juan Tirao (ed.): *Geometry and representation theory of real and p-adic groups.* Birkhäuser, 1998
- with Jeffrey Adams (ed.): *Representation theory of Lie groups.* American Mathematical Society, 2000
- *[The Character Table for E<sub>8</sub>](https://www.ams.org/notices/200709/).* In: *[Notices of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4977512)* Nr. 9, 2007 (PDF)
