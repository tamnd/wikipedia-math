# Alan Weiss (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alan Weiss"}

**Alan Weiss** (born December 5, 1955) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a pioneer in the usage of [large deviations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_deviations) theory in [performance evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Performance_evaluation) and related areas.

:::

Weiss received his [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University) taking courses from [Lajos Takács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajos_Takács) and being advised by [Arthur J. Lohwater](wikipedia://en/14380145) (1976). He received his [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in mathematics from [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) (1979) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1981; his advisor was [S. R. S. Varadhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._R._S._Varadhan), and his dissertation was entitled *Invariant Measures of Diffusion Processes on Domains with Boundaries*.[^o2] He worked at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs) (1981-2007), before joining [MathWorks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathWorks) of [Natick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natick). Weiss had appointments with [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (1986), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) (1993) and [Drew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_University) (2005).
