# Gérard Iooss

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gérard Iooss"}

**Gérard Iooss** (born 14 June 1944 in [Charbonnier-les-Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charbonnier-les-Mines), [Puy-de-Dôme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puy-de-Dôme)) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and mathematical problems of [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Iooss attended [school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School) in [Clermont-Ferrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clermont-Ferrand) and studied at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) from [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964) to [1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966). From 1967 to 1972 he was with the [*Office National d'Etudes et de Recherches Aérospatiales* (ONERA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ONERA). In 1971 he received his doctorate from the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6) with thesis *Théorie non linéaire de la stabilit des écoulements laminaires* under the supervision of Jean-Pierre Guireaud. Iooss was a professor from 1972 to 1974 at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in Orsay, and from 1974 at the [University of Nice Sophia-Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia-Antipolis), where he retired in 2007. From 1994 to 2004 he was at the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France). He is today at the Laboratoire J. A. Dieudonné of the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice).[^o5] (The Laboratoire J. A. Diedonné is a [unité mixte de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unité_mixte_de_recherche) (UMR) of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS).)[^o6]

He was also from 1970 to 1985 [Maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at the École Polytechnique.

He was a visiting professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) (1977/78), at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (1978), and at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) (1990, 1995, 1997), where he collaborated with Klaus Kirchgässner on reversible dynamical systems.

Iooss's research deals with functional analysis of the [Navier-Stokes equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equation), nonlinear hydrodynamic stability theory and water waves of different kinds, and general behavior (such as symmetry breaking and normal forms) of bifurcations (branching of solutions) in dynamic systems. In 1971, independently of David H. Sattinger, he treated the Hopf bifurcation in solutions of the [Navier-Stokes equation](wikipedia://en/48395) as an infinite dimensional dynamical system. He studied in particular the [Couette flow](wikipedia://en/2041980) (Taylor-Couette) and discovered there theoretically several waveforms, which were later confirmed experimentally.[^o5] He collaborated with [Alain Chenciner](wikipedia://en/48254929) on bifurcation of invariant tori.[^o7] Iooss, with Pierre Coullet, classified the instabilities of spatially periodic patterns in translation-invariant and mirror-symmetric systems.[^o5]

Iooss was elected in 1990 a corresponding member of the *[Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences)*. In 1993 he received the Max-Planck-Forschungspreis. He received in 2008 the *[Prix Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Ampère)* and in 1978 the *Prix Henri de Partille* of the *Académie des sciences*.

In 1998 he was Invited Speaker with talk *Traveling water waves as a paradigm for bifurcations in reversible infinite dimensional dynamical systems* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o8]
