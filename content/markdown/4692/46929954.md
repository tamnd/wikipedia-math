# Baruch Barzel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baruch Barzel"}

**Baruch Barzel** (ברוך ברזל; March 19, 1976) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) physicist and applied mathematician at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University),[^o3] a member of the Gonda Multidisciplinary Brain Research Center[^o4] and of the Bar-Ilan [Data Science](wikipedia://en/35458904) Institute.[^o5] His main research areas are [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics),[^o6][^o7] [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_systems),[^o8][^o9] [nonlinear dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamics) and [network science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_science).[^o10][^o11][^o12]

:::

In 2013 he introduced the concept of [universality](wikipedia://en/1752072) in the dynamics of [complex networks](wikipedia://en/1704711), showing that complex systems from different domains condense into discrete forms, or [universality classes](wikipedia://en/353890), of dynamic behavior. In the following years, Barzel and colleagues developed a theoretical framework to predict the observed behavior of complex networked systems: their patterns of information flow;[^o13] the timescales of their signal propagation;[^o14] their resilience against failures and disruptions[^o15][^o16] and their recoverability.[^o17]

During the COVID-19 Pandemic [Barzel's lab](https://www.barzellab.com/) published the alternating quarantine strategy[^o18] to mitigate the spread of [SARS-CoV-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severe_acute_respiratory_syndrome_coronavirus_2) alongside continuous socioeconomic activity. The strategy was implemented by several agencies in Israel[^o19][^o20] and around the world.[^o21]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Barzel completed his Ph.D. in physics at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) as a [Hoffman Fellow](http://hoffman-program.huji.ac.il/).[^o23] He then pursued his postdoctoral training at the [Center for Complex Network Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Complex_Network_Research)[^o24] at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University) and at the [Channing Division of Network Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Division_of_Network_Medicine),[^o25] [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School). Barzel is a recipient of the Racah prize (2007) and the [Krill prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krill_prize) of the [Wolf Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Foundation) (2019).[^o26] Barzel is also an active public lecturer on science and on Judaism, and presents a weekly corner on Jewish thought on [Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Public_Broadcasting_Corporation) .[^o27]

Dr. Barzel's research focuses on the dynamic behavior of complex networks, uncovering universal principles that govern the dynamics of diverse systems, such as disease spreading,[^o28][^o18][^o29] gene regulatory[^o11][^o30] networks, protein interactions or [population dynamics](wikipedia://en/501466).[^o31]
