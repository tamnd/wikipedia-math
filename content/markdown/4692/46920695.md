# David P. Williamson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David P. Williamson"}

*Image: David P. Williamson (2003).jpg.* David Paul Williamson

**David Paul Williamson** is a professor of [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University),[^o2] and the editor-in-chief of the *[SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/30666823)*.[^o3] He earned his Ph.D. in 1993 from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) under the supervision of [Michel Goemans](wikipedia://en/34561422), and is best known for his work with Goemans on [approximation algorithms](wikipedia://en/563105) based on [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539), for which they won the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) in 2000.[^o5] He also received the [Frederick W. Lanchester Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_W._Lanchester_Prize) in 2013. In 2022 he received the [AMS Steele Prize for Seminal Contribution to Research](wikipedia://en/1252550).[^o6]
