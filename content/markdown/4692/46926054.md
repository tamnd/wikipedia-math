# Cosmos (category theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cosmos"}

In the area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) known as [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **cosmos** is a [symmetric](wikipedia://en/3865249) [closed monoidal category](wikipedia://en/1396924) that is [complete](wikipedia://en/62781) and [cocomplete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocomplete_category). [Enriched category theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enriched_category_theory) is often considered over a cosmos.[^o3]

:::
