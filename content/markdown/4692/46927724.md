# P-adic cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="P-adic cohomology"}

In mathematics, **p-adic cohomology** means a [cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_theory) for varieties of characteristic *p* whose values are [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) over a ring of *p*-adic integers. Examples (in roughly historical order) include:

:::

- Serre's [Witt vector cohomology](wikipedia://en/51530038)
- [Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology](wikipedia://en/26005931)
- [Infinitesimal cohomology](wikipedia://en/51434698)
- [Crystalline cohomology](wikipedia://en/10220067)
- [Rigid cohomology](wikipedia://en/26002282)
- [Prismatic cohomology](wikipedia://en/83781807)
- [Habiro cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habiro_cohomology)
