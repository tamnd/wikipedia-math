# Luis Vázquez Martínez

Vázquez

*Image: LuisVazquezMartinez.png.* Martínez at the Faculty of Informatics (Complutense University of Madrid)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luis Vázquez Martínez"}

**Luis Vázquez Martínez** is a Spanish applied mathematician. He is a professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) in the faculty of [informatics](wikipedia://en/23997153) of the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid).[^o2]

:::

Vázquez was born on January 26, 1949, in [Narayola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narayola), a town in the municipality of [Camponaraya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camponaraya). He earned a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in physical sciences from the Complutense University of Madrid in 1971,[^o2] and a doctorate in physical sciences from the [University of Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zaragoza) in 1975, under the supervision of Antonio Fernández-Rañada Menéndez de Luarca. After working as a Visiting Research Associate at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), he returned to the Complutense University as an assistant professor in 1977.[^o2]

In 1995 the [Shanghai University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_University_of_Science_and_Technology) gave him an honorary doctorate.[^o2] Since 2005 has been a national corresponding member of the [Spanish Royal Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Royal_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]
