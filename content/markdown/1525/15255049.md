# Norwegian Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Norwegian Mathematical Society"}

The **Norwegian Mathematical Society** (Norsk matematisk forening, NMF) is a professional society for mathematicians. It was formed in 1918, with [Carl Størmer](wikipedia://en/1906523) elected as its first president.[^o3] It organizes mathematical contests and the annual [Abel symposium](wikipedia://en/159285) and also awards the [Viggo Brun](wikipedia://en/40647) Prize to young [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) for outstanding research in mathematics, including mathematical aspects of [information technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and [computational science](wikipedia://en/1181008).[^o4] The 2018 Prize winner was Rune Gjøringbø Haugseng.[^o5] The NMF is a member of the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227) and provides the Norwegian National Committee in the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868).[^o6]

:::
