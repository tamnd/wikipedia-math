# Jean-Louis Colliot-Thélène

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Louis Colliot-Thélène"}

**Jean-Louis Colliot-Thélène** (born 2 December 1947) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a Directeur de Recherches at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the [Université Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Saclay) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay). He studies mainly [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

- Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) "[Charles Louis de Saulces de Freycine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Freycinet)" (1985)
- Invited Speaker to the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (Berkeley 1986)
- [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) for mathematical research (1991)
- Grand prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) "[Léonid Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Léonid_Frank)" (2009)
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (2012)[^o4]
