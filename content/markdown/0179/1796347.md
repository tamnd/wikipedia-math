# Paul Montel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Montel"}

**Paul Antoine Aristide Montel** (29 April 1876 – 22 January 1975) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice), France and died in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), France. He researched mostly on [holomorphic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_functions) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

Montel was a student of [Émile Borel](wikipedia://en/356161) at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536), [Jean Dieudonné](wikipedia://en/1070084) and [Miron Nicolescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miron_Nicolescu) were among his students.

Montel's most important contribution to mathematics was the introduction and systematic development of the notion of [normal family](wikipedia://en/1480325).[^o3] This very influential book also contains the first exposition in the book form of the results of [Pierre Fatou](wikipedia://en/629100) and [Gaston Julia](wikipedia://en/249107) on [holomorphic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_dynamics). The notion of normal family was a predecessor of the notion of [compact space](wikipedia://en/6042) introduced by [Pavel Alexandrov](wikipedia://en/576245) and [Pavel Urysohn](wikipedia://en/23770) in 1929.[^o4]
