# Floris Takens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Floris Takens"}

**Floris Takens** (12 November 1940 – 20 June 2010)[^o3] was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for contributions to the theory of [chaotic dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/6295).

:::

Together with [David Ruelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ruelle), he predicted that [fluid turbulence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_turbulence) could develop through a [strange attractor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strange_attractor), a term they coined, as opposed to the then-prevailing theory of [accretion of modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau-Hopf_theory_of_turbulence). The prediction was later confirmed by [experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gollub-Swinney_circular_Couette_experiment). Takens also established the result now known as the [Takens's theorem](wikipedia://en/744335), which shows how to reconstruct a dynamical system from an observed [time-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-series). He was the first to show how chaotic attractors could be learned by neural networks.

Takens was born in [Zaandam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaandam) in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands). He attended schools in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague) and in Zaandam before serving in the Dutch army for one year (1960–1961). At the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), he concluded his undergraduate and graduate studies. He was granted a doctorate in mathematics in 1969 under the supervision of [Nicolaas Kuiper](wikipedia://en/3344929) for his thesis *The minimal number of critical points of a function on a compact manifold and the [Lusternik–Schnirelmann category](wikipedia://en/764433)*.

After his graduate work, Takens spent a year at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques), in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette), near Paris, where he worked with [David Ruelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ruelle), [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633), and [Jacob Palis](wikipedia://en/1540070). His friendship with Palis took him many times to the [Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_de_Matemática_Pura_e_Aplicada) (IMPA) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), Brazil. Their collaboration produced several joint publications.

Takens was a professor at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen), in [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen), the Netherlands, from 1972 until he retired from teaching in 1999.[^o4]

Takens was a member of:

- The [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (since 1991)[^o5]
- The Brazilian Academy of Sciences (since 1981), and
- The editorial board for the Springer-Verlag's *Lecture Notes in Mathematics.*
