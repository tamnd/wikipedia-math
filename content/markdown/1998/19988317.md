# Edward Ng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Ng"}

**Edward W Ng** (; September 23, 1939 – December 14, 2018) was an American [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) who had also held the positions of senior scientist, senior [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and technical manager in the [U.S. Space Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Space_Program). He is noted for his broad variety of mathematical applications in [space science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_science) and engineering. He has also contributed conscientiously in the spin-off of technology from the space program, with applications in such diverse subjects as [Bose–Einstein distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bose–Einstein_distribution) in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [symbolic and algebraic computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_and_algebraic_computation), [computational physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_physics) and [biomedical research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedical_research).

:::

Ng attended the [Queen Elizabeth School, Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_School,_Hong_Kong) for secondary education, and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) for his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree). He received his M.A. and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) degrees from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). He was employed at the [Jet Propulsion Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Propulsion_Laboratory) ([JPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPL)) of the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), a.k.a. [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) under contract with the [National Aeronautics and Space Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Aeronautics_and_Space_Administration) ([NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)). He retired in 2006.

Ng was elected a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (AAAS) and is cited in the [American Men and Women of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Men_and_Women_of_Science).[^o2] He was the editor of "Symbolic and Algebraic Computation" (),[^o3] in the [Springer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science) Lecture Notes Series. He was also a co-editor of the Proceedings of a [NATO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO) Advanced Study Institute in applied mathematics ().[^o4] He has published numerous technical papers of diverse mathematical applications in space sciences and engineering (selected list in external link below). His papers have appeared in [Astrophysical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysical_Journal), [Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation), [Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_Mechanics_and_Dynamical_Astronomy),[^o5] [Journal of Computational Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Computational_Physics), [M.I.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.I.T.) [Journal of Mathematics and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematics_and_Physics),[^o6] [ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Transactions_on_Mathematical_Software) and Proceedings of the [American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institute_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics).

## Notable quotes {#Notable_quotes}

- "In science, one man's noise is another man's signal." cited in New York Times (1990).[^o8] The context was in [space science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_science) data and the reference was to the scientific-engineering concept of [Signal-to-noise ratio](wikipedia://en/41706).
- *Why Do We Explore Space?*[^o9] an educational paper in **Space 2006**,[^o10]
- *Nobel Connection to the Space Program*,[^o11] a paper in **Space 2007**,[^o12] published by the [American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institute_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics).
- High-precision approximations of **Bose–Einstein Functions**[^o13] in the *[Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation)* published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
- Information Systems for **Space Astrophysics**\* [^o14]

## Awards {#Awards}

Ng has received a number of NASA Awards (cf attached citations),[^o16] including the special NASA Certificate of Apollo-Columbia Commemoration of the Space Transportation System–65 Flight in 1994. He also was the recipient of the American Astronomical Society Team Appreciation Award on the [Deep Space Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Space_Network).[^o17]
