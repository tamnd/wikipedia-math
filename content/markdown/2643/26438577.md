# Gilles Pisier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gilles Pisier"}

**Gilles I. Pisier** (born 18 November 1950) is a professor of mathematics at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) and a distinguished professor and A.G. and M.E. Owen Chair of Mathematics at the [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University).[^o3][^o4] He is known for his contributions to several fields of mathematics, including [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675). He has also made fundamental contributions to the theory of [C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebras).[^o5] Gilles is the younger brother of French actress [Marie-France Pisier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie-France_Pisier).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Pisier has obtained many fundamental results in various parts of mathematical analysis.

### Geometry of Banach spaces {#Geometry_of_Banach_spaces}

In the "local theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989)", Pisier and Bernard Maurey developed the theory of [Rademacher type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rademacher_type), following its use in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) by J. Hoffman–Jorgensen and in the characterization of [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932) among [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) by S. Kwapień. Using [probability](wikipedia://en/23542) in [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370), Pisier proved that [super-reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091#Super-reflexive_space) Banach spaces can be renormed with the [modulus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulus_of_convexity) of [uniform convexity](wikipedia://en/9133418) having "power type".[^o8][^o9] His work (with [Per Enflo](wikipedia://en/7180897) and [Joram Lindenstrauss](wikipedia://en/20653945)) on the "three–space problem" influenced the work on [quasi–normed](wikipedia://en/233801) spaces by [Nigel Kalton](wikipedia://en/34221149).

### Operator theory {#Operator_theory}

Pisier transformed the area of [operator spaces](wikipedia://en/20754678). In the 1990s, he solved two long-standing open problems. In the theory of [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184), he solved, jointly with [Marius Junge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marius_Junge), the problem of the uniqueness of C\* -norms on the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of two copies of *B(H)*, the bounded linear operators on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H*. He and Junge were able to produce two such tensor norms that are nonequivalent.[^o5] In 1997, he constructed an [operator](wikipedia://en/22213) that was polynomially bounded but not similar to a [contraction](wikipedia://en/10907456), answering a famous question of [Paul Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598).

## Awards {#Awards}

He was an invited speaker at the 1983 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842)[^o12] and a plenary speaker at the 1998 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o13][^o14] In 1997, Pisier received the [Ostrowski Prize](wikipedia://en/3159411) for this work. He is also a recipient of the Grands Prix de l'Académie des Sciences de Paris in 1992 and the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1979.[^o15] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o16]

- Pisier, Gilles. (1996). "The operator Hilbert space OH, complex interpolation and tensor norms". isbn:978-1-4704-0170-2
- Pisier, Gilles; Conference Board of the Mathematical Sciences. (1986). "Factorization of linear operators and geometry of Banach spaces". Published for the Conference Board of the Mathematical Sciences by the American Mathematical Society. isbn:0-8218-0710-2
