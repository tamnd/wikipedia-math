# Edinburgh Mathematical Notes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edinburgh Mathematical Notes"}

***Edinburgh Mathematical Notes*** was a [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published from 1909 until 1961 by the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).

:::

The journal was originally named *Mathematical Notes*, with the subtitle *A Review of Elementary Mathematics and Science*.[^o2] Its creation was the suggestion of George Alexander Gibson, a professor at [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow), who wished to remove the more elementary or pedagogical articles from the *Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society*.[^o3] Is founding editor was [Peter Pinkerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Pinkerton), who at the time headed the mathematics department of [George Watson's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson's_College) and later became rector of the [High School of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_School_of_Glasgow).[^o4]

21 issues of the journal were published from 1909 to 1916. During [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), the journal went on hiatus, resumed publishing for two issues in 1924 and 1925, and then it went on hiatus again until 1929. In 1939, it changed its name from *Mathematical Notes* to *Edinburgh Mathematical Notes*. In 1958 its issues began being published as issues of the *Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society*, and after 1961 they became incorporated into a department of the *Proceedings* rather than being published separately. After 1967 it stopped appearing altogether.[^o2]

The archives of the journal are available online to subscribers through the [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o3]
