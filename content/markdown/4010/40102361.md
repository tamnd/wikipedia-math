# Primecoin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Primecoin"}

**Primecoin** ([Abbreviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_4217#Cryptocurrencies): **XPM**) is a [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188) that implements a [proof-of-work system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof-of-work_system) that searches for chains of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Primecoin was launched in July 7, 2013 by Sunny King, who also founded [Peercoin](wikipedia://en/39074911).[^o6][^o8]

Unlike other cryptocurrencies, which are [mined](wikipedia://en/36662188#Mining) using algorithms that solved mathematical problems with no extrinsic value, mining Primecoin involves producing chains of prime numbers ([Cunningham](wikipedia://en/321790) and [bi-twin chains](wikipedia://en/40142946)). These are useful to scientists and mathematicians and meet the requirements for a [proof of work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_work) system of being hard to compute but easy to verify and having an adjustable difficulty.[^o5][^o10][^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]

Shortly after its launch, some trade journals reported that the rush of over 18,000 new users seeking to mine Primecoin overwhelmed providers of dedicated servers.[^o10][^o11] It was ranked as being one of the top ten currencies before 2014.[^o12]

Primecoin has a [block time](wikipedia://en/44065971#Block_time) of one minute, changes difficulty every block, and has a [block reward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_reward) that is a function of the difficulty.[^o6][^o9]
