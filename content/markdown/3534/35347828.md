# Steinerian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steinerian"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Steinerian** of a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101), introduced by (txt), is the [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) of the [singular points](wikipedia://en/981915) of its [polar quadrics](wikipedia://en/35326250).

:::
