# Cayleyan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cayleyan"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Cayleyan** is a [variety](wikipedia://en/248808) associated to a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) by (txt), who named it the **pippian** in (Cayley 1857) and also called it the **Steiner–Hessian**.

:::
