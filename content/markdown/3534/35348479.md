# Quippian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quippian"}

In mathematics, a **quippian** is a degree 5 class 3 contravariant of a plane cubic introduced by (txt) and discussed by (txt, p.157). In the same paper Cayley also introduced another similar invariant that he called the **pippian**, now called the [Cayleyan](wikipedia://en/35348245).

:::
