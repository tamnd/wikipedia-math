# Arthur Hirsch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Hirsch"}

**Arthur Hirsch** (1866–1948) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Hirsch completed his schooling in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg) in 1882 and then studied mathematics and physics in the universities of [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) and [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg). Among his teachers at Königsberg were [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) and [Adolf Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/771690).[^n1] In 1892 he received a doctorate from Königsberg for a thesis about [linear differential equations](wikipedia://en/379868).[^n2]

The following year, he took his [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at [Polytechnikum of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), where he was, successively, assistant professor from 1893, titular professor from 1897 and ordinary professor from 1903[^n3] until his retirement in 1936.

The work of Hirsch is primarily on [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [hypergeometric functions](wikipedia://en/2055223). He published seven papers about it in *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)*. Hirsch was a member of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society) from his foundation in 1910.[^n4]
