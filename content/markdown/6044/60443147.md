# Feodor Theilheimer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Feodor Theilheimer"}

**Feodor Theilheimer** (1909–2000) was a German mathematician who studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg). He lectured in mathematics at [Trinity College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_%28Connecticut%29) and worked for the U.S. Department of Defense on the research and development of ship design and construction.

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Theilheimer was born in [Gunzenhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunzenhausen), Germany on 18 June 1909, the youngest of six children, to Gustav Theilheimer and Rosa Theilheimer (née Waldmann). Gustav was a cattle dealer and hops buyer for breweries. Feodor went to [Realschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realschule) in Gunzenhausen and Oberrealschule in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg).[^o9]

He attended the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg) where he studied mathematics and physics. From 1928 to 1931 he studied at the [Telshe Yeshiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telshe_Yeshiva) in [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania). In 1931 he attended the [Hildesheimer Rabbinical Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildesheimer_Rabbinical_Seminary) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o9]

In 1932 he started studying mathematics at the [Friedrich-Wilhelms University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) and received his PhD in 1936 with the thesis *Ein Beitrag zur Theorie der charakteristischen Invarianten* (A contribution to the theory of characteristic invariants).[^o10] He was one of the last [Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews) to graduate from that university.[^o9] Some of the mathematicians working in Berlin while Theilheimer was there were [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186), [Alfred Brauer](wikipedia://en/22923580), [Georg Feigl](wikipedia://en/40614663), [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191), [Richard von Mises](wikipedia://en/821517), and [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522), his thesis advisor.[^o10]

He taught in Berlin for one year but as it became increasingly difficult to teach as a [Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) in Germany under the Nazi regime, he emigrated to the USA in 1937.[^o9][^o10] In the USA it was difficult to find work as a German immigrant and from 1937 to 1941 he tutored Jewish refugees in [St Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Louis).[^o10]

In 1941 he joined [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) and lectured a course entitled *Program of Advanced Research and Instruction in Mechanics*. In 1942 he was appointed lecturer in mathematics at [Trinity College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_%28Connecticut%29) and was promoted to assistant professor in 1946.[^o10]

In 1948 he moved to Washington, DC where he worked for the U.S. Department of Defense as a mathematician on the research and development of ship design and construction.[^o9][^o10]

Theilheimer attended several meetings of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) over the period 1947 to 1955 and presented papers at these meetings.[^o10]

## Works {#Works}

He completed his thesis on [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762) in 1936 despite the challenges of being a non-Aryan. Most of his later career was in aerodynamics and ship modeling for example on the use of high speed computers to ease the drawing of ship lines. For this work he received the David W Taylor Award for Scientific Achievement. He worked on [Fast Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/11512) algorithms and how to quickly factor matrices to a product of zeros.[^o10]

## Private life {#Private_life}

In 1948 Theilheimer married Henriette "Henny" Rubel and they had one daughter, Rachel born in 1950 in Silver Spring, [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland). They lived in [Chevy Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevy_Chase,_Maryland), Maryland.[^o9][^o10]

Theilheimer retired in 1977 but still taught mathematics courses at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) until 1983. Theilheimer died on 24 December 2000 in Chevy Chase. His wife then moved to New York where she died in 2013.[^o10]
