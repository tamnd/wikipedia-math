# Chern Medal

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Chern Medal"}

The **Chern Medal** is an international award recognizing outstanding lifelong achievement of the highest level in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). The prize is given at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM), which is held every four years.

:::

## Introduction {#Introduction}

Named in honor of the late Chinese mathematician [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905), the award is a joint effort of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU) and the **Chern Medal Foundation** (CMF) to be bestowed in the same fashion as the IMU's other three awards (the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), the [Abacus Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevanlinna_Prize), and the [Gauss Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss_Prize)), i.e. at the opening ceremony of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM), which is held every four years. The first such occasion was at the 2010 ICM in [Hyderabad, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India).[^o5]

Each recipient receives a medal decorated with Chern's likeness, a cash prize of \$250,000 ([USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)), and the opportunity to direct \$250,000 of charitable donations to one or more organizations for the purpose of supporting research, education, or outreach in mathematics.[^o5]

## Laureates {#Laureates}

::: {#o7 .table cue=table}

| Award year | Winner | Reasons |
|---|---|---|
| 2010 | [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725) | "For his role in the formulation of the modern theory of non-linear elliptic [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and for mentoring numerous students and post-docs in this area."[^o8] |
| 2014 | [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414) | "For his groundbreaking and transformative development of transcendental methods in [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), particularly his seminal work in [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590) and periods of algebraic varieties."[^o9][^o10] |
| 2018 | [Masaki Kashiwara](wikipedia://en/4376209) | "For his outstanding and foundational contributions to algebraic analysis and representation theory sustained over a period of almost 50 years."[^o11] |
| 2022 | [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679) | "For his profound discoveries in topology, arithmetic geometry and number theory, and his leadership and generosity in forming the next generation of Mathematicians."[^o12] |
| 2026 | [Graeme Segal](wikipedia://en/6713316) | "For his visionary mathematical insights that have had an enduring influence in a wide range of fields, including topology, mathematical physics, representation theory and category theory."[^o13] |

:::
