# Absolute difference

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Absolute difference"}

*Image: Absolute difference.svg.* Showing the absolute difference of real numbers $x$ and $y$ as the distance between them on the [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line).

The **absolute difference** of two [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) $x$ and $y$ is given by $|x-y|$, the [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991) of their [difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_%28mathematics%29). It describes the distance on the [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line) between the points corresponding to $x$ and $y$, and is a special case of the [L<sup>p</sup> distance](wikipedia://en/45194) for all $1\le p\le\infty$. Its applications in statistics include the [absolute deviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_deviation) from a [central tendency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_tendency).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Absolute difference has the following properties:

- For $x\ge 0$, $|x-0|=x$ (zero is the [identity element](wikipedia://en/14962) on non-negative numbers)[^o8]
- For all $x$, $|x-x|=0$ (every element is its own [inverse element](wikipedia://en/45199))[^o8]
- $|x-y|\ge 0$ (non-negativity)[^o4]
- $|x-y|= 0$ if and only if $x=y$ (nonzero for distinct arguments).[^o4]
- $|x-y|=|y-x|$ (*[symmetry](wikipedia://en/2714149)* or *[commutativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutativity)*).[^o8][^o4]
- $|x-z|\le|x-y|+|y-z|$ (the *[triangle inequality](wikipedia://en/53941)*);[^o4][^o5] equality holds if and only if $x\le y\le z$ or $x\ge y\ge z$.

Because it is non-negative, nonzero for distinct arguments, symmetric, and obeys the triangle inequality, the real numbers form a [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018) with the absolute difference as its distance, the familiar measure of distance along a line.[^o6] It has been called "the most natural metric space",[^n1] and "the most important concrete metric space".[^o4] This distance generalizes in many different ways to higher dimensions, as a special case of the [L<sup>p</sup> distances](wikipedia://en/45194) for all $1\le p\le\infty$, including the $p=1$ and $p=2$ cases ([taxicab geometry](wikipedia://en/408354) and [Euclidean distance](wikipedia://en/53932), respectively). It is also the one-dimensional special case of [hyperbolic distance](wikipedia://en/318742).

Instead of $|x-y|$, the absolute difference may also be expressed as

$$\max(x,y)-\min(x,y).$$

Generalizing this to more than two values, in any subset $S$ of the real numbers which has an [infimum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infimum) and a [supremum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum), the absolute difference between any two numbers in $S$ is less or equal than the absolute difference of the infimum and supremum of $S$.

The absolute difference takes non-negative integers to non-negative integers. As a binary operation that is commutative but not associative, with an identity element on the non-negative numbers, the absolute difference gives the non-negative numbers (whether real or integer) the algebraic structure of a [commutative magma](wikipedia://en/7741863) with identity.[^o8]

## Applications {#Applications}

The absolute difference is used to define the [relative difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_difference), the absolute difference between a given value and a reference value divided by the reference value itself.[^o11]

In the theory of [graceful labelings](wikipedia://en/8978968) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), vertices are labeled by [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) and edges are labeled by the absolute difference of the numbers at their two vertices. A labeling of this type is graceful when the edge labels are distinct and consecutive from 1 to the number of edges.[^o12]

As well as being a special case of the L<sup>p</sup> distances, absolute difference can be used to define [Chebyshev distance](wikipedia://en/1503963) (L<sup>∞</sup>), in which the distance between points is the maximum or supremum of the absolute differences of their coordinates.[^o13]

In statistics, the [absolute deviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_deviation) of a sampled number from a [central tendency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_tendency) is its absolute difference from the center, the [average absolute deviation](wikipedia://en/344242) is the average of the absolute deviations of a collection of samples, and [least absolute deviations](wikipedia://en/19048902) is a method for [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) based on minimizing the average absolute deviation.
