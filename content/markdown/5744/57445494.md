# Howard L. Resnikoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard L. Resnikoff"}

**Howard Leonard Resnikoff** (May 13, 1937 – March 5, 2018) was an American mathematician and business executive.

:::

Resnikoff was born in New York City on May 13, 1937. He obtained a bachelor's degree from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and earned a doctorate from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the supervision of [Ádám Korányi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ádám_Korányi).[^o7][^o2] Resnikoff began teaching at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 1967. He joined the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) faculty in 1975.[^o7] Between 1979 and 1981, Resnikoff was director of the Division of Information Science and Technology at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).[^o4] He became an associate vice president at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1981, serving until 1983. Resnikoff worked for [Thinking Machines Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Machines_Corporation) and founded [Aware, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aware,_Inc.) after leaving Harvard, as well as FutureWave, Inc.[^o7] He received thirteen patents for a variety of inventions,[^o7][^o5] and in later life served on the board of governors of the [Jacobs University Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobs_University_Bremen) and was president and chairman of the institution's Foundation of America.[^o6][^o7] Resnikoff's wife Joan is a painter and ceramic artist.[^o8] He died on March 5, 2018.[^o7]
