# Élisabeth Lutz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Élisabeth Lutz"}

**Élisabeth Lutz** (May 14, 1914 – July 31, 2008) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). The [Nagell–Lutz theorem](wikipedia://en/468321) in [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205) describes the [torsion points](wikipedia://en/3039330) of [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225); it is named after Lutz and [Trygve Nagell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trygve_Nagell), who both published it in the 1930s.[^o2]L37

:::

Lutz was a student of [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg), from 1934 to 1938. She earned a [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) for her research for him, on [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) over [$p$-adic fields](wikipedia://en/51423).[^o3][^o4] She completed her doctorate (*thèse d’état*) on $p$-adic [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558) at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Grenoble) in 1951 under the supervision of [Claude Chabauty](wikipedia://en/59969488); her dissertation was *Sur les approximations diophantiennes linéaires $p$-adiques*.

She became a professor of mathematics at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes).[^o6]
