# Marguerite's Theorem

::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Marguerite's Theorem"}

***Marguerite's Theorem*** (Le Théorème de Marguerite) is 2023 French-Swiss drama film co-written and directed by Anna Novion. It is about a female mathematics student at [ENS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) whose career is upended when an error is discovered in her work.[^o7][^o8] It premiered on 22 May 2023 at the [76th Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cannes_Film_Festival). It was distributed in France on 1 November 2023.

:::

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Marguerite is a young and brilliant mathematician, the only girl in her class at the [ENS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure), entirely devoted to her passion. The day an error is discovered in her thesis, she is devastated. In a dizzy spell, she leaves the school, wiping out the past. She then dives into the real world, discovers autonomy, befriends the young Noa, and has sex for the first time. Matured by her experiences, it is in this new momentum that she manages to find a correct proof of her theorem.

## Production {#Production}

To show mathematics realistically on the screen, Anna Novion worked with French mathematician [Ariane Mézard](wikipedia://en/67033462), who provided all the equations written by the characters in the film, so that they are all authentic. Moreover, Mézard even made real progress on [Goldbach's conjecture](wikipedia://en/44758), the goal of Marguerite's work, for the film.[^o12]

## Release {#Release}

*Marguerite's Theorem* was selected to be screened in the Special Screenings section at the [76th Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cannes_Film_Festival),[^o14] where it had its world premiere on 22 May 2023.[^o15]

The film was theatrically released in France by Pyramide on 1 November 2023.[^o16][^o17]
