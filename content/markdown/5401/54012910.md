# Ferdinand Gonseth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinand Gonseth"}

*Image: Ferdinand Gonseth ETH-Bib Portr 00116.jpg.* Ferdinand Gonseth

**Ferdinand Gonseth** (1890–1975) was a Swiss mathematician and philosopher.[^o2]

:::

He was born on 22 September 1890 at [Sonvilier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonvilier), the son of Ferdinand Gonseth, a clockmaker, and his wife Marie Bourquin. He studied at [La Chaux-de-Fonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Chaux-de-Fonds), and read physics and mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), from 1910 to 1914.[^o3]

In 1929 Gonseth succeeded [Jérôme Franel](wikipedia://en/37393927) as Professor of Higher Mathematics at ETH.[^o4] In 1947 he founded *[Dialectica](wikipedia://en/26204033)*, with [Paul Bernays](wikipedia://en/2556580) and [Gaston Bachelard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaston_Bachelard). In the same year he took the newly created chair of [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science) at ETH.[^o5]

Gonseth died on 17 December 1975 at [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne).[^o2] He was noted for his "open philosophy", according to which science and mathematics lacked absolute foundations.[^o6] See Idoneism.
