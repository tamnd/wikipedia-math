# Vern Paulsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vern Paulsen"}

**Vern Ival Paulsen** (born 1951) is an American mathematician, focusing in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras), [frame theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frame_theory), [C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebras), and [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Paulsen studied mathematics at [Western Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Michigan_University), obtaining a BA in 1973. He then moved to [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and obtained his Ph.D. in mathematics under [Carl Pearcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Pearcy) in 1977. He spent the following two years at [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) as an instructor. Since 1979, he has been a faculty member in the Department of Mathematics at [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston). He was since 1996 the John and Rebecca Moores Professor at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston).[^o4][^o5]

In 2015, Paulsen moved to Canada and became a professor in the department of pure mathematics at the [Institute for Quantum Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Quantum_Computing) and at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o6]
