# Yuri A. Kuznetsov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri A. Kuznetsov"}

**Yuri A. Kuznetsov** is a Russian-American mathematician currently the M. D. Anderson Chair Professor of Mathematics at [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) and Editor-in-Chief of *[Journal of Numerical Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Numerical_Mathematics)*.[^o2][^o3]

:::
