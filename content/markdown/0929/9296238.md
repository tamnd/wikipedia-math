# Chebyshev–Markov–Stieltjes inequalities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chebyshev–Markov–Stieltjes inequalities"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), the **Chebyshev–Markov–Stieltjes** [inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489) are inequalities related to the [problem of moments](wikipedia://en/1244992) that were formulated in the 1880s by [Pafnuty Chebyshev](wikipedia://en/149568) and proved independently by [Andrey Markov](wikipedia://en/915) and (somewhat later) by [Thomas Jan Stieltjes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jan_Stieltjes).[^o2] Informally, they provide sharp bounds on a [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) from above and from below in terms of its first [moments](wikipedia://en/368684).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formulation"}

## Formulation {#Formulation}

Given *m*<sub>0</sub>,...,*m*<sub>2*m*-1</sub> ∈ **R**, consider the collection **C** of measures *μ* on **R** such that

$$\int x^k d\mu(x) = m_k$$

for *k* = 0,1,...,2*m* − 1 (and in particular the integral is defined and finite).

Let *P*<sub>0</sub>,*P*<sub>1</sub>, ...,*P*<sub>*m*</sub> be the first *m* + 1 [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) with respect to *μ* ∈ **C**, and let *ξ*<sub>1</sub>,...*ξ*<sub>*m*</sub> be the zeros of *P*<sub>*m*</sub>. It is not hard to see that the polynomials *P*<sub>0</sub>,*P*<sub>1</sub>, ...,*P*<sub>*m*-1</sub> and the numbers *ξ*<sub>1</sub>,...*ξ*<sub>*m*</sub> are the same for every *μ* ∈ **C**, and therefore are determined uniquely by *m*<sub>0</sub>,...,*m*<sub>2*m*-1</sub>.

Denote

$$\rho_{m-1}(z) = 1 \Big/ \sum_{k=0}^{m-1} |P_k(z)|^2.$$

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** For *j* = 1,2,...,*m*, and any *μ* ∈ **C**,

$$\mu(-\infty, \xi_j] \leq \rho_{m-1}(\xi_1) + \cdots + \rho_{m-1}(\xi_j) \leq \mu(-\infty,\xi_{j+1}).$$

:::
