# List of things named after Alfred Tarski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Alfred Tarski"}

In the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920) (1901–1983) is one of the most important [logicians](wikipedia://en/46426065). His name is now associated with a number of [theorems](wikipedia://en/30977) and concepts in that field.

:::

## Other {#Other}

- [13672 Tarski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13672_Tarski), a main-belt asteroid
- In 2019, Polish entomologist Marcin Kamiński named a newly discovered species of South African darkling beetle, *Machleida tarskii*, in honor of Alfred Tarski's contributions to science. [^o32]
