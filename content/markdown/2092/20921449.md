# Cryptol

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptol"}

**Cryptol** is a [domain-specific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain-specific_language) [programming language](wikipedia://en/23015) for [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) developed by the [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon) based [software development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development) firm, Galois, Inc.[^o3][^o4] It is [free and open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software) released under a [BSD 3-clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSD_licenses) [software license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_license).

:::

The language was originally developed for use by the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939).[^o3][^o5] The language is also used by private firms that provide [information technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology) systems, such as the American company [Rockwell Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockwell_Collins) provides to [aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_manufacturer) and [defense contractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_contractor) in the United States.[^o3]

The programming language is used for all aspects of developing and using cryptography, such as the design and implementation of new [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244) and the verification of existing cryptographic [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775).[^o3][^o4][^o6] Cryptol is designed to allow a cryptographer to watch how [stream processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stream_processing) functions in the program manipulate ciphers or [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) algorithms.[^o4]
