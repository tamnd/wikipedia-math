# Annette Dobson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annette Dobson"}

**Annette Jane Dobson** AM (born 4 September 1945) is an Australian statistician. She is a Professor of [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland)'s Australian Women and Girl's Health Research (AWaGHR) Centre in the School of Public Health.[^o3][^o4] Dobson was Director of the Australian Longitudinal Study on Women's Health from 1995 to 2013.[^o5] She is a highly cited publication author,[^o6] a book author,[^o7] and has received an Australia Day award.[^o8]

:::

## Qualification {#Qualification}

Dobson has a bachelor's degree from the [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide), and a master's degree and PhD from [James Cook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cook_University). She has a postgraduate diploma in management from the [University of New England (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29).[^o10]

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

Her research interests lie in the fields of biostatistics, [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), [longitudinal studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_studies), and [social determinants of health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_determinants_of_health). In biostatistics, she is specifically interested in [generalized linear modeling](wikipedia://en/747122), [clinical biostatistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_biostatistics), and statistical methods in [longitudinal studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_studies). Dobson's topics in epidemiology include [tobacco control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobacco_control), [diabetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabetes), [cardiovascular disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiovascular_disease), [obesity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obesity) and health care service use.

## Positions {#Positions}

Dobson is the founding Director of the Australian Longitudinal Study on Women's Health (ALSWH)[^o5] and was director of the Centre for Longitudinal and Life Course Research from 2012 - 2021.[^o13]

She was the inaugural chair of the [BCA Master of Biostatistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCA_Master_of_Biostatistics) at its inception in 2000.

## Awards {#Awards}

Dobson was made a Member of the Order of Australia in 2010 for her service to [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) and biostatistics as a research and academic, particularly through the collection and analysis of data relating to cardiovascular disease and women's and veterans' health, which provided a basis for public health interventions and policies to reduce [disease burden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease_burden) in the population.[^o15]

Dobson won the Sidney Sax medal in 2003, the pre-eminent prize awarded by the Public Health Association of Australia.[^o16] Dobson received the 2012 Moyal Medal for her contributions to statistics[^o17] and in 2015 she was elected Fellow of the [Australian Academy of Health and Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Health_and_Medical_Sciences) (FAHMS).[^o18]

She is also an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o19]

## An introduction to GLM {#An_introduction_to_GLM}

She wrote the book *An introduction to [generalized linear models](wikipedia://en/747122)*.[^o21]
