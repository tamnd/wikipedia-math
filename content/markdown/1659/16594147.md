# Monroe D. Donsker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monroe D. Donsker"}

**Monroe David Donsker** (October 17, 1924 – June 8, 1991) was an American mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) (NYU). His research interest was [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Donsker was born in [Burlington, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington,_Iowa). He received a Ph.D. in mathematics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1948 under the supervision of [Robert Horton Cameron](wikipedia://en/37645495). He became a professor at NYU's [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) in 1962, about a year before his frequent co-author [S.R.S. Varadhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.R.S._Varadhan) started working there. Before joining NYU, Donsker taught at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). His doctoral students include [Glen E. Baxter](wikipedia://en/21092449).

Donsker also served as chair of the [Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._William_Fulbright_Foreign_Scholarship_Board), a U.S. government panel responsible for [student exchange programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_exchange_program), after being appointed by presidents [Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Ford) and [Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter).[^o4]

In probability theory, Donsker is known for his proof of the [Donsker invariance principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donsker’s_theorem) which shows the convergence in distribution of a rescaled random walk to the [Wiener process](wikipedia://en/149984).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Donsker was married to Mary Davis (1923 – 2013), who was a watercolor artist with a degree in economics from [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o8]
