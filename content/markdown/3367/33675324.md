# Quality & Quantity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quality & Quantity"}

***Quality & Quantity*** is a [double-blind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-blind) [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) dealing with [methodological issues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology) in the fields of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and [sociology, mathematics, and statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology). The journal is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).

:::
