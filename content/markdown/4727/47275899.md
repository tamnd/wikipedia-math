# Francisco Santos Leal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francisco Santos Leal"}

Santos **Francisco "Paco" Santos Leal** (born 28 May 1968) is a Spanish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Cantabria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cantabria), known for finding a [counterexample](wikipedia://en/143151) to the [Hirsch conjecture](wikipedia://en/10412371) in [polyhedral combinatorics](wikipedia://en/19696519).[^o2][^o3][^o4] In 2015 he won the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) for this research.[^o5]

*Image: santos francisco.jpg.* Santos in 2012

:::

Santos was born in [Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valladolid), Spain.[^o6] He earned a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in mathematics from the University of Cantabria in 1991, and a master's degree in pure mathematics from [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble), France in the same year.[^o6] He returned to Cantabria for his doctorate, which he finished in 1995, with a thesis on the [combinatorial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry) of [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260) and [Delaunay triangulations](wikipedia://en/8864) supervised by Tomás Recio. He also has a second licenciate, in physics, from Cantabria in 1996. After postdoctoral studies at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) he returned to Cantabria as a faculty member in 1997, and was promoted to full professor in 2008. From 2009 to 2013 he has been vice-dean of the Faculty of Sciences at Cantabria.[^o6]

As well as being honored by the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) in 2015 for a counter-example of the [Hirsch conjecture](wikipedia://en/10412371),[^o2][^o5] he was an invited sectional speaker at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o6]

Santos is an Editor-in-Chief of the [Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics).[^o8]
