# Limit and colimit of presheaves

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Limit and colimit of presheaves"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, a **limit** or a **colimit** of [presheaves](wikipedia://en/4840944) on a category *C* is a [limit or colimit](wikipedia://en/39248) in the [functor category](wikipedia://en/407039) $\widehat{C} = \mathbf{Fct}(C^{\text{op}}, \mathbf{Set})$.[^o2]

:::

The category $\widehat{C}$ admits small limits and small colimits.[^o3] Explicitly, if $f: I \to \widehat{C}$ is a functor from a small category *I* and *U* is an object in *C*, then $\varinjlim_{i \in I} f(i)$ is computed pointwise:

$$(\varinjlim f(i))(U) = \varinjlim f(i)(U).$$

The same is true for small limits. Concretely this means that, for example, a [fiber product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_product) exists and is computed pointwise.

When *C* is small, by the [Yoneda lemma](wikipedia://en/34359), one can view *C* as a [full subcategory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_subcategory) of $\widehat{C}$. If $\eta: C \to D$ is a functor, if $f: I \to C$ is a functor from a small category *I* and if the colimit $\varinjlim f$ in $\widehat{C}$ is [representable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representable_presheaf); i.e., isomorphic to an object in *C*, then,[^o4] in *D*,

$$\eta(\varinjlim f) \simeq \varinjlim \eta \circ f$$

(in particular the colimit on the right exists in *D*). In other words, if the Yoneda embedding of *C* preserves the colimit of a functor into *C*, then every functor out of *C* preserves that colimit.

The [density theorem](wikipedia://en/54487799) states that every presheaf is a colimit of representable presheaves.
