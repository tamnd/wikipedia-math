# Stochastic Processes and Their Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stochastic Processes and Their Applications"}

***Stochastic Processes and Their Applications*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) for the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Eva Löcherbach. The principal focus of this journal is theory and applications of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes). It was established in 1973.

:::
