# Heun

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heun"}

**Heun** is a German surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Conor Heun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Heun) (born 1979), American mixed martial artist
- Carl Heun, (1771–1854), German author, better known by his pseudonym [Heinrich Clauren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Clauren)
- [Dirk Heun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Heun) (born 1953), German footballer
- [Dustin Heun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Heun) (born 1984), German footballer
- [Jürgen Heun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jürgen_Heun) (born 1958), East German footballer
- [Karl Heun](wikipedia://en/27382617) (1859–1929), German mathematician
- [Wilhelm Heun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Heun) (1895–1986), German Wehrmacht general
- John Heun, (born 1974), American Human Resources Executive
