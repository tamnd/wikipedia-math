# Humbert polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Humbert polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Humbert polynomials** π${}^{\lambda}_{n,m}$(*x*) are a generalization of [Pincherle polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pincherle_polynomials) introduced by Pierre Humbert[^o2] given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

:::

$$\displaystyle (1-mxt+t^m)^{-\lambda}=\sum^\infty _{n=0}\pi^\lambda_{n,m}(x)t^n$$

[^o3]
