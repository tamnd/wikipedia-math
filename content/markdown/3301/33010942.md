# Actuarial polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Actuarial polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **actuarial polynomials** *a*${}^{(\beta)}_{n}$(*x*) are polynomials given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

:::

$$\displaystyle \sum_n \frac{a_n^{(\beta)}(x)}{n!}t^n = \exp(\beta t +x(1-e^t))$$
