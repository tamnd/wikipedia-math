# Essentially finite vector bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Essentially finite vector bundle"}

In mathematics, an **essentially finite vector bundle** is a particular type of [vector bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_bundle_%28algebraic_geometry%29) defined by [Madhav V. Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482),[^o2][^o3] as the main tool in the construction of the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612). Even if the definition is not intuitive there is a nice characterization that makes essentially finite vector bundles quite natural objects to study in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). The following notion of *finite vector bundle* is due to [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) and will be needed to define essentially finite vector bundles:

:::

## Finite vector bundles {#Finite_vector_bundles}

Let $X$ be a scheme and $V$ a vector bundle on $X$. For $f = a_0 + a_1 x + \ldots + a_n x^n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}[x]$ an integral polynomial with nonnegative coefficients define

$$f(V) := \mathcal{O}_X^{\oplus a_0} \oplus V^{\oplus a_1} \oplus \left(V^{\otimes 2}\right)^{\oplus a_2} \oplus \ldots \oplus \left(V ^{\otimes n}\right)^{\oplus a_n}$$

Then $V$ is called **finite** if there are two distinct polynomials $f,g\in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}[x]$ for which $f(V)$ is isomorphic to $g(V)$.

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The following two definitions coincide whenever $X$ is a reduced, connected and proper scheme over a perfect field.

### According to Borne and [Vistoli](wikipedia://en/74721751) {#According_to_Borne_and_Vistoli}

A vector bundle is **essentially finite** if it is the kernel of a morphism $u:F_1\to F_2$ where $F_1, F_2$ are finite vector bundles. [^o8]

### The original definition of [Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482) {#The_original_definition_of_Nori}

A vector bundle is **essentially finite** if it is a [subquotient](wikipedia://en/1639294) of a finite vector bundle in the category of [Nori-semistable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nori-semistable) vector bundles.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Let $X$ be a reduced and connected [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) over a perfect [field](wikipedia://en/10603) $k$ endowed with a section $x\in X(k)$. Then a vector bundle $V$ over $X$ is essentially finite if and only if there exists a [finite](wikipedia://en/2139357) $k$-[group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) $G$ and a $G$-[torsor](wikipedia://en/41910974) $p:P\to X$ such that $V$ becomes trivial over $P$ (i.e. $p^*(V)\cong O_P^{\oplus r}$, where $r=rk(V)$).
- When $X$ is a reduced, connected and proper scheme over a perfect field with a point $x\in X(k)$ then the category $EF(X)$ of essentially finite vector bundles provided with the usual tensor product $\otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X}$, the trivial object $\mathcal{O}_X$ and the fiber functor $x^*$ is a [Tannakian category](wikipedia://en/7221237).
- The $k$-affine [group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) $\pi_1(X,x)$ naturally associated to the Tannakian category $EF(X)$ is called the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612).

:::
