# Herman Auerbach

Herman Auerbach

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herman Auerbach"}

**Herman Auerbach** (October 26, 1901, [Tarnopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarnopol) – August 17, 1942) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and member of the [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328).

:::

Auerbach was professor at [Lwów University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów_University). He is known for introducing the [Auerbach's lemma](wikipedia://en/26974141), a theorem in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). During the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War), because of his [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) descent, he was imprisoned by the [Germans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) in the [Lwów Ghetto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów_Ghetto). In 1942, he was murdered at [Bełżec extermination camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bełżec_extermination_camp).[^o2]
