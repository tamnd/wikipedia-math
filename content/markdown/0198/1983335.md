# Roman Sikorski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roman Sikorski"}

**Roman Sikorski** (July 11, 1920 – September 12, 1983) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sikorski was a professor at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) from 1952 until 1982. Since 1962, he was a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

Sikorski's research interests included: [Boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/3959), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the [theory of distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649), and [descriptive set theory](wikipedia://en/396619).

## Works {#Works}

- *Boolean Algebras* (1960)
- *Funkcje rzeczywiste* (t. 1–2 1958–59)
- *The Mathematics of Metamathematics* (1963, together with [Helena Rasiowa](wikipedia://en/3482432))
- *Rachunek rózniczkowy i całkowy — funkcje wielu zmiennych* (1967)
