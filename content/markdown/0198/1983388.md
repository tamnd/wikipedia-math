# Tadeusz Czeżowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tadeusz Czeżowski"}

**Tadeusz Czeżowski** (July 26, 1889 – March 28, 1981) was a Polish [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He is considered one of the most prominent members of the [Lviv-Warsaw School](wikipedia://en/1426957).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Czeżowski was born in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) on July 26, 1889.[^o5][^o6] He was the son of a high-ranking state official a previous Prefect who later became the Counsellor of the Governorate of Galicia.[^o3] In 1907, he studied philosophy, mathematics, and physics at the [University of Lviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lviv).[^o3][^o6] His mother, Helena Kusche, was considered part of the *petite bourgeoisie* of the city.[^o6] He became a student of [Kazimierz Twardowski](wikipedia://en/1022980), who conferred to Czeżowski his doctorate in philosophy in 1914 after completing his dissertation entitled, *Teoria klas* (Theory of Classes).[^o3] He also became a member of the [Lwów–Warsaw School of Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów–Warsaw_School_of_Logic).

After qualifying as mathematics and physics teacher, he started teaching in a Lviv grammar school in 1912.[^o6] From 1923 to 1939 he was a professor at [Stefan Batory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Batory_University) in [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius), [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania), and from 1945 to 1960 a professor at [Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus_University_in_Toruń), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland). In 1948 he became editor of the magazine *Ruch Filozoficzny*. He died in [Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toruń) in 1981.[^o6]

He was named a member of the [Righteous Among the Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Righteous_Among_the_Nations) in 1963.[^o7]

## Works {#Works}

- *O metafizyce, jej kierunkach i zagadnieniach* (On Metaphysics, its directions and problems) (1948)
- *Logika* (1949)
- *Odczyty filozoficzne* (1958 and 1969)
- *Filozofia na rozdrożu* (1966)
- *Pisma z etyki i teorii wartości* (1989)
- *Knowledge, Science, and Values. A Program for Scientific Philosophy* Edited by Leon Gumanski, Amsterdam, Rodopi, 2000
