# Maggie Cheng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maggie Cheng"}

**Maggie Xiaoyan Cheng** is an [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) who works as a professor of applied mathematics at the [Illinois Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Institute_of_Technology), where she directs the Center for Interdisciplinary Scientific Computation. Her research interests include [cyber security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_security) and [Machine Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_Learning).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cheng has a bachelor's and master's degree from the [Beijing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beihang_University).[^o5] She completed a Ph.D. in computer science in 2003 from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o6]

After completing her doctorate, she became an assistant professor of computer science at the [Missouri University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_University_of_Science_and_Technology). She moved to the Martin Tuchman School of Management of the [New Jersey Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_Institute_of_Technology) in 2016, and moved to the Illinois Institute of Technology in 2018,[^o7] and she was promoted to full professor in 2020.
