# Hao Huang (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hao Huang"}

**Hao Huang** () is a Chinese mathematician known for solving the [sensitivity conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensitivity_conjecture).[^o2][^o3] Huang is currently an associate professor in the mathematics department at [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880).[^o4]

:::

Huang received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2007.[^o4] He obtained his Ph.D. in mathematics from his dissertation titled *Various Problems in Extremal Combinatorics* from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA) in 2012 advised by [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803).[^o5] His postdoctoral research was done at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) and [DIMACS](wikipedia://en/3684858) at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in 2012–2014, followed by a year at the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088) at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Huang then became an assistant professor from 2015 to 2021 in the Department of Mathematics at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University).[^o4]

In July 2019, Huang announced a breakthrough, which gave a proof of the sensitivity conjecture.[^o6] At that point the conjecture had been open for nearly 30 years, having been posed by [Noam Nisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Nisan) and [Mario Szegedy](wikipedia://en/4291061) in 1992.[^o7] Huang has received positive attention for his discovery, as theoretical computer scientist [Scott Aaronson](wikipedia://en/20420243) described, "I find it hard to imagine that even God knows how to prove the Sensitivity Conjecture in any simpler way than this."[^o8]

Huang received an [NSF Career Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) in 2019[^o9] and a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2020.[^o10]
