# Siegfried Bosch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegfried Bosch"}

**Siegfried Bosch** is a German mathematician working in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), focusing in particular on nonarchimedean [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202).

:::

He completed his Ph.D. in 1967 at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) with a dissertation entitled *Endliche analytische Homomorphismen* (Finite analytic homomorphisms), and received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) degree in 1972. Since 1974 he has been a professor at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

Bosch is the author of several books in algebra and geometry.
