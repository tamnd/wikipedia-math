# Minnesota Borderline Personality Disorder Scale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minnesota Borderline Personality Disorder Scale"}

The **Minnesota Borderline Personality Disorder Scale** (**MBPD**) is a measure of [borderline personality disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderline_personality_disorder) traits. The scale was created in 2011 by and uses items from the [Multidimensional Personality Questionnaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multidimensional_Personality_Questionnaire), an instrument commonly included in large [longitudinal](wikipedia://en/1557623) [data sets](wikipedia://en/8495), so that such past studies can be reanalyzed to study borderline personality disorder.[^o2][^o3]

:::
