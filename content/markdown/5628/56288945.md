# Ilijas Farah

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ilijas Farah"}

*Image: Portrait Summer 2020..jpg.* Ilijas Farah in 2020

**Ilijas Farah** (born 18 February 1966) is a Canadian-Serbian[^o2] mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University) in Toronto and at the Mathematical Institute of Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts, [Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade), Serbia.[^o3][^o4] His research focuses on applications of logic to operator algebras.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Farah was born in [Sremska Mitrovica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sremska_Mitrovica), [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia). He received his BSc and MSc in 1988 and 1992 respectively from [Belgrade University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade_University) and his PhD in 1997 from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o7][^o12]

He is a Research Chair in Logic and Operator Algebras at [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University), Toronto. Before moving to York University he was an NSERC Postdoctoral Fellow, York University (1997–99), a Hill Assistant Professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) (1999–2000), and a professor at CUNY–Graduate center and [College of Staten Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Staten_Island) (2000–02).[^o8]

Farah was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul) 2014, section on Logic and Foundations, where he presented his work on applications of logic to operator algebras.[^o9][^o10]

## Awards, distinctions, and recognitions {#Awards,_distinctions,_and_recognitions}

- Sacks prize for the best doctorate in Mathematical Logic, 1997[^o12]
- Governor General's gold medal for one of the two best doctorates at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), 1998
- The Canadian Association for Graduate Studies/University Microfilms International Distinguished Dissertation Award, for the best dissertation in engineering, medicine and the natural sciences in Canada, 1998.
- Dean's award for outstanding research, [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University), 2006.[^o8]
- Faculty Excellence in Research Award (Established Research Award), Faculty of Science, York University, 2017[^o13]
