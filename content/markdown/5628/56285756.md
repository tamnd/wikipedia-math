# Aleksander Pełczyński

*Image: Aleksander Pełczyński grób.JPG.* Pełczyński's grave in Warsaw

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksander Pełczyński"}

**Aleksander "Olek" Pełczyński** (2 July 1932, [Tarnopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternopil), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic) – 20 December 2012, [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław)) was a Polish mathematician who worked in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Pełczyński studied mathematics from 1950 to 1956 at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) and received there his doctorate in 1958 under [Stanisław Mazur](wikipedia://en/1023162) with dissertation *Własności izomorficzne przestrzeni Banacha związane ze słabą zbieżnością bezwarunkową szeregów* (Isomorphic properties of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) with regard to unconditional convergence of series). From 1967 to 2002 he worked at the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

From 1967 onwards, he was a member of the editorial staff of the journal *[Studia Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studia_Mathematica)*.

His doctoral students include [Nicole Tomczak-Jaegermann](wikipedia://en/29881866) and [Stanisław Szarek](wikipedia://en/45052273).

He died in December 2012[^o6] and was buried in Warsaw.

## Research {#Research}

Pełczyński's main field of research was [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), especially the theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989). The [Bessaga–Pełczyński selection principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessaga–Pełczyński_selection_principle) and the [Pełczyński decomposition method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pełczyński_decomposition_method) are associated with his name.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Pełczyński received the [Stefan Banach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Banach_Prize) in 1961. In 1986, he was elected a member of the [Akademie der Wissenschaften der DDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademie_der_Wissenschaften_der_DDR). He received the [Stefan Banach Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Banach_Medal) of the Polish Academy of Sciences in 1996. In 2005, he was granted an honorary doctorate from the [Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University_in_Poznań).

In 1966, Pełczyński was (with [Boris Mityagin](wikipedia://en/56279030)) an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Moscow.[^o9] In 1983, he was a plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Warsaw and gave the talk *Structural Theory of Banach Spaces and Its Interplay with Analysis and Probability*.
