# Juan de Alcega

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juan de Alcega"}

*Image: Juan de Alcega.png.* Three pages of *Geometria, Practica y Traça* (1580)

**Juan de Alcega** was a 16th-century hidalgo and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from [Basque country, Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Basque_Country).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Little is known about the life of Juan de Alcega. He was born in the province of [Gipuzkoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gipuzkoa), probably in the town of [Hondarribia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hondarribia).[^o4]

In 1580, he published in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) the book entitled *Libro de Geometría, practica, y traça (Book on Geometry, Practice, and Pattern)*, reedited in 1589. This book is a clear example of the application of mathematics to technology in its first modern states.[^o5] It was also the subject of studies on [philology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philology) by his terminology[^o6] and by his exposition of the mode of his times.[^o7][^o8] In the introduction to his book, he states that he had some difficulties publishing it due to the resistance of the medieval guild of tailors, who thought that he was revealing the secrets of his art.[^o9]
