# Isabelle Gallagher

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Isabelle Gallagher"}

**Isabelle Gallagher** (born 27 October 1973) is a French mathematician. Her research concerns [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) such as the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395), the [wave equation](wikipedia://en/33691), and the [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874), as well as [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) of the [Heisenberg group](wikipedia://en/448518).[^o3][^o8]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gallagher was born on 27 October 1973, in [Cagnes-sur-Mer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagnes-sur-Mer).[^o5] She earned her PhD from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1998. Her dissertation, supervised by [Jean-Yves Chemin](wikipedia://en/68418903), concerned [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034).

She worked at the French [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) and then, in 2004, became a professor at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University).[^o3]

She was elected president of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205) in June 2024.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2008, the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) awarded her the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602).[^o8]

She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o9]

She won the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 2016.[^o10]

She was awarded the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384) in 2018[^o11] by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).
