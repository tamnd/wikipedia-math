# Extranatural transformation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Extranatural transformation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), an **extranatural transformation**[^o2] is a generalization of the notion of [natural transformation](wikipedia://en/45022).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $F:A\times B^\mathrm{op}\times B\rightarrow D$ and $G:A\times C^\mathrm{op}\times C\rightarrow D$ be two [functors](wikipedia://en/10987) of categories. A family $\eta (a,b,c):F(a,b,b)\rightarrow G(a,c,c)$ is said to be **natural in** *a* **and extranatural in** *b* **and** *c* if the following holds:

- $\eta(-,b,c)$ is a natural transformation (in the usual sense).
- (extranaturality in *b*) $\forall (g:b\rightarrow b^\prime)\in \mathrm{Mor}\, B$, $\forall a\in A$, $\forall c\in C$ the following [diagram commutes](wikipedia://en/138484)

$$\begin{matrix} F(a,b',b) & \xrightarrow{F(1,1,g)} & F(a,b',b') \\ _{F(1,g,1)}\downarrow\qquad & & _{\eta(a,b',c)}\downarrow\qquad \\ F(a,b,b) & \xrightarrow{\eta(a,b,c)} & G(a,c,c) \end{matrix}$$

- (extranaturality in *c*) $\forall (h:c\rightarrow c^\prime)\in \mathrm{Mor}\, C$, $\forall a\in A$, $\forall b\in B$ the following diagram commutes

$$\begin{matrix} F(a,b,b) & \xrightarrow{\eta(a,b,c')} & G(a,c',c') \\ _{\eta(a,b,c)}\downarrow\qquad & & _{G(1,h,1)}\downarrow\qquad \\ G(a,c,c) & \xrightarrow{G(1,1,h)} & G(a,c,c') \end{matrix}$$

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Extranatural transformations can be used to define wedges and thereby [ends](wikipedia://en/3865669)[^o7] (dually co-wedges and co-ends), by setting $F$ (dually $G$) constant.

Extranatural transformations can be defined in terms of [dinatural transformations](wikipedia://en/3865755), of which they are a special case.[^o7]

:::
