# Vexillary permutation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vexillary permutation"}

In mathematics, a **vexillary permutation** is a [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027) *μ* of the positive integers containing no [subpermutation](wikipedia://en/24360911) isomorphic to the permutation (2143); in other words, there do not exist four numbers *i* \< *j* \< *k* \< *l* with *μ*(*j*) \< *μ*(*i*) \< *μ*(*l*) \< *μ*(*k*). They were introduced by (txt). The word "vexillary" means flag-like, and comes from the fact that vexillary permutations are related to [flags](wikipedia://en/390646) of [modules](wikipedia://en/276410).

:::

(Guibert Pergola Pinzani 2001) showed that vexillary [involutions](wikipedia://en/293450) are enumerated by [Motzkin numbers](wikipedia://en/853141).
