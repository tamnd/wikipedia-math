# Burr distribution

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Burr distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390), the **Burr Type XII distribution** or simply the **Burr distribution**[^o3] is a [continuous probability distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_probability_distribution) for a non-negative [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685). It is also known as the **Singh–Maddala distribution**[^o4] and is one of a number of different distributions sometimes called the "generalized [log-logistic distribution](wikipedia://en/15657106)".

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

### Probability density function {#Probability_density_function}

The Burr (Type XII) distribution has [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487):[^o8][^o9]

$$\begin{aligned} f(x;c,k) & = ck\frac{x^{c-1}}{(1+x^c)^{k+1}} \\[6pt] f(x;c,k,\lambda) & = \frac{ck}{\lambda} \left( \frac{x}{\lambda} \right)^{c-1} \left[1 + \left(\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)^c\right]^{-k-1} \end{aligned}$$

The $\lambda$ parameter scales the underlying variate and is a positive real.

### Cumulative distribution function {#Cumulative_distribution_function}

The [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) is:

$$F(x;c,k) = 1-\left(1+x^c\right)^{-k}$$

$$F(x;c,k,\lambda) = 1 - \left[1 + \left(\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)^c \right]^{-k}$$

:::

::: {#o12 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

It is most commonly used to model [household income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_income), see for example: [Household income in the U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_income_in_the_United_States#Income_distribution) and compare to [magenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magenta) graph at right.

:::

## Random variate generation {#Random_variate_generation}

Given a random variable $U$ drawn from the [uniform distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_distribution_%28continuous%29) in the interval $\left(0, 1\right)$, the random variable

$$X=\lambda \left (\frac{1}{\sqrt[k]{1-U}}-1 \right )^{1/c}$$

has a Burr Type XII distribution with parameters $c$, $k$ and $\lambda$. This follows from the inverse cumulative distribution function given above.

## Related distributions {#Related_distributions}

- When *c* = 1, the Burr distribution becomes the [Lomax distribution](wikipedia://en/32253318).
- When *k* = 1, the Burr distribution is a [log-logistic distribution](wikipedia://en/15657106) sometimes referred to as the [Fisk distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisk_distribution), a special case of the [Champernowne distribution](wikipedia://en/36020629).[^o15][^o16]
- The Burr Type XII distribution is a member of a system of continuous distributions introduced by [Irving W. Burr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_W._Burr) (1942), which comprises 12 distributions.[^o17]
- The [Dagum distribution](wikipedia://en/31102537), also known as the inverse Burr distribution, is the distribution of 1 / *X*, where *X* has the Burr distribution
