# F. Reese Harvey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="F. Reese Harvey"}

**Frank Reese Harvey** (born 1941)[^o3] is an American mathematician known for contributions to the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). In 1982, in collaboration with [Blaine Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaine_Lawson), Harvey introduced [calibrated geometry](wikipedia://en/19364294), which is instrumental in the formulation of the [SYZ conjecture](wikipedia://en/17511256).[^o4] He is Edgar Odell Lovett Professor and professor emeritus of mathematics at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University),[^o11]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Harvey graduated from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) in 1963 with both a bachelor's and master's degree.[^o6] He obtained his Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1966, under the direction of Hikosaburo Komatsu.

He joined the Rice University faculty in 1968, and retired as Edgar Odell Lovett Professor in 2003.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1983 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Warsaw.[^o10] In 2024, he was elected to the United States [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o11]
