# C. Robin Graham

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C. Robin Graham"}

**Charles Robin Graham** is professor emeritus of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), known for a number of contributions to the field of [conformal geometry](wikipedia://en/295907) and [CR geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CR_geometry); his collaboration with [Charles Fefferman](wikipedia://en/502831) on the [ambient construction](wikipedia://en/5784666) has been particularly widely cited. The [GJMS operators](wikipedia://en/25525269) are, in part, named for him.[^o2] He is a 2012 Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3] Graham received his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1981, under the direction of [Elias Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein).

:::
