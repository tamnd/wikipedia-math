# Elena Celledoni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elena Celledoni"}

**Elena Celledoni** (born 1967) is an Italian mathematician who works in Norway as a professor of mathematical sciences at the [Norwegian University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_and_Technology) (NTNU). Her research involves the [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [numerical algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_algorithm) for [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and for [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) computations, including the study of structure preserving algorithms.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Celledoni earned a master's degree at the [University of Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trieste) in 1993. She completed a Ph.D. at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) in 1997. Her dissertation, *Krylov Subspace Methods For Linear Systems Of ODEs*, was jointly supervised by Igor Moret and Alfredo Bellen.

Before becoming a faculty member at NTNU in 2004, she was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and at NTNU.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Celledoni is a member of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters).
