# Jian Ding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jian Ding"}

**Jian Ding** () is a Chinese mathematician and probability theorist. He is a chair professor at the School of Mathematical Sciences of [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), where he also serves as vice dean.[^o2] In 2023 he was awarded the [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ding entered Peking University in 2002 and received his bachelor's degree in mathematics in 2006.[^o6] He earned his PhD in statistics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2011, under the supervision of [Yuval Peres](wikipedia://en/36477942).[^o4][^o7]

After postdoctoral positions at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and as a Szegö Assistant Professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), Ding joined the faculty of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he became an associate professor in the Department of Statistics. He then moved to the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), where he was the Gilbert Helman Professor in the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School). In January 2022 he returned to Peking University as a chair professor.[^o4][^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Ding's research is in probability theory, in particular its interactions with [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392). His work has covered random walks, Gaussian processes, random constraint satisfaction problems, random planar geometry, spin models, and Anderson localization.[^o3][^o9] With Allan Sly and Nike Sun, he proved the satisfiability threshold conjecture for random [k-SAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-SAT) for large k.[^o10][^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [Alfred P. Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) (2015)[^o6]
- NSF CAREER Award (2015)[^o3]
- [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) (2017, shared with Nike Sun)[^o10][^o11]
- Invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (2022, joint lecture with Julien Dubédat and Ewain Gwynne)[^o13]
- Gold Medal of Mathematics of the International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians (2022)[^o14]
- [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize) (2023)[^o3][^o9]
- IMS Medallion Lecture (2023)[^o15]
- Plenary speaker at the International Congress on Mathematical Physics (2024)[^o6]
- Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2025)[^o16]
