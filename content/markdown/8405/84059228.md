# Applied Numerical Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Applied Numerical Mathematics"}

***Applied Numerical Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published monthly by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) on behalf of [International Association for Mathematics and Computers in Simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_for_Mathematics_and_Computers_in_Simulation). Established in 2001, it covers [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833) as well as its applications in physics and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering).[^o3] Its current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are Zdzislaw Jackiewicz ([Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University)) and Thiab Taha ([University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia)).[^o4]

:::
