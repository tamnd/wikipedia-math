# Richard Weber (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Weber"}

**Richard Robert Weber** (born 25 February 1953) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [operational research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_research).[^o4] He is [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) [Churchill Professor of Mathematics for Operational Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_Professor_of_Mathematics_of_Information) in the [Statistical Laboratory, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge). He was admitted to [The Magic Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magic_Circle_%28organisation%29) in 2025.

:::

Weber was educated at [Walnut Hills High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Hills_High_School), [Solihull School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solihull_School) and [Downing College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downing_College,_Cambridge). He graduated in 1974, and completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1980 under the supervision of Peter Nash. He has been on the faculty of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) since 1978, and a fellow of [Queens' College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge) since 1977 where he has been Vice President from 1996–2007 and again from 2018–2020. He was appointed [Churchill Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_Professor_of_Mathematics_of_Information) in 1994, and he became [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) Churchill Professor on retirement in 2017. He was Director of the [Statistical Laboratory](wikipedia://en/3254271) from 1999 to 2009, and was a trustee of the [Rollo Davidson Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize).[^o6]

He works on the mathematics of large [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_systems) subject to [uncertainty](wikipedia://en/63778). He has made contributions to stochastic scheduling, [Markov decision processes](wikipedia://en/1125883), [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), the [probabilistic analysis of algorithms](wikipedia://en/15383889), the theory of communications pricing and control, and [rendezvous search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendezvous_problem).

Weber and his co-authors were awarded the 2007 [INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) prize for their paper on the online [bin packing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bin_packing) algorithm.[^o7]
