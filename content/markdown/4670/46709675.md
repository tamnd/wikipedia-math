# Nikhil Srivastava

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Nikhil Srivastava"}

**Nikhil Srivastava** is an associate professor of Mathematics at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In July 2014, he was named a recipient of the [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize) with [Adam Marcus](wikipedia://en/39832362) and [Daniel Spielman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Spielman).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Nikhil Srivastava was born New Delhi, India. He attended [Union College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_College) in Schenectady, New York, graduating summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science degree in mathematics and computer science in 2005. He received a PhD in computer science from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 2010 (his dissertation was called "Spectral Sparsification and Restricted Invertibility").

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2013, together with [Adam Marcus](wikipedia://en/39832362) and [Daniel Spielman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Spielman), he provided a positive solution to the [Kadison–Singer problem](wikipedia://en/48769660),[^o8][^o9] a result that was awarded the 2014 [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize).

He gave an [invited lecture at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o10] He jointly won the 2021 Michael and Sheila Held Prize along with two others for solving long-standing questions on the Kadison-Singer problem and on Ramanujan graphs.<https://web.archive.org/web/20210201143140/https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/diaspora/indian-mathematician-nikhil-srivastava-wins-michael-and-sheila-held-prize-202075>

In 2022 The Ciprian Foias Prize in Operator Theory was awarded to Adam Marcus, Daniel Spielman, and Srivastava for their notable work in Operator Theory that was published in the preceding six years.[^o11][^o12]
