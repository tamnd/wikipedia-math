# SECG

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SECG"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), the **Standards for Efficient Cryptography Group (SECG)** is an international consortium founded by [Certicom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certicom) in 1998. The group exists to develop commercial [standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardization) for efficient and interoperable cryptography based on [elliptic curve cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_curve_cryptography) (ECC).

:::
