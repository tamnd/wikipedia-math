# Herman te Riele

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herman te Riele"}

te Riele **Hermanus Johannes Joseph te Riele** (born 5 January 1947) is a Dutch mathematician at [CWI](wikipedia://en/506063) in Amsterdam with a specialization in [computational number theory](wikipedia://en/511466). He is known for proving the correctness of the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125) for the first 1.5 billion non-trivial zeros of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) with Jan van de Lune and Dik Winter, for disproving the [Mertens conjecture](wikipedia://en/379847) with [Andrew Odlyzko](wikipedia://en/1986042), and for factoring large numbers of world record size. In 1987, he found a new upper bound for [π(*x*)](wikipedia://en/331826) − [Li(*x*)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_integral).

:::

In 1970, te Riele received an [engineer's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer's_degree) in mathematical engineering from [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology) and, in 1976, a PhD degree in mathematics and physics from [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) (1976).
