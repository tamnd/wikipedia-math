# Daniel Kressner

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Kressner"}

**Daniel Kressner** (born 7 April 1978) is a German [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506). He has a Chair of Numerical Algorithms and High Performance Computing in the Institute of Mathematics at [EPF Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPF_Lausanne).[^o2][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kressner was born in [Karl-Marx-Stadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Marx-Stadt). He studied Mathematics at [TU Chemnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Chemnitz) from 1997 to 2001 and gained his PhD from [TU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Berlin) in 2004. His PhD thesis ("Numerical Methods and Software for General and Structured Eigenvalue Problems") was supervised by [Volker Mehrmann](wikipedia://en/61754880). He was appointed assistant professor in Applied Mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 2007. In 2011, he was appointed tenure-track assistant professor in Mathematics at EPF Lausanne, where he became associate professor in 2012 and full professor in 2017. Kressner held visiting positions as an [Emmy Noether Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Noether_Program) of the [DFG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) at the [University of Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zagreb) in 2005 and [Umeå University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umeå_University) in 2006. In 2018, he was the John von Neumann visiting professor at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).[^o2]

Kressner has been the Editor-in-Chief of [ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Transactions_on_Mathematical_Software) in 2017, and he is on the editorial boards of journals including the SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and its Applications, [SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/31965557), and [Linear Algebra and Its Applications](wikipedia://en/42068102).

## Research {#Research}

Kressner is best known for his work on numerical methods, in particular for linear eigenvalue problems,[^o9] nonlinear eigenvalue problems, and low-rank approximation techniques for matrix problems.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He has been awarded a second [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214) from the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) in 2007. In 2011 he received the John Todd Award from the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach).[^o2]

He was elected as a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376), in the 2022 Class of SIAM Fellows, "for contributions in numerical linear and multilinear algebra and scientific computing".[^o11]
