# Chen–Gackstatter surface

*Image: Chen-Gackstatter-Thayer surfaces.png.* The first nine Chen–Gackstatter surfaces.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chen–Gackstatter surface"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **Chen–Gackstatter surface family** (or the **Chen–Gackstatter–Thayer** surface family) is a family of [minimal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_surfaces) that generalize the [Enneper surface](wikipedia://en/4688830) by adding handles, giving it nonzero [topological genus](wikipedia://en/61891).[^o2][^o3]

:::

They are not [embedded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_embedding), and have Enneper-like [ends](wikipedia://en/6862105). The members $M_{ij}$ of the family are indexed by the number of extra handles *i* and the winding number of the Enneper end; the total genus is *ij* and the total [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) is $-4\pi(i+1)j$. It has been shown that $M_{11}$ is the only genus one orientable complete minimal surface of total curvature $-8\pi$.[^o5]

It has been conjectured that continuing to add handles to the surfaces will in the limit converge to the [Scherk's second surface](wikipedia://en/8235776#Scherk.27s_second_surface) (for *j* = 1) or the [saddle tower](wikipedia://en/36763480) family for *j* > 1.[^o3]
