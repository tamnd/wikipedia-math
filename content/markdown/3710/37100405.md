# Triakis truncated tetrahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Triakis truncated tetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triakis truncated tetrahedron** is a [convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) made from 4 hexagons and 12 [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428). It can be used to [tessellate](wikipedia://en/321671) three-dimensional space, making the [triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/37070120).[^o3][^o4]

:::

The triakis truncated tetrahedron is the shape of the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of the [carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon) atoms in [diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond), which lie on the [diamond cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_cubic) crystal structure.[^o5][^o6] As the Voronoi cell of a symmetric space pattern, it is a [plesiohedron](wikipedia://en/53396858).[^o7]

::: {#o9 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

*Image: Triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb.jpg.* Triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb

For space-filling, the triakis truncated tetrahedron can be constructed as follows:

1. Truncate a regular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) such that the big faces are regular hexagons.
1. Add an extra vertex at the center of each of the four smaller tetrahedra that were removed.
