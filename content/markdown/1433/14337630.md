# May spectral sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="May spectral sequence"}

In mathematics, the **May spectral sequence** is a [spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/803894), introduced by (). It is used for calculating the initial term of the [Adams spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/4720689), which is in turn used for calculating the [stable homotopy groups of spheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_groups_of_spheres). The May spectral sequence is described in detail in (Ravenel 2003, 67–74).

:::
