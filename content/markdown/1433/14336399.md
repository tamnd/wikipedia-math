# Chromatic spectral sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chromatic spectral sequence"}

In mathematics, the **chromatic spectral sequence** is a [spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/803894), introduced by (Ravenel 1978), used for calculating the initial term of the [Adams spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/4720689) for [Brown–Peterson cohomology](wikipedia://en/13698492), which is in turn used for calculating the [stable homotopy groups of spheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_groups_of_spheres).

:::
