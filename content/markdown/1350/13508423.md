# Pan-African Congress of Mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pan-African Congress of Mathematicians"}

The **Pan-African Congress of Mathematicians** (**PACOM**) is an international congress of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), held under the auspices of the [African Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/1764260).

:::

## List of congresses {#List_of_congresses}

- 2022 – [Brazzaville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazzaville), Republic of Congo
- 2017 – [Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabat), Morocco
- 2013 – [Abuja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abuja), Nigeria
- 2009 – [Yamoussoukro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamoussoukro), Ivory Coast
- 2004 – [Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis), Tunisia
- 2000 – [Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Town), South Africa
- 1995 – [Ifrane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ifrane), Morocco
- 1991 – [Nairobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairobi), Kenya
- 1986 – [Jos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos), Nigeria
- 1976 – [Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabat), Morocco
