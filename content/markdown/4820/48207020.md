# Alexander Christison

*Image: Alexander Christison, 1753 - 1820. Professor of Humanity, University of Edinburgh.jpg.* Alexander Christison.

*Image: The grave of Alexander Christison, Greyfriars Kirkyard.JPG.* The grave of Alexander Christison, Greyfriars Kirkyard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Christison"}

**Alexander Christison** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (1751–1820) was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) educator, [Professor of Humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_Classics_%28Edinburgh%29) at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), and mathematician during the [Scottish Enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Enlightenment).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in 1753, at Redpath House, [Longformacus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longformacus), [Berwickshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwickshire). He was the eldest of seven children to a tenant sheep-farmer in the [Lammermuir Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lammermuir_Hills).[^o3]

After a local education he began employment as the local schoolteacher for the parish of [Edrom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edrom) before attending the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) to study Classics, graduating in 1775. This background gave him access to teach at a higher calibre of school and he taught both at [George Watson’s College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson’s_College), [Dalkeith Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalkeith_Grammar_School) and the [High School in Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_High_School,_Edinburgh).[^o4] In the 1780s he lived at Alexander's Land in the Bristo area.[^o5]

He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1800 his main proposer being the physician, [James Gregory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gregory_%28physician%29). He trained under [John Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hill_%28classicist%29) at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and graduated MA in 1806, and from that date he served as professor of humanity at the university.[^o6]

He died in Edinburgh on 25 June 1820 and is buried in [Greyfriars Kirkyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greyfriars_Kirkyard) in the city centre. He is buried in the plot of [Professor George Dunbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Dunbar_%28classical_scholar%29) at the north-west section of the western extension. He is also memorialised on [Robert Christison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Christison)'s grave at New Calton.

## Family {#Family}

Christison's son was Scottish toxicologist [Robert Christison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Christison) and Scottish minister (another) Alexander Chistison. His grandchildren included [Sir Alexander Christison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Alexander_Christison).[^o8] and the Australian explorer and pastoralist [Robert Christison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Christison_%28pastoralist%29).[^o9]

## Publications {#Publications}

- *On the General Diffusion of Knowledge* (1802) in which he said "genius is no respecter of ranks"
