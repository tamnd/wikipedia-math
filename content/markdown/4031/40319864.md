::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Square tiling honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **square tiling honeycomb** is one of 11 paracompact regular honeycombs. It is called *paracompact* because it has infinite [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29), whose vertices exist on [horospheres](wikipedia://en/13487128) and converge to a single [ideal point](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. Given by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,4,3}, it has three [square tilings](wikipedia://en/2863809), {4,4}, around each edge, and six square tilings around each vertex, in a [cubic](wikipedia://en/6285) {4,3} [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).[^o9]

:::

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The square tiling honeycomb is a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space. It is one of eleven regular paracompact honeycombs.

There are [fifteen uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb#.5B4.2C4.2C3.5D_family) in the \[4,4,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form, and its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope), the [order-4 octahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40319878), {3,4,4}.

The square tiling honeycomb is part of the [order-4 square tiling honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40319872) family, as it can be seen as a rectified order-4 square tiling honeycomb.

It is related to the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), {3,4,3}, which also has a cubic vertex figure. It is also part of a sequence of honeycombs with [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) cells:
