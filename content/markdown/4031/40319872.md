::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Order-4 square tiling honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-4 square tiling honeycomb** is one of 11 paracompact regular honeycombs. It is *paracompact* because it has infinite [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. Given by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,4,4}, it has four [square tilings](wikipedia://en/2863809) around each edge, and infinite square tilings around each vertex in a [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).[^o9]

:::
