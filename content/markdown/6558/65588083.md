# Michael Ortiz (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Ortiz"}

**Miguel Ortiz** (born August 6, 1954) is a dual [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaniards)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) scientist and researcher in the fields of [structural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_mechanics), [continuum](wikipedia://en/5918) and [computational mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_mechanics) and is a *[Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_E._Marble) and Ora Lee Marble Professor Emeritus* of [Aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautics) and [Mechanical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_Engineering) at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). Prior to his arrival at Caltech in 1995, he was Professor of Engineering at Brown University.

:::

Since 2020 he is a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) in the Engineering and Applied Science Division at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), also holding positions as Research Chair in the Institute of Applied Mathematics, [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn)[^o4] and as adjunct professor and Timoshenko Distinguished Fellow in the departments of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics and Astronautics of Stanford University.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Rodney Hill](wikipedia://en/20573612) Prize, International Union of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics (IUTAM) 2008.
- [Timoshenko Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoshenko_Medal), American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), 2015.[^o6]
- [Doctor Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Honoris_Causa), Universidad Politecnica de Madrid, Spain, 2019.
- [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) Medal, US Association for Computational Mechanics (USACM), 2019.[^o7]

## Academy Membership {#Academy_Membership}

- 2013 Elected Member of the U.S. National Academy of Engineering (NAE).
- 2007 Elected Fellow of the American Academy of Arts & Sciences (AAAS).
- 1999 Corresponding Member, Spanish Academy of Engineering.
