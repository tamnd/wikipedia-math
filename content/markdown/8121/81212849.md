# Cubic fourfold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic fourfold"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **cubic fourfold** is a [degree](wikipedia://en/2776558) 3 [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) of dimension 4 in 5-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). Although some cubic fourfolds are known to be [rational](wikipedia://en/865939), it is known that very general cubic fourfolds are not rational, as proven by [Katzarkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludmil_Katzarkov), [Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509), [Pantev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Pantev) and [Yu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Yue_Yu).

:::

Using [geometric invariant theory (GIT)](wikipedia://en/1639003), [Radu Laza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radu_Laza) constructed a compactification of cubic fourfolds with [ADE singularities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADE_singularities) (including all smooth cubic fourfolds). He further showed that this compactification is isomorphic, via the [period map](wikipedia://en/6010542), to [Looijenga](wikipedia://en/45224498)'s compactification of the complement of certain arrangement of hyperplanes in the period space. [Yuchen Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuchen_Liu_%28mathematician%29) further showed that the GIT compactification is isomorphic to the K-moduli of cubic fourfolds.
