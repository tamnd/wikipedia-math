# Maximum-minimums identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maximum-minimums identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **maximum-minimums identity** is a relation between the maximum element of a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) *S* of *n* numbers and the minima of the 2<sup>*n*</sup> − 1 [non-empty](wikipedia://en/9566) [subsets](wikipedia://en/27631) of *S*.

:::

Let *S* = {*x*<sub>1</sub>, *x*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *x*<sub>*n*</sub>}. The [identity](wikipedia://en/298428) states that

$$\begin{aligned} \max\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{n}\} & = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i - \sum_{i<j}\min\{x_i,x_j\} +\sum_{i<j<k}\min\{x_i,x_j,x_k\} - \cdots \\ & \qquad \cdots + \left(-1\right)^{n+1}\min\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n\},\end{aligned}$$

or conversely

$$\begin{aligned} \min\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{n}\} & = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i - \sum_{i<j}\max\{x_i,x_j\} +\sum_{i<j<k}\max\{x_i,x_j,x_k\} - \cdots \\ & \qquad \cdots + \left(-1\right)^{n+1}\max\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n\}. \end{aligned}$$

For a probabilistic proof, see the reference.
