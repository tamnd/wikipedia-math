# Ars Mathematica Contemporanea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ars Mathematica Contemporanea"}

***Ars Mathematica Contemporanea*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) in connection with other branches of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It is published by the [University of Primorska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Primorska) together with the [Society of Mathematicians, Physicists and Astronomers of Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Mathematicians,_Physicists_and_Astronomers_of_Slovenia), the [Institute of Mathematics, Physics, and Mechanics](wikipedia://en/1627082), and the [Slovenian Discrete and Applied Mathematics Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenian_Discrete_and_Applied_Mathematics_Society). It is a [platinum open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access#Diamond) journal,[^o3] with articles published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_Attribution_4.0) license.[^o4]

:::

- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2018 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.910.[^o6]
