# Annales scientifiques de l'École normale supérieure

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Annales scientifiques de l'École normale supérieure"}

***Annales Scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure*** is a French [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France). It was established in 1864 by the French chemist [Louis Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur) and published articles in mathematics, [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), and [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology). In 1900, it became a purely mathematical journal. It is published with help of the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique). Its web site is hosted by the mathematics department of the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure).
