# Gordan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gordan"}

**Gordan** (Гордан) is a [Slavic name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavic_names) derived [Proto-Slavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Slavic_language) *\*gъrdъ* (*gȏrd*) meaning "proud".

:::

## Given name {#Given_name}

- [Gordan Golik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Golik), Croatian football midfielder
- [Gordan Giriček](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Giriček), Croatian basketball player
- [Gordan Jandroković](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Jandroković), Croatian diplomat and politician
- [Gordan Lederer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Lederer), Croatian war journalist
- [Gordan Kičić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Kičić), Serbian actor, comedian and director
- [Gordan Nikolitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Nikolitch), Serbian violinist
- [Gordan Petrić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Petrić), Serbian footballer
- [Gordan Vidović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordan_Vidović), Belgian footballer
