# Circle packing in an isosceles right triangle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Circle packing in an isosceles right triangle"}

**Circle packing in a right isosceles triangle** is a [packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_problem) where the objective is to pack $n$ [unit circles](wikipedia://en/27072071) into the smallest possible [isosceles right triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_right_triangles#45–45–90_triangle).

:::

Minimum solutions (lengths shown are length of leg) are shown in the table below.[^o2] Solutions to the equivalent problem of maximizing the minimum distance between $n$ [points](wikipedia://en/593693) in an isosceles right triangle, were known to be [optimal](wikipedia://en/52033) for $n < 8$[^o3] and were extended up to $n = 10$.[^o4]

In 2011 a [heuristic algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heuristic_algorithm) found 18 improvements on previously known optima, the smallest of which was for $n = 13$.[^o5]
