# Jerome Goldstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jerome Goldstein"}

**Jerome (Jerry) Arthur Goldstein** (born August 5, 1941 in [Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh,_Pennsylvania)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose main interests are [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis), [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), [quantum theory](wikipedia://en/25202), and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Goldstein earned his B.S, M.S. and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degrees at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) in 1963, 1964 and 1967. His Ph.D. thesis *Stochastic Differential Equations and Nonlinear Semigroups* was supervised by [Malempati M. Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malempati_M._Rao).

He held appointments as [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Mathematics at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) (1968–91), and [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University) (1991–96). Since 1996 he is Professor of Mathematical Sciences at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis).

In addition, he has held visiting positions at universities in Austria, Brazil, England, France, Germany, Italy, Scotland and the United States.

## Work {#Work}

Jerry's papers and his book[^o6] on operator semigroups include fundamental contributions to his research areas. His list of publications presently includes more than 250 items.[^o7]

He is editor of more than 10 journals, including *[Semigroup Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semigroup_Forum)*, *Differential and Integral Equations*,[^o9] *Advances in Differential Equations*,[^o10] *Positivity*,[^o11] *Journal of Evolution Equations*,[^o12] and *[Mathematische Nachrichten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematische_Nachrichten)*.[^o13]

Jerome Goldstein has advised 29 PhD-students and has 78 coauthors.[^o15]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2006 Goldstein received Willard R. Sparks Eminent Faculty Award[^o17] from the University of Memphis, and in 2002 he was the recipient of the Distinguished Research Award from the University of Memphis.[^o18] In 2004-2007 he held the Dunavant Professorship at the University of Memphis.[^o19] During his years at Tulane University, Goldstein received the first Faculty Award for Excellence in Research (1985) given by Tulane University.

In 2013 he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to partial differential equations and its applications, and to the dissemination of mathematics to a wider public".[^o20]
