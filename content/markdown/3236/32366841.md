# Parallel parking problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parallel parking problem"}

*Image: ParallelParkingAnimation.gif.* Animation of a car parallel parking, turning only its front wheels

The **parallel parking problem** is a [motion planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_planning) problem in [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) to determine the path a car must take to [parallel park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_parking) into a parking space. The front wheels of a car are permitted to turn, but the rear wheels must stay aligned. When a car is initially adjacent to a parking space, to move into the space it would need to move in a direction perpendicular to the allowed path of motion of the rear wheels. The admissible motions of the car in its configuration space are an example of a [nonholonomic system](wikipedia://en/554248).

:::
