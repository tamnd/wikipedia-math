# Bobby Hersom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bobby Hersom"}

**Bobby Hersom** (born 1929 in [Cheshire, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheshire,_England)) is a British mathematician and computer scientist known for her early work on computers at [Elliott Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Brothers_%28computer_company%29), [Hatfield Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatfield_Polytechnic), and the Rothamstead Agricultural Research Station.[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hersom was born Beryl Lewis (not Roberta as in [^o4]) in Cheshire, England, the oldest of three children. She earned a maths degree from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1950. She then earned a teaching degree in 1951.[^o2] She was a member of The Round, an English Country Dance Club while at Cambridge.[^o5][^o6]

She married [Ed Hersom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Hersom), who she met through work, in 1954.[^o7]

Her son Colin also attended Cambridge[^o5] and was a mathematician and her daughter was a farmer.[^o2] She regretted having to quit her work as a computer scientist after her first child was born, which meant she could only work freelance.[^o8]

Her husband died in 2002.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

After earning her teaching degree, Hersom worked as a teacher, but realized she didn't enjoy that work.[^o2]

She started working in the Theory Division at Elliott Brothers in Borehamwood, UK, in 1953.[^o6] There she wrote software programs for Nicholas, a research computer. Some of the software she developed processed [radar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar) data.[^o2] In 1954, she left that job to start her family.[^o7][^o4]

Hersom then worked for Rothamstead for 11 years.[^o2] She later worked as a consultant for [Hatfield Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatfield_Polytechnic), along with her husband. She worked there until retirement.[^o2]
