# Dominique Haughton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dominique Haughton"}

**Dominique Marie-Annick Haughton** is a French statistician whose research interests include [business analytics](wikipedia://en/10147369), [standards of living](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_of_living), and applications of statistics to [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music). She is a professor of mathematical sciences at [Bentley University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bentley_University). She is also an associated researcher with the research center on Statistique, Analyse et Modélisation Multidisciplinaire at the [University of Paris 1 Pantheon-Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_1_Pantheon-Sorbonne).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After studying for the [baccalauréat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baccalauréat) at the Lycée Pierre de la Ramée in [Saint-Quentin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Quentin,_Aisne), in Northern France, and continued study at the [Lycée privé Sainte-Geneviève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_privé_Sainte-Geneviève), Haughton entered the [Ecole Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris in 1975, where she earned a master's degree in mathematics in 1976, a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in English in 1977, and a [Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in mathematics in 1977. After a year at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a Sachs scholar, she entered the Ph.D. program at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and completed a Ph.D. in mathematics in 1983. Her dissertation, *On the Choice of a Model to Fit Data from an Exponential Family*, was supervised by [Richard M. Dudley](wikipedia://en/3824977).

After working as an assistant professor at [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College), [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University), and the [University of Lowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Lowell), she joined the Bentley University faculty in 1991.

## Books {#Books}

Haughton is the co-author, with Mark-David McLaughlin, Kevin Mentzer, and Changan Zhang, of the book *Movie Analytics: A Hollywood Introduction to Big Data* (Springer, 2015). She is also the coauthor, with Jonathan Haughton, of *Living Standards Analytics: Development through the Lens of Household Survey Data* (Springer, 2011).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Haughton was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2011 "for outstanding collaborative research, outreach, and training in business analytics; for analysis of international living standards data; and for service to the profession".
