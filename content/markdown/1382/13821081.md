# Saint-Venant

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Saint-Venant"}

**Saint-Venant** (Papingem) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Pas-de-Calais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pas-de-Calais) [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_France) (administrative division) in the Hauts-de-France [region of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Region_of_France)[^o4] about 9 mi northwest of [Béthune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béthune) and 26 mi west of [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille), by the banks of the [Lys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lys_%28river%29).

:::
