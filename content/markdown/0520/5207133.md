# Consumption distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Consumption distribution"}

In [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), the **consumption distribution** or **consumption inequality** is an alternative to the [income distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_distribution) or [wealth distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_distribution) for judging [economic inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_inequality), comparing levels of [consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumption_%28economics%29) or spending rather than [income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income) or [wealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth).[^o2] This is an important measure of inequality as the basic utility of the wealth or income is the expenditure.[^o3] People [experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experience) the inequality directly in consumption, rather than income or wealth.[^o4][^o5] [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank) lists 118 countries based on consumption inequality compared to 68 countries based on [income inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_inequality).[^o6]

:::
