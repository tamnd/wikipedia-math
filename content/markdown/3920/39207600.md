# Amnon Yekutieli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amnon Yekutieli"}

**Amnon Yekutieli** (אמנון יקותיאלי) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [noncommutative algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_algebra), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [deformation quantization](wikipedia://en/4278245). He is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev).

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Born in [Rehovot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehovot), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), he earned both his bachelor's and master's degrees at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). His master thesis was done under the supervision of [Shimshon Amitsur](wikipedia://en/19937535). He received his Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1990, after studying there with [Michael Artin](wikipedia://en/1519821). Yekutieli received the [Alon Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alon_Fellowship) in 1993. He joined the Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in 1999.
