# Burkard Polster

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Burkard Polster"}

**Burkard Polster** (born 26 February 1965 in [Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Würzburg)) is a German[^o4] [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who runs and presents the *Mathologer* channel on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube).[^o5] He is a professor of mathematics at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) in [Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne), Australia.[^o6]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Polster earned a doctorate from the [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg) in 1993 under the supervision of Karl Strambach. Other universities that Polster has been affiliated with, before joining Monash University in 2000, include the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), [University at Albany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Albany), [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel), [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury), and [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide).[^o9]

Polster's research involves topics in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446), and the mathematics of everyday life, including how to tie [shoelaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoelaces)[^o10] or stabilize a wobbly table.[^o11]

- (2001). "Geometries on Surfaces". Cambridge University Press. isbn:0-521-66058-0. <https://archive.org/details/geometriesonsurf0000pols>[^o16]
- (2003). "The Mathematics of Juggling". Springer. isbn:0-387-95513-5[^o18]
- (2004). "Q.E.D.: Beauty in Mathematical Proof". Wooden Books. isbn:190426350X[^o20] Included in the four-book compilation *Scientia: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Astronomy for All* (2011) and translated into German as *Sciencia: Mathematik, Physik, Chemie, Biologie und Astronomie für alle verständlich* (Librero, 2014, in German).
- (2004). "Les Ambigrammes: l'art de symétriser les mots". Editions Ecritextes. isbn:2915633002
- (2006). "The Shoelace Book: A Mathematical Guide to the Best (and Worst) Ways to Lace Your Shoes". American Mathematical Society. isbn:0821839330. <https://archive.org/details/shoelacebookmath00pols>[^o23]

- (2012). "Math Goes to the Movies". Johns Hopkins University Press. isbn:9781421404837[^o26]
- (2017). "A Dingo Ate My Math Book: Mathematics from Down Under". American Mathematical Society. isbn:9781470435219
- (2022). "Putting Two and Two Together". American Mathematical Society. isbn:978-1-4704-6011-2
