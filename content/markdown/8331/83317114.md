# Borg cipher

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Borg cipher"}

The **Borg cipher** is an [encrypted](wikipedia://en/5244) manuscript, probably from the 1600s, describing treatment of symptoms and diseases. The manuscript consists of 408 handwritten pages. The only unencrypted parts are the first page in Arabic, some fragments and headings in Latin, and the last two pages in Italian. It is stored at the [Vatican Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_Library) under the name *[MSS-Borg.lat.898](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Borg.lat.898).[^o3][^o4]*

:::

## Cipher {#Cipher}

The cipher includes 34 different characters, including graphic signs and [Roman letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_script) with [diacritics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diacritic). It was decoded in 2016 by Nada Aldarrab, Kevin Knight and Beáta Megyesi.[^o6]
