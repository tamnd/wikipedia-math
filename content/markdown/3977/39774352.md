# V. S. Sunder

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="V. S. Sunder"}

**Vaikalathur Shankar Sunder** (born 6 April 1952) is an Indian mathematician who specialises in [subfactors](wikipedia://en/2780098), [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) in general.

:::

In 1996, he was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, in the mathematical sciences category.

Sunder is one of the first Indian operator algebraists. In addition to publishing about sixty papers, he has written six books including at least three monographs at the graduate level or higher on [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185). One of the books was co-authored with [Vaughan Jones](wikipedia://en/1227628), an operator algebraist, who has received the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859).[^o3][^o4]

He received a Doctor of Philosophy from [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 1977.
