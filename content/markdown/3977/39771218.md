# Kalyan Bidhan Sinha

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kalyan Bidhan Sinha"}

**Kalyan Bidhan Sinha (K.B. Sinha)** (born 3 June 1944) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a professor at the [Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru_Centre_for_Advanced_Scientific_Research),[^o3] and Professor Emeritus for life of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418).

:::

Sinha is the author of numerous scientific works[^o4] in [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory), [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) of [Schrödinger operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schrödinger_operator), [quantum stochastic calculus](wikipedia://en/41263617), [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), and, more broadly, in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kalyan Bidhan Sinha graduated from [Hindu School, Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_School,_Kolkata) in 1960. He studied Physics at [Presidency College, Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Calcutta), obtaining his Bachelor's degree from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) in 1963, and then studied at the [University of Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delhi) where he was awarded a Master's degree in 1965. He obtained his Ph.D. in Physics from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 1969. [^o6]

Sinha was a post-doctoral research associate at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva). During this time he co-authored, with Werner Amrein and [Josef-Maria Jauch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef-Maria_Jauch), a well-known book[^o7] on scattering theory, which used mathematically rigorous methods to develop the subject.

Sinha served on the faculty of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) from 1978 to 2005, serving as Director of the institute from 2000 to 2005. He has held numerous visiting faculty positions, at RIMS ([Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto)),[^o8] [University of Texas, Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas,_Austin), [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), among many other institutions. He was Ulam Visiting Chair Professor at the [University of Colorado, Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado,_Boulder) in 1980. He is currently Honorary Professor, [Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru_Centre_for_Advanced_Scientific_Research), [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore). He is also Distinguished Associate of Institute Mathematics Initiative, [Indian Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science), and Professor Emeritus, [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418).

Sinha is President of the *Association for Quantum Probability and Infinite Dimensional Analysis*[^o9] (since 2013). He has served on numerous editorial boards, including those of *Infinite Dimensional Analysis, Quantum Probability, and Related Topics*,[^o10] [Reviews in Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reviews_in_Mathematical_Physics), and the *Journal of Stochastic Analysis*.[^o11]

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2019 Sinha was awarded the [Srinivasa Ramanujan Medal](wikipedia://en/32501943) of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy).[^o13] He received the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), in the mathematical sciences category in 1988.[^o14] In 2004 Sinha was awarded the P.C. [Mahalanobis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahalanobis) Medal by the Indian Science Congress.[^o15] Sinha was elected Fellow of the [TWAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS)[^o16] in 2002. He was named Professor Emeritus for Life of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) in 2012.
