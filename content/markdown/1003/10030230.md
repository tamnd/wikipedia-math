# BMDP

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="BMDP"}

**BMDP** was a statistical package developed in 1965 by [Wilfrid Dixon](wikipedia://en/47219631) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). The acronym stands for Bio-Medical Data Package, the word package was added by Dixon as the software consisted of a series of programs (subroutines) which performed different parametric and nonparametric statistical analyses.[^o2][^o3]

:::

BMDP was originally distributed for free. It was later sold by [Statsols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statsols), who originally was a subsidiary of BMDP, but through a management buy-out formed the now independent company Statistical Solutions Ltd, known as Statsols. BMDP is no longer available .[^o4] The company decided to only offer its other statistical product [nQuery Sample Size Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NQuery_Sample_Size_Software).
