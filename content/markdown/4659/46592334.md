# Serre's theorem on affineness

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serre's theorem on affineness"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) discipline of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Serre's theorem on affineness** (also called **Serre's cohomological characterization of affineness** or **Serre's criterion on affineness**) is a theorem due to [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) which gives sufficient conditions for a [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) to be [affine](wikipedia://en/37291), stated in terms of [sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357).[^o2] The theorem was first published by Serre in 1957.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $X$ be a scheme with [structure sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466#Ringed_spaces_and_locally_ringed_spaces) $O_{X}.$ If:

(1) $X$ is quasi-compact, and

(2) for every [quasi-coherent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-coherent) [ideal sheaf](wikipedia://en/9508138) $I$ of $O_{X}$-modules, $H^{1}(X, I) = 0$,

then $X$ is [affine](wikipedia://en/37291).[^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Related results"}

## Related results {#Related_results}

- A special case of this theorem arises when $X$ is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808), in which case the conditions of the theorem imply that $X$ is an [affine variety](wikipedia://en/320468).
- A similar result has stricter conditions on $X$ but looser conditions on the cohomology: if $X$ is a [quasi-separated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-separated), quasi-compact scheme, and if $H^{1}(X, I) = 0$ for any quasi-coherent sheaf of ideals $I$ of finite type, then $X$ is affine.[^o9]

:::
