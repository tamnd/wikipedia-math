# G-spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="G-spectrum"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a **G-spectrum** is a [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) with an [action](wikipedia://en/12781) of a (finite) group.

:::

Let *X* be a spectrum with an action of a finite group *G*. The important notion is that of the homotopy fixed point set $X^{hG}$. There is always

$$X^G \to X^{hG},$$

a map from the fixed point spectrum to a homotopy fixed point spectrum (because, by definition, $X^{hG}$ is the [mapping spectrum](wikipedia://en/46797936) $F(BG_+, X)^G$).

Example: $\mathbb{Z}/2$ acts on the [complex *K*-theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_K-theory) *KU* by taking the [conjugate bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_bundle) of a [complex vector bundle](wikipedia://en/1538109). Then $KU^{h\mathbb{Z}/2} = KO$, the real *K*-theory.

The cofiber of $X_{hG} \to X^{hG}$ is called the [Tate spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_spectrum) of *X*.

## *G*-Galois extension in the sense of Rognes {#G-Galois_extension_in_the_sense_of_Rognes}

This notion is due to J. Rognes (Rognes 2008). Let *A* be an [**E**<sub>∞</sub>-ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-infinity_ring) with an action of a finite group *G* and *B* = *A*<sup>*hG*</sup> its invariant subring. Then *B* → *A* (the map of *B*-algebras in **E**<sub>∞</sub>-sense) is said to be a *G-Galois extension* if the natural map

$$A \otimes_B A \to \prod_{g \in G} A$$

(which generalizes $x \otimes y \mapsto (g(x) y)$ in the classical setup) is an equivalence. The extension is faithful if the [Bousfield classes](wikipedia://en/46605765) of *A*, *B* over *B* are equivalent.

Example: *KO* → *KU* is a $\mathbb{Z}$./2-Galois extension.
