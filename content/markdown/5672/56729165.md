# Tammo tom Dieck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tammo tom Dieck"}

*Image: Tammo tom Diek.jpg.* Tammo tom Dieck 1972

**Tammo tom Dieck** (29 May 1938, [São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Paulo)) is a German mathematician, specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

Tammo tom Dieck studied mathematics from 1957 at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University), where he received his [promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (Ph.D.) in 1964 under [Dieter Puppe](wikipedia://en/63815664) with thesis *Zur [$K$-Theorie](wikipedia://en/246748) und ihren [Kohomologie](wikipedia://en/194060)-Operationen*. In 1969 tom Dieck received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) under [Albrecht Dold](wikipedia://en/31337135). From 1970 to 1975 he was a professor at Saarland University. In 1975 he became a professor at the University of Göttingen.

Tammo tom Dieck is an expert in algebraic topology and author of several widely-used textbooks in topology. He has done research on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), [G-structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-structure),[^o3] and [cobordism](wikipedia://en/391832). In the 1990s and 2000s, his research dealt with [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) (and its algebras) and [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).

In 1986 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Geometric representation theory of compact Lie groups* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). In 1984 he was elected a full member of the [Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademie_der_Wissenschaften_zu_Göttingen).

His doctoral students include Stefan Bauer and [Wolfgang Lück](wikipedia://en/26197113).

Tammo tom Dieck is a grandson of the architect Walter Klingenberg, a brother of the chemist Heindirk tom Dieck,[^o4] and the father of the pianist Wiebke tom Dieck.
