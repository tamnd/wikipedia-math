# Raymond J. Carroll

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Raymond J. Carroll"}

**Raymond James Carroll** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), and [Distinguished Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Professor) of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [nutrition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutrition) and [toxicology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toxicology) at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). He is a recipient of 1988 [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) and 2002 [R. A. Fisher Lectureship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._A._Fisher_Lectureship). He has made fundamental contributions to [measurement error model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_error_model), [nonparametric](wikipedia://en/4536068) and [semiparametric modeling](wikipedia://en/4536125).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Carroll was born in Japan of military parents in 1949 and grew up in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), Germany and [Wichita Falls, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wichita_Falls,_Texas). He graduated with a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in 1971 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 1974 under the supervision of [Shanti S. Gupta](wikipedia://en/82364891).[^o37] He was on the faculty at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) from 1974 to 1987. He also had visiting positions at the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg), the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin), and the [National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Heart,_Lung,_and_Blood_Institute). Carroll has been a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) of statistics,[^o14] nutrition[^o15] and toxicology[^o16] at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) since 1987, was head of the Department of Statistics from 1987 to 1990,[^o45] and was named a [Distinguished Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Professor)[^o18][^o17] in 1997. He has visiting appointments at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), the [Humboldt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute). He was the founding director of the Texas A&M Center for Statistical [Bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214),[^o43] and has been the director of Texas A&M Institute for [Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [Computational Science](wikipedia://en/1181008) since 2010.[^o19] He holds an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from the Institut de Statistique, [Université Catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Catholique_de_Louvain) in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium).[^o20][^o21]

Carroll's many areas of research include [measurement error model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_error_model), [nonparametric](wikipedia://en/4536068) and [semiparametric regression](wikipedia://en/4536125), [inverse problem](wikipedia://en/203956), [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368), [case-control studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case-control_studies), among others. His work has a broad variety of application fields, including radiation and [nutritional epidemiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutritional_epidemiology), [molecular biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biology), [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics) and many others. He has authored or coauthored four books,[^o34] over 300 refereed papers and has given over 300 invited talks. He has supervised and mentored more than 30 [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) students[^o32] and can claim more than 90 descendants in his [mathematical genealogy](wikipedia://en/1152126).[^o31]

He received the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483)[^o28] in 1988 and gave the [Fisher Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._A._Fisher_Lectureship)[^o24] at the 2002 [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662). He was the first [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) given a Method to Extend Research in Time (MERIT) Award from the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute).[^o22] He served as editor of [Biometrics](wikipedia://en/11885692)[^o44] and [Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313) (Theory and Methods),[^o35][^o36] and chair of [ASA](wikipedia://en/341988)'s Section on Nonparametric Statistics.[^o40] A conference on "Statistical Methods for Complex Data" was held on the [Texas A&M University campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University_campus) in honor of Carroll in 2009.[^o33] In the same year, the Raymond J. Carroll Young Investigator Award was established to honor Carroll for his fundamental contributions in many areas of statistical methodology and practice.[^o41] The award is given bi-annually on odd numbered years to a statistician who has made important contributions to the area of statistics, with the recipients being [Samuel Kou](wikipedia://en/63812377) and [Marc A. Suchard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_A._Suchard), both are also [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) recipients.[^o42] Ray Carroll was in the selection committee of COPSS Presidents' Award during that period.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Carroll is married to Texas A&M behavioral scientist [Marcia G. Ory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcia_G._Ory).[^o5]
