# Gopinath Kallianpur

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gopinath Kallianpur"}

**Gopinath Kallianpur** (1925–2015)[^o3] was an [Indian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_American) mathematician and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who became the first director of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) (1976–79) under its new Memorandum of Association. During his tenure as the director the new centre of [ISI](wikipedia://en/2238418) at Bangalore, Karnataka was founded.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

He received his Bachelor's degree in 1945 and Master's degree in 1946 from the University of Madras.[^o5] In [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay) Kallianpur came in contact with [D. D. Kosambi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damodar_Dharmananda_Kosambi), a well-known mathematician and versatile scholar and with [Subbaramiah Minakshisundaram](wikipedia://en/30837114) at [Andhra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_University) which was then in [Guntur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur). Under the supervision of [Herbert Robbins](wikipedia://en/745193), of the Statistics Department of the [University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina,_Chapel_Hill) he obtained his doctoral degree in 1951 in the then developing field of [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).

## Academic life {#Academic_life}

In the start of his career he held the position of lecturer at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), during 1951–52, and was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Study,_Princeton), from 1952 to 1953. In 1953 Kallianpur joined [ISI](wikipedia://en/2238418) and stayed in [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta) until the summer of 1956. During the period distinguished scientists, like [Ronald Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824), [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185) and [Yuri Linnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Linnik) visited the Institute. [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185) collaborated with Kallianpur on topics including [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [prediction theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prediction_theory) and generalized [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147)[^o7] Between 1956 and 1976 Kallianpur held a number of faculty positions at the [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) (1956–59, 1961–63); [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) (1959–61); [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). At [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) Kallianpur collaborated with [Charlotte Striebel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Striebel), a member of the Research Group of [Lockheed Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Corporation) working on filtering and control problems.[^o8] From 1976 to 1979 he became the first director of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418). In 1979 he was appointed Alumni Distinguished Professor at the [University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina,_Chapel_Hill) (1979–2001). Six years after his retirement in 2001 Kallianpur moved to Nashville, Tennessee where he continued to work on mathematics, until moving to Cleveland, Ohio in 2014.[^o9]
