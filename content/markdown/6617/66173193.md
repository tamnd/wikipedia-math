# Opinion polling for the 2022 Israeli legislative election

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opinion polling for the 2022 Israeli legislative election"}

In the run up to the [2022 Israeli legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election), various organisations carried out [opinion polling](wikipedia://en/277315) to gauge voting intention in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) during the term of the [24th Knesset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24th_Knesset). This article lists the results of such polls.

:::

The date range for these opinion polls is from the [2021 Israeli legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Israeli_legislative_election), held on 23 March, to the present day. Due to the [political deadlock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_deadlock) that resulted after the previous election and the possibility of a fifth consecutive [snap election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snap_election), polling for the next election started two weeks after the previous election.[^o217] The election was held on 1 November 2022.[^o148] In keeping with the [election silence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_silence) tradition, no polls may be published from the end of the Friday before the election until the [polling stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polling_station) close on election day at 22:00.[^o2][^o3]

Polls are listed in reverse chronological order, showing the most recent first and using the dates when the survey fieldwork was done, as opposed to the date of publication. Where the fieldwork dates are unknown, the date of publication is given instead. The highest figure in each polling survey is displayed with its background shaded in the leading party's colour. If a tie ensues, this is applied to the highest figures. When a poll has no information on a certain party, that party is instead marked by a dash (–).

## Seat projections {#Seat_projections}

This section displays voting intention estimates referring to the next [Knesset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knesset) election. The figures listed are Knesset seat counts rather than percentages, unless otherwise stated.

This graph shows the polling trends from the [2021 Israeli legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Israeli_legislative_election) until the next election day using a 4-poll [moving average](wikipedia://en/990809). Scenario polls are not included here. For parties not crossing the [electoral threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_threshold) (currently 3.25%) in any given poll, the number of seats is calculated as a percentage of the 120 total seats.
