# Pao-sheng Hsu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pao-sheng Hsu"}

**Pao-sheng Hsu** is a [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471),

:::

## Career {#Career}

After primary school, Pao-sheng Hsu’s education was conducted in both Chinese and English, with Chinese history and literature taught in Chinese and other subjects taught in English.[^o4] Hsu completed her PhD under George Bachman at [Polytechnic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering) (previously the Polytechnic Institute of New York, now the New York University Tandon School of Engineering) in 1975; her dissertation was titled *An Application of Compactification: Some Theorems on Maximal Ideals*.

In 1999, 2000, and 2001, Hsu served in the "Human Rights of Mathematicians" committee of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

In 2006, Hsu, mathematician and past [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) president [Suzanne Lenhart](wikipedia://en/46919225) and middle school teacher Erica Voolich founded the AWM Teacher Partnership Program. "The goal of the program is to link teachers of mathematics in schools, museums, technical institutes, two-year colleges, and universities with other teachers working in an environment different from their own and with mathematicians working in business, government, and industry." , she is an organizer of the AWM Teacher Partnership, with Suzanne Lenhart and Erica Voolich.[^o6] She also translated a news article about the discussion "Complexities and Opportunities for Women in Mathematics" at the [2002 International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) for the AWM newsletter.[^o7]

With [Jacqueline Dewar](wikipedia://en/58699727) and [Harriet Pollatsek](wikipedia://en/42289968), she edited the volume *Mathematics Education: A Spectrum of Work in Mathematical Sciences Departments*, which was published by [Springer International Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_International_Publishing) on 26 November 2016 as part of the Association for Women in Mathematics series.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

Hsu was presented with an [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) Service Award in 2013 "*for her role in establishing the Teacher Partnership, long-time service on the \[Math\] Education Committee which included representing AWM at the CBMS Forum in 2009 and 2010, and service on the AWM Web Task Force (2008–2010).*"

She was elected a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the Class of 2019 "*for her sustained efforts and achievements as a researcher and leader in mathematics education, especially for AWM; for her building of bridges connecting the communities of mathematicians, mathematics educators, and K–12 teachers; and for her work as a teacher and scholar of mathematics*".
