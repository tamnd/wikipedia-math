# Ulla Dinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulla Dinger"}

**Ulla Margarete Dinger** (born 1955) is a Swedish mathematician specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). She was the first woman to earn a doctorate in mathematics at the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg).

:::

Dinger completed her doctorate at the University of Gothenburg in 1989. Her dissertation, *On the ball problem and the Laguerre maximal operators*, was jointly supervised by [Christer Borell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christer_Borell) (of the [Borell–Brascamp–Lieb inequality](wikipedia://en/11347127)) and Peter Sjögren.

She is a senior lecturer in mathematics at the [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology), where she used to teach [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and heads the program for the Preparatory Year in Natural Sciences.
