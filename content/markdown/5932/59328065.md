# Martin Grohe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Grohe"}

**Martin Grohe** (born 1967) is a German mathematician and computer scientist known for his research on [parameterized complexity](wikipedia://en/603026), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [finite model theory](wikipedia://en/1773596), the [logic of graphs](wikipedia://en/47857242), [database theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database_theory), [descriptive complexity theory](wikipedia://en/1212009), and [graph neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_neural_network). He is a University Professor of Computer Science at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University), where he holds the Chair for Logic and Theory of Discrete Systems.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Grohe earned his doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) in 1994. His dissertation, *The Structure of Fixed-Point Logics*, was supervised by [Heinz-Dieter Ebbinghaus](wikipedia://en/28870090). After postdoctoral research at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) and [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), he earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) in 1998. He became professor at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago) in 2000, reader at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 2001, and professor at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 2003, before becoming professor at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University) in 2012.

## Books {#Books}

Grohe is the author of *Descriptive Complexity, Canonisation, and Definable Graph Structure Theory* (Lecture Notes in Logic 47, Cambridge University Press, 2017). In 2011, Grohe and [Johann A. Makowsky](wikipedia://en/43507104) published as editors the 558th proceedings of the [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822)-[ASL](wikipedia://en/3770438) special session on *Model Theoretic Methods in Finite Combinatorics*, which was held on January 5-8 2009 in Washington, DC. With Jörg Flum, he is the co-author of *Parameterized Complexity Theory* (Springer, 2006).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Grohe won the [Heinz Maier–Leibnitz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Maier-Leibnitz-Preis) awarded by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) in 1999, and he was elected as an [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow) in 2017 for "contributions to logic in computer science, database theory, algorithms, and computational complexity". In 2022, he was awarded an ERC Advanced Grant "Symmetry and Similarity".
