# Algorismus (Norse text)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algorismus"}

***Algorismus*** is a short treatise on [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), written in [Old Icelandic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Icelandic). It is the oldest text on mathematics in a Scandinavian language and survives in the early fourteenth-century manuscript [Hauksbók](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hauksbók), a large book written and compiled by Icelanders and taken to Norway during the later part of the 13th century by [Haukur Erlendsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haukur_Erlendsson).[^o3] It primarily consists of a translation of *Carmen de Algorismo* by [Alexander of Villedieu](wikipedia://en/8918696) from c. 1200, which was in turn translated from the works of [al-Khwarismi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Khwarismi), and which, with *[Liber Abaci](wikipedia://en/167699)* by [Fibonacci](wikipedia://en/17949) of 1202, and *Algorismus Vulgaris* by [De Sacrobosco](wikipedia://en/960620) of 1230 formed the "three main Latin algorithmic works in the Middle Ages".[^o4]

:::
