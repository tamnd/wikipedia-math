# Timur Eneev

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Timur Eneev"}

**Timur Magomedovich Eneev** (September 23, 1924[^o3] – September 8, 2019) was a Russian mathematician specializing in mechanics and control processes. A minor planet Eneev discovered in 1978 is named after him.[^o4] He was the editor-in-chief of the journal *[Cosmic Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_Research)*.[^o5] He was a member of the [Balkar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkar) ethnic group.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1948 Eneev graduated from the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and until 1953 worked as a research worker at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). Since 1953 he is associated with the [Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984473).[^o4] He became a member of the [CPSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Soviet_Union) since 1957 and a corresponding member of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1968.[^o3]

Eneev received the [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) in 1957 and has been awarded the [Order of Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Lenin) and the [Order of the Red Banner of Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Red_Banner_of_Labour).[^o3] In 2006 he was awarded the prestigious [Demidov Prize](wikipedia://en/6905037) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in mathematics.
