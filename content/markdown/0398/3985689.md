# Tirukkannapuram Vijayaraghavan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tirukkannapuram Vijayaraghavan"}

**Tirukkannapuram Vijayaraghavan** (திருக்கண்ணபுரம் விஜயராகவன்; 30 November 1902 – 20 April 1955) was an Indian mathematician from the [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras) region. He worked with [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599) on [Pisot–Vijayaraghavan numbers](wikipedia://en/519303) when he went to [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford) in the mid-1920s. He was a fellow of [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) elected in 1934. His father was a [pandit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandit).

:::

Vijayaraghavan was well-versed in Sanskrit and Tamil. He was a close friend of [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019). Weil hired him in 1930 despite his lack of diploma, and they served together in [Aligarh Muslim University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aligarh_Muslim_University).[^o3] While Weil was away in Europe, [Ross Masood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Masood) planned to replace Weil's professorship with Vijayaraghavan, but Vijayaraghavan quit in protest and moved to the [University of Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dhaka).[^o4]

Vijayaraghavan proved a special case of [Herschfeld's theorem](wikipedia://en/2503693#Herschfeld's_convergence_theorem) on [nested radicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nested_radicals):[^o5] For $a_n>0$

$$\sqrt{a_1 + \sqrt{a_2 + \sqrt{a_3 + \sqrt{a_4 + \cdots}}}}$$

converges if and only if

$$\overline{\lim} (\log a_n)/2^n < +\infty,$$

where $\overline{\lim}$ denotes the [limit superior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_superior_and_limit_inferior).
