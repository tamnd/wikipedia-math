# Parikh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parikh"}

**Parikh** is a name found among [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu), [Jains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jainism) and [Memon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memon_People) from India. It means *assayer* in the [Gujarati language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarati_language) and has its roots in the [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) word for *examiner*. Both the [Oswal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswal) and [Porwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porwal) communities of India, Canada and United States have clans called *Parekh*.[^o2]

:::

People bearing the name, who may or may not be affiliated with the above communities, include:

- [Shrenu Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrenu_Parikh), Indian television actress
- [Narhari Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narhari_Parikh), Indian freedom fighter and social reformer
- [Rahul Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahul_Parikh), American pediatrician
- [Rasiklal Chhotalal Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasiklal_Chhotalal_Parikh), Gujarati writer, poet, and Indologist
- [Rohit Jivanlal Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohit_Jivanlal_Parikh), Indian-American mathematician and logician
- [Himanshu Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himanshu_Parikh), Indian architect
- [Jayant Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayant_Parikh), Indian painter, printmaker, and muralist
- [Vasant Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasant_Parikh), Indian politician and social worker
- [Deepak Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepak_Parikh), CEO of [Clariant Chemicals India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clariant_Chemicals_India)
- [Tej Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tej_Parikh), former Chief Economist, Institute of Directors
