# Siegel parabolic subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel parabolic subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Siegel parabolic subgroup**, named after [Carl Ludwig Siegel](wikipedia://en/716602), is the [parabolic subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) of the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) with abelian radical, given by the matrices of the symplectic group whose lower left quadrant is 0 (for the standard symplectic form).

:::
