# Heegner's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heegner's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Heegner's lemma** is a lemma used by [Kurt Heegner](wikipedia://en/621799) in his paper on the [class number problem](wikipedia://en/596282). His lemma states that if

:::

$$y^2=a_4x^4+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0$$

is a curve over a field with *a*<sub>4</sub> not a square, then it has a solution if it has a solution in an [extension](wikipedia://en/11634) of odd degree.
