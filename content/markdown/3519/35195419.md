# Kronecker's congruence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kronecker's congruence"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Kronecker's congruence**, introduced by [Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450), states that

:::

$$\Phi_p(x,y)\equiv (x-y^p)(x^p-y)\bmod p,$$

where *p* is a prime and Φ<sub>*p*</sub>(*x*,*y*) is the modular polynomial of order *p*, given by

$$\Phi_n(x,j) = \prod_\tau (x-j(\tau))$$

for *j* the [elliptic modular function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_modular_function) and τ running through classes of imaginary quadratic integers of discriminant *n*.
