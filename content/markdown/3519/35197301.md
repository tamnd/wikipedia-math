# Modular unit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modular unit"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **modular units** are certain units of [rings](wikipedia://en/48404) of integers of fields of [modular functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_function), introduced by (txt). They are functions whose [zeroes](wikipedia://en/264210) and [poles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_%28complex_analysis%29) are confined to the cusps (images of infinity).

:::
