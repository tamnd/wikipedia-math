# Siegel–Weil formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel–Weil formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Siegel–Weil formula**, introduced by (txt) as an extension of the results of (txt), expresses an [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458) as a [weighted average](wikipedia://en/189822) of [theta series](wikipedia://en/24016906) of lattices in a [genus](wikipedia://en/36597629), where the weights are proportional to the inverse of the order of the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the lattice. For the constant terms this is essentially the [Smith–Minkowski–Siegel mass formula](wikipedia://en/13362584).

:::
