# Grothendieck existence theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grothendieck existence theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Grothendieck existence theorem**, introduced by (txt, section 5), gives conditions that enable one to lift [infinitesimal](wikipedia://en/160990) deformations of a [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) to a deformation, and to lift schemes over infinitesimal neighborhoods over a subscheme of a scheme *S* to schemes over *S*.

:::

The theorem can be viewed as an instance of (Grothendieck's) [formal GAGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_GAGA).
