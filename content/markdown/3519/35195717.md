# Benno Erdmann

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Benno Erdmann"}

**Benno Erdmann** (; 30 May 1851 – 7 January 1921) was a German [neo-Kantian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Kantian) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), [psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist) and scholar of [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Erdmann received his Ph.D. in 1873 from the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) with a dissertation on Kant. The title of his thesis was *Die Stellung des Dinges an sich in Kants Aesthetik und Analytik* (*The Position of the Thing in Itself in Kant's Aesthetic and Analytic*). [Hermann von Helmholtz](wikipedia://en/53311) proposed Erdmann's publication *Die Axiome der Geometrie* (*The Axioms of Geometry*, 1877) as the basis for a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation).

In 1878 he became an [extraordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_professor) at the University of Berlin, in 1879 a full professor at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel), and in 1884 he went to the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau), in 1890 to the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle), in 1898 to the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and in 1909 he returned to Berlin.

He was the father of journalist [Lothar Erdmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothar_Erdmann).
