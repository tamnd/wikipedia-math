# Kuiper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kuiper"}

**Kuiper** is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language) [occupational surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupational_surname) meaning [cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_%28profession%29). Common spelling variants include **Kuyper**, [Kuipers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuipers), Kuijper, [Kuijpers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuijpers), [Kuypers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuypers), and [De Kuyper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Kuyper). Notable people with the name include:

:::

## Kuiper {#Kuiper}

- [Adrian Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Kuiper) (born 1959), South African cricketer
- [Barend Klaas Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barend_Klaas_Kuiper) (1877–1961), Dutch-American historian
- [David Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Kuiper) (born 1980), Dutch rower
- [Duane Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duane_Kuiper) (born 1950), American retired baseball player and broadcaster
- [Edith Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Kuiper) (born 1960), Dutch economist
- [F. B. J. Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F._B._J._Kuiper) (1907–2003), Dutch Indologist
- [Gerard Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Kuiper) (1905–1973), Dutch-American astronomer after whom the [Kuiper belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuiper_belt) was named
- [Glen Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Kuiper) (born 1963) American broadcaster
- [Hennie Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hennie_Kuiper) (born 1949), Dutch cyclist
- [J. P. Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._P._Kuiper) (1922–1985), Dutch professor of social medicine
- [Michael Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kuiper) (born 1989), Dutch martial artist
- [Nick Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Kuiper) (born 1982), Canadian ice hockey player
- [Nicky Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicky_Kuiper) (born 1989), Dutch footballer
- [Nicolaas Kuiper](wikipedia://en/3344929) (1920–1994), Dutch mathematician, known for [Kuiper's test](wikipedia://en/16720), [Kuiper's theorem](wikipedia://en/2993692), and the [Eells–Kuiper manifold](wikipedia://en/26564121)
- [Peter Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Kuiper) (1929–2007), Dutch-German actor
- [Piet Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piet_Kuiper) (1934–2017), Dutch botanist
- [Rienk Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rienk_Kuiper) (1886–1966), Frisian-Dutch president of Calvin College
- [Roel Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roel_Kuiper) (born 1962), Dutch historian, philosopher and politician
- [Taco Kuiper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Kuiper) (1941–2004), South African publisher and journalist

## Kuyper {#Kuyper}

- [Frans Kuyper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frans_Kuyper) (1629–1691), Dutch Socinian writer and printer
