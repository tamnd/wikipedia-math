# Gabriele Steidl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriele Steidl"}

**Gabriele Steidl** (born **Drauschke**, 1963) is a German mathematician whose research interests include computational [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411), and [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing). She is a professor of mathematics at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Steidl studied mathematics at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock), earning a doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) in 1988 and completing her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1991. Her doctoral dissertation, *Grundlagen schneller Algorithmen für verallgemeinerte diskrete Fouriertransformationen*, concerned fast algorithms for the [discrete Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/8811) and was supervised by Manfred Tasche.

After consulting for a German insurance association, she became an assistant professor at [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt) in 1993. She moved to the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim) as a professor in 1996, and moved again to the [Technical University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Kaiserslautern) in 2011 before taking her present position at Technische Universität Berlin in 2020. From 2020-21, she is program director of the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Imaging Science.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Steidl was elected as a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376), in the 2022 Class of [SIAM Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow), "for contributions to computational harmonic analysis and imaging sciences".
