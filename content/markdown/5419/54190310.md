# Johann Jakob Rebstein

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Jakob Rebstein"}

**Johann Jakob Rebstein** (1840–1907) was a Swiss mathematician and [surveyor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveyor).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Rebstein was born on 4 May 1840 in [Töss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Töss), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland), to his father, a baker and his mother, a doctor.[^o4]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rebstein attended post-secondary school in [Winterthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winterthur), and after graduating in 1860, went on to study for a year at [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France).[^o4] He was professor of mathematics and physics in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) from 1877 to 1898.[^o6] He was awarded his doctorate in 1895 from the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) for his work *Bestimmung aller reellen Minimalflächen, die eine Schaar ebener Curven enthalten, denen auf der Gauss'schen Kugel die Meridiane entsprechen*.[^o6][^o7] He is best known for his work in surveying, and for introducing the [traverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traverse_%28surveying%29) method in Switzerland. Throughout his career, Rebstein was appointed as surveying expert for a number of [cantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantons_of_Switzerland), including [Thurgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurgau) (1863–1881), [St. Gallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Gallen) (1881–1894), Zürich (1886–1892), and [Luzern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luzern) (1894–1907).[^o6]

In 1868 he was elected to the [Swiss Concordat of Geometers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Concordat_of_Geometers), and served as its president from 1887 until his death in 1907.[^o4]

In 1905 he was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from the [University of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich), for "outstanding contributions to actuarial sciences".[^o6]

Rebstein was a member of the organizing committee for the first meeting of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o4]

## Death {#Death}

Rebstein suffered from [kidney disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidney_disease) for the last several years of his life, and died in 1907 in Zürich.[^o4]

## Publications {#Publications}

Rebstein's publications included:[^o4]

- *Lehrbuch über praktische Geometrie mit besonderer Berücksichtigung der Theodolitmessung* (1868)
- *Die Kartographie der Schweiz, dargestellt in ihrer historischen Entwicklung* (1883)
- *Mitteilungen über die Stadtvermessung von Zürich1* (1892)
