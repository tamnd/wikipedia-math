::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **rectified 24-cell honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It is constructed by a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), containing [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) and [rectified 24-cell](wikipedia://en/6238212) cells.

:::

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Rectified icositetrachoric tetracomb
- Rectified icositetrachoric honeycomb
- Cantellated 16-cell honeycomb
- Bicantellated tesseractic honeycomb

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are five different symmetry constructions of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by different arrangements of colored [rectified 24-cell](wikipedia://en/6238212) and [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) facets. The [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) contains 4 rectified 24-cells capped by two opposite tesseracts.
