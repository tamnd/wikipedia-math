# Joseph Bernstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Bernstein"}

**Joseph Bernstein** (sometimes spelled **I. N. Bernshtein**; יוס(י)ף נאומוביץ ברנשטיין; Иосиф Наумович Бернштейн; born 18 April 1945) is a Soviet-born Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). He works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bernstein received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1972 under [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). In 1981, he emigrated to the United States due to growing [antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism) in the Soviet Union.[^o4] Bernstein was a professor at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) during 1983-1993. He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1985-86 and again in 1997-98.[^o5] In 1993, he moved to Israel to take a professorship at Tel Aviv University (emeritus since 2014).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Bernstein received a [gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_medal) at the 1962 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208). He was elected to the [Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) in 2002 and was elected to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2004. In 2004, Bernstein was awarded the [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize) for mathematics.[^o8][^o9] In 1998, he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o10] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]
