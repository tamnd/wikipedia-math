# Howell Peregrine

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Howell Peregrine"}

**Howell Peregrine** (30 December 1938 – 20 March 2007) was a British [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) noted for his contributions to [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), especially of free surface flows such as [water waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_waves), and [coastal engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_engineering).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Howell Peregrine joined the Mathematics Department of [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) in 1964 following his undergraduate and postgraduate training at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) and [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University).[^o7] He spent his entire career at Bristol. One of his most remarkable contributions was the theoretical prediction of a new nonlinear entity, now called the [Peregrine soliton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peregrine_soliton),[^o8] that may explain the formation of hydrodynamics [rogue waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_wave) and that has also been experimentally demonstrated more than 25 years later in the field of [nonlinear fiber optics](wikipedia://en/21723)[^o9][^o10] and then in 2011 in hydrodynamics with experiments in a water wave tank.[^o11]

He was an [associate editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_editor) of the *[Journal of Fluid Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Fluid_Mechanics)* for more than 25 years.

Howell Peregrine died suddenly after a short battle against [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer). He was at the time a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) of applied mathematics at the University of Bristol.

## Personal {#Personal}

Peregrine was known to be a good [photographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photographer) of natural phenomena. Some of the photographs which he took himself appeared in his papers.[^o4]
