# Pierre Pansu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Pansu"}

**Pierre Pansu** (born 13 July 1959) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a member of the [Arthur Besse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Besse) group and a close collaborator of [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29). He is a professor at the [Université Paris-Sud 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) and the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris. His main research field is geometry. His contribution to mathematics was celebrated by a double event (a conference and a workshop)[^o4] co-organized for his 60th birthday by the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655).

:::

Pierre Pansu is the grandson of French physician Félix Esclangon, and the great grand-nephew of mathematician and astronomer [Ernest Esclangon](wikipedia://en/524280), inventor of the [talking clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talking_clock), and brother of Robert Pansu, chemist and research director at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS).
