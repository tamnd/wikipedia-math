# Ronny Hadani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ronny Hadani"}

**Ronny Hadani** (רוני הדני) is an [Israeli-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli-American) mathematician, specializing in representation theory and harmonic analysis, with applications to signal processing.[^o3][^o4] He is known for developing [Orthogonal Time Frequency and Space (OTFS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_Time_Frequency_and_Space_%28OTFS%29) modulating techniques, a method used for making wireless [5G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G) communications faster,[^o5] that is also being considered for use in [6G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6G_%28network%29) technology.[^o6] The technology is being used by several wireless [5G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G) related companies and Cohere Technologies, a company he has co-founded.[^o7][^o8][^o20][^o9][^o10]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hadani received his MS degree in Computer Science from the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) in 1999 under the supervision of [David Harel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harel). He received his Ph.D. degree in Pure Mathematics from [Tel-Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel-Aviv_University) in 2006 under the supervision of [Joseph Bernstein](wikipedia://en/5815383).[^o3][^o4]

## Career {#Career}

### Academia {#Academia}

From the years 2006 to 2009, Hadani held an L.E. Dickson Postdoctoral Fellowship of Mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). Since 2009, he has been an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas, Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas,_Austin).[^o4][^o14][^o15]

### Cohere Technologies {#Cohere_Technologies}

In 2010, Hadani co-founded Cohere Technologies with Shlomo Rakib. The company is a Silicon Valley wireless startup that focuses on wireless improvements using OTFS and the Delay-Doppler model to improve FDD/TDD spectrum performance with channel detection, estimation, prediction, and precoding software for [4G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4G_LTE) and [5G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G) networks, compliant with 3GPP standards and O-RAN.[^o7][^o14][^o17]

In December 2022, during the inaugural 6G Evolution Summit event opening keynote, [Fierce Wireless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fierce_Wireless) moderator referred to Hadani as “The Father of OTFS.”[^o18]

## Patents {#Patents}

Hadani has been granted over 70 OTFS related patents, which include a communications method employing Orthogonal Time Frequency Space (OTFS) shifting and spectral shaping, which allows users to transmit and receive at least one frame of data (\[D\]) over a wireless communications link. He has also patented methods of operating and implementing wireless OTFS communications systems.[^o20][^o21][^o22] His OTFS technology has been tested by companies such as [C Spire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_Spire), 5TONIC,[^o22][^o23] [Telefónica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telefónica),[^o24] and [Deutsche Telekom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Telekom),[^o25][^o26]
