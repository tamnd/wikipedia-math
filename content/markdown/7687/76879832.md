# Anne Lemaître

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anne Lemaître"}

**Anne Lemaître** (born 1957) is a retired Belgian applied mathematician, formerly of the [Université de Namur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Namur). She is an expert in [orbital mechanics](wikipedia://en/182146) and [orbital resonance](wikipedia://en/22444), and their effects in the [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System) on bodies including [asteroids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid), [Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_%28planet%29), and [space debris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_debris).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lemaître completed her Ph.D. in 1984 at the Université de Namur. Her dissertation concerned [Kirkwood gaps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirkwood_gap), dips in [asteroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid) density caused by orbital resonances with [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter); it was supervised by Jacques Henrard. She is a professor emerita in the mathematics department of the Université de Namur.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Minor planet [7330 Annelemaître](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7330_Annelemaître) is named in her honor, "for her pioneering analytic studies of the dynamics of minor planets in mean-motion resonances".
