# Joseph Konhauser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Konhauser"}

**Joseph D. E. Konhauser** (1924 – February 1992) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Konhauser polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konhauser_polynomials). He was a professor at [Macalester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macalester_College).[^o2] He also organized many [mathematical problems](wikipedia://en/256700) competitions. The annual Konhauser Problemfest is named after him.

:::
