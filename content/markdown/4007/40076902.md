# Presymplectic form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Presymplectic form"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), especially [geometric mechanics](wikipedia://en/41499924), a **presymplectic form** is a [geometric structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_structure) on [differentiable manifolds](wikipedia://en/2119219). It is a generalization of [symplectic form](wikipedia://en/28356).

:::

Given a differentiable manifold, a symplectic form over it is [differential 2-form](wikipedia://en/221519) that is [closed](wikipedia://en/404181) and [nondegenerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondegenerate_quadratic_form). A presymplectic form relaxes the requirement for nondegeneracy. Instead, it is merely required to be closed and have constant [rank](wikipedia://en/26561) at all points on the manifold.[^o2] Note that a symplectic form, by virtue of nondegeneracy, necessarily have rank equaling the dimension of the underlying manifold, so it has constant rank.[^o3]

The definition is not standardized. Recently, Hajduk and Walczak defined a presymplectic form as a closed, differential 2-form, *of maximal rank on a manifold of odd dimension*.[^o4] This may be motivated thus: A symplectic form necessarily exists over a manifold of even dimension, so a manifold of odd dimension cannot have a symplectic form. However, it can at least attempt to reach a rank as high as possible, since a symplectic form, by virtue of nondegeneracy, necessarily have rank equaling the dimension of the underlying manifold, which is the maximal rank possible on the manifold.

::: {#o6 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Presymplectic forms have been used to study physical systems where there is no obvious symplectic geometry underlying it. Examples include dynamical systems with singular [Lagrangians](wikipedia://en/23371726), [Hamiltonian systems](wikipedia://en/1197531) with [constraints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_class_constraint), and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).[^o7]
