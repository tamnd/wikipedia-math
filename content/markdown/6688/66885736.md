### Foreigners in Indonesia {#Foreigners_in_Indonesia}

As of 27 January 2022, at least 6,190 foreigners were tested positive for COVID-19 in [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia), of which 5,840 recovered, 32 died, and 413 had returned to their respective countries or territories.[^o13]
