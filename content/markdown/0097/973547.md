# Crelle's Journal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crelle's Journal"}

Crell's Annalen

***Crelle's Journal***, or simply ***Crelle***, is the common name for ***Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*** ('Journal for Pure and Applied Mathematics'), a [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was founded by [August Leopold Crelle](wikipedia://en/973559) (Berlin) in 1826 and edited by him until his death in 1855. It was one of the first major mathematical journals that was not a proceedings of an [academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy).[^o4] It has published many notable papers, including works of [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573), [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216), [Gotthold Eisenstein](wikipedia://en/1452482), [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125)[^o5] and [Otto Hesse](wikipedia://en/740995). It was edited by [Carl Wilhelm Borchardt](wikipedia://en/482522) from 1856 to 1880, during which time it was known as *Borchardt's Journal*. The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Daniel Huybrechts](wikipedia://en/48322758) ([Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn)).[^o6]

## Past editors {#Past_editors}

- 1826–1856: [August Leopold Crelle](wikipedia://en/973559)
- 1856–1880: [Carl Wilhelm Borchardt](wikipedia://en/482522)
- 1881–1888: [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450), [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459)
- 1889–1892: Leopold Kronecker
- 1892–1902: [Lazarus Fuchs](wikipedia://en/2817087)
- 1903–1928: [Kurt Hensel](wikipedia://en/2242565)
- 1929–1933: Kurt Hensel, [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118), [Ludwig Schlesinger](wikipedia://en/26826869)
- 1934–1936: Kurt Hensel, Helmut Hasse
- 1937–1952: [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118)
- 1952–1977: Helmut Hasse, [Hans Rohrbach](wikipedia://en/51122295)
- 1977–1980: Helmut Hasse
