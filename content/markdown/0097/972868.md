# Hyperfinite type II factor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyperfinite type II factor"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), there are up to isomorphism exactly two separably acting [hyperfinite type II factors](wikipedia://en/294185); one infinite and one finite. Murray and von Neumann proved that up to [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) there is a unique [von Neumann algebra](wikipedia://en/294185) that is a [factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factor_%28functional_analysis%29) of type II<sub>1</sub> and also [hyperfinite](wikipedia://en/294185#Amenable_von_Neumann_algebras); it is called the **hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor**. There are an uncountable number of other factors of type II<sub>1</sub>. [Connes](wikipedia://en/340) proved that the infinite one is also unique.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

- The [von Neumann group algebra](wikipedia://en/294185#Examples) of a discrete group with the [infinite conjugacy class property](wikipedia://en/973790) is a factor of type II<sub>1</sub>, and if the group is [amenable](wikipedia://en/657430) and [countable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable) the factor is hyperfinite. There are many groups with these properties, as any [locally finite group](wikipedia://en/4939270) is amenable. For example, the von Neumann group algebra of the infinite symmetric group of all permutations of a countable infinite set that fix all but a finite number of elements gives the hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor.
- The hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor also arises from the [group-measure space construction](wikipedia://en/2749495#Construction) for ergodic free measure-preserving actions of countable amenable groups on probability spaces.
- The [infinite tensor product](wikipedia://en/294185#Tensor_products_of_von_Neumann_algebras) of a countable number of factors of type I<sub>*n*</sub> with respect to their tracial states is the hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor. When *n*=2, this is also sometimes called the Clifford algebra of an infinite separable Hilbert space.
- If *p* is any non-zero finite projection in a hyperfinite von Neumann algebra *A* of type II, then *pAp* is the hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor. Equivalently the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/294185#Type_II_factors) of *A* is the group of [positive real numbers](wikipedia://en/47172571). This can often be hard to see directly. It is, however, obvious when *A* is the infinite tensor product of factors of type I<sub>n</sub>, where n runs over all integers greater than 1 infinitely many times: just take *p* [equivalent](wikipedia://en/294185#Projections) to an infinite tensor product of projections *p*<sub>*n*</sub> on which the tracial state is either $1$ or $1- 1/n$.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The hyperfinite II<sub>1</sub> factor *R* is the unique smallest infinite dimensional factor in the following sense: it is contained in any other infinite dimensional factor, and any infinite dimensional factor contained in *R* is isomorphic to *R*.

The outer automorphism group of *R* is an infinite simple group with countable many conjugacy classes, indexed by pairs consisting of a positive integer *p* and a complex *p*th root of 1.

The projections of the hyperfinite II<sub>1</sub> factor form a [continuous geometry](wikipedia://en/32590824).

:::

## The infinite hyperfinite type II factor {#The_infinite_hyperfinite_type_II_factor}

While there are other factors of [type II<sub>∞</sub>](wikipedia://en/294185), there is a unique hyperfinite one, up to isomorphism. It consists of those infinite square matrices with entries in the hyperfinite type II<sub>1</sub> factor that define [bounded operators](wikipedia://en/455961).
