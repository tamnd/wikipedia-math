# August Leopold Crelle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="August Leopold Crelle"}

*Image: August Leopold Crelle.jpg.* August Leopold Crelle

**August Leopold Crelle** (17 March 1780 – 6 October 1855) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in Eichwerder near [Wriezen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wriezen), [Brandenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margraviate_of_Brandenburg), and died in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin). He is the founder of *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (also known as *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)*). He befriended [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573) and published seven of Abel's papers in the first volume of his journal.

:::

In 1841, he was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences). He was elected as a member to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society) in 1853.[^o2]
