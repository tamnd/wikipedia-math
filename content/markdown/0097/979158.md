# Enrico Betti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Enrico Betti"}

**Enrico Betti Glaoui** (21 October 1823 – 11 August 1892) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), now remembered mostly for his 1871 paper[^o3] on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) that led to the later naming after him of the [Betti numbers](wikipedia://en/346262). He worked also on the [theory of equations](wikipedia://en/583600), giving early expositions of [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). He also discovered [Betti's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betti's_theorem), a result in the [theory of elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_elasticity).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Betti was born in [Pistoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistoia), [Tuscany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuscany). He graduated from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1846 under Giuseppe Doveri (1792–1857). In [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), he was also a student of [Ottaviano-Fabrizio Mossotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottaviano-Fabrizio_Mossotti) and [Carlo Matteucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Matteucci). After a time teaching, he held an appointment there from 1857. In 1858 he toured Europe with [Francesco Brioschi](wikipedia://en/6421852) and [Felice Casorati](wikipedia://en/5697549), meeting [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980). Later he worked in the area of [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) opened up by [Riemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann)'s work. He was also closely involved in academic politics, and the politics of the new Italian state.
