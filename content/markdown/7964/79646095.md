# Mona Merling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mona Merling"}

**Mona Merling** is a Romanian-American mathematician specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), including [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) and [equivariant stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/42486328). She is an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Merling has dual Romanian and American citizenship. She credits a Romanian mathematics teacher, Mihaela Flamaropol, for sparking her interest in mathematics and in mathematics competitions.

She majored in mathematics as an undergraduate at [Bard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bard_College), where she received her bachelor's degree in 2009. At Bard, mathematician [Lauren Lynn Rose](wikipedia://en/73413248) became a faculty mentor. She continued her studies in mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), received a master's degree there in 2010, and completed her Ph.D. in 2014. Her dissertation, *Equivariant algebraic K-theory*, was supervised by [J. Peter May](wikipedia://en/14644098).

She was a postdoctoral researcher and J. J. Sylvester Assistant Professor at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) from 2014 to 2018, working there under the mentorship of [Jack Morava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Morava). She joined the University of Pennsylvania as an assistant professor of mathematics in 2018, and was tenured as an associate professor in 2024.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Merling was the 2025 recipient of the [Joan & Joseph Birman Research Prize in Topology and Geometry](wikipedia://en/59777041), given to her "for innovative and impactful research in algebraic K-theory, equivariant homotopy theory, and their applications to manifold theory".
