# 1911 United Kingdom census boycotters

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1911 United Kingdom census boycotters"}

*Image: Suffragettes boycotting 1911 census in Manchester.jpg.* Suffragettes boycotting 1911 census in [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester)[^o2]

These [British suffragettes and suffragists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_suffrage_in_the_United_Kingdom) are known to have participated in a [boycott of the 1911 United Kingdom census](wikipedia://en/54477956#Suffragette_boycott).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] The number of boycotters has been estimated to have been in the thousands, but was probably fewer than 100,000 in total.[^o5]

:::

Notable women who participated in the boycott include:

- [Laura Ainsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Ainsworth), 38 other women and one man [sought to hide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Ainsworth#If_women_don't_count,_then_they_were_not_going_to_be_counted) in [Jezreel's Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezreel's_Tower) in [Gillingham, Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillingham,_Kent), but they were betrayed and still counted by census officials.[^o7][^o8]
- [Rhoda Anstey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhoda_Anstey): participated on behalf of the [Anstey College of Physical Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anstey_College_of_Physical_Education), which she had founded in 1897[^o9][^o10][^o11]
- [Helen Archdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Archdale): hosted a mass census boycott party with Adela Pankhurst[^o48]
- [Hertha Ayrton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertha_Ayrton): hosted a boycott party for approximately 40 people at her home in 41 Norfolk Square.[^o12] Wrote on census form: "How can I answer all these questions if I have not the intelligence to choose between two candidates or parliament? I will not supply these particulars until I have my rights as a citizen. Votes for Women."[^o5]
- [Minnie Baldock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnie_Baldock): did not appear on the census, although her husband and sons were enumerated[^o13]
- [Inez Bensusan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inez_Bensusan): member of the [Actresses' Franchise League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actresses'_Franchise_League) who performed during a mass evasion party[^o14]
- [Rosa May Billinghurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_May_Billinghurst)
- [Mary Blathwayt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Blathwayt)
- Dorothy Bowker: wrote: "Dumb Politically, Blind to the Census, Deaf to the Enumerator. Being classed with criminals lunatics & paupers I prefer to give no further particulars*"*[^o22][^o15]
- [Nina Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Boyle): wrote "No Votes. No Census. Votes for Women" on her census form[^o16]
- [Georgina Brackenbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Brackenbury): hosted an evasion party for 25 women and 1 man.
- [Marie Brackenbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Brackenbury): hosted an evasion party for 25 women and 1 man. Wrote on the census form: "Miss Marie Brackenbury in charge takes this opportunity of registering her protest against the votelessness of the women of Great Britain by refusing to fill in this form."[^o17]

- [Charlotte Despard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Despard)[^o27]
