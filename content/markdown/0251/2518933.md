# Star product

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Star product"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **star product** is a method of combining [graded posets](wikipedia://en/2514970) with unique minimal and maximal elements, preserving the property that the posets are [Eulerian](wikipedia://en/26703335).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The star product of two [graded posets](wikipedia://en/2514970) $(P,\le_P)$ and $(Q,\le_Q)$, where $P$ has a unique [maximal element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximal_element) $\widehat{1}$ and $Q$ has a unique minimal element $\widehat{0}$, is a [poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poset) $P*Q$ on the set $(P\setminus\{\widehat{1}\})\cup(Q\setminus\{\widehat{0}\})$. We define the [partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order) $\le_{P*Q}$ by $x\le y$ if and only if:

\1. $\{x,y\}\subset P$, and $x\le_P y$;

\2. $\{x,y\}\subset Q$, and $x\le_Q y$; or

\3. $x\in P$ and $y\in Q$.

In other words, we pluck out the top of $P$ and the bottom of $Q$, and require that everything in $P$ be smaller than everything in $Q$.

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

The star product of [Eulerian posets](wikipedia://en/26703335) is Eulerian.
