# Subbundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Subbundle"}

*Image: Subbundle.png.* A subbundle $L$ of a vector bundle $E$ over a topological space $M$.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **subbundle** $L$ of a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) $E$ over a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $M$ is a subset of $E$ such that for each $x$ in $M,$ the set $L_x$, the intersection of the fiber $E_x$ with $L$, is a vector subspace of the fiber $E_x$ so that $L$ is a vector bundle over $M$ in its own right.

:::

In connection with [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) theory, a subbundle of the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) of a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) may be called a [distribution](wikipedia://en/2976052) (of [tangent vectors](wikipedia://en/191999)).

If locally, in a neighborhood $N_x$ of $x \in M$, a set of vector fields $Y_k$ [span](wikipedia://en/56353) the vector spaces $L_y, y \in N_x,$ and all [Lie commutators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_commutator) $\left[Y_i, Y_j\right]$ are linear combinations of $Y_1, \dots, Y_n$ then one says that $L$ is an [involutive distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involutive_distribution).
