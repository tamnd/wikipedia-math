# Henryk Struve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henryk Struve"}

*Image: Henryk Struve portret by Maleszewski.jpg.* Henryk Struve, portrait by Tytus Maleszewski, 1876

**Henryk Struve** (also known as **Florian Gąsiorowski**) (1840–1912) was a Polish philosopher. He has been called "perhaps the most remarkable person in logic in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) in the 19th century".[^o2] Struve taught philosophy at [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University) from 1862 to 1903.[^o3] He wrote works in Polish, German and Russian.

:::
