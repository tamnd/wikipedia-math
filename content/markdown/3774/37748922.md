# Adolf Piltz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Piltz"}

*Image: Piltz\_1884\_p40.png.* In Piltz's habilitation thesis (1884) the generalized Riemann hypothesis is mentioned.

**Adolf Piltz** (8 December 1855[^o3] – 1940) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed to number theory. Piltz was arguably the first to formulate a [generalized Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/152567), in 1884.[^o4]

:::
