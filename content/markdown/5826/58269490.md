# Nicholas Young (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicholas Young"}

**Nicholas John Young** is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [several complex variables](wikipedia://en/5759). He is a [research professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_professor) at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds).[^o2] Much of his work has been about the interaction of operator theory and [function theory](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o3]

:::

## Publications {#Publications}

Young has written more than a hundred papers,[^o5] over 30 of them in collaboration with [Jim Agler](wikipedia://en/50605185).[^o6][^o7] He is the author of the book *An Introduction to [Hilbert Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_Space)*.[^o8]

His Ph.D. adviser was [Vlastimil Pták](wikipedia://en/3116511), and he has had 5 Ph.D. students.[^o9]
