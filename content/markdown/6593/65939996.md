# Thomas Forster (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Forster"}

**Thomas Edward Forster** (born 12 April 1948) is a British [set theorist](wikipedia://en/27553) and philosopher. His work has focused on [Quine](wikipedia://en/33833)'s [New Foundations](wikipedia://en/945957), the theory of [well-quasi-orders](wikipedia://en/739775) and [better-quasi-orders](wikipedia://en/35727092), and various topics in philosophy.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Forster completed a PhD at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1977, with the dissertation *NF* on [New Foundations](wikipedia://en/945957), jointly supervised by [Adrian Mathias](wikipedia://en/63066808) and Maurice Boffa.[^o6]

Forster is an Affiliated Lecturer at [DPMMS](wikipedia://en/3254271), Cambridge,[^o7] a [bye-fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye-Fellow) at [Queens' College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge),[^o8] and holds honorary appointments for many other organisations worldwide, including the [Center for Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Philosophy_of_Science) in Pittsburgh, the Centre National de Recherches de Logique in Belgium, and the Centre for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).[^o3] Amongst his undergraduate supervisees are [Phebe Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phebe_Mann), [Rosi Sexton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosi_Sexton), [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931), [Rebecca Kitteridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Kitteridge), [Doug Gurr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Gurr), [Sarah Flannery](wikipedia://en/360380) and [Ursula Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ursula_Martin).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Forster was awarded the [J.T. Knight Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize) as a PhD student at [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1974.[^o3] His article "The Iterative Conception of Set"[^o10] was recognised by the [Philosophers' Annual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher's_Annual) as one of the ten best philosophy articles of 2008.[^o11]

## Books {#Books}

Forster's books include:

- *Quine's New Foundations: An Introduction* (Cabay, 1983)[^o13]
- *Set Theory with a Universal Set: Exploring an Untyped Universe* (Clarendon, 1992; 2nd ed., 1996)[^o14]
- *Reasoning about Theoretical Entities* (World Scientific, 2003)[^o15]
- *Logic, Induction and Sets* (Cambridge University Press, 2003)[^o16]
