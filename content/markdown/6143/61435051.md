# Sharon Lohr

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sharon Lohr"}

**Sharon Lynn Lohr** is an American statistician. She is an Emeritus Dean’s Distinguished Professor of Statistics at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University), and an independent statistical consultant. Her research interests include [survey sampling](wikipedia://en/27578), [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541), and applications of statistics in education and criminology.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lohr graduated from [Calvin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_College) in 1982. She completed her Ph.D. in statistics in 1987 at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). Her dissertation, *Accurate Multivariate Estimation Using Double and Triple Sampling*, was supervised by Mark Finster.

After retiring from Arizona State, she served a five-year term as vice president and senior statistician at [Westat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westat).

## Books {#Books}

Lohr is the author of:

- *Sampling: Design and Analysis* (Duxbury Press, 1999; 2nd ed., Cengage/CRC Press, 2010)
- *Measuring Crime: Behind the Statistics* (CRC Press, 2019)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lohr is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). In 2003 she became the inaugural winner of the Gertrude M. Cox Award of the Washington Statistical Society.
