# Apoorva Khare

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Apoorva Khare"}

**Apoorva Khare** ([Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language): अपूर्व खरे, born 1980[^o10]) is an Indian mathematician who works in matrix positivity and analysis, combinatorics and discrete mathematics, and representation theory. He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, for the year 2022 in Mathematical Sciences.[^o4][^o5]

:::

Khare did his schooling in [Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar), Odisha until 1997, at [Demonstration Multipurpose School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonstration_Multipurpose_School) and [Buxi Jagabandhu Bidyadhar College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buxi_Jagabandhu_Bidyadhar_College). He obtained his B.Stat. (2000) from the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) at Kolkata, and his M.S. (2001) and Ph.D. (2006) in Mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). He then held postdoctoral positions at the [University of California at Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Riverside) and [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University),[^o6] and a Research Associateship at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) before joining the [Indian Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science) in Bangalore, where he is currently a Professor in Mathematics.[^o7]

Besides the Bhatnagar Prize, Khare is also a recipient of the [Swarnajayanti Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarnajayanti_Fellowship) and the Ramanujan Fellowship from SERB/DST, Govt. of India. He was invited to deliver the 2022 Hansraj Gupta Memorial Award Lecture[^o8] and was awarded the A.K. Agarwal Award 2023 and (jointly) the B.N. Waphare Award 2024[^o9] by the Indian Mathematical Society.

Khare has been elected a Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o10] In a publication by the Government of India celebrating 75 years of Indian independence, Khare was listed as one of the 75 scientists aged under 50 who are "shaping today's India".[^o11] He also delivered a plenary talk (the ILAS Invited Address) at the 2023 AMS Joint Mathematics Meetings in Boston, USA.[^o12] In 2024, Khare became a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*. In 2025, he received the Ganit Ratna Award from Professor Thakare Gaurav Sanstha, and was also selected as a Simons Associate of the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics).
