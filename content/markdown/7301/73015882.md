# Margaret Sullivan Pepe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Sullivan Pepe"}

**Margaret Patricia O'Sullivan Pepe** (born June 24, 1961) is an Irish biostatistician specializing in the evaluation of tests and biomarkers for disease screening. She is a professor of biostatistics at the [University of Washington School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_School_of_Public_Health) and a researcher at the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Pepe was born June 24, 1961, in [Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_%28city%29), Ireland to Seamus O'Sullivan.[^o5] She attended [Mount Mercy College, Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Mercy_College,_Cork).[^o5] She completed a B.Sc. in mathematics science at the [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork) in 1981.[^o4] Pepe earned a M.S. in statistics in 1984 and a Ph.D. in biostatistics at the [University of Washington School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_School_of_Public_Health) in 1986.[^o4] Her dissertation was titled, *A new class of statistics for the two-sample survival analysis problem*.[^o5] [Thomas R. Fleming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_R._Fleming) was her doctoral advisor.

In 1997, she won the [Mortimer Spiegelman Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortimer_Spiegelman_Award).[^o7]

Pepe is a professor of biostatistics at the University of Washington School of Public Health.[^o4] She is a researcher at the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center).[^o4]
