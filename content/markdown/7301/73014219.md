# Ashkan Nikeghbali

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ashkan Nikeghbali"}

**Ashkan Nikeghbali Cisakht[^o3]** (اشکان نیک‌اقبالی; born 1975[^o3]) is a mathematician and university professor . He holds the chair of Financial Mathematics at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o4]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Nikeghbali obtained his PhD at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 2005 with the thesis "Temps aléatoires, filtrations et sous-martingales: quelques développements récents", supervised by [Marc Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor).[^o6] Prior to that, he was a researcher from February 2004 to July 2004 at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) on the topic of "Random matrix approaches in number theory."[^o7] After completing his PhD, Nikeghbali first worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005) (under the direction of Brian Conrey) at [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester).[^o8] In June 2006, he was appointed Heinz-Hopf Lecturer at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). In March 2007, Nikeghbali was appointed assistant professor at the Institute of Mathematics at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich). He was promoted to Extraordinary Professor of Applied Mathematics there in 2009.

## Research {#Research}

Nikeghbali's research focuses on [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).[^o4] In particular, he works in the areas of [portfolio theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portfolio_theory), [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), [random operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_operator) and [matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765), and the application of stochastics in number theory and combinatorics.

In [asymptotic probability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_probability_theory), Nikeghbali, together with Féray, Méliot, and Kowalski introduced the concept of [Mod-Ф convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mod-Ф_convergence). In number theory, he, together with Chhaibi and Najnudel, introduced the concept of [stochastic zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_zeta_function).

Nikeghbali supervised at least six PhD students during his time at the University of Zurich.[^o6]

## Other activities {#Other_activities}

Member of the [world.minds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World.minds) community since 2008. Member of the Scientific Advisory Board of [swissQuant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SwissQuant).[^o4] Member of the advisory board of [EVMTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EVMTech).[^o4] Strategy advisor for data analysis and modeling of stochastic processes at [Roche Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roche_Holding) in Basel.

## Awards {#Awards}

Honorary doctorate from the [University of Alba Iulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alba_Iulia) in Romania.[^o4]
