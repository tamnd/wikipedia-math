# Ker-Chau Li

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ker-Chau Li"}

**Ker-Chau Li** () is a Taiwanese statistician.

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1975, Li graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.S.) degree in mathematics. He then pursued graduate study in statistics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he earned his [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (M.S.) in 1979 and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1981.[^o4] Li's doctoral dissertation, titled "Contributions to Robust Design and Estimation Problems," was advised by [Jack Kiefer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Kiefer_%28statistician%29).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Li began his teaching career as an assistant professor in the statistics department of [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), then joined the mathematics department of the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1984. He was promoted to a full professorship in 1989, and transferred to the newly established UCLA statistics department in 1999. In 2009, Li was appointed a distinguished professor.[^o8][^o9] In his native Taiwan, Li served as editor of the *[Statistica Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistica_Sinica)*, and from 2006, served as director and distinguished research fellow of [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica)'s Institute of Statistical Science,[^o10][^o8][^o11] stepping down from the former position in 2012.[^o11]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Li was elected a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1989,[^o8][^o4] awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1993,[^o13] and elected member of [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) and fellow of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2012 and 2014, respectively.[^o11][^o14]
