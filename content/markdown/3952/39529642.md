# Centered icosahedral number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Centered icosahedral number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **centered icosahedral numbers** also known as **cuboctahedral numbers** are a sequence of numbers, describing two different representations for these numbers as three-dimensional [figurate numbers](wikipedia://en/325260). As centered icosahedral numbers, they are [centered numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centered_number) representing points arranged in the shape of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). As cuboctahedral numbers, they represent points arranged in the shape of a [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), and are a [magic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_%28chemistry%29) for the [face-centered cubic lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-centered_cubic_lattice). The centered icosahedral number for a specific $n$ is given by

$$\frac{(2n+1)\left(5n^2+5n+3\right)}{3}.$$
