# Wolstenholme number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolstenholme number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Wolstenholme number** is a [number](wikipedia://en/21474) that is the [numerator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerator) of the [generalized harmonic number](wikipedia://en/214729#Generalized_harmonic_numbers) *H*<sub>*n*,2</sub>.

:::

The first such numbers are 1, 5, 49, 205, 5269, 5369, 266681, 1077749, ... .

These numbers are named after [Joseph Wolstenholme](wikipedia://en/98915), who proved [Wolstenholme's theorem](wikipedia://en/1113185) on modular relations of the generalized harmonic numbers.
