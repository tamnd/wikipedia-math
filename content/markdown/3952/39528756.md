# Arakelyan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arakelyan"}

**Arakelyan** (Առաքելյան), also transliterated as **Arakelian**, is an Armenian surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

**Arakelyan**

- [Alik Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alik_Arakelyan) (Արայիկի Առաքելյան) (born 1996), Armenian footballer
- [Ararat Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ararat_Arakelyan) (Արարատ Առաքելյան) (born 1984), Armenian footballer
- [Babken Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babken_Arakelyan) (Բաբկեն Առաքելյան) (1912–2004), Armenian historian
- [Eduard Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Arakelyan) (Էդուարդ Առաքելյան) (born 1950), Armenian painter
- [Karine Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karine_Arakelyan) (Կարինե Առաքելյան) (born 1979), Armenian television presenter
- [Tigran Arakelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigran_Arakelyan), Armenian-American conductor

**Arakelian**

- [Artashes Arakelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artashes_Arakelian) (Արտաշես Առաքելյան) (1909–1993), Armenian economist
- [Hambardzum Arakelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hambardzum_Arakelian) (Համբարձում Առաքելյան) (1865–1918), Armenian journalist, writer and activist
- [Marat Arakelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marat_Arakelian) (Մարատ Արսեն Առաքելյան) (1929–1983), Armenian astrophysicist
- [Narine Arakelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narine_Arakelian) (born 1979), Armenian interdisciplinary feminist artist
- [Norair Arakelian](wikipedia://en/48552839) (Սենեքերիմ Առաքելյան) (1936–2023), Armenian and Soviet mathematician
- [Senekerim Arakelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senekerim_Arakelian) (Նորայր Հունանի Առաքելյան) (1902–2000), Armenian-American Holocaust survivor
