# Bryna Kra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bryna Kra"}

**Bryna Rebekah Kra** (born 1966) is an American mathematician and the Sarah Rebecca Roland Professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). Kra served as the president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) from 2023 to 2025. She is a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) and the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences). Her academic work centered on [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986). In particular Kra has made significant contributions to the structure theory of characteristic factors for multiple ergodic averages.[^o3] This work has allowed her to address problems in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kra was born in 1966 in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston).[^o6] She graduated with a bachelor's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1988, and obtained her Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1995 under the guidance of [Yitzhak Katznelson](wikipedia://en/5643045). She held postdoctoral positions at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) in Jerusalem, the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), the [IHÉS](wikipedia://en/14527) and the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) before joining the mathematics faculty at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) as an assistant professor. Since 2004 Kra has been a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), where she was department chair from 2009 to 2012.

## Book {#Book}

With Bernard Host, Kra is the author of the book *Nilpotent Structures in Ergodic Theory* ([Mathematical Surveys and Monographs](wikipedia://en/28100142) 236, [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2018).

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2010 Kra was awarded the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) for her expository article "The [Green–Tao theorem](wikipedia://en/7006166) on arithmetic progressions in the primes: an ergodic point of view".[^o10][^o11] In 2006 she was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) ("From combinatorics to ergodic theory and back again"),[^o12] and was named an AMS Centennial Fellow the same year.[^o13] In 2012 she became a fellow of the American Mathematical Society.[^o14] In 2016 she became a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o15] In 2019, she was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o16] She was named a 2021 [Simons Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) in Mathematics.[^o17] Kra was elected to the 2023 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) "for her vision and work creating programs to support women in mathematics, especially GROW (Graduate Research Opportunities for Women) and AWM student chapters; for her leadership in the mathematics community, including serving on the AWM Executive Committee and serving as president of AMS; and for making advocacy for women a priority throughout her career."[^o18] Kra was elected president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2021 and she served as president from February 2023 to January 2025.[^o19]

## Family relations {#Family_relations}

Kra is the daughter of mathematician [Irwin Kra](wikipedia://en/35432060).[^o21]
