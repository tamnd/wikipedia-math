# Moedomo Soedigdomarto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moedomo Soedigdomarto"}

**Moedomo Soedigdomarto**, also spelled **Mudomo Sudigdomarto**, (29 November 1927, [Magetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magetan_Regency) – 5 November 2005, [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung)) was an Indonesian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the [Bandung Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung_Institute_of_Technology), of which he was [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Soedigdomarto was one of the first Indonesians to obtain a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics, which he earned from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), with a dissertation titled "A Representation Theory for the Laplace Transform of Vector-Valued Functions", in 1959 at the age of 32, under the orientation of [Robert Gardner Bartle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gardner_Bartle).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

Soedigdomarto was the first Indonesian to have a paper recorded in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* (Moedomo and J. J. Uhl, Jr. "Radon-Nikodym theorems for the Bochner and Pettis integrals" published in the *[Pacific Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Journal_of_Mathematics)* in 1971).[^o2][^o3]
