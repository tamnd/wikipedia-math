# Bruce G. Lindsay

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce G. Lindsay"}

**Bruce George Lindsay** (March 7, 1947 – May 5, 2015) was an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) best known for his contributions to [mixture modeling](wikipedia://en/871681) and [likelihood theory](wikipedia://en/44968).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Lindsay was born in 1947 in [The Dalles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dalles), [Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon). He earned a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) in 1969 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [biomathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomathematics) from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1978.[^o4] Between his undergraduate and graduate studies he has served in the [U.S. Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Coast_Guard) for four years during the [Vietnam War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War).[^o5] He joined the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) faculty in 1979, heading the Department of Statistics in 1998–2000 and 2006–2012 and rising to the rank of Eberly Chair in Statistics in 2012.[^o4] He was also a visiting professor at the [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in 1987, the [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1990, and [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University) in 2004–2005.[^o4] He was elected to be a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1987, of the [Guggenheim Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Simon_Guggenheim_Memorial_Foundation) in 1996, and of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1998.[^o4]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Lindsay, Bruce G. "The geometry of mixture likelihoods: a general theory." The Annals of Statistics 11.1 (1983): 86–94.
- Lindsay, Bruce G. "Composite likelihood methods." Contemporary Mathematics 80.1 (1988): 221–39.
- Lindsay, Bruce G. "Efficiency versus robustness: the case for minimum Hellinger distance and related methods." The Annals of Statistics 22.2 (1994): 1081–1114.
- Lindsay, Bruce G. "The geometry of mixture likelihoods, part II: the exponential family." The Annals of Statistics 11.3 (1983): 783–792.
