# Clifford Clogg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Clifford Clogg"}

**Clifford Collier Clogg** (October 16, 1949 – May 7, 1995) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [sociologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociologist), [demographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographer), and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He is best known for his contributions to [population statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_statistics), [categorical data analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_data_analysis), and [latent class analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_class_analysis).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Clogg was born in 1949 in [Oberlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin,_Ohio), [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio). He earned a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in sociology in 1971 from [Ohio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_University), and continued his studies at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he earned an [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in sociology and an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in statistics in 1974, and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in sociology in 1977. Starting in 1976 he served at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) faculty, rising to the rank of Distinguished Professor of Sociology and Professor of Statistics.[^o4][^o5]

He served as an editor of several journals, including the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)*, *[Demography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demography_%28journal%29)*, *[Sociological Methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociological_Methodology)*, and the *[Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics](wikipedia://en/19685051)*.[^o4]

The Clifford C. Clogg Award of the [Population Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_Association_of_America),[^o6] the Clifford Clogg Award of the Methodology Section of the [American Sociological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Sociological_Association),[^o7] and the Clifford C. Clogg Scholarship of the [Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-university_Consortium_for_Political_and_Social_Research)[^o8] are named in his honor.
