# Ruggiero Torelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruggiero Torelli"}

**Ruggiero Torelli** (7 June 1884 in [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples) – 9 September 1915) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Torelli's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torelli's_theorem), a classical result of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) over the [complex number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_number_field).

:::
