# Matrix factorization (algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matrix factorization"}

In [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **matrix factorization** is a tool used to study infinitely long [resolutions](wikipedia://en/19745659), generally over [commutative rings](wikipedia://en/61346).

:::

## Motivation {#Motivation}

One of the problems with non-smooth algebras, such as [Artin algebras](wikipedia://en/29353834), are their [derived categories](wikipedia://en/985897) are poorly behaved due to infinite [projective resolutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_resolution). For example, in the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) $R = \mathbb{C}[x]/(x^2)$ there is an infinite resolution of the $R$-[module](wikipedia://en/276410) $\mathbb{C}$ where

> $$\cdots \xrightarrow{\cdot x} R \xrightarrow{\cdot x} R \xrightarrow{\cdot x} R \to \mathbb{C} \to 0$$

Instead of looking at only the derived category of the module category, [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845)[^o3] studied such resolutions by looking at their periodicity. In general, such resolutions are periodic with period $2$ after finitely many objects in the resolution.

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

For a commutative ring $S$ and an element $f \in S$, a **matrix factorization** of $f$ is a pair of *n*-by-*n* matrices $A,B$ such that $AB = f \cdot \text{Id}_n$. This can be encoded more generally as a $\mathbb{Z}/2$-[graded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graded_module) $S$-module $M = M_0\oplus M_1$ with an [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569)

> $$d = \begin{bmatrix}0 & d_1 \\ d_0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

such that $d^2 = f \cdot \text{Id}_M$.

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

### Examples {#Examples}

(1) For $S = \mathbb{C}[[x]]$ and $f = x^n$ there is a matrix factorization $d_0:S \rightleftarrows S:d_1$ where $d_0=x^i, d_1 = x^{n-i}$ for $0 \leq i \leq n$.

(2) If $S = \mathbb{C}[[x,y,z]]$ and $f = xy + xz + yz$, then there is a matrix factorization $d_0:S^2 \rightleftarrows S^2:d_1$ where

> $$d_0 = \begin{bmatrix} z & y \\ x & -x-y \end{bmatrix} \text{ } d_1 = \begin{bmatrix} x+y & y \\ x & -z \end{bmatrix}$$

:::

### Main theorem {#Main_theorem}

Given a [regular local ring](wikipedia://en/506959) $R$ and an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) $I \subset R$ generated by an $A$-sequence, set $B = A/I$ and let

$$\cdots \to F_2 \to F_1 \to F_0 \to 0$$

be a minimal $B$-free resolution of the ground [field](wikipedia://en/10603). Then $F_\bullet$ becomes periodic after at most $1 + \text{dim}(B)$ steps. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Jo5eCv9ZVY
