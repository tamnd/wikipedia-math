The **compound of two snub icosidodecadodecahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809), composed of the 2 [enantiomers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomers) of the [snub icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157687). Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform truncated icosidodecahedron.
