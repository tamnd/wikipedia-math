::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of two snub cubes"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of the 2 [enantiomers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomers) of the [snub cube](wikipedia://en/180249). As a holosnub, it is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) βr{4,3} and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) .

:::

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of this compound is shared by a convex nonuniform [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879), having rectangular faces, alongside irregular [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and [octagons](wikipedia://en/314575), each alternating with two edge lengths.

Together with its convex hull, it represents the snub cube-first projection of the nonuniform [snub cubic antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_cuboctahedral_prism#Related_polytopes).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) are all the [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of

(±1, ±*ξ*, ±1/*ξ*)

where *ξ* is the real solution to

$$\xi^3+\xi^2+\xi=1, \,$$

which can be written

$$\xi = \frac{1}{3}\left(\sqrt[3]{17+3\sqrt{33}} - \sqrt[3]{-17+3\sqrt{33}} - 1\right)$$

or approximately 0.543689. ξ is the reciprocal of the [tribonacci constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribonacci_constant).

Equally, the tribonacci constant, *t*, just like the [snub cube](wikipedia://en/180249#Cartesian_coordinates), can compute the coordinates as the permutations of:

(±1, ±$\tfrac{1}{t}$, ±*t*)
