::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of two snub dodecahedra"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of the 2 [enantiomers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomers) of the [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137).

:::

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of this compound is shared by a convex nonuniform [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534), with rectangular faces, alongside irregular [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393), each alternating two different edge lengths.

Together with its convex hull, it represents the snub dodecahedron-first projection of the nonuniform [snub dodecahedral antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_icosidodecahedral_prism#Related_polytopes).
