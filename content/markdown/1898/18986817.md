# William Floyd (mathematician)

*Image: Dr. Floyd.png.* Dr. Floyd discusses languages over the integer lattice.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Floyd"}

**William J. Floyd** is an American mathematician specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is currently a professor at [Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Polytechnic_Institute_and_State_University). Floyd received a PhD in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) 1978 under the direction of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190).[^o2]

:::

## Mathematical contributions {#Mathematical_contributions}

Most of Floyd's research is in the areas of [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732) and [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).

Floyd and [Allen Hatcher](wikipedia://en/1112366) classified all the incompressible [surfaces](wikipedia://en/27865) in punctured-[torus](wikipedia://en/74800) [bundles](wikipedia://en/827658) over the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220).[^o4]

In a 1980 paper[^o5] Floyd introduced a way to compactify a [finitely generated group](wikipedia://en/963042) by adding to it a boundary which came to be called the **Floyd boundary**.[^o6][^o7] Floyd also wrote a number of joint papers with [James W. Cannon](wikipedia://en/19404886) and Walter R. Parry exploring a combinatorial approach to the [Cannon conjecture](wikipedia://en/19404886#1990s_and_2000s:_Automatic_groups,_discrete_conformal_geometry_and_Cannon's_conjecture)[^o8][^o9][^o10] using [finite subdivision rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_subdivision_rules). This represents one of the few plausible lines of attack of the conjecture.[^o11]
