# Robert Smith (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Smith"}

**Robert Smith** (c. 16 October 1689 – 2 February 1768) was an English mathematician.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Smith was probably born at Lea near [Gainsborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gainsborough,_Lincolnshire), the son of John Smith, the rector of [Gate Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gate_Burton), Lincolnshire and his wife Hannah Cotes. After attending Queen Elizabeth's Grammar School, Gainsborough (now [Queen Elizabeth's High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth's_High_School)) he entered [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), in 1708, and becoming minor fellow in 1714, major fellow in 1715 and senior fellow in 1739, was chosen Master in 1742, in succession to [Richard Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Bentley). From 1716 to 1760 he was [Plumian Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumian_Professor_of_Astronomy_and_Experimental_Philosophy) of [Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), and he died in the Master's Lodge at Trinity.

In February 1719 he was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o5]

Besides editing two works by his cousin, [Roger Cotes](wikipedia://en/236798), who was his predecessor in the Plumian chair, he published *A Compleat System of Opticks* in 1738, which gained him the sobriquet of Old Focus, and *Harmonics, or the Philosophy of Musical Sounds* in 1749.

Smith never married but lived with his unmarried sister Elzimar (1683–1758) in the lodge at Trinity College. Although he is often portrayed as a rather reclusive character, [John Byrom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Byrom)'s journal shows that in the 1720s and 1730s Smith could be quite sociable. Yet ill health, particularly gout, took its toll and severely inhibited his academic work and social activities. He died at the lodge on 2 February 1768, and on 8 February he was buried in [Trinity College Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Chapel,_Cambridge), the funeral oration being delivered by [Thomas Zouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Zouch).

According to the *Oxford Dictionary of National Biography*, Smith helped to spread [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627)'s ideas in Europe and "Newton's successes in optics and mechanics dominated Smith's scientific career".

## The Smith Fund {#The_Smith_Fund}

In his will Smith left £3500 South Sea stock to the University of Cambridge. The net income on the fund is annually divided equally between the [Smith's Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize) and the stipend of the [Plumian Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumian_Professor_of_Astronomy_and_Experimental_Philosophy).
