# De Morgan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Morgan"}

**De Morgan** or **de Morgan** is a surname, and may refer to:

:::

- [Augustus De Morgan](wikipedia://en/18949289) (1806–1871), British mathematician and logician
  - [De Morgan's laws](wikipedia://en/64669) (or De Morgan's theorem), a set of rules from propositional logic
  - The [De Morgan Medal](wikipedia://en/1096532), a triennial mathematics prize awarded by the London Mathematical Society
- [Campbell De Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campbell_De_Morgan) (1811–1876), British surgeon
- [William De Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_De_Morgan) (1839–1917), English designer, potter, ceramics-worker, and novelist
- [Mary De Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_De_Morgan) (1850–1907), English author of literary fairytales
- [Evelyn De Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evelyn_De_Morgan) (1855–1919), English pre-Raphaelite painter
- [Jacques de Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_de_Morgan) (1857–1924), French archaeologist
