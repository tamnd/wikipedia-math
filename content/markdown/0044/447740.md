# Minkowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minkowski"}

**Minkowski**, **Mińkowski** or **Minkovski** (Slavic feminine: **Minkowska**, **Mińkowska** or **Minkovskaya**; plural: **Minkowscy**, **Mińkowscy**; מינקובסקי, Минковский) is a surname of Polish origin. It may refer to:

:::

- Minkowski or Mińkowski, a coat of arms of Polish nobility
- [Alyona Minkovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alyona_Minkovski) (born 1986), Russian-American correspondent and presenter
- [Eugène Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugène_Minkowski) (1885–1972), French psychiatrist
- [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537) (1864–1909) Russian-born German mathematician and physicist, known for:
  - [Minkowski addition](wikipedia://en/504105)
  - [Minkowski–Bouligand dimension](wikipedia://en/601025)
  - [Minkowski diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_diagram)
  - [Minkowski distance](wikipedia://en/20326689)
  - [Minkowski functional](wikipedia://en/3510908)
  - [Minkowski inequality](wikipedia://en/192022)
  - [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space)
    - [Null vector (Minkowski space)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_vector_%28Minkowski_space%29)
  - [Minkowski plane](wikipedia://en/4500167)
  - [Minkowski's theorem](wikipedia://en/53455)
  - [Minkowski's question mark function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski's_question_mark_function)
  - [Abraham–Minkowski controversy](wikipedia://en/1190648)
  - [Hasse–Minkowski theorem](wikipedia://en/1109126)
  - [Minkowski separation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separating_axis_theorem)
  - [Smith–Minkowski–Siegel mass formula](wikipedia://en/13362584)
- [Christopher Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Minkowski) (born 1953), American Indologist
- [Khristian Minkovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khristian_Minkovski) (born 1971), Bulgarian swimmer
- [Marc Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Minkowski) (born 1962), French conductor
- [Oskar Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oskar_Minkowski) (1858–1931), German physician
- [Peter Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Minkowski) (born 1941), Swiss physicist
- [Pinhas Minkowsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinhas_Minkowsky) (1859–1924), Russian *hazzan*
- [Rudolph Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolph_Minkowski) (1895–1976), German-American astronomer
- [Maurycy Minkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurycy_Minkowski) (1881–1930) Polish Painter

Minkowski, Minkowsky (Minkowska), Minkovsky (Minkovskaya), etc.
