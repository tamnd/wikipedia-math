# 400 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="400"}

**400** (**four hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 399 and preceding 401.

:::

## Mathematical properties {#Mathematical_properties}

- A [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) is divided into 400 [grads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grad_%28angle%29).
- The number 400 equals [1111](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1111_%28number%29) in [base 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_7).[^o4]
