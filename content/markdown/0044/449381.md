# List of ciphertexts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of ciphertexts"}

Some famous [ciphertexts](wikipedia://en/175560) (or [cryptograms](wikipedia://en/69794)), in chronological order by date, are:

:::

| Year of origin | Ciphertext | Decipherment status |
|---|---|---|
