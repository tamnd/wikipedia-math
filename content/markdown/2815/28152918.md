# Petru Soltan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Petru Soltan"}

**Petru Soltan** (29 June 1931 – 15 July 2016) was a Moldovan [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a member of the [Academy of Sciences of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Moldova)[^o3] and an honorary [member of the Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_Romanian_Academy).[^o4] Petru Soltan was a member of the Mathematical Society of Moldova.[^o5]

:::

Petru Soltan served as member of the [Parliament of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Moldova) (1990–1994)[^o6] and is a leader of the [Democratic Forum of Romanians in Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Forum_of_Romanians_in_Moldova).[^o7]

He died on 15 July 2016 at the age of 86.[^o8]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- Doctor Honoris Causa [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University), [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca) (1992),
- Doctor Honoris Causa Academy of Economic Studies of Moldova (1998)
- Doctor Honoris Causa al Universităţii Pedagogice de Stat din Tiraspol, cu sediul la Chişinău (2000).
- Doctor Magna cum Laudae al ULIM (1994),
- Honorary Professor [Petre Andrei University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petre_Andrei_University_of_Iași) (2001).
- Membru titular [Academy of Sciences of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Moldova) (din 1992),
- honorary member [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy), 1993.
- Om emerit al Republicii Moldova (1994),
- Medals Acad. S. I. Vavilov (Moscova, 1991),
- Medal "Meritul Civic" (1996),
- Medal Mihai Eminescu (2000),
- Medal Dimitrie Cantemir (Academy of Sciences of Moldova, 2001).
- Moldova's National Prize in Science and Technology, 2004[^o4]
