# Mathematical Society of the Republic of Moldova

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical Society of the Republic of Moldova"}

The **Mathematical Society of the Republic of Moldova** is (Societatea Matematică din Republica Moldova) is a [non-governmental organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-governmental_organisation) promoting interests of [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians).[^o3][^o4]

:::
