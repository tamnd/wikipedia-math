# Subanalytic set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Subanalytic set"}

In mathematics, particularly in the subfield of [real analytic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_analytic_geometry), a **subanalytic set** is a set of points (for example in [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697)) defined in a way broader than for **semianalytic sets** (roughly speaking, those satisfying conditions requiring certain real [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) to be positive there). Subanalytic sets still have a reasonable local description in terms of [submanifolds](wikipedia://en/525095).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definitions"}

## Formal definitions {#Formal_definitions}

A subset *V* of a given Euclidean space *E* is **semianalytic** if each point of *E* has a neighbourhood *U* in *E* such that the intersection of *V* and *U* lies in the [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) of sets generated by subsets defined by inequalities *f* > 0, where f is a [real analytic function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_analytic_function). There is no [Tarski–Seidenberg theorem](wikipedia://en/19389633) for semianalytic sets, and projections of semianalytic sets are in general not semianalytic.

A subset *V* of *E* is a **subanalytic set** if for each point there exists a [relatively compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relatively_compact) semianalytic set *X* in a Euclidean space *F* of dimension at least as great as *E*, and a neighbourhood *U* in *E*, such that the intersection of *V* and *U* is a linear projection of *X* into *E* from *F*.

In particular all semianalytic sets are subanalytic. On an open dense subset, subanalytic sets are submanifolds and so they have a definite dimension "at most points". Semianalytic sets are contained in a real-analytic subvariety of the same dimension. However, subanalytic sets are not in general contained in any subvariety of the same dimension. On the other hand, there is a theorem, to the effect that a subanalytic set *A* can be written as a [locally finite](wikipedia://en/11611098) union of submanifolds.

Subanalytic sets are not closed under projections, however, because a real-analytic subvariety that is not relatively compact can have a projection which is not a locally finite union of submanifolds, and hence is not subanalytic.

:::
