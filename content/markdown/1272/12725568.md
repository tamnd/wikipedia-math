# Jafar Zafarani

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Jafar Zafarani"}

**Jafar Zafarani** (جعفر زعفرانی) is an Iranian mathematician. A professor at the [University of Isfahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Isfahan) and chancellor at [Sheikhbahaee University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikhbahaee_University), Zafarani's research interests include [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [nonlinear functional analysis](wikipedia://en/8546912). Zafarani obtained his [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSc) in Mathematics at the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran) in 1969 and completed his [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc.) at the [University of Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liège), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) in 1974. He served as president of the [Iranian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/46332302) from 1989 to 1991.[^o4]

:::

## Professional Experience {#Professional_Experience}

Associate editor of the Journal of Sciences, Islamic Republic of Iran. Associate editor of the Journal of Optimization, Theory and Applications. Associate editor of the Journal of Nonlinear and Variational Analysis. President at Sheikhbahaee University
