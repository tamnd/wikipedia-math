# Open Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Open Mathematics"}

***Open Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering all areas of mathematics. It is published by [Walter de Gruyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_de_Gruyter) and the [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Salvatore Angelo Marano ([University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania)) and Vincenzo Vespri ([University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence)).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded), [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences, [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH), and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2018 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.726.[^o4]

## History {#History}

The journal was established in 2003 as the [*Central European Journal of Mathematics*](https://link.springer.com/journal/11533) and published by [Versita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versita), since 2012 part of Walter de Gruyter, in collaboration with [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). In 2014 it was moved to the Walter de Gruyter [imprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imprint_%28trade_name%29) and started charging [article processing charges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_processing_charge). In protest, the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) and the quasi-totality of the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) resigned and, in August 2014, established a new journal, the [*European Journal of Mathematics*](https://www.springer.com/mathematics/algebra/journal/40879).

### Editors-in-chief {#Editors-in-chief}

The following persons have been editors-in-chief of the journal:

- [Andrzej Białynicki-Birula](wikipedia://en/5608712) ([University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw); 2003–2004)
- [Grigory Margulis](wikipedia://en/1104948) ([Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University); 2004–2009)
- [Fedor Bogomolov](wikipedia://en/8823369) ([Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences); 2009–2014)
- Ugo Gianazza ([University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia); 2014–2019)
- Vincenzo Vespri ([University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence); 2014–present)
- Salvatore Angelo Marano ([University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania); 2020–present)
