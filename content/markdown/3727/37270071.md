# Nodary

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nodary"}

*Image: Nodary.svg.* Nodary curve.

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **nodary** is the curve that is traced by the focus of a [hyperbola](wikipedia://en/14052) as it rolls without slipping along the axis, a [roulette curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roulette_curve).[^o2]

:::

The differential equation of the curve is: $y^2 + \frac{2ay}{\sqrt{1+y'^2}}=b^2$.

Its parametric equation is:

$$x(u)=a\operatorname{sn}(u,k)+(a/k)\big((1-k^2)u - E(u,k)\big)$$

$$y(u)=-a\operatorname{cn}(u,k)+(a/k)\operatorname{dn}(u,k)$$

where $k= \cos(\tan^{-1}(b/a))$ is the elliptic modulus and $E(u,k)$ is the [incomplete elliptic integral of the second kind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incomplete_elliptic_integral_of_the_second_kind) and sn, cn and dn are [Jacobi's elliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi's_elliptic_functions).[^o2]

The surface of revolution is the [nodoid](wikipedia://en/11396581) [constant mean curvature surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constant_mean_curvature_surface).
