# William W. Tait

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William W. Tait"}

*Image: William Tait May 2004.jpg.* Tait in 2004

**William Walker Tait** (January 22, 1929 – March 15, 2024) was an American philosopher. He was emeritus professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he served as a faculty member from 1972 to 1996, and as department chair from 1981 to 1987.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Tait received his B.A. from [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University) in 1952, and his Ph.D. from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1958. [Frederic Fitch](wikipedia://en/4994707) served as his doctoral advisor.

Prior to teaching at Chicago he held positions at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 1958 to 1964, the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) from 1965 to 1971, and the [University of Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aarhus) from 1971 to 1972. In 1966, he signed a tax resistance vow to protest the Vietnam War.[^o3] In 2002, he was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o4][^o5]

Tait died in [Naperville, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naperville,_Illinois) on March 15, 2024, at the age of 95.[^o6]
