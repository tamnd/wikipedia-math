# Bernard Dacorogna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Dacorogna"}

**Bernard Dacorogna** (born 15 October 1953 in [Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria), Egypt) is a Swiss mathematician.[^o3] He completed his undergraduate studies at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) and his Ph.D. at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh), UK, in 1980 under the supervision of [John M. Ball](wikipedia://en/3578468). He is a professor at [Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland). He is a specialist of the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). He has written several articles and books. [The Chaire de la Vallée Poussin 2018](https://uclouvain.be/fr/instituts-recherche/irmp/chaire-de-la-vallee-poussin-2018.html) of the Université Catholique de Louvain (Belgium) is attributed to him.

:::
