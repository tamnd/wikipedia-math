# Just-About-Right scale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Just-About-Right scale"}

The **Just-About-Right scale** (**JAR scale**), is a [sensory evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensory_evaluation) tool used to measure the intensity of a particular attribute or characteristic of a product or service. The JAR scale typically consists of 5 levels ranging from "Much too little" to "Much too much."[^o2][^o3]

:::

The JAR scale focuses on specific attributes of a product such as sweetness, saltiness, texture, etc., or service such as expediency, cost, etc.

The JAR scale is criticized for measuring attribute intensity and acceptability simultaneously.[^o4]

*Just-About-Right Scale*

::: {#o5 .table cue=table name="Just-About-Right Scale"}

| Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Much too little |
| 2 | Too little |
| 3 | Just about right |
| 4 | Too much |
| 5 | Much too much |

:::
