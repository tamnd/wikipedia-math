# 2021 Estonian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2021 Estonian census"}

The **2021 Population and Housing Census** (**PHC 2021**) (Rahva ja eluruumide loendus (*REL 2021*)) was a [census](wikipedia://en/6889) that was carried out during 28 December 2021 – 22 January 2022 in [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) by [Statistics Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Estonia).[^o2]

:::

The total population recorded was 1,331,824 persons, compared to 1,294,455 persons counted in the [2011 Estonian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Estonian_census).[^o3]

The 2021 Estonian census was based on data from official registers that were supplemented with information from a survey of a random sample of 60,000 people. The survey took place from 28 December 2021 to 22 January 2022.[^o4][^o5].
