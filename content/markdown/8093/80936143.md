# Bálint Dömölki

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bálint Dömölki"}

**Bálint Dömölki** (11 July 1935 – 31 August 2025) was a Hungarian mathematician. A member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o3] he was a recipient of the [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) Award for the Information Society (2003).[^o4]

:::

Dömölki died on 31 August 2025, at the age of 90.[^o5]
