# Wirtinger's representation and projection theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wirtinger's representation and projection theorem"}

In mathematics, **Wirtinger's representation and projection theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) proved by [Wilhelm Wirtinger](wikipedia://en/2044921) in 1932 in connection with some problems of [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713). This theorem gives the representation formula for the [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/14110) [subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) $\left.\right. H_2$ of the simple, unweighted holomorphic [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) $\left.\right. L^2$ of functions [square-integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square-integrable) over the surface of the unit disc $\left.\right.\{z:|z|<1\}$ of the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628), along with a form of the [orthogonal projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_projection) from $\left.\right. L^2$ to $\left.\right. H_2$.

:::

Wirtinger's paper [^o2] contains the following theorem presented also in [Joseph L. Walsh](wikipedia://en/14002828)'s well-known monograph [^o3] (p. 150) with a different proof. *If* $\left.\right.\left. F(z)\right.$ *is of the class* $\left.\right. L^2$ on $\left.\right. |z|<1$, *i.e.*

$$\iint_{|z|<1}|F(z)|^2 \, dS<+\infty,$$

*where $\left.\right. dS$ is the [area element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_element), then the unique function $\left.\right. f(z)$ of the holomorphic subclass $H_2\subset L^2$, such that*

$$\iint_{|z|<1}|F(z)-f(z)|^2 \, dS$$

*is least, is given by*

$$f(z)=\frac1\pi\iint_{|\zeta|<1}F(\zeta)\frac{dS}{(1-\overline\zeta z)^2},\quad |z|<1.$$

The last formula gives a form for the orthogonal projection from $\left.\right. L^2$ to $\left.\right. H_2$. Besides, replacement of $\left.\right. F(\zeta)$ by $\left.\right. f(\zeta)$ makes it Wirtinger's representation for all $f(z)\in H_2$. This is an analog of the well-known [Cauchy integral formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_integral_formula) with the square of the Cauchy kernel. Later, after the 1950s, a degree of the Cauchy kernel was called [reproducing kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reproducing_kernel), and the notation $\left.\right. A^2_0$ became common for the class $\left.\right. H_2$.

In 1948 [Mkhitar Djrbashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkhitar_Djrbashian)[^o4] extended Wirtinger's representation and projection to the wider, weighted Hilbert spaces $\left.\right. A^2_\alpha$ of functions $\left.\right. f(z)$ holomorphic in $\left.\right.|z|<1$, which satisfy the condition

$$\|f\|_{A^2_\alpha}=\left\{\frac1\pi\iint_{|z|<1}|f(z)|^2(1-|z|^2)^{\alpha-1} \, dS\right\}^{1/2}<+\infty\text{ for some }\alpha\in(0,+\infty),$$

and also to some Hilbert spaces of entire functions. The extensions of these results to some weighted $\left.\right. A^2_\omega$ spaces of functions holomorphic in $\left.\right. |z|<1$ and similar spaces of entire functions, the unions of which respectively coincide with *all* functions holomorphic in $\left.\right. |z|<1$ and the *whole* set of entire functions can be seen in.[^o5]
