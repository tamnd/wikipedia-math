# Rainer Burkard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rainer Burkard"}

**Rainer Ernst Burkard** (born 28 January 1943, [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) ) is an Austrian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His research interests include [discrete optimization](wikipedia://en/420567), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), applied [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), and applied [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o2][^o3]

:::

He earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1967 and received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz) in 1971. From 1973–1981 Rainer Burkard was a full professor of Applied Mathematics at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) (Germany). Since 1981 Rainer Burkard is a full professor at the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology).

## Positions held {#Positions_held}

- 1984-1986 Vice President of [GMÖOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GMÖOR)
- 1986-1988 President of the [Austrian Society of Operations Research](wikipedia://en/50952279)
- 1995-1997 [EURO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_European_Operational_Research_Societies) Vice President of [IFORS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFORS)
- 1993-1996 Dean of the Faculty of Science, Graz University of Technology
- 1994-1998 Member of the Council of the [European Consortium of Mathematics in Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Consortium_of_Mathematics_in_Industry)
- 1991-2000 Member of the Senate of the [Christian Doppler Research Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Doppler_Research_Society)
- 2001-2002 Vice President of EURO

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384) in 1972
- The Scientific Prize of the Society of Mathematics, Economics and Operations Research in 1991
- The [EURO Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURO_Gold_Medal) 1997
- Since 1998 Honorary Member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences)
- Since 2011 Honorary Member of the Austrian Society of Operations Research
