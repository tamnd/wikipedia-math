# Topological Galois theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Topological Galois theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **topological Galois theory** is a [mathematical theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_theory) which originated from a [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) proof of [Abel's impossibility theorem](wikipedia://en/152518) found by [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490) and concerns the applications of some [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) concepts to some problems in the field of [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). It connects many ideas from [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) to ideas in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). As described in [Askold Khovanskii](wikipedia://en/43849047)'s book: "According to this theory, the way the [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) of an [analytic function](wikipedia://en/61478) covers the plane of [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) can obstruct the representability of this function by explicit formulas. The strongest known results on the unexpressibility of functions by explicit formulas have been obtained in this way."

:::
