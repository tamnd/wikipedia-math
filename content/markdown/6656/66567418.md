# Antonia J. Jones

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonia J. Jones"}

**Antonia Jane Jones** (1943 – 2010) was a British mathematician and computer scientist. Her research considered number theory and computer science.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Jones was born in 1943 in [Queen Charlotte's and Chelsea Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Charlotte's_and_Chelsea_Hospital). She was the first member of her family to attend university. Jones contracted polio as a child and lost both of her legs at the age of ten.[^o4][^o5] Jones attended the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading), where she studied mathematics and physics and graduated both with first class honours.[^o6] She was a doctoral student in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), where she completed her PhD in 1969. Jones joined the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham) after earning her doctorate, before joining [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) as a Senior Lecturer. She spent a year at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), after which she joined the faculty at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder).[^o7]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Jones returned to the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) in the 1970s, where she became a lecturer at [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London). Her interest switched from mathematics to computing and she began to explore acoustic pattern recognition.[^o5] Whilst Jones struggled with the early computers, when technology became more accessible for people with physical disabilities she launched her own firm creating random access video controllers.[^o5]

In 1983 Jones joined [Brunel University London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University_London) at a lecturer in Information Technology.[^o5] Jones later served as Professor of Evolutionary and Neural Computing at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University).[^o9] She exposed various security loopholes in banking infrastructure, including identifying significant potential fraud at [HSBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSBC).[^o10][^o11]

Alongside her scientific research, Jones was involved with science communication and public engagement. She served as an electronic data consultant on the 1986 film *[Rocinante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocinante)*.[^o12] She contributed to the 1998 [British Science Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Science_Association) Festival of Science.[^o13] In 2007, Jones retired from Cardiff University.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Antonia Jones spent many years with her partner Barbara Quinn at their shared farmhouse in the Brecon Beacons. Upon her retirement in 2007, Jones moved to [St. Augustine, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Augustine,_Florida). Jones died on 23 December 2010. She is survived by Quinn and her sister Jenny Carrl.[^o4]
