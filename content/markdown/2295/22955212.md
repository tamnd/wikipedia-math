# Mori dream space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mori dream space"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Mori dream space** is a [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469) whose cone of [effective divisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_divisor) has a well-behaved decomposition into certain convex sets called "Mori chambers".[^o2] Hu and Keel showed that Mori dream spaces are quotients of affine varieties by [torus actions](wikipedia://en/41405104).[^o2] The notion is named so because it behaves nicely from the point of view of [Mori's minimal model program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori's_minimal_model_program).

:::

## Examples and Properties {#Examples_and_Properties}

Any quasi-smooth projective spherical variety (in particular, any quasi-smooth projective toric variety) as well as any log Fano 3-fold is a Mori dream space.[^o2] In general, it is difficult to find a non-trivial example of a Mori dream space, as being a Mori Dream Space is equivalent to all (multi-)section rings being finitely generated.[^o4]

It has been shown that a variety which admits a surjective morphism from a Mori dream space is again a Mori dream space.[^o5]
