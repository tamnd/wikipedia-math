# List of actuaries

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of actuaries"}

An **[actuary](wikipedia://en/43405)** is a business professional who deals with a [financial situation of risk and uncertainty](wikipedia://en/189373). This is a list of notable actuaries and others who have influenced the profession.

:::

## Born in the 17th century {#Born_in_the_17th_century}

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="John Graunt"}

- [John Graunt](wikipedia://en/30875381) (1620–1674), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_people), constructed one of the first [life tables](wikipedia://en/1614128)

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="William Petty"}

- [William Petty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Petty) (1623–1687), English, much early writing on life tables

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Johan de Witt"}

- [Johan de Witt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_de_Witt) (1625–1672), [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people), early pioneering work on [life annuities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_annuity)

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Edmond Halley"}

- [Edmond Halley](wikipedia://en/36858805) (1656–1742), English, constructed one of the first life tables

:::

## Born in the 18th century {#Born_in_the_18th_century}

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="James Dodson"}

- [James Dodson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dodson_%28mathematician%29) (1705–1751), English, developed statistical [mortality tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortality_table)

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Alexander Webster"}

- [Alexander Webster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Webster) (1708–1784), Scottish, developed a scheme for providing pensions to widows of church ministers

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Richard Price"}

- [Richard Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Price) (1723–1791), [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_people), introduced correct methods for values of contingent reversions

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Edward Rowe Mores"}

- [Edward Rowe Mores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Rowe_Mores) (1731–1778), English, founder of [The Society for Equitable Assurances on Lives and Survivorships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Equitable_Life_Assurance_Society) and the first person to use the professional title "actuary"

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="William Morgan"}

- [William Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morgan_%28actuary%29) (1750–1833), Welsh

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="George Barrett"}

- [George Barrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Barrett_%28actuary%29) (1752–1821), English

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nathaniel Bowditch"}

- [Nathaniel Bowditch](wikipedia://en/362075) (1773–1838), probably the second [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) insurance actuary; Essex Fire and Marine

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Joshua Milne"}

- [Joshua Milne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Milne) (1776–1851), English

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Benjamin Gompertz"}

- [Benjamin Gompertz](wikipedia://en/501523) (1779–1865), English, developed the [Gompertz-Makeham law of mortality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gompertz-Makeham_law_of_mortality) and the [Gompertz function](wikipedia://en/3350262)

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="John Finlaison"}

- [John Finlaison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Finlaison) (1783–1860), [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people), first president of the [Institute of Actuaries](wikipedia://en/852871)

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Griffith Davies"}

- [Griffith Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith_Davies) (1788–1855), Welsh

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Thomas Galloway"}

- [Thomas Galloway](wikipedia://en/2901035) (1796–1851), Scottish

:::
