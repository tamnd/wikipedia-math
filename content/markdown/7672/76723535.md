# Kung-Yee Liang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kung-Yee Liang"}

**Kung-Yee Liang** (; born September 7, 1951[^o3][^o4]) is a Taiwanese biostatistician known for his work on [generalized estimating equations](wikipedia://en/16794199), which he introduced together with [Scott Zeger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Zeger) in 1986.[^o5][^o6] He is a distinguished chair professor at [Feng Chia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feng_Chia_University) and the chairman of [OBI Pharma, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBI_Pharma,_Inc.)

:::

From 1982 to 2010, Liang was a professor at the department of biostatistics at the [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health). In 2010, Liang returned to Taiwan, where he was the president of [National Yang-Ming University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Yang-Ming_University) from 2010 to 2017 and the president of the [National Health Research Institutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Research_Institutes) from 2017 to 2022.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Liang earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [National Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tsing_Hua_University) in 1973 before coming to the U.S., where he received a master's degree in statistics from the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina) in 1979. In 1982, Liang completed his PhD in biostatistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) under the supervision of [Norman Breslow](wikipedia://en/10816081). His dissertation was titled *The Asymptotic Equivalence of Conditional and Unconditional Inference Procedures*.[^o8]

That same year, he joined the biostatistics department faculty at the [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health) where he became a full professor in 1991 and stayed until he returned to Taiwan in 2010.[^o25]

Returning to his home country, Liang moved from conducting research towards administrative and leadership roles. In August 2010, Liang became president of [National Yang-Ming University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Yang-Ming_University), a position he remained in until November 2017.[^o9]

From December 2017 to December 2022, he was the president of the [National Health Research Institutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Research_Institutes).[^o10][^o25] During his time as a vice president of the organisation (2003–2006), he had already served as the acting president of the organisation between January and June 2006.[^o11][^o12]

Since February 2023, Liang has been Distinguished Chair Professor at [Feng Chia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feng_Chia_University).[^o13][^o25][^o14]

On December 29, 2023, he was elected as chairman of [OBI Pharma, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBI_Pharma,_Inc.)[^o15]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Liang has received multiple awards for his work, including the [Snedecor Award](wikipedia://en/47945620) from the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344) in 1987,[^o17][^o18] the [Rema Lapouse Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rema_Lapouse_Award) from the [American Public Health Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Public_Health_Association) in 2010,[^o19] and the [International Statistical Institute's Karl Pearson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute#Karl_Pearson_Prize) in 2015.[^o20][^o21] In 2025, he received the Presidential Science Prize of Taiwan, the country's highest honor for scientific research.[^o22][^o23]

In 1995, Liang was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o24] He was elected as an academician of the [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) in 2002,[^o25] as a fellow of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2012,[^o26][^o3] and as a member of the [National Academy of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Medicine) in 2015.[^o27]
