# Response-rate ratio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Response-rate ratio"}

**Response-rate ratio** is a measure of efficacy of therapy in [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717). It is defined as the proportion of improved patients in the [treatment group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treatment_groups), divided by the proportion of improved patients in the [control group](wikipedia://en/1640288). The same term has been used in [marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing).[^o2]

:::
