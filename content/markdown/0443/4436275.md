# Karel Petr

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karel Petr"}

*Image: Karel Petr (1868-1950).jpg.* Petr in 1928

**Karel Petr** (; 14 June 1868 – 14 February 1950) was a Czech [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Petr was born on 14 June 1868 in Zbyslav [Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemia), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) (today part of [Vrdy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrdy), Czech Republic).[^o3] He is known for the [Petr–Douglas–Neumann theorem](wikipedia://en/35762367) in [plane geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), which he proved in 1905 (in Czech)[^o4] and in 1908 (in German).[^o5][^o6] It was independently rediscovered by [Jesse Douglas](wikipedia://en/387571) in 1940[^o7] and by [Bernhard Neumann](wikipedia://en/5032588) in 1941.[^o6][^o8]

[Eduard Čech](wikipedia://en/859830) was a doctoral student of Petr at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague). Petr's doctoral students also included [Bohumil Bydžovský](wikipedia://en/50654124) and [Václav Hlavatý](wikipedia://en/4435172).

Petr died on 14 February 1950 in Prague.[^o3]
