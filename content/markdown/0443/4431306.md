# Polynormal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polynormal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is said to be **polynormal** if its [closure under conjugation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_closure) by any element of the group can also be achieved via closure by conjugation by some element in the subgroup generated.

:::

In symbols, a subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is called polynormal if for any $g \in G$ the subgroup $K = H^{<g>}$ is the same as $H^{H^{<g>}}$.

Here are the relationships with other subgroup properties:

- Every weakly [pronormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593621) is polynormal.
- Every [paranormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593691) is polynormal.[^o2]
