# Wedge product (topology)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wedge product"}

The **wedge product** in topology may refer to:

:::

- The [wedge sum](wikipedia://en/684143), which joins two spaces at a point
- The [smash product](wikipedia://en/698010), the product in the category of pointed spaces
