# Sujatha Ramdorai

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sujatha Ramdorai"}

**Sujatha Ramdorai** (born 1962)[^o3] is an [algebraic number theorist](wikipedia://en/174705) known for her work on [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150). She is a professor of mathematics and [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) at [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).[^o4][^o5] She was previously a professor at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research).

:::

## Education {#Education}

She completed her B.Sc. in 1982 at St. Joseph's college, Bangalore and then got her M.Sc. through correspondence from [Annamalai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annamalai_University) in 1985. After that she went for PhD at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) and was awarded her PhD under supervision of [Raman Parimala](wikipedia://en/11853200) in 1992.[^o7] Her dissertation was "Witt Groups of Real Surfaces and Real Geometry".

## Career {#Career}

Dr. Ramdorai initially worked in the areas of algebraic theory of quadratic forms and arithmetic geometry of elliptic curves.[^o9] Together with [Coates](wikipedia://en/230705), Fukaya, [Kato](wikipedia://en/4616818), and Venjakob she formulated a non-commutative version of the main conjecture of [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), on which much of the foundation of this important subject is based.[^o10] [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150) has its origins in the work of a great Japanese mathematician, [Kenkichi Iwasawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenkichi_Iwasawa).[^o11]

She holds an adjunct professorship position at [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science_Education_and_Research,_Pune).[^o12]

Working with her husband Srinivasan Ramdorai and Indian mathematics writer V.S. Sastry, Sujatha Ramdorai conceived of and partially funded the [Ramanujan Math Park](wikipedia://en/59442784) in [Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor,_Andhra_Pradesh), which was inaugurated at the end of 2017. The park is dedicated to mathematics education and honors the great Indian mathematician [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) (1887-1920).

She is a member of the Scientific Committee of several international research agencies such as the Indo-French Centre for Promotion of Advanced Research, Banff International Research Station, International Centre for Pure and Applied Mathematics. She was a member of the [National Knowledge Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Knowledge_Commission) from 2007 to 2009. She is at present a member of the Prime Minister's Scientific Advisory Council from 2009 onwards and also a member of the National Innovation Council.[^o13] She is also on the advisory board of [Gonit Sora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonit_Sora).[^o14]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Ramdorai became the first Indian to win the prestigious [ICTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICTP) [Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525) in 2006. She was also awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Award), the highest honour in scientific fields by the Indian Government in 2004.[^o16] She is also the recipient of the 2020 [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) for her exceptional contributions to mathematics research.[^o17] She has been bestowed with [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri) award by the Government of India for 2023 in the field of science and engineering.[^o18]

## Editorial position {#Editorial_position}

- Managing editor, [*International Journal of Number Theory* (*IJNT*)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Number_Theory)
- Editor, *Journal of Ramanujan Mathematical Society* (*JRMS*)[^o20]
- Associate editor, *Expositiones Mathematicae*[^o21]
