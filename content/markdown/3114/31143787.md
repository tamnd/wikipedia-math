# S. G. Dani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="S. G. Dani"}

**Shrikrishna Gopalrao Dani** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences, [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai) who works in the broad area of [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He did a master's degree from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) in 1969. He then joined the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai for a PhD which he was awarded in 1975. After that, he joined TIFR as a faculty member. After TIFR, he was the Chair Professor at the [Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology,_Bombay). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) during 1976–77 and 1983–84.[^o5]

## Administration {#Administration}

He has been a member of the [NBHM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBHM) since 1996 and was the Chairman of the NBHM. He is also the chairman, Commission for Development and Exchange (CDE) of [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868), for the period 2007–2010. He has served as Editor of Proceedings (Math. Sci.) of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences), Bangalore for many years since 1987.[^o7]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Dani was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) Prize in 1990. He gave an invited talk at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) held in Zurich, Switzerland in 1994. He received the World Academy of Sciences prize in 2007.[^o9]
