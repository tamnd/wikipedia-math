# Pierre Cartier (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Cartier"}

**Pierre Émile Cartier** (10 June 1932 – 17 August 2024) was a French mathematician. An associate of the [Bourbaki group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourbaki_group) and at one time a colleague of [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042), his interests have ranged over [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Cartier was born on 10 June 1932. He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) under [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) and [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019). After his 1958 [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) on algebraic geometry, he worked in a number of fields. He is known for the introduction of the [Cartier operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartier_operator) in algebraic geometry in characteristic *p*, and for work on duality of [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties) and on [formal groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_group). He is the eponym of [Cartier divisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartier_divisor) and [Cartier duality](wikipedia://en/35152335).

From 1961 to 1971, he was a professor at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). In 1970 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).[^o6] He was awarded the 1978 [Prize Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_Ampère) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

Cartier died in [Marcoussis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcoussis) on 17 August 2024, at the age of 92.[^o9]
