# Patricia Campbell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patricia Campbell"}

**Patricia F. Campbell** is an American mathematician and [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471). She is a professor in the Department of Teaching and Learning, Policy and Leadership at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park).[^o3] Her work has concerned the improvement of mathematics education in minority and lower-income secondary schools,[^o6] and the effectiveness of mathematics coaching in mathematics education.

:::

Campbell is a graduate of the [College of St. Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Francis). After earning a master's degree in mathematics at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), she completed a Ph.D. in mathematics education at the [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University).[^o4] She was co-chair of the [American Educational Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Educational_Research_Association) Special Interest Group on Research in Math Education for 2007–2009.[^o5]

In 2011 she was given the Twenty-First Annual [Louise Hay Award](wikipedia://en/51270001) for Contributions to Mathematics Education. The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) awarded it to her "for her contributions to the teaching and learning of mathematics in urban settings and for working in schools that serve predominantly minority populations from low-income backgrounds".[^o6]
