# Ninet Sinaii

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ninet Sinaii"}

**Ninet Sinaii** is an American [epidemiologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemiologist) of Armenian descent. She is a staff epidemiologist at the Biostatistics and Clinical Epidemiology Service (BCES) at the [National Institutes of Health Clinical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health_Clinical_Center).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sinaii is Armenian and American. Her family moved from [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran) to [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and later the United States. She was introduced to epidemiology and public health during her junior year abroad in England.[^o4] Sinaii received her bachelor of arts in psychobiology from [Occidental College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_College). She earned a Master of Public Health in Epidemiology and Biostatistics from [Milken Institute School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milken_Institute_School_of_Public_Health), and her Ph.D. in epidemiology from the [Columbian College of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbian_College_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Sinaii has been at [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) since 2000, first as a pre-doctoral fellow at the [Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eunice_Kennedy_Shriver_National_Institute_of_Child_Health_and_Human_Development), and since 2006, as a staff epidemiologist with the Biostatistics and Clinical Epidemiology Service (BCES) at the [National Institutes of Health Clinical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health_Clinical_Center). In BCES, Sinaii has collaborated in a wide range of research activities and study designs involving the fields of hospital epidemiology and infectious diseases, [bioethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioethics), adult and pediatric [endocrinology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endocrinology), women’s health, and other acute and chronic conditions. She has co-authored dozens of peer-reviewed articles searchable on [PubMed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PubMed) and is a member of the [Society for Epidemiologic Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Epidemiologic_Research). In addition, she takes part in the ancillary epidemiological/statistical teaching of medical and other graduate students, residents, and fellows; is involved in community public health projects; and is an appointed representative of the Clinical Center on the Prevention Research Coordinating Committee.[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Sinaii is married and has children. She was a [military wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_dependent) for 15 years. Sinaii is [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians) and a [polyglot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism).[^o4]
