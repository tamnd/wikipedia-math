# Uniform 2 k1 polytope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform 2 k1 polytope"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **2<sub>*k*1</sub> polytope** is a [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) in *n* dimensions (*n* = *k* + 4) constructed from the [E<sub>*n*</sub>](wikipedia://en/5351858) [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004). The family was named by their [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) as **2<sub>*k*1</sub>** by its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 2-node sequence. It can be named by an [extended Schläfli symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_Schläfli_symbol) {3,3,3<sup>*k*,1</sup>}.

:::

## Family members {#Family_members}

The family starts uniquely as [6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/26021427), but can be extended backwards to include the 5-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) ([pentacross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentacross)) in 5 dimensions, and the 4-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) ([5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821)) in 4 dimensions.

Each polytope is constructed from (*n* − 1)-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and **2<sub>*k*−1,1</sub>** (*n* − 1)-polytope facets, each having a [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) as an (*n* − 1)-[demicube](wikipedia://en/6833695), {3<sup>1,*n*−2,1</sup>}.

The sequence ends with *k* = 6 (*n* = 10), as an infinite hyperbolic tessellation of 9-space.

The complete family of 2<sub>*k*1</sub> polytopes are:

1. [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821): **2<sub>01</sub>**, (5 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) cells)
1. [Pentacross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentacross): **2<sub>11</sub>**, (32 [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) (**2<sub>01</sub>**) facets)
1. **[2<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19232473)**, (72 5-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 27 5-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) (**2<sub>11</sub>**) facets)
1. **[2<sub>31</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070163)**, (576 6-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 56 **2<sub>21</sub>** facets)
1. **[2<sub>41</sub>](wikipedia://en/19091447)**, (17280 7-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 240 **2<sub>31</sub>** facets)
1. **[2<sub>51</sub>](wikipedia://en/19550444)**, tessellates Euclidean 8-space (∞ 8-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and ∞ **2<sub>41</sub>** facets)
1. **[2<sub>61</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_61_honeycomb)**, tessellates hyperbolic 9-space (∞ 9-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and ∞ **2<sub>51</sub>** facets)
