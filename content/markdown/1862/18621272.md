# Uniform 1 k2 polytope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform 1 k2 polytope"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **1<sub>*k*2</sub> polytope** is a [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) in *n* dimensions (*n* = *k* + 4) constructed from the [E<sub>*n*</sub>](wikipedia://en/5351858) [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004). The family was named by their [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) **1<sub>*k*2</sub>** by its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 1-node sequence. It can be named by an [extended Schläfli symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_Schläfli_symbol) {3,3<sup>*k*,2</sup>}.

:::

## Family members {#Family_members}

The family starts uniquely as [6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/26021427), but can be extended backwards to include the 5-[demicube](wikipedia://en/6833695) ([demipenteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demipenteract)) in 5 dimensions, and the 4-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) ([5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821)) in 4 dimensions.

Each polytope is constructed from **1<sub>*k*−1,2</sub>** and (*n*−1)-[demicube](wikipedia://en/6833695) facets. Each has a [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of a {3<sup>1,*n*−2,2</sup>} polytope, is a birectified *n*-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781), t<sub>2</sub>{3<sup>*n*</sup>}.

The sequence ends with *k* = 6 (*n* = 10), as an infinite tessellation of 9-dimensional hyperbolic space.

The complete family of 1<sub>*k*2</sub> polytopes are:

1. [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821): **1<sub>02</sub>**, (5 [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells)
1. [**1<sub>12</sub>** polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demipenteract), (16 [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821), and 10 [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) facets)
1. [**1<sub>22</sub>** polytope](wikipedia://en/19070548), (54 [demipenteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demipenteract) facets)
1. [**1<sub>32</sub>** polytope](wikipedia://en/19070862), (56 **1<sub>22</sub>** and 126 [demihexeract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demihexeract) facets)
1. [**1<sub>42</sub>** polytope](wikipedia://en/19091290), (240 **1<sub>32</sub>** and 2160 [demihepteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demihepteract) facets)
1. [**1<sub>52</sub>** honeycomb](wikipedia://en/19550423), tessellates Euclidean 8-space (∞ **1<sub>42</sub>** and ∞ [demiocteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiocteract) facets)
1. [**1<sub>62</sub>** honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_62_honeycomb), tessellates hyperbolic 9-space (∞ **1<sub>52</sub>** and ∞ [demienneract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demienneract) facets)
