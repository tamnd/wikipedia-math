# Patricia Fauring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patricia Fauring"}

**Ana María Patricia Fauring** is an Argentine mathematician who won the [Paul Erdős Award](wikipedia://en/28001680) for being "the principal mathematician involved in training Argentine teams for the [IMO](wikipedia://en/82208) and other international events, where they have done respectably".[^o2]

:::

Fauring obtained her PhD in 1982 from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) under the supervision of Ángel Rafael Larotonda. Her thesis was titled *Una noción de estabilidad para campos vectoriales complejos* (*A notion of stability for complex vector fields*).
