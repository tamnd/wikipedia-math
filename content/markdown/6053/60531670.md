# Validated numerics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Validated numerics"}

**Validated numerics**, or **rigorous computation**, **verified computation**, **reliable computation**, **numerical verification** (Zuverlässiges Rechnen) is numerics including mathematically strict error (rounding error, truncation error, discretization error) evaluation, and it is one field of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). For computation, [interval arithmetic](wikipedia://en/2176160) is most often used, where all results are represented by intervals. Validated numerics were used by [Warwick Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick_Tucker) in order to solve the 14th of [Smale's problems](wikipedia://en/8294746),[^o26] and today it is recognized as a powerful tool for the study of [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).[^o27]

:::

## Importance {#Importance}

Computation without verification may cause unfortunate results. Below are some examples.

### Rump's example {#Rump's_example}

In the 1980s, Rump made an example.[^o28][^o29] He made a complicated function and tried to obtain its value. Single precision, double precision, extended precision results seemed to be correct, but its plus-minus sign was different from the true value.

### Phantom solution {#Phantom_solution}

Breuer–Plum–McKenna used the spectrum method to solve the boundary value problem of the Emden equation, and reported that an asymmetric solution was obtained.[^o30] This result to the study conflicted to the theoretical study by Gidas–Ni–Nirenberg which claimed that there is no asymmetric solution.[^o31] The solution obtained by Breuer–Plum–McKenna was a phantom solution caused by discretization error. This is a rare case, but it tells us that when we want to strictly discuss differential equations, numerical solutions must be verified.

### Accidents caused by numerical errors {#Accidents_caused_by_numerical_errors}

The following examples are known as accidents caused by numerical errors:

- Failure of intercepting missiles in the [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War) (1991)[^o32]
- Failure of the [Ariane 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariane_5) rocket (1996)[^o33]
- Mistakes in election result totalization[^o34]
