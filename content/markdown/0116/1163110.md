# Vijay Kumar Patodi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vijay Kumar Patodi"}

**Vijay Kumar Patodi** (12 March 1945 – 21 December 1976) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made fundamental contributions to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He was the first mathematician to apply [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415) methods to the proof of the [index theorem](wikipedia://en/324752) for elliptic operators. He was a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), [Mumbai (Bombay)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_%28Bombay%29).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Patodi was a graduate of Government High School, [Guna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guna,_India), [Madhya Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Pradesh). He received his bachelor's degree from Vikram University, [Ujjain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ujjain), his master's degree from the [Benaras Hindu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benaras_Hindu_University), and his Ph.D. from the [University of Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bombay) under the guidance of M. S. Narasimhan and S. Ramanan at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research).[^o4]

In the two papers based on his Ph.D. thesis, "Curvature and Eigenforms of the Laplace Operator" (*Journal of Differential Geometry*), and "An Analytical Proof of the Riemann-Roch-Hirzebruch Formula for Kaehler Manifolds" (also *Journal of Differential Geometry*), Patodi made his fundamental breakthroughs.[^o5]

## Research career {#Research_career}

He was invited to spend 1971–1973 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey), where he collaborated with [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698), [Isadore Singer](wikipedia://en/324564), and [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457). The joint work led to a series of papers, "Spectral Asymmetry and Riemannian Geometry" with Atiyah and Singer,[^o7][^o8][^o9] in which the [η-invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Η-invariant) was defined. This invariant was to play a major role in subsequent advances in the area in the 1980s.[^o10]

Patodi was promoted to full professor at Tata Institute at age 30, however, he died at age 31, as a result of complications prior to surgery for a kidney transplant.
