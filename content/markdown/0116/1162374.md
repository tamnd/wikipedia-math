# Network automaton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Network automaton"}

A **network automaton** (plural **network automata**) is a mathematical system consisting of a [network of nodes](wikipedia://en/12401) that evolves over [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) according to predetermined rules.[^o2] It is similar in concept to a [cellular automaton](wikipedia://en/54342), but much less studied.

:::

[Stephen Wolfram](wikipedia://en/52469)'s book *[A New Kind of Science](wikipedia://en/93070)*, which is primarily concerned with cellular automata, briefly discusses network automata, and suggests (without positive evidence) that the [universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe) might at the very lowest level be a network automaton.
