# Raj Chandra Bose

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Raj Chandra Bose"}

**Raj Chandra Bose (or Basu)** (19 June 1901 – 31 October 1987) was an [Indian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_American) mathematician and statistician best known for his work in [design theory](wikipedia://en/9541), [finite geometry](wikipedia://en/267061) and the theory of [error-correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error-correcting_code) in which the class of [BCH codes](wikipedia://en/40779) is partly named after him. He also invented the notions of [partial geometry](wikipedia://en/5744042), [association scheme](wikipedia://en/2238152), and [strongly regular graph](wikipedia://en/1399856) and started a systematic study of [difference sets](wikipedia://en/2992310) to construct symmetric [block designs](wikipedia://en/1188184). He was notable for his work along with [S. S. Shrikhande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Shrikhande) and [E. T. Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._T._Parker) in their disproof of the famous [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) made by [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) dated 1782 that for no *n* do there exist two mutually orthogonal [Latin squares](wikipedia://en/37447) of order 4*n* + 2.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Bose was born in [Hoshangabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoshangabad), India into a [Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengalis) family; he was the first of five children. His father was a physician and life was good until 1918 when his mother died in the [influenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influenza) [pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandemic). His father died of a stroke the following year. Despite difficult circumstances, Bose continued to study securing first class in both the Masters examinations in Pure and Applied mathematics in 1925 and 1927 respectively at the [Rajabazar Science College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajabazar_Science_College) campus of [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta). His research was performed under the supervision of the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) Professor [Syamadas Mukhopadhyaya](wikipedia://en/34620116) from Calcutta. Bose worked as a lecturer at [Asutosh College, Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asutosh_College,_Calcutta). He published his work on the [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) of convex curves.

## Academic life {#Academic_life}

Bose's course changed in December 1932 when [P. C. Mahalanobis](wikipedia://en/359931), director of the new (1931) [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), offered Bose a part-time job. Mahalanobis had seen Bose's geometrical work and wanted him to work on statistics. The day after Bose moved in, the secretary brought him all the volumes of *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178)* with a list of 50 papers to read and also [Ronald Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824)'s *[Statistical Methods for Research Workers](wikipedia://en/1645218)*. Mahalanobis told him, "You were saying that you do not know much statistics. You master the 50 papers ... and [Fisher's book](wikipedia://en/140824). This will suffice for your statistical education for the present." With [Samarendra Nath Roy](wikipedia://en/3894736), who joined the ISI a little later, Bose was the chief mathematician at the Institute.

He first worked with multivariate analysis where he collaborated with Mahalanobis and Roy. In 1938–39 Fisher visited India and talked about the design of experiments. Roy had the idea of using the theory of [finite fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_fields) and [finite geometry](wikipedia://en/267061) to solve problems in design. The development of a mathematical theory of design would be Bose's main preoccupation until the mid-1950s.

In 1935 Bose had become full-time at the Institute. In 1940 joined the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) where [C. R. Rao](wikipedia://en/1418009) and H. K. Nandi were in the first group of students he taught. In 1945 Bose became Head of the Department of Statistics. University authorities in the United States told him he needed to have a doctorate. So he submitted his published papers on multivariate analysis and the design of experiments and was awarded a [D. Litt.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._Litt.) in 1947.

In 1947 Bose went to the United States as a visiting professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). He received offers from American universities and he was also offered positions in India. The Indian jobs involved very heavy administration, which he saw as the end of his research work and in March 1949 he joined the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill as Professor of Statistics.

In the years at Chapel Hill Bose made important discoveries on [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869) (with [D.K. Ray-Chaudhuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.K._Ray-Chaudhuri)) and constructed (with [S. S. Shrikhande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Shrikhande) and E. T. Parker) a [Graeco-Latin square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graeco-Latin_square) of size 10, a counterexample to [Euler's](wikipedia://en/17902) conjecture that no [Graeco-Latin square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graeco-Latin_square) of size 4*k* + 2 exists. In 1971, he retired at the age of 70. He then accepted a chair at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University) of [Fort Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Collins) from which he retired in 1980. His final doctoral student finished after this second retirement.

Bose died in [Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado), aged 86, in 1987. He is survived by two daughters. The elder, Purabi Schur, is retired from the [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress) and the younger, Sipra Bose Johnson, is retired as a professor of anthropology from the [State University of New York at New Paltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_New_Paltz).
