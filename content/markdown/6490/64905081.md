# Integrable module

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Integrable module"}

In algebra, an **integrable module** (or **integrable representation**) of a [Kac–Moody algebra](wikipedia://en/1146292) $\mathfrak g$ (a certain [infinite-dimensional Lie algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite-dimensional_Lie_algebra)) is a [representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_of_a_Lie_algebra) of $\mathfrak g$ such that (1) it is a sum of weight spaces and (2) the Chevalley generators $e_i, f_i$ of $\mathfrak g$ are [locally nilpotent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_nilpotent_endomorphism).[^o2] For example, the [adjoint representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_representation_of_a_Lie_algebra) of a Kac–Moody algebra is [integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable).[^o3]

:::
