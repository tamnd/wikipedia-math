# Gregg Zuckerman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gregg Zuckerman"}

*Image: Zuckerman fefferman saltman.jpg.* From left: Gregg Zuckerman, [David J. Saltman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_J._Saltman), [Robert Fefferman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Fefferman), Yale 1979

**Gregg Jay Zuckerman** (born 1949) is a mathematician and professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) working in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He discovered [Zuckerman functors](wikipedia://en/10800208) and [translation functors](wikipedia://en/32176298), and with [Anthony W. Knapp](wikipedia://en/11072560) classified the irreducible [tempered representations](wikipedia://en/10995628) of [semisimple Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group).

:::

## Education {#Education}

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1975 after completing a doctoral dissertation, titled "Some character identities for semisimple [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945)", under the supervision of [Elias M. Stein](wikipedia://en/418073).[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

In the late 1970s, Zuckerman and [Jens Carsten Jantzen](wikipedia://en/32094339) independently introduced [translation functors](wikipedia://en/32176298).[^o5][^o6] In 1978, he introduced the [Zuckerman functor](wikipedia://en/10800208).[^o5]

In a series of papers from 1976 to 1984, [Anthony W. Knapp](wikipedia://en/11072560) and Zuckerman gave the classification of [tempered representations](wikipedia://en/10995628) of [semisimple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10]
