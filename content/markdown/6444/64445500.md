# Franco of Liège

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franco of Liège"}

**Franco of Liège** (died c. 1083) was an 11th-century mathematician who worked on [squaring the circle](wikipedia://en/201359). He was the chancellor (attested 1057) and later [scholaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholaster) of [Saint Lambert's Cathedral, Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lambert's_Cathedral,_Liège) (before 1066-after 1083).[^o2][^o3]

:::

His *De quadratura circuli*, explaining his efforts to square the circle, was written around 1050.[^o4] The musical treatise *Quaestiones in musica*, primarily ascribed to [Rudolf of St Trond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_of_St_Trond), has also been attributed to him.[^o5] [Sigebert of Gembloux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigebert_of_Gembloux) ascribes to him other works, including some saints' lives, a poem (ligno crucis) and a treatise on the ember days (De Jejunio IIIIor temporum).[^o3]

[Cosmas of Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmas_of_Prague) was his student.[^o6]
