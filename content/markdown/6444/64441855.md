# Günther Frei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Günther Frei"}

*Image: Frei Milnor Dold-Samplonius Dold.jpg.* Günther Frei (left) with [John Milnor](wikipedia://en/61632), [Yvonne Dold-Samplonius](wikipedia://en/56493375), [Albrecht Dold](wikipedia://en/31337135), Zürich 2007

**Günther Hans Frei** (born 19 May 1942 in [St. Gallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Gallen)) is a Swiss mathematician and historian of mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Frei studied mathematics, physics and languages at the University of Zurich. There he received his doctoral degree in 1968 with advisor [Bartel Leendert van der Waerden](wikipedia://en/91390) and dissertation on geometry (*Beiträge zur axiomatischen Inhaltstheorie*). He became an instructor in 1968 at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) and in 1970 at Quebec's [Université Laval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Laval), where he became in 1971 a professor. He remained there until he retired as professor emeritus and returned to Switzerland to live in [Hombrechtikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hombrechtikon).

Frei's research deals with [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and the history of mathematics, especially the history of number theory in the 19th and 20th centuries and Swiss mathematics. He has written extensively on the life and work of [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118). Frei is a member of the [Euler Committee of the Swiss Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Committee_of_the_Swiss_Academy_of_Sciences).
