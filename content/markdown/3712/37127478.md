# List of things named after Thomas Bayes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Thomas Bayes"}

[Thomas Bayes](wikipedia://en/149117) ( ; c. 1701 – 1761) was an English statistician, philosopher, and [Presbyterian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian) minister.

:::

**Bayesian** ( or ) may be either any of a range of concepts and approaches that relate to statistical methods based on [Bayes' theorem](wikipedia://en/49569), or a follower of these methods.[^o2]
