# List of things named after Felix Hausdorff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Felix Hausdorff"}

[Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989) (November 8, 1868 – January 26, 1942[^o2]) was a German mathematician. He made significant contributions to mathematics, specifically in the fields of [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::
