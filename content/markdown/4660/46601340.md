# Shisanji Hokari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shisanji Hokari"}

Dr. **Shisanji Hokari** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was admitted to the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 1966.[^o2] He was a professor emeritus of [Tokyo Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_University) and the president of [Josai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josai_University).

:::
