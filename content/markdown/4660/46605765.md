# Bousfield class

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bousfield class"}

In algebraic topology, the **Bousfield class** of, say, a [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) *X* is the set of all (say) spectra *Y* whose [smash product](wikipedia://en/698010) with *X* is zero: $X \otimes Y = 0$. Two objects are **Bousfield equivalent** if their Bousfield classes are the same.

:::

The notion applies to [module spectra](wikipedia://en/41276205) and in that case one usually qualifies a ring spectrum over which the smash product is taken.
