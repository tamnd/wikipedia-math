# Foundations and Trends in Econometrics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Foundations and Trends in Econometrics"}

***Foundations and Trends in Econometrics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes long survey and tutorial articles in the field of [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390). It was established in 2005 and is published by [Now Publishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_Publishers). The founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [William H. Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H._Greene) ([New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)).

:::
