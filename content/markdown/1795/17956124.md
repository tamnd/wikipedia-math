# HP-45

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="HP-45"}

The **HP-45** is the second [scientific pocket calculator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_pocket_calculator) introduced by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard), adding to the features of the [HP-35](wikipedia://en/449629). It was introduced in 1973[^o3] with an [MSRP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suggested_retail_price) of [US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US$)395[^o4] (US).US Especially noteworthy was its pioneering addition of a [shift key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_key) that gave other keys alternate functions.

:::

The calculator was code-named *Wizard*,[^o5] which is the first known use of a code name for a calculator. It also contained an [Easter egg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_egg_%28media%29) that allowed users to access a not-especially accurate [stopwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stopwatch) mode.[^o6][^o7] An accurate version of the stopwatch mode was officially featured in the 1975 successor of the HP-45, the [HP-55](wikipedia://en/23441708).

## Emulators {#Emulators}

Several individuals and companies make software emulators of the HP 45 series calculators.

**[Nonpareil, high-fidelity simulator for calculators](http://nonpareil.brouhaha.com/)**

Emulates, among other, the HP-45. Licensed under the [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License) (GPL). Available for [Microsoft Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows).

**[HP-45 Emulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20120405051955/http://my.opera.com/HP-45/about)**

HP-45 Emulator written in [Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java_%28programming_language%29). Licensed under the [GPL 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPL_3). Available for [Android](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_%28operating_system%29)[^o10] and [Symbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbian).[^o11]

An Emulator for [Windows Phone 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Phone_7).

**[HP-45 Emulator in JavaScript](https://norbertkehrer.github.io/hp45_js/hp45.html)**

The HP-45 Program ROM was translated to [JavaScript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JavaScript) to have an exact simulation of the original calculator for use in web browsers.

**[HP-45 Emulator in Python](https://sarahkmarr.com/retrohp1973.html)**

Simulates the HP-45 and displays and explains its inner workings. For Linux, MacOS, Windows, CP/M, and more, with minimal mode for low-power machines.
