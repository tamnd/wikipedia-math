# Antoine Song

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Antoine Song"}

**Antoine Y. Song** (born 18 July 1992 in Paris) is a French[^o3] mathematician whose research concerns [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474). He is a professor at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech).[^o4] In 2018, he proved [Yau's conjecture](wikipedia://en/56186448).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Antoine Song was a student at the [École Normale Supérieure de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 2012 to 2015. He obtained a bachelor's and a master's degree in mathematics from the [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6).[^o3] He obtained his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2019 under the supervision of [Fernando Codá Marques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Codá_Marques), with the thesis *In search of minimal hypersurfaces*.

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

It is known that any closed surface possesses infinitely many [closed geodesics](wikipedia://en/19903176). The first problem in the minimal submanifolds section of [Yau](wikipedia://en/303137)'s list ([Yau's conjecture](wikipedia://en/56186448)) asks whether any closed [three-manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-manifold) has infinitely many closed smooth [immersed](wikipedia://en/6993953#Immersed_plane_curves) minimal surfaces. At the time it was known from [Almgren–Pitts min-max theory](wikipedia://en/46587298) the existence of at least one minimal surface. Kei Irie, [Fernando Codá Marques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Codá_Marques), and [André Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André_Neves) solved this problem in the [generic](wikipedia://en/12886758) case [^o8] and later Song proved it in full generality.[^n1]

Together with Conghan Dong,[^n2] he proved a conjecture from 2001 by [G. Huisken](wikipedia://en/39508202) and [T. Ilmanen](wikipedia://en/76365326) on the mathematics of [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), about the [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) in spaces with very little [mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass).[^o11]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

He was a [Clay Research Fellow](wikipedia://en/7655) (2019–2024).[^o13]

He is a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o14][^o15]

He delivered the 2021–2022 [Peccot Lectures](wikipedia://en/60897406) (in 2022, due to the coronavirus pandemic).[^o16]

In 2024, he received the *Frontiers of Science Award*.[^o17]
