# Ioannis Kontoyiannis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ioannis Kontoyiannis"}

**Ioannis Kontoyiannis** (born January 1972) is a Greek mathematician and [information theorist.](wikipedia://en/14773) He is the [Churchill Professor of Mathematics of Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_Professor_of_Mathematics_of_Information) with the Statistical Laboratory, in the [Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Pure_Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Statistics), of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). He is also a Fellow of [Darwin College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_College,_Cambridge), and Chairman of the [Rollo Davidson Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize).[^o3]

:::

He is an affiliated member of the Division of Information Engineering, Cambridge,[^o4] a Research Fellow of the Foundation for Research and Technology - Hellas,[^o5] and a Senior Member of [Robinson College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_College,_Cambridge).

His research interests are in information theory, [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), including their applications in data compression, bioinformatics, [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), and the connections between core information-theoretic ideas and results in probability theory and [additive combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243392).

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

Kontoyiannis earned a B.S. in mathematics from [Imperial College, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) (1992), he obtained a distinction in Part III of the Cambridge University Pure Mathematics Tripos (1993), and he earned an M.S. in statistics (1997) and a Ph.D. in electrical engineering (1998), both from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). Between 1998 and 2018 he taught at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), and at the [Athens University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_University_of_Economics_and_Business). In January 2018 he joined the Information Engineering Division at Cambridge University, as Professor of Information and Communications, and Head of the Signal Processing and Communications Laboratory. Since June 2020 he has been with the [Statistical Laboratory](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/), in the [Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Pure_Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Statistics), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), where he holds the Churchill Chair in Mathematics.
