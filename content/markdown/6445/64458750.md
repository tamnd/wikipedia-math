# Applications of sensitivity analysis in epidemiology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Applications of sensitivity analysis in epidemiology"}

**Sensitivity analysis** studies the relation between the uncertainty in a model-based inference and the uncertainties in the model assumptions.[^o2][^o3] Sensitivity analysis can play an important role in epidemiology, for example in assessing the influence of the unmeasured confounding on the causal conclusions of a study.[^o4] It is also important in all mathematical modelling studies of epidemics.[^o5]

:::

Sensitivity analysis can be used in epidemiology, for example in assessing the influence of the unmeasured confounding on the causal conclusions of a study.[^o4][^o6] The use of [sensitivity analysis](wikipedia://en/620083) in [mathematical modelling of infectious disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modelling_of_infectious_disease) is suggested in [^o5] on the [Coronavirus disease 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019) outbreak. Given the significant [uncertainty](wikipedia://en/63778) at play, the use of [sensitivity analysis](wikipedia://en/620083) to apportion the output uncertainty into input parameters is crucial in the context of [Decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision-making). Examples of applications of sensitivity analysis to modelling of [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) are [^o7] and.[^o8] in particular, the time of intervention in containing the pandemic spread is identified as a key parameter.
