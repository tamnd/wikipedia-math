# FinSet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FinSet"}

In the mathematical field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), **FinSet** is the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) whose [objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object_%28category_theory%29) are all [finite sets](wikipedia://en/11742) and whose [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are all [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) between them. **FinOrd** is the category whose objects are all [finite ordinal numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_ordinal_number) and whose morphisms are all functions between them.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

**FinSet** is a [full subcategory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_subcategory) of **[Set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_%28category%29)**, the category whose objects are all sets and whose morphisms are all functions. Like **Set**, **FinSet** is a [large category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_category).

**FinOrd** is a full subcategory of **FinSet** as by the standard definition, suggested by [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942), each ordinal is the [well-ordered set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-ordered_set) of all smaller ordinals. Unlike **Set** and **FinSet**, **FinOrd** is a [small category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category).

**FinOrd** is a [skeleton](wikipedia://en/1812151) of **FinSet**. Therefore, **FinSet** and **FinOrd** are [equivalent categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivalent_categories).

:::

## Topoi {#Topoi}

Like **Set**, **FinSet** and **FinOrd** are [topoi](wikipedia://en/21391464). As in **Set**, in **FinSet** the [categorical product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_product) of two objects *A* and *B* is given by the [cartesian product](wikipedia://en/24104095) *A* × *B*, the [categorical sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_sum) is given by the [disjoint union](wikipedia://en/188946) *A* + *B*, and the [exponential object](wikipedia://en/2237858) *B*<sup>*A*</sup> is given by the set of all functions with [domain](wikipedia://en/50263) *A* and [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264) *B*. In **FinOrd**, the categorical product of two objects *n* and *m* is given by the [ordinal product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_of_ordinals) *n* · *m*, the categorical sum is given by the [ordinal sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_addition) *n* + *m*, and the [exponential object](wikipedia://en/2237858) is given by the [ordinal exponentiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_exponentiation) *n*<sup>*m*</sup>. The [subobject classifier](wikipedia://en/366909) in **FinSet** and **FinOrd** is the same as in **Set**. **FinOrd** is an example of a [PRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PRO_%28category_theory%29).
