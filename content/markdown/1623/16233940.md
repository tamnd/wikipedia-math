# Timeline of abelian varieties

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of abelian varieties"}

This is a **timeline of the theory of [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties)** in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), including elliptic curves.

:::

## Early history {#Early_history}

- 3rd century AD [Diophantus](wikipedia://en/9110) of Alexandria studies rational points on elliptic curves
- c. 1000 [Al-Karaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Karaji) writes on [congruent numbers](wikipedia://en/2334769)[^o3]

## Seventeenth century {#Seventeenth_century}

- Fermat studies [descent for elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/358069)
- 1643 Fermat poses an elliptic curve [Diophantine equation](wikipedia://en/9109)[^o5]
- 1670 Fermat's son published his *Diophantus* with notes

## Eighteenth century {#Eighteenth_century}

- 1718 [Giulio Carlo Fagnano dei Toschi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giulio_Carlo_Fagnano_dei_Toschi), studies the rectification of the [lemniscate](wikipedia://en/250908), addition results for [elliptic integrals](wikipedia://en/9960).[^o7]
- 1736 [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) writes on the [pendulum equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendulum_%28mathematics%29#Arbitrary-amplitude_period) without the small-angle approximation.[^o8]
- 1738 Euler writes on curves of genus 1 considered by Fermat and [Frenicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frenicle)
- 1750 Euler writes on elliptic integrals
- 23 December 1751 – 27 January 1752: Birth of the theory of [elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/69939), according to later remarks of Jacobi, as Euler writes on Fagnano's work.[^o9]
- 1775 [John Landen](wikipedia://en/1400598) publishes [Landen's transformation](wikipedia://en/21424424),[^o10] an [isogeny](wikipedia://en/1085176) formula.
- 1786 [Adrien-Marie Legendre](wikipedia://en/49695) begins to write on [elliptic integrals](wikipedia://en/9960)
- 1797 [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) discovers [double periodicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_periodicity) of the [lemniscate function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemniscate_function)[^o11]
- 1799 Gauss finds the connection of the length of a lemniscate and a case of the [arithmetic-geometric mean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic-geometric_mean), giving a numerical method for a [complete elliptic integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_elliptic_integral).[^o12]

## Nineteenth century {#Nineteenth_century}

- 1826 [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573), [Abel-Jacobi map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel-Jacobi_map)
- 1827 [Inversion of elliptic integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inversion_of_elliptic_integral) independently by Abel and [Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi](wikipedia://en/210340)
- 1829 Jacobi, *Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum*, introduces four [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745) of one variable
- 1835 Jacobi points out the use of the group law for [diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205), in *De usu Theoriae Integralium Ellipticorum et Integralium Abelianorum in Analysi Diophantea*[^o14]
- 1836-7 [Friedrich Julius Richelot](wikipedia://en/20346321), the [Richelot isogeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richelot_isogeny).[^o15]
- 1847 [Adolph Göpel](wikipedia://en/18550639) gives the equation of the [Kummer surface](wikipedia://en/647758)[^o16]
- 1851 [Johann Georg Rosenhain](wikipedia://en/34905444) writes a prize essay on the inversion problem in genus 2.[^o17]
- c. 1850 [Thomas Weddle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Weddle) - [Weddle surface](wikipedia://en/23439425)
- 1856 [Weierstrass elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/447181)
- 1857 [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980)[^o18] lays the foundations for further work on abelian varieties in dimension > 1, introducing the [Riemann bilinear relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_bilinear_relations) and [Riemann theta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_theta_function).
- 1865 [Carl Johannes Thomae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Johannes_Thomae), *Theorie der ultraelliptischen Funktionen und Integrale erster und zweiter Ordnung*[^o19]
- 1866 [Alfred Clebsch](wikipedia://en/1369957) and [Paul Gordan](wikipedia://en/1059850), *Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen*
- 1869 [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) proves an [abelian function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_function) satisfies an [algebraic addition theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_addition_theorem)
- 1879, [Charles Auguste Briot](wikipedia://en/33375467), *Théorie des fonctions abéliennes*
- 1880 In a letter to [Richard Dedekind](wikipedia://en/23475106), [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450) describes his *[Jugendtraum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker's_Jugendtraum)*,[^o20] to use [complex multiplication](wikipedia://en/537183) theory to generate [abelian extensions](wikipedia://en/174448) of [imaginary quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_quadratic_field)
- 1884 [Sofia Kovalevskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kovalevskaya) writes on the [reduction of abelian functions to elliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduction_of_abelian_functions_to_elliptic_functions)[^o21]
- 1888 [Friedrich Schottky](wikipedia://en/5872505) finds a non-trivial condition on the [theta constants](wikipedia://en/44466777) for curves of genus $g = 4$, launching the [Schottky problem](wikipedia://en/4125909).
- 1891 [Appell–Humbert theorem](wikipedia://en/23379150) of [Paul Émile Appell](wikipedia://en/3359510) and [Georges Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Humbert), classifies the [holomorphic line bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_line_bundle) on an [abelian surface](wikipedia://en/974181) by [cocycle](wikipedia://en/2870898) data.
- 1894 *Die Entwicklung der Theorie der algebräischen Functionen in älterer und neuerer Zeit*, report by [Alexander von Brill](wikipedia://en/28565454) and [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337)
- 1895 [Wilhelm Wirtinger](wikipedia://en/2044921), *Untersuchungen über Thetafunktionen*, studies [Prym varieties](wikipedia://en/4116892)
- 1897 [H. F. Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._F._Baker), *Abelian Functions: Abel's Theorem and the Allied Theory of Theta Functions*

## Twentieth century {#Twentieth_century}

- c.1910 The theory of [Poincaré normal functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_normal_function) implies that the [Picard variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picard_variety) and [Albanese variety](wikipedia://en/1814828) are [isogenous](wikipedia://en/1085176).[^o23]
- 1913 [Torelli's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torelli's_theorem)[^o24]
- 1916 [Gaetano Scorza](wikipedia://en/35074517)[^o25] applies the term "abelian variety" to [complex tori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_tori).
- 1921 [Solomon Lefschetz](wikipedia://en/384384) shows that any complex torus with Riemann matrix satisfying the necessary conditions can be embedded in some [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) using theta-functions
- 1922 [Louis Mordell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Mordell) proves [Mordell's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell's_theorem): the rational points on an elliptic curve over the rational numbers form a [finitely-generated abelian group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_abelian_group)
- 1929 [Arthur B. Coble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_B._Coble), *Algebraic Geometry and Theta Functions*
- 1939 [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927)[^o26]
- c. 1940 [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) defines "abelian variety"
- 1952 Weil defines an [intermediate Jacobian](wikipedia://en/3092994)
- [Theorem of the cube](wikipedia://en/7044103)
- [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862)
- [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) classifies [holomorphic vector bundles](wikipedia://en/4794668) on an elliptic curve
- 1961 [Goro Shimura](wikipedia://en/174724) and [Yutaka Taniyama](wikipedia://en/1077843), *Complex Multiplication of Abelian Varieties and its Applications to Number Theory*
- [Néron model](wikipedia://en/9190726)
- [Birch–Swinnerton–Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton–Dyer_conjecture)
- [Moduli space for abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_space_for_abelian_varieties)
- [Duality of abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duality_of_abelian_varieties)
- c.1967 [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) develops a new theory of the [equations defining abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/6990584)
- 1968 [Serre–Tate theorem on good reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre–Tate_theorem_on_good_reduction) extends the results of [Max Deuring](wikipedia://en/5638258) on elliptic curves to the abelian variety case.[^o27]
- c. 1980 [Mukai–Fourier transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukai–Fourier_transform): the [Poincaré line bundle](wikipedia://en/7045490#Poincaré_line_bundle) as Mukai–Fourier kernel induces an equivalence of the [derived categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_categories) of [coherent sheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coherent_sheaves) for an abelian variety and its dual.[^o28]
- 1983 [Takahiro Shiota](wikipedia://en/32594980) proves [Novikov's conjecture on the Schottky problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novikov's_conjecture_on_the_Schottky_problem)
- 1985 [Jean-Marc Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Marc_Fontaine) shows that any positive-dimensional abelian variety over the rationals has bad reduction somewhere.[^o29]

## Twenty-first century {#Twenty-first_century}

- 2001 Proof of the [modularity theorem](wikipedia://en/174475) for elliptic curves is completed.
