# Hemi-dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hemi-dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hemi-dodecahedron** is an [abstract](wikipedia://en/1211056), [regular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/333299), containing half the [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361). It can be realized as a [projective polyhedron](wikipedia://en/26952055) (a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of the [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325) by 6 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014)), which can be visualized by constructing the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) as a hemisphere where opposite points along the boundary are connected and dividing the hemisphere into three equal parts.

:::

It has 6 pentagonal faces, 15 edges, and 10 vertices.

## Petersen graph {#Petersen_graph}

From the point of view of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) this is an embedding of the [Petersen graph](wikipedia://en/194926) on a [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325). With this embedding, the [dual graph](wikipedia://en/2536864) is *K*<sub>6</sub> (the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) with 6 vertices) --- see [hemi-icosahedron](wikipedia://en/10393682).
