# Daniel Bonevac

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Bonevac"}

**Daniel Albert Bonevac** (born 1955)[^o3] is a professor of philosophy at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). His areas of interest are [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939), [ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics), and [Eastern philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_philosophy).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Bonevac is from [Baldwin, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin,_Pennsylvania), a borough near [Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh). His family moved to [Richmond, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia) when he was eight-years-old and later moved to [West Hartford, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hartford,_Connecticut). He was raised [Calvinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvinist) and attended a [Presbyterian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian) church in his youth, and he met his wife in high school.[^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bonevac majored in philosophy at [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College), graduating in 1975. He received a master's degree in philosophy from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) in 1977 and a Ph.D. in 1980.[^o7] His doctoral dissertation was "Ontological Reduction and Abstract Entities".[^o8]

He joined the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) as an assistant professor of philosophy in 1980, chaired the department from 1991 to 2001, and became full professor there in 1992.[^o7]

## Politics {#Politics}

In autumn 2016, Bonevac joined 145 other scholars and writers in declaring support for [Donald Trump for president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_presidential_campaign,_2016).[^o10][^o11] He was one of four philosophy professors in the United States, including fellow UT philosophy professor [Robert Koons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Koons), to be included on the list.[^o12] Bonevac's support is because he believes Trump is correct in pointing out the "dangers of a globalist attitude" to U.S. interests and because of Trump's opposition to the adminstrative state.[^o13]

On May 15, 2024, Bonevac and another UT-Austin professor filed a lawsuit in federal court demanding the right to fail students if they miss class for abortion, and to refuse to hire teaching assistants who have used mail-order abortion medicine. In his statement on the issue, Bonevac stated that he has "no intention of complying with" new [Title IX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_IX) regulations prohibiting discrimination based on gender identity and requiring accommodation for absences caused by abortion, will not use students' preferred pronouns, and will not allow "cross-dressing" teaching assistants.[^o14][^o15]
