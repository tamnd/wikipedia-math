# Elliott Mendelson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elliott Mendelson"}

**Elliott Mendelson** (May 24, 1931 – May 7, 2020) was an American [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He was a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Queens College of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens_College,_City_University_of_New_York),[^o3] and the [Graduate Center, CUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center,_CUNY). He was Jr. Fellow, [Society of Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Fellows), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), 1956–58.[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Mendelson earned his BA from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and PhD from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o6]

Mendelson taught mathematics at the college level for more than 30 years, and is the author of books on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

His *Introduction to Mathematical Logic*, first published in 1964, was reviewed by [Dirk van Dalen](wikipedia://en/26828544) who noted that it included "a large variety of subjects that should be part of the education of any mathematics student with an interest in foundational matters."[^o7]
