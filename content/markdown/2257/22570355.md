# Patrick Brosnan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Patrick Brosnan"}

**Patrick Brosnan** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work on [motives](wikipedia://en/430680), [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590), and [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1998 under the direction of [Spencer Bloch](wikipedia://en/25794633). Brosnan is the 2009 recipient of the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o3]

:::

In 2003, Brosnan (in joint work with [Prakash Belkale](wikipedia://en/48316436)) disproved the *Spanning Tree Conjecture* of [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509).[^o4]
