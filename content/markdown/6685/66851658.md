# Uniform boundedness conjecture for rational points

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform boundedness conjecture for rational points"}

In [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), the **uniform boundedness conjecture for rational points** asserts that for a given [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) $K$ and a positive integer $g \geq 2$, there exists a number $N(K,g)$ depending only on $K$ and $g$ such that for any [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) $C$ defined over $K$ having [genus](wikipedia://en/3115807) equal to $g$ has at most $N(K,g)$ $K$-[rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200). This is a refinement of [Faltings' theorem](wikipedia://en/172199), which asserts that the set of $K$-rational points $C(K)$ is necessarily finite.

:::

## Progress {#Progress}

The first significant progress towards the conjecture was due to [Caporaso](wikipedia://en/56679890), [Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251), and [Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679).[^o3] They proved that the conjecture holds if one assumes the [Bombieri–Lang conjecture](wikipedia://en/3142105).

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Mazur's conjecture B"}

## Mazur's conjecture B {#Mazur's_conjecture_B}

**Mazur's conjecture B** is a weaker variant of the uniform boundedness conjecture that asserts that there should be a number $N(K,g,r)$ such that for any algebraic curve $C$ defined over $K$ having genus $g$ and whose [Jacobian variety](wikipedia://en/489734) $J_C$ has [Mordell–Weil rank](wikipedia://en/23087727) over $K$ equal to $r$, the number of $K$-rational points of $C$ is at most $N(K,g,r)$.

Michael Stoll proved that Mazur's conjecture B holds for [hyperelliptic curves](wikipedia://en/499361) with the additional hypothesis that $r \leq g - 3$.[^o6] Stoll's result was further refined by [Katz](wikipedia://en/54434627), [Rabinoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Rabinoff), and [Zureick-Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Zureick-Brown) in 2015.[^o7] Both of these works rely on [Chabauty's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chabauty's_method).

Mazur's conjecture B was resolved by [Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886), [Gao](wikipedia://en/83288827), and [Habegger](wikipedia://en/67011825) in 2021 using the earlier work of Gao and Habegger on the geometric [Bogomolov conjecture](wikipedia://en/18992933) and Gao's work on Betti maps instead of Chabauty's method.[^o8]

:::
