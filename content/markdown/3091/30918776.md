# WWW Interactive Multipurpose Server

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="WWW Interactive Multipurpose Server"}

*Image: Wims-4.01e-accueil-fr.png.* Screen shot of version 4.01 at the original University of Nice hosting.

The **WWW Interactive Multipurpose Server (WIMS)** (sometimes referred to as *WWW Interactive Mathematics Server*) project is designed for supporting intensive [mathematical exercises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_exercise) via the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) or in a computer-equipped classroom with server-side interactivity, accessible at the address http://wims.unice.fr.

:::

The system has the following main features:

- A modular design allowing applications and software interfaces to be created and maintained independently from each other.
- Features interfaces for software including MuPAD, PARI/GP, [Gnuplot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnuplot), [POV-Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POV-Ray), Co.
- Dynamic rendering of mathematical formulas and animated graphics.
- A structure of virtual classes, including mechanisms for automatic score gathering and processing.

[^o5]

The program is open source and freely available under the [GNU General Public Licence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_Licence), however each WIMS module has its own copyright policy, which may differ from that of the server program.[^o3]

It is often cited and linked for its sophisticated "online calculator" tools capable of generating animated [GIFs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIF) of parametric 2D or 3D graphs or allowing prime tests with very large numbers.

## Author {#Author}

Xiao Gang was a professor at [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis). He was interested in solar energy and algebraic geometry. He was also the active site manager of the WIMS of the university he worked for.[^o7] Xiao Gang died on June 27, 2014.[^o8]

Xiao Gang taught himself during the [Up to the Mountains and Down to the Countryside Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_to_the_Countryside_Movement). He obtained his master's degree from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China). Xiao obtained his Ph.D. degree from [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 1984. Xiao Gang returned to China and became a lecturer at [East China Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Normal_University). He was promoted to professor in 1986, and was awarded the Shiing-Shen Chern Prize in Mathematics in 1991. In 1992, Xiao became a professor at [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis).[^o9]
