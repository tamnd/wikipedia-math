# Albert Leon Whiteman Memorial Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert Leon Whiteman Memorial Prize"}

The **Albert Leon Whiteman Memorial Prize** is awarded by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for notable exposition and exceptional scholarship in the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).

:::

The prize was endowed in 1998 with funds provided by Sally Whiteman in memory of her late husband Albert Leon Whiteman. Originally it was awarded every 4 years with the first prized handed out in 2001. Since 2009 the prize is awarded every 3 years and carries a prize money of \$5000.

## Past recipients {#Past_recipients}

- 2001 [Thomas Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_W._Hawkins,_Jr.)
- 2005 [Harold M. Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_M._Edwards)
- 2009 [Jeremy Gray](wikipedia://en/24721410)
- 2012 [Joseph Dauben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Dauben)
- 2015 [Umberto Bottazzini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umberto_Bottazzini)
- 2018 [Karen Parshall](wikipedia://en/36594635)
- 2021 [Judith Grabiner](wikipedia://en/14390402)
- 2024 [Leo Corry](wikipedia://en/50593572)
