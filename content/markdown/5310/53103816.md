# Sallie Ann Keller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sallie Ann Keller"}

**Sallie Ann Keller** (born 1956, also published as **Sallie Keller–McNulty**) is a statistician and a former president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (2006).[^o3]

:::

She is currently a Distinguished Professor in Biocomplexity and director of the Social and Decision Analytics Division within the Biocomplexity Institute at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia).[^o4] She also joined the [United States Census Bureau](wikipedia://en/57070) in October 2022 as Chief Scientist and Associate Director for Research and Methodology, replacing [John M. Abowd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_M._Abowd).[^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Keller received a B.S. and M.S. in mathematics from the [University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida), and her Ph.D. in statistics from the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) in 1983.[^o7]

She held administrative and academic positions at various universities, including [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech), [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), and [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University). Outside of academia, Keller has also held positions with several federal government institutions, including the [Science and Technology Policy Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_Technology_Policy_Institute), the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation), and the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory).[^o7]

## Affiliations {#Affiliations}

Keller is a fellow of the ASA (1997) and the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), as well as an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o5] She was elected a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 2020 for development and application of engineering and statistical techniques in support of national security and industry.

Keller has edited *Statistical Science*, the *Journal of Computational and Graphical Studies*, and the *Journal of the American Statistical Society*.[^o9]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2002 Keller-McNulty was recipient of the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o11] Keller won the [Wilks Memorial Award](wikipedia://en/15966180) in 2021.
