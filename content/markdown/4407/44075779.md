# George Herbert Swift Jr

*Image: George Herb Swift Jr 1954 (1926-2014).jpg.* Photo of George Herb Swift Jr in 1954

*Image: George Herb Swift Jr 2012 (1926-2014).jpg.* Photo of George Herb Swift Jr in 2012

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Herbert Swift Jr"}

**George Herbert Swift Jr** (July 1, 1926, [Minot, ND](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minot,_ND) – September 23, 2014) was an American mathematician and computer scientist. Swift attended the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), earning a master's degree in mathematics in 1951, before attending the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), where he earned a PhD in mathematics in 1954 under [Edwin Hewitt](wikipedia://en/7482590) on irregular [Borel measure](wikipedia://en/4322). He began at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 1954 as an instructor before being IBM hired him in 1956; he would spend 32 years with the company, where he contributed to the development of the IBM 5100. He retired in 1988 from IBM to teach full-time in mathematics as well as computer science.[^o2]
