# Symplectic category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symplectic category"}

In mathematics, Weinstein's **symplectic category** is (roughly) a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) whose objects are [symplectic manifolds](wikipedia://en/28356) and whose morphisms are **canonical relations**, inclusions of [Lagrangian submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_submanifold) *L* into $M \times N^{-}$, where the superscript minus means minus the given symplectic form (for example, the graph of a [symplectomorphism](wikipedia://en/312304); hence, minus). The notion was introduced by [Alan Weinstein](wikipedia://en/7344764), according to whom "Quantization problems[^o2] suggest that the category of symplectic manifolds and symplectomorphisms be augmented by the inclusion of canonical relations as morphisms." The composition of canonical relations is given by a [fiber product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_product).

:::

Strictly speaking, the symplectic category is not a well-defined category (since the composition may not be well-defined) without some transversality conditions.
