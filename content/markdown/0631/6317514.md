# Cantellation (geometry)

*Image: Small rhombicuboctahedron.png.* A [cantellated cube](wikipedia://en/26213) - Red faces are reduced. Edges are bevelled, forming new yellow square faces. Vertices are truncated, forming new blue triangle faces.

*Image: Cantellated\_cubic\_honeycomb.png.* A [cantellated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellated_cubic_honeycomb) [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) - Purple cubes are cantellated. Edges are bevelled, forming new blue cubic cells. Vertices are truncated, forming new red [rectified cube](wikipedia://en/6280) cells.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cantellation"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellation** is a 2nd-order [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) in any dimension that [bevels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bevel) a [regular polytope](wikipedia://en/518693) at its [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) and at its [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), creating a new [facet](wikipedia://en/7186647) in place of each edge and of each vertex. Cantellation also applies to [regular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_tiling) and [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654). Cantellating a polyhedron is also rectifying its [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366).

:::

Cantellation (for polyhedra and tilings) is also called *[expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070)* by [Alicia Boole Stott](wikipedia://en/3325459): it corresponds to moving the faces of the regular form away from the center, and filling in a new face in the gap for each opened edge and for each opened vertex.

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Notation"}

## Notation {#Notation}

A cantellated polytope is represented by an extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) ***t***<sub>0,2</sub>{*p*,*q*,...} or ***r***$\begin{Bmatrix}p\\q\\...\end{Bmatrix}$ or ***rr***{*p*,*q*,...}.

For [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470), a cantellation offers a direct sequence from a [regular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/333299) to its [dual](wikipedia://en/8815).

**Example: cantellation sequence between cube and octahedron:**

*Image: Cube cantellation sequence.svg.*

Example: a [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) is a cantellated [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606).

For higher-dimensional polytopes, a cantellation offers a direct sequence from a regular polytope to its [birectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) form.

:::
