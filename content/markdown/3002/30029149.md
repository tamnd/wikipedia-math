# Demographics of Cologne

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Cologne"}

[Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne) (Köln) is Germany's [fourth-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Germany_with_more_than_100,000_inhabitants) and the largest city in the Rhineland. As of 31 December 2011, there were officially 1,017,155 residents.[^o2] The city is center of the [Cologne/Bonn Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne/Bonn_Region) with around 3 million inhabitants (including the neighboring cities of [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), [Hürth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hürth), [Leverkusen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverkusen), and [Bergisch Gladbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergisch_Gladbach)).

:::

### Medieval Cologne {#Medieval_Cologne}

*Image: Nuremberg chronicles - colonia.png.* Woodcut of Cologne from the [Nuremberg Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg_Chronicle), 1493

Since the construction of the Medieval wall in 1180, the area of the old [imperial city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_imperial_city) of Cologne has not changed for more than 600 years and was only extended over the old city walls in 1794, just short before the arrival of French troops and Cologne's incorporation into the [First French Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_French_Empire).

### Modern Cologne {#Modern_Cologne}

#### 16th through 19th century {#16th_through_19th_century}

*Image: Köln-und-Deutz-nach-Wenzeslaw-Hollar-von-Prachna-1636.JPG.* Copper engraving of Cologne by Hollar von Prachna, Wenceslaus, 1636

*Image: Blick von Deutz auf die Stadt, Köln ca 1700.jpg.* Coloured drawing of Cologne, ca. 1700

After 1815, the [Kingdom of Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia) enforced the construction of fortifications which again hindered any growth for the city. Only with the acquisition of these fortifications in 1881, the city of Cologne had the possibility of a gradual territorial expansion. On 12. November 1883 a strip of territory from parts of the municipalities of [Ehrenfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrenfeld,_Cologne), Kriel, Longerich, Müngersdorf and Rondorf was added to the city. Since 1886, the [Cologne City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_City_Hall) intensified negotiations with the surrounding communities, and on 1 April 1888 ended in a first major incorporation.
