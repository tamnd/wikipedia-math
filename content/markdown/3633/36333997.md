# Rising moving average

*Image: Rising Moving Average.png.* 3-day Rising moving average on a 5-day close-price weighted moving average

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rising moving average"}

The **rising moving average** is a [technical indicator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_indicator) used in [stock market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market) trading. Most commonly found visually, the pattern is spotted with a moving average overlay on a stock chart or price series. When the moving average has been rising consecutively for a number of days, this is used as a buy signal, to indicate a rising trend forming.

:::

While the rising moving average indicator is commonly used by [investors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investor) without realising, there has been significant [backtesting](wikipedia://en/10283346) on historic stock data to calculate the performance of the rising moving average. Simulations have found that shorter rising averages, within the 3- to 10-day period, are more profitable overall than longer rising averages (e.g. 20 days). These have only been tested on US [equity stocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_stock) however.[^o2]
