# Jean Coulomb

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Coulomb"}

**Jean Marie François Joseph Coulomb**[^o3] (7 November 1904 – 26 February 1999) was a French [geophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and one of the early members of the [Bourbaki group](wikipedia://en/167394) of mathematicians.[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Coulomb was born in [Blida, Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blida,_Algeria). From April 1935 to 1937, he was a member of the Bourbaki group of mathematicians.[^o7]

He was a professor in the Faculty of Sciences of Paris from 1941 to 1972, and director of the [Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_Physique_du_Globe_de_Paris) from 1941 to 1959.

He was director general of CNRS, the French [National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) from 1957 to 1962, president of [CNES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNES) from 1962 to 1967, and president of the [Bureau des Longitudes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_des_Longitudes) from 1967 to 1969.

Coulomb was the President of the [Société astronomique de France (SAF)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_astronomique_de_France), the French astronomical society, from 1958 to 1960.[^o8]

From 1967 to 1971, he was also president of the [International Union of Geodesy and Geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Geodesy_and_Geophysics) (IUGG), and from 1972 to 1974 of the [International Council for Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Council_for_Science) (ICSU).

In 1960, he was elected to the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences), and was its president from 1976 to 1977.

His work was in the fields of [seismology](wikipedia://en/28935) (theory of surface waves), [geomagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomagnetism), and [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904) (atmospheric electricity and the physics of clouds).

## Awards and distinctions {#Awards_and_distinctions}

- 1956 — [Prix Charles Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Lagrange_Prize)[^o11]
- 1961 — [Prix Jules Janssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Jules_Janssen)
- 1971 — [Prix des Trois Physiciens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Physicists_Prize)
- 1991 — Grand Cross of the [Légion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Légion_d'Honneur)
- Grand Croix of the [Ordre du Mérite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_du_Mérite)
- Officer of the [Ordre du Mérite Saharien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_du_Mérite_Saharien)
