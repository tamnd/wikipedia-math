# Shintani's unit theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shintani's unit theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Shintani's unit theorem** introduced by (txt, proposition 4) is a refinement of [Dirichlet's unit theorem](wikipedia://en/460227) and states that a subgroup of finite index of the totally positive units of a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) has a fundamental domain given by a rational polyhedric cone in the [Minkowski space](wikipedia://en/39140988) of the field (Neukirch 1999, p. 507).

:::
