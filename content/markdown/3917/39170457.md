# Jeremy Avigad

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jeremy Avigad"}

**Jeremy Avigad** is a professor of philosophy and a professor of mathematical sciences at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University).

:::

He received a B.A. in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1989, and a Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) in 1995 under the supervision of [Jack Silver](wikipedia://en/21626512).[^o3] He has contributed to the areas of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [foundations](wikipedia://en/169358), [formal verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_verification) and [interactive theorem proving](wikipedia://en/1258607), and the [philosophy](wikipedia://en/46439) and [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).[^o4] He became Director of the Hoskinson Center for Formal Mathematics at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) after cryptocurrency entrepreneur [Charles Hoskinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Hoskinson) donated \$20 million in September 2021 to establish it.[^o5][^o6]
