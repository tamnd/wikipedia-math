# Support vertex

*Image: Support vertex.svg.* Each blue vertex in the graph shown is a **support vertex**, adjacent to at least one [leaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaf_%28graph_theory%29) (highlighted green). The darker blue vertex is a *strong* support vertex, and the two lighter blue vertices are *weak* support vertices.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Support vertex"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **support vertex** is a vertex that is adjacent to a [leaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaf_%28graph_theory%29) (a vertex of [degree](wikipedia://en/1374448) one). Support vertices play an important role in the study of [domination](wikipedia://en/1747972) in graphs, since every support vertex must belong to every minimum dominating set.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a graph. A vertex $v \in V$ is called a **support vertex** if $v$ is adjacent to at least one leaf of $G$.[^o2]

A support vertex is called a *weak* support vertex if it is adjacent to exactly one leaf, and a *strong* support vertex if it is adjacent to two or more leaves.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Every support vertex belongs to every minimum [dominating set](wikipedia://en/1747972) of a graph.[^o2]
- If a graph has no weak support vertex, then its [domination number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domination_number) equals its [certified domination number](wikipedia://en/82206278).[^o8]
- Every support vertex belongs to every minimum [certified dominating set](wikipedia://en/82206278) of a graph.[^o8]
- A tree $T$ of order $n$ has a [perfect matching](wikipedia://en/581797) if and only if $\gamma_t^{\text{gr}}(T) = n$, where $\gamma_t^{\text{gr}}$ denotes the Grundy total domination number. The characterization of trees achieving the lower bound for this parameter involves the structure of support vertices: among trees with no strong support vertex, the bound $\gamma_t^{\text{gr}}(T) \geq \tfrac{2}{3}(n + 1)$ holds.[^o9]

:::
