# Daniel Lazard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Lazard"}

**Daniel Lazard** (born December 10, 1941) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). He is emeritus professor at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Daniel Lazard was born in [Carpentras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpentras), in southern [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). His undergraduate education was at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). Following graduate work at the École Normale Supérieure and the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), he was granted a [doctorat d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'état) in 1968 by the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). His dissertation was supervised by the [commutative algebraist](wikipedia://en/245990) [Pierre Samuel](wikipedia://en/2604194), and was titled *"Autour de la platitude"* ("Around flatness", or literally "Around the platitude").[^o4][^o5]

After 1970, his main area of research changed to [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805), particularly [multivariate polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_polynomial), [computational algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997#Computational_algebraic_geometry) and [systems of polynomial equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_of_polynomial_equations). To mark his retirement at the end of 2004, there was a conference at Pierre and Marie Curie University devoted to his subject area.[^o6] In 2009, a special issue of the *[Journal of Symbolic Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Computation)* was published in his honor.[^o7] To date, Lazard has authored or co-authored more than 40 journal articles, book chapters, conference papers, and other publications.[^o8]
