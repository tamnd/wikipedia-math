# David Raymond Curtiss

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Raymond Curtiss"}

**David Raymond Curtiss** (January 12, 1878 – April 29, 1953) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He served as president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) from 1935 to 1936. He was also vice president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Curtiss was born in [Derby, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby,_Connecticut). He attended the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California), earning a bachelor's degree in 1899 and a master's degree in 1901. He earned a doctorate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under [Maxime Bôcher](wikipedia://en/3180285) and [William Fogg Osgood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Fogg_Osgood) in 1903. He completed a postdoctoral fellowship at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1904.

In 1904, Curtiss taught at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) for one year. He then served as a professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) from 1905 to 1943, including 20 years as Chair of the Mathematics Department. Curtiss authored [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261) and [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) with [Elton James Moulton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_James_Moulton). He also published the second [Carus Mathematical Monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carus_Mathematical_Monograph), *Analytic Functions of a Complex Variable.*[^o5]

His brother was astrophysicist [Ralph Hamilton Curtiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Hamilton_Curtiss). His son was computer pioneer [John Hamilton Curtiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hamilton_Curtiss). He and his wife, who was seriously ill, committed [suicide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide) by [carbon monoxide poisoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_monoxide_poisoning) in the garage of their home in [Redlands, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redlands,_California).[^o6]
