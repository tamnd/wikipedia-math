# Petersson trace formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Petersson trace formula"}

In [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), the **Petersson trace formula** is a kind of orthogonality relation between coefficients of a holomorphic [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000). It is a specialization of the more general [Kuznetsov trace formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuznetsov_trace_formula).

:::

In its simplest form the Petersson trace formula is as follows. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be an [orthonormal basis](wikipedia://en/195063) of $S_k(\Gamma(1))$, the space of cusp forms of weight $k>2$ on $SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$. Then for any positive integers $m,n$ we have

$$\frac{\Gamma(k-1)}{(4\pi \sqrt{mn})^{k-1}} \sum_{f \in \mathcal{F}} \bar{\hat{f}}(m) \hat{f}(n) = \delta_{mn} + 2\pi i^{-k} \sum_{c > 0}\frac{S(m,n;c)}{c} J_{k-1}\left(\frac{4\pi \sqrt{mn}}{c}\right),$$

where $\delta$ is the [Kronecker delta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker_delta_function), $S$ is the [Kloosterman sum](wikipedia://en/2993504) and $J$ is the [Bessel function](wikipedia://en/4700) of the first kind.
