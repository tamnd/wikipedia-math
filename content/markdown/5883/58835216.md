# Esther Arkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Esther Arkin"}

**Esther M.** (**Estie**) **Arkin** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): אסתר ארקין) is an Israeli–American mathematician and computer scientist whose research interests include [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476), [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555), and the design and analysis of [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775). She is a professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University). At Stony Brook, she also directs the undergraduate program in applied mathematics and statistics, and is an affiliated faculty member with the department of computer science.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Arkin graduated from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1981. She earned a master's degree at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1983, and completed her Ph.D. at Stanford in 1986. Her doctoral dissertation, *Complexity of Cycle and Path Problems in Graphs*, was supervised by [Christos Papadimitriou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christos_Papadimitriou). After working as a visiting professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), she joined the Stony Brook faculty in 1991.
