# Hannan Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hannan Medal"}

The **Hannan Medal in the Mathematical Sciences** is awarded every two years by the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) to recognize achievements by Australians in the fields of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210), [applied](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833), and [statistical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_science).

:::

This medal commemorates the work of the late [Edward J. Hannan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_J._Hannan), [FAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science), for his achievements in [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis).
