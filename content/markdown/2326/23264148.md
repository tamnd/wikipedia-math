# George Brown (inventor)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Brown"}

**George Brown** (1650–1730) was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) [arithmetician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetician), and inventor of two incomplete [mechanical calculating machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_calculating_machine) now kept at the [National Museum of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_Scotland). In 1698 he was granted a patent for his mechanical calculating device.

:::

He was minister of [Stranraer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranraer), schoolmaster in [Fordyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordyce,_Aberdeenshire), Banffshire, and in 1680 schoolmaster at [Kilmaurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilmaurs), Ayrshire. He invented a method of teaching the simple rules of [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), which he explained in his *Rotula Arithmetica*, 1700. He wrote other arithmetical works; the last of them, *Arithmetica Infinitorum*, was endorsed by [John Keill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Keill).
