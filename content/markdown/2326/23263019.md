# Józef Emanuel Jankowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Józef Emanuel Jankowski"}

**Józef Emanuel Jankowski** (1790–1847) was a professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at [Kraków University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Jankowski was [Feliks Jaroński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feliks_Jaroński)'s successor as professor of philosophy at [Kraków University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) from 1818 and author of a *Logic*.[^o4]

Jankowski was one of nearly all the university professors of philosophy in Poland before the [November 1830–31 Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_Uprising) who held a position that shunned both [Positivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivism) and [metaphysical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) speculation, affined to the [Scottish philosophers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_School_of_Common_Sense) but linked in certain respects to [Kantian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant) critique.[^o5]
