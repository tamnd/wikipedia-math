# Largest empty sphere

*Image: Espace octaedrique cubique faces centrees.svg.* The dashed circle is the outline of the largest empty sphere in the [close-packing of spheres](wikipedia://en/901260). See also *[Interstitial defect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstitial_defect)*.

*Image: Plus grand cercle vide voronoi.svg.* Finding the largest empty circle using the Voronoi diagram (two solutions).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Largest empty sphere"}

In [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), the **largest empty sphere** problem is the problem of finding a [hypersphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypersphere) of largest radius in *d*-dimensional space whose interior does not overlap with any given obstacles.

:::

## Two dimensions {#Two_dimensions}

The **largest empty circle** problem is the problem of finding a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) of largest radius in [the plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) whose interior does not overlap with any given obstacles.

A common special case is as follows. Given *n* points in the plane, find a largest circle centered within their [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) and enclosing none of them. The problem may be solved using [Voronoi diagrams](wikipedia://en/177668) in [optimal time](wikipedia://en/405944) $\Theta(n\, \log\, n)$.[^o3][^o4]
