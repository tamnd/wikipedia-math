# Normal plane (geometry)

*Image: Minimal surface curvature planes-en.svg.* [Saddle surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_surface) with normal planes in directions of principal [curvatures](wikipedia://en/60770).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normal plane"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **normal plane** is any plane containing the [normal vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_vector) of a [surface](wikipedia://en/27865) at a particular point.

:::

The normal plane also refers to the plane that is [perpendicular](wikipedia://en/76944) to the [tangent vector](wikipedia://en/191999) of a [space curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_curve); (this plane also contains the normal vector) see [Frenet–Serret formulas](wikipedia://en/666987).

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Normal section"}

## Normal section {#Normal_section}

The **normal section** of a [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) at a particular [point](wikipedia://en/593693) is the [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) produced by the [intersection](wikipedia://en/41780044) of that surface with a normal plane.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

The [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) of the normal section is called the *[normal curvature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_curvature)*. If the surface is bow or cylinder shaped, the maximum and the minimum of these curvatures are the *[principal curvatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_curvatures)*. If the surface is [saddle shaped](wikipedia://en/640249) the maxima of both sides are the principal curvatures. The product of the principal curvatures is the [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) of the surface (negative for saddle shaped surfaces). The mean of the principal curvatures is the *[mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210)* of the surface; if (and only if) the mean curvature is zero, the surface is called a *[minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734)*.

:::
