# Jean-Pierre Wintenberger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Wintenberger"}

**Jean-Pierre Wintenberger** (1954 – 23 January 2019) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor of mathematics at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). He was corecipient of the 2011 [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), along with [Chandrashekhar Khare](wikipedia://en/11350853), for his proof of [Serre's modularity conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_conjecture_%28number_theory%29).[^o3]

:::

Wintenberger earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in 1984, under supervision of [Jean-Marc Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Marc_Fontaine).

Wintenberger died on 23 January 2019.[^o4]
