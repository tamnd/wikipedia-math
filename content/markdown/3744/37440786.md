# Paul A. Catlin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul A. Catlin"}

**Paul Allen Catlin** (1948-6-25 – 1995) was a mathematician, professor of mathematics who worked in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He wrote a significant paper on the series of chromatic numbers and Brooks' theorem, titled *Hajós graph coloring conjecture: variations and counterexamples*.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Originally from [Bridgeport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgeport), [Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut), Catlin majored in Mathematics with a B.A. degree from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) in 1970.[^o3]

Catlin held a Doctorate in Mathematics degree from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). From 1972 to 1973, he was a research and teaching assistant at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), where he earned the Master of Science degree in Mathematics.[^o3]

In 1976, he went to work at [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University), where he concentrated the research on [chromatic numbers](wikipedia://en/426743#Definition_and_terminology) and [Brooks' theorem](wikipedia://en/21042117). As a result, Catlin published a significant paper in that series: *Hajós graph coloring conjecture: variations and counterexamples.*,[^o3][^o7] which showed that the conjecture raised by [Hugo Hadwiger](wikipedia://en/630451) is further strengthened not only by $k \le 4$ but also by $k \ge 7$, which led to the joint paper written with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956) titled *Hadwiger's conjecture is true for almost every graph*.[^o9]

He authored over fifty academic papers in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). Many of his contributions and collaborations have been published in *[The Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*, in *[The Journal of Number Theory](wikipedia://en/8422003)*, in the *Journal of [Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21988115)*, and many other academic publications.[^o5] He co-authored scholarly papers with Arthur M. Hobbs,[^o8] [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956) and [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284),[^o9] [Hong-Jian Lai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong-Jian_Lai), Zheng-Yiao Han, and Yehong Shao,[^o8] among others. He also published papers with [G. Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704), with whom he also completed his dissertation thesis in 1976.[^o3][^o10]
