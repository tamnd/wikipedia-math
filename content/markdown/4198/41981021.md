# Rational arrival process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rational arrival process"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), a discipline within the mathematical [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), a **rational arrival process** (**RAP**) is a mathematical model for the time between job arrivals to a system. It extends the concept of a [Markov arrival process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_arrival_process), allowing for dependent [matrix-exponential distributed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix-exponential_distributed) inter-arrival times.[^o2]

:::

The processes were first characterised by Asmussen and Bladt[^o3] and are referred to as rational arrival processes because the inter-arrival times have a rational [Laplace–Stieltjes transform](wikipedia://en/365929).

## Software {#Software}

- [Q-MAM](https://win.uantwerpen.be/~vanhoudt/) a [MATLAB](wikipedia://en/20412) toolbox which can solve queueing systems with RAP arrivals.[^o5]
