# Boas–Buck polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boas–Buck polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Boas–Buck polynomials** are sequences of polynomials $\Phi_n^{(r)}(z)$ defined from [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478) $B$ and $C$ by [generating functions](wikipedia://en/160993) of the form

:::

$$\displaystyle C(zt^r B(t))=\sum_{n\ge0}\Phi_n^{(r)}(z)t^n.$$

The case $r=1$, sometimes called [generalized Appell polynomials](wikipedia://en/2462837), was studied by Boas and Buck (1958).[^o2]
