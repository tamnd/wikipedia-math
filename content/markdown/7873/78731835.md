# Ultradistribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ultradistribution"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), an **ultradistribution** (also called an **ultra-distribution**[^o2]) is a [generalized function](wikipedia://en/1580246) that extends the concept of a [distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29) by allowing [test functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29#Definitions_of_test_functions_and_distributions) whose [Fourier transforms](wikipedia://en/52247) have [compact support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_support).[^o3] They form an element of the [dual space](wikipedia://en/7988) 𝒵′, where 𝒵 is the space of test functions whose Fourier transforms belong to 𝒟, the space of [infinitely differentiable functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) with compact support.[^o4]
