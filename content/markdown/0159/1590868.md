# Uniformly connected space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniformly connected space"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **uniformly connected space** or **Cantor connected space** is a [uniform space](wikipedia://en/32339) *U* such that every [uniformly continuous function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniformly_continuous_function) from *U* to a [discrete uniform space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_uniform_space) is constant.

:::

A [uniform space](wikipedia://en/32339) *U* is called **uniformly disconnected** if it is not uniformly connected.

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

A [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [uniform space](wikipedia://en/32339) is uniformly connected [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) it is [connected](wikipedia://en/6233).

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- Every [connected space](wikipedia://en/6233) is uniformly connected.
- The [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024) and the [irrational numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrational_numbers) are disconnected but uniformly connected.

:::
