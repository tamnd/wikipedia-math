# G. S. Carr

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="G. S. Carr"}

**George Shoobridge Carr** (1837–1914) was a British mathematician. He wrote *[Synopsis of Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31968302)* (1886). This book, first published in England in 1880, was read and studied closely by mathematician [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) when he was a teenager. Ramanujan had already produced many theorems by the age of 15.

:::

Carr was a private coach for the [Tripos mathematics examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and the *Synopsis* was written as a study guide for those examinations.
