# Tampering (quality control)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tampering"}

**Tampering** in the context of a [controlled process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_control) is adjusting the process on the basis of outcomes which are within the expected range of variability. The net result is to re-align the process so that an increased proportion of the output is out of specification. The term was introduced in this context by [W. Edwards Deming](wikipedia://en/70534), and he was a strong proponent of using control charts to avoid tampering.

:::
