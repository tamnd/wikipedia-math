# Frauke Kreuter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frauke Kreuter"}

**Frauke Kreuter** is a German [sociologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociologist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). She is a professor of the [Joint Program in Survey Methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Program_in_Survey_Methodology) (JPSM) of the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) and a professor in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904) at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich), Germany. Her research in [survey methodology](wikipedia://en/272134) includes work on [sampling error](wikipedia://en/1955561) and [observational error](wikipedia://en/22346).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kreuter earned a [diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom#Germany) in [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology) from the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim) in 1996. She received her doctorate (Dr. rer. soc.) in 2001 from the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) under the supervision of Rainer Schnell. After postdoctoral research and an adjunct position at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), she moved to [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland) in 2004 and joined [JPSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Program_in_Survey_Methodology) as an assistant professor. From 2010 to 2014 she was University Professor of Statistics at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich), while maintaining her position at the University of Maryland as an associate professor. She also became head of the Statistical Methods Research Department at the [Institute for Employment Research (IAB)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Employment_Research_%28IAB%29) in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg), Germany in 2010. In 2014 she was promoted to full professor at [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland) and moved her German position to the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim). From 2016 to 2020, she was director of [JPSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Program_in_Survey_Methodology) in [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland). She also founded the International Program for Survey and Data Science (IPSDS) at LMU Munich, Germany, and co-founded the Coleridge Initiative.[^o11]

## Publications {#Publications}

She is the author or co-author of several books, including *Data Analysis Using Stata* (3rd ed., Stata Press, 2012, with Ulrich Kohler),[^o4] *Practical Tools for Designing and Weighting Survey Samples* (Springer, 2013, with [Richard Valliant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Valliant) and [Jill Dever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jill_Dever))[^o5] and *Big Data and Social Science - Data Science Methods and Tools for Research and Practice* (2nd ed., CRC Press, 2021, with [Ian Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Foster_%28computer_scientist%29), [Rayid Ghani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayid_Ghani), [Ron S. Jarmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_S._Jarmin), and [Julia Lane](wikipedia://en/55680780)).[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Kreuter was the 2013 winner of the Gertrude Cox Award, given jointly by the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607) and [RTI International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTI_International).[^o8] In 2014 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for outstanding contributions to research in the field of [survey methodology](wikipedia://en/272134); for excellence in mentoring of junior researchers in [social statistics](wikipedia://en/285943) and [survey methodology](wikipedia://en/272134); and for extensive international research collaborations."[^o9] She was awarded the [Warren Mitofsky Innovators Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Mitofsky_Innovators_Award) of the [American Association for Public Opinion Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_Public_Opinion_Research) in 2020, and served as the association's president in 2025.[^o10][^o11]

## Other {#Other}

Kreuter also hosts the German language podcast #digdeep with Dr. [Christof Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christof_Horn), where they discuss the trends and impact of digitalization.[^o13][^o10]
