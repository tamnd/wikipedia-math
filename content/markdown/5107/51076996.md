# Magic hexagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Magic hexagram"}

*Image: Magic6star-sum26.svg.* Magic hexagram with sum 26

A **magic hexagram** of order 2 is an arrangement of numbers in a [hexagram](wikipedia://en/302441) with triangular cells with 2 cells on each edge, in such a way that the numbers in each row, in all three directions, sum to the same [magic constant](wikipedia://en/546969) *M*.

:::

## Magic star hexagram {#Magic_star_hexagram}

Magic star hexagram or **6-pointed magic star** is a [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {6/2} in which numbers are placed at each of the six [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) and six intersections, such that the four numbers on each line sum to the same magic constant.

## Magic star hexagram with triangular cell {#Magic_star_hexagram_with_triangular_cell}

There are two solutions of magic star hexagram with 12 triangular cells.

## Magic star hexagram with more than 12 vertices {#Magic_star_hexagram_with_more_than_12_vertices}

*Image: Magic hexagram-sum50.svg.* Magic hexagram with sum 50

Harold Reiter and David Ritchie calculated the solution of magic hexagrams with 19 vertices.[^o6]
