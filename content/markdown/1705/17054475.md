# Hans Kamp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Kamp"}

**Johan Anthony Willem** "**Hans**" **Kamp** (born 5 September 1940)[^o3] is a Dutch philosopher and [linguist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics), responsible for introducing [discourse representation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discourse_representation_theory) (DRT) in 1981.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kamp was born in [Den Burg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Den_Burg).[^o3] He received a Ph.D. in [Philosophy from UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_Department_of_Philosophy) in 1968, and has taught at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), [University of Texas, Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas,_Austin), and [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart).[^o7] His dissertation, *Tense Logic and the Theory of Linear Order* (1968)[^o8] was devoted to [functional completeness](wikipedia://en/5279259) in [tense logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tense_logic), the main result being that all temporal operators are definable in terms of "since" and "until", provided that the underlying temporal structure is a continuous [linear ordering](wikipedia://en/30330). Kamp's 1971 paper on "now" (published in *[Theoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoria_%28philosophy_journal%29)*) was the first employment of [double-indexing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-indexing) in [model theoretic](wikipedia://en/19858) [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107). His doctoral committee included [Richard Montague](wikipedia://en/610182) as chairman, [Chen Chung Chang](wikipedia://en/25413983), [David Kaplan](wikipedia://en/1479115), [Yiannis N. Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002), and [Jordan Howard Sobel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Howard_Sobel).

Kamp became a corresponding member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1997.[^o9] He was awarded the [Jean Nicod Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Nicod_Prize) in 1996[^o10] and was elected a [Fellow of the American Academy of Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_&_Sciences) in 2015.[^o11] In 2024 he was awarded the [Rolf Schock Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Schock_Prizes) jointly with [Irene Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Heim).[^o12]
