# René Goormaghtigh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="René Goormaghtigh"}

**René Goormaghtigh** (13 October 1893, [Ostend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostend) – 10 February 1960, [Ixelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ixelles)) was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_people) engineer, after whom the [Goormaghtigh Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goormaghtigh_Conjecture) is named.

:::

Goormaghtigh studied at [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University), gaining a Diploma in Civil Engineering from the Central Board of [Le Havre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Havre) in 1918. Throughout his subsequent life he worked as an engineer and industrial administrator. In 1952 he was appointed advisor to the [Société Générale de Belgique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Générale_de_Belgique). He was made a Knight of the [Order of Leopold II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Leopold_II) in 1947, and an Officer of the [Order of the Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Crown_%28Belgium%29) in 1956. After a heart attack in 1958, he retired to Saint-André-des-Bruges.[^o2]

He was a frequent contributor to mathematical journals, especially the Belgian journal *[Mathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathesis_%28journal%29)*.
