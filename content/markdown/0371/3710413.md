# NUTS statistical regions of Spain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Spain"}

In the **NUTS** (Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics) codes of **[Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain)** (ES), the following are the first-level [political and administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_divisions_of_Spain).

:::

## Overall {#Overall}

### NUTS Codes {#NUTS_Codes}

::: {#o4 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| [NUTS 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_level_NUTS_of_the_European_Union#Spain) | Groups of autonomous communities (*Agrupación de comunidades autónomas*) | 7 |
| NUTS 2 | [Autonomous communities and cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_communities_of_Spain) (*Comunidades y ciudades autónomas*) | 19 |
| NUTS 3 | [Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Spain) + [Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_Spain) + [Ceuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceuta) and [Melilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melilla) (*Provincias + islas + Ceuta y Melilla*) | 59 |

:::

### Local administrative units {#Local_administrative_units}

Below the NUTS levels, the two LAU (Local Administrative Units) levels are:

::: {#o7 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| LAU 1 | — (same as NUTS 3) | 59 |
| LAU 2 | [Municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Spain) (*Municipios*) | 8114 |

:::

*The LAU codes of Spain can be downloaded here:* <http://epp.eurostat.ec.europa.eu/portal/page/portal/nuts_nomenclature/documents/ES-LAU.xls>
