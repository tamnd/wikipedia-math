# George Johnson (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Johnson"}

**George Johnson** (1837–1911) was a Canadian statistician. He was born in [Annapolis Royal, Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annapolis_Royal,_Nova_Scotia) in 1837. For some years he was the Editor of *The Halifax Reporter* and was subsequently the Ottawa correspondent for *[The Toronto Mail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Toronto_Mail)*. From 1891 to 1904 he was the Dominion Statistician. He also edited the Canada Year Book from 1886 to 1904. He was the author of a number of works, including *The Handbook of Canada* and *Alphabet of First Things in Canada*. He was an honorary Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) and died in 1911.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
