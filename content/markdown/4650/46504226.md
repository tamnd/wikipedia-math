# Normal homomorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normal homomorphism"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **normal homomorphism** is a [ring homomorphism](wikipedia://en/26411) $R \to S$ that is [flat](wikipedia://en/492616) and is such that for every field extension *L* of the [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) $\kappa(\mathfrak{p})$ of any [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) $\mathfrak{p}$, $L \otimes_R S$ is a [normal ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_ring).

:::
