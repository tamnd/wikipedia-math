# Bernhard Keller

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bernhard Keller"}

**Bernhard Keller** (born 1962) is a Swiss mathematician, specializing in algebra. He is a professor at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_%282019%29).

:::

Keller received in 1990 his PhD from the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) under [Pierre Gabriel](wikipedia://en/30716698) with the thesis *On Derived Categories*.

His research is in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and the representation theory of [quivers](wikipedia://en/355100) and finite-dimensional algebras. He has applied [triangulated](wikipedia://en/1679700) Calabi–Yau categories to the (additive) categorification of [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423). In 2013, he received an honorary degree[^o4] from the [University of Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp). In 2014 he received the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384). He was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid in 2006,[^o5] with a talk *On differential graded categories.* Keller is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
