# Oleg Nagornov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Oleg Nagornov"}

**Oleg Viktorovich Nagornov** (Олег Викторович Нагорнов; born 15 August 1956) is a Russian [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Since 2010 he has been the first Vice-rector of [National Research Nuclear University MEPhI (Moscow Engineering Physics Institute)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Nuclear_University_MEPhI).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Oleg Nagornov was born on August 15, 1956, in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), Russia. In 1979 he graduated from MEPhI, where he studied theoretical [nuclear physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics). In 1979–1982 he was a post-graduate student in MEPhI. In 1983 Oleg Nagornov started his career in MEPhI as a junior research fellow, became [research assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_assistant) in 1985.

## Professional experience {#Professional_experience}

In 1988-2006 Oleg Nagornov worked as Associate Professor in MEPhI. He was a professor and the Head of the Department in MEPhI from 2007 to 2008. In 2008-2010 he served as Institute's Vice-rector. In 2010 he was appointed the first Vice-rector of MEPhI. Oleg Nagornov is a Member of the Expert Council of [Higher Attestation Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Attestation_Commission).

## Awards {#Awards}

Laureate of the following Government Awards:

- Award of the Russian Federation Government in the field of education (2013)
- Diploma of Merit, [Rosatom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosatom) State Corporation
- Medal "For Merit in the Conduct of the All-Russian Population Census"
- Diploma for the training of information security specialists in commemoration of the 70th anniversary of MEPhI (2012)
- Diploma of Merit, [The Federal Financial Monitoring Service of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosfinmonitoring) (2012)
- The [Kurchatov Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurchatov_Medal), IV class (2012).

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

The scope of scientific interests includes inverse problems of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), mathematical models in [paleoclimatology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoclimatology), Numerical Modelling for Environmental Problems, heat and mass transfer and wave dispersion in porous multiphase medium.[^o8]

Academic degrees:

- PhD of Science (1983)
- Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Science (2005) [^o9]

## International career {#International_career}

- Visiting professor in the Polar Ice Coring Office, [University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alaska_Fairbanks), US (1991-1994)
- The head of International Science and Technology Center's projects (1994–2004)
- Visiting lecturer in the Center of Theoretical Research, Facultad de Estudios Superiores Cuautitlan, [UNAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico), Mexico (1996, 1998–2000)
- Co-leader of the [CRDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRDF_Global) (U.S. Civilian Research and Development Foundation) project (1996–1999)
- Visiting lecturer in universities of Argentina: Master's program "Numerical Modelling for Environmental Problems", Facultad Regional de San Nicolas, [National Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technological_University) and [National University of San Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_San_Juan) (1997–2002)
