# Milman–Pettis theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milman–Pettis theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Milman–Pettis theorem** states that every [uniformly convex](wikipedia://en/9133418) [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) is [reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091).

:::

The theorem was proved independently by [D. Milman](wikipedia://en/5210923) (1938) and [B. J. Pettis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._J._Pettis) (1939). [S. Kakutani](wikipedia://en/2572542) gave a different proof in 1939, and John R. Ringrose published a shorter proof in 1959.

Mahlon M. Day (1941) gave examples of reflexive Banach spaces which are not isomorphic to any uniformly convex space.
