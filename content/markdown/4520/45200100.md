# Daniel K. Nakano

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel K. Nakano"}

**Daniel Ken Nakano** (born July 30, 1964)[^o2] is an American mathematician. Nakano is a Distinguished Research Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia); he specializes in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).[^o3]

:::

Nakano was born in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle), Washington.[^o2] His parents are [Japanese American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Americans), and he grew up in [Kirkland, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirkland,_Washington).[^o4] Nakano graduated from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1986,[^o2] and earned a doctorate in mathematics from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1990 under the supervision of [George B. Seligman](wikipedia://en/46217412) with thesis *Projective Modules over Lie Algebras of Cartan Type*.[^o2] After temporary positions at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University) and [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), he became an assistant professor at [Utah State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_University) in 1994 and moved to the University of Georgia in 2001.[^o2]

In 2010, Nakano was named Distinguished Research Professor.[^o2] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

In 2016, he received the Lamar Dodd Award Creative Research Award.
