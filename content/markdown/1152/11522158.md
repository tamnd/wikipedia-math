# Bohuslav Balcar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bohuslav Balcar"}

**Bohuslav Balcar** (; 1943 – 2017) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a senior researcher at the Center for Theoretical Study (CTS), and a professor at [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague). His research interests were mainly related to [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358).

:::

Balcar received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1966 from Charles University. His Ph.D. supervisor was [Petr Vopěnka](wikipedia://en/3105466).
