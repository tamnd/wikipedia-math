# Andrzej Schinzel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Schinzel"}

**Andrzej Bobola Maria Schinzel** (5 April 1937[^o3] – 21 August 2021[^o4]) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialised predominantly in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

*Image: WithProf.AndrzejSchinzel.jpg.* Schinzel and Adam Grygiel in 2010

## Education {#Education}

Schinzel earned an [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSc) in 1958 from [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1960 from the Institute of Mathematics of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) where he studied under [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122), with a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1962. He was a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Schinzel was a professor at the Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences (IM PAN). His principal interest was the theory of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000). His 1958 conjecture on the prime values of polynomials, known as [Schinzel's hypothesis H](wikipedia://en/604052), both extends the [Bunyakovsky conjecture](wikipedia://en/12511846) and broadly generalizes the [twin prime conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_prime_conjecture). He also proved [Schinzel's theorem](wikipedia://en/47566358) on the existence of [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) through any given number of integer points.

Schinzel authored over 200 research articles in various branches of number theory, including [elementary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_number_theory), [analytic](wikipedia://en/251513) and [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705). He served as the editor of *[Acta Arithmetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Arithmetica)* for over four decades.

Schinzel collaborated with many colleagues, including [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Barbara Rokowska](wikipedia://en/79790722).[^o9]

## Private life {#Private_life}

Andrzej Schinzel was the elder brother of Polish [chess master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_master) [Władysław Schinzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Władysław_Schinzel) (born 1943).[^o11]
