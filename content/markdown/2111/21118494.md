# Ring spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ring spectrum"}

In [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), a **ring spectrum** is a [spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_%28homotopy_theory%29) *E* together with a multiplication map

:::

*μ*: *E* ∧ *E* → *E*

and a unit map

*η*: *S* → *E*,

where *S* is the [sphere spectrum](wikipedia://en/21118593). These maps have to satisfy [associativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associativity) and unitality conditions up to homotopy, much in the same way as the multiplication of a [ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_%28algebra%29) is associative and unital. That is,

*μ* (id ∧ *μ*) ~ *μ* (*μ* ∧ id)

and

*μ* (id ∧ *η*) ~ id ~ *μ*(*η* ∧ id).

Examples of ring spectra include [singular homology](wikipedia://en/431041) with coefficients in a [ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_%28algebra%29), [complex cobordism](wikipedia://en/1033666), [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), and [Morava K-theory](wikipedia://en/1033664).
