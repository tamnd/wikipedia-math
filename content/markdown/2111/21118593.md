# Sphere spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sphere spectrum"}

In [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **sphere spectrum** *S* is the monoidal unit in the category of [spectra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_%28homotopy_theory%29). It is the [suspension spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_spectrum) of *S*<sup>0</sup>, i.e., a set of two points. Explicitly, the *n*-th space in the sphere spectrum is the *n*-dimensional [sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_%28geometry%29) *S*<sup>*n*</sup>, and the structure maps from the [suspension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_%28mathematics%29) of *S*<sup>*n*</sup> to *S*<sup>*n*+1</sup> are the canonical [homeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/13660). The *k*-th homotopy group of a sphere spectrum is the *k*-th [stable homotopy group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_group) of spheres.

:::

The [localization](wikipedia://en/13727822) of the sphere spectrum at a prime number *p* is called the **local sphere** at *p* and is denoted by $S_{(p)}$.
