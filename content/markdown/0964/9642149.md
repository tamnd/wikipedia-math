# William Fulton (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Fulton"}

**William Edgar Fulton** (born August 29, 1939) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received his undergraduate degree from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1961 and his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1966. His Ph.D. thesis, written under the supervision of [Gerard Washnitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Washnitzer), was on *The fundamental group of an algebraic curve*.

Fulton worked at Princeton and [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) from 1965 until 1970, when he began teaching at Brown. In 1987, he moved to the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o5] He moved to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1998, retiring in 2020.[^o6] As of 2026, Fulton had supervised the doctoral work of 24 students at Brown, Chicago, and Michigan.[^o4]

Fulton is known as the author or coauthor of a number of popular texts, including *Algebraic Curves* and *Representation Theory*.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1996, he received the [Steele Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steele_Prize) for mathematical exposition for his text *Intersection Theory*.[^o5] Fulton is a member of the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1997; a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) from 1998, and was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2000.[^o8] In 2010, he was awarded the [Steele Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steele_Prize) for Lifetime Achievement.[^o9] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
