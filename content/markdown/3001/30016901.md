# Mark Kisin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mark Kisin"}

**Mark Kisin** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for work in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). In particular, he is known for his contributions to the study of [p-adic representations](wikipedia://en/26086853#General_classification_of_p-adic_representations) and [p-adic cohomology](wikipedia://en/46927724).

:::

Born in [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius), Lithuania and raised from the age of five in [Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne), Australia, he won a silver medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1989[^o3] and received his B.Sc. from [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) in 1991.[^o5] He received his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1998 under the direction of [Nick Katz](wikipedia://en/1451427). From 1998 to 2001 he was a Research Fellow at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), after which he spent three years at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

After six years at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), Kisin took the post in 2009 of professor of mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o5]

He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2008.[^o6] He gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010, on the topic of "Number Theory".[^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2022.[^o9]
