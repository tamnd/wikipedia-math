# Julie Rowlett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Julie Rowlett"}

**Julie Marie Rowlett** (born 1978)[^o3] is an American mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics at the [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology).[^o4] Her primary research interest is in [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474) with a particular focus on geometric analysis on singular spaces, [dynamics](wikipedia://en/990632), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rowlett earned her Bachelor of Science from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 2001,[^o6] and her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 2006. Her dissertation, *Spectral Geometry and Asymptotically Conic Convergence*, was supervised by [Rafe Mazzeo](wikipedia://en/68648115).

Rowlett was a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) and [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in Montreal. After a short period as an instructor in the [Education Program for Gifted Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Program_for_Gifted_Youth) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and a position as visiting [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), Rowlett left the US in 2009 for Germany. There, she held a series of temporary positions at the [Hausdorff Center for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221) from 2009 to 2010, the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) from 2011 to 2012, the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) from 2012 to 2013, and [Leibniz University Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_University_Hannover) from 2013 to 2014. She received her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the University of Göttingen in 2013 and took a permanent position at the [University of Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ingolstadt) in 2014. In 2015 Rowlett moved to Sweden for her current position at [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology).[^o8][^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2016, she received the [Paul R. Halmos – Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_R._Halmos_–_Lester_R._Ford_Award) together with Zhiqin Lu of the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), for their joint work on [hearing the shape of a drum](wikipedia://en/1072325).[^o11] Although there exist pairs of drum shapes that sound the same, Rowlett and Lu showed that certain shapes of drums, such as [parallelograms](wikipedia://en/133496) and acute [trapezoids](wikipedia://en/201826), can be distinguished from others by their sounds.[^o12]
