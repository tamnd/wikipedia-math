# Pharmaceutical Statistics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pharmaceutical Statistics"}

***Pharmaceutical Statistics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to [pharmaceutical statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical_statistics). It is the official journal of [Statisticians in the Pharmaceutical Industry](wikipedia://en/8953858) and is published by [John Wiley & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons).

:::
