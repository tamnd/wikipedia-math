# Survey Methodology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Survey Methodology"}

***Survey Methodology*** (or ***Techniques d'enquête*** in the French version) is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_%28publishing%29) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to the development and application of [survey techniques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_survey). It is published by [Statistics Canada](wikipedia://en/186760), the national statistical office of [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language).

:::

The journal started publishing in 1975, publishes two issues each year, and is available open access in HTML online and PDF. The print version has been discontinued.[^o3]

As of 2021, the editor-in-chief is Jean-François Beaumont, senior statistical advisor at Statistics Canada.[^o4]
