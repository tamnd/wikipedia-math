# Jia Li

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jia Li"}

**Jia Li** is a Chinese-American statistician and computer scientist known for her research on [image annotation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_annotation) and [image retrieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_retrieval). She is a professor of statistics and of computer science at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Li studied information and control engineering at [Xi'an Jiaotong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an_Jiaotong_University), graduating with a bachelor's degree in 1993. She went to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for graduate study, earning master's degrees in electrical engineering and statistics in 1995 and 1998 respectively, and completing a Ph.D. in electrical engineering in 1999. Her dissertation, *Image Classification and Compression Based on a Two-Dimensional Multiresolution Hidden Markov Model*, was supervised by [Robert M. Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Gray).

She joined Penn State in 2000 after a year of research at the [Xerox Palo Alto Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerox_Palo_Alto_Research_Center). She worked at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) as a program director from 2011 to 2013, and has been editor-in-chief of the journal *Statistical Analysis and Data Mining* from 2018 to 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Li became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2019. She was named as a 2020 [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow), "for contributions to real-time automatic image annotation and image retrieval".
