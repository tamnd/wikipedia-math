# Nodoid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nodoid"}

*Image: Nodoid.svg.* Half of a nodoid surface.

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a **nodoid** is a [surface of revolution](wikipedia://en/329549) with [constant nonzero mean curvature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constant_mean_curvature_surface) obtained by rolling a [hyperbola](wikipedia://en/14052) along a fixed line, tracing the [focus](wikipedia://en/1032607), and revolving the resulting [nodary](wikipedia://en/37270071) curve around the line.[^o2]

:::
