# Journal of Topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Topology"}

The ***Journal of Topology*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) which publishes papers of high quality and significance in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and adjacent areas of mathematics. It was established in 2008, when the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of *[Topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology_%28journal%29)* resigned due to the increasing costs of [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier)'s subscriptions.[^o3]

:::

The journal is owned and managed by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and produced, distributed, sold and marketed by [John Wiley & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons). It appears quarterly with articles published individually online prior to appearing in a printed issue.[^o4]
