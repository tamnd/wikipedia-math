# Porteous formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Porteous formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Porteous formula**, or **Thom–Porteous formula**, or **Giambelli–Thom–Porteous formula**, is the expression for the [fundamental class](wikipedia://en/669440) of a degeneracy locus (or [determinantal variety](wikipedia://en/22920002)) of a [morphism of vector bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphism_of_vector_bundles) in terms of [Chern classes](wikipedia://en/294349). [Giambelli's formula](wikipedia://en/22269939) is roughly the special case when the vector bundles are sums of line bundles over [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). (txt) pointed out that the fundamental class must be a polynomial in the Chern classes and found this polynomial in a few special cases, and (txt) found the polynomial in general. (Kempf Laksov 1974) proved a more general version, and (Fulton 1992) generalized it further.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Given a morphism of vector bundles *E*, *F* of ranks *m* and *n* over a smooth variety, its *k*-th degeneracy locus (*k* ≤ min(*m*,*n*)) is the variety of points where it has rank at most *k*. If all components of the degeneracy locus have the expected [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) (*m* – *k*)(*n* – *k*) then Porteous's formula states that its fundamental class is the determinant of the matrix of size *m* – *k* whose (*i*, *j*) entry is the Chern class *c*<sub>*n*–*k*+*j*–*i*</sub>(*F* – *E*).

:::
