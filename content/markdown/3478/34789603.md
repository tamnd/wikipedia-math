# Poincaré–Lelong equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poincaré–Lelong equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Poincaré–Lelong equation**, studied by (txt), is the [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564)

:::

$$i\partial\overline\partial u=\rho$$

on a [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538), where ρ is a [positive](wikipedia://en/10592733) (1,1)-form.
