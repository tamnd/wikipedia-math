# Smale conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Smale conjecture"}

The **Smale conjecture**, named after [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294), is the statement that the [diffeomorphism group](wikipedia://en/8564) of the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) has the homotopy-type of its isometry group, the orthogonal group [O(4)](wikipedia://en/173954). It was proved in 1983 by [Allen Hatcher](wikipedia://en/1112366).[^o2]

:::

## Equivalent statements {#Equivalent_statements}

There are several equivalent statements of the Smale conjecture. One is that the component of the unknot in the space of smooth embeddings of the circle in 3-space has the homotopy-type of the round circles, equivalently, [O(3)](wikipedia://en/173954). Interestingly, this statement is not equivalent to the generalized Smale Conjecture, in higher dimensions.

Another equivalent statement is that the group of diffeomorphisms of the [3-ball](wikipedia://en/160556) which restrict to the identity on the boundary is contractible.

Yet another equivalent statement is that the space of constant-curvature Riemann metrics on the 3-sphere is contractible.

## Higher dimensions {#Higher_dimensions}

The (false) statement that the inclusion $O(n+1) \to \text{Diff}(S^n)$ is a weak equivalence for all $n$ is sometimes meant when referring to the *generalized Smale conjecture*. For $n = 1$, this is classical, for $n = 2$, Smale proved it himself.[^o5]

For $n\ge5$ the conjecture is false due to the failure of $\text{Diff}(S^n)/O(n+1)$ to be contractible.[^o6]

In late 2018, Tadayuki Watanabe released a preprint that proves the failure of Smale's conjecture in the remaining 4-dimensional case[^o7] relying on work around the [Kontsevich integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kontsevich_integral), a generalization of the [Gauss linking integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss_linking_integral). As of 2021, the proof remains unpublished in a mathematical journal.
