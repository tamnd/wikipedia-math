# Georges Bouligand

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Bouligand"}

**Georges Louis Bouligand** (13 October 1889 – 12 April 1979) was a French mathematician. He worked in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), [analytical](wikipedia://en/2202) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He is known for introducing the concept of [paratingent cones](wikipedia://en/34789305) and [contingent cones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contingent_cone).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Georges Bouligand was received at both the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) and the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in 1909, and chose the latter. He graduated in mathematics in 1912, and with the help of a scholarship from the Commercy Foundation, he prepared a thesis, defended in 1914, “On the [Green](wikipedia://en/311001) and [Neumann functions](wikipedia://en/4700) of the cylinder”. He was then appointed to [Tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tours) high school, then to the high school of [Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennes) where he taught the class in specialised mathematics (*Mathématiques spéciales*).

In 1921, after a year as a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) at the Faculty of Sciences of Rennes, he obtained the chair of [rational mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) at the [Faculty of Sciences of Poitiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Poitiers), then the chair of [differential and integral calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), after the departure for Paris of René Garnier. He was appointed to the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) in 1938, but it was only in 1948 that he took up the chair of Applications of Analysis to Geometry, which he kept until his retirement on September 30, 1961.
