# Paratingent cone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paratingent cone"}

In mathematics, the **paratingent cone** and **contingent cone** were introduced by (txt), and are closely related to [tangent cones](wikipedia://en/1416915).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $S$ be a nonempty subset of a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) $(X, \|\cdot\|)$.

1. Let some $\bar{x} \in \operatorname{cl}(S)$ be a point in the [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of $S$. An element $h \in X$ is called a *tangent* (or *tangent vector*) to $S$ at $\bar{x}$, if there is a sequence $(x_n)_{n\in \mathbb{N}}$ of elements $x_n \in S$ and a sequence $(\lambda_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ of positive real numbers $\lambda_n > 0$ such that $\bar{x} = \lim_{n \to \infty} x_n$ and $h = \lim_{n \to \infty} \lambda_n (x_n - \bar{x}).$
1. The set $T(S,\bar{x})$ of all tangents to $S$ at $\bar{x}$ is called the *contingent cone* (or the *Bouligand tangent cone*) to $S$ at $\bar{x}$.[^o5]

An equivalent definition is given in terms of a distance function and the limit infimum. As before, let $(X, \|\cdot \|)$ be a normed vector space and take some nonempty set $S \subset X$. For each $x \in X$, let the *distance function* to $S$ be

$$d_S(x) := \inf\{\|x - x'\| \mid x' \in S\}.$$

Then, the *contingent cone* to $S \subset X$ at $x \in \operatorname{cl}(S)$ is defined by[^o6]

$$T_S(x) := \left\{v : \liminf_{h \to 0^+} \frac{d_S(x + hv)}{h} = 0 \right\}.$$

:::
