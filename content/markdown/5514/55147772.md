# Pre-topological order

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pre-topological order"}

In the field of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), a **pre-topological order** or **pre-topological ordering** of a [directed graph](wikipedia://en/19721986) is a [linear ordering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_ordering) of its [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) such that if there is a directed path from vertex *u* to vertex *v* and *v* comes before *u* in the ordering, then there is also a directed path from vertex *v* to vertex *u*.[^o2][^o3]

:::

If the graph is a [directed acyclic graph](wikipedia://en/204002) (DAG), [topological orderings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_ordering) are pre-topological orderings and vice versa.[^o2] In other cases, any pre-topological ordering gives a [partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order).
