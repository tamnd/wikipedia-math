# Wall-crossing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wall-crossing"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), the phenomenon of **wall-crossing** describes the discontinuous change of a certain quantity, such as an integer [geometric invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_invariant), an [index](wikipedia://en/86113) or a space of [BPS state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BPS_state), across a [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149)-one wall in a space of [stability conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_point), a so-called [wall of marginal stability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_of_marginal_stability).

:::
