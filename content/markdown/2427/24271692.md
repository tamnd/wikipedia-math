# Étale algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Étale algebra"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), an **étale algebra** over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) is a special type of [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_%28ring_theory%29), one that is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/14828) to a finite product of [finite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_extension) [separable field extensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_field_extension). An étale algebra is a special sort of commutative [separable algebra](wikipedia://en/13205367).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $K$ be a field. Let $L$ be a [commutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative) [unital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unital_algebra) [associative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associative) $K$-algebra. Then $L$ is called an *étale $K$-algebra* if any one of the following equivalent conditions holds:[^o4] $L\otimes_{K} E\simeq E^n$ for some field extension $E$ of $K$ and some nonnegative integer $n$.; $L\otimes_{K} \overline{K} \simeq \overline{K}^n$ for any [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) $\overline{K}$ of $K$ and some nonnegative integer $n$.; $L$ is isomorphic to a finite product of finite separable field extensions of $K$.; $L$ is finite-dimensional over $K$, and the trace form $\operatorname{Tr}(xy)$ is nondegenerate.; The [morphism of schemes](wikipedia://en/7482797) $\operatorname{Spec} L \to \operatorname{Spec} K$ is an [étale morphism](wikipedia://en/3022771).

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra $\mathbb{Q}(i)$ is étale because it is a finite separable field extension.

The $\mathbb{R}$-algebra $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^2)$ of [dual numbers](wikipedia://en/42169) is not étale, since $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^2)\otimes_\mathbb{R}\mathbb{C} \simeq \mathbb{C}[x]/(x^2)$.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Let $G$ denote the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of $K$. Then the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of étale $K$-algebras is [equivalent](wikipedia://en/379051) to the category of finite [$G$-sets](wikipedia://en/12781) with continuous $G$-action. In particular, étale algebras of dimension $n$ are classified by [conjugacy classes](wikipedia://en/49176) of continuous [group homomorphisms](wikipedia://en/12396) from $G$ to the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) $S_{n}$. These globalize to e.g. the definition of [étale fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/3022906) and categorify to [Grothendieck's Galois theory](wikipedia://en/372470).

:::
