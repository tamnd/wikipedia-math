# Heptagonal prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptagonal prism"}

*Image: Prisma heptagonal 3D.stl.* 3D model of a uniform heptagonal prism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **heptagonal prism** is a [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) with [heptagonal](wikipedia://en/365349) base. This [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) has 9 faces (2 bases and 7 sides), 21 edges, and 14 vertices.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Area {#Area}

The [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of a right heptagonal prism with height $h$ and with a side length of $L$ and [apothem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apothem) $a_p$ is given by:[^o3]

$$A = 7L\cdot (a_p + h)$$

## Volume {#Volume}

The [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) is found by taking the area of the base, with a side length of $L$ and apothem $a_p$, and multiplying it by the height $h$, giving the formula:[^o3]

$$V = \frac{7}{2}\cdot h\cdot L\cdot a_p$$

This formula also works for the oblique prism due to the [Cavalieri's principle](wikipedia://en/21697672).
