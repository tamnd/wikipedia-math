# Bodil Branner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bodil Branner"}

**Bodil Branner** (born 5 February 1943, in [Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus)) is a retired Danish mathematician, one of the founders of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) and a former chair of the [Danish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22351308). Her research concerned [holomorphic dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518) and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Branner studied mathematics and physics at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University), where mathematician Svend Bundgaard was one of her mentors, and in 1967 earned a master's degree (the highest degree then available) under the supervision of Leif Kristensen. She had intended to travel to the U.S. for a doctorate, but her husband, a chemist, took an industry job in Copenhagen. Branner could not get a job as a high school teacher because she did not have a teaching qualification, but Bundgaard found her a position as a faculty assistant for Frederik Fabricius-Bjerre at the [Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark). Despite this not beginning as an actual faculty position, she eventually earned tenure there in the 1970s.[^o2]

[European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) was founded as an organization in 1986 by Branner, [Caroline Series](wikipedia://en/43764434), [Gudrun Kalmbach](wikipedia://en/59972579), [Marie-Françoise Roy](wikipedia://en/39889069), and [Dona Strauss](wikipedia://en/61909148), inspired by the activities of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the USA.[^o4]

Branner was the first woman to lead the [Danish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22351308), which she did from 1998 to 2002.[^o5]

She retired in 2008.[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

A symposium in honor of Branner's 60th birthday was held in [Holbæk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holbæk) in 2003, and published as a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in 2006.[^o7] In 2012, she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
