# Anthemius of Tralles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anthemius of Tralles"}

*Image: Anthemius Trallianus – Fragment d'un ouvrage grec d'Anthèmius sur des Paradoxes de mècanique, 1777 – BEIC 4780621.jpg.* Fragment of a Greek work by Anthemius on Paradoxes of mechanics (*Fragment d'un ouvrage grec d'Anthèmius sur des Paradoxes de mècanique*), 1777

**Anthemius of Tralles** (Ἀνθέμιος ὁ Τραλλιανός, [Medieval Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_Greek): , *Anthémios o Trallianós*; 474 – 533 x 558) was a [Byzantine Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Greeks) from [Tralles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tralles)[^o2] who worked as a [geometer](wikipedia://en/9417) and [architect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_architecture) in [Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople), the capital of the [Byzantine Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire). With [Isidore of Miletus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidore_of_Miletus), he designed the [Hagia Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia) for [Justinian&nbsp;I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justinian&nbsp;I).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Anthemius was one of the five sons of Stephanus of Tralles, a physician. His brothers were Dioscorus, [Alexander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_of_Tralles), Olympius, and Metrodorus. Dioscorus followed his father's profession in Tralles; Alexander did so in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) and became one of the most celebrated medical men of his time; Olympius became a noted [lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_law); and Metrodorus worked as a [grammarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammarian_%28Greco-Roman_world%29) in Constantinople.[^n1]

Anthemius was said to have annoyed his neighbor Zeno in two ways: first, by engineering a miniature earthquake by sending steam through leather tubes he had fixed among the [joists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joist) and flooring of Zeno's parlor while he was entertaining friends[^o5] and, second, by simulating [thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder) and [lightning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightning) and flashing intolerable light into Zeno's eyes from a slightly hollowed mirror.[^n1] In addition to his familiarity with steam, some dubious authorities credited Anthemius with a knowledge of [gunpowder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder) or other explosive compound.[^n1]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Anthemius was a capable [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_mathematics). In the course of his treatise *[On Burning Mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_Burning_Mirrors)*, he intended to facilitate the construction of surfaces to reflect light to a single point, he described the string construction of the [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277) and assumed a property of ellipses not found in [Apollonius of Perga](wikipedia://en/257242)'s *Conics*: the equality of the angles subtended at a focus by two tangents drawn from a point. His work also includes the first practical use of the [directrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directrix_%28conic_section%29): having given the focus and a double ordinate, he used the focus and directrix to obtain any number of points on a [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231).[^n1] This work was later known to [Arab mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_in_medieval_Islam) such as [Alhazen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Optics).

[Eutocius of Ascalon](wikipedia://en/12807894)'s commentary on Apollonius's *Conics* was dedicated to Anthemius.

## Architecture {#Architecture}

*Image: Hagia Sophia Mars 2013.jpg.* Exterior of the [Hagia Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia), 2013

As an architect, Anthemius is best known for his work designing the [Hagia Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia).[^n1] He was commissioned with [Isidore of Miletus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidore_of_Miletus) by [Justinian&nbsp;I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justinian&nbsp;I) shortly after the earlier church on the site burned down in 532 but died early on in the project. He is also said to have repaired the flood defenses at [Daras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_%28Mesopotamia%29).[^n2]
