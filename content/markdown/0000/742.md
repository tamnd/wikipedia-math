# Algorithms (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algorithms"}

***Algorithms*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), covering design, analysis, and experiments on [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775). The journal is published by [MDPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDPI) and was established in 2008. The founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) was [Kazuo Iwama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazuo_Iwama_%28computer_scientist%29) ([Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University)).[^o3] From May 2014 to September 2019, the editor-in-chief was Henning Fernau ([Universität Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Trier)). The current editor-in-chief is Frank Werner ([Otto-von-Guericke-Universität Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto-von-Guericke-Universität_Magdeburg)).

:::
