# Cecil J. Nesbitt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cecil J. Nesbitt"}

**Cecil James Nesbitt**, Ph.D., F.S.A., M.A.A.A. (1912–2001) was a mathematician who was a Ph.D. student of [Richard Brauer](wikipedia://en/605134) and wrote many influential papers in the early history of [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685).

:::

Nesbitt taught [actuarial mathematics](wikipedia://en/189373) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) from 1938 to 1980. Nesbitt was born in [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario), Canada. He received his mathematical education at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey). He served the [Society of Actuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Actuaries) from 1985 to 1987 as Vice-President for Research and Studies. He developed the [Schuette–Nesbitt formula](wikipedia://en/14182874) with [Donald R. Schuette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_R._Schuette).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- with Richard Brauer: (1937). "On the modular representations of groups of finite order". University of Toronto studies
- with [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) and [Robert M. Thrall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Thrall): (1944). "Rings with minimum condition. No. 1.". University of Michigan Press[^o5]
- with R. M. Thrall: (1946). "Some ring theorems with applications to modular representations". *Annals of Mathematics*. 47. p. 551–567. doi:10.2307/1969092
- with George H. Andrews: (1965). "Periodograms of graduation operators". *Transactions of the Society of Actuaries*. 17. p. 166–177
- with J. J. McCutcheon: (1971). "Actuarial Note: Further remarks on the basic mortality functions". *Transactions of the Faculty of Actuaries*. 33 (238). p. 81–91. jstor:41218645
- with Newton L. Bowers and [James C. Hickman](wikipedia://en/11566366): (1982). "Notes on the dynamics of pension funding". *Insurance: Mathematics and Economics*. 1 (4). p. 261–270. doi:10.1016/0167-6687(82)90026-9
- (1989). "A Century of Mathematics in America, Part III". p. 617–38
- with [Marjorie V. Butcher](wikipedia://en/65179490): *Mathematics of Compound Interest.* Ulrichs Books. 1971.
