# Don Ylvisaker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Don Ylvisaker"}

**Nils Donald Ylvisaker** (September 23, 1933 – March 20, 2022),[^o3] often known as **Don Ylvisaker**, was an American mathematical statistician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ylvisaker was born in [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis), [Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota), USA. He studied at [Concordia College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_College_%28Moorhead,_Minnesota%29) and obtained his BA in mathematics and economics in 1954. He then continued his study in mathematics at the [University of Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) and obtained an MA in 1956. He received his Ph.D. in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1960 under the supervision of [Emanuel Parzen](wikipedia://en/4291645). Ylvisaker was Parzen's first PhD student at Stanford.[^o5]

Ylvisaker taught at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), and the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), before moving to [the University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), where he remained until his retirement in 1996. He was one of the founding members of the Department of Statistics at UCLA.[^o11]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ylvisaker was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1991.[^o7]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Besides academic research, Ylvisaker served as a statistical consultant to the [California State Lottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Lottery).[^o11] Ylvisaker's wife Anna Ylvisaker (née Ricci) works at the UCLA Department of Mathematics.[^o9]

Ylvisaker died on March 20, 2022.[^o10][^o11]
