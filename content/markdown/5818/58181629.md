# Joseph H. M. Steenbrink

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph H. M. Steenbrink"}

*Image: Steenbrink joseph.jpg.* Steenbrink at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2006

**Joseph Henri Maria Steenbrink** (born 1947) is a Dutch mathematician, specializing in algebraic geometry.

:::

Steenbrink received in 1974 his doctorate from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) with thesis advisor [Frans Oort](wikipedia://en/58171100) and thesis *Limits of Hodge Structures and Intermediate Jacobians*. He is now a professor at [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen).

His research deals with singularity theory (including three-dimensional Calabi-Yau varieties), mixed Hodge structures (after [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544)), and variation of Hodge structures[^o3] (after [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414)).

Steenbrink was an Invited Speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in 1990 in Kyoto.[^o4]

His doctoral students include [Aise Johan de Jong](wikipedia://en/29023548).

In addition to his mathematical work, Steenbrink is a harpsichordist, organist, and choir singer.[^o5]
