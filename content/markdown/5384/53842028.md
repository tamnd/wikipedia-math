# Julia Gog

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Gog"}

**Julia Rose Gog** OBE is a British mathematician and professor of [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology) in the [faculty of mathematics](wikipedia://en/3254271) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o4] She is also a David N. Moore [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow), director of studies in [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) at [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge)[^o5] and a member of both the Cambridge immunology network and the infectious diseases interdisciplinary research centre.[^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Gog read mathematics in [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) where she was awarded a Master of Arts degree in 2001.[^o10] She became a research fellow in [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge),[^o11] and completed a PhD in 2003.[^o12]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Gog is a specialist in [mathematical and theoretical biology](wikipedia://en/662088) and the study of [infectious diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infectious_disease),[^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20] particularly [influenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influenza)[^o21][^o22][^o23][^o24] and [coronavirus disease 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019).[^o25] In 2020, she served on the [Scientific Advisory Group for Emergencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_Advisory_Group_for_Emergencies) (SAGE) advising the [government of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_United_Kingdom) on its response to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).[^o26]

Gog's paper *The influenza virus: it's all in the packaging* was included in the book *50 Visions of Mathematics*,[^o27] published to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) (IMA), a book "designed to showcase the beauty of mathematics ... without frying your brain".

Her research has been funded by the [Medical Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Research_Council_%28United_Kingdom%29) (MRC), [Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biotechnology_and_Biological_Sciences_Research_Council) (BBSRC) and [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) (EPSRC).[^o28]

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 2015 Gog was awarded Pilkington Prize for excellence in teaching by the University of Cambridge,[^o30] and in 2016 she was involved in the National Young Mathematicians' Awards, a project in which 490 schools competed.[^o31][^o32] She was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2017 by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097),[^o33] and the [Rosalind Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Franklin) prize by the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 2020.[^o34]

Gog was awarded a [Royal Society University Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_University_Research_Fellowship) (URF) which she held from 2004 to 2012.[^o5]

In 2017, Gog was one of 13 mathematicians featured in the touring photographic exhibition *Women of Mathematics*. It showed photographs by Noel Tovia Matoff and extracts from interviews with the women.[^o35][^o36]

In 2020, Gog won the [Rosalind Franklin Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Franklin_Award). She was appointed [Officer of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (OBE) in the [2020 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Birthday_Honours) for services to academia and the COVID-19 response.[^o38]
