# Lilly Sanathanan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lilly Sanathanan"}

**Lalitha** (**Lilly**) **Padman Sanathanan** is an Indian statistician.

:::

Sanathanan's early research concerned estimation of population size from sampled data, in the context of [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics). She completed her Ph.D. in 1969, at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago); her dissertation, *Estimating Population Size in the Particle Scanning Context*, was supervised by David Lee Wallace. After several years as an assistant and associate professor at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago), she moved to [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory) in the late 1970s.

By 1991 she had shifted career direction, to [drug development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_development). After working as a senior statistician at [Abbott Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbott_Laboratories), associate director of statistics for [Ciba-Geigy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciba-Geigy), and director of research statistics for [Smith, Kline & French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith,_Kline_&_French), she became a director in the [Center for Drug Evaluation and Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Drug_Evaluation_and_Research) of the [Food and Drug Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration). She was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1990. In 1991 she returned to private industry, as vice president of biostatistics and clinical data systems at the Institute for Biological Research and Development in [Irvine, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California). She later founded ClinWorld, a binational (US and Indian) clinical research organisation, which was purchased by Indian health corporation Sami Labs (now the Sami-Sabinsa Group) in 2003.

In 2008 she published a self-help book, *Life Vest*, in Bangalore. She continues to operate a Florida-based statistics firm, STATLINK, and is a contributor to a cross-cultural [podcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast), the Global India Podcast.
