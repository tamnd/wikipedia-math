# Louis Auslander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Auslander"}

**Louis Auslander** (July 12, 1928 – February 25, 1997) was a Jewish American mathematician.[^n1] He had wide-ranging interests both in pure and applied mathematics and worked on [Finsler geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_geometry), geometry of [solvmanifolds](wikipedia://en/4292247) and [nilmanifolds](wikipedia://en/3668693), locally affine spaces, many aspects of [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [solvable Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solvable_Lie_group), and multidimensional [Fourier transforms](wikipedia://en/52247) and the design of signal sets for communications and radar. He is the author of more than one hundred papers and ten books.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Auslander received his Ph.D. at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1955 under [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1955-57 and again in 1971-72.[^o5] After holding a variety of faculty positions at US universities, in 1965 Auslander joined the faculty at [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York) and since 1971 he had been a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics and Computer Science there.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Louis Auslander was married twice, first for over 25 years to Elinor Newstadt Auslander, with whom he had three children (Nathan, Rose, and Daniel), and later to Fernande Couturier Auslander.[^o7] His brother [Maurice Auslander](wikipedia://en/30337642) was also a mathematician.[^n2]

- Auslander, L.; Tolimieri, R. (1979). "Is computing the finite Fourier transform pure or applied mathematics?". *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*. 1. p. 847–897. doi:10.1090/s0273-0979-1979-14686-x

### Books {#Books}

- with L. Markus: *Flat Lorentz 3-Manifolds*, AMS 1957
- with Robert MacKenzie: *Introduction to differentiable Manifolds*, McGraw Hill 1963[^o14]
- with Leon W. Green and Frank J. Hahn: *Flows on homogeneous spaces*, Princeton University Press 1963 (with the assistance of Lawrence Markus and [William S. Massey](wikipedia://en/4967089) and an appendix by L. Greenberg)[^o15]
- with [Calvin C. Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_C._Moore): *Unitary representations of solvable [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945)*, AMS 1966
- *Abelian Harmonic Analysis, Theta Functions and Function Algebras on a Nilmanifold*, Springer, 1975
- *Lecture Notes on Nil-Theta Functions*, CBMS lectures, American Mathematical Society, 1977
- *Minimal flows and their extensions*, North-Holland 1988[^o16]
- as editor: *Signal processing theory*, 2 volumes, Springer 1990
