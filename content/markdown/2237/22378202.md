# Lajos Pukánszky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lajos Pukánszky"}

**Lajos Pukánszky** (1928-1996) was a Hungarian and American mathematician noted for his work in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). He was born in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest) on November 24, 1928, defended his thesis in 1955 at the [University of Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged) under [Béla Szőkefalvi-Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béla_Szőkefalvi-Nagy), but left Hungary in 1956. After taking several posts in the United States (at the Research Institute of Advanced Studies in Baltimore, the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA)), in 1965 he became a professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), where he stayed until his retirement. He gave an [invited address at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Nice in 1970. In 1988 a conference entitled "The Orbit Method in Representation Theory" was held at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) in honor of his sixtieth birthday. He died on February 15, 1996, in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia).

:::

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

Pukánszky's early work concerned [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185) and related subjects. In 1956 he constructed two nonisomorphic factors of type III. A bulk of his later work was devoted to the unitary representation theory of [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). He found a geometric condition, known as the **Pukánszky condition**, that allowed an extension of the [orbit method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirillov_orbit_theory) from nilpotent to solvable Lie groups and played a major role in the subsequent development of the theory. Later he considered unitary representations of general separable locally compact groups and, in particular, gave a characterization for their being CCR groups or type I groups.
