# Parry point (triangle)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parry point"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Parry point** is a special point associated with a plane [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is the [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) designated *X*(111) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s *[Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593)*. The Parry point and Parry circle are named in honour of the English geometer [Cyril Parry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyril_Parry_%28geometer%29), who studied them in the early 1990s.[^o2]

:::

## Parry circle {#Parry_circle}

*Image: Parry point.svg.* solid black solid orange \#e77aff solid red The Parry circle intersects the circumcircle at two points: the focus of the [Kiepert parabola](wikipedia://en/69991093), and the **Parry point**.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a plane triangle. The circle through the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) and the two [isodynamic points](wikipedia://en/5102885) of $\triangle ABC$ is called the **Parry circle** of $\triangle ABC$. The equation of the Parry circle in [barycentric coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycentric_coordinates) is[^o4]

$$3(b^2-c^2)(c^2-a^2)(a^2-b^2)(a^2yz+b^2zx+c^2xy) + (x+y+z)\left( \sum_\text{cyclic} b^2c^2(b^2-c^2)(b^2+c^2-2a^2)x\right) =0$$

The center of the Parry circle is also a triangle center. It is the center designated as *X*(351) in the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers. The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the center of the Parry circle are

$$a(b^2-c^2)(b^2+c^2-2a^2) : b(c^2-a^2)(c^2+a^2-2b^2) : c(a^2-b^2)(a^2+b^2-2c^2)$$

The center of the Parry circle is the centroid (average) of the centers of the three [circles of Apollonius](wikipedia://en/2932144), which are collinear on the [Lemoine axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemoine_axis). The center also lies on the [trilinear polar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trilinear_polar) of the intersection of the Lemoine axis and the [line at infinity](wikipedia://en/403142).[^o5]

The axes of the [Steiner ellipse](wikipedia://en/19106257) intersect the Lemoine axis on the Parry circle.[^o6]

## Parry point {#Parry_point}

The Parry circle and the [circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432) of triangle $\triangle ABC$ intersect in two points. One of them is the focus of the [Kiepert parabola](wikipedia://en/69991093) of $\triangle ABC$.[^o8] The other point of intersection is called the *Parry point* of $\triangle ABC$.

The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the Parry point are

$$\frac{a}{2a^2-b^2-c^2} : \frac{b}{2b^2-c^2-a^2} : \frac{c}{2c^2-a^2-b^2}$$

The Parry point, the centroid and the [Steiner point](wikipedia://en/35857329) of $\triangle ABC$ are collinear.

The point of intersection of the Parry circle and the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ which is the focus of the Kiepert parabola of $\triangle ABC$ is also a triangle center and it is designated as *X*(110) in [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593). The trilinear coordinates of this triangle center are

$$\frac{a}{b^2-c^2} : \frac{b}{c^2-a^2} : \frac{c}{a^2-b^2}$$

The focus of the Kiepert parabola, the centroid and the [Tarry point](wikipedia://en/14662924) of $\triangle ABC$ are collinear.
