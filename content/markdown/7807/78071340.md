# Reinhard Diestel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reinhard Diestel"}

**Reinhard Diestel** (born 1959) is a German mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), including the interplay among [graph minors](wikipedia://en/353042), [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory), [tree decomposition](wikipedia://en/159023), and infinite graphs. He holds the chair of discrete mathematics at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Diestel has a Ph.D. from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in England, completed in 1986. His dissertation, *Simplicial Decompositions and Universal Graphs*, was supervised by [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956).

He continued at Cambridge as a fellow of [St. John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge) until 1990. In 1994, he took a professorship at the [Chemnitz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemnitz_University_of_Technology), and in 1999 he was given his current chair at the University of Hamburg.

At Hamburg, his doctoral students have included [Daniela Kühn](wikipedia://en/37003499) and [Maya Stein](wikipedia://en/39582817).

## Books {#Books}

Diestel's books include:

- *Graph Decompositions: A Study in Infinite Graph Theory* (Oxford University Press, 1990)
- *Graph Theory* ([Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980) 173, Springer, 1997; 6th ed., 2024). Originally published in German as *Graphentheorie* (1996), and translated into Chinese, Japanese, and Russian.
- *Tangles: A Structural Approach to Artificial Intelligence in the Empirical Sciences* (Cambridge University Press, 2024; 2006.01830)
