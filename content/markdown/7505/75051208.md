# Andrea Troxel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrea Troxel"}

**Andrea Beth Troxel** is an American biostatistician whose research involves [longitudinal data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_data), [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676), the design of [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717), and [behavioral economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_economics) (the study of how financial incentives to medical patients such as [diet rewards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_rewards) can affect their health). She is a professor in the Department of Population Health and director of the Division of Biostatistics in the [New York University Grossman School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Grossman_School_of_Medicine).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Troxel studied applied mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), graduating [cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cum_laude) in 1991. She completed a doctorate (Sc.D.) at the [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health) in 1995. Her dissertation, *Methods for the Analysis of Longitudinal Measurements Subject to Nonignorable Non-Monotone Missing Data*, was supervised by David P. Harrington.

After postdoctoral research from 1995 to 1997 at the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Center), she became an assistant professor of clinical public health at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1997, changing her affiliation to Columbia's Department of Biostatistics in 2000. She moved to the [Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perelman_School_of_Medicine_at_the_University_of_Pennsylvania) in 2003, becoming a full professor there in 2011 before moving to her present position at New York University in 2016. Troxel now co-leads the Zoster Eye Disease Study (ZEDS) as Director and MPI, overseeing the study's Statistics and Data Coordinating Center. [^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Troxel was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2012.
