# Bettina Richmond

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bettina Richmond"}

**Martha Bettina Richmond** (née Zoeller, January 30, 1958 – November 22, 2009) was a German-American mathematician, mathematics textbook author, professor at [Western Kentucky University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Kentucky_University), and murder victim.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Richmond was born in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden) on January 30, 1958, earned a [vordiplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vordiplom) (the German equivalent of a bachelor's degree) from the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg),E and completed her Ph.D. at [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) in 1985. Her doctoral dissertation, *Freeness of Hopf algebras over grouplike subalgebras*, was supervised by Warren Nichols, a student of [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky).

She became a professor at [Western Kentucky University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Kentucky_University), teaching there for 23 years. Topics in her mathematical research included [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), [transformation semigroups](wikipedia://en/19313303), [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424), and [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714),AB including the proof of the [Nichols–Zoeller freeness theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nichols–Zoeller_freeness_theorem) in Hopf algebra.A With her husband, Thomas Richmond, she was the author of a mathematics textbook, *A Discrete Transition to Advanced Mathematics*.C She also published works in [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446).DE

## Murder {#Murder}

Richmond was stabbed to death on November 22, 2009, in the parking lot of a [racquetball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racquetball) facility in downtown [Bowling Green, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green,_Kentucky). According to the [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI), her murder was likely an opportunistic crime motivated by [armed robbery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_robbery). At the time of her death, she had been on leave from her faculty position to assist her father in Germany. The murder is still unsolved.
