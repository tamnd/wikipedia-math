# Ivan Oseledets

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Oseledets"}

**Ivan Oseledets** (Оселедец Иван Валерьевич; born July 6, 1983) is a Russian computer scientist and mathematician and professor at the [Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolkovo_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology).[^o3] He is best known for the [tensor train decomposition](wikipedia://en/18978398),[^o4] which is more commonly called a matrix product state in the area of [tensor networks](wikipedia://en/66567874).

:::

Oseledets joined the [Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolkovo_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) in 2013 and currently serves as the director of the centre for artificial intelligence technology.[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Oseledets was educated in Russia, receiving an M.Sc from the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) in 2006, and a Ph.D. from the G.I. Marchuk Institute of Numerical Mathematics[^o7] of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2007. He received the Russian [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) in 2012 also from the G.I. Marchuk Institute of Numerical Mathematics of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

On February 7, 2019, Russian President Vladimir Putin presented Oseledets with an award for, *proposing breakthrough computational technology for solving multidimensional problems in physics, chemistry, biology, and data analysis based on tensor expansions.*[^o9]

In April 2022, Oseledets was elected to receive the honorary title, [Professor of the Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_the_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o10]

Oseledets received a [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) from the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation). The starting date of the award was February 2022.[^o11]

## Early life and family {#Early_life_and_family}

Ivan Oseledets comes from a family of mathematicians. His grandfather, Ivan Bezhaev, was associate professor at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and reached the rank of [lieutenant general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_general) in the Soviet Union's [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) where he was responsible for various mathematical projects involving [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). His father, [Valeriy Oseledets](wikipedia://en/75401505), proved [Oseledets theorem](wikipedia://en/2537958) in ergodic systems theory.[^o13] He is married to a fellow mathematician Ekaterina Muravleva and has a daughter (born around 2011).
