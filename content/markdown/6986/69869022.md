# Anna Parker Fessenden

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Parker Fessenden"}

**Anna Parker Fessenden** (April 8, 1896 – May 3, 1972) was an American botanist and mathematics educator.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Anna Parker Fessenden was born in [Thomaston, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomaston,_Maine), and raised in [Mattapan, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan), the middle of three daughters of William S. Fessenden and Alida Mary Mehan Fessenden.[^o4] Her mother was assistant principal of [Sandwich High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandwich_High_School).[^o5]

Fessenden graduated from [Girls' Latin School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Latin_Academy) in 1914,[^o6][^o7] and graduated from [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College) in 1918.[^o8] As a college student, she was active in the Smith College [Unitarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitarian_Universalism) Club,[^o9] and she edited and wrote for the *Smith College Monthly*.[^o10] She earned a master's degree from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1920.[^o4] Her master's thesis, under advisor [Josephine Tilden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephine_Tilden),[^o11] was titled "Observations on Two Rare Australian Algae, *Myriocladia Sciurus*, Harvey and *Bactrophora Irregularis*, N. SP."[^o12]

## Career {#Career}

Fessenden taught botany at [Vassar College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassar_College),[^o14] [Wellesley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellesley_College)[^o15][^o16] and at the University of Minnesota.[^o17] She and Josephine Tilden co-authored a 22-page article on [brown algae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_algae) from Australia.[^o18] She taught mathematics at [Needham High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Needham_High_School) in Massachusetts for 36 years, and was a director of math programs for the Needham school district. She retired from teaching in 1962.[^o4]

Fessenden was an active member of several clubs including the [Audubon Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audubon), and a trustee of the Thomaston Historical Society.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Fessenden died in 1972, aged 76 years, in [Camden, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden,_Maine). Her grave is with her parents' graves, in [Sandwich, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandwich,_Massachusetts).[^o4]
