# West Wales and the Valleys

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="West Wales and the Valleys"}

*Image: NUTS 3 regions of Wales map.svg.* The region consisted of the green [principal areas of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_areas_of_Wales) on this map, with [East Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Wales) in shades of brown.

**West Wales and the Valleys** was a UK [International Territorial Level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Territorial_Level) 2 [statistical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Wales#Statistical_regions) covering the western areas of [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales) and the [South Wales Valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_Valleys). Created as part of [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat)'s [Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) (NUTS), it was used by the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) (EU) to deliver the [European Regional Development Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Regional_Development_Fund) to areas of Wales, until [the UK's withdrawal from the EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit) in 2020, after which it was mirrored onto the [Office for National Statistics](wikipedia://en/324029)' International Territorial Level (ITL) geo-classification system. The remainder of Wales was part of the [East Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Wales) statistical region. It was replaced in 2025, by three alternative regions of Wales.[^o2]

:::

## Description {#Description}

The statistical region covered all of western Wales from [Denbighshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denbighshire) in the north, to the [South Wales Valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_Valleys) and covering [Bridgend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgend_County_Borough), [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea), [Neath Port Talbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neath_Port_Talbot), as well as the [Isle of Anglesey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Anglesey) off the north-west coast of Wales.[^o4] It covers an area of 1240000 ha, with a coastline of 1150 km. In September 2010, it had around 1.28 million people, 64% of the [population of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Wales), although 60% of the statistical region's population resided in the South Wales Valleys.[^o5] The region was greatly impacted by the loss of its [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_in_Wales) and heavy industry.[^o6]

The 15 [principal areas of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_areas_of_Wales)[^o7] that were part of the region are: [Blaenau Gwent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaenau_Gwent), [Bridgend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgend_County_Borough), [Caerphilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caerphilly_County_Borough), [Carmarthenshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmarthenshire), [Ceredigion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceredigion), [Conwy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conwy_County_Borough), [Denbighshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denbighshire), [Gwynedd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwynedd), [Isle of Anglesey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Anglesey), [Merthyr Tydfil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merthyr_Tydfil_County_Borough), [Neath Port Talbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neath_Port_Talbot), [Pembrokeshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembrokeshire), [Rhondda Cynon Taf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhondda_Cynon_Taf), [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea) and [Torfaen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torfaen).[^o8][^o9]

The region was used to manage [European Union (EU) funding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Regional_Development_Fund) to this part of the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom),[^o4] with the funding applied in seven-year periods.[^o7] Between 2014 and 2020, €1.2 billion was allocated to the regions classified as 'less developed'[^o10] by the EU, having a [GDP per capita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP_per_capita_of_British_regions) lower than 75% of the EU average.[^o6] In 2016, the region had been awarded the highest level of structural (convergence) funding in the UK for a third time, compared to the two occasions on which [Cornwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall), [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool) and [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland)'s [Highlands and Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highlands_and_Islands) have received the highest level too. The funding is aimed at countries which have a [gross value added](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_value_added) (GVA) that is less than three quarters of the EU average. The region was awarded the highest level again, because while the region's GVA has increased, it had not increased as much as the EU average did.[^o7] The region was also specifically awarded "special financial assistance" by the EU,[^o11] alongside [South West England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_West_England).[^o12]

Following the UK's withdrawal from the European Union ([Brexit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit)), disputes over continued funding for the region were raised. The [Welsh Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Government) argued that it is not receiving the same levels of funding from the UK Government as the EU had provided, while the UK Government states it is fully replacing the EU funds for the regions. The UK Government, however, awards the funds under a more competitive bidding system, compared to the EU which set overall objectives but let priorities be decided locally.[^o11] Also, as a result of Brexit, [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat)'s [Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) (NUTS), was replaced by the UK [Office for National Statistics](wikipedia://en/324029)' [International Territorial Level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Territorial_Level) (ITL), a similar geoclassification system that is currently a mirror of the pre-existing NUTS regions.[^o13]

West Wales and the Valleys was an ITL 2 area, comprising eight ITL 3 areas, which were either individuals or groupings of the principal areas of Wales. The ITL 3 areas were:[^o13]

- [Bridgend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgend_County_Borough) and [Neath Port Talbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neath_Port_Talbot) (17)
- [Conwy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conwy_County_Borough) and [Denbighshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denbighshire) (13)
- Central [Valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_Valleys) (15)
- Gwent [Valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_Valleys) (16)
- [Gwynedd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwynedd) (12)
- [Isle of Anglesey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Anglesey) (11)
- South West Wales (corresponding to former [Dyfed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyfed)) (14)
- [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea) (18)

*The numbers in parentheses afterwards reference those used on the map at the top of this article.*
