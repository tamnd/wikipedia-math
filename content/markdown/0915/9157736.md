# André Joyal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="André Joyal"}

**André Joyal** (; born 1943) is a professor of mathematics at the [Université du Québec à Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_du_Québec_à_Montréal) who works on [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869). He was a member of the School of Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2013,[^o3] where he was invited to join the *Special Year on Univalent Foundations of Mathematics*.[^o4]

:::

## Research {#Research}

He discovered [Kripke–Joyal semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kripke–Joyal_semantics),[^o6] the theory of [combinatorial species](wikipedia://en/160076) and with [Myles Tierney](wikipedia://en/51793007) a generalization of the [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316) of [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042)[^o7] in the setup of locales. Most of his research is in some way related to [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013) and their applications. He did some work on [quasi-categories](wikipedia://en/28662219), after their invention by [Michael Boardman](wikipedia://en/8770110) and Rainer Vogt, in particular conjecturing[^o8] and proving the existence of a [Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519) [model structure](wikipedia://en/2895304) on the category of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784) whose weak equivalences generalize both equivalence of categories and [Kan equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan_equivalence) of spaces, which is now known as [Joyal model structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyal_model_structure). He co-authored the book "Algebraic Set Theory" with [Ieke Moerdijk](wikipedia://en/25499812) and recently started a web-based expositional project Joyal's CatLab [^o9] on categorical mathematics.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Joyal was born in [Drummondville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drummondville) (formerly Saint-Majorique). He has three children and lives in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal).
