# Albert algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **Albert algebra** is a 27-dimensional [exceptional](wikipedia://en/5280515) [Jordan algebra](wikipedia://en/936371). They are named after [Abraham Adrian Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Adrian_Albert), who pioneered the study of [non-associative algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-associative_algebras), usually working over the [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers). Over the real numbers, there are three such Jordan algebras [up to](wikipedia://en/44787) [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828).[^o2] One of them, which was first mentioned by (txt) and studied by (Albert 1934), is the set of 3×3 [self-adjoint matrices](wikipedia://en/189682) over the [octonions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octonions), equipped with the binary operation

:::

$$x \circ y = \frac12 (x \cdot y + y \cdot x),$$

where $\cdot$ denotes matrix multiplication. Another is defined the same way, but using [split octonions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_octonions) instead of octonions. The final is constructed from the non-split octonions using a different standard involution.

Over any [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018), there is just one Albert algebra, and its automorphism group *G* is the simple split group of type [F<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/292877).[^o5][^o6][^o7] (For example, the [complexifications](wikipedia://en/755604) of the three Albert algebras over the real numbers are isomorphic Albert algebras over the complex numbers.) Because of this, for a general field *F*, the Albert algebras are classified by the [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) group H<sup>1</sup>(*F*,*G*).[^o8][^o9]

The [Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction](wikipedia://en/30230650) applied to an Albert algebra gives a form of the [E7 Lie algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E7_Lie_algebra). The split Albert algebra is used in a construction of a 56-dimensional [structurable algebra](wikipedia://en/45662642) whose automorphism group has identity component the simply-connected algebraic group of type [E<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725).[^o10]

The space of [cohomological invariants](wikipedia://en/42868641) of Albert algebras over a field *F* (of characteristic not 2) with coefficients in **Z**/2**Z** is a [free module](wikipedia://en/326454) over the cohomology ring of *F* with a basis 1, *f*<sub>3</sub>, *f*<sub>5</sub>, of degrees 0, 3, 5.[^o11] The cohomological invariants with 3-torsion coefficients have a basis 1, *g*<sub>3</sub> of degrees 0, 3.[^o12] The invariants *f*<sub>3</sub> and *g*<sub>3</sub> are the primary components of the [Rost invariant](wikipedia://en/39486121).
