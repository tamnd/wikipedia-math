# Free parameter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Free parameter"}

Free variable A **free parameter** is a variable in a [mathematical model](wikipedia://en/20590) which cannot be predicted precisely or constrained by the model[^o2] and must be [estimated](wikipedia://en/160379)[^o3] experimentally or theoretically. A mathematical model, theory, or [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) is more likely to be right and less likely to be the product of [wishful thinking](wikipedia://en/386062) if it relies on few free parameters and is consistent with large amounts of data.

:::
