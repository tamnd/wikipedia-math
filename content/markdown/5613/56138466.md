# Mario Pascal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mario Pascal"}

**Mario Pascal** (31 May 1896 – 6 November 1949) was an Italian applied mathematician, specializing in fluid mechanics and aerodynamics.

:::

Pascal was born in [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia) into a family with origins in the French district of [Tarascon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarascon),[^o2] Mario Pascal was the son of the mathematician [Ernesto Pascal](wikipedia://en/60166070), brother of the mathematician Alberto Pascal, and nephew of the Latinist Carlo Pascal. Mario Pascal studied mathematics at the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples), where he received his *laurea* in 1919.

He was an assistant in [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) to [Roberto Marcolongo](wikipedia://en/30792193) and then became at the *Istituto Superiore Navale* a *libero docente* in rational mechanics and then a *professore incaricato*. He then became a *professore incaricato* at the [Accademia Aeronautica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Aeronautica) of [Caserta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caserta) and there shortly before his death in 1949 was appointed a full professor. His death was due to an illness contracted during WW I.

He dealt almost exclusively with mechanics, especially fluid mechanics, attempting with very limited success to extend the [Kutta-Joukowski theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kutta-Joukowski_theorem) to three dimensions of space.[^o3]

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Bologna in 1928.[^o4]
