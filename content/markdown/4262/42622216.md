# Lê Dũng Tráng

Lê

*Image: Le dung trang.jpg.* Tráng in 2004

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lê Dũng Tráng"}

**Lê Dũng Tráng** (27 July 1947 – 19 November 2025) was a Vietnamese-French mathematician.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

At the end of 1949, Tráng came to France. He attended the [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand) in Paris. He obtained a [Ph.D. degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1969 and 1971 under the supervision of [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040) and [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544). From 1975 to 1999, he was professor at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII) and research director of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). From 1983 to 1995, he was also a professor at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). From 2002 to 2009, he headed the department of mathematics at the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) (ICTP), in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), Italy.

Tráng was a frequent guest scientist at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (with [Heisuke Hironaka](wikipedia://en/13822)) and [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University) (with [Terence Gaffney](wikipedia://en/40737298) and [David B. Massey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_B._Massey)).

He was particularly concerned with singularity theory in the complex domain ([Milnor fibrations](wikipedia://en/2696842), [perverse sheaves](wikipedia://en/1714012)).

In 2000, he was involved in promoting scientific exchange between the United States and Vietnam.[^o4] For this, he received an honorary doctorate from the [Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Academy_of_Science_and_Technology) in 2004.[^o5] He was a Fellow of the [Third World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_World_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1993.

His students included [Hélène Esnault](wikipedia://en/5358788) and [Claude Sabbah](wikipedia://en/41608298).

Tráng died in [Pau, Pyrénées-Atlantiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pau,_Pyrénées-Atlantiques) on 19 November 2025, at the age of 78.[^o6]
