# Fischer group Fi22

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fischer group Fi22"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Fischer group** *Fi<sub>22</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

64,561,751,654,400

\= 2<sup>17</sup>3<sup>9</sup>5<sup>2</sup>71113

≈ 6.

## History {#History}

*Fi<sub>22</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and is the smallest of the three Fischer groups. It was introduced by (txt) while investigating [3-transposition groups](wikipedia://en/29399459).

The [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2, and the [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 6.

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

The Fischer group Fi<sub>22</sub> has a [rank 3 action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_3_action) on a graph of 3510 vertices corresponding to its 3-transpositions, with point stabilizer the double cover of the group PSU<sub>6</sub>(2). It also has two rank 3 actions on 14080 points, exchanged by an outer automorphism.

Fi<sub>22</sub> has an irreducible real representation of dimension 78. Reducing an integral form of this mod 3 gives a representation of Fi<sub>22</sub> over the field with 3 elements, whose quotient by the 1-dimensional space of fixed vectors is a 77-dimensional irreducible representation.

The perfect triple cover of Fi<sub>22</sub> has an irreducible representation of dimension 27 over the field with 4 elements. This arises from the fact that Fi<sub>22</sub> is a subgroup of <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub>(2<sup>2</sup>). All the ordinary and modular character tables of Fi<sub>22</sub> have been computed. (Hiss White 1994) found the 5-modular character table, and (Noeske 2007) found the 2- and 3-modular character tables.

The automorphism group of Fi<sub>22</sub> centralizes an element of order 3 in the [baby monster group](wikipedia://en/324799).

:::

## Generalized Monstrous Moonshine {#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) is not limited to the monster, but that similar phenomena may be found for other groups. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. For *Fi*<sub>22</sub>, the McKay-Thompson series is $T_{6A}(\tau)$ where one can set a(0) = 10 (),

$$\begin{aligned}j_{6A}(\tau) &=T_{6A}(\tau)+10\\ &=\left(\left(\tfrac{\eta(\tau)\,\eta(3\tau)}{\eta(2\tau)\,\eta(6\tau)}\right)^{3}+2^3 \left(\tfrac{\eta(2\tau)\,\eta(6\tau)}{\eta(\tau)\,\eta(3\tau)}\right)^{3}\right)^2\\ &=\left(\left(\tfrac{\eta(\tau)\,\eta(2\tau)}{\eta(3\tau)\,\eta(6\tau)}\right)^{2}+3^2 \left(\tfrac{\eta(3\tau)\,\eta(6\tau)}{\eta(\tau)\,\eta(2\tau)}\right)^{2}\right)^2-4\\ &=\frac{1}{q} + 10 + 79q + 352q^2 +1431q^3+4160q^4+13015q^5+\dots \end{aligned}$$

and *η*(*τ*) is the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020).
