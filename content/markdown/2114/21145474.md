# Conductor–discriminant formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conductor–discriminant formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **conductor–discriminant formula** or **Führerdiskriminantenproduktformel**, introduced by (txt) for [abelian extensions](wikipedia://en/174448) and by (txt) for [Galois extensions](wikipedia://en/457064), is a formula calculating the relative [discriminant](wikipedia://en/3022963) of a finite Galois extension $L/K$ of [local](wikipedia://en/145375) or [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358) from the [Artin conductors](wikipedia://en/34170016) of the [irreducible characters](wikipedia://en/691741) $\mathrm{Irr}(G)$ of the [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) $G = G(L/K)$.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of global fields with Galois group $G$. Then the [discriminant](wikipedia://en/3022963) equals

$$\mathfrak{d}_{L/K} = \prod_{\chi \in \mathrm{Irr}(G)}\mathfrak{f}(\chi)^{\chi(1)},$$

where $\mathfrak{f}(\chi)$ equals the global [Artin conductor](wikipedia://en/34170016) of $\chi$.[^n1]

:::

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

Let $L = \mathbf{Q}(\zeta_{p^n})/\mathbf{Q}$ be a [cyclotomic extension](wikipedia://en/30872462) of the rationals. The Galois group $G$ equals $(\mathbf{Z}/p^n)^\times$. Because $(p)$ is the only finite prime ramified, the global Artin conductor $\mathfrak{f}(\chi)$ equals the local one $\mathfrak{f}_{(p)}(\chi)$. Because $G$ is abelian, every non-trivial irreducible character $\chi$ is of degree $1 = \chi(1)$. Then, the local Artin conductor of $\chi$ equals the conductor of the $\mathfrak{p}$-adic completion of $L^\chi = L^{\mathrm{ker}(\chi)}/\mathbf{Q}$, i.e. $(p)^{n_p}$, where $n_p$ is the smallest natural number such that $U_{\mathbf{Q}_p}^{(n_p)} \subseteq N_{L^\chi_\mathfrak{p}/\mathbf{Q}_p}(U_{L^\chi_\mathfrak{p}})$. If $p > 2$, the Galois group $G(L_\mathfrak{p}/\mathbf{Q}_p) = G(L/\mathbf{Q}) = (\mathbf{Z}/p^n)^\times$ is cyclic of order $\varphi(p^n)$, and by [local class field theory](wikipedia://en/669532) and using that $U_{\mathbf{Q}_p}/U^{(k)}_{\mathbf{Q}_p} = (\mathbf{Z}/p^k)^\times$ one sees easily that if $\chi$ factors through a primitive character of $(\mathbf{Z}/p^i)^\times$, then $\mathfrak{f}_{(p)}(\chi) = p^i$ whence as there are $\varphi(p^i) - \varphi(p^{i-1})$ primitive characters of $(\mathbf{Z}/p^i)^\times$ we obtain from the formula $\mathfrak{d}_{L/\mathbf{Q}} = (p^{\varphi(p^n)(n - 1/(p-1))})$, the exponent is

$$\sum_{i = 0}^{n} (\varphi(p^i) - \varphi(p^{i-1}))i = n\varphi(p^n) - 1 - (p-1)\sum_{i=0}^{n-2}p^i = n\varphi(p^n) - p^{n-1}.$$

:::
