# MAA FOCUS

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MAA FOCUS"}

***MAA FOCUS*** is the [newsmagazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsmagazine) of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). It carries news items and short articles of interest to the organization's members.[^o3]

:::

## History and profile {#History_and_profile}

The magazine was first published in March 1981; the first editor was [Marcia P. Sward](wikipedia://en/21169373), who held that position until September 1985.[^o5] Beginning in 2009 the magazine is published six times a year; previously it was published nine times a year.[^o6] The magazine is printed on glossy paper with a final trim size of 8-1/4 inches wide by 10-5/8 inches high.[^o7] Circulation in 2008 was 22,400 copies.[^o8]
