::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="8-simplex honeycomb"}

In [eighth-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighth_dimension) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **8-simplex honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). The tessellation fills space by [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex), [rectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_8-simplex), [birectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_8-simplex), and [trirectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_8-simplex) facets. These facet types occur in proportions of 1:1:1:1 respectively in the whole honeycomb.

:::

## A8 lattice {#A8_lattice}

This [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the [A8 lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A8_lattice) or **8-simplex lattice**. The 72 vertices of the [expanded 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_8-simplex) vertex figure represent the 72 roots of the ${\tilde{A}}_8$ Coxeter group.[^o8] It is the 8-dimensional case of a [simplectic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplectic_honeycomb). Around each vertex figure are 510 facets: 9+9 [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex), 36+36 [rectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_8-simplex), 84+84 [birectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_8-simplex), 126+126 [trirectified 8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_8-simplex), with the count distribution from the 10th row of [Pascal's triangle](wikipedia://en/49497).

${\tilde{E}}_8$ contains ${\tilde{A}}_8$ as a subgroup of index 5760.[^o9] Both ${\tilde{E}}_8$ and ${\tilde{A}}_8$ can be seen as affine extensions of $A_8$ from different nodes:
