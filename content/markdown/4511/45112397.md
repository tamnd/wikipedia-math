# Anna Wienhard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Wienhard"}

*Image: Anna Wienhard MFO.jpg.* Wienhard at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2010

**Anna Katharina Wienhard** (born 1977)[^o2] is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerns [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and especially the use of higher [Teichmüller spaces](wikipedia://en/1835661) to study the [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory) of [symmetric](wikipedia://en/53741) geometric structures.[^o2] She is a director at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wienhard did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), earning a double degree in [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) and mathematics.[^o2] Continuing at Bonn, she earned a doctorate in 2004 under the joint supervision of [Hans Werner Ballmann](wikipedia://en/24812368) and Marc Burger.

After holding temporary positions at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), and [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), she took a faculty position at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2007. She moved to Heidelberg as a full professor in 2012[^o6] and to Leipzig as a research director at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506)[^o3] in 2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

From 2009 to 2013, Wienhard was a member of the Young Academy of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) and [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o6] In 2012, she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] She was elected as a regular member of the Leopoldina in 2023.[^o9]

She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2012,[^o10] and an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o11] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Clay Senior Scholar for Fall 2019.[^o12]
