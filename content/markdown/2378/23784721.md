# Frucht graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frucht graph"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Frucht graph** is a [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) with 12 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899), 18 edges, and no nontrivial [symmetries](wikipedia://en/15094186).[^o3] It was first described by [Robert Frucht](wikipedia://en/27069553) in 1949.[^o9]

:::

The Frucht graph can be constructed from the [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720): $[-5,-2,-4,2,5,-2,2,5,-2,-5,4,2]$. This describes it as a cubic graph in which two of the three adjacencies of each vertex form part of a [Hamiltonian cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_cycle) and the numbers specify how far along the cycle to find the third neighbor of each vertex.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Frucht graph is a [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508), because three vertices are incident to every vertex, thereby the [degree](wikipedia://en/1374448) of every vertex is 3. It is one of the five smallest cubic graphs possessing only a single [graph automorphism](wikipedia://en/15094186), the identity: every vertex can be distinguished topologically from every other vertex.[^o7] Such graphs are called [asymmetric](wikipedia://en/20249418) (or identity) graphs. [Frucht's theorem](wikipedia://en/26259599) states that any [finite group](wikipedia://en/19447) can be realized as the group of symmetries of a graph,[^o8] and a strengthening of this theorem, also due to Frucht, states that any finite group can be realized as the symmetries of a [3-regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821).[^o9] The Frucht graph provides an example of this strengthened realization for the [trivial group](wikipedia://en/193059).

*Image: Frucht graph as a convex polyhedron.svg.* Frucht graph as a convex polyhedron

The Frucht graph is a [Halin graph](wikipedia://en/19658548), a type of planar graph formed from a tree with no degree-two vertices by adding a cycle connecting its leaves.[^o3] Every Halin graph is [3-vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph): deleting two of its vertices cannot disconnect it. By [Steinitz's theorem](wikipedia://en/19762817), the Frucht graph is hence [polyhedral](wikipedia://en/24656270), meaning its 12 vertices and 18 edges form the skeleton of a convex polyhedron. It is also [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph).

It is [pancyclic](wikipedia://en/28691490),[^o11] with [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 3, and diameter 4. Its [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3. Its [independence number](wikipedia://en/325802#Independence) is 5.

The [characteristic polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_polynomial_of_a_graph) of the Frucht graph is $(x-3) (x-2) x (x+1) (x+2) (x^3+x^2-2 x-1) (x^4+x^3-6 x^2-5 x+4)$.
