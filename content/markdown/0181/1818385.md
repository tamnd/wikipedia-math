# Symplectic vector field

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symplectic vector field"}

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **symplectic vector field** is one whose flow preserves a [symplectic form](wikipedia://en/28356). That is, if $(M,\omega)$ is a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) with [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) $M$ and symplectic form $\omega$, then a [vector field](wikipedia://en/62641) $X\in\mathfrak{X}(M)$ in the [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) $\mathfrak{X}(M)$ of smooth vector fields on $M$ is symplectic if its [flow](wikipedia://en/2619735#Flows_of_vector_fields_on_manifolds) preserves the symplectic structure. In other words, the [Lie derivative](wikipedia://en/294437) of the vector field must vanish:

:::

$\mathcal{L}_X\omega=0$.[^o2]

An alternative definition is that a vector field is symplectic if its interior product with the symplectic form is closed.[^o2] (The interior product gives a map from vector fields to 1-forms, which is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) due to the nondegeneracy of a symplectic 2-form.) The equivalence of the definitions follows from the closedness of the symplectic form and [Cartan's magic formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan's_magic_formula) for the [Lie derivative](wikipedia://en/294437) in terms of the [exterior derivative](wikipedia://en/220782).

If the interior product of a vector field with the symplectic form is an [exact form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_form) (and in particular, a closed form), then it is called a [Hamiltonian vector field](wikipedia://en/1818270). If the first [De Rham cohomology](wikipedia://en/221530) group $H^1(M)$ of the manifold is trivial, all closed forms are exact, so all symplectic vector fields are Hamiltonian. That is, *the [obstruction](wikipedia://en/2179639) to a symplectic vector field being Hamiltonian lives in $H^1(M)$.* In particular, symplectic vector fields on [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) manifolds are Hamiltonian.

The [Lie bracket](wikipedia://en/10282799) of two symplectic vector fields is Hamiltonian, and thus the collection of symplectic vector fields and the collection of Hamiltonian vector fields both form [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944).
