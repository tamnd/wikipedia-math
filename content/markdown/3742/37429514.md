# Demazure conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demazure conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Demazure conjecture** is a conjecture about [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) over the integers made by (txt, p. 83). The conjecture implies that many of the results of his paper can be extended from complex algebraic groups to algebraic groups over [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) of other characteristics or over the integers. (txt, 432–435) showed that Demazure's conjecture (for [classical groups](wikipedia://en/10172878)) follows from their work on [standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718), and [Peter Littelmann](wikipedia://en/37398004) extended this to all reductive algebraic groups.

:::
