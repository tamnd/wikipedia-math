# Revaz Gamkrelidze

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Revaz Gamkrelidze"}

**Revaz Valerianovic Gamkrelidze** (რევაზ ვალერიანის ძე გამყრელიძე, [ISO 9984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_9984): *Revaz Valeris je Gamqrelije*; February 4, 1927 – May 5, 2025) was a Soviet and Georgian mathematician known for his work in [optimal control theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_control_theory) and related fields. He was a member of the Georgian Academy of Sciences[^o3] and of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]

:::

## Background {#Background}

After secondary school, Gamkrelidze attended [Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University).[^o6] In his sophomore year, he went to Moscow to study at the mechanics and mathematics faculty of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) where he became a student of [Pontryagin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontryagin).[^o6]

Gamkrelidze died on May 5, 2025, at the age of 98.[^o7] He was the brother of the linguist [Tamaz Gamkrelidze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamaz_Gamkrelidze).

## Work {#Work}

At Moscow State University, he initially worked in the fields of algebraic geometry and topology, and derived what is now known as [Gamkrelidze's formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamkrelidze's_formula).[^o9] In 1954 he began his work on optimal control. He wrote *Mathematical Theory of Optimal Processes* with Pontryagin, [Boltyanskii](wikipedia://en/20464140) and [Mishchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeniĭ_Frolovich_Mishchenko). This work was awarded the State Lenin Prize for Science and Technology in 1962.[^o10][^o11] Gamkrelidze was the founding editor of *[Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia_of_Mathematical_Sciences)*.[^o12]
