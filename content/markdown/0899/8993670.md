# Lawrie Brown

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrie Brown"}

**Lawrence Peter "Lawrie" Brown** is a cryptographer and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researcher, currently a (retired and now visiting) Senior Lecturer with [UNSW Canberra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Defence_Force_Academy#UNSW_Canberra_at_ADFA) at the [Australian Defence Force Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Defence_Force_Academy). His notable work includes the design of the [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) [LOKI](wikipedia://en/647844) and the AES candidate [LOKI97](wikipedia://en/611984). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales) in 1991 under the supervision of [Jennifer Seberry](wikipedia://en/4546010), with a dissertation on the design of LOKI and the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of the [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978). Subsequently, his research changed focus to the Safe Erlang mobile code system, to aspects of trust issues in eCommerce with some of his Ph.D. students, and with the use of Proxy Certificates for Client Authentication.

:::

## Personal interests {#Personal_interests}

According to his personal homepage, Brown's social activities include dancing a number of different styles. He has also composed some of his own dances. He enjoys reading science fiction and is an [amateur radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_radio) enthusiast. Brown is a practicing [Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptist).
