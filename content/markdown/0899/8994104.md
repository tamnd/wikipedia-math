# Augustin Banyaga

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Augustin Banyaga"}

**Augustin Banyaga** (born March 31, 1947) is a [Rwandan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwanda)-born [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research fields include [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology) and [contact geometry](wikipedia://en/476398). He is currently a professor of mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

While at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), he earned a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) degree in 1971 and an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.S.) in 1972.[^o4] He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree in 1976 at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) under the supervision of [André Haefliger](wikipedia://en/20280849). (Banyaga was the first person from Rwanda to obtain a Ph.D. in mathematics.[^o6]) He was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) (1977–1978),[^o7] [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1978–1982), and assistant professor at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) (1982–1984), before joining the faculty at Pennsylvania State University in 1984 as associate professor. He was promoted to full professor in 1992.

In 2009 Banyaga was elected a Fellow of the [African Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Academy_of_Sciences), and in 2015 he was named a Distinguished Senior Scholar by [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o8]

He has made significant contributions in symplectic topology, especially on the structure of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) of [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) preserving a symplectic form ([symplectomorphisms](wikipedia://en/312304)). One of his best-known results states that the group of [Hamiltonian](wikipedia://en/1818270) diffeomorphisms of a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), [connected](wikipedia://en/87141), [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) is a [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600); in particular, it does not admit any non-trivial [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) to the real line.

Banyaga is an editor of *[Afrika Matematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrika_Matematica)*, the journal of the [African Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/1764260), and an editor of the African Journal of Mathematics. He has supervised the theses of 9 Ph.D. students.[^o5]
