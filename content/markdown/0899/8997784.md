# What Is Mathematics?

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="What Is Mathematics?"}

***What Is Mathematics?*** is a classic book by [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238) and [Herbert Robbins](wikipedia://en/745193), published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press). Written in the belief that "the traditional place of mathematics in education is in grave danger," it is an introduction to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), intended to offer "vantage points from which the substance and driving forces of modern mathematics can be surveyed" both by students and by the general public.[^o3]

:::

First published in 1941, it discusses [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176). A posthumous edition was published in 1996 with an additional chapter on recent progress in mathematics, including the proofs of the [four-color map theorem](wikipedia://en/10949) and [Fermat's last theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953), written by [Ian Stewart](wikipedia://en/332183).

## Authorship {#Authorship}

The book was based on Courant's course material. Although Robbins assisted in writing a large part of the book, he had to fight for authorship. Courant alone held the copyright for the book. This resulted in Robbins receiving a smaller share of the royalties.[^o5][^o6]

According to [Constance Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constance_Reid),[^o6] Courant finalized the choice of the title after discussing it with [Thomas Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Mann).
