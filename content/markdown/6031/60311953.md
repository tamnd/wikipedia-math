# Symbolic language (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symbolic language"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **symbolic language** is a language that uses [characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_%28computing%29) or [symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbols) to represent concepts, such as [mathematical objects](wikipedia://en/19453961), [operations](wikipedia://en/4140245), and [statements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositions), and the entities or [operands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operands) on which the operations are performed.[^o2][^o3]

:::
