# Bessarion Makris

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bessarion Makris"}

**Bessarion Makris** (Βησσαρίων Μακρής, 1635- 1699) was a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks) scholar and theologian.

:::

He was born in [Ioannina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioannina), northwestern [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece), center of the 17th-18th century [Modern Greek Enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Greek_Enlightenment). In 1672 Makris became the head of the Goumas (later known as Balanos) School. He composed a manual named *Σταχυολογία* (Set of Gleanings), printed in 1686 in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice). This work introduced new teaching methods with a series of questions and answers on Greek grammar and was considered a create success in Greek education and especially among the schools of Ioannina.[^o2][^o3]

Makris got also involved in various theological discussions with another local scholar, [Georgios Sougdouris](wikipedia://en/32305773), who succeeded him as head of the Goumas School in 1683.[^o4]

At 1699 he published the philosophical work *Περί των τριών Αρετών Πίστεως Αγάπης και Ελπίδος* (On the three Virtues: Faith, Love and Hope).[^o5]
