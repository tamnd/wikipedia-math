# Theorycraft

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theorycraft"}

**Theorycraft** (or **theorycrafting**) is the mathematical analysis of [game mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_mechanics) (usually in [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games)) to discover optimal [strategies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy) and [tactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tactic_%28method%29). Theorycraft involves analyzing [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), hidden [systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System) or underlying game [code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_code) in order to glean information that is not apparent during normal [gameplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gameplay).[^o2] Theorycraft is similar to analyses performed in sports or other games such as baseball's [sabermetrics](wikipedia://en/28692). The term has been said to come from [StarCraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft) players as a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of "[game theory](wikipedia://en/11924)" and "StarCraft".[^o3]

:::

Theorycraft is prominent in [competitive gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitive_gaming) (such as [multiplayer games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplayer_video_game), [speedrunning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedrunning) and [racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing) events), where players attempt to gain competitive advantage by analyzing game systems. As a result, theorycraft can lower barriers between players and [game designers](wikipedia://en/42434747). Game designers must consider that players will have a comprehensive understanding of game systems and players can influence design by exploiting game systems and discovering dominant or unintended strategies.[^o4]

Theorycrafting proven to be potent usually finds inclusion in [the metagame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metagaming). Theorycrafting knowledge is often communicated through [online communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_community).[^o3][^o5]

The way players theorycraft varies from game to game but often games under the same [genres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_genre) will have similar theorycrafting methods. Communities develop standardized ways to communicate their findings, including use of specialized tools to measure and record game data; terminology; and simulations to represent certain data.

The term theorycraft can be used in a pejorative sense.[^o4] In this sense, "theorycraft" refers to naïve or impractical theorizing that would not succeed during actual gameplay.
