# Feebly compact space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Feebly compact space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is **feebly compact** if every [locally finite](wikipedia://en/11611098) cover by nonempty [open sets](wikipedia://en/39358) is finite. The concept was introduced by [Sibe Mardešić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibe_Mardešić) and P. Papić in 1955.[^o2]

:::

Some facts:

- Every [compact space](wikipedia://en/6042) is feebly compact.[^o2]
- Every feebly compact [paracompact space](wikipedia://en/48631) is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042).
- Every feebly compact space is [pseudocompact](wikipedia://en/5075659) but the converse is not necessarily true.[^o2]
- For a [completely regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completely_regular_space) [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) the properties of being feebly compact and pseudocompact are equivalent.
- Any maximal feebly compact space is [submaximal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submaximal_space).[^o3]
