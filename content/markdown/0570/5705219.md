# Resolvable space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Resolvable space"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is said to be **resolvable** if it is expressible as the union of two disjoint [dense subsets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dense_subset). For instance, the [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers) form a resolvable topological space because the [rationals](wikipedia://en/19727024) and [irrationals](wikipedia://en/20647689) are disjoint dense subsets. A topological space that is not resolvable is termed **irresolvable**.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The [product](wikipedia://en/24910) of two resolvable spaces is resolvable
- Every [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact) topological space without [isolated points](wikipedia://en/390557) is resolvable
- Every [submaximal space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submaximal_space) is irresolvable

:::
