# UCT Mathematics Competition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="UCT Mathematics Competition"}

The **UCT Mathematics Competition** is an annual [mathematics competition](wikipedia://en/19710) for schools in the [Western Cape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Cape) province of [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa), held at the [University of Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cape_Town).

:::

Around 7000 participants from Grade 8 to Grade 12 take part, writing a multiple-choice paper. Individual and pair entries are accepted, but all write the same paper for their grade. The current holder of the School Trophy is [Rondebosch Boys High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rondebosch_Boys_High_School) for the 2024 competition.[^o3]

The competition was established in 1977 by Mona Leeuwenberg and Shirley Fitton, who were teachers at [Diocesan College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocesan_College) and [Westerford High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerford_High_School), and since 1987 has been run by Professor John Webb of the University of Cape Town.[^o4]
