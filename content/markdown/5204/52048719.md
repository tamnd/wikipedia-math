# International Prize in Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="International Prize in Statistics"}

The **International Prize in Statistics** is awarded every two years to an individual or team "for major achievements using statistics to advance science, technology and human welfare". The International Prize in Statistics, along with the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483), are the two highest honours in the field of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685).

:::

The prize is modelled after the [Nobel Prizes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize), [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285), [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) and [Turing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_Award) and comes with a monetary award of \$80,000. The award ceremony takes place during the [World Statistics Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Statistics_Congress).

## Rules {#Rules}

The prize recognizes a single work or body of work, representing a powerful and original idea that had an impact in other disciplines or a practical effect on the world. The recipient must be alive when the prize is awarded.[^o10]

## Organization {#Organization}

The prize is awarded by the International Prize in Statistics Foundation, which comprises representatives of the following major [learned societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society):

- [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)
- [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society)
- [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)
- [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute)
- [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)

In addition to recognizing the contributions of a statistician, the Foundation also aims at educating the public about statistical innovations and their impact on the world and gaining wider recognition for the field.[^o12]

The recipient of the prize is chosen by a selection committee comprising international experts in the field. 2016, the committee members were [Xiao-Li Meng](wikipedia://en/31925930) ([Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University)), [Sally Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Morton) ([Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech)), [Stephen Senn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Senn) ([Luxembourg Institute of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_Institute_of_Health)), [Bernard Silverman](wikipedia://en/7785395) ([University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)), [Stephen Stigler](wikipedia://en/1276686) ([University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago)), [Susan Wilson](wikipedia://en/55900254) ([Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University)) and [Bin Yu](wikipedia://en/39209141) ([University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley)). As of May 2022, the members of the selection committee are [Yoav Benjamini](wikipedia://en/36596077), [Francisco Cribari-Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Cribari-Neto), [Vijay Nair](wikipedia://en/41548389), [Sonia Petrone](wikipedia://en/56697074), [Nancy Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Reid), [Sylvia Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvia_Richardson), and [Jane-Ling Wang](wikipedia://en/55557431).
