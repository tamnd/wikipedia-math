::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Hexapentakis truncated icosahedron"}

The **hexapentakis truncated icosahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) constructed as an [augmented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29) [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282). It is a [geodesic polyhedron](wikipedia://en/53024913) {3,5+}<sub>3,0</sub>, with pentavalent vertices separated by an edge-direct distance of 3 steps.

:::

## Construction {#Construction}

Geodesic polyhedra are constructed by subdividing faces of simpler polyhedra, and then projecting the new vertices onto the surface of a sphere. A geodesic polyhedron has straight edges and flat faces that approximate a sphere, but it can also be made as a [spherical polyhedron](wikipedia://en/10393861) (A [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) on a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859)) with true [geodesic](wikipedia://en/91096) curved edges on the surface of a sphere. and [spherical triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_triangle) faces.

The **pentakis truncated icosahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) constructed as an [augmented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29) [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282), adding pyramids to the 12 pentagonal faces, creating 60 new triangular faces.

It is geometrically similar to the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) where the 20 triangular faces are subdivided with a central hexagon, and 3 corner triangles.

#### Dual {#Dual}

Its dual polyhedron can be called a *pentatruncated pentakis dodecahedron*, a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), with its vertices augmented by pentagonal pyramids, and then truncated the apex of those pyramids, or adding a [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659) to each face of the dodecahedron. It is the net of a [dodecahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5981640).

*Image: Dual pentakis truncated icosahedron.png.*

The **hexakis truncated icosahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) constructed as an [augmented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29) [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282), adding pyramids to the 20 hexagonal faces, creating 120 new triangular faces.

It is visually similar to the chiral [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137) which has 80 triangles and 12 pentagons.

#### Dual {#Dual}

The dual polyhedron can be seen as a *hexatruncated pentakis dodecahedron*, a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) with its faces augmented by pentagonal pyramids (a [pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196)), and then its 6-valance vertices truncated.

*Image: Dk6tI.png.*

It has similar groups of irregular pentagons as the [pentagonal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/727271).
