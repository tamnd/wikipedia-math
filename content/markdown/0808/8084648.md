# Wawrzyniec Żmurko

Wawrzyniec Żmurko

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wawrzyniec Żmurko"}

**Wawrzyniec Żmurko** (9 July 1824, in [Jaworów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaworów,_Lwów_Voivodeship) – 3 April 1889, in [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów)) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor of [Lwów University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów_University) and [Lwów Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów_Polytechnic), [honoris causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) of Lwów University, member of [Polish Academy of Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Learning). He was a president of [Polish Copernicus Society of Naturalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Copernicus_Society_of_Naturalists) (1879-1881). In 1885-1886 he was Rector of the Lwów University. He was the father of a painter, [Franciszek Żmurko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franciszek_Żmurko).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Żmurko was active in a number of different fields in mathematics, including differential equations, analytic functions theory, linear algebra and applications of mathematics. He published in both Polish and German. He also invented instruments for drawing conic sections and a device which he called the *integrator* for graphical solving of calculus integral problems.
