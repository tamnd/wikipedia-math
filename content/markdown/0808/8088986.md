# Complex conjugate line

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Complex conjugate line"}

In [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), the **complex conjugate line** of a [straight line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_line) is the line that it becomes by taking the [complex conjugate](wikipedia://en/173918) of each point on this line.[^o2]

:::

This is the same as taking the complex conjugates of the [coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficients) of the line. So if the equation of $D$ is $D: ax + by + cz = 0$, then the equation of its conjugate $D*$ is $D*: a*x + b*y + c*z = 0$.

The conjugate of a [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line) is the line itself. The [intersection point](wikipedia://en/8240558) of two conjugated lines is always real.[^o3]
