# John Shepherdson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Shepherdson"}

**John Cedric Shepherdson**, [FBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_British_Academy) (7 June 1926 – 8 January 2015) was a British logician who was [Henry Overton Wills Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Overton_Wills_Professor_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) from 1976 to 1991.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Shepherdson was born in [Huddersfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield) on 7 June 1926, the son of Arnold Shepherdson, a chemist, and his wife Elsie, née Aspinall. He attended [Manchester Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Grammar_School) on a scholarship; excelling in mathematics, in 1943 he secured a state scholarship and an open scholarship to [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he read mathematics. He graduated with a first-class degree in 1946.[^o3]

## Career and later life {#Career_and_later_life}

Shepherdson was appointed to an assistant lectureship at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) in 1946. He spent the rest of his career at the university, being promoted to lecturer in 1949, [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29) in 1955 and professor in 1964,[^o5] before he was finally [Henry Overton Wills Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Overton_Wills_Professor_of_Mathematics) from 1977 until he retired in 1991.[^o6] He was elected a [fellow of the British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_British_Academy) in 1990.[^o7]

Shepherdson was a keen climber and skier. His wife, Margaret, *née* Smith, a biochemist and academic whom he had married in 1955, died in 2014. Shepherdson died on 8 January 2015.[^o3]
