# Tricomi–Carlitz polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tricomi–Carlitz polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Tricomi–Carlitz polynomials** or **(Carlitz–) Karlin–McGregor polynomials** are polynomials studied by Tricomi (1951),[^o2] (txt),[^o4] and (txt),[^o6] related to [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) on the [positive integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_integers).

:::

They are given in terms of [Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917) by

$$\ell_n(x)=(-1)^nL_n^{(x-n)}(x).$$

They are special cases of the [Chihara–Ismail polynomials](wikipedia://en/32809583).
