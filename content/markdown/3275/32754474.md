# Gould polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gould polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Gould polynomials** $G_n$, $n\in\mathbb{N}$, are [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) introduced by [Henry W. Gould](wikipedia://en/38149527).[^o2] They are defined in terms of the [falling factorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falling_factorial) by[^o3]

:::

$$G_n(x;a,b) = \frac{x}{x-an}\Big(\frac{x-an}{b}\Big)_n.$$

They form the [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596) for $f(t)=e^{at}(e^{bt}-1)$, $b\neq 0$, which has [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

$$\exp(x f^{-1}(t)) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} G_n(x;a,b)\frac{t^n}{n!}.$$

The [inverse function](wikipedia://en/14907) is given by

$$f^{-1}(t)=\frac{1}{b}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\binom{-(b+ak)/b}{k-1}\frac{t^k}{k}.$$
