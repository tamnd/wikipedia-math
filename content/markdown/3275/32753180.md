# Mott polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mott polynomials"}

In mathematics the **Mott polynomials** *s*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are polynomials given by the exponential [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993):

:::

$$e^{x(\sqrt{1-t^2}-1)/t}=\sum_n s_n(x) t^n/n!.$$

## Introduction {#Introduction}

They were introduced by [Nevill Francis Mott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevill_Francis_Mott) who applied them to a problem in the theory of electrons.[^o3]

## Logic {#Logic}

Because the factor in the exponential has the power series

$$\frac{\sqrt{1-t^2}-1}{t} = -\sum_{k\ge 0} C_k \left(\frac{t}{2}\right)^{2k+1}$$

in terms of [Catalan numbers](wikipedia://en/216488) $C_k$, the coefficient in front of $x^k$ of the polynomial can be written as

$[x^k] s_n(x) =(-1)^k\frac{n!}{k!2^n}\sum_{n=l_1+l_2+\cdots +l_k}C_{(l_1-1)/2}C_{(l_2-1)/2}\cdots C_{(l_k-1)/2}$, according to the general formula for [generalized Appell polynomials](wikipedia://en/2462837), where the sum is over all [compositions](wikipedia://en/550741) $n=l_1+l_2+\cdots+l_k$ of $n$ into $k$ positive odd integers. The [empty product](wikipedia://en/181289) appearing for $k=n=0$ equals 1. Special values, where all contributing Catalan numbers equal 1, are

$$[x^n]s_n(x) = \frac{(-1)^n}{2^n}.$$

$$[x^{n-2}]s_n(x) = \frac{(-1)^n n(n-1)(n-2)}{2^n}.$$

By differentiation the recurrence for the first derivative becomes

$$s'(x) =- \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor (n-1)/2\rfloor} \frac{n!}{(n-1-2k)!2^{2k+1}} C_k s_{n-1-2k}(x).$$

The first few of them are

$$s_0(x)=1;$$

$$s_1(x)=-\frac{1}{2}x;$$

$$s_2(x)=\frac{1}{4}x^2;$$

$$s_3(x)=-\frac{3}{4}x-\frac{1}{8}x^3;$$

$$s_4(x)=\frac{3}{2}x^2+\frac{1}{16}x^4;$$

$$s_5(x)=-\frac{15}{2}x-\frac{15}{8}x^3-\frac{1}{32}x^5;$$

$$s_6(x)=\frac{225}{8}x^2+\frac{15}{8}x^4+\frac{1}{64}x^6;$$

## Sheffer sequence {#Sheffer_sequence}

The polynomials *s*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) form the associated [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596) for –2*t*/(1–t<sup>2</sup>)[^o6]

## Generalized hypergeometric function {#Generalized_hypergeometric_function}

An explicit expression for them in terms of the [generalized hypergeometric function](wikipedia://en/361184) <sub>3</sub>F<sub>0</sub>:[^o8]

$$s_n(x)=(-x/2)^n{}_3F_0(-n,\frac{1-n}{2},1-\frac{n}{2};;-\frac{4}{x^2})$$
