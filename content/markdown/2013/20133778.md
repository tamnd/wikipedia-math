# Boris Kordemsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Kordemsky"}

**Boris A. Kordemsky** (Борис Анастасьевич Кордемский; 23 May 1907 – 29 March 1999) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator). He is best known for his [popular science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_science) books and [mathematical puzzles](wikipedia://en/366445). He is the author of over 70 books and popular mathematics articles.

:::

Kordemsky was born in [Kiknur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiknur), [Vyatka Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatka_Governorate), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). He received his Ph.D. in education in 1956 and taught mathematics at several [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) colleges.
