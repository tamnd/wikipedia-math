# Avner Friedman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Avner Friedman"}

**Avner Friedman** (אבנר פרידמן; born November 19, 1932) is Distinguished Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and Physical Sciences at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). His primary field of research is [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), with interests in [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), [mathematical modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modeling), [free boundary problems](wikipedia://en/32070938), and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).

:::

Friedman received his Ph.D. degree in 1956 from the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University). He was a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) (1962–1985), a Duncan Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) (1985–1987), and a professor of mathematics (Regents' Professor from 1996) at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) (1987–2001). He was director of the Institute for Mathematics and its Applications from 1987 to 1997. He was the founding director of [Minnesota Center for Industrial Mathematics](https://cse.umn.edu/math/minnesota-center-industrial-mathematics) (1994–2001). He was the founding director of the [Mathematical Biosciences Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences_Institute) at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), serving as its first director from 2002–2008.

Friedman has been the chair of the [Board of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Mathematical_Sciences) (1994–1997) and the president of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (1993–1994).[^o4] He has been awarded the [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) (1962–65), the [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) (1966–7), the [Stampacchia Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Prize) (1982), the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Special Creativity Award (1983–85; 1991–93). He is a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (since 1987) and a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (since 1993). In 2009 he became a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o5] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

He has been adviser to 27 doctoral students[^o7] and has published 25 books[^o8] and over 500 papers.[^o9]
