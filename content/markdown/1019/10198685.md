# Modular Lie algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modular Lie algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **modular Lie algebra** is a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of positive [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621).

:::

The theory of modular Lie algebras is significantly different from the theory of [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) and [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) Lie algebras. This difference can be traced to the properties of [Frobenius automorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_automorphism) and to the failure of the [exponential map](wikipedia://en/17945#The_exponential_map) to establish a tight connection between properties of a modular Lie algebra and the corresponding [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924).

Although serious study of modular Lie algebras was initiated by [Nathan Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Jacobson) in 1950s, their [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) in the [semisimple](wikipedia://en/1337587) case was advanced only recently due to the influential [Lusztig conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusztig_conjectures), which have been partially [proved](wikipedia://en/82285).
