# Olga Hahn-Neurath

*Image: Olga Hahn-Neurath.jpg.* Portrait of Hahn-Neurath

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olga Hahn-Neurath"}

**Olga Hahn-Neurath** (; 20 July 1882 – 20 July 1937) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). She is best known for being a member of the [Vienna Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Circle). She was sister of the mathematician [Hans Hahn](wikipedia://en/1075212).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), Hahn enrolled as a student for math and philosophy studies at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1902.[^o3] She became blind in 1904, when she was 22. In 1911, she became the third ever female graduate in philosophy at Vienna University.[^o3] Her doctoral thesis, published in 1911, received great compliments from her instructor, Adolf Stöhr, the successor to the chair of [Ludwig Boltzmann](wikipedia://en/544255). Her main interest in math was in the field of [Boolean algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_algebra_%28logic%29).

In 1912 she married [Otto Neurath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Neurath) whom she met during her studies.[^o4] Olga became a regular participant in the Vienna Circle discussions. Following the defeat of [Red Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Vienna) in the [Austrian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Civil_War) (February 1934), she fled, through [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) and [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) to the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), where she joined her husband. She died on her birthday three years later in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague), of a lung infection following an operation.[^o4]
