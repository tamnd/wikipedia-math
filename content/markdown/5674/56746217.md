# Robert Miller Hardt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Miller Hardt"}

**Robert Miller Hardt** (born June 24, 1945, [Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh))[^o3] is an American mathematician. His research deals with geometric measure theory, partial differential equations, and continuum mechanics. He is particularly known for his work with [Leon Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Simon) proving the boundary regularity of volume minimizing hypersurfaces.

:::

Hardt received in 1967 his bachelor's degree from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and in 1971 his Ph.D. from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) under [Herbert Federer](wikipedia://en/12185345) with thesis *Slicing and Intersection Theory for Chains Associated with Real Analytic Varieties*. In 1971 he became an instructor and later a professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). In 1988 he became a professor at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), where he is *W. L. Moody Professor*. His doctoral students include [Fang-Hua Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lin_Fanghua).

Hardt was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1976 and at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES) in 1978 and 1981. He was a visiting professor at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) in 1979, at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and at the [University of Wuppertal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wuppertal).

In 1986 Hardt was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). In 2015 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
