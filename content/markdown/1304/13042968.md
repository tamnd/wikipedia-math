# NUTS statistical regions of Austria

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Austria"}

The **Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics** (NUTS) is a [geocode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geocode) [standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardization) for referencing the [subdivisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_subdivision) of [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) for statistical purposes.[^o2] The standard is developed and regulated by the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union).[^o3] The NUTS standard is instrumental in delivering the European Union's [Structural Funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_Fund). The NUTS code for Austria is *AT* and a hierarchy of three levels is established by [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat). Below these is a further levels of geographic organisation - the local administrative unit (LAU). In Austria, the LAU 2 is [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Austria).

:::

## Overall {#Overall}

### NUTS Levels {#NUTS_Levels}

::: {#o6 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| [NUTS 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_level_NUTS_of_the_European_Union#Austria) | Groups of states (*Gruppen von Bundesländern*) | 3 |
| NUTS 2 | [States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Austria) (*Bundesländer*) | 9 |
| NUTS 3 | Groups of [Districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Austria) (*Gruppen von Politischen Bezirken*) | 35 |

:::

### Local administrative units {#Local_administrative_units}

Below the NUTS levels, the two LAU (Local Administrative Units) levels are:

::: {#o9 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| LAU 1 | — (same as NUTS 3) | 35 |
| LAU 2 | [Municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Austria) (*Gemeinden*) | 2357 |

:::

*The LAU codes of Austria can be downloaded here:* <http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/ramon/nuts/excel_files/AT_LAU_2007.xls>
