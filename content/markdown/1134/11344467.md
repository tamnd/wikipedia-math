# Quillen adjunction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quillen adjunction"}

In [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Quillen adjunction** between two [closed model categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_model_category) **C** and **D** is a special kind of [adjunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_functor) between [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) that induces an adjunction between the [homotopy categories](wikipedia://en/2040756) Ho(**C**) and Ho(**D**) via the [total derived functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_derived_functor) construction. Quillen adjunctions are named in honor of the mathematician [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Given two closed model categories **C** and **D**, a **Quillen adjunction** is a pair

(*F*, *G*): **C** $\leftrightarrows$ **D**

of [adjoint functors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_functor) with *F* left adjoint to *G* such that *F* preserves [cofibrations](wikipedia://en/1697216) and trivial cofibrations or, equivalently by the closed model axioms, such that *G* preserves [fibrations](wikipedia://en/316826) and trivial fibrations. In such an adjunction *F* is called the **left Quillen functor** and *G* is called the **right Quillen functor**.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

It is a consequence of the axioms that a left (right) Quillen functor preserves [weak equivalences](wikipedia://en/34615176) between cofibrant (fibrant) objects. The [total derived functor theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_derived_functor_theorem) of Quillen says that the total left derived functor

**L***F*: Ho(**C**) → Ho(**D**)

is a left adjoint to the total right derived functor

**R***G*: Ho(**D**) → Ho(**C**).

This adjunction (**L***F*, **R***G*) is called the **derived adjunction**.

If (*F*, *G*) is a Quillen adjunction as above such that

*F*(*c*) → *d*

with *c* cofibrant and *d* fibrant is a weak equivalence in **D** if and only if

*c* → *G*(*d*)

is a weak equivalence in **C** then it is called a **Quillen equivalence** of the closed model categories **C** and **D**. In this case the derived adjunction is an adjoint [equivalence of categories](wikipedia://en/379051) so that

**L***F*(*c*) → *d*

is an isomorphism in Ho(**D**) if and only if

*c* → **R***G*(*d*)

is an isomorphism in Ho(**C**).

:::
