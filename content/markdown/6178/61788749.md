# Collette Coullard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Collette Coullard"}

**Collette René Coullard** is an American mathematician, [industrial engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering), [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476), and [matroid](wikipedia://en/244321) theorist known for her research on [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555) problems that combine [facility location](wikipedia://en/10469986) and [stock management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_management). Formerly a professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), and [Lake Superior State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Superior_State_University), she has retired to become a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Coullard graduated in 1980 from [Lake Superior State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Superior_State_University), and earned her Ph.D. in 1985 from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). Her dissertation, *Minors of 3-Connected Matroids and Adjoints of Binary Matroids*, concerned [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory) and was supervised by [Robert E. Bixby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_E._Bixby).

She taught at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) and visited the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) before returning to Northwestern as a faculty member in 1990, and retired as a mathematics professor from Lake Superior State University in 2018. She has also visited the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) as a [Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Coullard received the National Science Foundation Presidential Young Investigator Award in 1989. She was a Charles Deering McCormick Professor of Teaching Excellence at Northwestern University from 1996 to 1999. In 2003, the Lake Superior State University Alumni Association gave her their annual Kenneth J. Shouldice Achievement Award.
