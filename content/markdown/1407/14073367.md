# Institute of Mathematics of National Academy of Sciences of Armenia

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Mathematics of National Academy of Sciences of Armenia"}

The **Institute of Mathematics of National Academy of Sciences of Armenia** ([Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_language): Հայաստանի ԳԱԱ մաթեմատիկայի ինստիտուտ) is owned and operated by the [Armenian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Academy_of_Sciences), located in [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan).

:::

## History {#History}

The Institute of Mathematics of National Academy of Sciences of Armenia originated as the Section for Mathematics and Mechanics, created within the newly formed [Armenian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1944. The section later developed into an Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics of the Armenian Academy of Sciences, whose first Director was academician [Artashes Shahinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artashes_Shahinian), known for his results in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). The Institute of Mathematics of Armenian Academy of Sciences separated from the latter Institute in 1971. The bearer of the office of the Director of Institute has been academician [Mkhitar Djrbashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkhitar_Djrbashian) (1971-1989, 1989-1994 Honorary Director).

The academicians [Sergey Mergelyan](wikipedia://en/5661318), [Norair Arakelian](wikipedia://en/48552839), [Alexandr Talalyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandr_Talalyan), [Raphayel Alexandrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphayel_Alexandrian), [Rouben V. Ambartzumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouben_V._Ambartzumian) and [Anry Nersesyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anry_Nersesyan) also have greatly influenced the formation of the scientific profile of the Institute and largely contributed to mathematics in general. In particular [Rouben V. Ambartzumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouben_V._Ambartzumian)[^o4] is famous for his work in [Stochastic Geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813) and [Integral Geometry](wikipedia://en/349336), where he created a new branch called Combinatorial Integral Geometry.[^o5] He has provided solutions to a number of classical problems in particular the solution to the Buffon Sylvester problem as well as the [Hilbert's fourth problem](wikipedia://en/1573991#cite_note-5) in dimensions 2[^o6] and 3.[^o7]

In the early years, the investigations carried out in the Institute concentrated on [Function Theory](wikipedia://en/5759). Gradually the sphere of investigations expanded and now includes [Differential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations) and [Integral](wikipedia://en/474234) Equations, [Functional Analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [Probability Theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/888711).

## Journals {#Journals}

The institute publishes a journal, *[Izvestia NAS RA Matematika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izvestia_NAS_RA_Matematika)*. The founder and the first Editor in Chief (1971–1990) of the journal was [Mkhitar Djrbashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkhitar_Djrbashian); under [Rouben V. Ambartzumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouben_V._Ambartzumian) Editor in Chief (1990 - 2010) the journal obtained international recognition and obtained an English version, *[Journal of Contemporary Mathematical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Contemporary_Mathematical_Analysis)*, published initially by Allerton Press, Inc. New York and later by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). The journal covers a wide range of topics, including: [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759); [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779); [integral geometry](wikipedia://en/349336) and [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813); [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations); [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685); [integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equations); [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923).

## Directors, faculty and members {#Directors,_faculty_and_members}

At present the Institute has about 30 main researchers as well as a number of associate researchers from [Yerevan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_State_University).

[Norair Arakelian](wikipedia://en/48552839) in 1970 (Nice) and [Mkhitar Djrbashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkhitar_Djrbashian), [Rouben V. Ambartzumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouben_V._Ambartzumian) in 1974 (Vancouver) were invited speakers at the [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians).
