# Last geometric statement of Jacobi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Last geometric statement of Jacobi"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **last geometric statement of Jacobi** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) named after [Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi](wikipedia://en/210340), which states:

:::

> *Every [caustic](wikipedia://en/880406) from any point $p$ on an [ellipsoid](wikipedia://en/145381) other than [umbilical points](wikipedia://en/9757480) has exactly four cusps*.[^o2]

Numerical experiments had indicated the statement is true[^o3] before it was [proven](wikipedia://en/82285) rigorously in 2004 by Itoh and Kiyohara.[^o4] It has since been extended to a wider class of [surfaces](wikipedia://en/4342970) beyond the ellipsoid.[^o5]
