# Grand Riemann hypothesis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grand Riemann hypothesis"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **grand Riemann hypothesis** is a generalisation of both the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125) and the [generalized Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/152567). It states that the non-trivial zeros of all [automorphic *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/32328300) lie on the critical line $1/2 + it$ with $t$ a real number variable and $i$ the [imaginary unit](wikipedia://en/52358).

:::

The **modified grand Riemann hypothesis** is the assertion that the nontrivial zeros of all automorphic *L*-functions lie on the critical line or the [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line).

## Notes {#Notes}

- [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986), in his general [functoriality conjectures](wikipedia://en/171905#Functoriality), asserts that all global *L*-functions should be automorphic.[^o3]
- The [Siegel zero](wikipedia://en/1967197), conjectured not to exist,[^o4] is a possible real zero of a [Dirichlet *L*-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_L-series), rather near *s* = 1.
- *L*-functions of Maass cusp forms can have trivial zeros which are off the real line.
