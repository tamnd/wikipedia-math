# Clay Research Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clay Research Award"}

The **Clay Research Award** is an annual award given by the Oxford-based [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) to [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) to recognize their achievements in mathematical research. The following mathematicians have received the award:
