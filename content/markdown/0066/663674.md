# E (complexity)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="E"}

In [computational complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543), the [complexity class](wikipedia://en/502426) **E** is the set of [decision problems](wikipedia://en/8336) that can be solved by a [deterministic Turing machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deterministic_Turing_machine) in time 2<sup>[*O*](wikipedia://en/44578)(*n*)</sup> and is therefore equal to the complexity class [DTIME](wikipedia://en/658538)(2<sup>*O*(*n*)</sup>).

:::

**E**, unlike the similar class [EXPTIME](wikipedia://en/54694), is not closed under [polynomial-time many-one reductions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial-time_many-one_reduction).[^o2]

## Relationship to other classes {#Relationship_to_other_classes}

E is contained in [NE](wikipedia://en/663673), as E is the deterministic-time analogue of the nondeterministic class NE.[^o4]
