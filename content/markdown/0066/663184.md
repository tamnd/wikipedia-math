# DEAL

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="DEAL"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **DEAL** (**Data Encryption Algorithm with Larger blocks**) is a [symmetric](wikipedia://en/53042) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) derived from the [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978) (DES). Its design was presented by [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen) at the [SAC](wikipedia://en/9545951) conference in 1997, and submitted as a proposal to the [AES contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AES_contest) in 1998 by [Richard Outerbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Outerbridge).

:::

DEAL is a [Feistel network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feistel_network) which uses [DES](wikipedia://en/7978) as the round function. It has a 128-bit [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) and a variable [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) of either 128, 192, or 256 bits; with 128-bit and 192-bit keys it applies 6 rounds, or 8 rounds with 256-bit keys. It has performance comparable to [Triple DES](wikipedia://en/48375), and was therefore relatively slow among AES candidates.
