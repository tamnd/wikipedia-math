# Secure Communication based on Quantum Cryptography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Secure Communication based on Quantum Cryptography"}

*Image: SECOQC Network Architecture.svg.* Brief network architecture of SECOQC

**Secure Communication based on Quantum Cryptography** (**SECOQC**) is a project that aims to develop [quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/28676005) (see there for further details). The [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) decided in 2004 to invest 11 million [EUR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro) in the project as a way of circumventing [espionage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espionage) attempts by [ECHELON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ECHELON). [Christian Monyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Monyk), the coordinator of SECOQC, said people and organizations in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), and [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) would participate in the project.

:::

On October 8, 2008 SECOQC was launched in Vienna.[^o2]

## Limitations of classical quantum cryptography {#Limitations_of_classical_quantum_cryptography}

[Quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/28676005), usually known as [quantum key distribution](wikipedia://en/51910) (QKD) provides powerful security. But it has some limitations. Following no-cloning theorem, QKD only can provide one-to-one connections. So the number of links will increase $N(N-1)/2$ as $N$ represents the number of nodes. If a node wants to participate into the QKD network, it will cause some issues like constructing quantum communication line. To overcome these issues, SECOQC was started.

## Brief architecture of SECOQC network {#Brief_architecture_of_SECOQC_network}

SECOQC network architecture can be divided into two parts: trusted private networks and quantum networks connected via QBBs (quantum backbones). The private networks are conventional networks with end-nodes and a QBB. Each QBB enables quantum channel communication with another QBB and consists of a number of QKD devices that are connected with other QKD devices over one-to-one connections.

From this, SECOQC can provide easier registration of new end-nodes in a QKD network, and quick recovery from threats on quantum channel links.
