# Erik Ivar Fredholm

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Erik Ivar Fredholm"}

**Erik Ivar Fredholm** (7 April 1866 – 17 August 1927) was a Swedish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose work on [integral equations](wikipedia://en/474234) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) foreshadowed the theory of [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fredholm was born in Stockholm in 1866. He obtained his PhD at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in 1898, under the supervision of [Gösta Mittag-Leffler](wikipedia://en/1438792). He was *docent* at [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) from 1898 to 1906 and professor from 1906 until his death.[^o5] In 1914 he was elected a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) and in 1922 a foreign member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o6] Beside his academic career he was recruited to the [Swedish Social Insurance Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Social_Insurance_Agency) when it was founded in 1902. He later served as an actuary at the insurance company Skandia (1904-1927), where his Fredholm equation was used to calculate buyback-prices.[^o7]

From 1911, he was married to Agnes Maria Liljeblad, the secretary of [Mittag-Leffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mittag-Leffler).[^o8]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

In (), Fredholm introduced and analysed a class of integral equations now called [Fredholm equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_equation). His [analysis](wikipedia://en/4355120) included the construction of [Fredholm determinants](wikipedia://en/2685562), and the proof of the [Fredholm theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_theorem).

## Honours {#Honours}

Fredholm was a member of the [Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) and of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei); he was awarded the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828) for 1908.[^o12][^o13]

The lunar crater *[Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_%28crater%29)* is named after him as is the asteroid [21659 Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21659_Fredholm).
