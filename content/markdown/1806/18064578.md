# Dijen K. Ray-Chaudhuri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dijen K. Ray-Chaudhuri"}

**Dwijendra Kumar Ray-Chaudhuri** (born November 1, 1933) is an [Indian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor emeritus at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). He and his student [R. M. Wilson](wikipedia://en/22615301) together solved the long-standing [Kirkman's schoolgirl problem](wikipedia://en/8876082) in 1968,[^o3] which contributed to developments in [design theory](wikipedia://en/2761968).

:::

He received his M.Sc. (1956) in mathematics from the famous [Rajabazar Science College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajabazar_Science_College), [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) and Ph.D. in combinatorics (1959) from [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). He served as consultant at [Cornell Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weill_Cornell_Medicine) and [Sloan Kettering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Sloan_Kettering_Cancer_Center), a professor and chairman of the Department of Mathematics at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), as well as a visiting professor of [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen) in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), and [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai).

He is best known for his work in [design theory](wikipedia://en/2761968) and the theory of [error-correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error-correcting_code), in which the class of [BCH codes](wikipedia://en/40779) is partly named after him and his Ph.D. advisor [Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._C._Bose).[^o4] Ray-Chaudhuri is the recipient of the [Euler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler) Medal by the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919) for his career contributions to combinatorics. In 2000, a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) appeared on the occasion of his 65th birthday.[^o5] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

- Distinguished Senior Research Award from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University)
- President for Forum in New Delhi
- Foundation Fellow of the [ICA](wikipedia://en/17545919)
- Euler Medal of [ICA](wikipedia://en/17545919).[^o8]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
