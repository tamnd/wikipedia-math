# 1971 census of India

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1971 census of India"}

The **1971 census of India** was the 11th in a series of [censuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_India) held in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) every decade since [1872](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_India_prior_to_independence).[^o3]

:::

The population of India was counted as 547,949,809 people.[^o4]
