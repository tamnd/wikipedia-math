# Billy James Pettis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Billy James Pettis"}

**Billy James Pettis** (1913 – 14 April 1979), was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his contributions to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).
