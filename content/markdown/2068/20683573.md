# Catalog of articles in probability theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catalog of articles in probability theory"}

This page lists articles related to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). In particular, it lists many articles corresponding to specific [probability distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_distributions). Such articles are marked here by a code of the form (X:Y), which refers to number of random variables involved and the type of the distribution. For example (2:DC) indicates a distribution with two random variables, discrete or continuous. Other codes are just abbreviations for topics. The list of codes can be found in the table of contents.

:::
