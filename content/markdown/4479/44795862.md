# Patricia E. Bauman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patricia E. Bauman"}

**Patricia E. Bauman** is an American mathematician who studies [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) that model the behavior of [liquid crystals](wikipedia://en/17973) and [superconductors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconductor).[^o5] She is a professor of mathematics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bauman received her Ph.D. from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1982, under the supervision of Eugene Fabes; her dissertation was *Properties of Non-Negative Solutions of Second-Order Elliptic Equations and their Adjoints*. She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) and a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) before joining the Purdue University faculty.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bauman was given an [AMS Centennial Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMS_Centennial_Fellowship) for 1994 to 1995.[^o7] In 2012, Bauman became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

She was elected chair of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Mathematical Aspects of Materials Science (SIAG/MS) for 2017–2019.[^o9] She is a former AMS Council member at large.[^o10]
