# Laura DeMarco

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Laura DeMarco"}

**Laura Grace DeMarco** is an American mathematician who is a professor of mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), whose research concerns [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

DeMarco received her Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2002 under the supervision of [Curtis T. McMullen](wikipedia://en/680168). She held an NSF Postdoctoral Fellowship and was an L. E. Dickson Instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) from September 2002 to August 2005. She was also an assistant professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and then she moved to the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago),[^o6] where she was tenured and promoted to professor. She moved to [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 2014,[^o7] and was promoted to Henry S. Noyes Professor of Mathematics in 2019, before she moved to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2020.[^o8] She has been appointed the [Hollis Professor of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy](wikipedia://en/31306720) as of 2026 [^o9]

DeMarco is an organizer of GROW (Graduate Research Opportunities for Women) [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) conference.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2013, DeMarco became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the inaugural class of fellows.[^o12] In 2017, she received the AMS [Ruth Lyttle Satter Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/37554140) in Mathematics for her contributions to [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), and the emerging field of [arithmetic dynamics](wikipedia://en/17584701).[^o13] In 2020, DeMarco was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o14]

She was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking in the section on [Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and Ordinary Differential Equations.[^o15][^o16] She is the 2023 [AWM-AMS Emmy Noether Lecturer](wikipedia://en/11566873) in recognition of her "fundamental and influential contributions to complex dynamics, arithmetic dynamics, and arithmetic geometry."[^o17]

Her work with [Holly Krieger](wikipedia://en/60416469) and Hexi Ye, "Uniform Manin–Mumford for a family of genus 2 curves", published in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*, won the 2020 Alexanderson Award of the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005).[^o18]

## Research {#Research}

DeMarco's research involves algebraic, [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/17584701) and [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518) with an emphasis on their interactions with each other. She has studied dynamically meaningful [compactifications](wikipedia://en/6617) of the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of [rational maps](wikipedia://en/361210) on $\mathbb{P}^1.$ [^o20][^o21] In joint work with [Curtis McMullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_McMullen), she showed how [simplicial](wikipedia://en/252686) [metrized](wikipedia://en/20018) [trees](wikipedia://en/48560) can be used to compactify the moduli space of [polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomials).[^o22] Together with Xander Faber, DeMarco has studied degenerations of complex dynamical systems from the viewpoint of [Berkovich spaces](wikipedia://en/34420832).[^o23]

With Matthew Baker, DeMarco pioneered the study of [unlikely intersections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zilber-Pink_conjecture) in arithmetic dynamics [^o24] and formulated the dynamical [André-Oort conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André-Oort_conjecture).[^o25] Together with [Holly Krieger](wikipedia://en/60416469) and Hexi Ye, DeMarco proved the first instances of the uniform dynamical [Manin-Mumford conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manin-Mumford_conjecture) for [Lattès maps](wikipedia://en/28886032)[^o26] and for [quadratic unicritical polynomials](wikipedia://en/11735693).[^o27] In collaboration with Niki Myrto Mavraki, DeMarco studied the notion of non-degeneracy for polarized dynamical systems and its relation to stability.[^o28] Later with Niki Myrto Mavraki and Hexi Ye, DeMarco proved a uniform version of the dynamical André-Oort conjecture.[^o29]
