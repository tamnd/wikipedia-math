# Marilyn Breen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marilyn Breen"}

**Marilyn Janet Breen** (born 1944)[^o3] is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma).[^o4] Her research involves geometry, including [visibility](wikipedia://en/12003132) and [orthogonal polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_polygon).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Breen graduated in 1966 from [Agnes Scott College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnes_Scott_College),[^o4] and received her Ph.D. from [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University) in 1970 under the supervision of William Ray Hare Jr. She joined the Oklahoma faculty in 1971 and was promoted to full professor in 1982.[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In her time at the University of Oklahoma, Breen won several awards for teaching and research, including an "outstanding teacher" award.[^o8]

In 2012, Breen became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

- Breen, Marilyn; Kay, David C. (1976). "General decomposition theorems for m-convex sets in the plane". *Israel Journal of Mathematics*. 24 (3–4). p. 217–233. doi:10.1007/BF02834753
