# Shelly Harvey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Shelly Harvey"}

**Shelly Lynn Harvey** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University). Her research interests include [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).[^o3]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Harvey grew up in [Rancho Cucamonga, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rancho_Cucamonga,_California) and graduated [California Polytechnic State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Polytechnic_State_University) in 1997.[^o3][^o5] She received her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from Rice University in 2002 under the supervision of [Tim Cochran](wikipedia://en/44770306).[^o3][^o5] After postdoctoral studies at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) and the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), she returned to Rice University in 2005 as the first female tenure-track mathematician there.[^o3][^o5]

## Recognitions {#Recognitions}

Harvey was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 2006. In 2012, she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
