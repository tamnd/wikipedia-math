# Pierre Petit (engineer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Petit"}

**Pierre Petit** (8 December 1594[^o3][^o2] – 20 August 1677) was a French astronomer, physicist, mathematician and instrument maker.

:::

*Image: Pierre Petit 1671 Dissertations académiques sur la nature.jpg.* *Dissertations académiques sur la nature*, 1671

Petit was born in [Montluçon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montluçon), [Bourbonnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourbonnais), France. He succeeded his father in his municipal office (*Contrôleur de l'élection*), but went to Paris in 1633 to dedicate himself to the sciences. He was a member of the circle around [Marin Mersenne](wikipedia://en/72947), and knew [Etienne Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etienne_Pascal), [Blaise Pascal](wikipedia://en/4068), and [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525). Later he was a member of the academy of [Montmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Louis_Habert_de_Montmor). On 4 April 1667 he became a fellow of [The Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Royal_Society).[^o3]

He also served as a military engineer and geographer to [Louis XIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIII) and [Louis XIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIV), in roles such as Superintendent of Fortifications.

He died in [Lagny-sur-Marne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagny-sur-Marne) in 1677.
