# Mu Sigma Rho

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mu Sigma Rho"}

**Mu Sigma Rho** (**ΜΣΡ**) is an American national [honor society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_society) for statistics.[^o3] It was established at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) in 1968.

:::

## History {#History}

Mu Sigma Rho was founded by Ted Bancroft, David Huntsberger, and Oscar Kempthorne at the Statistical Laboratory and Department of Statistics at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) in 1968.[^o5][^o6] It was formed as the National Statistics Honor Society.[^o5] Mu Sigma Rho seeks to promote and encourage scholarly activity in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), and to recognize outstanding achievement of students, faculty, and professional statisticians in academic and non-academic institutions.[^o3][^o6]

The society was incorporated in the state of Iowa.[^o6] Its first chapter, *Alpha*, was established at towa State University.[^o6] Donald K. Hotchkiss was the first president of the national society, while Eric West was the first president of the *Alpha chapter*.[^o6]

In February 2005, the society began allowing American Statistical Association chapters to join Mu Sigma Rho as affiliate chapters.[^o7] The allows students to join the society who attend universities and colleges that do not have a Mu Sigma Rho chapters.[^o7]

## Activities {#Activities}

Mu Sigma Rho provides outreach and professional service.[^o9][^o3] Some chapters host speakers and provide tutoring services to other students.[^o5] The society publishes an occasional newsletter, *The* *Mu Sigma Rhover*.[^o9]

Mu Sigma Rho presents annual awards, including the William D. Warde Statistics Education Award and the Mu Sigma Rho Early Career Undergraduate Impact Award.[^o3][^o10] The Early Career Undergraduate Impact Award is presented to a statistics faculty member for contributions to the success of undergraduate students.[^o11] The Warde Statistics Education Award known as the Statistics Education Award before 2000, is presented to a statistics instructor for excellence in the classroom and a lifetime of commitment to statistics education.[^o11] Faculty at any academic institution in the United States are eligible; recipients do not have to work for Mu Sigma Rho member institutions.[^o11][^o10] The names and photos of the winners are on the Mu Sigma Rho website.

## Membership {#Membership}

Mu Sigma Rho's membership consists of undergraduate students, graduate students, faculty, and honorary members.[^o5]

To be eligible for membership, undergraduates must have completed two years of college and eight semester hours of statistics courses, including five hours at a junior level, with a 3.40 GPA in those courses and a 3.20 GPA overall.[^o10][^o13] Graduate students seeking membership must have completed twelve semester hours with a 3.50 GPA.[^o10][^o14]
