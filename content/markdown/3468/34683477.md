# Frithiof Nevanlinna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frithiof Nevanlinna"}

*Image: Frithiof-Nevanlinna-1964.jpg.* Frithiof Nevanlinna in 1964.

**Frithiof Edvard Henrik Nevanlinna** (16 August 1894 – 20 March 1977) was a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor who worked on classical and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He was born in [Joensuu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joensuu), and was the older brother of mathematician [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna).

:::
