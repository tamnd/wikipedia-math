# Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy"}

The **Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy** is an [endowed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_endowment) [professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) established at [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) in 1727 by [Thomas Hollis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Hollis_%281659–1731%29).[^o2] The chair, now part of the Physics Department, is the second oldest at Harvard, and the oldest professorship in science in the United States.

:::

The spelling of the title of the chair has been retained since its establishment.[^o3] The original Rules and Orders relating to the chair are still extant.[^o4]

The incumbents have been:

- [Isaac Greenwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Greenwood) (1727–1737)[^o2]
- [John Winthrop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Winthrop_%28educator%29) (1737–1779)[^o2]
- [Samuel Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Williams_%28minister%29) (1779–1789)[^o2]
- [Samuel Webber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Webber) (1789–1806)[^o2]
- [John Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Farrar_%28scientist%29) (1807–1838)[^o2]
- [Joseph Lovering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Lovering) (1838–1888)[^o2]
- [Benjamin Osgood Peirce](wikipedia://en/31371822) (1888–1914)[^o2]
- [Wallace Clement Sabine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallace_Clement_Sabine) (1914–1919)[^o2]
- vacant (January 1919–September 1921)[^o5]
- [Theodore Lyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Lyman_IV) (1921–1926)
- [Percy Williams Bridgman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Williams_Bridgman) (1926–1950)
- [John Hasbrouck Van Vleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hasbrouck_Van_Vleck) (1951–1969)
- [Andrew Gleason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Gleason) (1969–1992)
- [Bertrand Halperin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Halperin) (1992–2018)
- [Cumrun Vafa](wikipedia://en/644848) (2018–2026)
- [Laura DeMarco](wikipedia://en/44795949) (2026–present)[^o6]
