::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 5-orthoplexes"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 5-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) with 3rd order [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination)) of the regular [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).

:::

There are 8 runcinations of the 5-orthoplex with [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of truncations, and [cantellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation). Four are more simply constructed relative to the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/31324900).

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a runcicantitruncated 5-orthoplex having an edge length of $\sqrt{2}$ are given by all permutations of coordinates and sign of:

$$\left(0, 1, 2, 3, 4\right)$$

### Snub 5-demicube {#Snub_5-demicube}

The **snub 5-demicube** defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 5-demicube is not uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram or and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502) \[3<sup>2,1,1</sup>\]<sup>\+</sup> or \[4,(3,3,3)<sup>\+</sup>\], and constructed from 10 [snub 24-cells](wikipedia://en/3704756), 32 [snub 5-cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snub_5-cell), 40 [snub tetrahedral antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_octahedral_prism#Related_polytopes), 80 [2-3 duoantiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-6_duoprism#Related_polytopes), and 960 irregular [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices.

:::
