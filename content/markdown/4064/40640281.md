# Jan Hemelrijk

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Hemelrijk"}

**Jan Hemelrijk** (28 May 1918 – 16 March 2005) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), Professor of Statistics at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), and authority in the field of [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hemelrijk received his PhD in 1950 at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) with a thesis entitled "Symmetry Keys and other applications of the theory of Neyman and Pearson" under supervision of [David van Dantzig](wikipedia://en/528853).[^o5]

After graduation Hemelrijk started his academic career as assistant to David van Dantzig at the [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) in Amsterdam, and later Head of the Statistical Consulting Department. He was Professor at the [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology) from 1952 to 1960. In 1960, he was appointed Professor of Statistics at the University of Amsterdam as successor of David van Dantzig.[^o6] Among his doctoral students were [Gijsbert de Leve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gijsbert_de_Leve) (1964), [Willem van Zwet](wikipedia://en/64492113) (1964), R. Doornbos (1966), Ivo Molenaar (1970), [Robert Mokken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Mokken) (1970) and J. Dik (1981).[^o7] [Jaap Wessels](wikipedia://en/40568668) in 1960 started his academic career Wessels as assistant to Jan Hemelrijk.

Hemelrijk was President of the [Netherlands Society for Statistics and Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Society_for_Statistics_and_Operations_Research),[^o8] and chief editor of the Journal of the association *Statistica Neerlandica*. He also provided the first television course*Statistics* of Teleac, broadcast in 1969 and 1970.

In 1963, he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o9]
