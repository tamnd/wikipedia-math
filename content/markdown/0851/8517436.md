# Karl Apfelbacher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Apfelbacher"}

**Karl Apfelbacher** was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who served as minister for higher public education in Upper Bavaria-East.[^o2] He was a student of [Arnold Sommerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Sommerfeld) and [Heinrich Tietze](wikipedia://en/1368572)[^o3] at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München), where he received his doctorate in 1939.[^o4] He went into teaching mathematics and science, as well as administration, in secondary schools. In 1964, he was cited as being Oberstudiendirektor[^o5] at the Oberrealschule[^o6] in [Burghausen, Altötting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burghausen,_Altötting). On October 16, 1964, the school was taken over by the Bayerisches Staatsministerium für Unterricht und Kultus.[^o7]
