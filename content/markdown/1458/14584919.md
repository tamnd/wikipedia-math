# Robert J. Elliott

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert J. Elliott"}

**Robert James Elliott** (born 1940) is a British-[Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his contributions to [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes) and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::

He was schooled at [Swanwick Hall Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swanwick_Hall_School) in [Swanwick, Derbyshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swanwick,_Derbyshire) and studied mathematics in which he earn a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) (1961) and [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts_%28Oxbridge_and_Dublin%29) (1965) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), as well as a [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (thesis *Some results in spectral synthesis* advised by John Hunter Williamson, 1965)[^o2] and [Sc.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science#The_Commonwealth_of_Nations_and_Ireland) (1983) from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o3]

He taught and conducted research at [University of Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_University) (1964), [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (1965–66), [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (1966–68), [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) (1969–73), [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) (1972–73), [University of Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull) (1973–86), [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta) (1985–2001), [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) (2001–2009) and [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide) (2009–2013). Most recently, he has been working at [University of South Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Australia) as a research professor.[^o2][^o4]
