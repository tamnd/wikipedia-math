# Edge-contracted icosahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edge-contracted icosahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **edge-contracted icosahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with [18](wikipedia://en/35942599) triangular [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 27 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 11 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It can be constructed from the regular [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), with one [edge contraction](wikipedia://en/3758115), removing one vertex, 3 edges, and 2 faces. This contraction distorts the [circumscribed sphere](wikipedia://en/991784) original vertices. With all [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285) faces, it has 2 sets of 3 coplanar equilateral triangles (each forming a half-[hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733)), and thus is not a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034).

If the sets of three coplanar triangles are considered a single face (called a [triamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triamond)[^o5]), it has 10 vertices, 22 edges, and 14 faces, 12 triangles and 2 triamonds.

It may also be described as having a hybrid [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29)-[pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) [antiprismatic](wikipedia://en/1851) core (an antiprismatic core with one square base and one pentagonal base); each base is then [augmented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29) with a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045).

:::

::: {#o7 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Dissected regular icosahedron"}

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The **dissected regular icosahedron** is a variant topologically equivalent to the [sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1142750) with the two sets of 3 coplanar faces as trapezoids. This is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of a 4D [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471), [grand antiprism](wikipedia://en/4058047#Vertex_figure). It has 10 vertices, 22 edges, and 12 equilateral triangular faces and 2 trapezoid faces.[^o8]

## In chemistry {#In_chemistry}

In [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), this polyhedron is most commonly called the [octadecahedron](wikipedia://en/35942599), for 18 triangular faces, and represents the [closo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closo)-boranate \[B11H11\](2-).
