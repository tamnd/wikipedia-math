# Discontinuous group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discontinuous group"}

A **discontinuous group** is a mathematical concept relating to mappings in [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $T$ be a topological space of points $\tau$, and let $\tau\to f(\tau,x)$, $x\in G$, be an open continuous representation of the [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) $G$ as a [transitive](wikipedia://en/200463) group of [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) mappings of $T$ on itself. The representation $\tau\to f(\tau,a)$ $a\in H$ of the discrete subgroup $H\subset G$ in $T$ is called discontinuous, if no sequence $f(\tau,a_n)$ ($n=1,2,\ldots$) converges to a point in $T$, as $a_n$ runs over distinct elements of $H$.[^o4]

:::
