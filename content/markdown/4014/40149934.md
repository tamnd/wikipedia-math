# Polykay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polykay"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **polykay**, or **generalised k-statistic**, (denoted $k_{r,s}$) is a statistic defined as a [linear combination](wikipedia://en/55632) of [sample moments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_moment).[^o2]

:::

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *polykay* was coined by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) mathematician [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974) in 1956, from *poly*, "many" or "much", and *kay*, the phonetic spelling of the letter "k", as in [k-statistic](wikipedia://en/40162275).[^o4]
