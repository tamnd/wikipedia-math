# Bi-twin chain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bi-twin chain"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **bi-twin chain** of length *k* + 1 is a sequence of natural numbers

:::

$$n-1,n+1,2n-1,2n+1, \dots, 2^k n - 1, 2^k n + 1 \,$$

in which every number is [prime](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o2]

The special case, when the four numbers $n-1,n+1,2n-1,2n+1$ are all primes, they are called **bi-twin primes**,[^o3] such *n* values are

6, 30, 660, 810, 2130, 2550, 3330, 3390, 5850, 6270, 10530, 33180, 41610, 44130, 53550, 55440, 57330, 63840, 65100, 70380, 70980, 72270, 74100, 74760, 78780, 80670, 81930, 87540, 93240, …

Except 6, all of these numbers are divisible by 30.

The numbers $n-1, 2n-1, \dots, 2^kn - 1$ form a [Cunningham chain](wikipedia://en/321790) of the first kind of length $k + 1$, while $n+1, 2n + 1, \dots, 2^kn + 1$ forms a Cunningham chain of the second kind. Each of the pairs $2^in - 1, 2^in+ 1$ is a pair of [twin primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_primes). Each of the primes $2^in - 1$ for $0 \le i \le k - 1$ is a [Sophie Germain prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Germain_prime) and each of the primes $2^in - 1$ for $1 \le i \le k$ is a [safe prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_prime).
