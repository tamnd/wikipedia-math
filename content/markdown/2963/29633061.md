# Kallman–Rota inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kallman–Rota inequality"}

In mathematics, the **Kallman–Rota inequality**, introduced by (Kallman Rota 1970), is a generalization of the [Landau–Kolmogorov inequality](wikipedia://en/8564970) to [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces). It states that if *A* is the [infinitesimal generator](wikipedia://en/17944) of a one-parameter [contraction](wikipedia://en/10907456) [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) then

:::

$$\|Af\|^2 \le 4\|f\|\|A^2f\|.$$
