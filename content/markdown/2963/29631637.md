# John Edwin Luecke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Edwin Luecke"}

**John Edwin Luecke** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). He got his Ph.D. in 1985 from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) and is now a professor in the department of mathematics at that institution.

:::

## Work {#Work}

Luecke specializes in knot theory and [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257). In a 1987 paper[^o4] Luecke, [Marc Culler](wikipedia://en/29258614), [Cameron Gordon](wikipedia://en/4695115), and [Peter Shalen](wikipedia://en/3520910) proved the [cyclic surgery theorem](wikipedia://en/29580519). In a 1989 paper[^o5] Luecke and Cameron Gordon proved that knots are determined by their complements, a result now known as the [Gordon–Luecke theorem](wikipedia://en/10208241).

Dr Luecke received a [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) [Presidential Young Investigator Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Young_Investigator_Award)[^o6][^o7] in 1992 and [Sloan Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Foundation) fellow[^o8] in 1994. In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
