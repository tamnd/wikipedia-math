# Linda Collins (psychologist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linda Collins"}

**Linda M. Collins** is an American quantitative psychologist who is a professor of global public health at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Collins earned a B.A. in psychology at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut).[^o3] She received a Ph.D. in [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) from the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).[^o3]

Collins was a tenured faculty member at the University of Southern California.[^o3] She was a distinguished professor of human development and family studies at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o3] At [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), she is a professor of global public health in the department of social and behavioral sciences.[^o3]

Collins is an elected fellow of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association), [Association for Psychological Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Psychological_Science), and the [Society of Behavioral Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Behavioral_Medicine).[^o3] She served as president of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) and the [Society for Prevention Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Prevention_Research).[^o4]

## Research and contributions {#Research_and_contributions}

Dr. Collins is known for her work on the Multiphase Optimization Strategy (MOST), which integrates concepts from engineering, behavioral science, multivariate statistics, health economics, and decision analysis.[^o6] MOST aims to enhance intervention effectiveness, affordability, scalability, and efficiency.[^o7] She has applied MOST in diverse areas, including HIV,[^o8] smoking cessation,[^o9] prevention of excessive alcohol use in college students,[^o10] and weight loss.[^o11]

Her research has received funding from institutions such as the [National Institute on Drug Abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_on_Drug_Abuse) (NIDA), the [National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_on_Alcohol_Abuse_and_Alcoholism) (NIAAA), the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) (NCI), the [National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Diabetes_and_Digestive_and_Kidney_Diseases) (NIDDK), and the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (NSF).[^o12][^o13]

Collins has published extensively in journals across behavioral sciences, quantitative methodology, medicine, and engineering.[^o14] She has given over 150 invited presentations on MOST worldwide.[^o3] Collaborating with Dr. Kate Guastaferro, she developed two massive open online courses on intervention optimization and MOST.[^o15]
