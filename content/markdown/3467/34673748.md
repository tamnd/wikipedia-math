# Annals of Probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annals of Probability"}

***Annals of Probability*** is a leading peer-reviewed probability [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), which is the main international society for researchers in the areas of probability and statistics. The journal was started in 1973 as a continuation in part of the *[Annals of Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/23772805)*, which was split into the *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)* and this journal.

:::

In July 2021,[^o3] the journal was ranked 7th in the field Probability & Statistics with Applications according to [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar).[^o4] It had an [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.470 (2010), according to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*.[^o5] The impact factor for 2018 is 2.085. Its [CiteScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CiteScore) is 4.3, and [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank) is 3.184, both from 2020.[^o6]

## Editors-in-chief: past and present {#Editors-in-chief:_past_and_present}

The following persons have been editor-in-chief of the journal:[^o8][^o9]

- Ronald Pyke (1972–1975)[^o10][^o11]
- [Patrick Billingsley](wikipedia://en/31735067) (1976–1978)[^o12]
- [Richard M. Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Mansfield_Dudley) (1979–1981)[^o13]
- [Thomas M. Liggett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Milton_Liggett) (1985–1987)[^o14]
- Peter E. Ney (1988–1990)
- Burgess Davis (1991–1993)[^o15]
- James W. Pitman (1994–1996)
- [Raghu Varadhan](wikipedia://en/9638038) (1997–1999)
- [Thomas Kurtz](wikipedia://en/51178615) (2000–2002)
- Steven Lalley (2003–2005)
- [Greg Lawler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lawler) (2006–2008)[^o16]
- [Ofer Zeitouni](wikipedia://en/56965332) (2009–2011)
- Krzysztof Burdzy (2012–2014)[^o17]
- [Maria Eulália Vares](wikipedia://en/55934667) (2015–2017)
- [Amir Dembo](wikipedia://en/60660200) (2018–2020)
- Christophe Garban & [Alice Guionnet](wikipedia://en/49986739) (2021–2023)[^o18]
