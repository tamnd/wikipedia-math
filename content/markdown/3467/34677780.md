# Murakami–Yano formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Murakami–Yano formula"}

In geometry, the **Murakami–Yano formula**, introduced by (Murakami Yano 2005), is a formula for the volume of a [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/241291) or spherical [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) given in terms of its [dihedral angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_angles).

:::
