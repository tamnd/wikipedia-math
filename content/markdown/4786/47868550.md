# Gerhard J. Woeginger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerhard J. Woeginger"}

*Image: GerhardWoeginger 2011 MFO14972.jpg.* Woeginger at the [Combinatorial Optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_Optimization) Workshop 2011 in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)

**Gerhard J. Woeginger** (31 May 1964 – 1 April 2022) was an Austrian mathematician and computer scientist who worked in Germany as a professor at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University), where he chaired the algorithms and complexity group in the department of computer science.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Woeginger was born on 31 May 1964 in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), Austria. He obtained a diploma from the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology) (TU Graz) in 1987,[^o6] and completed his Ph.D. at TU Graz 1991 under the supervision of Franz Rendl. He worked on the faculty of TU Graz from 1991 to 2001, where he completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1995. He then moved to the [University of Twente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Twente) from 2001 to 2004, to [TU Eindhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology),[^o6] from 2004 to 2016, and finally to [RWTH Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen) in 2016.

He was program chair of the [European Symposium on Algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Symposium_on_Algorithms) in 1997, of the algorithms track of the [International Colloquium on Automata, Languages and Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Colloquium_on_Automata,_Languages_and_Programming) in 2003, of the [European Conference on Operational Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Conference_on_Operational_Research) in 2009, and of several other conferences.

In 1996, Woeginger won the [Start-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-Preis), the highest Austrian award for scientists under the age of 35.[^o5] He won a [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 2011.[^o8] In 2014, he was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o6]

Until September 2016, Woeginger maintained a list of attempts to settle the [P versus NP problem](wikipedia://en/6115) on his website.[^o7][^o8] As of its last update, the list tallied 116 attempts dating back to 1986 to settle the question in various ways.

Woeginger died on 1 April 2022, at the age of 57.[^o9]
