# List of books on history of number systems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of books on history of number systems"}

This list compiles notable works that explore the history and development of number systems across various civilizations and time periods. These works cover topics ranging from ancient numeral systems and arithmetic methods to the evolution of mathematical notations and the impact of numerals on science, trade, and culture.

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

Number systems have been central to the development of human civilization, enabling record-keeping, commerce, astronomy, and scientific advancement. Early systems such as tally marks and Roman numerals gradually gave way to more abstract and efficient representations like the Babylonian base-60 system and the Hindu–Arabic numerals, now standard worldwide. The invention of zero, positional notation, and symbolic mathematics has had profound philosophical and technological implications.

## Historical texts {#Historical_texts}

::: {#o48 .table cue=table}

| Author | Title | Year | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331) | *[Elements](wikipedia://en/244107)* | c. 300 BCE | Laid foundations for [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) using [axiomatic methods](wikipedia://en/928). |
| [Aryabhata](wikipedia://en/297204) | *[Aryabhatiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryabhatiya)* | 499 CE | Described place-value numerals and concepts foundational to Indian mathematics. |
| [Brahmagupta](wikipedia://en/297206) | *[Brahmasphutasiddhanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta)* | 628 CE | Defined zero as a number and explained rules for its arithmetic. |
| [Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_ibn_Musa_al-Khwarizmi) | *[On the Calculation with Hindu Numerals](wikipedia://en/3393371)* | c. 825 CE | Early Arabic work spreading Hindu numeral use in the Islamic world. |
| [Fibonacci](wikipedia://en/17949) | *[Liber Abaci](wikipedia://en/167699)* | 1202 | Introduced Hindu–Arabic numerals and arithmetic to Europe. |
| [Ibn al-Banna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Banna) | *Talkhis amal al-hisab*[^o55] | 13th century | Arabic treatise integrating Indian and Greek arithmetic traditions. |
| [Nicolas Chuquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Chuquet) | *Triparty en la science des nombres*[^o57] | 1484 | One of the earliest European texts using exponents and large numbers. |
| [Boethius](wikipedia://en/195684) | *De Institutione Arithmetica*[^o59] | 6th century CE (pub. 1488) | Preserved Greek number theory in Latin during the early Middle Ages. |
| [Simon Stevin](wikipedia://en/72586) | *[De Thiende](wikipedia://en/36193619)* | 1585 | Promoted the use of decimal fractions in European mathematics. |
| [John Napier](wikipedia://en/15993) | *[Mirifici Logarithmorum Canonis Descriptio](wikipedia://en/26291704)* | 1614 | Introduced [logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860), aiding calculation with large numbers. |
| [Gottlob Frege](wikipedia://en/48416) | *[The Foundations of Arithmetic](wikipedia://en/8983001)* | 1884 | Philosophical treatise exploring the concept of number. |
| [Giuseppe Peano](wikipedia://en/13043) | *[Arithmetices principia, nova methodo exposita](wikipedia://en/51988929)* | 1889 | Introduced [Peano](wikipedia://en/13043)'s axioms for the natural numbers. |
| [Alfred North Whitehead](wikipedia://en/43395) and [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163) | *[Principia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/24133)* | 1910–1913 | Defined numbers using [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), constructing the natural numbers through [iteration](wikipedia://en/68833). |

:::
