# Logic Made Easy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Logic Made Easy"}

***Logic Made Easy: How to Know When Language Deceives You*** is a 2004 book by [Deborah J. Bennett](wikipedia://en/67026360) published by [W.W. Norton & Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.W._Norton_&_Company) (). Its theme is the analysis of what common words such as "some", "all", and "not" mean, and how logic relates to speech and writing. It discusses eliminating problems such as [ambiguity](wikipedia://en/677) and [imprecise language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imprecise_language) from [communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication), such as in [technical writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_writing).

:::
