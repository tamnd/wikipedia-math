# Jeffrey Weeks (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey Weeks"}

**Jeffrey Renwick Weeks** (born December 10, 1956) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a [geometric topologist](wikipedia://en/621732) and [cosmologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmologist). Weeks is a [1999 MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Weeks received his [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) in 1978, and his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1985, under the supervision of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190). Since then he has taught at [Stockton State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockton_State_College), [Ithaca College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca_College), and [Middlebury College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlebury_College), but has spent much of his time as a free-lance mathematician.[^o11]

## Research {#Research}

Weeks' research contributions have mainly been in the field of [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257) and [physical cosmology](wikipedia://en/5378).

The [Weeks manifold](wikipedia://en/4201216), discovered in 1985 by Weeks, is the [hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1237700) with the minimum possible volume. Weeks has written various computer programs to assist in mathematical research and [mathematical visualization](wikipedia://en/25948250). His [SnapPea](wikipedia://en/3025251) program is used to study [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700), while he has also developed interactive software to introduce these ideas to middle-school, high-school, and college students.

Weeks is particularly interested in using [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) to understand the spatial universe.[^o4] His book *The Shape of Space: How to Visualize Surfaces and Three-dimensional Manifolds* (Marcel Dekker, 1985, ) explores the geometry and topology of low-dimensional [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470).[^o5][^o6] The second edition (2002, ) explains some of his work in applying the material to cosmology.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Weeks became a [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Foundation) in 1999.[^o9] In 2007, he won the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) for his expository paper, "The Poincaré Dodecahedral Space and the Mystery of the Missing Fluctuations" (*Notices of the AMS* 2004),[^o10] and in 2008 he gave the first Levi Conant Lecture at the [Worcester Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Polytechnic_Institute),[^o11] where Conant was head of the Mathematics Department from 1908 until his untimely death.
