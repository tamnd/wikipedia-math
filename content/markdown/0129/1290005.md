# FISH (cipher)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FISH"}

The **FISH (FIbonacci SHrinking)** [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) is a fast [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) based stream cipher using [Lagged Fibonacci generators](wikipedia://en/45619), plus a concept from the [shrinking generator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrinking_generator) cipher. It was published by [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens) in 1993. FISH is quite fast in software and has a huge [key length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_length). However, in the same paper where he proposed [Pike](wikipedia://en/294128), [Ross Anderson](wikipedia://en/61571) showed that FISH can be broken with just a few thousand [bits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit) of known [plaintext](wikipedia://en/157935).

:::
