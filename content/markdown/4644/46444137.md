# Keti Tenenblat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Keti Tenenblat"}

**Keti Tenenblat** (born 27 November 1944) is a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people)-[Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), the applications of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) to [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), and [Finsler geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_geometry). Together with [Chuu-Lian Terng](wikipedia://en/12637839), she generalized Backlund theorem to higher dimensions.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Tenenblat was born on 27 November 1944 in [İzmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/İzmir), Turkey, where she attended elementary and junior high school at an Italian school. In 1957, her family emigrated to Brazil. In Rio de Janeiro, she graduated from high school at Bennett College and joined the National Faculty of Philosophy at the [University of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro) (today UFRJ), in the Mathematics Degree.[^o4]

From 1964 to 1968, she taught mathematics at a secondary school in Rio. She completed her university course in 1967 and began her higher education activities at the Institute of Mathematics of [UFRJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro) in 1968. Between 08/1968 to 07/1969, she attended a master's degree in mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), USA, while accompanying her husband who was study abroad. Upon returning to Brazil, she returned to teaching at UFRJ and began a doctoral program at [IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708). She defended her doctoral dissertation entitled "An estimate for the length of closed geodesics in Riemannian varieties" in 1972, under the direction of [Manfredo P. do Carmo](wikipedia://en/45041862).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

From 1973 she joined the faculty of the [University of Brasilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília) (UnB) where she became a Full Professor in 1989. From 1975 to 1978 she pursued a postdoctoral position at the Department of Mathematics at the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), Berkeley. During this period, she developed her research under the influence of [S. S. Chern](wikipedia://en/391905) and became interested in studying the interaction between [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).[^o4]

After 1978, her visits abroad were short-lived. She was a visiting professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), MSRI Berkeley, Institute of Theoretical Physics, Santa Barbara, IMA Minnesota, University of Montreal, McGill University, CRM Montreal, Nankai Institute and Fudan Univ. China.[^o4]

She is a recipient of [Brazil's National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit_%28Brazil%29) in Mathematics,[^o6] Emeritus Professor at the [University of Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília),[^o7] and was President of the [Brazilian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociedade_Brasileira_de_Matemática) in 1989–1991.[^o8][^o9] She has been a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1991.[^o4]

She is also the author of the books *Introdução à geometria diferencial* (1988), and *Transformações de superfícies e aplicações* (1981).[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1965, she married Moyses Tenenblat, an engineer graduated from the National School of Engineering. Children Dany (1970), Nitza (1973), Leo (1975) and grandchildren Gabriel (1995), Yuri (1998), Luisa (2000), Clara (2005), Milla (2007), Aylou (2009), and Luca (2014) were born of this marriage.[^o4]
