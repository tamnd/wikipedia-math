# Pertti Mattila

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Pertti Mattila"}

**Pertti Esko Juhani Mattila** (born 28 March 1948) is a Finnish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147).[^o4] He is Professor of Mathematics in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki), Finland.

:::

He is known for his work on [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694) and in particular applications to [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). His work include a counterexample to the general [Vitushkin's conjecture](wikipedia://en/727981#Vitushkin's_conjecture)[^o5] and with Mark Melnikov and Joan Verdera he introduced new techniques to understand the geometric structure of removable sets for complex analytic functions[^o6] which together with other works in the field eventually led to the solution of [Painlevé's problem](wikipedia://en/727981#Removable_sets_and_Painlevé's_problem) by Xavier Tolsa.[^o7][^o8]

His book *Geometry of Sets and Measures in Euclidean Spaces: Fractals and Rectifiability*[^o9] is now a widely cited[^o10] and a standard textbook in this field.[^o11] Mattila has been the leading figure on creating the geometric measure theory school in Finland and the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](wikipedia://en/1152126) cites he has supervised so far 15 PhD students in the field.

He obtained his PhD from the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) under the supervision of Jussi Väisälä in 1973. He worked at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at the [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) for postdoctoral research in 1979 and from 1989 as Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Jyväskylä](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jyväskylä),[^o2] until appointed as Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) in 2003.[^o12] In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o13] Mattila was the director of the [Academy of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Finland) funded Centre of Excellence of Geometric Analysis and Mathematical Physics from 2002 to 2007 and currently part of the Centre of Excellence in Analysis and Dynamics Research in the University of Helsinki.[^o8]
