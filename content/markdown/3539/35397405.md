# Matrix gamma distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matrix gamma distribution"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **matrix gamma distribution** is a generalization of the [gamma distribution](wikipedia://en/207079) to [positive-definite matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive-definite_matrix).[^o2] It is effectively a different parametrization of the [Wishart distribution](wikipedia://en/364996), and is used similarly, e.g. as the [conjugate prior](wikipedia://en/846412) of the [precision matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision_matrix) of a [multivariate normal distribution](wikipedia://en/50347) and [matrix normal distribution](wikipedia://en/437758). The [compound distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_distribution) resulting from compounding a matrix normal with a matrix gamma prior over the precision matrix is a [generalized matrix t-distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_matrix_t-distribution).[^o2]

:::

A matrix gamma distributions is identical to a [Wishart distribution](wikipedia://en/364996) with $\beta \boldsymbol\Sigma = 2 V, \alpha=\frac{n}{2}.$

Notice that the parameters $\beta$ and $\boldsymbol\Sigma$ are not identified; the density depends on these two parameters through the product $\beta\boldsymbol\Sigma$.
