# Random number book

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Random number book"}

*Image: Random\_digits.png.* Lines 10580–10594, columns 21–40, from *[A Million Random Digits with 100,000 Normal Deviates](wikipedia://en/1354812)*

A **random number book** is a [book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book) whose main content is a large number of [random numbers](wikipedia://en/41627) or random digits. These books were used in early [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [experimental design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_design), and were published by the [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rand_Corporation)[^o2] and others. The RAND Corporation book *[A Million Random Digits with 100,000 Normal Deviates](wikipedia://en/1354812)* was first published in 1955 and was reissued in 2001. A sequel, *A Million And One Random Digits* was published in 2022. Tables of random numbers have probably been used for multiple purposes at least since the Industrial Revolution. A table of random numbers was made by L.H.C. Tippett. [^o3]

:::

Random number books have been rendered obsolete for most purposes by the ready availability of [random number generators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_number_generator) running on [electronic computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_computer). However, they still have niche uses, particularly in the performance of [experimental music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_music) pieces that call for them, such as *Vision* (1959) and *Poem* (1960) by [La Monte Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Monte_Young).[^o4]
