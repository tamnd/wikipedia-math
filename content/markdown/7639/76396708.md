# Rowan Killip

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rowan Killip"}

**Rowan Killip** is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans)–[New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealanders) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) whose work focuses on [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), particularly [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). He won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2004[^o4] and a Simons Fellowship in Mathematics in 2015.[^o5] In 2023, he won, along with [Monica Vișan](wikipedia://en/58551830), the Frontiers of Science Award at the International Congress for Basic Science in Beijing, China for proving the global well-posedness of the [Korteweg–De Vries equation](wikipedia://en/344116) in the [Sobolev space](wikipedia://en/611964) [H<sup>−1</sup>](wikipedia://en/611964#Sobolev_spaces_with_integer_k).[^o6][^o7]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Killip was an undergraduate at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).[^o9] He completed his Ph.D. at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 2000. His doctoral advisor was [Barry Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Simon); his doctoral thesis was titled *Perturbations of One-Dimensional Schrödinger Operators Preserving the Absolutely Continuous Spectrum*.[^o10]

## Career {#Career}

Following his doctoral studies, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179),[^o12] and the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) before returning to [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) again.[^o13] He joined the faculty at UCLA as an assistant professor in 2003, becoming full professor in 2009.[^o14]
