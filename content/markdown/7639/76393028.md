# Gareth W. Peters

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gareth W. Peters"}

**Gareth W. Peters** is an Australian endowed chair [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373) at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara)[^o3] and an [honorary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_title_%28academic%29) professor of statistics at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London).[^o4] As at 2024, he is also a member of the international advisory board of the [Institute of Statistical Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4744135).[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Peters has both a [Bachelor of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Engineering) and [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) with first class honors from the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne),[^o7] a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Mathematics and Statistics from the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Before joining the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), Peters held academic positions at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University),[^o10] [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) and [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales).[^o7] He has published over 150 peer-reviewed articles on risk and insurance modelling and 2 research text books on Operational Risk and Insurance. He has also been the editor and contributor to 3 edited text books on Monte Carlo methods and spatial statistics.[^o11]

## Research work {#Research_work}

[Gareth W. Peters](wikipedia://en/76393028) is a researcher primarily in time series modelling and econometrics, quantitative risk, actuarial science and insurance. Peters' research shows that [mortality rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortality_rates) have [heteroscedastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heteroscedastic) behaviour and incorporating heteroscedasticity and [stochastic volatility](wikipedia://en/5740025) in the estimation of [life tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_tables) markedly improves model fit despite an increase of model complexity.[^o13] This is consistent with the [cohort effects](wikipedia://en/5947372)[^o14] widely documented[^o15] in mortality observations.

His research work has been cited by the [Swiss Association of Actuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Association_of_Actuaries)[^o16] and informed [central bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_bank) senior delegates around the world.[^o17]
