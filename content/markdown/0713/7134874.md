# Fondements de la Géometrie Algébrique

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fondements de la Géometrie Algébrique"}

***Fondements de la Géometrie Algébrique*** (***FGA***) is a book that collected together seminar notes of [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042). It is an important source for his pioneering work on [scheme theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_theory), which laid foundations for [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) in its modern technical developments. The title is a translation of the title of [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019)'s book *[Foundations of Algebraic Geometry](wikipedia://en/26402563).* It contained material on [descent theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descent_theory), and existence theorems including that for the [Hilbert scheme](wikipedia://en/6612581). The *Technique de descente et théorèmes d'existence en géometrie algébrique* is one series of seminars within *FGA*.

:::

Like the bulk of Grothendieck's work of the [IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHÉS) period, duplicated notes were circulated, but the publication was not as a conventional book.
