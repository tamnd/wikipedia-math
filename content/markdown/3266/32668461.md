# Susanna S. Epp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susanna S. Epp"}

**Susanna Samuels Epp** (born 1943)[^o4] is an [author](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Author), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor). Her interests include [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [cognitive psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_psychology), and [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), and she has written numerous articles, publications, and textbooks. She is currently professor emerita at [DePaul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DePaul_University), where she chaired the Department of Mathematical Sciences and was Vincent de Paul Professor in Mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Epp holds degrees in mathematics from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where she completed her doctorate in 1968 under the supervision of [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky). She taught at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) and at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) before becoming a professor at DePaul University.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2005, she received the [Louise Hay Award](wikipedia://en/51270001) from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in recognition for her contributions to mathematics education.[^o8]

Her textbook *Discrete Mathematics with Applications* received a 2005 Textbook Excellence Award from the [Textbook and Academic Authors Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook_and_Academic_Authors_Association).[^o9]
