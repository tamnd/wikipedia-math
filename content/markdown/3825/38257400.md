# Truncated order-6 square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-6 square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-6 square tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{4,6}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_order-6\_square\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated order-6 square tiling with \*443 symmetry mirror lines

The dual tiling represents the fundamental domains of the \*443 orbifold symmetry. There are two reflective subgroup kaleidoscopic constructed from \[(4,4,3)\] by removing one or two of three mirrors. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and cyan, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors.

A larger subgroup is constructed \[(4,4,3\*)\], index 6, as (3\*22) with gyration points removed, becomes (\*222222).

The symmetry can be doubled as [642 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/642_symmetry) by adding a mirror bisecting the fundamental domain.
