# Snub tetrahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub tetrahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub tetrahexagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{6,4}.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

The *snub tetrahexagonal tiling* is fifth in a series of snub polyhedra and tilings with [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) 3.3.4.3.*n*.
