# Truncated order-4 hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-4 hexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-4 hexagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{6,4}. A secondary construction tr{6,6} is called a **truncated hexahexagonal tiling** with two colors of [dodecagons](wikipedia://en/508486).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are two uniform constructions of this tiling, first from \[6,4\] [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope), and a lower symmetry by removing the last mirror, \[6,4,1<sup>\+</sup>\], gives \[6,6\], (\*662).

### Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_order-4\_hexagonal\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated order-4 hexagonal tiling with \*662 mirror lines

The dual of the tiling represents the fundamental domains of (\*662) [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) symmetry. From \[6,6\] (\*662) symmetry, there are 15 small index subgroup (12 unique) by mirror removal and [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) operators. Mirrors can be removed if its branch orders are all even, and cuts neighboring branch orders in half. Removing two mirrors leaves a half-order gyration point where the removed mirrors met. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors. The [subgroup index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgroup_index)-8 group, \[1<sup>\+</sup>,6,1<sup>\+</sup>,6,1<sup>\+</sup>\] (3333) is the [commutator subgroup](wikipedia://en/8847) of \[6,6\].

Larger subgroup constructed as \[6,6<sup>\*</sup>\], removing the gyration points of (6\*3), index 12 becomes (\*333333).

The symmetry can be doubled to [642 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/642_symmetry) by adding a mirror to bisect the fundamental domain.
