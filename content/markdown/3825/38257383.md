# Tetrahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tetrahexagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) r{6,4}.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are for uniform constructions of this tiling, three of them as constructed by mirror removal from the \[6,4\] [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope). Removing the last mirror, \[6,4,1<sup>\+</sup>\], gives \[6,6\], (\*662). Removing the first mirror \[1<sup>\+</sup>,6,4\], gives \[(4,4,3)\], (\*443). Removing both mirror as \[1<sup>\+</sup>,6,4,1<sup>\+</sup>\], leaving \[(3,∞,3,∞)\] (\*3232).

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual tiling, called a **rhombic tetrahexagonal tiling**, with [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V4.6.4.6, and represents the fundamental domains of a quadrilateral kaleidoscope, [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) (\*3232), shown here in two different centered views. Adding a 2-fold rotation point in the center of each rhombi represents a (2\*32) orbifold.
