# Jaqueline Mesquita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jaqueline Mesquita"}

*Image: Jaqueline Mesquita at Oberwolfach, 2018.jpg.* At the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669), 2018

**Jaqueline Godoy Mesquita** (born 1985) is a Brazilian mathematician specializing in [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [functional differential equations](wikipedia://en/52489082). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mesquita was born on 20 September 1985. After graduating from the University of Brasília in 2007, she earned a Ph.D. at the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) in 2012, with the dissertation *Measure functional differential equations and impulsive functional dynamic equations on time scales*, jointly supervised by Marcia Cristina Anderson Braz Federson and Antonín Slavík, based in part on work as a visiting student at the [Czech Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Academy_of_Sciences).

After postdoctoral study at the University of São Paulo and the [University of Santiago, Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago,_Chile), she became a professor at the University of Brasília in 2015.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

The International Society of Differential Equations gave Mesquita their Bernd Aulbach student prize in 2012.

Mesquita became an affiliate member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2018. She is also a young affiliate of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences). She was a 2019 winner of the Brazilian [L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards).

She is the current president of the [Brazilian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Mathematical_Society).
