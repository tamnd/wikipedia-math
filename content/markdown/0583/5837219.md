# William Revelle

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="William Revelle"}

**William Roger Revelle** (born c. 1944) is a [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) working in [personality psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personality_psychology). Revelle studies the biological basis of personality and [motivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motivation), [psychometric](wikipedia://en/24982) theory, the structure of daily [mood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mood_%28psychology%29), and models of [attention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention) and [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Revelle was raised in La Jolla, California.[^o6] His father, [Roger Revelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Revelle), was an early theorist in [global warming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_warming).[^o5]

Revelle graduated from Pomona College in 1965, abandoning a mathematics major in favor of psychology. He spent two years in [Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarawak), Malaysia, as a volunteer in the [Peace Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Corps) before earning his PhD in psychology from the University of Michigan in 1973.[^o6] He became a member of the Northwestern Faculty in 1973.

## Career {#Career}

Revelle has previously served as the President (2005–2009) of the International Society for the Study of Individual Differences (ISSID),[^o8] the President (2008–2009) of the [Association for Research in Personality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Research_in_Personality) (ARP), and the President (1984) of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) (SMEP).[^o9]

Currently, he is vice-chair of the Governing Board of the *[Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_of_the_Atomic_Scientists)*, having previously served as Chair (2009–2012).[^o10] He also serves as the President (2018–present) of the [International Society for Intelligence Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Intelligence_Research) (ISIR).[^o11]

Additionally, he is a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (AAAS; 1996–present),[^o12] the [Association for Psychological Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Psychological_Science) (APS; 1994–present),[^o13] the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association) (APA Division 5; 2011–present),[^o14] and the [Society for Personality and Social Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Personality_and_Social_Psychology) (SPSP; 2015–present).[^o15]

He resides in Evanston, Illinois.[^o6]
