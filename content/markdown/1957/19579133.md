# Clausen's formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clausen's formula"}

In mathematics, **Clausen's formula**, found by (txt), expresses the square of a [Gaussian hypergeometric series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_hypergeometric_series) as a [generalized hypergeometric series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_hypergeometric_series). It states

:::

$$\;_{2}F_1 \left[\begin{matrix} a & b \\ a+b+1/2 \end{matrix} ; x \right]^2 = \;_{3}F_2 \left[\begin{matrix} 2a & 2b &a+b \\ a+b+1/2 &2a+2b \end{matrix} ; x \right]$$

In particular, it gives conditions for a hypergeometric series to be positive. This can be used to prove several inequalities, such as the [Askey–Gasper inequality](wikipedia://en/19291343) used in the proof of [de Branges's theorem](wikipedia://en/252075).
