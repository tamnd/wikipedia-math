# Weakly symmetric space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weakly symmetric space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **weakly symmetric space** is a notion introduced by the Norwegian mathematician [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021) in the 1950s as a generalisation of [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877), due to [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274). Geometrically the spaces are defined as complete [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652) such that any two points can be exchanged by an [isometry](wikipedia://en/254777), the symmetric case being when the isometry is required to have period two. The classification of weakly symmetric spaces relies on that of periodic automorphisms of complex [semisimple Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/1337587). They provide examples of [Gelfand pairs](wikipedia://en/3018619), although the corresponding theory of [spherical functions](wikipedia://en/17708915) in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), known for symmetric spaces, has not yet been developed.

:::
