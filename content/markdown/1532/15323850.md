# Hsiao-Lan Kuo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hsiao-Lan Kuo"}

[Kuo (Guo)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guo_%28surname%29)

**Hsiao-Lan Kuo** (**Guo Xiaolan**; 1915–2006) was a [Chinese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [meteorologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorologist). He was a recipient of the [Carl-Gustaf Rossby Research Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl-Gustaf_Rossby_Research_Medal).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Born in [Mancheng County, Hebei Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mancheng_County,_Hebei_Province) on February 7, 1915, Kuo obtained his B.Sc. from [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) (1937), a M.Sc. from [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) (1942, under advisor [Coching Chu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coching_Chu)), and a Ph.D. from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (1948, advisor [Carl-Gustaf Rossby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl-Gustaf_Rossby)).

From 1949 to 1961, Kuo worked as a research associate, later a senior specialist, and finally the project director on the Hurricane Project at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT). He was Professor Emeritus at the Department of Geophysical Sciences at the University of Chicago.

Kuo was an academician of [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) (1988 election).

## Works {#Works}

Kuo developed important mathematical tools to describe the complex circulation patterns of atmospheric activity. He helped to mathematically model the birth of a [tropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone) (hurricane). Kuo's work is an important part of the theoretical foundation for modern meteorology.[^o5] He is considered as a member of the *Chicago School of Meteorology*, which started from Carl-Gustaf Rossby.[^o6]

For this reason, in 1970, Kuo was awarded the [Carl-Gustaf Rossby Research Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl-Gustaf_Rossby_Research_Medal) from the [American Meteorological Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Meteorological_Society) (AMS), which is the highest honor for atmospheric science.[^o7]

The *Rayleigh-Kuo theorem* or *Rayleigh-Kuo Criterion*, a necessary condition for barotropically unstable fluid, is named after him.
