# C-group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C-group"}

In mathematical [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **C-group** is a group such that the [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) of any [involution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involution_%28group_theory%29) has a normal [Sylow 2-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_2-subgroup). They include as special cases **CIT-groups** where the centralizer of any involution is a 2-group, and **TI-groups** where any Sylow 2-subgroups have trivial intersection.

:::

The simple C-groups were determined by (Suzuki 1965), and his classification is summarized by (Gorenstein 1980, 16.4). The classification of C-groups was used in Thompson's classification of [N-groups](wikipedia://en/29504243). The finite non-abelian simple C-groups are

- the projective special linear groups PSL<sub>2</sub>(*p*) for *p* a Fermat or Mersenne prime, and *p*≥5
- the projective special linear groups PSL<sub>2</sub>(9)
- the projective special linear groups PSL<sub>2</sub>(2<sup>*n*</sup>) for *n*≥2
- the projective special linear groups PSL<sub>3</sub>(2<sup>*n*</sup>) for *n*≥1
- the projective special unitary groups PSU<sub>3</sub>(2<sup>*n*</sup>) for *n*≥2
- the [Suzuki groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki-Ree_group) Sz(2<sup>*2n+1*</sup>) for *n*≥1

## CIT-groups {#CIT-groups}

The C-groups include as special cases the CIT-groups, that are groups in which the centralizer of any involution is a 2-group. These were classified by (txt), and the finite non-abelian simple ones consist of the finite non-abelian simple C-groups other than PSL<sub>3</sub>(2<sup>*n*</sup>) and PSU<sub>3</sub>(2<sup>*n*</sup>) for *n*≥2. The ones whose Sylow 2-subgroups are elementary abelian were classified in a paper of (Burnside 1899), which was forgotten for many years until rediscovered by Feit in 1970.

## TI-groups {#TI-groups}

The C-groups include as special cases the TI-groups (trivial intersection groups), that are groups in which any two Sylow 2-subgroups have trivial intersection. These were classified by (txt), and the simple ones are of the form PSL<sub>2</sub>(*q*), PSU<sub>3</sub>(*q*), Sz(*q*) for *q* a power of 2.
