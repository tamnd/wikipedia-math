# Strongly embedded subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Strongly embedded subgroup"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/323707), an area of [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **strongly embedded subgroup** of a finite group *G* is a [proper subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_subgroup) *H* of even order such that *H* ∩ *H*<sup>*g*</sup> has odd order whenever *g* is not in *H*. The **Bender–Suzuki theorem**, proved by (Bender 1971) extending work of (txt), classifies the groups *G* with a strongly embedded subgroup *H*. It states that either

:::

1. *G* has cyclic or [generalized quaternion Sylow 2-subgroups](wikipedia://en/153130#Generalized_quaternion_group) and *H* contains the [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) of an [involution](wikipedia://en/293450)
1. or *G*/*O*(*G*) has a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of odd index isomorphic to one of the [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600) PSL<sub>2</sub>(*q*), Sz(*q*) or PSU<sub>3</sub>(*q*) where *q*≥4 is a power of 2 and *H* is *O*(*G*)N<sub>*G*</sub>(*S*) for some Sylow 2-subgroup *S*.

(Peterfalvi 2000, part II) revised Suzuki's part of the proof.

(Aschbacher 1974) extended Bender's classification to groups with a proper 2-generated core.
