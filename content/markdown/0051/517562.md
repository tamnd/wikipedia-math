# Bondy

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Bondy"}

**Bondy** () is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the northeastern suburbs of Paris, France. It is located \10.9 km from the [centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre_zero) of Paris, in the [Seine-Saint-Denis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine-Saint-Denis) [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_France).

:::

## Name {#Name}

The name Bondy was recorded for the first time around AD 600 as *Bonitiacum*, meaning "estate of Bonitius", a [Gallo-Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallo-Roman) landowner.

## History {#History}

*Image: Forêt de Bondy carte de Cassini2.jpg.* Bondy, c. 1780

*Image: Hôtel Ville Bondy 2.jpg.* The [Hôtel de Ville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hôtel_de_Ville,_Bondy)

During the [Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages), Bondy was mostly forest, and the forest of Bondy was a well-known haunt of bandits and robbers and was considered extremely dangerous.

On January 3, 1905, a third of the territory of Bondy was detached and became the commune of [Les Pavillons-sous-Bois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Pavillons-sous-Bois).

The current [Hôtel de Ville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hôtel_de_Ville,_Bondy) was completed in 1970.[^o6]

On October 30, 2007, a gas explosion killed one person and injured 46 people.

Bondy and its integration into Paris is the subject of part of the second-last chapter of [Graham Robb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_Robb)'s book *[Parisians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parisians_%28book%29)*.

## Administration {#Administration}

Bondy is part of the [canton of Bondy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Bondy), created in 2015.

## Transport {#Transport}

Bondy is served by [Bondy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondy_%28Paris_RER%29) station on Paris [RER line E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RER_E) and the [Line 4 (T4) of the Tramways in Île-de-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Île-de-France_tramway_Line_4).

## Education {#Education}

, the commune had 27 state-funded primary schools, with 6,900 students. There were also three publicly funded [lycées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée), or senior high schools, and five junior high schools.[^o10]

- There are 13 *écoles maternelles*, or preschools,[^o11] and 14 publicly funded elementary schools[^o12]
- The junior high schools are named after: Pierre Brossolette, Henri Sellier, Jean Zay, Jean Renoir, and Pierre Curie[^o13]
- There are three state-funded high schools: Lycée Léo-Lagrange, Lycée Marcel-Pagnol, and Lycée Jean-Renoir.[^o14]

Bondy also has a private Roman Catholic high school, Institut privé de l'Assomption, which has its own elementary school.[^o14]
