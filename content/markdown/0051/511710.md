# Exotic probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Exotic probability"}

**Exotic probability** is a branch of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) that deals with probabilities which are outside the normal range of \[0, 1\].

:::

According to the author of various papers on exotic probability, [Saul Youssef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_Youssef), the valid possible alternatives for probability values are the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438), the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) and the [quaternions](wikipedia://en/51440).[^o2] Youssef also cites the work of [Richard Feynman](wikipedia://en/25523), [P. A. M. Dirac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._A._M._Dirac), [Stanley Gudder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Gudder) and [S. K. Srinivasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._K._Srinivasan) as relevant to exotic probability theories.

Of the application of such theories to [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), [Bill Jefferys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Jefferys) has said: "Such approaches are also not necessary and in my opinion they confuse more than they illuminate."[^o3]
