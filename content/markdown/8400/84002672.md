# Margaret Betts (Bombe operator)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Betts"}

**Margaret Betts** (née **Booth**; 27 December 1923 - 26 August 2023) was a [Bombe](wikipedia://en/615354) operator at Bletchley Park during World War II.

:::

*Image: Bletchley Park Mansion.jpg.* Bletchley Park Mansion

*Image: TuringBombeBletchleyPark.jpg.* A mockup of a [bombe](wikipedia://en/615354) at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037), as operated by Betts during World War II

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Margaret Betts was born on December 27 1923 in the port town of [Ipswich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich) in Suffolk, England. Her parents were Daniel and Dorothy (née Cubitt) Booth. She and her two brothers Patrick and John won scholarships and attended [Ipswich High School, Suffolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_High_School,_Suffolk).[^o4]

## Codebreaking {#Codebreaking}

In 1942 her older and newly married brother Patrick was reported missing and presumed dead after his ship [MV Abosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Abosso) was sunk by a German U-boat. She was determined to become involved in the war and at the age of 19 enlisted in the [Women's Royal Naval Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Royal_Naval_Service) (WRNS, known as the Wrens).[^o6]

After a selection process she started codebreaking in the summer of 1943. Originally assigned to [Eastcote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastcote) and [Gayhurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayhurst) she became a [Bombe](wikipedia://en/615354) operator at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) starting in mid 1945. On VE Day she was asked to stay on and work on Japanese codes until VJ Day.[^o7]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1947 she married Antony Sneezum. After marriage they changed their name to Betts. Antony had been a prisoner of war and survived forced labor by the Japanese on the [Burma Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma_Railway) (called the Death Railway). He died in 1994.[^o6]
