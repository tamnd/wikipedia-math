# Marcin Król of Żurawica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcin Król of Żurawica"}

**Marcin Król z Żurawicy** (Marcin Król z Żurawicy; c. 1422–1460), also known as **Martinus Ruthenus**, **Marcin of Żurawica** (Marcin z Żurawicy) **Marcin Król of Przemyśl** (Marcin Król z Przemyśla; Martinus Rex de Premislia), and **Martinus Polonus**, was a [Ruthenian-born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), and [doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physician).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Marcin Król, son of Stanisław Król, was born around 1422 in Żurawica near [Przemyśl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przemyśl).

He joined the [Cracow Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) to study the liberal arts in 1438. Six years later, he earned his *baccalarius*. In 1445 he gained the title *magister* and began to lecture on mathematics and astronomy. That same year, [Zbigniew Oleśnicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbigniew_Oleśnicki_%28cardinal%29), the academy's [chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_%28education%29), arranged for him to study abroad. Król attended the universities in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) (1445), [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University), [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) and Bologna, where he lectured on astronomy in 1448. In December 1449, he earned the title doctor of medicine. Shortly thereafter, Król left for Hungary, where he was the court physician to [John Hunyadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hunyadi). He probably also spent time at the court of [John Vitéz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Vitéz) w [Oradea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oradea).

Król returned to Cracow probably in the winter of 1450 through the intervention of Zbigniew Oleśnicki and [Jan Długosz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Długosz), and took up a position as professor at the Cracow Academy. He covered mathematics (geometry) and astronomy, founding the latter department. Sometime after 1450, he also established the department of [astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrology), which soon developed a European-wide reputation. He kept up close correspondence with other astronomers, including the Austrian [Georg von Peuerbach](wikipedia://en/172394) and the German [Regiomontanus](wikipedia://en/25929). In this period Król also served as doctor to the [bishop of Cracow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop_of_Kraków).

At his death in 1460, his substantial fortune went, in large part, to the University of Cracow, as was his wish. A small sum also went to his brother Florian, a peasant settled in the family village of Żurawica.

- *Nova compilatio algorismi minutiarum*.
- *Algorismus minuciarum*. 1445.[^o4]
- *Summa super Tabulas Alphonsi vel Correctiones Tabularum Alphonsi*. 1450–1451(?). Treatise correcting the astronomical tables of 1251.[^o5]
- *Iudicia*. Astrological almanacs for 1451 and 1452.[^o6]
- *Opus de geometria*. c.1450.[^o7]
- *Canones super calendarium*. 1456.[^o8]
