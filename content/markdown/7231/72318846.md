# George Roussas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Roussas"}

**George Gregory Roussas** (born June 29, 1933) is a Greek-American professor emeritus in statistics at [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis). He is noted for his contributions in [asymptotic statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_statistics) and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Roussas was born in the central Greece region of [Phthiotis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phthiotis). He studied mathematics at [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) in Greece and received his BSc in 1956. Afterwards, he moved to the US, studying for a PhD in statistics at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the supervision of [David Blackwell](wikipedia://en/2202900) and [Lucien Le Cam](wikipedia://en/2629104). He received his PhD from Berkeley in 1964.[^o4]

Roussas started his academic career in the statistics faculty at [San Jose State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_State_University) in 1964 as an assistant professor. He then moved to [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1966, where he progressed from an assistant professor to a full professor in 1972. During this time, he also took up administrative position at [University of Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Patras) and [University of Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Crete) in his home country, Greece. Roussas moved to [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) in 1985 and took up the chair in the Department of Statistics. He became distinguished Professor at University of California, Davis in 2003 and retired there in 2012.[^o5]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Roussas became a Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1975, a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1983, a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1986, and a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 2010.
