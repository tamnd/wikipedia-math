# Alexey Pokrovskiy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexey Pokrovskiy"}

**Alexey Pokrovskiy** is a mathematician specialising in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is an associate professor in the Department of Mathematics at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London).[^o3][^o4] He is known for his work in [extremal](wikipedia://en/2675917) and [probabilistic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), including a proof of [Ringel’s conjecture](wikipedia://en/8978968) with [Richard Montgomery](wikipedia://en/83003164) and [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803) for large graphs, as covered by [Quanta magazine.](wikipedia://en/51804976)[^o5] In 2019, he and Montgomery received the [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics).[^o6][^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pokrovskiy studied mathematics at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), receiving a BA in 2008 and an MMath with distinction in 2009. He completed a PhD in mathematics at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) in 2013 under the supervision of Jan van den Heuvel and Jozef Skokan.[^o9]

Pokrovskiy was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) from 2013 to 2015 and at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 2015 to 2018. He was then a lecturer at [Birkbeck, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkbeck,_University_of_London) from 2018 to 2020, before joining [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) in 2020.[^o10][^o11]

## Research {#Research}

Pokrovskiy's work is in discrete mathematics, especially extremal and probabilistic combinatorics. His notable results include an approximate version of the [Aharoni](wikipedia://en/22744244)-Berger conjecture and his work with Matija Bucić, Matthew Kwan and [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803) on Rota's basis conjecture.[^n1][^n2] His other significant works include a proof with Richard Montgomery and Benny Sudakov of [Ringel's conjecture for large graphs](wikipedia://en/8978968) and his work with Alp Müyesser giving a random version of the Hall–Paige conjecture.[^n3][^n4]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 2019, Pokrovskiy shared the [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics) with Richard Montgomery. The prize announcement cited their "deep contributions to extremal and probabilistic combinatorics".[^o18]
