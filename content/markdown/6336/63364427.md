# Euclid Speidell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euclid Speidell"}

**Euclid Speidell** (died 1702) was an English customs official and mathematics teacher known for his writing on logarithms. Speidell published revised and expanded versions of texts by his father, [John Speidell](wikipedia://en/45709472).[^o2] He also published a book called *Logarithmotechnia, or, The making of numbers called logarithms to twenty five places from a geometrical figure* in 1688.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Speidell lived in Angel Alley in the 1680s and 1690s, according to the [Survey of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survey_of_London).[^o5]

Speidell's name appears on an instrument made by his contemporary [Henry Sutton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Sutton_%28instrument_maker%29).[^o6]
