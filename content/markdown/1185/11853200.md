# Raman Parimala

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raman Parimala"}

**Raman Parimala** (born 21 November 1948)[^o3] is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for her contributions to [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). She is the Arts & Sciences Distinguished Professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University).[^o4] For many years, she was a professor at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai).

:::

She was on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) 2019—2022[^o5] and was on the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) selection Committee 2021–2023.[^o6]

## Background {#Background}

Parimala was born and raised in [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu), India.[^o8] She studied in Saradha Vidyalaya Girls' High School and Stella Maris College at [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai). She received her B. Sc. in 1968 and her M.Sc. in 1970 from [Madras University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_University). In 1976, Parimala earned her Ph.D. from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai); her advisor was [R. Sridharan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Sridharan) from TIFR.[^o9]

In 1987, she won the highest science award in India: The [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology).[^o10]

She is a fellow of the Indian National Science Academy (New Delhi).[^o10]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- 2021: "Local triviality for G-torsors", Gille, P.; Parimala, R.; Suresh, V., Math. Ann. 380, no. 1-2, 539–567.
- 2018: "Local-global principle for reduced norms over function fields of p-adic curves", Parimala, R.; Preeti, R.; Suresh, V., Compos. Math. 154, no. 2, 410–458.
- 2014: "Period-index and u-invariant questions for function fields over complete discretely valued fields", Parimala, R.; Suresh, V., Invent. Math. 197, no. 1, 215–235.
- 2001: "Hermitian analogue of a theorem of Springer", R Parimala, [R. Sridharan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Sridharan), V Suresh - Journal of Algebra, - Elsevier
- 1998: "Classical groups and the Hasse principle", [E Bayer-Fluckiger](wikipedia://en/28654438), R Parimala - Annals of Mathematics, jstor.org[^o12]
- 1995: "Galois cohomology of the classical groups over fields of cohomological dimension ≤2", Bayer-Fluckiger, E.; Parimala, R., Invent. Math. 122, no. 2, 195–229.
- 1990: "Real components of algebraic varieties and étale cohomology", [Colliot-Thélène, J.-L.](wikipedia://en/15256996); Parimala, R., Invent. Math. 101, no. 1, 81–99.
- 1982: "Quadratic spaces over polynomial extensions of regular rings of dimension 2", Mathematische Annalen, vol. 261, pp. 287–292
- 1976: "Failure of a quadratic analogue of Serre's conjecture", Bulletin of the AMS, vol. 82, pp. 962–964, Colliot-Thélène, J.-L.; Parimala, R., Invent. Math. 101, no. 1, 81–99.

## Honors {#Honors}

On [National Science Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Day) in 2020, [Smriti Irani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smriti_Irani), head of the Ministry of Women and Child Development of the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India), announced the establishment of chairs at institutes across India in the names of Raman Parimala and other ten Indian women scientists.[^o14] Parimala was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich) in 1994 and gave a talk [*Study of quadratic forms — some connections with geometry*](http://www.mathunion.org/ICM/ICM1994.1/Main/icm1994.1.0324.0332.ocr.pdf) (3 October 2016). <https://web.archive.org/web/20161003181416/http://www.mathunion.org/ICM/ICM1994.1/Main/icm1994.1.0324.0332.ocr.pdf>. She gave a plenary address *Arithmetic of linear algebraic groups over two dimensional fields* at the Congress in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_%28India%29) in 2010.

- Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o3]
- Fellow of [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy)[^o3]
- [Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Award) in 1987[^o3]
- Honorary Membership of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2024[^o15]
- Honorary doctorate from the [University of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lausanne) in 1999[^o3]
- [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) Birth Centenary Award in 2003.[^o3]
- [TWAS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS_Prize) for Mathematics (2005).[^o3][^o16]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (2012)[^o17]
