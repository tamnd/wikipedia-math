# Andreas Blass

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Blass"}

**Andreas Raphael Blass** (born October 27, 1947) is a mathematician, currently a professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He works in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), particularly [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).

:::

Blass graduated from the [University of Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Detroit), where he was a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) in 1965,[^o3] in 1966 with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1970 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), with a thesis on *Orderings of Ultrafilters* written under the supervision of Frank Wattenberg. Since 1970 he has been employed by the University of Michigan, first as a *T.H. Hildebrandt Research Instructor* (1970–72), then assistant professor (1972–76), associate professor (1976–84) and since 1984 he has been a full professor there.

In 2014, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Selected publications and results {#Selected_publications_and_results}

In 1984 Blass proved that the existence of a [basis](wikipedia://en/18420) for every [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) is equivalent to the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840). He made important contributions in the development of the [set theory of the reals](wikipedia://en/4937732) and [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205).

Blass was the first to point out connections between [game semantics](wikipedia://en/617121) and [linear logic](wikipedia://en/579675).

He has authored more than 200 research articles in mathematical logic and theoretical computer science, including:

- Blass, Andreas. (1984). "Axiomatic set theory". American Mathematical Society. p. 31–33. isbn:0-8218-5026-1. mr:763890
- Blass, Andreas; Shelah, Saharon. (1987). "There may be simple P\_{\\aleph\_1}- and P\_{\\aleph\_2}-points and the Rudin–Keisler ordering may be downward directed". *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic*. 33. p. 213–243. doi:10.1016/0168-0072(87)90082-0
- Blass, Andreas. (1992). "A game semantics for linear logic". *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic*. 56 (1–3). p. 183–220. doi:10.1016/0168-0072(92)90073-9
- Blass, Andreas; Gurevich, Yuri. (2003). "Algorithms: a quest for absolute definitions". *Bull. Eur. Assoc. Theor. Comput. Sci. EATCS*. 81. p. 195–225. <http://research.microsoft.com/~gurevich/Opera/164.pdf>
