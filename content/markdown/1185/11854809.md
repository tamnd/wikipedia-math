# Władysław Zajączkowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Władysław Zajączkowski"}

**Władysław Zajączkowski** (April 12, 1837, in [Strzyżów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strzyżów) near the [Rzeszów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rzeszów) – October 7, 1898, in [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów)) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Professor of [Warsaw Main School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Main_School), Imperial University of Warsaw (now [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw)), Technical Academy in Lviv (now [Lviv Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_Polytechnic); twice a [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29)). Member of [Polish Academy of Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Learning) and [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences). He was specialising mainly in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).
