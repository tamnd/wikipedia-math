# Catherine Goldstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catherine Goldstein"}

*Image: Catherine Goldstein ICM 2018 (44462085662).jpg.* Goldstein at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 2018

**Catherine Goldstein** (born July 5, 1958 in Paris) is a French [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) who works as a director of research at the Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu (IMJ). She was president of [L'association femmes et mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'association_femmes_et_mathématiques) in 1991.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Goldstein studied at the [Ecole normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_normale_supérieure) from 1976 to 1980, earning an [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics in 1978. She completed a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) of the third cycle in 1981, with a dissertation on [*p*-adic L-functions](wikipedia://en/22059815) and [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150) supervised by [John H. Coates](wikipedia://en/230705).

She worked at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) from 1980 until 2002, when she moved to IMJ.

## Contributions and recognition {#Contributions_and_recognition}

Goldstein has been listed as one of the plenary speakers at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). With [Norbert Schappacher](wikipedia://en/64764918) and [Joachim Schwermer](wikipedia://en/56118470), she is editor of the book *The shaping of arithmetic after C. F. Gauss's Disquisitiones arithmeticae*.
