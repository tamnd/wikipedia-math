# 5

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="5"}

**5** (**five**) is a [number](wikipedia://en/21690), [numeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numeral_%28linguistics%29) and [digit](wikipedia://en/280582). It is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474), and [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173), following [4](wikipedia://en/208161) and preceding [6](wikipedia://en/208171), and is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666).

:::

Humans, and many other animals, have 5 [digits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digit_%28anatomy%29) on their [limbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limb_%28anatomy%29).

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

*Image: Pythagoras' Special Triples.svg.* The first [Pythagorean triple](wikipedia://en/24172)

5 is a [Fermat prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime), a [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908) exponent, as well as a [Fibonacci number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_number). 5 is the first [congruent number](wikipedia://en/2334769), as well as the length of the [hypotenuse](wikipedia://en/251366) of the smallest integer-sided [right triangle](wikipedia://en/39420), making part of the smallest [Pythagorean triple](wikipedia://en/24172) ([3](wikipedia://en/208157), [4](wikipedia://en/208161), 5).

5 is the first [safe prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_prime) and the first [good prime](wikipedia://en/20819432). 11 forms the first pair of [sexy primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexy_prime) with 5. 5 is the second [Fermat prime](wikipedia://en/91127), of a total of five known Fermat primes. 5 is also the first of three known [Wilson primes](wikipedia://en/323646) (5, 13, 563).

### Geometry {#Geometry}

A shape with five sides is called a [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014). The equilateral pentagon is the first [regular polygon](wikipedia://en/333306) that does not [tile](wikipedia://en/321671) the [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) with copies of itself. The pentagon solid has the largest [face](wikipedia://en/149926) of any of the five regular three-dimensional regular [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905).

A [conic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conic) is [determined using five points](wikipedia://en/26269427) in the same way that two points are needed to determine a [line](wikipedia://en/946975).[^o12] A [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544), or five-pointed [polygram](wikipedia://en/45296926), is a [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) constructed by connecting some non-adjacent vertices of a [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon) as [self-intersecting edges](wikipedia://en/313303#Regular_star_polygon). The internal geometry of the pentagon and pentagram (represented by its [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{5/2\}$) appears prominently in [Penrose tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penrose_tilings). Pentagrams are [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) inside [Kepler–Poinsot star polyhedra](wikipedia://en/16814) and [Schläfli–Hess star polychora](wikipedia://en/18801624#Regular_star_%28Schläfli–Hess%29_4-polytopes).

There are five regular [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905) the [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), the [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), and the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084).[^o14]

The plane contains a total of five [Bravais lattices](wikipedia://en/661808), or arrays of [points](wikipedia://en/593693) defined by discrete [translation](wikipedia://en/204682) operations. [Uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/1548669) of the plane, are generated from combinations of only five regular polygons.[^o15]

Five-fold symmetry is associated with the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) $\frac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$. In a regular [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544), intersections of diagonals divide one another in the golden ratio.[^o16]

### Higher dimensional geometry {#Higher_dimensional_geometry}

A [hypertetrahedron](wikipedia://en/513821), or 5-cell, is the 4 dimensional analogue of the [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606). It has five vertices. Its orthographic projection is [homomorphic](wikipedia://en/13564) to the group *K*<sub>\5.</sub>[^o18]

There are five fundamental [mirror symmetry point group families in 4-dimensions](wikipedia://en/1645899#Convex_uniform_4-polytopes). There are also 5 [compact hyperbolic Coxeter groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram#Compact), or [4-prisms](wikipedia://en/1645899#Prismatic_uniform_4-polytopes), of rank 5, each generating uniform honeycombs in hyperbolic 4-space as permutations of rings of the Coxeter diagrams.[^o19]

*Image: Schlegel wireframe 5-cell.png.* The four-dimensional [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) is the simplest regular [polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polychoron).

### Arithmetic {#Arithmetic}

*Image: Magic Square Lo Shu.svg.* The smallest non-trivial [magic square](wikipedia://en/52202)

5 is the value of the central [cell](wikipedia://en/52202#Properties_of_magic_squares) of the first non-trivial [normal magic square](wikipedia://en/52202), called the [*Luoshu* square](wikipedia://en/532916). All [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) $n \ge 34$ can be expressed as the sum of five non-zero [squares](wikipedia://en/213919).[^o21][^o22] There are five countably infinite [Ramsey classes](wikipedia://en/40725476) of [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027).[^o23]

5 is [conjectured](wikipedia://en/6138) to be the only [odd](wikipedia://en/143135), [untouchable number](wikipedia://en/882410); if this is the case, then five will be the only odd prime number that is not the base of an [aliquot](wikipedia://en/486266) tree.[^o24]

*Image: SporadicGroups.png.* This diagram shows the [subquotient](wikipedia://en/1639294) relations of the twenty-six **sporadic groups**; the five [Mathieu groups](wikipedia://en/380207) form the simplest class (colored red *Image: EllipseSubqR.svg.*).

Every odd number greater than five is conjectured to be expressible as the sum of three prime numbers; [Helfgott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Andres_Helfgott) has provided a proof of this[^o25] (also known as the [odd Goldbach conjecture](wikipedia://en/147164)) that is already widely acknowledged by mathematicians as it still undergoes [peer-review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review). On the other hand, every odd number greater than one is the sum of at most five prime numbers (as a lower limit).[^o26]

### Group theory {#Group_theory}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), all [graphs](wikipedia://en/12401) with four or fewer vertices are [planar](wikipedia://en/24314), however, there is a graph with five vertices that is not: *K*<sub>5</sub>, the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) with five vertices. By [Kuratowski's theorem](wikipedia://en/54493), a finite graph is planar [if and only if](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iff) it does not contain a subgraph that is a subdivision of *K*<sub>5</sub>, or *K*<sub>3,3</sub>, the [utility graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_graph).[^o29]

There are five complex [exceptional Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/6573043). The five [Mathieu groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathieu_groups) constitute the [first generation](wikipedia://en/324806#First_generation_%285_groups%29:_the_Mathieu_groups) in the [happy family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_groups#Happy_Family) of [sporadic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_groups). These are also the first five sporadic groups [to have been described](wikipedia://en/7445#Timeline_of_the_proof).[^o30] A [centralizer](wikipedia://en/145661) of an element of order 5 inside the [largest sporadic group](wikipedia://en/47422) $\mathrm {F_1}$ arises from the product between [Harada–Norton](wikipedia://en/1196772) sporadic group $\mathrm{HN}$ and a group of order 5.[^o31][^o32]

## Evolution of the Arabic digit {#Evolution_of_the_Arabic_digit}

*Image: Seven-segment 5.svg.*

The evolution of the modern Western digit for the numeral for five is traced back to the [Indian system](wikipedia://en/1903587) of numerals, where on some earlier versions, the numeral bore resemblance to variations of the number four, rather than "5" (as it is represented today). The [Kushana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushan_Empire) and [Gupta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gupta_Empire) empires in what is now [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) had among themselves several forms that bear no resemblance to the modern digit. Later on, Arabic traditions transformed the digit in several ways, producing forms that were still similar to the numeral for four, with similarities to the numeral for three; yet, still unlike the modern five.[^o38] It was from those digits that Europeans finally came up with the modern 5 (represented in writings by Dürer, for example).

*Image: Evolution5glyph.png.*

While the shape of the character for the digit 5 has an [ascender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascender_%28typography%29) in most modern [typefaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typeface), in typefaces with [text figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_figures) the glyph usually has a [descender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descender), as, for example, in

*Image: Text figures 256.svg.*

.

On the [seven-segment display](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display) of a calculator and digital clock, it is often represented by five segments at four successive turns from top to bottom, rotating counterclockwise first, then clockwise, and vice versa. It is one of three numbers, along with 4 and 6, where the number of segments matches the number. This makes it often indistinguishable from the letter [S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S). Higher segment displays may sometimes may make use of a diagonal for one of the two.

### Religion {#Religion}

#### Judaism {#Judaism}

Five is according to [Maharal of Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judah_Loew_ben_Bezalel) the number defined as the center point which unifies four extremes.

#### Islam {#Islam}

The [Five Pillars of Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Pillars_of_Islam).[^o44] The [five-pointed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-pointed_star) [simple](wikipedia://en/1059530) star ☆ is one of the five used in Islamic [Girih tiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girih_tiles).[^o45]
