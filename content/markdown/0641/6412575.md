# Dipole graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dipole graph"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **dipole graph**, **dipole**, **bond graph**, or **linkage**, is a [multigraph](wikipedia://en/1774970) consisting of two [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) connected with a number of [parallel edges](wikipedia://en/5719783). A dipole graph containing $n$ edges is called the size-$n$ dipole graph, and is denoted by $D_{n}$. The size-$n$ dipole graph is [dual](wikipedia://en/2536864) to the [cycle graph](wikipedia://en/878962) $C_{n}$.

:::

The [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/2868762) as an abstract graph is the maximal abelian [covering graph](wikipedia://en/21123339) of the dipole graph $D_{3}$, while the [diamond crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_cubic) as an abstract graph is the maximal abelian covering graph of $D_{4}$.

Similarly to the [Platonic graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_graph), the dipole graphs form the skeletons of the [hosohedra](wikipedia://en/2316192). Their duals, the cycle graphs, form the skeletons of the [dihedra](wikipedia://en/6109457).
