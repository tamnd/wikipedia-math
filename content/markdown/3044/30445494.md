# Euler calculus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euler calculus"}

**Euler calculus** is a methodology from applied [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [integral geometry](wikipedia://en/349336) that integrates [constructible functions](wikipedia://en/906783) and more recently [definable functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Definable_function)[^o2] by integrating with respect to the [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) as a finitely-additive [measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory). In the presence of a metric, it can be extended to continuous integrands via the [Gauss–Bonnet theorem](wikipedia://en/139229).[^o3] It was introduced independently by [Pierre Schapira](wikipedia://en/41082949)[^o4][^o5][^o6] and [Oleg Viro](wikipedia://en/14465687)[^o7] in 1988, and is useful for enumeration problems in [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) and [sensor networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensor_network).[^o8]

:::
