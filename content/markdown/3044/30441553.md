# Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society"}

***Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society*** is a [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and intended to carry [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) book-length research publications ([monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph)).[^o4] As such, it has been described as having the characteristics both of a journal and of a [book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_series).[^o3] As with other journals, its publications are collected into volumes, numbering six per year until 2024 and twelve per year from 2025 on.[^o4] The start of the journal was announced in 1949,[^o5] and it began publication in 1950.

:::

The journal is indexed by [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH), [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index), Research Alert, [CompuMath Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CompuMath_Citation_Index), and [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents). Its dual nature as a journal and a book series has sometimes caused inconsistencies in its indexing.[^o6]
