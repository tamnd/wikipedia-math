# Joceline Lega

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joceline Lega"}

**Joceline Claude Lega** is a French physicist and [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969), interested in [nonlinear dynamics](wikipedia://en/146103). She is a professor in the departments of mathematics, applied mathematics, and epidemiology and biostatistics at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona), and editor-in-chief of *[Physica D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physica_D)*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After studying physics at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris from 1984 to 1988, and earning licentiate and maîtrise degrees in physics through [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1985, Lega earned a diplôme d'études approfondies in 1986 and a doctorate in theoretical physics in 1989, both at the [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis). Her dissertation was *Topological defects associated with the breaking of time translation invariance*.

She joined the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) in 1989, and took a leave from CNRS to join the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) in 1997. At Arizona, she was the director of the Program in Integrated Science (from 2008 to 2011), and the Institute for Mathematics & Education (from 2009 to 2013). Since 2016 she is the associate head for the Postdoctoral Program in Mathematics.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lega became a [Fellow of the Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Institute_of_Physics) in 2004. In 2017 she was elected as a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).
