# Hema Srinivasan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hema Srinivasan"}

**Hema Srinivasan** (born 1959) is a mathematician specializing in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). Originally from India, she is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri).

:::

Srinivasan was a [National Science Talent Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Talent_Search_Examination) in India beginning in 1975. She obtained her B.Sc.(Hons) from [Bombay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_University), where she won the Ghia Prize for mathematics in 1978, as well as an M.S. from [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 1982. She completed her Ph.D. at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1986. Her dissertation, supervised by [David Buchsbaum](wikipedia://en/30781131), was *Multiplicative Structures on Some Canonical Resolutions*. After working as a Visiting Instructor at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) from 1986 to 1988 and as a Research Assistant Professor [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) from 1988 to 1989, she joined the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) faculty as an assistant professor in 1989. At Missouri, she has supervised 6 doctoral students and is currently the faculty advisor for the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Student Chapter.

She is part of the 2018 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), elected "for contributions to algebra and algebraic geometry, mentoring, and service to the mathematical community".
