# Gross–Koblitz formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gross–Koblitz formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Gross–Koblitz formula**, introduced by (txt) expresses a [Gauss sum](wikipedia://en/6378204) using a product of values of the [*p*-adic gamma function](wikipedia://en/33869124). It is an analog of the [Chowla–Selberg formula](wikipedia://en/596179) for the usual [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316). It implies the [Hasse–Davenport relation](wikipedia://en/14619729) and generalizes the [Stickelberger theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stickelberger_theorem). (Boyarsky 1980) gave another proof of the Gross–Koblitz formula ("Boyarsky" being a pseudonym of [Bernard Dwork](wikipedia://en/1678313)), and (Robert 2001) gave an elementary proof.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The Gross–Koblitz formula states that the Gauss sum $\tau$ can be given in terms of the $p$-adic gamma function $\Gamma_p$ by

$$\tau_q(r) = -\pi^{s_p(r)}\prod_{0\leq i <f}\Gamma_p\!\left(\frac{r^{(i)}}{q-1} \right)$$

where

- $q$ is a power $p^f$ of a prime $p$,
- $r$ is an integer with $0 \leq r < q-1$,
- $r^{(i)}$ is the integer whose base-$p$ expansion is a cyclic permutation of the $f$ digits of $r$ by $i$ positions,
- $s_p(r)$ is the sum of the base-$p$ digits of $r$,
- $\tau_q(r) = \sum_{a^{q-1}=1} a^{-r} \zeta_\pi^{\text{Tr}(a)}$, where the sum is over roots of unity in the extension $\mathbb{Q}_p(\pi)$,
- $\pi$ satisfies $\pi^{p - 1} = -p$, and
- $\zeta_\pi$ is the $p$th root of unity congruent to $1 + \pi$ modulo $\pi^2$.

:::
