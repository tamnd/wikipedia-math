# Belphegor's prime

*Image: Belphegor's prime.svg.* Symbol of Belphegor's prime, represented by the Greek letter π upside down

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Belphegor's prime"}

**Belphegor's prime** is the [palindromic prime](wikipedia://en/424723) number **1000000000000066600000000000001** (10<sup>30</sup> + 666 × 10<sup>14</sup> + 1), a number which reads the same both backwards and forwards and is only divisible by itself and one.

:::

In the [short scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_scale), this number would be named "one nonillion, sixty-six quadrillion, six hundred trillion one." In the [long scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_scale), this number's name would be "one quintillion, sixty-six billiard, six hundred billion one."

## History {#History}

Belphegor's prime was first discovered by [Harvey Dubner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Dubner),[^o8] a mathematician known for his discoveries of many large prime numbers and prime number forms. For Belphegor's prime in particular, he discovered the prime while determining a sequence of primes it belongs to.

The name "Belphegor's prime" was coined by author [Clifford A. Pickover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_A._Pickover) in 2012.[^o3] [Belphegor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belphegor) is one of the [Seven Princes of Hell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Princes_of_Hell); specifically, "the demon of inventiveness".[^o3] The number itself contains [superstitious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstitious) elements that have given it its name: the number [666](wikipedia://en/463039) at the heart of Belphegor's prime is widely associated as being the [number of the beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_of_the_beast), used in symbolism to represent one of the creatures in the [apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalypse) or, more commonly, the devil. This number is surrounded on either side by thirteen zeroes and is 31 digits in length (thirteen reversed), with [thirteen](wikipedia://en/37231) itself long [regarded superstitiously as an unlucky number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triskaidekaphobia) in Western culture.

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Belphegor number"}

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

A *Belphegor number* is a palindromic number in the form of $1(0...)666(0...)1$. The sequence of the first four Belphegor numbers is:

$$16661, 1066601, 100666001, 10006660001, \ldots$$

Dubner noticed that 16661 is a prime number. By adding zeroes directly on both sides of the 666, while making it always start and end with a 1, Dubner found more palindromic prime numbers, including the Belphegor prime, which is second in the sequence. This sequence eventually became the *Belphegor primes*, named after the number.[^o8] The equal number of zeroes on each side of 666 in the first few Belphegor primes is:[^o6][^o8]

$$0, 13, 42, 506, 608, 2472, 2623, 28291, 181298, \ldots$$

Belphegor's prime contains 13 zeroes on either side of the central 666, and thus corresponds to the second number in this sequence.
