# Barbara Keyfitz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Keyfitz"}

**Barbara Lee Keyfitz** is a Canadian-American mathematician, formerly the Dr. Charles Saltzer Professor of Mathematics at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). In her research, she studies nonlinear [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and associated [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_laws).[^o8]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Keyfitz did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto),[^o8] and earned a Ph.D. in 1970 from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), under the supervision of [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365). Before taking her present position at [Ohio State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), she taught at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University), and the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston); at Houston, she was the John and Rebecca Moores Professor of Mathematics. She was also the director of the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) from 2004 to 2008.[^o8]

She was president of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from 2005 to 2006,[^o9] and in 2011 she became president of the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227).[^o5] She was Vice-President of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) from 2011 - 2014.[^o12]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Keyfitz is the 2005 winner[^o7] of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055), the 2011 [Noether Lecturer](wikipedia://en/11566873) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698),[^o8] the 2012 winner of the SIAM Prize for Distinguished Service to the Profession, and the 2012 AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecturer.[^o9] She was interviewed by [Patricia Clark Kenschaft](wikipedia://en/56707200) in her book *Change is Possible:Stories of Women and Minorities in Mathematics*.[^o10]

In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11] She is also a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376)[^o8] and the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768).[^o12]

In 2017, she was selected as a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the inaugural class.[^o13]

## Personal {#Personal}

Keyfitz was born in [Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa), and is the daughter of Canadian demographer [Nathan Keyfitz](wikipedia://en/38349113). She is married to [Marty Golubitsky](wikipedia://en/41446307) and has two children.[^o12]
