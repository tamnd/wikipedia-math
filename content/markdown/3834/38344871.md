# Itzhak Gilboa

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Itzhak Gilboa"}

**Itzhak Gilboa** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): יצחק גלבוע; born February 3, 1963, in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv)) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) with contributions in [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216). His work include the theory of Maxmin Expected Utility[^o5] with [David Schmeidler](wikipedia://en/26421806). This theory explains individual attitudes towards ambiguity that are consistent with the [Ellsberg paradox](wikipedia://en/1912480). He currently holds professorship positions at [HEC Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Paris) and [Reichman University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichman_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gilboa was born in Tel Aviv. After obtaining his BA in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), he earned his Ph.D. in 1987 under the supervision of [David Schmeidler](wikipedia://en/26421806). Gilboa joined [Kellogg School of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kellogg_School_of_Management) at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) after graduation and was promoted to a full professorship in 1992. From 1992 to 1997, he was the Frederic E. Nemmers Distinguished Professor of Decision Sciences at Northwestern University. Between 1997 and 1999, he was a part-time professor of economics as [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University).[^o9]

Gilboa joined Tel Aviv University in 1997 as a professor of economics until 2023, when he moved to [Reichman University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichman_University). He holds separate appointment in the Department of Economics and Decision Sciences at [HEC Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Paris) since 2008.[^o9]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Gilboa was elected a [Fellow of the Econometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Econometric_Society) in 2000. From 2001 to 2009, Gilboa was a Fellow of the [Cowles Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowles_Foundation) at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o8] In 2013, he became a Fellow of the [Society for the Advancement of Economic Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_the_Advancement_of_Economic_Theory). In 2025, he became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o9]
