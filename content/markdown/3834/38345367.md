# Bass number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bass number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the *i*th **Bass number** of a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) *M* over a [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) *R* with [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) *k* is the *k*-dimension of $\operatorname{Ext}^i_R(k,M)$. More generally the Bass number $\mu_i(p,M)$ of a module *M* over a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* at a [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) *p* is the Bass number of the [localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localization_of_a_module) of *M* for the [localization](wikipedia://en/252216#Localization_of_a_ring) of *R* (with respect to the prime *p*). Bass numbers were introduced by (txt, p.11).

:::

The Bass numbers describe the minimal [injective resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_resolution) of a [finitely-generated module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_module) *M* over a [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129): for each prime ideal *p* there is a corresponding [indecomposable](wikipedia://en/810549) [injective module](wikipedia://en/473775), and the number of times this occurs in the *i*th term of a minimal resolution of *M* is the Bass number $\mu_i(p,M)$.
