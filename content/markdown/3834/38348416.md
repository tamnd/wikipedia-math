# Hodge algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hodge algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Hodge algebra** or **algebra with straightening law** is a [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) that is a [free module](wikipedia://en/326454) over some [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R*, together with a given basis similar to the basis of [standard monomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_monomial) of the [coordinate ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_ring) of a [Grassmannian](wikipedia://en/373810). Hodge algebras were introduced by (txt), who named them after [W. V. D. Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430).

:::
