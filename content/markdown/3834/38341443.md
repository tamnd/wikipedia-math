# William B. Johnson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William B. Johnson"}

**William Buhmann Johnson** (born December 5, 1944) is an American mathematician, one of the namesakes of the [Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma](wikipedia://en/14855952). He is Distinguished Professor and A.G. & M.E. Owen Chair of Mathematics at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). His research specialties include the theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), [nonlinear functional analysis](wikipedia://en/8546912), and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).[^o2] He was born in [Palo Alto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California) and raised from an early age in [Dallas, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas,_Texas).

:::

Johnson graduated from [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University) in 1966,[^o3] and earned a doctorate from [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) in 1969 under the supervision of James A. Dyer. After faculty positions at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston), and [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), he joined the Texas A&M faculty in 1984.

In 2007, Johnson was awarded the [Stefan Banach Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Banach_Medal) of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5][^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] In 2018 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o8] His doctoral students include [Edward Odell](wikipedia://en/56893192).
