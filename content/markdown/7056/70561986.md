# Stojan Radenović

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stojan Radenović"}

**Stojan Radenović** (Стојан Раденовић; born 9 March 1948) is a [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia) academic and politician. An internationally respected [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), he has served in Serbia's [national assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Serbia) since 2022 as an independent representative endorsed by the [Serbian Progressive Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Progressive_Party) (SNS). He was acting [president of the assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_National_Assembly_of_Serbia) from February to March 2024.

:::

## Early life and academic career {#Early_life_and_academic_career}

Radenović was born in the village of [Dobra Voda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobra_Voda,_Bojnik) in the municipality of [Bojnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bojnik,_Serbia), in what was then the [People's Republic of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Republic_of_Serbia) in the [Federal People's Republic of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_People's_Republic_of_Yugoslavia). He received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1979 and taught for the next two decades at the [University of Kragujevac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kragujevac) Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics. From 2000 to 2013, he was a full professor at the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) Faculty of Mechanical Engineering.

In 2016, Radenović was personally responsible for the University of Belgrade ranking in the world's top three hundred universities for the first time on the prestigious [Shanghai list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Ranking_of_World_Universities), due to his large number of published works and citations. This accomplishment brought him to the attention of the wider public.[^o4][^o5]

## Politician {#Politician}

In the [2022 Serbian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Serbian_parliamentary_election), the Serbian Progressive Party reserved the lead positions on its *[Together We Can Do Everything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Together_We_Can_Do_Everything)* electoral list for non-party cultural figures and academics.[^o7] Radenović was given the second position on the list; this was [tantamount to election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantamount_to_election), and he was indeed elected when the list won a plurality victory with 120 out of 250 mandates.[^o8] During the campaign, he credited the SNS with improving conditions in Serbia over its decade in power.[^o9] In his first term, Radenović was a member of the education committee and the subcommittee on science and higher education.[^o10][^o11]

He was given the ninth position on the SNS's list in the [2023 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Serbian_parliamentary_election) and was re-elected when the list won a majority victory with 129 seats.[^o12] As the oldest member of the new parliament, Radenović was chosen as interim president when the assembly convened in February 2024.[^o13] He served in this role until 20 March, when [Ana Brnabić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Brnabić) was elected to the position.[^o14]

He is now once again a member of the assembly's education committee.[^o15]
