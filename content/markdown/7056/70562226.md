# Shabnam Akhtari

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Shabnam Akhtari"}

**Shabnam Akhtari** is a Canadian-Iranian mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and in particular in [Diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109), [Thue equations](wikipedia://en/1151323), and the [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153). She is a professor of mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Akhtari graduated from the [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_University_of_Technology), in [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran), in 2002 with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in mathematics. She earned a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree from [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) in 2004, before continuing her graduate work in mathematics at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), completing her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) there in 2008. Her dissertation, *Thue Equations and Related Topics*, was supervised by Mike Bennett.

She was a postdoctoral researcher at [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in Canada, the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Germany and the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) in Canada before joining the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) faculty as an assistant professor of mathematics in 2012. She was tenured as an associate professor there in 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Akhtari is the 2021–2022 winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).
