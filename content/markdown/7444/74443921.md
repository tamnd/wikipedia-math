# 1971 Indonesian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1971 Indonesian census"}

The **1971 Indonesian census** was the second census of [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia) as a sovereign state. It enumerated a total population of 119,208,229. Its population density during the 1971 census was 119208229 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.[^n1]

:::

It was the last and the only two Indonesian censuses held in a year ending with the number 1, the other the first [1961 census](wikipedia://en/72501743). The following census held in [1980](wikipedia://en/74443933) and onwards held in a year ending with the number 0.
