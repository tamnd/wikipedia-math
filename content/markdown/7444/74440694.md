# ODE/IM correspondence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ODE/IM correspondence"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **ODE/IM correspondence** is a link between [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) (ODEs) and [integrable models](wikipedia://en/2573213). It was first found in 1998 by Patrick Dorey and Roberto Tateo.[^o2] In this original setting it relates the [spectrum](wikipedia://en/506713) of a certain integrable model of [magnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetism) known as the [XXZ-model](wikipedia://en/4899779) to solutions of the one-dimensional [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) with a specific choice of potential, where the position coordinate is considered as a complex coordinate.

:::

Since then, such a correspondence has been found for many more ODE/IM pairs.[^o3]
