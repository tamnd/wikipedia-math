# 1980 Indonesian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1980 Indonesian census"}

The **1980 Indonesian census** was the third census of [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia) as a sovereign state. It enumerated a total population of 147,490,298. Its population density during the 1980 census was 147490298 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.[^n1]

:::

It was the first Indonesian census to be held in a year ending with the number 0, as opposed to the first two censuses ([1961](wikipedia://en/72501743) and [1971](wikipedia://en/74443921) censuses), which both held in a year ending with the number 1.
