# Semantics and Pragmatics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semantics and Pragmatics"}

***Semantics and Pragmatics*** (abbreviated ***S&P***) is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [diamond open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_open_access) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) covering research pertaining to [meaning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meaning_%28linguistics%29) in [natural language](wikipedia://en/21173).[^o4][^o5] A highly prestigious journal, it is one of the most important venues in [formal semantics](wikipedia://en/31395652), alongside [Natural Language Semantics](wikipedia://en/12327811), [Linguistics and Philosophy](wikipedia://en/22832531), and the [Journal of Semantics](wikipedia://en/12328052).[^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

It was established by [David Beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Beaver) and [Kai von Fintel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_von_Fintel) in 2007 and has been published by the [Linguistic Society of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_Society_of_America) since 2013.[^o4][^o5] The journal is funded by [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) and [UT Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UT_Austin), eliminating the need for article processing fees faced by many other open access initiatives.[^o9] Its current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Louise McNally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louise_McNally) and [Kjell Johan Sæbø](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kjell_Johan_Sæbø).[^o4][^o5]

The journal's establishment has been viewed as part of a trend towards a tighter integration between formal semantics and formal pragmatics.[^o10] Work published in the journal includes papers from the ongoing debate regarding whether [implicatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Implicatures) are computed within the grammar or via post-compositional Gricean reasoning.[^o11]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in the [Emerging Sources Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_Sources_Citation_Index), [DOAJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DOAJ), [International Bibliography of Periodical Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Bibliography_of_Periodical_Literature), [Linguistic Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_Bibliography), and the [Modern Language Association Database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Language_Association_Database).[^o13]
