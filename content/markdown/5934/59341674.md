# Israel Mathematical Union

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Israel Mathematical Union"}

The **Israel Mathematical Union** (**IMU**) (הַאִיגּוּד הַיִשְׂרְאֵלִי לְמָתֶמָטִיקָה) is an [association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_association) of professional [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians) in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel). It is a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) and the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227), and has reciprocity membership agreements with the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o3]

:::

The Union was founded on 2 March 1953 and held its first meeting with eleven short lectures on 28 September of that year.[^o4] Early members included [Binyamin Amirà](wikipedia://en/60040792), [Michael Fekete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fekete), and [Abraham Fraenkel](wikipedia://en/528451), who represented the Union at the 1954 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), as well as [Shmuel Agmon](wikipedia://en/32542224), [Jacob Levitzki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Levitzki), and Dov Jarden.

## Prizes {#Prizes}

The Israel Mathematical Union awards four major prizes:

- The [Anna and Lajos Erdős Prize in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize), awarded to an Israeli mathematician under the age of 41.[^o6]
- The Levitzki Prize in Algebra, awarded biennially to a young Israeli mathematician for research in [Algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) or related areas.[^o7]
- The Haim Nessyahu Prize in Mathematics, awarded for outstanding achievements in a mathematical [Ph.D. dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis).[^o8]
- The Abarbanel Prize in Applied Mathematics, awarded to an Israeli applied mathematician under the age of 48.[^o9]
