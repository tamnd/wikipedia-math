# Illinois Journal of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Illinois Journal of Mathematics"}

The ***Illinois Journal of Mathematics*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by [Duke University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University_Press) on behalf of the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois). It was established in 1957 by [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114), [Joseph L. Doob](wikipedia://en/1547599), [Abraham Taub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Taub), [George W. Whitehead](wikipedia://en/13641389), and [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352).[^o3]

:::

The journal published the proof of the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949) by [Kenneth Appel](wikipedia://en/311544) and [Wolfgang Haken](wikipedia://en/311546), which featured a then-unusual tabulation of computer-generated cases.
