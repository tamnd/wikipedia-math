# Péter Varjú

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Péter Varjú"}

**Péter Varjú** is a Hungarian mathematician who works in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986). He is a professor at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Varjú was born in [Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged). He did his undergraduate studies at the University of Szeged[^o10] and his doctoral studies at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where he defended his thesis *Random walks and spectral gaps in linear groups* in 2011 under the supervision of [Jean Bourgain](wikipedia://en/1024131).

He works at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), as a professor in the Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Varjú studied the construction of [expander graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expander_graphs) with [number-theoretic](wikipedia://en/21527) methods involving [arithmetic groups](wikipedia://en/630741)A and questions about the uniform distribution of [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) in arithmetic groups with BourgainB and in Euclidean isometries with [Elon Lindenstrauss](wikipedia://en/28371257).CD

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Varjú received the 2016 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize)[^o8] and the 2018 [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913).[^o9] As a graduate student at Princeton, he was also a [Fulbright Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Fellow).[^o10]
