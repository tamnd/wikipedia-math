# Maria Kokoszyńska-Lutmanowa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Kokoszyńska-Lutmanowa"}

**Maria Kokoszyńska-Lutmanowa** (born **Maria Kokoszyńska**, 6 December 1905 – 30 June 1981[^o4]) was a Polish logician, philosopher of language and epistemologist. She belonged to the third generation of the [Lwów–Warsaw school](wikipedia://en/1426957). She is mostly known as the author of the important argumentation against neopositivism of the [Vienna Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Circle) as well as one of the main critics of relativistic theories of truth.[^o5] She was also noted for popularising [Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)'s works on [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107).

:::

Kokoszyńska studied philosophy in [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv) under [Kazimierz Ajdukiewicz](wikipedia://en/900467) (and [Kazimierz Twardowski](wikipedia://en/1022980)) at the [Jan Kazimierz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Kazimierz_University), and at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) under [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741).[^o4][^o3]

She later held the chair of logic at the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław).[^o4][^o5]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Kokoszyńska, M., (1936), 'Über den absoluten Wahrheitsbegriff und einige andere semantische Begriffe', *[Erkenntnis](wikipedia://en/18761111)* 6, 143–156.
- Kokoszyńska, M., (1948), 'What means "relativity of truth"?', *Studia Philosophica* 3, 167–176.
- Kokoszyńska, M., (1951), 'A refutation of the relativism of truth', *Studia Philosophica* 4, 1–57.
