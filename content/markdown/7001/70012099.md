# Jiang Boju

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jiang Boju"}

**Jiang Boju** (; born September 1937[^o2]) is a Chinese mathematician and a professor of [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), School of Mathematical Sciences. He is known for his contributions to topology.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Honors {#Honors}

He was elected to be a member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1980[^o2] and a fellow of [TWAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS) in 1985.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Family {#Family}

Jiang Boju's father [Jiang Lifu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiang_Lifu) was a mathematician and educator widely regarded as the Father of modern Chinese mathematics.[^o11]
