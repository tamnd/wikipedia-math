# Hu Shihua

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hu Shihua"}

**Hu Shihua** (; 1912 – April 11, 1998) was a Chinese mathematician.[^o2] He was a member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::
