# Ismat Beg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ismat Beg"}

**Ismat Beg**, [FPAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Pakistan_Academy_of_Sciences), [FIMA](wikipedia://en/697139), (عصمت بیگ; born January 1951) is a Pakistani [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Researcher). Beg is a Distinguished National Professor at [Lahore School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_School_of_Economics),[^o4] [Higher Education Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Commission_of_Pakistan)[^o5] and an honorary full professor at the Mathematics Division at the Ruggero Santilli Institute for Basic Research, Florida, US. Beg's scientific contributions cover a wide range of topics spanning fixed point theory and approximations, order structure, fuzzy sets and systems, artificial intelligence and multicriteria decision theory.[^o6][^o7]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ismat Beg was born in the small hilly village of [Mohri Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohri_Sharif), west of [Kharian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharian) in 1951 to an immigrant [Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiris) family. He got his primary schooling in a village school "sitting under the trees". In 1961 his father took him to [Risalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risalpur) and he was sent to Sapper Boys High School, Risalpur Cantt. After completing his matriculation 1966, he joined Zamindar College, Bhimber Road, Gujrat (now [Government Zamindar College, Gujrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Zamindar_College,_Gujrat)) for his [Higher Secondary School Certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Secondary_School_Certificate) and undergraduate degree. He obtained his master's from Government College, Lahore [^o9] (now [Government College University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_College_University_%28Lahore%29)).[^o6] In 1977, Beg travelled to [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) on a scholarship and attended the [West University of Timișoara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_University_of_Timișoara) for [Romanian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language) and pre-doctoral courses and passed the PhD entrance exam for the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). He started his PhD in fall of 1978 under the supervision of [academician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academician) and famous mathematician Romulus Cristescu.[^o10] His area of research is ordered vector spaces and linear operators with specialization in integral representation of linear operators. He defended his thesis in December 1981 and the degree was awarded in 1982.[^o11][^o12]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Ismat Beg started teaching just after his PhD in 1982 and has taught at[^o3][^o4][^o14]

- [Lahore School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_School_of_Economics)
- [Lahore University of Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_University_of_Management_Sciences)
- [Kuwait University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_University)
- [Quaid-i-Azam University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-i-Azam_University)
- [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics)
- [Pakistan Navy Engineering College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Navy_Engineering_College), [National University of Sciences and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Sciences_and_Technology_%28Pakistan%29)
- [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University)
- [University of Central Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Punjab)

He has published research papers in the fields of mathematics, computer science, economics, game theory, engineering, decision theory and social sciences that have been well cited by other researchers.[^o15][^o16] He has contributed to the fields of [fixed point theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed_point_theory), [fuzzy set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzzy_set_theory), order structures, preference modeling and multi-criteria multi-agent decision making.[^o15][^o17] Beg has supervised 12 [M.Phil.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Phil.) theses, 7 Ph.D. dissertations, and 10 post doctoral researchers.[^o10] He is member of the [editorial boards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of

- *Proceeding [Pakistan Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Academy_of_Sciences)*[^o18]
- *Journal Function Spaces*[^o19]
- *Gulf Journal of Mathematics*[^o20]
- *Punjab University Journal of Mathematics*[^o21]
- [Military Technical Courier (journal)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Technical_Courier_%28journal%29)[^o22]
