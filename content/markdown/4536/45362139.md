# Deborah Frank Lockhart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Deborah Frank Lockhart"}

**Deborah Frank Lockhart** is a mathematician known for her work with the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Lockhart graduated in 1965 from the [Bronx High School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx_High_School_of_Science).[^o4] She received her BS in mathematics from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University),[^o5] and went on to receive her Ph.D. from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) in the area of [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918).[^o6]

Lockhart went on to work at [SUNY Geneseo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SUNY_Geneseo) before moving to [Michigan Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Technological_University) in 1976.[^o5] She began working as a program director at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) in 1988, later becoming a deputy division director and acting division director before, in 2016, being named deputy assistant director of the Directorate for Mathematical and Physical Sciences.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Lockhart became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] Also that year, she became a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o10] She is also the 2021 recipient of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) Prize for Distinguished Service to the Profession.[^o11]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Lockhart, Deborah F. Dynamic buckling of a damped imperfect column on a nonlinear foundation. *Quart. Appl. Math.* 36 (1978/79), no. 1, 49–55.
