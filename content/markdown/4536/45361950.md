# Birgit Speh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Birgit Speh"}

**Birgit Speh** (born 1949)[^o3] is a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o4] She is known for her work in [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups), including [Speh representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speh_representation) (also known as Speh's representations).[^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Speh received her Ph.D. from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1977. She was the first female mathematician to be given tenure by [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), and the first to receive the title of Professor.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Speh became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] She was selected to give the 2020 AWM-AMS Emmy [Noether Lecture](wikipedia://en/11566873) at the 2020 [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143).[^o11]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Speh, Birgit; Vogan, David A. Jr. Reducibility of generalized principal series representations. *Acta Math.* 145 (1980)
- Speh, Birgit. Unitary representations of Gl(*n*,**R**) with nontrivial (*g*,*K*)-cohomology. *Invent. Math.* 71 (1983), no. 3, 443–465.
- Speh, Birgit. The unitary dual of Gl(3,**R**) and Gl(4,**R**). *Math. Ann.* 258 (1981/82), no. 2, 113–133.
