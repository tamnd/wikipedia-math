# Probabilistic signature scheme

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Probabilistic signature scheme"}

**Probabilistic Signature Scheme** (**PSS**) is a [cryptographic](wikipedia://en/18934432) [signature scheme](wikipedia://en/59644) designed by [Mihir Bellare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mihir_Bellare) and [Phillip Rogaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillip_Rogaway).[^o8]

:::

RSA-PSS is an adaptation of their work and is standardized as part of [PKCS#1 v2.1](wikipedia://en/16706008). In general, RSA-PSS should be used as a replacement for RSA-PKCS#1 v1.5.

## Design {#Design}

PSS was specifically developed to allow modern methods of security analysis to prove that its security directly relates to that of the [RSA problem](wikipedia://en/1544741). There is no such proof for the traditional PKCS#1 v1.5 scheme.
