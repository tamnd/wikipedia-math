# Boris Feigin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Feigin"}

**Boris Lvovich Feigin** (בוריס פייגין; Бори́с Льво́вич Фе́йгин; born 20 November 1953) is a Russian and Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His research has spanned [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups) and Lie algebras, [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774), [homological](wikipedia://en/139410) and [homotopical algebra](wikipedia://en/1321050).[^o3]

:::

In 1969, Feigin graduated from the [Moscow Mathematical School No. 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Mathematical_School_No._2) ([Andrei Zelevinsky](wikipedia://en/32415867) was among his classmates). From 1969 until 1974, he was a [student](wikipedia://en/155526) in the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) (MSU) under joint supervision of [Dmitry Fuchs](wikipedia://en/56558134) and [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643).[^o4] His diploma thesis was dedicated to characteristic classes of flags of foliations. Feigin was not accepted to the graduate school of MSU due to increasingly antisemitic policies at that institution at that time. After working as a computer programmer in industry for some time, he was accepted in 1976 to the graduate school of [Yaroslavl State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslavl_State_University) and defended his thesis "Cohomology of current Lie algebras on smooth manifolds" in 1981 at [Steklov Institute in Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Department_of_Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics_of_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto) in 1990.[^o5]

Boris Feigin is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the [Independent University of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_University_of_Moscow) and a [senior research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_research_fellow) at [Landau Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics) since 1992. Since 2009, he is a professor of the [Faculty of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSE_Faculty_of_Mathematics) at the [Higher School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_School_of_Economics) (HSE). In 2013 he was promoted to [Distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor) at HSE. Since 2014, he is the head of the Laboratory of Representation Theory and Mathematical Physics at HSE.[^o6]

In 2023, Feigin immigrated to Israel and joined the faculty of the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).[^o7]

Boris Feigin is a member of the [editorial boards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_boards) of [mathematics journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) *[Functional Analysis and Its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_Analysis_and_Its_Applications)*, *[Moscow Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Mathematical_Journal)*, *Transformation groups*.[^o8][^o9]
