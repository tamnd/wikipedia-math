# Karl Kunisch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Kunisch"}

**Karl Kunisch** (born September 16, 1952 in [Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linz)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Kunisch studied mathematics at the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology) and at the [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), Evanston, USA. After his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1978 at the Graz University of Technology on the topic of neutral functional-differential equations and semigroup theory, he obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1980 at the same university. In the following years, he repeatedly held visiting professor positions at the Lefschetz Center for Dynamical Systems of [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), USA.[^o4]

From 1986 to 1993, Kunisch held a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) position at the Graz University of Technology before moving to [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). In 1996, he became Professor of Optimization and Optimal Control at the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz), where he retired in the fall of 2020. Since 2012, he is also Scientific Director of the [Johann Radon Institute for Computational and Applied Mathematics (RICAM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Radon_Institute_for_Computational_and_Applied_Mathematics_%28RICAM%29) in Linz.

Kunisch authored over 360 publications in peer-reviewed journals as well as several books.[^o5] He is a member of the editorial board of several mathematical journals including the SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization, SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis, and the [Journal of the European Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_European_Mathematical_Society). Between 2007 and 2018, Kunisch was speaker of the collaborative research center *Mathematical Optimization and Applications in Biomedical Sciences.*[^o6] funded by the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund). In 2008, he was awarded the [Alwin-Walther medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alwin-Walther_medal) of the [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt).[^o7] Kunisch was invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Hyderabad, 2010.[^o8] In 2015, he received an Advanced Grant of the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) on the topic of *From Open to Closed Loop Control*.[^o9] Since 2017, he is a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).[^o10] He was awarded the [W. T. and Idalia Reid Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._T._and_Idalia_Reid_Prize) 2021.[^o11]

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

- Optimization of partial differential equations
- Mathematical control theory
- Inverse problems and mathematical imaging
- Mathematical modelling in medicine
- Model reduction
- Scientific computing
- Mathematical data science
- Neutral functional-differential equations
