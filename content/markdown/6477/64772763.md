# Peter Schneider (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Schneider"}

*Image: Peter Schneider 2009.jpg.* Schneider at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2009

**Peter Bernd Schneider** (born 9 January 1953 in [Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe)) is a German mathematician, specializing in the *p*-adic aspects of algebraic number theory,[^o2] arithmetic algebraic geometry, and representation theory.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Peter Schneider studied mathematics in Karlsruhe and Erlangen. After his *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)* in 1977 from the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg),[^o3] he was an assistant from 1977 to 1983 at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg). There he received in 1980 his PhD with advisor [Jürgen Neukirch](wikipedia://en/17477195) and dissertation *Die Galoiscohomologie $p$-adischer Darstellungen über Zahlkörpern* (The [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) of $p$-adic representations of [number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field)). Schneider [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1982 at the University of Regensburg. He was a postdoc at Harvard University for the academic year 1983–1984 and a [C2-professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_Germany) at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) for the academic year 1984–1985. He was a [C4-professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_Germany) from 1985 to 1994 at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) and is since 1994 a C-4 professor at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).[^o3]

His research includes [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), special values of [$L$-functions](wikipedia://en/245283). and [$p$-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) [representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_Theory) (in the latter subject he has collaborated extensively with Jeremy Teitelbaum).

In 1992 Schneider, together with [Christopher Deninger](wikipedia://en/32349981), [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548) and [Thomas Zink](wikipedia://en/33323824), received the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) for their work in using arithmetic-algebraic geometry to solve [Diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109). In 2006 he was an invited speaker with talk *Continuous representation theory of p-adic Lie groups* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid. In 2016 he was elected a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina)[^o6] and the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).
