# Konstantin Khanin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Konstantin Khanin"}

**Konstantin** "**Kostya**" **Mikhailovich Khanin** (Константин Михайлович Ханин) is a Russian mathematician and physicist. He served as the [chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_%28academic%29) of the [Department of Mathematical and Computational Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Mathematical_and_Computational_Sciences_%28University_of_Toronto%29) at the [University of Toronto Mississauga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Mississauga).[^o3]

:::

## Background {#Background}

Khanin received his PhD from the [Landau Institute of Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_Institute_of_Theoretical_Physics) in Moscow and continued working there as a [Research Associate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Associate) until 1994.[^o5] Afterwards, he taught at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge), and at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) before joining the faculty at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto). Khanin was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Barcelona in 2000. He was a 2013 Simons Foundation Fellow.[^o6] He held the Jean-Morlet Chair at the [Centre International de Rencontres Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/54507572) in 2017, and he was an [Invited Speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_talk_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 2018 in Rio de Janeiro. In 2021 he was awarded [The Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Humboldt_Prize), also known as the Humboldt Research Award, in recognition of his lifetime's research achievements.[^o7][^o8]
