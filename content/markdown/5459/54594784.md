# Mariusz Lemańczyk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mariusz Lemańczyk"}

**Mariusz Tomasz Lemańczyk** (born 3 February 1958) is a Polish mathematician known for his contributions in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He graduated from the [Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus_University_in_Toruń) in 1981, in mathematics, and completed his PhD in 1985. In 1987, he received the [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075) Prize, considered one of the most prestigious awards for young Polish mathematicians.[^o5] He received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1991,[^o6] after which he joined the faculty at [Nicolaus Copernicus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus_University). In 1997, he received the Stefan Banach Prize from the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o7] In 2016, he held the Jean-Morlet Chair at the [Centre International de Rencontres Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/54507572).[^o8] On December 3rd 2021 he was [selected to join](https://translate.google.com/translate?hl=ru&prev=_t&sl=pl&tl=en&u=https://informacje.pan.pl/index.php/informacje/materialy-dla-prasy/3498-nowi-czlonkowie-pan-i-amu-pan) the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences). In July 2022 he gave an invited lecture entitled '[Furstenberg](wikipedia://en/6450152) disjointness, [Ratner](wikipedia://en/12687622) properties and [Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak)’s conjecture' at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o9]
