# Ian Goulden

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ian Goulden"}

**Ian P. Goulden** is a Canadian and British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He works as a professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in the department of [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [Optimization](wikipedia://en/52033). He obtained his PhD from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 1979 under the supervision of [David M. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_M._Jackson). His PhD thesis was titled *Combinatorial Decompositions in the Theory of Algebraic Enumeration*. Goulden is well known for his contributions in [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533) such as the Goulden-Jackson cluster method.

:::

Goulden was the dean at the [University of Waterloo Faculty of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4320685)[^o3][^o4] from 2010 to 2015 and served as chair of the Department of Combinatorics and Optimization three times.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2010 Goulden was elected as a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada). In 2009 received the [University of Waterloo Faculty of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4320685) Award for Distinction in Teaching,[^o6] and in 1976 he received the Alumni Gold Medal for highest academic achievement[^o7] at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Goulden and Jackson published the book *Combinatorial Enumeration*.[^o9][^o10] Goulden also published the book *Finite Mathematics* with R.G. Dunkley, R.J. MacKay, K.S. Brown, R.F. de Peiza, D.A. DiFelice, and D.E. Matthews. He has written over 90 research articles in the fields of [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [Enumerative Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enumerative_Combinatorics), and [Algebraic Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Geometry).[^o11]
