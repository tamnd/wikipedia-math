# Luke Tierney

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Luke Tierney"}

**Luke Tierney** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). A fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) since 1988 and of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) since 1991, Tierney is currently a professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa).[^o4] Through his past work on [programming languages](wikipedia://en/23015) such as [R](wikipedia://en/376707) and [Lisp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisp_%28programming_language%29), Tierney now holds a position on the developing team known as the R Core.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Tierney earned his BA and MA in [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in 1977 and later his PhD in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1980.[^o4] Formerly a statistics faculty member at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota),[^o7] he now serves as the Ralph E. Wareham Professor of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Iowa, a position he has held since 2002.[^o4]

## Work {#Work}

In 1990, Tierney wrote the [XLispStat](wikipedia://en/1988114) package using [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_%28programming_language%29) and Lisp[^o7] and has since published works such as *LISP-STAT: An Object-Oriented Environment for Statistical Computing and Dynamic Graphics (Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics)* describing its design and use.[^o9]

Tierney has also made contributions in areas such as reference counting, vectors, and compilation for the R programming language and environment.[^o4] During his time working with R, he has also become part of the R Core, a team of developers with write access to the R source. His work on [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876), [Bioconductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioconductor), Lisp-stat and the [Metropolis–Hastings algorithm](wikipedia://en/56107) have all been highly cited.
