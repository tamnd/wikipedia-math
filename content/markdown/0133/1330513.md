# Keith Geddes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Keith Geddes"}

**Keith Oliver Geddes** (born 1947) is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) in the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) within the [Faculty of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4320685) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in [Waterloo, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterloo,_Ontario). He is a former director of the [Symbolic Computation Group](http://www.scg.uwaterloo.ca) in the School of [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science). He received a BA in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saskatchewan) in 1968; he completed both his MSc and PhD in [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).

:::

Geddes is probably best known for co-founding the [Maple computer algebra system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple_computer_algebra_system), now in widespread academic use around the world. He is also the Scientific Director at the [Ontario Research Centre for Computer Algebra](http://www.orcca.on.ca/), and is a member of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery), as well as the [American](wikipedia://en/198822) and [Canadian](wikipedia://en/906055) Mathematical Societies.

## Research {#Research}

Geddes' primary research interest is to develop [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) for the mechanization of mathematics. More specifically, he is interested in the computational aspects of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). Currently, he is focusing on designing [hybrid symbolic-numeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic-numeric_computation) algorithms to perform definite [integration](wikipedia://en/15532) and solve ordinary and partial [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations).

Much of his work currently revolves around [Maple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple_computer_algebra_system).

## Teaching {#Teaching}

Geddes retired from teaching in December 2008. Geddes taught a mixture of both senior-level symbolic computation courses, at both the undergraduate and graduate level, as well as introductory courses on the principles of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).
