# Extended negative binomial distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Extended negative binomial distribution"}

In [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) the **extended negative binomial distribution** is a [discrete probability distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_probability_distribution) extending the [negative binomial distribution](wikipedia://en/45177). It is a [truncated](wikipedia://en/7179738) version of the negative binomial distribution[^o2] for which estimation methods have been studied.[^o3]

:::

In the context of [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), the distribution appeared in its general form in a paper by K. Hess, A. Liewald and K.D. Schmidt[^o4] when they characterized all distributions for which the extended [Panjer recursion](wikipedia://en/13739701) works. For the case $m = 1$, the distribution was already discussed by Willmot[^o5] and put into a parametrized family with the [logarithmic distribution](wikipedia://en/1119231) and the negative binomial distribution by H.U. Gerber.[^o6]

## Probability mass function {#Probability_mass_function}

For a natural number $m \ge 1$ and real parameters $p$, $r$ with $0 < p \le 1$ and $-m < r < -m + 1$, the [probability mass function](wikipedia://en/154725) of the ExtNegBin($m$, $r$, $p$) distribution is given by

$$f(k;m,r,p)=0\qquad \text{ for }k\in\{0,1,\ldots,m-1\}$$

and

$$f(k;m,r,p) = \frac{{k+r-1 \choose k} p^k}{(1-p)^{-r}-\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}{j+r-1 \choose j} p^j}\quad\text{for }k\in{\mathbb N}\text{ with }k\ge m,$$

where

$${k+r-1 \choose k} = \frac{\Gamma(k+r)}{k!\,\Gamma(r)} = (-1)^k\,{-r \choose k}\qquad\qquad(1)$$

is the (generalized) [binomial coefficient](wikipedia://en/4668) and $\Gamma$ denotes the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316).

## Probability generating function {#Probability_generating_function}

Using that $f\,(\,.\,;\,m,\,r,\,ps)$ for $s \in$0, 1 is also a probability mass function, it follows that the [probability generating function](wikipedia://en/236841) is given by

$$\begin{aligned}\varphi(s)&=\sum_{k=m}^\infty f(k;m,r,p)s^k\\ &=\frac{(1-ps)^{-r}-\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\binom{j+r-1}j (ps)^j} {(1-p)^{-r}-\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\binom{j+r-1}j p^j} \qquad\text{for } |s|\le\frac1p.\end{aligned}$$

For the important case $m = 1$, hence $r \in$–1, 0, this simplifies to

$$\varphi(s)=\frac{1-(1-ps)^{-r}}{1-(1-p)^{-r}} \qquad\text{for }|s|\le\frac1p.$$
