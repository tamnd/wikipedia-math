# Hosoya's triangle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hosoya's triangle"}

**Hosoya's triangle** or the **Hosoya triangle** (originally **Fibonacci triangle**; ) is a triangular arrangement of numbers (like [Pascal's triangle](wikipedia://en/49497)) based on the [Fibonacci numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_number). Each number is the sum of the two numbers above in either the left diagonal or the right diagonal.[^o2]

:::

## Name {#Name}

The name "Fibonacci triangle" has also been used for triangles composed of Fibonacci numbers or related numbers[^o4] or triangles with Fibonacci sides and integral area,[^o5] hence is ambiguous.

## Recurrence {#Recurrence}

The numbers in this triangle obey the [recurrence relations](wikipedia://en/146806)

$$H(0,0)=H(1,0)=H(1,1)=H(2,1)=1$$

and

$$\begin {align} H(n,j)&=H(n-1,j)+H(n-2,j)\\ &=H(n-1,j-1)+H(n-2,j-2). \end {align}$$

## Relation to Fibonacci numbers {#Relation_to_Fibonacci_numbers}

The entries in the triangle satisfy the identity

$$H(n,i)=F(i+1)\cdot F(n-i+1)$$

Thus, the two outermost diagonals are the Fibonacci numbers, while the numbers on the middle vertical line are the squares of the Fibonacci numbers. All the other numbers in the triangle are the product of two distinct Fibonacci numbers greater than 1. The row sums are the first [convolved Fibonacci numbers](wikipedia://en/8006956#Convolved_Fibonacci_sequences).[^o8]
