# David Matravers

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Matravers"}

**David Matravers** (26 September 1937 – 31 May 2022) was a British mathematician and [cosmologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmologist) and Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Portsmouth). His research interests were in Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Cosmology.

:::

He founded the [Institute of Cosmology and Gravitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Cosmology_and_Gravitation,_University_of_Portsmouth) at the University of Portsmouth.[^o3][^o4]

## Biography {#Biography}

Following BSc and MSc studies at [Natal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Natal) and [UNISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Africa), he did his PhD at [Rhodes University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_University) in South Africa under the supervision of [Hanno Rund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanno_Rund) in 1972.

In 1990 David Matravers joined the [University of Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Portsmouth) as Head of School of Mathematical Studies, and became Head of School of Computer Science and Mathematics in 1997. At Portsmouth he founded a research group in General Relativity and Cosmology,[^o3] that in 2002 became the [Institute of Cosmology and Gravitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Cosmology_and_Gravitation,_University_of_Portsmouth).

## Publications {#Publications}

David Matravers published more than 50 research papers in [General Relativity](wikipedia://en/12024) and [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology).

In 2019 he published the book "How Cosmologists Explain the Universe to Friends and Family", with Karim A. Malik, ISBN 978-3-030-32733-0.
