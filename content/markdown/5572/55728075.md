# Antonella Grassi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Antonella Grassi"}

**Antonella Grassi** is an Italian[^o3] mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305). She is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Grassi received her Ph.D. from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) under the supervision of [David R. Morrison](wikipedia://en/40896470). Her dissertation was entitled "Minimal Models of Elliptic Threefolds."

## Career and service {#Career_and_service}

Grassi is currently Professor of Mathematics at [Univeristà degli studi di Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna). She has supervised two doctoral students, one at the University of Pennsylvania and the other at [Università di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) in [Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin).[^o5] She is an active participant in Women in Math at the University of Pennsylvania.[^o7]

Grassi has been a leader and mentor in the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) Program for Women in Mathematics; in particular, she organized the 2007 program on Algebraic Geometry and Group Actions.[^o8]

## Honors {#Honors}

Grassi was elected to the 2018 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). Her citation read "For contributions to algebraic geometry and mathematical physics, and for leadership in mentoring programs."[^o10]
