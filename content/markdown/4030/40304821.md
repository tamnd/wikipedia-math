::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Triangular tiling honeycomb"}

The **triangular tiling honeycomb** is one of 11 paracompact regular space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742). It is called *paracompact* because it has infinite [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,6,3}, being composed of [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) cells. Each edge of the honeycomb is surrounded by three cells, and each vertex is ideal with infinitely many cells meeting there. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719).

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The triangular tiling honeycomb is a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space, and one of eleven paracompact honeycombs.

There are [nine uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/39591384#.5B3.2C6.2C3.5D_family) in the \[3,6,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form as well as the [bitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncation_%28geometry%29) form, t<sub>1,2</sub>{3,6,3}, with all [truncated hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864030) facets.

The honeycomb is also part of a series of [polychora](wikipedia://en/18801624) and honeycombs with triangular [edge figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure).
