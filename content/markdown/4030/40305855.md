# Ernesto Padova

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernesto Padova"}

**Ernesto Padova** (17 February 1845 – 9 March 1896) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Livorno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livorno), Italy.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1866 but he was also a student of the Scuola Normale of Pisa. He first taught in a high school in [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples) and in 1872, having been put forward by [Enrico Betti](wikipedia://en/979158), he was appointed professor of [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) at the University of Pisa. From there he moved on to [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua), where he remained until his premature death.

He has been author of about fifty works in the fields of mathematical analysis, [analytical mechanics](wikipedia://en/454450) and mathematical-physics (elasticity and electromagnetism). Regards to analytical mechanics Padova has been one of the first to study matters concerning movement stability. In this [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discipline_%28academia%29), among his students was [Tullio Levi-Civita](wikipedia://en/499180).

In 1891 he also became a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).
