# Haruzo Hida

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Haruzo Hida"}

**Haruzo Hida** (肥田 晴三 *Hida Haruzo*, born 6 August 1952, [Sakai, Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakai,_Osaka)) is a Japanese mathematician, known for his [research in number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_in_Number_Theory), algebraic geometry, and modular forms.

:::

Hida received from [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) a B.A. in 1975, an M.A. in 1977, and a Ph.D. in 1980 with thesis *On Abelian Varieties with Complex Multiplication as Factors of the Jacobians of Shimura Curves*, although he left Kyoto University in 1977. He was from 1977 to 1984 an assistant professor and from 1984 to 1987 an associate professor at [Hokkaidō University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokkaidō_University). Since 1987 he has been a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). From 1979 to 1981 he was a [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).

Hida was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (Berkeley) in 1986. In 1991 he was awarded the [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship).[^o3] Hida received in 1992 for his research on p-adic L-functions of algebraic groups and p-adic Hecke rings the Spring Prize of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786).[^o4] In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5] He received the 2019 [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Seminal Contribution to Research for his highly original paper "Galois representations into GL<sub>2</sub>($\mathbb{Z}$<sub>p</sub>X) attached to ordinary cusp forms," published in 1986 in [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae).[^o6]
