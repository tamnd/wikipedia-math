# Grzegorz Rempala

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Grzegorz Rempala"}

**Grzegorz (“Greg”) A. Rempala** (Rempała; born March 19, 1968) is a Polish-American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969)[^o3] who works on the theory and applications of complex [stochastic systems](wikipedia://en/47895).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rempala studied mathematics at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) from 1987 to 1991, and worked at the Computer Science Institute of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) from 1991 to 1992. In 1992 he moved to the US where in 1996 he completed his PhD thesis at [Bowling Green State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green_State_University). His advisor was Prof. [Arjun K Gupta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjun_Kumar_Gupta).[^o5] In 1998 he nostrified his degree at the University of Warsaw in the Department of Mathematics. The chair of his nostrification committee was [Prof. Stanisław Kwapień.](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0ygvqQoAAAAJ&hl=en) In 2007 he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from [Warsaw University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University_of_Technology). From 1996 until 2008 Rempala was a professor (full professor from 2007) in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Louisville). In 2008 he joined the Department of Biostatistics at the [Medical College of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_College_of_Georgia) and in 2013 moved to The [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) (OSU) where he is currently a professor in the Division of Biostatistics and in the Department of Mathematics.[^o6] In 2016 he was named the interim director of the [Mathematical Biosciences Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences_Institute) at OSU, and served in this position until 2018. His wife, Helena Rempala, is a professor of clinical psychology in the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Health at OSU. They have two sons, Jaś and Antoś.

## Scientific interests {#Scientific_interests}

Rempala is known for his work on random matrices, in particular on a random [permanent function](wikipedia://en/20749642).[^o8] He has also established some results in nonparametric statistics related to [central limit theorems](wikipedia://en/39406) for products of random variables.[^o9] He has worked on mathematical models of chemical reactions,[^o10] diversity of molecular populations,[^o11] and disease spread across contact networks.[^o12] His more recent work focuses on dynamical survival analysis for epidemic models.[^o13] He is a collaborator of the IBS Biomedical Mathematics Group.[^o14]
