# Gregory Gutin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gregory Gutin"}

**Gregory Z. Gutin** (גרגורי גוטין; born 17 January 1957) is a scholar in theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics. He received his PhD in Mathematics in 1993 from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) under the supervision of [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651). Since September 2000 Gutin has been Professor in [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science) at [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London). In 2024 he was appointed Distinguished Scientist at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology for the period 2024–2026.[^o3]

:::

Gutin's research interests are in algorithms and complexity, [access control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) and [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Gutin was the recipient of the [Royal Society Wolfson Research Merit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Wolfson_Research_Merit_Award) in 2014,[^o9] and the best paper awards at SACMAT 2015,[^o10] 2016,[^o11] 2021[^o12] and 2022.[^o13] In January 2017 there was a workshop celebrating Gutin's 60th birthday.[^o14] In 2017, he became a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o15]
