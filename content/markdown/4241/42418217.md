# Peter Duren

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Duren"}

**Peter Larkin Duren** (April 30, 1935 – July 10, 2020)[^o3] was an American mathematician. He specialized in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and was known for the monographs and textbooks he has written.

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Duren received in 1956 his bachelor's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and in 1960 his Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT) under [Gian-Carlo Rota](wikipedia://en/101891) with thesis *Spectral theory of a class of non-self-adjoint infinite matrix operators*. As a postdoc he was an instructor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). At the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), he became in 1962 an assistant professor, in 1966 an associate professor, in 1969 a professor, and in 2010 a professor emeritus. As a professor, Duren served on the thesis committee of [Ted Kaczynski](wikipedia://en/18621887).[^o6]

Duren was in 1968/69 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), in 1975 a visiting professor at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) in Haifa, in 1964/65 a visiting scientist at [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) and the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in Orsay, in 1982 a visiting professor at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) and in 1982/83 at the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452), the University of Paris-Sud and at the [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich). In 1989 he was a visiting scientist at Stanford University, in 1993 at the University of Hawaii and in 1996 at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) in Trondheim. He has also been a visiting scientist in Halle, at the [Max-Planck Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-Planck_Institute) in Leipzig, at the [University of Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Witwatersrand), in Santiago de Chile, at the [Autonomous University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Madrid), at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) and the [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) in Beijing.

In 1976/77 he was chief editor of the *[Michigan Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Mathematical_Journal)*. He was a co-editor of the *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)* and a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) for [Frederick Gehring](wikipedia://en/35051046).

Duren's research and expository writing deals with function theory and functional analysis, including [Hardy spaces](wikipedia://en/314780), [schlicht functions](wikipedia://en/252075#Schlicht_functions), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [geometric function theory](wikipedia://en/5458932), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), and [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function).

Duren died on July 10, 2020, at the age of 85.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

From 1964 to 1966, Duren was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellow). In 2012, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- 1970: *Theory of $H^p$-Spaces*, Academic Press, Dover 2000
- 1983: [Univalent Functions](https://books.google.com/books/about/Univalent_Functions.html?id=8mVIaZmsy0gC), Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften, Springer Verlag[^o10]
- 1988: (as editor with [Richard A. Askey](wikipedia://en/2836040) and [Uta C. Merzbach](wikipedia://en/56688455)) *A Century of Mathematics in America*, 3 vols., [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (Centenary of the AMS)[^o11]
- 2004: *Harmonic Maps in the Plane*, [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press)
- 2004: (with Alexander Schuster) [*Bergman Spaces*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Bergman_Spaces.html?id=5IAWL8MUGtQC), American Mathematical Society[^o12]
- 2012: [Invitation to Classical Analysis](https://books.google.com/books/about/Invitation_to_Classical_Analysis.html?id=Jyov-EMgbC0C), American Mathematical Society
