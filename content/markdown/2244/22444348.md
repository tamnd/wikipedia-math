# Louis Billera

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Billera"}

**Louis Joseph Billera** is a Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Billera completed his B.S. at the [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) in 1964. He earned his Ph.D. from the [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York) in 1968, under the joint supervision of Moses Richardson and [Michel Balinski](wikipedia://en/41213698).

Louis Billera served as the first Associate Director of the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) [Center for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science](wikipedia://en/3684858) (DIMACS) at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

In 2010 he gave the invited lecture, "*Flag enumeration in polytopes, Eulerian partially ordered sets and Coxeter groups*" at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad).[^o5]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

The common thread through much of his research is to study problems motivated by [discrete](wikipedia://en/386468) and [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882). A sampling includes constructing [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) to prove the sufficiency condition for the [g-theorem](wikipedia://en/22359636) (with Carl Lee), discovering fiber polytopes (with [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels)), and studying the space of [phylogenetic trees](wikipedia://en/149326) (with [Susan Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_P._Holmes) and [Karen Vogtmann](wikipedia://en/20455422)).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1994 Billera won the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) for his paper, *Homology of smooth splines*. This prize is given every three years to the best paper in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492).[^o8]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
