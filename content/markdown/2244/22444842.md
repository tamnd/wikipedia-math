# Monk's formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monk's formula"}

In mathematics, **Monk's formula**, found by (Monk 1959), is an analogue of [Pieri's formula](wikipedia://en/22269627) that describes the product of a linear [Schubert polynomial](wikipedia://en/31925388) by a Schubert polynomial. Equivalently, it describes the product of a special [Schubert cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schubert_cycle) by a Schubert cycle in the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of a [flag manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_manifold).

:::

Write *t*<sub>ij</sub> for the [transposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transposition_%28mathematics%29) *(i j)*, and *s*<sub>i</sub> = *t*<sub>i,i+1</sub>. Then 𝔖<sub>s<sub>r</sub></sub> = *x*<sub>1</sub> + ⋯ + *x*<sub>r</sub>, and Monk's formula states that for a permutation *w*,

$$\mathfrak{S}_{s_r} \mathfrak{S}_w = \sum_{{i \leq r < j} \atop {\ell(wt_{ij}) = \ell(w)+1}} \mathfrak{S}_{wt_{ij}},$$

where $\ell(w)$ is the [length](wikipedia://en/18542) of *w*. The pairs (*i*, *j*) appearing in the sum are exactly those such that *i* ≤ *r* \< *j*, *w*<sub>i</sub> \< *w*<sub>j</sub>, and there is no *i* \< *k* \< *j* with *w*<sub>i</sub> \< *w*<sub>k</sub> \< *w*<sub>j</sub>; each *wt*<sub>ij</sub> is a cover of *w* in [Bruhat order](wikipedia://en/10163003).
