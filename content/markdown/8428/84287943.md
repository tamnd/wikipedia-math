# Chiara Saffirio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chiara Saffirio"}

**Chiara Saffirio** (born 9 September 1985 in Rome, Italy) is an Italian mathematician working in kinetic theory and classical and quantum systems. She has been Full Professor of Mathematics at [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) since 2025.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chiara Saffirio studied at [University of Rome La Sapienza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_La_Sapienza), where she received her PhD degree in Mathematics in 2012.[^o4]

Later, Saffirio worked as postdoctoral researcher in the [Hausdorff Center for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), Germany, under the supervision of [Benjamin Schlein](wikipedia://en/76114592), and as an SNF Ambizione fellow at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich), Switzerland.[^o4] In 2019 she was appointed SNF Eccellenza Assistant Professor at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), Switzerland.[^o4] At the University of Basel Saffirio was appointed as head of the mathematical physics research group.[^o9]

In 2025 Saffirio become Associate Professor and Canada Research Chair of Complex Quantum Systems at the Mathematics Department of the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), Vancouver, Canada. In October 2025 Saffirio become full professor of Pure Mathematics at [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg), Germany.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Chiara Saffirio conducts research which lies at the interplay of analysis and mathematical physics. It focuses on kinetic theory, scaling limits in interacting particle systems (classical and quantum), semiclassical analysis and theoretical aspects of partial differential equations.[^o7]

## Awards and Honours {#Awards_and_Honours}

- In 2021 Saffirio received the [IUPAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IUPAP) Young Scientist Prize "for her important contributions towards the mathematical understanding of the dynamics of classical and quantum many-body systems, leading to rigorous derivations of effective evolution equations".[^o9]
- In 2022 Saffirio received an [ERC Starting Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ERC_Starting_Grant) as Principal Investigator (PI) of the "Advances in Effective Evolution Equations for Classical and Quantum Systems" project.[^o10]
- Saffirio has spoken at many prestigious events as the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematics and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematics_and_Physics) (ESI) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) (2025), the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) (INI) in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge), and the Kinetic Theory, Fluid Mechanics and Microscopic Systems conference in Rome (2026). In 2026 Saffirio was invited as plenary speaker in May conference, an event featured by the [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) (EWM).[^o11]
