::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Oblique lattice"}

The **oblique lattice** is one of the five two-dimensional [Bravais lattice](wikipedia://en/661808) types.[^o3] The [symmetry](wikipedia://en/53741) category of the lattice is [wallpaper group](wikipedia://en/386214) p2. The primitive translation vectors of the oblique lattice form an angle other than 90° and are of unequal lengths.

:::
