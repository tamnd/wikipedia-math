# Otto H. Kegel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Otto H. Kegel"}

**Otto Helmut Kegel** (; 20 July 1934 – 20 July 2025) was a German mathematician who worked on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He was a professor at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg).

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Kegel was born on 20 July 1934 in [Bethlehem, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Pennsylvania).[^o4][^o5] His parents were German, and the family returned to Germany in 1938.[^o6] He attended high school in [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main).[^o6]

Kegel received his PhD from [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt) in 1961, with a thesis titled *Vertauschbarkeit von Untergruppen und Kompositionsstruktur in endlichen Gruppen* (*Commutability of subgroups and composition structure in finite groups*) and supervised by [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114). He completed his habilitation at the same institution in 1966.[^o10]

In 1962, Kegel gave a talk titled *Locally finite groups with a partition* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), Sweden.[^o8]

Kegel started at [Queen Mary College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_College) in the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) as a reader in 1968, and was promoted to professor in 1970.[^o6] He moved to the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) beginning in 1975, where he worked as a professor until his retirement in 1999.[^o6][^o10][^o9] He was an editor for *[Archiv der Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archiv_der_Mathematik)* from 1975 until 1990.[^o10][^o15]

## Death {#Death}

Kegel died on 20 July 2025, his 91st birthday.[^o12]

## Research {#Research}

Kegel worked in the field of group theory, a part of [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). His book on [locally finite groups](wikipedia://en/4939270), written with Bertram Wehrfritz, was influential in the subfield.[^o6] [Prime graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_graph) are also known as Gruenberg-Kegel graphs due to early unpublished work of Kegel with [Karl W. Gruenberg](wikipedia://en/57806841) on the topic.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1985, *Archiv der Mathematik* dedicated an issue to Kegel, in honor of his contributions to the journal as an author, editor, and on the advisory board.[^o15] A conference *Algebra and Group Theory* was held in Kegel's honor at the [University of Upper Alsace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Upper_Alsace) in 2015.[^o16] The 2024 edition of the Ischia Group Theory conference was also dedicated to Kegel, marking his 90th birthday.[^o17]
