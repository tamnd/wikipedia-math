# Cubic mean

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic mean"}

The **cubic mean** (written as $\bar{x}_\mathrm{cubic}$) is a specific instance of the [generalized mean](wikipedia://en/13143) with $p=3$.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

For $n$ [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) $x_i \in \mathbb R$ the cubic mean is defined as:

$\bar{x}_\mathrm{cubic} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n{x_i^3}} = \sqrt[3]{{x_1^3 + x_2^3 + \cdots + x_n^3} \over n}.$ [^o4][^o5][^o6]

For example, the cubic mean of two numbers is:

$$\sqrt[3]{\frac{x_1^3+x_2^3}{2}}.$$

:::

::: {#o8 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The cubic mean is used to predict the [life expectancy of machine parts](wikipedia://en/348898).[^o6][^o9][^o10][^o11]

The cubic mean wind speed has been used a measure of local potential for [wind energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_energy).[^o12]

The cubic mean is also used in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) to measure the mean dimensions of spherical [bacteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacteria) ([cocci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocci))[^o13] and of larger animals that are approximately spheroidal in shape.[^o14] In this case using the conventional [arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/612) will not give an accurate result because the size of a spherical bacterium increases as the *cube* of the radius.

:::
