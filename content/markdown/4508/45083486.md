# William Crawley-Boevey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Crawley-Boevey"}

*Image: William Crawley-Boevey.jpg.* William Crawley-Boevey at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2014

**William Walstan Crawley-Boevey** (born 1960)[^o2] is an English mathematician. Since 2016, he has been [Alexander von Humboldt Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Professor) at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), on leave from his position as [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Pure Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_Mathematics) at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds).[^o3] His research concerns [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and the theory of [quivers](wikipedia://en/355100).

:::

Crawley-Boevey is the second son of [Sir Thomas Crawley-Boevey, 8th Baronet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawley-Boevey_baronets).[^o2] He studied at the [City of London School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_School) and read mathematics at [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge). He received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1986 from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) under the supervision of [Stephen Donkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Donkin). Before his appointment in Leeds, he held post-doctoral positions at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

He was the 1991 winner of the [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o5] In 2006, Crawley-Boevey presented an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o6] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
