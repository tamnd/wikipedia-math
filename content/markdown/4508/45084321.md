# Timeline of class field theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of class field theory"}

In mathematics, [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) is the study of abelian extensions of local and global fields.

:::

## Timeline {#Timeline}

- 1801 [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) proves the [law of quadratic reciprocity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_quadratic_reciprocity)
- 1829 [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573) uses special values of the lemniscate function to construct abelian extensions of $\mathbb{Q}(i)$.
- 1837 [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/101453).
- 1853 [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450) announces the [Kronecker–Weber theorem](wikipedia://en/1082550)
- 1880 Kronecker introduces his [Jugendtraum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jugendtraum) about abelian extensions of imaginary quadratic fields
- 1886 [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615) proves the Kronecker–Weber theorem (with a slight gap).
- 1896 [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) gives the first complete proof of the Kronecker–Weber theorem.
- 1897 Weber introduces ray class groups and general ideal class groups.
- 1897 Hilbert publishes his [Zahlbericht](wikipedia://en/33462303).
- 1897 Hilbert rewrites the law of quadratic reciprocity as a product formula for the [Hilbert symbol](wikipedia://en/5924217).
- 1897 [Kurt Hensel](wikipedia://en/2242565) introduced *p*-adic numbers.
- 1898 Hilbert conjectures the existence and properties of the (narrow) Hilbert class field, proving them in the special case of class number 2.
- 1907 [Philipp Furtwängler](wikipedia://en/895349) proves existence and basic properties of the Hilbert class field.
- 1908 Weber defines the class field of a general ideal class group.
- 1920 [Teiji Takagi](wikipedia://en/174754) shows that the abelian extensions of a number field are exactly the class fields of ideal class groups.
- 1922 Takagi's paper on reciprocity laws
- 1923 [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118) introduced the [Hasse principle](wikipedia://en/489707) (for the special case of quadratic forms).
- 1923 [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) conjectures his reciprocity law.
- 1924 Artin introduces [Artin L-functions](wikipedia://en/2774171).
- 1926 [Nikolai Chebotaryov](wikipedia://en/3033696) proves his [density theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebotarev's_density_theorem).
- 1927 Artin proves his reciprocity law giving a canonical isomorphism between Galois groups and ideal class groups.
- 1930 Furtwängler and Artin prove the [principal ideal theorem](wikipedia://en/3136164).
- 1930 Hasse introduces [local class field theory](wikipedia://en/669532).
- 1931 Hasse proves the [Hasse norm theorem](wikipedia://en/1108795).
- 1931 Hasse classifies simple algebras over local fields.
- 1931 [Jacques Herbrand](wikipedia://en/159901) introduces the [Herbrand quotient](wikipedia://en/6043076).
- 1931 The [Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem](wikipedia://en/23502488) proves the Hasse principle for simple algebras over global fields.
- 1933 Hasse classifies simple algebras over number fields.
- 1934 [Max Deuring](wikipedia://en/5638258) and [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) develop class field theory using algebras.
- 1936 [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040) introduces [ideles](wikipedia://en/1168608#Ideles).
- 1940 Chevalley uses ideles to give an algebraic proof of the second inequality for abelian extensions.
- 1948 [Shianghao Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xianghao) proves the [Grunwald–Wang theorem](wikipedia://en/20901695), correcting an error of Grunwald's.
- 1950 [Tate's thesis](wikipedia://en/3149490) uses analysis on adele rings to study zeta functions.
- 1951 [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) introduces [Weil groups](wikipedia://en/6375618).
- 1952 Artin and Tate introduce [class formations](wikipedia://en/6019404) in their notes on class field theory.
- 1952 [Gerhard Hochschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_Hochschild) and [Tadashi Nakayama](wikipedia://en/17772141) introduce [group cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635) into class field theory.
- 1952 [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933) introduces [Tate cohomology groups](wikipedia://en/6070279).
- 1964 [Evgeny Golod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeny_Golod) and [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815) prove that the [class field tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_field_tower) can be infinite.
- 1965 [Jonathan Lubin](wikipedia://en/33546642) and Tate use [Lubin–Tate formal group laws](wikipedia://en/31457610) to construct ramified abelian extensions of local fields.
