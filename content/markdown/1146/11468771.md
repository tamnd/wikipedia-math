# Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī"}

*Image: Kazi Zade Rumi Mausoleum 01.jpg.* Qāḍī Zāda al-Rūmī Mausoleum in [Shah-i-Zinda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah-i-Zinda), [Samarkand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarkand)

**ar** (1364 in [Bursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursa), [Ottoman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire) – 1436 in [Samarqand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarqand), [Timurid Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timurid_Empire)), whose actual name was **Salah al-Din Musa Pasha** (*ar* means "son of the judge", *al-rūmī* "the Roman" indicating he came from [Asia Minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor), which was once [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire)), was a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked at the [observatory in Samarkand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulugh_Beg_Observatory). He computed sin 1° to an accuracy of 10<sup>−12</sup>.

:::

Together with [Ulugh Beg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulugh_Beg), [al-Kāshī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Kāshī) and a few other astronomers, Qāḍī Zāda produced the *[Zij-i-Sultani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zij-i-Sultani)*, the first comprehensive stellar catalogue since the [Maragheh observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maragheh_observatory)'s *[Zij-i Ilkhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zij-i_Ilkhani)* two centuries earlier. The *Zij-i Sultani* contained the positions of 992 stars.

## His works {#His_works}

- *Sharh al-Mulakhkhas* (Commentary on Jaghmini's compendium on the science of Astronomy)
- *Sharh Ashkal al-Ta'sis* (Commentary on Samarkandi's Arithmetics)
