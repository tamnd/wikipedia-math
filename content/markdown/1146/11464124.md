# Lyubomir Ivanov (explorer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lyubomir Ivanov"}

*Image: Lyubomir-Ivanov.jpg.* Self-portrait, 2004

**Lyubomir Ivanov** (Любомир Иванов, born 7 October 1952 in [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia)) is a Bulgarian scientist, non-governmental activist, and [Antarctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctica) explorer. He is a graduate of the [St. Kliment Ohridski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Kliment_Ohridski) [University of Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_University) with M.S. degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1977. He earned his PhD from [Sofia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_University) in 1980, under the direction of [Dimiter Skordev](wikipedia://en/11557611) with a dissertation titled *Iterative Operative Spaces*, and was the 1987 winner of *Acad. Nikola Obreshkov Prize*, the highest Bulgarian award in mathematics.[^o2]

:::

## Academic and NGO work {#Academic_and_NGO_work}

Appointed head of the [Department of Mathematical Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Mathematical_Logic_%28Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences%29) at the [Institute of Mathematics and Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Informatics_%28Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences%29), [Bulgarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1990, Ivanov has since helped found the [Atlantic Club of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Club_of_Bulgaria), in which he held the position of chairman from 2001 to 2009. In 1994, he founded the [Manfred Wörner Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manfred_Wörner_Foundation), an organisation dedicated to trans-atlantic co-operation. He was a member of the Streit Council Advisory Board, Washington, DC since 2006[^o4] and, founding Chairman, [Antarctic Place-names Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctic_Place-names_Commission) since 1994.[^o5][^o6] He authored the modern Bulgarian system for [Romanization of Cyrillic alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Bulgarian),[^o7] adopted also for official use both by UN,[^o8] and also by the US and UK.[^o9]

In the course of his work for, among others, the Atlantic Club of Bulgaria, Ivanov has given interviews to various news outlets, at times espousing views that NATO must expand eastwards due to a deficit in its military capacity.[^o10]

## Political career {#Political_career}

Ivanov was a member of the [UDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Democratic_Forces_%28Bulgaria%29) Coordinating Council[^o12] and took part in the 1990 [Bulgarian Round Table Talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Round_Table_Talks).[^o13] He served as a Member of Parliament in Bulgaria (1990–1991), acting as Chairman of the Green Party parliamentary group, and co-authored the current [Constitution of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Bulgaria). He has also served as parliamentary secretary for the Bulgarian ministry of foreign affairs.[^o14]

## Antarctic expeditions {#Antarctic_expeditions}

Ivanov has taken part in several Antarctic expeditions. In 2004, Ivanov went with [Doychin Vasilev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doychin_Vasilev) on the [Tangra 2004/05](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangra_2004/05) topographic expedition, noted by [Discovery Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discovery_Channel_%28UK_and_Ireland%29), the [Natural History Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_History_Museum,_London), the [Royal Collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Collection) and the [British Antarctic Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Antarctic_Survey) as a timeline event in Antarctic exploration.[^o16][^o17][^o18] Awarded the jubilee medal *30th Bulgarian Antarctic Expedition* by the [Bulgarian Antarctic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Antarctic_Institute) for his active participation in the expeditions and in the building of [St. Kliment Ohridski polar base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Kliment_Ohridski_base). He took part, inter alia, in the first Bulgarian landing on [Smith Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_Island_%28South_Shetland_Islands%29) in 2025.[^o19] [^o20][^o21]
