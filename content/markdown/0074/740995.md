# Otto Hesse

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Hesse"}

**Ludwig Otto Hesse** (22 April 1811 – 4 August 1874) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Hesse was born in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg), [Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia), and died in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), [Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bavaria). He worked mainly on [algebraic invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_invariant), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). The [Hessian matrix](wikipedia://en/412108), the [Hesse normal form](wikipedia://en/20665580), the [Hesse configuration](wikipedia://en/35282956), the [Hessian group](wikipedia://en/35283230), [Hessian pairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hessian_pair), [Hesse's theorem](wikipedia://en/35654252), [Hesse pencil](wikipedia://en/35283082), and the [Hesse transfer principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesse_transfer_principle)[^o3] are named after him. Many of Hesse's research findings were presented for the first time in *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)* or Hesse's textbooks.[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hesse was born in Königsberg (today [Kaliningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaliningrad)) as the son of Johann Gottlieb Hesse, a businessman and brewery owner and his wife Anna Karoline Reiter (1788–1865). He studied in his hometown at the [Albertina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) under [Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi](wikipedia://en/210340). Among his teachers were count [Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel](wikipedia://en/11574) and [Friedrich Julius Richelot](wikipedia://en/20346321). He earned his doctorate in 1840 at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) with the dissertation *De octo punctis intersectionis trium superficium secundi ordinis*. In 1841, Hesse completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) thesis. In the same year he married Sophie Marie Emilie Dulk, the daughter of pharmacists and chemistry professor [Friedrich Philipp Dulk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Philipp_Dulk) (1788–1852). The couple had a son and five daughters. Hesse taught for some time physics and chemistry at the Vocational School in Königsberg and lectured at the Albertina. In 1845 he was appointed associate professor in Königsberg. In 1855 he moved to Halle and in 1856 to Heidelberg until 1868, when he finally moved to [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich) to the newly established [Polytechnic School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich). In 1869 he joined the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

His doctoral students include [Olaus Henrici](wikipedia://en/32096509), [Gustav Kirchhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Kirchhoff), [Jacob Lüroth](wikipedia://en/33271640), [Adolph Mayer](wikipedia://en/17419801), [Carl Neumann](wikipedia://en/915551), [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337), [Ernst Schröder](wikipedia://en/448015), and [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615).
