# Lev Schnirelmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lev Schnirelmann"}

**Lev Genrikhovich Schnirelmann** (also **Shnirelman**, **Shnirel'man**; Лев Ге́нрихович Шнирельма́н; 2 January 1905 – 24 September 1938) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Schnirelmann sought to prove [Goldbach's conjecture](wikipedia://en/44758). In 1930, using the [Brun sieve](wikipedia://en/18731525), he proved that any [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) greater than 1 can be written as the sum of not more than *C* [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666), where *C* is an effectively computable constant.[^o4][^o5]

His other fundamental work is joint with [Lazar Lyusternik](wikipedia://en/4201372). Together, they developed the *[Lusternik–Schnirelmann category](wikipedia://en/764433)*, as it is called now, based on the previous work by [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740), [George David Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/30871906), and [Marston Morse](wikipedia://en/856523). The theory gives a global invariant of spaces, and has led to advances in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). They also proved the [theorem of the three geodesics](wikipedia://en/48445265), that a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) topologically equivalent to a sphere has at least three simple [closed geodesics](wikipedia://en/19903176).

## Biography {#Biography}

Schnirelmann graduated from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1925 and then worked at the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) from 1934 to 1938. His advisor was [Nikolai Luzin](wikipedia://en/576228).

Schnirelmann committed suicide in Moscow on 24 September 1938, for reasons that are not clear. According to [Lev Pontryagin](wikipedia://en/582075)'s memoir from 1998, Schnirelmann gassed himself, due to depression brought on by feelings of inability to work at the same high level as earlier in his career.[^o7] On the other hand, according to an interview [Eugene Dynkin](wikipedia://en/1351540) gave in 1988, Schnirelman took his own life after the [NKVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD) tried to recruit him as an informer.[^o9]
