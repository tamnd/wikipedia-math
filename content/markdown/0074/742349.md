# Square wheel

*Image: Rolling-Square colored.gif.* An animation of a square wheel rolling smoothly over inverted [catenaries](wikipedia://en/7163).

*Image: Thinktank Science Garden - car with square wheels (13900854766).jpg.* A prototype square wheeled car with modified track at [Thinktank Science Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinktank,_Birmingham_Science_Museum).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Square wheel"}

A **square wheel** can roll smoothly if the ground consists of evenly shaped inverted [catenaries](wikipedia://en/7163) of the right size and curvature.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

A different type of square-wheeled vehicle was invented in 2006 by Jason Winckler of Global Composites, Inc. in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States). This has square wheels, linked together and offset by 22.5°, rolling on a flat surface. The prototype appears ungainly, but the inventor proposes that the system may be useful in microscopic-sized machines ([MEMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microelectromechanical_systems)).[^o5] In 1997 Macalester College mathematics professor [Stan Wagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Wagon) constructed the first prototype of a catenary tricycle. An improved model made out of modern materials was built when the original vehicle wore out in April, 2004.[^o6]

In 2012, *[MythBusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MythBusters_%282012_season%29#Square_Wheels)* experimented with modifying vehicles with square tires, determining that, with speed, a truck fitted with square wheels can deliver a relatively smooth ride.[^o7]
