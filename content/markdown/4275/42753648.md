::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrahedral-icosahedral honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **tetrahedral-icosahedral honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), and [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) cells, in an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring [Coxeter diagram](wikipedia://en/5358554) , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::

It represents a [semiregular honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_honeycomb) as defined by all regular cells, although from the Wythoff construction, the octahedron comes from the rectified tetrahedron .
