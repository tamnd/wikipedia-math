In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **cubic-icosahedral honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), and [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) cells, in an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.
