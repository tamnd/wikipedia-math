# Felix Finster

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Finster"}

*Image: Felix Finster.jpg.* Felix Finster (2009)

**Felix Finster** (born 6 August 1967, in [Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannheim)) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on problems in mathematical physics, [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Finster studied physics and mathematics at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), where he graduated in 1992 with [Franz Wegner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Wegner) and [Claus Gerhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claus_Gerhardt). In 1992–1995 he wrote his PhD thesis at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) with [Konrad Osterwalder](wikipedia://en/19188374).[^o3] In 1996–1998 he was post-doc at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). From 1998–2002 he was member of the [Max Planck Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute) for Mathematics in the Sciences in Leipzig. He got his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2000 at [Leipzig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University). Since 2002 he is full professor of mathematics at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg).

Finster works mainly on mathematical problems in [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024) and [quantum theory](wikipedia://en/25202). More specifically, in collaboration with [Niky Kamran](wikipedia://en/45345773), [Joel Smoller](wikipedia://en/60496463) and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) he studies the dynamics of waves in a [black hole](wikipedia://en/4650) geometry. Moreover, he developed the fermionic projector approach to quantum field theory. In this context, he formulated the [causal action principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Causal_action_principle). In recent years, the approach was extended to so-called [causal fermion systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Causal_fermion_system), which form a mathematical framework for describing [quantum geometries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_geometry).

Finster is married and has four children.
