# Monopole moduli space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monopole moduli space"}

In mathematics, the **monopole moduli space** is a space parametrizing monopoles (solutions of the [Bogomolny equations](wikipedia://en/35175454)). (txt) studied the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) for 2 monopoles in detail and used it to describe the scattering of monopoles.

:::
