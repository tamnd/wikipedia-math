# Connection (affine bundle)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Connection"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the classical notion of a [connection (mathematics)](wikipedia://en/308815), most commonly encountered in the context of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/391816), can be generalised to [affine bundles](wikipedia://en/39358329). Let $Y \to X$ be an affine bundle modelled over a vector bundle $\overline{Y} \to X$. A [connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_%28fibred_manifold%29) $\Gamma$ on $Y \to X$ is called an **affine connection** if, as a section $\Gamma : Y \to J^{1}Y$ of the [jet bundle](wikipedia://en/928060) $J^{1}Y \to Y$ of $Y$, it is an affine bundle morphism over $X$.

:::

The term "affine connection" as used in this article should not be confused with [its more common usage](wikipedia://en/607690), namely a connection on the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) $TX$ of a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) $X$, though as discussed below, the latter can be considered as a special example of the former.

With respect to affine bundle coordinates $(x^{\lambda}, y^{i})$ on $Y$, an affine connection $\Gamma$ on $Y \to X$ is given by the [tangent-valued connection form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_%28fibred_manifold%29)

$$\begin{aligned}\Gamma &=dx^\lambda\otimes \left(\partial_\lambda + \Gamma_\lambda^i\partial_i\right)\,, \\ \Gamma_\lambda^i&={{\Gamma_\lambda}^i}_j\left(x^\nu\right) y^j + \sigma_\lambda^i\left(x^\nu\right)\,. \end{aligned}$$

An affine bundle is a fiber bundle with a [general affine](wikipedia://en/298828) [structure group](wikipedia://en/276526) $\operatorname{GA}(m, \mathbb{R})$ of affine transformations of its typical fiber $V$ of dimension $m$. Therefore, an affine connection is associated to a [principal connection](wikipedia://en/470632). It always exists.

For any affine connection $\Gamma : Y \to J^{1}Y$, the corresponding [linear derivative](wikipedia://en/39358329) $\overline{\Gamma} : \overline{Y} \to J^{1}\overline{Y}$ of an affine morphism $\Gamma$ defines a unique [linear connection](wikipedia://en/391816) on a vector bundle $\overline{Y} \to X$. With respect to linear bundle coordinates $(x^{\lambda}, \overline{y}^{i})$ on $\overline{Y}$, this connection reads

$$\overline \Gamma=dx^\lambda\otimes\left(\partial_\lambda +{{\Gamma_\lambda}^i}_j\left(x^\nu\right) \overline y^j\overline\partial_i\right)\,.$$

Since every vector bundle is an affine bundle, any linear connection on a vector bundle also is an affine connection.

If $Y \to X$ is a vector bundle, both an affine connection $\Gamma$ and an associated linear connection $\overline{\Gamma}$ are connections on the same vector bundle $Y \to X$, and their difference is a basic soldering form on

$$\sigma= \sigma_\lambda^i(x^\nu) dx^\lambda\otimes\partial_i \,.$$

Thus, every affine connection on a vector bundle $Y \to X$ is a sum of a linear connection and a basic soldering form on $Y \to X$.

Due to the canonical vertical splitting $VY = Y \times Y$, this soldering form is brought into a [vector-valued form](wikipedia://en/3772116)

$$\sigma= \sigma_\lambda^i(x^\nu) dx^\lambda\otimes e_i$$

where $e_{i}$ is a fiber basis for $Y$.

Given an affine connection $\Gamma$ on a vector bundle $Y \to X$, let $R$ and $\overline{R}$ be the [curvatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_%28fibred_manifold%29) of a connection $\Gamma$ and the associated linear connection $\overline{\Gamma}$, respectively. It is readily observed that $R = \overline{R} + T$, where

$$\begin{aligned} T &=\tfrac12 T_{\lambda\mu}^i dx^\lambda\wedge dx^\mu\otimes \partial_i\,, \\ T_{\lambda \mu}^i &= \partial_\lambda\sigma_\mu^i - \partial_\mu\sigma_\lambda^i + \sigma_\lambda^h {{\Gamma_\mu}^i}_h - \sigma_\mu^h {{\Gamma_\lambda}^i}_h\,, \end{aligned}$$

is the [torsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_%28fibred_manifold%29) of $\Gamma$ with respect to the basic soldering form $\sigma$.

In particular, consider the tangent bundle $TX$ of a manifold $X$ coordinated by $(x^{\mu}, \text{ẋ}^{\mu})$. There is the canonical soldering form

$$\theta=dx^\mu\otimes \dot\partial_\mu$$

on $TX$ which coincides with the [tautological one-form](wikipedia://en/1855974)

$$\theta_X=dx^\mu\otimes \partial_\mu$$

on $X$ due to the canonical vertical splitting $\operatorname{VT}X = TX \times TX$. Given an arbitrary linear connection $\Gamma$ on $TX$, the corresponding affine connection

$$\begin{aligned} A&=\Gamma +\theta\,, \\ A_\lambda^\mu&={{\Gamma_\lambda}^\mu}_\nu \dot x^\nu +\delta^\mu_\lambda\,, \end{aligned}$$

on $TX$ is the [Cartan connection](wikipedia://en/412272). The torsion of the Cartan connection $A$ with respect to the soldering form $\theta$ coincides with the [torsion](wikipedia://en/4342484) of a linear connection $\Gamma$, and its curvature is a sum $R + T$ of the curvature and the torsion of $\Gamma$.
