# Yurii Reshetnyak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yurii Reshetnyak"}

**Yurii Grigorievich Reshetnyak** (Ю́рий Григо́рьевич Решетня́к, 26 September 1929 – 17 December 2021) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician and academician.[^o3]

:::

He worked in geometry and the theory of functions of a real variable. He was known for his work in the [Reshetnyak gluing theorem](wikipedia://en/40823382). Reshetnyak received the 2000 [Lobachevsky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobachevsky_Prize) from the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]

Reshetnyak died on 17 December 2021, at the age of 92.[^o5]
