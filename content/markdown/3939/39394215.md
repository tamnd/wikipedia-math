# Jan Hogendijk

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Hogendijk"}

**Jan Pieter Hogendijk** (born 21 July 1955) is a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [historian of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science). Since 2005, he is professor of history of mathematics at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht).

:::

Hogendijk became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2010.[^o3]

Hogendijk has contributed to the study of [Greek mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_mathematics) and [mathematics in medieval Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_in_medieval_Islam); he provides a list of Sources on his website (below). In 2012, he was awarded the inaugural [Otto Neugebauer Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Neugebauer_Prize) for History of Mathematics, by the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470), "for having illuminated how Greek mathematics was absorbed in the medieval Arabic world, how mathematics developed in medieval Islam, and how it was eventually transmitted to Europe."[^o4]

A [bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliography) of Hogendijk's publications is included in his website.
