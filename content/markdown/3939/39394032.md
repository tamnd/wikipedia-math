# Fred van der Blij

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fred van der Blij"}

**Frederik van der Blij** (13 May 1923 – 27 January 2018)[^o3] was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). From 1955 until his retirement in 1988 he was professor at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht). His research focused on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), among other fields.[^o4]

:::
