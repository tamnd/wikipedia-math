# Von Neumann's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Von Neumann's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **von Neumann's theorem** is a result in the [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) of [linear operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) on [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $G$ and $H$ be Hilbert spaces, and let $T : \operatorname{dom}(T) \subseteq G \to H$ be an [unbounded operator](wikipedia://en/1422584) from $G$ into $H.$ Suppose that $T$ is a [closed operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_operator) and that $T$ is [densely defined](wikipedia://en/13510193), that is, $\operatorname{dom}(T)$ is [dense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dense_%28topology%29) in $G.$ Let $T^* : \operatorname{dom}\left(T^*\right) \subseteq H \to G$ denote the [adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_operator) of $T.$ Then $T^* T$ is also densely defined, and it is [self-adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-adjoint). That is,

$$\left(T^* T\right)^* = T^* T$$

and the operators on the right- and left-hand sides have the same dense domain in $G.$[^o4]

:::
