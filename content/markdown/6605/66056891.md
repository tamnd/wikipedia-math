# Damir Filipović

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Damir Filipović"}

**Damir Filipović** (born 1970 in Switzerland) is a Swiss mathematician specializing in [quantitative finance](wikipedia://en/33589680). He holds the Swissquote Chair in Quantitative Finance and is the director of the [Swiss Finance Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Finance_Institute) at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Filipović studied mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and earned his Master's degree in 1995. He joined [Freddy Delbaen](wikipedia://en/72871254) as PhD student and graduated in 2000 with thesis on mathematical finance titled "Consistency problems for HJM interest rate models".[^o7]

As a postdoctoral [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) he joined [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) (2000), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (2001) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (2001) to work on consistency problems for [Heath-Jarrow-Morton](wikipedia://en/2876834) interest rate models[^o8] and affine processes and applications in finance.[^o9]

From 2002 to 2003, he was an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University)'s Department of [Operations Research](wikipedia://en/43476) and Financial Engineering. As scientific consultant for solvency testing and [risk analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management) in insurance ([Swiss Solvency Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Solvency_Test)) he joined the Swiss Federal Office of Private Insurance (BPV) in 2003. In 2004, he became full professor on the Chair of Financial and Insurance Mathematics at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich). In 2007, he was appointed as director of the Vienna Institute of Finance and full professor at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna).[^o10]

Since 2010, he has been the Swissquote Chair in Quantitative Finance and director of the [Swiss Finance Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Finance_Institute) at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Filipović's research focuses on quantitative finance by drawing in an interdisciplinary manner on fields such as [quantitative finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), [quantitative risk management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management), and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) in finance. It aims at both the advancement of theoretical understanding of [financial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_engineering), and its implementation in the [financial industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_services) and governmental policies.[^o12] His research interests encompass polynomial processes and applications in finance,[^o13] [systemic risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_risk) in financial networks,[^o14][^o15] Interest rates,[^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20] [credit risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_risk),[^o21][^o22] [stochastic volatility](wikipedia://en/5740025),[^o23][^o24] [Stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895),[^o25][^o26][^o27][^o28] quantitative risk management and regulation,[^o29][^o30] and machine learning in finance.[^o31][^o32]

He also teaches a [MOOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massive_open_online_course) on "Interest Rate Models" on [Coursera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coursera).[^o33]

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

Filipović is the 2016 recipient of the [Louis Bachelier Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bachelier_Prize) awarded by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), the Natixis Foundation for Quantitative Research and the [Société de Mathématiques Appliquées et Industrielles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_Mathématiques_Appliquées_et_Industrielles).[^o35]

He is a member and former president (2016–2017) of the Bachelier finance society.[^o36]
