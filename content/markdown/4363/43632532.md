::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Icosahedral pyramid"}

The **icosahedral pyramid** is a four-dimensional [convex polytope](wikipedia://en/1817885), bounded by one [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) as its base and by 20 [triangular pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_pyramid) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29) which meet at its apex. Since an icosahedron's circumradius is less than its edge length,[^o6] the tetrahedral pyramids can be made with regular faces.

:::

Having all regular cells, it is a [Blind polytope](wikipedia://en/72252379). Two copies can be augmented to make an [icosahedral bipyramid](wikipedia://en/72252654) which is also a Blind Polytope.

The regular [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) has icosahedral pyramids around every vertex.

The dual to the icosahedral pyramid is the [dodecahedral pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodecahedral_pyramid), seen as a [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/8407) base, and 12 regular [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) meeting at an apex.

*Image: Dodecahedral pyramid.png.*
