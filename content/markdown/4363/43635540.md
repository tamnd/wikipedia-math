# Ministry of Data and Statistics

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ministry of Data and Statistics"}

**Ministry of Data and Statistics** (**KOSTAT**; ) is a government organization responsible for managing [national statistics](wikipedia://en/18646178) in [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea). KOSTAT is headquartered in [Daejeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daejeon), South Korea and operates under the [Ministry of Economy and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_and_Finance_%28South_Korea%29).[^o4]

:::

The Ministry of Data and Statistics generates population and household [census](wikipedia://en/6889) yearly (every 5 years until 2015).[^o5] It also gathers analytic and administrative statistics.[^o6]

Originally named Statistics Korea, the [Lee Jae-myung government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung_government) elevated the organization to the ministerial level on October 1, 2025, renaming it to the Ministry of Data and Statistics.[^o7]
