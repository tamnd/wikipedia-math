# John Aston (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Aston"}

**Sir John Alexander David Aston** is a British statistician, Chief Scientific Adviser (CSA) at the Home Office (2018–2020), and Professor of Statistics, Statistical Laboratory, [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). From 2021 he is the Harding Professor of Statistics in Public Life.[^o3] And from July 2021 he has also served as a non-executive director on the board of the [UK Statistics Authority](wikipedia://en/16155568).

:::

Aston earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Cambridge, and a PhD in statistics from [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) and [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in Montreal, Quebec.[^o4] He was an academic at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) and at [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) in Taiwan, before becoming a professor at the University of Cambridge.[^o5]

Aston was one of the 23 attendees of the [Scientific Advisory Group for Emergencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_Advisory_Group_for_Emergencies) (Sage) during the early stage of the SARS-CoV2 pandemic owing to his CSA role at the time.[^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He and his wife, Karri, have three children.[^o4]

## Honours {#Honours}

He was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor) in the [2021 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Birthday_Honours) for services to statistics and public policymaking.
