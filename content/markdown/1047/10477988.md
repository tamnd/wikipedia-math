# Skoda–El Mir theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Skoda–El Mir theorem"}

The **Skoda–El Mir theorem** is a theorem of [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), stated as follows:

:::

::: {#o2 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** ([Skoda](wikipedia://en/48394269),[^o3] El Mir,[^o4] [Sibony](wikipedia://en/50021601)[^o5]). Let *X* be a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742), and *E* a closed complete [pluripolar set](wikipedia://en/2519069) in *X*. Consider a closed [positive current](wikipedia://en/11326411) $\Theta$ on $X \backslash E$ which is locally integrable around *E*. Then the trivial extension of $\Theta$ to *X* is closed on *X*.

:::
