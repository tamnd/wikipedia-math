# Harald Ganzinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harald Ganzinger"}

**Harald Ganzinger** (31 October 1950, [Werneck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werneck) – 3 June 2004, [Saarbrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarbrücken)) was a German [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) who together with [Leo Bachmair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Bachmair) developed the [superposition calculus](wikipedia://en/2648614), which is (as of 2007) used in most of the state-of-the-art [automated theorem provers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_theorem_prover) for [first-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983).

:::

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) in 1978. Before 1991 he was a Professor of Computer Science at [University of Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Dortmund). Then he joined the [Max Planck Institute for Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Computer_Science) in [Saarbrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarbrücken) shortly after it was founded in 1991. Until 2004 he was the Director of the Programming Logics department of the [Max Planck Institute for Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Computer_Science) and honorary professor at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University). His research group created the [SPASS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPASS_theorem_prover) [automated theorem prover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_theorem_prover).

He received the [Herbrand Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbrand_Award) in 2004 ([posthumous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posthumous_recognition)) for his important contributions to [automated theorem proving](wikipedia://en/2546).
