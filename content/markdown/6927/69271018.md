# Susan Ahmed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Ahmed"}

**Susan Mae Wolofski Ahmed** (born 1946) is an American statistician. After early work in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878), she became chief mathematical statistician in the [National Center for Education Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Education_Statistics) and president of the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Susan Wolofski graduated in 1968 from [Kalamazoo College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalamazoo_College), majoring in mathematics with an honors thesis concerning [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924). She earned a master's degree in public health in 1970 from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). In 1975, as Susan Wolofski Ahmed, she completed a Ph.D. at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), with the dissertation *Discriminant Analysis when the Initial Samples Are Contaminated* supervised by [Peter A. Lachenbruch](wikipedia://en/81317743).

In the 1980s, Ahmed performed biostatistics research, affiliated with the Department of Biostatistics and Epidemiology of the [Georgetown University School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University_School_of_Medicine), including highly cited work on how [inflammatory bowel disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflammatory_bowel_disease) and its treatment can affect [pregnancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pregnancy) outcomes. By the 1990s, she had shifted to government and policy applications of statistics, as chief mathematical statistician in the Statistical Standards and Services Group of the [National Center for Education Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Education_Statistics). After retiring from the government, she was named a senior fellow for statistical services at [Mathematica Policy Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica_Policy_Research) in 2006.

## Service and recognition {#Service_and_recognition}

Ahmed was president of the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607) for the 1994–1995 term. In 1995, she was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).
