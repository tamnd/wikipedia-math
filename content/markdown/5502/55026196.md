# Ivan Corwin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Corwin"}

**Ivan Zachary Corwin** (born May 24, 1984) is an American mathematician who is a professor of mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o3] His research concerns probability, mathematical physics, quantum integrable systems, stochastic PDEs, and random matrix theory. He is particularly known for work related to the [Kardar–Parisi–Zhang equation](wikipedia://en/30724348).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Corwin was born in [Poughkeepsie, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poughkeepsie,_New_York). He graduated from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2006 receiving an A.B. in mathematics, and subsequently received his Ph.D. from the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) under direction of [Gerard Ben Arous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Ben_Arous). He held the first Schramm Memorial Postdoctoral Fellowship at Microsoft Research, New England and [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) from 2012–2014, was a [Clay Research Fellow](wikipedia://en/7655) from 2012–2016, and held the first Poincaré Chair in 2014 at the [Institute Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_Henri_Poincaré). In 2021, he held a Miller visiting professorship at the [Miller Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Institute) as well as a Simons Fellowship. Corwin has taught at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) since 2013.[^o7] He is on the editorial board of the journal [Probability Theory and Related Fields](wikipedia://en/30852593).[^o8] He lives in New Rochelle, NY.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012 he received the Young Scientist Prize of the [International Union of Pure and Applied Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Pure_and_Applied_Physics).[^o10] In 2014, he was awarded a [David and Lucile Packard Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_and_Lucile_Packard_Foundation) Fellowship for Science and Engineering as well as the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize). Also in that year, he was invited to present his work at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

In 2017, along with [Alexei Borodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Borodin) and Patrik Ferrari, he received the inaugural Gerard L. Alexanderson Award from the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005). The following year, in 2018, he was elected as a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o11]

In 2021, Corwin was awarded the [Loeve Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loeve_Prize) and the following year, he was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to integrable probability, the Kardar-Parisi-Zhang universality class, and stochastic vertex models".[^o12] In 2022, Corwin was awarded a Simons Investigator grant.
