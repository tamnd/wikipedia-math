# Elongated pentagonal rotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal rotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal rotunda** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) (*J*<sub>21</sub>). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by elongating a [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491) (*J*<sub>6</sub>) by attaching a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) to its base. It can also be seen as an [elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128635) (*J*<sub>42</sub>) with one pentagonal rotunda removed.

:::

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{1}{12}\left(45+17\sqrt{5}+30\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)a^3\approx14.612...a^3$$

$$A=\frac{1}{2}\left(20+\sqrt{5\left(145+58\sqrt{5}+2\sqrt{30\left(65+29\sqrt{5}\right)}\right)}\right)a^2\approx32.3472...a^2$$

### Dual polyhedron {#Dual_polyhedron}

The dual of the elongated pentagonal rotunda has 30 faces: 10 isosceles triangles, 10 rhombi, and 10 quadrilaterals.
