# Pentagonal rotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal rotunda"}

The **pentagonal rotunda** is a [convex polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron) with regular polygonal faces. These faces comprise ten [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), six [regular pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and one [regular decagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_decagon), making a total of seventeen.[^o3] The pentagonal rotunda is an example of [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), enumerated as the sixth Johnson solid $J_6$.[^o4] It is another example of an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron) because by slicing it with a plane, the resulting smaller convex polyhedra do not have regular faces.[^o5]

:::

The pentagonal rotunda can be regarded as half of an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023), an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847), or as half of a [pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128619), another Johnson solid. Both polyhedra are constructed by attaching two pentagonal rotundas base-to-base. The difference is one of the pentagonal rotundas is twisted. Other Johnson solids constructed by attaching to the base of a pentagonal rotunda are [elongated pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128592), [gyroelongated pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128605), [pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196275), [pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196281), [elongated pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200006), [elongated pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200004), [elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128635), [elongated pentagonal gyrobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128634), [gyroelongated pentagonal cupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200024), and [gyroelongated pentagonal birotunda](wikipedia://en/1128638).[^o6]

*Image: J6 pentagonal rotunda.stl.* 3D model of a pentagonal rotunda

As an above, the surface area $A$ and volume $V$ of a pentagonal rotunda are the following:[^o3]

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \left(\frac{1}{2}\left(5\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{10\left(65+29\sqrt{5}\right)}\right)\right)a^2 \approx 22.347a^2, \\ V &= \left(\frac{1}{12}\left(45+17\sqrt{5}\right)\right)a^3\approx6.918a^3. \end{aligned}$$

A pentagonal rotunda has [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) $C_{5\mathrm{v}}$, a cyclic group of order ten. It has three kinds of [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046), angles between two polygonal faces. These angles are the pentagon-to-decagon around \142.6 deg, the triangle-to-pentagon around \79.2 deg, and the pentagon-to-decagon around \63.4 deg.[^o7]
