# Gyroelongated square cupola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated square cupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyroelongated square cupola** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) (*J*<sub>23</sub>). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by [gyroelongating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongation) a [square cupola](wikipedia://en/1128479) (*J*<sub>4</sub>) by attaching an [octagonal antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octagonal_antiprism) to its base. It can also be seen as a [gyroelongated square bicupola](wikipedia://en/1128650) (*J*<sub>45</sub>) with one square bicupola removed.

## Area and volume {#Area_and_volume}

The surface area is

$$A=\left(7+2\sqrt{2}+5\sqrt{3}\right)a^2\approx 18.4886811...a^2.$$

The volume is the sum of the volume of a square cupola and the volume of an octagonal prism,

$V=\left(1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{2} + \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2}+2\sqrt{146+103\sqrt{2}}}\right)a^3$ $\approx6.2107658...a^3.$

## Dual polyhedron {#Dual_polyhedron}

The dual of the gyroelongated square cupola has 20 faces: 8 kites, 4 rhombi, and 8 pentagons.
