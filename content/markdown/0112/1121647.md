# Axiom of real determinacy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Axiom of real determinacy"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **axiom of real determinacy** (abbreviated as **AD<sub>R</sub>**) is an [axiom](wikipedia://en/928) in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).[^o2] It states the following:

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/axiom cue=math-theorem name="Axiom"}

**Axiom.**

Consider infinite two-person [games](wikipedia://en/2596030#Games) with [perfect information](wikipedia://en/321438). Then, every game of length [ω](wikipedia://en/26547932) where both players choose [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) is determined, i.e., one of the two players has a [winning strategy](wikipedia://en/2596030#Winning_strategies).

:::

The axiom of real determinacy is a stronger version of the [axiom of determinacy](wikipedia://en/1002300) (AD), which makes the same statement about games where both players choose [integers](wikipedia://en/14563); AD<sub>R</sub> is [inconsistent](wikipedia://en/75802) with the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840). It also implies the existence of [inner models](wikipedia://en/1118686) with certain [large cardinals](wikipedia://en/383644).

AD<sub>R</sub> is equivalent to AD plus the [axiom of uniformization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_of_uniformization).
