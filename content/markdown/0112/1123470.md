# Gyroelongated square pyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated square pyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyroelongated square pyramid** is the [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) that can be constructed by attaching an [equilateral square pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_square_pyramid) to a [square antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860045). It occurs in chemistry; for example, the [capped square antiprismatic molecular geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capped_square_antiprismatic_molecular_geometry).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The gyroelongated square pyramid is [composite](wikipedia://en/77684519), since it can be constructed by attaching one [equilateral square pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_square_pyramid) to the [square antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860045), a process known as [gyroelongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongation). This construction involves the covering of one of two square faces and replacing them with the four [equilateral triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_triangles), so that the resulting polyhedron has twelve equilateral triangles and one square. Any convex polyhedron in which all of the faces are regular is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), and the gyroelongated square pyramid is one of them, enumerated as $J_{10}$, the tenth Johnson solid.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The surface area of a gyroelongated square pyramid with edge length $a$ is:

$$\left(1 + 3\sqrt{3}\right)a^2 \approx 6.196a^2,$$

the area of twelve equilateral triangles and a square. Its volume:

$$\frac{\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{4 + 3\sqrt{2}}}{6}a^3 \approx 1.193a^3,$$

can be obtained by slicing the square pyramid and the square antiprism, after which adding their volumes.

*Image: J10 gyroelongated square pyramid.stl.* 3D model of a gyroelongated square pyramid

It has the same [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the square pyramid, the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) $C_{4v}$ of order eight. Its [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) can be derived by calculating the angle of a square pyramid and square antiprism in the following:

- the dihedral angle of an equilateral square pyramid between two adjacent triangles, approximately $109.47^\circ$
- the dihedral angle of a square antiprism between two adjacent triangles, approximately $127.55^\circ$, and between a triangle to its base is $103.83^\circ$
- the dihedral angle between two adjacent triangles, on the edge where an equilateral square pyramid is attached to a square antiprism, is $158.57^\circ$, for which by adding the dihedral angle of an equilateral square pyramid between its base and its lateral face $54.74^\circ$ and the dihedral angle of a square antiprism between two adjacent triangles.

:::

::: {#o8 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

In [stereochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereochemistry), the [capped square antiprismatic molecular geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capped_square_antiprismatic_molecular_geometry) can be described as the [atom cluster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom_cluster) of the gyroelongated square pyramid. An example is \[LaCl(H, a [lanthanum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanthanum)(III) complex with a La–La bond.

:::
