# Metabidiminished icosahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Metabidiminished icosahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **metabidiminished icosahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{62}$). The name refers to one way of constructing it, by removing two [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) ($J_{2}$) from a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968), replacing two sets of five triangular faces of the icosahedron with two adjacent pentagonal faces. If two pentagonal pyramids are removed to form nonadjacent pentagonal faces, the result is instead the [pentagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860091).

:::
