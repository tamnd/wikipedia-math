# Square gyrobicupola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Square gyrobicupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **square gyrobicupola** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{29}$). Like the [square orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128614) ($J_{28}$), it can be obtained by joining two [square cupolae](wikipedia://en/1128479) ($J_{4}$) along their bases. The difference is that in this solid, the two halves are rotated 45 degrees with respect to one another.

:::

The *square gyrobicupola* is the second in an infinite set of [gyrobicupolae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicupola_%28geometry%29).

Related to the square gyrobicupola is the [elongated square gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128551). This polyhedron is created when an [octagonal prism](wikipedia://en/3026569) is inserted between the two halves of the square gyrobicupola.

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\left(2+\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}\right)a^3\approx3.88562...a^3$$

$$A=2\left(5+\sqrt{3}\right)a^2\approx13.4641...a^2$$

## Related polyhedra and honeycombs {#Related_polyhedra_and_honeycombs}

The square gyrobicupola forms space-filling [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654) with [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) and [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280); and with tetrahedra, [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455), and [elongated square bipyramids](wikipedia://en/1177952). (The latter unit can be decomposed into [elongated square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1177939), cubes, and/or square pyramids).[^o6]
