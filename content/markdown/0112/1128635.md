# Elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{42}$). Its [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) is [at5jP5](https://levskaya.github.io/polyhedronisme/?recipe=at5jP5&palette=%230000ff%20%23ffee00%20%2300ff00%20%23ff0000). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by elongating a [pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128619) ($J_{34}$) by inserting a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) between its congruent halves. Rotating one of the [pentagonal rotundae](wikipedia://en/1128491) ($J_{6}$) through 36 degrees before inserting the prism yields the [elongated pentagonal gyrobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128634) ($J_{43}$).

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{1}{6}\left(45+17\sqrt{5}+15\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)a^3\approx21.5297...a^3$$

$$A=\left(10+\sqrt{30\left(10+3\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}\right)}\right)a^2\approx39.306...a^2$$
