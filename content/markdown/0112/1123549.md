# Gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid

*Image: J11 gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid.stl.* 3D model of a gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid** is a polyhedron constructed by attaching a pentagonal [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851) to the base of a [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461). An alternative name is **diminished icosahedron** because it can be constructed by removing a pentagonal pyramid from a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid can be constructed from a [pentagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860091) by attaching a [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461) onto its pentagonal face. This pyramid covers the pentagonal faces, so the resulting polyhedron has 15 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) and 1 [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon) as its faces. Another way to construct it is started from the [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) by cutting off one of two pentagonal pyramids, a process known as [diminishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishment_%28geometry%29); for this reason, it is also called the *diminished icosahedron*. Because the resulting polyhedron has the property of [convexity](wikipedia://en/6292) and its faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306), the gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), enumerated as the 11th Johnson solid $J_{11}$. It is an example of [composite polyhedron](wikipedia://en/77684519).

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) of a gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid $A$ can be obtained by summing the area of 15 equilateral triangles and 1 regular pentagon. Its volume $V$ can be ascertained either by slicing it off into both a pentagonal antiprism and a pentagonal pyramid, after which adding them up; or by subtracting the volume of a regular icosahedron to a pentagonal pyramid. With edge length $a$, they are:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \frac{15 \sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5(5 + 2\sqrt{5})}}{4}a^2 \approx 8.215a^2, \\ V &= \frac{25 + 9\sqrt{5}}{24}a^3 \approx 1.880a^3. \end{aligned}$$

It has the same [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the pentagonal pyramid: the cyclic group $C_{5 \mathrm{v}}$ of order 10. Its [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) can be obtained by involving the angle of a pentagonal antiprism and pentagonal pyramid: its dihedral angle between triangle-to-pentagon is the pentagonal antiprism's angle between that 100.8°, and its dihedral angle between triangle-to-triangle is the pentagonal pyramid's angle 138.2°.

According to [Steinitz's theorem](wikipedia://en/19762817), the [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_%28topology%29) of any [convex polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron) can be represented as a [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314) that is [3-vertex connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph). A planar graph is one that can be drawn on a flat sheet with no edges crossing. A $k$-connected graph is one that remains connected whenever $k - 1$ vertices are removed. This graph is obtained by removing one of the [icosahedral graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icosahedral_graph)'s vertices, leaving 11 vertices, an odd number, resulting in a graph with a [perfect matching](wikipedia://en/44031). Hence, the graph is a 2-vertex connected [claw-free graph](wikipedia://en/12415907), an example of [factor-critical](wikipedia://en/7684634).

:::

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid has appeared in [stereochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereochemistry), wherein the shape resembles the molecular geometry known as [capped pentagonal antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capped_pentagonal_antiprism).
