# Pentagonal pyramid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal pyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentagonal pyramid** is a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) with a [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) base and five triangular faces, having a total of six faces. It is categorized as a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) if all of the edges are equal in length, forming [equilateral triangular](wikipedia://en/173285) faces and a [regular pentagonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon) base.

:::

Pentagonal pyramids occur as pieces and tools in the construction of many polyhedra. They also appear in the field of [natural science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science), as in [stereochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereochemistry) where the shape can be described as the [pentagonal pyramidal molecular geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_pyramidal_molecular_geometry), as well as the study of [shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsid) assembling in the underlying [potential energy surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_energy_surface) and [disclination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disclination) in [fivelings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiveling) and related shapes such as pyramidal [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) and other metal [nanowires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanowire).

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Special cases"}

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

### As a right pyramid {#As_a_right_pyramid}

A pentagonal pyramid has six vertices, ten edges, and six faces. One of its faces is [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014), a *base* of the pyramid; five others are [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654).[^o7] Five of the edges make up the pentagon by connecting its five vertices, and the other five edges are known as the lateral edges of the pyramid, meeting at the sixth vertex called the [apex](wikipedia://en/6022079).[^n1] A pentagonal pyramid is said to be *regular* if its base is [circumscribed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumscribed_polygon) in a circle that forms a [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and it is said to be *right* if its altitude is erected perpendicularly to the base's center.[^o9]

Like other right pyramids with a regular polygon as a base, this pyramid has [pyramidal symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramidal_symmetry) of [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) $C_{5\mathrm{v}}$: the pyramid is left invariant by rotations of one, two, three, four-fifths around its [axis of symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_of_symmetry), the line connecting the apex to the center of the base. It is also [mirror symmetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_symmetric) relative to any perpendicular plane passing through a bisector of the base.[^n2] It can be represented as the [wheel graph](wikipedia://en/1655876) $W_5$, meaning its [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_%28topology%29) can be interpreted as a pentagon in which its five vertices connects a vertex in the center called the [universal vertex](wikipedia://en/51352780).[^n3] It is [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual_polyhedron), meaning its [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) is the pentagonal pyramid itself.[^n4]

### As a Johnson solid {#As_a_Johnson_solid}

*Image: J2 pentagonal pyramid.stl.* 3D model of a pentagonal pyramid with regular faces

When all edges are equal in length, the five triangular faces are [equilateral](wikipedia://en/173285) and the base is a regular pentagon. Because this pyramid remains [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) and all of its faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306), it is classified as the second [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) $J_2$.[^n5] The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) between two adjacent triangular faces is approximately 138.19° and that between the triangular face and the base is 37.37°.[^n2] It is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedra), meaning that it cannot be separated by a plane to create two small convex polyhedra with regular faces.[^o16]

A [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470)'s [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) is the sum of the areas of its faces. Therefore, the surface area of a pentagonal pyramid is the sum of the areas of the five triangles and the one pentagon. The volume of every pyramid equals one-third of the area of its base multiplied by its height. So, the volume of a pentagonal pyramid is one-third of the product of the height and the pentagonal base area.[^n6] In the case of Johnson solid with edge length $a$, its surface area $A$ and volume $V$ are:[^n7]

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \frac{a^2}{2}\sqrt{\frac{5}{2}\left(10+\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}\right)} \approx 3.88554a^2, \\ V &= \frac{5 + \sqrt{5}}{24} a^3 \approx 0.30150a^3. \end{aligned}$$

The pentagonal pyramid, as a Johnson solid, can be constructed through [Cartesian coordinate system](wikipedia://en/7706), with the vertices

$$(\pm 1, 0, \phi), \quad (0, \pm \phi, 1), \quad (\phi, \pm 1, 0)$$

where $\phi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).[^n8]

## Applications {#Applications}

*Image: SmallStellatedDodecahedron.gif.* Pentagonal pyramids can be found in a small stellated dodecahedron

Pentagonal pyramids can be found as components of many polyhedrons. Attaching its base to the pentagonal face of another polyhedron is an example of the construction process known as [augmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29), and attaching it to [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) or [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851) is known as [elongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29) or [gyroelongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongation), respectively.[^n9] Examples of polyhedrons are the [pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196) is constructed from the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) by attaching the base of pentagonal pyramids onto each pentagonal face, similar to the [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843) by [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253), and a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) constructed from a [pentagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860091) by attaching two pentagonal pyramids onto its pentagonal bases.[^o23] Some Johnson solids are constructed by either augmenting pentagonal pyramids or augmenting other shapes with pentagonal pyramids: an [elongated pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1123547) $J_9$, a [gyroelongated pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1123549) $J_{11}$, a [pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/728038) $J_{13}$, an [elongated pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/1177953) $J_{16}$, an [augmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177990) $J_{58}$, a [parabiaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177993) $J_{59}$, a [metabiaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177994) $J_{60}$, and a [triaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177995) $J_{61}$.[^o24] Relatedly, the removal of a pentagonal pyramid from polyhedra is an example of a technique known as [diminishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishment_%28geometry%29); the [metabidiminished icosahedron](wikipedia://en/1123562) $J_{62}$ and [tridiminished icosahedron](wikipedia://en/1123559) $J_{63}$ are the examples in which their constructions begin by removing pentagonal pyramids from a regular icosahedron.[^n10]

In [stereochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereochemistry), an [atom cluster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom_cluster) can have a [pentagonal pyramidal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_pyramidal_molecular_geometry). This molecule has a main-group element with one active [lone pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_pair) of [electrons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron), which can be described by a model that predicts the geometry of molecules known as [VSEPR theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VSEPR_theory).[^n11] An example of a molecule with this structure is [nido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boranes#Chemical_formula_and_naming_conventions)-cage [carbonate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbonate) CB<sub>5</sub>H<sub>9</sub>.[^n12]

The formation of [virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virus) shells, known as [capsids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsid), can be modeled from pieces shaped like pentagonal and [hexagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/19659302). These shapes were chosen to resemble those of the protein subunits of natural viruses. By appropriately choosing the attractive and repulsive forces between pyramids, they found that the pyramids could self-assemble into icosahedral shells reminiscent of those found in nature.[^n13]

The [relaxation](wikipedia://en/5202789) of internal elastic [stress](wikipedia://en/228107) fields due to [disclinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disclination) in twinned [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) particles. Such a shape is the pentagonal pyramid, which allows growth to a large size and preserves symmetry. This can be done by activating [cathode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathode) by the process of initial crystal growth in the [electrolyte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrolyte), by the movement of [aluminum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminum) and [silicon oxides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_oxide)' abrasive particles.[^n14]
