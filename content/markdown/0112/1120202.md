# Dominic Joyce

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Dominic Joyce"}

**Dominic David Joyce** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o2] (born 8 April 1968) is a British mathematician, currently a professor at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and a fellow of [Lincoln College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_College,_Oxford) since 1995.[^o4][^o5][^o6] His undergraduate and doctoral studies were at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford). He undertook a [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPhil) in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) under the supervision of [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305), completed in 1992. After this he held short-term research posts at [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford), as well as [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in the United States.

:::

Joyce is known for his construction of the first known explicit examples of compact [Joyce manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_manifold) (i.e., [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) with [G2](wikipedia://en/292864) [holonomy](wikipedia://en/644921)). He has received the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) Junior [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) and the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) Young Mathematicians Prize. In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o9]
