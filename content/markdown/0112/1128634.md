# Elongated pentagonal gyrobirotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal gyrobirotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal gyrobirotunda** or elongated [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{43}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by elongating a "pentagonal gyrobirotunda," or [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) (one of the [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847)), by inserting a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) between its congruent halves. Rotating one of the [pentagonal rotundae](wikipedia://en/1128491) ($J_{6}$) through 36 degrees before inserting the prism yields an [elongated pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128635) ($J_{42}$).

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following formulae for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{1}{6}\left(45+17\sqrt{5}+15\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)a^3 \approx 21.5297 a^3$$

$$A=\left(10+\sqrt{30\left(10+3\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}\right)}\right)a^2 \approx 39.306 a^2$$
