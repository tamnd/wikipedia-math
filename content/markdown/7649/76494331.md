# Blue–seven phenomenon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Blue–seven phenomenon"}

The **blue–seven phenomenon** is a [social phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_phenomenon) in which a purported statistical majority of people in the US choose the color [blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue) and number [seven](wikipedia://en/208159) when asked to [randomly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randomly) select a color and a single digit number. The cause of the [cognitive bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_bias) is not understood,[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] though it has been posited that both colors and numbers are imbued with layers of significance.[^o6]

:::

The existence of the phenomenon has been disputed.[^o7][^o8] Outside of the US, these preferences may not hold.[^o9][^o10] In other countries, differences occur, particularly in Northern Ireland where a noted bias of green and eight results in a *green–eight* phenomenon.
