# MAGENTA

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="MAGENTA"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **MAGENTA** is a [symmetric key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_key_algorithm) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) developed by Michael Jacobson Jr. and Klaus Huber for [Deutsche Telekom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Telekom). The name MAGENTA is an [acronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym) for **Multifunctional Algorithm for General-purpose Encryption and Network Telecommunication Applications**. (The color [magenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magenta) is also part of the [corporate identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_identity) of Deutsche Telekom.) The cipher was submitted to the [Advanced Encryption Standard process](wikipedia://en/99431), but did not advance beyond the first round; [cryptographic weaknesses](wikipedia://en/5715) were discovered and it was found to be one of the slower ciphers submitted.[^o3]

:::

MAGENTA has a [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) of 128 [bits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit) and [key sizes](wikipedia://en/5749) of 128, 192 and 256 bits. It is a [Feistel cipher](wikipedia://en/352709) with six or eight rounds.

After the presentation of the cipher at the first AES conference, several cryptographers immediately found vulnerabilities.[^o4] These were written up and presented at the second AES conference (Biham et al., 1999).
