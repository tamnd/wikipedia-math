# Hasse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hasse"}

- [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118) (1898–1979), German mathematician
- [Henry Hasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hasse) (1913–1977), US writer of science fiction
- [Johann Adolph Hasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Adolph_Hasse) (1699–1783), German composer
- [Maria Hasse](wikipedia://en/56689677) (1921–2014), German mathematician
- [Peter Hasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hasse) (c. 1585–1640), German organist and composer
