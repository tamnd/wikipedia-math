# Donaldson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Donaldson"}

**Donaldson** is a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Scotland) and [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language) patronymic surname meaning "son of [Donald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald)". It is a simpler Anglicized variant for the name [MacDonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_%28name%29). Notable people with the surname include:
