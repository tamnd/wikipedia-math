# William Menasco

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Menasco"}

**William W. Menasco** is a [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist) and a professor at the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo). He is best known for his work in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Menasco received his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1975, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1981, where his advisor was [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424). He served as assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) from 1981 to 1984. He then taught as a visiting professor at the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo) where he became an assistant professor in 1985, an associate professor in 1991. In 1994 he became a professor at the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo) where he currently serves.[^o3]

## Work {#Work}

Menasco proved that a [link](wikipedia://en/1161784) with an [alternating diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_diagram), such as an [alternating link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_link), will be non-[split](wikipedia://en/3687789) if and only if the diagram is connected.

Menasco, along with [Morwen Thistlethwaite](wikipedia://en/16705411) proved the [Tait flyping conjecture](wikipedia://en/2749975), which states that, given any two reduced [alternating diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_diagram) $D1,D2$ of an oriented, prime [alternating link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_link), $D1$ may be transformed to $D2$ by means of a sequence of certain simple moves called [flypes](wikipedia://en/16891247).
