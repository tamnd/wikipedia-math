# Bankoff circle

*Image: Bankoff Circle.svg.* A Bankoff circle with the center C''<sub>6</sub>

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bankoff circle"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Bankoff circle** or **Bankoff triplet circle** is a certain [Archimedean circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_circle) that can be constructed from an [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197); an Archimedean circle is any circle with area equal to each of [Archimedes' twin circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes'_twin_circles). The Bankoff circle was first constructed by [Leon Bankoff](wikipedia://en/7733725) in 1974.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The Bankoff circle is formed from three [semicircles](wikipedia://en/617831) that create an [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197). A circle *C*<sub>1</sub> is then formed tangent to each of the three semicircles, as an instance of the [problem of Apollonius](wikipedia://en/2642185). Another circle *C*<sub>2</sub> is then created, through three points: the two points of tangency of *C*<sub>1</sub> with the smaller two semicircles, and the point where the two smaller semicircles are tangent to each other. *C*<sub>2</sub> is the Bankoff circle.

:::

## Radius of the circle {#Radius_of_the_circle}

If *r* = *AB*/*AC*, then the radius of the Bankoff circle is:

$$R=\frac{1}{2}r\left(1-r\right).$$
