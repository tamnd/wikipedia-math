# Edmond Bonan

*Image: Edmond Bonan.jpg.* Edmond Bonan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edmond Bonan"}

**Edmond Bonan** (born 27 January 1937 in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), [Mandatory Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine)) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known particularly for his work on [special holonomy](wikipedia://en/644921). Although not a single example of [G<sub>2</sub> manifold](wikipedia://en/648062) or [Spin(7) manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin%287%29_manifold) had been discovered until thirty years later, Edmond Bonan nonetheless made a useful contribution by showing in 1966, that such manifolds would carry at least a parallel 4-form, and would necessarily be Ricci-flat,[^o2] propelling them as candidates for [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After completing his undergraduate studies at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique), Bonan went on to write his 1967 University of [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) doctoral dissertation in [Differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) under the supervision of [André Lichnerowicz](wikipedia://en/2389188).[^o5] From 1968 to 1997, he held the post of lecturer and then professor at the University of Picardie [Jules Verne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jules_Verne) in [Amiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amiens), where he currently holds the title of professor emeritus. Early in his career, from 1969 to 1981, he also lectured at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).
