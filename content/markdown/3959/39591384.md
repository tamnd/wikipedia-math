::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Paracompact uniform honeycombs"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [uniform honeycombs in hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/22952807) are [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) of convex [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29). In 3-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742) there are 23 [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) families of [paracompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram#Paracompact_%28Koszul_simplex_groups%29) uniform honeycombs, generated as [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777), and represented by ring [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of the [Coxeter diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) for each family. These families can produce uniform honeycombs with infinite or unbounded [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) or [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719), including [ideal vertices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_vertex) at infinity, similar to the [hyperbolic uniform tilings in two dimensions](wikipedia://en/18640974#Ideal_triangle_domains).

:::

This is a complete enumeration of the 151 unique [Wythoffian](wikipedia://en/2950777) paracompact uniform honeycombs generated from tetrahedral fundamental domains (rank 4 paracompact coxeter groups). The honeycombs are indexed here for cross-referencing duplicate forms, with brackets around the nonprimary constructions.

The [alternations](wikipedia://en/4349117) are listed, but are either repeats or don't generate uniform solutions. Single-hole alternations represent a mirror removal operation. If an end-node is removed, another simplex (tetrahedral) family is generated. If a hole has two branches, a [Vinberg polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram#Vinberg_polytopes_with_rank_n.2B2_for_n_dimensional_space) is generated, although only Vinberg polytope with mirror symmetry are related to the simplex groups, and their uniform honeycombs have not been systematically explored. These nonsimplectic (pyramidal) Coxeter groups are not enumerated on this page, except as special cases of half groups of the tetrahedral ones. Seven uniform honeycombs that arise here as alternations have been numbered 152 to 158, after the 151 Wythoffian forms not requiring alternation for their construction.
