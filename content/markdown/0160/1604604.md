# Adrien Douady

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adrien Douady"}

**Adrien Douady** (; 25 September 1935 – 2 November 2006) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [La Tronche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Tronche), Isère. He was the son of Daniel Douady and Guilhen Douady.

:::

Douady was a student of [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), and initially worked in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410). His thesis concerned deformations of complex analytic spaces. Subsequently, he became more interested in the work of [Pierre Fatou](wikipedia://en/629100) and [Gaston Julia](wikipedia://en/249107) and made significant contributions to the fields of [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). Together with his former student [John H. Hubbard](wikipedia://en/6674772), he launched a new subject, and a new school, studying properties of iterated quadratic complex mappings. They made important mathematical contributions in this field of complex dynamics, including a study of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562). One of their most fundamental results is that the Mandelbrot set is connected; perhaps most important is their theory of renormalization of (polynomial-like) maps. The [Douady rabbit](wikipedia://en/17739976), a quadratic [filled Julia set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filled_Julia_set), is named after him.

Douady taught at the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice) and was a professor at the [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University), Orsay. He was a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394)[^o3] and an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 1966 at Moscow and again in 1986 in Berkeley.

He was elected to the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences) in 1997, and was featured in the French animation project [Dimensions](wikipedia://en/898778).

He died after diving into the cold Mediterranean from a favourite spot near his vacation home in the [Var](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Var_%28department%29).

His son, [Raphael Douady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_Douady), is also a noted mathematician and an economist.
