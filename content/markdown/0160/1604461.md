# Peter Jones (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Jones"}

*Image: Peter W. Jones 2012.jpg.* Peter Jones (mathematician)

**Peter Wilcox Jones** (born 1952) is a mathematician at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), known for his work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_geometry). He received his Ph.D. at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1978, under the supervision of [John B. Garnett](wikipedia://en/21917650). He received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1981. He is an elected member of the [U.S. National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (2008),[^o2] the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) (2008),[^o3] and the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (1998).[^o4] He is not related to the mathematician [Vaughan Jones](wikipedia://en/1227628).
