# Discrete modelling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrete modelling"}

**Discrete modelling** is the [discrete](wikipedia://en/56061) analogue of [continuous modelling](wikipedia://en/1460103). In discrete modelling, formulae are fit to [discrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_variable) [data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data)—data that could potentially take on only a [countable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable) set of values, such as the [integers](wikipedia://en/14563), and which are not infinitely divisible. A common method in this form of modelling is to use [recurrence relations](wikipedia://en/146806).

:::

[Discrete time](wikipedia://en/40240263) models are well-suited for step-by-step computer simulations and are often appropriate for modelling experimental data, which is typically collected in discrete form.[^o2]
