# Joseph Oesterlé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Oesterlé"}

**Joseph Oesterlé** (born 1954) is a French mathematician who, along with [David Masser](wikipedia://en/2172777), formulated the [*abc* conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108) which has been called "the most important unsolved problem in [diophantine analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantine_analysis)".[^o4][^o5]

:::

He is a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o6]
