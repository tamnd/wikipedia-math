# Elon Lages Lima

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elon Lages Lima"}

**Elon Lages Lima** (July 9, 1929 – May 7, 2017)[^o3] was a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerned [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o4] Lima was an influential figure in the development of mathematics in Brazil.[^o5]

:::

Lima was professor emeritus at [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708)[^o6] of which he was the director during three separate periods. Lima has been twice recipient of the [Prêmio Jabuti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prêmio_Jabuti) from the *[Câmara Brasileira do Livro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Câmara_Brasileira_do_Livro)*, for his textbooks *Espaços Métricos* and *Álgebra Linear*,[^o7] and of the Anísio Teixeira Prize from the Ministry of Education and Sports. His mathematical style was heavily influenced by [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394)'s.[^o8]

## Biography {#Biography}

He began his career as a high school teacher in [Fortaleza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortaleza,_Brazil), [Ceará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceará). Lima graduated with a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [Universidade do Brasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_do_Brasil) (today [UFRJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFRJ)) in 1953. He obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1958 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) with [Edwin Henry Spanier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin_Henry_Spanier) as advisor. He is a former [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow), and holds memberships in the *[Academia Brasileira de Ciências](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Brasileira_de_Ciências)* (Brazilian Academy of Sciences) and the [TWAS, the Academy of Sciences for the Developing World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS). He is a professor [Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_Causa) of the [Universidade Federal do Ceará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_Federal_do_Ceará) and of the [University of Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília), where he taught from 1964 to 1965.[^o11] He was a member of the Upper Board of FAPERJ from 1987 to 1991. He was also a member of the National Board of Education.

He wrote over thirty books in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), some of which are intended for [secondary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school) teachers. Between 1990 and 1995, he coordinated the IMPA-VITAE project, which held skills improvement courses for mathematics teachers in eleven cities from eight states throughout Brazil.

He received the *grã-cruz* ("Great Cross") of the *[Ordem Nacional do Mérito Científico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordem_Nacional_do_Mérito_Científico)* ("National Order of Scientific Merit") of Brazil.[^o12]
