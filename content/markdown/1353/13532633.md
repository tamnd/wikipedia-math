# Basu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Basu"}

**Basu** (বসু, ), also **[Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bose_%28surname%29)** ([anglicized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglicisation)), **Boshu**, **Bosu**, or **Bosh**, is a [Bengali Kayastha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_Kayastha) surname originating from the [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal) region of the [Indian subcontinent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_subcontinent).[^o2] The name stems from [Vāsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasu) (वसु), which means 'Bright one'.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Basus are considered as [Kulin Kayasthas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulin_Kayastha) of [Gautam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Maharishi) [gotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotra), along with [Ghoshes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosh), [Mitras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitra_%28surname%29) and Bangaja (Eastern Bengal) [Guhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guha_%28surname%29).[^o5][^o6]
