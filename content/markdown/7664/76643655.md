# International Slide Rule Museum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Slide Rule Museum"}

The **International Slide Rule Museum** (**ISRM**) is an American museum dedicated to the preservation and display of [slide rules](wikipedia://en/28743) and other mathematical artefacts. Established in 2003 by Michael Konshak, who serves as its [curator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curator),[^o5][^o6] the museum houses a collection of slide rules from diverse manufacturers and time periods, showcasing the evolution and importance of these instruments in the history of mathematics and engineering.

:::

It is often identified to be one of the largest repositories of slide rules and logarithmic calculators.[^o7][^o8]

## Key people {#Key_people}

### Board of Directors {#Board_of_Directors}

The museum's operations are overseen by its governing body, the Board of Directors, presently composed of nine members. Each member of the Board fulfills a role in the stewardship and guidance of the institution.[^o5]

**Leadership Positions:**

- Director and Curator – Michael V. Konshak[^o5][^o11]
- Trustee and Treasurer – Rebecca A. Konshak[^o5]
- Head of Public Communications, Education, etc. – *Marvin Clarence*[^o5][^o12]

**Advisors:**

- Dylan Thinnes[^o5]
- George Anderson[^o5]
- Michael Frey[^o5]
- Michael J. Syphers[^o5]
- Nathan Zeldes[^o5]
