# Suren Arakelov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suren Arakelov"}

**Suren Yurievich Arakelov** (Суре́н Ю́рьевич Араке́лов, Սուրեն Յուրիի Առաքելով) (born October 16, 1947 in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv)) is a Soviet mathematician of Armenian descent known for developing [Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

From 1965 onwards Arakelov attended the Mathematics department of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he graduated in 1971.

In 1974, Arakelov received his [candidate of sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_sciences) degree from the [Steklov Institute](wikipedia://en/984494) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), under the supervision of [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815). He then worked as a junior researcher at the [Gubkin Russian State University of Oil and Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gubkin_Russian_State_University_of_Oil_and_Gas) in Moscow until 1979. He did protest against arrest of [Alexander Solzhenitsyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Solzhenitsyn), and was arrested and committed to a mental hospital.[^o3] Then he stopped his research activity to pursue other life goals. As of 2014 he lives in Moscow with his wife and children.

## Arakelov theory {#Arakelov_theory}

[Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229) was exploited by [Paul Vojta](wikipedia://en/1802312) to give a new proof of the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture) and by [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226) in his proof of Lang's generalization of the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture).
