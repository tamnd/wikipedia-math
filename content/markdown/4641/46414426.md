# Continuous q-Legendre polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous q-Legendre polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **continuous *q*-Legendre polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (Koekoek Lesky Swarttouw 2010) give a detailed list of their properties.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$P_{n}(x|q)={}_4\phi_3\left(q^{-n},q^{n+1},q^{\frac14}e^{i\theta},q^{\frac14}e^{-i\theta};q,-q^{-\frac12},-q;q,q\right),\quad x=\cos\,\theta.$$

:::
