# Susan R. Wilson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan R. Wilson"}

author **Susan Ruth Wilson** (19 March 1948 – 16 March 2020) was an Australian statistician, known for her research in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) and [statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736), and for her work on the understanding of [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS) in Australia.[^o3][^o4][^o5] She edited the bulletin of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) from 1993 to 1998, and was president of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) from 1998 to 1999.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wilson was born in [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney). She earned her Ph.D. in 1972 from [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University); her dissertation, supervised by [P. A. P. Moran](wikipedia://en/6876839), was *Some Statistical Results in Genetics*. She became a lecturer at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield), but in 1974 returned to Australia to take a position as research fellow at the Australian National University. She became a fellow there in 1976, and a senior fellow in 1984. In 1994, she was given a professorship in Statistical Science in the Centre for Mathematics and its Applications. At her death, she was a professor emeritus of bioinformation science and statistical science at Australian National University and also an honorary professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was elected as a member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1979, as a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1991, as a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and as an honorary life member of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) in 2012.
