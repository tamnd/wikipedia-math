# Heronian mean

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heronian mean"}

In mathematics, the **Heronian mean** *H* of two non-negative [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) *A* and *B* is given by the formula

$$H = \frac{1}{3} \left(A + \sqrt{A B} +B \right).$$

:::

It is named after [Hero of Alexandria](wikipedia://en/68274).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Just like all [means](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean), the Heronian mean is symmetric (it does not depend on the order in which its two arguments are given) and idempotent (the mean of any number with itself is the same number).

The Heronian mean of the numbers *A* and *B* is a [weighted mean](wikipedia://en/33274) of their [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/612) and [geometric means](wikipedia://en/13046):

$$H = \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{A+B}{2} + \frac{1}{3}\cdot\sqrt{A B}.$$

Therefore, it lies between these two means, and between the two given numbers.

:::

## Application in solid geometry {#Application_in_solid_geometry}

*Image: Square frustum.svg.* A square frustum, with volume equal to the height times the Heronian mean of the square areas

The Heronian mean may be used in finding the [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of a [frustum](wikipedia://en/89405) of a [pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid) or [cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_%28geometry%29). The volume is equal to the product of the height of the frustum and the Heronian mean of the areas of the opposing parallel faces.

A version of this formula, for square frusta, appears in the [Moscow Mathematical Papyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Mathematical_Papyrus) from [Ancient Egyptian mathematics](wikipedia://en/1180105), whose content dates to roughly 1850 BC.
