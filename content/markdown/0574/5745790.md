# Conway puzzle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conway puzzle"}

*Image: Conway\_puzzle\_pieces.svg.* Pieces used in the Conway puzzle

**Conway's puzzle**, or **blocks-in-a-box**, is a [packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_problem) using rectangular blocks, named after its inventor, mathematician [John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807). It calls for packing thirteen 1 × 2 × 4 blocks, one 2 × 2 × 2 block, one 1 × 2 × 2 block, and three 1 × 1 × 3 blocks into a 5 × 5 × 5 box.

:::

## Solution {#Solution}

*Image: Conway puzzle\_hint.svg.* A possible placement for the three 1×1×3 blocks – the vertical block has corners touching corners of the two horizontal blocks

The solution of the Conway puzzle is straightforward once one realizes, based on [parity](wikipedia://en/143135) considerations, that the three 1 × 1 × 3 blocks need to be placed so that precisely one of them appears in each 5 × 5 × 1 slice of the cube.[^o4] This is analogous to similar insight that facilitates the solution of the simpler [Slothouber–Graatsma puzzle](wikipedia://en/5745928).
