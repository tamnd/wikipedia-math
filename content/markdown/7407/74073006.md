# Morris Marden

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Morris Marden"}

**Morris Marden** (1905–1991) was an American mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born on 12 February 1905 in [East Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Boston), United States.[^o4] He was the seventh child of Abram and Fannie B. Marden.[^o4]

He died in 1991.[^o4]

## Education {#Education}

He completed his PhD dissertation on *the Location of the Roots of the Jacobian of Two Binary Forms and of the Derivative of a Rational Function* under the supervision of [Joseph L. Walsh](wikipedia://en/14002828).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

He is known for the [Marden's theorem](wikipedia://en/17185722), which was proven by Jörg Siebeck.[^o4]

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

His publications include:[^o9]

- *Geometry of Polynomials*
- *The geometry of the zeros of a polynomial in a complex variable*
- *Geometry of Polynomials. Second Edition. Mathematical Surveys Number 3*
