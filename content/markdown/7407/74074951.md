# Christof Paar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christof Paar"}

*Image: Christof-Paar.png.* Christof Paar

**Christof Paar** (born July 18, 1963, in [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne)) is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432). He is one of the founding directors at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy in [Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochum), Germany, a "Scientific Member" of the [Max Planck Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society),[^o2] [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow),[^o3] and a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o4]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

After training as a telecommunications technician, Christof Paar studied telecommunication engineering at the [Cologne University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_University_of_Applied_Sciences) (then [FH Köln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FH_Köln)) from 1984 to 1988. After his [civilian service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_civilian_service), he studied [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) with a specialization in computer technology at the University of Siegen from 1989 to 1991. He wrote his diploma thesis at [Michigan Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Technological_University) on active noise control.[^o6] From 1991 to 1994, he was a doctoral student with [Han Vinck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.J._Han_Vinck) at the Institute for Experimental Mathematics at the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen) (then the [University of Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essen)).[^o7] His dissertation was on computer architectures for arithmetic in [finite fields](wikipedia://en/576387).[^o8] From 1995 to 2001, he was an assistant and later an associate professor at [Worcester Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Polytechnic_Institute) in [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts) for nearly seven years.[^o9] From 2001 to 2019, he held the Chair of Embedded Security at [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum), Germany. From 2008 to 2009 and from 2014 to 2016, he worked as a Research Professor at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst).[^o10] Since 2019, he is one of the two founding directors of the new [Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Security_and_Privacy) in Bochum.[^o11]

Paar's research deals with the engineering aspects of cryptography. He has significantly contributed to efficient implementing [symmetric](wikipedia://en/53042) and [asymmetric crypto](wikipedia://en/24222) algorithms, [side-channel analysis](wikipedia://en/667678), embedded systems security, and [hardware security](wikipedia://en/54213333).[^o2] In 1999, he co-founded, with Çetin Koç, [CHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHES) (Conference on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems) at [WPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Polytechnic_Institute), which has become one of the leading international conferences on cryptography.[^o12] At [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum), he established the [Horst Görtz Institute for Information Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horst_Görtz_Institute_for_Information_Security) together with [Hans Dobbertin](wikipedia://en/2210027) and was instrumental in building Bochum's bachelor's and master's degree programs in the field of IT security.[^o13] Since 2019, he is co-spokesperson for the Cluster of Excellence "CASA - Cyber Security in the Age of Large-Scale Attackers", together with Eike Kiltz and Angela Sasse.[^o14] In 2003, Paar founded Escrypt GmbH together with Willi Mannheims, one of the first companies to focus on industrial data security. Since 2012, Escrypt has been part of [Robert Bosch GmbH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Bosch_GmbH).[^o15]
