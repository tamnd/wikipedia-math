# 2E6 (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2E6"}

In mathematics, **<sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub>** is a family of [Steinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinberg_group_%28Lie_theory%29) or twisted [Chevalley groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group). It is a quasi-split form of [E<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725), depending on a quadratic extension of fields *K*⊂*L*. Unfortunately the notation for the group is not standardized, as some authors write it as <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub>(*K*) (thinking of <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub> as an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) taking values in *K*) and some as <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub>(*L*) (thinking of the group as a subgroup of E<sub>6</sub>(*L*) fixed by an outer involution).

:::

Over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615) these groups form one of the 18 infinite families of [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group), and were introduced independently by (Tits 1958) and (Steinberg 1959).

## Over finite fields {#Over_finite_fields}

The group <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub>(*q*<sup>2</sup>) has order *q*<sup>36</sup> (*q*<sup>12</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>9</sup> + 1) (*q*<sup>8</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>6</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>5</sup> + 1) (*q*<sup>2</sup> − 1) /(3,*q* + 1).[^o5] This is similar to the order *q*<sup>36</sup> (*q*<sup>12</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>9</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>8</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>6</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>5</sup> − 1) (*q*<sup>2</sup> − 1) /(3,*q* − 1) of E<sub>6</sub>(*q*).

Its Schur multiplier has order (3, *q* + 1) except for *q*=2, i. e. <sup>2</sup>*E*<sub>6</sub>(2<sup>2</sup>), when it has order 12 and is a product of cyclic groups of orders 2,2,3. One of the exceptional double covers of <sup>2</sup>*E*<sub>6</sub>(2<sup>2</sup>) is a subgroup of the baby monster group, and the exceptional central extension by the elementary abelian group of order 4 is a subgroup of the monster group.

The outer automorphism group has order (3, *q* + 1) · *f* where *q*<sup>2</sup> = *p*<sup>*f*</sup>.

## Over the real numbers {#Over_the_real_numbers}

Over the real numbers, <sup>2</sup>E<sub>6</sub> is the quasisplit form of E<sub>6</sub>, and is one of the five real forms of E<sub>6</sub> classified by [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274). Its maximal compact subgroup is of type [F<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/292877).
