# Alina Carmen Cojocaru

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alina Carmen Cojocaru"}

**Alina Carmen Cojocaru** () is a Romanian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and is known for her research on [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and [sieve theory](wikipedia://en/1035915). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) and a researcher in the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230).

:::

Cojocaru earned her Ph.D. from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario), in 2002. Her dissertation, *Cyclicity of Elliptic Curves Modulo p*, was jointly supervised by [M. Ram Murty](wikipedia://en/14988649) and Ernst Kani.

Cojocaru was elected to be an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Council member at large from February 1, 2023, to January 31, 2024.

## Books {#Books}

Cojocaru is an author of the book

- *An Introduction to Sieve Methods and their Applications* (with M. Ram Murty, London Mathematical Society Student Texts 66, Cambridge University Press, 2006).

She is also an editor of

- *Women in Numbers: Research Directions in Number Theory* (with [Kristin Lauter](wikipedia://en/26440781), [Rachel Justine Pries](wikipedia://en/55720674), and [Renate Scheidler](wikipedia://en/77148270), Fields Institute Communications 60, American Mathematical Society, 2011).
- *Scholar: A Scientific Celebration Highlighting Open Lines of Arithmetic Research: Conference in Honour of M. Ram Murty's Mathematical Legacy on His 60th Birthday* (with C. David and F. Pappalardi, Contemporary Mathematics 655, American Mathematical Society, 2016)
