# Integrable algorithm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Integrable algorithm"}

**Integrable algorithms** are numerical algorithms that rely on basic ideas from the mathematical theory of [integrable systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_systems).[^o2]

:::

## Background {#Background}

The theory of integrable systems has advanced with the connection between [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). For example, the discovery of solitons came from the numerical experiments to the [KdV equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KdV_equation) by [Norman Zabusky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Zabusky) and [Martin David Kruskal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_David_Kruskal).[^o4] Today, various relations between numerical analysis and integrable systems have been found ([Toda lattice](wikipedia://en/3240033) and [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660),[^o5][^o6] discrete soliton equations and [series acceleration](wikipedia://en/8737421)[^o7][^o8]), and studies to apply integrable systems to numerical computation are rapidly advancing.[^o9][^o10]

## Integrable difference schemes {#Integrable_difference_schemes}

Generally, it is hard to accurately compute the solutions of nonlinear differential equations due to its non-linearity. In order to overcome this difficulty, R. Hirota has made discrete versions of integrable systems with the viewpoint of "Preserve mathematical structures of integrable systems in the discrete versions".[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16]

At the same time, [Mark J. Ablowitz](wikipedia://en/18578839) and others have not only made discrete soliton equations with discrete [Lax pair](wikipedia://en/5527029) but also compared numerical results between integrable difference schemes and ordinary methods.[^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21] As a result of their experiments, they have found that the accuracy can be improved with integrable difference schemes at some cases.[^o22][^o23][^o24][^o25]
