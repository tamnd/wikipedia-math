# Gpg4win

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gpg4win"}

**Gpg4win** is an email and file encryption package for most versions of [Microsoft Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows) and [Microsoft Outlook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Outlook), which utilises the [GnuPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GnuPG) framework for [symmetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_encryption) and [public-key cryptography](wikipedia://en/24222), such as data encryption, [digital signatures](wikipedia://en/59644), [hash calculations](wikipedia://en/439526) etc.

:::

## History of Gpg4win {#History_of_Gpg4win}

The original creation of Gpg4win was initiated and funded by Germany's [Federal Office for Information Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_for_Information_Security) (BSI) in 2005,[^o4][^o5] resulting in the release of Gpg4win 1.0.0 on 6 April 2006;[^o6] however Gpg4win and all included tools are [free and open source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open_source_software), and it is typically the non-proprietary option for privacy recommended[^o7][^o8] to Windows users.

As Gpg4win v1 was a much overhauled derivate of GnuPP,[^o9] both were using [GnuPG v1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GnuPG#Branches) for cryptographic operations and thus only supported [OpenPGP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenPGP) as cryptography standard.

Hence in 2007 the development of a fundamentally enhanced version was started, also with support from the German BSI ([Federal Office for Information Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_for_Information_Security)); this effort culminated in the release of Gpg4win 2.0.0 on 7 August 2009 after a protracted beta testing phase,[^o10] which was based on GnuPG 2.0, included [S/MIME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S/MIME) support, Kleopatra as a new certificate manager, the [Explorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Explorer) plug-in GpgEX for cryptography operations on files, basic support of [smart cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_card), a full set of German dialogue texts in addition to the English ones, new manuals in English and German, plus many other enhancements.[^o11]

In contrast to Gpg4win v2, which focused on new features and software components, the development of Gpg4win v3 focused on usability, plus consolidation of code and features:[^o12] This resulted in the release of Gpg4win 3.0.0 on 19 September 2017 with proper support for [Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_Curve_Cryptography) by utilising GnuPG 2.2 (instead of 2.0), broadened, stabilised and enhanced smart card support, a fundamentally overhauled [Outlook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Outlook) plug-in GpgOL for Outlook 2010 and newer, support of 64-bit versions of Outlook 2010 and newer, supporting dialogues in all languages which KDE supports etc.[^o13] It is also distributed as GnuPG VS-Desktop with commercial support and approval for handling [NATO RESTRICTED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information#NATO_classifications), [RESTREINT UE/EU RESTRICTED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information#International_organizations) and [German VS-NfD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information#Table_of_equivalent_classification_markings_in_various_countries) documents, which in turn has become the major source of revenue for maintaining and further developing the GnuPG framework and Gpg4win.[^o14]

Gpg4win 4.0.0, released on 21 December 2021,[^o15] switched to using GnuPG 2.3 (from 2.2) and continued to refine and enhance the feature set of Gpg4win v3.[^o16]

## Contents of Gpg4win Installer {#Contents_of_Gpg4win_Installer}

- [GnuPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GnuPG): the core cryptography framework
- Certificate managers
  - Kleopatra: certificate manager for OpenPGP and X.509
  - GPA: an alternative certificate manager ([GNU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU)) for OpenPGP and X.509
- Plug-ins for email and file cryptography
  - GpgOL: a plug-in for [Microsoft Outlook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Outlook) to provide [email encryption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email_encryption) and signing
  - GpgEX: a plug-in for the [Windows Explorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Explorer) to provide file encryption, signing and hash calculations
- *Gpg4win Compendium*: an introduction to encryption (OpenPGP and [X.509](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X.509)) and user manual for Gpg4win
