# Kazimierz Abramowicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kazimierz Abramowicz"}

*Image: Kazimierz\_Abramowicz.jpg.* Kazimierz Abramowicz

**Kazimierz Abramowicz** (1889–1936) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the [University of Poznan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Poznan).[^o2][^o3] He worked in the field of theory of [analytic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_functions), in particular [hypergeometric functions](wikipedia://en/2055223).[^o4]

:::
