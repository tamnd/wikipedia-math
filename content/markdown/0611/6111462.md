# Dmitrii Sintsov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitrii Sintsov"}

**Dmitrii Matveyevich Sintsov** (Дмитрий Матвеевич Синцов; 20 November 1867, [Vyatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirov,_Kirov_Oblast) – 28 January 1946, [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv)) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in the theory of [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673) and [non-holonomic geometry](wikipedia://en/554248).

:::

He took a leading role in the development of mathematics at the [University of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Kharkiv), serving as chairman of the [Kharkiv Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/19928894) for forty years, from 1906 until his death at the age of 78.
