# Kajetan Garbiński

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kajetan Garbiński"}

*Image: Kajetan Garbiński.jpg.* Kajetan Garbiński

**Kajetan Garbiński** (1796–1847) was a Polish mathematician and prominent professor at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). Minister of religion and education in the revolutionary [Polish National Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_National_Government_%28November_Uprising%29) during the [November Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_Uprising) in 1831.[^o2][^o3]

:::
