# Feliks Barański

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Feliks Barański"}

*Image: Feliks Barański.jpg.* Feliks Barański

**Feliks Barański** (1915-2006) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and an active member of the so-called [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328).

:::

Born May 1915 in Lwów, [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) (modern [Lviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)), he joined the circle of young, talented mathematicians formed around [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646) and [Hugo Steinhaus](wikipedia://en/71735). He obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) under the supervision of [Władysław Orlicz](wikipedia://en/362718).[^o2] During the period of German occupation of his hometown he made his living as a [lice feeder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lice_feeder) in the institute of [Rudolf Weigl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Weigl). Expelled from Lwow after the war, he settled in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), where he joined the local [Kraków University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politechnika_Krakowska). He was also admitted into the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o3]
