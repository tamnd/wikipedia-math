# Invariant factor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Invariant factor"}

The **invariant factors** of a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) over a [principal ideal domain](wikipedia://en/25039) (PID) occur in one form of the [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](wikipedia://en/9902787).

:::

If $R$ is a [PID](wikipedia://en/25039) and $M$ a [finitely generated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_module) $R$-module, then

$$M\cong R^r\oplus R/(a_1)\oplus R/(a_2)\oplus\cdots\oplus R/(a_m)$$

for some integer $r\geq0$ and a (possibly empty) list of nonzero elements $a_1,\ldots,a_m\in R$ for which $a_1 \mid a_2 \mid \cdots \mid a_m$. The nonnegative integer $r$ is called the *free rank* or *Betti number* of the module $M$, while $a_1,\ldots,a_m$ are the *invariant factors* of $M$ and are unique up to [associatedness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associatedness).

The invariant factors of a [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) over a PID occur in the [Smith normal form](wikipedia://en/1106564) and provide a means of computing the structure of a module from a set of generators and relations.
