# List of irreducible Tits indices

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of irreducible Tits indices"}

In the mathematical theory of [linear algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/395885), a **[Tits index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tits_index)** (or **index**) is an object used to classify semisimple [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) defined over a base field *k*, not assumed to be [algebraically closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_closed). The possible irreducible indices were classified by [Jacques Tits](wikipedia://en/219570),[^o2] and this classification is reproduced below. (Because every index is a direct sum of irreducible indices, classifying *all* indices amounts to classifying irreducible indices.)

:::

## Organization of the list {#Organization_of_the_list}

An index can be represented as a [Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/277109) with certain vertices drawn close to each other (the orbit of the vertices under the \*-action of the Galois group of *k*) and with certain sets of vertices circled (the orbits of the non-distinguished vertices under the \*-action). This representation captures the full information of the index except when the underlying Dynkin diagram is D<sub>4</sub>, in which case one must distinguish between an action by the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) *C*<sub>3</sub> or the [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) *S*<sub>3</sub>.

Alternatively, an index can be represented using the name of the underlying Dynkin diagram together with additional superscripts and subscripts, to be explained momentarily. This representation, together with the labeled Dynkin diagram described in the previous paragraph, captures the full information of the index.

The notation for an index is of the form <sup>*g*</sup>*X*${}^{t}_{n,r}$, where

- *X* is the letter of the underlying Dynkin diagram (A, B, C, D, E, F, or G),
- *n* is the number of vertices of the Dynkin diagram,
- *r* is the **relative rank** of the corresponding algebraic group,

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Absolute Galois group"}

- *g* is the order of the quotient of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) that acts [faithfully](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29#Faithful) on the Dynkin diagram (so *g* = 1, 2, 3, or 6), and

:::

- *t* is either
  - the degree of a certain [division algebra](wikipedia://en/26549625) (that is, the square root of its dimension) arising in the construction of the algebraic group when the group is of classical type (A, B, C, or D), in which case *t* is written in parentheses, or
  - the dimension of the anisotropic kernel of the algebraic group when the group is of exceptional type (E, F, or G), in which case *t* is written without parentheses.

In the description, there are given (only for classical groups), a representative of the isogeny class of the group of the given Tits index.

The following complete list of all possible Tits indices over those special fields, which are the finite fields, the local and global fields (in any characteristic) is given (see [^o5] and [^o6] (with full proof)).

The related sources are,[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10] and.[^o11]

## A<sub>*n*</sub> {#An}

### <sup>1</sup>A<sub>*n*</sub> {#1An}

**Full name**

<sup>1</sup>A${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$

**Image**

*Image: tits\_index\_1An.svg.*

**Conditions**

*d* · (*r* + 1) = *n* + 1, *d* ≥ 1.

**Distinguished vertices**

*d*, 2*d*, ... , *rd*.

**Description**

Algebraic group : The [special linear group](wikipedia://en/144823) SL<sub>*r*+1</sub>(*D*) where *D* is a [central division algebra](wikipedia://en/404082) over *k*.

**Special fields**

Over a finite field, *d* = 1; over the reals, *d* = 1 or 2; over a *p*-adic field or a number field, or any local or global function field, *d* is arbitrary.

### <sup>2</sup>A<sub>*n*</sub> {#2An}

**Full name**

<sup>2</sup>A${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$

**Image**

*Image: tits\_index\_2An.svg.*

**Conditions**

*d* | *n* + 1, *d* ≥ 1, 2*rd* ≤ *n* + 1.

**Distinguished vertices**

$$(d,n+1-d),(2d,n+1-2d),...,(rd,n+1-rd).$$

**Description**

Algebraic group : The [special unitary group](wikipedia://en/173997) SU<sub>(*n*+1)/*d*</sub>(*D*,*h*), where *D* is a central division algebra of degree *d* over a separable quadratic extension *k'* of *k*, and where *h* is a nondegenerate [hermitian form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_form) of [index](wikipedia://en/10917170) *r* relative to the unique non-trivial *k*-automorphism of *k'*.

**Special fields**

Over a finite field, *d* = 1 and *r* = ⌊(*n*+1)/2⌋; over the reals, *d* = 1; over a *p*-adic field or local function field, *d* = 1 and *n* = 2*r* − 1, 2*r*, 2*r*+1. Over a real number field, *d* and *r* are arbitrary; over a totally imaginary number field, *d*=1 and *n* = 2*r* − 1, 2*r*, 2*r*+1, or *d>1* and (*n*+1)/*d*−2*r* =0,1; over a global function field, *d*=1 and *n* = 2*r*, 2*r*+1, 2*r*+2, or *d>1* and (*n*+1)/*d*−2*r* =0,1.

## B<sub>*n*</sub> {#Bn}

**Full name**

B<sub>*n,r*</sub>

**Image**

*Image: tits\_index\_Bn.svg.*

**Conditions**

None.

**Distinguished vertices**

1, 2, ... , *r*.

**Description**

Algebraic group : The [special orthogonal group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_orthogonal_group) SO<sub>2*n*+1</sub>(*k*,*q*), where *q* is a quadratic form of [index](wikipedia://en/10917170) *r*, and defect 1 if *k* has characteristic 2.

**Special fields**

Over a finite field, *r* = *n*; over a *p*-adic field or local function field, *r* = *n* or *n* − 1 (and if char.*k*=2, defect 1); over the reals or a real number field, *r* is arbitrary; over a totally imaginary number field or a global function field, *r* = *n* or *n* − 1 (and if char.*k*=2, defect 1).

## C<sub>*n*</sub> {#Cn}

**Full name**: C${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_Cn.svg.*

**Conditions**: *d* = 2<sup>*a*</sup> | 2*n*, *d* ≥ 1; *n* = *r* if *d* = 1.

**Distinguished vertices**: *d*, 2*d*,...,*rd*.

**Description: Algebraic group**: The [special unitary group](wikipedia://en/173997) SU<sub>2*n*/*d*</sub>(*D*,*h*), where *D* is a division algebra of degree *d* over *k* and *h* is a nondegenerate [antihermitian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antihermitian) form relative to a *k*-linear involution σ of *D* (also called an "involution of the first kind") such that the [fixed-point subring](wikipedia://en/47933712) *D*<sup>σ</sup> has dimension *d*(*d* + 1)/2; or equivalently, when *d* > 1 and char *k* ≠ 2, the group SU<sub>2*n*/*d*</sub> where *D* and *h* are as above except that *h* is hermitian and *D*<sup>σ</sup> has dimension *d*(*d* − 1)/2. When *d* = 1, this group is the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) Sp<sub>2*n*</sub>(*k*).

**Special fields**: Over a finite field, *d* = 1; over the reals or a real number field, *d* = 1 (and *r* = *n*) or *d* = 2; over a *p*-adic field, local function field, totally imaginary number field or global function field, *d* = 1 (and *r* = *n*) or *d* = 2, and *n* = 2*r* or 2*r* − 1.

## D<sub>*n*</sub> {#Dn}

### <sup>1</sup>D<sub>*n*</sub> {#1Dn}

**Full name**

<sup>1</sup>D${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$

**Image**

*Image: tits\_index\_1Dn.svg.*

**Conditions**

*d* = 2<sup>*a*</sup> | 2*n*, *d* ≥ 1, *rd* ≤ *n*, *n* ≠ *rd* + 1.

**Distinguished vertices**

*d*, 2*d*, ..., *rd*.

**Description**

Algebraic group : If *k* has characteristic ≠ 2, the [special unitary group](wikipedia://en/173997) SU<sub>2*n*/*d*</sub>(*D*,*h*), where *D* is a division algebra of degree *d* over *k* and *h* is a hermitian form of discriminant 1 and index *r*, relative to a *k*-linear involution σ of *D*, an "involution of the first kind such that the subring *D*<sup>σ</sup> has dimension *d*(*d* + 1)/2; or equivalently, when *d* > 1 and char *k* ≠ 2, the group SU<sub>2*n*/*d*</sub> where *D* and *h* are as above except that *h* is anti-hermitian form of discriminant 1 and index *r*, and *D*<sup>σ</sup> has dimension *d*(*d* − 1)/2.

**Special fields**

Over a finite field, *d* = 1 and *n* = *r*; over the reals, *d* = 1 and *n* − *r* = 2*m*, or *d* = 2 and *n* = 2*r*; over a *p*-adic field or local function field, *d* = 1 and *r* = *n* or *n* − 2, or *d* = 2 and *n* = 2*r* or 2*r* + 3; over a number field, *d* = 1 and *n* − *r* = 2*m*, or *d* = 2 and *n* − 2*r* = 2*m* or 3; over a totally imaginary number field or a global function field, *d*=1 and $n-r=0,2,$ or $d=2$ and $n-2r=0,3.$

### <sup>2</sup>D<sub>*n*</sub> {#2Dn}

**Full name**

<sup>2</sup>D${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$

**Image**

*Image: tits\_index\_2Dn.svg.*

**Conditions**

*d* = 2<sup>*a*</sup> | 2*n*, *d* ≥ 1, *rd* ≤ *n-1*.

**Distinguished vertices**

*d*, 2*d*, ... , *rd*. The last one is replaced by $(n-1,n)$ when $n=rd+1.$

**Description**

Algebraic group : The same as for <sup>1</sup>D${}^{(d)}_{n,r}$, except that all forms

in question have now discriminant ≠ 1.

**Special fields**

Over a finite field, $d=1, n=r+1$; over the reals, $d=1,n-r=2m+1$ or $d=2,n=2r+1.$ Over

a *p*-adic or local function field, $d=1, n = r + 1$, or $d=2, n-2r=1,2,3$; over a real number field, $d=1,r$ is arbitrary, $d=2,n-2r=1,2,3,$ if *D* is non-split over the reals, and $d=2,r$ arbitrary, if *D* is split over the reals. Over a totally imaginary number field or global function field, $d=1,n-r=2,4$ or $d=2,n-2r=1,2,3$.

## F<sub>4</sub> {#F4}

### F${}^{52}_{4,0}$ {#F}

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_F4,0;52.svg.*

**Description: Algebraic Group**: The automorphism group of an exceptional simple [Jordan algebra](wikipedia://en/936371) *J* that does not contain nonzero [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/252235) elements.

**Special fields**: This type exists only over the reals and over some number fields; does not exist over finite fields, local non-archimedean nor global function fields.

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_F4,1;21.svg.*

**Description: Algebraic Group**: The automorphism group of an exceptional simple Jordan algebra *J* containing nonzero nilpotent elements, no two of which are nonproportional and orthogonal.

**Special fields**: This type exists only over the reals and over some number fields; does not exist over any finite field, nor local non-archimedean nor global function field.

### F${}^{0}_{4,4}$ {#F}

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_F4,4;0.svg.*

**Description: Algebraic Group**: The automorphism group of an exceptional simple Jordan algebra *J* containing nonproportional orthogonal nilpotent elements.

**Special fields**: This type exists over any field.

## G<sub>2</sub> {#G2}

A group of type G<sub>2</sub> is always the automorphism group of an [octonion algebra](wikipedia://en/10277277).[^o59]

### G${}^{14}_{2,0}$ {#G}

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_G2,0;14.svg.*

**Description: Algebraic group**: the automorphism group of a [division](wikipedia://en/26549625) octonion algebra.

**Special fields**: This type exists over the reals and some number fields; does not exist over any finite field, nor local non-archimedean nor global function field.

### G${}^{0}_{2,2}$ {#G}

**Image**:

*Image: tits\_index\_G2,2;0.svg.*

**Description: Algebraic group**: the automorphism group of a [split octonion algebra](wikipedia://en/3861649).

**Special fields**: This type exists over any field.
