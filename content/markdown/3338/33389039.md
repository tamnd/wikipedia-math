# Kolmogorov integral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kolmogorov integral"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Kolmogorov integral** (or **Kolmogoroff integral**) is a generalized integral introduced by (txt) including the [Lebesgue–Stieltjes integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue–Stieltjes_integral), the [Burkill integral](wikipedia://en/33388790), and the [Hellinger integral](wikipedia://en/33389115) as special cases. The integral is a limit over a directed family of partitions, when the resulting limiting value is independent of the tags of each partition segment.

:::
