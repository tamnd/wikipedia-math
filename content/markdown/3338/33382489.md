# Claude Ambrose Rogers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claude Ambrose Rogers"}

**Claude Ambrose Rogers** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] (1 November 1920 – 5 December 2005) was an English mathematician who worked in analysis and geometry.[^o5]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Much of the work of Rogers concerns the [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153), [Hausdorff measures](wikipedia://en/504109), [analytic sets](wikipedia://en/634261), [geometry of Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_of_Banach_spaces), [selection theorems](wikipedia://en/27035751) and finite-dimensional [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882).[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] In the theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) and [summability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summability), he proved the Dvoretzky–Rogers lemma and the [Dvoretzky–Rogers theorem](wikipedia://en/61532#Rearrangements_and_unconditional_convergence), both with [Aryeh Dvoretzky](wikipedia://en/19901447).[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15] He constructed a counterexample to a conjecture related to the [Busemann–Petty problem](wikipedia://en/33294058). In the [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153), the Rogers bound is a bound for dense packings of spheres.

In 2002 he coauthored the book *Selectors* with [John E. Jayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_E._Jayne) published by Princeton University Press.[^o16]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Rogers was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 1959](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_1959). He won the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)'s [De Morgan Medal](wikipedia://en/1096532) in 1977.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Rogers was married to children's writer [Joan North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_North).[^o6] They had two daughters, Jane and Petra.
