# John Charles Burkill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Charles Burkill"}

**John Charles Burkill** [^o2] (1 February 1900 – 6 April 1993) was an English mathematician who worked on analysis and introduced the [Burkill integral](wikipedia://en/33388790).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Burkill was born in [Holt, Norfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holt,_Norfolk), and educated at [St Paul's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Paul's_School,_London) and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he won the [Smith's Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize). He became a research fellow at Trinity in 1922, and two years later was appointed Professor of Pure Mathematics at [Liverpool University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_University).[^o4] In 1929, he returned to Cambridge to take up a position as [Reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29) in Mathematical Analysis, as a fellow not of Trinity but of [Peterhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhouse,_Cambridge). In 1948, he won the [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110), and was elected a fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 1953.[^o2][^o5][^o6] He was [Master of Peterhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Peterhouse) from 1968 to 1973. His doctoral students included [Frederick Gehring](wikipedia://en/35051046).

## Private life {#Private_life}

In 1928 he married [Margareta Braun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margareta_Burkill), who was born in Germany but educated at [Newnham College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge). Her father was German and her mother was Russian. Burkill and his wife had three children of their own, but Margareta arranged for hundreds of refugee children to come to Britain and some joined their household. Two became noted academics.[^o8] After Margareta's death in 1984 Burkill lived in [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield), where his adopted son Harry was based, and died there in 1993.[^o5]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}
