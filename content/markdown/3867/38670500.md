# Dietrich Braess

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dietrich Braess"}

**Dietrich Braess** (born 16 June 1938) is a German mathematician. He is known for [Braess's paradox](wikipedia://en/826885), which deals with [traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_congestion) [equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_types_of_equilibrium).[^o3] Braess's focus has centered on numerical treatment of elliptical [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and nonlinear approximation theories.[^o4]
