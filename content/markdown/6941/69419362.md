# Archie Blake (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archie Blake"}

*Image: ArchieBlakeSignature.jpg.* Signature of Archie Blake

Charles Archibald Blake (November 24, 1906 - January 7, 1971), name officially changed to **Archie Blake** [^o2] was an American mathematician. He is well known for the [Blake canonical form](wikipedia://en/39986184), a [normal form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_form_%28mathematics%29) for [expressions](wikipedia://en/609125) in [propositional logic](wikipedia://en/18154). In order to compute the canonical form, he moreover introduced the concept of [consensus](wikipedia://en/8325288), which was a precursor of the [resolution](wikipedia://en/2724082) principle, today a common technique in [automated theorem proving](wikipedia://en/2546).

:::

## Career {#Career}

In 1930, he became a member of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS).[^o4][^o5] He presented his canonical form at the AMS meeting at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) on 29 Oct 1932.[^o6] In 1937, this work lead to a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), supervised by Raymond Walter Barnard.

He worked for the [United States Coast and Geodetic Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Coast_and_Geodetic_Survey) in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), from 1936 (or earlier) as a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902),[^o8] since 1938 as an Assistant Mathematician,[^o9] and since 1939 as an Associated Mathematician.[^o10][^o11] In 1946, he was appointed a Senior Statistician in the Office of the [Army Surgeon General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Surgeon_General), [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)[^o12] He also worked for the [Cornell Aeronautical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_Aeronautical_Laboratory) in [Buffalo, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York). From there, he changed in 1954 to the [Westinghouse Electric Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westinghouse_Electric_Corporation) in [Baltimore, Md.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore,_Md.), where he became an Advisory Engineer.[^o13] In 1956, he moved from Westinghouse to the [Bendix Aviation Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bendix_Aviation_Corporation), as a Systems Staff Mathematician.[^o14] In 1960, he became a Manager of the Analysis Section of [Raytheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raytheon) in [Sudbury, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury,_Massachusetts).[^o15]
