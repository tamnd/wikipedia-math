# Makoto Soejima

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Makoto Soejima"}

**Makoto Soejima** is a Japanese former [competitive programmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitive_programming).[^o3] He is one of three people to have won both the [Google Code Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Code_Jam) and the [Facebook Hacker Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Hacker_Cup) and the only one to have also won a gold medal with a perfect score at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO).[^o3][^o7][^o4][^o11] In [International Science Olympiads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Olympiad), he has won three gold medals and one bronze in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) as well as two silver medals in the [International Olympiad in Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympiad_in_Informatics) (IOI).[^o3][^o11][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Soejima was born in 1991.[^o7] He began competitive programming in 1999.[^o5] He attended [Junior and Senior High School at Komaba, University of Tsukuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_and_Senior_High_School_at_Komaba,_University_of_Tsukuba).[^o8] During his time at junior and senior high school, he participated in the [IMO](wikipedia://en/82208) multiple times (2005, 2007–2009) where he obtained three gold medals and one bronze.[^o4][^o11][^o8] On his final attempt in 2009, he achieved a perfect score.[^o4][^o8] At the same time, Soejima also participated in the 2008 and 2009 [IOI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympiad_in_Informatics) where he obtained a silver medal both times.[^o3][^o8]

Soejima then attended [The University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) where he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o8] He was part of the university team in the 2013 and 2015 [International Collegiate Programming Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Collegiate_Programming_Contest) which won third place both times.[^o3] Soejima also attended the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology at The University of Tokyo.[^o9]

Soejima's other significant achievements in competitive programming include winning the 2011 [Google Code Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Code_Jam), winning the 2016 [Facebook Hacker Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Hacker_Cup), and being [Topcoder Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topcoder_Open) [Algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) champion in 2010, 2011 and 2016.[^o3][^o11]

In December 2020, Soejima retired from competitive programming.[^o5][^o10] By 2021 Soejima worked at AtCoder, a company that organizes programming competitions.[^o5][^o10][^o11]

## Achievements {#Achievements}

### Competitive programming {#Competitive_programming}

A more comprehensive list of achievements can be found at the Competitive Programming Hall Of Fame website.[^o3]

- International Olympiad in Informatics: 2 Silver (2008, 2009)[^o3]
- International Collegiate Programming Contest World Finals: 2 Gold medals (third place in 2013 and 2015)[^o3][^o11]
- Google Code Jam: Champion (2011), Second place (2019 and 2015), Third place (2018)[^o3][^o11]
- Facebook Hacker Cup: Champion (2016), Second place(2018), Third place (2014)[^o3][^o11]
- TopCoder Open: Algorithm champion (2016, 2011 and 2010)[^o3]
- Codeforces: Legendary Grandmaster (peak rating 3115)[^o14]

### Mathematics {#Mathematics}

International Mathematical Olympiad: 3 Gold (2007, 2008, 2009 (Perfect Score)) and 1 Bronze (2005)[^o4]
