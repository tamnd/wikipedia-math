# Malabika Pramanik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malabika Pramanik"}

**Malabika Pramanik** is a Canadian mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia). Her interests include [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_variable), and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pramanik studied statistics at the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), earning a bachelor's degree in 1993 and a master's in 1995. She then moved to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where she completed a doctorate in mathematics in 2001. Her dissertation, *Weighted Integrals in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the Maximal Conjugated Calderon–Zygmund Operator*, was supervised by [F. Michael Christ](wikipedia://en/58516365). After short-term positions at the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin), [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), and [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), she joined the UBC faculty in 2006. She was appointed director of the [Banff International Research Station](wikipedia://en/39151817) in 2020. In 2025, she was elected vice president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). [^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Pramanik is the 2015–2016 winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), and the 2016 winner of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829), given annually by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) to an outstanding female researcher in mathematics. In 2018 the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed her in their inaugural class of fellows. She was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 "for contributions to complex and harmonic analysis and mentoring and support for the participation of under-represented groups in mathematics". Pramanik was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2022.
