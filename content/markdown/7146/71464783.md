# Vladimir Kondratiev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Kondratiev"}

**Vladimir Aleksandrovich Kondratiev** (Владимир Александрович Кондратьев; 2 July 1935 – 11 March 2010) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).[^o2] He worked particularly in the field of [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o3]

:::
