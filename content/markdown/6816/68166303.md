# Harold N. Gabow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harold N. Gabow"}

**Harold N. Gabow** is a computer scientist known for research on [combinatorial algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_algorithms), [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithm) and [data structures](wikipedia://en/8519). He is a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder), and founding Editor-in-Chief of *[ACM Transactions on Algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Transactions_on_Algorithms)*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gabow graduated from [Martin Van Buren High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Van_Buren_High_School), where he was mentored by Ira Ewen. He graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1968, with a bachelor's degree in mathematics. He completed his Ph.D. in computer science in 1973 at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University); his dissertation, *Implementations of algorithms for maximum matching on nonbipartite graphs*, was supervised by [Harold S. Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_S._Stone).

After working as an instructor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) for a year, he joined the University of Colorado Boulder faculty in 1973 as an Assistant Professor of Computer Science, becoming Associate Professor in 1979, Full Professor in 1986, Professor Emeritus in 2008.

Gabow was the founding Editor-in-Chief of *[ACM Transactions on Algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Transactions_on_Algorithms)* (TALG), which published its first issue in 2005, after the mass resignation of the editorial board of its predecessor, [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier)'s *Journal of Algorithms*. He stepped down as Editor-in-Chief on his retirement in 2008.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Gabow was named as an [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow) in 2002, "for contributions to efficient algorithms to flows, connectivity and matching". With co-authors M. Goemans, E. Tardos and D. Williamson he won the 2009 Glover-Klingman Prize for best paper of the year in [Networks: An International Journal](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10970037?journalRedirectCheck=true). He was awarded the [SIGACT Distinguished Service Prize](https://www.sigact.org/prizes/service.html) in 2010.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Gabow is married to physician and healthcare executive [Patricia A. Gabow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_A._Gabow).
