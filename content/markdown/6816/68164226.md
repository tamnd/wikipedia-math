# Wilberd van der Kallen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilberd van der Kallen"}

van der Kallen **Wilberd Leo Johan van der Kallen** (born 15 January 1947 in [Nieuwer-Amstel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuwer-Amstel))[^o2] is a Dutch mathematician.

:::

W. L. J. van der Kallen completed his undergraduate study of mathematics and physics at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University).[^o3] There he received his PhD in 1973 with thesis advisor [T. A. Springer](wikipedia://en/10836474) and thesis *Infinitesimally central extensions of Chevalley groups*. In 1969 Van der Kallen became a teaching assistant in Utrecht University's Mathematics Department and has spent his career there, eventually becoming a tenured professor. His research deals with algebraic K-theory and the representation theory of algebraic groups, among other topics. He has frequently been a visiting professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in [Evanston, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Illinois) and at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai).[^o3]

He is the author or coauthor of over 60 research articles.[^o3] In 1977 he published an analogue of a 1977 theorem of [Andrei Suslin](wikipedia://en/7111820)[^o5][^o6] and a generalization of a 1969 theorem of [Hideya Matsumoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hideya_Matsumoto).[^o7] In 1978 Van der Kallen was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Helsinki.[^o8] His 1980 paper *Homology stability for linear groups*[^o9] has over 200 citations. His 1977 paper *Rational and generic cohomology*, written with 3 other mathematicians,[^o10] has over 240 citations.
