# Damla Şentürk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Damla Şentürk"}

**Damla Şentürk** is a Turkish-American biostatistician and professor of biostatistics in the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) [Fielding School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_Fielding_School_of_Public_Health) whose interests include [longitudinal studies](wikipedia://en/1557623), [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368), and applications of biostatistics in the study of [autism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autism) and of [dialysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidney_dialysis) outcomes.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Şentürk studied mathematics at [Boğaziçi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boğaziçi_University), graduating in 1999. She came to the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) for graduate study in statistics, earned a master's degree in 2001, and completed her PhD at Davis in 2004. Her dissertation, *Covariate Adjusted Regression and Correlation*, was supervised by Hans-Georg Müller.

She became an assistant professor of statistics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 2004, and moved to the UCLA Department of Biostatistics in 2011, adding a joint appointment with the UCLA Department of Statistics in 2014.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Şentürk became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2006. She was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2020.
