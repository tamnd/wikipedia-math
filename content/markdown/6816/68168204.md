# Alexander A. Voronov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander A. Voronov"}

**Alexander A. Voronov** (Александр Александрович Воронов) (born November 25, 1962) is a [Russian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) and a Visiting Senior Scientist at the [Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe](wikipedia://en/34880544).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Voronov graduated from [Moscow State School 57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_School_57) in 1980. He received an M.S. in Mathematics in 1985 and a Ph.D. in Mathematics at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University) in 1988 under [Yuri I. Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086).[^o5] Alexander Voronov is known for his work on the super Mumford isomorphism (see [Mumford measure](wikipedia://en/37260652)), semi-infinite cohomology, operads in quantum field theory (see [Swiss-cheese operad](wikipedia://en/3126358#Swiss-cheese_operad)), [Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544#Concepts_named_after_Deligne)'s and [Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509)'s conjectures on Hochschild cohomology, cohomology of vertex operator algebras, and string topology (see [cactus operad](wikipedia://en/28725806#The_Batalin–Vilkovisky_structure)). He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), an AMS Centennial Fellow,, a [Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) Fellow, and a 2010 [Japan Society for the Promotion of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Society_for_the_Promotion_of_Science) (JSPS) Research Fellow.
