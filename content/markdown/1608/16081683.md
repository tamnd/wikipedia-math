# Imaginary element

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Imaginary element"}

In [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **imaginary element** of a structure is roughly a definable [equivalence class](wikipedia://en/9260). These were introduced by (Shelah 1990), and **elimination of imaginaries** was introduced by (Poizat 1983).

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

- *M* is a [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28model_theory%29) of some [theory](wikipedia://en/3512103).
- **x** and **y** stand for *n*-tuples of variables, for some [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) *n*.
- An **equivalence formula** is a [formula](wikipedia://en/404582) φ(**x**, **y**) that is a [symmetric](wikipedia://en/200459) and [transitive](wikipedia://en/200463) [relation](wikipedia://en/3931). Its domain is the [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of elements **a** of *M*<sup>hair*n*</sup> such that φ(**a**, **a**); it is an [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259) on its domain.
- An **imaginary element** **a**/φ of *M* is an equivalence formula φ together with an equivalence class **a**.
- *M* has **elimination of imaginaries** if for every imaginary element **a**/φ there is a formula θ(**x**, **y**) such that there is a unique tuple **b** so that the equivalence class of **a** consists of the tuples **x** such that θ(**x**, **b**).
- A model has **uniform elimination of imaginaries** if the formula θ can be chosen independently of **a**.
- A theory has **elimination of imaginaries** if every model of that theory does (and similarly for uniform elimination).

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- [ZFC set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZFC) has elimination of imaginaries.
- [Peano arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_arithmetic) has uniform elimination of imaginaries.
- A [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/38267) at least 2 over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with at least 3 elements does not have elimination of imaginaries.

:::
