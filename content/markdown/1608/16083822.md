# Baily–Borel compactification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baily–Borel compactification"}

In mathematics, the **Baily–Borel compactification** is a [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) of a quotient of a [Hermitian symmetric space](wikipedia://en/4127357) by an [arithmetic group](wikipedia://en/630741), introduced by ().

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

- If *C* is the quotient of the upper half plane by a [congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694) of SL<sub>2</sub>(**Z**), then the Baily–Borel compactification of *C* is formed by adding a finite number of cusps to it.

:::
