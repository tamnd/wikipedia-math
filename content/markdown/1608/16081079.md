# Population study

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Population study"}

**Population study** is an [interdisciplinary field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinarity) of [scientific study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_studies) that uses various [statistical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_methods) and models to analyse, determine, address, and predict population challenges and trends from data collected through various [data collection](wikipedia://en/11501746) methods such as [population census](wikipedia://en/6889), registration method, [sampling](wikipedia://en/160361), and some other systems of data sources.[^o2] In the various fields of healthcare, a **population study** is a study of a group of individuals taken from the general [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_%28human_biology%29) who share a common characteristic, such as age, [sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_sex), or health condition. This group may be studied for different reasons, such as their [response to a drug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_trial) or [risk of getting a disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_rate_test).

:::
