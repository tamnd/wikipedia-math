In [six-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six-dimensional_space) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **omnitruncated 6-simplex honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is composed entirely of [omnitruncated 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_6-simplex) facets.

:::

The facets of all [omnitruncated simplectic honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_simplectic_honeycomb) are called [permutahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutahedron) and can be positioned in *n+1* space with integral coordinates, permutations of the whole numbers (0,1,..,n).

## A\* lattice {#A_lattice}

The A\* lattice (also called A7) is the union of seven [A<sub>6</sub> lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A6_lattice), and has the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the dual to the *omnitruncated 6-simplex honeycomb*, and therefore the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of this lattice is the [omnitruncated 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_6-simplex).
