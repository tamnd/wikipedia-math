# Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences"}

*Image: LE Max Planck MIS MPI.jpg.* The building that hosts the MPI MiS

The **Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences** (MPI MiS) in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) is a [research institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute) of the [Max Planck Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society). Founded on March 1, 1996, the institute works on projects which apply [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in various areas of [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science), in particular [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) and [material science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_science).

:::

## Research groups {#Research_groups}

- Geometry and complex systems ([Jürgen Jost](wikipedia://en/41172240))
- [Pattern formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_formation), [energy landscapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_landscape) and [scaling laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scaling_law) ([Felix Otto](wikipedia://en/4248548))
- [Nonlinear algebra](wikipedia://en/58666409) ([Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels))
- Applied analysis (László Székelyhidi)
- Geometry, groups and dynamics ([Anna Wienhard](wikipedia://en/45112397))
- Discrete sets in geometry (Samantha Fairchild)
- Geometry on surfaces (James Reed Farre)
- Stochastic partial differential equations (Benjamin Gess)
- Mathematical software (Michael Joswig)
- Mathematical machine learning (Guido Montúfar)
- Learning and inference (Sayan Mukherjee)
- Interdisciplinary frontiers of algebraic geometry (İrem Portakal)
- Probability and variational methods in PDEs (Tobias Ried)
- Mathematical structures in physics (Daniel Roggenkamp)
- Stochastic topology and its applications (Érika Roldán)
- Numerical nonlinear algebra (Simon Telen)

The institute has an extensive visitors programme which has made [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) a main place for research in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

The MPI MiS is a member of ERCOM (European Research Centres in Mathematics).
