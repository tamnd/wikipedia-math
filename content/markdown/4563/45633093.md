# Paul C. Yang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul C. Yang"}

**Paul C. Yang** (; born 1947) is a [Taiwanese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [CR manifolds](wikipedia://en/3131794). He is best known for his work in Conformal geometry for his study of extremal metrics and his research on [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) and Q-curvature. In CR Geometry he is known for his work on the CR embedding problem, the CR [Paneitz operator](wikipedia://en/23695030) and for introducing the Q' curvature in CR Geometry.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Yang received a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.A.) in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 1969. He then earned his doctorate from Berkeley in 1973 under the supervision of Hung-Hsi Wu (). He held positions at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland), [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) and the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) before joining [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2001.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Yang was a [Sloan Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Foundation) Fellow in 1981.[^o5] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
