# Jennifer Hom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jennifer Hom"}

**Jennifer Cheung Hom** is an American mathematician whose research concerns [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), including Heegaard [Floer homology](wikipedia://en/2672856) and [link concordance](wikipedia://en/17519029). She is a professor of mathematics at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hom majored in applied physics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), with a minor in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), graduating [magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_cum_laude) in 2004. She became a doctoral student of Paul Melvin at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), completing a Ph.D. in 2011 with the dissertation *Heegaard Floer invariants and cabling*.

She returned to Columbia University as Ritt Assistant Professor from 2011 to 2015, when she moved to [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech). She was tenured there as an associate professor in 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Hom was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to low-dimensional topology, Heegaard Floer homology, and service to the mathematical community". In 2024 she was awarded the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) of the American Mathematical Society.[^o4]
