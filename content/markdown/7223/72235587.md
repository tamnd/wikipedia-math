# Moses Haida

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moses Haida"}

**Moses ben Joseph Haida** (משה בן יוסף היידא; 17th–18th centuries) was a German mathematician from [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg). He was a grandson of [Samuel Haida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Haida), author of *Zikkukin de-nura*. He was the author of *Sefer ma'aseh ḥoresh ve-ḥoshev*, an arithmetic, written at the time of the great fire of [Altona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altona,_Hamburg) (Frankfurt, 1711).[^o2]

:::
