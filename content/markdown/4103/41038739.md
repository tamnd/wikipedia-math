# Spherical category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spherical category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, a **spherical category** is a [pivotal category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pivotal_category) (a [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604) with [traces](wikipedia://en/7984007)) in which left and right traces coincide.[^o2] Spherical [fusion categories](wikipedia://en/53762899) give rise to a family of three-dimensional topological state sum models (a particular formulation of a [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757)[^o3]), the **Turaev-Viro model**, or rather **Turaev-Viro-Barrett-Westbury model**.[^o4]

:::
