# Ross Street

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ross Street"}

**Ross Howard Street** (born 29 September 1945, Sydney) is an Australian mathematician specialising in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Street completed his undergraduate and postgraduate study at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), where his dissertation advisor was [Max Kelly](wikipedia://en/18996838). He is an emeritus professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University), a fellow of the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582) (1995), and was elected [Fellow of the Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science) in 1989.[^o7] He was awarded the [Edgeworth David Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgeworth_David_Medal) of the Royal Society of New South Wales in 1977, and the Australian Mathematical Society's [George Szekeres Medal](wikipedia://en/23270711) in 2012.[^o3]
