# Farideh Firoozbakht

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Farideh Firoozbakht"}

**Farideh Firoozbakht** (فریده فیروزبخت; 18 March 1962 – 24 December 2019) was an Iranian mathematician. She stated [Firoozbakht's conjecture](wikipedia://en/36793030) on the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666) in 1982.[^o2][^o3] She studied [pharmacology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacology) and later mathematics at the [University of Isfahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Isfahan) and taught mathematics at Iranian universities including the University of Isfahan.[^o4]
