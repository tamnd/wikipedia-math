# Warren Goldfarb

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Warren Goldfarb"}

**Warren David Goldfarb** (born 1949) is an American logician and philosopher who is Walter Beverly Pearson Professor of Modern Mathematics and Mathematical Logic, emeritus, at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He specializes in the history of [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211) and in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), most notably the classical [decision problem](wikipedia://en/913118).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received his A.B. and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under the supervision of [Burton Dreben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burton_Dreben), and has been a member of the Harvard faculty since 1975. He received [tenure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenure) in 1982, the only philosopher to be promoted to tenure at Harvard between 1962 and 1999.[^o4]

Prof. Goldfarb is also one of the founders of the [Harvard Gay & Lesbian Caucus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Gay_&_Lesbian_Caucus) and was one of the first openly gay Harvard faculty members.[^o5][^o6]

## Philosophical work {#Philosophical_work}

Goldfarb was an editor of volumes III–V of [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736)'s *Collected Works*. He has also published articles on important analytic philosophers, including [Frege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frege), [Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), [Wittgenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittgenstein)'s early and later work, [Carnap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnap) and [Quine](wikipedia://en/33833).[^o8]
