# Epps effect

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Epps effect"}

In [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) and [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis), the **Epps effect**, named after T. W. Epps, is the phenomenon that the empirical [correlation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_correlation) between the returns of two different [stocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital) decreases with the length of the interval for which the price changes are measured. The phenomenon is caused by non-synchronous/asynchronous trading[^o2] and discretization effects.[^o3] Another study suggests that the effect also originates in investors' [herd behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herd_behavior_%28economics%29).[^o4]

:::
