# Delaram Kahrobaei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Delaram Kahrobaei"}

**Delaram Kahrobaei** is an [Iranian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). She is a full professor at [Queens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens_College,_City_University_of_New_York), [City University of New York (CUNY)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), with appointments in the Departments of Computer Science[^o5] and Mathematics.[^o6] Her research focuses on [post-quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/26605226), and the applied algebra.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Delaram Kahrobaei obtained her undergraduate degree in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_University_of_Technology) in 1998. She earned a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Claremont Graduate University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont_Graduate_University) and a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from [The City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York). In 2004, she completed her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics at the [CUNY Graduate Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUNY_Graduate_Center) under the supervision of [Gilbert Baumslag](wikipedia://en/11449648). Her doctoral thesis was on the residual solvability of generalized free products.[^o8]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Delaram Kahrobaei became an Assistant Professor in [Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of St. Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) in 2004. From 2006 she was faculty of Mathematics at [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) until 2018 that she joined the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York), where she was the Chair of [Cyber Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_Security) in the Department of Computer Science and served as the founding director of the York Interdisciplinary Centre for Cyber Security[^o10] until 2021. She is visiting honorary professor of computer science at University of York[^o11] since 2021. She is also Adjunct Professor of computer science and engineering at [New York University Tandon School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Tandon_School_of_Engineering)[^o12]

She has held visiting professor positions at [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (IHES), [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) (IHP), [Sorbonne Université](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University), [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), [Polytechnic University of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Catalonia), [University of Salerno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salerno), [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL).[^o13]

Kahrobaei is an active member of various international networks focused on [cybersecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) and responsible data science. She is involved with the Initiative for the Theoretical Sciences at [CUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), serves on the Friends of [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) Board of Directors,[^o14] and is a member of the Quantum Security via Algebras and Representation Theory (QUASAR) project[^o15] based at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa). She co-founded and served as President of the university start-up Infoshield, Inc. She has been a scientific board member for several organizations, including NodeQ (a US-UK based quantum start-up), and LifeNome Inc., a biotech company.
