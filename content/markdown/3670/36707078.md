# Frits Beukers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frits Beukers"}

**Frits Beukers** (; born 1953 in [Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara), Turkey) is a Dutch mathematician, who works on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [hypergeometric functions](wikipedia://en/2055223).

:::

In 1979 Beukers received his PhD at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) under the direction of [Robert Tijdeman](wikipedia://en/9006087) with thesis *The generalized [Ramanujan–Nagell Equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan–Nagell_Equation)*, published in *[Acta Arithmetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Arithmetica)*, vol. 38, 1980/1981. From 1979 to 1980 he was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o2] He became a professor in Leiden and in the 2000s at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University).

Beukers works on questions of [transcendence](wikipedia://en/30325) and [irrationality](wikipedia://en/20647689) in number theory, and on other topics. In connection with the famous proof by [Roger Apéry](wikipedia://en/1421250) (1978) on the irrationality of the values of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) evaluated at the points 2 and 3, Beukers gave a much simpler alternate proof using [Legendre polynomials](wikipedia://en/100349). He also published on questions in mechanics about [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and their exact solvability.
