# Szász–Mirakjan–Kantorovich operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Szász–Mirakjan–Kantorovich operator"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a discipline within [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Szász–Mirakjan–Kantorovich operators** are defined by

:::

$$[\mathcal{T}_n(f)](x)=ne^{-nx}\sum_{k=0}^\infty{\frac{(nx)^k}{k!}\int_{k/n}^{(k+1)/n}f(t)\,dt}$$

where $x\in[0,\infty)\subset\mathbb{R}$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}$.[^o2]
