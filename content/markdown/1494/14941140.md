# Mittenpunkt

*Image: Mandart inellipse - resized.svg.* solid #333333 solid red solid blue solid lime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mittenpunkt"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **mittenpunkt** (from [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *middle point*) of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488): a point defined from the triangle that is invariant under [Euclidean transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_transformation) of the triangle. It was identified in 1836 by [Christian Heinrich von Nagel](wikipedia://en/21413563) as the [symmedian](wikipedia://en/320997) point of the [excentral triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excentral_triangle) of the given triangle.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The mittenpunkt has [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790)[^o2]

$$(b+c-a): (c+a-b ):(a+b-c)$$

where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are the side lengths of the given triangle. Expressed instead in terms of the angles $A$, $B$, and $C$, the trilinears are[^o5]

$$\cot \frac{A}{2} : \cot \frac{B}{2} : \cot \frac{C}{2}=(\csc A+\cot A):(\csc B+\cot B):(\csc C+\cot C).$$

The [barycentric coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycentric_coordinates_%28mathematics%29) are[^o5]

$$a(b+c-a):b(c+a-b):c(a+b-c) = (1+\cos A):(1+\cos B):(1+\cos C).$$

## Collinearities {#Collinearities}

The mittenpunkt is at the intersection of the line connecting the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) and the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_point), the line connecting the [incenter](wikipedia://en/316837) and the [symmedian point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmedian_point) and the line connecting the [orthocenter](wikipedia://en/161244) with the [Spieker center](wikipedia://en/35728942), thus establishing three [collinearities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collinear_points) involving the mittenpunkt.[^o7]

## Related figures {#Related_figures}

The three lines connecting the excenters of the given triangle to the corresponding edge midpoints all meet at the mittenpunkt; thus, it is the [center of perspective](wikipedia://en/14909067) of the excentral triangle and the median triangle, with the [corresponding axis of perspective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desargues'_theorem) being the trilinear polar of the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_point).[^o9] The mittenpunkt is also the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) of the [Mandart inellipse](wikipedia://en/14974602) of the given triangle, the ellipse tangent to the triangle at its [extouch points](wikipedia://en/11125142).[^o10]

The mittenpunkt also serves as the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_point) of the [medial triangle](wikipedia://en/3339743).
