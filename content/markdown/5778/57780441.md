# Alexander L. Rosenberg

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander L. Rosenberg"}

**Alexander Lvovich Rosenberg** (Александр Львович Розенберг, 1946–2012)[^o4] was a Russian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [noncommutative algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/25493347).[^o5] He graduated from [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Moscow_State_University) in 1973, left the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) around 1987, and was a professor at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University) until 2012.

:::

He is known for his contributions to [Tannaka duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tannaka-Krein_duality) and [noncommutative algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/25493347). He introduced several notions of spectrum for an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063) (Rosenberg's spectrum), and the related [Gabriel-Rosenberg reconstruction theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel-Rosenberg_reconstruction_theorem) bears his name.[^o6]
