# Rachel Roberts (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rachel Roberts"}

**Rachel Roberts** is an American mathematician specializing in [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), including [foliations](wikipedia://en/371227) and [contact geometry](wikipedia://en/476398). She is the Elinor Anheuser Professor of Mathematics at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis).

:::

Roberts completed her Ph.D. at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1992. Her dissertation, supervised by [Allen Hatcher](wikipedia://en/1112366), was *Constructing Taut Foliations*. She was elected to the 2026 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
