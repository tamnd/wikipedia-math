# Julianna Tymoczko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julianna Tymoczko"}

**Julianna Sophia Tymoczko** (born 1975) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research connects [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), including [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [Schubert calculus](wikipedia://en/3061740), [equivariant cohomology](wikipedia://en/2976342), and [Hessenberg varieties](wikipedia://en/25287284). She is a professor of mathematics at [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tymoczko grew up in [Western Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Massachusetts), and studied [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) at Smith College as a high school student. She was an undergraduate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and wrote a senior thesis on the [homotopy groups of spheres](wikipedia://en/1104697), *The $p$-components of the stable homotopy groups of spheres*, with [Joe Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251) and [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569) as faculty mentors. After graduating in 1998, she moved to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) for graduate study, and completed her Ph.D. there in 2003. Her dissertation, *Decomposing Hessenberg Varieties over Classical Groups*, was supervised by [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121).

After being a Clay Liftoff Fellow, NSF Postdoctoral Fellow, and Hildebrandt Assistant Professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), she took a tenure-track position at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) in 2007. In 2011 she returned to Smith College as a faculty member. She was promoted to full professor in 2019.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Tymoczko was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 class, for "contributions to algebraic geometry and combinatorics, and for outreach and mentorship".

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Tymoczko is one of three children of [Thomas Tymoczko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Tymoczko), a logician and philosopher of mathematics at [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College), and comparative literature scholar [Maria Tymoczko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Tymoczko) of the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst). Her brother, [Dmitri Tymoczko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Tymoczko), is a music composer and music theorist. She is married to [Marshall Poe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Poe), a historian at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
