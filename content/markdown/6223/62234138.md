# Eric M. Opdam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eric M. Opdam"}

*Image: Opdam eric.jpg.* Opdam at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2014

**Eric Marcus Opdam** (born 1960) is a Dutch mathematician, specializing in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). He is one of the two namesakes of [Heckman–Opdam polynomials](wikipedia://en/32049415).

:::

Opdam received his PhD from [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) in 1988 under the supervision of [Gerrit van Dijk](wikipedia://en/75286185). Opdam is a professor at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). He has been at the [Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/66341573) (KdVI) since 1999. From 2015 he is the KdVI's director as the successor to Jan Wiegerinck.[^o3]

Opdam's research deals with analytic aspects of [Iwahori–Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/521808), with hypergeometric functions associated with Lie algebra root systems (Heckman-Opdam hypergeometric functions), and with [Dunkl operators](wikipedia://en/947234) on complex reflection groups.

Opdam was an invited speaker in 2000 with talk *Hecke algebras and harmonic analysis* at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Barcelona. In 2006 he was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid.[^o4] He was elected a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2012.[^o5] In 2010 he received an Advanced Grant from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC).

He is one of four managing editors of [Compositio Mathematica](wikipedia://en/31882322).
