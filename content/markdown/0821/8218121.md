# Computer-adaptive sequential testing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Computer-adaptive sequential testing"}

**Computer-adaptive sequential testing** (**CAST**) is another term for [multistage testing](wikipedia://en/16006433). A CAST test is a type of [computer-adaptive test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-adaptive_test) or [computerized classification test](wikipedia://en/7955447) that uses pre-defined groups of items called *[testlets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testlet)* rather than operating at the level of individual items.[^o2] CAST is a term introduced by [psychometricians](wikipedia://en/24982) working for the [National Board of Medical Examiners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Board_of_Medical_Examiners).[^o3] In CAST, the testlets are referred to as *panels*.

:::
