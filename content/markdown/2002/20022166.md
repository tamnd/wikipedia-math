# Charles Sims (mathematician)

*Image: Charles Sims.jpg.* Charles C. Sims at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2006

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Sims"}

**Charles Coffin Sims** (April 14, 1937 – October 23, 2017[^o2][^o3]) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for his work in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).[^o4] Together with [Donald G. Higman](wikipedia://en/7474917) he discovered the [Higman–Sims group](wikipedia://en/403178), one of the [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806). The [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) software developed by Sims also led to the proof of existence of the [Lyons group](wikipedia://en/1110773) (also known as the Lyons–Sims group) and the [O'Nan group](wikipedia://en/1196724) (also known as the O'Nan–Sims group).

:::

Sims was born and raised in [Elkhart, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elkhart,_Indiana), and received his [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o4] He did his graduate studies at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he was a student of [John G. Thompson](wikipedia://en/862189) and received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree in 1963. In his thesis, he enumerated [*p*-groups](wikipedia://en/24664), giving sharp asymptotic upper and lower bounds. Sims is one of the founders of [computational group theory](wikipedia://en/618584) and is the [eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym) of the [Schreier–Sims algorithm](wikipedia://en/893640). He was a faculty member at the Department of Mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University–New_Brunswick) from 1965 to 2007. During that period he served, in particular, as Department Chair (1982–84) and Associate Provost for Computer Planning (1984–87). Sims retired from Rutgers in 2007 and moved to [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg,_Florida), Florida.[^o5]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
