# Hellmuth Stachel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hellmuth Stachel"}

**Hellmuth Stachel** (born October 6, 1942, in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a professor of geometry at the [Technical University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Vienna), who is known due to his contributions to [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [kinematics](wikipedia://en/65914) and [computer-aided design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-aided_design).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Stachel was born on October 6, 1942, in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz) to the family of primary school teachers. He graduated from an elementary school in [Trofaiach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trofaiach) and secondary school (gymnasium) in [Leoben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leoben). In 1965 he graduated from the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz) and [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology), obtaining a diploma of a school teacher in “[mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)” and “[descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891).” As a Ph.D. student of Graz University he majored in “mathematics, [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy).” His doctoral advisor was Professor Fritz Hohenberg. Hellmuth Stachel obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1969 from Graz University and passed thought [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1971 at the Technical University of Graz. In 1980 he moved to the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology).

Stachel held several visiting positions in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China): in August 1984 he was a visiting professor at [South China University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_China_University_of_Technology) in [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou) and, in October 1989, at [Tongji University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongji_University) in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai).

Stachel takes an active part in the work of [“International Society for Geometry and Graphics”](http://www.isgg.net/) (ISGG). In 1990 he was elected a chairman and, in 1994, a vice-president of the society. In 1996 he has created the scientific journal [Journal for Geometry and Graphics](http://www.geometrie.tuwien.ac.at/jgg/) and since then serves it as the Editor-in-Chief.

## Research {#Research}

Stachel wrote three books (in cooperation with other scholars) and approximately 120 scientific articles on classical and descriptive [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [kinematics](wikipedia://en/65914) and the theory of mechanisms, as well as on [computer aided design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_aided_design). He studied [flexible polyhedra](wikipedia://en/9070022) in the 4-dimensional Euclidean space and 3-dimensional [Lobachevsky space](wikipedia://en/241291).

## Awards and prizes {#Awards_and_prizes}

In 1991 Stachel was elected a corresponding member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1993 he received the “German-Austrian University-Software Award” for the development of educational Cad-3D programs. In 2004 he received [The Steve M. Slaby Award](https://web.archive.org/web/20100115045137/http://www.isgg.net/slaby_award.html). On November, 1st 2010 Hellmuth Stachel received an honorary doctorate from the [Dresden University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_University_of_Technology).[^o6]
