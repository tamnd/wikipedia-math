# Mohammad Saleh Zarepour

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mohammad Saleh Zarepour"}

**Mohammad Saleh Zarepour** is an [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_peoples) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and senior lecturer at the Department of Philosophy of the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester). He is a winner of [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize) (2023) and a Life Member of [Clare Hall College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_Hall_College).[^o3] Zarepour is known for his works on [medieval Islamic philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_Islamic_philosophy), [philosophy of religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_religion), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), and [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Books {#Books}

- *Medieval Finitism*, Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2025
- *Necessary Existence and Monotheism: An Avicennian Account of the Islamic Conception of Divine Unity*, Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2022[^o8]

### edited {#edited}

- *Logic, Soul, and World: Essays in Arabic Philosophy in Honor of Tony Street*, edited with Asad Q. Ahmed and Riccardo Strobino, Leiden: Brill, 2025
- *Global Dialogues in the Philosophy of Religion From Religious Experience to the Afterlife*, edited with [Yujin Nagasawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yujin_Nagasawa), New York: Oxford University Press, 2024
- *Islamic Philosophy of Religion*, New York: Routledge, 2024[^o10]
- *Mathematics, Logic, and their Philosophies*, edited with Mojtaba Mojtahedi and Shahid Rahman, Dordrecht: Springer, 2021
