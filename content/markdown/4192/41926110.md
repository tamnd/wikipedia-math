In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 tesseractic honeycomb** is one of five compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,3,5}, it has five [8-cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cell) (also known as tesseracts) around each face. Acronym: pitest[^n1]

:::

Its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) is the [order-4 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926407), {5,3,3,4}.

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

It is related to the Euclidean 4-space (order-4) [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139), {4,3,3,4}, and the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215), {4,3,3,3} in Euclidean 5-space. The *5-cube* can also be seen as an *order-3 tesseractic honeycomb* on the surface of a [4-sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-sphere).
