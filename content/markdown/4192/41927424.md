In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-4 24-cell honeycomb** is one of two paracompact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is called *paracompact* because it has infinite [vertex figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_figures), with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,4,3,4}, it has four [24-cells](wikipedia://en/293485) around each face. Acronym: n/a[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [cubic honeycomb honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41927402).

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the regular Euclidean 4-space [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), {3,4,3,3}, with [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) facets.
