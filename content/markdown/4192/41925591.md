# Society for Economic Measurement

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Society for Economic Measurement"}

The **Society for Economic Measurement**, or **SEM**, is a scientific [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) in the field of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics). It was founded on August 24, 2013 by [William A. Barnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_A._Barnett) in order to "promote research on economic measurement, using advanced tools from economic theory, econometrics, aggregation theory, experimental economics, mathematics, and statistics".[^o5] Nobel Laureate [Apostolos Serletis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolos_Serletis) took over as the society's second president in 2019. In 2024, Gaetano Antinolfi became the third and current president of the society. The goal of the SEM is to promote in economics—given the constraints of a [social science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science)—the implementation of the strict rules of measurement and data gathering standards used in the [physical sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_sciences). [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), the [Center for Financial Stability](http://www.centerforfinancialstability.org/), and the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) are sponsors of the society.

:::

## Activities {#Activities}

- The **inaugural conference** of the SEM was held at the [Harper Center](http://www.chicagobooth.edu/about/campuses/hyde-park) of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago)'s [Booth School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booth_School_of_Business) on August 18–20, 2014.[^o7] It was co-sponsored by the [Becker Friedman Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Becker_Friedman_Institute_for_Research_in_Economics) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).
- The 2nd SEM conference was held at the OECD in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) on July 22–24, 2015.
- The 3rd SEM conference was held in [Thessaloniki, Greece](http://sem.society.cmu.edu/Thessaloniki.html) on July 6–8, 2016.
- The 4th SEM conference was held at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) on July 26–28, 2017.
- The 5th SEM conference was held at [Xiamen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen_University) on Xiamen Island in China in June 2108.
- The 6th SEM Conference was held at [Goethe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University) in Frankfurt, with cosponsorship by the [European Central Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Central_Bank), on August 16–18, 2019.
- The 7th SEM Conference was held at [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) in Calgary, on August 11–14, 2022.
- The 8th SEM Conference was held at [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milano-Bicocca) in Milan, with cosponsorship by the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan), on June 29–July 1, 2023.
- The 9th SEM Conference will be held at [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology) in Atlanta, on August 1–3, 2024.
