In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **cubic honeycomb honeycomb** is one of two paracompact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is called *paracompact* because it has infinite [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647), whose vertices exist on 3-[horospheres](wikipedia://en/13487128) and converge to a single [ideal point](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,4,3}, it has three [cubic honeycombs](wikipedia://en/3733318) around each face, and with a [{3,4,3}](wikipedia://en/293485) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). Acronym: chont[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [order-4 24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41927424).

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the Euclidean 4-space [16-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9409435), {3,3,4,3}, which also has a [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) vertex figure.

It is analogous to the paracompact [tesseractic honeycomb honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesseractic_honeycomb_honeycomb), {4,3,3,4,3}, in 5-dimensional hyperbolic space, [square tiling honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40319864), {4,4,3}, in 3-dimensional hyperbolic space, and the [order-3 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38758064), {∞,3} of 2-dimensional hyperbolic space, each with [hypercube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercube_honeycomb) facets.
