In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-4 120-cell honeycomb** is one of five compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,3,4}, it has four [120-cells](wikipedia://en/717358) around each face. Acronym: shitte[^n1]

:::

Its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) is the [order-5 tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926110), {4,3,3,5}.

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the (order-3) [120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976507), and [order-5 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926439).
