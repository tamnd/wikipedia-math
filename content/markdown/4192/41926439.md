In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 120-cell honeycomb** is one of five compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,3,5}, it has five [120-cells](wikipedia://en/717358) around each face. Acronym: phitte[^n1]

:::

It is self-[dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope). It also has 600 120-cells around each vertex.

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Birectified order-5 120-cell"}

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the (order-3) [120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976507), and [order-4 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926407). It is analogous to the [order-5 dodecahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3870545) and [order-5 pentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38358615).

:::

## Birectified order-5 120-cell honeycomb {#Birectified_order-5_120-cell_honeycomb}

The birectified order-5 120-cell honeycomb constructed by all [rectified 600-cells](wikipedia://en/2720121), with [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) cells, and triangle faces with a 5-5 [duoprism](wikipedia://en/2114155) vertex figure and has extended symmetry \[\[5,3,3,5\]\].
