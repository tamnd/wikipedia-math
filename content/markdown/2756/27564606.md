# Harro Heuser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harro Heuser"}

**Harro Heuser** (December 26, 1927 in [Nastätten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nastätten) – February 21, 2011 in [Bingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bingen_am_Rhein)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). In German-speaking countries he is best known for his popular two-volume introduction into real [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), *Lehrbuch der Analysis*.[^o4][^o3]

:::

Heuser studied mathematics, physics and philosophy from 1948 to 1954 at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) to receive a teaching degree (*Staatsexamen*) and went on to study for his PhD, which he received in 1957. The advisor of his thesis, entitled *Über Operatoren mit endlichen Defekten*, was [Helmut Wielandt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_Wielandt). After receiving his PhD he moved to the [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe), where he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1962. In 1963 he became a professor at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) and in 1964 at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz). Finally in spring 1969 he became a tenured professor at the [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe), where he remained until his retirement in 1996. He was also temporarily working as a visiting professor in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia) and [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).[^o3][^o4][^o5]
