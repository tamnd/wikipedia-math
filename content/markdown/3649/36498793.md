# Bauernfeind prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bauernfeind prism"}

*Image: Bauernfeind-Prism angles 60deg.svg.* Reflections in a Bauernfeind prism for 60 deg beam deviation. Green side requires reflective coating.

A **Bauernfeind prism** is a type of reflecting [prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prism_%28optics%29) used to deviate a beam of light by 45° to 60°, depending on its construction, while neither flipping nor rotating the image.[^o2] It is named for its inventor, the German expert of [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608) [Karl Maximilian von Bauernfeind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Maximilian_von_Bauernfeind).[^o3]

:::

The beam is reflected twice in the prism, with one reflection happening at less than the [critical angle](wikipedia://en/30426). Therefore, the prism requires a [reflective coating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror) for this surface to be usable in practice.

A Bauernfeind prism with 45° beam deviation is also known as a **half-penta prism** or **semipentaprism**.[^o4][^o5] A Bauernfeind prism is used together with a [Schmidt roof prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_roof_prism) to form a [Schmidt–Pechan prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt–Pechan_prism).

::: {#o7 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The Bauernfeind prism is commonly used in [microscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microscope) tubes and in geodesy to deviate the path to the [eyepiece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyepiece) in order make the device more ergonomic.[^o8]

More recent applications are glasses that deflect the viewing angle either downwards (for relaxed reading or watching TV[^o9]), or upwards (as in [belay glasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belay_glasses) for watching a climber while belaying).
