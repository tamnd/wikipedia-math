# Nikolay Krasovsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolay Krasovsky"}

*Image: Academician N.N. Krasovskii.jpg.* Academician Nikolay Nikolayevich Krasovsky

**Nikolay Nikolayevich Krasovsky** (Никола́й Никола́евич Красо́вский; 7 September 1924 – 4 April 2012, also transliterated *Nikolai Krasovskii*[^o2]) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) mathematician who worked in the mathematical [theory of control](wikipedia://en/7039), the theory of dynamical systems, and the theory of [differential games](wikipedia://en/11924).[^o3] He was the author of [Krasovskii-LaSalle principle](wikipedia://en/4021739) and the chief of the [Ural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_%28region%29) scientific school in mathematical theory of control and the theory of differential games.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nikolay Krasovsky was born in [Yekaterinburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterinburg) (renamed later to Sverdlovsk that year) in the family of a doctor. In 1949, he graduated summa cum laude from the department of metallurgical science at the [Ural State Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_Technical_University). In 1954, he presented his first thesis and received his *[Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)* degree in mathematics. In 1957, he defended his second thesis for the degree of *[Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences)* and became a professor of mathematics.

From 1949 to 1959, he worked at the Ural State Technical University. Since 1958, he worked at the [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University).

- 1949–1951 – assistant at the [Ural State Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_Technical_University)
- 1954–1955 – associate professor (docent) at the Ural State Technical University
- 1958–1959 – professor at the Ural State Technical University
- 1959–1960 – chief of the chair of theoretical mechanics at the [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University)
- 1961–1963 – chief of the chair of computing mathematics at the Ural State University

In 1963 [Stanford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University_Press) published a translation of his book *Stability of Motion: applications of Lyapunov's second method to differential systems and equations with delay* that had been prepared by [Joel Lee Brenner](wikipedia://en/38091021).

- 1965–1970 – chief of the chair of applied mathematics at the Ural State University
- 1970–1977 – director the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics of the Ural Branch of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences)
- 1971–1986 – professor at the chair of applied mathematics at the Ural State University
- since 1986 until his death – professor of the chair of theoretical mechanics at the Ural State University
- Advisor of the Ural branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences

He died in Yekaterinburg aged 87, and was buried at the Shirokorechenskoye Cemetery.
