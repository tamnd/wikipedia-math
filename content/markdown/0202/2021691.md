# Anti-psychologism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anti-psychologism"}

In [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), **anti-psychologism** (sometimes associated with **logical objectivism**[^o2] and, in stronger forms, with **logical realism**[^o3][^o4]) is the [theory](wikipedia://en/30746) that [logical truth](wikipedia://en/3986852) does not depend upon the contents of human ideas, but exists independent of human ideas.

:::

::: {#o6 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Psychologism dispute"}

## Overview {#Overview}

The anti-psychologistic treatment of logic originated in the works of [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant) and [Bernard Bolzano](wikipedia://en/302185).[^o7]

The concept of logical objectivism or anti-psychologism was further developed by [Johannes Rehmke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Rehmke) (founder of [Greifswald objectivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greifswald_objectivism))[^o8] and [Gottlob Frege](wikipedia://en/48416) (founder of [logicism](wikipedia://en/350672) the most famous anti-psychologist in the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439)), and has been the center of an important debate in early [phenomenology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenomenology_%28philosophy%29) and [analytical philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_philosophy). Frege's work was likely influenced by Bolzano.[^o9]

Elements of anti-psychologism in the [historiography of philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historiography_of_philosophy) can be found in the work of the members of the 1830s [speculative theist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speculative_theist) movement of [Christian Hermann Weisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Hermann_Weisse),[^o10] the late work of [Hermann Lotze](wikipedia://en/191379),[^o11] and the work of [neo-Kantians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Kantians) [Alois Riehl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alois_Riehl) and [Paul Natorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Natorp).[^o12]

The **psychologism dispute** (Psychologismusstreit)[^o13] in 19th-century German-speaking philosophy is closely related to the contemporary [internalism and externalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internalism_and_externalism) debate in [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology); [psychologism](wikipedia://en/2598499) is often construed as a kind of internalism (the thesis that no fact about the world can provide reasons for action independently of desires and beliefs) and anti-psychologism as a kind of externalism (the thesis that reasons are to be identified with objective features of the world).[^o14]

Psychologism was defended by [Theodor Lipps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Lipps), [Gerardus Heymans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerardus_Heymans), [Wilhelm Wundt](wikipedia://en/34128), [Wilhelm Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Jerusalem), [Christoph von Sigwart](wikipedia://en/885684), [Theodor Elsenhans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Elsenhans), and [Benno Erdmann](wikipedia://en/35195717).[^o15]

[Edmund Husserl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Husserl) was another important proponent of anti-psychologism, and this trait passed on to other phenomenologists, such as [Martin Heidegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Heidegger), whose doctoral thesis was meant to be a refutation of psychologism. They shared the argument that, because the proposition "no-p is a not-p" is not [logically equivalent](wikipedia://en/169324) to "It is thought that 'no-p is a not-p'", psychologism does not logically stand.

[Charles Sanders Peirce](wikipedia://en/6117)—whose fields included logic, philosophy, and experimental psychology[^o16]—could also be considered a critic of psychologism in logic.[^o17]

:::

## The return of psychologism {#The_return_of_psychologism}

Psychologism is not widely held amongst logicians today, but something like it has some high-profile defenders especially among those who do research at the intersection of logic and [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science), for example [Dov Gabbay](wikipedia://en/3662032) and [John Woods](wikipedia://en/31563512), who concluded that "whereas mathematical logic must eschew psychologism, the new logic cannot do without it".[^o19]
