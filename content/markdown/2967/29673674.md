# Kervaire manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kervaire manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), a **Kervaire manifold** $K^{4n+2}$ is a [piecewise-linear manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise-linear_manifold) of [dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_of_a_manifold) $4n+2$ constructed by (txt) by plumbing together the [tangent bundles](wikipedia://en/211794) of two [$(2n+1)$-spheres](wikipedia://en/39782), and then gluing a [ball](wikipedia://en/160556) to the result. In 10 dimensions this gives a piecewise-linear manifold with no [smooth structure](wikipedia://en/1542238).

:::
