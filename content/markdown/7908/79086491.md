# Giorgio Picci

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giorgio Picci"}

**Giorgio Picci** (26 May 1942 – 11 March 2026) was an Italian electrical engineer and control theorist. He made widely recognized contributions to the field of [Systems and Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_Theory), and in particular, to the areas of Stochastic Realization and [System Identification](wikipedia://en/552466). He had also been a pioneer in [Computer Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision).[^o3][^o4] He published a monograph (in English)[^o5] and nearly 200 papers in these areas.

:::

He was a Professor [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) with the Department of Information Engineering, [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padova), Italy.[^o6] He was also the Italian director of the [Confucius Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucius_Institute) at the University of Padua,[^o7] where he actively promoted cultural and scientific exchanges between China and Italy,[^o8] in collaborations with [Guangzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou_University) in China.

## Biography {#Biography}

Picci obtained the [Doctor in Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Engineering) (Dr. Eng.) degree (*[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)*) from the University of Padua in 1967. From 1980 to 2012, he was the [Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_%28academic%29) Professor in [System Identification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_Identification) at the Department of Information Engineering, University of Padua. During his career, he had a number of visiting positions with different universities on three [continents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continents), namely [North America](wikipedia://en/21139) (the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States)), [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe) (the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), and [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden)), and [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia) ([Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan) and [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)).[^o10]

Picci was a [Fellow of the IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_IEEE),[^o11] a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Engineering_Sciences),[^o12] a Fellow of the [International Federation of Automatic Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_Automatic_Control),[^o13] and a member of the [Galilean Academy of Sciences, Letters and Arts in Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Galileiana).[^o14]

Picci died on the morning of 11 March 2026, at the age of 83.
