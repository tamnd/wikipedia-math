# Wendelin Werner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wendelin Werner"}

**Wendelin Werner** (born 23 September 1968) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on random processes such as self-avoiding [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451), [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), [Schramm–Loewner evolution](wikipedia://en/6706108), and related theories in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). In 2006, at the 25th [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain) he received the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) "for his contributions to the development of [stochastic Loewner evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_Loewner_evolution), the geometry of two-dimensional Brownian motion, and conformal field theory". He is currently [Rouse Ball professor of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/649081) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Werner was born on 23 September 1968 in [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne), [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany). His parents moved to France when he was nine months old and he became a French citizen in 1977.[^o4] After a *[classe préparatoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preparatory_classes)* at [Lycée Hoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Hoche) in [Versailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles), he studied at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 1987 to 1991. His 1993 doctorate was written at the [Université Pierre-et-Marie-Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre-et-Marie-Curie) and supervised by [Jean-François Le Gall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-François_Le_Gall).

In 1982, Werner acted in the French film *[La Passante du Sans-Souci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Passante_du_Sans-Souci)*.[^o4]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Werner was a researcher at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) (National Center of Scientific Research, Centre national de la recherche scientifique) from 1991 to 1997, during which he also held a two-year Leibniz Fellowship, at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). He was Professor at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) from 1997 to 2013 and also taught at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 2005 to 2013.[^o6][^o7] He was then Professor at the [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 2013 to 2023.

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Werner has received several awards besides the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), including the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1998, the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602) in 1999, the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) in 2001, the [Grand Prix Jacques Herbrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_Jacques_Herbrand) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences#Medals,_awards_and_prizes) in 2003, the [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize) in 2005, the 2006 [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) [George Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28SIAM%29) with his collaborators [Gregory Lawler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Lawler) and [Oded Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580), and the [Heinz Gumin Prize](wikipedia://en/78691910) in 2016. In 2026 he was awarded the [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal) of the Royal Society.[^o9]

He became a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2008. He is also a member of other academies of sciences, including the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) and the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences) and is an honorary fellow of [Gonville and Caius College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonville_and_Caius_College).[^o6][^o7][^o10] He was elected a [Foreign Member of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Member_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2020.[^o11]
