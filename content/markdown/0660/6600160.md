# Outline of trigonometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of trigonometry"}

The following [outline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_%28list%29) is provided as an overview of and topical guide to trigonometry:

:::

**[Trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261)** – branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) that studies the relationships between the sides and the angles in [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654). Trigonometry defines the [trigonometric functions](wikipedia://en/30367), which describe those relationships and have applicability to cyclical phenomena, such as [waves](wikipedia://en/33516).

## Basics {#Basics}

*Image: Angle Symbol.svg.* ∠, the angle symbol in [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode) is [U+2220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_XML_and_HTML_character_entity_references)

- [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) – mathematics concerned with questions of shape, size, the relative position of figures, and the properties of space. Geometry is used extensively in trigonometry.
- [Angle](wikipedia://en/1196) – the angle is the figure formed by two rays, called the sides of the angle, sharing a common endpoint, called the vertex of the angle. Angles formed by two rays lie in a plane, but this plane does not have to be a Euclidean plane.
- [Ratio](wikipedia://en/87837) – a ratio indicates how many times one number contains another
