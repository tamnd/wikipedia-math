# Topological abelian group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Topological abelian group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **topological abelian group**, or **TAG**, is a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) that is also an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974). That is, a TAG is both a [group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_%28algebra%29) and a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450), the group operations are [continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_%28topology%29), and the group's binary operation is [commutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative).

:::

The theory of topological groups applies also to TAGs, but more can be done with TAGs. [Locally compact](wikipedia://en/48632) TAGs, in particular, are used heavily in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147).
