# Jean-Christophe Yoccoz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Christophe Yoccoz"}

**Jean-Christophe Yoccoz** (29 May 1957 – 3 September 2016) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was awarded a [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1994, for his work on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Yoccoz attended the [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand),[^o6] during which time he was a silver medalist at the 1973 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) and a gold medalist in 1974.[^o8] He entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1975, and completed an [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics in 1977.[^o9]

Yaccoz next completed a civil alternative to French military service, which he served at [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil).[^o10] He then completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) under [Michael Herman](wikipedia://en/28451358) in 1985 at [Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz](wikipedia://en/62392394), which is a research unit jointly operated by the [French National Center for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Center_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS) and [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).[^o9][^o11]

He took up a position at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 1987, and became a professor at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France) in 1997, where he remained until his death.[^o4] He was a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o13]

Yoccoz won the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1988. He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 1990 at Kyoto,[^o15] and was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1994 in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich).[^o14][^o15] He joined the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) and [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1994, became a chevalier in the [French Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Legion_of_Honor) in 1995, and was awarded the Grand Cross of the Brazilian [National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit) in 1998.[^o8]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

Yoccoz's worked on the theory of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). His contributions include advances to [KAM theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAM_theory), and the introduction of the method of Yoccoz puzzles, a combinatorial technique which proved useful to the study of [Julia sets](wikipedia://en/56434).[^o8]
