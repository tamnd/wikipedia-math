# Count On

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Count On"}

**Count On** is a major [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) project in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) which was announced by [education secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_State_for_Education) [David Blunkett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Blunkett) at the end of 2000. It was the follow-on to [Maths Year 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maths_Year_2000) which was the UK's contribution to [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF)'s [World Mathematical Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Mathematical_Year).[^o2]

:::

Count On had two main strands:

- The website www.counton.org[^o3] which won the 2002 [BETT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BETT) prize for best free online learning resource.[^o4]
- "MathFests", which were maths funfairs held around the country, aimed particularly at those who would not normally come into contact with mathematical ideas.[^o5]
