# Joan Hu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joan Hu"}

**Xiaoqiong Joan Hu** is a Chinese and Canadian statistician. Her research has involved [pseudolikelihood](wikipedia://en/4925416), estimating functions, [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676), and varied applications of statistics. She is a professor of statistics at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hu earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1983 and a master's degree in probability and statistics in 1987, both from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University). She completed her Ph.D. in 1995 at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) with Jerry Lawless as her [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor). Her dissertation was *Estimation from Truncated Data with Supplementary Information, with Applications to Field Reliability*.

After postdoctoral research at [Health Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Canada) and the [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health), she taught at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis) from 1998 to 2003, also holding an adjunct position at [St. Jude Children's Research Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Jude_Children's_Research_Hospital) in Memphis. She moved to Simon Fraser University in 2003, and in 2017 added an affiliated position with the [University of British Columbia (Okanagan Campus)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia_%28Okanagan_Campus%29).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Hu won the Pierre Robillard Award for 1996 for the best doctoral dissertation in statistics in Canada. In 1998, with Jerry Lawless and Kazuyuki Suzuki, she won the [Frank Wilcoxon Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Wilcoxon_Prize).

Hu has been an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) since 2007. In 2012, the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) named Hu as a fellow "for outstanding contributions to statistical methods for incomplete data analysis, statistical monitoring and the analysis of stochastic process data; for excellence in statistical applications in biomedical research and reliability; and for outstanding service to the profession".
