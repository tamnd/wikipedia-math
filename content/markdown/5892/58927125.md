# Ivan Smith (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Smith"}

**Ivan Smith** (born 1973) is a British mathematician who deals with [symplectic manifolds](wikipedia://en/28356) and their interaction with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), and dynamics. He is a professor at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Smith was born in 1973 as the second child of [Neil Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Smith_%28linguist%29), a professor of [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics) at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London), and Saras Smith. He studied at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), where he received his doctorate in 1999 under the supervision of [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305) with thesis *Symplectic Geometry of Lefschetz Fibrations*. Smith is now a professor in Cambridge at Gonville & Caius College.

Among other things, Smith derived nodal invariants from [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).

He received in 2007 the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) for his work in symplectic topology (highlighting the breadth of applied techniques from algebraic geometry and topology)[^o5] and in 2013 the [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110). In 2018 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.

Smith was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2023.[^o6]
