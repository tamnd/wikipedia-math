# Kim-Chuan Toh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kim-Chuan Toh"}

**Kim-Chuan Toh** is a Singaporean mathematician, and Leo Tan Professor in Science at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) (NUS). He is known for his contributions to the theory, practice, and application of [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411), especially [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539) and [conic programming](wikipedia://en/9786372).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Toh received BSc (Hon.) in 1990 and MSc in 1992, from NUS, and PhD in 1996 from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Toh received the 2017 INFORMS Optimization Society Farkas Prize,[^o4] and 2019 President's Science Award (Singapore).[^o5] He is a fellow of the Society of Industrial and Applied Mathematics (Class of 2018).[^o6] In 2020, Toh became a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*.[^o7]
