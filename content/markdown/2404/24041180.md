# Dyck graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dyck graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Dyck graph** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 32 vertices and 48 edges, named after [Walther von Dyck](wikipedia://en/752612).[^o3]

:::

It is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) with 120 distinct Hamiltonian cycles. It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 5, diameter 5 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 6. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) graph. It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o5] The graph is [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o6]

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Dyck graph is a group of order 192.[^o9] It acts transitively on the vertices, on the edges and on the arcs of the graph. Therefore, the Dyck graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896). It has automorphisms that take any vertex to any other vertex and any edge to any other edge. According to the *Foster census*, the Dyck graph, referenced as F32A, is the only cubic symmetric graph on 32 vertices.[^o10]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Dyck graph is equal to $(x-3) (x-1)^9 (x+1)^9 (x+3) (x^2-5)^6$.

:::

## Toroidal graph {#Toroidal_graph}

The Dyck graph is a [toroidal graph](wikipedia://en/1633057), contained in the skeleton of a hexagonal [regular map](wikipedia://en/21533914#Toroidal_polyhedra), {6,3}<sub>4,0</sub>, with 32 vertices, 48 edges, and 16 hexagonal cycles. It is the dual of its symmetric toroidal embedding is the [Shrikhande graph](wikipedia://en/13672415).

It can be visualized as net, a 4 by 4 array of hexagons, where left-right and top-bottom wrap into a flat torus.

## Dyck map {#Dyck_map}

The Dyck graph is the [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_%28topology%29) of a [symmetric tessellation](wikipedia://en/21533914) of a surface of [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_%28topology%29) three by twelve octagons, known as the **Dyck map** or **Dyck tiling**. The [dual graph](wikipedia://en/2536864) for this tiling is the [complete tripartite graph](wikipedia://en/525320) *K*<sub>4,4,4</sub>.[^o14][^o15]
