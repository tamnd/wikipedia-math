# C-normal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C-normal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $H$ of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) $G$ is called **c-normal** if there is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) $T$ of $G$ such that $HT = G$ and the [intersection](wikipedia://en/23476429) of $H$ and $T$ lies inside the [normal core](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_core) of $H$.

:::

For a **weakly c-normal subgroup**, we only require $T$ to be [subnormal](wikipedia://en/2774415).

Here are some facts about c-normal subgroups:

- Every [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) is c-normal
- Every [retract](wikipedia://en/4967125) is c-normal
- Every c-normal subgroup is weakly c-normal
