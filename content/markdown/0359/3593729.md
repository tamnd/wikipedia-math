# Abnormal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abnormal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), an **abnormal subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) *H* of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* such that for all *x* in *G*, *x* lies in the subgroup generated by *H* and *H*<sup>hair*x*</sup>, where *H*<sup>hair*x*</sup> denotes the [conjugate subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_subgroup) *xHx*<sup>−1</sup>.

:::

Here are some facts relating abnormality to other subgroup properties:

- Every abnormal subgroup is a [self-normalizing subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-normalizing_subgroup), as well as a [contranormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593761).
- The only normal subgroup that is also abnormal is the whole group.
- Every abnormal subgroup is a [weakly abnormal subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weakly_abnormal_subgroup), and every weakly abnormal subgroup is a self-normalizing subgroup.
- Every abnormal subgroup is a [pronormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593621), and hence a [weakly pronormal subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weakly_pronormal_subgroup), a [paranormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593691), and a [polynormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/4431306).
