# Pronormal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pronormal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **pronormal subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) that is embedded in a nice way. Pronormality is a simultaneous generalization of both [normal subgroups](wikipedia://en/21918) and [abnormal subgroups](wikipedia://en/3593729) such as [Sylow subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup), (Doerk Hawkes 1992, I.§6).

:::

A subgroup is **pronormal** if each of its [conjugates](wikipedia://en/49176) is conjugate to it already in the subgroup [generated](wikipedia://en/99945) by it and its conjugate. That is, *H* is pronormal in *G* if for every *g* in *G*, there is some *k* in the subgroup generated by *H* and *H*<sup>*g*</sup> such that *H*<sup>*k*</sup> = *H*<sup>*g*</sup>. (Here *H*<sup>*g*</sup> denotes the conjugate subgroup *gHg*<sup>*\-1*</sup>.)

Here are some relations with other subgroup properties:

- Every [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) is pronormal.
- Every [Sylow subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) is pronormal.
- Every pronormal [subnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/2774415) is normal.
- Every [abnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593729) is pronormal.
- Every pronormal subgroup is [weakly pronormal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weakly_pronormal_subgroup), that is, it has the [Frattini property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frattini_property).
- Every pronormal subgroup is [paranormal](wikipedia://en/3593691), and hence [polynormal](wikipedia://en/4431306).
