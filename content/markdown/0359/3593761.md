# Contranormal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Contranormal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **contranormal subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) whose [normal closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_closure) in the group is the whole group.[^o2] Clearly, a contranormal subgroup can be normal only if it is the whole group.

:::

Some facts:

- Every subgroup of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) is a contranormal subgroup of a [subnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/2774415). In general, every subgroup of a group is a contranormal subgroup of a [descendant subgroup](wikipedia://en/19175980).
- Every [abnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593729) is contranormal.
