# Modular subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modular subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **modular subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) that is a [modular element](wikipedia://en/1089311) in the [lattice of subgroups](wikipedia://en/3983172), where the meet operation is defined by the intersection and the join operation is defined by the subgroup [generated](wikipedia://en/99945) by the union of subgroups.

:::

By the **modular property of groups**, every [quasinormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/2774267) (that is, a subgroup that permutes with all subgroups) is modular. In particular, every [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) is modular.
