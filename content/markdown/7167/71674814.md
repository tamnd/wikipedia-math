# Ruth Mickey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth Mickey"}

**Ruth Mary Mickey** (born 1954) is a retired American statistician known for her research on [feature selection](wikipedia://en/1179950) to control the effects of [confounding](wikipedia://en/3105999) on [statistical inference](wikipedia://en/27577), and on the applications of statistics to issues of [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) and [natural resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_resources). She is a professor emerita in the [University of Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vermont) Department of Mathematics & Statistics.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mickey earned a master's degree in public health at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA) in 1978, and completed a Ph.D. in biostatistics at UCLA in 1983.

## Books {#Books}

Mickey is the coauthor of textbooks in statistics including:

- *Applied Statistics: Analysis of Variance and Regression* (with [Olive Jean Dunn](wikipedia://en/49872639) and [Virginia A. Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_A._Clark), Wiley, 3rd ed., 2004)
- *Bayesian Statistics for Beginners: A Step-by-Step Approach* (with Therese M. Donovan, Oxford University Press, 2019)
