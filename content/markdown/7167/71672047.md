# Jan Kohlhaase

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Kohlhaase"}

**Jan Kohlhaase** (born 1976)[^o2] is a German mathematician specialising in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [p-adic Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945#Related_concepts) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

From 1997 to 2002, Kohlhaase studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) and at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o5] In 2005 he obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) under the supervision of [Peter Schneider](wikipedia://en/64772763).[^o6][^o7] He subsequently worked at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Etudes_Scientifiques), the University of Münster as well as the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). Kohlhaase [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the University of Münster in 2011, and was appointed professor for arithmetic geometry at the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen) in 2014.[^o5]
