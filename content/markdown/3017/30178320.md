# Vertex of a representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vertex of a representation"}

In [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **vertex** of a representation of a finite group is a subgroup associated to it, that has a special [representation](wikipedia://en/12695) called a **source**. Vertices and sources were introduced by (txt).

:::
