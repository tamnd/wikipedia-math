# Constantine Dafermos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Constantine Dafermos"}

*Image: Kroner, Dafermos, and LeVeque.jpg.* Dafermos (center) at Oberwolfach in 2004. Photo courtesy [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)

**Constantine Michael Dafermos** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Κωνσταντίνος Δαφέρμος; born May 26, 1941, [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens)) is a [Greek-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek-American) [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician). He received a diploma in civil engineering from the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens) (1964) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mechanics from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) under the direction of [Jerald Ericksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerald_Ericksen) (1967). He has been an assistant professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) (1968–1971) and an associate professor (1971–1975) and professor (since 1975) in the Division of Applied Mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). Since 1984, he has been the Alumni-Alumnae University Professor at Brown.[^o2]

:::

In recent years, his research has focused on nonlinear hyperbolic systems of [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_law_%28physics%29) whose solutions spontaneously develop singularities propagating as [shock waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_waves). In particular, he is studying the interplay between [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952) and analysis in the theory of these systems and he is analyzing the fundamental role of [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) as a stabilizing agent.

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3] In 2016 he was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]
