# Shou-Wu Zhang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shou-Wu Zhang"}

Zhang Shou-Wu

**Shou-Wu Zhang** (; born October 9, 1962) is a Chinese-American mathematician known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He is currently a professor of mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Shou-Wu Zhang was born in [Hexian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexian), [Ma'anshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'anshan), [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China), on October 9, 1962.[^o5][^o6][^o7] Zhang grew up in a poor farming household and could not attend school until eighth grade [due to the Cultural Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Revolution#Academics_and_education).[^o5] He spent most of his childhood raising ducks in the countryside and self-studying mathematics textbooks that he acquired from [sent-down youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sent-down_youth) in trades for frogs.[^o5][^o6] By the time he entered junior high school at the age of fourteen, he had taught himself calculus and had become interested in number theory after reading about [Chen Jingrun](wikipedia://en/352985)'s proof of [Chen's theorem](wikipedia://en/1400840) which made substantial progress on [Goldbach's conjecture](wikipedia://en/44758).[^o5][^o6][^o8]

### Education {#Education}

Zhang was admitted to the [Sun Yat-sen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yat-sen_University) chemistry department in 1980 after scoring poorly on his mathematics entrance examinations, but he later transferred to the mathematics department after feigning [color blindness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_blindness) and received his bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1983.[^o10][^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8][^o11] He then studied under analytic number theorist [Wang Yuan](wikipedia://en/15303814) at the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) where he received his master's degree in 1986.[^o5][^o8][^o7][^o11] In 1986, Zhang was brought to the United States to pursue his doctoral studies at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) by [Dorian M. Goldfeld](wikipedia://en/7536770).[^o5][^o6] He initially studied under Goldfeld and then [Hervé Jacquet](wikipedia://en/25600925),[^o10] before deciding to work with [Lucien Szpiro](wikipedia://en/26992384), a visiting professor at Columbia at the time, and [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226) at Princeton University.[^o10] He received his PhD at Columbia University under the supervision of Szpiro in 1991.[^o10]

### Career {#Career}

Zhang was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and an assistant professor at Princeton University from 1991 to 1996.[^o7][^o11] In 1996, Zhang moved back to Columbia University where he was a tenured professor until 2013.[^o5][^o10][^o7][^o11] He has been a professor at Princeton University since 2011[^o10][^o11] was named the Henry Burchard Fine Professor from 2014 to 2015,[^o14] and was named a Eugene Higgins Professor in 2021.[^o15]

Zhang is on the editorial boards of: *[Acta Mathematica Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Mathematica_Sinica)*, *[Algebra & Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_&_Number_Theory)*, *[Forum of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/36308727)*, *[Journal of Differential Geometry](wikipedia://en/25823636)*, *National Science Review*, *Pure and Applied Mathematics Quarterly*, *Science in China*, and *Research in Number Theory*.[^o10] He has previously served on the editorial boards of: *[Journal of Number Theory](wikipedia://en/8422003)*, *[Journal of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society)*, *Journal of Algebraic Geometry*, and *[International Journal of Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Number_Theory)*.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Zhang's doctoral thesis *Positive line bundles on Arithmetic Surfaces* () proved a [Nakai–Moishezon type theorem](wikipedia://en/1186896#Intersection_theory) in [intersection theory](wikipedia://en/3148602) using a result from [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) already proved in [Tian Gang](wikipedia://en/6716707)'s doctoral thesis.[^o10] In a series of subsequent papers ((nb), (nb)), he further developed his theory of 'positive line bundles' in [Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229) which culminated in a proof of the [Bogomolov conjecture](wikipedia://en/18992933) with [Emmanuel Ullmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Ullmo) and a further generalization of the Bogomolov conjecture ((nb)).[^o10][^o21]

In 2001, Zhang proved a generalization of the [Gross–Zagier theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross–Zagier_theorem) to modular [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties) of [GL(2)](wikipedia://en/113564) type ((nb)).[^o10] In particular, the result led him to a proof of the rank one [Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture) for modular abelian varieties of GL(2) type over [totally real fields](wikipedia://en/312853) through his work relating the [Néron–Tate height](wikipedia://en/9236652) of Heegner points to special values of [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283) in ().[^o10][^o24] In 2013, Zhang and his former doctoral students [Xinyi Yuan](wikipedia://en/52461029) and [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911) established a full generalization of the Gross–Zagier theorem to all [Shimura curves](wikipedia://en/10389861) ((nb) (nb)).

In [arithmetic dynamics](wikipedia://en/17584701), (txt) posed conjectures on the [Zariski density](wikipedia://en/241744) of non-fibered [endomorphisms](wikipedia://en/9569) of [quasi-projective varieties](wikipedia://en/1321785) and (txt) proposed a dynamical analogue of the [Manin–Mumford conjecture](wikipedia://en/384577#Manin–Mumford_conjecture).[^o29][^o10]

In 2018, (txt) proved the [averaged Colmez conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colmez_conjecture) which was shown to imply the [André–Oort conjecture](wikipedia://en/32262980) for [Siegel modular varieties](wikipedia://en/7721927) by [Jacob Tsimerman](wikipedia://en/48497888).[^o31]

## Awards {#Awards}

Zhang has received a [Sloan Foundation Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) (1997) and a [Morningside Gold Medal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morningside_Gold_Medal_of_Mathematics) (1998). He is also a [Clay Foundation Prize Fellow](wikipedia://en/7655) (2003), [Guggenheim Foundation Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) (2009), Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (2011), and Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (2016).[^o33][^o34][^o10], Andrew Wiles Medal of [International Congress of Basic Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Basic_Sciences). He was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1998.[^o35][^o10][^o11][^o36]
