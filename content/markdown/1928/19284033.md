# CRM-Fields-PIMS prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="CRM-Fields-PIMS prize"}

The **CRM-Fields-PIMS Prize** is the premier [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) research prize in the [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436). It is awarded in recognition of exceptional research achievement in the mathematical sciences and is given annually by three Canadian mathematics institutes: the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) (CRM), the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), and the [Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/34978739) (PIMS).

:::

The prize was established in 1994 by the CRM and the Fields Institute as the **CRM-Fields Prize**. The prize took its current name when PIMS became a partner in 2005.

The prize carries a monetary award of \$10,000, funded jointly by the three institutes. The inaugural prize winner was [H.S.M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328).
