# McLaughlin sporadic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="McLaughlin sporadic group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **McLaughlin group** McL is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

898,128,000 = 2<sup>7</sup> ⋅ 3<sup>6</sup> ⋅ 5<sup>3</sup> ⋅ 7 ⋅ 11

≈ 9.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

McL is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was discovered by (txt) as an index 2 subgroup of a rank 3 permutation group acting on the [McLaughlin graph](wikipedia://en/46573575) with 275 = 1 + 112 + 162 vertices. It fixes a [2-2-3 triangle](wikipedia://en/345511#Other_sporadic_groups) in the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) and thus is a subgroup of the [Conway groups](wikipedia://en/345511) [Co<sub>0</sub>](wikipedia://en/344919#Symmetries), [Co<sub>2</sub>](wikipedia://en/36237532) and [Co<sub>3</sub>](wikipedia://en/36237581). Its [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 3, and its [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2. The group 3.McL:2 is a maximal subgroup of the [Lyons group](wikipedia://en/1110773).

McL has one conjugacy class of involution (element of order 2), whose centralizer is a maximal subgroup of type 2.A<sub>8</sub>. This has a center of order 2; the quotient modulo the center is isomorphic to the alternating group A<sub>8</sub>.

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

In the [Conway group](wikipedia://en/345511) Co<sub>3</sub>, McL has the normalizer McL:2, which is maximal in Co<sub>3</sub>.

McL has 2 classes of maximal subgroups isomorphic to the [Mathieu group](wikipedia://en/380207) M<sub>22</sub>. An outer automorphism interchanges the two classes of M<sub>22</sub> groups. This outer automorphism is realized on McL embedded as a subgroup of Co<sub>3</sub>.

A convenient representation of M<sub>22</sub> is in permutation matrices on the last 22 coordinates; it fixes a 2-2-3 triangle with vertices the origin and the [type 2](wikipedia://en/345511) points **x** = (−3, 1<sup>23</sup>) and **y** = (−4,-4,0<sup>22</sup>)'. The triangle's edge **x**-**y** = (1, 5, 1<sup>22</sup>) is [type 3](wikipedia://en/345511); it is fixed by a Co<sub>3</sub>. This M<sub>22</sub> is the [monomial](wikipedia://en/345511#Monomial_subgroup_N_of_Co0), *and a maximal*, subgroup of a representation of McL.

(Wilson 2009) (p. 207) shows that the subgroup McL is well-defined. In the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919), suppose a type 3 point **v** is fixed by an instance of $\mathrm{Co}_3$. Count the type 2 points **w** such that the inner product **v**·**w** = 3 (and thus **v**-**w** is type 2). He shows their number is 552 = 2<sup>3</sup>⋅3⋅23 and that this Co<sub>3</sub> is transitive on these **w**.

\|McL| = |Co3|/552 = 898,128,000.

McL is the only sporadic group to admit irreducible representations of [quaternionic type](wikipedia://en/695046). It has 2 such representations, one of dimension 3520 and one of dimension 4752.

:::

## Generalized Monstrous Moonshine {#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that monstrous moonshine is not limited to the monster. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. For the [Conway groups](wikipedia://en/345511#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine), the relevant McKay–Thompson series is $T_{2A}(\tau)$ and $T_{4A}(\tau)$.
