# Stephen G. West

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen G. West"}

**Stephen Gano West** (born 1946)[^o3] is an American [quantitative psychologist](wikipedia://en/3067624) and professor of psychology at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University). He was the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the *[Journal of Personality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Personality)* from 1986 to 1991, of *[Psychological Methods](wikipedia://en/37384857)* from 2001 to 2007,[^o4] and of *[Multivariate Behavioral Research](wikipedia://en/26785977)* in 2015.[^o5] He was also the president of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) from 2007 to 2008. He was educated at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), and received the [Society for Personality and Social Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Personality_and_Social_Psychology)'s Murray Award in 2000.[^o4]
