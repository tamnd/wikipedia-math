# Ramaswamy S. Vaidyanathaswamy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramaswamy S. Vaidyanathaswamy"}

**Ramaswamy S. Vaidyanathaswamy** (1894–1960) was an Indian mathematician who wrote the first textbook of [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology) in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India).

:::

He is notable for having been the first to discover the generalisation of the [crystallographic restriction theorem](wikipedia://en/2218040) to arbitrary dimensions.

## Life {#Life}

He was born in India on 24 October 1894.[^o3]

Vaidyanathaswamy studied Mathematics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in Scotland, under Prof [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Taylor_Whittaker) graduating around 1914. He then did postgraduate studies at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) under Prof [H. F. Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._F._Baker). After his return to India, he was a professor at the [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras), and after his retirement was associated with the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) in [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta).

He contributed extensively to [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology), and wrote a well-known textbook on the subject (and the first such textbook published in India), "Set Topology", which was first published in 1947. A second edition, published in 1960, was reprinted by [Dover Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Publications) in 1999.<sup><https://web.archive.org/web/20070222102810/http://store.doverpublications.com/0486404560.html></sup>

He was elected a fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1924.[^o4] His proposers were [Herbert Westren Turnbull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Westren_Turnbull), [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Taylor_Whittaker), [Ralph Allan Sampson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Allan_Sampson) and [James Hartley Ashworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hartley_Ashworth).[^o3]

He was president of the [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) from 1940 to 1942.[^o5][^o6]
