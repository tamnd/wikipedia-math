# Computational Statistics & Data Analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Computational Statistics & Data Analysis"}

***Computational Statistics & Data Analysis*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering research on and applications of [computational statistics](wikipedia://en/15832717) and data analysis. The journal was established in 1983 and is the official journal of the [International Association for Statistical Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_for_Statistical_Computing),[^o3] a section of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).

:::
