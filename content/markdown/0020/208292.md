# 19 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="19"}

**19** (**nineteen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [18](wikipedia://en/208294) and preceding [20](wikipedia://en/208259). It is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

*Image: Centered triangular number 19.svg.* 19 is a [centered triangular number](wikipedia://en/857110).

19 is the eighth [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666). 19 forms a [twin prime](wikipedia://en/41997) pair with [17](wikipedia://en/208288), a [cousin prime](wikipedia://en/343085) pair with [23](wikipedia://en/362203), and a [sexy prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexy_prime) pair with [13](wikipedia://en/37231). 19 is a strictly non-[palindromic number](wikipedia://en/105956) in any [base](wikipedia://en/21170), 19 is the maximum number of fourth powers needed to sum up to any natural number (see, [Waring's problem](wikipedia://en/33569)). There are 19 [compositions](wikipedia://en/550741) of [8](wikipedia://en/208174) into distinct parts.

There are 19 [nodes](wikipedia://en/638899) in [regular hexagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hexagon) with all [diagonals](wikipedia://en/349251) drawn is nineteen. The only nontrivial [normal magic hexagon](wikipedia://en/1629074) is composed of nineteen cells, where every diagonal of consecutive hexagons has sums equal to [38](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/38_%28number%29), or twice 19.[^o10] A [hexaflexagon](wikipedia://en/98316#Hexahexaflexagon) is a strip of nineteen alternating triangular faces that can *flex* into a regular hexagon, such that any two of six colorings on triangles can be oriented to align on opposite sides of the folded figure.[^o11]

$\tfrac {1} {19}$ can be used to generate the first full, non-normal [prime reciprocal magic square](wikipedia://en/213581) in decimal whose rows, columns *and* diagonals — in a 18 x 18 array — all generate a magic constant of [81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/81_%28number%29) = 9<sup>2</sup>.[^o12]

### Abstract Algebra {#Abstract_Algebra}

19 is a [supersingular prime](wikipedia://en/22965333), a member in the sequence of fifteen such primes that divide the [order](wikipedia://en/493527) of the [Friendly Giant](wikipedia://en/47422) $\mathrm {F_{1}}$. Additionally, 19 also divides the orders of the [Janko groups](wikipedia://en/380191) $\mathrm {J_{1}}$ and $\mathrm {J_{3}}$.[^o15]

19 is a [Heegner number](wikipedia://en/522147).

## Science {#Science}

*Image: JWST spacecraft model 3.png.* The [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope) features a design of 19 hexagons.

- The [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope)'s optical elements are in an array of 19 hexagons, wherein 18 segmented primary mirrors focus light into a centered secondary mirror situated above the collecting mirrors, that in-turn reflects back onto the telescope's central [imagers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_sensor). It is a shape akin to the order-3 magic hexagon.
- The [Metonic cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metonic_cycle) is almost exactly 19 years long.

## Music {#Music}

- Nineteen has been used as an alternative to twelve for a division of the octave into equal parts. This idea goes back to [Salinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_de_Salinas) in the sixteenth century, and is interesting in part because it gives a system of [meantone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meantone) tuning, being close to 1/3 comma meantone. *See [19 equal temperament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19_equal_temperament).*
- Some organs use the 19th harmonic to approximate a minor third.

## Religion {#Religion}

### Islam {#Islam}

- The number of angels [guarding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabaniyah) [Hell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hell) ("Hellfire") ("Saqar") according to the [Qur'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qur'an): "Over it are nineteen" ([74](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Muddaththir):30),[^o21] after which the Qur'an describes this number as being "a trial for those who disbelieve" (74:31), a sign for people of the scripture to be "convinced" (74:31) and that believers "will increase in faith" (74:31) due to it.[^o22][^o23]
- The Number of Verse and Sura together in the Qur'an which announces Jesus son of Maryam's (Mary's) birth (Qur'an 19:19).[^o24][^o25]

### Baháʼí Faith {#Baháʼí_Faith}

In the [Bábí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bábism) and [Baháʼí Faiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baháʼí_Faith), a group of 19 is called a *Váhid*, a Unity (واحد). The numerical value of this word in the [Abjad numeral system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abjad_numerals) is 19.

- The [Baháʼí calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baháʼí_calendar) is structured such that a year contains 19 months of 19 days each (along with the [intercalary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercalation_%28timekeeping%29) period of [Ayyám-i-Há](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayyám-i-Há)), as well as a 19-year cycle and a 361-year (19x19) supercycle.
- The [Báb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Báb) and his [disciples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letters_of_the_Living) formed a group of 19.
- There were 19 [Apostles of Bahá'u'lláh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostles_of_Bahá'u'lláh).

### Celtic paganism {#Celtic_paganism}

19 is a sacred number of the goddess [Brigid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigid) because it is said to represent the [19-year cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metonic_cycle) of the Great Celtic Year and the amount of time it takes the Moon to coincide with the winter solstice.[^o28]

## Literature {#Literature}

[Stephen King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_King)'s eight-book epic [*The Dark Tower*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_%28series%29) saga employs the number 19 in books *[The Dark Tower: The Gunslinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower:_The_Gunslinger)*, *[The Dark Tower V: Wolves of the Calla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_V:_Wolves_of_the_Calla)*, *[The Dark Tower VI: Song of Susannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_VI:_Song_of_Susannah)*, *[The Dark Tower VII: The Dark Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_VII:_The_Dark_Tower)*, and *[The Wind Through the Keyhole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wind_Through_the_Keyhole)* as a mysterious and important number. They refer to the "ka-tet of 19", "Directive Nineteen," many names add up to 19, 19 seems to permeate every aspect of Roland and his traveler's lives. In addition, the number ends up being a powerful key.

## Games {#Games}

Though the maximum score for a [cribbage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cribbage) hand is 29, there is no combination of cards that adds up to 19 points. Many cribbage players, therefore, jokingly refer to a zero-point hand as "a 19 hand".

## In sports {#In_sports}

In [golf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golf), the '19th hole' is the clubhouse bar and in match play, if there is a tie after 18 holes, an extra hole(s) is played. In [miniature golf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miniature_golf), it is an extra hole on which the winner earns an instant prize.

## Age 19 {#Age_19}

- In four countries ([Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algeria), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea), United States ([Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama) and [Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska)), and [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada)), 19 is the [age of majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority).
- In two countries (South Korea and Canada), 19 is the minimum [legal drinking age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_drinking_age).
- In three countries (Algeria, South Korea, and Canada), 19 is the minimum [legal smoking age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_smoking_age).
- The minimum age for the [NBA draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_draft) is 19 years old.
