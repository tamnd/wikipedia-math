# 10

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="10"}

\10.

**10** (**ten**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [9](wikipedia://en/173457) and preceding [11](wikipedia://en/208156). Ten is the [base](wikipedia://en/701207) of [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) numeral systems, the most common systems for denoting [numbers](wikipedia://en/21690) in both spoken and written language.

:::

The [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) name for the number "ten" originates from the [Proto-Germanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Germanic_language) root \*tehun, which in turn comes from the [Proto-Indo-European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Indo-European_language) root \*déḱm̥. This root is the source of similar words for "ten" in many other [Germanic languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germanic_languages). The widespread use of decimal systems is believed to be because humans have ten [fingers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger) and ten [toes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toe), which people may have used to count by.

## Linguistics {#Linguistics}

- A collection of ten items (most often ten years) is called a [decade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decade).
- The ordinal form is *tenth*. The adjectives *decimal* and *denary* refer to systems or quantities based on ten. \* Increasing a quantity by one [order of magnitude](wikipedia://en/22657) is most widely understood to mean multiplying the quantity by ten.
- To reduce something by one tenth is to *decimate*. (In ancient Rome, the killing of one in ten soldiers in a [cohort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohort_%28military_unit%29) was the punishment for [cowardice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowardice) or [mutiny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutiny); or, one-tenth of the able-bodied men in a village as a form of [retribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retributive_justice), thus causing a labor shortage and threat of starvation in agrarian societies.)

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

*Image: Tetractys.svg.* The [tetractys](wikipedia://en/1570530)

As the base of the [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) numeral system, powers of ten are used to express [orders of magnitude](wikipedia://en/22657) and form the basis of common [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715).

- 10 is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) and a [happy number](wikipedia://en/542994).[^o5]
- 10 is the smallest [noncototient](wikipedia://en/758718) number.[^o6]
- Ten is the fourth [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915) and the third [tetrahedral number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedral_number):

$$10=1+2+3+4=\binom{5}{2}=\binom{5}{3}.$$

This representation underlies the [tetractys](wikipedia://en/1570530), a triangular figure of ten points important in [Pythagoreanism](wikipedia://en/291170).

- A ten sided polygon is called a [decagon](wikipedia://en/327393).
