# 6

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="6"}

**6** (**six**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [5](wikipedia://en/6412297) and preceding [7](wikipedia://en/208159). It is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) and the smallest [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670).[^o5] It is also the first whole number that contains the letter [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X) in its name.[^o3]

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

A six-sided [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) is a [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733),[^o5] one of the three [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306) capable of [tiling the plane](wikipedia://en/321671). A hexagon also has 6 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) as well as 6 [internal and external angles](wikipedia://en/524003).

6 is the second smallest [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289).[^o5] It is also the first number that is the sum of its proper divisors, making it the smallest [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670).[^o6] It is also the only perfect number that doesn't have a [digital root](wikipedia://en/8286632) of 1.[^o7] 6 is the first [unitary perfect number](wikipedia://en/553027), since it is the sum of its positive proper [unitary divisors](wikipedia://en/5488222), without including itself. Only five such numbers are known to exist. 6 is the largest of the four [all-Harshad numbers](wikipedia://en/641307).[^o9]

6 is the 2nd [superior highly composite number](wikipedia://en/1759451),[^o10] the 2nd [colossally abundant number](wikipedia://en/1759217),[^o11] the 3rd [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915),[^o12] the 4th [highly composite number](wikipedia://en/208732),[^o13] a [pronic number](wikipedia://en/652733),[^o14] a [congruent number](wikipedia://en/2334769), a [harmonic divisor number](wikipedia://en/462698),[^o16] and a [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542). 6 is also the first [Granville number](wikipedia://en/31314683), or $\mathcal{S}$-perfect number. A [Golomb ruler](wikipedia://en/48211) of length 6 is a "perfect ruler".[^o18]

The [six exponentials theorem](wikipedia://en/15114520) guarantees that under certain conditions one of a set of six exponentials is [transcendental](wikipedia://en/30325).[^o19] The smallest non-[abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) is the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) $\mathrm {S_{3}}$ which has [3!](wikipedia://en/10606) = 6 elements.[^o5] 6 the answer to the two-dimensional [kissing number problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kissing_number_problem).[^o20]

*Image: 120px-Hexahedron-slowturn.gif.* A regular [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), with six [faces](wikipedia://en/149926)

A [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) has 6 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926). A [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) has 6 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034). In [four dimensions](wikipedia://en/1364622), there are a total of six [convex regular polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_regular_4-polytope).

In the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445), twenty of twenty-six [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806) in the [happy family](wikipedia://en/324806#Organization) are part of three families of groups which divide the order of the [friendly giant](wikipedia://en/47422), the largest sporadic group: five *first generation* [Mathieu groups](wikipedia://en/380207), seven *second generation* [subquotients](wikipedia://en/1639294) of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919), and eight *third generation* [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) of the friendly giant. The remaining **six** sporadic groups do not divide the order of the friendly giant, which are termed the *[**pariahs**](wikipedia://en/16569533)* ([*Ly*](wikipedia://en/1110773), [*O'N*](wikipedia://en/1196724), [*Ru*](wikipedia://en/1196760), [*J*<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/10796995), [*J*<sub>3</sub>](wikipedia://en/10796938), and [*J*<sub>1</sub>](wikipedia://en/10796779)).[^o21]

6 is the smallest integer which is not an exponent of a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666), making it the smallest integer greater than 1 for which there does not exist a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of that size.[^o22]

## Greek and Latin word parts {#Greek_and_Latin_word_parts}

### *Hexa*

***Hexa*** is classical [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language#Periods) for "six".[^o5] Thus:

- "[Hexadecimal](wikipedia://en/13263)" combines *hexa-* with the Latinate *decimal* to name a [number base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_base) of 16[^o28]
- A [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) is a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) with six sides[^o29]
  - *L'Hexagone* is a French nickname for the continental part of [Metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) for its resemblance to a [regular hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733#Regular_hexagon)
- A [hexahedron](wikipedia://en/237209) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with six faces, with a [cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_%28geometry%29) being a special case[^o30]
- [Hexameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexameter) is a poetic form consisting of six feet per line
- A "hex nut" is a [nut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nut_%28hardware%29) with six sides, and a hex [bolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screw) has a six-sided head
- The prefix "hexa-" also occurs in the [systematic name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systematic_name) of many [chemical compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_compound), such as [hexane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexane) which has 6 carbon atoms (C6H14).

### The prefix *sex-* {#The_prefix_sex-}

***Sex-*** is a [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) [prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefix_%28linguistics%29) meaning "six".[^o5] Thus:

- *Senary* is the ordinal adjective meaning "sixth"[^o32]
- People with [sexdactyly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexdactyly) have six fingers on each hand
- The measuring instrument called a [sextant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextant) got its name because its shape forms one-sixth of a whole [circle](wikipedia://en/6220)
- A group of six musicians is called a [sextet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextet)
- Six babies delivered in one birth are [sextuplets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextuplet)
- [Sexy prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexy_prime) pairs – Prime pairs differing by six are *sexy*, because sex is the Latin word for six.[^o33][^o34]

The [SI prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SI_prefix) for 1000<sup>6</sup> is [exa-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exa-) (E), and for its reciprocal [atto-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atto-) (a).

## Evolution of the Hindu-Arabic digit {#Evolution_of_the_Hindu-Arabic_digit}

*Image: Edicts of Ashoka numerals.jpg.* The first appearance of 6 is in the [Edicts of Ashoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edicts_of_Ashoka) c. 250 BCE. These are [Brahmi numerals](wikipedia://en/1903587), ancestors of Hindu-Arabic numerals.

*Image: Ashoka Brahmi numerals 256.jpg.* The first known digit "6" in the number "256" in Ashoka's [Minor Rock Edict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_Rock_Edict) No.1 in [Sasaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasaram), c. 250 BCE

The evolution of the modern digit 6 appears to be more simple when compared with the other digits. The modern 6 can be traced back to the [Brahmi numerals](wikipedia://en/1903587) of [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), which are first known from the [Edicts of Ashoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edicts_of_Ashoka) c. 250 BCE.[^o36][^o37][^o38][^o39] It was written in one stroke like a cursive lowercase e rotated 90 degrees clockwise. Gradually, the upper part of the stroke (above the central squiggle) became more curved, while the lower part of the stroke (below the central squiggle) became straighter. The Arabs dropped the part of the stroke below the squiggle. From there, the European evolution to our modern 6 was very straightforward, aside from a flirtation with a glyph that looked more like an uppercase G.[^o40]

On the [seven-segment displays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display) of calculators and watches, 6 is usually written with six segments. Some historical calculator models use just five segments for the 6, by omitting the top horizontal bar. This glyph variant has not caught on; for calculators that can display results in hexadecimal, a 6 that looks like a "b" is not practical.

Just as in most modern [typefaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typeface), in typefaces with [text figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_figures) the character for the digit 6 usually has an [ascender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascender_%28typography%29), as, for example, in

This digit resembles an inverted *9*. To disambiguate the two on objects and documents that can be inverted, the 6 has often been underlined, both in handwriting and on printed labels.

*Image: Bienenwabe mit Eiern und Brut 5.jpg.* The cells of a [beehive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beehive) are six-sided.

## Chemistry {#Chemistry}

- The sixfold [symmetry](wikipedia://en/53741) of [snowflakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowflake) arises from the [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) [crystal structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_structure) of [ordinary ice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice).[^o43]

## Anthropology {#Anthropology}

- A [coffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffin) is traditionally buried six feet under the ground; thus, the phrase "six feet under" means that a person (or thing, or concept) is dead.[^o45]
- Six is a lucky [number in Chinese culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numbers_in_Chinese_culture).[^o46]
- "Six" is used as an informal slang term for the British [Secret Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_Intelligence_Service), MI6.[^o47]
