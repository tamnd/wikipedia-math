# Orders of magnitude (area)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Orders of magnitude"}

*Image: Comparison of units of area.png.* Image comparing various units: [square mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_mile) (the entire yellow square), [square kilometre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre), [hectare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hectare) and [acre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre), as well as a [soccer field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_field) and a [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan) [block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_block).

*Image: 1 km2.svg.* An area of one [square kilometre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) consists of 100 [hectares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hectare) each containing 10,000 [square metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_metre).

This page is a progressive and labelled list of the SI [area](wikipedia://en/1209) orders of magnitude, with certain examples appended to some list objects.

:::
