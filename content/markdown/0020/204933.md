# Niels Ferguson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Niels Ferguson"}

**Niels T. Ferguson** (born 10 December 1965, in [Eindhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven)) is a Dutch [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and consultant. He has collaborated with others, including [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732), designing cryptographic [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms), testing algorithms and protocols, and writing papers and books. Among the designs Ferguson has contributed to is the [AES finalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AES_finalist) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) algorithm [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356) as well as the [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) [Helix](wikipedia://en/883596) and the [Skein hash function](wikipedia://en/20007592).

:::

In 1999, Niels Ferguson, together with [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732) and [John Kelsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Kelsey_%28cryptanalyst%29), developed the [Yarrow algorithm](wikipedia://en/74416), a [Cryptographically-Secure Pseudorandom Number Generator (CSPRNG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographically-secure_pseudorandom_number_generator). Yarrow was later further developed by Niels Ferguson and Bruce Schneier into the [Fortuna](wikipedia://en/1627154) CSPRNG.[^o2]

In 2001, he claimed to have broken the [HDCP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-bandwidth_Digital_Content_Protection) system that is incorporated into [HD DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_DVD) and [Blu-ray Discs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blu-ray_Disc) players, similar to the [DVDs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD) [Content Scramble System](wikipedia://en/18935238), but has not published his research, citing the [Digital Millennium Copyright Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Millennium_Copyright_Act) of 1998, which would make such publication illegal.

Ferguson worked for Microsoft for 21 years.[^o3] In 2006 he published a paper covering some of his work around [Bitlocker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitlocker) full disk encryption at Microsoft.[^o4]

At the CRYPTO 2007 conference rump session, [Dan Shumow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Shumow) and Niels Ferguson presented an informal paper describing a potential [kleptographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleptographic) backdoor in the [NIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) specified [Dual\_EC\_DRBG](wikipedia://en/14259066) [cryptographically secure pseudorandom number generator](wikipedia://en/182249). The kleptographic [backdoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backdoor_%28computing%29) was confirmed to be real in 2013 as part of the [Edward Snowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Snowden) leaks.[^o5][^o6]
