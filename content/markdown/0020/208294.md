# 18 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="18"}

**18** (**eighteen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [17](wikipedia://en/208288) and preceding [19](wikipedia://en/208292).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

18 is a [semiperfect number](wikipedia://en/321918) and an [abundant number](wikipedia://en/321831). In the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445), there are 18 infinite families of groups.[^o5]

Although 18 and σ(18) = 39 are not coprime, 18 is known to be a [solitary number](wikipedia://en/3821872). 18 is the smallest number known to have this property; for 10, 14, and 15, their status as friendly or solitary is not known.

## In science {#In_science}

### Chemistry {#Chemistry}

The [18-electron rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18-Electron_rule) is a [rule of thumb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_thumb) in [transition metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_metal) [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) for characterising and predicting the stability of [metal complexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_complex#Metal_complexes).

## In religion and literature {#In_religion_and_literature}

::: {#o10 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="18 in Judaism"}

### Judaism {#Judaism}

- The [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) word for "life" is חי (*[chai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chai_%28symbol%29)*), which has a [numerical value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria) of 18. Consequently, a custom has arisen in Jewish circles to give donations and monetary gifts in multiples of 18 as an expression of blessing for long life.[^o11][^o12]
- In [Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism), in the [Talmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talmud); [Pirkei Avot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirkei_Avot) (5:25), Rabbi Yehudah ben Teime gives the age of 18 as the appropriate age to get married (*"Ben shmonah esra lechupah"*, at eighteen years old to the [Chupah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chupah) (marriage canopy)). (See [Coming of age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_of_age), [Age of majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority)).
- The [Amidah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amidah) prayer is also known Shemoneh Esrei as (sh'MOH-nuh ES-ray, which means "eighteen") because it has eighteen blessings.

:::

### Other {#Other}

- In [Ancient Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Roman) custom the number 18 can symbolise a [blood relative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_relative).
- In Hinduism, in the [Mahabharata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharata), the number appears in 18 books (Parvas), the 18 chapters of the [Bhagavad Gita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagavad_Gita), the 18 days of the [Kurukshetra War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurukshetra_War), and the 18 [Akshauhini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akshauhini) divisions of armies. There are also 18 major and 18 minor [Puranas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puranas).

## As lucky or unlucky number {#As_lucky_or_unlucky_number}

- In [Chinese tradition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numbers_in_Chinese_culture), 18 is pronounced and is considered a lucky number due to similarity with 'definitely get rich', 'to get rich for sure'.

## Age 18 {#Age_18}

In most countries, 18 is the [age of majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority), in which a minor becomes a legal adult. It is also the [voting age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_age), [marriageable age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriageable_age), [drinking age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drinking_age) and [smoking age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoking_age) in most countries, though sometimes these ages are different than the age of majority. Many [websites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Website) restrict adult content to visitors [who claim to be at least 18 years old.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_%28law%29)

- In the United States, 18 is the:
  - [Age for sexual consent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_consent) in eleven states and under federal law.[^o17]
  - Minimum age to purchase firearms in thirty-eight states with the exception of handguns (21 under federal law).[^o18]
  - [Marriageable age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriageable_age) without parental consent except for [Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska) (19), [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi), (19) and Puerto Rico (21).
  - The minimum age at which one can purchase, rent, or buy tickets to [NC-17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NC-17)-rated films or buy video games with an [Adults Only](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Software_Rating_Board) rating.
- In the UK, 18 is the legal age to purchase a [BBFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Board_of_Film_Classification) "18" rated film.
- In Japan, 18 is the minimum age at which one can purchase, rent, or buy tickets to [R18+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eirin) rated movies or buy video games with a [Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Entertainment_Rating_Organization) rating, although 20 is their cultural age of adulthood.
