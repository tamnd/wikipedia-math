# 16 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="16"}

**16** (**sixteen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [15](wikipedia://en/208252) and preceding [17](wikipedia://en/208288). It is the [fourth](wikipedia://en/208161) [power of two](wikipedia://en/376948).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

16 is the ninth [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), and a [square number](wikipedia://en/213919): [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4_%28number%29)<sup>2</sup> = 4 × 4. It is the first fourth-power [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) of the form *p*<sup>4</sup>: 16 = *2*<sup>4</sup>. It is the smallest number with exactly five [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492): 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16.

Sixteen is the only integer that [equals *m*<sup>*n*</sup> and *n*<sup>*m*</sup>, for some unequal integers *m* and *n*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equation_x^y_=_y^x) ($m=4$, $n=2$, or vice versa).[^o4] It has this property because $2^{2}=2\times 2$. It is also equal to <sup>3</sup>2 (see [tetration](wikipedia://en/482379)).

The aliquot sum of 16 is [15](wikipedia://en/208252), within an [aliquot sequence](wikipedia://en/486266) of four composite members (16, [15](wikipedia://en/208252), [9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9_%28number%29), [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4_%28number%29), [3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3_%28number%29), [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), [0](wikipedia://en/34513)) that belong to the prime **3**-aliquot tree.

Sixteen is the largest known integer $n$, for which $2^n+1$ is prime.

There are sixteen partially ordered sets with four unlabeled elements.

Sixteen is the first [Erdős–Woods number](wikipedia://en/2616413).[^o6]

Sixteen is the only number that can be both the perimeter and area of the same square, due to $4^{2}$ being equal to $4\times 4.$

The [sedenions](wikipedia://en/51434) form a 16-dimensional [hypercomplex number](wikipedia://en/51438) system.

### Hexadecimal {#Hexadecimal}

Sixteen is the base of the [hexadecimal](wikipedia://en/13263) number system, which is used extensively in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).

## Technology {#Technology}

In some [computer programming languages](wikipedia://en/23015), the size in bits of certain data types, such as in [16-bit computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16-bit_computing). In the [16-bit era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16-bit_era), [16-bit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16-bit) microprocessor ran [16-bit applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16-bit_application).

A 16-bit integer can represent up to [65,536](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65,536#In_computing) values.

## Culture {#Culture}

### As a unit of measurement {#As_a_unit_of_measurement}

A low [power of two](wikipedia://en/376948), 16 was used in weighing light objects in several cultures. Early civilizations utilized the [weighing scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighing_scale) as a means to measure mass, which made splitting resources into equal parts a simple task.[^o11][^o12] In the [imperial system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_units), 16 [ounces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ounce) equivalates to one [pound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound_%28mass%29). Until the [State Council of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Council_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) decreed a decimal conversion for currency in 1959,[^o13] China equivalated 16 *[liǎng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tael)* to one *[jīn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jin_%28mass%29)*. Chinese Taoists did finger computation on the trigrams and hexagrams by counting the finger tips and joints of the fingers with the tip of the thumb. Each hand can count up to 16 in such manner. The Chinese abacus uses two upper beads to represent the 5s and 5 lower beads to represent the 1s, the 7 beads can represent a hexadecimal digit from 0 to 15 in each column.

### Age 16 {#Age_16}

- A "[sweet sixteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_sixteen_%28birthday%29)" is celebrated by many sixteen-year-old girls in the United States and Canada. It is a [coming-of-age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_of_age) celebration that [traditionally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tradition) marks a girl's transition into womanhood.
- In the United States and Canada, 16 is the most common [age of sexual consent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_consent), as well as the age in the United Kingdom and [several European countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ages_of_consent_in_Europe).[^o15][^o16] Sixteen is also the minimum age for being allowed a beginner's [driver's license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driver's_license) with parental consent in many US states and in Canada.
