# 4

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="4"}

*Image: Vier.jpg.* Two modern handwritten fours

**4** (**four**) is a [number](wikipedia://en/21690), [numeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numeral_%28linguistics%29) and [digit](wikipedia://en/280582). It is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [3](wikipedia://en/208157) and preceding [5](wikipedia://en/6412297). It is a [square number](wikipedia://en/213919), the smallest [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542) and [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), and is [considered unlucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetraphobia) in many [East Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asian) cultures.

:::

## Evolution of the Hindu-Arabic digit {#Evolution_of_the_Hindu-Arabic_digit}

*Image: Algund Dominikanerinnenklosterkirche Portal bezeichnet 1481 (cropped).jpg.* Sculpted date "1481" in the Convent church of Maria Steinach in [Algund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algund), [South Tirol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Tirol), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy). The upward loop signifies the number 4.

[Brahmi numerals](wikipedia://en/1903587) represented 1, 2, and 3 with as many lines. 4 was simplified by joining its four lines into a cross that looks like the modern plus sign.[^o5] The [Shunga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shunga_Empire) would add a horizontal line on top of the digit, and the [Kshatrapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Satraps) and [Pallava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty) evolved the digit to a point where the speed of writing was a secondary concern. The [Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab)' 4 still had the early concept of the cross, but for the sake of efficiency, was made in one stroke by connecting the "western" end to the "northern" end; the "eastern" end was finished off with a curve. The Europeans dropped the finishing curve and gradually made the digit less cursive, ending up with a digit very close to the original Brahmin cross.[^o6] [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287978171\_Numerical\_Superstition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287978171_Numerical_Superstition)

*Image: Evolution4glyph.png.*

While the shape of the character for the digit 4 has an [ascender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascender_%28typography%29) in most modern [typefaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typeface), in typefaces with [text figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_figures) the glyph usually has a [descender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descender), as, for example, in

On the [seven-segment displays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display) of pocket calculators and digital watches, as well as certain [optical character recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_character_recognition) fonts, 4 is seen with an open top:

This version resembles the [Canadian Aboriginal syllabics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Aboriginal_syllabics) letter ᔦ. The [magnetic ink character recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_ink_character_recognition) "CMC-7" font also uses this variety of "4".[^o8]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

[Lagrange's four-square theorem](wikipedia://en/326395) states that every positive integer can be written as the sum of at most four [squares](wikipedia://en/213919).[^o10][^o11] Four is one of four [all-Harshad numbers](wikipedia://en/641307). Each natural number divisible by 4 is a difference of squares of two natural numbers, i.e. $4x=y^{2}-z^{2}$.

A four-sided plane figure is a [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) or quadrangle, sometimes also called a *tetragon*. It can be further classified as a [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048) or *oblong*, [kite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite), [rhombus](wikipedia://en/183884), and [square](wikipedia://en/659939).

Four is the highest degree general [polynomial equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation) for which there is a [solution in radicals](wikipedia://en/1601833).[^o12]

Four is the only square number $N=n\times n$ where $N - 1$ is a prime number.

The [four-color theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-color_theorem) states that a [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314) (or, equivalently, a flat [map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map) of two-dimensional regions such as countries) can be colored using four colors, so that adjacent vertices (or regions) are always different colors.[^o13] Three colors are not, in general, sufficient to guarantee this.[^o14] The largest planar [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) has four vertices.[^o15]

A solid figure with four faces as well as four vertices is a [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), which is the smallest possible number of faces and vertices a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) can have.[^o16] The regular tetrahedron, also called a 3-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781), is the simplest [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905).[^o17] It has four [regular triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_triangle) as faces that are themselves at [dual positions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual_polytope) with the vertices of another tetrahedron.[^o18]

The smallest non-[cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) has four elements; it is the [Klein four-group](wikipedia://en/41928).[^o19] *A${}_{n}$* [alternating groups](wikipedia://en/49726) are not [simple](wikipedia://en/47600) for values $n$ ≤ $4$.

There are four [Hopf fibrations](wikipedia://en/580384) of [hyperspheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypersphere):

$$\begin{aligned} S^0 & \hookrightarrow S^1 \to S^1, \\ S^1 & \hookrightarrow S^3 \to S^2, \\ S^3 & \hookrightarrow S^7 \to S^4, \\ S^7 & \hookrightarrow S^{15}\to S^8. \\ \end{aligned}$$

They are defined as locally trivial [fibrations](wikipedia://en/316826) that map $f : S^{2n-1} \rightarrow S^{n}$ for values of $n=2,4,8$ (aside from the trivial fibration mapping between two [points](wikipedia://en/593693) and a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220)).[^o20]

In [Knuth's up-arrow notation](wikipedia://en/258733), $2+2=2\times2=2^{2}=2\uparrow\uparrow 2=2\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow2=\;...\; = 4$, and so forth, for any number of up arrows.[^o21]

There are four dimensions in the theory of [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space), three of space and the one being time.

## In culture {#In_culture}

- Four is the sacred number of the [Zia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zia_%28New_Mexico%29), an indigenous tribe located in the U.S. state of [New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico).[^o27]
- [Superstition about the number four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetraphobia) is most common in [East Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asian) nations, where it is a [homonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homonym) for death in many languages.[^o28]

## In technology {#In_technology}

- In [internet slang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_slang), "4" can replace the word "for" (as "four" and "for" are pronounced similarly). For example, messaging "4 u" instead of "for you" when talking to someone.
- In [Leet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet), "4" may be used to replace the letter "A".
