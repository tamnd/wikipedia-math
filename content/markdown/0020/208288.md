# 17 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="17"}

**17** (**seventeen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [16](wikipedia://en/208254) and preceding [18](wikipedia://en/208294). It is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

17 is a [Leyland number](wikipedia://en/4618420) and [Leyland prime](wikipedia://en/4618420#Leyland_primes), using 2 and 3 (2<sup>3</sup> + 3<sup>2</sup>), and using 3 and 4 (3<sup>4</sup> - 4<sup>3</sup>). 17 is one of five known [Fermat primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime), and one of six total [lucky numbers of Euler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_numbers_of_Euler).

Since seventeen is a Fermat prime, regular [heptadecagons](wikipedia://en/196890) can be [constructed](wikipedia://en/523430) with a [compass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass) and unmarked ruler. This was proven by [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) and ultimately led him to choose mathematics over philology for his studies.[^o9][^o10]

The minimum possible number of givens for a [sudoku](wikipedia://en/1365807) puzzle with a unique solution is 17.[^o11][^o12]

### Geometric properties {#Geometric_properties}

#### Two-dimensions {#Two-dimensions}

*Image: Wheel of Theodorus.png.* The [Spiral of Theodorus](wikipedia://en/17206552), with a maximum [right triangles](wikipedia://en/39420) laid edge-to-edge before one revolution is completed. The largest triangle has a [hypotenuse](wikipedia://en/251366) of $\sqrt {17}.$

There are seventeen [crystallographic space groups](wikipedia://en/463721#Classification_systems) in two dimensions. These are sometimes called [wallpaper groups](wikipedia://en/386214), as they represent the seventeen possible symmetry types that can be used for [wallpaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallpaper).

Also in two dimensions, seventeen is the number of combinations of regular polygons that completely [fill a plane vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417#Of_a_plane_tiling).[^o16] Eleven of these belong to [regular and semiregular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_tilings_of_convex_regular_polygons#Regular_tilings), while 6 of these (3.7.42,[^o17] [\3.8.24](wikipedia://en/24082273#Related_polygons),[^o18] [\3.9.18](wikipedia://en/8728724#Uses),[^o19] [\3.10.15](wikipedia://en/1496229#Uses),[^o20] [\4.5.20](wikipedia://en/445032#Uses),[^o21] and 5.5.10)[^o22] exclusively surround a point in the plane and fill it only when irregular polygons are included.[^o23]

Seventeen is the minimum number of [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) on a two-dimensional [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) such that, if the [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) are colored with three different colors, there is bound to be a [monochromatic triangle](wikipedia://en/1864042); see [Ramsey's theorem](wikipedia://en/184898#A_multicolour_example:_R%283,_3,_3%29_=_17).

Either 16 or 18 [unit squares](wikipedia://en/285301) can be formed into rectangles with perimeter equal to the area; and there are no other [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) with this property. The [Platonists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonist) regarded this as a sign of their peculiar propriety; and [Plutarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plutarch) notes it when writing that the [Pythagoreans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoreans) "utterly abominate" 17, which "bars them off from each other and disjoins them".[^o25]

17 is the least $k$ for the [Theodorus Spiral](wikipedia://en/17206552) to complete one [revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution). This, in the sense of [Plato](wikipedia://en/22954), who questioned why Theodorus (his tutor) stopped at $\sqrt{17}$ when illustrating adjacent [right triangles](wikipedia://en/39420) whose bases are units and heights are successive [square roots](wikipedia://en/29208), starting with $1$. In part due to Theodorus's work as outlined in Plato's *[Theaetetus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theaetetus_%28dialogue%29)*, it is believed that Theodorus had proved all the square roots of non-[square](wikipedia://en/213919) integers from 3 to 17 are [irrational](wikipedia://en/20647689) by means of this spiral.

#### Enumeration of icosahedron stellations {#Enumeration_of_icosahedron_stellations}

In three-dimensional space, there are seventeen distinct [fully supported stellations](wikipedia://en/180253#Stellating_polyhedra) generated by an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968).[^o28] The seventeenth prime number is [59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/59_%28number%29), which is equal to the total number of stellations of the icosahedron by [Miller's rules](wikipedia://en/180253#Miller's_rules).[^o29] Without counting the icosahedron as a *zeroth* stellation, this total becomes [58](wikipedia://en/399312), a count equal to the sum of the first seven prime numbers (2 + 3 + 5 + 7 ... + 17). Seventeen distinct fully supported stellations are also produced by [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529) and [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654).[^o28]

#### Four-dimensional zonotopes {#Four-dimensional_zonotopes}

Seventeen is also the number of four-dimensional [parallelotopes](wikipedia://en/25207334) that are [zonotopes](wikipedia://en/669402#Zonotopes). Another 34, or twice 17, are [Minkowski sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of zonotopes with the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), itself the simplest parallelotope that is not a zonotope.[^o33]

#### Abstract algebra {#Abstract_algebra}

Seventeen is the highest dimension for [paracompact Vineberg polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram#Hypercompact_Coxeter_groups_%28Vinberg_polytopes%29) with rank $n+2$ mirror [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647), with the lowest belonging to the third.[^o35]

17 is a [supersingular prime](wikipedia://en/22965333), because it divides the order of the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422). If the [Tits group](wikipedia://en/606719) is included as a *non-strict* group of [Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490), then there are seventeen total classes of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) that are simultaneously [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) and [simple](wikipedia://en/47600) (see [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445)). In [base ten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_ten), (17, 71) form the seventh permutation class of [permutable primes](wikipedia://en/421593).

### Other interesting properties {#Other_interesting_properties}

The sequence of residues (mod $n$) of a [googol](wikipedia://en/18994679#Properties) and [googolplex](wikipedia://en/12365#Mod_n), for $n=1, 2, 3, ...$, agree up until $n=17$.

A series of length seventeen is the longest sequence for which a solution exists in the [irregularity of distributions](wikipedia://en/10782668) problem.[^o39]

*Image: Standard Model of Elementary Particles.svg.* There are seventeen [elementary particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_particle) in the [Standard Model](wikipedia://en/47641) of physics.

## Other fields {#Other_fields}

### Music {#Music}

Where [Pythagoreans](wikipedia://en/291170) saw 17 in between 16 from its [Epogdoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epogdoon) of 18 in distaste.[^o42]

The ratio 18:17 was a popular approximation for the [equal tempered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equal_temperament) [semitone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semitone) (12-tone) during the [Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance).

### Art {#Art}

17 is the number of syllables in a traditional Japanese [haiku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiku), arranged in 3 lines of 5, 7, and 5 syllables.

::: {#o45 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Least random number"}

### Psychology {#Psychology}

17 was described at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) as "the least random number", according to the [Jargon File](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jargon_File).[^o46] This is supposedly because, in a study where respondents were asked to choose a random number from 1 to 20, 17 was the most common choice. This study has been repeated a number of times.[^o47]
