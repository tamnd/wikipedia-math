# 15 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="15"}

**15** (**fifteen**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [14](wikipedia://en/19223527) and preceding [16](wikipedia://en/208254).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

15 is the eighth [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) and the sixth [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542) and the first odd and fourth discrete semiprime. Its proper [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492) are 1, 3, and 5, so it is the first semiprime of the form 3 × q, where q is a higher prime. 15 is a [deficient number](wikipedia://en/321843) because the sum of the proper divisors of 15 is less than 15. The [prime factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_factor) of 15, 3 and 5, form the first [twin-prime](wikipedia://en/41997) pair.

Because 15 is the product of distinct [Fermat primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime), 3 and 5, a regular [pentadecagon](wikipedia://en/1496229) is [constructible](wikipedia://en/523430) with a compass and unmarked straightedge, and $\cos \frac {\pi}{15}$ is [expressible in terms of square roots](wikipedia://en/7439).

The first 15 [superabundant numbers](wikipedia://en/1759187) are the same as the first 15 [colossally abundant numbers](wikipedia://en/1759217). There are 15 [supersingular primes](wikipedia://en/22965333). There are 15 [truncatable primes](wikipedia://en/5839283) that are both right-truncatable and left-truncatable:

2, 3, 5, 7, 23, 37, 53, 73, 313, 317, 373, 797, 3137, 3797, 739397

There are 15 partitions of a set of size 4, making 15 a [Bell number](wikipedia://en/201022)

*Image: Cuadrado Mágico.png.* M = 15

15 is the [magic constant](wikipedia://en/546969) of the unique order-3 [normal magic square](wikipedia://en/52202).

15 is the first number to be [polygonal](wikipedia://en/213996) in 3 ways: it is the 5th [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915),[^o8] a [hexagonal number](wikipedia://en/494013), and [pentadecagonal](wikipedia://en/213996#Table_of_values) number. It is also a [centered tetrahedral number](wikipedia://en/39528968).

15 is a [lucky number](wikipedia://en/208183).

*Image: 15-square-difference.png.* 15 as the difference of two positive squares (in orange).

15 is the smallest positive number that can be expressed as the difference of two positive [squares](wikipedia://en/213919) in more than one way: $4^2-1^2$ or $8^2-7^2$ (see image).

In decimal, 15 contains the digits 1 and 5 and is the result of adding together the integers from 1 to 5 (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15). The only other number with this property (in decimal) is [27](wikipedia://en/369235).

*Image: Chord diagrams K6 matchings.svg.* The 15 perfect matchings of *K*<sub>6</sub>

There are 15 [perfect matchings](wikipedia://en/44031) of the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) *K*<sub>6</sub> and 15 [rooted binary trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooted_binary_tree) with four labeled leaves, both of these being among the types of objects counted by [double factorials](wikipedia://en/507208).

If a positive definite quadratic form with integer matrix represents all positive integers up to 15, then it represents all positive integers via the [15 and 290 theorems](wikipedia://en/5405018).

There are 15 [monohedral convex pentagonal tilings](wikipedia://en/4850275#Monohedral_convex_pentagonal_tilings), with eight being edge-to-edge. There are 15 [regular and semiregular tilings](wikipedia://en/1548669) when infinite (improper) [apeirogonal](wikipedia://en/5658261) forms are counted: three are [regular](wikipedia://en/1548669#Regular_tilings) (with one self-dual), eight are [semiregular](wikipedia://en/1548669#Archimedean,_uniform_or_semiregular_tilings) (with one chiral), and four are [apeirogonal](wikipedia://en/35870958) (from a total of 8, in-which 4 are duplicates).

Full [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) contains 15 mirror planes (2-fold axes). Specifically, the [symmetry order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_order) for both the [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) and [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) (which is made of [regular pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon)) is [120](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/120_%28number%29): equal to sum of the first 15 integers, and the [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) of 5, wherein the sum of the first 5 integers itself is 15. Expressed mathematically:

$\sum_{i=1}^{15}i = 120$, while $\sum_{i=1}^{5}i = 15$, and $5! = 120$.

## Religion {#Religion}

### Sunnism {#Sunnism}

The [Hanbali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanbali) Sunni madhab states that the age of fifteen of a solar or lunar calendar is when one's taklif (obligation or responsibility) begins and is the stage whereby one has his deeds recorded.[^o14]

### Judaism {#Judaism}

In the [Hebrew numbering system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_numerals), the number 15 is not written according to the usual method, with the letters that represent "10" and "5" (י-ה, *[yodh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yodh)* and *[heh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/He_%28letter%29)*), because those spell out [one of the Jewish names of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Names_of_God_in_Judaism#Ehyeh). Instead, the date is written with the letters representing "9" and "6" (ט-ו, *[teth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teth)* and *[vav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waw_%28letter%29)*).[^o16]
