# 20 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="20"}

**20** (**twenty**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [19](wikipedia://en/208292) and preceding [21](wikipedia://en/209248). A group of twenty units is sometimes referred to as a **score**.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

Twenty is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289). It is also the smallest [primitive abundant number](wikipedia://en/27077452) because all of its [proper divisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_divisors) are [deficient](wikipedia://en/321843).[^o6]

The [Happy Family](wikipedia://en/324806#Happy_Family) of [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806) is made up of twenty [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) that are all [subquotients](wikipedia://en/1639294) of the [friendly giant](wikipedia://en/47422), the largest of twenty-six sporadic groups.

### Geometry {#Geometry}

An [icosagon](wikipedia://en/445032) is a polygon with 20 edges. [Bring's curve](wikipedia://en/35477794) is a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181), whose [fundamental polygon](wikipedia://en/1668732) is a regular hyperbolic icosagon.[^o8]

#### Platonic solids {#Platonic_solids}

*Image: Icosahedron.svg.* An [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) has twenty triangular [faces](wikipedia://en/149926).

The largest number of faces a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905) can have is twenty faces, which make up a regular [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968).[^o10] A [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), on the other hand, has twenty vertices, likewise the most a regular polyhedron can have.[^o11] This is because the icosahedron and dodecahedron are [duals](wikipedia://en/8815) of each other.

## Other fields {#Other_fields}

### Science {#Science}

#### Physics {#Physics}

20 is the third [magic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_%28physics%29) in physics.

#### Biology {#Biology}

In some countries, the number 20 is used as an index in measuring [visual acuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_acuity). 20/20 indicates normal vision at 20 ft, although it is commonly used to mean "perfect vision" in countries using the [Imperial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_units) system. (The metric equivalent is 6/6.) When someone is able to see only after an event how things turned out, that person is often said to have had "20/20 hindsight".[^o16]

#### Psychology {#Psychology}

In many disciplines of [developmental psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_psychology), adulthood starts at age 20.

### Culture {#Culture}

#### Age 20 {#Age_20}

The traditional [age of majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority) in [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), although the voting age has been reduced to 18.[^o20] Japanese people commemorate the twentieth birthday with personal ceremonies, and it comes with a number of legal rights like the right to marry. To represent this, the Japanese language has a special word for "20-years-old" that does not follow the rest of their numbering system. Accordingly, the word 二十歳 is read all at once as "はたち" (*hatachi*) rather than the expected pronunciation of the three characters as "にじゅうさい" (*nijyuusai*, which is literally "two," "ten," and the counter for "years old").

#### Number systems {#Number_systems}

20 is the basis for [vigesimal](wikipedia://en/209196) number systems, used by several different civilizations in the past (and to this day), including the [Maya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_civilization).[^o22]

## Indefinite number {#Indefinite_number}

A 'score' is a group of twenty (often used in combination with a [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173), e.g. *fourscore* to mean 80),[^o24] but also often used as a large, [indefinite number](wikipedia://en/538913)[^o25] (e.g. the newspaper headline "Scores of Typhoon Survivors Flown to Manila").[^o26]
