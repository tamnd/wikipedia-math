# Crouzeix's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crouzeix's conjecture"}

**Crouzeix's conjecture** is a problem in [matrix analysis](wikipedia://en/40956141) proposed by [Michel Crouzeix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Crouzeix) in 2004,[^o2] and it can be stated as follows:

:::

$$\|f(A)\| \le 2 \sup_{z\in W(A)} |f(z)|,$$

where the set $W(A)$ is the [field of values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_of_values) of a *n*×*n* (i.e. [square](wikipedia://en/166022)) complex matrix $A$ and $f$ is a complex function that is [analytic](wikipedia://en/14110) in the interior of $W(A)$ and continuous up to the boundary of $W(A)$. Slightly reformulated, the conjecture can also be stated as follows: for all square complex matrices $A$ and all complex polynomials $p$:

$$\|p(A)\| \le 2 \sup_{z\in W(A)} |p(z)|$$

holds, where the norm on the left-hand side is the spectral operator 2-norm.

## History {#History}

**Crouzeix's theorem**, proved in 2007, states that:[^o4]

$$\|f(A)\| \le 11.08 \sup_{z\in W(A)} |f(z)|$$

(the constant $11.08$ is independent of the matrix [dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_%28mathematics%29), thus transferable to infinite-dimensional settings).

Michel Crouzeix and Cesar Palencia proved in 2017 that the result holds for $1+\sqrt{2}$,[^o5] improving the original constant of $11.08$. More recently, dimension-dependent improvements have been obtained: Malman, Mashreghi, O'Loughlin and Ransford showed that for each fixed dimension $N$ there exists a constant $C_N < 1+\sqrt{2}$ such that the inequality holds for all $N\times N$ matrices.[^o6]

Related work connects the constant in Crouzeix-type inequalities to configuration constants arising from the Neumann–Poincaré operator and yields domain-dependent improvements of the Crouzeix–Palencia bound in certain settings.[^o7]

The conjecture states that the constant can be refined to $2$.

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Special cases"}

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

Before the general case was resolved, the conjecture was shown to hold in several special cases. For instance, it holds for all [normal matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_matrices),[^o11] for tridiagonal 3×3 matrices with elliptic field of values centered at an [eigenvalue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue)[^o10] and for general *n*×*n* matrices that are nearly Jordan blocks.[^o11]. Furthermore, [Anne Greenbaum](wikipedia://en/56288604) and Michael L. Overton provided numerical support for Crouzeix's conjecture.[^o12]

:::

::: {#o14 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

On July 27, 2026, neurosurgeon Shanmu Jin posted a preprint claiming a proof of Crouzeix's conjecture.[^o15] Jin reported that the proof was obtained with the assistance of [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)'s [GPT-5.6 Sol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5), after which he checked the resulting argument.[^o16] Greenbaum and Alex Townsend reported that they and Crouzeix had thoroughly reviewed the proof and believed it to be correct.[^o16]

On August 4, 2026, Emiel Lorist and Felix Schwenninger posted an independent proof of the conjecture using a different argument.[^o16] They reported that [GPT-5.6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6) in its Sol variant "was used to explore proof strategies for this note".[^o17]
