# Digital Library of Mathematical Functions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Digital Library of Mathematical Functions"}

The **Digital Library of Mathematical Functions** (**DLMF**) is an online project at the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) (NIST) to develop a database of mathematical reference data for [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) and their applications. It is intended as an update of *[Abramowitz's and Stegun's Handbook of Mathematical Functions](wikipedia://en/1206236)* (A&S).[^o4] It was published online[^o5] on 7 May 2010, though some chapters appeared earlier. In the same year it appeared at [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press) under the title ***NIST Handbook of Mathematical Functions***.[^o6]

:::

In contrast to A&S, whose initial print run was done by the [U.S. Government Printing Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Government_Printing_Office) and was in the [public domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_domain), NIST asserts that it holds copyright to the DLMF under [Title 17 USC 105](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_status_of_work_by_the_U.S._government) of the U.S. Code.[^o7]
