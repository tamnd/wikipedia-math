# List of states and union territories of India by crime rate

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of states and union territories of India by crime rate"}

The crime rate (crime per 100,000 population) in India was 448.3 in 2023 according to the [National Crime Records Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Crime_Records_Bureau).[^o2] Crime rate varies from state to state and also by the type of crime. Other factors that affect crime rate include registration of complaint, cognisance of offence, which differs in different states.

:::

Violent crimes are particularly high in [East India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India), [Northeast India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_India), [National Capital Region (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Capital_Region_%28India%29). Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, West Bengal, Assam, Manipur, Tripura, Delhi, Haryana, Maharashtra have violent crime rate higher than the national average in 2023.[^o2]

Some causes of crimes are region specific. Insurgents committed 147 crimes in 2023, mostly in Manipur. Left wing extremists committed 538 crimes in 2023, mostly in Chhattisgarh & Jharkhand. Terrorists committed 153 crimes in 2023, mostly in Jammu & Kashmir.[^o3]

## State-wise statistics {#State-wise_statistics}

Data is based on the annual "Crime in India" publication by the [National Crime Records Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Crime_Records_Bureau).[^o2][^o3][^o5]

Key:

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="One lakh"}

- Crime Rate is calculated as crime per [one lakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_lakh) (100,000) of population. That is, rate of [cognizable crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognisable_offence) (under Indian Penal Code and Special & Local Laws)

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Indian Penal Code"}

- IPC: [Indian Penal Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Penal_Code)

:::

- SLL: Special & Local Laws
- Charge-sheet rate: Cases where charges were framed against accused, as a percentage of total cases disposed after investigation.
- Conviction rate: Cases where accused was convicted by court after completion of a trial, as a percentage of total cases where trial was completed
