# Shigeru Iitaka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shigeru Iitaka"}

**Shigeru Iitaka** (飯高 茂 Iitaka Shigeru, born May 29, 1942, [Chiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiba_%28city%29)) is a Japanese mathematician at [Gakushuin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gakushuin_University) working in algebraic geometry who introduced the [Kodaira dimension](wikipedia://en/1866873), and [Iitaka dimension](wikipedia://en/13024295). He was a world leader in the field of [Algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

He received his Ph.D. in 1970 from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) under [Kunihiko Kodaira](wikipedia://en/646326) with thesis「代数多様体のD-次元について」(*On D-dimensions of algebraic varieties*). He was awarded the Iyanaga Prize of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) in 1980 and the [Japan Academy Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Academy_Prize_%28academics%29) in 1990.
