# Angus Ellis Taylor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Angus Ellis Taylor"}

**Angus Ellis Taylor** (October 13, 1911 – April 6, 1999) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at various universities in the [University of California system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California).[^o3] He earned his undergraduate degree at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) *summa cum laude* in 1933 and his PhD at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1936 under Aristotle Michal with a dissertation on [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478). By 1944 he had risen to full professor at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), whose mathematics department he later chaired (1958–1964). Taylor was also an astute administrator and eventually rose through the UC system to become provost and then chancellor of [UC Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz). He authored a number of mathematical texts, one of which, *Advanced Calculus (1955 originally published by Ginn/Blaisdell)*, became a standard for a generation of mathematics students.[^o4]

:::
