# C. L. E. Moore instructor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C. L. E. Moore instructor"}

The job title of **C. L. E. Moore instructor** is given by the Math Department at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) to recent math Ph.D.s hired for their promise in pure mathematics research. The instructors are expected to do both teaching and research. Past C. L. E. Moore instructors include [John Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forbes_Nash,_Jr.), [Walter Rudin](wikipedia://en/5491766), [Elias Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein), as well as four [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) winners: [Paul Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Cohen_%28mathematician%29), [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519), [Curtis T. McMullen](wikipedia://en/680168) and [Akshay Venkatesh](wikipedia://en/6647964).

:::

The instructorships are named after [Clarence Lemuel Elisha Moore](wikipedia://en/41941795) (1876–1931), who was a mathematics professor, specializing in geometry, at MIT from 1904 until his death.
