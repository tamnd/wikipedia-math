# Wilfrid Norman Bailey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilfrid Norman Bailey"}

**Wilfrid Norman Bailey** (born 5 September 1893, [Consett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consett), [Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Durham); died 23 October 1961, [Eastbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastbourne)) was a mathematician who introduced [Bailey's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey's_lemma) and [Bailey pairs](wikipedia://en/19516582) into the theory of [basic hypergeometric series](wikipedia://en/2233526). [Bailey chains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey_chain) and [Bailey transforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey_transform) are named after him.

:::
