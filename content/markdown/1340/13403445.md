# Rate of reinforcement

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rate of reinforcement"}

In [behaviorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behaviorism), **rate of reinforcement** is number of [reinforcements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement) per time, usually per minute. Symbol of this [rate](wikipedia://en/332372) is usually *Rf*. Its first major exponent was [B.F. Skinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.F._Skinner) (1939). It is used in the [matching law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matching_law).

:::

*Rf* = *\# of reinforcements/unit of time* = *S<sup>R+</sup>*/*t*
