# Clarkson's inequalities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clarkson's inequalities"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Clarkson's inequalities**, named after [James A. Clarkson](wikipedia://en/38164836), are results in the theory of [*L*<sup>*p*</sup> spaces](wikipedia://en/45194). They give bounds for the *L*<sup>*p*</sup>-[norms](wikipedia://en/990534) of the sum and difference of two [measurable functions](wikipedia://en/44055) in *L*<sup>*p*</sup> in terms of the *L*<sup>*p*</sup>-norms of those functions individually.

:::

## Statement of the inequalities {#Statement_of_the_inequalities}

Let (*X*, Σ, *μ*) be a [measure space](wikipedia://en/45270); let *f*, *g* : *X* → **R** be measurable functions in *L*<sup>*p*</sup>. Then, for 2 ≤ *p* \< +∞,

$$\left\| \frac{f + g}{2} \right\|_{L^p}^p + \left\| \frac{f - g}{2} \right\|_{L^p}^p \le \frac{1}{2} \left( \| f \|_{L^p}^p + \| g \|_{L^p}^p \right).$$

For 1 \< *p* \< 2,

$$\left\| \frac{f + g}{2} \right\|_{L^p}^q + \left\| \frac{f - g}{2} \right\|_{L^p}^q \le \left( \frac{1}{2} \| f \|_{L^p}^p +\frac{1}{2} \| g \|_{L^p}^p \right)^\frac{q}{p},$$

where

$$\frac1{p} + \frac1{q} = 1,$$

i.e., *q* = *p* ⁄ (*p* − 1).
