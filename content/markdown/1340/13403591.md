# Rate of response

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rate of response"}

In [behaviorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behaviorism), **rate of response** is a ratio between two measurements with different units. Rate of responding is the number of responses per minute, or some other time unit. It is usually written as *R*. Its first major exponent was [B.F. Skinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.F._Skinner) (1939). It is used in the [matching law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matching_law).

:::

*R* = # of Responses/Unit of time = *B*/*t*
