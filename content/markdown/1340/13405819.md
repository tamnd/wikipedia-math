# Andrzej Ehrenfeucht

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Ehrenfeucht"}

**Andrzej Ehrenfeucht** (, born 8 August 1932) is a [Polish-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_American) mathematician and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Andrzej Ehrenfeucht formulated the [Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game](wikipedia://en/1040475), using the [back-and-forth method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor's_back-and-forth_method) given in [Roland Fraïssé](wikipedia://en/4234672)'s [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) thesis. Also named for Ehrenfeucht is the [Ehrenfeucht–Mycielski sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrenfeucht–Mycielski_sequence).

In 1971 Ehrenfeucht was a founding member of the Department of [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder). He currently teaches and does research at the University, where he runs a project, "breaking away", with Patricia Baggett; the project, using hands-on activities, aims at raising high-school students' interest in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology).

Two of Ehrenfeucht's students, [Eugene Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Myers) and [David Haussler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Haussler), contributed to the sequencing of the [human genome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_genome). They, with Harold Gabow, Ross McConnell, and [Grzegorz Rozenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grzegorz_Rozenberg), spoke at a 2012 University of Colorado two-day symposium honoring Ehrenfeucht's 80th birthday.[^o4]

Two journal issues have come out in his honor, one at his 65th birthday in [Lecture Notes in Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecture_Notes_in_Computer_Science),[^o5] and one at his 80th in [Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_Computer_Science_%28journal%29).[^o6]

### Private life {#Private_life}

Ehrenfeucht married [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)'s daughter Ina Tarski.[^o8]
