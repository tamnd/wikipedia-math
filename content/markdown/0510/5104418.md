# Demographics of Palestine

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Palestine"}

The [demographics](wikipedia://en/45642) of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine), as defined by the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) (PCBS), includes general [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_%28human_biology%29) information for all [Palestinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinians), including those not residing in the [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories). As of mid-2025, there are about 15.2 million Palestinians in the world, 7.8 million of them residing outside [historic Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine). Of those within the territories that comprise their historic homeland, 5.61 million Palestinians reside in the 1967 [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories), and 1.9 million in the 1948 territories (now modern [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel)).[^o3] Specific information on education level, health of the populace, economic status, religious affiliations and other aspects is provided by the PCBS mainly for the Palestinian territories alone.

:::

According to a commonly used definition as relating to an application of the [1949 Armistice Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949_Armistice_Agreement) [green line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_line_%28Israel%29), the [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories) have contributory parts of the [Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip) and the [West Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank) (including [East Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jerusalem)).

The [Palestinian National Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Authority), the [United Nations Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council),[^o4] the [United Nations General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly),[^o5] the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union),[^o6] the [International Court of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Court_of_Justice),[^o7] and the [International Committee of the Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Committee_of_the_Red_Cross)[^o8] use the terminology "Palestinian territories" or "occupied Palestinian territories". [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) refers to the administrative division encompassing Israeli-controlled Jewish-majority civilian areas of [Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C_%28West_Bank%29) of the West Bank, excluding East Jerusalem, as the *[Judea and Samaria Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judea_and_Samaria_Area)* (אֵזוֹר יְהוּדָה וְשׁוֹמְרוֹן, *Ezor Yehuda VeShomron*).[^o9]

### Population size and structure {#Population_size_and_structure}

#### Israeli estimates {#Israeli_estimates}

The [Israel Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) estimated (2017) that the collective population in the Palestinian territories amounted to 4,543,126 people in 2017. Thereof, 2,155,743 Arabs live in the West Bank, 1,795,183 Arabs live in the Gaza Strip, and 391,000 Jews live in the West Bank outside of East Jerusalem.[^o93] Approximately 214,600 Jews live in East Jerusalem.[^o30] East Jerusalem, once administered by Jordan, came under Israeli occupation after the 1967 [Six-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six-Day_War).[^o31] In the Palestinian territories, c. 86% of the population is Arab (predominantly [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam)), c. 13% is Jewish, other \<1% (cf. [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Israel): Jewish 74%, Arab 21%, other 5%).[^o93]

#### US CIA estimates {#US_CIA_estimates}

The demographic statistics of *The World Factbook* the 2023 estimated population of Israel including the Golan Heights and East Jerusalem is 9,043,387 (2023 est.). Of this population:[^o33]

- Approximately 236,600 Israeli settlers lived in East Jerusalem (2021)
- The split by ethnoreligious groups was Jewish 73.5% (of which Israel-born 79.7%, Europe/America/Oceania-born 14.3%, Africa-born 3.9%, Asia-born 2.1%), Palestinian and other Arab non-Jews 21.1%, other 5.4% (2022 est.)
- By religion, the split was Jewish 73.5%, Muslim 18.1%, Christian 1.9%, Druze 1.6%, other 4.9% (2022 est.)

#### Palestinian estimates {#Palestinian_estimates}

According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) (PCBS), the number of Palestinians in the Palestinian Territories was 3,935,249 in 2009, resulting in a calculated population density of 654 capita per km<sup>2</sup>, of which 433 capita/km<sup>2</sup> in the [West Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank) including Jerusalem and 4,073 capita/km<sup>2</sup> in [Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip).[^o35] In the mid-2009, the share of population less than 15 years was 41.9% and above 65 years 3%.[^o35]

#### UN estimates {#UN_estimates}

According to the [UN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN), the population in the [State of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Palestine) was c. 4.9 million in 2017, resulting in an estimated [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) of 817 [capita](wikipedia://en/357788) per km<sup>2</sup>.[^o37] However, a Census held on 1 December 2017 resulted in a total of 4,781.245. The estimate of the Palestine Central Bureau of Statistics for mid 2023 showed a population total of 5,483,450.

#### Reliability of data {#Reliability_of_data}

West Bank and Gaza population numbers appear to be unreliable, with estimates varying by a million.[^o39]

#### Key derived statistics {#Key_derived_statistics}

Out of 224 listed countries and territories, in 2018, the West Bank ranked 48th with a [total fertility rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_fertility_rate) (TFR) of 3.2, and the Gaza Strip ranked 31st with a TFR of 3.97 according to *The World Factbook*.[^o41] In 2018, the West Bank had an estimated [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_growth_rate) of 1.81% (country comparison to the world: 56th) and the Gaza Strip had a population growth rate of 2.25% (35th).[^o93][^o88][^o42]

The following demographic statistics are from *The World Factbook*, unless otherwise indicated.

### Population {#Population}

Current: 2,098,389 (2023 est.)[^o88]

In 2023 approximately 2.1 million Palestinians lived in the Gaza Strip,[^o88] around 1.6 million of them UN-registered refugees.[^o82]

### Age structure {#Age_structure}

0–14 years: 44.1% (male 415,746/female 394,195) 15–24 years: 21.3% (male 197,797/female 194,112) 25–54 years: 28.5% (male 256,103/female 267,285) 55–64 years: 3.5% (male 33,413/female 30,592) 65 years and over: 2.6% (male 24,863/female 22,607) (2018 est.)

[Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) ([Palestinian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Arabic), [Modern Standard Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Standard_Arabic)), [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) (spoken by many older Gaza Palestinians), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) (widely understood)

## Demographics of the West Bank {#Demographics_of_the_West_Bank}

*Image: Bevölkerungspyramide Gazastreifen 2016.png.* Population pyramid Gaza Strip 2016

*Image: Bevölkerungspyramide Westjordanland 2016.png.* Population pyramid West Bank 2016

The following demographic statistics are from *The World Factbook*, unless otherwise indicated.

### Population {#Population}

Total 3,243,369 (2024 est.); 71.72% of the population is Arab (predominantly Sunni), 28.28% is Jewish[^o93] (cf. [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Israel): Jewish 74%, Arab 21%, other 5%; and [Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_the_Palestinian_territories#Demographics_of_the_Gaza_Strip): Arab 99%)[^o94]

- Palestinian Arab 2,155,743 (July 2017 est.), 71.72%[^o93]
- Israeli Jew 850,481 (2016, 2020 est.), 28.28%[^o93]
  - Approximately 475,481 Israeli Jews thereof live in the West Bank (2020)[^o95]
  - Approximately 375,000 Israeli Jews thereof live in East Jerusalem (2016)[^o96]
- Around 380 [Samaritans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaritans)[^o97]

### Ethnic groups {#Ethnic_groups}

Palestinian Arab: 83% Israeli Jewish and other: 17%

### Religions {#Religions}

Muslim 80–85% (predominantly Sunni) Jewish 12–14% Christian 1.0–2.5%, (mainly Greek Orthodox)[^o93] Other religious minorities include [Palestinian Metawalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Metawalis), [Palestinian Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Druze), and [Palestinian Baha'is](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Baha'is).

### Languages {#Languages}

[Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) ([Palestinian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Arabic), [Modern Standard Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Standard_Arabic)), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) (compulsory in schools, widely spoken by Palestinians), and [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) (spoken by Israeli Jews in the West Bank, and spoken by many Palestinians) are commonly known.
