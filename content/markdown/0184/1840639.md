# Power closed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Power closed"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) a [p-group](wikipedia://en/24664) $G$ is called **power closed** if for every [section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_%28group_theory%29) $H$ of $G$ the product of $p^k$ powers is again a $p^k$th power.

:::

[Regular p-groups](wikipedia://en/15442661) are an example of power closed groups. On the other hand, [powerful p-groups](wikipedia://en/1841291), for which the product of $p^k$ powers is again a $p^k$th power are not power closed, as this property does not hold for all sections of powerful p-groups.

The power closed 2-groups of exponent at least eight are described in (Mann 2005, Th. 16).
