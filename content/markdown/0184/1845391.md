# Charles Hellaby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Hellaby"}

**Charles William Hellaby** is a South African mathematician who is an associate professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cape_Town), [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa), working in the field of [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology).[^o11] He is a member of the [International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union)[^o6] and a member of the [Baháʼí Faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baháʼí_Faith).[^o10]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hellaby was born to Rev. William Allen Meldrum Hellaby and Emily Madeline Hellaby. His twin brother, Mark Edwin Hellaby, pursued a career in literature while his younger brother, Julian Meldrum Hellaby, took to music as a career.[^o9] He obtained a BSc (Physics & Astronomy) at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) in 1977. He completed his MSc ([Relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Relativity)) at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario) in 1981 and his PhD (Relativity) at Queen's University in 1985.[^o11]

From 1985 to 1988 he was a Post Doctoral Researcher at the [University of Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cape_Town) under [George Ellis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_F._R._Ellis). In 1989 he was appointed a lecturer at the University of Cape Town.[^o11][^o7]

Hellaby is a member of the [International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union) (Division J Galaxies and Cosmology), having previously been a member of Division VIII Galaxies & the Universe and subsequently Commission 47 Cosmology.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

His research interests include:

- [Inhomogeneous cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhomogeneous_cosmology). Standard cosmology assumes a [smooth homogeneous universe](wikipedia://en/173937), but the real universe is very lumpy
- Inhomogeneous cosmological models - their evolution, geometry and [singularities](wikipedia://en/54244)
- Non-linear structure formation in the universe
- Extracting the geometry of the cosmos from observations
- The [Lemaitre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Lemaître)–[Tolman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_C._Tolman) cosmological model
- The [Szekeres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Szekeres) cosmological model
- Junction conditions in GR
- Dense [black holes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_holes)
- Local inhomogeneities and the Swiss cheese model

He has also worked on

- The models of [Vaidya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._C._Vaidya), [Schwarzschild](wikipedia://en/264606)–[Kruskal–Szekeres](wikipedia://en/2089093) & [Kinnersley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morris_Kinnersley)
- Classical signature change
- Cosmic strings
- Gravitational collapse

Hellaby co-authored *Structures in the Universe by Exact Methods: Formation, Evolution, Interactions*[^o8] in which applications of inhomogenous solutions to [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s field equations of cosmology are reviewed. The structure of galaxy clusters, galaxies with central [black holes](wikipedia://en/4650) and [supernovae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernova) dimming can be studied with the aid of inhomogenous models.[^o7]
