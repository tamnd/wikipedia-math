# Kiran Kedlaya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kiran Kedlaya"}

**Kiran Sridhara Kedlaya** ( ;[^o5] born July 1974) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He currently is a professor of mathematics and the [Stefan E. Warschawski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_E._Warschawski) chair in mathematics[^o6] at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kiran Kedlaya was born into a [Tulu Brahmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulu_Brahmin) family.[^o8] At age 16, Kedlaya won a gold medal at the [International Mathematics Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Olympiad),[^o9] which he later followed with a silver and another gold medal. While an undergraduate student at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), Kedlaya was a [three-time Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition#Putnam_Fellows) in 1993, 1994, and 1995.[^o10] A 1996 article by *[The Harvard Crimson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Harvard_Crimson)* described him as "the best college-age student in math in the United States".[^o11]

Kedlaya was runner-up for the 1995 [Morgan Prize](wikipedia://en/4180157), for a paper[^o12] in which he substantially improved on results of [László Babai](wikipedia://en/3461604) and [Vera Sós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_T._Sós) (1985)[^o13] on the size of the largest product-free subset of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) of [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_group) *n*.

Kedlaya gave an [invited talk](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2010, on the topic of "Number Theory".[^o14] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15]

## Game shows {#Game_shows}

Kedlaya was a contestant on the game show *[Jeopardy!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeopardy!)* in 2011, winning one episode.[^o17]
