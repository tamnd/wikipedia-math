# B. R. Bhat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="B. R. Bhat"}

**Beliyar Ramdas Bhat** was a professor and head of the department of statistics at [Karnatak University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnatak_University) in India for more than two decades. After retiring from Karnatak University, he became a professor at the [University of Botswana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Botswana) in Africa.[^o3] He was elected member of [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o4] and Fellow of [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664).

:::

Bhat obtained a M.A. degree in mathematics from [Madras University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_University) in 1954, and a M.A. degree in statistics from [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka) University,[^o4] prior to working on a Ph.D. from [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1961. His advisor was [David Blackwell](wikipedia://en/2202900).[^o5] Bhat was elected member of Institute of Mathematical Statistics, and South African Statistical Association. He was the Secretary, Editor and President of Indian Society for Probability and Statistics. He was also the past President of the Section of Statistics of the [Indian Science Congress Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Science_Congress_Association).

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

Bhat was the author of books including:

- (1986). "Modern Probability Theory: An Introductory Textbook". Wiley Eastern. isbn:0-85226-091-1
- (1996). "Stochastic Processes and Statistical Inference". New Age International. isbn:81-224-0836-2
- (2000). "Stochastic Models: Analysis and Applications". New Age International. isbn:9788122412284

He was the founding editor-in-chief of the *Journal of the Indian Society for Probability and Statistics*.[^o11]
