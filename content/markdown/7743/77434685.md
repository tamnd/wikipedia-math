# Categorical probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Categorical probability"}

In mathematics, the term **categorical probability** denotes a collection of [category-theoretic](wikipedia://en/5869) approaches to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and related fields such as [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).

:::

The earliest ideas in the field were developed independently by [Lawvere](wikipedia://en/872760) and by [Chentsov](wikipedia://en/77009002), where they defined a version of what we today call the [category of Markov kernels](wikipedia://en/75969668), and appeared in 1962 and 1965 respectively.[^o2][^o3]

Some of the most widely used structures in the theory are

- The [category of measurable spaces](wikipedia://en/75966255);
- [Markov categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_categories) such as the [category of Markov kernels](wikipedia://en/75969668);
- [Probability monads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_monad) such as [Giry monad](wikipedia://en/75998118).
