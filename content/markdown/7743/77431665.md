# Nicolas Bouleau

::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolas Bouleau"}

**Nicolas Bouleau** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose essays and responsibilities have taken him into other fields such as [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy). The common thread is *interpretation*. Heterodox understanding of a situation, a text, a [program](wikipedia://en/52033) or a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) is for him at the heart of the research activity. His most recent essays focus on biology, where he proposes a dictionary between the work of the mathematician and that of the [synthetic biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_biology).[^o8]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

His scientific career began after six years of ordinary service as a state [civil engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineer). It was described by Raphael Larrère in the preface to *Penser l'éventuel* [^o10] and by [Dominique Bourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominique_Bourg) in *Science et prudence* .[^o11] His main professors were [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485), [Jacques Neveu](wikipedia://en/56512426), [Gustave Choquet](wikipedia://en/17301193), [Paul-André Meyer](wikipedia://en/3653083). He was the founder of the mathematics research center at [École des Ponts ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_Ponts_ParisTech), then its director for ten years. He was a founding member of the journal *Potential Analysis* and [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of *Annales des Ponts et Chaussées*. He taught at the [École des Ponts ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_Ponts_ParisTech), the universities [Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) and [Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_1_Panthéon-Sorbonne_University), and the [Institut d'Etudes Politiques de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences_Po). He delivered over two hundred lectures and gave guest courses at the universities of [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) and [Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University) (Japan), [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University) (UK), [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) (Italy) and [Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_University_of_Rabat) (Morocco). He is now retired and devotes his time to the [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_protection). He denounces the fact that the [volatility](wikipedia://en/11930108) of [financial markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_market) erases information on resource [scarcity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarcity)[^o12]
