# Exact completion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Exact completion"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **exact completion** constructs a [Barr-exact category](wikipedia://en/9790950#Exact_%28effective%29_categories) from any [finitely complete category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely_complete_category). It is used to form the [effective topos](wikipedia://en/30939514) and other [realizability toposes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realizability_topos).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

Let *C* be a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) with finite [limits](wikipedia://en/39248). Then the *exact completion* of *C* (denoted *C*<sub>*ex*</sub>) has for its objects pseudo-equivalence relations in *C*.[^o4] A pseudo-equivalence relation is like an [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259) except that it need not be jointly monic. An object in *C*<sub>*ex*</sub> thus consists of two objects *X*<sub>0</sub> and *X*<sub>1</sub> and two parallel morphisms *x*<sub>0</sub> and *x*<sub>1</sub> from *X*<sub>1</sub> to *X*<sub>0</sub> such that there exist a reflexivity morphism *r* from *X*<sub>0</sub> to *X*<sub>1</sub> such that *x*<sub>0</sub>*r* = *x*<sub>1</sub>*r* = 1<sub>*X*<sub>0</sub></sub>; a symmetry morphism *s* from *X*<sub>1</sub> to itself such that *x*<sub>0</sub>*s* = *x*<sub>1</sub> and *x*<sub>1</sub>*s* = *x*<sub>0</sub>; and a transitivity morphism *t* from *X*<sub>1</sub> × <sub>*x*<sub>1</sub>, *X*<sub>0</sub>, *x*<sub>0</sub></sub> *X*<sub>1</sub> to *X*<sub>1</sub> such that *x*<sub>0</sub>*t* = *x*<sub>0</sub>*p* and *x*<sub>1</sub>*t* = *x*<sub>1</sub>*q*, where *p* and *q* are the two projections of the aforementioned [pullback](wikipedia://en/841689). A morphism from (*X*<sub>0</sub>, *X*<sub>1</sub>, *x*<sub>0</sub>, *x*<sub>1</sub>) to (*Y*<sub>0</sub>, *Y*<sub>1</sub>, *y*<sub>0</sub>, *y*<sub>1</sub>) in *C*<sub>*ex*</sub> is given by an equivalence class of morphisms *f*<sub>0</sub> from *X*<sub>0</sub> to *Y*<sub>0</sub> such that there exists a morphism *f*<sub>1</sub> from *X*<sub>1</sub> to *Y*<sub>1</sub> such that *y*<sub>0</sub>*f*<sub>1</sub> = *f*<sub>0</sub>*x*<sub>0</sub> and *y*<sub>1</sub>*f*<sub>1</sub> = *f*<sub>0</sub>*x*<sub>1</sub>, with two such morphisms *f*<sub>0</sub> and *g*<sub>0</sub> being equivalent if there exists a morphism *e* from *X*<sub>0</sub> to *Y*<sub>1</sub> such that *y*<sub>0</sub>*e* = *f*<sub>0</sub> and *y*<sub>1</sub>*e* = *g*<sub>0</sub>.

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- If the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840) holds, then **Set**<sub>*ex*</sub> is equivalent to **Set**.
- More generally, let *C* be a small category with finite limits. Then the [category of presheaves](wikipedia://en/4840944) **Set**<sup>*C*<sup>*op*</sup></sup> is equivalent to the exact completion of the [coproduct completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coproduct_completion) of *C*.[^o7]
- The effective topos is the exact completion of the category of [assemblies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_%28mathematics%29).[^o7]

:::

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- If *C* is an [additive category](wikipedia://en/60854), then *C*<sub>*ex*</sub> is an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063).[^o10]
- If *C* is [cartesian closed](wikipedia://en/237037) or locally cartesian closed, then so is *C*<sub>*ex*</sub>.[^o11]
