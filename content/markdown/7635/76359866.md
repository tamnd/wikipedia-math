# Classifying space for SU(n)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Classifying space for SU(n)"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **[classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556) $\operatorname{BSU}(n)$** **for the [special unitary group](wikipedia://en/173997)** $\operatorname{SU}(n)$ is the base space of the [universal](wikipedia://en/2179570) $\operatorname{SU}(n)$ [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) $\operatorname{ESU}(n)\rightarrow\operatorname{BSU}(n)$. This means that $\operatorname{SU}(n)$ principal bundles over a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) up to isomorphism are in bijection with [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) classes of its [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) maps into $\operatorname{BSU}(n)$. The isomorphism is given by [pullback](wikipedia://en/1665392). A particular application are [principal SU(2)-bundles](wikipedia://en/80364907).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

There is a canonical inclusion of complex oriented Grassmannians given by $\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k)\hookrightarrow\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^{k+1}), V\mapsto V\times\{0\}$. Its colimit is:

$$\operatorname{BSU}(n) :=\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty) :=\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k).$$

Since real oriented Grassmannians can be expressed as a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325) by:

$$\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k) =\operatorname{SU}(n+k)/(\operatorname{SU}(n)\times\operatorname{SU}(k))$$

the group structure carries over to $\operatorname{BSU}(n)$.

:::

## Simplest classifying spaces {#Simplest_classifying_spaces}

- Since $\operatorname{SU}(1) \cong 1$ is the [trivial group](wikipedia://en/193059), $\operatorname{BSU}(1) \cong\{*\}$ is the trivial topological space.
- Since $\operatorname{SU}(2) \cong\operatorname{Sp}(1)$, one has $\operatorname{BSU}(2) \cong\operatorname{BSp}(1) \cong\mathbb{H}P^\infty$.

## Classification of principal bundles {#Classification_of_principal_bundles}

Given a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$ the set of $\operatorname{SU}(n)$ principal bundles on it up to isomorphism is denoted $\operatorname{Prin}_{\operatorname{SU}(n)}(X)$. If $X$ is a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022), then the map:[^o6]

$$[X,\operatorname{BSU}(n)]\rightarrow\operatorname{Prin}_{\operatorname{SU}(n)}(X), [f]\mapsto f^*\operatorname{ESU}(n)$$

is [bijective](wikipedia://en/3942).

## Cohomology ring {#Cohomology_ring}

The [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723) of $\operatorname{BSU}(n)$ with coefficients in the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) $\mathbb{Z}$ of [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) is generated by the [Chern classes](wikipedia://en/294349):[^o8]

$$H^*(\operatorname{BSU}(n);\mathbb{Z}) =\mathbb{Z}[c_2,\ldots,c_n].$$

::: {#o10 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Classifying space for SU"}

## Infinite classifying space {#Infinite_classifying_space}

The canonical inclusions $\operatorname{SU}(n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SU}(n+1)$ induce canonical inclusions $\operatorname{BSU}(n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{BSU}(n+1)$ on their respective classifying spaces. Their respective colimits are denoted as:

$$\operatorname{SU} :=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\operatorname{SU}(n);$$

$$\operatorname{BSU} :=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\operatorname{BSU}(n).$$

$\operatorname{BSU}$ is indeed the classifying space of $\operatorname{SU}$.

:::
