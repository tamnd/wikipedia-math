# Euclid's Phaenomena

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euclid's Phaenomena"}

*Image: Euclidis Phaenomena 01.jpg.* Euclidis Phaenomena, published in Romæ, 1591

***Phaenomena*** is a work by [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331) on [spherical astronomy](wikipedia://en/2039039). The book is divided into 18 propositions, each dealing with "the important arcs on the celestial sphere". The book was fully translated into English in 1996, authors used two surviving copies for translation.[^o2][^o3]

:::
