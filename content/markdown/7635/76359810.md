# Classifying space for SO(n)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Classifying space for SO(n)"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **[classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556)** $\operatorname{BSO}(n)$ **for the [special orthogonal group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_orthogonal_group)** $\operatorname{SO}(n)$ is the base space of the [universal](wikipedia://en/2179570) $\operatorname{SO}(n)$ [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) $\operatorname{ESO}(n)\rightarrow\operatorname{BSO}(n)$. This means that $\operatorname{SO}(n)$ principal bundles over a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) up to isomorphism are in bijection with [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) classes of its [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) maps into $\operatorname{BSO}(n)$. The isomorphism is given by [pullback](wikipedia://en/1665392). A particular application are [principal SO(2)-bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_SO%282%29-bundle).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

There is a canonical inclusion of real oriented Grassmannians given by $\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{R}^k)\hookrightarrow\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{R}^{k+1}), V\mapsto V\times\{0\}$. Its colimit is:[^o4]

$$\operatorname{BSO}(n) :=\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{R}^\infty) :=\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{R}^k).$$

Since real oriented Grassmannians can be expressed as a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325) by:

$$\widetilde\operatorname{Gr}_n(\mathbb{R}^k) =\operatorname{SO}(n+k)/(\operatorname{SO}(n)\times\operatorname{SO}(k))$$

the group structure carries over to $\operatorname{BSO}(n)$.

:::

## Simplest classifying spaces {#Simplest_classifying_spaces}

- Since $\operatorname{SO}(1) \cong 1$ is the [trivial group](wikipedia://en/193059), $\operatorname{BSO}(1) \cong\{*\}$ is the trivial topological space.
- Since $\operatorname{SO}(2) \cong\operatorname{U}(1)$, one has $\operatorname{BSO}(2) \cong\operatorname{BU}(1) \cong\mathbb{C}P^\infty$.

## Classification of principal bundles {#Classification_of_principal_bundles}

Given a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$ the set of $\operatorname{SO}(n)$ principal bundles on it up to isomorphism is denoted $\operatorname{Prin}_{\operatorname{SO}(n)}(X)$. If $X$ is a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022), then the map:[^o7]

$$[X,\operatorname{BSO}(n)]\rightarrow\operatorname{Prin}_{\operatorname{SO}(n)}(X), [f]\mapsto f^*\operatorname{ESO}(n)$$

is [bijective](wikipedia://en/3942).

## Cohomology ring {#Cohomology_ring}

The [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723) of $\operatorname{BSO}(n)$ with coefficients in the [field](wikipedia://en/10603) $\mathbb{Z}_2$ of [two elements](wikipedia://en/11137263) is generated by the [Stiefel–Whitney classes](wikipedia://en/877761):[^o9][^o10][^o11]

$$H^*(\operatorname{BSO}(n);\mathbb{Z}_2) =\mathbb{Z}_2[w_2,\ldots,w_n].$$

The results holds more generally for every ring with [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) $\operatorname{char}=2$.

The cohomology ring of $\operatorname{BSO}(n)$ with coefficients in the field $\mathbb{Q}$ of [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024) is generated by [Pontrjagin classes](wikipedia://en/385944) and [Euler class](wikipedia://en/2932393):[^o12]

$$H^*(\operatorname{BSO}(2n);\mathbb{Q}) \cong\mathbb{Q}[p_1,\ldots,p_n,e]/(p_n-e^2),$$

$$H^*(\operatorname{BSO}(2n+1);\mathbb{Q}) \cong\mathbb{Q}[p_1,\ldots,p_n].$$

::: {#o14 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Classifying space for SO"}

## Infinite classifying space {#Infinite_classifying_space}

The canonical inclusions $\operatorname{SO}(n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SO}(n+1)$ induce canonical inclusions $\operatorname{BSO}(n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{BSO}(n+1)$ on their respective classifying spaces. Their respective colimits are denoted as:

$$\operatorname{SO} :=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\operatorname{SO}(n);$$

$$\operatorname{BSO} :=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\operatorname{BSO}(n).$$

$\operatorname{BSO}$ is indeed the classifying space of $\operatorname{SO}$.

:::
