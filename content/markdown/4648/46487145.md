# Robert Finn (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Finn"}

**Robert Samuel Finn** (August 8, 1922 – August 16, 2022) was an American mathematician who worked in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Finn was born in [Buffalo, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York). He earned a Bachelor of Science degree in physics from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) and a PhD in mathematics from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University). Studying under [Abe Gelbart](wikipedia://en/38968041), Finn completed a thesis titled *On some properties of the solution of a class of non-linear partial differential equations*.

## Career {#Career}

He completed post-doctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1953 and at the Institute for Hydrodynamics of the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) from 1953 to 1954. In 1954, he became an assistant professor at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) and in 1956 an associate professor at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). Beginning in 1959, he was a professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o6]

At the beginning of his career, Finn did research on [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) and [quasiconformal mappings](wikipedia://en/2749226) and later in his career on mathematical problems of hydrodynamics, such as mathematically rigorous treatments of capillary action. He was a visiting professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and several other universities. He was an exchange scientist in 1978 at the [Soviet Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) and in 1987 at the [German Academy of Sciences at Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin). In 1994 he received an honorary doctorate from the [Leipzig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University). For the academic years 1958–1959 and 1965–1966, he held [Guggenheim Fellowships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship).[^o7] From 1979, he was an editor of the *[Pacific Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Journal_of_Mathematics)*.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Finn [turned 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian) on August 8, 2022. He died eight days later, in [Palo Alto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California), on August 16, 2022.[^o9]
