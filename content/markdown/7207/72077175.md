# Sum of two cubes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sum of two cubes"}

*Image: Sum\_and\_difference\_of\_2\_cubes.svg.* Visual proof of the formulas for the sum and difference of two cubes

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **sum of two cubes** is a [cubed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubed) number added to another cubed number.

:::

## Factorization {#Factorization}

Every sum of two cubes may be factored according to the [identity](wikipedia://en/298428)

$$a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)$$

in [elementary algebra](wikipedia://en/9710).

[Binomial numbers](wikipedia://en/4861681) generalize this [factorization](wikipedia://en/4861681#Factorization) to higher odd powers.

::: {#o4 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

### Proof {#Proof}

Starting with the expression, $a^2-ab+b^2$ and multiplying by $a + b$

$$(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2) = a(a^2-ab+b^2) + b(a^2-ab+b^2).$$

distributing *a* and *b* over $a^2-ab+b^2$,

$$a^3 - a^2 b + ab^2 + a^2b - ab^2 + b^3$$

and canceling the like terms,

$$a^3 + b^3.$$

Similarly for the difference of two cubes,

$$\begin{aligned} (a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2) & = a(a^2+ab+b^2) - b(a^2+ab+b^2) \\ & = a^3 + a^2 b + ab^2 \; - a^2b - ab^2 - b^3 \\ & = a^3 - b^3. \end{aligned}$$

:::

## Fermat's Last Theorem {#Fermat's_Last_Theorem}

[Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953) in the case of exponent 3 states that the sum of two non-zero integer cubes does not result in a non-zero integer cube. The first recorded proof of the exponent 3 case was given by [Euler](wikipedia://en/17902).

:::

## Taxicab and Cabtaxi numbers {#Taxicab_and_Cabtaxi_numbers}

A [Taxicab number](wikipedia://en/513117) is the smallest positive number that can be expressed as a sum of two positive integer cubes in *n* distinct ways. The smallest taxicab number after Ta(1) = 2, is Ta(2) = 1729 (the [Ramanujan number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_number)),[^o11] expressed as

$1^3 +12^3$ or $9^3 + 10^3$

Ta(3), the smallest taxicab number expressed in 3 different ways, is 87,539,319, expressed as

$436^3 + 167^3$, $423^3 + 228^3$ or $414^3 + 255^3$

A [Cabtaxi number](wikipedia://en/678138) is the smallest positive number that can be expressed as a sum of two integer cubes in *n* ways, allowing the cubes to be negative or zero as well as positive. The smallest cabtaxi number after Cabtaxi(1) = 0, is Cabtaxi(2) = 91, expressed as:

$3^3 + 4^3$ or $6^3 - 5^3$

note a link between 1729 and 91: 19 is the total of the digits in 1729; 19 reversed is 91; 19 x 91 is 1729.

Cabtaxi(3), the smallest Cabtaxi number expressed in 3 different ways, is 4104, expressed as

$16^3 + 2^3$, $15^3 + 9^3$ or $-12^3+18^3$
