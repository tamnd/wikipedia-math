# Hansraj Gupta

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hansraj Gupta"}

**Hansraj Gupta** (9 October 1902 – 23 November 1988) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specialising in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), in particular the study of the [partition function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_%28number_theory%29).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gupta was born 9 October 1902 in [Rawalpindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi), then part of [British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_India). His father was Gulraj Gupta, an executive engineer with the [Bombay, Baroda and Central India Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay,_Baroda_and_Central_India_Railway).[^o3] He studied at the [Panjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University) in [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore), where he graduated with a [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in 1925. In 1928 he became a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at the Government College in [Hoshiarpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoshiarpur). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the Panjab University in 1936. By then he had already published several papers on [partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_%28number_theory%29).

Gupta was elected a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (INSA) in 1950. He became head of the Panjab University's new department of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1954. He served as president of the [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) (IMS) for the term 1963–64.[^o4] He retired as director of the Centre of Advanced Study in Mathematics in 1966, by which time he was already travelling [North America](wikipedia://en/21139) as a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor): at the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder) (1962), the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) (1966), and the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta) (1969). He represented the INSA at the 1974 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver). He continued actively researching and publishing mathematics for many years after retirement.

Gupta died 23 November 1988. Since 1990 the annual IMS conference has included a lecture in his honour, the Hansraj Gupta Memorial Award Lecture.[^o5]
