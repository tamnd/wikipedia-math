# Felice Casorati (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Felice Casorati"}

**Felice Casorati** (17 December 1835 – 11 September 1890) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who studied at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia). He was born in [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia) and died in [Casteggio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casteggio).

:::

He is best known for the [Casorati–Weierstrass theorem](wikipedia://en/33868) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). The theorem, named for Casorati and [Karl Theodor Wilhelm Weierstrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Theodor_Wilhelm_Weierstrass), describes the remarkable behaviour of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) near [essential singularities](wikipedia://en/80862), which is that every holomorphic function gets values from any complex [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485), in any neighbourhood of the singularity.

The Casorati matrix is useful in the study of [linear difference equations](wikipedia://en/146806), just as the [Wronskian](wikipedia://en/302846) is useful with [linear differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_differential_equations). It is calculated based on n functions of the single input variable.
