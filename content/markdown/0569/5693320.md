# Theodore Slaman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore Slaman"}

**Theodore Allen Slaman** (born April 17, 1954) is a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) who works in [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory).

:::

Slaman and [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071) formulated the Bi-interpretability Conjecture for the [Turing degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_degrees), which conjectures that the [partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order) of the Turing degrees is [logically equivalent](wikipedia://en/169324) to [second-order arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3542454). They showed that the Bi-interpretability Conjecture is equivalent to there being no nontrivial [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) of the Turing degrees. They also exhibited limits on the possible automorphisms of the Turing degrees by showing that any automorphism will be [arithmetically definable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_set).
