# Leo Harrington

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leo Harrington"}

**Leo Anthony Harrington** (born May 17, 1946) is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) who works in [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414), [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), and [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).

:::

His notable results include proving the [Paris–Harrington theorem](wikipedia://en/3875355) along with [Jeff Paris](wikipedia://en/11279983),[^o3] showing that if the [axiom of determinacy](wikipedia://en/2596030) holds for all [analytic sets](wikipedia://en/634261) then [*x*<sup>\#</sup>](wikipedia://en/248085#Other_sharps) exists for all [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438) *x*,[^o4] and proving with [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) that the [first-order theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-order_theory) of the [partially ordered set](wikipedia://en/23572) of [computably enumerable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computably_enumerable) [Turing degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_degrees) is [undecidable](wikipedia://en/15631055).[^o5]
