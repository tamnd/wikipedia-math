# Apollonian sphere packing

*Image: Apollonian spheres.jpg.* Apollonian sphere packing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Apollonian sphere packing"}

**Apollonian sphere packing** is the three-dimensional equivalent of the [Apollonian gasket](wikipedia://en/1049256). The principle of construction is very similar: with any four spheres that are [cotangent](wikipedia://en/31482) to each other, it is then possible to construct two more spheres that are cotangent to four of them, resulting in an infinite [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621).

:::

The [fractal dimension](wikipedia://en/285907) is approximately 2.473946 (±1 in the last digit).[^o2]

ApolFrac is software for generating and visualization of Apollonian sphere packing.[^o3]
