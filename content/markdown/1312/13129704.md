# Yuri Nesterenko (mathematician)

*Image: Yuri V. Nesterenko.jpeg.* Nesterenko at the workshop "Diophantic Approximations" in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2007

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Nesterenko"}

**Yuri Valentinovich Nesterenko** (Ю́рий Валенти́нович Нестере́нко; born 5 December 1946 in [Kharkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkov), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR), now [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)) is a Soviet and Russian mathematician who has written papers in [algebraic independence](wikipedia://en/287808) theory and [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698).

:::

In 1997, he was awarded the [Ostrowski Prize](wikipedia://en/3159411) for his proof that the numbers $\pi$ and $e^\pi$ are [algebraically independent](wikipedia://en/287808).[^o2] In fact, he proved the stronger result:

- the numbers $\pi, e^\pi$, and [$\Gamma$](wikipedia://en/12316)$(1/4)$ are algebraically independent over $\mathbb{Q}.$
- the numbers $\pi, e^{\pi\sqrt{3}}$, and $\Gamma(1/3)$ are algebraically independent over $\mathbb{Q}.$
- for all positive integers *n*, the numbers $\pi$ and $e^{\pi\sqrt{n}}$ are algebraically independent over $\mathbb{Q}.$

Nesterenko is a professor at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Moscow_State_University), where he completed the mechanical-mathematical program in 1969, then the doctorate program (Soviet habilitation) in 1973, and became a professor of the Number Theory Department in 1992.

He studied under [Andrei Borisovich Shidlovskii](wikipedia://en/7735427#The_Siegel–Shidlovsky_theorem). Nesterenko's students have included [Wadim Zudilin](wikipedia://en/4311292).
