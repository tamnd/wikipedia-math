# Benjamin–Ono equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Benjamin–Ono equation"}

In mathematics, the **Benjamin–Ono equation** is a nonlinear partial [integro-differential equation](wikipedia://en/11095324) that describes one-dimensional [internal waves](wikipedia://en/900160) in deep water. It was introduced by (Benjamin 1967) and (Ono 1975).

:::

The Benjamin–Ono equation is

$$u_t+uu_x+Hu_{xx}=0$$

where *H* is the [Hilbert transform](wikipedia://en/574024).

It possesses infinitely many conserved densities and symmetries; thus it is a completely integrable system.[^o4]
