# List of mathematical science schools in Australia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of mathematical science schools in Australia"}

This article lists **[universities in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Australia)** that offer higher education study options in the **[mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436)** at the [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) and [postgraduate level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postgraduate). 34 Australian universities currently offer higher education study options in the mathematical sciences. Of the 39 members of [Universities Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universities_Australia), only four do not offer mathematical science qualifications: [Charles Darwin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin_University), [Central Queensland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Queensland_University), [Southern Cross University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Cross_University) (although it does offer mathematics education degrees),[^o2] and [Victoria University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_%28Australia%29) (does offer qualifications in mathematics education).[^o3] Students can also study some mathematical science courses online via the ACE Network of the [Australian Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/14344543).[^o4]

:::
