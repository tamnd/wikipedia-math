# Hans Schneider Prize in Linear Algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Schneider Prize in Linear Algebra"}

The **Hans Schneider Prize in Linear Algebra** is awarded every three years by the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149). It recognizes research, contributions, and achievements at the highest level of [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and was first awarded in 1993. It may be awarded for an outstanding scientific achievement or for lifetime contributions and may be awarded to more than one recipient. The award honors[^o3] [Hans Schneider](wikipedia://en/33844957), "one of the most influential mathematicians of the 20th Century in the field of linear algebra and matrix analysis.”[^o4] The prize includes a plaque, certificate and/or a monetary award.[^o5]

:::
