# Concha Gómez

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Concha Gómez"}

**Concha Maria Gómez** is an American mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics at [Diablo Valley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diablo_Valley_College). Gómez is known for being one of the co-founders of the women's organization *The Noetherian Ring*[^o3] at the [University of California Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1991 while attending as a doctoral student. She is an advocate for diversity in the STEM fields and worked for the Wisconsin Emerging Scholars program of the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), whose goal was to promote retention of minority students in STEM.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gómez was born to Patricia M. Difanis Gómez and Nicolas Humberto Gómez.[^o12] She attended [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) for two years before dropping out due to lack of funds and support. She moved from Madison to [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco) at the age of 20 and worked odd jobs before taking classes for fun at a community college. Gómez eventually transferred to [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and earned a B.A. and Ph.D. in mathematics in 2000.[^o13]

Her dissertation was titled "Definability in p-adic [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) rings."[^o12] [Leo Harrington](wikipedia://en/5693354) was her doctoral advisor. [Jack Silver](wikipedia://en/21626512) and [Deborah A. Nolan](wikipedia://en/55742870) served on her dissertation committee. Gómez cites the support of [Jenny Harrison](wikipedia://en/4673481) and [Donald Sarason](wikipedia://en/53777531) for encouraging her to form relationships with mathematicians outside of UC Berkeley.[^o12] Gómez is known for being one of the co-founders of the women's organization *The Noetherian Ring* at the [University of California Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1991 while attending as a doctoral student.[^o11][^o13]

## Career {#Career}

Gómez was an assistant professor of mathematics at [Middlebury College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlebury_College). In the fall of 2004, she began working at [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in a non-tenure track position to teach math and direct the Wisconsin Emerging Scholars (WES) program whose goal was to promote retention of minority students in STEM.[^o13] In 2006, Gómez cited [Wisconsin's passing a constitutional amendment banning same-sex marriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage_in_Wisconsin) as a motivation to seek academic employment elsewhere.[^o9] She is a professor of mathematics at [Diablo Valley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diablo_Valley_College) where she is also fostering a support network of [Latinx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latinx) faculty and students. She is an advocate for diversity in the STEM fields.[^o10]

Gomez is included in a deck of playing cards featuring notable women mathematicians published by the [Association of Women in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Women_in_Mathematics).[^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Concha Gómez was diagnosed with [multiple sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis) as a doctoral student.[^o13]
