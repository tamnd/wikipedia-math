# Giacomo Bellacchi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giacomo Bellacchi"}

**Giacomo Bellacchi** (18381924) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

After graduating from [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa), he became a teacher at a military school and at the Tuscan Technical Institute, where one of his pupils was [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra).[^o4]

Over his career, he carried out research both in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). He wrote many works, among which the most prominent is probably *Introduzione storica alla teoria delle funzioni ellittiche (Historical Introduction to [Elliptic Function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_Function) Theory)*, which became well known and was used worldwide. He also wrote many university textbooks.[^o4]

The library of the *Fondazione Scienza e Tecnica* in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) has named after him its precious collection of math books and works.
