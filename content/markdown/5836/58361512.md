# August Gutzmer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="August Gutzmer"}

**Karl Friedrich August Gutzmer** (2 February 1860 – 10 May 1924) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was chairman of some German commissions about improvement of the teaching of mathematics.[^n1]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Gutzmer was born near [Schwerin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwerin) but his family moved to Berlin when he was eight years old. In Berlin he studied in *Friedrichswerdersche Gymnasium* till 1881.[^n2] From 1881 to 1884 he attended mathematics lectures at [Berlin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_University) despite not being registered as a student.[^n3] He graduated in 1887 in Berlin.

He began his academic career publishing five articles in the Portuguese journal *Jornal de Sciencias mathematicas e astronomicas* (best known as *Teixeira's Journal*) between 1887 and 1890.[^n4] In 1893 he was awarded a doctorate submitting at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle) a dissertation about certain partial differential equations. Married in the same year, he left his academic career to manage his wife's lands.[^n3] In 1894 he returned to teaching at the [Technische Hochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule) in Charlottenburg (now [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)) and the following year he obtained his *venia legendi* at the University of Halle where he taught as assistant professor till 1899.[^n5]

From 1900 to 1905 he was full professor at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena). In 1905 he returned to Halle University succeeding [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216),[^n6] where he remained till his death in 1924. He was rector of the university (1914–1915), chairman of the German Committee for Mathematical and Scientific Teaching (1908–1913), member of the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) from 1900 and his president from 1922 to 1924.[^n3] At the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) he was an invited speaker in 1904 in Heidelberg[^o12] and in 1908 in Rome.[^o13]

Gutzmer published more than forty works; among them, the reports about the activities of the teaching committee are specially relevant. He also wrote a history of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).[^n7]
