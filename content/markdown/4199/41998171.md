# Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute"}

**Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute** (**SAMSI**) was an [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) research organization based in [Research Triangle Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Triangle_Park), [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina). It was funded by the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation), and partnered with [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University), the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), and the [National Institute of Statistical Sciences](wikipedia://en/56043115).

:::

SAMSI was founded in 2002. In 2012, the National Science Foundation renewed SAMSI's funding for an additional five years.[^o3] SAMSI offered programs in [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214) and statistical [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) in 2014–15.[^o4] SAMSI closed its doors in August 2021, after 19 years of work.[^o5]
