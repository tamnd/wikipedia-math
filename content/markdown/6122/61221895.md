# Bobbie Hunter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bobbie Hunter"}

**Roberta Kathleen Hunter** is a New Zealand education academic of [Cook Islands Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Islands_Māori) descent and as of 2019 is a full professor at the [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University). She specialises in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471).[^o3]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After a 2002 [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSc) titled *'Constructing decimal concepts in an inquiry classroom'*[^o5] and a 2007 [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) titled *'Teachers developing communities of mathematical inquiry '* at the [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University), Hunter joined the staff, rising to full professor.[^o3][^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10]

In the [2020 Queen's Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Birthday_Honours_%28New_Zealand%29), Hunter was appointed a [Member of the New Zealand Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit), for services to mathematics education.[^o11]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- Hunter, Roberta, and Glenda Anthony. "Forging mathematical relationships in inquiry-based classrooms with Pasifika students." Journal of Urban Mathematics Education 4, no. 1 (2011): 98–119.
- Hunter, Roberta. "Facilitating communities of mathematical inquiry." Navigating currents and charting directions 1 (2008): 31–39.
- Hunter, Roberta. "Changing roles and identities in the construction of a community of mathematical inquiry." Journal of Mathematics Teacher Education 13, no. 5 (2010): 397–409.
