# American Epidemiological Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="American Epidemiological Society"}

The **American Epidemiological Society** is an American honorary society dedicated to [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997). It was established in 1927, and has held annual meetings since 1968.[^o2] It is the oldest epidemiology organization in the United States.[^o3] Past members of the society have included [Wade H. Frost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_H._Frost), [Alexander Langmuir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Langmuir), [Jonas Salk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_Salk) and [Abraham Lilienfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lilienfeld).[^o4]

:::
