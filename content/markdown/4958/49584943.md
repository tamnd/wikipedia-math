# Flemming Topsøe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Flemming Topsøe"}

**Flemming Topsøe** (born 25 August 1938) is a Danish mathematician, and is emeritus in the mathematics department of the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen).[^o2] He is the author of several mathematical science works, among them works about [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773). He was born in [Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus), son of the engineer [Haldor Topsøe (1913–2013)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldor_Topsøe_%281913–2013%29) and great-grandson of the crystallographer and chemist [Haldor Topsøe (1842–1935)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldor_Topsøe_%281842–1935%29).[^o3] He is the older brother of the engineer [Henrik Topsøe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik_Topsøe) (born 1944).

:::

Topsøe completed his magister degree in mathematics at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) in 1962. After spending a year at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1965–1966, he finished his PhD in 1971 at the University of Copenhagen. His thesis was titled *Topology and Measure*, and was later published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).[^o4] He was leader of the [Danish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22351308) 1978–1982 and dynamic leader of *Euromath* 1983–1998, a great project about expansion of [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet)-based services to mathematics societies in [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe) and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia). He received a Hlavka memorial medal in 1992 and a B. Bolzano honorary medal in 2006 of the [Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Academy_of_Sciences) for his mathematical contributions.
