# Volker Halbach

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Volker Halbach"}

**Volker Halbach** (born 21 October 1965 in [Ingolstadt, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingolstadt,_Germany)) is a German [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). His main research interests are in [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), and [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), with a focus on formal theories of [truth](wikipedia://en/29920). He is Professor of Philosophy at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), Tutorial Fellow of [New College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Volker Halbach's philosophical studies began at [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München). He graduated in 1991 with an M.A. ([Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts)) and in 1994 with a doctorate in philosophy ([D.Phil.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy), summa cum laude) with a dissertation titled "Tarski-Hierarchien".[^o6] In 2001 he earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) with a thesis on "Semantics and Deflationism".

Halbach was an assistant professor at [Universität Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Konstanz) from 1997 to 2004.

In 2004, he took up a role at New College, University of Oxford, where he teaches logic-related courses including Introduction to Logic and Elements of Deductive Logic in the first year, Philosophical Logic, Formal Logic, Philosophy of Logic & Language, and Philosophy of Mathematics.

He served as vice-president of the [British Logic Colloquium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Logic_Colloquium) until 2022.

## Philosophical work {#Philosophical_work}

Halbach is the author of several articles and books including *The Logic Manual*, a textbook on undergraduate logic, and *Axiomatic Theories of Truth*.[^o8]
