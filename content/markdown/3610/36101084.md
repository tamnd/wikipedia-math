# Allan M. Ramsay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Allan M. Ramsay"}

**Allan M. Ramsay** is a [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Formal Linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_Linguistics) in the [Department of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Computer_Science,_University_of_Manchester) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester).[^o4][^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ramsay's undergraduate degree was in [Logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex).[^o10] After completing a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in Logic from the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), he returned to Sussex to complete a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence). Prior to working at [UMIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) and the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), he was Professor of [Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence) at [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin).

## Research {#Research}

Ramsay's research focuses on [Natural language processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing),[^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17] including [morphology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphology_%28linguistics%29) and [syntax](wikipedia://en/26860). He has published papers on the analysis of free [word order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_order) languages, particularly morphology of the [Arabic language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language),[^o8][^o18] which poses a number of specific problems. Some of this research has been funded by the [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council).[^o19]
