# Richard M. Pollack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard M. Pollack"}

**Richard M. Pollack** (January 25, 1935 – September 18, 2018[^o4][^o5]) was an American [geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer) who spent most of his career at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), where he was Professor Emeritus until his death.

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), Pollack published several papers with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [János Pach](wikipedia://en/19900560).[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10]

Pollack also published papers in [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468).[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21] His work with [Jacob E. Goodman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_E._Goodman) includes the first nontrivial bounds on the number of order types and [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471),[^o11] and a generalization of the [Hadwiger transversal theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadwiger_transversal_theorem) to higher dimensions.[^o12] He and Goodman were the founding editors of the journal *[Discrete & Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_&_Computational_Geometry)*.[^o22]

In [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198), Pollack wrote a series of papers with [Saugata Basu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saugata_Basu) and [Marie-Françoise Roy](wikipedia://en/39889069),[^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19] as well as a book.[^o23]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2003, a collection of original research papers in [discrete](wikipedia://en/386468) and [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) entitled *Discrete and Computational Geometry: The Goodman–Pollack Festschrift* was published as a tribute to Jacob E. Goodman and Richard Pollack on the occasion of their 2/3 × 100 birthdays.[^o25]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o26]

A special memorial 556-page issue of *[Discrete & Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_&_Computational_Geometry)* for Pollack was published in October 2020.[^o27]
