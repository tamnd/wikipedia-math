# Stefan Nemirovski

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan Nemirovski"}

**Stefan Yuryevich Nemirovski** (Стефан Юрьевич Немировский; born 29 July 1973) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made notable contributions to [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and was awarded an [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 2000.[^o3]

:::

Nemirovski earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1998.
