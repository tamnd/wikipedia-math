# National Numeracy Strategy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Numeracy Strategy"}

The **National Numeracy Strategy** was designed to facilitate a sound grounding in maths for all primary school pupils. It arose out of the National Numeracy Project in 1996, led by a [Numeracy](wikipedia://en/397245) Task Force in England, and was launched in 1998 and implemented in schools in 1999. [^o2] The strategy included an outline of expected teaching in mathematics for all pupils from Reception to Year 6.[^o3]

:::

In 2003, the strategy, including the framework for teaching, was absorbed into the broader [Primary National Strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_National_Strategy). The framework for teaching was then updated in 2006, but ceased to operate in 2011.[^o3]
