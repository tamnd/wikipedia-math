# Traffic Safety (book)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Traffic Safety"}

***Traffic Safety***, , is a book authored by Leonard Evans, published in 2004 by the Science Serving Society of [Bloomfield Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomfield_Hills,_Michigan), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

The book uses the methods of science to examine the deaths, [injuries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injury), and [property damage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_damage) from traffic crashes. It is more focused on public policy and countermeasures than the author's 1991 book *Traffic Safety and the Driver*. Results derived from many disciplines, including [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology), [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology), [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine), [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), [criminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminology), [biomechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomechanics), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) are synthesized into easily understood relationships.[^o6]

## Chapter headings {#Chapter_headings}

Traffic Safety is organized as follows:

1. Introduction[^o8]
1. Data sources
1. Overview of [traffic fatalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_fatalities)
1. Vehicle mass and size
1. Environment, [roadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadway), and vehicle
1. Gender, age, and [alcohol effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_effects) on survival
1. Older drivers
1. Driver performance
1. Driver behavior
1. [Alcohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholic_drink)
1. Occupant protection
1. [Airbag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbag) benefits, airbag costs
1. Measures to improve [traffic safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_traffic_safety)
1. How you can reduce your risk
1. The dramatic failure of [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) safety policy
1. Conclusions[^o9]
