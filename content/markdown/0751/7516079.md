# Federal Statistical Office of Germany

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Federal Statistical Office of Germany"}

The **Federal Statistical Office** (Statistisches Bundesamt, shortened **Destatis**) is a federal authority of [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). It reports to the [Federal Ministry of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Ministry_of_the_Interior_%28Germany%29).

:::

The Office is responsible for collecting, processing, presenting and analysing [statistical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical) information concerning the topics economy, society and environment. The purpose is providing objective, independent and highly qualitative statistical information for the whole public. About 2300 staff members are employed in the departments in [Wiesbaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesbaden), [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn) and [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o4] The department in [Wiesbaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesbaden) is the main office and runs the largest library specialised in statistical literature in Germany. It is also the Office of the President who is also by tradition, but not by virtue of the office, the [Federal Returning Officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Returning_Officer). In this position, they are the supervisor of the elections of the German Parliament ("[Bundestag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundestag)") and of the [European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Parliament).

The Berlin Information Point is the service centre of the Federal Office in the [German capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and provides information and advisory services for the [German Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Germany), other federal authorities, embassies, industry and public, associations and all those who are interested in official [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in Berlin and [Brandenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg).
