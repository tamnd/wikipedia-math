# Metacyclic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Metacyclic group"}

In [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **metacyclic group** is an [extension](wikipedia://en/345807) of a [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) by a cyclic group. Equivalently, a metacyclic group is a group $G$ having a cyclic [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) $N$, such that the [quotient](wikipedia://en/11526) $G/N$ is also cyclic.

:::

Metacyclic groups are [metabelian](wikipedia://en/1959273) and [supersolvable](wikipedia://en/4966866). In particular, they are [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A group $G$ is metacyclic if it has a normal subgroup $N$ such that $N$ and $G/N$ are both cyclic.[^o4]

In some older books, an inequivalent definition is used: a group $G$ is metacyclic if the [commutator subgroup](wikipedia://en/8847) $[G,G]$ and $G/[G,G]$ are both cyclic.[^o5] This is a strictly stronger property than the one used in this article: for example, the [quaternion group](wikipedia://en/153130) is not metacyclic by this definition.

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- Any [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) is metacyclic.
- The [direct product](wikipedia://en/3706015) or [semidirect product](wikipedia://en/48246) of two cyclic groups is metacyclic. These include the [dihedral groups](wikipedia://en/100267) and the [quasidihedral groups](wikipedia://en/1050057).
- The [dicyclic groups](wikipedia://en/154963) are metacyclic. (Note that a dicyclic group is not necessarily a semidirect product of two cyclic groups.)
- Every [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) of [squarefree](wikipedia://en/29525) order is metacyclic.
- More generally every [Z-group](wikipedia://en/15757039#Groups_whose_Sylow_subgroups_are_cyclic) is metacyclic. A Z-group is a finite group whose [Sylow subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) are cyclic.

:::
