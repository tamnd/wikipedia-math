# Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion** states that if $A$ is an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) or [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) over a [local field](wikipedia://en/145375) $K$, and $\ell$ is a [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) not dividing the characteristic of the [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) of $K$, then $A$ has good reduction if and only if the $\ell$-adic [Tate module](wikipedia://en/3094921) $T_\ell$ of $A$ is [unramified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramification_of_Galois_representations).

:::

[Andrew Ogg](wikipedia://en/31047928) introduced the criterion for elliptic curves.[^n1] [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) and [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933)[^n2] used the results of [André Néron](wikipedia://en/9175241)[^n3] to extend it to abelian varieties, and named the criterion after Ogg, Néron and [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815) (commenting that Ogg's result seems to have been known to Shafarevich).
