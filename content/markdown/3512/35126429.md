# Mathai–Quillen formalism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathai–Quillen formalism"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Mathai–Quillen formalism** is an approach to [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757) introduced by (txt), based on the **Mathai–Quillen form** constructed by (txt). In more detail, using the [superconnection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconnectivity) formalism of Quillen, they obtained a refinement of the [Riemann–Roch formula](wikipedia://en/247261), which links together the [Thom classes](wikipedia://en/1243451) in [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) and [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060), as an equality on the level of differential forms. This has an interpretation in physics as the computation of the classical and quantum (super) [partition functions](wikipedia://en/312252) for the fermionic analogue of a [harmonic oscillator](wikipedia://en/13899) with source term. In particular, they obtained a nice [Gaussian shape](wikipedia://en/245552) representative of the [Thom class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_class) in cohomology, which has a peak along the zero section.

:::
