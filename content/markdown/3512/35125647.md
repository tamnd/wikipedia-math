# Berry–Robbins problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Berry–Robbins problem"}

In mathematics, the **Berry–Robbins problem** asks whether there is a continuous map from [configurations](wikipedia://en/7200539) of *n* points in **R**<sup>3</sup> to the [flag manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_manifold) *U*(*n*)/*T*<sup>*n*</sup> that is compatible with the [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) on *n* points. It was posed by [Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Berry_%28physicist%29) and Robbins in 1997,[^o2] and solved positively by [Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) in 2000.[^o3][^o4]

:::
