# Burnside category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Burnside category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) the **Burnside category** of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) *G* is a category whose objects are finite [*G*-sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-set) and whose morphisms are (equivalence classes of) [spans](wikipedia://en/10318351) of *G*-equivariant maps. It is a categorification of the [Burnside ring](wikipedia://en/4933121) of *G*.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let *G* be a finite group (in fact everything will work verbatim for a [profinite group](wikipedia://en/24266)). Then for any two finite *G*-sets *X* and *Y* we can define an equivalence relation among spans of [*G*-sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-set) of the form $X\leftarrow U \rightarrow Y$ where two spans $X\leftarrow U \rightarrow Y$ and $X\leftarrow W \rightarrow Y$are equivalent if and only if there is a *G*-equivariant [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) of *U* and *W* commuting with the projection maps to *X* and *Y*. This set of equivalence classes form naturally a monoid under disjoint union; we indicate with $A(G)(X,Y)$ the [group completion](wikipedia://en/1634790) of that monoid. Taking pullbacks induces natural maps $A(G)(X,Y)\times A(G)(Y,Z)\rightarrow A(G)(X,Z)$.

Finally we can define the **Burnside category** *A(G)* of *G* as the category whose objects are finite *G*-sets and the morphisms spaces are the groups $A(G)(X,Y)$.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- *A*(*G*) is an [additive category](wikipedia://en/60854) with direct sums given by the disjoint union of *G*-sets and zero object given by the empty *G*-set;
- The product of two *G*-sets induces a symmetric monoidal structure on *A*(*G*);
- The [endomorphism ring](wikipedia://en/59623) of the point (that is the *G*-set with only one element) is the [Burnside ring](wikipedia://en/4933121) of *G*;
- *A*(*G*) is equivalent to the full subcategory of the homotopy category of genuine *G*-spectra spanned by the suspension spectra of finite *G*-sets.
- The Burnside category is [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual).[^o6]

:::

## Mackey functors {#Mackey_functors}

If *C* is an [additive category](wikipedia://en/60854), then a *C*-valued **Mackey functor** is an additive functor from *A(G)* to *C*. Mackey functors are important in representation theory and stable equivariant homotopy theory.

- To every *G*-representation *V* we can associate a Mackey functor in vector spaces sending every finite *G*-set *U* to the vector space of *G*-equivariant maps from *U* to *V*.
- The homotopy groups of a [genuine *G*-spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_homotopy_theory) form a Mackey functor. In fact genuine *G*-spectra can be seen as additive functor on an appropriately higher categorical version of the Burnside category.
