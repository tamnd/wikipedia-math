# Redshift conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Redshift conjecture"}

In mathematics, more specifically in [chromatic homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/42245935), the **redshift conjecture** states, roughly, that [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) $K(R)$ has chromatic level one higher than that of a complex-oriented [ring spectrum](wikipedia://en/21118494) *R*.[^o2] It was formulated by John Rognes in a lecture at [Schloss Ringberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schloss_Ringberg), Germany, in January 1999, and made more precise by him in a lecture at the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669), Germany, in September 2000.[^o3] In July 2022, Robert Burklund, [Tomer Schlank](wikipedia://en/75230578) and Allen Yuan announced a solution of a version of the redshift conjecture for arbitrary $E_{\infty}$-ring spectra, after Hahn and Wilson did so earlier in the case of the truncated Brown-Peterson spectra $BP\langle{n}\rangle$.[^o4]

:::
