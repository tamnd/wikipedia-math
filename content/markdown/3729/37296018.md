# Birkhoff–Kellogg invariant-direction theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Birkhoff–Kellogg invariant-direction theorem"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the **Birkhoff–Kellogg invariant-direction theorem**, named after [G. D. Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/30871906) and [O. D. Kellogg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Dimon_Kellogg),[^o2] is a generalization of the [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](wikipedia://en/4101). The theorem[^o3] states that:

:::

Let *U* be a bounded open neighborhood of **0** in an infinite-dimensional normed linear space *V*, and let *F*:∂*U* → *V* be a compact map satisfying ||*F*(*x*)|| ≥ α for some α > 0 for all *x* in ∂*U*. Then *F* has an invariant direction, *i.e.*, there exist some *x*<sub>o</sub> and some *λ* > 0 satisfying *x*<sub>o</sub> = *λF*(*x*<sub>o</sub>).

The Birkhoff–Kellogg theorem and its generalizations by [Schauder](wikipedia://en/685314) and [Leray](wikipedia://en/245430) have applications to partial differential equations.[^o4]
