# Pavel Grinfeld

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pavel Grinfeld"}

**Pavel Grinfeld** (also known as **Greenfield**) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and associate professor of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) at [Drexel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drexel_University) working on problems in [moving surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Calculus_of_Moving_Surfaces) in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) (particularly [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882)), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Grinfeld received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Applied Mathematics from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 2003<https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/29345>; followed by two years as a postdoctoral fellow at the MIT Department of Earth, Atmosphere and Planetary Sciences, conducting research in [geodynamics](wikipedia://en/9134092). He joined the Department of Mathematics at Drexel University in 2005; currently teaching [Linear Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Algebra), [Differential Equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Equations), and [Tensor calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_calculus).

Grinfeld is the author of the [dynamic fluid film equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_Fluid_Film_Equations). Grinfeld co-authored with Haruo Kojima of [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) on the instability of the 2S [electron bubbles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_bubble).

He is the author of a textbook on tensor calculus,[^o4] a textbook on linear algebra,[^o5] and an interactive online course in linear algebra.[^o6] Many of his lectures are available on YouTube as well as other sites.

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

[Hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics) and [fluid films dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_fluid_film_equations),[^o8] [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952) and [phase transformations](wikipedia://en/54423), minimal surfaces and [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882).

## Other Activities {#Other_Activities}

Grinfeld is the founder of Lemma, Inc. which has developed the online learning system also called Lemma (https://www.lem.ma).
