# Some Remarks on Logical Form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Some Remarks on Logical Form"}

"**Some Remarks on Logical Form**" (1929[^o2]) was the only academic paper ever published by [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741). It contained Wittgenstein's thinking on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and the [philosophy of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittgenstein's_philosophy_of_mathematics) immediately before the rupture that divided the [early Wittgenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Wittgenstein) of the *[Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus](wikipedia://en/30062)* from the [later Wittgenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Later_Wittgenstein) represented in the *[Philosophical Investigations](wikipedia://en/23674)*.[^o3] The approach to [logical form](wikipedia://en/3331706) in the paper reflected [Frank P. Ramsey](wikipedia://en/238285)'s critique of Wittgenstein's account of color in the *Tractatus*, and has been analyzed by [G. E. M. Anscombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._E._M._Anscombe) and [Jaakko Hintikka](wikipedia://en/1710903), among others.[^o3] In a [letter to the editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_to_the_editor) of [*Mind*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_%28journal%29) in 1933 Wittgenstein referred to it as "a short (and weak) article".[^o4]

:::
