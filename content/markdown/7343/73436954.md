# Department of Statistics Malaysia

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Department of Statistics Malaysia"}

The **Department of Statistics Malaysia** (**DOSM**; [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Jabatan Perangkaan Malaysia*) is a government agency in [Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia) that operates under the [Ministry of Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_%28Malaysia%29). It is responsible for the collection and interpretation of reliable statistics related to the economy, population, society and environment of Malaysia which the government primarily uses to assess, review and implement national public policies.

:::

DOSM data are publicly accessible on the [OpenDOSM](https://open.dosm.gov.my/) website.[^o3]

## Responsibilities and activities {#Responsibilities_and_activities}

### MyCensus {#MyCensus}

The department conducts the [Population and housing census of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_in_Malaysia) (MyCensus) every 10 years.[^o6] As of the most recent census in 2020, DOSM had implemented 5 censuses since the establishment of Malaysia.[^o7]

### Economic statistics {#Economic_statistics}

The department supplies a wide variety of financial information.[^o9] Through its various divisions, the department is responsible for the production and compilation of national accounts such as quarterly and annual [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP), national income, consumption, savings, [balance of payments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_of_payments) (BOP).[^o10] Other economic statistics such as services statistics, the balance of payments, economic indicators, international trade statistics, industrial production and construction statistics as also frequently compiled by the department.[^o11]

## History {#History}

### Establishment {#Establishment}

The DOSM was established in 1949 under the Statistics Ordinance 1949.[^o14] It was then known as the Bureau of Statistics and mainly produced statistics related to external trade and estate agriculture[^o15]

In 1965, the Bureau of Statistics was renamed to its current name as provisioned by the Statistics Act 1965. The department's function and role had also been expanded to include data on the economy and society. Under the act, the Sabah and Sarawak statistics office became a branch of the DOSM. The establishment of [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_federal_territories_of_Malaysia) offices in [Peninsular Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_Malaysia) was done in stages from 1971 to 1982.
