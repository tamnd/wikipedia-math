# Einstein Institute of Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Einstein Institute of Mathematics"}

The **Einstein Institute of Mathematics** (מָכוֹן אַייְנְשְׁטַייְן לְמָתֶמָטִיקָה) is a centre for [scientific research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_research) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), founded in 1925 with the opening of the university. A leading [research institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute), the institute's faculty has included recipients of the [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize), [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), [Wolf Prize](wikipedia://en/714278), and [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize).[^o3]

:::

## History {#History}

*Image: Einstein Institute Scopus Building.jpg.* Historic home of the institute on [Mount Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Scopus)

About a year before the official inauguration of the Hebrew University, a Jewish-American philanthropist, Philip Wattenberg, endowed the new university with \$190,000 (equivalent to \$US million in US) for a research institute in the name of theoretical physicist [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736).[^o5][^o6]

The Einstein Mathematics-Physics Institute was established in 1925. Its [inaugural lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_lecture) was given by [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230) (on problems from [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)), the first lecture in higher mathematics to be delivered in [modern Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Hebrew).[^o7] The Institute moved to the Philip Wattenburg Building in 1928,Today part of the Estelle & Eugene Ferkauf Science Teaching Centre. designed by Benjamin Chaikin and Sir [Frank Mears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Mears), where it remained until the Hebrew University lost access to [Mount Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Scopus) in 1948.

[Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230) served as the university's first Professor of Mathematics, and negotiated the transfer of [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887)'s [private library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_library) from [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) to Jerusalem, which served as the basis for the new mathematical library in Jerusalem.[^o8] Other early faculty members included [Binyamin Amirà](wikipedia://en/60040792), [Abraham Fraenkel](wikipedia://en/528451), and [Michael Fekete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fekete).[^o9] A number of researchers arrived at the institute during the rise of the [Nazi regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_regime) in Germany, such as [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522) and [Otto Toeplitz](wikipedia://en/547682).

The *[Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics)* was founded at the institute in 1963 as a continuation of the *Bulletin of the Research Council of Israel* (Section F). A division for [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) was formed within the institute in 1969, which became the independent Institute for Computer Science in 1992.Now part of the Benin School of Computer Science and Engineering.
