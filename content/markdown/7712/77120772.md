# Dorette Pronk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dorette Pronk"}

**Dorothea Ariette** (**Dorette**) **Pronk** (born 1968) is a Dutch and Canadian mathematician specializing in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and categorical approaches to [differentiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentiation_%28mathematics%29). She is a professor of mathematics at [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University). As well as for her research, she is also known for her work promoting [mathematics competitions](wikipedia://en/19710) in Canada.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pronk is originally from [Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam). She became a mathematics student at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) in The Netherlands, where she earned a master's degree in 1991 and completed her Ph.D. in 1995. Her doctoral dissertation, *Groupoid Representations for Sheaves on Orbifolds*, was jointly [promoted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ius_promovendi) by [Dirk van Dalen](wikipedia://en/26828544) and [Ieke Moerdijk](wikipedia://en/25499812). She took a faculty position at [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University) in 2000, after previously being a postdoctoral researcher there. She is a professor of mathematics at Dalhousie.

Pronk began working in mathematics competitions as an observer for the Canadian team at the 1998 and 1999 [International Mathematical Olympiads](wikipedia://en/82208). She chaired Canada's committee on the IMO from 2014 to 2015, the mathematical competitions committee of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) beginning in 2016, and Canada's committee on the [European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/36583162) since 2018. She has also served as a leader of Canada's mathematics team. At Dalhousie, she has organized a mathematics challenge club and [Math circles](wikipedia://en/5163815) focused both on Nova Scotia students and on First Nations students.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Pronk was the 2023 recipient of the Graham Wright Award for Distinguished Service of the Canadian Mathematical Society. In the same year she was named as a Fellow of the Canadian Mathematical Society.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Pronk was raised in a strict denomination of [Reformed (Calvinist) Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Reformed_Protestantism) in the Netherlands. After resisting the call because of her strict background, she has been participating in [messianic dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worship_dance) through All Nations [Christian Reformed Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Reformed_Church_in_North_America) in [Halifax, Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_Nova_Scotia) since 2002.
