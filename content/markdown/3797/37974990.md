# Rogemar Mamon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rogemar Mamon"}

**Rogemar Sombong Mamon**, is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902),[^o2] [quant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_analyst), and academic. He is a co-editor of the [IMA](wikipedia://en/697139) [Journal of Management Mathematics](https://web.archive.org/web/20051120065826/http://imaman.oxfordjournals.org/) published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) since 2009.[^o3]

:::

Mamon is known for his contributions to the developments and applications of [regime-switching](wikipedia://en/22999330) framework useful in economic, financial and actuarial modeling. Majority of his works promote regime-switching paradigms modulated by either discrete- or continuous-time [hidden Markov models (HMM)](wikipedia://en/98770). A recurrent theme of his research is dynamic parameter estimation via HMM filtering recursions. He also made contributions in the areas of [derivative pricing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_pricing), [asset allocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asset_allocation), [risk measurement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_risk), [filtering to remove noise from data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_%28signal_processing%29) as well as [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problems) in quantitative finance. He was the lead editor of the handbook *Hidden Markov Models in Finance*, published by Springer.[^o4][^o5]

In 2010, he and two co-authors won the [Society of Actuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Actuaries) Award for the Best Paper published in the North American Actuarial Journal.[^o6]

Since 2006, he has taught, conducted research and held administrative roles at the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario), and garnered recognitions for excellence in teaching and research.[^o7][^o8] Previously, he held academic positions at [Brunel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University), London, UK; [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia); [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo); and [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).

He spent short-term research visits[^o9] at several institutions including the [Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton_Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), England; Maxwell Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Scotland; Centre for Mathematical Physics and Stochastics, [University of Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aarhus), Denmark; Institute for Mathematics and its Applications, [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), USA; [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide), Australia; [University of Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wollongong) in New South Wales, Australia; and [Centro de Investigacion en Matematicas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_de_Investigacion_en_Matematicas), Mexico.

Mamon holds [professional designations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_certification) conferred by various British learned societies.[^o10] He is a Fellow and Chartered Mathematician of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139); Chartered Scientist of the [Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Council); and Fellow of the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy). He is also a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Arts) and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), and was an elected member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).

He began [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) studies in [Mathematical Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Finance) at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta), and completed his dissertation during a research visit at the [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide), Australia.[^o11] He was supervised by [Robert J. Elliott](wikipedia://en/14584919).[^o2]
