# Group isomorphism problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Group isomorphism problem"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **group isomorphism problem** is the [decision problem](wikipedia://en/8336) of determining whether two given [finite group presentations](wikipedia://en/99494) refer to [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/14828) [groups](wikipedia://en/19447).

:::

The isomorphism problem was formulated by [Max Dehn](wikipedia://en/570602),[^o2] and together with the [word problem](wikipedia://en/33563) and [conjugacy problem](wikipedia://en/4942384), is one of three fundamental decision problems in group theory he identified in 1911.[^o3] All three problems, formulated as ranging over all finitely presented groups, are [undecidable](wikipedia://en/913118). In the case of the isomorphism problem, this means that there does not exist a computer algorithm that takes two finite group presentations and decides whether or not the groups are isomorphic, regardless of how (finitely) much time is allowed for the algorithm to run and how (finitely) much memory is available. In fact the problem of deciding whether a finitely presented group is trivial is undecidable,[^o4] a consequence of the [Adian–Rabin theorem](wikipedia://en/51425733) due to [Sergei Adian](wikipedia://en/5028401) and [Michael O. Rabin](wikipedia://en/298404).

However, there are some classes of finitely presented groups for which the restriction of the isomorphism problem is known to be decidable. They include [finitely generated abelian groups](wikipedia://en/58527), [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707), [Gromov-hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/3172817),[^o5] [virtually](wikipedia://en/10377971) torsion-free [relatively hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/21993809) with [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/144569) parabolics,[^o6] [one-relator groups](wikipedia://en/51159172) with non-trivial [center](wikipedia://en/7125),[^o7] and two-generator one-relator groups with torsion.[^o8]

The group isomorphism problem, restricted to the groups that are given by multiplication tables, can be reduced to a [graph isomorphism problem](wikipedia://en/1950766) but not vice versa.[^o9] Both have [quasi-polynomial-time](wikipedia://en/25269049) algorithms, the former since 1978 attributed to [Robert Tarjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Tarjan)[^o10] and the latter since 2015 by [László Babai](wikipedia://en/3461604).[^o11] A small but important improvement for the case [p-groups](wikipedia://en/24664) of class 2 was obtained in 2023 by Xiaorui Sun.[^o12][^o9]
