# Navin M. Singhi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Navin M. Singhi"}

**Navin Madhavprasad Singhi** (born 1949) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), Mumbai, specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is the recipient of the prestigious [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology).[^o3] Singhi is known for his research in [block designs](wikipedia://en/1188184), [projective planes](wikipedia://en/24350), [Intersection graphs of hypergraphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_graphs_of_hypergraphs), and [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869). He was a visiting professor at [IIT Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Mumbai), [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai), [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) and other various universities in the United States and Europe.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Singhi was born in [Indore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indore) and raised in [Goregaon, Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goregaon,_Mumbai) and earned a M.A. in mathematics from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai).

## Career {#Career}

Singhi earned a Ph.D. (1974) from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai), his advisor was [S. S. Shrikhande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Shrikhande). Professor Singhi is a Fellow of the Indian National Science Academy.
