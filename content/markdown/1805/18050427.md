# Vasanti N. Bhat-Nayak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vasanti N. Bhat-Nayak"}

**Vasanti N. Bhat-Nayak** (10 June 1938 – 12 February 2009) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerned [balanced incomplete block designs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balanced_incomplete_block_design), [bivariegated graphs](wikipedia://en/16647875), [graceful graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graceful_graph), [graph equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_equations) and [frequency partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequency_partition). She earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) in 1970 with the dissertation *Some New Results in PBIBD Designs and Combinatorics*. [S. S. Shrikhande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Shrikhande) was her advisor.

:::

After completing her doctorate, she remained on the faculty at the university, and eventually served as department head.[^o4] She had 44 publications in the field of [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o5]
