# Boris Tsygan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Tsygan"}

**Boris Tsygan** is a mathematician prominent in [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) who is currently a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).[^o3] He performed his undergraduate studies at [Kiev State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev_State_University) concluding in 1980 and earned his PhD in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1987. He is credited with independently discovering [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172) along with [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340).[^o4] He was advised by prominent mathematician [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086) when earning his PhD.

:::

## Research {#Research}

Much of Tsygan's research has been centered around [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172), but he has also contributed to several other branches of mathematics. Notably, he is namesake of the Tsygan Formality Conjecture[^o6] and has studied [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) with [Boris Feigin](wikipedia://en/53823921).
