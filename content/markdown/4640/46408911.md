# Wei Zhang (mathematician)

Zhāng (张) Zhāng Wěi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wei Zhang"}

**Wei Zhang** (; born 1981) is a Chinese mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is a professor of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Zhang grew up in [Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan) province and attended [Chengdu No.7 High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu_No.7_High_School).[^o5] He graduated from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematics in 2004 and earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2009 under the supervision of [Shou-Wu Zhang](wikipedia://en/3582724).[^o6][^o14]

## Career {#Career}

Zhang was a postdoctoral researcher and Benjamin Peirce Fellow at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 2009 to 2011. He was a member of the mathematics faculty at Columbia University from 2011 to 2017, initially as an assistant professor before becoming a full professor in 2015. He has been a full professor at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) since 2017.[^o14][^o8]

## Work {#Work}

His collaborations with [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005), [Xinyi Yuan](wikipedia://en/52461029) and [Xinwen Zhu](wikipedia://en/52460702) have received attention in publications such as [Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976) and [Business Insider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Insider).[^o10][^o11] In particular, his work with [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005) on the [Taylor expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_expansion) of L-functions is "already being hailed as one of the most exciting breakthroughs in an important area of number theory in the last 30 years."[^o10]

Zhang has also made substantial contributions to the global [Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture](wikipedia://en/58884863).

## Awards {#Awards}

He was a recipient of the [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) in 2010, for "far-reaching contributions by himself and in collaboration with others to a broad range of areas in mathematics, including [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283), trace formulas, [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).”[^o13] In 2013, Zhang received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship); in 2016 Zhang was awarded the [Morningside Gold Medal of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/39615068).[^o14][^o15] In December 2017 he was awarded [2018 New Horizons In Mathematics Prize](wikipedia://en/43125933) together with [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005), [Aaron Naber](wikipedia://en/77589855) and [Maryna Viazovska](wikipedia://en/50004967). In 2019 he received the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167).[^o16]

He was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to number theory, algebraic geometry and geometric representation theory".[^o17] He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2023.[^o18]
