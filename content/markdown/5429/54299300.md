# Giacomo Venturoli

*Image: Venturoli - Dialogo aritmetico nel quale si contengono i veri fondamenti dell'arte, dopo il 1664 - 4745958.tif.* *Dialogo aritmetico* (1664)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giacomo Venturoli"}

**Giacomo Venturoli** was a 17th-century Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Venturoli - Scorta di economia, 1717 - 4746065.tif.* *Scorta di economia* (1717)

Venturoli was a priest and taught [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118). He wrote mainly about [accountancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accountancy), contributing to the definition and promotion of this newly established discipline. In his book *Scorta di economia*, he mentioned as references the works by [Angelo Pietra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelo_Pietra) and by [Domenico Manzoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domenico_Manzoni).[^o3]
