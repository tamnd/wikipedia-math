# Alessandra Carbone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alessandra Carbone"}

**Alessandra Carbone** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). She is a professor in the computer science department of the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).[^o3] Since 2009 she has headed the laboratory of computational and [quantitative biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_biology).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

She gained her PhD in mathematics in 1993 at the [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York), supervised by [Rohit Jivanlal Parikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohit_Jivanlal_Parikh), after which she took up a post doctoral post at the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) until 1995 when she took a position at the [Technical University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Vienna) until 1996. She has taught computer science at the [Paris 12 Val de Marne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_12_Val_de_Marne_University) and the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques). She is currently a professor at Sorbonne University, previously known as Pierre and Marie Curie University

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Carbone is a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) and received the [Irène Joliot-Curie Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize) in 2010.[^o4] She is also a recipient in 2012 of the Grammaticakis-Neumann Prize[^o8] and the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour) in 2014.[^o9]
