# Shambhu Dayal Sinvhal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Shambhu Dayal Sinvhal"}

**Shambhu Dayal Sinvhal** (20 November 1923 – 27 May 2015) was an Indian mathematician, astronomist and a former vice chancellor of [Kumaon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumaon_University).[^o3] Born on 20 November 1923 at [Indore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indore) in the largest Indian state of [Madhya Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Pradesh) to B. D. Sinvhal and Tara Devi, he started his career as a member of the faculty of the department of mathematics and astronomy at [Lucknow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_University) in 1946 where he worked till 1954.[^o3] During this period, he secured a doctoral degree (PhD) in mathematics from Lucknow in 1951.[^o3] In 1954, he resigned from the university to take up the post of an assistant astronomer at Uttar Pradesh State Observatory and became its director[^o4] in 1960, a post he held till his superannuation 1978.[^o3] He also worked as a professor at the University of Rourkee at the department of Earth Sciences.[^o5] In 1978, he was appointed as the vice chancellor of [Kumaon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumaon_University).[^o3]

:::

Sinvhal was the president of *Bharat Ganita Parishad*[^o3] and a member of [International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union), London.[^o6] He served [Astronomical Society of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_Society_of_India) as a member of its executive council (1973–74), as its vice president (1975–76), as its president (1977–79) and was its life member.[^o3][^o7] He was a life member of the [National Academy of Sciences, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India)[^o5] and a member of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He chaired the Uttar Pradesh Higher Education Service Commission set up by the state government[^o4] and held memberships in the Instruments Society of India and the Optical Society of India.[^o3] He was a fellow of the [Royal Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Astronomical_Society), London.[^o3] The [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) awarded him the fourth highest Indian civilian award of [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri) in 1975.[^o9]
