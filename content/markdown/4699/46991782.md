# Ronald J. Stern

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald J. Stern"}

**Ronald John Stern** (born 20 January 1947) is a mathematician who works on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155).[^o2] He is emeritus professor at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine).[^o3]

:::

Stern was the first in his family to receive a college education and earned his bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Knox College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knox_College_%28Illinois%29) in [Galesburg, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galesburg,_Illinois).[^o4] He then earned his Ph.D. in 1973 from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) under the joint supervision of [Robert Duncan Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Duncan_Edwards) (de) and Robert F. Brown.[^o5]

Before joining the faculty at the University of California, Irvine in 1989, Stern was a professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) and a visiting professor at UCLA and the [University of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaii).[^o4]

He was an Invited Speaker at the 1998 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o6]

He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]

## Selected papers {#Selected_papers}

These are his most cited papers (according to [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar)), they are all joint work with [Ron Fintushel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Fintushel):[^o9]

- "Knots, links, and 4-manifolds", *[Inventiones mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_mathematicae)* 134 (2), pp. 363–400 (1998)
- "Rational blowdowns of smooth 4-manifolds", *[Journal of Differential Geometry](wikipedia://en/25823636)* 46 (2), pp. 181–235 (1997)
- "Instanton homology of Seifert fibred homology three spheres", *[Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_London_Mathematical_Society)* 61 (3), pp. 109–137 (1990)
- "Immersed spheres in 4-manifolds and the immersed Thom conjecture", *[Turkish Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Journal_of_Mathematics)* 19 (2), pp. 145–157 (1995)
- "Pseudofree orbifolds", *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*, pp. 335–364 (1985)
- "Constructing lens spaces by surgery on knots", *[Mathematische Zeitschrift](wikipedia://en/7457514)* 175 (1), pp. 33–51 (1980)
- "The blowup formula for Donaldson invariants", *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)* 143 (3), pp. 529–546 (1996)
