# Marc Culler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marc Culler"}

**Marc Edward Culler** (born November 22, 1953) is an American mathematician who works in [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774). A native Californian, Culler did his undergraduate work at the [University of California at Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Santa_Barbara) and his graduate work at [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) where he graduated in 1978. He is now a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago). Culler is the son of [Glen Jacob Culler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Culler) who was an important early innovator in the development of the Internet.

:::

## Work {#Work}

Culler specializes in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890),[^o4] low dimensional topology, [3-manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-manifolds), and [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291). Culler frequently collaborates with [Peter Shalen](wikipedia://en/3520910) and they have co-authored many papers.[^o5][^o6] Culler and Shalen did joint work that related properties of representation varieties of [hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1237700) groups to decompositions of 3-manifolds. In particular, Culler and Shalen used the [Bass–Serre theory](wikipedia://en/18673708), applied to the function field of the SL(2,C)-[Character variety](wikipedia://en/14688049) of a 3-manifold, to obtain information about [incompressible surfaces](wikipedia://en/896174) in the manifold. Based on this work, Shalen, [Cameron Gordon](wikipedia://en/4695115), [John Luecke](wikipedia://en/29631637), and Culler proved the [cyclic surgery theorem](wikipedia://en/29580519).

Another important contribution by Culler came in a 1986 paper with [Karen Vogtmann](wikipedia://en/20455422) called "Moduli of graphs and automorphisms of free groups".[^o7] This paper introduced an object that came to be known as [Culler–Vogtmann Outer space](wikipedia://en/12747184#Outer_space).

Culler is one of the authors of a 1994 paper called "Plane curves associated to character varieties of 3-manifolds"[^o8] which introduced the A-polynomial of a knot[^o9] or, more generally, of a 3-manifold with one torus boundary component.

Culler is an editor of *[The New York Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Journal_of_Mathematics)*. He was a [Sloan Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Foundation) Research Fellow[^o11] (1986–1988) and a UIC University Scholar[^o12][^o13] (2008). In 2014, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]
