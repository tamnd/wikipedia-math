# Subhash Khot

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Subhash Khot"}

**Subhash Khot** (born 10 June 1978 in [Ichalkaranji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ichalkaranji))[^o3] is an [Indian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_computer_scientist) who is the Julius Silver Professor of Computer Science in the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). Khot has contributed to the field of [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/7543), and is best known for his [unique games conjecture](wikipedia://en/3000842).[^o4]

:::

Khot received the 2014 [Rolf Nevanlinna Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna_Prize) by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) and received the [MacArthur Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellowship) in 2016.[^o5] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2017[^o6] and was inducted into the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2023.[^o7]

## Education {#Education}

Khot obtained his bachelor's degree in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Bombay) in 1999.[^o9] He received his doctorate degree in computer science from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2003 under the supervision of [Sanjeev Arora](wikipedia://en/21513331). His doctoral dissertation was titled "New Techniques for Probabilistically Checkable Proofs and Inapproximability Results."[^o10]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

Khot is a two time silver medallist representing India at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (1994 and 1995).[^o13] Khot topped the highly difficult IIT [JEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Entrance_Examination) entrance exam in 1995.

He has been awarded the Microsoft Research New Faculty Fellowship Award (2005),[^o14] the [Alan T. Waterman Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_T._Waterman_Award) (2010), the [Rolf Nevanlinna Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna_Prize) for his work on the [Unique Games Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_Games_Conjecture) (2014), and the MacArthur Fellowship (2016).[^o15]

He was elected a fellow of the Royal Society in 2017,[^o16] and was elected to the National Academy of Sciences in 2023.[^o17] In 2026, he received the Michael and Sheila Held Prize.[^o18]
