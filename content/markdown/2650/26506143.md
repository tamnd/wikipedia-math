# Intra-rater reliability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Intra-rater reliability"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), **intra-rater reliability** is the degree of agreement among repeated administrations of a diagnostic test performed by a single rater.[^o2][^o3] Intra-rater reliability and [inter-rater reliability](wikipedia://en/7837393) are aspects of [test validity](wikipedia://en/21526661).

:::
