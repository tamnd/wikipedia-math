# Svetlana Gannushkina

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Svetlana Gannushkina"}

Alekseevna

*Image: Светлана Ганнушкина - портрет (cropped).jpg.* Svetlana Gannushkina

**Svetlana Alekseevna Gannushkina** (Светла́на Алексе́евна Га́ннушкина, born 6 March 1942) is a mathematician and [human rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights) activist in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) who was reported to have been a serious contender for the 2010 [Nobel Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Peace_Prize).[^o2]

:::

Gannushkina became well-known in Russia as a human rights defender in 1990s when many conflicts broke in the countries of the former Soviet Union. Her human rights work dedicated especially to help refugees, [internally displaced persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internally_displaced_person), victims of war.[^o3]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gannushkina was born in Moscow on March 6, 1942. She graduated from the [Moscow State University's Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics).[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Gannushkina worked for many years as a professor of mathematics at the [Russian State University for the Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_State_University_for_the_Humanities).[^o5] In 1990, she helped to found the [Civic Assistance Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Assistance_Committee) (Komitet Grazhdanskoe Sodeistvie), an NGO which campaigns for human rights, particularly with regard to immigrants and refugees in Russian society.[^o7] Since 2015, the organization is labelled a "[foreign agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_foreign_agent_law)" by the [Russian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_government).[^o7]

Gannushkina was a member of [Presidential Council for Civil Society and Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Council_for_Civil_Society_and_Human_Rights) until 2011.[^o5][^o8] She is also on the council of [Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_%28society%29), a society dedicated to the remembrance of victims of Soviet repression.

Gannushkina is a member of the [Yabloko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yabloko) Federal Political Committee.[^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1997 [Open Society Foundations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Society_Foundations) prize for trailblazing work in Russia.[^o5]
- 2006 [Homo Homini Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_Homini_Award) for human rights activism by the Czech group [People in Need](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_in_Need_%28Czech_Republic%29).[^o11]
- 2016 [Right Livelihood Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_Livelihood_Award), often referred to as "Alternative Nobel Prize", in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), Sweden, "*for her decades-long commitment to promoting human rights and justice for refugees and forced migrants, and tolerance among different ethnic groups*".[^o12]
