# Advisory Committee on Mathematics Education

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Advisory Committee on Mathematics Education"}

The **Advisory Committee on Mathematics Education** (**ACME**) is a British policy council for the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) based in [London, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London,_England). Founded in 2002 by the Royal Society and the [Joint Mathematical Council](wikipedia://en/7779526), ACME analyzes mathematics education practices and provides advice on education policy. ACME is funded by the [Gatsby Charitable Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatsby_Charitable_Foundation) (2002–2015) and the [Department for Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_for_Education).[^o2]

:::
