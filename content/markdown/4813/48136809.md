# Petersen–Morley theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Petersen–Morley theorem"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Petersen–Morley theorem** states that, if $a$, $b$, $c$ are three general [skew lines](wikipedia://en/1975821) in space, if $a$, $b$, $c$ are the lines of shortest distance respectively for the pairs $(b,c)$, $(c,a)$ and $(a,b)$, and if $p$, $q$ and $r$ are the lines of shortest distance respectively for the pairs $(a,a)$, $(b,b)$ and $(c,c)$, then there is a single line meeting at right angles all of $p$, $q$, and $r$.

:::

The theorem is named after [Johannes Hjelmslev](wikipedia://en/30961758) (who published his work on this result under his original name Johannes Trolle Petersen) and [Frank Morley](wikipedia://en/589167).
