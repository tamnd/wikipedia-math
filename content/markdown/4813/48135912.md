# Ernst Ruh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Ruh"}

**Ernst Alfred Ruh** (born 23 February 1936) is a Swiss mathematician, specializing in differential geometry.

:::

Ernst Ruh received his doctorate in 1964 from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) under [Katsumi Nomizu](wikipedia://en/37009604) with thesis *On the Automorphism Groups of a G-structure*. He is a professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) and a professor of computer science at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) (1987/89).[^o3] In 1990 Ruh became a full professor (professor ordinarius) of mathematics at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg); he was the successor of Josef Schmid.[^o3] He retired in 2006 as professor emeritus.

His name is attached to the [Gromov-Ruh theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov-Ruh_theorem).[^o4] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at Berkeley in 1986. He became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.
