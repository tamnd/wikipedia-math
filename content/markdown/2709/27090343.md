# Vladimir Andrunakievich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Andrunakievich"}

*Image: Vladimir Andrunakievich 2017 stamp of Moldova.jpg.* Andrunakievich on a 2017 stamp of Moldova

**Vladimir Aleksandrovich Andrunakievich** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Language): Владимир Александрович Андрунакиевич; 3 April 1917 – 22 July 1997) was a Soviet and Moldovan [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). He was a doctor of physical and mathematical sciences (1958), academician (1961) and vice-president (1964—1969, 1979—1990) of the Moldavian Soviet Academy of Sciences. Laureate of the [State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Prizes_of_the_Soviet_Republics) of the [Moldavian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldavian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) (1972).[^o2]

:::

Andrunakievich was born in [Petrograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) and he graduated from the Faculty of Mathematics at the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iași). He received his Ph.D. from the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1947 under the supervision of [Aleksandr Gennadievich Kurosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Gennadievich_Kurosh) and [Otto Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Schmidt).
