# Ludwig Staiger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ludwig Staiger"}

*Image: Ludwig Staiger (2010).jpg.* Ludwig Staiger in 2010

**Ludwig Staiger** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) at the [Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg).

:::

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena) in 1976; Staiger wrote his doctoral thesis, *Zur Topologie der regulären Mengen*, under the direction of Gerd Wechsung and Rolf Lindner.

Previously he held positions at the [Academy of Sciences in Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin) (East), the [Central Institute of Cybernetics and Information Processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Institute_of_Cybernetics_and_Information_Processes), the [Karl Weierstrass Institute for Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute) and the [Technical University Otto-von-Guericke Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto-von-Guericke_University_Magdeburg). He was a visiting professor at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University), the universities [Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Dortmund), [Siegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Siegen), and [Cottbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg_University_of_Technology) in Germany and the [Technical University Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien), Austria. He is a member of the Managing Committee of the [Georg Cantor Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Cantor_Association) and an external researcher of the [Center for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Discrete_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Computer_Science) at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), New Zealand.[^o3]

He co-invented with [Klaus Wagner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Wagner) the [Staiger–Wagner automaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staiger–Wagner_automaton). Staiger is an expert in [ω-languages](wikipedia://en/3983826), an area in which he wrote more than 19 papers including the paper on this topic in the monograph.[^o5] He found surprising applications of ω-languages in the study of [Liouville numbers](wikipedia://en/50964).

Staiger is an active researcher in [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490), [automata theory](wikipedia://en/103356), [effective dimension theory](wikipedia://en/10978612),[^o6] and [algorithmic information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_information_theory). He has [Erdős Number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Number) 2 via to [Solomon Marcus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Marcus).
