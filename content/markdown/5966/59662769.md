# Mikel Harry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikel Harry"}

**Mikel J. Harry** (born: December 28, 1951; died: April 15, 2017) was a prolific author[^o4][^o5][^o6] who is credited for developing [Six Sigma](wikipedia://en/222448), along with [Bill Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Smith_%28Motorola_engineer%29). Harry was sometimes referred to as the 'father of Six Sigma'. One of his books, *Six Sigma: The Breakthrough Management Strategy Revolutionizing the World’s Top Corporations* (published by [Crown Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Business), 2000) has been on the bestseller list of [The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal), [Bloomberg Businessweek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Businessweek) and [Amazon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com).[^o7] He graduated with a PhD from Mary Lou Foulton Teachers College.

:::

At the age of 65, he died on 25 April 2017, in [Chandler, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler,_Arizona).[^o7]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Mikel J. Harry received the lifetime Engineering Excellence Award from [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University) in 2002.[^o7] He was inducted into the "[Who's Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who's_Who)" Registry of Global Business Leaders in 1993.
