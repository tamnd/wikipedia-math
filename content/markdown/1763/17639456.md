# Bertram Huppert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bertram Huppert"}

*Image: Huppert1.jpeg.* Bertram Huppert

**Bertram Huppert** (22 October 1927 – 1 October 2023) was a German mathematician specialised in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and the [representation theory of finite groups](wikipedia://en/874400). His *Endliche Gruppen* ([finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707)) is an influential textbook in group theory, and he has over 50 doctoral descendants.

:::

## Life {#Life}

### Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Bertram Huppert was born in [Worms, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worms,_Germany) on 22 October 1927. He went to school in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn) from 1934 until 1945. In 1950, he wrote his diploma thesis in mathematics at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz). The thesis discussed "*nicht fortsetzbare Potenzreihen*" (discontinuous [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)), and was written under the direction of [Helmut Wielandt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_Wielandt).

When Wielandt moved to the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) in April 1951, Huppert followed him later in the year, and gained his doctorate (as Wielandt's first doctoral student) with the work "*Produkte von paarweise vertauschbaren zyklischen Gruppen*" (products of pairwise [permutable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutable_subgroup) [cyclic groups](wikipedia://en/52327)), in which he showed, among other things, that such groups were [supersoluble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersoluble_group). This was the first of more than forty further scientific works, not including his books and [monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph). The focus of the dissertation was very close to Wielandt's interests at the time, whose 1951 work shows that the product of pairwise [permutable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutable_subgroup) [nilpotent groups](wikipedia://en/144569) is [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012).

### Academic career {#Academic_career}

Huppert spent the years 1963/64 as a visiting professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) and at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (Caltech) in [Pasadena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasadena,_California). In January 1965, he became a professor of pure mathematics at the University of Mainz, where he later became a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) in 1994. He put a lot of effort into building up the Mainz [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) research groups.

Following an assignment, he wrote a monumental standard text in the theory of [finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_groups), *Endliche Gruppen I*. The group around [Wolfgang Gaschütz](wikipedia://en/75172510) in [Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiel) provided important contributions in discussions to that volume. Volumes II and III appeared 14 years later in English with co-author [Norman Blackburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Blackburn_%28mathematician%29).

In 1984, Huppert founded, together with Gerhard O. Michler, the first [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) priority programme in Mathematics at the German universities of [Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen), [Bielefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen) and [Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz).

From 1964 to 1985, Huppert was a member of the editorial board of the [Journal of Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Algebra). Together with [Wolfgang Gaschütz](wikipedia://en/75172510) and [Karl W. Gruenberg](wikipedia://en/57806841), he organised [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) workshops on group theory over many years, and with Michler the Oberwolfach workshop on representation theory.

Huppert was a founding member of the Institute of Experimental Mathematics of the University of Essen and is a member of the *Akademie gemeinnütziger Wissenschaften zu Erfurt*.

## Death {#Death}

Huppert died on 1 October 2023, at the age of 95.[^o6]
