# Artin–Verdier duality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Artin–Verdier duality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Artin–Verdier duality** is a [duality](wikipedia://en/609737) theorem for constructible abelian [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) over the [spectrum of a ring](wikipedia://en/37291) of [algebraic numbers](wikipedia://en/1158), introduced by (txt), that generalizes [Tate duality](wikipedia://en/33900144).

:::

It shows that, as far as [etale](wikipedia://en/396231) (or [flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_cohomology)) [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) is concerned, the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) in a [number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) behaves like a [3-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1018257) [mathematical object](wikipedia://en/19453961).

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *X* be the [spectrum](wikipedia://en/37291) of the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) in a [totally imaginary](wikipedia://en/34962418) [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) *K*, and *F* a [constructible](wikipedia://en/26131119) [étale](wikipedia://en/5707590) [abelian sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_sheaf) on *X*. Then the [Yoneda pairing](wikipedia://en/46783690)

$$H^r(X,F)\times \operatorname{Ext}^{3-r}(F,\mathbb{G}_m)\to H^3(X,\mathbb{G}_m)=\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$$

is a [non-degenerate pairing](wikipedia://en/1191490) of finite abelian groups, for every integer *r*.

Here, *H<sup>r</sup>*(*X,F*) is the *r*-th [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) group of the [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) *X* with values in *F,* and Ext*<sup>r</sup>*(*F,G*) is the group of *r*-[extensions](wikipedia://en/840758) of the étale sheaf *G* by the étale sheaf *F* in the [category](wikipedia://en/45063) of étale abelian sheaves on *X.* Moreover, *G<sub>m</sub>* denotes the étale sheaf of [units](wikipedia://en/289666) in the [structure sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_sheaf) of *X.*

(txt) proved Artin–Verdier duality for constructible, but not necessarily torsion sheaves. For such a sheaf *F*, the above pairing induces isomorphisms

$$\begin{aligned} H^r(X, F)^* &\cong \operatorname{Ext}^{3-r}(F, \mathbb{G}_m) && r = 0, 1 \\ H^r(X, F) &\cong \operatorname{Ext}^{3-r}(F, \mathbb{G}_m)^* && r = 2, 3 \end{aligned}$$

where

$$(-)^* = \operatorname{Hom}(-, \mathbb{Q} /\mathbb{Z}).$$

:::

## Finite flat group schemes {#Finite_flat_group_schemes}

Let *U* be an open subscheme of the spectrum of the ring of integers in a number field *K*, and *F* a finite flat commutative [group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) over *U*. Then the [cup product](wikipedia://en/848684) defines a non-degenerate pairing

$$H^r(U,F^D)\times H_c^{3-r}(U,F)\to H_c^3(U,{\mathbb G}_m)=\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$$

of finite abelian groups, for all integers *r*.

Here *F<sup>D</sup>* denotes the [Cartier dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartier_dual) of *F*, which is another finite flat commutative group scheme over *U*. Moreover, $H^r(U,F)$ is the *r*-th [flat cohomology](wikipedia://en/10538912) group of the scheme *U* with values in the flat abelian sheaf *F*, and $H_c^r(U,F)$ is the *r*-th *flat cohomology with compact supports* of *U* with values in the flat abelian sheaf *F.*

The *flat cohomology with compact supports* is defined to give rise to a long exact sequence

$$\cdots\to H^r_c(U,F)\to H^r(U,F)\to \bigoplus\nolimits_{v\notin U} H^r(K_v,F)\to H^{r+1}_c(U,F) \to\cdots$$

The sum is taken over all [places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_%28mathematics%29) of *K*, which are not in *U*, including the archimedean ones. The local contribution *H<sup>r</sup>*(*K<sub>v</sub>*, *F*) is the [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) of the [Henselization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henselization) *K<sub>v</sub>* of *K* at the place *v*, modified a la [Tate](wikipedia://en/6070279):

$$H^r(K_v,F)=H^r_T(\mathrm{Gal}(K_v^s/K_v),F(K_v^s)).$$

Here $K_v^s$ is a separable closure of $K_v.$
