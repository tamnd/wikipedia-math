# Andrei Toom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrei Toom"}

*Image: Andre Toom 2014.jpg.* Andrei Toom in 2004

**Andrei Leonovich Toom** (in [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): **Андрей Леонович Тоом**), also known as **André Toom**, (1942 in [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent), [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) – 2022 in [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA)) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for the [Toom–Cook algorithm](wikipedia://en/330056) and [Toom's rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toom's_rule). Toom was a retired professor of the [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) department at [Federal University of Pernambuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Pernambuco) in Brazil. He was arrested in Boa Viagem, Recife, Brazil, in 2005 following allegations of sexual abuse involving a 16-year-old. He was later acquitted.

:::

Toom was a student of [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738). His father was the Russian-Estonian poet [Leon Toom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Toom) (ru). His grandfather was known soviet poet [Pavel Antokolsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Antokolsky).

At the age of 80, Toom died of prolonged illness on September 29th, 2022, in Queens, New York.

At his time of death, he had two children (one late), and one grandchild.
