# Abhyankar's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abhyankar's conjecture"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), **Abhyankar's conjecture for affine curves** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) of [Shreeram Abhyankar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreeram_Abhyankar) posed in 1957, on the [Galois groups](wikipedia://en/12673) of [algebraic function fields](wikipedia://en/447645) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) *p*.[^o2] The soluble case was solved by Serre in 1990[^o3] and the full conjecture was proved in 1994 by work of [Michel Raynaud](wikipedia://en/4760928) and [David Harbater](wikipedia://en/3521313).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The problem involves a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) *G*, a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*, and the [function field](wikipedia://en/2783843) *K(C)* of a nonsingular integral [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) *C* defined over an [algebraically closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_closed) [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *K* of characteristic *p*.

The question addresses the existence of a [Galois extension](wikipedia://en/457064) *L* of *K*(*C*), with *G* as Galois group, and with specified [ramification](wikipedia://en/384644). From a geometric point of view, *L* corresponds to another curve *C*, together with a [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891)

π : *C* → *C*.

Geometrically, the assertion that π is ramified at a finite set *S* of points on *C* means that π restricted to the complement of *S* in *C* is an [étale morphism](wikipedia://en/3022771). This is in analogy with the case of [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181). In Abhyankar's conjecture, *S* is fixed, and the question is what *G* can be. This is therefore a special type of [inverse Galois problem](wikipedia://en/739166).

:::

::: {#o10 .statement cue=heading name="Results"}

## Results {#Results}

The subgroup *p*(*G*) is defined to be the subgroup generated by all the [Sylow subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) of *G* for the prime number *p*. This is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918), and the parameter *n* is defined as the minimum number of generators of

*G*/*p*(*G*).

Raynaud proved the case where *C* is the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578) over *K*, the conjecture stating that *G* can be realised as a Galois group of *L*, unramified outside *S* containing *s* + 1 points, if and only if

*n* ≤ *s*.

The general case was proved by Harbater. Where *g* is the [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) of *C*, *G* can be realised if and only if

*n* ≤ *s* + 2 *g*.
