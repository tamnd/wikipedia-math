# S-PLUS

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="S-PLUS"}

S+

**S-PLUS** was a commercial implementation of the [S programming language](wikipedia://en/919313) sold by [TIBCO Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIBCO_Software) Inc.

:::

It features object-oriented programming capabilities and advanced analytical algorithms. Its statistical analysis capabilities are commonly used by [econometricians](wikipedia://en/10390). The S-PLUS FinMetrics software package was developed for econometric time series analysis.[^o3]

Due to the increasing popularity of the open source S successor [R](wikipedia://en/376707), TIBCO Software released the [TIBCO Enterprise Runtime for R (TERR)](https://docs.tibco.com/products/tibco-enterprise-runtime-for-r) as an alternative R interpreter.[^o4] It is available on [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows) and [UNIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNIX) operating systems.[^o5]

## Historical timeline {#Historical_timeline}

*Image: S-Plus floppy disks.png.* S-PLUS floppy disk installation media

Source:[^o7]

In 1988, S-PLUS was first developed and released by a Seattle-based start-up company called Statistical Sciences, Inc.[^o8] The company's founder and sole owner was R. Douglas Martin, professor of statistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), Seattle. Martin originally learned S while working at Bell Laboratories, where the S language was originally developed.[^o3] At the time of its release, S-PLUS was one of the first examples of software that implemented algorithms for [generalized linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_linear_models), second to [GLIM](wikipedia://en/14770223).[^o9]

Statistical Sciences acquired the exclusive license to distribute S and merged with MathSoft in 1993, becoming the firm's Data Analysis Products Division (DAPD).[^o10]

In 1995, S-PLUS 3.3 for [Windows 95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_95)/NT was released, including the Matrix library, command history capability, and Trellis graphics. This was followed by the release of S-PLUS 3.4 for UNIX in 1996. This version included a [non-linear mixed-effects modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear_mixed-effects_modeling_software) library, hexagonal binning, and cluster methods. S-PLUS 4 was released for Windows in 1997, with features such as an updated GUI, integration with [Excel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Excel), and editable graphics. S-PLUS 4.5 for Windows in 1998, with [Scatterplot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scatterplot) brushing, and the ability to create S-PLUS graphs from within [Excel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Excel) & [SPSS](wikipedia://en/179088). The software also became available for Linux & [Solaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solaris_%28operating_system%29).

In 1999, S-PLUS 5 was released for Solaris, Linux, [HP-UX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP-UX), [AIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIX_%28operating_system%29), [IRIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRIX), and [DEC Alpha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEC_Alpha). S-PLUS 2000 for Windows. nlme 3.3, [quality control](wikipedia://en/41613) charting, and new commands for data manipulation. This was followed by S-PLUS 6 for [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux)/[Unix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix) in 2000. This version had a Java-based GUI, Graphlets, survival5, and a library for handling missing data.

In 2001, MathSoft sold its Cambridge-based Engineering and Education Products Division (EEPD), was renamed Insightful Corporation, and moved headquarters to Seattle. This essentially reversed the previous merger between MathSoft and Statistical Sciences, Inc.[^o11] S-PLUS Analytic Server 2.0. S-PLUS 6 was released for Windows in 2001.

In 2002, StatServer 6 was released and the student edition of S-PLUS became free. S-PLUS 6.2 was released and ported to AIX. In 2004, Insightful purchased the S language from [Lucent Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucent_Technologies) for \$2 million, and released S+ArrayAnalyzer 2.0.

S-PLUS 7.0 was released in 2005, including the BigData library for working with larger-than-memory data sets and the S-PLUS Workbench ([Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eclipse_%28software%29) development tool). Insightful Miner 7.0 was also released. In 2007, S-PLUS 8 was released with a new package system, language extensions for [R](wikipedia://en/376707) package compatibility, and a workbench debugger.

TIBCO acquired Insightful Corporation for \$25 million in 2008.[^o12]

On 31st January 2026, TIBCO officially retired the product.[^o13]
