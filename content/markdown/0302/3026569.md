# Octagonal prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Octagonal prism"}

Prism even polyhedron stat table

*Image: Octagonal prism.stl.* 3D model of a uniform octagonal prism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **octagonal prism** is a [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) comprising eight rectangular sides joining two regular [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575) caps.

:::

## Use {#Use}

In [optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optics), octagonal prisms are used to generate flicker-free images in [movie projectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movie_projector).

:::
