# K-homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="K-homology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **K-homology** is a [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) theory on the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact) [Hausdorff spaces](wikipedia://en/13637). It classifies the elliptic [pseudo-differential operators](wikipedia://en/973479) acting on the [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over a space. In terms of [$C^*$-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184), it classifies the [Fredholm modules](wikipedia://en/20986604) over an [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923).

:::

An **operator [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250)** between two Fredholm modules $(\mathcal{H},F_0,\Gamma)$ and $(\mathcal{H},F_1,\Gamma)$ is a [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) [path](wikipedia://en/1189553) of Fredholm modules, $t \mapsto (\mathcal{H},F_t,\Gamma)$, $t \in [0,1].$ Two Fredholm modules are then equivalent if they are related by [unitary transformations](wikipedia://en/236007) or operator homotopies. The $K^0(A)$ [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is the [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of [equivalence classes](wikipedia://en/9259) of even Fredholm modules over A. The $K^1(A)$ group is the abelian group of equivalence classes of odd Fredholm modules over A. Addition is given by [direct summation](wikipedia://en/58899) of Fredholm modules, and the [inverse](wikipedia://en/14907) of $(\mathcal{H}, F, \Gamma)$ is $(\mathcal{H}, -F, -\Gamma).$
