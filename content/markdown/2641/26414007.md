# Rosemary A. Bailey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rosemary A. Bailey"}

**Rosemary A. Bailey** (born 1947) is a British [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who works in the [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541) and the [analysis of variance](wikipedia://en/634) and in related areas of [combinatorial design](wikipedia://en/2761968), especially in [association schemes](wikipedia://en/2238152). She has written books on the [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541), on [association schemes](wikipedia://en/2238152), and on [linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_models) in statistics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bailey's first degree and D.Phil. were in mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). After her first degree, Bailey did a year of Voluntary Service Overseas, teaching Mathematics and French at a school in Ilorin, Nigeria.

She was awarded her doctorate in 1974 for a dissertation on [permutation groups](wikipedia://en/24634), *Finite Permutation Groups* supervised by [Graham Higman](wikipedia://en/2600152). Bailey's career has not been in pure mathematics but in statistics where she has specialised in the algebraic problems associated with the design of experiments.

Bailey worked at [The Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Open_University) before becoming a Post-Doctoral Fellow at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) with [Desmond Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._D._Patterson). She spent 1981–91 in the Statistics Department of [Rothamsted Experimental Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rothamsted_Experimental_Station). In 1991 Bailey became Professor of Mathematical Sciences at Goldsmiths College in the University of London and then Professor of Statistics at [Queen Mary, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary,_University_of_London) where she is Professor Emerita of Statistics. After 12 years as Professor of Mathematics and Statistics in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), Scotland, she is currently Professor Emeritus there.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bailey is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o6] and in 2015 was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o7]
