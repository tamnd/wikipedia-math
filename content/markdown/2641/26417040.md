# U-Prove

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="U-Prove"}

**U-Prove** is a [free and open-source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source)[^o3] technology and accompanying [software development kit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_development_kit)[^o4] for user-centric [identity management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_management). The underlying [cryptographic protocols](wikipedia://en/591994) were designed in 1993 by Dr. [Stefan Brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Brands)[^o5] and further developed by Credentica and, subsequently, [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft).[^o6] The technology was developed to allow internet users to disclose only the minimum amount of [personal data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_data) when making [electronic transactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_transaction) as a way to reduce the likelihood of [privacy violations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy_violation).[^o7]

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

U-Prove enables application developers to reconcile seemingly conflicting [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security) and [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy) objectives (including [anonymity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anonymity)), and allows for digital identity claims to be efficiently tied to the use of tamper-resistant devices such as [smart cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_card). Application areas of particular interest include cross-domain enterprise identity and access management, e-government SSO and data sharing, electronic health records, anonymous electronic voting, policy-based digital rights management, social networking data portability, and electronic payments.

In 2008, Microsoft committed to opening up the U-Prove technology.[^o3] As the first step, in March 2010 the company released a cryptographic specification and [open-source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) API implementation code for part of the U-Prove technology as a [Community Technology Preview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_Technology_Preview) under Microsoft's [Open Specification Promise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Specification_Promise).[^o9] Since then, several extensions have been released under the same terms and the technology has been tested in real-life applications.

In 2010, the [International Association of Privacy Professionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Privacy_Professionals) (IAPP) honored U-Prove with the *2010 Privacy Innovation Award for Technology*.[^o10][^o11]

Microsoft also won the *European Identity Award* in the *Best Innovation* category for U-Prove at the European Identity Conference 2010.[^o12]

The U-Prove Crypto SDK for [C#](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_Sharp_%28programming_language%29) is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apache_License_2.0) and the source code is available on [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub).[^o13]

Microsoft also provides a [JavaScript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JavaScript) SDK that implements the client-side of the U-Prove Cryptographic Specification.[^o14]
