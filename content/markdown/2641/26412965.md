# Wrapped Lévy distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wrapped Lévy distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [directional statistics](wikipedia://en/1444970), a **wrapped Lévy distribution** is a [wrapped probability distribution](wikipedia://en/25329528) that results from the "wrapping" of the [Lévy distribution](wikipedia://en/1346871) around the [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071).

:::

## Description {#Description}

The pdf of the wrapped [Lévy distribution](wikipedia://en/1346871) is

$$f_{WL}(\theta;\mu,c)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^\infty \sqrt{\frac{c}{2\pi}}\,\frac{e^{-c/2(\theta+2\pi n-\mu)}}{(\theta+2\pi n-\mu)^{3/2}}$$

where the value of the summand is taken to be zero when $\theta+2\pi n-\mu \le 0$, $c$ is the scale factor and $\mu$ is the location parameter. [Expressing](wikipedia://en/25329528) the above pdf in terms of the [characteristic function](wikipedia://en/3229401) of the Lévy distribution yields:

$$f_{WL}(\theta;\mu,c)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{n=-\infty}^\infty e^{-in(\theta-\mu)-\sqrt{c|n|}\,(1-i\operatorname{sgn}{n})}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\left(1 + 2\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-\sqrt{cn}}\cos\left(n(\theta-\mu) - \sqrt{cn}\,\right)\right)$$

In terms of the circular variable $z=e^{i\theta}$ the circular moments of the wrapped Lévy distribution are the characteristic function of the Lévy distribution evaluated at integer arguments:

$$\langle z^n\rangle=\int_\Gamma e^{in\theta}\,f_{WL}(\theta;\mu,c)\,d\theta = e^{i n \mu-\sqrt{c|n|}\,(1-i\operatorname{sgn}(n))}.$$

where $\Gamma\,$ is some interval of length $2\pi$. The first moment is then the expectation value of *z*, also known as the mean resultant, or mean resultant vector:

$$\langle z \rangle=e^{i\mu-\sqrt{c}(1-i)}$$

The mean angle is

$$\theta_\mu=\mathrm{Arg}\langle z \rangle = \mu+\sqrt{c}$$

and the length of the mean resultant is

$$R=|\langle z \rangle| = e^{-\sqrt{c}}$$
