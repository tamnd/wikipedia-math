# Nicole El Karoui

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nicole El Karoui"}

**Nicole El Karoui** (*[née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name)* [Schvartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_words_of_Yiddish_origin)) is a French mathematician and pioneer in the development of [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), born 29 May 1944 in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). She is considered one of the pioneers on the French school of [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680) and trained many engineers and scientists in this field. She is Professor Emeritus of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University), and held professorship positions at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and [Université du Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine_%28France%29). Her research has contributed to the application of probability and [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454) to modeling and [risk management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_management) in [financial markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_markets).

:::

## Teaching {#Teaching}

The reputation of Professor El Karoui's classes is such that [Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_Journal) opines that there may be *too many* of her students in important positions handling [financial derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_derivatives).[^o4] In an interview with the Wall Street Journal, [Rama Cont](wikipedia://en/58065470), [Professor of Mathematical Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Finance) at the [University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850), described a degree with Ms. El Karoui's name on it as "the magic word that opened doors for young people."[^o5]

El Karoui was the co-director, with [Marc Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor) and Gilles Pagès, of the [Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree_in_Europe#France) program in Probability & Finance,[^o6] jointly operated by [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris VI), which she co-founded with [Hélyette Geman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helyette_Geman).[^o7] This program, usually called "DEA El Karoui", is one of the most prestigious programs in quantitative finance in the world and No 1 in France.[^o8]

## Scientific contributions {#Scientific_contributions}

Nicole El Karoui's research is focused on probability theory, stochastic control theory and mathematical finance. Her contributions focused on the mathematical theory of [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216), backward stochastic differential equations and their application in [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

She is particularly known for her work on the robustness of the Black-Scholes hedging strategy, superhedging of contingent claims and the change of numéraire method for option pricing.[^o10]

Among El Karoui's contributions to mathematical finance is her elegant formula for expressing the covariance relationship between the futures price and the forward price of an asset. The El Karoui futures-forward covariance formula states

$$H(t) - G(t) = \int_{t}^{T} \mathrm{Cov}^{Q(s)} [r(s),H(s)|\mathcal{F}_t] \ ds,$$

where $H$ is the price of a futures contract, $G$ is the price of a forward contract, $r$ is the spot interest rate process and $Q(s)$ is the probability measure under which asset prices are martingales with the discount bond of maturity $s$ selected as numeraire.[^o11]
