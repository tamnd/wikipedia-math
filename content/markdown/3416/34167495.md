# Abhorchdienst

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abhorchdienst"}

The **Abhorchdienst** (i.e. "Listening Bureau") was a German [code-breaking](wikipedia://en/5715) bureau which operated during the final years of the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War). It was established in 1916 and was composed mainly of [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians).[^o2] Other countries, such as [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) and [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary), had set up similar organisations at an earlier stage. Still, the military context did not necessitate the development of the German bureau until 1916.[^o3]
