# Stuck unknot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stuck unknot"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **stuck unknot** is a closed [polygonal chain](wikipedia://en/12056032) in three-dimensional space (a [skew polygon](wikipedia://en/6545461)) that is topologically equal to the [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075) but cannot be deformed to a [simple polygon](wikipedia://en/1059530) when interpreted as a [mechanical linkage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linkage_%28mechanical%29), by rigid length-preserving and non-self-intersecting motions of its segments.[^o2][^o3] Similarly a **stuck open chain** is an open polygonal chain such that the segments may not be aligned by moving rigidly its segments. Topologically such a chain can be unknotted, but the limitation of using only rigid motions of the segments can create [nontrivial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nontrivial) knots in such a chain.

:::

Consideration of such "stuck" configurations arises in the study of [molecular chains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer) in [biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemistry).
