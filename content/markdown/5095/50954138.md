# Anatoli N. Andrianov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anatoli N. Andrianov"}

**Anatoli** **Nikolaievich Andrianov** (also spelled **Anatoly**; Анатолий Николаевич Андрианов; 21 July 1936 – 2 May 2020)[^o2] is a Russian mathematician.

:::

Andrianov received in 1962 his Ph.D. under [Yuri Linnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Linnik) at the [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) with thesis *Investigation of quadratic forms by methods of the theory of correspondences* and in 1969 his Russian doctorate of sciences ([Doctor Nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Nauk)).[^o4] He is a professor at the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg).

His research deals with the multiplicative arithmetic of quadratic forms, zeta functions of automorphic forms, modular forms in several variables (such as Siegel modular forms, [Hecke operators](wikipedia://en/521837), spherical functions, and [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745)).

Andrianov was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1970 in Nice with talk *On the zeta function of the general linear group* and in 1983 in Warsaw with talk *Integral representation of quadratic forms by quadratic forms: multiplicative properties*.[^o5] He held visiting positions at several academic institutions, including the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn, at the Institut Fourier in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble) and at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1974).
