# Pablo Ferrari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pablo Ferrari"}

**Pablo Augusto Ferrari** (September 11, 1949) is an Argentine mathematician, member of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society), the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences), and the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). He is also co-principal investigator at the Brazilian research center [NeuroMat](wikipedia://en/50145611).[^o3] Ferrari investigates probabilistic models of microscopic phenomena and macroscopic counterpart. He is the son of the contemporary conceptual artist [León Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/León_Ferrari).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pablo Ferrari was born in 1949 in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires).[^o5] He is the son of the contemporary conceptual artist [León Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/León_Ferrari) with Alicia Barros Castro and has two sisters: Marialí and Ariel.[^o6][^o7]

He got a degree in mathematics from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) (UBA) in 1974, PhD in Statistics from the [University of Sao Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sao_Paulo) (USP) in 1982, with the dissertation *Invariant measures for the marked simple exclusion process*, advised by Enrique Daniel Andjel.[^o8] He also has a postdoctoral from [Rutgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o5]

Ferrari was Professor at the USP from 1978 to 2008 and was the vice Dean of the [Institute of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics,_University_of_São_Paulo) twice, one from 1994 to 1998 and the other from 1998 to 2002.[^o5]

He was a visiting professor at Rutgers, Paris, Rome, Santiago de Chile and Cambridge. He is a UBA Professor and Researcher of CONICET from 2009 and a member of the Group Probability of Buenos Aires. He received a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1999, the [Consecration Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consecration_Award) of the Academy of Exact, Physical and Natural Sciences in Buenos Aires in 2011 and was named a fellow of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2013.[^o9][^o10] Also, he is a honored member of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics.[^o11]
