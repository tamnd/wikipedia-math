# Friedrich Otto Rudolf Sturm

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Otto Rudolf Sturm"}

**Friedrich Otto Rudolf Sturm** (6 January 1841 – 12 April 1919) was a German mathematician. His Ph.D. advisor was [Heinrich Eduard Schroeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Eduard_Schroeter), and [Otto Toeplitz](wikipedia://en/547682) was one of his Ph.D. students. His best ever proposal type claim is commonly known as "[Sturm's Theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sturm's_Theorem)" based on finding the complex imaginary roots of an infinite arbitrary-integer series.

:::
