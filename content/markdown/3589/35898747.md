# Renkonen similarity index

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Renkonen similarity index"}

The **Renkonen similarity index** (P), is a measure of dissimilarity between two [communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_%28ecology%29) (sites), based on relative (proportional) [abundances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abundance_%28ecology%29) $p_i = n_i/\sum{n_i}$ of individuals of composite [species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species). It was developed by the botanist [Olavi Renkonen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olavi_Renkonen) and published in 1938.[^o2]

:::

$$P = \sum{min(p_1;p_2)},$$

$p_1$ - percentage structure of one set,

$p_2$ - percentage structure of second set.

The [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264) of this [distance function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_function) ranges from 1 (identical proportional abundances) to 0 (no [taxa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxon) shared).
