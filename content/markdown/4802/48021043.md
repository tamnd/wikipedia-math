# Sara van de Geer

Van de Geer Van de Geer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sara van de Geer"}

**Sara Anna van de Geer** (born 7 May 1958 in [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden))[^o3] is a Dutch mathematical [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who is a professor in the department of mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o4] Her main research areas include empirical process theory, statistical learning theory, and nonparametric and high-dimensional statistics.[^o5] She is the daughter of psychologist [John P. van de Geer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_van_de_Geer).

:::

## Education {#Education}

She earned a [Master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in Mathematics in 1982 and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics in 1987, both from the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).[^o4] Her dissertation, entitled *Regression Analysis and Empirical Processes*, was supervised by [Willem Rutger van Zwet](wikipedia://en/64492113) and [Richard D. Gill](wikipedia://en/7716518).

## Career {#Career}

Van de Geer was a Scientific Researcher at the University of Tillburg from 1982–1983, followed by two stints as a Scientific Researcher at the [Centre for Mathematics and Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Mathematics_and_Computer_Science) in Amsterdam from 1983–1987 and 1988–1989.[^o4] She taught as an Assistant Professor at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) from 1987–1988, an Assistant Professor at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) from 1989–1990, an Assistant Professor at the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) from 1990–1997 and 1999–2005, and at [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University) in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse), France, as an Associate Professor from 1997–1999, before moving to [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in September 2005 as a Full Professor.[^o4][^o9] There, Van de Geer was the first female professor at the Department of Mathematics.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Van de Geer's research is centered around formulating unifying principles for statistical theory and modelling, while identifying theoretical foundations behind existing methods.[^o5] She typically studies over-parametrized models, but in 2002 she turned her focus to sparsity inducing regularization after deriving entropy-based bounds for regularized estimators.[^o5] Recently, Van de Geer's work has concentrated on regularization with total variation type norms, the behavior of stationary points of empirical risk functions, inference, and lower bounds.[^o5] She is currently deriving the generalization error for interpolators and on small noise classification problems.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010[^o13] and received the [Van Wijngaarden Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Wijngaarden_Award) in 2016.[^o14] She is a member of the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) and of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), a corresponding member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences), a Knight in the Order of Orange-Nassau, and a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o4][^o15][^o16][^o9][^o5] She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2020.[^o17] She was president of the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) for the term 2015–2017.[^o18] In 2018 she was appointed as a member of the Scientific Committee of the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach).[^o19][^o20] In 2022 she was elected as an international member to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) (NAS).[^o21]

In April 2011, she was a plenary speaker at the 47th Dutch Mathematical Congress in [Twente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twente), and a few months later in August 2011 she was a plenary speaker at the ISI satellite meeting "Dynamic Statistical Models" in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen).[^o4] In June 2013 she was a plenary speaker at the German-Polish Joint Conference on Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics and in July 2023 she was the opening lecturer at the 29th European Meeting of Statisticians.[^o4] In September 2013 Van de Geer was a plenary speaker at Statistische Woche in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and in May 2014 she was an invited speaker at the Abel Symposium 2014.[^o4]
