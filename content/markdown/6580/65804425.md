# Tom Brown (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tom Brown"}

**Thomas Craig Brown** (born 1938) is an American-Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), Ramsey Theorist, and [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University).[^o3]

:::

## Collaborations {#Collaborations}

As a mathematician, Brown’s primary focus in his research is in the field of [Ramsey Theory](wikipedia://en/157175). When completing his Ph.D., his thesis was 'On Semigroups which are Unions of Periodic Groups'[^o5] In 1963 as a graduate student, he showed that if the positive integers are finitely colored, then some color class is *piece-wise syndetic*.[^o6]

In *A Density Version of a Geometric Ramsey Theorem,*[^o7] he and [Joe P. Buhler](wikipedia://en/58419310) showed that “for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is an $n(\varepsilon)$ such that if $n = dim(V) \geq n(\varepsilon)$ then any subset of $V$ with more than $\varepsilon|V|$ elements must contain 3 collinear points” where $V$ is an $n$-dimensional [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834) over the field with $p^d$ elements, and $p \neq 2$".

In *Descriptions of the characteristic sequence of an irrational*,[^o8] Brown discusses the following idea: Let $\alpha$ be a positive irrational real number. The characteristic sequence of $\alpha$ is $f(\alpha) = f_1 f_2 \ldots$; where $f_n = [ ( n+1 )\alpha] [\alpha]$.” From here he discusses “the various descriptions of the characteristic sequence of α which have appeared in the literature” and refines this description to “obtain a very simple derivation of an arithmetic expression for $[n\alpha]$.” He then gives some conclusions regarding the conditions for $[n\alpha]$ which are equivalent to $f_n = 1$.

He has collaborated with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), including *Quasi-Progressions and Descending Waves*[^o9] and *Quantitative Forms of a Theorem of Hilbert*.[^o10]
