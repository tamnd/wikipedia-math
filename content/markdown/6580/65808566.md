# Erwin Bolthausen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erwin Bolthausen"}

**Erwin Bolthausen** (born 15 October 1945 in [Rohr, Aargau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohr,_Aargau)) is a Swiss mathematician, specializing in probability theory, statistics, and stochastic models in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bolthausen received his doctorate in mathematics under [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365) in 1973 from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). Bolthausen's thesis was entitled *Einfache Isomorphietypen in lokalisierten Kategorien und einfache Homotopietypen von Polyeder* (Simple isomorphic types in localized categories and simple homotopy types of polyhedra). In 1978 he completed his habilitation at the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) and was then an associate professor of mathematics at the [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt) for the academic year 1978–1979. From 1979 to 1990 he was a full professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). Since 1990 he is a full professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich),[^o5] where he headed the *Institut für Mathematik* from 1998 to 2001.[^o6]

In the early years of his career Bolthausen did research on [martingale](wikipedia://en/274536) convergence theorems, combinatorial limit theorems, and the [large deviations theory](wikipedia://en/3772909). Later in his career he dealt with stochastic models in mathematical physics, such as wandering in random media, phenomena related to random interfaces ([entropic repulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_force), [wetting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wetting) phenomena), [spin glasses](wikipedia://en/148555), and [polymers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer) in random media.

Bolthausen has been a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) since 2007.\<ref name-Leopoldina[^o6] From 1995 to 2000 he was a member of the council of the [Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach). He is since 2002 a member of the scientific advisory board of the [*École d'Eté de Probabilités de Saint-Flour*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Flour,_Cantal#École_d'Eté_de_Probabilités) and since 1994 a member of the Board of Trustees of the [Swiss National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Science_Foundation).[^o5]

He was an associate editor from 1987 to 1989 for the *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)* and from 1988 to 1993 for the *[Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748)*. For the journal *[Probability Theory and Related Fields](wikipedia://en/30852593)* he was editor-in-chief from 1994 to 2000 and is an associate editor since 2000.[^o5]

Bolthausen was in 2002 in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) an invited speaker with talk *Localization-delocalization phenomena for random interfaces*[^o7] at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) and in 1996 in Budapest with talk *Large deviations and perturbations of random walks and random surfaces*[^o8] at the [European Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Congress_of_Mathematicians).
