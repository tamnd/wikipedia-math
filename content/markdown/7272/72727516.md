# Perdita Stevens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Perdita Stevens"}

**Perdita Emma Stevens** (born 1966) is a British mathematician, [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392), and [software engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering) who holds a personal chair in the mathematics of software engineering as part of the School of Informatics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh). Her research includes work on [model-driven engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model-driven_engineering), including [model transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_transformation), [model checking](wikipedia://en/321157), and the [Unified Modeling Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Modeling_Language).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Stevens read mathematics at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), earning a bachelor's degree in 1987. She went to the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) for graduate study in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), earning a master's degree in 1988 and completing a PhD in 1992. Her doctoral dissertation, *Integral Forms for Weyl Modules of $\mathrm{GL}(2,\mathrm{Q})$*, was supervised by [Sandy Green](wikipedia://en/10670093).

After working in industry as a software engineer, Stevens joined the Department of Computer Science at the University of Edinburgh in 1984. She became a reader there in 2003 and in 2014 was given a personal chair as Professor of Mathematics of Software Engineering.

## Books {#Books}

Stevens is the author of books including:

- *Using UML: Software Engineering with Objects and Components* (with Rob Pooley, Addison-Wesley, 1999; 2nd ed., 2006)
- *How to Write Good Programs: A Guide for Students* (Cambridge University Press, 2020)
