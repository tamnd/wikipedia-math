# Quincunx matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quincunx matrix"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859)

:::

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

is sometimes called the **quincunx matrix**. It is a 2×2 [Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/507553), and its rows form the basis of a diagonal [square lattice](wikipedia://en/2874853) consisting of the integer points whose coordinates both have the same [parity](wikipedia://en/143135); this lattice is a two-dimensional analogue of the three-dimensional [body-centered cubic lattice](wikipedia://en/666697).[^o2]
