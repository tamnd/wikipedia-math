# Donald Richards (statistician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Donald Richards"}

**Donald St. P. Richards** (born 1955, in [Mandeville, Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeville,_Jamaica)) is an American statistician conducting research on [multivariate statistics](wikipedia://en/19384), [zonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/8910528), [distance correlation](wikipedia://en/28217722), total positivity, and hypergeometric functions of matrix argument. He is a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor)[^o3] emeritus of statistics at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University),[^o4] and is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o5] and a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

:::

Richards obtained his PhD in 1978 at the [University of the West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_West_Indies), where the statistician [Rameshwar D. Gupta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rameshwar_D._Gupta) was his doctoral advisor. In 1999, he was elected a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o8] In 2012, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Richards became an American citizen in 1990. He was married to [Mercedes Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_Richards), an American Jamaican-born professor of astronomy and astrophysics, until her death in 2016.[^o11]
