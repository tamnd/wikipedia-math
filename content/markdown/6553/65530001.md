# 1864 Costa Rican census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1864 Costa Rican census"}

1864 The [Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica) 1864 Census was the first official census elaborated in the country by the Dirección General de Estadística y Censos, predecessor of current [National Institute of Statistics and Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Census_of_Costa_Rica). The total population was at the moment 120499.

:::
