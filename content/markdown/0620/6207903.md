# Thomas Jech

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Jech"}

**Thomas J. Jech** (Tomáš Jech, ; born 29 January 1944 in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague)) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) who was at [Penn State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State) for more than 25 years.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was educated at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) (his advisor was [Petr Vopěnka](wikipedia://en/3105466)) and from 2000 is at the [Institute of Mathematics](http://www.math.cas.cz/) of the [Academy of Sciences of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Czech_Republic).

## Work {#Work}

Jech's research also includes [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory).

Jech gave the first published proof of the consistency of the existence of a [Suslin line](wikipedia://en/228668). With [Karel Prikry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karel_Prikry), he introduced the notion of [precipitous ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitous_ideal).[^o4] He gave several models where the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840) failed, for example one with ω<sub>1</sub> [measurable](wikipedia://en/248102). The concept of a [Jech–Kunen tree](wikipedia://en/43031336) is named after him and [Kenneth Kunen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Kunen).
