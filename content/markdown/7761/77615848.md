# Michela Procesi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michela Procesi"}

**Michela Procesi** (born 1973) is an Italian mathematician specializing in Hamiltonian [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) such as the [nonlinear Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/1422748) or [wave equation](wikipedia://en/33691). The [Degasperis–Procesi equation](wikipedia://en/15054768) is named for her. She is a professor of mathematics at [Roma Tre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Tre_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Procesi was born in 1973 in Rome, the daughter of mathematician [Claudio Procesi](wikipedia://en/35219117). She earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in physics at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1998, and continued at la Sapienza for a PhD in mathematics in 2002. Her dissertation, *Estimates on Hamiltonian splittings: tree techniques in the theory of homoclinic splitting and Arnold diffusion for a-priori stable systems*, was supervised by [Luigi Chierchia](wikipedia://en/62545142).

She became a postdoctoral researcher at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste) and, with the support of the [Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/34249626), at Roma Tre University. After continued work as a researcher at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II) and la Sapienza, she obtained a position as an associate professor at Roma Tre University in 2015. She has been a full professor there since 2019.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Procesi was an invited speaker at the 2022 (virtual) [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).
