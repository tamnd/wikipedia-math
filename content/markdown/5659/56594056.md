# Lawrence Ein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence Ein"}

*Image: Lawrence Ein.jpg.* Lawrence Ein

**Lawrence Man Hou Ein** (born 18 November 1955) is a mathematician who works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lawrence Ein received in 1976 his bachelor's degree from [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles)[^o3] and in 1981 his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the direction of [Robin Hartshorne](wikipedia://en/5693379) with thesis *Stable vector bundles on projective spaces in* char *p > 0* (which was published in *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)*).[^o5][^o6]

For the academic year 1981–1982 he was an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). From 1982 to 1984 he was a [C.L.E. Moore Instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_Instructor) at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). He became from 1984 to 1987 an assistant professor, from 1987 to 1989 an associate professor, and from 1989 to the present a full professor at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago). He has been a visiting professor at the [University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hong_Kong), UCLA, the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), the [University of Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nancy), and the [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in Berkeley.[^o3]

Ein has served on the editorial boards of the Transactions and Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society from 1995 to 2003, [Communications in Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications_in_Algebra) from 1995 to the present, [Geometriae Dedicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometriae_Dedicata) from 2004 to 2013, and [Serdica Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serdica_Mathematical_Journal) from 2004 to the present.[^o3]

Ein was a Sloan Fellow from 1986 to 1989. He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006 in Madrid. He was elected a Fellow of the American Mathematical Society in 2012.[^o3]
