# Szekeres snark

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Szekeres snark"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Szekeres snark** is a [snark](wikipedia://en/727811) with 50 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 75 edges. It was the fifth known snark, discovered by [George Szekeres](wikipedia://en/2563194) in 1973.[^o4]

:::

As a snark, the Szekeres graph is a connected, bridgeless [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) with [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) equal to 4. The Szekeres snark is [non-planar](wikipedia://en/24314) but is [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o5] It is [non-hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) but is [hypohamiltonian](wikipedia://en/18301304). It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o7]

Another well known snark on 50 vertices is the [Watkins snark](wikipedia://en/24064939) discovered by John J. Watkins in 1989.[^o8]
