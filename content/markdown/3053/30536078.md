# Triangular matrix ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Triangular matrix ring"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **triangular matrix ring**, also called a **triangular ring**, is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) constructed from two rings and a [bimodule](wikipedia://en/492612).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If $T$ and $U$ are rings and $M$ is a $\left(U,T\right)$-bimodule, then the triangular matrix ring $R:=\left[\begin{array}{cc}T&0\\M&U\\\end{array}\right]$ consists of 2-by-2 [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) of the form $\left[\begin{array}{cc}t&0\\m&u\\\end{array}\right]$, where $t\in T,m\in M,$ and $u\in U,$ with ordinary [matrix addition](wikipedia://en/125276) and [matrix multiplication](wikipedia://en/125280) as its [operations](wikipedia://en/3948).

:::
