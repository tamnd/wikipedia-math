# Akhiezer's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Akhiezer's theorem"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), **Akhiezer's theorem** is a result about [entire functions](wikipedia://en/10372) proved by [Naum Akhiezer](wikipedia://en/9119648).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $f:\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ be an [entire function](wikipedia://en/10372) of [exponential type](wikipedia://en/4081509) $\tau$, with $f(x)\geq 0$ for real $x$. Then the following are equivalent:

- There exists an [entire function](wikipedia://en/10372) $F$, of [exponential type](wikipedia://en/4081509) $\tau/2$, having all its zeros in the (closed) upper half plane, such that

$$f(z)=F(z)\overline{F(\overline{z})}$$

- One has:

$$\sum_n |\operatorname{Im}(1/z_{n})|<\infty$$

where $z_n$ are the zeros of $f$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Related results"}

## Related results {#Related_results}

It is not hard to show that the [Fejér–Riesz theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fejér–Riesz_theorem) is a special case.[^o7]

:::
