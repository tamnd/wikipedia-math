# Path space (algebraic topology)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Path space"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a branch of mathematics, the **based path space** $PX$ of a [pointed space](wikipedia://en/1236666) $(X, *)$ is the space that consists of all maps $f$ from the interval $I = [0, 1]$ to *X* such that $f(0) = *$, called based [paths](wikipedia://en/1189553).[^o2] In other words, it is the [mapping space](wikipedia://en/78365422) from $(I, 0)$ to $(X, *)$.

:::

A space $X^I$ of all maps from $I$ to *X*, with no distinguished point for the start of the paths, is called the **free path space** of *X.*[^o3] The maps from $I$ to *X* are called free paths. The path space $PX$ is then the pullback of $X^I \to X, \, \chi \mapsto \chi(0)$ along $* \hookrightarrow X$.[^o2]

The natural map $PX \to X, \, \chi \to \chi(1)$ is a fibration called the [path space fibration](wikipedia://en/48842115).[^o4]
