# Goodman's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goodman's conjecture"}

**Goodman's conjecture on the coefficients of [multivalent functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivalent_function)** was proposed in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) in 1948 by [Adolph Winkler Goodman](wikipedia://en/37474095), an American mathematician.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formulation"}

## Formulation {#Formulation}

Let $f(z)= \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}{b_n z^n}$ be a $p$-valent function. The conjecture claims the following coefficients hold:

$$|b_n| \le \sum_{k=1}^{p} \frac{2k(n+p)!}{(p-k)!(p+k)!(n-p-1)!(n^2-k^2)}|b_k|$$

:::

## Partial results {#Partial_results}

It's known that when $p=2,3$, the conjecture is true for functions of the form $P \circ \phi$ where $P$ is a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) and $\phi$ is [univalent](wikipedia://en/1534382).
