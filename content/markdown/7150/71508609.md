# Category of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Category of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces"}

In mathematics, the **[category](wikipedia://en/53759) of [compactly generated](wikipedia://en/899382) [weak Hausdorff spaces](wikipedia://en/19500167),** **CGWH,** is a category used in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) as an alternative to the [category of topological spaces](wikipedia://en/435051), **Top**, as the latter lacks some properties that are common in practice and often convenient to use in proofs. There is also such a category for the **CGWH** analog of [pointed topological spaces](wikipedia://en/1236666), defined by requiring maps to preserve base points.[^o2]

:::

The articles [compactly generated space](wikipedia://en/899382) and [weak Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/19500167) define the respective topological properties. For the historical motivation behind these conditions on spaces, see [Compactly generated space#Motivation](wikipedia://en/899382#Motivation). This article focuses on the properties of the category.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

**CGWH** has the following properties:

- It is [complete](wikipedia://en/62781)[^o5] and cocomplete.[^o6]
- The [forgetful functor](wikipedia://en/844783) to the sets preserves small limits.[^o5]
- It contains all the [locally compact Hausdorff spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact_Hausdorff_space)[^o7] and all the [CW complexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CW_complexes).[^o8]
- An [internal Hom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_Hom) exists for any pairs of spaces *X* and *Y*;[^o9][^o10] it is denoted by $\operatorname{Map}(X, Y)$ or $Y^X$ and is called the (free) **[mapping space](wikipedia://en/78365422)** from *X* to *Y*. Moreover, there is a homeomorphism

$$\operatorname{Map}(X \times Y, Z) \simeq \operatorname{Map}(X, \operatorname{Map}(Y, Z))$$

that is natural in *X*, *Y*, and *Z*.[^o11] In short, the category is [Cartesian closed](wikipedia://en/237037) in an [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) sense.

- A finite product of CW complexes is a CW complex.[^o12]
- If $(X, *)$ and $(Y, \circ)$ are pointed spaces, then the [smash product](wikipedia://en/698010) of them exists.[^o13] The (based) **mapping space** $\operatorname{Map}( (X, *), (Y, \circ))$ from $(X, *)$ to $(Y, \circ)$ consists of all base-point-preserving maps from $(X, *)$ to $(Y, \circ)$ and is a closed subspace of the mapping space between the underlying spaces without base points.[^o14] It is a based space with the base point the unique constant map. Moreover, for based spaces $(X, *)$, $(Y, \circ)$, and $(Z, \star)$, there is a homeomorphism

$$\operatorname{Map}((X, *) \wedge (Y, \circ), (Z, \star)) \simeq \operatorname{Map}((X, *), \operatorname{Map}((Y, \circ), (Z, \star)))$$

that is natural in $(X, *)$, $(Y, \circ)$, and $(Z, \star)$.[^o15]
