# HP 33s

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="HP 33s"}

The **HP 33s** (F2216A) was a [scientific calculator](wikipedia://en/439711) marketed by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard). It was introduced in 2003 as the successor to the [HP&nbsp;32SII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP&nbsp;32SII),[^o7] and discontinued on the introduction of its successor, the [HP&nbsp;35s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP&nbsp;35s) in 2007.

:::

## Features {#Features}

Its main features are:

- [RPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_Polish_Notation) or traditional semi-algebraic (infix data/algebraic operator entry/postfix one-number function entry), user-selectable
- Two-line LCD
- Full scientific/engineering mathematical features
- [Keystroke-programmable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keystroke_programming) with full [boolean](wikipedia://en/54476844) and program-control command sets and line edit, insert and delete
- HP "equation list" equation editor (fully algebraic) in both the stand-alone list as well as in keystroke programs
- HP Solver feature (solves equations and functions for one unknown)
- Function [integration](wikipedia://en/15532) feature
- All mathematical operations and features fully functional in all modes
- Unit conversion and constants
- 31 [KB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilobyte) of [random-access user memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random-access_memory) (equivalent to about 7 KB on earlier HP programmable models)

The main differences from the HP 32SII are:

- A radical difference in keypad layout and appearance
- Memory is expanded from 2 KB to 32 KB
- Faster processor
- More functions
- Algebraic entry mode as well as RPN
- The display has two lines
- The length of an equation was now restricted to 255 characters (no arbitrary limit in the 32SII)[^o7]

The HP 33s was co-designed and manufactured by [Kinpo Electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinpo_Electronics) of [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan).[^o4]

## Reception {#Reception}

The HP 33s is generally considered to have fewer logic [bugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_bug) than the [HP&nbsp;35s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP&nbsp;35s). However, the unconventional [chevron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevron_%28insignia%29)-styled keypad has been regarded by reviewers as bizarre and difficult to use compared to other HP professional calculators.[^o6][^o7]

## Revisions {#Revisions}

The 33s went through numerous revisions that have solved two of the most pervasive issues people had with the early models (namely poor screen quality and bad keypad responsiveness).
