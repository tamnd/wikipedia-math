# Henri Padé

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Padé"}

**Henri Eugène Padé** (; 17 December 1863 – 9 July 1953) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who is now remembered mainly for his development of [Padé approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padé_approximation) techniques for functions using [rational functions](wikipedia://en/361210).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Padé studied at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris. He then spent a year at [Leipzig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University) and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where he studied under [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) and [Hermann Schwarz](wikipedia://en/3165834).

In 1890 Padé returned to France, where he taught in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille) while preparing his doctorate under [Charles Hermite](wikipedia://en/22870422). His doctoral thesis described what is now known as the [Padé approximant](wikipedia://en/3090820). He then became an assistant professor at [Université Lille Nord de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Lille_Nord_de_France), where he succeeded [Émile Borel](wikipedia://en/356161) as a professor of [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) at [École Centrale de Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale_de_Lille).

Padé taught at [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille) until 1902, when he moved to [Université de Poitiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Poitiers). He became [recteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of Académie de Besançon and Dijon in 1923. He later became Recteur of Académie de Aix-Marseilles until he retired in 1934.
