# GDES

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="GDES"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), the **Generalized DES Scheme** (**GDES** or **G-DES**) is a variant of the [DES](wikipedia://en/7978) [symmetric-key](wikipedia://en/53042) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed with the intention of speeding up the [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) process while improving its security. The scheme was proposed by Ingrid Schaumuller-Bichl in 1981.

:::

In 1990, [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920) and [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314) showed that GDES was vulnerable to [differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536), and that any GDES variant faster than DES is also less secure than DES.

GDES generalizes the [Feistel network](wikipedia://en/352709) structure of DES to larger [block sizes](wikipedia://en/294065). In each round, the DES round function is applied to the rightmost 32-bit subblock, and the result is [XORed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XOR) with all the other parts. Then the block is rotated 32 bits to the right.
