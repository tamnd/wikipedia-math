# Preda Mihăilescu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Preda Mihăilescu"}

**Preda V. Mihăilescu** () (born 23 May 1955) is a Romanian mathematician, best known for his proof of the [Catalan's conjecture](wikipedia://en/62382), first proposed in 1844.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest),[^o4] he is the brother of [Vintilă Mihăilescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vintilă_Mihăilescu).

After leaving Romania in 1973, he settled in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland).[^o4] He studied mathematics and computer science in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich), receiving a PhD from [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) in 1997.[^o5] His PhD thesis, titled *Cyclotomy of rings and primality testing*,[^o6] was written under the direction of [Erwin Engeler](wikipedia://en/12279301) and [Hendrik Lenstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lenstra).

For several years, he did research at the [University of Paderborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paderborn), Germany. Since 2005, he has held a professorship at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

## Major research {#Major_research}

In 2002, Mihăilescu proved [Catalan's conjecture](wikipedia://en/62382).[^o8][^o9][^o10] This number-theoretical conjecture, formulated by the French and Belgian mathematician [Eugène Charles Catalan](wikipedia://en/639553) in 1844, had stood unresolved for 158 years. Mihăilescu's proof appeared in *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)* in 2004.
