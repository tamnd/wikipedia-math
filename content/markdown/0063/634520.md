# Thiele

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thiele"}

**Thiele** is a German-language [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname).

:::

## Geographical distribution {#Geographical_distribution}

As of 2014, 78.0% of all known bearers of the surname *Thiele* were residents of [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), 10.9% of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), 2.3% of [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia), 2.0% of [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), 1.0% of [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) and 1.0% of [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa).

In Germany, the frequency of the surname was higher than national average (1:1,741) in the following states:[^o3]

1. [Brandenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg) (1:477)
1. [Saxony-Anhalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxony-Anhalt) (1:522)
1. [Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxony) (1:624)
1. [Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremen_%28state%29) (1:1,058)
1. [Thuringia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thuringia) (1:1,132)
1. [Lower Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Saxony) (1:1,241)
1. [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg) (1:1,465)
1. [Mecklenburg-Vorpommern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecklenburg-Vorpommern) (1:1,689)
1. [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) (1:1,708)
