# Axel Thue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Axel Thue"}

**Axel Thue** (; 19 February 1863 – 7 March 1922) was a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his original work in [diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Thue published his first important paper in 1909.

He stated in 1914 the so-called [word problem for semigroups](wikipedia://en/2452154#The_word_problem_for_semi-Thue_systems) or Thue problem, closely related to the [halting problem](wikipedia://en/21391870).[^o5]

His only known PhD student was [Thoralf Skolem](wikipedia://en/453765).

The esoteric programming language Thue is named after him.
