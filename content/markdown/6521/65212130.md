# Hyung Ju Park

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyung Ju Park"}

**Hyung Ju Park** (; born 1964) is a mathematician and former president of [Ajou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajou_University) in South Korea.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Born in Buyeo, South Korea, Park studied physics at [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) and received his undergraduate degree in 1986. He then received a Ph.D. degree in mathematics at University of California, Berkeley, USA in 1995.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

From 1995 to 2004, Park was with the Department of Mathematics at [Oakland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_University), California, USA as a professor. From 2004 to 2009, he was professor at School of Computational Sciences, [Korean Institute for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Institute_for_Advanced_Study). Park was a member of the executive committee of the [Korean Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Mathematical_Society) from 2005 to 2006. From 2009 to 2015, he was professor at School of Mathematics, [POSTECH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSTECH).[^o7] He was President of the [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326) from 2015 to 2017.[^o8] From 2015 to 2018, he was a chair professor at Ajou University. In 2018, he became the 16th president of Ajou University.[^o9]
