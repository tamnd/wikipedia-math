# The Crest of the Peacock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The Crest of the Peacock"}

***The Crest of the Peacock: Non-European Roots of Mathematics***is a book authored by [George Gheverghese Joseph](wikipedia://en/62174276), and was first published by [Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press) in 1991. The book was brought out as a response to view of the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) epitomized by [Morris Kline](wikipedia://en/826363)'s statement that, comparing to what the Greeks achieved, "the mathematics of Egyptians and Babylonians is the scrawling of children just learning to write, as opposed to great literature",[^o3] criticised by Joseph as "[Eurocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocentrism)".[^o4] The third edition of the book was released in 2011.[^o5]

:::

The book is divided into 11 chapters. Chapter 1 provides a lengthy justification for the book. Chapter 2 is devoted to a discussion of the mathematics of Native Americans and Chapter 3 to the mathematics of ancient Egyptians. The next two chapters consider the mathematics of Mesopotamia, then there are two chapters on Chinese mathematics, three chapters on Indian mathematics, and the final chapter discusses Islamic mathematics.

## Plagiarism {#Plagiarism}

[C. K. Raju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._K._Raju) accused Joseph and Dennis Almeida of plagiarism [^o7] of his decade long scholastic work [^o8] that began in 1998 for the [Project of History of Indian Science, Philosophy and Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_of_History_of_Indian_Science,_Philosophy_and_Culture) funded by the Indian Academy of Sciences concerning Indian mathematics and its possible knowledge transfer. An ethics investigation of the research team of Joseph and Almeida led University of Exeter to issue Almeida a formal written warning [^o9]. Later, the University of Manchester posted an erratum and acknowledgement of Raju's work.[^o10]

Joseph denies the charges.[^o11][^o12]
