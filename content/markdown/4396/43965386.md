# Khairulla Murtazin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Khairulla Murtazin"}

**Murtazin Khairulla Khabibullovich** (Муртазин Хайрулла Хабибуллович; 4 January 1941 – 17 November 2016) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Since 1978 he has been the Head of the Chair of Mathematical analysis [Bashkir State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkir_State_University).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Murtazin was born in the village Aznash in [Uchalinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uchalinsky_District), now in [Bashkortostan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkortostan). He graduated from the Department of Mathematics of [Bashkir State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkir_State_University) and defended his doctoral thesis in 1994. Since 1978 until the present day he is the head of the [Mathematical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Analysis) chair of the department[^o7]

Scientific activity is devoted to problems of quantum mechanics.

Murtazin investigated the asymptotic behavior of the discrete spectrum of the [Schrödinger operator](wikipedia://en/59874), the spectrum of perturbations of partial differential operators, results on the two-particle operators in the class of integrable potentials, conditions for the existence of virtual particles 4. The results of the studies were used in the work on [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), nuclear physics and acoustics, for geological and seismic work research and design institute of well logging VNIIGIS ([Oktyabrsky, Republic of Bashkortostan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oktyabrsky,_Republic_of_Bashkortostan)).

Author of more than 70 scientific papers.[^o8]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 2003 – Honored worker of science of the Republic of Bashkortostan[^o8]
- 2008 – Honorary Worker of Higher specialized Education of the Russian Federation[^o8]
- 2011 – laureate of the State Prize of the Republic of Bashkortostan in the field of science and technology[^o8]
