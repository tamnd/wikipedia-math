# Georges Matheron Lectureship

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Matheron Lectureship"}

The **Georges Matheron Lecture Series**[^o3] is sponsored by the [International Association for Mathematical Geosciences](wikipedia://en/43927898) (IAMG) to honor the legacy of the French [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) [Georges François Paul Marie Matheron](wikipedia://en/4415402), known as the founder of [geostatistics](wikipedia://en/51531) and a co-founder (together with [Jean Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Serra)) of [mathematical morphology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_morphology).[^o4][^o5] The Georges Matheron Lecture was established in 2005 and is given by a scientist with proven research ability in the field of [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349) or [mathematical morphology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_morphology).[^o6] It is presented annually if an eligible and worthy nominee is found. The first recipient of the award was [Jean Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Serra), for a long time a scientists with the [Centre of Mathematical Morphology, Fontainebleau](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803222024/http://cmm.ensmp.fr/index_eng.html). Serra delivered the first lecture at the IAMG conference in Liège, [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) in 2006.[^o7] The IAMG Lectures Committee seeks nominations and makes the selection.[^o8]

:::
