# Siegel's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698) and [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558), **Siegel's lemma** refers to bounds on the solutions of [linear equations](wikipedia://en/17570) obtained by the construction of [auxiliary functions](wikipedia://en/11826062). The existence of these [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) was proven by [Axel Thue](wikipedia://en/635533);[^o2] Thue's proof used what would be translated from German as Dirichlet's Drawers principle, which is widely known as the [Pigeonhole principle](wikipedia://en/54217). [Carl Ludwig Siegel](wikipedia://en/716602) published his lemma in 1929.[^o3] It is a pure [existence theorem](wikipedia://en/251177) for a [system of linear equations](wikipedia://en/113087).

:::

Siegel's lemma has been refined in recent years to produce sharper bounds on the estimates given by the lemma.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose we are given a system of *M* linear equations in *N* unknowns such that *N* > *M*, say

$$a_{11} X_1 + \cdots+ a_{1N} X_N = 0$$

$$\cdots$$

$$a_{M1} X_1 +\cdots+ a_{MN} X_N = 0$$

where the [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) are integers, not all 0, and bounded by *B*. The system then has a solution

$$(X_1, X_2, \dots, X_N)$$

with the *X*s all integers, not all 0, and bounded by

$(NB)^{M/(N-M)}.$[^o7]

(Bombieri Vaaler 1983) gave the following sharper bound for the *X**s:***

$$\max|X_j| \,\le \left(D^{-1}\sqrt{\det(AA^T)}\right)^{\!1/(N-M)}$$

where *D* is the [greatest common divisor](wikipedia://en/12354) of the *M* × *M* [minors](wikipedia://en/262107) of the [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) *A*, and *A*<sup>*T*</sup> is its [transpose](wikipedia://en/173844). Their proof involved replacing the [pigeonhole principle](wikipedia://en/54217) by techniques from the [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153).

:::
