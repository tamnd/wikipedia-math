# Nijaz Ibrulj

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nijaz Ibrulj"}

**Nijaz Ibrulj** (born 2 July 1956) is a [Bosnian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnians) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and a professor at the [University of Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sarajevo)'s Department of Philosophy and Sociology. He lectures on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211), methodology of social sciences, theory of knowledge, and [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science).[^o3][^o4] His interests also extend to the field of social ontology. Ibrulj was awarded a [Fulbright Visiting Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) during the 2000-2001 academic years to visit the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His application was sponsored by [John Searle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Searle) and [Donald Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Davidson_%28philosopher%29).[^o5]

:::

## Academic activities {#Academic_activities}

Ibrulj is the founder and president of the [Academia Analitica](wikipedia://en/26624799), a [scientific society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_society) for the development of logic and analytic philosophy in Bosnia and Herzegovina and leader of the "ZINK", a scientific and research incubator.[^o7][^o8] He is the founding editor of *SOPHOS*, a young researchers’ journal and of *The Logical Foresight*, a journal for logic and science.

## Work in philosophy {#Work_in_philosophy}

Ibrulj has written extensively on various topics of analytic philosophy, [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939), and philosophy of language, cognitive science and social ontology.

In his book *Philosophy of Logic (1999)*, he introduced a theory named the Principle of the Logical. He defines the Principle of the Logical as an ideal matrix of the logical principles or [laws of thought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laws_of_thought) (the [law of identity](wikipedia://en/1403190), the [law of non-contradiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_non-contradiction), the [law of excluded middle](wikipedia://en/17637), the law of sufficient reason).[^o10]

In the book *The Century of Rearrangement* (2005), Ibrulj investigated the concepts of relation between identity and knowledge in an ambient of intelligent space, which is designed by modern informational and communicational technology, nanoscience and [nanotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanotechnology), and the processes of [globalisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Globalization). He made a distinction between two theories of identity: a strong theory of identity (“anchored identity”) and a weak theory of identity (“mobile identity” or “identity in action”).[^o11][^o12]

Ibrulj has also translated from [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) (Donald Davidson), [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) (Gottlob Frege), and [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) (Bosnian–Greek edition of [Porphyry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porphyry_%28geology%29)'s [Isagoge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isagoge)).[^o13]
