# Academia Analitica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Academia Analitica"}

**Academia Analitica** is a [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) for the development of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211) based in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina). It was founded in 2007 at the Faculty of Philosophy of the [University of Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sarajevo). The society's founder and president is [Nijaz Ibrulj](wikipedia://en/26625068) (University of Sarajevo). The members of Academia Analitica take interest in analytic philosophy and research in the fields of logic, [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and other analytic and deductive sciences.[^o3]

:::

The society also takes part in several long-term projects, such as creating a [chrestomathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrestomathy) of texts dealing with [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939) and establishing a journal called *The Logical Foresight*.[^o4]
