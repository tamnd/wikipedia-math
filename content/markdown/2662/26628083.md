# Omega-categorical theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Omega-categorical theory"}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), an **omega-categorical theory** is a [theory](wikipedia://en/3512103) that has exactly one [countably infinite](wikipedia://en/6026) [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28logic%29) [up to](wikipedia://en/44787) [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828). Omega-categoricity is the special case κ = $\aleph_0$ = ω of [κ-categoricity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morley's_categoricity_theorem), and omega-categorical theories are also referred to as **ω-categorical**. The notion is most important for countable [first-order](wikipedia://en/10983) theories.

:::

## Equivalent conditions for omega-categoricity {#Equivalent_conditions_for_omega-categoricity}

Many conditions on a theory are equivalent to the property of omega-categoricity. In 1959 [Erwin Engeler](wikipedia://en/12279301), [Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski](wikipedia://en/1926283) and [Lars Svenonius](wikipedia://en/28868294), proved several independently.[^o3] Despite this, the literature still widely refers to the Ryll-Nardzewski theorem as a name for these conditions. The conditions included with the theorem vary between authors.[^o4][^o5]

Given a countable [complete](wikipedia://en/10086335) first-order theory *T* with infinite models, the following are equivalent:

- The theory *T* is omega-categorical.
- Every countable model of *T* has an [oligomorphic automorphism group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligomorphic_group) (that is, there are finitely many orbits on *M<sup>n</sup>* for every *n*).
- Some countable model of *T* has an oligomorphic automorphism group.[^o6]
- The theory *T* has a model which, for every natural number *n*, realizes only finitely many *n*-types, that is, the [Stone space](wikipedia://en/3741564#Stone_spaces) *S<sub>n</sub>*(*T*) is finite.
- For every natural number *n*, *T* has only finitely many *n*-types.
- For every natural number *n*, every *n*-type is [isolated](wikipedia://en/3741564).
- For every natural number *n*, up to equivalence modulo *T* there are only finitely many formulas with *n* free variables, in other words, for every *n*, the *n*th [Lindenbaum–Tarski algebra](wikipedia://en/990343) of *T* is finite.
- Every model of *T* is [atomic](wikipedia://en/3005202).
- Every countable model of *T* is atomic.
- The theory *T* has a countable atomic and [saturated model](wikipedia://en/535836).
- The theory *T* has a saturated [prime model](wikipedia://en/1663421).

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The theory of any countably infinite structure which is homogeneous over a finite relational language is omega-categorical.[^o9] More generally, the theory of the [Fraïssé limit](wikipedia://en/62592127) of any uniformly locally finite Fraïssé class is omega-categorical.[^o10] Hence, the following theories are omega-categorical:

- The theory of dense linear orders without endpoints ([Cantor's isomorphism theorem](wikipedia://en/68245955))
- The theory of the [Rado graph](wikipedia://en/7785594)
- The theory of infinite linear spaces over any [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615)
- The theory of atomless [Boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/3959)
