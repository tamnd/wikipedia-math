# Michael Rapoport

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Rapoport"}

**Michael Rapoport** (born 2 October 1948)[^o3] is an Austrian mathematician.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Rapoport received his PhD from [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) in 1976, under the supervision of [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544). He held a chair for [arithmetic algebraic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_algebraic_geometry) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn),[^o6] as well as a visiting appointment at the University of Maryland. In 1992, he was awarded the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize),[^o7] in 1999 he won the [Gay-Lussac Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay-Lussac_Humboldt_Prize),[^o8] and he is the recipient of the 2011 [Heinz Hopf Prize](wikipedia://en/24560211).[^o9] In 1994, he was an Invited Speaker (with talk *Non-Archimedean period domains*) at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zürich.

Rapoport's students include [Maria Heep-Altiner](wikipedia://en/60048114), Werner Baer, [Peter Scholze](wikipedia://en/39409306), [Eva Viehmann](wikipedia://en/56680625).[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Michael Rapoport is the son of pediatrician [Ingeborg Rapoport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingeborg_Rapoport) and biochemist [Samuel Mitja Rapoport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Mitja_Rapoport), and brother of biochemist [Tom Rapoport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Rapoport).
