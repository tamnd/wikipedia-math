# Sergei Tabachnikov

*Image: Tabachnikov Dmitry Fuchs.jpg.* Tabachnikov (on the left) with Dmitry Fuchs in [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669), 2006

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Tabachnikov"}

**Sergei Tabachnikov**, also spelled **Serge**, (born in 1956) is an American mathematician who works in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). He is currently a Professor of Mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He earned his Ph.D. from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1987 under the supervision of [Dmitry Fuchs](wikipedia://en/56558134) and [Anatoly Fomenko](wikipedia://en/649501).[^o3] He has been living and working in the USA since 1990.

From 2013 to 2015 Tabachnikov served as Deputy Director of the [Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31112547) (ICERM) in [Providence, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island).[^o4] He is now Emeritus Deputy Director of ICERM.[^o5]

He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] He has served as Editor-in-Chief of the journal *[Experimental Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18797498)*, and is currently serving as Editor-in-Chief of the *[Arnold Mathematical Journal](wikipedia://en/59868645)* and as co-Editor-in-Chief of the *[Mathematical Intelligencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Intelligencer)*.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

A paper on the [variability hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variability_hypothesis) by [Theodore Hill](wikipedia://en/815323) and Tabachnikov was accepted and retracted by *The Mathematical Intelligencer* and later *[The New York Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Journal_of_Mathematics)* (*NYJM*). There was some controversy over the mathematical model, the peer-review process, and the lack of an official retraction notice from the *NYJM*.[^o10]
