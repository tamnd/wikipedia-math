# Leon (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leon"}

**Leon** (Λέων) was a Greek [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and pupil of [Neocleides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neocleides).[^o2] Leon was active from around 370 to 340 BCE.[^o3] His book *Elements* was overshadowed by [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331)'s [work of the same name](wikipedia://en/244107).

:::

[Proclus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclus) states the following[^o4] in his *Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements*:

> But Neoclides was junior to [Leodamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leodamas_of_Thasos), and his disciple was Leon; who added many things to those thought of by former geometricians. So that Leon also constructed elements more accurate, both on account of their multitude, and on account of the use which they exhibit: and besides this, he discovered a method of determining when a problem, whose investigation is sought for, is possible, and when it is impossible.
