# Rudolf Inzinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Inzinger"}

**Rudolf Inzinger** (5 April 1907 – 26 August 1980) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) mathematician who made contributions to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the theory of [convex bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_bodies), and [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956) for [sound waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_wave).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), he was a student at the *[Technische Hochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology)* in the same city. In 1933 he defended his PhD *Die Liesche Abbildung* and in 1936 he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation). After the [Anschluss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anschluss) he had to leave. After his return from war captivity he started again working at the [Technische Hochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule) in Vienna in 1945 where he was appointed associated professor in 1946 and promoted to full professor one year later.

In 1946 he reestablished the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society) whose president he was until 1948. In 1968/69 he served as president of the Technische Hochschule in Vienna.
