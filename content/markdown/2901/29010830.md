# Károly Bezdek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Károly Bezdek"}

**Károly Bezdek** (born May 28, 1955, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people)-[Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) as well as a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and the director of the Centre for Computational and Discrete Geometry at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) in [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary), Alberta, Canada. Also he is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) (on leave) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Pannonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pannonia) in [Veszprém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veszprém), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary). His main research interests are in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) in particular, in [combinatorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial), [computational](wikipedia://en/176927), [convex](wikipedia://en/4165882), and [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468). He has authored 3 books and more than 130 research papers. He is a founding Editor-in-Chief of the e-journal Contributions to Discrete Mathematics (CDM).

:::

## Early life and family {#Early_life_and_family}

Károly Bezdek was born in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary, but grew up in [Dunaújváros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunaújváros), Hungary. His parents are Károly Bezdek Sr. (mechanical engineer) and Magdolna Cserey. His brother András Bezdek is also a mathematician. Károly and his brother have scored at the top level in several Mathematics and Physics competitions for high school and university students in Hungary. Károly's list of awards include winning the first prize in the traditional [KöMal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Középiskolai_Matematikai_és_Fizikai_Lapok) (Hungarian Math. Journal for Highschool Students) contest in the academic year 1972–1973, as well as winning the first prize for the research results presented at the National Science Conference for Hungarian Undergraduate Students (TDK) in 1978. Károly entered the [Faculty of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELTE_Faculty_of_Science) of the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) in Hungary, and completed his Diploma in Mathematics in 1978. Bezdek is married to Éva Bezdek, and has three sons: Dániel,[^o4] Máté [^o5] and Márk.[^o6][^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Károly Bezdek received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (1980) as well as his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) degree (1997) in mathematics from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary and his Candidate of Mathematical Sciences degree (1985) as well as his Doctor of Mathematical Sciences degree (1995) from the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o9] He has been a faculty member of the Department of Geometry at Eötvös Loránd University in Budapest since 1978. In particular, he has been the chair of that department between 1999-2006 and a full professor between 1998 and 2012. During 1978–2003, while being on a number of special leaves from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), he has held numerous visiting positions at research institutions in Canada, Germany, the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), and United States. This included a period of about 7 years at the Department of Mathematics of [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in [Ithaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca,_New_York), New York. Between 1998 and 2001 Bezdek was appointed a [Széchenyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Széchenyi) Professor of mathematics at [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary. From 2003 Károly Bezdek is the [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) of computational and discrete geometry at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics of the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) and is the director of the Center for Computational and Discrete Geometry at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary). Between 2006 and 2010 Bezdek was an associated member of the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary. From 2010 Bezdek is a full professor (on leave) at the Department of Mathematics of the [University of Pannonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pannonia) in [Veszprém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veszprém), Hungary. Between July–December, 2011 Bezdek was a program co-chair of the 6 month thematic program on discrete geometry and its applications at the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), Ontario, Canada. Also, he is one of the three founding [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the free [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) electronic journal Contributions to Discrete Mathematics.[^o10]

## Research interests and notable results {#Research_interests_and_notable_results}

His research interests are in [combinatorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry), [computational](wikipedia://en/176927), [convex](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468) including some aspects of [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), [rigidity](wikipedia://en/1458875) and [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization). He is the author of more than 130 research papers and has written three research monographs. In particular, he is known for the following works:

- A new part of [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468) is studied in K. Bezdek and Zs. Lángi, Volumetric Discrete Geometry, [Chapman and Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapman_and_Hall) - [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press), Boca Raton, FL, 2019, which is centered around several outstanding problems of [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468) where the [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) plays a significant role. The results and proofs reflect and stimulate the fruitful interplay between [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o12]
- A proof of the Goodman-Goodman Conjecture (1945) for centrally symmetric convex bodies in Euclidean *d*-space for *d* > 1 and a counterexample to it for convex bodies in general (joint work with Zsolt Lángi, [Budapest University of Technology and Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_University_of_Technology_and_Economics)); published in K. Bezdek and Zs. Lángi, On non-separable families of positive homothetic convex bodies, [Discrete and Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_and_Computational_Geometry) 56/3 (2016), 802–813.[^o13]
- A proof of the [Boltyanski](wikipedia://en/20464140)–[Hadwiger](wikipedia://en/630451) Conjecture (1960) for wide intersections of congruent balls (also called fat spindle convex bodies) in Euclidean spaces of dimensions greater than or equal to 15; published in K. Bezdek, Illuminating spindle convex bodies and minimizing the volume of spherical sets of constant width, [Discrete and Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_and_Computational_Geometry) 47/2 (2012), 275–287.[^o14]
- A variational characterization of shortest billiard trajectories in convex bodies of Euclidean *d*-space for *d* > 1 (joint work with Dániel Bezdek); published in D. Bezdek and K. Bezdek, Shortest billiard trajectories, [Geometriae Dedicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometriae_Dedicata) 141/1 (2009), 197–206.[^o15]
- A proof of tight bounds for the vertex index of (unit) balls in [normed spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) supporting a quantitative approach to the [Boltyanski](wikipedia://en/20464140)–[Hadwiger](wikipedia://en/630451) Conjecture (joint work with Alexander Litvak, [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta)); published in K. Bezdek and A. E. Litvak, On the vertex index of convex bodies, [Advances in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Mathematics) 215/2 (2007), 626–641.[^o16]
- A proof of the [Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650)–Poulsen Conjecture (1955) for hemispheres in spherical *d*-space for all *d* > 1 (joint work with [Robert Connelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Connelly), [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University)); published in K. Bezdek and R. Connelly, The Kneser–Poulsen conjecture for spherical polytopes, [Discrete and Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_and_Computational_Geometry) 32 (2004), 101–106.[^o17]
- A proof of the [Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650)–Poulsen Conjecture (1955) in the Euclidean plane (joint work with [Robert Connelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Connelly), [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University)); published in K. Bezdek and R. Connelly, Pushing disks apart – the Kneser–Poulsen conjecture in the plane, [Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_für_die_reine_und_angewandte_Mathematik) 553 (2002), 221–236.[^o18]
- A stronger form of Rogers's lemma and its application to the problem of minimizing surface area of [Voronoi cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cells) in unit ball packings; published in K. Bezdek, Improving Rogers' upper bound for the density of unit ball packings via estimating the surface area of Voronoi cells from below in Euclidean *d*-space for all *d* > 7, [Discrete and Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_and_Computational_Geometry) 28 (2002), 75–106[^o19] and in K. Bezdek, On a stronger form of Rogers's lemma and the minimum surface area of Voronoi cells in unit ball packings, [Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_für_die_reine_und_angewandte_Mathematik) 518 (2000), 131–143.[^o20]
- A solution of [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807)'s "fried potato problem" (joint work with András Bezdek, [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University)); published in A. Bezdek and K. Bezdek, A solution of Conway's fried potato problem, Bulletin of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) 27 (1995), 492–496.[^o21]
- A proof of the [Boltyanski](wikipedia://en/20464140)–[Hadwiger](wikipedia://en/630451) Conjecture (1960) for convex polyhedra with symmetry in Euclidean *3*-space; published in K. Bezdek, The problem of illumination of the boundary of a [convex body](wikipedia://en/8652020) by affine subspaces, [Mathematika](wikipedia://en/49068222) 38 (1991), 362–375.[^o22]
- A proof of [László Fejes Tóth](wikipedia://en/2388442)'s Hyperbolic Disk Packing Conjecture; published in K. Bezdek, Ausfüllung eines Kreises durch kongruente Kreise in der hyperbolischen Ebene, Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungarica 17 (1982), 353–366.[^o23]

## Books {#Books}

His three research monographs "Classical Topics in Discrete Geometry", CMS Books in Mathematics, [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), New York, 2010, "Lectures on Sphere Arrangements - the Discrete Geometric Side", [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) Monographs, [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), New York, 2013, and "Volumetric Discrete Geometry", Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications, [Chapman and Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapman_and_Hall) - CRC Press, Boca Raton, FL, 2019 (co-authored with Zs. Lángi), lead the reader to the frontiers of [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468). The conference proceedings "Discrete Geometry and Optimization", [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) Communications, [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), New York, 2013, edited jointly by him, Antoine Deza ([McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University)) and [Yinyu Ye](wikipedia://en/25608838) ([Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University)) reflects and stimulates the fruitful interplay between [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468) and [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization).[^o25]
