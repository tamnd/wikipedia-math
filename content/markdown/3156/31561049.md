# Cartesian monoid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartesian monoid"}

A **Cartesian monoid** is a [monoid](wikipedia://en/19652), with additional structure of pairing and projection operators. It was first formulated by [Dana Scott](wikipedia://en/161875) and [Joachim Lambek](wikipedia://en/1250498) independently.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Cartesian monoid is a [structure](wikipedia://en/4055928) with [signature](wikipedia://en/7788156) $\langle *,e,(-,-),L,R\rangle$ where $*$ and $(-,-)$ are [binary operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_operations), $L, R$, and $e$ are constants satisfying the following [axioms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axioms) for all $x,y,z$ in its [universe](wikipedia://en/229180):

**Monoid**

$*$ is a monoid with identity $e$

**Left Projection**

$$L * (x,\,y) = x$$

**Right Projection**

$$R * (x,\,y) = y$$

**Surjective Pairing**

$$(L*x,\,R*x) = x$$

**Right Homogeneity**

$$(x*z,\,y*z)=(x,\,y) * z$$

The interpretation is that $L$ and $R$ are left and right projection functions respectively for the pairing function $(-,-)$.

:::
