# Edward Wakeford

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Wakeford"}

**Edward Kingsley Wakeford** (**E. K. Wakeford**; 15 June 1894 – 26 July 1916) was an English geometer.

:::

Born at [Plymouth, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth,_England), the son of Edward W. Wakeford of [Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibraltar), E. K. was educated at [Borden Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borden_Grammar_School) and [Clifton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_College) then entered [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) with a mathematics scholarship in 1912.[^o4] As a scholar of mathematics, he extended the work of the English mathematician [James Joseph Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861) (1814–1897) on canonical binary forms for odd degrees, solving the forms for even degrees.[^o5][^o6] At the time, he was recognized by some mathematicians as a future leader in the field of geometry.[^o7] In September 1914 he was commissioned in the [Leicestershire Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicestershire_Regiment) of the [British Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army) and served during [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I). On 26 July 1916 he was killed in action on the [Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Front_%28World_War_1%29).[^o4]
