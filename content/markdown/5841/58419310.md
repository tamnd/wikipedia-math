# Joe P. Buhler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joe P. Buhler"}

**Joe Peter Buhler** (born 1950 in [Vancouver, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver,_Washington)) is an American mathematician known for his contributions to [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Buhler received his undergraduate degree from [Reed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_College) in 1972, and his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1977 with thesis *Icosahedral Galois Representations* and thesis advisor [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933).[^o4] Buhler was a professor at Reed College in [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon) from 1980 until his retirement in 2005.[^o5] From 2004 to 2017, he was director of the [IDA Center for Communications Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Defense_Analyses#Center_for_Communications_and_Computing) in La Jolla, California.[^o6]

In 1997, he introduced, with [Zinovy Reichstein](wikipedia://en/31869776), the concept of [essential dimension](wikipedia://en/16950168).[^o7]

Buhler is involved in a project to numerically verify the [Kummer–Vandiver conjecture](wikipedia://en/2478058) of [Harry Vandiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Vandiver) and [Ernst Eduard Kummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Eduard_Kummer) concerning the class number of [cyclotomic fields](wikipedia://en/30872462). Vandiver proved it with a desk calculator up to class number 600, [Derrick Lehmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Henry_Lehmer) (in the late 1940s) to about 5000, and Buhler with colleagues (in 2001) to 12 million.[^o8] He continues the project with David Harvey and others.[^o9]

He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.
