# Weibel's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weibel's conjecture"}

In mathematics, **Weibel's conjecture** gives a criterion for vanishing of negative [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) groups. The conjecture was proposed by (Weibel 1980) and proven in full generality by (Kerz Strunk Tamme 2018) using methods from [derived algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/40778281). Previously partial cases had been proven by (Haesemeyer 2004), (Cortiñas Haesemeyer Schlichting Weibel 2008), (Geisser Hesselholt 2010), (Cisinski 2013), (Kelly 2014), and (Morrow 2016).

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Statement of the conjecture"}

## Statement of the conjecture {#Statement_of_the_conjecture}

Weibel's conjecture asserts that for a Noetherian [scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_%28algebraic_geometry%29) *X* of finite [Krull dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) *d*, the *K*-groups vanish in degrees \< −*d*:

$$K_i(X) = 0 \text{ for } i<-d$$

and asserts moreover a [homotopy invariance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_invariance) property for negative *K*-groups

$$K_i(X) = K_i(X\times \mathbb A^r) \text{ for } i\le -d \text{ and arbitrary } r.$$

:::

::: {#o13 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalization"}

## Generalization {#Generalization}

Recently, (Kelly Saito Tamme 2024) have generalized Weibel's conjecture to arbitrary quasi-compact quasi-separated derived schemes. In this formulation the Krull dimension is replaced by the valuative dimension (that is, maximum of the Krull dimension of all blow-ups). In the case of Noetherian schemes, the Krull dimension is equal to the valuative dimension.

:::
