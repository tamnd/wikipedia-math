# Doug Stinson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Doug Stinson"}

**Douglas Robert Stinson** (born 1956 in [Guelph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guelph), [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario)) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432), currently a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o3]

:::

Stinson received his [B.Math](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Math) from the University of Waterloo in 1978, his [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1980, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the University of Waterloo in 1981. He was at the [University of Manitoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manitoba) from 1981 to 1989, and the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) from 1990 to 1998. In 2011 he was named as a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o5]

Stinson is the author of over 300 research publications as well as the mathematics-based cryptography textbook *Cryptography: Theory and Practice* ().
