# Archive for Mathematical Logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archive for Mathematical Logic"}

***Archive for Mathematical Logic*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). It was established in 1950 and publishes articles on [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:[^o4]

- [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)
- [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)
- [SCImago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank)

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.287.[^o5]
