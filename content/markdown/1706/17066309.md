# Conway notation (knot theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conway notation"}

*Image: Conway tangle transformations and operations.svg.* The full set of fundamental transformations and operations on 2-tangles, alongside the elementary tangles 0, ∞, ±1 and ±2.

*Image: Blue Trefoil Knot.png.* The [trefoil knot](wikipedia://en/404773) has Conway notation \[3\].

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), **Conway notation**, invented by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), is a way of describing [knots](wikipedia://en/366808) that makes many of their properties clear. It composes a knot using certain operations on [tangles](wikipedia://en/30858815) to construct it.

:::

## Basic concepts {#Basic_concepts}

### Tangles {#Tangles}

In Conway notation, the [tangles](wikipedia://en/30858815) are generally algebraic 2-tangles. This means their tangle diagrams consist of 2 arcs and 4 points on the edge of the diagram; furthermore, they are built up from [rational tangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_tangle) using the Conway operations.

\[The following seems to be attempting to describe only integer or 1/n rational tangles\] Tangles consisting only of positive crossings are denoted by the number of crossings, or if there are only negative crossings it is denoted by a negative number. If the arcs are not crossed, or can be transformed into an uncrossed position with the [Reidemeister moves](wikipedia://en/1152374), it is called the 0 or ∞ tangle, depending on the orientation of the tangle.

### Operations on tangles {#Operations_on_tangles}

If a tangle, *a*, is reflected on the NW-SE line, it is denoted by *<sup>−</sup>a*. (Note that this is different from a tangle with a negative number of crossings.) Tangles have three [binary operations](wikipedia://en/3948), *sum*, *product*, and *ramification*,[^o5] however all can be explained using tangle addition and negation. The tangle product, *a b*, is equivalent to *<sup>−</sup>a+b*. and ramification or *a,b*, is equivalent to *<sup>−</sup>a+<sup>−</sup>b*.

## Advanced concepts {#Advanced_concepts}

Rational [tangles](wikipedia://en/30858815) are equivalent [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) their fractions are equal. An accessible proof of this fact is given in (Kauffman and Lambropoulou 2004). A number before an asterisk, *\**, denotes the polyhedron number; multiple asterisks indicate that multiple polyhedra of that number exist.[^o7]
