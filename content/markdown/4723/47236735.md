# Demographics of Luton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Luton"}

*Image: Population Density Luton 2011 census.png.* Population density in the 2011 census in Luton

**[Luton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luton)**, [Bedfordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedfordshire), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) is an ethnically and culturally diverse town of 203,201 people.[^o3] It is primarily urban, with a population density of 4696 PD/sqkm. Luton has seen several waves of immigration. In the early part of the 20th century, there was immigration of [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people) and [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) people to the town. These were followed by [Afro-Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_African-Caribbean_community) and [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asia) immigrants. More recently immigrants from other [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) countries have made Luton their home. As a result of this Luton has a diverse ethnic mix, with a significant population of Asian descent, mainly [Pakistani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_people) 29,353 (14.4%) and [Bangladeshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_people) 13,606 (6.7%).

:::

[^o4]

Since the 2011 census, Luton has had a white British population less than 50%, one of three towns in the United Kingdom along with [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester) and [Slough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slough). Luton has a majority white population, when non-British white people such as the Irish and Eastern Europeans are included.[^o4] Identity wise, 81% of the population of Luton define themselves as British of any race.[^o5]

There was likely 600 to 700 'coloured' people within Luton in 1958, around 300 of these being Afro-Caribbeans, 250 Pakistanis and 60 Indians.[^o7]

Luton is a majority minority town in which no single ethnic group make up the majority of residents, however by broad multi-ethnic groups, [Whites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_people_in_the_United_Kingdom) make up 54.7% of the population, a decline of 25.5% from 1991 when Whites made up 4 out of every 5 residents (80.2%). [Asian British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Asian) people have grown in proportional size, having gone from 14% in 1991 to nearly one third of the town's population (30%) in 2011. [Black British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_British_people) residents have also increased from nearly 5% to nearly 10% in two decades as well as [Mixed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_%28United_Kingdom_ethnicity_category%29) residents who have gone from 2.6% in 2001 to 4.1% in 2011.

### Ethnicity of school pupils {#Ethnicity_of_school_pupils}

Luton's schools are [majority-minority](wikipedia://en/65970268), that being no single ethnic group makes up the majority, the single largest group is [British Pakistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Pakistanis) at 25.1% of the school pupil population and the largest multi-ethnic group being [Asian British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Asian) people at 47.4% in 2022, which was previous [White's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_people_in_the_United_Kingdom) at 53.5% in 2004. The white [White British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_British) population has declined from a near majority in 2004 at 49.5% to 17.2% in 2022. [Black British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_British_people) people and [Mixed people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_%28United_Kingdom_ethnicity_category%29) have also increased. However, other ethnicities have declined from a peak of 3.7% in 2016 to 2.2% in 2022.
