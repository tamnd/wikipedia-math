# Ana María Aguilera

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ana María Aguilera"}

[Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguilera_%28surname%29) **Ana María Aguilera del Pino** is a Spanish statistician whose research involves [principal component analysis](wikipedia://en/76340), [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368), [categorical data](wikipedia://en/821165), and multi-dimensional [contingency tables](wikipedia://en/935515). She is University Professor in the Department of Statistics and Operations Research at the [University of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Granada).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Aguilera earned a Ph.D. at the University of Granada in 1993. Her doctoral dissertation, *Métodos de aproximación de estimadores en el ACP de un proceso estocástico*, was supervised by Mariano José Valderrama Bonnet.

She was named University Professor at the University of Granada in 2012. She was editor-in-chief of the *Bulletin of Statistics and Operations Research*, the academic journal of the [Spanish Statistics and Operations Research Society](wikipedia://en/51218889), from 2013 to 2017.

## Books {#Books}

Aguilera is the author of two Spanish-language books on [contingency tables](wikipedia://en/935515):

- *Tablas de Contingencia Bidimensionales* (2001)
- *Modelización de Tablas de Contingencia Multidimensionales* (2006)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Aguilera is an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).
