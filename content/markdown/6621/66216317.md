# Nedret Billor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nedret Billor"}

**Nedret Billor** is a Turkish statistician known for her work on [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) and [outlier detection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlier_detection). She is a professor of statistics at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Billor graduated from [Ankara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_University) in 1983, and earned a master's degree at [Çukurova University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Çukurova_University) in 1985. She completed a Ph.D. in statistics at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield) in 1992; her dissertation, *Diagnostic Methods in Ridge Regression and Errors-in-variables Model*, was supervised by Robert Loynes.

She returned to Çukurova University as an assistant professor in 1993 and was promoted to associate professor in 1997 and professor in 2003. In 2014, she moved to her present position at Auburn University. In 2019–2020, she served as chair of the Auburn University Senate.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Billor became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2012.
