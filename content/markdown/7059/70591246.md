# John Hempel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Hempel"}

**John Paul Hempel** (October 14, 1935 – January 13, 2022[^o3]) was an American mathematician specialising in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732).

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Hempel was born in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City), Utah. In 1957 he graduated from the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) with a [degree in mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In 1962, he defended his thesis at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison), under the supervision of [R. H. Bing](wikipedia://en/967353). He was a professor at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) until the time of his death.[^o3]

In 2013, Hempel was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

He also introduced the study of the [curve complex](wikipedia://en/51024643) into 3-manifold topology.[^o8]

Hempel wrote a book about [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257) in 1976.[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He was married to Edith, whom he married on September 1, 1965, in [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston), Texas; they had one son.[^o3]
