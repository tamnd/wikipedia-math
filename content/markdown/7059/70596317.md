# Giorgio Kaniadakis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giorgio Kaniadakis"}

**Giorgio Kaniadakis** (Κανιαδάκης Γεώργιος; born on 5 June 1957 in [Chania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chania)-[Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crete), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece)) a Greek-Italian [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), is a Full Professor[^o3] of [Theoretical Physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) at [Politecnico di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politecnico_di_Torino), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), and is credited with introducing the concept of [Kaniadakis entropy](wikipedia://en/70596336)[^o4][^o5] and what is known as [Kaniadakis statistics](wikipedia://en/70596336).[^o4][^o5] Giorgio Kaniadakis is the founder and chairman of the *[SigmaPhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SigmaPhi_International_Conference_on_Statistical_Physics)* *[International Conference on Statistical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SigmaPhi_International_Conference_on_Statistical_Physics)*, which is organized every three years in collaboration with the [European Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Physical_Society).<http://www.sigmaphi.polito.it/>

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1975 Giorgio Kaniadakis moved to Italy where he obtained the [Bachelor's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) and [Master's degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in [Nuclear Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_engineering) in 1981 from [Politecnico di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politecnico_di_Torino). In 1985, he obtained a [Master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in [Nuclear Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics) from [Università di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) ([Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy)) and in 1989 he received a [Ph.D. degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D._degree) in [Physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from [Politecnico di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Turin) with grant of the [International Center for Theoretical Physics (ICTP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) of [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).

## Career {#Career}

Since 1991 Kaniadakis is a permanent [faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_%28division%29) member at Department of Physics (now Department of Applied Science and Technology) of the [Politecnico di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politecnico_di_Torino) as Researcher, Senior Researcher (1994), Aggregate Professor (2010), Associate Professor (2014), and Full Professor (2019).[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

[Kaniadakis statistics](wikipedia://en/70596336)[^o4] (known also as κ-statistics) is a theory based on [Kaniadakis entropy](wikipedia://en/70596336)[^o4] (known also as κ-entropy) that was introduced in the trilogy of papers published between 2001 and 2005.[^o10][^o11][^o12] [Kaniadakis statistics](wikipedia://en/70596336) can be traced back to the first principles of [Special Relativity](wikipedia://en/26962).[^o14] It answers the open problem on how the temperature of a body is transformed in a moving inertial frame by selecting the [Planck-Einstein transformation law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck-Einstein_Relation). Within [κ-statistics](wikipedia://en/70596336), Kaniadakis entropy emerges as the relativistic generalization of [Boltzmann's entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann's_entropy_formula) exactly as happens with relativistic energy which turns out to be the relativistic generalization of energy of [classical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_physics).[^o13] Kaniadakis' theoretical framework, alongside the proposed statistical theory, also provides a mathematical formalism (also known as [κ-mathematics](wikipedia://en/70596336)) which is isomorphic to ordinary mathematics.[^o14]

[Cosmic rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_ray) are a relativistic particle system composed of normal nuclei as in the standard cosmic abundances of matter, and approximately can be viewed as an equivalent statistical system of identical [relativistic particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relativistic_particle) with masses near the mass of the [proton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton). For a long time, it has been known that the cosmic ray spectrum is not exponential, and then it violates the [Boltzmann statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_statistics). The presence in the cosmic ray spectrum of power-law tails, which extends over 13 decades in energy and spans 33 decades in particle flux, represents an important experimental test of the correctness and predictability of [Kaniadakis statistics](wikipedia://en/70596336).[^o4]

The statistical theory rooted on [Kaniadakis entropy](wikipedia://en/70596336) has significantly influenced scientific literature, both in establishing its theoretical foundations and in numerous application areas.
