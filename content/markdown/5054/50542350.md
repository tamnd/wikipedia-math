# Dwork conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dwork conjecture"}

In mathematics, the **Dwork unit root zeta function**, named after [Bernard Dwork](wikipedia://en/1678313), is the [L-function](wikipedia://en/245283) attached to the [p-adic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic) [Galois representation](wikipedia://en/486929) arising from the p-adic [etale cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etale_cohomology) of an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) defined over a [global function field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_function_field) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) *p*. The **Dwork conjecture** (1973) states that his unit root zeta function is p-adic [meromorphic](wikipedia://en/80857) everywhere.[^o2] This conjecture was proved by [Wan](wikipedia://en/22098796) (2000).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
