# Kazamaki's condition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kazamaki's condition"}

In mathematics, **Kazamaki's condition** gives a sufficient criterion ensuring that the [Doléans-Dade exponential](wikipedia://en/16986027) of a [local martingale](wikipedia://en/7302795) is a true [martingale](wikipedia://en/274536). This is particularly important if [Girsanov's theorem](wikipedia://en/336940) is to be applied to perform a change of [measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_%28probability%29). Kazamaki's condition is more general than [Novikov's condition](wikipedia://en/9268061).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of Kazamaki's condition"}

## Statement of Kazamaki's condition {#Statement_of_Kazamaki's_condition}

Let $M = (M_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be a continuous local martingale with respect to a [right-continuous filtration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-continuous_filtration) $(\mathcal{F}_t)_{t \ge 0}$. If $(\exp(M_t/2))_{t \ge 0}$ is a [uniformly integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniformly_integrable) submartingale, then the Doléans-Dade exponential *Ɛ*(*M*) of M is a uniformly integrable martingale.

:::
