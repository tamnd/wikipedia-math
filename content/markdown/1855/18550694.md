# Claude-Louis Mathieu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claude-Louis Mathieu"}

**Claude-Louis Mathieu** (25 November 17835 March 1875) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) who began his career as an engineer. He worked with the [Bureau des Longitudes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_des_Longitudes) and tried to determine the distance of the stars.

:::

Awarded the [Lalande Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalande_Prize) twice, in 1808 and 1815.
