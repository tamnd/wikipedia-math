# Adolph Göpel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adolph Göpel"}

**Adolph Göpel** (29 September 1812 – 7 June 1847) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who wrote the first paper on [hyperelliptic functions](wikipedia://en/499361) and who introduced [Göpel tetrads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göpel_tetrad).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

His uncle was a diplomat so he attended his first mathematical lectures in Italy when he was 13. He entered the [Friedrich Wilhelm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 1829 and received his doctorate in 1835. He did not correspond with many mathematicians, excepting [August Leopold Crelle](wikipedia://en/973559). After his death some of his works were published in [Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547).
