# Protorus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Protorus"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **protorus** is a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [topological abelian group](wikipedia://en/584136). Equivalently, it is a [projective limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_limit) of [tori](wikipedia://en/74800) (products of a finite number of copies of the [circle group](wikipedia://en/508177)), or the [Pontryagin dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontryagin_dual) of a discrete torsion-free [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974).

:::

Some examples of protori are given by [solenoid groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solenoid_group).
