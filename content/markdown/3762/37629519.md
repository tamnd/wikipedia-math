# Eigencurve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eigencurve"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), an **eigencurve** is a [rigid analytic curve](wikipedia://en/5828941) that parametrizes certain [*p*-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) families of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000), and an **eigenvariety** is a higher-dimensional generalization of this. Eigencurves were introduced by [Coleman](wikipedia://en/26813984) and [Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679),[^n1] and the term "eigenvariety" seems to have been introduced by [Buzzard](wikipedia://en/9277730).[^n2]

:::
