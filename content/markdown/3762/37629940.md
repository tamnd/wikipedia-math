# Shimura subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shimura subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Shimura subgroup** Σ(*N*) is a subgroup of the Jacobian of the [modular curve](wikipedia://en/669552) *X*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) of level *N*, given by the kernel of the natural map to the Jacobian of *X*<sub>1</sub>(*N*). It is named after [Goro Shimura](wikipedia://en/174724). There is a similar subgroup Σ(*N*,*D*) associated to [Shimura curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimura_curve) of quaternion algebras.

:::
