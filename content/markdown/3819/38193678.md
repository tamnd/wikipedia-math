# Daniel Wise (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Wise"}

**Daniel T. Wise** (born January 24, 1971) is an American mathematician who specializes in [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257). He is a professor of mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).[^o3] [Wise's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wise's_conjecture) is named after him.[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Daniel Wise obtained his PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1996 supervised by [Martin Bridson](wikipedia://en/36378173). His thesis was titled *non-positively curved squared complexes, aperiodic tilings, and non-residually finite groups*.[^o6]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

*Image: Daniel T. Wise.jpg.* Daniel T. Wise

Wise's research has focused on the role of non-positively curved cube complexes within geometric group theory and their interplay with residual finiteness. His early work was taken to higher dimensions when he introduced with Frédéric Haglund the theory of special cube complexes.[^o8] In 2009 he announced a solution to the [virtually fibered conjecture](wikipedia://en/2823145) for cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds.[^o9] This was a consequence of his work on the structure of groups with a quasiconvex hierarchy[^o10] which proved the virtual specialness of a broad class of hyperbolic groups, and established a program for using cube complexes to understand many infinite groups. This subsequently played a key role in the proof of the [Virtually Haken conjecture](wikipedia://en/4692183).

### Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2016 he was awarded the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069)[^o18] and the [CRM-Fields-PIMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRM-Fields-PIMS_Prize).[^o19] In 2016 Wise was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship).[^o20] He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) (FRSC) in 2014 and a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (FRS) in 2018.[^o21] For the theory of special cube complexes and his establishment of subgroup separability for a wide class of groups, Daniel Wise together with [Ian Agol](wikipedia://en/32084718) was awarded in 2013 the Oswald [Veblen Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veblen_Prize) in Geometry.[^o22] In 2019, he was awarded the [Lobachevsky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobachevsky_Prize) for his contributions to the virtual Haken conjecture.
