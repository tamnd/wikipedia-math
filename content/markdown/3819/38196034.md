# CEREMADE

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="CEREMADE"}

The **CEREMADE** (**CEntre de REcherche en MAthématiques de la DÉcision**, French for *Research Centre in Mathematics of Decision*) is a research centre in applied mathematics within [Université Paris-Dauphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University). It was created in 1970.[^o3]

:::

## Structure {#Structure}

The centre has about 75 permanent members and around 150 staff in total. It is structured in 3 main research areas.[^o5][^o6]

### Mathematics of economics and finance {#Mathematics_of_economics_and_finance}

Covering economics, [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance), [risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk) and [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924). CEREMADE has strong links to the Paris Dauphine University Institute of Finance, the Europlace Institute of Finance and the Risk Foundation. The institute offers various masters programmes in actuarial science and finance.[^o8]

### Nonlinear analysis {#Nonlinear_analysis}

Covering [nonlinear partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_partial_differential_equations), [optimal transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_transport) theory, [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) across many applied areas.[^o10]

Notable staff include [Ivar Ekeland](wikipedia://en/31377247), [María J. Esteban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/María_J._Esteban), Mathieu Lewin and [Irène Waldspurger](wikipedia://en/66188089).

### Probability and statistics {#Probability_and_statistics}

The probability and statistics group research areas include [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412) and [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801), as well as the study of models of [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481).

Notable staff include [Christian Robert](wikipedia://en/68684290), [Judith Rousseau](wikipedia://en/56536026) and [Cristina Toninelli](wikipedia://en/73299179).
