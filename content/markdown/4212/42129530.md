# Volodin space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volodin space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), the **Volodin space** $X$ of a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* is a subspace of the [classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556) $BGL(R)$ given by

:::

$$X = \bigcup_{n, \sigma} B(U_n(R)^\sigma)$$

where $U_n(R) \subset GL_n(R)$ is the subgroup of upper [triangular matrices](wikipedia://en/374222) with 1's on the diagonal (i.e., the unipotent radical of the standard Borel) and $\sigma$ a [permutation matrix](wikipedia://en/238849) thought of as an element in [$GL_n(R)$](wikipedia://en/113564) and acting (superscript) by conjugation.[^o2] The space is [acyclic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acyclic_space) and the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) $\pi_1 X$ is the [Steinberg group](wikipedia://en/14994781) $\operatorname{St}(R)$ of *R*. In fact, (Suslin 1981) showed that *X* yields a model for [Quillen's plus-construction](wikipedia://en/581071) $BGL(R)/X \simeq BGL^+(R)$ in [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500).

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

An analogue of Volodin's space where GL(*R*) is replaced by the [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) $\mathfrak{gl}(R)$ was used by (Goodwillie 1986) to prove that, after tensoring with **Q**, relative *K*-theory K(*A*, *I*), for a nilpotent ideal *I*, is isomorphic to relative [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172) HC(*A*, *I*). This theorem was a pioneering result in the area of [trace methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_method).

:::
