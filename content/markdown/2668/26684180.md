# 63 knot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="63 knot"}

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **6<sub>3</sub> knot** is one of three [prime knots](wikipedia://en/315030) with [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298598) six, the others being the [stevedore knot](wikipedia://en/26652059) and the [6<sub>2</sub> knot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6₂_knot). It is [alternating](wikipedia://en/1236143), [hyperbolic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_knot), and [fully amphichiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphichiral_knot). It can be written as the [braid word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braid_word)

:::

$\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_2^2\sigma_1^{-2}\sigma_2. \,$[^o3]

6<sub>3</sub> is a [Lissajous knot](wikipedia://en/12328965) and can be represented with the following [parametric equation](wikipedia://en/576108):[^o4]

$$\begin{aligned} x &= \cos(2\phi) \\ y &= \cos(3\phi + 0.67319) \\ z &= \cos(11\phi + 0.89759) \end{aligned}$$

with $0\leq\phi\leq 2\pi$.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

Like the [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565), the 6<sub>3</sub> knot is [fully amphichiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphichiral_knot). This means that the 6<sub>3</sub> knot is [amphichiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphichiral_knot),[^o7] meaning that it is indistinguishable from its own mirror image. In addition, it is also [invertible](wikipedia://en/17351839), meaning that orienting the curve in either direction yields the same oriented knot.

:::

## Invariants {#Invariants}

The [Alexander polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647) of the 6<sub>3</sub> knot is

$$\Delta(t) = t^2 - 3t + 5 - 3t^{-1} + t^{-2}, \,$$

[Conway polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647#Alexander.E2.80.93Conway_polynomial) is

$$\nabla(z) = z^4 + z^2 + 1, \,$$

[Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642) is

$$V(q) = -q^3 + 2q^2 - 2q + 3 - 2q^{-1} + 2q^{-2} - q^{-3}, \,$$

and the [Kauffman polynomial](wikipedia://en/9565831) is

$L(a,z) = az^5 + z^5a^{-1} + 2a^2z^4 + 2z^4a^{-2} + 4z^4 + a^3z^3 + az^3 + z^3a^{-1} + z^3a^{-3} - 3a^2z^2 - 3z^2a^{-2} - 6z^2 - a^3z - 2az - 2za^{-1} - za^{}-3 + a^2 + a^{-2} +3. \,$ [^o9]

The 6<sub>3</sub> knot is a [hyperbolic knot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_knot), with its complement having a [volume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_volume_%28knot%29) of approximately 5.69302.
