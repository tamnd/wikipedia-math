# Oka coherence theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oka coherence theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Oka coherence theorem**, proved by [Kiyoshi Oka](wikipedia://en/381978) in 1950,[^n1] states that the [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}^n}$ of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) on $\mathbb{C}^n$ (and subsequently the sheaf $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ of holomorphic functions on a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) $X$) is [coherent](wikipedia://en/603780).[^o3][^o4]

:::
