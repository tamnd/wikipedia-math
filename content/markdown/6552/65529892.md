# 1927 Costa Rican census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1927 Costa Rican census"}

1864 The [Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica) 1927 census was elaborated by then Dirección General de Estadística y Censos, predecessor of current [National Institute of Statistics and Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Census_of_Costa_Rica). The total population was at the moment 471524.

:::
