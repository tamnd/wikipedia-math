# 1973 Costa Rican census

1864

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="1973 Costa Rican census"}

The **[Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica) 1973 census** was elaborated by then Dirección General de Estadística y Censos, predecessor of current [National Institute of Statistics and Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Census_of_Costa_Rica). The total population was at the moment 1871780.

:::
