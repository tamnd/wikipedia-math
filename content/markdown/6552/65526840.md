# YanYan Li

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="YanYan Li"}

**YanYan Li** (also stylized as Yanyan Li, Yan-yan Li, and Yan Yan Li) is a distinguished professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), specializing in [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787).[^o2] He received his Ph.D. at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1988, under the direction of [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725). He joined Rutgers University in 1990.

:::

Li was a Sloan Research Fellow from 1993 to 1995, an invited lecturer at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2002,[^o4] and is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Major publications {#Major_publications}

- Yan Yan Li and Itai Shafrir. *Blow-up analysis for solutions of*$-\Delta u = Ve^{u}$*in dimension two.* Indiana Univ. Math. J. 43 (1994), no. 4, 1255–1270.
- Yan Yan Li. *Prescribing scalar curvature on $S^{n}$ and related problems. I.* J. Differential Equations 120 (1995), no. 2, 319–410.
- Yan Yan Li. [*Prescribing scalar curvature on $S^{n}$ and related problems. II. Existence and compactness.*](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~yyli/Li96a.pdf) Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 49 (1996), no. 6, 541–597.
