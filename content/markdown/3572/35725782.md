# Iosif Vorovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Iosif Vorovich"}

**Iosif Izrailevich-Girshevich Vorovich** (born 21 June 1920, died 6 September 2001) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician, scientific engineer, author and was made a member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1970. He was a specialist in [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918) and the [theory of elasticity](wikipedia://en/458866). He was born in the city of [Starodub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starodub), [Gomel Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomel_Governorate).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Vorovich attended Moscow State University to study mathematics where he achieved outstanding levels in his first year. He later became one of a group of specialists who were able to achieve a crossover between mechanical engineering and mathematics, expressing and posing mechanical problems in mathematical form, solving the problems mathematically and still maintaining an engineer’s understanding of the results.[^o3]

Vorovich graduated from the [Zhukovsky Air Force Engineering Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhukovsky_Air_Force_Engineering_Academy) in 1944. His main works deal with mathematical problems of continuum mechanics, the nonlinear theory of shells, problems of stress concentration and thick plates, and mixed problems in the theory of elasticity about which he wrote or co-authored several books. He was awarded the [Order of the Badge of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Badge_of_Honour).[^o4]

In 1971, Vorovich founded the Research Institute of Mechanics and Applied Mathematics at Rostov State University, [Rostov-on-Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov-on-Don), now part of the [Southern Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_University), Russia.[^o5]
