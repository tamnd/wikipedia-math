# Minuscule representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minuscule representation"}

In mathematical [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **minuscule representation** of a [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587) or group is an [irreducible representation](wikipedia://en/385982) such that the [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332) acts transitively on the weights. Some authors exclude the [trivial representation](wikipedia://en/450573). A **quasi-minuscule representation** (also called a **basic representation**) is an irreducible representation such that all non-zero weights are in the same orbit under the Weyl group; each simple Lie algebra has a unique quasi-minuscule representation that is not minuscule, and the multiplicity of the zero weight is the number of short nodes of the Dynkin diagram. (The highest weight of that quasi-minuscule representation is the highest short root, which in the simply-laced case is also the highest long root, making the quasi-minuscule representation be the [adjoint representation](wikipedia://en/302188).)

:::

The minuscule representations are indexed by the [weight lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_lattice) modulo the [root lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_lattice), or equivalently by irreducible representations of the center of the simply connected [compact group](wikipedia://en/750326). For the simple Lie algebras, the dimensions of the minuscule representations are given as follows.

- A<sub>*n*</sub> (${}^{n+1}_{k}$) for 0 ≤ *k* ≤ *n* (exterior powers of vector representation). Quasi-minuscule: *n*<sup>2</sup>+2*n* (adjoint)
- B<sub>*n*</sub> 1 (trivial), 2<sup>*n*</sup> (spin). Quasi-minuscule: 2*n*+1 (vector)
- C<sub>*n*</sub> 1 (trivial), 2*n* (vector). Quasi-minuscule: 2*n*<sup>2</sup>–*n*–1 if *n*>1
- D<sub>*n*</sub> 1 (trivial), 2*n* (vector), 2<sup>*n*−1</sup> (half spin), 2<sup>*n*−1</sup> (half spin). Quasi-minuscule: 2*n*<sup>2</sup>–*n* (adjoint)
- E<sub>6</sub> 1, 27, 27. Quasi-minuscule: 78 (adjoint)
- E<sub>7</sub> 1, 56. Quasi-minuscule: 133 (adjoint)
- E<sub>8</sub> 1. Quasi-minuscule: 248 (adjoint)
- F<sub>4</sub> 1. Quasi-minuscule: 26
- G<sub>2</sub> 1. Quasi-minuscule: 7
