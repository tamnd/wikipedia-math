# Trevor West

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trevor West"}

**Timothy Trevor West** (8 May 1938 – 30 October 2012)[^o3] was an [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people) mathematician, academic and politician.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born on 8 May 1938 in [County Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Cork), the eldest of four sons of Timothy Roberts West, headmaster of [Midleton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midleton_College), and Dorothy West (née McNeill).[^o6]

He was educated at Midleton College,[^o7] and [The High School, Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_High_School,_Dublin). West was a graduate of [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (TCD), where he was [elected a scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scholars_of_Trinity_College_Dublin), and of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o8] In 1970 he was elected a Fellow of Trinity College Dublin and was also an associate professor of mathematics, Junior Dean, and a great supporter of sport at Trinity.[^o9] He was also a Member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy).[^o7] He subsequently was a Senior Fellow of TCD.[^o10]

On 19 November 1970, West was elected to [Seanad Éireann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seanad_Éireann), at a [by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/By-election) for the [Dublin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_University_%28constituency%29) constituency caused by the death of [Owen Sheehy-Skeffington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Sheehy-Skeffington).[^o4] After his election, he established a reputation as one of the few liberal voices in the Seanad.[^o3] He was re-elected to the Seanad in 1973 and 1977, lost his seat at the 1981 Seanad election but was re-elected in 1982, then lost his seat again at the 1983 Seanad election.[^o4]

With [John T. Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_T._Lewis) and D. McQuillan, West drew up the constitution of the [Irish Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Mathematical_Society).[^o11]

He was the author of *The Bold Collegians*, a history of sport in Trinity College,[^o9] and was an avid follower of Trinity College's sports programs. He also wrote a history of Midleton College, published for its [tercentenary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tercentenary) in 1996.[^o12]

## Memorials {#Memorials}

An event was held in his memory at the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy) in December 2013 where a memorial issue of the Mathematical Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy was presented.[^o14]

A book of essays celebrating his life, *Trevor West : The Bold Collegian*, was published in October 2016.[^o15]

On 14 September 2018, a new sports hall in Midleton College dedicated to the memory of Trevor West was opened by the Provost of Trinity College, [Patrick Prendergast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Prendergast_%28academic%29).[^o16]
