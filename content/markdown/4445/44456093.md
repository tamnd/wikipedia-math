# Cohomological descent

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohomological descent"}

In algebraic geometry, a **cohomological descent** is, roughly, a "[derived](wikipedia://en/985897)" version of a fully faithful descent in the classical [descent theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descent_theory). This point is made precise by the below: the following are equivalent:[^o2] in an appropriate setting, given a map *a* from a simplicial space *X* to a space *S*,

:::

- $a^*: D^+(S) \to D^+(X)$ is fully faithful.
- The natural transformation $\operatorname{id}_{D^+(S)} \to Ra_* \circ a^*$ is an isomorphism.

The map *a* is then said to be a morphism of cohomological descent.[^o3]

The treatment in [SGA](wikipedia://en/394508) uses a lot of [topos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topos_theory). Conrad's notes gives a more down-to-earth exposition.
