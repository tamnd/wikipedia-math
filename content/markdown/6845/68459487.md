# Aleš Žiberna

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aleš Žiberna"}

**Aleš Žiberna** is a [Slovene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenian_people) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), whose specialty is [network analysis](wikipedia://en/325726). His specific research interests include [blockmodeling](wikipedia://en/68458864), [multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) and [computer intensive methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_intensive_method) (e.g., [computer simulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_simulation), [resampling methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resampling_method)).[^o3]

:::

Currently, he is employed at the [Faculty of Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_of_Social_Sciences,_Ljubljana) of the [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana), specifically at the Chair of Social Informatics and Methodology,[^o4] and Centre for Methodology and Informatics.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

In 2007, he proposed a solution to the [generalized valued blockmodeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_valued_blockmodeling) by introducing [homogeneity blockmodeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneity_blockmodeling) with the basic idea "that the inconsistency of an empirical block with its ideal block can be measured by within block variability of appropriate values". The newly-formed ideal blocks, which are appropriate for blockmodeling of valued networks, are then presented together with the definitions of their block inconsistencies.[^o7]

He also (in 2007/08) developed an [implicit blockmodeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Implicit_blockmodeling) approach, based on previous work of [Batagelj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Batagelj) and [Ferligoj](wikipedia://en/29730180) (2000).[^o8][^o9][^o10]

In 2014, he proposed a new approach to the [blockmodeling](wikipedia://en/68458864) - [blockmodeling linked networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockmodeling_linked_networks).[^o11][^o12]
