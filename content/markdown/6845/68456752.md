# Sara Lombardo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sara Lombardo"}

*Image: Sara Lombardo.jpg.* Lombardo in 2007

**Sara Lombardo** is an Italian applied mathematician whose research topics include [nonlinear dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamics), [rogue waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_wave) and [solitons](wikipedia://en/51654), [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213), and automorphic [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944). She is Executive Dean of the School of Mathematical & Computer Sciences at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University). Previously she was professor of mathematics at [Loughborough University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University), and associate dean with teaching responsibilities.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lombardo studied mathematical physics at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), earning a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in physics in 2000. She completed her PhD at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 2004; her dissertation, *Reductions of integrable equations and automorphic Lie algebras*, was supervised by Alexander V. Mikhailov.

After postdoctoral research positions at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds), [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent), [Manchester University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_University), and [Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit_Amsterdam), she joined the academic staff at [Northumbria University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northumbria_University) in 2011, becoming head of mathematics there. She moved to Loughborough University in 2017 and to Heriot-Watt in 2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lombardo is a Fellow of the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy) and a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139). She was one of the 2020 winners of the [Suffrage Science award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage_Science_award) in maths and computing.
