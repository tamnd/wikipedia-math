# Journal of Mathematical Psychology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Mathematical Psychology"}

The ***Journal of Mathematical Psychology*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) established in 1964. It covers all areas of [mathematical](wikipedia://en/2081516) and [theoretical psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_psychology), including [sensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sense) and [perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception), [psychophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychophysics), learning and memory, problem solving, judgment and decision-making, and [motivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motivation). It is the official journal of the [Society for Mathematical Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Psychology) and is published on their behalf by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier).

:::
