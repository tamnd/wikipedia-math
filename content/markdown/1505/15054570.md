# Semi-s-cobordism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semi-s-cobordism"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a [cobordism](wikipedia://en/391832) (*W*, *M*, *M*<sup>−</sup>) of an (*n* + 1)-dimensional [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) (with boundary) *W* between its boundary components, two *n*-manifolds *M* and *M*<sup>−</sup>, is called a **semi-*s*-cobordism** if (and only if) the inclusion $M \hookrightarrow W$ is a [simple homotopy equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_equivalence) (as in an [*s*-cobordism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-cobordism)), with no further requirement on the inclusion $M^- \hookrightarrow W$ (not even being a homotopy equivalence).

:::

## Other notations {#Other_notations}

The original creator of this topic, Jean-Claude Hausmann, used the notation *M*<sub>−</sub> for the right-hand boundary of the cobordism.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

A consequence of (*W*, *M*, *M*<sup>−</sup>) being a semi-*s*-cobordism is that the [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) of the derived [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) on [fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/11004) $K = \ker(\pi_1(M^{-}) \twoheadrightarrow \pi_1(W))$ is [perfect](wikipedia://en/585271). A corollary of this is that $\pi_1(M^{-})$ solves the [group extension problem](wikipedia://en/345807) $1 \rightarrow K \rightarrow \pi_1(M^{-}) \rightarrow \pi_1(M) \rightarrow 1$. The solutions to the group extension problem for prescribed [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526) $\pi_1(M)$ and kernel group K are classified up to congruence by group cohomology (see Mac Lane's *Homology* pp. 124-129), so there are restrictions on which n-manifolds can be the right-hand boundary of a semi-*s*-cobordism with prescribed left-hand boundary M and superperfect kernel group K.

:::

## Relationship with Plus cobordisms {#Relationship_with_Plus_cobordisms}

Note that if (*W*, *M*, *M*<sup>−</sup>) is a semi-*s*-cobordism, then (*W*, *M*<sup>−</sup>, *M*) is a [plus cobordism](wikipedia://en/581071). (This justifies the use of *M*<sup>−</sup> for the right-hand boundary of a semi-*s*-cobordism, a play on the traditional use of *M*<sup>\+</sup> for the right-hand boundary of a plus cobordism.) Thus, a semi-*s*-cobordism may be thought of as an inverse to Quillen's Plus construction in the manifold category. Note that (*M*<sup>−</sup>)<sup>\+</sup> must be [diffeomorphic](wikipedia://en/8564) (respectively, [piecewise-linearly (PL) homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise_linear_homeomorphism)) to *M* but there may be a variety of choices for (*M*<sup>\+</sup>)<sup>−</sup> for a given closed [smooth](wikipedia://en/2119219) (respectively, [PL](wikipedia://en/2174184)) manifold *M*.
