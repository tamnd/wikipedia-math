# Alexandre Eremenko

Emanuilovych

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandre Eremenko"}

**Alexandre Emanuilovych Eremenko** (born 1954)[^o3] is a Ukrainian-American mathematician who works in the fields of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Eremenko was born into a medical family in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine). His father was a pathophysiologist, professor and head of the Department of pathophysiology at [Ternopil National Medical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternopil_National_Medical_University). His mother was an ophthalmologist. He obtained his master's degree from [Lviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_University) in 1976 and worked in the [Institute of Low temperature physics and Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkin_Institute_for_Low_Temperature_Physics_and_Engineering) in Kharkiv until 1990. He received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from [Rostov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_University) in 1979 *(Asymptotic Properties of Meromorphic and Subharmonic Functions),* and is currently a distinguished professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o5] He had visiting positions in [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky), [MSRI](wikipedia://en/401367), [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College), [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool), [University of Paris XII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XII), [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel), [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University), [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion), [Weizmann Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute) and [University of Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tel_Aviv).

In [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518), Eremenko explored [escaping sets](wikipedia://en/29115268) at the iteration of transcendental entire functions and conjectured that the [connected components](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_component_%28topology%29) of this escaping set are unbounded *(Eremenko's conjecture).* For decades, the conjecture remained open, and has been called "one of the most famous open problems in transcendental dynamics";[^o6] a counterexample was found in 2025.[^o7]

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

Eremenko was a recipient of the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in Mathematics. In 2013, he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to value distribution theory, geometric function theory, and other areas of analysis and complex dynamics".[^o9] He was an [invited speaker in the International congress of mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) in 2002.
