# Eckhard Meinrenken

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eckhard Meinrenken"}

**Eckhard Meinrenken** is a German-Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He is a professor at [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Meinrenken studied Physics at [Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg), where he obtained a [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in 1990 and a PhD in 1994, with a thesis entitled *Vielfachheitsformeln für die Quantisierung von Phasenräumen* (Multiplicity formulas for the quantization of phase spaces), under the supervision of Hartmann Römer.[^o4]

He was a postdoc at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1995 to 1997, and then he joined [University of Toronto Department of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics) in 1998 as assistant professor. In 2000 he become Associated Professor and since 2004 he is Full Professor at the same university.

Meinrenken was awarded in 2001 an [André Aisenstadt Prize](wikipedia://en/8534576),[^o5] in 2003 a McLean Award[^o6][^o7] and in 2007 a [NSERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Sciences_and_Engineering_Research_Council) Steacie Memorial Fellowship.[^o8]

In 2002 he was [invited speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians#2002,_Beijing) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing[^o9][^o10] and in 2008 he was elected [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o11][^o12]

## Research {#Research}

Meinrenken's research interests lie in the fields of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). In particular, he works on [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry).

Among his most important contributions, in 1998 he proved, together with Reyer Sjamaar the conjecture "quantisation commutes with reduction",[^o14][^o15] originally formulated in 1982 by [Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636) and [Sternberg](wikipedia://en/5375996).[^o16] In the same year, together with [Anton Alekseev](wikipedia://en/62391241) and Anton Malkin, he introduced [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945)-valued [moment maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) in symplectic geometry.[^o17]

Meinrenken is author of more than 50 research papers in peer-reviewed journals,[^o18] as well as a monograph on [Clifford algebras](wikipedia://en/45305).[^o19] He has supervised 9 PhD students as of 2021.[^o4]
