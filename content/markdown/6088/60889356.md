# James E. Gentle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James E. Gentle"}

**James E. Gentle** (born 1943) is an American statistician and author. He was a professor of statistics at [George Mason University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mason_University) until his retirement in 2016. He is Co-Editor-in-Chief of [Wiley Interdisciplinary Reviews: Computational Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley_Interdisciplinary_Reviews:_Computational_Statistics)[^o3] and Senior Editor of [Communications in Statistics](wikipedia://en/20150587).[^o4]

:::

Gentle has written nine books, several book chapters and many research papers. His research is focused on statistical computing, simulation, robust statistics, survey sampling, and computational finance.[^o5][^o6] He is a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science)[^o7] and a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). He is an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o8]

## Early life {#Early_life}

Gentle was born in [Statesville, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statesville,_North_Carolina). He received a B.S in Mathematics from [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina) in 1966 followed by an M.C.S in Computer Science in 1973 and a Ph.D. in Statistics in 1974 from [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) under the supervision of [Herman Otto Hartley](wikipedia://en/14562926).[^o10]

## Career {#Career}

Right after completing his Ph.D, Gentle joined the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) as an assistant professor of statistics. He was given tenure as an associate professor in 1978. A year later, he left Iowa State University to join IMSL, Inc., as the director of research and design.[^o12] In 1992, Gentle left [IMSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMSL_Numerical_Libraries) and joined George Mason University as university professor of computational statistics. During his career at Iowa State and George Mason, Gentle supervised 22 PhD dissertations.[^o13]

In 1997, Gentle began a two-year leave from George Mason to serve as a program director in the Division of Mathematical Sciences of the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).

He has been a visiting professor at Texas A&M University, [University of Texas at Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Dallas), [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), [University of Texas at Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Arlington), and [Harbin Institute of Technology Shenzhen Graduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbin_Institute_of_Technology_%28Shenzhen%29).

Throughout his career, Gentle has been active in the American Statistical Association,[^o14] more specifically the Statistical Computing Section and the Statistical Graphics Section.[^o15][^o16][^o17]
