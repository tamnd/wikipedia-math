# Sandrine Dudoit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sandrine Dudoit"}

**Sandrine Dudoit** is a professor of statistics and public health at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). Her research applies statistics to [microarray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microarray) and genetic data; she is known as one of the founders of the open-source [Bioconductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioconductor) project for the development of [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214) software.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dudoit studied for the French [baccalauréat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baccalauréat) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and physical sciences at the [Lycée Molière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Molière_%28Paris%29) in Paris. She moved to Canada for her undergraduate studies, completing a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1992 at [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University).

As a beginning graduate student in the department of statistics at the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), Berkeley, Dudoit was the recipient of the Gertrude Cox Scholarship of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)'s Committee on Women in Statistics. She earned her doctorate at Berkeley in 1999. Her dissertation, *Linkage Analysis of Complex Human Traits Using Identity by Descent Data*, was supervised by [Terry Speed](wikipedia://en/11272394).

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After postdoctoral research with [Patrick O. Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_O._Brown) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), she joined the division of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in the [UC Berkeley School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley_School_of_Public_Health) as an assistant professor in 2001. She added a joint appointment in the department of statistics in 2006.

Dudoit and [Mark van der Laan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_van_der_Laan) are the authors of the book *Multiple Testing Procedures with Applications to Genomics*.[^o6] Dudoit is the editor of the book *Selected Works of Terry Speed*[^o7] and co-editor of *Bioinformatics and Computational Biology Solutions Using R and Bioconductor*.[^o8]

### Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Dudoit was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2010. She became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2014, and a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2021.
