# Feodor Deahna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Feodor Deahna"}

**Heinrich Wilhelm Feodor Deahna** (8 July 1815 – 8 January 1844) was a German mathematician.[^o2] He is known for providing proof of what is now known as [Frobenius theorem](wikipedia://en/602480) in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), which he published in [Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547) in 1840.[^o3]

:::

Deahna was born near [Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth) on July 8, 1815, and was a student at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1834. In 1843 he became an assistant mathematics teacher at the [Fulda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda) [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29), but he died soon afterwards in Fulda, on January 8, 1844.[^o2]
