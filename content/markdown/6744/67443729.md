# Alfred Galichon

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Galichon"}

**Alfred Galichon** (; born May 4, 1977) is a French [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o5] His work focuses on quantitative economics and econometrics.[^o9] He is a professor of economics and of mathematics at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o5][^o9]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Galichon was born in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He is a professor at New York University in the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute), and the director of [NYU Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYU_Paris).[^o5][^o9][^o7] Previously, he had been a full professor at Ecole Polytechnique, and then at Sciences Po, Paris. He is a graduate of [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique) and [Corps des Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_des_Mines), and holds a PhD in Economics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o8]

His work lies within quantitative economics, in particular on the economic applications of [optimal transport](wikipedia://en/6631661). He has contributed to the econometrics of [matching markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_matching_theory), [discrete choice models](wikipedia://en/4902101), martingale optimal transport, and [quantile regression](wikipedia://en/14692673).[^o9][^o17]

He is a fellow of the [Econometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometric_Society)[^o19] and the author of *Optimal Transport Methods in Economics*.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Galichon is the author of more than forty peer-reviewed articles.[^o12] His research has been funded by the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (2017-2020) and twice by the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council), for a total amount of approximately 3 million Euros.[^o13][^o20][^o16]

## Awards and distinctions {#Awards_and_distinctions}

- [Starting grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council#Grants_offered#Schemes), European Research Council, 2013-2016.[^o16]
- ['Young Leader' of the French-American Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French-American_Foundation#Young_Leaders), 2018.[^o17]
- [Economic Theory Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_the_Advancement_of_Economic_Theory#Fellows), 2019.[^o18]

- [Consolidator grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council#Grants_offered#Schemes), European Research Council, 2020-2025.[^o20]
