# University of Liverpool Mathematics School

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="University of Liverpool Mathematics School"}

**University of Liverpool Mathematics School** (abbreviated as **University of Liverpool Maths School** and **ULMaS**) is a [coeducational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coeducational) [maths school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maths_school) in [Central, Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central,_Liverpool), in the English county of [Merseyside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merseyside).[^o3] It was opened by the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool)[^o4] in September 2020 as the third [specialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specialist_school) maths school in the country[^o5] and the first in [Northern England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_England).[^o6] It is located on the university's campus, in the Sir [Alastair Pilkington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alastair_Pilkington) Building, and offers a curriculum specialising in [A-Level mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-level_mathematics) (including [further mathematics](wikipedia://en/1362593)), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).[^o7]

:::

## History {#History}

The University of Liverpool Mathematics College will be a hub for the most able young mathematicians in the Liverpool city region so they can develop their knowledge and skills through the study of maths and related subjects. In today’s global economy it is essential that the UK develops the potential of our most able maths students and this initiative will help respond to that challenge.

In July 2018 the [Department for Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_for_Education), with [Lord Agnew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Agnew) and [Liz Truss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Truss), announced plans to establish the University of Liverpool Mathematics College. It would be a maths school offering the subjects of A-Level mathematics, further mathematics, physics, and computer science, and would enrol 80 students per year.[^o9] An offer of music was also considered.[^o10] The [New Schools Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Schools_Network), made to support [free schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_schools_in_England) (including maths schools), welcomed the announcement.[^o11] The University of Liverpool promoted this college to [Year Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Year_Eleven) pupils in multiple schools throughout April 2019.[^o12]

By June 2020 the college's name had been changed to University of Liverpool Mathematics School. A headteacher, Damian Haigh, was appointed. The first teaching staff were recruited through [video call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_call) as a result of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_England). The Department for Education reached a funding agreement with the [University of Liverpool Mathematics School Trust](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/11686521) to enable it to open the school in September 2020.[^o13] The college opened on 1 September 2020[^o3] but the official opening ceremony did not take place until 30 September 2021. Doctor Steve Garnett was the guest of honour at the official opening ceremony,[^o14] a business magnate who previously spoke at the college.[^o15]

Between January 2021 and the start of March 2021, due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), [distance education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_education) arrangements were in effect. Physical face-to-face teaching resumed on 8 March under new preventative measures, such as [compulsory face masks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_masks_during_the_COVID-19_pandemic) in areas where [social distancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_distancing) could not be enforced. Students were also offered campus [COVID-19 tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_tests) and some testing equipment for home usage.[^o16]
