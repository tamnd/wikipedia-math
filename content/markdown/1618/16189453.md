# Greg Kuperberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Greg Kuperberg"}

**Greg Kuperberg** (born July 4, 1967) is a Polish-born American mathematician known for his contributions to [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), [quantum algebra](wikipedia://en/28993982), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). Kuperberg is a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kuperberg is the son of two mathematicians, [Krystyna Kuperberg](wikipedia://en/1028291) and [Włodzimierz Kuperberg](wikipedia://en/12918859). He was born in Poland in 1967, but his family emigrated to Sweden in 1969 due to the [1968 Polish political crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Polish_political_crisis). In 1972, Kuperberg's family moved to the United States, eventually settling in [Auburn, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn,_Alabama).

Kuperberg wrote three computer games for the [IBM Personal Computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Personal_Computer) in 1982 and 1983 (which were [published](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_publisher) by Orion Software): *[Paratrooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratrooper_%28video_game%29)*, *PC-Man* and *J-Bird*. ([video game clones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_clone) of *[Sabotage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabotage_%28video_game%29)*, *[Pac-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-Man)* and *[Q\*bert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q*bert)*, respectively)[^o5]

He enrolled at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1983 and received a bachelor's degree in 1987. He was ranked Top 10 in the 1986 [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition).[^o6] Upon leaving Harvard, Kuperberg studied at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under [Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson), receiving a Ph.D. in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732) and quantum [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) in 1991. From 1991 until 1992, Kuperberg was a [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) postdoctoral fellow and adjunct assistant professor at Berkeley, and from 1992 to 1995 held a Dickson Instructorship at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). From 1995 through 1996, Kuperberg was Gibbs Assistant Professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) after which he joined the mathematics faculty at the University of California, Davis.[^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

Kuperberg is married to physicist Rena Zieve, who is a professor of physics at UC Davis.[^o3]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Kuperberg has over fifty publications, including two in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*.

- Kuperberg, Greg. (1994). "The quantum G2 link invariant". *International Journal of Mathematics*. 5 (1). p. 61–85. doi:10.1142/S0129167X94000048. arxiv:math/9201302
- Kuperberg, Greg. (1996). "Non-involutory Hopf algebras and 3-manifold invariants". *Duke Mathematical Journal*. 84. p. 83–129. doi:10.1215/S0012-7094-96-08403-3. arxiv:q-alg/9712047
- with [Krystyna Kuperberg](wikipedia://en/1028291): Kuperberg, Greg; Kuperberg, Krystyna. (1996). "Generalized counterexamples to the Seifert conjecture". *Annals of Mathematics*. 144 (3). p. 547–576. doi:10.2307/2118536. arxiv:math/9802040. jstor:2118536
- Kuperberg, Greg. (2002). "Symmetry classes of alternating-sign matrices under one roof". *Annals of Mathematics*. 156 (3). p. 835–866. doi:10.2307/3597283. arxiv:math/0008184. jstor:3597283
- Kuperberg, Greg. (2005). "A subexponential-time quantum algorithm for the dihedral hidden subgroup problem". *SIAM Journal on Computing*. 35 (1). p. 170–188. doi:10.1137/S0097539703436345. arxiv:quant-ph/0302112
- Kuperberg, Greg. (2006). "Numerical cubature using error-correcting codes". *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis*. 44 (3). p. 897–907. doi:10.1137/040615572. arxiv:math/0402047
- Kuperberg, Greg. (1 May 2014). "Knottedness is in NP, modulo GRH". *Advances in Mathematics*. 256. p. 493–506. doi:10.1016/j.aim.2014.01.007
