# Demographics of the Middle East

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of the Middle East"}

The **Demographics of the Middle East** describes [populations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_%28human_biology%29) of the [Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East), a region covering western and northern parts of the Asian and African continents respectively.

:::

**Encyclopedia Britannica* definition of [Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East)**

*Encyclopedia Britannica* stated in 2018 that "by the mid-20th century a common definition of the Middle East encompassed the states or territories of [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), [Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus), [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria), [Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon), [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine), [Jordan](wikipedia://en/7515964), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt), [Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan), [Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libya), and the various states and territories of Arabia proper ([Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia), [Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait), [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen), [Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman), [Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain), [Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar), and the [Trucial States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trucial_States), or Trucial Oman \[now [United Arab Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates)\])."[^o5]

## Historical {#Historical}

In the year 1600, the population of the Middle East stood at about 18.5 million. Within modern borders:[^o12]

- [Anatolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia) - c. 6,500,000[^o13]
- Iran - 4,472,000
- Yemen - 2,243,000
- Saudi Arabia - 1,809,000
- Syria - 1,175,000
- Iraq - 1,000,000
- Lebanon - 292,000
- Oman - 275,000
- Jordan - 191,000
- Palestine- 161,000
- Cyprus - 98,000
- Kuwait - 71,000
- Bahrain - 54,000
- United Arab Emirates - 35,000

In addition, the population of North Africa was 12 million, with the following breakdown within borders:

- Egypt - 5,000,000
- Algeria - 2,250,000
- Morocco - 2,250,000
- Tunisia - 2,000,000
- Libya - 500,000
