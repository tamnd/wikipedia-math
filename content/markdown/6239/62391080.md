# Pierre Auger (biologist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Auger"}

**Pierre Auger** (born March 8, 1953) is a French [bio-mathematician](wikipedia://en/662088) and a member of the [French Academy of sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o2] and Director of Exceptional Class Research at the [Research Institute for Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Institute_for_Development,_Communication_and_School_Partnership).[^o3] Pierre Auger's research field concerns the [mathematical modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modelling) of biological systems.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Auger was born in [Neuilly-sur-Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuilly-sur-Seine) and studied at the [University of Paris 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (1973-1977), where he obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [Nuclear Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics) at the [Institut de Physique Nucléaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_physique_nucléaire_d'Orsay) d'Orsay in 1979 and his Doctorate in Physics. "Mathematical models of hierarchical systems" at the [University of Angers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Angers) in 1982. He was admitted to the CAPES in [Physical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Sciences) in 1983, and became a certified Professor of Physics and Chemistry at the Lycée Technique Pasteur in [Hénin-Beaumont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hénin-Beaumont), Pas de Calais from 1984 to 1986 and then a certified Professor in Metallurgy at the Lycée Diderot in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) from 1986 to 1987. He continued his career as a lecturer at the Biophysics Laboratory of the Faculty of Pharmacy of Dijon from 1987 to 1990, and Professor of Universities at the Ecology Laboratory of the [University of Burgundy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Burgundy) in [Dijon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dijon) from 1990 to 1992 and then Professor at the [Claude Bernard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Bernard_University_Lyon_1) in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon) from 1993 to 2004. He has been a research director at IRD since 2004. He was elected correspondent (1999) and then member of the French Academy of sciences (2003) in the Integrative Biology section.[^o2]

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

Pierre Auger is a specialist in mathematical modelling in [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) and [environmental sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_science).[^o6][^o7][^o8] He has contributed to the development of "variable aggregation methods", the aim of which is to build from a detailed or "complete" model, a reduced model governing only a few global variables in the long term.[^o9] In the 1990s, with the mathematicians Robert Roussarie and Jean-Christophe Poggiale of the University of Dijon, he formalized the method as part of the centre variety theorem (2010-2014).[^o10][^o11][^o12]

Pierre Auger has been particularly interested with his collaborators in the applications of variable aggregation methods to the emergence of global behaviors in complex multi-scale systems. At IRD, he contributed to the modelling of population dynamics of large herbivore populations at Amboseli Natural Park in [Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya) with the ACC ([African Conservation Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Conservation_Centre)) (2010-2014).[^o13]

This study highlighted the need to maintain corridors between Amboseli and other parks and ecosystems in Kenya and [Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzania) in order to maintain biodiversity and avoid the extinction of certain species. During his expatriation stays as an IRD researcher in [Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco) (2008-2012) and [Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senegal) (2012-2017), Pierre Auger contributed to the development of new mathematical models combining ecological and economic dynamics. In particular, it proposed bio-economic models of fisheries with a variable price of the resource on the market depending on supply and demand.[^o14][^o15] These models have been successfully used to explain observed trends in catches, fishing effort and market price of a species of the grouper family, commonly known as "thiof", in overfishing[^o16][^o17] situation in Senegal, in collaboration with the Centre Océanographique de [Dakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar)-Thiaroye (CRODT).[^o18]

## Educational activities {#Educational_activities}

At the Claude Bernard University in Lyon, Pierre Auger contributed to the creation of an original Biomathematics course for the part concerning the mathematical modelling of biological systems (1993-2004). He is co-author of a book on mathematical modelling in ecology.[^o7] He has been co-supervisor of many thesis students in Africa and Vietnam, particularly since his recruitment at IRD.[^o20][^o21]
