# Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz"}

The **Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz** (CMLS) is a joint research unit ([UMR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Center_for_Scientific_Research#Organization) 7640) of France's [*Centre national de la recherche scientifique*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS) and the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). It is located on the site of *École Polytechnique* in [Palaiseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaiseau).

:::

## History of the Center {#History_of_the_Center}

The *École Polytechnique**s first CNRS laboratory*****Laboratoire Leprince-Ringuet*was created by [Louis Leprince-Ringuet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Leprince-Ringuet) in 1936. Later, in 1958, [Louis Michel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Michel_%28physicist%29) founded in [Essonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essonne) the*Centre de physique théorique*(Center for Theoretical Physics) and invited [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) to initiate a similar project for [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In 1965, General Ernest Mahieux (1910–1967), the school's commander, gave Schwartz approval for both the budget and the location.[^o5]***

The Mathematics Center was officially established on May 1, 1965, with a Scientific Council composed of [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485), [Pierre Samuel](wikipedia://en/2604194), [François Bruhat](wikipedia://en/9169813) and [Jean-Pierre Kahane](wikipedia://en/6949961), and actually began its activity in 1966. The Center has always remained very close to the *Centre de physique théorique*, sharing with it secretariat, library and materials.

## Organization and activities of the unit {#Organization_and_activities_of_the_unit}

The unit is composed of three research teams: algebra and arithmetic; analysis and partial differential equations; and geometry and dynamics. The CMLS also actively participates in training through research: mathematical seminar days sponsored by the *Union des Professeurs de classes préparatoires Scientifiques* (UPS, Union of Science Preparatory Class Teachers) organized for teachers of [preparatory classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preparatory_classes), masters courses, and internship opportunities for the students of the *École Polytechnique*.
