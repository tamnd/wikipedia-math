# Yvan Martel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yvan Martel"}

**Yvan Martel** (born January 5, 1970) is a French mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Martel matriculated in 1989 at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and graduated there in 1992 with an undergraduate degree and in 1993 with a *[Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies)* (DEA) in [nonlinear analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_analysis) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). At [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6) he graduated in 1996 with *Thèse de Doctorat* (PhD) under the supervision of Thierry Cazenave. At the [Cergy-Pontoise University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cergy-Pontoise_University), Martel habilitated in 2000[^o3] with advisor [Jean Ginibre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Ginibre).

Martel's research deals with partial differential equations from mathematical physics, especially [solitons](wikipedia://en/51654). He has collaborated extensively with [Frank Merle](wikipedia://en/48258489).

Martel was a *[Maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* at Cergy-Pontoise University from 1997 to 2004, on leave at École Polytechnique as a full-time associate professor ([*Professeur chargé de cours à temps complet*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France)) from 2002 to 2004. From 2004 to 2012 he was a part-time associate professor ([*Professeur chargé de cours à temps incomplet*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France)) at École Polytechnique and a professor at [Versailles Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles_Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines_University). He was the director of the [*laboratoire de mathématiques de Versailles* (UVSQ/CNRS UMR8100)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Scientific_and_Technical_Research_Establishment) from 2008 to 2012 and the director of the [Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz](wikipedia://en/62392394) (CMLS) from 2012 to 2017. Since 2012 he is a professor at École Polytechnique[^o3] at CMLS.[^o4]

In 2008 Martel was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Amsterdam. From 2008 to 2012, he was a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).[^o3] In 2011 he gave a one-hour lecture at the Rivière-Fabes symposium in Minneapolis.[^o5] In 2018 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).[^o6]

Martel was [Fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_medal) medalist [Hong Wang](wikipedia://en/79379843)'s mentor for her postgraduate reading course at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). Wang praised Martel for helping her regain her confidence in mathematics.
