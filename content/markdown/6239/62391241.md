# Anton Alekseev (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anton Alekseev"}

**Anton Yurevich Alekseev** (Антон Юрьевич Алексеев, born 9 August 1967) is a Russian mathematician.

:::

Alekseev was a student of [Ludvig Faddeev](wikipedia://en/4854639). Alekseev worked at the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) in Saint Petersburg and, at the beginning of the 1990s, at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), Sweden. He is now a professor ordinarius at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva).[^o2]

Alekseev does research on representation theory of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and algebras, moment theory, [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o2]

In 2006 he, with [Eckhard Meinrenken](wikipedia://en/34534043), published in *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)* a proof of the Kashiwara-Vergne conjecture.[^o3] In 2008 he gave a new proof with fr:Charles Torossian.[^o4]

In 2014 Alekseev was an invited speaker with talk *Three lives of the Gelfand-Zeitlin integrable system* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.

In 2020 Alekseev received the Medal of the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematics and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematics_and_Physics).[^o5]
