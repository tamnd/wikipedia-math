# Ernesto Laura

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernesto Laura"}

**Ernesto Laura** (23 March 1879 – 29 December 1949) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Porto Maurizio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Maurizio).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1901 at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), where he was a student of [Morera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giacinto_Morera) and of [Somigliana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Somigliana).

He taught [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) at the [Universities of Messina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Messina), [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) and [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua).

Laura has dealt with uncommon elasticity problems, namely related to indefinitely extended elastic means and, especially in the last part of his University career, the mechanics of flexible and inextensible surfaces.
