# Nicholas Shepherd-Barron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicholas Shepherd-Barron"}

**Nicholas Ian Shepherd-Barron**, [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (born 17 March 1955), is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is a professor of mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Shepherd-Barron was a scholar of [Winchester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester_College). He obtained his B.A. at [Jesus College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Cambridge) in 1976, and received his Ph.D. at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) under the supervision of [Miles Reid](wikipedia://en/5863755) in 1981.[^o3]

In 2013, he moved from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) to [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Shepherd-Barron works in various aspects of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), such as: singularities in the [minimal model program](wikipedia://en/15148924); compactification of [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609); the rationality of orbit spaces, including the moduli spaces of curves of genus 4 and 6; the geography of [algebraic surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311) in positive characteristic, including a proof of [Raynaud's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raynaud's_conjecture); canonical models of moduli spaces of abelian varieties; the Schottky problem at the boundary; the relation between algebraic groups and del Pezzo surfaces; the period map for elliptic surfaces.

In 2008, with the number theorists [Michael Harris](wikipedia://en/31954452) and [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931), he proved the original version of the [Sato–Tate conjecture](wikipedia://en/597837) and its generalization to [totally real fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_real_field), under mild assumptions.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Shepherd-Barron was elected [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2006.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He is the son of [John Shepherd-Barron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Shepherd-Barron), a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) inventor, who was responsible for inventing the first [cash machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_machine) in 1967.[^o11]
