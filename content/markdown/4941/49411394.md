# Johannes Mollerup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Mollerup"}

**Johannes Mollerup** (3 December 1872 – 27 June 1937) was a Danish mathematician.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Mollerup studied at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), and received his doctorate in 1903.

Together with [Harald Bohr](wikipedia://en/638305), he developed the [Bohr–Mollerup theorem](wikipedia://en/780918) which provides an easy [characterization](wikipedia://en/780886) of the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316).
