# National Institute of Statistics (Mozambique)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Institute of Statistics"}

The **National Institute of Statistics** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): **Instituto Nacional de Estatística**, **INE**) is an agency belonging to the [Government of Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Mozambique) and the principal agency for the collection of statistics in the country. It was created under Presidential Decree nº 9/96, of August 28, 1996.[^o4][^o5]

:::
