# Èlizbar Nadaraya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Èlizbar Nadaraya"}

**Èlizbar Nadaraya** is a Georgian mathematician who is currently a Full Professor and the Chair of the Theory of Probability and Mathematical Statistics at the [Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University).[^o2] He developed the [Nadaraya-Watson estimator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadaraya-Watson_estimator) along with [Geoffrey Watson](wikipedia://en/7688869), which proposes estimating the [conditional expectation](wikipedia://en/435099) of a [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685) as a locally weighted average using a [kernel](wikipedia://en/8771473) as a weighting function.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Nadaraya was born in 1936 in [Khobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khobi), [Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_%28country%29). He received his doctoral degree from the V.I. Romanovski Institute of Mathematics, [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent) in 1981. He has since co-authored over 120 publications including 5 textbooks in the area of probability and statistics.[^o6]
