# Marian Scott (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marian Scott"}

**Ethel Marian Scott** (born July 1956)[^o3] is a Scottish statistician, author and academic, specialising in [environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339) and [statistical modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_modelling). She is [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) of Environmental Statistics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow).[^o4][^o5] She is additionally vice-president (International) of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh),[^o6][^o7] and a member of the [Scottish Science Advisory Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Science_Advisory_Council).[^o8]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Scott has a degree in statistics and a PhD from the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow). Her thesis was on the sources of error in [radiocarbon dating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiocarbon_dating), and was supervised by Murdoch Baxter and Tom Aitchison.[^o10]

Her research interests include model uncertainty and sensitivity analysis, modelling how pollutants disperse in the environment, radiocarbon dating and assessing [animal welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_welfare).

In 2005, Scott was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) (FRSE), Scotland's [national academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_academy) of science and letters.[^o11] In the [2009 Queen's Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Queen's_Birthday_Honours), she was appointed an [Officer of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (OBE) for services to social science.

Professor Scott was awarded the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) Barnett Award in 2019[^o13] for "her outstanding, pioneering research into the application of innovative statistical techniques to environmental issues."
