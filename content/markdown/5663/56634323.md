# Ling Long (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ling Long"}

**Ling Long** is a Chinese American mathematician whose research concerns [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000), arithmetic hypergeometric functions, as well as [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) in general. She is the Micheal F. and Roberta Nesbit McDonald Chair Professor of mathematics at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Long studied mathematics and computer science at [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University), graduating in 1997. She went to [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) for her graduate studies; her dissertation, *Modularity of Elliptic Surfaces*, she worked on with [Noriko Yui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noriko_Yui), visiting from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), in her time as a graduate student. She was supervised and influenced by [Wen-Ching Winnie Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wen-Ching_Winnie_Li).

## Career {#Career}

After postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Long joined the faculty at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) in 2003. After a year at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 2012–2013, she moved to Louisiana State University.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Long was the 2012–2013 winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](https://sites.google.com/site/awmmath/programs/michler-prize/michler-prize-awardees) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698). She was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to hypergeometric arithmetic, noncongruence Modular Forms, and supercongruences".

She is included in a deck of playing cards featuring notable women mathematicians published by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o6]
