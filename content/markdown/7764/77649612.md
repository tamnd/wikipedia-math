# Penka Georgieva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Penka Georgieva"}

**Penka Vasileva Georgieva** is a mathematician whose research interests include [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092), [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), and [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291). Educated in Bulgaria and the US, she works in France as a professor at the [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche](wikipedia://en/57642956), affiliated with [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Georgieva was a student of Vasil Tsanov in Bulgaria, earning a master's degree in 2005 with the master's thesis *Quotients of the Ball of the Second Kind*. She went to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for doctoral study with [Eleny Ionel](wikipedia://en/56679268), and defended her dissertation, *Orientability of moduli spaces and open Gromov-Witten invariants*, in 2011. She completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2020.

After working as an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 2011 to 2014, she came to the Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu as a postdoctoral researcher in 2014, and continued as [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) in 2016. She was named as a professor there in 2021.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Georgieva was an invited speaker at the 2022 (virtual) [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

She received the [CNRS Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Bronze_Medal) in 2022.
