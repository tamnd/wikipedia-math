# List of countries and dependencies by population (United Nations)

*Image: United Nations geographical subregions.png.* Statistical subregions as [defined by](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_geoscheme) the [United Nations Statistics Division](wikipedia://en/2996969)[^o1]

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="List of countries and dependencies by population"}

This is a list of [countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states) and other [inhabited territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependent_territory) of the world by estimated total population. It is based on estimates published by the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations) in the 2024 revision of *[World Population Prospects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Population_Prospects)*. It presents population estimates from 1950 to the present.[^o3]

:::
