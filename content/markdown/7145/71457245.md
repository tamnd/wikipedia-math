# Yukio Matsumoto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yukio Matsumoto"}

**Yukio Matsumoto** (松本 幸夫, *Matsumoto Yukio*; born 1944) is a Japanese mathematician, who worked mostly in the field of [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774). He was a former professor for mathematics at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo).[^o2]

:::

He received his Ph.D. in 1973 from the [university of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) and his supervisor was [Ichiro Tamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ichiro_Tamura).

In 1984, he won the [Iyanaga Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iyanaga_Prize) of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786).[^o2][^o4][^o5]
