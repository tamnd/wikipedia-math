# Ottaviano Menni

*Image: Menni\_-\_Amussis\_munitoria,\_1702\_-\_5171.jpg.* Ottaviano Menni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ottaviano Menni"}

**Ottaviano Menni** was an Italian mathematician and [mestre de camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestre_de_camp) who lived between the 17th and 18th centuries.[^o2]

:::
