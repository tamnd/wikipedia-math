# Alberto Pappiani

*Image: Pappiani\_-\_Scienza\_delle\_grandezze,\_1747\_-\_4256369.tif.* Title page of the *Scienza delle grandezze* (1747)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Pappiani"}

**Alberto Pappiani** (1709–1790)[^o2] was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), and [theologian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theologian).

:::

He was a [Piarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarists) [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) and [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher) of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in the [Florentine College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florentine_College).[^o3]

In 1758, he became president of the [Academy of Dogmatic Theologians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Dogmatic_Theologians) in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence).[^o4][^o5]
