# Joseph-François Marie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph-François Marie"}

*Image: Marie\_-\_Leçons\_élémentaires\_de\_mathématiques,\_1793\_-\_4268004.tif.* *Lecons elementaires*, Italian ed.

**Joseph-François Marie** (1738 – 1801) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was an [abbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbot) and professor of mathematics at the [Collège Mazarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_des_Quatre-Nations).[^o3]
