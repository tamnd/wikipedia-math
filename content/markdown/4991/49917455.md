# Giovanni Battista Nicolai

*Image: Nicolai\_-\_Della\_possibilità\_della\_reale\_soluzione\_analitica\_del\_caso\_irreducibile,\_1783\_-\_723652.tif.* *Della possibilità della reale soluzione analitica del caso irreducibile*, 1783

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Battista Nicolai"}

**Giovanni Battista Nicolai** (30 March 1726 – 15 July 1793) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua).[^o3][^o4] He was [archpriest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archpriest) of Padernello, a *[frazione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazione)* of [Paese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese).[^o5]

:::
