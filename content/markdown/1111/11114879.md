# Eugenio Elia Levi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eugenio Elia Levi"}

**Eugenio Elia Levi** (18 October 1883 – 28 October 1917) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for his fundamental contributions in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), in the [theory of partial differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_operator) and in the [theory of functions of several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables). He was a younger brother of [Beppo Levi](wikipedia://en/8718312) and was killed in action during [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War).

:::

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

He wrote 33 papers, classified by his colleague and friend [Mauro Picone](wikipedia://en/10162009) according to the scheme reproduced in this section.

#### Differential geometry {#Differential_geometry}

#### Group theory {#Group_theory}

He wrote only three papers in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890): in the first one, (Levi 1905) discovered what is now called [Levi decomposition](wikipedia://en/2774198), which was [conjectured](wikipedia://en/6138) by [Wilhelm Killing](wikipedia://en/1531824) and proved by [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274) in a special case.

#### Function theory {#Function_theory}

In the theory of functions of several complex variables he introduced the concept of [pseudoconvexity](wikipedia://en/3235998) during his investigations on the domain of existence of such functions: it turned out to be one of the key concepts of the theory.

#### Cauchy and Goursat problems {#Cauchy_and_Goursat_problems}

#### Boundary value problems {#Boundary_value_problems}

His researches in the theory of partial differential operators lead to the method of the [parametrix](wikipedia://en/18491210), which is basically a way to construct [fundamental solutions](wikipedia://en/1820858) for [elliptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_differential_equation) [partial differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_operator) with variable coefficients: the parametrix is widely used in the theory of [pseudodifferential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudodifferential_operator).

A short note announcing the results of paper (Levi 1907b).

An important paper whose results were previously announced in the short note (Levi 1907a) with the same title. It was also translated in Russian by N. D. XVII (1). p. 61–87. doi:10.1007/BF02419336. An important paper in the [theory of functions of several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), where the problem of determining what kind of [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) can be the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) of a [domain of holomorphy](wikipedia://en/3236377).

"Sulle ipersuperficie dello spazio a 4 dimensioni che possono essere frontiera del campo di esistenza di una funzione analitica di due variabili complesse". *Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata*. XVIII (1). p. 69–79. doi:10.1007/BF02420535. Another important paper in the [theory of functions of several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), investigating further the theory started in (Levi 1910).
