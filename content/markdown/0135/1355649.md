# Josip Globevnik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Josip Globevnik"}

*Image: Josip Globevnik.jpg.* Josip Globevnik

**Josip Globevnik** (born December 6, 1945, in [Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana), [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia)) is a [Slovenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenes) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

Globevnik graduated in 1968 and obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1972 at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Technology (FNT) of [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana). He worked on the Faculty of Civil Engineering and Geodesy between 1969 and 1988, also as an associate professor (1978) and professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (1983). From 1988 on he taught as a professor of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) in the FNT and later the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics.

He taught as a guest on universities in the [U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) (1973/74, 1978/79, 1983–1985). For a shorter time, he visited several universities in [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), and [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).

Globevnik's main research interest is [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He published around 50 articles in international journals. Since 1985 he has been a correspondence member and since 1989 a regular member of the [Slovenian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts).

In 1976, he was awarded with [Kidric Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidric_Award).
