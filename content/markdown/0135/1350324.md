# Gromov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gromov"}

**Gromov** (Громов) is a Russian male surname, its feminine counterpart is **Gromova** (Громова).

:::

**Gromov** may refer to:

- [Alexander Georgiyevich Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Georgiyevich_Gromov) (born 1947), Russian politician and KGB officer
- [Alexander Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Gromov) (born 1959), Russian science fiction writer
- [Alexey Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Gromov) (born 1960), Russian politician
- [Boris Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Gromov) (born 1943), Soviet general, military commander in Afghanistan and Governor of Moscow Oblast
- [Dmitri Gromov (figure skater)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Gromov_%28figure_skater%29) (born 1967), Russian figure skater
- [Dmitri Gromov (ice hockey)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Gromov_%28ice_hockey%29) (born 1991), Russian ice hockey player
- [Feliks Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feliks_Gromov) (1937–2021), Russian admiral, commander in chief Russian Navy (1992–1997)
- [Kirill Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirill_Gromov) (born 1990), Russian ice hockey defenceman
- [Maxim Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Gromov) (born 1973), Russian political dissident
- [Mikhail Gromov (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28disambiguation%29)
  - [Mikhail Leonidovich Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov) (born 1943), French-Russian mathematician
  - [Mikhail Mikhailovich Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Mikhailovich_Gromov) (1899–1985), Russian aviator
- [Nikolai Gromov (footballer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Gromov_%28footballer%29) (1892–1943), Russian football player
- [Valeri Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valeri_Gromov) (born 1980), Russian football player

**Gromova** may refer to:

- [Lyudmila Gromova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyudmila_Gromova) (born 1942), Russian artistic gymnast
- [Mariya Gromova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariya_Gromova) (born 1984), Russian synchronized swimmer
- [Maria Gromova (swimmer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Gromova_%28swimmer%29) (born 1988), Russian backstroke and relay swimmer
- Serafima Gromova (1923–2013), engineer, Hero of Socialist Labour
- [Ulyana Gromova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulyana_Gromova) (1924–1943), Hero of the Soviet Union
- [Vera Gromova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_Gromova) (1891–1973), Russian paleontologist
- [Zhanna Gromova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhanna_Gromova) (born 1949), Russian figure skating coach
