# Tomoyuki Arakawa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tomoyuki Arakawa"}

**Tomoyuki Arakawa** (Japanese: 荒川 知幸; born 22 May 1968) is a Japanese mathematician, mathematical physicist, and a professor at the RIMS of the [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University). His research interests are [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [vertex algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_algebras) and he is known especially for the work in [W-algebras](wikipedia://en/4791570). He obtained his PhD from the [University of Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University) in 1999. In 2018 he was invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o2] He won the [MSJ Autumn Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSJ_Autumn_Prize) in 2017 for his work on representation theory of W-algebras.[^o3]

:::
