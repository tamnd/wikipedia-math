# Benson Farb

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Benson Farb"}

**Benson Stanley Farb** (born October 25, 1967) is an American mathematician at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). His research fields include [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

A native of [Norristown, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norristown,_Pennsylvania), Farb earned his bachelor's degree from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). In 1994, he obtained his doctorate from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), under the supervision of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190).

## Career {#Career}

Farb has advised over 40 students, including [Pallavi Dani](wikipedia://en/66646913), [Kathryn Mann](wikipedia://en/61738901), [Dan Margalit](wikipedia://en/63869540), [Karin Melnick](wikipedia://en/64427608) and [Andrew Putman](wikipedia://en/57070515).[^o4]

In 2012, Farb became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] In 2014, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), speaking in the section on Topology.[^o7] He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2021.[^o8] In 2024, he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition.[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Farb married [Amie Wilkinson](wikipedia://en/34750309), professor of mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), on December 28, 1996. They are professors in the same department.[^o15]
