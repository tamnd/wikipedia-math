# Ram Prakash Bambah

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ram Prakash Bambah"}

**Ram Prakash Bambah** (17 September 1925 – 26 May 2025) was an Indian mathematician working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Bambah was born on 17 September 1925.[^o3][^o8] He earned a bachelor's degree from [Government College University, Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_College_University,_Lahore), and a master's degree from the [University of the Punjab, Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Punjab,_Lahore).[^o8] He then went to England for his doctoral studies, earning his Ph.D. in 1950 from [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge) under the supervision of [Louis J. Mordell](wikipedia://en/991484).[^o8] Returning to India, he became a reader at [Panjab University, Chandigarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University,_Chandigarh), in 1952, and was promoted to professor there in 1957.[^o8] Maintaining his position at Panjab University, he also held a position as professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in the US from 1964 to 1969.[^o8] He retired from Panjab University in 1993.[^o8]

He was married to Saudamini, whose father was the well known Indian botanist [Prana Krushna Parija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prana_Krushna_Parija). Their daughter Bindu Bambah is a well-known Theoretical Physicist.

He was president of the [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) in 1969, and [vice chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_chancellor) of Panjab University from 1985 to 1991.[^o8]

Bambah died on 26 May 2025, at the age of 99.[^o5][^o6]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Bambah was elected to the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) in 1955.[^o8] In 1979 he was awarded the [Srinivasa Ramanujan Medal](wikipedia://en/32501943),[^o8] and in 1974 was elected to the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3] In 1988 he received the Aryabhata Medal of the Indian National Science Academy[^o8] and the [Padma Bhushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Bhushan) award.[^o9]
