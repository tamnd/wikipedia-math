# Kanakanahalli Ramachandra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kanakanahalli Ramachandra"}

**Kanakanahalli Ramachandra** (18 August 1933 – 17 January 2011) was an Indian mathematician working in both [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) and [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

## Early career {#Early_career}

Ramachandra went to the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR), [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay), for his graduate studies in 1958. He obtained his PhD from [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) in 1965; his doctorate was guided by [K. G. Ramanathan](wikipedia://en/436480).[^o4]

## Later career {#Later_career}

Between the years 1965 and 1995 he worked at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) and after retirement joined the [National Institute of Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Advanced_Studies), Bangalore where he worked till 2011, the year he died. During the course of his lifetime, he published over 200 articles, of which over 170 have been catalogued by [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646).

His work was primarily in the area of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), working on the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) and allied functions. Apart from analytic number theory, he made substantial contributions to the theory of [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698), in which he is known for his proof of the [six exponentials theorem](wikipedia://en/15114520), achieved independently of [Serge Lang](wikipedia://en/872991). He also contributed to many other areas of number theory.

In 1978 he founded the [Hardy–Ramanujan journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy–Ramanujan_journal), and published it on behalf of the Hardy–Ramanujan society until his death.

## Awards and distinctions {#Awards_and_distinctions}

- Elected President of the [Calcutta Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_Mathematical_Society) for the period; 2007–2010[^o7]
