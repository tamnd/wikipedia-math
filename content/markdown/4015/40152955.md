# Poretsky's law of forms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poretsky's law of forms"}

In [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844), **Poretsky's law of forms** shows that the single Boolean equation $f(X)=0$ is equivalent to $g(X)=h(X)$ if and only if $g=f\oplus h$, where $\oplus$ represents [exclusive or](wikipedia://en/105979).

:::

The law of forms was discovered by [Platon Poretsky](wikipedia://en/3033920).
