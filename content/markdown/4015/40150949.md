# Fifth power (algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fifth power"}

In [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **fifth power** or **sursolid**[^o2] of a [number](wikipedia://en/21690) *n* is the result of multiplying five instances of *n* together:

:::

$$n^{5} = n \times n \times n \times n \times n.$$

Fifth [powers](wikipedia://en/99491) are also formed by multiplying a number by its [fourth power](wikipedia://en/665027), or the [square](wikipedia://en/213919) of a number by its [cube](wikipedia://en/451445).

The sequence of fifth powers of [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) is:

0, 1, 32, 243, 1024, 3125, 7776, 16807, 32768, 59049, 100000, 161051, 248832, 371293, 537824, 759375, 1048576, 1419857, 1889568, 2476099, 3200000, 4084101, 5153632, 6436343, 7962624, 9765625, ...

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

For any integer *n*, the last [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) digit of *n*<sup>5</sup> is the same as the last (decimal) digit of *n*, i.e.

$$n \equiv n^5\pmod {10}$$

By the [Abel–Ruffini theorem](wikipedia://en/152518), there is no general [algebraic formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_formula) (formula expressed in terms of [radical expressions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_expression)) for the solution of [polynomial equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation) containing a fifth power of the [unknown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unknown_%28mathematics%29) as their highest power. This is the lowest power for which this is true. See [quintic equation](wikipedia://en/303368), [sextic equation](wikipedia://en/5918099), and [septic equation](wikipedia://en/25451646).

Along with the fourth power, the fifth power is one of two powers *k* that can be expressed as the sum of *k* − 1 other *k*-th powers, providing counterexamples to [Euler's sum of powers conjecture](wikipedia://en/9660). Specifically,

$27^{5} + 84^{5} + 110^{5} + 133^{5} = 144^{5}$ (Lander & Parkin, 1966)[^o5]

:::
