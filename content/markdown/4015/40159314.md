# Relativistic chaos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Relativistic chaos"}

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), **relativistic chaos** is the application of [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295) to [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) described primarily by [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), and also [special relativity](wikipedia://en/26962).

:::

[John D. Barrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Barrow) showed in 1982 that the [Einstein field equations](wikipedia://en/424540) exhibit chaotic behaviour and modelled the [Mixmaster universe](wikipedia://en/8014489) as a dynamical system.[^o2] Later work showed that relativistic chaos is coordinate invariant.[^o3]
