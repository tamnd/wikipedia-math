# Niels Erik Nørlund

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Niels Erik Nørlund"}

**Niels Erik Nørlund** [^o3] (26 October 1885, in [Slagelse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slagelse) – 4 July 1981, in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen)) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o6]

:::

His book *Vorlesungen über Differenzenrechnung* (1924, reprinted 1954) was the first book on [complex function](wikipedia://en/5759) solutions of [difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation). His doctoral students include [Georg Rasch](wikipedia://en/3253022).

The [Norlund Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norlund_Alps) and [Norlund Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norlund_Land) in [Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland) were named after him.[^o7]

He was also the brother of [Margrethe Nørlund Bohr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margrethe_Bohr) and brother-in-law of Nobel Prize winning physicist [Niels Bohr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niels_Bohr).
