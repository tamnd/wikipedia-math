# Jean-Luc Brylinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Luc Brylinski"}

**Jean-Luc Brylinski** (born in 1951) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Educated at the [Lycée Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Pasteur_%28Neuilly-sur-Seine%29) and the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), after an appointment as researcher with the [C. N. R. S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._N._R._S.) and as a faculty at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), he became a Professor of Mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1988. He proved the [Kazhdan–Lusztig conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhdan–Lusztig_conjecture) with [Masaki Kashiwara](wikipedia://en/4376209). He has also worked on [gerbes](wikipedia://en/822043), [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172), [Quillen bundles](wikipedia://en/539562), and [geometric class field theory](wikipedia://en/51692308), among other geometric and algebraic topics.

:::

Brylinski is currently residing in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), where he is a CTO and co-CEO of *Brylinski Research*, together with his wife [Ranee Brylinski](wikipedia://en/58677212).
