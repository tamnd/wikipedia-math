# Duality (electricity and magnetism)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Duality"}

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), the **electromagnetic dual** concept is based on the idea that, in the static case, [electromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetism) has two separate facets: [electric fields](wikipedia://en/41092) and [magnetic fields](wikipedia://en/36563).[^o2][^o3] Expressions in one of these will have a directly analogous, or dual, expression in the other. The reason for this can ultimately be traced to [special relativity](wikipedia://en/26962), where applying the [Lorentz transformation](wikipedia://en/18404) to the electric field will transform it into a magnetic field. These are special cases of [duality in mathematics](wikipedia://en/609737).

:::

- The [electric field](wikipedia://en/41092) ($\mathbf{E}$) is the dual of the [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563) ($\mathbf{H}$).[^o4]
- The [electric displacement field](wikipedia://en/1191101) ($\mathbf{D}$) is the dual of the [magnetic flux density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_flux_density) ($\mathbf{B}$).[^o4]
- [Faraday's law of induction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday's_law_of_induction) is the dual of [Ampère's circuital law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampère's_circuital_law).
- [Gauss's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss's_law) for electric field is the dual of [Gauss's law for magnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss's_law_for_magnetism).
- The [electric potential](wikipedia://en/59615) is the dual of the [magnetic potential](wikipedia://en/2395496).
- [Permittivity](wikipedia://en/53933) is the dual of [permeability](wikipedia://en/754487).
- [Electrostriction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostriction) is the dual of [magnetostriction](wikipedia://en/201002).
- [Piezoelectricity](wikipedia://en/24975) is the dual of [piezomagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piezomagnetism).
- [Ferroelectricity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferroelectricity) is the dual of [ferromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferromagnetism).
- An [electrostatic motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatic_motor) is the dual of a [magnetic motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_motor);
- [Electrets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electret) are the dual of [permanent magnets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnet);
- The [Faraday effect](wikipedia://en/518692) is the dual of the [Kerr effect](wikipedia://en/661039);
- The [Zeeman effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeeman_effect) is the dual of the [Stark effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stark_effect);
- The hypothetical [magnetic monopole](wikipedia://en/175470) is the dual of [electric charge](wikipedia://en/9804).

Additionally, in a sense different from the dualities above:

- The [Aharonov–Casher effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aharonov–Casher_effect) is the dual to the [Aharonov–Bohm effect](wikipedia://en/277664).

In the usual sense of electromagnetic duality, the (unobserved) dual of the Aharonov-Bohm effect would be the phase acquired by a hypothetical magnetic monopole traveling through electric field. In contrast, the [Aharonov–Casher effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aharonov–Casher_effect) is for a magnetic dipole (moment) in electric field, and its dual in the usual sense is for an electric dipole in magnetic field, which is distinct from the [Aharonov–Bohm effect](wikipedia://en/277664).
