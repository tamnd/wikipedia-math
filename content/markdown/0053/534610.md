# Ankeny–Artin–Chowla congruence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ankeny–Artin–Chowla congruence"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **Ankeny–Artin–Chowla congruence** is a result published in 1953 by [N. C. Ankeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._C._Ankeny), [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) and [S. Chowla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._Chowla). It concerns the [class number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_%28number_theory%29) *h* of a real [quadratic field](wikipedia://en/454781) of [discriminant](wikipedia://en/55607) *d* > 0. If the [fundamental unit](wikipedia://en/3995927) of the field is

:::

$$\varepsilon = \frac{t + u \sqrt{d}}{2}$$

with integers *t* and *u*, it expresses in another form

$$\frac{ht}{u} \pmod{p}\;$$

for any [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) *p* > 2 that divides *d*. In case *p* > 3 it states that

$$-2{mht \over u} \equiv \sum_{0 < k < d} {\chi(k) \over k}\lfloor {k/p} \rfloor \pmod {p}$$

where $m = \frac{d}{p}\;$ and $\chi\;$ is the [Dirichlet character](wikipedia://en/174515) for the quadratic field. For *p* = 3 there is a factor (1 + *m*) multiplying the [LHS](wikipedia://en/166352). Here

$$\lfloor x\rfloor$$

represents the [floor function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floor_function) of *x*.

A related result is that if *d=p* is congruent to one mod four, then

$${u \over t}h \equiv B_{(p-1)/2} \pmod{ p}$$

where *B*<sub>*n*</sub> is the *n*th [Bernoulli number](wikipedia://en/4964).

There are some generalisations of these basic results, in the papers of the authors.
