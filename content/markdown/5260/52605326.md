# Vincenzo Amato (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vincenzo Amato"}

**Vincenzo Amato** (2 June 1881 in [Taranto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taranto) – 2 March 1963 in [Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catania)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).
