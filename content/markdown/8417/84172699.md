# Cezary Kaliszyk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cezary Kaliszyk"}

**Cezary Kaliszyk** (born 1981) is a Polish computer scientist and professor of theoretical computer science at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne). His research focuses on [automated reasoning](wikipedia://en/2884728), formal mathematics, [proof assistants](wikipedia://en/1258607), and the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning to mathematical reasoning.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kaliszyk studied computer science and mathematics at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). He received a PhD in computer science from [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) in 2009[^o3] and completed his habilitation in computer science at the [University of Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Innsbruck) in 2016.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Kaliszyk held postdoctoral positions at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) and the [University of Tsukuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tsukuba) before joining the University of Innsbruck in 2012. In 2024, he joined the University of Melbourne as a professor of theoretical computer science.

In 2016, Kaliszyk received a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Starting Grant for the SMART project (*Strong Modular proof Assistance: Reasoning across Theories*).[^o6] The project investigated automated proof assistance and methods for combining automated reasoning with machine learning.[^o7] Kaliszyk was involved in [EuroProofNet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroProofNet), the European Research Network on Formal Proofs, and led its working group on machine learning in proofs.[^o8] In 2025, Kaliszyk received an AI for Math grant from Renaissance Philanthropy for research on artificial intelligence systems for mathematical and symbolic reasoning.[^o9]
