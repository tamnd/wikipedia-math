# Ana Cannas da Silva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ana Cannas da Silva"}

*Image: Ana cannas da silva96.jpg.* Ana Cannas da Silva in Berkeley, 1996

**Ana M. L. G. Cannas da Silva** (born 1968) is a Portuguese mathematician specializing in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732). She works in Switzerland as a professor in mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Cannas was born in [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon). After studying at [St. John de Britto College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John_de_Britto_College), she earned a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciatura) degree in mathematics in 1990 from the [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Superior_Técnico) in the [University of Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lisbon). She then went to the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) for graduate studies, earning a master's degree in 1994 and completing her Ph.D. in 1996. Her dissertation, *Multiplicity Formulas for Orbifolds*, was supervised by [Victor Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636).

## Career {#Career}

After a temporary position as Morrey Assistant Professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), Cannas returned to the Instituto Superior Técnico as a faculty member in 1997. She took a second position as a senior lecturer and research scholar in mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2006, keeping at the same time her position at the Instituto Superior Técnico. In 2011 she moved from Princeton and the Instituto Superior Técnico to ETH Zurich.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2009, the alumni of St. John de Britto College awarded Cannas their José Carlos Belchior Prize in honor of her achievements as an alumna of the school.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Cannas is married to mathematician [Rahul Pandharipande](wikipedia://en/8950386).[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

Cannas is the author or coauthor of:

- *Geometric Models for Noncommutative Algebras* (with [Alan Weinstein](wikipedia://en/7344764), Amer. Math. Soc., 1999)
- *Lectures on Symplectic Geometry* (Springer, 2001)
- *Introduction to Symplectic and Hamiltonian Geometry* (Publ. Mat. IMPA, 2003)
- *Symplectic Geometry of Integrable Hamiltonian Systems* (with [Michèle Audin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michèle_Audin) and Eugene Lerman, Birkhäuser 2003)
