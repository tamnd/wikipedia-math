# Charlie Lee (computer scientist)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charlie Lee"}

**Charlie Lee** (born 1977[^o3]) is an American [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), best known as the creator of [Litecoin](wikipedia://en/37395085). He serves as the managing director of the Litecoin Foundation.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Lee was born in [Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_Coast) to ethnic Chinese parents.[^o3] He moved to the United States at the age of 13, and graduated from high school in 1995.[^o7] He graduated from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) with bachelor's and master's degrees in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) in 2000.[^o7]

Lee's brother, Bobby C. Lee, is the founder and CEO of cryptocurrency exchange [BTC China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTCC_%28company%29).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

For a decade in the 2000s, Lee worked for [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google). His work for the company included writing code for [ChromeOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChromeOS).[^o10] In 2011, Lee became interested in [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265).[^o11] In October 2011, he released [Litecoin](wikipedia://en/37395085) on [Bitcointalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcointalk).[^o12] He had written the [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971) technology based on Bitcoin in his spare time while employed at Google. He released Litecoin to the public after mining only 150 coins.[^o10] Lee has stated that he did not intend to compete with Bitcoin but meant Litecoin to be used for smaller transactions.[^o11]

In July 2013, Lee left Google and began working at [Coinbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coinbase), before the [cryptocurrency exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_exchange) adopted the coin he had created.[^o10] Lee held the position of engineering director until 2017.[^o13]

In December 2017, Lee announced on [Reddit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddit) that he sold almost all of his Litecoin holdings due to a perceived conflict of interest.[^o14] He had been criticized for his tweets, which had a possible effect on the price of the coin.[^o15] Lee sold or donated all of his coins except for a few minted in physical form, which he kept as collectibles.[^o16][^o17]

Lee is currently working full-time with the Litecoin Foundation on fostering Litecoin adoption.[^o18] In June 2025, it was announced that Lee had joined the advisory board of digital infrastructure and technology company Luxxfolio.[^o19]
