# Cyrillic Projector

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyrillic Projector"}

The ***Cyrillic Projector*** is a [sculpture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sculpture) created by American artist [Jim Sanborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Sanborn) in the early 1990s, and purchased by the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Charlotte) in 1997. It is currently installed between the campus's Friday and Fretwell Buildings.

:::

## Related sculptures {#Related_sculptures}

The [encrypted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encrypted) sculpture *Cyrillic Projector* is part of an encrypted family of three intricate puzzle-sculptures by Sanborn, the other two named *[Kryptos](wikipedia://en/456619)* and *[Antipodes](wikipedia://en/30304538)*. The *Kryptos* sculpture (located at [CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency) headquarters in [Langley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley,_Virginia), [Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia)) has text which is duplicated on *Antipodes.* *Antipodes* has two sides — one with the [Latin alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_alphabet) and one with [Cyrillic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic_script). The Latin side is similar to *Kryptos*. The Cyrillic side is similar to the *Cyrillic Projector*.

## Solution {#Solution}

The encrypted text of the *Cyrillic Projector* was first reportedly solved by Frank Corr in early July 2003, followed by an equivalent decryption by Mike Bales in September of the same year. Both endeavors gave results in the Russian language. The first English translation of the text was led by [Elonka Dunin](wikipedia://en/32737227).[^o4][^o5]

The sculpture includes two messages. The first is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) text that explains the use of [psychological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological) control to develop and maintain potential sources of information. The second is a partial quote about the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [dissident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissident), Nobel Peace Prize awarded scientist [Sakharov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Sakharov). The text is from a [classified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information) [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB) memo, detailing concerns that his report at the 1982 [Pugwash conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pugwash_conference) was going to be used by the U.S. for [anti-Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Soviet) [propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda) purposes.
