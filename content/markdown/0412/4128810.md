# Homeotopy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Homeotopy"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), an area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **homeotopy group** of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is a [homotopy group](wikipedia://en/297506) of the group of [self-homeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/13660) of that space.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The [homotopy group](wikipedia://en/297506) [functors](wikipedia://en/10987) $\pi_k$ assign to each [path-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path-connected) topological space $X$ the group $\pi_k(X)$ of [homotopy classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_class) of continuous maps $S^k\to X.$

Another construction on a space $X$ is the [group of all self-homeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/1531409) $X \to X$, denoted ${\rm Homeo}(X).$ If *X* is a [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact), [locally connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_connected) [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) then a fundamental result of [R. Arens](wikipedia://en/1794523) says that ${\rm Homeo}(X)$ will in fact be a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) under the [compact-open topology](wikipedia://en/726335).

Under the above assumptions, the **homeotopy** groups for $X$ are defined to be:

$$HME_k(X)=\pi_k({\rm Homeo}(X)).$$

Thus $HME_0(X)=\pi_0({\rm Homeo}(X))=MCG^*(X)$ is the [mapping class group](wikipedia://en/793295) for $X.$ In other words, the mapping class group is the set of connected components of ${\rm Homeo}(X)$ as specified by the functor $\pi_0.$

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

According to the [Dehn-Nielsen theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehn-Nielsen_theorem), if $X$ is a closed surface then $HME_0(X)={\rm Out}(\pi_1(X)),$ i.e., the zeroth homotopy group of the automorphisms of a space is the same as the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) of its [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004).

:::
