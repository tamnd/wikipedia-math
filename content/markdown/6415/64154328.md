# Ietje Paalman-de Miranda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ietje Paalman-de Miranda"}

**Aïda Beatrijs “Ietje” Paalman-de Miranda** (20 February 1936 – 11 May 2020) was a [Surinamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname)-born [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor).

:::

She was born in [Uitvlugt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flora,_Suriname#Uitvlugt), [Paramaribo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramaribo).[^o3] When she was 17 years old she moved from [Suriname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname) to the Netherlands to study mathematics at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). In that era, it was very unusual to study mathematics and she was the only woman at the [faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_%28division%29). She graduated [cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cum_laude) on 23 November 1960. She started a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) with [Johannes de Groot](wikipedia://en/6022862) as her supervisor. She defended her PhD thesis "Topological Semigroups" and obtained her degree in 1960, also [cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cum_laude). In 1980 she became a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in pure mathematics, becoming the first female full professor of mathematics in Amsterdam.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Paalman's research was foremost in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and set theory. She was the PhD advisor of three students, co-advised by [Jan van Mill](https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/j.vanmill/).[^o6] She published a book (*Topological semigroups - Mathematical Centre Tracts*, 1964, Mathematisch Centrum, Amsterdam) and 11 research papers about W-groups, topological representations of semi-groups and about compact groups.[^o7] Paalman was also the author of numerous lecture notes for the courses she taught at the Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics at the University of Amsterdam.

## Personal {#Personal}

She married Dolf Paalman (chief [pharmacist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacist)). They had two children and three grand children.[^o4]
