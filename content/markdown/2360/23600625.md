# Lawson topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawson topology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392), the **Lawson topology**, named after Jimmie D. Lawson, is a [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) on [partially ordered sets](wikipedia://en/23572) (posets) used in the study of [domain theory](wikipedia://en/325077). The **lower topology** on a poset *P* is generated by the [subbasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subbasis) consisting of all complements of principal [filters](wikipedia://en/19719) on *P*. The Lawson topology on *P* is the smallest common refinement of the lower topology and the [Scott topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_topology) on *P*.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- If *P* is a [complete](wikipedia://en/755647#Complete_semilattices) upper [semilattice](wikipedia://en/755647), the Lawson topology on *P* is always a [complete](wikipedia://en/27351720) [T<sub>1</sub> topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T1_topology).
