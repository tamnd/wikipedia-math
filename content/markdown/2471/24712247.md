# Ya'ish ibn Ibrahim al-Umawi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ya'ish ibn Ibrahim al-Umawi"}

**Abū ʿAbdallāh Yaʿīsh ibn Ibrāhīm ibn Yūsuf ibn Simāk al-Andalusī al-Umawī** (يعيش بن إبراهيم بن يوسف بن سماك الأموي الأندلسي) (1400? in [Al-Andalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Andalus) – 1489 in [Damascus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damascus), [Mamluk Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamluk_Sultanate)) was a 15th-century Spanish-[Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab)[^o2] [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Works {#Works}

- *Marasim al-intisab fi'ilm al-hisab* ("On arithmetical rules and procedures"), first date written in 1373 and hence the birth date above is controversial.
- *Raf'al-ishkal fi ma'rifat al-ashkal* (a work on mensuration).
