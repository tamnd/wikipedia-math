# Daniel Friedrich Hecht

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Friedrich Hecht"}

**Daniel Friedrich Hecht** (8 July 1777 – 13 March 1833) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sosa,_Germany).[^o3] He was a mine manager, then a [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher) and finally a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He is most notable for writing [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school) [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on math and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He died in [Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxony).

:::
