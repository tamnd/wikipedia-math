# Marot ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marot ring"}

In mathematics, a **Marot ring** ( , ), introduced by (Marot 1969), is a commutative ring whose [regular ideals](wikipedia://en/24159446) are generated by regular elements.
