# Atiyah conjecture on configurations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Atiyah conjecture on configurations"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Atiyah conjecture on configurations** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) introduced by (txt) stating that a certain [*n* by *n*](wikipedia://en/166022) [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) depending on *n* points in **R**<sup>3</sup> is always [non-singular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-singular_matrix).

:::
