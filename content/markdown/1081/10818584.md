# Gabino Barreda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabino Barreda"}

*Image: Gabino Barreda.jpg.* Gabino Barreda.

*Image: Spomen-poprsje u meksičkome glavnom gradu.jpg.* Bust in Mexico City

**Gabino Barreda** (1818, [Puebla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puebla) – 1881, [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City)) was a Mexican [medical doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_doctor), [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and politician oriented to French [positivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivism). He served in both the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_Republic_%28Mexico%29) and the [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_%28Mexico%29).

:::

After participating in the [Mexican–American War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican–American_War) defending his country as a volunteer, he studied medicine in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) (1847–51). There he became acquainted with [Auguste Comte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Comte)'s doctrine of [positivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivism), before his first publications in philosophy. Upon returning to Mexico City, he introduced the positivistic school and taught in [Guanajuato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanajuato) (1863–67) until the fall of [Maximilian's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximilian_I_of_Mexico) empire.

In 1867, he headed the educational commission of President [Benito Juárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benito_Juárez), where he was able to implement Comte's positivism in higher education.[^o2] The commission established the [National Preparatory School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Preparatory_School) (*Escuela Nacional Preparatoria* (ENP)), where he served as director for a decade.[^o3] Because Barreda was closely associated with Juárez and his successor following his death, [Sebastián Lerdo de Tejada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastián_Lerdo_de_Tejada), the [Porfirio Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porfirio_Díaz) regime forced him out from the ENP in 1878, to be Mexican ambassador to the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire),[^o4] a prestigious post, but far from the action of Mexican power. Barreda influenced many traditionalist thinkers during the [Porfirian regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porfiriato).

To this day, he is acknowledged as an important figure of [education in Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Mexico), one of the fathers of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) and the undergraduate medal for the top student of each major's class is named in his honor.
