# Pentahexagonal pyritoheptacontatetrahedron

*Image: Pyritohedral\_near-miss\_johnson-polydron.jpg.* Model built with [polydron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polydron)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentahexagonal pyritoheptacontatetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentahexagonal pyritoheptacontatetrahedron** is a [near-miss Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/3881930) with [pyritohedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyritohedral_symmetry). This near-miss was discovered by Mason Green in 2006. It has 6 [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces, 12 [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) faces, and 56 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) in 3 symmetry positions. Mason calls it a *hexagonally expanded [snubbed](wikipedia://en/9428917) dodecahedron*.[^o3]

:::

With regular hexagons and pentagons it is a [symmetrohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetrohedron).[^o4] The triangles are not [equilateral](wikipedia://en/173285), with triangle-triangle edges compressed by 1.8%.

It has 3 vertex configurations, 3.3.5.6, 3.5.3.6, 3.3.3.3.5, with the last shared in the [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137).

## Dual Polyhedron {#Dual_Polyhedron}

The dual of the *pentahexagonal pyritoheptacontatetrahedron* is made of kites, quadrilaterals, and pentagonal faces. It has 60 faces.
