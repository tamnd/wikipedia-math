# Peter Lorimer (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Lorimer"}

**Peter James Lorimer** (16 April 1939 – 7 February 2010) was a New Zealand mathematician. His research concerned [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Born in [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch), Lorimer did a [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSc) / [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSc) in mathematics at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland) and won a [Commonwealth Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Scholarship) to do a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal), which he completed in 1963 under the supervision of [Hans Schwerdtfeger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Schwerdtfeger). He returned to New Zealand to lecture, first at [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) and then at [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).[^o5][^o6]
