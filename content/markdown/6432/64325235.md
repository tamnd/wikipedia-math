# Nagambal Shah

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nagambal Shah"}

**Nagambal D.** "**Swarna**" **Shah** is an American mathematician and statistician known for her mentorship of students at [Spelman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spelman_College). She is the founder of the annual StatFest of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), a leader of the association's Diversity Mentoring Program, and the former chair of the association's Committee on Minorities in Statistics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Shah is originally from India, where she did her undergraduate studies in mathematics and earned a master's degree in statistics. She completed a Ph.D. in statistics in 1970 at the [University of Windsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Windsor) in Canada.

She joined the Spelman College mathematics department in 1972, and retired to become a professor emerita in 2014.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2001, the [Rollins School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollins_School_of_Public_Health) of [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University) gave Shah their Martin Luther King Jr. Community Service Award. Shah was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2010. The [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356) gave her their Lifetime Achievement Award in 2017.. The [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) gave her their Lifetime Mentor Award in 2023..
