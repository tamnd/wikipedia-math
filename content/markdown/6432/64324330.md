# Paul McNicholas (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul McNicholas"}

**Paul D. McNicholas** is an Irish-Canadian statistician. He is a professor and University Scholar in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University).[^o4] In 2015, McNicholas was awarded the Tier 1 Canada Research Chair in Computational Statistics.[^o5] McNicholas uses computational statistics techniques, and mixture models in particular, to gain insight into large and complex datasets.[^o6] He is editor-in-chief of the [Journal of Classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Classification).[^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

McNicholas attended [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) where in 2004 he received a B.A. (Mod.) in Mathematics. He also holds an M.Sc. in High Performance Computing and a Ph.D. in Statistics.[^o9] His 2007 Ph.D. thesis is entitled *Topics in Unsupervised Learning*.[^o10]

McNicholas started his faculty career at University of Guelph in 2007. In 2014, he joined McMaster University.[^o9] He has authored more than 100 scientific works cited over 4000 times.[^o11] The majority of his research has been on [model-based clustering](wikipedia://en/76187094), specifically in developing novel finite [mixture models](wikipedia://en/1579244) for [clustering](wikipedia://en/669675) and [classification](wikipedia://en/1579244) of [multivariate](wikipedia://en/19384) data. He has published works on clustering high-dimensional data and the use of non-Gaussian mixtures.[^o12] McNicholas has published two monographs: *Mixture Model-Based Classification*[^o13] and *Data Science with Julia*.[^o14]
