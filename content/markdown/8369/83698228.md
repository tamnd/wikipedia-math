# Mark S. Handcock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mark S. Handcock"}

**Mark Stephen Handcock** is an Australian-American statistician. He is Distinguished Professor of Statistics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o3] His research focuses on statistical problems motivated by the social sciences, particularly statistical models for social networks, including exponential random graph models (ERGMs)[^o4] and latent space models,[^o5] as well as demography, spatial statistics,[^o6] social epidemiology,[^o7] and environmental statistics[^o8]

:::
