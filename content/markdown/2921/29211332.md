# Mihail Zervos

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mihail Zervos"}

**Mihail Zervos** is a Greek financial mathematician. He is [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Financial Mathematics at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).

:::

## Curriculum {#Curriculum}

Zervos received his MSc and PhD degrees from [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College) London in 1995. After completing his PhD, he was a lecturer at the Department of Statistics, [University of Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_University), where he stayed until 2000. He then joined [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), initially as a lecturer and then as a reader in the Department of Mathematics. In 2006 he was appointed to the chair in Financial Mathematics at the London School of Economics where he was tasked with founding a new Research Group in Financial Mathematics within the Departement of Mathematics.
