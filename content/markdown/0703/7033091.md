# Dodecahedral conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dodecahedral conjecture"}

The **dodecahedral conjecture** in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) is intimately related to [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621).

:::

[László Fejes Tóth](wikipedia://en/2388442), a 20th-century Hungarian geometer, considered the [Voronoi decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_decomposition) of any given packing of [unit spheres](wikipedia://en/27033090). He conjectured in 1943 that the minimal volume of any cell in the resulting Voronoi decomposition was at least as large as the volume of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) circumscribed to a unit sphere.[^o2]

[Thomas Callister Hales](wikipedia://en/2388356) and [Sean McLaughlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_McLaughlin_%28mathematician%29) proved the conjecture in 1998,[^o3] following the same strategy that led Hales to his proof of the [Kepler conjecture](wikipedia://en/368311). The proofs rely on extensive computations. McLaughlin was awarded the 1999 [Morgan Prize](wikipedia://en/4180157) for his contribution to this proof.
