# Schreier conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schreier conjecture"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/323707), the **Schreier conjecture** asserts that the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) of every [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600) is [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012). It was proposed by [Otto Schreier](wikipedia://en/4355201) in 1926, and is now known to be true as a result of the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445), but no simpler [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) is known.

:::
