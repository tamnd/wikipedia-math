# Results in Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Results in Mathematics"}

***Results in Mathematics/Resultate der Mathematik*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that covers all aspects of pure and applied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and is published by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser). It was established in 1978 and the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Catalin Badea ([University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille)).

:::
