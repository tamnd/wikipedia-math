# Fernando Q. Gouvêa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fernando Q. Gouvêa"}

**Fernando Quadros Gouvêa** is a Brazilian [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). He is the Carter Professor of Mathematics at [Colby College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colby_College) in [Waterville, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterville,_Maine). He won the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) in 1995 for his exposition of [Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/21950759). He also won the Beckenbach Book Prize of the MAA in 2007 for his book with William P. Berlinghoff, *Math through the Ages: A Gentle History for Teachers and Others* (Oxton House, 2002; 2nd ed., 2014).

:::

Gouvêa grew up in São Paulo, the son of a lawyer and banker, and was educated there in an English-language primary school and then at the [Colégio Bandeirantes de São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colégio_Bandeirantes_de_São_Paulo). He earned a bachelor's degree from the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo), and then a master's degree in 1981 under the supervision of César Polcino Milies. He moved to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1983 for continuing graduate study in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and completed his doctorate there in 1987; his dissertation, titled *Arithmetic of* $p$-*adic Modular Forms*, was supervised by [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679).

He became a faculty member at the University of São Paulo, took a visiting position at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario) in 1990, and was brought to Colby College by [Keith Devlin](wikipedia://en/574895), who had recently been hired as department chair there.

He is the editor of the *[Carus Mathematical Monographs](wikipedia://en/2941952)* book series, and of *MAA Reviews*, an online book review service published by the MAA.
