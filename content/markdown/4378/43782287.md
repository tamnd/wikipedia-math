# Permutation model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Permutation model"}

In mathematical [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), a **permutation model** is a [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28mathematical_logic%29) of set theory with [atoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom_%28set_theory%29) (ZFA) constructed using a [group](wikipedia://en/24634) of [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of the atoms. A **symmetric model** is similar except that it is a model of ZF (without atoms) and is constructed using a group of permutations of a [forcing poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forcing_poset). One application is to show the independence of the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840) from the other axioms of ZFA or ZF. Permutation models were introduced by (txt) and developed further by (txt). Symmetric models were introduced by [Paul Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Cohen_%28mathematician%29).

:::

## Construction of permutation models {#Construction_of_permutation_models}

Suppose that *A* is a set of atoms, and *G* is a group of permutations of *A*. A **normal filter** of *G* is a collection *F* of subgroups of *G* such that

- *G* is in *F*
- The intersection of two elements of *F* is in *F*
- Any subgroup containing an element of *F* is in *F*
- Any [conjugate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_subgroup) of an element of *F* is in *F*
- The subgroup fixing any element of *A* is in *F*.

If *V* is a model of ZFA with *A* the set of atoms, then an element of *V* is called symmetric if the subgroup fixing it is in *F*, and is called hereditarily symmetric if it and all elements of its transitive closure are symmetric. The **permutation model** consists of all hereditarily symmetric elements, and is a model of ZFA.

## Construction of filters on a group {#Construction_of_filters_on_a_group}

A filter on a group can be constructed from an invariant ideal on of the [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844) of subsets of *A* containing all elements of *A*. Here an ideal is a collection *I* of subsets of *A* closed under taking finite unions and subsets, and is called invariant if it is invariant under the action of the group *G*. For each element *S* of the ideal one can take the subgroup of *G* consisting of all elements fixing every element *S*. These subgroups generate a normal filter of *G*.
