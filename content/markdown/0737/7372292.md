# Taro Morishima

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taro Morishima"}

**Taro Morishima** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) who attended [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan). Morishima published at least thirteen papers, including his work on [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953).[^o2] and a collected works volume published in 1990 after his death.[^o3] He also corresponded several times with [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) mathematician [H. S. Vandiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Vandiver).[^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Morishima's Theorem on FLT"}

## Morishima's Theorem on FLT {#Morishima's_Theorem_on_FLT}

Let m be a prime number not exceeding 31. Let *p* be prime, and let *x*, *y*, *z* be [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) such that *x*<sup>*p*</sup> + *y*<sup>*p*</sup> + *z*<sup>*p*</sup> = 0. Assume that *p* does not divide the [product](wikipedia://en/24977) *xyz*. Then, *p*² must divide m<sup>*p* − 1</sup>-1.

:::
