# De Branges space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Branges space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **de Branges space** (sometimes written **De Branges space**) is a concept in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and is constructed from a **de Branges function**.

:::

The concept is named after [Louis de Branges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Branges) who proved numerous results regarding these spaces, especially as [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932), and used those results to prove the [Bieberbach conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieberbach_conjecture).

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="De Branges function"}

## De Branges functions {#De_Branges_functions}

A **Hermite-Biehler function**, also known as **de Branges function**, is an [entire function](wikipedia://en/10372) *E* from $\mathbb{C}$ to $\mathbb{C}$ that satisfies the inequality $|E(z)| > |E(\bar z)|$, for all *z* in the [upper half of the complex plane](wikipedia://en/287826) $\mathbb{C}^+ = \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$.

:::

## Definition 1 {#Definition_1}

Given a Hermite-Biehler function $E$, the de Branges space $B(E)$ is defined as the set of all [entire functions](wikipedia://en/10372) *F* such that [^o5]

$$B(E) = \bigg \{ F \text{ is entire } \bigg | \frac{F}{E}, \frac{F^{\#}}{E} \in H_2(\mathbb{C}^+), \int_{\mathbb{R}} \bigg | \frac{F(\lambda)}{E(\lambda)} \bigg |^2 \mathrm{d}\lambda < \infty \bigg \}$$

where:

- $\mathbb{C}^+ = \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$ is the open upper half of the complex plane.
- $$F^{\#}(z) = \overline{F(\bar z)}.$$
- $H_2(\mathbb{C}^+)$ is the usual [Hardy space](wikipedia://en/314780) on the open upper half plane.

## Definition 2 {#Definition_2}

A de Branges space can also be defined as all entire functions $F$ satisfying all of the following conditions:

- $$\int_{\mathbb{R}} |(F/E)(\lambda)|^2 d\lambda < \infty$$
- $$|(F/E)(z)|,|(F^{\#}/E)(z)| \leq C_F(\operatorname{Im}(z))^{(-1/2)}, \forall z \in \mathbb{C}^+$$

## Definition 3 {#Definition_3}

There exists also an axiomatic description, useful in operator theory.

## As Hilbert spaces {#As_Hilbert_spaces}

Given a de Branges space $B(E)$. Define the scalar product:

$$[F,G]=\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{\mathbb{R}} \overline{F(\lambda)} G(\lambda) \frac{d\lambda}{|E(\lambda)|^2}.$$

A de Branges space with such a scalar product can be proven to be a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932).
