# Modal algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modal algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), a **modal algebra** is a structure $\langle A,\land,\lor,-,0,1,\Box\rangle$ such that

:::

- $\langle A,\land,\lor,-,0,1\rangle$ is a [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959),
- $\Box$ is a unary operation on *A* satisfying $\Box1=1$ and $\Box(x\land y)=\Box x\land\Box y$ for all *x*, *y* in *A*.

Modal algebras provide models of [propositional](wikipedia://en/18154) [modal logics](wikipedia://en/333365) in the same way as Boolean algebras are models of [classical logic](wikipedia://en/55868). In particular, the [variety](wikipedia://en/1021753) of all modal algebras is the equivalent algebraic semantics of the modal logic *K* in the sense of [abstract algebraic logic](wikipedia://en/4613861), and the [lattice](wikipedia://en/469304) of its subvarieties is dually [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the lattice of [normal modal logics](wikipedia://en/911820).

[Stone's representation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone's_representation_theorem) can be generalized to the [Jónsson–Tarski duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jónsson–Tarski_duality), which ensures that each modal algebra can be [represented](wikipedia://en/4783135) as the algebra of admissible sets in a modal [general frame](wikipedia://en/6933049).

A **Magari algebra** (or **diagonalizable algebra**) is a modal algebra satisfying $\Box (-\Box x \lor x) = \Box x$. Magari algebras correspond to [provability logic](wikipedia://en/618119) and are named after Roberto Magari.
