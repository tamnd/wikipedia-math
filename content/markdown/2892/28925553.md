# Dirac spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dirac spectrum"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Dirac spectrum**, named after [Paul Dirac](wikipedia://en/24742), is the spectrum of [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of a [Dirac operator](wikipedia://en/1079448) on a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) with a [spin structure](wikipedia://en/2844303). The [isospectral problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isospectral_problem) for the Dirac spectrum asks whether two Riemannian spin manifolds have identical spectra. The Dirac spectrum depends on the [spin structure](wikipedia://en/2844303) in the sense that there exists a Riemannian manifold with two different spin structures that have different Dirac spectra.[^o2]

:::
