# Winnie Li

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Winnie Li"}

**Wen-Ch'ing (Winnie) Li** (; born December 25, 1948) is a Taiwanese-American mathematician and a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o3] She is a [number theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theorist), with research focusing on the theory of [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms) and applications of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) to [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869) and [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914). In particular, she has applied her research results in automorphic forms and number theory to construct efficient communication networks called [Ramanujan graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_graphs) and Ramanujan complexes.

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Li graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematics in 1970.[^o3][^o7] As an undergraduate, she was a classmate of mathematicians [Fan Chung](wikipedia://en/246562), [Sun-Yung Alice Chang](wikipedia://en/24637925) and [Jang-Mei Wu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jang-Mei_Wu).[^o5] She then earned her [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D). in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 1974, under the supervision of [Andrew Ogg](wikipedia://en/31047928).[^o3][^o7]

Before joining faculty of Pennsylvania State University in 1979, she was a Benjamin Pierce assistant professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) for 3.5 years from 1974 to 1977, and a tenure-track assistant professor at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) from 1978 to 1979.[^o3][^o7] She was also the director of the National Center of Theoretical Sciences in [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) from 2009 to 2014.[^o3][^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2010, Li was the winner of the [Chern Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_Prize_%28ICCM%29), given every three years to an outstanding Chinese mathematician.[^o9] In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] She was chosen to give the 2015 [Noether Lecture](wikipedia://en/11566873).[^o11]
