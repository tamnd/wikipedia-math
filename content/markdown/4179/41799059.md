# Harder–Narasimhan stratification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harder–Narasimhan stratification"}

In algebraic geometry and [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), the **Harder–Narasimhan stratification** is any of a stratification of the [moduli stack of principal *G*-bundles](wikipedia://en/41790574) by locally closed substacks in terms of "loci of instabilities". In the original form due to [Harder and Narasimhan](wikipedia://en/18909129), *G* was the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564); i.e., the moduli stack was the [moduli stack of vector bundles](wikipedia://en/54274216), but, today, the term refers to any of generalizations. The scheme-theoretic version is due to Shatz and so the term "Shatz stratification" is also used synonymously. The general case is due to [Behrend](wikipedia://en/31872403).[^n1][^o3]

:::
