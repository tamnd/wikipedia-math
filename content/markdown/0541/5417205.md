# Australian Statistician

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Australian Statistician"}

The **Australian Statistician** is the head of the [Australian Bureau of Statistics](wikipedia://en/4732815).

:::

On 18 June 1906, the first *Statistician of the Commonwealth of Australia* was appointed to carry out the provisions of the *Census and Statistics Act 1905*. Later in the same year the Commonwealth Bureau of Census and Statistics was formed (renamed the Australian Bureau of Statistics in 1975).[^o2]

[Timothy Augustine Coghlan](wikipedia://en/9641926) was offered the position in December 1905, but had to decline due to his obligations to the New South Wales government.[^o3]

## Commonwealth Statisticians {#Commonwealth_Statisticians}

- [George Handley Knibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Handley_Knibbs) (1906–1921[^o5])
- [Charles Henry Wickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Henry_Wickens) (August 1922 – April 1932,[^o6] although [Lyndhurst Falkiner Giblin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyndhurst_Falkiner_Giblin) was appointed acting Commonwealth Statistician following Wickens' stroke in 1931[^o7])
- [Edward Tannock McPhee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Tannock_McPhee) (1933–1936)[^o7]
- [Sir Roland Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_Wilson_%28economist%29) (1936–1940; 1946–1951)[^o7]
- Sir [Stanley Roy Carver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Roy_Carver) (acting from 1940[^o8] to 1946, and again from 1948 to 1951. Formally appointed Commonwealth Statistician from 20 August 1957 to 1961[^o7] or 6 February 1962[^o8])
- [Keith Archer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Archer) (1962–1970)[^o7]
- [Jack O'Neill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_O'Neill_%28statistician%29) (acting from 1969–1972. Commonwealth Statistician from 1972–1975)
