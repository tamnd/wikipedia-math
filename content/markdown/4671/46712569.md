# Corinna Ulcigrai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Corinna Ulcigrai"}

*Image: Corinna Ulcigrai on Chaotic Properties of Area Preserving Flows in 2019.jpg.* Ulcigrai lectures in 2019

**Corinna Ulcigrai** (born 3 January 1980, [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste))[^o3] is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o2] With Krzysztof Frączek in 2013, Ulcigrai is known for proving that in the [Ehrenfest model](wikipedia://en/18986538) (a mathematical abstraction of billiards with an infinite array of rectangular obstacles, used to model gas diffusion) most trajectories are not [ergodic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergodic).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ulcigrai obtained her Ph.D. in 2007 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) with [Yakov Sinai](wikipedia://en/1751626) as her thesis advisor.[^o2] She has worked at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol), [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).[^o8] and is currently a professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ulcigrai was awarded the [European Mathematical Society Prize](wikipedia://en/969470#Prizes) in 2012,[^o2][^o7] and the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2013.[^o8]

In 2020, Ulcigrai was the winner of the [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601), "for her fundamental work on the ergodic theory of locally Hamiltonian flows on surfaces, of translation flows on periodic surfaces and wind-tree models, and her seminal work on higher genus generalizations of Markov and Lagrange spectra".[^o9]
