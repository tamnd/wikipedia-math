# Fubini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fubini"}

**Fubini** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [David Fubini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fubini), American business lecturer and writer, son of Eugene Fubini
- [Eugene Fubini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Fubini) (1913–1997), American defense official, son of Guido Fubini, and father of David Fubini
- [Guido Fubini](wikipedia://en/349051) (1879–1943), Italian mathematician, father of Eugene Fubini
- [Sergio Fubini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Fubini) (1928–2005), Italian theoretical physicist
