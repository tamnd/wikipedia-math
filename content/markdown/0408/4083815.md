# Aleksandr Korkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksandr Korkin"}

**Aleksandr Nikolayevich Korkin** (Александр Николаевич Коркин; 3 March 1837 – 1 September 1908) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made contribution to the development of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753), and was second only to [Chebyshev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev) among the founders of the Saint Petersburg Mathematical School.[^o3] Among others, his students included [Yegor Ivanovich Zolotarev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yegor_Ivanovich_Zolotarev).

:::
