# Fitting's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fitting's theorem"}

**Fitting's theorem** is a [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) proved by [Hans Fitting](wikipedia://en/6069614).[^o2] It can be stated as follows:

:::

If *M* and *N* are [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/144569) [normal subgroups](wikipedia://en/21918) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G*, then their product *MN* is also a nilpotent normal subgroup of *G*; if, moreover, *M* is nilpotent of class *m* and *N* is nilpotent of class *n*, then *MN* is nilpotent of class at most *m* + *n*.[^o3]

By [induction](wikipedia://en/18881) it follows also that the subgroup generated by a finite collection of nilpotent normal subgroups is nilpotent. This can be used to show that the [Fitting subgroup](wikipedia://en/1416042) of certain types of groups (including all [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707)) is nilpotent. However, a subgroup generated by an *infinite* collection of nilpotent normal subgroups need not be nilpotent.[^o4]
