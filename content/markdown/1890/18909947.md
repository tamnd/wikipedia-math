# Information source (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Information source"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **information source** is a sequence of [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685) ranging over a [finite alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_%28computer_science%29) Γ, having a [stationary distribution](wikipedia://en/2524727).

:::

The **uncertainty**, or **[entropy rate](wikipedia://en/11071463)**, of an information source is defined as

$$H\{\mathbf{X}\} = \lim_{n\to\infty} H(X_n | X_0, X_1, \dots, X_{n-1})$$

where

$$X_0, X_1, \dots, X_n$$

is the sequence of random variables defining the information source, and

$$H(X_n | X_0, X_1, \dots, X_{n-1})$$

is the conditional [information entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_entropy) of the sequence of random variables. Equivalently, one has

$$H\{\mathbf{X}\} = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{H(X_0, X_1, \dots, X_{n-1}, X_n)}{n+1}.$$
