# Archibald Read Richardson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archibald Read Richardson"}

**Archibald Read Richardson** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society)[^o3] (21 August 1881 – 4 November 1954) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Richardson collaborated with [Dudley E. Littlewood](wikipedia://en/7603792) on invariants and group representation theory. They introduced [the immanant of a matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_immanant_of_a_matrix), studied [Schur functions](wikipedia://en/3147062) and developed the [Littlewood–Richardson rule](wikipedia://en/16004359) for their multiplication.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Richardson was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] on 21 March 1946.
