# Dudley E. Littlewood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dudley E. Littlewood"}

*Image: Dudley E. Littlewood.jpg.* Dudley E. Littlewood

**Dudley Ernest Littlewood** (7 September 1903, London – 6 October 1979, [Llandudno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llandudno)) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [group representation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_representation_theory).

:::

He read mathematics at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where his tutor was [John Edensor Littlewood](wikipedia://en/158371) (they were not related). He was a lecturer at [University College, Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Swansea) from 1928 to 1947, and in 1948 took up the chair of mathematics at [University College of North Wales, Bangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_North_Wales,_Bangor), retiring in 1970.

He worked on invariant theory and group representation theory, especially of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901), often in collaboration with [Archibald Read Richardson](wikipedia://en/7605515) of Swansea. They introduced [the immanant of a matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_immanant_of_a_matrix), studied [Schur functions](wikipedia://en/3147062) and developed the [Littlewood–Richardson rule](wikipedia://en/16004359) for their multiplication. Littlewood was also interested in the application of representation theory to [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202).
