# 5-manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="5-manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **5-manifold** is a [5-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-dimensional) [topological manifold](wikipedia://en/2119193), possibly with a [piecewise linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise_linear_structure) or [smooth structure](wikipedia://en/725272).

:::

Non-[simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) 5-manifolds are impossible to classify, as this is harder than solving the [word problem for groups](wikipedia://en/33563).[^o2] Simply connected [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactness) 5-manifolds were first classified by [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294)[^o3] and then in full generality by [Dennis Barden](wikipedia://en/7705698),[^o4] while another proof was later given by Aleksey V. Zhubr.[^o5] This turns out to be easier than the 3- or 4-dimensional case: the 3-dimensional case is the [Thurston geometrisation conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston_geometrisation_conjecture), and the [4-dimensional case](wikipedia://en/1362795) was solved by [Michael Freedman](wikipedia://en/658970) (1982) in the topological case,[^o6] but is a very hard unsolved problem in the smooth case.

In dimension 5, the smooth classification of simply connected manifolds is governed by classical [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801). Namely, two simply connected, smooth 5-manifolds are diffeomorphic if and only if there exists an isomorphism of their second [homology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_group) with integer coefficients, preserving the linking form and the second [Stiefel–Whitney class](wikipedia://en/877761). Moreover, any such isomorphism in second homology is induced by some diffeomorphism. It is [undecidable](wikipedia://en/15631055) if a given 5-manifold is homeomorphic to $S^5$, the 5-sphere.[^o2]

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Here are some examples of smooth, closed, simply connected 5-manifolds:

- $S^5$, the 5-sphere.
- $S^2\times S^3$, the product of a 2-sphere with a 3-sphere.
- $S^2\widetilde{\times} S^3$, the total space of the non-trivial $S^3$-[bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) over $S^2$.
- $\operatorname{SU}(3)/\operatorname{SO}(3)$, the [Wu manifold](wikipedia://en/77123741), the [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325) obtained as the quotient of the [special unitary group](wikipedia://en/173997) SU(3) by the [rotation subgroup SO(3)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotation_group_SO%283%29).
