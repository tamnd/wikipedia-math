# Fiber bundle construction theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fiber bundle construction theorem"}

*Image: Mobius transition functions.png.* The [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) can be constructed by a non-trivial gluing of two trivial bundles on open subsets *U* and *V* of the circle *S<sup>1</sup>*. When glued trivially (with *g<sub>UV</sub>=1*) one obtains the trivial bundle, but with the non-trivial gluing of *g<sub>UV</sub>=1* on one overlap and *g<sub>UV</sub>=-1* on the second overlap, one obtains the non-trivial bundle *E*, the Möbius strip. This can be visualised as a "twisting" of one of the local charts.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **fiber bundle construction theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) which constructs a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) with a [structure group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_group) from a given base space, fiber, group, and a suitable set of [transition functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_map). The theorem also gives conditions under which two such bundles are [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic).

:::

The theorem is used in the [associated bundle](wikipedia://en/398874) construction, where one starts with a given bundle and changes just the fiber, while keeping all other data the same.

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Formal statement"}

## Formal statement {#Formal_statement}

### Existence {#Existence}

Let *X* and *F* be [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) and let *G* be a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) with a [continuous left action](wikipedia://en/7522685) on *F*. Given an [open cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_cover) {*U*<sub>*i*</sub>} of *X* and a set of [continuous functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_function_%28topology%29)

$$t_{ij} : U_i \cap U_j \to G$$

defined on each nonempty overlap, such that the *cocycle condition*

$$t_{ik}(x) = t_{ij}(x)t_{jk}(x) \qquad \forall x \in U_i \cap U_j \cap U_k$$

holds, there exists a fiber bundle *E* → *X* with fiber *F* and structure group *G* that is trivializable over {*U*<sub>*i*</sub>} with transition functions *t*<sub>*ij*</sub>.

### Isomorphism {#Isomorphism}

Let *E*′ be another fiber bundle with the same base space, fiber, structure group, and trivializing neighborhoods, but transition functions *t*′<sub>*ij*</sub>. If the action of *G* on *F* is [faithful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithful_group_action#faithful), then *E*′ and *E* are isomorphic [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) there exist functions

$$t_i : U_i \to G$$

such that

$$t'_{ij}(x) = t_i(x)^{-1}t_{ij}(x)t_j(x) \qquad \forall x \in U_i \cap U_j.$$

i.e. a [gauge transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_transformation) on transition data.

In particular, given a base, fiber, structure group, group action on the fiber, trivializing neighborhoods, and a set of transition functions, if the action is faithful, then any two fiber bundles constructed are isomorphic. To see it, use the "if" direction of the isomorphism theorem with $t_i(x) = 1_G$, where $1_G \in G$ is the identity element of $G$. In other words, the construction is [unique up to isomorphism](wikipedia://en/44787).

### Smooth category {#Smooth_category}

The above pair of theorems hold in the [topological category](wikipedia://en/435051). A similar pair of theorems hold in the [smooth category](wikipedia://en/10059981), where *X* and *Y* are [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), *G* is a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) with a smooth left action on *Y* and the maps *t*<sub>*ij*</sub> are all smooth.

:::

## Construction {#Construction}

Existence is proven [constructively](wikipedia://en/360113) by the standard [coequalizer](wikipedia://en/431940) construction in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).

Take the [disjoint union](wikipedia://en/1564380) of the [product spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_space_%28topology%29) $U_i \times F$

$$T = \coprod_{i\in I}U_i \times F = \{(i,x,y) : i\in I, x\in U_i, y\in F\}.$$

Define the [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259)

$$(j,x,y) \sim (i,x,t_{ij}(x)\cdot y)\qquad \forall x\in U_i \cap U_j, y\in F.$$

Take the [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) $E := T / \sim$, with the projection map

$$\pi : E \to X, \quad \pi([(i, x, y)]) = x$$

The local trivializations are

$$\phi_i : \pi^{-1}(U_i) \to U_i \times F, \quad \phi_i^{-1}(x,y) = [(i,x,y)].$$

:::

## Associated bundle {#Associated_bundle}

Let *E* → *X* a fiber bundle with fiber *F* and structure group *G*, and let *F*′ be another left *G*-space. One can form an associated bundle *E*′ → *X* with a fiber *F*′ and structure group *G* by taking any local trivialization of *E* and replacing *F* by *F*′ in the construction theorem. If one takes *F*′ to be *G* with the action of left multiplication then one obtains the associated [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950).
