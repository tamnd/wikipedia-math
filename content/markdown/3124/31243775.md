# Double limit theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Double limit theorem"}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), [Thurston's](wikipedia://en/39190) **double limit theorem** gives condition for a sequence of [quasi-Fuchsian groups](wikipedia://en/30960521) to have a convergent subsequence. It was introduced in (Thurston 1998, theorem 4.1) and is a major step in Thurston's proof of the [hyperbolization theorem](wikipedia://en/9155837) for the case of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) that fiber over the circle.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

By [Bers's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bers's_theorem), [quasi-Fuchsian groups](wikipedia://en/30960521) (of some fixed [genus](wikipedia://en/61891)) are parameterized by points in *T*×*T*, where *T* is [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661) of the same genus. Suppose that there is a sequence of quasi-Fuchsian groups corresponding to points (*g*<sub>*i*</sub>, *h*<sub>*i*</sub>) in *T*×*T*. Also suppose that the sequences *g*<sub>*i*</sub>, *h*<sub>*i*</sub> converge to points μ,μ in the [Thurston boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston_boundary) of Teichmüller space of projective [measured laminations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measured_lamination). If the points μ,μ have the property that any nonzero measured lamination has positive intersection number with at least one of them, then the sequence of quasi-Fuchsian groups has a subsequence that converges algebraically.

:::
