# Teichmüller modular form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Teichmüller modular form"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Teichmüller modular form** is an analogue of a [Siegel modular form](wikipedia://en/7721927) on [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661).

:::
