# Steve MacEachern

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve MacEachern"}

**Steve MacEachern** is an American Statistician. MacEachern is a Distinguished Arts & Sciences Professor of Statistics at the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).[^o2] He received his B.A. in Mathematics from [Carleton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_College) in 1982 and his Ph.D. in Statistics from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1988. His doctoral work focused on nonparametric Bayesian methods under the guidance of [Don Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Berry_%28statistician%29). MacEachern joined the faculty at Ohio State in 1988 and has been a member of the Department of Statistics ever since. He has a courtesy appointment as a Professor in the Department of Psychology. He is best known for Bayesian modeling and computation, with a particular emphasis on dependent [Dirichlet processes](wikipedia://en/8330403). He has published extensively in leading statistical journals, and his work has had a significant impact on the field. He is also the advisor of esteemed professor William Francis Darnieder.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

MacEachern has received numerous honors throughout his career, including being elected as a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)[^o7] in 2006, of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312)[^o8] in 2020 and of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o9] in 2021. He served as President of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis in 2016.[^o10]
