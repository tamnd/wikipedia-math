# Jozef Dravecký

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jozef Dravecký"}

**Jozef Dravecký** (25 July 1947 – 23 January 2023) was a Slovak mathematician and diplomat. Between 1991 and 2013, he served as the ambassador of Slovakia to Bulgaria (1993–1998), the [Baltic States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_states) (2000 – 2005) and the [Holy See](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_See) (2007–2013).[^o3]

:::

Dravecký was born in [Spišská Nová Ves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spišská_Nová_Ves). [^o4] He studied Sciences at the [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University) graduating in 1971. In 1973 – 1990 he was a professor of [Mathematical Analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the university.[^o3]

In 2010, Dravecký was awarded the [Knights Grand Cross of the Order of Pope Pius IX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Pope_Pius_IX) by the Pope [Benedict XVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI).[^o5]

His death, on 23 January 2023 at the age of 75, was announced by the Chairman of the Christian Union of Pensioners Peter Mach in a Facebook post.[^o4]
