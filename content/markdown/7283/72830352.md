# Mike West (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mike West"}

**Mike West** is an English and American statistician. West works primarily in the field of [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412), with research contributions ranging from theory to applied research in areas including finance, commerce, macroeconomics, climatology, engineering, genomics and other areas of biology.[^o2] Since 1999, West has been the Arts & Sciences Distinguished Professor of Statistics & Decision Sciences in the Department of Statistical Science at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

West earned a BSc in mathematics in 1978, and then PhD in mathematics (Statistics) in 1982, from the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham). He worked at [Warwick University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) before joining [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 1988, where he became the Director of the Institute of Statistics and Decision Sciences (ISDS)[^o5] from 1990 to 2001.

West has made significant contributions to Bayesian theory and methods for [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) analysis and forecasting.[^o6][^o7][^o8] He has also conducted research on non-parametric Bayesian analysis with methodological and applied research that include implementations of [Dirichlet process](wikipedia://en/8330403) mixture models starting with a paper published in 1995.[^o9] His research with molecular geneticists and clinical researchers involved developing predictive models for identifying types of breast cancer using gene expression data,[^o10][^o11] contributing to the popularisation of [biomarker discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomarker_discovery) through gene expression profiling.

West has been a consultant with various companies, banks, government agencies and academic centres, co-founder of a biotech company, and board member of several financial and IT companies. West has also served the international statistics profession in founding roles and as a board member in a number of national and international centres and institutes as well as professional societies.

## Elected positions & honours {#Elected_positions_&_honours}

President of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2009–2010.

Elected Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1982, of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1993, of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1993, of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1997, and elected inaugural fellow of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2012.

Founding Chair of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) (ISBA) section on Economics, Finance, and Business in 2012-2013[^o13]

Chair of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) section on Bayesian Statistical Science in 2010.
