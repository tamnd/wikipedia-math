# Universal Teichmüller space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Universal Teichmüller space"}

In mathematical complex analysis, **universal Teichmüller space** *T*(1) is a [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661) containing the Teichmüller space *T*(*G*) of every [Fuchsian group](wikipedia://en/471365) *G*. It was introduced by (txt) as the set of [boundary values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_value) of [quasiconformal maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiconformal_map) of the [upper half-plane](wikipedia://en/287826) that [fix](wikipedia://en/449738) 0, 1, and ∞.

:::
