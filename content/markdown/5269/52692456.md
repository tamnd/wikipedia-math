# Ramdas L. Bhirud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramdas L. Bhirud"}

**Ramdas L. Bhirud** (**Ramdas Lotu Bhirud** or **R. L. Bhirud**) (7 April 1937 – 4 January 1997), was an [Indian mathematician](wikipedia://en/1158496) from [Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra) region of India worked in the field of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ramdas was born on 7 April 1937, in [Chinawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinawal), Maharashtra, India. He was one of six children. He did his elementary school education in [Chinawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinawal). After that moved to [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai) for middle school and high school education. He attended [Elphinstone College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elphinstone_College) in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai) for his bachelor's degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).

## Career {#Career}

After his bachelor's degree from [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) Ramdas set out for his master's degree and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in United States. For his PhD he contributed to the theory and construction of the [Padé table](wikipedia://en/11609441). After his studies for a brief period he worked as an [Assistant Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis) before returning to India where he worked as a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [MPKV Rahuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Phule_Krishi_Vidyapeeth).
