# Anish Ghosh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anish Ghosh"}

**Anish Ghosh** is an Indian mathematician specializing in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is a Professor in the School of Mathematics of the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), and is currently Dean of the School. He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in Mathematical Sciences in the year 2021.[^o2] Anish Ghosh is also a part of the [INFOSYS-Chandrasekharan Virtual Centre for Random Geometry](https://randomgeometry.in/) which is a group of scientists at TIFR Mumbai and ICTS Bengaluru working on topics related to random geometry.[^o3]

:::

Anish Ghosh was born on 25 December 1979.[^o4] He secured a BSc degree from St. Xavier’s College, Mumbai in 2001 and received his PhD degree from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in Waltham, Massachusetts in 2006. His research supervisor was Dmitry Yanovich Kleinbock.[^o5] After spending a short period as a post-doctoral fellow at the University of Texas at Austin, he joined University of East Anglia, UK as a Lecturer and after a few years he moved to TIFR as a faculty member,

## Recognitions {#Recognitions}

Besides the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology, Anish Ghosh has been the recipient of several recognitions, including the following:

- [NASI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India)-[Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) Young Scientist Award in 2017[^o7]
- DST Swarnajayanti Fellowship in 2017
- Fellow of the Indian Academy of Sciences in 2018[^o8]
- B M Birla Science Prize in 2017[^o9]
- [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100), *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*, 2022
- Fellow of the Indian National Science Academy, 2025 [^o10]
- J. C. Bose grant of the ANRF
