# Dyadic space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dyadic space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **dyadic compactum** is a [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) that is the [continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_function_%28topology%29) [image](wikipedia://en/579311) of a product of [discrete two-point spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_two-point_space), and a **dyadic space** is a topological space with a [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) which is a dyadic compactum. However, many authors use the term *dyadic space* with the same meaning as *dyadic compactum* above.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

Dyadic compacta and spaces satisfy the [Suslin condition](wikipedia://en/1550771), and were introduced by Russian mathematician [Pavel Alexandrov](wikipedia://en/576245).[^o2] [Polyadic spaces](wikipedia://en/45398062) are generalisation of dyadic spaces.[^o6]
