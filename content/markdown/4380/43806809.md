# Esenin-Volpin's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Esenin-Volpin's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Esenin-Volpin's theorem** states that [weight](wikipedia://en/59220#Weight_and_character) of an infinite [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [dyadic space](wikipedia://en/43808620) is the supremum of the weights of its points. It was introduced by (txt).[^o3] It was generalized by Efimov in 1965[^o4] and Turzański in 1992.[^o5]

:::
