# John Alexander Third

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Alexander Third"}

**John Alexander Third** (6 May 1865 – 11 August 1948) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Third, son of a stonemason, was educated at [Robert Gordon's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gordon's_College) before entering in 1885 in the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) where he graduated [D.Sc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc) in 1889, after spending some time studying in [Jena, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena,_Germany).[^o4] He was appointed rector of [Campbeltown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campbeltown) Grammar School and, five years later, in 1895, headmaster of [Spier's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spier's_School).

Third joined the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) in 1897 and he was an enthusiastic member publishing papers in the *Proceedings*. He was also president of the society in 1902.[^n1] He was director of education of [Ayrshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrshire) from 1919 until his retirement in 1927.[^n2]

After his death, his family made a bequest of £150 to found a prize in the Department of Mathematics of the university of Aberdeen.[^n3]
