# Ely Merzbach

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ely Merzbach"}

**Ely Yissachar Merzbach** (עלי יששכר מרצבך; born 11 February 1950) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and emeritus professor at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University)'s Department of Mathematics and the Gonda Brain Research Center.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ely Merzbach was born in 1950 in Paris, where he attended [École Yabné](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Yabné_%28Paris%29). He [immigrated to Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) at the age of 17, studying for a year at [Yeshivat Be'er Ya'akov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be'er_Ya'akov) and then enlisting in the [Nahal Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahal_Brigade) of the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces). He obtained a B.Sc. in mathematics and statistics and an M.Sc. in mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), and completed his doctoral studies in 1979 at [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev).

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After working as a [postdoctoral fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_fellow) at [Paris 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) and the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), Merzbach joined the faculty at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) in 1980, becoming [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) in 1993.

His research focuses on [point processes theory](wikipedia://en/2147685), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813), and applications thereof.[^o5][^o6]

He served as head of the Bar-Ilan's Department of Mathematics and Computer Science from 1991, academic head of [Ariel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_University) from 1996 to 1997, and was elected dean of the Faculty of Exact Sciences at Bar-Ilan in 1997.[^o7] Since April 2021 he serves as president of Michlalah Jerusalem College.
