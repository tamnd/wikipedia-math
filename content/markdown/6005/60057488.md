# NumWorks

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="NumWorks"}

**NumWorks** is a French [technology company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_company) that designs, develops, and sells [graphing calculators](wikipedia://en/335004). Their calculators are [source-available](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source-available) and have their hardware design available under a [Creative Commons license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license).[^o3] Its first calculator, the N0100, was released on August 29, 2017, in Europe and the United States and is geared towards high school classrooms and students.[^o4] The calculators use Python as their programming language, rather than a proprietary language (e.g. [TI-BASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TI-BASIC), which is used by Texas Instruments calculators).

:::

## Development {#Development}

Romain Goyet, the CEO of NumWorks, started the company in 2016. Before starting NumWorks, he was a software engineer at [Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc.) who also contributed to open-source projects such as [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux).[^o6]

## Products {#Products}

The NumWorks [graphing calculator](wikipedia://en/335004) was the first graphing calculator to be [programmable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programmable_calculator) using the [Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_%28programming_language%29) language. It features a 320x240 IPS display with a 2.8″ diagonal. Internally, it is powered by a 216 MHz [Cortex-M7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARM_Cortex-M) processor and 8 MB of [Quad-SPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_Peripheral_Interface) [Flash memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_memory). The calculator has a 1450 mAh [lithium polymer battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_polymer_battery). The calculator weights \5.9 oz and measures \8.2 x.

Four versions of the calculator were produced by NumWorks: the N0100 model, the N0110 model, the N0120 model, and the N0115 model. Technical limitations of the N0100 model's memory prevent any upgrade to an OS version higher than 19.5.17.

## Features {#Features}

The calculator was specifically designed to be modded using 3D printing. 3D models, schematics, and board layout details are available to the public under a Creative Commons license.[^o6] The software on the calculator is updated on a monthly cycle. Updates can be downloaded to the calculator from its website using [WebUSB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WebUSB) or by building the operating system from its direct source.

The NumWorks calculator also includes various "exam modes" which may disable certain features (e.g. the periodic table app, Python scripts, etc.) in accordance with regional exam regulations. Exam mode can be disabled by plugging the calculator into a power source and selecting disable on the popup that appears.

On March 22, 2019, NumWorks released an app for iOS and Android, free of charge.[^o10] It features the same functionality as the physical calculator except it does not have [data persistence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persistent_data_structure).
