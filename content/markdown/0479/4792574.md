# Supermathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Supermathematics"}

**Supermathematics** is the branch of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) which applies the mathematics of [Lie superalgebras](wikipedia://en/297501) to the [behaviour](wikipedia://en/352827) of [bosons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosons) and [fermions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermions). The driving force in its formation in the 1960s and 1970s was [Felix Berezin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Berezin).

:::

Objects of study include [superalgebras](wikipedia://en/469950) (such as [super Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Minkowski_space) and [super-Poincaré algebra](wikipedia://en/1057955)), [superschemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superscheme), [supermetrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermetric)/[supersymmetry](wikipedia://en/224636), [supermanifolds](wikipedia://en/891255), [supergeometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergeometry), and [supergravity](wikipedia://en/432632), namely in the context of [superstring theory](wikipedia://en/19595676).
