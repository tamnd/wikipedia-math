# Gustave Choquet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gustave Choquet"}

**Gustave Choquet** (; 1 March 1915 – 14 November 2006) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Choquet was born in [Solesmes, Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solesmes,_Nord). His contributions include work in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory). He is known for creating the [Choquet theory](wikipedia://en/3655598), the [Choquet integral](wikipedia://en/10252066) and the [theory of capacities](wikipedia://en/14368398).

He did postgraduate work at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) Paris where his advisor was [Arnaud Denjoy](wikipedia://en/1485520). He was Professor at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) (subsequently [Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University)) from 1940 to 1984 and was also Professor at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) from 1960 to 1969. His honours and awards included being a Member of the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences), and an Officier of the [Légion d’Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Légion_d’Honneur).

His students include [Haïm Brezis](wikipedia://en/18329570), [Gilles Godefroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilles_Godefroy), [Nassif Ghoussoub](wikipedia://en/18739787), [Michel L. Lapidus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_L._Lapidus), and [Michel Talagrand](wikipedia://en/11091583).

He was married to mathematician and mathematical physicist [Yvonne Choquet-Bruhat](wikipedia://en/22081008), with whom he had a son [Daniel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Choquet) and a daughter [Geneviève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneviève_Choquet). He died in Lyon in 2006.
