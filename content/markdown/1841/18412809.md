# Xavier Fernique

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xavier Fernique"}

*Image: Xavier Fernique.jpg.* Fernique in 1975

**Xavier Fernique** (3 May 1934 – 15 March 2020) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), noted mostly for his contributions to the theory of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes). [Fernique's theorem](wikipedia://en/17960827), a result on the [integrability](wikipedia://en/2573213) of [Gaussian measures](wikipedia://en/6572909), is named after him.

:::
