# Vladimir Berkovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Berkovich"}

**Vladimir Berkovich** ('ולדימיר ברקוביץ) is a mathematician at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) who introduced [Berkovich spaces](wikipedia://en/34420832). His Ph.D. advisor was [Yuri I. Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_I._Manin). Berkovich was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1991-92 and again in the summer of 2000.[^o3]

:::

In 1998 he was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o4] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
