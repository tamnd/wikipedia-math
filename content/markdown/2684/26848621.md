# Cipher security summary

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cipher security summary"}

This article summarizes publicly known [attacks](wikipedia://en/5715) against [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) and [stream ciphers](wikipedia://en/49007). Note that there are perhaps attacks that are not publicly known, and not all entries may be up to date.

:::

## Best attack {#Best_attack}

This column lists the complexity of the attack:

- If the attack doesn't break the full cipher, "rounds" refers to how many rounds were broken
- "time" — [time complexity](wikipedia://en/405944), number of cipher evaluations for the attacker
- "data" — required known plaintext-ciphertext pairs (if applicable)
- "memory" — how many blocks worth of data needs to be stored (if applicable)
- "related keys" — for [related-key attacks](wikipedia://en/1516144), how many related key queries are needed

| [DES](wikipedia://en/7978) | 2<sup>56</sup> | 2<sup>39</sup> – 2<sup>43</sup> time, 2<sup>43</sup> [known plaintexts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Known_plaintext) | 2001 | [Linear cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/59469).[^o18] In addition, broken by brute force in 2<sup>56</sup> time, no later than 1998-07-17, see [EFF DES cracker](wikipedia://en/839358).[^o19] Cracking hardware is available for purchase since 2006.[^o20] |
| [Triple DES](wikipedia://en/48375) | 2<sup>168</sup> | 2<sup>113</sup> time, 2<sup>32</sup> data, 2<sup>88</sup> memory; 64-bit block is vulnerable to SWEET32 attack. | 2016 | Extension of the [meet-in-the-middle attack](wikipedia://en/423418). Time complexity is 2<sup>113</sup> steps, but along with proposed techniques, it is estimated to be equivalent to 2<sup>90</sup> single DES encryption steps. The paper also proposes other [time–memory tradeoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time–memory_tradeoff).[^o21] SWEET32 attack demonstrated birthday attacks to recover plaintext with its 64-bit [block size](wikipedia://en/294065), vulnerable to protocols such as [TLS](wikipedia://en/187813), [SSH](wikipedia://en/28814), [IPsec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPsec), and [OpenVPN](wikipedia://en/858492).[^o12] |
| [KASUMI](wikipedia://en/612027) | 2<sup>128</sup> | 2<sup>32</sup> time, 2<sup>26</sup> data, 2<sup>30</sup> memory, 4 related keys | 2010-01-10 | The cipher used in [3G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3G) cell phone networks. This attack takes less than two hours on a single PC, but isn't applicable to 3G due to known plaintext and related key requirements.[^o23] |
| [RC4](wikipedia://en/25831) | *unpublished* | 2<sup>20</sup> time, 2<sup>\16.4</sup> related keys (95% success probability) | 2007 | Commonly known as PTW attack, it can break [WEP](wikipedia://en/422014) encryption in [Wi-Fi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wi-Fi) on an ordinary computer in negligible time.[^o25] This is an improvement of the original [Fluhrer, Mantin and Shamir attack](wikipedia://en/10461532) published in 2001.[^o26] |
