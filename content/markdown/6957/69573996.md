# Serene (pianist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serene"}

**Serene** is an American [concert pianist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_pianist)[^o3] and computer scientist. She is the inventor of [Snowflake](wikipedia://en/71968227), the [anti-censorship software](wikipedia://en/32936800) package used by [The Tor Project](wikipedia://en/43283728).[^o4] Serene is a touring classical musician. Her experimental performances combine [high technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_tech) with pianism, such as performing piano while accompanied by musical [robots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robots).[^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Serene spent her childhood in a variety of regions within North America.[^o3] She attended [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), graduating with a degree in computer science[^o3] in 2012.

## Career in software {#Career_in_software}

She worked as the first engineer for technology incubator Google Ideas,[^o8] now [Jigsaw LLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigsaw_%28company%29). While a senior fellow at the International Computer Science Institute in Berkeley, California, she was a recipient of the [Open Technology Fund](wikipedia://en/49417443)’s Information Controls Fellowship, where she created [Snowflake](wikipedia://en/71968227),[^o9][^o10] a pluggable transport enabling censorship circumvention for [The Tor Project](wikipedia://en/43283728) as its first use-case.

She is the founder of Snowstorm, the rearchitected V2 and commercialization of the Snowflake transport technology. In July 2023, Snowstorm finished its seed round of \$3 million.[^o11] Snowflake came to prominence when the [Tor network](wikipedia://en/20556944) began using it to prevent hostile actors from blocking access to Tor.[^o12][^o13][^o14]

Serene is an advisory board member of the World Ethical Data Foundation.[^o15]

## Career as a musician {#Career_as_a_musician}

As of 2021, SERENE is a [Bösendorfer Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bösendorfer#Bösendorfer_artists), the sole non-conservatory pianist with such an affiliation.[^o17] She presented her Bösendorfer debut with the symphony at the Vienna Konzerthaus.[^o18] She has performed with [Grimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grimes_%28musician%29), using [EEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroencephalography) and generative AI music technology.[^o19]

Serene has given performances at both concert halls and such non-traditional settings as San Francisco's Golden Gate Park and inside a decommissioned [Boeing 747](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747) at [Burning Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_Man). She received a composition credit on [Kanye West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanye_West)’s opera *Mary* (2019).[^o3] She has released two EPs: *Unraveling* (2019), a collection of piano works by [Maurice Ravel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Ravel), and *Rachmaninoff Concert No. 3* (2020). She has written code to project abstract, moving visualizations behind her performances.[^o20][^o21][^o22] She also has collaborated with [Blue Man Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Man_Group) founder [Chris Wink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Wink) on music-technology projects and social-distanced events at Las Vegas's [AREA15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area15).

In 2024, Serene premiered a piano robotics concert at a [fusion energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_energy) facility while improvising with [Jaron Lanier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaron_Lanier).[^o5]
