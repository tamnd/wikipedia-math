# Osculating plane

*Image: frenet.svg.* A space curve, [Frenet–Serret frame](wikipedia://en/666987), and the osculating plane ([spanned](wikipedia://en/56353) by $\mathbf{T}$ and $\mathbf{N}$).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Osculating plane"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), an **osculating plane** is a [plane](wikipedia://en/19469852) in a [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) or [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834) which meets a [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) at a [point](wikipedia://en/593693) in such a way as to have a second order of [contact](wikipedia://en/396068) at the point. The word *osculate* is la; an osculating plane is thus a plane which "kisses" a submanifold.

:::

The osculating plane in the geometry of Euclidean space curves can be described in terms of the [Frenet-Serret formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frenet-Serret_formulas) as the [linear span](wikipedia://en/56353) of the tangent and normal vectors.[^o2]
