# Mathematical Association

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical Association"}

The **Mathematical Association** is a professional society concerned with [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) in the United Kingdom.

:::

## History {#History}

It was founded in 1871 as the Association for the Improvement of Geometrical Teaching and renamed to the Mathematical Association in 1897.[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8] It was the first teachers' subject organisation formed in England. In March 1927, it held a three-day meeting in [Grantham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grantham) to commemorate the bicentenary of the death of Sir [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627), attended by Sir [J. J. Thomson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Thomson) (discoverer of the electron), Sir [Frank Watson Dyson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Watson_Dyson) – the [Astronomer Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer_Royal), Sir [Horace Lamb](wikipedia://en/1192944), and [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599).

In 1951, [Mary Cartwright](wikipedia://en/1529094) became the first female president of the Mathematical Association.[^o9]

In the 1960s, when comprehensive education was being introduced, the Association was in favour of the [11-plus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11-plus) system. For maths teachers training at university, a teaching award that was examined was the [Diploma of the Mathematical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma_of_the_Mathematical_Association), later known as the Diploma in Mathematical Education of the Mathematical Association.

## Function {#Function}

It exists to "bring about improvements in the teaching of mathematics and its applications, and to provide a means of communication among students and teachers of mathematics".[^o11] Since 1894 it has published *[The Mathematical Gazette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mathematical_Gazette)*. It is one of the participating bodies in the quadrennial British Congress of Mathematics Education, organised by the [Joint Mathematical Council](wikipedia://en/7779526), and it holds its annual general meeting as part of the Congress.[^o12]

## Structure {#Structure}

It is based in the south-east of Leicester on *London Road* ([A6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A6_road_%28England%29)), just south of the Charles Frears campus of [De Montfort University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Montfort_University).

Aside from the council, it has seven other specialist committees.
