# Jan Koenderink

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Koenderink"}

**Jan Johan Koenderink** (born 10 February 1943, [Stramproy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stramproy)[^o2]) is a Dutch [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist) known for his research in [visual perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_perception), [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).

:::

Koenderink earned a bachelor's degree from [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) in 1964, a master's in 1967, and a Ph.D. in 1972 on a thesis titled *Models of the visual system*. He was a full professor of physics and astronomy at Utrecht University from 1978 until his [mandatory retirement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_retirement) in 2008; since then, he has held fellow or visiting professor positions at Utrecht, the [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology), the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven).[^o3]

Koenderink is the author of the books *Color for the Sciences* (MIT Press, 2010), on [colorimetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorimetry),[^o4] and *Solid Shape* (MIT Press, 1990), on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o5][^o6]

In 1987, the Katholieke Universiteit Leuven awarded Koenderink an honorary doctorate.[^o3]

Koenderink became a fellow of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1990,[^o7] and of the [Royal Flemish Academy of Belgium for Science and the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Flemish_Academy_of_Belgium_for_Science_and_the_Arts) in 2010.[^o3]

Koenderink is the 2013 recipient of the [Azriel Rosenfeld Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azriel_Rosenfeld_Award) for lifetime achievement in computer vision.[^o8]

In 2017, Koenderink, along with Andrea Van Doorn, was awarded the [Kurt-Koffka medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Koffka_Medal) from [Justus Liebig University Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justus_Liebig_University_Giessen).[^o9]
