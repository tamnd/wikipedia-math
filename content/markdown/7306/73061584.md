# Ulrike Meier Yang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrike Meier Yang"}

**Ulrike Meier Yang** (born 1959) is a German-American applied mathematician and computer scientist specializing in [numerical algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_algorithm) for [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing). She directs the Mathematical [Algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) & Computing group in the Center for Applied Scientific Computing at the [Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Livermore_National_Laboratory), and is one of the developers of the [Hypre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypre) library of parallel methods for solving linear systems.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Meier Yang did her undergraduate studies in mathematics at [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum) in Germany, and worked in the Central Institute of Applied Mathematics of the [Forschungszentrum Jülich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forschungszentrum_Jülich) in Germany from 1983 to 1985 and at the [National Center for Supercomputing Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Supercomputing_Applications) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) from 1985 to 1995. She completed her doctorate through the University of Illinois in 1995 with the dissertation *A Family of Preconditioned Iterative Solvers for Sparse Linear Systems*, supervised by Kyle Gallivan.

She joined the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory research staff in 1998.

As of January 1st, 2023 Yang took office as a member of the [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Board of Trustees.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow), in the 2024 class of fellows, elected for "pioneering work on parallel algebraic multigrid and software, and broad impact on high-performance computing".
