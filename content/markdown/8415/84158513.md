# Digby's H

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Digby's H"}

**Digby's H** is a measure of [association](wikipedia://en/157057) for a 2 × 2 [contingency table](wikipedia://en/935515). It is a nonlinear transformation of the [odds ratio](wikipedia://en/406880), proposed by P. G. N. Digby in 1983 as a computationally simple approximation to the [tetrachoric correlation coefficient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrachoric_correlation_coefficient), which otherwise requires iterative numerical estimation.[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=named-heading name="Formula"}

## Formula {#Formula}

For a 2×2 table of [binary variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_variable) *U* and *V* with cell frequencies or proportions

::: {#o6 .table cue=table}

|  | *V* = 0 | *V* = 1 |
|---|---|---|
| *U* = 0 | *a* | *b* |
| *U* = 1 | *c* | *d* |

:::

the odds ratio is

$$\omega=\frac{ad}{bc}.$$

Digby's H is defined as

$$H=\frac{(ad)^{3/4}-(bc)^{3/4}}{(ad)^{3/4}+(bc)^{3/4}}.$$

Equivalently, in terms of the odds ratio,

$$H=\frac{\omega^{3/4}-1}{\omega^{3/4}+1}.$$

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Digby's H ranges from −1 to 1. It equals zero under statistical independence of *U* and *V*, since independence implies $ad=bc$ and hence $\omega=1$. Positive values indicate positive association; negative values indicate negative association.

Because H depends on the table only through the ratio *ad*/*bc*, it is invariant under multiplication of all four cell frequencies by the same positive constant, and — like any function of the odds ratio alone — its value is determined by ω independently of the row and column marginal totals.[^o3]

Digby's H belongs to a one-parameter family of odds-ratio transformations sometimes called generalized Yule coefficients,[^o9]

$$S_q=\frac{(ad)^q-(bc)^q}{(ad)^q+(bc)^q},$$

where $q=1$ gives [Yule's Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yule's_Q),

$$Q=\frac{ad-bc}{ad+bc},$$

$q=1/2$ gives [Yule's Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yule's_Y),

$$Y=\frac{\sqrt{ad}-\sqrt{bc}}{\sqrt{ad}+\sqrt{bc}},$$

and $q=3/4$ gives Digby's H. All members of this family are zero under independence and share the same sign as $ad-bc$.

:::
