# Kai Cieliebak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kai Cieliebak"}

**Kai Cieliebak** (\* [March 26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_26) [1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966) in [Hagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagen), Germany) is a [german](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [Mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is professor at the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Kai Cieliebak received his diploma in 1992 at the [Ruhruniversität Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhruniversität_Bochum) under the guidance of [Helmut Hofer](wikipedia://en/34920327) and subsequently found work at the University Bochum as [Wissenschaftlicher Assistent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wissenschaftlicher_Assistent). In 1993 he managed to get an assistant job at the [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), where he got his Doctorate in 1996, again under the guidance of Helmut Hofer. He then began working for [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1996. In 1997, Kai Cieliebak got a job at the [Swiss Bank Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS#Schweizerischer_Bankverein_%28SBV%29,_1854–1998), where he worked until 1998, when he switched to [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM). He got an [Assistant Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Professor) post at the [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1999. He swiched later to the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) for a professor job. In 2012 he switched to the University of Augsburg[^o3][^o6][^o4][^o5]

He is coeditor of the [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae) and member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o6][^o7][^o8] and was invited to speak at the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2022.
