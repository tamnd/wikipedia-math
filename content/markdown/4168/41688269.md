# List of dimensionless quantities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of dimensionless quantities"}

This is a list of well-known [dimensionless quantities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensionless_quantities) illustrating their variety of forms and applications. The tables also include [pure numbers](wikipedia://en/21690), dimensionless ratios, or [dimensionless physical constants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensionless_physical_constant); these topics are discussed in the article.

:::
