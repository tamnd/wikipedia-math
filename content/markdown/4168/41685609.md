# List of Greek mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Greek mathematicians"}

In historical times, [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece) civilization has played one of the major roles in the history and development of [Greek mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_mathematics). To this day, a number of Greek mathematicians are considered for their innovations and influence on [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).

:::

## Modern Greek mathematicians {#Modern_Greek_mathematicians}

::: {#o99 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leonidas Alaoglu"}

- [Leonidas Alaoglu](wikipedia://en/7689104) (1914–1981) - Known for *Banach- Alaoglu theorem*.[^o100]

:::

::: {#o101 .entry cue=list-entry name="Charalambos D. Aliprantis"}

- [Charalambos D. Aliprantis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charalambos_D._Aliprantis) (1946–2009) - Founder and Editor-in-Chief of the journals Economic Theory as well as Annals of Finance.[^o102]

:::

::: {#o103 .entry cue=list-entry name="Roger Apéry"}

- [Roger Apéry](wikipedia://en/1421250) (1916–1994) - Professor of mathematics and mechanics at the [University of Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Lower_Normandy). Proved the irrationality of $\zeta(3)$, known as [Apéry's constant](wikipedia://en/2216711).[^o104]

:::

::: {#o105 .entry cue=list-entry name="Tom M. Apostol"}

- [Tom M. Apostol](wikipedia://en/2196529) (1923–2016) - Professor of mathematics in [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology),[^o106] he has authored a number of books about mathematics.

:::

::: {#o107 .entry cue=list-entry name="Dimitri Bertsekas"}

- [Dimitri Bertsekas](wikipedia://en/23889091) (1942–2026) - Member of the National Academy of Engineering professor with the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. Author of fifteen books and research monographs, and coauthor of an introductory probability textbook[^o108]

:::

::: {#o109 .entry cue=list-entry name="Giovanni Carandino"}

- [Giovanni Carandino](wikipedia://en/41054654) (1784–1834)

:::

::: {#o110 .entry cue=list-entry name="Constantin Carathéodory"}

- [Constantin Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625) (1873–1950) - Mathematician who pioneered the Axiomatic Formulation of Thermodynamics.[^o111]

:::

::: {#o112 .entry cue=list-entry name="Demetrios Christodoulou"}

- [Demetrios Christodoulou](wikipedia://en/2407999) (born 1951) - Mathematician-physicist who has contributed in the field of general relativity.[^o113]

:::

::: {#o114 .entry cue=list-entry name="Constantine Dafermos"}

- [Constantine Dafermos](wikipedia://en/9688175) (born 1941) - Usually notable for hyperbolic conservation laws and control theory.[^o115]

:::

::: {#o116 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mihalis Dafermos"}

- [Mihalis Dafermos](wikipedia://en/23535913) (born 1976) - Professor of Mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and Lowndean Chair of Astronomy and Geometry at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge)[^o117]

:::

::: {#o118 .entry cue=list-entry name="Apostolos Doxiadis"}

- [Apostolos Doxiadis](wikipedia://en/1525019) (born 1953) - Australian born Mathematician.[^o119]

:::

::: {#o120 .entry cue=list-entry name="Athanassios S. Fokas"}

- [Athanassios S. Fokas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athanassios_S._Fokas) (born 1952) - Contributor in the field of integrable nonlinear partial differential equations.[^o121]

:::

::: {#o122 .entry cue=list-entry name="Michael Katehakis"}

- [Michael Katehakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Katehakis) (born 1952) - Professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o123]

:::

::: {#o124 .entry cue=list-entry name="Alexander S. Kechris"}

- [Alexander S. Kechris](wikipedia://en/2291355) (born 1946) - Made notable contribution for the theory of Borel equivalence relations.[^o125]

:::

::: {#o126 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nicholas Metropolis"}

- [Nicholas Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Metropolis) (1915–1999) - American born Greek physicist.[^o127]

:::

::: {#o128 .entry cue=list-entry name="Yiannis N. Moschovakis"}

- [Yiannis N. Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002) (1938) - Writer, also worked as theorist in [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o129]

:::

::: {#o130 .entry cue=list-entry name="Christos Papakyriakopoulos"}

- [Christos Papakyriakopoulos](wikipedia://en/1463636) (1914–1976) - Often called *Papa*, he specialized in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732).[^o131]

:::

::: {#o132 .entry cue=list-entry name="Athanasios Papoulis"}

- [Athanasios Papoulis](wikipedia://en/617543) (1921–2002) - Contributed a number of theories, such as *Papoulis–Gerchberg algorithm*,[^o133] *A eloquent proof*,[^o134] among others.

:::

::: {#o135 .entry cue=list-entry name="Themistocles M. Rassias"}

- [Themistocles M. Rassias](wikipedia://en/24914337) (born 1951) - Professor at the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens).[^o136]

:::

::: {#o137 .entry cue=list-entry name="Raphaël Salem"}

- [Raphaël Salem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphaël_Salem) (1898–1963) - Greek mathematician after whom are named the [Salem numbers](wikipedia://en/1066694) and whose widow founded the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize).

:::

::: {#o138 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cyparissos Stephanos"}

- [Cyparissos Stephanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyparissos_Stephanos) (1857–1917) - Notable contributor of [desmic systems](wikipedia://en/23457340).[^o139]

:::

::: {#o140 .entry cue=list-entry name="Katia Sycara"}

- [Katia Sycara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katia_Sycara) - Professor in the Carnegie Mellon School of Computer Science's Robotics Institute and the director of the Laboratory for Agents technology and Semantic web technologies.[^o141]

:::

::: {#o142 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nicholas Varopoulos"}

- [Nicholas Varopoulos](wikipedia://en/37551761) (born 1940) - Notable for his analysis on Lie groups.[^o143]

:::

::: {#o144 .entry cue=list-entry name="Stathis Zachos"}

- [Stathis Zachos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stathis_Zachos) (born 1947) - Published a number of writings on [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).

:::

::: {#o145 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mihail Zervos"}

- [Mihail Zervos](wikipedia://en/29211332) - Working in Department of Mathematics, [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).[^o146]

:::
