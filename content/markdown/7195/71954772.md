# Jan Vondrák

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Vondrák"}

**Jan Vondrák** is a Czech applied mathematician and theoretical computer scientist. He has been a professor of mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) since 2015.[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Vondrák completed a bachelor's degree in physics (1995) and an M.S. (1999) and Ph.D. (2007) in computer science at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) under advisor [Martin Loebl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Loebl).[^o2] Vondrák completed an additional Ph.D. in applied mathematics in 2005 at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) under advisor [Michel Goemans](wikipedia://en/34561422).[^o2]

## Career {#Career}

He was a postdoctoral researcher in the theory group at [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) from 2005 to 2006.[^o2] From 2006 to 2009, Vondrák was a postdoctoral teaching fellow at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o2] He was a research staff member in the theory group at the [IBM Almaden Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Almaden_Research_Center) from 2009 to 2015.[^o2]

### Since 2015, he has been a professor of mathematics at Stanford University.[^o2] {#Since_2015,_he_has_been_a_professor_of_mathematics_at_Stanford_University.}

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He met mathematician ***[Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655)*** in 2004 in Boston.[^o7] They married in 2008 on a mountain in [New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire).[^o7] They moved to California in 2009.[^o7] Their daughter Anahita was born in 2011.[^o7] Mirzakhani died of breast cancer in 2017.[^o7]
