# Adele Merritt

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adele Merritt"}

**Adele Johanna Josiger Merritt** is an American [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) and intelligence official who served as the [U.S. associate director of national intelligence and chief information officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Director_of_National_Intelligence_and_Chief_Information_Officer).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Merritt earned a Bachelor of Science and a Bachelor of Business Administration in Finance from [Pace University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pace_University).[^o4] She completed a Master of Science and Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island).[^o4] Her 1999 dissertation was titled *Advances in Z-cyclic whist and triple whist tournaments*.[^o5] Norman Joseph Finizio was her doctoral advisor.[^o5] Merritt was a national security fellow at the [Harvard Kennedy School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Kennedy_School).[^o4]

Merritt began her career at the [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939) as an applied research mathematician.[^o4] In addition to holding various positions within the [U.S. Intelligence Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Intelligence_Community) (IC), she completed a joint duty assignment at the [Federal Bureau of Investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation), was the principal deputy chief information officer for cyber at the [U.S. Department of Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Department_of_Energy), and served as a director and acting senior director for intelligence programs on the [National Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_%28United_States%29) staff during the [Obama administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obama_administration).[^o4] Merritt led a [public-private partnership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public–private_partnership) focused on addressing [cyber threats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_threats) to U.S. national security systems and critical infrastructure.[^o4] Most recently, she was the program director at MISI, a non-profit focused on advancing cybersecurity innovation and collaboration through partnerships with industry, academia, and government.[^o4] In 2022, Merritt was nominated by U.S. president [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden) to serve as the [associate director of national intelligence and chief information officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Director_of_National_Intelligence_and_Chief_Information_Officer).[^o4][^o6] She started in this role on January 24, 2022.[^o4][^o7] She resigned in December 2024 after being named CIO of the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health).[^o8]

Merritt and her husband are the parents of twins.[^o4]
