# Mia Hubert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mia Hubert"}

**Mia Hubert** is a Belgian [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711) known for her research on topics in [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) including [medoid](wikipedia://en/2421175)-based clustering,a [regression depth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regression_depth),b the [medcouple](wikipedia://en/46325747) for robustly measuring [skewness](wikipedia://en/28212),c [box plots](wikipedia://en/160960) for skewed data,f and robust [principal component analysis](wikipedia://en/76340),d and for her implementations of robust statistical algorithms in the [R statistical software system](wikipedia://en/376707), [MATLAB](wikipedia://en/20412),e and [S-PLUS](wikipedia://en/3830007).a She is a professor in the statistics and data science section of the department of mathematics at [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hubert earned a diploma in mathematics in 1992 from the [University of Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp), and obtained her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1997 at the same university. Her dissertation, *Robust Regression for Data Analysis*, was supervised by [Peter Rousseeuw](wikipedia://en/30760578). She joined the KU Leuven faculty in 2001.

She was the original developer of the [R package](wikipedia://en/65740838) *cluster* along with Peter Rousseeuw and Anja Struyf.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Hubert became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2013.
