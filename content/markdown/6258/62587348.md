# Jürgen Herzog

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jürgen Herzog"}

**Jürgen Reinhard Gerhard Herzog** (; 21 December 1941 – 23 April 2024) was an Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen), in [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen), Germany.[^o3] From 1969 to 1975, he was Lecturer at [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) and from 1975 to 2009 a professor of Mathematics at [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Herzog was born in [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg) on 21 December 1941 and raised in [Eberbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eberbach_%28Baden%29).[^o4][^o10] After military service in the German Army, he enrolled at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) from 1963[^o4] and began studying mathematics and physics. Herzog transferred to the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) in 1964, and completed his undergraduate studies there.[^o6] He received his Ph.D. with a thesis titled, *Generators and Relations of Abelian Semigroups and Semigroup Rings* at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University) in 1969 under the supervision of Ernst A. Kunz. He completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) in 1974.[^o4] He was an expert in the field of [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and its interactions to other mathematical fields such as [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Herzog died on 23 April 2024 in Germany, following a sudden heart attack.[^o9][^o10]
