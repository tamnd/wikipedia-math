# Colin Adams (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Colin Adams"}

**Colin Conrad Adams** (born October 13, 1956) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) primarily working in the areas of [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). His book, *The Knot Book*, has been praised for its accessible approach to advanced topics in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). He is currently [Francis Christopher Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Christopher_Oakley) Third Century Professor of Mathematics at [Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College), where he has been since 1985. He writes "Mathematically Bent", a column of math for the *[Mathematical Intelligencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Intelligencer)*. His nephew is popular American singer [Still Woozy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Still_Woozy).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Adams received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1978 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1983. His dissertation was titled "Hyperbolic Structures on Link Complements" and was supervised by [James Cannon](wikipedia://en/19404886).

## Work {#Work}

Among his earliest contributions is his theorem that the [Gieseking manifold](wikipedia://en/23010120) is the unique cusped [hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1237700) of smallest volume. The proof utilizes [horoball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horoball)-packing arguments. Adams is known for his clever use of such arguments utilizing horoball patterns and his work would be used in the later proof by Chun Cao and G. Robert Meyerhoff that the smallest cusped orientable hyperbolic 3-manifolds are precisely the [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565) [complement](wikipedia://en/1350120) and its sibling manifold.

Adams has investigated and defined a variety of geometric invariants of [hyperbolic links](wikipedia://en/1248299) and hyperbolic 3-manifolds in general. He developed techniques for working with volumes of special classes of hyperbolic links. He proved augmented alternating links, which he defined, were hyperbolic. In addition, he has defined almost alternating and toroidally alternating links. He has often collaborated and published this research with students from SMALL, an undergraduate summer research program at Williams.

In 1998, Adams received the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o5]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
