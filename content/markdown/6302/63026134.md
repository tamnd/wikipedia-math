# Naiomi Cameron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Naiomi Cameron"}

**Naiomi Tuere Cameron** is an American mathematician working in the field of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). She is a professor at [Spelman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spelman_College) as well as the vice president of [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356).[^o2][^o3] She was previously an associate professor at [Lewis & Clark College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_&_Clark_College) in Portland, OR.[^o4]

:::

Cameron was born in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) and raised in [Providence, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island). She attended [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University) for her undergraduate and graduate school, receiving both her B.S. and PhD in Mathematics.[^o2][^o5] In 2019, she was featured on *[Mathematically Gifted and Black](wikipedia://en/64552006)* as a Black History Month 2019 Honoree.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Cameron's academic work has been focused on [enumerative](wikipedia://en/3925533) and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). Her thesis for her dissertation in 2002 was *Random Walks, Trees and Extensions of Riordan Group Techniques*.[^o2] Her other publications include:

- Cameron, Naiomi T.; Killpatrick, Kendra. (2015-06-01). "Inversion polynomials for permutations avoiding consecutive patterns". *Advances in Applied Mathematics*. 67. p. 20–35. doi:10.1016/j.aam.2015.03.002. arxiv:1402.5211
- Cameron, Naiomi; Killpatrick, Kendra. (2013-11-01). "Symmetry and Log-Concavity Results for Statistics on Fibonacci Tableaux". *Annals of Combinatorics*. 17 (4). p. 603–618. doi:10.1007/s00026-013-0198-1

## Additional work {#Additional_work}

Cameron is the vice president of the [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356) for the 2019–2020 term.[^o3]
