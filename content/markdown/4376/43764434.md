# Caroline Series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caroline Series"}

**Caroline Mary Series** (born 24 March 1951[^o25]) is an English mathematician known for her work in [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

*Image: Caroline Series.jpg.* Caroline in 1976

Series was born on March 24, 1951, in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford) to Annette and [George Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Series).[^o25] She attended [Oxford High School for Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_High_School_for_Girls) and from 1969 studied at [Somerville College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerville_College,_Oxford), where she was interviewed for admission by [Anne Cobbe](wikipedia://en/58229105).[^o4] She obtained a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in Mathematics in 1972 and was awarded the university Mathematical Prize. She was awarded a [Kennedy Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Scholarship) and studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 1972, obtaining her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1976[^o5] supervised by [George Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mackey) on the *[Ergodicity](wikipedia://en/5456824) of product groups*.[^o5]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

In 1976–77 she was a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), and in 1977–78 she was a [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) at [Newnham College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge). From 1978 she was at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), first as a lecturer, then, from 1987, as a [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29), and from 1992 as a professor. From 1999 to 2004 she was [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) (EPSRC) Senior Research Fellow at the University of Warwick.

In the 1970s, Series found illustrations of [Rufus Bowen](wikipedia://en/24717250)'s Theory of Dynamic Systems in the geometry of [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199) and two-dimensional hyperbolic geometry, effect of [Fuchsian groups](wikipedia://en/471365). After that she investigated similar, including fractal, geometric patterns in three-dimensional hyperbolic spaces, with [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) as [symmetry groups](wikipedia://en/28186). The computer images led to a book project with [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) and David Wright, which took over ten years. Other coauthors with whom she published in this area include [Linda Keen](wikipedia://en/11567176) and [Joan Birman](wikipedia://en/8625257).

Series became the third woman to be president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) when she held the post in 2017–2019.[^o8][^o9]

She is emeritus professor in mathematics at the University of Warwick.[^o25]

### Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1987 she was awarded the Junior [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). In 1992 she held the Rouse Ball Lecture in Cambridge, and in 1986 she was the [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Berkeley (*Symbolic Dynamics for Geodesic Flows*). From 1990 to 2001 she was the editor of the Student Texts of the London Mathematical Society. In 1986 she was a founding member of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) (EWM).[^o16] In 2009 she was the Emmy Noether visiting professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). She was elected a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in its inaugural class of 2013. She is an Honorary Fellow of Somerville College.[^o17]

- 1972–74 Kennedy Scholarship, Harvard University
- 1987 Junior Whitehead Prize, London Mathematical Society [^o18]
- 2014 [Senior Anne Bennett Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Anne_Bennett_Prize), London Mathematical Society [^o19][^o18]
- 2016 [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o20]
- 2017 Elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o21]
- 2021 [David Crighton Medal](wikipedia://en/61378347), London Mathematical Society[^o22]

Series was appointed [Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (CBE) in the [2023 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Birthday_Honours) for services to mathematics.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

She is the daughter of the physicist [George Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Series).
