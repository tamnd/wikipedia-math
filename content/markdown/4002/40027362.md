# R. K. Rubugunday

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="R. K. Rubugunday"}

**Raghunath Krishna Rubugunday** (1918–2000) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in number theory notable for his contribution to [Waring's problem](wikipedia://en/33569).[^o3]

:::

Rubugunday was born in [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai) in 1918. The famous mathematician [K. Ananda Rau](wikipedia://en/35226529) was an uncle on his father's side. He completed his B.A. Hons from [Presidency College, Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Chennai) and [Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_Mathematical_Tripos) from [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1938. He returned to [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) and among other positions he was the Head of the Department of Mathematics at [Saugar university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Hari_Singh_Gour_University).[^o3]
