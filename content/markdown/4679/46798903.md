# Ewan Clague

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ewan Clague"}

**Ewan Clague** (1896–1987) was the commissioner of the [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Bureau_of_Labor_Statistics) (BLS) from 1946 to 1965.[^o4][^o5]

:::

In 1952 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o6]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Clague was born to [Manx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manx_people) immigrant parents and grew up on a homestead in Washington state.[^o8] Clague graduated from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and earned a doctorate from the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin).[^o9]
