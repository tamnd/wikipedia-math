# Derek W. Moore

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Derek W. Moore"}

**Derek William Moore** (19 April 1931 – 15 July 2008) was a British mathematician.

:::

He was born in South Shields, where his father was a head of department at the nautical college. He was educated at the local grammar school and [Jesus College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Cambridge).

In 1956 he began research into theoretical fluid dynamics at the [Cavendish Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavendish_Laboratory), followed by spells at [Bristol University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_University) and the [NASA Goddard Space Flight Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_Goddard_Space_Flight_Center) in New York. In 1967 he moved to [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) to become a Professor of Applied Mathematics, holding the post for the rest of his career.[^o2]

In 2001, he was awarded the [Senior Whitehead Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Whitehead_Prize) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3] He was a foreign honorary member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) and a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o4]
