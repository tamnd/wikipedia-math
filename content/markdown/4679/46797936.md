# Mapping spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mapping spectrum"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **mapping spectrum** $F(X, Y)$ of [spectra](wikipedia://en/1033697) *X*, *Y* is characterized by

:::

$[X \wedge Y, Z] = [X, F(Y, Z)].$[^o2]
