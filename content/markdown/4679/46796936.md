# David Edmunds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Edmunds"}

**David Eric Edmunds [FLSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales)** is a British mathematician working in analysis.

:::

He obtained his PhD in 1955 at the [University of Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cardiff) under the supervision of [Rosa M. Morris](wikipedia://en/67025149).[^o2]

In 1996, he was awarded the [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28LMS%29) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3]

In 2011, he was elected a [Fellow of the Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales).[^o4]

[John M. Ball](wikipedia://en/3578468) was one of his PhD students.
