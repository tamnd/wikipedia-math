# Michael Weiss (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Weiss"}

*Image: Galatius weiss.jpg.* Weiss (left) with [Søren Galatius](wikipedia://en/45122752) at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2003

**Michael Weiss** (born 14 December 1955) is a German mathematician and an expert in [algebraic and geometric topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is a professor at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He completed his PhD in 1982 at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) under the supervision of Brian Sanderson. He was then affiliated as a researcher with the [Institute of Advanced Scientific Studies near Paris](wikipedia://en/14527) and the universities of [Bielefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_University), and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_University). In 1999, he joined the faculty of [Aberdeen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_University) where he stayed until 2011, when he was awarded a [Alexander von Humboldt Professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Professorship) at the University of Münster.[^o4][^o5]

## Academic Work {#Academic_Work}

His research is on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562). In work with [Ib Madsen](wikipedia://en/38416618), he resolved the [Mumford Conjecture](wikipedia://en/793295#Stable_mapping_class_group) about rational [characteristic classes](wikipedia://en/363530) of [surface bundles](wikipedia://en/1631920) in the limit as the genus tends to infinity.[^o7] Building on earlier work of [Thomas Goodwillie](wikipedia://en/64493172), he developed Embedding Calculus, a [Calculus of functors](wikipedia://en/23694557) for embeddings of manifolds.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2006, he was awarded the [Fröhlich Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fröhlich_Prize) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o9]
