# Étienne Fouvry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Étienne Fouvry"}

**Étienne Fouvry** (, born 1953[^o3]) is a French mathematician working primarily in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).[^o5]

:::

Fouvry defended his dissertation in 1981 at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux) under the joint direction of [Henryk Iwaniec](wikipedia://en/9338319) and [Jean-Marc Deshouillers](wikipedia://en/40678456). He is an emeritus professor at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University) and the 2021 recipient of the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384).[^o5]

In 1985, Fouvry showed that the first case of [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953) is true for infinitely many primes.[^o6]
