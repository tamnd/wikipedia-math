# Square root of 7

*Image: Rectangle that bounds equilateral triangle.png.* The rectangle that bounds an equilateral triangle of side 2, or a regular hexagon of side 1, has size [square root of 3](wikipedia://en/9126665) by [square root of 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_root_of_4), with a diagonal of square root of 7.

*Image: Slide rule with square roots of 6 and 7.jpg.* A Logarex system Darmstadt [slide rule](wikipedia://en/28743) with 7 and 6 on A and B scales, and [square roots of 6](wikipedia://en/62223323) and of 7 on C and D scales, which can be read as slightly less than 2.45 and somewhat more than 2.64, respectively

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Square root of 7"}

The **square root of 7** is the positive [real number](wikipedia://en/20646438) that, when multiplied by itself, gives the [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) [7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29).

:::

It is an [irrational](wikipedia://en/20647689) [algebraic number](wikipedia://en/1158). The first thirty significant digits of its [decimal expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_expansion) are: 2.64575.

More than a million decimal digits of the square root of seven have been published.[^o4]

## Rational approximations {#Rational_approximations}

*Image: 1797 square root of 7.png.* Explanation of how to extract the square root of 7 to 7 places and more, from Hawney, 1797

The extraction of decimal-fraction approximations to square roots by various methods has used the square root of 7 as an example or exercise in textbooks, for hundreds of years. Different numbers of digits after the decimal point are shown: 5 in 1773[^o6] and 1852,[^o7] 3 in 1835,[^o8] 6 in 1808,[^o9] and 7 in 1797.[^o10] An extraction by [Newton's method](wikipedia://en/22145) (approximately) was illustrated in 1922, concluding that it is 2.646 "to the nearest thousandth".[^o11]

## Geometry {#Geometry}

*Image: Root rectangles up to 6.png.* Root rectangles illustrate a construction of the square root of 7 (the diagonal of the root-6 rectangle).

In [plane geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_geometry), the square root of 7 can be constructed via a sequence of [dynamic rectangles](wikipedia://en/21227262), that is, as the largest diagonal of those rectangles illustrated here.[^o13][^o14][^o15]

The minimal enclosing rectangle of an [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285) of edge length 2 has a diagonal of the square root of 7.[^o16]

Due to the [Pythagorean theorem](wikipedia://en/26513034) and [Legendre's three-square theorem](wikipedia://en/40708124), $\sqrt{7}$ is the smallest square root of a [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) that cannot be the distance between any two points of a cubic [integer lattice](wikipedia://en/3333402) (or equivalently, the length of the [space diagonal](wikipedia://en/1337505) of a [rectangular cuboid](wikipedia://en/38848329) with integer side lengths). $\sqrt{15}$ is the next smallest such number.
