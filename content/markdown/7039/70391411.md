# Scagnostics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Scagnostics"}

*Image: ScagnosticsExampleSplom.svg.* Scatterplot matrix of the scagnostics measures for the 91 scatterplots of the variables of the Boston Housing data set

**Scagnostics** (**sca**tterplot dia**gnostics**) is a series of measures that characterize certain properties of a point cloud in a [scatter plot](wikipedia://en/412544). The term and idea was coined by [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974) and [Paul Tukey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Tukey), though they didn't publish it; later it was elaborated by Wilkinson, Anand, and Grossman. The following nine dimensions are considered:[^o2][^o3]

:::

1. For the outliers in the data:
   1. outlying
1. For the density of data points:
   1. skewed
   1. clumpy
   1. sparse
   1. striated
1. For the shape of the point cloud:
   1. convex
   1. skinny
   1. stringy
1. For trends in the data:
   1. monotony
