# Ramon E. Moore

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramon E. Moore"}

**Ramon Edgar (Ray) Moore** (1929-12-27 2015[^o2]) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his pioneering work in the field of [interval arithmetic](wikipedia://en/2176160).

:::

Moore received an [AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) degree in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1950, and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1963. His early career included work on the earliest computers (including [ENIAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENIAC)). He was awarded the [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award) for U.S. senior scientists twice, in 1975 and 1980.[^o2]

His most well known work is his first book, *Interval Analysis*, published in 1966. He wrote several more books and many journal articles and technical reports.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## R. E. Moore Prize {#R._E._Moore_Prize}

The **R. E. Moore Prize for Applications of Interval Analysis** is an award in the interdisciplinary field of [rigorous numerics](wikipedia://en/2840305). It is awarded biennially by the Computer Science Department at the [University of Texas at El Paso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_El_Paso),[^o7] and judged by the editorial board of the journal *Reliable Computing*.[^o8] The award was named in honor of Moore's contributions to [interval analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interval_analysis).[^o9]
