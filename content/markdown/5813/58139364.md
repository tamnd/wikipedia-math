# Nira Dyn

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nira Dyn"}

**Nira (Richter) Dyn** (נירה דין; born 1942) is an Israeli mathematician who studied [geometric modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_modeling), [subdivision surfaces](wikipedia://en/1079432), [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), and [image compression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_compression). She is a professor emeritus of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), and has been called a "pioneer and leading researcher in the subdivision community".

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dyn earned a bachelor's degree from the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in 1965. She went on to graduate study at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), where she earned a master's degree in 1967 and completed her doctorate in 1970. Her dissertation, *Optimal and Minimum Norm Approximations to Linear Functionals in Hilbert Spaces, and their application to Numerical Integration*, was supervised by [Philip Rabinowitz](wikipedia://en/15046429). After postdoctoral research in the Institute of Fundamental Studies at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), she joined the Tel Aviv faculty in 1972, and retired in 2010.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dyn was an invited speaker at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in the section on numerical analysis and scientific computing.

## Books {#Books}

Dyn is the author of:

- *Stochastic Models in Biology* (as Nira Richter-Dyn, with Narendra S. Goel, Academic Press, 1974)
- *Approximation of Set-valued Functions: Adaptation of Classical Approximation Operators* (with Elza Farkhi and Alona Mokhov, Imperial College Press, 2014)
