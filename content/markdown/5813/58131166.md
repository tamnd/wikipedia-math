# Fanny Kassel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fanny Kassel"}

**Fanny Kassel** (born 1983[^o3]) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in the theory of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kassel entered the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in 2003 and received her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) under the direction of [Yves Benoist](wikipedia://en/48465632) at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 2009. Her thesis was on "Compact quotients of real or p-adic homogeneous spaces". She then entered the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) and worked at the Paul-Painlevé Laboratory of the [University of Lille I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology) until 2016, when she joined the [IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) as detached CNRS researcher.[^o5]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2015, she was awarded the CNRS Bronze Medal and an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) starting grant the following year.[^o7] In 2018, she was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) at [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro); her lecture was on "Geometric structures and representations of discrete groups".[^o8] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Chern Professor for Fall 2020.[^o9] In 2024, she was the recipient of the mathematics medal of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o10]
