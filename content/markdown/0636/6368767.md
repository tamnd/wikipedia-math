# Roland Dobrushin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Dobrushin"}

**Roland Lvovich Dobrushin** (Рола́нд Льво́вич Добру́шин) (July 20, 1929 – November 12, 1995) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made important contributions to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542),[^n1] [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416),[^n2] and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773).[^n3]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Dobrushin received his Ph.D. at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161).

In [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), he introduced (simultaneously with Lanford and Ruelle) the [DLR equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobrushin–Lanford–Ruelle_equations) for the [Gibbs measure](wikipedia://en/3085914). Together with Kotecký and Shlosman, he studied the formation of droplets in [Ising-type models](wikipedia://en/292744), providing mathematical justification of the [Wulff construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulff_construction).[^n4]

He was a foreign member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences), [Academia Europæa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europæa) and [US National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences).

The Dobrushin prize was established in his honour.[^o8]
