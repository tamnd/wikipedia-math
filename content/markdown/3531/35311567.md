# Charles Minshall Jessop

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Minshall Jessop"}

**Charles Minshall Jessop** (1861 – March 9, 1939) was a mathematician at the [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Durham) working in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::
