# Pierre Bieliavsky

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Bieliavsky"}

**Pierre Bieliavsky** (born 1970 in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Brussels), Belgium), is a Belgian mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pierre Bieliavsky graduated from the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) in 1991. He completed a doctorate in 1995 under the supervision of Michel Cahen at the Université libre de Bruxelles on Symmetric symplectic spaces.[^o4][^o5]

He is currently professor of mathematics at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain).[^o6] His research subjects are theory of [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).
