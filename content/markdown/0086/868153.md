# Quasiperiodic tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasiperiodic tiling"}

A **[quasiperiodic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiperiodic) tiling** is a [tiling](wikipedia://en/321671) of the plane that exhibits local periodicity under some transformations: every finite subset of its tiles reappears infinitely often throughout the tiling, but there is no nontrivial way of superimposing the whole tiling onto itself so that all tiles overlap perfectly.[^o2]

:::
