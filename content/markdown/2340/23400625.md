# List of militants fatality reports in Pakistan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of militants fatality reports in Pakistan"}

The **militants fatality reports** are the official reports and [battle logs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_Logs) gathered and published by the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_Pakistan) on the accounts of the rate of human casualties suffered by the [Taliban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehrik-i-Taliban_Pakistan) in the [frontier war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_North-West_Pakistan) since 2005. Reports are based on the official [ISPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Services_Public_Relations) accounts and [news media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_channels_in_Pakistan) sources in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and FATA.

:::

This article incorporates yearly numbers of militant fatalities. Details on the numbers of killed in some major battles and operations are also provided.

## Totals {#Totals}

At least 28,907 militants (total casualties: 43,346+[^o3][^o4][^o12][^o11][^o5][^o6][^o7]) have been reportedly killed so far (from January 2005 to present).[^o8] This figure was updated, adding those counted by the South Asia Terrorism Portal but only concerning FATA and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and not the whole regions of the country.[^o3][^o4][^o6][^o7]
