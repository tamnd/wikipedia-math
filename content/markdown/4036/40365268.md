::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Quarter 6-cubic honeycomb"}

In [six-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six-dimensional_space) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **quarter 6-cubic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It has half the vertices of the [6-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12557781), and a quarter of the vertices of a [6-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-cube_honeycomb).[^o7] Its facets are [6-demicubes](wikipedia://en/10275945), [stericated 6-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stericated_6-demicube), and {3,3}×{3,3} [duoprisms](wikipedia://en/2114155).
