::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Quarter 5-cubic honeycomb"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **quarter 5-cubic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It has half the vertices of the [5-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/10120241), and a quarter of the vertices of a [5-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-cube_honeycomb).[^o7] Its facets are [5-demicubes](wikipedia://en/7186232) and [runcinated 5-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_5-demicube).

:::
