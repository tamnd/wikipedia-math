In [seven-dimensional](wikipedia://en/25546470) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **quarter 7-cubic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It has half the vertices of the [7-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677782), and a quarter of the vertices of a [7-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube_honeycomb).[^o7] Its facets are [7-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube), [pentellated 7-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentellated_7-demicube), and {3<sup>1,1,1</sup>}×{3,3} [duoprisms](wikipedia://en/2114155).
