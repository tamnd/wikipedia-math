::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Quarter 8-cubic honeycomb"}

In [seven-dimensional](wikipedia://en/25555117) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **quarter 8-cubic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It has half the vertices of the [8-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677988), and a quarter of the vertices of a [8-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube_honeycomb).[^o7] Its facets are [8-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube) h{4,3<sup>6</sup>}, [pentic 8-cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentic_8-cube) h<sub>6</sub>{4,3<sup>6</sup>}, {3,3}×{3<sup>2,1,1</sup>} and {3<sup>1,1,1</sup>}×{3<sup>1,1,1</sup>} [duoprisms](wikipedia://en/2114155).
