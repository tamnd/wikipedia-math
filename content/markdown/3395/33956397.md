# Cognitive holding power

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cognitive holding power"}

**Cognitive holding power** is a concept introduced and measured by John C. Stevenson in 1994 using a [questionnaire](wikipedia://en/956483), the Cognitive Holding Power Questionnaire (CHPQ). This tool assesses first- or second-order [cognitive processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_process) preferences as a result of the characteristics of the learning environment.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Impact {#Impact}

Studies involving cognitive holding power have been able to suggest improvements to mathematical education.[^o5]
