# Martin Eichler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Eichler"}

**Martin Maximilian Emil Eichler** (29 March 1912 – 7 October 1992) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18831).

:::

Eichler received his Ph.D. from the [Martin Luther University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University) of Halle-Wittenberg in 1936.

Eichler and [Goro Shimura](wikipedia://en/174724) developed a method to construct [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) from certain [modular forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_forms). The [converse notion](wikipedia://en/174475) that every elliptic curve has a corresponding modular form would later be the key to the proof of [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953).[^o3][^o4]
