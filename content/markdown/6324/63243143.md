# Dihua Jiang

Jiang Dihua

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dihua Jiang"}

**Dihua Jiang** (, born 1958)[^o3] is a Chinese-born American mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms), and the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

In 1958, Jiang was born in the [Lucheng District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucheng_District,_Wenzhou) of [Wenzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenzhou), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang).[^o5][^o6][^o7] He studied at Wenzhou No. 3 Middle School before studying at [Zhejiang Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_Normal_University), where he received his bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1982.[^o5] He received a master's degree from [East China Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Normal_University) in 1987[^o5] and a PhD in mathematics from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1994 under the supervision of [Stephen Rallis](wikipedia://en/52065873).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Jiang joined the faculty at the Department of Mathematics at the University of Minnesota in 1998 and became a full professor in 2004.[^o5][^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Jiang was a recipient of a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2000,[^o5] was inducted as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2019,[^o12] and was awarded a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Fellow) in 2024.[^o13]
