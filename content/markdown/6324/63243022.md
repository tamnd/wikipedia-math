# David Soudry

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Soudry"}

**David Soudry** (born 1956[^o4]) is a professor of mathematics at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Soudry was born in 1956.[^o4] He received his PhD in mathematics from Tel Aviv University in 1983 under the supervision of [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738). From 1983 to 1984, he was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o6] He is a professor of mathematics at Tel Aviv University.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Together with [Stephen Rallis](wikipedia://en/52065873) and [David Ginzburg](wikipedia://en/58203934), Soudry wrote a series of papers about automorphic descent culminating in their book *The descent map from automorphic representations of GL(*n*) to classical groups.* Their automorphic descent method constructs an explicit inverse map to the (standard) [Langlands functorial lift](wikipedia://en/171905#Functoriality) and has had major applications to the analysis of functoriality.[^o9] Also, using the "Rallis tower property" from Rallis's 1984 paper on the [Howe duality conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_duality_conjecture), they studied global exceptional correspondences and found new examples of functorial lifts.[^o10]
