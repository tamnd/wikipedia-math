# Gail S. Nelson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gail S. Nelson"}

**Gail Susan Nelson** (born 1959) is a mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at [Carleton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_College).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nelson did her undergraduate studies at the [University of North Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Dakota). She completed her Ph.D. in 1988 at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Her dissertation concerned [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and was supervised by Eugene Barry Fabes; it was *Bounds for the Fundamental Solutions of Degenerate Parabolic Partial Differential Equations*. She joined the Carleton College faculty in the same year.

## Books {#Books}

Nelson is the author of two textbooks in mathematics:

- *Recurrence and Topology* (with John M. Alongi, [Graduate Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871519) 85, American Mathematical Society, 2007), on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).
- *A User-Friendly Introduction to Lebesgue Measure and Integration* (Student Mathematical Library 78, American Mathematical Society, 2015), on [Lebesgue integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_integration).

She is also the editor-in-chief of the "Problem Books" book series of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).
