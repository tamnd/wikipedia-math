# Aram Arutyunov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aram Arutyunov"}

Арутюнов, Арам Владимирович

**Aram Arutyunov** (Ара́м Влади́мирович Арутю́нов) (born 1956) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC) and the [Peoples' Friendship University of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoples'_Friendship_University_of_Russia).[^o3]

:::

He defended the thesis «Perturbation of optimal control problems and necessary conditions for the extremum of the first and second order» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1988). and was awarded the title of [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) (1991). He has authored seven books and 318 scientific articles.[^o4][^o5]
