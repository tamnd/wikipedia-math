# Cristina Pereyra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cristina Pereyra"}

**María Cristina Pereyra** (born 1964) is a Venezuelan mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics and statistics at the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico), and the author of several books on [wavelets](wikipedia://en/50903) and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). Pereyra was an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Council member at large from 2019 - 2021.[^o2]

:::

## Education and employment {#Education_and_employment}

Pereyra was a member of the Venezuelan team for the 1981 and 1982 [International Mathematical Olympiads](wikipedia://en/82208). She earned a [licenciado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciado) (the equivalent of a bachelor's degree) in mathematics in 1986 from the [Central University of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_University_of_Venezuela).

She went to [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) for graduate studies, completing her Ph.D. there in 1993. Her dissertation, *Sobolev Spaces On Lipschitz Curves: Paraproducts, Inverses And Some Related Operators*, was supervised by [Peter Jones](wikipedia://en/1604461). After working for three years as an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), she joined the University of New Mexico faculty in 1996.

## Books {#Books}

Pereyra is the author or editor of:

- *Lecture Notes on Dyadic Harmonic Analysis* (Second Summer School in Analysis and [Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/173416), Cuernavaca, 2000; Contemporary Mathematics 289, American Mathematical Society, 2001)
- *Wavelets, Their Friends, and What They Can Do For You* (with Martin Mohlenkamp, EMS Lecture Series in Mathematics, [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470), 2008)
- *Harmonic Analysis: from Fourier to Wavelets* (with [Lesley Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_Ward), Student Mathematical Library 63, American Mathematical Society, 2012)
- *Harmonic Analysis, Partial Differential Equations, Complex Analysis, Banach Spaces, and Operator Theory: Celebrating Cora Sadosky's Life*, Vols. I, II (edited with S. Marcantognini, A. M. Stokolos, and W. Urbina, [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Series, Springer, 2016 and 2017)
