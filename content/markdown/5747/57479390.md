# Alexey Izmailov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexey Izmailov"}

Измаилов, Алексей Феридович

**Alexey Feridovich Izmailov** (Алексе́й Фери́дович Измаи́лов) (born 1967) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician) who is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3][^o4]

:::

He defended the thesis *Stable methods of finding special solutions of non-linear problems* for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) in 1998.

He has authored 12 books and more than 150 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]
