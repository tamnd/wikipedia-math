# Vladimir Zakharov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Zakharov"}

Захаров, Владимир Анатольевич

**Vladimir Zakharov** (Влади́мир Анато́льевич Заха́ров) (born 1960) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3][^o4]

:::

He defended the thesis «The problem of program equivalence: models, algorithms, complexity» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (2012).

Author of 2 books and more than 70 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]
