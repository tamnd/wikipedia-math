# Michel Broué

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Broué"}

**Michel Broué** (born 28 October 1946) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He holds a chair at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). Broué has made contributions to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

:::

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]

He is the son of French historian [Pierre Broué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Broué) and of Simone Charras,[^o4] and the father of French director and screenwriter Isabelle Broué and of French journalist and radio producer [Caroline Broué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Broué).[^o5] He married actress [Anouk Grinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anouk_Grinberg), his partner since 2003, in 2016.[^o5][^o6]

## Biography {#Biography}

After completing his senior year at the high school in [Montereau-Fault-Yonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montereau-Fault-Yonne) (Seine-et-Marne) and preparatory classes at the Lycée Saint-Louis, he enrolled in 1966 at the École normale supérieure de Saint-Cloud,[^o8] from which he graduated in 1970 with an agrégation in mathematics and a postgraduate doctorate (thesis supervised by Claude Chevalley and, in part, by [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483)).
