# Complex convexity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Complex convexity"}

**Complex convexity** is a general term in [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A set $\Omega$ in $\mathbb{C}^n$ is called $\mathbb{C}$-convex if its intersection with any complex line is contractible.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Background {#Background}

In [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), the notion of [convexity](wikipedia://en/245568) and its generalizations play an important role in understanding [function](wikipedia://en/185427) behavior. Examples of classes of functions with a rich structure are, in addition to the convex functions, the [subharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/3237359) and the [plurisubharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/2519085).

Geometrically, these classes of functions correspond to convex domains and pseudoconvex domains, but there are also other types of domains, for instance lineally convex domains which can be generalized using [convex analysis](wikipedia://en/3237201).

A great deal is already known about these domains, but there remain some fascinating, unsolved problems. This theme is mainly theoretical, but there are computational aspects of the domains studied, and these computational aspects are certainly worthy of further study.
