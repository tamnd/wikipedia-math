# George Batchelor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George Batchelor"}

**George Keith Batchelor** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society)[^o3] (8 March 1920 – 30 March 2000) was an Australian [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [fluid dynamicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_dynamicist).

:::

He was for many years a professor of applied mathematics in the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and was founding head of the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics](wikipedia://en/3254271) (DAMTP). In 1956 he founded the influential *[Journal of Fluid Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Fluid_Mechanics)*[^o4] which he edited for some forty years. Prior to Cambridge he studied at [Melbourne High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_High_School_%28Victoria%29) and [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).[^o5]

As an applied mathematician (and for some years at Cambridge a co-worker with [Sir Geoffrey Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Ingram_Taylor) in the field of [turbulent flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbulent_flow)), he was a keen advocate of the need for physical understanding and sound experimental basis.

His *An Introduction to Fluid Dynamics* (CUP, 1967) is still considered a classic of the subject, and has been re-issued in the *Cambridge Mathematical Library* series, following strong current demand.[^o6] Unusual for an 'elementary' textbook of that era, it presented a treatment in which the properties of a real [viscous fluid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscous_liquid) were fully emphasised.[^o7] He was elected a Foreign Honorary Member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1959.[^o8]

The [Batchelor Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batchelor_Prize) award, is named in his honour and is awarded every four years at the meeting of the International Congress on Theoretical and Applied Mechanics.[^o9]
