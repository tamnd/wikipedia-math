# Tadeusz Iwaniec

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tadeusz Iwaniec"}

**Tadeusz Iwaniec** (born October 9, 1947 in [Elbląg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbląg)) is a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and since 1996 John Raymond French Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University).[^o3]

:::

He and mathematician [Henryk Iwaniec](wikipedia://en/9338319) are twin brothers.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Iwaniec was given the Prize of the President of the Polish Academy of Sciences, 1980, the Alfred Jurzykowski Award in Mathematics in 1997, the Prix 2001 Institut Henri-Poincaré Gauthier-Villars,[^o3] and the 2009 Sierpinski Medal of the Polish Mathematical Society and Warsaw University.[^o5] In 1998 he was elected as a foreign member of the Academia di Scienze Fisiche e Matematiche, Italy [^o3] and in 2012 as a foreign member of the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o6]
