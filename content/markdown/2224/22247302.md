# Tony F. Chan

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Tony F. Chan"}

**Tony Fan-Cheong Chan** (Chinese: 陳繁昌; born 20 January 1952) is a Hong Kong mathematician who served as the 3rd president of the [King Abdullah University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdullah_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia) from September 2018 to August 2024. He previously served as 3rd president of the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology) from September 2009 to October 2018.[^o6] Chan has been a professor emeritus of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) since 2009.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Born in Hong Kong, Chan completed his secondary education at [Salesian English School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesian_English_School) and [Queen's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Hong_Kong) in Hong Kong.

Chan received a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) with a major in engineering and a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in aeronautics from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1973. He received a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1978.[^o8]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Before joining [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology), he was the assistant director of the Mathematical and Physical Sciences Directorate at the US [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)[^o10] from 2006 to 2009. He pursued postdoctoral research at Caltech as a research fellow, and taught computer science at Yale University before joining [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) as Professor of Mathematics in 1986.

He was appointed chair of the Department of Mathematics in 1997 and served as dean of physical sciences from 2001 to 2006. He was one of the principal investigators who made the successful proposal to the NSF to form the [Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/12023558), an NSF-funded institute at UCLA. He served as its director from 2000 to 2001.

He has been listed as an ISI Highly Cited Author in Mathematics by the [ISI Web of Knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_Web_of_Knowledge), Thomson Scientific Company.[^o11]

He has been a professor emeritus of mathematics at the University of California, Los Angeles since 2009.[^o12]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- Member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering), 2014, "for numerical techniques applied to image processing and scientific computing, and for providing engineering leadership at the national and international levels."[^o14]
- [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) Fellow, 2016, "for contributions to computational models and algorithms for image processing".[^o15]
- Honorary Doctor of the University, University of Strathclyde, UK, 2015.[^o16]
- Honorary Doctor of Mathematics, University of Waterloo, Canada, 2022.[^o17]
