# Jan te Nijenhuis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan te Nijenhuis"}

**Jan te Nijenhuis** is a Dutch psychologist. He is a lecturer of psychology at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), known for his research on [human intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_intelligence).[^o3][^o4] He studied at [Groningen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_University) and the [Free University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Amsterdam).

:::

His publications on the intelligence of [immigrants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration_to_the_Netherlands) have provoked a lot of criticism and controversy in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands).[^o5][^o6]

He has controversially published several papers in the *[Mankind Quarterly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mankind_Quarterly)* which is widely regarded as a [racist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racist) pseudoscience journal and has attended the [London Conference on Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Conference_on_Intelligence).[^o7][^o8]

In June 2019, te Nijenhuis spoke at a meeting of the JFVD, the youth association of the [far-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-right) [FvD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FvD) party.[^o9]
