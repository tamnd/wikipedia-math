In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **hexagonal tiling-triangular tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858), [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719), and [trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864397) cells, in a [rhombitrihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864311) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A lower symmetry form, index 6, of this honeycomb can be constructed with \[(6,3,6,3<sup>\*</sup>)\] symmetry, represented by a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) fundamental domain, and an octahedral [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram)
