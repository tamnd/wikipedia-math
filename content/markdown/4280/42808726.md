In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **cubic-triangular tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858), and [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) cells, in a [rhombitrihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864311) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.
