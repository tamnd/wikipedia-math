In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **octahedron-hexagonal tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719), and [trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864397) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A lower symmetry form, index 6, of this honeycomb can be constructed with \[(6,3,4,3<sup>\*</sup>)\] symmetry, represented by a [trigonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3504388) fundamental domain, and a [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram)

*Image: CDel\_K6\_634\_10.png.*

.

The **cyclotruncated octahedral-hexagonal tiling honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719), [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), and [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654) cells, in a [triangular antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_antiprism) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a Coxeter diagram .
