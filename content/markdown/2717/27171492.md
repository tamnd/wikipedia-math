# Bertil Gustafsson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bertil Gustafsson"}

**Bertil Gustafsson** (born 1939) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedes) applied mathematician and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506). He is currently a Professor emeritus in the Department of Information Technology at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden).[^o3] Gustafsson is known for his work in [numerical methods](wikipedia://en/21506) for time-dependent [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and its applications in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034). He is the G in GKS (Gustafsson–[Kreiss](wikipedia://en/2070195)–Sundstrom) theory for initial-boundary value problems which discusses the stability criterion for numerical approximations of initial–boundary value problems.[^o4][^o5] Gustafsson has also authored a couple of books on the topic numerical methods applied to PDE.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gustafsson attended senior high school in [Norrköping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norrköping), a city in Sweden. In 1962, he joined Uppsala University, where there was a tradition of prominent mathematicians, including [Arne Beurling](wikipedia://en/286530) and [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541).[^o7] At Uppsala, Gustafsson developed a strong preference for applied mathematics and when he was in his second year, [Heinz-Otto Kreiss](wikipedia://en/2070195) joined Uppsala University. It was Kreiss who introduced young Gustafsson to [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and the then new field of [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing). In 1971, Gustafsson received his Ph.D. degree under the supervision of Kreiss. His dissertation was on difference methods for [hyperbolic equations](wikipedia://en/1575825). Gustafsson has continued to pursue numerical methods in differential equations for most of his career, and much of his early work consisted of applying his theoretical developments to practical problems. These included [weather prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting), both in Sweden and at the [National Center for Atmospheric Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Atmospheric_Research) based in [Boulder, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulder,_Colorado).[^o7]

In 2002, he was elected a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences).
