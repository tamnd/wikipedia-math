# Cédric Villani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cédric Villani"}

**Cédric Patrice Thierry Villani** (; born 5 October 1973) is a French [mathematician and politician](wikipedia://en/61347231) working primarily on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 2010, and he was the director of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University)'s [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) from 2009 to 2017. As of September 2025, he is a professor at the [École normale supérieure de Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Rennes).[^o4]

:::

Villani has given two lectures at the [Royal Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institution), the first titled 'Birth of a Theorem'.[^o5] The English translation of his book *Théorème vivant* (*Living Theorem*) has the same title. In the book he describes the links between his research on [kinetic theory](wikipedia://en/64204) and that of the mathematician [Carlo Cercignani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Cercignani). Villani, in fact, proved the so-called [Cercignani's conjecture](wikipedia://en/79400320). His second lecture at the Royal Institution is titled 'The Extraordinary Theorems of [John Nash](wikipedia://en/102567)'.[^o6]

Villani was elected as the deputy for [Essonne's 5th constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essonne's_5th_constituency) in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_%28France%29), the lower house of the [French Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Parliament), during the [2017 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_French_legislative_election).[^o7] He was elected as a member of [La République En Marche!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_République_En_Marche!) (LREM), but in May 2020 left the party to form a new party, [Ecology, Democracy, Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology,_Democracy,_Solidarity) (EDS).[^o8][^o9] Following the dissolution of EDS, Villani joined [Ecology Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology_Generation), and ran for re-election [in 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_legislative_election) under the banner of the [NUPES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NUPES),[^o10] ultimately [losing his seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_MPs_who_lost_their_seat_in_the_2022_French_legislative_election) to LREM candidate [Paul Midy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Midy) by 19 votes.[^o11]

He was elected vice president of the French [Parliamentary Office for the Evaluation of Scientific and Technological Choices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Office_for_the_Evaluation_of_Scientific_and_Technological_Choices) in July 2017.

## Biography {#Biography}

After attending the [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand), Villani was admitted at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris and studied there from 1992 to 1996, after which he was appointed an [agrégé préparateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégé_préparateur) at the same school.[^o13] He received his doctorate at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University) in 1998, under the supervision of [Pierre-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/1270068), and became professor at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) in 2000. He is now professor at the [University of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lyon). He was director of the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) in Paris from 2009 to 2017.[^o14][^o15]

He has held various visiting positions at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech) (Fall 1999), the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (Spring 2004), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) (Spring 2009).[^o16]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

Villani has worked on the theory of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) involved in [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), specifically the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522), where, with Laurent Desvillettes, he was the first to prove how quickly convergence occurs for initial values not near equilibrium.[^o15] He has written with Giuseppe Toscani on this subject. With [Clément Mouhot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clément_Mouhot), he has worked on nonlinear [Landau damping](wikipedia://en/698720).[^o18] He has worked on the theory of [optimal transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_transport) and its applications to differential geometry, and with [John Lott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lott_%28mathematician%29) has defined a notion of bounded [Ricci curvature](wikipedia://en/276582) for general measured length spaces.[^o19] He also served on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) in 2015 and 2016.

Villani received the Fields Medal for his work on [Landau damping](wikipedia://en/698720) and the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522).[^o15] He described the development of his theorem in his autobiographical book *Théorème vivant* (2012), published in [English translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) as *Birth of a Theorem: A Mathematical Adventure* (2015).[^o20][^o21] He gave a [TED talk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TED_%28conference%29) at the 2016 conference in Vancouver.[^o22]

## Political career {#Political_career}

*Image: Réunion publique de La République En Marche ! Japon, avec le député Cédric Villani le jeudi 26 novembre de à Akasaka, Tokyo.jpg.* Cédric Villani at a public meeting of En Marche in Tokyo

In 2017, it was announced that Villani had been selected as a candidate for *[En Marche!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_République_En_Marche!)* (LREM) in the [2017 French legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_French_legislative_election), for [Essonne's 5th constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essonne's_5th_constituency).[^o24][^o25] In the first round of voting, Villani obtained 47% of the vote and was thus strongly placed for the second round[^o26] which he won with 69.36% of the vote.[^o27]

In 2019, Villani applied to be selected to lead the LREM candidate slate for [the 2020 Paris election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_municipal_elections). By July 2019, he was one of three LREM candidates, all [deputies in the](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_deputies_of_the_15th_National_Assembly_of_France) [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_%28France%29), still seeking the position; the other two were [Benjamin Griveaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Griveaux) (who had been the [government spokesperson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Government_spokespeople_of_France)) and [Hugues Renson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugues_Renson) (who had been the vice-president of the National Assembly). On 10 July, the nomination committee picked Griveaux.[^o28] On 4 September, Villani officially announced his candidacy for the municipal election.[^o29]

## Other activities {#Other_activities}

- France China Foundation, former Member of the Strategic Committee[^o31]

In 2018, the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine) [Challenges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenges_%28magazine%29) said that Villani has been approached by Europanova [thinktank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinktank). He presided over the jury of Digital In-Pulse, a startup program dedicated to accompanying entrepreneurs and start-ups, managed by [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) [corporate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate) [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei). The magazine also says that Villani is still the President of the endowment funds of the French [Henri Poincaré Institute](wikipedia://en/10513595), and [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei) is among the top private donors.[^o32] Villani declined the journalist's request for comment, and the article indicates that the French [counter-intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-intelligence) service presented him as "too naïve" regarding those opportunities.

### Extra-academic distinctions {#Extra-academic_distinctions}

- 2011: Knight of the [Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honor)

- 2022: Fellow of the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council)

In 2020, a supposed new spider species of the family [Araneidae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araneidae), *Araniella villanii*, was named after him. However, this species was later shown to be a junior synonym of *[Araniella tbilisiensis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araniella_tbilisiensis)*.[^o40]
