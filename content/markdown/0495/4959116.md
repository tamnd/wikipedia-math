# Poinsot's spirals

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poinsot's spirals"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Poinsot's spirals** are two [spirals](wikipedia://en/61563) represented by the [polar equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_equation)

:::

$$r = a\ \operatorname{csch} (n\theta)$$

$$r = a\ \operatorname{sech} (n\theta)$$

where csch is the [hyperbolic cosecant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_cosecant), and sech is the [hyperbolic secant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_secant).[^o2] They are named after the French mathematician [Louis Poinsot](wikipedia://en/764639).
