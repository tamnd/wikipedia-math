# Arnold Walfisz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arnold Walfisz"}

**Arnold Walfisz** (2 July 1892 – 29 May 1962) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Walfisz was born in 1892 in a [Polish-Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Jews_in_Poland) family. After the *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* in Warsaw, Poland, he studied (1909−14 and 1918−21) in Germany at [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen). [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230) was his doctoral-thesis supervisor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). Walfisz lived in [Wiesbaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesbaden) from 1922 through 1927, then he returned to Warsaw, worked at an insurance company and at the mathematical institute of the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) obtaining his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1930.[^o6] In 1935, together with Salomon Lubelski, he founded the mathematical journal *[Acta Arithmetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Arithmetica)*. In 1936, Walfisz became professor at the [University of Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University) in [Soviet Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Georgia). He wrote approximately 100 mathematical articles and three books.

## Work {#Work}

By using a theorem by [Carl Ludwig Siegel](wikipedia://en/716602) providing an upper bound for the real zeros (see [Siegel zero](wikipedia://en/1967197)) of [Dirichlet L-functions](wikipedia://en/400092) formed with real non-principal characters, Walfisz obtained the [Siegel–Walfisz theorem](wikipedia://en/5075154), from which the [prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/23692#Prime_number_theorem_for_arithmetic_progressions) can be deduced.

By using estimates on exponential sums due to [I. M. Vinogradov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Matveyevich_Vinogradov) and N.M. Korobov , Walfisz obtained the currently best [O-estimates](wikipedia://en/44578) for the remainder terms of the summatory functions of both the sum-of-divisors function $\sigma$ and the [Euler function $\phi$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler's_phi) (in: "Weylsche Exponentialsummen in der neueren Zahlentheorie", see below).
