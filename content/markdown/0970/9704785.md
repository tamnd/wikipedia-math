# John Friedlander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Friedlander"}

**John Friedlander** is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513). He received his B.Sc. from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1965, an M.A. from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 1966, and a Ph.D. from [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1972. He was a lecturer at [M.I.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.I.T.) in 1974–1976, and has been on the faculty of the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) since 1977, where he served as [Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_%28academic%29) during 1987–1991. He has also spent several years at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). In addition to his individual work, he has been notable for his collaborations with other well-known number theorists, including Enrico Bombieri, [William Duke](wikipedia://en/50393209), [Andrew Granville](wikipedia://en/2910434), and especially [Henryk Iwaniec](wikipedia://en/9338319).[^o3]

:::

In 1997, in joint work with [Henryk Iwaniec](wikipedia://en/9338319), Friedlander proved that infinitely many [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers) can be obtained as the sum of a square and fourth power: $a^{2} + b^{4}$.[^o4][^o5] Friedlander and Iwaniec improved [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475)'s "asymptotic sieve" technique to construct their proof.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 1999, Friedlander received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069).
- In 1988, Friedlander became a fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o8]
- In 2002, [CRM-Fields-PIMS prize](wikipedia://en/19284033)
- In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
- In 2017, he received the [Joseph L. Doob prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_L._Doob_prize), jointly with Henryk Iwaniec, for their book *Opera de Cribro*.[^o10]
