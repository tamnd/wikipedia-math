# Elektronika MK-18M

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elektronika MK-18M"}

The [Elektronika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektronika) MK-18M (Электро́ника МК-18М) was a [scientific calculator](wikipedia://en/439711) manufactured in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). It was released in 1986.

*Image: MK18M.JPG.*

:::

## Technical specifications {#Technical_specifications}

Source:[^o3]

- Display: [vacuum fluorescent display](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_fluorescent_display), green color, contains 8 digits + minus sign + error sign
- Power: 4 x AA batteries or AC adapter (with charging function) with 3-pin connector, power consumption ≤0.7W
- 20 buttons
- Case: [aluminium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium) + [plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic)
- Supported numbers range: ±(10E-7)...±(10E8-1)
- Size: 170x86.5x27 mm, weighing around 350 [grams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram)

## Novelty {#Novelty}

The Elektronika MK-18M calculator has no new novel functionality. Despite this, the aluminum casing was an improvement from previous models' plastic casing. In addition, the model was produced at Billur ([Kirovograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganja,_Azerbaijan)), in contrast to the Elektronica B3-18 and B3-18A models which were manufactured at [Angstrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angstrem_%28company%29) ([Zelenograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zelenograd)) and NPO Elektronika ([Boguchar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boguchar), [Voronezh Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh_Region)).
