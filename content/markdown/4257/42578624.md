# Brouwer Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brouwer Medal"}

The **Brouwer Medal** is a triennial award presented by the [Royal Dutch Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/43504918) and the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Sciences). The Brouwer Metal gets its name from Dutch mathematician [L. E. J. Brouwer](wikipedia://en/172896) and is the Netherlands’ most prestigious award in mathematics.[^o2]

:::
