# Doi–Naganuma lifting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Doi–Naganuma lifting"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Doi–Naganuma lifting** is a map from [elliptic modular forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_modular_form) to [Hilbert modular forms](wikipedia://en/2333880) of a [real quadratic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_quadratic_field), introduced by (Doi Naganuma 1969) and (Naganuma 1973). It was a precursor of the [base change lifting](wikipedia://en/32338538).

:::

It is named for Japanese mathematicians Kōji Doi (土井公二) and Hidehisa Naganuma (長沼英久).
