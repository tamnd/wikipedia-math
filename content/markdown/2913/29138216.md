# Freydoon Shahidi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Freydoon Shahidi"}

**Freydoon Shahidi** (فریدون شهیدی; born June 19, 1947) is an [Iranian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in the U.S. He is known for a method of [automorphic](wikipedia://en/782146) [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283) which is now known as the [Langlands–Shahidi method](wikipedia://en/31789393).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Shahidi graduated from the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran) with a bachelor's degree in 1969. He received his Ph.D. in 1975 from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) with dissertation *On Gauss Sums Attached to the Pairs and the Exterior Powers of the Representations of the General Linear Groups over Finite and Local Fields* with advisor [Joseph Shalika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Shalika). As a postdoc Shahidi was for the academic year 1975–1976 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and for the academic year 1976–1977 a visiting assistant professor at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) in Bloomington. At Purdue University he became in 1977 an assistant professor, in 1982 an associate professor, and 1986 a full professor. There he since 2001 is a Distinguished Professor.

He returned to the Institute for Advanced Study in 1983–1984, in 1990–1991, in October–November 1999, and in several other brief visits. He has held visiting positions from 1981 to 1982 at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), in June 1990 at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII), in May 1993 and again in February 1997 as a Fellow of the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science at [Kyōto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyōto_University), in June–July 1993 at the [Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Eichstätt-Ingolstadt), in May–June 1995 at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure), and at several other institutions.

Shahidi was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) for the academic year 2001–2002.[^o5] He was in 2002 an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) with talk *Automorphic L-functions and functoriality*.[^o6] He is a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) since 2010.[^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He is a member of the editorial board of the *[American Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Journal_of_Mathematics)*.
