# William M. Boothby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William M. Boothby"}

**William Munger Boothby** (April 1, 1918 – February 14, 2021) was an American mathematician and professor emeritus of mathematics at [Arts and Sciences at Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_and_Sciences_at_Washington_University_in_St._Louis),[^o3] known for his work in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) including the book *An introduction to differentiable manifolds and Riemannian geometry* (1975; 2nd ed. 1986).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Boothby was originally from [Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit). He received a six-year scholarship to attend the [Cranbrook Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cranbrook_Academy_of_Art) in [Bloomfield Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomfield_Hills,_Michigan), and completed a degree at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1940. His graduate studies in mathematics were interrupted by [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II); he became a pilot for the [United States Army Air Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Air_Forces) between 1942 and 1945.[^o6]

He would complete a PhD at University of Michigan in 1949.[^o3] His dissertation, *A Topological Study of the Level Curves of Harmonic Functions*, was supervised by [Wilfred Kaplan](wikipedia://en/46189346).

He then joined as junior faculty [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). A one-year fellowship at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) followed in 1950-51. He joined as a professor the Mathematics Department at the [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), until his retirement in 1988. In 1960-61 he visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o3][^o6]

## Research {#Research}

His early research concerned [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), including [harmonic functions](wikipedia://en/55762), [Hermitian manifolds](wikipedia://en/2640459) and [contact structures](wikipedia://en/476398). In particular, he worked on contact manifolds together with [Hsien Chung Wang](wikipedia://en/50742441), describing what is now known as the [Boothby-Wang construction](wikipedia://en/84007440).[^o6]

In 1975 he published a very popular textbook, *An Introduction to Differentiable Manifolds and Riemannian Geometry*, which "defined the curriculum and standard of the introductory graduate differential geometry course worldwide".[^o4][^o6]

After publishing this book, his research interests shifted to [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).[^o3][^o6]
