# Paul Harzer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Harzer"}

**Paul Harzer** (1857, [Großenhain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Großenhain) – 1932) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) best known for his papers arguing with [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736) regarding the [Sagnac effect](wikipedia://en/1636296) and its relationship to [Special Relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Relativity). Harzer was Professor of Astronomy at the University of Kiel and Director of its observatory.[^o2] His published article on the experiment of [Franz Harress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Harress) drew two reply articles from Einstein.

:::
