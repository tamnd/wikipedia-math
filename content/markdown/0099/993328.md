# Contingent cooperator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Contingent cooperator"}

In [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), a **contingent cooperator** is a person or agent who is willing to act in the collective interest, rather than his short-term selfish interest, if he observes a majority of the other agents in the collective doing the same. The apparent contradiction in this stance is resolved by [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), which shows that in the right circumstances, cooperation with a sufficient number of other participants will have a better outcome for cooperators than pursuing short-term selfish interests.

:::
