# 2000 Estonian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2000 Estonian census"}

**2000 Population and Housing Census** (**PHC 2000**) (Rahva ja eluruumide loendus (*REL 2000*)) was a [census](wikipedia://en/6889) that was carried out during 31 March 2000 – 9 April 2000 in [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) by [Statistics Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Estonia).[^o2]

:::

The total population recorded was 1,370,052 persons and 67.9% of them were Estonians. Compared to the [1989 Estonia Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Estonia_Census), the population had decreased by 195,000 persons (12.5%).[^o2]
