# Theodore James Courant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore James Courant"}

**Theodore James "Ted" Courant** is an American mathematician who has conducted research in the fields of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586). In particular, he made seminal contributions to the study of Dirac manifolds,[^o2][^o3] which generalize both [symplectic manifolds](wikipedia://en/28356) and [Poisson manifolds](wikipedia://en/882902), and are related to the [Dirac theory of constraints](wikipedia://en/13657747) in physics. Some mathematical objects in this field have since been named after him, including the [Courant bracket](wikipedia://en/6855275) and [Courant algebroid](wikipedia://en/10109649).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Courant received his B.A. degree from [Reed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_College),[^o5] and his Ph.D. from [The University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he was a student of [Alan Weinstein](wikipedia://en/7344764).

After teaching at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), Courant moved to secondary education at private schools in California including [The Branson School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Branson_School) and [Wildwood School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildwood_School).[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Ted Courant is the grandson of [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238).[^o8]
