# Web intelligence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Web intelligence"}

**Web intelligence** is the area of scientific research and development that explores the roles and makes use of [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) and [information technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology) for new products, services and frameworks that are empowered by the [World Wide Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Web).[^o2]

:::

The term was coined in a paper written by Ning Zhong, Jiming Liu Yao and Y.Y. Ohsuga in the Computer Software and Applications Conference in 2000.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

The research about the web intelligence covers many fields – including [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253) (in particular [web mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_mining)), [information retrieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_retrieval), [pattern recognition](wikipedia://en/126706), [predictive analytics](wikipedia://en/4141563), the [semantic web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_web), web [data warehousing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_warehouse) – typically with a focus on [web personalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_personalization) and [adaptive websites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_website).[^o5]
