# Carrie Ichikawa Jenkins

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Carrie Ichikawa Jenkins"}

**Carrie Ichikawa Jenkins** is a Canadian philosopher who holds a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair)[^o3] and is Professor of Philosophy at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia).[^o4] She is also a professor at the Northern Institute of Philosophy, [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen).[^o5] Her primary research areas are [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).[^o6] She is one of the principal editors of the journal *[Thought](wikipedia://en/38464325)*.[^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jenkins holds BA, MPhil and PhD degrees in philosophy from [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge).[^o6]

She has held teaching and research positions at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham).[^o6] She was head of the department of philosophy at the University of Nottingham from 2010 to 2011.[^o6]

Jenkins was awarded a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) by the Canadian Government in 2011.[^o3]

Jenkins is also a member of the philosophy-themed musical group The 21st Century Monads.[^o9]

Jenkins has advocated for a more holistic view of academic life, blending creative writing, public engagement, and even tarot readings into her philosophical practice. She refers to this interdisciplinary integration as a form of "life crafting" and "love crafting," emphasizing flexibility and resistance to institutional constraints.[^o10]

Jenkins became involved in a dispute with [Brian Leiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Leiter), a professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and then editor of the [Philosophical Gourmet Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophical_Gourmet_Report). His emailed remarks about Jenkins led to hundreds of philosophers refusing to provide data for the report, and ultimately led to his stepping down as its editor.[^o11][^o12]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

As of 2017, Jenkins has both a boyfriend and a husband. That year she stated, "I've been openly nonmonogamous for five or six years. Over the last four years, I’ve been involved in several long-term simultaneous relationships, and so the term [polyamory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyamory) has felt more appropriate."[^o14][^o15]
