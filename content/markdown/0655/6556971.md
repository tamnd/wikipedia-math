# Outline of calculus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of calculus"}

**[Calculus](wikipedia://en/5176)** is a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) focused on [limits](wikipedia://en/26551602), [functions](wikipedia://en/185427), [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921), [integrals](wikipedia://en/15532), and [infinite series](wikipedia://en/15287). This subject constitutes a major part of contemporary [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471). Calculus has widespread applications in [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) and can solve many problems for which [algebra](wikipedia://en/9710) alone is insufficient.

:::
