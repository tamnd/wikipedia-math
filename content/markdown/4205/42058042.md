# Cox ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cox ring"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Cox ring** (or **total coordinate ring**) is a sort of universal [homogeneous coordinate ring](wikipedia://en/23833909) for a [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), and is (roughly speaking) a [direct sum](wikipedia://en/2084687) of the spaces of sections of all isomorphism classes of [line bundles](wikipedia://en/361366).

:::

Cox rings were introduced by Hu and Keel in 2000,[^n1] based on an earlier construction by [David A. Cox](wikipedia://en/41248158) in 1995 for [toric varieties](wikipedia://en/737087).[^n2]
