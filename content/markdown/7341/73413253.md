# Keisha Cook

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Keisha Cook"}

academics

**Keisha Cook** is an assistant professor for the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Cook was born in the suburbs just outside Marietta, Georgia.[^o4] After receiving her Bachelor of Science from the [University of Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alabama_at_Birmingham) for applied mathematics she then began work on her Masters of Arts, which she completed in 2016.[^o5][^o6]

## Education and research {#Education_and_research}

Cook received her Ph.D. in 2019 for Applied Mathematics and Computational Biology at the University of Alabama under the supervision of [Rodger B. Sidje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodger_B._Sidje); her thesis concerned [parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) [stochastic simulation](wikipedia://en/7210212) of biochemical reaction systems.[^o8] She held a postdoctoral position jointly at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) and the Southeast Center for Mathematics and Biology.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2021 Cook was a part of a team that received a \$3,799 grant to fund their Math for All conference.[^o11] Later the same year a \$49,951 for the annual Math for All conference was granted to the same team.[^o12] In 2022 Dr. Cook received a grant from the Clemson University research fellows for \$150,000.[^o13]

Cook has been invited to many talks including a two part talk series at the University of Alabama in 2020, and a collaborative [AWM](wikipedia://en/9858698) and [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) meeting in 2021.[^o14][^o15]

In 2022 Cook was awarded a [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Early Career Fellowship.[^o16]

In 2024, Cook was named a Gilliam Advisor to Gilliam Fellow Jakini Kauba. They were one of 50 mentor/student pairs taking part in a prestigious program recognizing outstanding advisor/graduate student partnerships organized by the [Howard Hughes Medical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Hughes_Medical_Institute).[^o17]
