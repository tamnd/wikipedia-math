# Nandi Olive Leslie

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nandi Olive Leslie"}

**Nandi Olive Leslie** is an [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) and senior engineering fellow at [Raytheon Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raytheon_Technologies).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

**Leslie** grew up in [Evanston, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Illinois) where her father, Joshua Leslie, was a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). She enrolled in university math programs at Northwestern and would accompany her father to various math conferences and speeches around the United States.[^o4]

Leslie graduated *[magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_cum_laude)* with a B.S. in Mathematics from [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University)[^o8] and then a M.A.[^o5] and PhD in Applied and Computational Mathematics and Ecology and Evolutionary Biology from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). Her thesis at Princeton was *Spatial Stochastic Models for Forest Degradation and Deforestation in [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia) and Brazil*.

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Leslie serves as a Senior Engineering Fellow at [Raytheon Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raytheon_Technologies) and currently serves as the Raytheon Intelligence and Space Internal Research Development Chief Engineer and Chief [Data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist). Her research interests include [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), stochastic processes, [cybersecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybersecurity), and sensor performance.[^o8] In 2019, Leslie became the first African American woman at Raytheon to achieve the distinction of engineering fellow.[^o4] She currently serves on five different scientific advisory boards and was received the Black Engineer of the Year Award for Outstanding Technical Contribution in Industry in 2020.[^o8]

Beginning in 2020, Leslie has served as a Lecturer and Research Advisor for Master's Degree Theses and Computational Mathematics and Data Science Programs at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University).[^o8]
