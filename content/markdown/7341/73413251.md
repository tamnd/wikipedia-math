# Mimi Dai

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mimi Dai"}

**Mimi Dai** is a mathematician who specializes in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), and [complex fluids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_fluids). She is a professor at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dai received her PhD in applied mathematics from the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) in 2012. Her dissertation, *Nematic liquid crystal systems and magneto-hydrodynamics system: The properties of their solutions,* was supervised by her advisor, [Maria E. Schonbek](wikipedia://en/44807542). She is currently a professor in the Department of Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago.[^o6] She specializes in partial differential equations, fluid dynamics, complex fluids.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Dai became an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Centennial Fellow in 2022.[^o8] In 2021 Dai also became an [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (IAS) von Neuman Fellow in 2021.[^o9] Dai has been an IAS Scholar since 2022.[^o10] In 2018 Dai was awarded a [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (NSF) grant for *Mathematical Studies of Magnetohydrodynamic Flows with Hall Effect*,[^o11] and another grant from the NSF for *Mathematical Analysis of Magnetohydrodynamic Flows with Hall Effect* in 2020.[^o12]
