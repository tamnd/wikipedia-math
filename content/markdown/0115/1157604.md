# Raking

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raking"}

**Raking** (also called "raking ratio estimation" or "[iterative proportional fitting](wikipedia://en/23416870)") is the statistical process of adjusting data sample weights of a contingency table to match desired marginal totals.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
