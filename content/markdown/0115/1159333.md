# Chartered Mathematician

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chartered Mathematician"}

**Chartered Mathematician** (**CMath**) is a [professional qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_certification) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) awarded to professional practising mathematicians by the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) (IMA) in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).

:::

Chartered Mathematician is the IMA's highest professional qualification; achieving it is done through a rigorous peer-reviewed process.

The required standard[^o2] for Chartered Mathematician registration is typically an accredited UK [MMath degree](wikipedia://en/2108291), at least five years of peer-reviewed professional practise of advanced Mathematics, attainment of a senior-level of technical standing, and an ongoing commitment to Continuing Professional Development.

A Chartered Mathematician is entitled to use the post-nominal letters **CMath**, in accordance with the [Royal Charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_charter) granted to the IMA by the [Privy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privy_Council_of_the_United_Kingdom). The profession of Chartered Mathematician is a 'regulated profession' under the [European professional qualification directives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_professional_qualification_directives).[^o3]
