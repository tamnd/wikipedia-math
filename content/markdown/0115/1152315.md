# Rokhlin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rokhlin"}

Рохлин **Rokhlin** is a Slavic language-influenced [Jewish surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_surname) of [matronymic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matronymic) derivation. It literally means "Rokhl's", where "Rokhl" is a transcription of *Rochl*, a [Yiddish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiddish) form of the name [Rachel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_%28given_name%29). Variants include **Rohlin**, **Rochlin** and (via French) **Rochline**. The feminine form in Slavic cultures is Rokhlina / Rochlina.

:::
