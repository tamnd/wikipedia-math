# List of Indian mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Indian mathematicians"}

*Image: Srinivasa Ramanujan - OPC - 1.jpg.* [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717)

Indian mathematicians have made a number of contributions to mathematics that have significantly influenced scientists and mathematicians in the modern era. One of such works is [Hindu numeral system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_numeral_system) which is predominantly used today and is likely to be used in the future.

:::
