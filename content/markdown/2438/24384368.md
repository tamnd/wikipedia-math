# Tutte 12-cage

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tutte 12-cage"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Tutte 12-cage** or **Benson graph**[^o13] is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 126 vertices and 189 edges. It is named after [W. T. Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021).

:::

The Tutte 12-cage is the unique (3-12)-[cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cage_graph) . It was discovered by C. T. Benson in 1966.[^o4] It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2 ([bipartite](wikipedia://en/244431)), [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, girth 12 (as a 12-cage) and diameter 6. Its [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298594) is known to be less than 165, [see Wolfram MathWorld.](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Tutte12-Cage.html)[^o5][^o6]

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The Tutte 12-cage is a [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and can be defined by the [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720) \[17, 27, −13, −59, −35, 35, −11, 13, −53, 53, −27, 21, 57, 11, −21, −57, 59, −17\]<sup>7</sup>.[^o9]

There are, up to isomorphism, precisely two [generalized hexagons](wikipedia://en/4027364) of order *(2,2)* as proved by Cohen and Tits. They are the split Cayley hexagon *H(2)* and its point-line dual. Clearly both of them have the same incidence graph, which is in fact isomorphic to the Tutte 12-cage.[^o13]

The [Balaban 11-cage](wikipedia://en/23761806) can be constructed by excision from the Tutte 12-cage by removing a small subtree and suppressing the resulting vertices of degree two.[^o10]

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Tutte 12-cage is of order 12096 and is a [semi-direct product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-direct_product) of the [projective special unitary group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_unitary_group) PSU(3,3) with the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) **Z**/2**Z**.[^o13] It acts transitively on its edges but not on its vertices, making it a [semi-symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/2554240), a regular graph that is [edge-transitive](wikipedia://en/584406) but not [vertex-transitive](wikipedia://en/581175). In fact, the automorphism group of the Tutte 12-cage preserves the bipartite parts and acts primitively on each part. Such graphs are called bi-primitive graphs and only five cubic bi-primitive graphs exist; they are named the Iofinova-Ivanov graphs and are of order 110, 126, 182, 506 and 990.[^o14]

All the cubic semi-symmetric graphs on up to 768 vertices are known. According to [Conder](wikipedia://en/38305099), Malnič, Marušič and Potočnik, the Tutte 12-cage is the unique cubic semi-symmetric graph on 126 vertices and is the fifth smallest possible cubic semi-symmetric graph after the [Gray graph](wikipedia://en/2554738), the [Iofinova–Ivanov graph on 110 vertices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/110-vertex_Iofinova-Ivanov_graph), the [Ljubljana graph](wikipedia://en/24131381) and a graph on 120 vertices with girth 8.[^o15]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Tutte 12-cage is

$$(x-3)x^{28}(x+3)(x^2-6)^{21}(x^2-2)^{27}.\$$

It is the only graph with this characteristic polynomial; therefore, the 12-cage is determined by its [spectrum](wikipedia://en/534914).
