# Pierre Humbert (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Humbert"}

**Pierre Humbert** (13 June 1891, [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) – 17 November 1953, [Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montpellier)) was a French mathematician who worked on the theory of [elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/69939) and introduced [Humbert polynomials](wikipedia://en/33013259). He was the son of the mathematician [Georges Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Humbert) and married the daughter of [Henri Andoyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Henri_Andoyer).

:::

Pierre Humbert was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 in Bologna.[^o2]
