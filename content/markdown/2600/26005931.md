# Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology** is a *p*-adic [cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_theory) defined for non-singular [affine varieties](wikipedia://en/320468) over [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) of positive [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) *p* introduced by (txt), who were motivated by the work of (txt). The idea is to [lift](wikipedia://en/10504570) the variety to characteristic 0, and then take a suitable subalgebra of the [algebraic de Rham cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_de_Rham_cohomology) of (Grothendieck 1966). The construction was simplified by (van der Put 1986). Its extension to more general varieties is called [rigid cohomology](wikipedia://en/26002282).

:::
