# HN group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="HN group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **HN group** or **hypernormalizing group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) with the property that the [hypernormalizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypernormalizer) of any [subnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/2774415) is the whole group.

:::

For [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707), this is equivalent to the condition that the [normalizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalizer) of any subnormal subgroup be subnormal.

Some facts about HN groups:

- Subgroups of [solvable](wikipedia://en/105012) HN groups are solvable HN groups.
- [Metanilpotent](wikipedia://en/4939343) [A-groups](wikipedia://en/4938898) are HN groups.
