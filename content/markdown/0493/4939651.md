# Noga Alon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noga Alon"}

**Noga M. Alon** (נוגה אלון; born 1956) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is professor emeritus at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) and a professor of mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) noted for his contributions to [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392), having authored hundreds of papers.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Alon was born in 1956 in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), where he graduated from the [Hebrew Reali School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_Reali_School) in 1974. He graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in 1979, earned a master's degree in mathematics in 1980 from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University),[^o5] and received his Ph.D. in Mathematics at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1983 with the dissertation *Extremal Problems in Combinatorics* supervised by [Micha Perles](wikipedia://en/35294296).

After postdoctoral research at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), Alon returned to Tel Aviv University as a senior lecturer in 1985, obtained a permanent position as an associate professor there in 1986, and was promoted to full professor in 1988. He was head of the School of Mathematical Science from 1999 to 2001, and was awarded the Florence and Ted Baumritter Combinatorics and Computer Science Chair.[^o5] He retired as professor emeritus from Tel Aviv and became a professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2018.[^o6]

He was editor-in-chief of the journal *Random Structures and Algorithms* from 2008 through 2023.[^o39]

## Research {#Research}

Alon has published more than five hundred research papers, mostly in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392), and one book, on the [probabilistic method](wikipedia://en/173525).[^o8][^o9] He has also published under the pseudonym "A. Nilli", based on the name of his daughter Nilli Alon.[^o10]

His research contributions include the [combinatorial Nullstellensatz](wikipedia://en/7960510#Combinatorial_Nullstellensatz), an algebraic tool with applications in combinatorics; [color-coding](wikipedia://en/22469695), a technique for [fixed-parameter tractability](wikipedia://en/603026) of pattern-matching algorithms in graphs; and the [Alon–Boppana bound](wikipedia://en/62480574) in [spectral graph theory](wikipedia://en/534914).

## Awards {#Awards}

Alon has received a number of awards, including the following:

- 1989 – [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize);[^o23]
- 2000 – [George Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize) in Applied Combinatorics of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376)[^o24]
- 2001 – Michael Bruno Memorial Award of the Israel Institute for Advanced Studies;[^o25]
- 2005 – [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize), with [Yossi Matias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yossi_Matias) and [Mario Szegedy](wikipedia://en/4291061), for their paper "The space complexity of approximating the frequency moments" on [streaming algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming_algorithm)[^o26]
- 2008 – [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize), for mathematics.[^o27][^o28]
- 2011 – [EMET Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMET_Prize), with [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211), for mathematics.[^o29]
- 2019 – [Paris Kanellakis Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Kanellakis_Award), with [Phillip Gibbons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillip_Gibbons), [Yossi Matias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yossi_Matias) and [Mario Szegedy](wikipedia://en/4291061), "for foundational work on streaming algorithms and their application to large scale data analytics"[^o30]
- 2021 – [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition, with [Joel Spencer](wikipedia://en/4244250), for *The Probabilistic Method*[^o31]
- 2022 – [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in Mathematical Sciences, with [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981), "for their remarkable contributions to discrete mathematics and model theory with interaction notably with algebraic geometry, topology and computer sciences"[^o32]
- 2022 – [Knuth Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knuth_Prize), "for foundational contributions in combinatorics and graph theory and applications to fundamental topics in computer science"[^o33]
- 2024 – [Wolf Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/714278) "for his fundamental contributions to Combinatorics and Theoretical Computer Science”.

Alon gave plenary addresses at the 1996 [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846), at the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842),[^o39] at the 2009 Turán Memorial Lectures,[^o34] and a lecture in the 1990 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o39] In 2015 he gave the [Łojasiewicz Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łojasiewicz_Lecture) (on the "Signrank and its applications in combinatorics and complexity") at the [Jagiellonian University in Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o35] He was given an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) by [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 2013[^o36] and by the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 2015.[^o37]

In addition, Alon has been a member of the [Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) since 1997.[^o38] He was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2008.[^o39] In 2015 he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o40] In 2017 he became a [Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery).[^o41] In 2019 he was named an honorary member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o42]

In 2024, Alon gave the opening plenary talk on "Distance problems for typical norms" at the 33rd Cumberland Conference on Combinatorics, Graph Theory, and Computing at [Mississippi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_State_University).[^o43]
