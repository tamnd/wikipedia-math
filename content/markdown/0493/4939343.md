# Metanilpotent group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Metanilpotent group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **metanilpotent group** is a group that is nilpotent by nilpotent. In other words, it has a normal [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/144569) subgroup such that the [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526) is also nilpotent.

:::

In symbols, $G$ is metanilpotent if there is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) $N$ such that both $N$ and $G/N$ are nilpotent.

The following are clear:

- Every metanilpotent group is a [solvable group](wikipedia://en/105012).
- Every subgroup and every quotient of a metanilpotent group is metanilpotent.
