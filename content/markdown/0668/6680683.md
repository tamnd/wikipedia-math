# Cubic form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic form"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **cubic form** is a [homogeneous polynomial](wikipedia://en/1698977) of degree 3, and a **cubic hypersurface** is the [zero set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_set) of a cubic form. In the case of a cubic form in three variables, the zero set is a [cubic plane curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_plane_curve).

:::

In (Delone Faddeev 1964), [Boris Delone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Delone) and [Dmitry Faddeev](wikipedia://en/48446570) showed that binary cubic forms with integer coefficients can be used to parametrize [orders](wikipedia://en/1114171) in [cubic fields](wikipedia://en/22076179). Their work was generalized in (Gan Gross Savin 2002, §4) to include all cubic rings (a **cubic ring** is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) that is isomorphic to **Z**<sup>3</sup> as a [**Z**-module](wikipedia://en/276410)),[^o4] giving a [discriminant](wikipedia://en/55607)-preserving [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) between [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) of a GL(2, **Z**)-[action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on the space of integral binary cubic forms and cubic rings up to [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828).

The classification of real cubic forms $a x^3 + 3 b x^2 y + 3 c x y^2 + d y^3$ is linked to the classification of [umbilical points](wikipedia://en/9757480) of surfaces. The [equivalence classes](wikipedia://en/9260) of such cubics form a three-dimensional [real projective space](wikipedia://en/1014694) and the subset of [parabolic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabolic_form) define a surface – the [umbilic torus](wikipedia://en/4910264).[^o5]
