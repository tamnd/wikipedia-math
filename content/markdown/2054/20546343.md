# Endoscopic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Endoscopic group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **endoscopic groups** of [reductive algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) were introduced by (txt) in his work on the [stable trace formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_trace_formula).

:::

Roughly speaking, an endoscopic group *H* of *G* is a [quasi-split group](wikipedia://en/34287621) whose [L-group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands_dual) is the connected component of the centralizer of a semisimple element of the L-group of *G*.

In the [stable trace formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_trace_formula), unstable [orbital integrals](wikipedia://en/23462941) on a group *G* correspond to stable orbital integrals on its endoscopic groups *H*. The relation between them is given by the [fundamental lemma](wikipedia://en/25431009).
