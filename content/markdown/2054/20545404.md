# Knots in Washington

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Knots in Washington"}

**Knots in Washington** is an international conference on [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) and its ramifications held twice a year since 1995. The main organizers are [Józef Przytycki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Przytycki), Alexander Shumakovitch, Yongwu Rong and [Valentina Harizanov](wikipedia://en/23587882), all of whom are at [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University).[^o2]

:::

This conference has become an important [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) event in the [Washington Metropolitan Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Metropolitan_Area) and regularly attracts well-known topologists from other areas of the US and from other countries.[^o3] For example, Knots in Washington XVIII, held in May 2004, was the first conference fully devoted to the [Khovanov homology](wikipedia://en/2639906), with [Mikhail Khovanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Khovanov) giving a series of talks and leading experts [Dror Bar-Natan](wikipedia://en/10363723), Lev Rozansky, [Oleg Viro](wikipedia://en/14465687), and [Ciprian Manolescu](wikipedia://en/3034280) giving plenary talks.[^o4] Knots in Washington XX was dedicated to the 60th birthday of [Louis H. Kauffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_H._Kauffman).[^o5] Other related conferences include Knots in Poland (1995, 2003),[^o6] and Knots in Hellas in 1998, where [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) winner [Vaughan Jones](wikipedia://en/1227628) spoke about his work on [knot invariants](wikipedia://en/166898).[^o7]

Knots in Washington 50 will take place Dec 6th-8th, 2024 at the George Washington University with updated website: https://blogs.gwu.edu/ccas-knotsinwashington/
