# Simon Sheather

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simon Sheather"}

**Simon J. Sheather** is an Australian-American academic. He became the 8th dean of the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky)’s [Gatton College of Business and Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatton_College_of_Business_and_Economics) on July 1st, 2018.[^o2][^o3] A Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), Sheather is known for the Sheather-Jones bandwidth selection method for [kernel density estimation](wikipedia://en/2090057).[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sheather was born and raised in Australia, the son of a bank clerk and a nurse.[^o6] Sheather graduated with 1st Class Honours in mathematical statistics from the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) and earned his Ph.D. in statistics from [La Trobe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trobe_University).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

His academic career includes professorships at the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales) and Texas A&M University as well as visiting professorships at Pennsylvania State University and the Stern School of Business at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o9] While at Texas A&M, he started two professional master’s programs.

In 2021, Sheather was named as the first holder of the Truist Chair in Data Analytics.[^o10]
