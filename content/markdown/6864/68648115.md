# Rafe Mazzeo

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rafe Mazzeo"}

**Rafe Roys Mazzeo** (born 1961) is an American mathematician working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841), and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).[^o4] He is currently a professor of mathematics. He served as the department chair at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 2007 to 2010 and 2019–2022.[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mazzeo obtained his [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) degree from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1982. He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at MIT under the supervision of [Richard Burt Melrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burt_Melrose) in 1986. His Ph.D. thesis was titled "Hodge cohomology of negatively curved manifolds."[^o2] After obtaining his Ph.D. degree, Mazzeo joined Stanford University, where he became a full professor in 1997.[^o2]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Mazzeo has published more than 150 mathematics papers,[^o7][^o8] and his work has been cited more than 5000 times.[^o8] His work has been published in many prestigious mathematics journals, including *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*,[^o9] *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)*,[^o10] and *[Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o11] He has had 11 doctoral students.[^o12] He is one of the founders of the [Stanford University Mathematics Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University_Mathematics_Camp). He is Faculty Director of the Stanford Online High School, and has been Director of the IAS/Park City Mathematics Institute since 2015.

## Awards and fellowships {#Awards_and_fellowships}

Mazzeo has received many awards, including a [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Postdoctoral Fellowship, [Alfred P. Sloan Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Foundation) Research Fellowship, National Science Foundation Young Investigator Fellowship, and Louis and Claude Rosenberg Jr. University Fellowship in Undergraduate Education.[^o2]

In 2013, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14] He was elected as a Member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2022.[^o15]
