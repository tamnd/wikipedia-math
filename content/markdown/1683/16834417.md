# Lentoid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lentoid"}

*Image: Lentoid.jpg.* Lentoid

A **lentoid** is a geometric shape of a [three-dimensional](wikipedia://en/3054853) body, best described as a circle viewed from one direction and a [convex lens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lens_%28optics%29) viewed from every orthogonal direction. It has no strict mathematical definition, but may be described as the volume enclosed within overlapping [paraboloids](wikipedia://en/145845).

:::

The term is most often used in describing [jewelry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery) and [cellular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28biology%29) phenomena in [microbiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microbiology).

## In history {#In_history}

Since ancient times, the lentoid shape has been used to fashion [jewelry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery) and seals for identification made from a variety of [gemstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemstone) and metals. In [Minoan Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan_Crete), for example, [Minoan seals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan_seals) have been found with complex carving on lentoid stones.[^o3] The lentoid shape was one of the most commonly recovered seal shapes from Minoan [Knossos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knossos) on Crete dating to the [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age), as evidenced by the finds at that [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age) palace.[^o4]
