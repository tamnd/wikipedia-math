# Vivek Shende

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vivek Shende"}

**Vivek Vijay Shende** is an American mathematician known for his work on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220). He is a professor of Quantum Mathematics at [Syddansk Universitet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syddansk_Universitet)[^o3] while on leave from [University of California Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_Berkeley).[^o4]

:::

## Doctoral studies and early career {#Doctoral_studies_and_early_career}

Shende defended his Ph.D. dissertation "Hilbert schemes of points on integral plane curves" at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2011 under the supervision of [Rahul Pandharipande](wikipedia://en/8950386).[^o6] From 2011 to 2013, he was a Simons Postdoctoral Fellow at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) mentored by [Paul Seidel](wikipedia://en/27678785). Shende joined Berkeley as an assistant professor in 2013 and became an associate professor in 2019. He supervised at least four doctoral degrees at Berkeley.[^o6]

## Awards and accomplishments {#Awards_and_accomplishments}

In 2021, after moving to Denmark, Shende received sizable grants intended to support the creation of a new research group. The Danish National Research Foundation awarded Shende its DNRF Chair.[^o8] The [Villum Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villum_Foundation) funded Shende's research in mathematical aspects of [String theory](wikipedia://en/28305) through the Villum Investigator program.[^o9] This is one of the largest and most prestigious grants for individual researchers in Denmark.

As a Berkeley professor, Shende received the [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award) in 2017[^o10] and a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in Mathematics in 2015.[^o11]

In 2010, Shende proved, together with Martijn Kool and [Richard Thomas](wikipedia://en/33826774), the [Göttsche](wikipedia://en/22142294) conjecture on the universality of formulas counting nodal curves on surfaces,[^o12] a problem in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) whose history stretches back more than a century.[^o13]

During his undergraduate studies at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), he performed computer science research with [Igor L. Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_L._Markov) and [John P. Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_P._Hayes). Shende shared in 2004 the [IEEE Donald O. Pederson Award in Solid-State Circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Donald_O._Pederson_Award_in_Solid-State_Circuits)[^o14] as the lead author of the work on synthesis of reversible logic circuits.[^o15] This paper proved the existence of reversible circuits that implement certain permutations and developed algorithms for finding such circuits. Shende was also the lead author of the work on synthesis of [quantum circuits](wikipedia://en/707404)[^o16] that developed the quantum Shannon decomposition and algorithms for finding asymptotically optimal quantum circuits that implement a given $n$-[qubit](wikipedia://en/25284) [unitary matrix](wikipedia://en/129222), as well as quantum circuits that construct a given $n$-qubit [quantum state](wikipedia://en/30876419). Shende obtained formulas and algorithms for implementing smallest possible quantum circuits for 2-qubit unitary matrices.[^o17][^o18] For the 3-qubit [Toffoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toffoli) gate, he proved that six [CNOT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNOT) gates are necessary in a circuit that implements it,[^o19] showing that the widely used six-CNOT decomposition is optimal. These publications are highly cited (per [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar)) and their results laid the foundation of compilers for [quantum computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_computers).

## Mathematics education {#Mathematics_education}

Shende taught college-level [Calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), [Discrete Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics) as well as [Linear Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Algebra) and [Differential Equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Equations) courses at Berkeley. In 2021 he cosigned, along with many professional mathematicians, an open letter to Governor [Gavin Newsom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavin_Newsom) and other California officials asking to replace the proposed new California Math curriculum framework.[^o21] The framework was adopted in 2023 despite these objections.[^o22]
