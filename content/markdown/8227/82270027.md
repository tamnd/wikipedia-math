# P1 (space group)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="P1"}

**P1** may refer to either of the following [space groups](wikipedia://en/463721) in three dimensions:

:::

- [P1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_space_groups#List_of_triclinic), space group no. 1
- [P$\overline{1}$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_space_groups#List_of_triclinic), space group no. 2
