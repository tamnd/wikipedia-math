# Chamfered dodecahedron

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Chamfered dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **chamfered dodecahedron** is a [convex polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron) with 80 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 42 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926): 30 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014). It is constructed as a [chamfer](wikipedia://en/43468103) (edge-truncation) of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361). The pentagons are reduced in size and new hexagonal faces are added in place of all the original edges. Its dual is the [pentakis icosidodecahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentakis_icosidodecahedron).

:::

It is also called a **truncated rhombic triacontahedron**, constructed as a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025). It can more accurately be called an **order-5 truncated rhombic triacontahedron** because only the order-5 vertices are truncated.

## Structure {#Structure}

These 12 order-5 vertices can be truncated such that all edges are of equal length. The original 30 [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) faces become non-regular hexagons, and the truncated vertices become regular pentagons.

The hexagon faces can be [equilateral](wikipedia://en/214547) but not [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) with D${}_{2}$ symmetry. The angles at the two vertices with [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) $6.6.6$ are $\arccos\left(\frac{-1}{\sqrt{5}}\right) \approx 116.565^{\circ}$ and at the remaining four vertices with $5.6.6$, they are $\approx121.717^{\circ}$ each.

It is the [Goldberg polyhedron](wikipedia://en/39781140) $G_{V}(2,0)$, containing pentagonal and hexagonal faces.

It also represents the exterior envelope of a cell-centered [orthogonal projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_projection) of the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358), one of six [convex regular 4-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_regular_4-polytope#Visualizations).

## Chemistry {#Chemistry}

The chamfered dodecahedron is the shape of the [fullerene](wikipedia://en/10628) $C_{80}$. Occasionally, this shape is denoted $C_{80}(I_{h})$, describing its icosahedral symmetry and distinguishing it from other less-symmetric 80-vertex fullerenes. It is one of only four fullerenes found by (Deza Deza Grishukhin 1998) to have a [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_%28topology%29) that can be isometrically embeddable into an [L${}_{1}$ space](wikipedia://en/408354).[^o7]
