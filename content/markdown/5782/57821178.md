# Eliza Edwards

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eliza Edwards"}

**Eliza Edwards** (1779–1846) was a [human computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_computer) and daughter of [Mary Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Edwards_%28human_computer%29).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Edwards was born in [Ludlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludlow) to [Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Edwards_%28human_computer%29) and John Edwards.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Eliza was a human computer who took on the job of her mother [Mary Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Edwards_%28human_computer%29), working on the [Nautical Almanac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nautical_almanac).[^o5] She lost her job in 1829, during the formation of the [Nautical Almanac Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HM_Nautical_Almanac_Office).[^o5] She was paid by the [Board of Longitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Longitude).[^o6][^o7]
