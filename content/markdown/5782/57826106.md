# Mario Primicerio

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mario Primicerio"}

**Mario Primicerio** (13 November 1940 – 30 May 2025) was an Italian mathematician and politician who served as Mayor of [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Graduating with a degree in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence), in 1970 Primicerio started teaching at the university after working as a researcher at the ionized gas laboratory in [Frascati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frascati).[^o4] During these years he became a close collaborator of the former Mayor of Florence [Giorgio La Pira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_La_Pira).

In [1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Italian_local_elections), Primicerio ran for the office of Mayor himself on an independent center-left platform, which was supported by the former communist [Democratic Party of the Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_of_the_Left) and various other progressive parties, such as the new-born [Federation of the Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_the_Greens) and the [Communist Refoundation Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Refoundation_Party).[^o5] The main opposition to Primicerio's coalition came from [Giorgio Morales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Morales), the outgoing mayor, former member of the [Italian Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Socialist_Party) and later a supporter of [Silvio Berlusconi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvio_Berlusconi)'s [Forza Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_%281994%29) party.

Primicerio won the election in the first round and became the first elected Mayor, remaining in office until he retired from politics in [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Italian_local_elections).[^o6]

Layer he was appointed a visiting professor at several universities, in places such as [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas), Beijing, [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki), [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis), [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk), [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford), Paris, [Rosario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosario,_Argentina), [São Carlos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Carlos), and [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv).

Primicerio died on 30 May 2025, at the age of 84.[^o7]
