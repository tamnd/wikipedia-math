# Hyune-Ju Kim

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyune-Ju Kim"}

**H. Hyune-Ju Kim** is a Korean-American statistician known for her research on [change point detection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_point_detection), [segmented regression](wikipedia://en/11683021), and applications to the analysis of [mortality](wikipedia://en/3560385) and [incidence](wikipedia://en/167008) of [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer). She is a professor in the department of mathematics at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University).

:::

Kim earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) in 1983, and completed a Ph.D. in statistics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1988. Her dissertation, *Change-Point Problems in Regression*, was supervised by [David Siegmund](wikipedia://en/28862740). She joined Syracuse University as an assistant professor in 1989, and became full professor there in 2009.
