::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of twenty triangular prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 20 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), aligned in pairs with the axes of three-fold rotational symmetry of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084).

:::

It results from composing the two [enantiomorphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomorphs) of the [compound of 10 triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/14826806). In doing so, the vertices of the two enantiomorphs coincide, with the result that the full compound has two triangular prisms incident on each of its vertices.
