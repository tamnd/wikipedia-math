::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of ten triangular prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160) symmetric arrangement of 10 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), aligned with the axes of three-fold rotational symmetry of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084).
