# Frederick Purser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frederick Purser"}

**Frederick Purser** (1839–30 January 1910) was an Irish mathematician, author, and member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy). He was a younger brother to mathematician [John Purser](wikipedia://en/54277928).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Born in Dublin, Frederick attended a boarding school run by his uncle Richard Biggs in [Wiltshire, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiltshire,_England), then attended [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (TCD), where he was Scholar (1859), and got BA (1860) and MA (1864). He was elected a Fellow there in 1879. He was appointed [Professor of Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin_Chair_of_Natural_Philosophy) in 1902 and served in the role until his death. He contributed articles to the 1911 [Encyclopedia Britannica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia_Britannica).[^o5]
