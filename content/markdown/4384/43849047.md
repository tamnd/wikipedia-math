# Askold Khovanskii

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Askold Khovanskii"}

**Askold Georgievich Khovanskii** (Аскольд Георгиевич Хованский; born 3 June 1947) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_people)-[Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) currently at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o3] His areas of research are [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). His research is in the development of the theory of [toric varieties](wikipedia://en/737087) and [Newton polyhedra](wikipedia://en/31041066) in algebraic geometry. He is also the inventor of the theory of [fewnomials](wikipedia://en/67960783), and the [Bernstein–Khovanskii–Kushnirenko theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernstein–Khovanskii–Kushnirenko_theorem) is named after him.

:::

He obtained his Ph.D. in 1973 from the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) in Moscow under the supervision of [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490).[^o4] In his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) thesis, he developed a [topological version](wikipedia://en/51121181) of [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). He studied the theory of [Newton–Okounkov bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton-Okounkov_body).

Among his graduate students are Olga Gel'fond, Feodor Borodich, H. Petrov-Tan'kin, Kiumars Kaveh,[^o5] Farzali Izadi, Ivan Soprunov,[^o6] Jenya Soprunova,[^o7] Vladlen Timorin,[^o8] Valentina Kirichenko,[^o9] Sergey Chulkov, V. Kisunko, Mikhail Mazin,[^o10] O. Ivrii, K. Matveev, Yuri Burda, and J. Yang.

In 2014, he received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) for outstanding contributions to mathematical research in Canada.[^o11] He was elected to the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 2020.[^o12]
