# ChatSecure

::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="ChatSecure"}

**ChatSecure** is a messaging application for [iOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOS) which allows [OTR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-the-Record_Messaging) and [OMEMO](wikipedia://en/48005845) encryption for the [XMPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XMPP) protocol. ChatSecure is [free and open source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open_source_software) available under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License) license.

:::

ChatSecure has been used by international individuals[^o7][^o8] and governments,[^o9] businesses,[^o10] and those spreading [jihadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jihadi) propaganda.[^o11][^o12]

The app has not been maintained since 2021.[^o13]

## History {#History}

ChatSecure was originally released in 2011, and was the first iOS application to support OTR messaging.[^o15] In 2012, ChatSecure formed a partnership with [The Guardian Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian_Project_%28software%29) and the Gibberbot app was rebranded to "ChatSecure Android".[^o16]

In late 2016, the [Android](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_%28operating_system%29) branding partnership was ended,[^o17][^o18] with ChatSecure Android becoming 'Zom',[^o19] and ChatSecure iOS remaining as ChatSecure. ChatSecure iOS remains in active development and is unaffected by this change. Version 4.0 was released on January 17, 2017.[^o20]

ChatSecure is censored from the [App Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/App_Store_%28iOS/iPadOS%29) in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).[^o21]

## Reception {#Reception}

In November 2014, "ChatSecure + [Orbot](wikipedia://en/34650947)" received a perfect score on the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Frontier_Foundation)'s "Secure Messaging Scorecard";[^o23] the combination received points for having communications encrypted in transit, having communications encrypted with keys the provider doesn't have access to ([end-to-end encryption](wikipedia://en/1065362)), making it possible for users to independently verify their correspondents' identities, having past communications secure if the keys are stolen ([forward secrecy](wikipedia://en/2191130)), having the code open to independent review ([open source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software)), having the security designs well-documented, and having a recent independent security audit.[^o23]
