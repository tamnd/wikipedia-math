# Orders of magnitude (entropy)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Orders of magnitude"}

The following list shows different [orders of magnitude](wikipedia://en/22657) of [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891).

:::

::: {#o2 .table cue=table}

| Factor (J⋅K${}^{-1}$) | Value | Item |
|---|---|---|
| × 10<sup>−24</sup> | \9.5699 × 10<sup>−24</sup> J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Entropy equivalent of one [bit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit) of information, equal to *[k](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_constant)* times [ln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_log)(2)[^o3] |
| × 10<sup>−23</sup> | \1.381 × 10<sup>−23</sup> J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | [Boltzmann constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_constant), entropy equivalent of one [nat](wikipedia://en/3070481) of information. |
| × 10<sup>−21</sup> | \2.15879432e-21 J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Perfectly shuffled stanrad [deck of cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck_of_cards), equal to *[k](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_constant)* times ln(52!) |
| × 10<sup>\-16</sup> | \1.1e-16 J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Amount of information stored during [language acquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_acquisition)[^o4] |
| × 10<sup>1</sup> | \5.74 J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Standard entropy of 1 [mole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mole_%28unit%29) of [graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite)[^o5] |
| × 10<sup>33</sup> | ≈ × 10<sup>35</sup> J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Entropy of the [Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun) (given as ≈ × 10<sup>42</sup> erg⋅K${}^{-1}$ in Bekenstein (1973))[^o6] |
| × 10<sup>54</sup> | \1.5 × 10<sup>54</sup> J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | Entropy of a [black hole](wikipedia://en/4650) of one [solar mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_mass) (given as ≈ × 10<sup>60</sup> erg⋅K${}^{-1}$ in Bekenstein (1973))[^o6] |
| × 10<sup>81</sup> | \4.3 × 10<sup>81</sup> J⋅K${}^{-1}$ | One estimate of the theoretical maximum entropy of the [universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe)[^o7][^o8] |

:::
