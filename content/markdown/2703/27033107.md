# 420 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="420"}

**420** (**four hundred \[and\] twenty**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 419 and preceding 421.[^o3]

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

420 is a [zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_function) of the [Mertens function](wikipedia://en/435639), a [sparsely totient](wikipedia://en/2011159) number, a [pronic number](wikipedia://en/652733),[^o3] a balanced number, a [largely composite number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Largely_composite_number), and a [cluster prime](wikipedia://en/69356423).[^o10]

420 is the number of [minimal covers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_cover) on 7 objects with 6 members.[^o11]

### Divisors {#Divisors}

The [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492) of 420 is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 14, 15, 20, 21, 28, 30, 35, 42, 60, 70, 84, 105, 140, 210, and 420.[^o3][^o13] Because the first seven divisors is going from 1 to 7, it is the smallest number that is divisible by 1 through 7.[^o14]

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

[420](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/420_%28cannabis_culture%29) (pronounced four-twenty) is a [cannabis culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_culture) slang for [cannabis consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_consumption).[^o17]
