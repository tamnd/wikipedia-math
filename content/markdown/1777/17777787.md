# E. C. Rhodes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="E. C. Rhodes"}

**Edmund Cecil Rhodes** (1892–1964), a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), was born in [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire) and named after [Cecil Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cecil_Rhodes). He went to [Bradford Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_Grammar_School) and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he graduated as [Wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) (B-star) in 1914. In 1924 he became reader at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics), where he remained until he retired in 1958. He wrote for *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178)* and the *[Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143)*, and [Edgeworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Ysidro_Edgeworth) called him a "pathbreaker" (see obituary by [Grebenik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Grebenik)).

:::

Rhodes originated the [Rhodes algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_algorithm) in [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730). He worked with [Arthur Bowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Bowley), and also with Sir [Alexander Carr-Saunders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Carr-Saunders) on his study of [juvenile delinquency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juvenile_delinquency), and he contributed to [population studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_studies) and the [statistical study of examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_study_of_examinations) (see his book with Sir [Philip John Hartog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_John_Hartog) entitled *[An Examination of Examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Examination_of_Examinations)*.

## Papers {#Papers}

- *Young Offenders, an Enquiry Into Juvenile Delinquency*, [A.M. Carr-Saunders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Carr-Saunders) e [Hermann Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Mannheim), 1942
- *An examination of examinations : being a summary of investigations on the comparison of marks allotted to examinations scripts by independent examiners and boards of examiners, together with a section on a viva voce examination*, with Sir [Philip John Hartog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_John_Hartog), 1936
