# Tadashi Nakayama (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tadashi Nakayama"}

**Tadashi Nakayama** was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made important contributions to [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He received his degrees from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) and the [University of Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Osaka) and held permanent positions at the University of Osaka and [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University). He had visiting positions at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), and the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). [Nakayama's lemma](wikipedia://en/1835001), [Nakayama algebras](wikipedia://en/30388216), [Nakayama's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34633682), and the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](wikipedia://en/43510390) are named after him.
