# Bordiga surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bordiga surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Bordiga surface** is a certain sort of [rational surface](wikipedia://en/2380148) of degree 6 in *P*<sup>4</sup>, introduced by [Giovanni Bordiga](wikipedia://en/40217805).

:::

A Bordiga surface is isomorphic to the projective plane [blown up](wikipedia://en/1817790) in 10 points, the embedding into *P*<sup>4</sup> is given by the 5-dimensional space of quartics passing through the 10 points. [White surfaces](wikipedia://en/23333947) are the generalizations using more points.
