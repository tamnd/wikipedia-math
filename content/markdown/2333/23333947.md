# White surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="White surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **White surface** is one of the [rational surfaces](wikipedia://en/2380148) in *P*<sup>*n*</sup> studied by (White 1923), generalizing [cubic surfaces](wikipedia://en/526126) and [Bordiga surfaces](wikipedia://en/23333699), which are the cases *n* = 3 or 4.

:::

A White surface in *P*<sup>*n*</sup> is given by the embedding of *P*<sup>2</sup> [blown up](wikipedia://en/1817790) in *n*(*n* + 1)/2 points by the linear system of degree *n* curves through these points.
