# Alexander von Brill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander von Brill"}

**Alexander von Brill** (born **Alexander Wilhelm Brill**; 20 September 1842 – 18 June 1935)[^o3] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt), [Hesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesse), Brill was educated at the [University of Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Giessen), where he earned his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) under supervision of [Alfred Clebsch](wikipedia://en/1369957). He held a chair at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen), where [Max Planck](wikipedia://en/19848) was among his students.

In 1874, [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337) and von Brill introduced the study of special divisors known as [Brill–Noether theory](wikipedia://en/599865).[^o5]

In 1933, he joined the [National Socialist Teachers League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialist_Teachers_League) as one of the first members from Tübingen.[^o3]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The [London Science Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Science_Museum) contains [sliceform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sliceform) objects prepared by Brill and [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887).[^o7]
