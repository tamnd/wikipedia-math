# Ackermann–Teubner Memorial Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ackermann–Teubner Memorial Award"}

*Image: Alfred\_Ackermann-Teubner.jpg.* Portrait of Alfred Ackermann-Teubner

The **Alfred Ackermann–Teubner Memorial Award for the Promotion of Mathematical Sciences** recognized work in mathematical analysis. It was established in 1912 by engineer Alfred Ackermann-Teubner and was an [endowment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_endowment) of the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig).[^o2]

:::

It was awarded 14 times between 1914 and 1941.[^o3] Subsequent awards were to be made every other year until a surplus of 60,000 marks was accumulated within the [endowment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_endowment), at which time, the prize was to be awarded annually. The subjects included:[^o4]

- History, philosophy, teaching
- [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923)
- [Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics)
- [Mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)
- Mathematics, especially [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396)
- [Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [theory of errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_errors)
- Mathematics, especially [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446)
- [Applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), especially [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608) and [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics).

## Honorees {#Honorees}

The fifteen honorees between 1914 and 1941 are:[^o6]

- 1914: [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887)[^o3]
- 1916: [Ernst Zermelo](wikipedia://en/178655), prize of 1,000 marks[^o7]
- 1918: [Ludwig Prandtl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Prandtl)[^o8]
- 1920: [Gustav Mie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Mie)[^o9]
- 1922: [Paul Koebe](wikipedia://en/4313643)[^o10]
- 1924: [Arnold Kohlschütter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Kohlschütter)[^o11]
- 1926: [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998)[^o12]
- 1928: [Albert Defant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Defant)[^o13]
- 1930: [Johannes Tropfke](wikipedia://en/59764977)
- 1932: [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) and [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351), co-honorees[^o14]
- 1934: [Erich Trefftz](wikipedia://en/79501318)<sup>(de)</sup>[^o15]
- 1937: [Pascual Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascual_Jordan)[^o16]
- 1938: [Erich Hecke](wikipedia://en/1233990)[^o17]
- 1941: [Paul ten Bruggencate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_ten_Bruggencate)[^o18]

## Jurists {#Jurists}

In 1937, [Constantin Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625) and [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191) were invited to jury the award.[^o20] Along with [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998), Carathéodory was invited again in 1944 by the German Union of Mathematicians.[^o21]
