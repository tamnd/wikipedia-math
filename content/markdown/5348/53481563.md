# Hans Munthe-Kaas

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Munthe-Kaas"}

**Hans Zanna [Munthe-Kaas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaas_%28noble_family%29)** (born 28 March 1961) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working at [UiT The Arctic University of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UiT_The_Arctic_University_of_Norway) and the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen). The main focus of is work lies in the area of [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833) in the borderland between pure and applied mathematics and computer science.

:::

He took his PhD at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) in 1989, called to full Professor 1997 and has since 2005 been Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). Since 2021 he is mainly working at [UiT The Arctic University of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UiT_The_Arctic_University_of_Norway) in Tromsø where he is co-director of the newly established [Lie-Størmer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie-Størmer_Center) for fundamental structures in computational and pure mathematics.

## Research {#Research}

The work of Munthe-Kaas is centred on applications of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) techniques in [geometric integration](wikipedia://en/2208188) and structure preserving algorithms for numerical integration of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). A central aspect is the analysis of structure preservation by algebraic and combinatorial techniques ([B-series](wikipedia://en/23338010#Butcher_series_and_Runge–Kutta_method) and [Lie–Butcher series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie–Butcher_series)).[^o5]

In the mid-1990s Munthe-Kaas developed what are now known as [Runge–Kutta–Munthe-Kaas methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge–Kutta–Munthe-Kaas_methods),[^o6][^o7][^o8] a generalisation of [Runge–Kutta methods](wikipedia://en/213671) to integration of differential equations evolving on Lie groups. The analysis of numerical [Lie group integrators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_group_integrators)[^o9] leads to the study of new types of formal power series for flows on manifolds (Lie–Butcher series). Lie–Butcher theory combines classical [B-series](wikipedia://en/23338010) with Lie-series.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Munthe-Kaas received Exxon Mobil Award for best PhD at [NTNU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_and_Technology), 1989, and the [Carl-Erik Frōberg Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl-Erik_Frōberg_Prize) in Numerical Mathematics 1996 for the paper "Lie–Butcher theory for Runge–Kutta Methods". Munthe-Kaas is elected member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea), the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters), the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) and the [Norwegian Academy of Technological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Technological_Sciences). Munthe-Kaas was the chair of the international [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) committee (2018–2022), he was President of the Scientific Council of [Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées](wikipedia://en/50319899) (CIMPA) (2017–2024) and he was Editor-in-Chief of [Journal Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://link.springer.com/journal/10208) (2017–2022). Munthe-Kaas was secretary of [Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) (2005–2011) and member of the Board of the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) in Mathematics (2010–2018).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Munthe-Kaas married [Antonella Zanna](wikipedia://en/63588650), an Italian [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506), in 1997; they have four children.[^o12]
