# Cristian Dumitru Popescu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cristian Dumitru Popescu"}

**Cristian Dumitru Popescu** (born 1964) is a [Romanian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian-American) mathematician at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). His research interests are in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and in particular, in [special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

The son of historian Dumitru Micu Popescu and biologist Rodica Jerișteanu, Popescu was born in 1964 in [Novaci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novaci,_Romania), [Gorj County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorj_County).[^o4] After completing his undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Mathematics of the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest),[^o4] he obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1996, with thesis "On a refined Stark conjecture for function fields" written under the direction of [Karl Rubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Rubin). He became a professor at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), after which he moved to his current position as a professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

Popescu formulated and proved [function field](wikipedia://en/447645) versions of the [Gras conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gras_conjectures) and Rubin's integral refinement of the abelian [Stark conjectures](wikipedia://en/5685631). He has also made important contributions to the Stark conjectures over number fields, formulating an alternative to Rubin's refinement, known as [Popescu's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popescu's_conjecture). Although slightly weaker than Rubin's conjecture, it has the advantage that it can presently be shown to remain true under raising the base field or lowering the top field of the extension. Popescu and [Cornelius Greither](wikipedia://en/68876514) formulated equivariant versions of [Iwasawa's main conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwasawa's_main_conjecture) over function fields and number fields,[^o7] proving unconditionally the function field version and conditionally the number field version. These conjectures have important implications for the [Brumer–Stark conjecture](wikipedia://en/20510092), the Coates-Sinnott conjecture and [Gross' conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross'_conjecture) on special values of L-functions.

Popescu, in collaboration with his former post-doctoral fellows Joseph Ferrara and Nathan Green and his former PhD student Zachary Higgins, formulated and proved the Equivariant Tamagawa Number Formula for Goss L-functions associated to Drinfeld modules and, more generally, to abelian t-motives. This formula generalizes and refines Lenny Taelman's class-number formula for Drinfeld modules and has important implications in the study of the Galois module structure of Taelman class modules of abelian t-motives.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Popescu was awarded the [Simion Stoilow Prize](wikipedia://en/20268944) by the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy) in 2002.[^o9] In 2015-2016, he was a Simons Fellow at Harvard University.[^o10] In 2025-2026, he was a Simons Fellow at Princeton University[^o11]. He was a member of the School of Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Study,_Princeton) in the Fall Semester of 2024 and the academic year 2025-2026. He was elected to the 2021 class of fellows[^o12] of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to number theory and arithmetic geometry". He is an honorary member of the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230).[^o13]

In 2024, Popescu was awarded the honorary title "Gorjeanul Anului 2023"[^o14] by [Gorj County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorj_County), Romania, for his contributions to mathematics and mathematics education. In 2025, he was named "Honorary Citizen"[^o15] by the mayor's office of his native city of [Novaci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novaci,_Romania).
