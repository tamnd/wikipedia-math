# Doomsday conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Doomsday conjecture"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **doomsday conjecture** was a conjecture about [Ext groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ext_group) over the [Steenrod algebra](wikipedia://en/2146220) made by Joel Cohen, named by Michael Barratt, published by (Milgram 1971, conjecture 73) and disproved by (Mahowald 1977). (Minami 1995) stated a modified version called the **new doomsday conjecture**.

:::

The original doomsday conjecture was that for any prime *p* and positive integer *s* there are only a finite number of permanent cycles in

$$\text{Ext}_{A_*}^{s,*}(Z/pZ,Z/pZ). \,$$

(Mahowald 1977) found an infinite number of permanent cycles for *p* = *s* = 2, disproving the conjecture. Minami's new doomsday conjecture is a weaker form stating (in the case *p* = 2) that there are no nontrivial permanent cycles in the image of (Sq<sup>0</sup>)<sup>*n*</sup> for *n* sufficiently large depending on *s*.
