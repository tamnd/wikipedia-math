# Algebraic link

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebraic link"}

*Image: Algebraic Borromean link diagram.svg.* Decomposition of the [Borromean rings](wikipedia://en/873118) by a Conway sphere (black dotted vertical midline) into two 2-tangles, showing that the Borromean rings form an algebraic link

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), an **algebraic link** is a [link](wikipedia://en/1161784) that can be decomposed by [Conway spheres](wikipedia://en/34978322) into [2-tangles](wikipedia://en/30858815).[^o2] Algebraic links are also called **arborescent links**.[^o3] Although algebraic links and algebraic tangles were originally defined by [John H. Conway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Conway) as having two pairs of open ends, they were subsequently generalized to more pairs.[^o4]

:::
