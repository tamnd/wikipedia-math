# Friedrich Engel (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Engel"}

**Friedrich Engel** (26 December 1861 – 29 September 1941) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Engel was born in [Lugau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugau), [Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Saxony), as the son of a [Lutheran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheran) pastor. He attended the Universities of both [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) and [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), before receiving his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from Leipzig in 1883.

Engel studied under [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) at Leipzig, and collaborated with [Sophus Lie](wikipedia://en/54010) for much of his life. He worked at Leipzig (1885–1904), [Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald) (1904–1913), and [Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Giessen) (1913–1931). He died in Giessen.

Engel was the co-author, with Sophus Lie, of the three volume work *Theorie der Transformationsgruppen* (publ. 1888–1893; tr., "Theory of transformation groups"). Engel was the editor of the collected works[^o3][^o4] of Sophus Lie with six volumes published between 1922 and 1937; the seventh and final volume was prepared for publication but appeared almost twenty years after Engel's death. He was also the editor of the collected works of [Hermann Grassmann](wikipedia://en/99242).[^o5] Engel translated the works of [Nikolai Lobachevski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Lobachevski) from Russian into German, thus making these works more accessible. With [Paul Stäckel](wikipedia://en/9075676) he wrote a history of [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610) (*Theorie der Parallellinien von Euklid bis auf Gauss*, 1895). With his former student Karl Faber, he wrote a book on the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) of the first order using methods of [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) theory.[^o6] In 1910 Engel was the president of the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung).
