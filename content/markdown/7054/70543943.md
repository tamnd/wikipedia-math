# Emily E. Witt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emily E. Witt"}

**Emily Elspeth Witt** is an American mathematician, an associate professor and Keeler Intra-University Professor of mathematics at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas). Her research involves [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Witt is a 2005 graduate of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where she majored in mathematics with a specialization in computer science. She completed her Ph.D. in 2011 at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). Her dissertation, *Local cohomology and group actions*, was supervised by [Melvin Hochster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Hochster).

Witt worked as a Dunham Jackson Assistant Professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) from 2011 to 2014, and as a research assistant professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) from 2014 to 2015. In 20155, she obtained a tenure-track assistant professorship at the University of Kansas. She was promoted to associate professor in 2020, and named Keeler Intra-University Professor for 2021–2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Witt is the 2022–2023 winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).
