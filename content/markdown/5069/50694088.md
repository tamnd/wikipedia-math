# Francesco Maria De Regi

*Image: De\_Regi\_-\_Theoremata,\_1757\_-\_4667146.tif.* *Theoremata*, title page (1757)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Maria De Regi"}

**Francesco Maria De Regi** ([Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan), 1720 – 1794) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was a [Barnabite priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnabites). He worked particularly on [hydraulic engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic_engineering). At twenty-four he took the chair of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specially created for him, in the school of the Collegio of Sant'Alessandro in Milan.[^o2]
