# Furio Honsell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Furio Honsell"}

**Furio Honsell** (born 20 August 1958) is an Italian [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was the mayor of [Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udine) from 2008 to 2018.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Honsell received a mathematics degree at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1980, with a subsequent diploma in mathematics at the [Normale of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in 1983.

### Academic career {#Academic_career}

Honsell has held research and tenured positions at the computer science department of the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and the [University of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Udine). His research concerned, among other subjects, [lambda calculus](wikipedia://en/18203) and language semantics.

In 1990 he was appointed full professor of computer science at the [University of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Udine), where he directed the computing center until 1992, the department of mathematics and computer science from 1992 to 1995, and was Dean of the faculty of mathematical, physical and natural sciences from 1995 to 1998. In 2001, Honsell was elected Rector of the [University of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Udine), an office he held until 2008, when he ran for the position of [Mayor of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_Udine).

### Political career {#Political_career}

#### Mayor of Udine {#Mayor_of_Udine}

In the local elections of 2008, Honsell was elected Mayor of Udine for the [centre-left coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left_coalition_%28Italy%29), endorsed by the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_%28Italy%29), [The Left – The Rainbow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Left_–_The_Rainbow) and the [Italy of Values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_of_Values). He was re-elected in 2013, endorsed by the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_%28Italy%29), [Left Ecology Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_Ecology_Freedom) and the [Federation of the Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_the_Left).

#### Regional councillor {#Regional_councillor}

On 18 January 2018, Honsell resigned as mayor in order to stand as a candidate in the [regional elections in Friuli-Venezia Giulia the following spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Friuli-Venezia_Giulia_regional_election) with *Open - Sinistra FVG*, a list supported by [Article One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_One_%28political_party%29), in support of the [centre-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left_coalition_%28Italy%29) presidential candidate Sergio Bolzonello. Honsell was elected to the regional council and later re-elected in the [following regional election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Friuli-Venezia_Giulia_regional_election).

He was a candidate in the [2019 European elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Parliament_election_in_Italy) with the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_%28Italy%29) list as an independent close to [Article One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_One_%28political_party%29) in the [North-East Italy constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-East_Italy_%28European_Parliament_constituency%29); with 28,535 preferences, he came twelfth and was not elected.[^o8]

In the general elections of 2022, he ran for a seat at the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_Republic_%28Italy%29) in the single-member constituency of [Friuli-Venezia Giulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friuli-Venezia_Giulia) by the [centre-left coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left_coalition_%28Italy%29), obtaining 25.98% and losing the challenge against the [centre-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-right_coalition_%28Italy%29) candidate [Luca Ciriani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Ciriani).[^o9]
