# Ferdinando Pio Rosellini

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinando Pio Rosellini"}

**Ferdinando Pio Rosellini** (1814–1872) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [botanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanist).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

The son of a wealthy family of merchants of [Pescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pescia), his elder brother was the Egyptologist [Ippolito Rosellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ippolito_Rosellini). He studied mathematics at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), and mathematical physics at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) under [Leopoldo Nobili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopoldo_Nobili).[^o4]

From 1836 he was the tutor for the four sons of Giorgio Doria in [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa); one of them became the known botanist [Giacomo Doria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giacomo_Doria), the namesake of the [Natural History Museum of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_History_Museum_of_Giacomo_Doria).

In 1844, botanist [Giuseppe De Notaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_De_Notaris) published a genus of [fungi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungi), in the family [Xylariaceae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xylariaceae), called *[Rosellinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosellinia)* in Ferdinando's honour.[^o5]

In 1846, three years after the death of his brother Ippolito, he married his widow and adopted his three sons: Eugenio, Angela and Giovanbattista.

In 1848, during the Revolutionary period, he was very active in politics; he was a member of the Circolo Patriottico Milanese, and editor of the political journal *La Croce di Savoia*.

In 1853 he was appointed director of the *Istituto di Commercio e Industria* of [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin), founded by [Cavour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camillo_Benso_di_Cavour). From 1859 he was director of *Istituto Tecnico Leardi* in [Casale Monferrato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casale_Monferrato) where he had as a student a young [Vilfredo Pareto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilfredo_Pareto).[^o6] After his death in 1872, his herbarium and manuscripts remained in the 'Istituto'.[^o7]

In 1921, botanist Edvard August Vainio published *[Roselliniella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roselliniella)* a genus of [fungi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungi), (in the [Hypocreales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocreales) order).[^o8] Then in 1986, botanist [Rolf Santesson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Santesson) published *[Rosellinula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosellinula)* a genus of [fungi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungi), (in the class [Dothideomycetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dothideomycetes)).[^o9] Lastly, in 1990, [Mario Matzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Matzer) and [Josef Hafellner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Hafellner) published both *[Roselliniomyces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roselliniomyces)* and *[Roselliniopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roselliniopsis)* (two fungi genera from order [Sordariales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sordariales)), also named in Ferdinando Pio Rosellini's honour.[^o10]
