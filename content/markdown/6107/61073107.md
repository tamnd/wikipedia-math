# Journal of Singularities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Singularities"}

The ***Journal of Singularities*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) which publishes research in the area of [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). It was established in 2010 by [David B. Massey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_B._Massey), who remains [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief). It is published by the [Worldwide Center of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worldwide_Center_of_Mathematics).[^o3]

:::
