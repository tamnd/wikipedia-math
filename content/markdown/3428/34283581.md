# Tau additivity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tau additivity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), **τ-additivity** is a certain property of [measures](wikipedia://en/19873) on [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450).

:::

A measure or [set function](wikipedia://en/25911217) $\mu$ on a space $X$ whose domain is a [sigma-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma-algebra) $\Sigma$ is said to be τ-additive if for any [upward-directed](wikipedia://en/9165) family $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \Sigma$ of nonempty [open sets](wikipedia://en/39358) such that its [union](wikipedia://en/47949) is in $\Sigma,$ the measure of the union is the [supremum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum) of measures of elements of $\mathcal{G};$ that is,:

$$\mu\left(\bigcup \mathcal{G}\right) = \sup_{G\in\mathcal{G}} \mu(G).$$
