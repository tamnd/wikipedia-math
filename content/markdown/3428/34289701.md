# Alexandru Ioan Lupaș

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandru Ioan Lupaș"}

**Alexandru Ioan Lupaș** (5 January 1942 – 14 August 2007) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was born in [Arad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arad,_Romania), where he attended the [Moise Nicoară High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moise_Nicoară_National_College).[^o3] He pursued his studies at [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University) in [Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca), obtaining a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) degree in Mathematics in 1964. He earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree in 1972 from the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart), under the direction of Werner Meyer-König and [Friedrich Moritz Lösch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Moritz_Lösch).

Lupaș then returned to work at Babeș-Bolyai University, obtaining a second Ph.D. degree in 1976 under the supervision of [Tiberiu Popoviciu](wikipedia://en/39854514) and [Dimitrie D. Stancu](wikipedia://en/82141362). That year he moved to [Lucian Blaga University of Sibiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucian_Blaga_University_of_Sibiu), starting as lecturer and advancing to full professor in 1990. He died in [Sibiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibiu).
