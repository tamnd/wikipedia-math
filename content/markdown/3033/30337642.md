# Maurice Auslander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maurice Auslander"}

**Maurice Auslander** (August 3, 1926 – November 18, 1994) was an American mathematician who worked on [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [Artin algebras](wikipedia://en/29353834) (e.g. finite-dimensional associative algebras over a field). He proved the [Auslander–Buchsbaum theorem](wikipedia://en/30774055) that [regular local rings](wikipedia://en/506959) are [factorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorial_ring), the [Auslander–Buchsbaum formula](wikipedia://en/30774458), and, in collaboration with [Idun Reiten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idun_Reiten), introduced [Auslander–Reiten theory](wikipedia://en/30350056) and [Auslander algebras](wikipedia://en/30350487).

:::

Born in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), Auslander received his bachelor's degree and his Ph.D. (1954) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1956-57.[^o2] He was a professor at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) from 1957 until his death in [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim), [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway), aged 68.[^o3] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1971.[^o4]

Auslander was married to Bernice Auslander,[^o3] a professor emerita of mathematics at [University of Massachusetts at Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_at_Boston).[^o5][^o6] He had two children.[^o3] His son Philip Auslander became a professor in the School of Literature, Media, and Communication at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech),[^o7] and his daughter [Leora Auslander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leora_Auslander) became a professor of history at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o8] Maurice Auslander's brother [Louis Auslander](wikipedia://en/22378651) was also a mathematician.

- with [Oscar Goldman](wikipedia://en/37060377): *The Brauer group of a commutative ring*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., vol. 97, no. 3, 1960, pp. 367–409
- [Modules over unramified regular local rings](http://projecteuclid.org/euclid.ijm/1255631585), Illinois J. Math., vol. 5, 1961, pp. 631–647

- with Idun Reiten: *On a generalized version of the Nakayama conjecture*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc., vol. 52, 1975, pp. 69–74
