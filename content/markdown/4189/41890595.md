# Suslin homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suslin homology"}

In mathematics, the **Suslin homology** is a [homology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) attached to [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808). It was proposed by [Andrei Suslin](wikipedia://en/7111820) in 1987, and developed by (txt). It is sometimes called singular homology as it is analogous to the [singular homology](wikipedia://en/431041) of topological spaces.

:::

By definition, given an abelian group *A* and a [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) *X* of finite type over a field *k*, the theory is given by the [Tor group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tor_group)

$$H_i(X, A) = \operatorname{Tor}_i^\mathbb{Z}(C, A)$$

where *C* is a free graded abelian group whose degree *n* part is generated by integral subschemes of $\triangle^n \times X$, where $\triangle^n$ is an *n*-simplex, that are finite and surjective over $\triangle^n$.
