# Alessandro Faedo

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Alessandro Faedo"}

**Alessandro Faedo** (18 November 1913 – 16 June 2001)[^o2] (also known as **Alessandro Carlo Faedo** or **Sandro Faedo**) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician), born in [Chiampo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiampo). He is known for his work in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), leading to the [Faedo–Galerkin method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faedo–Galerkin_method): he was one of the pupils of [Leonida Tonelli](wikipedia://en/10018991) and, after his death, he succeeded him on the chair of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), becoming dean of the faculty of sciences and then rector and exerting a strong positive influence on the development of the university.

:::
