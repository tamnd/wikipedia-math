# Internal set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Internal set"}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), in particular in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) and [nonstandard analysis](wikipedia://en/21708), an **internal set** is a set that is a member of a model.

:::

The concept of internal sets is a tool in formulating the [transfer principle](wikipedia://en/637138), which concerns the logical relation between the properties of the [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) numbers **R**, and the properties of a larger [field](wikipedia://en/10603) denoted \***R** called the [hyperreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51429). The field \***R** includes, in particular, [infinitesimal](wikipedia://en/160990) ("infinitely small") numbers, providing a rigorous mathematical justification for their use. Roughly speaking, the idea is to express [analysis over **R**](wikipedia://en/26478) in a suitable language of mathematical logic, and then point out that this language applies equally well to \***R**. This turns out to be possible because at the [set-theoretic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set-theoretic) level, the propositions in such a language are interpreted to apply only to **internal sets** rather than to all sets (note that the term "language" is used in a loose sense in the above).

[Edward Nelson](wikipedia://en/387561)'s [internal set theory](wikipedia://en/865686) is an axiomatic approach to nonstandard analysis (see also Palmgren at [constructive nonstandard analysis](wikipedia://en/25454823)). Conventional infinitary accounts of nonstandard analysis also use the concept of internal sets.

## Internal sets in the ultrapower construction {#Internal_sets_in_the_ultrapower_construction}

Relative to the [ultrapower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrapower) construction of the [hyperreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51429) as equivalence classes of sequences $\langle u_n\rangle$ of reals, an internal subset \[*A<sub>n</sub>*\] of \***R** is one defined by a sequence of real sets $\langle A_n \rangle$, where a hyperreal $[u_n]$ is said to belong to the set $[A_n]\subseteq \; ^*\!{\mathbb R}$ if and only if the set of indices *n* such that $u_n \in A_n$, is a member of the [ultrafilter](wikipedia://en/31911) used in the construction of \***R**.

More generally, an internal entity is a member of the natural extension of a real entity. Thus, every element of \***R** is internal; a subset of \***R** is internal if and only if it is a member of the natural extension ${ } ^* \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R})$ of the power set $\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R})$ of **R**; etc.

## Internal subsets of the reals {#Internal_subsets_of_the_reals}

Every internal subset of \***R** that is a subset of (the embedded copy of) **R** is necessarily *finite* (see Theorem 3.9.1 Goldblatt, 1998). In other words, every internal infinite subset of the hyperreals necessarily contains nonstandard elements.
