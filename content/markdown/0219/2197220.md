# SYSTAT (statistics package)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="SYSTAT"}

**SYSTAT** is a graphical [statistical software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_software) package.[^o3][^o4] It was one of the earliest statistical software ever to be ported for [personal computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer). It was originally developed by [Leland Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leland_Wilkinson) in the late 1970s, who was at the time an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) of psychology at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago).[^o5] Wilkinson based SYSTAT loosely off of a statistical software application developed by the [SAS Institute](wikipedia://en/194872) for [mainframe computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainframe_computer).[^o6] He developed the base algorithms starting in the late 1970s[^o6] and delivered the first version of the software on [CP/M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CP/M) for the [Cromemco Z-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cromemco_Z-2) in 1980.[^o7][^o8] In order to market SYSTAT, Wilkinson and his wife Ruth Van Demark incorporated Systat Software Inc. in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), Illinois, in 1983. The company eventually grew to over 50 employees.[^o9] After the decline of CP/M, the company ported SYSTAT to [PC DOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PC_DOS) in 1984.[^o8][^o10] It was ported to the [Macintosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_%28computer%29) in 1986[^o11] and Windows in 1992.[^o12]

:::

In 1995, SYSTAT was sold to [SPSS Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPSS_Inc.), who marketed the product to a scientific audience under the SPSS Science division. By 2002, SPSS had changed its focus to [business analytics](wikipedia://en/487132) and decided to sell SYSTAT to Cranes Software in [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore), India. Cranes formed Systat Software, Inc. to market and distribute SYSTAT in the US, and a number of other divisions for global distribution. The headquarters are in San Jose.[^o7][^o5]

By 2005, SYSTAT was in its eleventh version having a revamped codebase completely changed from [Fortran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran) into [C++](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C++). Version 13 came out in 2009, with improvements in the user interface and several new features.[^o7] On January 15, 2024, SYSTAT was transferred to Grafiti LLC.[^o13]
