# Peter Comrie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Comrie"}

**Peter Comrie** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) LLD EIS (1868 – 1944) was a Scottish mathematician and educator. He served as Rector of [Leith Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leith_Academy) 1922 to 1933 and President of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society 1916–17.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 17 July 1868 in [Muthill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muthill) the son of Peter Comrie, master blacksmith and Elizabeth Ritchie.[^o3] He was educated at [Muthill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muthill) School and then [Morrison's Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morrison's_Academy) in [Crieff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crieff). He then won a place at [St Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews_University) studying Mathematics, graduating BSc, MA.[^o4] From 1895 he began working as a mathematics teacher, firstly in [Greenock Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenock_Academy), then [Hutcheson's Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutcheson's_Grammar_School), and [Robert Gordon's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gordon's_College). In 1904 he moved to [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh) and remained there for the rest of his life, but teaching in various schools. Firstly in [Boroughmuir High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boroughmuir_High_School) then in 1917 received his first role as headmaster: at Castlehill School at the head of the [Royal Mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Mile). In 1922 he moved to be headmaster of [Leith Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leith_Academy) where he remained until retiral in 1933.[^o5]

He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1909 his main proposer being [Sir James Donaldson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Donaldson_%28classical_scholar%29). [St Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews_University) awarded him an honorary doctorate (LLD) in 1928.

He died at home 19 Craighouse Terrace in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh) on 20 December 1944.[^o5]

## Memberships {#Memberships}

- President of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society (1916 and 1917)
- President of the Educational Institute of Scotland (1928)
- Co-opted member of the Education Committee for Edinburgh Corporation (1933–44)
- Governor of [George Heriot's Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Heriot's_School)
- Member of the Edinburgh Provincial Committee
- Member of the Central Executive Committee for Training of Teachers (in Scotland)

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Chambers New Commercial Arithmetic* (1911)

## Family {#Family}

Comrie was married to Charlotte Aikman from St Andrews.[^o9]
