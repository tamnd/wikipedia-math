# Sofiya Ostrovska

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sofiya Ostrovska"}

**Sofiya Ostrovska** (born 1958) is a Ukrainian mathematician interested in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), and known for her research on q-Bernstein polynomials, the [q-analogs](wikipedia://en/1619396) of the [Bernstein polynomials](wikipedia://en/340136). She has also published works in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) concerning [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering). She is a professor of mathematics at [Atılım University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atılım_University) in Turkey.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ostrovska was born on 26 September 1958 in [Sloviansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloviansk), then part of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). Her parents, Larisa Semenovna Kudina and [Iossif Ostrovskii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iossif_Ostrovskii), were both mathematicians, and her younger brother Mikhail Ostrovskii, became a mathematics professor at [St. John's University (New York City)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_University_%28New_York_City%29).

She studied mathematics at Kharkov State University (renamed as the [National University of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Kharkiv) in 1999), earning a bachelor's degree in 1977 and master's degree in 1980. She completed her Ph.D. in 1989 at Kyiv State University, again later renamed as the [Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv).

## Career {#Career}

From 1984 to 1993 she was an assistant professor at the [Kharkiv Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_Polytechnic_Institute), and from 1993 to 1995 she was an associate professor at Kharkov State University. In 1995 she moved to the [H.S. Skovoroda Kharkiv National Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.S._Skovoroda_Kharkiv_National_Pedagogical_University) and in 1996 she relocated to Turkey, initially as an associate professor at [Dokuz Eylül University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dokuz_Eylül_University) in [İzmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/İzmir). She became a full professor at the [İzmir Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/İzmir_Institute_of_Technology) in 2000, and took her present position at Atılım University in 2001.
