# Good prime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Good prime"}

A **good prime** is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) whose [square](wikipedia://en/659942) is greater than the product of any two primes at the same number of positions before and after it in the [sequence](wikipedia://en/51421) of primes.

:::

That is, good prime satisfies the [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489)

$$p_n^2 > p_{n-i} \cdot p_{n+i}$$

for all 1 ≤ *i* ≤ *n*−1, where *p<sub>k</sub>* is the *k*th prime.

Example: the first primes are 2, 3, 5, 7 and 11. Since for 5 both the conditions

$$5^2 >3 \cdot 7$$

$$5^2 >2 \cdot 11$$

are fulfilled, 5 is a good prime.
