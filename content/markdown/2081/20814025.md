# Fuzzy Sets and Systems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fuzzy Sets and Systems"}

***Fuzzy Sets and Systems*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) international [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) on behalf of the [International Fuzzy Systems Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Fuzzy_Systems_Association) (IFSA) and was founded in 1978. The editors-in-chief (as of 2010) are Bernard De Baets of the Department of Data Analysis and Mathematical Modelling (at [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) in Belgium), [Didier Dubois](wikipedia://en/5997638) (of IRIT, [Université Paul Sabatier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paul_Sabatier) in Toulouse, France) and Eyke Hüllermeier (of the Department of Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Science, [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) (LMU), Germany). The journal publishes 24 issues a year.[^o3] *Fuzzy Sets and Systems* is abstracted and indexed by [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) and the [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index). According to the [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports) released in 2010, its 2-year impact factor calculated for 2020 is 3.343 and its 5-year impact factor for 2020 is 3.213.

:::
