# Peter Bürgisser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Bürgisser"}

**Peter Bürgisser** (born 1962) is a Swiss mathematician and theoretical computer scientist who deals with algorithmic algebra and algebraic complexity theory.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bürgisser received in 1990 his doctorate from the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) with thesis *Degenerationsordnung und Trägerfunktional bilinearer Abbildungen*[^o4] under the supervision of [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960). Bürgisser was a postdoc at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) from 1991 to 1993 and then at the [University of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich).[^o6] He was a professor at the [University of Paderborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paderborn) and since 2013 a professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) (TU Berlin).[^o7]

His research deals with efficient algorithms for the solution of algebraic problems and lower bounds in the [complexity of algebraic problems](wikipedia://en/7543), as well as with symbolic and numerical algorithms and the probabilistic analysis of numerical algorithms.

With [Felipe Cucker](wikipedia://en/65477623) in 2011 he contributed to the solution of [Smale's Problem](wikipedia://en/8294746) No. 17.

Bürgisser was a visiting scholar at the [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Institute_for_the_Theory_of_Computing) in Berkeley.[^o8] He was also a visiting scholar at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).

In 2010, he was an invited speaker with talk *Smoothed Analysis of Condition Numbers*[^o9] at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad). He was a plenary speaker at the 2008 conference of the organization [Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) (FoCM) in Hong Kong[^o10] and organized workshops on complexity theory at the 2005, 2008 and 2011 workshops and in the 2009 and 2012 [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) workshops. He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.

He is a member of the editorial staff of *[Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics)*.

In 2018 he was awarded an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Advanced Grant.[^o11]

## Further Activities {#Further_Activities}

In Bürgisser\`s youth, he acted in [four short-films](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1154803/?ref_=nv_sr_srsg_0) by his school-mate [Roger Steinmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Steinmann) as lead-actor. 'Die Flutkatastrophe' and 'Die Türe' were aired in the Swiss national TV DRS, the latter accompanied with an interview by Bürgisser.[^o13]
