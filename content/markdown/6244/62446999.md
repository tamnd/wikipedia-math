# Christian Lubich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Lubich"}

*Image: Lubich christian.jpg.* Lubich at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2016

**Christian Lubich** (born 29 July 1959) is an Austrian mathematician, specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After secondary education at the Bundesrealgymnasium in Innsbruck, Lubich studied mathematics at the [University of Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Innsbruck) from 1977 to graduation with [Magister degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magister_degree) in 1981. He was from 1979 to 1981 a student assistant in Innsbruck and from 1981 to 1983 a research associate in the [*Sonderforschungsbereich*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_Research_Centres) 123 *Stochastische mathematische Modelle* at the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg).[^o4] He received his doctorate in 1983 from the University of Innsbruck with dissertation *Zur Stabilität linearer Mehrschrittverfahren für Volterra-Gleichungen* (On stability of linear multistep methods for Volterra equations) under [Ernst Hairer](wikipedia://en/31446016) and [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 1987. Lubich was from 1983 to 1987 a university assistant in Innsbruck, in 1986/87 an assistant at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), in 1987/88 a visiting professor at the IRMAR at the University of Rennes, and in 1988 a visiting professor at the University of Geneva. He was from 1991 to 1992 an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and from 1992 to 1994 a professor of applied mathematics at [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg). He is since 1994 a professor of numerical mathematics at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen).[^o4]

Lubich received in 2001 the Dahlquist Prize of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) and in 1985 the Research Prize of the city of Innsbruck.[^o4] In 2018 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Rio de Janeiro, he was a plenary speaker with talk *Dynamics, numerical analysis, and some geometry*, written jointly with his former doctoral student Ludwig Gauckler and with Ernst Hairer.[^o5]

Lubich has been a member of the editorial board of *[Numerische Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerische_Mathematik)* since 1995, of *[Ricerche di Matematica](wikipedia://en/62454077)* and of the *[IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/64635313)* since 2006, of *[BIT Numerical Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BIT_Numerical_Mathematics)* since 1996, and of *[SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Scientific_Computing)* from 1996 to 2001.[^o4]
