# Kenneth P. Bogart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth P. Bogart"}

**Kenneth Paul Bogart** (August 6, 1943 – March 30, 2005)[^o4] was an American mathematician known for his work on [preference structures](wikipedia://en/28737247)[^o3] and for his textbook on [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o8] He was a professor at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bogart was originally from [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati), and was a 1965 graduate of [Marietta College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marietta_College).[^o4] He earned his Ph.D. in 1968 at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). His dissertation, *Structure Theorems for Local Noether Lattices*, was supervised by [Robert P. Dilworth](wikipedia://en/9581646).

He joined the faculty of the Dartmouth College mathematics department in 1968, was promoted to full professor in 1980, and was chair of the department from 1989 to 1995.[^o4] While at Dartmouth, he supervised 17 doctoral students,[^o6] and was also active in the mentorship of freshman women in research projects in mathematics, including [Tara S. Holm](wikipedia://en/45294890), later to become a professional mathematician.[^o4]

## Textbook {#Textbook}

Bogart was the author of the textbook *Introductory Combinatorics* (Pitman, 1983; 3rd ed., Academic Press, 2000).[^o8]
