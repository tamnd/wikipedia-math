# John Leech (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Leech"}

**John Leech** (21 July 1926 in [Weybridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weybridge), [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey) – 28 September 1992 in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland)) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834). He is best known for his discovery of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) in 1965. He also discovered [Ta(3)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxicab_numbers) in 1957. Leech was married to [Jenifer Haselgrove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenifer_Haselgrove), a British radio scientist.
