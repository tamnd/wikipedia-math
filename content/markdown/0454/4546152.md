# SOSEMANUK

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SOSEMANUK"}

**Sosemanuk** is a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) developed by Come Berbain, Olivier Billet, [Anne Canteaut](wikipedia://en/63868117), [Nicolas Courtois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Courtois), Henri Gilbert, Louis Goubin, [Aline Gouget](wikipedia://en/63642582), Louis Granboulan, Cédric Lauradoux, Marine Minier, Thomas Pornin and Hervé Sibert. Along with HC-128, [Rabbit](wikipedia://en/4565952), and [Salsa20](wikipedia://en/2701329)/12, Sosemanuk is one of the final four Profile 1 (software) ciphers selected for the [eSTREAM](wikipedia://en/2287686) Portfolio.

:::

According to the authors, the structure of the cipher is influenced by the stream cipher [SNOW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNOW) and the [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) [Serpent](wikipedia://en/385154). The cipher has an improved performance compared with Snow, more specifically by having a faster initialization phase. The cipher [key length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_length) can vary between 128 and 256 bits, but the guaranteed security is only 128 bits. The cipher uses an initialization vector of 128 bits. Of note, during the [eSTREAM Phase 1 evaluation](http://www.ecrypt.eu.org/stream/sosemanuk.html) (2008-09-30). <https://web.archive.org/web/20080930121913/http://www.ecrypt.eu.org/stream/sosemanuk.html> process, it was shown that a theoretical attack with cost 2<sup>224</sup> could be applied, which does not contradict the [security claim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_claim) of "128 bits".

Sosemanuk is not patented and is "free for any use". The name means "snow snake" in the [Cree Indian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cree_%28language%29) because it depends both on Snow and Serpent.
