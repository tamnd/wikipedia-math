# Alexandrov space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandrov space"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Alexandrov spaces with curvature ≥ *k*** form a generalization of [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652) with [sectional curvature](wikipedia://en/651361) ≥ *k*, where *k* is some real number. By definition, these spaces are [locally compact](wikipedia://en/48632) [complete](wikipedia://en/7832) [length spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_space) where the lower curvature bound is defined via comparison of [geodesic triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_triangle) in the space to geodesic triangles in standard constant-curvature Riemannian surfaces.[^o2][^o3]

:::

One can show that the [Hausdorff dimension](wikipedia://en/14294) of an Alexandrov space with curvature ≥ *k* is either a non-negative integer or infinite.[^o2] One can define a notion of "angle" (see [Comparison triangle#Alexandrov angles](wikipedia://en/5845706#Alexandrov_angles)) and "tangent cone" in these spaces.

Alexandrov spaces with curvature ≥ *k* are important as they form the limits (in the [Gromov–Hausdorff metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov–Hausdorff_metric)) of sequences of Riemannian manifolds with sectional curvature ≥ *k*,[^o4] as described by [Gromov's compactness theorem](wikipedia://en/6657292).

Alexandrov spaces with curvature ≥ *k* were introduced by the Russian mathematician [Aleksandr Danilovich Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov) in 1948[^o4] and should not be confused with [Alexandrov-discrete spaces](wikipedia://en/542198) named after the Russian topologist [Pavel Alexandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._S._Alexandrov). They were studied in detail by [Burago](wikipedia://en/8688682), [Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov) and [Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266) in 1992[^o5] and were later used in Perelman's proof of the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798).
