# Bion of Abdera

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bion of Abdera"}

**Bion of Abdera** (c. 430 – 370 BC)[^o2] (Βίων ὁ Ἀβδηρίτης, *gen.* Βίωνος) was a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greece) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) of [Abdera, Thrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdera,_Thrace), and a pupil of [Democritus](wikipedia://en/8211). [Strabo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strabo) refers to him as an astrologer. [^o3] He wrote both in the [Ionic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionic_Greek) and [Attic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attic_Greek) dialects, and was the first who said that there were some parts of the Earth in which it was [night for six months](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_night), while the [remaining six months](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_sun) were one uninterrupted day.[^o4][^o5] He also engaged in correlating the direction of [winds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winds) with [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) and is believed to have traveled to distant regions. [^o6]
