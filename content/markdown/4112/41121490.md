# Bunch–Nielsen–Sorensen formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bunch–Nielsen–Sorensen formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in particular [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), the **Bunch–Nielsen–Sorensen formula**,[^o2] named after James R. Bunch, Christopher P. Nielsen and Danny C. Sorensen, expresses the eigenvectors of the sum of a [symmetric matrix](wikipedia://en/126474) $A$ and the [outer product](wikipedia://en/103109), $v v^T$, of [vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_%28mathematics%29) $v$ with itself.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $\lambda_i$ denote the eigenvalues of $A$ and $\tilde\lambda_i$ denote the eigenvalues of the updated matrix $\tilde A = A + v v^T$. In the special case when $A$ is diagonal, the eigenvectors $\tilde q_i$ of $\tilde A$ can be written

$$(\tilde q_i)_k = \frac{N_i v_k}{\lambda_k - \tilde \lambda_i}$$

where $N_i$ is a number that makes the vector $\tilde q_i$ normalized.

:::

## Derivation {#Derivation}

This formula can be derived from the [Sherman–Morrison formula](wikipedia://en/2556798) by examining the poles of $(A-\tilde\lambda I+vv^T)^{-1}$.

## Remarks {#Remarks}

The eigenvalues of $\tilde A$ were studied by Golub.[^o7]

Numerical stability of the computation is studied by Gu and Eisenstat.[^o8]
