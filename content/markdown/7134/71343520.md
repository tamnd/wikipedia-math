# Kaniadakis Erlang distribution

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kaniadakis Erlang distribution"}

The **Kaniadakis Erlang distribution** (or **κ-Erlang Gamma distribution**) is a family of [continuous statistical distributions](wikipedia://en/23543), which is a particular case of the [κ-Gamma distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaniadakis_Gamma_distribution), when $\alpha = 1$ and $\nu = n =$ positive integer.[^o6] The first member of this family is the [κ-exponential distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaniadakis_Exponential_distribution) of Type I. The κ-Erlang is a κ-deformed version of the [Erlang distribution](wikipedia://en/199028). It is one example of a [Kaniadakis distribution](wikipedia://en/71322459).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Characterization"}

## Characterization {#Characterization}

### Probability density function {#Probability_density_function}

The Kaniadakis *κ*-Erlang distribution has the following [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487):[^o6]

$$f_{_{\kappa}}(x) = \frac{1}{ (n - 1)! } \prod_{m = 0}^n \left[ 1 + (2m -n)\kappa \right] x^{n - 1} \exp_\kappa(-x)$$

valid for $x \geq 0$ and $n = \textrm{positive} \,\,\textrm{integer}$, where $0 \leq |\kappa| < 1$ is the entropic index associated with the [Kaniadakis entropy](wikipedia://en/70596336).

The ordinary [Erlang Distribution](wikipedia://en/199028) is recovered as $\kappa \rightarrow 0$.

### Cumulative distribution function {#Cumulative_distribution_function}

The [cumulative distribution](wikipedia://en/5793) function of *κ*-Erlang distribution assumes the form:[^o6]

$$F_\kappa(x) = \frac{1}{ (n - 1)! } \prod_{m = 0}^n \left[ 1 + (2m -n)\kappa \right] \int_0^x z^{n - 1} \exp_\kappa(-z) dz$$

valid for $x \geq 0$, where $0 \leq |\kappa| < 1$. The cumulative [Erlang distribution](wikipedia://en/199028) is recovered in the classical limit $\kappa \rightarrow 0$.

### Survival distribution and hazard functions {#Survival_distribution_and_hazard_functions}

The survival function of the *κ*-Erlang distribution is given by:

$$S_\kappa(x) = 1 - \frac{1}{ (n - 1)! } \prod_{m = 0}^n \left[ 1 + (2m -n)\kappa \right] \int_0^x z^{n - 1} \exp_\kappa(-z) dz$$

The survival function of the *κ*-Erlang distribution enables the determination of hazard functions in closed form through the solution of the *κ*-rate equation:

$$\frac{ S_\kappa(x) }{ dx } = -h_\kappa S_\kappa(x)$$

where $h_\kappa$ is the hazard function.

:::

## Family distribution {#Family_distribution}

A family of *κ*-distributions arises from the *κ*-Erlang distribution, each associated with a specific value of $n$, valid for $x \ge 0$ and $0 \leq |\kappa| < 1$. Such members are determined from the *κ*-Erlang cumulative distribution, which can be rewritten as:

$$F_\kappa(x) = 1 - \left[ R_\kappa(x) + Q_\kappa(x) \sqrt{1 + \kappa^2 x^2} \right] \exp_\kappa(-x)$$

where

$$Q_\kappa(x) = N_\kappa \sum_{m=0}^{n-3} \left( m + 1 \right) c_{m+1} x^m + \frac{N_\kappa}{1-n^2\kappa^2} x^{n-1}$$

$$R_\kappa(x) = N_\kappa \sum_{m=0}^{n} c_{m} x^m$$

with

$$N_\kappa = \frac{1}{ (n - 1)! } \prod_{m = 0}^n \left[ 1 + (2m -n)\kappa \right]$$

$$c_n = \frac{ n\kappa^2 }{ 1 - n^2 \kappa^2}$$

$$c_{n - 1} =0$$

$$c_{n - 2} = \frac{ n - 1 }{ (1 - n^2 \kappa^2) [1 - (n-2)^2\kappa^2]}$$

$$c_m = \frac{ (m + 1)(m+2) }{ 1 - m^2 \kappa^2} c_{m+2} \quad \textrm{for} \quad 0 \leq m \leq n-3$$

### First member {#First_member}

The first member ($n = 1$) of the *κ*-Erlang family is the [*κ*-Exponential distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaniadakis_Exponential_distribution) of type I, in which the [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) and the [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) are defined as:

$$f_{_{\kappa}}(x) = (1 - \kappa^2) \exp_\kappa(-x)$$

$$F_\kappa(x) = 1-\Big(\sqrt{1+\kappa^2 x^2} + \kappa^2 x \Big)\exp_k({-x)}$$

### Second member {#Second_member}

The second member ($n = 2$) of the *κ*-Erlang family has the [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) and the [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) defined as:

$$f_{_{\kappa}}(x) = (1 - 4\kappa^2)\,x \,\exp_\kappa(-x)$$

$$F_\kappa(x) = 1-\left(2\kappa^2 x^2 + 1 + x\sqrt{1+\kappa^2 x^2} \right) \exp_k({-x)}$$

### Third member {#Third_member}

The second member ($n = 3$) has the [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) and the [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) defined as:

$$f_{_{\kappa}}(x) = \frac{1}{2} (1 - \kappa^2) (1 - 9\kappa^2)\,x^2 \,\exp_\kappa(-x)$$

$$F_\kappa(x) = 1-\left\{ \frac{3}{2} \kappa^2(1 - \kappa^2)x^3 + x + \left[ 1 + \frac{1}{2}(1-\kappa^2)x^2 \right] \sqrt{1+\kappa^2 x^2}\right\} \exp_\kappa(-x)$$

## Related distributions {#Related_distributions}

- The [*κ*-Exponential distribution of type I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaniadakis_Exponential_distribution) is a particular case of the *κ*-Erlang distribution when $n = 1$;
- A *κ*-Erlang distribution corresponds to am ordinary exponential distribution when $\kappa = 0$ and $n = 1$;
