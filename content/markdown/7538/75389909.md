# Spherical braid group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spherical braid group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **spherical braid group** or **Hurwitz braid group** is a [braid group](wikipedia://en/550138) on $n$ strands. In comparison with the usual braid group, it has an additional group relation that comes from the strands being on the [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859). The group also has relations to the [inverse Galois problem](wikipedia://en/739166).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The spherical braid group on $n$ strands, denoted $SB_n$ or $B_n(S^2)$, is defined as the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) of the [configuration space](wikipedia://en/54981574) of the sphere:[^o5][^o6]

$$B_n(S^2) = \pi_1(\mathrm{Conf}_n(S^2)).$$

The spherical braid group has a [presentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_presentation) in terms of generators $\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \cdots, \sigma_{n - 1}$ with the following relations:[^o7]

- $\sigma_i \sigma_j = \sigma_j \sigma_i$ for $|i-j| \geq 2$
- $\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n - 2$ (the [Yang–Baxter equation](wikipedia://en/3720810))
- $$\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \cdots \sigma_{n-1} \sigma_{n-1} \sigma_{n-2} \cdots \sigma_{1} = 1$$

The last relation distinguishes the group from the usual braid group.
