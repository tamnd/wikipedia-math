# Björn Sandstede

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Björn Sandstede"}

**Björn Sandstede** is a German applied mathematician. He is currently the Alumni-Alumnae University Professor of Applied Mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where he serves as chair of the department.[^o3]

:::

Sandstede earned his [Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.) in 1993 from the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart), under the supervision of [Bernold Fiedler](wikipedia://en/56983960).

In 2001 he was awarded the [J.D. Crawford Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Crawford_Prize) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) for outstanding research in [nonlinear science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_science).[^o5] In 2014 Sandstede was awarded the Jack K. Hale Award for his contributions to partial differential equations and the study of spiral waves in reaction diffusion systems.[^o6] He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.[^o7]
