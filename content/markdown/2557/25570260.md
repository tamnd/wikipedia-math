# Karl Svozil

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Svozil"}

**Karl Svozil** (born 18 December 1956) is an Austrian [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) educated at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). Visiting scholar at the [Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_Laboratory) of the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), US (1982–1983), the [Lebedev Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebedev_Institute) of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), and the [Ioffe Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioffe_Institute), St. Petersburg (1986). Docent in Theoretical Physics at the [Vienna Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Technical_University). Ao. Univ. Professor at the Institute for Theoretical Physics of the Vienna Technical University. External Researcher at the Centre for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science[^o3] of the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).

:::

Research in [quantum theory](wikipedia://en/25202), applications of [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414), [algorithmic information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_information_theory), [constructive mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_mathematics) (in [Errett Bishop](wikipedia://en/321995)'s sense) in theoretical physics, equilibrium dynamics.[^o4]
