# Classifying topos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Classifying topos"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **classifying topos** for some sort of structure is a [topos](wikipedia://en/21391464) *T* such that there is a natural equivalence between geometric morphisms from a cocomplete topos *E* to *T* and the category of models for the structure in *E*.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The classifying topos for objects of a topos is the topos of [presheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaf) over the opposite of the category of finite sets.
- The classifying topos for rings of a topos is the topos of presheaves over the opposite of the category of finitely presented rings.
- The classifying topos for local rings of a topos is the topos of sheaves over the opposite of the category of finitely presented rings with the [Zariski topology](wikipedia://en/241744).
- The classifying topos for [linear orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_order) with distinct largest and smallest elements of a topos is the topos of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784).
- If *G* is a [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536), the classifying topos for *G*-[torsors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsor) over a topos is the topos *BG* of *G*-sets.
- The [classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556) of [topological groups](wikipedia://en/42315) in [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831).

:::
