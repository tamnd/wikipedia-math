# Beilinson regulator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beilinson regulator"}

In mathematics, especially in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Beilinson regulator** is the [Chern class](wikipedia://en/294349) map from [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) to [Deligne cohomology](wikipedia://en/31615715):

:::

$$K_n (X) \rightarrow \oplus_{p \geq 0} H_D^{2p-n} (X, \mathbf Q(p)).$$

Here, *X* is a complex smooth [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), for example. It is named after [Alexander Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613). The Beilinson regulator features in [Beilinson's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beilinson_conjecture) on [special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882).

The *Dirichlet regulator* map (used in the proof of [Dirichlet's unit theorem](wikipedia://en/460227)) for the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) $\mathcal O_F$ of a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) *F*

$$\mathcal O_F^\times \rightarrow \mathbf R^{r_1 + r_2}, \ \ x \mapsto (\log |\sigma (x)|)_\sigma$$

is a particular case of the Beilinson regulator. (As usual, $\sigma: F \hookrightarrow \mathbf C$ runs over all [complex embeddings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_embedding) of *F*, where conjugate embeddings are considered equivalent.) Up to a factor 2, the Beilinson regulator is also generalization of the [Borel regulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_regulator).
