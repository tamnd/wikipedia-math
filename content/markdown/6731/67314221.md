# Nicholas Horton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicholas Horton"}

**Nicholas (Nick) Horton** is an American statistics professor and author. He is the Beitzel Professor in Technology and Society at [Amherst College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst_College).[^o2] In 2022, he began a 3-year term as the vice president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Horton completed his [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) at [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) and his [Sc.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) at the [Harvard School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health).[^o2]

## Work {#Work}

Horton has written multiple books focusing on [R](wikipedia://en/376707) and [SAS](wikipedia://en/19060492).[^o2][^o6] He is also an author in the fields of statistics education and missing data. He is one of the authors of the GAISE guidelines.[^o7] With [Ben Baumer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Baumer) and Daniel Kaplan, he is the author of Modern Data Science with R.[^o8] Other notable works include:

- *Normal Sexual Dimorphism of the Adult Human Brain Assessed by In Vivo Magnetic Resonance Imaging* [^o9]
- *Much ado about nothing: A comparison of missing data methods and software to fit incomplete data regression models*[^o10]

He is an editor for the [Journal of Statistics and Data Science Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Statistics_Education) (JSDSE).[^o11]

## Awards {#Awards}

Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o13]

Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Horton resides in [Northampton, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northampton,_Massachusetts) with his wife, Julia Riseman.[^o16] The two are advocates for bicycle trails.[^o17]
