# Thomas Lumley (statistician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Lumley"}

**Thomas Lumley** is an Australian statistician who serves as the chair of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland) in New Zealand.[^o4] Lumley is also a member of the "R Core Team."[^o5]

:::

He was elected as a fellow of the [ASA](wikipedia://en/341988) (American Statistical Association) in 2012.[^o6] Lumley was also elected a fellow of the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Te_Apārangi) in 2015.[^o7]

## Education {#Education}

Lumley received his Bachelors of Science at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) in Melbourne, Australia in 1991,[^o9] a Masters of Science in Applied Statistics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in Oxford, United Kingdom in 1993,[^o9] and his Ph.D. in Biostatistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in Seattle, Washington in 1998.[^o9]

## Work {#Work}

Lumley is a professor of statistics at the University of Auckland where he researches regression modelling, clinical trials, semiparametric inference, statistical computing, foundations, and genomics.[^o9] His statistics publications are commonly cited in the statistics and biostatistics fields. He makes contributions to R as a member of the R Core Team[^o5] and contributes to the StatsChat blog.[^o11]

## Awards {#Awards}

- Howard Hughes Medical Institute Predoctoral Fellowship from 1995 to 1998[^o9]
- Donovan J Thompson Award for Academic Excellence in Biostatistics in 1996[^o9]
- Best Oral Presentation at Biometric Society Student Paper Competition in 1997[^o9]
- Gertrude Cox Award for contributions to Statistical Practice in 2008[^o9]
- Elected as an ASA Fellow in 2012[^o6]
- Elected as a fellow of the Royal Society of New Zealand in 2015[^o7]

## Notable works {#Notable_works}

Some of Lumley's notable publications from 2019 include:

- *Estimating prediction error for complex samples*[^o14]
- *Numerical evaluation of methods approximating the distribution of a large quadratic form in normal variables*[^o15]
- *Fast Generalized Linear Models by Database Sampling and One-Step Polishing*[^o16]
