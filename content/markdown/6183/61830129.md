# Brigitte Jaumard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brigitte Jaumard"}

**Brigitte Jaumard** is a computer scientist and expert on [mathematical programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_programming). She earned a doctorate in 1986 from [ENST, today ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENST,_today_ParisTech) under the supervision of Michel Minoux. She was a professor at [Polytechnique Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnique_Montréal). from 1989 until 2000. She is now a professor in the department of computer science and software engineering at [Concordia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_University), where she is the holder of an Honorary Concordia Research Chair in Optimization of Communication Networks.

:::
