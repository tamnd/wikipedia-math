# Quanta Magazine

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Quanta Magazine"}

***Quanta Magazine*** is an editorially independent[^o3] [online publication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_publication) of the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) covering developments in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).

:::

## History {#History}

*Quanta Magazine* was initially launched as *Simons Science News*[^o5] in October 2012, but it was renamed to its current title in July 2013.[^o6]

It was founded by the former *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times)* journalist Thomas Lin, who was the magazine's [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) until 2024.[^o7][^o8] The deputy editor is Michael Moyer, formerly of *[Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American)*, and the art director is Samuel Velasco. In 2024, Samir Patel became the magazine's second editor in chief.[^o9]

## Content {#Content}

The articles in the magazine are freely available to read online.[^o11] *[Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American)*,[^o12] [*Wired*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_%28magazine%29),[^o13] *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic)*, and *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post)*, as well as international science publications like *[Spektrum der Wissenschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spektrum_der_Wissenschaft)*,[^o14] have reprinted articles from the magazine. Articles from the magazine have been translated into German, Spanish, French, Chinese, Italian and Japanese.[^o15]

In November 2018, [MIT Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Press) published two collections of articles from *Quanta Magazine*, *Alice and Bob Meet the Wall of Fire*[^o16] and *The Prime Number Conspiracy*.[^o17]

The magazine's YouTube channel has more than one million subscribers and publishes award-winning animated explainer videos and short documentaries.[^o18][^o19]

The magazine also has three podcasts, two of which are hosted by [Steven Strogatz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Strogatz).[^o20] [Janna Levin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janna_Levin) joined as cohost of *The Joy of Why* for the third season in 2024.[^o21]

## Reception {#Reception}

*[Undark Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undark_Magazine)* described *Quanta Magazine* as "highly regarded for its masterful coverage of complex topics in science and math."[^o23] The science news aggregator *RealClearScience* ranked *Quanta Magazine* first on its list of "The Top 10 Websites for Science in 2018."[^o24]

In 2020, the magazine received a National Magazine Award for General Excellence from the [American Society of Magazine Editors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_of_Magazine_Editors) for its "willingness to tackle some of the toughest and most difficult topics in science and math in a language that is accessible to the lay reader without condescension or [oversimplification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oversimplification)."

In May 2022, the magazine's staff, notably [Natalie Wolchover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Wolchover), were awarded the [Pulitzer Prize for Explanatory Reporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Explanatory_Reporting) for an article Wolchover wrote about the [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope) and an accompanying short documentary by Emily Buder.[^o25][^o26][^o27]
