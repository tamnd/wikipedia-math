# Modular graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modular graph"}

*Image: 2d modular lattice.svg.* A modular graph derived from a [modular lattice](wikipedia://en/1089311)

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a branch of mathematics, the **modular graphs** are [undirected graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) in which every three [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) $x$, $y$, and $z$ have at least one *median vertex* $m(x, y, z)$ that belongs to [shortest paths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortest_path) between each pair of $x$, $y$, and $z$.[^o2] Their name comes from the fact that a finite [lattice](wikipedia://en/469304) is a [modular lattice](wikipedia://en/1089311) if and only if its [Hasse diagram](wikipedia://en/338199) is a modular graph.[^o3]

:::

It is not possible for a modular graph to contain a cycle of odd length. For, if $C$ is a shortest odd cycle in a graph, $x$ is a vertex of $C$, and $yz$ is the edge of $C$ farthest from $x$, there could be no median $m(x, y, z)$. In this case, the only vertices on the shortest path $yz$ are $y$ and $z$ themselves. Neither can belong to a shortest path from $x$ to the other without shortcutting $C$ and creating a shorter odd cycle. Because they have no odd cycles, every modular graph is a [bipartite graph](wikipedia://en/244431).[^o2]

The modular graphs contain as a special case the [median graphs](wikipedia://en/16970848), in which every triple of vertices has a unique median;[^o2] median graphs are related to [distributive lattices](wikipedia://en/310883) in the same way that modular graphs are related to modular lattices. However, the modular graphs also include other graphs such as the [complete bipartite graphs](wikipedia://en/525320) where the medians are not unique: when the three vertices $x$, $y$, and $z$ all belong to one side of the bipartition of a complete bipartite graph, every vertex on the other side is a median.[^o3] Every [chordal bipartite graph](wikipedia://en/44355462) (a class of graphs that includes the complete bipartite graphs and the bipartite [distance-hereditary graphs](wikipedia://en/17315337)) is modular.[^o2]
