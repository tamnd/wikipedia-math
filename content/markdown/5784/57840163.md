# Rational thermodynamics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rational thermodynamics"}

**Rational thermodynamics** is a school of thought in [statistical thermodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_thermodynamics) developed in the 1960s. Its introduction is attributed to [Clifford Truesdell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Truesdell), Bernard Coleman and [Walter Noll](wikipedia://en/14964890).[^o2][^o3] The aim was to develop a mathematical model of thermodynamics that would go beyond the traditional "thermodynamics of [irreversible processes](wikipedia://en/416681)" or TIP developed in the late 19th to early 20th centuries. Truesdell's "flamboyant style" and "satirical verve" caused controversy between "rational thermodynamics" and proponents of traditional thermodynamics.[^o4]

:::
