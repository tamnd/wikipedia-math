# Oum Sang-il

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oum Sang-il"}

Oum

**Oum Sang-il** (; born 1976) is a South Korean mathematician working in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492). He is a distinguished research fellow and the chief investigator of the Discrete Mathematics Group in the Pioneer Research Center for Mathematical and Computational Sciences at the [Institute for Basic Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Basic_Science). He was a tenured professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST). He is known for his work on structural graph theory and in particular for structures and algorithms relating to [rank-width](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank-width), [clique-width](wikipedia://en/16795502), and branch-width. He published more than 80 journal papers.

:::

He won the Young Scientist Award from the South Korean government in 2012.[^o4] and the TJ Park Young Professor Fellowship[^o5] from the POSCO TJ Park Foundation in 2009. He has been an editor of *Graphs and Combinatorics*, *SIAM Journal on Computing*, and *Journal of the Korean Mathematical Society* and a founding member of Young Korean Academy of Science and Technology.

He wrote a monthly article for the monthly magazine *Math Donga* for 4 years from 2016, sharing the latest research breakthroughs in mathematics.[^o6]

In the [Korean Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Mathematical_Society), he was appointed twice (2011–2012, 2017–2018) as an executive member of the board of trustees in charge of the [Korean Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/53554072). He was a member of the Korean Mathematical Olympiad Committee for 2011–2018 and was the deputy leader of the South Korean team at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 2012 and 2018.

## Education {#Education}

section Born in [Yecheon County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yecheon_County), Oum attended [Daegu Science High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu_Science_High_School) in [Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu) from 1992. He then went to [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) where he majored in mathematics and graduated with a B.S. in 1998. Studying in the Program of Applied and Computational Mathematics of [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), he majored in graph theory and discrete mathematics. His dissertation was overseen by Professor [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708) and Ph.D. was awarded in 2005.

## Career {#Career}

From 1999 to 2002, Oum worked as a computer programmer in Korea. He was a visiting assistant professor in the School of Mathematics, [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech) for 2005–2006 and a postdoctoral fellow under [Jim Geelen](wikipedia://en/24393682) in the Department of Combinatorics & Optimization in the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 2007. In 2008, he joined the Department of Mathematical Sciences [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) as an assistant professor before coming an associate professor and then full professor in 2011 and 2016, respectively. He was an affiliate professor in the School of Mathematics, [Korea Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/16984) for several years. In 2018, he was appointed as the CI (Chief Investigator) of the Discrete Mathematics Group in the Pioneer Research Center for Mathematical and Computational Sciences at the [Institute for Basic Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Basic_Science) (IBS). This and the Pioneer Research Center for Biomolecular and Cellular Structure are the first of two such centers at IBS.[^o9][^o10][^o11]

In 2023, Oum became a KAIST Endowed Chair Professor[^o12] and the following year resigned from KAIST and became a Distinguished Research Fellow at IBS.[^o13][^o14]
