# Walter Trump

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Trump"}

**Walter Trump** (born 1953[^o2]) is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from Bavaria and a retired high school teacher. He is known for his work in [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446).

:::

He has made contributions working on both the [square packing problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_packing_in_a_square) and the magic tile problem. In 1979 he discovered the optimal known packing of 11 equal squares in a larger square,[^o3] and in 2003, along with [Christian Boyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Boyer), developed the first known [magic cube](wikipedia://en/372356) of order 5.[^o4] In 2012, Trump *et al.* described a model for retention of liquid on random surfaces.[^o5] In 2014, he and [Francis Gaspalou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Gaspalou) were able to calculate all 8 × 8 [bimagic squares](wikipedia://en/372452#Bimagic_square).[^o6]

Until he retired in 2015,[^o7] Trump worked as a teacher for mathematics and physics at the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) in [Stein, Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stein,_Bavaria).
