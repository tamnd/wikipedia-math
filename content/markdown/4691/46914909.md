# Richard Canary

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Canary"}

**Richard Douglas Canary** (born in 1962) is an American mathematician working mainly on [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774). He is a professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o3]

:::

Canary obtained his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1989 under the supervision of [William Paul Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), with the thesis *Hyperbolic Structures on 3-Manifolds with Compressible Boundaries*.

He received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1993.[^o5]

In 2015 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to low-dimensional topology and [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) as well as for service and teaching in mathematics."[^o6]
