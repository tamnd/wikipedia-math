::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform tiling symmetry mutations"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **symmetry mutation** is a mapping of [fundamental domains](wikipedia://en/469365) between two symmetry groups.[^o6] They are compactly expressed in [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678). These mutations can occur from [spherical tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_tiling) to [Euclidean tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_tiling) to [hyperbolic tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_tiling). Hyperbolic tilings can also be divided between compact, paracompact and divergent cases.

:::

The [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/12126635) are the simplest application of these mutations, although more complex patterns can be expressed within a fundamental domain.

This article expressed progressive sequences of uniform tilings within symmetry families.

::: {#o8 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Mutation of orbifolds"}
