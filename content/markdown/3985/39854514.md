# Tiberiu Popoviciu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tiberiu Popoviciu"}

**Tiberiu Popoviciu** (February 16, 1906–October 29, 1975) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician[^o3] and the namesake of [Popoviciu's inequality](wikipedia://en/24109458) and [Popoviciu's inequality on variances](wikipedia://en/24107159).

:::

The [Tiberiu Popoviciu High School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiberiu_Popoviciu_High_School_of_Computer_Science) in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca) is named after him.[^o4]

In 1951 he founded a research institute which now bears his name: [Tiberiu Popoviciu Institute of Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/51408178).

## Biography {#Biography}

Popoviciu was born in [Arad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arad,_Romania) in 1906, and attended high school in his hometown, the school which is now the [Moise Nicoară National College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moise_Nicoară_National_College). He graduated from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest), and got his doctorate in 1933 under [Paul Montel](wikipedia://en/1796347) from [Paris-Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sorbonne_University).

He was a lecturer at the Universities of [Cernăuți](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernivtsi_University), [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest), and [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University). In 1946 he was appointed professor at the [University of Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University). On June 4, 1937 Popoviciu was elected a corresponding member of the [Romanian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy_of_Sciences). In November 1948 he was elected a corresponding member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy). He became full member of the mathematical sciences section of the Academy on March 20, 1963.

He married his former student, [Elena Moldovan Popoviciu](wikipedia://en/61661129), in 1964; she also became a notable functional analyst. He died in 1975 in Cluj-Napoca, and is buried in the city's [Hajongard Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajongard_cemetery).
