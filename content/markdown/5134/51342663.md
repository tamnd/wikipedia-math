# Tristan Rivière

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tristan Rivière"}

**Tristan Rivière** (born 1967)[^o3] is a French mathematician, working on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rivière studied at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and obtained his PhD in 1993 at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), under the supervision of [Fabrice Bethuel](wikipedia://en/28586899), with a thesis on harmonic maps between manifolds. In 1992 he was appointed [chargé de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France) at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique). In 1997 he received his habilitation at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in Orsay. From 1999 to 2000 he was a visiting associate professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) (New York University). Since 2003 he is full professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and since 2009 he is the Director of the [Institute for Mathematical Research](wikipedia://en/33084376) at ETH.[^o6]

## Research activity {#Research_activity}

His research interests include [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) ([liquid crystals](wikipedia://en/17973), [Bose–Einstein condensates](wikipedia://en/4474), [micromagnetics](wikipedia://en/4153112), [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286) of superconductivity, [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155)) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) ([harmonic maps](wikipedia://en/4577484) between manifolds, [geometric flows](wikipedia://en/14617515), [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734), the [Willmore functional](wikipedia://en/2072431) and [Yang–Mills fields](wikipedia://en/672202)). His work focuses in particular on non-linear phenomena, formation of vortices, energy quantization and regularity issues.

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 1996 he received the Bronze Medal of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), while in 2003 he was awarded the first [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal). In 2002 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing), where he gave a talk on bubbling, quantization and regularity issues in geometric non-linear analysis.
