# Dario Graffi

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Dario Graffi"}

**Dario Graffi** (10 January 1905 – 28 December 1990) was an influential Italian [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist), known for his researches on the [electromagnetic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_field), particularly for a mathematical explanation of the [Luxemburg effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxemburg–Gorky_effect),[^o5] for proving an important uniqueness theorem for the solutions of a class of [fluid dynamics equations](wikipedia://en/11034) including the [Navier-Stokes equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equation),[^o6] for his researches in continuum mechanics and for his contribution to [oscillation theory](wikipedia://en/10144971).[^o7]

:::

## Life and academic career {#Life_and_academic_career}

Dario Graffi was born in Rovigo, the son of Michele, a [yarn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarn) [wholesale trader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wholesaling) and of Amalia Tedeschi.[^o9] He attended the [Istituto tecnico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education_in_Italy#Istituto_tecnico) in his home town, specializing in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), but got his [diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma) in Bologna in 1921, where his family had moved a year before.[^o9]

He graduated from the University of Bologna in Physics in 1925,[^o10] when he was 20,[^o11] and in mathematics in 1927,[^o10] when he was 22:[^o11] both the degrees were awarded [cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cum_laude),[^o12]

### Honors {#Honors}

He was awarded the Golden medal "[Benemeriti della Scuola, della Cultura, dell'Arte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benemeriti_della_Scuola,_della_Cultura,_dell'Arte)" in 1964, and a year later, the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_dei_Lincei) awarded him the [Prize of the President of the Italian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_of_the_President_of_the_Italian_Republic).[^o3]

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

Graffi is known for his researches on the [electromagnetic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_field), particularly for a mathematical explanation of the [Luxemburg effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxemburg–Gorky_effect), for proving an important uniqueness theorem for the solutions of a class of [fluid dynamics equations](wikipedia://en/11034) including the [Navier-Stokes equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equation),[^o16] for his researches in continuum mechanics and for his contribution to [oscillation theory](wikipedia://en/10144971).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Graffi published 181 works.[^o18] lists of his publications are included in references (Cercignani 1992, 108–114) and in the biographical section of his "Selected works" (1999, pp. XX–XXVI): however, the set of lecture notes (Graffi 1981) is not listed in any of his publication lists.

### Scientific works {#Scientific_works}

#### Scientific papers {#Scientific_papers}

- Graffi, Dario. (1936). "Una teoria ereditaria dell'effetto Lussemburgo". *Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico della Università di Padova*. 7. p. 36–54. zbl:0015.13705. <http://www.numdam.org/item?id=RSMUP_1936__7__36_0>. In this paper, written only few years after the discovery of the effect itself, Dario Graffi proposes a theory of the Luxemburg effect based on Volterra's theory of hereditary phenomena.
- Graffi, Dario. (1949). "Sul modello del Rocard per le oscillazioni di rilassamento". *Atti della Accademia delle Scienze di Ferrara*. 21–22 – Anni Accademici (1938–1943). p. 255–257
- Graffi, Dario. (1953). "Il Teorema di Unicita nella Dinamica dei Fluidi Compressibili". *Journal of Rational Mechanics and Analysis*. 2 (1). p. 99–106. doi:10.1512/iumj.1953.2.52004. mr:0052270. zbl:0050.19604. In this paper, Graffi extends to compressible viscous fluids a uniqueness theorem for the solutions to [Navier-Stokes equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equation) in [bounded](wikipedia://en/48258) [domains](wikipedia://en/16796643), previously proved only for incompressible fluids by [Emanuele Foà](wikipedia://en/48259346) and rediscovered by [David Dolidze](wikipedia://en/48171936).[^o26]
- Graffi, Dario. (1955). "Il teorema di unicità per i fluidi incompressibili, perfetti, eterogenei". *Revista de la Unión Matemática Argentina y de la Asociación Física Argentina*. XVII. p. 73–77. mr:0082829. zbl:0074.20206. <http://inmabb.criba.edu.ar/revuma/pdf/v17/p073-077.pdf>.
- Graffi, Dario. (2 November 1959). "Sur un théorème d'unicité pour le mouvement d'un fluide visqueux dans un domaine illimité". *Comptes rendus hebdomadaires des séances de l'Académie des sciences*. 249 (2). p. 1741–1743. mr:0109550. zbl:0088.41201. <http://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k731d/f603.image>, available at [Gallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallica). A short research note announcing the results of the author on the uniqueness of solutions of the [Navier-Stokes equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equations) on unbounded [domains](wikipedia://en/16796643) under the [hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypothesis) of constant [fluid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid) [velocity](wikipedia://en/20491903) at [infinity](wikipedia://en/20110824).
- Graffi, Dario. (1960). "Sul teorema di unicità nella dinamica dei fluidi". *Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata*. 50. p. 379–387. doi:10.1007/BF02414524. mr:122198. zbl:0102.41103 (online version 1618-1891). In this paper, Graffi extends his uniqueness theorem for the solutions of Navier-Stokes equations on unbounded [domains](wikipedia://en/16796643) relaxing previously assumed hypotheses on the behaviour of the velocity at infinity.
- Graffi, Dario. (1962). "Sui teoremi di unicita nella dinamica dei fluidi". *Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico e Fisico di Milano*. 32 (1). p. 80–91. doi:10.1007/BF02925666. mr:0148304. zbl:0128.43503. The published text of a conference held at the [Seminario Matematico e Fisico di Milano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminario_Matematico_e_Fisico_di_Milano), exposing mainly his researches on the uniqueness of the solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations.
- Graffi, Dario. (December 1974). "Sull'espressione dell'energia libera nei materiali viscoelastici lineari". *Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata*. 98. p. 273–279. doi:10.1007/BF02414027. mr:0345497. zbl:0281.73027 (online version 1618-1891). In this paper, Graffi introduces the [free energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic_free_energy) now called [Graffi–Volterra free energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffi–Volterra_free_energy) after him.
- Graffi, Dario; Fabrizio, Mauro. (1989). "Sulla nozione di stato per materiali viscoelastici di tipo "rate"". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Rendiconti. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. 83 (1). p. 201–208. mr:1142459. zbl:0732.73023. <http://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLIN_1989_8_83_1_201_0>.
- Graffi, Dario; Fabrizio, Mauro. (1989). "Non unicità dell'energia libera per materiali viscoelastici". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Rendiconti. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. 83 (1). p. 209–214. mr:1142460. zbl:0732.73024. <http://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLIN_1989_8_83_1_209_0>.
