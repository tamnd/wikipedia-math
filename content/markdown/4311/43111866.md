# Luigi Amerio

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Luigi Amerio"}

**Luigi Amerio** (15 August 1912 – 28 September 2004), was an Italian [electrical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for his work on [almost periodic functions](wikipedia://en/405512), on [Laplace transforms](wikipedia://en/18610) in one and several dimensions, and on the theory of [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787).

:::

## Works {#Works}

A selection of Luigi Amerio's scientific papers is published in the two volumes of his "*Selecta*" (Amerio 1990): he is also the author of several university textbooks and, jointly with his pupil [Giovanni Prouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Prouse), he wrote the influential monograph on almost periodic functions (Amerio Prouse 1971).

- Amerio, Luigi. (1943). "Su alcune questioni relative alla trasformazione di Laplace". *Istituto Lombardo di Scienze e Lettere, Rendiconti, Classe di Scienze Matematiche e Naturali*. 76 (7). p. 191–216. mr:0011501. zbl:0060.24909. In this work, Luigi Amerio proves an important theorem on Laplace transform.
- Amerio, Luigi. (1945). "Sull'integrazione delle equazioni lineari a derivate parziali del secondo ordine di tipo ellittico". *Acta Pontificia Academia Scientarum*. 9. p. 213–228. mr:0025665. zbl:0061.22502. <http://www.casinapioiv.va/content/dam/accademia/pdf/acta9/acta9-amerio.pdf>. A research announcement disclosing the results published in (Amerio 1945) and (Amerio 1947).
- Amerio, Luigi. (1945). "Sull'integrazione dell'equazione in un dominio di connessione qualsiasi". *Istituto Lombardo di Scienze e Lettere, Rendiconti, Classe di Scienze Matematiche e Naturali*. 78 (9). p. 79–102. mr:0021206. zbl:0061.23703. In this paper Amerio obtained the first theoretical results on [Mauro Picone](wikipedia://en/10162009)'s method of solving [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) for [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787) by the [Riesz-Fischer theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riesz-Fischer_theorem).
- Amerio, Luigi. (July 1947). "Sul Calcolo delle Soluzioni dei Problemi al Contorno per le Equazioni Lineari del Secondo Ordine". *American Journal of Mathematics*. 69 (3). p. 447–489. doi:10.2307/2371879. mr:0021207. zbl:0034.20202. jstor:2371879. A continuation of the research initiated in (Amerio 1945).
- Amerio, Luigi; Prouse, Giovanni. (1971). "Almost-periodic functions and functional equations". Van Nostrand Reinhold. p. viii+184. isbn:0-442-20295-4. mr:275061. zbl:0215.15701.
- Amerio, Luigi. (1990). "Selecta". CUSL. p. 520 (Volume I), pp. 521–1036 (Volume II). Luigi Amerio's "*Selecta*" in two volumes, collecting a selection of his scientific contributions.
