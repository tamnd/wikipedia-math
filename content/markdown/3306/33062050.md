# Śleszyński–Pringsheim theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Śleszyński–Pringsheim theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Śleszyński–Pringsheim theorem** is a statement about [convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_%28continued_fraction%29) of certain [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199). It was discovered by [Ivan Śleszyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Śleszyński)[^o2] and [Alfred Pringsheim](wikipedia://en/1068126)[^o3] in the late 19th century.[^o4]

:::

It states that if $n$ is a positive [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) and $(a_n)$, $(b_n)$ are [sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) such that $|b_n| \geq |a_n| + 1$ for all $n$, then

$$\cfrac{a_1}{b_1 + \cfrac{a_2}{b_2 + \cfrac{a_3}{b_3 + \ddots}}}$$

converges absolutely to a number $x$ satisfying $|x| \leq 1$,[^o5] meaning that the series

$$x = \sum_n \left\{ \frac{A_n}{B_n} - \frac{A_{n - 1}}{B_{n - 1}} \right\},$$

where $A_n / B_n$ are the [convergents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_%28continued_fraction%29) of the continued fraction, [converges absolutely](wikipedia://en/61532).

::: {#o7 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

Recall that the $n$th convergents of $x$, which will be denoted by $\tfrac{A_n}{B_n}$ in this article, can be computed from the following [recurrence relation](wikipedia://en/146806):

$$\begin{aligned} A_n &:= b_n A_{n - 1} + a_n A_{n - 2} \\ B_n &:= b_n B_{n - 1} + a_n B_{n - 2}, \qquad n \geq 2 \end{aligned}$$

where $A_0 = 0$, $A_1 = a_1$, $B_0 = 1$, and $B_1 = b_1$. See [this article](wikipedia://en/739199#Formulation) for more detail.

### $n$th convergent as a series {#th_convergent_as_a_series}

First, we will prove the following claim via [mathematical induction](wikipedia://en/18881)

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/claim cue=math-theorem name="Claim"}

**Claim.**

For all positive integers $n$, then

$$A_n B_{n - 1} - A_{n - 1} B_n = (-1)^{n - 1} a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n$$

:::

::: {#o10 .proof cue=math-proof}

The case $n = 1$ is trivial.

Suppose the claim is true for $n = k$. By using the two recurrence relation above, it follows that

$$\begin{aligned} A_{k + 1} B_k - A_k B_{k + 1} &= \left( b_{k + 1} A_k + a_{k + 1} A_{k - 1} \right) B_k - A_k \left( b_{k + 1} B_k + a_{k + 1} B_{k - 1} \right) \\ &= b_{k + 1} A_k B_k + a_{k + 1} A_{k - 1} B_k - b_{k + 1} A_k B_k - a_{k + 1} A_k B_{k - 1} \\ &= a_{k + 1} A_{k - 1} B_k - a_{k + 1} A_k B_{k - 1} \\ &= - a_{k + 1} \left( A_k B_{k - 1} - A_{k - 1} B_k \right) \\ &= - a_{k + 1} \cdot (-1)^{k - 1} a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k \\ &= (-1)^k a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k a_{k + 1} \end{aligned}$$

which finishes the induction step.

:::

By dividing [both sides](wikipedia://en/166352) of the claim by $B_n \cdot B_{n - 1}$, the equation becomes

$$\dfrac{A_n}{B_n} - \dfrac{A_{n - 1}}{B_{n - 1}} = (-1)^{n - 1} \dfrac{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}{B_{n - 1} \cdot B_n}$$

Thus,

$$\begin{aligned} \sum_{i \, = \, 1}^{n} \dfrac{A_i}{B_i} - \dfrac{A_{i - 1}}{B_{i - 1}} &= \sum_{i \, = \, 1}^{n} (-1)^{i - 1} \dfrac{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_i}{B_{i - 1} B_i} \\ \dfrac{A_n}{B_n} - \dfrac{A_0}{B_0} &= \dfrac{a_1}{B_0 B_1} - \dfrac{a_1 a_2}{B_1 B_2} + \dfrac{a_1 a_2 a_3}{B_2 B_3} - \ldots + (-1)^{n - 1} \dfrac{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}{B_{n - 1} B_n} \\ \dfrac{A_n}{B_n} &= \dfrac{a_1}{B_0 B_1} - \dfrac{a_1 a_2}{B_1 B_2} + \dfrac{a_1 a_2 a_3}{B_2 B_3} - \ldots + (-1)^{n - 1} \dfrac{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}{B_{n - 1} B_n} \end{aligned}$$

### Absolute value of $n$th convergent as a series {#Absolute_value_of_th_convergent_as_a_series}

Now suppose that $|b_n| \geq |a_n| + 1$ for all $n$. Using the recurrence relation of $(B_n)$, note that

$$\left| b_n B_{n - 1} \right| = \left| B_n - a_n B_{n - 2} \right| \leq \left| B_n \right| + \left| a_n B_{n - 2} \right|.$$

Thus,

$$\begin{aligned} \left| B_n \right| &\geq \left| b_n \right| \left| B_{n - 1} \right| - \left| a_n \right| \left| B_{n - 2} \right| \\ &\geq \left( \left| a_n \right| + 1 \right) \left| B_{n - 1} \right| - \left| a_n \right| \left| B_{n - 2} \right| \\ \left| B_n \right| - \left| B_{n - 1} \right| &\geq \left| a_n \right| \left( \left| B_{n - 1} \right| - \left| B_{n - 2} \right| \right) \end{aligned}$$

Since $\left| B_1 \right| - \left| B_0 \right| = \left| b_1 \right| - 1 \geq \left| a_1 \right|$ by assumption, then by using mathematical induction, one can show that

$$\left| B_n \right| - \left| B_{n - 1} \right| \geq \prod_{i \, = \, 1}^{n} \left| a_i \right|.$$

Consequently, the sequence of $\left| B_n \right|$ is [monotone nondecreasing](wikipedia://en/48260) and [bounded from below](wikipedia://en/311509) by $\left| B_0 \right| = 1$. Moreover,

$$\dfrac{1}{\left| B_n B_{n - 1} \right|} \prod_{i \, = \, 1}^{n} \left| a_i \right| \leq \dfrac{\left| B_n \right| - \left| B_{n - 1} \right|}{\left| B_n B_{n - 1} \right|} = \dfrac{1}{\left| B_{n - 1} \right|} - \dfrac{1}{\left| B_n \right|}$$

Since the right-hand side forms a [telescoping series](wikipedia://en/508070), it is easy to see that

$$\dfrac{\left| a_1 \right|}{\left| B_0 B_1 \right|} + \dfrac{\left| a_1 a_2 \right|}{\left| B_1 B_2 \right|} + \ldots + \dfrac{\left| a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n \right|}{\left| B_{n - 1} B_n \right|} \leq \dfrac{1}{\left| B_0 \right|} - \dfrac{1}{\left| B_n \right|} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{\left| B_n \right|} < 1$$

for all values of $n$. Furthermore, the nondecreasing property of the sequence $\left| B_n \right|$ also implies the nondecreasing property of the sequence $1 - \tfrac{1}{\left| B_n \right|}$. Thus, the sequence $1 - \tfrac{1}{\left| B_n \right|}$ must converge, by the [monotone convergence theorem](wikipedia://en/146263).

Note that the left-hand side is the upper bound of series representation of $\left| \tfrac{A_n}{B_n} \right|$ after applying [triangle inequality](wikipedia://en/53941), which completes the proof.

:::
