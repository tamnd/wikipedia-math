# Amir Leshem

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amir Leshem"}

**Amir Leshem** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): אמיר לשם; born: 1966) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelites) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research). He is a full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the Alexander Kofkin Faculty of Engineering at [Bar‑Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University), where he heads the Signal Processing, Information Networks & Radio Astronomy (SPIRAL) Lab.[^o3]

:::

His research spans [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324), [multi‑agent learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-agent_learning), [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [wireless communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wireless_communications), and [radio astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_astronomy) imaging. Leshem is an [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) "for contributions to multi-channel and multi-agent signal processing".[^o4]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Amir Leshem was born in [Petah Tikva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petah_Tikva). He graduated the [IDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces) [Talpiot program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talpiot_program) with a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at the Hebrew University under the supervision of [Menachem Magidor](wikipedia://en/17115328).[^o6][^o7]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Leshem was a postdoctoral researcher at [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology) from 1998 to 2000. He joined Bar‑Ilan University in 2002 as a senior lecturer and was the first tenured faculty member of the Alexander Kofkin Faculty of Engineering. He was promoted to full professor in 2010 and served as head of the Signal Processing track and Communication track.[^o3][^o9]

Leshem was an [associate editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_editor) of IEEE Trans. on Signal Processing and associate editor of , IEEE Transactions on Signal and Information processing over Networks.[^o3]

He was also a [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) and Delft University of Technology.[^o6][^o10]

Leshem has supervised more than 30 graduate students and authored over 200 scientific publications and 4 [patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent).[^o11][^o12][^o13]

In 2024 Leshem, together with Vered Tohar, created the first Hebrew poetry book authored by an artificial intelligence.[^o14][^o15]

## Research {#Research}

His research work focuses on wireless networks, learning over networks, applications of game theory to networks, multi-channel communication, dynamic and adaptive [spectrum management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_Management_and_Telecommunications), [statistical signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_signal_processing), multi-agent learning and radio-astronomical imaging. He is also interested in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) and [information processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_processing) in [social networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_networking_service).[^o6]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Leshem is an IEEE fellow "for contributions to multi-channel and multi-agent signal processing" and fellow of the Asia‑Pacific Artificial Intelligence Association. He also received the Bar‑Ilan University Rector’s Award for Scientific Innovation.[^o9][^o18][^o19]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Leshem is also a landscape [photographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photographer). He participated in the group exhibition *The Magic of Nature* at the Tel‑Hai Photography Museum and won the excellence prize in the photography category at the 9th Geoje International Art Festival in [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea).[^o21]
