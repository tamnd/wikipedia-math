# IAPM (mode)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="IAPM"}

**Integrity-aware parallelizable mode** (**IAPM**) is a [mode of operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_cipher_modes_of_operation) for cryptographic [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594). As its name implies, it allows for a parallel mode of operation for higher throughput.

:::

*Image: IAPM-Jutla-redacted.jpg.* Charanjit Jutla's hand-drawn IAPM figure in IBM Technical Notebook (Oct 2000)

## Encryption and authentication {#Encryption_and_authentication}

At the time of its creation, IAPM was one of the first cipher modes to provide both [authentication](wikipedia://en/47967) and [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy) in a single pass.[^o3][^o4][^o5] (In earlier [authenticated encryption](wikipedia://en/2244296) designs, two passes would be required to: one to encrypt, and the second to compute a [MAC](wikipedia://en/567523).)

IAPM was proposed for use in [IPsec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPsec).[^o6]

Other [AEAD](wikipedia://en/2244296) schemes also provide all of the single pass, privacy and authentication properties. IAPM has mostly been supplanted by [Galois/counter mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois/counter_mode).
