# Admissible algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Admissible algebra"}

In mathematics, an **admissible algebra** is a (possibly non-associative) [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) whose enveloping [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) of [derivations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivation_%28abstract_algebra%29) splits into the sum of an even and an odd part. Admissible algebras were introduced by (txt).
