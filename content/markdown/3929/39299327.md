# Malcev-admissible algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malcev-admissible algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **Malcev-admissible algebra**, introduced by (txt), is a (possibly [non-associative](wikipedia://en/6533841)) [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) that becomes a [Malcev algebra](wikipedia://en/5378206) under the bracket \[*a*, *b*\] = *ab* − *ba*. Examples include [alternative algebras](wikipedia://en/3160), Malcev algebras and [Lie-admissible algebras](wikipedia://en/39299080).

:::
