# Lie-admissible algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lie-admissible algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **Lie-admissible algebra**, introduced by (txt), is a (possibly [non-associative](wikipedia://en/6533841)) [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) that becomes a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) under the bracket \[*a*, *b*\] = *ab* − *ba*. Examples include [associative algebras](wikipedia://en/2112),[^o3] Lie algebras, and [Okubo algebras](wikipedia://en/39255698).

:::
