# Noncommutative Jordan algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncommutative Jordan algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **noncommutative Jordan algebra** is an [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788), usually over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_of_a_field) not 2, such that the four operations of left and right multiplication by *x* and *x*<sup>2</sup> all commute with each other. Examples include [associative algebras](wikipedia://en/2112) and [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371).

:::

Over fields of characteristic not 2, noncommutative Jordan algebras are the same as [flexible](wikipedia://en/36035264) Jordan-admissible algebras,[^o2] where a **Jordan-admissible algebra** – introduced by (txt) and named after [Pascual Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascual_Jordan) – is a (possibly [non-associative](wikipedia://en/6533841)) algebra that becomes a Jordan algebra under the product *a* ∘ *b* = *ab* + *ba*.
