# Geoffrey Colin Shephard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Colin Shephard"}

**Geoffrey Colin Shephard** (16 August 1927 - 3 August 2016)[^o2] was a British mathematician who worked on convex geometry and reflection groups. He asked [Shephard's problem](wikipedia://en/10614436) on the volumes of projected convex bodies, posed [another problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shephard's_conjecture) on polyhedral nets, proved the [Shephard–Todd theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shephard–Todd_theorem) in invariant theory of finite groups, began the study of [complex polytopes](wikipedia://en/8581934), and classified the [complex reflection groups](wikipedia://en/5381096).

:::

Shephard earned his Ph.D. in 1954 from [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge), under the supervision of [J. A. Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._A._Todd). He was a professor of mathematics at the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia) until his retirement.[^o4]
