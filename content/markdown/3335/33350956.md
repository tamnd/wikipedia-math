# Projection body

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Projection body"}

In [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882), the **projection body** $\Pi K$ of a [convex body](wikipedia://en/8652020) $K$ in *n*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) is the convex body such that for any vector $u\in S^{n-1}$, the [support function](wikipedia://en/9377661) of $\Pi K$ in the direction *u* is the (*n* – 1)-dimensional volume of the projection of *K* onto the [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) orthogonal to *u*.

:::

[Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537) showed that the projection body of a convex body is convex. (Petty 1967) and (txt) used projection bodies in their solution to [Shephard's problem](wikipedia://en/10614436).

For $K$ a convex body, let $\Pi^\circ K$ denote the [polar body](wikipedia://en/1764022) of its projection body. There are two remarkable affine [isoperimetric inequality](wikipedia://en/326182) for this body. (Petty 1971) proved that for all convex bodies $K$,

$$V_n(K)^{n-1} V_n(\Pi^\circ K)\le V_n(B^n)^{n-1} V_n(\Pi^\circ B^n),$$

where $B^n$ denotes the *n*-dimensional unit ball and $V_n$ is *n*-dimensional volume, and there is equality precisely for ellipsoids. (txt) proved that for all convex bodies $K$,

$$V_n(K)^{n-1} V_n(\Pi^\circ K)\ge V_n(T^n)^{n-1} V_n(\Pi^\circ T^n),$$

where $T^n$ denotes any $n$-dimensional simplex, and there is equality precisely for such simplices.

The **intersection body** *IK* of *K* is defined similarly, as the star body such that for any vector *u* the radial function of *IK* from the origin in direction *u* is the (*n* – 1)-dimensional volume of the intersection of *K* with the hyperplane *u*<sup>⊥</sup>. Equivalently, the radial function of the intersection body *IK* is the [Funk transform](wikipedia://en/23253927) of the radial function of *K*. Intersection bodies were introduced by (txt).

(Koldobsky 1998a) showed that a centrally symmetric star-shaped body is an intersection body [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) the function 1/||*x*|| is a positive definite distribution, where ||*x*|| is the [homogeneous function](wikipedia://en/622844) of degree 1 that is 1 on the boundary of the body, and (Koldobsky 1998b) used this to show that the unit balls l${}^{p}_{n}$, 2 \< *p* ≤ ∞ in *n*-dimensional space with the [l<sup>*p*</sup> norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lp_norm) are intersection bodies for *n*=4 but are not intersection bodies for *n* ≥ 5.
