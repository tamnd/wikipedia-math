# Ince equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ince equation"}

In mathematics, the **Ince equation**, named for [Edward Lindsay Ince](wikipedia://en/33356146), is the [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309)

:::

$$w^{\prime\prime}+\xi\sin(2z)w^{\prime}+(\eta-p\xi\cos(2z))w=0. \,$$

When *p* is a non-negative integer, it has polynomial solutions called **Ince polynomials**. In particular, when $p=1, \eta\pm\xi=1$, then it has a closed-form solution[^o2]

$$w(z)=Ce^{-iz}(e^{2iz}\mp 1)$$

where $C$ is a constant.
