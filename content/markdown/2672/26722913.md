# Mathematical instrument

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical instrument"}

*Image: Regular Pentagon Inscribed in a Circle 240px.ogv.* Animation of construction of a pentagon using a compass and straightedge

A **mathematical instrument** is a tool or device used in the study or practice of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), construction of various proofs was done using only a [compass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_%28drafting%29) and [straightedge](wikipedia://en/147854); arguments in these proofs relied only on idealized properties of these instruments and literal construction was regarded as only an approximation. In [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), mathematical instruments were used for measuring angles and distances, in [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), [navigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navigation), [surveying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveying) and in the measurement of time.[^o2]

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

*Image: Astrolabe PSF.svg.* The [astrolabe](wikipedia://en/73664) was an early mathematical instrument used in astronomy and navigation.

Instruments such as the [astrolabe](wikipedia://en/73664), the [quadrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrant_%28instrument%29), and others were used to measure and accurately record the relative positions and movements of planets and other celestial objects. The [sextant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextant) and other related instruments were essential for navigation at sea.

Most instruments are used within the field of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), including the [ruler](wikipedia://en/65593), [dividers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caliper#Divider_caliper), [protractor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protractor), [set square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_square), compass, [ellipsograph](wikipedia://en/24365561), [T-square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-square) and [opisometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opisometer). Others are used in [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) (for example the [abacus](wikipedia://en/655), [slide rule](wikipedia://en/28743) and [calculator](wikipedia://en/7593)) or in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) (the [integraph](wikipedia://en/14230769)). In astronomy, many have said the pyramids (along with Stonehenge) were actually instruments used for tracking the stars over long periods or for the annual planting seasons.

## In schools {#In_schools}

The [Oxford Set of Mathematical Instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Set_of_Mathematical_Instruments) is a set of instruments used by generations of school children in the United Kingdom and around the world in mathematics and geometry lessons. It includes two set squares, a 180° protractor, a 15 cm ruler, a metal compass, a metal divider, a 9 cm pencil, a pencil sharpener, an eraser and a 10mm stencil.
