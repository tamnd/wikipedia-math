# Tellegen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tellegen"}

**Tellegen** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Auke Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auke_Tellegen) (1930–2024), Dutch-born American psychologist of the personality
- [Bernard D. H. Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_D._H._Tellegen), Dutch electrical engineer
- [Frits Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frits_Tellegen) (1919–2020), Dutch urban designer
- [Jan Willem Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Willem_Tellegen) (1859–1921), Dutch politician
- [Lou Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Tellegen), Dutch American silent film and stage actor, director, and screenwriter
- Marie Anne Tellegen, Dutch feminist, resistance fighter and director of the Kabinet der Koningin.
- [Ockje Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ockje_Tellegen), Dutch politician
- [Toon Tellegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toon_Tellegen), Dutch children's writer and poet
