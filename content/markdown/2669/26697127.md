# Peter J. Bickel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter J. Bickel"}

**Peter John Bickel** (born 1940) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661)[^o3] and Professor of Statistics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o4] Bickel has made contributions to [bootstrapping](wikipedia://en/6885770), [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), and other areas of statistics.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bickel studied physics at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o6] He graduated from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), with a Ph.D., in 1963, where he studied under [Erich Leo Lehmann](wikipedia://en/16388476).[^o7]

His students include [C.F. Jeff Wu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.F._Jeff_Wu), [Jianqing Fan](wikipedia://en/28282149), [Katerina Kechris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katerina_Kechris), [Elizaveta Levina](wikipedia://en/55774300), [Jingyi Jessica Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingyi_Jessica_Li), and [Donald Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Andrews).[^o8]

## Personal {#Personal}

He married Nancy Kramer in 1964; they have two children.[^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1970 [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow)[^o12]
- 1973 [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association)[^o13]
- 1981 the recipient of [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483)[^o14]
- 1984 [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program#1984)
- 1986 Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences)[^o15]
- 1986 Member of the National Academy of Sciences[^o16]
- 1986 Honorary Doctorate degree from [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University), Jerusalem
- 1995 Foreign member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences)[^o17]
- 2006 Commander of the [Order of Orange-Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Orange-Nassau)[^o18]
- 2013 [R. A. Fisher Lectureship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._A._Fisher_Lectureship)[^o19]
- 2014 Honorary Doctorate degree from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich)[^o20]
