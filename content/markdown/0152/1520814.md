# Jean-Pierre Bourguignon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Bourguignon"}

**Jean-Pierre Bourguignon** (born 21 July 1947) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), he studied at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in [Palaiseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaiseau), graduating in 1969. For his graduate studies he went to [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University), where he obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1974 under the direction of [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363).

He was president of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France) from 1990 to 1992. From 1995 to 1998, he was president of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470). He was director of the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) near [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) from 1994 to 2013. Between 1 January 2014 and 31 December 2019 he was the President of the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council).

Prof. Bourguignon received the [Prix Paul Langevin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Paul_Langevin) in 1987 and the [Prix du Rayonnement Français](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_du_Rayonnement_Français) in Mathematical Sciences and Physics from the [Académie des Sciences de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1997. He is a foreign member of the [Royal Spanish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Spanish_Academy_of_Sciences). In 2005, he was elected honorary member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and has been the secretary of the mathematics section of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o5]He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2026.[^o6]
