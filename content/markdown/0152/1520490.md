# Bart Preneel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bart Preneel"}

**Bart Preneel** (born 15 October 1963 in [Leuven, Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leuven,_Belgium)[^o3]) is a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [cryptanalyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptanalyst). He is a professor at [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven), in the [COSIC](wikipedia://en/8116603) group.[^o4]

:::

He was the president of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043)[^o5] in 2008–2013 and project manager of [ECRYPT](wikipedia://en/1635837).[^o6]

## Education {#Education}

In 1987, Preneel received a degree in Electrical Engineering from the [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven).[^o6]

In 1993, Preneel received a PhD in Applied Sciences from the [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven).[^o8][^o6] His dissertation in computer science, entitled *Analysis and Design of Cryptographic Hash Functions*, was advised by [Joos (Joseph) P. L. Vandewalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joos_Vandewalle) and [René J. M. Govaerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/René_Govaerts).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Along with [Shoji Miyaguchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoji_Miyaguchi), he independently invented the [Miyaguchi–Preneel scheme](wikipedia://en/3851622#Miyaguchi–Preneel),[^o10][^o11][^o12] a structure that converts a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) into a [hash function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hash_function), used e.g. in the hash function [Whirlpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whirlpool_%28algorithm%29).[^o13] He is one of the authors of the [RIPEMD-160](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIPEMD-160) hash function.[^o14] He was also a co-inventor of the [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) [MUGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUGI)[^o15] which would later become a Japanese standard,[^o16][^o17] and of the stream cipher [Trivium](wikipedia://en/2620653)[^o18] which was a well-received entrant to the [eSTREAM](wikipedia://en/2287686) project.[^o19]

He has also contributed to the cryptanalysis of [RC4](wikipedia://en/25831),[^o20] [SOBER-t32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOBER-t32),[^o21] [MacGuffin](wikipedia://en/719065),[^o22] [Helix](wikipedia://en/883596),[^o23] [Phelix](wikipedia://en/883596),[^o24] [Py](wikipedia://en/3955084),[^o25] [TPypy](wikipedia://en/3955084),[^o26] the [HAVAL](wikipedia://en/1018362) [cryptographic hash function](wikipedia://en/439526),[^o27] and the [SecurID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SecurID) hash function.[^o28]
