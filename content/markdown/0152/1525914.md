# Johann Benedict Listing

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Benedict Listing"}

**Johann Benedict Listing** (25 July 1808 – 24 December 1882) was a German mathematician and physicist. He is remembered as a pioneer of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), which also takes its name from his work.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

J. B. Listing was born in [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt) and died in [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen). He studied mathematics and architecture at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), but his interests extended more broadly to topics such as [terrestrial magnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrestrial_magnetism) and physiological [optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optics). In 1834 he received his doctorate under [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125).[^o4] He then spent three years traveling with [Wolfgang Sartorius von Waltershausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Sartorius_von_Waltershausen) to study the volcanic activity of [Mount Etna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Etna) in [Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicily).

## Career {#Career}

In 1837 Listing became a teacher of machine drawing, machine theory, and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [Höhere Gewerbeschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_University_Hanover) in [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover). Two years later, in 1839, he was appointed professor of physics as the successor to [Wilhelm Eduard Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Eduard_Weber), and in 1849 he became professor of mathematics in Göttingen. Together with his friend, the ophthalmologist [Christian Georg Theodor Ruete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Georg_Theodor_Ruete),[^o6] he researched the laws of eye movement.

Encouraged by his mentor, Gauss, Listing began to specialize in topology, then known as analysis situs. In 1847 he published *Vorstudien zur Topologie (Preliminary Studies in Topology)*, although he had already used the term “topology” in letters a decade earlier.[^o7] This book helped introduce the term into general use. Independently of [August Ferdinand Möbius](wikipedia://en/37875), Listing also discovered the special properties of the [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) in 1858 and went further in exploring the properties of strips with higher-order twists ([paradromic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradromic) rings). He discovered topological invariants which came to be called [Listing numbers](wikipedia://en/28345268).[^o8]

In [ophthalmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophthalmology), [Listing's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Listing's_law) describes an essential element of extraocular eye muscle coordination.

In [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608), he coined in 1872 the term "[geoid](wikipedia://en/176244)" for the idealized geometric surface of the [figure of the Earth](wikipedia://en/233668), as previously conceptualized by his doctoral adviser, [Gauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss).[^o9]

Listing was elected a member of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1861 and became an Honorary Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1879.[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Listing's granddaughter was British War Artist [Anna Airy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Airy), by his younger daughter Anna.[^o12]
