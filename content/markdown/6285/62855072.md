# Log (unit)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Log"}

*Image: Samuel and Saidye Bronfman Archaeology WingDSCN5021.JPG.* Jars from the [Second Temple period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Temple_period) ([Israel Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Museum))

A ***log*** (Hebrew לוג or וג, Romanized *lōḡ*) is a biblical and [halakhic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halakhic) unit of liquid [volume](wikipedia://en/32498). [^o2] The word *log* occurs in the Bible, in Lev. 14:10, 15, 21 which prescribes the [korban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korban) (*asham*, "guilt-offering") of a poor *[metzorah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzaraath)*:

:::

ואם דל הוא ואין ידו משגת ולקח כבש אחד אשם לתנופה לכפר עליו ועשרון סלת אחד בלול בשמן למנחה ולג שמן

And if he be poor, and his means suffice not, then he shall take one he-lamb for a guilt-offering to be waved, to make atonement for him, and one tenth part of an ephah of fine flour mingled with oil for a meal-offering, and a log of oil;

## Unit definition, conversion, mnemonics and supports {#Unit_definition,_conversion,_mnemonics_and_supports}

The Talmud, citing the [gematria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria) of an extra scriptural word *ZeH* "this," which equals twelve (seven plus five), explains that one [hin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblical_and_Talmudic_units_of_measurement#Liquid_measure) is twelve log:

הין תריסר לוגי הויין דכתיב (שמות ל, כד) שמן זית הין וכתיב (שמות ל, לא) שמן משחת קדש יהיה זה לי לדורותיכם זה בגימטריא תריסר הויין:

The Gemara elaborates: Now, one hin is twelve log, as it is written: “And of olive oil a hin” (Exodus 30:24), and it is written afterward in the same verse: “Sacred anointing oil, this \[*zeh*\] shall be for Me, throughout your generations.” The numerical value \[gematria\] of zeh is twelve.[^o4]

The Mishnah immediately preceding, which this Gemara comes to explain, states that a half-hin is six log. Thus a hin is twelve log.[^o4]

A list of conversions follows:

- 1 *hin* (הִין *hîn*) = 12 *logs*
- 1 *log* = 6 *[Beitzah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblical_and_Talmudic_units_of_measurement#Volume_and_capacity)* (egg)[^o5]

The log is believed to have been equal to a little over 500; thus, a *hin* was a little over 6.[^o6]
