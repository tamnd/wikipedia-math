# Milton Sobel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milton Sobel"}

**Milton Sobel** (August 30, 1919 – December 31, 2002) was an American mathematician and statistician professor emeritus of statistics at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara).[^o2][^o3] He made notable contributions in the areas of [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216), [sequential analysis](wikipedia://en/3509530), [selection and ranking](wikipedia://en/160986), [reliability analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliability_theory), combinatorial problems, and [Dirichlet processes](wikipedia://en/8330403). Of particular note are his contributions in selection and ranking, a series of work on sequential analysis and reliability along with [Benjamin Epstein](wikipedia://en/82479734).[^o2][^o4]

:::

He obtained his B.A. in mathematics (1940) from the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), M.A. in mathematics (1946) and Ph.D. in mathematical statistics (advisor: [Abraham Wald](wikipedia://en/1239430), 1951) from Columbia University.[^o2]

During 1960-1975 he was Professor of Statistics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o2]

## Books {#Books}

- 1968: Sequential Identification and Ranking Procedures (with Robert E. Bechhofer and Jack C. Kiefer)
