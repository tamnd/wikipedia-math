# Sarah J. Greenwald

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sarah J. Greenwald"}

**Sarah J. Greenwald** is professor of mathematics at [Appalachian State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_State_University) and faculty affiliate of gender, women's and sexuality studies.[^o3]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Greenwald's research interests include [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and the [scholarship of teaching and learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarship_of_teaching_and_learning). She also investigates connections between mathematics and society, such as women, minorities and popular culture. For example, she was part of a team that looked into allusions to mathematics in *[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons).*[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

In 1991, Greenwald graduated summa cum laude with honors in mathematics from [Union College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_College) with a [BS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree.[^o7] In 1998, she earned a PhD in mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania). Her thesis in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) was entitled *Diameters of Spherical Alexandrov Spaces and Constant Curvature One Orbifolds* and was supervised by [Wolfgang Ziller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Ziller)[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Greenwald is a professor of mathematics at [Appalachian State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_State_University) and faculty affiliate of the gender, women's and sexuality studies program there. She has published numerous articles and books in the areas of [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), [math education](wikipedia://en/326471), and math in society.[^o10] Greenwald has been trying to get young women in [Girl Scouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_Scouts_of_the_USA) interested in STEM. She and Appalachian State colleagues Amber Mellon and Jill Thomley have created a merit badge in mathematics to foster interest in mathematics.[^o11][^o12] Greenwald has also created a series of short video interviews of women in STEM who were former Girl Scout members. This is a work in progress. Greenwald was elected in 2020 to serve as Member at Large of the Council of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) during 2021–2024.[^o13]

## Honors {#Honors}

Greenwald was a 2005 winner of the [Henry L. Alder Award](wikipedia://en/65434512) for Distinguished Teaching by a Beginning College or University Mathematics Faculty Member of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA).[^o15] In 2017, Greenwald was selected as a plenary speaker at spring southeast section meeting of the MAA at [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University) in March 2018.[^o16] She received the [AWM](wikipedia://en/9858698) Service Award in 2018 for her work on the executive committee and as associate editor of the AWM newsletter.[^o17] In 2020 she was named a Fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o18] Her citation reads "*For her creative and effective efforts to spark interest in mathematics among young people, especially girls; for her extensive contributions to advancing women in mathematics through writing, lectures and working with the AWM and other professional societies; and for her mentorship of students*". The Mathematical Association of America selected Greenwald as a [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya) Lecturer from 2021 to 2022.[^o19]

## Publications {#Publications}

Greenwald co-edited with [Jill Thomley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jill_Thomley) the 3-volume *Encyclopedia of Mathematics & Society,*[^o21] which was named a "Best Reference of 2011" by *[Library Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_Journal) and* was reviewed extensively.[^o22][^o23] She also co-edited the 2018 Springer volume *Women in Mathematics: Celebrating the Centennial of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).*[^o24] She was part of the cast in 'Futurama': Bite My Shiny Metal X[^o25] and served as a consultant on Flatland: The Movie.[^o26]
