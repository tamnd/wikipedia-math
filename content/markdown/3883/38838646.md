# Non-autonomous system (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Non-autonomous system"}

In mathematics, an [autonomous system](wikipedia://en/312398) is a dynamic equation on a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold). A **non-autonomous system** is a dynamic equation on a smooth [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) $Q\to \mathbb R$ over $\mathbb R$. For instance, this is the case of [non-autonomous mechanics](wikipedia://en/23635894).

:::

An *r*-order differential equation on a fiber bundle $Q\to \mathbb R$ is represented by a closed subbundle of a [jet bundle](wikipedia://en/928060) $J^rQ$ of $Q\to \mathbb R$. A dynamic equation on $Q\to \mathbb R$ is a differential equation which is algebraically solved for a higher-order derivatives.

In particular, a first-order dynamic equation on a fiber bundle $Q\to \mathbb R$ is a kernel of the [covariant differential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariant_differential) of some connection $\Gamma$ on $Q\to \mathbb R$. Given bundle coordinates $(t,q^i)$ on $Q$ and the adapted coordinates $(t,q^i,q^i_t)$ on a first-order jet manifold $J^1Q$, a first-order dynamic equation reads

$$q^i_t=\Gamma (t,q^i).$$

For instance, this is the case of [Hamiltonian non-autonomous mechanics](wikipedia://en/23635894).

A second-order dynamic equation

$$q^i_{tt}=\xi^i(t,q^j,q^j_t)$$

on $Q\to\mathbb R$ is defined as a holonomic connection $\xi$ on a jet bundle $J^1Q\to\mathbb R$. This equation also is represented by a connection on an [affine](wikipedia://en/39358329) jet bundle $J^1Q\to Q$. Due to the canonical embedding $J^1Q\to TQ$, it is equivalent to a geodesic equation on the tangent bundle $TQ$ of $Q$. A [free motion equation](wikipedia://en/38847178) in non-autonomous mechanics exemplifies a second-order non-autonomous dynamic equation.
