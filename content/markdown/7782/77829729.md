# Catherine O'Sullivan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Catherine O'Sullivan"}

**Catherine O'Sullivan** is an Irish environmental engineer who is Professor of Particulate Soil Mechanics and Head of the Geotechnics Section at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London). She uses [discrete element modelling](wikipedia://en/63473) to understand fundamental processes in sand and soils.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

O'Sullivan is from [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland). She was an undergraduate at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork), and started a master's degree in [soil mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil_mechanics). She spent six months working in a geotechnical engineering consultancy.[^o4] She was awarded a scholarship to complete a master's degree in geotechnical engineering completed her doctorate at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where she developed discrete element methods to model granular materials.[^o5] After graduating she moved to [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin), where she spent two years as a College Lecturer.[^o6][^o7]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

In 2004, O'Sullivan joined [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) as a lecturer. She was promoted to Professor in 2017.[^o6] Her research considers the behaviour of granular materials, including soil and sand.[^o7][^o9]

In 2023, O'Sullivan became the first woman to be editor of the *[Journal of Geotechnical & Geoenvironmental Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Geotechnical_&_Geoenvironmental_Engineering)*.[^o10] She is enthusiastic about sustainability, and was involved with the development of a graduate course in geotechnical engineering for offshore renewables.[^o11]
