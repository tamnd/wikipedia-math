# Toric stack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toric stack"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **toric stack** is a [stacky](wikipedia://en/13185596) generalization of a [toric variety](wikipedia://en/737087). More precisely, a toric stack is obtained by replacing in the construction of a toric variety a step of taking [GIT quotients](wikipedia://en/41139312) with that of taking [quotient stacks](wikipedia://en/40579087). Consequently, a toric variety is a coarse approximation of a toric stack. A **toric orbifold** is an example of a toric stack.

:::
