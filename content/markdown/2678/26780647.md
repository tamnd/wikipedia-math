# Shrawan Kumar (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shrawan Kumar"}

*Image: Shrawan Kumar.jpg.* Shrawan Kumar at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2005

**Shrawan Kumar** is the [John R. and Louise S. Parker distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowed_chair) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)[^o2] at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). He has written two books: *Kac-Moody groups, their flag varieties, and representation theory* and *Frobenius splitting methods in geometry and representation theory* (jointly with Michel Brion).

:::

Born and raised in [Ghazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazipur), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), Shrawan Kumar earned a Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) and the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR), Mumbai in 1986. His advisor was [S. Ramanan](wikipedia://en/24022327). Shrawan Kumar is the younger brother of [Gopal Prasad](wikipedia://en/26770296), a professor of mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), and the elder brother of [Dipendra Prasad](wikipedia://en/38106303), a professor of mathematics at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
