# Sharafutdinov's retraction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sharafutdinov's retraction"}

In mathematics, **Sharafutdinov's retraction** is a construction that gives a [retraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_retract) of an open non-negatively curved [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) onto its soul.

:::

It was first used by [Sharafutdinov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Sharafutdinov) to show that any two [souls](wikipedia://en/936932) of a complete Riemannian manifold with non-negative sectional curvature are [isometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometry_%28Riemannian_geometry%29).[^o2] [Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266) later showed that in this setting, Sharafutdinov's retraction is in fact a [submersion](wikipedia://en/356413), thereby essentially settling the [soul conjecture](wikipedia://en/936932).[^o3]

For open non-negatively curved Alexandrov space, Perelman also showed that there exists a Sharafutdinov retraction from the entire space to the soul. However it is not yet known whether this retraction is [submetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submetry) or not.
