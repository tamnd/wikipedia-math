# Multivariate Behavioral Research

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Multivariate Behavioral Research"}

***Multivariate Behavioral Research*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [Taylor & Francis Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis_Group) on behalf of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Peter Molenaar](wikipedia://en/57976933) ([Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University)). Its 2017 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) is 3.691.

:::
