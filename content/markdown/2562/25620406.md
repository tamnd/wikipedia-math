# Leroy Milton Kelly

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leroy Milton Kelly"}

**Leroy Milton Kelly** (May 8, 1914 – February 21, 2002[^o2]) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research primarily concerned [combinatorial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry). In 1986 he settled a conjecture of [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) by proving that n points in complex 3-space, not all lying on a plane, determine an [ordinary line](wikipedia://en/1052632)—that is, a line containing only two of the n points. He taught at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).

:::

Kelly received his Ph.D. at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) in 1948, advised by [Leonard Mascot Blumenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Mascot_Blumenthal).[^o3][^o4]
