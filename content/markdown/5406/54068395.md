# Ferdinando Piretti

*Image: Piretti - Lumi aritmetici, 1725 - 4646851.tif.* *Lumi aritmetici*, 1725

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinando Piretti"}

**Ferdinando Piretti** (17th century – 18th century) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2] He lived at the San Vitale monastery in [Ravenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenna) and later at the San Benedetto monastery in [Ferrara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrara).

:::
