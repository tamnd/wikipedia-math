# Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics"}

The **Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics** (**ICERM**), founded in 2011, is an American research institute in mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), funded since 2010 by a grant from the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).[^o3]

:::

## About {#About}

At the time of its founding, the institute was the eighth of its kind in the nation and the first in [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England).[^o5] It is located in downtown [Providence, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island) in a building it shares with the [Brown University School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University_School_of_Public_Health).[^o6]

The Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics (ICERM), holds numerous events and workshops throughout the year. Workshops range from one day events all the way up to week-long conferences and conventions.[^o7] A notable ICERM workshop was, "Illustrating Mathematics" (2016), which brought mathematicians and digital artist together.[^o8]
