# Agnes Bell Collier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Agnes Bell Collier"}

**Agnes Bell Collier** (31 January 1860 – 2 January 1930) was a British mathematician who was a pioneer female mathematician, associated with [Newnham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

:::

Collier was born in [Hyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde,_Cheshire), [Cheshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheshire). She was educated at [Ellerslie Ladies' College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellerslie_Ladies'_College), Manchester and Newnham College, Cambridge from 1880 to 1883, passing the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) in 1883. She was College Lecturer in Mathematics from 1883 to 1925 and Director of Studies 1883–1920. She was College Vice-Principal 1920–25, and a College Associate from 1893 to 1917.
