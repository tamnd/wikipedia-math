# Truncated order-3 apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-3 apeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-3 apeirogonal tiling** is a [uniform tiling](wikipedia://en/18640974) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) with a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{∞,3}.

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Infinite-order triakis triangular tiling"}

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling, the **infinite-order triakis triangular tiling**, has [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V3.∞.∞.

*Image: Ord-infin triakis triang til.png.*

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This hyperbolic tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (3.2n.2n), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.
