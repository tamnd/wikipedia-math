# Infinite-order triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite-order triangular tiling"}

*Image: H3 33inf UHS plane at infinity.png.* The [{3,3,∞}](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite-order_tetrahedral_honeycomb) honeycomb has {3,∞} vertex figures.

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **infinite-order triangular tiling** is a [regular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hyperbolic_tiling) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) with a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {3,∞}. All vertices are [ideal](wikipedia://en/4995922), located at "infinity" and seen on the boundary of the [Poincaré hyperbolic disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_hyperbolic_disk) projection.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A lower symmetry form has alternating colors, and represented by cyclic symbol {(3,∞,3)}, . The tiling also represents the fundamental domains of the [\*∞∞∞ symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iii_symmetry), which can be seen with 3 colors of lines representing 3 mirrors of the construction.

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is topologically related as part of a sequence of regular polyhedra with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,p}.
