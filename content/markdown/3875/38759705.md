# Truncated infinite-order triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated infinite-order triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated infinite-order triangular tiling** is a [uniform tiling](wikipedia://en/18640974) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) with a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{3,∞}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_infinite-order\_triangular\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated infinite-order triangular tiling with mirror lines, .

The dual of this tiling represents the fundamental domains of \*∞33 symmetry. There are no mirror removal subgroups of \[(∞,3,3)\], but this symmetry group can be doubled to [∞32 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I32_symmetry) by adding a mirror.

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This hyperbolic tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (6.n.n), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.
