# Carmen Batanero

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carmen Batanero"}

**Carmen Batanero** is a Spanish [statistics educator](wikipedia://en/24985094), and a Senior Lecturer in the Mathematics Department at the [University of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Granada), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain).[^o3] She is known as an advocate for statistics education.[^o4] Batanero is a lifetime member of the [International Association for Statistical Education](wikipedia://en/62609633), and served as the association's president from 2001 to 2003.[^o5] She has co-authored many scholarly articles in the fields of mathematics and statistics education over the past two decades, and is credited on two statistics textbooks.[^o6] Her contributions rank her as one of the most influential researchers at her institution.[^o7] Dr. Batanero obtained her doctorate from the [University of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Granada) in 1983, with the thesis paper *Modelos de choque y exposición intermitente a riesgo de fallo*.[^o8]

:::
