# COVID-19 datasets

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="COVID-19 datasets"}

**COVID-19 datasets** are public databases for sharing case data and medical information related to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).

:::

### Global {#Global}

- [Johns Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) Coronavirus Resource Center: Global aggregated data including cases, testing, contact tracing, and vaccine development[^o23]
- [World Health Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Organization) (WHO) Coronavirus Disease Dashboard: a database of confirmed cases and deaths reported globally and broken down by region.[^o24] This database is part of the WHO Health Data Platform.[^o25]
- COVID-19 Africa Open Data Project: a volunteer-run database and dashboard reporting region, country and district level case counts, deaths, healthcare worker infections, healthcare services and urgent needs.[^o26]

## Data hubs {#Data_hubs}

- [Health Data Research UK](https://hdruk.ac.uk) provides a searchable registry of health data resources from the United Kingdom, including [COVID-19 related datasets](https://web.www.healthdatagateway.org/search?search=COVID-19&tab=Datasets).
- NIH Open Access Datasets: The [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) provide open-access data and computational resources related to COVID-19.[^o28]
- COVID-19 Open Research Dataset (CORD-19): The [Semantic Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_Scholar) project of the [Allen Institute for AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Institute_for_AI) hosts CORD-19, a public dataset of academic articles about COVID-19 and related research.[^o29] The dataset is updated daily and includes both peer-reviewed articles and preprints.[^o30] CORD-19 was originally released on March 16, 2020, by researchers and leaders from the Allen Institute for AI, [Chan Zuckerberg Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chan_Zuckerberg_Initiative), [Georgetown University's Center for Security and Emerging Technhology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Security_and_Emerging_Technology), [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft), and the [National Library of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Library_of_Medicine).[^o31] The dataset is created through the use of [text mining](wikipedia://en/318439) of the current research literature.[^o32]

## Topic-specific and special-interest resources {#Topic-specific_and_special-interest_resources}

### Genomics {#Genomics}

- Consensus genome data for [SARS-CoV-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SARS-CoV-2) is available through [GISAID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GISAID) for registered users[^o35] and included in an interactive [Phylogenetic tree](wikipedia://en/149326) dashboard [^o36] on [Nextstrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nextstrain), an open-source pathogen [genome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome) data project.[^o37]

### Imaging (Radiology) {#Imaging_(Radiology)}

- Characteristic imaging features on chest [radiographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiographs) and [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography) (CT) of people who are symptomatic include asymmetric peripheral [ground-glass opacities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground-glass_opacity) without [pleural effusions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleural_effusion).[^o39] The [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal) and [Mila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mila_%28research_institute%29) created the "COVID-19 Image Data Collection" in March which is a public data repository of chest imaging.[^o40][^o41][^o42] The Medical Imaging Databank in Valencian Region released a large dataset of chest imaging from Spain.[^o43][^o44] The [Italian Radiological Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Society_of_Medical_and_Interventional_Radiology) is compiling an international online database of imaging findings for confirmed cases.[^o45] Online radiology case sharing platforms such as [Eurorad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Society_of_Radiology#Eurorad) and [Radiopaedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiopaedia) serve as platforms for sharing COVID-19 case data and imaging.[^o46][^o47]
