# Median triangle

*Image: Median triangle.svg.* Reference triangle: $\triangle ABC$ solid red 2px Median triangle of $\triangle BGF$: $\triangle BKH$ [Similarity](wikipedia://en/39377): $\triangle BGF \sim \triangle BKH$

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Median triangle"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **median triangle** of a given (reference) triangle is a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654), the sides of which are equal and parallel to the [medians](wikipedia://en/1072006) of its reference triangle. The [area](wikipedia://en/9194858) of the median triangle is 3/4 of the area of its reference triangle:

:::

$$| \triangle BGF| = \frac{3}{4} |\triangle ABC|$$

The median triangle of the median triangle is similar to the reference triangle of the first median triangle with a [scaling factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scaling_factor) of 3/4:[^o2][^o3][^o4]

$$\frac{|BH|}{|BC|} = \frac{|HK|}{|AB|} = \frac{|BK|}{|AC|} = \frac{3}{4}$$
