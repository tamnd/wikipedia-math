# Imperfect induction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Imperfect induction"}

**Imperfect induction** is the process of inferring from a [sample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_%28statistics%29) of a group to what is characteristic of the whole group.[^o2][^o3]

:::
