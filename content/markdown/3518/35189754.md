# Serre–Tate theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serre–Tate theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Serre–Tate theorem** says that an [abelian scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_scheme) and its [p-divisible group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-divisible_group) have the same infinitesimal [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_Theory). This was first proved by [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) when the reduction of the abelian variety is ordinary, using the Greenberg functor; then [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933) gave a proof in the general case by a different method. Their proofs were not published, but they were summarized in the notes of the Lubin–Serre–Tate seminar (Woods Hole, 1964). Other proofs were published by [Messing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Messing) (1962) and [Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747) (1976).

:::
