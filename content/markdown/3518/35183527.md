# Normal degree

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normal degree"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **normal degree** of a rational curve *C* on a surface is defined to be –*K*.*C*–2 where *K* is the [canonical divisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_divisor) of the surface.

:::
