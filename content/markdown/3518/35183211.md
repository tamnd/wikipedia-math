# Signature defect

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Signature defect"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **signature defect** of a singularity measures the correction that a singularity contributes to the signature theorem. (Hirzebruch 1973) introduced the signature defect for the cusp singularities of [Hilbert modular surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_modular_surface). (txt, 131–177) defined the signature defect of the boundary of a manifold as the [eta invariant](wikipedia://en/35063240), the value as *s* = 0 of their eta function, and used this to show that Hirzebruch's signature defect of a cusp of a Hilbert modular surface can be expressed in terms of the value at *s* = 0 or 1 of a [Shimizu L-function](wikipedia://en/35100193).

:::
