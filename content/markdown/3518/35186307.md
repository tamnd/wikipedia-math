# Deuring–Heilbronn phenomenon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deuring–Heilbronn phenomenon"}

In mathematics, the **Deuring–Heilbronn phenomenon**, discovered by (txt) and (txt), states that a counterexample to the [generalized Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/152567) for one [Dirichlet L-function](wikipedia://en/400092) affects the location of the zeros of other Dirichlet L-functions.

:::
