# Anadi Sankar Gupta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anadi Sankar Gupta"}

**Anadi Sankar Gupta** (1 November 1932, [Barisal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barisal) – 14 June 2012, [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata)) was an Indian mathematician.[^o3] Till his death, he was an [INSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) Senior Scientist and [emeritus faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) with the Department of Mathematics, [IIT Kharagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Kharagpur).[^o4]

:::

He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) for [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) in 1972 by the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) for his "significant contributions in the field of [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310), notably on [heat transfer](wikipedia://en/184726) in [free convection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_convection) flow in the presence of [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563)," amongst other things.[^o5]

## Life {#Life}

Anadi Sankar Gupta was born to Pramode Chandra and Usharani Gupta in Goila of [Barisal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barisal_District) in the then [British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj) (present [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh)). He had his initial schooling at Domohani Kelejora High School in [Asansol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asansol), West Bengal. Gupta completed his [bachelors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) from [Presidency College, Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_University,_Kolkata) in 1952 and followed it up with a [master's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) in 1954.[^o7]

Prof. Gupta died in [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata) on 14 June 2012 after suddenly taking ill.[^o3]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Gupta's [doctoral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) thesis was done under the supervision of G. Bandyopadhyay at the [Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Kharagpur) in 1958. He further acquired a [D. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) from the same institute in 1966.

He joined the faculty of IIT Kharagpur in 1957 as an associate lecturer and rose through the ranks to become a professor and subsequently, an emeritus. He was President of the annual Congress of the [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) in 1999.[^o3] He also served on the editorial boards of various scientific journals.[^o7] He retired in 1993, but continued to be active in research.[^o3]

### Research areas {#Research_areas}

- [Boundary layer theory](wikipedia://en/224301)
- [Heat](wikipedia://en/184726) and [mass transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_transfer) in fluid flows
- [Hydrodynamic stability](wikipedia://en/21757046)
- Stability of flows
