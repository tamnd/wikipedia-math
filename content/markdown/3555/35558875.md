# National Mathematics Year

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Mathematics Year"}

*Image: Srinivasa Ramanujan-Add. MS a94 version2 (cropped).jpg.* [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717), whom the year honored.

In [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) and in [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria) the year 2012 [CE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) was celebrated as **National Mathematics Year**. In India, the National Mathematics Year was a tribute to the mathematical genius [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) who was born on 22 December 1887 and whose 125th birthday falls on 22 December 2012.[^o2][^o3] In Nigeria, the year 2012 was observed as National Mathematics Year as part of the federal government's effort to promote and popularize the study of mathematics.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## National Mathematics Year in India {#National_Mathematics_Year_in_India}

The decision to designate the year 2012 CE as National Mathematics Year was announced by [Dr Manmohan Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr_Manmohan_Singh), [Prime Minister of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_India) , during the inaugural ceremony of the celebrations to mark the 125th [birth anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_anniversary) of Srinivasa Ramanujan held at the [Madras University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_University) Centenary Auditorium on 26 February 2012. The Prime Minister also announced that December 22 would be celebrated as **[National Mathematics Day](wikipedia://en/35599234)** from 2012 onwards.

An Organising Committee with Professor M.S. Raghunathan, President of the Ramanujan Mathematical Society as chair, and Professor [Dinesh Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinesh_Singh_%28academic%29), Secretary of the [Ramanujan Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_Mathematical_Society) as secretary, has been formed to formulate and implement programmes and projects as part of the observance of the National Mathematics Year. A National Committee with Minister for [Kapil Sibal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapil_Sibal) as the chair supervises the activities of the Organising Committee.[^o7]

## National Mathematics Year in Nigeria {#National_Mathematics_Year_in_Nigeria}

In Nigeria, the various activities planned as part of the celebration of National Mathematics Year would be centred on the theme *Mathematics: The Key to Transformation*. The events were inaugurated on 1 March 2012 at a function in [Musa Yar’adua Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musa_Yar’adua_Dome), [Abuja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abuja). Thirteen projects of national importance are planned as part of the celebrations.
