# Al-Akhdari

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Al-Akhdari"}

**Sayyidi ʻAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Muḥammad al-Ṣaghīr ibn Muḥammad ibn Sayyidi ʿĀmir al-Akẖḍarī al-Bīsīkrī** (سيدي عبد الرحمن بن محمد الصغير بن محمد بن سيدي عمرو الأخضري; 1512–1575), better known as **Al-Akẖḍari** (الأخضري), was an [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabs) [poet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poet), [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065), [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) and [Maliki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maliki_school) [jurist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurist).

:::

He was the author of the highly popular didactic poem "*Al-Sullam al-murawnaq fī ʻilm al-manṭiq* ("The Ornamented Ladder into the Science of Logic"). The 144-line poem, a versification of [Al-Abhari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athīr_al-Dīn_al-Abharī)'s *Kitab al-Isaghuji*, outlines the principles of [Islamic logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_logic) and explains how logic could be used to support the Islamic creed (['aqidah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/'aqidah)) and jurisprudence ([fiqh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiqh)). The work is studied across the Muslim world as a primer on logic[^o3] and is often read in conjunction with al-Akhdari's own prose commentary.

He is also known to have written another work, "*al-Jawhar ul-Maknun* or "*Al-Jawahir al-Maknuna fi'l-ma'ni wa'l-bayan wa'l-badi*.

## Origin {#Origin}

Al-Akẖḍarī was born in [Biskra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biskra) in present-day [Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algeria), plus an account in an [Sherifian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif) [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabs) family (noble descendant) of the Arab tribe *Banu al-Akhdari* (بنو الأخضري) the region of [Ibb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibb) in [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen) present in Algeria since the 680s, best known in Morocco, Algeria and [Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libya) as *Kābilāt Al-Akḥdārīyyāh* (قبيلة الأخضرية).

## Works {#Works}

Al-Akhdari wrote many works on several sciences including intellectual, Shari'a, fiqh, linguistics, mathematics, and astronomy.

- *Al-Sullam al-munawraq fī ʻilm al-manṭiq* ("The Ornamented Ladder into the Science of Logic"; السلم المُنَوْرَقِ في علم المنطق): a 144-line poem on [Islamic logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_logic). This work was translated by the French orientalist Lucien in 1921 CE and he considered it one of the greatest international books.
- «مختصر الأخضري في العبادات» على مذهب الإمام مالك
- «نظم الجوهر المكنون في ثلاثة فنون: في علم البلاغة والبيان والبديع»
- «حلية اللب المصون على الجواهر المكنون»
- «منظومة الدرة البيضاء في الفرائض والحساب»
- «منظومة الدرة البيضاء في أحسن الفنون والأشياء»
- «نظم السلم المرونق في المنطق»
- «شرح السلم المرونق المذكور»
- «نظم السراج في علم الفلك»
- «نظم منثور ابن آجروم الدرر البهية على نظم الاجرومية»
- «نظم أزهر المطالب في هيئة الأفلاك والكواكب في علم الاسطرلاب»
- «قصيدة مدح النبي خالد بن سنان»
- «شعر القدسية واللامية في التصوف»
