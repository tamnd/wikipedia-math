# Andrew R. Barron (statistician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew R. Barron"}

**Andrew R. Barron** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [information theorist](wikipedia://en/14773). He was the Charles C. and Dorothea S. Dilley Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_Science) at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) until his retirement in 2024.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Barron received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Electrical Engineering and Mathematical Sciences from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 1981, and a [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (1982) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (1985) in Electrical Engineering from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where his doctoral advisor was [Thomas M. Cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_M._Cover).[^o5]

After completing his doctorate, Barron joined the faculty at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), where he held appointments in the departments of Statistics and Electrical and Computer Engineering.[^o3] In 1992, he moved to [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University),[^o6] where he served as Chair of the Department of Statistics from 2001 to 2006.[^o3]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Barron is a [FAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fédération_Aéronautique_Internationale) [free flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_flight_%28model_aircraft%29) model glider competitor in the F1A class. He is a five-time U.S. National Champion, winning in 1984, 1987, 1992, 2007, and 2009.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [Claude E. Shannon Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_E._Shannon_Award), [IEEE Information Theory Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Information_Theory_Society) (2024)[^o10]
- Medallion Lecture, [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2005)[^o11]
- Browder J. Thompson Memorial Prize, [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) (1992)[^o12]
- [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow)[^o3]
