# Coordinate-induced basis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coordinate-induced basis"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **coordinate-induced basis** is a basis for the [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) or [cotangent space](wikipedia://en/6620) of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) that is induced by a certain coordinate system. Given the coordinate system $x^a$, the coordinate-induced basis $e_a$ of the tangent space is given by

:::

$$e_a = \frac{\partial}{\partial x^a}$$

and the dual basis $\omega^a$ of the cotangent space is

$$\omega^a=dx^a. \,$$
