# Halo mass function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Halo mass function"}

In [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology), the **halo mass function** is a mass distribution of [dark matter halos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_matter_halo). Specifically, it gives the number density of [dark matter halos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_matter_halo) per mass interval.[^o2]

:::
