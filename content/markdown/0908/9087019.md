# Inverse Symbolic Calculator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Inverse Symbolic Calculator"}

The **Inverse Symbolic Calculator** is an online [number checker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_checker) established July 18, 1995 by [Peter Benjamin Borwein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Benjamin_Borwein), [Jonathan Michael Borwein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Michael_Borwein) and [Simon Plouffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Plouffe) of the Canadian Centre for Experimental and Constructive Mathematics (Burnaby, Canada). A user will input a number and the Calculator will use an algorithm to search for and calculate [closed-form expressions](wikipedia://en/585143) or suitable functions that have roots near this number. Hence, the calculator is of great importance for those working in numerical areas of [experimental mathematics](wikipedia://en/256363).

:::

The ISC contains 54 million mathematical constants. [Plouffe's Inverter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plouffe's_Inverter) (opened in 1998) contains 214 million. A newer version of the tables with 3.702 billion entries (as of June 19, 2010) exists.

In 2016, Plouffe released a portable version of Plouffe's Inverter containing 3 billion entries.[^o3]
