# Gaussian isoperimetric inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaussian isoperimetric inequality"}

In mathematics, the **Gaussian isoperimetric inequality**, proved by [Boris Tsirelson](wikipedia://en/1661441) and [Vladimir Sudakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Sudakov),[^o2] and later independently by [Christer Borell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christer_Borell),[^o3] states that among all sets of given [Gaussian measure](wikipedia://en/6572909) in the *n*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697), [half-spaces](wikipedia://en/647297) have the minimal Gaussian [boundary measure](wikipedia://en/15667957).

:::

## Mathematical formulation {#Mathematical_formulation}

Let $\scriptstyle A$ be a [measurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurable) subset of $\scriptstyle\mathbf{R}^n$ endowed with the standard Gaussian measure $\gamma^n$ with the density ${\exp(-\|x\|^2/2)}/(2\pi)^{n/2}$. Denote by

$$A_\varepsilon = \left\{ x \in \mathbf{R}^n \, | \, \text{dist}(x, A) \leq \varepsilon \right\}$$

the ε-extension of *A*. Then the *Gaussian isoperimetric inequality* states that

$$\liminf_{\varepsilon \to +0} \varepsilon^{-1} \left\{ \gamma^n (A_\varepsilon) - \gamma^n(A) \right\} \geq \varphi(\Phi^{-1}(\gamma^n(A))),$$

where

$$\varphi(t) = \frac{\exp(-t^2/2)}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\quad{\rm and}\quad\Phi(t) = \int_{-\infty}^t \varphi(s)\, ds.$$

::: {#o6 .proof cue=heading name="Proofs and generalizations"}

## Proofs and generalizations {#Proofs_and_generalizations}

The original proofs by Sudakov, Tsirelson and Borell were based on [Paul Lévy](wikipedia://en/556462)'s [spherical isoperimetric inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_isoperimetric_inequality).

[Sergey Bobkov](wikipedia://en/42666668) proved [Bobkov's inequality](wikipedia://en/73966770), a functional generalization of the Gaussian isoperimetric inequality, proved from a certain "two point analytic inequality".[^o7] Bakry and Ledoux gave another proof of Bobkov's functional inequality based on the [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) techniques which works in a much more abstract setting.[^o8] Later Barthe and Maurey gave yet another proof using the [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436).[^o9]

The Gaussian isoperimetric inequality also follows from [Ehrhard's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrhard's_inequality).[^o10][^o11]
