# Antonella Zanna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonella Zanna"}

**Antonella Munthe-Kaas Zanna** is an Italian applied mathematician and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) whose research includes work on [numerical integration](wikipedia://en/170089) of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and applications to [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging). She is a professor and head of the mathematics department at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) in Norway.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Zanna was born in [Molfetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molfetta), in southern Italy, and earned a degree in mathematics from the [University of Bari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bari). She completed her PhD in the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics](wikipedia://en/3254271#Applied_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Physics) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1998. Her dissertation, *Numerical Solution of Isospectral Flows*, was supervised by [Arieh Iserles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arieh_Iserles).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Zanna won the Second Prize in the [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214) in 1997. She is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Technological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Technological_Sciences).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Zanna married to Norwegian mathematician [Hans Munthe-Kaas](wikipedia://en/53481563) in 1997; they have four children.
