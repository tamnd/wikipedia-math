# Caleb Andrew Stewart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caleb Andrew Stewart"}

**Caleb Andrew Stewart** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (16 September 1888 – 5 August 1959) was a 20th-century Scottish mathematician. He is noted as a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 16 September 1888 in [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow) the son of John Stewart, a patternmaker. He attended [Barrowfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrowfield) Primary School then John Street Higher Grade School, completing his education at the [High School of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_School_of_Glasgow). He then studied Mathematics and Physics at [Glasgow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_University) graduating MA in 1909, BSc in 1910. Winning a Ferguson Scholarship he attended [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) gaining a further BA in 1912. He then began lecturing at the City and Guilds College in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).[^o3]

In the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War) he served as a 2nd Lieutenant in the 8th [Wiltshire Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiltshire_Regiment), serving in France and [Salonika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salonika), before transferring to the [Royal Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Engineers) as a full Lieutenant. After the war he began lecturing at [Sheffield University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_University). Glasgow University awarded him an honorary doctorate (DSc) in 1928.[^o3]

In 1950 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [Robert Pollock Gillespie](wikipedia://en/51040007), [Richard Robb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Robb), [Alexander Aitken](wikipedia://en/3348690) and [Daniel Edwin Rutherford](wikipedia://en/41979802).[^o4]

He died in [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol) on 5 August 1959.
