# Thomson cubic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomson cubic"}

*Image: Thomson cubic.svg.* The Thomson cubic (black curve) contains the point *X* whose isogonal conjugate (*X'*) is on the line *X*(2)-*X*.

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Thomson cubic** of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) is the [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) of centers of [circumconics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumconic) whose normals at the [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) are [concurrent](wikipedia://en/2206157).

:::
