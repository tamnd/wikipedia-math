# Yau's conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Yau's conjecture"}

**Yau's conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yau's conjecture"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Yau's conjecture** is a [mathematical conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_conjecture) which states that any closed [Riemannian](wikipedia://en/144652) [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) contains infinitely many [smooth](wikipedia://en/2119219#Definition) [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) [immersed](wikipedia://en/6993953#Immersed_plane_curves) [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734). It is named after [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137), who posed it as the 88th entry in his 1982 list of open problems in differential geometry.[^o3]

:::

The conjecture was first resolved by [Fernando Codá Marques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Codá_Marques) and [André Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André_Neves) in the case of positive [Ricci curvature](wikipedia://en/276582). Then, for the case of [generic](wikipedia://en/12886758) metrics, there are independent solutions from Kei Irie, [Fernando Codá Marques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Codá_Marques) and [André Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André_Neves)[^o4], Otis Chodosh and Christos Mantoulidis[^o5], and also Xin Zhou[^o6]. Finally, [Antoine Song](wikipedia://en/64457708) proved the conjectures for all metrics.[^o7] The proofs above use [min-max theory](wikipedia://en/46587298), which is a form of infinite dimensional [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560) for the area functional. A proof for the generic metric case via gluing construction was also later found by Adrian Chun-Pong Chu and Daniel Stern[^o8], in which the minimal surfaces constructed have bounded area but unbounded genus.
