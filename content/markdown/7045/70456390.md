# Mark de Cataldo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mark de Cataldo"}

*Image: Mark A. de Cataldo Oberwolfach 2017.jpg.* De Cataldo at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2017

**Mark Andrea de Cataldo** is an Italian mathematician.

:::

De Cataldo earned a doctorate from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in 1995, and began teaching at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in 1998–1999, after completing postdoctoral research at the [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960), and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o2][^o3] He is a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and has been awarded the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) fellowship.[^o4][^o5] In 2019, de Cataldo was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
