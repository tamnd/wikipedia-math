# Davar Khoshnevisan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Davar Khoshnevisan"}

**Davar Khoshnevisan** (born 21 July 1964) is an American mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Khoshnevisan completed bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematical sciences at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) and a doctorate at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), advised by Warry Millar.[^o4][^o5] Khoshnevisan began his teaching career at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). After one year, he joined the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) faculty. In 1993, Khoshnevisan moved to the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah).[^o4][^o6] Khoshnevisan was elected a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2015,[^o7] and awarded an equivalent honor by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2020.[^o6]
