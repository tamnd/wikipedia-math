# Lek-Heng Lim

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lek-Heng Lim"}

**Lek-Heng Lim** ([^o3]) is a Singaporean mathematician.

:::

Lim earned a bachelor's degree at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880), studied for his master's at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and completed a doctorate at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o2] Lim started his teaching career at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley),[^o3] where he served as Charles Morrey Assistant Professor.[^o2] He later joined the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) faculty.[^o4][^o5] While affiliated with Chicago, Lim won the [James H. Wilkinson Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Wilkinson_Prize_in_Numerical_Analysis_and_Scientific_Computing)[^o6][^o7] and Stephen Smale Prize in 2017,[^o8] followed by the 2020 [Hans Schneider Prize in Linear Algebra](wikipedia://en/58527033).[^o9] In 2020, Lim was also elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]

Lim was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in April 2022.[^o11][^o12]
