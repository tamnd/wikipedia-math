# David Conlon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Conlon"}

**David Conlon** (born 1982) is an Irish mathematician who is a Professor of Mathematics at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). His research interests are in Hungarian-style [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), particularly [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568), [combinatorial number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_number_theory), and probabilistic methods in combinatorics.[^o3] He proved the first superpolynomial improvement on the Erdős–Szekeres bound on diagonal [Ramsey numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_number). He won the [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics) in 2011 for his work in Ramsey theory and for his progress on [Sidorenko's conjecture](wikipedia://en/62480458), and the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2019.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Conlon represented Ireland in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1998 and 1999.[^o5] He was an undergraduate in [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin), where he was [elected a Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scholars_of_Trinity_College_Dublin) in 2001[^o6] and graduated in 2003. He earned a PhD from [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in 2009.

In 2019 he moved to California Institute of Technology, having been a fellow of [Wadham College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College,_Oxford) and Professor of Discrete Mathematics in the Mathematics Institute at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o8]

Conlon has worked in [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), and he proved the first superpolynomial improvement on the [Erdős–Szekeres bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Szekeres_bound) on diagonal [Ramsey numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_number).[^o9] He won the [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics) in 2011, for his work in Ramsey theory and for his progress on [Sidorenko's conjecture](wikipedia://en/62480458) that, for any [bipartite graph](wikipedia://en/244431) *H*, uniformly random graphons have the fewest [subgraphs isomorphic to *H*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgraph_isomorphism) when the edge density is fixed.[^o10] He was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2019 "in recognition of his many contributions to combinatorics".[^o11]

From 2015 to 2020, Conlon was the Editor-in-Chief of the [Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics).[^o8]
