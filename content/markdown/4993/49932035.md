# Martin Scharlemann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Scharlemann"}

**Martin George Scharlemann** (born 6 December 1948) is an American [topologist](wikipedia://en/29954) who is a professor at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara).[^o2] He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the guidance of [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424) in 1974.[^o3]

:::

A conference in his honor was held in 2009 at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).[^o4] He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for his "contributions to low-dimensional topology and knot theory."[^o5]

[Abigail Thompson](wikipedia://en/44841077) was a student of his.[^o3] Together they solved the [graph planarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_planarity) problem: There is an algorithm to decide whether a finite graph in 3-space can be moved in 3-space into a plane.[^o6]

He gave the first [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of the classical theorem that [knots](wikipedia://en/366808) with [unknotting number](wikipedia://en/26711383) one are [prime](wikipedia://en/315030). He used hard combinatorial arguments for this. Simpler proofs are now known.[^o7][^o8]
