# Antony Wassermann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antony Wassermann"}

**Antony John Wassermann** (born 1957) is a British mathematician working in [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras). He is known for his works on [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774) (providing several series of [subfactors](wikipedia://en/2780098)), the actions of [compact groups](wikipedia://en/750326) on [von Neumann algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebras), and his proof of the [Baum–Connes conjecture](wikipedia://en/11774498) for connected [reductive linear Lie groups](wikipedia://en/1088386).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wassermann was born in 1957.[^o10] He is the son of the quantum physicist Gerhard Dietrich Wassermann and the brother of the mathematician Alexander Simon Wassermann.[^o6] He was educated at the [Royal Grammar School, Newcastle upon Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Grammar_School,_Newcastle_upon_Tyne), and [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge),[^o7] before going on to earn his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1981 under the supervision of [Jonathan Rosenberg](wikipedia://en/41108807). His doctoral dissertation was titled, "Automorphic actions of compact groups on operator algebras".[^o9] Afterwards, he was *Directeur de Recherches* of the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) at [Aix-Marseille University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University) from 1999 to 2013.[^o10]

## Honours {#Honours}

- Bronze medal, [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), 1974.[^o12]
- [Miller Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Research_Fellows) recipient in 1986–88.[^o13]
- Winner of the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 1990.[^o14][^o3]
- Invited speaker, [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians), 1994, Zürich.[^o15]
