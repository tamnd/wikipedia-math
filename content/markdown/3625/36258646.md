# Gérald Tenenbaum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gérald Tenenbaum"}

*Image: Gérald-Tenenbaum2004.jpg.* Gérald Tenenbaum, 10 July 2004.

**Gérald Tenenbaum** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [novelist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novelist), born in [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France) on 1 April 1952.[^o2]

:::

He is one of the namesakes of the [Erdős–Tenenbaum–Ford constant](wikipedia://en/56804318).[^o3]

## Biography {#Biography}

An alumnus of the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), he has been professor of mathematics at the [Institut Élie Cartan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Élie_Cartan) at [Université de Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Lorraine) (formally université Henri Poincaré, Nancy-1) since 1981.

An associate of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and specialist in [analytic](wikipedia://en/251513) and [probabilistic number theory](wikipedia://en/3644492), Gérald Tenenbaum received the A-X Gaston Julia prize in 1976, the [Albert Châtelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Châtelet) medal in algebra and number theory in 1985 and, together with [Michel Mendès France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Mendès_France), the [Paul Doistau - Émile Blutet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Doistau_-_Émile_Blutet) prize from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1999 [^o5]

While continuing his mathematical research activities, he started publishing literary works from the 1980s on: movie criticism in the Belgian magazine *Regards*, a theater play in 1999, and novels from 2002 on. His novel *L'Ordre des jours*, published in 2008 by Héloïse d'Ormesson, received the [Prix Erckmann-Chatrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Erckmann-Chatrian) the same year.
