# Quantum topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum topology"}

**Quantum topology** is a branch of mathematics that connects [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) with [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).

:::

[Dirac notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_notation) provides a viewpoint of quantum mechanics which becomes amplified into a framework that can embrace the amplitudes associated with [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) and the related embedding of one space within another such as [knots](wikipedia://en/366808) and [links](wikipedia://en/1161784) in three-dimensional space. This [bra–ket notation](wikipedia://en/4542) of kets and bras can be generalised, becoming maps of [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) associated with [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) that allow [tensor products](wikipedia://en/30844).[^o2]

Topological [entanglement](wikipedia://en/30858815) involving [linking](wikipedia://en/1161784) and [braiding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braid_theory) can be intuitively related to [quantum entanglement](wikipedia://en/25336).[^o2]
