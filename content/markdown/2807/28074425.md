# Bollobás–Riordan polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bollobás–Riordan polynomial"}

The **Bollobás–Riordan polynomial** can mean a 3-[variable](wikipedia://en/3728109) [invariant polynomial](wikipedia://en/1798843) of graphs on [orientable surfaces](wikipedia://en/187446#Orientable_surfaces), or a more general 4-variable invariant of [ribbon graphs](wikipedia://en/56039802), generalizing the [Tutte polynomial](wikipedia://en/3892303).

:::

## History {#History}

These polynomials were discovered by (txt).[^o4][^o5]

::: {#o7 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

The 3-variable Bollobás–Riordan polynomial of a graph $G$ is given by

$$R_G(x,y,z) =\sum_F x^{r(G)-r(F)}y^{n(F)}z^{k(F)-bc(F)+n(F)},$$

where the sum runs over all the [spanning subgraphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_graph_theory_terms#S) $F$ and

- $v(G)$ is the number of vertices of $G$;
- $e(G)$ is the number of its edges of $G$;
- $k(G)$ is the number of components of $G$;
- $r(G)$ is the rank of $G$, such that $r(G) = v(G)- k(G)$;
- $n(G)$ is the nullity of $G$, such that $n(G) = e(G)-r(G)$;
- $bc(G)$ is the number of connected components of the boundary of $G$.

:::
