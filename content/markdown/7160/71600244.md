# Ciphr

Companies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ciphr"}

**Ciphr** is a secure messaging company that produces [encrypted phones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encrypted_phone).

:::

In September 2021, after the closure of [AN0M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Trojan_Shield), Ciphr closed its operations in Australia.[^o2] A source told Vice magazine that the company had stopped operations in America, but provided no evidence.

The company provided the following apps running on Android software:

::: {#o3 .table cue=table}

| App | Description |
|---|---|
| Ciphr Text | Instant-messaging app, encrypted end-to-end[^o2] |
| Ciphr Mail | Email app[^o2] |
| Ciphr Vault | Encrypted storage on device[^o2] |

:::

The company was used heavily by criminal groups, in particular those who smuggled large amounts of illegal narcotics.[^o2]

A former distributor of [Phantom Secure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_Secure) phones said of a meeting with Ciphr representatives: "I felt like I was in a mafia-esque situation during that meeting".[^o6][^o4]

In 2022, it changed its reseller business model.[^o6] The move was part of the attempt to clean-up its client base after a series law enforcement actions against similar companies that cater to the black market.[^o6] The new model shifted responsibility to resellers of its encryption technology.[^o6] The company later announced that it will cease operations after resellers refused to sell Ciphr's products due to the assumed legal liability.[^o5][^o6]

In 2023 OnyxCorp, the company behind the Ciphr app, announced the beta-test of an app called Mode, intended for enterprise applications.[^o7]
