# Lamp cord trick

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lamp cord trick"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of mathematics, and specifically [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **lamp cord trick** is an observation that two certain spaces are [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic), even if one of the components is knotted. The spaces are $M^3\backslash T_i,i=1,2$, where $M^3$ is a hollow ball homeomorphic to $S^2\times[0,1]$ and $T_i$ a tube connecting the boundary components of $M^3$. The name comes from R. H. Bing's book "The Geometric Topology of 3-manifolds".[^o2]

:::
