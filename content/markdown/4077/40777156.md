# Commutative ring spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Commutative ring spectrum"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a **commutative ring spectrum**, roughly equivalent to a [$E_\infty$-ring spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-infinity_ring_spectrum), is a [commutative monoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_monoid) in a good[^o2] category of [spectra](wikipedia://en/1033697).

:::

The category of commutative ring spectra over the field $\mathbb{Q}$ of rational numbers is [Quillen equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quillen_equivalent) to the category of [differential graded algebras](wikipedia://en/2415863) over $\mathbb{Q}$.

Example: The [Witten genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witten_genus) may be realized as a [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) of commutative ring spectra [MString](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MString) →**[tmf](wikipedia://en/10274436)**.

See also: [simplicial commutative ring](wikipedia://en/40782000), [highly structured ring spectrum](wikipedia://en/27342975) and [derived scheme](wikipedia://en/40776948).

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Terminology"}

## Terminology {#Terminology}

Almost all reasonable categories of commutative ring spectra can be shown to be [Quillen equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quillen_equivalent) to each other. Thus, from the point view of the [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), the term "commutative ring spectrum" may be used as a synonymous to an $E_\infty$-ring spectrum.

:::
