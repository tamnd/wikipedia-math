# Richard Beals (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Beals"}

**Richard William Beals** (28 May 1938, [Erie, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie,_Pennsylvania))[^o3] is an American mathematician who works on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). He is known as the author or co-author of several mathematical textbooks.

:::

Beals studied at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) earning a B.A. in 1960, an M.A. in 1962, and a Ph.D. in 1964 under [Felix Browder](wikipedia://en/10201445) with thesis *Non-Local Boundary Value Problems for Elliptic Partial Differential Operators*. In the academic year 1965/1966 he was a visiting assistant professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he became in 1966 an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) and later a professor. In 1977 he became a professor at Yale University.[^o5]

Beals received the [Quantrell Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantrell_Award).[^o6]

Beals works on [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956) in [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory), [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213), [pseudodifferential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudodifferential_operator), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [global analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474) and [transport theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_phenomena). He has been married since 1962 and has three children.

He should not be confused with the mathematics professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) named R. Michael Beals (born in 1954), who is Richard Beals's brother.
