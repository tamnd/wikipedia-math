# Tong Yang (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tong Yang"}

**Tong Yang** (杨彤) is a Chinese mathematician known for his contributions to the field of partial differential equations and kinetic theory.[^o25] He has been serving as a Chair Professor of Mathematical Science in the Department of Applied Mathematics at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hong_Kong_Polytechnic_University) (PolyU)[^o26][^o27] since 2020. Previously, he held the position of President of The Hong Kong Mathematical Society from 2016 to 2020[^o28] in 2016-2020.[^o29][^o30]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Yang completed his Ph.D. in 1993 at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) in USA,[^o32] after earning his bachelor's and master's degrees from [Zhongshan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongshan_University) in 1987 and 1990,[^o33] respectively.

## Research {#Research}

Yang's research focuses on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [kinetic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_theory).[^o35] In collaboration with [Tai-Ping Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai-Ping_Liu), he introduced the Liu-Yang functional, which is used to evaluate the stability of BV solutions in systems of hyperbolic [conservation laws](wikipedia://en/6956).[^o36][^o37] This work addressed significant challenges in the well-posedness theory for conservation laws and has applications in various fields.[^o38][^o39]

Additionally, Yang and his collaborators developed a new macro-micro decomposition for the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522) to study its fluid phenomena,[^o40][^o41] facilitating the application of analytical techniques used in conservation laws.

## Awards {#Awards}

Yang has been recognized as one of the World's Top 2% most-cited scientists by [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 2020 to 2024.[^o43] He received the [Morningside Silver Medal of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/39615068) "for his contributions to the well-posedness theory for hyperbolic conservation laws".[^o4] In 2011/12, he was awarded The Croucher Senior Research Fellowship for his work on mathematical theories related to the Boltzmann equation [^o7] He also received the second-class award of 2012 [State Natural Science Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Natural_Science_Award) in China for his project on "conversation laws and the Boltzmann equation"[^o9][^o10]

In 2019, Yang earned the first-class award in Natural Sciences of the Higher Education Outstanding Scientific Research Output Award (Science and Technology) by [the Ministry of Education of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_%28China%29) for his joint work on well-posedness of the Prandtl equation in Sobolev spaces.[^o44][^o12][^o45] He received another first-class award in 2022 [^o19][^o20] for his work on mathematical theory of compressible Navier-Stokes equations and related models.[^o46][^o47] In 2020/2021, he was awarded the RGC Senior Research Fellowship by the [University Grants Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Grants_Committee_%28Hong_Kong%29) for his project "Some Mathematical Theories for Kinetic Systems".[^o13][^o14]

Yang has been elected to various prestigious memberships and fellowships. In 2018, he became a Foreign Fellow of the European Academy of Sciences for his contributions to science and technology.[^o48] He was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his work on hyperbolic conservation laws and kinetic equations.[^o15][^o49] In 2021, he was elected as a member of the World Academy of Sciences for his fundamental contributions to hyperbolic conservation laws, kinetic theories, boundary layer theories, and high [Reynolds number](wikipedia://en/23868856) limit.[^o16][^o17] He became a member of Academia Europaea in 2022[^o21][^o22]
