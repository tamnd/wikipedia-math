# Andrzej Nowak (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Nowak"}

**Andrzej Nowak** (born 29 October 1952 in [Żagań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Żagań))[^o3] is a Polish mathematician known for his contributions to [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [theory](wikipedia://en/30746) of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and finance.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1977, he graduated with a degree in mathematics from the Faculty of Fundamental Problems of Technology of [Wrocław University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław). He was a PhD student at the Institute of Mathematics at the Wroclaw University of Technology from 1977 to 1981, where he obtained his doctorate in 1981 based on the dissertation *On zero sum stochastic games with general state space,* written under the supervision of [Rastislav Telgarsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rastislav_Telgarsky).[^o7][^o8][^o9] In 1993, he defended the dissertation at Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, [Wrocław University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University), based on the work *Optimal and equilibrium strategies in stochastic games.*[^o10]

After earning his doctoral degree in mathematics on 24 June 1981, he was employed as an assistant professor at the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science at Wrocław University of Technology until 30 September 1999. After that, he moved to the Faculty of Mathematics at the [University of Zielona Góra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zielona_Góra), where he has been affiliated with the Faculty of Mathematics, heading the Division of Mathematical Economics and Optimization from 2008 to 2016. In 2004, he was appointed professor of mathematics.[^o11][^o12] He also served as the Head of Department of Finance at Faculty of Economic Sciences of [University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Applied_Sciences) in [Nysa, Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nysa,_Poland).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

His research articles can be found in [Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748), [Mathematics of Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Operations_Research), [SIAM Journal of Control and Optimization](wikipedia://en/1883376#Publications), [Games and Economic Behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Games_and_Economic_Behavior), [Journal of Economic Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Economic_Theory), [Automatica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatica). References to his papers can be found in mathematical databases.[^o14][^o15][^o16] His most frequently cited works deal with stochastic games[^o17][^o18][^o19] and cooperative games.[^o20][^o21]

His editorial activities include participation on the editorial board of the journals: [Dynamic Games and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Dynamic_Games#ISDG_publications), [International Journal of Game Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Dynamic_Games#ISDG_publications) (2001–2008), Mathematical Methods of Operations Research (2006–2008).

He was the advisor of 3 PhD theses: [Anna Jaśkiewicz](wikipedia://en/82192030),[^o22] Piotr Szajowski, Łukasz Balbus.

He is the member of the [International Society of Dynamic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Dynamic_Games). In 2007–2011 he was a member of the Mathematical Committee of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

He became the recipient of [The Isaacs Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Dynamic_Games#The_Isaacs_Award) (2018)[^o23] and recognized Distinguish alumni of Wrocław University of Science and Technology.[^o24]
