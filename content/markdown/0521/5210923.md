# David Milman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Milman"}

**David Pinhusovich Milman** (Дави́д Пи́нхусович Ми́льман; 15 January 1912 in [Chechelnyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chechelnyk) near [Vinnytsia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinnytsia) – 12 July 1982 in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and later [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o3] He was one of the major figures of the Soviet school of functional analysis. In the 70s he emigrated to [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) and was on the faculty of [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).

:::

Milman is known for his development of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) methods, particularly in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), in close connection with concrete problems coming from [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), in particular [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [normal modes](wikipedia://en/478195). The [Krein–Milman theorem](wikipedia://en/3206654) and the [Milman&ndash;Pettis theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milman&ndash;Pettis_theorem) are named after him.

Milman received his Ph.D. from [Odessa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa_State_University) in 1939 under direction of [Mark Krein](wikipedia://en/1190470).

He is the father of the mathematicians [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman) and [Pierre Milman](wikipedia://en/12011763); and the grandfather to the mathematician [Emanuel Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emanuel_Milman) and biochemist [Pavel Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Eismann).
