# Generalized variance

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Generalized variance"}

The **generalized variance** is a scalar value which generalizes [variance](wikipedia://en/32344) for [multivariate random variables](wikipedia://en/49821). It was introduced by [Samuel S. Wilks](wikipedia://en/8136395).

:::

The generalized variance is defined as the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of the [covariance matrix](wikipedia://en/191752), $\det(\Sigma)$. It can be shown to be related to the multidimensional scatter of points around their mean.[^o2]

Minimizing the generalized variance gives the [Kalman filter](wikipedia://en/180855) gain.[^o3]
