# Halanay inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Halanay inequality"}

**Halanay inequality** is a [comparison theorem](wikipedia://en/6626644#Differential_equations) for [differential equations with delay](wikipedia://en/3941333).[^o2] This inequality and its generalizations have been applied to analyze the [stability](wikipedia://en/5493795) of delayed differential equations, and in particular, the stability of industrial processes with dead-time[^o3] and delayed neural networks.[^o4][^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $t_{0}$ be a real number and $\tau$ be a non-negative number. If $v: [t_{0}-\tau, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{+}$ satisfies

$$\frac{d}{dt} v(t) \leq-\alpha v(t)+\beta\left[\sup _{s \in[t-\tau, t]} v(s)\right], t \geq t_{0}$$

where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are constants with $\alpha>\beta>0$, then

$$v(t) \leq k e^{-\eta\left(t-t_{0}\right)}, t \geq t_{0}$$

where $k>0$ and $\eta>0$.

:::
