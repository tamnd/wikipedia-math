# Dennis K.J. Lin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dennis K.J. Lin"}

**Dennis K.J. Lin** is a Taiwanese-American statistician who works in [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541), [quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_assurance), [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253), and [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904).

:::

## Education and early life {#Education_and_early_life}

He was born in Taiwan and obtained a bachelor's degree (in mathematics) in June 1981 from National Tsing-Hua University, ROC. He received a Ph.D. (in statistics) in December 1988 from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, with a minor in computer science.

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

From 1995 to 2020, Lin worked as a statistician for Pennsylvania State University, and became a university distinguished professor.[^o5][^o6] Since July 2020 - August 2023, he has been the head at the department of statistics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) and currently serves as a distinguished professor of statistics in Purdue.[^o6]

Lin published a total of over 200 papers in professional journals.[^o7]

Lin is known for his contributions to the design of experiments, industrial statistics and Ghost Data.[^o8]
