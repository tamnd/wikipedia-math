# Karl von der Mühll

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karl von der Mühll"}

**Karl von der Mühll** (September 13, 1841, in [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) – May 9, 1912, in Basel) was a Swiss mathematician and physicist.[^o3][^o4]

:::

He was born into the [Von der Mühll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_der_Mühll) family, of the Basel [patriciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrician_%28post-Roman_Europe%29) (see [Daig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daig_%28Switzerland%29)), to Karl Georg and Emilie Merian, of the [Merian family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merian_family), a granddaughter of [Peter Merian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Merian).

After graduating from high school in 1859, Von der Mühll studied natural sciences and mathematics at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), with amongst others, [Gustav Heinrich Wiedemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Heinrich_Wiedemann), and from 1861 studied at the [Georg-August University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in Göttingen with [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980), [Wilhelm Eduard Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Eduard_Weber), [Wilhelm Klinkerfues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Klinkerfues) and [Friedrich Wöhler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wöhler). From 1863, he continued his studies at the [Albertus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) in Königsberg, where he turned to mathematical physics under [Franz Ernst Neumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Ernst_Neumann) and received his doctorate in 1866. He also studied there with the mathematician [Friedrich Julius Richelot](wikipedia://en/20346321). In 1866/67, he studied at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in Paris. Shortly after, in 1868, he completed his studies in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) in mathematical physics and in 1872 he became an associate professor at the university.[^o5] In 1889, he left Leipzig for Basel and became associate professor and in 1890 professor for mathematical physics at Basel University. In 1896, he became the university's financial administrator (*Curator fiscorum academicorum*). In the academic years 1895/96 and 1910/11, he was [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the university. In 1887, he was elected a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o4]

He was president of the Swiss Euler Commission (and thus editor of [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902)'s works) and published Franz Neumann's lectures on electrical currents in 1884. From 1872 he was co-editor of the *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)*.[^o6]

In 1875 he married Katharina His, daughter of Eduard His, also of the Basel Daig.[^o3][^o4]

Having suffered from several bouts of depression throughout his life, Von der Mühll committed suicide in 1912 and is buried in the Wolfgottesacker cemetery in Basel.[^o6]
