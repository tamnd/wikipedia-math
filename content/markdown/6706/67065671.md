# Simion Filip

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Simion Filip"}

**Simion Filip** is a mathematician and professor of mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) who works in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Filip was born in [Chișinău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chișinău), where he grew up and attended the Moldo-Turkish "Orizont" Lyceum, graduating in 2005.[^o4][^o5] He is a dual citizen of Romania and Moldova.[^o6] In 2004 and 2005, Filip won a bronze medal and a silver medal respectively while representing Moldova at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o7]

Filip graduated with an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2009.[^o6] He attended [Part III of the Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) where he received a master's degree with distinction in 2010.[^o6] He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) under the supervision of [Alex Eskin](wikipedia://en/31883666) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2016.[^o11]

## Career {#Career}

Filip spent two postdoctoral years as a Junior Fellow at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 2016 to 2018, and another year at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o6] He joined the University of Chicago as an associate professor in 2019 and became a full professor in 2023.[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

Filip received a five-year [Clay Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Research_Fellowship) lasting from 2016 to 2021.[^o6][^o11] In 2020, Filip was one of the recipients of the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize).[^o12][^o13]

## Research {#Research}

Filip's research focuses on the interactions between [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o11][^o12] In particular, he studies dynamics on [Teichmüller spaces](wikipedia://en/1835661) and studies [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590) in [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101).[^o11][^o12]
