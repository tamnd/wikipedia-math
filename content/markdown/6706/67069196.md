# Jan Philip Solovej

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Philip Solovej"}

**Jan Philip Solovej** (born 14 June 1961) is a Danish mathematician and mathematical physicist working on the mathematical theory of quantum mechanics. He is a professor at [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Solovej obtained his Ph.D. in 1989 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) with the thesis on "Universality in the Thomas-Fermi-von Weizsäcker Model of Atoms and Molecules" supervised by [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb). As a post-doctoral researcher, he went to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1989/90 and to the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1990. In 1991 (and 2003/04) he was a member at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). From 1991 to 1995, he was Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). From 1995 to 1997, he was a research professor at the [University of Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aarhus). Since 1997, he is professor in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). Since 2016, he is also leader of VILLUM Centre of Excellence for the Mathematics of Quantum Theory (QMATH).[^o4]

He is President of [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) during 2023-2026.

He is Editor in Chief of *[Journal of Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematical_Physics)* (since 2019).[^o5]

He is married and has two children.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2021, he received the [Henri Poincaré Prize](wikipedia://en/4890712) from the [International Association of Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897340), "for outstanding contributions to the analysis of quantum many-body problems ranging from the electronic structure of large atoms to the Lee-Huang-Yang asymptotics of the ground state energy of dilute Bose gases".

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2022, an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in 1996 and 2004, and a plenary speaker at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905) in 1991, 2003 and 2021.[^o7]

Solovej is a member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters)[^o8] (elected 2000) and of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o9](elected 2020). He was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to the rigorous analysis of quantum systems, particularly many-body systems".[^o10]

## Works {#Works}

Solovej deals with mathematical questions in atomic physics (large atoms and molecules in the [Thomas-Fermi model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas-Fermi_model) and the [Hartree-Fock method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartree-Fock_method)), solid-state physics (the [Bose-Einstein condensate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bose-Einstein_condensate), [Bogoliubov transformation](wikipedia://en/1146338), [quantum dot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_dot), [Heisenberg model](wikipedia://en/4899779) and others) and in many-body quantum mechanics (the stability of matter, the [Lieb-Thirring inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieb-Thirring_inequality) and others). He is co-author, together with [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb), [Robert Seiringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Seiringer), and [Jakob Yngvason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakob_Yngvason), of a monograph on the mathematics of the [Bose gas](wikipedia://en/1064839).

In 1995, with [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb) and [Michael Loss](wikipedia://en/45224765), he proved the stability of matter in magnetic fields.

In 2003, he established the ionization conjecture for atoms within the Hartree-Fock theory, namely the excess charge, the ionization energy and the radius of an atom are uniformly bounded independently of the nuclear charge. Related questions for many-body [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) remain open, which are Problems 9, 10, 11 of the [Simon problems](wikipedia://en/57577573) on Schrödinger operators.

In 2012, with [Rupert L. Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_L._Frank), Christian Hainzl and [Robert Seiringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Seiringer), he derived the [Ginzburg-Landau theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginzburg-Landau_theory) from the [BCS theory](wikipedia://en/4403).

In 2014, with [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb), he proved Wehrl's conjecture on the mininimum entropy of quantum spin systems.

In 2020, with [Søren Fournais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Søren_Fournais), he proved the Lee-Huang-Yang conjecture on the ground state energy of dilute Bose gases.
