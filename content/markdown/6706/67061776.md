# Patricia S. Hu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patricia S. Hu"}

**Patricia S. Hu** is a [Taiwanese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese-American) public servant who was the fourth director of the [Bureau of Transportation Statistics](wikipedia://en/1704636) at the [United States Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Transportation) from 2011 to 2025, the first woman to hold that position.[^o3] She was previously the director of the Center for Transportation Analysis at [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Ridge_National_Laboratory).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Hu received statistics degrees from the [National Chengchi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chengchi_University) and the [University of Guelph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Guelph).[^o5] She was chairman of the [Transportation Research Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transportation_Research_Board).[^o6]

She worked as a biostatistician for [Brookhaven National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookhaven_National_Laboratory), and then joined Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) in 1982.[^o7] She was director of ORNL's Center for Transportation Analysis for nine years, and co-authored many of its annual *Summary of Travel Trends: Nationwide Personal Travel Survey* bulletins, detailing household vehicle travel patterns and usage.[^o8] In addition, she co-authored various journal articles investigating the relationship between aging and car crashes, and the different risk factors found among older male and female drivers.[^o9][^o10][^o11]

Hu started at the Bureau of Transportation Statistics as Director in 2011. It is a civil service position. "Hu has led many applied research projects, published extensively, and received the TRB Pyke Johnson Award in 1984."[^o12][^o13] She has also received the [YMCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YMCA)'s Tribute to Women Award, and the [Association for Women in Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Women_in_Science) Award.[^o14]

As the Director of the BTS, Hu worked to increase the efficiency of the agency by better leveraging the sharing of [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151), such as data from the [Waze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waze) app to mine highway safety information in order to best position emergency vehicles to respond to accidents quickly and efficiently.[^o15]
