# Bloch's formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bloch's formula"}

In [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Bloch's formula**, introduced by [Spencer Bloch](wikipedia://en/25794633) for $K_2$, states that the [Chow group](wikipedia://en/4282051) of a smooth variety *X* over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) is isomorphic to the cohomology of *X* with coefficients in the K-theory of the structure sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$; that is,

:::

$$\operatorname{CH}^q(X) = \operatorname{H}^q(X, K_q(\mathcal{O}_X))$$

where the right-hand side is the sheaf cohomology; $K_q(\mathcal{O}_X)$ is the sheaf associated to the presheaf $U \mapsto K_q(U)$, *U* Zariski open subsets of *X*. The general case is due to Quillen.[^o2] For *q* = 1, one recovers $\operatorname{Pic}(X) = H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X^*)$. (see also [Picard group](wikipedia://en/1850134).)

The formula for the [mixed characteristic](wikipedia://en/30222103) is still open.
