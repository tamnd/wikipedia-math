# Advances in Difference Equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Advances in Difference Equations"}

***Advances in Difference Equations*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) covering research on [difference equations](wikipedia://en/146806), published by [Springer Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Open).

:::

The journal was established in 2004 and publishes articles on theory, methodology, and application of difference and differential equations. Originally published by [Hindawi Publishing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindawi_Publishing_Corporation), the journal was acquired by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) in early 2011.[^o3] The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Ravi Agarwal](wikipedia://en/41929528), Martin Bohner, and Elena Braverman.

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed by the [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index), [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences, and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*. According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.803.[^o5] from July 1, the journal has been transitioning to a new title that opens the scope of the journal to broader developments in theory and applications of models. Under the new title, Advances in Continuous and Discrete Models: Theory and Modern Applications, the journal will cover developments in machine learning, data driven modeling, differential equations, numerical analysis, scientific computing, control, optimization, and computing.[^o5]
