# Leo Goodman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leo Goodman"}

**Leo Aria Goodman** (August 7, 1928 – December 22, 2020) was an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He was known particularly for developing statistical methods for the [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science), including statistical methods for analyzing [categorical data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_data) and data from [statistical surveys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_survey).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Goodman was born in Brooklyn. He attended [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School)[^o4] and he then went on to earn his AB degree *summa cum laude* from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) in 1948, majoring in mathematics and sociology.[^o5] He was class valedictorian. He moved to Princeton for postgraduate work in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), receiving his masters and doctorate in 1950.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Goodman began his career in 1950 at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he would stay, save for a number of visiting professorships, until 1987.[^o5] Since 1987, he has been Class of 1938 Professor in the Sociology Department and the Statistics Department at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o5]

## Awards and distinctions {#Awards_and_distinctions}

He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1956,[^o8] a member of the American Academy of Sciences in 1973, a member of the United States [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1974,[^o9] and a member of the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society) in 1976.[^o10]

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

He was married to Ann Davidow; the marriage ended in divorce. He and his ex-wife had two children and were godparents to [Sylvia Plath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvia_Plath)'s first child, [Frieda Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frieda_Hughes).[^o12]

Goodman died from [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) during the [COVID-19 pandemic in California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_California).[^o13]
