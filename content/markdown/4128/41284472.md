# Suspension of a ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suspension of a ring"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), more specifically in [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500), the **suspension** $\Sigma R$ of a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* is given by[^o2] $\Sigma(R) = C(R)/M(R)$ where $C(R)$ is the ring of all [infinite matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_matrix) with entries in *R* having only finitely many nonzero elements in each row or column and $M(R)$ is its [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) having only finitely many nonzero elements. It is an analog of [suspension](wikipedia://en/1251702) in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

One then has: $K_i(R) \simeq K_{i+1}(\Sigma R)$.
