# 1,000,000,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1,000,000,000,000"}

**1,000,000,000,000** (**one trillion** on the [short scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_scale); **one billion** on the [long scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_scale); one thousand [billion](wikipedia://en/1136363); one million [million](wikipedia://en/1045999)) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [999,999,999,999](wikipedia://en/59910736#900,000,000,000_to_999,999,999,999) and preceding 1,000,000,000,001. It is known as 10 kharab, 1000 arab, or 1 lakh crore in the [Indian numbering system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system).

:::

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word **trillion** was created in the 1680s, borrowed from French *trillion* and Italian *trilione*, which is from *tri-* ("three") + *million* ("a thousand thousands"). Originally in [British English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_English), the word "trillion" referred to [million million millions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintillion) (while "billion" referred to million millions, which is equal to trillion in [American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_English)); however, to many people, the word refers to one thousand billion (1,000,000,000,000) in the present day.[^o5]

Trillion is often abbreviated as **T**, **t**, or **tn**. Rarer forms include **tril**, **trill**, **tln**, and **trn**.[^o6][^o7]

## Scientific usage {#Scientific_usage}

For [physical quantities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_quantities), the [metric prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_prefix) **tera-** (symbol **T**) indicates 1,000,000,000,000 times the base unit. For example, 10<sup>12</sup> [bits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit) of data is called a **terabit**, 10<sup>12</sup> [joules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule) of energy is called a **terajoule**, and 10<sup>12</sup> [watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watt) of power is called a **terawatt**.

The computing performance of 10<sup>12</sup> [floating point operations per second](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floating_point_operations_per_second) (teraFLOPS) is known as **[terascale computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_performance_by_orders_of_magnitude#Terascale_computing_%281012%29)**. It is the performance of several [supercomputers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercomputer), such as [NEC SX-4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEC_SX-4),[^o13] Intel's [ASCI Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASCI_Red), and [iPhone 15 Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone_15_Pro) September 2023 [A17 Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A17_Pro) processor.

## Sense of scale {#Sense_of_scale}

*Image: Visualisation\_1\_Trillion.svg.* Visualization of powers of ten from one to 10<sup>12</sup>

Some comparisons below give a sense of how large 1,000,000,000,000 (10<sup>12</sup>) is, according to current evidence.

### Time {#Time}

- 10<sup>12</sup> seconds (a [terasecond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terasecond), about 31,688 years) ago, [homo sapiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_sapiens) were living as [hunter-gatherers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter-gatherer).
- 10<sup>12</sup> minutes (about 1.9 million years) ago, [Homo erectus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_erectus) arose in Africa, and [early expansions of hominins out of Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_expansions_of_hominins_out_of_Africa) started as [archaic humans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaic_humans) moved throughout Eurasia.
- 10<sup>12</sup> hours (about 114 million years) ago, [Late Cretaceous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Cretaceous) dinosaurs dominated the land, and [giant squids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_squid) thrived in the oceans.
- 10<sup>12</sup> days (about 2.74 × 10<sup>9</sup> years) ago, Earth was a world where only single-celled [microorganisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microorganism) existed. 10<sup>12</sup> days from now, Earth's overall surface temperature will reach 420 K. All life on Earth will be extinct, except single-celled microorganisms.[^o16][^o17]
- 10<sup>12</sup> months (about 83 × 10<sup>9</sup> years) ago, the [universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe) did not exist. 10<sup>12</sup> months from now, the Moon may collide with the Earth or be torn apart to form an orbital ring, if both have not already been destroyed.[^o18]
- 10<sup>12</sup> years—a [teraannus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teraannus)—is 70 times the [age of the universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_the_universe). 10<sup>12</sup> years from now, [star formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_formation) may end in galaxies as they lack [gas clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_cloud) to form new stars,[^o19] and evidence of [Big Bang](wikipedia://en/4116) may become undetectable.[^o20]

### Distance {#Distance}

- 10<sup>12</sup> inches is about 15 e6mile, approximately 1/6th the distance from the Earth to the Sun.
- 10<sup>12</sup> metres (a [terametre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terametre)) is over six times the distance from the Earth to the Sun. It is the estimated diameter of [Betelgeuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betelgeuse), a red supergiant star.[^o22]
- 10<sup>12</sup> kilometres (a [petametre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petametre)) is about 1/10th a [light-year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-year).

### Area {#Area}

- 10<sup>12</sup> square inches is approximately the total area of [Saint Lucia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucia), an island country in the eastern Caribbean.
- 10<sup>12</sup> square feet is larger than the total area of [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal).
- 10<sup>12</sup> square metres is approximately the total area of [Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopia), the 26th largest country on Earth.
- 10<sup>12</sup> square kilometres is almost 2,000 times the Earth's total surface area.

### Volume {#Volume}

- If 10<sup>12</sup> cubic metres of water were spread evenly over the Moon's surface, it would create a layer of water approximately \2.6 cm deep.
- 10<sup>12</sup> grains of sand would occupy a volume of about 2 m3.[^o25]

### Economy {#Economy}

- [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain)'s GDP in 2024 was approximately US\$1.73 × 10<sup>12</sup>.[^o27]
- [Elon Musk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk)'s estimated net worth reached US\$1 × 10<sup>12</sup> in 2026.[^o28]
- [SpaceX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX)'s market capitalization reached US\$2 × 10<sup>12</sup> in 2026.[^o29]
- [Nvidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia)'s market capitalization reached US\$5.5 × 10<sup>12</sup> in 2026.[^o30]

### Nature {#Nature}

- The surface of the human body houses around 10<sup>12</sup> bacteria.[^o32][^o33]
- An average-sized mountain weighs about 10<sup>12</sup> kilograms.[^o34]
- [Andromeda Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andromeda_Galaxy), [Milky Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milky_Way)'s neighbor, contains about 10<sup>12</sup> stars.[^o35]
- There are around 2 × 10<sup>12</sup> galaxies in the [observable universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observable_universe).[^o36][^o37]
- There were around 3 × 10<sup>12</sup> trees on Earth in 2015.[^o38]
- There are around 3.5 × 10<sup>12</sup> fish in the ocean.[^o39]
