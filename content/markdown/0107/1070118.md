# The Treachery of Images

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="The Treachery of Images"}

***The Treachery of Images*** (**La Trahison des images**) is a 1929 painting by Belgian [surrealist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrealist) painter [René Magritte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/René_Magritte). It is also known as ***This Is Not a Pipe***,[^o4] ***Ceci n'est pas une pipe***[^o4] and ***The Wind and the Song***.[^o5] It is on display at the [Los Angeles County Museum of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Museum_of_Art).[^o2]

:::

The painting shows an image of a [tobacco pipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobacco_pipe). Below it, Magritte painted, "Ceci n'est pas une pipe" (, French for "This is not a pipe".)

The theme of pipes with the text "Ceci n'est pas une pipe" is extended in *Les Mots et Les Images*,[^o7] *La Clé des Songes*,[^o8] *Ceci n'est pas une pipe (L'air et la chanson)*,[^o9] *The Tune and Also the Words*,[^o10] *Ceci n'est pas une pomme*,[^o11] and *Les Deux Mystères*.[^o12]

The painting is sometimes given as an example of [meta message](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_message)[^o13] like [Alfred Korzybski](wikipedia://en/784)'s "The word is not the thing" and "[The map is not the territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map–territory_relation)", as well as [Denis Diderot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Diderot)'s *[This is not a story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_is_not_a_story)*.

On December 15, 1929, [Paul Éluard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Éluard) and [André Breton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André_Breton) published an essay about poetry in *[La Révolution surréaliste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Révolution_surréaliste)* (The Surrealist Revolution) as a reaction to the publication by poet [Paul Valéry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Valéry) "Notes sur la poésie" in *[Les Nouvelles littéraires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Nouvelles_littéraires)* of September 28, 1929. When Valéry wrote "Poetry is a survival", Breton and Éluard made fun of it and wrote "Poetry is a pipe", as a reference to Magritte's painting.[^o14][^o15]

In the same edition of *La Révolution surréaliste*, Magritte published "*Les mots et les images*" (his founding text which illustrated where words play with images), his answer to the survey on love, and *Je ne vois pas la \[femme\] cachée dans la forêt*, a painting tableau surrounded by photos of sixteen surrealists with their eyes closed, including Magritte himself.

The away kit of the [Belgian National Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium_national_football_team) for the [2026 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup) was described by the [Royal Belgian FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Belgian_Football_Association) as “a tribute to René Magritte and Belgian Surrealism,” featuring a surrealist theme. A reference to The Treachery of Images is made through the inscription “Ceci n'est pas un maillot” (“This is not a jersey”) printed behind the collar.[^o16]
