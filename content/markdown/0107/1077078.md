# E. W. Hobson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="E. W. Hobson"}

**Ernest William Hobson** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] (27 October 1856 – 19 April 1933) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), now remembered mostly for his books, some of which broke new ground in their coverage in English of topics from [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He was [Sadleirian Professor of Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/291556) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) from 1910 to 1931.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [Derby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby), and was educated at [Derby School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_School), the [Royal School of Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_School_of_Mines), and [Christ's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_College,_Cambridge), graduating [Senior Wrangler](wikipedia://en/19355609) in 1878. He was the brother of the [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) [John A. Hobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_A._Hobson). He became a Fellow of Christ's almost immediately after graduation. He made his way into research mathematics only gradually, becoming an expert in the theory of [spherical harmonics](wikipedia://en/203056).

His 1907 work on [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) was something of a watershed in the British mathematical tradition; and was lauded by [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599).[^o6] It included material on [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649) and [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038) that was topical at the time; and included mistakes that were noticed later (for example by [R. L. Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._L._Moore)).

From 1924 to 1927, [Robert Pollock Gillespie](wikipedia://en/51040007) studied under him.[^o7]

He is buried in the [Parish of the Ascension Burial Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascension_Parish_Burial_Ground,_Cambridge) in Cambridge, with his wife Seline, born 25 March 1860, died 10 June 1940, by whom he had four sons, one of whom Walter William (1894–1930) is buried with them in the same grave.
