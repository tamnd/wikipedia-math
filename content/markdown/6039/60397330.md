# Leetsch C. Hsu

[Xu (Hsu)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xu_%28surname_徐%29)

*Image: Leetsch Hsu.jpg.* Leetsch C. Hsu in [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), 1950

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leetsch C. Hsu"}

**Xu Lizhi** (; 23 September 1920 – 11 March 2019), better known as **Leetsch Charles Hsu**, was a Chinese mathematician and educator. He co-founded the Department of Mathematics of [Jilin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilin_University) and founded the Institute of Applied Mathematics and the *Journal of Mathematical Research with Applications* at the [Dalian University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalian_University_of_Technology). Together with American mathematician [Henry W. Gould](wikipedia://en/38149527), he established the Gould–Hsu Matrix Inversion Formula in 1973.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

*Image: Leetsch Hsu diploma.jpg.* Hsu's diploma from [National Southwestern Associated University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Southwestern_Associated_University)

Hsu was born on 23 September 1920 in [Zhangjiagang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhangjiagang), [Jiangsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangsu), Republic of China. His original name was **Xu Quanyong** (徐泉涌).[^o3]

Hsu studied at Jiangsu Provincial Luoshe Normal School before the outbreak of the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War). In 1940, he passed the entrance examination for the temporary [National Southwestern Associated University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Southwestern_Associated_University) formed by [Tsinghua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) and other universities in [Kunming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunming), and studied mathematics under [Hua Luogeng](wikipedia://en/2337582) and [Pao-Lu Hsu](wikipedia://en/42380697). Upon graduation in 1945, he was hired by the university as a teaching assistant to Hua.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

*Image: Leetsch Hsu Tsinghua ID.jpg.* Hsu's faculty ID issued by [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University)

After the end of the Sino-Japanese War, National Southwestern Associated University was disbanded in 1946 and Hsu joined [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University), which had been reestablished in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing).[^o3]

In 1949, Hsu was awarded a scholarship by the [British Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Council) for a two-year study in the United Kingdom. He spent the first year at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) and the second at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o3][^o5]

In 1951, Hsu returned to China and became an associate professor of mathematics at Tsinghua University. He also taught at [Beijing Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Normal_University) on a part-time basis.[^o3] In 1952, Hsu, together with [Wang Xianghao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xianghao) and Jiang Zejian (江泽坚), moved to [Changchun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changchun) to help establish the Department of Mathematics at Northeast People's University (later renamed as [Jilin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilin_University)). He served as deputy chair of the department. He was promoted to full professor in 1956, and founded the study of [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833) at the university.[^o3]

In 1980, Hsu moved to the [Dalian University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalian_University_of_Technology), where he founded the Institute of Applied Mathematics. He also established the *Journal of Mathematical Research and Exposition* (later renamed as *Journal of Mathematical Research with Applications* 数学研究及应用) and served as its chief editor.[^o5] He also served as Chair of the Mathematics Department of the [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huazhong_University_of_Science_and_Technology).[^o3] From 1986 to 1987, he taught as a visiting professor at the [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) in the United States.[^o3] He was named Honorary Dean of the School of Science of the [Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_University_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics).[^o6] In 2009, he was named by the Dalian University of Technology as one of the most important faculty members in the history of the university.[^o5]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Hsu made significant contributions to [asymptotic analysis](wikipedia://en/641995), [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o3] He began collaborating with American mathematician [Henry W. Gould](wikipedia://en/38149527) in 1965, years before US President [Richard Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) established official relations with the People's Republic of China.[^o8] Their collaboration resulted in the establishment of the Gould–Hsu Matrix Inversion Formula in 1973, which is important for computing combinatorial identities.[^o9][^o10][^o11]

Hsu published over 170 research papers and more than 10 [monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monographs). He advised 20 doctoral students. and won multiple national and international prizes.[^o5]

## Death {#Death}

Hsu died in Beijing on 11 March 2019, at the age of 98.[^o3][^o5]
