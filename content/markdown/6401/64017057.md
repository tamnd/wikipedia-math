# Charles Henry Sampson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Henry Sampson"}

**Charles Henry Sampson** (1859-1936)[^o2] was [Principal of Brasenose College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Principals_of_Brasenose_College,_Oxford), from 1920[^o3] until his death.[^o4]

:::

Sampson was born in [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol) and educated at [Bristol Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Grammar_School) and [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford). A [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), he became a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of Brasenose in 1882; a [Tutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutor) in 1884; and a [Senior Tutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutor) in 1894.

His son Ronald died in the [Great War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I).[^o6] Sampson himself died on 5 November 1936.[^o7]
