# IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics"}

**The *IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics*** is a publication of [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on behalf of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139). Created in 1965, the Journal covers topics related to the application of mathematics.[^o3][^o4]

:::
