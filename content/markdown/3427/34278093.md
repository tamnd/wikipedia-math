# Anatolii Goldberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anatolii Goldberg"}

**Anatolii Asirovich Goldberg** (Анатолий Асирович Гольдберг, Анатолій Асірович Гольдберг, אנטולי גולדברג; April 2, 1930, in [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv) – October 11, 2008, in [Netanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netanya)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet) and [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). His main area of research was the theory of [entire](wikipedia://en/10372) and [meromorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meromorphic) functions.[^o3]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Goldberg received his PhD in 1955 from [Lviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_University) under the direction of Lev Volkovyski. He worked as a docent in [Uzhhorod National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzhhorod_National_University) (1955–1963), then in [Lviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lviv) (1963–1997), where he became a full professor in 1965, and in [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University) (1997–2008). Goldberg, jointly with [Iossif Ostrovskii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iossif_Ostrovskii) and [Boris Levin](wikipedia://en/49586896), was awarded the State Prize of Ukraine in 1992.

Among his main achievements are:

- construction of meromorphic functions with infinitely many deficient values,
- solution of the inverse problem of [Nevanlinna theory](wikipedia://en/393135) for finitely many deficient values,
- development of the integral with respect to a semi-additive measure.

He authored a book (Goldberg Ostrovskii 2008) and over 150 research papers.

Several things are named after him: Goldberg's examples,[^o6] Goldberg's constants,[^o7] and Goldberg's conjecture.[^o8] [^o9]
