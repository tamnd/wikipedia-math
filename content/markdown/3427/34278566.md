# Raymond Woodard Brink

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raymond Woodard Brink"}

**Raymond Woodard Brink** (4 January 1890 in [Newark, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_New_Jersey) – 27 December 1973 in [La Jolla, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Jolla,_California)) was an American mathematician. His Ph.D. advisor at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard) was [George David Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/30871906).

:::

Brink entered [Kansas State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University) at age 14 and by age 19 had two [bachelor's degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree)[^o5] and was employed as an instructor of mathematics in [Moscow, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Idaho); he taught at the state preparatory school of the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho).[^o3][^o4] He returned to school at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and earned a doctorate in 1916 and was a longtime professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), and also authored numerous math textbooks. He served as president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) from 1941–42.[^o5]

## Personal {#Personal}

Brink was the husband of author [Carol Ryrie Brink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Ryrie_Brink), whom he had met during his first year in Idaho when both were teenagers. She was nearly six years his junior; they wed nine years later in 1918, following her graduation from college.[^o4] Married for over 55 years at the time of his death, they had two children, [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Brink) and Nora (Hunter).[^o7] David (b. 1919) became an attorney and later headed the [American Bar Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Bar_Association).[^o8][^o9]
