# Daniela De Silva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniela De Silva"}

**Daniela De Silva** is an Italian mathematician known for her expertise in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). She is an associate professor of mathematics at [Barnard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnard_College) and [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

De Silva did her undergraduate studies in mathematics at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II), and earned a bachelor's degree there in 1997. She completed her doctorate at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2005. Her dissertation, *Existence and Regularity of Monotone Solutions to a Free Boundary Problem*, was supervised by [David Jerison](wikipedia://en/34839472).

After postdoctoral research at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) and a term as J. J. Sylvester Assistant Professor at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), she joined the Barnard and Columbia faculty in 2007.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

De Silva won the 2016 [Sadosky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadosky_Prize) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) for "fundamental contributions to the regularity theory of nonlinear elliptic partial differential equations and non-local integro-differential equations". In 2018, Barnard honored her with their Tow Professorship for Distinguished Scholars and Practitioners.
