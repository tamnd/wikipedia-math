# Andreas Seeger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Seeger"}

**Andreas Seeger** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in the field of [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). He received his PhD from [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt) in 1985 under the supervision of Walter Trebels.

:::

He was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2014 for his contributions to [Fourier integral operators](wikipedia://en/10138003), local smoothing, [oscillatory integrals](wikipedia://en/3224242), and [Fourier multipliers](wikipedia://en/995746).[^o3] In 2017, he was awarded the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o4] He was awarded a Simons Fellowship in 2019.[^o5]
