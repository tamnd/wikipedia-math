# Charles Smith (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Smith"}

**Charles Smith** (b [Huntingdon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntingdon), 11 May 1844; d [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge) 13 November 1916)[^o2] was a 20th-century British academic.[^o3]

:::

Smith was educated at [Sidney Sussex College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Sussex_College,_Cambridge). He became a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of [Sidney Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Sussex) in 1868; [Tutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutor) in 1875; and [Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_%28college%29) in 1890. He was [Vice-Chancellor of the University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Vice-Chancellors_of_the_University_of_Cambridge) from 1895 to 1897.[^o5]
