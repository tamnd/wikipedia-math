# Akihiro Kanamori

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Akihiro Kanamori"}

**Akihiro Kanamori** is a Japanese-born American mathematician. He specializes in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) and is the author of the monograph on [large cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_cardinal_property), *[The Higher Infinite](wikipedia://en/63122422)*.[^o3] He has written several essays on the history of mathematics, especially set theory.

:::

Kanamori graduated from [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and earned a Ph.D. from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) ([King's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge)), and is a professor of mathematics at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University).

With [Matthew Foreman](wikipedia://en/3332703), Kanamori is the editor of the *Handbook of Set Theory* (2010).[^o4]
