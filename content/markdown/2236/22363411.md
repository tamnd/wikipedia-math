# K. S. Amur

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="K. S. Amur"}

**Krishna Shyamacharya Amur** (born 1931) was a professor emeritus of mathematics in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625)[^o3][^o4] was head of the department of mathematics, [Karnatak University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnatak_University), Dharwar.[^o5] Amur was vice-president of Karnatak Education Board, Dharwar.[^o6] and a brother of [G. S. Amur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._S._Amur).

:::

Born and raised in Suranagi village of [Haveri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haveri) taluka, he earned a M.Sc. and a Ph.D. (1964) in mathematics from the Karnataka University, Dharwar.

Amur was a postdoctoral fellow at the department of mathematics, [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), from 1967 to 1968 and again in 1984, he went to the USA on a fellowship program for a year.[^o7] He was also acting registrar of the Karnatak University, Dharwar from 1978-80.

Amur was the President of Sri Aurobindo society,[^o8] Karnataka State.
