# Roberto Markarian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roberto Markarian"}

**Roberto Markarian Abrahamian** (12 December 1946) is a Uruguayan mathematician of [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Uruguayans) descent, expert in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He started studying at the University of the Republic in the 1960s. During the civic-military dictatorship he was arrested due to political reasons. Later he went to Brazil, where he graduated from the [Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul](wikipedia://en/889448). Later on, his degree was validated in Uruguay.

Markarian served as [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [University of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Republic) (2014-2018).[^o3]

He is brother of the football coach [Sergio Markarián](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Markarián).
