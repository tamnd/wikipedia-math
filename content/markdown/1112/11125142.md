# Extouch triangle

*Image: Extouch Triangle and Nagel Point.svg.* solid #333333 solid orange solid red solid #1e90ff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Extouch triangle"}

In [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **extouch triangle** of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) is formed by joining the points at which the three [excircles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excircle) touch the triangle.

:::

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) of the extouch triangle are given in [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) by:

$$\begin{array}{rccccc} T_A =& 0 &:& \csc^2 \frac{B}{2} &:& \csc^2 \frac{C}{2} \\ T_B =& \csc^2 \frac{A}{2} &:& 0 &:& \csc^2 \frac{C}{2} \\ T_C =& \csc^2 \frac{A}{2} &:& \csc^2 \frac{B}{2} &:& 0 \end{array}$$

or equivalently, where $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides opposite angles $A, B, C$ respectively,

$$\begin{array}{rccccc} T_A =& 0 &:& \frac{a \, - \, b \, + \, c}{b} &:& \frac{a \, + \, b \, - \, c}{c} \\ T_B =& \frac{-a \, + \, b \, + \, c}{a} &:& 0 &:& \frac{a \, + \, b \, - \, c}{c} \\ T_C =& \frac{-a \, + \, b \, + \, c}{a} &:& \frac{a \, - \, b \, + \, c}{b} &:& 0 \end{array}$$

Also, with $s$ denoting the [semiperimeter](wikipedia://en/886617) of the triangle, the vertices of the extouch triangle are given in [barycentric coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycentric_coordinates) by:

$$\begin{array}{rccccc} T_A =& 0 &:& s-b &:& s-c \\ T_B =& s-a &:& 0 &:& s-c \\ T_C =& s-a &:& s-b &:& 0 \end{array}$$

## Related figures {#Related_figures}

The triangle's [splitters](wikipedia://en/14961391) are lines connecting the vertices of the original triangle to the corresponding vertices of the extouch triangle; they bisect the triangle's perimeter and meet at the [Nagel point](wikipedia://en/9489914). This is shown in blue and labelled "N" in the diagram.

The [Mandart inellipse](wikipedia://en/14974602) is tangent to the sides of the reference triangle at the three vertices of the extouch triangle.[^o4]

## Area {#Area}

The area of the extouch triangle, $K_{T}$, is given by:

$$K_T= K\frac{2r^2s}{abc}$$

where $K$ and $r$ are the area and radius of the [incircle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incircle), $s$ is the [semiperimeter](wikipedia://en/886617) of the original triangle, and $a, b, c$ are the side lengths of the original triangle.

This is the same area as that of the [intouch triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intouch_triangle).[^o6]
