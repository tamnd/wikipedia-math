# Siegfried Becher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegfried Becher"}

Siegfried Becher

**Siegfried Becher** (28 February 1806 – 4 March 1873) was an Austrian [political economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_economy).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Becher was born in [Planá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planá), [Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemia). He studied at [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague) and [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866). In 1831, he entered government service. He was appointed professor at the [Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology), Vienna, in 1835. From 1848 to 1852, he was employed in the ministry of commerce, for which he made a trip of investigation in Germany and [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) in 1849. He died in Vienna.

## Works {#Works}

- *Das österreichische Münzwesen von 1524-1838 in historischer, statistischer und legislativer Hinsicht* (two volumes, Vienna 1838)
- *Statistiche Uebersicht des Handels der österreichischen Monarchie mit dem Auslande während der Jahre 1829-38* (Stuttgart 1841)
- *Ergebnisse des Handels- und Zolleinkommens der österreichischen Monarchie von den Jahren 1819–43* (1846)
- *Die deutschen Zoll-und Handelsverhältnisse zur Anbahnung der österreichisch-deutschen Zoll-und Handelseinigung* (Leipzig 1850)
- *Die Volkswirtschaft* (Vienna 1853)
