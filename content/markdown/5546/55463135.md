# Advances in Group Theory and Applications

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Advances in Group Theory and Applications"}

***Advances in Group Theory and Applications*** (AGTA) is a [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review), [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [research journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_journal) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). It was founded in 2015 by the council of the no-profit association [AGTA - Advances in Group Theory and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGTA_-_Advances_in_Group_Theory_and_Applications), and is published by [Aracne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aracne).

:::

The journal is composed of three sections. The main one contains mathematical research papers, while the two other sections are respectively devoted to historical papers and open problems.

The journal is published as a [diamond open access journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_open_access_journal), meaning that the content is immediately freely available to the readers, and the authors do not have to pay any author publication fees.[^o3]

The journal is [abstracted and indexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstracting_and_indexing) by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)[^o4]* and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*.[^o5]

## Sections {#Sections}

### A<sub>D</sub>V – The History behind Group Theory {#ADV_–_The_History_behind_Group_Theory}

The purpose of this section is to present historical documents, biographical notes and discussions on relevant aspects of group theory and its applications.

### A<sub>D</sub>V – Perspectives in Group Theory {#ADV_–_Perspectives_in_Group_Theory}

The open problems submitted to the journal are published in this section.

## Reinhold Baer Prize sponsorship {#Reinhold_Baer_Prize_sponsorship}

The journal started co-sponsoring the [Reinhold Baer Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aracne#Reinhold_Baer_Prize) in 2017.[^o10]

## Proceedings {#Proceedings}

Volume 15 publishes the proceedings of the conference Groups & Algebras in Bicocca for Young algebraists 2022.[^o12][^o13]
