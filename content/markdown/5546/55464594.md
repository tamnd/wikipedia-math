# ABACABA pattern

*Image: Binary number dabacaba pattern.png.* DABACABA patterns in (3-[bit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit)) [binary numbers](wikipedia://en/238686)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ABACABA pattern"}

The **ABACABA pattern** is a [fractal pattern](wikipedia://en/10913) that shows up in many places in the real world (such as in geometry, art, music, poetry, [number systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_systems), literature and [higher dimensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_dimensions)).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] Patterns often show a **D**ABACABA type subset. ***AA***, *A**BB**A*, and *AB**AA**BA* type forms are also considered.[^o6]

:::

## Generating the pattern {#Generating_the_pattern}

In order to [generate](wikipedia://en/505225) the next sequence, first take the previous pattern, add the next letter from the alphabet, and then repeat the previous pattern. The first few steps are listed here.[^o5]

::: {#o8 .table cue=table}

| Step | Pattern | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A** | 2<sup>1</sup> − 1 = 1 |
| 2 | A**B**A | 3 |
| 3 | ABA**C**ABA | 7 |
| 4 | ABACABA**D**ABACABA | 15 |
| 5 | ABACABADABACABA**E**ABACABADABACABA | 31 |
| 6 | ABACABADABACABAEABACABADABACABA**F**ABACABADABACABAEABACABADABACABA | 63 |

:::

ABACABA is a "quickly [growing](wikipedia://en/191933) word", often described as *[chiastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiastic)* or "symmetrically organized around a central axis" (see: [Chiastic structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiastic_structure) and [Χ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_%28letter%29)).[^o5] The number of members in each iteration is $a(n) = 2^{n} - 1$, the [Mersenne numbers](wikipedia://en/18908) (). Replacing each letter with their respective numbers yields the [ruler function](wikipedia://en/69715647).
