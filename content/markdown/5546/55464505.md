# Joan Bagaria

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joan Bagaria"}

**Joan Bagaria Pigrau** (born August 17, 1958) is a Catalan mathematician, logician and [set theorist](wikipedia://en/27553) at [ICREA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_Institution_for_Research_and_Advanced_Studies) and [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona). He has made many contributions concerning [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205), [large cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_cardinals), infinite combinatorics and their applications to other areas of mathematics.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

### Early life (1958 – 1992) {#Early_life_(1958_–_1992)}

Bagaria was born in 1958 in [Manlleu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manlleu), Catalonia, Spain.[^o5] He earned a B.A. in philosophy from the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) in 1981, and an M.A. in philosophy there in 1984.[^o5] He earned his PhD in Logic & the Methodology of Science at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1991 under the supervision of Haim Judah and [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071).[^o5] He also held various other positions at UC Berkeley between January 1986 and May 1992, including as a reader, a graduate student instructor, a junior specialist, and a summer session instructor.[^o5]

### Career (1992 – present) {#Career_(1992_–_present)}

In June 1992, Bagaria returned to Catalonia as an invited researcher at the [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525), where he remained until that September.[^o5] From October 1992 to September 1995, he was an interim research professor at the [Autonomous University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Barcelona).[^o5] He was an invited professor at [Pompeu Fabra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompeu_Fabra_University) from October 1995 to September 1996.[^o5] Then he got an interim associate professor position at University of Barcelona, which he held until October 2001.[^o5]

Since 2001, he has been ICREA Research Professor at [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona).[^o8] He served as the first president of the European Set Theory Society (2007–11).

His research work is widely cited,[^o9] and he has given talks to the general public.[^o10][^o11] He has had nine PhD students.[^o6]

### Personal life {#Personal_life}

He is also an active [Catalan independentist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_independence_movement).[^o13]
