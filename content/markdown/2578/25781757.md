# Dogbone space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dogbone space"}

*Image: Bing's\_Dogbone.tiff.* The first stage of the dogbone space construction.

In [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), the **dogbone space**, constructed by [R. H. Bing](wikipedia://en/967353),[^o2] is a [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) of three-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) $\mathbb{R}^3$ such that all [inverse images](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_image) of points are points or [tame arcs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_arc), yet it is not [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to $\mathbb{R}^3$. The name "dogbone space" refers to a fanciful resemblance between some of the diagrams of genus 2 surfaces in Bing's paper and a dog bone. Bing showed that the [product](wikipedia://en/24910) of the dogbone space with $\mathbb{R}^1$ is [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to $\mathbb{R}^4$.[^o3]

:::

Although the dogbone space is not a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470), it is a [generalized homological manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_homological_manifold) and a [homotopy manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_manifold).
