# Journal of the American Statistical Association

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of the American Statistical Association"}

The ***Journal of the American Statistical Association*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). It covers work primarily focused on the application of statistics, statistical theory and methods in [economic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [social](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science), [physical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Science), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), and [health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_science) sciences. The journal also includes reviews of books which are relevant to the field. The journal was established in 1888 as the *Publications of the American Statistical Association*. It was renamed *Quarterly Publications of the American Statistical Association* in 1912, obtaining its current title in 1922.

:::

## Reception {#Reception}

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2025 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 4.0.[^o4]

In a 2003 survey of statisticians, the journal was ranked first in the category "Applications of Statistics" and second (after *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)*) for "Mathematical Statistics".[^o5]
