# Amèle El Mahdi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amèle El Mahdi"}

**Amèle El Mahdi**, born in 1956 in [Blida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blida), is an Algerian professor of mathematics and writer. She lived in many of the cities in southern Algeria, which inspired many of her writings. She has written for the Algerian newspaper *[El Watan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Watan)*.[^o3][^o4]

:::

- *Yamsel, son of the Ahaggar*, Algiers, Casbah Editions, 2014, 275 p.
- *Tin Hinan, My Queen*, Algiers, Casbah Editions, 2014, 141 p.
