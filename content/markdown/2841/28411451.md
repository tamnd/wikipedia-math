# Kundt spacetime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kundt spacetime"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), **Kundt spacetimes** are [Lorentzian manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentzian_manifold) admitting a geodesic null congruence with vanishing [optical scalars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_scalars) (expansion, twist and shear). A well known member of Kundt class is [pp-wave](wikipedia://en/1447921). [Ricci-flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci-flat) Kundt spacetimes in arbitrary dimension are [algebraically special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrov_Type). In four dimensions Ricci-flat Kundt metrics of [Petrov type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrov_type) III and N are completely known.[^o2] All [VSI spacetimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VSI_spacetime) belong to a subset of the Kundt spacetimes.[^o3]

:::
