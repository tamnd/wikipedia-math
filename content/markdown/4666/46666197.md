# Antonio Maria Lorgna

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Maria Lorgna"}

**Antonio Maria Lorgna** (18 October 1735 – 28 June 1796) or **Antonio Mario Lorgna** (as he signed his works) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from Italy in the 18th century, founder of the [Accademia nazionale delle scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

*Image: Lorgna - Opuscula mathematica et physica, 1770 - 1399875.jpg.* *Opuscula mathematica et physica*, 1770

Little is known about the first twenty years of Lorgna's life. He was the son of a cavalry officer of the Venetian army and in 1757 he was in [Dalmatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalmatia) probably following his father in his military duties (Dalmatia was at this time under Venetian rule). The general governor of Dalmatia, Alvise Contarini, knowing his abilities and versatility, appointed him as his secretary and interpreter.

Returned to [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice) in 1759, Lorgna became a student at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua); he didn't graduate, but learned physics, astronomy and mathematics under [Giovanni Poleni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Poleni) and Giovanni Alberto Colombo.[^o5] In 1762 he enrolled in the Venetian army and went to [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia) for some time, then was appointed professor of mathematics in the military academy for engineers in Castelvecchio in [Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verona).[^o6] Lorgna worked at the Military College of Castelvecchio for the rest of his career, teaching courses on trigonometry, mechanics, statics for construction, ballistics and hydraulics. From 1784 he was also the general governor of the college, attaining the military rank of [Brigadier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier).

Lorgna never married. He died while the French army was occupying Verona.[^o7] He is best known as the founder in 1782 of the *Società Italiana delle Scienze detta dei XL*, now the *[Accademia nazionale delle scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze)*, a leading research Italian institution.[^o8]
