# Pro-simplicial set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pro-simplicial set"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **pro-simplicial set** is an [inverse system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_system) of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784).

:::

A pro-simplicial set is called *pro-finite* if each term of the inverse system of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784) has [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [homotopy groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_groups).

Pro-simplicial sets show up in [shape theory](wikipedia://en/18767352), in the study of localization and completion in homotopy theory, and in the study of homotopy properties of schemes (e.g. [étale homotopy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Étale_homotopy_theory)).
