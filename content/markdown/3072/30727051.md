# Stone–Čech remainder

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stone–Čech remainder"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Stone–Čech remainder** of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X*, also called the **corona** or **corona set**, is the [complement](wikipedia://en/54347) β*X* \\ *X* of the space in its [Stone–Čech compactification](wikipedia://en/52897) β*X*.

:::

A topological space is said to be [σ-compact](wikipedia://en/346681) if it is the union of [countably](wikipedia://en/6026) many [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) subspaces, and [locally compact](wikipedia://en/48632) if every point has a [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) with compact [closure](wikipedia://en/42309). The Stone–Čech remainder of a σ-compact and locally compact [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) is a [sub-Stonean space](wikipedia://en/30727217), i.e., any two [open](wikipedia://en/39358) σ-compact [disjoint](wikipedia://en/39359) subsets have disjoint compact closures.
