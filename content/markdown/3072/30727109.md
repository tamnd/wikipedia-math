# Rickart space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rickart space"}

In mathematics, a **Rickart space** (after [Charles Earl Rickart](wikipedia://en/45114564)), also called a **basically disconnected space**, is a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) in which open σ-compact subsets have [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) open closures.

:::

(Grove Pedersen 1984) named them after (txt), who showed that Rickart spaces are related to monotone σ-complete [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184) under [Gelfand duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelfand_duality), in the same way that [Stonean spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonean_space) are related to [AW\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/39435930).

Rickart spaces were also studied by [Paul Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598) under the name *Boolean σ-spaces*, as they correspond to Boolean σ-algebras via [Stone duality](wikipedia://en/379619).[^o4] The concept of Rickart spaces resurfaced in (Jamneshan Tao 2023) under the name *Stone<sub>σ</sub>-spaces*.

Both algebraic descriptions (namely, the [C\*-algebraic](wikipedia://en/7184) and [Boolean algebraic](wikipedia://en/54476844) ones) are explicitly discussed in (Fritz Lorenzin 2025).[^o7]

Rickart spaces are [totally disconnected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_disconnected) and [sub-Stonean spaces](wikipedia://en/30727217).
