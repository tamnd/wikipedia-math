# Orders of magnitude (probability)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Orders of magnitude"}

This page lists events in order of increasing [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), grouped by **orders of magnitude**. These probabilities were calculated given assumptions detailed in the relevant articles and references. For example, the probabilities of obtaining the different [poker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker) hands assume that the cards are dealt fairly.

:::
