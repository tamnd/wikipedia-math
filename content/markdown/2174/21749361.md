# Vadim G. Vizing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vadim G. Vizing"}

**Vadim Georgievich Vizing** (Вади́м Гео́ргиевич Визинг, Вадим Георгійович Візінг; 25 March 1937 – 23 August 2017) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and especially for [Vizing's theorem](wikipedia://en/5449464) stating that the edges of any simple graph with maximum degree Δ can be [colored](wikipedia://en/690647) with at most Δ + 1 colors.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Vizing was born in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev) on March 25, 1937. His mother was half-German, and because of this the Soviet authorities [forced his family to move to Siberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_transfer_in_the_Soviet_Union) in 1947. After completing his undergraduate studies in mathematics in [Tomsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomsk_State_University) in 1959, he began his Ph.D. studies at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), on the subject of [function approximation](wikipedia://en/336897), but he left in 1962 without completing his degree. Instead, he returned to [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk), working from 1962 to 1968 at the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) there and earning a Ph.D. in 1966. In Novosibirsk, he was a regular participant in A. A. Zykov's seminar in graph theory. After holding various additional positions, he moved to [Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa) in 1974, where he taught mathematics for many years at the Academy for Food Technology (originally known as Одесский технологический институт пищевой промышленности им. М. В. Ломоносова, "Odessa Technological Institute of Food Industry named after [Mikhail Lomonosov](wikipedia://en/63375)").

## Research results {#Research_results}

The result now known as [Vizing's theorem](wikipedia://en/5449464), published in 1964, when Vizing was working in Novosibirsk, states that the edges of any graph with at most Δ edges per vertex can be [colored](wikipedia://en/690647) using at most Δ + 1 colors.V64 It is a continuation of the work of [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693), who showed that any [multigraph](wikipedia://en/1774970) can have its edges colored with at most (3/2)Δ colors (a tight bound, as a triangle with Δ/2 edges per side requires this many colors).[^o4] Although Vizing's theorem is now standard material in many graph theory textbooks, Vizing had trouble publishing the result initially, and his paper on it appears in an obscure journal, *Diskret. Analiz*.[^o5]

Vizing also made other contributions to graph theory and [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743), including the introduction of [list coloring](wikipedia://en/690669),V76 the formulation of the [total coloring](wikipedia://en/690702) conjecture (still unsolved) stating that the edges and vertices of any graph can together be colored with at most Δ + 2 colors,V68[^o6] [Vizing's conjecture](wikipedia://en/8175877) (also unsolved) concerning the [domination number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domination_number) of [cartesian products of graphs](wikipedia://en/2856852),V68 and the 1974 definition of the [modular product of graphs](wikipedia://en/13518199) as a way of reducing [subgraph isomorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgraph_isomorphism) problems to finding [maximum cliques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_clique) in graphs.V74 He also proved a stronger version of [Brooks' theorem](wikipedia://en/21042117) that applies to list coloring.

From 1976, Vizing stopped working on graph theory and studied problems of [scheduling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_Shop_Scheduling) instead, only returning to graph theory again in 1995.
