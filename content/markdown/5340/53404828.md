# Stechkin's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stechkin's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) – more specifically, in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) – **Stechkin's lemma** is a result about the [ℓ<sup>*q*</sup> norm](wikipedia://en/45194) of the tail of a [sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence_%28mathematics%29), when the whole sequence is known to have finite ℓ<sup>*p*</sup> norm. Here, the term "tail" means those terms in the sequence that are not among the *N* largest terms, for an arbitrary [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) *N*. Stechkin's lemma is often useful when [analysing](wikipedia://en/1092713) best-*N*-term approximations to [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) in a given basis of a [function space](wikipedia://en/340630). The result was originally proved by Stechkin in the case $q = 2$.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let $0 < p < q < \infty$ and let $I$ be a countable [index set](wikipedia://en/393055). Let $(a_{i})_{i \in I}$ be any sequence indexed by $I$, and for $N \in \mathbb{N}$ let $I_{N} \subset I$ be the indices of the $N$ largest terms of the sequence $(a_{i})_{i \in I}$ in [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991). Then

$$\left( \sum_{i \in I \setminus I_{N}} | a_{i} |^{q} \right)^{1/q} \leq \left( \sum_{i \in I} | a_{i} |^{p} \right)^{1/p} \frac{1}{N^{r}}$$

where

$$r = \frac{1}{p} - \frac{1}{q} > 0.$$

Thus, Stechkin's lemma controls the ℓ<sup>*q*</sup> norm of the tail of the sequence $(a_{i})_{i \in I}$ (and hence the ℓ<sup>*q*</sup> norm of the difference between the sequence and its approximation using its $N$ largest terms) in terms of the ℓ<sup>*p*</sup> norm of the full sequence and a rate of decay.

:::

::: {#o5 .proof cue=heading name="Proof of the lemma"}

## Proof of the lemma {#Proof_of_the_lemma}

W.l.o.g. we assume that the sequence $(a_{i})_{i \in I}$ is sorted by $|a_{i+1}| \leq |a_{i}|, i \in I$ and we set $I= \mathbb{N}$ for notation.

First, we reformulate the statement of the lemma to

$$\left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i \in I \setminus I_{N}} | a_{i} |^{q} \right)^{1/q} \leq \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j \in I} | a_{j} |^{p} \right)^{1/p}.$$

Now, we notice that for $d \in \mathbb{N}$

$$|a_i| \leq |a_{dN}|, \quad \text{for} \quad i=dN+1, \dots, (d+1)N;$$

$$|a_{dN}| \leq |a_j|, \quad \text{for} \quad j=(d-1)N+1, \dots, dN;$$

Using this, we can estimate

$$\left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i \in I \setminus I_{N}} | a_{i} |^{q} \right)^{1/q} \leq \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} N | a_{dN} |^{q} \right)^{1/q} = \left( \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} | a_{dN} |^{q} \right)^{1/q}$$

as well as

$$\left( \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} | a_{dN} |^{p} \right)^{1/p} = \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} N | a_{dN} |^{p} \right)^{1/p} \leq \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i \in I} | a_{i} |^{p} \right)^{1/p}.$$

Also, we get by $\ell^{p}$ norm equivalence:

$$\left( \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} | a_{dN} |^{q} \right)^{1/q} \leq \left( \sum_{d \in \mathbb{N}} | a_{dN} |^{p} \right)^{1/p}.$$

Putting all these ingredients together completes the proof.
