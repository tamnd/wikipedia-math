# Giovanni Frattini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Frattini"}

**Giovanni Frattini** (8 January 1852 – 21 July 1925) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), noted for his contributions to [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Frattini entered the [University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1869, where he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) with [Giuseppe Battaglini](wikipedia://en/34349418), [Eugenio Beltrami](wikipedia://en/543760), and [Luigi Cremona](wikipedia://en/1846002), obtaining his [Laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in 1875.

In 1885 he published a paper where he defined a certain [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707). This subgroup, now known as the [Frattini subgroup](wikipedia://en/540707), is the subgroup $\Phi(G)$ generated by all the non-generators of the group $G$. He showed that $\Phi(G)$ is [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/144569) and, in so doing, developed a method of proof known today as [Frattini's argument](wikipedia://en/6532631).[^o4]

Besides group theory, he also studied differential geometry and the analysis of second degree indeterminates. [^o5]
