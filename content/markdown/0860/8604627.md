::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Great grand 120-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great grand 120-cell** or **great grand polydodecahedron** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,5/2,3}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope).

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}
