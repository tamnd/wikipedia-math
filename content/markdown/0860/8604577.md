::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Small stellated 120-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small stellated 120-cell** or **stellated polydodecahedron** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5/2,5,3}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope).

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [great grand 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604627), and also shares its 120 vertices with the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) and eight other regular star 4-polytopes. It may also be seen as the *first [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253)* of the 120-cell. In this sense it could be seen as analogous to the three-dimensional [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843), which is the first stellation of the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).[^o9] Indeed, the small stellated 120-cell is dual to the [icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604585), which could be taken as a 4D analogue of the [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441), dual of the small stellated dodecahedron.

The edges of the small stellated 120-cell are [τ](wikipedia://en/12386)<sup>2</sup> as long as those of the 120-cell core inside the 4-polytope.
