In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great 120-cell** or **great polydodecahedron** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,5/2,5}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope). It is one of the two such polytopes that is self-dual.

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), [icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604585) as well as the same [face arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_arrangement) as the [grand 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604599).

Due to its self-duality, it does not have a good three-dimensional analogue, but (like all other star polyhedra and polychora) is analogous to the two-dimensional [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544).
