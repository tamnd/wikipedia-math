::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Icosahedral 120-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **icosahedral 120-cell**, **polyicosahedron**, **faceted 600-cell** or **icosaplex** is a regular [star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,5,5/2}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope).

:::

It is constructed by 5 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084) around each edge in a [pentagrammic](wikipedia://en/197544) figure. The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441).

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), [grand 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604599) and [great 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604660), and shares its vertices with all other [Schläfli–Hess 4-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli–Hess_4-polytope) except the [great grand stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604513) (another stellation of the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358)).

As a faceted 600-cell, replacing the [simplicial](wikipedia://en/30606) cells of the 600-cell with [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/43856084) [pentagonal polytope](wikipedia://en/33020517) cells, it could be seen as a four-dimensional analogue of the [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441), which replaces the triangular faces of the icosahedron with pentagonal faces. Indeed, the icosahedral 120-cell is dual to the [small stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604577), which could be taken as a 4D analogue of the [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843), dual of the great dodecahedron.
