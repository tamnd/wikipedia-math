In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great stellated 120-cell** or **great stellated polydodecahedron** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5/2,3,5}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope).

:::

It is one of four *regular star 4-polytopes* discovered by [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408). It is named by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), extending the naming system by [Arthur Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034) for the [Kepler-Poinsot solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_solid).

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The great stellated 120-cell has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [great icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604644) and [grand 600-cell](wikipedia://en/8604538), and the same [face arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_arrangement) as the [grand stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604676).
