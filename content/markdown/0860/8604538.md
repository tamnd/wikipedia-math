::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Grand 600-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **grand 600-cell** or **grand polytetrahedron** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3, 3, 5/2}. It is one of 10 regular Schläfli-Hess polytopes. It is the only one with 600 cells.

:::

It is one of four *regular star 4-polytopes* discovered by [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408). It was named by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), extending the naming system by [Arthur Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034) for the [Kepler-Poinsot solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_solid).

The grand 600-cell can be seen as the four-dimensional analogue of the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898) (which in turn is analogous to the [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544)); both of these are the only regular *n*-dimensional star polytopes which are derived by performing stellational operations on the [pentagonal polytope](wikipedia://en/33020517) which has [simplectic](wikipedia://en/39781) faces. It can be constructed analogously to the pentagram, its two-dimensional analogue, via the extension of said (*n-1*)-D simplex faces of the core *n*D polytope ([tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra) for the grand 600-cell, [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285) for the great icosahedron, and [line segments](wikipedia://en/22634860) for the pentagram) until the figure regains regular faces.

The Grand 600-cell is also dual to the [great grand stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604513), mirroring the great icosahedron's duality with the [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878) (which in turn is also analogous to the pentagram); all of these are the final stellations of the *n*-dimensional "dodecahedral-type" pentagonal polytope.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [great stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604610), and [grand stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604676), and same [face arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_arrangement) as the [great icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604644).
