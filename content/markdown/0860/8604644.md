::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Great icosahedral 120-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great icosahedral 120-cell**, **great polyicosahedron** or **great [faceted](wikipedia://en/8613537) 600-cell** is a [regular star 4-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_star_4-polytope) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,5/2,5}. It is one of 10 regular [Schläfli-Hess polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli-Hess_polytope).

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has the same [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) as the [great stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604610), and [grand stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604676), and [face arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_arrangement) of the [grand 600-cell](wikipedia://en/8604538).
