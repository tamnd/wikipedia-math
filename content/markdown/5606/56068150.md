# Ralph Kaufmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ralph Kaufmann"}

**Ralph Martin Kaufmann** (born August 4, 1969) is a German-American mathematician working in the United States.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Kaufmann studied mathematics, physics and philosophy at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). He obtained a master's degree in Physics in 1994 under the supervision of [Werner Nahm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Nahm) and a master's degree in philosophy under the supervision of Rainer Stuhlmann-Laeisz in 1996. His doctoral studies were carried out at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) under the supervision of [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086), and he graduated summa cum laude from [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1997 with a thesis entitled "The geometry of the moduli space of pointed curves, the tensor product in the theory of Frobenius manifolds and the explicit Künneth formula in quantum cohomology".[^o4]

He remained at the Max Planck Institute for one year after his graduation as a researcher before moving to the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) for a year with a Marie Curie Fellowship from the European Union.

In 1999 Kaufmann moved to the United States, where he has held several positions. He arrived at his current institution, [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), in 2007 as an associate professor, being promoted to full professor in 2012.

Kaufmann has been a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University),[^o5] and has held visiting positions at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques), the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California)[^o6] and the Hamburg Institute for Advanced Study.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Kaufmann's research has covered different areas of mathematics and theoretical physics. After briefly working on the [Virasoro algebra](wikipedia://en/348029),[^o9] he started to work on the quantum cohomology of a product[^o10] and the Künneth formula, obtaining results on explicit formulas[^o11] and global results.[^o12] In collaboration with [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086) and [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557) he started the study of higher Weil–Peterson volumes,[^o13] later continued by Losef–Manin and [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655). Kaufmann then started to study stringy and mirror phenomena for [orbifolds](wikipedia://en/293504)[^o14] and singularities.[^o15] This led to the invention of stringy K-theory.[^o16][^o17]

Kaufmann has also worked on [string topology](wikipedia://en/28725806), invented by Moira Chas and [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608), and [operad theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operad_theory).[^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22] Here he first proved a cyclic version of [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544)'s conjecture in deformation theory[^o23] and provided an extension of string topology to moduli spaces.[^o24][^o25][^o26][^o27]

More recently Kaufmann has introduced the notion of Feynman categories[^o28] to give a common framework for various aspects of algebra, geometry, topology, and category theory.[^o29][^o30][^o31]

In mathematical physics, he has also studied the geometry of wire networks[^o32] as well as periodic systems[^o33][^o34] and topological insulators.[^o35]

In philosophy, he has recently worked on [Hegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegel)'s theory of mathematics[^o36][^o37] and on [Friedrich Hölderlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Hölderlin).[^o38]
