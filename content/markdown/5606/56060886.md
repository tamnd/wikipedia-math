# Sylvie Benzoni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvie Benzoni"}

**Sylvie Benzoni-Gavage** (born 1967) is a French mathematician known for her research in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), [traffic flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_flow), [shock waves](wikipedia://en/146253), and [phase transitions](wikipedia://en/54423). In 2017 she was named as the director of the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Benzoni was a student at the [École normale supérieure de Saint-Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Saint-Cloud). She completed her Ph.D. in 1991 at the [Claude Bernard University Lyon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Bernard_University_Lyon_1); her dissertation, supervised by [Denis Serre](wikipedia://en/45107336), was *Analyse numerique des modeles hydrodynamiques d'ecoulements diphasiques instationnaires dans les reseaux de production petroliere*.

She became a researcher at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) in 1992, and in 2003 became a professor at Claude Bernard University. After five years as assistant director at the Camille Jordan Institute in Lyon, she became director there in 2016.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

With her advisor Denis Serre, Benzoni is the author of *Multi-dimensional Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equations: First-Order Systems and Applications* (Oxford University Press, 2007) and the editor of *Hyperbolic Problems: Theory, Numerics, Applications* (Springer, 2008). She is the author of a French textbook on [differential calculus](wikipedia://en/50416) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), *Calcul différentiel et équations différentielles: Cours et exercices corrigés* (Dunod, 2010; 2nd ed., 2014).

Benzoni is also active in communicating mathematics to the public, through her work with the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470), and is a supporter of [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) publishing of research.
