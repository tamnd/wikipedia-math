# Giulio Pittarelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giulio Pittarelli"}

**Giulio Pittarelli** (3 February 1852, [Campochiaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campochiaro), [Campobasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campobasso) – 2 March 1934, Rome[^o2]) was an Italian mathematician, specializing in [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) and algebraic geometry.[^o3]

:::

Pittarelli received from the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples) his *laurea* in mathematics in 1874 and in engineering in 1876. For many years he was a professor of descriptive geometry at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome). In addition to his mathematical career, he was a painter, an excellent pianist, and an author, who wrote a biography of [Luigi Cremona](wikipedia://en/1846002).[^o3][^o2]

Pittarelli was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1908 in Rome.[^o4]
