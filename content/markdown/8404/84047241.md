# Advances in Computational Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Advances in Computational Mathematics"}

***Advances in Computational Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published bimonthly by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). Established in 1993 by founding [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) [Charles Anthony Micchelli](wikipedia://en/68219860) and John C. Mason,[^o3] it covers developments in [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833), with a focus on [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954).[^o4] Its current editor-in-chief is Alexander Barnett ([Flatiron Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatiron_Institute)).[^o5]

:::

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [EBSCO databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_Information_Services)[^o8]
- [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)[^o8]
- [ProQuest databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProQuest)[^o8]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o7]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o8]
- [zbMATH](wikipedia://en/1701961)[^o8]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2025 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.6.[^o9]
