# Alfred Enneper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Enneper"}

**Alfred Enneper** (June 14, 1830, [Barmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barmen) – March 24, 1885 [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover)) was a German mathematician. Enneper earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [Georg-August-Universität Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg-August-Universität_Göttingen) in 1856, under the supervision of [Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet](wikipedia://en/240002), for his dissertation about [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) with [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) arguments.[^o2][^o4] After his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1859 in Göttingen, he was from 1870 on Professor (Extraordinarius) at Göttingen.[^o4]

:::

He studied [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) and [parametrized](wikipedia://en/453198) [Enneper's minimal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneper's_minimal_surface) in 1864. A contemporary of [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459), the two created a whole class of parameterizations, the [Enneper–Weierstrass parameterization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneper–Weierstrass_parameterization).[^o5][^o6]
