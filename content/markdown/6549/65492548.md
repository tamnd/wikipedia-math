# Maximal surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maximal surface"}

In the mathematical field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a **maximal surface** is a certain kind of [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) of a [Lorentzian manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentzian_manifold). Precisely, given a Lorentzian manifold $(M, g)$, a maximal surface is a [spacelike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacelike) submanifold of $M$ whose [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210) is zero.[^o2] As such, maximal surfaces in Lorentzian geometry are directly analogous to [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243). The difference in terminology between the two settings has to do with the fact that small regions in maximal surfaces are [local maximizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_maxima) of the area [functional](wikipedia://en/1374948), while small regions in minimal surfaces are [local minimizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_minima) of the area functional.[^o3]

:::

In 1976, [Shiu-Yuen Cheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiu-Yuen_Cheng) and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) resolved the [Bernstein problem for maximal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernstein_problem_for_maximal_surfaces) of [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space) which are properly embedded, showing that any such [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) is a plane. This was part of the body of work for which Yau was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1982. The Bernstein problem was originally posed by [Eugenio Calabi](wikipedia://en/621000) in 1970, who proved some special cases of the result. Simple examples show that there are a number of hypersurfaces of Minkowski space of zero mean curvature which fail to be spacelike.[^o4]

By an extension of Cheng and Yau's methods, Kazuo Akutagawa considered the case of spacelike hypersurfaces of constant mean curvature in Lorentzian manifolds of positive [constant curvature](wikipedia://en/3638803), such as [de Sitter space](wikipedia://en/301666). Luis Alías, Alfonso Romero, and Miguel Sánchez proved a version of Cheng and Yau's result, replacing Minkowski space by the warped product of a [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) with an interval.

As a problem of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [Robert Bartnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Bartnik) and [Leon Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Simon) studied the boundary-value problem for maximal surfaces in Minkowski space. The general existence of maximal hypersurfaces in asymptotically flat Lorentzian manifolds, due to Bartnik, is significant in [Demetrios Christodoulou](wikipedia://en/2407999) and [Sergiu Klainerman](wikipedia://en/25808826)'s renowned proof of the nonlinear stability of Minkowski space under the [Einstein field equations](wikipedia://en/424540). They use a *maximal slicing* of a general spacetime; the same approach is common in [numerical relativity](wikipedia://en/1797708).[^o5]
