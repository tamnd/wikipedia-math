# James Thomas Beale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Thomas Beale"}

**James Thomas (J. Thomas "Tom") Beale** (born 1947) is an American mathematician, specializing in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o2]

:::

J. Thomas Beale grew up in [Savannah, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah,_Georgia).[^o3] In 1967 he graduated from [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (Caltech) with a B.S. in mathematics.[^o4] In 1973 he received his PhD in mathematics from Stanford University. His PhD thesis *Purely imaginary scattering frequencies for exterior domains*.[^o5] was written under the supervision of [Ralph S. Phillips](wikipedia://en/27858707). Soon after receiving his PhD Beale became a faculty member at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University). In 1983 he resigned from Tulane University and became a professor at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), where he retired as professor emeritus in 2016.[^o7]

In 1994 Beale was an invited speaker with talk *Analytical and numerical aspects of fluid interfaces* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.[^o8]

From June 28 to 30, 2010, the mathematics department of Duke University held a conference in his honor.[^o2]
