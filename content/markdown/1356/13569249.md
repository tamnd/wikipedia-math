# Felix Klein Protocols

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Klein Protocols"}

"**The Felix Klein Protocols**" is a collection of handwritten records of the [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) seminar lectures of [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) and his school. They span over 8000 pages in 29 volumes, and are regarded as one of the richest records of mathematical activity in modern times.[^o3] The previously unpublished Klein Protocols were made available digitally in 2006.[^o4] The collection has been compiled by Eugene Chislenko and [Yuri Tschinkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Tschinkel).

:::

A searchable index of the protocols can be found at [*Felix Klein Protokolle*](http://page.mi.fu-berlin.de/moritz/klein/).

## Years covered {#Years_covered}

From 1872 to 1896 Klein conducted his seminars alone, mainly in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210). The years 1897–1913 show collaborations with mathematicians such as [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), [Karl Schwarzschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Schwarzschild), [Ludwig Prandtl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Prandtl), [Carl Runge](wikipedia://en/747636) and [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537).
