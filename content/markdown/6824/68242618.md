# Group analysis of differential equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Group analysis of differential equations"}

**Group analysis of differential equations** is a branch of mathematics that studies the symmetry properties of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) with respect to various transformations of independent and dependent variables. It includes methods and applied aspects of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and [algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) theory, [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and is, in turn, a powerful research tool in theories of [ODEs](wikipedia://en/32742753), [PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564), [mathematical](wikipedia://en/173416) and [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
