# Tianyi Zheng

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tianyi Zheng"}

**Tianyi Zheng** is a Chinese-American mathematician specializing in [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), including the theory of [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) and [harmonic functions](wikipedia://en/55762) on groups. She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zheng was an undergraduate mathematics student at [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University), graduating in 2008. She completed a Ph.D. in 2013 at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), with the dissertation *Random Walks On Some Classes Of Solvable Groups* advised by [Laurent Saloff-Coste](wikipedia://en/45608368).

She became a postdoctoral Szegő Assistant Professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 2013 to 2016 before obtaining a regular-rank faculty position as assistant professor at the University of California, San Diego in 2016.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Zheng was named a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow) in 2019. She was an invited speaker in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the 2022 (virtual) [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

In 2024, she was a recipient of the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize), given "for her deep results and resolution of long-standing conjectures on random walks on groups".
