# Ryll-Nardzewski fixed-point theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ryll-Nardzewski fixed-point theorem"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a branch of mathematics, the **Ryll-Nardzewski fixed-point theorem** states that if $E$ is a [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) and $K$ is a nonempty [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) subset of $E$ that is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) under the [weak topology](wikipedia://en/33662), then every [group](wikipedia://en/19447) (or equivalently: every [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799)) of [affine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_map) [isometries](wikipedia://en/254777) of $K$ has at least one fixed point. (Here, a *fixed point* of a set of maps is a point that is [fixed](wikipedia://en/449738) by each map in the set.)

:::

This theorem was announced by [Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski](wikipedia://en/1926283).[^o2] Later Namioka and Asplund [^o3] gave a proof based on a different approach. Ryll-Nardzewski himself gave a complete proof in the original spirit.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The Ryll-Nardzewski theorem yields the existence of a [Haar measure](wikipedia://en/40645) on compact groups.[^o7]
