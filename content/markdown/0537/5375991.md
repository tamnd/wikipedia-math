# Mertens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mertens"}

**Mertens** () is a [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) of [Flemish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flanders) origin, meaning "son of Merten" ([Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_%28name%29)). It is the fifth most common name in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) with 18,518 people in 2008.

:::

## Geographical distribution {#Geographical_distribution}

As of 2014, 43.4% of all known bearers of the surname *Mertens* were residents of [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) (frequency 1:2,728), 34.8% of [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) (1:487), 8.8% of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) (1:60,847), 5.9% of the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) (1:4,188), 1.7% of [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) (1:57,632) and 1.0% of [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil) (1:299,871).

In [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), the frequency of the surname was higher than national average (1:487) only in one region: [Flemish Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Region) (1:367).

In [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), the frequency of the surname was higher than national average (1:2,728) in the following regions:

1. [North Rhine-Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Rhine-Westphalia) (1:970)
1. [Saxony-Anhalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxony-Anhalt) (1:1,361)

In the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), the frequency of the surname was higher than national average (1:4,188) in the following provinces:.[^o3]

1. [Limburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limburg_%28Netherlands%29) (1:959)
1. [North Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Brabant) (1:2,002)
