# Christine Guenther

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christine Guenther"}

**Christine Guenther** is an American mathematician known for her research on the [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), including the [Ricci flow](wikipedia://en/288291). She is a distinguished professor of mathematics at [Pacific University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_University).

:::

Guenther graduated from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1989 with a bachelor's degree in music. After earning a master's degree in mathematics in 1993 from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), she completed her Ph.D. in 1998 at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), under the supervision of [James A. Isenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_A._Isenberg). She joined the Pacific University faculty in 1988 and became a Distinguished University Professor at Pacific University in 2016.

She is a co-author of the four-volume book series *Ricci Flow: Techniques and Applications* (American Mathematical Society, 2007, 2008, 2010, and 2015) and of *[Extrinsic Geometric Flows](wikipedia://en/72029076)* ([Graduate Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871519) 206, 2020).
