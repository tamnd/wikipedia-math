# Dorina Mitrea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dorina Mitrea"}

**Dorina Irena-Rita Mitrea** (born April 30, 1965) is a Romanian-American mathematician known for her work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and the theory of [distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29), and in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471). She is a professor of mathematics and former chair of the mathematics department at [Baylor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baylor_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mitrea earned a master's degree in 1987 from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). Her thesis, *Riemann’s Theorem for Simply Connected Riemann Surfaces*, was supervised by [Cabiria Andreian Cazacu](wikipedia://en/61778977). She completed her doctorate in 1996 from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Her dissertation, *Layer Potential Operators and Boundary Value Problems for Differential Forms on Lipschitz Domains*, was supervised by Eugene Barry Fabes.

Mitrea joined the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) mathematics faculty in 1996, and became M. & R. Houchins Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at the University of Missouri in 2016. She moved to Baylor as professor and chair in 2019.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mitrea was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

She is married to Marius Mitrea. Her husband is also a mathematician, and moved with Mitrea from Missouri to Baylor.
