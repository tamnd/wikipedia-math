# Tanya Christiansen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tanya Christiansen"}

**Tanya Julie Christiansen** is an American mathematician who works in [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory) and the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). She is Luther Marion Defoe Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Christiansen graduated *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors)* in 1989 from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), with a bachelor's degree in mathematics. She completed her Ph.D. in 1993 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Her dissertation, *Scattering Theory on Compact Manifolds with Boundary*, was supervised by [Richard Burt Melrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burt_Melrose).

After postdoctoral positions at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) and [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), she joined the University of Missouri faculty in 1995. She became Defoe Distinguished Professor in 2019.
