# Hal S. Stern

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hal S. Stern"}

**Hal Stern** is an American statistician, with a focus on [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412) and is one of the authors of the textbook *Bayesian Data Analysis*.[^o3]

:::

Stern has developed several statistical methods in [forensic science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_science).[^o4] He also participates in the Forensic Science Advisory Committee of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o5] He is a part of the leadership team of the Center for Statistics and Applications in Forensic Evidence (CSAFE).[^o6][^o4]

He is the Provost and Executive Vice Chancellor at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) (UCI) and Chancellor's Professor in the Department of Statistics.[^o7]

Stern is a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/51140705),[^o8] [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312).[^o9] In 2022, Stern received the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o10]
