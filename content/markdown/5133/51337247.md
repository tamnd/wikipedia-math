# Sreenivasa Rao Jammalamadaka

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sreenivasa Rao Jammalamadaka"}

**Sreenivasa Rao Jammalamadaka**, also known as **J.S. Rao**, is an Indian-American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who is a Distinguished Professor in the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and Applied Probability at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara) (UCSB).[^o3][^o4] His research has focused on several areas including nonparametric statistical inference,[^o5] goodness-of-fit testing,[^o6] [inference based on spacings](wikipedia://en/20410675),[^o7] statistics of directional data in two- three- and higher dimensions,[^o8] probability and [large-sample theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large-sample_theory),[^o9] and [linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_models).[^o10] He has also worked on [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571)[^o11] and development of [statistical methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_methodology) in disciplines such as [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology) and [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology). He has held professional and visiting appointments at universities and research institutions in the United States, Europe, Australia, and Asia. His honors include fellowships of most major statistical societies and "Lifetime Achievement Awards" from the Indian Society for Probability and Statistics (2019)[^o12] and most recently from the International Indian Statistical Association (2025).[^o13]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Jammalamadaka was born in a small village called [Munipalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munipalle,_Uppalaguptam_Mandal), in [Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh), India. He was educated for a year at the YRS and VRN College in Chirala, followed by the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Kolkata (B. Stat.1964, M.Stat. 1965, and Ph.D. 1969), while serving as a Research Scholar there during 1965-1969.

## Career {#Career}

Jammalamadaka began his academic career at the Department of Mathematics, [Indiana University, Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University,_Bloomington), where he served as a Visiting Assistant Professor and Assistant Professor from 1969 to 1976. In 1976, he joined the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), as an Assistant Professor, progressing thro the ranks to become a Full Professor in 1983,[^o16] and was appointed to the special rank of Distinguished Professor in 2007.[^o4]

Jammalamadaka held visiting positions at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds), [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Bombay), [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology), the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg), the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), the [Curtin University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtin_University_of_Technology), the [University of Malaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malaya), [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology), the [University of Trento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trento), the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland), the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), and the [Gandhi Institute of Technology and Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhi_Institute_of_Technology_and_Management) (GITAM), in India.[^o17][^o16]

At UCSB, he served as vice-chairman of the Department of Mathematics, Director of the Statistical Consulting Center that he helped establish, Graduate Advisor in statistics, and as the first Chairman of the Department of Statistics and Applied Probability that he had a major role in getting started. He also served on several UCSB Academic Senate committees, including the Council on Planning and Budget, the Committee on Academic Personnel, and the Council on Faculty Issues and Awards.[^o17]

Jammalamadaka's doctoral students, numbering over 50 at this point, include such well-known names as Martin Wells at Cornell, and Siddhartha Chib at the Olin Business School in Washington Univ, St. Louis. They have worked on topics including nonparametric testing, goodness-of-fit procedures, directional statistics, reliability theory, Bayesian methods, spatial statistics, financial modeling, image analysis, machine learning, and statistical inference. His teaching has included [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [statistical theory](wikipedia://en/27579), [sampling techniques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_techniques), [order statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_statistics), [experimental design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_design), [time-series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-series_analysis), [multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis), [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), [directional data analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directional_data), [statistical decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216), and [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), besides math courses like [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) and [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422).[^o18]

Jammalamadaka is the author of over 200 publications and has written 3 scholarly books and edited conference proceedings and other special volumes \\https://jammalam.faculty.pstat.ucsb.edu/. For his research guidance and mentoring activity at UCSB and worldwide, the University of California, SB recognized him in 2017 with an Outstanding Graduate Mentor Award.[^o19] His students and co-workers brought out a [Festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in his honor in 2011.[^o20]

## Research and scholarly works {#Research_and_scholarly_works}

Jammalamadaka's research has covered several areas of [theoretical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_statistics) and [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics). His work includes [nonparametric tests](wikipedia://en/223369),[^o5], [asymptotic efficiencies](wikipedia://en/3107)[^o22] statistical inference based on spacings, and statistical methods for circular and directional data.[^o23]

His work on [directional statistics](wikipedia://en/1444970) addressed inference for observations represented by directions or angles and included [goodness-of-fit tests](wikipedia://en/2474821),[^o6] correlation and regression methods,[^o24] and development of probability models for such data. His research interests also include limit distribution theory, asymptotic efficiencies of statistical tests, and linear models.

A recurring theme in his research has been the use of spacings the differences between ordered observations in [statistical inference](wikipedia://en/27577).[^o25] His publications have examined the asymptotic properties and efficiencies of tests based on spacings,[^o22] including applications to goodness-of-fit and two-sample problems.[^o6]

Another major area of his work is directional and circular statistics.[^o26] His research has included statistical inference for [circular distributions](wikipedia://en/1518031), tests of uniformity, circular regression, wrapped probability distributions, skew-symmetric circular models, and statistical methods for spherical data.[^o27] Later work extended these methods to applications involving biological and medical data, including circular functional analysis of [Optical Coherence Tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_Coherence_Tomography) (OCT) data, and analysis of [retinal nerve fiber layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retinal_nerve_fiber_layer) measurements to explore connections to problems like [glaucoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaucoma).[^o28]

His research has also addressed goodness-of-fit testing, Bayesian and semiparametric inference, censored lifetime data, general linear models, multivariate statistics, time-series methods, and machine-learning-related statistical models. His publications include work on [Bayesian structural time-series models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_structural_time_series),[^o11] mixture models,[^o29] [generalized linear models](wikipedia://en/747122),[^o30] lifetime-data analysis,[^o31] and statistical methods for multimodal and high-dimensional data. His later publications include studies of circular and spherical distributions,[^o32] multivariate skewness[^o33] and [kurtosis](wikipedia://en/16848), and applications of circular statistics to biological data as in chronobiology.

He has authored or coauthored books including *Essential Statistics* (1998), *Topics in Circular Statistics* (2001),[^o34] *Linear Models, An Integrated Approach* (2003),[^o10] *Essential Statistics with Python and R* (2019), and *Linear Models and Regression with R* (2019).[^o35]

Jammalamadaka has served on editorial boards of several statistical journals. He founded and directed the Statistical Consulting Center (StatLab) at UCSB and has provided statistical consulting on problems ranging from environmental studies and engineering to medical and biological research.[^o36]
