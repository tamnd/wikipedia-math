# Bakhadyr Khoussainov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bakhadyr Khoussainov"}

**Bakhadyr M. Khoussainov** (Бахадыр Хусаинов)[^o2] is a [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who was born and educated in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), works in the fields of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414), [computable model theory](wikipedia://en/11574361) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).[^o3] With [Anil Nerode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anil_Nerode), he is the co-founder of the theory of automatic structures,[^o4] which is an extension of the theory of [automatic groups](wikipedia://en/4224977).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Khoussainov received his undergraduate degree from the Mathematics Department of [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1984. In 1988, he received his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree (equivalent to a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy)) in Algebra and Logic from [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) with the supervision of an Academician of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) Sergei Goncharov.

In 1991 Khoussainov joined [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and held an H.C. Wang Assistant Professorship at the Mathematics Department from 1995 to 1997.

In 1996 Khoussainov joined the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), and remained until 2021 when he moved to the [UESTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Electronic_Science_and_Technology_of_China).

Khoussainov has held visiting positions at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880), [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), [JAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Advanced_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) and the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison).

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Khoussainov is a co-winner (together with [Cristian Calude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristian_S._Calude), Sanjay Jain, Wei Li and Frank Stephan) of the [STOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Theory_of_Computing) 2017 best paper award[^o7] for a [quasi-polynomial time](wikipedia://en/25269049) algorithm deciding [parity games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parity_game). For this work, Khoussainov and this paper's co-authors were awarded the 2021 EATCS-IPEC [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize).[^o8] In 2023 while working in the UESTC, China, Khoussainov has also been elected as a foreign member of The [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea), the European Academy of Sciences, Humanities, Letters, and Law.

A fellow of the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Te_Apārangi),[^o9] Khoussainov has also received:

- the 2020 [Humboldt prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize);[^o10]
- the 2019 Aitken Lecturership;[^o11]
- multiple [JSPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Society_for_the_Promotion_of_Science) Invitation Fellowships (2001, 2012, and 2014);[^o12]
- multiple [Marsden Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsden_grant) grants (2001, 2004, 2008 and 2012);[^o13]
- the 2002 Research Excellence Award of the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society).[^o14]
