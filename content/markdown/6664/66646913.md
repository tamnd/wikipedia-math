# Pallavi Dani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pallavi Dani"}

**Pallavi Dani** is an Indian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and an associate professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University) (LSU) in [Baton Rouge, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge,_Louisiana). Her research area is [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164); in particular, she studies [quasi-isometry](wikipedia://en/12747259) [invariants](wikipedia://en/1126638) of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dani received a B.Sc. degree in mathematics from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai) in [Mumbai, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai,_India). She earned an M.S. in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2001 and her Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Chicago in 2005. Her dissertation "Statistical properties of elements in infinite group" was directed by [Benson Farb](wikipedia://en/32518776).

After completing her Ph.D. at University of Chicago, Dani held postdoctoral positions at the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma) in [Norman, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman,_Oklahoma) and [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University) in [Atlanta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta,_Georgia). Dani joined the tenure stream faculty at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University) (LSU) in 2009. She is a professor of mathematics at LSU.

Dani delivered a series of invited lectures on geometric group theory at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) in June 2015.

She served on the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)-Simons Travel Grants Committee from 2015 to 2017.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2016, the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) awarded Dani the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910). The prize, awarded annually to a recently promoted associate professor, allows the recipient to spend a semester in the mathematics department at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in [Ithaca, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca,_New_York) without teaching obligations. Dani spent the spring 2017 semester at Cornell, where she worked with Tim Riley and interacted with other Cornell faculty members.[^o8]

Dani's research has been supported by grants from the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation). She received a [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) Collaboration Grant to support her research from 2016 to 2018.
