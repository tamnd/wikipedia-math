# Jan Trlifaj

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Trlifaj"}

**Jan Trlifaj** (born 30 December 1954) is a Czech mathematician. He is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) whose research interests include [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).[^o3]

:::

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Jan Trlifaj studied mathematics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University), from which he received MSc. in 1979, Ph.D. in 1989 under Ladislav Bican.[^o5] and Prof. of Mathematics in the field Algebra and number theory in 2009.

In the academic year 1994/95 he had the position as Postdoctoral Fellow of the Royal Society at Department of Mathematics at University of Manchester. In Fall 1998 he received the J.W.Fulbright Scholarship at the Department of Mathematics, University California at Irvine. During Fall 2002 and 2006 he was a visiting professor at [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525), Barcelona. Since 1990, he has completed numerous short term visiting appointments and given over 100 invited lectures at conferences and seminars worldwide.[^o6]

Since 2017, he is Fellow of [Learned Society of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_the_Czech_Republic).[^o7]

He served in the organizing committee of 18th International Conference on Representations of Algebras (ICRA 2018), held for 250 participants from 34 countries in August 2018 in Prague, Czech Republic.[^o8]

He has been elected Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) in 2020, for contributions to homological algebra and tilting theory for non finitely generated modules.[^o9]

He serves as Member of the Science board for Neuron prize that is awarded to best Czech scientists by Neuron Endowment Fund.[^o10]
