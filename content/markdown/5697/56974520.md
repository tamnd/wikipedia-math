# Albin Herzog

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Albin Herzog"}

**Albin Herzog** (1852–1909) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was director of the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Herzog studied in the high schools of [Steckborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steckborn) and [Frauenfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frauenfeld).[^n1] Between 1870 and 1874 he studied mathematics al [Polytechnicum of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich)[^n2] and in 1875 he was awarded a doctorate from the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich). The same year he began teaching at Polytechnicum of Zurich, where he remained the rest of his life, as director of the Mechanical Technical Department and General Manager from 1895 to 1899.[^n1] During his time as general manager, [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736) flunked the entrance exam, failing in French, chemistry, and biology, but Herzog seeing his extraordinary marks in physics and mathematics, recommended Einstein spend a year in the cantonalschule of [Aarau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarau), promising to admit him the following year.[^n3]

Herzog was an outstanding professor who connected the mathematical basis of mechanics with his practical applications.[^n4] He also published several articles on mathematical mechanics.

Besides his academic activities, Herzog was president of the escolar commission (1889–1993) and councilor of the city of Zurich.[^n2]
