# 25 great circles of the spherical octahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="25 great circles of the spherical octahedron"}

*Image: 25 great circles colored.png.* The 25 great circles with domains colored by their symmetry positions

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **25 great circles of the spherical octahedron** is an arrangement of 25 [great circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_circles) in [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805).[^o2] It was first identified by [Buckminster Fuller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckminster_Fuller) and is used in construction of [geodesic domes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_dome).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The 25 great circles can be seen in 3 sets: 12, 9, and 4, each representing edges of a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) projected onto a sphere. Nine great circles represent the edges of a [disdyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/724752), the dual of a [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879). Four more great circles represent the edges of a [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), and the last twelve great circles connect edge-centers of the octahedron to centers of other triangles.
