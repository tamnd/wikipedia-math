# Eric Katz

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Eric Katz"}

**Eric Katz** is a mathematician working in [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/386468) [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He is currently a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor)[^o4] in the Department of Mathematics at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).

:::

In joint work with [Karim Adiprasito](wikipedia://en/47874333) and [June Huh](wikipedia://en/54434626), he resolved the Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture on the log-concavity of the [characteristic polynomial of matroids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_polynomial_of_matroids).[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8] With [Joseph Rabinoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Rabinoff) and [David Zureick-Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Zureick-Brown), he has given bounds on rational and [torsion points on curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsion_points_on_curves).[^o9]

## Education {#Education}

Katz went to [Beachwood High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beachwood_High_School), in [Beachwood, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beachwood,_Ohio), a suburb of [Cleveland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland). After earning a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Mathematics from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1999, he pursued graduate studies at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), obtaining his [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 2004 with a thesis written under the direction of [Yakov Eliashberg](wikipedia://en/20652422) and [Ravi Vakil](wikipedia://en/5981037).
