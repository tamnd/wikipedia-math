# Total subset

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Total subset"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a subset $T$ of a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) $X$ is said to be a **total** subset of $X$ if the [linear span](wikipedia://en/56353) of $T$ is a [dense subset](wikipedia://en/23523594) of $X.$[^n1] This condition arises frequently in many theorems of functional analysis.

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Unbounded [self-adjoint operators](wikipedia://en/187408) on [Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/20598932) are defined on total subsets.

:::
