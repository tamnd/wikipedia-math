# Erdős sumset conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erdős sumset conjecture"}

In [additive combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243392), the **Erdős sumset conjecture** is a conjecture which states that if a subset $A$ of the [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) $\mathbb{N}$ has a positive upper [density](wikipedia://en/1819983) then there are two infinite subsets $B$ and $C$ of $\mathbb{N}$ such that $A$ contains the [sumset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumset) $B+C$.[^o2][^o3] It was posed by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), and was proven in 2019 in a paper by Joel Moreira, Florian Richter and Donald Robertson.[^o4]

:::
