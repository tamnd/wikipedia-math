# Pieranna Garavaso

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pieranna Garavaso"}

**Pieranna Garavaso** is an [analytic philosopher](wikipedia://en/159211) and professor emerita at the [University of Minnesota Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota_Morris).[^o2] Her areas of interest include [epistemological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology) and [metaphysical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) issues in [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741), [Gottlob Frege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotlob_Frege), [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), and [feminist epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminist_epistemology). She received her doctorate in philosophy from the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln). She is the recipient of two distinguished teaching awards: the University of Minnesota, Morris Alumni Association Teaching Award in 2003[^o3] and the Horace T. Morse University of Minnesota Alumni in 2004.[^o4]

:::
