# Krikor Peshtimaldjian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krikor Peshtimaldjian"}

**Krikor Peshtimaldjian** (Գրիգոր Փեշտիմալջյան, Born [Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople), [Ottoman Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Turkey) 1778 - died [Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople), [Ottoman Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Turkey) January, 1839) was an ethnic [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_people) philosopher, educator, translator, and linguist.[^o2] He was a key figure in the [Armenian national awakening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_national_awakening) and reformist movement in the 19th century.[^o3][^o4]
