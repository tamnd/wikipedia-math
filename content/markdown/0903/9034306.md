# Félix Pollaczek

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Félix Pollaczek"}

**Félix Pollaczek** (1 December 1892 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) – 29 April 1981 at [Boulogne-Billancourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulogne-Billancourt)) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria)-[French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) engineer and mathematician, known for numerous contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He is best known for the [Pollaczek&ndash;Khinchine formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollaczek&ndash;Khinchine_formula) in [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578) (1930), and the [Meixner-Pollaczek polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meixner-Pollaczek_polynomials).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pollaczek studied at the [Technical University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Vienna), got a [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from [Technical University of Brno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Brno) (1920), and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) (1922) with a dissertation titled *Über die Kreiskörper der l-ten und l<sup>2</sup>-ten Einheitswurzeln*, advised by [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522) and based on results published first in 1917.[^o6][^o7]

Pollaczek was employed by [AEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEG_%28German_company%29) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) (1921–23), and later worked for [Reichspost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichspost) (1923–33). In 1933, he was fired because he was Jewish.[^o4] He moved to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), where he was consulting [teletraffic engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletraffic_engineer) to various institutions from 1933 onwards, including the Société d'Études pour Liaisons Téléphoniques et Télégraphiques (SELT) and the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS).[^o2]

In 1977, Pollaczek was awarded the [John von Neumann Theory Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Theory_Prize), although his age prevented him from receiving the prize in person. He was posthumously elected to the 2002 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He married mathematician [Hilda Geiringer](wikipedia://en/7553047) in 1921, and they had a child, Magda, in 1922.[^o10] However, their marriage did not last, and Magda was brought up by Hilda. Pollaczek became physicist [László Tisza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/László_Tisza)'s father-in-law due to Magda's marriage.
