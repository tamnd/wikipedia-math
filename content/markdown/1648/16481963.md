# Lefschetz duality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lefschetz duality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Lefschetz duality** is a version of [Poincaré duality](wikipedia://en/540732) in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), applying to a [manifold with boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_with_boundary). Such a formulation was introduced by (txt), at the same time introducing [relative homology](wikipedia://en/1248647), for application to the [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](wikipedia://en/582530).[^o3] There are now numerous formulations of Lefschetz duality or **Poincaré–Lefschetz duality**, or **Alexander–Lefschetz duality**.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Formulations"}

## Formulations {#Formulations}

Let *M* be an [orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientable) [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) of dimension *n*, with boundary $\partial(M)$, and let $z\in H_n(M,\partial(M); \mathbb{Z})$ be the [fundamental class](wikipedia://en/669440) of the manifold *M*. Then [cap product](wikipedia://en/5230810) with *z* (or its dual class in cohomology) induces a pairing of the (co)[homology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_group) of *M* and the relative (co)homology of the pair $(M,\partial(M))$. Furthermore, this gives rise to isomorphisms of $H^k(M,\partial(M); \mathbb{Z})$ with $H_{n-k}(M; \mathbb{Z})$, and of $H_k(M,\partial(M); \mathbb{Z})$ with $H^{n-k}(M; \mathbb{Z})$ for all $k$.[^o6]

Here $\partial(M)$ can in fact be empty, so Poincaré duality appears as a special case of Lefschetz duality.

There is a version for triples. Let $\partial(M)$ decompose into subspaces *A* and *B*, themselves compact orientable manifolds with common boundary *Z*, which is the intersection of *A* and *B*. Then, for each $k$, there is an isomorphism[^o7]

$$D_M\colon H^k(M,A; \mathbb{Z})\to H_{n-k}(M,B; \mathbb{Z}).$$
