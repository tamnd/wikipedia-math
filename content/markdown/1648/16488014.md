# Yuri Gurevich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Gurevich"}

*Image: Yuri Gurevich ETH Zurich cropped.JPG.* Gurevich at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Federal_Institute_of_Technology_Zürich) in May 2004, photograph by [Bertrand Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Meyer)

**Yuri Gurevich**, [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), is an American [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and the inventor of [abstract state machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_state_machines).

:::

Gurevich was born and educated in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^o2] He taught mathematics there and then in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) before moving to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1982. The best-known work of his Soviet period is on the *classical decision problem*.[^o3] In Israel, Gurevich worked with [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) on [monadic](wikipedia://en/8015680) [second-order](wikipedia://en/341428) [theories](wikipedia://en/3512103).[^o4] The *Forgetful Determinacy Theorem* of Gurevich–[Harrington](wikipedia://en/5693354) is of that period as well.[^o5]

From 1982 to 1998, Gurevich taught [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), where he started to work on various aspects of computational complexity theory[^o6] including average case complexity.[^o7] He became one of the founders of the emerging field of [finite model theory](wikipedia://en/1773596).[^o8]

Most importantly, he became interested in the problem of what an [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) is. This led him to the theory of [abstract state machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_state_machines) (ASMs). The ASM Thesis says that, behaviorally, every algorithm is an ASM.[^o9] A few convincing axioms enabled derivation of the sequential ASM thesis[^o10] and the Church–Turing thesis.[^o11] The ASM thesis has also been proven for some other classes of algorithms.[^o12][^o13]

From 1998 to 2018, Gurevich was with [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) where he founded a group on Foundations of Software Engineering. The group built [Spec Explorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spec_Explorer) based on the theory of abstract state machines. The tool was adopted by the [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows) team; a modified version of the tool helped [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft) meet the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) demands for high-level executable specifications. Later, Gurevich worked with different Microsoft groups on various efficiency, safety, and security issues,[^o14] including access control,[^o15] differential compression,[^o16] and privacy.[^o17]

Since 1988, Gurevich has managed the column on Logic in Computer Science in the Bulletin of the European Association for Theoretical Computer Science.[^o2] Since 2013 Gurevich has worked primarily on [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220),[^o18] while continuing research in his traditional areas.

Gurevich is a 2020 [AAAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) Fellow,[^o19] a 1997 [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow),[^o20] a 1995 [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship),[^o21] an inaugural fellow of the [European Association for Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_for_Theoretical_Computer_Science),[^o22] a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea), and Dr. [Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_Causa) of [Hasselt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasselt_University) in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) and of [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University) in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).
