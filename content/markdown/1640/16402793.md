# Idempotent measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Idempotent measure"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **idempotent measure** on a [metric group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_group) is a [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437) that equals its [convolution](wikipedia://en/7519) with itself; in other words, an idempotent measure is an [idempotent element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent_element) in the [topological semigroup](wikipedia://en/17359213) of probability measures on the given metric group.

:::

Explicitly, given a metric group *X* and two probability measures *μ* and *ν* on *X*, the convolution *μ* ∗ *ν* of *μ* and *ν* is the measure given by

$$(\mu * \nu) (A) = \int_{X} \mu (A x^{-1}) \, \mathrm{d} \nu (x) = \int_{X} \nu (x^{-1} A) \, \mathrm{d} \mu (x)$$

for any Borel subset *A* of *X*. (The equality of the two integrals follows from [Fubini's theorem](wikipedia://en/255245).) With respect to the topology of [weak convergence of measures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_convergence_of_measures), the operation of convolution makes the space of probability measures on *X* into a topological semigroup. Thus, *μ* is said to be an idempotent measure if *μ* ∗ *μ* = *μ*.

It can be shown that the only idempotent probability measures on a [complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_space), [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) metric group are the normalized [Haar measures](wikipedia://en/40645) of [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011).
