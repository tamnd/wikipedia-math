# Serge Massar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serge Massar"}

**Serge Alexandre Massar** (born 11 February 1970)[^o3][^o4] is a Belgian physicist. He studies [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory), [nonlinear optics](wikipedia://en/21723), [optical neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_neural_network), and [reservoir computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_computing).[^o4][^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Serge Massar was born in [Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambia) in 1970.[^o4] He obtained a degree in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), then a PhD under the direction of [Robert Brout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Brout) from the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) (ULB) in 1991 and 1995, respectively.[^o4] He completed his post-doctoral research at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) from 1995 to 1997, and subsequently at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) from 1997 to 1998.[^o4][^o6]

## Career {#Career}

In 1998, Massar returned to ULB as a Research Associate of the [FRS-FNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRS-FNRS).[^o4] In 2008 he became the Research Director of the [FRS-FNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRS-FNRS).[^o4]

In 2012, Massar integrated into the ULB faculty, holding the rank of "Professeur Ordinaire." His tenure as the Physics Department Director at ULB spanned 2014 to 2015.[^o4] Additionally, he has been at the head of the Laboratoire d’Information Quantique at ULB since 2004.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Massar's recognition in the field includes awards such as the 2003 Alcatel-Bell Prize, the 2010 La Recherche Prize, and the best paper award at the [Symposium on Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Theory_of_Computing) in 2012.[^o4]

He has been a member of the [Royal Academy of Science, Letters, and Fine Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Science,_Letters_and_Fine_Arts_of_Belgium) since 2021.[^o3]

In 2023, he received the [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize) for research on [extension complexity](wikipedia://en/68641808).[^o9]
