# Tate pairing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tate pairing"}

In mathematics, **Tate pairing** is any of several closely related [bilinear pairings](wikipedia://en/8605953) involving [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) or [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties), usually over [local](wikipedia://en/145375) or [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615), based on the [Tate duality](wikipedia://en/33900144) pairings introduced by (txt) and extended by (Lichtenbaum 1969). (Rück Frey 1994) applied the Tate pairing over finite fields to cryptography.

:::
