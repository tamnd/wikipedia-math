# Juan de Yciar

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Juan de Yciar"}

**Juan de Yciar** or **Iciar** (16th century) was a [calligraphist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calligraphy) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) active in [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza) in the middle of the 16th century.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Little is known about the life of Juan de Yciar and it is known by his self-explanations in the preface of one of his books.[^o4]

Born in Durango (north of actual Spain) c. 1523, he went to [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza) at a young age,[^o5] probably due to some familiar adverse circumstances.[^o6] When he was fifty years old, he was ordered priest and he was living in [Logroño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logroño),[^o7] where it is supposed he died sometime after 1573.

Yciar is known mainly by his work on calligraphy, edited first time in [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza) in 1548[^o8] titled *Recopilacion subtilissima: intitulada Ortographia pratica: por la qual se enseña á escreuir perfectamente*,[^o9] which was extended, modified and reedited many times, with different titles, during the 16th century. It is a book to teach writing in which there are from pedagogic rules[^o10] to different types of letters,[^o11] going by formulas to make inks or ways to cut quills.[^o12] The success of the book resides in the fact that codification of a standard legible hand was indispensable to the functioning of an imperial bureaucracy that the rising Spanish Empire needed.[^o13] The plate engravings had been made by the French engraver Juan de Vingles, who worked in several Spanish towns during the 16th century.[^o14] The editions of 1564 and onward had attached and annex on arithmetic written by [Juan Gutiérrez de Gualda](wikipedia://en/48348780).

In 1549, and also in Zaragoza, he published a book on elementary arithmetic, with pedagogical goals, titled *Arithmetica practica, muy util y provechoso para toda persona que quisiere ejercitar se en aprender a contar*.[^o15][^o16] Despite the small success of this book, it is also a sample of the learning needs of the tradesmen in an era of growing of trade.[^o17]
