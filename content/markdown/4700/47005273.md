# Demographics of Leicester

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Leicester"}

*Image: Population Density Leicester 2011 Census.png.* Population density in the 2011 census in Leicester.

**[Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester)**, [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) is an ethnically and culturally diverse city. It is the thirteenth most populous city in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).

*Image: Leicester industry sectors over time.svg.* Industry sectors over time in Leicester

:::

## Population {#Population}

Leicester's total population, according to the [2011 UK census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_UK_census), was 329,839. The population density was 4,494 people per square km.

## Ethnicity {#Ethnicity}

*Image: Ethnic demography of Leicester from 1961 to 2021.gif.* Ethnic demography of Leicester from 1961 to 2021

*Image: Population pyramid of Leicester by ethnicity in 2021.svg.* Population pyramid of Leicester by ethnicity in 2021

*Image: Ethnic makeup of Leicester by single year ages in 2021.svg.* Ethnic makeup of Leicester by single year ages in 2021

The following table shows the ethnic group of respondents in the 2001 and 2011 censuses in Leicester. The local district has been in a majority-minority state since around 2008[^o5] and was reported in the 2011 census to be that, with the [White British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_British) population at 45% of the population of Leicester.[^o6][^o7] [Asian British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Asian) residents, especially [Indians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Indians), have risen since post-war migration to the UK began, famously [Asians from Uganda who were expelled in 1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expulsion_of_Asians_from_Uganda).[^o6] In 1991, Asians as a broad multi-ethnic group made up nearly a quarter of the city's population but have risen to above a third of the population at 37.1%. Black British people have also risen as a group with the increase mostly coming from Black Africans who have risen from 0.3% of the city's population to nearly 4%.

### Ethnicity of school pupils {#Ethnicity_of_school_pupils}

The ethnicity of school pupils within Leicester has been in flux and in a [majority-minority](wikipedia://en/65970268) state since statistics have been first collected in 2003. For instance, the most profound change can be seen with the [White British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_British), who have declined from 45.6% in 2004 to 23.4% in 2022. [Asian British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Asian) school pupils have risen in percentage, going from 37.9% of the areas school pupil population to a near majority (47%). All other groups have grown in size as well. [Other White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Other_White) school pupils have risen from 1.7% to 7.8%, [Black British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_British_people) school pupils have risen from 6.4% to 10.4% in 2016 but then declined to 9.5% in 2022, [Mixed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_%28United_Kingdom_ethnicity_category%29) have risen from 5.5% to 7.5% and Other ethnicities have gone from 0.8% to 2.7%.
