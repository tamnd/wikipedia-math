# Shepherd (name)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shepherd"}

**Shepherd** is a [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname), [cognate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognate) of the English word "[Shepherd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepherd)". Several common spelling variations exist, including **Shepperd**, **Sheperd**, **Shephard**, **Sheppard**, and **Shepard**.
