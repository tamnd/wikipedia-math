# Florian Pop

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Florian Pop"}

*Image: Florian Pop.jpg.* Florian Pop at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2006

**Florian Pop** (born 1952 in [Zalău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalău)) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician, a [professor of mathematics](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

:::

Pop received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1987 and his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1991, both from the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg). He has been a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), and (from 1996 to 2003) a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) prior to joining the University of Pennsylvania faculty.[^o3]

Pop's research concerns [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265), and [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). (Kuhlmann Kuhlmann Marshall 2003) call his habilitation thesis, concerning the [characterization](wikipedia://en/780886) of certain [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) by their [absolute Galois groups](wikipedia://en/2996781), a "milestone".[^o5]

In 1996, Pop was awarded the [Gay-Lussac&ndash;von Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay-Lussac&ndash;von_Humboldt_Prize) for Mathematics,[^o6] and in 2003 he was awarded the [Romanian Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Merit_%28Romania%29), Commander rank.[^o3][^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
