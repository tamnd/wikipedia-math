# Bergman–Weil formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bergman–Weil formula"}

In mathematics, the **Bergman–Weil formula** is an integral representation for [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) of several variables generalizing the [Cauchy integral formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_integral_formula). It was introduced by (Bergmann 1936) and (Weil 1935).

:::

## Weil domains {#Weil_domains}

A Weil domain (Weil 1935) is an [analytic polyhedron](wikipedia://en/21663267) with a domain *U* in **C**<sup>*n*</sup> defined by inequalities *f*<sub>*j*</sub>(*z*) \< 1 for functions *f*<sub>*j*</sub> that are holomorphic on some neighborhood of the closure of *U*, such that the faces of the Weil domain (where one of the functions is 1 and the others are less than 1) all have dimension 2*n* − 1, and the intersections of *k* faces have [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) at least *k*.
