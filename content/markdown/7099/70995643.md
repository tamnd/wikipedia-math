# David Liggins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Liggins"}

**David Liggins** is a philosopher at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) with research interests in [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Liggins received his PhD in 2005 from the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield). He then spent a year at [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge)'s faculty of philosophy before becoming a lecturer at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 2006.[^o3] In 2016, he was appointed joint editor of [*Analysis*](wikipedia://en/7579257) with Chris Daly.[^o4] He served as the sole editor of the journal from 2017 to 2021 when it was announced that he would be joint editor alongside Stacie Friend and Lee Walters.[^o5][^o6]
