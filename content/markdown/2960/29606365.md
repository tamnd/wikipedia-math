# Richard Bronson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Bronson"}

**Richard D. Bronson** (born August 5, 1941) is an American professor emeritus of mathematics at [Fairleigh Dickinson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairleigh_Dickinson_University)[^o3] where he served as chair of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, acting dean of the College of Science and Engineering, interim provost of the Metropolitan Campus, director of government affairs, and senior executive assistant to the president.[^o4] He served as an officer (2008–2011) of the [International Association of University Presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_University_Presidents),[^o5] where he was actively involved in the creation of the United Nations Academic Impact initiative[^o6] and the World Innovative Summit in Education, held annually in Qatar. He is also the author of the political thriller *[Antispin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antispin)*.

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Richard D. Bronson was born in New York City on August 5, 1941. He attended [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevens_Institute_of_Technology), where he earned his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. in applied mathematics. He is married and has two children.

## Writing {#Writing}

Bronson has written eleven books in mathematics, some in their third edition with many translated into multiple languages. He has published children's poetry in magazines, including *[Highlights for Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highlights_for_Children)*. He was on the editorial staff of *Simulation Magazine*[^o9] and *SIAM News*[^o9] and the children's magazine *Kids Club*. *Antispin* is his first novel.

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1994, Richard Bronson was awarded the Distinguished College or University Teaching award by the New Jersey Section of Mathematical Association of America.[^o11] He also received the Fairleigh Dickinson University Distinguished Faculty Award for Research & Scholarship,[^o12] and the University College Outstanding Teacher Award.

## Research {#Research}

Bronson's research interests are in mathematical modeling and computer simulation with a focus on macro-sociological theory. He has written extensively on the topic in articles for professional journals and a general-interest trade magazine.
