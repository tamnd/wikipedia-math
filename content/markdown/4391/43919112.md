# Pantachy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pantachy"}

In mathematics, a **pantachy** or **pantachie** (from the Greek word πανταχη meaning everywhere) is a maximal totally ordered [subset](wikipedia://en/27631) of a [partially ordered set](wikipedia://en/23572), especially a set of equivalence classes of sequences of real numbers. The term was introduced by (txt) to mean a [dense subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dense_subset) of an ordered set, and he also introduced "infinitary pantachies" to mean the ordered set of equivalence classes of real functions ordered by domination, but as [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989) pointed out this is not a totally ordered set.[^o3] (Hausdorff 1907) redefined a pantachy to be a maximal totally ordered subset of this set.

:::
