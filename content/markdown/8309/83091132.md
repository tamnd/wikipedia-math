# Elise Lockwood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elise Lockwood"}

**Elise Nicole Lockwood** is an American scholar of [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) and a professor of mathematics at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University). Her research focuses on [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/5170) and [algorithmic](wikipedia://en/775) thought processes in undergraduate mathematics students.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lockwood was an undergraduate at [Wheaton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheaton_College_%28Illinois%29), an [Evangelical Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelical_Christian) school in [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois). She graduated [magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_cum_laude) with a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 2004, and continued her studies in mathematics education at [Portland State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_State_University), initially intending to become a high school mathematics teacher. At Portland State, she earned a master's degree in 2006 and completed a Ph.D. in 2011. Her doctoral dissertation, *Student Approaches to Combinatorial Enumeration: The Role of Set-Oriented Thinking*, was supervised by Sean Larsen.

From 2011 to 2013 she was a postdoctoral research fellow at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). She joined Oregon State University as an assistant professor in 2013, and was promoted to associate professor in 2018 and full professor in 2023. From 2021 to 2024 she took a leave from Oregon State to work as a program officer at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation). In 2019 she became a [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Scholar), funding her travel to the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in Norway and again in 2025 to [Maynooth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maynooth_University) in Ireland.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lockwood was the 2018 recipient of the Annie and John Selden Prize for Research in Undergraduate Mathematics Education of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). She was a 2025 recipient of the [Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Early_Career_Award_for_Scientists_and_Engineers).
