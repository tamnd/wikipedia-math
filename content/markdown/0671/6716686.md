# Sidney Graham

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sidney Graham"}

**Sidney West Graham** is a mathematician interested in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) and professor at [Central Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Michigan_University). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.), which was supervised by [Hugh Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Montgomery_%28mathematician%29), from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1977. In his Ph.D. thesis he lowered the upper bound for [Linnik's constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linnik's_constant) to 36 and subsequently reduced the bound further to 20.[^o4]

:::
