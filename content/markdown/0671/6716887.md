# Matti Jutila

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matti Jutila"}

**Matti Ilmari Jutila** (16 March 1943 – 7 March 2026) was a mathematician and a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku).[^o3] He researched in the field of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jutila was born on 16 March 1943 in Vesilahti, Finland.[^o10] He completed a doctorate at the University of Turku in 1970, with a dissertation related to Linnik's constant supervised by Kustaa Inkeri.

Jutila's work has repeatedly succeeded in lowering the [upper bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_bound) for [Linnik's constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linnik's_constant).[^o6] He is the author of a [monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph), *Lectures on a method in the theory of exponential sums* (1987).[^o7] According to the *Mathematics Genealogy Project*, he advised five PhD students, all at the University of Turku.[^o5] He was elected to membership in the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 1982.[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Jutila died on 7 March 2026.[^o10]
