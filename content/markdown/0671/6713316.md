# Graeme Segal

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Graeme Segal"}

**Graeme Bryce Segal** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o4] (born 21 December 1941) is an Australian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and professor at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Segal was educated at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), where he received his [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSc) degree in 1961. He went on to receive his [D.Phil.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Phil.) in 1967 from [St Catherine's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catherine's_College,_Oxford); his thesis, written under the supervision of [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698), was titled *Equivariant K-theory*.

His thesis was in the area of equivariant [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748). The [Atiyah–Segal completion theorem](wikipedia://en/18017949) in that subject was a major motivation for the [Segal conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segal_conjecture), which he formulated. He has made many other contributions to [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) in the past four decades, including an approach to [infinite loop spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_loop_space). He was also a pioneer of [elliptic cohomology](wikipedia://en/10295395), which is related to his interest in [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757).

Segal was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1970 in Nice[^o6] and in 1990 in Kyoto.[^o7] He taught at Oxford University from 1964 to 1990, then became the [Lowndean Professor of Astronomy and Geometry](wikipedia://en/648990) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) from 1990 to 1999, and then a Senior Research Fellow of [All Souls College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Souls_College,_Oxford) from 1999 to 2009. He was elected the President of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2011.

He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1982 and was awarded the [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal) by the Royal Society in 2010.[^o8]

At the 2026 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842), Segal was awarded the [Chern Medal](wikipedia://en/26347082) "for his visionary mathematical insights that have had an enduring influence in a wide range of fields, including topology, mathematical physics, representation theory and category theory."[^o9]

He is married to writer [Marina Warner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Warner). They live in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).[^o10]
