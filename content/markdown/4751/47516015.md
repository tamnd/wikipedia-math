# Florian Luca

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Florian Luca"}

**Florian Luca** (16 March 1969 – 28 July 2026[^o2][^o3]) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician who specialized in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) with emphasis on [Diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109), [linear recurrences](wikipedia://en/146806) and the distribution of values of [arithmetic functions](wikipedia://en/3170). He made notable contributions to the proof that irrational automatic numbers are transcendental and the proof of a conjecture of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) on the intersection of the [Euler Totient function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Totient_function) and the [sum of divisors function](wikipedia://en/407249).

:::

Luca was born in [Galați](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galați) and graduated with a BS in Mathematics from [Alexandru Ioan Cuza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University) in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași) (1992), and Ph.D. in Mathematics from the [University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alaska_Fairbanks) (1996). He held various appointments at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University), [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), [Czech Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Academy_of_Sciences), [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico), the [University of the Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Witwatersrand), and [Stellenbosch University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellenbosch_University). He co-authored over 800 papers in mathematics with more than 350 co-authors.[^o4]

He was a recipient of the award of a 2005 [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) for Natural Sciences, Latin America & Caribbean.[^o5] In 2024 he received an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Synergy Grant and the next year he was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) by the [University of Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen).[^o6]

He served as an [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of *[Research in Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_in_Number_Theory)* [^o7] and *INTEGERS: the Electronic Journal of Combinatorial Number Theory*,[^o8] and an editor of the *[Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*.[^o9]
