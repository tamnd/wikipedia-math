# Kolchin's problems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kolchin's problems"}

**Kolchin's problems** are a set of [unsolved problems](wikipedia://en/1333335) in [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587), outlined by [Ellis Kolchin](wikipedia://en/15900659) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1966 [(Moscow)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Kolchin Catenary Conjecture"}

## Kolchin Catenary Conjecture {#Kolchin_Catenary_Conjecture}

The **Kolchin Catenary Conjecture** is a fundamental open problem in [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587) related to [dimension theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_theory).

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

### Statement {#Statement}

"Let $\Sigma$ be a differential [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) $d$. By a *long gap chain* we mean a chain of irreducible differential subvarieties $\Sigma_0 \subset \Sigma_1 \subset \Sigma_2 \subset \cdots$ of [ordinal number](wikipedia://en/26547932) length $\omega^m \cdot d$."

Given an irreducible differential variety $\Sigma$ of dimension $d > 0$ and an arbitrary point $p \in \Sigma$ , does there exist a long gap chain beginning at $p$ and ending at $\Sigma$?

The positive answer to this question is called the Kolchin catenary conjecture.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]
