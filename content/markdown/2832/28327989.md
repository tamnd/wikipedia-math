# Giulio Giorello

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giulio Giorello"}

**Giulio Giorello** (; 14 May 1945 – 15 June 2020) was an Italian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [epistemologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemologist).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Giorello graduated with a degree in [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) in 1968 and in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1971 at the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan). While there, he studied under the philosopher [Ludovico Geymonat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludovico_Geymonat).[^o5] He then taught physics and natural sciences at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia), [University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania), [University of Insubria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Insubria)[^o6] and the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan).[^o5] Giorello was a professor of philosophy of science at the University of Milan;[^o7][^o8] he was also President of SILFS (Italian Society of Logic and Philosophy of Science).[^o6] He directed the "Scienza e idee" series by Raffaello Cortina Editore[^o9] and collaborated on the cultural pages of the newspaper [Corriere della Sera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corriere_della_Sera).[^o5][^o10][^o11]

In 2010, Giorello expressed his [atheistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheistic) thought with work *Senza Dio. Del buon uso dell'ateismo*,[^o12] but in the last years of his life he expressed an [agnostic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnostic) thought.

In March 2012 he was a speaker at the national congress of the [Grand Orient of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Orient_of_Italy) in [Rimini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimini).[^o13][^o14] Giorello won the 4th edition of the 2012 Frascati Philosophy National Award.[^o15]

Giorello died in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan) on 15 June 2020 due to [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) during the [COVID-19 pandemic in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Italy).[^o16] Three days before his death, he married his partner Roberta Pelachin.[^o17]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Giorello was a "comic book expert"; he wrote essays about [Tex Willer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tex_Willer) and [Topolino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topolino), and he also wrote the prefaces to [Logicomix](wikipedia://en/24664148) and *Rat-Man: Superstorie di un supernessuno*.[^o19] In 2014, he co-created the comic "The philosophy of Donald Duck".[^o19]
