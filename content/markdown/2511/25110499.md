# Jean-Michel Coron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Michel Coron"}

**Jean-Michel Coron** (born August 8, 1956) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He first studied at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), where he worked on his PhD thesis advised by [Haïm Brezis](wikipedia://en/18329570). Since 1992, he has studied the [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and which includes both control and stabilization.[^o3][^o4] His results concern partial differential equations related to [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), with emphasis on nonlinear phenomena, and part of them found applications to control channels.

:::

He had previously worked in the field of non-linear functional analysis, where he also obtained significant results. Jean-Michel Coron was awarded numerous prizes, such as the [Fermat prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) in 1993, the [Peccot lecture](wikipedia://en/60897406) in 1987, the Jaffé prize in 1995 by the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences), and the Dargelos prize in 2002.

He was invited at the 1990 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (Kyoto) in the section Partial Differential Equations, and he was also invited as a plenary speaker at the 2010 International Congress of Mathematicians, Hyderabad, India.[^o5] He is an emeritus Professor at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) in Paris, and was a Senior member of the Institut Universitaire de France between 2003 and 2013.[^o6] He is also a member of the Académie des Sciences[^o7] and the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o8]

Jean-Michel Coron is the husband of [Claire Voisin](wikipedia://en/5980475) who was also plenary speaker at the 2010 International Congress of Mathematicians and who is the 2016 recipient of the [Gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_medal) of the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research), the highest scientific research award in France.[^o9] They have five children.[^o10]
