# Lawrence Rudner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence Rudner"}

**Lawrence M. Rudner** is a research statistician and consultant whose work spans domains, including, statistical analysis, [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming), [web development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_development), and [oyster farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyster_farming). He is the owner and president of Oyster Girl Oysters,[^o3] and is an instructor at the Chesapeake Forum[^o4] and the Chesapeake Bay Maritime Museum. He is the founder and former editor of the *Practical Assessment, Research, and Evaluation* journal.[^o5]

:::

Rudner has conducted research on measurement topics, including using Item Response Theory, IRT, for assessing item bias, parameter invariance, [person fit validity](wikipedia://en/10055524), and classification accuracy, utilizing research methods, [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982), [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954), and [programming languages](wikipedia://en/23015).[^o6] He has written more than 150 scholarly papers and 40 grants, with contributions featured in books and chapters published by institutions,[^o7] earning him the Award for Outstanding Dissemination of Educational Measurement Concepts to the Public from [National Council on Measurement in Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_on_Measurement_in_Education),[^o8] and the Career Achievement Award from the Association of Test Publishers.[^o9]

Rudner held the position of senior fellow at the [American Institutes for Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institutes_for_Research).[^o10]

## Education {#Education}

Rudner earned his Bachelor of Science in mathematics from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) in 1971. He continued his education with a Master of Science in [Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education) from [Western Connecticut State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Connecticut_State_University) in 1972 and completed his Doctorate in [Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and [Evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaluation) at [The Catholic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_America).[^o12] In 1991, he pursued an MBA in [Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) and [Entrepreneurship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurship) at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park).[^o13]

## Career {#Career}

Rudner served as the head of the Educational Resources Information Center (ERIC) Clearing House on Assessment,[^o15] and as project officer for the [National Assessment of Educational Progress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assessment_of_Educational_Progress). From 2004 to 2014, he was the vice president for Research and Psychometrics at the [Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Management_Admission_Council).[^o16][^o17]

Prior to joining GMAC, Rudner was a senior associate (GS-15) in the US Government, where he was the project officer for the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), director of the [ERIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Resources_Information_Center) Clearinghouse on Assessment and Evaluation,[^o18] and a faculty member at universities.[^o19] Since 2021, he has been an instructor at the Chesapeake Forum, teaching courses on [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), [cybersecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber-security_regulation), and [internet safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_safety) and the Chesapeake Bay Maritime Museum, teaching classes on oyster gardening.[^o4]

Rudner has been serving as the owner and president of Oyster Girl Oysters. Along with Willem Roosenburg, he invented a cost-effective oyster cage flipping system for which he received a patent in 2024. He has also authored a paper on predicting oyster volume for the East Coast Shellfish Growers Association.[^o20]

## Contributions to the field {#Contributions_to_the_field}

Rudner created some of the first tools for first engines for [automated essay scoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_essay_scoring), automated test assembly, and [computer adaptive testing](wikipedia://en/1743830).[^o22] He devised an AI method for documenting [copyright infringements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_infringement) receiving a US patent in 2009[^o23] and developed the Bayesian Essay Test Scoring System, a Windows-based [open-source program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) for automated essay scoring applicable to various text classification tasks.[^o24][^o25] He created an online interactive tutorial on [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216) comparing it with [Item Response Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Item_Response_Theory) and exploring the calibration sample size needed for accurate classifications.[^o26]

Rudner directed the activities of the ERIC Clearinghouse on Assessment and Evaluation.[^o25] One of his key projects was developing the Search ERIC Wizard, a search engine for the ERIC database that used a [thesaurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaurus) as a front end.[^o27]

Rudner's most cited research project was an examination of the achievement levels of a large sample of home school students.[^o28] This study received coverage in newspapers.[^o29] He also appeared on *[CSPAN's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-SPAN)* *[Washington Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Journal)* and the *[Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post)* to discuss the report.[^o30]

In the publication *What Teachers Need to Know About Assessment*, part of [NEA's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Education_Association) Student Assessment Series, Rudner alongside William D. Schafer, provided comprehensive information on [high-stakes testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-stakes_testing), [test scoring](wikipedia://en/15731195), and [classroom management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classroom_management).[^o31] He also conducted a report evaluating the IntelliMetric automated essay scoring system's performance compared to human raters using essays from the GMAT Analytic Writing Assessment.[^o32]

In 2019, Rudner examined the application of measurement decision theory for classifying examinees, evaluating its effectiveness in terms of classification accuracy, [sequential testing procedures](wikipedia://en/3509530), and the minimum number of items required for accurate classification.[^o33]
