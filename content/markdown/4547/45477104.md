# Jill P. Mesirov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jill P. Mesirov"}

**Jill P. Mesirov** (also published as Jill P. Mesirov-Kazdan)[^o3] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), and [computational biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) who is the Associate Vice Chancellor for Computational Health Sciences at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). She previously held an adjunct faculty position at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) and was the associate director and chief informatics officer at the [Eli and Edythe L. Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_Institute).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mesirov did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania),[^o7] and earned a doctorate in mathematics from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1974, under the supervision of [Richard Palais](wikipedia://en/34779708).

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Her research concerns [high-performance computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-performance_computing).[^o7] Effective July 1, 2015, she has been appointed Associate Vice Chancellor for Computational Health Sciences and Professor of Medicine at the [University of California, San Diego School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego_School_of_Medicine) and [Moores Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moores_Cancer_Center).[^o8]

She has worked at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), [Thinking Machines Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Machines_Corporation), and [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) before joining the Whitehead Institute of MIT in 1997, which eventually became part of the Broad Institute.[^o7] She was president of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from 1989 to 1991.[^o9] She served on the Board of Trustees for the [Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31112547) (ICERM) from 2017 to 2021.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Mesirov became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 1996.[^o12] In 2012, she was elected an [ISCB Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCB_Fellow) by the [International Society for Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Computational_Biology) in 2012,[^o13][^o14] and one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15] In 2017, she was selected as a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the inaugural class.[^o16]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Mesirov first met her husband [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863) at a party hosted by [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986).[^o18] They married in 1982 and had two sons.[^o18] Gross died on December 19, 2025.[^o19][^o20][^o21]
