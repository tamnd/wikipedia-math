# Barry D. Nussbaum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barry D. Nussbaum"}

**Barry D. Nussbaum** is an American statistician. Nussbaum earned a bachelor's degree from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) and a master's and doctorate from [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University).[^o6]

:::

Nussbaum joined the [United States Environmental Protection Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Environmental_Protection_Agency) in 1975 and served as chief statistician from 2006 to 2016.[^o2] Early in his career, Nussbaum helped lead a recall of 208,000 cars for excessive carbon monoxide emissions, based on a sample of just ten cars.[^o3] He is a recipient of two [EPA Silver Medals for Superior Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awards_and_decorations_of_the_United_States_government#Environmental_Protection_Agency) and the [EPA's Distinguished Career Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awards_and_decorations_of_the_United_States_government#Environmental_Protection_Agency).[^o4][^o5]

Nussbaum was [president](wikipedia://en/14833025) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) for 2017.[^o6]

He has also taught graduate statistics courses for [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech) and [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) universities.[^o6]

In 2024 Nussbaum was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o7]
