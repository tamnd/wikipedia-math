# Robert McNaughton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert McNaughton"}

**Robert Forbes McNaughton, Jr.** (March 13, 1924 – June 5, 2014) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) with several key contributions in [formal languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_languages), [grammars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar_%28computer_science%29) and [rewriting systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewriting_system), and [word combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_combinatorics).

:::

McNaughton was originally from [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), and earned a bachelor's degree from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). He completed his Ph.D. at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University); his dissertation, *On Establishing the Consistency of Systems*, was supervised by [Willard Van Orman Quine](wikipedia://en/33833). He taught at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) and then at the [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute).

He died in 2014 in [Troy, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy,_New_York).
