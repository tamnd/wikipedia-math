# Alexander Guts

Konstantinovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Guts"}

**Alexander Konstantinovich Guts** (Александр Константинович Гуц; 30 August 1947 – 5 March 2026) was a Russian mathematician.[^o2]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Guts was born on 30 August 1947 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), in what was then the [Soviet occupation zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_occupation_zone_in_Germany). Throughout his career, he published more than 500 works and about 30 books and textbooks on mathematics, computer science, physics, sociology, history and ecology.[^o4]

From 1974 to 2024, he worked at [Omsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omsk_State_University), and was the [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the Faculty of Computer Science (2001–2022).[^o5]

Guts died on 5 March 2026, at the age of 78.[^o6]
