# Amir Goharshady

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amir Goharshady"}

**Amir Kafshdar Goharshady** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): امیر کفشدار گوهرشادی[^o3]) is a [theoretical computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_computer_scientist). He is a Professor at the [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Sasso_Science_Institute)[^o4] and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)[^o5] and a fellow of [St Catherine's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catherine's_College,_Oxford).[^o6] He is known for his work in [formal methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_methods), [compilers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compilers) and [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971),[^o7][^o8] as well as his history in informatics and mathematical Olympiads.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Goharshady began university studies at the age of 16[^o10] and obtained his [undergraduate degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate_degree) in computer science and mathematics from the [University of Yazd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazd_University)[^o11] and the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London)[^o6] at the age of 19.[^o10] He obtained his [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) from the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech)[^o6] and completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 2020 at the [Institute of Science and Technology Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Science_and_Technology_Austria), supervised by [Krishnendu Chatterjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishnendu_Chatterjee)[^o12].

## Career {#Career}

Previously, he was an assistant professor of computer science and mathematics at [HKUST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology).[^o14][^o6] In July 2024, he joined the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) as an associate professor of computer science.[^o15] He was promoted to professor and joined the [Gran Sasso Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Sasso_Science_Institute) Institute in 2026.[^o4] He has served as a Program Committee Chair for several international conferences in [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971), including MARBLE[^o16][^o17] and SETTA,[^o18][^o18][^o19][^o20] as well as a Program Committee Member at [AAAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAAI_Conference_on_Artificial_Intelligence),[^o21] [POPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Principles_of_Programming_Languages),[^o22] [TACAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TACAS),[^o23] [PLDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programming_Language_Design_and_Implementation),[^o24] [CAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Aided_Verification),[^o25] and [ESOP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Symposium_on_Programming).[^o26] He is also a visiting professor at the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Bombay)[^o27] and served as an advisor and trainer to the [Pakistani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) team at the [International Olympiad in Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympiad_in_Informatics).[^o28][^o29]
