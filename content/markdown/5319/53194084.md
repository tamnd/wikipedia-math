# Lao Statistics Bureau

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lao Statistics Bureau"}

**Lao Statistics Bureau** is the state agency charged with the collection and publication of statistics related to the economy, population and society of [Laos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laos).[^o3]
