# Sumner Byron Myers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sumner Byron Myers"}

**Sumner Byron Myers** (February 19, 1910 – October 8, 1955) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under [H. C. Marston Morse](wikipedia://en/856523),[^o3] where he graduated with a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1932.[^o4] Myers then pursued postdoctoral studies at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1934–1936)[^o5] before becoming a professor for mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He died unexpectedly from a heart attack during the [1955 Michigan–Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955_Michigan_Wolverines_football_team) football game at [Michigan Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Stadium).[^o6]

:::

## Sumner B. Myers Prize {#Sumner_B._Myers_Prize}

The Sumner B. Myers Prize was created in his honor for distinguished theses within the [LSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Literature,_Science,_and_the_Arts) Mathematics Department.[^o8][^o9] Notable recipients include [June Huh](wikipedia://en/54434626), [Mary Wootters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Wootters), and [Theodore J. Kaczynski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_J._Kaczynski).[^o10][^o11][^o12]
