# Impossible differential cryptanalysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Impossible differential cryptanalysis"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **impossible differential cryptanalysis** is a form of [differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536) for [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594). While ordinary differential cryptanalysis tracks differences that propagate through the cipher with greater than expected [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), impossible differential cryptanalysis exploits differences that are impossible (having probability 0) at some intermediate [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_%28computer_science%29) of the cipher algorithm.

:::

[Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen) appears to be the first to use a form of this attack, in the 1998 paper where he introduced his [AES candidate](wikipedia://en/99431), [DEAL](wikipedia://en/663184).[^o2] The first presentation to attract the attention of the cryptographic community was later the same year at the rump session of [CRYPTO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRYPTO) '98, in which [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920), [Alex Biryukov](wikipedia://en/9189491), and [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314) introduced the name "impossible differential"[^o3] and used the technique to break 4.5 out of 8.5 rounds of [IDEA](wikipedia://en/15076)[^o4] and 31 out of 32 rounds of the [NSA](wikipedia://en/21939)-designed cipher [Skipjack](wikipedia://en/474702).[^o5] This development led cryptographer [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732) to speculate that the NSA had no previous knowledge of impossible differential cryptanalysis.[^o6] The technique has since been applied to many other ciphers: [Khufu and Khafre](wikipedia://en/634695), [E2](wikipedia://en/8441917), variants of [Serpent](wikipedia://en/385154), [MARS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MARS_%28cryptography%29), [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356), [Rijndael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijndael) (AES), [CRYPTON](wikipedia://en/7237011), [Zodiac](wikipedia://en/8680492), [Hierocrypt-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hierocrypt-3), [TEA](wikipedia://en/528720), [XTEA](wikipedia://en/530334), [Mini-AES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini-AES), [ARIA](wikipedia://en/9013560), [Camellia](wikipedia://en/532475), and [SHACAL-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHACAL-2).

Biham, Biryukov and Shamir also presented a relatively efficient specialized method for finding impossible differentials that they called a *miss-in-the-middle* attack. This consists of finding "two events with probability one, whose conditions cannot be met together."[^o7]
