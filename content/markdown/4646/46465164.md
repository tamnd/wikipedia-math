# Labs septic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Labs septic"}

In mathematics, the **Labs septic surface** is a degree-7 ([septic](wikipedia://en/25451646)) [nodal surface](wikipedia://en/46573101) with 99 [nodes](wikipedia://en/981915#Nodes) found by (Labs 2006). As of 2015, it has the largest known number of nodes of a degree-7 surface, though this number is still less than the best known upper bound of 104 nodes given by (Varchenko 1983).[^o4]
