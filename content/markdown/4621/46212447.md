# Vladimir Vragov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Vragov"}

**Vladimir Vragov** (Владимир Николаевич Врагов; 2 October 1945, [Urgench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urgench) — 4 June 2002) — was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)/[Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and scientist.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Vladimir Vragov was born in 1945 in [Urgench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urgench), [Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) in a driver\`s family. It was a hard period after [war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Front_%28World_War_II%29) and the family had to emigrate a lot. Vladimir finished school in 1963 and entered Department of Mechanics and Mathematics of [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University). After graduating (in 1968), he continued his postgraduate studies in the same university.

Practically all of Vragov\`s scientific activities were connected with [Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobolev_Institute_of_Mathematics) of Siberian Department of [RAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). Vladimir became PhD in 1971. In 1993 he was elected as a chancellor of [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) and remained at this position till 1997. Vladimir Vragov had been the head of [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO) chair of SD [RAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) since the spring of 1997. One time he came so hard there was nothing left in him and he died.
