# Jorge Urrutia Galicia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jorge Urrutia Galicia"}

Urrutia

*Image: JorgeUrrutia.jpg.* At LATIN 2016

**Jorge Urrutia Galicia** is a Mexican mathematician and computer scientist in the Institute of Mathematics of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM).[^o2] His research primarily concerns [discrete](wikipedia://en/386468) and [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Urrutia earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 1980, under the supervision of [Ronald C. Read](wikipedia://en/18154982). He worked for many years at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) before moving to UNAM in 1999.[^o5] With [Jörg-Rüdiger Sack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jörg-Rüdiger_Sack) in 1991, he was founding co-editor-in-chief of the [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) *[Computational Geometry: Theory and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_Geometry_%28journal%29)*.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Urrutia is a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o8] The Mexican Conference on Discrete Mathematics and Computational Geometry, held in 2013 in [Oaxaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oaxaca), was dedicated to Urrutia in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o9]
