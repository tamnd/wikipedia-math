# Linda Sharples

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Linda Sharples"}

**Linda Sharples** is a British statistician who is Professor of Medical Statistics at the [London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_&_Tropical_Medicine). Her research considers statistical analysis of medical interventions. She has provided expert advice to clinical trials on cardiovascular disease, diabetes and cancer.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sharples was trained at the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham), where she focussed on mathematics and statistics. Her doctoral research considered [robustness](wikipedia://en/2885691) and approximation in [hierarchical models](wikipedia://en/4521038).[^o4] She joined [Newcastle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_University) as a postdoctoral fellow in 1986.

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

In 1989, Sharples joined the [Medical Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Research_Council_%28United_Kingdom%29) Biostatistics Unit[^o6] in Cambridge. She was made a programme leader in 2000.[^o7] In this capacity, she developed statistical methods for assessing health technologies. The outcomes of her research were used to update experimental studies and decision models in the [Royal Papworth Hospital NHS Foundation Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Papworth_Hospital_NHS_Foundation_Trust).[^o8] She applied clinical epidemiology to cardiothoracic transplants.[^o9] She evaluated surgical procedure and developed multi-state models to describe the history of chronic disease.[^o10]

In 2013, Sharples left the MRC to join the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) Clinical Trials Unit as a professor of statistics, where she oversaw the Comprehensive Health Research Division, which focussed on trials in musculoskeletal and cardiovascular medicine.[^o8] She served on the [Government of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_United_Kingdom) Commission on Human Medicines.[^o11] She moved to the [London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_&_Tropical_Medicine) as a professor of Medical Statistics in 2017.[^o12] She studies how medical statistics can be used to evaluate different interventions.[^o12] She is involved with an investigation into the care pathways of bowel cancer patients.[^o13]
