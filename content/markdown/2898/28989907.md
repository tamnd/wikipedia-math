# Demographics of Oceania

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Oceania"}

[Oceania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania) is a [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Region) centered on the [islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) of the tropical [Pacific Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Ocean).[^o3] Conceptions of what constitutes Oceania vary, with it being defined in various ways, often [geopolitically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopolitics) or [geographically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography). In the geopolitical conception used by the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_geoscheme), [International Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee), and many atlases, the Oceanic region includes [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia) and the nations of the Pacific from [Papua New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papua_New_Guinea) east, but not the [Malay Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_Archipelago) or [Indonesian New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_New_Guinea).[^o4][^o5][^o6] The term is sometimes used more specifically to denote Australasia as a geographic [continent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continent),[^o7][^o8] or biogeographically as a synonym for either the [Australasian realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasian_realm) ([Wallacea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallacea) and [Australasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasia)) or the [Oceanian realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanian_realm) ([Melanesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanesia), [Polynesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynesia), and [Micronesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micronesia) apart either from [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand)[^o9] or from mainland [New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Guinea)[^o10]).

:::

Although [Christmas Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Island) and the [Cocos (Keeling) Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocos_%28Keeling%29_Islands) belong to the Commonwealth of Australia and are inhabited, they are nearer [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia) than the Australian mainland, and are commonly associated with [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia) instead of Oceania.
