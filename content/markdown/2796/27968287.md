# Savić

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Savić"}

**Savić** (; Савич, Савiч), **Savich** or **Savitch** is a [Slavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavs) surname, sometimes used as a first name, most common among [South Slavs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Slavs). It can be related to the name [Sava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sava_%28name%29) or [Sava (river)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sava).

:::
