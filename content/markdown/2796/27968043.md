# Kari Karhunen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kari Karhunen"}

**Kari Onni Uolevi Karhunen** (April 12, 1915 – September 16, 1992)[^o3] was a Finnish [probabilist](wikipedia://en/23542) and a [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711). He is best known for the [Karhunen–Loève theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karhunen–Loève_theorem) and [Karhunen–Loève transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karhunen–Loève_transform).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Karhunen received his master's degree in 1938 and his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1950 from the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki). The topic of his thesis was (in German) *Über lineare Methoden in der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung*,[^o5] in English *On linear methods in probability and statistics*. The advisor of his thesis was the mathematician [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna).[^o6]

Karhunen worked as a lecturer at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) before leaving the academic world to be employed by the insurance corporation [Suomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suomi_%28insurance_company%29), becoming CEO of the company in 1963.

Karhunen served in 1955 on the Finnish Committee for Mathematical Machines, which developed the first Finnish computer [ESKO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESKO).[^o7]
