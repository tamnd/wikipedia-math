# Jacqueline Jensen-Vallin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacqueline Jensen-Vallin"}

**Jacqueline Ann Jensen-Vallin** is an American mathematician. She is a chair and professor of mathematics at [Lamar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_University), former [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of *[MAA FOCUS](wikipedia://en/21147475)*, the newsletter of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA), and a former governor of the Texas Section of the MAA. Her research interests include [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834), [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008); she is also known for her work in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) and the history of [women in mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_mathematics).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jensen-Vallin did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut), completing a [double major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_major) in mathematics and psychology in 1995. She went to the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) for her graduate studies, and completed her doctorate there in 2002. Her dissertation, *Finding $\pi_2$-Generators for Exotic Homotopy Types of Two-Complexes*, concerned [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and was supervised by Micheal Dyer.

After completing her doctorate, she joined the faculty at [Sam Houston State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Houston_State_University) in 2002. She moved to Lamar University in 2014.

## Book {#Book}

With [Janet Beery](wikipedia://en/59100464), Sarah J. Greenwald, and Maura B. Mast, Jensen-Vallin is an editor of the book *Women in Mathematics: Celebrating the Centennial of the Mathematical Association of America* (Springer, 2017).[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Jensen-Vallin was one of the 2008 winners of the Henry L. Alder Award for Distinguished Teaching by a Beginning College or University Mathematics Faculty Member.

The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) gave her their Service Award in 2018.[^o6]
