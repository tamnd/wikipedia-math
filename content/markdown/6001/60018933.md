# Beno Arbel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Beno Arbel"}

**Beno Arbel** (בנו ארבל; 20 January 1939 – 9 April 2013) was an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) who worked as professor of mathematics at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).

:::

He should be distinguished from Benjamin Arbel, a professor emeritus of history at Tel Aviv University.

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Drăgănești-Olt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drăgănești-Olt), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), Arbel began his academic studies at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest), which he continued at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) upon [immigrating to Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) in 1961. He completed his [baccalaureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in mathematics and physics there in 1963, and his master's degree in mathematics in April 1965 (under the supervision of [Aryeh Dvoretzky](wikipedia://en/19901447)), a month after which he enlisted in the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces). Arbel received his Ph.D. in 1987 from the Hebrew University under Marcel Herzog, and went on to teach at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), [Kibbutzim College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibbutzim_College), [Beit Berl Academic College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Berl_Academic_College), and the [Academic College of Tel Aviv-Yafo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_College_of_Tel_Aviv-Yafo).

From the late 1980s, Arbel served as director of the program for [gifted young students](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gifted_education) in mathematics and computer science at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).[^o4]
