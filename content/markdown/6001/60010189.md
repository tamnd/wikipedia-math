# List of spirals

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of spirals"}

This **list of spirals** includes named [spirals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirals) that have been described mathematically.

:::

::: {#o2 .table cue=table}

| Image | Name | First described | Equation | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Image: Circle - black simple.svg.* | [Circle](wikipedia://en/6220) |  | $r= k$ | The trivial spiral |
| *Image: Archimedean spiral.svg.* | [Archimedean spiral](wikipedia://en/61559) (also *arithmetic spiral*) | \-300 | $r=a+b \cdot \theta$ |  |
| *Image: Fermat's spiral.svg.* | [Fermat's spiral](wikipedia://en/199584) (also parabolic spiral) | 1636 | $r^{2} = a^{2} \cdot \theta$ | Encloses equal area per turn |
| *Image: PSM V79 D459 Logarithmic spiral in flower growth configuration.png.* | [Doyle spiral](wikipedia://en/64400151) | 1980—1990 |  | circle packing, using circles of structured radii |
| *Image: Cornu Spiral.svg.* | [Euler spiral](wikipedia://en/19107713) (also Cornu spiral or polynomial spiral) | 1696[^o3] | $x(t) = \operatorname{C}(t),\,$$y(t) = \operatorname{S}(t)$ | Using [Fresnel integrals](wikipedia://en/271143)[^o4] |
| *Image: Hyperspiral.svg.* | [Hyperbolic spiral](wikipedia://en/183045) (also *reciprocal spiral*) | 1704 | $r = \frac{a}{\theta}$ |  |
| *Image: Lituus.svg.* | [Lituus](wikipedia://en/20144966) | 1722 | $r^{2} \cdot \theta = k$ |  |
| *Image: Logarithmic Spiral Pylab.svg.* | [Logarithmic spiral](wikipedia://en/42373) (also known as *equiangular spiral*) | 1638 | $r=a\cdot e^{b \cdot \theta}$ | Constant [pitch angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_angle_%28spiral%29). Approximations of this are found in nature |
| *Image: Fibonacci spiral.svg.* | [Fibonacci spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_spiral) |  | [Circular arcs](wikipedia://en/5578920) connecting the opposite corners of squares in the Fibonacci tiling | Approximation of the golden spiral |
| *Image: Golden spiral in rectanglesflip.png.* | [Golden spiral](wikipedia://en/219370) |  | $r = \varphi^{\frac{2 \cdot \theta}{\pi}}\,$ | Special case of the logarithmic spiral |
| *Image: Spiral of Theodorus.svg.* | [Spiral of Theodorus](wikipedia://en/17206552) (also known as *Pythagorean spiral*) | \-500 | Contiguous right triangles composed of one leg with unit length and the other leg being the hypotenuse of the prior triangle | Approximates the Archimedean spiral |
| *Image: Involute of circle.png.* | [Involute](wikipedia://en/840704) | 1673 | $x(t)=r(\cos(t+a)+t\sin(t+a)),$ $y(t)=r(\sin(t+a)-t \cos(t+a))$ | Involutes of a circle appear like Archimedean spirals |
| *Image: Helix.svg.* | [Helix](wikipedia://en/179924) |  | $r(t) = 1,\,$ $\theta(t) = t,\,$ $z(t) = t$ | A three-dimensional spiral |
| *Image: Loxodrome.png.* | [Rhumb line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhumb_line) (also loxodrome) |  |  | Type of spiral drawn on a sphere |
| *Image: Epi half spirals.svg.* | [Cotes's spiral](wikipedia://en/18683059) | 1722 | $\frac{1}{r} = \begin{cases} A \cosh(k\theta + \varepsilon) \\ A \exp(k\theta + \varepsilon) \\ A \sinh(k\theta + \varepsilon) \\ A (k\theta + \varepsilon) \\ A \cos(k\theta + \varepsilon) \\ \end{cases}$ | Solution to the two-body problem for an inverse-cube central force |
| *Image: PoinsotSpiral.svg.* | [Poinsot's spirals](wikipedia://en/4959116) |  | $r = a \cdot \operatorname{csch}(n \cdot \theta),\,$ $r = a \cdot \operatorname{sech}(n \cdot \theta)$ |  |
| *Image: Nielsen's spiral.png.* | [Nielsen's spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen's_spiral) | 1993 | $x(t) = \operatorname{ci}(t),\,$ $y(t) = \operatorname{si}(t)$ | A variation of Euler spiral, using [sine integral](wikipedia://en/245560#Sine_integral) and cosine integrals |
| *Image: Polygon spiral.png.* | [Polygonal spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygonal_spiral) |  |  | Special case approximation of arithmetic or logarithmic spiral |
| *Image: Fraser spiral.svg.* | [Fraser's Spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraser_spiral_illusion) | 1908 |  | Optical illusion based on spirals |
| *Image: Conchospiral.svg.* | [Conchospiral](wikipedia://en/2749223) |  | $r = \mu^{t} \cdot a,\,$$\theta = t,\,$$z = \mu^{t} \cdot c$ | A three-dimensional spiral on the surface of a cone. |
| *Image: Calkin-Wilf spiral.svg.* | [Calkin–Wilf spiral](wikipedia://en/22672164) |  |  |  |
| *Image: Ulam spiral howto primes only.svg.* | [Ulam spiral](wikipedia://en/67378) (also prime spiral) | 1963 |  |  |
| *Image: Sacks spiral.svg.* | [Sacks spiral](wikipedia://en/67378#Variants) | 1994 |  | Variant of Ulam spiral and Archimedean spiral. |
|  | [Seiffert's spiral](wikipedia://en/47565724) | 2000[^o5] | $r = \operatorname{sn}(s, k),\,$$\theta = k \cdot s$$z = \operatorname{cn}(s, k)$ | Spiral curve on the surface of a sphere using the [Jacobi elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/450004)[^o6] |
| *Image: Tractrix Spiral.svg.* | [Tractrix](wikipedia://en/841685) spiral | 1704 | $\begin{cases} r = A \cos(t) \\ \theta = \tan(t ) -t\end{cases}$ |  |
|  | [Pappus spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pappus_spiral) | 1779 | $\begin{cases} r=a \theta \\ \psi = k \end{cases}$ | 3D conical spiral studied by [Pappus](wikipedia://en/509300) and [Pascal](wikipedia://en/4068) |
| *Image: Doppler spiral.svg.* | [Doppler spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppler_spiral) |  | $x = a \cdot ( t \cdot \cos(t) + k \cdot t),\,$$y = a \cdot t \cdot \sin(t)$ | 2D projection of Pappus spiral |
| *Image: Atzema spiral.svg.* | [Atzema spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atzema_spiral) |  | $x = \frac{\sin(t)}{t} - 2 \cdot \cos(t) - t \cdot \sin(t),\,$$y = -\frac{\cos(t)}{t} - 2 \cdot \sin(t) + t \cdot \cos(t)$ | The curve that has a [catacaustic](wikipedia://en/880406) forming a circle. Approximates the Archimedean spiral. |
| *Image: AtomicSpiral.svg.* | [Atomic spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_spiral) | 2002 | $r = \frac{\theta}{\theta - a}$ | This spiral has two [asymptotes](wikipedia://en/3107); one is the circle of radius 1 and the other is the line $\theta=a$ |
| *Image: Galaxy NGC1079.jpg.* | [Galactic spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galactic_spiral) | 2019 | $\begin{cases} dx=R \cdot \frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2 +y^2}} d\theta \\ dy=R \cdot \left[\rho(\theta)- \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}} \right] d\theta \end{cases} \begin{cases} x= \sum dx \\ \\ \\ y= \sum dy + R \end{cases}$ | The differential spiral equations were developed to simulate the spiral arms of disc galaxies, have 4 solutions with three different cases:$\rho < 1, \rho = 1, \rho >1$, the spiral patterns are decided by the behavior of the parameter $\rho$. For $\rho <1$, spiral-ring pattern; $\rho = 1,$ regular spiral; $\rho > 1,$ loose spiral. R is the distance of spiral starting point (0, R) to the center. The calculated x and y have to be rotated backward by ($-\theta$) for plotting. |
