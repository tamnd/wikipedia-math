# Mayuko Yamashita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mayuko Yamashita"}

Yamashita Mayuko **Mayuko Yamashita** (山下 真由子, born 1995) is a Japanese mathematician and mathematical physicist whose research combines the areas of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [differential cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_cohomology), and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). She is an associate professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Yamashita represented Japan in the 2013 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), earning a silver medal. She studied engineering at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo), earning a bachelor's degree in 2017. She earned a master's degree in mathematical sciences at the University of Tokyo in March 2019, and completed a Ph.D. there in 2022. Her dissertation, *Differential models for the Anderson dual to bordism theories and invertible QFT's*, was supervised by Yasuyuki Kawahigashi.

Meanwhile, in 2019, she became an assistant professor in the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) of [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), while only 23 years old. She was promoted to associate professor in 2023 and joined the [Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics) in April 2025.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Yamashita was one of the recipients of the 2021 Takebe Katahiro Prize for Encouragement of Young Researchers of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786). She received the 2022 Grand Prize in the Marie Sklodowska Curie Awards of the [Japan Science and Technology Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Science_and_Technology_Agency) "for her work on mathematical applications to particle physics". She was named in the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) list in 2023 and 2024. She was a 2024 recipient of the Maryam Mirzakhani New Frontiers Prize, associated with the [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933), "for contributions to mathematical physics and index theory". 2024 she was awarded the [Dannie Heineman Prize (Göttingen)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dannie_Heineman_Prize_%28Göttingen%29) of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) (today called Niedersächsische Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen) for her contribution to the precise description of anomalies in quantum field theories.
