# Ermanno Bencivenga

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ermanno Bencivenga"}

**Ermanno Angelo Bencivenga** is an Italian philosopher and Professor Emeritus of Philosophy at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine). Bencivenga is known for his works on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), ethics, and [political philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_philosophy).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After graduating in philosophy from the [State University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan), Bencivenga soon left Italy, moving first to Canada for PhD studies and then to the United States, where he embarked on his academic career teaching, since 1979, at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine).

Several of his contributions have appeared over the years in various Italian newspapers like the *[La Stampa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Stampa)*, *[il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Sole_24_Ore)*, and *[l'Unità](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Unità)*.[^o7]

Since 2021 he hosts on the Italian television channel [Byoblu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byoblu) the programs titled *Spazio d'Illusione* (Space of Illusion), dedicated to the [history of cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cinema),[^o8] and *Spazio di Libertà* (Space of Freedom), which is dedicated to philosophical issues.[^o9]

He currently speaks Italian, English, French, Spanish, and German. He also knows [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) and [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek).
