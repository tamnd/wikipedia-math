# Howard Wright Alexander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Wright Alexander"}

**Howard Wright Alexander** (19 June 1911, [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto) – 28 June 1985, [Richmond, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Indiana)) was a Canadian-American mathematician.

:::

Alexander graduated from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1933 with a bachelor's degree.[^o2] He emigrated from [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1937. He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1939 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). As a [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaker), he was a conscientious objector during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) and did alternative national service. At [Earlham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earlham_College) he became an associate professor of mathematics in 1952 and retired there in 1976 as professor emeritus. He was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1950 in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts).

He married Mary Alice Nace in 1942; they had six children.
