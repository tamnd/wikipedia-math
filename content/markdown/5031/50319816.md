# Continuous module

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous module"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **continuous module** is a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) *M* such that every [submodule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submodule) of *M* is [essential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essential_submodule) in a [direct summand](wikipedia://en/58899) and every submodule of *M* [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Module_isomorphism) to a direct summand is itself a direct summand. The [endomorphism ring](wikipedia://en/59623) of a continuous module is a [clean ring](wikipedia://en/50769242).[^o2]

:::
