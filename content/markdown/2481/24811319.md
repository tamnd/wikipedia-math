# Effective complexity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Effective complexity"}

**Effective complexity** is a [measure of complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_systems) defined in a 1996 paper by [Murray Gell-Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Gell-Mann) and [Seth Lloyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Lloyd) that attempts to measure the amount of non-[random](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random) [information](wikipedia://en/18985062) in a system.[^o2][^o3] It has been criticised as being dependent on the subjective decisions made as to which parts of the information in the system are to be discounted as random.[^o4]

:::
