# Vitaly Bergelson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vitaly Bergelson"}

**Vitaly Bergelson** (born 1950 in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev)[^o6]) is a mathematical researcher and professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in [Columbus, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio). His research focuses on [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

Bergelson received his Ph.D. in 1984 under [Hillel Furstenberg](wikipedia://en/6450152) at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).[^o6] He gave an [invited address at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2006 in Madrid.[^o3] Among Bergelson's best known results is a polynomial generalization of [Szemerédi's theorem](wikipedia://en/591703).[^o4] The latter provided a positive solution to the famous Erdős–Turán conjecture from 1936 stating that any set of integers of positive upper density contains arbitrarily long [arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/168389). In a 1996 paper Bergelson and Leibman obtained an analogous statement for "polynomial progressions".[^o5] The Bergelson-Leibman theorem[^o6] and the techniques developed in its proof spurred significant further applications and generalizations, particularly in the recent work of [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498).[^o7][^o8]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
