# Hans Werner Ballmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Werner Ballmann"}

*Image: Atiyah ballmann.jpg.* [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) (left) and Ballmann (right), 2007

**Hans Werner Ballmann** (known as **Werner Ballmann**; born 11 April 1951) is a German mathematician. His area of research is [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) with focus on [geodesic flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_flow), spaces of [negative curvature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_curvature) as well as [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) of [Dirac operators](wikipedia://en/1079448)

:::

Ballmann earned his doctorate from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1979, under the supervision of [Wilhelm Klingenberg](wikipedia://en/33387331). He was a professor at the University of Bonn, and a director of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn, Germany, from 2007 to 2019.[^o3] He has advised 16 doctoral students at Bonn, including [Christian Bär](wikipedia://en/46586294) and [Anna Wienhard](wikipedia://en/45112397).[^o2]

He is a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) since 2007,[^o4] and a member of the scientific committee of the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) since 2004.
