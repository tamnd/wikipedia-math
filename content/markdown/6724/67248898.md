# Anca Muscholl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anca Muscholl"}

**Anca Muscholl** (born 1967) is a Romanian-German [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) known for her work on [formal verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_verification), [model checking](wikipedia://en/321157), and [two-variable logic](wikipedia://en/43571383). She is a researcher at the Laboratoire bordelais de recherche en informatique (LaBRI), a professor at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux), and a former junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Muscholl was born in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), came to Germany as a teenage refugee in 1984, and won first place in two German national mathematics competitions (the Bundeswettbewerb Mathematik) in 1985 and 1986. She earned a master's degree at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich), and completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) in 1994. Her dissertation, *Über die Erkennbarkeit unendlicher Spuren*, was supervised by Volker Diekert and published by Teubner Verlag in 1996. She also earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the University of Stuttgart in 1999.

After becoming a professor at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1999, she moved to the University of Bordeaux in 2006.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Muscholl was a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) from 2007 to 2012. She won the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 2010.
