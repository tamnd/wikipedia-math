# Graham Priest

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graham Priest"}

**Graham Priest** (born 1948) is a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) philosopher and logician who is distinguished professor of philosophy at the [CUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUNY) [Graduate Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center), as well as a regular visitor at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), where he was Boyce Gibson Professor of Philosophy and also at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Priest was educated at [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge)[^o6] and the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics). His thesis advisor was [John Lane Bell](wikipedia://en/25759134). He also holds a [DLitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DLitt) from the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).[^o7]

Priest was elected a corresponding fellow of the [Australian Academy of the Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_the_Humanities) in 1995.[^o8]

In addition to his work in philosophy and logic, Priest practised [karate-do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karate-do). He is 3rd dan, International Karate-do Shobukai; 4th dan, [shitō-ryū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shitō-ryū), and an Australian National [kumite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumite) referee and [kata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kata) judge. Presently, he practices [tai chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_chi).

## Philosophical work {#Philosophical_work}

Priest is known for his defence of [dialetheism](wikipedia://en/591359), his in-depth analyses of the [logical paradoxes](wikipedia://en/24390) (holding the thesis that there is a uniform treatment for many well-known paradoxes, such as the semantic, set-theoretic and [liar paradoxes](wikipedia://en/17967)), and his many writings related to [paraconsistent](wikipedia://en/421085) and other [non-classical logics](wikipedia://en/3192556). In these he draws on the history of philosophy, including [Asian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_philosophy).

Priest, a long-time resident of Australia, now residing in New York City, is the author of numerous books (most notably the textbook *[An Introduction to Non-Classical Logic](wikipedia://en/78805640)*), and has published articles in nearly every major philosophical and logical journal. He was a frequent collaborator with the late [Richard Sylvan](wikipedia://en/433347), a fellow proponent of dialetheism and paraconsistent logic.

Priest has also published on [metaphilosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphilosophy) (*Beyond the Limits of Thought*, 1995/2002).

### Hegel {#Hegel}

In his 1989 essay, *Dialectic and Dialetheic*,[^o11] Priest has put forward an interpretation of [Hegel](wikipedia://en/12598)'s *[Science of Logic](wikipedia://en/644374)* as a kind of "paraconsistent" logic, i.e. a dialetheic logic that allows for the existence of true contradictions.[^o12]
