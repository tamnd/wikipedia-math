# Universal logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Universal logic"}

**Universal logic** is the field of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) that studies the features common to all logical systems, aiming to be to logic what [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200) is to [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). A number of approaches to universal logic have been proposed since the twentieth century, using [model theoretical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_theoretical), and [categorical](wikipedia://en/5869) approaches.

:::

## History and development {#History_and_development}

The roots of universal logic as general theory of logical systems may go as far back as some work of [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920) in the early twentieth century and [Paul Herz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Herz) in 1922, but the modern notion was first presented in the 1990s by Swiss logician [Jean-Yves Béziau](wikipedia://en/3661969).[^o3][^o4] The term 'universal logic' has also been separately used by logicians such as [Richard Sylvan](wikipedia://en/433347) and [Ross Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Brady) to refer to a new type of (weak) [relevant logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relevant_logic).[^o5]

In the context defined by Béziau, three main approaches to universal logic have been explored in depth:[^o6]

- An [abstract model theory](wikipedia://en/31474975) system axiomatized by [Jon Barwise](wikipedia://en/912858),[^o7]
- a [topological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological)/categorical approach based on [sketches](wikipedia://en/20494787) (sometimes called categorical model theory),[^o8]
- a [categorical](wikipedia://en/5869) approach originating in Computer Science based on [Goguen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Goguen) and [Burstall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rod_Burstall)'s notion of [institution](wikipedia://en/2156522).[^o9]

While logic has been studied for centuries, Mossakowski et al commented in 2007 that "it is embarrassing that there is no widely acceptable formal definition of "a logic".[^o10] These approaches to universal logic thus aim to address and formalize the nature of what may be called 'logic' as a form of "sound reasoning".[^o10]

## Community {#Community}

Since 2005, [Béziau](wikipedia://en/3661969) has been organizing world congresses and schools on universal logic.

- [First World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/one1.html), 26 March–3 April 2005, [Montreux, Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreux,_Switzerland). Participants included [Béziau](wikipedia://en/3661969), [Dov Gabbay](wikipedia://en/3662032), and [David Makinson](wikipedia://en/3662198). (Secret Speaker: [Saul Kripke](wikipedia://en/48039).)
- [Second World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/enter2.html), 16–22 August 2007, [Xi'an, China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an,_China).
- [Third World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/enter-lisbon), 18–25 April 2010, [Lisbon, Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon,_Portugal). (Secret Speaker: [Jaakko Hintikka](wikipedia://en/1710903).)
- [Fourth World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/enter-rio), 29 March–7 April 2013, [Rio de Janeiro, Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro,_Brazil).
- [Fifth World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/enter-istanbul), 20–30 June 2015, [Istanbul, Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul,_Turkey).
- [Sixth World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://www.uni-log.org/vichy2018), 16–26 June 2018, [Vichy, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichy,_France).
- [Seventh World Congress and School on Universal Logic](http://sites.google.com/view/unilog-2022), 1–11 April 2022, [Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crete).

## Publications in the field {#Publications_in_the_field}

A journal dedicated to the field, *[Logica Universalis](wikipedia://en/25898954)*, with Béziau as [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) started to be published by [Birkhäuser Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser_Basel) (an imprint of [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media)) in 2007.[^o13] Springer also started to publish a [book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_series) on the topic, *Studies in Universal Logic*, with Béziau as series editor.[^o14]

An anthology titled *Universal Logic* was published in 2012, giving a new light on the subject.[^o15]
