# Quasi-isomorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasi-isomorphism"}

In [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quasi-isomorphism** or **quism** is a morphism *A* → *B* of [chain complexes](wikipedia://en/138404) (respectively, cochain complexes) such that the induced morphisms

:::

$$H_n(A_\bullet) \to H_n(B_\bullet)\ (\text{respectively, } H^n(A^\bullet) \to H^n(B^\bullet))$$

of [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) groups (respectively, of cohomology groups) are [isomorphisms](wikipedia://en/14828) for all *n*.

In the theory of [model categories](wikipedia://en/2895304), quasi-isomorphisms are sometimes used as the class of [weak equivalences](wikipedia://en/34615176) when the objects of the category are chain or cochain complexes. This results in a homology-local theory, in the sense of [Bousfield localization](wikipedia://en/40776006) in [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831).
