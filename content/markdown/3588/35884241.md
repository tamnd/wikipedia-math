# Gabriel–Popescu theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel–Popescu theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Gabriel–Popescu theorem** is an embedding theorem for certain [abelian categories](wikipedia://en/45063), introduced by (txt). It characterizes certain abelian categories (the [Grothendieck categories](wikipedia://en/30051538)) as [quotients](wikipedia://en/48897477) of [module categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Module_categories).

:::

There are several generalizations and variations of the Gabriel–Popescu theorem, given by (Kuhn 1994) (for an [AB5 category](wikipedia://en/30051646) with a set of [generators](wikipedia://en/9550415)), (Lowen 2004), (Porta 2010) (for [triangulated categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangulated_categories)).

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Theorem"}

## Theorem {#Theorem}

Let *A* be a [Grothendieck category](wikipedia://en/30051538) (an [AB5 category](wikipedia://en/30051646) with a generator), *G* a generator of *A* and *R* be the [ring of endomorphisms](wikipedia://en/59623) of *G*; also, let *S* be the [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) from *A* to Mod-*R* (the category of right *R*-modules) defined by *S*(*X*) = Hom(*G*,*X*). Then the Gabriel–Popescu theorem states that *S* is [full](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_functor) and [faithful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithful_functor) and has an [exact](wikipedia://en/453945) [left adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_functor).

This implies that *A* is [equivalent](wikipedia://en/379051) to the [Serre quotient category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_quotient_category) of Mod-*R* by a certain [localizing subcategory](wikipedia://en/44974120) *C*. (A localizing subcategory of Mod-*R* is a full subcategory *C* of Mod-*R*, closed under arbitrary [direct sums](wikipedia://en/2084687), such that for any [short exact sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_exact_sequence) of modules $0\rightarrow M_1\rightarrow M_2\rightarrow M_3\rightarrow 0$, we have *M<sub>2</sub>* in *C* if and only if *M<sub>1</sub>* and *M<sub>3</sub>* are in *C*. The Serre quotient of Mod-*R* by any localizing subcategory is a Grothendieck category.) We may take *C* to be the [kernel](wikipedia://en/142616) of the left adjoint of the functor *S*.

Note that the embedding *S* of *A* into Mod-*R* is [left-exact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_exact_functor) but not necessarily right-exact: cokernels of morphisms in *A* do not in general correspond to the cokernels of the corresponding morphisms in Mod-*R.*

:::
