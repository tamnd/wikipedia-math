# Wolfe duality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfe duality"}

In [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033), **Wolfe duality**, named after [Philip Wolfe](wikipedia://en/41154952), is type of [dual problem](wikipedia://en/3372717) in which the [objective function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Objective_function) and constraints are all [differentiable functions](wikipedia://en/330206). Using this concept a lower bound for a minimization problem can be found because of the [weak duality](wikipedia://en/34390896) principle.[^o2]

:::

## Mathematical formulation {#Mathematical_formulation}

For a minimization problem with inequality constraints,

$$\begin{aligned} &\underset{x}{\operatorname{minimize}}& & f(x) \\ &\operatorname{subject\;to} & &g_i(x) \leq 0, \quad i = 1,\dots,m \end{aligned}$$

the [Lagrangian dual problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_duality) is

$$\begin{aligned} &\underset{u}{\operatorname{maximize}}& & \inf_x \left(f(x) + \sum_{j=1}^m u_j g_j(x)\right) \\ &\operatorname{subject\;to} & &u_i \geq 0, \quad i = 1,\dots,m \end{aligned}$$

where the objective function is the Lagrange dual function. Provided that the functions $f$ and $g_1, \ldots, g_m$ are convex and continuously differentiable, the [infimum](wikipedia://en/39382) occurs where the gradient is equal to zero. The problem

$$\begin{aligned} &\underset{x, u}{\operatorname{maximize}}& & f(x) + \sum_{j=1}^m u_j g_j(x) \\ &\operatorname{subject\;to} & & \nabla f(x) + \sum_{j=1}^m u_j \nabla g_j(x) = 0 \\ &&&u_i \geq 0, \quad i = 1,\dots,m \end{aligned}$$

is called the Wolfe dual problem.[^o4] This problem employs the [KKT conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KKT_conditions) as a constraint. Also, the equality constraint $\nabla f(x) + \sum_{j=1}^m u_j \nabla g_j(x)$ is nonlinear in general, so the Wolfe dual problem may be a nonconvex optimization problem. In any case, weak duality holds.[^o5]
