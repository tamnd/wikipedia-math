# Differential algebraic geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Differential algebraic geometry"}

**Differential algebraic geometry** is an area of [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587) that adapts concepts and methods from [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and applies them to systems of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), especially [algebraic differential equations](wikipedia://en/9501827).

:::

Another way of generalizing ideas from algebraic geometry is [diffiety theory](wikipedia://en/22279266).
