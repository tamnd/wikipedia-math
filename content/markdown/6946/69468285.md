# Satoshi Suzuki (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Satoshi Suzuki"}

**Satoshi Suzuki** (24 June 1930 – 11 August 1991) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902),[^o3] and a professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).[^o4]

:::

- 5 articles[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16] written by Suzuki are cited in the textbook "Homologie des algèbres commutatives".[^o17]

## Life {#Life}

Satoshi Suzuki was born on 24 June 1930 in [Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya). He entered [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1949. He studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in his undergraduate cours. His adviser in graduate course was [Yasuo Akizuki](wikipedia://en/70092474) from 1953 to 1958, but his research ideas were developed by his own. He wrote a paper "Note on the existence of rational points"[^o19] published in the Proceedings of the Japan Academy in 1958.[^o20]

- 1958 - 59 taught at Kyoto Women's University
- 1959 - 61 taught at Momoyama Gakuin, Sakai, Osaka
- 1963 associate professor at Kyoto University
- 1964 submitted his doctoral thesis "Some results on m-adic differentials" to Kyoto University
- 1965 - 67 Purdue University, West Lafayette, Indiana, USA
- 1967 - 68 Queen's University, Kingston, Canada
- 1970 full professor at Kyoto University

[Paulo Ribenboim](wikipedia://en/9129242) wrote about Suzuki that "I was very interested in his work on differentials, especially the higher order differentials and I attended his lectures with profit. At my suggestion, Suzuki wrote up his lecture notes ....[^o21]"
