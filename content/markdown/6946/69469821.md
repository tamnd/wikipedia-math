# John A. Adam (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John A. Adam"}

**John Anthony Adam** is a British-American applied mathematician known for his work on [patterns in nature](wikipedia://en/35659147) and on mathematical modeling of the growth patterns of [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer) and [blood vessels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_vessel). He is University Professor of Mathematics at [Old Dominion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Dominion_University) in [Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Adam is a 1971 graduate, with first-class honours, from [Queen Elizabeth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_College). He completed his Ph.D. in 1974 at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London). His dissertation, *A Theoretical Study of Magnetohydrodynamic Processes in Solar Active Regions*, was jointly supervised by astrochemist Gillian Peach and astrophysicist [Carole Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carole_Jordan).

After working as a researcher in theoretical astronomy and applied mathematics, respectively at the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex) and [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), he became a lecturer in mathematics at the [New University of Ulster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_University) in 1978, while also taking a research affiliation at the [Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Institute_for_Advanced_Studies). In 1983 he came to the US as a [Fulbright scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_scholar) at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), and in 1984 he moved to Old Dominion University. He was named University Professor there in 1999.

## Book {#Book}

Adam is the author of books including:

- *A Survey of Models for Tumor-Immune System Dynamics* (edited with Nicola Bellomo, Springer, 1997)
- *Mathematics in Nature: Modeling Patterns in the Natural World* (Princeton University Press, 2003)
- *Guesstimation: Solving the World's Problems on the Back of a Cocktail Napkin* (with Lawrence Weinstein, Princeton University Press, 2008)
- *A Mathematical Nature Walk* (Princeton University Press, 2009)
- *X and the City: Modeling Aspects of Urban Life* (Princeton University Press, 2012)
- *Rays, Waves, and Scattering: Topics in Classical Mathematical Physics* (Princeton University Press, 2017)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2007, the State Council of Higher Education in Virginia (SCHEV) gave Adam their Outstanding Faculty Award.

In 2012, Adam won the [Carl B. Allendoerfer Award](wikipedia://en/40343255) of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for an exposition of blood vessel modeling.
