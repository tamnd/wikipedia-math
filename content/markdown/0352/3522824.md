# Triangular bifrustum

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Triangular bifrustum"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triangular bifrustum** is the second in an infinite series of [bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3521796) polyhedra. It has 6 [trapezoid](wikipedia://en/201826) and 2 [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) faces. It may also be called the **truncated triangular bipyramid**; however, that term is ambiguous, as it may also refer to polyhedra formed by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) all five vertices of a [triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/646933).[^o3]

:::

This polyhedron can be constructed by taking a [triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/646933) and truncating the polar axis vertices, making it into two end-to-end [frustums](wikipedia://en/89405). It appears as the form of certain nanocrystals.[^o4][^o5]

A truncated triangular bipyramid can be constructed by connecting two stacked regular [octahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octahedra) with 3 pairs of [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra) around the sides. This represents a portion of the [gyrated alternated cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyrated_alternated_cubic_honeycomb).
