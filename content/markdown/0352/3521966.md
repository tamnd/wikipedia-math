# Truncated hexagonal trapezohedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated hexagonal trapezohedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated hexagonal trapezohedron** is the fourth in an infinite series of [truncated trapezohedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_trapezohedra). It has 12 [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) and 2 [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) faces. It can be constructed by taking a [hexagonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3504442) and [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the polar axis vertices.

:::

## Weaire–Phelan structure {#Weaire–Phelan_structure}

The [Weaire–Phelan structure](wikipedia://en/2795211) contains another form of this polyhedron that has *D*<sub>*2d*</sub> symmetry and is a part of a [space-filling honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654) along with an irregular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).
