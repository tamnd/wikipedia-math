# Assertoricity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Assertoricity"}

**Assertoric** is an [adjectival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective) expression in [Aristotelian logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotelian_logic) that refers to [propositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositions) which merely assert that something is (or is not) the case. **Assertoricity** is the corresponding [abstract noun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_noun).

:::

*Assertoric propositions* contrast with [problematic propositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problematic_proposition) which assert the possibility of something being true, and [apodeictic propositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apodeictic_proposition) which assert things which are necessarily or [self-evidently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-evident) true or false.[^o2] For instance, "Chicago is larger than Omaha" is assertoric. "A corporation could be wealthier than a country" is problematic. "Two plus two equals four" is apodeictic.
