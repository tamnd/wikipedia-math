# Donald Marquardt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Donald Marquardt"}

**Donald W. Marquardt** (March 13, 1929, New York City – July 5, 1997,[^o4] [New Castle, Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Castle,_Delaware)) was an American statistician, the rediscoverer of the [Levenberg–Marquardt nonlinear least squares fitting algorithm](wikipedia://en/892446).[^o2]

:::

Marquardt was educated at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) with bachelor's degree in 1950 in physics and mathematics and at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) with master's degree in 1956 in mathematics and statistics.[^o3] Marquardt joined [DuPont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DuPont) in 1953 and worked there for 39 years. He also founded and managed the DuPont Quality Management & Technology Center.[^o4] In 1963 he published his famous paper "algorithm for least-squares estimation of nonlinear problems" in [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) journal. Marquardt developed his algorithm to solve fitting nonlinear chemical models to laboratory data.[^o2] In 1975 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o5]

As manager of the DuPont Applied Statistics Group, he led development of the [Product Quality Management methodology and computer systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_Management) that implemented the company's continuous improvement initiatives from the mid-1970s to the late 1990s.[^o6] He won the [Shewhart Medal](wikipedia://en/831964) in 1986.

In 1991, he established his own company, Donald W. Marquardt and Associates, which provides consulting and training in quality management, quality assurance, ISO 9000 standards, applied statistics, strategic planning and organizational change.[^o4]

He died from a heart attack at the age of 68.[^o4]

## Awards and achievements {#Awards_and_achievements}

- [President of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) (1986)
- Elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).
- Leader of the U.S. delegation to the [ISO 9000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_9000) International Quality Standard writing group.
- [American Society for Quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_for_Quality) (ASQ) [Shewhart Medal](wikipedia://en/831964) (1987)
- [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) Statistician of the Year (1993–94)
- [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224) (1995)
- [American National Standards Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_National_Standards_Institute) Meritorious Service Award
