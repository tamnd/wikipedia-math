# Truncated trapezohedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated trapezohedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an $n$-gonal **truncated trapezohedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) formed by a $n$-gonal [trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/719845) with $n$-gonal [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045) [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) from its two polar axis [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).

:::

The vertices exist as 4 $n$-gons in four parallel planes, with alternating orientation in the middle creating the [pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagons).

The [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) is the most common polyhedron in this class, being a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905), with 12 [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) pentagonal faces.

A truncated trapezohedron has all vertices with 3 faces. This means that the [dual polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polyhedra), the set of [gyroelongated dipyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_dipyramid), have all triangular faces. For example, the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) is the dual of the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

- [Triangular truncated trapezohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_truncated_trapezohedron) ([Dürer's solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dürer's_solid)) – 6 pentagons, 2 triangles,[^n1] dual [gyroelongated triangular dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_triangular_dipyramid)
- [Truncated square trapezohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_square_trapezohedron) – 8 pentagons, 2 squares, dual [gyroelongated square dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_square_dipyramid)
- *Truncated pentagonal trapezohedron* or [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) – 12 pentagonal faces, dual [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084)
- [Truncated hexagonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3521966) – 12 pentagons, 2 hexagons, dual [gyroelongated hexagonal dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_hexagonal_dipyramid)
- ...
- **Truncated *n*-gonal trapezohedron** – 2*n* pentagons, 2 *n*-gons, dual [gyroelongated dipyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_dipyramid)
