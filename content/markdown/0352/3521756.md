::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Hexagonal bifrustum"}

The **hexagonal bifrustum** or **truncated hexagonal bipyramid** is the fourth in an infinite series of [bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3521796) polyhedra. It has 12 [trapezoid](wikipedia://en/201826) and 2 [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces. This polyhedron can be constructed by taking a [hexagonal dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexagonal_dipyramid) and truncating the polar axis vertices, making it into two end-to-end [frustums](wikipedia://en/89405).

:::

Several types of [crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal) take this shape.[^o5] It has also been used in the design of 14-sided [dice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice), which may be used to generate randomly chosen [playing cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playing_card).[^o6]
