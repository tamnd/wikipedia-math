# Elongated bipyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated bipyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated bipyramids** are an infinite set of [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra), constructed by elongating an $n$-gonal [bipyramid](wikipedia://en/4153) (by inserting an $n$-gonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) between its congruent halves).

:::

There are three *elongated bipyramids* that are [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034):

- [Elongated triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/1177950) ($J_{14}$),
- [Elongated square bipyramid](wikipedia://en/1177952) ($J_{15}$), and
- [Elongated pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/1177953) ($J_{16}$).

Higher forms can be constructed with [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428).

## Applications {#Applications}

Elongated bipyramids are sometimes used as [dice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice), especially to make dice with atypical side count, such as 5 or 7. Such a die has numbers written on the square faces, which are usually heightened into rectangles for convenience in rolling. Whichever number comes face-up when the die is rolled is the side that is to be read.
