# Bifrustum

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bifrustum"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an $n$-gonal **bifrustum** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) composed of three parallel planes of [$n$-gons](wikipedia://en/23621), with the middle plane largest and usually the top and bottom congruent.

:::

It can be constructed as two congruent [frusta](wikipedia://en/89405) combined across a plane of symmetry, and also as a [bipyramid](wikipedia://en/4153) with the two polar vertices truncated.[^o3]

They are [duals](wikipedia://en/8815) to the family of [elongated bipyramids](wikipedia://en/3522878).

## Formulae {#Formulae}

For a regular $n$-gonal bifrustum with the equatorial polygon sides $a$, bases sides $b$ and semi-height (half the distance between the planes of bases) $h$, the [lateral surface](wikipedia://en/16402429) [area](wikipedia://en/1209) $A_{l}$, total area $A$ and [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) $V$ are:[^o5]

$$\begin{aligned} A_l &= n (a+b) \sqrt{\left(\tfrac{a-b}{2} \cot{\tfrac{\pi}{n}}\right)^2+h^2} \\[4pt] A &= A_l + n \frac{b^2}{2 \tan{\frac{\pi}{n}}} \\[4pt] V &= n \frac{a^2+b^2+ab}{6 \tan{\frac{\pi}{n}}}h \end{aligned}$$

The volume V is twice the volume of a [frustum](wikipedia://en/89405).

## Forms {#Forms}

Three bifrusta are [duals](wikipedia://en/8815) to three [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034), $J_{14-16}$. In general, an **$n$-gonal bifrustum** has $2n$ trapezoids, 2 $n$-gons, and is dual to the [elongated dipyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongated_dipyramid).
