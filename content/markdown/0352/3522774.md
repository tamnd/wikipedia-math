# Pentagonal bifrustum

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal bifrustum"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal bifrustum** or **truncated pentagonal bipyramid** is the third in an infinite series of [bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3521796) polyhedra. It has 10 [trapezoidal](wikipedia://en/201826) and 2 [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) faces.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

The pentagonal bifrustum is the [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) of a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), the [elongated pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/1177953).

This polyhedron can be constructed by taking a [pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/728038) and [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the polar axis vertices. In [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705), it can be represented as the polyhedron "$t5\operatorname{dP}5$", meaning the truncation of the degree-five vertices of the dual of a [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659).[^o5]

Alternatively, it can be constructed by gluing together two end-to-end pentagonal [frustums](wikipedia://en/89405), or (if coplanar faces are allowed) by gluing together two pentagonal prisms on their pentagonal faces.

:::

::: {#o7 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

In [nanoparticles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanoparticle), a 15-site truncated pentagonal bipyramid structure may form the [nucleus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleation) of larger twinned structures with [five-fold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiveling) or [icosahedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icosahedral_twins) symmetry.[^o8]

:::
