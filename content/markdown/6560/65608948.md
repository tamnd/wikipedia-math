# Paula Norwood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paula Norwood"}

**Paula King Norwood** is a retired American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) who worked in the pharmaceutical industry on statistical aspects of [drug development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_development) and [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717). She is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), and a former chair of the Biopharmaceutical Section of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Norwood attended [Clarksville High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarksville_High_School_%28Arkansas%29) in [Clarksville, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarksville,_Arkansas), and graduated in 1968 from [Hendrix College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrix_College) in [Conway, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway,_Arkansas) with a major in mathematics. She has a master's degree in biostatistics from the [University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas_for_Medical_Sciences), and completed her Ph.D. in 1974 at [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech); her dissertation, *Statistical Analysis of Association between Disease and Genotype*, was supervised by Klaus Hinkelmann.

Norwood founded the statistics department of [Ortho Pharmaceutical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortho_Pharmaceutical), and became Vice President of Global Biostatistics and Data Processing for [Johnson & Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_&_Johnson). She served as chair of the Biopharmaceutical Section of the American Statistical Association in 1986.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Norwood was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1994.
