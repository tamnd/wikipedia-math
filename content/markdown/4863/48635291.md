# Stephen Bigelow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Bigelow"}

*Image: Stephen Bigelow.jpg.* Stephen Bigelow

**Stephen John Bigelow** is an Australian mathematician and professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara).[^o2] He is known for his proof that [braid groups](wikipedia://en/550138) are [linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_group), concurrently with and independently of another proof by Daan Krammer.[^o3]

:::

Bigelow earned bachelor's and master's degrees in 1992 and 1994 from the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).[^o2] He completed his PhD in 2000 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the joint supervision of [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424) and [Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson). He returned to Melbourne for two years as a research fellow before joining the UCSB faculty in 2002.[^o2]

Bigelow was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2002, speaking on [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) of braid groups.[^o5] He was a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) for 2002–2006.[^o2] In 2012 he was designated as one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
