# Pañcabodha

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pañcabodha"}

**Pañcabodha** is the title of several different [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) treatises on [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) composed by members of the [Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics](wikipedia://en/3099132). All these works are *karaṅa* texts, that is, books which explain the various computations in astronomy especially with regard to those related to the preparation of [Panchangam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panchangam)-s ([calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calendar)). They are essentially manuals of computations. The authorship of only three *Pañcabodha*-s have been identified.[^o2]

:::

The *Pañcabodha*-s generally contain five sections dealing with five topics. But this is not a strict rule. There are *Pañcabodha*-s which treat less than five topics and which treat more than five topics. The topics treated are not always the same. However many of them treat the five advanced topics in traditional Indian astronomy, namely, *[Vyātīpāta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatipāta)* (computation of the instants at which the sum of the (true) longitudes of the Sun and the Moon amounts to half a circle), *Grahaṇa* (computation of eclipses), *Chāyā* (shadow computations), *Śṛṅgonnati* (the computation of the elevation of the lunar horn, or the angle between the line of cusps and the horizontal plane) and *Mauḍhya* (computation of the instants when a planet becomes invisible due to its direction/longitude being close to that of the Sun).

## The works titled *Pañcabodha* and their commentaries {#The_works_titled_Pañcabodha_and_their_commentaries}

The various *Pañcabodha*-s are listed below. Following K. V. Sarma, the various texts are identified by assigning them numbers written in Roman numerals.[^o4]

1. *Pañcabodha* I (also called *Prakīrṇasaṅgraha*) by anonymous author: Has a commentary in Malayalam also of anonymous authorship. The work has been published with a modern commentary in Malayalam by [Kanippayyoor Sankaran Namputirippad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanippayyur_Shankaran_Namboodiripad), by Panchangam Press, [Kunnamkulam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunnamkulam), Kerala.
1. *Pañcabodha* II by anonymous author: Has a Malayalam commentary, called *pañcabodhakriyākrma* by [Mahishamangalam Sankaran Namputiri](wikipedia://en/75482735).
1. *Pañcabodha* III by [Puthumana Somayaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puthumana_Somayaji): Divided into five section dealing with *[Vyātīpāta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatipāta)*, *Grahaṇa*, *Chāyā*, *Śṛṅgonnati* and *Mauḍhya*. Has an anonymous Malayalam commentary.
1. *Pañcabodha* IV by anonymous author: Has five sections dealing with *[Vyātīpāta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatipāta)*, *Grahaṇa*, *Chāyā*, *Śṛṅgonnati* and *Mauḍhya* in 105 verses. This has several commentaries.
   1. *Laghuvivṛtī* by Nārāyaṇa (1529 CE)
   1. *Pañcabodhārpadarpaṅaṃ* by [Mahishamangalam Sankaran Namputiri](wikipedia://en/75482735)
   1. *Bālasaṅkaraṃ* by Mahishamangalam Sankaran Namputiri
   1. *Pañcabodhakriyākrama* by anonymous author
1. *Pañcabodha* V by Purușotttama
1. *Pañcabodha* VI by anonymous author. Has commentary in Malayalam called *Pañcabodhaṃ Bhāșā* of anonymous authorship.
1. *Pañcabodha* VII by anonymous author. treats the topics *[Vyātīpāta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatipāta)*, *Grahaṇa*, *Chāyā*, *Śṛṅgonnati* and *Mauḍhya*. Has a commentary in Malayalam.
1. *Pañcabodha* VIII by anonymous author. The work is in a mixture of Sanskrit and Malayalam languages.
1. *Pañcabodha* IX by anonymous author.
1. *Pañcabodha* X by also called*Pañcabodhagaṇitaṃ Bhāṣā*. the text is in Malayalam.
1. *Pañcabodha* XI by anonymous author. Has a commentary in Malayalam by Vāsuṇṇi Mūssatu of Veḷḷānaśśeri
