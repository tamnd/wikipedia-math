# Aitken Lectureship

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aitken Lectureship"}

The **Aitken Lectureship** is awarded by the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society) to a research mathematician from New Zealand. The lectureship is named for New Zealand professor [Alexander Aitken](wikipedia://en/3348690), who was a highly distinguished mathematician who spent most of his career at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh).[^o3] The lectureship was funded in 2009 by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and the New Zealand Mathematical Society, and is normally awarded every two years, alternating with the [Forder Lectureship](wikipedia://en/59046614). Recipients of the lectureship will give a several-week lecturing tour of UK universities and a lecture at the annual meeting of the London society.[^o4]

:::

## Recipients {#Recipients}

The recipients of the Aitken Lectureship are:[^o4]

- 2011: Geoff Whittle[^o6]
- 2013: Robert Mclachlan[^o7]
- 2015: Steven Galbraith[^o8]
- 2017: [Hinke Osinga](wikipedia://en/48129280)[^o9]
- 2019: Bakh Khoussainov[^o10]
- 2022: [Lisa Orloff Clark](wikipedia://en/75552327)[^o11]
