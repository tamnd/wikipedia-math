# Mauro Francaviglia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mauro Francaviglia"}

**Mauro Francaviglia** (22 June 1953 – 24 June 2013) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was a professor at [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) and he worked mainly on [geometric](wikipedia://en/8625) methods applied to [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) and [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024).

## Biography {#Biography}

Francaviglia obtained a degree in Mathematics at [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) in 1975 under the supervision of [Dionigi Galletto](wikipedia://en/41000669). In the same university, he was a fellow of the [CNR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) (the Italian National Research Council) from 1975 to 1978 and a teaching assistant from 1978 to 1980. In 1976 he was awarded the Bonavera Prize from the Accademia delle Scienze di Torino nel 1977.[^o4]

He became full Professor at University of Turin in 1980, when he was 27 years old (the youngest full professor in his discipline).[^o5] He spent the last years of his life at the [University of Calabria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calabria), in Arcavacata di Rende.

Besides his scientific activities, Francaviglia was a prominent [philatelist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philately). He has been president of the Unione Filatelica Subalpina di Torino,[^o6] vice-president of the Federazione tra le società filateliche italiane, council member of the Unione stampa filatelica italiana, member of the [Royal Philatelic Society London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Philatelic_Society_London) and council member of the Académie européenne de philatélie.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Francaviglia's scientific interests covered a wide range of topics, including the application of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586), [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024) and [field theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_theory_%28physics%29), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [symmetries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetries) and [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_laws), [quantization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantization_%28physics%29) and [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952).

He was author of over 250 papers,[^o9] three monographs and 11 encyclopaedia long entries, and he supervised 6 PhD students.[^o10]

He was director of several courses at Centro internazionale matematico estivo; he organized 20 national and international conferences, among which the 14th World Conference on General Relativity in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) (1995).[^o11]

He was member of the Scientific Council of the [INdAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INdAM) National Group for Mathematical Physics (GNFM) (1980–1996) and life member of the [International Society on General Relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_on_General_Relativity_and_Gravitation). In 1990 he founded the [Italian Society for General Relativity and Gravitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Society_for_General_Relativity_and_Gravitation) (SIGRAV), of which he was president for two terms (1992–1996 and 2008–2012). He also served as a member of the Board of the [International Society on General Relativity and Gravitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_on_General_Relativity_and_Gravitation) (IGRG) for nine years (1986–1995).

Francaviglia was co-founder (1984) and Managing Editor of *[Journal of Geometry and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Geometry_and_Physics)*,[^o12] Associate Editor of the *[Journal of General Relativity and Gravitation](wikipedia://en/27839280)* since 1999 and Managing Editor of the *[International Journal of Geometric Methods in Modern Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Geometric_Methods_in_Modern_Physics)*. A special issue of the last journal has published a collection of scientific contributions from several Francaviglia's collaborators,[^o13] presented in a workshop in his memory two years after his death.[^o14][^o15]
