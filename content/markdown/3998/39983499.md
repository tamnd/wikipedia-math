# Wait list control group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wait list control group"}

A **wait list control group**, also called a **wait list comparison**, is a group of participants included in an [outcome study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outcomes_research) that is assigned to a waiting list and receives intervention after the active treatment group. This [control group](wikipedia://en/1765232) serves as an untreated comparison group during the study, but eventually goes on to receive treatment at a later date. Wait list control groups are often used when it would be unethical to deny participants access to treatment, provided the wait is still shorter than that for routine services.[^o2]

:::
