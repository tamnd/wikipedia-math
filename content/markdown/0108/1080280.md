# SOBER-128

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SOBER-128"}

**SOBER-128** is a synchronous [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) designed by Hawkes and Rose (2003) and is a member of the [SOBER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOBER_%28cipher%29) family of [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244). SOBER-128 was also designed to provide [MAC](wikipedia://en/567523) (message authentication code) functionality.

:::

Watanabe and Furuya (2004) showed a weakness in the MAC generation of SOBER-128 which means an attack could forge a message with probability 2<sup>−6</sup>.[^o2] MAC functionality was deleted by Qualcomm from SOBER-128 reference code.

SOBER-128 takes a [key](wikipedia://en/53039) up to 128 bits in length.[^o3]
