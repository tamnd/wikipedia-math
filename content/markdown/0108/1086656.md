# Taut submanifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taut submanifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a (compact) **taut submanifold** *N* of a [space form](wikipedia://en/1087009) *M* is a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) with the property that for every $q\in M$ the [distance function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_function)

:::

$$L_q:N\to\mathbf R,\qquad L_q(x) = \operatorname{dist}(x,q)^2$$

is a perfect [Morse function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function).

If *N* is not compact, one needs to consider the restriction of the $L_q$ to any of their [sublevel sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sublevel_set).
