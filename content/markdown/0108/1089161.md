# Euler–Tricomi equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euler–Tricomi equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Euler–Tricomi equation** is a [linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear) [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) useful in the study of [transonic](wikipedia://en/247737) [flow](wikipedia://en/2684988). It is named after mathematicians [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) and [Francesco Giacomo Tricomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Giacomo_Tricomi).

:::

$$u_{xx}+xu_{yy}=0. \,$$

It is [elliptic](wikipedia://en/662787) in the half plane *x* > 0, [parabolic](wikipedia://en/7466971) at *x* = 0 and [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/1575825) in the half plane *x* \< 0. Its [characteristics](wikipedia://en/751933) are

$$x\,dx^2+dy^2=0, \,$$

which have the integral

$$y\pm\frac{2}{3}x^{3/2}=C,$$

where *C* is a constant of [integration](wikipedia://en/15532). The characteristics thus comprise two families of [semicubical parabolas](wikipedia://en/2669305), with cusps on the line *x* = 0, the curves lying on the right hand side of the *y*-axis.

## Particular solutions {#Particular_solutions}

A general expression for particular solutions to the Euler–Tricomi equations is:

$$u_{k,p,q}=\sum_{i=0}^k(-1)^i\frac{x^{m_i}y^{n_i}}{c_i} \,$$

where

$$k \in \mathbb{N}$$

$$p, q \in \{0,1\}$$

$$m_i = 3i+p$$

$$n_i = 2(k-i)+q$$

$$c_i = m_i!!! \cdot (m_i-1)!!! \cdot n_i!! \cdot (n_i-1)!!$$

These can be linearly combined to form further solutions such as:

for *k = 0*:

$$u=A + Bx + Cy + Dxy \,$$

for *k = 1*:

$$u=A(\tfrac{1}{2}y^2 - \tfrac{1}{6}x^3) + B(\tfrac{1}{2}xy^2 - \tfrac{1}{12}x^4) + C(\tfrac{1}{6}y^3 - \tfrac{1}{6}x^3y) + D(\tfrac{1}{6}xy^3 - \tfrac{1}{12}x^4y) \,$$

etc.

The Euler–Tricomi equation is a limiting form of [Chaplygin's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplygin's_equation).
