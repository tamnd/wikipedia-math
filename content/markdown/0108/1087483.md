# Projectionless C\*-algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Projectionless C*-algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **projectionless C\*-algebra** is a [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) with no nontrivial [projections](wikipedia://en/519182#Orthogonal_projections). For a [unital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unital_ring) C\*-algebra, the projections 0 and 1 are trivial. While for a non-unital C\*-algebra, only 0 is considered trivial. The problem of whether [simple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_algebra) [infinite-dimensional](wikipedia://en/38267) C\*-algebras with this property exist was posed in 1958 by [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky),[^o2] and the first example of one was published in 1981 by [Bruce Blackadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Blackadar).[^o2][^o3] For [commutative](wikipedia://en/294390) C\*-algebras, being projectionless is equivalent to its [spectrum](wikipedia://en/918414) being [connected](wikipedia://en/6233). Due to this, being projectionless can be considered as a [noncommutative](wikipedia://en/662624) analogue of a connected [space](wikipedia://en/30450).

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- **C**, the algebra of [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826).
- The [reduced group C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/381010#The_reduced_group_C.2A-algebra_Cr.2A.28G.29) of the [free group](wikipedia://en/59735) on finitely many generators.[^o6]
- The [Jiang-Su algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiang-Su_algebra) is simple, projectionless, and [KK-equivalent](wikipedia://en/13314087) to **C**.[^o7]

:::

## Dimension drop algebras {#Dimension_drop_algebras}

Let $\mathcal{B}_0$ be the class consisting of the C\*-algebras $C_0(\mathbb{R}), C_0(\mathbb{R}^2), D_n, SD_n$ for each $n \geq 2$, and let $\mathcal{B}$ be the class of all C\*-algebras of the form

$$M_{k_1}(B_1) \oplus M_{k_2}(B_2) \oplus ... \oplus M_{k_r}(B_r),$$

where $r, k_1, ..., k_r$ are [integers](wikipedia://en/14563), and where $B_1, ..., B_r$ belong to $\mathcal{B}_0$.

Every C\*-algebra A in $\mathcal{B}$ is projectionless, moreover, its only projection is 0. [^o9]
