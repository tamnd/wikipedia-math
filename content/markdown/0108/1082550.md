# Kronecker–Weber theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kronecker–Weber theorem"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), it can be shown that every [cyclotomic field](wikipedia://en/30872462) is an [abelian extension](wikipedia://en/174448) of the [rational number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_number_field) **Q**, having [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) of the form [$(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times$](wikipedia://en/20087). The **Kronecker–Weber theorem** provides a partial converse: every finite abelian extension of **Q** is contained within some cyclotomic field. In other words, every [algebraic integer](wikipedia://en/98981) whose [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) is [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) can be expressed as a sum of [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) with rational coefficients. For example,

:::

$\sqrt{5} = e^{2 \pi i / 5} - e^{4 \pi i / 5} - e^{6 \pi i / 5} + e^{8 \pi i / 5},$ $\sqrt{-3} = e^{2 \pi i / 3} - e^{4 \pi i / 3},$ and $\sqrt{3} = e^{\pi i / 6} - e^{5 \pi i / 6}.$

The theorem is named after [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450) and [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615).

## Field-theoretic formulation {#Field-theoretic_formulation}

The Kronecker–Weber theorem can be stated in terms of [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) and [field extensions](wikipedia://en/11634). Precisely, the Kronecker–Weber theorem states: every finite abelian extension of the rational numbers **Q** is a subfield of a cyclotomic field. That is, whenever an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) has a Galois group over **Q** that is an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974), the field is a subfield of a field obtained by adjoining a [root of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) to the rational numbers.

For a given abelian extension *K* of **Q** there is a *minimal* cyclotomic field that contains it. The theorem allows one to define the [conductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conductor_%28algebraic_number_theory%29) of *K* as the smallest integer *n* such that *K* lies inside the field generated by the *n*-th roots of unity. For example, the [quadratic fields](wikipedia://en/454781) have as conductor the [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991) of their [discriminant](wikipedia://en/3022963), a fact generalised in [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695).

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

(txt) proved the local Kronecker–Weber theorem which states that any abelian extension of a [local field](wikipedia://en/145375) can be constructed using cyclotomic extensions and [Lubin–Tate extensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubin–Tate_extension). (txt), (txt) and (txt) gave other proofs.

[Hilbert's twelfth problem](wikipedia://en/2164726) asks for generalizations of the Kronecker–Weber theorem to describe abelian extensions of arbitrary number fields and asks for the analogues of the roots of unity for those fields. Currently, the generalization is proven only for [CM-fields](wikipedia://en/10240442).

A different approach to abelian extensions is given by [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695).
