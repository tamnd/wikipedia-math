# Otto Frostman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Frostman"}

**Otto Albin Frostman** (3 January 1907 – 29 December 1977) was a Swedish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

Frostman earned his Ph.D. in 1935 at [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University) under the Hungarian-born mathematician [Marcel Riesz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Riesz), the younger brother of [Frigyes Riesz](wikipedia://en/1073126). In potential theory, [Frostman's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostman's_lemma) is named after him.[^o3] He supervised the 1971 Stockholm University Ph.D. thesis of Bernt Lindström, which initiated the "Stockholm School" of [topological combinatorics](wikipedia://en/17633579) (combining [simplicial homology](wikipedia://en/1060236) and [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533)).
