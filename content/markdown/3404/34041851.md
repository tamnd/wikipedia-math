# François Châtelet (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Châtelet"}

**François Châtelet** (11 September 1912 – 19 April 1987, age 75) was a mathematician at the [Université de Besançon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Besançon) who introduced the [Weil–Châtelet group](wikipedia://en/1970852) and [Châtelet surfaces](wikipedia://en/23342300). His father was the mathematician [Albert Châtelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Châtelet).

:::
