# Projective cone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Projective cone"}

A **projective cone** (or just **cone**) in [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) is the [union](wikipedia://en/47949) of all [lines](wikipedia://en/946975) that [intersect](wikipedia://en/41780044) a [projective subspace](wikipedia://en/242135)[^o2] *R* (the apex of the cone) and an arbitrary [subset](wikipedia://en/27631) *A* (the [basis](wikipedia://en/18420)) of some other [subspace](wikipedia://en/5308894) *S*, [disjoint](wikipedia://en/39359) from *R*.

:::

In the special case that *R* is a single [point](wikipedia://en/593693), *S* is a [plane](wikipedia://en/19469852), and *A* is a [conic section](wikipedia://en/19008673) on *S*, the projective cone is a [conical surface](wikipedia://en/706244); hence the name.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *X* be a projective space over some field *K*, and *R*, *S* be disjoint subspaces of *X*. Let *A* be an arbitrary subset of *S*. Then we define *RA*, the cone with top *R* and basis *A*, as follows :

- When *A* is empty, *RA* = *A*.
- When *A* is not empty, *RA* consists of all those [points](wikipedia://en/593693) on a line connecting a point on *R* and a point on *A*.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- As *R* and *S* are disjoint, one may deduce from [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and the definition of a projective space that every point on *RA* not in *R* or *A* is on exactly one line connecting a point in *R* and a point in *A*.
- (*RA*) $\cap$ *S* = *A*
- When *K* is the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of order *q*, then $|R A|$ = $q^{r+1}$$|A|$ + $\frac{q^{r+1}-1}{q-1}$, where *r* = dim(*R*).

:::
