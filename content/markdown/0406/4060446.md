# Tensor bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tensor bundle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **tensor bundle** of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) is the [direct sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_sum_of_vector_bundles) of all tensor products of the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) and the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) of that manifold. To do [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) on the tensor bundle a [connection](wikipedia://en/308815) is needed, except for the special case of the [exterior derivative](wikipedia://en/220782) of antisymmetric tensors.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A tensor bundle is a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) where the fiber is a tensor product of any number of copies of the [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) and/or [cotangent space](wikipedia://en/6620) of the base space, which is a manifold. As such, the fiber is a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) and the tensor bundle is a special kind of [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524). Explicitly, for fixed non-negative integers $p$ and $q$, a tensor bundle is the fiber bundle

$$V \otimes \cdots \otimes V \otimes V^* \otimes \cdots \otimes V^* = V^{\otimes p}\otimes (V^*)^{\otimes q}$$

where *V* is the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) of *M* and *V*<sup>∗</sup> is the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) of *M*, and its fibers are

$$V_x \otimes \cdots \otimes V_x \otimes V_x^* \otimes \cdots \otimes V_x^* = V_x^{\otimes p}\otimes (V_x^*)^{\otimes q}$$

where $V_x$ is the tangent space of *x* and $V_x^*$ is the cotangent space of *x*, for every *x* in *M*. The elements of the fibers are tensors of type $(p,q) \in \mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}$.

The direct sum of vector bundles allows to consider all these posibilities of tensor bundle for fixed $(p,q)$ as a single mathematical object

$$\bigoplus_{(p,q) \in \mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}} V^{\otimes p}\otimes (V^*)^{\otimes q}.$$

:::
