# Édouard Le Roy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Édouard Le Roy"}

*Image: Albert Leroy.jpg.* Édouard Le Roy

**Édouard Louis Emmanuel Julien Le Roy** (; 18 June 1870, Paris – 10 November 1954, Paris) was a French philosopher and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Le Roy entered the *[École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure)* in 1892, and received the *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1895. He became Doctor in Sciences in 1898, taught in several [high schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_France), and in 1909 became professor of mathematics at the *[Lycée Saint-Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Saint-Louis)* in Paris.

From then on, Le Roy took a major interest in [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics). A friend of [Teilhard de Chardin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teilhard_de_Chardin) and [Henri Bergson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Bergson)'s closer disciple,[^o3] he succeeded Bergson at the [College of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_France) (1922) and, in 1945, at the *[Académie française](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_française)*. In 1919, Le Roy was also elected a member of the *[Académie des Sciences morales et politiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences_morales_et_politiques)*.

Le Roy was especially interested in the relations between science and morality. Along with [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740) and [Pierre Duhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Duhem), he supported a [conventionalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conventionalism) thesis on the [foundation of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_of_mathematics). Although a fervent [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic), he extended this conventionalist theory to [revealed truths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revelation), which did not, according to him, withdraw any of their strength. In the domain of religious [dogmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dogma), he rejected abstract reasoning and speculative [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) in favour of instinctive faith, heart and sentiment. He was one of those close to Bergson who encouraged him to turn to the study of [mysticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysticism), explored in his later works. His conventionalism led his works, accused of [modernism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modernism), to be placed on the [Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_Librorum_Prohibitorum) by the [Holy See](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_See).[^o4]

Le Roy was married and had one child.[^o5]
