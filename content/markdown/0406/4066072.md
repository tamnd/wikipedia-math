# Fish curve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fish curve"}

*Image: Fish curve.svg.* The fish curve with scale parameter *a* = 1

A **fish curve** is an ellipse [negative pedal curve](wikipedia://en/8892726) that is shaped like a [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish). In a fish curve, the pedal point is at the [focus](wikipedia://en/1032607) for the special case of the squared [eccentricity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eccentricity_%28geometry%29) $e^2=\tfrac{1}{2}$. The [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108) for a fish curve correspond to those of the associated [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277).

:::

## Equations {#Equations}

For an ellipse with the parametric equations

$$\textstyle {x=a\cos(t), \qquad y=\frac {a\sin(t)}{\sqrt {2}}},$$

the corresponding fish curve has parametric equations

$$\textstyle {x=a\cos(t)-\frac {a\sin^2 (t)}{\sqrt 2}, \qquad y=a\cos(t)\sin(t)}.$$

When the origin is [translated](wikipedia://en/47331006) to the node (the crossing point), the [Cartesian equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_equation) can be written as:

$$\left(2x^2+y^2\right)^2-2 \sqrt {2} ax\left(2x^2-3y^2\right)+2a^2\left(y^2-x^2\right)=0.$$

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

### Area {#Area}

The area of a fish curve is given by:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \frac {1}{2}\left|\int{\left(xy'-yx'\right)dt}\right| \\ &= \frac {1}{8}a^2\left|\int{\left[3\cos(t)+\cos(3t)+2\sqrt {2}\sin^2(t)\right]dt}\right|, \end{aligned}$$

so the area of the tail and head are given by:

$$\begin{aligned} A_{\text{Tail}} &= \left(\frac {2}{3}-\frac {\pi}{4\sqrt {2}}\right)a^2, \\ A_{\text{Head}} &= \left(\frac {2}{3}+\frac {\pi}{4\sqrt {2}}\right)a^2, \end{aligned}$$

giving the overall area for the fish as:

$$A = \frac {4}{3}a^2.$$

### Curvature, arc length, and tangential angle {#Curvature,_arc_length,_and_tangential_angle}

The [arc length](wikipedia://en/1898401) of the curve is given by

$$a\sqrt {2}\left(\frac {1}{2}\pi+3\right).$$

The curvature of a fish curve is given by:

$$K(t) = \frac {2\sqrt {2}+3\cos(t)-\cos(3t)}{2a\left[\cos^4 t+\sin^2 t+\sin^4 t+\sqrt {2}\sin(t)\sin(2t)\right]^\frac {3}{2}},$$

and the [tangential angle](wikipedia://en/15785733) is given by:

$$\phi(t)=\pi-\arg\left(\sqrt {2}-1-\frac {2}{\left(1+\sqrt {2}\right)e^{it} -1}\right),$$

where $\arg(z)$ is the complex argument.
