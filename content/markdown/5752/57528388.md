# María Manzano

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="María Manzano"}

Manzano

**María Gracia Manzano Arjona** (born 1950) is a Spanish philosopher specializing in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858).

:::

Manzano earned her Ph.D. in 1977 from the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona). Her dissertation, *Sistemas generales de la lógica de segundo orden* \[General systems of [second-order logic](wikipedia://en/341428)\], was supervised by [Jesús Mosterín](wikipedia://en/22198560). She is a professor of logic and the [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science) at the [University of Salamanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salamanca).

She is the author of several books on logic and model theory:

- Manzano, María. (1989). "Teoría de modelos". Alianza Editorial. isbn:9788420681269. <https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277269984>

Manzano, María. (1999). "Model Theory". 37. Oxford University Press. isbn:978-0198538516

- Manzano, María. (1996). "Extensions of First Order Logic". 19. Cambridge University Press. isbn:0521354358
- Manzano, María; Huertas, Antonia. (2004). "Lógica para principiantes". Alianza Editorial
