# Aimee Johnson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aimee Johnson"}

**Aimee Sue Anastasia Johnson** is an American mathematician who works in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). She is a professor of mathematics at [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College), the winner of the [George Pólya Award](wikipedia://en/36409098), and the co-author of the book *Discovering Discrete Dynamical Systems*.

:::

Johnson graduated from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1984. She completed her Ph.D. in 1990 at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park); her dissertation, *Measures on the Circle Invariant for a Nonlacunary Subsemigroup of the Integers*, was supervised by Daniel Rudolph.

In dynamical systems, Johnson is known for her work on a conjecture of [Hillel Furstenberg](wikipedia://en/6450152) on the classification of invariant measures for the action of two independent modular multiplication operations on an interval. In 1998, Johnson and [Kathleen Madden](wikipedia://en/57522707) won the George Pólya Award for their joint paper on [aperiodic tiling](wikipedia://en/868145), "Putting the Pieces Together: Understanding Robinson's Nonperiodic Tilings". In 2017, Madden, Johnson, and their co-author [Ayşe Şahin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayşe_Şahin) published the textbook *Discovering Discrete Dynamical Systems* through the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). With Joseph Auslander and Cesar E. Silva she is also the co-editor of *Ergodic Theory, Dynamical Systems, and the Continuing Influence of John C. Oxtoby* (Contemporary Mathematics 678, American Mathematical Society, 2016).
