# Temporal mean

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Temporal mean"}

The **temporal mean** is the [arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/612) of a [series of values over a time period](wikipedia://en/406624). Assuming equidistant measuring or sampling times, it can be computed as the sum of the values over a period divided by the number of values. A simple [moving average](wikipedia://en/990809) can be considered to be a sequence of temporal means over periods of equal duration. (If the time variable is [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122), the average value during the time period is the [integral](wikipedia://en/15532) over the period divided by the length of the duration of the period.)[^o2]

:::
