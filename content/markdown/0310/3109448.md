# Kruskal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kruskal"}

**Kruskal** may refer to any of the following, of whom the first three are brothers:

:::

- [William Kruskal](wikipedia://en/3613880) (1919–2005), American mathematician and statistician
- [Martin David Kruskal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_David_Kruskal) (1925–2006), American mathematician and physicist
- [Joseph Kruskal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kruskal) (1928–2010), American mathematician, statistician and computer scientist, known for [Kruskal's algorithm](wikipedia://en/53776)
- [Clyde Kruskal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clyde_Kruskal) (born 1954), American computer scientist, son of Martin
