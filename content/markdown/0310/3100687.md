# Quasiregular representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasiregular representation"}

In mathematics, **quasiregular representation** is a concept of representation theory, for a [locally compact group](wikipedia://en/1651204) *G* and a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325) *G*/*H* where *H* is a [closed subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_subgroup).[^o2]

:::

In line with the concepts of [regular representation](wikipedia://en/360011) and [induced representation](wikipedia://en/357452), *G* acts on functions on *G*/*H*. If however [Haar measures](wikipedia://en/40645) give rise only to a [quasi-invariant measure](wikipedia://en/2749048) on *G*/*H*, certain 'correction factors' have to be made to the action on functions, for

*L*<sup>2</sup>(*G*/*H*)

to afford a [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of *G* on [square-integrable functions](wikipedia://en/28680558). With appropriate scaling factors, therefore, introduced into the action of *G*, this is the *quasiregular representation* or modified induced representation.
