# Barlow surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barlow surface"}

In mathematics, a **Barlow surface** is one of the [complex surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_surface) discovered by (txt). They are simply connected surfaces of general type with *p<sub>g</sub>* = 0. They are homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic to a projective plane blown up in 8 points. ## Detection of exotic smooth structures {#Detection_of_exotic_smooth_structures}

According to [Freedman's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedman_classification), a simply connected oriented closed [topological](wikipedia://en/2119193) 4-manifold with [intersection form](wikipedia://en/19057150) $[+1]\oplus 8[-1]$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{C}P^2\# 8\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2}$. Any such smooth manifold with a non-vanishing [Seiberg–Witten invariant](wikipedia://en/14407762) has an exotic smooth structure since $\mathbb{C}P^2\# 8\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2}$ with the induced smooth structure allows a [Riemannian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) of positive [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) (psc metric) and therefore has only vanishing Seiberg–Witten invariants.[^o4]
