# Horrocks–Mumford bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horrocks–Mumford bundle"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Horrocks–Mumford bundle** is an indecomposable rank 2 [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) on 4-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) *P*<sup>4</sup> introduced by (txt). It is the only such bundle known, although a generalized construction involving [Paley graphs](wikipedia://en/5719307) produces other rank 2 [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) (Sasukara et al. 1993). The zero sets of sections of the Horrocks–Mumford bundle are [abelian surfaces](wikipedia://en/974181) of degree 10, called **Horrocks–Mumford surfaces**.

:::

By computing [Chern classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_classes) one sees that the second [exterior power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_power) $\wedge^2 F$ of the Horrocks–Mumford bundle *F* is the line bundle *O(5)* on *P<sup>4</sup>*. Therefore, the zero set *V* of a general section of this bundle is a [quintic threefold](wikipedia://en/23562429) called a **Horrocks–Mumford quintic**. Such a *V* has exactly 100 nodes; there exists a small resolution *V′* which is a [Calabi–Yau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabi–Yau) threefold fibered by Horrocks–Mumford surfaces.
