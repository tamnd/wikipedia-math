# Ruziewicz problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruziewicz problem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Ruziewicz problem** (sometimes **Banach–Ruziewicz problem**) in [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory) asks whether the usual [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) on the [*n*-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782) is characterised, up to proportionality, by its properties of being [finitely additive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely_additive), invariant under [rotations](wikipedia://en/39789), and defined on all [Lebesgue measurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_measurable) sets.

:::

This was answered affirmatively and independently for *n* ≥ 4 by [Grigory Margulis](wikipedia://en/1104948) and [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608) around 1980, and for *n* = 2 and 3 by [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747) (published 1984). It fails for the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220).

The problem is named after [Stanisław Ruziewicz](wikipedia://en/1821290).
