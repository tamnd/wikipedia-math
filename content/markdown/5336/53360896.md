# Nataša Jonoska

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nataša Jonoska"}

**Nataša Jonoska** ([Macedonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macedonian_language): Наташа Јоноска, ; born 1961,[^o3] also spelled **Natasha Jonoska**) is a Macedonian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the [University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida) known for her work in [DNA computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_computing).[^o4] Her research is about how biology performs computation, "in particular using formal models such as cellular or other finite types of automata, formal language theory symbolic dynamics, and topological graph theory to describe molecular computation".[^o5]

:::

She received her [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in mathematics and computer science from [Ss. Cyril and Methodius University of Skopje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ss._Cyril_and_Methodius_University_of_Skopje) in [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia) (now [North Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Macedonia)) in 1984. She earned her PhD in mathematics from the [State University of New York at Binghamton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binghamton_University) in 1993 with the dissertation "Synchronizing Representations of [Sofic Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofic_system)".[^o4] Her dissertation advisor was [Tom Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Head).[^o6]

In 2007, she won the [Rosenberg Tulip Award in DNA Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Nanoscale_Science,_Computation,_and_Engineering#Tulip_Award_in_DNA_Computing) for her work in applications of [Automata theory](wikipedia://en/103356) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) to [DNA nanotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_nanotechnology).[^o7] She was elected a [AAAS Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAAS_Fellow) in 2014[^o8] for advancements in understanding information processing in [molecular self-assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_self-assembly).[^o6] She is a board member for many journals including [*Theoretical Computer Science*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_Computer_Science_%28journal%29),[^o9] the *[International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Foundations_of_Computer_Science)*, *Computability*, and [*Natural Computing*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Computing_%28journal%29).[^o4] In 2022 she was awarded a [Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) Fellowship.[^o10]
