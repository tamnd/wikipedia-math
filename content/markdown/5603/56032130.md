# Loubignac iteration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Loubignac iteration"}

In applied mathematics, **Loubignac iteration** is an [iterative method](wikipedia://en/15237) in [finite element methods](wikipedia://en/18233581). It gives continuous [stress field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_field). It is named after Gilles Loubignac, who published the method in 1977.

:::
