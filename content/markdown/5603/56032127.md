# Johannes Finsterbusch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Finsterbusch"}

**Johannes F. Finsterbusch** (1 June 1855, [Colditz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colditz) – 1921) was a German mathematician,[^o2] known for his work on [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849).[^o3]

:::

Finsterbusch studied from 1873 to 1880 at Dresden and Leipzig. From 1882 to 1900 he taught as a schoolmaster (*Oberlehrer*) at the *[Realschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realschule)* in [Werdau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werdau). From 1900 he taught as a professor (*Gymnasialprofessor*) at the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in [Zwickau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zwickau). He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1904 at Heidelberg,[^o4] in 1908 at Rome,[^o5] and in 1912 at Cambridge UK.[^o6]

In 1889 he married Marie Emma Sophie Gräszer; the marriage produced five children.
