# Esakia duality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Esakia duality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Esakia duality** is a theorem establishing a correspondence between [Heyting algebras](wikipedia://en/309343) and certain ordered [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) called [Esakia spaces](wikipedia://en/26600306); more precisely, it states that the category of Heyting algebras is [dually equivalent](wikipedia://en/379051) to the category of Esakia spaces.

:::

Let **Esa** denote the category of Esakia spaces and [Esakia morphisms](wikipedia://en/26600306#Esakia_morphisms).

Let $H$ be a Heyting algebra, $X$ denote the set of [prime filters](wikipedia://en/19719) of $H$, and $\le$ denote set-theoretic inclusion on the prime filters of $H$. Also, for each $a \in H$, let $\varphi(a) = \{x \in X : a \in x$}, and let $\tau$ denote the topology on $X$ generated by $\{\varphi(a), X - \varphi(a) : a \in H$}.

Theorem:[^o2] $(X, \tau, \le)$ is an Esakia space, called the *Esakia dual* of $H$. Moreover, $\varphi$ is a Heyting algebra [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) from $H$ onto the Heyting algebra of all [clopen](wikipedia://en/233398) [up-sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Up-set) of $(X,\tau,\le)$. Furthermore, each Esakia space is isomorphic in **Esa** to the Esakia dual of some Heyting algebra.

This representation of Heyting algebras by means of Esakia spaces is [functorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functorial) and yields a dual equivalence between the categories

- **HA** of Heyting algebras and Heyting algebra [homomorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homomorphisms)

and

- **Esa** of Esakia spaces and Esakia morphisms.

Theorem:[^o2][^o3][^o4] **HA** is dually equivalent to **Esa**.

The duality can also be expressed in terms of [spectral spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_spaces), where it says that the category of Heyting algebras is dually equivalent to the category of Heyting spaces.[^o5]
