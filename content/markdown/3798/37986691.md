# Spitzer's formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spitzer's formula"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Spitzer's formula** or **Spitzer's identity** gives the joint distribution of partial sums and maximal partial sums of a collection of random variables. The result was first published by [Frank Spitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Spitzer) in 1956.[^o2] The formula is regarded as "a stepping stone in the theory of sums of independent random variables".[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Statement of theorem"}

## Statement of theorem {#Statement_of_theorem}

Let $X_1,X_2,...$ be [independent and identically distributed random variables](wikipedia://en/30876902) and define the partial sums $S_n=X_1 + X_2 + ... + X_n$. Define $R_n=\text{max}(0,S_1,S_2,...S_n)$. Then[^o8]

$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \phi_n(\alpha,\beta)t^n = \exp \left[ \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{t^n}{n} \left( u_n (\alpha) + v_n(\beta) -1 \right) \right]$$

where

$$\begin{aligned} \phi_n(\alpha,\beta) &= \operatorname E(\exp\left[ i(\alpha R_n + \beta(R_n-S_n)\right])\\ u_n(\alpha) &= \operatorname E(\exp \left[i\alpha S_n^+\right]) \\ v_n(\beta) &= \operatorname E(\exp \left[i \beta S_n^-\right]) \end{aligned}$$

and *S*<sup>±</sup> denotes (|*S*| ± *S*)/2.

::: {#o7 .proof cue=heading name="Proofs"}

### Proofs {#Proofs}

The formula was first proved by Spitzer.[^o2] A short proof was later given by Wendel.[^o8] The formula can be understood in terms of a [Rota-Baxter algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota-Baxter_algebra).
