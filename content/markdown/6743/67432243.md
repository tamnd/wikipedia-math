# Convex compactification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Convex compactification"}

In mathematics, specifically in [convex analysis](wikipedia://en/3237201), the **convex compactification** is a [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) which is simultaneously a convex subset in a [locally convex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space) in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). The convex compactification can be used for [relaxation](wikipedia://en/6347835) (as continuous extension) of various problems in [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus) and [optimization theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_theory). The additional linear structure allows e.g. for developing a differential calculus and more advanced considerations e.g. in relaxation in variational calculus or optimization theory.[^o2] It may capture both fast oscillations and concentration effects in optimal controls or solutions of variational problems. They are known under the names of relaxed or chattering controls (or sometimes [bang-bang controls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang-bang_control)) in [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) problems.

:::

The linear structure gives rise to various maximum principles as first-order necessary optimality conditions, known in optimal-control theory as [Pontryagin's maximum principle](wikipedia://en/831689). In variational calculus, the relaxed problems can serve for modelling of various microstructures arising in modelling [Ferroics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferroics), i.e. various materials exhibiting e.g. [Ferroelasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferroelasticity) (as [Shape-memory alloys](wikipedia://en/665728)) or [Ferromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferromagnetism). The first-order optimality conditions for the relaxed problems leads Weierstrass-type maximum principle.

In [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), relaxation leads to the concept of measure-valued solutions.

The notion was introduced by Roubíček in 1991.[^o2]
