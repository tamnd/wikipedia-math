# Des MacHale

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Des MacHale"}

**Desmond MacHale** (born 28 January 1946) is an Irish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18831) who is Emeritus [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Mathematics at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork).[^o7][^o3] He is an author and speaker on several subjects, including [George Boole](wikipedia://en/38298), lateral thinking puzzles, and humour. He has published over 80 books, some of which have been translated into languages including Danish, Italian, Norwegian, Spanish, German, Korean, and Japanese.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Des MacHale was born in [Castlebar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlebar), County Mayo. He earned his BSc and MSc in mathematical science at [University College Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Galway) in 1967 and 1968, and completed his PhD at the [University of Keele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keele_University) in 1972 under Hans Liebeck. Since then he has been at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork), where his research has focussed on [group](wikipedia://en/41890) and [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424), especially [Boolean rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_rings).[^o7]

In 1985 MacHale published *George Boole: His Life and Work*, the first book length biography of Boole.[^o8] In 2014, a year ahead of Boole's bicentennial, this was reissued in revised and expanded form as *The Life and Work of George Boole: A Prelude to the Digital Age*. He is considered the world's leading expert on Boole and in 2018 published another book *New Light on George Boole*, co-authored with Yvonne Cohen.[^o9][^o24]

MacHale has also authored books on other subjects, including brainteasers and he has written more than 30 books of jokes and discussions of humour.[^o10] His *Comic Sections: The Book of Mathematical Jokes, Humour, Wit and Wisdom* is a book which combines two of his interests. He has written over a dozen books of [lateral thinking](wikipedia://en/298235) problems with author [Paul Sloane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sloane_%28speaker%29); and many of these problems are featured on the [Futility Closet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futility_Closet) website.[^o11] He has written several books about the 1952 American film, *[The Quiet Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Quiet_Man)*. He has spoken at schools, on radio, and television on the subjects of mathematics, humour, and puzzles.[^o12]

MacHale designed the logo of the [Irish Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Mathematical_Society).[^o13]

He is a longtime opponent of smoking, and for decades has played a role within the Irish Association of Non-Smokers. He appeared on [RTÉ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTÉ)'s *[The Late Late Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Late_Show_%28Irish_talk_show%29)* as early as the 1980s in an attempt to educate the public about the dangers of smoking.[^o8]

His son [Dominic MacHale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_MacHale) is an actor, known for [The Young Offenders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Offenders_%28film%29).[^o14][^o15]

## Superbrain competition {#Superbrain_competition}

From 1984 to 2007, MacHale ran the Superbrain Competition at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork), including setting the questions and grading the papers.[^o17] A book of the questions (with solutions) from 1984 to 2008 was published in 2011 as *The First Twenty-Five Years of the Superbrain* by Diarmuid Early & Des MacHale.[^o18]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

On George Boole Day, 2 November 2015, University College Cork awarded MacHale an honorary doctorate of literature in recognition of his contributions to scholarship on Boole.[^o22][^o23]

On 15 October 2016 [Maths Week Ireland](wikipedia://en/65912318) presented MacHale with the inaugural "Raising Public Awareness of Maths" award.[^o24][^o25] In 2026, he was made a member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy).[^o26]
