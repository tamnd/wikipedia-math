# Richard Allen Hunt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Allen Hunt"}

**Richard Allen Hunt** (16 June 1937 – 22 March 2009) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He graduated from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) in 1965 with a dissertation entitled *Operators acting on [Lorentz Spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_Spaces)*. An important result of (Hunt 1968) states that the [Fourier expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_expansion) of a function in *L*<sup>*p*</sup>, *p* > 1, converges almost everywhere. The case *p=2* is due to [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541), and for this reason the general result is called the [Carleson-Hunt theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleson-Hunt_theorem). Hunt was the 1969 recipient of the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize). He was a faculty member at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) from 1969 to 2000, when he retired as professor emeritus.[^o3]
