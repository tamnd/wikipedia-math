# Charles C. Lindner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles C. Lindner"}

**Charles Curtis "Curt" Lindner** (21 July 1938 – 21 February 2023) was an American mathematician who was interested in block designs in combinatorics such as Steiner triple systems.[^o2]

:::

Lindner was born in Tampa, Florida on 21 July 1938. He grew up in [Decatur, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decatur,_Georgia) where he graduated from [Decatur High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decatur_High_School_%28Georgia%29) in 1956. Lindner then was a part of the United States Army Reserve between 1956 and 1964. Lindner studied mathematics at [Presbyterian College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian_College) in [Clinton, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton,_South_Carolina), where he received his bachelor's degree in 1960.[^o3] Lindner taught for a year at a high school in [Jacksonville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Florida). Then he went to Emory University where he received his master's degree in 1963. After marrying, he taught for four years as an assistant professor at [Coker College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coker_University) in [Hartsville, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartsville,_South_Carolina), a women's college. He received his doctorate from Emory University in 1969 under [Trevor Evans](wikipedia://en/78108233), his dissertation *Some Embedding Theorems for Partial Latin Squares*.[^o4] He became an assistant professor in 1969, an associate professor in 1973, and a professor at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University) in 1976. From 1985 to 1990, he was an alumni professor there, and since 1994 a distinguished university professor.

He taught regularly at the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland), where he held an honorary professorship since 1994. He was also an honorary professor at the [Universita di Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania). He has been a visiting professor in [Université de Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Montréal) on several occasions and a visiting professor at the [University of Messina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Messina), the University of Waterloo, the [University of Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle_%28Australia%29) in Australia, [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech), [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University), [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) in New Zealand, *[Universidad de Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Puerto_Rico)*, [Istanbul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_University), and [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University).

In 2013 he was awarded the [Euler Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Medal).

On 10 March 1961, Lindner married Ann Richards. They had three sons: Charles Lindner, Curtis Lindner, and James Lindner.[^o3]
