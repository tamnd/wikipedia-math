# NLab

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NLab"}

The ***n*Lab** is a [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki) for research-level notes, expositions and collaborative work. It includes original research in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) with a focus on methods from [type theory](wikipedia://en/40282), [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), and [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831). The *n*Lab espouses the "*n*-point of view"[^o4] (a deliberate pun on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia)'s "neutral point of view"), which is that type theory, homotopy theory, category theory, and [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013) provide a useful, unifying viewpoint for mathematics, physics and philosophy.

:::

The *n* in *n*-point of view could refer to either [*n*-categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-category) as found in higher category theory, *n*-groupoids as found in both [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) and higher category theory, or *n*-types as found in [homotopy type theory](wikipedia://en/33974223).

## Overview {#Overview}

The *n*Lab was originally conceived to provide a repository for ideas (and even new research) generated in the comments on posts at the [*n*-Category Café](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-Category_Café), a group blog run at the time by [John C. Baez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Baez), [David Corfield](wikipedia://en/4553868) and [Urs Schreiber](wikipedia://en/41104045). Eventually the *n*Lab developed into an independent project, which has since grown to include whole research projects and encyclopedic material.[^o6]

Associated with the *n*Lab is the nForum: an online discussion forum for announcement and discussion of *n*Lab edits (the analog of Wikipedia's "talk" pages) as well as for general discussion of the topics covered in the *n*Lab. The preferred way of contacting the *n*Lab steering committee is to post on the nForum.[^o7] An experimental sub-project of the *n*Lab is the '*Publications of the*n*Lab**, intended as a [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) for refereed research articles that are published online and cross-hyperlinked with the main wiki. This sub-project appears to be inactive as of 2014.[^o8]***

The *n*Lab was set up on November 28, 2008 by [Urs Schreiber](wikipedia://en/41104045) using the [Instiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instiki) software provided and maintained by [Jacques Distler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Distler). Since May 2015 it runs on a server at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) that is funded in the context of [Steve Awodey](wikipedia://en/26946267)'s Homotopy Type Theory [MURI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MURI_%28grant%29) grant.[^o9] The domain ncatlab.org is owned by [Urs Schreiber](wikipedia://en/41104045).

The *n*Lab is listed on [MathOverflow](wikipedia://en/26227167) as a standard online mathematics reference to check before asking questions.[^o10] Many questions and answers link to the *n*Lab for background material.[^o11] It is one of two wikis mentioned by the mathematical physicist John C. Baez in his review of math blogs for the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]

There is an informal steering committee, which does not run the *n*Lab,[^o7] but exists in order to resolve issues that could cause the whole project to run into trouble.

The content of this wiki is not placed under a specific [copyright license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_license).[^o13]
