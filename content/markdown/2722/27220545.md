# Rob Grootendorst

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rob Grootendorst"}

**Rob Grootendorst** (11 February 1944, [Schiedam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schiedam) – 23 February 2000, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam)) was a Dutch [communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication) and [argumentation theory](wikipedia://en/1317383) scholar. He was professor for Dutch speech communication at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). His contributions to the argumentation field include the co-foundation of the [pragma-dialectic school](wikipedia://en/2938955) in argumentation theory.

:::

He also wrote several books on the life and works of the Dutch writer and politician [Theo Thijssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_Thijssen).

## Biography {#Biography}

Grootendorst was a teacher at an elementary school in the 1960s. He studied [Dutch studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_the_Netherlands) at the University of Amsterdam since 1980, and became a Ph.D. in 1982 in Speech Communication. The dissertation was jointly written with [Frans H. van Eemeren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frans_H._van_Eemeren). They jointly founded the [International Society for the Study of Argumentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_the_Study_of_Argumentation) in 1986.

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Argumentation, Communication, and Fallacies: A Pragma-Dialectical Perspective* (Hillsdale: Erlbaum, 1992), with van Eemeren[^o4]
- *Fundamentals of Argumentation Theory: A Handbook of Historical Backgrounds and Contemporary Developments* (Mahwah: Erlbaum, 1996), with van Eemeren[^o5]
