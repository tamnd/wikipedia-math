# Conical function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conical function"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **conical functions** or **Mehler functions** are [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) which can be expressed in terms of [Legendre functions](wikipedia://en/196402) of the first and second kind, $P^\mu_{-(1/2)+i\lambda}(x)$ and $Q^\mu_{-(1/2)+i\lambda}(x).$

:::

The functions $P^\mu_{-(1/2)+i\lambda}(x)$ were introduced by [Gustav Ferdinand Mehler](wikipedia://en/31395561), in 1868, when expanding in series the distance of a point on the axis of a cone to a point located on the surface of the cone. Mehler used the notation $K^\mu(x)$ to represent these functions. He obtained integral representation and series of functions representations for them. He also established an addition theorem for the conical functions. [Carl Neumann](wikipedia://en/915551) obtained an expansion of the functions $K^\mu(x)$ in terms of the [Legendre polynomials](wikipedia://en/100349) in 1881. Leonhardt introduced for the conical functions the equivalent of the [spherical harmonics](wikipedia://en/203056) in 1882.
