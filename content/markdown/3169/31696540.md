# Erhard Heinz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erhard Heinz"}

*Image: Erhard Heinz.jpg.* Erhard Heinz

**Erhard Heinz** (30 April 1924, [Bautzen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bautzen) – 29 December 2017, Göttingen) was a German mathematician known for his work on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), in particular the [Monge–Ampère equation](wikipedia://en/4914066). He worked as professor in Stanford, Munich and from 1966 until his retirement 1992 at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).[^o2]

:::

Heinz obtained his PhD in 1951 under the supervision of [Franz Rellich](wikipedia://en/4172897) at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

His most important scientific work deals with the existence and regularity theory of systems of non-linear partial differential equations, with applications to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He obtained important results in the theory of surfaces with prescribed mean curvature, in particular of [minimal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_surfaces), for the Weyl embedding problem, and for systems of [Monge-Ampère type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monge-Ampère_equation).

In 1994 he was awarded the [Cantor medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_medal).[^o3] His doctoral students include [Hans Wilhelm Alt](wikipedia://en/56765498), Wolf von Wahl, [Willi Jäger](wikipedia://en/33490633), Helmut Werner, Reinhold Böhme, Friedrich Tomi, and Friedrich Sauvigny.
