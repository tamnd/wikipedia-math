# Stephan Luckhaus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephan Luckhaus"}

*Image: Stephan Luckhaus.jpg.* Luckhaus at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2009

**Stephan Luckhaus** is a German mathematician who is a professor at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) working in pure and applied analysis.[^o2] His PhD was obtained in 1978 under the supervision of [Willi Jäger](wikipedia://en/33490633) at the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg). He was elected to the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) in 2007.[^o2]
