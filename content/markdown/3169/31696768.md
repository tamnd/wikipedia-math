# Emil Hilb

*Image: Emil Hilb 1908.gif.* Emil Hilb 1908

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emil Hilb"}

**Emil Hilb** (born 26 April 1882 in Stuttgart;[^o2] died 6 August 1929[^o3] in Würzburg) was a German-Jewish[^o4] mathematician who worked in the fields of [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), and [difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation). He was one of the authors of the *Enzyklopädie der mathematischen Wissenschaften* (*Encyclopedia of mathematical sciences*), contributing on the topics of [trigonometric series](wikipedia://en/4099125) and differential equations. He wrote a book on [Lamé functions](wikipedia://en/17212216).[^o5]

:::

Hilb obtained his PhD in 1903 under the supervision of [Ferdinand von Lindemann](wikipedia://en/335736). He worked as a high school mathematics teacher in [Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg) until 1906, when [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337) hired him as an assistant; in 1908 he found a position as a lecturer at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen). He won a position as a professor at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg) in 1909, in preference over [Ernst Zermelo](wikipedia://en/178655).[^o7] His students at Würzburg included [Richard Bär](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_J._Bär), who later became a distinguished experimental physicist,[^o8] [Otto Haupt](wikipedia://en/31696723), and Axel Schur.[^o5][^o6]
