# John Gordon Skellam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Gordon Skellam"}

**John Gordon Skellam** (1914-1979) was a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [ecologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecologist), who discovered the [Skellam distribution](wikipedia://en/1192757).

:::

Skellam was born in [Staffordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire). He was educated at [Hanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanley,_Staffordshire) High School where he won several scholarships including free admission to [New College in Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford). He was one of the most respected members of the British Region of the [Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society).[^o2]

In 1951, John G. Skellam developed the [reaction-diffusion](wikipedia://en/8635379) model of [invasion biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_biology_terminology). This model describes the dynamics of populations, which simultaneously develops and spreads, and provides that the invasion front moves with constant speed.[^o3] He explained on the basis of habitation [muskrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskrat) introduced to [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe) that only by chance the species may be in a place where it would have to grow.[^o4]

Skellam has provided a model that allows to take the dynamics of populations as a [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685) at any time t. Its [stochastic form](wikipedia://en/47895) is much more flexible than previous deterministic equations.[^o5]
