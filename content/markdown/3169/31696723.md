# Otto Haupt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Haupt"}

*Image: Otto Haupt 4.jpeg.* Otto Haupt.

**Otto Haupt** (born 5 March 1887 in [Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Würzburg); died 10 November 1988 in [Bad Soden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Soden)) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Haupt obtained his PhD in 1911 under the supervision of [Georg Rost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Rost) and [Emil Hilb](wikipedia://en/31696768) at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), and became a professor at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg). He retired from teaching in 1953, but continued his mathematical research for many subsequent years.[^o4]

Since 1918 he was married to Edith Hughes. Despite her Jewish ancestry, she survived the Nazi period unharmed in Erlangen, and lived to 1981.[^o4]

## Research and publications {#Research_and_publications}

Haupt specialized in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478); many of his research publications related to the [four-vertex theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-vertex_theorem) on local minima and maxima of curvature. He also wrote textbooks on [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176).[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1987, his centenary year, a birthday conference was given in his honor at the University of Erlangen.[^o4][^o7]

He was awarded honorary doctorates from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), the [University of Nantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nantes) and the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg).[^o4][^o7][^o8]
