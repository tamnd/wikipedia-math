# Ilie Popa (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ilie Popa"}

**Ilie Popa** (July 20, 1907 – July 26, 1983)[^o3] was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician and Head of the Mathematical Analysis Department at the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University). He is known for his contributions to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), he attended the [Costache Negruzzi High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costache_Negruzzi_National_College) in his native city. In 1927, he enrolled at the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iași), graduating in 1931.[^o3] Upon graduation, he became an assistant professor at the Faculty of Science of the University of Iași and began his research activity under the guidance of his advisor, [Alexander Myller](wikipedia://en/70713199). In 1932, Popa published his first papers (in differential geometry), in collaboration with Mendel Haimovici. He obtained his Ph.D. in 1934 with thesis *Contributions to Centro-Affine Differential Geometry*, in which he pursued research themes of his two mentors, Myller and [Octav Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octav_Mayer). In 1936, the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy) awarded him a two-year scholarship to pursue his postdoctoral studies in Italy and Germany; during this period, he visited [Enrico Bompiani](wikipedia://en/48385220) at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998) at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). While away, he was promoted to associate professor in the Department of Higher Algebra at the University of Iași. After a brief stint in 1942 at the [Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gheorghe_Asachi_Technical_University_of_Iași), he returned to the University of Iași as Professor. In 1948 he became the Head of the Mathematical Analysis Department, a position that he held until his retirement in 1973.[^o4] From 1944 to 1945, and again from 1965 to 1971, he was [pro-rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the university.[^o3]
