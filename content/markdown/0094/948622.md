In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **57-cell** (**pentacontaheptachoron**) is a [self-dual](wikipedia://en/609737) [abstract regular 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1211056) ([four-dimensional polytope](wikipedia://en/24979)). Its 57 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) are [hemi-dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/10393833). It also has 57 vertices, 171 edges and 171 two-dimensional faces.

:::

The symmetry order is 3420, from the product of the number of cells (57) and the symmetry of each cell (60). The symmetry abstract structure is the [projective special linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) of the 2-dimensional vector space over the finite field of 19 elements, L<sub>2</sub>(19).

It has [Schläfli type](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,5} with 5 hemi-dodecahedral cells around each edge. It was discovered by (txt).
