# Hellenic Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hellenic Mathematical Society"}

The **Hellenic Mathematical Society (HMS)** (Ελληνική Μαθηματική Εταιρεία) is a learned society which promotes the study of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece). It was founded in 1918 in [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens),[^o3] and publishes the *Bulletin of the Hellenic Mathematical Society* (Δελτίο της Ελληνικής Μαθηματικής Εταιρίας)[^o4] among other research and educational publications. It is a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).[^o5] The HMS also organizes national math competitions.[^o6]

:::

## Objectives {#Objectives}

Amongst the main objectives of the HMS are:[^o8]

- the advancement of mathematics;
- the fostering of free exchange of information between mathematicians, scientists and the society at large;
- the substantial and continuous improvement of mathematical education and the advancement of general education;
- to inform Greek mathematicians on recent progress made in science and technology and offer them practical assistance in matters which may occur during their work at school.

## Members {#Members}

As of July 2022, the HMS has 12,000 members and 39 regional branches across Greece.[^o10]
