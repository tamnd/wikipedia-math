# Bruce Bowman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce Bowman"}

**Bruce Ronald Bowman** (February 4, 1945 – December 31, 2020) was an American [aerospace engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineer). He was an [astrodynamicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrodynamicist) at the [U.S. Air Force Space Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force_Space_Command) from 2000 to 2013. He developed the [Jacchia-Bowman Density Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacchia_Reference_Atmosphere), a [reference atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_atmosphere) used widely for spacecraft [orbit determination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_determination) and [atmospheric entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry) simulations.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Bowman was born in 1945.[^o8] As a child, he saw [Sputnik 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sputnik_1) orbit overhead in 1957.[^o5] He earned a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in astronomy from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1967.[^o8] He later earned a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) from the [University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaiʻi_at_Mānoa) in 1974, where he studied satellite [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608) and [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

After graduating, Bowman joined the [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force) as a [commissioned officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioned_officer), serving as a [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_%28U.S._Air_Force%29).[^o5] He was a contractor in the federal civilian service for twenty years.

Bowman joined the [Air Force Space Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Space_Command) as an astrodynamicist in 2000. He worked on the High Accuracy Satellite Density Model (HASDM) and associated [satellite constellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_constellation) used for calibration,[^o6] serving until 2013.[^o7][^o8] He was appointed a senior [research scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Scientist) at Space Environment Technologies, where he worked until 2020. He was a senior member of the [American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institute_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics) (AIAA).

His research focused on the analysis of atmospheric [drag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_%28physics%29) on satellite orbits caused by [solar heating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_energy) and [geomagnetic storms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_storm),[^o9] influenced by research from [Luigi Giuseppe Jacchia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Giuseppe_Jacchia), Franco Verniani, and [Charles E. Roberts](wikipedia://en/82111728). In 2006 and 2008, he published a revision of Jacchia's Reference Atmosphere called the Jacchia-Bowman Density Model (JB2008)[^o10] in which he parametrized important processes in the [upper atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_atmosphere), including [density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density) variations, semiannual perturbations, and drag variations.[^o11][^o12] The model is considered the most significant improvement on [thermosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermosphere) density modeling since the 1970s. It is widely used for modeling [ballistic coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballistic_coefficient), improving collision avoidance, analyzing spacecraft [accelerometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerometer) data, and simulating [orbit decay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_decay) in transitional and [free molecular flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_molecular_flow).[^o13]

The [Committee on Space Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Space_Research) (COSPAR) adopted the Jacchia-Bowman model in the [COSPAR International Reference Atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COSPAR_International_Reference_Atmosphere), and the [International Organization for Standardization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Organization_for_Standardization) adopted the model as the standard for the upper atmosphere (IS 14222).[^o8] The [United States Space Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Space_Command) uses the model to propagate orbit predictions for every satellite in [low Earth orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_Earth_orbit).[^o8]

He died in [Colorado Springs, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Springs,_Colorado) in 2020, aged 75.[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He married Esther Bowman in 1979. The couple resided in Colorado and had three children.[^o5]

## Awards {#Awards}

- Outstanding Senior Civilian Scientist Award, [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force) (2007)[^o5]
- Rotary National Award for Space Achievement (2008)[^o5]
- Analyst Lifetime Achievement Award, [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force) (2015)[^o5]
