# Robin Sibson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robin Sibson"}

**Robin Sibson** (4 May 1944 – 19 March 2017)[^o3] was a British mathematician and educator.

:::

He was a fellow of [King's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge), professor of statistics at the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) and then vice-chancellor of the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent). He was chief executive of the [Higher Education Statistics Agency](wikipedia://en/1289636) from 2001[^o4] until 2009.[^o5] He was also a member of the Committee for Higher Education and Research (CC-HER),[^o6] the predecessor to the Steering Committee for Higher Education and Research (CDESR).[^o7]

He died on 19 March 2017 at the age of 72.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

He was the developer of [natural neighbour interpolation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_neighbour_interpolation) on discrete sets of points in space.[^o10]
