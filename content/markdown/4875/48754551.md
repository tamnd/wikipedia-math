# William Bigelow Easton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Bigelow Easton"}

**William Bigelow Easton** is an American mathematician and computer scientist who proved [Easton's theorem](wikipedia://en/4691250) about the possible values of the [continuum function](wikipedia://en/5488499). His advisor at Princeton was the mathematician and computer scientist [Alonzo Church](wikipedia://en/49731).[^o2] As of 2026, he lives in [Charlottesville, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottesville,_Virginia).[^o3]

:::
