# Ming-Jun Lai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ming-Jun Lai"}

**Ming-Jun Lai** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia). His area of research is [splines](wikipedia://en/457680) and their numerical analysis. He has published a text on splines called *Splines Functions on Triangulations*. He was born in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).

:::

Lai received a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) from [Hangzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_University) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) in 1989.[^o3] His dissertation was entitled "On Construction of Bivariate and Trivariate Vertex Splines on Arbitrary Mixed Grid Partitions" and supervised by Charles K. Chui.
