# Generalized Gauss–Newton method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Generalized Gauss–Newton method"}

The **generalized Gauss–Newton method** is a generalization of the least-squares method originally described by [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) and of [Newton's method](wikipedia://en/22145) due to [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627) to the case of constrained nonlinear least-squares problems.[^o2]

:::
