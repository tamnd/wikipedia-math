# Emmy Noether bibliography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emmy Noether bibliography"}

[Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) was a German mathematician. This article lists the publications upon which her reputation is built (in part).

:::

## Second epoch (1920–1926) {#Second_epoch_(1920–1926)}

In the second epoch, Noether turned her attention to the theory of rings. With her paper *Moduln in nichtkommutativen Bereichen, insbesondere aus Differential- und Differenzenausdrücken*, [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544) states, "It is here for the first time that the Emmy Noether appears whom we all know, and who changed the face of algebra by her work."
