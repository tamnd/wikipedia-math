# Bonnie Averbach

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bonnie Averbach"}

**Bonnie Averbach** (née **Rudolph**; 1933 – October 16, 2019) was an American mathematics and [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373) educator who worked for many years on the faculty of [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) and was known for her books in mathematics.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Averbach was born in 1933, and earned a master's degree in mathematics from [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) in 1955, with the thesis "Definitions of the Lebesgue integral". Around 1965, she returned to Temple University as a faculty member. Originally affiliated with the Department of Mathematics, she changed her affiliation in 1987, to the Department of Risk Management and Insurance, where for many years she directed Temple's [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373) program. In 2007, Temple's [Fox School of Business and Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_School_of_Business_and_Management) gave her their lifetime achievement award. She died on October 16, 2019.

## Books {#Books}

Averbach's books include:

- *[Problem Solving Through Recreational Mathematics](wikipedia://en/70238590)* (with Orin Chein, W. H. Freeman, 1980; reprinted by Dover, 2000)
- *Mathematics with Applications for the Management, Life, and Social Sciences* (with Bernard Kolman and Howard Anton, 3rd ed., Harcourt Brace Jovanovich, 1988; 4th ed., Saunders College Publishing, 1992)
- *Applied Finite Mathematics* (with Bernard Kolman and Howard Anton, 5th ed., Saunders College Publishing, 1992)
