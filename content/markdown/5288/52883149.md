# Uriel Rothblum

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Uriel Rothblum"}

**Uriel George** "**Uri**" **Rothblum** (אוריאל ג'ורג' "אורי" רוטבלום; Tel Aviv, March 16, 1947 – Haifa, March 26, 2012) was an Israeli mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476). From 1984 until 2012 he held the [Alexander Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Goldberg_%28chemical_engineer%29) Chair in Management Science at the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in Haifa, Israel.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Rothblum was born in Tel Aviv to a family of Jewish immigrants from Austria.[^o5] He went to [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), where [Robert Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Aumann) became his mentor; he earned a bachelor's degree there in 1969 and a master's in 1971. He completed his doctorate in 1974 from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), in operations research, under the supervision of [Arthur F. Veinott](wikipedia://en/82736707). After postdoctoral research at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), he joined the [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) faculty in 1975, and moved to the Technion in 1984.[^o4]

Rothblum became president of the Israeli Operational Research Society (ORSIS) for 2006–2008, and editor-in-chief of *[Mathematics of Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Operations_Research)* from 2010 until his death.[^o4] He was elected to the 2003 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o6]
