# Matias D. Cattaneo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Matias D. Cattaneo"}

**Matias Damian Cattaneo** (born May 16, 1978) is an Argentine scientist, Professor of Economics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). His research focuses on econometrics, statistics, data science and decision science, with applications to program evaluation and causal inference. He is known for his work on [Regression discontinuity designs](wikipedia://en/16722187) and [Nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369).

:::

Cattaneo is a co-editor of [Econometric Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometric_Theory), and has served in editorial boards of leading academic journals across various disciplines, including the [Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313), [Econometrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometrica), and [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research_%28journal%29).

## Education and academic career {#Education_and_academic_career}

Cattaneo received his [Licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) from [UBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) in 2000, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2008, under supervision of [James L. Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_L._Powell_%28economist%29).[^o4] From 2008 to 2019, Cattaneo taught at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He joined [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) as a Professor in the Department of Operations Research and Financial Engineering in 2019.[^o5] He later moved to the Department of Economics in 2026.
