# Elisabeth Hagemann

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Elisabeth Hagemann"}

**Elisabeth Hagemann** (born 6 Mar 1906 in [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen),[^o6] died 1989)[^o4] was among the first female German [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) to obtain a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Her parents were Otto Hagemann, a department director at [Friedrich Krupp AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Krupp_AG), and Else Hagemann, née Clausius. Elisabeth Hagemann got her [abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur) from the Victoria school Essen (de) on 6 March 1926. She enlisted at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) in spring 1926, then at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in spring 1928, where she got her [Staatsexamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staatsexamen) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) on 4 March 1932.[^o6]

Then she worked as a school teacher in [Bad Godesberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Godesberg), [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), and [Rhine Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhine_Province). In May 1935, she became a [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) (*[Wissenschaftlicher Assistent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wissenschaftlicher_Assistent)*) of [Otto Toeplitz](wikipedia://en/547682) at Bonn University. On 28 Apr 1937, she obtained her Ph.D.[^o6][^o7][^o8] At that time, her main advisor, Otto Toeplitz, had already been dismissed due to the Nazi [Civil Service Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Service_Law).[^o9]
