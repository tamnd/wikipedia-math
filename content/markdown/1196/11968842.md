# William Arthur Kirk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Arthur Kirk"}

**William Arthur ("Art") Kirk** was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His research interests include [nonlinear functional analysis](wikipedia://en/8546912), the geometry of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) and [metric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_spaces). In particular, he made notable contributions to the [fixed point theory](wikipedia://en/449738) of metric spaces; for example, he is one of the two namesakes of the [Caristi-Kirk fixed point theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caristi-Kirk_fixed_point_theorem) of 1976. He is also known for the [Kirk theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirk_theorem) of 1964.

:::

He completed his PhD, entitled "Metrization of Surface Curvature", at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) in August 1962 under the supervision of [Leonard Blumenthal](wikipedia://en/2354487). He then became an assistant professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) from 1962 to 1967. Starting in 1967 he worked at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa), as a full professor of mathematics since 1971 and as department chair from 1985 to 1991.

He held an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from [Maria Curie-Skłodowska University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Curie-Skłodowska_University), an institution which was an early centre of study for the fixed point theory of metric spaces.

He died on October 20th, 2022. [^o2]
