# Italian Mathematical Union

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Italian Mathematical Union"}

The **Italian Mathematical Union** (Unione Matematica Italiana) is a [mathematical society](wikipedia://en/1098209) based in [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).

:::

It was founded on December 7, 1922, by [Luigi Bianchi](wikipedia://en/701065), [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra), and most notably, [Salvatore Pincherle](wikipedia://en/11950956), who became the Union's first President.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## History {#History}

[Salvatore Pincherle](wikipedia://en/11950956), professor at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna), sent on 31 March 1922 a letter to all Italian mathematicians in which he planned the establishment of a national mathematical society. The creation was inspired by similar initiatives in other countries, such as the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205) (1872), the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung) (1891), the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (1891) and, above all, the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (1920).

The most important Italian mathematicians of the time - among all [Luigi Bianchi](wikipedia://en/701065) and [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra) - encouraged Pincherle's initiative also by personally sending articles for the future Bulletin; overall, about 180 mathematicians replied to Pincherle's letter. On December 7 of the same year the first meeting was held.

In 1928 the Italian Mathematical Union hosted the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna).

## Activities {#Activities}

The Union's journal is the *[Bollettino dell'Unione Matematica Italiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollettino_dell'Unione_Matematica_Italiana)*, which contains two sections: one for research papers, and one for expository articles.

The Italian Mathematical Union awards the following prizes:

- the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize)
- the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352)
- the [Baldassarri Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldassarri_Prize)
- the [Vinti Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinti_Prize)
- the [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal)
- the Tricerri Prize
