# Rake (angle)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rake"}

**Rake** describes the [angle](wikipedia://en/1196) made by a sloped surface with respect to a horizontal or vertical reference. The usages of this term include:

:::

- A [motorcycle or bicycle fork rake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_and_motorcycle_geometry#Fork_offset), the angle at which the forks are angled down towards the ground
- [Rake angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rake_angle) in [machining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machining) and [sawing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saw), the angle of a cutting head
- [Rake (geology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rake_%28geology%29), the angle at which one rock moves against another in a geological fault
- [Rake (theatre)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rake_%28theatre%29), the slope of a stage angled towards the audience for a better view
