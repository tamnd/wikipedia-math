# Sündüz Keleş

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sündüz Keleş"}

**Sündüz Keleş** is a Turkish-American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) specializing in statistical methods in [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics). She is a professor of statistics and of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) and [medical informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_informatics) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). Her research has included the development of the FreeHi-C system for generating synthetic Hi-C (all-versus-all [chromosome conformation capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromosome_conformation_capture)) data.

:::

Keleş studied [industrial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering) at [Bilkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_University), but became interested in statistics after working there on a project involving [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259). She then moved to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) for graduate study, working with [Mark van der Laan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_van_der_Laan) on [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878). She completed her Ph.D. there in 2003, and took her faculty position at Wisconsin after completing her doctorate, with a delay of a year to do postdoctoral research on [microarray analysis techniques](wikipedia://en/7766542) with van der Laan and [Sandrine Dudoit](wikipedia://en/60883328).

Keleş was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2023.
