# Forum Geometricorum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Forum Geometricorum"}

***Forum Geometricorum: A Journal on Classical Euclidean Geometry*** was a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that specialized in mathematical research papers on [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417).[^o3]

:::

Founded in 2001, it was published by [Florida Atlantic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Atlantic_University) and was indexed by [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)[^o4] and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).[^o5] Its founding editor-in-chief was [Paul Yiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Yiu), a professor of mathematics at Florida Atlantic.[^o6]

In 2019, Forum Geometricorum published what was later announced to be its final issue, and stopped accepting submissions, after the retirement of Yiu.[^o6]

Prior issues are still available. Volumes for 2001 to 2009 can be accessed as a single searchable file (see below). Individual articles up to 2019 are available from Internet Archive (see below).
