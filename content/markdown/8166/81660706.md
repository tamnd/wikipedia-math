# Alexander Lytchak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Lytchak"}

**Alexander Lytchak** is a German mathematician and a professor of mathematics at the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology). He is known for his work in differential and metric geometry and also as a chess player.[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Alexander Lytchak was born in 1978 in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad). As a school student, he participated in the Russian mathematical olympiads and in the German national mathematics competition, the *Bundeswettbewerb Mathematik*. He studied mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). He earned his PhD in 2001 under the supervision of [Werner Ballmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Ballmann).

In 2012, he was appointed professor of mathematics at the University of Cologne. He later joined the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology).
