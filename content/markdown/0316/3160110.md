# Robert M. Solovay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert M. Solovay"}

**Robert Martin Solovay** (born December 15, 1938) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Solovay earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1964 under the direction of [Saunders Mac Lane](wikipedia://en/336574), with a dissertation on *A Functorial Form of the Differentiable [Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/247261)*. Solovay has spent his career at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where his Ph.D. students include [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071) and [Matthew Foreman](wikipedia://en/3332703).[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Solovay's theorems include:

- [Solovay's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solovay's_theorem) showing that, if one assumes the existence of an [inaccessible cardinal](wikipedia://en/248076), then the statement "every [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) is [Lebesgue measurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_measurable)" is consistent with [Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory](wikipedia://en/152214) without the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840);
- Isolating the notion of [0<sup>\#</sup>](wikipedia://en/248085);
- Proving that the existence of a [real-valued measurable cardinal](wikipedia://en/248102#Real-valued_measurable) is [equiconsistent](wikipedia://en/3435716) with the existence of a measurable cardinal;
- Proving that if $\lambda$ is a strong limit [singular cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cardinal), greater than a [strongly compact cardinal](wikipedia://en/1957057) then $2^\lambda=\lambda^+$ holds;
- Proving that if $\kappa$ is an uncountable regular cardinal, and $S\subseteq\kappa$ is a [stationary set](wikipedia://en/1679067), then $S$ can be decomposed into the union of $\kappa$ disjoint stationary sets;
- With [Stanley Tennenbaum](wikipedia://en/33807699), developing the method of iterated forcing and showing the consistency of [Suslin's hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suslin's_hypothesis);
- With [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), showed the consistency of [Martin's axiom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin's_Axiom) with arbitrarily large [cardinality of the continuum](wikipedia://en/1574901);
- Outside of set theory, developing (with [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960)) the [Solovay–Strassen primality test](wikipedia://en/1013089), used to identify large [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) that are [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) with high [probability](wikipedia://en/22934). This method has had implications for [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432);
- Regarding the [P versus NP problem](wikipedia://en/6115), he proved with T. P. Baker and J. Gill that relativizing arguments cannot prove [$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$.](wikipedia://en/6115)[^o7]
- Proving that GL (the [normal modal logic](wikipedia://en/911820) which has the instances of the schema $\Box(\Box A\to A)\to\Box A$ as additional axioms) completely axiomatizes the logic of the provability predicate of [Peano arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_Arithmetic);
- With [Alexei Kitaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Kitaev), proving that a finite set of [quantum gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_gate) can efficiently approximate an arbitrary [unitary operator](wikipedia://en/225999) on one [qubit](wikipedia://en/25284) in what is now known as [Solovay–Kitaev theorem](wikipedia://en/56073671).
