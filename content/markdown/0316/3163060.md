# Menger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Menger"}

**Menger** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Andreas Menger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Menger) (born 1972), former German football player
- [Anton Menger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Menger) (1841–1906), Austrian economist and author; brother of Carl Menger
- [Carl Menger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Menger) (1840–1921), Austrian economist and author; founder of the Austrian school of economics
- [Howard Menger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Menger) (1922–2009), American who claimed to have met extraterrestrials
- [Karl Menger](wikipedia://en/149431) (1902–1985), Austrian-born mathematician and son of economist Carl Menger
- [Kirsten Menger-Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Menger-Anderson) (born 1969), American fiction writer
- [Rudolph Menger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolph_Menger) (1851–1921), American naturalist and physician
