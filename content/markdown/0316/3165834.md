# Hermann Schwarz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Schwarz"}

**Karl Hermann Amandus Schwarz** (; 25 January 1843 – 30 November 1921) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Schwarz was born in [Hermsdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobieszów), [Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Silesia) (now [Sobieszów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobieszów), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)). In 1868 he married Marie Kummer,[^o4] who was the daughter to the mathematician [Ernst Eduard Kummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Eduard_Kummer)[^o5] and Ottilie née Mendelssohn (a daughter of Nathan Mendelssohn and granddaughter of [Moses Mendelssohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Mendelssohn)). Schwarz and Kummer had six children, including his daughter Emily Schwarz.[^o5]

Schwarz originally studied [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) in Berlin but Ernst Eduard Kummer and [Karl Theodor Wilhelm Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) persuaded him to change to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o6] He received his Ph.D. from the Universität Berlin in 1864 and was advised by Kummer and Weierstrass.[^o7] Between 1867 and 1869 he worked at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle), then at the [Swiss Federal Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o8] From 1875 he worked at [Göttingen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_University),[^o8] dealing with the subjects of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). He died in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) on 30 November 1921.

## Work {#Work}

Schwarz's works include *Bestimmung einer speziellen Minimalfläche*, which was crowned by the Berlin Academy in 1867 and printed in 1871, and *Gesammelte mathematische Abhandlungen* (1890).

Among other things, Schwarz improved the proof of the [Riemann mapping theorem](wikipedia://en/26215),[^o10] developed a special case of the [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality](wikipedia://en/38128), and gave a proof that the ball [has less surface area](wikipedia://en/326182) than any other body of equal volume.[^o11] His work on the latter allowed [Émile Picard](wikipedia://en/42000) to show solutions of differential equations exist (the [Picard–Lindelöf theorem](wikipedia://en/666177)).[^o6]

In 1892 he became a member of the [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) [Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Science) and a professor at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), where his students included [Lipót Fejér](wikipedia://en/3934357), [Paul Koebe](wikipedia://en/4313643) and [Ernst Zermelo](wikipedia://en/178655). In total, he advised at least 22 Ph. D students.[^o7] In 1914 Schwarz's friends and former students published a volume with 34 articles in celebration of the 50th anniversary of his doctoral dissertation.[^o12]

His name is attached to many ideas in mathematics,[^o5] including the following:

- [Abstract additive Schwarz method](wikipedia://en/16269602)
- [Additive Schwarz method](wikipedia://en/400296)
- [Schwarz alternating method](wikipedia://en/16252015)
- [Schwarzian derivative](wikipedia://en/555745)
- [Schwarz function](wikipedia://en/62662441)
- [Schwarz lantern](wikipedia://en/58278053)
- [Schwarz lemma](wikipedia://en/1432589)
- [Schwarz's list](wikipedia://en/26956489)
- [Schwarz minimal surface](wikipedia://en/36763333)
- [Schwarz theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarz_theorem) (also known as Clairaut's theorem)
- [Schwarz integral formula](wikipedia://en/10945580)
- [Schwarz–Christoffel mapping](wikipedia://en/1990796)
- [Schwarz–Ahlfors–Pick theorem](wikipedia://en/1487910)
- [Schwarz reflection principle](wikipedia://en/3213223)
- [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325)
- [Schwarz triangle function](wikipedia://en/53050926)
- [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality](wikipedia://en/38128)
- [Theorem of Pohlke and Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohlke's_theorem)
