# Lower convex envelope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lower convex envelope"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and particularly [convex analysis](wikipedia://en/3237201), the **lower convex envelope** $\breve f$ of a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438)-valued [function](wikipedia://en/185427) $f$ defined on a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) $V$ is defined [pointwise](wikipedia://en/4786797) as the [supremum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum) of all [convex functions](wikipedia://en/245568) that lie under that function, i.e.[^o2]

:::

$$\breve f (x) = \sup\{ g(x) \mid g \text{ is convex and } g \leq f \text{ over } V \}.$$

The lower convex envelope coincides with the [biconjugate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biconjugate) $f^{**}$ of $f$, but can differ with the biconjugate when this definition is extended to functions whose domains are not the entire vector space or whose values are [extended reals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_reals), i.e., allowing $\pm\infty$. In such cases, one has the inequalities[^o2]

$$f^{**}(x)\le \breve f(x)\le f(x).$$
