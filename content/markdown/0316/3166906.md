# Francesco Paolo Cantelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Paolo Cantelli"}

**Francesco Paolo Cantelli** (20 December 1875 – 21 July 1966) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made contributions to [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Cantelli was born in [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo). He received his doctorate in mathematics in 1899 from the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo) with a thesis on celestial mechanics and continued his interest in astronomy by working until 1903 at [Palermo Astronomical Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo_Astronomical_Observatory) (*osservatorio astronomico cittadino*), which was under the direction of [Annibale Riccò](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annibale_Riccò).[^o4] Cantelli's early papers were on problems in [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182).

From 1903 to 1923 Cantelli worked at the *Istituto di Previdenza della [Cassa Depositi e Prestiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassa_Depositi_e_Prestiti)* (Pension Fund for the Government Deposits and Loans Bank). During these years he did research on the mathematics of [finance theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance_theory) and [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), as well as the probability theory. Cantelli's later work was all on [probability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_Theory). [Borel&ndash;Cantelli lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel&ndash;Cantelli_lemma), [Cantelli's inequality](wikipedia://en/36703918) and the [Glivenko–Cantelli theorem](wikipedia://en/3828409) are result of his work in this field. In 1916–1917 he made contributions to the theory of [stochastic convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_convergence). In 1923 he resigned his actuarial position when he was appointed professor of actuarial mathematics at the [University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania). From there, he went to the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples), where he worked as a professor and then in 1931 to the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) where he remained until his retirement in 1951. He died in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome).

Cantelli made fundamental contributions to the foundations of probability theory and to the clarification of different types of probabilistic convergence.[^o5] He also made seminal contributions to actuarial science.[^o5] He was the founder of the *Istituto Italiano degli Attuari* for the applications of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) to [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics). Cantelli was the editor of the *Giornale dell'Istituto Italiano degli Attuari* (GIIA) from 1930 to 1958.[^o6]

## Works {#Works}

- *Sull'adattamento delle curve ad una serie di misure o di osservazioni*, Palermo, 1905
- *Genesi e costruzione delle tavole di mutualità*, 1914
- *Sulla legge dei grandi numeri*, 1916
- *La tendenza a un limite nel senso del calcolo delle probabilità*, 1916
- *Sulla probabilità come limite della frequenza* in "Rendiconti della Reale Accademia dei Lincei", 1917
- *Una teoria astratta del calcolo delle probabilità*, GIIA, vol. 3, pp. 257–265, Roma, 1932
- *Considerazioni sulla legge uniforme dei grandi numeri e sulla generalizzazione di un fondamentale teorema del Sig. Paul Levy*, 1933
- *Sulla determinazione empirica delle leggi di probabilità*, 1933
- *Su una teoria astratta del calcolo delle probabilità e sulla sua applicazione al teorema detto "delle probabilità zero e uno"*, 1939
