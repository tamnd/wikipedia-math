# Norbert Schappacher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Norbert Schappacher"}

*Image: Norbert Schappacher.jpeg.* Schappacher at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach), 2004

**Norbert Schappacher** (born 8 October 1950 in [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen)) is a German mathematician and historian of mathematics. He was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2010 in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After secondary education at Essen's *Burggymnasium*, Schappacher studied from 1969 to 1971 at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where he was taught by (among others) [Günter Harder](wikipedia://en/50792386) and [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495). Schappacher obtained his [*Vordiplom*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in 1971 at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where he studied from 1971 to 1974 and was taught by (among others) [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031), [Ulrich Stuhler](wikipedia://en/32886316), and [Martin Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650). For the academic year 1974–1975, Schappacher studied as an exchange student at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where his teachers included [Tsit Yuen Lam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsit_Yuen_Lam) and [Robin Hartshorne](wikipedia://en/5693379). At the University of Göttingen, Schappacher received his *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)* in 1975 and from 1977 to 1986 held the position of assistant at the Göttingen Mathematical Institute.[^o4] There in 1978 he received his doctorate with advisor Martin Kneser and thesis *Eine diophantische Invariante von Singularitäten über nichtarchimedischen Körpern* (A diophantine invariant of singularities over non-Archimedean fields).

He was from 1979 to 1981 at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) (University of Paris XI) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay) with [John H. Coates](wikipedia://en/230705) and for the academic year 1983–1984 at the [Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-Planck-Institut_für_Mathematik) (MPI) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn) with Günter Harder. Schappacher in 1985 was an assistant professor (*Professeur associé*) at Paris-Sud University and habilitated in 1986 at the University of Göttingen with thesis *Periods of Hecke operators*. He was in 1986 at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI) in Berkeley, then worked in 1987 as an assistant professor in Orsay and was from 1987 to 1991 a Heisenberg fellow at the MPI in Bonn. He was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) for five months in 1990.[^o4]

Schappacher is since 1991 a professor at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). From 2002 to 2004 he was a visiting professor at [TU Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Darmstadt) and returned in 2004 to the University of Strasbourg. He has held visiting positions at several academic institutions, including the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) (for 2 months in 1997), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai)'s [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (for 2 months in 2000–2001), and the [Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademie_der_Wissenschaften_zu_Göttingen) (as Gauss-Professor in summer 2007). In 2011–2012 he was a Fellow of *Lichtenberg-Kolleg*, Göttingen.[^o4]

Schappacher's research is primarily on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and the history of mathematics. He has done research on the history of mathematics in Germany during the era of National Socialism (including [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230), [Oswald Teichmüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Teichmüller) and the Mathematical Institute in Göttingen), as well as historical research on [Kurt Heegner](wikipedia://en/621799), [Bartel Leendert van der Waerden](wikipedia://en/91390), [Diophantus of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantus_of_Alexandria), and [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902). Since 2008 Schappacher is a member of the [Euler Committee of the Swiss Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Committee_of_the_Swiss_Academy_of_Sciences). He was from 2009 to 2016 editor-in-chief of the *Revue d'histoire des mathématiques* and is the managing editor of the *[Elemente der Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elemente_der_Mathematik)*.[^o4]

In 2011 he was elected a corresponding member of the [Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademie_der_Wissenschaften_zu_Göttingen).[^o4]
