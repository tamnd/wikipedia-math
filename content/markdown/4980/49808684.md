# Filippo Antonio Revelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Filippo Antonio Revelli"}

*Image: Revelli\_-\_Elementi\_dell'aritmetica\_universale\_e\_della\_geometria\_piana\_e\_solida,\_1778\_-\_578390.tif.* *Elementi dell'aritmetica universale e della geometria piana e solida* (1778)

**Filippo Antonio Revelli** (1716 – 1801) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was professor of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) for 26 years at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin).[^o4]

He had among his pupils [Joseph-Louis Lagrange](wikipedia://en/87793).[^o2][^o5]

His son Vincenzo Antonio Revelli (1764-1835) was a philosopher and painter.[^o6][^o5]
