# Giovanni Battista Pisani

*Image: Pisani - Giardino aritmetico, 1646 - 4648193.tif.* *Giardino aritmetico*, title page

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Battista Pisani"}

**Giovanni Battista Pisani**, also known as **Gio. Battista Pisani**, was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th-century) [Genoese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He wrote *The First Book of Modern Cursive Letters* (*Il primo libro di lettere corsive moderne*, 1641) about [calligraphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calligraphy),[^o4] followed by other educational works, *Arithmetic Memorial* (*Memoriale aritmetico*, 1644) and *Arithmetic Garden* (*Giardino aritmetico*, 1646), intended to solve arithmetic problems that were especially related to [merchant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchant) activity.[^o5]
