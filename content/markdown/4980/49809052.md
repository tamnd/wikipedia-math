# Bassano Politi

*Image: Politi\_-\_Questio\_de\_modalibus\_-\_4621816.tif.* *Questio de modalibus*, title page

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bassano Politi"}

**Bassano Politi** was a [16th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_century) [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He published *Questio de modalibus*, a book where he collected several [medieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages) [treatises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treatise) by [Thomas Bradwardine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Bradwardine), [Nicole Oresme](wikipedia://en/593659), [Biagio Pelacani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biagio_Pelacani), and [Giovanni de Casali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_de_Casali).[^o2][^o3]
