# Kari Vilonen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kari Vilonen"}

**Kari Kaleva Vilonen** (born 1955) is a Finnish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in geometric representation theory. He is currently a professor at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He received in 1983 his [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D) from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) under [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121) with thesis *The Intersection Homology*D*\-module on Hypersurfaces with Isolated Singularities*.

## Career {#Career}

From 1983 to 1986 was a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), on leave in 1984–1985 at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). Afterward, Vilonen was a Benjamin Pierce Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 1986 to 1989. From 1989 to 2000 he was a faculty member at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University), rising to the rank of Professor in 1996.[^o3] After that, he was a professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University),[^o7] and then a professor at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) from 2010 to 2015.[^o8] Starting in 2015, Vilonen has been a professor at the University of Melbourne in [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia).

In 2002, with [Dennis Gaitsgory](wikipedia://en/6571030) and [Edward Frenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Frenkel), he proved the geometrical [Langlands conjecture](wikipedia://en/171905) for curves over finite fields.[^o9]

In 2004, Vilonen, [Mark Goresky](wikipedia://en/10238162), Dennis Gaitsgory and Edward Frenkel were awarded a multimillion dollar grant from the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency ([DARPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DARPA)) to work on a project aimed at establishing links between the Langlands program and dualities in quantum field theory. Later, Frenkel wrote, "We felt like we were in uncharted territory: no mathematicians we knew had ever received grants of this magnitude before." The funds were used to coordinate the work of dozens of mathematicians with the goal of making a concerted effort in a significant area of research.[^o10]

In 2007, with Ivan Mirković, he published "Geometric Langlands duality and representations of algebraic groups over commutative rings", which proved the geometric Satake equivalence, a geometric version of the [Satake isomorphism](wikipedia://en/32319857).[^o11]

In 2013, Vilonen received a [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o12] In 2014, he was awarded a Simons Fellowship from the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o13]

In 2020, the [Australian Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Research_Council) awarded Vilonen an [Australian Laureate Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Laureate_Fellowship), their highest award to an individual. This five year grant will allow him to address questions about real groups, algebraic objects which describe the basic symmetries occurring in nature.[^o14]

### Awards and keynote addresses {#Awards_and_keynote_addresses}

Vilonen was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) for the academic year 1997/98.[^o16] In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Topological methods in representation theory* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o17] In 2004 he was elected a member of the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).
