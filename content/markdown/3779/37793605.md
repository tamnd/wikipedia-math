# Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics"}

***Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [John Wiley & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons) on behalf of the Department of Economics, [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). The journal was established in 1939 as the *Bulletin of the Oxford University Institute of Economics and Statistics* and became the *Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics* in 1973. The journal publishes articles on [applied economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_economics) with emphasis placed on the practical importance, theoretical interest and policy-relevance of their results. General topics include [macroeconomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macroeconomics), [microeconomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microeconomics), [derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_%28finance%29), [investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investment) and [interest rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interest_rates).

:::

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.791, ranking it 33rd out of 52 journals in the category "Social Sciences, Mathematical Methods", 53rd out of 125 journals in the category "Statistics & Probability" and 204th out of 378 journals in the category "Economics".[^o3][^o4][^o5]
