# Heptagonal antiprism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptagonal antiprism"}

Prism polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **heptagonal antiprism** is the fifth in an infinite set of [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms) formed by two parallel polygons separated by a strip of triangles. In the case of the heptagonal antiprism, the caps are two regular [heptagons](wikipedia://en/365349). Consequently, this polyhedron features 14 vertices and 14 equilateral triangular faces. There are 14 edges where a triangle meets a heptagon, and an additional 14 edges where two triangles meet.

:::

The heptagonal antiprism was first illustrated by [Johannes Kepler](wikipedia://en/15736) as an example of the general construction of antiprisms.[^o2]
