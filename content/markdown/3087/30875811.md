# Non-standard model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Non-standard model"}

In [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), a discipline within [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), a **non-standard model** is a model of a theory that is not [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the [intended model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intended_interpretation) (or standard model).[^o2]

:::

## Existence {#Existence}

If the intended model is infinite and the language is [first-order](wikipedia://en/10983), then the [Löwenheim–Skolem theorems](wikipedia://en/341482) guarantee the existence of non-standard models. The non-standard models can be chosen as [elementary extensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_extension) or [elementary substructures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_substructure) of the intended model.

## Importance {#Importance}

Non-standard models are studied in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis) and [non-standard models of arithmetic](wikipedia://en/6251420).
