# Dishant Mayurbhai Pancholi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dishant Mayurbhai Pancholi"}

Academics

**Dishant Mayurbhai Pancholi** is a professor in the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai](wikipedia://en/2718996) with research interests in contact and symplectic topology.[^o2] Before taking up the position in The Institute Of Mathematical Sciences in 2016, Pancholi was an assistant professor at [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675).[^o3] He is also a von Newmann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey).[^o4] The Singapore based magazine *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)* selected Pancholi in 2020 for their [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) list as a top researcher in Asia.[^o5][^o6] Pancholi was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in Mathematical Sciences in the year 2019.[^o7]

:::

Pancholi obtained his PhD degree from School of Mathematics, [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai) in 2006 for a thesis titled "Knots, mapping class groups and Kirby calculus", and MSc degree from [Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharaja_Sayajirao_University_of_Baroda), [Vadodara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vadodara) in 1998.
