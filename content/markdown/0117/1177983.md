# Triaugmented hexagonal prism

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Triaugmented hexagonal prism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triaugmented hexagonal prism** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{57}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by triply augmenting a hexagonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) by attaching [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455) ($J_{1}$) to three of its nonadjacent equatorial faces.

:::
