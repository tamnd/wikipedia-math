# Paragyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paragyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **paragyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{77}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with one [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) rotated through 36 [degrees](wikipedia://en/1195294), and the opposing pentagonal cupola removed.
