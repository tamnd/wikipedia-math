# Elongated pentagonal gyrobicupola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal gyrobicupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal gyrobicupola** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{39}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by elongating a [pentagonal gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177965) ($J_{31}$) by inserting a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) between its congruent halves. Rotating one of the [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) through 36 degrees before inserting the prism yields an [elongated pentagonal orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177967) ($J_{38}$).

:::

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{1}{6}\left(10+8\sqrt{5}+15\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)a^3\approx12.3423...a^3$$

$$A=\left(20+\sqrt{\frac{5}{2}\left(10+\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}\right)}\right)a^2\approx27.7711...a^2$$
