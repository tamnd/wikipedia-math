# Triangular hebesphenorotunda

*Image: J92 triangular hebesphenorotunda.stl.* 3D model of a triangular hebesphenorotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Triangular hebesphenorotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triangular hebesphenorotunda** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) with 13 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 3 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), 3 [regular pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and 1 [regular hexagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hexagon), meaning the total of its faces is 20.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The original name is attributed to (Johnson 1966), with two affixes. The prefix *hebespheno-* refers to a blunt wedge-like complex formed by three adjacent *lunes*, a figure where two equilateral triangles are attached at the opposite sides of a square, whereas the suffix (triangular) *\-rotunda* refers to the complex of three equilateral triangles and three regular pentagons surrounding another equilateral triangle, which bears a structural resemblance to the [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491). Therefore, the triangular hebesphenorotunda has twenty faces: thirteen [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), three [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), three [regular pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and one [regular hexagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hexagon). The faces are all [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306), categorizing the triangular hebesphenorotunda as a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), enumerated the last one $J_{92}$. It is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedra), meaning that it cannot be separated by a plane into two small regular-faced polyhedra.

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) of a triangular hebesphenorotunda of edge length $a$ is:

$$A = \left(3+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{1308+90\sqrt{5}+114\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}}\right)a^2 \approx 16.389a^2,$$

and its [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) is:

$$V = \frac{1}{6}\left(15+7\sqrt{5}\right)a^3\approx5.10875a^3.$$

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The triangular hebesphenorotunda with edge length $\sqrt{5} - 1$ can be constructed by the union of the orbits of the [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate):

$$\begin{aligned} \left( 0,-\frac{2}{\tau\sqrt{3}},\frac{2\tau}{\sqrt{3}} \right), \qquad &\left( \tau,\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}\tau^2},\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \right) \\ \left( \tau,-\frac{\tau}{\sqrt{3}},\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}\tau} \right), \qquad &\left(\frac{2}{\tau},0,0\right), \end{aligned}$$

under the action of the [group](wikipedia://en/28186) generated by rotation by 120° around the z-axis and the reflection about the yz-plane. Here, $\tau$ denotes the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).
