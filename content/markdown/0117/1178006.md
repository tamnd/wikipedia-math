# Metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{74}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with two non-opposing [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) rotated through 36 degrees. It is also a [canonical polyhedron](wikipedia://en/4709281#Canonical_polyhedron).

*Image: J74 metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

Alternative Johnson solids, constructed by rotating different cupolae of a rhombicosidodecahedron, are:

- The [gyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131111) ($J_{72}$) where only one cupola is rotated;
- The [parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177997) ($J_{73}$) where two opposing cupolae are rotated;
- And the [trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133367) ($J_{75}$) where three cupolae are rotated.
