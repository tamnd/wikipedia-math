# Augmented dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Augmented dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **augmented dodecahedron** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) combining a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) and a [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461).

*Image: J58 augmented dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of an augmented dodecahedron

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

An augmented dodecahedron is constructed from a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361), a twelve-sided polyhedron with regular pentagons, by attaching a regular-faced [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461) to one of the regular dodecahedron's faces; the regular polygons mean that all of its internal angles and edges are equal. The resulting polyhedron covers one pentagon from a dodecahedron with five equilateral triangles from the pyramid. Ergo, the augmented dodecahedron has eleven pentagonal faces and five equilateral triangular faces, totaling sixteen faces. The augmented is [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), a convex polyhedron with regular faces, enumerated as the fifty-eighth $J_{58}$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The surface area of an augmented dodecahedron $A$ is obtained by summing the area of its faces, eleven regular pentagons and five equilateral triangles. Its volume $V$ is obtained by adding the volume of a regular dodecahedron and a pentagonal pyramid, as suggested by the construction:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= 11 \cdot \frac{{\sqrt {25 + 10\sqrt 5} }}{4}a^2 + 5 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 \approx 21.09a^2, \\ V &= \frac{15 + 7\sqrt{5}}{4}a^3 + \frac{5 + \sqrt{5}}{24} a^3 \approx 7.965a^3. \end{aligned}$$
