# Elongated pentagonal bipyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal bipyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal bipyramid** or **bicapped pentagonal prism** is a polyhedron constructed by attaching two [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) onto the base of a [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659). It is an example of [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The elongated pentagonal bipyramid is constructed from a [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659) by attaching two [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) onto its bases, a process called [elongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29). These pyramids cover the pentagonal faces so that the resulting polyhedron ten [equilateral triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_triangles) and five [squares](wikipedia://en/659939). A [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all of the faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306) is the [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034). The elongated pentagonal bipyramid is among them, enumerated as the sixteenth Johnson solid $J_{16}$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) of an elongated pentagonal bipyramid $A$ is the sum of all polygonal faces' area: ten equilateral triangles, and five squares. Its volume $V$ can be ascertained by dissecting it into two pentagonal pyramids and one regular pentagonal prism and then adding its volume. Given an elongated pentagonal bipyramid with edge length $a$, they can be formulated as:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \frac{5}{2} \left(2+\sqrt{3}\right)a^2 \approx 9.330a^2, \\ V &= \frac{1}{12} \left(5+\sqrt{5}+3 \sqrt{5 \left(5+2 \sqrt{5}\right)}\right)a^3 \approx 2.324a^3. \end{aligned}$$

*Image: J16 elongated pentagonal bipyramid.stl.* 3D model of an elongated pentagonal bipyramid

It has the same [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the pentagonal prism, the dihedral group $D_{5\mathrm{h}}$ of order 20. Its [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) can be calculated by adding the angle of the pentagonal pyramid and pentagonal prism:

- the dihedral angle of an elongated pentagonal bipyramid between two adjacent triangular faces is that of a pentagonal pyramid between those, 138.19°.
- the dihedral angle of an elongated pentagonal bipyramid between two adjacent square faces is that of a regular pentagonal prism, the [internal angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_angle) of a [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), 108°.
- the dihedral angle of an elongated pentagonal bipyramid between square-to-triangle is the sum of the dihedral angle of a pentagonal pyramid between triangle-to-pentagon with that of a pentagonal prism between square-to-pentagon, 37.38° + 90° = 127.38°.

The dual of the elongated square bipyramid is a [pentagonal bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3522774).
