# Parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{73}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with two opposing [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) rotated through 36 [degrees](wikipedia://en/1195294). It is also a [canonical polyhedron](wikipedia://en/4709281#Canonical_polyhedron).

*Image: J73 parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

Alternative Johnson solids, constructed by rotating different cupolae of a rhombicosidodecahedron, are:

- The [gyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131111) ($J_{72}$) where only one cupola is rotated;
- The [metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1178006) ($J_{74}$) where two non-opposing cupolae are rotated;
- And the [trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133367) ($J_{75}$) where three cupolae are rotated.
