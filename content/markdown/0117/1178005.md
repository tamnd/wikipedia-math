# Gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{82}$).

*Image: J82 gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with two non-opposing [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) removed and a third is rotated 36 degrees. Related Johnson solids are:

- The [diminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131101) ($J_{76}$) where one cupola is removed,
- The [parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133303) ($J_{80}$) where two opposing cupolae are removed,
- The [metabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133317) ($J_{81}$) where two non-opposing cupolae are removed,
- And the [tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133338) ($J_{83}$) where three cupolae are removed.
