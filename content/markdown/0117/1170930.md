# Augmented sphenocorona

*Image: J87 augmented sphenocorona.stl.* 3D model of an augmented sphenocorona

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Augmented sphenocorona"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **augmented sphenocorona** is the [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) that can be constructed by attaching an [equilateral square pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_square_pyramid) to one of the square faces of the [sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1142750). It is the only Johnson solid arising from "cut and paste" manipulations where the components are not all prisms, antiprisms or sections of [Platonic](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Archimedean](wikipedia://en/1847) solids.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The augmented sphenocorona is constructed by attaching [equilateral square pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilateral_square_pyramid) to the [sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1142750), a process known as the [augmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29). This pyramid covers one square face of the sphenocorona, replacing it with four equilateral triangles. As a result, the augmented sphenocorona has 16 equilateral triangles and 1 square as its faces. The convex polyhedron with [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) faces is the [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034); the augmented sphenocorona is one of them, enumerated as $J_{87}$, the 87th Johnson solid.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

For the edge length $a$, the surface area of an augmented sphenocorona is by summing the area of 16 equilateral triangles and 1 square:

$$\left(1+4\sqrt{3}\right)a^2\approx7.92820a^2,$$

Its [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) can be calculated by slicing it into a sphenocorona and an equilateral square pyramid, and adding the volume subsequently:

$$\left(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{1 + 3 \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}} + \sqrt{13 + 3 \sqrt{6}}}+\frac{1}{3\sqrt{2}}\right)a^3\approx1.75105a^3.$$

:::
