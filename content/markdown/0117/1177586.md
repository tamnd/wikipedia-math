# Tanaka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tanaka"}

**Tanaka** is the fourth most common [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_name) surname. It is typically written with the [kanji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanji) for ricefield & middle.[^o4] Less common variants include many & middle, many & relationship, another & middle, shelf & low, shelf & summer and many & name & congratulation.

:::
