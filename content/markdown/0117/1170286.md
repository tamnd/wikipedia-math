# Bilunabirotunda

*Image: J91 bilunabirotunda.stl.* 3D model of a bilunabirotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bilunabirotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **bilunabirotunda** is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) with faces of 8 equilateral triangles, 2 squares, and 4 regular pentagons.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The bilunabirotunda consists of two (*bi‑*) *lunes*, each consisting of a square between two triangles, and two (*bi‑*) partial *rotundae*, in which two pentagons and two triangles alternate around a single vertex as in the [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491). Therefore, the faces of a bilunabirotunda possess 8 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 2 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), and 4 [regular pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon) as it faces. It is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034)—a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all of the faces are [regular polygon](wikipedia://en/333306)—enumerated as 91st Johnson solid $J_{91}$.

The surface area of a bilunabirotunda with edge length $a$ is:

$$\left(2 + 2\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5(5 + 2\sqrt{5})}\right)a^2 \approx 12.346a^2,$$

and the volume of a bilunabirotunda is:

$$\frac{17 + 9\sqrt{5}}{12}a^3 \approx 3.0937a^3.$$

:::

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The bilunabirotunda is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron): it cannot be separated by a plane into two small regular-faced polyhedra. One way to construct a bilunabirotunda is by attaching two [wedges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedge) and two [tridiminished icosahedrons](wikipedia://en/1123559).

For edge length $\sqrt{5} - 1$, the coordinates of the bilunabirotunda may be given as

$$\begin{aligned} & (0, 0, \pm 1), \\ & \left( \pm \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}, \pm 1, \pm \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2} \right), \\ & \left( \pm \frac{\sqrt{5} + 1}{2}, \pm \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}, 0 \right). \end{aligned}$$

:::

::: {#o8 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

(Reynolds 2004) discusses the bilunabirotunda as a shape that could be used in architecture.

:::

## Related polyhedra and honeycombs {#Related_polyhedra_and_honeycombs}

*Image: Bilunabirotunda augmented cube.png.* Six bilunabirotundae around a cube

Six bilunabirotundae can be augmented around a cube with [pyritohedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyritohedral_symmetry). [B. M. Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnie_Stewart) labeled this six-bilunabirotunda model as 6J<sub>91</sub>(P<sub>4</sub>).[^o11] Such clusters combine with regular [dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/8407) to form a space-filling honeycomb.
