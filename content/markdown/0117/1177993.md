# Parabiaugmented dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Parabiaugmented dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **parabiaugmented dodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{59}$). It can be seen as a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) with two [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) ($J_{2}$) attached to opposite faces. When pyramids are attached to a dodecahedron in other ways, they may result in an [augmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177990) ($J_{58}$), a [metabiaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177994) ($J_{60}$), a [triaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177995) ($J_{61}$), or even a [pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196) if the faces are made to be irregular.
