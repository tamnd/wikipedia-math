# The American Mathematical Monthly

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The American Mathematical Monthly"}

***The American Mathematical Monthly*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was established by [Benjamin Finkel](wikipedia://en/23166485) in 1894 and is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). It is an expository journal intended for a wide audience of mathematicians and has since become "the most widely read mathematics journal in the world".[^o3] The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Annalisa Crannell. The journal gives the **Lester R. Ford Award** annually to "authors of articles of expository excellence" published in the journal.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

In January of 1894, [Benjamin Finkel](wikipedia://en/23166485) established the journal naming it "The American Mathematical Monthly." Finkel and [John Marvin Colaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Marvin_Colaw) were the founding editors of the journal.[^o6] The journal was founded to be "a journal for teachers of mathematics in the collegiate and advanced secondary fields."[^o7]

Between 1913 and 1916 the journal was self-published by its owners at the time: the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), [University of Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado), University of Illinois, [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University), [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa), [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas), [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri), University of Nebraska, [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), [Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), [Colorado College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_College), and [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College).[^o7]

Since 2018, the journal has been published in English by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) when they formed a publishing partnership.[^o8][^o9] The journal generally releases an issue every month except July and August.[^o7][^o8]
