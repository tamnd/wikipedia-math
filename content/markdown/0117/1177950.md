# Elongated triangular bipyramid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated triangular bipyramid"}

The **elongated triangular bipyramid** or **elongated triangular dipyramid**[^n1] is a polyhedron constructed from a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) by attaching two [regular tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_tetrahedra) to its bases. It is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) and a [composite polyhedron](wikipedia://en/77684519). This polyhedron is found in the African musical instrument *nirrosula* and the [raphide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphide) crystal structure in [plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant).

:::

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The elongated triangular bipyramid is constructed from a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) by attaching two [regular tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_tetrahedra) to its triangular bases.[^n2] Such a process known as [*elongation*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29).[^n3] The triangular prism is [*uniform*](wikipedia://en/2537223) (that is, the solid's symmetry maps each vertex to every other vertex), and made of two [equilateral triangular](wikipedia://en/173285) bases and three lateral [square](wikipedia://en/659939) faces. When two regular tetrahedra cover the bases of a prism, the resulting polyhedron has six equilateral triangles and three squares (nine faces in total), fifteen edges, and eight vertices.[^n2] The elongated triangular bipyramid is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), named after American mathematician [Norman Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527) who listed the 92 [convex polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedra) with [regular polygonal](wikipedia://en/333306) faces.[^n4] The elongated triangular bipyramid is enumerated as the fourteenth Johnson solid $J_{14}$.[^n1][^n5] It is a [composite polyhedron](wikipedia://en/77684519), because it can be sliced by a plane to produce convex, regular-faced polyhedra, namely a triangular prism and regular tetrahedra.[^n6]

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

If the solid's edge-length is $a$, then its height $h$ is the sum of twice the distance from a vertex to the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) of a triangular face in a tetrahedron ($\sqrt{6}a/3$) and the height of a triangular prism ($a$):[^n7]

$$h = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}a + \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}a + a = \left(\frac{2\sqrt{6}}{3} + 1\right)a \approx 2.633a.$$

The surface area $A$ of an elongated triangular bipyramid is the sum of the areas of its polygonal faces, which are six equilateral triangles and three squares:[^n2][^n7]

$$A = 6\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2\right) + 3a^2 = \left(\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} + 3\right)a^2 \approx 5.598a^2.$$

The volume of an elongated triangular bipyramid is the sum of twice the volume of a tetrahedron and triangular prism:[^n2][^n7]

$$V = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}a^3 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}a^3 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^3 = \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{6} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \right) a^3 \approx 0.669a^3.$$

*Image: 3D Johnson J14.stl.* 3D model of an elongated triangular bipyramid

The elongated triangular bipyramid has the same [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the triangular prism, the [three-fold prismatic symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prismatic_symmetry) $D_{3 \mathrm{h}}$ of order twelve. It has an axis of threefold [rotational symmetry](wikipedia://en/701096) (0°, 120°, 240°) through the apexes of the pyramids, three planes of mirror symmetry containing that axis, and a fourth plane of mirror symmetry perpendicular to that axis and passing through the solid's centroid.[^n8][^n7]

The [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046) of an elongated triangular bipyramid can be calculated by adding the angles of the tetrahedron and the triangular prism:[^n8]

- its dihedral angle between two adjacent triangular faces is that angle of a tetrahedron between two adjacent triangular faces: $\arccos(\frac{1}{3}) \approx 70.5^\circ$;
- its dihedral angle between a square and a triangle is the sum of a triangular prism's square-to-triangle angle and a tetrahedron's triangle-to-triangle angle: $\arccos(\frac{1}{3}) + \frac{\pi}{2} \approx 160.5^\circ$;
- the dihedral angle between two squares is that angle of a triangular prism's square-to-triangle, the [internal angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_angle) of an equilateral triangle, $\frac{\pi}{3} = 60^\circ$.

:::

## Appearances {#Appearances}

The nirrosula, an African musical instrument woven out of strips of plant leaves, is made in the form of a series of elongated bipyramids, although there are non-equilateral-triangular faces of their end caps.[^n9]

The elongated triangular bipyramid, together with the [helicoid](wikipedia://en/684207), is commonly found in the micromorphological structure of [raphides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphide), needle-shape crystals made of [calcium oxalate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_oxalate) in [plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant). These structures are specialized to regulate the [products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_%28biology%29) of [metabolic activities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metabolic_activity) by transmitting, storing, and making them functionable, depending on the shapes.[^n10]
