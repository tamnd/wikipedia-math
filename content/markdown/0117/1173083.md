# Hendecagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hendecagram"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hendecagram** (also **endecagram** or **endekagram**) is a [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) that has eleven [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).

:::

The name *hendecagram* combines a Greek [numeral prefix](wikipedia://en/1705121), *hendeca-*, with the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) suffix *\-gram*. The *hendeca-* prefix derives from Greek ἕνδεκα (ἕν + δέκα, one + ten) meaning "[eleven](wikipedia://en/208156)". The *\-gram* suffix derives from γραμμῆς (*grammēs*) meaning a line.[^o3]

## Regular hendecagrams {#Regular_hendecagrams}

There are four **regular hendecagrams**,[^o5] which can be described by the notation {11/2}, {11/3}, {11/4}, and {11/5}; in this notation, the number after the slash indicates the number of steps between pairs of points that are connected by edges. These same four forms can also be considered as [stellations](wikipedia://en/180253#Stellating_polygons) of a regular [hendecagon](wikipedia://en/614840).[^o6]

Since 11 is prime, all hendecagrams are star polygons and not compound figures.

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

As with all odd regular polygons and star polygons whose orders are not products of distinct [Fermat primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime), the regular hendecagrams cannot be constructed with compass and straightedge.[^o9] However, (Hilton Pedersen 1986) describe folding patterns for making the hendecagrams {11/3}, {11/4}, and {11/5} out of strips of paper.[^o11]

:::

## Applications {#Applications}

*Image: Flickr - The U.S. Army - The Golden Knights land at Statue of Liberty in New York City (2).jpg.* [Fort Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Island#Fort_Wood)'s star-shaped walls became the base of the Statue of Liberty.

[Prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) over the hendecagrams {11/3} and {11/4} may be used to approximate the shape of [DNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA) molecules.[^o14]

*Image: Momine Fragment.jpg.* An 11-pointed star from the [Momine Khatun Mausoleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momine_Khatun_Mausoleum)

[Fort Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Island#Fort_Wood), now the base of the [Statue of Liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statue_of_Liberty) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), is a [star fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_fort) in the form of an irregular 11-point star.[^o15]

The [Topkapı Scroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topkapı_Scroll) contains images of an 11-pointed star [Girih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girih) form used in [Islamic art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_art). The star in this scroll is not one of the regular forms of the hendecagram, but instead uses lines that connect the vertices of a [hendecagon](wikipedia://en/614840) to nearly-opposite midpoints of the hendecagon's edges.[^o16] 11-pointed star Girih patterns are also used on the exterior of the [Momine Khatun Mausoleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momine_Khatun_Mausoleum); Eric Broug writes that its pattern "can be considered a high point in Islamic geometric design".[^o17]

An 11-point star-shaped cross-section was used in the [Space Shuttle Solid Rocket Booster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Solid_Rocket_Booster), for the core of the forward section of the rocket (the hollow space within which the fuel burns). This design provided more surface area and greater thrust in the earlier part of a launch, and a slower burn rate and reduced thrust after the points of the star were burned away, at approximately the same time as the rocket passed the [sound barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_barrier).[^o18]
