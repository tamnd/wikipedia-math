# Bigyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bigyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **bigyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{79}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with two [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) rotated through 36 [degrees](wikipedia://en/1195294), and a third pentagonal cupola removed. (None of the cupolae can be adjacent.)
