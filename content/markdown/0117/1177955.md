# Elongated pentagonal cupola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal cupola"}

The **elongated pentagonal cupola** is a polyhedron, constructed by attaching [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) to a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) to its base. It is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The elongated pentagonal cupola is constructed from a [ten-sided prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) by attaching a [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) onto one of its bases, a process known as [elongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29). This cupola covers one of the prism's two decagons, so that the resulting polyhedron has five [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), fifteen [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), one [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and one [regular decagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_decagon). Because of this construction, the elongated pentagonal cupola is [composite](wikipedia://en/77684519). A [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) polyhedron in which all of the faces are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306) is called a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034). The elongated pentagonal cupola is one of them, enumerated as the twentieth Johnson solid $J_{20}$.

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

*Image: J20 elongated pentagonal cupola.stl.* 3D model of an elongated pentagonal cupola

The [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) $A$ of an elongated square cupola is the sum of the areas of all faces: five equilateral triangles, fifteen squares, one regular pentagon, and one regular decagon. Its volume $V$ can be obtained by dissecting it into a pentagonal cupola and a regular decagon, and then adding their volumes. Let $a$ be the edge length of an elongated pentagonal cupola; then its surface area and volume are:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= \left(\frac{1}{4}\left(60+\sqrt{10\left(80+31\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{2175+930\sqrt{5}}\right)}\right)\right)a^2 \\ &\approx 26.5797a^2, \\ V &= \left(\frac{1}{6}\left(5+4\sqrt{5}+15\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)\right)a^3 \\ &\approx 10.0183a^3. \end{aligned}$$
