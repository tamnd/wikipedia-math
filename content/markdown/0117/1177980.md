# Parabiaugmented hexagonal prism

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Parabiaugmented hexagonal prism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **parabiaugmented hexagonal prism** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{55}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by doubly augmenting a hexagonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) by attaching [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455) ($J_{1}$) to two of its nonadjacent, parallel (opposite) equatorial faces. Attaching the pyramids to nonadjacent, nonparallel equatorial faces yields a [metabiaugmented hexagonal prism](wikipedia://en/1177982) ($J_{56}$). (The solid obtained by attaching pyramids to adjacent equatorial faces is not convex, and thus not a Johnson solid.)
