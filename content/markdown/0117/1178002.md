# Metagyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Metagyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **metagyrate diminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{78}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with one [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) rotated through 36 [degrees](wikipedia://en/1195294), and a non-opposing pentagonal cupola removed. (The cupolae cannot be adjacent.)
