# Bálint Tóth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bálint Tóth"}

**Bálint Tóth** (born 1955, [Cluj/Kolozsvár/Klausenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca)) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) mathematician whose work concerns [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) and probabilistic aspects of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He obtained PhD in 1988 from the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences), worked as senior researcher at the [Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [HAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) and as professor of mathematics at [TU Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_University_of_Technology_and_Economics). He holds the Chair of Probability at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) and is a research professor at the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670), Budapest.

:::

He has worked on microscopic models of [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), quantum spin systems, limit theorems for [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) with long memory, and non-conventional [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), hydrodynamic limits, etc. In particular, Tóth contributed to the theory of self-interacting motions, that is, motions that are "reinforced", "self-avoiding" or "self-repellent". In collaboration with [Wendelin Werner](wikipedia://en/6607797) he constructed[^o2] the random geometric object later called the [Brownian web](wikipedia://en/47886440).

Tóth was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) ICM-2018 (Rio de Janeiro),[^o3] and of the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) ECM-2000 (Barcelona). He was plenary speaker at three Conferences on Stochastic Processes and their Applications SPA-2005 (Santa Barbara),[^o4] SPA-2014 (Buenos Aires),[^o5] and IMS Medallion Lecturer[^o6] at SPA-2022 (Wuhan).[^o7]

He is a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o8] and a corresponding member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o9]

Tóth was Editor-in-Chief of the journals [Electronic Journal of Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Probability) (2009-2011)[^o10] and [Annals of Applied Probability](wikipedia://en/31018340) (2016-2018).[^o10] Currently he is co-Editor-in-Chief (jointly with [Fabio Toninelli](wikipedia://en/66360967)) of [Probability Theory and Related Fields](wikipedia://en/30852593).[^o11]
