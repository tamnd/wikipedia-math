# Spiridon Popescu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spiridon Popescu"}

**Spiridon Popescu** (August 13, 1864 – May 8, 1933) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) prose writer.

:::

Born in [Rogojeni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suceveni), [Galați County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galați_County), his parents were the peasant Constantin Dumitrașcu al Popei and his wife Safta (*née* Tofan). He attended seminary in [Galați](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galați) and at [Socola Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socola_Monastery) in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), earning his high school degree at age 26. He studied physics and mathematics at the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University) and took courses at the higher normal school, earning a mathematics degree at age 31. He taught mathematics in [Bârlad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bârlad), [Vaslui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaslui), [Tulcea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulcea), Galați and, from 1904, in the national capital, [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest). Within the [Education Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_and_Scientific_Research_%28Romania%29), Popescu was responsible for normal primary education nationwide. He was elected both to the [Assembly of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_%28Romania%29) and to the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_%28Romania%29).[^o2]

Popescu made his literary debut in the Iași-based *Arhiva* in 1890. Encouraged by his brother-in-law [Constantin Stere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantin_Stere), he wrote for *Evenimentul literar*, and for *Albina*. As an active participant in the policy of village education promoted by [Spiru Haret](wikipedia://en/1194762), he also contributed to *[Viața Românească](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viața_Românească)*, where he published the [Poporanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poporanist) novellas *Moș Gheorghe la expoziție* (1907) and *Rătăcirea din Stoborăni* (1909). His first book was *Considerațiuni psihologice din viața poporului român* (1893), followed by *Din povestirile unui vânător de lupi* (1905), *Moș Gheorghe la expoziție* (1912) and the short story collection *Zori de iulie* (1912).[^o2]
