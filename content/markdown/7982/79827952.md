# Andreea Erciulescu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreea Erciulescu"}

**Andreea Luisa Erciulescu** is a Romanian statistician who has worked in agricultural and [official statistics](wikipedia://en/18646178), [small area estimation](wikipedia://en/6150200), and [survey methodology](wikipedia://en/272134). She is an associate vice president for statistics and data science at [Westat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westat).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Erciulescu majored in mathematics at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University), supported by a scholarship from the Romanian government and graduating in 2011. During her studies there, she discovered an interest in statistics, and won in 2010 an [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) undergraduate poster presentation award for *Solving [Kakuro Puzzles](wikipedia://en/900804)*. She continued at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) for graduate study in statistics, where she received a master's degree and completed her Ph.D., in 2015. Her dissertation, *Small area prediction based on unit level models when the covariate mean is measured with error*, was supervised by [Wayne Fuller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Fuller).

She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [National Institute of Statistical Sciences](wikipedia://en/56043115) from 2015 to 2018, working there on projects associated with the [National Agricultural Statistics Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Agricultural_Statistics_Service). In 2018 she moved to Westat as a senior statistician, and has since become a vice president.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Erciulescu is an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). She was elected to the 2025 class of [Fellows of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellows_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).

She was a third-place recipient of the 2022 Prize for Young Statisticians of the [International Association for Official Statistics](wikipedia://en/38407748), the first US-based statistician to receive this award.
