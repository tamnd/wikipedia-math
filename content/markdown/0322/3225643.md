# Reflection theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reflection theorem"}

article In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), a **reflection theorem** or **Spiegelungssatz** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) for *reflection theorem* – see *[Spiegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiegel_%28disambiguation%29)* and *[Satz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satz_%28disambiguation%29)*) is one of a collection of theorems linking the sizes of different [ideal class groups](wikipedia://en/150907) (or [ray class groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_class_group)), or the sizes of different [isotypic components](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotypic_component) of a class group. The original example is due to [Ernst Eduard Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117), who showed that the class number of the [cyclotomic field](wikipedia://en/30872462) $\mathbb{Q} \left( \zeta_p \right)$, with *p* a prime number, will be divisible by *p* if the class number of the maximal real subfield $\mathbb{Q} \left( \zeta_p \right)^{+}$ is. Another example is due to Scholz.[^o2] A simplified version of his theorem states that if 3 divides the class number of a [real quadratic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_quadratic_field) $\mathbb{Q} \left( \sqrt{d} \right)$, then 3 also divides the class number of the [imaginary quadratic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_quadratic_field) $\mathbb{Q} \left( \sqrt{-3d} \right)$.

:::

## Leopoldt's Spiegelungssatz {#Leopoldt's_Spiegelungssatz}

Both of the above results are generalized by [Leopoldt](wikipedia://en/27700368)'s "Spiegelungssatz", which relates the [p-ranks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-rank) of different isotypic components of the class group of a number field considered as a [module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module) over the [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) of a Galois extension.

Let *L*/*K* be a finite Galois extension of number fields, with group *G*, degree prime to *p* and *L* containing the *p*-th roots of unity. Let *A* be the *p*-Sylow subgroup of the class group of *L*. Let φ run over the irreducible characters of the group ring **Q**<sub>*p*</sub>\[*G*\] and let *A*<sub>φ</sub> denote the corresponding direct summands of *A*. For any φ let *q* = *p*<sup>φ(1)</sup> and let the *G*-rank *e*<sub>φ</sub> be the exponent in the index

$$[ A_\phi : A_\phi^p ] = q^{e_\phi} .$$

Let ω be the character of *G*

$$\zeta^g = \zeta^{\omega(g)} \text{ for } \zeta \in \mu_p .$$

The reflection (*Spiegelung*) φ<sup>\*</sup> is defined by

$$\phi^*(g) = \omega(g) \phi(g^{-1}) .$$

Let *E* be the unit group of *K*. We say that ε is "primary" if $K(\sqrt[p]\epsilon)/K$ is unramified, and let *E*<sub>0</sub> denote the group of primary units modulo *E*<sup>*p*</sup>. Let δ<sub>φ</sub> denote the *G*-rank of the φ component of *E*<sub>0</sub>.

The Spiegelungssatz states that

$$| e_{\phi^*} - e_\phi | \le \delta_\phi .$$

:::

## Extensions {#Extensions}

Extensions of this Spiegelungssatz were given by Oriat and Oriat-Satge, where class groups were no longer associated with characters of the Galois group of *K*/*k*, but rather by ideals in a [group ring](wikipedia://en/349223) over the Galois group of *K*/*k*. Leopoldt's Spiegelungssatz was generalized in a different direction by Kuroda, who extended it to a statement about [ray class groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_class_group). This was further developed into the very general "*T*-*S* reflection theorem" of [Georges Gras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Gras).[^o7] [Kenkichi Iwasawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenkichi_Iwasawa) also provided an [Iwasawa-theoretic](wikipedia://en/396150) reflection theorem.

:::
