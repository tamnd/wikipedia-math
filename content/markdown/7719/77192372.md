# Persistent random walk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Persistent random walk"}

The **persistent random walk** is a modification of the [random walk](wikipedia://en/235451) model.

:::

A population of particles are distributed on a line, with constant speed $c_0$, and each particle's velocity may be reversed at any moment. The reversal time is exponentially distributed as $e^{-t/\tau}/\tau$, then the population density $n$ evolves according to[^o2]

$$(2\tau^{-1} \partial_t + \partial_{tt} - c_0^2 \partial_{xx}) n = 0$$

which is the [telegrapher's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegrapher's_equation).
