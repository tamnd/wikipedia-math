# Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics"}

The ***Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics*** is an [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), published by [SpringerOpen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media)[^o3] on behalf of the [Swiss Society of Economics and Statistics](wikipedia://en/77259553), and financially supported by the Swiss Academy of Humanities and Social Sciences and the [Swiss National Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Bank).[^o4][^o5] The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Marius Brülhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marius_Brülhart).[^o6]

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was established in 1864 as the *Zeitschrift für Schweizerische Statistik* (in [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language)) and *Journal de Statistique suisse* (in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language)).[^o3] Its first editor was [Johannes Stössel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Stössel), the secretary of the [Federal Statistical Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistics_Bureau_%28Switzerland%29), who served as an editor from 1864 to 1869.[^o8]

In 1916, the journal was titled *Zeitschrift für schweizerische Statistik und Volkswirtschaft* (in German) and *Journal de statistique et Revue économique suisse* (in French), and from 1945 onwards, it was named *Schweizerische Zeitschrift für Volkswirtschaft und Statistik* (in German), *Revue suisse d'Économie politique et de Statistique* (in French), and *Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics* (in English).[^o9]

Although the journal is now published entirely in English, issues prior to the first edition of 2007 also accepted articles written in German, French and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language).[^o10][^o11]
