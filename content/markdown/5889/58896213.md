# Raquel Prado

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raquel Prado"}

**Raquel Prado** (born 1970) is a Venezuelan [Bayesian](wikipedia://en/49571) statistician. She is a professor of statistics in the [Jack Baskin School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Baskin_School_of_Engineering) of the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz), and has been elected president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) for the 2019 term.

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Prado specializes in [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571) for [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) data. With Mike West, she is the author of the book *Time Series: Modeling, Computation, and Inference* (Texts in Statistical Science, CRC Press, 2010).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Prado was born on 24 April 1970, in [Caracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas), and graduated from [Simón Bolívar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simón_Bolívar_University_%28Venezuela%29) in 1993. She completed her Ph.D. in statistics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 1998. Her dissertation, *Latent Structure in Non-Stationary Time Series*, was supervised by Mike West.

After completing her Ph.D. she returned to Simón Bolívar University as a faculty member before moving to Santa Cruz.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1999, Prado and her co-authors Andrew Krystal and Mike West won the Outstanding Statistical Application Award of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) for their work on statistical analysis of [electroencephalography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroencephalography) data. In 2013, Prado became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).
