# Gerald L. Alexanderson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gerald L. Alexanderson"}

**Gerald Lee Alexanderson** (November 13, 1933 – December 16, 2020) was an American mathematician who studied [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o3] He was the Michael & Elizabeth Valeriote Professor of Science at [Santa Clara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_University), and in 1997–1998 was president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). He was also president of [The Fibonacci Association](wikipedia://en/11360573) from 1980 to 1984.[^o4][^o6]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Alexanderson did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), graduating with a B.A. in 1955. He earned a master's degree from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1958, and in the same year joined the [Santa Clara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_University) faculty. At Santa Clara, he chaired the Department of Mathematics (later the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science) for 35 years, from 1967 to 2002.[^o4][^o6] He also served as the Division Director of Physical Sciences and Mathematics.[^o3] Abraham Hillman was his most frequent co-author.[^o7] As a student, his greatest influences have been [Ivan Niven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_M._Niven) and [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya).[^o3]

He was the co-author, editor, or co-editor of 15 books. He served as the editor of *[Mathematics Magazine](wikipedia://en/4230329)* from 1986 to 1990,[^o4][^o6] and was the associate editor of *[The American Mathematical Monthly](wikipedia://en/1179483)* and *[The College Mathematics Journal](wikipedia://en/21340776)*.[^o3]

He actively promoted competitions in mathematics, and served as the associate director of the [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition).[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2005, Alexanderson won both one of the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Awards for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics),[^o4][^o9] and the Yueh-Gin Gung and Dr. Charles Y. Hu Award for Distinguished Service to Mathematics,[^o4][^o10] both from the Mathematical Association of America.
