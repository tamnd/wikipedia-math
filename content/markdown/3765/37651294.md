# Kuga fiber variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kuga fiber variety"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Kuga fiber variety**, introduced by (txt), is a [fiber space](wikipedia://en/276526) whose fibers are [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) and whose base space is an [arithmetic quotient](wikipedia://en/23038858) of a [Hermitian symmetric space](wikipedia://en/4127357).

:::
