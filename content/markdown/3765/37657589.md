# Steinberg formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steinberg formula"}

In mathematical [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), **Steinberg's formula**, introduced by (txt), describes the [multiplicity](wikipedia://en/19211790) of an [irreducible representation](wikipedia://en/385982) of a semisimple complex [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) in a [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of two [irreducible representations](wikipedia://en/385982). It is a consequence of the [Weyl character formula](wikipedia://en/5011916), and for the Lie algebra [sl<sub>2</sub>](wikipedia://en/19571780) it is essentially the [Clebsch–Gordan formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clebsch–Gordan_formula).

:::

Steinberg's formula states that the multiplicity of the irreducible representation of highest weight *ν* in the tensor product of the irreducible representations with highest weights *λ* and *μ* is given by

$$\sum_{w,w^\prime\in W} \epsilon(ww^\prime)P(w(\lambda+\rho)+w^\prime(\mu+\rho)-(\nu+2\rho))$$

where *W* is the [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332), ε is the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of an element of the Weyl group, ρ is the [Weyl vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_vector), and *P* is the [Kostant partition function](wikipedia://en/23994987) giving the number of ways of writing a vector as a sum of positive roots.
