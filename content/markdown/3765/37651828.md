# Tate topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tate topology"}

In mathematics, the **Tate topology** is a [Grothendieck topology](wikipedia://en/12910) of the space of [maximal ideals](wikipedia://en/48164) of a [*k*-affinoid algebra](wikipedia://en/5828941), whose [open sets](wikipedia://en/39358) are the [admissible open subsets](wikipedia://en/5138993) and whose coverings are the admissible [open coverings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_covering).

:::
