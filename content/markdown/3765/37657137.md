# Faltings' product theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Faltings' product theorem"}

In [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), **Faltings' product theorem** gives sufficient conditions for a [subvariety](wikipedia://en/248808) of a product of [projective spaces](wikipedia://en/242135) to be a product of varieties in the projective spaces. It was introduced by (txt) in his proof of Lang's conjecture that subvarieties of an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) containing no translates of non-trivial abelian subvarieties have only finitely many rational points.

:::

(Evertse 1995) and (Ferretti 1996) gave explicit versions of Faltings' product theorem.
