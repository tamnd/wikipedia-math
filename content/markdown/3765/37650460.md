# Haim Hanani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Haim Hanani"}

**Haim Hanani** (חיים חנני; 1912-9-11 as **Chaim Chojnacki**– 8 April 1991)[^o3] was a Polish-born Israeli mathematician, known for his contributions to [combinatorial design theory](wikipedia://en/5170#Design_theory), in particular for the theory of [pairwise balanced designs](wikipedia://en/2761968#pairwise_balanced_design) and for the proof of an existence theorem for [Steiner quadruple systems](wikipedia://en/28016).[^o4][^o5] He is also known for the [Hanani–Tutte theorem](wikipedia://en/42787595) on [odd crossings](wikipedia://en/18298594) in [non-planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hanani (Chojnacki) was born in [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland), studied in Vienna and Warsaw, and graduated with an M.A. from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1934. He emigrated to the [British Mandate of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), later to become [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), in 1935 and in 1938 received the first Ph.D. in Mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).

In 1955 he was appointed to the faculty at [Technion Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) and from 1969 to 1973 he served as the rector of [Ben-Gurion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) in [Beersheba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beersheba). In 1980 he was awarded the title of Professor Emeritus at that institution.[^o4]

His early research led to the proof of the theorem devised by [Richard M. Wilson](wikipedia://en/22615301) on pairwise balance designs.[^o4]

He wrote scholarly papers with [Andries Brouwer](wikipedia://en/22709916), [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), [Alexander Schrijver](wikipedia://en/35280549), and [Richard M. Wilson](wikipedia://en/22615301), among others.[^o7]

His papers were published in journals such as *[Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21988115)*, the *[Journal of Combinatorial Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory)*, the *[European Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*, and the *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)*.[^o4]
