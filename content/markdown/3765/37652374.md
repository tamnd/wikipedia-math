# Period domain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Period domain"}

In mathematics, a **period domain** is a parameter space for a polarized [Hodge structure](wikipedia://en/10297305). They can often be represented as the [quotient](wikipedia://en/11526) of a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) by a [compact](wikipedia://en/750326) [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011).

:::
