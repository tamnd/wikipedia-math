# Regular part

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regular part"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **regular part** of a [Laurent series](wikipedia://en/61213) consists of the series of terms with positive powers.[^o2] That is, if

:::

$$f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n (z - c)^n,$$

then the regular part of this Laurent series is

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n (z - c)^n.$$

In contrast, the series of terms with negative powers is the [principal part](wikipedia://en/1225337).[^o2]
