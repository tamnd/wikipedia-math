# Sol Garfunkel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sol Garfunkel"}

**Solomon "Sol" Garfunkel** born 1943, in Brooklyn, New York, is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has dedicated his career to [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471). Since 1980, he has served as the executive director of the award-winning [non-profit organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit_organization) "[Consortium for Mathematics and Its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consortium_for_Mathematics_and_Its_Applications)",[^o3] working with teachers, students, and business people to create learning environments where mathematics is used to investigate and model real issues in our world.

:::

Garfunkel is best known for hosting the 1987 [PBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBS) series titled "For All Practical Purposes: An Introduction to Contemporary Mathematics", followed by the 1991 series, "Algebra: In Simplest Terms", both often used in classrooms.

## Early life {#Early_life}

At the age of 24, Garfunkel received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [Mathematical Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Logic) from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). While in attendance he worked with [Howard Jerome Keisler](wikipedia://en/10962250), [Michael D. Morley](wikipedia://en/2921675), and [Stephen Kleene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Kleene). Garfunkel then worked at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) at [Storrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storrs,_Connecticut).

Garfunkel continued his work advocating for the improvement of mathematics in public school systems. He coauthored the article "How to Fix Our Math Education"[^o5] with [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594), emeritus professor of mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). Since published, this article has been credited with successfully bringing new awareness to the topic. The article has become a topic for a vast number of blogs, and has been translated into several languages. Garfunkel has served as project director for several [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) curriculum projects, and in 2009 was awarded the Glenn Gilbert National Leadership Award from the [National Council of Supervisors of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_of_Supervisors_of_Mathematics).

Most recently, Garfunkel co-founded the [International Mathematical Modeling Challenge](wikipedia://en/48157955).
