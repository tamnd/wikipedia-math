# Alexander Abian

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Abian"}

**Alexander** (**Smbat**) **Abian** (January 1, 1923 – July 24, 1999)[^o3] was an Iranian-born [Armenian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who taught for over 25 years at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) and became notable for his frequent posts to various [Usenet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet) [newsgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsgroup), and his advocacy for the [destruction of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destruction_of_the_Moon).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Abian was born in [Tabriz, Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabriz,_Iran), and was of [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenians) ethnicity. After earning an undergraduate degree in Iran, he emigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1950, where he received a master's degree from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). Abian then obtained a Ph.D. from the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati), where he wrote a dissertation on a topic in [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762) under the direction of Isaac Barnett. After teaching posts in [Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania), and [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio), he joined the faculty of Iowa State in 1967. He wrote three books and published more than two hundred papers. He retired in 1993.[^o6]

::: {#o8 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Moonless Earth theory"}

## Moonless Earth theory {#Moonless_Earth_theory}

Abian gained international notoriety for his claim that blowing up the Moon would eliminate virtually all natural disasters. He made this claim in 1991 in a campus newspaper,[^o9] stating that a Moonless Earth would not wobble, eliminating both the seasons and its associated events like [heat waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_waves), [snowstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowstorms) and [hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricanes).[^o10] Abian said that "Those critics who say 'Dismiss Abian's ideas' are very close to those who dismissed [Galileo](wikipedia://en/29688374)."[^o11]

The proposed nuclear destruction of the Moon has been rejected by astronomers on several grounds: the nuclear arsenal of mankind would fail to do more than crack the Moon's crust; if successful, the heating of Earth's atmosphere by a hail of falling lunar debris would be destructive to all life; and an increase, not decrease, in the Earth's wobble without a stabilizing Moon, leading to an Earth [axial tilt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axial_tilt) of 45 degrees and more drastic [seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasons) would occur.[^o12][^o13]

:::
