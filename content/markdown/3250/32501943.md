# Srinivasa Ramanujan Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Srinivasa Ramanujan Medal"}

The **Srinivasa Ramanujan Medal**, named after the [Indian mathematician](wikipedia://en/1158496) [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717), is awarded by the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) for work in the [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436).

:::

Past recipients include:

- 1962 – [S. Chandrasekhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._Chandrasekhar)
- 1964 – [B. P. Pal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._P._Pal)
- 1966 – [K. S. Chandrasekharan](wikipedia://en/22907213)
- 1968 – [P. C. Mahalanobis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._C._Mahalanobis)
- 1972 – [G. N. Ramachandran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._N._Ramachandran)
- 1974 – [Harish-Chandra](wikipedia://en/373190)
- 1979 – [R. P. Bambah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._P._Bambah)
- 1982 – [S. Chowla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._Chowla)
- 1985 – [C. S. Seshadri](wikipedia://en/5633765)
- 1988 – [M. S. Narasimhan](wikipedia://en/6980576)
- 1991 – [M. S. Raghunathan](wikipedia://en/18730540)
- 1997 – [K. Ramachandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._Ramachandra)
- 2003 – [C. R. Rao](wikipedia://en/1418009)
- 2006 – [R. Parimala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Parimala)
- 2008 – [S. Ramanan](wikipedia://en/24022327)[^o2]
- 2013 – [K. R. Parthasarathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._R._Parthasarathy_%28probabilist%29)
- 2016 – [Tyakal Nanjundiah Venkataramana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyakal_Nanjundiah_Venkataramana)
- 2019 – [K. B. Sinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._B._Sinha)
- 2022 - [Sudesh K. Khanduja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudesh_K._Khanduja)
