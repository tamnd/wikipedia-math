# Victor Puiseux

Victor\_Puiseux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Puiseux"}

**Victor Alexandre Puiseux** (; 16 April 1820 – 9 September 1883) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer). [Puiseux series](wikipedia://en/2604429) are named after him, as is in part the [Bertrand–Diquet–Puiseux theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand–Diquet–Puiseux_theorem). His work on [algebraic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_functions) and [uniformization](wikipedia://en/288270) makes him a direct precursor of [Bernhard Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980), for what concerns the latter's work on this subject and his introduction of [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181).[^o2] He was also an accomplished amateur [mountaineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountaineer). A [peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Pelvoux) in the French alps, which he climbed in 1848, is named after him.

:::

A species of [gecko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gecko), *[Ptyodactylus puiseuxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ptyodactylus_puiseuxi)*, is named in his honor.[^o3]

## Life {#Life}

He was born in 1820 in [Argenteuil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argenteuil), [Val-d'Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val-d'Oise). He occupied the chair of [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182) at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). Excelling in mathematical analysis, he introduced new methods in his account of [algebraic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_functions), and by his contributions to [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182) advanced knowledge in that direction. In 1871, he was unanimously elected to the [French Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy).

One of his sons, [Pierre Henri Puiseux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Puiseux), was a famous astronomer.

He died in 1883 in [Frontenay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontenay), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) and was buried in [Montparnasse Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montparnasse_Cemetery).
