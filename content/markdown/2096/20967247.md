# Collage theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Collage theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **collage theorem** characterises an [iterated function system](wikipedia://en/505225) whose [attractor](wikipedia://en/191094) is close, relative to the [Hausdorff metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausdorff_metric), to a given set. The IFS described is composed of [contractions](wikipedia://en/6239) whose images, as a **collage** or [union](wikipedia://en/47949) when mapping the given set, are arbitrarily close to the given set. It is typically used in [fractal compression](wikipedia://en/60022).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $\mathbb{X}$ be a [complete metric space](wikipedia://en/7832). Suppose $L$ is a nonempty, [compact subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_subset) of $\mathbb{X}$ and let $\varepsilon >0$ be given. Choose an [iterated function system](wikipedia://en/505225) (IFS) $\{ \mathbb{X} ; w_1, w_2, \dots, w_N\}$ with contractivity factor $s$, where $0 \leq s < 1$ (the contractivity factor $s$ of the IFS is the maximum of the contractivity factors of the maps $w_i$). Suppose

$$h\left( L, \bigcup_{n=1}^N w_n (L) \right) \leq \varepsilon,$$

where $h(\cdot,\cdot)$ is the [Hausdorff metric](wikipedia://en/429296). Then

$$h(L,A) \leq \frac{\varepsilon}{1-s}$$

where $A$ is the attractor of the IFS. Equivalently,

$$h(L,A) \leq (1-s)^{-1} h\left(L,\bigcup_{n=1}^N w_n(L)\right)$$

for all nonempty, compact subsets $L$ of $\mathbb{X}$.

Informally, if $L$ is close to being stabilized by the IFS, then $L$ is also close to being the attractor of the IFS.
