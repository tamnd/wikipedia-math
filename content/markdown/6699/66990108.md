# Martin Goldstern

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Goldstern"}

**Martin Goldstern** (born 7 May 1963 in Austria) is an Austrian mathematician and university professor[^o3] for [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) at the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) and head of the [research unit 8](https://dmg.tuwien.ac.at/fb8/ST.html) of the [Institute of Discrete Mathematics and Geometry](https://dmg.tuwien.ac.at/).[^o4] His main research lies in [set theory of the real line](wikipedia://en/4937732) and [forcing theory](wikipedia://en/152205), and applications of set theory in [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200).

:::

He is cousin of [Martin Karplus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Karplus) and great-nephew of [Eugenie Goldstern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenie_Goldstern).[^o5][^o6]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Goldstern earned a Ph.D. in 1986[^o8] at the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) under the direction of [Robert F. Tichy](wikipedia://en/66615202),[^o9] with a dissertation in equidistribution;[^o10] and another in set theory in 1991 at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley)[^o11] under the direction of [Jack Silver](wikipedia://en/21626512) and Haim Judah. As postdoc he held temporary positions at [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University), [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin) and [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University). He acquired [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at TU Wien in 1993[^o12] with the thesis *Tools for your forcing construction*, which greatly simplified, and made widely accessible, a general preservation theorem of [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) for countable support [proper forcing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_forcing) iterations. In 1993 he started working at TU Wien, where he is now full professor.[^o3]

In 1996, Goldstern won the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384).[^o13] In 2015, 2018 and 2023 he held visiting professor positions at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). Together with Jakob Kellner and Shelah he showed the consistency (assuming [large cardinals](wikipedia://en/383644)) of Cichoń's maximum, i.e., the statement that the ten "independent" entries in [Cichoń's diagram](wikipedia://en/5709055) are all different.
