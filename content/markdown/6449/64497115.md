# Dag Normann

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Dag Normann"}

**Dag Normann** is a Norwegian [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636). He was born in 1947 and is [Professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo). His research focuses on [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414) with an emphasis on mathematical models for typed algorithms and applications of the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Normann obtained his doctoral degree from the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) under the supervision of [Jens Erik Fenstad](wikipedia://en/20270698) in 1976.[^o6] He was [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the University of Oslo where he retired in 2015.[^o7]

He published numerous books and research papers; in particular, together with John Longley, he published the book *Higher-Order Computability*, the standard research reference of the field, in the book series *Theory and Applications of Computability* in 2015.[^o8] Normann is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) (DNVA) in the Natural Sciences Division. In the past, he was the head of the Group of Mathematical Sciences within DNVA.[^o9] From 1983 to 1985 and from 2000 to 2003, he was the President of the [Norwegian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/15255049).[^o10] He was President of the [Association Computability in Europe](wikipedia://en/22521356) from 2015 to 2016 and currently serves as Treasurer of the Association.[^o11] He was also President of the [Scandinavian Logic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavian_Logic_Society) from 2012 to 2017.
