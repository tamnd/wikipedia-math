# Willem van Zwet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Willem van Zwet"}

Van ZwetVan Zwet

**Willem Rutger van Zwet** (31 March 1934 – 2 July 2020) was a Dutch mathematical statistician.[^o3] He was a professor at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) between 1968 and 1999.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Van Zwet was born on 31 March 1934 in [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden).[^o5] Van Zwet obtained his doctoral degree in 1964 under the supervision of [Jan Hemelrijk](wikipedia://en/40640281) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) with a thesis titled "Convex Transformations of Random Variables".[^o6] After that, he worked at the [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), and became a lector of statistics at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) in 1964 and was named professor in 1968. He retired in 1999.[^o5]

From 1992 to 1999, Van Zwet was the Director of the Thomas Stieltjes Institute of Mathematics. He co-founded Eurandom in 1997, and served as its director until 2000. From 1997 to 1999, he was also the President of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Van Zwet was a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) and a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) since 1990.[^o8] He received the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 2006. He won the [Adolphe Quetelet](wikipedia://en/184215) Medal in 1993, and had been a Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) since 1978. In 1979, he became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o9] In 1996, he was made Knight of the [Order of the Netherlands Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion), and was named Doctoris Honoris causa of [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) the following year. He died on 2 July 2020 in [Oegstgeest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oegstgeest).[^o5]
