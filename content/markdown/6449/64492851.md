# Basilis Gidas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Basilis Gidas"}

**Basilis Gidas** is an applied mathematician at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), interested in many [applications of mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o2] Following degrees in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, and mathematics, he obtained a combined Ph.D. in physics and nuclear engineering at the University of Michigan in 1970.[^o3][^o4] He is an elected fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). He has had past appointments in various mathematics and physics departments at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), [Rockefeller University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_University), [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), and [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).

:::

His collaborations with [Luis Caffarelli](wikipedia://en/2070173), [Wei-Ming Ni](wikipedia://en/64524853), [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725), and [Joel Spruck](wikipedia://en/64474699) resulted in six papers in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210), five of which rank among the most cited in the field of elliptic [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). The collaboration of Gidas, Ni, and Nirenberg in particular was cited in the awarding of the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) to Nirenberg.[^o5]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- B. Gidas, [Wei Ming Ni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wei_Ming_Ni), and [L. Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725). *Symmetry and related properties via the maximum principle.* Comm. Math. Phys. 68 (1979), no. 3, 209–243.
- B. Gidas, [Wei Ming Ni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wei_Ming_Ni), and [L. Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725). *Symmetry of positive solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations in $\mathbf{R}^{n}$.* Mathematical analysis and applications, Part A, pp. 369–402, Adv. in Math. Suppl. Stud., 7a, Academic Press, New York-London, 1981.
- B. Gidas and [J. Spruck](wikipedia://en/64474699). *A priori bounds for positive solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations.* Comm. Partial Differential Equations 6 (1981), no. 8, 883–901.
- B. Gidas and [J. Spruck](wikipedia://en/64474699). *Global and local behavior of positive solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations.* Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 34 (1981), no. 4, 525–598.
- [Luis A. Caffarelli](wikipedia://en/2070173), Basilis Gidas, and [Joel Spruck](wikipedia://en/64474699). *Asymptotic symmetry and local behavior of semilinear elliptic equations with critical Sobolev growth.* Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 42 (1989), no. 3, 271–297.
