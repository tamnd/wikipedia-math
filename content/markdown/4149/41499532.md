# M. B. W. Tent

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="M. B. W. Tent"}

**Margaret Bunting Wyman Tent** (born **Margaret Bunting Wyman**; November 2, 1944 – September 20, 2014), also known by her [pen name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen_name) **M. B. W. Tent**, was an American mathematics educator and writer. She was the author of several bestselling books.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Tent was born in [Westfield, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield,_Massachusetts) and grew up in [Amherst, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst,_Massachusetts).[^o4][^o5] She worked as a teacher at Altamont high school in Birmingham, Alabama beginning in 1985.[^o6][^o5] In 2010, she and her husband moved to [Frederick, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick,_Maryland) to retire from teaching and continue writing.[^o4]

On September 20, 2014 she died of [breast cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breast_cancer) at a [hospice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospice) in [Mount Airy, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Airy,_Maryland) at age 69.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Tent attended [Amherst Regional High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst_Regional_High_School_%28Massachusetts%29)[^o6] and is a graduate of [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College).[^o5] She received her bachelor's and master's degree from the [University of Alabama, Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alabama_at_Birmingham).[^o6]

Before teaching post-secondary education, she taught adult education at business colleges and for two years for the American Military in Berlin, Germany.[^o4]

She has been teaching and writing about mathematics for many years.[^o5]

## Critical reception {#Critical_reception}

Most of her books have received good reviews from organizations such as the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772),[^o9] [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) and [Goodreads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodreads).[^o5][^o10]

Her books have received hundreds of citations in the academic press.[^o11]

Her books have also received praise from other authors and mathematicians like [William Dunham](wikipedia://en/17961926), [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365), [Cathleen Synge Morawetz](wikipedia://en/1254108), [Charles Ashbacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Ashbacher) and [Peter M. Neumann](wikipedia://en/7151472).[^o12][^o6]
