# L'Intermédiaire des mathématiciens

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="L'Intermédiaire des mathématiciens"}

***L'Intermédiaire des mathématiciens*** was a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by [Gauthier-Villars et fils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauthier-Villars_et_fils). It was established in 1894 by [Émile Lemoine](wikipedia://en/15956333) and [Charles-Ange Laisant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles-Ange_Laisant) and was published until 1920. A second series started in 1922 and was published until 1925.

:::
