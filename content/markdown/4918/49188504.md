# Jean-Yves Jaffray

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Yves Jaffray"}

**Jean-Yves Jaffray** (1939–2009) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) who made influential contributions in the fields of [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216) and [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711).[^o3][^o4] He pioneered methods in decision theory such as [linear utility theory for belief functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_utility_theory_for_belief_functions),[^o5][^o6] bridging the gap between expected utility and the [maximin rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximin_rule) by using [subjective probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subjective_probability) to encompass [belief functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belief_function).[^o3]
