# Fleming–Viot process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fleming–Viot process"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), a **Fleming–Viot process (F–V process)** is a member of a particular subset of [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437)-valued [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process) on [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [metric spaces](wikipedia://en/20018), as defined in the 1979 paper by [Wendell Helms Fleming](wikipedia://en/35262774) and Michel Viot. Such processes are [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536) and [diffusions](wikipedia://en/1667059).

:::

The Fleming–Viot processes have proved to be important to the development of a mathematical basis for the theories behind [allele drift](wikipedia://en/72016). They are generalisations of the Wright–Fisher process and arise as infinite population limits of suitably rescaled variants of [Moran processes](wikipedia://en/21700432).
