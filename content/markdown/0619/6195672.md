# Michael Dinneen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Dinneen"}

**Michael J. Dinneen** is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States)-[New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) working as a senior lecturer at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), New Zealand.[^o2] He is deputy director of the [Center for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Discrete_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Computer_Science).[^o3] He does research in [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555), [distributed computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_computing), and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o2]

:::

Dinneen was educated at the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho) (BS), [Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_University), and [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria) (MS and PhD). He worked at the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory) before moving to New Zealand and the University of Auckland.[^o2]
