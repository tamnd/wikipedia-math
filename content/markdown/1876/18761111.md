# Erkenntnis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erkenntnis"}

***Erkenntnis*** is a journal of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) that publishes papers in [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211). Its name is the German word "Erkenntnis", meaning "knowledge, recognition, insight". The journal was also linked to organisation of conferences, such as the [Second Conference on the Epistemology of the Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Conference_on_the_Epistemology_of_the_Exact_Sciences), of which it published the papers and accounts of the discussions.

:::

## First series (1930–1940) {#First_series_(1930–1940)}

When [Hans Reichenbach](wikipedia://en/1546863) and [Rudolf Carnap](wikipedia://en/57987) took charge of *Annalen der Philosophie und philosophischen Kritik*[^o4] in 1930, they renamed it *Erkenntnis*,[^o5] under which name it was published from 1930 to 1938. The journal was published by the *Gesellschaft für Empirische Philosophie*, or the [Berlin Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Circle) and the [Verein Ernst Mach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verein_Ernst_Mach), [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866). In the first issue Reichenbach wrote that the editors hoped to gain a better understanding of the nature of all human knowledge through consideration of the procedures and results of a variety of scientific disciplines, while also hoping that philosophy need not remain a series of "systems" but could reach the state of objective knowledge.[^o6] The final issue of the first series, Volume 8, No. 1, was published in 1939 and retitled *The Journal of Unified Science (Erkenntnis)* (1939–1940) and included as associate editors [Philipp Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Frank), [Jørgen Jørgensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jørgen_Jørgensen_%28philosopher%29) (da:Jørgen Jørgensen\_filosof), [Charles W. Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_W._Morris), [Otto Neurath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Neurath), and [Louis Rougier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Rougier). The advent of [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) led to the cessation of publication.[^o6]

## Second series (1975–present) {#Second_series_(1975–present)}

In 1975 the journal was "refounded" by [Wilhelm K. Essler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_K._Essler), [Carl G. Hempel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_G._Hempel), and [Wolfgang Stegmüller](wikipedia://en/7895438), and it has been published continuously since.[^o6][^o8] The editor-in-chief is [Hannes Leitgeb](wikipedia://en/40520896) ([LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich)), and the supervisory board comprises [Michael Friedman](wikipedia://en/18889068) ([Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University)), Hans Rott ([University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg)), and [Wolfgang Spohn](wikipedia://en/41440788) ([Konstanz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstanz_University)).[^o9]
