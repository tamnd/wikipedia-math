# Martin J. Newell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin J. Newell"}

**Martin Joseph Newell** (**Máirtín Ó Tnúthail**) (1910 - 1985) was an [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and educationalist, who served as President of [University College Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Galway) from 1960 to 1975.[^o2]

:::

Martin J. Newell was born in 1910, and received his secondary education at [St. Joseph's Patrician College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph's_Patrician_College), [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway), before entering [University College Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Galway) (UCG).[^o2] He was auditor of the college's [Literary and Debating Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literary_and_Debating_Society_%28NUI,_Galway%29) for the 1928-1929 session, and graduated with a B.Sc. in 1929 and an M.Sc. in 1930, both with first class honours. He then proceeded to [St. John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge), where he spent three years, eventually engaging in research on [quantum theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Theory:_Concepts_and_Methods) with [Ralph Fowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fowler). Newell returned to UCG as a lecturer in mathematics in 1935, and was appointed Professor of Mathematics in 1955. In 1960, he was appointed to succeed Monsignor [Pádraig de Brún](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pádraig_de_Brún) as President of the college. He held the office of president until his retirement in 1975.[^o3] A lecture theatre in the Arts Millennium Building is named in his honour.

Newell was the recipient of a D.Sc. (1952) from the [National University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland), and an LL.D. (honoris causa) from the [University of Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dublin). He died in 1985.

Martin J. Newell is the father of mathematician and Gaelic footballer [Martin Leonard Newell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_L._Newell).[^o4]
