# Dannie Heineman Prize for Mathematical Physics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dannie Heineman Prize for Mathematical Physics"}

**Dannie Heineman Prize for Mathematical Physics** is an award given each year since 1959 jointly by the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society) and [American Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institute_of_Physics).[^o2] It is established by the Heineman Foundation in honour of [Dannie Heineman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dannie_Heineman). As of 2010, the prize consists of US\$10,000 and a certificate citing the contributions made by the recipient plus travel expenses to attend the meeting at which the prize is bestowed.

:::
