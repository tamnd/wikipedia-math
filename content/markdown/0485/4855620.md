# Tetrahedroid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrahedroid"}

*Image: Tetrahedroid.jpg.* A tetrahedroid

In algebraic geometry, a **tetrahedroid** (or **tétraédroïde**) is a special kind of [Kummer surface](wikipedia://en/647758) studied by (txt), with the property that the intersections with the faces of a fixed [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) are given by two conics intersecting in four nodes. Tetrahedroids generalize Fresnel's [wave surface](wikipedia://en/35360069).

:::
