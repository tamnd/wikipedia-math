# Single-equation methods (econometrics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Single-equation methods"}

A variety of methods are used in [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) to **estimate [models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28economics%29) consisting of a single [equation](wikipedia://en/9284)**. The oldest and still the most commonly used is the [ordinary least squares](wikipedia://en/1651906) method used to estimate [linear regressions](wikipedia://en/48758386).[^o2]

:::

A variety of methods are available to estimate [non-linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear) models. A particularly important class of non-linear models are those used to estimate relationships where the [dependent variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependent_variable) is discrete, [truncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_dependent_variable) or censored. These include [logit](wikipedia://en/172068), [probit](wikipedia://en/1689101) and [Tobit](wikipedia://en/3722064) models.

Single equation methods may be applied to [time-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-series), cross section or panel data.
