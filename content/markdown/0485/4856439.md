# John Irwin Hutchinson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Irwin Hutchinson"}

**John Irwin Hutchinson** (12 April 1867 – 1 December 1935) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Bangor, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine). He was educated at [Bates College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_College), (A.B., 1889), [Clark University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_University) (1890–92), and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (Ph.D., 1896). With [Virgil Snyder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgil_Snyder) he was coauthor of *Differential and Integral Calculus* (1902) and *Elementary Treatise on the Calculus* (1912).

:::
