# Tridyakis icosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tridyakis icosahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Tridyakis icosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a tridyakis icosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tridyakis icosahedron** is the [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), [icositruncated dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157510). It has 44 vertices, 180 edges, and 120 scalene triangular faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(\frac{3}{5})\approx 53.130\,102\,354\,16^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(\frac{1}{3}+\frac{4}{15}\sqrt{5})\approx 21.624\,633\,927\,143^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{4}{15}\sqrt{5})\approx 105.245\,263\,718\,70^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(-\frac{7}{8})\approx 151.044\,975\,628\,14^{\circ}$. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.
