# Peter McCullagh

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Peter McCullagh"}

**Peter McCullagh** (born 8 January 1952) is a Northern Irish-born American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and John D. MacArthur [Distinguished Service Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Service_Professor) in the Department of Statistics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).

:::

## Education {#Education}

McCullagh is from [Plumbridge, Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumbridge,_Northern_Ireland). He attended [St Columb's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Columb's_College) Derry (1963-1970), studied mathematics at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham) and completed his PhD at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) in 1977, supervised by [David Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771) and Anthony Atkinson.[^o2]

## Research {#Research}

McCullagh is the coauthor with [John Nelder](wikipedia://en/5865261) of *Generalized Linear Models* (1983, [Chapman and Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapman_and_Hall) – second edition 1989), a seminal text on the subject of [generalized linear models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_linear_models) (GLMs) with more than 23,000 citations. He also wrote "Tensor Methods in Statistics", published in 1987, and "Ten Projects in Applied Statistics" [Springer, 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer,_2023).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

McCullagh is a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) and the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences). He won the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) in 1990. He was the recipient of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)'s [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in Bronze in 1983, Silver in 2005 and Gold in 2026 [^o8].

He was also the recipient of the inaugural Karl Pearson Prize of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), with [John Nelder](wikipedia://en/5865261), "for their monograph Generalized Linear Models (1983)".[^o9] He won a Notable Alumni Award in 2007 from his grammar school, [St Columb's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Columb's_College).
