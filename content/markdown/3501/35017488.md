# Dini–Lipschitz criterion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dini–Lipschitz criterion"}

In mathematics, the **Dini–Lipschitz criterion** is a [sufficient condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficient_condition) for the [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038) of a [periodic function](wikipedia://en/76848) to [converge uniformly](wikipedia://en/50652) at all [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers). It was introduced by (txt), as a strengthening of a weaker criterion introduced by (txt). The criterion states that the Fourier series of a periodic function *f* converges uniformly on the real line if

:::

$$\lim_{\delta\rightarrow0^+}\omega(\delta,f)\log(\delta)=0$$

where $\omega$ is the [modulus of continuity](wikipedia://en/964161) of *f* with respect to $\delta$.
