# International Commission on the History of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Commission on the History of Mathematics"}

The **International Commission on the History of Mathematics** was established in 1971 to promote the study of [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). [Kenneth O. May](wikipedia://en/719577) provided its initial impetus. In 1974, its official journal [Historia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/10841658) began publishing. Every four years the Commission bestows the [Kenneth O. May Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_O._May_Medal) upon a deserving historian of mathematics.

:::

In 1981, in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), the first in a series of symposia was held in conjunction with the [International Congress of History of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_History_of_Science). In 1985, the ICHM became an inter-union commission of both the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) and the [International Union of History and Philosophy of Science](wikipedia://en/33072433). In 1989 the first [Kenneth O. May Prize](wikipedia://en/25758309) was awarded to [Dirk Struik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Struik) and [Adolf P. Yushkevich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_P._Yushkevich).

[Joseph Dauben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Dauben) became chair of the executive committee of the ICHM in 1985 and proceeded to assemble the global contributions from 40 historians for the 2002 publication *Writing the History of Mathematics: Its Historical Development*, published by Birkhäuser. In his review, Donald Cook noted, "Because the book is not designed to completely explore issues, it may raise questions for readers."[^o2]

The ICHM began awarding the Montucla Prize, for the best article by an early career scholar in *Historia Mathematica*, in 2009. The award is given every four years.[^o3]
