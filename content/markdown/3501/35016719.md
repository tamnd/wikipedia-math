# Picard modular surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Picard modular surface"}

In mathematics, a **Picard modular surface**, studied by (txt), is a [complex surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_surface) constructed as a quotient of the unit ball in **C**<sup>2</sup> by a [Picard modular group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picard_modular_group). Picard modular surfaces are some of the simplest examples of [Shimura varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimura_varieties) and are sometimes used as a test case for the general theory of Shimura varieties.

:::
