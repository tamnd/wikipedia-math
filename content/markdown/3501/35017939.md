# Wolfgang Rautenberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Rautenberg"}

**Wolfgang Rautenberg** (27 February 1936 − 4 September 2011) was a German mathematician and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) whose areas of research were [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), [non-classical logic](wikipedia://en/3192556), [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365), [temporal logic](wikipedia://en/321481) and [self reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self_reference).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rautenberg was born in [Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potsdam). He graduated with an [abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur) from the [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in [Ludwigslust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwigslust). He studied mathematics and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [Humboldt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in East Berlin ([GDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany)), and there in 1963 Rautenberg received a doctorate and worked as a logician.[^o4][^o5] In 1968 Rautenberg graduated from the Humboldt University as an academic lecturer.[^o5] From 1969 to 1973 Rautenberg was [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) at the Humboldt University.[^o5][^o6] Because the [Stasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stasi) contacted him to force him into co-operation, Rautenberg left East Germany in 1973 and in 1974 became Professor in [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany).[^o5] In 1976 he accepted a call to chair the [academic department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_department)[^o5] for [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin).

Wolfgang Rautenberg was also a very active contributor in the [HP48](wikipedia://en/333248) and [HP49G / HP49G+](wikipedia://en/334998#49G) calculator community,[^o7][^o8][^o9] with many important contributions.[^o10]
