# Journal of Difference Equations and Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Difference Equations and Applications"}

***Journal of Difference Equations and Applications*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published monthly by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis). Established in 1995, it covers research on theory and applications of [difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation) and [discrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_system) [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o3] Its current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Saber Elaydi ([Trinity University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_University)).[^o3]

:::
