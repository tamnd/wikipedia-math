# Leonhard Euler Gold Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonhard Euler Gold Medal"}

The **Leonhard Euler Gold Medal** (Золотая медаль имени Леонарда Эйлера) is a medal named after the Swiss, German, and Russian mathematician [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902), awarded by the Отделением математических наук (Branch of Mathematical Sciences of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences)) for outstanding results in mathematics and physics. The medal was awarded once in 1957 to two scientists and since 1991 has been awarded every five years.[^o2]

:::

## Laureates {#Laureates}

- 1957 — [Igor Kurchatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Kurchatov)[^o4] and [Felix Frankl](wikipedia://en/67622198) for outstanding results in mathematics and physics
- 1991 — [Aleksandr Danilovich Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov) for fundamental contributions to the development of mathematics
- 1997 — [Yury Osipov](wikipedia://en/7047998) for outstanding results in mathematics and physics
- 2002 — [Ludvig Faddeev](wikipedia://en/4854639) for outstanding results in mathematics and physics
- 2007 — [Valery Vasilevich Kozlov](wikipedia://en/67899164) for a series of papers on nonlinear Hamiltonian systems of differential equations
- 2012 — [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515) for his deep contribution to the application of topological methods in quantum physics
- 2017 — [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815) for outstanding contributions to number theory and algebraic geometry
- 2022 — [Sergei Godunov](wikipedia://en/5665800) for outstanding results in the field of computational mathematics
