# Cohen algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohen algebra"}

In mathematical [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), a **Cohen algebra**, named after [Paul Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Cohen_%28mathematician%29), is a type of [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) used in the theory of [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205). A Cohen algebra is a Boolean algebra whose [completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completion_%28ring_theory%29) is isomorphic to the completion of a [free Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3058220) (Koppelberg 1993).

:::
