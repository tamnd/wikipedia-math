# Superstrong approximation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Superstrong approximation"}

**Superstrong approximation** is a generalisation of [strong approximation in algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_approximation_in_algebraic_groups) *G*, to provide [spectral gap](wikipedia://en/7999250) results. The spectrum in question is that of the [Laplacian matrix](wikipedia://en/1448472) associated to a family of quotients of a discrete group Γ; and the gap is that between the first and second eigenvalues (normalisation so that the first eigenvalue corresponds to constant functions as eigenvectors). Here Γ is a subgroup of the rational points of *G*, but need not be a [lattice](wikipedia://en/15782871): it may be a so-called [thin group](wikipedia://en/42832340). The "gap" in question is a lower bound (absolute constant) for the difference of those eigenvalues.

:::

A consequence and equivalent of this property, potentially holding for [Zariski dense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zariski_dense) subgroups Γ of the [special linear group](wikipedia://en/144823) over the integers, and in more general classes of algebraic groups *G*, is that the sequence of [Cayley graphs](wikipedia://en/358277) for reductions Γ<sub>*p*</sub> modulo prime numbers *p*, with respect to any fixed set *S* in Γ that is a [symmetric set](wikipedia://en/22260858) and [generating set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_set), is an [expander family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expander_family).[^o2]

In this context "strong approximation" is the statement that *S* when reduced generates the full group of points of *G* over the prime fields with *p* elements, when *p* is large enough. It is equivalent to the Cayley graphs being connected (when *p* is large enough), or that the locally constant functions on these graphs are constant, so that the eigenspace for the first eigenvalue is one-dimensional. Superstrong approximation therefore is a concrete quantitative improvement on these statements.

## Background {#Background}

**Property $(\tau)$** is an analogue in discrete group theory of [Kazhdan's property (T)](wikipedia://en/1362724), and was introduced by [Alexander Lubotzky](wikipedia://en/22754130).[^o4] For a given family of normal subgroups *N* of finite index in Γ, one equivalent formulation is that the Cayley graphs of the groups Γ/*N*, all with respect to a fixed symmetric set of generators *S*, form an expander family.[^o5] Therefore superstrong approximation is a formulation of property $(\tau)$, where the subgroups *N* are the kernels of reduction modulo large enough primes *p*.

The **Lubotzky–Weiss conjecture** states (for special linear groups and reduction modulo primes) that an expansion result of this kind holds independent of the choice of *S*. For applications, it is also relevant to have results where the modulus is not restricted to being a prime.[^o6]
