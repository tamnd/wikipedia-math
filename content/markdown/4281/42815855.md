# Yuri Kondratiev

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Kondratiev"}

**Yuri Kondratiev** (Кондратьєв Юрій Григорович; 23 October 1953 – 5 September 2023) was a Ukrainian mathematician and a professor at the [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) in Germany.

:::

Kondratiev died on 5 September 2023, at the age of 69.[^o3]

- His research interests included functional analysis, mathematical physics and stochastic calculus.
- Kondratiev was a member of the Kyiv school of functional analysis founded by [M. Krein](wikipedia://en/1190470) and led, for many years, by Y. Berezansky.
- His interests in mathematical physics were inspired by cooperation with the Moscow seminar in statistical Physics ([R. Dobrushin](wikipedia://en/6368767), [R. Minlos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Adol'fovich_Minlos), [Y. Sinai](wikipedia://en/1751626)). Yuri's collaboration with [A. Skorokhod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoliy_Skorokhod) has influenced most of his subsequent papers on stochastics.

## Biography {#Biography}

- In 1975, Yuri Kondratiev graduated with honours from [Kyiv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_University).
- In 1979, Kondratiev completed his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) with thesis entitled “Generalized functions in problems of infinite dimensional analysis” at the [Kyiv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_University).
- In 1987, Kondratiev obtained the Degree of [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) in the area "Spectral Analysis of Infinite Dimensional Elliptic Differential Operators”.
- From 1991 Kondratiev worked in Ukraine ([Institute of Mathematics of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_of_the_National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Ukraine), National Pedagogical Dragomanov University) as well as in Germany ([University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University)) and the UK ([University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading)). From 2008 he held a full Professor position at the [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University).

## Work {#Work}

- Kondratiev wrote a book entitled "Spectral Methods in Infinite Dimensional Analysis" (1988, with Y. Berezansky, in Russian; translated into English in 1995).
- Kondratiev co-authored a monograph "The Statistical Mechanics of Quantum Lattice Systems" (with [S. Albeverio](wikipedia://en/28869024), Y. Kozitsky and M. Röckner).
- Together with [S. Albeverio](wikipedia://en/28869024) and M. Röckner Yuri initiated a systematical study of Continuous Configuration Spaces. One space of stochastic distributions is named after him («Kondratiev space»).[^o6]
