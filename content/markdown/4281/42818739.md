In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **cubic-square tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) and [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::

It represents a [semiregular honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_honeycomb) as defined by all regular cells, although from the Wythoff construction, rectified square tiling r{4,4}, becomes the regular [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) {4,4}.

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A lower symmetry form, index 6, of this honeycomb can be constructed with \[(4,4,4,3\*\] symmetry, represented by a [trigonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3504388) fundamental domain, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . Another lower symmetry constructions exists with symmetry \[(4,4,(4,3)\*)\], index 48 and an ideal regular octahedral fundamental domain.
