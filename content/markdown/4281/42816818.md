In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **tetrahedral-triangular tiling honeycomb** is a [paracompact uniform honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb), constructed from [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858), [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), and [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) cells, in an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.

:::

It represents a [semiregular honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_honeycomb) as defined by all regular cells, although from the Wythoff construction, rectified tetrahedral r{3,3}, becomes the regular [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) {3,4}.
