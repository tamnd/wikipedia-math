# Weyl module

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weyl module"}

In algebra, a **Weyl module** is a [representation](wikipedia://en/12695) of a [reductive](wikipedia://en/1088386) [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924), introduced by (txt) and named after [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544). In [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 0 these representations are [irreducible](wikipedia://en/385982), but in positive characteristic they can be reducible, and their decomposition into irreducible components can be hard to determine.

:::
