# Jantzen filtration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jantzen filtration"}

In [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **Jantzen filtration** is a [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) of a [Verma module](wikipedia://en/2975234) of a [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587), or a [Weyl module](wikipedia://en/32084687) of a [reductive algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) of positive characteristic. Jantzen filtrations were introduced by (txt).

:::

## Jantzen filtration for Verma modules {#Jantzen_filtration_for_Verma_modules}

If *M*(λ) is a Verma module of a semisimple Lie algebra with highest weight λ, then the Janzen filtration is a decreasing filtration

$$M(\lambda)=M(\lambda)^0\supseteq M(\lambda)^1\supseteq M(\lambda)^2\supseteq\cdots.$$

It has the following properties:

- *M*(λ)<sup>1</sup>=*N*(λ), the unique maximal proper submodule of *M*(λ)
- The quotients *M*(λ)<sup>*i*</sup>/*M*(λ)<sup>*i*+1</sup> have non-degenerate [contravariant bilinear forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contravariant_form).
- The **Jantzen sum formula** holds:

$$\sum_{i>0}\text{Ch}(M(\lambda)^i) = \sum_{\alpha>0, s_\alpha(\lambda)<\lambda}\text{Ch}(M(s_\alpha \cdot \lambda))$$

where $\text{Ch}(\cdot)$ denotes the [formal character](wikipedia://en/10242885).
