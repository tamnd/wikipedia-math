# Mirabolic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mirabolic group"}

In mathematics, a **mirabolic subgroup** of the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564) GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*k*) is a subgroup consisting of automorphisms fixing a given non-zero vector in *k*<sup>*n*</sup>. Mirabolic subgroups were introduced by (Gelfand Kajdan 1975). The image of a mirabolic subgroup in the [projective general linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_general_linear_group) is a [parabolic subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) consisting of all elements fixing a given point of [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). The word "mirabolic" is a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of "miraculous parabolic". As an algebraic group, a mirabolic subgroup is the [semidirect product](wikipedia://en/48246) of a vector space with its group of automorphisms, and such groups are called **mirabolic groups**. The mirabolic subgroup is used to define the [Kirillov model](wikipedia://en/20671987) of a representation of the general linear group.

:::

As an example, the group of all matrices of the form $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ where $a$ is a nonzero element of the [field](wikipedia://en/10603) $k$ and $b$ is any element of $k$ is a mirabolic subgroup of the 2-dimensional general linear group.
