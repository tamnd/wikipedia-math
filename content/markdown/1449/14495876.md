# Conny Palm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conny Palm"}

*Image: Conny Palm (6979247547).jpg.* Palm and [BARK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BARK_%28computer%29)

**Conrad "Conny" Rudolf Agaton Palm** (May 31, 1907 – December 27, 1951)[^o3][^o4] was a Swedish [electrical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineer) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), known for several contributions to [teletraffic engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletraffic_engineering) and [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Palm enrolled at the School of Electrical Engineering at the [Royal Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Technology) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm) in 1925, being awarded his [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) (1940) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (1943) on a dissertation entitled *Intensitätsschwankungen im Fernsprechverkehr* (Intensity Fluctuations in Telephone Traffic). Palm's work was also joint with [L. M. Ericsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._M._Ericsson), cooperating with [Christian Jacobæus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Jacobæus). He attended [Harald Cramér](wikipedia://en/223129)'s queueing theory group, met [William Feller](wikipedia://en/206644) (1937).[^o5] Later, Palm was in the [Swedish Board for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Board_for_Computing_Machinery) (*Matematikmaskinnämnden*), where he led the project that developed the first Swedish computer, the [BARK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BARK_%28computer%29) (1947–51), informally referred to as CONIAC (Conny \[Palm\] Integrator And Calculator). He was adjunct professor in [telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications) at Royal Institute of Technology as well.

Professor [Håkan Sterky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Håkan_Sterky), who was Palm's thesis advisor, has characterised Palm as a [bohemian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemianism) and a brilliant statistician. He had started his research before he graduated, which seemed to be due to a lack of interest for some undergraduate courses rather than the level of difficulty of the courses. To apply pressure on him, it was finally agreed that his monthly salary from [Ericsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ericsson) would only be paid out if he had passed one of his remaining undergraduate exam that month. Sterky related how Palm would typically show up a few days before pay day wishing to sit for an oral exam.[^o7]
