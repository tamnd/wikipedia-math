# Alfred Menezes

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Menezes"}

**Alfred Menezes** is co-author of several books on [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), including the *Handbook of Applied Cryptography*, and is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Alfred Menezes' family is from [Goa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa), a state in western India, but he was born in [Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzania) and grew up in [Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait) except for a few years at a boarding school in India. His undergraduate and post-graduate degrees are from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o6]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After five years teaching at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University), in 1997 he returned to the University of Waterloo, where he is now a professor of mathematics in the Department of Combinatorics and Optimization. He co-founded and is a member of the [Centre for Applied Cryptographic Research](wikipedia://en/999894), and has served as its Managing Director.[^o8] Menezes' main areas of research are [Elliptic Curve Cryptography](wikipedia://en/9966) (ECC), [provable security](wikipedia://en/1182348), and related areas. He is a Canadian citizen.

Menezes' book *Elliptic Curve Public Key Cryptosystems*, published in 1993,[^o9] was the first book devoted entirely to ECC. He co-authored the widely-used reference book *Handbook of Applied Cryptography*.[^o10]

In 2001 Menezes won the [Hall Medal](wikipedia://en/17545919#List_of_Hall_Medal_Winners) of the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919).

Menezes has been a conference organizer or program committee member for approximately fifty conferences on Cryptography.[^o11] He was Program Chair for Crypto 2007, and in 2012 he was an invited speaker at Eurocrypt.[^o12]

Menezes, in co-operation with [Neal Koblitz](wikipedia://en/1587628), authored a series of *Another Look* papers that describe errors or weaknesses in existing security proofs, the first being *Another look at HMAC* (2013). The two now maintain a website dedicated to this type of papers.[^o13]
