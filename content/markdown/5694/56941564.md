# Kenny Easwaran

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Kenny Easwaran"}

**Kenny Easwaran** is an Indian American philosopher and Associate Professor of Philosophy at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine).

:::

His expertise lies in [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439). Easwaran is an associate editor of *[Journal of Philosophical Logic](wikipedia://en/11506760)*. Two of his articles have been selected as among the "ten best" of their year by the *[Philosopher's Annual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher's_Annual)*.[^o4][^o5]
