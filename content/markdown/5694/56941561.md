# Henry Gordon Dawson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Gordon Dawson"}

**Henry Gordon Dawson** (2 August 1862 in [Omagh, County Tyrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omagh) – 22 February 1918 in [Hastings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hastings), [East Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Sussex)) was an Irish mathematician. The [Dawson function](wikipedia://en/406903) is named after him.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dawson was educated at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (BA 1882, 1st Senior Moderator and Gold Medallist)[^o4] and at [Christ's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_College,_Cambridge) (BA as 19th wrangler 1886, MA 1890). In 1897 he created the function that has since been named after him.[^o5] His career included stints as Mathematical Lecturer at Cambridge (at least 1888–94) and at [Firth College, Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firth_College,_Sheffield) (1901–10). He was a member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy).[^o6]
