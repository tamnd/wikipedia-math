# Rolf Schneider

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rolf Schneider"}

**Rolf Georg Schneider** (born 17 March 1940, [Hagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagen), Germany)[^o3] is a mathematician. Schneider is a professor emeritus at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg). His main research interests are [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Schneider completed his PhD 1967 with [Ruth Moufang](wikipedia://en/2003831) at [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt) with a thesis titled (*Elliptisch gekrümmte Hyperflächen in der affinen Differentialgeometrie im Großen*). In 1969, he got his Habilitation in Bochum. In 1970, he was appointed as a full professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) and in 1974 at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg).

He became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o5] in 2015 and received an honorary doctorate of the [University of Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salzburg) in 2004.[^o6][^o7]

### Research {#Research}

Rolf Schneider is known for his solution of [Shephard's problem](wikipedia://en/10614436),[^o9] his books on stochastic and integral geometry in long-standing collaboration with [Wolfgang Weil](wikipedia://en/82761238),[^o10][^o11][^o12] and his comprehensive monography on the Brunn–Minkowski theory.[^o13][^o14]
