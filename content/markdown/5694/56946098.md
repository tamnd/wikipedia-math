# Christof Schütte

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christof Schütte"}

*Image: Christof Schütte.jpg.* Christof Schütte

**Christof Schütte** (born April 10, 1966) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in applied and computational mathematics at the [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin) and the Zuse Institute Berlin.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Christof Schütte was born in [Warburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warburg). He graduated in physics from [Paderborn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Paderborn) in 1991 and then obtained his PhD in mathematics under the supervision of Peter Deuflhard in 1994. He is currently a Professor in Numerical Mathematics and Scientific Computing at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin), and the president of the Zuse Institute Berlin.

Schütte has been one of the driving forces behind the Research Center “Mathematics for Key Technologies” (MATHEON) and has been its co-chair since 2008. Since 2015, he has acted as the head of the [Research Campus MODAL](http://www.forschungscampus-modal.de), a public-private partnership between mathematics research institutes and 15 industrial companies, and as the co-chair of the [Einstein Center for Mathematics in Berlin](https://www.ecmath.de).

2021 Schütte has been elected chairman of the board of the NHR association (Verein für Nationales Hochleistungsrechnen e.V. - NHR-Verein).

## Research {#Research}

Schütte's research has focused on multiscale modelling and simulation for complex systems, numerical mathematics, data-driven modelling and statistical learning with applications in the natural, materials and [life sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_Sciences). He is a co-inventor of the transfer operator approach[^o4] to [metastability](wikipedia://en/19833) that has led to the development of widely used computational methods like [Markov state models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_state_model)[^o5] in molecular dynamics or the (extended) dynamic mode decomposition.

Schütte has been an invited speaker at the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606431) (ICIAM) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich), 2007, and at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), 2010.[^o6][^o7]

## Publications {#Publications}

Christof Schütte has published more than 150 articles in scientific journals.[^o9]
