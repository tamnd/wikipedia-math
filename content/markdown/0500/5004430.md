# John McWhirter (mathematician)

*See [John McWhirter (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McWhirter_%28disambiguation%29) for other people of the same name.*

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John McWhirter"}

**John Graham McWhirter** (born 28 March 1949) is a British mathematician and engineer in the field of [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324).[^o3]

:::

John McWhirter attended [Newry High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newry_High_School). He graduated in mathematics from [Queen's University Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast) in 1970, and did his PhD there in 1973 on "The Virial Theorem in Collision Theory" under Benjamin Moiseiwitsch.[^o4] He started working in the Signal Processing Group at the [Royal Signals and Radar Establishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Signals_and_Radar_Establishment), [Great Malvern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Malvern), in the late 1970s, and has worked there for RSRE's successor organisations, currently [QinetiQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QinetiQ). McWhirter left QinetiQ on 31 August 2007 to take up his current post as Distinguished Research Professor in Engineering at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University).

His work has mainly been in military areas including [radar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar), [sonar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonar) and communications, recently branching into civil applications. A particular interest is "blind" signal detection in which one does not know whether a signal is present, or its nature.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 1986 honorary [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor) at Queen's University Belfast
- 1988 visiting professor at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)
- 1996 Elected as a [Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Academy_of_Engineering) (FREng)
- 1999 Elected as a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society).
- 2000 Honorary Doctorate from the Queen's University Belfast
- 2002 Honorary Doctorate from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh)
- 2003 [EURASIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURASIP_Journal_on_Advances_in_Signal_Processing) European Group Technical Achievement Award [^o6]

He is also a Fellow of the [Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Physics). He is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) (IMA) and in 2002/3 its president. He is also a Founding Fellow of the [Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales).
