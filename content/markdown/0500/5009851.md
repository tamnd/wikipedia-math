# Tsuruichi Hayashi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tsuruichi Hayashi"}

**Tsuruichi Hayashi** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian) of [Japanese mathematics](wikipedia://en/1055369). He was born in [Tokushima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokushima), Japan.[^o2]

:::

He was the founder of the *[Tohoku Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o3]
