# Correlation inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Correlation inequality"}

Korrelationsungleichung A **correlation inequality** is any of a number of inequalities satisfied by the [correlation functions](wikipedia://en/59500109) of a model. Such inequalities are of particular use in [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and in [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805).[^o2]

:::

Examples include:

- [Bell's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell's_inequality)
- [FKG inequality](wikipedia://en/20283423)
- [Griffiths inequality](wikipedia://en/32014314), and its generalisation, the [Ginibre inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginibre_inequality)
- [Gaussian correlation inequality](wikipedia://en/53673584)
