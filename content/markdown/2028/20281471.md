# Christophe Breuil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christophe Breuil"}

**Christophe Breuil** (; born 1968) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who works in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

## Work {#Work}

With [Fred Diamond](wikipedia://en/8356786), [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931) and [Brian Conrad](wikipedia://en/4099038) in 1999, he proved the [Taniyama–Shimura conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taniyama–Shimura_conjecture), which previously had only been proved for semistable elliptic curves by [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027) and Taylor in their proof of [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953). Later, he worked on the [*p*-adic Langlands conjecture](wikipedia://en/7676971).

## Academic life {#Academic_life}

Breuil attended schools in [Brive-la-Gaillarde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brive-la-Gaillarde) and [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse) and studied from 1990 to 1992 at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). In 1993, he obtained his DEA degree at the [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) located in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay). From 1993 to 1996 he conducted research at the École Polytechnique and taught simultaneously at the University of Paris-Sud, Orsay, and in 1996 received his PhD from the École Polytechnique, supervised by [Jean-Marc Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Marc_Fontaine) with the thesis "Cohomologie log-cristalline et représentations galoisiennes p -adiques".[^o5] In 1997, he gave the [Cours Peccot](wikipedia://en/60897406) at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France). In 2001 he obtained a habilitation degree entitled "Aspects entiers de la théorie de Hodge p-adique et applications" at Paris-Sud 11 University. Between 2002 and 2010 he was at the [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES). From 2010 he has been in the Mathematics Department of [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) as Director of Research with the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). In 2007–2008 he was a visiting professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 1993 he was awarded the [Prix Gaston Julia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Gaston_Julia) at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).

In 2002 he received the Grand Prix Jacques Herbrand of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) and the 2006 [Prix Dargelos Anciens Élèves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Dargelos_Anciens_Élèves) of the École Polytechnique.

He was an invited speaker in [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 2010, [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India) on the topic of "*Number Theory.*"[^o7]
