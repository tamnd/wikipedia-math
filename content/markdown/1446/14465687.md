# Oleg Viro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oleg Viro"}

*Image: Oleg Viro.jpg.* Oleg Viro in 2008 at the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)

**Oleg Yanovich Viro** (Олег Янович Виро) (b. 13 May 1948, [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the fields of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), most notably [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198), [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Viro developed a "patchworking" technique in algebraic geometry, which allows real [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808) to be constructed by a "cut and paste" method. Using this technique, Viro completed the [isotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) classification of non-singular plane projective curves of degree 7. The patchworking technique was one of the fundamental ideas which motivated the development of [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041). In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), Viro is most known for his joint work with [Vladimir Turaev](wikipedia://en/56815253), in which the Turaev–Viro invariants (relatives of the [Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reshetikhin-Turaev_invariant)) and related [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757) notions were introduced.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Viro studied at the [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University) where he received his Ph.D. degree in 1974; his advisor was [Vladimir Rokhlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Rokhlin_%28Soviet_mathematician%29). Viro taught from 1973 until 1991 at [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University). Since 1986 he has been a member of the [Saint Petersburg Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Department_of_Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics_of_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) of the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). In 1992-1997, Viro was a F. B. Jones chair professor in Topology at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside).

In 1994-2003 he was a professor at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden). On 8 February 2007, Viro and his colleague [Burglind Juhl-Jöricke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burglind_Juhl-Jöricke) were forced to resign from the university.[^o5] There had been a history of conflict at the Mathematics Institute, with allegations of disagreeable behavior by several parties in the conflict.[^o6] A number of Swedish, European and American mathematicians protested the manner in which the two Professors of Mathematics were forced to resign. These protests include the following:

- an open letter by [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541), former president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868),
- a letter by [Ari Laptev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Laptev), current president of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470), and
- a letter from [M. Salah Baouendi](wikipedia://en/44998696), [Arthur Jaffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Jaffe), [Joel Lebowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Lebowitz), [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb) and [Nicolai Reshetikhin](wikipedia://en/7487626).[^o7][^o8]

As of 2009, Viro is a senior researcher at the St. Petersburg Department of the Steklov Institute of Mathematics, and a professor at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Viro was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 1983 ([Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw)) and the [European Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 2000 ([Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona)). He was awarded the [Göran Gustafsson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göran_Gustafsson_Prize) (1997) by the Swedish government.

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
