# Rebecca Waldecker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rebecca Waldecker"}

*Image: Prof. Dr. Rebecca Waldecker (34646320790) (cropped).jpg.* Rebecca Waldecker, 2017

**Rebecca Anne Hedwig Waldecker** (born 1979) is a German mathematician specializing in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). She is professor for algebra at [Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Waldecker is originally from [Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aachen). She earned her doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) in 2007, under the supervision of Helmut Bender, and in 2014 completed her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg.

After postdoctoral research as a Leverhulme Fellow at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham), Waldecker joined Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg as a junior professor in 2009. She became professor for algebra in 2015.

## Books {#Books}

Waldecker is the author of the book *Isolated Involutions in Finite Groups* (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society, 2013), developed from her doctoral dissertation.

With [Lasse Rempe-Gillen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasse_Rempe-Gillen), she is the coauthor of *Primzahltests für Einsteiger: Zahlentheorie, Algorithmik, Kryptographie* (Vieweg+Teubner, 2009; 2nd ed., Springer, 2016), a book on [primality tests](wikipedia://en/183751) that was translated into English as *[Primality Testing for Beginners](wikipedia://en/66154359)* (Student Mathematical Library 70, American Mathematical Society, 2014).

She became a coauthor to the 2012 textbook *Elementare Algebra und Zahlentheorie* of Gernot Stroth, in its second edition (Mathematik Kompakt, Springer, 2019).
