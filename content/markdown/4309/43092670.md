# Batch cryptography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Batch cryptography"}

**Batch cryptography** is a field of [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology) focused on the design of [cryptographic protocols](wikipedia://en/591994) that perform operations—such as [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294), decryption, [key exchange](wikipedia://en/578092), and [authentication](wikipedia://en/47967)—on multiple inputs simultaneously, rather than processing each input individually.[^o2] Batching cryptographic operations can significantly reduce the marginal cost of handling individual inputs—a principle that was first introduced by Amos Fiat in 1989.[^o3]

:::
