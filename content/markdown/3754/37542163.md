# Horrocks construction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horrocks construction"}

In mathematics, the **Horrocks construction** is a method for constructing [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524), especially over [projective spaces](wikipedia://en/242135), introduced by (txt, section 10). His original construction gave an example of an indecomposable rank 2 vector bundle over 3-dimensional projective space, and generalizes to give examples of vector bundles of higher ranks over other projective spaces. The Horrocks construction is used in the [ADHM construction](wikipedia://en/5165589) to construct [instantons](wikipedia://en/296077) over the [4-sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-sphere).

:::
