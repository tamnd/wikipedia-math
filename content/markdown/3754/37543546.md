# George Casella

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="George Casella"}

**George Casella** (January 22, 1951 – June 17, 2012) was a Distinguished Professor in the Department of Statistics at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida). He died from [multiple myeloma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_myeloma).[^o5]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Casella completed his undergraduate education at [Fordham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordham_University) and graduate education at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). He served on the faculty of [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), and the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida). His contributions focused on the area of statistics including [Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/56098), [model selection](wikipedia://en/3664073), and [genomic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics).[^o5] He was particularly active in [Bayesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian) and [empirical Bayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_Bayes) methods, with works connecting with the [Stein phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stein_phenomenon), on assessing and accelerating the convergence of [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) methods, as in his [Rao–Blackwellization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rao–Blackwellization) technique,[^o6] and recasting [lasso](wikipedia://en/22349350) as [Bayesian posterior mode estimation](wikipedia://en/1792433) with independent [Laplace](wikipedia://en/1106213) priors.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

Casella was named as a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1988, and he was made an Elected Fellow of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1989. In 2009, he was made a Foreign Member of the [Spanish Royal Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Royal_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o9]
