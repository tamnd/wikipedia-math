# David Tong (physicist)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Tong"}

**David Tong** is a British [theoretical physicist](wikipedia://en/19594028). He is a professor at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), working in the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Applied_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Physics) (DAMTP). He is also a fellow of [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge).[^o3] His research mainly concerns [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). He is the joint recipient of the 2008 [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110)[^o4] and is currently a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation#Simons_Investigators_awardees).[^o5] He is also known for his outreach activities and for his freely available lecture notes covering a wide range of topics in physics.[^o6]

:::

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Tong grew up in Crawley, UK. He attended [Hazelwick School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazelwick_School) before undertaking his undergraduate studies in Mathematical Physics at the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham). He completed his MSc in Mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), and his PhD in Theoretical Physics at [Swansea University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University).[^o8]

He held research positions at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR), Mumbai, at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) and at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o9] Between 2001 and 2004 he was a Pappalardo Fellow at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) Center for Theoretical Physics.[^o10] He joined the faculty at Cambridge in 2004.

Tong works on quantum field theory and its application to different areas of physics. His early work was on [solitons](wikipedia://en/51654) and includes the discovery (with Amihay Hanany) of a vortex solution in [supersymmetric Yang-Mills theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetric_Yang–Mills_theory_%28disambiguation%29).[^o11] With [Eva Silverstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Silverstein) and Mohsen Alishahia, he proposed a new, predictive mechanism for cosmic inflation based on the [Dirac–Born–Infeld (DBI) action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born–Infeld_model) which helped motivate more systematic analyses of primordial non-Gaussianity.[^o12] In work with Andreas Karch, he uncovered a web of dualities in 3d gauge theories.[^o13]

Tong is known for his widely watched videos on theoretical physics, including a [Royal Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institution) lecture on quantum field theory,[^o14] and a *[Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976)* primer on the [Standard Model](wikipedia://en/47641).[^o15]
