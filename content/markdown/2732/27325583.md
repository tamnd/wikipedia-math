# Frank W. J. Olver

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank W. J. Olver"}

**Frank William John Olver** (December 15, 1924 – April 23, 2013) was a professor of mathematics at the Institute for Physical Science and Technology and Department of Mathematics at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) who worked on [asymptotic analysis](wikipedia://en/641995), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). He was the editor in chief of the NIST [Digital Library of Mathematical Functions](wikipedia://en/12647205).

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

1969: Silver Medal of the [US Department of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Commerce)

1974: Fellow of the U.K. [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139)

1996: Foreign Member of the [Royal Society of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Sciences_in_Uppsala), [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), Sweden

2011: Gold Medal of the [US Department of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Commerce)
