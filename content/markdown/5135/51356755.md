# Gerhard Christoph Hermann Vechtmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerhard Christoph Hermann Vechtmann"}

**Gerhard Christoph Hermann Vechtmann** (10 April 1817 – 2 August 1857) was a German mathematician, who is best known for his work on [lemniscates](wikipedia://en/5210590).

:::

Vechtman was born in [Wittmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittmund) in the northern part of the [Kingdom of Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hanover) in Germany, where his father worked as a preacher. From the ages 10 to 15 he attended the local school in Wittmund before attending the [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) (high school) in neighbouring [Aurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurich) for three years. After school Vechtmann went on to study math and sciences in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen). In Göttingen he became a member of the pedagogic seminar (institute for teacher education) and taught at the local gymnasium until he was appointed *Hofmeister* at the *Ritterakademie* in [Lüneburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lüneburg) in 1841. In 1843 he handed in his dissertation *De curvis lemniscatis* at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and was awarded a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy). At the end of 1845 he became a teacher for math and sciences at a school in [Eutin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eutin) and 1848 he was made the vice principal at school in [Meldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meldorf). Finally he was made the principal of the newly founded gymnasium in [Rendsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendsburg) in 1856. On 18 July 1857 he left Rendsburg with his wife and his two children for a vacation at his in-laws. There he suddenly fell ill and died within three days on 2 August 1857.[^o2][^o3]

In his dissertation *De curvis lemniscatis* he examined the [lemniscate of Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/250908) and discovered a [surprising property of certain angles occurring in it](wikipedia://en/250908#Angles).[^o4]
