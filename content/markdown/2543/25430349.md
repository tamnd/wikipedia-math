# Steve Shnider

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Shnider"}

**Steve Shnider** (סטיב שניידר) is a retired professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University).[^o2] He received a PhD in Mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1972, under [Shlomo Sternberg](wikipedia://en/5375996).[^o3] His main interests are in the [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) of [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526); algebraic methods in the theory of [deformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory) of geometric structures; [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298); [supersymmetry](wikipedia://en/224636); [operads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operad_theory); and [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714). He retired in 2014.[^o4]

:::

## Book on operads {#Book_on_operads}

A 2002 book of Markl, Shnider and Stasheff *Operads in algebra, topology, and physics* was the first book to provide a systematic treatment of [operad theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operad_theory), an area of mathematics that came to prominence in 1990s and found many applications in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), graph cohomology, [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609), and other areas. The book was the subject of a *Featured Review* in [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646) by [Alexander A. Voronov](wikipedia://en/68168204) which stated, in particular: "The first book whose main goal is the theory of operads per se ... a book such as this one has been long awaited by a wide scientific readership, including mathematicians and theoretical physicists ... a great piece of mathematical literature and will be helpful to anyone who needs to use operads, from graduate students to mature mathematicians and physicists."[^o6]
