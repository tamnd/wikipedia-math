# Lefschetz pencil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lefschetz pencil"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Lefschetz pencil** is a construction in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) considered by [Solomon Lefschetz](wikipedia://en/384384), used to analyse the [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) of an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) $V$.

:::

## Description {#Description}

A *pencil* is a particular kind of [linear system of divisors](wikipedia://en/1186886) on $V$, namely a one-parameter family, parametrised by the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578). This means that in the case of a [complex algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/39219687) $V$, a Lefschetz pencil is something like a [fibration](wikipedia://en/316826) over the [Riemann sphere](wikipedia://en/30876799); but with two qualifications about singularity.

The first point comes up if we assume that $V$ is given as a [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), and the divisors on $V$ are [hyperplane sections](wikipedia://en/3459767). Suppose given hyperplanes $H$ and $H'$, spanning the pencil — in other words, $H$ is given by $L=0$ and $H'$ by $L'=0$ for linear forms $L$ and $L'$, and the general hyperplane section is $V$ intersected with

$$\lambda L + \mu L^\prime = 0.\$$

Then the intersection $J$ of $H$ with $H'$; has [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) two. There is a [rational mapping](wikipedia://en/2783119)

$$V \rightarrow P^1\ :\ p\mapsto \left[ L'(p):-L(p) \right]$$

which is in fact well-defined only outside the points on the intersection of $J$ with $V$. To make a well-defined mapping, some [blowing up](wikipedia://en/1817790) must be applied to $V$.

The second point is that the fibers may themselves 'degenerate' and acquire [singular points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_singularity) (where [Bertini's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertini's_lemma) applies, the *general* hyperplane section will be smooth). A Lefschetz pencil restricts the nature of the acquired singularities, so that the topology may be analysed by the [vanishing cycle](wikipedia://en/5859204) method. The fibres with singularities are required to have a unique quadratic singularity, only.

It has been shown that Lefschetz pencils exist in [characteristic zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_zero). They apply in ways similar to, but more complicated than, [Morse functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function) on [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold). It has also been shown that Lefschetz pencils exist in [characteristic p](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_p) for the étale topology.

[Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305) has found a role for Lefschetz pencils in [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), leading to more recent research interest in them.
