# Segal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Segal"}

**Segal**, and its variants including [Sagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagal), [Segel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segel), [Sigal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigal_%28disambiguation%29#People) or [Siegel](wikipedia://en/4479540), is a family name which is primarily [Ashkenazi Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jewish).

:::

The name is said to be derived from [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) *segan leviyyah* (assistant to the [Levites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levites))[^o2][^o3][^o4] although a minority of sources claim that "Segal" is instead a Hebrew abbreviation for *segan le-kehunah* (assistant to the [Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohen_%28surname%29) – assistant to the priest).[^o5][^o6]
