# Kurushima Kinai

Kurushima

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kurushima Kinai"}

**Kurushima Kinai**,[^o2] also known as **Kurushima Yoshita**[^o3] and **Kurushima Yoshihiro**,[^o4] was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period).[^o5]

:::

The Japanese board game of [shogi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shogi) attracted Kurushima's interest; and he was recognized in his own time as a master player.[^o6] Among shogi players, he continues today to be well known for seven "puzzle ring" [gambits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambit) with subsequent sequenced maneuvers—including the "silver puzzle ring."[^o7]

In his lifetime, he was recognized among the most prominent intellectuals. His mathematical gift was highly esteemed.[^o8] Kurushima, like most of his contemporaries, was very interested in the mathematical problems involved in "magic squares."[^o9]
