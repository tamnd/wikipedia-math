# Icosahedral bipyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Icosahedral bipyramid"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **icosahedral bipyramid** is the [direct sum](wikipedia://en/2084687) of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) and a segment, {3,5} + { }. Each face of a central icosahedron is attached with two [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra), creating 40 tetrahedral cells, 80 triangular faces, 54 edges, and 14 vertices.[^o3] An icosahedral bipyramid can be seen as two [icosahedral pyramids](wikipedia://en/43632532) augmented together at their [bases](wikipedia://en/9579379).

:::

It is the dual of a [dodecahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5981640), [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) , so the bipyramid can be described as . Both have [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502) symmetry \[2,3,5\], order 240.

Having all regular cells (tetrahedra), it is a [Blind polytope](wikipedia://en/72252379).
