# Goodstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goodstein"}

**Goodstein** is a [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname). It is the surname of:

:::

- [Anastasia Goodstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Goodstein), American web content producer and author
- [David Goodstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Goodstein) (1939–2024), American physicist, married to Judith
- [David B. Goodstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_B._Goodstein) (1932–1985), American publisher and LGBT activist
- [Eban Goodstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eban_Goodstein) (born 1960), American economist
- [Judith R. Goodstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_R._Goodstein) (born 1939), American historian of science, married to David
- [Reuben Goodstein](wikipedia://en/1586970) (1912–1985), English mathematician and philosopher of mathematics

## Origins {#Origins}

Goodstein is a part-translation from the name [Gutstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gutstein), a German-origin name with gut meaning good, and Stein meaning stone.[^o3]
