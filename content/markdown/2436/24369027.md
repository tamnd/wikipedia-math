# Small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron** (or **fat star**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [small ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157630). It is visually identical to the [small dodecicosacron](wikipedia://en/24081988). Its faces are darts. A part of each dart lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Faces have two angles of $\arccos(\frac{5}{12}+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{5})\approx 12.661\,078\,804\,43^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{5}{12}-\frac{1}{60}\sqrt{5})\approx 116.996\,396\,851\,70^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(-\frac{1}{12}-\frac{19}{60}\sqrt{5})\approx 217.681\,445\,539\,45^{\circ}$. Its [dihedral angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_angles) equal $\arccos({\frac{-44-3\sqrt{5}}{61}})\approx 146.230\,659\,755\,53^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{31+5\sqrt{5}}{38}\approx 1.110\,008\,944\,41$.
