# Medial icosacronic hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Medial icosacronic hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Medial icosacronic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a medial icosacronic hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **medial icosacronic hexecontahedron** (or **midly sagittal ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157622). Its faces are darts. Part of each dart lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Faces have two angles of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4})\approx 41.409\,622\,109\,27^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{8}+\frac{7}{24}\sqrt{5})\approx 58.184\,446\,117\,59^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(-\frac{1}{8}-\frac{7}{24}\sqrt{5})\approx 218.996\,309\,663\,87^{\circ}$. Its [dihedral angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_angles) equal $\arccos(-\frac{5}{7})\approx 135.584\,691\,402\,81^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{27+7\sqrt{5}}{22}\approx 1.938\,748\,901\,93$.
