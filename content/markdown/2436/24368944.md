# Small hexagonal hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small hexagonal hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small hexagonal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small hexagonal hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small hexagonal hexecontahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [small snub icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157567). It is partially [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952), having coincident [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), as its dual has coplanar triangular faces.

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

Treating it as a simple non-convex solid (without intersecting surfaces), it has 180 faces (all triangles), 270 edges, and 92 vertices (twelve with degree 10, twenty with degree 12, and sixty with degree 3), giving an [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) of 92 − 270 + 180 = +2.

## Faces {#Faces}

The faces are irregular hexagons. Denoting the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) by $\phi$ and putting $\xi = \frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{1+4\phi}\approx -0.433\,380\,199\,59$, the hexagons have five equal angles of $\arccos(\xi)\approx 115.682\,268\,170\,75^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(\phi^{-2}\xi-\phi^{-1})\approx 141.588\,659\,146\,23^{\circ}$. Each face has four long and two short edges. The ratio between the edge lengths is

$$1/2 +1/2\times\sqrt{(1-\xi)/(\phi^{3}-\xi)}\approx 0.777\,024\,337\,46.$$

The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\xi/(1+\xi))\approx 139.893\,813\,264\,51^{\circ}$.

## Construction {#Construction}

Disregarding self-intersecting surfaces, the small hexagonal hexecontahedron can be constructed as a [Kleetope](wikipedia://en/31165912) of a [pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196). It is therefore a second order Kleetope of the [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361). In other words, by adding a shallow pentagonal pyramid to each face of a regular dodecahedron, we get a pentakis dodecahedron. By adding an even shallower triangular pyramid to each face of the pentakis dodecahedron, we get a small hexagonal hexecontahedron.

The 60 vertices of degree 3 correspond to the apex vertex of each triangular pyramid of the Kleetope, or to each face of the pentakis dodecahedron. The 20 vertices of degree 12 and 12 vertices of degree 10 correspond to the vertices of the pentakis dodecahedron, and also respectively to the 20 hexagons and 12 pentagons of the [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282), the dual solid to the pentakis dodecahedron.

:::
