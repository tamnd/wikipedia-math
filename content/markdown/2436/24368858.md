# Great hexagonal hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great hexagonal hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great hexagonal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great hexagonal hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great hexagonal hexecontahedron** (or **great astroid ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [great snub dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157674). It is partially [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952), having coincident [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), as its dual has coplanar pentagrammic faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The faces are nonconvex hexagons. Denoting the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) by $\phi$, the hexagons have one angle of $\arccos(-\phi^{-1})\approx 128.172\,707\,627\,01^{\circ}$, one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(-\phi^{-1})\approx 231.827\,292\,372\,99^{\circ}$, and four angles of $90^{\circ}$. They have two long edges, two of medium length and two short ones. If the long edges have length $2$, the medium ones have length $1+\phi^{-3/2}\approx 1.485\,868\,271\,76$ and the short ones $1-\phi^{-3/2}\approx 0.514\,131\,728\,24$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $90^{\circ}$.
