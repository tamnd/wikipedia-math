# Great triakis icosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great triakis icosahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great triakis icosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great triakis icosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great triakis icosahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [great stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157457). Its faces are isosceles triangles. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(-\frac{3}{20}+\frac{3}{20}\sqrt{5})\approx 79.314\,951\,312\,25^{\circ}$ and two of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{20}\sqrt{5})\approx 50.342\,524\,343\,87^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-24+15\sqrt{5}}{61})\approx 81.001\,410\,024\,84^{\circ}$.
