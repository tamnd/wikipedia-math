# Great disdyakis dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great disdyakis dodecahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great disdyakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great disdyakis dodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great disdyakis dodecahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [great truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157407). It has 48 triangular faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{2})\approx 22.062\,191\,157\,54^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{6}-\frac{1}{12}\sqrt{2})\approx 106.530\,027\,150\,22^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{12}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2})\approx 51.407\,781\,692\,24^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-71+12\sqrt{2}}{97})\approx 123.848\,891\,579\,44^{\circ}$.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The *great disdyakis dodecahedron* is topologically identical to the convex [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), [disdyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/724752), which is dual to the [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879).
