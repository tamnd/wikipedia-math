# Small icosacronic hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small icosacronic hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small icosacronic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small icosacronic hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small icosacronic hexecontahedron** (or **small lanceal trisicosahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [small icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157496). Its faces are [kites](wikipedia://en/84840). Part of each kite lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The kites have two angles of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{20}\sqrt{5})\approx 50.342\,524\,343\,87^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{12}-\frac{19}{60}\sqrt{5})\approx 142.318\,554\,460\,55^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(-\frac{5}{12}-\frac{1}{60}\sqrt{5})\approx 116.996\,396\,851\,70^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-44-3\sqrt{5}}{61})\approx 146.230\,659\,755\,53^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{31+5\sqrt{5}}{38}\approx 1.110\,008\,944\,41$.
