# Small hexagrammic hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small hexagrammic hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small hexagrammic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small hexagrammic hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small hexagrammic hexecontahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [small retrosnub icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157694). It is partially [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952), having coincident [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), as its dual has coplanar triangular faces.

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

Its faces are hexagonal stars with two short and four long edges. Denoting the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) by $\phi$ and putting $\xi = \frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{1+4\phi}\approx 0.933\,380\,199\,59$, the stars have five equal angles of $\arccos(\xi)\approx 21.031\,988\,967\,51^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(\phi^{-2}\xi-\phi^{-1})\approx 254.840\,055\,162\,43^{\circ}$. Each face has four long and two short edges. The ratio between the edge lengths is

$$1/2 -1/2\times\sqrt{(1-\xi)/(\phi^{3}-\xi)}\approx 0.428\,986\,992\,12.$$

The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\xi/(1+\xi))\approx 61.133\,452\,273\,64^{\circ}$. Part of each face is inside the solid, hence is not visible in solid models.
