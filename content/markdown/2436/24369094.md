# Medial disdyakis triacontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Medial disdyakis triacontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Medial disdyakis triacontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a medial disdyakis triacontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **medial disdyakis triacontahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [truncated dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157518). It has 120 triangular faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{10})\approx 95.739\,170\,477\,27^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(\frac{3}{8}+\frac{11}{40}\sqrt{5})\approx 8.142\,571\,179\,89^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(-\frac{3}{8}+\frac{11}{40}\sqrt{5})\approx 76.118\,258\,342\,85^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(-\frac{9}{11})\approx 144.903\,198\,772\,42^{\circ}$. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.
