# Medial hexagonal hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Medial hexagonal hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Medial hexagonal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a medial hexagonal hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **medial hexagonal hexecontahedron** (or **midly dentoid ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [snub icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157687).

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The faces of the medial hexagonal hexecontahedron are irregular nonconvex hexagons. Denote the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) by $\phi$, and let $\xi\approx -0.377\,438\,833\,12$ be the real zero of the polynomial $8x^3-4x^2+1$. The number $\xi$ can be written as $\xi=-1/(2\rho)$, where $\rho$ is the [plastic ratio](wikipedia://en/2148532). Then each face has four equal angles of $\arccos(\xi)\approx 112.175\,128\,045\,27^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(\phi^2\xi+\phi)\approx 50.958\,265\,917\,31^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(\phi^{-2}\xi-\phi^{-1})\approx 220.341\,221\,901\,59^{\circ}$. Each face has two long edges, two of medium length and two short ones. If the medium edges have length $2$, the long ones have length $1+\sqrt{(1-\xi)/(-\phi^{-3}-\xi)}\approx 4.121\,448\,816\,41$ and the short ones $1-\sqrt{(1-\xi)/(\phi^{3}-\xi)}\approx 0.453\,587\,559\,98$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\xi/(\xi+1))\approx 127.320\,132\,197\,62^{\circ}$.
