# Great dodecacronic hexecontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great dodecacronic hexecontahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great dodecacronic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great dodecacronic hexecontahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great dodecacronic hexecontahedron** (or **great lanceal ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [great dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157482). Its 60 intersecting [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) faces are [kites](wikipedia://en/84840). Part of each kite lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each kite has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{5}{8}-\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{5})\approx 69.788\,198\,194\,11^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{10}\sqrt{5})\approx 91.512\,394\,720\,74^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{8}-\frac{9}{40}\sqrt{5})\approx 128.911\,208\,891\,04^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-19+8\sqrt{5}}{41})\approx 91.553\,403\,672\,16^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{21+3\sqrt{5}}{22}\approx 1.259\,463\,815\,11$.
