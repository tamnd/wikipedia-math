# David Hedgley

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Hedgley"}

**David Rice Hedgley Jr.** (born January 21, 1937 in Chicago, Illinois and died April 13, 2024 in Seal Beach, California) was an American [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made major contributions to the field of [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics). One of his contributions was the solution of the hidden-line problem in computer 3D graphics.[^o3]

:::
