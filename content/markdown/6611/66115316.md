# Anne-Sophie Kaloghiros

Academics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anne-Sophie Kaloghiros"}

**Anne-Sophie Kaloghiros** is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) researcher in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and reader in Mathematics at [Brunel University London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University_London).[^o3] Kaloghiros was awarded the [London Mathematical Society (LMS)](wikipedia://en/591097) [Emmy Noether Fellowship](wikipedia://en/66190328) in 2020.[^o4]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Kaloghiros earned her BA in pure mathematics (honours) from the [Université d’Orsay, Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 2001, a Degree of the [Ecole Centrale Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale_Paris) in 2003, and a Certificate of Advanced Studies in Mathematics, Part III (Distinction) at [Queens’ College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 2003 as well.

Kaloghiros earned her PhD in [Algebraic Geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) from [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 2007. Her dissertation, *The Topology of Terminal Quartic 3-Folds*, was supervised by [Alessio Corti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessio_Corti). She was a junior research fellow, [Trinity Hall, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge) 2007–2011,[^o7] a postdoctoral fellow, [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 2011, and research associate, [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) 2012–2014. She has held visiting and postdoctoral positions at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI, Berkeley) in 2009 and 2019,[^o8] at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) (RIMS, Kyoto University) 2009–2010, [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago) in 2011, and the [Hausdorff Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221) (HIM, Bonn, Germany) in 2014. She is currently a senior lecturer in Maths/Op at [Brunel University London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University_London).[^o9] She is an expert on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [birational geometry](wikipedia://en/382733). She serves on the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) Mentoring African Research in Mathematics (MARM) board.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Kaloghiros received the Brunel Athena Swan Award for 2017–2018.[^o12] In 2020, she was awarded the [London Mathematical Society (LMS)](wikipedia://en/591097) [Emmy Noether Fellowship](wikipedia://en/66190328).[^o4] Her research has been funded by the [Heilbronn Institute for Mathematical Research](wikipedia://en/61922775), [the Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council), the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940), and [Glasgow Mathematical Journal Trust](wikipedia://en/21523412).
