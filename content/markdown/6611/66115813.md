# Kirsten Morris

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kirsten Morris"}

**Kirsten Anna Morris** (born 1960) is a Canadian [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) specializing in [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), including work on flexible structures, [smart materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_material), [hysteresis](wikipedia://en/147003), and [infinite-dimensional optimization](wikipedia://en/1300939). She is a professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), the former chair of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Control and Systems, the author of two books on control theory, and an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Morris was motivated to study mathematical economics at [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) by a job doing [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) at a bank, but lost interest in the economic applications of mathematics after a year, instead switching into a program in mathematics and engineering, which she finished in 1982. She became interested in control theory while studying for a master's degree at the University of Waterloo. After completing the degree in 1984, she continued at Waterloo as a doctoral student, and earned her Ph.D. there in 1989. Her dissertation, *Finite-Dimensional Control of Infinite-Dimensional Systems*, was supervised by [Mathukumalli Vidyasagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathukumalli_Vidyasagar).

After a year as a staff scientist at the [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) [Langley Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley_Research_Center), she returned to Waterloo as an assistant professor in the Department of Applied Mathematics in 1990. She became a full professor there in 2003, and also holds a cross-appointment in the Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering. She chaired the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Control and Systems from 2018 to 2019, and has held leadership positions in the [IEEE Control Systems Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Control_Systems_Society) and the [International Federation of Automatic Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_Automatic_Control).

## Books {#Books}

Morris is the author of the books *Introduction to Feedback Control* (Harcourt-Brace, 2001) and *Controller Design for Distributed Parameter Systems* (Springer, 2020). She is the editor of *Control of Flexible Structures: Papers from the Workshop on Problems in Sensing, Identification and Control of Flexible Structures held in Waterloo, Ontario, June 1992* ([American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 1993).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2020, Morris was named an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow), affiliated with the [IEEE Control Systems Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Control_Systems_Society), "for contributions to control and estimator design for infinite-dimensional systems". She was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in the 2021 class of fellows, "for contributions to modeling, approximation, and control design for distributed parameter systems". She is also a Fellow of the [International Federation of Automatic Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_Automatic_Control).
