# Corestriction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Corestriction"}

In mathematics, a **corestriction**[^o2] of a function is a notion analogous to the notion of a [restriction](wikipedia://en/1589032) of a function. The duality prefix co- here denotes that while the restriction changes the [domain](wikipedia://en/50263) to a [subset](wikipedia://en/27631), the corestriction changes the [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264) to a subset. However, the notions are not [categorically dual](wikipedia://en/310885).

:::

Given any subset $S\subset A,$ we can consider the corresponding [inclusion](wikipedia://en/492516) of sets $i_S:S\hookrightarrow A$ as a function. Then for any function $f:A\to B$, the [restriction](wikipedia://en/1589032) $f|_S:S\to B$ of a function $f$ onto $S$ can be defined as the [composition](wikipedia://en/195947) $f|_S = f\circ i_S$.

Analogously, for an inclusion $i_T:T\hookrightarrow B$ the corestriction $f|^T:A\to T$ of $f$ onto $T$ is the unique function $f|^T$ such that there is a [decomposition](wikipedia://en/11691) $f = i_T\circ f|^T$. The corestriction exists if and only if $T$ contains the [image](wikipedia://en/579311) of $f$. In particular, the corestriction onto the image always exists and it is sometimes simply called the corestriction of $f$. More generally, one can consider corestriction of a morphism in general [categories](wikipedia://en/5869) with images.[^o3] The term is well known in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), while rarely used in print.[^o4]

Andreotti[^o5] introduces the above notion under the name coastriction, while the name corestriction reserves to the notion categorically dual to the notion of a restriction. Namely, if $p^U:B\to U$ is a [surjection](wikipedia://en/27873) of sets (that is a [quotient map](wikipedia://en/237213)) then Andreotti considers the composition $p^U\circ f:A\to U$, which surely always exists.
