# Alexandrov theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandrov theorem"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), the **Alexandrov theorem**, named after [Aleksandr Danilovich Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov), states that if $U$ is an [open subset](wikipedia://en/39358) of [$\mathbb{R}^n$](wikipedia://en/9697) and $f\colon U\to \mathbb{R}^m$ is a [convex function](wikipedia://en/245568), then $f$ has a second derivative almost everywhere.

:::

In this context, having a second derivative at a point means having a second-order [Taylor expansion](wikipedia://en/30448) at that point with a local error smaller than any quadratic.

The result is closely related to [Rademacher's theorem](wikipedia://en/7764195).
