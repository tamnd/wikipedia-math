# Star number

*Image: Chinese checkers start.svg.* The Chinese checkers board has 121 holes.

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Star number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **star number** is a [centered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centered_number) [figurate number](wikipedia://en/325260), a centered [hexagram](wikipedia://en/302441) (six-pointed star), such as the [Star of David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_of_David), or the board [Chinese checkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_checkers) is played on. The numbers are also called **centered dodecagonal numbers** because star numbers are [centered polygonal numbers](wikipedia://en/860507) with a [twelve-sided shape](wikipedia://en/508486).

The *n*th star number is given by the formula *S<sub>n</sub>* = 6*n*(*n* − 1) + 1. The first 45 star numbers are 1, 13, 37, 73, 121, 181, 253, 337, 433, 541, 661, 793, 937, 1093, 1261, 1441, 1633, 1837, 2053, 2281, 2521, 2773, 3037, 3313, 3601, 3901, 4213, 4537, 4873, 5221, 5581, 5953, 6337, 6733, 7141, 7561, 7993, 8437, 8893, 9361, 9841, 10333, 10837, 11353, and 11881.

The [digital root](wikipedia://en/8286632) of a star number is always 1 or 4, and progresses in the sequence 1, 4, 1. The last two digits of a star number in base 10 are always 01, 13, 21, 33, 37, 41, 53, 61, 73, 81, or 93.

Unique among the star numbers is 35113, since its prime factors (i.e., 13, 37 and 73) are also consecutive star numbers.

## Relationships to other kinds of numbers {#Relationships_to_other_kinds_of_numbers}

*Image: Star\_number\_visual\_proof.svg.* [Proof without words](wikipedia://en/18045517) that the *n*-th star number is 12 times the (*n*−1)-th triangular number, plus one

Geometrically, the *n*th star number is made up of a central point and 12 copies of the (*n*−1)th [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915) — making it numerically equal to the *n*th [centered dodecagonal number](wikipedia://en/860507), but differently arranged. As such, the formula the *n*th star number can be written as *S<sub>n</sub>* = 1 + 12*T<sub>n−1</sub>* where *T<sub>n</sub>* = $\tfrac{n\,(n + 1)}{2}$.

Infinitely many star numbers are also [triangular numbers](wikipedia://en/213915), the first four being *S*<sub>1</sub> = 1 = *T*<sub>1</sub>, *S*<sub>7</sub> = 253 = *T*<sub>22</sub>, *S*<sub>91</sub> = 49141 = *T*<sub>313</sub>, and *S*<sub>1261</sub> = 9533161 = *T*<sub>4366</sub> .

Infinitely many star numbers are also [square numbers](wikipedia://en/213919), the first four being *S*<sub>1</sub> = 1<sup>2</sup>, *S*<sub>5</sub> = 121 = 11<sup>2</sup>, *S*<sub>45</sub> = 11881 = 109<sup>2</sup>, and *S*<sub>441</sub> = 1164241 = 1079<sup>2</sup> , for square stars .

A **star prime** is a star number that is [prime](wikipedia://en/23666). The first few star primes are 13, 37, 73, 181, 337, 433, 541, 661, 937.

A **superstar prime** is a star prime whose prime index is also a star number. The first two such numbers are 661 and 1750255921.

A **reverse superstar prime** is a star number whose index is a star prime. The first few such numbers are 937, 7993, 31537, 195481, 679393, 1122337, 1752841, 2617561, 5262193.

The term "star number" or "stellate number" is occasionally used to refer to [octagonal numbers](wikipedia://en/538132).

## Other properties {#Other_properties}

The [harmonic series](wikipedia://en/142488) of [unit fractions](wikipedia://en/491316) with the star numbers as denominators is:

$$\begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}& \frac{1}{S_n}\\ &=1+\frac{1}{13}+\frac{1}{37}+\frac{1}{73}+\frac{1}{121}+\frac{1}{181}+\frac{1}{253}+\frac{1}{337}+\cdots\\ &=\frac\pi{2\sqrt3}\tan (\frac \pi {2\sqrt3})\\ &\approx 1.159173.\\ \end{aligned}$$

The [alternating series](wikipedia://en/494308) of [unit fractions](wikipedia://en/491316) with the star numbers as denominators is:

$$\begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}& (-1)^{n-1}\frac{1}{S_n}\\ &=1-\frac{1}{13}+\frac{1}{37}-\frac{1}{73}+\frac{1}{121}-\frac{1}{181}+\frac{1}{253}-\frac{1}{337}+\cdots\\ &\approx 0.941419.\\ \end{aligned}$$
