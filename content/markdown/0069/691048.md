# Landon Curt Noll

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Landon Curt Noll"}

**Landon Curt Noll** (born October 28, 1960)[^o3][^o4] is an American [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), co-discoverer of the 25th [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908) and discoverer of the 26th,[^o5] which he found while still enrolled at [Hayward High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayward_High_School_%28California%29) and concurrently at [California State University, Hayward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_East_Bay).[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Noll was born in [Walnut Creek, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Creek,_California), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States). At age 18, he became the youngest person to break the record for the [largest known prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Largest_known_prime). He has held or co-held the record three times.[^o8] He is also the co-inventor (with [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807)) of a system for naming arbitrarily large powers of 10.[^o9][^o10] He also helped start the [International Obfuscated C Code Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Obfuscated_C_Code_Contest),[^o11] and is a co-inventor of the [Fowler Noll Vo hash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fowler_Noll_Vo_hash) function.[^o12]

He was also a member of the [Amdahl Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdahl_Six)[^o13] team (John S. Brown, Bodo Parady, Curt Landon Noll, Gene W. Smith, Joel F. Smith, and Sergio E. Zarantonello) which discovered another record prime in 1989; this prime remains unusual as a record large prime as it was not a Mersenne prime.[^o14][^o15]

Noll is an amateur astronomer.[^o16][^o17] His work includes measuring the [Solar parallax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_parallax) during the 2004 [Transit of Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transit_of_Venus)[^o18] as well as the search for [Vulcanoid asteroids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulcanoid_asteroid).[^o19]

He was also involved in politics as a [Sunnyvale, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunnyvale,_California) city council member and vice-mayor.[^o20]
