# The Mathematical Diary

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Mathematical Diary"}

***The Mathematical Diary*** was an early [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) and mathematics magazine, published between 1825 and 1833.

:::

The *Mathematical Diary* was founded by [Robert Adrain](wikipedia://en/467339) at Columbia College (now [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University)) after two unsuccessful attempts, in 1808 and 1814, to start a more purely academic mathematics journal, *[The Analyst, or, Mathematical Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Analyst,_or,_Mathematical_Museum)*. The *Mathematical Diary* contained, in addition to some serious mathematics, articles of general interest such as [mathematical puzzles](wikipedia://en/366445) aimed at the amateur problem-solver, which may have helped it attract more laypeople as subscribers and contributed to its greater longevity. Adrain edited the first four issues; after he left Columbia in 1826 for [Rutgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), James Ryan, previously the publisher, took over the editorship. A total of thirteen issues were published.
