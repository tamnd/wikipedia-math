# Ana Bela Cruzeiro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ana Bela Cruzeiro"}

**Ana Bela Cruzeiro** (born 1957) is a Portuguese and Swiss mathematician whose research concerns [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) and the [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875), as well as the [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) used to model [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Superior_Técnico), part of the [University of Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lisbon).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After earning a master's degree in mathematics at the University of Lisbon in 1980, Cruzeiro studied mathematics at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris, earning a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in 1982, a [doctorat de troisième cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_de_troisième_cycle) in 1983, and a [doctorat d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'état) in 1985. Her 1985 doctoral dissertation, *Equations différentielles ordinaires et équations différentielles stochastiques sur l'espace de Wiener*, was supervised by [Paul Malliavin](wikipedia://en/10459388). She also earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) (agregação) at the University of Lisbon in 1991.

She has been on the faculty of the University of Lisbon since 1986, and became a full professor in Instituto Superior Técnico in 2002.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Cruzeiro is a corresponding member of the [Lisbon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon_Academy_of_Sciences), elected in 2020.
