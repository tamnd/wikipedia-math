# Adélia Sequeira

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adélia Sequeira"}

**Adélia da Costa Sequeira** is a Portuguese [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) specializing in the [mathematical modeling](wikipedia://en/20590) of [blood flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_flow) and the [circulatory system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circulatory_system). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Superior_Técnico), part of the [University of Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lisbon), where she is coordinator for the Scientific Area on Numerical Analysis and Applied Analysis and director of the Research Center for Computational and Stochastic Mathematics.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Sequeira earned a [doctorat de troisième cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_de_troisième_cycle) in France in 1981, at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). Her dissertation, *Couplage entre la méthode des éléments finis et la méthode des équations integrales: application au problème de Stokes stationnaire dans le plan*, was supervised by Jean-Claude Nédélec. She has a second doctorate in mathematics, earned in 1985 at the University of Lisbon, where she also earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2001.

## Books {#Books}

Sequeira is a coauthor of the book *Hemomath: the Mathematics of Blood* (Springer, 2017), and is the editor of several [edited volumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edited_volume).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is a corresponding member of the [Lisbon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon_Academy_of_Sciences), elected in 2018.
