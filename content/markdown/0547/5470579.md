# Jan Mikusiński

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Mikusiński"}

**Jan Mikusiński** (April 3, 1913 – July 27, 1987) was a Polish mathematician based at the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław) known for his pioneering work in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

:::

Mikusiński was born in [Stanisławów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivano-Frankivsk) in 1913 and developed an [operational calculus](wikipedia://en/8950551), known as the *Calculus of Mikusiński* ([MSC](wikipedia://en/3008091) 44A40), which is relevant for solving [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations). His operational calculus is based upon an [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) of the convolution of functions with respect to the [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247). From the convolution product he goes on to define what in other contexts is called the [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) or a quotient field. These ordered pairs of functions Mikusiński calls "operators", the "Mikusiński operators".

He is also well known for [Mikusinski's cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikusinski's_cube), the [Antosik–Mikusinski theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antosik–Mikusinski_theorem), and [Mikusinski convolution algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikusinski_convolution_algebra).

Mikusiński died in [Katowice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katowice) in 1987. A street in Katowice is named after him.

- *An Introduction to Analysis - From Number to Integral.* Wiley 1993
- *The Operational Calculus.* Pergamon Press, Oxford 1983
- *Operatorenrechnung.* VEB Deutscher Verlag der Wissenschaften, Berlin 1957.
- *The Bochner Integral.* Birkhäuser 1978.
- with Piotr Antosik, [Roman Sikorski](wikipedia://en/1983335): *Theory of distributions – the sequential approach.* Elsevier 1973.
- with Stanisław Hartman: *The theory of Lebesgue Measure and Integration.* Pergamon Press, Oxford 1961.
