# Henna Koivusalo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henna Koivusalo"}

**Henna L. L. Koivusalo** (born 1985) is a Finnish mathematician who works in the UK as a lecturer at the School of Mathematics of the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol). Her research involves [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_geometry), [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558), and the cut-and-project method for generating [aperiodic tilings](wikipedia://en/868145) and [quasicrystals](wikipedia://en/25350).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Koivusalo received bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics from the [University of Jyväskylä](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jyväskylä) in 2009. She went on to doctoral study in mathematics at the [University of Oulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oulu), and completed her Ph.D. in 2013. Her doctoral dissertation, *Dimensions of random fractals*, was jointly supervised by Esa and [Maarit Järvenpää](wikipedia://en/80396856).

She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York) from 2013 to 2016, and from 2016 to 2020 worked towards a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). She has been a lecturer at the University of Bristol since 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Koivusalo is the 2025 recipient of the [Anne Bennett Prize](wikipedia://en/61347778) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), awarded "for her work on cut-and-project sets, dynamical systems and fractals and her dedication to the advancement of women in mathematics".
