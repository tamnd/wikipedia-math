# Fusiform

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fusiform"}

*Image: Spindle with cotton yarn.jpg.* [Spindle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spindle_%28textiles%29) with yarn

*Image: Lemon (geometry).png.* A *[lemon](wikipedia://en/62247933)* in geometry

**Fusiform** (from Latin *fusus* ‘spindle’) means having a [spindle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spindle_%28textiles%29)-like shape that is wide in the middle and tapers at both ends. It is similar to the [lemon-shape](wikipedia://en/62247933), but often implies a focal broadening of a structure that continues from one or both ends, such as an [aneurysm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aneurysm) on a [blood vessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_vessel).

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- Fusiform, a body shape common to many [aquatic animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquatic_animal), characterized by being tapered at both the head and the tail[^o4]
- Fusiform, a classification of [aneurysm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aneurysm)
- Fusiform bacteria (spindled rods, that is, fusiform bacilli), such as the [Fusobacteriota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusobacteriota)
- Fusiform [cell (biology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28biology%29)
- [Fusiform face area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusiform_face_area), a part of the human visual system which seems to specialize in facial recognition
- [Fusiform gyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusiform_gyrus), part of the temporal lobe of the brain
- [Fusiform muscle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusiform_muscle), where the fibres run parallel along the length of the muscle
- Fusiform neuron, a spindle-shaped [neuron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuron)

:::
