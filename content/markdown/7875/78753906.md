# International Conference on Fibonacci Numbers and their Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Conference on Fibonacci Numbers and their Applications"}

The **International Conference on Fibonacci Numbers and their Applications** (ICFNTA) is a five-day biennial conference of the Fibonacci Association.[^o2][^o3][^o4] Typically, 50 to 100 mathematicians from around the world participate in the event, which takes place at an American university every four years, and alternately at a university outside the United States; see the **History** section below. Most participants are academics whose research is in number theory or combinatorics.[^o2] Central to the Fibonacci Association and the ICFNTA conferences is *The [Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*.[^o5][^o4]

:::

## Program {#Program}

The primary component of the ICFNTA is a series of 20-minute research presentations, most of which are subsequently published in conference *Proceedings*.[^o7][^o8][^o9] Several events are, on one of the days of the conference, open to the public.[^o2][^o3] These include an hour-long lecture by an invited speaker.[^o10] Other activities include an opening session, social events, city tours, excursions to area attractions, a problem session, a formal banquet, and a closing session.[^o2][^o3]

## History {#History}

section In 1963, [Verner Emil Hoggatt Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verner_Emil_Hoggatt_Jr.) and Brother [Alfred Brousseau](wikipedia://en/11633991), along with Ivan Dale Ruggles, founded the [Fibonacci Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_Association), which, also in 1963, began publishing *The [Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*, a journal that specializes in the [Fibonacci sequence](wikipedia://en/10918)[^o12][^o13] and its connections to many mathematical and scientific subjects.[^o14][^o4]

The first ICFNTA took place in 1984 in Patras, Greece, under the leadership of Andreas Philippou,[^o15][^o16] who was Chairman of the Local and International Committees of the Conference and Vice Rector of the [University of Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Patras). Among the organizers[^o17] were Gerald E. Bergum,[^o18] editor of the *Fibonacci Quarterly* and co-chairman of the International Committee; Marjorie Bicknell-Johnson, Secretary of the Fibonacci Association, and Alwyn Francis Horadam.[^o19][^o20] [Herta Freitag](wikipedia://en/6095407) and Karel de Bouvère[^o21] both participated in the Conference, and each wrote a report.[^o22] Subsequent ICFNTA conferences are described below. Formal group pictures from most of these conferences have been collected and made available online: [Fibonacci Number-Theorists](https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/bstud/fnt.html).

### 21st ICFNTA {#21st_ICFNTA}

The 21st ICFNTA took place at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College), [Claremont, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California), July 8–12, 2024. This conference marked a turning point in the history of the Fibonacci Association, as the publisher of *The Fibonacci Quarterly* would become, beginning in 2025, [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis).[^o24] The Lucas Memorial Lecture was given by [Jennifer Quinn](wikipedia://en/33815350), "Lessons that Really Count: The Fibonacci Edition".[^o2]

### 20th ICFNTA {#20th_ICFNTA}

The 20th ICFNTA was held at the [University of Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sarajevo), July 25–29, 2022. The 403-page *Proceedings*[^o26] show the conference logo, names of editors, a conference report, and a list of speakers and talks, followed by full texts, with abstracts, of 25 papers. [Florian Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015) gave the plenary talk on "p-adic orders of Tribonacci numbers."[^o8]

### 19th ICFNTA {#19th_ICFNTA}

Initially planned for the University of Sarajevo, the 19th ICFNTA was instead conducted online, July 21–23, during the covid pandemic. The 236-page *Proceedings*[^o28] gives full texts with abstracts for 23 papers. The plenary speakers were [Florian Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015) and [Katherine Stange](https://math.katestange.net/).

### 18th ICFNTA {#18th_ICFNTA}

The 18th ICFNTA took place at Dalhousie University, [Halifax, Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_Nova_Scotia), Canada, July 1–7, 2018. The *Proceedings*,[^o30] consisting of 183 pages, including 14 papers and 12 problems, were published for open access in [*The Fibonacci Quarterly*](https://www.fq.math.ca/57-5.html). Hugh C. Williams from the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) presented the Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecture, "Mersenne, Fibonacci and Lucas: The [Mersenne Prime](wikipedia://en/18908) Story and Beyond."[^o30]

### 17th ICFNTA {#17th_ICFNTA}

The venue for the 17th ICFNTA was Université de Caen-Normandie, Caen, France, June 26 to July 2, 2016; *Proceedings*.[^o32] The Édouard Lucas Memorial Talk, given by Jean-Paul Allouche,[^o33] was entitled "Variations on the Binary Fibonacci Sequence."[^o32] Three special presentations open to the public were given by Andreas Hinz, Ron Knott, and Arthur Benjamin, and there were two one-hour problem sessions.[^o32]

### 16th ICFNTA {#16th_ICFNTA}

The 16th ICFNTA was held at [Rochester Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester_Institute_of_Technology), Rochester, New York, July 20–27, 2014. The *Proceedings*[^o35] of 221 pages includes 21 papers and a chapter consisting of 14 problems. The Édouard Lucas Memorial lecture was given by [Jeffrey Lagarias](wikipedia://en/4327781).[^o35]

### 15th ICFNTA {#15th_ICFNTA}

The 15th ICFNTA took place at the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics Eszerházy Károly College, Eger, Hungary, June 25–30, 2012. The *Proceedings*[^o37] has 274 pages, covering 21 research papers and a chapter consisting of 14 problems. [Neil Sloane](wikipedia://en/400062), founder of the Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences,[^o38] gave the Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecture titled "The [On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](wikipedia://en/500004)".[^o39]

### 14th ICFNTA {#14th_ICFNTA}

The 14th ICFNTA convened at Instituto de Matemáticas de la UNAM in Morelia, Michoacan, Mexico, July 5–9, 2010. The *Proceedings*[^o41] has 283 pages, including 22 papers and a chapter of 7 problems. The Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecture on "Combinatorial Trigonometry (and a method to DIE for)" was given by [Art Benjamin](wikipedia://en/12428320).[^o42]

### 13th ICFNTA {#13th_ICFNTA}

The 13th ICFNTA met at the University of Patras in Patras, Greece, July 7–11, 2008. (The 1st ICFNTA also met in Patras.) The *Proceedings*, initially published as volume 201 of *Congressus Numerantium* in 2010, are available online: [Combinatorial Press](https://combinatorialpress.com/cn/vol201/). The *Proceedings* consist of 384 pages, 28 papers, and 12 problem proposals. The Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecturer, [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159), spoke on "Primality testing - variations on a theme of Lucas."

### 11th ICFNTA {#11th_ICFNTA}

The 11th ICFNTA was held at Technical University Carolo-Wilhelmina, Braunschweig, Germany, July 5–9, 2004. The *Proceedings*, initially published as volume 194 of *Congressus Numerantium* in 2009, are available online: [Combinatorial Press](https://combinatorialpress.com/cn/vol194/).

### 10th ICFNTA {#10th_ICFNTA}

The 10th ICFNTA met at [Northern Arizona University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Arizona_University), June 24–28, 2002. The *Proceedings*.,[^o47] edited by Frederic T. Howard, were published in 2004 by Springer Netherlands. The 311 pages include 28 papers and a subject index. A copy is available online: [10th ICFNTA Proceedings](https://archive.org/details/springer_10.1007-978-0-306-48517-6).

### 9th ICFNTA {#9th_ICFNTA}

The 9th ICFNTA took place in Luxembourg City, Luxembourg, August 13–16, 1986; although no *Proceedings* were published, there is a collection of Photographic Memories.[^o49]

### 8th ICFNTA {#8th_ICFNTA}

The 8th ICFNTA took place at the Rochester Institute of Technology, Rochester, New York, June 22–26, 1998. The *Proceedings* include pages i-xxiii and 1–384, with a total of 33 papers. There is a collection of Photographic Memories.[^o51]

### 7th ICFNTA {#7th_ICFNTA}

They are available online: [Springer Nature Link](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-011-5020-0/).

### 6th ICFNTA {#6th_ICFNTA}

The 6th ICFNTA was held at Washington State University, Spokane, Washington, July 18–22, 1994. The *Proceedings*, including pages i-xxviii and 1–551, with a total of 43 papers from the 55 presented, were published in 1996. They are available online: [Springer Nature Link](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-009-0223-7/).

### 5th ICFNTA {#5th_ICFNTA}

They are available online: [Springer Nature Link](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-011-2058-6/).

The 3rd ICFNTA met at the University of Pisa in Pisa, Italy, July 25–29, 1988. ## The *Proceedings* {#The_Proceedings}

The title of the *Proceedings* of the 1st ICFNTA is *Fibonacci Numbers and Their Applications*, but title for the 2nd through 8th and 10th *Proceedings* is slightly different: *Applications of Fibonacci Numbers.* These nine books are hard-cover publications by Kluwer Academic Publishers,[^o61] formerly headquartered in Dordrecht, The Netherlands. Kluwer Academic Publishers was merged with Springer Science and Business media in 2004. The *Proceedings* for conferences 1-7 were all edited by G. E. Bergum, A. N. Philippou, and A. F. Horadam, and for conferences 8 and 10, by Frederic T. Howard, president of the Fibonacci Association, 2000–2005. Of all the ICFNTA conferences, there is only one for which *Proceedings* were not published, and that was the 9th, hence the fact that Kluwer's volume 9 is for the 10th conference. There does exist, however, a one-page report on the 9th conference,[^o62] written by George M. Phillips.

The *Proceedings* for conferences 11, 12, and 13 were published as volumes of *Congressus Numerantium*.[^o63] The 11th and 12th conference *Proceedings* were edited by William Webb,[^o64] president of the Fibonacci Association, 2006–2018. The editors for the 13th were [Florian Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015) and [Pante Stanica](https://faculty.nps.edu/pstanica/VitaeStanica.pdf).

The *Proceedings* for the 14th conference were edited by [Florian Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015) and Pante Stanica,[^o65] and published in *Aportaciones Matemáticas*, Invertigación 20, Sociedad Matemática Mexicana, 2011.

The *Proceedings* for the 15th conference were edited by Kálmán Liptai, Ferenc Mátyás, and Tibor Juhász, and published in *Annales Mathematicae et Informaticae*, vol. 41, Institute of Mathematics and Informatics, Eszerházy Károly College, Eger, Hungary.

## Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecture {#Édouard_Lucas_Memorial_Lecture}

The first Édouard Lucas Memorial Lecture was given at the 13th ICFNTA. The Lecture is named in honor of [Édouard Lucas](wikipedia://en/150102), for whom the [Lucas sequence](wikipedia://en/734635) is named.[^o68]
