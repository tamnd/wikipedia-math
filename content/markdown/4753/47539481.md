# Unemployment in Scotland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Unemployment in Scotland"}

**Unemployment in Scotland** measured by the [Office for National Statistics](wikipedia://en/324029) show unemployment in Scotland at 155,000 (5.6%) as of August 2015.[^o2]

:::
