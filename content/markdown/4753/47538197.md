# Mina Aganagić

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mina Aganagić"}

**Mina Aganagić** is a [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) who works as a professor at the [Center for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley_Center_for_Theoretical_Physics), the Department of Mathematics, and the Department of Physics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Aganagić was raised in [Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarajevo), [Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia).[^o7] She has a bachelor's degree and a doctorate from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), in 1995 and 1999 respectively; her PhD advisor was [John Henry Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Henry_Schwarz). She was a [postdoctoral fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) physics department from 1999 to 2003. She then joined the physics faculty at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), where she became a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship)[^o7] and a [DOE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Energy) Outstanding Junior Investigator.[^o8] She moved to UC Berkeley in 2004. In 2016 the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) gave her a Simons Investigator Award[^o9] and the same year [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society) had awarded her with its fellowship.[^o10][^o11]
