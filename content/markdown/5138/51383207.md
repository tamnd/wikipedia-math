# Verschiebung operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Verschiebung operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Verschiebung** or **Verschiebung operator** *V* is a [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) between affine commutative [group schemes](wikipedia://en/510523) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of nonzero [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) *p*. For finite group schemes it is the [Cartier dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartier_dual) of the [Frobenius homomorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_homomorphism). It was introduced by (Witt 1937) as the shift operator on [Witt vectors](wikipedia://en/5573710) taking (*a*<sub>0</sub>, *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>, ...) to (0, *a*<sub>0</sub>, *a*<sub>1</sub>, ...). ("Verschiebung" is German for "shift", but the term "Verschiebung" is often used for this operator even in other languages.)

:::

The Verschiebung operator *V* and the Frobenius operator *F* are related by *FV* = *VF* = \[*p*\], where \[*p*\] is the *p*th power homomorphism of an abelian group scheme.

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- If *G* is the discrete group with *n* elements over the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) *F*<sub>*p*</sub> of order *p*, then the Frobenius homomorphism *F* is the identity homomorphism and the Verschiebung *V* is the homomorphism \[*p*\] (multiplication by *p* in the group). Its dual is the group scheme of *n*th [roots of unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roots_of_unity), whose Frobenius homomorphism is \[*p*\] and whose Verschiebung is the identity homomorphism.
- For Witt vectors, the Verschiebung takes (*a*<sub>0</sub>, *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>, ...) to (0, *a*<sub>0</sub>, *a*<sub>1</sub>, ...).
- On the [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) of symmetric functions, the Verschiebung *V*<sub>*n*</sub> is the algebra [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) that takes the complete symmetric function *h*<sub>*r*</sub> to *h*<sub>*r*/*n*</sub> if *n* divides *r* and to 0 otherwise.

:::
