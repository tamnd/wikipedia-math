# Infrabarrelled space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infrabarrelled space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a discipline within mathematics, a [locally convex](wikipedia://en/518844) [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) is said to be **infrabarrelled** (also spelled **infrabarreled**) if every [bounded](wikipedia://en/1789812) [barrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barreled_space) is a neighborhood of the origin.[^n1]

:::

Similarly, **quasibarrelled spaces** are [topological vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_vector_spaces) (TVS) for which every [bornivorous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bornivorous) barrelled set in the space is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin. Quasibarrelled spaces are studied because they are a weakening of the defining condition of [barrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/1733592), for which a form of the [Banach–Steinhaus theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach–Steinhaus_theorem) holds.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A subset $B$ of a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) $X$ is called **bornivorous** if it absorbs all bounded subsets of $X$; that is, if for each bounded subset $S$ of $X,$ there exists some scalar $r$ such that $S \subseteq r B.$ A **barrelled set** or a **barrel** in a TVS is a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) which is [convex](wikipedia://en/6292), [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238), [absorbing](wikipedia://en/1728179) and [closed](wikipedia://en/47279). A **quasibarrelled space** is a TVS for which every bornivorous barrelled set in the space is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin.[^n2][^n3]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Characterizations"}

## Characterizations {#Characterizations}

If $X$ is a Hausdorff locally convex space then [the canonical injection](wikipedia://en/22124711) from $X$ into its bidual is a topological embedding if and only if $X$ is infrabarrelled.[^n4]

A Hausdorff topological vector space $X$ is **quasibarrelled** if and only if every bounded closed linear operator from $X$ into a [complete metrizable TVS](wikipedia://en/200440) is continuous.[^n5] By definition, a linear $F : X \to Y$ operator is called **closed** if its graph is a closed subset of $X \times Y.$

For a [locally convex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space) $X$ with continuous dual $X^{\prime}$ the following are equivalent:

1. $X$ is quasibarrelled.
1. Every bounded lower semi-continuous semi-norm on $X$ is continuous.
1. Every $\beta(X', X)$-bounded subset of the continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is equicontinuous.

If $X$ is a metrizable locally convex TVS then the following are equivalent:

1. The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of $X$ is quasibarrelled.
1. The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of $X$ is barrelled.
1. The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of $X$ is [bornological](wikipedia://en/1804457).

:::

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every [quasi-complete](wikipedia://en/63948433) infrabarrelled space is barrelled.[^n1]

A locally convex Hausdorff quasibarrelled space that is [sequentially complete](wikipedia://en/64126151) is barrelled.[^n6]

A locally convex Hausdorff quasibarrelled space is a [Mackey space](wikipedia://en/1804899), [quasi-M-barrelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-M-barrelled), and countably quasibarrelled.[^n7]

A locally convex quasibarrelled space that is also a [σ-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space) is necessarily a [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592).[^n3]

A locally convex space is [reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091) if and only if it is [semireflexive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semireflexive_space) and quasibarrelled.[^n3]

:::

::: {#o19 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592) is infrabarrelled.[^n1] A closed vector subspace of an infrabarrelled space is, however, not necessarily infrabarrelled.[^n8]

Every product and locally convex direct sum of any family of infrabarrelled spaces is infrabarrelled.[^n8] Every [separated](wikipedia://en/13637) quotient of an infrabarrelled space is infrabarrelled.[^n8]

Every Hausdorff [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592) and every Hausdorff [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457) is quasibarrelled.[^n9] Thus, every [metrizable TVS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable_TVS) is quasibarrelled.

Note that there exist quasibarrelled spaces that are neither barrelled nor bornological.[^n3] There exist [Mackey spaces](wikipedia://en/1804899) that are not quasibarrelled.[^n3] There exist [distinguished spaces](wikipedia://en/64266856), [DF-spaces](wikipedia://en/63703274), and $\sigma$-barrelled spaces that are not quasibarrelled.[^n3]

The [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) $X_b^{\prime}$ of a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) $X$ is [distinguished](wikipedia://en/64266856) if and only if $X$ is quasibarrelled.[^o28]

### Counter-examples {#Counter-examples}

There exists a [DF-space](wikipedia://en/63703274) that is not quasibarrelled.[^n3]

There exists a quasibarrelled [DF-space](wikipedia://en/63703274) that is not [bornological](wikipedia://en/1804457).[^n3]

There exists a quasibarrelled space that is not a [σ-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space).[^n3]

:::
