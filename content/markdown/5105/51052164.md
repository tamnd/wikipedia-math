# Kate Calder

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kate Calder"}

**Catherine A.** "**Kate**" **Calder** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who works as chair of Statistics and Data Sciences at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). She was previously a professor of statistics at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). Calder earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 1999, and completed her Ph.D. in statistics from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 2003[^o2] under the joint supervision of David Higdon and Michael L. Lavine. She joined the Ohio State faculty in 2003, and was promoted to full professor in 2015.[^o2]

:::

In 2013 she won the Young Investigator Award of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA),[^o4] and in 2014 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the ASA "for outstanding contributions to the development of Bayesian statistical methodology for spatial and spatiotemporal data; for significant multidisciplinary collaborations; for excellence in teaching and mentoring graduate students both in statistics and in other disciplines; and for service to the profession."[^o5] She was elected to the 2022 class of Fellows of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (AAAS).[^o6] In 2025 Calder was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o7]
