# European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology"}

The **European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology** (**ESMTB**) is a non-profit academic society designed to bring together mathematicians and biologists interested in the use of mathematical and theoretical techniques for the solution of biological and medical problems. It was founded in 1991 during the holding of its first conference in [Alpe d'Huez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpe_d'Huez), France. It is a member society of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) (EMS) and the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227) (ICIAM). The society's official journal is the *[Journal of Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematical_Biology)*.

:::

## History {#History}

### Origins {#Origins}

The European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology was founded in 1991 at its first conference, entitled *European Conference on Mathematics Applied to Biology and Medicine*, held in Alpe d'Huez.[^o5] The mention of medicine in the title of this conference was relevant because much of the applied mathematics researched by members has related to medical topics. The founding President, [James Murray](wikipedia://en/30862391) developed models of wound healing[^o6] as well as applying mathematics to many other areas of medicine and biology.[^o7] Andrea De Gaetano is a qualified physician and has published many papers on applied mathematics in medicine.[^o23] Ellen Baake has worked on mathematical immunobiology.[^o8]

## Activities {#Activities}

### Aims {#Aims}

The aims of the Society are to promote the application of mathematical and theoretical solutions to biological problems through organisation of meetings, support of publications, and benefits to members. The aims of the Society also involve collaborations with other organisations in the same and related fields.[^o27]

### Affiliations and Partnerships {#Affiliations_and_Partnerships}

The academic credibility of the European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology has been established through its affiliations and partnerships. The ESMTB is an Associate Member of the European Mathematical Society.[^o43] It is an affiliated Member of the International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics.[^o44] The ESMTB has partnerships with the [Society for Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Biology), the Japanese Society for Mathematical Biology,[^o45] the NVTB (Dutch Society for Theoretical Biology)[^o46] and the SFTB (Société Francophone de Biologie Théorique).[^o47] Existing members of these organisations get discounts when applying for membership.[^o48] The partnership with the Society for Mathematical Biology is particularly relevant, as the Society for Mathematical Biology states on its website: "The Society has agreed to hold a joint annual meeting with the European Society for Mathematical & Theoretical Biology every four years".[^o49] The 2026 European Conference on Mathematical & Theoretical Biology was a joint event of ESMTB and the Society for Mathematical Biology.[^o50]

### Publications {#Publications}

The [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) *[Journal of Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematical_Biology)* is the official journal of the Society.[^o52] The ESMTB also publishes the *European Communications in Mathematical and Theoretical Biology* annually to its members.[^o53] Members of the Society also receive the monthly *ESMTB Newsletter*.[^o54] Under the title *Perspectives in Mathematical Biology* the ESMTB also invited short contributions on mathematical and theoretical biology that were published in the *Journal of Mathematical Biology* between 2006[^o5] and 2020.[^o55] Since 2020 these contributions have been discontinued.[^o56]

### Meetings {#Meetings}

The ESMTB holds biennial conferences.[^o58] It also supports online colloqia,[^o59] and summer schools (in conjunction with the European Mathematical Society).[^o60]

### Prizes {#Prizes}

#### Reinardt Heinrich Award {#Reinardt_Heinrich_Award}

The Society awards an annual [Reinhardt Heinrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinhardt_Heinrich) Doctoral Thesis Award in memory of Reinhardt Heinrich,[^o63] to recognise outstanding doctoral thesis research, and to commemorate the role of Heinrich in mentoring young scientists.[^o64] Recent recipients of the award are shown in the table below.

#### Karl-Peter Hadeler Prize {#Karl-Peter_Hadeler_Prize}

The Society's official journal, the Journal of Mathematical Biology, awards the Karl-Peter Hadeler Prize to recognise outstanding publications in that journal.[^o71] Karl-Peter Hadeler was both a founder of the Journal of Mathematical Biology and of the European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology.[^o71]

#### Ovide Arino Award {#Ovide_Arino_Award}

In collaboration with the Société Francophone de Biologie Théorique (SFBT) the ESMTB awards a prize to a researcher from a "Southern country"[^o73] where "Southern" may be read as equivalent to [developing country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developing_country) although in fact there is a specific list of eligible countries.[^o74] The successful candidate must have been carrying out research in an eligible country at the time of application, in collaboration with a European or other French-speaking country. The award commemorates the French mathematician Ovide Arino.[^o75]
