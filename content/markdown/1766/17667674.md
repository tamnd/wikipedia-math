# Encrypting PIN pad

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Encrypting PIN pad"}

*Image: ATM pinpad in german.jpg.* An ATM encrypting PIN pad (EPP) with German markings

An **encrypting PIN pad** is an apparatus for [encrypting](wikipedia://en/10294) an identifier such as a PIN entered on a keypad. These are used in [automated teller machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_teller_machine) to ensure that the unencrypted PIN is not stored or transmitted anywhere in the rest of the system and thus cannot be revealed accidentally or through manipulations of the system.

:::

## Apparatus {#Apparatus}

The apparatus includes a [keypad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keypad), an encrypting circuit that is adjacent the pad, and a link coupling the pad and the encrypting circuit. The pad is for entering an identifier, and the circuit for encrypting the entered identifier.[^o3] The pad may be a physical [touch pad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_pad) such as an N-wire technology touch pad. Alternatively, the pad may be a [virtual touch screen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_Touch_Screen). The encrypting circuit may be a [CPU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPU) along with a memory coupled to the CPU and programmed to encrypt. The CPU and programmed [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory) may be the first CPU programmable to encrypt the entered [identifier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identifier), through which the identifier passes. The encrypting circuit may be a micro controller programmed to encrypt. In still another variation, the encrypting circuit may be an application-specific [integrated circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_circuit) ([ASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application-specific_integrated_circuit)). The apparatus may include a housing that encloses the encrypting circuit and link. The housing would be resistant to access, tampering or [tapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapping). The housing may be at least partially of chip-on-glass technology. The encrypting circuit may be [embedded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embedded_system) in the housing, as may the link. A method for encrypting an identifier includes placing a pad for entering an identifier, a circuit for encrypting an identifier and a link communicative coupling the pad and the circuit adjacent in an access-resistant housing. An identifier is entered on the pad and communicated to the encrypting [circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_circuit). The encrypting circuit encrypts the identifier. The encrypted identifier may be forwarded for [verification](wikipedia://en/47967).
