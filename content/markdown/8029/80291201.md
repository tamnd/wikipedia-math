# Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups"}

***Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups*** is a book about [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) by [John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) and [Neil Sloane](wikipedia://en/400062), with contributions by other mathematicians, first published in 1988.

:::

[H. S. M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328) noted, "Much of the book is inspired by the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919)."[^n1] The book covers many results that, as [Richard K. Guy](wikipedia://en/816308) phrased it, "appear little short of miraculous", such as how the Leech lattice, a set of points in 24-dimensional space, is related via [special relativity](wikipedia://en/26962) to [stacking cannonballs](wikipedia://en/28045698).[^n2] The interrelated topics featured in the book include [Golay codes](wikipedia://en/344971), [Mathieu groups](wikipedia://en/380207) and [Monstrous Moonshine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monstrous_Moonshine). Many of the chapters are updates of papers that had been published in journals previously.[^n2] Portions of the text were coauthored by Eiichi Bannai, [Richard Borcherds](wikipedia://en/292169), [John Leech](wikipedia://en/4548516), [Simon Norton](wikipedia://en/1641166), [Andrew M. Odlyzko](wikipedia://en/1986042), [Richard A. Parker](wikipedia://en/4582441), Larissa Queen and B. B. Venkov.[^n1]

Francis Fung called it "monumental",[^n3] and Nick Lord described it as "epochal".[^n4] [Gian-Carlo Rota](wikipedia://en/101891) wrote:[^n5][^n6]

> This is the best survey of the best work in the best fields of combinatorics written by the best people. It will make the best reading by the best students interested in the best mathematics that is now going on.
