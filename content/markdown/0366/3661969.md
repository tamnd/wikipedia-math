# Jean-Yves Béziau

*Image: jyb-jerusalem-2016.jpg.* Jean-Yves Béziau in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem), January 2016

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Yves Béziau"}

**Jean-Yves Beziau** (; born January 15, 1965, in [Orléans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orléans), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France)) is a Swiss Professor in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) at the [University of Brazil, Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro), and Researcher of the Brazilian Research Council. He is permanent member and former president of the Brazilian Academy of Philosophy. Before going to Brazil, he was Professor of the [Swiss National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Science_Foundation) at the [University of Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Neuchâtel) in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) and researcher at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) working with [Patrick Suppes](wikipedia://en/4824664). Beziau created the World Logic Day, January 14, 2019. He then visited UNESCO in Paris and the day was recognized at unanimity the same year by the general assembly of this organization (194 countries).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Béziau works in the field of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065)—in particular, [paraconsistent logic](wikipedia://en/421085), the [square of opposition](wikipedia://en/246976) and [universal logic](wikipedia://en/3659669). He holds a Maîtrise in [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) from [Pantheon-Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_1), a [DEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies#France_and_francophone_countries) in Philosophy from Pantheon-Sorbonne University, a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Philosophy from the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo), a [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) and a PhD in Logic and Foundations of [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science) from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_7).

Béziau is the editor-in-chief of the journal *[Logica Universalis](wikipedia://en/25898954)*[^o3] and of the *South American Journal of Logic*—an online, open-access journal—as well as of the [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) book series *Studies in Universal Logic.*[^o4][^o5] He is also the editor of *College Publication's* book series *Logic PhDs*[^o6]

He has launched four major international series of events: UNILOG (World Congress and School on Universal Logic), SQUARE (World Congress on the Square of Opposition), WOCOLOR (World Congress on Logic and Religion), LIQ (Logic in Question).[^o7][^o8]

Later on Beziau launched SALOME (South American Logic Meeting), PANAFRICAL (Pan African Logic Meeting) and LoChaMo (Logic, Chance and Money).

## Criticism {#Criticism}

### Importance of his work {#Importance_of_his_work}

Béziau’s works concern, among other things, [paraconsistent logic](wikipedia://en/421085), which is a central field of logic, mainly created by his advisor Newton da Costa. Paraconsistent logic rejects the [principle of explosion](wikipedia://en/591394), which states that from a proposition and its negation, one can conclude anything. Beziau has organized the world congress on paraconsistency and is the editor of the paraconsistent newsletter.

## Controversy around an article in Synthese {#Controversy_around_an_article_in_Synthese}

In 2015, the academic journal *Synthese* published an article in which Béziau associates "logical pluralism" with homosexuality and complains about the "absurdity" and "hypocrisy" of the "politically correct" <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-014-0419-0>.

One year later, the *Synthese* published an [apology](https://dailynous.com/2016/01/27/statement-from-synthese-editors-moratorium-on-special-issues/).
