# Dov Gabbay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dov Gabbay"}

**Dov M. Gabbay** (, דב גבאי; born October 26, 1945) is an Israeli logician. He is [Augustus De Morgan](wikipedia://en/18949289) [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of [Logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) at the Group of [Logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language) and [Computation](wikipedia://en/5926), Department of [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science), [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Gabbay has authored over four hundred and fifty research papers and over thirty research monographs. He is editor of several international journals, and of many reference works and handbooks of logic, including the *Handbook of Philosophical Logic* (with [Franz Guenthner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Guenthner)), the *Handbook of Logic in Computer Science* (with [Samson Abramsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samson_Abramsky) and [T. S. E. Maibaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Maibaum)), the *Handbook of the History of Logic* (with [John Woods](wikipedia://en/31563512)), the *Handbook of Deontic Logic and Normative Systems* (with [John F. Horty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Horty), Xavier Parent, Ron van der Meyden and [Leendert van der Torre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_van_der_Torre)), and the *Handbook of Logic in Artificial Intelligence and Logic Programming* (with C.J. Hogger and [J.A. Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Alan_Robinson)).

He is well-known for pioneering work on logic in computer science and artificial intelligence, especially the application of (executable) [temporal logics](wikipedia://en/321481) in computer science, in particular [formal verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_verification), the logical foundations of [non-monotonic reasoning](wikipedia://en/341086) and artificial intelligence, the introduction of fibring logics and the theory of labelled deductive systems.

He is chairman and founder of several international conferences, executive of the European Foundation of Logic, Language and Information and President of the International IGPL Logic Group. He is founder, and joint President of the International Federation of Computational Logic.[^o5][^o6] He is also one of the four founders and council member for many years of FoLLI, the Association of Logic, Language and Information, from which he is now retired. He remains a life member.

He is co-founder with Jane Spurr of College Publications, a not-for-profit, start-up [academic publisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_publisher), intended to compete with major expensive publishers at affordable prices, and not requiring [copyright assignment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_assignment) from authors.[^o7] A two volume *[Festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift)* in his honor was published in 2005 by College Publications.[^o8]

## Dov Gabbay Prize {#Dov_Gabbay_Prize}

The *Dov Gabbay Prize for Logic and Foundations* is an international research prize awarded to scholars for their contributions to logic. The prize was established in 2022 on the occasion of Dov Gabbay's 77th birthday in honour of his scientific and editorial work. The recipients of the prize are decided by an independent committee of six internationally well-known logicians representing [mathematical](wikipedia://en/19636), [philosophical](wikipedia://en/304939), and [computational logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_logic).[^o11]

The list of past recipients is maintained by the Initiative for Logic and Foundations at the [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg).[^o12]

- \2023. [Dale Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Miller_%28academic%29) and Mirek Truszczynski.
- \2024. David Asperó and Ralf Schindler.
- \2025. Alexandru Baltag.

## Regular positions {#Regular_positions}

- 1968–1970 – Instructor, [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem)
- 1970–1973 – Assistant Professor of Philosophy, [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University)
- 1973–1975 – Associate Professor of Philosophy, Stanford University
- 1975–1977 – Associate Professor, [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University)
- 1977–1983 – Lady Davis Professor of Logic, Bar-Ilan University
- 1983–1998 – Professor of Computing, [Imperial College of Science, Technology and Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), London
- 1998–present – Professor of Computing, Professor of Philosophy, Augustus De Morgan Professor of Logic, King's College, London
- 2009–present – Special Professor Bar-Ilan University
- 2015–2017 – Professor of Logics, [Ashkelon Academic College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkelon_Academic_College)
