# Carathéodory–Jacobi–Lie theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carathéodory–Jacobi–Lie theorem"}

The **[Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625)–[Jacobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gustav_Jakob_Jacobi)–[Lie](wikipedia://en/54010) theorem** is a theorem in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) which generalizes [Darboux's theorem](wikipedia://en/1543358).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *M* be a 2*n*-dimensional [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) with symplectic form ω. For *p* ∈ *M* and *r* ≤ *n*, let *f*<sub>1</sub>, *f*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *f*<sub>r</sub> be [smooth functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) defined on an [open neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_neighborhood) *V* of *p* whose [differentials](wikipedia://en/221519) are [linearly independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linearly_independent) at each point, or equivalently

$$df_1(p) \wedge \ldots \wedge df_r(p) \neq 0,$$

where {f<sub>i</sub>, f<sub>j</sub>} = 0. (In other words, they are pairwise in involution.) Here {–,–} is the [Poisson bracket](wikipedia://en/294340). Then there are functions *f*<sub>r+1</sub>, ..., *f*<sub>n</sub>, *g*<sub>1</sub>, *g*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *g*<sub>n</sub> defined on an open neighborhood *U* ⊂ *V* of *p* such that (f<sub>i</sub>, g<sub>i</sub>) is a [symplectic chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_chart) of *M*, i.e., ω is expressed on *U* as

$$\omega = \sum_{i=1}^n df_i \wedge dg_i.$$

:::

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

As a direct application we have the following. Given a [Hamiltonian system](wikipedia://en/1197531) as $(M,\omega,H)$ where *M* is a symplectic manifold with symplectic form $\omega$ and *H* is the [Hamiltonian function](wikipedia://en/198319), around every point where $dH \neq 0$ there is a symplectic chart such that one of its coordinates is *H*.

:::
