# GRE Mathematics Test

::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="GRE Mathematics Test"}

The **[GRE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Record_Examination) subject test in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)** is a [standardized test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardized_test) in the United States created by the [Educational Testing Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service) (ETS), and is designed to assess a candidate's potential for graduate or post-graduate study in the field of mathematics. It contains questions from many fields of mathematics; about 50% of the questions come from [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) (including pre-calculus topics, multivariate calculus, and [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations)), 25% come from [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) (including [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)), and 25% come from a broad variety of other topics typically encountered in undergraduate mathematics courses, such as [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and real [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134).[^o7][^o8]

:::

Up until the September 2023 administration, the GRE subject test in Mathematics was paper-based, as opposed to the GRE general test which is usually computer-based. Since then, it's been moved online. It contains approximately 66 multiple-choice questions,[^o7] which are to be answered within 2 hours and 50 minutes.[^o8] Scores on this exam are required for entrance to most math Ph.D. programs in the United States.

Scores are scaled and then reported as a number between 200 and 990;[^o10] however, in recent versions of the test, the maximum and minimum reported scores have been 920 and 400, which correspond to the 99th percentile and the 1st percentile, respectively. The mean score for all test takers from July 1, 2011, to June 30, 2014, was 659, with a standard deviation of 137.[^o9]

Prior to October 2001, a significant percentage of students were achieving perfect scores on the exam, which made it difficult for competitive programs to differentiate between students in the upper percentiles. As a result, the test was reworked and renamed "The Mathematics Subject Test (Rescaled)".[^o10] According to ETS, "Scores earned on the test after October 2001 should not be compared to scores earned prior to that date."[^o10]

Tests generally take place three times per year, within an approximately 14-day window in each of September, October, and April. Students must register for the exam approximately five weeks before the administration of the exam.[^o11]

## Test content {#Test_content}

The test covers topics from a typical undergraduate mathematics degree.[^o13]

### [Calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) (50%) {#Calculus_(50%)}

- [Differential calculus](wikipedia://en/50416) of one or more variables:
  - [Integral](wikipedia://en/15532)
  - [Analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202)
  - [Trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261)
  - [Differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309)

### [Algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) (25%) {#Algebra_(25%)}

- [Secondary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school) mathematical operations
- [Linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422):
  - [Matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859)
  - [System of linear equations](wikipedia://en/113087)
  - [Vector space](wikipedia://en/32370)
  - [Linear map](wikipedia://en/18102)
  - [Characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268)
  - [Eigenvalues and eigenvectors](wikipedia://en/2161429)
- [Abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384):
  - [Group theory](wikipedia://en/41890)
  - [Ring](wikipedia://en/48404)
  - [Module](wikipedia://en/276410)
  - [Field](wikipedia://en/10603)
  - [Number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)

### Other topics (25%) {#Other_topics_(25%)}

Specialized topics taught in most universities.

- [Real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478):
  - [Number](wikipedia://en/21690)
  - [Function](wikipedia://en/185427)
  - [Sequence](wikipedia://en/27838)
  - [Series](wikipedia://en/15287)
  - [Continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122)
  - [Differentiable function](wikipedia://en/330206)
  - [Riemann integral](wikipedia://en/26390)
  - [Topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) of $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{R}^n$
- [Discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492):
  - [Logic](wikipedia://en/46426065)
  - [Set theory](wikipedia://en/27553)
  - [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170)
  - [Graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401)
  - [Algorithm](wikipedia://en/775)
- [General topology](wikipedia://en/178649)
- [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446)
- [Complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759)
- [Probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)
- [Numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506)
