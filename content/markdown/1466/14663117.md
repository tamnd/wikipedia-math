# Crocco

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crocco"}

**Crocco** is an Italian surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Carmine Crocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmine_Crocco) (1830–1905), also known as Donatello and Italian guerrilla head
- [Claudio Crocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Crocco) (born 1958), Argentine footballer
- [Gaetano Arturo Crocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaetano_Arturo_Crocco) (1877–1968), Italian mathematician and space scientist
- [Mario Crocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Crocco) (born 1926), Italo-Argentinian neurobiologist
- [Matt Crocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Crocco) (born 1971), American musician, writer, and producer
