# Tarry point

*Image: Tarry point.svg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tarry point"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Tarry point** $T$ for a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) $\triangle ABC$ is a [point](wikipedia://en/593693) of [concurrency](wikipedia://en/2206157) of the [lines](wikipedia://en/22634860) through the [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) of the triangle [perpendicular](wikipedia://en/76944) to the corresponding sides of the triangle's first [Brocard triangle](wikipedia://en/14884335) $\triangle DEF$. The Tarry point lies on the other endpoint of the [diameter](wikipedia://en/8007) of the [circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432) drawn through the [Steiner point](wikipedia://en/35857329). The point is named for [Gaston Tarry](wikipedia://en/473996).[^o3]

:::
