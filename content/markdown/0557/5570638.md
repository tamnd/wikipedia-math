# Tamil numerals

*Image: Milestone tanjore old.jpg.* A milestone which uses both Tamil and Indo-Arabic Numerals (Tanjore Palace Museum).

*Image: Mauritian rupees 100.png.* Modern Tamil numerals featured on a 100 [Mauritian rupee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritian_rupee) note.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tamil numerals"}

The [Tamil language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language) has [number words](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numeral_%28linguistics%29) and dedicated symbols for them in the [Tamil script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_script). They have mostly been supplanted by [Arabic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_numeral) in common usage.

:::

## Basic numbering in Tamil (Tamil Illakkam) {#Basic_numbering_in_Tamil_(Tamil_Illakkam)}

### Zero {#Zero}

Old Tamil possesses a special numerical character for zero *(see Old Tamil numerals below),* which is read as ta (literally, no/nothing). Modern Tamil words for zero include சுழியம் (ta) or பூஜ்ஜியம் (ta).

## Transcribing other numbers {#Transcribing_other_numbers}

### Reproductive and attributive prefixes {#Reproductive_and_attributive_prefixes}

Tamil has a numeric prefix for each number from 1 to 9, which can be added to the words for the powers of ten (ten, hundred, thousand, etc.) to form multiples of them. For instance, the word for fifty, ஐம்பது (ta) is a combination of ஐ (ta, the prefix for five) and பத்து (ta, which is ten). The prefix for nine changes with respect to the succeeding base 10. தொ + the unvoiced consonant of the succeeding base 10 forms the prefix for nine. For instance, 90 is தொ + ண் (ண் being the unvoiced version of ணூ), hence, தொண்ணூறு).

### Partitive numerals (பகுத்தல்) {#Partitive_numerals_()}

#### Fractions (பின்னம்) {#Fractions_()}

Proposals to encode Tamil [fractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraction_%28mathematics%29) and symbols to [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode) were submitted.[^o21][^o22] As of version 12.0, [Tamil characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Supplement) used for fractional values in traditional accounting practices were added to the Unicode Standard.

##### Transcribing fractions (பின்னம் எழுத்தல்) {#Transcribing_fractions_()}

Any fraction can be transcribed by affixing -இல் (-il) after the [denominator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denominator) followed by the [numerator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerator). For instance, 1/41 can be said as நாற்பத்து ஒன்றில் ஒன்று (ta).

The suffixing of the -இல் (ta) requires the last consonant of the number to be changed to its இ (ta) form. For example, மூன்று + இல் (ta + ta) becomes மூன்றில் (ta); note the உ (ta) has been omitted.

**Common fractions (பொது பின்னங்கள்)** have names already allocated to them, hence, these names are often used rather than the above method.

#### Decimals (பதின்மம்) {#Decimals_()}

Decimal point is called புள்ளி (ta) in [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language). For example, 1.1 would be read as ஒன்று புள்ளி ஒன்று (ta). In Sri Lankan Tamil, Thasam தசம்.

#### Percentage (விழுக்காடு) {#Percentage_()}

Percentage is known as விழுக்காடு (ta) in [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language) or சதவீதம் (ta). These words are simply added after a number to form percentages. For instance, four percent is நான்கு சதவீதம் (ta) or நான்கு விழுக்காடு (ta). Percentage symbol (%) is also recognised and used.

## History {#History}

Before the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) unveiled as the new [rupee symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_rupee_sign), people in Tamil Nadu used the Tamil letter ௹ as the symbol. This symbol continues to be used occasionally as rupee symbol by Indian Tamils. It is also used by Tamils in Sri Lanka.[^o38]

The symbol ௳ is also known as the ta (Curl of ta), a symbol that most [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_people) Hindus will start off any auspicious document with. It is written to invoke the god ta, known otherwise as [Ganesha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganesha), who is the remover of obstacles.[^o39]
