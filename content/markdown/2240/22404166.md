# Commutant-associative algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Commutant-associative algebra"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **commutant-associative algebra** is a [nonassociative algebra over a field](wikipedia://en/191788#Non-associative_algebras) whose [multiplication](wikipedia://en/24977) satisfies the following axiom:

:::

$$([A_1,A_2], [A_3,A_4], [A_5,A_6]) =0,$$

where \[*A*, *B*\] = *AB* − *BA* is the [commutator](wikipedia://en/7193) of *A* and *B* and (*A*, *B*, *C*) = (*AB*)*C* – *A*(*BC*) is the [associator](wikipedia://en/943321) of *A*, *B* and *C*.

In other words, an algebra *M* is commutant-associative if the commutant, i.e. the subalgebra of *M* generated by all [commutators](wikipedia://en/7193) \[*A*, *B*\], is an [associative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associativity) algebra.
