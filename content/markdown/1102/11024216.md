# Mathematics in Education and Industry

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematics in Education and Industry"}

**MEI** (**Mathematics in Education and Industry**) is an independent educational charity and [curriculum development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum_development) body for [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) in the United Kingdom. Income generated through its work is used to support the teaching and learning of mathematics.

:::

## History {#History}

MEI was founded in 1963 with a grant from the Schools & Industry Committee of the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403). In 1965 it produced its first exam, [Additional Mathematics](wikipedia://en/26673495), then produced an A level course two years later. MEI's A-level exams were the first to include [probability](wikipedia://en/23542).

It was incorporated as a company on 18 October 1996.

## Structure {#Structure}

Although independent, MEI works in partnership with many organisations, including the [UK Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Her_Majesty's_Government). MEI is a [registered charity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Registered_charity)[^o5] with a [board of directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors) and a small professional staff.[^o6]

## Qualifications {#Qualifications}

- [GCE AS/A level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCE_Advanced_Level) Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Further Mathematics (Additional) (Published by [OCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_Cambridge_and_RSA_Examinations)[^o8])
- AS Level Statistics
- [GCSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCSE) Mathematics
- Foundations of Advanced Mathematics (FAM) – a freestanding course
- Introduction to Quantitative Methods (in association with OCR[^o9])
- OCR MEI Level 3 Core Maths Qualifications (Level 3 Certificate in Quantitative Reasoning and Level 3 Certificate in Quantitative Problem Solving)[^o10]

## Competitions {#Competitions}

MEI organises an annual online competition called Ritangle[^o12] for teams of students of [A level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_level) Mathematics, the [International Baccalaureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Baccalaureate) and [Scottish Highers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Highers). Questions are posted on the Integral website, with correct answers releasing a clue for the final question.
