# Karl Schröter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Schröter"}

**Karl Walter Schröter** (7 September 1905 in [Biebrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biebrich_%28Wiesbaden%29) near [Wiesbaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesbaden) – 22 August 1977 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).[^o10] Later on, after the war, he made important contributions concerning [semantic consequences](wikipedia://en/37531624#Semantic_consequence) (semantische Folgerungsrelationen) and [provability logic](wikipedia://en/618119) (syntaktische Ableitbarkeitsrelationen).[^o10] He worked as a mathematical theoretician and [cryptanalyst](wikipedia://en/5715) for the civilian [Pers Z S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pers_Z_S), the cipher bureau of the Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt), from the spring of 1941 to the end of [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).

:::

## Education {#Education}

From 1928 to 1936, Schröter studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), and [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) at the Universities of [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) and [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt). Due to family reasons he had to interrupt his studies several times. He then worked in the [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) group at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) led by [Heinrich Scholz](wikipedia://en/41316662). From 1 April 1939 he was a [research assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_assistant) at the Department of Philosophy at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). On 20 December 1941 he took his examination for promotion of [Dr. phil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) under the logician Heinrich Scholz studying mathematics, logic, and [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) with a thesis titled *Ein allgemeiner Kalkülbegriff* (A General Concept of Calculus). On 1 April 1941 he took a leave of absence to join [Pers Z S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pers_Z_S), the Foreign Office civilian cipher bureau, working as a mathematician. However, even during this time he continued to work on problems of basic mathematical research.[^o10]

## Career {#Career}

On 19 March 1943 he presented the application to the Faculty of Philosophy and Natural Sciences, with the publication *Axiomatisierung der Fregeschen Aussagenkalküle* ([Axiomatisation](wikipedia://en/928) of [Frege's](wikipedia://en/48416) [Propositional Calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_Calculus)), to be admitted to his Habilitation. On the basis of the positive opinions of [Heinrich Scholz](wikipedia://en/41316662) and [Adolf Kratzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Kratzer), the degree of Doctor *rerum naturalium habilitatus* was awarded to him by certificate of 22 May 1943. On 9 June 1943 the report on the completed Habilitation was given to the Reich Minister. On 1, 2 and 3 July 1943 he held a public trial lecture on the topic *Der Nutzen der mathematischen Logik für die Mathematik* (The Benefits of Mathematical Logic for Mathematics) as a prerequisite for a civil service position as a lecturer. On 18 August 1943 he was appointed lecturer with the authorisation to teach "Mathematical Logic and Fundamental Research", all the while still being employed in Berlin as a "scientific assistant worker" at Pers Z S.[^o10]

Karl Schröter remained at the University of Münster from 31 December 1943 until his contract was up on 31 April 1945, when he was taken prisoner by the [Counterintelligence Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterintelligence_Corps) (CIC). From early May to 30 September 1945 he appeared before a joint Anglo-American Commission, first in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), then in [Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg), concerning his work at Pers Z S during the war. After completion, he was dismissed in Marburg after the CIC had determined his political attitude. In the winter semester of 1945/46 he was affiliated as a lecturer in Münster.[^o10]

From May 1946 until his appointment to Berlin, Schröter was working at Münster as a substitute for the Review Committee of the [Denazification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denazification) Main Committee at the Westfälische Landesuniversität. In 1948, Karl Schröter was appointed Professor Extraordinarius for mathematical logic at [Humboldt University in Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin).[^o10]

In 1967, he became director of the Institute for pure mathematics of the [German Academy of Sciences at Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin).[^o10]

He was elected corresponding member in 1962 and two years later ordinary member of the German Academy of Sciences at Berlin.

In 1955, Schröter together with [Günter Asser](wikipedia://en/27547630) founded the *Zeitschrift für Mathematische Logik und Grundlagen der Mathematik* (Journal of mathematical logic and foundations of mathematics) which since 1991 is known as *Mathematical Logic Quarterly*.[^o5]
