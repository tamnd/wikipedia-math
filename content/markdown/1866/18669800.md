# Fake 4-ball

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fake 4-ball"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **fake 4-ball** is a [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactness_%28topology%29) [contractible](wikipedia://en/1752414) topological [4-manifold](wikipedia://en/1362795). [Michael Freedman](wikipedia://en/658970) proved that every three-dimensional [homology sphere](wikipedia://en/585388) bounds a fake 4-ball. His construction involves the use of [Casson handles](wikipedia://en/7024176) and so does not work in the [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) category.

:::
