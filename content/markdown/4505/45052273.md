# Stanisław Szarek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Szarek"}

*Image: Szarek.png.* Stanisław J. Szarek (2014)

**Stanisław J. Szarek** (born November 13, 1953) is a Polish professor of mathematics at both [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University) in the USA (since 1983) and [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in France (since 1996).[^o2] His research concerns [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o6][^o4]

:::

Szarek was born in [Lądek-Zdrój](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lądek-Zdrój), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland).[^o2] He earned a master's degree from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1976, and a Ph.D. from the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1979 under the supervision of [Aleksander Pełczyński](wikipedia://en/56285756).[^o2] He continued at the Polish Academy as a research fellow for four years before taking a faculty position at Case,[^o2] where he is now the Kerr Professor of Mathematics.[^o4]

Szarek won a gold medal in the 1971 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o2] He was an invited speaker at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o2][^o4][^o5] In 2007 he won the Langevin Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] In 2012 he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o4][^o7] and in 2017 he was awarded the Sierpiński medal.[^o8]
