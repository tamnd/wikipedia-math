# Annette Huber-Klawitter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annette Huber-Klawitter"}

**Annette Huber-Klawitter** (née Huber, born 23 May 1967) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg). Her research interests includes [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), in particular the [Bloch–Kato conjectures](wikipedia://en/25305882).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Annette Huber-Klawitter was born on 23 May 1967 in [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main). Between 1984 and 1986, she won the *Bundeswettbewerb Mathematik*, a national mathematics competition, three times in a row. [^o4]

She began her academic career at the [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt) and obtained her [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) in 1994. Her PhD dissertation was titled *Realisierung von gemischten Motiven in derivierten Kategorien und ihre Kohomologie* ("Realisation of mixed motives in derived categories and their cohomologies") and completed under the supervision of [Christopher Deninger](wikipedia://en/32349981). From 1995 to 1996, she spent two years as a postdoctoral fellow at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

In 1995, Huber-Klawitter won the [Heinz Maier-Leibnitz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Maier-Leibnitz_Prize),[^o8] and in 1996, she won an [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize).

She completed her Habilitation in 1999 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). In 2000, she became a professor of pure mathematics at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig). Since 2008, she is the chair of [Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_Theory) at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg).

She was an invited speaker at the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing).[^o7]

In 2008, she was elected a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o8] In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
