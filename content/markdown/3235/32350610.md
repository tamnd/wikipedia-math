# Dmitry Kramkov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitry Kramkov"}

**Dmitry Olegovich Kramkov** (Дмитрий Олегович Крамков) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University). His research field are [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics).

:::

Kramkov obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) in 1992, under supervision of [Albert Shiryaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Shiryaev). In 1996 he was awarded an [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) for his work in filtered statistical experiments.[^o3]

[Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem](wikipedia://en/74059230) is named after him.
