# Heinrich-Wolfgang Leopoldt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich-Wolfgang Leopoldt"}

*Image: Leopoldt.jpg.* Heinrich-Wolfgang Leopoldt, Karlsruhe 1989

**Heinrich-Wolfgang Leopoldt** (22 August 1927 – 28 July 2011) was a German mathematician who worked on [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

Leopoldt earned his Ph.D. in 1954 at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) under [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118) with the thesis *Über Einheitengruppe und Klassenzahl reeller algebraischer Zahlkörper* (*On group of unity and class number of real algebraic number fields*). As a postdoc, he was from 1956 to 1958 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). In 1959, he obtained his habilitation degree at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen) and was then at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen). From 1964, he was [ordentlicher Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordentlicher_Professor) at the [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe), where he was also Director of the Mathematics Institute.

Leopoldt and [Tomio Kubota](wikipedia://en/19487263) introduced and investigated [p-adic L-functions](wikipedia://en/22059815) (now named after them).[^o2] These functions are a component of [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150) and are a p-adic version of the [Dirichlet L-functions](wikipedia://en/400092). With [Hans Zassenhaus](wikipedia://en/1376825) he also worked on computer algebra and its applications in number theory.

Leopoldt and [Peter Roquette](wikipedia://en/41199245) edited the collected works of Hasse.[^o3]

In 1979, Leopoldt became a member of the [Heidelberger Akademie der Wissenschaften](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberger_Akademie_der_Wissenschaften).
