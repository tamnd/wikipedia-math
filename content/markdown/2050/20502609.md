# Local trace formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Local trace formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **local trace formula** (Arthur 1991) is a local analogue of the [Arthur–Selberg trace formula](wikipedia://en/20419621) that describes the character of the representation of *G*(**F**) on the discrete part of *L*<sup>2</sup>(*G*(**F**)), for *G* a [reductive algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) over a [local field](wikipedia://en/145375) **F**.

:::
