# Xavier Tolsa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xavier Tolsa"}

**Xavier Tolsa** (born 1966) is a [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaniards) mathematician, specializing in analysis.

:::

Tolsa is a professor at the [Autonomous University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Barcelona) and at the [*Institució Catalana de Recerca i Estudis Avançats* (ICREA), the Catalan Institute for Advanced Scientific Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_Institution_for_Research_and_Advanced_Studies).

Tolsa does research on [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) (Calderón-Zygmund theory), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342). Specifically, he is known for his research on [analytic capacity](wikipedia://en/727981) and [removable sets](wikipedia://en/727981#Removable_sets_and_Painlevé's_problem). He solved the problem of [A. G. Vitushkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoli_Georgievich_Vitushkin)[^o3][^o4] about the semi-additivity of analytic capacity. This enabled him to solve an even older problem of [Paul Painlevé](wikipedia://en/402504) on the geometric characterization of removable sets. Tolsa succeeded in solving the Painlevé problem by using the concept of so-called curvatures of measures introduced by [Mark Melnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Melnikov) in 1995. Tolsa's proof involves estimates of Cauchy transforms. He has also done research on the so-called [David](wikipedia://en/61630330)-[Semmes](wikipedia://en/31859356) problem involving [Riesz transforms](wikipedia://en/18673900) and rectifiability.[^o5]

In 2002 he was awarded the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize).[^o6] In 2006 in Madrid he was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) with talk *Analytic capacity, rectifiability, and the Cauchy integral*. He received in 2004 the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize)[^o7] and was an Invited Lecturer at the 2004 [ECM](wikipedia://en/13505846) with talk *Painlevé's problem, analytic capacity and curvature of measures*. In 2013 he received the [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181134) for his monograph *Analytic capacity, the Cauchy transform, and non-homogeneous Calderón-Zygmund theory* (Birkhäuser Verlag, 2013}.[^o8] In 2019 he received the [Rei Jaume I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premis_Rei_Jaume_I) prize for his contributions to Mathematics.
