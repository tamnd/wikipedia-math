# Science and Hypothesis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Science and Hypothesis"}

***Science and Hypothesis*** (La Science et l'Hypothèse) is a book by French mathematician [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740), first published in 1902. Aimed at a non-specialist readership, it deals with mathematics, [space](wikipedia://en/27667), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and nature.[^o3][^o4][^o5] It puts forward the theses that absolute truth in science is unattainable, and that many commonly held beliefs of scientists are held as convenient conventions rather than because they are more valid than the alternatives.[^o6]

:::

In this book, Poincaré describes open scientific questions regarding the photo-electric effect, Brownian motion, and the relativity of physical laws in space. Reading this book inspired [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s subsequent [Annus Mirabilis papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annus_Mirabilis_papers) published in 1905.[^o7]

This book was put into the Encyclopedia Britannica series [Great Books of the Western World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Books_of_the_Western_World). A new translation was published in November 2017.[^o8]
