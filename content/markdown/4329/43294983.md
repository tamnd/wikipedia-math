# Jonathan Bennett (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Bennett"}

**Jonathan Bennett** is a British mathematician and Professor of Mathematical Analysis at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham). He was a recipient of the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2011 for "his foundational work on multilinear inequalities in [harmonic](wikipedia://en/14147) and [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), and for a number of major results in the theory of [oscillatory integrals](wikipedia://en/3224242)."[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1995 he graduated with a BA in mathematics from [Hertford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertford_College,_Oxford) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). He went on to study for a PhD in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) under Anthony Carbery at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), graduating in 1999.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Bennett has done postdoctoral work at the University of Edinburgh, the [Universidad Autonoma de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Autonoma_de_Madrid) and [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin). He joined the University of Birmingham in 2005.[^o7] Bennett is an editor for the journals *[Mathematika](wikipedia://en/49068222)* and *[Quarterly Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterly_Journal_of_Mathematics)*.[^o8][^o9]

Bennett is known for his work in harmonic analysis, particularly in applying the methods of heat flow monotonicity and induction-on-scale arguments to prove inequalities arising in harmonic and geometric analysis,[^o10] in particular for his work (jointly with Anthony Carbery and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498)) on the [multilinear Kakeya conjecture](wikipedia://en/588260).[^o11] Bennett has an Erdős number of 3, via his collaboration with Tao.
