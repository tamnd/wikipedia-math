# Andrei Bolibrukh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrei Bolibrukh"}

Andreevich

**Andrei Andreevich Bolibrukh** (Андрей Андреевич Болибрух) (30 January 1950 – 11 November 2003) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was known for his work on [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) especially [Hilbert's twenty-first problem](wikipedia://en/1965023) ([Riemann–Hilbert problem](wikipedia://en/9710396)).[^o3] Bolibrukh was the author of about a hundred research articles on theory of [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations) including [Riemann–Hilbert problem](wikipedia://en/9710396) and [Fuchsian system](wikipedia://en/16987249#Fuchsian_systems_and_Schlesinger's_equations).[^o4]

:::

## Work {#Work}

Bolibrukh was born on 30 January 1950 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) and studied at the [45th Physics-Mathematics School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Physics-Mathematics_School) in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg).[^o6] After receiving his mathematical education at the [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Moscow_State_University), with [Mikhail Mikhailovich Postnikov](wikipedia://en/19633564) and Alexey Chernavskii as thesis advisers, he started working on the proof of the existence of linear differential equations having a prescribed [monodromic group](wikipedia://en/251250). He applied modern methods of [complex analytic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_geometry) to classical problems about [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) and was an expert on [Hilbert's twenty-first problem](wikipedia://en/1965023). In 1989, Bolibrukh produced his famous counterexamples which invalidated the [Josip Plemelj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josip_Plemelj)'s 1908 solution of Hilbert's twenty-first problem.[^o7] Bolibrukh dedicated much of his efforts to the Riemann-Hilbert problem in order to find full necessary and sufficient conditions for given [monodromy data](wikipedia://en/16987249#Monodromy_data) to be those of a Fuchsian system.

During his short career he served as Deputy Director of the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) and professor at the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology).[^o8]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1994 Bolibrukh was elected to the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zürich in 1994.[^o10] He was awarded the Lyapunov Prize from the Academy of Sciences, Russia in 1995. In 2001 Bolibrukh received the [State Prize of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Prize_of_the_Russian_Federation).
