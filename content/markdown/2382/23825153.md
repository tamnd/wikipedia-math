# Ranald Roderick Macdonald

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ranald Roderick Macdonald"}

**Ranald Roderick Macdonald** (1945–2007) was a British [mathematician and psychologist](wikipedia://en/2081516). He was known for his contribution to the foundations of [significance testing](wikipedia://en/160995).[^o3]

:::
