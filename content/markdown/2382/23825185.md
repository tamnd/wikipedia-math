# British Journal of Mathematical and Statistical Psychology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="British Journal of Mathematical and Statistical Psychology"}

The ***British Journal of Mathematical and Statistical Psychology*** is a British [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) founded in 1947. It covers the fields of [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), and [mathematical psychology](wikipedia://en/2081516). It was established as the ***British Journal of Psychology (Statistical Section)***, was renamed the ***British Journal of Statistical Psychology*** in 1953, and was renamed again to its current title in 1965.

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is indexed in *[Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910)*, *[PsycINFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PsycINFO)*, *[Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index)*, *[Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)* / *Social & Behavioral Sciences*, *[Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)*, and *[Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)*.
