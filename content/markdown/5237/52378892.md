# Borell–TIS inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Borell–TIS inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), the **Borell–TIS inequality** is a result bounding the probability of a deviation of the [uniform norm](wikipedia://en/349755) of a centered [Gaussian](wikipedia://en/6572909) [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) above its [expected value](wikipedia://en/9653). The result is named for [Christer Borell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christer_Borell) and its independent discoverers [Boris Tsirelson](wikipedia://en/1661441), [Ildar Ibragimov](wikipedia://en/56275141), and [Vladimir Sudakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Sudakov). The inequality has been described as "the single most important tool in the study of Gaussian processes."[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $T$ be a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450), and let $\{ f_t \}_{t \in T}$ be a centered (i.e. mean zero) [Gaussian process](wikipedia://en/302944) on $T$, with

$$\| f \|_T := \sup_{t \in T} | f_t |$$

[almost surely](wikipedia://en/351908) finite, and let

$$\sigma_T^2 := \sup_{t \in T} \operatorname{E}| f_t |^2.$$

Then[^o2] $\operatorname{E}(\| f \|_T)$ and $\sigma_T$ are both finite, and, for each $u > 0$,

$$\operatorname{P} \big( \| f \|_T > \operatorname{E}(\| f \|_T) + u \big) \leq \exp\left( \frac{- u^2}{2\sigma_T^2} \right).$$

Another related statement which is also known as the Borell-TIS inequality[^o2] is that, under the same conditions as above,

$$\operatorname{P}\big(\sup_{t\in T}f_t>\operatorname{E}(\sup_{t\in T}f_t)+u\big) \le \exp\bigg(\frac{-u^2}{2\sigma_T^2}\bigg),$$

and so by symmetry

$$\operatorname{P}\big(|\sup_{t\in T}f_t-\operatorname{E}(\sup_{t\in T}f_t)|>u\big) \le 2\exp\bigg(\frac{-u^2}{2\sigma_T^2}\bigg).$$

:::
