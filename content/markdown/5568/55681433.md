# Rosemary Roberts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rosemary Roberts"}

**Rosemary A. Roberts** is a [statistics educator](wikipedia://en/24985094) who led the creation of the [AP Statistics](wikipedia://en/4973089) course and exam for US secondary school students, and who later chaired the Statistical Education Section of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). Educated in England and Canada, she spent many years working in the US before her 2013 retirement.

:::

Roberts earned a bachelor's degree in England, at the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading). She completed her Ph.D. in statistics at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). Among her early works, she co-authored the [Chang and Roberts algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang_and_Roberts_algorithm) in 1979.[^o3] She joined the mathematics department at [Bowdoin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowdoin_College) in 1984, retired in 2013, and is now a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) there.

In 1987, with Tom Moore of [Grinnell College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grinnell_College), she co-founded the Statistics in the Liberal Arts Workshop (SLAW), an annual meeting of statisticians at liberal arts colleges held every summer at Grinnell. With [Ann E. Watkins](wikipedia://en/55645644), Chris Olsen, and Richard Scheaffer, She is the coauthor of *The Teacher's Guide for AP Statistics* (The College Board, 1997).

Roberts was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1997.
