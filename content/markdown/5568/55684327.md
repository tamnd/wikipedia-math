# Horndeski's theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horndeski's theory"}

**Horndeski's theory** is the most general theory of gravity in four dimensions whose Lagrangian is constructed out of the [metric tensor](wikipedia://en/195795) and a [scalar field](wikipedia://en/161879) and leads to [second order equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_order_equation) of motion. The theory was first proposed by [Gregory Horndeski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Horndeski) in 1974[^o2] and has found numerous applications, particularly in the construction of [cosmological models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmological_model) of [Inflation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflation_%28cosmology%29) and [dark energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_energy).[^o3] Horndeski's theory contains many theories of gravity, including [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), [Brans–Dicke theory](wikipedia://en/944442), [quintessence](wikipedia://en/39137), [dilaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilaton), [chameleon particle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chameleon_particle) and covariant Galileon[^o4] as special cases.

:::

## Action {#Action}

Horndeski's theory can be written in terms of an [action](wikipedia://en/312881) as[^o6]

$$S[g_{\mu\nu},\phi] = \int\mathrm{d}^{4}x\,\sqrt{-g}\left[\sum_{i=2}^{5}\frac{1}{8\pi G_{\text{N}}}\mathcal{L}_{i}[g_{\mu\nu},\phi]\,+\mathcal{L}_{\text{m}}[g_{\mu\nu},\psi_{M}]\right]$$

with the [Lagrangian densities](wikipedia://en/40752010)

$$\mathcal{L}_{2} = G_{2}(\phi,\, X)$$

$$\mathcal{L}_{3} = G_{3}(\phi,\,X)\Box\phi$$

$$\mathcal{L}_{4} = G_{4}(\phi,\,X)R+G_{4,X}(\phi,\,X)\left[\left(\Box\phi\right)^{2}-\phi_{;\mu\nu}\phi^{;\mu\nu}\right]$$

$$\mathcal{L}_{5} = G_{5}(\phi,\,X)G_{\mu\nu}\phi^{;\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{6}G_{5,X}(\phi,\,X)\left[\left(\Box\phi\right)^{3}+2{\phi_{;\mu}}^{\nu}{\phi_{;\nu}}^{\alpha}{\phi_{;\alpha}}^{\mu}-3\phi_{;\mu\nu}\phi^{;\mu\nu}\Box\phi\right]$$

Here $G_N$ is [Newton's constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton's_constant), $\mathcal{L}_m$ represents the matter Lagrangian, $G_2$ to $G_5$ are generic functions of $\phi$ and $X$ , $R,G_{\mu\nu}$ are the [Ricci scalar](wikipedia://en/285622) and [Einstein tensor](wikipedia://en/1057638), $g_{\mu\nu}$ is the [Jordan frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_and_Einstein_frames) metric, semicolon indicates [covariant derivatives](wikipedia://en/431848), commas indicate [partial derivatives](wikipedia://en/52565), $\Box\phi \equiv g^{\mu\nu}\phi_{;\mu\nu}$ ,$X\equiv -1/2g^{\mu\nu}\phi_{;\mu}\phi_{;\nu}$ and repeated indices are summed over following [Einstein's convention](wikipedia://en/195407).

## Constraints on parameters {#Constraints_on_parameters}

Many of the free parameters of the theory have been constrained, $\mathcal{L}_{1}$ from the coupling of the scalar field to the top field and $\mathcal{L}_{2}$ via coupling to jets down to low coupling values with proton collisions at the [ATLAS experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATLAS_experiment).[^o8] $\mathcal{L}_{4}$ and $\mathcal{L}_{5}$, are strongly constrained by the direct measurement of the speed of gravitational waves following [GW170817](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GW170817).[^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14]
