# Charles Rezk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Rezk"}

**Charles Waldo Rezk** (born 26 January 1969) is an American mathematician, specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rezk matriculated at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1987 and graduated there in 1991 with B.A. and M.A. in mathematics.[^o4] In 1996 he received his PhD from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) with thesis *Spaces of Algebra Structures and Cohomology of Operads* and advisor [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569).[^o6] At [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) Rezk was a faculty member from 1996 to 2001. At the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) he was an assistant professor from 2001 to 2006 and an associate professor from 2006 to 2014, and has been a full professor since 2014.[^o4]

He was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in the fall of 1999, the spring of 2000, and the spring of 2001.[^o7] He held visiting positions at MIT in 2006 and at Berkeley's [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in 2014. Since 2015 he has been a member of the editorial board of *[Compositio Mathematica](wikipedia://en/31882322)*.[^o4]

Rezk was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul in 2014.[^o8] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the class of 2015 (announced in late 2014).[^o4]

The [Ando–Hopkins–Rezk orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ando–Hopkins–Rezk_orientation) is named after him.
