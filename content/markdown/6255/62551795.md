# Survey of Consumer Expectations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Survey of Consumer Expectations"}

The **Survey of Consumer Expectations** is a monthly survey of U.S. households by the [New York Federal Reserve Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Federal_Reserve_Bank). The people are asked about how much the expect to spend, how high they expect inflation to be, their employment situation, and whether they are searching for a job.

:::

## Methodology {#Methodology}

About 4500 consumers have been surveyed each year since 2013. Survey results are calibrated to be demographically representative of U.S. households.[^o3] Monthly survey results and detailed microdata are published at the New York Fed site.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Results"}

## Results {#Results}

The survey's results are used to make inferences about public [expectations of U.S. dollar inflation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflation#Inflation_expectations).[^o9][^o10]

The survey has illuminated the degree to which employed workers are offered much higher-wage jobs than the unemployed, even holding constant the observable attributes of the workers.[^o11]

:::
