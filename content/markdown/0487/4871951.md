# Bob Vaughan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bob Vaughan"}

**Robert Charles Vaughan** (born 24 March 1945) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in the field of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Vaughan was born 24 March 1945. He read mathematics at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London), earning a bachelor's degree with second class honours in 1966.[^o4] He completed his PhD in 1970 at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) under supervision of [Theodor Estermann](wikipedia://en/4872148). He supervised [Trevor Wooley](wikipedia://en/4651458)'s PhD.[^o5]

After postdoctoral research at the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham) and [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield), he became a lecturer in 1972 at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London). He was promoted to reader in 1976 and professor in 1980, and headed the Pure Mathematics Section from 1988 to 1990. Since 1999, he has been Professor at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

Vaughan was a 1979 recipient of the [Junior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_Berwick_Prize).[^o7]

Since 1990 Vaughan has been a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o8] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

In 1990, he was given an honorary doctorate (D.Sc.) by the University of London.[^o4]
