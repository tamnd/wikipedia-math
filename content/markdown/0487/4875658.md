# Crosscap number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crosscap number"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **crosscap number** of a [knot](wikipedia://en/366808) *K* is the minimum of

:::

$$C(K) \equiv 1 - \chi(S), \,$$

taken over all [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), [connected](wikipedia://en/6233), [non-orientable](wikipedia://en/187446) [surfaces](wikipedia://en/27865) *S* bounding *K*; here $\chi$ is the [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420). The crosscap number of the [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075) is zero, as the Euler characteristic of the disk is one.

## Knot sum {#Knot_sum}

The crosscap number of a [knot sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_sum) is bounded:

$$C(k_1) + C(k_2) - 1 \leq C(k_1 \mathbin{\#} k_2) \leq C(k_1) + C(k_2).\,$$

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The crosscap number of the [trefoil knot](wikipedia://en/404773) is 1, as it bounds a [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) and is not trivial.
- The crosscap number of a [torus knot](wikipedia://en/1165182) was determined by M. Teragaito.

:::
