# Professor of Logic and Rhetoric

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Professor of Logic and Rhetoric"}

The **Professor of Logic and Rhetoric** is a professorship at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow). The Nova Erectio of [King James VI of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_James_VI_of_Scotland) shared the teaching of [moral philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_philosophy), logic and natural philosophy among the Regents.[^o2]

:::

In 1727, separate chairs were instituted, one of which was the Chair of Logic and Rhetoric.[^o3]

## Holders of the Chair of Logic and Rhetoric {#Holders_of_the_Chair_of_Logic_and_Rhetoric}

- John Loudon MA (1727)
- [Adam Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Smith) MA LLD (1751) Later [Professor of Moral Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_Moral_Philosophy,_Glasgow) and Rector
- James Clow MA (1752)
- [George Jardine](wikipedia://en/11767842) MA (1774)
- [Robert Buchanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Buchanan_%28playwright%29) MA LLD (1827)
- [John Veitch](wikipedia://en/206355) MA LLD (1864)
- [Robert Adamson](wikipedia://en/1281895) MA LLD (1895)
- Robert Latta MA DPhil LLD (1902)
- Archibald Allan Bowman MA LittD (1925) Later Professor of Moral Philosophy
- [Herbert James Paton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_James_Paton) MA DLitt (1927)
- Charles Arthur Campbell MA DLitt (1938)

- [Alexander Broadie](wikipedia://en/30277296) MA PhD DLitt FRSE FBA (1995)
- Fraser MacBride MA MPhil PhD (2013–16)
- Jack Lyons BA PhD (2020-)[^o5]
