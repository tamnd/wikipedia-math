# Wilhelm Wirtinger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Wirtinger"}

**Wilhelm Wirtinger** (19 July 1865 – 16 January 1945) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born at [Ybbs on the Danube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ybbs_an_der_Donau) and studied at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), where he received his doctorate in 1887, and his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1890. Wirtinger was greatly influenced by [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) with whom he studied at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

### Honours {#Honours}

In 1907 the [Royal Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_London) awarded him the [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal), for his contributions to the general theory of functions.

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

He worked in many areas of mathematics, publishing 71 works.[^o7] His first significant work, published in 1896, was on [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745). He proposed as a generalization of [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalues), the concept of the [spectrum of an operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_of_an_operator), in an 1897 paper; the concept was further extended by [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) and now it forms the main object of investigation in the field of [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713). Wirtinger also contributed papers on [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). He collaborated with [Kurt Reidemeister](wikipedia://en/520188) on [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), showing in 1905 how to compute the [knot group](wikipedia://en/1164126).[^o8] Also, he was one of the editors of the *Analysis* section of [Klein's encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klein's_encyclopedia).

During a conversation, Wirtinger attracted the attention of [Stanisław Zaremba](wikipedia://en/1028345) to a particular [boundary value problem](wikipedia://en/436779), which later became known as the mixed boundary value problem.[^o9]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Wirtinger, Wilhelm. (1927). "Zur formalen Theorie der Funktionen von mehr komplexen Veränderlichen". *Mathematische Annalen*. 97 (1). p. 357–375. doi:10.1007/BF01447872, available at [DigiZeitschirften](http://www.digizeitschriften.de/). In this important paper, Wirtinger introduces several important concepts in the [theory of functions of several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), namely [Wirtinger derivatives](wikipedia://en/28479408) and the [tangential Cauchy–Riemann condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangential_Cauchy–Riemann_condition). The paper is deliberately written from a formal point of view, i.e. without giving a rigorous derivation of the properties deduced.
- Wirtinger, Wilhelm. (1936). "Eine Determinantenidentität und ihre Anwendung auf analytische Gebilde in euklidischer und Hermitescher Maßbestimmung". *Monatshefte für Mathematik*. 44 (1). p. 343–365. doi:10.1007/BF01699328. mr:1550581. zbl:0015.07602.
- Wirtinger, Wilhelm. (1936). "Ein Integralsatz über analytische Gebilde im Gebiete von mehreren komplexen Veränderlichen". *Monatshefte für Mathematik*. 45 (1). p. 418–431. doi:10.1007/BF01708005. mr:1550660. zbl:0016.40802.
