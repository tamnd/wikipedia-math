# Segre classification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Segre classification"}

The **Segre classification** is an algebraic classification of rank two [symmetric tensors](wikipedia://en/23087738). It was proposed by the Italian mathematician [Corrado Segre](wikipedia://en/2422061) in 1884.

:::

The resulting types are then known as **Segre types**. It is most commonly applied to the [energy–momentum tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy–momentum_tensor) (or the [Ricci tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci_tensor)) and primarily finds application in the classification of [exact solutions in general relativity](wikipedia://en/1686520).
