# Mikael Rørdam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikael Rørdam"}

*Image: Mikael Rørdam Oberwolfach 2010.jpg.* Rørdam at [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) in 2010

**Mikael Rørdam** (born 7 January 1959, [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen)) is a Danish mathematician,[^o2] specializing in the theory of [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and its applications.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rørdam graduated with master's degree from the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) in 1984.[^o5] He received his PhD from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) with thesis *The theory of unitary rank and regular approximation* under the supervision of [Richard Kadison](wikipedia://en/25798650). In the spring of 1988 Rørdam was a postdoc at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto). At [Odense University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odense_University) he was an *Adjunkt* (assistant professor) from 1988 to 1991 and a *Lektor* (associate professor) from 1991 to 1997. He was a *Lektor* at the University of Copenhagen from 1998 to 2002 and full professor from 2002 to 2007 at the [University of Southern Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_Denmark). Since 2008 he is a full professor at the University of Copenhagen.[^o5]

Rørdam was elected a member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters) in 2004.[^o5] He was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006 in Madrid. He was a member of the board of the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) from 2010 to 2016. He was a plenary speaker at the [International Workshop on Operator Theory and its Applications](wikipedia://en/47771159) (IWOTA) in 2018 in Shanghai.[^o5]
