# T. S. Broderick

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="T. S. Broderick"}

**TS "Stan" Broderick** (22 May 1893 – 4 April 1962) was an Irish mathematician and academic who served as [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) (1944-1962) at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (TCD). He was the father of Irish academic [Edna Longley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edna_Longley).[^o2]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Timothy Stanislaus Broderick was born in [Youghal, Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youghal). He studied mathematics at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork) (BA 1913, MA 1916) where he won a [National University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland) Travelling Studentship Prize in 1916. He then went to TCD where he was a mathematics Scholar (1917) and got a BA in [Mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Physics) and [Experimental Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_Physics) (1918).[^o4] After teaching for a few years in [Exeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter) in England, he was appointed to the staff at TCD, serving as [Donegall Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegall_Lecturer) (1926-1943) and then [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) (1944-1962). In 1930 he became a Fellow of TCD, in 1958 a Senior Fellow, and in 1959 acting Vice Provost.[^o2]
