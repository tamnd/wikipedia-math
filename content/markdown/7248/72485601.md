# Nancy Blachman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nancy Blachman"}

**Nancy Blachman** (born 1956 in [Palo Alto, CA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California)) is an American educator, supporter of [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446) and mathematical [outreach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outreach), software book author, and supporter of [indie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film) [documentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary_film) films.[^o3] In 2007, she founded the [Julia Robinson Mathematics Festival](wikipedia://en/57295923) (JRMF),[^o4] which has grown into a successful math enrichment enterprise for teenagers in the USA and beyond. She is a long time supporter of the educational foundation [Gathering 4 Gardner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_4_Gardner).[^o5] She is a former chair and remains on the advisory board of Berkeley’s Industrial Engineering and Operations Research Department (IEOR).[^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Nancy Blachman was born in 1956 in Palo Alto, California, her father Nelson being an electrical engineer.[^o8][^o9] The family spent some time living in Spain in the 1960s, and Nancy's interest in mathematics began during high school back in [Palo Alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California) when she took a course based on [George Polya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya)'s *[Mathematics and Plausible Reasoning](wikipedia://en/45418224)*.[^o12] She was also inspired by the mathematics contest produced by [Saint Mary's College of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Mary's_College_of_California)[^o10] then popular with secondary schools throughout the San Francisco Bay Area.[^o11]

She did undergraduate work at [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) (1974 to 1976), got honours B.Sc. in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham) in the [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) (1978), an M.S. in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (1979) and an M.S. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (1988).[^o12][^o13]

## Mathematics in education {#Mathematics_in_education}

Blachman taught a course in problem solving with [Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica) at Stanford from 1990 to 1997.[^o15]

In 2004 she created Google Guide, an online interactive tutorial and reference about the capabilities of [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google).[^o16]

In 2005 while attending an education forum that promoted [STEM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STEM) \[Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math\][^o17] she remembered how the Saint Mary's College Mathematics Contest had inspired her as a student.[^o11][^o18] Working with Joshua Zucker and Jim Sotiros she decided to revive the structure and spirit of this long defunct competition.[^o19] This led to the founding of the Julia Robinson Mathematics Festival in 2007.[^o4] JRMF events now occur throughout the United States and have reached more than 100,000 students worldwide.[^o20]

Blachman has been a board member of Gathering 4 Gardner since 2008, served as its chair from 2012 to 2020, and is currently board president.[^o5]

She is the president and founder of Variable Symbols, a company that provides training, books, and tools to make complex software easier to use.[^o21]

## Indie documentary films {#Indie_documentary_films}

Blachman has been working since 2015 with filmmakers to shine a light on people in difficult situations, to uplift marginalized voices, humanize them, and make their stories accessible—with the aim of encouraging viewers to take action. Documentaries she has supported include *Aftershock, Art Spiegelman: Disaster is My Muse, Ailey, Athlete A, Counted Out, Crip Camp, Dark Money, Free for All, Patrice: The Movie, The Fight*, and *The Social Dilemma*.[^o23]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

On January 1, 1999, she married David desJardins. They have two children, Sarah and Louis.[^o25]
