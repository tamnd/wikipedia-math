# Beno Eckmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Beno Eckmann"}

**Beno Eckmann** (31 March 1917 – 25 November 2008[^o7]) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) mathematician who made contributions to algebraic topology, homological algebra, group theory, and differential geometry.

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Eckmann Hilton Serre Haefliger.jpg.* Beno Eckmann, [Peter Hilton](wikipedia://en/8951286), [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483), and [André Haefliger](wikipedia://en/20280849) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) in 2007

Born to a Jewish family in [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern), Eckmann received his master's degree from [Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) (ETH) in 1939. Later, he studied there under [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082), obtaining his Ph.D. in 1941. His dissertation, on [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), was jointly supervised by [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082) and [Ferdinand Gonseth](wikipedia://en/54012910).

In 1942 he obtained a lecturer position at the [University of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lausanne). He became an extraordinary professor there before, in 1948, taking a full professorship at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), where he remained until his 1984 retirement. He was also President of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society) for 1961–1962, and the founding head of the Mathematics Research Institute at ETH Zurich from 1964 until his retirement.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

A colloquium in honor of Eckmann's 60th birthday was held in Zurich in 1977.[^o5] Eckmann was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 1993.[^o7] He was the 2008 recipient of the [Albert Einstein Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein_Medal).[^o8]

He was awarded honorary degrees by the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) in 1964, the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in 1967, and the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in 1983.[^o5]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

[Calabi–Eckmann manifolds](wikipedia://en/22670842), [Eckmann–Hilton duality](wikipedia://en/23191477), the [Eckmann–Hilton argument](wikipedia://en/488809), and the [Eckmann–Shapiro lemma](wikipedia://en/18665459) are named after Eckmann.

## Family {#Family}

Eckmann's son is [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) [Jean-Pierre Eckmann](wikipedia://en/31446069).[^o11]
