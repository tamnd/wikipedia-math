# Vladimir Tretyakov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Tretyakov"}

**Vladimir Evgenyevich Tretyakov** (Влади́мир Евге́ньевич Третьяко́в; 12 December 1936 – 8 January 2021) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), corresponding member of [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). He was the rector of the [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University) from 1993 to 2006 and was its President from 2006 to 2011.[^o3][^o4] He was an advisor of the rector of [Ural Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_Federal_University) from 2011 to 2021.

:::
