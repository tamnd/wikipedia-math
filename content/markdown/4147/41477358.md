In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-7 tetrahedral honeycomb** is a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,7}. It has seven [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra) {3,3} around each edge. All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with infinitely many tetrahedra existing around each vertex in an [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365) [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644).

:::
