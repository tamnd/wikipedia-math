# Michael Gage (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Gage"}

**Michael Eaton Gage** is a mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester). He is known for his work on the [curve-shortening flow](wikipedia://en/44089758), and in particular for the Gage–Hamilton–Grayson theorem, proved by Gage with [Richard S. Hamilton](wikipedia://en/758911) and Matthew Grayson, which describes the behavior of any [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_curve) [Jordan curve](wikipedia://en/267444) under the curve-shortening flow.[^o2][^o3][^o4] He is also one of the original developers of the [WeBWorK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeBWorK) online homework delivery system.[^o5]

:::

Gage did his undergraduate studies at [Antioch College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioch_College),[^o8] and completed his Ph.D. in mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1978, under the supervision of [Robert Osserman](wikipedia://en/19663336). He has worked as a systems programmer for [Intel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel),[^o7] and joined the Rochester faculty in 1984.[^o8]

Gage was the 1996–1997 winner of the distinguished teaching award of the Seaway Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o8]
