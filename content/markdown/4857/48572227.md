# Nataša Šešum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nataša Šešum"}

**Nataša Šešum** is a professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [geometric flow](wikipedia://en/14617515).[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Šešum earned her PhD in 2004 from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) under the supervision of [Gang Tian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gang_Tian). Her dissertation was *Limiting Behavior of Ricci Flows*.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Šešum was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o6] In 2015 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] She was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for 2015–2016.[^o8] She was awarded the 2023 AMS [Ruth Lyttle Satter Prize](wikipedia://en/37554140) in Mathematics.[^o9]
