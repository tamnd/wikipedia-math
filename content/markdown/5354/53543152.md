# Dominique Perrin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dominique Perrin"}

**Dominique Pierre Perrin**[^o3] (b. 1946) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) known for his contributions to [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869) and to [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490). He is a professor of the [University of Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne-la-Vallée) and currently serves as the President of [ESIEE Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESIEE_Paris).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Perrin earned his PhD from [Paris 7 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1975. In his early career, he was a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) researcher (1970–1977) and taught at the [University of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chile) (1972–1973). Later, he worked as a professor at the [University of Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rouen) (1977–1983), [Paris 7 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (1983–1993), and [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) (1982–2002). Since 1993, Perrin is a professor at the [University of Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne-la-Vallée), and since 2004, he is the President of [ESIEE Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESIEE_Paris).

Perrin is a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) since 1989.

## Scientific contributions {#Scientific_contributions}

Perrin has been a member of the [Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474) group of mathematicians that developed the foundations of combinatorics on words. He has co-authored three scientific monographs: "Theory of Codes" (1985),[^o6] "Codes and Automata" (2009),[^o7] and "Infinite Words" (2004),[^o8] as well as the three [Lothaire books](wikipedia://en/32232474).[^o9][^o10][^o11] Perrin has published around 50 research articles in [formal language theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_language_theory).
