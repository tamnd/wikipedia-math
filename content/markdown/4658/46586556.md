# Gudkov's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gudkov's conjecture"}

In [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198), **Gudkov's conjecture**, also called **Gudkov’s congruence**, (named after [Dmitry Gudkov](wikipedia://en/46586611)) was a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138), and is now a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977), which states that a [M-curve](wikipedia://en/5209604) of [even](wikipedia://en/143135) degree $2d$ obeys the [congruence](wikipedia://en/20087)

:::

$$p - n \equiv d^2\, (\!\bmod 8),$$

where $p$ is the number of positive [ovals](wikipedia://en/2027249) and $n$ the number of negative ovals of the M-curve. (Here, the term M-curve stands for "maximal curve"; it means a [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) over the [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438) whose genus is $k-1$, where $k$ is the number of maximal components of the curve.[^o2])

The theorem was [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by the combined works of [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490) and [Vladimir Rokhlin](wikipedia://en/7703611).[^o3][^o4][^o5]
