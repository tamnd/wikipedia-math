# Sumihiro's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sumihiro's theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Sumihiro's theorem**, introduced by (Sumihiro 1974), states that a [normal algebraic variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_variety) with an [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of a [torus](wikipedia://en/360136) can be [covered](wikipedia://en/317552) by torus-invariant affine open subsets.

:::

The "normality" in the hypothesis cannot be relaxed.[^o3] The hypothesis that the group acting on the variety is a torus can also not be relaxed.[^o4]
