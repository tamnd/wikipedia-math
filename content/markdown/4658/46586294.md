# Christian Bär

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Bär"}

**Christian Bär** (born 17 September 1962 in [Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaiserslautern)) is a German mathematician, whose research concerns [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o3]

:::

Bär enrolled on Ph.D. studies at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) as a student of [Hans Werner Ballmann](wikipedia://en/24812368), and obtained his Ph.D. in 1990.[^o4]

He was elected president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973), having assumed the post in 2011.[^o5]
