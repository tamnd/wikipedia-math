# Weighted geometric mean

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weighted geometric mean"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **weighted geometric mean** is a generalization of the [geometric mean](wikipedia://en/13046) using the [weighted arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/33274).

:::

Given a [sample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_%28statistics%29) $x=(x_1,x_2\dots,x_n)$ and [weights](wikipedia://en/245523) $w=(w_1, w_2,\dots,w_n)$, it is calculated as:[^o2]

$$\bar{x} = \left(\prod_{i=1}^n x_i^{w_i}\right)^{1 / \sum_{i=1}^n w_i} = \quad \exp \left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n w_i \ln x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^n w_i \quad} \right)$$

The second form above illustrates that the [logarithm](wikipedia://en/17860) of the geometric mean is the weighted arithmetic mean of the logarithms of the individual values. If all the weights are equal, the weighted geometric mean simplifies to the ordinary unweighted geometric mean.[^o2]
