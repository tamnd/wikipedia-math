# MacTutor History of Mathematics Archive

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MacTutor History of Mathematics Archive"}

The **MacTutor History of Mathematics Archive** is a website maintained by John J. O'Connor and [Edmund F. Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_F._Robertson) and hosted by the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland). It contains detailed [biographies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biography) on many historical and contemporary [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), as well as information on famous [curves](wikipedia://en/89246) and various topics in the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

The History of Mathematics archive was an outgrowth of **Mathematical MacTutor system**, a [HyperCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HyperCard) database by the same authors,[^o6] which won them the European Academic Software award in 1994. In the same year, they founded their web site. It has since expanded to include biographies of more than 3200 mathematicians and scientists.[^o8][^o7]

In 2015, O'Connor and Robertson won the Hirst Prize of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for their work.[^o8][^o9] The citation for the Hirst Prize calls the archive "the most widely used and influential web-based resource in history of mathematics".[^o9]
