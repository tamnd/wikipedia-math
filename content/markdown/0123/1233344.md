# Augmented truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Augmented truncated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **augmented truncated dodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{68}$). As its name suggests, it is created by attaching a [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) onto one [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) face of a [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538).

:::
