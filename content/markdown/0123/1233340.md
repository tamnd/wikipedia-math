# Augmented truncated cube

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Augmented truncated cube"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **augmented truncated cube** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{66}$). As its name suggests, it is created by attaching a [square cupola](wikipedia://en/1128479) ($J_{4}$) onto one [octagonal](wikipedia://en/314575) face of a [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529).

:::
