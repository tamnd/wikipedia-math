# Triaugmented truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Triaugmented truncated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triaugmented truncated dodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{71}$). Of them, it has the greatest surface area and volume for a given side length, as well as the greatest numbers of edges and vertices; it is tied for the greatest number of faces with [$J_{72}$](wikipedia://en/1131111), [$J_{73}$](wikipedia://en/1177997), [$J_{74}$](wikipedia://en/1178006), and [$J_{75}$](wikipedia://en/1133367). As its name suggests, it is created by attaching three [pentagonal cupolas](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) onto three nonadjacent [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) faces of a [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538).
