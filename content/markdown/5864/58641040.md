# Atsuko Miyaji

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Atsuko Miyaji"}

Miyaji Atsuko **Atsuko Miyaji** (宮地充子, born 1965) is a Japanese [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) known for her research on [elliptic-curve cryptography](wikipedia://en/9966) and [software obfuscation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obfuscation_%28software%29). She is a professor in the Division of Electrical, Electronic and Information Engineering, at [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Miyaji was born in [Osaka Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Prefecture) and became interested in mathematics as an elementary school student after learning of the [Epimenides paradox](wikipedia://en/9638). She studied mathematics as an undergraduate at Osaka University, and chose to go into industry instead of continuing as a graduate student, working from 1990 to 1998 for [Matsushita Electric Industrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matsushita_Electric_Industrial).

During this time she returned to graduate school, and earned a doctorate from Osaka University in 1997. She became an associate professor at the [Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Advanced_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) in 1998, and returned to Osaka University as a professor in 2015. She has also held short-term teaching or visiting positions at [Osaka Prefecture University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Prefecture_University), the [University of Tsukuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tsukuba), the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis), and [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).

## Book {#Book}

Miyaji is the author of a 2012 [Japanese language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language) book on cryptography, "代数学から学ぶ暗号理論：整数論の基礎から楕円曲線暗号の実装まで".
