# Alpha Sigma Kappa

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alpha Sigma Kappa"}

**Alpha Sigma Kappa – Women in Technical Studies** (**ΑΣΚ – WiTS**) is a social [sorority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraternities_and_sororities_in_North_America) for women in the fields of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), [technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology) and the [sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences).

:::

The sorority was founded at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1989 by a group of women who had formerly been affiliated with the Sisters of [Triangle Fraternity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_Fraternity) program. Alpha Sigma Kappa became a national organization in 1996.

## History {#History}

Alpha Sigma Kappa originally grew from a Little Sisters of Triangle organization at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). In the late 1980s, Little Sister programs were being phased out by fraternal organizations across the country; [Triangle Fraternity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_Fraternity)'s National Council resolved to do so with their local Little Sisters organizations.[^o4] To maintain a formal relationship, the University of Minnesota's Little Sisters group chose to found Alpha Sigma Kappa. When Alpha Sigma Kappa was founded, scientific careers were filled primarily by men. In 1989, only seventeen percent of the students enrolled in the Institute of Technology at the University of Minnesota were female.[^o7] The Founding Sisters of the organization wished to create a sorority dedicated to supporting women who entered these fields. Alpha Sigma Kappa was intended to bring women pursuing technical studies together in a social setting: working to develop, encourage, and support the academic and social needs of these women.[^o5] At the time of its founding, Alpha Sigma Kappa was the only social sorority for technical women whose scope included those in architecture and non-engineering sciences.[^o7]

The stated purpose of Alpha Sigma Kappa is to promote friendship, academic achievement, unity within the organization, and philanthropy throughout the community. The sorority supports women in their academic goals and promotes women in technical fields through leadership, friendship, and support.

On March 4, 1996, an Interest Group of women was formed at the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma), which would ultimately become the *Beta chapter* on September 13, 1997. The National Organization of Alpha Sigma Kappa was officially formed on April 29, 1996, at the first national meeting in [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis), Minnesota. Between 1999 and 2005, four more chapters were installed.[^o8] In 2010, Alpha Sigma Kappa became the first sorority established on the campus of [New Mexico Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_Tech).[^o9] Since 2015, the organization's growth has accelerated rapidly, establishing a presence on an additional ten universities across the United States as a social sorority for women in STEM.[^o10][^o5]

The National Organization of Alpha Sigma Kappa is not affiliated with a National Panhellenic governing body; however, chapters may be affiliated with councils at their respective universities.[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14] As of 2025, it has initiated 3,000+ members and has 400+ active collegiate members in fifteen chapters.[^o15] Its headquarters is at 200 Golden Harvest Circle in Blacksburg, Virginia.[^o16]

## Symbols {#Symbols}

The Greek letters Alpha Sigma Kappa represent social aspects; "Women in Technical Studies" specifies its membership.[^o18] The sorority's colors are royal blue and silver.[^o19] Its flower is the white rose and its jewel is the blue topaz.[^o19] Alpha Sigma Kappa's symbols are the [infinity sign](wikipedia://en/1605389) and the lioness.[^o19] Its motto is A Posse Ad Esse or "From Possibility to Reality".

## Membership {#Membership}

Active membership in Alpha Sigma Kappa requires that a student pursues a major with a minimum of 1/3 of all credit hours in architecture, computer science, engineering, mathematics, physical sciences, or biological sciences, with a minimum of 1/6 of all credit hours as upper division courses in these fields. Specific majors at each university are defined in the respective chapter or Petitioning Group bylaws.[^o21]

Once a woman is Initiated, her membership is for a lifetime. Alumnae remain connected through alumnae chapters as well as informal networks of social and professional support.[^o22][^o23]

## Philanthropy {#Philanthropy}

The National philanthropy for Alpha Sigma Kappa is [DonorsChoose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DonorsChoose). Individual chapters may choose local classroom projects to support through this organization, emphasizing assisting STEM projects.[^o25]

Additionally, chapters of Alpha Sigma Kappa organize and participate in local philanthropic events in their communities. Many events work to promote science and technical careers to young women.[^o26][^o27] However, these events provide direct service or raise funds for a variety of [nonprofit organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonprofit_organizations), including: the [American Cancer Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Cancer_Society), [Charity: Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charity:_Water), [Girl Scouts of the USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_Scouts_of_the_USA), and the [Susan G Komen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_G_Komen) Foundation.[^o28][^o29][^o30][^o31][^o32]

In 2014, the Alpha Sigma Kappa Educational Foundation, a [501(c)(3) organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/501%28c%29%283%29_organization), was created to support women in technical fields by managing academic scholarships.[^o5][^o33]
