# Louis Saalschütz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Saalschütz"}

*Image: Das Fotoalbum für Weierstraß 031 (Louis Saalschütz).jpg.* Louis Saalschütz's Portrait

**Louis Saalschütz** (1 December 1835 — 25 May 1913) was a [Prussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia)-[Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Louis Saalschütz was born to a Jewish family in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg), [Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia), the son of [archaeologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeologist) [Joseph Levin Saalschütz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Levin_Saalschütz).[^o3] From 1854 to 1860 he studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg). In 1861 he received his doctorate under the supervision of [Franz Ernst Neumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Ernst_Neumann), with the dissertation *Ueber die Wärmeveränderungen in den Höheren Erdschichten Unter dem Einfluss des Nicht-periodischen Temperaturwechsels an der Oberfläche*.

From 1861 to 1882 he was teacher of mathematics, mechanics, and engineering at the Royal School of Mechanics in Königsberg and the University of Königsberg, where he became an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in 1875 and [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 1888.[^o4]

## Hypergeometric Series {#Hypergeometric_Series}

F. J. Whipple coined the phrase "Saalschützian" for generalized hypergeometric $_3F_2$ series where one of the numerator parameters is a negative integer and the sum of the denominator parameters is one plus the sum of the numerator parameters.[^o6][^o7] Nowadays it is also used for more general $_{p+1}F_p$ series with that property. [^o8]

The "Saalschutz theorem" is [^o9] [^o10]

$$_3F_2(a,b,-n;c,1+a+b-c-n;1) = \frac{(c-a)_n(c-b)_n}{(c)_n(c-a-b)_n}.$$
