# Gabriel Oltramare

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel Oltramare"}

**Gabriel Oltramare** (19 July 1816 – 10 April 1906) was a Swiss mathematician, known for his book *Essai sur le Calcul de Généralisation*.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Oltramare was born in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) on 19 July 1816, the son of cousins Anne Oltramare and Louis-David-Benjamin Oltramare, a [watchcase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchcase) maker.[^o3] He belonged to a prominent family of Italian [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant) origin that settled in Geneva in the 16th century.[^o7] Oltramare studied mathematics and natural sciences in Geneva from 1836 to 1839, then studied higher mathematics at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_Faculty_of_Humanities), graduating with a *[licence ès sciences mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29#France)* in 1840.[^o3] He was a teacher in [Aarau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarau).[^o8] From 1843 to 1844, Oltramare worked in Egypt as a tutor for [Isma'il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isma'il_Pasha), son of [Ibrahim Pasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Pasha_of_Egypt) of the [Muhammad Ali dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali_dynasty). He was *[privat-docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)* of mechanics at Geneva's industrial school from 1845 to 1870, as well as professor of mathematics at the [Collège de Genève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_Calvin) from 1848 to 1850. In 1848, Oltramare was appointed professor ordinarius of higher mathematics at the [Academy of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva).[^o3]

At the beginning of his career he did research on number theory, on which he published several works;[^o3] according to [Henri Fehr](wikipedia://en/33233022), the most important of these is the 1855 *Note sur les relations qui existent entre les formes linéaires et les formes quadratiques des numbers premiers*.[^o9][^o10] Later in his career Oltramare worked on mathematical analysis. In 1893 he published his treatise *Essai sur le calcul de généralisation*, with 2nd edition in 1899 and a Russian translation in 1895. He published several articles on astronomy and meteorology in scientific journals. He was one of the founders in 1853 of the *Institut national genevois* and presided from 1894 to 1902 over its section of natural sciences. He was a member in 1848 of the *Conseil administratif de Genève* and member from 1848 to 1854 of the [Grand Council of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Council_of_Geneva), over which he presided several times.[^o3] He was one of the organizers of the first [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), which was held in Geneva in 1897.[^o11]
