# 2009 Belarus census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2009 Belarus census"}

The **2009 Belarusian census** was the second [census](wikipedia://en/6889) in [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus) after it became an independent state after the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union). The census was carried out during October 14–24, 2009.[^o2] The initial results are to be announced by February 1, 2010. Full processing of census data is expected to take about two years.[^o3]

:::

97% of residents of the country took part in the census.[^o4]

In 2008, it was announced that the [United Nations Commission on Population and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Commission_on_Population_and_Development) approved a grant of \$800 million to Belarus for this purpose.
