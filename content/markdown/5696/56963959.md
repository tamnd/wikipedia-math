# A Primer of Real Functions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="A Primer of Real Functions"}

***A Primer of Real Functions*** is a revised edition of a classic [Carus Monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carus_Monograph) on the [theory of functions of a real variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_functions_of_a_real_variable). It is authored by [Ralph P. Boas Jr.](wikipedia://en/3088231) and the fourth edition updated by his son [Harold P. Boas](wikipedia://en/11754242).[^o3]

:::
