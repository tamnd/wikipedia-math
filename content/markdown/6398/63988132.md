# Ordered topological vector space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ordered topological vector space"}

In mathematics, specifically in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924), an **ordered topological vector space**, also called an **ordered TVS**, is a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) *X* that has a [partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order) ≤ making it into an [ordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221) whose positive cone $C := \left\{ x \in X : x \geq 0\right\}$ is a closed subset of *X*.[^n1] Ordered TVSes have important applications in [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713).

:::

## Normal cone {#Normal_cone}

If *C* is a cone in a TVS *X* then *C* is **normal** if $\mathcal{U} = \left[ \mathcal{U} \right]_{C}$, where $\mathcal{U}$ is the neighborhood filter at the origin, $\left[ \mathcal{U} \right]_{C} = \left\{ \left[ U \right] : U \in \mathcal{U} \right\}$, and $[U]_{C} := \left(U + C\right) \cap \left(U - C\right)$ is the [*C*-saturated](wikipedia://en/63990850) hull of a subset *U* of *X*.[^n2]

If *C* is a cone in a TVS *X* (over the real or complex numbers), then the following are equivalent:[^n2]

1. *C* is a normal cone.
1. For every filter $\mathcal{F}$ in *X*, if $\lim \mathcal{F} = 0$ then $\lim \left[ \mathcal{F} \right]_{C} = 0$.
1. There exists a neighborhood base $\mathcal{B}$ in *X* such that $B \in \mathcal{B}$ implies $\left[ B \cap C \right]_{C} \subseteq B$.

and if *X* is a vector space over the reals then also:[^n2]

1. There exists a neighborhood base at the origin consisting of convex, [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238), [*C*-saturated](wikipedia://en/63990850) sets.
1. There exists a generating family $\mathcal{P}$ of semi-norms on *X* such that $p(x) \leq p(x + y)$ for all $x, y \in C$ and $p \in \mathcal{P}$.

If the topology on *X* is locally convex then the closure of a normal cone is a normal cone.[^n2]

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

### Properties {#Properties}

If *C* is a normal cone in *X* and *B* is a bounded subset of *X* then $\left[ B \right]_{C}$ is bounded; in particular, every interval $[a, b]$ is bounded.[^n2] If *X* is Hausdorff then every normal cone in *X* is a proper cone.[^n2]

:::

::: {#o13 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Let *X* be an [ordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221) over the reals that is finite-dimensional. Then the order of *X* is Archimedean if and only if the positive cone of *X* is closed for the unique topology under which *X* is a Hausdorff TVS.[^n1]
- Let *X* be an ordered vector space over the reals with positive cone *C*. Then the following are equivalent:[^n1]

<!-- -->

1. the order of *X* is regular.
1. *C* is sequentially closed for some Hausdorff locally convex TVS topology on *X* and $X^{+}$ distinguishes points in *X*
1. the order of *X* is Archimedean and *C* is normal for some Hausdorff locally convex TVS topology on *X*.

:::
