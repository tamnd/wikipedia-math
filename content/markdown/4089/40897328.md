# Pavel Etingof

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pavel Etingof"}

**Pavel Ilyich Etingof** (Павло Ілліч Етінгоф, Павел Ильич Этингоф; born 1969) is an American mathematician of Ukrainian origin. He does research on the intersection of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) (exactly [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213)) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), e.g., [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Etingof was born in [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv), [Ukrainian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_SSR), and studied in the [Kyiv Natural Science Lyceum No. 145](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Natural_Science_Lyceum_No._145) in 1981–1984, and at the Department of Mathematics and Mechanics of the [Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv) in 1984–1986. He received his M.S. in applied mathematics from the [Oil and Gas Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gubkin_Russian_State_University_of_Oil_and_Gas) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1989 and then went to the US in 1990. In 1994, he received his PhD in mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) under [Igor Frenkel](wikipedia://en/5033014) with thesis *Representation Theory and Holonomic Systems*. After his PhD, he became [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and in 1998 an assistant professor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Since 2005 he is a professor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).

In 1999, he was a Fellow of the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655).

In 2002, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) (*On the dynamical [Yang–Baxter equation](wikipedia://en/3720810)*). He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

In 2010, together with [Slava Gerovitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slava_Gerovitch) he co-founded the MIT Program for Research In Mathematics, Engineering and Science (PRIMES) for high school students, and has since served as its Chief Research Advisor.[^o5]

In 2016, he became a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o6]
