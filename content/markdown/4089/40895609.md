# Dan Freed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Freed"}

*Image: Daniel S. Freed Oberwolfach 2010.jpg.* Freed at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2010

**Daniel Stuart Freed** (born 17 April 1959) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in global analysis and its applications to [supersymmetry](wikipedia://en/224636), [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). He is currently the Shiing-Shen Chern Professor of Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Freed studied at Harvard University, where he received his [bachelor's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's) and [master's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's) degrees in 1981. He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1985 with thesis *The geometry of loop groups* under [Isadore Singer](wikipedia://en/324564). As a [postdoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoc), Freed was a [Moore Instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and then became an assistant professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). Beginning in 1989, he was an associate professor, and from 1994, a professor at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). From 1996 to 1998, he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (IAS) and he was a visiting scientist at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (1995, 1999). From 1989 to 2022, he was a professor at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

In the academic year 2002/2003, Freed was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow),[^o4] and from 1988 to 1992, a Sloan Fellow. In 2002, he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) (Twisted K-theory and loop groups).[^o5] He is one of the founders of the IAS/Park City Mathematics Institute and is, since 2006, a member of the board of trustees of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), where he has belonged to the scientific advisory board since 2002. He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
