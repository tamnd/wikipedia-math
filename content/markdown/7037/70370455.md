# Adriana Pesci

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adriana Pesci"}

**Adriana Irma Pesci** is an Argentine [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), specialising in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034). Her research topics have included [lattice models of polymer solutions](wikipedia://en/3010589),A [Hele-Shaw flow](wikipedia://en/16691615),B [flagellar motion of organisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flagellum) in fluids,C [soap films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap_films) on [Möbius strips](wikipedia://en/37817),D and the [Leidenfrost effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leidenfrost_effect).E

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pesci is originally from Argentina, and earned her Ph.D. in 1986 at the [National University of La Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_La_Plata) in Argentina. She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), under the mentorship of [Leo Kadanoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Kadanoff) and Norman Lebovitz.

She joined the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) as a lecturer in physics in 1999, becoming a senior lecturer in 2003. In 2007 she moved to the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), where she is a senior research associate in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics, a fellow of [King's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge), and a former Darley Fellow in Mathematics of [Downing College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downing_College,_Cambridge).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Pesci married [Raymond E. Goldstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_E._Goldstein), a frequent coauthor who was also a postdoctoral researcher in Chicago and moved with her to Arizona and Cambridge.
