# Tropical compactification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tropical compactification"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **tropical compactification** is a compactification ([projective completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_completion)) of a [subvariety](wikipedia://en/248808) of an [algebraic torus](wikipedia://en/504697), introduced by Jenia Tevelev.[^o2][^o3] Given an algebraic torus and a connected closed subvariety of that torus, a compactification of the subvariety is defined as a closure of it in a [toric variety](wikipedia://en/737087) of the original torus. The concept of a tropical compactification arises when trying to make compactifications as "nice" as possible. For a torus $T$ and a toric variety $\mathbb{P}$, the compactification $\bar{X}$ is tropical when the map

:::

$$\Phi: T \times \bar{X} \to \mathbb{P},\ (t,x) \to tx$$

is [faithfully flat](wikipedia://en/542520) and $\bar{X}$ is proper.
