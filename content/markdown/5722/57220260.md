# Andreas Dress

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Dress"}

**Andreas Dress** (26 August 1938 – 23 February 2024) was a German mathematician specialising in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dress earned his PhD from the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) in 1962, under the supervision of [Friedrich Bachmann](wikipedia://en/59096172) and [Karl-Heinrich Weise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Heinrich_Weise). His thesis is entitled *Konstruktion metrischer Ebenen*.

Dress was a professor of mathematics at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) beginning in 1969.[^o6] He was the first director of the [Max Planck Partner Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society) for Computational Biology in Shanghai.[^o7] In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o8]

Dress died on 23 February 2024, at the age of 85.[^o7]
