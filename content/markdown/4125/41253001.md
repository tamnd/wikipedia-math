# Vicumpriya Perera

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vicumpriya Perera"}

**Vicumpriya Perera** ([Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): විකුම්ප්‍රිය පෙරේරා) is a Sri Lankan born mathematician, lyricist, poet and music producer.[^o3][^o4] He has published three books of Sinhala poetry, *Mekunu Satahan* (Sinhala: මැකුනු සටහන්) in 2001,[^o5] *Paa Satahan* (Sinhala: පා සටහන්) in 2013,[^o6][^o7] and *Mawbime Suwandha* (Sinhala: මව්බිමේ සුවඳ) in 2023.[^o8] He has written over 200 songs and has produced eleven Sinhala song albums. He currently works as a mathematics professor in [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio), US.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Vicumpriya Perera is originally from [Wattala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wattala), Sri Lanka. He is a graduate of St. Anthony's College, Wattala and [Ananda College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ananda_College), [Maradana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maradana), Sri Lanka. He received a Bachelor of Science degree in mathematics with first class honors from [University of Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colombo), [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka) and continued his graduate studies at [Indiana University - Purdue University at Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_-_Purdue_University_at_Indianapolis). He obtained a doctorate degree from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in [Pure Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_Mathematics) with research concentrating on [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) in 1993.[^o10]

Vicumpriya Perera lives in Ohio, US, where he has worked as a mathematics professor at [Kent State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University) (Trumbull campus) since 1998.[^o11][^o12] He works in [operator algebra](wikipedia://en/455987), which is an area of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210).

Vicumpriya Perera is a past president of the Ohio Mathematical Association of Two year Colleges (OhioMATYC),[^o13] the Ohio Affiliate organization of the American Mathematical Association of Two Year Colleges ([AMATYC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMATYC)). His term of presidency was from 2021 to 2023. He also serves as the immediate past president of the Colombo University Faculty of Science Alumni Association - North America (CUFSAA),[^o14] since the conclusion of his term as president of the association (2021–2023).

## Other productions {#Other_productions}

In 2005, Vicumpriya Perera (along with [Nalin Jayawardena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalin_Jayawardena), and Jaanaka Wimaladharma) produced a compact disc set, *Dhammapadaya* (Sinhala: ධම්මපදය), under the Lanka Heritage label.[^o33] The set contained four discs, and consisted of complete the [Dhammapada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhammapada) stanzas in the original [Pali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali) language followed by the [Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language) translations chanted by venerable Beruwala Siri Sobhitha Thero of the Sri Lanka Buddhist [Vihara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vihara) in [Perth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perth), Australia. In 2006, this disc set had an English release called *Dhammapada*.[^o33] This version had the original Dhammapada stanzas (again in Pali) followed by the English translations written and rendered by Dr. [Gil Fronsdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Fronsdal),[^o34] director and resident teacher Insight Meditation Center, [Redwood City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redwood_City), California, US.

In 2012 Vicumpriya Perera (in collaboration with Nalin Jayawardena) produced a Sinhala [Audiobook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiobook) called *Kulageyin Kulageyata* (Sinhala: කුලගෙයින් කුලගෙයට) under the Lanka Heritage, LLC. The book was written in 2009 by Bhadraji Mahinda Jayatilaka, who provided most of the voice work . The audiobook has a total length of five compact discs, and was published by Sarasavi Publishers,[^o35] [Nugegoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nugegoda), Sri Lanka.
