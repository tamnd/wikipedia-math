# Umberto Zannier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Umberto Zannier"}

**Umberto Zannier** (born 25 May 1957, in [Spilimbergo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spilimbergo), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy)) is an Italian mathematician, specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Zannier earned a [Laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) degree from [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) and studied at the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) with Ph.D. supervised by [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475).

## Career {#Career}

Zannier was from 1983 to 1987 a researcher at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua), from 1987 to 1991 an associate professor at the [University of Salerno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salerno), and from 1991 to 2003 a full professor at the [Università IUAV di Venezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_IUAV_di_Venezia). From 2003 to the present he has been a Professor in Geometry at the Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa.[^o6]

In 2010 he gave the Hermann Weyl Lectures at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o7] He was a visiting professor at several institutions, including the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) in Paris, the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), and the [Erwin Schrödinger Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_Institute) in Vienna.

With [Jonathan Pila](wikipedia://en/31960316) he developed a method (now known as the Pila-Zannier method) of applying [O-minimality](wikipedia://en/30865852) to number-theoretical and algebro-geometric problems. Thus they gave a new proof of the [Manin–Mumford conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manin–Mumford_conjecture) (which was first proved by [Michel Raynaud](wikipedia://en/4760928) and [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981)). Zannier and [Pietro Corvaja](wikipedia://en/62816286) in 2002 gave a new proof of [Siegel's theorem on integral points](wikipedia://en/3989092) by using a new method based upon the [subspace theorem](wikipedia://en/8830237).[^o8]

## Awards & Service {#Awards_&_Service}

Zannier was an Invited Speaker at the 4th European Mathematical Congress in Stockholm in 2004. Zannier was elected a corresponding member of the [Istituto Veneto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Veneto_di_Scienze,_Lettere_ed_Arti) in 2004, a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei) in 2006, and a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2012.[^o6] In 2014 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).[^o10]

In 2005 Zannier received the Mathematics Prize of the [Accademia dei XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_XL) and in 2011 an Advanced Grant from the European Research Council (ERC). He is chief editor of the *Annali di Scuola Normale Superiore* and a co-editor of *[Acta Arithmetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Arithmetica)*.[^o6]
