# Simple homotopy theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simple homotopy theory"}

In mathematics, **simple homotopy theory** is a [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) (a branch of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801)) that concerns with the [simple-homotopy type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple-homotopy_type) of a space. It was originated by [J. H. C. Whitehead](wikipedia://en/385179) in his 1950 paper "Simple homotopy types".[^o2]

:::
