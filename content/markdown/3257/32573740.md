# Hautus lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hautus lemma"}

In [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and in particular when studying the properties of a [linear time-invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_time-invariant) system in [state space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_space) form, the **Hautus lemma** (after Malo L. J. Hautus), also commonly known as the **Popov-Belevitch-Hautus test** or **PBH test**,[^o2][^o3] gives equivalent conditions for certain properties of control systems.

:::

A special case of this result appeared first in 1963 in a paper by [Elmer G. Gilbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmer_G._Gilbert),[^o2] and was later expanded to the current PBH test with contributions by [Vasile M. Popov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasile_M._Popov) in 1966,[^o4][^o5] [Vitold Belevitch](wikipedia://en/23413599) in 1968,[^o6] and Malo Hautus in 1969,[^o6] who emphasized its applicability in proving results for linear time-invariant systems.

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

There exist multiple forms of the lemma:

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Hautus Lemma for controllability"}

### Hautus Lemma for controllability {#Hautus_Lemma_for_controllability}

The Hautus lemma for controllability says that given a square matrix $\mathbf{A}\in M_n(\Re)$ and a $\mathbf{B}\in M_{n\times m}(\Re)$ the following are equivalent:

1. The pair $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B})$ is [controllable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controllable)
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}]=n$
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ that are eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}]=n$

:::

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Hautus Lemma for stabilizability"}

### Hautus Lemma for stabilizability {#Hautus_Lemma_for_stabilizability}

The Hautus lemma for stabilizability says that given a square matrix $\mathbf{A}\in M_n(\Re)$ and a $\mathbf{B}\in M_{n\times m}(\Re)$ the following are equivalent:

1. The pair $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B})$ is [stabilizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizability)
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ that are eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$ and for which $\Re(\lambda)\ge 0$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}]=n$

:::

::: {#o14 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Hautus Lemma for observability"}

### Hautus Lemma for observability {#Hautus_Lemma_for_observability}

The Hautus lemma for observability says that given a square matrix $\mathbf{A}\in M_n(\Re)$ and a $\mathbf{C}\in M_{m\times n}(\Re)$ the following are equivalent:

1. The pair $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{C})$ is [observable](wikipedia://en/871280).
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A};\mathbf{C}]=n$
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ that are eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A};\mathbf{C}]=n$

:::

::: {#o16 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Hautus Lemma for detectability"}

### Hautus Lemma for detectability {#Hautus_Lemma_for_detectability}

The Hautus lemma for detectability says that given a square matrix $\mathbf{A}\in M_n(\Re)$ and a $\mathbf{C}\in M_{m\times n}(\Re)$ the following are equivalent:

1. The pair $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{C})$ is [detectable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detectability)
1. For all $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ that are eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$ and for which $\Re(\lambda)\ge 0$ it holds that $\operatorname{rank}[\lambda \mathbf{I}-\mathbf{A};\mathbf{C}]=n$

:::
