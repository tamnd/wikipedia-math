# Vikram Bhagvandas Mehta

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vikram Bhagvandas Mehta"}

**Vikram Bhagvandas Mehta** (August 15, 1946 – June 4, 2014) was an Indian mathematician who worked on algebraic geometry and vector bundles. Together with [Annamalai Ramanathan](wikipedia://en/32574686) he introduced the notion of [Frobenius split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_split) varieties, which led to the solution of several problems about [Schubert varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schubert_varieties).[^o2] He is also known to have worked, from the 2000s onward, on the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612). In 2002, he and Subramanian published a proof of a conjecture by [Madhav V. Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482)[^o3] that brought back into the limelight the theory of this object that until then had met with little success.[^o4]

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

The [Council of Scientific and Industrial Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Scientific_and_Industrial_Research) awarded him the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in 1991 for his work in algebraic geometry.[^o6]
