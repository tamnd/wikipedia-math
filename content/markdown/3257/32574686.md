# Annamalai Ramanathan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annamalai Ramanathan"}

**Annamalai Ramanathan** (29 August 1946 – 12 March 1993) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the field of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), who introduced the notion of [Frobenius splitting](wikipedia://en/32580947) of algebraic varieties jointly with [Vikram Bhagvandas Mehta](wikipedia://en/32577513) in (Mehta Ramanathan 1985). The notion of Frobenius splitting led to the solution of many classical problems, in particular a proof of the [Demazure character formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demazure_character_formula) and results on the equations defining [Schubert varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schubert_varieties) in general [flag manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_manifolds).

:::

## Research career {#Research_career}

Ramanathan got his BSc in mathematics at [Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramakrishna_Mission_Vivekananda_College), and was recruited to attend [TIFR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIFR), where he got his Ph.D. in mathematics in 1976. His thesis on moduli for principal bundles was published in 1996 in two papers in Proc. Indian Acad. Sci. three years after his death.[^o4]

Ramanathan, was a professor of mathematics at the [TIFR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in Bombay, India. He has also been employed at [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) and [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign). Ramanathan made significant contributions to many areas of mathematics, including moduli of vector bundles, [Gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155), algebraic geometry in positive characteristic and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

## Awards {#Awards}

The [Council of Scientific and Industrial Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Scientific_and_Industrial_Research) awarded he and his collaborator [Vikram Bhagvandas Mehta](wikipedia://en/32577513) the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) (the Indian presidential award for achievement in the mathematical sciences) in 1991 for his work in algebraic geometry.

He was also elected a [Fellow of Indian Academy of Sciences](https://fellows.ias.ac.in/profile/v/FL1991029) in 1991.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

During his tenure as a visiting professor at [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign), Ramanathan died in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), Friday, 12 March 1993, of complications following treatment for a heart attack. He is survived by his wife RM. Vasantha and three daughters Lakshmi, Valli, and Priya.[^o7]
