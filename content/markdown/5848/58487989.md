# Jean-Benoît Bost

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Benoît Bost"}

*Image: Jean-Benoit Bost.jpg.* Jean-Benoit Bost, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2005

**Jean-Benoît Bost** (born 27 July 1961, in [Neuilly-sur-Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuilly-sur-Seine)) is a French mathematician.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

In 1977, Bost graduated from the [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand) and finished first in the [Concours général](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concours_général), a national competition. Bost studied from 1979 to 1983 (qualifying in 1981 for the *agrégation des mathématiques*) at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) (ENS), where he was from 1984 to 1988 *[agrégé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)-préparateur* (teacher) and worked under the direction of [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340).

## Career {#Career}

From 1988, Bost was *chargé de recherches* and from 1993 *directeur de recherches* at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Scientific_Research). From 1993 to 2006, he was *maître de conferences* at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique). He has been a professor at l'[Université Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Saclay) (Paris XI) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay) since 1998.

## Research {#Research}

Bost deals with [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) (partly in collaboration with [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340)) with applications to [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). The eponymous [Bost conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farrell-Jones_conjecture#Bost_conjecture) is a variant of the [Baum–Connes conjecture](wikipedia://en/11774498).[^o5]

His work with François Charles on Archimedean overflow[^o6] generalizes the arithmetic holonomicity theorem of [Calegari](wikipedia://en/63299027)-Dimitrov-[Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056).[^o7] It also establishes an arithmetic counterpart of theorems of Lefschetz and [Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482) on fundamental groups of complex surfaces.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1990, he received the Prix Peccot-Vimont of the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France). In 2002, he received the [Prix Élie Cartan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elie_Cartan_Prize) of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences). In 1986 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905) in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille).[^o9] In 2006, he was an invited speaker with talk *Evaluation maps, slopes, and algebraicity criteria* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid).[^o10]

From 2005 to 2015, Bost was a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France). He was elected in 2012 a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and in 2016 a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o11]
