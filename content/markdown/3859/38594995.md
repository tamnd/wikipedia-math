# Georges Gonthier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Gonthier"}

**Georges Gonthier** is a Canadian [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and practitioner in [formal mathematics](wikipedia://en/1197184). He led the formalization of the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949)[^o2] and [Feit–Thompson proof of the odd-order theorem](wikipedia://en/1656473).[^o3] (Both were written using the [proof assistant](wikipedia://en/1258607) [Rocq](wikipedia://en/581974).)

:::

In 2011, as a principal researcher at Microsoft Research Cambridge, he received the EADS Foundation Grand Prize in Computer Science, given jointly by the European Aeronautic Defence and Space Company and the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]
