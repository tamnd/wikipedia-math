# Wall theorems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wall theorems"}

In [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562) in mathematics, the **Wall theorems** are four results, which connect the [smooth structure](wikipedia://en/1542238), auto-[diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) and the [intersection form](wikipedia://en/19057150) of a smooth [4-manifold](wikipedia://en/1362795) with [h-cobordisms](wikipedia://en/681049), stable diffeomorphisms and induced form automorphisms. The theorems are named after [C. T. C. Wall](wikipedia://en/2613478), who proved them in 1964.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Wall's theorem on h-cobordisms"}

## Wall's theorem on h-cobordisms {#Wall's_theorem_on_h-cobordisms}

**Wall's theorem on h-cobordisms** claims, that [simply connected](wikipedia://en/523879) closed smooth 4-manifolds with isomorphic [intersection form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_form) are even [h-cobordant](wikipedia://en/681049).[^o4][^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Wall's theorem on stabilization"}

## Wall's theorem on stabilization {#Wall's_theorem_on_stabilization}

**Wall's theorem on stabilization** claims, that for h-cobordant simply connected closed smooth 4-manifolds $M$ and $N$, there exists a natural number $n\in\mathbb{N}$ and a [diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564) $M\#n(S^2\times S^2)\cong N\#n(S^2\times S^2)$.[^o8][^o9] Becoming diffeomorphic after [connected sums](wikipedia://en/490134) with $S^2\times S^2$ is also called stably diffeomorphic. A combination with Wall's theorem on h-cobordisms yields that simply connected smooth 4-manifolds with isomorphic intersection form are even stably diffeomorphic.

Since both the classification of smooth structures on 4-manifolds and the existence of [exotic 4-spheres](wikipedia://en/1114931) are important open problems in differential topology, it seems that a connection could exist using connected sums with exotic 4-spheres. However, if $\Sigma$ is an exotic 4-sphere, then Wall's theorem on stabilization guarantees the existence of a natural number $n\in\mathbb{N}$ and a diffeomorphism:

$$\Sigma\#n(S^2\times S^2) \cong S^4\#n(S^2\times S^2) \cong n(S^2\times S^2),$$

meaning that the connected sum with $\Sigma$ causes no change in the smooth structure.

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Wall's theorem on automorphisms"}

## Wall's theorem on automorphisms {#Wall's_theorem_on_automorphisms}

**Wall's theorem on automorphisms** claims, that for a symmetric [unimodular](wikipedia://en/593773) [bilinear form](wikipedia://en/449477) $Q\colon\mathbb{Z}^n\times\mathbb{Z}^n\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}$ with $\operatorname{rk}(Q)-|\sigma(Q)|\geq 4$ and two elements $x,y\in\mathbb{Z}$ with same divisibility, self-intersection $Q(x,x)=Q(y,y)$ and type, there exists an automorphism $f\colon\mathbb{Z}^n\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}^n$ with $f(x)=y$.[^o12]

Divisibility is the largest integer able to divide the element. Type refers to whether the element is characteristic or not. $\operatorname{rk}(Q)-|\sigma(Q)|$ is always even, hence the above condition only excludes definite forms with $\operatorname{rk}(Q)-|\sigma(Q)|=0$ and near-definite forms with $\operatorname{rk}(Q)-|\sigma(Q)|=2$. According to [Serre's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_classification), this includes only the hyperbolic form $H=Q_{S^2\times S^2} =\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ as well as $[+1]\oplus n[-1]$ and $n[+1]\oplus[-1]$.

:::

::: {#o14 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Wall's theorem on diffeomorphisms"}

## Wall's theorem on diffeomorphisms {#Wall's_theorem_on_diffeomorphisms}

**Wall's theorem on diffeomorphisms** claims, that for a simply connected smooth 4-manifold $M$ with [indefinite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indefinite_matrix) [intersection form](wikipedia://en/19057150) $Q_M$, every [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) of $Q_{M\#(S^2\times S^2)} \cong Q_M\oplus H$ (with the hyperbolic form $H=Q_{S^2\times S^2} =\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$) comes from a self-[diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564) on $M\#(S^2\times S^2)$.[^o15] This shows an important difference between smooth and [topological](wikipedia://en/2119193) 4-manifolds since the latter require no stabilization. For a simply connected topological 4-manifold $M$ with indefinite intersection form $Q_M$, every automorphism of $Q_M$ comes from a self-[homeomorphism](wikipedia://en/13660) on $M$.[^o15]

:::
