# Snub cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub cube"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub cube**, or **snub cuboctahedron**, is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847) with 38 faces: 6 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) and 32 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285). It has 60 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) and 24 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417). [Kepler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler) first named it in [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) as *cubus simus* in 1619 in his [Harmonices Mundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonices_Mundi). [H. S. M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328), noting it could be derived equally from the octahedron as the cube, called it **snub cuboctahedron**, with a vertical extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $s \scriptstyle\begin{Bmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{Bmatrix}$, and representing an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of a [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879), which has Schläfli symbol $t \scriptstyle\begin{Bmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{Bmatrix}$.

:::

The snub cube, like the [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137), is [chiral](wikipedia://en/32703814), which means it does not equal its mirror image; it has two equally valid forms.

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The snub cube can be generated by taking the six faces of the cube, [pulling them outward](wikipedia://en/6341070) so they no longer touch, then giving them each a small rotation on their centers (all clockwise or all counter-clockwise) until the spaces between can be filled with [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285).

*Image: A5-A7.gif.* Process of snub cube's construction by rhombicuboctahedron

The snub cube may also be constructed from a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213). It started by twisting its square face (in blue), allowing its triangles (in red) to be automatically twisted in opposite directions, forming other square faces (in white) to be skewed quadrilaterals that can be filled in two equilateral triangles.[^n1]

The snub cube can also be derived from the [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879) by the process of [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117). 24 vertices of the truncated cuboctahedron form a polyhedron topologically equivalent to the snub cube; the other 24 form its mirror-image. The resulting polyhedron is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) but not uniform.

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) of a snub cube are all the [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of

$$\left(\pm 1, \pm \frac{1}{t}, \pm t \right),$$

with an even number of plus signs, along with all the [odd permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odd_permutation) with an odd number of plus signs, where $t \approx 1.83929$ is the [tribonacci constant](wikipedia://en/83982864). Taking the even permutations with an odd number of plus signs, and the odd permutations with an even number of plus signs, gives a different snub cube, the mirror image. Taking them together yields the [compound of two snub cubes](wikipedia://en/14797458).

This snub cube has edges of length $\alpha = \sqrt{2+4t-2t^2}$, a number which satisfies the equation

$$\alpha^6-4\alpha^4+16\alpha^2-32=0,$$

and can be written as

$$\begin{aligned} \alpha &= \sqrt{\frac{4}{3}-\frac{16}{3\beta}+\frac{2\beta}{3}}\approx1.609\,72 \\ \beta &= \sqrt[3]{26+6\sqrt{33}}. \end{aligned}$$

To get a snub cube with unit edge length, divide all the coordinates above by the value *α* given above.

## Properties {#Properties}

The snub cube is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847), meaning it is a highly symmetric and semi-regular polyhedron, and two or more different regular polygonal faces meet in a vertex. It is [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160), meaning there are two distinct forms whenever being [mirrored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_image). Therefore, the snub cube has the rotational [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805) $\mathrm{O}$. The polygonal faces that meet for every vertex are four equilateral triangles and one square, and the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of a snub cube is $3^4 \cdot 4$. The [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) of a snub cube is [pentagonal icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/725126), a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393). This is also chiral: In the notation of David McCooey for the two chiral forms of each polyhedron, the dual of a dextro snub cube is a laevo pentagonal icositetrahedron and the dual of a laevo snub cube is a dextro pentagonal icositetrahedron.[^o9]

For a snub cube with edge length $a$, whether the form is left- or right-chiral, its surface area $A$ and volume $V$ are:

$$A = \left(6+8\sqrt{3}\right)a^2 \approx 19.856a^2, \qquad V = \frac{8t+6}{3\sqrt{2(t^2-3)}}a^3 \approx 7.889a^3.$$

The [circumscribed radius](wikipedia://en/991784) $r_\text{c}$ (the radius of a sphere tangent to the vertices) and the [midscribed radius](wikipedia://en/4709281) $r_\text{m}$ (the radius of a sphere tangent to the edges) of a snub cube are formulated in terms of the tribonacci constant:[^o10]

$$r_\text{c} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{t^3 +1}, \quad r_\text{m} =\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{t^3}.$$

The snub cube has a square radius center $r_4$ and a triangle radius center $r_3$, formulated as:

$$r_4 = \frac12 \sqrt{t^3 -1}, \quad r_3 =\tfrac12 \sqrt{t^3 -\tfrac13 }.$$

The snub cube has two [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046) (an angle that is formed by two polygonal faces). Respectively, these angles are formed by a square and a triangle and two adjacent triangles

$$\arccos\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} (2 -t^2) \right), \quad \arccos\left( \tfrac13 (1 -2t) \right).$$

A snub cube has a [subtended angle](wikipedia://en/1999527) by an edge from the center

$$\arccos\left( (t^2 -2)^{-1} \right).$$

## Appearance {#Appearance}

A snub cube is at the fountain of [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).

In the study of [supramolecular chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supramolecular_chemistry), the snub cube is an application of an artificial polyhedron to mimic the structure of [viral capsids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_capsid) and a protein of [ferritin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferritin).
