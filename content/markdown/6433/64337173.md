# Frédéric Marty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frédéric Marty"}

**Frédéric Ladislas Joseph Marty** (23 June 1911 in [Albi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albi), [Tarn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarn_%28department%29) – 14 June 1940, [Gulf of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Finland))[^o2][^o3] was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Frédéric Marty's father was the mathematician Joseph Marty (1885–1914), who taught at the lycée d'Albi and as a French army officer was killed in action in WW I.[^o4]

Frédéric Marty received his doctorate in 1931 from the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) (ENS). After that he was a *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* at [Aix-Marseille University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University). He was a [French Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Air_Force) lieutenant in WW II and was a victim of the [Aero Flight 1631 shootdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleva_%28airplane%29) when he was a diplomatic courier on board a Finnish plane that was shot down by the [Soviet Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Air_Force).

Frédéric Marty, who entered the ENS in 1928, wrote, between 1931 and 1937, several papers on the distribution of the values of a meromorphic function, algebraic functions, and coverings. He was one of the devotees of the Hadamard Seminar and he also wrote two talks for the Julia Seminar.[^o5]

Marty is known in the theory of [normal families](wikipedia://en/1480325) for [Marty's theorem](wikipedia://en/1480325#Criteria).[^o6][^o7] This theorem from his dissertation[^o8] states that for any family $F$ of [meromorphic functions](wikipedia://en/80857), $F$ is normal if and only if $F$'s derived family of spherical derivatives is locally bounded. Marty also founded[^o9] the theory of hypergroups and [hyperstructures](wikipedia://en/1938461).[^o10] He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematics) (ICM) 1936 in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo).[^o11]
