# Rinad Yulmukhametov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rinad Yulmukhametov"}

**Rinad Salavatovich Yulmukhametov** (Ринад Салаватович Юлмухаметов; born 17 January 1957) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Yulmukhametov was born in the village Urge Atkol in [Ishimbaysky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishimbaysky_District), now in [Bashkortostan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkortostan). He graduated from the Department of Mathematics of [Bashkir State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkir_State_University) and defended his doctoral thesis in 1987. 1995-2009 he is the head of the [Department of Programming and Economic Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Programming_and_Economic_Informatics).

## Advances in science {#Advances_in_science}

1. Existence theorems for entire functions with given asymptotic behavior;
1. Solved the problem of spectral synthesis in the space of solutions of a homogeneous convolution equation;
1. Solved factorization problem Ehrenpreis.
