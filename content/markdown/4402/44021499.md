# Hermann Boerner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Boerner"}

**Hermann Boerner** (or **Börner**[^o3]) (11 July 1906 – 3 June 1982) was a German mathematician who worked on [variation calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variation_calculus), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and [group representation](wikipedia://en/12695) theory.

:::
