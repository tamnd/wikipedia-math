# Michael Stillman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Stillman"}

**Michael Eugene Stillman** (born March 24, 1957) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in computational [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He is a professor of mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). He is known for being one of the creators (with Daniel Grayson) of the [Macaulay2](wikipedia://en/3184149) computer algebra system.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Michael Stillman completed his PhD at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1983 under the direction of [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594). He had postdoctoral positions at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University), and the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) before moving to a permanent position at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1987.

Stillman is best known for his work on computer algebra systems. In 1983, he began work with [Dave Bayer](wikipedia://en/1442686) on the [Macaulay computer algebra system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaulay_computer_algebra_system), which they continued to improve until 1993. To get beyond several limitations in the design of Macaulay, Stillman and Daniel Grayson began work on the [Macaulay2](wikipedia://en/3184149) system in 1993.[^o4] Macaulay2 remains in active development as of 2019,[^o5] and has been cited in over 4350 articles.[^o6]

Stillman has over 30 mathematical publications, and has advised 11 PhD students.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 2015 Stillman was selected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his work in symbolic computation[^o10] (such as that on Macaulay2).
- Stillman was recognized for his teaching by [Business Insider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Insider) in a 2013 feature on the Best Colleges in America,[^o11] where he was named as one of the top 10 professors at Cornell.
