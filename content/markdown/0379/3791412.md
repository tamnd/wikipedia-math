# Johannes van der Corput

Van der CorputVan der Corput

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes van der Corput"}

**Johannes Gaultherus van der Corput** (4 September 1890 – 13 September 1975) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in the field of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

He was appointed professor at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) (Switzerland) in 1922, at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) in 1923, and at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 1946. He was one of the founders of the [Mathematisch Centrum](wikipedia://en/506063) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), of which he also was the first director. From 1953 on, Van der Corput worked in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), and the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).

Van der Corput introduced the [Van der Corput lemma](wikipedia://en/24993240), a technique for creating an upper bound on the measure of a set drawn from [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), and the [Van der Corput theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_der_Corput_theorem) on equidistribution modulo 1.

He became member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1929, and foreign member in 1953.[^o3] He was a Plenary Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1936 in Oslo.[^o4]
