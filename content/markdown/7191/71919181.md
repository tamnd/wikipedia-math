# Grzegorz Świątek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grzegorz Świątek"}

**Grzegorz Świątek** (born 1964) is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor at the [Warsaw University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University_of_Technology). He is known for his contributions to [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).

:::

Świątek earned his PhD from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) under supervision of [Michał Misiurewicz](wikipedia://en/29006092) in 1987.[^o2] Then he has held academic positions in [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) and the [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US) (including the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University)). He is currently a professor at the [Warsaw University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University_of_Technology).[^o3] He published his scientific work in such journals as *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*, *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)* and *[Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o4] With Jacek Graczyk he provided a rigorous proof of the real [Fatou conjecture](wikipedia://en/35338559).[^o5]

Świątek was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) in 1998,[^o6] and at the conference Dynamics, Equations and Applications in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) in 2019.[^o7]

In 2007, he received the [Stefan Banach Prize](wikipedia://en/2121791#Stefan_Banach_Prize) of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o8]
