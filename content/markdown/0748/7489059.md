# Constraint (information theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Constraint"}

**Constraint** in [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) is the degree of statistical dependence between or among variables.

:::

Garner[^o2] provides a thorough discussion of various forms of constraint (internal constraint, external constraint, total constraint) with application to [pattern recognition](wikipedia://en/126706) and [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology).
