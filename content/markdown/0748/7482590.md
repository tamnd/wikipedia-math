# Edwin Hewitt

the South African rugby union player

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edwin Hewitt"}

**Edwin Hewitt** (January 20, 1920, [Everett, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everett,_Washington) – June 21, 1999) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in abstract [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and for his discovery, in collaboration with [Leonard Jimmie Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Jimmie_Savage), of the [Hewitt–Savage zero–one law](wikipedia://en/6595367).

:::

He received his Ph.D. in 1942 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and served on the faculty of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) from 1954.

Hewitt pioneered the construction of the [hyperreals](wikipedia://en/51429) by means of an [ultrapower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrapower) construction (Hewitt, 1948).

Hewitt wrote the 1975 English translation of [A. A. Kirillov's](wikipedia://en/3772515) 1972 Russian [monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph) *Elements of the Theory of Representations* (Элементы Теории Представлений), and co-authored *Abstract Harmonic Analysis* with [Kenneth A. Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_A._Ross) (1st edn., 1st vol. in 1963; 1st edn., 2nd vol. in 1970), an extensive work in two volumes.
