# Nicolai Reshetikhin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolai Reshetikhin"}

**Nicolai Yuryevich Reshetikhin** (Николай Юрьевич Решетихин, born October 10, 1958, in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad), [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)) is a [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416), currently a professor of mathematics at [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) and a professor of mathematical physics at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) ([Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/66341573)). He is also a professor emeritus at the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California), [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His research is in the fields of [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [quantum groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_groups). His major contributions are in the theory of quantum integrable systems, in representation theory of quantum groups and in [quantum topology](wikipedia://en/28075395). He and [Vladimir Turaev](wikipedia://en/56815253) constructed invariants of 3-manifolds which are expected to describe quantum Chern–Simons field theory introduced by [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894).

:::

He earned his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) and [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) from [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University) in 1982, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) in 1984.

He gave a [plenary lecture at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010.[^o3] He was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to the theory of quantum groups, integrable systems, topology, and quantum physics".[^o4]

In 2022, Reshetikhin received the inaugural [Weyl-Wigner Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Colloquium_on_Group_Theoretical_Methods_in_Physics#Weyl-Wigner_Award) from [ICGTMP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Colloquium_on_Group_Theoretical_Methods_in_Physics).
