# Totative

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Totative"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **totative** of a given positive integer $n$ is an integer $k$ such that $0 < k \le n$ and $k$ is [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to $n$. [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452) φ(*n*) counts the number of totatives of *n*. The totatives under multiplication modulo *n* form the [multiplicative group of integers modulo *n*](wikipedia://en/1195234).

:::

## Distribution {#Distribution}

The distribution of totatives has been a subject of study. [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) conjectured that, writing the totatives of *n* as

$$0 < a_1 < a_2 \cdots < a_{\phi(n)} < n ,$$

the mean square gap satisfies

$$\sum_{i=1}^{\phi(n)-1} (a_{i+1}-a_i)^2 < C n^2 / \phi(n)$$

for some constant *C*, and this was proven by [Bob Vaughan](wikipedia://en/4871951) and [Hugh Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Montgomery_%28mathematician%29).[^o3]
