# Graphocentrism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graphocentrism"}

**Graphocentrism** or **scriptism** is a typically unconscious interpretative bias in which [writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writing) is privileged over [speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech).[^o2][^o3]

:::

[Biases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biases) in favor of the written or printed word are closely associated with the ranking of [sight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sight) above [sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound), the [eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye) above the [ear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ear), which has been called 'ocularcentrism'.[^o4] It opposes [phonocentrism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonocentrism), which is the bias in favor of speech.
