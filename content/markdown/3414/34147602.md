# Jeremy Quastel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeremy Quastel"}

**Jeremy Daniel Quastel** , is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes) and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). He currently University Professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics), and previously served as department head from 2017 until 2021.[^o3].

:::

## Career {#Career}

Quastel grew up in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver), [British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Columbia). He earned his PhD at [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1990; his advisor was [S. R. Srinivasa Varadhan](wikipedia://en/9638038). He was a postdoc at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in Berkeley, then a faculty member at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) for the next six years.[^o11] He returned to Canada in 1998.[^o15]

## Research {#Research}

Quastel is recognized as one of the top probabilists in the world in the fields of [hydrodynamic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamic), [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239), and integrable probability.[^o11] In particular, his research is on the large scale behaviour of interacting particle systems and stochastic partial differential equations.[^o15] Together with Konstantin Matetski and Daniel Remenik, Quastel gave an exact formulation of the [KPZ fixed point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KPZ_fixed_point) in terms of its transition probabilities.[^o6]

## Awards, distinctions, and recognitions {#Awards,_distinctions,_and_recognitions}

- [Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Sciences_and_Engineering_Research_Council) (NSERC) John C. Polanyi Award (2025)[^o8]
- [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (2021)[^o9]
- [CMS Jeffery–Williams Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffery-Williams_Prize) (2019)[^o10]
- [CRM-Fields-PIMS prize](wikipedia://en/19284033) (2018)[^o11]
- [Royal Society of Canada Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada) (2016)[^o12]
- Killam Research Fellowship (2013) for his research of stochastic processes and partial differential equations used to describe natural processes of change and evolution[^o13]
- invited speaker at the Current Developments in Mathematics (2011)[^o14]
- invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Hyderabad (2010)
- [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellows) (1996–98)[^o15]

## Family {#Family}

Jeremy Quastel is the grandson of biochemist [Juda Hirsch Quastel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juda_Hirsch_Quastel).
