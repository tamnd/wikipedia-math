# Reinhold Strassmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reinhold Strassmann"}

**Reinhold Strassmann** (or **Straßmann**) (24 January 1893 in Berlin – late October 1944 in [Auschwitz concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp)) was a German mathematician who proved [Strassmann's theorem](wikipedia://en/12742172). His Ph.D. advisor at [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg) was [Kurt Hensel](wikipedia://en/2242565).

:::

Born into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) family,[^o3] Strassmann refused to leave [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany), and he was eventually detained and deported to [Theresienstadt concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theresienstadt_concentration_camp) in 1943. On October 23, 1944, he was deported from Theresienstadt to [Auschwitz concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp), where he was murdered soon after.[^o4]

He was the son of the forensic pathologist [Fritz Strassmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Strassmann_%28physician%29).
