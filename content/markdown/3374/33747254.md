# Fritz Gassmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz Gassmann"}

**Fritz Gassmann** (1899–1990) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) mathematician and geophysicist.

:::

## Life {#Life}

His Ph.D. advisors at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) were [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya) and [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544). He was a geophysics professor at the ETH Zurich.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Gassmann is the eponym for the [Gassmann triple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gassmann_triple) and [Gassmann's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gassmann's_equation).
