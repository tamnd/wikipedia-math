# Kunita–Watanabe inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kunita–Watanabe inequality"}

In [stochastic calculus](wikipedia://en/228001), the **Kunita–Watanabe inequality** is a generalization of the [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality](wikipedia://en/38128) to integrals of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes). It was first obtained by Hiroshi Kunita and [Shinzo Watanabe](wikipedia://en/48084343) and plays a fundamental role in their extension of Ito's [stochastic integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_integral) to square-integrable martingales.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let *M*, *N* be continuous [local martingales](wikipedia://en/7302795) and *H*, *K* [measurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurable) processes. Then

$$\int_0^t \left| H_s \right| \left| K_s \right| \left| \mathrm{d} \langle M,N \rangle_s \right| \leq \sqrt{\int_0^t H_s^2 \,\mathrm{d} \langle M \rangle_s} \sqrt{\int_0^t K_s^2 \,\mathrm{d} \langle N \rangle_s}$$

where the angled brackets indicates the [quadratic variation and quadratic covariation](wikipedia://en/2143889) operators. The integrals are understood in the [Lebesgue–Stieltjes](wikipedia://en/368525) sense.

:::
