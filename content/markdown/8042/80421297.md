# Constantin Teleman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Constantin Teleman"}

**Constantin S. Teleman** (born in 1968)[^o3] is a Romanian-American mathematician. He studies [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and [loop groups](wikipedia://en/152664) as well as their representations and their connections to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), notably [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).

:::

Teleman began studying at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1987 (that same year, he was a Putnam Fellow[^o4]) and obtained his master's degree in 1991.[^o5] He then received his doctoral degree in 1994 under the direction of [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457) (and [Graeme Segal](wikipedia://en/6713316) after he worked at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge)) with a thesis titled "Lie Algebra Cohomology and the Fusion Rules".[^o6] He was then the Szegö assistant professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and Research Fellow at St John's College at the University of Cambridge. In 1999, became an assistant professor at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) and lecturer in 2001, then in 2003 a reader for St John's College at Cambridge. In 2007, he was a professor at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh). He is now a professor at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley).[^o7]

## Prizes and honors {#Prizes_and_honors}

In 2002, he received the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) for his contributions to [representations](wikipedia://en/19378200) of infinite-dimensional groups, particularly loop groups. In 2015, he was a Senior [Berwick prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize) laureat along with [Daniel Freed](wikipedia://en/40895609) and [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569) for their article *Loop groups and twisted K-theory*.[^o9] They proved the [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) between the twisted equivariant [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) of a [compact Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) and the Verlinde algebra of its loop group.[^o10] In 2014, he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.[^o11]
