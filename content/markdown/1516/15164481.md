# Souček space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Souček space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Souček spaces** are generalizations of [Sobolev spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobolev_spaces), named after the [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Jiří Souček](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiří_Souček). One of their main advantages is that they offer a way to deal with the fact that the Sobolev space *W*<sup>1,1</sup> is not a [reflexive space](wikipedia://en/210091); since *W*<sup>1,1</sup> is not reflexive, it is not always true that a bounded sequence has a [weakly convergent](wikipedia://en/33662) [subsequence](wikipedia://en/236098), which is highly desirable in many applications.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let Ω be a [bounded domain](wikipedia://en/48258) in *n*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) with smooth [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824). The **Souček space** *W*<sup>1,*μ*</sup>(Ω; **R**<sup>*m*</sup>) is defined to be the space of all [ordered pairs](wikipedia://en/22362) (*u*, *v*), where

- *u* lies in the [Lebesgue space](wikipedia://en/45194) *L*<sup>1</sup>(Ω; **R**<sup>*m*</sup>);
- *v* (thought of as the gradient of *u*) is a [regular](wikipedia://en/3390453) [Borel measure](wikipedia://en/4322) on the [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of Ω;
- there exists a sequence of functions *u*<sub>*k*</sub> in the Sobolev space *W*<sup>1,1</sup>(Ω; **R**<sup>*m*</sup>) such that

$$\lim_{k \to \infty} u_{k} = u \mbox{ in } L^{1} (\Omega; \mathbf{R}^{m})$$

and

$$\lim_{k \to \infty} \nabla u_{k} = v$$

[weakly-∗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_convergence) in the space of all [**R**<sup>*m*×*n*</sup>-valued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector-valued_measure) regular Borel measures on the closure of Ω.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The Souček space *W*<sup>1,*μ*</sup>(Ω; **R**<sup>*m*</sup>) is a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) when equipped with the [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) given by

$$\| (u, v) \| := \| u \|_{L^{1}} + \| v \|_{M},$$

i.e. the sum of the *L*<sup>1</sup> and [total variation](wikipedia://en/683561) norms of the two components.

:::
