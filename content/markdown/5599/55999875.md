# Therese Biedl

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Therese Biedl"}

**Therese Charlotte Biedl** is an Austrian [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) known for her research in [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) and [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813). Currently she is a professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in Canada.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Biedl received her Diploma in Mathematics at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), graduating in 1996 and earned a Ph.D. from [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in 1997 under the supervision of Endre Boros.[^o3][^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Biedl's research is in developing algorithms related to graphs and geometry. [Planar graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planar_graphs) are graphs that can be drawn without crossings. Biedl develops algorithms that minimize or approximate the [area](wikipedia://en/43176265) and the height of such drawings.A With Alam, Felsner, Gerasch, Kaufmann, and Kobourov, Biedl found provably optimal [linear time algorithms](wikipedia://en/405944#Linear_time) for proportional contact representation of a maximal planar graph.C

## Awards {#Awards}

Biedl was named a Ross & Muriel Cheriton Faculty Fellow in 2011, a recognition of the reach and importance of her scholarly works.[^o9]
