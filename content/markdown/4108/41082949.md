# Pierre Schapira (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Schapira"}

*Image: Pierre Schapira ICM 2018 (30584816458).jpg.* Pierre Schapira at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 2018

**Pierre Schapira** (born April 28, 1943) is a French mathematician. He specializes in [algebraic analysis](wikipedia://en/20846707), especially [Mikio Sato](wikipedia://en/373336)'s [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841), together with the mathematical concepts of [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) and [derived categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_categories).

:::

Schapira received his doctorate for work on [hyperfunctions](wikipedia://en/1678626). Although these were already in use in France by [André Martineau](wikipedia://en/50811617), they were further developed by Schapira and [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500). This work earned Shapira an invitation to [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), where he met [Masaki Kashiwara](wikipedia://en/4376209). Together, they developed the microlocal theory of sheaves,[^o2] and have co-authored many papers spanning several decades.

He served as a professor at the [Paris 13 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_13_University) in the 1980s and has been a professor at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) since the 1990s.

In 1990, he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto), speaking on sheaf theory for [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o3]

Schapira was inducted as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) with the Society's inaugural class of Fellows in 2013.[^o4]
