# Victor Panaretos

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Panaretos"}

**Victor Michael Panaretos** (born 1982) is a Greek [mathematical statistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Statistician). He is currently Professor and Director at the Institute of Mathematics of the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL), where he holds the chair of [Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Statistics).

:::

Panaretos works at the interface of [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), [random processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_process), and [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813). He is known for contributions to the [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368) of random functions, operators, and measures.[^o4] He has published widely in statistical theory and methods as well as in applied probability, and is the author of two books.[^o5][^o6]

## Education {#Education}

Panaretos received his PhD from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2007, advised by [David R. Brillinger](wikipedia://en/70523091).[^o2][^o8] His doctoral thesis was awarded the [Erich Lehmann](wikipedia://en/16388476) award.[^o9]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After graduation Panaretos took up a faculty position at [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL), becoming the youngest person ever to be appointed to a chair at the institution.[^o11] He is an elected member[^o12] of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow)[^o13] of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). He was a recipient of a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC) Starting Grant Award in 2010,[^o14] and the 2019 [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) Forum Lecturer.[^o15]

An active member of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) and its parent organization, the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), Panaretos has served the probability and statistics community from several posts, notably as the editor of Bernoulli News,[^o16] a member of the ISI Council,[^o17] and as [President-Elect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President-Elect) of the Bernoulli Society.[^o18]

Panaretos has served on the [editorial boards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of the *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)*, the *[Annals of Applied Statistics](wikipedia://en/36006136)*, *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178)*, the *[Electronic Journal of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Statistics)*, and the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)*.
