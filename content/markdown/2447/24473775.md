::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Cantellated 5-simplexes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 5-simplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) of the regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex).

:::

There are unique 4 degrees of cantellation for the 5-simplex, including truncations.

The **cantellated 5-simplex** has 60 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 240 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 290 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) (200 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 90 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29)), 135 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) (30 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), 30 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), 15 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280) and 60 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584)), and 27 [4-faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-face) (6 [cantellated 5-cell](wikipedia://en/6397293), 6 [rectified 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2720430), and 15 [tetrahedral prisms](wikipedia://en/5977290)).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cantellated hexateron
- Small rhombated hexateron (Acronym: sarx) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *cantellated 5-simplex* can be most simply constructed on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 6-space as permutations of (0,0,0,1,1,2) *or* of (0,1,1,2,2,2). These represent positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [*cantellated hexacross* and *bicantellated hexeract*](wikipedia://en/6821728#Coordinates_2) respectively.

## Related uniform 5-polytopes {#Related_uniform_5-polytopes}

The cantellated 5-simplex, sarx, as well as sibrid, garx, and gibrid are in a set of 19 [uniform 5-polytopes](wikipedia://en/31337632) based on the \[3,3,3,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004), all shown here in A<sub>5</sub> [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052). (Vertices are colored by projection overlap order, red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple having progressively more vertices)
