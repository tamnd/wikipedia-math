# Abelian Lie group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abelian Lie group"}

In geometry, an **abelian Lie group** is a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) that is an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974).

:::

A connected abelian real Lie group is isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^k \times (S^1)^h$.[^n1] In particular, a connected abelian (real) [compact Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group) is a [torus](wikipedia://en/74800); i.e., a Lie group isomorphic to $(S^1)^h$. A connected [complex Lie group](wikipedia://en/2187847) that is a compact group is abelian and a connected compact complex Lie group is a [complex torus](wikipedia://en/3055042); i.e., a quotient of $\mathbb{\mathbb{C}}^n$ by a lattice.

Let *A* be a compact abelian Lie group with the identity component $A_0$. If $A/A_0$ is a [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327), then $A$ is topologically cyclic; i.e., has an element that generates a dense subgroup.[^n2] (In particular, a torus is topologically cyclic.)
