# Zeeman conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zeeman conjecture"}

In mathematics, the **Zeeman conjecture** or **Zeeman's collapsibility conjecture** asks whether given a finite [contractible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contractible) 2-dimensional [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) $K$, the space $K\times [0,1]$ is [collapsible](wikipedia://en/20814602). It can nowadays be restated as the claim that for any 2-complex $G$ which is homotopy equivalent to a point, some [barycentric subdivision](wikipedia://en/583651) of $G \times [0, 1]$ is collapsible.[^o2]

:::

The conjecture, due to [Christopher Zeeman](wikipedia://en/871415), implies the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) and the [Andrews–Curtis conjecture](wikipedia://en/7754592).
