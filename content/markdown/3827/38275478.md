# Projection formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Projection formula"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **projection formula** states the following:[^o2][^o3]

:::

For a morphism $f:X\to Y$ of [ringed spaces](wikipedia://en/248014), an $\mathcal{O}_X$-module $\mathcal{F}$ and a [locally free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_free_sheaf) $\mathcal{O}_Y$-module $\mathcal{E}$ of finite rank, the natural maps of sheaves

$$R^i f_* \mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{E} \to R^i f_* (\mathcal{F} \otimes f^* \mathcal{E})$$

are isomorphisms.

There is yet another projection formula in the setting of [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231).
