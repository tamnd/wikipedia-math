# List of polling organizations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of polling organizations"}

This is a list of notable [polling](wikipedia://en/277315) organizations by country. All the major [television networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_network), alone or in conjunction with the largest newspapers or magazines, in virtually every country with elections, operate their own versions of polling operations, in collaboration or independently through various applications.

:::

Several organizations try to monitor the behavior of polling firms and the use of polling and statistical data, including the [Pew Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pew_Research_Center) and, in Canada, the Laurier Institute for the Study of Public Opinion and Policy.[^o2]

## Australia {#Australia}

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="ACNielsen"}

- [ACNielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACNielsen) – formerly published in [Fairfax Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfax_Media) newspapers, withdrawn in 2014[^o16]

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Essential Media Communications"}

- [Essential Media Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essential_Media_Communications) – formerly published on [Crikey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crikey) website, now in *[Guardian Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Australia)*[^o16]

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="MRP modelling"}

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Galaxy Research"}

- [Galaxy Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_Research) – now part of YouGov[^o16]

- [Ipsos Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipsos_Australia) – published in [Nine Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Entertainment)'s newspapers, *[Sydney Morning Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Morning_Herald)*, *[The Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Age)* and the *[Australian Financial Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Financial_Review)*,[^o16] but withdrew from political polling after an unpredicted result in the [2019 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Australian_federal_election),[^o9] although continues the relationship with other polls[^o10]

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Newspoll"}

- [Newspoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newspoll) – published in [News Corp Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_Corp_Australia)'s *[The Australian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Australian)* newspaper[^o16]

:::

- [Pyxis Polling and Insights](https://pyxispolling.com/) - Contracted to conduct Newspoll for the Australian, also conducts internal polling for the Australian Labor Party and corporate clients.

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="ReachTEL"}
