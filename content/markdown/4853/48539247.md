# Pooh-pooh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pooh-pooh"}

To **pooh-pooh** an [argument](wikipedia://en/13018310) is to dismiss it as being unworthy of serious consideration. It is an [informal fallacy](wikipedia://en/1655450).[^o2]

:::

Scholars generally characterize the fallacy as a rhetorical device in which the speaker ridicules an argument without responding to the substance of the argument.[^o3]

Some authors have also described the fallacy as the act of "ridicul\[ing\]" an argument as though it were "a myth",[^o4] and some characterize it as the act of dismissing an argument "with insults without responding to its substance in any way".[^o3] Other authors describe the fallacy as the act of dismissing an argument "with the wave of a hand".[^o5] Some sources also suggest the fallacy is an expression that involves "sneer\[ing\]",[^o5] "ridicule",[^o2] or "malicious comments about the proponent of the argument".[^o3] Some authors also suggest the term originated as a "representation of the act of spitting in sign of contemptuous rejection".[^o6]
