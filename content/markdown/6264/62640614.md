# Stephen A. Fulling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen A. Fulling"}

**Stephen Albert Fulling** (born 29 April 1945, [Evansville, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_Indiana)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist), specializing in the [mathematics of quantum theory](wikipedia://en/20728), [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), and the [spectral](wikipedia://en/506713) and [asymptotic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_theory) of [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886).[^o3] He is known for preliminary work that led to the discovery of the hypothetical [Unruh effect](wikipedia://en/691838) (also known as the Fulling-Davies-Unruh effect).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After secondary education at Missouri's [Lindbergh High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindbergh_High_School_%28Missouri%29),[^o8] Fulling graduated in 1967 with A.B. in physics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). At [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) he became a graduate student in physics and received M.S. in 1969 and Ph.D. in 1972.[^o6] His thesis *Scalar Quantum Field Theory in a Closed Universe of Constant Curvature* was supervised by [Arthur Wightman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Wightman). Fulling was a postdoc from 1972 to 1974 at the [University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Milwaukee) and from 1974 to 1976 at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London). At [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) he joined the mathematics faculty in 1976[^o8] and was promoted to full professor in 1984. In addition to mathematics, he holds a joint appointment in physics and astronomy.[^o6]

In addition to more than a hundred papers and publications, he has authored two books, *Aspects of Quantum Field Theory in Curved Space-Time* ([Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), 1989) and *Linearity and the Mathematics of Several Variables* ([World Scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Scientific), 2000).[^o8]

In 2018 Fulling was elected a fellow of the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society).[^o8] He has also been elected a foreign member of the [Royal Society of Sciences in Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Sciences_in_Uppsala).[^o9]
