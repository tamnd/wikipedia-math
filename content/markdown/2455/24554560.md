# John P. Burgess

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="John P. Burgess"}

**John Patton Burgess** (born 5 June 1948) is an American philosopher. He is John N. Woodhull Professor of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) where he specializes in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Burgess received his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley)'s Group in Logic and Methodology of Science. His interests include [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) and selected topics in [metaethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaethics) and [philosophy of mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_mind). He is the author of numerous articles on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), and the history of [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211). In 2012, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5] In 2026 he delivered the 37th [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431). He is the brother of [Barbara Burgess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Burgess).

- \2022. *Set Theory*, Cambridge Elements, Cambridge University Press.
- \2024. *A Logician's Perspective on the Relation Between the Mind and Body: Mentality and Modality*, Cambridge Scholars Publishing.
