# Nonlinear Dynamics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nonlinear Dynamics"}

***Nonlinear Dynamics*** is a semimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering all aspects of research on [nonlinear phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_phenomena) in engineering and applied sciences. The journal is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).[^o3] The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Walter Lacarbonara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Lacarbonara) ([Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome)).[^o4]

:::

## Related activities {#Related_activities}

### NODYCON conference series {#NODYCON_conference_series}

Selected papers presented at the NODYCON (Nonlinear Dynamics Conference), a biennial international conference on nonlinear dynamics in engineering systems, have been published in special issues of the journal.[^o7]

### Cassyni seminars {#Cassyni_seminars}

Selected articles have been featured in [Cassyni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassyni), an online seminar platform for discussions of published research work.[^o9]
