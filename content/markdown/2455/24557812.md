# Kathrin Bringmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kathrin Bringmann"}

**Kathrin Bringmann** (born 8 May 1977) is a German [number theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theorist) in the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), who has made fundamental contributions to the theory of [mock theta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mock_theta_function).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kathrin Bringmann was born on 8 May 1977, in Muenster, Germany. She passed the State Examinations in Mathematics and Theology at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), Germany, in 2002, and obtained a Diploma in Mathematics at Würzburg in 2003. She received PhD in 2004 from [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) under the supervision of Winfried Kohnen.

During 2004–07, she was Edward Burr Van Vleck Assistant Professor with the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) where she began her collaboration with [Ken Ono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Ono). After briefly serving as an assistant professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), she joined the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne), Germany, as Professor.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bringmann has been awarded the Alfried Krupp-Förderpreis for Young Professors, a one-million-Euro prize instituted by the [Alfried Krupp von Bohlen und Halbach Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfried_Krupp_von_Bohlen_und_Halbach_Foundation). She is the third mathematician to win this prize.[^o5] She has also been awarded the [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) in 2009 for her contributions to "areas of mathematics influenced by the genius [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717)."[^o6][^o7]

She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2015.[^o8]

A book by Bringmann with [Amanda Folsom](wikipedia://en/59411356), [Ken Ono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Ono), and Larry Rolen, *Harmonic Maass Forms and Mock Modular Forms: Theory and Applications* (Amer. Math. Soc., 2018),[^o9] won the 2018 [Prose Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PROSE_Awards) for Best Scholarly Book in Mathematics from the [Association of American Publishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_American_Publishers).[^o10]
