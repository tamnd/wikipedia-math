# Tara S. Holm

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tara S. Holm"}

**Tara Suzanne Holm** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Holm graduated summa cum laude from [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College).[^o5] Holm received her Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2002 under the supervision of [Victor Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636). She went on to a three-year postdoc at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), before eventually joining the faculty at [Cornell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Holm became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

In 2013, Holm was awarded a [Simons Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o9]

In 2019, Holm was awarded the Sze/Hernandez Teaching prize at Cornell.[^o10]

In 2019, Holm was the [AWM/MAA Falconer Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM/MAA_Falconer_Lecturer) at MAA MathFest.[^o11]

From 2011-2013, Holm was an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Council member at large,[^o12] and from 2013-2016 she was a member of the American Mathematical Society Executive Committee.[^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Holm is married to Timothy Riley, also a mathematics professor at Cornell.[^o15] Through her marriage, she is a member of the [Cobbold family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobbold_family).[^o16]
