# William J. Ellison

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William J. Ellison"}

*Image: William John Ellison.jpg.* William John Ellison

**William John Ellison** (1943 - 16 March 2022[^o2]) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

Ellison studied at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), where he earned his bachelor's degree and then, after spending the academic year 1969/70 at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), his PhD in 1970 under [John Cassels](wikipedia://en/1709206) with thesis *Waring's and Hilbert's 17th Problems*. Subsequently, he became a postdoc at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux). In 1972 he received the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) and a [Lester Randolph Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Randolph_Ford_Award) for his article "Waring's Problem“,[^o4] an exposition of [Waring's problem](wikipedia://en/33569)
