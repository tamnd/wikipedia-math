# Hubert Lilliefors

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hubert Lilliefors"}

**Hubert Whitman Lilliefors** (June 14, 1928 – February 23, 2008 in [Bethesda, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda,_Maryland)) was an American statistician, noted for his introduction of the [Lilliefors test](wikipedia://en/2690426).

:::

Lilliefors received a BA in mathematics from [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) in 1952[^o2] and his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at the George Washington University in 1964 under the supervision of [Solomon Kullback](wikipedia://en/506420). At the same university, he was a professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) for 39 years. In the 1970s he participated in the randomizing process for the [US military draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscription_in_the_United_States).[^o3]

A memorial session in his honor was held at the [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662) in August 2009 in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)
