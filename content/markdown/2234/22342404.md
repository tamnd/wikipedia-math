# Tsallis distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tsallis distribution"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **Tsallis distribution** is a [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) derived from the maximization of the [Tsallis entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsallis_entropy) under appropriate constraints. There are several different families of Tsallis distributions, yet different sources may reference an individual family as "the Tsallis distribution". The [q-Gaussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-Gaussian) is a generalization of the Gaussian in the same way that [Tsallis entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsallis_entropy) is a generalization of standard [Boltzmann–Gibbs entropy](wikipedia://en/4701125) or [Shannon entropy](wikipedia://en/15445).[^o2] Similarly, if the domain of the variable is constrained to be positive in the [maximum entropy](wikipedia://en/1813193) procedure, the [q-exponential distribution](wikipedia://en/32195081) is derived.

:::

The Tsallis distributions have been applied to problems in the fields of [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology), [anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatomy), [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance), and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488). The distributions are often used for their [heavy tails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_tails).

Note that Tsallis distributions are obtained as [Box–Cox transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box–Cox_transformation)[^o3] over usual distributions, with deformation parameter $\lambda=1-q$. This deformation transforms exponentials into q-exponentials.

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Procedure"}

## Procedure {#Procedure}

In a similar procedure to how the [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462) can be derived using the standard Boltzmann–Gibbs entropy or Shannon entropy, the [q-Gaussian](wikipedia://en/32185446) can be derived from a maximization of the [Tsallis entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsallis_entropy) subject to the appropriate constraints.[^o6][^o7]

:::
