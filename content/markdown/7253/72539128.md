# Elja Arjas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elja Arjas"}

**Elja Arjas** (born February 9, 1943, in [Tampere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampere)) is a Finnish mathematician and statistician. He is professor emeritus at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Arjas studied mathematics at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) and graduated with a bachelor's degree in philosophy in 1964. He graduated with a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in mathematics and statistics in 1970 and received his doctorate in mathematics in 1972, under the supervision of [Olli Lokki](wikipedia://en/72547650) and [Gustav Elfving](wikipedia://en/31600984). He was a research fellow at the [Center for Operations Research and Econometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Operations_Research_and_Econometrics) at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain) until 1973, before moving back to Finland.[^o4]

Arjas was a professor of applied mathematics and statistics at the [University of Oulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oulu) between 1975 and 1997. Between 1992 and 1997, he worked as an academy professor at the [Academy of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Finland), and from 1997 to 2009 as a part-time professor of biometrics at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki) and as a research professor at the Institute of Health and Welfare. Arjas was a visiting professor at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) between 1978 and 1979, a visiting professor at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center) from 1984 to 1985.[^o4]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Arjas was elected a fellow of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1977, a fellow of the a member of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1982, and a member of the [Finnish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2001. He received an honorary doctorate from the [University of Oulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oulu) in 2006.[^o4]
