# William John Greenstreet

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="William John Greenstreet"}

**William John Greenstreet** (1861–1930) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was editor of *[The Mathematical Gazette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mathematical_Gazette)* for more than thirty years.[^n1]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Greenstreet was son of a Royal Artillery's Sergeant. He was educated at [Southwark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwark) and he entered [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge) in 1879, graduating there in 1883. Then he was mathematics professor in different schools in [Framlingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham), [East Riding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Riding_of_Yorkshire) and [Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff) before he became Head Master at [Marling School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marling_School) in 1891. In 1910 he retired to [Burghfield Common](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burghfield_Common) with the intention of devoting to literary work.[^n2]

Greenstreet was founding member of the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403) and he started the Association's Library given a large collection of books.[^n3]
