# Liao Shantao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Liao Shantao"}

[Liao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liao_%28surname%29)

**Liao Shantao** (; 4 January 1920 - 6 June 1997) was a Chinese mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Liao was born on January 4, 1920, to a family of farmers in [Hengshan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengshan_County), [Hunan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunan). His father was Liao Zihao () and his mother was Zeng Ping (). He attended Mingde Middle School and Changsha No. 1 High School in [Changsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changsha), capital of Hunan province.[^o4][^o5] In 1938 he was accepted to [National Southwestern Associated University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Southwestern_Associated_University) and graduated in 1941. After graduation, he taught at Mingde Middle School. He moved to Peking University in 1946 as an associate professor and then to [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) as a research assistant in 1948. He pursued advanced studies in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), earning his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1952. His doctoral dissertation was directed by [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905).[^o6] He did post-doctoral research at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 1953 to 1955.

Liao gave up the job that mathematician [Norman Steenrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Steenrod) had arranged for him in scientific research at Princeton University and returned to China in 1956 as a professor at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o7]

In 1986 he was elected a fellow of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences).

In 1991 he was elected an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Liao married Wang Hongyi () in 1942, the couple had three sons.

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1982 Second Class Prize of State Natural Science Award
- 1988 First Class Prize of State Natural Science Award
