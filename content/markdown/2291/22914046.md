# 3-fold

the plants known as "threefold"

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="3-fold"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **3-fold** or **threefold** is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/253251) 3.

:::

The [Mori program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori_program) showed that 3-folds have minimal models.
