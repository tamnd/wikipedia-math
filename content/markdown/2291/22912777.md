# András Frank

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="András Frank"}

Frank András

**András Frank** (born 3 June 1949) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), especially in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and [combinatorial optimisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_optimisation). He is director of the Institute of Mathematics of the [Faculty of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELTE_Faculty_of_Sciences) of the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest).

:::

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

Using the [LLL-algorithm](wikipedia://en/1735228), Frank, and his student, [Éva Tardos](wikipedia://en/2979149) developed a general method, which could transform some polynomial-time algorithms into strong polynomial.[^o4] He solved the problem of finding the minimum number of edges to be added to a given undirected graph so that in the resulting graph the edge-connectivity between any two vertices *u* and *v* is at least a predetermined number *f*(*u*,*v*).[^o5]

## Degrees, awards {#Degrees,_awards}

He received the Candidate of Mathematical Science degree in 1980, advisor: [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938),[^o7] and the Doctor of Mathematical Science degree (1990) from the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o8] He was awarded the [Tibor Szele](wikipedia://en/22826039) Prize of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) in 2002[^o9] and the [Albert Szent-Györgyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Szent-Györgyi) Prize in 2009.[^o10] In June 2009 the ELTE Mathematical Institute sponsored a workshop in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o10][^o11] He is a member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) (corresponding member since 2016, full member since 2022).[^o12]
