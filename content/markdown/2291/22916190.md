# András Sárközy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="András Sárközy"}

**András Sárközy** (born 1941-Jan-16 in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [analytic](wikipedia://en/251513) and [combinatorial number theory](wikipedia://en/21527#Combinatorial_number_theory), although his first works were in the fields of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis). He has the largest number of papers co-authored with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) (a total of 62); he has an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) of one. He proved the [Furstenberg–Sárközy theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furstenberg–Sárközy_theorem) that every sequence of [natural numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_numbers) with positive [upper density](wikipedia://en/1819983) contains two members whose difference is a full square. He was elected a corresponding member (1998), and a full member (2004) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). He received the [Széchenyi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Széchenyi_Prize) (2010). He is the father of the mathematician [Gábor N. Sárközy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gábor_N._Sárközy).
