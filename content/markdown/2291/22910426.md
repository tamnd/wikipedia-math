# Association for Logic, Language and Information

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Association for Logic, Language and Information"}

The **Association for Logic, Language and Information** (**FoLLI**) is an international, especially European, [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society). It was founded in 1991 "to advance the practicing of research and education on the interfaces between Logic, Linguistics, Computer Science and Cognitive Science and related disciplines." The [Journal of Logic, Language and Information](wikipedia://en/22773901) (JoLLI) is published under its auspices; it co-ordinates summer schools such as the European Summer School in Logic, Language and Information (ESSLLI), the *[North American Summer School in Logic, Language, and Information](https://folli.info/?page_id=49)* (NASSLLI), and the *International Conference and Second East-Asian School on Logic, Language and Computation* (EASLLC); and it awards the [E. W. Beth Dissertation Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._W._Beth_Dissertation_Prize) to outstanding dissertations in the fields of Logic, Language, and Information.[^o3]

:::

## Governance {#Governance}

The current president of FoLLI is [Sonja Smets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonja_Smets) (since 2024). The current management board consists of [Sonja Smets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonja_Smets) (president), [Benedikt Löwe](wikipedia://en/39430307) (vice president), Larry Moss (senior member & treasurer), [Pritty Patel-Grosz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pritty_Patel-Grosz) (secretary), [Nina Gierasimczuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Gierasimczuk), [Francesca Poggiolesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Poggiolesi), [Philippe Schlenker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippe_Schlenker), [Jakub Szymanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Szymanik) (ESSLLI Standing Committee Chair).[^o5] Past members of the FoLLI management board include [Louise McNally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louise_McNally) (secretary, 2022-2024).

Past Presidents include [Johan van Benthem](wikipedia://en/1498176) (1991–1995), [Wilfrid Hodges](wikipedia://en/1789771) (1995–1996), Erhard Hinrichs (1997–1998), [Paul Gochet](wikipedia://en/15963544) (1999–2001), Hans Uzskoreit (2002–2003), [Luigia Carlucci Aiello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigia_Carlucci_Aiello) (2004–2007), Michael Moortgat (2007–2012), [Ann Copestake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Copestake) (2012–2016), [Valentin Goranko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Goranko) (2016–2020), and Larry Moss (2020-2024).
