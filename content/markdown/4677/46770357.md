# Alfredo Noel Iusem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfredo Noel Iusem"}

**Alfredo Noel Iusem** (born 10 November 1949, in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires)) is an [Argentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina)-born [Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033).[^o3]

:::

He earned his Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1981 under the supervision of [George Bernard Dantzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Bernard_Dantzig).

He is a recipient of [Brazil's National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit_%28Brazil%29) in mathematics.[^o5] Since 2001, he has been a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

[Regina Burachik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_Burachik) was a student of his at [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708).[^o4]
