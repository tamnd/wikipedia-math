# Networks and Heterogeneous Media

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Networks and Heterogeneous Media"}

***Networks and Heterogeneous Media*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published bimonthly by AIMS Press, an operation of the American Institute of Mathematical Sciences. The journal was established in 2006 and focuses on [networks](wikipedia://en/766409), heterogeneous media, and related fields. The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Benedetto Piccoli ([Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University)).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*,[^o4] [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683), [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus),[^o5] [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences,[^o6] and [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded).[^o6] According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2013 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.952.[^o7]
