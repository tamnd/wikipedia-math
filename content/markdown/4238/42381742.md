# Hsu–Robbins–Erdős theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hsu–Robbins–Erdős theorem"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), the **Hsu–Robbins–Erdős theorem** states that if $X_1, \ldots ,X_n$ is a sequence of i.i.d. [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685) with zero mean and finite variance and

:::

$$S_n = X_1 + \cdots + X_n, \,$$

then

$$\sum\limits_{n \geqslant 1} P( | S_n | > \varepsilon n) < \infty$$

for every $\varepsilon > 0$.

The result was proved by [Pao-Lu Hsu](wikipedia://en/42380697) and [Herbert Robbins](wikipedia://en/745193) in 1947.

This is an interesting strengthening of the classical strong [law of large numbers](wikipedia://en/157055) in the direction of the [Borel–Cantelli lemma](wikipedia://en/44987). The idea of such a result is probably due to Robbins, but the method of proof is vintage Hsu.[^o2] Hsu and Robbins further conjectured in [^o3] that the condition of finiteness of the variance of $X$ is also a necessary condition for $\sum\limits_{n \geqslant 1} P(| S_n | > \varepsilon n) < \infty$ to hold. Two years later, the famed mathematician [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) proved the conjecture.[^o4]

Since then, many authors extended this result in several directions.[^o5]
