# Theograph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theograph"}

A **theograph** (or **theoplot**[^o2]) is a type of graph to visualise patient journeys.[^o3][^o4] It has also been used in education.[^o5]

:::

## Structure {#Structure}

Theographs are a type of longitudinal history or health and care event timeline in a graphical form. They show the contacts an individual has with different services over time. Time is shown on x-axis, while different symbols represent different types of contact on the y-axis.[^o4]
