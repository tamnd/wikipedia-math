# Brauner space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brauner space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) a **Brauner space** is a [complete](wikipedia://en/32339) [compactly generated](wikipedia://en/899382) [locally convex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space) $X$ having a sequence of compact sets $K_n$ such that every other compact set $T\subseteq X$ is contained in some $K_n$.

:::

Brauner spaces are named after [Kalman George Brauner](https://www.genealogy.math.ndsu.nodak.edu/id.php?id=32432), who began their study.[^n1] All Brauner spaces are [stereotype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereotype_space) and are in the stereotype duality relations with [Fréchet spaces](wikipedia://en/233801):[^n2][^n3]

- for any Fréchet space $X$ its stereotype dual space[^o5] $X^\star$ is a Brauner space,
- and vice versa, for any Brauner space $X$ its stereotype dual space $X^\star$ is a Fréchet space.

Special cases of Brauner spaces are [Smith spaces](wikipedia://en/40716202).

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- Let $M$ be a $\sigma$-compact [locally compact topological space](wikipedia://en/48632), and ${\mathcal C}(M)$ the [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) of all continuous functions on $M$ (with values in ${\mathbb R}$ or ${\mathbb C}$), endowed with the usual topology of uniform convergence on compact sets in $M$. The dual space ${\mathcal C}^\star(M)$ of [Radon measures](wikipedia://en/521933) with compact support on $M$ with the topology of uniform convergence on compact sets in ${\mathcal C}(M)$ is a Brauner space.
- Let $M$ be a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), and ${\mathcal E}(M)$ the [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) of all smooth functions on $M$ (with values in ${\mathbb R}$ or ${\mathbb C}$), endowed with the usual topology of uniform convergence with each derivative on compact sets in $M$. The dual space ${\mathcal E}^\star(M)$ of distributions with compact support in $M$ with the topology of uniform convergence on bounded sets in ${\mathcal E}(M)$ is a Brauner space.
- Let $M$ be a [Stein manifold](wikipedia://en/1053909) and ${\mathcal O}(M)$ the [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) of all holomorphic functions on $M$ with the usual topology of uniform convergence on compact sets in $M$. The dual space ${\mathcal O}^\star(M)$ of analytic functionals on $M$ with the topology of uniform convergence on bounded sets in ${\mathcal O}(M)$ is a Brauner space.

In the special case when $M=G$ possesses a structure of a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) the spaces ${\mathcal C}^\star(G)$, ${\mathcal E}^\star(G)$, ${\mathcal O}^\star(G)$ become natural examples of [stereotype group algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_algebra_of_a_topological_group#Stereotype_group_algebras).

- Let $M\subseteq{\mathbb C}^n$ be a complex [affine algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/320468). The space ${\mathcal P}(M)={\mathbb C}[x_1,...,x_n]/\{f\in {\mathbb C}[x_1,...,x_n]:\ f\big|_M=0\}$ of polynomials (or regular functions) on $M$, being endowed with the strongest locally convex topology, becomes a Brauner space. Its stereotype dual space ${\mathcal P}^\star(M)$ (of currents on $M$) is a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801). In the special case when $M=G$ is an [affine algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_algebraic_group), ${\mathcal P}^\star(G)$ becomes an example of a stereotype group algebra.
- Let $G$ be a compactly generated [Stein group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stein_group).[^o8] The space ${\mathcal O}_{\exp}(G)$ of all holomorphic functions of exponential type on $G$ is a Brauner space with respect to a natural topology.[^n4]
