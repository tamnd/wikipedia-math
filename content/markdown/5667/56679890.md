# Lucia Caporaso

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lucia Caporaso"}

*Image: Caporaso lucia.jpg.* Lucia Caporaso in [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669), 2006

**Lucia Caporaso** is an Italian mathematician, holding a professorship in mathematics at [Roma Tre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Tre_University). She was born in Rome, Italy, on 22 May 1965. Her research includes work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041) and [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Caporaso earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1989. She completed her Ph.D. at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1993. Her dissertation, *On a Compactification of the Universal Picard Variety over the Moduli Space of Stable Curves*, was supervised by [Joe Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251).

She became a Benjamin Pierce Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Harvard, a researcher at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata), an assistant professor at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and an associate professor at the [University of Sannio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sannio), before moving to Roma Tre as a professor in 2001. From 2013 to 2018, she has headed the Department of Mathematics and Physics at Roma Tre.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Caporaso was the 1998 winner of the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize).

She is an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking in the section on algebraic and complex geometry.
