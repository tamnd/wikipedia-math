# Eleny Ionel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eleny Ionel"}

**Eleny-Nicoleta Ionel** (born April 1969) is a Romanian mathematician whose research concerns [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), including the study of the [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291) and [Gopakumar–Vafa invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopakumar–Vafa_invariant). Among her most significant results are the proofs of Gopakumar-Vafa conjectures (joint with Thomas H. Parker et al.), and the proof of Getzler's conjecture, asserting vanishing in codimension at least *g* of the [tautological ring](wikipedia://en/46187545) of the [moduli space of genus-*g* curves](wikipedia://en/3010057).

:::

She is a professor of mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where she was chair of the mathematics department from 2016 to 2019.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ionel is from [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași). She is the daughter of Adrian Ionel, a professor at the [Ion Ionescu de la Brad University of Agricultural Sciences and Veterinary Medicine of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Ionescu_de_la_Brad_University_of_Agricultural_Sciences_and_Veterinary_Medicine_of_Iași). She attended the prestigious [Costache Negruzzi National College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costache_Negruzzi_National_College), graduating in 1987. She earned a bachelor's degree from [Alexandru Ioan Cuza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University) in 1991, and completed her Ph.D. in 1996 from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University). Her dissertation, *Genus One Enumerative Invariants in $\mathbf{P}^n$*, was supervised by Thomas H. Parker.

After postdoctoral research at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) and a position as [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), she joined the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) faculty in 1998, and moved to Stanford in 2004.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ionel is a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) and a Simons Fellow. She was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2002. She was selected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to symplectic geometry and the geometric analysis approach to Gromov–Witten Theory".
