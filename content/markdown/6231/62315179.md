# Roberto Longo (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Roberto Longo"}

**Roberto Longo** (born 9 May 1953) is an Italian mathematician, specializing in [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Roberto Longo graduated in Mathematics[^o5] at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1975 under the supervision of the mathematical physicist [Sergio Doplicher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Doplicher).[^o6] From 1975 to 1977 Longo was a [predoctoral fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predoctoral_fellow) of the [Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consiglio_Nazionale_delle_Ricerche) and later [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the Sapienza University of Rome, where he became an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in 1980. In 1987, he was nominated [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata), and since 2010, he has been the director of the [Center for Mathematics and Theoretical Physics](wikipedia://en/29305046) in Rome.

Between 1978 and 1979, he was [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) and the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). He has been a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor) in numerous research centers, including the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), the [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).[^o7]

Since 2024, Roberto Longo has been an Emeritus Professor at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata).

Longo is an expert in the theory of operator algebras and its applications to quantum field theory. His work influenced the structural analysis of quantum field theory, especially of [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774), and opened up to new developments in model-building methods of interest in [local quantum physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_quantum_physics).[^o8]

Roberto Longo is known in particular for his work with Sergio Doplicher on split inclusions of [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185)[^o9] and for having solved, independently with [Sorin Popa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorin_Popa), the Stone-Weierstrass conjecture for factorial states.[^o10] He also found the relationship between the statistical dimension[^o11] and the Jones index.[^o12] In a work with Yasuyuki Kawahigashi, Longo classified the discrete series of conformal chiral networks of von Neumann algebras.[^o13] Together with Vincenzo Morinelli and Karl-Henning Rehren, he also showed that particles with infinite spin cannot appear in a local theory.[^o14] His most recent works concern [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) and [information](wikipedia://en/14773) for infinite [quantum systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_system).[^o15]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1994 Longo was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.[^o17] He was invited speaker at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905) in 1981 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin),[^o18] in 1988 in [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea),[^o19] in 1994 in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989),[^o20] and in 2003 in [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon).[^o21] In 2004 he was Andrejewski Lecturer in Göttingen.[^o22] He was a plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in 2009 in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague)[^o23] and at *Strings 2018* in [Okinawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Prefecture).[^o24]

In 2013 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o25] and in 2021 a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o26] He was awarded in 2016 the [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award)[^o8][^o27] and in 2021 the XL medal from the [Accademia Nazionale delle Scienze detta dei XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_delle_Scienze_detta_dei_XL) for his in-depth and innovative research in operator algebras and in conformal field theory.[^o28] In 2013 the conference *Mathematics and Quantum Physics* at the [Lincei National Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincei_National_Academy) was dedicated to him on the occasion of his 60th birthday.[^o29] In 2008[^o30] and in 2015[^o31] he received two [Advanced Grants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council#Advanced_Grants) from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council).[^o32] In 2018 he was member of the sectional panel *Mathematical Physics* of the International Congress of Mathematicians in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).[^o33]
