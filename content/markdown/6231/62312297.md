# Ronald Rousseau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald Rousseau"}

**Ronald Rousseau** (born 1949 in [Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp)) is a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [information scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informetrics). He has obtained an international reputation for his research on indicators and citation analysis in the fields of [bibliometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliometrics) and [scientometrics](wikipedia://en/1235963).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ronald Rousseau obtained his doctorate in mathematics at the [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven) in 1977 and his doctorate in documentation and library science at the [UIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universitaire_Instelling_Antwerpen) in 1992. He was Professor of Mathematics at the Department of Industrial Science and Technology at the [KHBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Hogeschool_Brugge-Oostende) in [Ostend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostend), Belgium. Rousseau has focused his research on the development and use of indicators to measure the quality of research and main trends in science. He is an expert in [citation analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation_analysis) and research evaluation. In 1990 Ronald Rousseau and Leo Egghe wrote the first manual of bibliometrics.[^o4] Rousseau has published more than 200 scientific articles on various aspects of [bibliometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliometrics) and [scientometrics](wikipedia://en/1235963) in, among other journals, [Scientometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientometrics_%28journal%29), [Journal of the American Society for Information Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Society_for_Information_Science_and_Technology), and the [Journal of Informetrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Informetrics). He is considered to be one of the most productive writers in this discipline. Rousseau, along with [Leo Egghe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Egghe), received the [Derek de Solla Price award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_de_Solla_Price_Memorial_Medal) in 2001.[^o5] From 2007 till 2015, he was the seventh chairperson of [ISSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Scientometrics_and_Informetrics).[^o6] In 2015 he became co-editor-in-chief of the [Journal of Data and Information Science](https://content.sciendo.com/view/journals/jdis/jdis-overview.xml).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- For his mathematical work Ronald Rousseau was Laureate of the [Royal Belgian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Flemish_Academy_of_Belgium_for_Science_and_the_Arts), class Natural Sciences in 1979.
- Ronald Rousseau received the [Derek de Solla Price award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_de_Solla_Price_Memorial_Medal) together with Leo Egghe in 2001.
- He is an Honorary professor at the [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou), China (2015).[^o8][^o9]
- He is an Honorary professor at the [Henan Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Normal_University) in [Xinxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinxiang), China (2001).[^o10]
- He is a guest professor at the National Library of Sciences of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing), China.
- He is a Fellow of the Institute of Scientometrics in [Dharwad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharwad), India.
- He is a guest professor at the Library and Information Center, library of the [CAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in [Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu), China.
- He is a guest professor at [Nanjing Agricultural University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Agricultural_University) (NJAU) in [Nanjing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing), China.
- He is an Honorary Professor of the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent) in [Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canterbury), UK.
- Ronald Rousseau received the 2020 West Lake Friendship Award from [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang) Province, China.
- In 2021, he received the President's International Fellowship Inititative (PIFI) award as Distinguished Scientist from the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o11]
