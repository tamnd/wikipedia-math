# Transverse knot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transverse knot"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **transverse knot** is a smooth [embedding](wikipedia://en/52119) of a [circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circle_%28topology%29) into a three-dimensional [contact manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_manifold) such that the [tangent vector](wikipedia://en/191999) at every point of the knot is [transverse](wikipedia://en/22667049) to the contact plane at that point.

:::

Any [Legendrian knot](wikipedia://en/7404755) can be *C*<sup>0</sup>-perturbed in a direction transverse to the contact planes to obtain a transverse knot. This yields a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) between the set of [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) classes of transverse knots and the set of isomorphism classes of Legendrian knots [modulo](wikipedia://en/9259) negative Legendrian stabilization.
