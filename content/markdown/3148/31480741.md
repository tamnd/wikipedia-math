# Nilpotence theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nilpotence theorem"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **nilpotence theorem** gives a condition for an element in the [homotopy groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_group) of a [ring spectrum](wikipedia://en/21118494) to be [nilpotent](wikipedia://en/252235), in terms of the [complex cobordism](wikipedia://en/1033666) spectrum $\mathrm{MU}$. More precisely, it states that for any ring spectrum $R$, the kernel of the map $\pi_\ast R \to \mathrm{MU}_\ast(R)$ consists of nilpotent elements.[^o2] It was [conjectured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenel_conjectures) by (txt) and proved by (txt).

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Nishida's theorem"}

## Nishida's theorem {#Nishida's_theorem}

(txt) showed that elements of positive degree of the [homotopy groups of spheres](wikipedia://en/1104697) are nilpotent. This is a special case of the nilpotence theorem.
