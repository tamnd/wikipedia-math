# Takeo Wada

Academics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takeo Wada"}

**Takeo Wada** was a Japanese mathematician at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) working in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He suggested the [Lakes of Wada](wikipedia://en/5774599) to [Kunizo Yoneyama](wikipedia://en/48146254), who wrote about them and named them after Wada.

:::
