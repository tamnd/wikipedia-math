# Tee (symbol)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tee"}

The **tee** (**⊤**, `\top` in [LaTeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaTeX)), also called **down tack** (as opposed to the [up tack](wikipedia://en/25016871)) or **verum**,[^o2] is a [symbol](wikipedia://en/37673) used to represent:

:::

- The [truth value](wikipedia://en/161711) of being true in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), or a sentence (e.g., [formula](wikipedia://en/164040) in [propositional calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_calculus)) which is unconditionally true.[^o3][^o4][^o5] By definition, every [tautology](wikipedia://en/4495335) is [logically equivalent](wikipedia://en/169324) to the verum.
- The [top type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_type) in [type theory](wikipedia://en/40282).
- The [top element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_element) in [lattice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_theory).
- [Mixed radix](wikipedia://en/249726) encoding in the [APL programming language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APL_%28programming_language%29).
- A [lowered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raising_%28sound_change%29) [phonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonetic) in the [International Phonetic Alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet) and [phonetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonetics). In this usage, it is usually written under the primary IPA symbol.

A similar-looking superscript T may be used to mean the [transpose](wikipedia://en/173844) of a matrix.

## Encoding {#Encoding}

In [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode), the tee character is encoded as 22A4.[^o7] The symbol is encoded in [LaTeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaTeX) as `\top`.

A large variant is encoded as 27D9 in the Unicode block [Miscellaneous Mathematical Symbols-A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miscellaneous_Mathematical_Symbols-A).
