# Graduate Studies in Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graduate Studies in Mathematics"}

**Graduate Studies in Mathematics** (**GSM**) is a series of [graduate-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_School) [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbooks) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS). The books in this series are published in hardcover and e-book formats.[^o2]

:::
