# Shahriar Shahriari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shahriar Shahriari"}

**Shahriar Shahriari** (born May 30, 1956) is an American mathematician. He is the William Polk Russell Professor of Mathematics at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College).[^o2]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Shahriari was born on May 30, 1956, in [Tehran, Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran,_Iran), to Parviz and Zomorod Shahriari.[^o4] He attended [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College), graduating in 1977, and subsequently received his doctorate from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1986.[^o2]

## Career {#Career}

Shahriari began teaching at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College) in 1989.[^o2] In 2006, he published a [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) textbook titled *Approximately Calculus*.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1998, Shahriari shared the [Carl B. Allendoerfer Award](wikipedia://en/40343255) with Dan Kalman and Robert Mena for their paper "Variations on an irrational theme—Geometry, dynamics, algebra".[^o8] In 2015, he received a [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) [Deborah and Franklin Tepper Haimo Award for Distinguished Teaching in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Tepper_Haimo_Award_for_Distinguished_Teaching_in_Mathematics).[^o2]
