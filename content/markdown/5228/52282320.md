# Constructional system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Constructional system"}

A **constructional system** or a **constitution system** is a system of objects or concepts of a certain domain in which all objects or concepts of that domain can be logically constructed from a proper subset of those objects or concepts, called the *basis* of the system. [^o2]

:::

The notion of constructional systems can be traced back to [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), who wrote in 1914[^o3] that The supreme maxim in scientific philosophising is this: wherever possible logical constructions are to be substituted for inferred entities.

German philosopher [Rudolf Carnap](wikipedia://en/57987) in his *Der logische Aufbau der Welt* (1928) (referring to them as "Konstitutionssystems") and American philosopher [Nelson Goodman](wikipedia://en/1179596) in his *The Structure of Appearance* (1951) studied the structure of constructional systems.
