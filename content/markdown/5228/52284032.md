# Ravindra Bapat

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ravindra Bapat"}

**Ravindra B. Bapat** is an Indian mathematician known for the [Bapat–Beg theorem](wikipedia://en/10545671).

:::

## Education {#Education}

He obtained B.Sc. from [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai), M.Stat. from the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi) and Ph.D. from the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) in 1981.[^o4] His research interests include major contributions to matrix inequalities, [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), generalized inverses, and matrix analysis.

## Career {#Career}

In addition to the numerous research papers in the reputed journals, Bapat has written books on linear algebra published by Hindustan Book Agency,[^o6] Springer, and Cambridge University Press. He has served on the editorial boards of *Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra*, *Indian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics*, *Kerala Mathematical Association Bulletin*, and *Linear and Multilinear Algebra*. He is national coordinator for the [Mathematics Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_Olympiad) and was head of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Delhi Centre from 2007 to 2011.[^o4]

He is a [Visiting Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_Professor) of Mathematics at [Ashoka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashoka_University), India.

He held the position of President of [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) from 2007 to 2008.[^o4]
