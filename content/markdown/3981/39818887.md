# Carr–Madan formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carr–Madan formula"}

In [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), the **Carr–Madan formula** of Peter Carr and Dilip B. Madan[^o2] shows that the [analytical solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_solution) of the [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_option) [option price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Option_price) can be obtained once the explicit form of the [characteristic function](wikipedia://en/3229401) of $\log S_T$, where $S_T$ is the price of the underlying asset at maturity time $T$, is available.[^o3] This analytical solution is in the form of the [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247), which then allows for the [fast Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/11512) to be employed to numerically compute option values and [Greeks](wikipedia://en/250251) in an efficient manner.

:::
