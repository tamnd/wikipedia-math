# Kapil Hari Paranjape

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kapil Hari Paranjape"}

**Kapil Hari Paranjape** is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997)[^o3] . He is Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Mohali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science_Education_and_Research,_Mohali) and Distinguished Professor at the Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence [^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), Maharashtra near the [Kabootar Khana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabootar_Khana) in [Dadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadar) but grew up in [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi). He completed his schooling from the [Sardar Patel Vidyalaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardar_Patel_Vidyalaya) in 1977. He then joined the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Kanpur) where he pursued a five-years integrated Master’s programme in Mathematics and graduated in 1982.[^o6] He was awarded the General Proficiency Prize for Mathematics from IIT Kanpur in 1982.

He joined the School of Mathematics, [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) and was awarded his PhD in Mathematics in 1992.

Paranjape is also involved in the promotion of [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux) and [GNU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU)[^o7] and writes a blog titled Mast Kalandar.[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

He worked as a Reader at TIFR from 1993-1998. During this time he also held various visiting positions at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) and [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick). He was appointed as Professor at the Theoretical Statistics and Mathematics Unit of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Bangalore. He moved to the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai](wikipedia://en/2718996) in 1996. Between 2001 and 2009 he held visiting positions at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). Since 2009 he is a professor of Mathematics at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Mohali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science_Education_and_Research,_Mohali). He was Clark Way Harrison Visiting Professor in [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) in 2019. He also spent time at the Lodha Mathematical Sciences Institute in 2026. [^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in 2005, the highest science award in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), in the mathematical sciences category. His citation read "Dr Paranjape has made outstanding contributions in the field of algebraic geometry, especially the theory of [algebraic cycles](wikipedia://en/9520954). He has made highly significant contributions in connecting [Hodge Theory](wikipedia://en/501590) to the study of [Chow Groups](wikipedia://en/4282051). He has also established deep relations between Calabi-Yau varieties and [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000)".[^o12][^o13] He is also a recipient of various other honors, among them are

- Fellowship of [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) Bangalore, 1997
- Fellowship of [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India), Allahabad, 1999
- Associate of the [ICTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics), [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), 1999-2001
- [B. M. Birla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brij_Mohan_Birla) award for young scientists, 1999
- [DST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Science_and_Technology_%28India%29) Swarnajayanti (Golden Anniversary) Grant, 2001
- [NBHM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Board_for_Higher_Mathematics) National Lecturer, India, 2004-2005
- [Debian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debian) Developer, 2007
- Fellowship of [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy), New Delhi, 2009
- [J C Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagadish_Chandra_Bose) National Fellowship, 2010
- Fellowship of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) ([TWAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS)), 2019
