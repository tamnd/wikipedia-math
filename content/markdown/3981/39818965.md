# Integer-valued function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Integer-valued function"}

*Image: Floor function.svg.* The floor function on real numbers. Its discontinuities are pictured with white discs outlines with blue circles.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **integer-valued function** is a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) whose values are [integers](wikipedia://en/14563). In other words, it is a function that assigns an integer to each member of its [domain](wikipedia://en/50263).

:::

The [floor and ceiling functions](wikipedia://en/54267) are examples of integer-valued [functions of a real variable](wikipedia://en/1268958), but on [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) and, generally, on (non-disconnected) [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) integer-valued functions are not especially useful. Any such function on a [connected space](wikipedia://en/6233) either has [discontinuities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discontinuity_%28mathematics%29) or is [constant](wikipedia://en/320861). On the other hand, on [discrete](wikipedia://en/56061) and other [totally disconnected spaces](wikipedia://en/303356) integer-valued functions have roughly the same importance as [real-valued functions](wikipedia://en/22284647) have on non-discrete spaces.

Any function with [natural](wikipedia://en/21474), or [non-negative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-negative) integer values is a partial case of an integer-valued function.

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Integer-valued functions defined on the domain of all real numbers include the floor and ceiling functions, the [Dirichlet function](wikipedia://en/630005), the [sign function](wikipedia://en/505449) and the [Heaviside step function](wikipedia://en/87299) (except possibly at 0).

Integer-valued functions defined on the domain of non-negative real numbers include the [integer square root](wikipedia://en/981655) function and the [prime-counting function](wikipedia://en/331826).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

On an arbitrary [set](wikipedia://en/26691) $X$, integer-valued functions form a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) with [pointwise](wikipedia://en/4786797) operations of addition and multiplication,[^o6] and also an [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_ring) over the ring $\mathbf{Z}$ of integers. Since the latter is an [ordered ring](wikipedia://en/525697), the functions form a [partially ordered ring](wikipedia://en/22211384):

$$f \le g \quad\iff\quad \forall x: f(x) \le g(x).$$

:::

## Uses {#Uses}

### Graph theory and algebra {#Graph_theory_and_algebra}

Integer-valued functions are ubiquitous in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). They also have similar uses in [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), where *[length function](wikipedia://en/3021393)* represents the concept of [norm](wikipedia://en/990534), and *[word metric](wikipedia://en/1289716)* represents the concept of [metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_%28mathematics%29).

[Integer-valued polynomials](wikipedia://en/585797) are important in [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424).

### Mathematical logic and computability theory {#Mathematical_logic_and_computability_theory}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), such concepts as [primitive recursive functions](wikipedia://en/24829) and [μ-recursive functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Μ-recursive_function) represent integer-valued functions of several natural variables or, in other words, functions on $\mathbf{N}^{n}$. [Gödel numbering](wikipedia://en/207790), defined on [well-formed formulae](wikipedia://en/404582) of some [formal language](wikipedia://en/10939), is a natural-valued function.

[Computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414) is essentially based on natural numbers and natural (or integer) functions on them.

### Number theory {#Number_theory}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), many [arithmetic functions](wikipedia://en/3170) are integer-valued.

### Computer science {#Computer_science}

In [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming), many [functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Function_%28programming%29) return values of [integer type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_%28computer_science%29) due to simplicity of implementation.
