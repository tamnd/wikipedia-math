# M. D. Chow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. D. Chow"}

**M. D. Chow** (1878 – February 13, 1949 Shanghai), also known by the Chinese names **Zhou Jinjue** () and **Zhou Mingda** (), was a Chinese philatelist and mathematician. He was nicknamed the "king of Chinese [philately](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philately)".[^o2]

:::

## Names {#Names}

Having multiple names was the custom during the Republic era. He was also known as Zhou Meiquan ( or 周梅泉), Zhou Jinjue (; formerly romanised Chow Chin Tso).

## Early life {#Early_life}

He was born into a salt merchant family in [Yangzhou, Jiangsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangzhou) and moved to [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai) in 1912. He was home schooled.

## Philately {#Philately}

*Image: smallone.jpg.* The block of four of the Small One Dollar [Red Revenues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Revenue); now owned by Ding Jinsong ()

He was most noted as the founding father of Chinese philately and was crowned the King of Chinese Stamps after his acquisition of the rarest stamp, the block of four [Red Revenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Revenue) stamps from the original owner R. A. de Villard in 1927.[^o6][^o7][^o8] He championed the study of the Red Revenues.[^o9]

To entertain his sick son [Wei-Liang Chow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wei-Liang_Chow) in 1923, he brought home many colorful foreign stamps during his recuperation. Soon they both caught the bug and began learning and collecting stamps.[^o10] He found Chinese Philatelic Society on November 15, 1925.[^o11] His bi-lingual *Philatelic Bulletin* won a Special Bronze Medal at the [International Philatelic Exhibition in New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_philatelic_exhibitions_%28by_country%29#United_States) in 1926.[^o12] Chinese stamps eventually became a gold medal contender in 1927 at the [Strasbourg International Exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_philatelic_exhibitions_%28by_country%29#France) in France.[^o7][^o8] He's the first Chinese to be granted a fellow of F.R.P.S.L., the [Royal Philatelic Society London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Philatelic_Society_London).[^o7][^o8]

## Math clubs {#Math_clubs}

In Yangzhou in 1900, he created Zhixin Math Club () with Bao Mofen () and Yu Yudong (). As one of the finest mathematicians in China, he was highly praised by Japanese scholars.[^o14] In the 1920s, he created [Science Society of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Society_of_China) () with Ren Hongjuan () and Hu Mingxia (), and was named co-honorary president with [Zhang Jian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Jian_%28politician%29).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

His bi-lingual *Philatelic Bulletin* won a Special Bronze Medal at the International Philatelic Exhibition in New York in 1926.
