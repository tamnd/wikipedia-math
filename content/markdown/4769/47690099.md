# Summer School Marktoberdorf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Summer School Marktoberdorf"}

The International **Summer School Marktoberdorf** is an annual two-week [summer school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_school) for international [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) postgraduate students and other young researchers, held annually since 1970 in [Marktoberdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marktoberdorf), near [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich) in southern [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany).[^o2] Students are accommodated in the [boarding house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_house) of a local high school, [Gymnasium Marktoberdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_Marktoberdorf).[^o3] [Proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings) are published when appropriate.[^o10]

:::

## Status {#Status}

This is a summer school for [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) researchers,[^o5] with some directors/co-directors who are [Turing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_Award) winners (the nearest equivalent to the [Nobel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize) in computer science).[^o6]

The summer school is supported as an Advanced Study Institute of the [NATO Science for Peace and Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_Science_for_Peace_and_Security) Program.[^o7] It is administered by the Faculty of Informatics at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).[^o8]
