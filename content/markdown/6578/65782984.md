# Free matroid

*Image: Free matroid.svg.* The [graphic matroid](wikipedia://en/745714) of a [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_%28graph_theory%29) with 4 edges, which is the **free matroid** with a ground set of size 4 (also the [uniform matroid](wikipedia://en/27967069) $U{}^4_4$). More generally, the graphic matroid of a forest with $n$ edges is $U{}^{n}_{n}$.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Free matroid"}

In mathematics, the **free matroid** over a given ground-set $E$ is the [matroid](wikipedia://en/244321) in which the independent sets are all subsets of $E$. It is a special case of a [uniform matroid](wikipedia://en/27967069); specifically, when $E$ has cardinality $n$, it is the uniform matroid $U{}^{n}_{n}$.[^o2] The unique [basis](wikipedia://en/65673969) of this matroid is the ground-set itself, $E$. Among matroids on $E$, the free matroid on $E$ has the most independent sets, the highest rank, and the fewest circuits.

:::

Every free matroid with a ground set of size $n$ is the [graphic matroid](wikipedia://en/745714) of an $n$-edge [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_%28graph_theory%29).[^o3]

## Free extension of a matroid {#Free_extension_of_a_matroid}

The **free extension** of a matroid $M$ by some element $e\not\in M$, denoted $M+e$, is a matroid whose elements are the elements of $M$ plus the new element $e$, and:

- Its [circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_of_a_matroid) are the circuits of $M$ plus the sets $B\cup \{e\}$ for all [bases](wikipedia://en/65673969) $B$ of $M$.[^o5]
- Equivalently, its independent sets are the independent sets of $M$ plus the sets $I\cup \{e\}$ for all independent sets $I$ that are *not* bases.
- Equivalently, its [bases](wikipedia://en/65673969) are the bases of $M$ plus the sets $I\cup \{e\}$ for all independent sets of size $\text{rank}(M)-1$.
