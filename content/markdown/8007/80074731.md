# Mohammed Abouzaid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mohammed Abouzaid"}

**Mohammed Abouzaid** (محمد أبوزيد; born 1981) is a Moroccan mathematician working in [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology). He is a professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). He obtained his PhD in 2007 at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) under [Paul Seidel](wikipedia://en/27678785)'s supervision.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mohammed Abouzaid was born in 1981.[^o4] In 2007, he received his PhD under the supervision of [Paul Seidel](wikipedia://en/27678785) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) with thesis *Homological Mirror Symmetry for Toric Varieties*.[^o5][^o6] As a postdoctoral researcher, he was a [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) Research Fellow from 2007 to 2012, concurrently, he was also a postdoctoral fellow at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). From 2012 to 2013, he was a visiting professor at the [Simons Center for Geometry and Physics](wikipedia://en/25568388). From 2012 to 2023, he was an associate professor at Columbia University.[^o7][^o8] Since 2023 he has been Professor of Mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o5]

As of 2026, he supervised 7 PhD students.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Abouzaid was an invited speaker the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2014.[^o11] In 2017, he won the [New Horizons in Mathematics Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Horizons_in_Mathematics_Prize) "For distinguishing cotangent bundles of exotic spheres, constructing the wrapped [Fukaya category](wikipedia://en/31598730) with Paul Seidel, and other decisive contributions to [symplectic topology](wikipedia://en/294298) and [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/1390873)."[^o12] In 2018 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]
