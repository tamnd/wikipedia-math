# Nearby Lagrangian conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nearby Lagrangian conjecture"}

::: {#o2 .problem cue=unsolved}

Prove or disprove: Any closed exact [Lagrangian submanifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_submanifold) of the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) of a [closed manifold](wikipedia://en/669475) is [Hamiltonian isotopic](wikipedia://en/312304#Flows) to the [zero section](wikipedia://en/276524#Sections_and_locally_free_sheaves).

:::

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), the **nearby Lagrangian conjecture**, is an [open](wikipedia://en/183091)[^o3] [mathematical problem](wikipedia://en/256700) often attributed to [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490). It states that every closed exact [Lagrangian submanifold](wikipedia://en/28356) of a [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) ***T∗M*** (with symplectic structure) is [Hamiltonian isotopic](wikipedia://en/312304#Flows) to the [zero section](wikipedia://en/276524#Sections_and_locally_free_sheaves).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

It is closely related to the theory of [generating functions](wikipedia://en/160993).[^o7]
