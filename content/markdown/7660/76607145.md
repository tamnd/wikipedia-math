# Marion Campbell (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marion Campbell"}

**Marion Kay Campbell**[^o2] is a Scottish medical statistician, clinical trialist, and academic administrator who is the dean of research for life sciences and medicine and a professor of [health services research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_services_research) at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Campbell completed a BSc in statistics with honours, a MSc in statistics, and a PhD in public health from the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen).[^o4]

Campbell worked in [operational research](wikipedia://en/43476) and the statistics of [medical audits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_audit) at the [National Health Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Service).[^o4] In 1993, she returned to her alma mater, the University of Aberdeen where she works in the [health services research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_services_research) unit.[^o4][^o5] She is a [medical statistician](wikipedia://en/11082896) and clinical trialist who serves as a professor of health services research.[^o4] In 2007, she became the director of the unit and dean of research for life sciences and medicine in 2015.[^o5] As of 2023, she is the vice principal for research. Campbell is a fellow of the [Academy of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Medical_Sciences_%28United_Kingdom%29) (2023) and the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o4][^o5]
