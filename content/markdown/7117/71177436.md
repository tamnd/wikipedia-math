# Patrick Brendan Kennedy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patrick Brendan Kennedy"}

**Patrick Brendan Kennedy** (20 July 19298 June 1966) was an [Irish chess champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Chess_Championship), and professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), notable for his work in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

## Early life, family, and personal life {#Early_life,_family,_and_personal_life}

Kennedy was the third child of Pat Kennedy and Kit O'Sullivan, his father, a master [carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpentry) by trade, decided instead to join the [police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_force) in 1923, many on his mother's side were [blacksmiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blacksmith) near [Castlemaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlemaine,_County_Kerry).[^o4] His parents moved to [Ballylongford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballylongford) in 1936, and secured a transfer for Kennedy to attend the [North Monastery secondary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Monastery) in [Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_%28city%29).[^o4]

Whilst at the [North Monastery School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Monastery), Kennedy won the Honan Scholarship to [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork), where in 1949 was awarded his [Bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in mathematics and mathematical physics.[^o4][^o5] In the same year, Kennedy also took part in the [Irish Chess Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Chess_Championship), and won 7 games out of 7, becoming the Irish chess Champion, he has been the only [Irish Chess champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Chess_Championship) to win in such a way.[^o6] After this, Kennedy was described as having a falling off in the quality of his play, and lost his title at the 1950 Championship.[^o6]

In 1951, Kennedy completed his [Master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree), and was recommended by his examiner V. C. A. Ferraro, who at the time was a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University College of the South West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_the_South_West) at [Exeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter), to study for a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) with [Walter Hayman](wikipedia://en/34191584) at Exeter.[^o4][^o5][^o7]

Kennedy's first paper was published in 1953, titled *On a conjecture of Heins*, which concerned a conjecture of Heins on [subharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/3237359) and gives positive results.[^o4][^o5][^o8] That same year he was appointed as an [assistant lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_lecturer) in mathematics at the [University of Aberystwyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberystwyth), and by 1954 he was awarded a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) by the [National University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland) for his thesis *Asymptotic Values on Integral Functions.*[^o4][^o5]

He married Pamela Fishwick in March 1954, and had three children, Anne Catherine Deirdre Kennedy, David Patrick Leslie Kennedy, and Jane C Deborah Kennedy.[^o4]

Since Kennedy lived in [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales) at the time, he had wanted to avoid [national service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_service) for the English, and so took a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) position at [University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork) in 1954, his objectives were to modernise courses and raise standards, and his research output increased.[^o4][^o5] [Hayman](wikipedia://en/34191584) characterises Kennedy's attitude to academic politics as "black and white", and Kennedy wasn't afraid to work hard both in his research and on committee to achieve productive outcomes.[^o4]

In 1956, he was appointed [professor of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_mathematics) at [Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_%28city%29), and awarded the [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc.) by the [National University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland) in 1960, he was elected a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy) in 1962.[^o4][^o5] He was appointed the first professor of mathematics at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York) in 1962, which planned to open the next year. He worked at building the mathematics [library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library) and appointed staff to the mathematics department.[^o4][^o5]

Kennedy took his life in 1966 on the night of 8 June; the coroner explained it was a combination of a [psychiatric illness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatric_illness) and added pressure of work, with his wife, Fishwick stating: "\[He\] set himself too high a standard and drove himself too hard".[^o9]

## List of academic works {#List_of_academic_works}

[Walter Hayman](wikipedia://en/34191584), a prominent British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and colleague of Kennedy, describes Kennedy's work as "extremely successful in all three fields in which he wrote.[^o4] Kennedy collaborated with several of his peers in his papers, and was capable of constructing examples to his own and others results that were "simple although far from obvious".[^o4] Kennedy's work can be broken into three fields:[^o4]

### Theory of functions of a complex variable {#Theory_of_functions_of_a_complex_variable}

- "On a conjecture of Heins".[^o8]
- "Conformal mapping of bounded domains".[^o12]
- "A class of integral functions bounded on certain curves".[^o13]
- With W. K. Hayman, "On the growth of multivalent functions ".[^o14]
- "On a theorem of Hayman concerning quasibounded functions ".[^o15]
- "A property of bounded regular functions ".[^o16]
- "A problem on bounded analytic functions ".[^o17]
- "On the derivative of a function of bounded characteristic".[^o18]
- With J. B. Twomey, "Some properties of bounded univalent functions and related classes of functions".[^o19]

### Fourier series {#Fourier_series}

- "Fourier series with gaps".[^o21]
- "Fourier series with gaps. II".[^o22]
- "Remark on a theorem of Zygmund".[^o23]
- "On the coefficients in certain Fourier series".[^o24]
- "A remark on continuity conditions".[^o25]
- "Note on Fourier series with Hadamard gaps".[^o26]

::: {#o28 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Tauberian theorems"}

### Tauberian theorems {#Tauberian_theorems}

- "Integrability theorems for [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)".[^o29]
- " A note on uniformly distributed sequences ".[^o30]
- "General integrability theorems for power series".[^o31]
- With P. Sziisz, " On a bounded increasing power series".[^o32]
- "On a theorem of Sziisz".[^o33]

:::
