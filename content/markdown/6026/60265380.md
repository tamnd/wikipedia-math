# Robert Liptser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Liptser"}

**Robert Sh. Liptser** (Роберт Шевилевич Липцер; רוברט ליפצר; 20 March 1936 – 2 January 2019) was a Russian-Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made contributions to the theory and applications of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), in particular to [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216) and [nonlinear filtering](wikipedia://en/12433418).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Liptser was born in [Kirovograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kropyvnytskyi), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) and spent his youth in [Odesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odesa), Ukraine. In 1959 he graduated with the M.Sc. degree in electrical engineering from [Moscow Aviation Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Aviation_Institute) and in 1965 he graduated with the second M.Sc. in mathematics from [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). In 1968 he obtained his Ph.D. degree from [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) (MIPT).[^o4]

He held a Professor position at MIPT and worked in the [Institute of Control Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Control_Sciences) until 1990, when he joined the [Institute for Information Transmission Problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Information_Transmission_Problems) as the head of the Stochastic Dynamic Systems Laboratory.

In 1993 emigrated to [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) and lived in [Kfar Saba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar_Saba). In Israel he held a Professor position at the School of Electrical Engineering in [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), until his retirement in 2005.

## Research {#Research}

Liptser made several important contributions to the theory of martingales and to their applications in engineering and statistics.[^o6][^o7][^o8] This includes his study of the [conditionally Gaussian processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditionally_Gaussian_process),[^o9] which play an important role in the [separation principle in stochastic control](wikipedia://en/58383744#Nonlinear_filtering_solutions).[^o10]

He coauthored a number of influential books.[^o11] His monograph "Statistics of Random processes: General Theory and Applications",[^o12][^o13] written together with [Albert Shiryaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Shiryaev) in 1974, has become an internationally renowned reference textbook among scholars working in stochastic analysis and related fields.
