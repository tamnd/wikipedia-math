# Heptagonal number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptagonal number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **heptagonal number** is a [figurate number](wikipedia://en/325260) that is constructed by combining [heptagons](wikipedia://en/365349) with ascending size. The *n*-th heptagonal number is given by the formula

:::

$$H_n=\frac{5n^2 - 3n}{2}.$$

*Image: Heptagonal numbers.svg.* The first five heptagonal numbers.

The first few heptagonal numbers are:

[0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0_%28number%29), [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), [7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29), [18](wikipedia://en/208294), [34](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/34_%28number%29), [55](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/55_%28number%29), [81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/81_%28number%29), [112](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/112_%28number%29), [148](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/148_%28number%29), [189](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/189_%28number%29), [235](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/235_%28number%29), 286, 342, 403, 469, 540, [616](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/616_%28number%29), 697, 783, 874, 970, 1071, 1177, 1288, 1404, 1525, 1651, 1782, …

## Parity {#Parity}

The parity of heptagonal numbers follows the pattern odd-odd-even-even. Like [square numbers](wikipedia://en/213919), the [digital root](wikipedia://en/8286632) in base 10 of a heptagonal number can only be 1, 4, 7 or 9. Five times a heptagonal number, plus 1 equals a [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915).

## Additional properties {#Additional_properties}

- The heptagonal numbers have several notable formulas:

$$H_{m+n}=H_m+H_n+5mn$$

$$H_{m-n}=H_m+H_n-5mn+3n$$

$$H_m-H_n=\frac{(5(m+n)-3)(m-n)}{2}$$

$$40H_n+9=(10n-3)^2$$

## Sum of reciprocals {#Sum_of_reciprocals}

A formula for the [sum of the reciprocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sums_of_reciprocals) of the heptagonal numbers is given by:[^o5]

$$\begin{aligned}\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{2}{n(5n-3)} &= \frac{1}{15}{\pi}{\sqrt{25-10\sqrt{5}}}+\frac{2}{3}\ln(5)+\frac{{1}+\sqrt{5}}{3}\ln\left(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{10-2\sqrt{5}}\right)+\frac{{1}-\sqrt{5}}{3}\ln\left(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{10+2\sqrt{5}}\right)\\ &=\frac13\left(\frac{\pi}{\sqrt[4]{5\,\phi^6}}+\frac52\ln(5) -\sqrt5 \ln(\phi)\right)\\ &=1.3227792531223888567\dots \end{aligned}$$

with [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) $\phi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt5}2$.

## Heptagonal roots {#Heptagonal_roots}

In analogy to the [square root](wikipedia://en/29208) of *x,*one can calculate the heptagonal root of *x*, meaning the number of terms in the sequence up to and including *x*.

The heptagonal root of *x* is given by the formula

$$n = \frac{\sqrt{40x + 9} + 3}{10},$$

which is obtained by using the [quadratic formula](wikipedia://en/59217) to solve $x = \frac{5n^2 - 3n}{2}$ for its unique positive root *n*.
