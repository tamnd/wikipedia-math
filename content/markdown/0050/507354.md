# Glossary of differential geometry and topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glossary of differential geometry and topology"}

This is a [glossary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary) of terms specific to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562). The following three glossaries are closely related:

:::

- [Glossary of general topology](wikipedia://en/30562)
- [Glossary of algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/48781047)
- [Glossary of Riemannian and metric geometry](wikipedia://en/490861).

See also:

Words in *italics* denote a self-reference to this glossary.

- **[Atlas](wikipedia://en/47436)**

::: {#o4 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Basic element"}

## B {#B}

- **Bundle** – see *fiber bundle*.
- **Basic element** – A **basic element** *$x$* with respect to an element *$y$* is an element of a [cochain complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochain_complex) $(C^*, d)$ (e.g., complex of *differential forms* on a manifold) that is closed: $dx = 0$ and the contraction of *$x$* by *$y$* is zero.

:::

## C {#C}

- **[Characteristic class](wikipedia://en/363530)**
- **[Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chart_%28topology%29)**
- **[Cobordism](wikipedia://en/391832)**
- **[Codimension](wikipedia://en/525149)** – The codimension of a submanifold is the dimension of the ambient space minus the dimension of the submanifold.
- **[Connected sum](wikipedia://en/490134)**
- **[Connection](wikipedia://en/308815)**
- **[Cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600)** – the vector bundle of cotangent spaces on a manifold.
- **[Cotangent space](wikipedia://en/6620)**
- [**Covering**](wikipedia://en/250323)
- **[Cusp](wikipedia://en/6851758)**
- **[CW-complex](wikipedia://en/353022)**[^o6]

## D {#D}

- **[Dehn twist](wikipedia://en/765970)**
- **[Diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564)** – Given two [differentiable manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470#Differentiable_manifolds) *$M$* and *$N$*, a [bijective map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijective_map) $f$ from *$M$* to *$N$* is called a **diffeomorphism** – if both $f:M\to N$ and its inverse $f^{-1}:N\to M$ are [smooth functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function).
- **[Differential form](wikipedia://en/221519)**
- [**Domain invariance**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_invariance_theorem)
- **Doubling** – Given a manifold *$M$* with boundary, doubling is taking two copies of *$M$* and identifying their boundaries. As the result we get a manifold without boundary.

## E {#E}

- **[Embedding](wikipedia://en/52119)**
- **Exotic structure** – See [exotic sphere](wikipedia://en/1114931) and [exotic $\mathbb{R}^4$](wikipedia://en/2180637).

## F {#F}

- **Fiber** – In a fiber bundle, *$\pi:E \to B$* the [preimage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preimage) *$\pi^{-1}(x)$* of a point *$x$* in the base *$B$* is called the fiber over *$x$*, often denoted *$E_x$*.
- **[Fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526)**
- **Frame** – A **frame** at a point of a [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) *M* is a [basis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basis_of_a_vector_space) of the [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) at the point.
- **[Frame bundle](wikipedia://en/366550)** – the principal bundle of frames on a smooth manifold.
- **[Flow](wikipedia://en/2619735)**

- **[Genus](wikipedia://en/61891)**

## H {#H}

- **[Handle decomposition](wikipedia://en/902592)**
- **[Hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101)** – A hypersurface is a submanifold of *codimension* one.

## J {#J}

- [**Jet**](wikipedia://en/1521924)
- **[Jordan curve theorem](wikipedia://en/267444)**

## L {#L}

- **[Lens space](wikipedia://en/885725)** – A lens space is a quotient of the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) (or (2*n* + 1)-sphere) by a free isometric [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of [**Z** – <sub>k</sub>](wikipedia://en/52327).
- **[Local diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/1728483)**

## M {#M}

- **[Manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470)** – A topological manifold is a locally Euclidean [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) (usually also required to be [second-countable](wikipedia://en/901459)). For a given regularity (e.g. piecewise-linear, $C^k$ or $C^\infty$ differentiable, [real or complex analytic](wikipedia://en/61478), [Lipschitz](wikipedia://en/51731), [Hölder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hölder_continuity), [quasi-conformal](wikipedia://en/2749226)...), a manifold of that regularity is a topological manifold whose charts transitions have the prescribed regularity.
- **[Manifold with boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_with_boundary)**
- **[Manifold with corners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_with_corners)**
- **[Mapping class group](wikipedia://en/793295)**
- **[Morse function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function)**

## N {#N}

- **[Neat submanifold](wikipedia://en/34260152)** – A submanifold whose boundary equals its intersection with the boundary of the manifold into which it is embedded.

## O {#O}

- **[Orbifold](wikipedia://en/293504)**
- **[Orientation of a vector bundle](wikipedia://en/44844703)**

## P {#P}

- **[Pair of pants](wikipedia://en/2812977)** – An orientable compact surface with 3 boundary components. All compact orientable surfaces can be reconstructed by gluing pairs of pants along their boundary components.
- **[Parallelizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallelizable)** – A smooth manifold is parallelizable if it admits a smooth [global frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_frame). This is equivalent to the tangent bundle being trivial.
- **[Partition of unity](wikipedia://en/48636)**
- **[PL-map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PL-manifold)**
- **[Poincaré lemma](wikipedia://en/600030)**
- **[Principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950)** – A principal bundle is a fiber bundle *$P \to B$* together with an [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on *$P$* by a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) *$G$* that preserves the fibers of *$P$* and acts simply transitively on those fibers.
- **[Pullback](wikipedia://en/9413508)**

## R {#R}

- **[Rham cohomology](wikipedia://en/221530)**

## S {#S}

- **[Section](wikipedia://en/367621)**
- **[Seifert fiber space](wikipedia://en/1631931)**
- **[Submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095)** – the image of a smooth embedding of a manifold.
- **[Submersion](wikipedia://en/356413)**
- **[Surface](wikipedia://en/27865)** – a two-dimensional manifold or submanifold.
- **[Systole](wikipedia://en/2545455)** – least length of a noncontractible loop.

## T {#T}

- **[Tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794)** – the vector bundle of tangent spaces on a differentiable manifold.
- **Tangent field** – a *section* of the tangent bundle. Also called a *vector field*.
- **[Tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807)**
- **[Thom space](wikipedia://en/1243451)**
- **[Torus](wikipedia://en/74800)**
- **[Transversality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transversality_%28mathematics%29)** – Two submanifolds *$M$* and *$N$* intersect transversally if at each point of intersection *p* their tangent spaces $T_p(M)$ and $T_p(N)$ generate the whole tangent space at *p* of the total manifold.
- [**Triangulation**](wikipedia://en/1440976)
- **[Trivialization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivialization_%28mathematics%29)**
- **[Tubular neighborhood](wikipedia://en/3192042)**

## V {#V}

- **[Vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524)** – a fiber bundle whose fibers are vector spaces and whose transition functions are linear maps.
- **[Vector field](wikipedia://en/62641)** – a section of a vector bundle. More specifically, a vector field can mean a section of the tangent bundle.

## W {#W}

- **[Whitney sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_sum)** – A Whitney sum is an analog of the [direct product](wikipedia://en/8887) for vector bundles. Given two vector bundles *$\alpha$* and *$\beta$* over the same base *$B$* their [cartesian product](wikipedia://en/24104095) is a vector bundle over $B\times B$. The diagonal map $B\to B\times B$ induces a vector bundle over *$B$* called the Whitney sum of these vector bundles and denoted by *$\alpha \oplus \beta$*.
- **[Whitney topologies](wikipedia://en/28291162)**
