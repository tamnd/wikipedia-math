# Pyramidal number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pyramidal number"}

*Image: Square pyramidal number.svg.* Geometric representation of the square pyramidal number 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 = 30.

A **pyramidal number** is the number of points in a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) with a [polygonal](wikipedia://en/23621) base and triangular sides.[^o5] The term often refers to [square pyramidal numbers](wikipedia://en/542343), which have a [square](wikipedia://en/659939) base with four sides, but it can also refer to a pyramid with any number of sides. The numbers of points in the base and in layers parallel to the base are given by [polygonal numbers](wikipedia://en/213996) of the given number of sides, while the numbers of points in each triangular side is given by a [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915). It is possible to extend the pyramidal numbers to higher dimensions.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=named-heading name="Formula"}

## Formula {#Formula}

The formula for the $n$th $r$-gonal pyramidal number is

$$P_n^r= \frac{3n^2 + n^3(r-2) - n(r-5)}{6},$$

where $r \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \ge 3$.

This formula can be factored:

$$P_n^r=\frac{n(n+1)\bigl(n(r-2)-(r-5)\bigr)}{(2)(3)}=\left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)\left(\frac{n(r-2)-(r-5)}{3}\right)=T_n \cdot \frac{n(r-2)-(r-5)}{3},$$

where $T_{n}$ is the $n$th [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915).

:::
