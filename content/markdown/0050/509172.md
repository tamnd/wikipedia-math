# Markov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Markov"}

**Markov** ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language), Марков), **Markova**, and **Markoff** are common surnames used in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) and [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria). Notable people with the name include:

:::

## Academics {#Academics}

- [Ivana Markova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivana_Markova) (1938–2024), Czechoslovak-British emeritus professor of psychology at the University of Stirling
- [Igor Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Markov) (born 1973), American professor, computer scientist and engineer
- [John Markoff (sociologist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Markoff_%28sociologist%29) (born 1942), American professor of sociology and history at the University of Pittsburgh
- [Konstantin Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantin_Markov) (1905–1980), Soviet geomorphologist and quaternary geologist
