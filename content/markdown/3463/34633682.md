# Nakayama's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nakayama's conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Nakayama's conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) about [Artinian rings](wikipedia://en/489273), introduced by (txt). The **generalized Nakayama conjecture** is an extension to more general [rings](wikipedia://en/48404), introduced by (txt). (Leuschke Huneke 2004) [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) some cases of the generalized Nakayama conjecture.

:::

Nakayama's conjecture states that if all the [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) of a minimal [injective resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_resolution) of an [Artin algebra](wikipedia://en/29353834) *R* are [injective](wikipedia://en/473775) and [projective](wikipedia://en/364488), then *R* is [self-injective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-injective_ring).
