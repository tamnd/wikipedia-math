# Oka's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oka's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Oka's lemma**, proved by [Kiyoshi Oka](wikipedia://en/381978), states that in a [domain of holomorphy](wikipedia://en/3236377) in $\mathbb{C}^n$, the function $-\log d(z)$ is [plurisubharmonic](wikipedia://en/2519085), where $d$ is the distance to the boundary. This property shows that the domain is [pseudoconvex](wikipedia://en/3235998). Historically, this lemma was first shown in the Hartogs domain in the case of two variables. Furthermore, Oka's lemma is the inverse of Levi's problem (unramified Riemann domain over $\mathbb{C}^n$). This may be why Oka referred to Levi's problem as "problème inverse de Hartogs", and could explain why Levi's problem is occasionally referred to as **Hartogs' Inverse Problem**.

:::
