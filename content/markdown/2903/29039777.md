# Emil Müller (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emil Müller"}

**Emil Adalbert Müller** (22 April 1861 – 1 September 1927) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Lanškroun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanškroun), he studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology). In 1898 he defended his dissertation (*Die Geometrie orientierter Kugeln nach Grassmann’schen Methoden*) at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) with [Wilhelm Franz Meyer](wikipedia://en/57755859). One year later he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the same university. Since 1902 he was professor for [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) at the Vienna University of Technology and founder of the Vienna school of descriptive geometry. He also served as dean and president (1912–13). In 1903 he founded the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society) together with [Ludwig Boltzmann](wikipedia://en/544255) and [Gustav von Escherich](wikipedia://en/29030428). In 1904 Müller was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Heidelberg.[^o3]

He was a member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).
