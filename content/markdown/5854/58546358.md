# Marc Oliver Rieger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marc Oliver Rieger"}

**Marc Oliver Rieger** (born September 22, 1974) is a German mathematician and finance professor. His research covers the areas of [behavioural finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_economics), [decision](wikipedia://en/446216) and [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [financial derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_%28finance%29), and calculus of variation.

:::

Together with [Thorsten Hens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorsten_Hens) and [Mei Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mei_Wang), he was one of the authors of the INTRA survey (International Test on Risk Attitudes) that collected risk and time preferences in 53 countries worldwide leading to various follow-up studies by other researchers and practitioners and media coverage by leading newspapers in Germany and elsewhere.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

## Biography {#Biography}

Rieger is Professor of Banking and Finance at the [University of Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trier), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), since 2010,[^o9] and Director of the [Confucius Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucius_Institute) of the [University of Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trier) since 2018.[^o10] In 2018, he was also adjunct professor at [National Chengchi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chengchi_University), [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei), [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) and in 2019 guest professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o11] Since 2019, he is core member of the research group "Quantitative Finance and Risk Analysis",[^o12] and project leader of the research cluster "Globalization and Re-Nationalization,"[^o13] both financed by the research initiative of the state of [Rheinland-Pfalz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rheinland-Pfalz).

Rieger studied mathematics at [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) from 1993 to 1998, and obtained a PhD at [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506), [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig), in 2001, under the supervision of [Stefan Müller](wikipedia://en/24863845). He worked as research scholar and postdoc at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore), [University of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich) and [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich).
