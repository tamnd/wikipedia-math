# Double affine Hecke algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Double affine Hecke algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **double affine Hecke algebra**, or **Cherednik algebra**, is an [algebra](wikipedia://en/2112) containing the [Hecke algebra](wikipedia://en/10361630) of an [affine Weyl group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_Weyl_group), given as the [quotient](wikipedia://en/184635) of the [group ring](wikipedia://en/349223) of a [double affine braid group](wikipedia://en/32034473). They were introduced by [Cherednik](wikipedia://en/33333323), who used them to [prove](wikipedia://en/82285) [Macdonald's constant term conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald's_constant_term_conjecture) for [Macdonald polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_polynomial). Infinitesimal Cherednik algebras have significant implications in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and therefore have important applications in [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics) and [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry).

:::
