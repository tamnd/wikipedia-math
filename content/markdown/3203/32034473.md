# Double affine braid group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Double affine braid group"}

In mathematics, a **double affine braid group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) containing the [braid group](wikipedia://en/550138) of an [affine Weyl group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_Weyl_group). Their group rings have quotients called [double affine Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/32034345) in the same way that the group rings of [affine braid groups](wikipedia://en/32040747) have quotients that are [affine Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/10361630).

:::

For affine *A*<sub>*n*</sub> groups, the double affine braid group is the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) of the space of *n* distinct points on a 2-dimensional torus.
