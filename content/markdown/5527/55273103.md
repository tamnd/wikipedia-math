# Elizabeth A. Thompson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elizabeth A. Thompson"}

**Elizabeth Alison Thompson** (born May 22, 1949) is a British-born American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). Her research concerns the use of genetic data to infer relationships between individuals and populations. She was the 2017–2018 president of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Thompson studied at [Newnham College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge), earning [first-class honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) in the [mathematical tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_tripos) in 1970 and completing a diploma in mathematical statistics in 1971. She continued at Cambridge for graduate studies, earning a Ph.D. in statistics in 1974 under the supervision of [A. W. F. Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._W._F._Edwards).

After postdoctoral studies at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) she returned to Cambridge as a lecturer in mathematics and mathematical statistics and fellow of [King's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge). She became a fellow of Newnham in 1981. She moved to the Department of Statistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1985, and added a joint appointment to the Department of Biostatistics in 1988. She became a U.S. citizen in 1997.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Thompson was awarded the degree of Doctor of Science (ScD) by Cambridge University in 1988 based on her published work, and became an honorary fellow of Newnham in 2013.

She became a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1998. In 2008 she joined the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences). She was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2023.

She is the [Carnegie Centenary Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Centenary_Professor) for 2017 at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).
