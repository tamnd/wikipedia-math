# Yuri Gastev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Gastev"}

*Image: Yuri Gastev.jpg.* Yuri Gastev

**Yuri Gastev** (22 March 1928 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) – 12 October 1993 in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston)) was a Soviet mathematician and cybernetician who became an active dissident, finally emigrating to the USA.

:::

Yuri was the son of [Aleksei Gastev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksei_Gastev), the revolutionary poet who later became an advocate of [scientific management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_management). Aleksei was arrested in 1938 as part of the [Great Purge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Purge) and murdered the following year. Yuri left school at 15 and started attending [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University). However two years later in 1945 he was arrested for anti-Soviet activity. Upon release he settled in [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) working as a labourer and as a teacher.[^o2]

At the time of [Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalin)'s death in 1953 Gastev was in hospital. When it was announced on the radio that Stalin had fallen into [Cheyne-Stokes breathing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheyne-Stokes_breathing), another patient, being a doctor, recognised the significance of this and declared "Cheyne-Stokes is the end, time to get out the bottle". He included references to Cheyne-Stokes when defending his degree thesis, and added references to it in a book he wrote for the [Soviet Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Cybernetics) Council. This later cases was spotted leading to the editor being sacked. Gastev had already been sacked so no action was taken against him.[^o3]

In 1981 he emigrated to [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), then to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).

Gastev died of cancer in [New England Deaconess Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_Israel_Deaconess_Medical_Center) on 12 October 1993.[^o2]
