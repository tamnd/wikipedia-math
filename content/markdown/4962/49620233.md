# Antonio Sangiovanni

*Image: Sangiovanni - Seconda squadra mobile et aritmetica, 1686 - 4682449.tif.* An illustration from *Seconda squara mobile, et aritmetica*, 1686

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Sangiovanni"}

**Antonio Sangiovanni** (or **San Giovanni**) was a 17th-century Italian [agronomist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agronomist) and mathematician.

:::

A [nobleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobleman) from [Vicenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicenza), he wrote *Seconda squara mobile*, a noteworthy work in the field of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).[^o2]
