# Francesco Ricci (mathematician)

*Image: Ricci\_-\_Nuova\_pratica\_mercantile,\_1659\_-\_4672269.tif.* *Nuova pratica mercantile* (1659)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Ricci"}

**Francesco Ricci** was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th_century) [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo).[^o2][^o3]

:::
