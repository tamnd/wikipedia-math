# Charles E. Roberts (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charles E. Roberts"}

**Charles Emery Roberts Jr.** (October 9, 1942 – March 26, 2023) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He was a professor of mathematics at [Indiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_State_University) from 1971 to 2017. He developed the [Jacchia-Roberts Atmospheric Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacchia_Reference_Atmosphere) used widely for [orbit determination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_determination) and spacecraft [atmospheric entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry) simulations in [aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Roberts was born in 1942 in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) to Evelyn and Charles Roberts Sr.[^o5] He attended [North Posey High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Posey_High_School) and graduated as valedictorian in 1960.[^o5] He studied mathematics at [Indiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_State_University), where he graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) in 1964 before attending the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), where he earned a master's in 1965.[^o7] He studied under [Geneva G. Belford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_G._Belford)[^o6] and produced a thesis on the [integral curves](wikipedia://en/1322061) of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), earning a doctorate in 1968.[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

After graduating from the University of Illinois, Roberts began working with [Pan-American Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-American_Airlines) in [Cape Canaveral, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Canaveral_Space_Force_Station) from 1968 to 1970. He wrote satellite tracking programs in conjunction with [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) during the Apollo missions, including [Apollo 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_11).[^o3]

In 1971, he returned to Indiana State and became a professor of mathematics. Roberts wrote multiple textbooks on [mathematical proofs](wikipedia://en/82285) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).[^o9] In 1971, he published a modified version of the [reference atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_atmosphere) developed by [Luigi Giuseppe Jacchia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Giuseppe_Jacchia) in 1970.[^o10] In it, he used an [asymptotic function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_function) to derive an integrable form of the [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) used to model [barometric](wikipedia://en/861530) and [molecular diffusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_diffusion) in the [upper atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_atmosphere).[^o11] The Jacchia-Roberts Density Model is widely used in aerospace applications to model satellite orbital decay, compute [orbit determination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_determination), and re-entry trajectories.[^o12][^o13][^o14]

He also published papers on celestial mechanics and developed methods for using power series to generate trajectories for orbital and suborbital vehicles.[^o15] His mathematics research also dealt with Hammerstein operators[^o16] and combinatorial theory.[^o17] Roberts received the [Theodore Dreiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Dreiser) Distinguished Research Award in 2010. He retired in 2017.[^o3]

Roberts died in 2023 in [Naperville, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naperville,_Illinois), aged 80.[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He married Imogene Roberts on August 25, 1963. They lived in [Terre Haute, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terre_Haute,_Indiana) and were married for 59 years until her death two weeks before his own. According to his wishes, his body was donated to the Anatomical Gift Association of [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago).[^o5]
