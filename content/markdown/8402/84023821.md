# Betty O'Connell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Betty O'Connell"}

*Image: Bletchley Park Mansion.jpg.* Bletchley Park Mansion

*Image: Colossus.jpg.* A [Colossus computer](wikipedia://en/6229) Mark 2 being operated by Wrens at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037), similar to the one operated by O'Connell during World War II (she operated a Mark 1)

**Margaret Elizabeth O'Connell** (née **Oliver**; 25 July 1925 - 24 July 2021), better known as **Betty O'Connell**, was a [Colossus computer](wikipedia://en/6229) operator at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Margaret Elizabeth Oliver was born in [Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry) in 1925, the second of four children of Ann (née Bennett) and Christopher Oliver, an aircraft engineer.[^o4] As World War II approached, her family moved near [Whitehaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehaven) in [Cumbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbria). She attended Calder Girls' School in [Seascale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seascale), where she was head girl and captain of the tennis, hockey, and cricket teams. Unable to attend university due to the war, she became a Wren ([Women's Royal Naval Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Royal_Naval_Service)) volunteer at age 17.[^o4][^o6]

## Codebreaking {#Codebreaking}

Partly because of her ability to solve [crossword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossword) puzzles, O'Connell was assigned to [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) in 1942, working in the [Newmanry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newmanry), [Max Newman](wikipedia://en/19867)'s section to automate codebreaking. The first [Colossus computer](wikipedia://en/6229) was installed at Newmanry in early 1944.[^o6] O'Connell was a member of the Colossus C Watch, a group of 39 women assigned to work on the Colossus computer.[^o7] O'Connell remained at Bletchley until 1945.[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1958, while playing tennis in [Putney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putney), Betty met Guy O'Connell, a former Royal Marine commando, and they married a year later. In 1974, the family moved to [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran), Iran. Guy died in 1977 and Betty moved back to Britain.[^o4] The couple had three children.[^o6]
