# Jacob Grommer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacob Grommer"}

**Jacob Grommer** (1879–1933) was a Belarusian mathematician.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in 1879 in [Brest-Litovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest-Litovsk) (then Russia, currently Brest, Belarus).[^o3] At a young age, he became interested in mathematics, and became a doctoral student under [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) in Gottingen.[^o3]

After completing his doctoral thesis in 1914, for more than 10 years he served as an assistant of [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736), working with him in his (unsuccessful) attempts to build a [unified field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_field_theory).[^o3]

As early as 1917, Einstein asked [Paul Ehrenfest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Ehrenfest) for help finding a place for Grommer, a “true Russian” in his words, in the USSR. Later Einstein facilitated Grommer’s contacts with Russian physicists to arrange his appointment as a professor of the Belarusian State University in Minsk in 1929. Shortly afterwards, Grommer was elected as a member of the Belarusian Academy of Sciences.[^o3]

He died peacefully in 1933. Starting from 1937, he was a non-person in the annals of the Belarusian Academy, and in Russia in general.[^o3]

Grommer made contributions to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).[^o3]

Grommer suffered from [acromegaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acromegaly), giving him a deformed appearance that impeded his career.[^o4][^o5]
