# Polar action

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polar action"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **polar action** is a proper and [isometric](wikipedia://en/254777) [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) *G* on a complete [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) *M* for which there exists a complete [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) Σ that meets all the [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) and meets them always orthogonally; such a submanifold is called a **section**. A section is necessarily [totally geodesic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_geodesic). If the sections of a polar action are [flat](wikipedia://en/12756616) with respect to the induced metric, then the action is called **hyperpolar**.

:::

In the case of linear orthogonal actions on [Euclidean spaces](wikipedia://en/9697), polar actions are called **polar [representations](wikipedia://en/12695)**. The isotropy representations of Riemannian [symmetric spaces](wikipedia://en/1642877) are basic examples of polar representations. Conversely, Dadok has classified polar representations of compact Lie groups on Euclidean spaces, and it follows from his classification that such a representation has the same orbits as the isotropy representation of a symmetric space.
