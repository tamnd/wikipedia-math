# Siu's semicontinuity theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siu's semicontinuity theorem"}

In [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), the **Siu semicontinuity theorem** implies that the [Lelong number](wikipedia://en/37716636) of a closed [positive current](wikipedia://en/11326411) on a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) is [semicontinuous](wikipedia://en/57824). More precisely, the points where the Lelong number is at least some constant form a complex [subvariety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subvariety_%28algebraic_geometry%29). This was conjectured by (Harvey King 1972) and proved by (txt). (Demailly 1987) generalized Siu's theorem to more general versions of the Lelong number.

:::
