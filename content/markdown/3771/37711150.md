# Killing–Hopf theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Killing–Hopf theorem"}

In geometry, the **Killing–Hopf theorem** states that complete connected [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652) of constant curvature are [isometric](wikipedia://en/254777) to a [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) of a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859), [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697), or [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742) by a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) acting [freely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) and [properly discontinuously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Properly_discontinuous_action).[^o2] These manifolds are called [space forms](wikipedia://en/1087009).
