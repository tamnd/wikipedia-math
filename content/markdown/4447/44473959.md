# Outline of vector identities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of vector identities"}

There are two lists of mathematical identities related to vectors:

:::

- [Vector algebra relations](wikipedia://en/28810270) — regarding operations on individual vectors such as dot product, cross product, etc.
- [Vector calculus identities](wikipedia://en/3114930) — regarding operations on vector fields such as divergence, gradient, curl, etc.
