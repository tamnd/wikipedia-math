# Igusa group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igusa group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **Igusa group** or **Igusa subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of the [Siegel modular group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_modular_group) defined by some congruence conditions. They were introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) Sp<sub>2*g*</sub>(**Z**) consists of the [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859)

$$\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D \end{pmatrix}$$

such that *AB<sup>t</sup>* and *CD<sup>t</sup>* are [symmetric](wikipedia://en/126474), and *AD<sup>t</sup>* − *CB<sup>t</sup>* = *I* (the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718)).

The Igusa group Γ<sub>g</sub>(*n*,2*n*) = Γ<sub>*n*,2*n*</sub> consists of the matrices

$$\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D \end{pmatrix}$$

in Sp<sub>2*g*</sub>(**Z**) such that *B* and *C* are congruent to 0 mod *n*, *A* and *D* are congruent to the identity matrix *I* mod *n*, and the diagonals of *AB<sup>t</sup>* and *CD<sup>t</sup>* are congruent to 0 mod 2*n*. We have Γ<sub>g</sub>(2*n*) ⊆ Γ<sub>g</sub>(*n*,2*n*) ⊆ Γ<sub>g</sub>(*n*) where Γ<sub>g</sub>(*n*) is the subgroup of matrices congruent to the identity modulo *n*.

:::
