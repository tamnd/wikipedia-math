# Sibylle Schroll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sibylle Schroll"}

**Sibylle Schroll** is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) in Germany. She specializes in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and is an editor-in-chief of the *Annals of Representation Theory*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Schroll grew up near [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart) in Germany, and moved to France for her undergraduate education. She obtained her doctorate through [Sorbonne Paris North University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_Paris_North_University) in France in 2003. Her dissertation, *On Alvis-Curtis duality and general linear groups*, was jointly supervised by [Michel Broué](wikipedia://en/27210822) and Markus Linckelmann.

After postdoctoral research at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), she became a research fellow at the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester), both in England. She stayed on at Leicester as a member of the academic staff and eventually as a professor. She returned to Germany as a professor at the University of Cologne in 2021. Soon afterward, she also took an adjunct professorship at the [Norwegian University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_and_Technology).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Schroll is an invited speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).
