# Hermann Schubert

**Hermann Cäsar Hannibal Schubert** (22 May 1848 – 20 July 1911) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

Schubert was one of the leading developers of [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092), which considers those parts of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) that involve a finite number of solutions. In 1874, Schubert won a prize for solving a question posed by [Zeuthen](wikipedia://en/1732797). [Schubert calculus](wikipedia://en/3061740) was named after him.

Schubert tutored [Adolf Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/771690) at the Realgymnasium Andreanum in [Hildesheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildesheim), [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover), and arranged for Hurwitz to study under [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) at University.
