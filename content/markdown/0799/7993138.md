# Heinrich Scherk

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Scherk"}

**Heinrich Ferdinand Scherk** (27 October 1798 – 4 October 1885) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) notable for his work on [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) and the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666). He is also notable as the doctoral advisor of [Ernst Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117).

:::
