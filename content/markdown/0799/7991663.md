# Buchdahl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Buchdahl"}

- [Gerd Buchdahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerd_Buchdahl) (1914–2001), German-born English philosopher of science
- [Hans Adolf Buchdahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Adolf_Buchdahl) (1919–2010), German-born Australian physicist, brother of Gerd
- [Kate Buchdahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Buchdahl) (1964–1992), Australian violinist, daughter of Hans
