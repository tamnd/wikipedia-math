# Xavier Buff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xavier Buff"}

**Xavier Buff** (born 16 December 1971) is a French mathematician, specializing in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

Buff received in 1996 his Ph.D. ([promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate)) from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) under [Adrien Douady](wikipedia://en/1604604) with thesis *Points fixe de renormalisation*. As a postdoc he was in the academic year 1997–1998 the H. C. Wang Assistant Professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). At the [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University) (Université Toulouse III) he became in 1998 a *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)*, achieved in 2006 his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) with habilitation thesis *Disques de Siegel et ensembles de Julia d'aire strictement positive*, and became in 2008 a full professor.[^o4]

In 2010 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Hyderabad and gave a talk *Quadratic Julia Sets with Positive Area* based on joint work with [Arnaud Chéritat](wikipedia://en/45468443). In 2006 Buff and Chéritat received the *Prix Leconte* of the French Academy of Sciences for their collaborative work on [Julia sets](wikipedia://en/56434) with positive mass; they proved the existence of quadratic polynomials that have positive Lebesgue measure.[^o5] In 2008 Buff, Chéritat, and Pascale Roesch received a Young Researchers grant[^o6] from [Agence Nationale de la Recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agence_Nationale_de_la_Recherche). In 2009 Buff became a member of the Institut Universitaire de France.
