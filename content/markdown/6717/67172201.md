# Boundary (graph theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boundary"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **outer boundary** of a subset $S$ of the [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) of a [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) $G$ is the set of vertices in $G$ that are adjacent to vertices in $S$, but not in $S$ themselves. The **inner boundary** is the set of vertices in $S$ that have a neighbor outside $S$. The **edge boundary** is the set of edges with one endpoint in the inner boundary and one endpoint in the outer boundary.[^o2]

:::

These boundaries and their sizes are particularly relevant for [isoperimetric problems in graphs](wikipedia://en/7762273), [separator theorems](wikipedia://en/18978005), [minimum cuts](wikipedia://en/3562453), [expander graphs](wikipedia://en/9313), and [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805).
