# Ethelwynn Rice Beckwith

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ethelwynn Rice Beckwith"}

**Mary Ethelwynn Rice Beckwith** (January 7, 1879 – August 31, 1955) was an American mathematics educator. She held a PhD in economics from [Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College), and taught for over forty years, at the [Emma Willard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Willard_School), [Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University), [Vassar College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassar_College), and [Milwaukee-Downer College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee-Downer_College).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Mary Ethelwynn Rice was born in [Hartford, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_Connecticut), the daughter of William Holden Price and Elizabeth P. Kinney Rice.[^o4] Her father was an ordained minister, and she spent some of her girlhood in Hawaii, where she was valedictorian in the [Punahou School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punahou_School) class of 1896.[^o5]

Rice graduated from [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) in 1900,[^o6] and earned a master's degree at Western Reserve University in 1909.[^o7] She pursued graduate studies at Vassar College and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and completed a doctorate in economics at Radcliffe College in 1925, with a dissertation titled "Inequalities in the Distribution of Income, their Meaning and Measurement."[^o8] She was treasurer of the Radcliffe Graduate Club.[^o9][^o10]

## Career {#Career}

Beckwith was a widow in her mid-twenties when she taught mathematics at the Emma Willard School in New York from 1905 to 1907. She taught mathematics at Western Reserve University from 1913 to 1920,[^o7] and at Vassar College from 1921 to 1925. She was professor of mathematics and astronomy[^o12] and head of the mathematics department at Milwaukee-Downer College in Wisconsin from 1925 to 1947.[^o13][^o14] She was an active member of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o15][^o16] She traveled to [Trujillo, Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trujillo,_Peru) in 1936 to witness a [total solar eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_June_8,_1937), and wrote about the trip and the event for the *Oberlin Alumnae Magazine*, quipping that "no subject under the sun is more worthy of study than the sun itself".[^o17]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1900, Ethelwynn Rice married William Erastus Beckwith. They lived in [Paia, Maui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paia,_Hawaii),[^o19] where he died in 1904. She died from injuries sustained in a car accident in 1955, aged 77 years, in [Manitowoc, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manitowoc,_Wisconsin).[^o20][^o21]
