# 2021 Czech census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2021 Czech census"}

Sčítání lidu, domů a bytů 2021 v Česku

The **2021 Census of the Czech Republic** took place between 27 March and 9 April 2021.[^o4] It was conducted by the [Czech Statistical Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Statistical_Office) at a cost estimated to be 2.23 billion [Czech koruna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_koruna).[^o5] Failure to complete the census could lead to a fine of 10,000 [Czech koruna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_koruna).[^o6]

:::

## Preliminary results {#Preliminary_results}

Preliminary results were released in January 2022. The national population was 10.52 million, which represented an increase of approximately 90,000 from the previous census in 2011. At the same time, the average age was reported at 42.7 years, an increase of 1.7 years in the last ten years.[^o8]
