# Melih Onuş

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Melih Onuş"}

**Melih Onuş** (8 February 1981 – 10 September 2020) was a Turkish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [computer engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_engineering) and [scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientist). He represented [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) in 1998 and 1999, at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) and was awarded a bronze and a silver medal, respectively. He died in the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Melih Onuş, who spent his early years in Ankara, completed his primary education in Iltekin Primary School and his secondary education in [Atatürk Anatolian High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_Atatürk_Anadolu_Lisesi). During his secondary education he represented Turkey at the 39th and 40th [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) and was awarded a bronze and a silver medal, respectively.[^o4]

In 2003, he graduated from [Bilkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_University) Computer Engineering Department by completing his [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) Between 2003 and 2005, he worked as a research assistant in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University) in the United States. In the same period, in 2004, he attended a summer school in the Computer Science department at the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). In 2006, at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory) in [New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico), he conducted his work with Hristo N. Djidjev entitled "A Scalable and Accurate Graph Clustering and Community Structure Detection".[^o5] After completing his doctorate study entitled "Overlay Network Construction in Decentralized Networks" at Arizona State University under the supervision of Dr. Andr´ea W. Richa he returned to Turkey.[^o6]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After returning to Turkey, he worked at [TOBB University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TOBB_University_of_Economics_and_Technology), [Bilkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_University) and [Çankaya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Çankaya_University) Computer Engineering Departments. He continued his academic life at [Bartın University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartın_University) from 2016 until his death.[^o8]

## Death {#Death}

Melih Onuş was infected with [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19), while visiting his family in Ankara amid [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic) in 2020. He died at a hospital in Ankara on 10 September 2020.[^o10] His father died 3 days later also from the virus. Both were buried in Ankara Ortaköy Cemetery.[^o11]
