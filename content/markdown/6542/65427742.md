# Ilya Kapovich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ilya Kapovich"}

**Ilya Kapovich** is a Russian-American mathematician and a [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York). He is known for his contributions to [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), and [complexity theory](wikipedia://en/6511).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He received his [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York) in 1996 under the direction of [Gilbert Baumslag](wikipedia://en/11449648). After postdoc positions at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) and [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University), he worked at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) from 2000 to 2018.[^o5] While at Illinois, he has had several visiting positions. Most notably, he was an [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation) research fellow at [Frankfurt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_University) during Spring 2006, and Ada Peluso Visiting Professor at [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College) during Spring 2017.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012, he was named an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He delivered an invited address at 2008 Spring Eastern Sectional meeting of the AMS.[^o8]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

He has worked on problems related to automorphisms of free groups, free-by-cyclic groups, mapping class groups as well as complexity and decision problems in group theory. He has supervised 9 PhD students to completion as of 2022.

Between 2010 and 2015, he served on the editorial board of the [LMS Journal of Mathematics and Computation](wikipedia://en/63370948).[^o5]

His older brother [Michael Kapovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kapovich) was a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis), and his twin brother [Vitali Kapovitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Kapovitch) is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).
