# Philip Rabinowitz (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Rabinowitz"}

**Philip Rabinowitz** (פיליפ רבינוביץ; August 14, 1926 – July 21, 2006) was an American and [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o3] He was best known for his work in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), including his books *A First Course in Numerical Analysis* with Anthony Ralston and *Methods of Numerical Integration* with [Philip J. Davis](wikipedia://en/3753768). He was the author of numerous articles on numerical computation.

:::

He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1951 under [Walter Gottschalk](wikipedia://en/37710667) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). He worked for the [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [National Bureau of Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Standards) and taught at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).
