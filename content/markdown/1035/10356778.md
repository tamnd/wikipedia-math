# Leonard Carlitz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leonard Carlitz"}

**Leonard Carlitz** (December 26, 1907 – September 17, 1999) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Carlitz supervised 44 doctorates at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) and published more than 770 papers.

:::

## Chronology {#Chronology}

- 1907 Born [Philadelphia, PA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia), USA
- 1927 BA, [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania)
- 1930 PhD, University of Pennsylvania, 1930 under Howard Mitchell, who had studied under [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen) at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University)
- 1930–31 at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) with [E. T. Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._T._Bell)
- 1931 married Clara Skaler
- 1931–32 at [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) with [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599)
- 1932 Joined the faculty of Duke University where he served for 45 years
- 1938 to 1973 Editorial Board [Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal) (Managing Editor from 1945.)
- 1939 Birth of son Michael
- 1940 Supervision of his first doctoral student E. F. Canaday, awarded 1940
- 1945 Birth of son Robert
- 1964 First [James B. Duke Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_B._Duke_Professor) in Mathematics
- 1971 Birth of granddaughter Natasha
- 1977 Supervised his 44th and last doctoral student, Jo Ann Lutz, awarded 1977
- 1977 Retired
- 1982 Birth of granddaughter Ruth
- 1990 Death of wife Clara, after 59 years of marriage
- 1999 September 17 Died in Pittsburgh, PA

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

- The [Carlitz module](wikipedia://en/5578871#Examples_of_Drinfeld_modules) is generalized by the [Drinfeld module](wikipedia://en/5578871)
- [An identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_numbers#Assorted_identities) regarding [Bernoulli numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_numbers)
- Carlitz wrote about [Bessel polynomials](wikipedia://en/6523885)
- He introduced [Al-Salam–Carlitz polynomials](wikipedia://en/32680463).
- [Carlitz' identity for bicentric quadrilaterals](wikipedia://en/23335118#Carlitz'_identity)
- He conjectured the [Carlitz-Wan conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlitz-Wan_conjecture), later proved by [Daqing Wan](wikipedia://en/22098796).

## Publications {#Publications}

Leonard Carlitz published about 771 technical papers comprising approximately 7,000 pages. The effort to edit his collected works, undertaken originally by Professor [John Brillhart](wikipedia://en/11176492), is ongoing.[^o6]
