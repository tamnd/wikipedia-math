# Minnesota statistical areas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minnesota statistical areas"}

The [U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [State of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota) currently has 34 [statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_area_%28United_States%29) that have been delineated by the [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget) (OMB). On July 21, 2023, the OMB delineated six [combined statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_statistical_area), nine [metropolitan statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area), and 19 [micropolitan statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micropolitan_statistical_area) in Minnesota.[^o2] As of 2025, the largest of these is the [Minneapolis–St. Paul, MN-WI CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis-St._Paul-St._Cloud,_MN-WI_CSA), comprising the area around the state's [twin cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_cities) — [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis), its largest city, and [St. Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Paul,_Minnesota), its capital.
