# Guozhen Lu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guozhen Lu"}

*Image: Guozhenlu.jpg.* Guozhen Lu

**Guozhen Lu** (; born 1963) is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut). He is known for his contributions to [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lu graduated from [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) in 1983 and earned his Ph.D. from [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in 1991. He was a Bateman Research Instructor in the Department of Mathematics at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) from 1991 to 1993, an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at [Wright State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright_State_University) from 1993 to 1997, and an associate professor of the same department from 1997 to 2000 before he moved to Wayne State University. He became a professor of mathematics at [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University) in 2002. He joined the University of Connecticut in 2016 as a professor. Lu has co-organized many international conferences, including the series of the East Asian Conference in Harmonic Analysis and Applications, which is held annually, rotating between China, [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), and [Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea).[^o3][^o4] Lu is the founding organizer, since the beginning of the pandemic in 2020, together with his past and current PhD students, of the worldwide online seminar "[Geometric and Functional Inequalities and Applications.](https://geometricinequalities.com/)"

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Lu was elected Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science),[^o6] class of 2023, and elected to the [Connecticut Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences),[^o7] class of 2023-2024, and to the [Connecticut Academy of Science and Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Academy_of_Science_and_Engineering),[^o8] class of 2025. In 2015 and 2023, Lu was awarded a Simons Fellowship in Mathematics from the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o9] In 2017, he was elected to the 2018 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to harmonic analysis and partial differential equations, and for service to the mathematical community."[^o10] The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) named him to their 2025 Class of AWM Fellows.[^o11] Lu was elected in March 2026 as an honorary member of [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa).[^o12]

## Editorial board service {#Editorial_board_service}

Lu has served as an editor of numerous mathematical research journals including *Nonlinear Analysis*,[^o14] *Advanced Nonlinear Studies* [^o15] *Acta Mathematica Sinica* (English Series),[^o16] *Communications on Pure and Applied Analysis*,[^o17] *Journal of Pseudo-Differential Operators and Applications*,[^o18] *The Journal of Geometric Analysis*,[^o19] and *Bulletin des Sciences Mathématiques*, also known as the *Darboux Journal*, founded by Guston Darboux in 1870.[^o20]

Since January 2022, Lu has served as the Editor-in-Chief of *Advanced Nonlinear Studies* (De Gruyter).[^o21] Since 2023, Lu serves as Editor-in-Chief of the book series *de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics*.[^o22] As of 2024, Lu is the Editor-in-Chief of *Forum Mathematicum*.[^o23]

## Community service {#Community_service}

Lu has served as a member of the [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan) governor's advisory council on Asian Pacific American Affairs from 2005 to 2009,[^o25][^o26] and as a commissioner of Michigan Asian Pacific American Affairs Commission from 2009 to 2016.[^o27][^o28]
