# Martin Krause (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Krause"}

*Image: Martin Krause (HeidICON 33488) (cropped).jpg.* Martin Krause

**Martin Johann Krause** (29 June 1851 in [Wilknit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilknity),[^o2] [East Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Prussia) – 2 March 1920 in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden)) was a German mathematician, specializing in analysis.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Martin Krause, the son of a landowner, studied from 1870 to 1874 at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg), where he was taught by [Friedrich Julius Richelot](wikipedia://en/20346321) and [Franz Ernst Neumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Ernst_Neumann), and also in Heidelberg and Berlin.[^o4] In 1873 Krause received his doctorate from [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). His doctoral thesis *Zur Transformation der Modulargleichungen der elliptischen Functionen* (On the transformation of the modular equations of the elliptic functions) was supervised by [Leo Königsberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Königsberger). In 1875 Krause habilitated at Heidelberg University with thesis *Über die Discriminante der Modulargleichungen der elliptischen Functionen*. From 1876 to 1878 he was a *[Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)* at the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau). From 1878 to 1888 he was a professor ordinarius at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock).[^o4] In 1888 he became the successor to [Axel Harnack](wikipedia://en/1925276) as professor at [TU Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Dresden) in 1888. He founded the Mathematical Association there in 1903 and was awarded in 1912 the right to award TU Dresden doctorates.[^o6] From 1894 to 1896 and again from 1919 to 1920 he was rector there.[^o7] His son was the law professor [Herbert Kraus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Kraus).[^o4]

Krause did research on [elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/69939). In 1909 he was president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973). He was a member of the [Saxon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxon_Academy_of_Sciences).

*Image: The grave of Prof Martin Krause, Johannisfriedhof, Dresden.JPG.* The grave of Martin Krause, Johannisfriedhof, Dresden

Upon his death in 1920 he was buried in Dresden's [Johannis cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannisfriedhof,_Dresden).[^o8]
