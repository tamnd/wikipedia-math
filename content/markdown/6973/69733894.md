# 2022 opinion polling on the Biden administration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2022 opinion polling on the Biden administration"}

This is a list of opinion polls taken on the [presidency of Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Joe_Biden) in 2022. To navigate between years, see [opinion polling on the Biden administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Biden_administration). For 2023 opinion polling, see [2023 opinion polling on the Biden administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_opinion_polling_on_the_Biden_administration).

:::

Throughout 2022, Biden continued having a higher disapproval than approval rating, which had begun in [August 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_opinion_polling_on_the_Joe_Biden_administration#August).[^o2] Biden's approval was steady throughout the year at approximately 42% with the exception of a significant dip during the summer which saw [a period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021–2023_inflation_surge) of high gas prices and high inflation.[^o3][^o4] At certain points, two-thirds of Americans disapproved of Biden's handling of the economy.[^o5]

According to the [Gallup poll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallup,_Inc.), during Biden's seventh quarter in office (defined as July 20 to October 19) he had approximately a 42% approval rating. This was found comparable to other presidents at the same time during their first term since [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter), with the exception of the majority approval enjoyed by both [George H. W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) and [George W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush).[^o6]

## By race {#By_race}

In December 2021 a PBS/Marist poll, found that just 33% of Hispanics approve of Biden, while 65% disapprove. In contrast, 40% of whites approve, while 56% disapprove.[^o271]

In May 2022, a Quinnipiac poll found that Biden had an approval rating from Hispanics at 26%, White voters at 29%, and 63% from Black voters.[^o272] In June 3–6, 2022, a Quinnipiac poll found that Biden had his then lowest approval rating from Hispanics at 24%, and 49% of Blacks.[^o273]

On July 19, 2022, a Quinnipiac poll found that Biden had his lowest approval rating from Hispanics at 19%, while 70% disapprove.[^o274]
