# Chentsov's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chentsov's theorem"}

In [information geometry](wikipedia://en/487312), **Chentsov's theorem** states that the [Fisher information metric](wikipedia://en/488687) is, up to rescaling, the unique [Riemannian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) on a [statistical manifold](wikipedia://en/36408395) that is invariant under [sufficient statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficient_statistics).

:::

The theorem is named after mathematician [Nikolai Chentsov](wikipedia://en/77009002), who proved it in his 1981 paper.
