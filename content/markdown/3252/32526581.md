# Michele Marsonet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michele Marsonet"}

**Michele Marsonet** (born 1950) is professor of [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science) and methodology of the human sciences, chairman of the [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) department and vice-rector for international relations at the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa) in Italy. Having worked as associate professor, first of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and then of philosophy of science, at the University of Genoa from 1992 to 1999, he was then a full professor of theoretical philosophy and institutions of philosophy. He was also dean of the faculty of arts and humanities of the University of Genoa from 2002 to 2008. His main areas of study are in [pragmatism](wikipedia://en/95170), philosophy of science, [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), methodology of the social sciences, political philosophy and [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939). He has published extensively on the works of [Nicholas Rescher](wikipedia://en/576633).

:::

He graduated from the University of Genoa (1973), then earned a PhD from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) (1979), with subsequent periods of study at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford), [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester) and [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). He has been a visiting professor at the Universities of [Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Seville) (Spain, Department of Philosophy), [Málaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Málaga) (Spain, Department of Philosophy), Iceland ([Reykjavík University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reykjavík_University), Department of Philosophy), Ireland ([University College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Cork), Department of Philosophy), [Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malta) (Faculty of Humanities), [Beirut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beirut) ([Université Libanaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Libanaise), Faculty of Social Sciences), London ([King's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London)), [Hertfordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hertfordshire) (Department of Philosophy), [Stirling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stirling) (UK, Department of Philosophy), [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) (Norway, Institute of Philosophy), [Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) (U.S., Department of Philosophy).
