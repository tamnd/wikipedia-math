# Morris Hirsch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morris Hirsch"}

**Morris William Hirsch** (born June 28, 1933) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), formerly at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).

:::

A native of [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois), Hirsch attained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1958, under supervision of [Edwin Spanier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin_Spanier) and [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294). His thesis was entitled *Immersions of Manifolds*. In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]

Hirsch had 23 doctoral students, including [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), [William Goldman](wikipedia://en/1298822), and [Mary Lou Zeeman](wikipedia://en/54900921).
