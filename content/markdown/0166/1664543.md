# Dodecagonal number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dodecagonal number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **dodecagonal number** is a [figurate number](wikipedia://en/325260) that represents a [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486). The dodecagonal number for *n* is given by the formula

:::

$$D_{n}=5n^2 - 4n$$

The first few dodecagonal numbers are:

[0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0_%28number%29), [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), [12](wikipedia://en/208155), [33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/33_%28number%29), [64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64_%28number%29), [105](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/105_%28number%29), [156](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/156_%28number%29), [217](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/217_%28number%29), [288](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/288_%28number%29), 369, 460, [561](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/561_%28number%29), 672, 793, 924, 1065, 1216, 1377, 1548, [1729](wikipedia://en/512549), ...

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The dodecagonal number for *n* can be calculated by adding the square of *n* to four times the (*n* - 1)th [pronic number](wikipedia://en/652733), or to put it algebraically, $D_n = n^2 + 4(n^2 - n)$.
- Dodecagonal numbers consistently alternate [parity](wikipedia://en/143135), and in base 10, their units place digits follow the pattern 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 0.
- By the [Fermat polygonal number theorem](wikipedia://en/847886), every number is the sum of at most 12 dodecagonal numbers.
- $D_n$ is the sum of the first n natural numbers congruent to 1 mod 10.
- $D_{n+1}$ is the sum of all odd numbers from 4n+1 to 6n+1.

:::

## Sum of reciprocals {#Sum_of_reciprocals}

A formula for the [sum of the reciprocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sums_of_reciprocals) of the dodecagonal numbers is given by

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{5n^{2}-4n}=\frac{5}{16}\ln\left(5\right)+\frac{\sqrt{5}}{8}\ln\left(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)+\frac{\pi}{8}\sqrt{1+\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}}.$$
