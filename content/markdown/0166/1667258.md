# Semi-Hilbert space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semi-Hilbert space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **semi-Hilbert space** is a generalization of a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), in which, roughly speaking, the [inner product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_product) is required only to be [positive semi-definite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_semidefinite_quadratic_form) rather than [positive definite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Definite_bilinear_form), so that it gives rise to a [seminorm](wikipedia://en/292067) rather than a [vector space norm](wikipedia://en/990534).

:::

The quotient of this space by the kernel of this seminorm is also required to be a Hilbert space in the usual sense.
