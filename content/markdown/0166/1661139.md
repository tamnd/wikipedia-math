# Brauer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brauer"}

**Brauer** or **Bräuer** is a [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) of German origin, meaning "brewer". Notable people with the name include:-

:::

- [Alfred Brauer](wikipedia://en/22923580) (1894–1985), German-American mathematician, brother of Richard
- [Arik Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arik_Brauer) (1929–2021), Austrian painter, poet, and actor, father of Timna Brauer
- [August Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Brauer) (1863–1917), German zoologist
- [Cole Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Brauer) (born 1994), American sailor
- [Erich Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_Brauer) (1895–1942), German illustrator, ethnographer, and ethnologist
- [Friedrich Moritz Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Moritz_Brauer) (1832–1904), Austrian entomologist and museum director
- [Georg Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Brauer) (1908–2001), German chemist
- [Helga Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helga_Brauer) (1936–1991) was a of Schlager singer of the GDR.
- [Ingrid Arndt-Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingrid_Arndt-Brauer) (born 1961), German politician; member of the Bundestag
- [Jono Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jono_Brauer) (born 1981), Australian Olympic skier
- [Max Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Brauer) (1887–1973), German politician; First Mayor of Hamburg
- [Michael Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Brauer) (contemporary), American audio engineer
- [Rich Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_Brauer) (born 1954), American politician from Illinois; state legislator since 2003
- [Richard Brauer](wikipedia://en/605134) (1901–1977), German-American mathematician
- [Richard H. W. Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_H._W._Brauer) (contemporary), American art museum director; eponym of the Brauer Museum of Art in Valparaiso, Indiana
- [Simón Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simón_Brauer) (born 1973), Ecuadorian photographer and cinematographer
- [S. H. Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._H._Brauer) (1899–1976), American politician from Nebraska
- [Tage Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tage_Brauer) (1894–1988), Swedish Olympic high jumper
- [Timna Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timna_Brauer) (born 1961), Austrian singer and songwriter, daughter of Arik Brauer
- [Tiny Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiny_Brauer) (1909–1990), American actor
- [Wilfried Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfried_Brauer) (1937–2014), German computer scientist

**Bräuer**

- [Bruno Bräuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Bräuer) (1893–1947), German general officer and paratrooper; executed in Greece for war crimes
- [Curt Bräuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curt_Bräuer) (1889–1969), German diplomat; ambassador to Norway at the time of the German invasion
