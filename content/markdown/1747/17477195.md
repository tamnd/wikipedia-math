# Jürgen Neukirch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jürgen Neukirch"}

**Jürgen Neukirch** (24 July 1937 – 5 February 1997[^o3]) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work on [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Neukirch received his diploma in mathematics in 1964 from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). For his Ph.D. thesis, written under the direction of [Wolfgang Krull](wikipedia://en/964328), he was awarded in 1965 the [Felix-Hausdorff-Gedächtnis-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix-Hausdorff-Gedächtnis-Preis). He completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) one year later. From 1967 to 1969 he was guest professor at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario) and at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), after which he was a professor in Bonn. In 1971 he became a professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg).[^o5]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

He is known for his work on the [embedding problem](wikipedia://en/12692795) in algebraic number theory, the [Báyer–Neukirch theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Báyer–Neukirch_theorem) on [special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882), arithmetic Riemann existence theorems and the [Neukirch–Uchida theorem](wikipedia://en/42095155) in birational [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265). He gave a simple description of the reciprocity maps in [local](wikipedia://en/669532) and global [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695).

## Books {#Books}

Neukirch wrote three books on [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695), [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), and the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of number fields:

- Neukirch, Jürgen. (1986). "Class Field Theory". 280. Springer-Verlag. isbn:3-540-15251-2[^o9]
- Neukirch, Jürgen. (1999). "Algebraic Number Theory". 322. Springer-Verlag. isbn:978-3-540-65399-8. zbl:0956.11021
- Neukirch, Jürgen; Schmidt, Alexander; Wingberg, Kay. (2008). "Cohomology of Number Fields". 323. Springer-Verlag. isbn:978-3-540-37888-4. zbl:1136.11001[^o12]
- Neukirch, Jürgen. (2013). "Class Field Theory — The Bonn Lectures". Springer. isbn:978-3-642-35436-6[^o14]
