# Transgression map

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transgression map"}

In algebraic topology, a **transgression map** is a way to transfer [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) classes. It occurs, for example in the [inflation-restriction exact sequence](wikipedia://en/31364787) in [group cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635), and in [integration in fibers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integration_in_fiber). It also naturally arises in many [spectral sequences](wikipedia://en/803894); see [spectral sequence#Edge maps and transgressions](wikipedia://en/803894#Edge_maps_and_transgressions).

:::

## Inflation-restriction exact sequence {#Inflation-restriction_exact_sequence}

The transgression map appears in the [inflation-restriction exact sequence](wikipedia://en/31364787), an [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215) occurring in [group cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635). Let *G* be a [group](wikipedia://en/19447), *N* a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918), and *A* an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) which is equipped with an action of *G*, i.e., a [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) from *G* to the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/1160) of *A*. The quotient group $G/N$ acts on

$$A^N = \{ a \in A : na = a \text{ for all } n \in N\}.$$

Then the inflation-restriction exact sequence is:

$$0 \to H^1(G/N, A^N) \to H^1(G, A) \to H^1(N, A)^{G/N} \to H^2(G/N, A^N) \to H^2(G, A).$$

The transgression map is the map $H^1(N, A)^{G/N} \to H^2(G/N, A^N)$.

Transgression is defined for general $n\in \mathbb{N}$,

$$H^n(N, A)^{G/N} \to H^{n+1}(G/N, A^N),$$

only if $H^i(N, A)^{G/N} = 0$ for $i\le n-1$.[^o3]
