# John Williamson (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Williamson"}

**John Williamson** (23 May 1901 – 1949) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in the fields of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), and [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422). Among other contributions, he is known for the Williamson construction of [Hadamard matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard_matrices).[^o3] Williamson graduated from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) with [first-class honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_honours) in 1922. Awarded a newly established [Commonwealth Fund Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Fund_Fellowship) in 1925, he studied at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) under the direction of [L. E. Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247) and [E. H. Moore](wikipedia://en/1532860), receiving the Ph.D. in 1927. He held a Lectureship in Mathematics at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) and an Associate Professorship in Mathematics at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University).
