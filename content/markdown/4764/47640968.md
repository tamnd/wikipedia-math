# Joan Moschovakis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joan Moschovakis"}

**Joan Rand Moschovakis** is a [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) focusing on [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262) and mathematics. She is professor emerita at [Occidental College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_College)[^o3] and a guest at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA).[^o4]

:::

Moschovakis earned her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1965 under the direction of [Stephen Kleene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Kleene), with a dissertation titled *Disjunction, Existence and \*-Eliminability in Formalized Intuitionistic Analysis*.

Moschovakis is married to [Yiannis Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002), with whom she gave the 2014 [Lindström](wikipedia://en/28868368) Lectures at the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg).[^o5]
