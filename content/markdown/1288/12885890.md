# Toric manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toric manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **toric manifold** is a topological analogue of [toric variety](wikipedia://en/737087) in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). It is an even-dimensional [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) with an effective smooth [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of an $n$-dimensional compact torus which is locally standard with the orbit space a simple convex [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471).[^o2][^o3]

:::

The aim is to do combinatorics on the quotient polytope and obtain information on the manifold above. For example, the [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) and the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) ring of the manifold can be described in terms of the polytope.

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="The Atiyah and Guillemin-Sternberg theorem"}

## The [Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) and [Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636)-[Sternberg](wikipedia://en/5375996) theorem {#The_Atiyah_and_Guillemin-Sternberg_theorem}

This theorem states that the image of the [moment map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) of a Hamiltonian toric action is the convex hull of the set of moments of the points fixed by the action. In particular, this image is a convex polytope.

:::
