# Emmanuel Grenier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emmanuel Grenier"}

**Emmanuel Grenier** (born 3 November 1970) is a French mathematician. His research interests include [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics) (e.g. [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395), [Euler equations](wikipedia://en/396022)) and [mathematical biology](wikipedia://en/662088).

:::

Grenier attended [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 1990 to 1994, before attaining his doctorate at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), under supervision of [Yann Brenier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_Brenier). He won an [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 2000.[^o4]
