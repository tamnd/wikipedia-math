# Anna Fino

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Fino"}

**Anna Maria Fino** is an Italian mathematician specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), and [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). She is a professor of mathematics in the Giuseppe Peano Department of Mathematics at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), and founding editor-in-chief of the journal *Complex Manifolds*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fino earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics in 1992 from the University of Turin. She completed her Ph.D. in 1997 through the Genoa-Turin University Consortium, with a dissertation *Geometria e topologia degli spazi omogenei* \[*Geometry and topology of homogeneous spaces*\] supervised by Simon Salamon.

She remained as a researcher at the University of Turin until 2005, when she became an associate professor. She earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2013 and was promoted to full professor in 2015.

She has been editor-in-chief of *Complex Manifolds* since 2014 when it first began publication, as part of De Gruyter's "Emerging Science Journals" line of open-access journals.
