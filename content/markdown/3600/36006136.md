# The Annals of Applied Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Annals of Applied Statistics"}

***The Annals of Applied Statistics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), covering all areas of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), featuring papers in the applied half of this range. It was established in 2007, with [Bradley Efron](wikipedia://en/7250868) as founding editor-in-chief.[^o3] According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.8.[^o4]

:::
