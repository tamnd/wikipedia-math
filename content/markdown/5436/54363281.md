# Zbyněk Šidák

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Zbyněk Šidák"}

**Zbyněk Šidák** (24 October 1933 – 12 November 1999) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_%28people%29) mathematician. He is known for developing the [Šidák correction](wikipedia://en/38510169).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Šidák was born and raised in [Golčův Jeníkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golčův_Jeníkov).[^o6] He completed his undergraduate studies in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) in 1956, received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1961, and a [DrSc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) in 1973.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Beginning in 1956, and continuing until his death, Šidák was a researcher in the mathematical department of the [Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Academy_of_Sciences) and spent several years as head of the Department of Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics.[^o6]

During his life, Šidák held posts as a visiting faculty member at the [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University), [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina), [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), and others.[^o6] He also served as chief editor of the scholarly journal *Applications of Mathematics*.[^o6]

The Šidák correction, a method used to counteract the problem of [multiple comparisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_comparisons), was credited to a 1967 paper by Šidák.[^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1958 Šidák married Krista Štěpničková. They had two sons and a daughter.[^o6]
