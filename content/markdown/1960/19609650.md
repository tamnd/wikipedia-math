# Meixner polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Meixner polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Meixner polynomials**, also called **discrete Laguerre polynomials**, are a family of [discrete orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32730613) introduced by [Josef Meixner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Meixner).[^n1] They are given in terms of [binomial coefficients](wikipedia://en/4668) and the (rising) [Pochhammer symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pochhammer_symbol) by

:::

$$M_n(x,\beta,\gamma) = \sum_{k=0}^n (-1)^k{n \choose k}{x\choose k}k!(x+\beta)_{n-k}\gamma^{-k}.$$

Meixner polynomials belong to the [Hahn class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hahn_polynomial) of polynomials, along with Hahn polynomials, [Charlier polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585960), and [Kravchuk polynomials](wikipedia://en/10997054).
