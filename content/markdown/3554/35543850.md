# Gray's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gray's conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Gray's conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) made by Brayton Gray in 1984 about maps between [loop spaces](wikipedia://en/2174332) of spheres.[^o2] It was later [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by John Harper.[^o3]

:::
