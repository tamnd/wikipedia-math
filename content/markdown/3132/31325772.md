::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stericated 5-cubes"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **stericated 5-cube** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) with fourth-order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication)) of the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

:::

There are eight degrees of sterication for the 5-cube, including permutations of [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination), [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation), and [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429). The simple stericated 5-cube is also called an **expanded 5-cube**, with the first and last nodes ringed, for being [constructible](wikipedia://en/523430) by an [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) operation applied to the regular 5-cube. The highest form, the **steriruncicantitruncated 5-cube**, is more simply called an omnitruncated 5-cube with all of the nodes ringed.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *stericated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(\pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2})\right)$$

### Images {#Images}

The stericated 5-cube is constructed by a [sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication) operation applied to the 5-cube.

### Dissections {#Dissections}

The stericated 5-cube can be dissected into two [tesseractic cupolae](wikipedia://en/689743) and a [runcinated tesseract](wikipedia://en/2386666) between them. This dissection can be seen as analogous to the 4D runcinated tesseract being dissected into two [cubic cupolae](wikipedia://en/40384846) and a central [rhombicuboctahedral prism](wikipedia://en/28998802) between them, and also the 3D rhombicuboctahedron being dissected into two [square cupolae](wikipedia://en/1128479) with a central [octagonal prism](wikipedia://en/3026569) between them.t04

### Full snub 5-cube {#Full_snub_5-cube}

The **full snub 5-cube** or **omnisnub 5-cube**, defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 5-cube is not uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502) \[4,3,3,3\]<sup>\+</sup>, and constructed from 10 [snub tesseracts](wikipedia://en/2386666#Full_snub_tesseract), 32 [snub 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2386577#Full_snub_5-cell), 40 [snub cubic antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_cuboctahedral_prism#Related_polytopes), 80 [snub tetrahedral antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_octahedral_prism#Related_polytopes), 80 [3-4 duoantiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-8_duoprism#Related_polytopes), and 1920 irregular [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices.
