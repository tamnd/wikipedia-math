::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 5-cubes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 5-cube** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) that is a [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination) (a 3rd order truncation) of the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

:::

There are 8 unique degrees of runcinations of the 5-cube, along with permutations of truncations and cantellations. Four are more simply constructed relative to the [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *runcinated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(\pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2})\right)$$
