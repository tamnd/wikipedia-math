# Arason invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arason invariant"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Arason invariant** is a [cohomological invariant](wikipedia://en/42868641) associated to a [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) of even rank and trivial [discriminant](wikipedia://en/55607#Discriminant_of_a_quadratic_form) and [Clifford invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_invariant) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *k* of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) not 2, taking values in H<sup>3</sup>(*k*,**Z**/2**Z**). It was introduced by Jón Arason in 1975.[^o2]

:::

The [Rost invariant](wikipedia://en/39486121) is a generalization of the Arason invariant to other algebraic groups.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *W*(*k*) is the [Witt ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witt_ring_%28forms%29) of quadratic forms over a field *k* and *I* is the ideal of forms of even dimension. The Arason invariant is a [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) from *I*<sup>3</sup> to the [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) group H<sup>3</sup>(*k*,**Z**/2**Z**). It is determined by the property that on the 8-dimensional diagonal form with entries 1, –*a*, –*b*, *ab*, -*c*, *ac*, *bc*, -*abc* (the 3-fold [Pfister form](wikipedia://en/7204602)«*a*,*b*,*c*») it is given by the [cup product](wikipedia://en/848684) of the classes of *a*, *b*, *c* in H<sup>1</sup>(*k*,**Z**/2**Z**) = *k*\*/*k*\*<sup>2</sup>. The Arason invariant vanishes on *I*<sup>4</sup>, and it follows from the [Milnor conjecture](wikipedia://en/2914126) proved by Voevodsky that it is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/12397) from *I*<sup>3</sup>/*I*<sup>4</sup> to H<sup>3</sup>(*k*,**Z**/2**Z**).
