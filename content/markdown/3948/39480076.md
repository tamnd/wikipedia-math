# Ruth Kellerhals

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth Kellerhals"}

**Ruth Kellerhals** (born 17 July 1957) is a Swiss mathematician at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg), whose field of study is [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and polylogarithm identities.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

As a child, she went to a gymnasium in Basel and then studied at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), graduating in 1982 with a diploma directed by Heinz Huber "On finiteness of the isometry group of a compact negatively curved [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652)". She received her PhD in 1988, from the same university, with a thesis entitled "On the volumes of hyperbolic polytopes in dimensions three and four". Her advisor was Hans-Christoph Im Hof. During the year 1983–84 she also studied at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Grenoble) ([Fourier Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_Institute)).

In 1995 she received her habilitation from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where she worked at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) since 1989 until 1995. There, she was an assistant with Professor [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495). Since 1995 she has been an assistant professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and since 1999 a distinguished professor at the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1). In 2000 she became a professor at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg), Switzerland, where she was in 1998 to 1999 as a visiting professor.

## Research {#Research}

Her main research fields include hyperbolic geometry, geometric group theory, geometry of [discrete groups](wikipedia://en/468536) (especially [reflection groups](wikipedia://en/2422023), [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004)), convex and polyhedral geometry, volumes of hyperbolic polytopes, manifolds and polylogarithms. She does historical research into the works and life of [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408), a Swiss [geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer).[^o6]

She has been a guest researcher at [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES), [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452), the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook), [RIMS](wikipedia://en/1978962) in Kyoto, [Osaka City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_City_University), [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) and the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland). Also she visited numerous research institutes and universities in Helsinki, Berlin and Budapest.
