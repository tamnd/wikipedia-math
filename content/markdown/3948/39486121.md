# Rost invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rost invariant"}

In mathematics, the **Rost invariant** is a [cohomological invariant](wikipedia://en/42868641) of an [absolutely simple](wikipedia://en/4966802) simply connected [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) *G* over a field *k*, which associates an element of the [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) group H<sup>3</sup>(*k*, **Q**/**Z**(2)) to a [principal homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363441) for *G*. Here the coefficient group **Q**/**Z**(2) is the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of the group of [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) of an [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of *k* with itself. [Markus Rost](wikipedia://en/6842771)[^o2] first introduced the invariant for groups of type F<sub>4</sub> and later extended it to more general groups in unpublished work that was summarized by Serre in 1995.[^o3]

:::

The Rost invariant is a generalization of the [Arason invariant](wikipedia://en/39484059).

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *G* is an absolutely almost simple simply connected algebraic group over a field *k*. The Rost invariant associates an element *a*(*P*) of the Galois cohomology group H<sup>3</sup>(*k*,**Q**/**Z**(2)) to a *G*-torsor *P*.

The element *a*(*P*) is constructed as follows. For any [extension](wikipedia://en/11634) *K* of *k* there is an [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215)

$$0\rightarrow H^3(K,\mathbf{Q}/\mathbf{Z}(2)) \rightarrow H^3_{et}(P_K, \mathbf{Q}/\mathbf{Z}(2)) \rightarrow \mathbf{Q}/\mathbf{Z}$$

where the middle group is the [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) group and **Q**/**Z** is the geometric part of the cohomology. Choose a finite extension *K* of *k* such that *G* splits over *K* and *P* has a rational point over *K*. Then the exact sequence [splits](wikipedia://en/1531404) canonically as a direct sum so the étale cohomology group contains **Q**/**Z** canonically. The invariant *a*(*P*) is the image of the element 1/\[*K*:*k*\] of **Q**/**Z** under the trace map from H${}^{3}_{\operatorname{et}}$(*P*<sub>*K*</sub>,**Q**/**Z**(2)) to H${}^{3}_{\operatorname{et}}$(*P*,**Q**/**Z**(2)), which lies in the subgroup H<sup>3</sup>(*k*,**Q**/**Z**(2)).

These invariants *a*(*P*) are functorial in field extensions *K* of *k*; in other words the fit together to form an element of the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) Inv<sup>3</sup>(*G*,**Q**/**Z**(2)) of cohomological invariants of the group *G*, which consists of morphisms of the functor *K*→H<sup>1</sup>(*K*,*G*) to the functor *K*→H<sup>3</sup>(*K*,**Q**/**Z**(2)). This element of Inv<sup>3</sup>(*G*,**Q**/**Z**(2)) is a generator of the group and is called the Rost invariant of *G*.
