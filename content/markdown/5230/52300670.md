# Ngaiming Mok

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ngaiming Mok"}

Mok Ngaiming

**Ngaiming Mok** (; born 1956) is a [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in complex [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is currently a professor at the [University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hong_Kong).[^o4]

:::

After graduating from [St. Paul's Co-educational College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Paul's_Co-educational_College) in Hong Kong in 1975,[^o5] Mok studied at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), obtaining his M.A. in Mathematics from Yale in 1978.[^o6] He obtained his Ph.D. from Stanford University under the guidance of [Yum-Tong Siu](wikipedia://en/6085925).[^o7] He taught at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and the [University of Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Saclay) before joining the faculty of the University of Hong Kong in 1994. He has been the director of the University of Hong Kong's Institute of Mathematical Research since 1999.

The awards Mok has received include a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 1984, the [Presidential Young Investigator Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Young_Investigator_Award) in Mathematics in 1985, and the [Stefan Bergman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bergman_Prize) in 2009.[^o8] Mok was an [invited speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich and served on the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) committee at the 2010 ICM in Hyderabad. He was on the editorial board of [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae) from 2002 to 2014,[^o9] and he is currently an editor of [Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381).[^o10] He was elected as Member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (Division of Mathematics and Physics) in 2015,[^o11] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2019. [^o12] In 2023, Mok became a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*.[^o13]

Mok is a [polyglot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyglot), being able to speak Chinese (including Mandarin and Cantonese), English, French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese and more.
