# Lexing Ying

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lexing Ying"}

**Lexing Ying** is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where he is also a member of the Institute for Computational and Mathematical Engineering. He specializes in [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). In particular, his research concerns the design of numerical algorithms for problems in scientific computing.[^o2]

:::

Ying received his bachelor's degree in computer science and applied mathematics from [Shanghai Jiaotong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiaotong_University) in 1998. He received his Ph.D. from the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 2004, under the guidance of [Denis Zorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Zorin).[^o3] Before joining Stanford in 2012, he was a post-doc at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and a professor at [University of Texas, Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas,_Austin).

The awards Ying has received include a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 2007, an [NSF Career Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSF_Career_Award) in 2009, the [James H. Wilkinson](wikipedia://en/301380) Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing in 2013 (for "his outstanding contributions in many areas, including the rapid evaluation of oscillatory integral transforms, high frequency wave propagation and the computation of electron structure in metallic systems"),[^o4] and a silver [Morningside Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) in 2016.[^o5] He is an invited speaker of International Congress of Mathematicians 2022.
