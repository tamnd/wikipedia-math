# Icositruncated dodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Icositruncated dodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Icositruncated dodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of an icositruncated dodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **icositruncated dodecadodecahedron** or **icosidodecatruncated icosidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>45</sub>.

:::

## Convex hull {#Convex_hull}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of an icositruncated dodecadodecahedron are all the even permutations of

$$\begin{array}{crrlc} \Bigl(& \pm\bigl[2-\frac{1}{\varphi}\bigr],& \pm\,1,& \pm\bigl[2+\varphi\bigr] &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,1,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& \pm\bigl[3\varphi-1\bigr] &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,2,& \pm\,\frac{2}{\varphi},& \pm\,2\varphi &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,3,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& \pm\,\varphi^2 &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\varphi^2,& \pm\,1,& \pm\bigl[3\varphi-2\bigr] &\Bigr), \end{array}$$

where $\varphi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

### Tridyakis icosahedron {#Tridyakis_icosahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table The **tridyakis icosahedron** is the [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) of the icositruncated dodecadodecahedron. It has 44 vertices, 180 edges, and 120 scalene triangular faces.
