# Small stellated truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small stellated truncated dodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small stellated truncated dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small stellated truncated dodecahedron

In [three-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-dimensional) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small stellated truncated dodecahedron** (or **quasitruncated small stellated dodecahedron** or **small stellatruncated dodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>58</sub>. It has 24 faces (12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 90 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t{$\tfrac{5}{3}$,5}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) .

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with three other [uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2537223): the convex [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486), the [small dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157181) and the [small rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157204).

It also has the same vertex arrangement as the [uniform compounds](wikipedia://en/8606809) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848541) or [12 pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/14848692).
