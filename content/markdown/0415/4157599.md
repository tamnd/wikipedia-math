# Small dodecahemicosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small dodecahemicosahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecahemicosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecahemicosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small dodecahemicosahedron** (or **great dodecahemiicosahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>62</sub>. It has 22 faces (12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 10 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 60 edges, and 30 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

It is a [hemipolyhedron](wikipedia://en/24084383) with ten [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces passing through the model center.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). It also shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157379) (having the pentagrammic faces in common), and with the [great dodecahemicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157609) (having the hexagonal faces in common).
