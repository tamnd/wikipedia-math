# Great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron** (or **great dodekified icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>42</sub>. It has 44 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its vertex figure is an [isosceles trapezoid](wikipedia://en/511691).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157192) (having the triangular and pentagonal faces in common) and the [great dodecicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157654) (having the decagrammic faces in common).
