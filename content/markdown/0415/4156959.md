# Great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron** (or **great ditrigonary icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>47</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014)), 60 edges, and 20 vertices.[^o2] It has 4 [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325) equivalent constructions, for example [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548) **3 | 3 $\tfrac{5}{4}$** gives [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) = . It has extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) a{$\tfrac{5}{2}$,3} or c{3,$\tfrac{5}{2}$}, as an *altered great stellated dodecahedron* or *converted great icosahedron*.

:::

Its [circumradius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumradius) is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ times the length of its edge,[^o3] a value it shares with the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285).

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157350) (having the triangular faces in common), the [ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157586) (having the pentagonal faces in common), and the regular [compound of five cubes](wikipedia://en/8010502).
