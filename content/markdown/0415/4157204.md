# Small rhombidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small rhombidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small rhombidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small rhombidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small rhombidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>39</sub>. It has 42 faces (30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [small stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157444) and the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848541) or [12 pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/14848692). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) (having the square faces in common), and with the [small dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157181) (having the decagonal faces in common).

### Small rhombidodecacron {#Small_rhombidodecacron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small rhombidodecacron.stl.* 3D model of a small rhombidodecacron

The **small rhombidodecacron** (or **small dipteral ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the small rhombidodecahedron. It is visually identical to the [Small dodecacronic hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_dodecacronic_hexecontahedron). It has 60 intersecting [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627) faces.
