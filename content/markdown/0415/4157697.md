# Great retrosnub icosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great retrosnub icosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great retrosnub icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great retrosnub icosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great retrosnub icosidodecahedron** or **great inverted retrosnub icosidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as $U_{74}$. It has 92 faces (80 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 150 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\operatorname{sr}\{\tfrac{3}{2},\tfrac{5}{3}\}.$

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $\xi\approx -1.8934600671194555$ be the smallest (most negative) zero of the polynomial $x^3+2x^2-\phi^{-2}$, where $\phi$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Let the point $p$ be given by

$$p= \begin{pmatrix} \xi \\ \phi^{-2}-\phi^{-2}\xi \\ -\phi^{-3}+\phi^{-1}\xi+2\phi^{-1}\xi^2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

Let the matrix $M$ be given by

$$M= \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 & -\phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) \\ \phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) & -1/2 \\ 1/(2\phi) & 1/2 & \phi/2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

$M$ is the rotation around the axis $(1, 0, \phi)$ by an angle of $2\pi/5$, counterclockwise. Let the linear transformations $T_0, \ldots, T_{11}$ be the transformations which send a point $(x, y, z)$ to the [even permutations](wikipedia://en/49727) of $(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z)$ with an even number of minus signs. The transformations $T_i$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521). The transformations $T_i M^j$ $(i = 0,\ldots, 11$, $j = 0,\ldots, 4)$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). Then the 60 points $T_i M^j p$ are the vertices of a great snub icosahedron. The edge length equals $-2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi}$, the circumradius equals $-\xi\sqrt{2-\xi}$, and the midradius equals $-\xi$.

For a great snub icosidodecahedron whose edge length is 1, the circumradius is

$$R = \frac12\sqrt{\frac{2-\xi}{1-\xi}} \approx 0.5800015046400155$$

Its midradius is

$$r=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{1}{1-\xi}} \approx 0.2939417380786233$$

The four positive real roots of the [sextic](wikipedia://en/5918099) in $R^{2}$,

$$4096R^{12} - 27648R^{10} + 47104R^8 - 35776R^6 + 13872R^4 - 2696R^2 + 209 = 0$$

are the circumradii of the [snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/287137) (U<sub>29</sub>), [great snub icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157558) (U<sub>57</sub>), [great inverted snub icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157679) (U<sub>69</sub>), and great retrosnub icosidodecahedron (U<sub>74</sub>).
