# Rhombidodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombidodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Rhombidodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a rhombidodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombidodecadodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>38</sub>. It has 54 faces (30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 120 edges and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t<sub>0,2</sub>$\{\tfrac{5}{2},5\}$, and by the [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) this polyhedron can also be named a *[cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441)*.

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a uniform great rhombicosidodecahedron are all the even permutations of

(±1/φ<sup>2</sup>, 0, ±φ<sup>2</sup>)

(±1, ±1, ±$\sqrt{5}$)

(±2, ±1/φ, ±φ)

where φ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its vertex arrangement with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [10](wikipedia://en/14826806) or [20 triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/14826884). It additionally shares its edges with the [icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157622) (having the pentagonal and pentagrammic faces in common) and the [rhombicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157189) (having the square faces in common).

### Medial deltoidal hexecontahedron {#Medial_deltoidal_hexecontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Medial deltoidal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a medial deltoidal hexecontahedron

The **medial deltoidal hexecontahedron** (or **midly lanceal ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the rhombidodecadodecahedron. It has 60 intersecting [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) faces.
