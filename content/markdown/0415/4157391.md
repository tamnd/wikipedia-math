# Great icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great icosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great icosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great icosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great icosidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>54</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 60 edges, and 30 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) r{3,$\tfrac{5}{2}$}. It is the [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878) and the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898). It was discovered independently by (txt), (txt) and (txt).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The figure is a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898) or the [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878), much as the (small) [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) is related to the (small) [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) and (small) [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), and the [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) to the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) and [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635).

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the icosidodecahedron, which is its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634). Unlike the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898) and [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441), the great icosidodecahedron is not a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the icosidodecahedron, but a [faceting](wikipedia://en/8613537) of it instead.

It also shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great icosihemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157613) (having the triangle faces in common), and with the [great dodecahemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157594) (having the pentagram faces in common).

The [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) *great stellated dodecahedron* is a [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952#Convex_polyhedron) polyhedron, with 20 triangular faces from the truncated vertices, and 12 (hidden) pentagonal faces as truncations of the original pentagram faces, the latter forming a [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441) inscribed within and sharing the edges of the icosahedron.

### Great rhombic triacontahedron {#Great_rhombic_triacontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great rhombic triacontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great rhombic triacontahedron

The [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the great icosidodecahedron is the *great rhombic triacontahedron*; it is nonconvex, [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) and [isotoxal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotoxal). It has 30 intersecting [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) faces. It can also be called the great stellated triacontahedron.

The great rhombic triacontahedron can be constructed by expanding the size of the faces of a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025) by a factor of *τ*<sup>3</sup> = 1+2*τ* = 2+√5, where *τ* is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).
