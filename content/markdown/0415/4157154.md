# Small dodecicosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small dodecicosahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecicosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecicosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small dodecicosahedron** (or **small dodekicosahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>50</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its vertex arrangement with the [great stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157457). It additionally shares its edges with the [small icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157496) (having the hexagonal faces in common) and the [small ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157630) (having the decagonal faces in common).
