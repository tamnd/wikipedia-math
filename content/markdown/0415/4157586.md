# Ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **[ditrigonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditrigonal) dodecadodecahedron** (or **ditrigonary dodecadodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>41</sub>. It has 24 faces (12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 60 edges, and 20 vertices.[^o2] It has extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) b{5,$\tfrac{5}{2}$}, as a *blended great dodecahedron*, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . It has 4 [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325) equivalent constructions, for example [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548) **3 | $\tfrac{5}{3}$ 5**, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram)

*Image: ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron cd.png.*

.

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157350) (having the pentagrammic faces in common), the [great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4156959) (having the pentagonal faces in common), and the regular [compound of five cubes](wikipedia://en/8010502).

Furthermore, it may be viewed as a [facetted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facetting) [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407): the [pentagrammic](wikipedia://en/197544) faces are inscribed in the dodecahedron's pentagons. Its [dual](wikipedia://en/8815), the [medial triambic icosahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medial_triambic_icosahedron), is a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084).

It is topologically equivalent to a quotient space of the [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/241291) [order-6 pentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38875431), by distorting the [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) back into regular [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014). As such, it is a [regular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/333299) of index two:[^o7]
