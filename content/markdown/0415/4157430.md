# Truncated great dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated great dodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Truncated great dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a truncated great dodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated great dodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>37</sub>. It has 24 faces (12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 90 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t{5,5/2}.

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with three other [uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2537223): the [nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157639), the [great dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157482), and the [great rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157661); and with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848503) or [12 pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/14848694).

This polyhedron is the [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441):

### Small stellapentakis dodecahedron {#Small_stellapentakis_dodecahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small stellapentakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small stellapentakis dodecahedron

The [small stellapentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/24161157) (or **small astropentakis dodecahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the truncated great dodecahedron. It has 60 intersecting triangular faces.
