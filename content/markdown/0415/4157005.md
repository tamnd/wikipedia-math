# Small rhombihexahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small rhombihexahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small rhombihexahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small rhombihexahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small rhombihexahedron** (or **small rhombicube**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>18</sub>. It has 18 faces (12 squares and 6 octagons), 48 edges, and 24 vertices. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

This polyhedron shares the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [stellated truncated hexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157363). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the convex [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) (having 12 square faces in common) and with the [small cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157031) (having the octagonal faces in common).
