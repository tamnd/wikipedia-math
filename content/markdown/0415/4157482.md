# Great dodecicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great dodecicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great dodecicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great dodecicosidodecahedron.

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great dodecicosidodecahedron** (or **great dodekicosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>61</sub>. It has 44 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 120 edges and 60 vertices.[^o2]

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [truncated great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157430) and the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848503) or [12 pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/14848694). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157639) (having the triangular and pentagrammic faces in common), and with the [great rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157661) (having the decagrammic faces in common).
