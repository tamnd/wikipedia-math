# Icosidodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Icosidodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Icosidodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of an icosidodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **icosidodecadodecahedron** (or **icosified dodecadodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>44</sub>. It has 44 faces (12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 120 edges and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its vertex arrangement with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [10](wikipedia://en/14826806) or [20 triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/14826884). It additionally shares its edges with the [rhombidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157502) (having the pentagonal and pentagrammic faces in common) and the [rhombicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157189) (having the hexagonal faces in common).
