# Rhombicosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombicosahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Rhombicosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a rhombicosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombicosahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>56</sub>. It has 50 faces (30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 120 edges and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

A **rhombicosahedron** shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [10](wikipedia://en/14826806) or [20 triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/14826884). It additionally shares its edges with the [rhombidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157502) (having the square faces in common) and the [icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157622) (having the hexagonal faces in common).

## Rhombicosacron {#Rhombicosacron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Rhombicosacron.stl.* 3D model of a rhombicosacron

The [rhombicosacron](wikipedia://en/24083203) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron) rhombicosahedron, U<sub>56</sub>. It has 50 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 60 [crossed-quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed-quadrilateral) faces.
