# Great rhombihexahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great rhombihexahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great rhombihexahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great rhombihexahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great rhombihexahedron** (or **great rhombicube**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>21</sub>. It has 18 faces (12 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 6 [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613)), 48 edges, and 24 vertices.[^o2] Its [dual](wikipedia://en/609737) is the [great rhombihexacron](wikipedia://en/24083321). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the convex [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157120) (having 12 square faces in common), and with the [great cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157372) (having the octagrammic faces in common).

It may be constructed as the [exclusive or](wikipedia://en/105979) (blend) of three [octagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/24232091). Similarly, the [small rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157005) may be constructed as the exclusive or of three [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569).

### Great rhombihexacron {#Great_rhombihexacron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great rhombihexacron.stl.* 3D model of a great rhombihexacron

The **great rhombihexacron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) great rhombihexahedron (U<sub>21</sub>). It has 24 identical [bow-tie](wikipedia://en/5817627)-shaped [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 18 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), and 48 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034).

It has 12 outer vertices which have the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), and 6 inner vertices with the vertex arrangement of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635).

As a surface geometry, it can be seen as visually similar to a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), the [disdyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/724752), with much taller rhombus-based pyramids joined to each face of a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631).
