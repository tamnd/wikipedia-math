# Pentagrammic crossed-antiprism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagrammic crossed-antiprism"}

Prism star polyhedron stat table

*Image: Crossed pentagrammic antiprism.stl.* 3D model of a (uniform) pentagrammic crossed-antiprism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagrammic crossed-antiprism** is one in an infinite set of nonconvex [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms) formed by triangle sides and two regular [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) caps, in this case two [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544).

:::

It differs from the [pentagrammic antiprism](wikipedia://en/4157315) by having triangular lateral faces which intersect (or "cross") the antiprism's axis of symmetry; in other words, for each lateral face, one of the triangle's vertices is on the opposite side of the axis of symmetry from the other two.

This polyhedron is identified with the indexed name U<sub>80</sub> as a [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223).

| *Image: Pentagrammic crossed-antiprism.png.* An alternative representation with hollow pentagrams. |
|---|

The pentagrammic crossed-antiprism may be inscribed within an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), and has ten triangular faces in common with the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898). It has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the [pentagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860091). In fact, it may be considered as a **parabidiminished great icosahedron**.
