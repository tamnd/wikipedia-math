# Great truncated cuboctahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great truncated cuboctahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great truncated cuboctahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great truncated cuboctahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great truncated cuboctahedron** (or **quasitruncated cuboctahedron** or **stellatruncated cuboctahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>20</sub>. It has 26 faces (12 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), 8 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and 6 [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613)), 72 edges, and 48 vertices.[^o2] It is represented by the [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) tr{<sup>4</sup>/<sub>3</sub>,3}, and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) . It is sometimes called the quasitruncated cuboctahedron because it is related to the [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879), , except that the octagonal faces are replaced by {<sup>8</sup>/<sub>3</sub>} octagrams.

:::

## Convex hull {#Convex_hull}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879). The truncated cuboctahedron and the great truncated cuboctahedron form [isomorphic graphs](wikipedia://en/247577) despite their different geometric structure.

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a great truncated cuboctahedron with side length 2 centered at the origin are all permutations of

$$\Bigl( \pm 1, \ \pm\left[1-\sqrt 2 \right], \ \pm\left[1-2\sqrt 2\right]\Bigr).$$
