# Nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron.stl.* 3D model of a nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the nonconvex **great rhombicuboctahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>17</sub>. It has 26 faces (8 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 18 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939)), 48 edges, and 24 vertices.[^o2] It is represented by the Schläfli symbol rr{4,$\tfrac{3}{2}$} and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) of . Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

This model shares the name with the convex *great rhombicuboctahedron*, also called the [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879).

An alternative name for this figure is **quasirhombicuboctahedron**. From that derives its Bowers acronym: **querco**.

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a *nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron* centered at the origin with edge length 1 are all the permutations of

$$\Bigl(\pm \left[\sqrt 2 - 1\right],\ \pm 1,\ \pm 1\Bigr).$$

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the convex [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157372) (having the triangular faces and 6 square faces in common), and with the [great rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157645) (having 12 square faces in common). It has the same vertex figure as the [pseudo great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/35694594), which is not a uniform polyhedron.
