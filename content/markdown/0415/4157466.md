# Truncated great icosahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Truncated great icosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a truncated great icosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated great icosahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated great icosahedron** (or **great truncated icosahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>55</sub>. It has 32 faces (12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 90 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $t\{3,\tfrac{5}{2}\}$ or $t_{0,1}\{3,\tfrac{5}{2}\}$ as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a *truncated great icosahedron* centered at the origin are all the [even permutations](wikipedia://en/49727) of

$$\begin{array}{crccc} \Bigl(& \pm\,1,& 0,& \pm\,\frac{3}{\varphi} &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,2,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^3} &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm \bigl[1+\frac{1}{\varphi^2}\bigr],& \pm\,1,& \pm\,\frac{2}{\varphi} &\Bigr) \end{array}$$

where $\varphi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Using $\tfrac{1}{\varphi^2} = 1 - \tfrac{1}{\varphi}$ one verifies that all vertices are on a sphere, centered at the origin, with the radius squared equal to $10-\tfrac{9}{\varphi}.$ The edges have length 2.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

This polyhedron is the [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898):

The [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) *great stellated dodecahedron* is a degenerate polyhedron, with 20 triangular faces from the truncated vertices, and 12 (hidden) pentagonal faces as truncations of the original pentagram faces, the latter forming a [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441) inscribed within and sharing the edges of the icosahedron.

### Great stellapentakis dodecahedron {#Great_stellapentakis_dodecahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great stellapentakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great stellapentakis dodecahedron

The **great stellapentakis dodecahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the dual of the truncated great icosahedron. It has 60 intersecting triangular faces.
