# Great dodecicosahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great dodecicosahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great dodecicosahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great dodecicosahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great dodecicosahedron** (or **great dodekicosahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>63</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#Complex_quadrilaterals).

:::

It has a composite [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548), 3 $\tfrac{5}{3}$ ($\tfrac{3}{2}$ $\tfrac{5}{2}$) |, requiring two different [Schwarz triangles](wikipedia://en/2069325) to generate it: (3 $\tfrac{5}{3}$ $\tfrac{3}{2}$) and (3 $\tfrac{5}{3}$ $\tfrac{5}{2}$). (**3 $\tfrac{5}{3}$ $\tfrac{5}{2}$** **\|** represents the *great dodecicosahedron* with an extra 12 $\{\tfrac{10}{2}\}$ pentagons, and 3 $\tfrac{5}{3}$ $\tfrac{3}{2}$ | represents it with an extra 20 $\{\tfrac{6}{2}\}$ triangles.)[^o3]

Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) 6.$\tfrac{10}{3}$.$\tfrac{6}{5}$.$\tfrac{10}{7}$ is also ambiguous, having two clockwise and two counterclockwise faces around each vertex.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157192) (having the hexagonal faces in common) and the [great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157474) (having the decagrammic faces in common).
