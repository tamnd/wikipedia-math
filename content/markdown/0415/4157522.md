# Great truncated icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great truncated icosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great truncated icosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great truncated icosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great truncated icosidodecahedron** (or **great quasitruncated icosidodecahedron** or **stellatruncated icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>68</sub>. It has 62 faces (30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733), and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 180 edges, and 120 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $t_{0,1,2}\{\tfrac{5}{3},3\},$ and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), .

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a great truncated icosidodecahedron centered at the origin are all the [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of

$$\begin{array}{ccclc} \Bigl(& \pm\,\varphi,& \pm\,\varphi,& \pm \bigl[3-\frac{1}{\varphi}\bigr] &\Bigr),\\ \Bigl(& \pm\,2\varphi,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^3} &\Bigl), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\varphi,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& \pm \bigl[1+\frac{3}{\varphi}\bigr] &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\sqrt{5},& \pm\,2,& \pm\,\frac{\sqrt{5}}{\varphi} &\Bigr), \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,3,& \pm\,\frac{2}{\varphi} &\Bigr), \end{array}$$

where $\varphi = \tfrac{1 + \sqrt 5}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

### Great disdyakis triacontahedron {#Great_disdyakis_triacontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great disdyakis triacontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great disdyakis triacontahedron

The **great disdyakis triacontahedron** (or **trisdyakis icosahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the great truncated icosidodecahedron. Its faces are triangles.

#### Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos\left(\tfrac{1}{6}+\tfrac{1}{15}\sqrt{5}\right) \approx 71.594\,636\,220\,88^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos\left(\tfrac{3}{4}+\tfrac{1}{10}\sqrt{5}\right) \approx 13.192\,999\,040\,74^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos\left(\tfrac{3}{8}-\tfrac{5}{24}\sqrt{5}\right) \approx 95.212\,364\,738\,38^{\circ}.$ The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos\left(\tfrac{-179+24\sqrt{5}}{241}\right) \approx 121.336\,250\,807\,39^{\circ}.$ Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::
