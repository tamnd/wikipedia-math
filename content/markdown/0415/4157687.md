# Snub icosidodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub icosidodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Snub icosidodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a snub icosidodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub [icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157622)** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>46</sub>. It has 104 faces (80 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 180 edges, and 60 vertices. As the name indicates, it belongs to the family of [snub polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snub_polyhedra).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $\rho=$ $1.324717957244746$ be the real zero of the polynomial $x^3-x-1$. The constant $\rho$ is known as the [plastic ratio](wikipedia://en/2148532). Denote by $\phi=$ $1.618033988749894$ the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

Let the point $p$ be given by

$$p= \begin{pmatrix} \rho \\ (\phi-\rho)/(\rho+1) \\ (\rho-\phi)/ \rho \end{pmatrix}$$

Let the matrix $M$ be given by

$$M= \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 & -\phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) \\ \phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) & -1/2 \\ 1/(2\phi) & 1/2 & \phi/2 \end{pmatrix}$$

$M$ is the rotation around the axis $(1, 0, \phi)$ by an angle of $2\pi/5$, counterclockwise. Let the linear transformations $T_0, \ldots, T_{11}$ be the transformations which send a point $(x, y, z)$ to the [even permutations](wikipedia://en/49727) of $(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z)$ with an even number of minus signs. The transformations $T_i$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521). The transformations $T_i M^j$ $(i = 0,\ldots, 11$, $j = 0,\ldots, 4)$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). Then the 60 points $T_i M^j p$ are the vertices of a snub icosidodecadodecahedron.

The edge length $\lambda =2\sqrt{(\rho^5 -\phi^2) \rho^{-5}}$, the circumradius equals $\sqrt{(\rho^5-\phi^2)(1 +\rho^{-5})},$ and the midradius $\sqrt{\rho^5-\phi^2}.$ To get a snub icosidodecadodecahedron with unit edge length, divide all above coordinates by $\lambda$.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The snub icosidodecadodecahedron was among the ten snub polyhedra discovered by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Scott_MacDonald_Coxeter) and [Miller](wikipedia://en/20706909) in the early 1930's, but published only in 1954. Its equation is in Table 4 of *Uniform polyhedra*.[^o5]

The measures listed on David McCooey's can be written in terms of $\sqrt{5}$ and plastic constant $\rho$.[^o6]

*The snub icosidodecadodecahedron of unit edge length*

::: {#o7 .table cue=table name="The snub icosidodecadodecahedron of unit edge length"}

| element | size |
|---|---|
| circumscribed radius | $R =\tfrac12 \sqrt{\rho^5 +1}$ |
| midscribed radius | $r =\tfrac12 \sqrt{\rho^5}$ |
| pentagon center radius | $r_5 =\tfrac12 \sqrt{\rho^4 -\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}} }$ |
| triangle center radius | $r_3 =\tfrac12 \sqrt{\rho^5 -\tfrac13 }$ |
| pentagram center radius | $r_{\tfrac53} =-\tfrac12 \sqrt{\rho^4 +\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}} }$ |
| triangle-triangle angle | $\alpha_{33} =\arccos\left( \tfrac13 (1 -2\rho^2) \right)$ |
| pentagram-triangle angle | $\alpha_{\tfrac53 3} =\arccos\left( \sqrt{\tfrac13 (u -v)} \right)$ |
| pentagon-triangle angle | $\alpha_{53} =\arccos\left( -\sqrt{\tfrac13 (u +v)} \right)$ |

:::

With $u =2\rho^2 -2\rho +1,$ $v =(\rho^2 -3\rho +1) \tfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}}.$

McCooey's metrics panel gives volume $4.620684...$, but no formula. However, (see Answer) is correct.

:::
