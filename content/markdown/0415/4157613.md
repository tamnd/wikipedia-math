# Great icosihemidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great icosihemidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great icosihemidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great icosihemidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great icosihemidodecahedron** (or **great icosahemidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>71</sub>. It has 26 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 6 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 60 edges, and 30 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#Complex_quadrilaterals).

:::

It is a [hemipolyhedron](wikipedia://en/24084383) with 6 [decagrammic](wikipedia://en/19546742) faces passing through the model center.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). It also shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157391) (having the triangular faces in common), and with the [great dodecahemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157594) (having the decagrammic faces in common).
