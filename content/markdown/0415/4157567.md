# Small snub icosicosidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small snub icosicosidododecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small snub icosicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small snub icosicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small snub icosicosidodecahedron** or **snub disicosidodecahedron** is a [uniform star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8464397), indexed as U<sub>32</sub>. It has 112 faces (100 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 180 edges, and 60 vertices. Its [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) core is a [truncated pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/43483314). It also called a **holosnub icosahedron**, $\text{ß}\{3,5\}.$

:::

The 40 non-snub triangular faces form 20 coplanar pairs, forming star hexagons that are not quite regular. Unlike most [snub polyhedra](wikipedia://en/3752558), it has reflection symmetries.

## Convex hull {#Convex_hull}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $\xi=-\frac32+\frac12\sqrt{1+4\phi}\approx -0.1332396008261379$ be largest (least negative) zero of the polynomial $P=x^2+3x+\phi^{-2}$, where $\phi$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Equivalently, $\xi = -2+\sqrt{\phi + \sqrt{\phi + \sqrt{\phi + \cdots}}}\, = -2+\beta$ where $\beta \approx 1.86676039$ () is a root of $\beta^2-\beta-\phi=0.$ Let the point $p$ be given by

$$p= \begin{pmatrix} \phi^{-1}\xi+\phi^{-3} \\ \xi \\ \phi^{-2}\xi+\phi^{-2} \end{pmatrix}.$$

Let the matrix $M$ be given by

$$M= \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 & -\phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) \\ \phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) & -1/2 \\ 1/(2\phi) & 1/2 & \phi/2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

$M$ is the rotation around the axis $(1, 0, \phi)$ by an angle of $2\pi/5$, counterclockwise. Let the linear transformations $T_0, \ldots, T_{11}$ be the transformations which send a point $(x, y, z)$ to the [even permutations](wikipedia://en/49727) of $(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z)$ with an [even number](wikipedia://en/143135) of minus signs. The transformations $T_i$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521). The transformations $T_i M^j$ $(i = 0,\ldots, 11$, $j = 0,\ldots, 4)$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). Then the 60 points $T_i M^j p$ are the vertices of a small snub icosicosidodecahedron. The edge length equals $-2\xi$, the circumradius equals $\sqrt{-4\xi-\phi^{-2}}$, and the midradius equals $\sqrt{-\xi}$.

For a small snub icosicosidodecahedron whose edge length is 1, the circumradius is

$$R = \frac12\sqrt{\frac{\xi-1}{\xi}} \approx 1.4581903307387025$$

Its midradius is

$$r = \frac12\sqrt{\frac{-1}{\xi}} \approx 1.369787954633799$$

The other zero of $P$ plays a similar role in the description of the [small retrosnub icosicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157694).
