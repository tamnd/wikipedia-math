# Small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small [ditrigonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditrigonal) [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023)** (or **small ditrigonary icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>30</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 60 edges, and 20 vertices.[^o2] It has extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) a{5,3}, as an *altered dodecahedron*, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) or .

:::

It is constructed from [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325) (3 3 $\tfrac{5}{2}$) with [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548) **3 | $\tfrac{5}{2}$ 3**. Its [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) alternates [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285) and [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544) faces.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4156959) (having the triangular faces in common), the [ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157586) (having the pentagrammic faces in common), and the regular [compound of five cubes](wikipedia://en/8010502). As a simple polyhedron, it is also a [hexakis truncated icosahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexakis_truncated_icosahedron) where the triangles touching the pentagons are made coplanar, making the others concave.
