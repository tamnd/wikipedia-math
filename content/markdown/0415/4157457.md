# Great stellated truncated dodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great stellated truncated dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great stellated truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great stellated truncated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great stellated truncated dodecahedron** (or **quasitruncated great stellated dodecahedron** or **great stellatruncated dodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>66</sub>. It has 32 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 12 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 90 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $t_{0,1}\{5/3,3\}.$

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a great stellated truncated dodecahedron are all the even permutations of

$$\begin{array}{crclc} \Bigl(& 0,& \pm\,\varphi,& \pm \bigl[2-\frac{1}{\varphi}\bigr] &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\varphi,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,\frac{2}{\varphi} &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,2 &\Bigr) \end{array}$$

where $\varphi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).
