# Cubitruncated cuboctahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubitruncated cuboctahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Cubitruncated cuboctahedron.stl.* 3D model of a cubitruncated cuboctahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **cubitruncated cuboctahedron** or **cuboctatruncated cuboctahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>16</sub>. It has 20 faces (8 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733), 6 [octagons](wikipedia://en/314575), and 6 [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613)), 72 edges, and 48 vertices,[^o2] and has a shäfli symbol of tr{4,<sup>3</sup>/<sub>2</sub>}

:::

## Convex hull {#Convex_hull}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a nonuniform [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879).

### Tetradyakis hexahedron {#Tetradyakis_hexahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Tetradyakis hexahedron.stl.* 3D model of a tetradyakis hexahedron

The **tetradyakis hexahedron** (or **great disdyakis dodecahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It has 48 intersecting [scalene triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalene_triangle) faces, 72 edges, and 20 vertices.

#### Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4})\approx 41.409\,622\,109\,27^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(\frac{1}{6}+\frac{7}{12}\sqrt{2})\approx 7.420\,694\,647\,42^{\circ}$ and one of $\arccos(\frac{1}{6}-\frac{7}{12}\sqrt{2})\approx 131.169\,683\,243\,31^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(-\frac{5}{7})\approx 135.584\,691\,402\,81^{\circ}$. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) cubitruncated cuboctahedron.
