# Snub dodecadodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Snub dodecadodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a snub dodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub dodecadodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub dodecadodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as $U_{40}$. It has 84 faces (60 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 150 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\operatorname{sr}\{\tfrac{5}{2},5\},$ as a [snub](wikipedia://en/9428917) [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $\xi\approx 1.2223809502469911$ be the smallest real zero of the polynomial $P=2x^4-5x^3+3x+1$. Denote by $\phi$ the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Let the point $p$ be given by

$$p= \begin{pmatrix} \phi^{-2}\xi^2-\phi^{-2}\xi+\phi^{-1}\\ -\phi^{2}\xi^2+\phi^{2}\xi+\phi\\ \xi^2+\xi \end{pmatrix}.$$

Let the matrix $M$ be given by

$$M= \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 & -\phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) \\ \phi/2 & 1/(2\phi) & -1/2 \\ 1/(2\phi) & 1/2 & \phi/2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

$M$ is the rotation around the axis $(1, 0, \phi)$ by an angle of $2\pi/5$, counterclockwise. Let the linear transformations $T_0, \ldots, T_{11}$ be the transformations which send a point $(x, y, z)$ to the [even permutations](wikipedia://en/49727) of $(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z)$ with an even number of minus signs. The transformations $T_i$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521). The transformations $T_i M^j$ $(i = 0,\ldots, 11$, $j = 0,\ldots, 4)$ constitute the group of rotational symmetries of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). Then the 60 points $T_i M^j p$ are the vertices of a snub dodecadodecahedron. The edge length equals $2(\xi+1)\sqrt{\xi^2-\xi}$, the circumradius equals $(\xi+1)\sqrt{2\xi^2-\xi}$, and the midradius equals $\xi^2+\xi$.

For a great snub icosidodecahedron whose edge length is 1, the circumradius is

$$R = \frac12\sqrt{\frac{2\xi-1}{\xi-1}} \approx 1.2744398820380232$$

Its midradius is

$$r=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{\xi}{\xi-1}} \approx 1.1722614951149297$$

The other real root of P plays a similar role in the description of the [Inverted snub dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157669)

### Medial pentagonal hexecontahedron {#Medial_pentagonal_hexecontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Medial pentagonal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a medial pentagonal hexecontahedron

The [medial pentagonal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24161196) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the snub dodecadodecahedron. It has 60 intersecting irregular pentagonal faces.
