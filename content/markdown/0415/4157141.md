# Small icosihemidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small icosihemidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small icosihemidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small icosihemidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small icosihemidodecahedron** (or **small icosahemidodecahedron**) is a [uniform star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8464397), indexed as $U_{49}$. It has 26 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 6 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 60 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 30 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) alternates two regular triangles and [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393) as a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/5817627). It is a [hemipolyhedron](wikipedia://en/24084383) with its six decagonal faces passing through the model center.

:::

It is given a [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548), $\tfrac{3}{2} 3 | 5,$ but that construction represents a double covering of this model.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) (its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634), having the triangular faces in common), and with the [small dodecahemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157127) (having the decagonal faces in common).
