# Great cubicuboctahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great cubicuboctahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great cubicuboctahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great cubicuboctahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great cubicuboctahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>14</sub>. It has 20 faces (8 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 6 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 6 [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613)), 48 edges, and 24 vertices.[^o2] Its square faces and its octagrammic faces are parallel to those of a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), while its triangular faces are parallel to those of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635): hence the name *cubicuboctahedron*. The prefix *great* serves to distinguish it from the [small cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157031), which also has faces in the aforementioned directions.[^o3]

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the convex [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529) and two other nonconvex uniform polyhedra. It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [nonconvex great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157120) (having the triangular faces and 6 square faces in common), and with the [great rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157645) (having the octagrammic faces in common).

### Great hexacronic icositetrahedron {#Great_hexacronic_icositetrahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great hexacronic icositetrahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great hexacronic icositetrahedron

The [great hexacronic icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/20826087) (or **great lanceal disdodecahedron**) is the dual of the great cubicuboctahedron.
