# Small dodecahemidodecahedron

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecahemidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecahemidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small dodecahemidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small dodecahemidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as $U_{51}$. It has 18 faces (12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and 6 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 60 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 30 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) alternates two regular [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393) as a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/5817627).

:::

It is a [hemipolyhedron](wikipedia://en/24084383) with six decagonal faces passing through the model center.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) (its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634), having the pentagonal faces in common), and with the [small icosihemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157141) (having the decagonal faces in common).
