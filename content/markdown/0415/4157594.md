# Great dodecahemidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great dodecahemidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great dodecahemidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great dodecahemidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great dodecahemidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>70</sub>. It has 18 faces (12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 6 [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742)), 60 edges, and 30 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

Aside from the regular [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843) {<sup>5</sup>/<sub>2</sub>,5} and [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878) {<sup>5</sup>/<sub>2</sub>,3}, it is the only [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron) whose faces are all non-convex regular polygons ([star polygons](wikipedia://en/313303)), namely the [star polygons](wikipedia://en/313303) [{<sup>5</sup>/<sub>2</sub>}](wikipedia://en/197544) and [{<sup>10</sup>/<sub>3</sub>}](wikipedia://en/19546742).

It is a [hemipolyhedron](wikipedia://en/24084383) with 6 [decagrammic](wikipedia://en/19546742) faces passing through the model center.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). It also shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157391) (having the pentagrammic faces in common) and the [great icosihemidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157613) (having the decagrammic faces in common).
