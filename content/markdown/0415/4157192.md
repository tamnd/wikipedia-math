# Great icosicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great icosicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great icosicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great icosicosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great icosicosidodecahedron** (or **great icosified icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>48</sub>. It has 52 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157474) (having the triangular and pentagonal faces in common) and the [great dodecicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157654) (having the hexagonal faces in common).
