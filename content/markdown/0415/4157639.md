# Nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the nonconvex **great rhombicosidodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>67</sub>. It has 62 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] It is also called the **quasirhombicosidodecahedron**. It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\operatorname{rr}\{\tfrac{5}{3},3\}.$ Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

This model shares the name with the convex *great rhombicosidodecahedron*, also known as the [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron are all the even permutations of

$$\begin{array}{ccclc} \Bigl(& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& 0,& \pm \bigl[2-\frac{1}{\varphi}\bigr] &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,1,& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^3},& \pm\,1 &\Bigr) \\ \Bigl(& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi},& \pm\,\frac{1}{\varphi^2},& \pm\,\frac{2}{\varphi} &\Bigr) \end{array}$$

where $\varphi = \tfrac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [truncated great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157430), and with the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848503) or [12 pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/14848694). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157482) (having the triangular and pentagrammic faces in common), and the [great rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157661) (having the square faces in common).

### Great deltoidal hexecontahedron {#Great_deltoidal_hexecontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great deltoidal hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great deltoidal hexecontahedron

The **great deltoidal hexecontahedron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the nonconvex great rhombicosidodecahedron. It is visually identical to the [great rhombidodecacron](wikipedia://en/24368340). It has 60 intersecting [cross quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_quadrilateral) faces, 120 edges, and 62 vertices.

It is also called a *great strombic hexecontahedron.*
