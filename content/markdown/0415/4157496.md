# Small icosicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small icosicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small icosicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small icosicosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small icosicosidodecahedron** (or **small icosified icosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>31</sub>. It has 52 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544), and 20 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2]

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its vertex arrangement with the [great stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157457). It additionally shares its edges with the [small ditrigonal dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157630) (having the triangular and pentagrammic faces in common) and the [small dodecicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157154) (having the hexagonal faces in common).
