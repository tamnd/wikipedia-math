# Small dodecicosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small dodecicosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecicosidodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small dodecicosidodecahedron** (or **small dodekicosidodecahedron**) is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>33</sub>. It has 44 faces (20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393)), 120 edges, and 60 vertices.[^o2] Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [crossed quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278#More_quadrilaterals).

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with the [small stellated truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157444) and the [uniform compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound#Uniform_compounds) of [6](wikipedia://en/14848541) or [12 pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/14848692). It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) (having the triangular and pentagonal faces in common), and with the [small rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157204) (having the decagonal faces in common).

### Dual {#Dual}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecacronic hexecontahedron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecacronic hexecontahedron

The [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) to the small dodecicosidodecahedron is the **small dodecacronic hexecontahedron** (or **small sagittal ditriacontahedron**). It is visually identical to the [small rhombidodecacron](wikipedia://en/24083182). Its faces are darts. A part of each dart lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

#### Proportions {#Proportions}

Faces have two angles of $\arccos(\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{5})\approx 25.242\,832\,961\,52^{\circ}$, one of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{8}+\frac{9}{40}\sqrt{5})\approx 67.783\,011\,547\,44^{\circ}$ and one of $360^{\circ}-\arccos(-\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{10}\sqrt{5})\approx 241.731\,322\,529\,52^{\circ}$. Its [dihedral angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_angles) equal $\arccos({\frac{-19-8\sqrt{5}}{41}})\approx 154.121\,363\,125\,78^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long and short edges is $\frac{7+\sqrt{5}}{6}\approx 1.539\,344\,662\,92$.
