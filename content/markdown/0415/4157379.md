# Dodecadodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dodecadodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Dodecadodecahedron\_(fixed geometry).stl.* 3D model of a dodecadodecahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **dodecadodecahedron** is a [nonconvex uniform polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron), indexed as U<sub>36</sub>.[^o2] It is the [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441) (and that of its dual, the [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843)). It was discovered independently by (txt), (txt) and (txt).

:::

The edges of this model form 10 central [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733), and these, projected onto a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859), become 10 [great circles](wikipedia://en/48082). These 10, along with the great circles from projections of two other polyhedra, form the [31 great circles of the spherical icosahedron](wikipedia://en/54384759) used in construction of [geodesic domes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_dome).

## Wythoff constructions {#Wythoff_constructions}

It has four [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) between four [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325) families: **2 | 5 5/2**, **2 | 5 5/3**, **2 | 5/2 5/4**, **2 | 5/3 5/4**, but represent identical results. Similarly it can be given four extended [Schläfli symbols](wikipedia://en/489394): r{5/2,5}, r{5/3,5}, r{5/2,5/4}, and r{5/3,5/4} or as [Coxeter-Dynkin diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): , , , and .

## Net {#Net}

A shape with the same exterior appearance as the dodecadodecahedron can be constructed by folding up these nets:

*Image: Dodecadodecahedron net.png.*

12 pentagrams and 20 [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) clusters are necessary. However, this construction replaces the crossing pentagonal faces of the dodecadodecahedron with non-crossing sets of rhombi, so it does not produce the same internal structure.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). It also shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [small dodecahemicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157599) (having the pentagrammic faces in common), and with the [great dodecahemicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157609) (having the pentagonal faces in common).

This polyhedron can be considered a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441). It is center of a truncation sequence between a [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843) and [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441):

The [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) small stellated dodecahedron looks like a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) on the surface, but it has 24 faces: 12 [pentagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagons) from the truncated vertices and 12 overlapping as (truncated pentagrams). The truncation of the dodecadodecahedron itself is not uniform and attempting to make it uniform results in a [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952) polyhedron (that looks like a [small rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157204) with {10/2} polygons filling up the dodecahedral set of holes), but it has a uniform quasitruncation, the [truncated dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157518).

### Medial rhombic triacontahedron {#Medial_rhombic_triacontahedron}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table The **[medial rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/20823052)** is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the dodecadodecahedron. It has 30 intersecting [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) faces.

#### Related hyperbolic tiling {#Related_hyperbolic_tiling}

It is topologically equivalent to a quotient space of the [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/241291) [order-5 square tiling](wikipedia://en/9421082), by distorting the rhombi into [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29). As such, it is topologically a [regular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/333299) of index two:[^o20]

Note that the order-5 square tiling is dual to the [order-4 pentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/9421111), and a quotient space of the order-4 pentagonal tiling is topologically equivalent to the dual of the medial rhombic triacontahedron, the dodecadodecahedron.
