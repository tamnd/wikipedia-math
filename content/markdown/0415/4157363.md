# Stellated truncated hexahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stellated truncated hexahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table

*Image: Stellated truncated hexahedron.stl.* 3D model of a stellated truncated hexahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **stellated truncated hexahedron** (or **quasitruncated hexahedron**, and **stellatruncated cube**[^o2]) is a [uniform star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8464397), indexed as U<sub>19</sub>. It has 14 faces (8 [triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangles) and 6 [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613)), 36 edges, and 24 vertices.[^o3] It is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t'{4,3} or t{4/3,3}, and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), . It is sometimes called quasitruncated hexahedron because it is related to the [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529), , except that the square faces become inverted into {8/3} octagrams.

:::

Even though the stellated truncated hexahedron is a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [truncated hexahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_hexahedron), its core is a regular octahedron.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

It shares the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) with three other [uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2537223): the convex [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213), the [small rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157005), and the [small cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157031).
