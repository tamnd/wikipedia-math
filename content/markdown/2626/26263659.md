# Peixoto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peixoto"}

**Peixoto** () is a [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Alvarenga Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvarenga_Peixoto) (1743–1793), Brazilian poet, born in Rio de Janeiro
- [António Augusto de Rocha Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/António_Augusto_de_Rocha_Peixoto) (1866–1909), naturalist, ethnologist and archaeologist
- [Bruno Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Peixoto) (born 1974), Brazilian politician
- [César Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/César_Peixoto) (born 1980), Portuguese footballer who plays for Sport Lisboa e Benfica in the Portuguese first division
- [Floriano Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floriano_Peixoto) (1839–1895), Brazilian soldier and politician, a veteran of the Paraguayan War, second President of Brazil
- [Floriano Peixoto (actor)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floriano_Peixoto_%28actor%29), known as Floriano Peixoto (born 1959), Brazilian film and television actor from Rio de Janeiro
- [Floriano Peixoto Vieira Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floriano_Peixoto_Vieira_Neto), (born 1954), Brazilian Army officer, commander of the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
- [Guilherme Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guilherme_Peixoto), known as Padre Guilherme (born 1974), Catholic priest, DJ, and EDM recording artist from Portugal
- [Júlio Afrânio Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Júlio_Afrânio_Peixoto) (1876–1947), Brazilian physician, writer, politician, historian, university president, and eugenicist
- [José Luís Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/José_Luís_Peixoto) (born 1974), Portuguese writer who has written fiction, poetry, drama and lyrics
- [Kerwin Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerwin_Peixoto) (born 1988), professional football defender from Peru
- [Leonardo Henrique Peixoto dos Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_Henrique_Peixoto_dos_Santos) (born 1977), Brazilian football centre-back
- [Mário Peixoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mário_Peixoto) (1908–1992), mainly known for his only film *Limite*, a silent experimental movie filmed in 1930
- [Maurício Peixoto](wikipedia://en/7427536) (1921–2019), Brazilian engineer who pursued a career as a mathematician
