# David Gieseker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Gieseker"}

**David Arends Gieseker** (born 23 November 1943 in [Oakland, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California))[^o2] is an American mathematician, specializing in algebraic geometry.

:::

Gieseker received his bachelor's degree in 1965 from [Reed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_College) and his master's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1967. In 1970 he received his Ph.D. under [Robin Hartshorne](wikipedia://en/5693379) with thesis *Contributions to the Theory of Positive Embeddings in Algebraic Geometry*. Gieseker became a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1975 and became professor emeritus in 2022.

The topics of his research include [geometric invariant theory](wikipedia://en/1639003) and moduli of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260).
