# Knut Erik Tranøy

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Knut Erik Tranøy"}

**Knut Erik Tranøy** (10 December 1918 – 19 March 2012) was a Norwegian philosopher.

:::

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) Tranøy, along with 700 other Norwegian students, was deported to the [Buchenwald concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchenwald_concentration_camp) in Germany. He was appointed professor at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) from 1959, and at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) from 1978. His main contributions have been in fields of ethics, particularly in medicine and science. He is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) from 1979. He was decorated as Knight, First Class of the [Royal Norwegian Order of St. Olav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Order_of_St._Olav) in 2002.[^o5][^o6]

He resided at Fossum terrasse.[^o7]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Tysklandsstudentene* (1946) (co-authored with Michael Sars)
- *On the Logic of Normative Systems* (1953, thesis)
- (1957). "Thomas Aquino som moralfilosof"
- *The Moral Import of Science. Essays on Normative Theory, Scientific Activity and Wittgenstein* (1998)
