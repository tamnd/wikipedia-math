# Sivaguru S. Sritharan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sivaguru S. Sritharan"}

**Sivaguru S. Sritharan** (also known as **S. S. Sritharan**) is an American [aerodynamicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerodynamicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

Sritharan served in civilian universities such as [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) and [University of Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wyoming) as faculty member and head of the department and also in the Department of Defense ([U. S. Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U._S._Navy) and [U. S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U._S._Air_Force)) in various capacities ranging from scientist to leadership roles, and also held visiting positions at several international institutions.[^o3]

He served as the [vice chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_chancellor) at the [Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramaiah_University_of_Applied_Sciences) in [Bengaluru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengaluru), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India).[^o3]

## Education {#Education}

Sritharan had his high schooling at [Jaffna Central College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffna_Central_College). He then joined at [University of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sri_Lanka) ([Peradeniya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peradeniya)) and obtained a BSc (Honors) degree in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering). He obtained a Master of Science degree in [aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautics) and [astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronautics) from [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and a master's degree and Ph.D. in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona).[^o5][^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Sritharan served as the first [provost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provost_%28education%29) and vice chancellor of the [Air Force Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Institute_of_Technology) at [Dayton, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio) and as the dean of the Graduate School of Engineering and Applied Sciences at the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School), [Monterey, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California).[^o3]

He was a professor and head of the Department of Mathematics at [University of Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wyoming) and head of the Science and Technology Branch at the [Naval Information Warfare Systems Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Information_Warfare_Systems_Command) in [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego).[^o3]

## Public engagements {#Public_engagements}

*Image: Sivaguru S. Sritharan at Port City Colombo.jpg.* Sritharan, during a meeting with the leadership team of [Port City Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_City_Colombo) at the site of the future Colombo International Financial Centre (CIFC) in June 2026.

In 2025, Sritharan served as Global Validation Chair of the [Asian Digital Finance Forum & Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Digital_Finance_Forum_&_Awards), held at [Port City Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_City_Colombo) as part of the Asia International Digital Economy & AI in Finance Summit. In this capacity, he oversaw the independent validation process for recognising contributions to [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)–driven [financial innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_innovation) across Asia.[^o8]

He also delivered a keynote address at the forum examining the evolution of algorithmic finance and its convergence with advanced mathematics and artificial intelligence.[^o9]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Sritharan is known for his research contributions in rigorous mathematical theory, [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) and [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) of [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988) and [magneto-hydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310).[^o11][^o12]

His notable contributions include:

\1. Developing [dynamic programming](wikipedia://en/125297) method for the [equations of fluid dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equations_of_fluid_dynamics). This subject is closely related to reinforcement learning in the language of machine learning.[^o13]

\2. First complete proof of the [Pontryagin’s Maximum Principle](wikipedia://en/831689) for [fluid dynamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_dynamic) equations with state constraints, as a joint work with [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) mathematician [Hector. O. Fattorini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector._O._Fattorini).[^o14]

\3. Developing robust [(H-infinity) control theory](wikipedia://en/314366) for fluid dynamics as a joint work with Romanian mathematician [Viorel P. Barbu](wikipedia://en/53177930).[^o15]

\4. First successful rigorous theory establishing a direct [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) analogy to the famous [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500) and G. Prodi (1959) on existence and uniqueness theorem for the two dimensional [Navier-Stokes equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equation) as a joint work with [J. L. Menaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._L._Menaldi) utilizing a subtle local [monotonicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monotonicity) property.[^o16]

\5. Proving Large Deviation Principle for stochastic Navier-Stokes equation as a joint work with [P. Sundar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padmanabhan_Sundar) to estimate the probability of rare events.[^o17]

\6. Systematic development of differential geometric methods for aerodynamics of supersonic delta wings.[^o18][^o19]

\7. Advancing geometric theory of hydrodynamic transition to turbulence.[^o20]

\8. Developing nonlinear filtering theory for stochastic Navier-Stokes equations for the estimation of turbulence based on partial measurements and similar theories for models describing quantum dynamics.[^o21][^o22]
