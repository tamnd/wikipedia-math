# Roxanne Johnson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Roxanne Johnson"}

**Roxanne L. Johnson** is an American [chemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemist) specialized in developing and applying [quantitative methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_Methods) to determine nutrients, organic and [inorganic carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inorganic_carbon_compound) and [total suspended solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_suspended_solids) in [estuarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estuarine) seawater systems. She works for the [United States Environmental Protection Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Environmental_Protection_Agency).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Johnson completed a B.A. in chemical science at [Connecticut College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_College) in 1965. In 1997, she earned a M.S. in statistics at the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island) (URI).[^o4]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

From 1978 to 1982, Johnson was a research assistant in the URI biochemistry department. She was a marine research specialist at URI working for the [United States Environmental Protection Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Environmental_Protection_Agency) (EPA) from 1982 to 1988. She worked as an associate chemist for the [Science Applications International Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Applications_International_Corporation) on contract with the EPA from 1988 to 1995. In 1995, Johnson joined the Atlantic Ecology Division of the EPA's National Health and Environmental Effects Research Laboratory.[^o4][^o6]

Johnson develops and applies [quantitative methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_Methods) to determine nutrients, organic and [inorganic carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inorganic_carbon_compound) and [total suspended solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_suspended_solids) in [estuarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estuarine) seawater systems. She has developed and applied [experimental designs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_designs) and [statistical analyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_analyses), using Primer and [SAS](wikipedia://en/990677), to the study of [benthic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benthic_zone) communities as indicators of estuarine health in several [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England) estuarine systems.[^o4]
