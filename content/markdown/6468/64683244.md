# Dorothée Normand-Cyrot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dorothée Normand-Cyrot"}

**Dorothée Normand-Cyrot** is a French [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [control theorist](wikipedia://en/7039), known for her work on discrete-time nonlinear control systems.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

As a teenager entering the French university system in 1971, Normand-Cyrot found the [grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande_école) closed off to her because she was female; instead she went to a lesser university to study mathematics. Her mentors included algebraist [Andrée Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/56710713) and, a few years later, control theorist Michel Fliess.

Normand-Cyrot worked for two years for [Électricité de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Électricité_de_France), earned a [doctorat de troisième cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_de_troisième_cycle) in mathematics in 1978 at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University), became a researcher for the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS) in 1981, and completed her [doctorat d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'état) in 1983 at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University). She became a director of research for CNRS in 1991, and was posted by CNRS to the Laboratoire des signaux et systèmes at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Normand-Cyrot was named an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) in 2005, "for contributions to discrete-time and digital nonlinear control systems".
