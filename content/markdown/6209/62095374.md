# Detlef Müller (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Detlef Müller"}

*Image: Müller detlef oberwolfach 2014.jpg.* Müller at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2014

**Detlef Horst Müller** (born 13 June 1954 in [Dissen, Lower Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissen,_Lower_Saxony))[^o2] is a German mathematician, specializing in analysis.[^o3]

:::

Müller received 1981 his doctorate from [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) with thesis *Das Syntheseverhalten glatter Hyperflächen mit homogenen Krümmungsverhältnissen im $\mathbb R^n$* (The synthesis behavior of smooth hypersurfaces with homogeneous curvature ratios in $\mathbb R^n$) under the supervision of Horst Leptin (1927–2017). Müller [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1984 in Kiel. He spent the academic year 1990–1991 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He was from 1992 to 1994 a professor at the [Université Louis Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Louis_Pasteur) in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg) and is since 1994 a professor at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel).[^o5]

His research deals with [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) (especially related to [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945)) with applications to [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

In 1998 Müller was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o6] He became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the class of 2018. He is a member of the editorial boards of the *Journal of Lie Theory* and the *[Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annali_di_Matematica_Pura_ed_Applicata)*.
