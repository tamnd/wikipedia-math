# Elias Koutsoupias

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elias Koutsoupias"}

**Elias Koutsoupias** is a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece) computer scientist working in [algorithmic game theory](wikipedia://en/16334749).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Koutsoupias received his bachelor's degree in electrical engineering from the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens) and his doctorate in computer science in 1994 from the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) under the supervision of [Christos Papadimitriou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christos_Papadimitriou).[^o4] He subsequently taught at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens), and is now a professor at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o4][^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In 2012, he was one of the recipients of the [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize) for his contributions to [algorithmic game theory](wikipedia://en/16334749), specifically the introduction of the [price of anarchy](wikipedia://en/17201042) concept with Papadimitriou in the paper 'Worst-case equilibria'.[^n1][^o8][^o9] His work has also spanned [complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543), [design and analysis of algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_and_analysis_of_algorithms), [online algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_algorithms), [networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_networks), uncertainty decisions and [mathematical economics](wikipedia://en/18967255).[^o4] In 2019, he gave a lecture on [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) at [CERN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERN).[^o10]

In 2016, Koutsoupias worked with [Aggelos Kiayias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggelos_Kiayias), Maria Kyropoulou and Yiannis Tselekounis, on the paper “Blockchain Mining Games”. He contributed aspects of game theory for stake pools in the [Ouroboros](wikipedia://en/64882330) consensus protocol. This was used in the [Cardano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardano_%28blockchain_platform%29) blockchain, and Koutsoupias became a senior research fellow at IOHK, the blockchain engineering company developing Cardano.[^o11][^o12][^o13]
