# Bruce Reznick

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce Reznick"}

**Bruce Reznick** (born February 3, 1953, in New York City) is an American mathematician long on the faculty at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign). He is a prolific researcher[^o3] noted for his contributions to number theory and the combinatorial-algebraic-analytic investigations of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000).[^o4] In July 2019, to mark his 66th birthday, a day long symposium "Bruce Reznick 66 fest: A [mensch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mensch) of [Combinatorial-Algebraic](wikipedia://en/14835582) Mathematics" was held at the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern), Switzerland.[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Reznick got his B.S. in 1973 from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and his Ph.D. in 1976 from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) under [Per Enflo](wikipedia://en/7180897) for the thesis ["Banach Spaces Which Satisfy Linear Identities"](https://msp.org/pjm/1978/74-1/pjm-v74-n1-p23-s.pdf).[^o4][^o7]

He was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) (1983–1986) and is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS).[^o4] From 1983 to 1985 he was on the [Putnam Competition](wikipedia://en/378276) Preparation Committee of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA). As an undergraduate he had been a member of the first place team in the [Putnam Competition](wikipedia://en/378276) twice, also being ranked twice in the top ten as an individual [^o4]

Reznick is a frequent author on matters relating to teaching and mentoring, and the overall training of graduate students. He wrote the popular article "Chalking It Up: Advice to a New TA".[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Reznick has done a systematic analysis of the representation of real forms of even degree as sums of powers of [linear forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_forms). This work was described in his monograph *Sum of Even Powers of Real Linear Forms* (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society, 1992)[^o10]

Reznick specializes in [combinatorial methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_principle) in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), often involving polynomials, [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) and [integer sequences](wikipedia://en/51421).[^o11] He is known for his contributions to the study of sums of squares and positivity of polynomials. In joint work with M.D. Choi and [T. Y. Lam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._Y._Lam), he developed the [Gram matrix](wikipedia://en/987959) method for writing real polynomials as sums of squares; this method has important applications to other areas of mathematics including optimization.[^o12]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 2013 fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o14]
- 2008-2009 Campus Award for Excellence in Undergraduate Teaching in the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign
- Reznick has an [Erdős–Bacon number](wikipedia://en/2625993) of 3 which puts him in a tie for the lead with MIT math professor [Daniel Kleitman](wikipedia://en/5299894).[^o15][^o16]
