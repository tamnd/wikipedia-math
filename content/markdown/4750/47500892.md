# European countries by health expense per person

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="European countries by health expense per person"}

This is a list of total public and private health expenditure for European countries, divided by the population of the country to give expenditure per capita. It includes health services, family planning, nutrition activities, and emergency health aid.[^o2]

:::

## Map {#Map}

The table uses 2013 data from the [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank).[^o2] Numbers are in international dollars.
