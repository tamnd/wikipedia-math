# Ohsawa–Takegoshi L2 extension theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ohsawa–Takegoshi L2 extension theorem"}

In [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), the **Ohsawa–Takegoshi *L*<sup>2</sup> extension theorem** is a fundamental result concerning the [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/14110#Several_variables) extension of an [$L^2$](wikipedia://en/28680558)-holomorphic function defined on a bounded [Stein manifold](wikipedia://en/1053909) (such as a [pseudoconvex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoconvex) [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_%28mathematics%29) set in $\mathbb{C}^n$ of dimension less than $n$) to a domain of higher dimension, with a bound on the growth. It was discovered by [Takeo Ohsawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeo_Ohsawa) and Kensho Takegoshi in 1987,[^o2] using what have been described as *ad hoc* methods involving twisted [Laplace–Beltrami operators](wikipedia://en/1763356), but simpler proofs have since been discovered.[^o3] One of the deepest result in complex analysis.[^o4] Many generalizations and similar results exist, and are known as theorems of Ohsawa–Takegoshi type.

:::
