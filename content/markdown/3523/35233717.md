# Frederick Bagemihl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frederick Bagemihl"}

**Frederick Bagemihl** (June 25, 1920 – April 12, 2002) was an American mathematician at [University of Wisconsin&ndash;Milwaukee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin&ndash;Milwaukee). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1953 to 1955.[^o2]

:::
