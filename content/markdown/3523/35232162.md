# György Alexits

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="György Alexits"}

**György Alexits** (5 January 1899 - 14 October 1978), born in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), was a [Hungarian mathematician](wikipedia://en/40842906), and a member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

Alexits is the author of *Konvergenzprobleme der Orthogonalreihen* (1960), translated as *Convergence Problems of Orthogonal Series* (Pergamon, 1961).
