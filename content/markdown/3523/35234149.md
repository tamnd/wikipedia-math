# Mischa Cotlar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mischa Cotlar"}

*Image: Mischa Cotlar.jpg.* Mischa Cotlar in 1964

**Mischa Cotlar** (1913, [Sarny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarny), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) – January 16, 2007, [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires), [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina))[^o2] was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who started his scientific career in [Uruguay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay) and worked most of his life on it in [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina) and [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela).

:::

His contributions to mathematics are in the fields of [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713). He introduced the [Cotlar–Stein lemma](wikipedia://en/8203600). He was the author or co-author of over 80 articles in refereed journals.[^o3]

According to [Alberto Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Calderón), Cotlar showed in 1955 "that theorems on singular integrals can be generalized and put in the framework of ergodic theory."[^o4][^o5] According to Krause, Lacey, and Wierdl, Karl E. Petersen in 1983 published an "especially direct proof"[^o6] of Cotlar's 1955 theorem.[^o7]

In January 1994 in [Caracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas), an international conference was held in his honor.[^o8]
