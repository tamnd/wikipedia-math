::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 7-cubes"}

In seven-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 7-cube** is a convex [uniform 7-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821719) with 3rd order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination)) of the regular [7-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube).

:::

There are 16 unique runcinations of the 7-cube with permutations of truncations, and cantellations. 8 are more simply constructed from the [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex).

These polytopes are among 127 [uniform 7-polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821719) with B<sub>7</sub> symmetry.
