# Guy Henniart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guy Henniart"}

*Image: Henniart Le Gall.jpg.* Henniart (right) with [Jean-François Le Gall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-François_Le_Gall) at the 2006 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid, Spain.

**Guy Henniart** (born 1953, [Santes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santes)) is a French mathematician at [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University). He is known for his contributions to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905), in particular his proof of the [local Langlands conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Langlands_conjecture) for [GL(*n*)](wikipedia://en/113564) over a *p*-adic [local field](wikipedia://en/145375)—independently from [Michael Harris](wikipedia://en/31954452) and [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931)—in 2000.[^o2]

:::

Henniart attained his doctorate from the [University of Paris V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_V) in 1978, under supervision of [Pierre Cartier](wikipedia://en/1470549) with thesis *Représentations du groupe de Weil d’un corps local*. He was a member of [Nicolas Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o3] Henniart was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 2006 at Madrid and gave a talk *On the local Langlands and Jacquet-Langlands correspondences*.
