# An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers"}

***An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers*** is a classic textbook in the field of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), by [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599) and [E. M. Wright](wikipedia://en/529941). It is on the list of 173 books essential for undergraduate math libraries.[^o3]

:::

The book grew out of a series of lectures by Hardy and Wright and was first published in 1938.

The third edition added an elementary proof of the [prime number theorem](wikipedia://en/23692), and the sixth edition added a chapter on [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225).
