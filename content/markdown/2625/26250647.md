# Fourier–Deligne transform

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fourier–Deligne transform"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Fourier–Deligne transform**, or **ℓ-adic Fourier transform**, or **geometric Fourier transform**, is an operation on objects of the [derived category](wikipedia://en/985897) of [*ℓ*-adic sheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-adic_sheaf) over the affine line. It was introduced by [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544) on November 29, 1976 in a letter to [David Kazhdan](wikipedia://en/3052833) as an analogue of the usual [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247). It was used by [Gérard Laumon](wikipedia://en/22565856) to simplify Deligne's proof of the [Weil conjectures](wikipedia://en/244705).

:::
