# Reinhardt Kiehl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reinhardt Kiehl"}

Reinhardt Kiehl

**Reinhardt Kiehl** (31 May 1935 – 26 January 2026) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Kiehl was born in [Herne, North Rhine-Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herne,_North_Rhine-Westphalia) on 31 May 1935.[^o3] From 1955, he studied mathematics, physics and astronomy at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg). He received in 1965 his Ph.D. (promotion) under [Friedrich Karl Schmidt](wikipedia://en/36084772) at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) with thesis *Äquivalenzrelationen in analytischen Räumen*.

## Career {#Career}

From 1966 to 1968, he was a research assistant and in 1968–1969 a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) at the University of Münster, where he received his habilitation in 1968. From 1969 to 1972, he was a professor ordinarius at the [Goethe-Universität Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe-Universität_Frankfurt_am_Main). From 1972, he was a professor ordinarius at the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim) and retired in 2003 as professor emeritus.

## Research {#Research}

Kiehl's research dealt with algebraic and arithmetic geometry and non-archimedean function theory. He wrote a textbook on the [Weil conjectures](wikipedia://en/244705) and [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) with [Eberhard Freitag](wikipedia://en/50890271). In 1970 Kiehl was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842#List_of_Congresses) in Nice, France with [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031),*Kohärenzsätze für stetige und differenzierbare Familien komplexer Räume*.

## Death {#Death}

Kiehl died on 26 January 2026, at the age of 90.[^o8]
