# Théodore Olivier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Théodore Olivier"}

**Théodore Olivier** (1793–1853) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Olivier studied in the *Licée Imperial* of Lyon, where he obtained in 1811 a degree in mathematics with high honours. After this, he went to the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).[^o4] Olivier looked like [Napoleon](wikipedia://en/69880), but nobody could prove that Olivier was an illegitimate son of the Emperor.[^o5]

In 1815, he was an adjunct professor in the Artillery School at [Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metz) and, in 1819, he became a full professor. In 1821, at the request of the King of [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), [Charles XIV John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_XIV_John_of_Sweden) (Jean-Baptiste Bernadotte), he went to Sweden to organize the military school of Marieberg.[^o6]

Returning to France, Oliver criticized the pedagogical system in the École Polytechnique and in 1829, jointly with [Alphonse Lavallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Lavallée), [Jean-Baptiste Dumas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste_Dumas) and [Jean Claude Eugène Péclet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Claude_Eugène_Péclet), founded the [École Centrale des Arts et Manufactures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale_de_Paris), where he was professor of geometry and mechanics for the rest of his life.[^o7] He also was, between 1830 and 1844, a professor at the École Polytechnique and, from 1838, a professor at the [École Nationale Supérieure des Arts et Métiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_et_Métiers_ParisTech).[^o8]

*Image: Hiperboloide Theodore Olivier.jpg.* Example of an Olivier's geometrical model conserved in the [Canada Science and Technology Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Science_and_Technology_Museum)

Olivier is mainly known for the construction of three-dimensional models of geometry for pedagogical purposes.[^o7] Most of them were sold to North American institutions such as [Union College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_College), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and [West Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy), where they are preserved.[^o9]

Olivier also studied the theory of [gears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gear), writing an extensive treatise on the subject and constructing models, preserved in the Musée des Arts et Offices in Paris.[^o10]

Olivier had no children, but he was the uncle of the French explorer [Aimé Olivier de Sanderval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aimé_Olivier_de_Sanderval).
