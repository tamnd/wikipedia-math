# Renate Scheidler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Renate Scheidler"}

**Renate Scheidler** (born 1960) is a German and Canadian mathematician and computer scientist specializing in [computational number theory](wikipedia://en/511466) and its applications in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). She is a professor at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary), in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics and the Department of Computer Science. She is the co-editor-in-chief of *Contributions to Discrete Mathematics* and one of the founders of the Women in Number Theory research community and the Women in Numbers conference series.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Scheidler was born in 1960. She earned a [diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) (the German equivalent of a combined bachelor's and master's degree) in mathematics at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) in 1987. She first came to Canada for doctoral study in mathematics at the [University of Manitoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manitoba), where she completed her Ph.D. in 1993. Her dissertation, *Applications of Algebraic Number Theory to Cryptography*, was supervised by [Hugh C. Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_C._Williams).

She joined the mathematics faculty at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) in 1993, adding a courtesy appointment in the university's Department of Computer & Information Sciences in 1995. She was promoted to associate professor there in 1999. In 2001 she moved to her present position, jointly appointed to the Department of Mathematics & Statistics and the Department of Computer Science at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary); she has been a full professor since 2008. In 2022 she was [Helene Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helene_Lange) Visiting Professor at the [University of Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oldenburg) in Germany.

In 2008, with [Kristin Lauter](wikipedia://en/26440781) and [Rachel Justine Pries](wikipedia://en/55720674), she became one of the founders of the Women in Numbers conference, which became the first of a series of conferences for the Women in Number Theory research community. She has been co-editor-in-chief of *Contributions to Discrete Mathematics* since 2023.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Scheidler was named as a Fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), recognizing her "for her vision and role in founding the Women in Numbers Research Network; for her continuing leadership in that research community; and for impactful work mentoring women at all career stages".

She is the 2024 recipient of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829), given "in recognition of her important and significant contributions to research, particularly in the fields of computational number theory and [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705)".
