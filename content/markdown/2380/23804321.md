# Alberto Bressan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Bressan"}

**Alberto Bressan** (born 15 June 1956) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University). His primary field of research is [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) including [hyperbolic systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_system) of [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_law_%28physics%29), [impulsive control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impulsive_control) of [Lagrangian systems](wikipedia://en/24585634), and non-cooperative [differential games](wikipedia://en/17507367).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bressan was born in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice) on 15 June 1956. After earning a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) (the Italian equivalent of a master's degree) in 1978 from the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua),[^o4] he obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics from the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder) under the supervision of [Jerrold Bebernes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerrold_Bebernes) in 1982.

After postdoctoral research at the University of Padua and the Mathematics Research Center of the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison), he became an associate professor at the University of Colorado at Boulder in 1986. Bressan received a full professorship at the [SISSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in Trieste, Italy in 1991. In 2003, he moved to [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University) to assume a full professorship there.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He won the [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) in 2008[^o7] and the Analysis of Partial Differential Equations Prize of the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2007 for his work in [PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o8] He was appointed to the Eberly Family Chair in Mathematics at Penn State in September 2007.[^o9]

In addition to the above, his honors include the *[A. Feltrinelli prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feltrinelli_Prize) for Mathematics, Mechanics and Applications* of the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_dei_Lincei) in Rome.[^o10] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]

Bressan was invited to give a [plenary talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) at Beijing in August 2002.[^o12][^o13]
