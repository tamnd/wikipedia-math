# Catalan surface

*Image: Catalan surface.gif.* A Catalan surface.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catalan surface"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **Catalan surface**, named after the Belgian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Eugène Charles Catalan](wikipedia://en/639553), is a [ruled surface](wikipedia://en/705158) all of whose generators are parallel to a fixed [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29).

:::

## Equations {#Equations}

The [vector equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_equation) of a Catalan surface is given by

***r*** = ***s***(*u*) + *v* ***L***(*u*),

where ***r*** = ***s***(*u*) is the space curve and ***L***(*u*) is the [unit vector](wikipedia://en/167053) of the ruling at *u* = *u*. All the vectors ***L***(*u*) are parallel to the same plane, called the *[directrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directrix_%28rational_normal_scroll%29) plane* of the surface. This can be characterized by the condition: the [mixed product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_triple_product) \[***L***(*u*), ***L'***(*u*), ***L"***(*u*)\] = 0.<https://books.google.com/books?id=K31Nzi_xhoQC&dq=catalan+surface&pg=PA279>

The [parametric equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parametric_equations) of the Catalan surface are <http://www.mathcurve.com/surfaces/catalan/catalan.shtml>

$$x=f(u)+vi(u),\quad y=g(u)+vj(u),\quad z=h(u)+vk(u) \,$$

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Special cases"}

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

If all the generators of a Catalan surface intersect a fixed [line](wikipedia://en/946975), then the surface is called a [conoid](wikipedia://en/6932317).

Catalan proved that the [helicoid](wikipedia://en/684207) and the [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) were the only [ruled](wikipedia://en/705158) [minimal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_surfaces).

:::
