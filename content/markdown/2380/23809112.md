# Lorraine Foster

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lorraine Foster"}

**Lorraine Lois Foster** (December 25, 1938, [Culver City, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culver_City,_California)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). In 1964 she became the first woman to receive a Ph.D. in mathematics from [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3] Her thesis advisor at Caltech was [Olga Taussky-Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Taussky-Todd). Foster's [Erdos number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdos_number) is 2.

:::

Born Lorraine Lois Turnbull, she attended [Occidental College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_College) where she majored in physics. She was admitted to Caltech after receiving a Woodrow Wilson Foundation fellowship. In 1964 she joined the faculty of [California State University, Northridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Northridge). She works in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and the theory of [mathematical symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_symmetry).
