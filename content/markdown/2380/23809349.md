# Matthias Flach (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Matthias Flach"}

**Matthias Flach** is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and former executive officer for mathematics (department chair) at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Professional overview {#Professional_overview}

Research interests includes:[^o4]

- Arithmetic [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) (see [Glossary of arithmetic and Diophantine geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_arithmetic_and_Diophantine_geometry)).
- [Special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882).
- [Conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjectures) of:
  - [Bloch](wikipedia://en/11772883)
  - [Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613)
  - [Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544)
  - [Bloch–Kato conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch–Kato_conjecture) (see also [List of conjectures](wikipedia://en/600011)).
- [Galois module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module) theory.
- [Motivic cohomology](wikipedia://en/809314).

## Education overview {#Education_overview}

- Ph.D. [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) UK 1991 Dissertation: [Selmer groups](wikipedia://en/1970862) for the [Symmetric Square](wikipedia://en/654098) of an [Elliptic Curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_Curve) – Algebraic geometry [^o7]
- Diplom, [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), 1986

## Publications {#Publications}

- [Iwasawa Theory](wikipedia://en/396150) and Motivic L-functions (2009) – Flach, Matthias
- On [Galois structure](wikipedia://en/61316) invariants associated to [Tate motives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_motive#Geometric_realizations) – Matthias Flach and D. Burns, [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London)[^o9]
- On the [Equivariant Tamagawa Number Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_Tamagawa_Number_Conjecture) for Tate Motives, Part II. (2006) – Burns, David; Flach, Matthias.
- [Euler characteristics](wikipedia://en/148420) in relative [K-groups](wikipedia://en/246748) – Matthias Flach[^o10]
- The [equivariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant) [Tamagawa number](wikipedia://en/1168608) conjecture: A survey (with an appendix by [C. Greither](wikipedia://en/68876514)) – Matthias Flach[^o11][^o12]
- A geometric example of non-[abelian](wikipedia://en/174448) [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), June 2004, Canadian [Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_Theory) Association VIII Meeting – Flach, Matthias.
- The [Tamagawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsuneo_Tamagawa) number conjecture of adjoint motives of [modular forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_forms) (2004) – Diamond, Fred; Flach, Matthias; Guo, Li.
- Adjoint motives of modular forms and the Tamagawa number conjecture (2001) – [Fred Diamond](wikipedia://en/8356786); Matthias Flach; Li Guo.
