# Butterfly graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Butterfly graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **butterfly graph** (also called the **bowtie graph** and the **hourglass graph**) is a [planar](wikipedia://en/24314), [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 5 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 6 edges.[^o4] It can be constructed by joining 2 copies of the [cycle graph](wikipedia://en/878962) $C_{3}$ with a common vertex and is therefore [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/247577) to the [friendship graph](wikipedia://en/24363442) $F_{2}$.

:::

The butterfly graph has [diameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_diameter) 2 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3, radius 1, [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 4 and is both [Eulerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eulerian_graph) and a [penny graph](wikipedia://en/53242630) (this implies that it is [unit distance](wikipedia://en/7158665) and [planar](wikipedia://en/24314)). It is also a 1-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 2-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

There are only three [non-graceful](wikipedia://en/8978968) simple graphs with five vertices. One of them is the butterfly graph. The two others are cycle graph $C_{5}$ and the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) $K_{5}$.

::: {#o7 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Bowtie-free graph"}

## Bowtie-free graphs {#Bowtie-free_graphs}

A graph is **bowtie-free** if it has no butterfly as an [induced subgraph](wikipedia://en/1655188). The [triangle-free graphs](wikipedia://en/7667996) are bowtie-free graphs, since every butterfly contains a triangle.

In a [*k*-vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) graph, an edge is said to be *k*-contractible if the [contraction of the edge](wikipedia://en/3758115) results in a *k*-connected graph. Ando, Kaneko, [Kawarabayashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken-ichi_Kawarabayashi) and Yoshimoto proved that every *k*-vertex-connected bowtie-free graph has a *k*-contractible edge.[^o8]

:::

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The full automorphism group of the butterfly graph is a group of order 8 isomorphic to the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) *D*<sub>4</sub>, the group of symmetries of a [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), including both rotations and reflections.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the butterfly graph is $-(x-1)(x+1)^2(x^2-x-4)$.

:::
