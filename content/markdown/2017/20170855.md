# Itô's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Itô's theorem"}

In [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), **Itô's theorem** generalizes the well-known result that the dimension of an [irreducible](wikipedia://en/385982) [representation](wikipedia://en/12695) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) must divide the [order](wikipedia://en/493527) of that group.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Given an irreducible representation $V$ of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) $G$ and a maximal [normal](wikipedia://en/21918) [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $A\subseteq G$, the dimension of $V$ must divide $[G:A]$.

:::
