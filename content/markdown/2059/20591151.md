# Hierarchical decision process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hierarchical decision process"}

For [group decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_decision-making), the **hierarchical decision process** (**HDP**) refines the classical [analytic hierarchy process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_hierarchy_process) (AHP) a step further in eliciting and evaluating subjective judgements. These improvements, proposed initially by Dr. Jang Ra (a student of Dr. [Thomas L. Saaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_L._Saaty) who developed and refined AHP) include the constant-sum measurement scale (1–99 scale) for comparing two elements, the logarithmic [least squares method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_squares_method) (LLSM) for computing normalized values, the sum of inverse column sums (SICS) for measuring the degree of (in)consistency, and sensitivity analysis of pairwise comparisons [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859). These subtle modifications address issues concerning normal AHP consistency and applicability in the process of constructing hierarchies: generating criteria, classifying/selecting criteria, and screening/selecting decision alternatives.[^o2]
