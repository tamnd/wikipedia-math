# Cantor tree

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cantor tree"}

In mathematical set theory, the **Cantor tree** is either the full [binary tree](wikipedia://en/4321) of height ω + 1, or a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) related to this by joining its points with intervals.

:::

It was introduced by [Robert Lee Moore](wikipedia://en/1077133) in the late 1920s as an example of a non-metrizable [Moore space](wikipedia://en/8016525) (Jones 1966).
