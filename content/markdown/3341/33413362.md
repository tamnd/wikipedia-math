# Jacob's ladder surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacob's ladder surface"}

*Image: Jacob s ladder surface.png.*

In mathematics, **Jacob's ladder** is a [surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-dimensional_manifold) with infinite [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) and two [ends](wikipedia://en/6862105). It was named after [Jacob's ladder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob's_ladder) by [Étienne Ghys](wikipedia://en/7561937),[^o2] because the surface can be constructed as the boundary of a ladder that is infinitely long in both directions.
