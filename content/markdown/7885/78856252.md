# Pedro Luis García Pérez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pedro Luis García Pérez"}

**Pedro Luis García Pérez** (23 March 1938 – 2 January 2025) was a Spanish mathematician, physicist, and academic. He was president of the [Royal Spanish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/65501555) from 1982 to 1988.

:::

## Death {#Death}

García Pérez died on 2 January 2025, at the age of 86.[^o3]
