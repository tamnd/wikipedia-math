# Frank Natterer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Natterer"}

**Frank Natterer** (20 July 1941) is a German mathematician. He was born in [Wangen im Allgäu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wangen_im_Allgäu), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). Natterer pioneered and shaped the field of mathematical methods in [imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging) including [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography) (CT), [magnetic resonance imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_resonance_imaging) (MRI) and [ultrasonic imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrasonic_imaging) and is considered a pioneer in the area of [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problems) .[^o15][^o9]

:::

## Career {#Career}

After studies at the Universities of [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) and [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) Frank Natterer in 1968 earned his PhD with a thesis "Einschließungen für die großen Eigenwerte gewöhnlicher Differentialgleichungen zweiter und vierter Ordnung"[^o22] under the supervision of [Prof. Lothar Collatz](wikipedia://en/1859481). In 1971, he made the habilitation "Verallgemeinerte Splines und singuläre Rand-Eigenwertaufgaben gewöhnlicher Differentialgleichungen". Following a visiting assistant professorship at [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington), [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana) (USA) he was full professor at the [Universität des Saarlandes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University), Saarbrücken (Germany), from 1973-1981. He was Director of the "Institut für Numerische und instrumentelle Mathematik" of the [Westfälische Wilhelms Universität](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfälische_Wilhelms-Universität), [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster), Germany, from 1981 until he retired from active teaching in 2006.[^o18]

In 2002, he received an honorary doctorate at [Universität des Saarlandes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University) in recognition of his leading role and achievements in the field of mathematical methods in imaging and [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problems).[^o15][^o13]

He has published close to 100 scientific papers and two books and is in possession of numerous patents.[^o21]

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

In 1975, Natterer proved pointwise convergence of [finite element methods](wikipedia://en/18233581).[^o14] Starting in 1977, he focused on mathematical methods in [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography). In this field, he not only developed algorithms but also worked on tomographic scanners.[^o20] His two books on this topic, "The Mathematics of Computerized Tomography" (1986, translated to Russian in 1990, new edition in 2001 in the series „Classics in Applied Mathematics"),[^o12] and "Mathematical Methods in Image Reconstruction" (2001))[^o10] are considered standard works in this field of science.[^o15][^o11] His main scientific contributions to the area of [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography) are:

- Stability analysis of the [Radon transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_transformation) in [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964)
- Consistency conditions for the exponential [Radon transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_transformation) with applications in [positron emission tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positron_emission_tomography)
- Regularization of [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956) with discretization and projection methods.
- [sampling theorems](wikipedia://en/37864) (optimal resolution with minimal number of measurements) in tomography
- Fourier reconstruction
- Fast algorithms in ultrasonic tomography. In this field, he successfully regularized the classic example of [Hadamard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard), the [Cauchy problem](wikipedia://en/1725027) for [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787)

Natterer’s scientific work has been very relevant in the development of modern methods of imaging in [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography) (CT), [magnetic resonance imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_resonance_imaging) (MRI), [Ultrasonic Imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrasonic_Imaging) and [positron emission tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positron_emission_tomography) (PET).[^o20][^o19]

## Service to the scientific community {#Service_to_the_scientific_community}

From 1995 to 1999, Natterer was the honorary editor of the journal *Inverse Problems* and since 2000 he has been a member of the "International Advisory Panel of Inverse Problems".[^o24] Since 1997, he has been on the editorial board of *The Journal of Fourier Analysis and Applications*.[^o25] He has also been involved with "IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging", "Journal of Inverse and Ill-Posed Problems", "International Journal of Imaging Systems and Technology", and the *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics*.

He was member of the Committee on the Mathematics and Physics of Emerging Dynamic Biomedical Imaging of the National Research Council of the USA.[^o26]

Natterer has organized numerous conferences on the topics of [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problems) and on the mathematical methods of [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography). In 1980, he founded the series of conferences "Mathematical Methods in Tomography“[^o15][^o30][^o29][^o27][^o28] at the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach). He was a faculty member in numerous scientific summer schools.[^o16][^o17]

## Other work {#Other_work}

Frank Natterer is a member of the German [Proust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Proust) Society[^o31] and has published an article on Proust and mathematics.[^o23]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He has been married to Renate Natterer since 1967. They have two adult sons. He is the father in law of Chinese pop star [Karen Mok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Mok).[^o32]
