# Tudor Ratiu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tudor Ratiu"}

**Tudor Stefan Rațiu** (born March 18, 1950) is a Romanian-American mathematician who has made contributions to [geometric mechanics](wikipedia://en/41499924) and [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Rațiu was born in [Timișoara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timișoara).[^o4] His father, Mircea Rațiu, an engineer, was the younger brother of [Ion Rațiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Rațiu), a well-known Romanian politician, while his mother, Rodica Bucur, was a piano professor at the Conservatory of Music in Timișoara.[^o5][^o6] Ratiu did his undergraduate studies at the [Politehnica University of Timișoara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politehnica_University_of_Timișoara), completing his [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) in 1973 and his [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.S.) in 1974.[^o4][^o7]

After moving to the United States, he completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1980; his dissertation, written under the supervision of [Jerrold E. Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerrold_E._Marsden), was titled *Euler-Poisson Equations on Lie Algebras*.

## Career {#Career}

From 1980 to 1983 Ratiu was a T. H. Hildebrandt Research Assistant Professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), after which he became an Associate Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona). In 1987 he moved to the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz), where he became a Professor of Mathematics in 1988.[^o10]

In 1998 Ratiu moved to the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne), where he was a professor until 2015.[^o7] In 2014–15, he was a Professor of Mathematics at the [Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolkovo_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) in Russia. Since 2016 he is a professor at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiao_Tong_University) in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).[^o7][^o11]

Ratiu received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1980,[^o7] and he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o12] In 2003, he was awarded the [Order of the Star of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Star_of_Romania), Commander rank.[^o13]
