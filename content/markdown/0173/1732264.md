# Francis Joseph Murray

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francis Joseph Murray"}

**Francis Joseph Murray** (February 3, 1911 – March 15, 1996) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his foundational work (with [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942)) on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), and what subsequently became known as [von Neumann algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebras). He received his BA from [Columbia College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_College_%28New_York%29) in 1932 and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1936.[^o3][^o4] He taught at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).

:::

In 1967 he was awarded the [Outstanding Civilian Service Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outstanding_Civilian_Service_Medal) by the U. S. Army.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- 1936 (with J. von Neumann), "[On rings of operators](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1968693)," *Ann. of Math*. 2(37): 116–229. The original paper on [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185).
- 1937 (with J. von Neumann), "[On rings of operators II](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1989620)," *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc*. 41: 208–248.
- 1943 (with J. von Neumann), "[On rings of operators IV](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1969107)," *Ann. of Math*. 2(44): 716–808.
- \1941. *An Introduction to Linear Transformations in Hilbert Space*. Annals of Mathematics Studies, no. 4. Princeton Univ. Press.[^o6]
- \1947. *The theory of mathematical machines*. Columbia Univ. Press.[^o7]
- \1954. (with Kenneth S. Miller). *Existence Theorems for Ordinary Differential Equations*.[^o8] ; 2nd printing Krieger 1976; [reprint](https://books.google.com/books/about/Existence_Theorems_for_Ordinary_Differen.html?id=mpi3BrPwbE0C) Dover 2013.
- \1978. *Applied Mathematics: An Intellectual Orientation*. [^o9]
