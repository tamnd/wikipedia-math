# Barrelled set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barrelled set"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a subset of a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) is called a **barrel** or a **barrelled set** if it is closed, [convex](wikipedia://en/6292), [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238), and [absorbing](wikipedia://en/1728179).

:::

Barrelled sets play an important role in the definitions of several classes of topological vector spaces, such as [barrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/1733592).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $X$ be a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS). A subset of $X$ is called a barrel if it is closed [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) and [absorbing](wikipedia://en/1728179) in $X.$ A subset of $X$ is called [bornivorous](wikipedia://en/64267682)[^n1] and a bornivore if it [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) every [bounded subset](wikipedia://en/1789812) of $X.$ Every bornivorous subset of $X$ is necessarily an absorbing subset of $X.$

Let $B_0 \subseteq X$ be a subset of a topological vector space $X.$ If $B_0$ is a [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) [absorbing subset](wikipedia://en/1728179) of $X$ and if there exists a sequence $\left(B_i\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ of balanced absorbing subsets of $X$ such that $B_{i+1} + B_{i+1} \subseteq B_i$ for all $i = 0, 1, \ldots,$ then $B_0$ is called a suprabarrel[^n2] in $X,$ where moreover, $B_0$ is said to be a(n):

- bornivorous suprabarrel if in addition every $B_i$ is a closed and [bornivorous subset](wikipedia://en/64267682) of $X$ for every $i \geq 0.$[^n2]
- ultrabarrel if in addition every $B_i$ is a [closed subset](wikipedia://en/47279) of $X$ for every $i \geq 0.$[^n2]
- bornivorous ultrabarrel if in addition every $B_i$ is a closed and bornivorous subset of $X$ for every $i \geq 0.$[^n2]

In this case, $\left(B_i\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ is called a defining sequence for $B_0.$[^n2]

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Note that every bornivorous ultrabarrel is an ultrabarrel and that every bornivorous suprabarrel is a suprabarrel.

:::

::: {#o13 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- In a [semi normed vector space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi_normed_vector_space) the closed [unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_ball) is a barrel.
- Every [locally convex topological vector space](wikipedia://en/518844) has a [neighbourhood basis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_basis) consisting of barrelled sets, although the space itself need not be a barreled space.

:::
