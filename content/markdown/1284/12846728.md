# Society for Quantitative Analysis of Behavior

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Society for Quantitative Analysis of Behavior"}

Organizations The **Society for the Quantitative Analyses of Behavior** was founded in 1978 by [Michael Lamport Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Commons) and [John Anthony Nevin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Anthony_Nevin). The first president was [Richard J. Herrnstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Herrnstein). In the beginning it was called the **Harvard Symposium on Quantitative Analysis of Behavior** (**HSQAB**).

:::

## Activities {#Activities}

This society meets once a year to discuss various topic in [quantitative analysis of behavior](wikipedia://en/13055663) including: [behavioral economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_economics), [behavioral momentum](wikipedia://en/12728577), [Connectionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectionist) systems or [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_networks), [hyperbolic discounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_discounting), [foraging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foraging), [errorless learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errorless_learning), [learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning) and the [Rescorla-Wagner model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescorla-Wagner_model), [matching law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matching_law), [Melioration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melioration_theory), [scalar expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_expectancy), [signal detection](wikipedia://en/1156527) and [stimulus control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stimulus_control), [connectionism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectionism) or [Neural Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_neural_network). Mathematical models and data are presented and discussed. The field is a branch of [mathematical psychology](wikipedia://en/2081516). Some papers resulting from the symposium are published as a special issue of the journal *[Behavioural Processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioural_Processes)*.
