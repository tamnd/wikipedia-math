# Truncated infinite-order square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated infinite-order square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated infinite-order square tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{4,∞}.

:::

## Uniform color {#Uniform_color}

In (\*∞44) symmetry this tiling has 3 colors. Bisecting the isosceles triangle domains can double the symmetry to [\*∞42 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I42_symmetry).

### Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual of the tiling represents the fundamental domains of (\*∞44) [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) symmetry. From \[(∞,4,4)\] (\*∞44) symmetry, there are 15 small index subgroup (11 unique) by mirror removal and alternation operators. Mirrors can be removed if its branch orders are all even, and cuts neighboring branch orders in half. Removing two mirrors leaves a half-order gyration point where the removed mirrors met. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors. The symmetry can be doubled to [\*∞42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I42_symmetry) by adding a bisecting mirror across the fundamental domains. The [subgroup index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgroup_index)-8 group, \[(1<sup>\+</sup>,∞,1<sup>\+</sup>,4,1<sup>\+</sup>,4)\] (∞22∞22) is the [commutator subgroup](wikipedia://en/8847) of \[(∞,4,4)\].
