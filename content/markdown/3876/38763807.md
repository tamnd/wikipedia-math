# Infinite-order square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite-order square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **infinite-order square tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {4,∞}. All vertices are *ideal*, located at "infinity", seen on the boundary of the [Poincaré hyperbolic disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_hyperbolic_disk) projection.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents the mirror lines of [\*∞∞∞∞ symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iiii_symmetry). The dual to this tiling defines the fundamental domains of (\*2<sup>∞</sup>) [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) symmetry.
