# Truncated order-4 apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-4 apeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-4 apeirogonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{∞,4}.

:::

## Uniform colorings {#Uniform_colorings}

A half symmetry coloring is tr{∞,∞}, has two types of apeirogons, shown red and yellow here. If the apeirogonal curvature is too large, it doesn't converge to a single ideal point, like the right image, red apeirogons below. [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) are shown with dotted lines for these divergent, [ultraparallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraparallel) mirrors.

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

From \[∞,∞\] symmetry, there are 15 small index subgroup by mirror removal and alternation. Mirrors can be removed if its branch orders are all even, and cuts neighboring branch orders in half. Removing two mirrors leaves a half-order gyration point where the removed mirrors met. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors. The symmetry can be doubled as [∞42 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I42_symmetry) by adding a mirror bisecting the fundamental domain. The [subgroup index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgroup_index)-8 group, \[1<sup>\+</sup>,∞,1<sup>\+</sup>,∞,1<sup>\+</sup>\] (∞∞∞∞) is the [commutator subgroup](wikipedia://en/8847) of \[∞,∞\].
