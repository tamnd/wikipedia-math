# Apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Apeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **apeirogonal tiling** is a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410), [hyperbolic plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_plane), or some other two-dimensional space by [apeirogons](wikipedia://en/5658261). Tilings of this type include:

:::

- [Order-2 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/35870958), Euclidean tiling of two half-spaces
- [Order-3 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38758064), hyperbolic tiling with 3 apeirogons around a vertex
- [Order-4 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38763903), hyperbolic tiling with 4 apeirogons around a vertex
- [Order-5 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/42309636), hyperbolic tiling with 5 apeirogons around a vertex
- [Infinite-order apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38796502), hyperbolic tiling with an infinite number of apeirogons around a vertex

The vertices of an order-$k$ apeirogonal tiling form a [Bethe lattice](wikipedia://en/1410431), a regular infinite tree.[^o2]
