# Pakistan Bureau of Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pakistan Bureau of Statistics"}

The **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS)** (پاکستان ادارہ شماریات) is a under the [Government of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Pakistan).[^o3] It is an attached department of the [Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Planning_Development_&_Special_Initiatives). It works for collecting statistics in the country.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

In 1947, the *Central Statistical Office* (CSO) was set up by the government of [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Pakistan) [Liaquat Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaquat_Ali_Khan). In 1950, CSO became an attached department of the Economic Affairs Division. In 1972, on the recommendation of [IBRD Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Bank_for_Reconstruction_and_Development), Prime Minister [Zulfikar Ali Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulfikar_Ali_Bhutto) upgraded the Central Statistical Office to a full-fledged government division. In 1981, the bureau was reorganized and its technical wing (CSO) was converted into the then *Federal Bureau of Statistics*. [Former](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance_Minister_of_Pakistan#List_of_Finance_Ministers_of_Pakistan) [Finance Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance_Minister_of_Pakistan) Dr. [Mahbub ul Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahbub_ul_Haq) further reorganized the bureau.
