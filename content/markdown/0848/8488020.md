# Bruce Kleiner

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce Kleiner"}

**Bruce Alan Kleiner** is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [differential geometry and topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_geometry_and_topology) and [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).

:::

He received his Ph.D. in 1990 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His advisor was [Wu-Yi Hsiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu-Yi_Hsiang). Kleiner is a professor of mathematics at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

Kleiner has written expository papers on the [Ricci flow](wikipedia://en/288291). Together with [John Lott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lott_%28mathematician%29) of the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), he filled in details of [Grigori Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266)'s proof of the [Geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642) (from which the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) follows) in the years 2003–2006. Theirs was the first publication acknowledging Perelman's accomplishment (in May, 2006), which was shortly followed by similar papers by [Huai-Dong Cao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai-Dong_Cao) and [Xi-Ping Zhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi-Ping_Zhu) (in June) and [John Morgan](wikipedia://en/8487966) and [Gang Tian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gang_Tian) (in July).

Kleiner found a relatively simple proof of [Gromov's theorem on groups of polynomial growth](wikipedia://en/635483). He also proved the [Cartan–Hadamard conjecture](wikipedia://en/62358280) in dimension 3.[^o5]
