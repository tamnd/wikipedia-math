# Alexander Givental

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Givental"}

**Alexander Givental** (Александр Борисович Гивенталь[^o3]) is a Russian-American mathematician who is currently Professor of Mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His main contributions have been in [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology) and [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), as well as their relation to topological string theories.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Givental graduated from the famed Moscow high school No. 2 (Лицей «Вторая школа»), one of the math teachers was [Valery Senderov](wikipedia://en/41863078), but was not able to enter a program at a top university due to [antisemitism in Soviet mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism_in_Soviet_mathematics). He completed his undergraduate and master studies at the [Gubkin Russian State University of Oil and Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gubkin_Russian_State_University_of_Oil_and_Gas), and defended his PhD under the supervision of [V. I. Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._I._Arnold) in 1987.[^o5] He emigrated to the United States in 1990.

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

He provided the first proof of the [mirror conjecture](wikipedia://en/644671#History) for [Calabi–Yau manifolds](wikipedia://en/188932) that are complete intersections in toric ambient spaces, in particular for [quintic](wikipedia://en/303368) [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101) in **P**<sup>4</sup>.[^o7] As an extracurricular activity, he translates Russian poetry into English[^o8] and publishes books, including his own translation of a textbook (Элементарная геометрия (Киселёв)) in geometry by [Andrey Kiselyov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Kiselyov) and poetry of [Marina Tsvetaeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Tsvetaeva).[^o9][^o10]

## Honours {#Honours}

Givental was a speaker at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.[^o12]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Givental is a father of two.
