# Yuri Burago

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Burago"}

**Yuri Dmitrievich Burago** (Ю́рий Дми́триевич Бура́го; born 21 June 1936)[^o4] is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He works in [differential](wikipedia://en/8625) and [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882).

:::

## Early life, family and education {#Early_life,_family_and_education}

Burago studied at [Leningrad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University), where he obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) and [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) degrees. His advisors were [Victor Zalgaller](wikipedia://en/4235763) and [Aleksandr Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov).

## Career {#Career}

Yuri Burago created what is known now as Alexandrov Geometry with his students [Grigori Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266) and Petrunin, and [M. Gromov](wikipedia://en/771562). Also brought geometric inequalities to the state of art.

Burago is the head of the Laboratory of Geometry and Topology that is part of the [St. Petersburg Department of Steklov Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Department_of_Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics_of_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7] He took part in a report for the United States Civilian Research and Development Foundation for the Independent States of the former [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^o8]

## Students {#Students}

He has advised [Grigori Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266), who solved the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798), one of the seven [Millennium Prize Problems](wikipedia://en/7655). Burago was an advisor to Perelman during the latter's [post-graduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-graduate) research at [St. Petersburg Department of Steklov Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Department_of_Steklov_Institute_of_Mathematics_of_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).
