# The Bridges Organization

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="The Bridges Organization"}

**The Bridges Organization** is a non-profit organization that was founded in [Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas), United States, in 1998 with the goal of promoting interdisciplinary work in [mathematics and art](wikipedia://en/22468661).[^o4][^o5] The Bridges Conference is an annual conference on connections between [art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o6][^o7][^o8] The conference features [papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_publishing#Scholarly_paper), educational workshops, an [art exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_exhibition), a mathematical poetry reading, and a [short movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_movie) festival.[^o9]

:::
