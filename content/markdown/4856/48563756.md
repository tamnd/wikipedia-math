# Glossary of symplectic geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glossary of symplectic geometry"}

This is a glossary of properties and concepts in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) in mathematics. The terms listed here cover the occurrences of symplectic geometry both in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) as well as in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) (over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) for definiteness). The glossary also includes notions from [Hamiltonian geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_geometry), [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry) and [geometric quantization](wikipedia://en/1146267).

:::

In addition, this glossary also includes some concepts (e.g., virtual fundamental class) in [intersection theory](wikipedia://en/3148602) that appear in symplectic geometry as they do not naturally fit into other lists such as the [glossary of algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/35457783).

## S {#S}

::: {#o39 .entry cue=term name="shifted symplectic structure"}

**shifted symplectic structure**

A generalization of [symplectic structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_structure), defined on derived Artin stacks and characterized by an integer degree; the concept of symplectic structure on smooth algebraic varieties is recovered when the degree is zero.[^o40]

:::

::: {#o43 .entry cue=term name="symplectic action"}

**symplectic action**

A [Lie group action](wikipedia://en/39904936) (or an action of an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924)) that preserves the symplectic form that is present.

:::

::: {#o44 .entry cue=term name="symplectic reduction"}

**symplectic reduction**

::: {#o45 .entry cue=term name="symplectic variety"}

**symplectic variety**

An algebraic variety with a symplectic form on the smooth locus.[^o46] The basic example is the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) of a [smooth algebraic variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_algebraic_variety).

:::

::: {#o47 .entry cue=term name="symplectomorphism"}

**symplectomorphism**

A [symplectomorphism](wikipedia://en/312304) is a diffeomorphism preserving the symplectic forms.

**[virtual fundamental class](wikipedia://en/50529391)**

A generalization of the [fundamental class](wikipedia://en/669440) concept from [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) to a wider notion of space in [higher geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_geometry), in particular to [orbifolds](wikipedia://en/293504).
