# Eléna Wexler-Kreindler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eléna Wexler-Kreindler"}

**Eléna Wexler-Kreindler** (15 October 1931 – August 1992) was a Romanian mathematician. She spent most of her professional career in France, where she specialized in modern algebra and studied the [Ore extensions](wikipedia://en/3182658), the theory of the [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) of [rings](wikipedia://en/48404), or algebraic [microlocalisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localization_of_a_ring).[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Kreindler was born on 15 October 1931 in [Brăila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brăila), Romania into a Jewish family. She obtained in 1951 a fellowship and spent the next four years in the USSR studying mathematics at the [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University), located in Sverdlovsk (nowadays [Yekaterinburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterinburg)).[^o2] In 1955, she completed a master thesis on *"Multiplicative Lattices with Additive Basis"* under the supervision of [Petr Grigor'evich Kontorovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petr_Grigor'evich_Kontorovich),[^o4] before returning to Bucharest to join the faculty of Mathematics at the [Polytechnic Institute of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politehnica_University_of_Bucharest). Next to her duties as assistant professor, she continued with her research in the field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) under the guidance of [Grigore Moisil](wikipedia://en/4000394).[^o2] She earned her Ph.D. thesis in mathematics on the "*Theory of Pseudolinear Operators"*. In 1969 she was promoted to associate professor. Kreindler married fellow mathematician Dinu Wexler and changed her name to Eléna Wexler-Kreindler. She left Romania with her husband in 1972 to move to France.

She had to start a new professional career in Paris, first as untenured and later tenured associate professor at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University). She was eventually promoted to associate professor in 1989. Her work in France was dedicated to the study of problems in modern algebra, such as the [Ore extensions](wikipedia://en/3182658),[^o5] the theory of the [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) of [rings](wikipedia://en/48404),[^o6] or algebraic [microlocalisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localization_of_a_ring).[^o2][^o7] She published with [Marie José Bertin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_José_Bertin) a collection of solved problems of algebra[^o8] and a companion to the book of Marie Paule Maliaving *"Algèbre commutative: applications en géométrie et théorie des nombres".*[^o9]
