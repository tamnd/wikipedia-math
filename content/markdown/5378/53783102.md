# Karl Mikhailovich Peterson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Mikhailovich Peterson"}

**Karl Mikhailovich Peterson** (Карл Михайлович Петерсон; 25 May 1828 – 1 May 1881) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known by an earlier formulation of the [Gauss–Codazzi equations](wikipedia://en/9633335).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Peterson was born in a peasant family. He studied at the *Gymnasium* of [Riga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga) and, after, at the [Imperial University of Dorpat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Dorpat) (now [Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartu)) under [Ferdinand Minding](wikipedia://en/22182418).[^o4]

Nothing of his life is known about the ten years after his graduation. In unknown date he went to [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) where he taught in the German *Gymnasium Peter and Paul* of this city from 1865.[^o5] Peterson never had an academic position at university level, but he was one of the founders of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251) with [Nikolai Brashman](wikipedia://en/1005204) and [August Davidov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Davidov). Peterson was a notable collaborator in the journal of the Society and he is considered the founder of the Moscow school of geometry.[^o6]

Peterson gave, in his graduation dissertation (1853, but not published until later), an earliest formulation of the fundamental equations of the surface theory,[^o7] now usually known as [Gauss–Codazzi equations](wikipedia://en/9633335), sometimes Peterson–Codazzi equations.

During his time in Moscow, Peterson published some important papers about [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o8] In 1879, the [university of Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Odessa) awarded him an [honorary degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree).
