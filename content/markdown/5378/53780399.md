# Maksym Radziwill

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Maksym Radziwill"}

**Maksym Radziwiłł** (born 24 February 1988)[^o3] is a Polish-Canadian mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is currently a professor of mathematics at the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1988. His family moved to Poland in 1991 where he graduated from high school and in 2006 to Canada.[^o5][^o6] Radziwill graduated from [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in Montreal in 2009, and in 2013 earned a PhD under [Kannan Soundararajan](wikipedia://en/8053624) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in California. In 2013–2014, he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) as a visiting member, and in 2014 became a Hill assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). In 2016, he became an assistant professor at McGill. In 2018, he became Professor of Mathematics at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), and in 2022 he moved to the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o7] In 2023, Radziwill joined [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) as the Wayne and Elizabeth Jones Professor of Mathematics.[^o8]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2016, along with [Kaisa Matomäki](wikipedia://en/53776011) of the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku), Radziwill was awarded the [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize).[^o10][^o11][^o12]

In February 2017, Maksym Radziwill was awarded the prestigious [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship).[^o13][^o14]

In 2018, he was awarded the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o15] In 2018 he was invited with Matomäki to present their work at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

With Matomäki, he is one of five winners of the 2019 New Horizons Prize for Early-Career Achievement in Mathematics, associated with the [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).[^o16] In the same year he was awarded the [Stefan Banach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Banach_Prize) (2018) of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791).[^o17] For 2023 he received the [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in Number Theory of the AMS.[^o18] In 2023, he was also invited to give a [Łojasiewicz Lecture](wikipedia://en/1018150#Łojasiewicz_Lestures) by the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o19]
