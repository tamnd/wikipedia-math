# Robert Santos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Santos"}

**Robert Luis Santos** (born 1957) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who was the [director of the United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_of_the_United_States_Census_Bureau) from 2022 to 2025. He served as the 116th president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2021.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Santos was born in [San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio). After graduating from [Holy Cross of San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Cross_of_San_Antonio), he earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in mathematics from [Trinity University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_University_%28Texas%29) and a Master of Arts in statistics from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Santos has worked at the University of Michigan's Survey Research Center, [NORC at the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORC_at_the_University_of_Chicago), and the Institute for Survey Research at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University). He was the 116th president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988),[^o7] 2013-2014 president of the [American Association of Public Opinion Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_Public_Opinion_Research), and a member of the [National Center for Health Statistics](wikipedia://en/1042734) board of counselors.[^o8][^o9] Santos was the vice president for statistical methods and chief methodologist at the [Urban Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_Institute)[^o10] in Washington, D.C.

Santos was confirmed as United States Census Bureau Director by the U.S. Senate on November 4, 2021,[^o11] and was sworn in on January 5, 2022.[^o12] Santos is of [Mexican American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Americans) heritage. He is the first Latino person to serve as permanent, Senate-approved director of the Census Bureau (James F. Holmes, a Black man, was acting director of the Census Bureau in January-October 1998).[^o13]

In 2006 Santos was recipient of the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o14]

In 2023 he was awarded an honorary doctorate by [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University).[^o15]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Santos identifies as a [Chicano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicano). From 2012 to 2019, he was a [SXSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest) Festival photographer and later helped manage the photo crew of about 100 photographers at this annual film and music event in Austin.[^o17][^o18]
