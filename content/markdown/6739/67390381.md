# Elizabeth Cuthill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elizabeth Cuthill"}

**Elizabeth Hahnemann Cuthill** (October 16, 1923 – January 11, 2011) was an American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) known for her work on [sparse matrix](wikipedia://en/341015) algorithms, on block iterative methods for the numerical approximation of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), and on the development of computer simulations of [nuclear reactors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reactor). She was a researcher for the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) at the [David Taylor Model Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Taylor_Model_Basin). The [Cuthill–McKee algorithm](wikipedia://en/1019406) and reverse Cuthill–McKee algorithm are heuristics for permuting matrices into forms with small [bandwidth](wikipedia://en/1451476) and for associated problems in [graph bandwidth](wikipedia://en/27414778), named for the work of Cuthill with James McKee.C

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cuthill was born on October 16, 1923, in Connecticut, the daughter of Paul Richard Hahnemann and Barbara Baumann Hahnemann.

She earned a master's degree in applied mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1946, with a master's thesis "On the velocity distribution and stability of flow through a two-dimensional channel" supervised by [Chia-Chiao Lin](wikipedia://en/10242665). After this, she completed a Ph.D. at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1951; her dissertation, *Integrals on Spaces of Functions which are Real and Continuous on Finite and Infinite Intervals*, was supervised by [Robert Horton Cameron](wikipedia://en/37645495). While completing her doctorate, she became an instructor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). In 1953, she became a researcher for the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy), working at the [David Taylor Model Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Taylor_Model_Basin), where she became Numerical Analysis Coordinator for the Computation, Mathematics, and Logistics Department.

She died on January 11, 2011, in [Frederick, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick,_Maryland).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Cuthill was named a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 1963. In 1976, she won the David W. Taylor Award of the US Navy "for her valuable contributions in the development and exploitation of mathematical and computational techniques for significant Navy applications".
