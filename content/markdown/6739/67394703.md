# Nikolai Piskunov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Piskunov"}

**Nikolai Semenovich Piskunov** (Пискунов, Николай Семенович) (9 May 1908 – 1977)[^o3][^o4] was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working mainly in the field of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). He is known for the [Kolmogorov–Petrovsky–Piskunov equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov–Petrovsky–Piskunov_equation),[^o3] a key model in mathematical population dynamics, and his textbook on differential and [integral calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_calculus) which was used at many technical universities and was translated into several languages.[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in Froltsovo ([Ivanovo Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanovo_Oblast)) and graduated from Yaroslavl State Pedagogical University in 1929. He received his [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) in 1939. From 1941 he worked at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) of the [Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union). He is a Honored Scientist of the RSFSR (1965).[^o4]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The Faculty of Engineering of the [National University of Asunción](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Asunción) (FIUNA) awards the Piskunov Prize[^o7] since 2019 to the best two students at the faculty.
