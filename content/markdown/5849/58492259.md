# Arthur Bartels

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Bartels"}

**Arthur Bartels** (born 12 October 1971) is a German mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bartels was born in [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tübingen). After completing his *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* in Wiesbaden and then *Zivildienst* (alternative civilian service instead of military service), he studied mathematics from 1992 at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) and the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) with *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)* in Mainz in 1997 under [Matthias Kreck](wikipedia://en/33582824) with *Diplom* thesis *Morsetheorie und Faserbündel über den Kreis* (Morse theory and fiber bundles over the circle). Bartels received his PhD in 1999 under the direction of [Peter Teichner](wikipedia://en/63453744) at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) with doctoral thesis *Link homotopy in codimension 2*. As a postdoc Bartels was at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster), where he habilitated in 2005 and was an assistant. He became in 2007 a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) and in 2008 a full professor at the University of Münster.

He is concerned with topology, including the [Farrell–Jones conjecture](wikipedia://en/20643804) about the algebraic structure of the [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) and [L-theory](wikipedia://en/696568) of [group rings](wikipedia://en/349223), which he proved in special cases with colleagues; specifically, he proved the case of [mapping class groups](wikipedia://en/793295) with [Mladen Bestvina](wikipedia://en/26130054) and the cases of [hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/3172817) and [CAT(0)](wikipedia://en/12941912)-groups with [Wolfgang Lück](wikipedia://en/26197113) and Holger Reich.

In 2018 in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) Bartels was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) with talk *K-theory and actions on Euclidean retracts*.[^o4]
