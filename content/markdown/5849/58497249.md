# Ernst Fiedler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Fiedler"}

**Ernst Fiedler** (22 July 1861 – 16 July 1954) was a German-born Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Fiedler was the son of [Wilhelm Fiedler](wikipedia://en/42762029), mathematics professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 1867.[^n1] From 1879 to 1882 he studied mathematics at ETH Zurich; in 1882 he moved to Berlin where he studied under [Weierstrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass), [Frobenius](wikipedia://en/341048) and other outstanding mathematicians. In 1885 he moved to Leipzig, where he was awarded a doctorate under [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) in 1885.[^n2]

Returned to Zürich, he was *privatdozent* at ETH Zurich. In 1889 he was named full professor at the *Industrieschule* (in 1904 renamed as *Oberrealschule*, and now *[Kantonsschule Rämibühl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kantonsschule_Rämibühl)*). He was the principal of the school from 1904 to 1926 when he retired.[^n2]

Fiedler only produced some secondary school textbooks and no research papers. But he has left some lecture notes on courses given by his father and other professors like Weierstrass.[^n3]

He also joined the Swiss Army and became the youngest colonel in the army. From 1889 he lectured on ballistics at the Polytechnicum. He was also member of a number of committees to improve the secondary schools.[^n4]
