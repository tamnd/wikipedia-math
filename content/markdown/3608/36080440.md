# Da Ruan

Ruan Da

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Da Ruan"}

**Da Ruan** (; September 10, 1960 – July 31, 2011) was a Chinese-Belgian mathematician, scientist, professor. He had a Ph.D. from [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University).[^o3]

:::
