# Georges Fournier (Jesuit)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Fournier"}

*Image: Couronne IMG 7007.JPG.* The [*Couronne*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_ship_Couronne_%281636%29). [Frontispiece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_frontispiece) of *Hydrographie* by Georges Fournier, 1643.

**Georges Fournier** (31 August 1595 – 13 April 1652) was a French [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus) [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_priest), [geographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fournier served as a naval [military chaplain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_chaplain) on a ship of the line,[^o3] and acquired a strong knowledge of technical and naval matters.

In 1642, he published the treaty *Hydrographie*, where he attempted to provide a scientific foundation to the design of ships.[^o4][^o5] At the time, results like [*Couronne*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_ship_Couronne_%281636%29) or [*Sovereign of the Seas*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_ship_Sovereign_of_the_Seas) were obtained by empirical trials and errors.

He also authored a *Treaty of fortifications or military architecture, drawn from the most estimated places of our times, for fortifications*,[^o6] whose original edition was published in Paris in 1649 by Jean Hénault at the Salle Dauphine of l'ange gardien. Another edition was published in 1668 in Mayence by Louis Bourgeat.[^o7]

Georges Fournier taught [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525).

## Works {#Works}

*Image: Georges s.j. fournier, trattato delle fortificazioni o architettura militare, parigi 1654, mont saint michel.jpg.* *Des fortifications...*, 1654

In 1642, Fournier published his *Commentaires géographiques*.[^o9] The following year he published in Paris the work which will give him the essential part of his celebrity: the *Hydrographie contenant la théorie et la pratique de toutes les parties de la navigation*.[^o10] Resolutely scientific,[^o5] it is the first French maritime [encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia).[^o11] Dedicated to [Louis XIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIII), it was reprinted many times (1667, 1679, 1973). Geographer, astronomer, hydrographer, mathematician, Fournier took an interest in everything: winds and tides, ports, fishing, maritime trade, shipbuilding, the conduct of officers and the art of command.[^o11] The *Hydrographie* was therefore a first-rate source for the history of the navy during the first half of the seventeenth century, which explains the "magnificent re-edition" of this work in 1973 (Etienne Taillemite).[^o9] Georges Fournier also published in 1643 a *Traité de la sphere*[^o11] and in 1644 a *Traité de géométrie*.[^o9] His *Traité des fortifications ou Architecture militaire* (published in Paris in 1649 [^o12]) was translated into Dutch, Spanish and German.[^o6] His works on [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417) (Paris, 1644 and 1654) were translated into English, and gave rise to three successive editions.[^o11] In 1656, posthumously appeared his *Asiae nova descriptio* (*New Description of Asia*).[^o11] The book was made in collaboration with several jesuit missionaries.[^o11]
