# Puig subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Puig subgroup"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Puig subgroup**, introduced by (txt), is a [characteristic subgroup](wikipedia://en/7198) of a [*p*-group](wikipedia://en/24664) analogous to the [Thompson subgroup](wikipedia://en/5662011).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If *H* is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G*, then *L*<sub>*G*</sub>(*H*) is the subgroup of *G* generated by the [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) subgroups normalized by *H*.

The subgroups *L<sub>n</sub>* of *G* are defined recursively by

- *L*<sub>0</sub> is the [trivial](wikipedia://en/193059) subgroup
- *L*<sub>*n*+1</sub> = *L*<sub>*G*</sub>(*L*<sub>*n*</sub>)

They have the property that

- *L*<sub>0</sub> ⊆ *L*<sub>2</sub> ⊆ *L*<sub>4</sub>... ⊆ ...*L*<sub>5</sub> ⊆ *L*<sub>3</sub> ⊆ *L*<sub>1</sub>

The **Puig subgroup** *L*(*G*) is the [intersection](wikipedia://en/23476429) of the subgroups *L*<sub>*n*</sub> for *n* [odd](wikipedia://en/143135), and the subgroup *L*<sub>\*</sub>(*G*) is the [union](wikipedia://en/47949) of the subgroups *L*<sub>*n*</sub> for *n* [even](wikipedia://en/143135).

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Puig [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) that if *G* is a ([solvable](wikipedia://en/105012)) group of odd [order](wikipedia://en/493527), *p* is a [prime](wikipedia://en/23666), and *S* is a [Sylow *p*-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) of *G*, and the *p*-core of *G* is trivial, then the [center](wikipedia://en/7125) *Z*(*L*(*S*)) of the Puig subgroup of *S* is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of *G*.

:::
