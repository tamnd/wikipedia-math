# Zahlbericht

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zahlbericht"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the ***Zahlbericht*** (number report) was a report on [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) by (txt).

:::

## History {#History}

In 1893 the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) invited Hilbert and [Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537) to write reports on the theory of numbers. They agreed that Minkowski would cover the more elementary parts of number theory while Hilbert would cover algebraic number theory. Minkowski eventually abandoned his report, while Hilbert's report was published in 1897. It was reprinted in volume 1 of his collected works, and republished in an English translation in 1998. (Corry 1996) and (Schappacher 2005) and the English introduction to (Hilbert 1998) give detailed discussions of the history and influence of Hilbert's *Zahlbericht*.

Some earlier reports on number theory include the report by [H. J. S. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._J._S._Smith) in 6 parts between 1859 and 1865, reprinted in (Smith 1965), and the report by (Brill Noether 1894). (txt) wrote an update of Hilbert's *Zahlbericht* that covered [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) (republished in 1 volume as (Hasse 1970)).

## Contents {#Contents}

Part 1 covers the theory of general [number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field), including ideals, [discriminants](wikipedia://en/55607), differents, units, and ideal classes.

Part 2 covers [Galois number fields](wikipedia://en/457064), including in particular [Hilbert's theorem 90](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_theorem_90).

Part 3 covers [quadratic number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_number_field), including the theory of genera, and [class numbers](wikipedia://en/150907) of quadratic fields.

Part 4 covers [cyclotomic fields](wikipedia://en/30872462), including the [Kronecker–Weber theorem](wikipedia://en/1082550) (theorem 131), the [Hilbert–Speiser theorem](wikipedia://en/1067670) (theorem 132), and the [Eisenstein reciprocity law](wikipedia://en/36969573) for *l*th power residues (theorem 140) .

Part 5 covers [Kummer number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kummer_extension), and ends with Kummer's proof of Fermat's last theorem for [regular primes](wikipedia://en/323689).
