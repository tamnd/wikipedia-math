# Kelly network

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kelly network"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), a discipline within the mathematical [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), a **Kelly network** is a general multiclass queueing network.[^o2] In the network each node is [quasireversible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasireversible) and the network has a [product-form stationary distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product-form_stationary_distribution), much like the single-class [Jackson network](wikipedia://en/3374374).

:::

The model is named after [Frank Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Kelly_%28mathematician%29) who first introduced the model in 1975 in his paper *Networks of Queues with Customers of Different Types*.[^o3]
