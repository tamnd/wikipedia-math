# Robert D. Russell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert D. Russell"}

**Robert D. (Bob) Russell** is [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University).

:::

Russell together with Uri Ascher and Robert Mattheij is the author of the seminal *Numerical Solution of Boundary Value Problems for Ordinary Differential Equations* which was subsequently republished as a SIAM Classic.[^o2] His latest book is *Adaptive Moving Mesh Methods* with Weizhang Huang[^o3]

In 2009 Russell was made a [SIAM Fellow](wikipedia://en/1883376) for his contributions to the [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of differential equations.[^o4]

## Notable publications {#Notable_publications}

- *Numerical Solution of Boundary Value Problems for Ordinary Differential Equations* (1988) with Uri M. Ascher and Robert M. Mattheij.
- *Adaptive Moving Mesh Methods* (2011) with Weizhang Huang.
