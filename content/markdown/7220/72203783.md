# Arnold conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arnold conjecture"}

The **Arnold conjecture**, named after mathematician [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490), is a mathematical conjecture in the field of [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), a branch of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Strong Arnold conjecture"}

## Strong Arnold conjecture {#Strong_Arnold_conjecture}

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a closed (compact without boundary) [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356). For any smooth function $H: M \to {\mathbb R}$, the symplectic form $\omega$ induces a [Hamiltonian vector field](wikipedia://en/1818270) $X_H$ on $M$ defined by the formula

$$\omega( X_H, \cdot) = dH.$$

The function $H$ is called a [Hamiltonian function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_function).

Suppose there is a smooth 1-parameter family of Hamiltonian functions $H_t \in C^\infty(M)$, $t \in [0,1]$. This family induces a 1-parameter family of Hamiltonian vector fields $X_{H_t}$ on $M$. The family of vector fields integrates to a 1-parameter family of [diffeomorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffeomorphisms) $\varphi_t: M \to M$. Each individual $\varphi_t$ is a called a **Hamiltonian diffeomorphism** of $M$.

The **strong Arnold conjecture** states that the number of fixed points of a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of $M$ is greater than or equal to the number of critical points of a smooth function on $M$.[^o5][^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Weak Arnold conjecture"}

## Weak Arnold conjecture {#Weak_Arnold_conjecture}

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a closed symplectic manifold. A Hamiltonian diffeomorphism $\varphi:M \to M$ is called **nondegenerate** if its graph intersects the diagonal of $M\times M$ transversely. For nondegenerate Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms, one variant of the Arnold conjecture says that the number of fixed points is at least equal to the minimal number of critical points of a [Morse function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function) on $M$, called the **Morse number** of $M$.

In view of the [Morse inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_inequality), the Morse number is greater than or equal to the sum of [Betti numbers](wikipedia://en/346262) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) ${\mathbb F}$, namely $\sum_{i=0}^{2n} \dim H_i (M; {\mathbb F})$. The **weak Arnold conjecture** says that

$$\# \{ \text{fixed points of } \varphi \} \geq \sum_{i=0}^{2n} \dim H_i (M; {\mathbb F})$$

for $\varphi : M \to M$ a nondegenerate Hamiltonian diffeomorphism.[^o5][^o6]

:::

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Arnold–Givental conjecture"}

## Arnold–Givental conjecture {#Arnold–Givental_conjecture}

The **Arnold–Givental conjecture**, named after Vladimir Arnold and [Alexander Givental](wikipedia://en/8488782), gives a lower bound on the number of intersection points of two [Lagrangian submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_submanifold) $L$ and $L'$ in terms of the Betti numbers of $L$, given that $L'$ intersects $L$ transversally and $L'$ is Hamiltonian isotopic to $L$.

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a compact $2n$-dimensional symplectic manifold, let $L \subset M$ be a compact Lagrangian submanifold of $M$, and let $\tau : M \to M$ be an anti-symplectic involution, that is, a diffeomorphism $\tau : M \to M$ such that $\tau^* \omega = -\omega$ and $\tau^2 = \text{id}_M$, whose fixed point set is $L$.

Let $H_t\in C^\infty(M)$, $t \in [0,1]$ be a smooth family of [Hamiltonian functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_function) on $M$. This family generates a 1-parameter family of diffeomorphisms $\varphi_t: M \to M$ by flowing along the [Hamiltonian vector field](wikipedia://en/1818270) associated to $H_t$. The Arnold–Givental conjecture states that if $\varphi_1(L)$ intersects transversely with $L$, then

$\# (\varphi_1(L) \cap L) \geq \sum_{i=0}^n \dim H_i(L; \mathbb Z / 2 \mathbb Z)$.[^o11]

### Status {#Status}

The Arnold–Givental conjecture has been proved for several special cases.

- [Alexander Givental](wikipedia://en/8488782) proved it for $(M, L) = (\mathbb{CP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n)$.[^o13]
- [Yong-Geun Oh](wikipedia://en/70007916) proved it for real forms of compact Hermitian spaces with suitable assumptions on the [Maslov indices](wikipedia://en/3231634).[^o14]
- Lazzarini proved it for negative monotone case under suitable assumptions on the minimal Maslov number.
- [Kenji Fukaya](wikipedia://en/40788879), Yong-Geun Oh, Hiroshi Ohta, and [Kaoru Ono](wikipedia://en/58024023) proved it for $(M, \omega)$ semi-positive.[^o15]
- [Urs Frauenfelder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urs_Frauenfelder) proved it in the case when $(M, \omega)$ is a certain symplectic reduction, using [gauged](wikipedia://en/64324611) [Floer theory](wikipedia://en/2672856).[^o11]
