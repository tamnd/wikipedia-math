# Cubic honeycomb

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic honeycomb"}

The **cubic honeycomb** or **cubic cellulation** is the only proper regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space) made up of [cubic](wikipedia://en/6285) cells. It has 4 cubes around every edge, and 8 cubes around each vertex. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a regular [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635). It is a [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual_tessellation) tessellation with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,4}. [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) called this honeycomb a **cubille**.[^o3]

:::

## Description {#Description}

The cubic honeycomb is a space-filling or three-dimensional [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) consisting of many [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) that attach each other to the faces; the cube is known as [cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) of a honeycomb. The [parallelepiped](wikipedia://en/23975) is the member of a [parallelohedron](wikipedia://en/25207334), generated from three line segments that are not all parallel to a common plane. The cube is the special case of a parallelepiped for having the most symmetric form, generated by three perpendicular unit-length line segments.[^o5] In three-dimensional space, the cubic honeycomb is the only proper regular space-filling tessellation.[^o6] It is [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual).[^o7]

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The *rectified cubic honeycomb* or *rectified cubic cellulation* is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 3-space. It is composed of [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) and [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280) in a ratio of 1:1, with a [square prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_prism) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **cuboctahedrille**, and its dual an [oblate octahedrille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblate_octahedrille).

The *truncated cubic honeycomb* or *truncated cubic cellulation* is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 3-space. It is composed of [truncated cubes](wikipedia://en/280529) and [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) in a ratio of 1:1, with an isosceles [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **truncated cubille**, and its dual [pyramidille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramidille).

*Image: Cubes-A4 ani.gif.* The bitruncated cubic honeycomb shown here in relation to a cubic honeycomb

The [bitruncated cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3736299) is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space) made up of [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654) (or, equivalently, [bitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncation_%28geometry%29) cubes). It has four [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654) around each vertex, in a [tetragonal disphenoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetragonal_disphenoid) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). Being composed entirely of [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), it is [cell-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-transitive). It is also [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive), with 2 hexagons and one square on each edge, and [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive). It is one of 28 [uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/2961). [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **truncated octahedrille** in his [Architectonic and catoptric tessellation](wikipedia://en/38829912) list, with its dual called an *oblate tetrahedrille*, also called a [disphenoid tetrahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedral_honeycomb). Although a regular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) can not tessellate space alone, this dual has identical [disphenoid tetrahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedron) cells with [isosceles triangle](wikipedia://en/315428) faces.

The *alternated bitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *bisnub cubic honeycomb* is non-uniform, with the highest symmetry construction reflecting an alternation of the uniform bitruncated cubic honeycomb. A lower-symmetry construction involves regular icosahedra paired with golden icosahedra (with 8 equilateral triangles paired with 12 golden triangles). There are three constructions from three related [Coxeter diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram): , , and . These have symmetry \[4,3<sup>\+</sup>,4\], \[4,(3<sup>1,1</sup>)<sup>\+</sup>\] and \[3<sup>\[4\]</sup>\]<sup>\+</sup> respectively. The first and last symmetry can be doubled as \[\[4,3<sup>\+</sup>,4\]\] and \[\[3<sup>\[4\]</sup>\]\]<sup>\+</sup>. This honeycomb is represented in the boron atoms of the [α-rhombohedral crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allotropes_of_boron#.CE.B1-rhombohedral_boron). The centers of the icosahedra are located at the fcc positions of the lattice.[^n1]

*Image: Cantellated cubic tiling.png.* Cantellated cubic tiling

The *cantellated cubic honeycomb* or *cantellated cubic cellulation* is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 3-space. It is composed of [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213), [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280), and [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) in a ratio of 1:1:3, with a [wedge](wikipedia://en/2951640) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **2-RCO-trille**, and its dual [quarter oblate octahedrille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarter_oblate_octahedrille).

*Image: Cantitruncated cubic tiling.png.* Cantitruncated cubic tiling

The *cantitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *cantitruncated cubic cellulation* is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 3-space, made up of [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879), [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), and [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) in a ratio of 1:1:3, with a [mirrored sphenoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirrored_sphenoid) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **n-tCO-trille**, and its dual [triangular pyramidille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_pyramidille). Its dual of the *cantitruncated cubic honeycomb* is called a **triangular pyramidille**, with [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram), . These honeycomb cells represent the fundamental domains of ${\tilde{B}}_3$ symmetry. A cell can be as 1/24 of a translational cube with vertices positioned: taking two corners, ne face center, and the cube center. The edge colors and labels specify how many cells exist around the edge.

The *alternated cantitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *snub rectified cubic honeycomb* contains three types of cells: [snub cubes](wikipedia://en/180249), [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084) (with *T<sub>h</sub>* symmetry), [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as tetragonal disphenoids), and new tetrahedral cells created at the gaps. Although it is not uniform, constructionally it can be given as [Coxeter diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) or . Despite being non-uniform, there is a near-miss version with two edge lengths shown below, one of which is around 4.3% greater than the other. The snub cubes in this case are uniform, but the rest of the cells are not.

The *cantic snub cubic honeycomb* is constructed by snubbing the [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654) in a way that leaves only [rectangles](wikipedia://en/70048) from the [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) (square prisms). It is not uniform but it can be represented as [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . It has [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213) (with *T<sub>h</sub>* symmetry), [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084) (with *T<sub>h</sub>* symmetry), and [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) (as *C<sub>2v</sub>*-symmetry wedges) filling the gaps.[^o10]

The *runcitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *runcitruncated cubic cellulation* is a uniform [space-filling tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessellation_of_space) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 3-space. It is composed of [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213), [truncated cubes](wikipedia://en/280529), [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569), and [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) in a ratio of 1:1:3:3, with an [isosceles-trapezoidal](wikipedia://en/511691) [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). Its name is derived from its [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram), with three ringed nodes representing 3 active mirrors in the [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) from its relation to the [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_Euclidean_3-space) cubic honeycomb. [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **1-RCO-trille**, and its dual [square quarter pyramidille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_quarter_pyramidille). Its dual is **square quarter pyramidille**, with [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . Faces exist in 3 of 4 hyperplanes of the \[4,3,4\], ${\tilde{C}}_3$ Coxeter group. Cells are irregular pyramids and can be seen as 1/24 of a cube, using one corner, one mid-edge point, two face centers, and the cube center.

*Image: Square quarter pyramidille cell.png.*

An *alternated omnitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *omnisnub cubic honeycomb* can be constructed by [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated cubic honeycomb, although it can not be made uniform, but it can be given [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram): and has symmetry \[\[4,3,4\]\]<sup>\+</sup>. It makes [snub cubes](wikipedia://en/180249) from the [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879), [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045) from the [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569), and creates new [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells from the gaps. Its dual is a space-filling honeycomb constructed as the dual of the alternated omnitruncated cubic honeycomb. The 24 cells fit around a vertex, making a chiral [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805) that can be stacked in all 3-dimensions:

*Image: Altbasetet-24-in-cube.png.*

The *runcic cantitruncated cubic honeycomb* or *runcic cantitruncated cubic cellulation* is constructed by removing alternating long rectangles from the octagons and is not uniform, but it can be represented as [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . It has [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213) (with *T<sub>h</sub>* symmetry), [snub cubes](wikipedia://en/180249), two kinds of [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285): square prisms and rectangular trapezoprisms (topologically equivalent to a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) but with *D<sub>2d</sub>* symmetry), and [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) (as *C<sub>2v</sub>*-symmetry wedges) filling the gaps.

The *biorthosnub cubic honeycomb* is constructed by removing alternating long rectangles from the octagons orthogonally and is not uniform, but it can be represented as [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) . It has [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213) (with *T<sub>h</sub>* symmetry) and two kinds of [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285): square prisms and rectangular trapezoprisms (topologically equivalent to a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) but with *D<sub>2d</sub>* symmetry).

*Image: Truncated square prismatic honeycomb.png.* Truncated square prismatic honeycomb

The *truncated square prismatic honeycomb* or *tomo-square prismatic cellulation* is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space). It is composed of [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569) and [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) in a ratio of 1:1. It is constructed from a [truncated square tiling](wikipedia://en/2864244) extruded into prisms. It is one of 28 [convex uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/2961).

*Image: Snub square prismatic honeycomb.png.* Snub square prismatic honeycomb

The *snub square prismatic honeycomb* or *simo-square prismatic cellulation* is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space). It is composed of [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) and [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) in a ratio of 1:2. It is constructed from a [snub square tiling](wikipedia://en/2864884) extruded into prisms. It is one of 28 [convex uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/2961).

A *snub square antiprismatic honeycomb* can be constructed by [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the truncated square prismatic honeycomb, although it can not be made uniform, but it can be given [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram): and has symmetry \[4,4,2,∞\]<sup>\+</sup>. It makes [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045) from the [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569), [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as tetragonal disphenoids) from the [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285), and two tetrahedra from the [triangular bipyramids](wikipedia://en/646933).
