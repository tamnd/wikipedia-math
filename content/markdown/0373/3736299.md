*Image: Cubes-A4 ani.gif.* The bitruncated cubic honeycomb shown here in relation to a cubic honeycomb

The **bitruncated cubic honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space) made up of [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654) (or, equivalently, [bitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncation_%28geometry%29) cubes). It has 4 truncated octahedra around each vertex. Being composed entirely of [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), it is [cell-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-transitive). It is also [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive), with 2 hexagons and one square on each edge, and [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive). It is one of 28 [uniform honeycombs](wikipedia://en/2961).

:::

[John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls this honeycomb a **truncated octahedrille** in his [Architectonic and catoptric tessellation](wikipedia://en/38829912) list, with its dual called an *oblate tetrahedrille*, also called a [disphenoid tetrahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedral_honeycomb). Although a regular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) can not tessellate space alone, this dual has identical [disphenoid tetrahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedron) cells with [isosceles triangle](wikipedia://en/315428) faces.

## Geometry {#Geometry}

It can be realized as the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [body-centred cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body-centred_cubic) lattice. [Lord Kelvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Kelvin) conjectured that a variant of the *bitruncated cubic honeycomb* (with curved faces and edges, but the same combinatorial structure) was the optimal soap bubble foam. However, a number of less symmetrical structures have later been found to be more efficient foams of soap bubbles, among which the [Weaire–Phelan structure](wikipedia://en/2795211) appears to be the best.

The honeycomb represents the [permutohedron](wikipedia://en/12745947) tessellation for 3-space. The coordinates of the vertices for one octahedron represent a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) of integers in 4-space, specifically [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of (1,2,3,4). The tessellation is formed by translated copies within the hyperplane.

*Image: Symmetric group 4; permutohedron 3D; l-e factorial numbers.svg.*

The tessellation is the highest tessellation of [parallelohedrons](wikipedia://en/25207334) in 3-space.

### Projections {#Projections}

The *bitruncated cubic honeycomb* can be orthogonally projected into the Euclidean plane with various symmetry arrangements. The highest (hexagonal) symmetry form projects into a nonuniform [rhombitrihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864311). A square symmetry projection forms two overlapping [truncated square tiling](wikipedia://en/2864244), which combine together as a [chamfered square tiling](wikipedia://en/43468546).

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The vertex figure for this honeycomb is a [disphenoid tetrahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disphenoid_tetrahedron), and it is also the [Goursat tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/24104450) ([fundamental domain](wikipedia://en/469365)) for the ${\tilde{A}}_3$ [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004). This honeycomb has four uniform constructions, with the truncated octahedral cells having different [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) and [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777). These uniform symmetries can be represented by coloring differently the cells in each construction.

The \[4,3,4\], , [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) generates 15 permutations of uniform tessellations, 9 with distinct geometry including the alternated cubic honeycomb. The [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) cubic honeycomb (also known as the runcinated tesseractic honeycomb) is geometrically identical to the cubic honeycomb.

The \[4,3<sup>1,1</sup>\], , [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) generates 9 permutations of uniform tessellations, 4 with distinct geometry including the alternated cubic honeycomb.

### Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

Nonuniform variants with \[4,3,4\] symmetry and two types of truncated octahedra can be doubled by placing the two types of truncated octahedra to produce a nonuniform honeycomb with [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654) and [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668) (as ditrigonal trapezoprisms). Its vertex figure is a *C<sub>2v</sub>*-symmetric [triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/646933).

This honeycomb can then be alternated to produce another nonuniform honeycomb with [pyritohedral icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084), [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (as triangular antiprisms), and [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as sphenoids). Its vertex figure has *C<sub>2v</sub>* symmetry and consists of 2 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014), 4 [rectangles](wikipedia://en/70048), 4 [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428) (divided into two sets of 2), and 4 [scalene triangles](wikipedia://en/30654).
