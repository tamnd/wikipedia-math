# Giuseppe Lauricella

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giuseppe Lauricella"}

**Giuseppe Lauricella** (15 December 1867 – 9 January 1913) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed to analysis and [theory of elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_elasticity).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Agrigento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrigento) (Sicily), Lauricella studied at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), where his professors included [Luigi Bianchi](wikipedia://en/701065), [Ulisse Dini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulisse_Dini) and [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra). He taught in secondary schools from 1895 to 1898, then became a professor at the [University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania). In 1907 he became a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei). He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1908 in Rome.[^o6]

Lauricella died in [Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catania) at age 45 from a [scarlet fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlet_fever) he contracted from one of his children.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

- [Lauricella hypergeometric series](wikipedia://en/4016820) $F_A, F_B, F_C, F_D$
- [Lauricella's theorem](wikipedia://en/18517425) (regarding orthogonal functions)
