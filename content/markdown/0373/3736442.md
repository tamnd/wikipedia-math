# Roger Jones (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roger Jones"}

**Roger L. Jones** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He obtained a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematics in 1971 from [University at Albany, SUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Albany,_SUNY),[^o4] and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mathematics in 1974 from [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), with thesis *Inequalities for the Ergodic Maximal Function* written under the direction of Richard Floyd Gundy. He has recently retired from a professorship in mathematics at [DePaul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DePaul_University) in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago). There he taught everything from remedial math to graduate-level courses. During his tenure at DePaul, Roger published numerous research papers in math, was awarded an excellence in teaching award, chaired the DePaul University Mathematics Department, and was awarded [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) grants related to teaching mathematics. He has also worked with the [Chicago Public Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Public_Schools) on improving math instruction.

Roger was honored for his research work at the *International Conference on Harmonic Analysis and Ergodic theory* that was held in the name of Roger and his colleague [Marshall Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Ash).

Roger has since retired from teaching at DePaul and moved to Northern Wisconsin, where he teaches mathematics at [Conserve School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conserve_School).

## Appointments {#Appointments}

- 1974-1977: DePaul University, Assistant Professor
- 1977-1984: DePaul University, Associate Professor
- 1982-1985: DePaul University, Chairman: Department of Mathematics
- 1984-2004: DePaul University, Professor
- 2004–present: DePaul University, Professor Emeritus
