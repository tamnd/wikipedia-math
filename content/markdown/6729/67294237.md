# Denise Kirschner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denise Kirschner"}

**Denise Ellen Kirschner** is an American [mathematical biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology) and [immunologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunology) whose research topics include [granulomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granuloma), [HIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV), [tuberculosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuberculosis), and the mechanisms by which disease pathogens interact with and persist in their hosts. She is a professor of microbiology and immunology at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), co-editor-in-chief of the *[Journal of Theoretical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Theoretical_Biology)*, and former president of the [Society for Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Biology).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kirschner graduated from [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) in 1985, and completed her Ph.D. there in 1991. Her dissertation, *Mathematical Modeling of the AIDS Virus in Epidemiology and Immunology*, was jointly supervised by [Jerome Goldstein](wikipedia://en/32366276) and [James (Mac) Hyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_%28Mac%29_Hyman).

After postdoctoral research at [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University), she was an assistant professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) from 1994 to 1997 before moving to the University of Michigan.

She was president of the Society for Mathematical Biology from 2017 to 2019. She is a speaker of the IBS Biomedical Mathematics Group.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kirschner was listed as one of the inaugural Fellows of the Society for Mathematical Biology, in the class of 2017. She was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in the 2021 class of fellows, "for contributions to modeling pathogen-host interactions and host immune response in infectious diseases and training in mathematical biology/immunology". In 2025, Kirschner was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Microbiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Microbiology).[^o5]
