# Michel Bierlaire

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Bierlaire"}

**Michel Bierlaire** (born 9 February 1967) is a Belgian-Swiss applied mathematician specialising in transportation systems. He is Professor of Operations Research at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL) and directs the university’s Transport and Mobility Laboratory.[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Bierlaire studied mathematics at the [University of Namur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Namur), obtaining his PhD in 1996 for research on transportation-demand models supervised by Philippe Toint.[^o2]

## Career {#Career}

From 1995 to 1998 he was a research associate in the Intelligent Transportation Systems Program at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), where he co-developed **DynaMIT**, a real-time traffic-prediction tool.[^o7] Bierlaire joined EPFL in 1998 as a senior scientist, became Associate Professor in 2006 and Full Professor in 2012.[^o2] He founded the Transport and Mobility Laboratory in 2006[^o4] and directed EPFL’s Doctoral Program in Civil and Environmental Engineering from 2009 to 2017.[^o2] In 2012 he co-founded the European Association for Research in Transportation (**hEART**) and served as its first president until 2015.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Bierlaire combines discrete-choice theory, operations research and simulation to analyse travel behaviour, design transport policies and evaluate environmental impacts.[^o2] His methods have been applied to public-transport planning, logistics, pedestrian dynamics and land-use modelling. He leads **Biogeme**, an open-source Python package for maximum-likelihood estimation of discrete-choice models.[^o10] Bierlaire created several massive open online courses, including “Introduction to Discrete Choice Models” on edX,[^o11] and co-teaches the professional course “Discrete Choice Analysis: Predicting Individual Behaviour and Market Demand” with colleagues from MIT and UC Berkeley.[^o12]

## Honours and service {#Honours_and_service}

- Founding Editor-in-Chief, *EURO Journal on Transportation and Logistics* (2011–2019)[^o14]
- Associate Editor, *Operations Research* (since 2012)[^o2]
