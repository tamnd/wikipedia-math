# Ana Casís

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ana Casís"}

**Ana G. Casís** was a Panamanian sociologist, statistician, and demographer who directed the 1950 Panamanian census, and later worked for the Inter-American Statistical Institute in Washington, DC. She is also known for her work with [Kingsley Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsley_Davis) on [urbanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization) in [Latin America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_America), Davis's first work on urbanization.

:::

Casís earned a bachelor's degree in Panama in 1943, and a master's degree at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) in 1945, with the master's thesis "Population studies in Mexico, Panama, and Puerto Rico, with special reference to urbanization processes.

In 1979, she was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).

She died before 2012, when she was listed as a deceased former member by the Washington Statistical Society.
