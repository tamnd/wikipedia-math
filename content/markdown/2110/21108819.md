# Michael Griebel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Griebel"}

**Michael Griebel** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His research focus lies on [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing), and he helped develop computer algorithms for [sparse grids](wikipedia://en/646088).

:::

Griebel was director of the Institute for Numerical Simulation at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) from 2003 to 2016. He is currently director of the Fraunhofer Institute for Algorithms and Scientific Computing in [Sankt Augustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankt_Augustin).[^o3]
