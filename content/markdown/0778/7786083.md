# HoDoMS

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="HoDoMS"}

**HoDoMS** (Heads of Departments of Mathematical Sciences) is an educational company that acts as a body to represent the heads of [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) [higher education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education) departments of [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436). It aims to discuss and promote the interests of higher education mathematics in the UK and to facilitate dialogue between departments.[^o3]

:::

## Governance {#Governance}

HoDoMS is operated by a committee including four officer roles which are listed below with incumbents.[^o5] The committee includes observers from the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139), [The OR Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_Research_Society), the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), the [Council for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/7779218) and the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).[^o5][^o6]

## Activities {#Activities}

The main activity of HoDoMS is to run an annual conference bringing members together for briefings and discussion on current issues.[^o9][^o10] For example, the 2020 conference heard briefings on policy issues such as research funding, the [Research Excellence Framework 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Excellence_Framework), the [Teaching Excellence Framework](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_Excellence_Framework) as well as practicalities such as online marking, knowledge exchange, teaching as a career for mathematics undergraduates, and academics and mental health.[^o11]

HoDoMS also collaborates with other organisations, for example with the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) on an 'Education Day' in 2019[^o12] and with the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) and the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) on an 'Induction Course for New Lecturers in the Mathematical Sciences' in 2021[^o13]

## History {#History}

The first meeting of HoDoMS took place on 14 September 1995 at [University College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_London) under its first chair, [Graham Wilks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_Wilks).[^o5] On 14 August 2018, HoDoMS was incorporated as a [Private company limited by guarantee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_company_limited_by_guarantee).[^o15]
