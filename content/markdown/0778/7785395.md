# Bernard Silverman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Silverman"}

**Sir Bernard Walter Silverman**, (born 22 February 1952) is a British statistician and former[^o4] Anglican clergyman. He was [Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_%28college%29) of [St Peter's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Peter's_College,_Oxford), from 1 October 2003 to 31 December 2009. He is a member of the Statistics Department at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), and has also been attached to the [Wellcome Trust Centre for Human Genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellcome_Trust_Centre_for_Human_Genetics), the Smith School of Enterprise and the Environment, and the [Oxford-Man Institute of Quantitative Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford-Man_Institute_of_Quantitative_Finance). He has been a member of the Council of the University of Oxford and of the Council of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society).[^o5] He was briefly president of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in January 2010, a position from which he stood down upon announcement of his appointment as [Chief Scientific Adviser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Chief_Scientific_Adviser) to the [Home Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Office).[^o6] He was awarded a knighthood in the 2018 New Year Honours List, "For public service and services to Science".[^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Silverman was educated at the [City of London School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_School),[^o9] an independent day school in Central London, from 1961 to 1969, on a Carpenter Scholarship (similar to today's full bursary),[^o10] followed by [Jesus College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Cambridge).[^o10]

## Career {#Career}

section

- 1970–73 Undergraduate, Jesus College, Cambridge
- 1973–74 Graduate Student, Jesus College, Cambridge
- 1974–75 Research Student, Statistical Laboratory, Cambridge
- 1975–77 Research Fellow of Jesus College, Cambridge
- 1976–77 Calculator Development Manager, [Sinclair Radionics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinclair_Radionics) Ltd
- 1977–78 Junior Lecturer in Statistics, University of Oxford, and Weir Junior Research Fellow of [University College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford)
- 1978–80 Lecturer in Statistics, [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath)
- 1981–84 Reader in Statistics, University of Bath
- 1984 and 1992–93 Head of Statistics Group, University of Bath
- 1984–93 Professor of Statistics, University of Bath
- 1988–91 Head of School of Mathematical Sciences, University of Bath
- 1993–2003 Professor of Statistics, [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol)
- 1993–97 and 1998–99 Head of Statistics Group, University of Bristol
- 1999–2003 Henry Overton Wills Professor of Mathematics, University of Bristol
- 2000–03 Provost of the Institute for Advanced Studies, University of Bristol
- 2003–09 Master of St Peter's College, Oxford
- 2010– Senior Research Fellow, [Smith School of Enterprise and the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_School_of_Enterprise_and_the_Environment), Oxford (part-time)
- 2010– Professorial Research Associate, [Wellcome Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellcome_Trust) Centre for Human Genetics, Oxford (part-time)
- 2010–17 Chief Scientific Adviser, Home Office
- 2018–22 Professor of Modern Slavery Statistics, [Rights Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rights_Lab), [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham)[^o12]

### Ecclesiastical career {#Ecclesiastical_career}

Silverman was [ordained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordained) in the [Church of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_England) as a [deacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deacon#Anglicanism) in 1999 and as a [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest#Anglican_or_Episcopalian) in 2000. From 1999 to 2005, he was an honorary [assistant curate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_curate) of [Cotham Parish Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotham_Parish_Church) in the [Diocese of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Bristol).[^o18] Between 2005 and 2009, he held [Permission to Officiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permission_to_Officiate) in the [Diocese of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Oxford).[^o18] Then, from 2009 to 2015, he was an honorary assistant curate of [St Giles' Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Giles'_Church,_Oxford) and [St Margaret's Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Margaret's_Church,_Oxford).[^o18] From 2015 to 2022, he held Permission to Officiate in both the Diocese of Oxford and in the [Diocese of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_London).[^o18] He retired from ordained ministry in 2022.[^o4]
