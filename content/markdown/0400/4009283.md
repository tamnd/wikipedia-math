# Closed concept

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Closed concept"}

A **closed concept** is a concept where all the [necessary and sufficient conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necessary_and_sufficient_conditions) required to include something within the concept can be listed. For example, the concept of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) is closed because it is both necessary and sufficient that something (1) be a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) and (2) have three sides for it to be a triangle. All the conditions required to call something a triangle can be and are listed.

:::

Its opposite is an "open concept".[^o2]
