In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 24-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) (4-dimensional uniform [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471)) formed as the [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).

:::

There are two degrees of truncations, including a [bitruncation](wikipedia://en/6792316).

The **truncated 24-cell** or **truncated icositetrachoron** is a uniform 4-dimensional polytope (or [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899)), which is bounded by 48 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 24 [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285), and 24 [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654). Each vertex joins three truncated octahedra and one cube, in an equilateral triangular pyramid [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

::: {#o13 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

The **truncated 24-cell** can be constructed from polytopes with three symmetry groups:

- F<sub>4</sub> \[3,4,3\]: A [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).
- B<sub>4</sub> \[3,3,4\]: A [cantitruncation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantitruncation) of the [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), with two families of truncated octahedral cells.
- D<sub>4</sub> \[3<sup>1,1,1</sup>\]: An [omnitruncation](wikipedia://en/7083690) of the [demitesseract](wikipedia://en/6833695), with three families of truncated octahedral cells.

### Zonotope {#Zonotope}

It is also a [zonotope](wikipedia://en/669406): it can be formed as the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of the six line segments connecting opposite pairs among the twelve permutations of the vector (+1,−1,0,0).

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a truncated 24-cell having edge length sqrt(2) are all coordinate permutations and sign combinations of:

(0,1,2,3) \[4!×2<sup>3</sup> = 192 vertices\]

The dual configuration has coordinates at all coordinate permutation and signs of

(1,1,1,5) \[4×2<sup>4</sup> = 64 vertices\]

(1,3,3,3) \[4×2<sup>4</sup> = 64 vertices\]

(2,2,2,4) \[4×2<sup>4</sup> = 64 vertices\]

### Structure {#Structure}

The 24 cubical cells are joined via their square faces to the truncated octahedra; and the 24 truncated octahedra are joined to each other via their hexagonal faces.

### Projections {#Projections}

The parallel projection of the truncated 24-cell into 3-dimensional space, truncated octahedron first, has the following layout:

- The projection envelope is a [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879).
- Two of the truncated octahedra project onto a truncated octahedron lying in the center of the envelope.
- Six cuboidal volumes join the square faces of this central truncated octahedron to the center of the octagonal faces of the great rhombicuboctahedron. These are the images of 12 of the cubical cells, a pair of cells to each image.
- The 12 square faces of the great rhombicuboctahedron are the images of the remaining 12 cubes.
- The 6 octagonal faces of the great rhombicuboctahedron are the images of 6 of the truncated octahedra.
- The 8 (non-uniform) truncated octahedral volumes lying between the hexagonal faces of the projection envelope and the central truncated octahedron are the images of the remaining 16 truncated octahedra, a pair of cells to each image.

The **bitruncated 24-cell**. **48-cell**, or **tetracontoctachoron** is a 4-dimensional uniform [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471) (or [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899)) derived from the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope.

It is constructed by [bitruncating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncation_%28geometry%29) the 24-cell (truncating at halfway to the depth which would yield the [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) 24-cell).

Being a uniform 4-polytope, it is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive). In addition, it is [cell-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-transitive), consisting of 48 [truncated cubes](wikipedia://en/280529), and also [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive), with 3 [truncated cubes](wikipedia://en/280529) cells per edge and with one triangle and two octagons around each edge.

The 48 cells of the bitruncated 24-cell correspond with the 24 cells and 24 vertices of the 24-cell. As such, the centers of the 48 cells form the [root system](wikipedia://en/277087) of type [F<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/292877).

Its vertex figure is a *[tetragonal disphenoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetragonal_disphenoid)*, a tetrahedron with 2 opposite edges length 1 and all 4 lateral edges length √(2+√2).

### Structure {#Structure}

The truncated cubes are joined to each other via their octagonal faces in *anti* orientation; i. e., two adjoining truncated cubes are rotated 45 degrees relative to each other so that no two triangular faces share an edge.

The sequence of truncated cubes joined to each other via opposite octagonal faces form a cycle of 8. Each truncated cube belongs to 3 such cycles. On the other hand, the sequence of truncated cubes joined to each other via opposite triangular faces form a cycle of 6. Each truncated cube belongs to 4 such cycles.

Seen in a [configuration matrix](wikipedia://en/56419724), all incidence counts between elements are shown. The diagonal [f-vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-vector) numbers are derived through the [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777), dividing the full group order of a subgroup order by removing one mirror at a time. Edges exist at 4 symmetry positions. Squares exist at 3 positions, hexagons 2 positions, and octagons one. Finally the 4 types of cells exist centered on the 4 corners of the fundamental simplex.

### Related regular skew polyhedron {#Related_regular_skew_polyhedron}

The [regular skew polyhedron](wikipedia://en/19193037), {8,4|3}, exists in 4-space with 4 octagonal around each vertex, in a zig-zagging nonplanar vertex figure. These octagonal faces can be seen on the bitruncated 24-cell, using all 576 edges and 288 vertices. The 192 triangular faces of the bitruncated 24-cell can be seen as removed. The dual regular skew polyhedron, {4,8|3}, is similarly related to the square faces of the [runcinated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_24-cell).

The **disphenoidal 288-cell** is the [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) of the bitruncated 24-cell. It is a 4-dimensional [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471) (or [polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polychoron)) derived from the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485). It is constructed by doubling and rotating the 24-cell, then constructing the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634).

Being the dual of a uniform polychoron, it is [cell-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-transitive), consisting of 288 congruent [tetragonal disphenoids](wikipedia://en/8476166). In addition, it is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) under the group Aut(F<sub>4</sub>).[^o47]

#### Geometry {#Geometry}

The vertices of the 288-cell are precisely the 24 [Hurwitz unit quaternions](wikipedia://en/772241) with norm squared 1, united with the 24 vertices of the dual 24-cell with norm squared 2, projected to the unit [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792). These 48 vertices correspond to the [binary octahedral group 2O](wikipedia://en/10997598) or \<2,3,4>, order 48.

Thus, the 288-cell is the only non-regular 4-polytope which is the convex hull of a quaternionic group, disregarding the infinitely many [dicyclic](wikipedia://en/154963) (same as binary dihedral) groups; the regular ones are the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) (≘ [2T](wikipedia://en/10997586) or \<2,3,3>, order 24) and the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) (≘ [2I](wikipedia://en/3195145) or \<2,3,5>, order 120). (The [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) corresponds to the [binary dihedral group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_dihedral_group) 2D<sub>2</sub> or \<2,2,2>, order 16.)

The inscribed 3-sphere has radius 1/2+$\sqrt{2}$/4 ≈ 0.853553 and touches the 288-cell at the centers of the 288 tetrahedra which are the vertices of the dual bitruncated 24-cell.

The vertices can be [coloured in 2 colours](wikipedia://en/292877#Roots_of_F4), say red and yellow, with the 24 Hurwitz units in red and the 24 duals in yellow, the yellow [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) being congruent to the red one. Thus the product of 2 equally coloured quaternions is red and the product of 2 in mixed colours is yellow.

Placing a fixed red vertex at the north pole (1,0,0,0), there are 6 yellow vertices in the next deeper “latitude” at ($\sqrt{2}$/2,x,y,z), followed by 8 red vertices in the latitude at (1/2,x,y,z). The complete coordinates are given as linear combinations of the quaternionic units $1, \mathrm{i}, \mathrm{j}, \mathrm{k}$, which at the same time can be taken as the elements of the group 2O. The next deeper latitude is the equator hyperplane intersecting the 3-sphere in a 2-sphere which is populated by 6 red and 12 yellow vertices.

Layer *2* is a 2-sphere circumscribing a regular octahedron whose edges have length 1. A tetrahedron with vertex north pole has 1 of these edges as long edge whose 2 vertices are connected by short edges to the north pole. Another long edge runs from the north pole into layer *1* and 2 short edges from there into layer *2*.

There are 192 long edges with length 1 connecting equal colours and 144 short edges with length $\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2}}$ ≈ 0.765367 connecting mixed colours. 192\*2/48 = 8 long and 144\*2/48 = 6 short, that is together 14 edges meet at any vertex.

The 576 faces are [isosceles](wikipedia://en/30654) with 1 long and 2 short edges, all congruent. The angles at the base are arccos($\sqrt{4+\sqrt{8}}$/4) ≈ 49.210°. 576\*3/48 = 36 faces meet at a vertex, 576\*1/192 = 3 at a long edge, and 576\*2/144 = 8 at a short one.

The 288 cells are tetrahedra with 4 short edges and 2 antipodal and perpendicular long edges, one of which connects 2 red and the other 2 yellow vertices. All the cells are congruent. 288\*4/48 = 24 cells meet at a vertex. 288\*2/192 = 3 cells meet at a long edge, 288\*4/144 = 8 at a short one. 288\*4/576 = 2 cells meet at a triangle.
