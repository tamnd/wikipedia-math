# Joachim Cuntz

Joachim Cuntz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joachim Cuntz"}

**Joachim Cuntz** (born 28 September 1948 in [Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannheim)) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Joachim Cuntz has made fundamental contributions to the area of [C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebras) and to the field of [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) in the sense of [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340). He initiated the analysis of the structure of simple C\*-algebras and introduced new methods and examples, including the [Cuntz algebras](wikipedia://en/4463325) and the [Cuntz semigroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuntz_semigroup). He was one of the first to apply [K-theory](wikipedia://en/13314265) to noncommutative operator algebras and contributed to the development of that theory. In collaboration with [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519), he developed a new approach to [cyclic cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_cohomology) and proved the excision property of periodic cyclic theory. In recent years, he has been working mainly on C\*-algebras that are related to structures from [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o4]

Joachim Cuntz had dozens of PhD students and research assistants, many of which are professors in mathematics today.[^o5] Among them are:

- Xin Li (PhD 2010, professor of mathematics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow))
- Ralf Meyer (PhD 1999, professor of mathematics at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen))
- [Andreas Thom](wikipedia://en/65001949) (PhD 2003, professor of geometry at the [TU Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Dresden))
- Christian Voigt (PhD 2003, professor of mathematics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow))
- Moritz Weber (PhD 2011, junior professor of mathematics at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University))
- [Wilhelm Winter](wikipedia://en/58159348) (PhD 2000, professor of mathematics at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster))
- Joachim Zacharias (PhD 1996, professor of mathematics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow))

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 1990 [Invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Kyoto (Cyclic Cohomology and K-homology).
- Max Planck Research Award (together with G. Kasparov), 1993
- Medal of the Collège de France, 1997
- [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) of the DFG 1999,
- [Honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen)
- ERC Advanced Investigators Grant, 2010
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012[^o7]
