# Theta operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theta operator"}

In mathematics, the **theta operator** is a [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886) defined by[^o3]

:::

$$\theta = z {d \over dz}.$$

This is sometimes also called the **homogeneity operator**, because its [eigenfunctions](wikipedia://en/228601) are the [monomials](wikipedia://en/357416) in *z*:

$$\theta (z^k) = k z^k,\quad k=0,1,2,\dots$$

In *n* variables the homogeneity operator is given by

$$\theta = \sum_{k=1}^n x_k \frac{\partial}{\partial x_k}.$$

As in one variable, the [eigenspaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenspace) of *θ* are the spaces of [homogeneous functions](wikipedia://en/622844). ([Euler's homogeneous function theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler's_homogeneous_function_theorem))
