# F\* (programming language)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="F*"}

**F\*** (pronounced *F star*) is a [high-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-level_programming_language), [multi-paradigm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_multi-paradigm_programming_languages), [functional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_programming) and [object-oriented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object-oriented_programming) [programming language](wikipedia://en/23015) inspired by the languages [ML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ML_%28programming_language%29), [Caml](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caml), and [OCaml](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OCaml), and intended for [program verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Program_verification). It is a joint project of [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) and the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413) (Inria).[^o2] Its [type system](wikipedia://en/199701) includes [dependent types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependent_types), [monadic](wikipedia://en/579061) [effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Side_effect_%28computer_science%29), and [refinement types](wikipedia://en/24449039). This allows expressing precise specifications for programs, including functional correctness and security properties. The F\* type-checker aims to prove that programs meet their specifications using a combination of [satisfiability modulo theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satisfiability_modulo_theories) (SMT) solving and [manual proofs](wikipedia://en/1258607). For execution, programs written in F\* can be translated to [OCaml](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OCaml), [F#](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F_Sharp_%28programming_language%29), [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_%28programming_language%29), [WebAssembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WebAssembly) (via KaRaMeL tool), or [assembly language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_language) (via Vale toolchain). Prior F\* versions could also be translated to [JavaScript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JavaScript).

:::

It was introduced in 2011[^o4][^o5] and is under active development on [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub).[^o6]

## History {#History}

### Versions {#Versions}

Until version 2022.03.24, F\* was written entirely in a common subset of F\* and [F#](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F_Sharp_%28programming_language%29) and supported bootstrapping in both [OCaml](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OCaml) and F#. This was dropped starting in version 2022.04.02.[^o9][^o10]

## Overview {#Overview}

### Operators {#Operators}

F\* supports common arithmetic [operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_%28computer_programming%29) such as `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/`. Also, F\* supports relational operators like `<`, `<=`, `==`, `!=`, `>`, and `>=`.[^o13]

### Data types {#Data_types}

Common primitive [data types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_data_type) in F\* are `bool`, `int`, `float`, `char`, and `unit`.[^o13]
