::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Cantitruncated 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **cantitruncated 24-cell honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can be seen as a [cantitruncation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantitruncation) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), containing [truncated tesseract](wikipedia://en/4023176), [cantitruncated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantitruncated_24-cell), and [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290) cells.

:::
