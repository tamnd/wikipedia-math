::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Stericated 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **stericated 24-cell honeycomb** (or **stericated 16-cell honeycomb**) is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can be seen as a [sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication_%28geometry%29) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), containing [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), [octahedral prism](wikipedia://en/8813885), [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290), and 3-3 [duoprism](wikipedia://en/2114155) cells.

:::
