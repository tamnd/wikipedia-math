::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Bitruncated 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **bitruncated 24-cell honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can be seen as a [bitruncation](wikipedia://en/6792316) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), constructed by [truncated tesseract](wikipedia://en/4023176) and [bitruncated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_24-cell) cells.

:::
