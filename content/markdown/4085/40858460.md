::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **runcinated 24-cell honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can be seen as a [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination_%28geometry%29) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), containing [runcinated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_24-cell), [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), [octahedral prism](wikipedia://en/8813885), and 3-3 [duoprism](wikipedia://en/2114155) cells.

:::
