# Martin Aigner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Aigner"}

**Martin Aigner** (28 February 1942 – 11 October 2023) was an Austrian mathematician and professor at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin) from 1974 with interests in [combinatorial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_mathematics) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Martin Aigner was born on 28 February 1942. He received his PhD from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). His book *[Proofs from THE BOOK](wikipedia://en/4500115)* (co-written with [Günter M. Ziegler](wikipedia://en/14628647)) has been translated into 12 languages.[^o6]

Aigner died on 11 October 2023, at the age of 81.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

Aigner was a recipient of a 1996 [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for his expository article *[Turán's Graph Theorem](wikipedia://en/360601)*.[^o9] In 2018, Aigner received the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition (jointly with Günter M. Ziegler).[^o10]
