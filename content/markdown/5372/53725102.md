# Deanna Needell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deanna Needell"}

**Deanna Needell** is an American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). She has authored over 200 research papers, graduated many PhD students and mentored a large number of postdocs. She is known for work on [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization), and [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324) as well as applications involving community nonprofits in medicine and social justice.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Deanna Needell received her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics from the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2009. Her dissertation title was *Topics in Compressed Sensing*.[^o3] She was a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University from 2009-2011.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Deanna Needell received the [IMA](wikipedia://en/10324088) Prize in Mathematics and Applications in 2016 along with collaborator [Rachel Ward](wikipedia://en/53724765). The award recognized their theoretical work related to compressed sensing with application to [MRIs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_resonance_imaging), with Needell recognized in particular for her contributions to sparse approximation, signal processing, and stochastic optimization.[^o5] She is also the recipient of the [Alfred P. Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Fellowship) and a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award). She was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to compressed sensing and the mathematics of data".[^o6] She is also a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow), in the 2024 class of fellows, elected "for contributions to compressed sensing, stochastic optimization, and applied data science".[^o7] She has given a large number of plenary and keynote at conferences around the world, including the 2024 Falconer Lecture at the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) Mathfest.
