# Rachel Ward (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rachel Ward"}

**Rachel Angela Ward** is an American applied mathematician at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). She is known for work on [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization), and [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324). At the University of Texas, she is W. A. "Tex" Moncrief Distinguished Professor in Computational Engineering and Sciences—Data Science, and professor of mathematics.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ward received her BS in mathematics from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in 2005.[^o5] She earned her PhD in applied and [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2009. Her doctoral advisor was [Ingrid Daubechies](wikipedia://en/1430989).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Ward was an instructor at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) from 2009-2011[^o8] and then joined the faculty at the University of Texas at Austin in 2011.[^o8] In 2018, she was a Visiting Research Scientist at [Facebook Artificial Intelligence Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Artificial_Intelligence_Research)[^o8] and in 2019 she was a Von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o9] She serves on the Scientific Advisory Board for the [Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31112547) (ICERM).[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Ward is known for her work across a wide variety of applied math areas including image processing, compressed sensing, and stochastic and adaptive gradient descent.[^o12] Ward also worked on a project funded by the [Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense), with faculty from UT Austin's College of Natural Sciences and Cockrell School, to develop [unmanned aerial vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle).[^o13]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Ward was awarded an [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in Mathematics in 2012.[^o15] Ward and [Deanna Needell](wikipedia://en/53725102) received the [IMA](wikipedia://en/10324088) Prize in Mathematics and Applications in 2016.[^o16] In 2020, Ward was named as a Simons Fellow.[^o12] Ward was an invited speaker at the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o17]
