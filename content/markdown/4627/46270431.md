# European Society for Mathematics and the Arts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="European Society for Mathematics and the Arts"}

**European Society for Mathematics and the Arts (ESMA)** is a [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe) society to promoting [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and the arts.[^o2] The first Conference of ESMA, took place in July 2010 at the [Institute Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_Henri_Poincaré) in Paris.[^o3][^o4]

:::
