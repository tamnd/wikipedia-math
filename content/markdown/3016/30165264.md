# Edward Fulton Denison

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Fulton Denison"}

**Edward Fulton Denison** (December 18, 1915, [Omaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omaha) – October 23, 1992, [Washington D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_D.C.)) was an American economist.[^o3][^o4][^o5] He was a pioneer in the measurement of the United States [gross national product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_national_product)[^o3] and one of the founders of [growth accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growth_accounting).[^o5]

:::

Denison earned a bachelor's degree in economics in [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) in 1936, a master's degree in [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1938, and a doctorate from Brown in 1941.[^o3] In 1948, he became acting chief of the National Income Division of the [Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Foreign_and_Domestic_Commerce).[^o5] The next year, Edward also acted as assistant director and Chief Economist of the [Office of Business Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Business_Economics).[^o3][^o5] In 1956 he left OBE to work for the [Committee for Economic Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_for_Economic_Development).[^o5] From 1963, he served as a senior member of the [Brookings Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookings_Institution) on economic research.[^o3][^o5]

In 1966 Denison was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o6] He became a distinguished fellow of the [American Economic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Economic_Association) in 1981.[^o7]

He married Elsie Lightbown. His daughter, [Janet Howell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Howell), served in the Virginia Senate 1992–2024.[^o8]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Trends in American economic growth, 1929-1982* (1962), published on *[The Journal of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Journal_of_Business)*
- *The Residual Factor and Economic Growth* ([Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), 1962)
- *The Sources of Economic Growth in the United States & the Alternatives Before us* ([New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), 1962), one that describes his theory mostly
- *Why growth rates differ; postwar experience in nine western countries* ([Washington D. C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_D._C.), 1967)
- *Accounting for United States economic growth, 1929-1969* (Washington D. C., 1974)
- *Accounting for slower economic growth : the United States in the 1970s* (Washington D. C., 1979)
