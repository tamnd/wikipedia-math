# Thomas William Körner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas William Körner"}

**Thomas William Körner** (born 17 February 1946) is a British [pure mathematician](wikipedia://en/457210) and the author of three books on popular mathematics. He is titular Professor of [Fourier Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_Analysis) in the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and a Fellow of [Trinity Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge). He is the son of the philosopher [Stephan Körner](wikipedia://en/7870826) and of [Edith Körner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Körner).

:::

He studied at [Trinity Hall, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge), and wrote his PhD thesis ***Some Results on Kronecker, Dirichlet and Helson Sets*** there in 1971, studying under [Nicholas Varopoulos](wikipedia://en/37551761).[^o3] In 1972 he won the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize).[^o4]

He has written academic mathematics books aimed at undergraduates:

- *Fourier Analysis*[^o5]
- *Exercises for Fourier Analysis*
- *A Companion to Analysis*
- *Vectors, Pure and Applied*
- *Calculus for the Ambitious*

He has also written three books aimed at secondary school students, the popular 1996 title *The Pleasures of Counting*, *Naive Decision Making* (published 2008) on [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), and *Where Do Numbers Come From?*[^o6] (published October 2019).
