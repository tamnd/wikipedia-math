# Journal d'Analyse Mathématique

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal d'Analyse Mathématique"}

The ***Journal d'Analyse Mathématique*** is a triannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) on behalf of Magnes Press ([Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem)). It was established in 1951 by [Binyamin Amirà](wikipedia://en/60040792).[^o3][^o4]

:::

The journal covers research in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis) and related areas such as [complex function theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_function_theory), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), and [quasiconformal mapping](wikipedia://en/2749226).[^o3]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in

- [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)[^o6]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o7]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o8]
- [ZbMATH Open](wikipedia://en/1701961)[^o9]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.0.[^o10]
