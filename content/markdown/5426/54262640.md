# Blumenthal's zero–one law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Blumenthal's zero–one law"}

In the mathematical theory of probability, **Blumenthal's [zero–one law](wikipedia://en/890891)**,[^o2] named after [Robert McCallum Blumenthal](wikipedia://en/56586935), is a statement about the nature of the beginnings of [right continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_continuous) [Feller process](wikipedia://en/6891551). Loosely, it states that any [right continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_continuous) [Feller process](wikipedia://en/6891551) on $[0,\infty)$ starting from deterministic point has also deterministic initial movement.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that $X=(X_t:t\geq 0)$ is an adapted [right continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_continuous) [Feller process](wikipedia://en/6891551) on a [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\{\mathcal{F}_{t}\}_{t\geq 0},\mathbb{P})$ such that $X_0$ is constant with probability one. Let $\mathcal{F}^X_t:=\sigma(X_s; s\leq t), \mathcal{F}^X_{t^+}:=\bigcap_{s>t}\mathcal{F}^X_s$. Then any event in the [germ](wikipedia://en/352714) [sigma algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_algebra) $\Lambda \in \mathcal{F}^X_{0+}$ has either $\mathbb{P}(\Lambda)=0$ or $\mathbb{P}(\Lambda)=1.$

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalization"}

## Generalization {#Generalization}

Suppose that $X=(X_t:t\geq 0)$ is an adapted [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) on a [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\{\mathcal{F}_{t}\}_{t\geq 0},\mathbb{P})$ such that $X_0$ is constant with probability one. If $X$ has [Markov property](wikipedia://en/306422) with respect to the filtration $\{\mathcal{F}_{t^+}\}_{t\geq 0}$ then any event $\Lambda \in \mathcal{F}^X_{0+}$ has either $\mathbb{P}(\Lambda)=0$ or $\mathbb{P}(\Lambda)=1.$ Note that every [right continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_continuous) [Feller process](wikipedia://en/6891551) on a [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\{\mathcal{F}_{t}\}_{t\geq 0},\mathbb{P})$ has [strong Markov property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_Markov_property) with respect to the filtration $\{\mathcal{F}_{t^+}\}_{t\geq 0}$.

:::
