# Characteristic variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Characteristic variety"}

In mathematical analysis, the **characteristic variety** of a [microdifferential operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microdifferential_operator) *P* is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) that is the zero set of the [principal symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_symbol) of *P* in the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600). It is invariant under a [quantized contact transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantized_contact_transformation).

:::

The notion is also defined more generally in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). A basic theorem says a characteristic variety is [involutive](wikipedia://en/293450).
