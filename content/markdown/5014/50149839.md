# Snub infinite-order triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub infinite-order triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub infinite-order triangular tiling** is a [uniform tiling](wikipedia://en/18640974) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) with a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{3,∞}.

:::
