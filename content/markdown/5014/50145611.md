# NeuroMat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NeuroMat"}

The **Research, Innovation, and Dissemination Center for Neuromathematics** (**RIDC NeuroMat**, or simply **NeuroMat**) is a Brazilian research center established in 2013 at the [Institute of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics,_University_of_São_Paulo) in the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) that is dedicated to integrating [mathematical modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modeling) and [theoretical neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_neuroscience). Among the core missions of NeuroMat are the creation of a new mathematical system to understanding neural data and the development of neuroscientific open-source computational tools, keeping an active role under the context of open knowledge, open science and scientific dissemination. The research center is headed by [Antonio Galves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Galves), from USP's Institute of Mathematics and Statistics, and is funded by the [São Paulo Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Paulo_Research_Foundation) (FAPESP). As of 2019, the co-principal investigators are [Oswaldo Baffa Filho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswaldo_Baffa_Filho) (USP), [Pablo A. Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_A._Ferrari) (USP/UBA), [Fernando da Paixão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_da_Paixão) (UNICAMP), [Antonio Carlos Roque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Carlos_Roque) (USP), [Jorge Stolfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Stolfi) (UNICAMP), and [Cláudia D. Vargas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cláudia_D._Vargas) (UFRJ). [Ernst W. Hamburger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_W._Hamburger) (USP) was the former director of scientific dissemination. NeuroMat's International Advisory Board consists of [David R. Brillinger](wikipedia://en/70523091) (UC Berkeley), [Leonardo G. Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_G._Cohen) (NIH), [Markus Diesmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markus_Diesmann) (Jülich), [Francesco Guerra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Guerra) (La Sapienza), [Wojciech Szpankowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojciech_Szpankowski) (Purdue).[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

NeuroMat has been involved in the development of what has been called the [Galves-Löcherbach model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galves-Löcherbach_model), a model with intrinsic stochasticity for biological neural nets, in which the probability of a future spike depends on the evolution of the complete system since the last spike. This model of spiking neurons was developed by mathematicians Antonio Galves and Eva Löcherbach. In the first article on the model, in 2013, they called it a model of a "system with interacting stochastic chains with memory of variable length.[^o5]
