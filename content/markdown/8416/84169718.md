# Arnold–Kuiper–Massey theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arnold–Kuiper–Massey theorem"}

In [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) in mathematics, the **Arnold–Kuiper–Massey theorem** (or **AKM theorem**) includes three connected results about [projective planes](wikipedia://en/24350) and their connection to [spheres](wikipedia://en/39782). The Arnold–Kuiper–Massey theorem is named after [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490), [Nicolaas Kuiper](wikipedia://en/3344929) und William Massey, who described its complex version in 1971, 1974 and 1973, respectively. The quaternionic generalization was described by Vladimir Arnold in 1999 as well as by [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) and [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894) in 2001. The octonionic generalization was described by Michael Atiyah and Jürgen Berndt in 2002.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Complex AKM theorem"}

## Complex AKM theorem {#Complex_AKM_theorem}

The first [orthogonal group](wikipedia://en/173954) $\operatorname{O}(1)\cong S^0=\{-1,+1\}\subsetneq\mathbb{R}$ of unit real numbers acts on the [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) $\mathbb{C}P^n$ by [complex conjugation](wikipedia://en/173918). For the complex projective plane, the [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859):[^o4][^o5]

$$\mathbb{C}P^2/\operatorname{O}(1) \cong S^4.$$

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Quaternionic AKM theorem"}

## Quaternionic AKM theorem {#Quaternionic_AKM_theorem}

The first [unitary group](wikipedia://en/173993) $\operatorname{U}(1)\cong S^1\subsetneq\mathbb{C}$ of [unit complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_complex_numbers) acts on the imaginary components of the [quaternionic projective space](wikipedia://en/2808931) $\mathbb{H}P^n$ by multiplication. For the quaternionic projective plane, the quotient space is homeomorphic to a sphere:[^o8][^o5]

$$\mathbb{H}P^2/\operatorname{U}(1) \cong S^7.$$

:::

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Octonionic AKM theorem"}

## Octonionic AKM theorem {#Octonionic_AKM_theorem}

The first [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) $\operatorname{Sp}(1)\cong S^3\subsetneq\mathbb{H}$ of [unit quaternions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_quaternions) acts on the second octonionic projective space or [Cayley plane](wikipedia://en/9410951) $\mathbb{O}P^2$ (there is no $\mathbb{O}P^n$ for $n\geq 3$ due to the octonions not being [associative](wikipedia://en/1335)) with:[^o5]

$$\mathbb{O}P^2/\operatorname{Sp}(1) \cong S^{13}.$$

:::
