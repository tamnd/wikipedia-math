# Box–Cox distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Box–Cox distribution"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **Box–Cox distribution** (also known as the **power-normal distribution**) is the distribution of a [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685) *X* for which the [Box–Cox transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box–Cox_transformation) on *X* follows a [truncated normal distribution](wikipedia://en/11753597). It is a continuous [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) having [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) (pdf) given by

:::

$$f(y) = \frac{1}{\left[1-I(f<0)-\operatorname{sgn}(f)\Phi(0,m,\sqrt{s})\right]\sqrt{2 \pi s^2}} \exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2s^2}\left(\frac{y^f}{f} - m\right)^2\right\}$$

for *y* > 0, where *m* is the [location parameter](wikipedia://en/17516) of the distribution, *s* is the dispersion, *ƒ* is the family parameter, *I* is the [indicator function](wikipedia://en/240790), Φ is the [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) of the [standard normal distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_normal_distribution), and sgn is the [sign function](wikipedia://en/505449).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Special cases"}

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

- *ƒ* = 1 gives a [truncated normal distribution](wikipedia://en/11753597).

:::
