# Christine Riedtmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christine Riedtmann"}

**Christine Riedtmann** (born 1952) is a Swiss mathematician specializing in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). She earned her PhD in 1978 from the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) under the supervision of [Pierre Gabriel](wikipedia://en/30716698), and is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) (since 2016) at the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern).

:::

In 2012–2013 she was president of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society).
