# Pietro Boselli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pietro Boselli"}

**Pietro Boselli** (born 3 December 1988) is an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28person%29), [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer), and former mathematics [postgraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postgraduate_education) [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher) at [University College London.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) He completed his PhD in November 2014 in the Department of Engineering, on the topic of "An Inverse Design Methodology for Long Last-Stage Steam Turbine Blades."[^o10]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pietro, the eldest of four brothers, was born in [Negrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negrar_di_Valpolicella), [Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Verona), but was raised in [Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brescia), which he considers his hometown.[^o4][^o5] When he was 6 years old, he was discovered by an agent, and began his modelling career with [Armani Junior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armani_Junior).[^o6] He completed his [secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education_in_Italy) at [Liceo Scientifico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liceo_scientifico) Statale Annibale Calini in 2007.[^o7][^o8]

He subsequently relocated to [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) at the age of 18 to pursue studies in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) (UCL). He graduated with a [first-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_honours) degree ([BEng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Engineering)) in 2010 and obtained a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924) in 2016.[^o9][^o10] During his doctoral studies, he served as a [teaching assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_assistant_%28United_Kingdom%29) from September 2010 to June 2013, a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) from September 2013 to June 2014, and a [visiting lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_lecturer) from October 2014 to March 2015 at UCL.[^o11][^o12]

In 2015, a [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook) post from 2014 by one of his students, which mentioned that he was also a successful model, went viral.[^o13][^o14][^o15] In response to his rising prominence, Boselli has expressed appreciation but has also publicly opposed the 'dumb model' [stereotype](wikipedia://en/18956166) and similar [prejudices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prejudice) on multiple occasions.[^o4][^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20]

In subsequent years, Boselli expanded his presence across various social media platforms, including [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram), [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook), [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter), [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok), and [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube), as well as subscription-based channels.[^o21] He characterizes his content as advocating for fitness, the symbiotic relationship between body and mind, and philosophical discourse.[^o22] Furthermore, Boselli remains faithful to his Italian heritage and scientific background in his content, promoting Mediterranean cuisine and employing scientific reasoning.[^o22] This combination, together with his inherent [masculine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masculinity) physique, has enabled him to amass and sustain a cumulative following of over 5 million users.[^o23] Consequently, he can be considered a mega social media influencer.[^o24][^o25] Boselli’s social media presence and approach appeal to and engage an audience that is not solely attracted by a [masculine physique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masculinity) but also appreciates [intellectual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intellectual) engagement.[^o21]

## Modeling {#Modeling}

Boselli is represented by British [modelling agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modeling_agency) Models 1.[^o27] In 2014, he won first place at the World Beauty Fitness & Fashion (WBFF) European ProAm event in the fitness model category.[^o14] He has appeared in [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_%28word%29) modeling campaigns for clothing retailer [Abercrombie & Fitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abercrombie_&_Fitch) and fitness club [Equinox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equinox_Fitness),[^o6] as well as for the fashion house [Moschino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moschino) and [*Vogue*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_%28magazine%29) magazine. He has also appeared in spreads for *[GQ Style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ_Style)* and has been featured on the cover of *[Attitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attitude_%28magazine%29)*.[^o28][^o29]

In 2016, he was signed by [Armani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armani) for their EA7 campaign.[^o30][^o31] In the same year, he founded [sportswear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportswear) brand Petra Design.[^o32] In August 2020, he was featured on a [lenticular cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenticular_printing) of *GQ Italia*, which alternated between his image and an image of [Michelangelo's David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_%28Michelangelo%29). The magazine was successfully sued by the [Galleria dell'Accademia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleria_dell'Accademia), which holds the image rights to the statue, for using a photograph of the statue without their permission.[^o33]
