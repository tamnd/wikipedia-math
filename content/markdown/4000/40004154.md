# Hoon Balakram

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hoon Balakram"}

**Hoon Balakram** (1876–1929)[^o3] was an Indian mathematician, civil servant and briefly a judge of the [Bombay High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_High_Court).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Balakram was born in [Jalandhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalandhar), [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_region) in 1876. He received his master's degree from [Panjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University) and later passed the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_Mathematical_Tripos) from the [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge).[^o3]

In 1899 he passed the [Indian Civil Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Services_of_India) examination. Among other posts in his career he was appointed a [Bombay High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_High_Court) judge in 1929, a month before his death.[^o3] He also served as the president of [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society) from 1921 to 1926.[^o5]

## Mathematical achievements {#Mathematical_achievements}

Balakram proved in 1929[^o3] that the expression ${(2n)! \over (n+1)! (n+1)!}$is an integer for infinitely many values of $n.$ He further showed that the expression is an integer for only eight values of $n\le 100$, namely, 5, 14, 27, 41, 44, 65, 76, and 90.
