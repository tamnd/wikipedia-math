# International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education"}

The ***International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) on behalf of the [National Science Council of Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Council). It covers [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_education) and [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) topics and research methods, particularly ones with cross-curricular dimensions or which explore the area from different cultural perspectives.[^o3] The journal was established in 2004 with Fou-Lai Lin ([National Taiwan Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_Normal_University)). The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Hsin-Kai Wu ([National Taiwan Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_Normal_University)).

:::
