# Young subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Young subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Young subgroups** of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) $S_n$ are special subgroups that arise in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). When $S_n$ is viewed as the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$, and if $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_\ell)$ is an [integer partition](wikipedia://en/104790) of $n$, then the Young subgroup $S_\lambda$ indexed by $\lambda$ is defined by

$$S_\lambda = S_{\{1, 2, \ldots, \lambda_1\}} \times S_{\{\lambda_1 + 1, \lambda_1 + 2, \ldots, \lambda_1 + \lambda_2\}} \times \cdots \times S_{\{n - \lambda_\ell + 1, n - \lambda_\ell + 2, \ldots, n\}},$$

where $S_{\{a, b, \ldots\}}$ denotes the set of permutations of $\{a, b, \ldots\}$ and $\times$ denotes the [direct product of groups](wikipedia://en/3706015). Abstractly, $S_\lambda$ is isomorphic to the product $S_{\lambda_1} \times S_{\lambda_2} \times \cdots \times S_{\lambda_\ell}$. Young subgroups are named for [Alfred Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Young_%28mathematician%29).[^o2]

:::

When $S_n$ is viewed as a [reflection group](wikipedia://en/2422023), its Young subgroups are precisely its [parabolic subgroups](wikipedia://en/75737969). They may equivalently be defined as the subgroups generated by a subset of the [adjacent transpositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjacent_transposition) $(1 \ 2), (2 \ 3), \ldots, (n - 1 \ n)$.[^o3]

In some cases, the name *Young subgroup* is used more generally for the product $S_{B_1} \times \cdots \times S_{B_\ell}$, where $\{B_1, \ldots, B_\ell\}$ is any [set partition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_partition) of $\{1, \ldots, n\}$ (that is, a collection of [disjoint](wikipedia://en/39359), nonempty subsets whose union is $\{1, \ldots, n\}$).[^o4] This more general family of subgroups consists of all the [conjugates](wikipedia://en/49176) of those under the previous definition.[^o5] These subgroups may also be characterized as the subgroups of $S_n$ that are generated by a set of [transpositions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transposition_%28mathematics%29).[^o6]
