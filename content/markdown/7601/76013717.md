# Kim Jae Kyoung

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Kim Jae Kyoung"}

**Kim Jae Kyoung** (; born December 14, 1982) is a biomedical mathematician and associate professor at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) in the Department of Mathematical Sciences[^o4][^o5] and a chief investigator in the Pioneer Research Center for Mathematical and Computational Sciences at the [Institute for Basic Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Basic_Science). His research focuses on [mathematical biology](wikipedia://en/662088) and [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine), specifically the combination of nonlinear dynamics, the theory of [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and [computational science](wikipedia://en/1181008), to better understand disease mechanisms and develop relevant treatment strategies, including drug and digital medicine for sleep disorders.[^o6][^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kim was born in [Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu), South Korea in 1982. He attended Yeungnam Middle School and [Yeungnam High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeungnam_High_School) before moving to Seoul where he graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) with a B.A. in mathematics education from [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) in 2005. Reading a news article about using math to study heart disease led him to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) as it was known for mathematical biology.[^o9] He received his Ph.D. in applied and interdisciplinary mathematics in 2013. He received the [Sumner Byron Myers](wikipedia://en/3327747) Prize for this thesis on cellular clocks.[^o10][^o11] His advisors were Daniel Forger and Victoria Booth while [Trachette Jackson](wikipedia://en/47404029) was a thesis committee member.

## Career {#Career}

Kim was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Mathematical Biosciences Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences_Institute) within [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).[^o13][^o14][^o15] Returning to Korea, he served as an assistant[^o16] and associate professor in the Department of Mathematical Science in [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST).[^o17] He also worked as an associate member at the [Korea Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/16984) from 2018 and then as a visiting scholar in the Department of Mathematics in the University of Michigan from 2019 to 2020. He became a member of Young Korean Academy of Science and Technology in 2019.[^o81]

In March 2021, Kim became a chief investigator of the Pioneer Research Center for Mathematical and Computational Sciences at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS), heading the Biomedical Mathematics Group (BIMAG).[^o18][^o19][^o20] Three teams conduct research at BIMAG. The Intracellular Dynamics Team develops reduction methods for multi-scale stochastic systems and identifies molecular mechanisms underlying robust circadian rhythms. The Intercellular Dynamics Team develops inference methods for dynamic networks and causes of and treatment strategies for circadian rhythm sleep disorders at the network level. The Systemic Dynamics Team develops methods for analyzing noisy human activity data from wearable devices and their application to identify optimal sleep patterns, and develops chronotherapy for sleep disorders and cancer.[^o21]

### Mechanisms for period regulation of circadian clock {#Mechanisms_for_period_regulation_of_circadian_clock}

Despite temperature changes, Kim found a mathematical model which underlies a molecular mechanism for regulation of the [circadian clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_clock). This biological clock was discovered in 1954, but the method for how it operates took more than 60 years to understand. Relevant factors include the topology of biochemical network underlying the circadian clock, phospho-switch that controls stability of key repressor proteins can compensate the effects of temperature, cells in the mammalian circadian clocks are coupled to generate synchronized rhythms, and a mechanism that filters [stochasticity](wikipedia://en/292222) in the circadian clock.[^o23][^o24]

### Mathematical models to treat diseases caused by disrupted circadian rhythms {#Mathematical_models_to_treat_diseases_caused_by_disrupted_circadian_rhythms}

Misalignment of [circadian rhythms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_rhythm) with the external environment can cause significant physiological problems. Kim studied the drug [PF-670462](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casein_kinase_1_isoform_epsilon#PF-670462_and_PF-4800567) which aims to realign the circadian clock. Using [pharmacokinetic data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacokinetics), he built a pharmacology model and analyzed the phase response curve to the drug and light which helped to predict the drug's effect under various environmental conditions.[^o26] As disruption of circadian rhythms increases the risk of getting cancer, he studied the interactions between [p53](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P53) and [PER2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PER2).[^o27]

### Derivation of validity condition for stochastic QSSA {#Derivation_of_validity_condition_for_stochastic_QSSA}

Kim was the first to propose and then prove multiple results in justifying the stochastic [quasi-steady state approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steady_state_%28chemistry%29) (QSSA).[^o29][^o30][^o31][^o32][^o33] The approximation has been widely used to reduce stochastic model of various biological systems.[^o34] Despite its common use, there has been a critical problem in understanding whether the stochastic QSSA is accurate and under what conditions it may fail. Kim has worked to developing a method to compute the error of the stochastic QSSA directly. This provided a clear guideline for when and how to use the stochastic QSSA. Furthermore, he developed a new method for simplifying models when the stochastic QSSA is invalid.[^o35][^o33]

### Discovering and correcting the error in the equation of FDA guidelines {#Discovering_and_correcting_the_error_in_the_equation_of_FDA_guidelines}

Kim found errors in the US [Food and Drug Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration)'s (FDA) current guidance for predicting how [drugs interact in the body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_interaction) and created a more accurate formula. The current FDA method is inaccurate, requiring artificial adjustments. The new formula is based on an improved mathematical foundation and can accurately predict drug metabolism rates without artificial factors. When tested, the new formula predicted drug interactions accurately 80% of the time compared to 38% with the equation in FDA guidance. This improvement could greatly enhance drug development and safety in clinical use.[^o37][^o38][^o39]

### Software development {#Software_development}

BIMAG has also made various software during their research, including mathematical models for [biological oscillators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscillation#Biological), [Bayesian inference algorithms](wikipedia://en/49571), [stochastic analysis](wikipedia://en/228001), [causality inference](wikipedia://en/37103476), diagnosis for [sleep disorders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_disorder), and [sleep data imputation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_tracking).[^o41]

## Journal editing {#Journal_editing}

Jae Kyoung has been an editorial board member of *[Journal of Biological Rhythms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Biological_Rhythms)*,[^o43] *[PLOS One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLOS_One)*,[^o44] *[Journal of Theoretical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Theoretical_Biology)*,[^o45] *[Mathematical Biosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences)*,[^o46] *[Current Opinion in Systems Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Opinion_in_Systems_Biology)*,[^o47] *npj Systems Biology and Applications*,[^o48] *npj Biological Timing and Sleep*,[^o49] and *[PLOS Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLOS_Computational_Biology)*,[^o50] as well as a guest associate editor of *[PLOS Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLOS_Computational_Biology)*.[^o51] He will serve as an editorial board member of *[SIAM Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Review)* from 2025 to 2028.[^o52][^o53] He is the Editor-in-Chief of *[Current Opinion in Systems Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Opinion_in_Systems_Biology)*.[^o54]
