# Vissarion Alekseyev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vissarion Alekseyev"}

*Image: Vissarion Aleksejev.jpg.* Vissarion Alekseyev

**Vissarion Grigorievich Alekseyev** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Виссарион Григорьевич Алексеев; 18 June 1866 – 1943) was a Russian mathematician. 1909-1914 and 1917–1918 he was the [rector of Tartu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_of_Tartu_University).[^o2]

:::

He was graduated from Moscow University. Since 1891 he worked at the [Imperial University of Dorpat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Dorpat).[^o2]

In 1920 he moved permanently to Estonia.[^o2]

He died in 1943 in Poland.[^o2]
