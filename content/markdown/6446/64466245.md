# Matrix factorization of a polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matrix factorization of a polynomial"}

In mathematics, a **matrix factorization of a polynomial** is a technique for factoring [irreducible polynomials](wikipedia://en/188725) with [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859). [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845) proved that every [multivariate real-valued polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_polynomial) *p* without linear terms can be written as *AB* = *pI*, where *A* and *B* are [square matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_matrices) and *I* is the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718).[^o2] Given the polynomial *p*, the matrices *A* and *B* can be found by elementary methods.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

The polynomial *x*<sup>2</sup> + *y*<sup>2</sup> is irreducible over **R**\[*x*,*y*\], but can be written as

$$\left[\begin{array}{cc} x & -y \\ y & x \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{cc} x & y \\ -y & x \end{array}\right] = (x^2 + y^2) \left[\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{array}\right]$$

:::
