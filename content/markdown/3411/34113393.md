# Sendov's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sendov's conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Sendov's theorem** (formally a conjecture), sometimes also called **Ilieff's conjecture**, concerns the relationship between the locations of [roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_polynomial) and [critical points](wikipedia://en/1951424) of a [polynomial function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_function) of a [complex variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_variable). It is named after [Blagovest Sendov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blagovest_Sendov).

:::

The [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) states that for a polynomial

$$f(z) = (z - r_1)\cdots (z-r_n),\quad n\ge 2,$$

with all roots *r*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *r*<sub>*n*</sub> inside the [closed unit disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_unit_disk), each of the *n* roots is at a distance no more than 1 from at least one [critical point](wikipedia://en/1951424).

The [Gauss–Lucas theorem](wikipedia://en/9397319) says that all of the critical points lie within the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the roots. It follows that the critical points must be within the unit disk, since the roots are.

The conjecture was previously [proven](wikipedia://en/82285) for *n* \< 9 by Brown and Xiang and for *n* [sufficiently large](wikipedia://en/10077292) by [Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498).[^o2][^o3]

## History {#History}

The conjecture was first proposed by [Blagovest Sendov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blagovest_Sendov) in 1959; he described the conjecture to his colleague [Nikola Obreshkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikola_Obreshkov). In 1967 the conjecture was misattributed[^o8] to Ljubomir Iliev by [Walter Hayman](wikipedia://en/34191584).[^o9] In 1969 Meir and Sharma proved the conjecture for polynomials with *n* \< 6. In 1991 Brown proved the conjecture for *n* \< 7. Borcea extended the proof to *n* \< 8 in 1996. Brown and Xiang[^o10] proved the conjecture for *n* \< 9 in 1999. In 2020, [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) proved the conjecture in the special case of polynomials with sufficiently high [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652).[^o11]
