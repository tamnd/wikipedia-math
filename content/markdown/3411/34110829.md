# Maciej Zworski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maciej Zworski"}

**Maciej Zworski** (born 8 October 1963) is a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)-[Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His mathematical interests include [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841), [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory), and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).

:::

He was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing in 2002,[^o7][^o8] and a plenary speaker at the conference Dynamics, Equations and Applications in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) in 2019.[^o9][^o10]

Zworski and Semyon Dyatlov are the recipients[^o11] of the 2026 Joseph L. Doob prize, for their 2019 book *Mathematical Theory of Scattering Resonances*.[^o12][^o13]
