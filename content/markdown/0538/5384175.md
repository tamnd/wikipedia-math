# Guido Hoheisel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guido Hoheisel"}

*Image: Hoheisel,Guido 1930 Jena.jpg.* Guido Hoheisel (1930)

**Guido Karl Heinrich Hoheisel** (14 July 1894 – 11 October 1968) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne).

:::

## Academic life {#Academic_life}

He did his PhD in 1920 from the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) under the supervision of [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191).[^o3] During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) Hoheisel was required to teach classes simultaneously at three universities, in [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne), [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), and [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster).[^o4] His doctoral students include [Arnold Schönhage](wikipedia://en/1354452).

Hoheisel contributed to the journal [Deutsche Mathematik](wikipedia://en/31018614).

## Selected results {#Selected_results}

Hoheisel is known for a result on [gaps between prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaps_between_prime_numbers):[^o6] He proved that if π(x) denotes the [prime-counting function](wikipedia://en/331826), then there exists a constant θ \< 1 such that

π(*x* + *x*<sup>θ</sup>) − π(*x*) ~ *x*<sup>θ</sup>/log(*x*),

as *x* tends to infinity, implying that if *p*<sub>*n*</sub> denotes the *n*-th [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) then

*p*<sub>*n*+1</sub> − *p*<sub>*n*</sub> \< *p*<sub>*n*</sub><sup>*θ*,</sup>

for all [sufficiently large](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficiently_large) *n*. He showed that one may take

*θ = 32999/33000 = 1 - 0.000(03),*

with (03) denoting periodic repetition.

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Gewöhnliche Differentialgleichungen* 1926;[^o8] 2nd edition 1930;[^o9] 7th edition 1965
- *Partielle Differentialgleichungen* 1928; 3rd edition 1953
- *Aufgabensammlung zu den gewöhnlichen und partiellen Differentialgleichungen* 1933[^o10]
- *Integralgleichungen* 1936;[^o11] revised and expanded 2nd edition 1963
- *Existenz von Eigenwerten und Vollständigkeitskriterium* 1943
- *Integral equations* translated by [A. Mary Tropper](wikipedia://en/76885484) \[1968, c1967\]
