# Christopher Hooley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christopher Hooley"}

**Christopher Hooley** (7 August 1928 – 13 December 2018)[^o3] was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University).

:::

He did his PhD under the supervision of [Albert Ingham](wikipedia://en/5380138). He won the [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110) of [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in 1973. He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1983.[^o4] He was also a Founding Fellow of the [Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales).

He showed that the [Hasse principle](wikipedia://en/489707) holds for non-singular [cubic forms](wikipedia://en/6680683) in at least nine variables.[^o5]
