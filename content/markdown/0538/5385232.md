# Martin Huxley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Huxley"}

**Martin Neil Huxley** [FLSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales) (born in 1944) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in the field of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

He was awarded a PhD from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1970, the year after his supervisor [Harold Davenport](wikipedia://en/991111) had died. He is a professor at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University).

Huxley proved a result on [gaps between prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaps_between_prime_numbers),[^o3] namely that if *p*<sub>*n*</sub> denotes the *n*-th prime number and if θ > 7/12, then

$$p_{n+1} - p_n < p_n^\theta,$$

for all [sufficiently large](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficiently_large) *n*.

Huxley also improved the known bound on the [Dirichlet divisor problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_divisor_problem).[^o4]

In 2011, Huxley was elected a [Fellow of the Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales).[^o5]
