# Manin obstruction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Manin obstruction"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [arithmetic algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), the **Manin obstruction** (named after [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086)) is attached to a variety *X* over a [global field](wikipedia://en/288358), which measures the failure of the [Hasse principle](wikipedia://en/489707) for *X*. If the value of the obstruction is non-trivial, then *X* may have points over all [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375) but not over the [global field](wikipedia://en/288358). The Manin obstruction is sometimes called the **Brauer–Manin obstruction**, as Manin used the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078#The_Brauer–Manin_obstruction) of X to define it.

:::

For [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) the Manin obstruction is just the [Tate–Shafarevich group](wikipedia://en/25711736) and fully accounts for the failure of the local-to-global principle (under the assumption that the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite). There are however examples, due to [Alexei Skorobogatov](wikipedia://en/25642036), of varieties with trivial Manin obstruction which have points everywhere locally and yet no global points.
