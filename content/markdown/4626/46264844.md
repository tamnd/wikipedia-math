# Johannes Kemperman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Kemperman"}

**Johannes Henricus Bernardus Kemperman** (July 16, 1924 – June 13, 2011) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He taught at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) for 25 years, and also worked at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) for ten years, each.[^o3]

:::

Born in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), he received his education from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam).[^o4]
