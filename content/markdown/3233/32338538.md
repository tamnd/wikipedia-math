# Base change lifting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Base change lifting"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **base change lifting** is a method of constructing new [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) from old ones, that corresponds in [Langlands philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands_philosophy) to the operation of restricting a representation of a [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) to a subgroup.

:::

The [Doi–Naganuma lifting](wikipedia://en/32411640) from 1967 was a precursor of the base change lifting. Base change lifting was introduced by (txt) for [Hilbert modular forms](wikipedia://en/2333880) of cyclic totally real fields of prime degree, by comparing the trace of twisted [Hecke operators](wikipedia://en/521837) on Hilbert modular forms with the trace of Hecke operators on ordinary modular forms. (Shintani 1979) gave a representation theoretic interpretation of Saito's results and used this to generalize them. (Langlands 1980) extended the base change lifting to more general automorphic forms and showed how to use the base change lifting for GL<sub>2</sub> to prove the Artin conjecture for tetrahedral and some octahedral 2-dimensional representations of the Galois group.

(Gelbart 1977), (Gérardin 1979) and (Gérardin Labesse 1979) gave expositions of the base change lifting for GL<sub>2</sub> and its applications to the Artin conjecture.

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

If *E*/*F* is a finite cyclic [Galois extension](wikipedia://en/457064) of [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358), then the base change lifting of (Arthur Clozel 1989) gives a map from automorphic forms for GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*F*) to automorphic forms for GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*E*) = Res<sub>*E*/*F*</sub>GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*F*). This base change lifting is the special case of [Langlands functoriality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands_functoriality), corresponding (roughly) to the diagonal embedding of the Langlands dual GL<sub>*n*</sub>(**C**) of GL<sub>*n*</sub> to the Langlands dual GL<sub>*n*</sub>(**C**)×...×GL<sub>*n*</sub>(**C**) of Res<sub>*E*/*F*</sub>GL<sub>*n*</sub>.

:::
