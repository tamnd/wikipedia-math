# Miroslav Fiedler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miroslav Fiedler"}

**Miroslav Fiedler** (7 April 1926 – 20 November 2015) was a Czech [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [algebraic graph theory](wikipedia://en/1620216).

:::

His article, "Algebraic Connectivity of Graphs", published in the *Czechoslovak Math Journal* in 1973, established the use of the [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of the [Laplacian matrix](wikipedia://en/1448472) of a graph to create tools for measuring [algebraic connectivity](wikipedia://en/3396069) in [algebraic graph theory](wikipedia://en/1620216).[^o5] Fiedler is honored by the *[Fiedler eigenvalue](wikipedia://en/3396069)* (the second smallest eigenvalue of the [graph Laplacian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_Laplacian)), with its associated *[Fiedler eigenvector](wikipedia://en/3396069)*, as the names for the quantities that characterize algebraic connectivity. Since Fiedler's original contribution, this structure has become essential to large areas of research in [network theory](wikipedia://en/766409), [flocking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flocking_%28behavior%29), [distributed control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_control), [clustering](wikipedia://en/669675), [multi-robot applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-robot_applications) and [image segmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_segmentation).[^o6]
