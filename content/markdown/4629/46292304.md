# Boris Komrakov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Komrakov"}

**Boris Petrovich Komrakov** (Борис Петрович Комраков; born 30 October 1948, in [Ivano-Frankivsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivano-Frankivsk)) is a Russian mathematician, who works on [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups).

:::

Komrakov received his PhD in 1991 at the [University of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu) in [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) with thesis *Primitive Actions and the Sophus Lie Problem*;[^o2] however, while living in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk) he had published papers since the 1970s. He is a professor and the director of the International Sophus Lie Center founded in 1990 in Minsk; the center collaborates with the University of Oslo. In 1997 Komrakov received the [Lobachevsky Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobachevsky_Medal) fof his work on the theory of Lie groups, in particular for *Primitive Actions and the Sophus Lie Problem* and the book *Structures on Manifolds and Homogeneous Spaces*.[^o3] Komrakov has contributed to the theory of the Lie groups with applications in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and the theory of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).
