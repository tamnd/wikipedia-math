# Leticia Brambila Paz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leticia Brambila Paz"}

[Brambila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brambila) **Gloria Leticia Brambila Paz** (born 1953) is a Mexican mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and the [moduli of algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/3010057). She is a professor in the [Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas](wikipedia://en/1631822) (CIMAT) in [Guanajuato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanajuato), Mexico.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Brambila was born on 26 January 1953. She went to [Swansea University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University) in the United Kingdom for doctoral study in mathematics, completing her PhD in 1986 with the dissertation *Homomorphisms of Vector Bundles over Compact Riemann Surface* supervised by Alan Thomas.

She worked as an assistant professor at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) from 1973 to 1976, and as a professor of mathematics at [UAM Iztapalapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAM_Iztapalapa) in Mexico City from 1983 to 1998, when she moved to her present position at CIMAT. She also became a life fellow of [Clare Hall, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_Hall,_Cambridge) in 2011.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Brambila was elected to the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2001.
