# Willem van der Woude

Van der WoudeVan der Woude

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Willem van der Woude"}

**Willem van der Woude** (15 January 1876 – 23 September 1974) was a Dutch mathematician and [*rector magnificus*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) (chancellor) of the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leiden).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

*Image: WvdWoude1945.jpg.* Van der Woude (middle) as rector in Leiden (1945)

Van der Woude studied at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen), and subsequently, from 1901 to 1916, worked as a secondary school teacher in [Deventer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deventer). In 1908 he received his Ph.D. from the University of Groningen under [Pieter Hendrik Schoute](wikipedia://en/6022750) with a thesis titled *Over elkaar snijdende normalen aan een ellipsoide en een hyperellipsoide* (On intersecting normals to an [ellipsoid](wikipedia://en/145381) and a hyperellipsoid). From 1916 until his retirement as professor emeritus in 1947 he was professor of mathematics and mechanics at the University of Leiden.

In 1924, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Toronto.[^o6] In the years 1923, 1924, 1939 and 1940 he chaired the [Royal Dutch Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/43504918).

He acted as *rector magnificus* of the University of Leiden during three separate periods: 1934–1935, 1941–43 and 1945 (until he was succeeded by [Berend George Escher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berend_George_Escher)).
