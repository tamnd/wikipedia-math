# Lüroth quartic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lüroth quartic"}

*Image: Luroth quartic.svg.* Some examples of **Lüroth quartics**

In mathematics, a **Lüroth quartic** is a nonsingular quartic [plane curve](wikipedia://en/971691) containing the 10 vertices of a complete pentalateral. They were introduced by (txt). (txt) showed that the Lüroth quartics form an open subset of a degree 54 [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101), called the **Lüroth hypersurface**, in the space **P**<sup>14</sup> of all quartics. (Böhning von Bothmer 2011) proved that the moduli space of Lüroth quartics is rational.

:::
