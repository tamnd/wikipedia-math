# Frank Ruskey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Ruskey"}

**Frank Ruskey** is a [combinatorialist](wikipedia://en/5170) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), and professor at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria). His research involves algorithms for exhaustively listing discrete structures, combinatorial [Gray codes](wikipedia://en/50564), [Venn](wikipedia://en/61701) and [Euler diagrams](wikipedia://en/2427526), [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490), and [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533).[^o3] Frank Ruskey is the author of the Combinatorial Object Server (COS), a website for information on and generation of combinatorial objects.[^o4]

:::
