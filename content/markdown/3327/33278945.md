# Leonard Roth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonard Roth"}

**Leonard Roth** (29 August 1904 – 28 November 1968) was a mathematician working in the [Italian school of algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/383424). He introduced an example of a [unirational variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unirational_variety) that was not rational (though his proof that it was not rational was incomplete).

:::

Roth was born in [Edmonton, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton,_London). He was educated at [Latymer Upper School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latymer_Upper_School), [Dulwich College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulwich_College) and [Clare College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_College,_Cambridge), where he graduated as a [Wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) in 1926.[^o2] He died in 1968 in [Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh,_Pennsylvania). His sister was [Queenie Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenie_Roth), literary critic and wife of [F. R. Leavis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F._R._Leavis).
