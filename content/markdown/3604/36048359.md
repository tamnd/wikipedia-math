# Michael Lin (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Lin"}

**Michael Lin** (מיכאל לין; born June 8, 1942) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who has published scientific articles in the field of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) concentrating on [Markov chains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chains) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).[^o3][^o4] He serves as professor emeritus at the Department of Mathematics in [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) (BGU).[^o5] Additionally, he is a member of the academic board and serves as the academic coordinator at [Achva Academic College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achva_Academic_College).[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Michael Lin was born in Israel. He holds a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Mathematics and Physics from [The Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) (1963), [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (1967) and a PhD in Mathematics also from [The Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) (1971). In 1971 he was appointed as an assistant professor in [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). In 1976 he returned to Israel and became a senior lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at [Ben-Gurion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) of the Negev. Only 4 years later, at 1979, he became an associate professor and in 1984 he became a full professor. In 2011, Professor Lin retired and nowadays he serves as professor emeritus. During his career at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev he acted as:

- Computer Science Coordinator, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, BGU.
- Member of BGU Computer Policy committee.
- Chairman and Computer Science Coordinator, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, BGU.
- Senate representative to Executive Committee of Board of Trustees of BGU.
- Senate representative to the BGU Executive Committee's subcommittee for student affairs.
- President, [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674).[^o8][^o9]
- Head of the Ethical Code Committee of BGU.[^o10]

In 2004 Professor Lin also acted as a member of the committee electing the recipients of the [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize) in mathematics.[^o11]

## Research and publications {#Research_and_publications}

Professor Lin's published work focuses on two main areas of research in the field of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934): [Ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986)[^o13][^o14] and [Markov chain](wikipedia://en/60876).[^o15][^o16] More specifically, he researched in several areas: mean and individual [Ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986),[^o17][^o18] [Central limit theorem](wikipedia://en/39406)[^o19] and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o13][^o20]
