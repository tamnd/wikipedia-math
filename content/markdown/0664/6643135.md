# Edna Grossman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edna Grossman"}

**Edna Grossman** (born **Edna Kalka**) is an American mathematician. She was born in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), grew up in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), and graduated with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College). She earned her [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in mathematics from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)'s [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), where she also received her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics in 1972; her thesis, supervised by [Wilhelm Magnus](wikipedia://en/8337666), concerned the [symmetries](wikipedia://en/53741) of [free groups](wikipedia://en/59735). Grossman worked for [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), where she was part of the team that designed and analyzed the [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978).[^o3] She is known for her development, along with [Bryant Tuckerman](wikipedia://en/1211923), of the first [slide attack](wikipedia://en/1821327) in [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715).[^o4]

:::
