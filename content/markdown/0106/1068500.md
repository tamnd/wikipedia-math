# John Pollard (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Pollard"}

**John M. Pollard** (born 1941) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has invented algorithms for the [factorization](wikipedia://en/15491) of large numbers and for the calculation of [discrete logarithms](wikipedia://en/181334).

:::

His factorization algorithms include the [rho](wikipedia://en/580668), [*p* − 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollard's_p_&minus;_1_algorithm), and the first version of the [special number field sieve](wikipedia://en/589132), which has since been improved by others.

His discrete logarithm algorithms include the [rho algorithm for logarithms](wikipedia://en/1630817) and the [kangaroo algorithm](wikipedia://en/12928899). He received the [RSA Award for Excellence in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_Award_for_Excellence_in_Mathematics).
