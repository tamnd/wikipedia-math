# BATON

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="BATON"}

**BATON** is a [Type 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_1_encryption) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) in use since at least 1995 by the [United States government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_government) to secure [classified information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information_in_the_United_States).

:::

While the BATON algorithm itself is secret (as is the case with all algorithms in the NSA's [Suite A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSA_Suite_A_Cryptography)), the public PKCS#11 standard includes some general information about how it is used. It has a 320-bit key and uses a 128-bit block in most modes, and also supports a 96-bit [electronic codebook mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_codebook#Electronic_codebook_%28ECB%29). 160 bits of the key are [checksum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checksum) material. It supports a ["shuffle" mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/"shuffle"_mode) of operation, like the NSA cipher JUNIPER. It may use up to 192 bits as an initialization vector, regardless of the block size.[^o3]

In response to a [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Senate) question about encrypted video links, the NSA said that BATON could be used for encryption at speeds higher than those possible with [Skipjack](wikipedia://en/474702).[^o4]

## Usage {#Usage}

BATON is used in a variety of products and standards:

- [APCO Project 25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APCO_Project_25) (Public standard for land mobile radio) (Algorithm IDs 01 and 41)
- [PKCS#11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKCS11) (Public standard for encryption tokens)
- [CDSA/CSSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDSA/CSSM) (Another public standard)
- [HAIPE-IS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAIPE-IS) ([NSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSA)'s version of [IPsec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPsec))
- [FNBDT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FNBDT) (Advanced flexible voice security protocol)
- [Thales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group) [Datacryptor 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datacryptor_2000) (a British network-encryption box)
- [SecNet-11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SecNet-11) (a crypto-secure [802.11b](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/802.11b) [PC Card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PC_Card), based on the [Sierra chip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_%28microchip%29))
- [Fortezza Plus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KOV-14) (a [PC Card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PC_Card) product, used in the [STE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Terminal_Equipment))
- [SafeXcel-3340](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SafeXcel-3340) (a HAIPIS network-encryption box)
- Numerous embeddable encryption modules: [AIM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_INFOSEC_Machine), [CYPRIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CYPRIS_%28microchip%29), [MYK-85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MYK-85), [Sierra (microchip)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_%28microchip%29), etc.
