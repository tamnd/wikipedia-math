# Rudolf Benesh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Benesh"}

**Rudolf Benesh** (16 January 1916 – 3 May 1975) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who created the *[Benesh Movement Notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benesh_Movement_Notation)* for [dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rudolf Benesh was son of a Czech father and an Anglo-Italian mother. He worked as a mathematician while his wife [Joan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Benesh) was a dancer in [Sadler's Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadler's_Wells) [ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet) in the late 1940s. She was having difficulty with her attempts to write and then decipher dance steps. He then devised a dance notation system while at work in the office. He wrote some lines to represent a movement of someone at a desk, then asked someone else to decipher them. The system evolved in the period 1947 to 1955. Dame [Ninette de Valois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninette_de_Valois) announced that the [Royal Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House) would be using the Benesh Movement Notation. In the following year, Rudolf and Joan wrote *An Introduction to Benesh Dance Notation*.[^o4]

In 1957 the first dance notated with the system was [Stravinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Stravinsky)'s *Petroushka*. Faith Worth was the first professional Benesh notator. In 1962 the Benesh Institute of Choreology was established. In 1968 some dances of the [Australian Aboriginal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Aborigine) dancers of Northern Territory were notated by a group of [anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology) students. Joan and Rudolf also wrote *Reading Dance: The Birth of Choreology*. The [Royal Academy of Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Dance), in conjunction with the [University of Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Surrey), produced software in the 1990s for inputting the notation and printing it out.
