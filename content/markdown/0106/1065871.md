# Superreal number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Superreal number"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **superreal numbers** are a class of extensions of the [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers), introduced by [H. Garth Dales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Garth_Dales) and [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071) as a generalization of the [hyperreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51429) and primarily of interest in [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis), [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), and the study of [Banach algebras](wikipedia://en/4665). The [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of superreals is itself a subfield of the [surreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51432).

:::

Dales and Woodin's superreals are distinct from the [super-real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super-real_number_%28David_O._Tall%29) of [David O. Tall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_O._Tall), which are [lexicographically ordered](wikipedia://en/567667) fractions of [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012) over the reals.[^o2]

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Suppose *X* is a [Tychonoff space](wikipedia://en/30554) and C(*X*) is the [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) of continuous real-valued functions on *X*. Suppose *P* is a [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) in C(*X*). Then the [factor algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factor_ring) *A* = C(*X*)/*P* is by definition an [integral domain](wikipedia://en/15462) that is a real algebra and that can be seen to be [totally ordered](wikipedia://en/30330). The [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) *F* of *A* is a **superreal field** if *F* strictly contains the real numbers $\mathbb{R}$, so that *F* is not order isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}$.

If the prime ideal *P* is a [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164), then *F* is a field of hyperreal numbers (Robinson's [hyperreals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperreals) being a very special case).

:::
