# Jan Śniadecki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Śniadecki"}

**Jan Śniadecki** (29 August 1756 – 9 November 1830) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) at the turn of the 18th and 19th centuries.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Born in [Żnin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Żnin), Śniadecki studied at [Kraków Jagellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) and in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He was rector of the [Imperial University of Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Vilnius), a member of the [Commission of National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_of_National_Education), and director of astronomical observatories at Kraków ([055](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAU_code#055)) and [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University_Astronomical_Observatory). He died at [Jašiūnai Manor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jašiūnai_Manor) near [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius).

Śniadecki published many works, including his observations on recently discovered [planetoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid). His *O rachunku losów* (On the Calculation of Chance, 1817) was a work in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934).

He was brother to [Jędrzej Śniadecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jędrzej_Śniadecki).

## Honours {#Honours}

The lunar crater *[Sniadecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sniadecki_%28crater%29)* and the main-belt asteroid [1262 Sniadeckia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1262_Sniadeckia) were named in his honour.

## Works {#Works}

- "Rachunku algebraicznego teoria" (1783)
- "Geografia, czyli opisanie matematyczne i fizyczne ziemi" (1804)
- "Rozprawa o Koperniku" (*Discourse on [Nicolaus Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus)*, biography, 1802)
- "O rachunku losów" (1817)
- "Trygonometria kulista analitycznie wyłożona" (1817)
- "O pismach klasycznych i romantycznych", Dziennik Wileński (1819)
- "Filozofia umysłu ludzkiego" (1821)
