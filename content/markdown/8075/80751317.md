# Aravind Srinivasan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aravind Srinivasan"}

**Aravind Srinivasan** (born 1968) is a Distinguished University Professor of computer science at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), with simultaneous appointments in the Department of Computer Science, UMIACS (University of Maryland Institute for Advanced Computer Studies), and AMSC. His research focuses on algorithms particularly randomized and probabilistic methods and their applications in fields ranging from machine learning, data science, health, and algorithmic fairness to networks, cloud computing, and sustainable systems.

:::

## Education and academic career {#Education_and_academic_career}

Srinivasan earned his B.Tech. from [Indian Institute of Technology Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology_Madras) (1989) and his M.S. and Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) (1993). He conducted postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (Princeton) and [DIMACS](wikipedia://en/3684858) at Rutgers, worked at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) and in industrial research at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs), and joined the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) faculty in the early 2000s.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

His research spans machine learning, [randomized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randomized) and [probabilistic algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_algorithms),[^o6] combinatorial and continuous optimization, and applications in [computational epidemiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_epidemiology),[^o7] [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics), [Internet economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_economy), social networks, energy systems, and fairness in AI.[^o8]

He has held editorial leadership roles, serving as [Editor‑in‑Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of *[ACM Transactions on Algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Transactions_on_Algorithms)* (2014–2020),[^o9] Managing Editor/Editor of *[Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Computing)* (2006–2019),[^o10] Editor of *Journal of Discrete Algorithms ([Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier))* (2004–2012) and as an Editor for the *Journal of the IISc*[^o11]
