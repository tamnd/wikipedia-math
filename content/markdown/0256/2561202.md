# Grundzüge der Mengenlehre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grundzüge der Mengenlehre"}

**Grundzüge der Mengenlehre** (English: *Basics of Set Theory*) is a book on [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) written by [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989).

:::

First published in April 1914, Grundzüge der Mengenlehre was the first comprehensive introduction to set theory. In addition to the systematic treatment of known results in set theory, the book also contains chapters on [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), which were then still considered parts of set theory. Hausdorff presented and developed original material that later became the basis for those areas.

In 1927, Hausdorff published an extensively revised second edition under the title *Mengenlehre* (English: *Set Theory*), omitting many topics from the first edition. In 1935, a third German edition was released, which in 1957 was translated into English by John R. Aumann [et al.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Et_al.) under the title *Set Theory*.
