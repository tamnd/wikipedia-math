# Volodymyr Korolyuk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volodymyr Korolyuk"}

*Image: Володимир Королюк 11-2019.jpg.* Korolyuk in 2019

**Volodymyr Semenovych Korolyuk** (Володимир Семенович Королюк, 19 August 1925 – 4 April 2020) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and Ukrainian mathematician who made significant contributions to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and its applications, academician of the [National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Ukraine) (1976).

:::

Korolyuk was born in [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv) in August 1925. Between 1949 and 2005 Volodymyr Korolyuk published over 300 papers and 22 monographs. He died in Kyiv in April 2020 at the age of 94.[^o2]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Volodymyr Korolyuk has been awarded a number of scientific prizes.

- Krylov Prize of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, 1976
- State Prize of the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, 1978
- Glushkov Prize of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, 1988
- Bogolyubov Prize of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, 1995
- Ostrogradsky Medal, 2002
- State Prize of Ukraine, 2003
