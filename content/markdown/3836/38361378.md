# Palash Sarkar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Palash Sarkar"}

**Palash Sarkar** (born 28 September 1969) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor at the [Applied Statistics Unit](wikipedia://en/2238418#Applied_Statistics_Unit) at the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418). His main research interest is [Cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology).

:::

He was awarded in 2011 [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), in the mathematical sciences category.[^o2]
