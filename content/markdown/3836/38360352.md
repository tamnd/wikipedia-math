# Truncated order-5 pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-5 pentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-5 pentagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{5,5}, constructed from one pentagons and two decagons around every vertex.
