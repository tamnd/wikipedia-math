# Izabella Łaba

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Izabella Łaba"}

**Izabella Łaba** (born 1966)[^o3] is a Polish-Canadian mathematician, a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia). Her main research specialties are [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and [additive combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243392).

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Łaba earned a master's degree in 1986 from the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław).[^o9] She received her PhD from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1994, under the supervision of [Israel Michael Sigal](wikipedia://en/33973309),[^o9] after which she was a postdoctoral scholar at [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) and then an assistant professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) before moving to UBC in 2000.[^o9]

She is one of three founding editors of the *Online Journal of Analytic Combinatorics*.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Łaba's thesis research proved the asymptotic completeness of many [n-body systems](wikipedia://en/23859945) in the presence of a constant magnetic field.[^o9][^o10] While at UCLA, with [Nets Katz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nets_Katz) and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498), she made important contributions to the theory of [Kakeya sets](wikipedia://en/588260), including the best known [lower bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_bound) on these sets in three-dimensional Euclidean spaces.[^o9][^o10] Her more recent work concerns [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [periodic tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periodic_tiling), and [Falconer's conjecture](wikipedia://en/38368203) on sets of distances of points.[^o10]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Łaba was the 2004 winner of the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444), an annual prize of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) for outstanding young mathematicians.[^o9] In 2008, the CMS honoured her again with their [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829), given to an outstanding woman in mathematics.[^o10]

In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o11], and in 2016 she was the Etta Z. Falconer lecturer for the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o12]
