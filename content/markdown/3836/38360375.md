# Snub pentapentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub pentapentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub pentapentagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{5,5}, constructed from two regular pentagons and three equilateral triangles around every vertex.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A double [symmetry coloring](wikipedia://en/18624814) can be constructed from \[5,4\] symmetry with only one color pentagon. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) s{5,4}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) .
