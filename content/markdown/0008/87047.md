# Cryptology ePrint Archive

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptology ePrint Archive"}

The **Cryptology ePrint Archive** is an electronic archive ([eprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eprint)) of new results in the field of [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), maintained by the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043). It contains articles covering many of the most recent advances in cryptography, that did not necessarily undergo any [refereeing process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review).[^o2]

:::
