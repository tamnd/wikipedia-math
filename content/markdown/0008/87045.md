# Journal of Cryptology

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Cryptology"}

The ***Journal of Cryptology*** (0933-2790) is a [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) in the field of [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology) and [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). The journal is published quarterly by the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043). Its editor-in-chief is [Vincent Rijmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Rijmen).[^o3]
