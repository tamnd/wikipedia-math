# Thompson subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thompson subgroup"}

"Thompson subgroup" can also mean an analogue of the Weyl group used in the [classical involution theorem](wikipedia://en/29949380) In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Thompson subgroup** $J(P)$ of a [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [*p*-group](wikipedia://en/24664) *P* refers to one of several [characteristic subgroups](wikipedia://en/7198) of *P*. (txt) originally defined $J(P)$ to be the subgroup generated by the [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) of *P* of maximal [rank](wikipedia://en/18720088). More often the Thompson subgroup $J(P)$ is defined to be the subgroup generated by the abelian subgroups of *P* of maximal [order](wikipedia://en/493527) or the subgroup generated by the [elementary abelian](wikipedia://en/4843202) subgroups of *P* of maximal rank. In general these three subgroups can be different, though they are all called the Thompson subgroup and denoted by $J(P)$.

:::
