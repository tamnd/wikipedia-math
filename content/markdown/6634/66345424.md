# Lynn Schreyer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lynn Schreyer"}

**Lynn G. Schreyer** (formerly **Lynn Laverne Schreyer Bennethum**) is an American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) whose research concerns the [mathematical modeling](wikipedia://en/20590) of [porous media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous_media). She is associate professor of mathematics and statistics at [Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_University), and full professor of the department of mathematics and statistics at Washington State University in March 2021.[^o3][^o4] Schreyer server as chair of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Geosciences from 2004 to 2006.[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Schreyer studied [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) at the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico), graduating *[magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors)* in 1986. After earning a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in mechanical engineering at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 1988, she switched to mathematics, earning a second master's degree in 1990 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1994, both from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o7] Her dissertation, *Multiscale Hybrid Mixture Theory for Swelling Systems with Interfaces*, was jointly supervised by Jim Douglas Jr. and John H. Cushman.

After postdoctoral research in [agronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agronomy) at Purdue, Schreyer became an assistant professor of mathematics at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver), earning tenure there as an associate professor in 2002. She moved to her present position at Washington State University in 2016.[^o7]

Schreyer chaired the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) Activity Group on Geosciences (SIAG-GS) from 2004 to 2006.[^o5]
