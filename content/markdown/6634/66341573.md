# Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics"}

The **Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics (KdVI)** is the institute for mathematical research at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). The KdVI is located in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam) at the [Amsterdam Science Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Science_Park).[^o4]

:::

[Robbert Dijkgraaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbert_Dijkgraaf), [Alexander Schrijver](wikipedia://en/35280549), [Nicolai Reshetikhin](wikipedia://en/7487626), [Jacob Korevaar](wikipedia://en/39389708), [Miranda Cheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Cheng), [Harry Buhrman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Buhrman) and Jan van de Craats are notable researchers connected to the institute. The KdVI is an institutional member of the [Royal Dutch Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/43504918)[^o5] and the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470). [^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Among the core research directions of the KdVI are:[^o8][^o4]

- [Algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), prof.dr. Lenny Taelman
- [Representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), prof.dr. Jasper Stokman and prof.dr. [Eric Opdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Opdam)
- [Theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), prof.dr. Sergey Shadrin
- [Discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), prof.dr. [Jo Ellis-Monaghan](wikipedia://en/56013267)[^o9]
- [Quantum Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Information), dr. Jeroen Zuiddam
- [Pure analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28math%29) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), prof.dr. Han Peters
- [Numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and applied analysis, prof.dr. Rob Stevenson
- [Mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), prof.dr. Joris Mooij
- [Probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), prof.dr. [Michel Mandjes](wikipedia://en/19720235)
- [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research), prof.dr. Sindo Nunez Queija
- [History of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), dr. Gerard Alberts
- [Didactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didactic_method), dr. André Heck

The institute is involved in several interdisciplinary research collaborations, including The Amsterdam String Theory Group,[^o10] the NETWORKS programme[^o11] and the QuSoft research center for quantum software[^o12]

## Education {#Education}

Besides its research activities, the KdVI runs the education programmes in mathematics at the University of Amsterdam, namely the bachelor programme *Mathematics*[^o14] and the master programmes *Mathematics*[^o15] and *Stochastics and Financial Mathematics*,[^o16] and jointly organises interdisciplinary double bachelor programmes *Mathematics and Physics* and *Mathematics and Computer Science*.[^o14]

## Name {#Name}

The institute is named after the mathematicians [Diederik Johannes Korteweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diederik_Johannes_Korteweg) and [Gustav de Vries](wikipedia://en/2377117). Korteweg was professor of mathematics at the University of Amsterdam from 1881 to 1918, and De Vries was Korteweg's student. Together they worked on the [Korteweg–de Vries equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korteweg–de_Vries_equation).[^o18]
