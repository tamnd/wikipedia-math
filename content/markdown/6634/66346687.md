# Alexander–Hirschowitz theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander–Hirschowitz theorem"}

The **Alexander–Hirschowitz theorem** shows that a specific collection of $k$ double points in the projective space $P^{r}$ will impose independent types of conditions on [homogenous polynomials](wikipedia://en/1698977) and the [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) of dimension $d$ with many known lists of exceptions.[^o2] In which case, the classic [polynomial interpolation](wikipedia://en/203565) that is located in several variables can be generalized to points that have larger [multiplicities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplicities).

:::
