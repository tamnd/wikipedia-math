# Robin Thomas (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Robin Thomas"}

**Robin Thomas** (August 22, 1962 – March 26, 2020) was a mathematician working in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

Thomas received his doctorate in 1985 from [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) (now the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic)), under the supervision of [Jaroslav Nešetřil](wikipedia://en/6948446).[^o4] He joined the faculty at Georgia Tech in 1989, and became a Regents' Professor there,[^o5][^o6] briefly serving as the department chair.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Thomas was married to Icelandic [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476) [Sigrún Andradóttir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigrún_Andradóttir), also a professor at Georgia Tech.[^o8]

On March 26, 2020, he died of [amyotrophic lateral sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amyotrophic_lateral_sclerosis) at the age of 57 after 12 years of struggle with the illness.[^o9][^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Thomas was awarded the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) for outstanding papers in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) twice:[^o12] in 1994 as co-author of a paper on the [Hadwiger conjecture](wikipedia://en/1657860),[^o13] and in 2009 for the proof of the [strong perfect graph theorem](wikipedia://en/744171).[^o14] In 2011, he was awarded the [Karel Janeček](wikipedia://en/44105073) Foundation Neuron Prize for Lifetime Achievement in Mathematics.[^o15] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o16] He was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in 2018.[^o17] The January 2023 issue of the [Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory,_Series_B) was a tribute to his work.[^o18]
