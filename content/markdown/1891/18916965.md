# Duistermaat–Heckman formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Duistermaat–Heckman formula"}

In mathematics, the **Duistermaat–Heckman formula**, due to (txt), states that the pushforward of the canonical ([Liouville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liouville_measure)) measure on a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) under the [moment map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) is a piecewise polynomial measure. Equivalently, the [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) of the canonical measure is given *exactly* by the [stationary phase approximation](wikipedia://en/1459010).

:::

(Berline Vergne 1982) and, independently, (Atiyah Bott 1984) showed how to deduce the Duistermaat–Heckman formula from a [localization theorem](wikipedia://en/44300155) for [equivariant cohomology](wikipedia://en/2976342).
