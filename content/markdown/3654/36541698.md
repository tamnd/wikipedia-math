# Henry Roy Brahana

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Roy Brahana"}

**Henry Roy Brahana** (16 August 1895 in [Lowell, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell,_Vermont) – 15 October 1972 in [Dennis, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis,_Massachusetts)) was a mathematician, specializing in [metabelian groups](wikipedia://en/1959273) and related geometric structures.

:::

H. Roy Brahana received his PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1920 under the direction of [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen). In the autumn of 1920,[^o2] he joined the mathematics department of the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) and remained there until his retirement in 1963. Brahana was the editor for the publication by the [University of Illinois Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Press) of the collected works of [George Abram Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Abram_Miller) in 5 volumes, coming out in the years 1935, 1939, 1946, 1955, and 1959. The H. Roy Brahana Prize for undergraduates at U. of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign was established in his honor.[^o3] His doctoral students include [Robert M. Thrall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Thrall).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- "Systems of circuits on two-dimensional manifolds." Annals of Mathematics (1921): 144–168.
- "Regular maps on an anchor ring." American Journal of Mathematics 48, no. 4 (1926): 225–240.
- "Regular maps and their groups." American Journal of Mathematics 49, no. 2 (1927): 268–284.
