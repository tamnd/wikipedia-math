# The Math Book

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Math Book"}

***The Math Book*** (Sterling Publishing, 2009. ) is a book by American author [Clifford A. Pickover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_A._Pickover).

:::

## Summary {#Summary}

The book contains 250 one-page articles in historical order on milestones in the history of math. Each article is followed by a related full-page color image. It also contains a bibliography for further reading.

## Reception {#Reception}

The book has consistently received good reviews[^o5][^o6] although one review mentions a lack of coverage of Chinese accomplishments[^o7] and another says "Alas, not all of the great mathematical milestones are included in this book ... Conversely, not all the chosen entries can be considered milestones in mathematics".[^o8]

The book has been praised by [Martin Gardner](wikipedia://en/20435).[^o9]

The book is the winner of the [Neumann Prize](wikipedia://en/7795793#Neumann_Prize).[^o10]

The book has been praised by [Boing Boing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boing_Boing).[^o11]
