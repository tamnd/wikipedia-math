# Alp Eden

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alp Eden"}

**Osman Alp Eden** (born 1958) is a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientist) and professor of mathematics. He is a retired member of the [Boğaziçi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boğaziçi_University) Mathematics Department in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul), Turkey.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Alp Eden was born in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul) in 1958. He finished the high school [Robert College of Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_College_of_Istanbul) in 1976. He graduated from Boğaziçi University Civil Engineering and Mathematics departments in 1981.[^o3] He received his PhD in Mathematics under the supervision of [Ciprian Ilie Foiaș](wikipedia://en/28465086) at the [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in the United States in 1989.[^o4]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

He worked in [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University) in 1989–1992. Then he became a full time member of the Department of Mathematics, at Boğaziçi University, Istanbul.[^o6] In years he served as the chair of the department and the vice-dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences. He retired in 2015 and moved to [İzmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/İzmir). He is one of the founders of the Masters program in Financial Engineering at Boğaziçi University. Between 2006-2016 he was a member of the Steering Committee of the [Istanbul Center for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/39297167) (IMBM).[^o7] He served as the Chief Editor of the [Turkish Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Journal_of_Mathematics). After retirement he served as the Editor of the popular mathematics journal Matematik Dünyası published by the [Turkish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/56457493) (TMD).

He has been an active researcher, having published more than 50 scientific manuscripts with more than 600 citations. In 1995 he was awarded the Science Award for Young Scientists given by the [Scientific and Technological Research Council of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_and_Technological_Research_Council_of_Turkey) (TUBITAK).[^o8] He was awarded the Boğaziçi University Award for Excellence in Research in 1998 (for young scientists) and again in 2009.[^o9] A one-day PDE workshop was held in his honor in 2015 by TMD.[^o10] There is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/60044557) in the field of dynamical systems named after Alp Eden.

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

His research interests include non-linear PDEs, dynamical systems, finance mathematics and mathematical modelling.
