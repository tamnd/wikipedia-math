# Giuseppe Maria Figatelli

*Image: Figatelli, Giuseppe Maria – Trattato aritmetico, 1692 – BEIC 1501158.jpg.* *Trattato aritmetico*, 1692

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giuseppe Maria Figatelli"}

**Giuseppe Maria Figatelli** or **Giuseppe Maria da Cento** ([Casumaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cento), 11 March 1611 – [Mirandola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirandola), 20 November 1682) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [Capuchin friar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin).[^o3]

:::

Not to be confused with the painter [Giuseppe Maria Ficatelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Maria_Ficatelli) from Cento (1639 – 1703).
