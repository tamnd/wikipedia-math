# David Savitt (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Savitt"}

**David Savitt** is a Canadian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in algebraic number theory and arithmetic aspects of the [Langlands Program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

Savitt received the [Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Early_Career_Award_for_Scientists_and_Engineers) in 2014,[^o3][^o4] and was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2017.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Savitt earned his Bachelor's degree in 1996 from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia). After completing his doctorate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2001 under the supervision of [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931), he held postdoctoral fellowships at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) and [IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) From 2005 to 2015 he served on the faculty of the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona), after which he moved to [Johns Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), where he has been department chair since 2018.[^o7][^o8]

With [Toby Gee](wikipedia://en/58462234) and Tong Liu, he proved the weight part of [Serre's conjecture](wikipedia://en/2908546) for [Hilbert modular forms](wikipedia://en/2333880),[^o9] and with [Matthew Emerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Emerton) and [Toby Gee](wikipedia://en/58462234) he proved [Breuil](wikipedia://en/20281471)'s conjecture on local-global compatibility for lattices in the cohomology of Shimura curves.[^o10]

## Outreach {#Outreach}

Savitt is a director of [Canada/USA Mathcamp](wikipedia://en/1786983),[^o12] an academic summer program for middle and high school students in mathematics, where he was also an instructor for many years.[^o13]
