# Advances in Operator Theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Advances in Operator Theory"}

***Advances in Operator Theory*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) established in 2016 by [Mohammad Sal Moslehian](wikipedia://en/69796226) and published by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser) on behalf of the [Tusi Mathematical Research Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tusi_Mathematical_Research_Group). It covers [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and related topics.

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus),[^o4] [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683),[^o5] [Emerging Sources Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_Sources_Citation_Index), and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*.[^o6]
