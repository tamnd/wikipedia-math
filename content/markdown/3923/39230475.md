# Configurational mechanics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Configurational mechanics"}

**Configurational mechanics** is a subdiscipline of [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918) in which particular emphasis is placed on reckoning from the perspective of the material [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470). By contrast, in [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586), reckoning is commonly made from the perspective of [spatial coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_coordinates).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Configurational mechanics has been applied to the analysis of [crack growth](wikipedia://en/843211). In these applications, growth of the crack corresponds to a material [displacement](wikipedia://en/859275), and the configurational force causing crack growth emerges as the [Energy release rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_release_rate). [^o4]
