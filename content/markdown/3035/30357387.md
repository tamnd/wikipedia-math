# William Beckner (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Beckner"}

**William Beckner** (born September 15, 1941) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), especially geometric inequalities. He is the Paul V. Montgomery Centennial Memorial Professor in Mathematics at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Beckner earned his Bachelor of Science in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) in [Columbia, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_Missouri) in 1963, where he became a member of [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa) society. He later earned his Ph.D. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), where his doctoral adviser was [Elias Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein). He also completed some postgraduate work in mathematics under adviser [A.P. Calderon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._P._Calderón) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).
