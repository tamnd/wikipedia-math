# Denis-Charles Cisinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denis-Charles Cisinski"}

**Denis-Charles Cisinski** (born March 10, 1976) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) focussing on [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013), [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2][^o3] In 2001, [Cisinski model structures](wikipedia://en/79817570) on [topoi](wikipedia://en/21391464) were introduced and later named after him. Since 2016, Denis-Charles Cisinski works at the [Universität Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Regensburg).[^o2]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Denis-Charles Cisinski obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 2002 at the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) with a thesis supervised by Georges Maltsiniotis and titled *Les préfaisceaux comme modèles des types d'homotopie* ([Presheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaves) as models for [homotopy types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_type)).[^o5] It was expanded and released as a book in 2006, further developing the theory from *[Pursuing Stacks](wikipedia://en/40783144)* by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042). In 2015, Denis-Charles Cisinski gave a talk at the [Séminaire Nicolas Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/2904840) summarizing the current state of research titled *Catégories supérieures et théorie des topos* (Higher categories and theory of toposes).[^o6] *[Higher Categories and Homotopical Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Categories_and_Homotopical_Algebra)*, a mathematical textbook about higher category theory by Denis-Charles Cisinski, was published in 2019.
