# Baire measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baire measure"}

In mathematics, a **Baire measure** is a [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) on the [σ-algebra](wikipedia://en/29586) of [Baire sets](wikipedia://en/3976202) of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) whose value on every compact Baire set is finite. In compact [metric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_spaces) the [Borel sets](wikipedia://en/43327) and the [Baire sets](wikipedia://en/3976202) are the same, so Baire measures are the same as [Borel measures](wikipedia://en/4322) that are finite on [compact sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_set). In general Baire sets and Borel sets need not be the same. In spaces with non-Baire Borel sets, Baire measures are used because they connect to the properties of [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) more directly.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Variations"}

## Variations {#Variations}

There are several inequivalent definitions of [Baire sets](wikipedia://en/3976202), so correspondingly there are several inequivalent concepts of Baire measure on a topological space. These all coincide on spaces that are locally compact [σ-compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-compact) [Hausdorff spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausdorff_spaces).

:::

## Relation to Borel measure {#Relation_to_Borel_measure}

In practice Baire measures can be replaced by [regular Borel measures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_Borel_measure). The relation between Baire measures and regular Borel measures is as follows:

- The restriction of a finite Borel measure to the Baire sets is a Baire measure.
- A finite Baire measure on a compact space is always regular.
- A finite Baire measure on a compact space is the restriction of a unique regular Borel measure.
- On compact (or σ-compact) metric spaces, Borel sets are the same as Baire sets and Borel measures are the same as Baire measures.

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Counting measure](wikipedia://en/191786) on the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324) is a measure on the Baire sets that is not regular (or σ-finite).
- The (left or right) [Haar measure](wikipedia://en/40645) on a [locally compact group](wikipedia://en/1651204) is a Baire measure invariant under the left (right) action of the group on itself. In particular, if the group is an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974), the left and right Haar measures coincide and we say the Haar measure is [translation invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_invariant). See also [Pontryagin duality](wikipedia://en/366136).

:::
