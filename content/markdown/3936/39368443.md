# AKNS system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AKNS system"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **AKNS system** is an [integrable system](wikipedia://en/2573213) of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), introduced by and named after [Mark J. Ablowitz](wikipedia://en/18578839), David J. Kaup, [Alan C. Newell](wikipedia://en/38482776), and Harvey Segur from their publication in [Studies in Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studies_in_Applied_Mathematics): (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The AKNS system is a pair of two partial [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) for two [complex-valued functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex-valued_function) *p* and *q* of 2 variables *t* and *x*:

$$p_t=+ip^2q-\frac{i}{2}p_{xx}$$

$$q_t=-iq^2p+\frac{i}{2}q_{xx}$$

If *p* and *q* are [complex conjugates](wikipedia://en/173918) this reduces to the [nonlinear Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/1422748).

[Huygens' principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huygens'_principle) applied to the [Dirac operator](wikipedia://en/1079448) gives rise to the AKNS hierarchy.[^o5]

:::

## Applications to General Relativity {#Applications_to_General_Relativity}

In October of 2021, the dynamics of [three-dimensional (extremal) black holes on General Relativity with negative cosmological constant](wikipedia://en/30557864) were shown equivalent to two independent copies of the AKNS system.[^o7] This duality was addressed through the imposition of suitable boundary conditions to the [Chern-Simons action](wikipedia://en/391908). In this scheme, the involution of conserved charges of the AKNS system yields an infinite-dimensional commuting asymptotic symmetry algebra of gravitational charges.
