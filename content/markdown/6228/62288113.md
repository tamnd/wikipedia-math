# Kurt Johansson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kurt Johansson"}

**Kurt Johansson** (born 1960) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) mathematician, specializing in probability theory.

:::

Johansson received his PhD in 1988 from [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) under the supervision of [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541) and is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in mathematics at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTH_Royal_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3]

In 2000 Johansson was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in Probability theory. In 2002 he was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing[^o4] and was awarded the [Göran Gustafsson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göran_Gustafsson_Prize). In 2006 he was elected a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences). In 2012 he was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
