# François Ledrappier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Ledrappier"}

*Image: Ledrappier.jpg.* François Ledrappier, Oberwolfach 1976

**François Ledrappier** (born 17 January 1946) is a French mathematician.

:::

Ledrappier graduated from the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 1967 and received his doctorate from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris 6) in 1975 under the supervision of [Jacques Neveu](wikipedia://en/56512426). Ledrappier taught at Paris 6 and then became a professor at [South Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend)'s [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), where he is now professor emeritus.

His research deals with asymptotic properties of [group actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) and related problems. He has published articles on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) theory, compact negatively curved manifolds and their abelian covers, linear actions and random walks on [linear groups](wikipedia://en/3983656), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and zero entropy algebraic actions of [free abelian groups](wikipedia://en/247296).[^o3]

In 1994 Ledrappier was an invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Zurich.[^o4] In 2016 he received the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384).[^o5]

His doctoral students include [Nalini Anantharaman](wikipedia://en/41980659).[^o2]
