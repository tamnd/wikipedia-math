# Lamination (topology)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lamination"}

*Image: Lavaurs-12.png.* Lamination associated with Mandelbrot set

*Image: Rabbit-lamination.png.* Lamination of [rabbit Julia set](wikipedia://en/17739976)

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of mathematics, a **lamination** is a :

:::

- "[topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) [partitioned](wikipedia://en/340240) into subsets"
- decoration (a structure or property at a point) of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) in which some subset of the manifold is partitioned into sheets of some lower dimension, and the sheets are locally [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497).

A lamination of a surface is a [partition](wikipedia://en/340240) of a closed subset of the surface into smooth curves.

It may or may not be possible to fill the gaps in a lamination to make a [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227).[^o3]

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

*Image: Geodesic Lamination.jpg.* Geodesic lamination of a closed surface

- A **[geodesic](wikipedia://en/91096) lamination** of a 2-dimensional [hyperbolic manifold](wikipedia://en/1589135) is a closed subset together with a foliation of this closed subset by geodesics.[^o6] These are used in [Thurston's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston's_classification) of elements of the [mapping class group](wikipedia://en/793295) and in his theory of [earthquake maps](wikipedia://en/31112690).
- Quadratic laminations, which remain invariant under the angle [doubling map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubling_map).[^o7] These laminations are associated with [quadratic maps](wikipedia://en/11735693#Map).[^o8][^o9] It is a closed collection of chords in the unit disc.[^o10] It is also a topological model of [Mandelbrot](wikipedia://en/19562) or [Julia set](wikipedia://en/56434).

:::
