# BestCrypt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="BestCrypt"}

**BestCrypt**, developed by [Jetico](https://www.jetico.com/), is a commercial [disk encryption app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_encryption_software) available for [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows), [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux), [macOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacOS) and [Android](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_%28operating_system%29).[^o5]

:::

BestCrypt comes in two editions: BestCrypt Volume Encryption to encrypt entire [disk volumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_volume);[^o6] BestCrypt Container Encryption to encrypt [virtual disks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_image) stored as computer files.[^o7]

BestCrypt also provides the complimentary [data erasure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_erasure) utility [BCWipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCWipe).[^o8]

## Cryptographic Algorithms {#Cryptographic_Algorithms}

BestCrypt supports a wide variety of block cipher algorithms including [AES](wikipedia://en/1260), [Serpent](wikipedia://en/385154), [Blowfish](wikipedia://en/3940), [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356), [DES](wikipedia://en/7978), [Triple DES](wikipedia://en/48375), [GOST 28147-89](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GOST_28147-89). All ciphers support [CBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_cipher_modes_of_operation#Cipher-block_chaining_%28CBC%29) and [LRW](wikipedia://en/3395256#Liskov,_Rivest,_and_Wagner_%28LRW%29) modes of operation while AES, Twofish and Serpent also support [XTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XTS_mode) mode.

## Features {#Features}

- Create and mount a [virtual drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAM_drive) encrypted using [AES](wikipedia://en/1260), [Blowfish](wikipedia://en/3940), [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356), [CAST-128](wikipedia://en/240998)[^o11] and various other encryption methods. BestCrypt v.8 and higher can alternatively mount a subfolder on a [NTFS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NTFS) disk instead of a drive. Encrypted virtual disk images are compatible across Windows, Linux and Mac OS X.
- Encrypt a set of files into a single, self-extracting archive.
- Transparently encrypt entire partitions or volumes together with pre-boot authentication for encrypted boot partitions.
- [Two-factor authentication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-factor_authentication).
- Support for size-efficient Dynamic Containers with the Smart Free Space Monitoring technology.[^o12]
- Hardware accelerated encryption.
- Anti-keylogging facilities to protect container and volume passwords.
- [Data erasure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_erasure) utility [BCWipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCWipe) to erase unprotected copies of data to complement encryption.
- [Secret sharing](wikipedia://en/458253) and [Public Key](wikipedia://en/24222) authentication methods in addition to basic password-based authentication.
