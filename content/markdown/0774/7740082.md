# Parry–Sullivan invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parry–Sullivan invariant"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Parry–Sullivan invariant** (or **Parry–Sullivan number**) is a numerical quantity of interest in the study of [incidence matrices](wikipedia://en/420919) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and of certain one-dimensional [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). It provides a partial classification of non-trivial irreducible incidence matrices.

:::

It is named after the English mathematician [Bill Parry](wikipedia://en/7618438) and the American mathematician [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608), who introduced the invariant in a joint paper published in the journal [*Topology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology_%28journal%29) in 1975.[^o2][^o3]

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *A* be an *n* × *n* [incidence matrix](wikipedia://en/420919). Then the **Parry–Sullivan number** of *A* is defined to be

$$\mathrm{PS} (A) = \det (I - A),$$

where *I* denotes the *n* × *n* identity matrix.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

It can be shown that, for nontrivial irreducible incidence matrices, flow equivalence is completely determined by the Parry–Sullivan number and the [Bowen–Franks group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowen–Franks_group).

:::
