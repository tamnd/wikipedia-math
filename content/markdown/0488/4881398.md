# Ferdinand Joachimsthal

*Image: Ferdinand Joachimsthal.jpg.* Ferdinand Joachimsthal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinand Joachimsthal"}

**Ferdinand Joachimsthal** was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was born on March 9, 1818, at Goldberg ([Złotoryja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Złotoryja)), [Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silesia) and died on April 5, 1861, at [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau) (Wrocław). In the year of his graduation ([Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.), [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), 1842) he was appointed teacher at a *[Realschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realschule)* in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), and in 1846 was admitted to the philosophical faculty of the university as *[privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)*. In 1856, he was appointed [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle), and in 1858 at [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau).

Joachimsthal, who was [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish), contributed essays to *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)*, 1846, 1850, 1854, 1861, and to [Olry Terquem](wikipedia://en/161565)'s *[Nouvelles Annales de Mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nouvelles_Annales_de_Mathématiques)*.

He is known for *Joachimsthal's Equation*[^o2] and *Joachimsthal Notation*,[^o3] both associated with [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673).[^o4]

## Works {#Works}

- 1848: "Sur les normales infiniment voisines d'une surface courbe", [Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547) 13: 415–22
- 1863: *[Elemente der analytischen Geometrie der Ebene](https://archive.org/details/elementederanal00joacgoog/page/n10/mode/2up)* via [Internet Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Archive)
- 1872: *[Anwendung der Differential- und Integralrechnung](https://archive.org/details/anwendungderdif01joacgoog/page/n5/mode/2up)* via Internet Archive
