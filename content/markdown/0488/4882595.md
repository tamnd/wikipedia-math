# Minimax approximation algorithm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minimax approximation algorithm"}

A **minimax approximation algorithm** (or **L<sup>∞</sup> approximation** or **uniform approximation**) is a method to find an approximation of a [mathematical function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_function) that minimizes maximum error.[^o2][^o3]

:::

For example, given a function $f$ defined on the interval $[a,b]$ and a degree bound $n$, a minimax polynomial approximation algorithm will find a polynomial $p$ of degree at most $n$ to minimize

$\max_{a \leq x \leq b}|f(x)-p(x)|.$[^o4]

## Polynomial approximations {#Polynomial_approximations}

The [Weierstrass approximation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_approximation_theorem) states that every continuous function defined on a closed interval \[a,b\] can be uniformly approximated as closely as desired by a polynomial function.[^o3] For practical work it is often desirable to minimize the maximum absolute or relative error of a polynomial fit for any given number of terms in an effort to reduce computational expense of repeated evaluation.

Polynomial expansions such as the [Taylor series](wikipedia://en/30448) expansion are often convenient for theoretical work but less useful for practical applications. Truncated [Chebyshev series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev_series), however, closely approximate the minimax polynomial.

One popular minimax approximation algorithm is the [Remez algorithm](wikipedia://en/4882514).
