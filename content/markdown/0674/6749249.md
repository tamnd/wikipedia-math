# Gabriel Sudan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel Sudan"}

*Image: SudanGabriel Zurich1932.tif.* [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) 1932

**Gabriel Sudan** (April 14, 1899 – June 22, 1977) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for the [Sudan function](wikipedia://en/3925287),[^n1] an important example in the [theory of computation](wikipedia://en/30402), similar to the [Ackermann function](wikipedia://en/2925).[^n2]

:::

Born in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), Sudan received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1925 for his thesis *Über die geordneten Mengen* ("On the theory of ordered sets"), supervised by [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302). He taught at the [Polytechnic University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Bucharest) from 1941 until his retirement, in 1966.

Sudan constructed the function that bears his name with the same aim as [Wilhelm Ackermann](wikipedia://en/62727): to solve in the affirmative a problem raised by Hilbert. The Ackermann and Sudan functions are chronologically the first examples of [recursive functions](wikipedia://en/4044867) which are not [primitive recursive](wikipedia://en/24829).
