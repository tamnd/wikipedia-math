# Steven Zucker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steven Zucker"}

**Steven Mark Zucker** (12 September 1949 – 13 September 2019) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced the [Zucker conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zucker_conjecture), proved in different ways by [Eduard Looijenga](wikipedia://en/45224498) (1988) and by Leslie Saper and [Mark Stern](wikipedia://en/50511729) (1990).

:::

Zucker completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1974 at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Spencer Bloch](wikipedia://en/25794633). His work with [David A. Cox](wikipedia://en/41248158) led to the creation of the [Cox–Zucker machine](wikipedia://en/16273876), an algorithm for determining if a given set of sections provides a basis (up to torsion) for the [Mordell–Weil group](wikipedia://en/23087727) of an elliptic surface $E \to S$, where $S$ is isomorphic to the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578).

He was part of the mathematics faculty at the [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University). In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
