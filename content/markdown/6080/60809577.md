# Fabrizia Mealli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fabrizia Mealli"}

**Fabrizia Mealli** (born 22 July 1966) is an Italian statistician at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence), known for her research on [causal inference](wikipedia://en/37103476), [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676), and the statistics of [employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment). In 2013 she was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).

:::

Mealli earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in economics from the University of Florence in 1990, and completed her Ph.D. in statistics at the same university in 1994. After postdoctoral research at the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester) she returned to Florence as a faculty member in statistics.
