# Williamson conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Williamson conjecture"}

In [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/5170) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [combinatorial design theory](wikipedia://en/2761968) and [combinatorial matrix theory](wikipedia://en/59047031), the **Williamson conjecture** is that Williamson matrices of order $n$ exist for all positive [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) $n$. Four [symmetric](wikipedia://en/126474) and [circulant](wikipedia://en/889936) [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are called *Williamson matrices* if their entries are $\pm1$ and they satisfy the relationship

:::

$$A^2 + B^2 + C^2 + D^2 = 4n\, I$$

where $I$ is the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718) of order $n$. [John Williamson](wikipedia://en/36284380) showed that if $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are Williamson matrices then

$$\begin{bmatrix} A & B & C & D \\ -B & A & -D & C \\ -C & D & A & -B \\ -D & -C & B & A \end{bmatrix}$$

is an [Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/507553) of order $4n$.[^o2] It was once considered likely that Williamson matrices exist for all orders $n$ and that the structure of Williamson matrices could provide a route to proving the [Hadamard conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard_conjecture) that Hadamard matrices exist for all orders $4n$.[^o3] However, in 1993 the Williamson conjecture was shown to be false by Dragomir Ž. Ðoković through an exhaustive computer search, which demonstrated that Williamson matrices do not exist of order $n=35$.[^o4] In 2008, the [counterexamples](wikipedia://en/143151) 47, 53, and 59 were additionally discovered.[^o5]

Following the negative result of Ðoković, which ruled out the existence of Williamson matrices of order $n=35$, it was shown in 2019 that relaxing the symmetry and circulant requirements nevertheless permits a Hadamard matrix of this block form to exist for that order.[^o6] One such instance is given by the sequences

```text
a = --+--+--+++-+----+-+++--+--+-------
b = +---+---+-++-+--+-++-+---+---++++++
c = -++---+-+--+--++--+++++-+-+++++-+--
d = +----+++-+-+--++--+-+-+++----++---+
```

with the associated matrices defined by

```text
A = circulant(a)
B = circulant(b)
C = fliplr(circulant(c))
D = circulant(d)
```
