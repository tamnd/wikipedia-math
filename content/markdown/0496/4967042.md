# CEP subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="CEP subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is said to have the **Congruence Extension Property** or to be a **CEP subgroup** if every [congruence](wikipedia://en/48167) on the subgroup lifts to a congruence of the whole group. Equivalently, every [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of the subgroup arises as the intersection with the subgroup of a normal subgroup of the whole group.

:::

In symbols, a subgroup $H$ is a CEP subgroup in a group $G$ if every normal subgroup $N$ of $H$ can be realized as $H \cap M$ where $M$ is normal in $G$.

The following facts are known about CEP subgroups:

- Every [retract](wikipedia://en/4967125) has the CEP.
- Every [transitively normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/4938714) has the CEP.
