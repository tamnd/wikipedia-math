# Baer norm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baer norm"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **norm** of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is the intersection of the [normalizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalizer) of all its [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011). This is also termed the **Baer norm**, after [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114).

:::

The following facts are true for the Baer norm:

- It is a [characteristic subgroup](wikipedia://en/7198).
- It contains the [center](wikipedia://en/7125) of the group.
- It is contained inside the second term of the [upper central series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_central_series).
- It is a [Dedekind group](wikipedia://en/153106), so is either abelian or has a direct factor isomorphic to the [quaternion group](wikipedia://en/153130).
- If it contains an element of infinite order, then it is equal to the center of the group.
