# FC-group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FC-group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), an **FC-group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) in which every [conjugacy class](wikipedia://en/49176) of elements has finite [cardinality](wikipedia://en/6174).

:::

The following are some facts about FC-groups:

- Every [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) is an FC-group.[^o2]
- Every [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) is an FC-group.[^o3]
- The following property is stronger than the property of being FC: every [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) has finite index in its [normal closure](wikipedia://en/153099).
