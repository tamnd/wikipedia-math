# Veniamin Myasnikov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Veniamin Myasnikov"}

**Veniamin Petrovich Myasnikov** (Вениамин Петрович Мясников; 4 December 1936 — 29 February 2004) was a Soviet [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [mechanician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanician) and a member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (1992).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Veniamin Myasnikov was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) ([USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR)) in 1936. He was educated in [MSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) ([Mechanics-Mathematics Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics)), which was completed in 1959.[^o4]

He established the Department of the Computational Mechanics in the [MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) and headed it in 1998-2000 yy.[^o5]

After of [Yevgenii Zolotov](wikipedia://en/42742165) recommendation Myasnikov lived to [Vladivostok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladivostok) and was elected as Director of [IACP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Automatics_and_Control_Processes) of the Far-Eastern Branch of the [Soviet Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Academy_of_Sciences) (1988-2004).[^o6]

In 1992 Myasnikov was elected a Member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7]

## Main research fields {#Main_research_fields}

- [Fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034)
- [Mathematical theory of plasticity](wikipedia://en/240123)
- [Geomechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomechanics)

## Family life {#Family_life}

Veniamin Myasnikov died of cancer in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) at 67 on the leap day of 2004 and buried at Vostryakovskoe cemetery.[^o10] He is survived by his wife Svetlana Grigorievna and two children, daughter Anna and son. His father Peter Veniaminovich Myasnikov and mother Varvara Akimovna Myasnikova were graduated in [MSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where the father further was a professor of the "[Analytical mechanics](wikipedia://en/454450)" Department.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Veniamin Myasnikov was appointed an [Order of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Honour_%28Russia%29) in 1997 for achievements in scientific research resulting in significant Russian scientific and technological advantage in mechanics and technology.

Honorary Professor of [MSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) (2000).
