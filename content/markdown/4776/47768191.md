# Heinz Bachmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz Bachmann"}

**Heinz Bachmann** (1924 – 22 April 2022)[^o3] was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked at the Eidgenössische Sternwarte (federal observatory) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich). He introduced the [Bachmann–Howard ordinal](wikipedia://en/12732453) and [ordinal collapsing functions](wikipedia://en/16968563), which are essential tools in studying [proof-theoretic ordinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_theoretic_ordinal).[^o4]

:::
