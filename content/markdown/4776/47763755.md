# Ilse Ipsen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ilse Ipsen"}

**Ilse Clara Franziska Ipsen** is a German-American mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University). She was formerly associate director of the [Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/41998171), a joint venture of North Carolina State and other nearby universities.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ipsen earned a diploma from the [Kaiserslautern University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaiserslautern_University_of_Technology) in 1977,[^o3] and completed her doctorate from the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1983 under the supervision of Don Heller. Her dissertation was *Systolic Arrays for VLSI* and concerned [Very Large Scale Integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_Large_Scale_Integration) hardware implementations of the [systolic array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systolic_array) [parallel computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) architecture.[^o3]

After working at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) for ten years beginning in 1983, she joined North Carolina State University in 1993.[^o3]

Ipsen is Founding Editor in Chief of the SIAM Book Series on Data Science.

## Book {#Book}

Ipsen is the author of the book *Numerical Matrix Analysis: Linear Systems and Least Squares* ([SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376), 2009), an introductory graduate textbook on the [sensitivity analysis](wikipedia://en/620083) of computations in [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422).[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2011, she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for contributions to numerical linear algebra, perturbation theory, and applications."[^o3][^o9] She was elected as a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 2018.[^o10]
