# Quillen's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quillen's lemma"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), **Quillen's lemma** states that an [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) of a [simple module](wikipedia://en/29430) over the [enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *k* is algebraic over *k*. In contrast to a version of [Schur's lemma](wikipedia://en/521696) due to Dixmier, it does not require *k* to be [uncountable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncountable). [Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519)'s original short [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) uses [generic flatness](wikipedia://en/26374518).

:::
