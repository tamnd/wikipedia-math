# Joint Mathematics Meetings

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joint Mathematics Meetings"}

The **Joint Mathematics Meetings** (**JMM**) is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) [conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) hosted annually in early January by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS). Frequently, several other national mathematics organizations also participate. From 1998 to 2020, the JMM was jointly organized and managed by the AMS and the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA).[^o2]

:::

The meeting is the largest gathering of mathematicians in the United States, and the largest annual meeting of mathematicians in the world.[^o3] For example, more than 6000 people attended the 2017 JMM.[^o4] Several thousand talks, panels, minicourses, and poster sessions are held each year.

The JMM also hosts an *Employment Center*, which is a focal point for the hiring process of academic [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), especially for [liberal arts colleges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_arts_college). Many employers conduct their preliminary interview process at the meeting.[^o5] Often these interviews take place outside the confines of the conference, so the employers may not appear on the official Employment Center listing.
