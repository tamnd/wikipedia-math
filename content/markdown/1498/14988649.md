# M. Ram Murty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. Ram Murty"}

**Maruti Ram Pedaprolu Murty**, [FRSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) (born 16 October 1953)[^o3] is an [Indo-Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Canadian) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), where he holds a Queen's Research Chair[^o4] in mathematics.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

M. Ram Murty is the brother of mathematician [V. Kumar Murty](wikipedia://en/34926069).[^o6]

Murty graduated with a B.Sc. from [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) in 1976.[^o7] He received his Ph.D. in 1980 from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), supervised by [Harold Stark](wikipedia://en/788807) and [Dorian Goldfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorian_Goldfeld). He was on the faculty of [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) from 1982 until 1996, when he joined [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston). Murty is also cross-appointed as a professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at Queen's, specialising in [Indian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_philosophy).[^o9]

## Research {#Research}

Specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), Murty is a researcher in the areas of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000), [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), and [sieve theory](wikipedia://en/1035915).

Murty has [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) 1 and frequently collaborates with his brother, V. Kumar Murty.

## Awards {#Awards}

Murty received the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) in 1988.[^o12] He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1990,[^o13] was elected to the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (INSA) in 2008,[^o14] and became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o15] In 2024, he won the [CRM-Fields-PIMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRM-Fields-PIMS_Prize).[^o16]
