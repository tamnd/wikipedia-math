# MathFest

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MathFest"}

*Image: MathFest Conference 2016 10.jpg.* Exhibit booth at the MathFest Conference 2016

**MathFest** is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) [conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_%28meeting%29) hosted annually in late summer by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). It is known for its dual focus on [teaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching) and [research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) in mathematics, as well as for student participation.

:::

## Events {#Events}

MathFest features many annual lectures, such as the [Earle Raymond Hedrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earle_Raymond_Hedrick) Lecture Series, which consists of up to three lectures by the same presenter, on three consecutive days, [^o11] and the [AWM-MAA Falconer Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM/MAA_Falconer_Lecturer), which is given by a distinguished female mathematician or mathematics educator.[^o12]
