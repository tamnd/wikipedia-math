# Project NExT

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Project NExT"}

**MAA Project NExT** (New Experiences in Teaching) is a program sponsored by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) to aid in the professional development of [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661), and [mathematics educators](wikipedia://en/326471) after they receive their [PhDs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD). It involves workshops and lectures on [teaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching), [academic research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_research), [academic scholarship](wikipedia://en/772062), and professional activities. The participants in the program are called **Project NExT Fellows** or sometimes **Dots**, and the program also provides ample [networking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_networking) opportunities for them. Each fellow is also provided with a consultant, who serves as a mentor for them.

:::

## History {#History}

Project NExT was founded by James (Jim) Leitzel ([Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University)) and [Chris Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._Christine_Stevens) ([Saint Louis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis_University_%28United_States%29)). The first fellows were selected in 1994. Jim Leitzel died in 1998, and [Aparna Higgins](wikipedia://en/56615271) ([University of Dayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dayton)) and Joe Gallian ([University of Minnesota Duluth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota_Duluth)) became co-directors of Project NExT. Chris Stevens stepped down as director in 2010, and was succeeded by Aparna Higgins and Joe Gallian. Judith Covington ([Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University), Shreveport) and Gavin LaRose ([University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan)) first served as Associate Co-Directors and later became Co-Directors. In 2007, the total number of fellows surpassed 1000.[^o3] By 2017 the total number of fellows reached 1700. In 2023 [Christine Kelley](wikipedia://en/77485815) became director.[^o4]

## Selection of fellows {#Selection_of_fellows}

The program is aimed at faculty who are in the early stages of their higher ed teaching career, in a mathematics (or closely related) department, after receiving their doctorate. Fellows are selected based on an application, including a short [curriculum vitae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum_vitae), a [research statement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_statement), and a [teaching statement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_statement) expressing interest in the program. The application also requires a letter from the applicant's department chair guaranteeing funding to attend several conferences. The number of selected fellows depends on funding. Currently, just under 100 are selected each year.[^o6]

## The program {#The_program}

Project NExT is a professional development program for college-level faculty interested in teaching. The program provides workshops and an electronic mailing list for its members.[^o8] Fellows participate in [MathFest](wikipedia://en/14984993) during the year of their selection and the year after, and in the [Joint Mathematics Meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Mathematics_Meeting) in the January after their selection as fellows. Each fellow is also assigned a consultant outside of their own institution. NExT fellows organize several sessions at the Joint Meeting and MathFest, on topics of their choosing.[^o9]

Since 2016, all MAA Project NExT events at the [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143) have been open to all conference attendees.

## Affiliation with other professional development organizations {#Affiliation_with_other_professional_development_organizations}

The national Project NExT program is strongly affiliated with *Section NExT* programs, which are run by local sections of the [MAA](wikipedia://en/198772), and involve many of the same activities. Section NExT fellows can also participate in the national workshops.[^o11][^o12]

Project NExT is also strongly associated with the [Young Mathematicians Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Mathematicians_Network).
