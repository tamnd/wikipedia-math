# Turán's inequalities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Turán's inequalities"}

In mathematics, **Turán's inequalities** are some inequalities for [Legendre polynomials](wikipedia://en/100349) found by Pál Turán[^o2] (and first published by (Szegö 1948)[^o4]). There are many generalizations to other polynomials, often called Turán's inequalities, given by (, 1–10)[^o6] and other authors.

:::

If $P_n$ is the $n$th [Legendre polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legendre_polynomial), Turán's inequalities state that

$$\,\! P_n(x)^2 > P_{n-1}(x)P_{n+1}(x)\ \text{for}\ -1<x<1.$$

For $H_n$, the $n$th [Hermite polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_polynomial), Turán's inequalities are

$$H_n(x)^2 - H_{n-1}(x)H_{n+1}(x)= (n-1)!\cdot \sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{2^{n-i}}{i!}H_i(x)^2>0 ,$$

whilst for [Chebyshev polynomials](wikipedia://en/184539) they are

$$T_n(x)^2 - T_{n-1}(x)T_{n+1}(x)= 1-x^2>0 \ \text{for}\ -1<x<1 .$$
