# Andrew M. Stuart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew M. Stuart"}

**Andrew M. Stuart** [^o3] is a British and American mathematician, working in applied and computational mathematics. In particular, his research has focused on the [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), applications of [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454) and [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239), the [Bayesian](wikipedia://en/404412) approach to [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956), [data assimilation](wikipedia://en/2458875), and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Andrew Stuart graduated in mathematics from [Bristol University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_University) in 1983, and then obtained his DPhil from the [Oxford University Computing Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Computing_Laboratory) in 1986.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

After [postdoctoral research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research) in applied mathematics at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), Stuart held permanent positions at the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) (1989–1992), in mathematics, at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (1991–1999), in engineering, and at [Warwick University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick_University) (1999–2016), in mathematics. He is currently Bren Professor of Computing and Mathematical Sciences at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Stuart has been honored with several awards, including the 1989 [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214), the [Monroe H. Martin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroe_Martin_Prize) from the Institute for Physical Science and Technology at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), the SIAM [James Wilkinson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Wilkinson_Prize_in_Numerical_Analysis_and_Scientific_Computing), the SIAM [Germund Dahlquist Prize](https://www.siam.org/prizes-recognition/major-prizes-lectures/detail/germund-dahlquist-prize) in 1997, the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) from the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2000, and the SIAM [J.D. Crawford Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Crawford_Prize) in 2007.[^o9] He was an invited speaker at the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227) (ICIAM) in Zurich in 2007[^o10] and Tokyo in 2023,[^o11] and at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Seoul, 2014.[^o12] In 2009 he was elected an inaugural fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM),[^o13] and in 2020 he was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o3] In 2022, he was named a [Vannevar Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vannevar_Bush) Faculty Fellow.[^o14]

## Publications {#Publications}

The majority of Stuart's published work is in academic journals. In addition to mathematics research published in journals, Stuart is also the author of several books in mathematics, including a research monograph concerning Dynamical Systems and Numerical Analysis,[^o16] a research text on Multiscale Methods,[^o17] a graduate text on Continuum Mechanics,[^o18] a text on Data Assimilation,[^o19] and a text on Inverse Problems.[^o20]

- Stuart, A., Humphries, A.R. (1998). *Dynamical Systems and Numerical Analysis.* Cambridge University Press.
- G. A. Pavliotis, Andrew Stuart (2008). *Multiscale Methods: Averaging and Homogenization.* Springer Science & Business Media.
- Gonzalez, O., & Stuart, A. (2008). *A First Course in Continuum Mechanics* (Cambridge Texts in Applied Mathematics). Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
- Kody Law, Andrew Stuart, Konstantinos Zygalakis (2015). *Data Assimilation: A Mathematical Introduction.* Springer.
- Sanz-Alonso, D., Stuart, A., & Taeb, A. (2023). *Inverse Problems and Data Assimilation* (London Mathematical Society Student Texts). Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
