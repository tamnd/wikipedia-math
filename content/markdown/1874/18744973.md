# Analysis on fractals

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Analysis on fractals"}

**Analysis on fractals** or **calculus on fractals** is a generalization of [calculus on smooth manifolds](wikipedia://en/2119219) to [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) on [fractals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractals).

:::

The theory describes dynamical phenomena which occur on objects modelled by fractals. It studies questions such as "how does heat diffuse in a fractal?" and "how does a fractal vibrate?"

In the smooth case the operator that occurs most often in the equations modelling these phenomena is the [Laplacian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplacian), so the starting point for the theory of analysis on fractals is to define a Laplacian on fractals. This turns out not to be a full [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886) in the usual sense but has many of the desired properties. There are a number of approaches to defining the Laplacian: probabilistic, analytical or measure theoretic.
