# Semialgebraic space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semialgebraic space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198), a **semialgebraic space** is a space which is locally [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/14828) to a [semialgebraic set](wikipedia://en/13329722).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *U* be an open subset of **R**<sup>*n*</sup> for some *n*. A **semialgebraic function** on *U* is defined to be a [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) [real](wikipedia://en/20646438)-valued function on *U* whose restriction to any [semialgebraic set](wikipedia://en/13329722) contained in *U* has a [graph](wikipedia://en/87352) which is a semialgebraic subset of the product space **R**<sup>*n*</sup>×**R**. This endows **R**<sup>*n*</sup> with a sheaf $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{R}^n}$ of semialgebraic functions.

(For example, any polynomial mapping between semialgebraic sets is a [semialgebraic function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semialgebraic_function), as is the maximum of two semialgebraic functions.)

A **semialgebraic space** is a [locally ringed space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_ringed_space) $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ which is locally isomorphic to **R**<sup>*n*</sup> with its sheaf of semialgebraic functions.[^o4]

:::
