# Varadhan's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Varadhan's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Varadhan's lemma** is a result from the [large deviations theory](wikipedia://en/3772909) named after [S. R. Srinivasa Varadhan](wikipedia://en/9638038). The result gives information on the [asymptotic distribution](wikipedia://en/641995) of a statistic *φ*(*Z*<sub>*ε*</sub>) of a family of [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685) *Z*<sub>*ε*</sub> as *ε* becomes small in terms of a [rate function](wikipedia://en/3772816) for the variables.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let *X* be a [regular topological space](wikipedia://en/53058); let (*Z*<sub>*ε*</sub>)<sub>*ε*>0</sub> be a family of random variables taking values in *X*; let *μ*<sub>*ε*</sub> be the law ([probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437)) of *Z*<sub>*ε*</sub>. Suppose that (*μ*<sub>*ε*</sub>)<sub>*ε*>0</sub> satisfies the [large deviation principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_deviation_principle) with good rate function *I* : *X* → \[0, +∞\]. Let *ϕ* : *X* → **R** be any [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122). Suppose that at least one of the following two conditions holds true: either the tail condition

$$\lim_{M \to \infty} \limsup_{\varepsilon \to 0} \big(\varepsilon \log \mathbf{E} \big[ \exp\big(\phi(Z_{\varepsilon}) / \varepsilon\big)\,\mathbf{1}\big(\phi(Z_{\varepsilon}) \geq M\big) \big]\big) = -\infty,$$

where **1**(*E*) denotes the [indicator function](wikipedia://en/240790) of the event *E*; or, for some *γ* > 1, the [moment](wikipedia://en/368684) condition

$$\limsup_{\varepsilon \to 0} \big(\varepsilon \log \mathbf{E} \big[ \exp\big(\gamma \phi(Z_{\varepsilon}) / \varepsilon\big) \big]\big) < \infty.$$

Then

$$\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0} \varepsilon \log \mathbf{E} \big[ \exp\big(\phi(Z_{\varepsilon}) / \varepsilon\big) \big] = \sup_{x \in X} \big( \phi(x) - I(x) \big).$$

:::
