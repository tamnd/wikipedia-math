# Yair Censor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yair Censor"}

**Yair Censor** (יאיר צנזור; born November 29, 1943) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor emeritus at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa), specializing in [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833) and [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29), as well as applications of these fields, in particular to [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging) and [radiation therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_therapy) treatment planning.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Yair Censor was born in [Rishon LeZion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishon_LeZion). After serving in the [IDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces), he studied at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_-_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in Haifa, where he earned his [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc.) in 1975 under the supervision of Professor Adi Ben-Israel.

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Censor joined the Department of Mathematics at the University of Haifa in 1979, became [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 1989, and became professor [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in 2012.[^o3] His research focuses on mathematical aspects of Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy ([IMRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMRT)). In 2002, he founded the Center for Computational Mathematics and Scientific Computation at the University of Haifa. In recent years he is involved with research about the Superiorization Methodology.[^o6]

Together with S.A. Zenios, he co-authored the book *Parallel Optimization: Theory, Algorithms, and Applications* ([Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press), New York, NY, USA, 1997), for which he received the 1999 ICS ([INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) Computing Society) Prize for Research Excellence in the Interface Between Operations Research and Computer Science.[^o7]

Active in the struggle to preserve the [academic freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_freedom) of the research universities in Israel, Censor was one of the founders of the Inter-Senate Committee (ISC) of the Universities for the Protection of Academic Independence.[^o8]
