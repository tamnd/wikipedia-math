# Tata Subba Rao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tata Subba Rao"}

**Tata Subba Rao** (1942 – 13 April 2018[^o2]) was a professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in the [School of Mathematics, University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics,_University_of_Manchester). He gained his MA at [NIT Surathkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Technology_Karnataka), his PhD from [Gauhati University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauhati_University) in 1966 under the guidance of [Jyotiprasad Medhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyotiprasad_Medhi), and DSc from Manchester in 1988. He was a specialist in [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis) especially non-stationary and non-linear time series analysis, higher order spectral analysis, theory of random fields, time series methods for analysis of environmental variables (detection of climatic changes etc.) and multivariate nonlinear models. The [Subba Rao–Liporace models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subba_Rao–Liporace_model), and [Subba Rao Gabr window](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subba_Rao_Gabr_window) for bivariate spectra are named after him.[^o3]

:::

Subba Rao retired,[^o4] but was given an emeritus chair, in 2009 having worked at [UMIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UMIST) and the University of Manchester for 42 years. He published over 70 papers and supervised 15 PhD students. Even after retirement he remained active.[^o5]
