# George F. C. Griss

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George F. C. Griss"}

**George François Cornelis Griss** (30 January 1898, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam) – 2 August 1953, [Blaricum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaricum)), usually cited as **G. F. C. Griss**, was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), who was occupied with [Hegelian idealism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_idealism) and Brouwers [intuitionism](wikipedia://en/19513) and stated a negationless mathematics.

:::

Griss was a student of [L. E. J. Brouwer](wikipedia://en/172896) and formulated an [intuitionism](wikipedia://en/19513) based on a [Hegelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegel) idealism. He obtained his Ph.D. with [Roland Weitzenböck](wikipedia://en/35767598) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in July 1925. He was largely influenced by L. E. J. Brouwer, [Gerrit Mannoury](wikipedia://en/20420210), [Carry van Bruggen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carry_van_Bruggen) and [Gerard Bolland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Bolland), who brought Hegelian thought to the Netherlands. He published a number of articles about a negationless mathematics and one small book about idealistic philosophy, called *Idealistische Filosofie* (17 February 1946, Gouda), in which he lays down a typically Hegelian idealism, and incorporates elements from [Bergson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergson)'s *[Creative Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Evolution_%28book%29)* (*L'Evolution créatrice*).
