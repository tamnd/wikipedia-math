# Marek Fila

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marek Fila"}

**Marek Fila** (29 August 1959 – 20 April 2023)[^o3] was a Slovak mathematician. His main research focus was [diffusion equations](wikipedia://en/645335) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). He was among the most productive scientists in Slovakia.[^o4]

:::

Fila was born in [Bratislava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratislava) to an artistic family. His father was the painter [Rudolf Fila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Fila) and his mother [Dorota Filová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorota_Filová) was a well-known renovator of medieval art.[^o5]

Fila studied Mathematics at the [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University), graduating in 1989. His doctoral advisor was [Pavol Brunovský](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavol_Brunovský).[^o6] Following his graduation, he remained at the university and became a full professor in 2008.[^o7] In the 1990s, he was a visiting researcher at the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) and later a recurrent visitor at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo).[^o5] He founded the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of the Comenius University and led it for 11 years.[^o8]

Fila had two children; son [Lukáš Fila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukáš_Fila), who is a journalist and musician, and daughter Natália, who is an architect.[^o5]

Fila's death was announced by his department on the faculty website on 23 April 2023.[^o8]
