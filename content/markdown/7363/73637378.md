# Lisa Weissfeld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lisa Weissfeld"}

**Lisa Anderson Weissfeld** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) whose publications include work on the risks, prognoses, and treatment outcomes for [pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia), [sepsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepsis), and [end-of-life care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End-of-life_care); she is one of the authors of the [pneumonia severity index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia_severity_index). She has also published [basic research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_research) on sparse data in [meta-analysis](wikipedia://en/62329), on [multicollinearity](wikipedia://en/1486691), and on the dichotomization of [ordinal data](wikipedia://en/52904244), and is one of the namesakes of the Wei–Lin–Weissfeld model in [recurrent event analysis](wikipedia://en/71292446). She worked for many years as a professor at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Weissfeld earned a Ph.D. in 1982 at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), with the dissertation *Bounds on the Efficiencies of Commonly Used Nonparametric Statistics* supervised by Sam Wieand. She became a professor of biostatistics at the University of Pittsburgh in 1990.

In the mid-1990s, she became one of the founders and leaders of the Risk Analysis Section of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), and one of its early chairs; she also served as secretary–treasurer for the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344). She left academia to become a statistical consultant in [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_DC) in 2014.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Weissfeld was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1999.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Weissfeld is married to Joel Weissfeld, an [epidemiologist](wikipedia://en/66997).
