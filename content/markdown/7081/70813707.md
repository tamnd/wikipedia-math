# Anatol Odzijewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anatol Odzijewicz"}

**Anatol Odzijewicz** (November 10, 1947 – April 18, 2022)[^o3][^o4]\[ was Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist). The main areas of his research were the theory of Banach [groupoids](wikipedia://en/310923) and [algebroids](wikipedia://en/310921) related to the structure of [W\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W*-algebra), [quantization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantization_%28physics%29) of physical systems by means of the [coherent state](wikipedia://en/277213) map, as well as quantum and classical [integrable systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_systems).

:::

Anatol Odzijewicz was also a social activist. He founded a branch of the Society for the Preservation of Monuments in [Białystok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Białystok) and chaired it for many years. He was the founder of the open-air museum in Białowieża.[^o4][^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In the years 1975-1979 Odzijewicz was employed at [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University). Since 1979 Odzijewicz was working at [University of Białystok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Białystok) (till 1997 it was a branch of Warsaw University). In the years 1997-2005 he was the dean of the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, and from 2008 to 2016 - the dean of the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, [University of Białystok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Białystok). He was also the director of the Institute of Mathematics (2005-2008 and 2016-2019). He founded and was leading a group of researchers working in the area of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o7]

He was also a founder and main organizer of the conference series [Workshop on Geometric Methods in Physics](wikipedia://en/69563987).[^o4][^o8][^o9]
