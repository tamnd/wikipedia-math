# Judith O'Fallon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Judith O'Fallon"}

**Judith O'Fallon**, née **Judith Rich**, is a retired American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).[^o2][^o3] She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/51140705) in 1984,[^o4] and is also a lifetime member of it.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1973 she received a PhD in statistics from the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). Her [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) was "Discriminant Analysis Under Truncation" and her advisor was [N.L. Johnson](wikipedia://en/2590640).[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Work {#Work}

She was president of the Eastern North American Region (ENAR) of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) in 1994.[^o11][^o12]

She worked as a research statistician at the [Mayo Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic) until she retired.[^o2]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

She married William Michael (Mike) O’Fallon on September 2, 1961. They had two sons, Liam and Sean. William died on February 19, 2022.[^o3][^o2]

She became a [Rotarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_International) in 1989.[^o15]
