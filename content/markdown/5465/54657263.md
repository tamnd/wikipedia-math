# Betty Shannon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Betty Shannon"}

**Betty Shannon** (née Mary Elizabeth Moore) (April 14, 1922 – May 1, 2017) was an American mathematician and the main research collaborator of [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693).[^o3] Betty inspired and assisted Claude in building some of his most famous inventions.[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Shannon was born on April 14, 1922[^o6] in New York City to Vilma Ujlaky Moore and James E. Moore. She was awarded a full scholarship to the [New Jersey College for Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglass_Residential_College), where she graduated [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa) after studying mathematics.[^o7]

She worked as a numerical analyst at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs), where as a [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_computer) she supported work on microwaves, and then on radar. She published her own research on "Composing Music by a Stochastic Process";[^o8] an "exceptional" accomplishment in an era when it was a "significant and unusual achievement for a woman to get her name on a research report".[^o7]

While at Bell Labs she met the shy and insular [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693). Claude "didn’t have much patience with people who weren’t as smart as he was" and the two of them got on well.[^o4] In 1948 he asked her on a date and they ended up dining each night together; they were married in 1949.[^o9]

In addition to her research, Shannon was a member of the Weavers' Guild of Boston, served as Dean of the Guild from 1976 to 1978 and received the Guild's Distinguished Achievement Award.[^o10]

Shannon had three children, Robert James Shannon, Andrew Moore Shannon, and Margarita Shannon, and raised their family in [Winchester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester,_Massachusetts). Her oldest son, Robert Shannon, died in 1998 at the age of 45. Betty died on May 1, 2017, at her home at Brookhaven in [Lexington, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington,_Massachusetts).[^o11]
