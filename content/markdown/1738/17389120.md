# Duncan Lawson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Duncan Lawson"}

**Duncan Austin Lawson** is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for work in mathematics education including university-wide mathematics and statistics support.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Lawson attended [Bury Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bury_Grammar_School) and later obtained a [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) and [D.Phil.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Lawson worked for [British Gas plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Gas_plc) before moving to [Coventry University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_University) in 1987. His research interests are in efficient computational methods for the calculation of thermal [radiative heat transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiative_heat_transfer).

From 2005 to 2010 Lawson with [Tony Croft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Croft) established the [sigma Centre for Excellence in University-wide mathematics and statistics support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Centre_for_Excellence_in_University-wide_mathematics_and_statistics_support), which was awarded the 2011 *Times Higher Education* Award for Outstanding Support for Students.[^o6] The centre later developed under the National HE [STEM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STEM) Programme into the [sigma Network for Excellence in Mathematics and Statistics Support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Network_for_Excellence_in_Mathematics_and_Statistics_Support).[^o7][^o8]

Lawson was awarded [National Teaching Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Teaching_Fellowship) in 2005.[^o9] Lawson also acted as Assistant Chief Executive of the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy) from 2012-13.

Lawson was professor and associate dean of the faculty of science and engineering at [Coventry University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_University) (2002–13), director of the [Maths, Stats & OR Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maths,_Stats_&_OR_Network) of the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy) (2005-9), and chair of the executive group of the [More Maths Grads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_Maths_Grads) project (2008–10).

From 2013-18, Lawson was pro-vice-chancellor of formative education at [Newman University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newman_University,_Birmingham) in [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham), before returning to Coventry.[^o10]

Lawson received the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139)' Gold Medal in 2016 jointly with Croft for "an outstanding contribution to the improvement of the teaching of mathematics" by establishing Sigma.[^o8][^o11]

Lawson was appointed [Member of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (MBE) in the [2019 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Birthday_Honours) for services to mathematics in higher education.

Lawson is currently director of the sigma maths support centre at Coventry University.[^o13]
