# Frank H. Berkshire

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank H. Berkshire"}

**Frank H. Berkshire** is a British mathematician, an expert on [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), [biomechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomechanics), and the [mathematics of gambling](wikipedia://en/7804447).[^o2] He is a coauthor of the textbook *[Classical Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_Mechanics_%28Kibble_and_Berkshire_book%29)*.[^o8][^o9]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Berkshire is an alumnus of [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge).[^o4] He joined the faculty in the department of mathematics at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) in 1967. There he became a senior lecturer, director of undergraduate studies since 1987, and teaching fellow in 1996.[^o4]

In 2000, he won Imperial College's Rector's Medal for Outstanding Contribution to Teaching Excellence.[^o5] He retired in 2011 and remains principal teaching fellow in dynamics at Imperial.[^o6]

## Textbook {#Textbook}

In 1997, Berkshire became a co-author on the 4th edition of [Tom Kibble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Kibble)'s textbook *[Classical Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_Mechanics_%28Kibble_and_Berkshire_book%29)*.[^o8] The fifth edition was published by the [Imperial College Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_Press) in 2004.[^o9]
