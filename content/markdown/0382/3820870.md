# List of Ukrainian Jews

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Ukrainian Jews"}

Presented below are lists of famous or notable [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) people of [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) [descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_heritage) and other Jews born in the territory of present-day [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine), before 20 century borderland region in [Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish–Lithuanian_Commonwealth) (later in [Russian Partition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Partition) and [Austrian Partition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Partition)).

:::
