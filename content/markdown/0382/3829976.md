# Genstat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Genstat"}

**Genstat (General Statistics)** is a [statistical software package](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_statistical_packages) with [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954) capabilities, particularly in the field of [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture).[^o4][^o5] It was developed in 1968 by the [Rothamsted Research](wikipedia://en/165688) in the United Kingdom and was designed to provide [modular design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_design), linear [mixed models](wikipedia://en/5431921)[^o6] and graphical functions.[^o7] It was developed and distributed by VSN International (VSNi),[^o8] which was owned by The [Numerical Algorithms Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Algorithms_Group) and Rothamsted Research.

:::

Genstat is used in a number of research areas, including [plant science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_science), [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry), [animal science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_science), and [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine).[^o5]
