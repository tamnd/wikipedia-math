# R-matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="R-matrix"}

The term **R-matrix** has several meanings, depending on the field of study. Its original use has been to mathematically describe [nuclear reactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reaction).[^o2] In particular the general problem of nuclear reactions is to relate the values of the [scattering](wikipedia://en/164483) or collision matrix elements (which in principle can be obtained from measurements) to the (slow) dynamics of [nuclear structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_structure). The R-matrix formalism describes the effects of the interaction of the nucleus with the outside world. Its interior is not specified, i.e. it is considered a "black box". The original formulations of the theory came from nuclear scientists [Wigner](wikipedia://en/10101),[^o3][^o4] [Eisenbud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Eisenbud), [Breit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Breit),[^o5] [Blatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_M._Blatt), [Weisskopf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Weisskopf), and others.[^o6] Related theories are U-matrix, [S-matrix](wikipedia://en/622053), by [M-matrix](wikipedia://en/7939066), or [T-matrix](wikipedia://en/32671041).[^o7]

:::

## Other meanings and uses {#Other_meanings_and_uses}

The term *R-matrix* is used in connection with the [Yang–Baxter equation](wikipedia://en/3720810), first introduced in the field of [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) in the works of J. B. McGuire in 1964[^o9] and [C. N. Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._N._Yang) in 1967[^o10] and in the [group algebra](wikipedia://en/349223) $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) in the work of [A. A. Jucys](wikipedia://en/31618545) in 1966.[^o11] The classical R-matrix arises in the definition of the classical Yang–Baxter equation.[^o12]

In [quasitriangular Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/3727020), the R-matrix is a solution of the [Yang&ndash;Baxter equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang&ndash;Baxter_equation).

The numerical modeling of diffraction gratings in [optical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_science) can be performed using the R-matrix propagation algorithm.[^o13]

## R-matrix method in quantum mechanics {#R-matrix_method_in_quantum_mechanics}

There is a method in computational [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) for studying scattering known as the R-matrix. Using the original R-matrix theory as a basis, a method was developed for [electron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron), [positron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positron) and [photon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon) scattering by [atoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom).[^o15] This approach was later adapted for electron, positron and photon scattering by [molecules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecule).[^o16][^o17][^o18]

## Other applications {#Other_applications}

R-matrix method is used in [UKRmol](wikipedia://en/53992715)[^o20] and UKRmol+[^o21] code suits. The user-friendly software [Quantemol Electron Collisions (Quantemol-EC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantemol) and its predecessor [Quantemol-N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantemol) are based on UKRmol/UKRmol+ and employ [MOLPRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOLPRO) package for electron configuration calculations.
