# Wolfgang Hahn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Hahn"}

**Wolfgang Hahn** (April 30, 1911 – January 10, 1998) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), in particular [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718).

:::

He introduced [Hahn polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585658), Hahn difference, Hahn q-addition (or [Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F._H._Jackson)-Hahn-Cigler q-addition), and the [Hahn–Exton q-Bessel function](wikipedia://en/32779191). He was an honorary member of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society).
