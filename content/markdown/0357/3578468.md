# John M. Ball

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="John M. Ball"}

**Sir John Macleod Ball** (born 19 May 1948) is a British mathematician and former [Sedleian Professor of Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedleian_Professor_of_Natural_Philosophy) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). He was the president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) from 2003 to 2006 and a Fellow of [Queen's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford).

:::

Ball was educated at [St. John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge) and [Sussex University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sussex_University), and prior to taking up his Oxford post was a professor of mathematics at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh).[^o2]

Ball's research interests include [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29), the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), and infinite-dimensional [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). He was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor) in the [New Year Honours list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year_Honours_2006) for 2006 "for services to Science".[^o5] He is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters)[^o6] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]

He was a member of the first Abel Prize Committee in 2002[^o8] and for the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) Committee in 1998. From 1996 to 1998 he was president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), and from 2003 to 2006 he was president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868), IMU. In October 2011, he was elected on the executive board of [ICSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICSU) for a three-year period starting January 2012. Ball is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o9]

Along with [Stuart S. Antman](wikipedia://en/44788178) he won the [Theodore von Kármán Prize](wikipedia://en/24419775) in 1999.[^o10] In 2018, he received the [King Faisal International Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Faisal_International_Prize) in Mathematics.[^o11]

Ball received an Honorary Doctorate from [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in 1998.[^o12]

He was elected a Fellow of [The Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1980.[^o13] He also holds a visiting position at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh).[^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He is married to Lady Sedhar Chozam-Ball, actress, and has three children.[^o2]
