# Nan Laird

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Nan Laird"}

**Nan McKenzie Laird** (born September 18, 1943) is the [Harvey V. Fineberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_V._Fineberg) Professor of Public Health, Emerita in [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health). She served as Chair of the Department from 1990 to 1999. She was the Henry Pickering Walcott Professor of Biostatistics from 1991 to 1999.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Laird began her undergraduate studies at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 1961, first majoring in mathematics, before switching to French. She left Rice in her junior year in college and moved to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). Later, she resumed studies at [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) in computer science before eventually switching to statistics and earned her [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in 1969.[^o5] Laird worked between 1969 and 1971 as a computer programmer on the [Apollo program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_program) at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT)'s [Draper Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draper_Laboratory) before starting her graduate studies at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in statistics in 1971. She received her PhD from Harvard in 1975 under [Arthur Dempster](wikipedia://en/7040761) and was hired as a faculty member directly after graduation. She remained at Harvard until her retirement, when she became an [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor).[^o7]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Laird is well known for many seminal papers in biostatistics applications and methods, including the [expectation–maximization algorithm](wikipedia://en/470752).

### Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- DerSimonian, R.; Laird, N. (1986). "Meta-analysis in clinical trials". *Controlled Clinical Trials*. 7 (3). p. 177–188. doi:10.1016/0197-2456(86)90046-2
- Laird NM and [Ware, JH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Ware). (1982) "Random effects models for longitudinal data: an overview of recent results". *Biometrics*; [38:963-974](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7168798/).
- Dempster, A. P.; Laird, N.; Rubin, D. B. (1977). "Maximum likelihood from incomplete data via the EM algorithm". *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B*. 39 (1). p. 1–38. doi:10.1111/j.2517-6161.1977.tb01600.x. jstor:2984875

### Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Her honors include the third [International Prize in Statistics](wikipedia://en/52048719) in 2021,[^o13] the 25th Annual Distinguished Statistician Lecture from the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut), the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and [Pfizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfizer) in 2016, the 25th Annual Lowell Reed Lecturer, from the [American Public Health Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Public_Health_Association) in 2011, the [Samuel S. Wilks](wikipedia://en/8136395) Award, from the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2011, the Myra Samuels Lecturer award from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 2004, the [Janet L. Norwood](wikipedia://en/44595999) Award in 2003 from the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), the [Florence Nightingale David Award](wikipedia://en/58964720) in 2001 from the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344), and several other fellowships.

Laird is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), as well as the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). She is an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o14]
