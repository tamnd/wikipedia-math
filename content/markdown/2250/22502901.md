# Kushner equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kushner equation"}

In [filtering theory](wikipedia://en/12433418) the **Kushner equation** (after [Harold Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_J._Kushner)) is an equation for the [conditional probability](wikipedia://en/24104134) [density](wikipedia://en/43487) of the state of a [stochastic](wikipedia://en/47895) non-linear [dynamical system](wikipedia://en/9087), given noisy measurements of the state.[^o2] It therefore provides the solution of the [nonlinear filtering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_filter) problem in [estimation theory](wikipedia://en/1565926). The equation is sometimes referred to as the **Stratonovich–Kushner**[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] (or Kushner–Stratonovich) **equation**.

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

Assume the state of the system evolves according to

$$dx = f(x,t) \, dt + \sigma\, dw$$

and a noisy measurement of the system state is available:

$$dz = h(x,t) \, dt + \eta\, dv$$

where *w*, *v* are independent [Wiener processes](wikipedia://en/149984). Then the conditional probability density *p*(*x*, *t*) of the state at time *t* is given by the Kushner equation:

$$dp(x,t) = L[p(x,t)] dt + p(x,t) \big(h(x,t)-E_t h(x,t) \big)^\top \eta^{-\top}\eta^{-1} \big(dz-E_t h(x,t) dt\big).$$

where

$$L[p] := -\sum \frac{\partial (f_i p)}{\partial x_i} + \frac{1}{2} \sum (\sigma \sigma^\top)_{i,j} \frac{\partial^2 p}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}$$

is the [Kolmogorov forward](wikipedia://en/166896) operator and

$$dp(x,t) = p(x,t + dt) - p(x,t)$$

is the variation of the conditional probability.

The term $dz - E_t h(x,t) dt$ is the [innovation](wikipedia://en/21957017), i.e. the difference between the measurement and its expected value.

### Kalman–Bucy filter {#Kalman–Bucy_filter}

One can use the Kushner equation to derive the [Kalman–Bucy filter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman–Bucy_filter) for a linear [diffusion process](wikipedia://en/1667059). Suppose we have $f(x,t) = A x$ and $h(x,t) = C x$. The Kushner equation will be given by

$$dp(x,t) = L[p(x,t)] dt + p(x,t) \big( C x- C \mu(t) \big)^\top \eta^{-\top}\eta^{-1} \big(dz-C \mu(t) dt\big),$$

where $\mu(t)$ is the mean of the conditional probability at time $t$. Multiplying by $x$ and integrating over it, we obtain the variation of the mean

$$d\mu(t) = A \mu(t) dt + \Sigma(t) C^\top \eta^{-\top}\eta^{-1} \big(dz - C\mu(t) dt\big).$$

Likewise, the variation of the variance $\Sigma(t)$ is given by

$$\tfrac{d}{dt}\Sigma(t) = A\Sigma(t) + \Sigma(t) A^\top + \sigma^\top \sigma-\Sigma(t) C^\top\eta^{-\top} \eta^{-1} C \,\Sigma(t).$$

The conditional probability is then given at every instant by a normal distribution $\mathcal{N}(\mu(t),\Sigma(t))$.
