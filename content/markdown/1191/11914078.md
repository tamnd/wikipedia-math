# Gerland (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerland"}

**Gerland** was an 11th-century mathematician who flourished in [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) following the [Norman Conquest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_conquest_of_England). Little is known of his personal life. His treatise on the [Computus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computus) may be found in the [British Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Museum) and his work on the [abacus](wikipedia://en/655) is preserved in the [Bibliothèque nationale de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliothèque_nationale_de_France) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989).[^o2]

:::
