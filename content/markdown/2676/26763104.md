# Jacobi zeta function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacobi zeta function"}

In mathematics, the **Jacobi zeta function** *Z*(*u*) is the [logarithmic derivative](wikipedia://en/470327) of the [Jacobi theta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_theta_function) Θ(u). It is also commonly denoted as $\operatorname{zn}(u,k)$[^o2]

:::

$$\Theta(u)=\Theta_{4}\left(\frac{\pi u}{2K}\right)$$

$Z(u)=\frac{\partial}{\partial u}\ln\Theta(u)$ $=\frac{\Theta'(u)}{\Theta(u)}$[^o3]

$Z(\phi|m)=E(\phi|m)-\frac{E(m)}{K(m)}F(\phi|m)$[^o4]

Where E, K, and F are generic Incomplete [Elliptical Integrals](wikipedia://en/9960) of the first and second kind. Jacobi Zeta Functions being kinds of Jacobi theta functions have applications to all their relevant fields and application.

$\operatorname{zn}(u,k)=Z(u)=\int_{0}^{u}\operatorname{dn}^{2}v-\frac{E}{K}dv$[^o2]

This relates Jacobi's common notation of, $\operatorname{dn}{u}=\sqrt{1-m \sin{\theta}^2}$, $\operatorname{sn}u= \sin{\theta}$, $\operatorname{cn}u= \cos{\theta}$.[^o2] to Jacobi's Zeta function.

Some additional relations include ,

$\operatorname{zn}(u,k)=\frac{\pi}{2K}\frac{\Theta_1'\frac{\pi u}{2K}}{\Theta_1\frac{\pi u}{2K}}-\frac{\operatorname{cn}{u}\,\operatorname{dn}{u}}{\operatorname{sn}{u}}$[^o2]

$\operatorname{zn}(u,k)=\frac{\pi}{2K}\frac{\Theta_2'\frac{\pi u}{2K}}{\Theta_2\frac{\pi u}{2K}}-\frac{\operatorname{sn}{u}\,\operatorname{dn}{u}}{\operatorname{cn}{u}}$[^o2]

$\operatorname{zn}(u,k)=\frac{\pi}{2K}\frac{\Theta_3'\frac{\pi u}{2K}}{\Theta_3\frac{\pi u}{2K}}-k^2\frac{\operatorname{sn}{u}\,\operatorname{cn}{u}}{\operatorname{dn}{u}}$[^o2]

$\operatorname{zn}(u,k)=\frac{\pi}{2K}\frac{\Theta_4'\frac{\pi u}{2K}}{\Theta_4\frac{\pi u}{2K}}$[^o2]
