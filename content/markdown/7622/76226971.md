# Debbie Cheng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Debbie Cheng"}

**Debbie Mein-Pay Cheng** is an American biostatistician and a professor at the [Boston University School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University_School_of_Public_Health). She researches applied statistics and [clinical trial](wikipedia://en/241717) design.[^o2] Cheng was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2023.[^o3] She earned a B.S. in industrial engineering from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).[^o2] She received a Sc.D. in biostatistics from the [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health).[^o2] Her 1999 dissertation was titled, *Analysis of Data from Eradication Studies of Chronic Viral Infections*.[^o4] [Stephen Lagakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Lagakos) was her doctoral advisor.[^o4]
