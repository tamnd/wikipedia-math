# Aubrey J. Kempner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aubrey J. Kempner"}

**Aubrey John Kempner** (22 September 1880, in [Greater London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_London), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) – 18 November 1973, in [Boulder, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulder,_Colorado)) was an English-born American mathematician, known for the [Kempner function](wikipedia://en/11147109) and the [Kempner series](wikipedia://en/17961670).

:::

Aubrey Kempner received in 1911 his PhD with the dissertation *Über das [Waringsche Problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waring_problem) und einige Verallgemeinerungen* under [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230) at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He then went to the US and taught at the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) and from 1925 at the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder), where he remained until 1949 and from 1944 to 1949 was chair of the mathematics department. From 1950 he also taught at the [Colorado School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_School_of_Medicine) and at the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) in Boulder.

Kempner's research was mainly in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and the theory of calculating the center of mass of a region defined by [polynomial equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation). In his honor the mathematics faculty of the University of Colorado at Boulder established the Kempner Colloquium. In the years 1937 and 1938 he was president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o3]
