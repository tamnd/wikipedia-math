# List of scientific publications by John von Neumann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of scientific publications by John von Neumann"}

[John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) (1903–1957) was a Hungarian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and [polymath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath). He had perhaps the widest coverage of any mathematician of his time, integrating [pure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_research) and [applied sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_science#Applied_research) and making major contributions to many fields, including [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing), and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). He was a pioneer of the application of [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) to quantum mechanics in the development of functional analysis, the development of game theory and the concepts of [cellular automata](wikipedia://en/54342), the [universal constructor](wikipedia://en/8597086) and the [digital computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer). His analysis of the structure of [self-replication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-replication) preceded the discovery of the structure of [DNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA).

:::

## Collected works {#Collected_works}

Von Neumann's collected works were published as a six-volume set, edited by [Abraham H. Taub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_H._Taub) and printed by Pergamon Press.

- \1961. *Volume I: Logic, Theory of Sets and Quantum Mechanics*
- \1961. *Volume II: Operators, Ergodic Theory and Almost Periodic Functions in a Group*
- \1961. *Volume III: Rings of Operators*
- \1962. *Volume IV: Continuous Geometry and other topics*
- \1963. *Volume V: Design of Computers, Theory of Automata and Numerical Analysis*
- \1963. *Volume VI: Theory of Games, Astrophysics, Hydrodynamics and Meteorology*
