# French Statistical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="French Statistical Society"}

The **French Statistical Society** (in French, *Société française de statistique*, **SFdS**) is a French [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) founded in 1997[^o2][^o3][^o4] specialising in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). Its vocation is to promote the use of statistics, to enhance its public understanding, and to encourage associated methodological developments.

:::

## Actions {#Actions}

### Publications {#Publications}

The *SFdS* publishes several [scientific journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal):[^o3][^o7]

- *[Journal de la société française de statistique](http://journal-sfds.fr/)* (Journal of the French Statistical Society, J-SFdS), in French and English.
- *[Statistique et Enseignement](http://statistique-et-enseignement.fr)* in French.[^o8]
- *[Statistique et Société](http://statistique-et-societe.fr)* in French.
- *[Case Studies In Business, Industry And Government Statistics](http://csbigs.fr)* (CSBIGS) in collaboration with [Bentley University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bentley_University).

### Events and outreach {#Events_and_outreach}

The SFdS organizes every year the *journées de statistiques*[^o10][^o11] (JdS).

### Awards {#Awards}

Source:[^o3][^o13]

- *[Prix du Docteur Norbert Marx](https://www.sfds.asso.fr/fr/prix_et_bourses/543-prix_du_docteur_norbert_marx/)* for contributions in [epidepiology](wikipedia://en/66997), [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) or [health economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_economics).
- *[Prix Marie-Jeanne Laurent-Duhamel](https://www.sfds.asso.fr/fr/prix_et_bourses/544-le_prix_marie_jeanne_laurent_duhamel/)* for a PhD thesis from a French speaking statistician.
- *[Prix Pierre-Simon de Laplace](https://www.sfds.asso.fr/fr/prix_et_bourses/548-le_prix_pierre_simon_de_laplace/)* awarded every three years to an accomplished French-speaking statistician.

## History {#History}

The society was founded on 6 August 1997, and was recognised to serve the public benefit by the French administration on 3 December 1998.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o15] The *SFdS* results from the merging of the *[société de statistique de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_statistique_de_Paris)* (SSP), founded in 1860, the *[association pour la statistique et ses utilisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_pour_la_statistique_et_ses_utilisations)* (ASU), founded in 1969 and the *[société de statistique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_statistique_de_France)* (SSF) funded in 1976[^o4][^o16][^o17]
