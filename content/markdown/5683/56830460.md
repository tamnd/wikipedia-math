# Kyoji Saito

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kyoji Saito"}

*Image: Saito pham trung greuel.jpg.* from left: Ngô Việt Trung, Kyoji Saito, [Frédéric Pham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frédéric_Pham), Gert-Martin Greuel at [Dalat University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalat_University) in 2008

**Kyōji Saitō** (齋藤 恭司, Saitō Kyōji; born 25 December 1944)[^o2] is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [complex analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/186101).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Saito received in 1971 his [promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) under [Egbert Brieskorn](wikipedia://en/34748690), with thesis *Quasihomogene isolierte Singularitäten von Hyperflächen* (Quasihomogeneous isolated singularities of hypersurfaces). Saito is a professor at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) (RIMS) of [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).

Saito's research deals with the interplay among [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944), [reflection groups](wikipedia://en/2422023) ([Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004)),[^o5] [braid groups](wikipedia://en/550138), and [singularities](wikipedia://en/981915) of [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101).

From the 1980s, he did research on underlying symmetries of period integrals in complex hypersurfaces. Saito introduced higher-dimensional generalizations of [elliptic integrals](wikipedia://en/9960). These generalizations are integrals of "primitive forms",[^o6] first considered in the study of the unfolding of isolated singularities of complex hypersurfaces, associated with infinite-dimensional Lie algebras. He also studied the corresponding new automorphic forms.[^o7] The theory has a geometric connection to "flat structures" (now called "Saito Frobenius manifolds"),[^o8] [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671), [Frobenius manifolds](wikipedia://en/29218464), and [Gromov–Witten theory](wikipedia://en/2301291) in algebraic geometry and various topics in mathematical physics related to [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).

Saito supervised the thesis of 7 Ph.D. students at Kyoto University, including [Hiroaki Terao](wikipedia://en/56374155) and Masahiko Yoshinaga.[^o4]

He was an Invited Speaker with talk *The limit element in the configuration algebra for a discrete group: a précis* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 1990 in Kyoto. In 2011 he was awarded the Geometry Prize of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786).
