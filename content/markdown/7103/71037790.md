# Operations Support Branch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Operations Support Branch"}

The **Operations Support Branch** (**OSB**) is a unit of the cyber-intelligence division of the [Central Intelligence Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency) (CIA).[^o3] It is located on the ninth floor of a secret facility in the suburbs of northern Virginia, west of [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)[^o3] [Patrick Radden Keefe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Radden_Keefe) described the OSB as the CIA's "secret hacker unit, in which a cadre of élite engineers create cyberweapons" in a June 2022 article for *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker)*.[^o3]

:::

The OSB specializes in [physical access operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_access) in which 'physical access' is gained to electronic devices owned by high value individual targets such as foreign government officials and terrorists.[^o3] The OSB is able to quickly develop tools that can be utilised in cyberintelligence missions at short notice.[^o3]

According to anonymous witnesses who testified at [Joshua Schulte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Schulte)'s trial, the OSB was filled with workspace pranks, like stealing coworkers' things, name calling, shoving matches, rubber band and [Nerf gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerf_Blaster) wars. Asked if she was aware of this, the former head of CIA's Center for Cyber Intelligence Bonnie Stith said she was not.[^o4]

The employees of the OSB numbered about a dozen in the 2010s.[^o3] Radden Keefe described the extreme secrecy of the OSB at this period as resulting in staff unable to " ... take work home, or talk with anyone on the outside about what they did all day. Their office was a classified sanctum, a locked vault. Like the crew of a submarine, they forged strong bonds—and strong antagonisms".[^o3] The software engineer [Joshua Schulte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Schulte) was employed by the OSB from 2010 to 2016. He was convicted of being the leaker of the [Vault 7](wikipedia://en/53412768) documents which detail electronic surveillance and [cyber warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_warfare) tools developed by the CIA.[^o3][^o5][^o6][^o7] Schulte was nicknamed '[Voldemort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voldemort)' during his time at the OSB.[^o3] The leak and publication of the Vault 7 documents was a significant blow to the CIA, a senior official likened it to the 'digital' equivalent of the [attack on Pearl Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Pearl_Harbor) in its scope and fallout.[^o3]
