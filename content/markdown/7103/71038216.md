# Puncture (topology)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Puncture"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), **puncturing** a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) is removing a [finite set](wikipedia://en/11742) of [points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_%28topology%29) from that manifold.[^n1] The set of points can be small as a single point. In this case, the manifold is known as **once-punctured**. With the removal of a second point, it becomes **twice-punctured**, and so on.

:::

Examples of punctured manifolds include the [open disk](wikipedia://en/329542) (which is a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) with a single puncture), the [cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylinder_%28geometry%29) (which is a sphere with two punctures),[^n1] and the [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) (which is a [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) with a single puncture).[^n2]

The set of non-zero [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) is the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) once punctured at the origin, sometimes denoted $\mathbb{C}^*$.[^o5]
