# Jacobi group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacobi group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Jacobi group**, introduced by (Eichler Zagier 1985), is the [semidirect product](wikipedia://en/48246) of the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) Sp<sub>2*n*</sub>(**R**) and the [Heisenberg group](wikipedia://en/448518) **R**<sup>1+2*n*</sup>. The concept is named after [Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi](wikipedia://en/210340). [Automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) in the Jacobi group are called [Jacobi forms](wikipedia://en/23991325).

:::
