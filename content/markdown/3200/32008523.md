# Marcello Boldrini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcello Boldrini"}

*Image: Marcello Boldrini.jpg.* Marcello Boldrini

**Marcello Boldrini** (9 February 1890, in [Matelica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matelica) – 5 March 1969, in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan)) was an Italian [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Beginning in 1922, he taught courses in statistics, [biometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometry), and [demography](wikipedia://en/45642) at [Bocconi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocconi_University) of Milan, and then at the [University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) as Emeritus Professor. He was also a member of several academies and institutes in Italy and abroad, serving for several years as president of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). His scientific research was on both methodological and applied statistics, particularly on demography, [anthropometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropometry), and economics. As a statistician, he has been particularly interested in the foundations of the method, and he proposed a view of statistics as an empirical history of all the positive sciences. To deepen this research he founded a statistical Laboratory at the [Catholic University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Milan). The results are widely discussed in the volumes, Statistica: Teoria e metodi (first edition, 1942), and Teoria della Statistica (1963), which have been studied by thousands of students. For Boldrini, Statistics is a formal science like mathematics and logic, but it is different from them as a scientific inquiry method in the inductive and deductive phases of research.

Beyond academic commitment, he also had several management positions in the state oil industry: he was first named president of [Agip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agip) (1948–1953),[^o4] was then vice-president of [ENI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eni) (1953–1962), before succeeding [Enrico Mattei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrico_Mattei) as president in 1962,[^o5] remaining in the post until 1967.

## Academic Positions {#Academic_Positions}

Professor of Statistics and Demography at the Universities of [Messina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Messina), [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua), Milan and Rome. Dean of Political Sciences, [Milan Catholic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Catholic_University) (1043-1945).

## Honours, memberships {#Honours,_memberships}

Member of the [Pontifical Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Academy), [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei), Honoris Causa at the [University of Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro). Professor emeritus at Rome University.
