# Tilemann Stella

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tilemann Stella"}

**Tilemann Stella** (c. 1525-1589) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

*Image: Tilemann Stellas karta öfver Zweibrücken.jpg.* Zweibruecken on a map from 1564 by Tilemann Stella

Tilemann was born as Tilemann Stoltz in 1525 in [Siegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegen).[^o5] He studied at the Latin school in Siegen before attending the [Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg), the [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg), and the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne).[^o5] He was taught by mathematicians such as [Johannes Dryander](wikipedia://en/73666883) and [Erasmus Reinhold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Reinhold).[^o2] Stella became a close student of [Philipp Melanchthon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Melanchthon), who entrusted him with creating cartographic representations for [biblical studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblical_studies). He created maps of the [Holy Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Land), [Exodus route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exodus), and [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), with plans for further maps that were never completed.[^o2]

In 1552, [John Albert I, Duke of Mecklenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Albert_I,_Duke_of_Mecklenburg) started funding his work.[^o2] He began mapping [Mecklenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecklenburg) and made a celestial globe in 1555.[^o2] In 1560, he was employed as a court mathematician and [librarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Librarian) in [Schwerin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwerin).[^o2] He documented his travels with Duke Johann Albrecht and created further maps in Mecklenburg, including planning and supervising the construction of a canal between [Dömitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dömitz) and [Wismar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wismar).[^o2] He also charted [Mansfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfeld) and [Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg) for the [Counts of Mansfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Mansfeld) and created maps for [Duke Wolfgang von Pfalz-Zweibrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang,_Count_Palatine_of_Zweibrücken).[^o2]

Following Duke Johann Albrecht's death in 1576, Stella's connection with Mecklenburg weakened, and he worked for the [courts of Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electorate_of_Saxony) and Brandenburg-Ansbach.[^o2] Around 1582, he entered the service of [Duke John I of Palatinate-Zweibrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_I,_Count_Palatine_of_Zweibrücken), becoming the head of the court library and planning a canal between the Rhine and Saar. Stella died in 1589, and his estate was eventually sold to the "[Bibliotheca Bipontina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliotheca_Bipontina)."[^o2]
