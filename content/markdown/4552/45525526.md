# Andrea R. Nahmod

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrea R. Nahmod"}

**Andrea Rica Nahmod** (born 1964)[^o3] is an American mathematician at [University of Massachusetts, Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts,_Amherst).[^o4] She is known for her work in [nonlinear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/16678376) nonlinear Fourier analysis, and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147).[^o5]

:::

## Eearly Life and Education {#Eearly_Life_and_Education}

Nahmod was born in [Buenos Aires, Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires),[^o8] into a family of physicians and medical researchers.[^o7]

During her childhood and early university years in Argentina, which coincided with the military dictatorship (1976–1983), she found mathematics to be a source of intellectual stability amid political turmoil. Although she initially intended to study medicine, her interest in the mathematical foundations of genetics and the encouragement of a former neighbor working in astrophysics led her to pursue a career in mathematics.[^o7]

She earned a Licenciatura en Matemática (equivalent to an M.Sc. in Mathematics) from the Faculty of Exact and Natural Sciences at the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires).[^o8] Her Thesis advisor was [Cora Sadosky](wikipedia://en/42346687) who also helped her pursue her Ph.D in the US.[^o9]

Nahmod received her Ph.D. from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1991 under the supervision of [Ronald R. Coifman](wikipedia://en/1558980).[^o9] She went to work as a research fellow at Macquarie University from 1992 to 1994, followed by positions at [University of Texas, Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas,_Austin), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), before coming to work at [University of Massachusetts, Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts,_Amherst) in 1998.[^o4][^o11][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

She has organized research programs at institutions including the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](wikipedia://en/401367) (MSRI) at the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California), Berkeley, and the [Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31112547) (ICERM) at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2013, Nahmod became a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o14]

In 2014, Nahmod became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15] The society cited her “contributions to nonlinear Fourier analysis, harmonic analysis, and partial differential equations, as well as service to the mathematical community.”[^o16]

Nahmod was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for 2015-2016.[^o17]

She was also awarded the Sargent-Faul Fellowship at the [Radcliffe Institute for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Radcliffe_Institute) and the Chern Professorship.[^o5]
