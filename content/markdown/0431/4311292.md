# Wadim Zudilin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wadim Zudilin"}

*Image: Wadim Zudilin.jpg.* Wadim Zudilin

**Wadim Zudilin** (Вадим Валентинович Зудилин) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) who is active in studying [hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypergeometric_series) and [zeta constants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeta_constants). He studied under [Yuri V. Nesterenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Valentinovich_Nesterenko) and worked at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494), the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) and the [University of Newcastle, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle,_Australia). He works at [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) in the Netherlands.[^o2]

:::

He has reproved [Apéry's theorem](wikipedia://en/1711336) that $\zeta(3)$ is irrational, and expanded it. Zudilin proved that at least one of the four numbers $\zeta(5)$, $\zeta(7)$, $\zeta(9)$, or $\zeta(11)$ is irrational.[^o3] For that accomplishment, he won the Distinguished Award of the [Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599)-[Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717) Society in 2001.[^o4]

With [Doron Zeilberger](wikipedia://en/1724825), Zudilin[^o5] improved the known upper bound of the [irrationality measure](wikipedia://en/5968488) for $\pi$, which as of November 2022 is the current best estimate.
