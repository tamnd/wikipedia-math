# Pan-African Mathematics Olympiads

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pan-African Mathematics Olympiads"}

The **Pan-African Mathematics Olympiads (P.A.M.O.)** are the African version of the IMO, [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).

:::

## Description {#Description}

This event organized each year by the [African Mathematics Union](wikipedia://en/1764260) (AMU) is a competition among the best pupils in Mathematics of Secondary Education who are less than twenty (20) years old.

## History {#History}

The first PAMO was organized in 1987.[^o4]

## Format {#Format}

The competition is made of two rounds. Each round is made of 3 problems for four hours and thirty minutes while each problem's total score is 7 points. There are up to six candidates per country.
