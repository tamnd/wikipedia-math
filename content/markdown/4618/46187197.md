# Karl-Heinz Boseck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl-Heinz Boseck"}

**Karl-Heinz Boseck** (born 11 December 1915)[^o2] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

According to Segal (2003), Boseck was a fanatical [National Socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialist) and a student leader.[^o3] He was an informer of the [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo)[^o4][^o5] since 1939.[^o2] In 1944, shortly after his diploma graduation he was made an [Untersturmführer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untersturmführer) of the [Nazi SS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_SS) and established a department for numerical computation in the [Sachsenhausen concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachsenhausen_concentration_camp)[^o4][^o5] He was exempted from war service due to a disease. He was an assistant of the German mathematician [Alfred Klose](wikipedia://en/71876602) at [Berlin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_University), and had great influence in the faculty during World War II.[^o3] At the first mathematicians camp 1–3 July 1938 in the youth hostel of Ützdorf<sup>(de)</sup> near [Bernau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernau_bei_Berlin), he lectured "On the development of student science work".[^o6] He was department chairman for natural science at Berlin University, and had great influence on [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186) who was leader of the "seminar" (may be institute); with course of time even more power shifted from Bieberbach to Boseck.[^o7][^o8]
