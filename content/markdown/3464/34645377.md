# Carlo Rosati

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carlo Rosati"}

**Carlo Rosati** ([Livorno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livorno), 24 April 1876 – [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), 19 August 1929) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) who introduced the [Rosati involution](wikipedia://en/34645248).[^o2][^o3]

:::
