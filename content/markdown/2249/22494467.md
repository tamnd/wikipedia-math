# Henk Tijms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henk Tijms"}

**Henk Tijms** ([Beverwijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverwijk), April 23, 1944) is a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Emeritus Professor of Operations Research at the [VU University Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit).

:::

He studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam) where he graduated from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 1972 under supervision of [Gijsbert de Leve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gijsbert_de_Leve).

Tijms is the author of several articles on applied mathematics and [stochastics](wikipedia://en/47895) and books on [probability](wikipedia://en/23542). His best-known books are *Stochastic Modeling and Analysis* (Wiley, 1986) and *Understanding Probability* (Cambridge University Press, 2004).

On October 12, 2008, Tijms became the first non-American to receive the [INFORMS Expository Writing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS#Awards).[^o2] The award honoured his achievements in the field of mathematics.
