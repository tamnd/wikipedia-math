# Bjarni Jónsson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bjarni Jónsson"}

the artist Bjarni

**Bjarni Jónsson** (February 15, 1920 – September 30, 2016)[^o3] was an Icelandic [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) working in [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200), [lattice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_theory), [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) and [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He was [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) and the honorary editor in chief of *[Algebra Universalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_Universalis)*. He received his PhD in 1946 at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley) under supervision of [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920).[^o5]

:::

In 1974 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver) where he spoke on Congruence varieties.

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Work {#Work}

[Jónsson's lemma](wikipedia://en/17398087#JonssonsLemma) as well as several mathematical objects are named after him, among them [Jónsson algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jónsson_algebra), ω-[Jónsson functions](wikipedia://en/18455668), [Jónsson cardinals](wikipedia://en/3317988), [Jónsson terms](wikipedia://en/54457435), [Jónsson–Tarski algebras](wikipedia://en/43193940) and [Jónsson–Tarski duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jónsson–Tarski_duality).
