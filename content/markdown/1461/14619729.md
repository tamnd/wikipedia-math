# Hasse–Davenport relation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hasse–Davenport relation"}

The **Hasse–Davenport relations**, introduced by (txt), are two related identities for [Gauss sums](wikipedia://en/6378204), one called the **Hasse–Davenport lifting relation**, and the other called the **Hasse–Davenport product relation**. The Hasse–Davenport lifting relation is an equality in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) relating Gauss sums over different fields. (Weil 1949) used it to calculate the zeta function of a [Fermat hypersurface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_hypersurface) over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615), which motivated the [Weil conjectures](wikipedia://en/244705).

:::

Gauss sums are analogues of the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316) over finite fields, and the Hasse–Davenport product relation is the analogue of Gauss's multiplication formula

$$\Gamma(z) \; \Gamma\left(z + \frac{1}{k}\right) \; \Gamma\left(z + \frac{2}{k}\right) \cdots \Gamma\left(z + \frac{k-1}{k}\right) = (2 \pi)^{ \frac{k-1}{2}} \; k^{1/2 - kz} \; \Gamma(kz). \,\!$$

In fact the Hasse–Davenport product relation follows from the analogous multiplication formula for [*p*-adic gamma functions](wikipedia://en/33869124) together with the [Gross–Koblitz formula](wikipedia://en/33875057) of (Gross Koblitz 1979).

## Hasse–Davenport lifting relation {#Hasse–Davenport_lifting_relation}

Let *F* be a finite field with *q* elements, and *F*<sub>s</sub> be the field such that \[*F*<sub>s</sub>:*F*\] = *s*, that is, *s* is the [dimension](wikipedia://en/38267) of the [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) *F*<sub>s</sub> over *F*.

Let $\alpha$ be an element of $F_s$.

Let $\chi$ be a [multiplicative](wikipedia://en/20059) [character](wikipedia://en/293418) from *F* to the complex numbers.

Let $N_{F_s/F}(\alpha)$ be the norm from $F_s$ to $F$ defined by

$$N_{F_s/F}(\alpha):=\alpha\cdot\alpha^q\cdots\alpha^{q^{s-1}}.\,$$

Let $\chi'$ be the multiplicative character on $F_s$ which is the composition of $\chi$ with the [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) from *F*<sub>s</sub> to *F*, that is

$$\chi'(\alpha):=\chi(N_{F_s/F}(\alpha))$$

Let ψ be some nontrivial additive character of *F*, and let $\psi'$ be the additive character on $F_s$ which is the composition of $\psi$ with the [trace](wikipedia://en/43270) from *F*<sub>s</sub> to *F*, that is

$$\psi'(\alpha):=\psi(Tr_{F_s/F}(\alpha))$$

Let

$$\tau(\chi,\psi)=\sum_{x\in F}\chi(x)\psi(x)$$

be the Gauss sum over *F*, and let $\tau(\chi',\psi')$ be the Gauss sum over $F_s$.

Then the **Hasse–Davenport lifting relation** states that

$$(-1)^s\cdot \tau(\chi,\psi)^s=-\tau(\chi',\psi').$$

## Hasse–Davenport product relation {#Hasse–Davenport_product_relation}

The Hasse–Davenport product relation states that

$$\prod_{a\bmod m} \tau(\chi\rho^a,\psi) = -\chi^{-m}(m)\tau(\chi^m,\psi)\prod_{a\bmod m} \tau(\rho^a,\psi)$$

where ρ is a multiplicative character of exact order *m* dividing *q*–1 and χ is any multiplicative character and ψ is a non-trivial additive character.
