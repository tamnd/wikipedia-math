# Nym Technologies

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Nym Technologies"}

**Nym Technologies** is a Swiss company that provides [internet privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_privacy) technology utilizing [Nym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nym_%28mixnet%29) [mix network](wikipedia://en/6200269) and noise generation networks. Nym offers [VPN](wikipedia://en/147130) and [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971)-related software and [network services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_service). Its most notable service, **NymVPN**, advertises itself as a [VPN service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VPN_service) for privacy and [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_security).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

Nym Technologies was formed in [Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuchâtel), Switzerland in 2018.[^o9] NymVPN was in [development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle) and [testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing) for several years,[^o7][^o8] with the public release in March 2025. It is [cross-platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-platform_software), [free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_software) and [open source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) software ([FOSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software)). Developed by [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researchers, it was presented at [conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security_conference) such as [Chaos Communication Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_Communication_Congress),[^o9] [HOPE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackers_on_Planet_Earth),[^o10] and at the [Free Software Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Software_Foundation)'s [LibrePlanet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LibrePlanet)[^o11] during its alpha-beta development and after its public release. The Nym mixnet technology was also tested at some of these events. NymVPN has undergone [security audits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_security_audit)[^o12][^o13] by JP Aumasson (2021),[^o14] Oak Security (2023),[^o15] [Cure53](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cure53) (2024),[^o16] and Cryspen. Nym Wallet has also been independently audited (2023).[^o17]

NymVPN operates separate entry and exit nodes using the AmneziaWG implementation of the [WireGuard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WireGuard) protocol on its "fast" connection mode and five-hop mixnet on "anonymous" mode and maintains [no logs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-knowledge_service). It supports a [command-line interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command-line_interface), [split tunneling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_tunneling), custom entry and exit [node](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Node_%28networking%29) selection, an internet connection kill switch, zero-knowledge credentials and payments, diskless architecture, [IPv6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPv6) with leak protection, customizable [DNS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_Name_System) servers, DNS leak protection, perfect [forward secrecy](wikipedia://en/2191130), [QUIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QUIC), and [SOCKS5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOCKS), and the app's user interface is available in multiple languages. It is written in [Rust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_%28programming_language%29).[^o18] Nym announced that it will launch a public [bug bounty program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_bounty_program) to incentivize [vulnerability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulnerability_%28computer_security%29) investigation and reporting.

NymVPN payments are accepted in various [cryptocurrencies](wikipedia://en/36662188). Nym Technologies offers a [crypto wallet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_wallet), and it incentivizes volunteers running nodes with payments in its **NYM** token.[^o6]

As of January 2026 the Nym Technologies [advisory board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_board) consists of [George Danezis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Danezis), [Aggelos Kiayias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggelos_Kiayias), [Ben Laurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Laurie), and [Bart Preneel](wikipedia://en/1520490). Additional advisors include [Daniel J. Bernstein](wikipedia://en/198983), [Chelsea Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_Manning), Karthikeyan Bhargavan, and [Carmela Troncoso](wikipedia://en/64244993).[^o19] Nym partners with academic organizations such as the Security and Privacy Engineering Laboratory (SPRING) at [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL), the Computer Security and Industrial Cryptography (COSIC) research group at [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven), and Cryspen.[^o18]

Alternative services in the security and privacy focused VPN market include [Mullvad](wikipedia://en/56112003), [Proton VPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_VPN), [IVPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IVPN), and ultimately [Tor](wikipedia://en/20556944) VPN when it comes out of beta development and testing. NymVPN exceeds VPN service guidelines recommended by the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Frontier_Foundation) (EFF)[^o20] and the [Freedom of the Press Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_the_Press_Foundation).[^o21]

Disadvantages of the mixnet method include increased bandwidth and server load with consequently greater [energy usage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IT_energy_management).[^o22] Although connection [speeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_throughput) have increased significantly, there are noticeable [latency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_delay) issues that aren't easily fully resolved and can impact certain users.[^o6] Unlike some other VPN providers, Nym Tech has not announced specific plans to address [post-quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/26605226) but has said it is working on the issue and intends to not rush an implementation prematurely.[^o23] Mixnet compute paths and nodes could potentially be compromised.[^o24][^o25] NymVPN specifically and mixnets generally are also [emerging technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_technologies) with shorter windows of time that have undergone testing, and at lesser scale, although Nym undergoes security reviews and is FOSS.
