# Joint source and channel coding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joint source and channel coding"}

In [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773), **joint source–channel coding** is the encoding of a redundant [information source](wikipedia://en/18909947) for transmission over a [noisy channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noisy_channel), and the corresponding [decoding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code), using a single code instead of the more conventional steps of [source coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_coding) followed by [channel coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_coding).

:::

Joint source–channel coding has been proposed and implemented for a variety of situations, including speech and videotransmission.[^o2][^o3]
