# FROSTBURG

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FROSTBURG"}

*Image: Frostburg.jpg.* FROSTBURG on display at the [National Cryptologic Museum](wikipedia://en/734289). The light panels were used to check the usage of the processing nodes, and to run diagnostics.

**FROSTBURG** was a [Connection Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_Machine) 5 (CM-5) [massively parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massively_parallel_%28computing%29) [supercomputer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercomputer) used by the US [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939) (NSA) to perform mathematical calculations. The CM-5 was built by the [Thinking Machines Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Machines_Corporation), based in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), at a cost of [US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US$)25 million. The system was installed at NSA in 1991, and operated until 1997.[^o2] It was the first massively [parallel processing computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) bought by NSA, originally containing 256 processing nodes. The system was upgraded in 1993 with another 256 nodes, for a total of 512 nodes.[^o3] The system had a total of 500 billion 32-bit words (≈ 2 terabytes) of [storage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_data_storage), 2.5 billion words (≈ 10 gigabytes) of [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_memory), and could perform at a theoretical maximum 65.5 giga[FLOPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FLOPS). The operating system *CMost* was based on [Unix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix), but optimized for parallel processing.

:::

FROSTBURG is now on display at the [National Cryptologic Museum](wikipedia://en/734289).[^o4]
