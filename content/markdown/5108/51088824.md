# Olimpíada de Matemática do Grande ABC

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olimpíada de Matemática do Grande ABC"}

The **Olimpíada de Matemática do Grande ABC** (English:*Grande ABC Mathematical Olympiad*), or **OMABC** is a mathematical competition for pre-[collegiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) Brazilian students of [Grande ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande_ABC) region, composed by the following cities:[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

- *Image: BandeiraSantoAndre.svg.* [Santo André](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_André_%28São_Paulo%29)
- *Image: Bandeira de São Caetano do Sul.svg.* [São Caetano do Sul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Caetano_do_Sul)
- *Image: Bandeira\_de\_S%C3%A3o\_Bernardo\_do\_Campo.jpg.* [São Bernardo do Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Bernardo_do_Campo)
- *Image: Bandeira\_de\_Diadema-SP,\_Brasil.png.* [Diadema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diadema,_São_Paulo)
- *Image: Bandeira\_Maua\_SaoPaulo\_Brasil.svg.* [Mauá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauá)
- *Image: Bandeira-ribeiraopires.jpg.* [Ribeirão Pires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribeirão_Pires)
- *Image: Bandeira-riograndedaserra.png.* [Rio Grande da Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Grande_da_Serra)

The Faculdade de Ciências Exatas e Tecnológicas da [Universidade Metodista de São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_Metodista_de_São_Paulo) is the main organizer of this event, who create the tests and correct then. The main purpose of this olympiad is improve the mathematical knowledge, encouraging the study and research in [scientific areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science).,[^o5] and contributing to participate in national mathematical competitions, like [Olimpíada Brasileira de Matemática das Escolas Públicas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olimpíada_Brasileira_de_Matemática_das_Escolas_Públicas) and [Olimpíada Brasileira de Matemática](wikipedia://en/17667262). The first edition was held in 2004.

## Awards {#Awards}

### Students {#Students}

The participants are ranked based on their individual scores. Medals are awarded to the highest ranked participants, such that slightly less than half of them receive a medal. Subsequently, the cutoffs (minimum scores required to receive a gold, silver or bronze medal respectively) are chosen such that the ratio of gold to silver to bronze medals awarded approximates 1 : 2 : 3.[^o8]

- *Image: Gold medal.svg.* **[Gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_medal)**
- *Image: Silver medal.svg.* **[Silver medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_medal)**
- *Image: Bronze medal.svg.* **[Bronze medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_medal)**

### Schools {#Schools}

Special prizes are awarded for the schools:[^o10]

- *Image: Trophy.jpg.* **[Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trophy)**: For the schools whose students received at least a golden medal.
- **[Honorable Mention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorable_Mention)**: For the schools at least one student received an award.
