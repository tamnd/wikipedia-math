# Bayesian survival analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bayesian survival analysis"}

[Survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259) is normally carried out using [parametric models](wikipedia://en/2883137), [semi-parametric models](wikipedia://en/4415208), [non-parametric models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-parametric_model) to estimate the [survival rate](wikipedia://en/6641085) in clinical research. However recently Bayesian models[^o2] are also used to estimate the [survival rate](wikipedia://en/6641085) due to their ability to handle design and analysis issues in clinical research.[^o3]

:::
