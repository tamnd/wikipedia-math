# Gautam Kamath

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gautam Kamath"}

**Gautam Kamath** is a computer scientist known for his work in [algorithmic robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) and [differential privacy](wikipedia://en/23473696). He is an assistant professor at the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), a faculty member at the [Vector Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_Institute_%28Canada%29), and a Canada CIFAR AI Chair.[^o3] He received the 2026 [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize) and the 2026 [Presburger Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presburger_Award).[^o9][^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kamath received a Bachelor of Science from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 2012, and a Master of Science (2014) and PhD (2018) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o5] He was a Microsoft Research Fellow at the [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Institute_for_the_Theory_of_Computing) before joining the University of Waterloo as an assistant professor in 2019.[^o6] He is expected to join the Computer Science department at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in September 2026.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Kamath's research concerns algorithms and machine learning under constraints such as data privacy and robustness.[^o3]

With Ilias Diakonikolas, [Daniel Kane](wikipedia://en/12013216), Jerry Li, [Ankur Moitra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankur_Moitra), and Alistair Stewart, Kamath co-authored the paper "Robust Estimators in High-Dimensions without the Computational Intractability", first presented at the 2016 [Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Foundations_of_Computer_Science) and published in the *[SIAM Journal on Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Computing)* in 2019.[^o9] The paper gave the first polynomial-time algorithms for high-dimensional estimation in the presence of adversarially corrupted data with error guarantees independent of the dimension, resolving a longstanding problem in robust statistics.[^o9] The work is credited with establishing the area of algorithmic high-dimensional robust statistics.[^o10]

With Clément Canonne and Thomas Steinke, Kamath developed the discrete Gaussian mechanism for differential privacy, presented at [NeurIPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NeurIPS) 2020, which was deployed in the [2020 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize), 2026, with Ilias Diakonikolas, Daniel Kane, Jerry Li, Ankur Moitra, and Alistair Stewart, for "Robust Estimators in High-Dimensions without the Computational Intractability"[^o9]
- [Presburger Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presburger_Award) of the [European Association for Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_for_Theoretical_Computer_Science), 2026[^o7]
- Caspar Bowden Award for Outstanding Research in Privacy Enhancing Technologies, 2024, with Clément Canonne and Thomas Steinke, for "The Discrete Gaussian for Differential Privacy"[^o11]
- Best paper award, [International Conference on Machine Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Conference_on_Machine_Learning) (ICML), 2024, with Florian Tramèr and Nicholas Carlini[^o13]
- Canada CIFAR AI Chair[^o3]
