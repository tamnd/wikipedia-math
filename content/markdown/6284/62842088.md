# Barbara Kaltenbacher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Kaltenbacher"}

**Barbara Kaltenbacher** is an Austrian mathematician whose research concerns [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956), [regularization](wikipedia://en/2009061), and [PDE-constrained optimization](wikipedia://en/63526503), with applications including the mathematical modeling of [piezoelectricity](wikipedia://en/24975) and [nonlinear acoustics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_acoustics). She is a professor of Applied Analysis at the [University of Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Klagenfurt), a member of the executive committee of the European Mathematical Society and was editor in chief of the *[Journal of the European Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_European_Mathematical_Society)*.. Barbara Kaltenbacher has published more than 130 scientific papers and is (co-)author of four monographs.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kaltenbacher studied mathematics at [Johannes Kepler University Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kepler_University_Linz), earning a diploma in 1993 and a doctorate in 1996. Her dissertation, *Some Newton type methods for the regularization of nonlinear ill-posed problems*, was supervised by [Heinz Engl](wikipedia://en/45470428). She was head of a Hertha Firnberg Project funded by the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund) FWF 1999-2001 and an [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Junior Research group funded by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) DFG 2003–2006. After taking temporary professor positions at the [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg), and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), she became a professor of Optimization at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) in 2006. She moved to the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz) in 2010 as a professor of Applied Mathematics, and to her present position in Klagenfurt in 2011. Barbara Kaltenbacher was president of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society), 2018–2021, chair of the scientific advisory board of the [Weierstrass Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute), 2015–2021, and was elected as a corresponding member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences), in 2021.

## Books {#Books}

Barbara Kaltenbacher is (co-)author of:

- *Iterative Regularization Methods for Nonlinear Ill-Posed Problems* (with Andreas Neubauer and Otmar Scherzer, de Gruyter, 2008)
- *Regularization Methods in Banach Spaces* (with Thomas Schuster, Bernd Hofmann, and Kamil S. Kazimierskide, Gruyter, 2012)
- *Mathematical Theory of Evolutionary Fluid-Flow Structure Interactions* (with Igor Kukavica, [Irena Lasiecka](wikipedia://en/44759993), Roberto Triggiani, Amjad Tuffaha, and Justin T. Webster, Birkhäuser, 2018)
- *Inverse Problems for Fractional Partial Differential Equations* (with William Rundell). AMS, Graduate Studies in Mathematics, 2023
