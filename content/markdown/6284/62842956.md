# Charles-Michel Marle

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charles-Michel Marle"}

**Charles-Michel Marle** (; born 26 November 1934) is a French [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently a Professor Emeritus at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) (formerly [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University)).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Charles-Michel Marle completed in 1951 his primary and secondary education in [Constantine, Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine,_Algeria). He was a pupil of the [preparatory classes for the grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classe_préparatoire_aux_grandes_écoles) at the Lycée Émir-Abdelkader in [Algiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algiers): Mathématiques supérieures in 1951-1952, then Mathématiques spéciales in 1952-1953. He was admitted to the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 1953. When he left this school in 1955, he opted for the [Corps des mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_des_mines).

He did his military service as a [sub-lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-lieutenant) at the Engineering School in [Angers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angers) from October 1955 to February 1956, then in Algeria during the [war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_War) until 30 December 1956.

In 1957 he began attending the [École Nationale Supérieure des Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mines_Paris_–_PSL) in Paris and from October 1957 to September 1958 he attended the [École nationale supérieure du pétrole et des moteurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Nationale_Supérieure_du_Pétrole_et_des_Moteurs) and completed various internships in the oil industry in France and Algeria. Returning to the [École](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) des mines in October 1958, his last year of study was interrupted in 1959 by the decision of the Minister of Industry, to send all junior civil servants of category A to Algeria to participate in the [Constantine Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_Plan). He was then attached to the short-lived Organisation commune des régions sahariennes and worked in Algiers, the [Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara) and Paris on various industrial projects.

Between 1959 and 1969 he was seconded by the Corps des Mines to the [French Institute of Petroleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Institute_of_Petroleum) (IFP), where he was research engineer, head of department and director of division. While working at this Institute he obtained a degree in mathematics and in 1968 he defended a [doctoral thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_thesis) under the supervision of [André Lichnerowicz](wikipedia://en/2389188).[^o5]

In 1969 he changed his career and entered higher education, becoming a lecturer at the [University of Besançon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Franche-Comté). In 1975 he moved to [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris, and was appointed professor at this university in 1977. In 1983 he was elected corresponding member of the [French Academy of sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] In 1989, he was appointed visiting professor at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), for a semester of scientific courses, collaborating in particular with professors [Jerrold E. Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerrold_E._Marsden) and [Alan Weinstein](wikipedia://en/7344764). He retired in September 2000 and since then he is [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).

Charles-Michel Marle is the great-great-grandson of the grammarian L. C. Marle (1799-1860), author of an attempt at [spelling reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reforms_of_French_orthography) around 1840.

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

While working at the French Institute of Petroleum, Marle's research focused on fluid flows in [porous media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous_medium), which are being investigated for applications in [hydrocarbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrocarbon) field development.[^o8][^o9] He also published a book on the subject, developing a course that he taught at the École nationale du pétrole et des moteurs.[^o10]

Transitioning from applied to pure mathematics, in his PhD thesis he worked on [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and the [Boltzmann relativistic equation](wikipedia://en/1026522).[^o11][^o12]

Since the early 1970s he has worked mainly in the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), notably on [Hamiltonian group actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_group_action)[^o13][^o14] and [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry),[^o15][^o16] and its applications to [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics).[^o17][^o18][^o19]

With his colleague [Paulette Libermann](wikipedia://en/46726637) (1919-2007) he published in 1987 a research-level book on [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [geometric mechanics](wikipedia://en/41499924).[^o20] He has recently published another book, taking up part of the previous one, exposing recent results obtained in this field since 1987.[^o21]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- French Academy of sciences laboratory prize for his thesis work, 1973.
- Member of the [Mathematical Society of France](wikipedia://en/1691205),[^o23] the [French Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Française_de_Physique)[^o24] and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o25]
