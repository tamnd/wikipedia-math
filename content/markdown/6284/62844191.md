# Dimitri Leemans

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dimitri Leemans"}

**Dimitri Leemans** (born 1972) is a Belgian mathematician born in [Uccle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uccle).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Leemans obtained his Licence en Sciences Mathématiques at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) in 1994 and his doctorate degree, under the supervision of [Francis Buekenhout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Buekenhout) and Michel Dehon in 1998.[^o4]

He is currently professor at the mathematics department of the Faculty of Sciences of the Université libre de Bruxelles.[^o5] He lived five years in [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand) from 2011 until 2016 where he worked for the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland) as an associate professor. He returned to Belgium after experiencing problems with Immigration New Zealand because of his stepson's autism.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Leemans's principal area of research is at the interface of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He has made major contributions in the study of regular and [chiral polytopes](wikipedia://en/20861435) whose [automorphism groups](wikipedia://en/56661772) are finite almost simple groups. He has published more than 90 papers in peer-reviewed scientific journals and two memoirs of the [Academie Royale des Sciences de Belgique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academies_for_Science_and_the_Arts_of_Belgium).[^o13] He is the developer of the [Magma (computer algebra system)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magma_%28computer_algebra_system%29) package on [incidence geometry](wikipedia://en/1576323) and [coset](wikipedia://en/98759) geometry since 1999.[^o14]
