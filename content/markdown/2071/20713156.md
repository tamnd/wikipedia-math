# Daniel Hershkowitz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Hershkowitz"}

**Daniel Hershkowitz** (דניאל הרשקוביץ; born 2 January 1953 in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), Israel) is an Israeli politician, [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthodox_Judaism) [rabbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi). Since 2018, he has headed the Israel Civil Service Commission. He is [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [the Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Technion), and is also rabbi of the [Ahuza neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa#Neighborhoods) in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa). He was president of [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) from 2013-17.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Hershkowitz was born in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa). His parents were [Holocaust survivors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_survivors) from [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary), and his father was wounded in the [1948 Arab-Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab-Israeli_War). Hershkowitz studied at a religious high school, and graduated at age 16. He served for five years in the [Intelligence Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Corps_%28Israel%29) of the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces), reaching the rank of Major.

Hershkowitz earned his BSc in mathematics in 1973, MSc in 1976, and DSc in 1982, all from the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology). His [yeshiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva) [studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torah_study) were conducted at [Mercaz HaRav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercaz_HaRav); he received his *[Semikha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semikha)* (ordination) in 1995 from Rabbis [She'ar Yashuv Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She'ar_Yashuv_Cohen), Shlomo Chelouche, and Nehemyah Roth, as well as an additional ordination ["Rabbi of the City"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Rabbinate_of_Israel#Semikhah) from the [Chief Rabbinate of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Rabbinate_of_Israel) (2001).[^o7]

## Academia {#Academia}

He has published over 80 mathematics articles in [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal). He was President of the *[International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149)*[^o5] (2002-2008[^o6]), and was previously a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).[^o9][^o7] In 1982, he was awarded the Landau Research Prize in Mathematics; in 1990, the New England Academic Award for Excellence in Research; in 1990, the Technion's Award for Excellence in Teaching; and in 1991, the Henri Gutwirth Award for Promotion of Research.[^o9]

## Political career {#Political_career}

In 2009, he was elected to the [Knesset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knesset) as the leader of [the Jewish Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jewish_Home), and was appointed [Minister of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_Technology_Minister_of_Israel) after joining [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu)'s [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty-second_government_of_Israel).[^o9][^o10][^o11] He did not contest the [2013 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Israeli_legislative_election), and subsequently left the Knesset. Since September 2018, he is the Head of the Civil Service Commission under the office of the Prime Minister of Israel.

## Bar-Ilan University {#Bar-Ilan_University}

He was president of [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) from 2013 to 2017, succeeding [Moshe Kaveh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Kaveh) and followed by [Arie Zaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arie_Zaban).[^o9][^o13][^o14]
