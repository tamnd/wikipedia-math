# Humbert surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Humbert surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Humbert surface**, studied by (txt), is a [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) in the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of principally polarized [abelian surfaces](wikipedia://en/974181) consisting of the surfaces with a symmetric [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) of some fixed [discriminant](wikipedia://en/55607).

:::
