# Inoue–Hirzebruch surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Inoue–Hirzebruch surface"}

In mathematics, a **Inoue–Hirzebruch** surface is a [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) with no [meromorphic functions](wikipedia://en/80857) introduced by (Inoue 1977). They have [Kodaira dimension](wikipedia://en/1866873) κ = −∞, and are non-algebraic [surfaces of class VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_class_VII) with positive second [Betti number](wikipedia://en/346262). (Sankaran 1987) studied some higher-dimensional analogues.

:::
