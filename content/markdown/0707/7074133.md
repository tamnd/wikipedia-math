# Mikhail Goussarov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Goussarov"}

**Mikhail Goussarov** (March 8, 1958, [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad) – June 25, 1999, [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774). He and [Victor Vassiliev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Anatolyevich_Vassiliev) independently discovered [finite type invariants](wikipedia://en/3014395) of [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_knot) and links. He drowned at the age of 41 in an accident in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).
