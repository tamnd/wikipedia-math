# List of impossible puzzles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of impossible puzzles"}

Lists This is a list of [puzzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle) that cannot be solved. An impossible puzzle is a puzzle that cannot be resolved, either due to lack of sufficient information, or any number of [logical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical) impossibilities.

:::

::: {#o2 .entry cue=list-entry name="15 Puzzle"}

- [15 Puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15_Puzzle) – Slide fifteen numbered tiles into numerical order. It is impossible to solve in half of the starting positions.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Five room puzzle"}

- [Five room puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_room_puzzle) – Cross each wall of a diagram exactly once with a continuous line.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="MU puzzle"}

- [MU puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MU_puzzle) – Transform the string MI to MU according to a set of rules.[^o7]

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mutilated chessboard problem"}

- [Mutilated chessboard problem](wikipedia://en/9308202) – Place 31 dominoes of size 2×1 on a chessboard with two opposite corners removed.[^o9]

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Petersen graph"}

- Coloring the edges of the [Petersen graph](wikipedia://en/194926#Coloring) with three colors.[^o11]

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Seven Bridges of Königsberg"}

- [Seven Bridges of Königsberg](wikipedia://en/192753) – Walk through a city while crossing each of seven bridges exactly once.[^o13]

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Squaring the circle"}

- [Squaring the circle](wikipedia://en/201359), the impossible problem of constructing a square with the same area as a given circle, using only a [compass and straightedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_and_straightedge).[^o15]

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Three cups problem"}

- [Three cups problem](wikipedia://en/9078218) – Turn three cups right-side up after starting with one wrong and turning two at a time.[^o17]

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Three utilities problem"}

- [Three utilities problem](wikipedia://en/58862) – Connect three cottages to gas, water, and electricity without crossing lines.[^o19]

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Thirty-six officers problem"}

- [Thirty-six officers problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty-six_officers_problem) – Arrange six regiments consisting of six officers each of different ranks in a 6 × 6 square so that no rank or regiment is repeated in any row or column.[^o21]

:::
