# Sean Murphy (cryptographer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sean Murphy"}

**Sean Murphy** is a cryptographer, currently a professor at [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London). He worked on the [NESSIE](wikipedia://en/497430) and [ECRYPT](wikipedia://en/1635837) projects. His notable research includes the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of [FEAL](wikipedia://en/592532) and the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260), and the use of [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) and [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685) techniques in [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology). With [Donald Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Davies) he also developed [Davies' attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davies'_attack) on [DES](wikipedia://en/7978).

:::

Murphy received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1989 from the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath).
