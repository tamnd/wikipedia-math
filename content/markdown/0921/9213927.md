# Matt Robshaw

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matt Robshaw"}

**Matthew John Barton "Matt" Robshaw** is a cryptographer. Formerly a lecturer at [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London) and a member of the cryptography research group at [France Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_Telecom)'s Orange Labs, he is now a Technical Fellow at [Impinj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impinj). He coordinated the Symmetric Techniques Virtual Lab (STVL) for [ECRYPT](wikipedia://en/1635837). Robshaw's notable work includes the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of a number of [cryptographic primitives](wikipedia://en/4928985), including the extension of [linear cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/59469) to use multiple approximations, and the design of the [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) [Crab](wikipedia://en/7866313) and [RC6](wikipedia://en/160216).

:::

Robshaw received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1992 from [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London).
