# Gyula O. H. Katona

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gyula O. H. Katona"}

*Image: Gyula Katona.jpg.* Gyula Katona

**Gyula O. H. Katona** (born 16 March 1941 in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in combinatorial set theory, and especially for the [Kruskal–Katona theorem](wikipedia://en/1536947)[^o2] and his beautiful and elegant proof of the [Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem](wikipedia://en/296188) in which he discovered a new method, now called [Katona's cycle method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katona's_cycle_method).[^o3] Since then, this method has become a powerful tool in proving many interesting results in [extremal set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremal_set_theory). He is affiliated with the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

**Katona** was secretary-general of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) from 1990 to 1996. In 1966 and 1968 he won the [Grünwald Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grünwald_Prize), awarded by the Bolyai Society to outstanding young mathematicians, he was awarded the [Alfréd Rényi Prize](wikipedia://en/26466805) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1975, and the same academy awarded him the Prize of the Academy in 1989. In 2011 the Alfréd Rényi Institute, the János Bolyai Society and the Hungarian Academy of Sciences organized a conference in honor of Katona's 70th birthday.[^o4]

Gyula O.H. Katona is the father of [Gyula Y. Katona](wikipedia://en/9214056), another Hungarian mathematician with similar research interests to those of his father. His youger son is Zsolt Katona is a professor at University of California, Berkeley[^o5].
