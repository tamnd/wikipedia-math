# Carlisle Adams

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Carlisle Adams"}

**Carlisle M. Adams** is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) cryptographer and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researcher. Formerly senior cryptographer at [Entrust](wikipedia://en/16088938),[^o3] he is currently a professor at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa). His notable work includes the design (with [Stafford Tavares](wikipedia://en/9546396)) of the [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) [CAST-128](wikipedia://en/240998) and [CAST-256](wikipedia://en/612143), whose [S-boxes](wikipedia://en/240991) are based on the non-linear properties of [bent functions](wikipedia://en/24465207). He also helped organize the first [Selected Areas in Cryptography](wikipedia://en/9545951) (SAC) workshop in 1994. He is also the security advisor of the [Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa)-based [electronic signature](wikipedia://en/627040) company [Signority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signority).[^o4]

:::
