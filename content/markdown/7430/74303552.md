# Carl-Erik Sjöstedt

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Carl-Erik Sjöstedt"}

**Carl-Erik Sjöstedt** (born 1900, died 1979) was a Swedish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher), and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy). Sjöstedt focused much of his work on the teaching of mathematics in the Swedish [curriculum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum), especially in the fields of geometry and logic. Sjöstedt was a follower of the Swedish philosopher [Adolf Phalén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Phalén), and a supporter of *Uppsala philosophy*. He also was a speaker and author in the [constructed language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructed_language) [Interlingue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlingue), founding a national association for the language in 1928.

:::

## Life {#Life}

### Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sjöstedt was born on 31 July 1900 to [foreman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supervisor) Karl August Sjöstedt and Matilda Åström in [Eskilstuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eskilstuna) [parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parish), [Södermanland County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Södermanland_County), Sweden. He began his education in the city, graduating in 1919. Sjöstedt would complete the rest of his education at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), gaining a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) (filosofie kandidat) in 1921, a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in 1927, and becoming a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1930. His thesis for the latter was [defended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis_defence) in 1929 and was on the subject of geometry.[^n1]

During his research, Sjöstedt met philosopher [Adolf Phalén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Phalén), who would remain a friend of Sjöstedt's and influence his philosophical theories; Sjöstedt presented Phalén with a [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) on the [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology) of geometry, which would be factored into a memorial to him in 1937. During the 1930s, Phalén, along with a group of other philosophers including Sjöstedt held private meetings to discuss the place of philosophy as a subject within the [education system of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Sweden).[^n2] After Phalén's death, Sjöstedt collected much of Phalén's unfinished and unpublished work, mainly concerning the history of the [theory of knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Knowledge), and published it under the title *Mindre Arbeten* (*Minor Works*)*.*[^o7] Throughout his life, like Phalén, Sjöstedt was a proponent of Uppsalafilosofin (Uppsala philosophy), countering opposing philosophies such as those of [Hans Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Larsson).[^o8]

### Career {#Career}

Sjöstedt worked as a teacher for much of his career, teaching at various [private school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_school) until 1931, before lecturing in mathematics at the Jämtlands Gymnasium Wargentin (then the *Högre allmänna läroverket i Östersund;* Higher General Education Agency in Östersund). In 1939, Sjöstedt acted as [headmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headmaster) at the Läroverk (form of Swedish secondary school, similar to a [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29)) in [Borås](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borås), [Västergötland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Västergötland). After this, Sjöstedt was *Undervisningsråd* (counsel of [pedagogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedagogy)) of the *Skolöverstyrelsen* (former Swedish Board of Education) from 1940 until 1962.[^n2]

Sjöstedt was an opponent of the 1950s movement to introduce [comprehensive schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_school), calling the movement "an educational utopia of colossal proportions"; his opposition was successful, as the 1962 Education Act replaced Sweden's previous schooling system which he had supported.[^o11] Sjöstedt later lead an inquiry into the role of [vocational schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocational_school), supporting them as a default, as opposed to [Upper Secondary Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Secondary_School), but later resigned from the position.

### Personal life {#Personal_life}

Aside from work in pedagogy and mathematics, Sjöstedt was a supporter of the [international auxiliary language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_auxiliary_language) [Interlingue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlingue) (then known as Occidental), created by the Estonian [Edgar de Wahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_de_Wahl). Founding the Sved Occidental-Federation (Swedish Occidental-Federation) in 1928,[^o13] Sjöstedt published several books in the language throughout his life. Sjöstedt published many articles in the organisation's magazine, *Li Sved Occidentalist* (*The Swedish Occidentalist*), as well as the magazine [Cosmoglotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmoglotta).

Sjöstedt married Svea Augusta Malmberg on 7 July 1927,[^n2] and died on 8 February 1979 in the parish of [Uppsala Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_Cathedral), [Uppsala County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_County), aged 78.[^n1]

## Works {#Works}

Sjöstedt wrote several [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) about geometry, publishing courses for high schools in 1936 and 1938, as well as a series of other textbooks through the 1950s. Sjöstedt also wrote for the Swedish journal [Natur & Kultur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natur_&_Kultur) from 1947. Aside from these, Sjöstedt also published several books about geometry theory and philosophy, including *De filosofiska problemens historia* (*The history of [philosophical problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophical_problems)*) in 1936, for use in teaching high school philosophy, alongside *Översikt av den formella logiken* (*An overview of [formal logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_logic)*).[^n2] Other works included:

- *Occidental - de internatuinella ordens språk,* 1928
- *Dubbel Occidental*-*Ordbok*, 1930 (in Occidental, Swedish)
- *Projektiv geometri,* 1932, second edition published 1947
- *Geometriens axiomsystem*, 1937
- *Zur Erkenntnistheorie der Geometrie,* 1937 (in German)
- *Icke-euklidisk geometri*, 1945
- *Filosofisk läsebok*, 1950
- *Le Axiome de Paralleles de Euclides a Hilbert*, a mathematical encyclopaedia of around 1000 pages; 1968 (in Occidental)
- *Vocabularie mathematic in interlingue: Con traduction in angles, frances e german*, 1970; published in [Natur och Kultur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natur_och_Kultur). (in Occidental, English, French, and German)
