# Head count ratio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Head count ratio"}

*Image: South Africa Headcount Index, First Tier Cities (5457977904).jpg.* Head count ratio in [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa)

The **head count ratio** (**HCR**) is the [population proportion](wikipedia://en/49489200) that exists, or lives, below the [poverty threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_threshold).[^o2] One of the undesirable features of the head count ratio is that it ignores the depth of [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty); if the poor become poorer, the head count index does not change.[^o3] For example, the poverty head count ratio at national poverty line (percentage of population) in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India) was last reported at 21.9% in 2011.[^o4] In July 2012, *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times)* reported the poverty head count ratio as 11.1% of the population of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1973, 15.2% in 1983 and 11.3% in 2000.[^o5]

:::
