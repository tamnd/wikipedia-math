# Eugen Netto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eugen Netto"}

*Image: Eugen Netto.jpeg.* Eugen Netto

**Eugen Otto Erwin Netto** (30 June 1848 – 13 May 1919) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle,_Saxony-Anhalt) and died in [Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giessen).[^o2]

:::

[Netto's theorem](wikipedia://en/70997704), on the dimension-preserving properties of continuous [bijections](wikipedia://en/3942), is named for Netto. Netto published this theorem in 1878, in response to [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216)'s proof of the existence of discontinuous bijections between the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324) and [unit square](wikipedia://en/285301). His proof was not fully rigorous, but its errors were later repaired.[^o4]
