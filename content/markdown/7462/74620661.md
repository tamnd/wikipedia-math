# Goldine Gleser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Goldine Gleser"}

**Goldine C. Gleser** (1915 – 2004) was an American psychologist and statistician known for her research on the statistics of [psychological testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_testing), on [generalizability theory](wikipedia://en/7914038), on [defence mechanisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_mechanism), on the psychological effects on child survivors of the [Buffalo Creek flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_Creek_flood), for her work with [Mildred Trotter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mildred_Trotter) on [estimation of stature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estimation_of_stature), and for her participation in the [Cincinnati Radiation Experiments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_Radiation_Experiments). She was a professor of psychiatry and psychology at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gleser was originally from [St. Louis, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis,_Missouri). She studied mathematics at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), graduating [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa) in 1935 and earning a master's degree in 1936. Although she was working towards a doctorate in mathematics, she interrupted her studies to marry a civil engineer, and later switched to psychology, completing a Ph.D. at Washington University in 1950.

## Career {#Career}

Gleser began part-time work at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati) in 1956, and in 1964 became a full professor of psychiatry and psychology. She was director of the university's psychology division beginning in 1967, and chief outpatient psychologist at [Cincinnati General Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_General_Hospital) from 1968 to 1972. Glesner was also a visiting professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University).[^o5]

In 1974, Gleser was elected as a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association). She was also a fellow of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association), the [American College of Neuropsychopharmacology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_College_of_Neuropsychopharmacology), a member of the [New York Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Academy_of_Sciences), a trustee of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500), and president of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) for 1977–1978.
