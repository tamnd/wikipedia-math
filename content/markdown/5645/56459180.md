# Kurt Strebel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kurt Strebel"}

**Kurt Strebel** (20 April 1921 – 26 October 2013) was a Swiss mathematician, specializing in [geometric function theory](wikipedia://en/5458932).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Strebel was born on 20 April 1921 in [Wohlen, Aargau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wohlen,_Aargau). received in 1953 his PhD from the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) under [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna) with thesis *Über das Kreisnormierungsproblem der konformen Abbildung* (On the circle normalization problem of conformal mapping). From 1953 to 1955 he was at the Institute for Advanced Study and at Stanford University. He became a professor at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) in 1955 and then successor to Nevanlinna at the University of Zurich in 1963.

Strebel founded the Nevanlinna Colloquium in Zürich (later also elsewhere) with another of Nevanlinna's former students, the professor Hans Künzi, to maintain contacts with Nevanlinna.[^o4] The Nevanlinna Colloquium is usually held in Europe and covers most of classical complex analysis.[^o5]

In 1977 Strebel was elected a member of the [Finnish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Sciences). He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1974 in Vancouver.[^o6]

He died on 26 October 2013 in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich). Named in his honor is the Strebel differential in the [Teichmüller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_theory).

## Strebel differential {#Strebel_differential}

Definition A [meromorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meromorphic) [quadratic differential](wikipedia://en/2610010) is a Strebel differential if the union of its [noncompact](wikipedia://en/6042) [leaves](wikipedia://en/371227) forms a set of [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) 0.[^o8]
