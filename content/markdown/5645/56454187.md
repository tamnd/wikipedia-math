# Bundle of principal parts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bundle of principal parts"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), given a [line bundle](wikipedia://en/361366) *L* on a [smooth variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) *X*, the **bundle of *n*-th order principal parts** of *L* is a vector bundle of rank $\tbinom{n+\text{dim}(X)}{n}$ that, roughly, parametrizes *n*-th order Taylor expansions of sections of *L*.

:::

Precisely, let *I* be the ideal sheaf defining the [diagonal embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagonal_embedding) $X \hookrightarrow X \times X$ and $p, q: V(I^{n+1}) \to X$ the restrictions of projections $X \times X \to X$ to $V(I^{n+1}) \subset X \times X$. Then the bundle of *n*-th order principal parts is[^o2]

$$P^n(L) = p_* q^* L.$$

Then $P^0(L) = L$ and there is a natural [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215) of vector bundles[^o3]

$$0 \to \mathrm{Sym}^n(\Omega_X) \otimes L \to P^n(L) \to P^{n-1}(L) \to 0.$$

where $\Omega_X$ is the sheaf of differential one-forms on *X*.
