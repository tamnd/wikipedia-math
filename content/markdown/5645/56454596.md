# Carl Posy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Posy"}

**Carl Jeffrey Posy** (קרל פוזי) is an Israeli philosopher. He is a full professor [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in the Department of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem), Israel.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Carl Posy received his [PhD degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD_degree) from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1971.[^o3]

## Publications {#Publications}

Posy's areas of research interest include the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939), the [history of philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_philosophy) (particularly [Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kant) and his predecessors).[^o3] His publications include:

- *Kant’s Philosophy of Mathematics: Modern Essays*, [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_%28publisher%29), 1992.
- *Computability: [Turing](wikipedia://en/1208), [Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736), [Church](wikipedia://en/49731), and Beyond* (with [Jack Copeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Copeland) and [Oron Shagrir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oron_Shagrir)), [MIT Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Press), 2013. .[^o5]

Posy is a former Academic Director of the [National Library of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Israel).[^o6][^o7][^o8]
