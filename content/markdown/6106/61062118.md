# Amanda Chetwynd

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amanda Chetwynd"}

**Amanda G. Chetwynd** is a British mathematician and statistician specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349). She is Professor of Mathematics and Statistics and Provost for Student Experience, Colleges and the Library at [Lancaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_University), and a Principal Fellow of the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy).

:::

## Education and research {#Education_and_research}

Chetwynd earned a Ph.D. from the [Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_University) in 1985. Her dissertation, *Edge-colourings of graphs*, was jointly supervised by [Anthony Hilton](wikipedia://en/42746762) and [Robin Wilson](wikipedia://en/2715277). She did postdoctoral research at the [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) before joining Lancaster University. Her research interests include [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [edge coloring](wikipedia://en/690647), and [latin squares](wikipedia://en/37447) in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), as well as [geographical clustering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographical_cluster) in [medical statistics](wikipedia://en/11082896).

## Recognition and service {#Recognition_and_service}

In 2003, Chetwynd won a National Teaching Fellowship recognizing her teaching excellence. She was vice president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2005, at a time when university study of mathematics was shrinking, and as vice president encouraged the UK government to counter the decline by providing more funds for mathematics education.

## Books {#Books}

With [Peter Diggle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Diggle), Chetwynd is the author of the books *Discrete Mathematics* (Modular Mathematics series, Arnold, 1995) and *Statistics and Scientific Method: An Introduction for Students and Researchers* (Oxford University Press, 2011). With Bob Burn she is the author of *A Cascade of Numbers: An Introduction to Number Theory* (Arnold, 1995).
