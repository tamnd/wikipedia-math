# Buckmaster equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Buckmaster equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Buckmaster equation** is a second-order nonlinear [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564), named after [John D. Buckmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Buckmaster), who derived the equation in 1977.[^o2] The equation models the surface of a thin sheet of viscous liquid. The equation was derived earlier by S. H. Smith and by P Smith,[^o3][^o4] but these earlier derivations focused on the steady version of the equation.

:::

The Buckmaster equation is

$$u_t = (u^4)_{xx} + \lambda (u^3)_x$$

where $\lambda$ is a known parameter.
