# Robert Daverman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Daverman"}

**Robert Jay Daverman** (September 28, 1941 – February 9, 2026) was an American [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Daverman was born in [Grand Rapids, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Rapids,_Michigan), on September 28, 1941. He earned a bachelor's degree in 1963 from [Calvin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_College) and pursued doctoral study under [R. H. Bing](wikipedia://en/967353) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). After completing his thesis *Locally Fenced 2-spheres in S<sup>3</sup>* in 1967, Daverman began teaching at the [University of Tennessee–Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee–Knoxville).[^o3] While on the Knoxville faculty, Daverman served on the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)'s Committee on Science Policy.[^o4] By the time he was selected as one of the inaugural fellows of the AMS in 2012, Daverman had gained emeritus status.[^o5] Daverman died on February 9, 2026, at the age of 84.[^o6]
