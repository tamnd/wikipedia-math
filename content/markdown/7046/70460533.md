# Alison Ramage

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alison Ramage"}

**Alison Ramage** is a British [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) specialising in [preconditioning](wikipedia://en/5165246) methods for [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660), and their applications to the numerical solution of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). She is a reader in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ramage is a graduate of the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews). She completed her PhD in 1991 at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol). Her dissertation, *Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient Methods for Galerkin Finite Element Equations*, was supervised by Andrew Wathen, and involved preconditioners for the [Galerkin method](wikipedia://en/2664839).

She is a member of the board of trustees of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).

She currently serves as Chair of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) Board of Trustees.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

Ramage's thesis involved preconditioners for the [Galerkin method](wikipedia://en/2664839), and applications to the numerical solution of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) in [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924), including those for [incompressible flow](wikipedia://en/461477) and [advection–diffusion equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advection–diffusion_equation). Subsequently, she has applied her research to other areas including [geotechnical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geotechnical_engineering), [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), modeling [liquid crystals](wikipedia://en/17973), [weather forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting), and [sensor networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensor_network).
