# Nielsen realization problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nielsen realization problem"}

The **Nielsen realization problem** is a question asked by (txt, pp. 147–148) about whether finite subgroups of mapping class groups can act on surfaces, that was answered positively by (txt).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Given an oriented surface, we can divide the group Diff(*S*), the group of [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) of the surface to itself, into [isotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) classes to get the [mapping class group](wikipedia://en/793295) π<sub>0</sub>(Diff(*S*)). The conjecture asks whether a finite subgroup of the mapping class group of a surface can be realized as the isometry group of a hyperbolic metric on the surface.

The mapping class group acts on [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661). An equivalent way of stating the question asks whether every finite subgroup of the mapping class group fixes some point of Teichmüller space.

:::

## History {#History}

(txt, pp. 147–148) asked whether finite subgroups of mapping class groups can act on surfaces. (Kravetz 1959) claimed to solve the Nielsen realization problem but his proof depended on trying to show that [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661) (with the [Teichmüller metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_metric)) is negatively curved. (Linch 1971) pointed out a gap in the argument, and (Masur 1975) showed that Teichmüller space is not negatively curved. (txt) gave a correct proof that finite subgroups of mapping class groups can act on surfaces using [left earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_earthquake).
