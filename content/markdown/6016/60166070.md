# Ernesto Pascal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernesto Pascal"}

**Ernesto Pascal** (1865–1940) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Pascal graduated in mathematics from the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples) in 1887. In the following two years he attended courses in the universities of [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen); in the last one Pascal studied under [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) who influenced him.[^n1] From 1890 to 1907 he was teaching at the [university of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) and in 1907 he returned to the university of Naples where he taught until his death. Here, as Dean of the Faculty of Sciences he reorganised the teaching of mathematics, creating for each professorship a laboratory equipped with models and instruments.[^n2]

Pascal was remembered for his work on [elliptic functions](wikipedia://en/69939) based on [Jacobi theta function](wikipedia://en/449745#Jacobi_theta_function).[^n3]
