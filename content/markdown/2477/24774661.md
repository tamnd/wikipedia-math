# Geoffrey Hellman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Hellman"}

**Geoffrey Hellman** (born August 16, 1943[^o4]) is an American professor and philosopher. He is Professor of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in [Minneapolis, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis,_Minnesota).[^o5] He obtained his B.A. (1965) and Ph.D. (1972) degrees in philosophy from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2007.

:::
