# Guy Hirsch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guy Hirsch"}

**Guy Hirsch** (20 September 1915 – 4 August 1993)[^o3] was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and philosopher of mathematics, who worked on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology) of mathematics.

:::

He became a member of the [Royal Flemish Academy of Belgium for Science and the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Flemish_Academy_of_Belgium_for_Science_and_the_Arts) in 1973.

He is known for the [Leray–Hirsch theorem](wikipedia://en/8620449), a basic result on the algebraic topology of [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526) that he proved independently of [Jean Leray](wikipedia://en/245430) in the late 1940s.
