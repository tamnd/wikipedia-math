# Nathalie Sinclair

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nathalie Sinclair"}

**Nathalie Michelle Sinclair** (born 1970)[^o3] is a Canadian researcher in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) who holds the [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in Tangible Mathematics Learning at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver).[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sinclair was born in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble), the daughter of Canadian academics who were on sabbatical there; she grew up in [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary). She began her undergraduate studies at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in business, but quickly switched to mathematics, and then earned a master's degree with Len Berggren at Simon Fraser on the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and [mathematics in medieval Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_in_medieval_Islam).[^o6]

She became a middle school teacher of mathematics and French on [Bowen Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowen_Island).[^o6] Sinclair then earned her Ph.D. in 2002 from [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), under the joint supervision of Peter Taylor of the Department of Mathematics and Statistics and William Higginson of the Faculty of Education.

## Career {#Career}

*Image: Nathalie.sinclairRSC.SFU.jpg.* Sinclair in 2017

In 2003, Sinclair accepted a joint appointment in the College of Natural Sciences and the College of Education at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).[^o9]

In 2014, Sinclair and [Nicholas Jackiw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Jackiw) developed a mathematical app targeted at children ages three to eight. The purpose of the app is to teach children math through "hands-on" learning.[^o10] Two years later, she was named a Tier 2 [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in Tangible Mathematics Learning.[^o11]

In March 2017, Sinclair was named Canada's Mathematics Ambassador by Partners in Research for her contributions to the field of mathematics.[^o12] Later that year, Sinclair was elected a Member of the College of New Scholars, Artists and Scientists of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o13] In 2019, Sinclair was awarded the Svend Pedersen Lecture Award.[^o14]
