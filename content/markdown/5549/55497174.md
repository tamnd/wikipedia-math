# Ione Roseveare

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ione Roseveare"}

**Ione Roseveare** (née Katherine Ione Jay) (9 November 1920, [Weobley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weobley) – 24 September 2010, [Uppingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppingham)) was a Temporary Junior Administrative Officer at [Hut 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hut_6), [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037).[^o2] She met her husband [Bob Roseveare](wikipedia://en/9499962) while working there.

:::

Ione was studying for [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) of the [Royal Academy of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Music) in [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol) in 1941. However she was evacuated to [Wells, Somerset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wells,_Somerset). Here she was interviewed by [Gordon Welchman](wikipedia://en/2385693), who was recruiting for Bletchley Park.[^o3]

In 2005 she contributed a chapter "Memories of Bletchley Park 1941-5" to a local history volume in Uppingham, Rutland, UK.[^o4]
