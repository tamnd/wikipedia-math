# Alexander Morgan (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Morgan"}

**Alexander Morgan** PEIS [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) OBE LLD (21 August 1860 – 17 March 1946) was a Scottish mathematician and educator. He was President of the [Educational Institute of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Institute_of_Scotland) 1911/12. He was involved in the creation of the [Education Act 1918](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Act_1918).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [Leith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leith) on 21 August 1860 the son of Ann and William Morgan originally from [Aberdeenshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeenshire). The family returned to [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen) during his infancy and he was educated there at the North Parish School.[^o4]

Returning to Edinburgh he initially studied for the ministry at the Church of Scotland Training College but then abandoned this and instead studied mathematics and natural philosophy (physics) at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) graduating with an MA in 1886. On graduating he began lecturing in mathematics at the Church of Scotland Training College, becoming its Principal in 1903.

In 1896 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [George Chrystal](wikipedia://en/14008803), [Simon Somerville Laurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Somerville_Laurie), [John Sturgeon Mackay](wikipedia://en/54492344) and [Peter Guthrie Tait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Guthrie_Tait). He was President of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) 1898/99.[^o5]

In later life in lived at 1 Midmar Gardens in south-west Edinburgh.[^o6]

From 1920 to 1925 he was Director of Studies for the Provincial Training College.

He retired in 1925 and died in Edinburgh on 17 March 1946.[^o7]

## Family {#Family}

In 1888 he married Isobel Duthie (died 1941). They had two sons: General [William Duthie Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Duthie_Morgan) and Alexander Morgan.
