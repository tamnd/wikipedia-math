# Constantin Climescu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Constantin Climescu"}

*Image: Constantin Climescu.jpg.* Constantin Climescu

**Constantin Climescu** (30 November 1844 – 6 August 1926) was a [Moldavian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldavia), later [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician and politician.

:::

Born in [Bacău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacău), he attended the princely academy in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), followed by the sciences faculty of [Iași University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași_University). He then left for the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris, and in 1870, took his undergraduate degree from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), in mathematics and physical sciences. He was a professor of [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) and [spherical trigonometry](wikipedia://en/650405) at Iași University from 1871 to 1909, served as dean of the sciences faculty from 1880 to 1901,[^o3] and as rector of the university from 1901 to 1907.[^o2]

Meanwhile, from 1884 to 1896, he taught at the upper normal school of Iași, and was among the founders of *[Recreații Științifice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recreații_Științifice)* periodical in 1883;[^o3] its chief contributor, he wrote articles on arithmetic, elementary and analytical geometry, algebra and mathematical analysis.[^o2] He also belonged to the editorial board of *[Gazeta Matematică](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazeta_Matematică)*, where he wrote on the historiography of mathematics. He wrote several textbooks that were widely used at the time, on algebra (1887), rational-number arithmetic (1890), elementary geometry (1891) and analytic geometry (1898);[^o3] the last volume was the second of its type to appear in Romania.[^o2] He was elected a corresponding member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy) in 1892.[^o3]

A member of the [National Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Liberal_Party_%28Romania,_1875%29), he was elected to the [Assembly of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Chamber_of_Deputies) for Bacău [in 1884](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1884_Romanian_general_election), and represented Iași in the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Senate) from 1889 to 1910.[^o2][^o4] He was an officer of the [Order of the Star of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Star_of_Romania), and a commander of the [Order of the Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Crown_%28Romania%29).[^o2] He left the university when he reached the retirement age in 1909, died in 1926 and was buried in [Eternitatea cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternitatea_cemetery).[^o2]
