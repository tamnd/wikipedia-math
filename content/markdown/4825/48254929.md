# Alain Chenciner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alain Chenciner"}

**Alain Chenciner** (born 23 October 1943, in [Villeneuve-sur-Lot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villeneuve-sur-Lot)) is a French mathematician, specializing in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) with applications to [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182).

:::

*Image: Chenciner alain.jpg.* Alain Chenciner, Oberwolfach 2011

Chenciner studied from 1963 to 1965 at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) and did research from 1966 for [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) (Attachée de Recherche) at the École Polytechnique (at the Centre de Mathématiques founded by [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485)). In 1971 he received his Ph.D. from the [University of Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XI) under [Jean Cerf](wikipedia://en/56530120) with thesis *Sur la géométrie des strates de petites codimensions de l'espace des fonctions différentiables réelles sur une variété* (Chenciner's thesis defense involved [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536), [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) and [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633)). Chenciner became a *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* at the University of Paris XI, then in 1973 at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII), and in 1975 at the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice). He returned in 1978 as *maître de conférences* to the University of Paris VII, where in 1981 he became professor extraordinarius (*professeur de première classe*) and in 1991 professor ordinarius (*professeur en classe exceptionelle*). In 2012 he became professor emeritus.

At the beginning of his career Chenciner worked on differential topology and its applications to dynamical systems, following the pioneering efforts of [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294), and also worked on [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). Later in his career he worked on mathematical problems of celestial mechanics (specifically, the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420)[^o3] and the [n-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945)) and studied bifurcations at elliptical fixed points of dynamical systems.

Chenciner, with [Jacques Laskar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Laskar), founded in 1992 the research group *astronomie et systèmes dynamiques* at the [Observatory of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observatory_of_Paris). In 2002 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Beijing and gave a talk *Action minimizing solutions of the Newtonian n-body problem: from homology to symmetry*. He became in 2012 a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He gave a eulogy on 9 July 2012 at the [Montparnasse Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montparnasse_Cemetery) to mark the centenary of the death of [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740).[^o4][^o5]

Chenciner's doctoral students include [Daniel Bennequin](wikipedia://en/48247312).
