# Wolfgang Krull

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Krull"}

*Image: Krull,Wolfgang 1920 Göttingen.jpg.* Wolfgang Krull, Göttingen 1920

**Wolfgang Krull** (26 August 1899 – 12 April 1971) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made fundamental contributions to [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), introducing concepts that are now central to the subject.

:::

Krull was born and went to school in [Baden-Baden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baden-Baden). He attended the Universities of [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg), [Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) and finally [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) from 1919–1921,[^o2] where he earned his doctorate under [Alfred Loewy](wikipedia://en/30407447). He worked as an instructor and professor at Freiburg, then spent a decade at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen). In 1939, Krull moved to become chair at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where he remained for the rest of his life. Wolfgang Krull was a member of the [Nazi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party).[^o3]

His 35 doctoral students include [Wilfried Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfried_Brauer), [Karl-Otto Stöhr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Otto_Stöhr) and [Jürgen Neukirch](wikipedia://en/17477195).
