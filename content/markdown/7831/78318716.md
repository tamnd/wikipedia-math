# Eran Tromer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eran Tromer"}

**Eran Tromer** (ערן טרומר) is a professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University). His research focuses on [information security](wikipedia://en/15036), [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775).[^o2][^o3] He is a founding scientist of [Zcash](wikipedia://en/51849458).[^o2][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Tromer received his PhD from the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science).[^o2] In 2024, he joined Boston University's Hariri Institute for Computing.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

In 2014, Tromer and his colleagues demonstrated an RSA key extraction attack using [acoustic cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/1561321), a type of side-channel attack.[^o7][^o4]

In 2014, Tromer coauthored the design of Zerocash,[^o8] which laid the foundation for the [Zcash](wikipedia://en/51849458) protocol. The work was recognized by the Test of Time Award at the [IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Symposium_on_Security_and_Privacy).[^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

- [ACM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) CCS The Test-of-Time Award (2019)[^o11]
- [IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Symposium_on_Security_and_Privacy) Test-of-Time Award (2024)[^o9][^o12]
