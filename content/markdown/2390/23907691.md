# Cristian Calude

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cristian Calude"}

**Cristian Sorin Calude** (born 21 April 1952) is a New Zealand mathematician and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After graduating from the [Vasile Alecsandri National College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasile_Alecsandri_National_College_%28Galați%29) in [Galați](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galați), he studied at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest), where he was student of [Grigore C. Moisil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigore_C._Moisil) and [Solomon Marcus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Marcus).[^o5] Calude received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in Mathematics from the University of Bucharest under the direction of Solomon Marcus in 1977.

He is currently chair professor at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland),[^o7] New Zealand and also the founding director of the Centre for Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science.[^o8] Visiting professor in many universities in Europe, North and South America, Australasia, South Africa, including [Monbusho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monbusho) Visiting professor, [JAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JAIST), 1999 and visiting professor [ENS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), Paris, 2009, [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), Paris, 2011; visiting fellow, [Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton_Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences), 2012; guest professor, [Sun Yat-sen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yat-sen_University), [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou), China, 2017–2020; visiting fellow [ETH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH) Zurich, 2019. Former professor at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). Calude is author or co-author of more than 270 research articles and 8 books,[^o9] and is cited by more than 550 authors.[^o10] He is known for research in [algorithmic information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_information_theory), [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220), [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and the [history and](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_computation) [philosophy of computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_computation).[^o11]

In 2017, together with Sanjay Jain, [Bakhadyr Khoussainov](wikipedia://en/66640504), Wei Li, and Frank Stephan, he announced an algorithm for deciding [parity games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parity_games) in [quasipolynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasipolynomial_time).[^o12] Their result was presented by [Bakhadyr Khoussainov](wikipedia://en/66640504) at the [Symposium on Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Theory_of_Computing) 2017[^o13] and won a Best Paper Award.[^o14]

Calude was awarded the [National Order of Faithful Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Faithful_Service) in the degree of Knight[^o15] by the [President of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Romania), [Klaus Iohannis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Iohannis), in June 2019.

In 2021, together with Sanjay Jain, [Bakhadyr Khoussainov](wikipedia://en/66640504), Wei Li, and Frank Stephan, he won the [EATCS Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize)[^o16] for their [quasipolynomial time algorithm](wikipedia://en/405944#Polynomial_time) for deciding [parity games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parity_games).

Calude has [Erdős Number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Number) 2 via to [Solomon Marcus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Marcus).

## Distinctions and prizes {#Distinctions_and_prizes}

- "Computing Reviews Award", [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery), New York City, 1986.
- "Gheorghe Lazăr" Mathematics Prize, [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy), Romania, 1988.
- Excellence in Research Award, University of Bucharest, Romania, 2007.
- Dean's Award for Excellence in Teaching, University of Auckland, 2007.
- [Hood Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hood_Fellow), 2008–2009.
- Member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea), 2008.[^o18]
- Romanian [National Order of Faithful Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Faithful_Service) in the degree of Knight,[^o15] June 2019.
- "EATCS-IPEC [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize)", 2021.[^o19]
- "Doctor Honoris Causa, Apolonia University, Iasi, Romania", 2023, [^o20]
- "Full Member SIGMA XI", 2024.[^o21]
