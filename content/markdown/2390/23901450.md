# NUTS statistical regions of Romania

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Romania"}

In the **NUTS** (Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics) codes of **[Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania)** (RO), the three levels are:

:::

::: {#o2 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| [NUTS 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_level_NUTS_of_the_European_Union#Romania) | Macroregions (*Macroregiuni*) | 4 |
| NUTS 2 | [Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_regions_of_Romania) (*Regiuni*) | 8 |
| NUTS 3 | [Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Romania) + [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest) (*Județe + București*) | 42 |

:::

## Local administrative units {#Local_administrative_units}

Below the NUTS levels, the two LAU (Local Administrative Units) levels are:

::: {#o6 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| LAU 1 | — (same as NUTS 3) | 42 |
| LAU 2 | [Communes + Towns + Municipalities (Cities)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Romania) (*Comune + Orașe + Municipii*) | 3180 |

:::

*The LAU codes of Romania can be downloaded here:* <http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/ramon/nuts/excel_files/RO_LAU_2007.xls>
