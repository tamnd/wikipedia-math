# E. Glenadine Gibb

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="E. Glenadine Gibb"}

**E. Glenadine Gibb** was a mathematics education researcher who studied children's thinking about arithmetic operations.[^o3] She served as the president of [National Council of Teachers of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1561691) (NCTM) from 1974 to 1976.[^o4]

:::

Gibb was the first faculty member of the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas) Department of Curriculum and Instruction to be appointed to an endowed professorship, the Catherine Mae Parker Centennial Professorship in Education.[^o4]
