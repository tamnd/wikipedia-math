# James Garson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="James Garson"}

**James Garson** is an American [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He has made significant contributions in the study of [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365)[^o3] and [formal semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_semantics_%28logic%29). He is author of *Modal Logic for Philosophers*[^o4] and *What Logics Mean* by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o5] Garson is Professor of Philosophy at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) and has taught at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) (where he was a visiting professor in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science)), and [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University). He is a nominalist[^o6]
