# Dianalytic manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dianalytic manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **dianalytic manifolds** are possibly [non-orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-orientable) generalizations of [complex analytic manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_manifold). A dianalytic structure on a manifold is given by an [atlas](wikipedia://en/47436) of [charts](wikipedia://en/47436#Charts) such that the [transition maps](wikipedia://en/47436#Transition_maps) are either [complex analytic maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_map) or [complex conjugates](wikipedia://en/173918) of complex analytic maps. Every dianalytic manifold is given by the [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) of an analytic manifold (possibly non-connected) by a fixed-point-free [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) changing the complex structure to its complex conjugate structure. Dianalytic manifolds were introduced by (txt), and dianalytic manifolds of 1 complex dimension are sometimes called [Klein surfaces](wikipedia://en/20478442).
