# Bochner–Kodaira–Nakano identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bochner–Kodaira–Nakano identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Bochner–Kodaira–Nakano identity** is an analogue of the [Weitzenböck identity](wikipedia://en/5468083) for [hermitian manifolds](wikipedia://en/2640459), giving an expression for the [antiholomorphic](wikipedia://en/464178) Laplacian of a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) over a hermitian manifold in terms of its complex conjugate and the curvature of the bundle and the torsion of the metric of the manifold. It is named after [Salomon Bochner](wikipedia://en/987847), [Kunihiko Kodaira](wikipedia://en/646326), and [Shigeo Nakano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigeo_Nakano).

:::
