# Ihara's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ihara's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Ihara's lemma**, introduced by [Ihara](wikipedia://en/37592148)[^n1] and named by [Ribet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Alan_Ribet),[^n2] states that the kernel of the sum of the two *p*-degeneracy maps from *J*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) × *J*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) to *J*<sub>0</sub>(*Np*) is [Eisenstein](wikipedia://en/3772744) whenever the prime *p* does not divide *N*. Here *J*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) is the [Jacobian](wikipedia://en/489734) of the compactification of the [modular curve](wikipedia://en/669552) of Γ<sub>0</sub>(*N*).

:::
