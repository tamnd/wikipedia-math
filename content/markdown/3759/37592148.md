# Yasutaka Ihara

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yasutaka Ihara"}

**Yasutaka Ihara** (伊原 康隆, *Ihara Yasutaka*; born May 3, 1938[^o3] in [Tokyo Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Prefecture)) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor emeritus at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962). His work in number theory includes [Ihara's lemma](wikipedia://en/37591550) and the [Ihara zeta function](wikipedia://en/1342362).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Ihara received his PhD at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1967 with thesis *Hecke polynomials as congruence zeta functions in elliptic modular case*.

From 1965 to 1966, Ihara worked at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He was a professor at the University of Tokyo and then at the Research Institute for Mathematical Science (RIMS) of the [University of Kyōto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kyoto). In 2002 he retired from RIMS as professor emeritus and then became a professor at [Chūō University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chūō_University).

In 1970, he was an invited speaker (with lecture *Non abelian class fields over function fields in special cases*) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice). In 1990, Ihara gave a plenary lecture *Braids, Galois groups and some arithmetic functions* at the ICM in [Kyōto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyōto).

His doctoral students include [Kazuya Katō](wikipedia://en/4616818).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Ihara has worked on geometric and number theoretic applications of [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). In the 1960s, he introduced the eponymous Ihara zeta function.[^o7] In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) the Ihara zeta function has an interpretation, which was conjectured by [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) and proved by [Toshikazu Sunada](wikipedia://en/22019166) in 1985. Sunada also proved that a [regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) is a [Ramanujan graph](wikipedia://en/849412) if and only if its Ihara zeta function satisfies an analogue of the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125).[^o8]
