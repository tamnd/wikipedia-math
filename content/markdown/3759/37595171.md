# Georges Glaeser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Glaeser"}

**Georges Glaeser** (8 November 1918 – 1 September 2002) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was director of the IREM of [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). He worked in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [mathematical education](wikipedia://en/326471) and introduced [Glaeser's composition theorem](wikipedia://en/37595113) and [Glaeser's continuity theorem](wikipedia://en/47321247). Glaeser was a Ph.D. student of [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485).

:::

On 3 July 1973, Glaeser filed a complaint against [Vichy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichy_regime) collaborator [Paul Touvier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Touvier) in the Lyon Court, charging him with crimes against humanity. Glaeser accused Touvier of the 1944 massacre at Rillieux-la-Pape, in which Glaeser's father was murdered. Touvier was eventually imprisoned for life on this charge in 1994.
