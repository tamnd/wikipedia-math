# Enoch Beery Seitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Enoch Beery Seitz"}

*Image: Enoch Beery Seitz.jpeg.* Enoch Beery Seitz

**Enoch Beery Seitz** (1846 - 1883)[^o2] was an American mathematician who was Chair of Mathematics at [North Missouri State Normal School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truman_State_University) (now know as Truman State University).

:::

Enoch Berry Seitz was born 24 August 1846 in [Fairfield County, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfield_County,_Ohio).

Seitz was elected to the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) on 11 March 1880, only the fifth American to be so honored.[^o3] Over 500 of his solutions[^o2] were published in the *Analyst*, the *Mathematical Visitor*, the *Mathematical Magazine*, the *School Visitor* and the *Educational Times* of London, England.[^o4]

He died 8 October 1883 at [Adair, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adair,_Missouri)
