# Hyper-finite field

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyper-finite field"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **hyper-finite field** is an [uncountable](wikipedia://en/32248) [field](wikipedia://en/10603) similar in many ways to [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615). More precisely a field *F* is called hyper-finite if it is uncountable and [quasi-finite](wikipedia://en/4644135), and for every subfield *E*, every absolutely entire *E*-algebra ([regular field extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_field_extension) of *E*) of smaller [cardinality](wikipedia://en/6174) than *F* can be embedded in *F*. They were introduced by (Ax 1968). Every hyper-finite field is a [pseudo-finite field](wikipedia://en/37613028), and is in particular a model for the first-order theory of finite fields.

:::
