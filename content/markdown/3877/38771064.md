# Shafarevich–Weil theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shafarevich–Weil theorem"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), the **Shafarevich–Weil theorem** relates the fundamental class of a [Galois extension](wikipedia://en/457064) of [local](wikipedia://en/145375) or [global](wikipedia://en/288358) fields to an [extension](wikipedia://en/345807) of [Galois groups](wikipedia://en/12673). It was introduced by (txt) for local fields and by (txt) for global fields.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that *F* is a global field, *K* is a [normal extension](wikipedia://en/929925) of *F*, and *L* is an [abelian extension](wikipedia://en/174448) of *K*. Then the Galois group Gal(*L*/*F*) is an extension of the group Gal(*K*/*F*) by the abelian group Gal(*L*/*K*), and this extension corresponds to an element of the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/248635) group H<sup>2</sup>(Gal(*K*/*F*), Gal(*L*/*K*)). On the other hand, [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) gives a fundamental class in H<sup>2</sup>(Gal(*K*/*F*),*I*<sub>*K*</sub>) and a reciprocity law map from *I*<sub>*K*</sub> to Gal(*L*/*K*). The Shafarevich–Weil theorem states that the class of the extension Gal(*L*/*F*) is the image of the fundamental class under the homomorphism of cohomology groups induced by the reciprocity law map (Artin Tate 2009, p.246).

Shafarevich stated his theorem for local fields in terms of [division algebras](wikipedia://en/26549625) rather than the fundamental class (Weil 1967). In this case, with *L* the maximal abelian extension of *K*, the extension Gal(*L*/*F*) corresponds under the reciprocity map to the [normalizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalizer) of *K* in a division algebra of degree \[*K*:*F*\] over *F*, and Shafarevich's theorem states that the [Hasse invariant](wikipedia://en/36426069) of this division algebra is 1/\[*K*:*F*\]. The relation to the previous version of the theorem is that division algebras correspond to elements of a second cohomology group (the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078)) and under this correspondence the division algebra with Hasse invariant 1/\[*K*:*F*\] corresponds to the fundamental class.
