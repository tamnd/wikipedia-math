# Twisted sheaf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Twisted sheaf"}

In mathematics, a **twisted sheaf** is a variant of a [coherent sheaf](wikipedia://en/603780). Precisely, it is specified by: an [open covering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_covering) in the [étale topology](wikipedia://en/5707590) *U*<sub>*i*</sub>, coherent sheaves *F*<sub>*i*</sub> over *U*<sub>*i*</sub>, a [Čech 2-cocycle](wikipedia://en/856614) *θ* for $\mathbb{G}_m$ on the covering *U*<sub>*i*</sub> as well as the isomorphisms

:::

$$g_{ij}: F_j|_{U_{ij}} \overset{\sim}\to F_i|_{U_{ij}}$$

satisfying

- $$g_{ii} = \operatorname{id}_{F_i},$$
- $$g_{ij} = g_{ji}^{-1},$$
- $$g_{ij} \circ g_{jk} \circ g_{ki} = \theta_{ijk} \operatorname{id}_{F_i}.$$

The notion of twisted sheaves was introduced by [Jean Giraud](wikipedia://en/572753). The above definition due to Căldăraru is down-to-earth but is equivalent to a more sophisticated definition in terms of [gerbe](wikipedia://en/822043); see § 2.1.3 of (Lieblich 2007).
