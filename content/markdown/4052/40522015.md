# Chiral homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chiral homology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **chiral homology**, introduced by [Alexander Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613) and [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747), is, in their words, "a “quantum” version of (the algebra of functions on) the space of global horizontal sections of an affine [$\mathcal{D}_X$-scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D-scheme) (i.e., the space of global solutions of a system of non-linear differential equations)."

:::

[Jacob Lurie](wikipedia://en/26663863)'s **topological chiral homology** gives an analog for [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470).
