# Hannes Leitgeb

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hannes Leitgeb"}

*Image: Hannes Leitgeb.jpg.* Leitgeb in 2012

**Hannes Leitgeb** (born 26 June 1972 in [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salzburg)) is an Austrian philosopher and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is Professor of Philosophy at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) and has received a [Humboldt Professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Professorship) in 2010. His areas of research include [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) (theories of truth and [modality](wikipedia://en/333365), [paradox](wikipedia://en/24390), [conditionals](wikipedia://en/658808), [nonmonotonic reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonmonotonic_reasoning), dynamic [doxastic logic](wikipedia://en/12065590)), [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology) ([belief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belief), [inference](wikipedia://en/317465), [belief revision](wikipedia://en/1187311), foundations of probability, [Bayesianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesianism)), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) ([structuralism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structuralism_%28mathematics%29), informal provability, [abstraction](wikipedia://en/2972), criteria of identity), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language) ([indeterminacy of translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indeterminacy_of_translation), [compositionality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compositionality)), [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science) (symbolic representation and [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_networks), metacognition), [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science) (empirical content, measurement theory), and [history of philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_philosophy) ([logical positivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_positivism), [Carnap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnap), [Quine](wikipedia://en/33833)).[^o2]

:::

Leitgeb studied mathematics at the [University of Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salzburg) and graduated with a master's degree in 1997. After his PhDs in mathematics (1998) and philosophy (2001), also in Salzburg, he was offered a position as assistant professor at the university's faculty of philosophy. In 2003, he received an *Erwin Schrödinger Scholarship* by the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund) to do research at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) Department of Philosophy/CSLI. From 2005 on, he worked at the Departments of Philosophy and Mathematics at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol). Two years later, he became Professor of Mathematical Logic and Philosophy of Mathematics.

In autumn 2010, he took up an invitation to occupy the Chair of Logic and Philosophy of Language at LMU Munich, where he became director of the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy.[^o3]

In 2016, Leitgeb became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o4] In 2025, he received the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize).[^o5][^o6]
