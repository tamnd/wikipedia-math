# Schaefer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schaefer"}

**Schaefer** is an alternative spelling and [cognate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognate) for the [German word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) *schäfer*, meaning 'shepherd',[^o2] which itself descends from the [Old High German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_High_German) *scāphare*. Variants "Shaefer", "Schäfer" (a standardized spelling in many German-speaking countries after 1880), the additional alternative spelling "Schäffer", and the [anglicised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglicisation) forms "Schaeffer", "Schaffer", "Shaffer", "Shafer", and "Schafer" are all common [surnames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname).

:::
