# Christian Zeller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Zeller"}

**Julius Christian Johannes Zeller** (24 June 1822, [Mühlhausen am Neckar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart) – 31 May 1899, [Cannstatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born to Gottlob Zeller and Christiana Friedrike Moser.[^o2]

:::

Originally trained in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) and [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology), in 1874 Zeller became Director of the Seminary in [Markgröningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markgröningen) and a girls' [orphanage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orphanage). In 1882 he became a member of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France). The following year, on 16 March 1883, he delivered a short account of his [congruence relation](wikipedia://en/48167) ([Zeller's congruence](wikipedia://en/1207119)), which was published in the society's journal.

He was later awarded the [Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_%28decoration%29) of Friedrich, First Class, and the [Ritterkreuz of Württemberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Merit_Order_%28Württemberg%29). He retired in 1898, and died in the following summer.

## Works {#Works}

### On calendrical calculations {#On_calendrical_calculations}

Each of these four similar papers deals firstly with the day of the week and secondly with the date of [Easter Sunday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Sunday), for the [Julian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Calendar) and [Gregorian Calendars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_Calendar).

- *[Die Grundaufgaben der Kalenderrechnung auf neue und vereinfachte Weise gelöst](https://web.archive.org/web/20140111181443/http://www.merlyn.demon.co.uk/zel-82px.htm)*, Zeller, Chr., Württembergische Vierteljahrshefte für Landesgeschichte, Jahrgang V 1882.
- *[Problema duplex Calendarii fundamentale](https://web.archive.org/web/20140111181832/http://www.merlyn.demon.co.uk/zel-83px.htm)* par M. Ch. Zeller, Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France, vol.11, Séance du 16 mars 1883
- *[Kalender-Formeln](https://web.archive.org/web/20140111180710/http://www.merlyn.demon.co.uk/zel-85px.htm)* von Rektor Chr. Zeller, Mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche Mitteilungen des mathematisch-naturwissenschaftlichen Vereins in Württemberg, Ser. 1, 1 1885
- *[Kalender-Formeln](https://web.archive.org/web/20140111181708/http://www.merlyn.demon.co.uk/zel-86px.htm)* von Chr. Zeller, Acta Mathematica, Vol. 9 1886-87, Nov 1886

He also produced a reference card, [Das Ganze der Kalender-Rechnung](https://web.archive.org/web/20140111181913/http://www.merlyn.demon.co.uk/zel-card.htm).
