# David Eppstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Eppstein"}

**David Arthur Eppstein** (born 1963) is an American [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and mathematician. He is a distinguished professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine),[^o13][^o5] known for his work in [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), [graph algorithms](wikipedia://en/12401), and [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446). Eppstein is also a [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia) editor and an administrator on the [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Wikipedia).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Eppstein received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1984, and later an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (1985) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (1989) in computer science from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), after which he took a postdoctoral position at [Xerox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerox)'s [Palo Alto Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto_Research_Center).[^o7] He joined the UC Irvine faculty in 1990, and was co-chair of the Computer Science Department there from 2002 to 2005.[^o8] In 2014, he was named a Chancellor's Professor.[^o9]

He obtained his Ph.D. in computer science in 1989, from Columbia University, advised by [Zvi Galil](wikipedia://en/7913127). He has supervised thirteen PhD. students, all at University of California, Irvine.[^o10]

Eppstein was named an [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow) in 2011.[^o11] In October 2017, he was one of 396 members elected as fellows of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o12]

Eppstein is an amateur [digital photographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_photographer).[^o13] He is also a [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia) editor and tries to bring more experts to the project.[^o14]

Eppstein served as the program chair for the theory track of the ACM [Symposium on Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Computational_Geometry) in 2001[^o15], the program chair of the ACM-SIAM [Symposium on Discrete Algorithms](wikipedia://en/44632347) in 2002[^o16], and the co-chair for the [International Symposium on Graph Drawing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Symposium_on_Graph_Drawing) in 2009.[^o17]

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

In computer science, Eppstein's research has included work on [minimum spanning trees](wikipedia://en/41795), [shortest paths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortest_path), dynamic [graph data structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_data_structure), [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743), [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813) and [geometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric) [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29). He has published also in application areas such as [finite element meshing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element_meshing), which is used in engineering design, and in [computational statistics](wikipedia://en/15832717), particularly in [robust](wikipedia://en/2885691), [multivariate](wikipedia://en/19384), [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369).
