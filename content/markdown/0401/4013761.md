# KYK-13

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="KYK-13"}

*Image: KYK-13.nsa.jpg.* KYK-13 on display at the [National Cryptologic Museum](wikipedia://en/734289).

The **KYK-13 Electronic Transfer Device** is a common [fill device](wikipedia://en/4319754) designed by the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939) for the transfer and loading of [cryptographic keys](wikipedia://en/53039) with their corresponding check word. The KYK-13 is battery powered and uses the [DS-102 protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DS-102_protocol) for key transfer. Its [National Stock Number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Stock_Number) is 5810-01-026-9618.

:::

Even though the KYK-13 was first introduced in 1976 and was supposed to have been made obsolete by the [AN/CYZ-10 Data Transfer Device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AN/CYZ-10), it is still widely used because of its simplicity and reliability.[^o2] A simpler device than the CYZ-10, the [KIK-30](wikipedia://en/38878690) "Really Simple Key Loader" (RASKL) is now planned to replace the KYK-13, with up to \$200 million budgeted to procure them in quantity.[^o3][^o4]

## Components {#Components}

*Image: KYK-13 Electronic Transfer Device.jpg.* KYK-13

- P1 and J1 Connectors – electrically the same connection; used to connect to a fill cable, COMSEC device, KOI-18, KYX-15, another KYK-13, or AN/CYZ-10.
- Battery compartment – holds battery which powers KYK-13.
- Mode switch – three-position rotary switch used to select operation modes.
  - "Z" – used to [zeroize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeroize) selected keys.
  - ON – used to fill and transfer keys.
  - OFF CHECK – used to conduct parity checks.
- Parity Lamp – blinks when parity is checked or fill is transferred.
- Initiate push button – push this button when loading or zeroizing the KYK-13.
- Address select switch – seven-position rotary switch.
  - "Z" ALL – zeroizes all six storage registers when Mode Switch is set to "Z".
  - 1 through 6 – six storage registers for storing keys in KYK-13.
