# Fulton–MacPherson compactification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fulton–MacPherson compactification"}

In geometry, the **Fulton–MacPherson [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617)** of the [configuration space](wikipedia://en/54981574) of *n* distinct labeled points in a compact complex manifold is a compact complex manifold that contains the configuration space as an open dense subset and is constructed in a canonical way.[^o2] The notion was introduced by (Fulton MacPherson 1994).

:::
