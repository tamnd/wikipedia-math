# Nicola Fusco

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nicola Fusco"}

**Nicola Fusco** (born August 14, 1956 in [Napoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples)) is an Italian mathematician[^o3] mainly known for his contributions to the fields of [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), regularity theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and the theory of [symmetrization](wikipedia://en/8227451). He is currently professor at the [Università di Napoli "Federico II"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II). Fusco also taught and conducted research at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University) at Canberra, the [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) at Pittsburgh and at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence).

:::

He is the Managing Editor of the scientific journal *Advances in Calculus of Variations*, and member of the editorial boards of various scientific journals.

## Awards {#Awards}

Fusco won the 1994 edition of the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) of the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092),[^o5] and, in 2010, the Tartufari Prize from the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei). In 2008 he was an invited speaker at [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) and in 2010 he was [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) on the topic of "*Partial Differential Equations.*"[^o6]

From 2010 he is a corresponding member of [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).
