# Fridrikh Karpelevich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fridrikh Karpelevich"}

**Fridrikh Israilevich Karpelevich** (Фридрих Израилевич Карпелевич; 2 October 1927 – 5 July 2000) was a Russian mathematician known for his work on [semisimple Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/1337587), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).[^o2] Together with [Simon Gindikin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Gindikin), he discovered the [Gindikin–Karpelevich formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gindikin–Karpelevich_formula).[^o3]

:::
