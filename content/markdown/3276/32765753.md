# Mahler polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mahler polynomial"}

In mathematics, the **Mahler polynomials** *g*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are polynomials introduced by Mahler[^o2] in his work on the zeros of the [incomplete gamma function](wikipedia://en/456050).

:::

Mahler polynomials are given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

$$\displaystyle \sum g_n(x)t^n/n! = \exp(x(1+t-e^t))$$

Which is close to the generating function of the [Touchard polynomials](wikipedia://en/342592).

The first few examples are

$$g_0=1;$$

$$g_1=0;$$

$$g_2=-x;$$

$$g_3=-x;$$

$$g_4=-x+3x^2;$$

$$g_5=-x+10x^2;$$

$$g_6=-x+25x^2-15x^3;$$

$$g_7=-x+56x^2-105x^3;$$

$$g_8=-x+119x^2-490x^3+105x^4;$$
