# Norm group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Norm group"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **norm group** is a group of the form $N_{L/K}(L^\times)$ where $L/K$ is a finite [abelian extension](wikipedia://en/174448) of nonarchimedean [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375), and $N_{L/K}$ is the [field norm](wikipedia://en/450555). One of the main theorems in [local class field theory](wikipedia://en/669532) states that the norm groups in $K^\times$ are precisely the open subgroups of $K^\times$ of finite [index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_%28group_theory%29).

:::
