# Gottlieb polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gottlieb polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Gottlieb polynomials** are a family of discrete [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) given by

:::

$$\displaystyle \ell_n(x,\lambda) = e^{-n\lambda}\sum_k(1-e^\lambda)^k\binom{n}{k}\binom{x}{k} =e^{-n\lambda}{}_2F_1(-n,-x;1;1-e^\lambda)$$

[^o2]
