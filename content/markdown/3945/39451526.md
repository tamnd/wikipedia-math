# Gianni Dal Maso

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gianni Dal Maso"}

**Gianni Dal Maso** (born 1954) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is active in the fields of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

:::

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

Dal Maso studied at [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) under the guidance of [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401) and is professor of mathematics at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Internazionale_Superiore_di_Studi_Avanzati) at [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), where he also serves as deputy director. Dal Maso has dealt with a number of questions related to partial differential equations and calculus of variations, covering a range of topics going from lower semicontinuity problems for multiple integrals to existence theorem for so called free discontinuity problems,[^o4] from the study of asymptotic behaviour of variational problems via so called [Γ-convergence](wikipedia://en/2991580) methods[^o5] to fine properties of solutions to obstacle problems.[^o6] In the last years he has been considerably involved in the study of problems arising from applied mathematics, developing methods aimed at describing the evolution of fractures in plasticity problems.[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dal Maso has been awarded the [Caccioppoli prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) in 1990, the "Medaglia dei XL per la Matematica" by the [Accademia Nazionale delle Scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze), the Prize of the Minister for the Cultural Heritage for Mathematics and Mechanics by the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei) in 2003 and the Amerio prize by the Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere in 2005. He has been invited speaker and the third [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in 2000 and EMS lecturer in 2002.[^o9] In 2012 he has been awarded an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) grant.[^o10]
