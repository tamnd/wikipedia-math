# Carolyn Yackel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carolyn Yackel"}

**Carolyn Yackel** is an American mathematician who has been Professor of Mathematics at [Mercer University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercer_University) in [Macon, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macon,_Georgia) since 2001. From 1998 to 2001 she was [Max Zorn](wikipedia://en/19740) Visiting Assistant Professor of Mathematics at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University).[^o2]

:::

Yackel's mother, [Erna Beth Yackel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erna_Beth_Yackel), was a mathematics educator on the faculty at [Purdue University Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University_Northwest).[^o3] Originally trained as a [commutative algebraist](wikipedia://en/245990), her current interests center on [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) and [mathematics in art](wikipedia://en/22468661), particularly as applied to [fiber art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_art).[^o4] She specializes in the realization of geometric and topological structures through [quilting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quilting), [cross-stitching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-stitching), [crocheting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crocheting), [knitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting), and [embroidery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embroidery).

She is on the Board of the [Gathering 4 Gardner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_4_Gardner)[^o5] and also has a long association with The [Bridges Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridges_Conference).[^o6]

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Yackel was born in [West Lafayette, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Lafayette,_Indiana).

She received her S.B. in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (1992) and her M.S. in mathematics from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) (1994). She completed her PhD with the dissertation “Asymptotic Behavior of Annihilator Lengths in Certain Quotient Rings” under [Melvin Hochster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Hochster) at the University of Michigan (1998).[^o8]

Combining her interests in mathematics, quilting and knitting she is one of 24 mathematicians and artists who make up the [Mathemalchemy](wikipedia://en/70985345) Team.[^o9]
