# Yaiza Canzani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yaiza Canzani"}

**Yaiza Canzani García** is a Spanish and Uruguayan mathematician known for her work in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), and particularly in [spectral geometry](wikipedia://en/20635289) and [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841). She is an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Canzani was born in Spain and grew up in Uruguay. She was an undergraduate at the [University of the Republic (Uruguay)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Republic_%28Uruguay%29), where she earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 2008. She completed a Ph.D. in 2013 at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal), Canada, with the dissertation *Spectral Geometry of Conformally Covariant Operators* jointly supervised by Dmitry Jakobson and John Toth.

After postdoctoral study at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and as a [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Fellow at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), she became an assistant professor of mathematics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2016. In 2021 she was promoted to associate professor.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Canzani is a recipient of a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) and a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship). She is the 2022 winner of the [Sadosky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadosky_Prize) in analysis of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698). The award was given "in recognition of outstanding contributions in spectral geometry and microlocal analysis", citing her "breakthrough results on nodal sets, random waves, Weyl Laws, $L^p$-norms, and other problems on eigenfunctions and eigenvalues on [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652)".

She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.
