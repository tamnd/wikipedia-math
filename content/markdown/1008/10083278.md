::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="5-cubic honeycomb"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **5-cubic honeycomb** or **penteractic honeycomb** is the only regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 5-space](wikipedia://en/2851410). Four [5-cubes](wikipedia://en/7184215) meet at each cubic cell, and it is more explicitly called an *order-4 [penteractic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penteractic_honeycomb)*.

:::

It is analogous to the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) of the plane and to the [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) of [3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-space), and the [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139) of [4-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-space).

::: {#o7 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are many different [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) of this honeycomb. The most symmetric form is [regular](wikipedia://en/518693), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3<sup>3</sup>,4}. Another form has two alternating [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) facets (like a checkerboard) with Schläfli symbol {4,3,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>}. The lowest symmetry Wythoff construction has 32 types of facets around each vertex and a prismatic product Schläfli symbol {∞}<sup>(5)</sup>.

:::

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The \[4,3<sup>3</sup>,4\], , Coxeter group generates 63 permutations of uniform tessellations, 35 with unique symmetry and 34 with unique geometry. The [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) 5-cubic honeycomb is geometrically identical to the 5-cubic honeycomb.

The *5-cubic honeycomb* can be [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) into the [5-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/10120241), replacing the 5-cubes with [5-demicubes](wikipedia://en/7186232), and the alternated gaps are filled by [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615) facets.

It is also related to the regular [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462) which exists in 6-space with three 5-cubes on each cell. This could be considered as a tessellation on the [5-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782), an *order-3 penteractic honeycomb*, {4,3<sup>4</sup>}.

The [Penrose tilings](wikipedia://en/26611936) are 2-dimensional [aperiodic tilings](wikipedia://en/868145) that can be obtained as a projection of the 5-cubic honeycomb along a 5-fold rotational axis of symmetry. The vertices correspond to points in the 5-dimensional cubic lattice, and the tiles are formed by connecting points in a predefined manner.[^o9]

::: {#o11 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Tritruncated 5-cubic honeycomb"}

### Tritruncated 5-cubic honeycomb {#Tritruncated_5-cubic_honeycomb}

A **tritruncated 5-cubic honeycomb**, , contains all [bitruncated 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-orthoplex) facets and is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [D<sub>5</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-demicube_honeycomb#D5_lattice). Facets can be identically colored from a doubled ${\tilde{C}}_5$×2, \[\[4,3<sup>3</sup>,4\] symmetry, alternately colored from ${\tilde{C}}_5$, \[4,3<sup>3</sup>,4\] symmetry, three colors from ${\tilde{B}}_5$, \[4,3,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry, and 4 colors from ${\tilde{D}}_5$, \[3<sup>1,1</sup>,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry.

:::
