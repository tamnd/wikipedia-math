# Samuel James Patterson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel James Patterson"}

**Samuel James Patterson** (September 7, 1948 in Belfast)[^o3] is a [Northern Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Irish) mathematician specializing in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513). He has been a professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) since 1981.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Patterson was born in Belfast and grew up in the east of the city, attending [Grosvenor High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grosvenor_High_School). He went to [Clare College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_College,_Cambridge), in 1967, and received his BA in mathematics in 1970, and his Ph.D. (completed in 1974, awarded in 1975) on "The limit set of a Fuchsian group" under [Alan Beardon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Beardon).[^o6] He spent 1974–1975 at Göttingen, 1975–1979 he was back at Cambridge, and 1979–1981 he was at Harvard as [Benjamin Pierce Lecturer](wikipedia://en/364270#Eponyms). From 1981 to his retirement in 2011 he was professor of mathematics at Göttingen.

His 18 PhD students include Jörg Brüdern and Bernd Otto Stratmann.[^o7]

He is the brother of the Northern Irish taxonomist [David Joseph Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Joseph_Patterson).

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Subjects that Patterson deals with include discontinuous groups ([Fuchsian groups](wikipedia://en/471365)), different [zeta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeta_function) (for example those of [Ruelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ruelle) and [Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021), in particular those associated with certain groups of infinite covolume[^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13]), [metaplectic groups](wikipedia://en/2981795),[^o14] generalized [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745), and [exponential sums](wikipedia://en/468937) in analytical number theory.

In 1978, together with [Roger Heath-Brown](wikipedia://en/2984550), he disproved the [Kummer conjecture on cubic Gauss sums](wikipedia://en/6006062#Patterson's_conjecture).[^o15][^o16]

He proposed a new conjecture[^o17] which was based on insights from his determination of the coefficients of the cuspidal Fourier expansions of the metaplectic cubic theta function.[^o18][^o19] This revised conjecture remained open until 2021, when it was finally proved by Alexander Dunn and Maksym Radziwiłł at Caltech.[^o20][^o21]

In 1976 Patterson introduced what later became known as the Patterson-Sullivan measure.[^o6] The concept was further developed and extended by [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608) starting in 1979.[^o22] It has proved to be a useful tool in studying [Fuchsian](wikipedia://en/471365) and [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) (and certain generalizations) and their limit sets.[^o23][^o24]

## History of mathematics {#History_of_mathematics}

Patterson is also interested in the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). For example, together with Ralf Meyer, he contributed an updated introduction to a new edition of a classic textbook by [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544),[^o32] and an introduction to the classic textbook of Whittaker and Watson.[^o26] He has collaborated with [Norbert Schappacher](wikipedia://en/64764918) on elucidating the biography of [Kurt Heegner](wikipedia://en/621799).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 1984 Patterson received the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o28] He is on the executive committee of the Leibniz Archives based in [Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannover)[^o29] and has been a member of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1998.[^o30] From 1982 to 1994 he was an editor of [Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547).[^o31]

To mark his 60th birthday friends and colleagues in Göttingen organized a three-day conference to celebrate his life in July, 2009.[^o32] Speakers at this gathering included [Daniel Bump](wikipedia://en/50510967), [Dorian Goldfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorian_Goldfeld), [David Kazhdan](wikipedia://en/3052833), and [Andrew Ranicki](wikipedia://en/56693002).[^o33] A commemorative volume, *Contributions in Analytic and Algebraic Number Theory* (Springer 2012), edited by Valentin Blomer & [Preda Mihăilescu](wikipedia://en/639556), collecting articles related to or developed at the conference, was issued as a [Festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) for him.[^o34]
