# Analysis (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Analysis"}

***Analysis*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) established in 1933 that is published quarterly by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on behalf of the Analysis Trust.[^o3][^o4] Prior to January 2009, the journal was published by [Blackwell Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley-Blackwell).[^o5] Electronic access to this journal is available via [JSTOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSTOR) (1933–2013), [Wiley InterScience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons) (1996–2008), and [Oxford Journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) (2009–present).[^o7] The journal publishes short, concise articles (of up to 4000 words, excluding bibliography) in virtually any field of the [analytic](wikipedia://en/159211) tradition.

:::

The journal was founded in 1933 by philosophers from Oxford, Cambridge, and London, who were influenced by the views and methods of philosophers like [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), [G.E. Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.E._Moore), and [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741). Its first issued appeared in November of that year.[^o8]

- 2024–present: Stacie Friend, Lee Walters, and Roger Clark

## Noteworthy articles {#Noteworthy_articles}

A number of noteworthy works by notable authors have been published in the journal. These include{

- [Toulmin, Stephen](wikipedia://en/1543748). (1948). "The Logical Status of Psycho-Analysis". **9** (2), pp. 23–29.
- [Church, Alonzo](wikipedia://en/49731). (1950). "On Carnap's Analysis of Statements of Assertion and Belief". **10** (5), pp. 97–99.
- [Anderson, Alan Ross](wikipedia://en/3793208). (1951). "A Note on Subjunctive and Counterfactual Conditionals". **12** (2), pp. 35–38.
- [Nuel Belnap](wikipedia://en/1251311). (1962) "Tonk, Plonk and Plink". **22** (6) pp. 130–134.
- [Gettier, Edmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Gettier). (1963). "[Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Is_Justified_True_Belief_Knowledge?)". **23** (6), pp. 121–123.
- [Lewis, David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lewis_%28philosopher%29). (1988). "Vague Identity: Evans Misunderstood". **48** (3), pp. 128–130.
- [Clark, Andy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Clark) and [Chalmers, David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Chalmers). (1998). "The Extended Mind". **58** (1), pp. 7–19.
- [Knobe, Joshua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Knobe). (2003). "Intentional Action and Side Effects in Ordinary Language". **63** (279), pp. 190–194.
