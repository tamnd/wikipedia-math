# Georg Helm

*Image: TU Dresden Grab Hettner u.a..jpg.* Memorial Tablet to Georg Helm at the old Annenfriedhof in Dresden (Bust is of [Hermann Hettner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Hettner)).

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Georg Helm"}

**Georg Ferdinand Helm** (15 March 1851 – 13 September 1923) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Helm graduated from high school from the [Annenschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annenschule_%28Dresden%29) in Dresden in 1867. Thereafter he studied mathematics and natural sciences at the Dresden Polytechnical School, and then at the universities of [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) and [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) from 1871 to 1873.

Helm first taught at the Annenschule, his high school alma mater.[^o3] Then he taught [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the Royal Saxon Polytechnic (now [TU Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_University_of_Technology)) from 1888 until 1922. He was an interdisciplinarian, whose teaching responsibilities included a seminar on insurance statistics. Helm coined the term “[mathematical chemistry](wikipedia://en/1570072)”, and was the first to classify physical and chemical properties as [intensive or extensive](wikipedia://en/204464) in 1898.[^o4]

His work in the area of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) postulated that money was the economic equivalent of the lowest form of [social entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_entropy) as described in his work “Teachings on Energy” (*in German*, *Lehre von der Energie*, 1887<https://archive.org/details/dielehrevondere00helmgoog>).
