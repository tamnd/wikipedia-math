# The College Mathematics Journal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The College Mathematics Journal"}

The ***College Mathematics Journal*** is an expository magazine aimed at teachers of college mathematics, particularly those teaching the first two years. It is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) and is a continuation of the *Two-Year College Mathematics Journal*. It covers all aspects of mathematics. It publishes articles intended to enhance undergraduate instruction and classroom learning, including expository articles, short notes, problems, and "mathematical ephemera" such as fallacious proofs, quotations, cartoons, poetry, and humour.[^o3] Paid circulation in 2008 was 9,000, and total circulation was 9,500.[^o4]

:::

The MAA gives the [George Pólya Awards](wikipedia://en/36409098) annually "for articles of expository excellence" published in the *College Mathematics Journal*.[^o5]
