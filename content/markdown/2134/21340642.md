# Math Horizons

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Math Horizons"}

***Math Horizons*** is a magazine aimed at undergraduates interested in mathematics, published by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). It publishes expository articles about "beautiful mathematics" as well as articles about the culture of mathematics covering mathematical people, institutions, humor, games, cartoons, and book reviews.[^o3]

:::

The MAA gives the [Trevor Evans Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Evans_Award) annually to "authors of exceptional articles that are accessible to undergraduates" that are published in *Math Horizons*.[^o4]
