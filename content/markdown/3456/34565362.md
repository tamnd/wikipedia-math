# Flory–Rehner equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Flory–Rehner equation"}

In [polymer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer_science) **Flory–Rehner equation** is an equation that describes the mixing of [polymer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer) and [liquid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid) [molecules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecules) as predicted by the equilibrium swelling theory of Flory and Rehner.[^o2] It describes the equilibrium swelling of a lightly crosslinked polymer in terms of crosslink density and the quality of the [solvent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solvent).

:::

The Flory–Rehner equation is written as:

$$-\left[ \ln{\left(1 - \nu_2\right)}+\nu_2+ \chi_1 \nu_2^2 \right] = V_1 n \left(\nu_2^\frac{1}{3}-\frac{\nu_2}{2}\right)$$

where, $\nu_2$ is the volume fraction of polymer in the swollen mass, $V_1$ the molar volume of the solvent, $n$ is the number of network chain segments bounded on both ends by crosslinks, and $\chi_1$ is the Flory solvent-polymer interaction term.[^o5]

In its full form, the Flory–Rehner equation is written as:[^o3]

$$-\left[ \ln{\left(1 - \nu_2\right)}+\nu_2+ \chi_1 \nu_2^2 \right] = \frac{V_1}{\bar{\nu}M_c} \left(1-\frac{M_c}{M}\frac{f}{f - 2}\right) \left(\nu_2^\frac{1}{3}-\frac{\nu_2}{2}\right)$$

where $\bar{\nu}$ is the [specific volume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_volume) of the polymer; $M$ is the primary [molecular mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_mass); $f$ is the crosslink functionality (4 for perfect network); and $M_c$ is the average molecular mass between crosslinks or the network parameter.[^o3]

## Flory–Rehner theory {#Flory–Rehner_theory}

The Flory–Rehner theory gives the change of [free energy](wikipedia://en/2057973) upon swelling of the polymer gel similar to the [Flory–Huggins solution theory](wikipedia://en/3010589):

$$\Delta F = \Delta F_\mathrm{mix} + \Delta F_\mathrm{elastic}.$$

The theory considers forces arising from three sources:[^o5]

1. The [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) change $\Delta S_\mathrm{mix}$ caused by mixing of polymer and solvent
1. The heat of mixing of polymer and solvent $\Delta U_\mathrm{mix}$, which may be positive, negative, or zero so, that $\Delta F_\mathrm{mix}=\Delta U_\mathrm{mix}-T\Delta S_\mathrm{mix}$
1. The entropy change caused by reduction in numbers of possible chain conformations via swelling $\Delta F_\mathrm{elastic}$

The Flory–Rehner equation was used to model the cooking of steaks in a journal article in 2020[^o6]
