# Michel Goemans

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Goemans"}

**Michel Xavier Goemans** (born December 1964) is a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and the RSA Professor of Mathematics at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) working in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555) at [CSAIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSAIL) and MIT Operations Research Center.[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Goemans earned his doctorate in 1990 from MIT.[^o2] Goemans is the "Leighton Family Professor" of Applied Mathematics at MIT and an adjunct professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). He was also a professor at the [University of Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Louvain) and a visiting professor at the [RIMS](wikipedia://en/1978962) of the [University of Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kyoto).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1991 he received the [A.W. Tucker Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucker_Prize). From 1995 to 1997 he was a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship). In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o7] For the academic year 2007–2008 he [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow).

Goemans is a [Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery) (2008),[^o8] a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (2012),[^o9] and a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (2013).[^o10] In 2000 he was awarded the MOS-AMS [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916)[^o11] for joint work with [David P. Williamson](wikipedia://en/46920695) on the [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539) approximation algorithm for the [maximum cut](wikipedia://en/19636775) problem. In 2012 Goemans was awarded the Farkas Prize.[^o12] In 2022 he received the [AMS Steele Prize for Seminal Contribution to Research](wikipedia://en/1252550).[^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

His hobby is sailing. Goemans has Belgian and US citizenship.[^o15]
