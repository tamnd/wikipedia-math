# Michael Drmota

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Drmota"}

**Michael Drmota** (born 17 July 1964 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien).

:::

He studied Mathematics at [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) and finished his PhD in 1986[^o3] under the supervision of [Robert F. Tichy](wikipedia://en/66615202).[^o4] At the same university he acquired [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1990[^o5] and is now full professor[^o5] at the [Institute of Discrete Mathematics and Geometry](https://dmg.tuwien.ac.at/), where he also served as head of institute[^o5] from 2004 to 2012.

He had visiting professor positions at [UVSQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles_Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines_University), [University Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), and [University of Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Provence) (Marseille). From 2013 until 2019 he was [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the [Faculty of Mathematics and Geoinformation](https://institute.tuwien.ac.at/fakmatgeo/), from 2010 to 2013 president of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society).[^o6] Since 2013 he is corresponding member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7]

In 1992 he won the *Edmund und Rosa Hlawka-Preis* of the Austrian Academy of Sciences,[^o8] and 1996 the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384).[^o9] In 2023, he got awarded the [Flajolet Lecture Prize](wikipedia://en/67160348).

His research areas are [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533), [analysis of algorithms](wikipedia://en/2230) and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) on combinatorial structures.
