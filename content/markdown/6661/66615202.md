# Robert F. Tichy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert F. Tichy"}

**Robert Franz Tichy** (born 30 September 1957 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) mathematician and professor at [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology).[^o3]

:::

He studied mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and finished 1979[^o4] with a Ph.D. thesis on [uniform distribution](wikipedia://en/1030420) under the supervision of [Edmund Hlawka](wikipedia://en/5442007). He received his habilitation at [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) in 1983. Currently he is a professor at the Institute for Analysis and Number Theory at TU Graz. Previous positions include head of the Department of Mathematics and Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Geodesy at TU Graz, President of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society),[^o5] and Member of the Board (Kuratorium) of the [FWF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund), the Austrian Science Foundation.

His research deals with [Number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29) and [Actuarial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actuarial_mathematics), and in particular with number theoretic algorithms, digital expansions, [diophantine](wikipedia://en/9109) problems, combinatorial and [asymptotic analysis](wikipedia://en/641995), [quasi Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/56098) and [actuarial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actuarial) [risk models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_assessment). Among his contributions are results in discrepancy theory, a criterion (joint with Yuri Bilu) for the finiteness of the solution set of a separable diophantine equation, as well as investigations of graph theoretic indices and of combinatorial algorithms with analytic methods. He also investigated (with Istvan Berkes and Walter Philipp) pseudorandom properties of lacunary sequences.

In the theory of [equidistribution](wikipedia://en/1030420) he solved (with [Harald Niederreiter](wikipedia://en/47275252)) an open problem of [Donald Knuth](wikipedia://en/8095)'s book [The Art of Computer Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Art_of_Computer_Programming), by showing that for any sequence $(n_0,n_1,\dots)$ of distinct natural numbers the sequence $(\alpha^{n_0},\alpha^{n_1},\alpha^{n_2},\dots)$ is [completely uniformly distributed](wikipedia://en/1030420#Complete_uniform_distribution) for [almost all](wikipedia://en/44688) real numbers $\alpha > 1$; as a corollary, for almost all real numbers $\alpha > 1$ the sequence $(\alpha,\alpha^2,\alpha^3,\dots)$ is random in the sense of Knuth's definition R4.[^o6]

Tichy is interested in the history of [Alpinism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpinism) and is also an avid [climber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing).

In 1985 he received the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384).[^o7] Since 2004 he has been a Corresponding Member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o8] In 2017 he received an honorary doctorate from the [University of Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen).[^o9] He taught as a visiting professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) and the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research). In 2017 he was a guest professor at [Paris 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_7); in the winter semester 2020/21 he held the [Morlet chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Morlet#Legacy)[^o10] at the [Centre International de Rencontres Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/54507572) in Luminy.
