# Glasgow Mathematical Journal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Glasgow Mathematical Journal"}

The ***Glasgow Mathematical Journal*** is a [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) that publishes original [research papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_publishing#Scholarly_paper) in any branch of [pure](wikipedia://en/457210) and [applied](wikipedia://en/24295969) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It covers a wide variety of research areas, which in recent issues have included [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), and the application of such methods in applied mathematics.[^o3]

:::

The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is currently A. Bartel ([University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow)).
