# State Data Agency

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="State Data Agency"}

The **State Data Agency of Lithuania** (Valstybinė duomenų agentūra), sometimes referred as the **National Data Agency**, and until 2023 known as the *Department of Statistics of Lithuania* (Lietuvos statistikos departamentas), officially the *Department of Statistics to the Government of the Republic of Lithuania*, is an institution in [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) which is responsible for collecting, processing, presenting and analysing [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) concerning the topics [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Lithuania), [society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Lithuania) and environment, and [governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governance) regarding the state data. It is subordinate directly to the [Government of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Lithuania). The agency also supplies data to [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat).

:::

A Director General, appointed by the [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Lithuania), oversees its operations. Several commissions and working groups analyze its operations and suggest improvements. An advisory body, the Statistical Council, consists of representatives from the governmental bodies, [NGOs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NGO), researchers, the media, and other interested parties; the council's makeup and operations are subject to governmental regulation.

## History {#History}

The origins of statistics collection in Lithuania can be traced back to the 16th century, with the [1528 census of the Grand Duchy of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1528_census_of_the_Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania).[^o2] An institution to collect statistics in the Republic of Lithuania was first established on 6 September 1919, as the *General Department of Statistics*. The first [census](wikipedia://en/6889) of Lithuania was carried out in 1923. The institution in its current form was [re-established](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_of_the_Re-Establishment_of_the_State_of_Lithuania) on 18 April 1990 and codified by the *Law on Statistics of the Republic of Lithuania*.[^o5] The institution became a member of the [European Statistical System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Statistical_System) in 2004.

On 1 January 2023, the department was reorganized into an agency, as part of the Lithuanian Governance reform.[^o6][^o7]
