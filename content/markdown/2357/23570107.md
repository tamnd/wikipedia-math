# Isador M. Sheffer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Isador M. Sheffer"}

**Isador Mitchell Sheffer** (October 15, 1901–April 20, 1992) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for the [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596) of polynomials.[^o3] Born in [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts), he lived a large portion of his life in [State College, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_College,_Pennsylvania), where he was a Professor of Mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

:::

He received his PhD from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1927, under the direction of [George Birkhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Birkhoff).
