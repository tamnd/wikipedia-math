# John W. Dawson Jr.

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John W. Dawson Jr."}

**John William Dawson, Jr.** (born February 4, 1944) is an American academic who is an emeritus professor of mathematics at [Penn State York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_York).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Born in [Wichita, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wichita,_Kansas), Dawson attended the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) as a [National Merit Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Merit_Scholar) before earning a doctorate in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1972.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

An internationally recognized authority on the life and work of [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736), Dawson is the author of numerous articles on [axiomatic set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatic_set_theory) and the history of [modern logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_logic). From 1982 to 1984, he catalogued Gödel's papers at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He also served as a co-editor of *Gödel's Collected Works*. He retired as co-editor-in-chief of the journal *History and Philosophy of Logic*.[^o7]
