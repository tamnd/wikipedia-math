# Robert Edouard Moritz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Edouard Moritz"}

**Robert Edouard Moritz** (2 Jun 1868 – 28 Dec 1940) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He published about 75 books and papers. For over 30 years he was head of the mathematics department at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Moritz was born in [Schleswig-Holstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein) to Karl R. and Maria Stahlhut Moritz, and emigrated to the United States at the age of twelve where the family settled on a farm in [Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska). From 1885 to 1892 he attended [Hastings College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hastings_College) in Hastings, Nebraska, and then studied another year at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). After two summer quarters in the next years he received his MA in mathematics in 1896.[^o3]

In 1893 he started his academic career as assistant professor in mathematics and physics back at [Hastings College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hastings_College). In 1898 he moved to the [University of Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln), where he was appointed instructor in mathematics, and obtained his PhD in 1901. The next year he studied in Europe under [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615) and [Theodor Reye](wikipedia://en/27942014), obtaining a second PhD at the [University of Strassburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strassburg) in 1902. Back in the United States he worked two years at the University of Nebraska, before moving to the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) where he was appointed professor and head of the Department of Mathematics and Astronomy.[^o3]

He died in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle) in the state of Washington in 1940 at the age of 72.[^o3]
