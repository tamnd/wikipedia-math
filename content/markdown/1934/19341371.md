# Zoll surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zoll surface"}

*Image: Surfacedezoll.png.* A Zoll surface discovered by Zoll in 1903. A closed geodesic is pictured in red.

In mathematics, particularly in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a **Zoll surface**, named after [Otto Zoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Zoll), is a [surface](wikipedia://en/27865) [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to the [2-sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-sphere), equipped with a [Riemannian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) all of whose [geodesics](wikipedia://en/91096) are [closed](wikipedia://en/19903176) and of equal length. While the usual unit-sphere metric on *S*<sup>2</sup> obviously has this property, it also has an infinite-dimensional family of geometrically distinct deformations that are still Zoll surfaces. In particular, most Zoll surfaces do not have constant [curvature](wikipedia://en/651361).

:::

Zoll, a student of [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), discovered the first non-trivial examples.
