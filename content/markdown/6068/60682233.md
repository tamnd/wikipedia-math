# Nikiel's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikiel's conjecture"}

In mathematics, **Nikiel's conjecture** in [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649) was a conjectural characterization of the continuous image of a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [total order](wikipedia://en/30330). The conjecture was first formulated by Jacek Nikiel in 1986.[^o2] The conjecture was proven by [Mary Ellen Rudin](wikipedia://en/2976742) in 1999.[^o3]

:::

The conjecture states that a compact [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is the continuous image of a total order if and only if it is a [monotonically normal space](wikipedia://en/11291250).
