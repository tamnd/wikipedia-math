# International Journal of Data Science and Analytics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Journal of Data Science and Analytics"}

The ***International Journal of Data Science and Analytics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904). It was established in 2015 and is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). The founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Longbing Cao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longbing_Cao) ([University of Technology Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Technology_Sydney)). Current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is João Gama ([INESC TEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INESC_TEC) and [University of Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Porto)).[^o3]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.4.[^o5] The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [EI Compendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EI_Compendex)[^o6]
- [Emerging Sources Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_Sources_Citation_Index)[^o7]
- [Inspec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspec)[^o8]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o9]
