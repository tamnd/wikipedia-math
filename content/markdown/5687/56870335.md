# Adolf Weiler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Weiler"}

**Adolf Weiler** (1851–1916) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

After his studies in the Department of Mathematics Teachers of the [Polytechnicum of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), he went to study at [university of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and [university of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen) under [Alfred Clebsch](wikipedia://en/1369957) and [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887). He was awarded doctor in 1874 with a dissertation on quadratic line complexes.[^n1] Some years before, in 1872, he constructed a model of the Clebsch's diagonal surface.[^n2]

Returned to Switzerland, he was mathematics professor at *Ryffel Institute* and he obtained the *venia legendi* both at Polytechnicum as the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich). His main research was in algebraic geometry.[^o6]
