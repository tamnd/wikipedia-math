# Uwe Jannsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uwe Jannsen"}

*Image: Uwe Jannsen (cropped).jpg.* Jannsen at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2009

**Uwe Jannsen** (born 11 March 1954)[^o2] is a German mathematician, specializing in [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Meddewade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meddewade), Jannsen studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) with [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in mathematics in 1978 and with [Promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (PhD) in 1980 under Helmut Brückner and [Jürgen Neukirch](wikipedia://en/17477195) with thesis *Über Galoisgruppen lokaler Körper* (On Galois groups of local fields).[^o4] In the academic year 1983–1984 he was a postdoc at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). From 1980 to 1989 he was an assistant and then [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg), where he received in 1988 his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation). From 1989 to 1991 he held a research professorship at the [Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-Planck-Institut_für_Mathematik) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn). In 1991 he became a full professor at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) and since 1999 he has been a professor at the University of Regensburg.

Jannsen's research deals with, among other topics, the [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316) of [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822), the theory of [motives](wikipedia://en/430680) in algebraic geometry, the Hasse principle (local–global principle), and resolution of singularities. In particular, he has done research on a [cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_theory) for [algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_varieties), involving their extension in mixed motives as a development of research by [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544), and a [motivic cohomology](wikipedia://en/809314) as a development of research by [Vladimir Voevodsky](wikipedia://en/104482). In the 1980s with Kay Wingberg he completely described the absolute Galois group of *p*-adic number fields, *i.e.* in the local case.[^o5]

In 1994 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Mixed motives, motivic cohomology and Ext-groups* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich).[^o6]

He was elected in 2009 a full member of the [Bayerische Akademie der Wissenschaften](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayerische_Akademie_der_Wissenschaften) and in 2011 a full member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).

His doctoral students include Moritz Kerz.
