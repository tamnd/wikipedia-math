# Yang Feng (statistician)

Feng Yang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yang Feng"}

**Yang Feng** () is a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist). He is a professor of biostatistics in the School of Global Public Health at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He is also serving as an affiliate faculty member at both the [NYU Center for Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Center_for_Data_Science) and the NYU Center for Practice and Research at the Intersection of Information, Society, and Methodology.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Feng received his B.S. in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Special Class for the Gifted Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Class_for_the_Gifted_Young) in [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China) in 2006. He earned his Ph.D. in [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2010, under the supervision of [Jianqing Fan](wikipedia://en/28282149).

Following his doctorate, Feng joined the faculty of the Department of Statistics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), where he served before moving to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 2019.

## Research {#Research}

Feng’s research lies at the intersection of modern statistics and machine learning, emphasizing both theoretical development and methodological innovation. His work covers:

- [High-dimensional statistics](wikipedia://en/19330658) such as [variable selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_selection), screening, and [graphical models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphical_models).
- [Machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) topics including [transfer learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_learning), [multi-task learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-task_learning), and [federated learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federated_learning).
- [Network science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_science) including [community detection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_detection) and network embedding.
- Applications to [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health), [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics), [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience), and [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision).

## Honors and recognition {#Honors_and_recognition}

In 2016, Feng received an [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Award).[^o6]

In 2017, Feng became an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (ISI).

In 2022, he was elected a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) (ASA) “for development of effective, practical, and efficient statistical methods that are backed by theory and are relevant and accessible to practitioners; for wide dissemination of methods in publicly available software; and for outstanding teaching.”

In 2023, he was named a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS) "for outstanding contributions to high-dimensional statistics, nonparametric statistics, social network analysis, and statistical machine learning; for statistical software development; and for dedicated service to the profession."

### Editorial service {#Editorial_service}

Feng has held editorial positions at several leading statistics journals. He currently serves as Reviews Editor for the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)* and for *[The American Statistician](wikipedia://en/16876079)* for the 2026–2028 term. He has also served as Associate Editor for journals including:

- *[Annals of Applied Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annals_of_Applied_Statistics)*
- *[Computational Statistics & Data Analysis](wikipedia://en/34877794)*
- *[Journal of Business and Economic Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Business_and_Economic_Statistics)*
- *[Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics](wikipedia://en/31074852)*
- *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313): Theory and Methods*
- *[Stat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stat_%28journal%29)*
- *[Statistica Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistica_Sinica)*
- *[Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Analysis_and_Data_Mining:_The_ASA_Data_Science_Journal)*
