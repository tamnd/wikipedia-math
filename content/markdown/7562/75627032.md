# Slavik Vlado Jablan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Slavik Vlado Jablan"}

**Slavik Vlado Jablan** (Славик Владо Јаблан; 10 June 1952 – 26 February 2015) was a Serbian mathematician and crystallographer. Jablan is known for his contributions to [antisymmetry](wikipedia://en/73498718), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the theory of [symmetry](wikipedia://en/53741) and [ornament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ornament_%28art%29), and [ethnomathematics](wikipedia://en/324872).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Jablan was born on 10 June 1952 in [Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarajevo). Jablan graduated in mathematics from the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) (1977), where he also gained his M.A. degree (1981) and Ph.D. degree (1984) with the dissertation *Theory of Simple and Multiple Antisymmetry in E2 and E2\\{O}*. He was a [Fulbright scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) in 2003/4. Jablan was a professor of geometry at the [University of Niš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Niš) until 1999; subsequently he was a researcher at the Mathematical Institute of the [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

Jablan established the online journal *VisMath* in 2005 and was its editor from its inception until 2014. He joined the editorial board of the journal *[Symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_%28journal%29)* in 2009 and was editor-in-chief from 2012 until 2015. After his death the journal printed a 14-page obituary.[^o4] *[Journal of Knot Theory and Its Ramifications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Knot_Theory_and_Its_Ramifications)* printed a special issue in his memory in 2016.[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

Books published by Jablan:

- Theory of symmetry and ornament (1995)[^o8]
- Symmetry, ornament and modularity (2002)[^o9]
- LinKnot: knot theory by computer (2007)[^o10]

Jablan published 65 academic papers. Selected papers available in English:

Antisymmetry and [coloured symmetry](wikipedia://en/73619197):

- Groups of conformal antisymmetry and complex antisymmetry In E2\\{0} (1985)[^o11]
- A new method of generating plane groups of simple and [multiple antisymmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_antisymmetry) (1986)[^o12]
- Enantiomorphism of antisymmetric figures (1986)[^o13]
- Colored antisymmetry (1992)[^o14]
- *Farbgruppen* and their place in the history of colored symmetry (2007)[^o15]

Knot theory:

- Nonplanar graphs derived from Gauss codes of virtual knots and links (2011)[^o16]
- Knots in art (2012)[^o17]
- Delta diagrams (2016)[^o18]

Ornament and ethnomathematics:

- Antisymmetry and modularity in ornamental art (2001)[^o19]
- Elementary constructions of Persian mosaics (2006)[^o20]
- Knots and links in architecture (2012)[^o21]
