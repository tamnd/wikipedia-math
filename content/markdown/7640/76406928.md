# Erica Brittain

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Erica Brittain"}

**Erica Hyde Brittain** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Allergy_and_Infectious_Diseases), where she is deputy branch chief in the Biostatistics Research Branch. Her research includes work on [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717). She is a coauthor of a book on [statistical hypothesis tests](wikipedia://en/30284), *Statistical Hypothesis Testing in Context: Reproducibility, Inference, and Science* (with Michael P. Fay, Cambridge University Press, 2022).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Brittain majored in mathematics at [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University), graduating in 1977. After earning a master's degree in statistics in 1980 from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), she completed a Ph.D. in 1984 at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina) (UNC). Her dissertation, *Determination of $p$-values for a $k$-sample extension of the Kolmogorov-Smirnov procedure*, was advised by Thomas Fleming of the [Mayo Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic) (where her fiancé worked) but officially supervised by Clarence E. (Ed) Davis at UNC.

After working for the [Center for Drug Evaluation and Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Drug_Evaluation_and_Research) and [National Heart Lung and Blood Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Heart_Lung_and_Blood_Institute), she moved to the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases in 2003, and became deputy branch chief in 2013.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Brittain's book on hypothesis testing was a finalist in mathematics and statistics in the 2023 [PROSE Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PROSE_Awards).

She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2023.
