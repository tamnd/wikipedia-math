# Dodecagram

Regular star polygon stat table

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dodecagram"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **dodecagram** (el[^o2]) is a [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) or compound with 12 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417). There is one regular dodecagram polygon (with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{12/5\}$ and a [turning number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_number) of 5). There are also 4 regular compounds $\{12/2\},$ $\{12/3\},$ $\{12/4\},$ and $\{12/6\}.$

:::

## Regular dodecagram {#Regular_dodecagram}

There is one regular form: {12/5}, containing 12 vertices, with a [turning number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_number) of 5. A regular dodecagram has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a regular [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486), which may be regarded as {12/1}.

### Dodecagrams as regular compounds {#Dodecagrams_as_regular_compounds}

There are four regular compound dodecagram [star figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_figure): {12/2}=2{6}, {12/3}=3{4}, {12/4}=4{3}, and {12/6}=6{2}. The first is a compound of two [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733), the second is a compound of three [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), the third is a compound of four [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), and the fourth is a compound of six straight-sided [digons](wikipedia://en/5442728). The last two can be considered compounds of two compound [hexagrams](wikipedia://en/302441) and the last as three compound tetragrams.

## Dodecagrams as isotoxal figures {#Dodecagrams_as_isotoxal_figures}

An [isotoxal polygon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotoxal_polygon) has two vertices and one edge type within its symmetry class. There are 5 isotoxal dodecagram star with a degree of freedom of angles, which alternates vertices at two radii, one simple, 3 compounds, and 1 unicursal star.

## Dodecagrams as isogonal figures {#Dodecagrams_as_isogonal_figures}

A regular dodecagram can be seen as a quasitruncated hexagon, t{6/5}={12/5}. Other isogonal ([vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive)) variations with equally spaced vertices can be constructed with two edge lengths.

## Complete graph {#Complete_graph}

Superimposing all the dodecagons and dodecagrams on each other – including the [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952) *compound of six [digons](wikipedia://en/5442728)* (line segments), {12/6} – produces the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) *K*<sub>12</sub>.

## Dodecagram symbolism {#Dodecagram_symbolism}

*Image: DongSonBronzeDrum.JPG.* The twelve-pointed star is a prominent feature on the ancient Vietnamese [Dong Son drums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong_Son_drum)

Dodecagrams or twelve-pointed stars have been used as symbols for the following:

- [the twelve tribes of Israel, in Judaism](https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/the-twelve-tribes-of-israel)
- [the twelve disciples, in Christianity](http://www.religionfacts.com/twelve-point-star)
- [the twelve olympians, in Hellenic Polytheism](https://www.britannica.com/list/12-greek-gods-and-goddesses)
- [the twelve signs of the zodiac](https://gottssymbols.tumblr.com/post/173871082894/the-zodiac-part-15-when-a-12-pointed-star-made)
- the [International Order of Twelve Knights and Daughters of Tabor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Order_of_Twelve_Knights_and_Daughters_of_Tabor), an African-American fraternal group
- [the fictional secret society Manus Sancti, in the *Knights of Manus Sancti* series by Bryn Donovan](https://www.bryndonovan.com/2017/03/27/star-symbolism-and-meaning-for-tattoos-or-whatever-you-like/)
- [The twelve tribes of Nauru on the national flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Nauru).
