# Michael Scott Jacobson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Scott Jacobson"}

**Michael S. Jacobson** is a mathematician, and Professor of Mathematical & Statistical Sciences in the Department of Mathematical & Statistical Science at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver). He served as Chair from 2003 to 2012 and was on loan serving as a program director in EHR/DUE at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Jacobson did his undergraduate studies at [SUNY Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SUNY_Stony_Brook) in mathematics, graduating in 1975 and received his MS and Ph.D. in 1977 and 1980, respectively, from [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University) under the supervision of [Henry S. Sharp Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_S._Sharp_Jr.)[^o3] He spent 23 years at the [University of Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Louisville), where in addition to being promoted to Professor in 1988, also served as Department Chair and Associate Dean for Research and Graduate Studies.[^o4]

## Current work {#Current_work}

Jacobson has been at the University of Colorado Denver since 2003.

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Jacobson specializes in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and in particular [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He has published more than 140 mathematical papers on topics including [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), [Hamiltonian graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph), [domination in graphs](wikipedia://en/1747972) and [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568). He has had over 70 collaborators including [Kenneth Bogart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Bogart), [Stefan Burr](wikipedia://en/26467605), [Gary Chartrand](wikipedia://en/22782409), [Guantao Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guantao_Chen), [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), [Ralph Faudree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Faudree), [Ron Gould](wikipedia://en/31983045), [András Gyárfás](wikipedia://en/614969), [Frank Harary](wikipedia://en/1731731), [Stephen Hedetniemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Hedetniemi), [Linda Lesniak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Lesniak), [Fred McMorris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_McMorris), [K. Brooks Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._Brooks_Reid), [Richard Schelp](wikipedia://en/33222421), [Edward Scheinerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scheinerman) and [Douglas B. West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_B._West).[^o7]
