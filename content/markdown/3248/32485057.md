# Bass conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bass conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Bass conjecture** says that certain [algebraic *K*-groups](wikipedia://en/598500) are supposed to be finitely generated. The conjecture was proposed by [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Statement of the conjecture"}

## Statement of the conjecture {#Statement_of_the_conjecture}

Any of the following equivalent statements is referred to as the Bass conjecture.

- For any [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/11985661) **Z**-algebra *A*, the groups *K'*<sub>*n*</sub>(*A*) are [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/58527) (*K*-theory of finitely generated *A*-modules, also known as G-theory of *A*) for all *n* ≥ 0.
- For any finitely generated **Z**-algebra *A*, that is a [regular ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_ring), the groups *K*<sub>*n*</sub>(*A*) are finitely generated (*K*-theory of finitely generated locally free *A*-modules).
- For any [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) *X* of [finite type](wikipedia://en/70728366) over [Spec](wikipedia://en/37291)(**Z**), *K'*<sub>*n*</sub>(*X*) is finitely generated.
- For any regular scheme *X* of finite type over **Z**, *K*<sub>*n*</sub>(*X*) is finitely generated.

The equivalence of these statements follows from the agreement of *K*- and *K'*-theory for regular rings and the localization sequence for *K'*-theory.

:::

## Known cases {#Known_cases}

[Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519) showed that the Bass conjecture holds for all (regular, depending on the version of the conjecture) rings or schemes of dimension ≤ 1, i.e., [algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curves) over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615) and the spectrum of the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) in a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field).

The (non-regular) ring *A* = **Z**\[x, y\]/x<sup>2</sup> has an infinitely generated *K*<sub>1</sub>(*A*).

## Implications {#Implications}

The Bass conjecture is known to imply the [Beilinson&ndash;Soulé vanishing conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beilinson&ndash;Soulé_vanishing_conjecture).[^o6]
