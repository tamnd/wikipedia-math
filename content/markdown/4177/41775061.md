::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Trigonal trapezohedral honeycomb"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **trigonal trapezohedral honeycomb** is a [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb) space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space). Cells are identical [trigonal trapezohedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonal_trapezohedra) or [rhombohedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombohedra). Conway, Burgiel, and Goodman-Strauss call it an **oblate cubille**.[^o9]

:::

## Related honeycombs and tilings {#Related_honeycombs_and_tilings}

This honeycomb can be seen as a [rhombic dodecahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_dodecahedral_honeycomb), with the [rhombic dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/607631) [dissected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissection_%28geometry%29) with its center into 4 [trigonal trapezohedra](wikipedia://en/3504388) or [rhombohedra](wikipedia://en/2706406).

It is analogous to the regular hexagonal being dissectable into 3 rhombi and tiling the plane as a [rhombille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombille). The rhombille tiling is actually an orthogonal projection of the *trigonal trapezohedral honeycomb*. A different orthogonal projection produces the [quadrille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrille_%28geometry%29) where the rhombi are distorted into squares.
