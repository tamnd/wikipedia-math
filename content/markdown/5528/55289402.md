# Chaim Samuel Hönig

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Chaim Samuel Hönig"}

**Chaim Samuel Hönig** (1 February 1926[^o3] – 19 March 2018) was a Brazilian mathematician. He was the main proposer of the Brazilian Mathematical Colloquium (1957), one of the founders of the Brazilian Mathematical Society (SBM), and its first president.[^o4] Hönig was a full professor at the [Institute of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics) of the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo)[^o5] and a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] Hönig made relevant contributions to functional analysis.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

:::
