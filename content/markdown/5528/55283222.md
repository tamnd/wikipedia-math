# George Szpiro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Szpiro"}

**George Geza Szpiro** (ג'ורג' גזה שפירא; born 18 February 1950 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866))[^o2] is an Israeli–Swiss author, journalist, and mathematician. He has written articles and books on [popular mathematics](wikipedia://en/2069923) and related topics.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Szpiro was born in Vienna in 1950, and moved to [Zug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zug), Switzerland, in 1961.[^o5] He obtained a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in mathematics and physics from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). He also obtained an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), in 1975.[^o5] Afterward, he worked as a [management consultant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_consultant) at [McKinsey & Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKinsey_&_Company).[^o4][^o10] In 1984, he obtained a Ph.D. in [mathematical economics](wikipedia://en/18967255) from [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).[^o5][^o10]

Szpiro was an assistant professor at the [Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania), during 1984–1986. He was a lecturer in mathematical economics at Hebrew University, during 1986–1992. He also taught at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o4][^o10] He has published research papers related to mathematics, finance, and statistics.[^o6]

Since 1986, Szpiro has worked as a journalist at *[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neue_Zürcher_Zeitung)*.[^o5] At *NZZ*, he has been the Israel [correspondent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correspondent) and mathematics [columnist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columnist).[^o5][^o7] For his mathematics columns, Szpiro was awarded the Prix Média by the [Swiss Academy of Natural Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Academy_of_Natural_Sciences), in 2003.[^o8][^o9] He was also awarded the Media Prize by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973), in 2006.[^o10] Beside writing for *NZZ*, he has also written non-research mathematics columns for journals such as *[Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_%28journal%29)* and *[Notices of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4977512)*.[^o6][^o7]

## Books {#Books}

- *Poincaré's Prize: The Hundred-Year Quest to Solve One of Math's Greatest Puzzles* ([Dutton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutton_Penguin), 2007)
- *Numbers Rule: The Vexing Mathematics of Democracy, from Plato to the Present* ([Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press), 2010)
- *A Mathematical Medley: Fifty Easy Pieces on Mathematics* ([American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2010)
- *Pricing the Future: Finance, Physics, and the 300-year Journey to the Black-Scholes Equation* ([Basic Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Books), 2011)
