# Discrimination ratio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrimination ratio"}

In [Six Sigma](wikipedia://en/222448), the **discrimination ratio** or **reliability design index** is a performance metric of [attribute agreement analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attribute_agreement_analysis) which assesses the level of agreement between how well the appraisers or inspectors can differentiate between acceptable and unacceptable items.[^o2][^o3]

:::
