# Isoparametric function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isoparametric function"}

In differential geometry, an **isoparametric function** is a function on a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) whose level surfaces are parallel and of constant mean curvatures. They were introduced by (txt).

:::
