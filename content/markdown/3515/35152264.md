# Shimura's reciprocity law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shimura's reciprocity law"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Shimura's reciprocity law**, introduced by (txt), describes the action of [ideles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idele) of [imaginary quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_quadratic_field) on the values of [modular functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_function) at [singular moduli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_moduli). It forms a part of the [Kronecker Jugendtraum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker_Jugendtraum), explicit [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) for such fields. There are also higher-dimensional generalizations.

:::
