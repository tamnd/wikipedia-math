# Eichler order

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eichler order"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **Eichler order**, named after [Martin Eichler](wikipedia://en/4224478), is an [order](wikipedia://en/1114171) of a [quaternion algebra](wikipedia://en/2776890) that is the intersection of two [maximal orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximal_order).

:::
