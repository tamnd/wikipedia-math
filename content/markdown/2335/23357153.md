# Adolph Stöhr

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolph Stöhr"}

**Adolph Stöhr** (February 24, 1855 – February 10, 1921) was professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). His lectures and publications covered subjects such as [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [experimental psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_psychology), [psychology of perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perceptual_psychology), and [psychology of association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_%28psychology%29).

:::

Stöhr was born in [St. Pölten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Pölten) in Lower Austria on February 24, 1855. He went on to study law, [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), and philosophy at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). He started teaching at the university in 1885, becoming a full professor in 1911.[^o3]

## Publications {#Publications}

During his academic career, Stöhr published around 30 works in the fields of philosophy and logic.[^o3]

- *Philosophische Konstruktionen und Reflexionen* 1974 (posthumous)
- *Lehrbuch der Logik in psychologisierender Darstellung* 1910
- *Philosophie der unbelebten Materie* 1907
- *Zur Hypothese der Sehstoffe und Grundfarben* 1898
- *Die erste Volkshochschule* 1965 (posthumous)
- *Letzte Lebenseinheiten und ihr Verband in einem Keimplasma, vom philosophischen Standpunkte ...* 1897
- *Philosophie der unbelebten Materie: Hypothetische Darstellung der Einheit des Stoffes und seines ...* 1907
- *Zur nativistischen Behandlung des Tiefensehens* 1892
- *Umriss einer Theorie der Namen* 1889
