# Nathalie Japkowicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nathalie Japkowicz"}

**Nathalie Japkowicz** is a Canadian computer scientist specializing in [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488). She is a professor and department chair of computer science at the [American University College of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University_College_of_Arts_and_Sciences).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Japkowicz completed a B.Sc. at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in 1988.[^o4] She earned an M.Sc. from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1990.[^o4] She completed a Ph.D. at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in 1999.[^o4]

Japkowicz worked at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) in the school of [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) and computer science.[^o4] She was the lead of its laboratory for research on [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) for defense security.[^o4]

Japkowicz is a professor and department chair of computer science at the [American University College of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University_College_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o4] She researches [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), machine learning, [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253), and [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) analysis.[^o5]
