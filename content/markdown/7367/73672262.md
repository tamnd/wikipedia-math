# Cheng Hsiao

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Cheng Hsiao"}

**Cheng Hsiao** (; born 28 July 1943)[^o7] is a Taiwanese-American economist, [econometrician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometrician), and statistician who is a professor of economics at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California). Hsiao is known for his works in [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis) and [panel data](wikipedia://en/2499813) analysis.[^o7][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hsiao was born in [Chongqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqing), China. Because his father was a member of the [Kuomintang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuomintang), Hsiao's family moved to Taiwan through [British Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Hong_Kong) during the [Great Retreat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retreat_of_the_government_of_the_Republic_of_China_to_Taiwan) after the [Chinese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Civil_War).[^o7]

Hsiao was raised in Taiwan and received his primary and secondary education there. He graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) (B.A.) in economics in 1965, then briefly served in the [Republic of China Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_Air_Force). After finishing his military service, Hsiao was introduced by his father to Nobel laureate [Friedrich Hayek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Hayek), who wrote a letter recommending Hsiao for studies in England at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). As a member of [Balliol College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford), Hsiao earned a [Bachelor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Philosophy) (B.Phil.) in 1968.[^o7] He completed his B.Phil. thesis, "A probablistic analysis of optimum competitive tendering policies," under economist [John Flemming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Flemming).[^o6]

After graduating from Oxford, Hsiao completed further graduate studies in the United States at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), earning his [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (M.S.) in statistics in 1970 and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in economics from Stanford in 1972. His doctoral thesis, titled "The Combined Use of Cross-Section and Time-Series Data in Econometric Analysis," was supervised by professors [Theodore Wilbur Anderson](wikipedia://en/22021377) and [Takeshi Amemiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeshi_Amemiya).[^o7]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Hsiao was an assistant professor in economics at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), from 1972 to 1977. He then moved to Canada, becoming an associated professor in economics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) between 1977 and 1980, and was a full professor from 1980 to 1985. He relocated to the US afterwards and went to the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California), where he has been a professor of economics since 1985.[^o9]
