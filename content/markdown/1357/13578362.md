# Ernst Jacobsthal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Jacobsthal"}

**Ernst Erich Jacobsthal** (16 October 1882, [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) – 6 February 1965, [Überlingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Überlingen))[^o2] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and brother to the archaeologist [Paul Jacobsthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Jacobsthal).

:::

In 1906, he earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), where he was a student of [Georg Frobenius](wikipedia://en/341048), [Hermann Schwarz](wikipedia://en/3165834) and [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522); his dissertation, *Anwendung einer Formel aus der Theorie der quadratischen Reste* (*Application of a Formula from the Theory of Quadratic Residues*),[^o3] provided a proof that [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666) of the form 4*n* + 1 are the sum of two [square numbers](wikipedia://en/213919). In 1934, he was fired from his professorship at the [Technische Hochschule Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Berlin), because of his Jewish origins. In 1939 he fled to Norway and became after the war a professor at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) in [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim).[^o2]
