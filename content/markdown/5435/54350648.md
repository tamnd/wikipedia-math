# John Croucher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Croucher"}

**John Sydney Croucher** is an Australian statistician and Professor of Actuarial Studies and Business Analytics in the Macquarie Business School at [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University) in Sydney Australia. He has written around 33 books, 120 research papers, and 500 newspaper articles.

:::

His recent works include *[The Kid from Norfolk Island](https://www.woodslane.com.au/Book/9781922131614/Kid-from-Norfolk-Island)* ([Woodslane](https://www.woodslane.com.au/), 2014), *[Mistress of Science](https://www.amberley-books.com/mistress-of-science-9916.html)* ([Amberley](https://www.amberley-books.com/), 2018), *[Women of Science](https://www.amberley-books.com/women-of-science.html)* ([Amberley](https://www.amberley-books.com/), 2019) and *A Concise History of New South Wales* ([Woodslane](https://www.woodslane.com.au/), 2020). Croucher has written many textbooks on mathematics and statistics for [McGraw-Hill Australia](https://www.mheducation.com.au/), including *[Quantitative Analysis for Management 6e](https://www.mheducation.com.au/quantitative-analysis-for-management-6e-9781760425029-aus)* (2020), [*Mathematics and Statistics for Business 6e* (revised)](https://www.mheducation.com.au/introductory-mathematics-and-statistics-6th-edition-revised-9781743766149-aus-group) (2016) and *[Statistics: Making Business Decisions](https://www.mheducation.com.au/statistics-making-business-decisions-9780074710418-aus)* (2001),

Croucher has also written many trade books, including *Love by [Numbers](https://www.woodslane.com.au/Book/9781921683602/Love-by-Numbers)* ([Woodslane](https://www.woodslane.com.au/), 2011), *[The Secret Language](https://www.harpercollins.com.au/9780730445333/the-secret-language/)* ([ABC Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_Books_%28imprint%29), 2010). *[Number Crunch](https://books.google.com/books?id=Yy9ozzpA2ioC)* ( [Pan Macmillan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Macmillan), 2006), *[Eighteen Months on the Toilet](https://www.woodslane.com.au/Book/9781921606113/Eighteen-Months-on-the-Toilet-and-other-Feats-Facts-Astonishing-Stats)* ([Woodslane](https://www.woodslane.com.au/), 2010 ), *[637 Gorillas on the Run](https://www.woodslane.com.au/Book/9781921606182/637-Gorillas-on-the-Run-and-other-Feats-Facts-and-Astonishing-Stats)* ([Woodslane](https://www.woodslane.com.au/), 2010), *[Exam Scams](https://www.amazon.com.au/Exam-Scams-Cheating-Stories-Excuses/dp/186448165X)* ([Allen and Unwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_&_Unwin), 1996) and *[Great Frauds and Everyday Scams](https://www.amazon.com/Great-Frauds-Everyday-Scams-Adventurers/dp/1864484519)* ([Allen and Unwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_&_Unwin), 1997)

Croucher has PhD degrees in history from both [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University) and the [University of Technology Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Technology_Sydney). He also holds a PhD in Mathematics and Statistics from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in the United States. Croucher also has an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from [Divine Word University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_Word_University) in [Papua New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papua_New_Guinea).[^o2] and was the recipient of the Prime Minister's Australian University Teacher of the Year award in 2013.[^o3][^o4] He is also a renowned speaker[^o5] and the subject of many articles.[^o6] Between 200 and 2012 he was the author of the popular *Number Crunch* column for the Good Weekend[^o7] in the [Sydney Morning Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sydney_Morning_Herald) and [The Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Age) in Melbourne.

He is married to fellow academic [Rosalind Croucher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Croucher) and they both became [Members of the Order of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_Australia) as part of the [2015 Australia Day Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Australia_Day_Honours).[^o8][^o9] John Croucher is also a Fellow of the [Royal Society of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_the_Arts) and a Fellow of the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582).[^o10]
