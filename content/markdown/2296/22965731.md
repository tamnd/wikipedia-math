# Probability Surveys

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Probability Surveys"}

***Probability Surveys*** is an open-access electronic journal that is jointly sponsored by the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society) and the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). It publishes review articles on topics of interest in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::
