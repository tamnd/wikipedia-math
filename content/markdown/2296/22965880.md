# Statistics Surveys

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Statistics Surveys"}

***Statistics Surveys*** is an [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_%28publishing%29) electronic journal, founded in 2007, that is jointly sponsored by the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society), the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) and the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327). It publishes review articles on topics of interest in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). [Wendy L. Martinez](wikipedia://en/55909686) serves as the coordinating editor.

:::
