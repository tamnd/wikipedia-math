# Fano fibration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fano fibration"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Fano fibration** or **Fano fiber space**, named after [Gino Fano](wikipedia://en/2452888), is a morphism of [varieties](wikipedia://en/248808) whose general fiber is a [Fano variety](wikipedia://en/3095929) (in other words has [ample](wikipedia://en/1186896) [anticanonical bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticanonical_bundle)) of positive dimension. The ones arising from extremal contractions in the [minimal model program](wikipedia://en/15148924) are called **Mori fibrations** or **Mori fiber spaces** (for [Shigefumi Mori](wikipedia://en/794811)). They appear as standard forms for varieties without a minimal model.

:::
