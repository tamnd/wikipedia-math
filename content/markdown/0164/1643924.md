# Einar Hille

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Einar Hille"}

**Carl Einar Hille** (28 June 1894 – 12 February 1980) was an American mathematics professor and scholar. Hille authored or coauthored twelve mathematical books and a number of mathematical papers.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hille was born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). His parents were both Swedish but never married. His father, Carl August Heuman, was a civil engineer. Hille was brought up solely by his mother, Edla Eckman, who took the surname Hille. When Einar was two years old, he and his mother returned to [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm). Hille spent the next 24 years in Sweden, returning to the United States when he was 26 years old. He entered [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) in 1911, and was awarded his first degree in mathematics in 1913 and the equivalent of a master's degree in the following year. He received a Ph.D. from Stockholm in 1918 for a doctoral dissertation entitled *Some Problems Concerning Spherical Harmonics*.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In 1919 Hille was awarded the [Mittag-Leffler](wikipedia://en/10096452) Prize and was given the right to teach at the Stockholm University. He subsequently taught at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). In 1933, he became an endowed professor of mathematics in the Graduate School of [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), retiring in 1962.[^o7]

Hille's main work was on [integral equations](wikipedia://en/474234), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258) and [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038). Later in his career his interests turned more towards [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). His name persists among others in the [Hille&ndash;Yosida theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hille&ndash;Yosida_theorem). Hille was a member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and the [Circolo Matematico di Palermo](wikipedia://en/32146789). Hille was president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (1947–48) and was the Society's Colloquium lecturer in 1944. He received many honours including election to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (1953) and the [Swedish Royal Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Royal_Academy_of_Sciences). He was awarded by Sweden with the [Order of the Polar Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Polar_Star).[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

*Image: Stormer Hille Walsh Giambelli Fjeldstad Gonseth Polya Hille Mordell Riesz Fejer Wilkosz Stormer Lovenskiold Bohr Brouwer Zurich1932.tif.* Einar Hille (top right with glasses, immediately behind his mother, who is wearing a headscarf), at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 1932

Hille was married to Kirsti Ore Hille (1906–2001) in 1937, sister of [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegians) mathematician [Øystein Ore](wikipedia://en/1643733). They had two sons, Harald and [Bertil Hille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertil_Hille).[^o10]

## Works {#Works}

- *Analytic Function Theory.* 2 vols., 1959,[^o13] 1964.
- *Analysis.* 2 vols., 1964, 1966.
- *Lectures on Ordinary Differential Equations.* 1969.
- *Methods in Classical and Functional Analysis.* 1972.
- *Ordinary Differential Equations in the Complex Domain.* 1976.[^o14]
- *In Retrospect.* Mathematical Intelligencer, Vol.3, 1980/81, No.1, pp. 3–13.
