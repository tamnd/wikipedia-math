# Gabriel Andrew Dirac

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel Andrew Dirac"}

**Gabriel Andrew Dirac** (13 March 1925 – 20 July 1984) was a Hungarian-British mathematician who mainly worked in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o3] He served as [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) from 1964 to 1966.[^o4] In 1952, he gave a sufficient condition for a graph to contain a [Hamiltonian circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_circuit). The previous year, he conjectured that n points in the plane, not all collinear, must span at least $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ [two-point lines](wikipedia://en/1052632), where $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the largest integer not exceeding $x$. This conjecture was proven for sufficiently large $n$ by [Green](wikipedia://en/1927952) and [Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) in 2012.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Dirac started his studies at [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge) in 1942, but in that same year, the war saw him serving in the aircraft industry.[^o3] He received his MA in 1949, and moved to the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), getting his Ph.D. "On the Colouring of Graphs: Combinatorial topology of Linear Complexes" there under [Richard Rado](wikipedia://en/614984).

## Career {#Career}

Dirac's main academic positions were at the [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) (1948-1954), [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) (1952-1953), [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) (1954-1958), [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) (1958-1963), [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) ([Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics), 1964-1966), [University of Wales at Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales_at_Swansea) (1967-1970), and [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) (1970-1984).[^o3]

## Family {#Family}

He was born Balázs Gábor in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), to Richárd Balázs, a military officer and businessman, and Margit "Manci" Wigner (sister of [Eugene Wigner](wikipedia://en/10101)).[^o10] When his mother married [Paul Dirac](wikipedia://en/24742) in 1937, he and his sister resettled in England and were formally adopted, changing their family name to Dirac. He married Rosemari Paulsen and they had four children together.[^o12]
