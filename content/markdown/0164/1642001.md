# J. V. Uspensky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="J. V. Uspensky"}

**James Victor Uspensky** (Яков Викторович Успенский; April 29, 1883 – January 27, 1947) was a Russian and American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) notable for writing *Theory of Equations*.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Uspensky graduated from the [University of St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Petersburg) in 1906 and received his doctorate from the [University of St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Petersburg) in 1910. He was a member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) from 1921.[^o7]

Uspensky joined the faculty of [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1929-30 and 1930–31 as acting professor of mathematics. He was professor of mathematics at Stanford from 1931 until his death.[^o7] Uspensky was the one who kept alive [Vincent's theorem](wikipedia://en/35623596) of 1834 and 1836, carrying the torch (so to speak) from Serret.[^o8]
