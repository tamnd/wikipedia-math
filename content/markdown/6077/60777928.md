# Luminița Vese

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luminița Vese"}

**Luminița Aura Vese** is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), known for her research in [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing), including work on [active contour models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_contour_model), [level-set methods](wikipedia://en/1203063), [image segmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_segmentation), and [inpainting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inpainting).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

The Chan–Vese method of image segmentation using active contours is named after her and [Tony F. Chan](wikipedia://en/22247302); Chan and Vese published the method in 2001.

The Vese–Osher and Osher–Solé–Vese models are optimization problems used for [noise reduction](wikipedia://en/476836) of images, by decomposing an image into a sum of signal and noise in a way that optimizes a combination of measures of the smoothness of the image and the total amount of noise. They are again named after Vese, and her co-authors [Stanley Osher](wikipedia://en/3008100) and Andrés Solé on two papers published in 2003.

With Carole Le Guyader, Vese is the author of the book *Variational Methods in Image Processing* (CRC Press, 2016).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Vese earned bachelor's and master's degrees in 1992 at the [West University of Timișoara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_University_of_Timișoara) in Romania. She then moved to the [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis) in France, earning a second master's degree in 1992 and completing her doctorate in 1997. Her dissertation, *Problèmes variationnels et EDP pour l’analyse d’image et l’évolution des courbes*, was jointly supervised by Gilles Aubert and Michel Rascle. After taking a temporary position at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University), she joined the University of California, Los Angeles faculty in 2000. She received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2003.
