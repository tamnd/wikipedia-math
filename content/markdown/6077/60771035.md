::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="3-4-3-12 tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of the Euclidean plane, the **3-4-3-12 tiling** is one of 20 [2-uniform tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410) [by regular polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiling_by_regular_polygons), containing regular [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), and [dodecagons](wikipedia://en/508486), arranged in two [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745): 3.4.3.12 and 3.12.12.[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

The *\3.12.12* vertex figure alone generates a [truncated hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864030), while the *\3.4.3.12* only exists in this 2-uniform tiling. There are 2 3-uniform tilings that contain both of these vertex figures among one more.

It has [square](wikipedia://en/2863809) [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2868583#Wallpaper_groups), p4m, \[4,4\], (\*442). It is also called a [demiregular tiling](wikipedia://en/46891994) by some authors.

## Circle Packing {#Circle_Packing}

This 2-uniform tiling can be used as a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665). Cyan circles are in contact with 3 other circles (1 cyan, 2 pink), corresponding to the V3.12<sup>2</sup> planigon, and pink circles are in contact with 4 other circles (2 cyan, 2 pink), corresponding to the V3.4.3.12 planigon. It is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the ambo operation on the tiling, with the cyan and pink gap polygons corresponding to the cyan and pink circles (one dimensional duals to the respective planigons). Both images coincide.

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling has [kite](wikipedia://en/84840) ('ties') and [isosceles triangle](wikipedia://en/315428) faces, defined by [face configurations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration): V3.4.3.12 and V3.12.12. The kites meet in sets of 4 around a center vertex, and the triangles are in pairs making [planigon rhombi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_convex_uniform_tilings). Every four kites and four isosceles triangles make a square of side length $2+\sqrt{3}$.

::: {#o12 .table cue=table}

| *Image: Small Star Uniform 2 Tiling-2.svg.* Dual tiling | *Image: Semiplanigon\_V.3.4.3.12\_(Desmos\_Generated).png.* V3.4.3.12 Semiplanigon *Image: Tiling face 3-12-12.svg.* V3.12.12 [Planigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_convex_uniform_tilings) |
|---|---|

:::

This is one of the only dual uniform tilings which only uses planigons (and semiplanigons) containing a 30° angle. Conversely, 3.4.3.12; 3.12<sup>2</sup> is one of the only uniform tilings in which every vertex is contained on a dodecagon.
