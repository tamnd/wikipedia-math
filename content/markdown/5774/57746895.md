# Ali Reza Ashrafi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ali Reza Ashrafi"}

**Ali Reza Ashrafi** (10 May 1964 – 9 January 2023) was an Iranian mathematician who worked in [computational group theory](wikipedia://en/618584) and [mathematical chemistry](wikipedia://en/1570072). Ashrafi was a professor at the department of pure mathematics of the [University of Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kashan) and the vice president of the [International Academy of Mathematical Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_Mathematical_Chemistry).[^o4]

:::

He received his PhD in 1995 from the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran), with thesis *Irreducible character table of the group* $\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{SL}_5(3))$ written under the direction of [Mohammad Reza Darafsheh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Reza_Darafsheh).

## Death {#Death}

Ashrafi died in a car accident on the way home in [Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashan) on 9 January 2023.[^o7]
