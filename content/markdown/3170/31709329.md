# Terry Lyons (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Terry Lyons"}

**Terence John Lyons** [FLSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales) is a British mathematician, specialising in [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis). Lyons, Professor at Imperial College and at the University of Oxford (both part time) and previously the [Wallis Professor of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/45546398), is a fellow of [St Anne's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Anne's_College,_Oxford). He was the director of the [Oxford-Man Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford-Man_Institute) from 2011 to 2015 and the president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) from 2013 to 2015.[^o6] His mathematical contributions have been to [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), the [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [stochastic differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_differential_equations), and [quantitative finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_finance). In particular he developed what is now known as the theory of [rough paths](wikipedia://en/38309455). Together with Patrick Kidger he proved a [universal approximation theorem for neural networks of arbitrary depth](wikipedia://en/18543448#Arbitrary-depth_case).[^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Lyons obtained his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) and his [D.Phil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

## Career {#Career}

Lyons has held positions at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA), [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and since 2000 has been [Wallis Professor of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/45546398) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o11] He was the Director of the [Oxford-Man Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford-Man_Institute) at the University of Oxford from 15 June 2011 to 15 December 2015. He also held a number of visiting positions in Europe and North America.

Together with Zhongmin Qian he wrote the monograph *System Control and Rough Paths*,[^o12] and together with Michael J. Caruana and Thierry Lévy the book *Differential Equations Driven by Rough Paths*.[^o13]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1985 he was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize). In 1986 he was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). In 2000 he was awarded the [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28LMS%29) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).

He was elected a fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh in 1988, and elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2002; he was made a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics),[^o15] in 2005 and a fellow of the [Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales) in 2011. In 2013, he was elected president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o6]

In 2007 he was awarded a Doctor Honoris Causa from the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse), he was made an Honorary Fellow of [Aberystwyth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberystwyth_University) in 2010 and [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University) in 2012. In 2017 he was awarded an honorary Doctor of Mathematics from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).
