# István Gyöngy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="István Gyöngy"}

**István Gyöngy** (born 1951) is a Hungarian mathematician working in the fields of [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454), [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239) and their applications to nonlinear filtering and stochastic control. Recently, he has focused his attention on numerical analysis[^o3] and especially accelerated numerical methods,[^o4][^o5] making use of [Richardson extrapolation](wikipedia://en/650022).

:::

He obtained his Candidate degree at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1981 under the supervision of [Nicolai V. Krylov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolai_V._Krylov).

Formerly at the Department of Probability Theory and Statistics of the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) of Budapest, he is currently a professor at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), where he is head of the Probability and Stochastic Analysis research group.[^o6]
