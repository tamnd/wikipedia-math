# Michael Röckner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Röckner"}

*Image: Michael Röckner.jpg.* Michael Röckner

**Michael Röckner** is a mathematician working in the fields of [Stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) and Mathematical Physics.

:::

He obtained his PhD at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) in 1984 under the supervision of [Sergio Albeverio](wikipedia://en/28869024) and [Christopher John Preston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_John_Preston).

Together with Claudia Prévôt, he wrote the book *A Concise Course on Stochastic Partial Differential Equations*.[^o2]

- Prévôt, Claudia. (2007). "A concise course on stochastic partial differential equations". Springer. isbn:978-3-540-70781-3
