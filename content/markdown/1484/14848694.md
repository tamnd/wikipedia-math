::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of twelve pentagonal prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 12 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659), aligned in pairs with the axes of fivefold rotational symmetry of a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

:::

It results from composing the two [enantiomorphs](wikipedia://en/1170160) of the [compound of six pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/14848503). In doing so, the vertices of the two enantiomorphs coincide, with the result that the full compound has two pentagonal prisms incident on each of its vertices.
