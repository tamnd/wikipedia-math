# Pseudo-zero set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo-zero set"}

In [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) (a branch of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396)), the **pseudo-zero set** or **root neighborhood** of a [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652)-*m* polynomial *p*(*z*) is the set of all [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) that are roots of polynomials whose [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) differ from those of *p* by a small amount. Namely, given a [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) $\lvert \cdot\rvert$ on the space $\mathbb{C}^{m+1}$ of polynomial coefficients, the pseudo-zero set is the set of all zeros of all degree-*m* polynomials *q* such that $\lvert \boldsymbol{p} - \boldsymbol{q}\rvert$ (as vectors of coefficients) is less than a given *ε*.

:::
