::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of six pentagrammic prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160) symmetric arrangement of 6 [pentagrammic prisms](wikipedia://en/2860528), aligned with the axes of fivefold rotational symmetry of a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

:::
