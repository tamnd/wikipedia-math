::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of six pentagonal prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160) symmetric arrangement of six [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659), aligned with the axes of fivefold rotational symmetry of a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

:::
