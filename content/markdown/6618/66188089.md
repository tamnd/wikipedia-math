# Irène Waldspurger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Irène Waldspurger"}

**Irène Waldspurger** is a French mathematician and a researcher at the [Research Centre in Mathematics of Decision](wikipedia://en/38196034) (CEREMADE) where her research focuses on algorithms to solve [phase problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_problem),[^o3] a class of problem relevant for a large number of imaging techniques used in science and medicine. She is also a professor at [Paris Sciences et Lettres University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Sciences_et_Lettres_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Waldspurger competed for France in the 2006 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), winning a bronze medal.[^o5]

Waldspurger was a student of the prestigious [Ecole Normale Superieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Normale_Superieure), in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), where she was ranked first at the entrance exam in 2008.[^o6] She pursued her doctoral research at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), working on [phase retrieval](wikipedia://en/11081803) techniques using [wavelet transforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelet_transforms)[^o7] under the supervision of [Stephane Mallat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephane_Mallat), which she completed in 2015. She then joined the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) for a postdoctoral fellowship, before returning to France in 2017 to join the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2020, Waldspurger was one of the [Peccot Lecturers](wikipedia://en/60897406) and Peccot Prize winners of the [College de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_de_France),[^o9] and won the [CNRS Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_Medal).[^o10]
