# Gérard Laumon

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gérard Laumon"}

**Gérard Laumon** (; 1952 – 4 October 2025) was a French mathematician working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Background {#Background}

Laumon was born in 1952. He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) and [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University), Orsay. Laumon died on 4 October 2025.[^o5][^o6]

## Work {#Work}

In 1987, [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747) and Laumon formulated the [geometric Langlands conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_Langlands_conjecture) for [general linear groups](wikipedia://en/113564) $GL(n,K)$ over a [function field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_function_field) $K$.[^o8][^o9]

In 2008, Laumon and [Ngô Bảo Châu](wikipedia://en/11133659) proved the [fundamental lemma](wikipedia://en/25431009) for [unitary groups](wikipedia://en/173993), a component in the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905) in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o12][^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Laumon was awarded the Silver Medal of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) in 1987, the E. Dechelle prize of the French Academy of the Sciences in 1992, and the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) in 2004.[^o12] Laumon and Ngô received the Clay Research Award for the proof of the [fundamental lemma for unitary groups](wikipedia://en/25431009), a component in the Langlands program in number theory, in 2004.[^o12]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]
