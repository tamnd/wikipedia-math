# The Econometrics Journal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Econometrics Journal"}

***The Econometrics Journal*** was established in 1998 by the [Royal Economic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Economic_Society) to promote the general advancement and application of econometric methods and techniques to problems of relevance to modern economics. It aims to publish high quality research papers relevant to contemporary [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) in which primary emphasis is placed on important and original contributions of substantive direct or potential value in applications. It is particularly interested in path-breaking articles in econometrics and empirical economics that address leading cases rather than provide an exhaustive treatment.

:::

The journal is published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on behalf of the [Royal Economic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Economic_Society). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 4.571.[^o3]
