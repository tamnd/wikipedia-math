# Witt vector cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Witt vector cohomology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Witt vector cohomology** was an early [*p*-adic cohomology](wikipedia://en/46927724) theory for [algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_varieties) introduced by (txt). Serre constructed it by defining a [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of truncated [Witt rings](wikipedia://en/5573710) *W*<sub>*n*</sub> over a variety *V* and then taking the [inverse limit](wikipedia://en/15109) of the [sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357) groups *H*<sup>*i*</sup>(*V*, *W<sub>n</sub>*) of these sheaves. Serre observed that though it gives cohomology groups over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 0, it cannot be a [Weil cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weil_cohomology) theory because the cohomology groups vanish when *i* > dim(*V*). For [Abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties), (Serre 1958b) showed that one could obtain a reasonable first cohomology group by taking the direct sum of the Witt vector cohomology and the Tate module of the Picard variety.

:::
