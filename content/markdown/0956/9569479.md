# Maclaurin's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maclaurin's inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Maclaurin's inequality**, named after [Colin Maclaurin](wikipedia://en/7824), is a refinement of the [inequality of arithmetic and geometric means](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inequality_of_arithmetic_and_geometric_means).

:::

Let $a_1, a_2,\ldots,a_n$ be [non-negative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-negative_number) [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438), and for $k=1,2,\ldots,n$, define the averages $S_k$ as follows:

$$S_k = \frac{\displaystyle \sum_{ 1\leq i_1 < \cdots < i_k \leq n}a_{i_1} a_{i_2} \cdots a_{i_k}}{\displaystyle {n \choose k}}.$$

The numerator of this fraction is the [elementary symmetric polynomial](wikipedia://en/1440695) of degree $k$ in the $n$ variables $a_1, a_2,\ldots,a_n$, that is, the sum of all products of $k$ of the numbers $a_1, a_2,\ldots,a_n$ with the indices in increasing order. The denominator is the number of terms in the numerator, the [binomial coefficient](wikipedia://en/4668) $\tbinom n k.$ Maclaurin's inequality is the following chain of [inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489):

$$S_1 \geq \sqrt{S_2} \geq \sqrt[3]{S_3} \geq \cdots \geq \sqrt[n]{S_n},$$

with equality [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) all the $a_i$ are equal.

Maclaurin's inequality can be proved using [Newton's inequalities](wikipedia://en/10101991) or a generalised version of [Bernoulli's inequality](wikipedia://en/4734).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

For $n=2$, Maclaurin's inequality gives the [arithmetic mean-geometric mean inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_mean-geometric_mean_inequality) for two non-negative numbers.

For $n=4$, Maclaurin's inequality states:

$$\begin{aligned} &\quad \frac{a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4}{4} \\[8pt] &\ge \sqrt{\frac{a_1a_2+a_1a_3+a_1a_4+a_2a_3+a_2a_4+a_3a_4}{6}} \\[8pt] &\ge \sqrt[3]{\frac{a_1a_2a_3+a_1a_2a_4+a_1a_3a_4+a_2a_3a_4}{4}} \\[8pt] &\ge \sqrt[4]{a_1a_2a_3a_4}. \end{aligned}$$

:::
