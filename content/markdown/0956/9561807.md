# Michinori Yamashita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michinori Yamashita"}

**Michinori Yamashita** (born 1953 in Japan) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [Rissho University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rissho_University). He studied at [Sophia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_University) under Yukiyoshi Kawada and [Kiiti Morita](wikipedia://en/12034472).
