# Amitai Regev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amitai Regev"}

**Amitai Regev** (אמיתי רגב; born December 7, 1940) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424).

:::

He is the Herman P. Taubman Professor of Mathematics at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science). He received his doctorate from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1972, under the direction of [Shimshon Amitsur](wikipedia://en/19937535).

Regev has made significant contributions to the theory of [polynomial identity rings](wikipedia://en/25386269) (PI rings). In particular, he proved [Regev's theorem](wikipedia://en/31883149) that the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of two PI rings is again a PI ring.[^o3] He developed so-called "Regev theory" that connects PI rings to representations of the symmetric group, and hence to Young tableaux. He has made seminal contributions to the asymptotic enumeration of Young tableaux and tableaux of hook shape, and together with William Beckner proved the [Macdonald-Selberg conjecture](wikipedia://en/18583020) for the infinite [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras) of type *B*, *C*, and *D*.
