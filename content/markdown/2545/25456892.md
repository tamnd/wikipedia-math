# William A. Massey (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="William A. Massey"}

**William Alfred Massey** is an American mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476), the Edwin S. Wilsey Professor of Operations Research and Financial Engineering at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He is an expert in [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Massey was born in [Jefferson City, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_City,_Missouri) in 1956,[^o4] the son of Juliette and Richard Massey Sr., both educators. His family moved to [St. Louis, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis,_Missouri) when he was four.[^o7] He went to college at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), graduating in 1977.[^o7] Massey obtained his Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1981, with a thesis on [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578) supervised by [Joseph Keller](wikipedia://en/4454126).[^o5] His first research publication was developed during a summer program at [Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Laboratories) while he was a graduate student and was published in 1978. After earning his doctorate, he became a permanent staff member at Bell Labs.[^o7] In 2001, Massey moved to his current position at Princeton, becoming the first African-American Princeton undergraduate alumnus to return as a faculty member.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2006, Massey won the Blackwell–Tapia Prize of the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088) for his "outstanding record of achievement in mathematical research and his mentoring of minorities and women in the field of mathematics".[^o7] He was elected to the 2006 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o8] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] Massey's accomplishments have earned him recognition by [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) as a Black History Month 2018 Honoree.[^o10]
