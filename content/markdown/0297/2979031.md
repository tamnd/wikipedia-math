# Hans Grauert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Grauert"}

**Hans Grauert** (8 February 1930 in [Haren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haren,_Germany), [Emsland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emsland), Germany – 4 September 2011) was a German mathematician. He is known for major works on [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742)[^o3] and the application of [sheaf theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheaf_theory) in this area, which influenced later work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o4] Together with [Reinhold Remmert](wikipedia://en/856331) he established and developed the theory of [complex-analytic spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex-analytic_space).[^o5]

:::

## Professorship {#Professorship}

Grauert attended school at the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in [Meppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meppen,_Germany) before studying for a [semester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semester) at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) in 1949, and then at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster), where he was awarded his doctorate in 1954.[^o9]

He became professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1958, as successor to [C. L. Siegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._L._Siegel). The lineage of this chair traces back through an eminent line of mathematicians: [Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544), [Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), [Riemann](wikipedia://en/41980), and ultimately to [Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125).[^o7] Until his death, he was professor emeritus at Göttingen.

## Awards {#Awards}

Grauert was awarded a fellowship of the [Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina)[^o9] and the [von Staudt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Karl_Georg_Christian_von_Staudt_Prize).[^o9]
