# Nilpotent cone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nilpotent cone"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **nilpotent cone** $\mathcal{N}$ of a finite-dimensional [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587) $\mathfrak{g}$ is the set of elements that act nilpotently in all [representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_of_Lie_algebras) of $\mathfrak{g}.$ In other words,

:::

$$\mathcal{N}=\{ a\in \mathfrak{g}: \rho(a) \mbox{ is nilpotent for all representations } \rho:\mathfrak{g}\to \operatorname{End}(V)\}.$$

The nilpotent cone is an [irreducible subvariety](wikipedia://en/248808) of $\mathfrak{g}$ (considered as a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370)).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

The nilpotent cone of $\operatorname{sl}_2$, the Lie algebra of 2×2 [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) with vanishing [trace](wikipedia://en/43270), is the variety of all 2×2 traceless matrices with [rank](wikipedia://en/26561) less than or equal to $1.$

:::
