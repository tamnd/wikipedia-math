# Harnack's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harnack's inequality"}

In mathematics, **Harnack's inequality** is an inequality relating the values of a positive [harmonic function](wikipedia://en/55762) at two points, introduced by (txt). Harnack's inequality is used to prove [Harnack's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnack's_theorem) about the convergence of sequences of harmonic functions. (txt), and (txt) generalized Harnack's inequality to solutions of elliptic or parabolic [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). Such results can be used to show the interior [regularity](wikipedia://en/4005892) of [weak solutions](wikipedia://en/3555416).

:::

[Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266)'s solution of the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) uses a version of the Harnack inequality, found by (txt txt), for the [Ricci flow](wikipedia://en/288291).

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="The statement"}

## The statement {#The_statement}

*Image: Graph of Harnack's inequality.png.* A harmonic function (green) over a disk (blue) is bounded from above by a function (red) that coincides with the harmonic function at the disk center and approaches infinity towards the disk boundary.

**Harnack's inequality** applies to a non-negative function *f* defined on a closed ball in **R**<sup>*n*</sup> with radius *R* and centre *x*<sub>0</sub>. It states that, if *f* is continuous on the closed ball and [harmonic](wikipedia://en/55762) on its interior, then for every point *x* with |*x* − *x*<sub>0</sub>| = *r* \< *R*,

$$\frac{1-(r/R)} {[1+(r/R)]^{n-1}} f(x_0)\le f(x) \le {1+(r/R)\over [1 - (r/R)]^{n-1}} f(x_0).$$

In the plane **R**<sup>2</sup> (*n* = 2) the inequality can be written:

$${R-r\over R+r} f(x_0)\le f(x)\le {R+r\over R-r}f(x_0).$$

For general domains $\Omega$ in $\mathbf{R}^n$ the inequality can be stated as follows: If $\omega$ is a bounded domain with $\bar{\omega} \subset \Omega$, then there is a constant $C$ such that

$$\sup_{x \in \omega} u(x) \le C \inf_{x \in \omega} u(x)$$

for every twice differentiable, harmonic and nonnegative function $u(x)$. The constant $C$ is independent of $u$; it depends only on the domains $\Omega$ and $\omega$.

:::

::: {#o9 .proof cue=heading name="Proof of Harnack's inequality in a ball"}

## Proof of Harnack's inequality in a ball {#Proof_of_Harnack's_inequality_in_a_ball}

By [Poisson's formula](wikipedia://en/2214583)

$$f(x) = \frac 1 {\omega_{n-1}} \int_{|y-x_0|=R} \frac{R^2 -r^2}{R|x - y|^n} \cdot f(y) \, dy,$$

where *ω*<sub>*n* − 1</sub> is the area of the [unit sphere](wikipedia://en/27033090) in **R**<sup>*n*</sup> and *r* = |*x* − *x*<sub>0</sub>|.

Since

$$R-r \le |x-y| \le R+r,$$

the kernel in the integrand satisfies

$$\frac{R -r}{R (R+r)^{n-1}} \le \frac{R^2 -r^2}{R|x-y|^n}\le \frac{R+r}{R(R-r)^{n-1}}.$$

Harnack's inequality follows by substituting this inequality in the above integral and using the fact that the average of a harmonic function over a sphere equals its value at the center of the sphere:

$$f(x_0)= \frac 1 {R^{n-1}\omega_{n-1}} \int_{|y-x_0|=R} f(y)\, dy.$$

:::

## Elliptic partial differential equations {#Elliptic_partial_differential_equations}

For [elliptic partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_partial_differential_equations), Harnack's inequality states that the [supremum](wikipedia://en/39382) of a positive solution in some connected open region is bounded by some constant times the infimum, possibly with an added term containing a functional [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) of the data:

$$\sup u \le C ( \inf u + \|f\|)$$

The constant depends on the ellipticity of the equation and the connected open region.

## Parabolic partial differential equations {#Parabolic_partial_differential_equations}

There is a version of Harnack's inequality for linear parabolic PDEs such as [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415).

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a smooth (bounded) domain in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and consider the linear elliptic operator

$$\mathcal{L}u=\sum_{i,j=1}^n a_{ij}(t,x)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x_i\,\partial x_j} + \sum_{i=1}^n b_i(t,x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x_i} + c(t,x)u$$

with smooth and bounded coefficients and a [positive definite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive-definite_matrix) matrix $(a_{ij})$. Suppose that $u(t,x)\in C^2((0,T)\times\mathcal{M})$ is a solution of

$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}-\mathcal{L}u=0$ in $(0,T)\times\mathcal{M}$

such that

$$\quad u(t,x)\ge0 \text{ in } (0,T)\times\mathcal{M}.$$

Let $K$ be compactly contained in $\mathcal{M}$ and choose $\tau\in(0,T)$. Then there exists a constant *C* > 0 (depending only on *K*, $\tau$, $t-\tau$, and the coefficients of $\mathcal{L}$) such that, for each $t\in(\tau,T)$,

$$\sup_K u(t-\tau,\cdot)\le C \inf_K u(t,\cdot).$$
