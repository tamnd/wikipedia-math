# Mary Pugh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Pugh"}

**Mary Claire Pugh** is an [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) known for her research on [thin films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin_film), including the [thin-film equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin-film_equation) and [Hele-Shaw flow](wikipedia://en/16691615). She is a professor of mathematics and chair and graduate chair of the [Department of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Mathematics_%28University_of_Toronto%29) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), and was awarded the [Faculty of Arts and Science's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Faculty_of_Arts_and_Science) Outstanding Teaching Award in 2025.

:::

Pugh obtained her BA in mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and completed her Ph.D. in 1993 at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). Her dissertation, *Dynamics of Interfaces of Incompressible Fluids: The Hele-Shaw Problem*, was supervised by [Peter Constantin](wikipedia://en/82683896). Before moving to Toronto, she worked at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) and then as a faculty member at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), where she won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1999.
