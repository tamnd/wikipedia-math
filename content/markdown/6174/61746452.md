# Natalia Komarova

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Natalia Komarova"}

**Natalia L. Komarova** (born 1971) is a Russian-American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) whose research concerns the [mathematical modeling](wikipedia://en/20590) of cancer, the [evolution of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_linguistics), [gun control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_control), [pop music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music), and other [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system). She is a Professor of Mathematics and Dean's Scholar at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Komarova studied physics at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), earning a master's degree there in 1993. She completed her Ph.D. in 1998 at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona). Her dissertation, *Essays on Nonlinear Waves: Patterns under Water; Pulse Propagation through Random Media*, was supervised by [Alan C. Newell](wikipedia://en/38482776).

After postdoctoral research at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), Komarova became a lecturer at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 2000. She moved to [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in 2003 and to the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) in 2004. At UC Irvine, she was named a Chancellor's Professor in 2017. In 2024 she moved to [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Komarova won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2005. In 2023, Komarova was elected a [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o5]

## Books {#Books}

Komarova is married to UC Irvine [evolutionary biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_biology) Dominik Wodarz. She has written three books with Wodarz:

- *Computational Biology of Cancer: Lecture Notes and Mathematical Modeling* (World Scientific, 2005)
- *Dynamics Of Cancer: Mathematical Foundations Of Oncology* (World Scientific, 2014)
- *Targeted Cancer Treatment in Silico: Small Molecule Inhibitors and Oncolytic Viruses* (Birkhäuser, 2014)
