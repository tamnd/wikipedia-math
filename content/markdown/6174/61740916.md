# Covariant (invariant theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Covariant"}

In [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), a branch of algebra, given a group *G*, a **covariant** is a *G*-[equivariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant) [polynomial map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_map) $V \to W$ between [linear representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_representation) *V*, *W* of *G*.[^o2] It is a generalization of a classical convariant, which is a homogeneous [polynomial map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_map) from the space of binary *m*-forms to the space of binary *p*-forms (over the complex numbers) that is [$SL_2(\mathbb{C})$-equivariant](wikipedia://en/422711).[^o3]

:::
