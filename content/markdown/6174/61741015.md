# Jean-Pierre Ramis

*Image: Jean-Pierre Ramis.jpg.* Jean-Pierre Ramis in [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) in 2008

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Ramis"}

**Jean-Pierre Ramis**, born in 1943, is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o2]

:::

His work concerns the dynamic systems of complex field functions, discrete ([difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equations) and [q-differences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-difference)) and continuous ([differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309)), in particular the notions of [integrability](wikipedia://en/2573213) (Morales-Ramis theory) and the Galois differential theory.

In 1982, Ramis received the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602).
