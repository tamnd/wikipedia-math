# Alain Bensoussan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alain Bensoussan"}

**Alain Bensoussan** (born 12 May 1940) is a French mathematician. He is Professor Emeritus at the [University of Paris-Dauphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University) and Professor at the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Dallas) at [Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Alain Bensoussan was born on 12 May 1940 in [Tunis, Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis,_Tunisia). Bensoussan is a former student of the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) (X1960), a graduate of [ENSAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENSAE_ParisTech) and a doctor of mathematics from the Faculty of Sciences in Paris (1969)[^o5] under the supervision of [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500).

## Career {#Career}

He was a lecturer at the École polytechnique from 1970 to 1986 and a professor at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 1980 to 1985. He was Director of the [European Institute for Advanced Studies in Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Institute_for_Advanced_Studies_in_Management), Brussels from 1975 to 1977. He was President of [INRIA](wikipedia://en/318413) from 1984 to 1996, President of the [National Centre for Space Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNES) (CNES) from 1996 to 2003, President of the Council of the [European Space Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Space_Agency) (ESA) from 1999 to 2002.[^o5]

His former students include [Peng Shige](wikipedia://en/22631944), [Guy Pujolle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Pujolle), [Étienne Pardoux](wikipedia://en/47085794), [Jean-Michel Lasry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Michel_Lasry).[^o6]

- Member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o9]
- [Gay-Lussac Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay-Lussac_Humboldt_Prize) (1984)

- Commandeur of the [Ordre National du Mérite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_national_du_Mérite) (2000)
- Member of the [French Academy of Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Technologies) (2000)[^o10]
- Member of the [International Academy of Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_Astronautics) (2001)

- Officier of the Légion d'Honneur (2003)[^o11]
- Officer of the Order of Merit of the [Federal Republic of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Republic_of_Germany) (2003)
- [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Fellow, 2009
