# 500 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="500"}

**500** (**five hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 499 and preceding [501](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/501_%28number%29).

:::

## Mathematical properties {#Mathematical_properties}

500 = 2<sup>2</sup> × 5<sup>3</sup>. It is an [Achilles number](wikipedia://en/7894744), meaning that it is divisible by the [squares](wikipedia://en/213919) of its [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) factors (i.e. [4](wikipedia://en/208161) and [25](wikipedia://en/362204)) but is not a power itself, and a [Harshad number](wikipedia://en/641307), meaning it is divisible by the sum of its digits. It is the number of [planar partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planar_partitions) of 10.
