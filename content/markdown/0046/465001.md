# 700 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="700"}

**700** (**seven hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 699 and preceding 701.

:::

It is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) and the sum of four consecutive [primes](wikipedia://en/23666) (167 + 173 + 179 + 181).
