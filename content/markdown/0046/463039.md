# 666 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="666"}

**666** (**six hundred \[and\] sixty-six** or **triple six**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 665 and preceding 667. It is a [composite](wikipedia://en/82289) number.

:::

In [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity), 666 is referred to in most manuscripts of chapter 13 of the [Book of Revelation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Revelation) of the [New Testament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Testament) as the "[number of the beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_of_the_beast)" or "number of (a) man".[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

666 is the [sum](wikipedia://en/61338) of the first thirty-six [natural numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_numbers), which makes it a [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915):

$$\sum_{i=1}^{36} i = 1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 34 + 35 + 36 = 666.$$

666 is the largest triangular number that is also a [repdigit](wikipedia://en/216551).[^o8] It is also a [smith number](wikipedia://en/631045).

## In Christian religion {#In_Christian_religion}

### Number of the beast {#Number_of_the_beast}

*Image: Inferno Canto 34 (148619628).jpg.* 666 is often associated with [Satan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satan), who is illustrated here in an engraved interpretation by [Gustave Doré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Doré).

In the *[Textus Receptus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textus_Receptus)* manuscripts of the [New Testament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Testament), the [Book of Revelation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Revelation) () cryptically asserts 666 to be "the man's number" or "the number of a man" (depending on how the text is translated) associated with [the Beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beast_%28Revelation%29), an antagonistic creature that appears briefly about two-thirds into the apocalyptic vision. Some manuscripts of the original [Koine Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koine_Greek), such as *[Editio Regia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editio_Regia)* use the symbols χξϛ [chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_%28letter%29) [xi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi_%28letter%29) [stigma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stigma_%28letter%29) (or χξϝ with a [digamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digamma)),[^o12] while later translations of the New Testament, such as [Westcott and Hort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westcott_and_Hort), spell out the number in words.[^o13]

In modern popular culture, 666 has become one of the most widely recognized symbols for the [Antichrist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antichrist) or, alternatively [Satanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satanism). Earnest references to the number occur both among [apocalypticist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalypticism) Christian groups and in explicitly anti-Christian subcultures. References in contemporary Western art or literature are, more likely than not, intentional references to *the Beast* symbolism. Such popular references are therefore too numerous to list.

It is common to see the symbolic role of the [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) 666 transferred to the [numerical digit](wikipedia://en/280582) sequence 6-6-6. Some people take the Satanic associations of 666 so seriously that they actively avoid things related to 666 or the digits 6-6-6. This is known as [hexakosioihexekontahexaphobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexakosioihexekontahexaphobia).

There is a broad consensus in contemporary scholarship that the number of the beast refers to the [Roman emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_emperor) [Nero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nero), who reigned from 55 to 68 AD.[^o14][^o15] The Greek version of his name (Νέρων Καῖσαρ, "Neron Kaisar") transliterates into Hebrew as נרון קסר (NRON QSR), and, using [gematria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria), yields a numerical value of 666. His Latin name, "Nero Caesar", transliterated as **נרו קסר** (NRO QSR), produces the number [616](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/616_%28number%29#The_Number_of_the_Beast) instead, which is also cited as the Number of the Beast in some early biblical manuscripts.[^o16][^o17]

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

*Image: 666parade.jpeg.* A 666 float in a Paris street parade

- The [Roman numeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numeral) for 666, DCLXVI, has exactly one occurrence of all symbols whose value is less than 1000 in decreasing order (D = 500, C = 100, L = 50, X = 10, V = 5, I = 1).[^o20]
- *[The Number of the Beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Number_of_the_Beast_%28album%29)*, the 1982 album by English [heavy metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band [Iron Maiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Maiden), references 666 in its title and [the album's title song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Number_of_the_Beast_%28song%29).
- Is the magic sum, or sum of the [magic constants](wikipedia://en/546969) of a six by six [magic square](wikipedia://en/52202), any row or column of which adds up to 111.
- Is the sum of all the numbers on a [roulette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roulette) wheel (0 through 36).[^o21] This is a [corollary](wikipedia://en/168865) of the fact that the number is a [Triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915), as mentioned earlier.
- Was a winning lottery number in the [1980 Pennsylvania Lottery scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Pennsylvania_Lottery_scandal), in which equipment was tampered to favor a [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4_%28number%29) or [6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_%28number%29) as each of the three individual random digits.[^o22]
- Was the original name of the Macintosh [SevenDust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SevenDust_%28computer_virus%29) computer virus that was discovered in 1998. It is also the name of an extension that SevenDust can add to an uninfected Macintosh.[^o23]
- The number is a frequent visual element of [Aryan Brotherhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryan_Brotherhood) [tattoos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tattoo).[^o24]
- [Aleister Crowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleister_Crowley) adopted the title "the Beast 666".[^o25] As such, 666 is also associated with him, his work, and his religious philosophy of [Thelema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelema).
- [Molar mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molar_mass) of the [high-temperature superconductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-temperature_superconductor) [YBa<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yttrium_barium_copper_oxide).
- In [Chinese numerology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_numerology), the number is considered to be lucky and is often displayed in shop windows and neon signs.[^o26][^o27] In China, 666 can mean "everything goes smoothly" (the number six has the same pronunciation as the character 溜, which means "smooth".[^o28]
- Is commonly used by [Internet service providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_service_provider) to [blackhole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_hole_%28networking%29) traffic using [BGP communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BGP_communities).[^o29]
- [666 Fifth Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/666_Fifth_Avenue) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), which was bought for \$1.8 billion in 2007, was the most expensive real estate deal in New York's history.[^o30]
- 666 dating rule: a varied [manosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manosphere) trope exploring the notion of high standards of modern western women wherein a man dating is required to be six feet in height, making a six figure salary, six-pack abdomen, a six-hundred horsepower vehicle, six years of higher education and having a six inch phallus.[^o31][^o32]
- 666 kHz - a radio frequency on amplitude modulation/[medium wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_wave) broadcast band using the [Geneva Frequency Plan of 1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Frequency_Plan_of_1975).
