# Ralf J. Spatzier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ralf J. Spatzier"}

**Ralf Jürgen Spatzier** is a [British American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_American) mathematician specialising in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).

:::

Spatzier received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) in 1983 under the joint supervision of [Caroline Series](wikipedia://en/43764434) and [Anatole Katok](wikipedia://en/43275327) and joined [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) as an assistant professor. In 1990 he moved to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) where he is now a full professor.[^o4] He is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
