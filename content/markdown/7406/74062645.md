# Nedda Friberti

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nedda Friberti"}

**Nedda Friberti** (born 13 September 1913, date of death unknown) was an Italian [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_refugees_from_German-occupied_Europe_in_the_United_Kingdom) mathematician who fled Italy as a result of the German occupation of the [Italian Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Social_Republic) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).[^o3][^o4] She was denied entry into the United States by the [Emergency Committee in Aid of Displaced Foreign Scholars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Committee_in_Aid_of_Displaced_Foreign_Scholars).[^o5] In 1939, she was able to flee to [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) to escape persecution.[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Friberti was born in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste) in 1913.[^o4] She received a double degree in Mathematics and Physics. She worked as an assistant professor of Mathematics and Geometry at the [Technical Institute Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foligno) in 1937.[^o8][^o9]

Before teaching mathematics Friberti worked at the Geophysical Institute of the [Royal Italian Talassografic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Italy) (Council of Italy).

In 1939 Friberti applied for migration into the United States by submitting an application for the Emergency Committee in Aid of Displaced Foreign Scholars.[^o10] Thousands of scholars, including 80 female scientists and mathematicians, applied to this committee during the second World War.[^o10] Friberti's application was denied by the committee because it was deemed that she would not have an easy time finding a new job in the United States.[^o8]

Ultimately, Friberti escaped to Switzerland.[^o11]
