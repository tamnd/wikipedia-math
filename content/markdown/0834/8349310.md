# Biangular coordinates

*Image: BiangularCoordinateSystem.JPG.* Biangular coordinates

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Biangular coordinates"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **biangular coordinates** are a [coordinate system](wikipedia://en/81931) for the [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) where $C_1$ and $C_2$ are two fixed points, and the position of a point *P* not on the [line](wikipedia://en/946975) $\overline{C_1C_2}$ is determined by the [angles](wikipedia://en/1196) $\angle PC_1C_2$ and $\angle PC_2C_1.$[^o2]

:::

The [sine rule](wikipedia://en/113222) can be used to convert from biangular coordinates to [two-center bipolar coordinates](wikipedia://en/8367611).

::: {#o4 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Biangular coordinates can be used in geometric modelling and [CAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAD).[^o5][^o6]
