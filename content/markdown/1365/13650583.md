# Malliavin's absolute continuity lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malliavin's absolute continuity lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) — specifically, in [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory) — **Malliavin's absolute continuity lemma** is a result due to the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Paul Malliavin](wikipedia://en/10459388) that plays a foundational rôle in the regularity ([smoothness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function)) [theorems](wikipedia://en/30977) of the [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875). Malliavin's lemma gives a sufficient condition for a [finite](wikipedia://en/4316185) [Borel measure](wikipedia://en/4322) to be [absolutely continuous](wikipedia://en/252670) with respect to [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let *μ* be a finite Borel measure on *n*-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. Suppose that, for every *x* ∈ **R**<sup>*n*</sup>, there exists a constant *C* = *C*(*x*) such that

$$\left| \int_{\mathbf{R}^{n}} \mathrm{D} \varphi (y) (x) \, \mathrm{d} \mu(y) \right| \leq C(x) \| \varphi \|_{\infty}$$

for every *C*<sup>∞</sup> function *φ* : **R**<sup>*n*</sup> → **R** with [compact support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_support). Then *μ* is absolutely continuous with respect to *n*-dimensional Lebesgue measure *λ*<sup>*n*</sup> on **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. In the above, D*φ*(*y*) denotes the [Fréchet derivative](wikipedia://en/2735727) of *φ* at *y* and ||*φ*||<sub>∞</sub> denotes the [supremum norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum_norm) of *φ*.

:::
