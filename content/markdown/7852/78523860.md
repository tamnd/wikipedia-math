# Eilenberg–Watts theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eilenberg–Watts theorem"}

In mathematics, specifically [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), the **Eilenberg–Watts theorem** tells when a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) between the [categories of modules](wikipedia://en/11231812) is given by an application of a [tensor product](wikipedia://en/2993836). Precisely, it says that a functor $F : \mathbf{Mod}_R \to \mathbf{Mod}_S$ is additive, is right-exact and preserves coproducts if and only if it is of the form $F \simeq - \otimes_R F(R)$.[^o2]

:::

For a proof, see [The theorems of Eilenberg & Watts (Part 1)](https://specksofmath.wordpress.com/2015/04/26/the-theorems-of-eilenberg-watts-part-1/)
