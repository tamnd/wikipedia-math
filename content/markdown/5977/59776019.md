# Ruth I. Michler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth I. Michler"}

**Ruth Ingrid Michler** (March 8, 1967 to November 1, 2000)[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] was an American-born mathematician of German descent who lived and worked in the United States. She earned her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley),[^o7] and she was a tenured associate professor at the [University of North Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas). She died at the age of 33 while visiting [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University), after which at least three memorial conferences were held in her honor, and the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) was established in her memory.

:::

## Early years {#Early_years}

Michler was the daughter of German mathematician Gerhard O. Michler and was born in [Ithaca, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca,_New_York) while her family was visiting [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) from Germany.[^o3] She grew up in Germany, living in [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tübingen), [Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giessen), and [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen).[^o4] She completed her undergraduate studies in 1988 at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), graduating summa cum laude.[^o5][^o9]

## Doctoral studies and research {#Doctoral_studies_and_research}

Michler earned her Ph.D. in mathematics in 1993 from the University of California, Berkeley. Her dissertation is titled "Hodge components of cyclic homology of affine hypersurfaces."[^o7][^o11] Her advisors were [Mariusz Wodzicki](wikipedia://en/57858922) and [Arthur Ogus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Ogus). She spent the 1993–1994 academic year as a postdoc at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) working with Leslie Roberts. In 1994, she joined the tenure-track faculty at the University of North Texas where she earned tenure in 2000.

## Memorial conferences and prize {#Memorial_conferences_and_prize}

Michler was killed in an accident in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston) on November 1, 2000, when she was struck by a construction vehicle while on her bicycle.[^o6][^o13][^o9][^o14] Several conferences were organized in her honor.[^o15][^o16] Two conferences resulted in a volume of papers dedicated to her memory[^o17] In 2007 the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) inaugurated the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) which is "awarded annually to a woman recently promoted to Associate Professor or an equivalent position in the mathematical sciences".[^o18]
