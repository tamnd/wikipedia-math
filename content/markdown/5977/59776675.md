# AWM–Microsoft Research Prize in Algebra and Number Theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AWM–Microsoft Research Prize in Algebra and Number Theory"}

The **AWM–Microsoft Research Prize in Algebra and Number Theory** and is a prize given every other year by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) to an outstanding young female researcher in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) or [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). It was founded in 2012 by [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) and first issued in 2014.

:::

## Winners {#Winners}

- [Sophie Morel](wikipedia://en/36360630) (2014), for her research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), particularly her contributions to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905), an application of her results on weighted [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060), and a new proof of Brenti's combinatorial formula for [Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhdan-Lusztig_polynomials).
- [Lauren Williams](wikipedia://en/43551379) (2016), for her research in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), particularly her contributions on the totally nonnegative [Grassmannian](wikipedia://en/373810), her work on [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423), and her proof (with Musiker and Schiffler) of the famous Laurent positivity conjecture.
- [Melanie Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanie_Wood) (2018), for her research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), particularly her contributions in arithmetic statistics and [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041), as well as her work with [Ravi Vakil](wikipedia://en/5981037) on the limiting behavior of natural families of varieties.
- [Melody Chan](wikipedia://en/61332845) (2020), in recognition of her advances at the interface between algebraic geometry and combinatorics.
- [Jennifer Balakrishnan](wikipedia://en/58512088) (2022), in recognition of her advances in computing rational points on algebraic curves over number fields.
- [Yunqing Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056) (2024), for "work in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), including results on the Grothendieck–Katz $p$-curvature conjecture, a conjecture of Ogus on algebraicity of cycles, arithmetic intersection theory, and the unbounded denominators conjecture of Atkin and Swinnerton-Dyer"
