# Juan Andres (Burrul)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juan Andres"}

*Image: Juan Andres.png.* Front page of *Sumario breve de la practica de la aritmetica* (1515)

**Juan Andres** was a 16th-century [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known by his book on [arithmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetics).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Juan Andres was also the author of a book against Muslim religion entitled *Confusion de la secta mahomatica y d'l Alcorā* where he states that he was [faqih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faqih) of [Xàtiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xàtiva).[^o4]

Juan Andres is the writer of the book *Sumario breue d'la pratica d'la arithmetica d'todo el curso de larte mercantivol bien declarado*.[^o5] The book, as it is stated in the last page, was written in [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza) in 1514 and printed in [Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia) in 1515 by the printer Joan Joffre. The book is devoted to D. Serafin, earl of La Oliva and lord of [Nules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nules) and [Pego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pego,_Alicante).

Andres states in his book that his principal referent is [Luca Pacioli](wikipedia://en/18473) (Lucas de Burgo),[^o6] however he did not include nothing of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923).[^o7] Other characteristic of the book is that, contrary of other Spanish scholastic who considered usury the interest rate, Andres considers that arithmetic and well done accounting is in benefit of trade, providing it with honesty and safety.[^o8]
