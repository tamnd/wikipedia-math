# Qualitative theory of differential equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Qualitative theory of differential equations"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **qualitative theory of differential equations** studies the behavior of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) by means other than finding their solutions. It originated from the works of [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740) and [Aleksandr Lyapunov](wikipedia://en/65425). There are relatively few differential equations that can be solved explicitly, but using tools from [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), one can "solve" them in the qualitative sense, obtaining information about their properties.

:::

It was used by [Benjamin Kuipers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Kuipers) in the book *Qualitative reasoning: modeling and simulation with incomplete knowledge* to demonstrate how the theory of [PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564) can be applied even in situations where only qualitative knowledge is available.
