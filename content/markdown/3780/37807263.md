# Argument shift method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Argument shift method"}

In mathematics, the **argument shift method** is a method for constructing functions in involution with respect to [Poisson–Lie brackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson–Lie_bracket), introduced by (txt). They used it to prove that the [Poisson algebra](wikipedia://en/345704) of a finite-dimensional [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587) contains a complete commuting set of polynomials.

:::
