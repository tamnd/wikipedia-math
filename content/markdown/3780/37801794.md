# Shigeru Mukai

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Shigeru Mukai"}

**Shigeru Mukai** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Work {#Work}

He introduced the [Fourier–Mukai transform](wikipedia://en/8465724) in 1981 in a paper on [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties), which also made up his doctoral thesis. His research since has included work on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) on [K3 surfaces](wikipedia://en/646116), three-dimensional [Fano varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fano_varieties), [moduli theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_theory), and non-commutative [Brill–Noether theory](wikipedia://en/599865). He also found a new counterexample to [Hilbert's 14th problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_14th_problem) (the first counterexample was found by [Nagata](wikipedia://en/3086117) in 1959).
