# Outline of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz

*The following [outline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_%28list%29) is provided as an overview of and topical guide to Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz"}

**[Gottfried Wilhelm (von) Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281)** (1 July 1646 \[O.S. 21 June\] – 14 November 1716); German polymath, philosopher logician, mathematician.[^o2] Developed differential and integral calculus at about the same time and independently of Isaac Newton. Leibniz earned his keep as a lawyer, diplomat, librarian, and genealogist for the House of Hanover, and contributed to diverse areas. His impact continues to reverberate, especially his original contributions in logic and binary representations.[^o3]

:::
