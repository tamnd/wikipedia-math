# Irrelevant ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Irrelevant ideal"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **irrelevant ideal** is the [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of a [graded ring](wikipedia://en/103094) generated by the [homogeneous elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_element) of degree greater than zero. It corresponds to the origin in the [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834), which cannot be mapped to a point in the [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). More generally, a [homogeneous ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_ideal) of a graded ring is called an **irrelevant ideal** if its [radical](wikipedia://en/252009) contains the irrelevant ideal.[^o2]

:::

The terminology arises from the connection with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). If *R* = *k*\[*x*<sub>0</sub>, ..., *x<sub>n</sub>*\] (a [multivariate polynomial ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_polynomial_ring) in *n*+1 variables over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) *k*) is graded with respect to [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652), there is a [bijective correspondence](wikipedia://en/3942) between [projective algebraic sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_algebraic_set) in [projective *n*-space over *k*](wikipedia://en/242135) and homogeneous, [radical ideals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_ideal) of *R* not equal to the irrelevant ideal; this is known as the [projective Nullstellensatz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_Nullstellensatz).[^o3] More generally, for an arbitrary graded ring *R*, the [Proj construction](wikipedia://en/2285000) disregards all irrelevant ideals of *R*.[^o4]
