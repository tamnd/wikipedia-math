# Denjoy–Luzin–Saks theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denjoy–Luzin–Saks theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Denjoy–Luzin–Saks theorem** states that a function of generalized [bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673) in the restricted sense has a [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) [almost everywhere](wikipedia://en/44688), and gives further conditions of the set of values of the function where the derivative does not exist. [N. N. Luzin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._N._Luzin) and [A. Denjoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Denjoy) proved a weaker form of the theorem, and (txt, theorem 7.2, page 230) later strengthened their theorem.

:::
