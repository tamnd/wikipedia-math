# Determinantal conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Determinantal conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **determinantal conjecture** of (txt) and (txt) asks whether the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of a sum *A* + *B* of two *n*-by-*n* [normal](wikipedia://en/170366) [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) *A* and *B* lies in the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the *n*! points Π<sub>*i*</sub> (*λ*(*A*)<sub>*i*</sub> + *λ*(*B*)<sub>*σ*(*i*)</sub>), where the numbers *λ*(*A*)<sub>*i*</sub> and *λ*(*B*)<sub>*i*</sub> are the [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of *A* and *B*, and *σ* is an element of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) *S<sub>n</sub>*.

:::
