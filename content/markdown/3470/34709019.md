# Denjoy–Luzin theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denjoy–Luzin theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Denjoy–Luzin theorem**, introduced independently by (txt) and (txt) states that if a [trigonometric series](wikipedia://en/4099125) converges absolutely on a set of positive measure, then the sum of its [coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficients) converges absolutely, and in particular the trigonometric series converges absolutely everywhere.

:::
