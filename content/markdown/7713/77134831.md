# Matt Davison

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matt Davison"}

**Matt Davison** is a Canadian mathematician and university professor. He is a currently serving as Professor at the Departments of Statistical & Actuarial Science[^o3] and Mathematics,[^o4] Dean of the Faculty of Science[^o5] at [Western University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_University_%28Canada%29). Davison is also the Director of Canada's Financial Wellness lab.[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

After graduating from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) with a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering Science (1991), Davison attended [Western University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_University_%28Canada%29) where he earned a M.Sc (1993) and Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics (1995).

## Career and work {#Career_and_work}

Before entering academia, Davison worked as Assistant Vice President of Equity Arbitrage at [Deutsche Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Bank) Canada from 1997 to 1999.[^o9] Prior to this role, he was a [postdoctoral research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research_fellow) at the Physiology Institute of the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern), Switzerland, from 1995 to 1997.[^o9]

Since joining Western University in 1999,[^o9] Davison has focused his research in the field of [quantitative finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_finance). His significant contributions to the field include authoring *Quantitative Finance: A Simulation-Based Introduction Using Excel in 2014*,[^o10] 9 book chapters, and publishing over 80 papers.[^o11] Davison has 23 PhD students graduated from his research group, and 4 PhD students in current progress.[^o9]

Davison held a Tier 2 [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in [Quantitative Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_finance) from 2006 to 2016.[^o12][^o13] In 2015, he was named a [Fellow of the Fields Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Fields_Institute) for Research in the Mathematical Sciences,[^o14] further recognizing his impact in the academic community. Davison's research has been well funded by [MITACS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitacs),[^o15] [NSERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Sciences_and_Engineering_Research_Council)[^o16] and private sector partners throughout his academic career.

Davison was Chair of the Department of Statistical & Actuarial Sciences from 2014 to 2017 and Acting Chair of the Applied Math Department from 2015 - 2017.[^o17] From 2017 to 2018, he served as the founding director of Western's School of Mathematical & Statistical Sciences,[^o18] which was designed to enhance collaborative research and education.[^o19] That same year, he was appointed Dean of the Faculty of Science,[^o20] a role he continues to fulfill.[^o5]

In addition to his university duties, Davison has served the broader academic community. His roles included President of The Canadian Applied and Industrial Mathematics Society[^o21] from 2017 to 2019[^o22] and a board member of the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) for Research in the Mathematical Sciences from 2018 to 2021 [^o23] and of the Institute for Catastrophic Loss Reduction[^o24] since 2018.[^o25]

In 2021, Davison co-founded Canada's Financial Wellness lab,[^o6][^o26] which he now leads. This multi-university,[^o27] multi-stakeholder[^o28] initiative focuses on developing quantitative finance and data analytics solutions that will enable Canadian households to enhance their financial resilience.[^o29] The lab is a collaboration of several universities,[^o27] including [Wilfrid Laurier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfrid_Laurier_University), [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University), the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia_Okanagan), and the [University of Winnipeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Winnipeg), along with industry partners[^o28] including [Coast Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast_Capital_Savings), [Libro Credit Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libro_Credit_Union), [National Payroll Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Payroll_Association), [Ceridian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceridian), [Aligned Capital Partners (CI Financial)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CI_Financial), and [Environics Analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environics_Analytics), to broaden its impact and reach.
