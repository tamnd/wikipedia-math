# Fred Piper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fred Piper"}

**Fred C. Piper** (1940–2024)[^o2][^o3] was a [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Information Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Security) and [Honorary Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Fellow) at [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London) and is known for being the founder of the [Information Security Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Security_Group).[^o4]

:::

Piper studied [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) (Imperial College) in 1962 and received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in the same subject in 1964. He began his academic career as an Assistant Lecturer in Mathematics at Royal Holloway, University of London. He transferred to [Westfield College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield_College) (University of London) in 1969 and became a professor at Royal Holloway in 1975. Piper was the Founding Director of the Information Security Group at Royal Holloway that was awarded the [Queen's Anniversary Prize for Higher and Further Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_Anniversary_Prize) in 1998. During his time as a professor at the Information Security Group he supervised over 80 PhD students, including [Kenny Paterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Paterson).[^o5]

In addition to being a professor at Royal Holloway he held a number of visiting positions at other universities, including the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois), [State University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York), [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) and [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).

Piper was the first person in Europe that was awarded an honorary [CISSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CISSP), and was also awarded an honorary [CISM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISACA).[^o2]
