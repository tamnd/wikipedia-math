# Kirwan map

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kirwan map"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **Kirwan map**, introduced by British mathematician [Frances Kirwan](wikipedia://en/9007670), is the [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564)

:::

$$H^*_G(M) \to H^*(M /\!/_p G)$$

where

- $M$ is a [Hamiltonian G-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_group_action); i.e., a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) acted by a Lie group *G* with a [moment map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) $\mu: M \to {\mathfrak g}^*$.
- $H^*_G(M)$ is the [equivariant cohomology ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_cohomology_ring) of $M$; i.e.. the cohomology ring of the [homotopy quotient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_quotient) $EG \times_G M$ of $M$ by $G$.
- $M /\!/_p G = \mu^{-1}(p)/G$ is the [symplectic quotient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_quotient) of $M$ by $G$ at a regular central value $p \in Z({\mathfrak g}^*)$ of $\mu$.

It is defined as the map of equivariant cohomology induced by the inclusion $\mu^{-1}(p) \hookrightarrow M$ followed by the canonical isomorphism $H_G^*(\mu^{-1}(p)) = H^*(M /\!/_p G)$.

A theorem of Kirwan[^o2] says that if $M$ is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), then the map is surjective in rational coefficients. The analogous result holds between the [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) of the symplectic quotient and the equivariant topological K-theory of $M$.[^o3]
