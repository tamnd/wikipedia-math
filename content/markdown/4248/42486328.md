# Equivariant stable homotopy theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Equivariant stable homotopy theory"}

In mathematics, more specifically in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), the **equivariant stable homotopy theory** is a subfield of [equivariant topology](wikipedia://en/41144410) that studies a [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) with [group action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) instead of a space with group action, as in [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655). The field has become more active recently because of its connection to [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500).[^o2]

:::
