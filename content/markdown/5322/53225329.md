# Sigeru Mizohata

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sigeru Mizohata"}

**Sigeru (Shigeru) Mizohata** (溝畑 茂(みぞはた しげる); December 30, 1924 – June 25, 2002) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who specialized in the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sigeru Mizohata graduated from the Faculty of Science at the [Kyoto Imperial University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1947, where he was studying under [Hiroshi Okamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiroshi_Okamura). From 1954 to 1957 he studied in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) as an [international student](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_student); this left a lasting impact, with many of his research papers subsequently published in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language). His research interests mainly concerned [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825) and the use of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) in the theory of PDEs. He was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) by the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1986.

An observation Mizohata made on some work of Jiro Takeuchi related to the [Cauchy problem](wikipedia://en/1725027) evolved into the [Mizohata-Takeuchi conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizohata-Takeuchi_conjecture), to which [Hannah Cairo](wikipedia://en/80417092) found a counterexample [^o5] in 2025.
