# Gender survey question

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gender survey question"}

*Image: Wikimedia Foundation code of conduct survey gender question.png.* gender survey question from a July 2020 Wikimedia Foundation questionnaire for the Code of Conduct project

A **gender survey question** is the question in a [survey](wikipedia://en/13437523) asking for the respondent to report their [gender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender). In [questionnaire construction](wikipedia://en/269927) the survey designer may make this an [open-ended question](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-ended_question) or [multiple choice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_choice).

:::

In 2018 the [General Social Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Social_Survey) began releasing data on respondents' self-identified gender.[^o2]

Historically surveys have only offered options for respondents to indicate being either male or female.[^o3] More inclusive surveys may offer more options.[^o3]

Some respondents will not want to indicate being either male or female and will wish for additional options.[^o4]

Sex and gender are important demographic characteristics to understand in social research, but for information on these things to be meaningful, researchers must be thoughtful in collecting the data.[^o5]
