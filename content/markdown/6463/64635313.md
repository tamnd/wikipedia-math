# IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis"}

The ***IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on behalf of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139). It was established in 1981 and covers all aspects of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), including theory, development or use of practical [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms), and interactions between these aspects. The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are C.M. Elliott ([University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick)), A. Iserles ([University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge)), and E. Süli ([University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)).

:::
