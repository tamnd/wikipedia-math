# Normally flat ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normally flat ring"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **normally flat ring** along a proper ideal $I$ is a [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) $A$ such that $I^n/I^{n+1}$ is [flat](wikipedia://en/492616) over $A/I$ for all $n \geq 0$.

:::

The notion was introduced by [Hironaka](wikipedia://en/13822) in his proof of the [resolution of singularities](wikipedia://en/9914115) as a refinement of [equimultiplicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equimultiplicity) and was later generalized by [Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) and others.
