# Rajaditya (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rajaditya"}

**Rajaditya** ( c. 1190 CE) was a [Jain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jain) mathematician, scholar, and poet.[^o2] In the court of [Hoysala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoysala_Kingdom) king [Vishnuvardhana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishnuvardhana) he was known as the earliest author of mathematical works in [Kannada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kannada). He is remembered for his contributions to medieval Indian mathematics and literature in [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and background {#Early_life_and_background}

He described himself as a devotee of [Neminatha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neminatha) Tirthankara and was a native of "**Puvina Bage**" in North [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka) (today's [Raybag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raybag), [Belagavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belagavi) district), thought to be near modern [Hoovinahadagali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoovinahadagali) or [Bagewadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagewadi).[^o6]

He was known for his poetic talent and scholarly ability, likely serving in a royal court. His patrons included "[Bahubali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahubali)" and "**Bharatha**". He described his father as Shripathi, his mother as Vasantha, and his guru as **Shubhachandra Siddhanta deva**.[^o7]

Rajaditya was conferred several honorific titles for his mastery, including Ganita Vilasa, Ojevedanga, Padyavidyadhara, Uttamabhavyabhushana, and Jinapadakamalamadhukara.[^o8]

## Works {#Works}

Rajaditya is credited with authoring several mathematical and poetic works in Kannada. These include ***Vyavahāragaṇita***, ***Kṣetragaṇita***, ***Jainagaṇita Sūtrodāharaṇa***, ***Vyavahāraratna and Līlāvati***, and ***Chitrahasuge***.[^o10][^o11][^o12]

He is considered the first known author of mathematical treatises in Kannada. *Vyavahāragaṇita* is the only one fully preserved and was published in 1955 by the Government of Madras, edited by M. Mariappa Bhat.[^o13]
