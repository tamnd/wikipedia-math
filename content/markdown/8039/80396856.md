# Maarit Järvenpää

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maarit Järvenpää"}

**Maarit Järvenpää** is a Finnish mathematician specializing in [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_geometry), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). She is a professor of mathematics and dean of the Faculty of Natural Sciences at the [University of Oulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oulu).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Järvenpää has a Ph.D. from the [University of Jyväskylä](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jyväskylä), completed in 1994. Her doctoral dissertation, *On the Upper Minkowski Dimension, the Packing Dimension, and Orthogonal Projections*, was supervised by [Pertti Mattila](wikipedia://en/44664389).

At the time when she was hired as a full professor at the University of Oulu in 2009, she was one of only two women mathematics professors in Finland.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Järvenpää was named to the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 2019.
