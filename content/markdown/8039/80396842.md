# Patrick Dorey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Patrick Dorey"}

**Patrick Edward Dorey** is a British mathematical physicist. He is a Professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Dorey completed his PhD in 1990 at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University), where he was supervised by [Ed Corrigan](wikipedia://en/33985770).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Dorey returned to Durham in 1993 after working as a [postdoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoc) at [Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Theoretical_Physics,_Saclay) and [CERN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERN). His main research interest is conformal and integrable field theories.[^o3] He was elected a [Fellow of the Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Institute_of_Physics) in 2000.[^o3]

He has also served as a visiting lecturer at the [African Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) (AIMS) in [Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Town), South Africa, where he remains an associate faculty member.[^o7][^o8]
