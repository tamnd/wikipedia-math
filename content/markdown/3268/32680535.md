# Waleed Al-Salam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Waleed Al-Salam"}

**Waleed Al-Salam** (born 15 July 1926 in [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad), [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Mandate_of_Mesopotamia) – died 14 April 1996 in [Edmonton, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton,_Canada)) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials](wikipedia://en/32680308), [Al-Salam–Carlitz polynomials](wikipedia://en/32680463), [q-Konhauser polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-Konhauser_polynomials), and [Al-Salam–Ismail polynomials](wikipedia://en/32809449). He was a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).

:::

Born in Iraq, Baghdad, Al-Salam received his bachelor's degree in engineering physics (1950) and M.A. in mathematics (1951) from [University of California Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). He completed his education at [Duke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), receiving his [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy). for his dissertation on [Bessel polynomials](wikipedia://en/6523885) (1958).[^o2]
