# Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials** *Q*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*a*,*b*;*q*) are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081), introduced by (txt). (txt, 14.8) give a detailed list of the properties of Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$Q_n(x;a,b;q) = \frac{(ab;q)_n}{a^n}{}_3\phi_2(q^{-n}, ae^{i\theta}, ae^{-i\theta}; ab,0; q,q)$$

where *x* = cos(θ).

:::
