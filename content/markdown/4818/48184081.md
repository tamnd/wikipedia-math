# Mark A. Lewis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mark A. Lewis"}

**Mark Alun Lewis** (born 7 December 1962) is the Gilbert and Betty Kennedy Chair in [Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biology) at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria), [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) and former [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) of [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology) in the Department of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences and Department of Biological Sciences at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).[^o3] Among other topics, he has written extensively on the [Allee effect](wikipedia://en/1466225), [invasive species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasive_species), [parasitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parasitism), and biological dispersal.[^o4]

:::

Mark Lewis completed a B.Sc. with a double major in biology and a combined major in mathematics and computer science at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria) in 1987, where he worked with [Pauline van den Driessche](wikipedia://en/47807735). He earned his Ph.D. in mathematical biology from [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) in 1990. After obtaining his Ph.D., he spent a year at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) as an NSERC postdoctoral research associate, and then became an assistant professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1992.

In 2015, he was elected as a fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o5] He was elected as a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2017, "for contributions to mathematical biology and the study of spatial dynamics processes".[^o6] In 2018 the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o7] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2025.[^o8]
