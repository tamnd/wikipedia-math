# 8191 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="8191"}

**8191** (**eight thousand one hundred \[and\] ninety-one**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 8190, and preceding [8192](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8192). It is also known as M(13).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

8191 is an [odd number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odd_number) and the 1028th [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o4] It is also a [Jacobsthal-Lucas number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobsthal-Lucas_number). It is a [palindrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palindrome) and [repdigit](wikipedia://en/216551) in [base 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_2), which has 13 repeated digits of the number [one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One).[^o6]

### Mersenne prime {#Mersenne_prime}

It is most notable for being a [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908).[^o4][^o8] Since it is a Mersenne prime, it can be expressed as 2<sup>13</sup> − 1.[^o9] And since the [exponent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponent) number is prime, it is considered one.[^o10] It was discovered anonymously before 1461.[^o12]
