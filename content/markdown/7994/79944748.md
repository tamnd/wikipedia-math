# Raphael Yuster

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raphael Yuster"}

**Raphael** "**Raphy**" **Yuster** (רפאל יוסטר) is an Israeli mathematician specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa). He is a recipient of the [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize) for his work on [color-coding](wikipedia://en/22469695),A and is also known for the [Alon–Yuster conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alon–Yuster_conjecture) relating the [chromatic numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) of graphs to the number of disjoint copies of a smaller graph that can be found in a larger one, later proven by [János Komlós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/János_Komlós_%28mathematician%29), [Gábor N. Sárközy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gábor_N._Sárközy), and [Endre Szemerédi](wikipedia://en/663489).B

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Yuster was a student at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), where he received a bachelor's degree in 1989, a master's degree in 1991, and a Ph.D. in 1995. His doctoral dissertation, *Non Constructive Graph Theoretic Proofs and Their Algorithmic Aspects*, was supervised by [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651).

He has been a faculty member at the University of Haifa since 2004.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

With Noga Alon and [Uri Zwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uri_Zwick), Yuster was a recipient of the 2019 [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize), given for their work on [color coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_coding), an application of the [probabilistic method](wikipedia://en/173525) to [subgraph isomorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgraph_isomorphism).A

His work with Zwick on sparse [matrix multiplication](wikipedia://en/125280) received the 2023 [European Symposium on Algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Symposium_on_Algorithms) Test-of-Time Award.C
