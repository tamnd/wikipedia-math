# Hodge–Arakelov theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hodge–Arakelov theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Hodge–Arakelov theory** of [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) is an analogue of classical and [p-adic Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/26086853) for elliptic curves carried out in the framework of [Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229). It was introduced by (txt). It bears the name of two mathematicians, [Suren Arakelov](wikipedia://en/10705349) and [W. V. D. Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430). The main comparison in this theory remains unpublished as of 2019.

:::

Mochizuki's main comparison theorem in Hodge–Arakelov theory states (roughly) that the space of [polynomial functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_function) of degree less than *d* on the universal extension of a smooth elliptic curve in [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 0 is naturally [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/14828) (via restriction) to the *d* <sup>2</sup>-dimensional space of functions on the *d*-[torsion points](wikipedia://en/3039330). It is called a 'comparison theorem' as it is an analogue for Arakelov theory of comparison theorems in cohomology relating [de Rham cohomology](wikipedia://en/221530) to [singular cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cohomology) of complex varieties or [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) of *p*-adic varieties.

In (txt) and (txt) he pointed out that arithmetic [Kodaira–Spencer map](wikipedia://en/35205007) and [Gauss–Manin connection](wikipedia://en/4550529) may give some important hints for [Vojta's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34682455), [ABC conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_conjecture) and so on; in 2012, he published his [Inter-universal Teichmuller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-universal_Teichmuller_theory), in which he didn't use Hodge-Arakelov theory but used the theory of [frobenioids](wikipedia://en/36985868), anabelioids and [mono-anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265).
