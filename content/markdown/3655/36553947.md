# Antonio Maria Bordoni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Maria Bordoni"}

**Antonio Maria Bordoni** (19 July 1789 – 26 March 1860) was an Italian mathematician who did research on [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics). Joining the faculty of the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) in 1817, Bordoni is generally considered to be the founder of the mathematical school of Pavia. He was a member of various learned academies, notably the [Accademia dei XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze_detta_dei_XL). Bordoni's famous students were [Francesco Brioschi](wikipedia://en/6421852), [Luigi Cremona](wikipedia://en/1846002), [Eugenio Beltrami](wikipedia://en/543760), [Felice Casorati](wikipedia://en/5697549) and [Delfino Codazzi](wikipedia://en/703285).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Antonio Bordoni was born in Mezzana Corti (province of [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia)) on 19 July 1788, and graduated in Mathematics from Pavia on 7 June 1807.

After just two months he was appointed teacher of mathematics at the military School of Pavia, established by Napoleon, and held such office until 1816 when the school was closed due to the political situation of the times.

On 1 November 1817 he became full professor of elementary pure mathematics at the university and in 1818 he held the chair of [infinitesimal calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitesimal_calculus), [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608) and [hydrometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrometry), a discipline he taught for 23 years.

In 1827 and 1828 he was dean of the university itself. In 1854, as the Faculty of Mathematics of the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) (it previously belonged to the [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) faculty) was established, he was elected director of mathematical studies and held such office until his death, which occurred 26 March 1860, just one month after being appointed senator.
