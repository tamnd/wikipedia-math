# Per-comparison error rate

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Per-comparison error rate"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), **per-comparison error rate (PCER)** is the probability of a [Type I error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_error) in the absence of any [multiple hypothesis testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_comparisons) correction.[^o2] This is a liberal error rate relative to the [false discovery rate](wikipedia://en/4574271) and [family-wise error rate](wikipedia://en/4621448), in that it is always less than or equal to those rates.

:::
