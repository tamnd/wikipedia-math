# Ami Harten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ami Harten"}

*Image: Ami Harten PASSPORT Photo.jpg.*

**Amiram Harten** (1946–1994) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States)-[Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician). Harten made fundamental contribution to the development of [high-resolution schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-resolution_scheme) for the solution of [hyperbolic partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_partial_differential_equations). Among other contributions, he developed the [total variation diminishing](wikipedia://en/5677733) scheme, which gives an oscillation free solution for flow with [shocks](wikipedia://en/146253).[^o4]

:::

In 1980s, Harten along with [Björn Engquist](wikipedia://en/2070192), [Stanley Osher](wikipedia://en/3008100), and Sukumar R. Chakravarthy developed the essentially non-oscillatory (ENO) schemes. The article on ENO, titled, *Uniformly High Order Accurate Essentially Non-oscillatory Schemes, III* was published in [Journal of Computational Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Computational_Physics), in 1987 [^o5] and is one of the most cited papers in the field of [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing). It was republished in 1997 in the same journal.[^o6] Harten is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o7]

In 1990 Harten gave a talk on "Recent developments in shock-capturing schemes" at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Kyoto.[^o8]
