# Friedrich Julius Richelot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Julius Richelot"}

**Friedrich Julius Richelot** (6 November 1808 – 31 March 1875) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), born in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg). He was a student of [Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi](wikipedia://en/210340).

:::

He was promoted in 1831 at the Philosophical Faculty of the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) with a dissertation on the division of the circle into 257 equal parts (see references) and was a professor there.

Richelot authored numerous publications in German, French and [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin), among them — with his 1832 dissertation — the first known guide to the Euclidean construction of the [regular 257-gon](wikipedia://en/523430) with [compass and straightedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_and_straightedge_constructions).

In 1825, he joined the [Corps Masovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_Masovia_Königsberg_%28Potsdam%29).[^o3]

He died in Königsberg in 1875.
