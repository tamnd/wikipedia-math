# Isomorphism extension theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isomorphism extension theorem"}

In [field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_theory_%28mathematics%29), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **isomorphism extension theorem** is an important [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) regarding the extension of a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) to a larger field.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Isomorphism extension theorem"}

## Isomorphism extension theorem {#Isomorphism_extension_theorem}

The theorem states that given any field $F$, an [algebraic extension](wikipedia://en/2125) field $E$ of $F$ and an isomorphism $\phi$ mapping $F$ [onto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onto) a field $F'$ then $\phi$ can be extended to an isomorphism $\tau$ mapping $E$ onto an algebraic extension $E'$ of $F'$ (a [subfield](wikipedia://en/11634) of the [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of $F'$).

The proof of the isomorphism extension theorem in its most general setting, i.e. for the case of a field extension of infinite degree, depends on [Zorn's lemma](wikipedia://en/51442).

:::
