# Mathematische Zeitschrift

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematische Zeitschrift"}

***Mathematische Zeitschrift*** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) for *Mathematical Journal*) is a [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) for pure and applied mathematics published by [Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was founded in 1917, with its first issue appearing in 1918. It was initially edited by [Leon Lichtenstein](wikipedia://en/31433458) together with [Konrad Knopp](wikipedia://en/5630788), [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191), and [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522). Because Lichtenstein was Jewish, he was forced to step down as editor in 1933 under the [Nazi rule of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany);[^o5] he fled to Poland and died soon after.[^o4] The editorship was offered to [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118), but he refused,[^o5] and [Konrad Knopp](wikipedia://en/5630788) took it over. Other past editors include [Erich Kamke](wikipedia://en/22079478), [Friedrich Karl Schmidt](wikipedia://en/36084772), [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna), [Helmut Wielandt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmut_Wielandt), and [Olivier Debarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Debarre).[^o6]
