# Karl Mollweide

Carl Brandan Mollweide

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Mollweide"}

**Karl Brandan Mollweide** (3 February 1774 – 10 March 1825) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)[^o3] and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) who taught in [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_%28Saale%29) and [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig). In [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261), he rediscovered the formula now known as [Mollweide's formula](wikipedia://en/22213676). He invented a [map projection](wikipedia://en/51784) called the [Mollweide projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mollweide_projection).
