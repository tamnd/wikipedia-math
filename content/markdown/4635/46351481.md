# John E. Freund

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John E. Freund"}

**John Ernst Freund** (August 6, 1921 – August 14, 2004) was a prominent author of university level textbooks on [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and a mathematics professor at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University). Born in Berlin, Germany, he emigrated to [Mandatory Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) in the 1930s. He studied at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) and at the [University of California at Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Los_Angeles), from which he received his bachelor's degree. He did graduate work at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), from which he received his doctorate in 1952.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

In 1960 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o7]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Freund, John E. (1962). "Mathematical statistics". Prentice-Hall
