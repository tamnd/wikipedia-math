# Gaisi Takeuti

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gaisi Takeuti"}

**Gaisi Takeuti** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478).[^o4]

:::

After graduating from [Tokyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University), he went to [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) to study under [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736).

He later became a professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign). Takeuti was president (2003–2009) of the [Kurt Gödel Society](wikipedia://en/2129012), having worked on the book *Memoirs of a Proof Theorist: Godel and Other Logicians*. His goal was to prove the [consistency](wikipedia://en/75802) of the real numbers. To this end, [Takeuti's conjecture](wikipedia://en/2372007) speculates that a [sequent](wikipedia://en/252329) formalisation of [second-order logic](wikipedia://en/341428) has [cut-elimination](wikipedia://en/910234).[^o5] He is also known for his work on [ordinal diagrams](wikipedia://en/6108552) with Akiko Kino.
