# Backhouse's constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Backhouse's constant"}

**Backhouse's constant** is a [mathematical constant](wikipedia://en/48779551) named after Nigel Backhouse. Its value is approximately 1.456074948.

:::

It is defined by using the [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) such that the [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) of successive terms are the [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers),

$$P(x)=1+\sum_{k=1}^\infty p_k x^k=1+2x+3x^2+5x^3+7x^4+\cdots$$

and its [multiplicative inverse](wikipedia://en/229940) as a [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012),

$$Q(x)=\frac{1}{P(x)}=\sum_{k=0}^\infty q_k x^k.$$

Then:

$$\lim_{k \to \infty}\left | \frac{q_{k+1}}{q_k} \right \vert = 1.45607\ldots.$$

This limit was conjectured to exist by Backhouse, and later proven by [Philippe Flajolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippe_Flajolet).
