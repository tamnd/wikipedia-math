# Daniel Kráľ

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Kráľ"}

**Daniel Kráľ** (born June 30, 1978) is a Czech mathematician and computer scientist who works as a professor of mathematics and computer science at the [Masaryk University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masaryk_University). His research primarily concerns [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and graph [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He obtained his Ph.D. from [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) in 2004, under the supervision of [Jan Kratochvíl](wikipedia://en/46199120). After short-term positions at [TU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Berlin), Charles University, and the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), he returned to Charles University as a researcher in 2006, and became a tenured associate professor there in 2010. He was awarded the degree of [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) by the [Academy of Sciences of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Czech_Republic) in 2012, and in the same year moved to a professorship at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o3][^o6]

In 2018, Kráľ moved back to the Czech Republic and started working at Faculty of Informatics, [Masaryk University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masaryk_University), accepting the Donald Knuth professorship chair.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In the 1970s, [Michael D. Plummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_D._Plummer) and [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938) conjectured that every [bridgeless](wikipedia://en/1305071) [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) has an exponential number of [perfect matchings](wikipedia://en/44031), strengthening [Petersen's theorem](wikipedia://en/38481437) that at least one perfect matching exists. In a pair of papers with different sets of co-authors, Kráľ was able to show that this conjecture is true.[^o11][^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kráľ won first place and a gold medal at the [International Olympiad in Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympiad_in_Informatics) in 1996.[^o14]

In 2011, Kráľ won the [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics) for his work in graph theory, particularly citing his solution to the Plummer–Lovász conjecture and his results on [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743).[^o15] In 2014, he won a [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize) in Mathematics and Statistics; the award citation again included Kráľ's research on the Plummer–Lovász conjecture, as well as other publications of Kráľ on [pseudorandom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudorandom) [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) and systems of equations.[^o16]

He was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to extremal combinatorics and graph theory, and for service to the profession".[^o17]
