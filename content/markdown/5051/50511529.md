# Johan Jacob Ferguson

*Image: Ferguson\_-\_Labyrinthus\_algebrae,\_1667\_-\_883583.jpg.* *Labyrinthus algebrae* (1667)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johan Jacob Ferguson"}

**Johan Jacob Ferguson** was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th-century) [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who corresponded with [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born around 1630, probably in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague), and died before 24 November 1706, probably on 6 October 1691 in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam).[^o2]

In his 1667 book *Labyrinthus algebrae*, written in low [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language),[^o4] he shows the solutions of [cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_Equation) and [biquadratic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biquadratic_equation) using new methods.[^o5] The book had a partial translation in Latin (lost) and was sent to [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627).[^o6][^o7]
