# Daniel Bump

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Bump"}

**Daniel Willis Bump** (born 13 May 1952) is a mathematician who is a professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) working in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2015, for "contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), representation theory, [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and [random matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_matrix_theory), as well as mathematical exposition".[^o2]

:::

He has a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Reed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_College), where he graduated in 1974.[^o3] He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1982 under the supervision of [Walter Lewis Baily, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Lewis_Baily,_Jr.) Among Bump's doctoral students is president of the [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356), [Edray Goins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edray_Goins).
