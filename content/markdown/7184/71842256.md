# Aleksander Spivakovsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksander Spivakovsky"}

*Image: Олександр Володимирович Співаковський.jpg.* Spivakovsky in 2025

**Aleksander Spivakovsky** (Олександр Співаковський) is a Ukrainian academic administrator and mathematician. He is the rector of [Kherson State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kherson_State_University) and a professor and chair of [informatics](wikipedia://en/23997153), [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering), and [economic cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_cybernetics).[^o2] On October 21, 2016, Spivakovsky was elected an academician and corresponding member of the [National Academy of Educational Sciences of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Educational_Sciences_of_Ukraine).[^o2][^o3]
