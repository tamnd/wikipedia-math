# Marijka Batterham

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marijka Batterham"}

**Professor Marijka J. Batterham** is a multidisciplinary statistician, Director of the National Institute for Applied Statistics Research Australia,[^o2] and the first dedicated appointment as Director of the Statistical Consulting Centre[^o3] at the [University of Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wollongong).

:::

Batterham's educational background spans across multiple disciplines, including statistics, nutrition and dietetics, and biochemistry. She holds a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Science from the Faculty of Medicine[^o4] at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), a Master of Medical Statistics from the Centre for Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the [University of Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle_%28Australia%29), and a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in Nutrition and Dietetics from the [University of Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wollongong).

As of April 2023, in her position as Coordinator of the Data and Decision Science Initiative,[^o5] Batterham is responsible for directing and developing the initiative's research, teaching, and industry engagement programs. She holds dual professional qualifications as an Accredited Statistician[^o6] from [Statistical Society of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Society_of_Australia) and as an Advanced Accredited Practising Dietitian[^o7] from Dietitians Australia.[^o8] Throughout her career, Batterham has published primarily in the areas of statistical methodology, nutrition, and public health, and has a particular research interest in the design and analysis on lifestyle and health related interventions.[^o9][^o10]
