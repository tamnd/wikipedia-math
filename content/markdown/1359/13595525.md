# ICTP Ramanujan Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ICTP Ramanujan Prize"}

The **DST-ICTP-IMU Ramanujan Prize for Young Mathematicians from Developing Countries** is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) [prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize) awarded annually by the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) in Italy.[^o5] The prize is named after the Indian mathematician [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717). It was founded in 2004,[^o6] and was first awarded in 2005.

:::

The prize is awarded to a researcher from a developing country less than 45 years of age who has conducted outstanding research in a developing country.[^o7] The prize is supported by the [Ministry of Science and Technology (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Science_and_Technology_%28India%29) and [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) through the [Abel Fund](wikipedia://en/159285), with the cooperation of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868).
