# Yves Colin de Verdière

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yves Colin de Verdière"}

**Yves Colin de Verdière** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) in the late 1960s, obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1973, and then spent the bulk of his working life as faculty at [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble). He retired in December 2005.

## Work {#Work}

Colin de Verdière is known for work in [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713), in particular on the [semiclassical limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiclassical_physics) of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) (including [quantum chaos](wikipedia://en/313779)); in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) where he introduced a new [graph invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_invariant), the [Colin de Verdière graph invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_de_Verdière_graph_invariant); and on a variety of other subjects within [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

His contributions have been recognized by several awards: senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) from 1991 to 2001; [Prize Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_Ampère) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1999; Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2004; [Émile Picard Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émile_Picard_Medal) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2018. He was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), held in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) in 1986.
