# Ostrowski Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ostrowski Prize"}

The **Ostrowski Prize** is a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) [award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Award) given biennially for outstanding research accomplishments in mathematics and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o2][^o3] [Alexander Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091), a longtime professor at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), left his estate to the Ostrowski Foundation[^o4] in order to establish the prize.[^o3]

:::

## Recipients {#Recipients}

- 1989: [Louis de Branges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Branges)[^o2][^o3]
- 1991: [Jean Bourgain](wikipedia://en/1024131)[^o2][^o3]
- 1993: [Miklós Laczkovich](wikipedia://en/6021299) and [Marina Ratner](wikipedia://en/12687622)[^o2][^o3]
- 1995: [Andrew J. Wiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_J._Wiles)[^o2][^o3]
- 1997: [Yuri V. Nesterenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Valentinovich_Nesterenko) and [Gilles I. Pisier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilles_I._Pisier)[^o2][^o3]
- 1999: [Alexander A. Beilinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_A._Beilinson) and [Helmut H. Hofer](wikipedia://en/34920327)[^o2][^o3]
- 2001: [Henryk Iwaniec](wikipedia://en/9338319), [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak), and [Richard L. Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931)[^o2][^o3]
- 2003: [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708)[^o2][^o3]
- 2005: [Ben Green](wikipedia://en/1927952) and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498)[^o2][^o3]
- 2007: [Oded Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580)[^o2][^o3]
- 2009: [Sorin Popa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorin_Popa)[^o2][^o3]
- 2011: [Ib Madsen](wikipedia://en/38416618), [David Preiss](wikipedia://en/19372849), and [Kannan Soundararajan](wikipedia://en/8053624)[^o2][^o3][^o6]
- 2013: [Yitang Zhang](wikipedia://en/39441844)[^o2][^o3][^o7]
- 2015: [Peter Scholze](wikipedia://en/39409306)[^o2][^o3][^o8]
- 2017: [Akshay Venkatesh](wikipedia://en/6647964)[^o2][^o3][^o9]
- 2019: [Assaf Naor](wikipedia://en/38620456)[^o2][^o3][^o10]
- 2021: Tim Austin (mathematician)[^o2][^o3][^o11]
- 2023: [Jacob Tsimerman](wikipedia://en/48497888)[^o2][^o3][^o12]
- 2025: [Hong Wang](wikipedia://en/79379843)[^o2][^o13]
