# Poncelet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poncelet"}

The **poncelet** (symbol **p**) is an obsolete unit of power, once used in France and replaced by cheval-vapeur (ch, [metric horsepower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_horsepower)). The unit was named after [Jean-Victor Poncelet](wikipedia://en/764818).[^o3]

:::

One poncelet is defined as the power required to raise a hundred-kilogram mass ([quintal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintal)) at a velocity of one metre per second (100 [kilogram-force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilogram-force)·m/s).

1 p = 980.665 [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watt) = $\tfrac{4}{3}$ ch ≈ 1.315 hp (imperial horsepower)
