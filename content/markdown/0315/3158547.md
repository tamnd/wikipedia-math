# BID/60

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="BID/60"}

**BID/60**, also called **Singlet**,[^o3] was a British [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) machine. It was used by the British intelligence services from around 1949 or 1950 onwards. The system is a [rotor machine](wikipedia://en/196223), and would appear to have used 10 rotors. There are some apparent similarities between this machine and the US / NATO [KL-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KL-7) device.

:::

*Image: Singlet-rotor-tube-front.jpg.* The rotor tube of a Singlet machine, with 10 windows and stepping levers (similar to those on a [KL-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KL-7), suggesting 10 rotors.

In 2005, a Singlet machine was exhibited in the *Enigma and Friends* display at the [Bletchley Park museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bletchley_Park_museum).
