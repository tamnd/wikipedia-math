# Thurston elliptization conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Thurston elliptization conjecture"}

**Thurston elliptization conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thurston elliptization conjecture"}

[William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190)'s **elliptization conjecture** states that a closed [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) with finite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) is [spherical](wikipedia://en/1205989), i.e. has a [Riemannian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) of constant positive sectional curvature.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Relation to other conjectures"}

## Relation to other conjectures {#Relation_to_other_conjectures}

A 3-manifold with a Riemannian metric of constant positive sectional curvature is covered by the 3-sphere, moreover the group of covering transformations are isometries of the 3-sphere. If the original 3-manifold had in fact a trivial fundamental group, then it is [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) (via the [covering map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_map)). Thus, proving the elliptization conjecture would prove the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) as a corollary. In fact, the elliptization conjecture is [logically equivalent](wikipedia://en/169324) to two simpler conjectures: the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) and the [spherical space form conjecture](wikipedia://en/1424595).

The elliptization conjecture is a special case of Thurston's [geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642), which was proved in 2003 by [G. Perelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._Perelman).

:::
