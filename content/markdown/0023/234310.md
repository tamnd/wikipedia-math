# List of things named after James Joseph Sylvester

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after James Joseph Sylvester"}

The mathematician [J. J. Sylvester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Sylvester) was known for his ability to coin new names and new notation for mathematical objects,[^o2] not based on his own name. Nevertheless, many objects and results in mathematics have come to be named after him:[^o3]

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester–Gallai theorem"}

- The [Sylvester–Gallai theorem](wikipedia://en/1052632), on the existence of a line with only two of *n* given points.[^o5]

:::

  - [Sylvester–Gallai configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester–Gallai_configuration), a set of points and lines without any two-point lines.
  - [Sylvester matroid](wikipedia://en/36827387), a matroid without any two-point lines.[^o6]

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's determinant identity"}

- [Sylvester's determinant identity](wikipedia://en/61109403).

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's formula"}

- Sylvester's matrix theorem, a.k.a. [Sylvester's formula](wikipedia://en/8768758), for a matrix function in terms of eigenvalues.

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Bertrand's postulate"}

- Sylvester's theorem on the product of *k* consecutive integers > *k*, that generalizes [Bertrand's postulate](wikipedia://en/232526).

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's law of inertia"}

- [Sylvester's law of inertia](wikipedia://en/1127460) a.k.a. Sylvester's rigidity theorem, about the signature of a quadratic form.

:::

- Sylvester's identity about determinants of submatrices.[^o11]

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's criterion"}

- [Sylvester's criterion](wikipedia://en/11437896), a characterization of positive-definite Hermitian matrices.

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester domain"}

- [Sylvester domain](wikipedia://en/33813990).

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester matrix"}

- The [Sylvester matrix](wikipedia://en/2174514) for two polynomials.

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's sequence"}

- [Sylvester's sequence](wikipedia://en/4535485), where each term is the product of previous terms plus one.

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester cyclotomic number"}

- [Sylvester cyclotomic numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_cyclotomic_number).

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester equation"}

- The [Sylvester equation](wikipedia://en/10105237), *AX* + *XB* = *C* where *A*, *B*, *C* are given matrices and *X* is an unknown matrix.

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's four point problem"}

- [Sylvester's four point problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester's_four_point_problem) of geometric probability.

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester expansion"}

- The [Sylvester expansion](wikipedia://en/7277012) or Fibonacci–Sylvester expansion of a rational number, a representation as a sum of unit fractions found by a greedy algorithm.

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's rank inequality"}

- [Sylvester's rank inequality](wikipedia://en/26561#Properties) rank(*A*) + rank(*B*) − *n* ≤ rank(*AB*) on the rank of the product of an *m* × *n* matrix *A* and an *n* × *p* matrix *B*.

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylver coinage"}

- [Sylver coinage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylver_coinage), a number-theoretic game.[^o22]

:::

::: {#o23 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sylvester's bijection"}

- [Sylvester's bijection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester's_bijection), a correspondence between partitions into distinct and odd parts.

:::
