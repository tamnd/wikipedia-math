# Fermat's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fermat's theorem"}

The works of the 17th-century mathematician [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966) engendered many [theorems](wikipedia://en/30977). **Fermat's theorem** may refer to one of the following theorems:

:::

- [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953), about integer solutions to *a*<sup>*n*</sup> + *b*<sup>*n*</sup> = *c*<sup>*n*</sup>
- [Fermat's little theorem](wikipedia://en/53453), a property of prime numbers
- [Fermat's theorem on sums of two squares](wikipedia://en/1850216), about primes expressible as a sum of squares
- [Fermat's theorem (stationary points)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_theorem_%28stationary_points%29), about local maxima and minima of differentiable functions
- [Fermat's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_principle), about the path taken by a ray of light
- [Fermat polygonal number theorem](wikipedia://en/847886), about expressing integers as a sum of polygonal numbers
- [Fermat's right triangle theorem](wikipedia://en/29559494), about squares not being expressible as the difference of two fourth powers
