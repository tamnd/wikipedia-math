# Representation theory of diffeomorphism groups

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Representation theory of diffeomorphism groups"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a source for the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) of a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) *M* is the initial observation that (for *M* connected) that group acts transitively on *M*.

:::

## History {#History}

A survey paper from 1975 of the subject by [Anatoly Vershik](wikipedia://en/9036441), [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) and [M. I. Graev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._I._Graev)[^o3] attributes the original interest in the topic to research in [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) of the [local current algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_current_algebra), in the preceding years. Research on the *finite configuration* representations was in papers of [R. S. Ismagilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._S._Ismagilov) (1971), and [A. A. Kirillov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._A._Kirillov) (1974). The representations of interest in physics are described as a [cross product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_product_%28ring_theory%29) *C*<sup>∞</sup>(*M*)·Diff(*M*).

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

Let therefore *M* be a *n*-dimensional [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219), and *x* be any point on it. Let Diff(*M*) be the orientation-preserving [diffeomorphism group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffeomorphism_group) of *M* (only the [identity component](wikipedia://en/1088787) of mappings [homotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopic) to the identity diffeomorphism if you wish) and Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*) the [stabilizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizer_%28group_theory%29) of *x*. Then, *M* is identified as a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325)

Diff(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*).

From the algebraic point of view instead, $C^\infty(M)$ is the [algebra](wikipedia://en/2112) of [smooth functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) over *M* and $I_x(M)$ is the [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of smooth functions vanishing at *x*. Let $I_x^n(M)$ be the ideal of smooth functions which vanish up to the n-1th [partial derivative](wikipedia://en/52565) at *x*. $I_x^n(M)$ is invariant under the group Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*) of diffeomorphisms fixing x. For *n* > 0 the group Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*) is defined as the [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*) which acts as the identity on $I_x(M)/I_x^n(M)$. So, we have a descending chain

Diff(*M*) ⊃ Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(M) ⊃ ... ⊃ Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*) ⊃ ...

Here Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*) is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*), which means we can look at the [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526)

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*).

Using [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), a real- or complex-valued function (with some sufficiently nice topological properties) on the diffeomorphism group can be [decomposed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decompose) into Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*) representation-valued functions over *M*.

:::

## The supply of representations {#The_supply_of_representations}

So what are the representations of Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)? Let's use the fact that if we have a [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) φ:*G* → *H*, then if we have a *H*-representation, we can obtain a restricted *G*-representation. So, if we have a rep of

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*),

we can obtain a rep of Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*).

Let's look at

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>2</sup>(*M*)

first. This is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564) GL<sup>\+</sup>(*n*, **R**) (and because we're only considering orientation preserving diffeomorphisms and so the determinant is positive). What are the reps of GL<sup>\+</sup>(*n*, **R**)?

$$GL^+(n,\mathbb{R})\cong \mathbb{R}^+\times SL(n,\mathbb{R}).$$

We know the reps of SL(*n*, **R**) are simply [tensors](wikipedia://en/29965) over *n* dimensions. How about the **R**<sup>\+</sup> part? That corresponds to the *density*, or in other words, how the tensor rescales under the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of the [Jacobian](wikipedia://en/195351) of the diffeomorphism at *x*. (Think of it as the [conformal weight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_weight) if you will, except that there is no conformal structure here). (Incidentally, there is nothing preventing us from having a complex density).

So, we have just discovered the tensor reps (with density) of the diffeomorphism group.

Let's look at

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*).

This is a finite-dimensional group. We have the chain

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*) ⊂ ... ⊂ Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*) ⊂ ...

Here, the "⊂" signs should really be read to mean an injective homomorphism, but since it is canonical, we can pretend these quotient groups are embedded one within the other.

Any rep of

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*)/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*m*</sup>(*M*)

can automatically be turned into a rep of

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*n*</sup>(*M*)

if *n* > *m*. Let's say we have a rep of

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*p* + 2</sup>

which doesn't arise from a rep of

Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*p* + 1</sup>.

Then, we call the [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) with that rep as the [fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber) (i.e. Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>/Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>*p* + 2</sup> is the [structure group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_group)) a **[jet bundle](wikipedia://en/928060)** of order *p*.

Side remark: This is really the method of [induced representations](wikipedia://en/357452) with the smaller group being Diff<sub>x</sub><sup>1</sup>(M) and the larger group being Diff(*M*).

## Intertwining structure {#Intertwining_structure}

In general, the space of sections of the tensor and jet bundles would be an irreducible representation and we often look at a subrepresentation of them. We can study the structure of these reps through the study of the [intertwiners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intertwiner) between them.

If the fiber is not an irreducible representation of Diff<sub>*x*</sub><sup>1</sup>(*M*), then we can have a nonzero intertwiner mapping each fiber pointwise into a smaller [quotient representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_representation). Also, the [exterior derivative](wikipedia://en/220782) is an intertwiner from the space of [differential forms](wikipedia://en/221519) to another of higher order. (Other derivatives are not, because [connections](wikipedia://en/308815) aren't invariant under diffeomorphisms, though they are [covariant](wikipedia://en/157059).) The [partial derivative](wikipedia://en/52565) isn't diffeomorphism invariant. There is a derivative intertwiner taking sections of a jet bundle of order *p* into sections of a jet bundle of order *p* + 1.
