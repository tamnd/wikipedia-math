# Hofstadter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hofstadter"}

**Hofstadter** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Albert Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Hofstadter) (1910–1989), American philosopher, brother of Robert and uncle of Douglas
- [Douglas Hofstadter](wikipedia://en/8758) (born 1945), American professor, author of *Gödel, Escher, Bach*, nephew of Albert and son of Robert
- [Richard Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hofstadter) (1916–1970), American historian, nephew of Samuel
- [Robert Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Hofstadter) (1915–1990), American Nobel Prize-winner in physics, brother of Albert and father of Douglas
- [Samuel H. Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_H._Hofstadter) (1894–1970), New York politician and judge, uncle of Richard

**Fictional characters:**

- [Beverly Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hofstadter), a character in the television series *The Big Bang Theory*, mother of Leonard
- [Leonard Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Hofstadter), a character in the television series *The Big Bang Theory*
- [Penny Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_Hofstadter), a character in the television series *The Big Bang Theory*, wife of Leonard
