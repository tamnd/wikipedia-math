# Asymptotic curve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Asymptotic curve"}

In the [differential geometry of surfaces](wikipedia://en/15513875), an **asymptotic curve** is a [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) always [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) to an **asymptotic direction** of the surface (where they exist). It is sometimes called an **asymptotic line**, although it need not be a [line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_%28mathematics%29).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

There are several equivalent definitions for asymptotic directions, or equivalently, asymptotic curves.

- The asymptotic directions are the same as the [asymptotes](wikipedia://en/3107) of the hyperbola of the [Dupin indicatrix](wikipedia://en/9758831) through a [hyperbolic point](wikipedia://en/2072472#Classification_of_points_on_a_surface), or the unique asymptote through a [parabolic point](wikipedia://en/2072472#Classification_of_points_on_a_surface).[^o4]
- An asymptotic direction is a direction along which the normal [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) is zero: take the [plane](wikipedia://en/19469852) spanned by the direction and the surface's [normal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_normal) at that point. The curve of intersection of the plane and the surface has zero curvature at that point.
- An asymptotic curve is a curve such that, at each point, the plane tangent to the surface is an osculating plane of the curve.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Asymptotic directions can only occur when the [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) is negative (or zero).

There are two asymptotic directions through every point with negative Gaussian curvature, bisected by the [principal directions](wikipedia://en/2072472). There is one or infinitely many asymptotic directions through every point with zero Gaussian curvature.

If the surface is [minimal](wikipedia://en/276734) and not flat, then the asymptotic directions are orthogonal to one another (and 45 degrees with the two principal directions).

For a [developable surface](wikipedia://en/2878111), the asymptotic lines are the [generatrices](wikipedia://en/24801595), and them only.

If a straight line is included in a surface, then it is an asymptotic curve of the surface.

:::

## Related notions {#Related_notions}

A related notion is a [curvature line](wikipedia://en/2072472#Line_of_curvature), which is a curve always tangent to a principal direction.
