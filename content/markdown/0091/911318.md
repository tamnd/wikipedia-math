# Dag Prawitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dag Prawitz"}

**Dag Prawitz** (born 1936, [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm)) is a Swedish [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He is best known for his work on [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478) and the foundations of [natural deduction](wikipedia://en/51072), and for his contributions to [proof-theoretic semantics](wikipedia://en/910505).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Prawitz is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters),[^o5] of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Letters and Antiquity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Letters_and_Antiquity) and the [Royal Swedish Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Science).

In the Fall of 1991, he was a Fellow at the [Swedish Collegium for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Collegium_for_Advanced_Study) in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden).[^o6]

Prawitz was awarded the [Rolf Schock Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Schock_Prize) in Logic and Philosophy in 2020.
