# Jens Kraft

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jens Kraft"}

**Jens Kraft** (2 October 1720 – 18 March 1765) was a [Dano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_people)-[Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). He was born in [Frederikshald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederikshald) in [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway), but at age 5 he became an orphan and was subsequently raised by his uncle in [Thy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thy_%28district%29) in [Jutland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jutland). As a philosopher he introduced the study of [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology) to [Scandinavian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavia) academic circles.

:::

He was influenced by [Christian Wolff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Wolff_%28philosopher%29) whilst still a student at [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen), attending a lecture by Wolff while visiting [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle-Wittenberg). He was appointed professor of philosophy at the [Sorø Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorø_Academy), where he responded to [Baumgarten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Gottlieb_Baumgarten)'s *Metaphsica* with his own text *metaphysik*. Both divided their work into *[cosmologie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology)*, *[ontologie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology)*, *[psykologie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology)* and *[naturlig theologie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_theology)*.

Kraft made a distinction between [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) and [eternity](wikipedia://en/217472) writing that "the finite can never obtain eternity, but it can obtain an infinite time, (Aevum) or a time with beginning but without end." The infinite by contrast has permanence (*sempiternité*)".
