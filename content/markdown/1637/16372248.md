# Johann Samuel König

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Samuel König"}

**Johann Samuel König** (31 July 1712 – 21 August 1757) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

[Johann Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/268886) instructed both König and [Pierre Louis Maupertuis](wikipedia://en/196221) as pupils during the same period.[^o4] König is remembered largely for his disagreements with [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902), concerning the [principle of least action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_least_action).[^o5] He is also remembered as a tutor to [Émilie du Châtelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émilie_du_Châtelet), one of the few female physicists of the 18th century.[^o6]
