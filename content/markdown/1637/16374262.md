# William Haboush

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Haboush"}

**William Joseph Haboush** (born 1942)[^o2] is an American mathematician, a professor of mathematics at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign).[^o3] An [algebraic geometer](wikipedia://en/1997), Haboush is the namesake of [Haboush's theorem](wikipedia://en/632762), which he proved in 1975, resolving a conjecture of [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594).[^o4]

:::

Haboush completed a Ph.D. in 1969 at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). His dissertation, *A Theory of Codimension One Phenomena with an Application to the Theory of Purely Inseparable Descent*, was jointly supervised by [Heisuke Hironaka](wikipedia://en/13822) and Raymond Hoobler.
