# Barry C. Arnold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Barry C. Arnold"}

**Barry C. Arnold** (born December 6, 1939)[^o3] is a British-born American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He was a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor) in the department of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside).[^o4]

:::

In 2001, Arnold was named a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o5]
