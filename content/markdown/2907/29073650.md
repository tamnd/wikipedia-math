# Ernest Corominas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernest Corominas"}

**Ernest Corominas i Vigneaux** (1913 – 24 January 1992) was a Spanish-French mathematician.

:::

Born in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona), he studied [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona), graduating in 1936. He served as in officer of engineering in the [Spanish Republican Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Spanish_Republic) during the [Spanish Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War). In 1939 he fled to France, before moving to South America in 1940. After working six months as an architect in Chile, he moved to [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina), where [Julio Rey Pastor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Rey_Pastor) offered him a lecturer position at the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires).

Corominas returned to Europe, where he attained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1952, under the supervision of [Arnaud Denjoy](wikipedia://en/1485520). He then lectured in Barcelona, [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), and [Caracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_City_of_Caracas), before settling in France at the [University of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lyon). In 1966, Corominas became a French citizen.

He was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Guggenheim_Fellowships_awarded_in_1953) in 1953.
