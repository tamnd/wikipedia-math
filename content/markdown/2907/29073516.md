::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 8-orthoplexes"}

In eight-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 8-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex).

:::

There are 7 truncation for the 8-orthoplex. Vertices of the truncation 8-orthoplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 8-orthoplex. Vertices of the bitruncated 8-orthoplex are located on the triangular faces of the 8-orthoplex. Vertices of the tritruncated 7-orthoplex are located inside the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells of the 8-orthoplex. The final truncations are best expressed relative to the 8-cube.

::: {#o14 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *truncated 8-orthoplex*, one with the C<sub>8</sub> or \[4,3,3,3,3,3,3\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with the D<sub>8</sub> or \[3<sup>5,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.

:::

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a truncated 8-orthoplex, centered at the origin, are all 224 vertices are sign (4) and coordinate (56) [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of

(±2,±1,0,0,0,0,0,0)
