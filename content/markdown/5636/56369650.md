# James Henry Weaver

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Henry Weaver"}

**James Henry Weaver** (10 June 1883 in [Madison County, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_County,_Ohio) – 7 April 1942 in [Franklin County, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_County,_Ohio)) was an American mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Weaver received B.A. in 1908 from [Otterbein College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otterbein_College) and M.A. in 1911 from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). He was a teaching assistant at Ohio State University from 1910 to 1912. He entered the mathematics doctoral program at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1912 and graduated there in 1916 with advisor Maurice Babb and thesis *Some Extensions of the Work of Pappus and Steiner on Tangent Circles*.[^o2]

From 1912 to 1917 he was head of the mathematics department of West Chester High School in [West Chester, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Chester,_Pennsylvania). In 1917, Weaver became an instructor at Ohio State University, advancing to assistant professor in 1920.[^o2][^o4]

Additionally, he was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924.
