# Kaushal Kumar Verma

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Kaushal Kumar Verma"}

**Kaushal Kumar Verma** (born 7 March 1971) is an [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_people) mathematician who specializes in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He earned a B.Tech degree in engineering physics from [IIT Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Bombay) in 1992. He then obtained a PhD in mathematics from [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington), and also worked at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) for a year, followed by working at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), Ann Arbor, for 3 years.[^o4] He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize) in 2014.[^o5][^o6][^o7] He is a mathematics professor and dean of mathematical and physical sciences at [Indian Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science), Bangalore.[^o8]
