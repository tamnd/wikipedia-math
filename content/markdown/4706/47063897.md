# Young Turks (Bell Labs)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Young Turks"}

The **Young Turks** were a group of leading scientists who worked at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs), who were insatiably curious about the science behind communications. Many of them were encouraged to take risks, free from the responsibilities of applying for grants or from teaching.[^o2][^o3][^o4] "We were first-class troublemakers," [Richard Hamming](wikipedia://en/99326) later recalled. "We did unconventional things in unconventional ways and still got valuable results. Thus management had to tolerate us and let us alone a lot of the time."[^o5]

:::
