# UCL Faculty of Mathematical and Physical Sciences

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="UCL Faculty of Mathematical and Physical Sciences"}

The **UCL Faculty of Mathematical and Physical Sciences** is one of the [11 constituent faculties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London#Faculties_and_departments) of [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) (UCL).[^o4] The Faculty, the [UCL Faculty of Engineering Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCL_Faculty_of_Engineering_Sciences) and the [UCL Faculty of the Built Environment (The Bartlett)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bartlett) together form the UCL School of the Built Environment, Engineering and Mathematical and Physical Sciences.

:::

## Departments {#Departments}

The Faculty currently comprises the following departments:[^o6][^o7]

- UCL Department of Chemistry
- UCL Department of Earth Sciences
- UCL Department of Mathematics
  - *Chalkdust* is an online mathematics interest magazine published by Department of Mathematics students starting in 2015[^o8]
- UCL Department of Natural Sciences
- UCL Department of Physics & Astronomy
- [UCL Department of Science and Technology Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCL_Department_of_Science_and_Technology_Studies)
- [UCL Department of Space & Climate Physics (Mullard Space Science Laboratory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullard_Space_Science_Laboratory)
- UCL Department of Statistical Science
- [London Centre for Nanotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Centre_for_Nanotechnology) - a joint venture between UCL and [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) established in 2003 following the award of a £13.65m higher education grant under the Science Research Infrastructure Fund.[^o9][^o10]

## Research centres and institutes {#Research_centres_and_institutes}

*Image: UCL Portico Building.jpg.* [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London)

The Faculty is closely involved with the following research centres and institutes:[^o7]

- UCL Centre for Materials Research
- UCL Centre for Mathematics and Physics in the Life Sciences and Experimental Biology (CoMPLEX) - an [inter-disciplinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-disciplinary) virtual centre that seeks to bring together [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), [physical scientists](wikipedia://en/22939), [computer scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) upon the problems posed by [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity) in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) and [biomedicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedicine). The centre works with 29 departments and Institutes across UCL. It has a MRes/PhD program that requires that its students also belong to at least one of these Departments/Institutes. The centre is based in the Physics Building on the UCL main campus.
- Centre for Planetary Science at UCL/Birkbeck
- UCL Clinical Operational Research Unit (CORU) - CORU[^o12] sits within the Department of Mathematics and is a team of researchers dedicated to applying operational research, data analysis and mathematical modelling to problems in health care.
- UCL Institute of Origins
- UCL Institute for Healthcare Engineering (IHE) - IHE[^o13] is concerned with digital and medical technologies.
- UCL Institute for Risk and Disaster Reduction
- The [Thomas Young Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Young_Centre)

## Rankings {#Rankings}

In the 2013 *[Academic Ranking of World Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Ranking_of_World_Universities)*, UCL is ranked joint 51st to 75th in the world (and joint 12th in Europe) for Natural Sciences and Mathematics.[^o15]

In the 2013 *[QS World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QS_World_University_Rankings)*, UCL is ranked 38th in the world (and 12th in Europe) for Natural Sciences.[^o16] In the 2014 *QS World University Rankings by Subject*, UCL is ranked joint 51st-100th in the world (and joint 12th in Europe) for Chemistry,[^o17] joint 27th in the world (and 8th in Europe) for Earth & Marine Sciences,[^o18] joint 51st-100th in the world (and joint 13th in Europe) for Materials Science,[^o19] joint 36th in the world (and joint 10th in Europe) for Mathematics,[^o20] 35th in the world (and 13th in Europe) for Physics & Astronomy,[^o21] and 47th in the world (and 9th in Europe) for Statistics & Operational Research.[^o22]

In the 2013/14 *[Times Higher Education World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Higher_Education_World_University_Rankings)*, UCL is ranked 51st in the world (and 16th in Europe) for Physical Sciences.[^o23]
