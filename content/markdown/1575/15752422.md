# Jeroen Groenendijk

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Jeroen Groenendijk"}

**Jeroen Antonius Gerardus Groenendijk** (; 20 July 1949 – 17 October 2023) was a Dutch [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065), [linguist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), working on the [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), [formal semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_semantics_%28logic%29), [pragmatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pragmatics).

:::

Groenendijk wrote a joint [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) dissertation with [Martin Stokhof](wikipedia://en/3888996) on the formal semantics of questions, under the supervision of [Renate Bartsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Bartsch) and [Johan van Benthem](wikipedia://en/1498176). He was also an important figure in the development of [dynamic semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_semantics) (together with Stokhof, [Veltman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Veltman) and others, following earlier work by [Irene Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Heim) and [Hans Kamp](wikipedia://en/17054475)). His later work was mainly focused on studying and developing the recently founded framework of [inquisitive semantics](wikipedia://en/58208186).

He was briefly the director of the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) (ILLC) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) and a member of the group collectively publishing under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) [L. T. F. Gamut](wikipedia://en/1091808).[^o5]
