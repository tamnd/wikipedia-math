# Hsiang–Lawson's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hsiang–Lawson's conjecture"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Lawson's [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138)** states that the [Clifford torus](wikipedia://en/7950934) is the only minimally embedded [torus](wikipedia://en/74800) in the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) *S*<sup>3</sup>.[^o2][^o3] The conjecture was featured by the Australian Mathematical Society Gazette as part of the *Millennium Problems* series.[^o4]

:::

In March 2012, [Simon Brendle](wikipedia://en/36493319) gave a proof of this conjecture, based on maximum principle techniques.[^o5]
