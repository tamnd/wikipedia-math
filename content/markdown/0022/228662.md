# Rolf Schock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rolf Schock"}

**Rolf Schock** (; 5 April 1933 – 5 December 1986) was a Swedish–American philosopher and artist, born in [Cap-d'Ail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-d'Ail), France of German parents.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Schock was born at [Cap-d'Ail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-d'Ail) on the French Riviera. His parents, who had left Germany, would eventually settle in the United States, where Schock would go on to obtain a bachelor's degree in geology at the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico).[^o4] After completing a [bachelor of arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in 1955, he pursued studies in [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) from 1956 to 1960 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and then in [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), and in 1960 moved to [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), to specialize in theoretical philosophy at [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) with a particular interest in [free logic](wikipedia://en/1635110). He was awarded an intermediate post-graduate degree in 1964 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy)[^o4]

During the period 1964–1969 he also studied at the [University College of Arts, Crafts and Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_Arts,_Crafts_and_Design) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm) and he dedicated a lot of his time to painting and photography in addition to conducting research. He led a simple life as an independent scholar who was devoted to researching the field of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and related areas in philosophy, and never held a permanent position, earning a living by temporary teaching assignments at universities and night-schools. For some years he was affiliated with the [Royal Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Technology) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), where he taught a series of courses.[^o4]

In 1986 Schock was killed in an accident in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o4] To much surprise he left a large estate. Schock bequeathed half of the funds for prizes in the arts and sciences, and beginning in 1993, [Schock Prizes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Schock_Prizes) (Schockprisen) instituted by his will are awarded every two or three years.[^o4]

In his later life Schock criticized Albert Einstein's [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001).[^o4]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Logics Without Existence Assumptions* (1968)
- *New Foundations for Concept Theory* (1969)
- [*The Inconsistency of the Theory of Relativity*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2FBF01801199) (1981)
- *On the Nature of Time* (1983)
