# Carl Adam Petri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Adam Petri"}

**Carl Adam Petri** (12 July 1926 in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) – 2 July 2010 in [Siegburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegburg))[^o4][^o5] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

*Image: Petri net example.svg.* Petri net example

Petri created his major scientific contribution, the concept of the [Petri net](wikipedia://en/225192), in 1939 at the age of 13, for the purpose of describing chemical processes.[^o7] In 1941, his father told him about [Konrad Zuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konrad_Zuse)'s work on computing machines, and Carl Adam started building his own [analog computer](wikipedia://en/2428).

After earning his [Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur) at [Thomasschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomasschule_zu_Leipzig) in 1944, he was drafted into the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht). He was taken into British captivity until 1949, when he departed England.[^o8]

Petri started studying mathematics at the Technische Hochschule Hannover (today, the [Leibniz University Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_University_Hannover)) in 1950. He documented Petri nets in 1962 as part of his dissertation, Kommunikation mit Automaten (Communication with automata). From 1959 until 1962 he worked at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in 1962 from the [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt). From 1963 to 1968, he established and directed the computing centre of [Bonn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o8] In 1968, he became head of Forschungsinstitut für Informationssysteme of the newly founded [Gesellschaft für Mathematik und Datenverarbeitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Mathematik_und_Datenverarbeitung) (GMD). He retired in 1991.

In 1988, Petri became honorary professor of the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).[^o9] He was a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).

Petri's work significantly advanced the fields of [parallel computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) and [distributed computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_computing), and it helped define the modern studies of [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system) and [workflow management systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workflow_management_system). His contributions have been in the broader area of [network theory](wikipedia://en/766409), which includes coordination models and theories of interaction, and eventually led to the formal study of software connectors.

## Awards {#Awards}

Petri was honoured with the following awards:

- 1993: [Konrad Zuse Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konrad_Zuse_Medal) of the [Gesellschaft für Informatik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Informatik).[^o26]
- 1997: [Werner von Siemens Ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_von_Siemens_Ring), a prestigious German award in technical sciences.[^o27][^o28]
- 1999: Doctorate *[Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_causa)* from the [Universidad de Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zaragoza)[^o29]
- 2003: honoured by [Her Majesty the Queen of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatrix_of_the_Netherlands) with the title *Commander in the [Order of the Netherlands Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion)*.[^o29]
- 2007: honoured for his lifetime achievements by the "Academy of Transdisciplinary Learning and Advanced Studies (ATLAS)" with an "Academy Gold Medal of Honour".[^o30]
- 2008: [Computer Pioneer Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Pioneer_Award) from the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) for his inspiration of [Petri nets](wikipedia://en/225192).[^o31][^o29]
