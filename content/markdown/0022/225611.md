# Floradora

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Floradora"}

**"Floradora"**, also called **Keyword,** was a doubly [enciphered](wikipedia://en/5244) diplomatic [code](wikipedia://en/838989) used by the Germans during the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War). The Allies used [tabulating equipment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_record_equipment), created by [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), to break the code over period of more than a year in 1941 and 1942.

:::
