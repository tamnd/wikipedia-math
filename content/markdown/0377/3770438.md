# Association for Symbolic Logic

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Association for Symbolic Logic"}

The **Association for Symbolic Logic** (**ASL**) is an [international organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_organization) of specialists in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939). The ASL was founded in 1936, and its first president was [Curt John Ducasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curt_John_Ducasse). The current president of the ASL is [Natasha Dobrinen](wikipedia://en/80636352).[^o3]

:::

## Publications {#Publications}

The ASL publishes [books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book) and [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal). Its three official journals are:

- *[Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic)* – publishes research in all areas of mathematical logic. Founded in 1936, 0022-4812.
- *Bulletin of Symbolic Logic* – publishes primarily expository articles and reviews. Founded in 1995, ISSN 1079-8986. ([Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-symbolic-logic))
- *Review of Symbolic Logic* – publishes research relating to logic, philosophy, science, and their interactions. Founded in 2008, 1755-0203.

In addition, the ASL has a sponsored journal:

- *Journal of Logic and Analysis* publishes research on the interactions between mathematical logic and pure and applied analysis. Founded in 2009 as an [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access_journal) successor to the Springer journal *Logic and Analysis*. 1759-9008.

The organization played a part in publishing the collected writings of [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736).[^o5]

## Meetings {#Meetings}

The ASL holds two main meetings every year, one in North America and one in Europe (the latter known as the *Logic Colloquium*). In addition, the ASL regularly holds joint meetings with both the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) ("AMS") and the [American Philosophical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Association) ("APA"), and sponsors meetings in many different countries every year.
