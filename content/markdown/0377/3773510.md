# Jürgen Mittelstraß

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jürgen Mittelstraß"}

*Image: Die Zukunft der Wissensspeicher - Jürgen Mittelstraß 5.JPG.* Jürgen Mittelstraß at a Conference in 2015.

**Jürgen Mittelstraß** (born 11 October 1936) is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) especially interested in the [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Mittelstraß studied [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) and [protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant) [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) at the universities of [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen), [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) and [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) from 1956 until 1961. He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen) in 1961, where he afterwards wrote his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation), completing in 1968. He was influenced by the *Erlanger Konstruktivismus*.

In 1970 Mittelstraß became a professor of philosophy in the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) and from 1970 to 2005 he was a full professor of philosophy of science at Konstanz.

He was the [lead editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_editor)[^o3] of the *Enzyklopädie Philosophie und Wissenschaftstheorie* (4 volumes, 1980–1996; second edition 2005–2018, 8 volumes).[^o4]

## Academic recognition {#Academic_recognition}

Mittelstraß is member of numerous scientific and philosophical societies and has received several awards. He has been awarded honorary doctorates from six universities, the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), the Universities of [Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), [Iaşi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iaşi), [Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen) and the [Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu).

Between 2005 and 2015 Mittelstraß held the Chair of the former Austrian Science Council[^o6][^o7] (integrated into the [Austrian Council for Sciences, Technology, and Innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Council_for_Sciences,_Technology,_and_Innovation) since 2023).

Mittelstraß is a member of the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities), the [Pontifical Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (1988).[^o8]

## Private life {#Private_life}

Jürgen Mittelstraß is married and has four daughters.
