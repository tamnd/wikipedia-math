# Alexandre Kirillov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandre Kirillov"}

**Alexandre Aleksandrovich Kirilloff** (Алекса́ндр Алекса́ндрович Кири́ллов, born 1936) is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) mathematician,[^o3] known for his works in the fields of [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [topological groups](wikipedia://en/42315) and [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). In particular he introduced the [orbit method](wikipedia://en/4231851)[^o4] into representation theory. He is an emeritus professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Kirillov studied at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) where he was a student of [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643). His [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) ([kandidat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat)) dissertation *Unitary representations of nilpotent Lie groups* was published in 1962. He was awarded the degree of [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science). At the time he was the youngest Doctor of Science in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). He worked at the Moscow State University until 1994 when he became the Francis J. Carey Professor of Mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania.

During his school years, Kirillov was a winner of many [mathematics competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_competitions), and he is still an active organizer of Russian mathematical contests. Kirillov is an author of many popular school-oriented books and articles.

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

Kirillov's son, [Alexander Kirillov, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Kirillov,_Jr.), is also a mathematician, working on the [representation theory of Lie groups](wikipedia://en/292786) at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook).
