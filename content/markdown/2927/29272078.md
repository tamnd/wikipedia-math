# World Statistics Day

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="World Statistics Day"}

**World Statistics Day** is an [international day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_minor_secular_observances#October) to celebrate statistics. Created by the [United Nations Statistical Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Statistical_Commission),[^o3] it was first celebrated on 20 October 2010, 20-10-2010. The day is celebrated every five years.[^o4]

:::

, 103 countries celebrate a national Statistics Day, including 51 African countries that jointly celebrate African Statistics Day annually on 18 November.[^o5] India celebrates its statistics day on 29 June, the birthday of the statistician [Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis](wikipedia://en/359931).[^o6] The [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in the UK also launched its *getstats* [statistical literacy](wikipedia://en/3223282) campaign on the same day at 20:10 (on 20 October 2010).[^o7][^o8]

On **20 October 2025**, the world will celebrate **United Nations World Statistics Day (WSD)** under the theme: **Driving change with quality statistics and data for everyone**

For the first time ever, the **UN Regional Commissions** will unite to host a **24-hour non-stop global webinar marathon** — bringing voices, stories, and [innovations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innovation) from every corner of the globe.

From **Bangkok to Beirut, Addis Ababa to Geneva, and Santiago to New York**, the webinar will move with the sun, passing the virtual baton across time zones, showcasing how **data is powering [sustainable development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_development) and [inclusive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inclusion_%28disability_rights%29) [decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision-making)**.
