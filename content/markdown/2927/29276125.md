# Basis pursuit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Basis pursuit"}

**Basis pursuit** is the [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) problem of the form

:::

$$\min_x \|x\|_1 \quad \text{subject to} \quad y = Ax,$$

where *x* is a *N*-dimensional solution vector (signal), *y* is a *M*-dimensional vector of observations (measurements), *A* is a *M* × *N* transform matrix (usually measurement matrix) and *M* \< *N*. The version of basis pursuit that seeks to minimize the *L*<sub>0</sub> norm is NP-hard.[^o2]

It is usually applied in cases where there is an [underdetermined system](wikipedia://en/15890311) of linear equations *y* = *Ax* that must be exactly satisfied, and the [sparsest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparse_vector) solution in the *L*<sub>1</sub> sense is desired.

When it is desirable to trade off exact equality of *Ax* and *y* in exchange for a sparser *x*, [basis pursuit denoising](wikipedia://en/29276362) is preferred.

Basis pursuit problems can be converted to [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730) problems in [polynomial time](wikipedia://en/405944) and vice versa, making the two types of problems polynomially equivalent.[^o3]

## Equivalence to Linear Programming {#Equivalence_to_Linear_Programming}

A basis pursuit problem can be converted to a linear programming problem by first noting that

$$\begin{aligned} \|x\|_1 &= |x_1| + |x_2| + \ldots + |x_n| \\ &= (x_1^+ + x_1^-) + (x_2^+ + x_2^-) + \ldots + (x_n^+ + x_n^-)\end{aligned}$$

where $x_i^+, x_i^- \geq 0$. This construction is derived from the constraint $x_i = x_i^+ - x_i^-$, where the value of $|x_i|$ is intended to be stored in $x_i^+$ or $x_i^-$ depending on whether $x_i$ is greater or less than zero, respectively. Although a range of $x_i^+$ and $x_i^-$ values can potentially satisfy this constraint, solvers using the [simplex algorithm](wikipedia://en/349458) will find solutions where one or both of $x_i^+$ or $x_i^-$ is zero, resulting in the relation $|x_i| = (x_i^+ + x_i^-)$.

From this expansion, the problem can be recast in [canonical form](wikipedia://en/515096) as:[^o3]

$$\begin{aligned} & \text{Find a vector} && (\mathbf{x^+}, \mathbf{x^-}) \\ & \text{that minimizes} && \mathbf{1}^T \mathbf{x^+} + \mathbf{1}^T \mathbf{x^-} \\ & \text{subject to} && A \mathbf{x^+} - A \mathbf{x^-} = \mathbf{y} \\ & \text{and} && \mathbf{x^+}, \mathbf{x^-} \geq \mathbf{0}. \end{aligned}$$
