# Ian Ford (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ian Ford"}

*Image: Boyd Orr Building.jpg.* The [Robertson Centre for Biostatistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson_Centre_for_Biostatistics) is in the [Boyd Orr Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyd_Orr_Building), [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow)

**Ian Ford** is a Scottish doctor who is a professor of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) and a director of the [Robertson Centre for Biostatistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson_Centre_for_Biostatistics), and a former [Dean of Faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_of_Faculty) of Information and Mathematical Sciences, at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ford was born in Glasgow and educated at the former [Hamilton Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Academy). He studied at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow), graduating with a BSc and in 1976, was awarded a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Statistics. From 1976-77, he was visiting Lecturer to the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison) and since then successively [Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer), Senior Lecturer, [Reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29), Personal Professor and [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the University of Glasgow.[^o4] He is the first holder of the Chair of Biostatistics at the University.[^o5]

Ford has been the Director of the [Robertson Centre for Biostatistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson_Centre_for_Biostatistics) since 1991[^o6] and Director of the Glasgow Clinical Trials Unit. He is also a member of the editorial boards for the international journals, *Statistical Methods for Medical Research*;[^o7] *Journal of Evaluation in Clinical Practice*[^o8] and *Public Library of Science Medicine*.[^o9][^o10] He has written numerous papers.[^o11][^o12][^o13]

In 1999, Ford was elected Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o14] He is also a Fellow of both the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and the [Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Physicians_and_Surgeons_of_Glasgow).[^o15] A member of the Cholesterol Triallists Collaboration and the Fibrinogen Studies Collaboration, Ford is also a member of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988); Statisticians in the Pharmaceutical Industry; the [Drug Information Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_Information_Association); the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664); and the International Society for Clinical Biostatistics.[^o16]

From 2000–04, Ford served as Dean of the Faculty of Information and Mathematical Sciences, University of Glasgow.[^o4]
