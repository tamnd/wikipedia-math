# Ahmes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ahmes"}

*Image: Rhind Mathematical Papyrus.jpg.* A portion of the [Rhind Mathematical Papyrus](wikipedia://en/9210114)

**Ahmes** (jꜥḥ-ms “, a common Egyptian name also transliterated **[Ahmose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmose_%28disambiguation%29)**) was an [ancient Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egypt) [scribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scribe) who lived towards the end of the [Fifteenth Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_Dynasty) (and of the [Second Intermediate Period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Intermediate_Period)) and the beginning of the [Eighteenth Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighteenth_dynasty_of_Egypt) (and of the [New Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Kingdom_of_Egypt)). He transcribed the [Rhind Mathematical Papyrus](wikipedia://en/9210114), a work of [ancient Egyptian mathematics](wikipedia://en/1180105) that dates to approximately 1550 BC;[^o2] he is the earliest contributor to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) whose name is known.[^o3][^o4] Ahmes claimed not to be the writer of the work but rather just the scribe. He claimed the material came from an even older document from around 2000 B.C.
