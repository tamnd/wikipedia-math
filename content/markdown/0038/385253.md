# Kronecker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kronecker"}

**Kronecker** is a [German surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_surname). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Hugo Kronecker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Kronecker) (1839–1914), German physiologist, brother of Leopold
- [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450) (1823–1891), German mathematician
