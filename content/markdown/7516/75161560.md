# Herzberger Quader

*Image: Herzberger Quader.jpg.* The Herzberger Quader, as it was published on the occasion of the 50th anniversary of high school graduation in Herzberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herzberger Quader"}

The **Herzberger Quader** is a [solid dissection puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_puzzle#Assembly) invented by German mathematics teacher Gerhard Schulze.[^o2] It was named after his home-town [Herzberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzberg_%28Elster%29), and Quader is the latin-derived German word for a [rectangular cuboid](wikipedia://en/38848329).

:::

## Design {#Design}

*Image: herzberger\_quader\_cuboid.svg.* The Herzberger Quader pieces packed into a 2 × 4 × 5 cuboid

The Herzberger Quader consists of a set of all possible [polycubes](wikipedia://en/710426) from dicube to tetracubes.[^o4] The eleven pieces together have 40 unit cubes and thus can be stored in a 2 × 4 × 5 box.[^o4]

## Possible problems {#Possible_problems}

*Image: Soma\_cube\_figures.svg.* Some Soma cube figures assembled from the non-cuboidal Herzberger Quader pieces

Besides stowing the Herzberger Quader in its box there are a lot of figures that can be built using all parts. Various subsets can be used to form a 3 × 3 × 3 cube, one of them is the famous [Soma cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soma_cube).[^o4]

Much more demanding tasks ask for the number of all different possibilities to arrange the initial parts in a certain figure. Or proofs are to be given for which figures can be realized or not realized with which polycubes.[^o4]

## History {#History}

Author of the Herzberger Quader is [Oberstudienrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberstudienrat) Gerhard Schulze (1919–1995), who was intensively engaged in [mathematical games](wikipedia://en/26446) during his extracurricular activities in the years 1982–1994.[^o7] On the occasion of the 800th anniversary of his hometown Herzberg in 1984, the Herzberger Quader was produced for the first time and thus made known to a broad public. Today, the Herzberger Quader is suggested to be used in the context of [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471).[^o8][^o9]
