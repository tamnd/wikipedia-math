# Wolfgang Soergel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Soergel"}

*Image: Wolfgang Soergel.jpg.* Wolfgang Soergel

**Wolfgang Soergel** (born 12 June 1962 in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva)) is a German mathematician, specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Wolfgang Soergel is the son of the physicist Volker Soergel and a grandson of the paleontologist Johannes Wolfgang Adolf Werner Soergel (1887–1946).

Soergel received his [*Promotion*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (PhD) in 1988 from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). His PhD dissertation *Universelle versus relative Einhüllende: Eine geometrische Untersuchung von Quotienten von universellen Einhüllenden halbeinfacher Lie-Algebren* (Universal versus relative envelopes: a geometric investigation of quotients of universal envelopes of semi-simple Lie algebras) was supervised by [Jens Carsten Jantzen](wikipedia://en/32094339).

## Career {#Career}

After postdoctoral positions at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), Soergel completed his *[Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)* at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1991. In 1994 he was appointed to a professorial chair at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg). He was an invited speaker at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich. Since 2008 he has been a full member of the [Heidelberg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o5]

He is the author or coauthor of over 30 research articles.[^o6] His research in representation theory has important applications to [Kazhdan-Lusztig theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhdan-Lusztig_theory) and [Koszul duality](wikipedia://en/42478011). The category of Soergel bimodules is named in his honor.[^o7][^o8]

His doctoral students include Karen Günzl, Peter Fiebig, [Catharina Stroppel](wikipedia://en/50534879) and [Geordie Williamson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geordie_Williamson).[^o3]
