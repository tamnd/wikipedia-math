# Simion Stoilow Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simion Stoilow Prize"}

The **Simion Stoilow Prize** (Premiul Simion Stoilow) is the prize offered by the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy) for achievements in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It is named in honor of [Simion Stoilow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simion_Stoilow).

:::

The prize is awarded either for a mathematical work or for a cycle of works. The award consists of 2,000 [lei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_leu) and a diploma. The prize was established in 1963 and is awarded annually. Prizes of the Romanian Academy for a particular year are awarded two years later.
