# Georges Darmois

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Darmois"}

**Georges Darmois** (24 June 1888 – 3 January 1960) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and statistician. He pioneered in the theory of [sufficiency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficiency_%28statistics%29), in [stellar statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_statistics), and in [factor analysis](wikipedia://en/253492). He was also one of the first French mathematicians to teach British mathematical statistics.[^o3]

:::

He is one of the eponyms of the [Koopman&ndash;Pitman&ndash;Darmois theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koopman&ndash;Pitman&ndash;Darmois_theorem) and sufficient statistics and [exponential families](wikipedia://en/339174).

## Biography {#Biography}

Darmois was born on 24 June 1888 in [Éply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Éply). He was admitted to [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in 1906 and passed subsequently the [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) de mathematiques in 1909. From 1911 to 1914, he was a qualified assistant (agrégé préparateur) at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure), where his scientific activities were directed by Émile Borel who rapidly appreciated his talent.

Darmois earned his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1921. He defended a thesis on algebraic curves and partial differential equations before the jury consisting of [Émile Picard](wikipedia://en/42000) and [Édouard Goursat](wikipedia://en/1674255). In 1949, he succeeded [Maurice René Fréchet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_René_Fréchet) as the Chair of Calculus of Probabilities and Mathematical Physics at the University of Paris, who had himself succeeded [Émile Borel](wikipedia://en/356161).[^o5] His research spanned several fields of pure and applied mathematics, including [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [time series](wikipedia://en/406624), and [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390).[^o6]

He was elected fellow of the [Econometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometric_Society) in 1952.[^o7] In 1955 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o8] He was also the president of [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) from 1953 until 1960.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

His scientific contributions include:

- Giving the first rigorous proof of [Fréchet–Darmois–Cramér–Rao inequality](wikipedia://en/581124), also known as Cramér–Rao bound, in 1945 independently from Rao and Cramér.
- Developing the notion of [Koopman–Darmois family](wikipedia://en/339174) of distributions also known as the exponential family of distributions.
- Establishing [Koopman&ndash;Pitman&ndash;Darmois theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koopman&ndash;Pitman&ndash;Darmois_theorem)
- Characterizing Gaussian distributions with [Darmois–Skitovich theorem](wikipedia://en/62469606)
