# Raphael Høegh-Krohn

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Raphael Høegh-Krohn"}

**Jan Raphael Høegh-Krohn** (10 February 1938 – 24 January 1988) was a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He finished his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1966, titled *On Partly Gentle Perturbation with Application to Perturbation by Annihilation-Creation Operator,* under the supervision of [Kurt Friedrichs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Friedrichs) at the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

He authored over 150 papers and is known for the discovery of a fundamental duality in relativistic [quantum statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/712450) by representing the basic correlation functions in terms of a certain stochastic process, now known as the Høegh-Krohn process.
