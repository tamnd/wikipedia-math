# Annals of Applied Probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annals of Applied Probability"}

The ***Annals of Applied Probability*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). The journal was established in 1991 with [J. Michael Steele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Michael_Steele) as founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief).[^o3][^o4] It is indexed by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*, *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*, and the [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2024 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.8.[^o5]

:::
