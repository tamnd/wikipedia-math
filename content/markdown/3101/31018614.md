# Deutsche Mathematik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deutsche Mathematik"}

***Deutsche Mathematik*** (German Mathematics) was a mathematics journal founded in 1936 by [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186) and [Theodor Vahlen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Vahlen). Vahlen was publisher on behalf of the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) (DFG), and Bieberbach was chief editor. Other editors were Fritz Kubach, [Erich Schönhardt](wikipedia://en/25330786), [Werner Weber](wikipedia://en/53613774) (all volumes), [Ernst August Weiß](wikipedia://en/45656690) (volumes 1–6), Karl Dörge, [Wilhelm Süss](wikipedia://en/1368716) (volumes 1–5), [Günther Schulz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Günther_Schulz_%28mathematician%29) (de), Erhard Tornier (volumes 1–4), [Georg Feigl](wikipedia://en/40614663), [Gerhard Kowalewski](wikipedia://en/27415779) (volumes 2–6), Maximilian Krafft, [Willi Rinow](wikipedia://en/38412624), Max Zacharias (volumes 2–5), and [Oswald Teichmüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Teichmüller) (volumes 3–7).[^o3] In February 1936, the journal was declared the official organ of the [German Student Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Student_Union) (DSt) by its *Reichsführer*, and all local DSt mathematics departments were requested to subscribe and actively contribute.[^o4] In the 1940s, issues appeared increasingly delayed and bunched; the journal ended with a triple issue (due Dec 1942) in June 1944.[^o5][^o6]

:::

***Deutsche Mathematik*** is also the name of a movement closely associated with the journal whose aim was to promote "German mathematics" and eliminate "Jewish influence" in mathematics, similar to the [Deutsche Physik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Physik) movement. As well as articles on mathematics, the journal published propaganda articles giving the Nazi viewpoint on the relation between mathematics and race[^o7][^o8][^o9] (though these political articles mostly disappeared after the first two volumes). As a result of this many mathematics libraries outside Germany did not subscribe to it, so copies of the journal can be hard to find. This caused some problems in [Teichmüller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_theory), as [Oswald Teichmüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Teichmüller) published several of his foundational papers in the journal.
