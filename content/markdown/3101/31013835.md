::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chain linking"}

**Chain linking** is a statistical method, defined by the [Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OECD) as:

:::

Chain linking is popularly used with [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP)/[gross national income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNP) data, to measure changes over time, giving a [chained volume series](wikipedia://en/24929135).[^o2]
