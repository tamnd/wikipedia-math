# André Fortin (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="André Fortin"}

**André Fortin** (born 1956) is a French Canadian mathematician, known for his research in applied and industrial mathematics. He holds a [NSERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSERC) Research Chair in High Performance Scientific Computing at [Université Laval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Laval).[^o3]

:::

Fortin earned his Ph.D. from the Université Laval in 1984.[^o4] His thesis advisor was Michel Fortin, a now emeritus professor of the Université Laval, recipient of Prix Summa in 1987 and prize CAIMS-SCMAI in 2005 and a member of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1999.[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

He is the director of the Groupe Interdisciplinaire de Recherche en Éléments Finis ([GIREF](http://www.giref.ulaval.ca)).[^o10]

In addition to over 150 research papers on mathematics, he has written a student textbook on numerical analysis for engineers "Analyse numérique pour ingénieurs" in 1994.[^o11][^o12][^o13] The textbook has been awarded the Prix Roberval in 1996.[^o14]

Moreover, Fortin has received the CAIMS-Fields Industrial Mathematics Prize in 2012 in recognition of his exceptional contribution throughout his career to research in industrial mathematics.[^o15]
