# Stefano Montaldo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefano Montaldo"}

**Stefano Montaldo** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor working at the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari)[^o2] in the fields of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498), and [biharmonic maps](wikipedia://en/52948288).

:::

Montaldo earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 1996, under the supervision of [John C. Wood](wikipedia://en/45284500).
