# Kalyna (cipher)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kalyna"}

**Kalyna** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Калина, [Viburnum opulus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viburnum_opulus)) is a symmetric [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594). It supports block sizes of 128, 256 or 512 bits; the key length is either equal to or double the block size.

:::

Kalyna was adopted as the national encryption standard of [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) in 2015 (standard DSTU 7624:2014) after holding Ukrainian national cryptographic competition. Kalyna is a [substitution–permutation network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution–permutation_network) and its design is based on the [Rijndael](wikipedia://en/1260) (AES) encryption function having quite different key schedule, another set of four different S-boxes and increased [MDS matrix](wikipedia://en/8288796) size.

Kalyna has 10 rounds for 128-bit keys, 14 rounds for 256-bit keys and 18 rounds for 512-bit keys. Independent researchers proposed some attacks on reduced-round variants of Kalyna, but all of them have a very high complexity and none of them are practical.
