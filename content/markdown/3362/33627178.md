# Scholz's reciprocity law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Scholz's reciprocity law"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Scholz's reciprocity law** is a [reciprocity law](wikipedia://en/247622) for quadratic residue symbols of real [quadratic number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_number_field) discovered by (txt) and rediscovered by (txt).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that *p* and *q* are rational [primes](wikipedia://en/23666) congruent to 1 mod 4 such that the [Legendre symbol](wikipedia://en/18567) (*p*/*q*) is 1. Then the ideal (*p*) factorizes in the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) of **Q**($\sqrt{q}$) as (*p*)=𝖕𝖕' and similarly (*q*)=𝖖𝖖' in the ring of integers of **Q**($\sqrt{p}$). Write ε<sub>*p*</sub> and ε<sub>*q*</sub> for the fundamental units in these quadratic fields. Then Scholz's reciprocity law says that

\[ε<sub>*p*</sub>/𝖖\] = \[ε<sub>*q*</sub>/𝖕\]

where \[\] is the quadratic residue symbol in a quadratic number field.

:::
