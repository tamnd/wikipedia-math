# Jens Erik Fenstad

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Jens Erik Fenstad"}

**Jens Erik Fenstad** (15 April 1935 – 13 April 2020) was a Norwegian mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fenstad graduated as *mag.scient.* from the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1959, and worked as a [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) there and at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). He was a professor at the University of Oslo from 1968 to 2003, except for the years 1989 to 1993, when he was vice rector (*prorektor*). On 29 May 1998 Fensted received an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) from the Faculty of Science and Technology at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden).[^o6][^o7] He served in the Executive Committee of the [Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science of the International Union of History and Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DLMPST) as Treasurer from 1975 to 1983 and as President from 1991 to 1995.[^o8]

He chaired the [Norwegian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/15255049) and the World Commission on Ethics (COMEST) of the [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO), and co-founded the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285). He has also edited the journal *[Nordisk Matematisk Tidsskrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordisk_Matematisk_Tidsskrift)*.[^o7] He was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o9]

Fenstad resided in [Østerås](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Østerås).[^o10] He died from [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) on 13 April 2020, two days before his 85th birthday.[^o11]
