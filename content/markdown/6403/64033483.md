# Richard Harington

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Harington"}

[The Rev.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverend) **Richard Harington** (5 May 1800 – 13 December 1853) was an [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colleges_of_the_University_of_Oxford) [head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_executive_officer) in the 19th century.[^o2]

:::

He was born in [Hanover Square, Westminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_Square,_Westminster) and educated at [Brasenose College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brasenose_College,_Oxford). A mathematician, he was Principal of Brasenose[^o4] from 1842[^o5] until his death.[^o6]

A son was [Sir Richard Harington, 11th Baronet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Richard_Harington,_11th_Baronet).
