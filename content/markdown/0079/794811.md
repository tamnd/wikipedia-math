# Shigefumi Mori

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shigefumi Mori"}

**Shigefumi Mori** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), particularly in relation to the classification of [three-folds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-fold). He won the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1990.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Mori completed his Ph.D. titled "The Endomorphism Rings of Some Abelian Varieties" under [Masayoshi Nagata](wikipedia://en/3086117) at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1978. He was a visiting professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) during 1977–1980, the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1981–82, [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) 1985–87 and the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) for periods during 1987–89 and again during 1991–92. He has been a professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) since 1990.

## Work {#Work}

*Image: Widden Mori.jpg.* With [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894) (1990)

He generalized the classical approach to the classification of [algebraic surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_surfaces) to the classification of algebraic [three-folds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-folds). The classical approach used the concept of [minimal models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_model_%28birational_geometry%29) of [algebraic surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_surfaces). He found that the concept of [minimal models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_model_%28birational_geometry%29) can be applied to [three-folds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-folds) as well if we allow some [singularities](wikipedia://en/54240) on them. The extension of Mori's results to dimensions higher than three is called the [minimal model program](wikipedia://en/15148924) and is an active area of research in algebraic geometry.

He has been elected president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868), becoming the first head of the group from East Asia.[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

He was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1990 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

In 2021, he received the [Order of Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Culture).[^o8]
