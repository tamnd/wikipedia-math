# HP-18C

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="HP-18C"}

*Image: HP-18C Financial Calculator from Hewlett-Packard, introduced 1986 (image changed to plan view, improved colours).jpg.* An HP-18C in the state used for operating it. When not in use or for transport, it can be folded together due to its [clamshell design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clamshell_design), so that its width becomes half of what it is in the image.

The **HP-18C** is a [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard) business [calculator](wikipedia://en/7593) which was quickly followed by the very similar but greatly improved [HP-19B](wikipedia://en/30220535). The HP-18C is HP's first [RPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RPL_programming_language)-based calculator internally,[^o4] even though this was not visible on user-level in this non user-programmable model. The user has a [solver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solver) (another HP first) available, but only had about 1.5 [KB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilobyte) of [continuous memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_Continuous_memory) available to store equations.

:::

The calculator has many functions buried in a menu structure. The clamshell design is fairly robust, but the battery door is the shortcoming of this whole line; 18C, 19B, and [28C/S models](wikipedia://en/5791181).

The HP-18C was introduced in June 1986.[^o4]
