# 1887 Puerto Rico census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1887 Puerto Rico census"}

The **1887 Puerto Rico census** was the ninth population census held in [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico) and the last census count under [Spanish rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Spain). Under Spanish rule, censuses had been taken at irregular intervals in Puerto Rico between 1765 and 1887.[^o3] The population count was 798,565 - an increase of 66,917 or 9.0% over the previous census in 1877.[^o4][^o5] After 1887, the following Puerto Rican census of 1899 was held during US rule.

:::

## Race {#Race}

Afro–Puerto Ricans The 1887 census had two primary race options; one for white people and one for "colored" people (non-white). At 474,933 people, 59.5% of the population was [white](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Puerto_Ricans). The total "colored" population was 323,632 people. 246,647 people were sub-classified as [Mulattos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulatto), making up 76.2% of the total non-white population.[^o7]
