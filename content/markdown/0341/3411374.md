# Soft cell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Soft cell"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **soft cell** is a shape with curved edges that can tile the 2D plane or 3D space.[^o2] The class of shapes was discovered in 2024 by [Gábor Domokos](wikipedia://en/52055656), [Alain Goriely](wikipedia://en/64431963), Ákos G. Horváth and Krisztina Regős.[^o3][^o4]

:::

The shapes are found in a wide variety of phenomena in nature, such as [river estuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estuary), [muscle fibres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_fibre), and the seashell chambers of the [nautilus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nautilus#Shell).[^o5][^o6]

According to Maths.ox.ac.uk, "The geometry of \[... these\] cells is similar to polyhedra, defined by a set of [vertices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertice), edges and faces. However \[...\] the edges of \[... these\] cells need not be straight and their faces need not be planar".[^o7]
