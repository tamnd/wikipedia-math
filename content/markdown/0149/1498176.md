# Johan van Benthem (logician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johan van Benthem"}

*Image: Leiden University Library interiors, Leiden, the Netherlands, January 2025 - 04.jpg.* Van Benthem: *Informatie is de enige grondstof die groeit in het gebruik* ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language) for *Information is the only commodity that grows while being used*). Quote on a glass door, [Leiden University Libraries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University_Libraries), 2025.

**Johannes Franciscus Abraham Karel** (**Johan**) **van Benthem** (born 12 June 1949 in [Rijswijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijswijk)) is a University Professor (*universiteitshoogleraar*) of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), emeritus, at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) at the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (at [CSLI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_the_Study_of_Language_and_Information)). He was awarded the [Spinozapremie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinozapremie) in 1996[^o4] and elected a Foreign Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_&_Sciences) in 2015.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Van Benthem studied [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) ([B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) 1969), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) ([M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) 1972) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) ([M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) 1973) at the University of Amsterdam and received a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the same university under supervision of [Martin Löb](wikipedia://en/7268088) in 1977. Before becoming University Professor in 2003, he held appointments at the University of Amsterdam (1973–1977), at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) (1977–1986), and as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the University of Amsterdam (1986–2003).

In 1992 he was elected member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o6]

Van Benthem is known for his research in the area of [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365). This research has resulted in [Van Benthem's theorem](wikipedia://en/84216575), which states that propositional modal logic is the fragment of [first-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983) that is invariant under [bisimulation](wikipedia://en/397571).[^o7]

He has also been active in the fields of [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), logical structures in natural language ([generalized quantifiers](wikipedia://en/7959499), [categorial grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorial_grammar), [substructural](wikipedia://en/412909) [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478)), [dynamic logic](wikipedia://en/31631871) and [update logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Update_logic), and applications of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) to [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), as well as applications of [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) to [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) ([game semantics](wikipedia://en/617121)). Van Benthem is a member of the group collectively publishing under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) [L. T. F. Gamut](wikipedia://en/1091808). He has also taught in China. He made an effort to encourage and organize international collaboration between Chinese and Western logicians.

Professor van Benthem retired from the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) in September 2014.
