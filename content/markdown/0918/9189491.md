# Alex Biryukov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alex Biryukov"}

**Alex Biryukov** (אלכס ביריוקוב) is a [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer), currently a full professor at the [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

His notable work includes the design of the [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) [LEX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LEX_%28cipher%29), as well as the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of numerous [cryptographic primitives](wikipedia://en/4928985). In 1998, he developed [impossible differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/3329225) together with [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920) and [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314).[^o4] In 1999, he developed the [slide attack](wikipedia://en/1821327) together with [David Wagner](wikipedia://en/298253). In 2009 he developed, together with [Dmitry Khovratovich](wikipedia://en/58758465), the first cryptanalytic attack on full-round [AES](wikipedia://en/1260)-192 and AES-256 that is faster than a [brute-force attack](wikipedia://en/53784).[^o5] In 2015 he developed the [Argon2](wikipedia://en/48963187) [key derivation function](wikipedia://en/449781) with Daniel Dinu and Dmitry Khovratovich.[^o6] Since 1994 Alex Biryukov is a member of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043).
