# François Fages

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Fages"}

**François Fages** (August 23, 1959) is a French [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) known for contributions in the areas of [unification theory](wikipedia://en/54432), [rule-based modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expert_system), [logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming),[^o3] [concurrent constraint logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrent_constraint_logic_programming),[^o4][^o5] [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) and [systems biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_biology).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Fages was born in Paris, France. He studied Mathematics and Physics at [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud), Computer Science at the [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre_et_Marie_Curie) and received his PhD from the [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre_et_Marie_Curie) under the supervision of [Gérard Huet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gérard_Huet), in 1983 at age 23.

## Career {#Career}

Fages took a junior researcher position from [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at [Ecole Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Normale_Supérieure), and became in addition, part-time teacher at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique) from 1985 to 1998, and part-time consultant at Thomson-CSF (now [Thales Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group)) research center from 1985 to 1996. Since 1999, he is a senior researcher at the [Institut national de recherche en informatique et en automatique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_national_de_recherche_en_informatique_et_en_automatique) (INRIA), the French national research institute on [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [control](wikipedia://en/7039).[^o8]

He is known in [unification theory](wikipedia://en/54432) for having shown the non-existence of minimal sets of unifiers in some equational theories (conjecture of Plotkin, 1972),[^o9] and the decidability of associative-commutative unification[^o10] in presence of several function symbols (conjecture of Stickel, 1981[^o11]).

In [rule-based modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_system_%28computer_science%29), he is known for having created in 1988 a reactive rule-based language at Thomson-CSF (now Thalès group), which was later industrialized by [ILOG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ILOG) (now IBM-Ilog) and became ILOG-Rules in 1996.

In [logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming), Fages' theorem states that if a [normal logic program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming) is positive-order-consistent—that is, there is no infinite sequence of ground atoms a<sub>0</sub>, a<sub>1</sub>, a<sub>2</sub>, ... related by positive dependencies from a<sub>i+1</sub> to a<sub>i</sub> in the atom dependency graph—then the [stable models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_model_semantics) of the program coincide with the two-valued Herbrand models of its [Clark's completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negation_as_failure). [^o12] This result has shown useful for implementing [stable model semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_model_semantics) with classical [propositional satisfiability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_satisfiability) solvers.[^o13] This theorem has also been generalized to [disjunctive logic programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming). [^o14]

In [concurrent constraint logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrent_constraint_logic_programming), he has established with Paul Ruet and Sylvain Soliman the logical semantics of concurrent constraint programs in [Jean-Yves Girard](wikipedia://en/579673)'s [linear logic](wikipedia://en/579675). This result has been generalized to [Constraint handling rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constraint_handling_rules)[^o15] and to the asynchronous [Pi-calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi-calculus).[^o16]

In 2010 Fages co-ordinated a project to use mathematics to improve the packing of light bulbs and other oddly shaped products.[^o17]

In 2014 Fages works in [computational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) [systems biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_biology), coordinates the development of the Biochemical Abstract Machine (BIOCHAM) rule-based modeling and logical analysis software and studies biochemical processes in the [cell cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_cycle) and in [cell signaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_signaling).

In 2014, he received the Monpetit Prize from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o18]
