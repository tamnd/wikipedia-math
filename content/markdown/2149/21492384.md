# Ronald Loui

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald Loui"}

**Ronald Prescott Loui** is an American computer scientist and adjunct professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University). He previously taught at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) and [University of Illinois Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Springfield).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Loui grew up in Hawaii, where he was a classmate of [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) at [Punahou School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punahou_School).[^o4][^o5] He went on to earn a bachelor's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in applied mathematics in 1982, where his undergraduate thesis won an ACM award.[^o6] He completed a Ph.D. advised by [Henry E. Kyburg Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_E._Kyburg_Jr.), at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 1987.

He was a postdoctoral researcher at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1987–1988, working there with [Patrick Suppes](wikipedia://en/4824664) and [Amos Tversky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_Tversky), among others. From 1988 to 2008, he was a professor of computer science at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) in the [McKelvey School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKelvey_School_of_Engineering). He was also associated with multiple departments outside of engineering.[^o8]

Loui has published papers on [defeasible reasoning](wikipedia://en/2628057) in [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)[^o9] and he is a proponent of [scripting languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripting_language).[^o10] He is co-patent holder of a [deep packet inspection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_packet_inspection) hardware device that could read and edit the contents of packets as they stream through a network.[^o11]
