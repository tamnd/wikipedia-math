# Convexoid operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Convexoid operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), a **convexoid operator** is a [bounded](wikipedia://en/455961) [linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) *T* on a complex [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* such that the closure of the [numerical range](wikipedia://en/15581094) coincides with the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of its [spectrum](wikipedia://en/206137).

:::

An example of such an operator is a [normal operator](wikipedia://en/190167) (or some of its generalization).

A closely related operator is a [spectraloid operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectraloid_operator): an operator whose [spectral radius](wikipedia://en/386169) coincides with its [numerical radius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_radius). In fact, an operator *T* is convexoid if and only if $T - \lambda$ is spectraloid for every [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) $\lambda$.
