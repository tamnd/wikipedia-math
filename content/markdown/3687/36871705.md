# Saeid Abbasbandy

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Saeid Abbasbandy"}

**Saeid Abbasbandy** is an Iranian mathematician[^o4] and university [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Imam Khomeini International University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Khomeini_International_University). Abbasbandy was born on March 17, 1967, in [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran). He finished his high school course in Shariati High School and attended in the university entrance exam, then could enter [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran).

:::

His paper "Homotopy analysis method for quadratic Riccati differential equation" was singled out by *[Science Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Watch)* as a "Hot Paper in Mathematics" in March 2009.[^o5] Abbasbandy is editor-in-chief of a non-profit journal, *[Communications in Numerical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications_in_Numerical_Analysis)*.

## Education {#Education}

He received a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in September 1991 and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in February 1996 from [Kharazmi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharazmi_University). The title of his thesis was *Numerical Galerkin Methods for Integral Equations of the First kind*.[^o7]
