# Zassenhaus lemma

*Image: Butterfly lemma.svg.* [Hasse diagram](wikipedia://en/338199) of the Zassenhaus "butterfly" lemma – smaller subgroups are towards the top of the diagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zassenhaus lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **butterfly lemma** or **Zassenhaus lemma**, named after [Hans Zassenhaus](wikipedia://en/1376825), is a technical result on the [lattice of subgroups](wikipedia://en/3983172) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) or the [lattice of submodules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_of_submodules) of a [module](wikipedia://en/276410), or more generally for any [modular lattice](wikipedia://en/1089311).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=math-theorem name="Lemma"}

**Lemma.**

Suppose $G$ is a group with [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) $A$ and $C$. Suppose $B\triangleleft A$ and $D\triangleleft C$ are [normal subgroups](wikipedia://en/21918). Then there is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/12397) of [quotient groups](wikipedia://en/11526):

$$\frac{(A\cap C)B}{(A\cap D)B} \cong \frac{(A\cap C)D}{(B\cap C)D}.$$

:::

This can be generalized to the case of a [group with operators](wikipedia://en/4276393) $(G, \Omega)$ with [stable subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_subgroup) $A$ and $C$, the above statement being the case of $\Omega=G$ [acting](wikipedia://en/12781) on itself by [conjugation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugation_%28group_theory%29).

Zassenhaus [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) this lemma specifically to give the most direct proof of the [Schreier refinement theorem](wikipedia://en/947167). The 'butterfly' becomes apparent when trying to draw the [Hasse diagram](wikipedia://en/338199) of the various groups involved.

Zassenhaus' lemma for groups can be derived from a more general result known as [Goursat's theorem](wikipedia://en/5114212) stated in a [Goursat variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goursat_variety) (of which groups are an instance); however the group-specific [modular law](wikipedia://en/3983172#Properties) also needs to be used in the derivation.[^o4]
