# Michael Walker (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Walker"}

**Michael Walker** OBE (14 January 1947 – 27 September 2018) was an English mathematician who was instrumental in developing the standards that apply to [mobile telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_telecommunications) and particularly in respect of the [SIM card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIM_card).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::
