# Fioralba Cakoni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fioralba Cakoni"}

*Image: CakoniFioralba2012 MFO16075.jpg.* Fioralba Cakoni at the [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2012

**Fioralba Cakoni** is an American-Albanian mathematician and an expert on [inverse scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_scattering_theory). She is a professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cakoni earned bachelor's and master's degrees from the [University of Tirana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tirana) in 1987 and 1990 respectively. She completed her Ph.D. in 1996, jointly between the University of Tirana and [University of Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Patras), supervised by [George Dassios](wikipedia://en/63746199). Her dissertation was *Some Results on the Abstract Wave Equation. Problems of the Scattering Theory in Elasticity and Thermoelasticity in Low-Frequency*.

She became a lecturer at the University of Tirana and then, from 1998 to 2000, a Humboldt Research Fellow at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart). She came to the US for additional postdoctoral research at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) in 2000, and stayed at Delaware as an assistant professor beginning in 2002. She moved to [Rutgers University-New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University-New_Brunswick) in 2015 where she is now distinguished professor of mathematics. She serves on the Scientific Advisory Board for the [Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31112547) (ICERM).

## Books {#Books}

Cakoni is the author or coauthor of:

- *Qualitative Methods in Inverse Scattering Theory* (with David Colton, Springer, 2006)
- *The Linear Sampling Method in Inverse Electromagnetic Scattering* (with David Colton and Peter Monk, Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2011)
- *A Qualitative Approach to Inverse Scattering Theory* (with David Colton, Springer, 2014)
- *Inverse Scattering Theory and Transmission Eigenvalues* (with David Colton and Houssem Haddar, Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2016)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Cakoni was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to analysis of partial differential equations especially in inverse scattering theory". In 2020 Cakoni was elected foreign member of the [Academy of Sciences of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Albania). She was elected to the 2023 Class of [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Fellows.
