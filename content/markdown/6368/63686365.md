# Karen M. Bliss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karen M. Bliss"}

**Karen M. Bliss** is an American [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) who specializes in [biomedical applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedical_sciences) and [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science). She has co-authored many modeling handbooks, most notably, *Math Modeling: Getting Started and Getting Solutions*, for [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) in 2014 which is used by teachers and students learning the basics along with those participating in the [Mathematical Contest in Modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Contest_in_Modeling).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

For her undergraduate degree, Bliss attended the University of Missouri – Rolla ([Missouri University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_University_of_Science_and_Technology)) from 1994 to 1998 and graduated summa cum laude with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in applied mathematics. She attended [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University) for her graduate studies, earning her Master of Science in applied mathematics in May 2000. From 2000 to 2004, Bliss served in the [US Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) as a mathematics instructor for machinery operators. Under supervision of Harvey Banks and Hien Tran, she earned her PhD in Applied Mathematics in 2011 from [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University), with her dissertation on modeling red blood cells in kidney disease patients.[^o4]

After postdoctoral research at the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) as a Davies Fellow and teaching at the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy), Bliss became an assistant professor of Mathematics at [Quinnipiac University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinnipiac_University).[^o5] After moving to the [Virginia Military Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Military_Institute) as an associate professor in the department of Applied Mathematics and VCUR Programs Coordinator,[^o6] Bliss left academia in 2023 to work as Senior Manager of Education and Outreach for the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o7]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

With Kathleen Fowler and Benjamin J. Galluzzo, Bliss published *[Math Modeling: Getting Started and Getting Solutions](https://m3challenge.siam.org/sites/default/files/uploads/siam-guidebook-final-press.pdf)* for the MathWorks Math Modeling Challenge.[^o2] In 2018, Bliss published a more advanced handbook titled, *[Math Modeling: Computing and Communicating](https://m3challenge.siam.org/sites/default/files/uploads/siam-technical-guidebook-web.pdf)* with Benjamin J. Galluzzo, [Kathleen R. Kavanagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Kavanagh), and [Rachel Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Levy_%28mathematician%29).[^o9]
