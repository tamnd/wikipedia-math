# Ptak space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ptak space"}

A [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) $X$ is ***B*-complete** or a **Ptak space** if every subspace $Q \subseteq X^{\prime}$ is closed in the weak-\* topology on $X^{\prime}$ (i.e. $X^{\prime}_{\sigma}$ or $\sigma\left(X^{\prime}, X \right)$) whenever $Q \cap A$ is closed in $A$ (when $A$ is given the subspace topology from $X^{\prime}_{\sigma}$) for each equicontinuous subset $A \subseteq X^{\prime}$.[^n1]

:::

*B*-completeness is related to $B_r$-completeness, where a [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) TVS $X$ is **$B_r$-complete** if every dense subspace $Q \subseteq X^{\prime}$ is closed in $X^{\prime}_{\sigma}$ whenever $Q \cap A$ is closed in $A$ (when $A$ is given the subspace topology from $X^{\prime}_{\sigma}$) for each equicontinuous subset $A \subseteq X^{\prime}$.[^n1]

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Characterizations"}

## Characterizations {#Characterizations}

Throughout this section, $X$ will be a [locally convex](wikipedia://en/518844) [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS).

The following are equivalent:

1. $X$ is a Ptak space.
1. Every continuous [nearly open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearly_open) linear map of $X$ into any locally convex space $Y$ is a topological homomorphism.[^n2]
   - A linear map $u : X \to Y$ is called **nearly open** if for each neighborhood $U$ of the origin in $X$, $u(U)$ is dense in some neighborhood of the origin in $u(X).$

The following are equivalent:

1. $X$ is $B_r$-complete.
1. Every continuous [biunivocal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biunivocal), [nearly open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearly_open) linear map of $X$ into any locally convex space $Y$ is a TVS-isomorphism.[^n2]

:::

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every Ptak space is [complete](wikipedia://en/53695856). However, there exist complete Hausdorff [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) space that are not Ptak spaces.

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Homomorphism Theorem"}

**Homomorphism Theorem.**

Every continuous linear map from a Ptak space onto a barreled space is a topological homomorphism.[^n3]

:::

Let $u$ be a nearly open linear map whose domain is dense in a $B_r$-complete space $X$ and whose range is a locally convex space $Y$. Suppose that the graph of $u$ is closed in $X \times Y$. If $u$ is injective or if $X$ is a Ptak space then $u$ is an open map.[^n4]

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

There exist B<sub>r</sub>-complete spaces that are not B-complete.

Every [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) is a Ptak space. The strong dual of a [reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091) Fréchet space is a Ptak space.

Every closed vector subspace of a Ptak space (resp. a B<sub>r</sub>-complete space) is a Ptak space (resp. a $B_r$-complete space).[^n1] and every [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) of a Ptak space is a Ptak space.[^n4] If every Hausdorff quotient of a TVS $X$ is a B<sub>r</sub>-complete space then $X$ is a *B*-complete space.

If $X$ is a locally convex space such that there exists a continuous [nearly open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearly_open) surjection $u : P \to X$ from a Ptak space, then $X$ is a Ptak space.[^n3]

If a TVS $X$ has a closed [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) that is B-complete (resp. B<sub>r</sub>-complete) then $X$ is B-complete (resp. B<sub>r</sub>-complete).
