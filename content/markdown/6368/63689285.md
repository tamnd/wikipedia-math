# Evelyn Buckwar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Evelyn Buckwar"}

**Evelyn Buckwar** is a German mathematician specializing in [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454). She is Professor for Stochastics at the [Johannes Kepler University Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kepler_University_Linz) in Austria.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Buckwar earned a diploma in mathematics in 1992 from the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin), and completed her doctorate there in 1997. Her dissertation, *Iterative Approximation of the Positive Solutions of a Class of Nonlinear Volterra-type Integral Equations*, was supervised by [Rudolf Gorenflo](wikipedia://en/41334386).

## Career {#Career}

After working as a [Marie Curie Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Skłodowska-Curie_Actions) at the University of Manchester and then as a researcher at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), where she completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2005, she became a visiting professor at [Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_von_Guericke_University_Magdeburg) and [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) before becoming a lecturer at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in 2007. She took her present position at the Johannes Kepler University Linz in 2011.
