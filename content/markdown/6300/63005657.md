# Marius Crainic

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marius Crainic"}

**Marius Nicolae Crainic** (; February 3, 1973, [Aiud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aiud)) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician working in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Aiud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aiud), Romania, Crainic competed in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) of 1990 receiving a Bronze medal.[^o4] He obtained a bachelor's degree at [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University) ([Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca)) in 1995. He then moved to the Netherlands and obtained a master's degree in 1996 at [Nijmegen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 2000 from [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) under the supervision of [Ieke Moerdijk](wikipedia://en/25499812). His Ph.D. thesis is titled "*Cyclic cohomology and characteristic classes for foliations*".

He was a [Miller Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Research_Fellows)[^o6] at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 2001 to 2002. He then returned to Utrecht University as a Fellow of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (KNAW). In 2007 he became an associate professor at Utrecht University, and since 2012 he is a full professor. In 2016 he was elected member of KNAW.[^o7]

In 2008 Crainic was awarded the [André Lichnerowicz Prize in Poisson Geometry](wikipedia://en/67093003)[^o8][^o9] and in 2016 he received the [De Bruijn](wikipedia://en/3166399) Prize.[^o10][^o11] In 2026 he was awarded the Dutch Mathematics Prize for his pioneering work in the field of Poisson geometry.[^o12][^o13][^o14]

In July 2020 he was an invited speaker to the 8th [European congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846),[^o15] which has been rescheduled to 2021 due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).[^o16]

## Research {#Research}

Crainic's research interests lie in the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and its interactions with [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). His specialty is [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry)[^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23] and modern aspects of [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681), with several contributions to [foliation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foliation_theory),[^o24][^o25] [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298),[^o26] [Lie groupoids](wikipedia://en/310923),[^o27][^o28][^o29][^o30] [non-commutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917),[^o31] [Lie pseudogroups](wikipedia://en/1255458)[^o32] and the [geometry of PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o33]

Among his most well-known results are a solution to the long-standing problem of describing the obstructions to the integrability of [Lie algebroids](wikipedia://en/310921)[^o34] and a new geometric proof of Conn's linearization theorem,[^o35] both written in collaboration with [Rui Loja Fernandes](wikipedia://en/54037954), as well as the development of the theory of [representations up to homotopy](wikipedia://en/32387425).[^o36][^o37]

He is the author of more than 40 research papers in peer-reviewed journals[^o38] and has supervised 13 PhD students as of 2025.[^o5]
