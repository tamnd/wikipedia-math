# Tibor Šalát

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tibor Šalát"}

**Tibor Šalát** (1926-5-13 – 2005) was a Slovak mathematician, professor of mathematics, and Doctor of Mathematics who specialized in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). He was the author and co-author of undergraduate and graduate textbooks in mathematics, mostly in Slovak. And most of his scholarly papers have been published in various scientific journals.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Originally from [Žitava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radvaň_nad_Dunajom) by the southern region of [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia), he studied at the Faculty of Natural Sciences of [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague), where in 1952 he defended a dissertation entitled *Príspevok k teorii súčtov a nekonečných radov s reálnými členami* and supervised by Miloš Kössler and [Vojtěch Jarník](wikipedia://en/2175894).[^o4] In 1952 he went to work at the Faculty of Natural Sciences of [Comenius University in Bratislava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University_in_Bratislava), where he became an assistant professor in 1962. He was appointed to a full professorship position in 1972. And in 1974, he earned a Ph.D. in Mathematics from the same institution.[^o5]

He specialized in [Cantor's expansions](wikipedia://en/320819#Generalizations), [uniform distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_distribution_%28discrete%29), [statistical convergence](wikipedia://en/50723), [summation methods](wikipedia://en/876428#Properties_of_summation_methods) and [theory of numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_numbers).[^o5]

He wrote several undergraduate and graduate textbooks.
