# Conic optimization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conic optimization"}

**Conic optimization** is a subfield of [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411) that studies problems consisting of minimizing a [convex function](wikipedia://en/245568) over the intersection of an [affine subspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_subspace) and a [convex cone](wikipedia://en/3558266).

:::

The class of conic optimization problems includes some of the most well known classes of convex optimization problems, namely [linear](wikipedia://en/43730) and [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Given a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) *X*, a [convex](wikipedia://en/245568), real-valued [function](wikipedia://en/185427)

$$f:C \to \mathbb R$$

defined on a [convex cone](wikipedia://en/3558266) $C \subset X$, and an affine subspace $\mathcal{H}$ defined by a set of [affine](wikipedia://en/38449) constraints $h_i(x) = 0 \$, a conic [optimization problem](wikipedia://en/1126536) is to find the point $x$ in $C \cap \mathcal{H}$ for which the number $f(x)$ is smallest.

Examples of $C$ include the positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) $\mathbb{R}_+^n = \left\{ x \in \mathbb{R}^n : \, x \geq \mathbf{0}\right\}$, [positive semidefinite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive-semidefinite_matrix) matrices $\mathbb{S}^n_{+}$, and the **second-order cone** $\left \{ (x,t) \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\times \mathbb{R} : \lVert x \rVert \leq t \right \}$. Often $f \$ is a linear function, in which case the conic optimization problem reduces to a [linear program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_program), a [semidefinite program](wikipedia://en/4993539), and a [second order cone program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_order_cone_programming), respectively.

:::

## Duality {#Duality}

Certain special cases of conic optimization problems have notable closed-form expressions of their dual problems.

### Conic LP {#Conic_LP}

The dual of the conic linear program

minimize $c^T x \$

subject to $Ax = b, x \in C \$

is

maximize $b^T y \$

subject to $A^T y + s= c, s \in C^* \$

where $C^*$ denotes the [dual cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_cone) of $C \$.

Whilst [weak duality](wikipedia://en/34390896) holds in conic linear programming, [strong duality](wikipedia://en/34390810) does not necessarily hold.[^o8]

### Semidefinite Program {#Semidefinite_Program}

The dual of a semidefinite program in inequality form

minimize $c^T x \$

subject to $x_1 F_1 + \cdots + x_n F_n + G \leq 0$

is given by

maximize $\mathrm{tr}\ (GZ)\$

subject to $\mathrm{tr}\ (F_i Z) +c_i =0,\quad i=1,\dots,n$

$$Z \geq0$$
