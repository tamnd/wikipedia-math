# Aiyub Khan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aiyub Khan"}

**Aiyub Khan**(born on 1 July 1967) is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [Jai Narain Vyas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Narain_Vyas_University),[^o2][^o3] and politician in [Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan) from [Indian national congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_national_congress) [^o4] of Soorsagar constituency [Jodhpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodhpur)[^o5] candidate in 2018. Khan is member of [Rajasthan Public Service Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Public_Service_Commission).[^o6][^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Khan did his education in [Jai Narain Vyas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Narain_Vyas_University), he did [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) under supervision of [Prem Kumar Bhatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prem_Kumar_Bhatia),[^o9][^o10][^o11] in 1993. Khan is [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of [Royal Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Astronomical_Society)[^o12]

## Career {#Career}

Khan worked in SD PG college, Sriganganagar before joining [Jai Narain Vyas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Narain_Vyas_University) . Presently he is active in politics and also member of [Rajasthan Public Service Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Public_Service_Commission), under his supervision 4 students have done PhD. Khan is member of nine professional academies . Khan is member of Academic council of [Jai Narain Vyas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Narain_Vyas_University).[^o14]
