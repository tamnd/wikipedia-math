# Weak continuum hypothesis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weak continuum hypothesis"}

The term **weak continuum hypothesis** can be used to refer to the hypothesis that $2^{\aleph_0}<2^{\aleph_1}$, which is the negation of the [second continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/75602409).p. 80p. 3616 It is equivalent to a weak form of [◊](wikipedia://en/691736) on $\aleph_1$.p. 2 [F. Burton Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F._Burton_Jones) proved that if it is true, then every [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) [normal](wikipedia://en/48629) [Moore space](wikipedia://en/8016525) is [metrizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable).

:::

*Weak continuum hypothesis* may also refer to the assertion that every uncountable set of real numbers can be placed in bijective correspondence with the set of all reals. This second assertion was [Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216)'s original form of the [Continuum Hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuum_Hypothesis) (CH). Given the [Axiom of Choice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_of_Choice), it is equivalent to the usual form of CH, that $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1$.p. 155p. 289
