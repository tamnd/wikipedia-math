# Situational logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Situational logic"}

**Situational logic** (also **situational analysis**)[^o2] is a concept advanced by [Karl Popper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Popper) in his *[The Poverty of Historicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Poverty_of_Historicism)*.[^o3] Situational logic is a process by which a social scientist tries to reconstruct the problem situation confronting an [agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_%28economics%29) in order to understand that agent's choice.

:::

[Noretta Koertge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noretta_Koertge) (1975) provides a helpful clarificatory summary.[^o4]

First provide a description of the situation:

''Agent A was in a situation of type C''.

This situation is then analysed

''In a situation of type C, the appropriate thing to do is X.''

The [rationality principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_rationality) may then be called upon:

''agents always act appropriately to their situation''

Finally we have the *explanadum*:

''(therefore) A did X.''[^o5]
