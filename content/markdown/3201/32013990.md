# Spin geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spin geometry"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **spin geometry** is the area of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) where objects like [spin manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_manifold) and [Dirac operators](wikipedia://en/1079448), and the various associated index theorems have come to play a fundamental role both in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

An important generalisation is the theory of [symplectic](wikipedia://en/294298) Dirac operators in symplectic spin geometry and [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), which have become important fields of mathematical research.
