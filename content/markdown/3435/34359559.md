# William Arthur (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Arthur"}

**William Arthur** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) [MC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Cross) (12 January 1894 – 22 February 1979) was a Scottish mathematician.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 12 January 1894[^o4] at Fergushill near [Kilwinning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilwinning) in [Ayrshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrshire). He studied at Queen's Park High School in [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow)[^o5] then studied Mathematics at [Glasgow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_University) graduating MA in 1915.[^o4] As most, his career was interrupted by the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War) during which he served in the [Welsh Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Guards). He won the [Military Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Cross) for his bravery.

When demobbed in 1919 he began lecturing in mathematics at Glasgow University. He rose to Senior Lecturer.

In 1921 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [George Alexander Gibson](wikipedia://en/50996810), [Andrew Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Gray_%28physicist%29), [James Gordon Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gordon_Gray), and [Robert Alexander Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Alexander_Houston).[^o6]

He retired in 1959 but then went to teach in America, at Bethany College in [West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia) 1960 to 1962.
