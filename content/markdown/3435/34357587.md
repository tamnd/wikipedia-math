# Vogan diagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vogan diagram"}

In mathematics, a **Vogan diagram**, named after [David Vogan](wikipedia://en/10229758), is a variation of the [Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/277109) of a real semisimple [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) that indicates the maximal compact subgroup. Although they resemble [Satake diagrams](wikipedia://en/20514575) they are a different way of classifying simple Lie algebras.

:::
