# Appell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Appell"}

**Appell** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Dave Appell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Appell) (1922–2014), American arranger, producer, and musician
- [Olga Appell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Appell) (born 1963), Mexican-American long-distance runner
- [Paul Émile Appell](wikipedia://en/3359510) or M. P. Appell (1855–1930), French mathematician and rector of the University of Paris
  - [Appell polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appell_polynomials), a polynomial sequence named after Paul Appell
  - [Appell's equation of motion](wikipedia://en/11717900), an alternative formulation of classical mechanics
