# Philippe Biane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philippe Biane"}

*Image: Philippe Biane Oberwolfach 2005.jpg.* Biane at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2005

**Philippe Biane** (born 1962) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [group representation](wikipedia://en/12695). He was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1995, together with [Yuval Peres](wikipedia://en/36477942).[^o2]

:::

Together with Marc Yor he studied the [connections between the Brownian motion and Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/80149811).[^o3]
