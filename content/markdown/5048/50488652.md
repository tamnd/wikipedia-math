# Marta Lewicka

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marta Lewicka"}

**Marta Lewicka** (born 23 November 1972) is a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)[\-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). Lewicka has contributed results in the theory of hyperbolic systems of conservation laws, [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), nonlinear elasticity, nonlinear potential theory and [differential games](wikipedia://en/17507367).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Lewicka earned bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics in 1996 from the [University of Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gdańsk), and a second engineering bachelor's degree in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) in 1998 from [Częstochowa University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Częstochowa_University_of_Technology).[^o5] She completed her Ph.D. in 2000 from the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) under the supervision of [Alberto Bressan](wikipedia://en/23804321).[^o5] After postdoctoral research at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and a term as L.E. Dickson Instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), she joined the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) faculty in 2005. She moved in 2010 to [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), and again in 2011 to the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh).[^o5]

In 2023-2024 she is the Vice Chair of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics Activity Group on Analysis of Partial Differential Equations.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2016, she gave an AMS invited address at the AMS/MAA Joint Mathematical Meetings in the area of nonlinear elasticity and geometry of prestrained materials.[^o8] In 2017, she gave a Howard Rowlee Lecture.[^o9] In 2017, she received Professor's scientific title, awarded in Poland by the President of the Republic of Poland.[^o10] In 2018, she received a Simons Fellowship in Mathematics.[^o11]

She was elected a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in the Class of 2021,[^o12] with citation "For contributions to partial differential equations, calculus of variations, and continuum mechanics." In 2022 she was a fellow of the Lady Davis Foundation[^o13] and a visiting professor at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. She was named as a Fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), in the 2027 class of fellows, "for sustained leadership and impact in advancing women and girls in mathematics through mentorship, community building, and outreach initiatives".[^o14]
