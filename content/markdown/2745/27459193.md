# Malmquist

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Malmquist"}

**Malmquist** is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_language) surname, which means "ore-branch". Alternative spellings include Malmqvist and Malmkvist. The name may refer to:

:::

- [Axel Johannes Malmquist](wikipedia://en/42074599) (1882–1952), Swedish mathematician
- [Göran Malmqvist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göran_Malmqvist) (1924–2019), Swedish linguist
- [Gunnar Malmquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunnar_Malmquist) (1893–1982), Swedish astronomer
- [Linus Malmqvist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linus_Malmqvist) (born 1981), Swedish football player
- [Robin Malmkvist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Malmkvist) (born 1987), Swedish football player
- [Siw Malmkvist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siw_Malmkvist) (born 1936), Swedish singer
- [Sten Malmquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sten_Malmquist) (1917–2004), Swedish economist
- [Tom Malmquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Malmquist) (born 1978), Swedish writer
- [Walter Malmquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Malmquist) (born 1956), American skier

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

- [1527 Malmquista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1527_Malmquista), a main-belt asteroid
- [Malmquist bias](wikipedia://en/2073846), in observational astronomy
- [Malmquist index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malmquist_index), to compare the production technology of two economies
- [Malmquist's theorem](wikipedia://en/42074715) in differential equations
