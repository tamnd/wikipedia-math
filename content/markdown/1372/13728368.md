# Robert Alyngton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Alyngton"}

**Robert Alyngton** (died September 1398) was an English [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) who developed new [logical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical), [semantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic), [metaphysical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysical), and [ontological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontological) theories in 14th century thought. Alyngton is credited with creating the ideological foundation for the **Oxford realists** by substituting reference to objective reality with reference to mental and linguistic reality.[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Alyngton was a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_%28college%29) of [The Queen's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford)[^o4] from 1379 until 1386. He was deeply influenced by the [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) of [John Wyclif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wyclif) who began his theological studies at Queen's College in 1363. Alyngton was [Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_%28education%29) of [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) in 1394–5.[^o5] He later became [Rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28ecclesiastical%29) of [Long Whatton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Whatton), [Leicestershire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicestershire), until his death 1398.

## Philosophical works {#Philosophical_works}

- *Litteralis sententia super Praedicamenta Aristotelis* – a commentary on Aristotle's Categories.
- *Tractatus de suppositionibus terminorum* – a treatise on the supposition of terms. Early [linguistic philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_philosophy).
- A commentary on the *[Liber sex principiorum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liber_sex_principiorum)*.
- *Tractatus generum* – a treatise on the genera of being.
