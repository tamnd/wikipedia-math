::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Cantellated 5-cubes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 5-cube** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) of the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

:::

There are 6 unique cantellation for the 5-cube, including truncations. Half of them are more easily constructed from the dual [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615)

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *cantellated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(\pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2})\right)$$

- Bicantellated penteract, bicantellated 5-orthoplex, or bicantellated pentacross
- Small birhombated penteractitriacontaditeron (Acronym: sibrant) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *bicantellated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

(0,1,1,2,2)
