# Ladislav Rieger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ladislav Rieger"}

**Ladislav Svante Rieger** (25 June 1916 – 14 February 1963) was a Czech [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He worked in the areas of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [axiomatic set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatic_set_theory). He is considered to be the founder of mathematical logic in Czechoslovakia, having begun his work around 1957.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Ladislav Rieger was born on 25 June 1916 in [Malmö](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malmö), Sweden. His father, Ladislav Rieger Sr., was a philosopher and university professor at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University). He attended a secondary school in [Žilina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Žilina) and graduated in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague). He then studied mathematics at the [Faculty of Science, Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_of_Science,_Charles_University) and completed his studies, interrupted by the war, in 1945. Until 1958, he worked at the [Czech Technical University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Technical_University_in_Prague). From 1958 until his death, he worked at the Institute of Mathematics of the [Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Academy_of_Sciences). He died in Prague on 14 February 1963.[^o6] He is buried at [Vyšehrad Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyšehrad_Cemetery).[^o7]
