# Bounding point

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bounding point"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **bounding point** of a [subset](wikipedia://en/27631) of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) is a conceptual extension of the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) of a [set](wikipedia://en/26691).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $A$ be a subset of a vector space $X$. Then $x \in X$ is a *bounding point* for $A$ if it is neither an [internal point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_point) for $A$ nor its [complement](wikipedia://en/54347).[^o4]

:::
