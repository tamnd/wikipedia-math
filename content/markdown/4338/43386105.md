# Victor-Amédée Lebesgue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor-Amédée Lebesgue"}

**Victor-Amédée Lebesgue** (2 October 1791 – 10 June 1875; sometimes written **Le Besgue**) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

Lebesgue was born in [Grandvilliers, Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandvilliers,_Oise) in 1791. He was elected a member of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences) in 1847. He died in [Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordeaux), Gironde, in 1875.
