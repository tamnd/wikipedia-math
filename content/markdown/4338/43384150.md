# Mary Schaps

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Schaps"}

**Mary Elizabeth Schaps** (מלכה אלישבע שפס; born August 6, 1948), also known as **Malka Elisheva Schaps**, is an Israeli-American mathematician. She is Professor of Mathematics and Dean of the Faculty of Exact Sciences at [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University). She received her Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and has published in [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). She is also a writer, authoring several novels under the pseudonym **Rachel Pomerantz**.

:::

## Early and personal life {#Early_and_personal_life}

Mary Elizabeth Kramer was born on August 6, 1948, in [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland,_Ohio), United States.[^o4][^o5][^o6] From 1965 to 1969, she was educated at [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College) in Pennsylvania, [majoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majoring) in mathematics, philosophy and history, and graduating *summa cum laude* in 1969.[^o7] She then attended [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a mathematics graduate student: she completed her [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) (MA) degree in 1971 and her [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (PhD) degree in 1972.[^o7] Her doctoral thesis was titled "Non-singular deformations of space curves, using determinantal schemes": her advisors were [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) and [Heisuke Hironaka](wikipedia://en/13822).[^o6]

She was brought up a [Presbyterian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian) and then a [Unitarian Universalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitarian_Universalism).[^o4] She became interested in Judaism, and [formally converted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_to_Judaism) to [Conservative Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Judaism) in college.[^o4][^o11] In 1968, she married David Schaps, a classics professor who was then also studying for a PhD at Harvard.[^o8][^o9] They both gradually felt more and more drawn to [Orthodox Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthodox_Judaism) and considered themselves [Haredi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haredi_Judaism) by the time they had completed their doctorates.[^o4][^o11] The couple made [aliyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) (moved to Israel) in 1972.[^o5]

Together, she and her husband had two children: this was a small family by Haredi standards, and they raised four more [foster children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_children).[^o4][^o11] They live in [Bnei Brak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bnei_Brak), a Haredi city near Tel Aviv.[^o11]

## Career {#Career}

Schaps had some teaching experience at Harvard University: she was [teaching assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_assistant) from 1971 to 1972, and a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at the 1975 [Harvard Summer School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Summer_School).[^o6] In 1972, having moved to Israel, she was appointed a lecturer in mathematics at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).[^o6][^o11] In her second year in Israel the [Yom Kippur War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yom_Kippur_War) occurred: the change in language, culture and the threat to her new family were a shock.[^o4] In 1977, she moved to [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) as a [senior lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_lecturer): she was promoted to [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in 1991 and [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 2006.[^o7] She was the only female [Haredi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haredi_Judaism) professor in Israel.[^o5] In October 2013, she was appointed [Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of Exact Sciences at Bar-Ilan University: having been the only female Haredi professor, she now became the highest ranking Haredi woman in Israeli academia.[^o5][^o12] She stepped down as dean in 2015, and was made [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) on retirement in 2016.[^o6]
