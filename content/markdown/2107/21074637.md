# Jekuthiel Ginsburg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jekuthiel Ginsburg"}

**Jekuthiel Ginsburg** (1889–1957) was a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University). He established the journal *[Scripta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/13550249)*. He also was honored as a fellow of the [New York Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::
