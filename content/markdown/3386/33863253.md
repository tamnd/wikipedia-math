# John W. Pratt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John W. Pratt"}

**John Winsor Pratt** (born 1931) is the [William Ziegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Ziegler_%28industrialist%29) Professor of Business Administration, Emeritus, at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). His former education was conducted at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where he specialized in mathematics and statistics.[^o3] Pratt spent most of his academic career at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He was an editor of the *Journal of the American Statistical Association* from 1965 to 1970.[^o3] His researches on risk aversion, risk sharing incentives, and the nature and discovery of stochastic laws, statistical relationships that describe the effects of decisions.[^o3] He has made contributions to research in [risk aversion](wikipedia://en/177700) theory, notably with [Kenneth Arrow](wikipedia://en/49894) on measures of risk aversion.[^o4][^o5]

:::

In 1962 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o6]
