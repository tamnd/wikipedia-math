# British Society for the History of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="British Society for the History of Mathematics"}

The **British Society for the History of Mathematics** (**BSHM**) was founded in 1971 to promote research into the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) at all levels and to further the use of the history of [mathematics in education](wikipedia://en/326471).

:::

The BSHM is concerned with all periods and cultures, and with all aspects of mathematics. It participates in the [Joint Mathematical Council](wikipedia://en/7779526) of the United Kingdom.

The Society's journal, the British Journal for the History of Mathematics, is published on behalf of BSHM by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis).[^o2]

::: {#o4 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Neumann Prize"}

## Neumann Prize {#Neumann_Prize}

The Neumann prize is awarded biennially by the BSHM for "a book in English (including books in translation) dealing with the history of mathematics and aimed at a broad audience."[^o5] The prize was named in honour of [Peter M. Neumann](wikipedia://en/7151472), who was a longstanding supporter of and contributor to the society. It carries an award of £600.The previous winners are:

- 2025: *The Waltz of Reason: The Entanglement of Mathematics and Philosophy*, Karl Sigmund[^o6]
- 2023: *[Casanova's Lottery: The History of a Revolutionary Game of Chance](wikipedia://en/78329089)*, Stephen M. Stigler[^o7]
- 2021: *The Flying Mathematicians of World War I*, Tony Royle
- 2019: *Going Underground*, Martin Beech[^o8][^o9]
- 2017: *A Mind at Play*, [Jimmy Soni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Soni) & Rob Goodman
- 2015: *[The Thrilling Adventures of Lovelace and Babbage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thrilling_Adventures_of_Lovelace_and_Babbage)*, [Sydney Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Padua).
- 2013: *[The History of Mathematics: A Very Short Introduction](wikipedia://en/68366991)*, [Jacqueline Stedall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqueline_Stedall).
- 2011: *[The Math Book](wikipedia://en/36549055)*, [Clifford A. Pickover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_A._Pickover).
- 2009: *The Archimedes Codex*, [Reviel Netz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reviel_Netz) and [William Noel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Noel_%28archivist%29).

:::
