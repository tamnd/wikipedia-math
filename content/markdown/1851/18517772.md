# Mary Rees

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Rees"}

**Susan Mary Rees** (born 31 July 1953[^o2]) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and an emeritus professor of mathematics at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) since 2018, specialising in research in [complex dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_dynamical_system).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Rees was born in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge). After obtaining her BA in 1974 and MSc in 1975 at [St Hugh's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh's_College,_Oxford), she did research in mathematics under the direction of [Bill Parry](wikipedia://en/7618438) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), obtaining a PhD in 1978. Her first postdoctoral position was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1978 to 1979. Later she worked at [Institut des hautes études scientifiques](wikipedia://en/14527) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Following this she worked at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) until her retirement. She became professor of mathematics in 2002 and retired in 2018, becoming an emeritus professor.

She was awarded a [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 1988. The citation[^o6] notes that, in particular,

> Her most spectacular theorem[^o7] has been to show that in the space of [rational maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_map) of the [Riemann sphere](wikipedia://en/30876799) of degree *d* ≥ 2 those maps that are [ergodic](wikipedia://en/5456824) with respect to [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) and leave invariant an [absolutely continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolutely_continuous) [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437) form a set of positive measure.

She also spoke at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) at [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto) in 1990.[^o8] In recent years, much of Rees' work has focused on the dynamics of quadratic rational maps; i.e. rational maps of the Riemann sphere of degree two, including an extensive monograph.[^o9] In 2004, she also presented an alternative proof of the [Ending Laminations Conjecture](wikipedia://en/30997240) of [Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190),[^o10] which had been proved by Brock, [Canary](wikipedia://en/46914909) and [Minsky](wikipedia://en/46905726) shortly before.[^o11]

## FRS {#FRS}

She was elected to a [Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_P,_Q,_R#R) of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 2002.

## Family {#Family}

Her father [David Rees](wikipedia://en/15398568) was also a distinguished mathematician, who worked on [Enigma](wikipedia://en/9256) in [Hut 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hut_6) at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037). Her sister [Sarah Rees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Rees_%28professor%29) is also a mathematician.[^o8]
