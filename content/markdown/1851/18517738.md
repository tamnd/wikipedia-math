# Ulrike Tillmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrike Tillmann"}

**Ulrike Luise Tillmann** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) is a mathematician specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), who has made important contributions to the study of the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of [algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curves). She was the president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in the period 2021–2022.

:::

She is titular Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and a Fellow of [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford).[^o3][^o4] In 2021 she was appointed Director of the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and N.M. Rothschild & Sons Professor of Mathematical Sciences at Cambridge, but continued to hold a part-time position at Oxford.[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Tillmann completed her *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* at Gymnasium Georgianum (Vreden) in [Vreden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vreden).[^o3] She received a [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1985, followed by a [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1987. She read for a PhD under the supervision of [Ralph Cohen](wikipedia://en/48490486) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where she was awarded her doctorate in 1990.[^o3] She was awarded [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1996 from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o4]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 2004 she was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o9]

She was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society in 2008[^o10] and a Fellow of the American Mathematical Society in 2013.[^o11] She has served on the council of the Royal Society and in 2018 was its vice-president.[^o12] In 2017, she became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o13]

Tillmann was awarded the Bessel Prize by the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation) in 2008[^o14] and was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2009.[^o15]

She was elected as president-designate of the London Mathematical Society in June 2020 and took over the presidency from [Jonathan Keating](wikipedia://en/46796537) in November 2021.[^o16] She was elected to the European Academy of Sciences (EURASC) in 2021.[^o17] In October 2021 she became the director of the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837), taking a post which lasts for five years.[^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Tillmann's parents are Ewald and Marie-Luise Tillmann. In 1995 she married Jonathan Morris with whom she has had three daughters.[^o4]

Tillmann is a member of the Oxford Jewish Congregation. As part of the celebration of the 750th anniversary of the founding of Merton College, she organized a symposium on the history of Merton College and its relationship with Oxford's Jewish community.[^o19]
