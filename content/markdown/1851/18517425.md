# Lauricella's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lauricella's theorem"}

In the theory of [orthogonal functions](wikipedia://en/453041), **Lauricella's theorem** provides a condition for checking the [closure of a set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closure_of_a_set) of [orthogonal functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_function), namely:

:::

*Theorem* – A necessary and sufficient condition that a normal orthogonal set $\{u_k\}$ be closed is that the formal series for each function of a known closed normal orthogonal set $\{v_k\}$ in terms of $\{u_k\}$ [converge in the mean](wikipedia://en/50723#Convergence_in_mean) to that function.

The theorem was proved by [Giuseppe Lauricella](wikipedia://en/3734008) in 1912.
