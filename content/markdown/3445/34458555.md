# Böttcher's equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Böttcher's equation"}

**Böttcher's equation**, named after [Lucjan Böttcher](wikipedia://en/36448971), is the [functional equation](wikipedia://en/364377)

:::

$$F(h(z)) = (F(z))^n$$

where

- $h$ is a given [analytic function](wikipedia://en/61478) with a superattracting [fixed point](wikipedia://en/449738) of order $n$ at $a$, (that is, $h(z)=a+c(z-a)^n+O((z-a)^{n+1}) ~,$ in a [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) of $a$), with *n* ≥ 2
- $F$ is a sought function.

The [logarithm](wikipedia://en/17860) of this functional equation amounts to [Schröder's equation](wikipedia://en/5834541).

## Solution {#Solution}

Solution of [functional equation](wikipedia://en/364377) is a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) in [implicit form](wikipedia://en/250896).

[Lucian Emil Böttcher](wikipedia://en/36448971) sketched a proof in 1904 on the existence of solution: an analytic function *F* in a neighborhood of the fixed point *a*, such that:[^o3]

$$F(a)= 0$$

This solution is sometimes called:

- the Böttcher coordinate
- the Böttcher function[^o4]
- [the Boettcher map](wikipedia://en/4804435).

The complete proof was published by [Joseph Ritt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Ritt) in 1920,[^o5] who was unaware of the original formulation.[^o6]

Böttcher's coordinate (the logarithm of the [Schröder function](wikipedia://en/5834541)) conjugates $h(z)$ in a neighbourhood of the fixed point to the function $z^{n}$. An especially important case is when $h(z)$ is a polynomial of degree $n$, and $a$ = ∞ .[^o7]

### Explicit {#Explicit}

One can explicitly compute Böttcher coordinates for:[^o9]

- power maps $z\to z^d$
- [Chebyshev polynomials](wikipedia://en/184539)

::: {#o11 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

#### Examples {#Examples}

For the function h and n=2[^o12]

$$h(x)= \frac{x^2}{1 - 2x^2}$$

the Böttcher function F is:

$$F(x)= \frac{x}{1 + x^2}$$

:::

::: {#o14 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Böttcher's equation plays a fundamental role in the part of [holomorphic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_dynamics) which studies [iteration](wikipedia://en/68833) of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) of one [complex variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_variable).

Global properties of the Böttcher coordinate were studied by [Fatou](wikipedia://en/629100)[^o15] [^o16] and [Douady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Douady) and [Hubbard](wikipedia://en/6674772).[^o17]

:::
