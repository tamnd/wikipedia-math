# Arnaud Chéritat

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arnaud Chéritat"}

**Arnaud Chéritat** (born June 7, 1975) is a French mathematician who works as a director of research at the [Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse](wikipedia://en/20908760).[^o3] His research concerns [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518) and the shape of [Julia sets](wikipedia://en/56434).[^o4]

:::

Chéritat earned a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in mathematics in 1995 from the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies) in pure mathematics in 1996 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), and a master's degree in pure and applied mathematics and informatics in 1998 from the École Normale Supérieure.[^o3] He defended his doctoral thesis in 2001 from the University of Paris-Sud, under the supervision of [Adrien Douady](wikipedia://en/1604604), and completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2008 from the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse).[^o3] He worked as a [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_rank_in_France) at the University of Toulouse from 2002 until 2007, when he moved to the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse.[^o3]

In 2006, Chéritat won the [Leconte Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leconte_Prize) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010.[^o7] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
