# Giuliana Davidoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giuliana Davidoff"}

**Giuliana P. Davidoff** is an American mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [expander graphs](wikipedia://en/9313). She is the Robert L. Rooke Professor of Mathematics and the chair of mathematics and statistics at [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Davidoff is a graduate of [Rollins College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollins_College). She completed her Ph.D. in 1984 at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), with [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak) as her doctoral advisor; her dissertation was *Statistical Properties of Certain Exponential Sums*.

## Books {#Books}

Davidoff is a coauthor of:

- *[Elementary Number Theory, Group Theory and Ramanujan Graphs](wikipedia://en/66228643)* (with [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak) and Alain Valette, 2003)
- *[The Geometry of Numbers](wikipedia://en/66108522)* (with [Carl D. Olds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_D._Olds) and [Anneli Cahn Lax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anneli_Cahn_Lax), 2001)
- *Laboratories in Mathematical Experimentation: A Bridge to Higher Mathematics* (1997)
