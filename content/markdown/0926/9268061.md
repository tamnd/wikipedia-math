# Novikov's condition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Novikov's condition"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Novikov's condition** is the sufficient condition for a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) which takes the form of the [Radon–Nikodym derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon–Nikodym_derivative) in [Girsanov's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girsanov's_theorem) to be a [martingale](wikipedia://en/274536). If satisfied together with other conditions, Girsanov's theorem may be applied to a [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) stochastic process to change from the original [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) to the new measure defined by the Radon–Nikodym derivative.

:::

This condition was suggested and proved by [Alexander Novikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Novikov_%28mathematician%29).[^o2] There are other results which may be used to show that the Radon–Nikodym derivative is a martingale, such as the more general criterion [Kazamaki's condition](wikipedia://en/30125554), however Novikov's condition is the most well-known result.

Assume that $(X_t)_{0\leq t\leq T}$ is a real valued adapted process on the probability space $\left (\Omega, (\mathcal{F}_t), \mathbb{P}\right)$ and $(W_t)_{0\leq t\leq T}$ is an adapted [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436):[^o3]

If the condition

$$\mathbb{E}\left[e^{\frac12\int_0^T|X|_t^2\,dt} \right]<\infty$$

is fulfilled then the process

$$\ \mathcal{E}\left( \int_0^t X_s \; dW_s \right) \ = e^{\int_0^t X_s\, dW_s -\frac{1}{2}\int_0^t X_s^2\, ds},\quad 0\leq t\leq T$$

is a martingale under the probability measure $\mathbb{P}$ and the [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) $\mathcal{F}$. Here $\mathcal{E}$ denotes the [Doléans-Dade exponential](wikipedia://en/16986027).
