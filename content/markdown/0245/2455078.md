# Edward Jones (statistician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Jones"}

**Edward Davis Jones** (October 7, 1856 – February 16, 1920)[^o3] was an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/26685) and [journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalist).[^o4] Jones is best known as the "Jones" in the [Dow Jones Industrial Average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dow_Jones_Industrial_Average) and as a co-founder of *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal).*

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Jones was born on October 7, 1856, in [Worcester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts). Jones' parents, Reverend John Jones and Clarissa (Day) Jones, were of [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_people) descent.[^o6] Jones graduated from [Worcester Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Academy) and attended [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) before dropping out in his junior year.[^o7][^o8] After leaving Brown, Jones worked as a reporter for the *Providence Morning Star* and *Evening Press*, where he met [Charles Dow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dow).

## Dow Jones {#Dow_Jones}

The company which is famous for the *[Dow Jones Industrial Average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dow_Jones_Industrial_Average)*, *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal)* was founded by Jones and [Charles Dow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dow) in 1882[^o10] "in the basement of the [New York Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Stock_Exchange)";[^o11][^o12] [Charles Bergstresser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Bergstresser) was a silent partner.

Jones had met Dow while both had worked as fellow reporters in [Providence, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island).[^o13] He died in New York in 1920 at the age of 63.
