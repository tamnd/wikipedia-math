# Cristina Manolache

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cristina Manolache"}

**Cristina Manolache** is a mathematician and Senior Lecturer in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Manolache received her PhD in Mathematics from [SISSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SISSA) in 2009.[^o5] Her dissertation, Virtual Intersections,[^o6] was supervised by [Barbara Fantechi](wikipedia://en/60435526).[^o6] Manolache specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and has expertise in birational geometry and wall crossings.[^o7] She has contributed to publications of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press). Notable publications include Reduced invariants from cuspidal maps (2020),[^o8] co-authored with Luca Battistella and Francesca Carocci; Stable maps and stable quotients (2014);[^o9] Virtual pull-backs\] (2012);[^o10] and Virtual push-forwards (2012).[^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Manolache was awarded the [Emmy Noether Fellowship](wikipedia://en/66190328) in 2020.[^o13]
