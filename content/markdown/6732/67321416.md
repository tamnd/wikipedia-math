# Besicovitch inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Besicovitch inequality"}

In mathematics, the **Besicovitch inequality** is a [geometric inequality](wikipedia://en/18973446) relating volume of a set and distances between certain subsets of its boundary. The inequality was first formulated by [Abram Besicovitch](wikipedia://en/404566).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Consider the n-dimensional cube $[0,1]^n$ with a [Riemannian](wikipedia://en/195243) metric $g$. Let $d_i= dist_g(\{x_i=0\}, \{x_i=1\})$ denote the distance between opposite faces of the cube. The Besicovitch inequality asserts that $\prod_i d_i \leq Vol([0,1]^n,g)$ The inequality can be generalized in the following way. Given an n-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) M with connected boundary and a smooth map $f: M \rightarrow [0,1]^n$, such that the restriction of f to the boundary of M is a [degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_of_a_map) 1 map onto $\partial [0,1]^n$, define $d_i= dist_M(f^{-1}(\{x_i=0\}), f^{-1}(\{x_i=1\}))$ Then $\prod_i d_i \leq Vol(M)$.

The Besicovitch inequality was used to prove [systolic inequalities](wikipedia://en/2545455) on surfaces.[^o5][^o6]

:::
