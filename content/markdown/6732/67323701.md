# Jane Pendergast

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jane Pendergast"}

**Jane Pendergast** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) specializing in [multivariate statistics](wikipedia://en/19384) and [longitudinal data](wikipedia://en/1557623). She is a professor in the Department of Biostatistics & Bioinformatics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pendergast majored in mathematics at the [University of Dayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dayton), graduating in 1974, and went to the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) for graduate study, earning a master's degree in 1976 and completing her Ph.D. in 1979. Her dissertation was *Robust Estimation in Growth Curve Models*.

After working as research faculty at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida) beginning in 1980, she moved to the University of Iowa as an associate professor in 1999, and was promoted to full professor there in 2005. She moved to her incumbent position at Duke University in 2015.

She was regional president for the Eastern North American Region of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) in 2006, and chair of the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344) for 2013–2015.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Pendergast was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1998, and a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 2016 "for advancing statistics within public health and for her skilled, creative and dedicated service to the profession, including effectively advocating for improved recognition of AAAS Sections".

She was a recipient of the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224) in 2017, for her service to the association.
