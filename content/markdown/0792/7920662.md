# William A. Stein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William A. Stein"}

**William Arthur Stein** (born February 21, 1974) is an American [software developer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_developer) and previously a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).

:::

Stein received his PhD in 2000, from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), for his dissertation ‘Explicit Approaches to Modular Abelian Varieties’,[^o3] supervised by [Hendrik W. Lenstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lenstra).

Stein is the lead developer of [SageMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SageMath) and founder of [CoCalc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoCalc). Stein does computational and theoretical research into the problem of [computing](wikipedia://en/5926) with [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) and the [Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](wikipedia://en/435161).[^o4] He is considered "a leading expert in the field of computational arithmetic".[^o5]

Stein was the 2013 recipient[^o6] of the Richard Dimick Jenks Memorial Prize Award, presented by [SIGSAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGSAM), a special interest group of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery).
