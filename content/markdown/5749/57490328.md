# Ruslan Smelyansky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruslan Smelyansky"}

Leonidovich Смелянский, Руслан Леонидович

**Ruslan Leonidovich Smelyansky** (Руслан Леонидович Смелянский; born 12 November 1950) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC), [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

:::

He defended the thesis «Analysis of the performance of multiprocessor systems based on the invariant behavior of programs» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1990).[^o4] He is the author of six books.[^o5][^o6]
