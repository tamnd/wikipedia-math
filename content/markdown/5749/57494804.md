# Dmitry Sychugov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitry Sychugov"}

Сычугов, Дмитрий Юрьевич

**Dmitry Sychugov** (Дми́трий Ю́рьевич Сычу́гов) (born 1955) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^o3]

:::

He graduated from the faculty [MSU CMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC) (1976). Has been working at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) since 1980.

He defended the thesis «Mathematical modeling of plasma confinement processes in toroidal traps» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (2013).

He is the author of five books and more than 80 scientific articles.[^o4][^o5]

**Area of scientific interests**: mathematical modeling, computational physics of plasma.[^o6]
