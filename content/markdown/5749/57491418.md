# Igor Simonenko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igor Simonenko"}

**Igor Borisovich Simonenko** (Игорь Борисович Симоненко; 16 August 1935, [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev) — 22 March 2008, [Rostov-on-Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov-on-Don)) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences), Honoured Scientist of the Russian Federation.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Igor Borisovich Simonenko was born on 16 August 1935 in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev). In 1947 he entered [Luhansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luhansk) Machine-Building Technical School and later worked at factory. Since 1953 he studied at the Faculty of Mechanics of [Rostov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_State_University) and in 1959 graduated it with honours.[^o6] The results of his diploma work were published in the reports of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]

Then he continued to study in graduate school, his scientific supervisor was Professor [Fyodor Gakhov](wikipedia://en/57430695). In 1961 he defended his [candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) thesis. Six years later, at the age of 32, he wrote his [doctoral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) dissertation.[^o4]

[Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) since 1970. In 1972 he was appointed head of the newly created Department of Algebra and Discrete Mathematics of Rostov State University.[^o5]

Simonenko wrote two monographs on the application of the local principle. During his research he made discoveries in the field of one-dimensional and multidimensional singular integral equations. The results were published in the academic press and were later translated into [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) by The [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

Igor Simonenko supervised the defense of 22 candidate and 4 doctoral dissertations.[^o6]

He died on 22 March 2008 in Rostov-on-Don.[^o6]
