# Representation rigid group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Representation rigid group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), a group is said to be **representation rigid** if for every $n$, it has only finitely many isomorphism classes of complex [irreducible representations](wikipedia://en/385982) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) $n$.

:::
