# Demographics of Rotselaar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Rotselaar"}

This table shows the total **population of the [Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) of [Rotselaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotselaar)** in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), as well as that of each of its constituent [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commune_%28subnational_entity%29), on January 1 of each year since the merger of Rotselaar, [Werchter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werchter) and Wezemaal in 1977.

:::
