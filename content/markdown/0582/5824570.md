# Nodec space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nodec space"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$ is a **nodec space** if every [nowhere dense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowhere_dense) [subset](wikipedia://en/27631) of $X$ is [closed](wikipedia://en/47279). This concept was introduced and studied by (van Douwen 1993).

:::
