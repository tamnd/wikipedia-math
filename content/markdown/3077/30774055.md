# Auslander–Buchsbaum theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Auslander–Buchsbaum theorem"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), the **Auslander–Buchsbaum theorem** states that [regular local rings](wikipedia://en/506959) are [unique factorization domains](wikipedia://en/60546).

:::

The theorem was first [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by (txt). They showed that regular [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391) of dimension 3 are unique factorization domains, and (txt) had previously shown that this implies that all regular local rings are unique factorization domains.
