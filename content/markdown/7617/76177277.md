# Pekka K. Sinervo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pekka K. Sinervo"}

**Pekka K. Sinervo** is a [Finnish-Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish-Canadian)[^o4] experimental particle physicist who has studied quarks,[^o5] gluons[^o6] and vector bosons[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10] using high-energy electron and proton collisions.

:::

## Career {#Career}

He is a professor of physics at [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), where he has taught and pursued research since 1990.[^o12][^o13][^o14] [^o15]His earlier work involved the study of mesons with charm quarks and strange quarks, and hadrons with bottom quarks (see, e.g.,[^o16][^o17][^o18]). He is currently working on searches for new particles (see, e.g.,[^o19][^o20]) and dark matter[^o21] at the SNOLAB Underground Laboratory, as a member of the SuperCDMS Collaboration.[^o22]

Sinervo served as the 16th [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the [Faculty of Arts and Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Faculty_of_Arts_and_Science) at the University of Toronto[^o23] from 2003 to 2008, and as senior vice-president, research at the [Canadian Institute for Advanced Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Institute_for_Advanced_Research)[^o24] from 2009 to 2015. He has written on international research collaborations, science and Judaism, and popular science (see, e.g.,[^o25][^o26]). He is a member of the Reform Jewish Community of Canada,[^o27] serving as its president from 2017 to 2021, and has served in leadership roles in the Union for Reform Judaism[^o28] and the World Union for Progressive Judaism.[^o29]

Fellowships in the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society),the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada), the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) and the [Canadian Association of Physicists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Association_of_Physicists) recognize his contributions to the discovery of the top quark. He was appointed a member of the [Order of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Canada) in 2018.[^o30]
