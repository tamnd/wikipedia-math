# Spencer Bloch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spencer Bloch"}

**Spencer Janney Bloch** (born May 22, 1944)[^o3] is an American mathematician known for his contributions to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [algebraic *K*-theory](wikipedia://en/598500). Bloch is the R. M. Hutchins Distinguished Service Professor Emeritus in the Department of Mathematics of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Bloch introduced the [Bloch group](wikipedia://en/32933846) in 1978.[^o5] He introduced [Bloch's higher Chow group](wikipedia://en/47095205), a generalization of [Chow groups](wikipedia://en/4282051), in 1986.[^o6] He also introduced [Bloch's formula](wikipedia://en/41048991) in [Algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500).[^o7]

Bloch and [Kazuya Kato](wikipedia://en/4616818) formulated the [motivic Bloch–Kato conjecture](wikipedia://en/27126857) relating [Milnor K-theory](wikipedia://en/2914128) and [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) in 1986[^o8] and the [Bloch–Kato conjectures for special values](wikipedia://en/25305882#Conjectures) of [*L*-functions](wikipedia://en/245283) in 1990.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Bloch is a member of the [U.S. National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._National_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o11] and a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences)[^o12][^o13] and of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]

He received a [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 1996.[^o15] He also received a 2021 [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Lifetime Achievement.[^o16]

At the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), he gave an invited lecture in 1978[^o17] and a plenary lecture in 1990.[^o13][^o18] He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1981–82.[^o19]
