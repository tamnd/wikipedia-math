# Percy Deift

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Percy Deift"}

**Percy Alec Deift** (born September 10, 1945)[^o3] is a mathematician known for his work on [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713), [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213), [random matrix theory](wikipedia://en/1648765) and [Riemann–Hilbert problems](wikipedia://en/9710396).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Deift was born in Durban, [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa), where he obtained degrees in [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and received a Ph.D. in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1977.[^o5] He is a Silver Professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Deift is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (elected 2012),[^o7] a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (elected 2003),[^o8] and of the [U.S. National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._National_Academy_of_Sciences) (elected 2009).[^o9][^o10]

He is a co-winner of the 1998 [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28SIAM%29),[^o3][^o11] and was named a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1999.[^o3][^o12] He gave an [invited address at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Berlin in 1998[^o3][^o13][^o14] and plenary addresses in 2006 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid and at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o15] Deift gave the Gibbs Lecture at the Joint Meeting of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2009.[^o16] Along with [Michael Aizenman](wikipedia://en/22411175) and [Giovanni Gallavotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Gallavotti), he won the [Henri Poincaré Prize](wikipedia://en/4890712) in 2018.

- with Jinho Baik and Toufic Suidan (2016-06-22). "Combinatorics and Random Matrix Theory". American Mathematical Society. isbn:978-0-8218-4841-8. <http://bookstore.ams.org/gsm-172/>
