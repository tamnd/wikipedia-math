# Peter Dalgaard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Dalgaard"}

**Peter Dalgaard** (born 1959) is a Danish [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and one of the core developers of the [R statistical programming language](wikipedia://en/376707).[^o3] He is a professor at [Copenhagen Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen_Business_School)[^o4] and was previously a professor of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), where he obtained his MSc in 1985 and PhD in 1991.[^o5]

:::

Dalgaard co-edited the *[Scandinavian Journal of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavian_Journal_of_Statistics)* from 2016 to 2018.[^o6]
