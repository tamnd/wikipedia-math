# Roger Bivand

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Roger Bivand"}

**Roger Simon Bivand** (born 5 April 1951) is a British [geographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographer), [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and professor at the [Norwegian School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_School_of_Economics). He specialises in [open source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) for [spatial analysis](wikipedia://en/3190431),[^o4] and played a major role in developing functions for spatial data in the [R statistical programming language](wikipedia://en/376707), including the [R packages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R_packages) sp, rgdal, maptools and rgrass7.[^o5][^o6] His book *Applied Spatial Data Analysis with R* (2008), coauthored with [Edzer Pebesma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edzer_Pebesma) and [Virgilio Gómez-Rubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgilio_Gómez-Rubio), is considered "the authoritative resource on R's spatial capabilities".[^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bivand was born in [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol) on 5 April 1951.[^o9] He studied geography at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and obtained his PhD from the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) in 1975,[^o4] with a [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_thesis) on the [economic geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_geography) of [Sogn og Fjordane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sogn_og_Fjordane) in Norway.[^o10] He went on to receive a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from the [Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University_in_Poznań) in 1982.[^o4]

Bivand joined the Norwegian School of Economics in 1988 and was appointed a professor in 1996.[^o4] In 1991-92 he was the director of the Business School (MBA studies) of the Warsaw University of Technology (Poland). He has been an editor of several academic journals, including the *[Journal of Statistical Software](wikipedia://en/2179145)*, *[The R Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_R_Journal)*, the *[Journal of Geographical Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Geographical_Systems)*, *[Geographical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographical_Analysis_%28journal%29)* and the *[Norwegian Journal of Geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Journal_of_Geography)*.[^o4] In 2018 he received a Lifetime Achievement Award from the OpenGeoHub Foundation.[^o11]
