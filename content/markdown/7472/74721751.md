# Angelo Vistoli

Vistoli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Angelo Vistoli"}

**Angelo Vistoli** (born 1 June 1958, [Massa Lombarda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massa_Lombarda))[^o3] is an Italian mathematician working on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Vistoli earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) (the Italian equivalent of a master's degree) in 1981 at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).[^o3] He became a student of [Michael Artin](wikipedia://en/1519821) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), where he completed his Ph.D. in 1987 with the dissertation *Duality in the Intersection Theory of Moduli Spaces*.

After becoming Benjamin Pierce Lecturer and assistant professor in mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), he returned to Italy as a professor at the [University of Basilicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basilicata) in 1990. He moved to the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) in 1993, and to the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in 2006, where he is professor of geometry.[^o3]

In 2024, he succeeded [Andrea Ferrara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Ferrara_%28astrophysicist%29) as president of the Classe di Scienze (Faculty of Science) of the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore).[^o6]
