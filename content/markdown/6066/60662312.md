# Christina Kendziorski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christina Kendziorski"}

**Christina Marie Kendziorski** is a [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) whose research involves [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics), [statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736), and the statistical analysis of data from [high-throughput sequencing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics#High-throughput_sequencing). She is a professor in the Department of Biostatistics & Medical Informatics at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison),

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kendziorski grew up in the [housing projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing_in_the_United_States) of Chicago, in an apartment shared by her mother and grandmother, neither of whom had a college education. She earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1992 from the [University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Eau_Claire), and completed her Ph.D. in 1998 from [Marquette University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquette_University), where she studied mathematics with a specialization in biostatistics. Her dissertation, supervised by Peter J. Tonellato, was *A Physiologially Based Mathematical Model of Arterial Pressure Recordings*.

After completing her doctorate, Kendziorski became a postdoctoral scholar at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. She was hired as regular-rank faculty there in 2001. Since 2010, she has headed the Statistical Genetics and Genomics program in Wisconsin's Institute for Translational and Clinical Research.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kendziorski became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2018.
