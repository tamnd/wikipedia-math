# Amir Dembo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amir Dembo"}

**Amir Dembo** (אמיר דמבו; born October 25, 1958, [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa)) is an Israeli-American mathematician, specializing in probability theory. He was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2022,[^o3] and of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2023.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dembo received his bachelor's degree in electrical engineering in 1980 from the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion). He obtained in 1986 his doctorate in electrical engineering under the supervision of [David Malah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Malah) with the thesis "Design of Digital FIR Filter Arrays". He joined Stanford University as Assistant Professor of Statistics and Mathematics in 1990, and is currently the Marjorie Mhoon Fair Professor in Quantitative Science there.

His research deals with probability theory and stochastic processes, the theory of [large deviations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_deviations), the spectral theory of [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765), [random walks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_walks), and interacting particle systems.

He was Invited Speaker with the talk *Simple random covering, disconnection, late and favorite points* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) in 2006. Dembo is a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

His doctoral students include [Scott Sheffield](wikipedia://en/40341916) and [Jason P. Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_P._Miller).
