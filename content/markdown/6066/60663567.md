# Margaret Armstrong (geostatistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Armstrong"}

**Margaret Armstrong** is an Australian [geostatistician](wikipedia://en/51531), mathematical geoscientist, and textbook author. She works as an associate professor in the School of Applied Mathematics at the [Fundação Getúlio Vargas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundação_Getúlio_Vargas) in Brazil, and as a research associate in the Centre for Industrial Economics of [Mines ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mines_ParisTech) in France.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Armstrong graduated from the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland) in 1972, with a bachelor's degree in mathematics and a diploma of education. After working as a mathematics teacher she returned to graduate study, first with a master's degree in mathematics from Queensland in 1977, and then with [Georges Matheron](wikipedia://en/4415402) at the [École des Mines de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_Mines_de_Paris). She completed her doctorate there in 1980.

## Books {#Books}

Armstrong is the author of the textbook *Basic Linear Geostatistics* (Springer, 1998), and co-author of the book *Plurigaussian Simulations in Geosciences* (Springer, 2003; 2nd ed., 2011). With Matheron, she edited *Geostatistical Case Studies* (Springer, 1987).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1998, Armstrong was the winner of the [John Cedric Griffiths Teaching Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cedric_Griffiths_Teaching_Award) of the [International Association for Mathematical Geosciences](wikipedia://en/43927898). The award statement noted "her aptitude at the blackboard", the international demand for her short courses, and the "great clarity" of her book *Basic Linear Geostatistics*.
