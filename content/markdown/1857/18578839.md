# Mark J. Ablowitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mark J. Ablowitz"}

**Mark Jay Ablowitz** (born 1945) is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in the department of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) at the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder), [Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ablowitz was born on June 5, 1945, in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City).[^o5] He received his [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 1967,[^o5] and completed his Ph.D. in mathematics under the supervision of [David Benney](wikipedia://en/49032906) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1971.[^o3][^o6]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Ablowitz was an assistant professor of mathematics at [Clarkson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarkson_University) during 1971–1975 and an associate professor during 1975–1976. He visited the Program in Applied Mathematics founded by [Ahmed Cemal Eringen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Cemal_Eringen) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) during 1977–1978. He was a professor of mathematics at [Clarkson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarkson_University) during 1976–1985, where he became the Chairman of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science in 1979. On July 1, 1985, he was appointed as the Dean of Science of Clarkson University and served there until he joined to the department of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) (APPM) at [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) on June 30, 1989.[^o8]

### Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship), 1975–1977.
- [Clarkson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarkson_University) Graham Research Award, 1976.
- [John Simon Guggenheim Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Simon_Guggenheim_Foundation) Fellowship, 1984.
- [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Fellow, 2011.
- [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) Symposium on Soliton Theory Kiev, USSR 1979.
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012.[^o10]
