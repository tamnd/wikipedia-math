# Giulia Di Nunno

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giulia Di Nunno"}

**Giulia Di Nunno** (born 1973) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [stochastic analysis](wikipedia://en/228001) and [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics) who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), with an adjunct appointment at the [Norwegian School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_School_of_Economics).

:::

As well as for her research, Di Nunno is known for promoting mathematics in Africa.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Di Nunno earned a degree in mathematics from the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan) in 1998, including research on stochastic functions with Yurii Rozanov. She moved to the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) for doctoral studies, continuing with Rozanov as an informal mentor but under the official supervision of Eugenio Regazzini. She completed her Ph.D. in 2003; her dissertation was *On stochastic differentiation with applications to minimal variance hedging*.

She joined the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 2003, and added her affiliation with the [Norwegian School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_School_of_Economics) in 2009.

## Activism {#Activism}

Di Nunno is the chair of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470)'s Committee for Developing Countries, and has worked to promote young researchers to visit Africa and to establish "Emerging Regional Centres of Excellence" there. The [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227) gave her their 2019 [Su Buchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su_Buqing) Prize for this work, citing her "long-lasting record actively and efficiently encouraging top-level mathematical research and education in developing African countries".

## Books {#Books}

With Bernt Karsten Øksendal and Frank Proske, Di Nunno is a co-author of the book *Malliavin calculus for Lévy processes with applications to finance* (Springer, 2009). She also co-edited *Advanced Mathematical Methods for Finance* (Springer, 1011) with Øksendal.
