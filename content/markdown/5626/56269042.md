# Victor Pavlovich Palamodov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Pavlovich Palamodov"}

**Victor Pavlovich Palamodov**, ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Виктор Павлович Паламодов; born 1938) is a Russian and Israeli mathematician, specializing in analysis.

:::

Palamodov received from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1959 his Russian candidate degree (Ph.D) under [Georgiy Shilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgiy_Shilov). Palamodov then taught at Moscow State University. Later he taught at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).

Palamodov has done research on partial differential equations, integral geometry, and complex analysis in several variables (deformations of [complex analytic spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_space)).

In 1966 he was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Moscow.[^o3]
