# Johann Schröder (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Schröder"}

**Johann Wiards Albert Schröder** (4 April 1925, [Norden, Lower Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norden,_Lower_Saxony) – 3 January 2007) was a German mathematician.

:::

Schröder studied mathematics and physics at [Leibniz University Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_University_Hannover) and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). In 1952 at Leibniz University Hannover he received his [Promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (Ph.D.) under [Lothar Collatz](wikipedia://en/1859481) for his thesis *Fehlerabschätzungen zur Störungsrechnung bei linearen Eigenwertproblemen*.

In 1955 Schröder received his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation). From 1955 to 1957 he taught at the [Braunschweig University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braunschweig_University_of_Technology) and from 1957 to 1963 at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), where from 1961 to 1963 he was an adjunct professor.

In 1963 Schröder was appointed professor at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne), where he retired in 1986 as professor emeritus. He was a visiting professor at the [University of Wisconsin&ndash;Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin&ndash;Madison) for the academic year 1960–1961 and at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle) for the academic years 1964–1965 and 1969–1970.

In 1966 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) he was a Plenary Speaker with his talk *Ungleichungen und Fehlerabschätzungen* (Inequalities and error estimates).

He died as a result of an accident and was buried in the Bensberg cemetery.
