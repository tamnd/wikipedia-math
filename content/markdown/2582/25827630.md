# Crumpled cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crumpled cube"}

In [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), a branch of mathematics, a **crumpled cube** is any space in [**R**<sup>3</sup>](wikipedia://en/9697) [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to a [2-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782) together with its [interior](wikipedia://en/55610).[^o2] Lininger showed in 1965 that the [union](wikipedia://en/47949) of a crumpled cube and an [open 3-ball](wikipedia://en/160556) glued along their [boundaries](wikipedia://en/72824) is a 3-sphere.[^o3]

:::
