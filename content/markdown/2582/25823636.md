# Journal of Differential Geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Differential Geometry"}

The ***Journal of Differential Geometry*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by International Press on behalf of [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University) in 3 volumes of 3 issues each per year. The journal publishes an annual supplement in book form called ***Surveys in Differential Geometry***. It covers [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and related subjects such as [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), and [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) of [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was established in 1967 by [Chuan-Chih Hsiung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuan-Chih_Hsiung),[^o4][^o5] who was a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in the Department of Mathematics at Lehigh University at the time. Hsiung served as the journal's editor-in-chief, and later co-editor-in-chief, until his death in 2009.

In May 1996, the annual Geometry and Topology conference which was held at Harvard University was dedicated to commemorating the 30th anniversary of the journal and the 80th birthday of its founder.[^o6] Similarly, in May 2008 Harvard held a conference dedicated to the 40th anniversary of the *Journal of Differential Geometry*.[^o7]

## Reception {#Reception}

In his 2005 book *Mathematical Publishing: A Guidebook*, Steven Krantz writes: "At some very prestigious journals, like the *Annals of Mathematics* or the *Journal of Differential Geometry*, the editorial board meets every couple of months and debates *each* paper in detail."[^o9]

The journal is abstracted and indexed in *[MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683)*, *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*, [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences, and the [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2013 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.093.[^o10]
