# Bing shrinking

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bing shrinking"}

In [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), a branch of mathematics, the **Bing shrinking criterion**, introduced by (Bing 1952), is a method for showing that a [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) of a space is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the space.

:::
