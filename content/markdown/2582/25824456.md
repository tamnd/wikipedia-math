# Double suspension theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Double suspension theorem"}

In [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), the **double suspension theorem** of [James W. Cannon](wikipedia://en/19404886) ((Cannon 1979)) and Robert D. Edwards states that the double [suspension](wikipedia://en/1251702) *S*<sup>2</sup>*X* of a [homology sphere](wikipedia://en/585388) *X* is a topological sphere.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

If *X* is a [piecewise-linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise-linear_manifold) homology sphere but not a sphere, then its double suspension *S*<sup>2</sup>*X* (with a triangulation derived by applying the double suspension operation to a triangulation of *X*) is an example of a triangulation of a topological sphere that is not piecewise-linear. The reason is that, unlike in piecewise-linear manifolds, the link of one of the suspension points is not a sphere.
