# Hans Wilhelm Alt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Wilhelm Alt"}

*Image: Alt hans wilhelm.jpg.* Alt at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

**Hans Wilhelm Alt** (born 1945, [Hilden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilden)) is a German mathematician, specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and their applications.

:::

Alt received his *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* in 1965 from the prestigious secondary school Helmholtz-Gymnasium Hilden. In 1971 he received his PhD from the [Georg-August-Universität Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg-August-Universität_Göttingen) under [Erhard Heinz](wikipedia://en/31696540) with thesis *Verzweigungspunkte von H-Flächen* (Branching points of H-surfaces.) Alt became a professor at the Institute for Applied Mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where he retired as professor emeritus in 2010. In 2011 he was made an honorary professor at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).[^o3]

His research deals with, among other topics, free [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) for [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787) and [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825) with applications to [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanic) and [thermodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic). For example, he has done research on axially symmetric jet flows and propagation of fluids in inhomogeneous porous media. More recently, he has studied the mathematical theory of [phase transitions](wikipedia://en/54423).

in 1986 Alt was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). He is the author of a textbook on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), which has been translated into English.

His doctoral students include [Harald Garcke](wikipedia://en/48325437) and [Barbara Niethammer](wikipedia://en/50965646). His brother is the [biomathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomathematician) Wolfgang Alt.
