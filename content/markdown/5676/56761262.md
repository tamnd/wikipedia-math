# Modern Stochastics: Theory and Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modern Stochastics: Theory and Applications"}

***Modern Stochastics: Theory and Applications*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) that was established in 2014. It is published cooperatively by [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University) (Lithuania), [Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv) (Ukraine), and VTeX (Lithuania). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Kestutis Kubilius (Vilnius University) and [Yuliya Mishura](wikipedia://en/63515731) (Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv). The journal covers all aspects of [stochastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastics).

:::

## Focus and scope {#Focus_and_scope}

The journal publishes original research papers in:

- [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542)
- [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711)
- theory of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes) and random fields
- [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) and [stochastic differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_differential_equations);
- probabilistic aspects of [fractal analysis](wikipedia://en/18745015)
- [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813)
- [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics)
- [actuarial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actuarial_mathematics) and [risk theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_theory)
- applications in economics, biology, physics, engineering
- optimization and control
