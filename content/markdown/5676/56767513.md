# Zariski's finiteness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zariski's finiteness theorem"}

In algebra, **Zariski's finiteness theorem** gives a positive answer to [Hilbert's 14th problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_14th_problem) for the [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065) in two variables, as a special case.[^o2] Precisely, it states:

:::

Given a [normal domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_domain) *A*, [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/11985661) as an algebra over a field *k*, if *L* is a subfield of the [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) of *A* containing *k* such that the [transcendence degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendence_degree) $\operatorname{tr.deg}_k(L) \le 2$, then the *k*-subalgebra $L \cap A$ is finitely generated.
