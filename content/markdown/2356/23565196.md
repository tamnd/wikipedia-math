# Lambert of Auxerre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lambert of Auxerre"}

**Lambert of Auxerre** was a medieval 13th century logician best known for writing the book "*Summa Lamberti*" or simply "*Logica*" [^o2] in the mid 1250s which became an authoritative textbook on logic in the Western tradition.[^o3] He was a Dominican in the Dominican house at [Auxerre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auxerre). His contemporaries were [Peter of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_of_Spain_%28author%29), [William of Sherwood](wikipedia://en/2884630), and [Roger Bacon](wikipedia://en/25879).[^o4]

:::
