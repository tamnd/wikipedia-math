# Outline of cryptography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of cryptography"}

The following [outline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_%28list%29) is provided as an overview of and topical guide to cryptography:

:::

**[Cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432)** (or **cryptology**) – practice and study of hiding [information](wikipedia://en/18985062). Modern cryptography intersects the disciplines of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering). Applications of cryptography include [ATM cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_teller_machine), computer passwords, and [electronic commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_commerce).

## [Cryptographic hash functions](wikipedia://en/439526) {#Cryptographic_hash_functions}

  - [HMAC](wikipedia://en/17112) – [NESSIE](wikipedia://en/497430) selection MAC; [ISO/IEC 9797-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_9797-1), [FIPS PUB 113](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIPS_PUB_113) and IETF [RFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Request_for_Comments)

- [RTR0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTR0) – one of Retter series; developed by [Maciej A. Czyzewski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maciej_A._Czyzewski); 160-bit digest

- [Snefru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snefru_%28cryptography%29) – NIST hash function competition

## Organizations and selection projects {#Organizations_and_selection_projects}

### Cryptography standards {#Cryptography_standards}

- [Federal Information Processing Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Information_Processing_Standard) (FIPS) Publication Program – run by [NIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIST) to produce standards in many areas to guide operations of the US Federal government; many FIPS publications are ongoing and related to cryptography
- [American National Standards Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_National_Standards_Institute) (ANSI) – standardization process that produces many standards in many areas; some are cryptography related, ongoing)
- [International Organization for Standardization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Organization_for_Standardization) (ISO) – standardization process produces many standards in many areas; some are cryptography related, ongoing
- [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) (IEEE) – standardization process produces many standards in many areas; some are cryptography related, ongoing
- [Internet Engineering Task Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Engineering_Task_Force) (IETF) – standardization process that produces many standards called [RFCs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Request_for_Comments)) in many areas; some are cryptography related, ongoing)

### General cryptographic {#General_cryptographic}

- [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939) (NSA) – internal evaluation/selections, charged with assisting NIST in its cryptographic responsibilities
- [Government Communications Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters) (GCHQ) – internal evaluation/selections, a division is charged with developing and recommending cryptographic standards for the UK government
- [Defence Signals Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Signals_Directorate) (DSD) – Australian [SIGINT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGINT) agency, part of [ECHELON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ECHELON)
- [Communications Security Establishment](wikipedia://en/231712) (CSE) – Canadian intelligence agency

### Open efforts {#Open_efforts}

- [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978) (DES) – [NBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIST) selection process, ended 1976
- RIPE – division of the RACE project sponsored by the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union), ended mid-1980s
- [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260) (AES) – a "break-off" competition sponsored by [NIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIST), ended in 2001
- [NESSIE](wikipedia://en/497430) Project – an evaluation/selection program sponsored by the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union), ended in 2002
- [eSTREAM](wikipedia://en/2287686)– program funded by [ECRYPT](wikipedia://en/1635837); motivated by the failure of all of the [stream ciphers](wikipedia://en/49007) submitted to [NESSIE](wikipedia://en/497430), ended in 2008
- [CRYPTREC](wikipedia://en/549159) – evaluation/recommendation program sponsored by the Japanese government; draft recommendations published 2003
- [CrypTool](wikipedia://en/3305892) – an e-learning freeware programme in English and German— exhaustive educational tool about cryptography and cryptanalysis
