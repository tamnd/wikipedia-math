# Cubic threefold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic threefold"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **cubic threefold** is a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) of degree 3 in 4-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). Cubic threefolds are all [unirational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unirational), but (Clemens Griffiths 1972) used [intermediate Jacobians](wikipedia://en/3092994) to show that non-singular cubic threefolds are not rational. The space of lines on a non-singular cubic 3-fold is a [Fano surface](wikipedia://en/5311946).

:::

[Geometric invariant theory](wikipedia://en/1639003) (GIT) gives a [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of smooth cubic threefolds, with one point for each isomorphism class of smooth cubic threefolds. (Allcock 2003) compactified this moduli space by means of the GIT quotient of all polystable cubic forms in 5 variables. In particular, this compactification is isomorphic to the [K-moduli space](wikipedia://en/70175623#Moduli_spaces_of_K-stable_Fano_varieties) of cubic threefolds, as proven by (Liu Xu 2019).
