# Fermat quintic threefold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fermat quintic threefold"}

*Image: CalabiYau5.jpg.* Two-dimensional cross-section of the Fermat quintic threefold

In mathematics, a **Fermat quintic threefold** is a special [quintic threefold](wikipedia://en/23562429), in other words a [degree](wikipedia://en/35326250#D) 5, [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) 3 [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) in 4-dimensional complex [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135), given by the equation

:::

$$V^5+W^5+X^5+Y^5+Z^5=0.$$

This threefold, so named after [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966), is a [Calabi–Yau manifold](wikipedia://en/188932).

The [Hodge diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodge_diamond) of a non-singular quintic 3-fold is 1

## Rational curves {#Rational_curves}

(txt) conjectured that the number of rational curves of a given degree on a generic quintic threefold is finite. The Fermat quintic threefold is not generic in this sense, and (txt) showed that its lines are contained in 50 1-dimensional families of the form

$$(x : -\zeta x : ay : by : cy)$$

for $\zeta^5=1$ and $a^5+b^5+c^5=0$. There are 375 lines in more than one family, of the form

$$(x : -\zeta x : y :-\eta y :0)$$

for fifth [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) $\zeta$ and $\eta$.
