# Klein cubic threefold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klein cubic threefold"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Klein cubic threefold** is the non-singular [cubic threefold](wikipedia://en/23560713) in 4-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) given by the equation

:::

$$V^2W+W^2X+X^2Y+Y^2Z+Z^2V =0 \,$$

studied by (Klein 1879). Its automorphism group is the group PSL<sub>2</sub>(11) of order 660 (Adler 1978). It is [unirational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unirational) but not a [rational variety](wikipedia://en/865939). (Gross Popescu 2001) showed that it is birational to the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of (1,11)-polarized [abelian surfaces](wikipedia://en/974181).
