# Variational bicomplex

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Variational bicomplex"}

In mathematics, the [Lagrangian theory](wikipedia://en/24585634) on [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526) is globally formulated in algebraic terms of the **variational bicomplex**, without appealing to the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). For instance, this is the case of [classical field theory](wikipedia://en/1293340) on fiber bundles ([covariant classical field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariant_classical_field_theory)).

:::

The variational bicomplex is a [cochain complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochain_complex) of the [differential graded algebra](wikipedia://en/2415863) of [exterior forms](wikipedia://en/221519) on [jet manifolds](wikipedia://en/928060) of sections of a fiber bundle. [Lagrangians](wikipedia://en/24585634) and [Euler–Lagrange operators](wikipedia://en/24585634) on a fiber bundle are defined as elements of this bicomplex. [Cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of the variational bicomplex leads to the global first variational formula and first [Noether's theorem](wikipedia://en/150159).

Extended to Lagrangian theory of even and odd fields on [graded manifolds](wikipedia://en/24519420), the variational bicomplex provides strict mathematical formulation of classical field theory in a general case of reducible degenerate Lagrangians and the Lagrangian [BRST theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRST_formalism).
