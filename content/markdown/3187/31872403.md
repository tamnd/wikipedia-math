# Kai Behrend

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kai Behrend"}

**Kai A. Behrend** is a German mathematician. He is a professor at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in [Vancouver, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver,_British_Columbia), Canada.

:::

His work is in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and he has made important contributions in the theory of [algebraic stacks](wikipedia://en/717377), [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291) and [Donaldson–Thomas theory](wikipedia://en/32335603) (cf. [Behrend function](wikipedia://en/50529465)). He is also known for [Behrend's formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behrend's_formula), the generalization of the [Grothendieck–Lefschetz trace formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck–Lefschetz_trace_formula) to algebraic stacks.

He is the recipient of the 2001 [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444),[^o3] the 2011 [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069),[^o4] and the 2015 [CRM-Fields-PIMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRM-Fields-PIMS_Prize).[^o5] He was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] He was also an invited speaker at the ICM 2014[^o7].
