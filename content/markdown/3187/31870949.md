# Nils Dencker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nils Dencker"}

**Nils Jonas Dencker** (born 14 December 1953 in [Lund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund)) is a Swedish mathematician. Dencker earned his doctorate from [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University) in 1981 under supervision of [Lars Hörmander](wikipedia://en/500958), and is a professor of mathematics at Lund University. From 1981 until 1983, he was the [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o2]

:::

He won the 2005 [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) for his proof of the [Nirenberg–Treves conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nirenberg–Treves_conjecture).[^o3]

Dencker has been a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) since 2008. He was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Amsterdam in 2008, and at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), India, in 2010.[^o4] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
