# Scott A. Wolpert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Scott A. Wolpert"}

*Image: Scott Wolpert.jpg.* Scott Wolpert at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 1987

**Scott A. Wolpert** is an American mathematician specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He is a professor emeritus at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland).[^o2]

:::

Wolpert received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1976.

In 1986 he was an [Invited Speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). In 2012, Wolpert became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
