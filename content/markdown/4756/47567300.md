# Lollipop graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lollipop graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) discipline of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **(*m*,*n*)-lollipop graph** is a special type of [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) consisting of a [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) ([clique](wikipedia://en/524466)) on *m* vertices and a [path graph](wikipedia://en/4587190) on *n* vertices, connected with a [bridge](wikipedia://en/1305071).[^o3]

:::

The special case of the (2*n*/3,*n*/3)-lollipop graphs are known to be graphs which achieve the maximum possible [hitting time](wikipedia://en/7059985),[^o4] [cover time](wikipedia://en/81712895)[^o5] and [commute time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commute_time).[^o6]
