# David Madigan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Madigan"}

**David Bennett Madigan** (born 11 December 1962) is an Irish-American statistician and academic. He is currently Professor of Statistics and [Special Advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_advisor) to the President at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University).[^o3] From 2020 to 2025 he was Provost and Senior Vice-President for Academic Affairs at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University).[^o4] Previously he was Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)[^o5] at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). From 2013 to 2018 he was also the Executive Vice-President for Arts and Sciences and Dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences[^o6] and from 2008 to 2013 he served as Chair of the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), both at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). He was Dean of [Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_and_Mathematical_Sciences) at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) (2005–2007), Director of the Institute of Biostatistics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) (2003–2004), and Professor in the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) (2001–2007).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Madigan received his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (1984, First Class Honours, Gold Medal) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) (1990), both from [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin). His Ph.D. thesis, titled *An Investigation of Weights of Evidence in the Context of Probablilistic Expert Systems*, was written under the supervision of Krzysztof Mosurski.

## Career {#Career}

### Early career {#Early_career}

Madigan began his academic career in the 1990s at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) where he was an Assistant and then Associate Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). He received the University's Distinguished Teaching Award in 1995.[^o11] Madigan has also served as Vice President, Data Mining, Soliloquy, Inc. (2000-2001), Principal Technical Staff Member, [AT&T Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Labs)-Research (1999-2000), [Information Technology Consultant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology_consulting), [KPMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KPMG) (1989-1990), Technology Manager, Peregrine Expert Systems Ltd. (1986-1989), Expert System Consultant, [SkillSoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SkillSoft), (1985-1986), and Actuarial Associate, Hibernian Life Assurance, [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland) (1984-1985).

### Honors {#Honors}

Madigan is an elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (2014),[^o13] a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (2012), a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2006),[^o14] and a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (1999).[^o15] He has served as program co-chair for the FODS,[^o16] KDD[^o17] and AI-STATS conferences,[^o18] Action Editor for the [Journal of Machine Learning Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Machine_Learning_Research), Editor-in-Chief of [Statistical Science](wikipedia://en/15936018), and Editor-in-Chief of [Statistical Analysis and Data Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Analysis_and_Data_Mining), the ASA Data Science Journal.

### Scholarship {#Scholarship}

He has over 200 publications[^o20] in such areas as [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412), [text mining](wikipedia://en/318439), [Monte Carlo methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_methods), [pharmacovigilance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacovigilance) and probabilistic [graphical models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphical_models). He has advised 18 Ph.D. students.[^o21] In recent years he has focused on statistical methodology for generating reliable evidence from large-scale healthcare data.[^o22] From 2011 to 2014 he was a member of the FDA's Drug Safety and Risk Management Advisory Committee.[^o23]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Madigan grew up in [Athlone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athlone) in the center of [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland), where he attended the [Marist Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marist_Brothers) schools.
