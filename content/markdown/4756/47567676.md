# W. Stephen Wilson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="W. Stephen Wilson"}

**W. Stephen Wilson** is a mathematician based in [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) specializing in [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831).[^o2]

:::

Wilson received his Ph.D. from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1972 under the supervision of [Franklin Paul Peterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Paul_Peterson).

In 2012, Wilson became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
