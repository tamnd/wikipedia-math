# Richard Alan Wentworth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Alan Wentworth"}

**Richard Alan Wentworth** is an American mathematician based at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland).[^o2]

:::

Wentworth received his Ph.D. from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1990, where he was supervised by [Duong Hong Phong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duong_Hong_Phong). He took his BS from the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) in 1985.

In 2012, Wentworth became a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society).[^o4] He has served as Editor-in-Chief of *[Geometriae Dedicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometriae_Dedicata)* since January 2023.[^o5]
