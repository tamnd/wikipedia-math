# John Wermer

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="John Wermer"}

**John Wermer** was a mathematician specializing in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o4]

:::

Wermer received his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1951 under the supervision of [George Whitelaw Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Whitelaw_Mackey). He became an instructor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), after which he was hired as a professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1954. He retired in 1994.[^o6]

In 1962 Wermer was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Stockholm.[^o7]

In 2012, Wermer became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

On August 29, 2022, Wermer died in [Providence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island), [Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhode_Island) at the age of 95. [^o9]
