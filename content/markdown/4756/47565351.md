## Street names {#Street_names}

Names of streets are numbered in some communities. Systematically in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), New York City, NY State, USA e.g. [42nd Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/42nd_Street_%28Manhattan%29) (west–east), [Fifth Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_Avenue) (south–north); only *Dritter Südgürtel* (Third South Ring) in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz); *\1. Straße* (and so on) since 1966 – after a visit to New York – in [Kematen an der Ybbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kematen_an_der_Ybbs), Austria.

In Graz (A) exist further *Dreihackengasse* (three axes), *Dreierschützengasse* (3 shooters (?), after a historical military unit) and *Siebenundvierzigergasse* (47ers, a military unit). In the surrounding of [Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linz), Austria still exist some of the numbered towers, ancient ammunition / powder depots like *Zwanzigerturm* (tower #20).

In [Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klagenfurt) (and elsewhere) streetnames remember historical dates by day and month like *\10.-Oktober-Straße*.

## First Nations in Canada {#First_Nations_in_Canada}

*Image: Dunne-za\_cemetery\_at\_Eleske,\_Alberta.jpg.* Graveyard at [Child Lake 164A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_Lake_164A), Canada

In Canada it is customary to call indigenous communities, reserves and tribes, "[First Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Nations_in_Canada)" or *Premières Nations*, an element which is common in place names. It is also common to combined the names of reserves with numbers such as the [Long Lake 58 First Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Lake_58_First_Nation) and [Shoal Lake 40 First Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoal_Lake_40_First_Nation). In the [list of Indian reserves in Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indian_reserves_in_Saskatchewan), we can see over seven hundred place names, many of which have numeric and alphanumeric codes attached to them.
