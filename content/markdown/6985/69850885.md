# Victor Moll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Moll"}

**Victor Hugo Moll** (born 1956) is a Chilean American mathematician specializing in analysis.

:::

Moll studied at the [Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Técnica_Federico_Santa_María) and at the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) with a master's degree in 1982 and a doctorate in 1984 with [Henry P. McKean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_P._McKean) (*Stability in the Large for Solitary Wave Solutions to McKean's Nerve Conduction Caricature*).[^o6] He was a post-doctoral student at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) and became an assistant professor in 1986 and an associate professor in 1992 and in 2001 Professor at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University).

In 1990–1991, he was a visiting professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah), in 1999 at the [Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Técnica_Federico_Santa_María) in [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valparaíso), and in 1995 a visiting scientist at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of New York University.

He deals with classical analysis, symbolic arithmetic and [experimental mathematics](wikipedia://en/256363), special functions and number theory.

## Projects {#Projects}

Inspired by a 1988 paper in which Ilan Vardi proved several integrals in *[Table of Integrals, Series, and Products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_of_Integrals,_Series,_and_Products)*,[^o7] a well-known comprehensive table of integrals originally compiled by the Russian mathematicians [Iosif Moiseevich Ryzhik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iosif_Moiseevich_Ryzhik) (Russian: Иосиф Моисеевич Рыжик) and [Izrail Solomonovich Gradshteyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izrail_Solomonovich_Gradshteyn) (Израиль Соломонович Градштейн) in 1943 and subsequently expanded and translated into several languages, Victor Moll and [George Boros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Boros) started a project to prove all integrals listed in *Gradshteyn and Ryzhik* and add additional commentary and references.[^o8] In the foreword of the book *Irresistible Integrals* (2004), they wrote:[^o9] It took a short time to realize that this task was monumental. Nevertheless, the efforts have resulted in about 900 entries from *Gradshteyn and Ryzhik* discussed in a series of more than 30 articles of which papers 1 to 28 have been published in issues 14 to 26 of [Scientia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientia_%28UTFSM_journal%29), [Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Técnica_Federico_Santa_María) (UTFSM), between 2007 and 2015[^o10] and compiled into a two-volume book series *Special Integrals of Gradshteyn and Ryzhik: the Proofs* (2014–2015).[^o11][^o12] Moll also assisted [Daniel Zwillinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Zwillinger) editing the eighth English edition of [Gradshteyn and Ryzhik](wikipedia://en/49343041) in 2014.[^o13]

Moll also took on the task to revise and expand the classical landmark work "[A Course of Modern Analysis](wikipedia://en/2139612)" by [Whittaker and Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whittaker_and_Watson), which was originally published in 1902 and last revised in 1927, to publish a new edition in 2021.
