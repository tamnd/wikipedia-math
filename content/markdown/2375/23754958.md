# Lucio Lombardo-Radice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lucio Lombardo-Radice"}

**Lucio Lombardo-Radice** ([Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catania), 10 July 1916 – [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Brussels), 21 November 1982) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). A student of [Gaetano Scorza](wikipedia://en/35074517), Lombardo-Radice contributed to [finite geometry](wikipedia://en/267061) and [geometric combinatorics](wikipedia://en/20898181) together with [Guido Zappa](wikipedia://en/23748418) and [Beniamino Segre](wikipedia://en/3534031), and wrote important works concerning the [Non-Desarguesian plane](wikipedia://en/21092112). He was also a leading member of the [Italian Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Communist_Party) and a member of its central committee.[^o3] He had a long professional and political friendship with German natural scientist and dissident [Robert Havemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Havemann).[^o4]

:::

Lombardo-Radice's parents were [Giuseppe Lombardo Radice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Lombardo_Radice) and [Gemma Harasim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Harasim).[^o5] His children included the writer Marco Lombardo Radice and actor [Giovanni Lombardo Radice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Lombardo_Radice).[^o6][^o7]

The Istituto Tecnico Statale Commerciale "Lucio Lombardo Radice" per Programmatori, a school in [Rome, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome,_Italy), founded in 1982 as the XXV Istituto Tecnico Commerciale per Programmatori, was in 1992 renamed after Lombardo-Radice. It is now named Istituto di Istruzione Superiore Lombardo Radice[^o8]
