# David Kent Harrison

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Kent Harrison"}

**David Kent Harrison** (April 6, 1931, Massachusetts – December 21, 1999, [Barnstable, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnstable,_Massachusetts)) was an American mathematician, specializing in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), particularly [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and [valuation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_theory).

:::

He completed his PhD at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1957; his dissertation, titled *On torsion free abelian groups*, was written under the supervision of [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351).

Harrison was a faculty member from 1959 to 1963 at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania)[^o4] and from 1963 to 1993 at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), retiring there as professor emeritus in 1993.[^o5]

He developed a commutative [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) theory for [commutative algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_algebra_%28structure%29).[^o6] Along with his colleague [Marie A. Vitulli](wikipedia://en/56778095), he developed a unified valuation theory for [rings](wikipedia://en/48404) with [zero divisors](wikipedia://en/51441) that generalized both Krull and Archimedean valuations.[^o7]

He was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) for the academic year 1963–1964.[^o8] He supervised 28 doctoral students including [Joel Cunningham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Cunningham).[^o3] Ann Hill Harrison endowed the Harrison Memory Award for outstanding mathematical students at the University of Oregon.[^o5] He is survived by his son, composer and pianist [Michael Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Harrison_%28musician%29), a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) for the academic year 2018–2019,[^o9] and his daughter Jo Ellen Harrison.
