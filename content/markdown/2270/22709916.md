# Andries Brouwer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andries Brouwer"}

**Andries Evert Brouwer** (born 1951) is a Dutch mathematician and computer programmer, Professor Emeritus at [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) (TU/e). He is known as the creator of the greatly expanded 1984 to 1985 versions of the [roguelike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike) computer game *[Hack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hack_%28Unix_video_game%29)* that formed the basis for *[NetHack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NetHack)*.[^o3] He is also a [Linux kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux_kernel) [hacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacker_%28programmer_subculture%29). He is sometimes referred to by the handle *aeb*.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in Amsterdam, Brouwer attended the [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29), and obtained his MSc in mathematics at the University of Amsterdam in 1971. In 1976 he received his Ph.D. in mathematics from [Vrije Universiteit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit) with a thesis entitled "Treelike Spaces and Related Topological Spaces", under the supervision of Maarten Maurice and Pieter Baayen, both of whom were in turn students of [Johannes de Groot](wikipedia://en/6022862).[^o14] In 2004 he received an honorary doctorate from [Aalborg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_University).

After graduation Brouwer started his academic career at the Mathematisch Centrum, later [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063). From 1986 to 2012 he was Professor at [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) (TU/e).

## Work {#Work}

Brouwer's varied research interests include several branches of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), particularly [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [finite geometry](wikipedia://en/267061) and [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869).

He has published dozens of papers in graph theory and other areas of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), many of them in collaboration with other researchers. His co-authors include at least 9 of the co-authors of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), giving him an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) of 2.[^o15]

### Hack {#Hack}

In December 1984, while at the [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) (CWI), he made the first public release of [*Hack*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hack_%28Unix_video_game%29) on [Usenet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet). *Hack* was an implementation of *[Rogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_%28computer_game%29)* originally written in 1982 by [Jay Fenlason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Fenlason) and a few others, but Brouwer heavily modified and expanded it. He distributed a total of four versions of *Hack* between December 1984 and July 1985.

The [source code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_code) was released as [free software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_software), and it was widely copied, played, and [ported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porting) to multiple computer platforms. When Mike Stephenson brought together a large development team via Usenet to produce an enhanced version in 1987 incorporating changes from many of the *Hack* derivatives, they respected Brouwer's wishes by renaming their game *[NetHack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NetHack)*, as Brouwer might "...eventually release a new version of his own."[^o16]

### Linux kernel {#Linux_kernel}

Brouwer has also been involved with the development of [Unix-like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix-like) computer [operating systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system) based on the Linux kernel. He was previously the [maintainer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_maintainer) of the [man pager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_page) program `man`[^o17] and the maintainer of the Linux man-pages project (from 1995 to 2004),[^o20] and he is a kernel maintainer in the areas of disk geometry and [partition handling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_partitioning).[^o18]

Brouwer also serves as specialist in security aspects of Unix and Linux for EiPSI (Eindhoven Institute for the Protection of Systems and Information), TU/e's [information security](wikipedia://en/15036) research institute.[^o19]
