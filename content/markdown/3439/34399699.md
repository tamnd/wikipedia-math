# William Boone (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Boone"}

**William Werner Boone** (16 January 1920 in [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati) – 14 September 1983 in [Urbana, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbana,_Illinois)) was an American mathematician. He completed his undergrad degree as a part time student at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati).[^o3]

:::

[Alonzo Church](wikipedia://en/49731) was his Ph.D. advisor at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), and [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736) was his friend at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).

[Pyotr Novikov](wikipedia://en/1139033) showed in 1955 that there exists a [finitely presented group](wikipedia://en/99494#Finitely_presented_groups) *G* such that the [word problem](wikipedia://en/33563) for *G* is undecidable.[^o4] A different proof was obtained by Boone in a paper published in 1958.[^o5]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}
