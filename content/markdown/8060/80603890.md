# Galois Theory (Cox)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Galois Theory"}

**Galois Theory** is a 2004 mathematics textbook by [David A. Cox](wikipedia://en/41248158) for undergraduates, on [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316). A revised second edition was published in 2012.

:::
