# Anirban Pathak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anirban Pathak"}

**Anirban Pathak** is an Indian physicist, professor, and the Head of the Department of Physics and Materials Science & Engineering at the [Jaypee Institute of Information Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaypee_Institute_of_Information_Technology) (JIIT), Noida.[^o3] He has worked in quantum optics, quantum information, and quantum cryptography. He is a fellow of The [National Academy of Sciences, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India) (NASI),[^o21] the [Optical Society of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_Society_of_India) (OSI),[^o22] and the [Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution_of_Electronics_and_Telecommunication_Engineers) (IETE).[^o3] He is also the 2017 recipient of the [Om Prakash Bhasin Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Om_Prakash_Bhasin_Award) in the field of Electronics & Information Technology.[^o20]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Anirban Pathak received his Ph.D. in Physics from [Visva-Bharati University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visva-Bharati_University), Santiniketan. Following his doctoral studies, he was a post-doctoral research fellow at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin), Germany.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

He joined the [Jaypee Institute of Information Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaypee_Institute_of_Information_Technology), Noida, in 2002. He is currently a professor and the Head of the Department of Physics and Materials Science & Engineering.[^o3] He also heads the Centre for Quantum Science and Technology at JIIT.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

His research focuses on quantum optics, quantum information theory, quantum cryptography, and the applications of non-classical light. He has published over 170 research papers in peer-reviewed journals and has an h-index of 38 as of August 2024.[^o8]

### Academic community involvement {#Academic_community_involvement}

He serves as an Associate Editor for the journal *Quantum Information Processing* (Springer Nature).[^o16] He is also on the editorial board of *Quantum Review Letters* (KeAi Publishing).[^o17]

In a 2020 interview with *[Mint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mint_%28newspaper%29)*, he commented on the state of quantum research in India, highlighting the need for increased investment and focus to compete globally in the field.[^o18]
