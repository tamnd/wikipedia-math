# Institute of Statisticians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Statisticians"}

The **Institute of Statisticians** was a British [professional organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_organization) founded in 1948 to protect the interests of professional [statisticians](wikipedia://en/26685).[^o2] It was originally named *The Association of Incorporated Statisticians Limited*, but this was later changed.[^o3] The institute was formed after the [Royal Economic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Economic_Society) prevented a 1947 extension to the [royal charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_charter) of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) which would have allowed it to carry out examinations.[^o2]

:::

The Institute of Statisticians attempted to obtain a royal charter of its own in 1978 and this led to discussions with the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) about a possible merger. Talks foundered over disagreement on how to arrange the various membership categories. Within a few years, however, the talks were revisited, and on 1 January 1993, the two organisations merged, becoming simply the *Royal Statistical Society*.[^o2]

From 1950 the institute published a [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal), first called *The Incorporated Statistician* ([ISSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Standard_Serial_Number) 14669404) then renamed *The Statistician* (0039-0526) in 1962, before being subsumed into the *[Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143)* as *Series D* upon the merger in 1993.[^o4][^o5]
