# Decahedron

*Image: 10 sided die.svg.* A 10-sided [die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **decahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with ten [faces](wikipedia://en/149926). There are 32300 [topologically](wikipedia://en/29954) distinct decahedra,[^o2] and none are regular, so this name does not identify a specific type of polyhedron except for the number of faces.

:::

Some decahedra have regular faces:

- [Octagonal prism](wikipedia://en/3026569) (uniform 8-[prism](wikipedia://en/283007))
- [Square antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860045) (uniform 4-[antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851))
- [Square cupola](wikipedia://en/1128479) ([Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) 4)
- [Pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/728038) (Johnson solid 13, 5-[bipyramid](wikipedia://en/4153))
- [Augmented pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/1177974) (Johnson solid 52)
- [Augmented tridiminished icosahedron](wikipedia://en/1128627) (Johnson solid 64)

Decahedra with irregular faces include:

- [Pentagonal trapezohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_trapezohedron) (5-[trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/719845), antiprism dual) – often used as a [die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice) in [role playing games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role_playing_games), known as a **d10**
- [Truncated square trapezohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_square_trapezohedron)
- [Enneagonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneagon) pyramid (9-[pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045))
- [Ten of diamonds decahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_of_diamonds_decahedron) - a space-filling polyhedron with D<sub>2d</sub> symmetry
- [Chamfered tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/43468103)
- [Square bifrustum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_bifrustum)
