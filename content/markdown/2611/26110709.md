# B-admissible representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="B-admissible representation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the formalism of ***B*-admissible representations** provides constructions of [full](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_subcategory) [Tannakian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tannakian_category) [subcategories](wikipedia://en/310959) of the category of [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* on [finite-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite-dimensional) [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) over a given [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *E*. In this theory, *B* is chosen to be a so-called **(*E*, *G*)-regular ring**, i.e. an [*E*-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_ring) with an [*E*-linear action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_representation) of *G* satisfying certain conditions given below. This theory is most prominently used in [*p*-adic Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/26086853) to define important subcategories of [*p*-adic Galois representations](wikipedia://en/486929) of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of [local](wikipedia://en/145375) and [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358).

:::

## (*E*, *G*)-rings and the functor *D* {#(E,_G)-rings_and_the_functor_D}

Let *G* be a group and *E* a field. Let Rep(*G*) denote a non-trivial [strictly full subcategory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_full_subcategory) of the Tannakian category of *E*-linear representations of *G* on finite-dimensional vector spaces over *E* stable under [subobjects](wikipedia://en/540476), [quotient objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_object), [direct sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_sum_of_representations), [tensor products](wikipedia://en/30844), and [duals](wikipedia://en/1442471).[^o3]

An **(*E*, *G*)-ring** is a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) *B* that is an *E*-algebra with an *E*-linear action of *G*. Let *F* = *B<sup>G</sup>* be the [*G*-invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-invariant) of *B*. The [covariant functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariant_functor) *D<sub>B</sub>* : Rep(*G*) → Mod<sub>*F*</sub> defined by

$$D_B(V):=(B\otimes_EV)^G$$

is *E*-linear (Mod<sub>*F*</sub> denotes the category of [*F*-modules](wikipedia://en/276410)). The inclusion of *D<sub>B</sub>*(V) in *B* ⊗*<sub>E</sub>V* induces a homomorphism

$$\alpha_{B,V}:B\otimes_FD_B(V)\longrightarrow B\otimes_EV$$

called the **comparison morphism**.[^o4]

## Regular (*E*, *G*)-rings and *B*-admissible representations {#Regular_(E,_G)-rings_and_B-admissible_representations}

An (*E*, *G*)-ring *B* is called **regular** if

1. *B* is [reduced](wikipedia://en/539749);
1. for every *V* in Rep(*G*), α<sub>*B,V*</sub> is [injective](wikipedia://en/45196);
1. every *b* ∈ *B* for which the line *bE* is *G*-stable is [invertible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invertible_element) in *B*.

The third condition implies *F* is a field. If *B* is a field, it is automatically regular.

When *B* is regular,

$$\dim_FD_B(V)\leq\dim_EV$$

with equality if, and only if, α<sub>*B,V*</sub> is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828).

A representation *V* ∈ Rep(*G*) is called ***B*-admissible** if α<sub>*B,V*</sub> is an isomorphism. The full subcategory of *B*-admissible representations, denoted Rep<sub>*B*</sub>(*G*), is Tannakian.

If *B* has extra structure, such as a [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) or an *E*-linear [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569), then *D<sub>B</sub>*(*V*) inherits this structure and the functor *D<sub>B</sub>* can be viewed as taking values in the corresponding category.

## Examples {#Examples}

- Let *K* be a field of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) *p* (a prime), and *K<sub>s</sub>* a [separable closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_closure) of *K*. If *E* = **F**<sub>*p*</sub> (the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with *p* elements) and *G* = Gal(*K<sub>s</sub>*/*K*) (the absolute Galois group of *K*), then *B* = *K<sub>s</sub>* is a regular (*E*, *G*)-ring. On *K<sub>s</sub>* there is an injective [Frobenius endomorphism](wikipedia://en/1088270) σ : *K<sub>s</sub>* → *K<sub>s</sub>* sending *x* to *x<sup>p</sup>*. Given a representation *G* → GL(*V*) for some finite-dimensional **F**<sub>*p*</sub>-vector space *V*, $D=D_{K_s}(V)$ is a finite-dimensional vector space over *F*=(*K*<sub>*s*</sub>)<sup>*G*</sup> = *K* which inherits from *B* = *K<sub>s</sub>* an injective function φ<sub>*D*</sub> : *D* → *D* which is σ-semilinear (i.e. φ(*ad*) = σ(*a*)φ(*d*) for all a ∈ *K* and all d ∈ *D*). The *K<sub>s</sub>*-admissible representations are the continuous ones (where *G* has the [Krull topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krull_topology) and *V* has the [discrete topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_topology)). In fact, $D_{K_s}$ is an [equivalence of categories](wikipedia://en/379051) between the *K<sub>s</sub>*-admissible representations (i.e. continuous ones) and the finite-dimensional vector spaces over *K* equipped with an injective σ-semilinear φ.

:::

## Potentially *B*-admissible representations {#Potentially_B-admissible_representations}

A **potentially *B*-admissible representation** captures the idea of a representation that becomes *B*-admissible when [restricted](wikipedia://en/1589032) to some [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of *G*.
