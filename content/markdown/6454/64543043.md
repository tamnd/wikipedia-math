# Martina Zähle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martina Zähle"}

**Martina Zähle** (born 1950) is a German mathematician specializing in [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694) and [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena), where she holds the chair for geometry.

:::

Zähle completed her doctorate in 1978 at the University of Jena. Her dissertation, *Ergodizitätseigenschaften von Strömungsmodellen mit Anwendungen in der Bedienungstheorie*, was supervised by Joseph Mecke. She earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at Jena in 1982.

She is the coauthor of the book *Curvature Measures of Singular Sets* (with Jan Rataj, Springer, 2019).
