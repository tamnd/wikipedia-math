# Conditional change model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conditional change model"}

The **conditional change model** in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) is the analytic procedure in which change scores are [regressed](wikipedia://en/48758386) on baseline values, together with the [explanatory variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explanatory_variable) of interest (often including indicators of [treatment groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treatment_groups)). The method has some substantial advantages over the usual [two-sample t-test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-sample_t-test) recommended in [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook).

:::
