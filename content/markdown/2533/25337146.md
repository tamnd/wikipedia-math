# Luigi Ambrosio

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Luigi Ambrosio"}

**Luigi Ambrosio** (born 27 January 1963) is a professor at [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in [Pisa, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa,_Italy). His main fields of research are the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ambrosio entered the Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa in 1981. He obtained his degree under the guidance of [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401) in 1985 at [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), and the Diploma at Scuola Normale. He obtained his PhD in 1988.

He is currently professor at the Scuola Normale, having taught previously at the University of Rome "Tor Vergata", the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), and the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia). Ambrosio also taught and conducted research at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), the [ETH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH) in Zurich, and the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in Leipzig.

He is the Managing Editor of the scientific journal *[Calculus of Variations and Partial Differential Equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_Variations_and_Partial_Differential_Equations)*, and member of the editorial boards of scientific journals.

Since May 9, 2019 Ambrosio is the director of the Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa.[^o5]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1998 Ambrosio won the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) of the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092).[^o7] In 2002 he was [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Beijing and in 2003 he has been awarded with the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195). From 2005 he is a corresponding member of Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Ambrosio is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o8] In 2018 he was a [plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plenary_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Rio de Janeiro. In 2019 he received the [Balzan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Balzan_Prize_recipients) in Mathematics (Theory of Partial Differential Equations).[^o9] In 2024 he was awarded the [Nemmers Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1252473).[^o10]
