# Sergey Bolotin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergey Bolotin"}

*Image: Болотин СВ.jpg.*

**Sergey Vladimirovich Bolotin** (Сергей Владимирович Болотин, born 1 December 1954 in Moscow) is a Russian mathematician, specializing in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) of [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bolotin graduated in 1976 from the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). There he received in 1981 his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree (PhD)[^o4] with thesis Либрационные движения обратимых механических систем ([Librational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libration) motions of reversible mechanical systems). He received in 1998[^o2] his Russian [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) degree (habilitation) with thesis Двоякоасимптотические траектории и условия интегрируемости гамильтоновых систем[^o5] (Double-asymptotic trajectories and [integrability](wikipedia://en/2573213) conditions for [Hamiltonian systems](wikipedia://en/198319)).

Since 1998 Bolotin is a professor in the Department of Theoretical Mechanics, Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Moscow State University.[^o4] He is now the head of the Mechanics Department of the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494).[^o6] His research deals with dynamical systems of classical mechanics, Hamiltonian systems, and [variational methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_methods).[^o2] He has supervised four PhD (Candidate of Sciences) students. He is the author or coauthor of over 75 scientific publications, including a textbook on [theoretical mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_mechanics) (2010).[^o4] He has served on the editorial board of the journal *Regular and Chaotic Dynamics*.

In 1994 he was an invited speaker with talk *Invariant Sets of Hamiltonian Systems and Variational Methods* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.[^o7] In 2016 he was elected a corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]

As a hobby, Bolotin sails in Olympic class [Finn dinghies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_%28dinghy%29).[^o8]

His brother Yuri Vladimirovich Bolotin (born December 1, 1954) is a professor at Moscow State University.[^o9] Both brothers in 2020 became champions of Russia in the class of yachts "Carter 30".[^o10]
