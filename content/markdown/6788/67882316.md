# John Rognes (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Rognes"}

*Image: RognesJohn2005 MFO7554.jpg.* Rognes at the workshop *Modern foundations for stable [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831)*, [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) 2005

**John Rognes** (born 28 April 1966 in Oslo) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a professor at the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo).

:::

Rognes mathematical talent was visible from a young age; in 1984 he won a bronze medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in Prague.[^o2]

As a 19-year-old, he received his master's degree. He then went to Princeton University, where he received a PhD as a 24-year-old with the dissertation "The Rank Filtration in Algebraic K-theory".[^o3]

In 1994, Rognes became an associate professor at Oslo, and in 1998 he was promoted to the rank of professor. He became Norway's youngest professor of mathematics. Rognes' research is about topological mathematics and [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500). In 2004, he became one of the 26 who received a five-year scholarship as a Young Outstanding Researcher from the Research Council of Norway. From 2010 to 2014 was the editor of [Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708). In 2014, he was invited to speak at the International Congress of Mathematicians (ICM) in Seoul.[^o4] From 2014 to 2018, he was chair of the [Abel Committee](wikipedia://en/159285).[^o5]
