# Martha M. Gardner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martha M. Gardner"}

**Martha M. Gardner** is an American statistician associated with [GE Global Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GE_Global_Research), and the former chair of the Quality & Productivity Section of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).

:::

As an undergraduate at the [University of Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alabama), Gardner majored in both mathematics and classical languages. She liked classics better than mathematics, but was advised by a classics professor that her job prospects would be much better in mathematics, and that she should look harder for a branch of mathematics that she enjoyed. After earning a master's degree in statistics at Alabama, focused on [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), she moved to [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University) (NCSU) for her doctoral studies. There, she built connections with [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) that she carried over to her work with [General Electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Electric), where she has also worked on projects in [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering), aircraft engine design, and [quality management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_management).

In 2011, the NCSU College of Physical and Mathematical Sciences gave Gardner their Medal of Achievement. In 2014, the American Statistical Association honored her by electing her as one of their [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).
