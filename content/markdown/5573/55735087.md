# Jacopo Silvestri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacopo Silvestri"}

*Image: Silvestri\_-\_Opus\_novum\_praefectis\_arcium\_-\_4701899.tif.* *Opus novum praefectis arcium*

**Jacopo Silvestri** was an Italian [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432) and author active in the early 16th century.[^o2][^o3]

:::

He was a citizen of the [Republic of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Florence).[^o4] Little is known about his life; his Opus novum "New work" provides some information about him and his work, and is considered the second printed work about [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).[^o3][^o5]
