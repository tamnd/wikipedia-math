# Anton Deusing

*Image: Antonius\_Deusing.\_Line\_engraving\_by\_C.\_de\_Passe,\_junior,\_165\_Wellcome\_V0001566.jpg.* Antonius Deusing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anton Deusing"}

**Anton Deusing**, in Latin **Antonius Deusingius** (October 15, 1612 – January 30, 1666) was a German [physician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physician), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Deusing\_-\_De\_vero\_systemate\_mundi\_dissertatio\_mathematica,\_1643\_-\_178360.jpg.* *De vero systemate mundi dissertatio mathematica*, 1643

Born in [Moers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moers) in 1612, Deusing was an educated German physician, scholar of [Oriental language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oriental_language), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy). Between 1630 and 1637 he was enrolled at the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leiden), where he became a protégé of [Jacobus Golius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobus_Golius), the famous professor of Oriental Languages and Mathematics. In 1646 he became the first professor of [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine) at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen). He wrote and published several works in [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin). He died in [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen) in 1666.
