# Highest-weight category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Highest-weight category"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **highest-weight category** is a [*k*-linear category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_category) **C** (here *k* is a [field](wikipedia://en/10603)) that

:::

- is *locally artinian*[^o2]
- has [enough injectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enough_injectives)
- satisfies

$$B\cap\left(\bigcup_\alpha A_\alpha\right)=\bigcup_\alpha\left(B\cap A_\alpha\right)$$

for all subobjects *B* and each family of subobjects {*A*<sub>α</sub>} of each object *X*

and such that there is a [locally finite poset](wikipedia://en/19357321) Λ (whose elements are called the **weights** of **C**) that satisfies the following conditions:[^o3]

- The poset Λ indexes an exhaustive set of non-isomorphic [simple objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_object) {*S*(*λ*)} in **C**.
- Λ also indexes a collection of objects {*A*(*λ*)} of objects of **C** such that there exist embeddings *S*(*λ*) → *A*(*λ*) such that all [composition factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composition_factor) *S*(*μ*) of *A*(*λ*)/*S*(*λ*) satisfy *μ* \< *λ*.[^o4]
- For all *μ*, *λ* in Λ,

$$\dim_k\operatorname{Hom}_k(A(\lambda),A(\mu))$$

is finite, and the [multiplicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composition_factor)[^o5]

$$[A(\lambda):S(\mu)]$$

is also finite.

- Each *S*(*λ*) has an [injective envelope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_envelope) *I*(*λ*) in **C** equipped with an increasing [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014)

$$0=F_0(\lambda)\subseteq F_1(\lambda)\subseteq\dots\subseteq I(\lambda)$$

such that

  1. $$F_1(\lambda)=A(\lambda)$$
  1. for *n* > 1, $F_n(\lambda)/F_{n-1}(\lambda)\cong A(\mu)$ for some *μ* = *λ*(*n*) > *λ*
  1. for each *μ* in Λ, *λ*(*n*) = *μ* for only finitely many *n*
  1. $$\bigcup_iF_i(\lambda)=I(\lambda).$$

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The module category of the $k$-algebra of upper triangular $n\times n$ matrices over $k$.
- This concept is named after the category of [highest-weight modules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest-weight_module) of Lie-algebras.
- A finite-dimensional $k$-algebra $A$ is [quasi-hereditary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-hereditary) iff its module category is a highest-weight category. In particular all module-categories over [semisimple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_ring) and [hereditary](wikipedia://en/5123176) algebras are highest-weight categories.
- A [cellular algebra](wikipedia://en/31504702) over a field is quasi-hereditary (and hence its module category a highest-weight category) [iff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iff) its Cartan-determinant is 1.

:::
