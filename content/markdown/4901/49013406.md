# Hans-Bjørn Foxby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans-Bjørn Foxby"}

**Hans-Bjørn Foxby** (1947 – 2014) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and a professor of mathematics at [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). [Foxby classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxby_class) are named after him.[^o2]

:::

Foxby's research was in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He died from [Alzheimer's disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzheimer's_disease) on 8 April 2014.[^o3]
