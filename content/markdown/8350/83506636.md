# Farrukh Mukhamedov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Farrukh Mukhamedov"}

**Farrukh Mukhamedov** is an Uzbek mathematician[^o3][^o4] and professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [United Arab Emirates University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_University).[^o4]

:::

In 2005, he received [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) (TWAS) prize for Young Scientists, and in 2022, he was elected a fellow member of TWAS in the mathematical sciences section[^o5] and is a corresponding fellow of [International Core Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Core_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Mukhamedov obtained an MSc in pure mathematics with distinction from the [National University of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Uzbekistan) and completed his PhD in 1998.[^o5][^o7]

In 2005, he defended a [DSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (Habilitation) degree, at the National University of Uzbekistan.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Mukhamedov worked as a graduate student at the Institute of Mathematics of the Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan,[^o7][^o9] a junior scientific fellow at the same institute, a postdoctoral researcher at the [National University of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Uzbekistan), an associate professor there, and a senior scientific fellow.[^o9]

In 2005, Mukhamedov held a postdoctoral position at the [University of Aveiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aveiro) in Portugal.[^o5][^o10] He subsequently joined the Faculty of Science at [International Islamic University Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Islamic_University_Malaysia), where he was promoted from associate professor to professor.[^o5] From 2009 to 2015, Mukhamedov was a Junior Associate of the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) (ICTP).[^o5] In 2016, he joined the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the [United Arab Emirates University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_University) as a professor.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Mukhamedov's research has contributed to several areas of pure mathematics, including quantum probability, [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), dynamical systems, and [p-adic analysis](wikipedia://en/291327). In quantum probability, he applied methods from [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) to establish the existence of [phase transitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_transitions) in [quantum Markov chains](wikipedia://en/7941780) and disproved a conjecture concerning the occurrence of type III₁ factors in systems with non-trivial Hamiltonian interactions. He developed the theory of quantum quadratic operators and related stochastic processes. In p-adic mathematics, Mukhamedov established results in p-adic statistical mechanics, including the study of phase transitions, and contributed to the development of p-adic dynamical systems by proving the existence of chaotic behavior in p-adic [Potts models](wikipedia://en/1162226).[^o5]

## Editorial activities {#Editorial_activities}

Mukhamedov has served on editorial boards of *p-Adic Numbers, Ultrametric Analysis and Applications*,[^o13] *Open Journal of Mathematical Analysis*,[^o14] *[Open Systems & Information Dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Systems_&_Information_Dynamics)*,[^o15] and the *Uzbek Mathematical Journal*.[^o16]

## Honours and fellowship {#Honours_and_fellowship}

In 1999, Mukhamedov received the Mathematics Prize of the Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Uzbekistan.[^o18] In 2005, he received the TWAS Prize for Young Scientists in Developing Countries.[^o5]

Mukhamedov is a corresponding fellow of the [International Core Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Core_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) (CORE Academy).[^o5] In 2022, He was elected a fellow of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) in the mathematical sciences section.[^o3] He is also a fellow of the Industry Academy of the International Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance.[^o19]
