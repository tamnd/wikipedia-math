# Matematicheskii Sbornik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matematicheskii Sbornik"}

***Matematicheskii Sbornik*** (Математический сборник, abbreviated *Mat. Sb.*) is a [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) Russian mathematical journal founded by the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251) in 1866.[^o3] It is the oldest successful Russian mathematical journal.[^o4] The English translation is *Sbornik: Mathematics*. It is also sometimes cited under the alternative name *Izdavaemyi Moskovskim Matematicheskim Obshchestvom* or its French translation *Recueil mathématique de la Société mathématique de Moscou*,[^o5] but the name *Recueil mathématique* is also used for an unrelated journal, *[Mathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathesis_%28journal%29)*. Yet another name, *Sovetskii Matematiceskii Sbornik*, was listed in a statement in the journal in 1931 apologizing for the former editorship of [Dmitri Egorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Egorov), who had been recently discredited for his religious views; however, this name was never actually used by the journal.[^o6]

:::

The first editor of the journal was [Nikolai Brashman](wikipedia://en/1005204), who died before its first issue (dedicated to his memory) was published. Its current editor-in-chief is [Boris Kashin](wikipedia://en/56931072).[^o8]

The journal is indexed in [Russian Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Science_Citation_Index).[^o9]

## Selected articles {#Selected_articles}

Notable articles published in *Matematicheskii Sbornik* have included:

- Соболев, С. (1938). *Mat. Sb.*. 4. p. 471–497. <http://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?wshow=paper&jrnid=sm&paperid=5759&option_lang=eng>. Translated in *Transl. Amer. Math. Soc.* 34 (2): 39–68, 1963. This paper by [Sergei Sobolev](wikipedia://en/380798) introduced [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964) and [Sobolev inequalities](wikipedia://en/4665038). In 2009, Laurent Saloff-Coste wrote that "there are few articles that have turned out to be as influential and important."[^o12]

## English translation {#English_translation}

From 1967 to 1993 (volumes 1–74) the English version was titled *Mathematics of the USSR. Sbornik* (ISSN 0025-5734).

Since 1993 (volumes 75–) it has been titled *Sbornik. Mathematics* and abbreviated *Sb. Math.* (ISSN 1064-5616).

From 1995 the translation has been published jointly by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), Turpion Ltd,[^o14] and the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). The volume numbering was also changed and now follows the Russian original journal *Matematicheskii Sbornik*.

## Impact {#Impact}

As of 2018, the [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of *Sb. Math.* in [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports) is 1.057.[^o16] Alternatively, mathnet.ru calculates its impact factor for 2018 as 1.089.[^o8]
