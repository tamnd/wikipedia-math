# Dimiter Skordev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dimiter Skordev"}

*Image: Dimiter-Skordev.jpg.* Dimiter Skordev

**Dimiter Skordev** (Димитър Скордев) (born 1936 in [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia)) is a professor in the Department of Mathematical Logic and Applications, Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science at the [University of Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sofia). Chairman of the department in 1972-2000. Doyen and pioneer of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) research in [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria) who developed a Bulgarian school in the theory of computability, namely the algebraic (or axiomatic) recursion theory. He was the 1981 winner of *Acad. Nikola Obreshkov Prize*, the highest Bulgarian award in mathematics, bestowed for his monograph *Combinatory Spaces and Recursiveness in Them*.[^o2]

:::

Skordev's field of scientific interests include [computability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory) and [complexity](wikipedia://en/7543) in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), generalized [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory), and theory of [programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programs) and [computation](wikipedia://en/30402).

Skordev has more than 45 years of lecturing experience in [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [logic programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_programming), [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science). He has authored about 90 scientific publications including two monographs, and was one of the authors of the new Bulgarian [phonetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonetics) [keyboard layout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keyboard_layout#Bulgarian) proposed (but rejected) to become a state [standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardization) in 2006.
