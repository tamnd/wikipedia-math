# Allen Hatcher

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Allen Hatcher"}

**Allen Edward Hatcher** (born October 23, 1944) is an American mathematician specializing in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hatcher was born in [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis), [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana).[^o5] After obtaining his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) and [B.Mus.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Music) from [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) in 1966,[^o4] he went for his graduate studies to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1971.[^o5] His thesis, *A K<sub>2</sub> Obstruction for Pseudo-Isotopies*, was written under the supervision of [Hans Samelson](wikipedia://en/17290837).

Afterwards, Hatcher went to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where he was an [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) postdoc for a year, then a lecturer for another year, and then Assistant Professor from 1973 to 1979. He was also a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1975–76 and 1979–80.[^o5] Hatcher moved to the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) as an assistant professor in 1977. From 1983 he has been a professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University); he is now a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus).[^o7]

In 1978 Hatcher was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Helsinki.[^o8]

In 1983 Hatcher proved the [Smale conjecture](wikipedia://en/34785639), named after [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294).[^o9][^o10]
