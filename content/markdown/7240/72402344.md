# Catherine Searle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catherine Searle"}

**Catherine Searle** is an American mathematician specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and in particular on the [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) and symmetry of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) and [Alexandrov spaces](wikipedia://en/14609741). She is a professor of mathematics at [Wichita State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wichita_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Searle majored in mathematics and physics at [Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College), graduating in 1984. She went to the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) for graduate study in mathematics, completing her Ph.D. in 1992. Her dissertation, *Manifolds of Positive Curvature with Large Symmetry Groups*, was supervised by [Karsten Grove](wikipedia://en/49517887).

For approximately the next 20 years she worked as a researcher in Mexico, initially at [CINVESTAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CINVESTAV) from 1992 to 1998, and then at the Cuernavaca unit of the Institute of Mathematics of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) from 1996 to 2011. She was also affiliated with the National System of Researchers beginning in 1993.

In 2012 she returned to the US as a visiting professor at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University). She became an assistant professor at [Wichita State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wichita_State_University) in 2014, earned tenure there as an associate professor in 2017, and was promoted to full professor in 2019.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Searle was elected to the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2010.
