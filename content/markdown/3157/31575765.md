# Haynsworth inertia additivity formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Haynsworth inertia additivity formula"}

In mathematics, the **Haynsworth inertia additivity formula**, discovered by [Emilie Virginia Haynsworth](wikipedia://en/61816794) (1916–1985), concerns the number of positive, negative, and zero [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of a [Hermitian matrix](wikipedia://en/189682) and of [block matrices into which it is partitioned](wikipedia://en/457464).[^o2]

:::

The *inertia* of a Hermitian matrix *H* is defined as the ordered triple

$$\mathrm{In}(H) = \left( \pi(H), \nu(H), \delta(H) \right)$$

whose components are respectively the numbers of positive, negative, and zero eigenvalues of *H*. Haynsworth considered a partitioned Hermitian matrix

$$H = \begin{bmatrix} H_{11} & H_{12} \\ H_{12}^\ast & H_{22} \end{bmatrix}$$

where *H*<sub>11</sub> is [nonsingular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonsingular_matrix) and *H*<sub>12</sub><sup>\*</sup> is the [conjugate transpose](wikipedia://en/217548) of *H*<sub>12</sub>. The formula states:[^o3][^o4]

$$\mathrm{In} \begin{bmatrix} H_{11} & H_{12} \\ H_{12}^\ast & H_{22} \end{bmatrix} = \mathrm{In}(H_{11}) + \mathrm{In}(H/H_{11})$$

where *H*/*H*<sub>11</sub> is the [Schur complement](wikipedia://en/372620) of *H*<sub>11</sub> in *H*:

$$H/H_{11} = H_{22} - H_{12}^\ast H_{11}^{-1}H_{12}.$$

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalization"}

## Generalization {#Generalization}

If *H*<sub>11</sub> is [singular](wikipedia://en/245528), we can still define the generalized Schur complement, using the [Moore–Penrose inverse](wikipedia://en/331258) $H_{11}^+$ instead of $H_{11}^{-1}$.

The formula does not hold if *H*<sub>11</sub> is singular. However, a generalization has been proven in 1974 by Carlson, Haynsworth and Markham,[^o7] to the effect that $\pi(H) \ge \pi(H_{11}) + \pi(H/H_{11})$ and $\nu(H) \ge \nu(H_{11}) + \nu(H/H_{11})$.

Carlson, Haynsworth and Markham also gave sufficient and necessary conditions for equality to hold.

:::
