# AB5 category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AB5 category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), (txt) in his "[Tôhoku paper](wikipedia://en/44572732)" introduced a sequence of [axioms](wikipedia://en/928) of various kinds of [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) over the [symmetric monoidal category](wikipedia://en/3865249) of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974). [Abelian categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_categories) are sometimes called **AB2 categories**, according to the axiom (AB2). **AB3 categories** are [abelian categories](wikipedia://en/45063) possessing arbitrary [coproducts](wikipedia://en/310889) (hence, by the existence of quotients in abelian categories, also all [colimits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colimit)). **AB5 categories** are the AB3 categories in which [filtered colimits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filtered_colimit) of [exact sequences](wikipedia://en/138215) are exact. [Grothendieck categories](wikipedia://en/30051538) are the AB5 categories with a [generator](wikipedia://en/9550415).
