# Alexander John Thompson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander John Thompson"}

**Alexander John Thompson** (1885 in [Plaistow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaistow,_Newham), Essex – 17 June 1968 in [Wallington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallington,_London), Surrey) is the author of the last great table of logarithms, published in 1952. This table, the [Logarithmetica britannica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmetica_britannica) gives the [logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860) of all numbers from 1 to 100000 to 20 places and supersedes all previous tables of similar scope, in particular the tables of [Henry Briggs](wikipedia://en/210399), [Adriaan Vlacq](wikipedia://en/4292863) and [Gaspard de Prony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaspard_de_Prony).[^o2]

:::

  - Alexander John Thompson: Logarithmetica britannica, being a standard table of logarithms to twenty decimal places. Part IV, Numbers 40,000 to 50,000, 1928, Cambridge: University Press

  - Alexander John Thompson: Logarithmetica britannica, being a standard table of logarithms to twenty decimal places. Part VIII, Numbers 80,000 to 90,000, 1927, Cambridge: University Press
  - Alexander John Thompson: Logarithmetica britannica, being a standard table of logarithms to twenty decimal places. Part IX, Numbers 90,000 to 100,000, 1924, Cambridge: University Press
