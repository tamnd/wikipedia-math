# Rocket City Math League

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rocket City Math League"}

**Rocket City Math League (RCML)** is a student-run [mathematics competition](wikipedia://en/19710) in the United States. Run by students at [Virgil I. Grissom High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgil_I._Grissom_High_School) in [Huntsville, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsville,_Alabama), RCML gets its name from Huntsville's nickname as the "Rocket City".[^o2] RCML was started in 2001 and has been annually sponsored by the [Mu Alpha Theta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Alpha_Theta) Math Honor Society. The competition consists of three individual rounds and a team round that was added in 2008. It is divided into five divisions named for [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) programs: [Explorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explorer) (pre-algebra), [Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Mercury) (algebra I), [Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Gemini) (geometry), [Apollo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_program) (algebra II), and [Discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Discovery) (comprehensive).

:::

## Individual rounds {#Individual_rounds}

Each of the 3 individual rounds consists of a 10 question test with a 30-minute time limit. Out of the 10 questions, there are four 1-point questions, three 2-point questions, two 3-point questions, and one 4-point question, with the more difficult questions having larger point values. The maximum score on an individual test is 20, and individual tests often contain many interesting space-themed questions.

## Team round {#Team_round}

The team round is divided into a senior division and a junior division that take separate tests for the team round. It consists of a 15 question test with a 30-minute time limit, in which team members work together to get as many correct answers as possible. Out of the 15 questions, there are five 1-point questions, four 2-point questions, three 3-point questions, two 4-point questions, and one 5-point question, making the maximum score on the team test a 35.
