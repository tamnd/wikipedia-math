# Richard Townsend (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Townsend"}

**Richard Townsend** (April 1821 – October 1884) was an Irish mathematician, author, and academic.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Townsend was born in [Baltimore, County Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore,_County_Cork) and was educated at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (BA 1842, MA 1852). He was appointed [Professor of Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin_Chair_of_Natural_Philosophy) there in 1870 and altogether spent four decades on the staff at TCD.[^o3] His book on geometry was highly regarded.[^o4] He wrote many articles for the *[Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_and_Dublin_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o3]

## Books {#Books}

- *Chapters on the Modern Geometry of the Point, Line, and Circle (Vol. I)* (Hodges, Smith, and Co, 1863)
