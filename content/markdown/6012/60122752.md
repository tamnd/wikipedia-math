::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="33344-33434 tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of the Euclidean plane, a **33344-33434 tiling** is one of two of 20 [2-uniform tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410) [by regular polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiling_by_regular_polygons). They contains regular [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) and [square](wikipedia://en/659939) faces, arranged in two [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745): 3.3.3.4.4 and 3.3.4.3.4.[^o6]

:::

The first has triangles in groups of 3 and square in groups of 1 and 2. It has 4 types of faces and 5 types of edges.

The second has triangles in groups of 4, and squares in groups of 2. It has 3 types of face and 6 types of edges.

## Circle Packings {#Circle_Packings}

These 2-uniform tilings can be used as a [circle packings](wikipedia://en/16879665).

In the first 2-uniform tiling (whose dual resembles a key-lock pattern): cyan circles are in contact with 5 other circles (**3** cyan, **2** pink), corresponding to the V3<sup>3</sup>.4<sup>2</sup> planigon, and pink circles are also in contact with 5 other circles (**4** cyan, **1** pink), corresponding to the V3<sup>2</sup>.4.3.4 planigon. It is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the ambo operation on the tiling, with the cyan and pink gap polygons corresponding to the cyan and pink circles (mini-vertex configuration polygons; one dimensional duals to the respective planigons). Both images coincide.

In the second 2-uniform tiling (whose dual resembles jagged streams of water): cyan circles are in contact with 5 other circles (**2** cyan, **3** pink), corresponding to the V3<sup>3</sup>.4<sup>2</sup> planigon, and pink circles are also in contact with 5 other circles (**3** cyan, **2** pink), corresponding to the V3<sup>2</sup>.4.3.4 planigon. It is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the ambo operation on the tiling, with the cyan and pink gap polygons corresponding to the cyan and pink circles (mini-vertex configuration polygons; one dimensional duals to the respective planigons). Both images coincide.

### Dual tilings {#Dual_tilings}
