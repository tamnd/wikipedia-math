# Ludwig Maurer

*Image: Ludwig Maurer.jpg.* Ludwig Maurer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ludwig Maurer"}

**Ludwig Maurer** (11 December 1859 – 10 January 1927) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen). He was the eldest son of [Konrad Maurer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konrad_Maurer) (1823–1902) and Valerie Maurer, née von Faulhaber (1833–1912). His 1887 dissertation at the [University of Strassburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strassburg) was on the theory of linear substitutions, known today as [matrix groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_group). A survey of his important contributions is contained in chapter V, §4 of (Borel 2001).
