# Maryam Mirzakhani Prize in Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maryam Mirzakhani Prize in Mathematics"}

The **Maryam Mirzakhani Prize in Mathematics** (ex-**NAS Award in Mathematics** until 2012) is awarded by the U.S. [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) "for excellence of research in the mathematical sciences published within the past ten years."

:::

The original prize was for \$5,000 and was awarded every four years; this was suspended after 2012.[^o2]

In 2018, the prize was renamed after the [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran) mathematician [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655); the prize money was increased to \$20,000 and was to be awarded every two years.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

## Award winners {#Award_winners}

Source:[^o6]

- 2026: [Roman Bezrukavnikov](wikipedia://en/24842390) "for varied and profound contributions to [geometric representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200)."<https://www.nasonline.org/award/maryam-mirzakhani-prize-in-mathematics/>
- 2024: [Sylvia Serfaty](wikipedia://en/28587610) "for outstanding contributions to nonlinear partial differential equations and statistical physics."[^o3][^o7]
- 2022: [Camillo De Lellis](wikipedia://en/39351243) "for his fundamental contributions to the study of dissipative solutions to the incompressible Euler equations and to the regularity theory of minimal surfaces."[^o8]
- 2020: [Larry Guth](wikipedia://en/31875947) "for developing surprising, original, and deep connections between geometry, analysis, topology, and combinatorics, which have led to the solution of, or major advances on, many outstanding problems in these fields."[^o9]
- 2012: [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569) "For his leading role in the development of homotopy theory, which has both reinvigorated algebraic topology as a central field in mathematics and led to the resolution of the Kervaire invariant problem for framed manifolds."[^o4][^o10]
- 2008: [Clifford H. Taubes](wikipedia://en/6893920) "For groundbreaking work relating to Seiberg-Witten and Gromov-Witten invariants of symplectic 4-manifolds, and his proof of Weinstein conjecture for all contact 3-manifolds."[^o4][^o11]
- 2004: [Dan-Virgil Voiculescu](wikipedia://en/7656821) "For the theory of free probability, in particular, using random matrices and a new concept of entropy to solve several hitherto intractable problems in von Neumann algebras."[^o4][^o12]
- 2000: [Ingrid Daubechies](wikipedia://en/1430989) "For fundamental discoveries on wavelets and wavelet expansions and for her role in making wavelet methods a practical basic tool of applied mathematics."[^o4][^o13]
- 1996: [Andrew J. Wiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_J._Wiles) "For his proof of Fermat's Last Theorem by discovering a beautiful strategy to establish a major portion of the Shimura-Taniyama conjecture, and for his courage and technical power in bringing his idea to completion."[^o4][^o14]
- 1992: [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121) "For his role in the introduction and application of radically new approaches to the topology of singular spaces, including characteristics classes, intersection homology, perverse sheaves, and stratified Morse theory."[^o4]
- 1988: [Robert P. Langlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_P._Langlands) "For his extraordinary vision, which has brought the theory of group representations into a revolutionary new relationship with the theory of automorphic forms and number theory."[^o4]
