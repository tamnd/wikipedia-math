# Suslin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suslin"}

**Suslin** or **Souslin** (Russian: Суслин) is a Russian masculine surname; its feminine counterpart is **Suslina** or **Souslina**. It may refer to:

:::

- [Alexander Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Suslin) (died 1349), German Orthodox rabbi and Talmudist
- [Andrei Suslin](wikipedia://en/7111820) (1950–2018), Russian mathematician known for
  - [Suslin homology](wikipedia://en/41890595)
  - [Quillen–Suslin theorem](wikipedia://en/539562)
- [Galina Yermolayeva (rower)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galina_Yermolayeva_%28rower%29) (née Suslina in 1948), Russian rower
- [Inna Suslina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inna_Suslina) (born 1979), Russian team handball player
- [Lyudmila Suslina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyudmila_Suslina) (born 1946), Russian figure skater
- [Mikhail Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Suslin) (1894–1919), Russian mathematician known for
  - [Suslin algebra](wikipedia://en/43236771)
  - [Suslin cardinal](wikipedia://en/7652107), a transfinite cardinal number at which one obtains new Suslin sets
  - [Suslin operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suslin_operation)
  - [Suslin's problem](wikipedia://en/228668)
  - [Suslin representation](wikipedia://en/7652984), a set of real numbers built up in a certain way
- [Sergey Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Suslin) (1944–1989), Soviet judoka and sambo competitor
- [Viktor Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Suslin) (1942–2012), Russian composer
- [Viktor Suslin (rower)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Suslin_%28rower%29) (born 1944), Russian Olympic rower
- [Yury Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yury_Suslin) (born 1935), Russian Olympic rower, brother of Viktor
