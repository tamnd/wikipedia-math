# Surface bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Surface bundle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **surface bundle**[^o2] is a [bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) in which the fiber is a [surface](wikipedia://en/27865). When the base space is a circle the total space is [three-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1018257) and is often called a [surface bundle over the circle](wikipedia://en/2011690).

:::
