# Richard P. Stanley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard P. Stanley"}

**Richard Peter Stanley** (born June 23, 1944) is an Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and an Arts and Sciences Distinguished Scholar at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami). From 2000 to 2010, he was the [Norman Levinson](wikipedia://en/1090122) Professor of [Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at MIT.[^o4] He received his Ph.D. at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1971 under the supervision of [Gian-Carlo Rota](wikipedia://en/101891). He is an expert in the field of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and its applications to other mathematical disciplines.[^o12]

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Stanley is known for his two-volume book *Enumerative Combinatorics* (1986–1999).[^o7][^o8] He is also the author of *Combinatorics and Commutative Algebra* (1983) and well over 200 research articles in mathematics. He has served as thesis advisor to 60 doctoral students, many of whom have had distinguished careers in combinatorial research.[^o5] [Donald Knuth](wikipedia://en/8095) named Stanley as one of his combinatorial heroes in a 2023 interview.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Stanley's distinctions include membership in the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (elected in 1995), the 2001 [Leroy P. Steele Prize for Mathematical Exposition](wikipedia://en/1252550),[^o11] the 2003 [Schock Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schock_Prize),[^o12] a [plenary lecture at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plenary_lecture_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) (in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain)),[^o13] and election in 2012 as a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14] In 2022 he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize for Lifetime Achievement](wikipedia://en/1252550).[^o15]
