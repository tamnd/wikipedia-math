# Vincent Olatunji

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vincent Olatunji"}

**Vincent Olatunji** is a [Nigerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerians) policy innovator, public sector analyst,[^o3] and the National Commissioner of the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Commission) (NDPC).[^o4] He was previously the acting director general of the [National Information Technology Development Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Information_Technology_Development_Agency) (NITDA)[^o5][^o6] and also served as chairman of Nigeria Data Protection Commission Implementation Committee.[^o7]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

He was born in [Ekiti state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekiti_State).[^o9] He attended the [University of Lagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lagos) in 1996 where he obtained a diploma in computer science. He holds a doctorate degree (PhD) in Geography and Planning from University of Lagos and an Advanced Diploma in Computer Science.[^o10] He is a certified Public Private Partnership Specialist (IP3 Specialist) and Certified Data Protection Officer.[^o11]

## Career {#Career}

After his graduation from the university, he joined the [Federal Civil Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Civil_Service_Commission_%28Nigeria%29) in Nigeria as a research officer under the Federal Urban Mass Transit Agency.[^o13] He joined [National Information Technology Development Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Information_Technology_Development_Agency) (NITDA) in 2002 in the planning research and statistics department. He became NITDA Director in 2014 and Acting Director General in 2016.[^o14][^o15] He also worked as Director of eGovernment and Regulations at NITDA.[^o16] He was appointed the national commissioner of the Nigeria Data Protection Agency (NDPC) in 2022 by President [Muhammadu Buhari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammadu_Buhari).[^o17] In October 2023, he was re-appointed by President [Bola Tinubu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bola_Tinubu).[^o18] He was appointed to the Forbes Technology Council in 2023.[^o19]

## Work {#Work}

He has developed strategies and framework for implementation to the Nigerian’s eGovernment and National ICT Transformation Agenda,[^o21] which led to the development of various policies like; Nigeria Inter-Operability Framework,[^o22] Nigeria Government [Enterprise Architecture Framework](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enterprise_architecture_framework),[^o23] Cloud First Policy,[^o24][^o25] [Government Digital Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Digital_Service) Policy,[^o26][^o27] Nigeria Data Protection Regulation[^o28] and Smart Initiatives Framework to enhance Nigeria Digital Transformation.[^o29]

1. Top 50 [Digital Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_economy) Enablers in Nigeria by IT Edge News in February 2023.[^o32][^o33][^o34]
1. 100 leading Telecom and ICT personalities in a Nigeria by the Association of Telecommunications Companies of Nigeria.[^o35]
1. Outstanding Commitments Award by University of Lagos Alumni Association.[^o36][^o37]
1. Eminent Personality Award by [Ekiti State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekiti_State_University).[^o38]
1. Honorary [Doctor of Public Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Public_Administration) by Ekiti State University in June 2018.[^o39][^o40]
