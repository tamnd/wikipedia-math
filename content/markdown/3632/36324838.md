# Michel Duflo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Duflo"}

**Michel Duflo** (born 1943) is a French mathematician who works in the representation theory of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).

:::

## Life {#Life}

From 1962, Duflo studied at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) and received a doctorate under the supervision of [Jacques Dixmier](wikipedia://en/9169420). Currently, he is an [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII) (Denis Diderot) at the [Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu](wikipedia://en/57642956), and at the École normale supérieure.

Duflo has worked on the [orbit method](wikipedia://en/4231851) of [Alexander Kirillov](wikipedia://en/3772515). He introduced the [Duflo isomorphism](wikipedia://en/34050946), an isomorphism between the center of the enveloping algebra of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra and the invariants of its symmetric algebra.

In 1974 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver) (*Inversion formula and invariant differential operators on solvable Lie groups*). Duflo received the [Prix Le Conte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Le_Conte) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences); in 1986 he became a corresponding member of the Academy.

His students include [Laurent Clozel](wikipedia://en/51408473).

His daughter is the [Nobel prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Economics)-winning economist [Esther Duflo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Duflo).
