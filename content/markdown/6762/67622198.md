# Felix Frankl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Frankl"}

**Felix Issidorowitsch Frankl** (12 March 1905, [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) – 7 Aprile 1961, [Nalchik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalchik) Феликс Исидорович Франкль) was an Austrian mathematician, who went to live in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) where he had an academic career as a university professor.[^o2]

:::

He studied topology at the Faculty of Mathematics of the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) under [Hans Hahn](wikipedia://en/1075212), gaining his doctorate in 1927.[^o3]

Frankl joined the Austrian Communist Party in 1928 and (with the assistance of [Pavel Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Aleksandrov)) emigrated to the Soviet Union in 1929.[^o4] Here he initially collaborated with [Lev Pontryagin](wikipedia://en/582075) in topology (they a paper co-authored a paper published in 1930 in the *[Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381)*. His interests then shifted to certain particular differential equations which are important for high-speed aerodynamics. These differential equations were of mixed elliptic-hyperbolic type. They determined the transition in aerodynamics between transonic and supersonic speeds.

He attended the [First International Topological Conference](wikipedia://en/60495654) held in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1935.[^o5] In 1957 he was awarded the [Leonhard Euler Gold Medal](wikipedia://en/67910317) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o2]

In 1950 he was expelled from the communist party and exiled to [Bishkek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishkek).[^o4] He died in 1961 in [Nalchik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalchik).[^o4]
