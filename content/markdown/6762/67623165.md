# Julia Rozanska

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Rozanska"}

**Iulia Antonivna Rozhanska** (Юлія Антонівна Рожанська; Юлия Антоновна Рожанская; 1901 – 23 April 1967), also [spelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polonisation) **Julia Różańska**, was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist). After studying under [Pavel Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Aleksandrov), she was an associate professor at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). She was a member of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251).[^o2] She attended the [First International Topological Conference](wikipedia://en/60495654), Moscow, 1935.[^o2][^o3]

:::
