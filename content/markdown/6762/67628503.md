# Komei Fukuda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Komei Fukuda"}

**Komei Fukuda** (福田 公明, 1951 - July 27, 2026)[^o3][^o4] was a Japanese mathematician known for his contributions to [optimization](wikipedia://en/420555), [polyhedral computation](wikipedia://en/19696519) and [oriented matroid](wikipedia://en/24338041) theory. Fukuda was a professor in [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization) and [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) in the Department of Mathematics and in the Institute of Theoretical Computer Science at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fukuda studied administration engineering at [Keio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keio_University), graduating in 1974 and earning a master's degree in 1976. He began doctoral work in the same field, but in 1976 transferred to the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) to their PhD program in combinatorics and optimization.[^o6] He completed his PhD in 1982, with [Jack Edmonds](wikipedia://en/3625459) as his [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor).

After completing his PhD, he returned to Japan as an assistant professor at the [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Institute_of_Technology). He moved to the [University of Tsukuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tsukuba) as an associate professor in 1989. After visiting the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) and ETH Zurich in 1993–1994 and 1995–1996 respectively, as an invited professor, he took a joint position as a professor in the departments of mathematics at both universities in 1996. He also held a tenured professorship at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in 2002–2003. In 2008 he gave up his position at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, becoming affiliated only with ETH Zurich, and since 2012 he has held a joint appointment in mathematics and computer science at ETH Zurich.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Fukuda has studied finite pivot algorithms in various settings, including linear programming, linear complementarity and their combinatorial abstractions in oriented matroids. With [Tamás Terlaky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamás_Terlaky), Fukuda worked on a particular class of pivot algorithms, known as the [criss-cross method](wikipedia://en/31255067).[^o9]FT92FT97

With [David Avis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Avis), Fukuda proposed a [reverse-search algorithm](wikipedia://en/71470682) for the [vertex enumeration problem](wikipedia://en/19558316); their algorithm generates all of the [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) of a [convex polytope](wikipedia://en/1817885) or, dually, of an [arrangement of hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/864438).[^o10][^o11]AF92AF96
