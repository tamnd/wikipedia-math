# Aleksei Parshin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksei Parshin"}

**Aleksei Nikolaevich Parshin** (Алексей Николаевич Паршин; 7 November 1942 – 18 June 2022) was a Russian mathematician, specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He is most well-known for his role in the proof of the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Parshin entered the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1959 and graduated in 1964.[^o6][^o4] He then enrolled as a graduate student at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494), where he received his *[Kand. Nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)* (Ph.D.) in 1968 under [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815).[^o4][^o5] In 1983, he received his *[Doctor Nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Nauk)* (doctorate of sciences) from Moscow State University.[^o13][^o20]

Parshin became a junior research fellow at the Steklov Institute of Mathematics in Moscow in 1968, later becoming a senior and leading research fellow.[^o20][^o6] He became the head of its Department of Algebra in 1995.[^o20][^o6] He also taught at Moscow State University.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

In his 1968 thesis, Parshin proved that the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture) is a logical consequence of [Shafarevich's finiteness conjecture](wikipedia://en/172199#Proofs) concerning isomorphism classes of [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) via what is known as *Parshin's trick*, which gives an embedding of an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) into the [Siegel modular variety](wikipedia://en/61144542).[^o8][^o9] Shafarevich proved his finiteness conjecture for the case with genus *g* = 1. Parshin proved a special case (for $S$ = the empty set) of the following theorem: If $B$ is a smooth complex curve and $S$ is a finite subset of $B$ then there exist only finitely many families (up to isomorphism) of smooth curves of fixed genus *g* ≥ 2 over $B \backslash S$.[^o10] The general case (for non-empty $S$) of the preceding theorem was proved by [Suren Arakelov](wikipedia://en/10705349) in 1971.[^o10][^o11] At the same time, Parshin gave a new proof (without an application of the Shafarevich finiteness condition) of the Mordell conjecture in function fields (already proved by [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086) in 1963 and by [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031) in 1965).[^o12] In 1983, [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226) completed the program and proved Shafarevich's finiteness conjecture, thereby proving the Mordell conjecture.[^o9]

His other research dealt with generalizations of [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) in higher dimensions, with integrable systems, and with the history of mathematics.[^o13][^o6]

He was an editor for the Russian edition of the collected works of [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) and was a co-editor, with [V. I. Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490), of selected works of [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544).[^o13][^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Parshin was born on 7 November 1942 in [Sverdlovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterinburg)[^o13][^o6] and died on 18 June 2022.[^o20][^o16][^o17]

Parshin was longtime friends with Russian philosopher [Aleksei Losev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksei_Losev) and started the Russian philosophy seminar at the Dom Loseva Library in Moscow.[^o18] Parshin was [Orthodox Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodoxy) and wrote about the relationship between Russian religious philosophy and the modern sciences.[^o18]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1971, Parshin received the Prize of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251) for young mathematicians.[^o20] He was awarded the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 1996.[^o6][^o20] He received the [Vinogradov Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Named_prizes_and_medals_of_the_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences#Mathematics) in 2004 and the [Chebyshev Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Named_prizes_and_medals_of_the_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences#Mathematics) in 2012 from the Russian Academy of Sciences.[^o6][^o20]

The [Université Paris-Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Nord) granted Parshin an honorary doctorate in 2001.[^o20][^o13] Parshin was elected a corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2006 and then a full member in 2011.[^o20] He was elected a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2017.[^o20]

Parshin was an invited speaker at the 1970 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) with his talk titled *Quelques conjectures de finitude en géométrie diophantienne*.[^o20][^o21] He was a plenary speaker at the 2010 ICM with his talk titled *Representations of higher adelic groups and arithmetic*.[^o20][^o22]
