# Francis Bonahon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francis Bonahon"}

*Image: Bonahon francis.jpg.* Francis Bonahon, Oberwolfach 1987

**Francis Bonahon** (born 9 September 1955) is a French mathematician, specializing in [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bonahon received in 1972 his *[baccalauréat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baccalauréat)*, and was accepted in 1974 into the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). He received in 1975 his *maîtrise* in mathematics from the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII), and in 1979 his doctorate from the [University of Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XI) under [Laurence Siebenmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Siebenmann) with thesis *Involutions et fibrés de Seifert dans les variétés de dimension 3*. As a postdoc he was for the academic year 1979/80 a *Procter Fellow* at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). In 1980 he became an *attaché de recherche* and in 1983 a *chargé de recherche* of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). In 1985 he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from the University of Paris XI under Siebenmann with thesis *Geometric structures on 3-manifolds and applications*. Bonahon became in 1986 an assistant professor, in 1988 an associate professor, and in 1989 a full professor at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles).[^o4]

He was a visiting professor in 1990 at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis), in 1996 at the Centre Émile Borel and at the [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES), in 1997 at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), in 2000 at IHES, and in 2015 at the [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute).[^o4]

Bonahon's research deals with three-dimensional topology, knot theory, surface diffeomorphisms, hyperbolic geometry, and [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652).

He received in 1985 a bronze medal from [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) and from 1989 to 1994 a Presidential Young Investigator Award. From 1987 to 1989 he was a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship). In 1990 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Ensembles limites et applications* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto). He was elected a Fellow of [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.

His doctoral students include [Frédéric Paulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frédéric_Paulin).
