# Cryptographic Module Validation Program

*Image: CMVPlogo.png.* Logo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptographic Module Validation Program"}

The **Cryptographic Module Validation Program** (**CMVP**) is a joint American and Canadian security accreditation program for [cryptographic modules](wikipedia://en/5161053). The program is available to any vendors who seek to have their products certified for use by the [U.S. Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Government) and regulated industries (such as financial and health-care institutions) that collect, store, transfer, share and disseminate "[sensitive, but not classified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensitive_but_unclassified)" information. All of the tests under the CMVP are handled by third-party laboratories that are accredited as [Cryptographic Module Testing Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMTL) by the [National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Voluntary_Laboratory_Accreditation_Program) (NVLAP). Product certifications under the CMVP are performed in accordance with the requirements of [FIPS 140-3](wikipedia://en/25191344).

:::

The CMVP was established by the U.S. [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) (NIST) and the [Communications Security Establishment](wikipedia://en/231712) (CSE) of the [Government of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Canada) in July 1995.

The Cryptographic Algorithm Validation Program (CAVP), which provides guidelines for validation testing for FIPS approved and NIST recommended cryptographic algorithms and components of algorithms, is a prerequisite for CMVP.[^o2]
