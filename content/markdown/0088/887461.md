# Marianna Csörnyei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marianna Csörnyei"}

Csörnyei Marianna **Marianna Csörnyei** (born October 8, 1975, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works as a professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). She does research in [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and geometric nonlinear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). She proved the equivalence of the [zero measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_measure) notions of infinite dimensional [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Csörnyei received her doctorate from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) in 1999, supervised by György Petruska. She was a professor at the Mathematics Department of [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) between 1999 and 2011, and spent the 2009–2010 academic year at Yale University as visiting professor. Currently, she is at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o4]

She is contributing editor of the mathematical journal *[Real Analysis Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Analysis_Exchange)*.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Csörnyei won a 2002 [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913)[^o7] from the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and a [Royal Society Wolfson Research Merit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Wolfson_Research_Merit_Award) that same year. She was also awarded the [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize)[^o8] for Mathematics and Statistics in 2008 for her work in geometric measure theory.[^o9]

She won a gold medal in the [International Mathematics Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o10]

She was an invited sectional speaker[^o11] at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2010.

Csörnyei was selected to deliver the AWM-AMS [Noether Lecture](wikipedia://en/11566873) at the 2022 [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143) in [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington). The title of her talk is *The Kakeya needle problem for rectifiable sets*.[^o12]

She is included in a deck of playing cards featuring notable women mathematicians published by the [Association of Women in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Women_in_Mathematics).[^o13]
