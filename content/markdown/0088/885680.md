# Heinrich Christoph Wilhelm Sigwart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Christoph Wilhelm Sigwart"}

*Image: Hc Wilhelm Sigward.jpg.* Heinrich Christoph Wilhelm von Sigwart

**Heinrich Christoph Wilhelm von Sigwart** (31 August 1789 – 16 November 1844) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065). He was the father of [Christoph von Sigwart](wikipedia://en/885684) (28 March 1830 – 4 August 1904), who also was a philosopher and logician.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Sigwart was born into a family with a long history of philosophers, theologians and physicians at [Remmingsheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neustetten) in [Württemberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Württemberg). From 1813 he served as a repentant at [Tübinger Stift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tübinger_Stift) in Tübingen, and obtained an associate professorship at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) in 1816. He became a full professor of philosophy at Tübingen in 1818 and wrote numerous books on the [history of philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_philosophy).[^n1] He died in [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart).[^o4]
