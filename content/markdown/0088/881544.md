# L. C. Schnürlein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="L. C. Schnürlein"}

**L. C. Schnürlein** (*fl.* nineteenth century) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator).

:::

Having studied under [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125), he became a teacher at the gymnasium in [Hof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hof,_Germany), tutoring, among others, [Carl Culmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Culmann) and [Philipp Ludwig von Seidel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Ludwig_von_Seidel).
