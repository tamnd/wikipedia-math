# Berger's isoembolic inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Berger's isoembolic inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Berger's isoembolic inequality** is a result in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) that gives a lower bound on the volume of a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) and also gives a [necessary and sufficient condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necessary_and_sufficient_condition) for the manifold to be [isometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometry_%28Riemannian_geometry%29) to the $m$-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) with its usual "round" metric. The theorem is named after the [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363), who derived it from an inequality proved by [Jerry Kazdan](wikipedia://en/12870493).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $(M, g)$ be a [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) $m$-dimensional Riemannian manifold with [injectivity radius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injectivity_radius) $\operatorname{inj}(M)$. Let $\operatorname{vol}(M)$ denote the Riemannian volume of $M$ and let $c_{m}$ denote the volume of the standard $m$-dimensional sphere of radius one. Then

$$\mathrm{vol} (M) \geq c_m \left(\!\frac{\mathrm{inj}(M)}{\pi}\!\right)^{\!\!m},$$

with equality [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) $(M, g)$ is isometric to the [$m$-sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) with its usual round metric. This result is known as Berger's *isoembolic inequality*. The proof relies upon an analytic inequality proved by Kazdan. The original work of Berger and Kazdan appears in the appendices of [Arthur Besse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Besse)'s book "Manifolds all of whose geodesics are closed." At this stage, the isoembolic inequality appeared with a non-optimal constant. Sometimes Kazdan's inequality is called *Berger–Kazdan inequality*.

:::
