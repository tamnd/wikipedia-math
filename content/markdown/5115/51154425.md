# Jean Céa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Céa"}

**Jean Céa** (8 February 1932 – 9 January 2024) was a French mathematician.

:::

Born to Spanish immigrants in [Aïn Témouchent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aïn_Témouchent), Algeria (he learnt French in school), he studied at the [Ecole normale d'instituteurs d'Oran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_normale_d'instituteurs_d'Oran) and the [École normale supérieure de Saint-Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Saint-Cloud), obtaining his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1964 with his dissertation *Approximation variationnelle des problèmes aux limites*.[^o3] In his thesis he proved the [Céa's lemma](wikipedia://en/11076807), an important result related to error estimation in the [Finite Element Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_Element_Method).

Céa was emeritus professor at the [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis) and member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea). In 1975 he was awarded the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828).

Céa died on 9 January 2024 in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice), at the age of 91.[^o4]
