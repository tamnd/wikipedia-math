# Istituto per le Applicazioni del Calcolo Mauro Picone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Istituto per le Applicazioni del Calcolo Mauro Picone"}

The **Istituto per le Applicazioni del Calcolo Mauro Picone** (Institute for applied mathematics "Mauro Picone"), abbreviated **IAC**, is an [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) [institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute), part of the [Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consiglio_Nazionale_delle_Ricerche). It was founded in 1927 as a private research institute by [Mauro Picone](wikipedia://en/10162009),[^o4] and as such it is considered the first applied and computational mathematics institute of such kind ever founded.[^o5]

:::

## Historical notes {#Historical_notes}

The IAC was founded 1927 by Mauro Picone,[^o4] while working at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II) and at the [Istituto Universitario Navale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Universitario_Navale) as professor of [infinitesimal calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitesimal_calculus).[^o7] [Luigi Amoroso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Amoroso) also contributed to the founding of the institute, by providing to his former *[Normale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore)* schoolfellow Picone the funding for the creation of the Institute by means of the [Banco di Napoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banco_di_Napoli).[^o8] It was only in 1932, when Picone moved from the University of Naples to the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), that the Institute became part of the Italian National Research Council.[^o9]
