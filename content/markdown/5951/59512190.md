# Permutation category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Permutation category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **permutation category**[^o2] is the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) where

:::

1. the objects are the natural numbers,
1. the morphisms from a natural number *n* to itself are the elements of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) $S_n$ and
1. there are no morphisms from *m* to *n* if $m\neq n$.

It is [equivalent as a category](wikipedia://en/379051) to the category of finite sets and bijections between them.
