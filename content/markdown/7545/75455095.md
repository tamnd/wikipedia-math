# Anthony C. Davison

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anthony C. Davison"}

**Anthony Christopher Davison** (born 28 February 1958)[^o5] is a British mathematical statistician and educator. He made seminal contributions to [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732), [likelihood inference](wikipedia://en/60224882) and [environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339).[^o3][^o13]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Davison was born in London. He attended the [Warwick School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick_School), followed by the [St. John's College, University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Oxford), where he obtained a B.A. in mathematics in 1980. He then studied at the [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) and obtained a MSc in statistics in 1981 with a project supervised by [Martin Beale](wikipedia://en/10662685). He went on the obtain a PhD in statistics in 1984 at the [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) under the supervision of environmental scientist [Helen ApSimon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_ApSimon) and statistician Richard L. Smith. Between 1984 and 1986, Davison was an assistant professor at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in the United States. He was a lecturer at the Department of Mathematics at the Imperial College London from 1986 to 1989, and a university lecturer at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) from 1989 to 1996.[^o5]

Davison joined [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL) in Switzerland in 1996 as a professor of statistics. He became the chair of the statistics department.[^o5] In 2021, Davison, [Holger Rootzén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holger_Rootzén) and other published work using the longevity data and [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732) to predict the age limit of humans.[^o6][^o7][^o8] Davison retired from EPFL in June 2025.[^o9]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

Davison was awarded the [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in Silver of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 2015.[^o11] He gave the Medallion lecture of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2018.[^o12] He is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), the Institute of Mathematical Statistics, and the International Statistical Institute.[^o13]
