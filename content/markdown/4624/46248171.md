# Sabir Gusein-Zade

*Image: Gusein-zade.jpg.* Sabir Gusein-Zade (2010), El Escorial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sabir Gusein-Zade"}

**Sabir Medgidovich Gusein-Zade** (Сабир Меджидович Гусейн-Заде; born 29 July 1950 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)[^o2]) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) mathematician and a specialist in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763) and its applications.[^o3]

:::

He studied at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he earned his Ph.D. in 1975 under the joint supervision of [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515) and [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490). Before entering the university, he had earned a [gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_medal) at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o3]

Gusein-Zade co-authored with V. I. Arnold and [A. N. Varchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Varchenko) the textbook *Singularities of Differentiable Maps* (published in English by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser)).[^o3]

A professor in both the Moscow State University and the [Independent University of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_University_of_Moscow), Gusein-Zade also serves as co-editor-in-chief for the *[Moscow Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o5] He shares credit with [Norbert A'Campo](wikipedia://en/34945743) for results on the singularities of plane curves.[^o6][^o7][^o8]
