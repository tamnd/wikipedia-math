# Olvi L. Mangasarian

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Olvi L. Mangasarian"}

**Olvi Leon Mangasarian** (12 January 1934 – 15 March 2020) was the *John von Neumann Professor Emeritus* of Mathematics and Computer Sciences in Department of Mathematics, [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) and Professor Emeritus of Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a recognised expert on [optimization](wikipedia://en/52033), [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253), and [classification](wikipedia://en/126706).[^o3] In 2000, while professor in the Computer Science Department of the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), he was awarded the [Frederick W. Lanchester Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_W._Lanchester_Prize) for pioneering work in introducing the use of [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) techniques to the field of data mining with a particularly notable application being to [breast cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breast_cancer) diagnosis.[^o4]

:::
