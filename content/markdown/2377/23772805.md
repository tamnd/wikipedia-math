# Annals of Mathematical Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annals of Mathematical Statistics"}

The ***Annals of Mathematical Statistics*** was a peer-reviewed statistics [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) from 1930 to 1972. It was superseded by the *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)* and the *[Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748)*. In 1938, [Samuel Wilks](wikipedia://en/8136395) became editor-in-chief of the *Annals* and recruited a remarkable editorial staff: [Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824), [Neyman](wikipedia://en/2386106), [Cramér](wikipedia://en/223129), [Hotelling](wikipedia://en/935621), [Egon Pearson](wikipedia://en/1643759), [Georges Darmois](wikipedia://en/28719892), Allen T. Craig, [Deming](wikipedia://en/70534), [von Mises](wikipedia://en/821517), [H. L. Rietz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._L._Rietz), and [Shewhart](wikipedia://en/433679).[^o3]

:::
