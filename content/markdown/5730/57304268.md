# Stanley Farlow

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stanley Farlow"}

**Stanley Jerome Farlow** (born 1937) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). For many years he has been a professor at the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Farlow earned a bachelor's degree in physics at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) and a master's degree in mathematics at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa).[^o4] From 1962 to 1968 he was a lieutenant commander in the PHS, completing his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University) in 1967. His doctoral supervisor was Ronald Bernard Guenther, and his doctoral dissertation was on *Existence Theorems for Periodic Solutions of Parabolic Partial Differential Equations*.

He is currently a Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine).[^o4]

## Books {#Books}

Farlow is the author of several books in mathematics, including

- *Partial Differential Equations for Scientists and Engineers* (Wiley, 1982; Russian translation, Moscow: Mir, 1985; Dover, 1993)[^o7]
- Applied Mathematics for Management, Life Science, and Social Science (with Gary M. Haggard, Random House, 1988)
- *Finite Mathematics and Its Applications* (with Gary M. Haggard, Random House, 1988; 2nd ed., McGraw Hill, 1994)
- *Introduction to Calculus with Applications* (with Gary M. Haggard, McGraw Hill, 1990)
- *An Introduction to Differential Equations and Their Applications* (McGraw Hill, 1994; Dover, 2006)
- Differential Equations and Linear Algebra (with James E. Hall, Jean Marie Mc Dill, and Beverly H. West, Prentice Hall, 2002)
- *Paradoxes in Mathematics* (Dover, 2014)[^o8]
- Advanced Mathematics: A Transitional Reference (Wiley, 2020)

He is also the editor of:

- *Self-Organizing Methods in Modeling: GMDH Type Algorithms* (Marcel Dekker, 1984)
