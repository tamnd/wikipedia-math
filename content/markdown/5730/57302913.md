# Fiona Steele

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fiona Steele"}

**Fiona Alison Steele**, is a British [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). Since 2013, she has been Professor of Statistics at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) (LSE).

:::

After graduating with a degree in mathematics and statistics from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 1992, Steele completed her master's degree (in 1993) and [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in statistics (in 1996) at the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton). She then joined the LSE as a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) in statistics and research methodology. She was appointed to a research lectureship at the [Institute of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Education) in 2001. In 2005 moved to the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) to be a [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29) in social statistics; she was promoted to [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) three years later. Steele was also Director of Bristol's Centre for [Multilevel Modelling](wikipedia://en/4521038) from 2010 until she took up her post at the LSE in 2013.[^o2][^o3][^o4] According to her [British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy) profile, her research relates to the "development and application of statistical methods for the analysis of [longitudinal data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_data), including multilevel [event history models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event_history_analysis) and [simultaneous equation models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simultaneous_equation_model)" and their "applications in [demography](wikipedia://en/45642) and education".[^o4]

## Honours {#Honours}

In 2008, Steele was awarded the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)'s [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in Bronze.[^o3] The following year she was elected a [Fellow of the British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_British_Academy) (FBA), the United Kingdom's [national academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_academy) for the humanities and social sciences.[^o4] She was appointed [Officer of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (OBE) in the [2011 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_New_Year_Honours) for services to social science[^o6] and [Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (CBE) in the [2022 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Birthday_Honours) for services to statistics in the social sciences.

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- (Co-authored with P. Clarke, G. Leckie, J. Allan and D. W. Johnston) "Multilevel structural equation models for longitudinal data where predictors are measured more frequently than outcomes: an application to the effects of stress on cognitive function of nurses", *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series A* (2016).
- (Co-authored with R. French and M. Bartley) "Adjusting for selection bias in longitudinal analyses of the relationship between employment transitions and health using simultaneous equations modelling", *Epidemiology*, vol. 24, no. 5 (2013), pp. 703–11.
- (Co-authored with P. Clarke and E. Washbrook) "Modelling household decisions using longitudinal data from household panel surveys, with applications to residential mobility", *Sociological Methodology*, vol. 43, no. 1 (2013), pp. 225–76.
- (Co-authored with J. Rasbash and J. Jenkins) "A multilevel simultaneous equations model for within-cluster dynamic effects, with an application to reciprocal parent-child and sibling effects", *Psychological Methods*, vol. 18, no. 1 (2013), pp. 87–100.
- (Co-authored with W. Sigle-Rushton and Ø. Kravdal) "Consequences of family disruption on children's educational outcomes in Norway", *Demography*, vol. 46, no. 3 (2009), pp. 553–74.
- (Co-authored with D. J. Bartholomew, I. Moustaki and J. Galbraith) *Analysis of Multivariate Social Science Data*, 2nd edition (CRC Press, 2008).
