# W. G. Brown

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="W. G. Brown"}

**William G. Brown** is a Canadian mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Brown earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1963, under the joint supervision of [Harold Scott MacDonald Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Scott_MacDonald_Coxeter) and [W. T. Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021). His dissertation was *Enumeration Problems Of Linear Graph Theory (Problems in the Enumeration of Maps)*.

In 1968, he moved to McGill from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) as an associate professor.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Brown's dissertation research concerned [graph enumeration](wikipedia://en/9982439), and his early publications continued in that direction.ET However, much of his later work was in [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568). He is known for formulating the [Ruzsa–Szemerédi problem](wikipedia://en/59842050) on the density of systems of triples in which no six points contain more than two triples in joint work with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Vera T. Sós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_T._Sós),AB and for his constructions of dense $K_{3,3}$-free graphs in connection with the [Zarankiewicz problem](wikipedia://en/3480707).Z
