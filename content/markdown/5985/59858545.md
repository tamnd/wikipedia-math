# Harold Weitzner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harold Weitzner"}

**Harold Weitzner** is an American applied mathematician and physicist whose primary research is [plasma physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_physics). He is Professor [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of Mathematics at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) and has served as Director of the Magneto-Fluid Dynamics Division [^o2] at Courant since 1981, succeeding [Harold Grad](wikipedia://en/39523702). He has published over 120 research articles[^o3] on the topics of [plasma physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_physics), [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310), [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), [fractional equations and kinetics](wikipedia://en/39605149), and [chaos](wikipedia://en/6295).

:::

Professor Weitzner received his Ph.D. in 1958 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) on the topic of "Hyperon-Nucleon Interactions".

In 1981 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society).[^o4]
