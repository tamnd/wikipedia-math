# Lyusternik–Fet theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lyusternik–Fet theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Lyusternik–Fet theorem** states that on every compact [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) there exists a [closed geodesic](wikipedia://en/19903176). It is named after [Lazar Lyusternik](wikipedia://en/4201372) and [Abram Ilyich Fet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abram_Ilyich_Fet), who published it in 1951.[^o3]

:::
