# Paul J. Nahin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul J. Nahin"}

**Paul J. Nahin** (born November 26, 1940) is an American electrical engineer, author, and former college professor. He has written over 20 books on topics in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

*Image: Science at Village Books - Flickr - brewbooks (1).jpg.* A copy of Nahin's book *The Logician and the Engineer*

Born in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), Nahin graduated from [Brea Olinda High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brea_Olinda_High_School) in 1958, and thereafter received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1962, an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1963, and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), in 1972, all in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering).[^o4]

Nahin thereafter taught at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College), the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), and the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School) in [Monterey, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California).[^o4] He retired in 2004 from his position as professor of [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) at the [University of New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Hampshire), becoming a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o5]

As an author, Nahin's publications include biographies of [Oliver Heaviside](wikipedia://en/22831), [George Boole](wikipedia://en/38298), and [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693), books on mathematical concepts such as [Euler's formula](wikipedia://en/9613), the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809), and the [imaginary unit](wikipedia://en/52358), and a number of books on the physics and philosophical puzzles of [time travel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_travel). His book *[Chases and Escapes](wikipedia://en/63183507)* has been rated as essential for inclusion in undergraduate mathematics libraries by The Basic Library List Committee of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o6]

In 1979, Nahin received the first Harry Rowe Mimno writing award,[^o7] from the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society. He received the 2017 Chandler Davis Prize for Excellence in Expository Writing in Mathematics.[^o8]
