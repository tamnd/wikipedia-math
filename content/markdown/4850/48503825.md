::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="3-4-6-12 tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of the Euclidean plane, the **3-4-6-12 tiling** is one of 20 [2-uniform tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410) [by regular polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiling_by_regular_polygons), containing regular [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and [dodecagons](wikipedia://en/508486), arranged in two [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745): 3.4.6.4 and 4.6.12.[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

It has [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/2863719) [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2868583#Wallpaper_groups), p6m, \[6,3\], (\*632). It is also called a [demiregular tiling](wikipedia://en/46891994) by some authors.

## Geometry {#Geometry}

Its two [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) are shared with two 1-uniform tilings:

::: {#o10 .table cue=table}

| [rhombitrihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864311) | [truncated trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864170) |
|---|---|
| *Image: 1-uniform\_n6.svg.* 3.4.6.4 | *Image: 1-uniform\_n3.svg.* 4.6.12 |

:::

It can be seen as a type of [diminished](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishment_%28geometry%29) [rhombitrihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864311), with dodecagons replacing periodic sets of hexagons and surrounding squares and triangles. This is similar to the [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), a [diminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131101), which is a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with faces removed, leading to new decagonal faces. The dual of this variant is shown to the right (deltoidal hexagonal insets).

## Circle Packing {#Circle_Packing}

This 2-uniform tiling can be used as a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665). Cyan circles are in contact with 3 other circles (2 cyan, 1 pink), corresponding to the V4.6.12 planigon, and pink circles are in contact with 4 other circles (1 cyan, 2 pink), corresponding to the V3.4.6.4 planigon. It is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the ambo operation on the tiling, with the cyan and pink gap polygons corresponding to the cyan and pink circles (mini-vertex configuration polygons; one dimensional duals to the respective planigons). Both images coincide.

### Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling has [right triangle](wikipedia://en/39420) and [kite](wikipedia://en/84840) faces, defined by [face configurations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration): V3.4.6.4 and V4.6.12, and can be seen combining the [deltoidal trihexagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deltoidal_trihexagonal_tiling) and [kisrhombille tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisrhombille_tiling).
