# Marja Holecyová

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marja Holecyová"}

**Marja Holecyová** (born Mária Holecyová[^o3] 28 August 1988) is a [Slovak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia) mathematician and fantasy writer.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Holecyová was born in Nitra. She studied Mathematics at the [Comenius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comenius_University). Her PhD thesis *Maximum Principle for Infinite Horizon Discrete Time Optimal Control Problems* was supervised by professor [Pavel Brunovský](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Brunovský) and defended in 2016.[^o5]

## Writing {#Writing}

Holecyová started writing [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) [fan fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_fiction) as a 16 years old under the pseudonym Marja Holecyová. Encouraged by the popularity of her writing she send a manuscript of her own fantasy story set in [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia) and based on local mythology Mariotovi dediči (Heirs of Mariot) to the Slovak branch of Czech fantasy and sci-fi published Fragment at the age of 20.[^o7] The success of the book resulted in three sequels published over the course of 2010 and 2011.[^o3] In 2016, she published a [historical fantasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_fantasy) novel set in the 16th century [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary) called Korene Hriechu (Roots of sin)[^o8]
