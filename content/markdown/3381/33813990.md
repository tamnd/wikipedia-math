# Sylvester domain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvester domain"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Sylvester domain**, named after [James Joseph Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861) by (Dicks Sontag 1978), is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) in which Sylvester's law of nullity holds. This means that if *A* is an *m* by *n* [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859), and *B* is an *n* by *s* matrix over *R*, then

:::

ρ(*AB*) ≥ ρ(*A*) + ρ(*B*) – *n*

where ρ is the inner rank of a matrix. The inner rank of an *m* by *n* matrix is the smallest integer *r* such that the matrix is a product of an *m* by *r* matrix and an *r* by *n* matrix.

(Sylvester 1884) showed that [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) satisfy Sylvester's law of nullity and are, therefore, Sylvester domains.
