# Pseudo-monotone operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo-monotone operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **pseudo-monotone operator** from a [reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091) [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) into its [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) is one that is, in some sense, almost as [well-behaved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-behaved) as a [monotone operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monotone_operator). Many problems in the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) can be expressed using operators that are pseudo-monotone, and pseudo-monotonicity in turn implies the existence of solutions to these problems.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let (*X*, || ||) be a reflexive Banach space. A map *T* : *X* → *X*<sup>∗</sup> from *X* into its continuous dual space *X*<sup>∗</sup> is said to be **pseudo-monotone** if *T* is a [bounded operator](wikipedia://en/455961) (not necessarily continuous) and if whenever

$$u_{j} \rightharpoonup u \mbox{ in } X \mbox{ as } j \to \infty$$

(i.e. *u*<sub>*j*</sub> [converges weakly](wikipedia://en/33662) to *u*) and

$$\limsup_{j \to \infty} \langle T(u_{j}), u_{j} - u \rangle \leq 0,$$

it follows that, for all *v* ∈ *X*,

$$\liminf_{j \to \infty} \langle T(u_{j}), u_{j} - v \rangle \geq \langle T(u), u - v \rangle.$$

:::

## Properties of pseudo-monotone operators {#Properties_of_pseudo-monotone_operators}

Using a very similar proof to that of the [Browder–Minty theorem](wikipedia://en/11908534), one can show the following:

Let (*X*, || ||) be a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438), reflexive Banach space and suppose that *T* : *X* → *X*<sup>∗</sup> is [bounded](wikipedia://en/311509), [coercive](wikipedia://en/8971904) and pseudo-monotone. Then, for each [continuous linear functional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_linear_functional) *g* ∈ *X*<sup>∗</sup>, there exists a solution *u* ∈ *X* of the equation *T*(*u*) = *g*.
