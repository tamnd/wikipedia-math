# James embedding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James embedding"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **James embedding** is an embedding of a real, complex, or hyperbolic [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) into a sphere, introduced by [Ioan James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioan_James).

:::
