# Egbert Brieskorn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Egbert Brieskorn"}

*Image: Egbert Brieskorn.jpeg.* Egbert Brieskorn

**Egbert Valentin Brieskorn** (7 July 1936 in [Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostock) – 11 July 2013 in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn)) was a German mathematician who introduced [Brieskorn spheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brieskorn_sphere) and the [Brieskorn–Grothendieck resolution](wikipedia://en/35104544).[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Brieskorn was born in 1936 as the son of a mill construction engineer in East Prussia. He grew up in [Freudenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freudenberg_%28Siegerland%29) (Siegerland) and studied mathematics and physics at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) and the [Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rheinische_Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität_Bonn). In 1963 he received his doctorate at Bonn under [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495) with thesis *Zur differentialtopologischen und analytischen Klassifizierung gewisser algebraischer Mannigfaltigkeiten*, followed by his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1968.

## Career {#Career}

From 1969 until 1973 he was professor ordinarius at [Georg-August-Universität Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg-August-Universität_Göttingen) and from 1973 to 1975 at the Sonderforschungsbereich Theoretische Mathematik in Bonn (since 1980 called the [Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-Planck-Institut_für_Mathematik)). From 1975 until his retirement as professor emeritus in 2001 he was a professor ordinarius at Bonn. He held temporary academic positions at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT) (where in 1965 he was [Moore Instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore_Instructor)), the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (IHES), the [Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eidgenössische_Technische_Hochschule_Zürich) (ETH Zürich), the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leiden), the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool), and the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Brieskorn was one of the editors of the collected works of [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989). In 1970 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice) (*Singular elements of semi-simple algebraic groups*). His doctoral students include [Horst Knörrer](wikipedia://en/35226873), [Peter Slodowy](wikipedia://en/35104673), [Kyoji Saito](wikipedia://en/56830460), and [Erhard Scholz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erhard_Scholz).
