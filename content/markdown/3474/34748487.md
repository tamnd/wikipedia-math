# Brieskorn manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brieskorn manifold"}

In mathematics, a **Brieskorn manifold** or **Brieskorn–Phạm manifold**, introduced by (txt), is the intersection of a small sphere around the origin with the singular, complex hypersurface

:::

$$x_1^{k_1}+\cdots+x_n^{k_n}=0$$

studied by (txt).

Brieskorn manifolds give examples of [exotic spheres](wikipedia://en/1114931),[^o4][^o5] for example the [Gromoll–Meyer sphere](wikipedia://en/79124644).
