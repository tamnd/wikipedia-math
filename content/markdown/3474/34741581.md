# Phillip Griffith

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Phillip Griffith"}

**Phillip Alan Griffith** (born December 29, 1940) is a mathematician and [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) who works on commutative algebra and ring theory. He received his PhD from the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) in 1968.[^o2] Griffith is the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the *[Illinois Journal of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/61381311)*[^o2] In 1971, Griffith received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship).[^o3]

:::
