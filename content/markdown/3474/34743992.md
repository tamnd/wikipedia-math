# Warfield group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Warfield group"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **Warfield group**, studied by R. B. Warfield Jr. in 1972, is a summand of a simply presented [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974).

:::
