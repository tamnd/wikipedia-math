# Gesine Reinert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gesine Reinert"}

*Image: Gesine Reinert.jpg.* Gesine Reinert

**Gesine Reinert** is a German statistician who is University Professor in Statistics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). She is a Fellow of [Keble College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keble_College,_Oxford), a Fellow of the [Alan Turing Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing_Institute), and a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). Her research concerns the [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and statistics of [biological sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence_%28biology%29) and [biological networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_network).

:::

Reinert has also been associated with the [M. Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474) pseudonymous mathematical collaboration on [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490).

## Education {#Education}

Reinert earned a diploma in mathematics from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1989. She went on to graduate study in applied mathematics at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich), completing her Ph.D. in 1994. Her dissertation, in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), was *A Weak Law of Large Numbers for Empirical Measures via Stein's Method, and Applications*, and was supervised by Andrew Barbour.

## Career {#Career}

Reinert worked as a lecturer at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) from 1994 to 1996 and the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) from 1996 to 1998, and as a senior research fellow at [King's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge) from 1998 to 2000. She joined the Oxford faculty in 2000, and was given a professorship there in 2004.
