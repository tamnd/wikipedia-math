# Jean Cerf

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Cerf"}

**Jean Cerf** (born 1928) is a French mathematician, specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jean Cerf was born in Strasbourg, France, in 1928. He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), graduating in sciences in 1947. After passing his *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* in mathematics in 1950, he obtained a doctorate with thesis supervised by [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536). Cerf became a *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* at the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille) and was later appointed a professor at the [University of Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XI). He was also a director of research at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS).[^o4]

Cerf's research deals with [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), [cobordism](wikipedia://en/391832), and [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology). In 1966 he was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow).[^o5] In 1968 Cerf proved that every orientation-preserving [diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564) of [$S^3$](wikipedia://en/39792) is [isotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotopy_%28topology%29) to the identity.[^o6] In 1970 Cerf proved the pseudo-isotopy theorem for [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470). In 1970 he was awarded the *prix Servant*, together with [Bernard Malgrange](wikipedia://en/9170142) and [André Néron](wikipedia://en/9175241) (for independent work). 1971 he was the president of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France).
