# Daniel Goroff

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Goroff"}

**Daniel L. Goroff** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [academic administrator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_administration). He is currently the Vice President and Program Director at the [Alfred P. Sloan Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Foundation), where he manages programs centered on [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and the quality of [graduate education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postgraduate_education).[^o3] He was a [technology policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_policy) official in the [Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden) [White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Goroff received his B.A. and M.A. degrees from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He then attended the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) as a Churchill Scholar, earning an M.Phil. He completed his Ph.D. in mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Goroff spent over twenty years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a Professor of the Practice of Mathematics and Associate Dean of Harvard College. He later served as the Vice President for Academic Affairs and Dean of the Faculty at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College).[^o8]

In the public sector, Goroff has twice worked at the [White House Office of Science and Technology Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Office_of_Science_and_Technology_Policy) (OSTP), most recently as Deputy Director for Social, Behavioral, and Economic Sciences.[^o9]

## Research and publications {#Research_and_publications}

His research focuses on [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). He is particularly noted for his work on the history of mathematics, including his extensive editing and translation of [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740)'s *Les Méthodes Nouvelles de la Mécanique Céleste*.
