# Lévy's stochastic area

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lévy's stochastic area"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Lévy's stochastic area** is a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) that describes the enclosed area of a [trajectory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trajectory) of a two-dimensional [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) and its [chord](wikipedia://en/297947). The process was introduced by [Paul Lévy](wikipedia://en/556462) in 1940,[^o2] and in 1950[^o3] he computed the [characteristic function](wikipedia://en/3229401) and conditional characteristic function.

:::

The process has many unexpected connections to other objects in mathematics such as the [soliton](wikipedia://en/51654) solutions of the [Korteweg–De Vries equation](wikipedia://en/344116)[^o4] and the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809).[^o5] In the [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875), the process can be used to construct a process that is [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) in the sense of Malliavin but that has no [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) modification with respect to the [Banach norm](wikipedia://en/3989).[^o6]

## Lévy's stochastic area {#Lévy's_stochastic_area}

Let $W=(W_s^{(1)},W_s^{(2)})_{s\geq 0}$ be a two-dimensional Brownian motion in $\mathbb{R}^2$ then **Lévy's stochastic area** is the process

$$S(t,W)=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^t \left(W_s^{(1)}dW_s^{(2)}-W_s^{(2)}dW_s^{(1)}\right),$$

where the [Itō integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itō_integral) is used.[^o3]

Define the [1-Form](wikipedia://en/221519) $\vartheta=\tfrac{1}{2}(x^1dx^2-x^2dx^1)$ then $S(t,W)$ is the stochastic integral of $\vartheta$ along the curve $\varphi:[0,t]\to \mathbb{R}^2, s\mapsto (W_s^{(1)},W_s^{(2)})$

$S(t,W)=\int_{W[0,t]} \vartheta.$[^o8]

::: {#o10 .statement cue=named-heading name="Area formula"}

### Area formula {#Area_formula}

Let $x=(x_1,x_2)\in \mathbb{R}^2$, $a\in \mathbb{R}$, $b=at/2$ and $S_t=S(t,W)$ then Lévy computed

$$\mathbb{E}[\exp(iaS_t)]=\frac{1}{\cosh(b)}$$

and

$$\mathbb{E}[\exp(iaS_t)\mid W_t=x]=\frac{b}{\sinh(b)}\exp\left(\frac{\|x\|_2}{2t}\left(1-b\coth\left(b\right)\right)\right),$$

where $\|x\|_2$ is the [Euclidean norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_norm).[^o3]

:::

## Further topics {#Further_topics}

- In 1980 [Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor) found a short probabilistic proof.[^o12]
- In 1983 Helmes and Schwane found a higher-dimensional formula.[^o13]
