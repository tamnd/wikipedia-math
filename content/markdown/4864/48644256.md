# Christoph Koutschan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christoph Koutschan"}

**Christoph Koutschan** is a German mathematician and computer scientist. He is currently with the Johann Radon Institute for Computational and Applied Mathematics (RICAM) of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Christoph Koutschan (born 12 December 1978 in [Dillingen an der Donau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dillingen_an_der_Donau), Germany) is a German mathematician and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).[^o5] He studied computer science at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg) in Germany from 1999 to 2005 and then moved to the [Research Institute for Symbolic Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Institute_for_Symbolic_Computation) (RISC) in Linz, Austria, where he completed his PhD in [symbolic computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_computation) in 2009 under the supervision of [Peter Paule](wikipedia://en/45070912).[^o6][^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Koutschan is working on [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805), particularly on [holonomic functions](wikipedia://en/5817043), with applications to [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), and physics.

Together with [Doron Zeilberger](wikipedia://en/1724825) and [Manuel Kauers](wikipedia://en/40133395), Koutschan proved two famous open conjectures in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) using large scale computer algebra calculations. Both proofs appeared in the [Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_National_Academy_of_Sciences). The first concerned a conjecture formulated by Ira Gessel on the number of certain lattice walks restricted to the quarter plane. The second conjecture proven by Koutschan, Kauers, and Zeilberger was the so-called q-TSPP conjecture, a product formula for the orbit generating function of [totally symmetric plane partitions](wikipedia://en/2933308), which was formulated by [George Andrews](wikipedia://en/3356273) and [David Robbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_P._Robbins) in the early 1980s.

He is currently with the Johann Radon Institute for Computational and Applied Mathematics (RICAM) of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2016, with [Manuel Kauers](wikipedia://en/40133395) and [Doron Zeilberger](wikipedia://en/1724825) he received the [David P. Robbins Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_P._Robbins_Prize) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Koutschan is married and has two children.
