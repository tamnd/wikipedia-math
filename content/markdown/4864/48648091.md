# Michel Ledoux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Ledoux"}

*Image: Michel Ledoux 2011 Oberwolfach.jpg.* Ledoux at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2011

**Michel Ledoux** (born 1958) is a French mathematician, specializing in probability theory. He is a professor at the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse).

:::

Ledoux received in 1985 his PhD from the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) with thesis *Propriétés limites des variables aléatoires vectorielles* which was made under the supervision of [Xavier Fernique](wikipedia://en/18412809).

He has done important research on the [isoperimetric inequality](wikipedia://en/326182) in analysis and probability theory.[^o3]

In 2010, he received the Servant Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences). In 2014, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Seoul and gave a talk *Heat flows, geometric and functional inequalities*.
