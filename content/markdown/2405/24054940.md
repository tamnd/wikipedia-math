# F26A graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="F26A graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **F26A graph** is a [symmetric](wikipedia://en/1577896) [bipartite](wikipedia://en/244431) [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) with 26 vertices and 39 edges.

:::

It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, [diameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_diameter) 5, radius 5 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 6.[^o4] It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) graph. The graph is [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o5]

The F26A graph is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and can be described by the [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720) \[−7, 7\]<sup>13</sup>.

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/15094186) of the F26A graph is a group of order 78.[^o8] It acts transitively on the vertices, on the edges, and on the arcs of the graph. Therefore, the F26A graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896) (though not [distance transitive](wikipedia://en/7768222)). It has automorphisms that take any vertex to any other vertex and any edge to any other edge. According to the [Foster census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_census), the F26A graph is the only cubic symmetric graph on 26 vertices.[^o4] It is also a [Cayley graph](wikipedia://en/358277) for the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) *D*<sub>26</sub>, generated by *a*, *ab*, and *ab*<sup>4</sup>, where:[^o9]

$$D_{26} = \langle a, b | a^2 = b^{13} = 1, aba = b^{-1} \rangle .$$

The F26A graph is the smallest cubic graph where the automorphism group [acts regularly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_group_action) on arcs (that is, on edges considered as having a direction).[^o10]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the F26A graph is equal to

$$(x-3)(x+3)(x^4-5x^2+3)^6. \,$$

:::

## Other properties {#Other_properties}

The F26A graph can be embedded as a [chiral](wikipedia://en/32703814) [regular map](wikipedia://en/21533914) in the torus, with 13 hexagonal faces. The [dual graph](wikipedia://en/2536864) for this embedding is isomorphic to the [Paley graph](wikipedia://en/5719307) of order 13.
