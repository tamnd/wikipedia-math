# Blanuša snarks

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Blanuša snarks"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Blanuša snarks** are two 3-[regular graphs](wikipedia://en/85821) with 18 vertices and 27 edges. They were discovered by [Yugoslavian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Danilo Blanuša](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danilo_Blanuša) in 1946 and are named after him.[^o5] When discovered, only one snark was known—the [Petersen graph](wikipedia://en/194926).

:::

As [snarks](wikipedia://en/727811), the Blanuša snarks are connected, bridgeless [cubic graphs](wikipedia://en/980508) with [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) equal to 4. Both of them have [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, diameter 4 and girth 5. They are [non-hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) but are [hypohamiltonian](wikipedia://en/18301304).[^o6] Both have [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o7] Both graphs are [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o8]

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the first Blanuša snark is of order 8 and is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) to the [Dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) *D*<sub>4</sub>, the group of symmetries of a square.

The automorphism group of the second Blanuša snark is an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of order 4 isomorphic to the [Klein four-group](wikipedia://en/41928), the [direct product](wikipedia://en/3706015) of the [Cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) **Z**/2**Z** with itself.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the first and the second Blanuša snark are respectively :

$$(x-3)(x-1)^3(x+1)(x+2)(x^4+x^3-7x^2-5x+6)(x^4+x^3-5x^2-3x+4)^2\$$

$$(x-3)(x-1)^3(x^3+2x^2-3x-5)(x^3+2x^2-x-1)(x^4+x^3-7x^2-6x+7)(x^4+x^3-5x^2-4x+3).\$$

:::

## Generalized Blanuša snarks {#Generalized_Blanuša_snarks}

There exists a generalisation of the first and second Blanuša snark in two infinite families of snarks of order 8*n*+10 denoted $B_n^1$ and $B_n^2$. The Blanuša snarks are the smallest members those two infinite families.[^o12]

In 2007, J. Mazák proved that the circular chromatic index of the type 1 generalized Blanuša snarks $B_n^1$ equals $3+{\frac {2} {n}}$.[^o13]

In 2008, M. Ghebleh proved that the circular chromatic index of the type 2 generalized Blanuša snarks $B_n^2$ equals $3+{\frac {1} {\lfloor 1+3n/2\rfloor}}$.[^o14]
