# Sectigo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sectigo"}

**Sectigo** is an American [cybersecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) company specializing in digital certificates and automated certificate lifecycle management.[^o3] It is one of the ten largest [certificate authorities](wikipedia://en/296370).[^o4] It provides public [root certificates](wikipedia://en/296362) distributed with operating systems,[^o5][^o6] and it issues [public key certificates](wikipedia://en/223796) for websites. Formerly known as **Comodo Certification Authority** (**Comodo CA**), the company was founded under [Comodo Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comodo_Group) and was later acquired by [Francisco Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Partners) in 2017 and by [GI Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GI_Partners) in 2020. Comodo CA was rebranded to Sectigo in 2018. The company has been headquartered in [Scottsdale, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottsdale,_Arizona) since 2023.

:::

## History and operations {#History_and_operations}

### Comodo CA {#Comodo_CA}

Sectigo was established as Comodo Certification Authority (Comodo CA), a [certificate authority](wikipedia://en/296370) (CA) business under the parent company [Comodo Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comodo_Group), which was founded by [Melih Abdulhayoğlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melih_Abdulhayoğlu) in 1998.[^o9][^o10] Comodo Group and Comodo CA contributed to the [Internet Engineering Task Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Engineering_Task_Force) standard for [DNS Certification Authority Authorization](wikipedia://en/46270750) Resource Records.[^o11]

In March 2011, a user account with an affiliate registration authority was compromised and was used to create a new user account that issued nine [certificate signing requests](wikipedia://en/1124588).[^o12] See Xcitium.

In 2016, Comodo CA launched the [cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing)-based Comodo Certificate Manager, a [digital certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_certificate) management platform to help companies manage [Internet of things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things) (IoT) devices.[^o15][^o13]

Comodo CA was acquired by [Francisco Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Partners) in 2017. Comodo CA and Comodo Group were legally and operationally separated. Abdulhayoğlu kept a minority ownership stake, took an observer role on Comodo CA's [board of directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors), and continued to operate Comodo Group as a distinct security business.[^o9][^o10] (Comodo Group rebranded as [Xcitium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xcitium) in 2022.[^o14]) As part of the 2017 purchase, Bill Holtz became the CEO, and [Bill Conner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Conner) of the cybersecurity company [SonicWall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SonicWall) was named the [chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_%28officer%29).[^o9][^o15]

The Comodo CA business initially continued with the Comodo brand, as leadership considered a rebrand.[^o10] Comodo CA issued approximately 55 million certificates in 2017,[^o9] bringing the total number of certificates issued to approximately 91 million, distributed to approximately 200,000 customers in 150 countries.[^o16][^o17] In 2018, the company acquired Atlanta-based CodeGuard to help customers protect websites from [malware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malware) via [backup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup), [IT disaster recovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IT_disaster_recovery), and [maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_maintenance).[^o20][^o18]

### Sectigo {#Sectigo}

Comodo CA rebranded as Sectigo in 2018, in part to avoid confusion with the parts of Comodo Group not acquired by Francisco Partners.[^o20][^o21] The company also began supporting IoT devices and introduced new products in 2018.[^o20] In 2019, Sectigo announced changes to its [channel partner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_partner) program and launched additional certifications, products, rewards, and tools.[^o22] The company also acquired the [West Des Moines, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Des_Moines,_Iowa)-based IoT security provider Icon Labs,[^o23][^o24] which merged with Sectigo's IoT Security Platform and continued as a brand and subsidiary under Sectigo.[^o25][^o26]

[GI Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GI_Partners) acquired Sectigo in 2020.[^o27] Sectigo had approximately 700,000 customers at the time.[^o28] In late 2020, Sectigo announced the purchase of the SSL certificate and web security provider SSL247, which operated in Europe and Latin America, as well as the Netherlands-based SSL certificates and digital signatures provider Xolphin. Both of the businesses initially continued to operate as brands under Sectigo.[^o29]

Sectigo acquired [Scottsdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottsdale,_Arizona)-based SiteLock in 2021.[^o3] SiteLock had approximately 70 employees in the [Phoenix metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_metropolitan_area). The acquisition of SiteLock increased Sectigo's employee count to 450 globally.[^o30] Previously based in [Roseland, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseland,_New_Jersey),[^o31][^o32] Sectigo has been headquartered in Scottsdale since 2023.[^o3] Approximately 26 employees, including 13 [programmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programmer) and [software engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineer), work in the Phoenix metropolitan area as of 2025.[^o3]

Sectigo acquired [Entrust](wikipedia://en/16088938)'s public certificate business in early 2025. Prior to the purchase, Entrust allowed its customers to use certificates from Sectigo.[^o33] The migration of Entrust's public certificate business included over half a million certificates and was completed in September 2025.[^o34] Sectigo also began offering Certificates as a Service (CaaS) in 2025.[^o35][^o36] In 2026, Sectigo began offering Sectigo Partner Platform, a multi-tenant platform intended for use by [managed service providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Managed_service_providers) and channel partners to manage digital certificates and related lifecycle processes.[^o37]

Sectigo is a [Certificate Transparency](wikipedia://en/41061790) log provider.[^o38]
