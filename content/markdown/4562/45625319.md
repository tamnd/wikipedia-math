# Anton Zorich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anton Zorich"}

Vladimirovich

*Image: Anton zorich.jpg.* Zorich in 2011

**Anton V. Zorich** (in [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): *Антон Владимирович Зорич*; born 3 September 1962) is a Russian mathematician at the [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu](wikipedia://en/57642956). He is the son of [Vladimir A. Zorich](wikipedia://en/48551031). He received his Ph.D. from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515).[^o2]

:::

He was an invited speaker at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid. The theme was: "Geodesics on flat surfaces".[^o3]

At least two of his papers concern the explanation of mathematical discoveries he made by experimenting with computers.[^o4][^o5]
