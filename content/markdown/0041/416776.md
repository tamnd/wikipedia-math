# Timeline of algorithms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of algorithms"}

The following **timeline of algorithms** outlines the development of [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) (mainly "mathematical recipes") since their inception.

:::

## Antiquity {#Antiquity}

- Before – [writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writing) about "[recipes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recipes)" (on [cooking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooking), [rituals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritual), [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) and other themes)
- c. 1700–2000 BC – Egyptians develop earliest known algorithms for [multiplying](wikipedia://en/3975814) two numbers
- c. 1600 BC – [Babylonians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylonia) develop earliest known algorithms for [factorization](wikipedia://en/82341) and finding [square roots](wikipedia://en/29208)
- c. 300 BC – [Euclid's algorithm](wikipedia://en/10377)
- c. 200 BC – the [Sieve of Eratosthenes](wikipedia://en/73415)
- 263 AD – [Gaussian elimination](wikipedia://en/13035) described by [Liu Hui](wikipedia://en/202719)

## Medieval Period {#Medieval_Period}

- 628 – [Chakravala method](wikipedia://en/4050658) described by [Brahmagupta](wikipedia://en/297206)
- c. 820 – [Al-Khawarizmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Khawarizmi) described algorithms for solving [linear equations](wikipedia://en/17570) and [quadratic equations](wikipedia://en/25175) in his *[Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Compendious_Book_on_Calculation_by_Completion_and_Balancing)*; the word *algorithm* comes from his name
- 825 – [Al-Khawarizmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Khawarizmi) described the [algorism](wikipedia://en/417534), algorithms for using the [Hindu–Arabic numeral system](wikipedia://en/3393371), in his treatise *On the Calculation with Hindu Numerals*, which was [translated into Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_translations_of_the_12th_century) as *Algoritmi de numero Indorum*, where "Algoritmi", the translator's rendition of the author's name gave rise to the word [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) *algorithmus*) with a meaning "calculation method"
- c. 850 – [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) and [frequency analysis](wikipedia://en/157934) algorithms developed by [Al-Kindi](wikipedia://en/272065) (Alkindus) in *A Manuscript on Deciphering Cryptographic Messages*, which contains algorithms on breaking [encryptions](wikipedia://en/10294) and [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244)[^o4]
- c. 1025 – [Ibn al-Haytham](wikipedia://en/1645) (Alhazen), was the first mathematician to derive the formula for the sum of the fourth [powers](wikipedia://en/99491), and in turn, he develops an algorithm for determining the general formula for the sum of any [integral](wikipedia://en/15532) powers[^o5]
- c. 1400 – [Ahmad al-Qalqashandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_al-Qalqashandi) gives a list of [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244) in his *Subh al-a'sha* which include both [substitution](wikipedia://en/48356) and [transposition](wikipedia://en/48358), and for the first time, a cipher with multiple substitutions for each [plaintext](wikipedia://en/157935) letter; he also gives an exposition on and worked example of [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715), including the use of tables of [letter frequencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_frequencies) and sets of letters which can not occur together in one word

## Before 1940 {#Before_1940}

- 1540 – [Lodovico Ferrari](wikipedia://en/18720) discovered a method to find the roots of a [quartic polynomial](wikipedia://en/308152)
- 1545 – [Gerolamo Cardano](wikipedia://en/13145) published Cardano's method for finding the roots of a [cubic polynomial](wikipedia://en/243709)
- 1614 – [John Napier](wikipedia://en/15993) develops method for performing calculations using [logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860)
- 1671 – [Newton–Raphson method](wikipedia://en/22145) developed by [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627)
- 1690 – [Newton–Raphson method](wikipedia://en/22145) independently developed by [Joseph Raphson](wikipedia://en/4849065)
- 1706 – [John Machin](wikipedia://en/274756) develops a quickly converging inverse-tangent series for π and computes π to 100 decimal places
- 1768 – [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) publishes his method for numerical integration of ordinary differential equations in problem 85 of Institutiones calculi integralis[^o7]
- 1789 – [Jurij Vega](wikipedia://en/40546) improves Machin's formula and computes π to 140 decimal places,
- 1805 – [FFT-like algorithm](wikipedia://en/352702#History) known by [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125)
- 1842 – [Ada Lovelace](wikipedia://en/974) writes the first algorithm for a computing engine
- 1903 – A [fast Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/11512) algorithm presented by [Carle David Tolmé Runge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carle_David_Tolmé_Runge)
- 1918 - [Soundex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundex)
- 1926 – [Borůvka's algorithm](wikipedia://en/197253)
- 1926 – [Primary decomposition](wikipedia://en/1208391) algorithm presented by [Grete Hermann](wikipedia://en/1208377)[^o8]
- 1927 – [Hartree–Fock method](wikipedia://en/475393) developed for simulating a quantum many-body system in a stationary state.
- 1934 – [Delaunay triangulation](wikipedia://en/8864) developed by [Boris Delaunay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Delaunay)
- 1936 – [Turing machine](wikipedia://en/30403), an [abstract machine](wikipedia://en/60492) developed by [Alan Turing](wikipedia://en/1208), with [others](wikipedia://en/30403#Models_equivalent_to_the_Turing_machine_model) developed the modern notion of *algorithm*.

## 1940s {#1940s}

- 1942 – A [fast Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/11512) algorithm developed by [G.C. Danielson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.C._Danielson) and [Cornelius Lanczos](wikipedia://en/1657748)
- 1945 – [Merge sort](wikipedia://en/20039) developed by [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942)
- 1947 – [Simplex algorithm](wikipedia://en/349458) developed by [George Dantzig](wikipedia://en/13111)

## 1950s {#1950s}

- 1950 – [Hamming codes](wikipedia://en/41226) developed by [Richard Hamming](wikipedia://en/99326)
- 1952 – [Huffman coding](wikipedia://en/13883) developed by [David A. Huffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_A._Huffman)
- 1953 – [Simulated annealing](wikipedia://en/172244) introduced by [Nicholas Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Metropolis)
- 1954 – [Radix sort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radix_sort) computer algorithm developed by [Harold H. Seward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_H._Seward)
- 1964 – [Box–Muller transform](wikipedia://en/60758) for fast generation of normally distributed numbers published by [George Edward Pelham Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Edward_Pelham_Box) and [Mervin Edgar Muller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mervin_Edgar_Muller). Independently pre-discovered by [Raymond E. A. C. Paley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_E._A._C._Paley) and [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185) in 1934.
- 1956 – [Kruskal's algorithm](wikipedia://en/53776) developed by [Joseph Kruskal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kruskal)
- 1956 – [Ford–Fulkerson algorithm](wikipedia://en/53777) developed and published by [R. Ford Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Ford_Jr.) and [D. R. Fulkerson](wikipedia://en/239238)
- 1957 – [Prim's algorithm](wikipedia://en/53783) developed by [Robert Prim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Prim)
- 1957 – [Bellman–Ford algorithm](wikipedia://en/221244) developed by [Richard E. Bellman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Bellman) and [L. R. Ford, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._R._Ford,_Jr.)
- 1959 – [Dijkstra's algorithm](wikipedia://en/45809) developed by [Edsger Dijkstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edsger_Dijkstra)
- 1959 – [Shell sort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_sort) developed by [Donald L. Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_L._Shell)
- 1959 – [De Casteljau's algorithm](wikipedia://en/656099) developed by [Paul de Casteljau](wikipedia://en/2268580)
- 1959 – [QR factorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_factorization) [algorithm](wikipedia://en/594072) developed independently by [John G.F. Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_G.F._Francis) and [Vera Kublanovskaya](wikipedia://en/18387851)[^o11][^o12]
- 1959 – [Rabin–Scott powerset construction](wikipedia://en/1163167) for converting [NFA](wikipedia://en/653406) into [DFA](wikipedia://en/653404) published by [Michael O. Rabin](wikipedia://en/298404) and [Dana Scott](wikipedia://en/161875)

## 1960s {#1960s}

- 1960 – [Karatsuba multiplication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karatsuba_multiplication)
- 1961 – [CRC (Cyclic redundancy check)](wikipedia://en/38838) invented by [W. Wesley Peterson](wikipedia://en/10901372)
- 1962 – [AVL trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AVL_tree)
- 1962 – [Quicksort](wikipedia://en/3268249) developed by [C. A. R. Hoare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._A._R._Hoare)

- 1962 – [Gale–Shapley 'stable-marriage' algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_marriage_problem) developed by [David Gale](wikipedia://en/1227675) and [Lloyd Shapley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Shapley)
- 1964 – [Heapsort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heapsort) developed by [J. W. J. Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._W._J._Williams)
- 1964 – [multigrid methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multigrid_methods) first proposed by [R. P. Fedorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._P._Fedorenko)
- 1965 – [Cooley–Tukey algorithm](wikipedia://en/352702) rediscovered by [James Cooley](wikipedia://en/1565795) and [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974)
- 1965 – [Levenshtein distance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levenshtein_distance) developed by [Vladimir Levenshtein](wikipedia://en/3072406)
- 1965 – [Cocke–Younger–Kasami (CYK) algorithm](wikipedia://en/53929) independently developed by [Tadao Kasami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadao_Kasami)
- 1965 – [Buchberger's algorithm](wikipedia://en/696317) for computing [Gröbner bases](wikipedia://en/357328) developed by [Bruno Buchberger](wikipedia://en/1924233)
- 1965 – [LR parsers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LR_parser) invented by [Donald Knuth](wikipedia://en/8095)
- 1966 – [Dantzig algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dantzig_algorithm) for shortest path in a graph with negative edges
- 1967 – [Viterbi algorithm](wikipedia://en/228015) proposed by [Andrew Viterbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Viterbi)
- 1967 – [Cocke–Younger–Kasami (CYK) algorithm](wikipedia://en/53929) independently developed by [Daniel H. Younger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_H._Younger)
- 1968 – [A\* graph search algorithm](wikipedia://en/100558) described by [Peter Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_E._Hart), [Nils Nilsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nils_Nilsson_%28researcher%29), and [Bertram Raphael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertram_Raphael)
- 1968 – [Risch algorithm](wikipedia://en/289450) for indefinite integration developed by [Robert Henry Risch](wikipedia://en/62778769)
- 1969 – [Strassen algorithm](wikipedia://en/771965) for matrix multiplication developed by [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960)

## 1970s {#1970s}

- 1970 – [Dinic's algorithm](wikipedia://en/23635821) for computing maximum flow in a flow network by Yefim (Chaim) A. Dinitz
- 1970 – [Knuth–Bendix completion algorithm](wikipedia://en/614147) developed by [Donald Knuth](wikipedia://en/8095) and [Peter B. Bendix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_B._Bendix)
- 1970 – [BFGS method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BFGS_method) of the [quasi-Newton](wikipedia://en/7883127) class
- 1970 – [Needleman–Wunsch algorithm](wikipedia://en/1004679) published by [Saul B. Needleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_B._Needleman) and [Christian D. Wunsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_D._Wunsch)
- 1972 – [Edmonds–Karp algorithm](wikipedia://en/239230) published by [Jack Edmonds](wikipedia://en/3625459) and [Richard Karp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Karp), essentially identical to [Dinic's algorithm](wikipedia://en/23635821) from 1970
- 1972 – [Graham scan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_scan) developed by [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694)
- 1972 – [Red–black trees](wikipedia://en/26397) and [B-trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B-tree) discovered
- 1973 – [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28algorithm%29) encryption algorithm discovered by [Clifford Cocks](wikipedia://en/578040)
- 1973 – [Jarvis march](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarvis_march) algorithm developed by [R. A. Jarvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._A._Jarvis)
- 1973 – [Hopcroft–Karp algorithm](wikipedia://en/5944391) developed by [John Hopcroft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hopcroft) and [Richard Karp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Karp)
- 1974 – [Pollard's *p* − 1 algorithm](wikipedia://en/578753) developed by [John Pollard](wikipedia://en/1068500)
- 1974 – [Quadtree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadtree) developed by [Raphael Finkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_Finkel) and [J.L. Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.L._Bentley)
- 1975 – [Genetic algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_algorithm) popularized by [John Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Henry_Holland)
- 1975 – [Pollard's rho algorithm](wikipedia://en/580668) developed by [John Pollard](wikipedia://en/1068500)
- 1975 – [Aho–Corasick string matching algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aho–Corasick_string_matching_algorithm) developed by [Alfred V. Aho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_V._Aho) and [Margaret J. Corasick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_J._Corasick)
- 1975 – [Cylindrical algebraic decomposition](wikipedia://en/12218154) developed by [George E. Collins](wikipedia://en/41768958)
- 1976 – [Salamin–Brent algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamin–Brent_algorithm) independently discovered by [Eugene Salamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Salamin_%28mathematician%29) and [Richard Brent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Brent_%28scientist%29)
- 1976 – [Knuth–Morris–Pratt algorithm](wikipedia://en/253227) developed by [Donald Knuth](wikipedia://en/8095) and [Vaughan Pratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaughan_Pratt) and independently by [J. H. Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._H._Morris)
- 1977 – [Boyer–Moore string-search algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyer–Moore_string-search_algorithm) for searching the occurrence of a string into another string.
- 1977 – [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28algorithm%29) encryption algorithm rediscovered by [Ron Rivest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Rivest), [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314), and [Len Adleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Len_Adleman)
- 1977 – [LZ77](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZ77) algorithm developed by [Abraham Lempel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lempel) and [Jacob Ziv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Ziv)
- 1977 – [multigrid methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multigrid_methods) developed independently by [Achi Brandt](wikipedia://en/4102320) and [Wolfgang Hackbusch](wikipedia://en/27379358)
- 1978 – [LZ78](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZ78) algorithm developed from [LZ77](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZ77) by [Abraham Lempel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lempel) and [Jacob Ziv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Ziv)
- 1978 – [Bruun's algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruun's_FFT_algorithm) proposed for powers of two by [Georg Bruun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Bruun)
- 1979 – Khachiyan's [ellipsoid method](wikipedia://en/4331203) developed by [Leonid Khachiyan](wikipedia://en/565462)
- 1979 – [ID3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ID3_algorithm) decision tree algorithm developed by [Ross Quinlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Quinlan)

## 1980s {#1980s}

- 1980 – [Brent's Algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cycle_detection#Brent.27s_algorithm) for cycle detection [Richard P. Brendt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_P._Brent)
- 1981 – [Quadratic sieve](wikipedia://en/582340) developed by [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159)
- 1981 – [Smith–Waterman algorithm](wikipedia://en/1606195) developed by [Temple F. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_F._Smith) and [Michael S. Waterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_S._Waterman)
- 1983 – [Simulated annealing](wikipedia://en/172244) developed by [S. Kirkpatrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._Kirkpatrick), [C. D. Gelatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._D._Gelatt) and [M. P. Vecchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._P._Vecchi)
- 1983 – [Classification and regression tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_and_regression_tree) (CART) algorithm developed by [Leo Breiman](wikipedia://en/4909283), *et al.*
- 1984 – [LZW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lempel–Ziv–Welch) algorithm developed from [LZ78](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZ78) by [Terry Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Welch)
- 1984 – [Karmarkar's interior-point algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karmarkar's_interior-point_algorithm) developed by [Narendra Karmarkar](wikipedia://en/1162259)
- 1984 – [ACORN PRNG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACORN_PRNG) discovered by Roy Wikramaratna and used privately
- 1985 – [Simulated annealing](wikipedia://en/172244) independently developed by [V. Cerny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._Cerny)
- 1985 – [Car–Parrinello molecular dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car–Parrinello_molecular_dynamics) developed by [Roberto Car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Car) and [Michele Parrinello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Parrinello)
- 1985 – [Splay trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splay_tree) discovered by [Sleator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Dominic_Sleator) and [Tarjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Endre_Tarjan)
- 1986 – [Blum Blum Shub](wikipedia://en/39599) proposed by [L. Blum](wikipedia://en/1884319), [M. Blum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Blum), and [M. Shub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shub)
- 1986 – [Push relabel maximum flow algorithm](wikipedia://en/3444072) by Andrew Goldberg and Robert Tarjan
- 1986 – [Barnes–Hut tree method](wikipedia://en/9394772) developed by [Josh Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Barnes) and [Piet Hut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piet_Hut) for fast approximate simulation of [n-body problems](wikipedia://en/23859945)
- 1987 – [Fast multipole method](wikipedia://en/8280771) developed by [Leslie Greengard](wikipedia://en/30091589) and [Vladimir Rokhlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Rokhlin_%28American_scientist%29)
- 1988 – [Special number field sieve](wikipedia://en/589132) developed by [John Pollard](wikipedia://en/1068500)
- 1989 – [ACORN PRNG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACORN_PRNG) published by Roy Wikramaratna
- 1989 – [Paxos protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paxos_%28computer_science%29) developed by [Leslie Lamport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Lamport)
- 1989 – [Skip list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skip_list) discovered by [William Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Pugh_%28computer_scientist%29)

## 1990s {#1990s}

- 1990 – [General number field sieve](wikipedia://en/152734) developed from [SNFS](wikipedia://en/589132) by [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159), [Joe Buhler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Buhler), [Hendrik Lenstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lenstra), and [Leonard Adleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Adleman)
- 1990 – [Coppersmith–Winograd algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppersmith–Winograd_algorithm) developed by [Don Coppersmith](wikipedia://en/540090) and [Shmuel Winograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shmuel_Winograd)
- 1990 – [BLAST algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLAST_%28biotechnology%29) developed by [Stephen Altschul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Altschul), [Warren Gish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Gish), [Webb Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webb_Miller), [Eugene Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Myers), and [David J. Lipman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_J._Lipman) from [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health)
- 1991 – [Wait-free synchronization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock-free_and_wait-free_algorithms) developed by [Maurice Herlihy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Herlihy)
- 1992 – [Deutsch–Jozsa algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsch–Jozsa_algorithm) proposed by [D. Deutsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._Deutsch) and [Richard Jozsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Jozsa)
- 1992 – [C4.5 algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C4.5_algorithm), a descendant of [ID3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ID3_algorithm) decision tree algorithm, was developed by [Ross Quinlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Quinlan)
- 1993 – [Apriori algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apriori_algorithm) developed by Rakesh Agrawal and Ramakrishnan Srikant

- 1994 – [Shor's algorithm](wikipedia://en/42674) developed by [Peter Shor](wikipedia://en/68338)
- 1994 – [Burrows–Wheeler transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burrows–Wheeler_transform) developed by [Michael Burrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Burrows_%28computer_scientist%29) and [David Wheeler](wikipedia://en/532495)

- 1995 – soft-margin [support vector machine](wikipedia://en/65309) algorithm was published by [Vladimir Vapnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Vapnik) and [Corinna Cortes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinna_Cortes). - 1995 – [Ukkonen's algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukkonen's_algorithm) for construction of suffix trees
- 1996 – [Bruun's algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruun's_FFT_algorithm) generalized to arbitrary even composite sizes by [H. Murakami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Murakami)
- 1996 – [Grover's algorithm](wikipedia://en/58498) developed by [Lov K. Grover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lov_K._Grover)
- 1996 – [RIPEMD-160](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIPEMD-160) developed by [Hans Dobbertin](wikipedia://en/2210027), [Antoon Bosselaers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoon_Bosselaers), and [Bart Preneel](wikipedia://en/1520490)
- 1997 – [Mersenne Twister](wikipedia://en/45538) a pseudo random number generator developed by [Makoto Matsumoto](wikipedia://en/73756160) and [Tajuki Nishimura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajuki_Nishimura)
- 1998 – [PageRank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PageRank) algorithm was published by [Larry Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Page)
- 1998 – [rsync algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rsync_algorithm) developed by [Andrew Tridgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Tridgell)
- 1999 – [gradient boosting](wikipedia://en/26649339) algorithm developed by [Jerome H. Friedman](wikipedia://en/40671069)
- 1999 – [Yarrow algorithm](wikipedia://en/74416) designed by [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732), [John Kelsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Kelsey_%28cryptanalyst%29), and [Niels Ferguson](wikipedia://en/204933)

## 2000s {#2000s}

- 2000 – [Hyperlink-induced topic search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HITS_algorithm) a hyperlink analysis algorithm developed by Jon Kleinberg
- 2001 – [Lempel–Ziv–Markov chain algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lempel–Ziv–Markov_chain_algorithm) for compression developed by [Igor Pavlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Pavlov_%28programmer%29)
- 2001 – [Viola–Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola–Jones_object_detection_framework) algorithm for real-time face detection was developed by Paul Viola and Michael Jones.
- 2001 – [DHT (Distributed hash table)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_hash_table) is invented by multiple people from academia and application systems
- 2001 – [BitTorrent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitTorrent) a first fully decentralized peer-to-peer file distribution system is published
- 2001 – [LOBPCG](wikipedia://en/26951185) Locally Optimal Block Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient method finding extreme eigenvalues of symmetric eigenvalue problems by [Andrew Knyazev](wikipedia://en/26948388)
- 2002 – [AKS primality test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AKS_primality_test) developed by [Manindra Agrawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manindra_Agrawal), [Neeraj Kayal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neeraj_Kayal) and [Nitin Saxena](wikipedia://en/2297722)
- 2002 – [Girvan–Newman algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girvan–Newman_algorithm) to detect communities in complex systems
- 2002 – [Packrat parser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packrat_parser) developed for generating a parser that parses [PEG (Parsing expression grammar)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsing_expression_grammar) in linear time parsing developed by [Bryan Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Ford)
- 2009 – [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265) a first trust-less decentralized cryptocurrency system is published

## 2010s {#2010s}

- 2013 – [Raft consensus protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raft_%28computer_science%29) published by [Diego Ongaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Ongaro) and [John Ousterhout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ousterhout)
- 2015 – YOLO (“[You Only Look Once](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Only_Look_Once)”) is an effective real-time object recognition algorithm, first described by [Joseph Redmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Redmon) et al.[^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23]
