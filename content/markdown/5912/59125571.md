# Andrew C. Berry

Andrew C. Berry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew C. Berry"}

**Andrew Campbell Berry** (November 23, 1906 – January 13, 1998) was an American mathematician. The [Berry–Esseen theorem](wikipedia://en/508012) is named after him.

:::

Berry was born in [Somerville, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerville,_Massachusetts), US on November 23, 1906. He spent eight years (1921–1929) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), receiving his A.B. degree in 1925, A.M. degree in 1926, and a Ph.D. in 1929. After two years at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) on a National Research Fellowship, he joined the faculty of [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1931, where he was assistant professor from 1935 to 1941. In 1941, he joined [Lawrence University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_University) as associate professor.[^o3]

In 1944, during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), the university "loaned" him to the 5th and 13th Air Forces in the [Pacific War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War). There he worked as an operations analyst, including "development of an improved gunsight for waist-gunners on B-24 aircraft". He received the [Medal of Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_Freedom) in 1946 for his actions during the [Battle of Guadalcanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Guadalcanal).[^o4]

Berry died in [Appleton, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appleton,_Wisconsin) on January 13, 1998.[^o4]
