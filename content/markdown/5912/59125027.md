# Joseph A. Ball (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph A. Ball"}

**Joseph Anthony Ball** (born 4 June 1947) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is currently a professor emeritus [^o4] at the [Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Polytechnic_Institute_and_State_University), often referred to as [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech). He is a 2019 fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for contributions to [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [analytic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_functions), and service to the profession.[^o5]

:::

He was awarded Bachelor of Science from [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University) in 1969. He obtained his Master of Science in 1970 and Doctor of Philosophy in 1973 both from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), [Charlottesville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottesville). The title of his doctoral dissertation is "Unitary Perturbations of Contractions", supervised by Marvin Rosenblum.[^o6] According to current [Mathematics Genealogy Project](wikipedia://en/1152126) database, Ball has 12 students and 18 descendants.[^o7]
