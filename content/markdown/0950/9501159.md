# Well-pointed category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Well-pointed category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a category with a [terminal object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_object) $1$ is **well-pointed** if for every pair of arrows $f,g:A\to B$ such that $f\neq g$, there is an arrow $p:1\to A$ such that $f\circ p\neq g\circ p$. (The arrows $p$ are called the [global elements](wikipedia://en/14717987) or *points* of the category; a well-pointed category is thus one that has "enough points" to distinguish non-equal arrows.)

:::
