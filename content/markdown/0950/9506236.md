# Truncated differential cryptanalysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated differential cryptanalysis"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **truncated differential cryptanalysis** is a generalization of [differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536), an attack against [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594). [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen) developed the technique in 1994. Whereas ordinary differential cryptanalysis analyzes the full difference between two texts, the truncated variant considers differences that are only partially determined. That is, the attack makes predictions of only some of the bits instead of the full block. This technique has been applied to [SAFER](wikipedia://en/630497), [IDEA](wikipedia://en/15076), [Skipjack](wikipedia://en/474702), [E2](wikipedia://en/8441917), [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356), [Camellia](wikipedia://en/532475), [CRYPTON](wikipedia://en/7237011), and even the [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) [Salsa20](wikipedia://en/2701329).

:::
