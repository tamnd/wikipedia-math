# Kaplansky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kaplansky"}

**Kaplansky** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Abraham Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Kaplansky) (1860–1939), Canadian printer
- [Kalmen Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalmen_Kaplansky) (1912–1997), Canadian labour and human rights activist
- [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky) (1917–2006), Canadian mathematician
- [Lucy Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Kaplansky) (born 1960), an American folk musician, daughter of Irving Kaplansky
- [Shlomo Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shlomo_Kaplansky) (1884–1950), Zionist politician and president of Technion – Israel Institute of Technology
