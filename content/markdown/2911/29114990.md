# Robert Minlos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Minlos"}

**Robert Adol'fovich Minlos** (Роберт Адольфович Минлос; 28 February 1931 – 9 January 2018) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has made important contributions to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). [His theorem](wikipedia://en/14816679) on the extension of [cylindrical measures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylindrical_measure) to [Radon measures](wikipedia://en/521933) on the [continuous dual](wikipedia://en/7988) of a [nuclear space](wikipedia://en/2702957) is of fundamental importance in the theory of [generalized random processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_random_process).

:::

He died on 9 January 2018 at the age of 86.[^o2]
