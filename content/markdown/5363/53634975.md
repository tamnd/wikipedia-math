# Franck Barthe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Franck Barthe"}

**Franck Barthe** is a French mathematician.[^o3] He was awarded the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) (ECM) prize in 2004.[^o4] He is working as a professor of mathematics at [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Franck Barthe is known for his reverse form of the [Brascamp-Lieb inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brascamp-Lieb_inequality).[^o6] With [Keith M. Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Martin_Ball), [Shiri Artstein](wikipedia://en/58836710), and [Assaf Naor](wikipedia://en/38620456), he solved Shannon's problem of the monotonic [entropy](wikipedia://en/15445) increase of sums of [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685).[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2004, he received the EMS Prize (prize presentation: [isoperimetric inequalities](wikipedia://en/326182), [probability measures](wikipedia://en/45437) and [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882)) for his leading role in the application of mass-theoretical transport techniques.[^o4]

In 2005, he received the [Grand Prix Jacques Herbrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_Jacques_Herbrand).
