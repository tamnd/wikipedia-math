# Bengt Beckman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bengt Beckman"}

**Bengt Beckman** (30 September 1925 – 13 March 2012) was a Swedish mathematician, university professor and bestselling author.

:::

He worked as a research engineer at Tandem laboratory in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), Sweden.[^o2] As an author, he wrote about [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) in Sweden, particularly about [Arne Beurling](wikipedia://en/286530).

## Publications {#Publications}

Some of his books and papers are:[^o4]

- *Swedish cryptography achievements*, [Bonniers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonniers), 1996,
- *The world's first encryption machine - Gripenstiernas cipher-Machine 1786*, the National Defense Radio Establishment, [Bromma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromma), 1999
- *Thus the Z machine was broken - reconstruction of the Lorenz SZ40 / 42*, the National Defense Radio Establishment, Bromma, 1999
- *Swedish Signal Intelligence: 1900-1945*, [Frank Cass Publishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Cass_Publishers), 2002,
- *In front of your eyes - drawings and comments*, [Johanneshov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johanneshov), 2010
