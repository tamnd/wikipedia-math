# Gady Kozma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gady Kozma"}

**Gady Kozma** (גדי קוזמא) is an Israeli mathematician. Kozma obtained his PhD in 2001 at the [University of Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) with [Alexander Olevskii](wikipedia://en/68265421). He is a scientist at the [Weizmann Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science). In 2005, he demonstrated the existence of the scaling limit value (that is, for increasingly finer lattices) of the *[loop-erased random walk](wikipedia://en/1109958)* in three dimensions and its invariance under rotations and dilations.[^o4]

:::

A loop-erased random walk consists of a random walk, whose loops, which form when it intersects itself, are removed. This was introduced to the study of self-avoiding random walk by [Gregory Lawler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lawler) in 1980,[^o5] but is an independent model in another universality class. In the two-dimensional case, conformal invariance was proved by Lawler, [Oded Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580) and [Wendelin Werner](wikipedia://en/6607797) (with [Schramm–Loewner evolution](wikipedia://en/6706108)) in 2004.[^o6] The cases of four and more dimensions were treated by Lawler, the scale limiting value is Brownian motion, in four dimensions. Kozma treated the two-dimensional case in 2002 with a new method. In addition to probability theory, he also deals with Fourier series.[^o7]

In 2008 he received the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) and in 2010 the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize). He is an editor of the *[Journal d'Analyse Mathématique](wikipedia://en/71721837)*.[^o8]

In 2024 together with Shahaf Nitzan he published a reduction of [$\theta(p_c)=0$ conjecture](wikipedia://en/83117639) to various [correlation inequalities](wikipedia://en/20283423) decreasing in strength[^o9]. They are supported by numerical evidence, and if true, any of them will resolve the conjecture.
