# Ronald Coifman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald Coifman"}

**Ronald Raphael Coifman** (רונלד קויפמן; born June 29, 1941) is a [Sterling professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_professor) of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). Coifman earned a doctorate from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in 1965, supervised by [Jovan Karamata](wikipedia://en/3130497).

:::

Coifman is a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences), the [Connecticut Academy of Science and Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Academy_of_Science_and_Engineering), and the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences). He is a recipient of the 1996 [DARPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DARPA) Sustained Excellence Award, the 1996 Connecticut Science Medal, the 1999 Pioneer Award of the International Society for Industrial and Applied Science, and the 1999 [National Medal of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medal_of_Science).[^o5][^o6]

Prior to teaching at Yale, Coifman taught at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o7]

In 2013, he co-founded [ThetaRay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ThetaRay), a [cyber security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) and [big data analytics](wikipedia://en/27051151) company.[^o8]

In 2018, he received the [Rolf Schock Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Schock_Prizes) for Mathematics.[^o9] In 2024 he was awarded the [George David Birkhoff Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_David_Birkhoff_Prize).[^o10]
