# N. G. W. H. Beeger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="N. G. W. H. Beeger"}

**Nicolaas George Wijnand Henri Beeger** (1884, in [Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_%28city%29) – 1965, in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam)) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His 1916 [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) was on [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258). He worked for most of his life as a teacher, working on mathematics papers in his spare evenings. After his retirement as a teacher at 65, he began corresponding with many contemporary mathematicians and dedicated himself to his work.

:::

He is known for having proved that 3511 is a [Wieferich prime](wikipedia://en/323631) in 1922 [^o2] and for introducing the term [Carmichael number](wikipedia://en/7723) in 1950.

## Beeger Lecture {#Beeger_Lecture}

In 1989 the board of trustees of the Mathematical Centre in Amsterdam established the **Beeger lectures**, in honor of N.G.W.H. Beeger, to be held biannually at the congress of the [Royal Dutch Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/43504918). Their purpose is to promote research and exchange of ideas in the field of algorithmic and computational number theory. The first Beeger Lecture was delivered in 1992.
