# Simplicial Lie algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simplicial Lie algebra"}

In algebra, a **simplicial Lie algebra** is a [simplicial object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_object) in the category of [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944). In particular, it is a [simplicial abelian group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_abelian_group), and thus is subject to the [Dold–Kan correspondence](wikipedia://en/39431504).

:::
