# Moduli stack of formal group laws

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moduli stack of formal group laws"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **moduli stack of formal group laws** is a [stack](wikipedia://en/13185596) classifying [formal group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_group) laws and isomorphisms between them. It is denoted by $\mathcal{M}_{\text{FG}}$. It is a "geometric object" that underlies the [chromatic](wikipedia://en/42245935) approach to the [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), a branch of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

Currently, it is not known whether $\mathcal{M}_{\text{FG}}$ is a [derived stack](wikipedia://en/42074131) or not. Hence, it is typical to work with stratifications. Let $\mathcal{M}^n_{\text{FG}}$ be given so that $\mathcal{M}^n_{\text{FG}}(R)$ consists of formal group laws over *R* of height exactly *n*. They form a stratification of the moduli stack $\mathcal{M}_{\text{FG}}$. $\operatorname{Spec} \overline{\mathbb{F}_p} \to \mathcal{M}^n_{\text{FG}}$ is [faithfully flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithfully_flat_morphism). In fact, $\mathcal{M}^n_{\text{FG}}$ is of the form $\operatorname{Spec} \overline{\mathbb{F}_p} / \operatorname{Aut}(\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}, f)$ where $\operatorname{Aut}(\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}, f)$ is a [profinite group](wikipedia://en/24266) called the **Morava stabilizer group**. The [Lubin–Tate theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubin–Tate_theory) describes how the strata $\mathcal{M}^n_{\text{FG}}$ fit together.
