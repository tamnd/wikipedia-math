# Chiral Lie algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chiral Lie algebra"}

In algebra, a **chiral Lie algebra** is a [D-module](wikipedia://en/3440755) on a curve with a certain structure of [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944). It is related to an [$\mathcal{E}_2$-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E_n_ring) via the [Riemann–Hilbert correspondence](wikipedia://en/10478956).

:::
