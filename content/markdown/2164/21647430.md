# David Budescu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Budescu"}

**David Budescu** (דוד בודסקו) is a [psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist) and [academic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_staff). He is the Anne Anastasi Professor of Psychometrics and Quantitative Psychology at [Fordham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordham_University).

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Budescu graduated from the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), Israel and received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) from the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina) in 1980.[^o3] From 1982 to 1992 he taught at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) and, in 1992, accepted an appointment at the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois).[^o4] He is a fellow of the [American Psychological Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Society) and is a past director of the [European Association of Decision Making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_of_Decision_Making). In 2016 he won the [Exeter Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_Prize) for the best paper published in the fields of [experimental economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_economics), [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216), and [behavioural economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioural_economics).[^o5] In 1999 he edited "Games and Human Behavior: Essays in Honor of Amnon Rapoport."

## Current work {#Current_work}

Budescu researches the effects of uncertainty in human decision-making as well as psychometrics.[^o7] He has also done work on group decision-making and markets.[^o8] Budescu is currently the editor of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association) journal Decision.[^o9] He also sits on the editorial board of the [Journal of Behavioral Decision Making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Behavioral_Decision_Making). In 2009 he was appointed Anne Anastasi Professor of Psychometrics and Quantitative Psychology at [Fordham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordham_University).[^o10] His regular collaborators include [Amnon Rapoport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amnon_Rapoport), [Boris Maciejovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Maciejovsky), and [Dan Ariely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Ariely).
