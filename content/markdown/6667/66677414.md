# Martin Schlichenmaier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Schlichenmaier"}

**Martin Schlichenmaier** is a German-Luxembourgish mathematician whose research deals with algebraic, geometric and analytic mathematical methods which partly have relations to theoretical and mathematical physics.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

In 1990 Schlichenmaier earned a doctoral degree in mathematics at the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim) with [Rainer Weissauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainer_Weissauer) with the thesis *Verallgemeinerte Krichever – Novikov Algebren und deren Darstellungen*.[^o5] His research topics are, beside other fields, the geometric foundations of quantisation, e.g. [Berezin-Toeplitz-Quantisierung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantization_%28physics%29) and infinite dimensional [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) of geometric origin, like the algebras of Krichever- Novikov type. [^o6]

From 1986 until 2003 he worked at the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim). In the year 1996 he habilitated with the thesis *Zwei Anwendungen algebraisch-geometrischer Methoden in der theoretischen Physik: Berezin-Toeplitz-Quantisierung und globale Algebren der zweidimensionalen konformen Feldtheorie* [^o7]

Since 2003 he has been professor at the [University of Luxemburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg),[^o8] recently as Emeritus . From 2005 until 2017 he was director of the Mathematical Research Unit, Department of Mathematics [^o9] at the University of Luxemburg. He is a member of the editorial boards of the mathematical journals *Journal of Lie Theory*,[^o10] and *Analysis and Mathematical Physics*[^o11]

From 2010 until 2022 he was president of the Luxembourgish Mathematical Society, SML.[^o12] He received the Grand Prix 2016 en sciences mathematiques de L'Institut Grand-Ducal -prix de la Bourse de Luxembourg.[^o13] 2019 he was appointed as full member of the [Institut Grand-Ducal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Ducal_Institute), Section des Sciences[^o14]
