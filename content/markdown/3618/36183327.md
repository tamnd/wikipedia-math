# Jean-Louis Loday

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Louis Loday"}

**Jean-Louis Loday** (12 January 1946 – 6 June 2012) was a French mathematician who worked on [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172) and who introduced [Leibniz algebras](wikipedia://en/5365466) (sometimes called Loday algebras) and [Zinbiel algebras](wikipedia://en/36166886).[^o3] He occasionally used the pseudonym **Guillaume William Zinbiel**, formed by reversing the last name of [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Loday studied at [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand) and at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) in 1975 under the supervision of [Max Karoubi](wikipedia://en/30942178), with a dissertation titled *K-Théorie algébrique et représentations de groupes*. He went on to become a senior scientist at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) and a member of the Institute for Advanced Mathematical Research (IRMA) at the University of Strasbourg.
