# Gossard perspector

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gossard perspector"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) the **Gossard perspector**[^o2] (also called the **Zeeman–Gossard perspector**[^o3]) is a special point associated with a [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) and it is designated as X(402) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593). The point was named *Gossard perspector* by [John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) in 1998 in honour of [Harry Clinton Gossard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Clinton_Gossard) who discovered its existence in 1916. Later it was learned that the point had appeared in an article by [Christopher Zeeman](wikipedia://en/871415) published during 1899 – 1902. From 2003 onwards the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers has been referring to this point as *Zeeman–Gossard perspector*.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: Gossard perspector.svg.* *H*, *H<sub>A</sub>*, *H<sub>B</sub>*, *H<sub>C</sub>*, *H<sub>g</sub>* are [orthocenters](wikipedia://en/161244), and *G*, *G<sub>A</sub>*, *G<sub>B</sub>*, *G<sub>C</sub>*, *G<sub>g</sub>* are centroids of triangles *ABC*, *AEF*, *BFD*, *CDE*, *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* respectively.

### Gossard triangle {#Gossard_triangle}

Let *ABC* be any triangle. Let the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622) of triangle *ABC* meet the sidelines *BC*, *CA* and *AB* of triangle *ABC* at *D*, *E* and *F* respectively. Let *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* be the triangle formed by the Euler lines of the triangles *AEF*, *BFD* and *CDE*, the vertex *A<sub>g</sub>* being the intersection of the Euler lines of the triangles *BFD* and *CDE*, and similarly for the other two vertices. The triangle *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* is called the **Gossard triangle** of triangle *ABC*.[^o7]

### Gossard perspector {#Gossard_perspector}

Let *ABC* be any triangle and let *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* be its Gossard triangle. Then the lines *AA<sub>g</sub>*, *BB<sub>g</sub>* and *CC<sub>g</sub>* are concurrent. The point of concurrence is called the *Gossard perspector* of triangle *ABC*.

:::

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Let *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* be the Gossard triangle of triangle *ABC*. The lines *B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>*, *C<sub>g</sub>A<sub>g</sub>* and *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>* are respectively parallel to the lines *BC*, *CA* and *AB*.[^o11]
- Any triangle and its Gossard triangle are congruent.
- Any triangle and its Gossard triangle have the same Euler line.
- The Gossard triangle of triangle *ABC* is the reflection of triangle *ABC* in the Gossard perspector.

### Trilinear coordinates {#Trilinear_coordinates}

The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the Gossard perspector of triangle *ABC* are

( *f* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) : *f* ( *b*, *c*, *a* ) : *f* ( *c*, *a*, *b* ) )

where

*f* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) = *p* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) *y* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) / *a*

where

*p* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) = 2*a*<sup>4</sup> − *a*<sup>2</sup>*b*<sup>2</sup> − *a*<sup>2</sup>*c*<sup>2</sup> − ( *b*<sup>2</sup> − *c*<sup>2</sup> )<sup>2</sup>

and

*y* ( *a*, *b*, *c* ) = *a*<sup>8</sup> − *a*<sup>6</sup> ( *b*<sup>2</sup> + *c*<sup>2</sup> ) + *a*<sup>4</sup> ( 2*b*<sup>2</sup> − *c*<sup>2</sup> ) ( 2*c*<sup>2</sup> − *b*<sup>2</sup> ) + ( *b*<sup>2</sup> − *c*<sup>2</sup> )<sup>2</sup> \[ 3*a*<sup>2</sup> ( *b*<sup>2</sup> + *c*<sup>2</sup> ) − *b*<sup>4</sup> − *c*<sup>4</sup> − 3*b*<sup>2</sup>*c*<sup>2</sup> \]

*Image: Gossard perspector generalisation.gif.* In the figure, *DEF* is the Euler line of triangle *ABC*. The line *XYZ* moves parallel to the line *DEF*. The triangle *A'B'C'* remains congruent to triangle *ABC* whatever be the position of the line *XYZ*. The blue 'inverted' triangle is the Gossard triangle of triangle *ABC*.

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

The construction yielding the Gossard triangle of a triangle *ABC* can be generalised to produce triangles *A'B'C'* which are [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) to triangle *ABC* and whose sidelines are [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to the sidelines of triangle *ABC*.

### Zeeman’s Generalization {#Zeeman’s_Generalization}

This result is due to Christopher Zeeman.[^o11]

Let *l* be any line parallel to the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622) of triangle *ABC*. Let *l* intersect the sidelines *BC*, *CA*, *AB* of triangle *ABC* at *X*, *Y*, *Z* respectively. Let *A'B'C'* be the triangle formed by the Euler lines of the triangles *AYZ*, *BZX* and *CXY*. Then triangle *A'B'C'* is congruent to triangle *ABC* and its sidelines are parallel to the sidelines of triangle *ABC*.[^o11]

### Yiu’s Generalization {#Yiu’s_Generalization}

*Image: Gossard perspector generalisation02.svg.* Paul Yiu's generalisation of Gossard triangle.

This generalisation is due to [Paul Yiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Yiu).[^o2][^o17]

Let *P* be any point in the plane of the [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) *ABC* different from its [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) *G*.

Let the line *PG* meet the sidelines *BC*, *CA* and *AB* at *X*, *Y* and *Z* respectively.

Let the centroids of the triangles *AYZ*, *BZX* and *CXY* be *G<sub>a</sub>*, *G<sub>b</sub>* and *G<sub>c</sub>* respectively.

Let *P<sub>a</sub>* be a point such that *YP<sub>a</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *CP* and *ZP<sub>a</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *BP*.

Let *P<sub>b</sub>* be a point such that *ZP<sub>b</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *AP* and *XP<sub>b</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *CP*.

Let *P<sub>c</sub>* be a point such that *XP<sub>c</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *BP* and *YP<sub>c</sub>* is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *AP*.

Let *A'B'C'* be the triangle formed by the lines *G<sub>a</sub>P<sub>a</sub>*, *G<sub>b</sub>P<sub>b</sub>* and *G<sub>c</sub>P<sub>c</sub>*.

Then the triangle *A'B'C'* is [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) to triangle *ABC* and its sides are parallel to the sides of triangle *ABC*.

When *P* coincides with the [orthocenter](wikipedia://en/161244) *H* of triangle *ABC* then the line *PG* coincides with the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622) of triangle *ABC*. The triangle *A'B'C'* coincides with the Gossard triangle *A<sub>g</sub>B<sub>g</sub>C<sub>g</sub>* of triangle *ABC*.

### Dao's Generalisation {#Dao's_Generalisation}

The theorem was further generalized by [Dao Thanh Oai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dao_Thanh_Oai). Let *ABC* be a triangle. Let *H* and *O* be two points in the plane, and let the line *HO* meets *BC, CA, AB* at *A<sub>0</sub>, B<sub>0</sub>, C<sub>0</sub>* respectively. Let *A<sub>H</sub> and A<sub>O</sub>* be two points such that *C<sub>0</sub>A<sub>H</sub>* [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *BH*, *B<sub>0</sub>A<sub>H</sub>* [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *CH* and *C<sub>0</sub>A<sub>O</sub>* [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *BO*, *B<sub>0</sub>A<sub>O</sub>* [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to *CO*. Define *B<sub>H</sub>, B<sub>O</sub>, C<sub>H</sub>, C<sub>O</sub>* cyclically. Then the triangle formed by the lines *A<sub>H</sub>A<sub>O</sub>, B<sub>H</sub>B<sub>O</sub>, C<sub>H</sub>C<sub>O</sub>* and triangle *ABC* are homothetic and [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330), and the [homothetic center](wikipedia://en/15685517) lies on the line *OH*. Dao Thanh Oai's result is generalization of all results above.[^o19][^o20][^o21]

- When *HO* is the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622), Dao's result is the Gossard perspector theorem.
- When *PQ* [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622), Dao's result is the Zeeman's generalization.
- When *P* is the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926), Dao's result is the Yiu's generalization.

The homothetic center in [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593) named **Dao-Zeeman perspector** of the line *OH*.[^o20]
