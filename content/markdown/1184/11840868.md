# Entropy power inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Entropy power inequality"}

In [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773), the **entropy power inequality** (**EPI**) is a result that relates to so-called "entropy power" of [random variables](wikipedia://en/25685). It shows that the entropy power of suitably [well-behaved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-behaved) random variables is a [superadditive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superadditive) [function](wikipedia://en/185427). The entropy power inequality was proved in 1948 by [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693) in his seminal paper "[A Mathematical Theory of Communication](wikipedia://en/828061)". Shannon also provided a sufficient condition for equality to hold; Stam (1959) showed that the condition is in fact necessary.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Statement of the inequality"}

## Statement of the inequality {#Statement_of_the_inequality}

For a random vector $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$ with [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, the [differential entropy](wikipedia://en/3504168) of $X$, denoted $h(X)$, is defined to be

$$h(X) = - \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} f(x) \log f(x) \, dx$$

and the entropy power of $X$, denoted $N(X)$, is defined to be

$$N(X) = \frac{1}{2\pi e} e^{ \frac{2}{n} h(X) }.$$

In particular, $N(X) = |K|^{1/n}$ when $X$ is normally distributed with covariance matrix $K$.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be [independent random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_random_variables) with probability density functions in the [$L^p$ space](wikipedia://en/45194) $L^p(\mathbb{R}^n)$ for some $p > 1$. Then

$$N(X + Y) \geq N(X) + N(Y).$$

Moreover, equality holds [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) $X$ and $Y$ are [multivariate normal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_normal) random variables with proportional [covariance matrices](wikipedia://en/191752).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=named-heading name="Alternative form of the inequality"}

## Alternative form of the inequality {#Alternative_form_of_the_inequality}

The entropy power inequality can be rewritten in an equivalent form that does not explicitly depend on the definition of entropy power (see Costa and Cover reference below).

Let $X$ and $Y$ be [independent random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_random_variables), as above. Then, let $X'$ and $Y'$ be independent random variables with Gaussian distributions and proportional covariance matrices such that

$h(X') = h(X)$ and $h(Y') = h(Y)$

Then,

$$h(X + Y) \geq h(X' + Y')$$

:::
