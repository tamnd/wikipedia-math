# Lane P. Hughston

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lane P. Hughston"}

**Lane P. Hughston** (born 24 December 1951) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Lane P. Hughston was born in Corpus Christi, Texas, and raised in Dallas, Texas, where he attended J. J. Pershing Elementary School, Benjamin Franklin Junior High School, and [Hillcrest High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillcrest_High_School_%28Dallas%29). He is the son of Edward Wallace Hughston and Joan Palmer Hughston.[^o4]

In 1969 he took first place in the nationwide [Westinghouse Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regeneron_Science_Talent_Search).[^o5] He holds an MA and a DPhil from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), where he was a [Rhodes Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_Scholar) based at [Magdalen College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalen_College,_Oxford).[^o6] His doctoral studies in mathematics were carried out under the supervision of [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

After completing his doctorate he held a Junior Research Fellowship at [Wolfson College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfson_College,_Oxford) and then was Darby Fellow and Tutor in Applied Mathematics at [Lincoln College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_College,_Oxford).[^o9]

Later, Hughston worked as a financial engineer at [Robert Fleming & Co. Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Fleming_&_Co.), London, and at [Merrill Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrill_Lynch), London, and then as Professor of Mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), [Brunel University London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University_London) and, more recently, [Goldsmiths, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldsmiths,_University_of_London).[^o10]

He has held visiting appointments at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), the [Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics), and [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London). He has carried out research in [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology), [twistor theory](wikipedia://en/260836), [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274), [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680) and [music theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_theory).[^o11] Hughston served for sixteen years as Editor-in-Chief at [International Journal of Theoretical and Applied Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Theoretical_and_Applied_Finance) over the period 2007 to 2022,[^o12] the final year being as co-Editor-in-Chief alongside M. R. Grasselli. In 2008, in collaboration with [Mark H. A. Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_H._A._Davis), he organized the Fifth World Congress of the Bachelier Finance Society.[^o13]
