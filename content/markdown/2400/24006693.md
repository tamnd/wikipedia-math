# Inverse bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Inverse bundle"}

In mathematics, the **inverse bundle** of a [fibre bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibre_bundle) is its inverse with respect to the [Whitney sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_sum) operation.

:::

Let $E \rightarrow M$ be a [fibre bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibre_bundle). A bundle $E' \rightarrow M$ is called the *inverse bundle* of $E$ if their [Whitney sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_sum) is a trivial bundle, namely if

$$E \oplus E' \cong M \times \mathbb{R}^n. \,$$

Any [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) over a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) base has an inverse bundle.
