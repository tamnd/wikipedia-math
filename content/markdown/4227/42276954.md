# Exotic affine space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Exotic affine space"}

In algebraic geometry, an **exotic affine space** is a [complex algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/39219687) that is [diffeomorphic](wikipedia://en/8564) to $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ for some *n*, but is not isomorphic as an algebraic variety to $\mathbb{C}^n$.[^o2][^o3][^o4] An example of an exotic $\mathbb C^3$ is the [Koras–Russell cubic threefold](wikipedia://en/23576643),[^o5] which is the subset of $\mathbb C^4$ defined by the polynomial equation

:::

$$\{(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4)\in\mathbb C^4|z_1+z_1^2z_2+z_3^3+z_4^2=0\}.$$
