# Pareigis Hopf algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pareigis Hopf algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **Pareigis Hopf algebra** is the [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) over a field *k* whose left [comodules](wikipedia://en/1200537) are essentially the same as complexes over *k*, in the sense that the corresponding [monoidal categories](wikipedia://en/330604) are isomorphic. It was introduced by (Pareigis 1981) as a natural example of a Hopf algebra that is neither commutative nor [cocommutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocommutative).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

As an algebra over *k*, the Pareigis algebra is generated by elements *x*,*y*, 1/*y*, with the relations *xy* + *yx* = *x*<sup>2</sup> = 0. The coproduct takes *x* to *x*⊗1 + (1/*y*)⊗*x* and *y* to *y*⊗*y*, and the [counit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counit) takes *x* to 0 and *y* to 1. The antipode takes *x* to *xy* and *y* to its inverse and has order 4.

:::

## Relation to complexes {#Relation_to_complexes}

If *M* = ⊕*M*<sub>*n*</sub> is a complex with differential *d* of degree –1, then *M* can be made into a comodule over *H* by letting the coproduct take *m* to Σ *y*<sup>*n*</sup>⊗*m*<sub>*n*</sub> + *y*<sup>*n*+1</sup>*x*⊗*dm*<sub>*n*</sub>, where *m*<sub>*n*</sub> is the component of *m* in *M*<sub>*n*</sub>. This gives an equivalence between the monoidal category of complexes over *k* with the monoidal category of comodules over the Pareigis Hopf algebra.

## See also {#See_also}

- [Sweedler's Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/32682991) is the quotient of the Pareigis Hopf algebra obtained by putting *y*<sup>2</sup> = 1.
