# Bénédicte Haas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bénédicte Haas"}

**Bénédicte Haas** (born 1976) is a French mathematician specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and especially the theories of fragmentation processes, [random trees](wikipedia://en/22192834), and [self-similar Markov processes](wikipedia://en/12394086). She is a professor of mathematics at [Sorbonne Paris North University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_Paris_North_University), a member of the Laboratoire Analyse, Géométrie et Applications (LAGA) of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) and [Paris 8 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_8_University), and head of the Paris Graduate School of Mathematical Science program.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

In 2001, Haas received a master's degree in mathematics from [Louis Pasteur University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur_University) in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg), and a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in probability and its applications through [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University). She sustained a 2004 doctorate from Pierre and Marie Curie University, directed by [Jean Bertoin](wikipedia://en/56969293), and was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 2004 and 2005.

Before taking her current professor position, she was [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University) from 2005 to 2015, and at the [École normale supérieure (Paris)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 2011 to 2014. She completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) through Paris Dauphine University in 2010. In 2015, she became a professor at Sorbonne Paris North University and a member of LAGA.

She was editor-in-chief of the *[Electronic Journal of Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Probability)* from 2021 to 2023.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Haas was elected as a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2026, "for seminal contributions to the study of fragmentation processes, random trees and their scaling limits".
