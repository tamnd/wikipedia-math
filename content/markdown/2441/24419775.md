# Theodore von Kármán Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore von Kármán Prize"}

The **Theodore von Kármán Prize** in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) is awarded every five years to an individual who has made a significant contribution to the application of mathematics in mechanics and/or engineering sciences.[^o2] Established and funded in 1968 by the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM), the prize honors the legacy of [Theodore von Kármán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_von_Kármán).[^o3]

:::

## List of recipients {#List_of_recipients}

- 1972 [Geoffrey Ingram Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Ingram_Taylor)[^o2]
- 1979 [George F. Carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_F._Carrier) and [Joseph B. Keller](wikipedia://en/4454126)[^o2]
- 1984 [Julian D. Cole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_D._Cole)[^o2]
- 1989 [Paul R. Garabedian](wikipedia://en/22618676)[^o2]
- 1994 [Herbert B. Keller](wikipedia://en/24424009)[^o2]
- 1999 [Stuart S. Antman](wikipedia://en/44788178), [John M. Ball](wikipedia://en/3578468), [Zuccher Simone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuccher_Simone)[^o2][^o5]
- 2004 [Roland Glowinski](wikipedia://en/20952046)[^o2][^o6]
- 2009 [Mary F. Wheeler](wikipedia://en/26586432)[^o2][^o7]
- 2014 [Weinan E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weinan_E) and [Richard D. James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_D._James_%28scientist%29)[^o8]
- 2020 [Kaushik Bhattacharya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaushik_Bhattacharya)[^o9]
- 2024 [Karen Willcox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Willcox)[^o10]
