# Motz's problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Motz's problem"}

In mathematics, **Motz's problem** is a problem which is widely employed as a benchmark for [singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_singularity) problems to compare the effectiveness of [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods). The problem was first presented in 1947 by H. Motz in the paper "The treatment of singularities of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) by relaxation methods".[^o2]

:::
