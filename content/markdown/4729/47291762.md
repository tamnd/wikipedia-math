# Giovanni Forni

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Forni"}

**Giovanni Forni** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) known for his research in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

After graduating from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) in 1989,[^o3] he obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1993 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), under the supervision of [John Mather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mather_%28mathematician%29).[^o4]

He was an invited speaker at the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing.[^o5]

For his work on solutions of [cohomological](wikipedia://en/194060) equations for flows on surfaces, and on the [Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509)–[Zorich](wikipedia://en/45625319) conjecture concerning deviation of [ergodic](wikipedia://en/258986) averages, he was awarded the 2008 [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601).[^o6]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
