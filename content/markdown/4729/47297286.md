# N conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="N conjecture"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the ***n* conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) stated by (Browkin Brzeziński 1994) as a generalization of the [*abc* conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108) to more than three [integers](wikipedia://en/14563).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formulations"}

## Formulations {#Formulations}

Given $n \ge 3$, let $a_1,a_2,...,a_n \in \mathbb{Z}$ satisfy three conditions:

(i) $\gcd(a_1,a_2,...,a_n)=1$

(ii) $a_1 + a_2 + ... + a_n = 0$

(iii) no proper subsum of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ equals $0$

**First formulation**

The *n* conjecture states that for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a constant $C$ depending on $n$ and $\varepsilon$, such that:

> $$\operatorname{max}(|a_1|,|a_2|,...,|a_n|)< C_{n,\varepsilon}\operatorname{rad}(|a_1| \cdot |a_2| \cdot \ldots \cdot |a_n|)^{2n - 5 + \varepsilon}$$

where $\operatorname{rad}(m)$ denotes the [radical](wikipedia://en/1634886) of an integer $m$, defined as the product of the distinct [prime factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_factor) of $m$.

**Second formulation**

Define the *quality* of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ as

$$q(a_1,a_2,...,a_n) = \frac{\log(\operatorname{max}(|a_1|,|a_2|,...,|a_n|))}{\log(\operatorname{rad}(|a_1| \cdot |a_2| \cdot ... \cdot |a_n|))}$$

The *n* conjecture states that $\limsup q(a_1,a_2,...,a_n)= 2n-5$.

:::

## Stronger form {#Stronger_form}

(Vojta 1998) proposed a stronger variant of the *n* conjecture, where setwise [coprimeness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ is replaced by pairwise coprimeness of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$.

There are two different formulations of this ***strong n* conjecture**.

Given $n \ge 3$, let $a_1,a_2,...,a_n \in \mathbb{Z}$ satisfy three conditions:

(i) $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ are pairwise coprime

(ii) $a_1 + a_2 + ... + a_n = 0$

(iii) no proper subsum of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ equals $0$

**First formulation**

The *strong n* conjecture states that for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a constant $C$ depending on $n$ and $\varepsilon$, such that:

> $$\operatorname{max}(|a_1|,|a_2|,...,|a_n|)< C_{n,\varepsilon}\operatorname{rad}(|a_1| \cdot |a_2| \cdot \ldots \cdot |a_n|)^{1 + \varepsilon}$$

**Second formulation**

Define the *quality* of $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ as

$$q(a_1,a_2,...,a_n) = \frac{\log(\operatorname{max}(|a_1|,|a_2|,...,|a_n|))}{\log(\operatorname{rad}(|a_1| \cdot |a_2| \cdot ... \cdot |a_n|))}$$

The *strong n* conjecture states that $\limsup q(a_1,a_2,...,a_n) = 1$.

(Hölzl, Kleine and Stephan 2025) have shown that for $n \geq 5$ the above limit superior is for odd $n$ at least $5/3$ and for even $n$ is at least $5/4$. For the cases $n=3$ (abc-conjecture) and $n=4$, they did not find any nontrivial lower bounds. It is also open whether there is a common constant upper bound above the limit superiors for all $n \geq 3$. For the exact status of the case $n=3$ see the article on the [abc conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108).
