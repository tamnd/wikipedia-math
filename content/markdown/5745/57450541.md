# BFR algorithm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="BFR algorithm"}

The **BFR algorithm**, named after its inventors Bradley, Fayyad and Reina, is a variant of [k-means algorithm](wikipedia://en/1860407) that is designed to cluster data in a high-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697). It makes a very strong assumption about the shape of clusters: they must be [normally distributed](wikipedia://en/21462) about a [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926). The [mean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean) and [standard deviation](wikipedia://en/27590) for a cluster may differ for different dimensions, but the dimensions must be independent.[^o2] In other words, the data must take the shape of axis-aligned ellipses.

:::
