# Philip Welch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Welch"}

**Philip David Welch** (born 6 January 1954) is a British mathematician known for his contributions to [logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He is Professor of Pure Mathematics at the School of Mathematics, [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol).[^o3][^o4] He was the Coordinating Editor of the *[Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic)* in 2016, President of the [British Logic Colloquium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Logic_Colloquium) from 2017 to 2022 and President of the European Set Theory Society from 2021 to 2025.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

section Welch attended [Lancing College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancing_College). After obtaining a BSc in mathematics from [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) in 1975, he attended [Exeter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), taking an MSc in mathematical logic in 1976 and his [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1979, under the supervision of [Robin Gandy](wikipedia://en/3155895). His dissertation was entitled *Combinatorial Principles in the Core Model*.

He worked as an assistant at the Seminar für Logik at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) from 1980 to 1981, then as an SERC Research Fellow at [Wolfson College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfson_College,_Oxford), Oxford, from 1981 until 1983. Following a Royal Society European Research Fellowship at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), and the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) (1984), he held a position as an assistant professor at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) until 1986. In 1997 he left Bristol in order to set up a research group in set theory at the Graduate School of Science and Technology at [Kobe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_University), Japan. He subsequently held a Guest Professorship at the Kurt Gödel Research Center at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) (2000–2001) and a Mercator Professorship at the University of Bonn (2001) before returning to Bristol in 2002. He was appointed as a Professor there in 2004. Apart from research articles he is co-author with Aaron Beller and [Ronald Jensen](wikipedia://en/3785996) of *Coding the Universe*.[^o7] In 2023, Welch was awarded a Senior Fellowship of the Zukunftskollegs at the University of Konstanz.[^o8]
