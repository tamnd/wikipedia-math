::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="7-simplex honeycomb"}

In [seven-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventh_dimension) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **7-simplex honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). The tessellation fills space by [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex), [rectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-simplex), [birectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_7-simplex), and [trirectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_7-simplex) facets. These facet types occur in proportions of 2:2:2:1 respectively in the whole honeycomb.

:::

## A7 lattice {#A7_lattice}

This [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the [A7 lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A7_lattice) or **7-simplex lattice**. The 56 vertices of the [expanded 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_7-simplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) represent the 56 roots of the ${\tilde{A}}_7$ Coxeter group.[^o8] It is the 7-dimensional case of a [simplectic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplectic_honeycomb). Around each vertex figure are 254 facets: 8+8 [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex), 28+28 [rectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-simplex), 56+56 [birectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_7-simplex), 70 [trirectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_7-simplex), with the count distribution from the 9th row of [Pascal's triangle](wikipedia://en/49497).

${\tilde{E}}_7$ contains ${\tilde{A}}_7$ as a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of index 144.[^o9] Both ${\tilde{E}}_7$ and ${\tilde{A}}_7$ can be seen as affine extensions from $A_7$ from different nodes:
