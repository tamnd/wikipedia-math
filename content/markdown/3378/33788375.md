# Mutual knowledge (logic)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mutual knowledge"}

**Mutual knowledge** is a fundamental concept about information in [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), (epistemic) [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), and [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology). An [event](wikipedia://en/22961) is mutual knowledge if all agents know that the event occurred.[^o2] However, mutual knowledge by itself implies nothing about what agents know about other agents' knowledge: i.e. it is possible that an event is mutual knowledge but that each agent is unaware that the other agents know it has occurred.[^o3] [Common knowledge](wikipedia://en/1409006) is a related but stronger notion; any event that is common knowledge is also mutual knowledge.

:::

The philosopher [Stephen Schiffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Schiffer), in his book *Meaning*, developed a notion he called "mutual knowledge" which functions quite similarly to [David K. Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lewis_%28philosopher%29)'s "common knowledge".[^o4]

[Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication) (verbal or [non-verbal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonverbal_communication)) can turn mutual knowledge into common knowledge. For example, in the [Muddy Children Puzzle](wikipedia://en/2062017#Muddy_Children_Puzzle) with two children (Alice and Bob, $G=\{a,b\}$), if they both have muddy face (viz. $M_{a}\land M_{b}$), both of them know that there is at least one muddy face. Written formally, let $p=[\exists x\!\in\! G(M_{x})]$, and then we have $K_{a}p\land K_{b}p$. However, neither of them know that the other child knows ($(\neg K_{a}K_{b}p)\land(\neg K_{b}K_{a}p)$), which makes $p=[\exists x\!\in\! G(M_{x})]$ *mutual knowledge*. Now suppose if Alice tells Bob that she knows $p$ (so that $K_{a}p$ becomes *common knowledge*, i.e. $C_{G} K_{a}p$), and then Bob tells Alice that he knows $p$ as well (so that $K_{b}p$ becomes *common knowledge*, i.e. *$C_{G} K_{b}p$*), this will turn $p$ into *common knowledge* (*$C_{G}E_{G} p \Rightarrow C_{G} p$*), which is equivalent to the effect of a [public announcement](wikipedia://en/48791499#Public_Events) "there is at least one muddy face".
