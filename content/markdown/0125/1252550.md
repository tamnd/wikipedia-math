# Leroy P. Steele Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leroy P. Steele Prize"}

The **Leroy P. Steele Prizes** are awarded every year since 1970 by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for distinguished research work and writing in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). Since 1993, the awards have been split into three categories: lifetime achievement, mathematical exposition, and research.

:::

The prizes were begun from a bequest of [Leroy P. Steele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leroy_P._Steele) and were set up in honor of [George David Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/30871906), [William Fogg Osgood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Fogg_Osgood) and [William Caspar Graustein](wikipedia://en/52975930). The way the prizes are awarded was changed in 1976 and 1993, but the initial aim of honoring expository writing as well as research has been retained. The prizes—\$10,000 for lifetime achievement,[^o2] and \$5,000 for mathematical exposition[^o3] and for research[^o4]—are not given on a strict national basis, but relate to writing in English (originally, or in translation) and mathematical activity in the United States.
