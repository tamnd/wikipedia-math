# Lara Alcock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lara Alcock"}

**Lara Alcock** is a British [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471). She is a professor in mathematics education at [Loughborough University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University), head of the Mathematics Education Centre at Loughborough, and the author of several books on mathematics.[^o7] Alcock won the Selden Prize for her research in mathematics education and the inaugural [John Blake University Teaching Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Blake_University_Teaching_Medal) in 2021.[^o21] Alcock is a [National Teaching Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Teaching_Fellowship).[^o20]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Alcock earned bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), and in 2001 completed a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics education at Warwick. Her PhD research on *Categories, definitions and mathematics: Student reasoning about objects in analysis*, was supervised by Adrian Simpson.[^o5]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After working as an assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in New Jersey, she returned to the UK as a [teaching fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_fellow) at [Essex University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex_University). She moved to Loughborough in 2007.[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11]

### Publications {#Publications}

Alcock's publications[^o3] include:

- *Ideas from Mathematics Education: An Introduction for Mathematicians* (with Adrian Simpson, Higher Education Academy, 2009)
- *How to Study for a Mathematics Degree* / *How to Study as a Mathematics Major* (UK/US; Oxford University Press, 2013); *Wie man erfolgreich Mathematik studiert* (translated into German by Bernhard Gerl, Springer Spektrum, 2017)[^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16]
- *How to Think about Analysis* (Oxford University Press, 2014)[^o17]
- *Mathematics Rebooted: A Fresh Approach to Understanding* (Oxford University Press, 2017)[^o8]
- *How to Think About Abstract Algebra* (Oxford University Press, 2021)[^o18]

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Alcock is the 2012 winner of the Annie and John Selden Prize for research in undergraduate [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), given by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o11] She was named a National Teaching Fellow by the [Higher Education Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Academy) in 2015.[^o20]

Alcock was the inaugural winner of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) John Blake University Teaching Medal in 2021. [^o21]
