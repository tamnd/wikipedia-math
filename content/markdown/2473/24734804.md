# Uriel Feige

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uriel Feige"}

**Uriel Feige** (אוריאל פייגה) is an Israeli [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) who was a doctoral student of [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Uriel Feige currently holds the post of Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Applied Mathematics, the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), Rehovot in Israel.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

He is notable for co-inventing the [Feige–Fiat–Shamir identification scheme](wikipedia://en/7116196) along with [Amos Fiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_Fiat) and [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

He won the [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize) in 2001 "for the [PCP theorem](wikipedia://en/3001241) and its applications to [hardness of approximation](wikipedia://en/20677277)".
