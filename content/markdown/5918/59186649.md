# Alessandra Lunardi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alessandra Lunardi"}

**Alessandra Lunardi** (born 1958) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). She is a professor in the department of mathematics and computer science at the [University of Parma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Parma). She is particularly interested in [Kolmogorov equations](wikipedia://en/29973465) and [free boundary problems](wikipedia://en/32070938).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lunardi was educated at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), completing her undergraduate studies there in 1980 and earning a Ph.D. there in 1983. Her dissertation, *Analyticity of the maximal solution to fully nonlinear equations in Banach spaces*, was supervised by Giuseppe Da Prato.

After continuing on at Pisa as a researcher from 1984 to 1987, she was hired as a full professor at the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari) in 1987, and moved to Parma in 1994.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Lunardi is the author of *Analytic semigroups and optimal regularity in parabolic problems* ([Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser), 1995, reprinted 2013) and of *Interpolation theory* (Edizioni della Normale, 1998, 3rd ed., 2018). With [G. Da Prato](wikipedia://en/75002307), P. C. Kunstmann, [I. Lasiecka](wikipedia://en/44759993), R. Schnaubelt, and L. Weis, she is a co-author of *Functional Analytic Methods for Evolution Equations* (Springer, 2004).

Lunardi is one of six [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the journal *Nonlinear Differential Equations and Applications (NoDEA)*. She also served as editor-in-chief of *[Rivista di Matematica della Università di Parma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivista_di_Matematica_della_Università_di_Parma)* for Series 7 of the journal, from 2002 to 2008.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1987, Lunardi won the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize) of the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092). In 2017, she won the Lucio & Wanda Amerio Gold Medal Prize of the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere).
