# Gabriella Tarantello

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriella Tarantello"}

*Image: Gabriella Tarantello Berkeley.jpg.* Berkeley, 1987

**Gabriella Tarantello** (born 15 October 1958) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155). She is a professor in the department of mathematics at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tarantello was born in [Pratola Peligna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratola_Peligna). She did her undergraduate studies at the [University of L'Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_L'Aquila), earning a bachelor's degree there in 1982. She then came to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) for graduate study at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), earning a master's degree in 1984 and completing her Ph.D. there in 1986. Her dissertation, *Some Results on the Minimal Period Problem for Nonlinear Vibrating Strings and Hamiltonian Systems; and on the Number of Solutions for Semilinear Elliptic Equations*, was supervised by [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725).

After postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and a visiting assistant professorship at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), she joined the [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) faculty in 1989. She returned to Italy as an associate professor at Tor Vergata in 1993, moved to the [University of Basilicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basilicata) as a full professor in 1994, and returned to Tor Vergata as a full professor in 1995.

## Books {#Books}

Tarantello is the author of the book *Selfdual Gauge Field Vortices: An Analytical Approach* (Progress in Nonlinear Differential Equations and Their Applications 72, [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser), 2008). With Matthew J. Gursky, Ermanno Lanconelli, [Andrea Malchiodi](wikipedia://en/39307807), and Paul C. Yang, she is a co-author of *Geometric Analysis and PDEs: Lectures given at the C.I.M.E. Summer School held in Cetraro, Italy, June 11–16, 2007* ([Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235) 1977, Springer, 2009).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2014, Tarantello won the Lucio & Wanda Amerio Gold Medal Prize of the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere). She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2020.
