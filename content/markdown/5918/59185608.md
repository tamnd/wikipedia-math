::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated triakis icosahedron"}

The **truncated triakis icosahedron**, or more precisely an **order-10 truncated [triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/722873)**, is a convex polyhedron with 72 faces: 20 sets of 3 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) arranged in an [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/2877844) arrangement, with 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393) in the gaps.

:::

## Triakis icosahedron {#Triakis_icosahedron}

It is constructed from a [triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/722873) by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the order-10 vertices. This creates 12 regular decagon faces, and leaves 60 mirror-symmetric pentagons.

## Decakis truncated dodecahedron {#Decakis_truncated_dodecahedron}

The dual of the *truncated triakis icosahedron* is called a **decakis truncated dodecahedron**. It can be seen as a [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538) with [decagonal pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_pyramid) augmented to the faces.
