::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated triakis octahedron"}

The **truncated triakis octahedron**, or more precisely an **order-8 truncated [triakis octahedron](wikipedia://en/722549)**, is a convex polyhedron with 30 faces: 8 sets of 3 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) arranged in an [octahedral](wikipedia://en/2900805) arrangement, with 6 [octagons](wikipedia://en/314575) in the gaps.

:::

## Triakis octahedron {#Triakis_octahedron}

It is constructed from a [triakis octahedron](wikipedia://en/722549) by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the order-8 vertices. This creates 6 regular octagon faces, and leaves 24 mirror-symmetric pentagons.

## Octakis truncated cube {#Octakis_truncated_cube}

The dual of the *order-8 truncated triakis octahedron* is called a **octakis truncated cube**. It can be seen as a [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529) with [octagonal pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octagonal_pyramid) augmented to the faces.
