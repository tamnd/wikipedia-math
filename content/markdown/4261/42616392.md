# Yvette Kosmann-Schwarzbach

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yvette Kosmann-Schwarzbach"}

**Yvette Kosmann-Schwarzbach** (born 30 April 1941)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kosmann-Schwarzbach obtained her [doctoral degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_degree) in 1970 at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) under supervision of [André Lichnerowicz](wikipedia://en/2389188) on a dissertation titled *Dérivées de Lie des spineurs* ([Lie derivatives](wikipedia://en/294437) of [spinors](wikipedia://en/29276)).

She worked at [Lille University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology), and since 1993 at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique).

## Research {#Research}

Kosmann-Schwarzbach is the author of over fifty articles on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), of two books on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and on the [Noether theorem](wikipedia://en/150159), as well as the co-editor of several books concerning the theory of [integrable systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_systems). The [Kosmann lift](wikipedia://en/31984047) in differential geometry is named after her.[^o7][^o8]
