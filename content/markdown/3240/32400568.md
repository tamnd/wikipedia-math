# Family (U.S. census)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Family"}

A **family** is defined by the [United States Census Bureau](wikipedia://en/57070) for statistical purposes as "a group of two people or more (one of whom is the [householder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household)) related by birth, marriage, or adoption, and residing together; all such people (including related subfamily members) are considered as members of one family."

:::

A **family household** is more inclusive, consisting of "a household maintained by a householder who is in a family (as defined above), and includes any unrelated people (unrelated subfamily members and/or secondary individuals) who may be residing there."[^o2]

In 2014, the US Census Bureau began including [same-sex marriages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriages) in their counts of families and family households. Prior to this, they were counted as [cohabiting partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohabitation); thus, they were not considered as forming a family.[^o3]
