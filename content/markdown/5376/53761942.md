# Kirsten Eisenträger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kirsten Eisenträger"}

**Anne Kirsten Eisenträger** is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at The [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), known for her research on [computational number theory](wikipedia://en/511466), [Hilbert's tenth problem](wikipedia://en/101851), and applications in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).

:::

Eisenträger earned a *[Vordiplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vordiplom)* in mathematics in 1996 from the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) and a Master's degree (1998) and a Ph.D. (2003) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley); her dissertation, titled *Hilbert’s Tenth Problem and Arithmetic Geometry*, was supervised by [Bjorn Poonen](wikipedia://en/16283918). After temporary positions at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), she joined the Pennsylvania State University faculty in 2007.

Eisenträger appears in [George Csicsery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Csicsery)'s documentary film *Julia Robinson and Hilbert's Tenth Problem* (2008). In 2017, she became a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to computational number theory and number-theoretic undecidability".
