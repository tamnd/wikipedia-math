# Uniform algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform algebra"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **uniform algebra** *A* on a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X* is a [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) (with respect to the [uniform norm](wikipedia://en/349755)) [subalgebra](wikipedia://en/191788) of the [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) *C*(*X*) (the [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) [complex](wikipedia://en/5826)-valued functions on *X*) with the following properties:[^o2]

:::

the constant functions are contained in *A*

for every $x, y \in X$ there is $f \in A$ with $f(x) \ne f(y)$. This is called separating the points of *X*.

As a closed subalgebra of the [commutative algebra (structure)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_algebra_%28structure%29) [Banach algebra](wikipedia://en/4665) *C*(*X*), a uniform algebra is itself a unital commutative Banach algebra (when equipped with the uniform norm). Hence, it is (by definition) a [Banach function algebra](wikipedia://en/4827724).

A uniform algebra *A* on *X* is said to be **natural** if the [maximal ideals](wikipedia://en/48164) of *A* are precisely the [ideals](wikipedia://en/25977) $M_x$ of functions vanishing at a point *x* in *X*.

## Abstract characterization {#Abstract_characterization}

If *A* is a [unital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unital_algebra) commutative Banach algebra such that $\|a^2\| = \|a\|^2$ for all *a* in *A*, then there is a compact Hausdorff *X* such that *A* is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) as a Banach algebra to a uniform algebra on *X*. This result follows from the [spectral radius formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_radius_formula) and the [Gelfand representation](wikipedia://en/364372).
