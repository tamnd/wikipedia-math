::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Omnitruncated tesseractic honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **omnitruncated tesseractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It has [omnitruncated tesseract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_tesseract), [truncated cuboctahedral prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_cuboctahedral_prism), and [8-8 duoprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-8_duoprism) facets in an irregular [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).
