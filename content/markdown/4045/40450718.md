In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **cantellated tesseractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space. It is constructed by a [cantellation](wikipedia://en/6317514) of a [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139) creating [cantellated tesseracts](wikipedia://en/3715231), and new [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) and [octahedral prism](wikipedia://en/8813885) facets at the original vertices.
