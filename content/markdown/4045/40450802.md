::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated tesseractic honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **runcinated tesseractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space. It is constructed by a [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination_%28geometry%29) of a [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139) creating [runcinated tesseracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_tesseract), and new [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112), [rectified tesseract](wikipedia://en/4023286) and [cuboctahedral prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuboctahedral_prism) facets.
