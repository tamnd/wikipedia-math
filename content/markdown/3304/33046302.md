# Sergey Fomin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sergey Fomin"}

**Sergey Vladimirovich Fomin** (Сергей Владимирович Фомин) (born 16 February 1958 in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has made important contributions in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and its relations with [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). Together with [Andrei Zelevinsky](wikipedia://en/32415867), he introduced [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fomin received his [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in 1979 and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1982 from [St. Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_State_University) under the direction of [Anatoly Vershik](wikipedia://en/9036441) and Leonid Osipov. Previous to his appointment at the University of Michigan, he held positions at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1992 to 2000, at the [St. Petersburg Institute for Informatics and Automation of the Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_Institute_for_Informatics_and_Automation_of_the_Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), and at the [Saint Petersburg Electrotechnical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_Electrotechnical_University). Sergey Fomin studied at the [45th Physics-Mathematics School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Physics-Mathematics_School) and later taught mathematics there.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Fomin's contributions include

- Discovery (with [A. Zelevinsky](wikipedia://en/32415867)) of [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423).
- Work (jointly with [A. Berenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkady_Berenstein) and [A. Zelevinsky](wikipedia://en/32415867)) on [total positivity](wikipedia://en/2097524).
- Work (with [A. Zelevinsky](wikipedia://en/32415867)) on the Laurent phenomenon, including its applications to [Somos sequences](wikipedia://en/24612539).
