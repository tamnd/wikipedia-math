# Benny Sudakov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Benny Sudakov"}

**Benny Sudakov** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): בני סודקוב; born October 1969)[^o4] is an Israeli mathematician, who works mainly on [extremal](wikipedia://en/2675917) and [probabilistic combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_combinatorics).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sudakov was born in [Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilissi), Georgia,[^o4] and completed his undergraduate studies at [Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University) in 1990.[^o7] After emigrating to Israel, he received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1999, under the supervision of [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651). From 1999 until 2002, he held a [Veblen Research Instructorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veblen_Research_Instructorship),[^o6] a joint position between [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). Until 2007, he was an assistant professor at Princeton University. Until 2014, he was a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o7] In July 2013, Sudakov joined [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) as a professor.[^o8]

Sudakov has broad interests within the field of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), having written papers on [extremal combinatorics](wikipedia://en/2675917), [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477), and [positional games](wikipedia://en/25981951).[^o7]

In 2012, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] He gave an [invited talk](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2010 at [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), on the topic of "Combinatorics".[^o10]
