# Siegel identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Siegel's identity** refers to one of two formulae that are used in the resolution of [Diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The first formula is

$$\frac{x_3 - x_1}{x_2 - x_1} + \frac{x_2 - x_3}{x_2 - x_1} = 1 .$$

The second is

$$\frac{x_3 - x_1}{x_2 - x_1} \cdot\frac{t - x_2}{t - x_3} + \frac{x_2 - x_3}{x_2 - x_1} \cdot \frac{t - x_1}{t - x_3} = 1 .$$

:::

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

The identities are used in translating Diophantine problems connected with integral points on [hyperelliptic curves](wikipedia://en/499361) into [S-unit equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-unit_equation).

:::
