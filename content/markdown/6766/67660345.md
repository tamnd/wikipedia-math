# Behnke–Stein theorem on Stein manifolds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Behnke–Stein theorem on Stein manifolds"}

In mathematics, especially [several complex variables](wikipedia://en/360243), the **Behnke–Stein theorem** states that a [connected](wikipedia://en/6233), non-[compact](wikipedia://en/6042) (open) [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) is a [Stein manifold](wikipedia://en/1053909).[^o2] In other words, it states that there is a nonconstant [single-valued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-valued_function) [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) ([univalent function](wikipedia://en/1534382)) on such a Riemann surface.[^o3] It is a generalization of the [Runge approximation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge_approximation_theorem) and was proved by [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678) and [Karl Stein](wikipedia://en/7218529) in 1948.[^o4]

:::

## Method of proof {#Method_of_proof}

The study of Riemann surfaces typically belongs to the field of [one-variable complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), but the proof method uses the approximation by the [polyhedron domain](wikipedia://en/21663267) used in the proof of the [Behnke–Stein theorem on domains of holomorphy](wikipedia://en/21663799)[^o6] and the [Oka–Weil theorem](wikipedia://en/66786706).
