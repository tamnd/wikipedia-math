# Thomas Ranken Lyle Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Ranken Lyle Medal"}

The **Thomas Ranken Lyle Medal** is awarded at most every two years by the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) to a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) or [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) for his or her outstanding research accomplishments.[^o2] It is named after [Thomas Ranken Lyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Ranken_Lyle), an Irish [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) who became a professor at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne). The award takes the form of a [bronze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze) medal[^o4] bearing the design of the head of Thomas Lyle, as sculpted by [Rayner Hoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayner_Hoff).[^o3]

:::

The medal was founded by the [Australian National Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_Research_Council) (ANRC) in 1932,[^o4][^o5] and first awarded in 1935.[^o2][^o3] When the Australian Academy of Science was established in 1954, it took over the roles of the ANRC, including administration of the medal.
