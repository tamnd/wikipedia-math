# Maria Chudnovsky

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Chudnovsky"}

**Maria Chudnovsky** (born January 6, 1977) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) mathematician working on [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555).[^o4] She is a [2012 MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program).[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chudnovsky is a professor in the department of mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). She grew up in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) (attended [Saint Petersburg Lyceum 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_Lyceum_30)) and [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), studying at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology),[^o8] and received her Ph.D. in 2003 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708). After postdoctoral research at the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655),[^o8] she became an assistant professor at Princeton University in 2005, and moved to [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2006. By 2014, she was the Liu Family Professor of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at Columbia. She returned to Princeton as a professor of mathematics in 2015.[^o4]

Chudnovsky is an editor for a number of mathematical journals, including *[Combinatorica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorica)*, *[Journal of Combinatorial Theory Series B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory)*, *[Journal of Graph Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Graph_Theory)* and *[Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_London_Mathematical_Society)*.

## Research {#Research}

Chudnovsky's contributions to [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) include the proof of the [strong perfect graph theorem](wikipedia://en/744171) (with [Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704), [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708), and [Robin Thomas](wikipedia://en/24641679)) characterizing [perfect graphs](wikipedia://en/670531) as being exactly the graphs with no odd [induced](wikipedia://en/1655188) [cycles](wikipedia://en/168609) of length at least 5 or their [complements](wikipedia://en/1175649).[^o11][^o12][^o13] Other research contributions of Chudnovsky include co-authorship of the first [polynomial-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time) algorithm for recognizing perfect graphs (time bounded by a polynomial of degree 9),[^n1] a structural characterization of the [claw-free graphs](wikipedia://en/12415907),[^n2] and progress on the [Erdős–Hajnal conjecture](wikipedia://en/42585591).[^o16]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2004 Chudnovsky was named one of the "Brilliant 10" by [Popular Science magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Science).[^o23] Her work on the strong perfect graph theorem won for her and her co-authors the 2009 [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916).[^o24] In 2012 she was awarded a "genius award" under the [MacArthur Fellows Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program).[^o25][^o26] She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows.[^o27] Elected to the [National Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2026.[^o28]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 2011, she married Daniel Panner, a viola player who teaches at [Mannes School of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannes_School_of_Music) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). They have a son named Rafael.[^o30]
