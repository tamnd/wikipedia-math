# Isabelle Chalendar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isabelle Chalendar"}

**Isabelle Chalendar** is a French mathematician whose research interests include [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), and the theory of [semigroups](wikipedia://en/27799). She is a professor of mathematics and head of the mathematics department at [Gustave Eiffel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Eiffel_University), a member of the university's Laboratoire d'analyse et de mathématiques appliquées (LAMA), and also holds affiliations with [Université Paris Est Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris_Est_Marne-la-Vallée) (UPEM), [Paris-East Créteil University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-East_Créteil_University) (UPEC), and the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chalendar received her Ph.D. in 1996 from the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1), with the dissertation *Autour du probleme du sous-espace invariant et theorie des algebres duales* on the [invariant subspace problem](wikipedia://en/691927) supervised by Bernard Gustave Chevreau.

She was [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at the Camille Jordan Institute of [Claude Bernard University Lyon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Bernard_University_Lyon_1), before moving to her present position at Gustave Eiffel University. She was named the department's gender equality officer in 2016, and head of the department in 2022.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Chalendar is coauthor of the book *Modern Approaches to the Invariant-Subspace Problem* (with [Jonathan Partington](wikipedia://en/5074250), Cambridge University Press, 2011).

Her work with Partington on [phase retrieval](wikipedia://en/11081803), in their paper "Phase retrieval on circles and lines" (*Canadian Mathematical Bulletin*, 2024) was given the 2025 G. de B. Robinson Award of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).
