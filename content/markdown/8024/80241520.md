# Retsef Levi

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Retsef Levi"}

**Retsef Levi** (רצף לוי; born March 7, 1971) is an Israeli [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) and [management scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_scientist). He is the J. Spencer Standish Professor of Operations Management at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT).[^o4]

:::

In June 2025, [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy_Jr.), [U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Secretary_of_Health_and_Human_Services), appointed Levi as a member of the [Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_Committee_on_Immunization_Practices) (ACIP) at the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention) (CDC).

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Levi was born on March 7, 1971, in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv), Israel.[^o6] He is a brother of Israeli screenwriter [Reshef Levi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reshef_Levi) and Israeli author [Yannets Levi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yannets_Levi).[^o7]

From 1990 to 2001, Levi served in the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces) (IDF). Beginning in 1991, he was an [intelligence officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_officer) of the elite [Israeli Intelligence Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Intelligence_Corps), first as a lieutenant [intelligence analyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_analysis) (1990–1992), then as a captain (1992–1994) and then major, or *[Rav séren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces_ranks)* (1994–1996).[^o9]

While serving in the Israeli military, Levi graduated *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)* from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 2001 with a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematics. He then completed doctoral studies in the United States at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), where he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) and [mathematical programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_programming)[^o8] in 2005 under professors Robin Roundy and [David Shmoys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Shmoys). His dissertation was entitled "Computing Provably Near-Optimal Policies for Stochastic Inventory Control Models".[^o9] Professors [James Renegar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Renegar) and Shane Henderson also advised Levi's thesis.[^o9]

## Career {#Career}

Levi is a data scientist and former intelligence officer in the Israeli military.[^o11] After receiving his doctorate, Levi completed [postdoctoral research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research) at the [IBM T.J. Watson Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_T.J._Watson_Research_Center), where he was the Goldstine Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Mathematical Sciences. He became an assistant professor of management in 2006 at the [MIT Sloan School of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Sloan_School_of_Management), where he held a position as a Robert N. Noyce Career Development Professor. He was promoted to an associate professor with [tenure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_tenure) in July 2009 and received the school's appointment as its J. Spencer Standish Professor of Operations Management in July 2010.[^o9]

Levi was [associate editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_editor) of the peer-reviewed academic journals [*Management Science*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_Science_%28journal%29) (from 2009 to 2014), *[Mathematics of Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Operations_Research)* (from 2009 to 2015), *[Naval Research Logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Research_Logistics)* (from 2009 to 2015), and [*Operations Research*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research_%28journal%29) (from 2011 to 2018).[^o9]

In 2021, Israeli minister of education [Yoav Gallant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoav_Gallant) appointed Levi to a panel of experts tasked with advising the government on the reopening of schools during the [COVID-19 pandemic in Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Israel).[^o7] Levi was a critic of the [Israeli Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Ministry_of_Health)'s policy of closing schools during the pandemic, which he considered unnecessary.[^o7]

### Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices, 2025 {#Advisory_Committee_on_Immunization_Practices,_2025}

Levi is a critic of [COVID-19 lockdowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_lockdowns) and [mRNA vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRNA_vaccines),[^o15] and a [COVID-19 vaccine skeptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccine_skeptic).[^o13] He has published papers on the [mortality rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortality_rates) of COVID-19 vaccines.[^o14] He wrote on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter) in 2023 that “the evidence is mounting and indisputable that mRNA vaccines cause serious harm including death, especially among young people".[^o15] On June 11, 2025, [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy_Jr.), the [U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._secretary_of_health_and_human_services), announced that he appointed Levi as one of the members of the [Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_Committee_on_Immunization_Practices) (ACIP).[^o15] However, Levi did not have medical, epidemiological, or immunological training, experience, or background.[^o16][^o17][^o18] Levi was removed from his position on the ACIP in 2026 when a ruling in *[American Academy of Pediatrics v. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_Committee_on_Immunization_Practices#American_Academy_of_Pediatrics_v._Kennedy)* invalidated the entire committee's appointments.[^o19]
