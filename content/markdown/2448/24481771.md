# Sourav Chatterjee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sourav Chatterjee"}

**Sourav Chatterjee** FRS (born 26 November 1979)[^o3] is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from West Bengal, specialising in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). Chatterjee is credited with work on the study of fluctuations in random structures, concentration and super-concentration inequalities, Poisson and other non-normal limits, first-passage percolation, [Stein's method](wikipedia://en/12933953) and [spin glasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_glasses).[^o4][^o5] He has received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in mathematics, Tweedie Award,[^o6] [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize), [Doeblin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doeblin_Prize), [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize), and [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) in mathematical sciences. He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2014.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Chatterjee received a Bachelor and Master of Statistics from [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Kolkata, and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 2005, where he worked under the supervision of [Persi Diaconis](wikipedia://en/65467).[^o8] Chatterjee joined [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), as a visiting assistant professor, then received a tenure-track assistant professor position in 2006. In July 2009 he became an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) of statistics and mathematics at University of California, Berkeley. Then in September 2009, Chatterjee became an associate professor of mathematics at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o9] He spent the academic year 2012–2013 as a visiting associate professor of mathematics and statistics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). Since autumn 2013 he has joined the faculty of Stanford University as a full professor with joint appointments in the departments of Mathematics and Statistics.[^o10]

He has served as an associate editor of *Annals of Probability*, *Annales de l'[Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595)* (B) *Probabilities et Statistiques*, "Probability Theory and Related Fields". He currently serves as an editor of *Communications in Mathematical Physics*.[^o11]
