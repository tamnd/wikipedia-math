# David Pointcheval

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Pointcheval"}

**David Pointcheval** is a French cryptographer. He is currently the Chief scientific officer (CSO) of Cosmian (on leave from CNRS), a French deeptech company in public cloud security.[^o2] He is a Senior Researcher at CNRS, and the former head of the Computer Science Department and Cryptography Laboratory at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure). He is mainly known for his contributions in the area of provable security, including the [Forking lemma](wikipedia://en/19075862),[^o3] the [Pointcheval-Stern signature algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointcheval-Stern_signature_algorithm), and his contributions to [Password-authenticated key agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Password-authenticated_key_agreement).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

An alumnus of the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), David Pointcheval obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1996 from the [University of Caen Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Normandy). In 1998, he joined the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research), working within the Computer Science department of [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). Since then, his research has focused mostly on [asymmetric cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymmetric_cryptography) and [Provable security](wikipedia://en/1182348), of which he was one of the pioneers. He has authored more than 100 international publications, and co-invented a dozen patents. He was awarded a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Advanced Grant in 2015.[^o6] In 2021 he received the [RSA Award for Excellence in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_Award_for_Excellence_in_Mathematics).[^o7]
