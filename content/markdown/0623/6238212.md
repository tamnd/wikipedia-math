In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rectified 24-cell** or **rectified icositetrachoron** is a uniform 4-dimensional polytope (or [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899)), which is bounded by 48 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 24 [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285), and 24 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280). It can be obtained by [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the 24-cell, reducing its octahedral cells to cubes and cuboctahedra.[^n1]

:::

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as tC<sub>24</sub>.

It can also be considered a **cantellated 16-cell** with the lower symmetries B<sub>4</sub> = \[3,3,4\]. B<sub>4</sub> would lead to a bicoloring of the [cuboctahedral](wikipedia://en/6280) cells into 8 and 16 each. It is also called a **runcicantellated demitesseract** in a D<sub>4</sub> symmetry, giving 3 colors of cells, 8 for each.

::: {#o10 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The rectified 24-cell can be derived from the 24-cell by the process of [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366): the 24-cell is truncated at the midpoints. The vertices become [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285), while the [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) become [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

A rectified 24-cell having an edge length of $\sqrt{2}$ has vertices given by all permutations and sign permutations of the following [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate):

(0,1,1,2) \[4!/2!×2<sup>3</sup> = 96 vertices\]

The dual configuration with edge length 2 has all coordinate and sign permutations of:

(0,2,2,2) \[4×2<sup>3</sup> = 32 vertices\]

(1,1,1,3) \[4×2<sup>4</sup> = 64 vertices\]

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are three different symmetry constructions of this polytope. The lowest ${D}_4$ construction can be doubled into ${C}_4$ by adding a mirror that maps the bifurcating nodes onto each other. ${D}_4$ can be mapped up to ${F}_4$ symmetry by adding two mirror that map all three end nodes together.

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584), containing two cubes and three cuboctahedra. The three symmetries can be seen with 3 colored cuboctahedra in the lowest ${D}_4$ construction, and two colors (1:2 ratio) in ${C}_4$, and all identical cuboctahedra in ${F}_4$.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The convex hull of the rectified 24-cell and its dual (assuming that they are congruent) is a nonuniform polychoron composed of 192 cells: 48 [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285), 144 [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045), and 192 vertices. Its vertex figure is a [triangular bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3522824).
