# Reeb foliation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reeb foliation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Reeb foliation** is a particular [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) of the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792), introduced by the French mathematician [Georges Reeb](wikipedia://en/20126484) (1920–1993).

:::

It is based on dividing the sphere into two [solid tori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_tori), along a 2-[torus](wikipedia://en/74800): see [Clifford torus](wikipedia://en/7950934). Each of the solid tori is then foliated internally, in [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) 1, and the dividing torus surface forms one more leaf.

By [Novikov's compact leaf theorem](wikipedia://en/26463161), every smooth foliation of the 3-sphere includes a compact torus leaf, bounding a solid torus foliated in the same way.
