# Pierre Samuel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Samuel"}

**Pierre Samuel** (12 September 1921 – 23 August 2009[^o4]) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and its applications to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). The two-volume work *Commutative Algebra* that he wrote with [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352) is a classic. Other books of his covered [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) and [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Samuel studied at the Lycée Janson-de-Sailly in Paris before attending the École Normale Supérieure where he studied for his Agrégé de mathematique. He received his Master of Arts and then a Ph.D. from Princeton University in 1947, under the supervision of Oscar Zariski, with a thesis "Ultrafilters and Compactification of Uniform Spaces".

## Career {#Career}

Samuel ran a [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) seminar during the 1960s, and became Professeur émérite at the [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud) (Orsay). His lectures on unique factorization domains published by the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) played a significant role in computing the [Picard group](wikipedia://en/1850134) of a [Zariski surface](wikipedia://en/3096979) via the work of Jeffrey Lang and collaborators. The method was inspired by earlier work of [Nathan Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Jacobson) and [Pierre Cartier](wikipedia://en/1470549), another outstanding member of the [Bourbaki group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourbaki_group). [Nicholas Katz](wikipedia://en/1451427) related this to the concept of *p*-curvature of a connection introduced by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042).

He was a member of the [Bourbaki group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourbaki_group), and filmed some of their meetings. A French television documentary on Bourbaki broadcast some of this footage in 2000.

Samuel was also active in issues of [social justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_justice), including concerns about [environmental degradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_degradation) (where he was influenced by Grothendieck), and [arms control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arms_control).[^o7] He died in Paris in August 2009.[^o4]

His doctoral students include [Lucien Szpiro](wikipedia://en/26992384) and [Daniel Lazard](wikipedia://en/34227839).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1958 he was an invited speaker (*Relations d'équivalence en géométrie algébrique*) at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh). In 1969 he won the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award).[^o9]
