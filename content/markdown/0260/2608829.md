# Peetre's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peetre's inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Peetre's inequality,** named after [Jaak Peetre](wikipedia://en/68037102), says that for any [real number](wikipedia://en/20646438) $t$ and any [vectors](wikipedia://en/32370) $x$ and $y$ in $\mathbb{R}^n,$ the following [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489) holds:

$$\left(\frac{1+|x|^2}{1+|y|^2}\right)^t ~\leq~ 2^{|t|} (1+|x-y|^2)^{|t|}.$$

:::

The inequality was proved by J. Peetre in 1959 and has founds applications in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964).
