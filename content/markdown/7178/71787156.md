# Lorna Stewart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lorna Stewart"}

**Lorna Kay Stewart** is a retired Canadian [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [discrete mathematician](wikipedia://en/8492) whose research concerns [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and special classes of graphs, including [cographs](wikipedia://en/1175666), [permutation graphs](wikipedia://en/17625375), [interval graphs](wikipedia://en/363423), [comparability graphs](wikipedia://en/6978672) and their [complements](wikipedia://en/1175649), [well-covered graphs](wikipedia://en/32696387), and asteroidal triple-free graphs. She earned her Ph.D. in 1985 at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) under the supervision of [Derek Corneil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Corneil), and is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).

:::
