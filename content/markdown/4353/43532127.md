# Oscar Bruno

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Oscar Bruno"}

**Oscar P. Bruno** is Professor of Applied & Computational Mathematics in the Computing and Mathematical Sciences Department at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). He is known for research on [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506).

:::

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

Bruno received the Licenciado degree from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) in 1982, and he completed the PhD in mathematics at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1989.[^o4] His adviser was [Robert V. Kohn](wikipedia://en/48477231), and his dissertation was titled *The Effective Conductivity of an Infinitely Interchangeable Mixture*.[^o5] He taught at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) from 1989 to 1991, and he was at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology) from 1991 to 1995.[^o4] He has been on the faculty of the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) since 1995.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1994, Bruno was awarded an [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship).[^o8] He was inducted as a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) in 2013.[^o9]
