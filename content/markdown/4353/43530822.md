# List of shapes with known packing constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of shapes with known packing constant"}

The [packing constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_constant) of a geometric body is the largest average density achieved by packing arrangements of [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) copies of the body. For most bodies the value of the packing constant is unknown.[^o2] The following is a list of bodies in Euclidean spaces whose packing constant is known.[^o2] [Fejes Tóth](wikipedia://en/2388442) proved that in the plane, a [point symmetric](wikipedia://en/12487536) body has a packing constant that is equal to its [translative](wikipedia://en/204682) packing constant and its [lattice](wikipedia://en/661808) packing constant.[^o3] Therefore, any such body for which the lattice packing constant was previously known, such as any [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277), consequently has a known packing constant. In addition to these bodies, the packing constants of [hyperspheres](wikipedia://en/39782) in 8 and 24 dimensions are almost exactly known.[^o4]
