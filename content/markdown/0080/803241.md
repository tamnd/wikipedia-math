# Schelling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schelling"}

**Schelling** is a surname. Notable persons with that name include:

:::

- [Friedrich Wilhelm Joseph Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wilhelm_Joseph_Schelling) (1775–1854), German philosopher
- [Caroline Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Schelling) (1763–1809), German intellectual and wife of Friedrich Wilhelm Joseph Schelling
- [Felix Emanuel Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Emanuel_Schelling) (1858–1945), American educator
- [Ernest Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Schelling) (1876–1939), American composer
- [Erich Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_Schelling) (1904–1986), German architect
- [Thomas Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Schelling) (1921–2016), American economist
- [Andrew Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Schelling) (born 1953), American poet and translator
- [Hans Jörg Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Jörg_Schelling) (born 1953) Austrian entrepreneur
- [Hans Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Schelling) (1954–2008), Dutch sailor
- [Florence Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Schelling) (born 1989), Swiss ice hockey goaltender
- [Patrick Schelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Schelling) (born 1990), Swiss cyclist

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

- Schelling, Dutch name for the [shilling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shilling)
- Schelling, old name of the Dutch island of [Terschelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terschelling)
- [Schelling-Salon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schelling-Salon), building in Bavaria
