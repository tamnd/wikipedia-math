# Discrete category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrete category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **discrete category** is a category whose only [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are the [identity morphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_morphism):

:::

hom<sub>*C*</sub>(*X*, *X*) = {id<sub>*X*</sub>} for all objects *X*

hom<sub>*C*</sub>(*X*, *Y*) = ∅ for all objects *X* ≠ *Y*

Since by axioms, there is always the identity morphism between the same object, we can express the above as condition on the cardinality of the hom-set

\| hom<sub>*C*</sub>(*X*, *Y*) | is 1 when *X* = *Y* and 0 when *X* is not equal to *Y*.

Some authors prefer a weaker notion, where a discrete category merely needs to be [equivalent](wikipedia://en/379051) to such a category.

## Simple facts {#Simple_facts}

Any [class](wikipedia://en/20696) of objects defines a discrete category when augmented with identity maps.

Any [subcategory](wikipedia://en/310959) of a discrete category is discrete. Also, a category is discrete if and only if all of its subcategories are [full](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_category).

The [limit](wikipedia://en/39248) of any [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) from a discrete category into another category is called a [product](wikipedia://en/237868), while the [colimit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colimit) is called a [coproduct](wikipedia://en/310889). Thus, for example, the discrete category with just two objects can be used as a [diagram](wikipedia://en/7651625) or [diagonal functor](wikipedia://en/4606683) to define a product or coproduct of two objects. Alternately, for a general category **C** and the discrete category **2**, one can consider the [functor category](wikipedia://en/407039) **C**<sup>**2**</sup>. The diagrams of **2** in this category are pairs of objects, and the limit of the diagram is the product.

The [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) from **Set** to **Cat** that sends a set to the corresponding discrete category is [left adjoint](wikipedia://en/53991) to the functor sending a small category to its set of objects. (For the right adjoint, see [indiscrete category](wikipedia://en/46876400).)
