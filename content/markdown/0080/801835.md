# Jules Antoine Lissajous

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jules Antoine Lissajous"}

**Jules Antoine Lissajous** (; 4 March 1822 in [Versailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles_%28city%29) – 24 June 1880 in [Plombières-les-Dijon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plombières-lès-Dijon)) was a French [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), after whom [Lissajous figures](wikipedia://en/753756) are named. Among other innovations, Lissajous invented the Lissajous apparatus, a device that creates the figures that bear his name. In it, a beam of light is bounced off a mirror attached to a vibrating [tuning fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuning_fork), and then reflected off a second mirror attached to a perpendicularly oriented vibrating tuning fork (usually of a different [pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_%28music%29), creating a specific harmonic interval), onto a wall, resulting in a Lissajous figure. This led to the invention of other apparatus such as the [harmonograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonograph).
