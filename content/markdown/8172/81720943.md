# Lei Ni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lei Ni"}

**Lei Ni** (born 1969) is a mathematician. He holds [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) status at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) (UCSD). He was elected a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in 2018.

:::

Ni was born in 1969,[^o2] and from 1987 to 1993 attended [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in China, from where he received a B.S. and M.S. degree in mathematics. He studied at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), from 1994 to 1998, and received a Ph.D. in mathematics,[^o3] advised by [Peter Wai-Kwong Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Wai-Kwong_Li).[^o4] Ni was an assistant professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) from 1998 to 2000 and at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 2000 to 2002, before moving to UC San Diego in 2002.[^o3]

From 2002 to 2005, Ni worked as an assistant professor at UCSD, and from 2005 to 2009 as an associate professor.[^o3] He received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2004.[^o5] Ni served as chair of the mathematics department from 2016 to 2020,[^o3] during which time, in 2018,[^o6] he was elected a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) for "contributions to [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), particularly to [Ricci flow](wikipedia://en/288291), and for mentorship".[^o7] Since 2025, Ni has been an emeritus professor with Distinguished Professor title,[^o3] in recognition of his career at the university.[^o8]

His research areas include geometric analysis, [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o9] He has given talks on these topics to the [Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/401367).[^o10] His publications on the Ricci flow include a 2006 book in the [Graduate Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871519) series,[^o11][^o12] and a four-part book in [Mathematical Surveys and Monographs](wikipedia://en/28100142).[^o13][^o14]
