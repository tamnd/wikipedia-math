# Heinz Bauer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz Bauer"}

**Heinz Bauer** (31 January 1928 – 15 August 2002) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Bauer studied at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg) and received his PhD there in 1953 under the supervision of [Otto Haupt](wikipedia://en/31696723) and finished his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1956, both for work with Otto Haupt. After a short time from 1961 to 1965 as professor at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) he stayed his whole career at the University of Erlangen-Nuremberg. His research focuses were [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

Bauer received the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) in 1980[^o3] and became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) in 1986. Bauer died in Erlangen.
