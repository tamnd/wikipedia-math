# Bernadette Perrin-Riou

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernadette Perrin-Riou"}

**Bernadette Perrin-Riou** (born 1 August 1955) is a French [number theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theorist).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Perrin-Riou was born on 1 August 1955 in [Les Vans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Vans), [Ardèche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardèche), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). Her parents had both had a scientific education;[^o3] her mother and father were a physicist and chemist, respectively. She was brought up, along with her sisters, in [Neuilly-sur-Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuilly-sur-Seine).[^o4][^o5]

## Education {#Education}

She entered the [Ecole Normale Supérieure de Jeunes Filles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1974, completing her undergraduate work in 1977. She then took a research assistant position at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris.[^o5] She received an advanced degree from [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 1979, working with [Georges Poitou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Poitou);[^o4] she then obtained a doctoral degree from the Pierre and Marie Curie University in 1983. Her thesis advisor was [John H. Coates](wikipedia://en/230705), and her thesis was entitled "Arithmetique des courbes elliptiques et théorie d'Iwasawa" ("Arithmetic of elliptic curves and Iwasawa theory").[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

She became [maître de conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conferences) at UPMC in 1983, and was then invited to spend a year as a visiting professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University); she subsequently became a professor at the same university.[^o5]

In 1994 she moved to a position at [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay)[^o4] In the same year, she was invited to give an address at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), which was held in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich),[^o5] which she gave on "Fonctions L p-adiques" ("*p*-adic *L*-functions").[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Perrin-Riou's research is in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), concentrating on [*p*-adic *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/291327) and [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150).[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

She was awarded the [Ruth Lyttle Satter Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/37554140) prize in 1999, a prize established in 1990 for women in maths.[^o3]
