# Taft Hopf algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taft Hopf algebra"}

In algebra, a **Taft Hopf algebra** is a [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) introduced by (txt) that is neither [commutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative) nor [cocommutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocommutative) and has an [antipode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipode_%28algebra%29) of large even order.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

Suppose that *k* is a [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_%28algebra%29) with a primitive *n**th [root of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) ζ for some positive integer*****n*. The Taft algebra is the*n*<sup>2</sup>-dimensional [associative algebra](wikipedia://en/2112) generated over*k*by*c*and*x*with the relations*c*<sup>*n*</sup>=1,*x*<sup>*n*</sup>=0,*xc*\=ζ*cx*. The coproduct takes*c*to*c*⊗*c*and*x*to*c*⊗*x*\+*x*⊗1.*** The counit takes *c* to 1 and *x* to 0. The antipode takes *c* to *c*<sup>−1</sup> and *x* to –*c*<sup>−1</sup>*x*: the order of the antipode is 2*n* (if *n* > 1).

:::
