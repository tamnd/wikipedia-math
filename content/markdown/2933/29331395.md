# National Bureau of Statistics (Tanzania)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="National Bureau of Statistics"}

The **National Bureau of Statistics** is a branch of the [Government of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Tanzania) which has the mandate to provide official statistics to the Government of [Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzania), business community and the public at large. It is based in [Dodoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodoma) and obtains a wide range of economic, social and demographic statistics about the country. The bureau compiled data on every village in Tanzania for the censuses of [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzanian_census_%282002%29), 2012 and 2022, but also provides the results of the previous censuses of 1967, 1978 and 1988.[^o3]

:::
