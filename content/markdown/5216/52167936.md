# Secant plane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Secant plane"}

*Image: Ellipsoid\_circular\_sections.gif.* Animation showing the circular secant planes of the ellipsoid

A **secant plane** is a [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) containing a nontrivial [section](wikipedia://en/1337370) of a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) or an [ellipsoid](wikipedia://en/145381), or such a plane that a sphere is projected onto. Secant planes are similar to tangent planes, which contact the sphere's surface at a point, while secant planes contact the surface along curves.

:::

The two-dimensional representations of secant planes are [secant lines](wikipedia://en/241886), the lines that join two distinct points on a curve.

::: {#o3 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

300px Secant planes are used in [map projections](wikipedia://en/51784). The secant plane intersects a globe along a small circle with no distortion, forming a [standard parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_parallel_%28map_projections%29) which has true scale.[^o4]
