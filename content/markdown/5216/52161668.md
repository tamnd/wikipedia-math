# Nairi Sedrakyan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nairi Sedrakyan"}

**Nairi Sedrakyan** (born 1961 in [Ninotsminda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninotsminda), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR)) is Erdős Award 2022 winner [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) involved in national and international Olympiads, including American Mathematics Competitions (USA) and [IMO](wikipedia://en/82208), having been the president of the Armenian Mathematics Olympiads, the Leader of Armenian [IMO](wikipedia://en/82208) Team,[^o3] a jury member and problem selection committee member of the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208),[^o4] a jury member and problem selection committee member of the Zhautykov International Mathematical Olympiad (IZhO),[^o5] a jury member and problem selection committee member of the International Olympiad of Metropolises,[^o6] the president and organizer of the International Mathematical Olympiad [Tournament of the Towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tournament_of_the_Towns) in the Republic of Armenia (1986-2013).[^o7] He has also authored a large number of problems proposed in these Olympiads. The [government of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Armenia) awarded the author the title of the best teacher of Armenia and he received a special gift from the Prime Minister.[^o8] Nairi Sedrakyan's son Hayk Sedrakyan is also a professional mathematician and former IMO competitor.[^o9][^o10]

:::

Nairi Sedrakyan is known for [Sedrakyan's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedrakyan's_inequality).[^o11]

Nairi Sedrakyan has authored 14 books and around 70 articles in different countries (USA, Switzerland, South Korea, Russia) on the topic of [problem solving](wikipedia://en/1467948) and Olympiad style mathematics.[^o12][^o13]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Nairi Sedrakyan was born in the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) in the town of [Ninotsminda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninotsminda) in the [Georgian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_SSR). At the age of 14 years old he left [Ninotsminda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninotsminda) for [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan), [Armenian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_SSR) to advance his mathematical knowledge and to study mathematics at the [PhysMath School after A. Shahinyan (Yerevan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhysMath_School_after_A._Shahinyan_%28Yerevan%29), one of the leading schools in Armenia. He continued his Bachelor, Master and PhD studies at the [Yerevan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_State_University) in the faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics, widely regarded by students as one of the toughest faculties in the university.[^o8]

## Mathematics coaching and Olympiads {#Mathematics_coaching_and_Olympiads}

The students of Nairi Sedrakyan have obtained 20 medals (1 gold medal, 4 silver medals, 15 bronze medals, one of which was received by his son Hayk Sedrakyan) in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), providing more than half of the medals that Armenia received in the history of its participation in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208). So far it is the single Gold Medal of Armenia in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o16]
