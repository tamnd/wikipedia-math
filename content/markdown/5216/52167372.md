# Heike Hofmann

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Heike Hofmann"}

**Heike Hofmann** (born 16 April 1972) is a [statistician](wikipedia://en/26685) and Professor in the Department of Statistics at [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) and was previously at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University).[^o6][^o7][^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

She earned an [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in Mathematics, with a minor in Computer Science, and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Statistics, from the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg), [Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) in 1998 and 2000, respectively.[^o2]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Hofmann's research interests are in [statistical graphics](wikipedia://en/15934463), [exploratory data analysis](wikipedia://en/35458904), [visual inference](wikipedia://en/317465), [visualization of large data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization) and [statistical computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_computing)[^o5][^o11][^o12] She is currently Professor in the Department of Statistics at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University),[^o13] and faculty member of the [Bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214) and [Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_Biology) and [Human Computer Interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Computer_Interaction) programs.

In her research on [interactive data visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interactive_data_visualization) she has provided new approaches for plotting [multivariate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) [categorical data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_data) using [mosaic plots](wikipedia://en/42637526), and making interactions with these plots, and linking between plots. She was the primary development of the software [MANET](https://rosuda.org/MANET/) and contributed to the development of the software [GGobi](wikipedia://en/13543254). More recent software include the [R](wikipedia://en/376707) packages x3prplus, geomnet, nullabor, gglogo, peptider, discreteRV, ggboxplots, ggparallel, dbData, HLMdiag, lvboxplots, MergeGUI, MissingDataGUI. Her work on examining the inflow of corporate cash into the 2012 US presidential election can be read in [*Chance* magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chance_%28statistics_magazine%29).[^o14]

Heike Hofmann is the author of more than 50 journal articles, conference proceedings, book chapters and edited one book. She is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). She has supervised or co-supervised 8 doctoral theses,[^o2] including [Hadley Wickham](wikipedia://en/41916270)[^o15] and [Yihui Xie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yihui_Xie).[^o3]

### Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

She was elected a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2014.[^o17]
