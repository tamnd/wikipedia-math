# Turán's method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Turán's method"}

In mathematics, **Turán's method** provides lower bounds for [exponential sums](wikipedia://en/468937) and complex [power sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_sum). The method has been applied to problems in [equidistribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equidistribution).

:::

The method applies to sums of the form

$$s_\nu = \sum_{n=1}^N b_n z_n^\nu \$$

where the *b* and *z* are [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) and *ν* runs over a range of integers. There are two main results, depending on the size of the complex numbers *z*.

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Turán's first theorem"}

## Turán's first theorem {#Turán's_first_theorem}

The first result applies to sums *s*<sub>ν</sub> where $|z_n| \ge 1$ for all *n*. For any range of *ν* of length *N*, say *ν* = *M* + 1, ..., *M* + *N*, there is some *ν* with |*s*<sub>*ν*</sub>| at least *c*(*M*, *N*)|*s*<sub>0</sub>| where

$$c(M,N) = \left({ \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \binom{M+k}{k} 2^k }\right)^{-1} \ .$$

The sum here may be replaced by the weaker but simpler $\left({ \frac{N}{2e(M+N)} }\right)^{N-1}$.

We may deduce the [Fabry gap theorem](wikipedia://en/39697327) from this result.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Turán's second theorem"}

## Turán's second theorem {#Turán's_second_theorem}

The second result applies to sums *s*<sub>ν</sub> where $|z_n| \le 1$ for all *n*. Assume that the *z* are ordered in decreasing absolute value and scaled so that |*z*<sub>1</sub>| = 1. Then there is some ν with

$$|s_\nu| \ge 2 \left({ \frac{N}{8e(M+N)} }\right)^N \min_{1\le j\le N} \left\vert{\sum_{n=1}^j b_n }\right\vert \ .$$

:::
