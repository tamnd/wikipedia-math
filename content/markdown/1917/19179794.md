# Horikawa surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horikawa surface"}

In mathematics, a **Horikawa surface** is one of the [surfaces of general type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_general_type) introduced by [Eiji Horikawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiji_Horikawa).

:::

These are surfaces with *q* = 0 and *p<sub>g</sub>* = *c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>/2 + 2 or *c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>/2 + 3/2 (which implies that they are more or less on the [Noether line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_line) edge of the region of possible values of the [Chern numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_number)).

They are all simply connected, and Horikawa gave a detailed description of them.
