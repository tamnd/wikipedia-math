# Ernest Vinberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernest Vinberg"}

**Ernest Borisovich Vinberg** (Эрне́ст Бори́сович Ви́нберг; 26 July 1937 – 12 May 2020) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician, who worked on Lie groups and algebraic groups, [discrete subgroups](wikipedia://en/468536) of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), invariant theory, and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He introduced [Vinberg's algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinberg's_algorithm) and the [Koecher–Vinberg theorem](wikipedia://en/37016909).

:::

He was a recipient of the 1997 [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o4] He was on the executive committee of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251). In 1983, he was an Invited Speaker with a talk on *Discrete reflection groups in Lobachevsky spaces* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw). In 2010, he was elected an International Honorary Member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5]

Ernest Vinberg died from [pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia) caused by [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) on 12 May 2020.[^o6][^o7] His ashes were interred in the columbarium at [Vagankovo Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vagankovo_Cemetery).
