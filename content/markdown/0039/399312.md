# 58 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="58"}

**58** (**fifty-eight**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/57_%28number%29) and preceding [59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/59_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

58 is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), a [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542),[^o4] and a [square-free integer](wikipedia://en/29525).[^o5]

58 is the sum of the [fir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_%28number%29)[st](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3_%28number%29) [sev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_%28number%29)[en](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29) [pr](wikipedia://en/208156)[im](wikipedia://en/37231)[es](wikipedia://en/208288).
