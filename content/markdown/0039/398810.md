# List of graphical methods

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of graphical methods"}

Included are [diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagram) techniques, [chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chart) techniques, [plot](wikipedia://en/19774918) techniques, and other forms of [visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization).

:::
