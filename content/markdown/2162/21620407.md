# Supersolvable arrangement

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Supersolvable arrangement"}

In mathematics, a **supersolvable arrangement** is a [hyperplane arrangement](wikipedia://en/864438) that has a maximal [flag](wikipedia://en/390646) consisting of [modular](wikipedia://en/1089311) elements. Equivalently, the [intersection semilattice](wikipedia://en/864438#The_intersection_semilattice_and_the_matroid) of the arrangement is a [supersolvable lattice](wikipedia://en/75629307), in the sense of [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397).[^o2] As shown by [Hiroaki Terao](wikipedia://en/56374155), a complex hyperplane arrangement is supersolvable if and only if its complement is fiber-type.[^o3]

:::

Examples include arrangements associated with [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) of type A and B.

The [Orlik–Solomon algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlik–Solomon_algebra) of every supersolvable arrangement is a [Koszul algebra](wikipedia://en/7987653); whether the converse is true is an open problem.[^o4]
