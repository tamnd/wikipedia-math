# Alfred Frölicher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Frölicher"}

**Alfred Frölicher** (often misspelled Fröhlicher) was a Swiss mathematician (8 October 8 1927 – 1 July 2010). He was a full professor at the [Université de Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Fribourg) (1962–1965), and then at the [Université de Genève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Genève) (1966–1993). He introduced the [Frölicher spectral sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frölicher_spectral_sequence), the [Frölicher–Nijenhuis bracket](wikipedia://en/3291722), [Frölicher spaces](wikipedia://en/2229523) and [Frölicher groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frölicher_group).

:::

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 1954, with thesis *Zur Differentialgeometrie der komplexe Strukturen* written under the direction of [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365) and [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082).
