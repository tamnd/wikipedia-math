# Éric Leichtnam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Éric Leichtnam"}

**Éric Leichtnam** is director of research at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the [Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris. His fields of interest are [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [Dirichlet problem](wikipedia://en/571109), [non-commutative residue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-commutative_residue).

:::
