# Holomorphic Lefschetz fixed-point formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Holomorphic Lefschetz fixed-point formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Holomorphic Lefschetz formula** is an analogue for [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) of the [Lefschetz fixed-point formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lefschetz_fixed-point_formula) that relates a sum over the [fixed points](wikipedia://en/449738) of a holomorphic [vector field](wikipedia://en/62641) of a compact complex manifold to a sum over its [Dolbeault cohomology](wikipedia://en/3189601) groups.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

If *f* is an [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) of a compact complex manifold *M* with isolated fixed points, then

$$\sum_{f(p)=p}\frac{1}{\det (1-A_p)} = \sum_q(-1)^q\operatorname{trace}(f^*|H^{0,q}_{\overline\partial}(M))$$

where

- The sum is over the fixed points *p* of *f*
- The linear transformation *A*<sub>*p*</sub> is the action induced by *f* on the holomorphic [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) at *p*

:::
