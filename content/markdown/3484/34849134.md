# Ludvig Oppermann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ludvig Oppermann"}

**Ludvig Henrik Ferdinand Oppermann** (September 7, 1817 – August 17, 1883) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danes) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [philologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philologist) who formulated [Oppermann's conjecture](wikipedia://en/29543748) on the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o2]

:::
