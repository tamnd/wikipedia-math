# Yuri Aleksandrovich Brychkov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Aleksandrovich Brychkov"}

**Yury Aleksandrovich Brychkov** (Юрий Александрович Брычков; born 29 February 1944 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He graduated from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1966 and worked on [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) at the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), under the supervision of [Yuri Mikhailovich Shirokov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yurii_Shirokov). He received his PhD in 1971 and he has been with the [Dorodnicyn Computing Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorodnicyn_Computing_Centre) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1969.

Yu. A. Brychkov has worked on various topics of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210), and he has made contributions to the fields of [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) and [integral transforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_transforms). He has also worked on the computer implementation of special functions at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo),[^o3] [Maplesoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maplesoft), and [Wolfram Research](wikipedia://en/594109).[^o4] He is a founding editor of the *Journal of Integral Transforms and Special Functions*,[^o5] and has authored a number of handbooks, including the five volume *Integrals and Series* (Gordon and Breach Science Publishers, 1986–1992).[^o6]
