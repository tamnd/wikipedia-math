# Demiregular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demiregular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **demiregular tilings** are a set of Euclidean [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) made from 2 or more [regular polygon](wikipedia://en/333306) faces. Different authors have listed different sets of tilings. A more systematic approach looking at [symmetry orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_orbit) are the [2-uniform tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling) of which there are 20. Some of the demiregular ones are actually [3-uniform tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-uniform_tiling).

:::
