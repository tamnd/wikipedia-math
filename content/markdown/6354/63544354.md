# Rigidity (K-theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rigidity"}

In mathematics, **rigidity of *K*-theory** encompasses results relating [algebraic *K*-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) of different rings.

:::

## Suslin rigidity {#Suslin_rigidity}

*Suslin rigidity*, named after [Andrei Suslin](wikipedia://en/7111820), refers to the invariance of mod-*n* algebraic *K*-theory under the base change between two [algebraically closed fields](wikipedia://en/1018): (Suslin 1983) showed that for an [extension](wikipedia://en/11634)

$$E / F$$

of algebraically closed fields, and an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) *X* / *F*, there is an isomorphism

$$K_*(X, \mathbf Z/n) \cong K_*(X \times_F E, \mathbf Z/n),$$

between the mod-*n* *K*-theory of coherent sheaves on *X*, respectively its base change to *E*. A textbook account of this fact in the case *X* = *F*, including the resulting computation of *K*-theory of algebraically closed fields in characteristic *p*, is in (Weibel 2013).

This result has stimulated various other papers. For example (Röndigs Østvær 2008) show that the base change functor for the mod-*n* [stable A<sup>1</sup>-homotopy category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_homotopy_theory)

$$\mathrm{SH}(F, \mathbf Z/n) \to \mathrm{SH}(E, \mathbf Z/n)$$

is fully faithful. A similar statement for non-commutative motives has been established by (Tabuada 2018).

## Gabber rigidity {#Gabber_rigidity}

Another type of rigidity relates the mod-*n* K-theory of an [henselian ring](wikipedia://en/14623383) *A* to the one of its [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) *A*/*m*. This rigidity result is referred to as *Gabber rigidity*, in view of the work of (Gabber 1992) who showed that there is an isomorphism

$$K_*(A, \mathbf Z/n) = K_*(A / m, \mathbf Z/n)$$

provided that *n*≥1 is an integer which is invertible in *A*.

If *n* is not invertible in *A*, the result as above still holds, provided that K-theory is replaced by the fiber of the [cyclotomic trace map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclotomic_trace_map) between K-theory and [topological cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172). This was shown by (Clausen Mathew Morrow 2021).

::: {#o11 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

(Jardine 1993) used Gabber's and Suslin's rigidity result to reprove Quillen's computation of [K-theory of finite fields](wikipedia://en/42177602).

:::
