# Neža Mramor Kosta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Neža Mramor Kosta"}

**Neža Mramor Kosta**, also known as **Nežka Mramor Kosta,** is a Slovenian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) retired as a professor of computer and information science at the [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana).[^o3] She works in [computational logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_logic). She is an alumni of [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington).[^o4]

:::
