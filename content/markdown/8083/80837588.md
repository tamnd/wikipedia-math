# Barry Mitchell (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barry Mitchell"}

**Barry M. Mitchell** (25 June 1933 – 26 January 2021) was a Canadian[^o3] mathematician known for his contribution in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869). He wrote one of the earliest books on category theory (1965).[^o4]

:::

He proved what is now known as [Mitchell's embedding theorem](wikipedia://en/453755),[^o5] also known as *Freyd-Mitchell embedding theorem or full imbedding theorem.* This is a well-known result about [abelian categories](wikipedia://en/45063).

## Education and academic life {#Education_and_academic_life}

He graduated from the University of Toronto with both his bachelor's and master's degrees and obtained his Ph.D. at Brown University. His PhD advisor is [David Alvin Buchsbaum](wikipedia://en/30781131) and thesis title is *Homological Tic Tac Toe*.[^o7]

He taught at Columbia University, Bowdoin College, and Rutgers University during his academic career.[^o8] Among his works are two textbooks: Calculus Without Analytic Geometry and the pioneering Theory of Categories.[^o8]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Barry Mitchell, Theory of Categories.[^o4]
- Barry Mitchell, Calculus: Without Analytic Geometry.[^o10]
- Barry Mitchell, *The Full Imbedding Theorem*, American Journal of Mathematics **86** 3 (1964) 619–637
