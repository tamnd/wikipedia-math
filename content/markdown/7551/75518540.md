# Walter E. Hoadley

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Walter E. Hoadley"}

**Walter E. Hoadley** (1916 – 2003) was an American business economist, statistician, and Federal Reserve Bank chair. He served as president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and the [American Finance Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Finance_Association).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Hoadley earned three academic degrees at the University of California, Berkeley, an A.B. in 1938, an M.A. in 1940, and a Ph.D. in economics in 1946.[^o4]

He went on to serve as an economics professor at UC Berkeley, and a vice president of the [Bank of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_America).[^o4] Hoadley retired from the [Bank of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_America) in 1981. He developed expertise in economic forecasting, global financial markets, planning, and management.[^o5] The [Commonwealth Club of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Club_of_California) invited him to give public speeches 25 times.

After retiring from the Bank of America in 1981, he joined the [Hoover Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoover_Institution) as a senior scholar.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Hoadley married college classmate Virginia Alm and they were together more than 60 years. He was a devoted Methodist and spoke frequently of the importance of his religion to him (Miller, p.v).[^o7]
