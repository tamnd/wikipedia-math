# David Wood (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Wood"}

*Image: David Ronald Wood, October 2018.jpg.* David Ronald Wood, October 2018

**David Ronald Wood** (born in [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch), [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand) in 1971) is a Professor in the School of Mathematics at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) in [Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne), Australia. His research area is [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392), especially structural graph theory, [extremal graph theory](wikipedia://en/529568), [geometric graph theory](wikipedia://en/3313527), [graph colouring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_colouring), [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813), and [combinatorial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry).

:::

Wood received a Ph.D. in computer science from Monash University in 2000. His thesis "Three-Dimensional Orthogonal Graph Drawing", supervised by Graham Farr, was awarded a [Mollie Holman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mollie_Holman) Doctoral Medal. He held postdoctoral research positions at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), at [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) in Ottawa, at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in Prague, at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in Montreal, at [Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universitat_Politècnica_de_Catalunya) in Barcelona, and at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne). Since 2012 he has been at Monash University, where he was promoted to Professor in 2016.[^o2]

He has been awarded distinguished research fellowships including a [Marie Curie Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie_Fellowship) from the [European Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commission) (2006–2008), a QEII Fellowship from the [Australian Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Research_Council) (2008–2012), and a Future Fellowship from the Australian Research Council (2014–2017).[^o3] David Wood was an invited speaker at the 9th European Congress of Mathematics.[^o4]

Wood is a Fellow of the [Australian Mathematics Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Mathematics_Society) and life member of the [Combinatorial Mathematics Society of Australasia](wikipedia://en/53927291) (CMSA). He was president of the CMSA in 2015–2016 and Vice-President in 2011–2014.[^o5] He is a Deputy Director of The Mathematical Research Institute MATRIX.[^o6] Wood is an editor of the *[Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*,[^o7] Editor-in-Chief of the MATRIX Book Series,[^o8] and an editor of the *[Journal of Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Computational_Geometry)*,[^o9] [Journal of Graph Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Graph_Theory),[^o10] and [SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/30666823).[^o11] His main research contributions are in graph product structure theory, extremal graph minor theory, graph [treewidth](wikipedia://en/3987086), graphs on surfaces, graph colouring, geometric graph theory, poset dimension, and graph drawing.
