# Curtis Cooper (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Curtis Cooper"}

**Curtis Niles Cooper** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was a professor at the [University of Central Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Missouri), in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science.

:::

## GIMPS {#GIMPS}

Using software from the [GIMPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIMPS) project, Cooper and Steven Boone found the 43rd known [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908) on their 700 [PC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer) [cluster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_cluster) on December 15, 2005. The prime, 2<sup>30,402,457</sup> − 1, is 9,152,052 digits long and is the ninth Mersenne prime for GIMPS.[^o4]

Cooper and Boone became the first GIMPS contributors to find two primes when they also found the 44th known Mersenne prime, 2<sup>32,582,657</sup> − 1 (or M<sub>32,582,657</sub>), which has 9,808,358 digits. This prime was discovered on September 4, 2006, using a PC cluster of over 850 machines. This is the tenth Mersenne prime for GIMPS.[^o5]

On January 25, 2013, Cooper found his third Mersenne prime of 2<sup>57,885,161</sup> − 1.[^o6]

On September 17, 2015, Cooper's computer reported yet another Mersenne prime, 2<sup>74,207,281</sup> − 1, which was the [largest known prime number](wikipedia://en/1669332) at 22,338,618 decimal digits. The report was, however, unnoticed until January 7, 2016.[^o7]

## Areas of research {#Areas_of_research}

Cooper's own work has mainly been in elementary [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), especially work related to digital representations of numbers. He collaborated extensively with Robert E. Kennedy. They have worked with [Niven numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niven_number), among other results, showing that no 21 consecutive integers can all be Niven numbers,[^o9] and introduced the notion of [tau numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_number), numbers whose total number of divisors are itself a divisor of the number.[^o10] Independent of Kennedy, Cooper has also done work about generalizations of [geometric series](wikipedia://en/12630), and their application to [probability](wikipedia://en/22934).[^o11]

Cooper is also the editor of the publication *[Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*.
