# Michael Barr (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Barr"}

**Michael Barr** (born January 22, 1937) is an American mathematician who is the [Peter Redpath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Redpath) Emeritus Professor of Pure Mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

He was born in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia), [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania), and graduated from the 202nd class of [Central High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_High_School_%28Philadelphia%29) in June 1954. He graduated from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in February 1959 and received a PhD from the same school in June 1962.

## Career {#Career}

Barr studied mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania, graduating with a bachelor's degree in 1959 and a doctorate in 1962 under [David Kent Harrison](wikipedia://en/58145902) *(Cohomology of Commutative Algebras*). He was then an instructor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and from 1964 Assistant Professor and later Associate Professor at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign). In 1968 he became Associate Professor and in 1972 Professor at McGill University.

In 1967 and 1975/76 he was a visiting scientist at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and in 1970/71 at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) and in 1989/90 a visiting professor at the University of Pennsylvania.

In 1970 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice) *(Non-abelian full embedding: outline*).

His earlier work was in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), but his principal research area for a number of years has been [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869). He is well known to [theoretical computer scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_computer_scientist) for his book *Category Theory for Computing Science* (1990) with [Charles Wells](wikipedia://en/34749968), as well as for the development of [\*-autonomous categories](wikipedia://en/3865506) and [Chu spaces](wikipedia://en/4362087) which have found various applications in computer science. His monograph *\*-autonomous categories* (1979), and his books *Toposes, Triples, and Theories* (1985),[^o6][^o7] also coauthored with Wells, and *Acyclic Models* (2002), are aimed at more specialized audiences. In 2011 Michael Barr and his wife Marcia published an English translation of [Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042)'s fundamental [*Tôhoku* paper](wikipedia://en/44572732).

Barr is on the editorial boards of *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science* and the electronic journal *[Homology, Homotopy and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology,_Homotopy_and_Applications)*, and is editor of the electronic journal *Theory and Applications of Categories*.
