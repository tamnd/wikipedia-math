# David Williams (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Williams"}

**David Williams** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) is a Welsh mathematician who works in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

David Williams was born at [Gorseinon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorseinon), near [Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea), Wales. He was educated at [Gowerton Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gowerton_Comprehensive_School), winning a mathematics scholarship to [Jesus College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Oxford), and went on to obtain a [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPhil) under the supervision of [David George Kendall](wikipedia://en/4631370) and [Gerd Edzard Harry Reuter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerd_Edzard_Harry_Reuter), with a thesis titled *Random time substitution in Markov chains*.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Williams held posts at the [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (1962–63), [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Durham), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (1966–69), and at [Swansea University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University) (1969–85), where he was promoted to a [personal chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_chair) in 1972.

In 1985, he was elected to the [Professorship of Mathematical Statistics, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship_of_Mathematical_Statistics,_University_of_Cambridge), where he remained until 1992, serving as Director of the [Statistical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge) between 1987 and 1991.[^o6] Following this, he held the Chair of Mathematical Sciences jointly with the Mathematics and Statistics Groups at the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath).

In 1999, he returned to Swansea University, where he currently holds a Research Professorship.

Williams's research interests encompass [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/149984), [diffusions](wikipedia://en/1667059), [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536) and [Wiener&ndash;Hopf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiener&ndash;Hopf) theory. Recognition for his work includes being elected Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) in 1984, where he was cited for his achievements on the construction problem for Markov chains and on path decompositions for Brownian motion,[^o7] and being awarded the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)'s [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28LMS%29) in 1994.[^o8]

One of his main discoveries is the decomposition of Brownian paths with respect to their maximum.[^o9]

He is the author of *Probability With Martingales* and *Weighing the Odds*, and co-author (with [L. C. G. Rogers](wikipedia://en/22806119)) of both volumes of *Diffusions, Markov Processes and Martingales*.
