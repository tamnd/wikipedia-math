# Clausen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clausen"}

**Clausen** is a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language) [patronymic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic) surname, literally meaning *child of Claus*, Claus being a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) form of the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) Νικόλαος, Nikolaos, (cf. [Nicholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas)), used in Denmark at least since the 16th century.[^o2] The spelling variant **Klausen** has identical pronunciation (as does the often interchangeable **Claussen**). The two variants are number 34 and 85 on the top 100 of surnames in Denmark.[^o3] Occurrences of Clausen/Klausen as a surname outside Denmark and [Schleswig-Holstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein) are due to [immigration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration). Immigrants to English-speaking countries sometimes changed the spelling to [Clauson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clauson).

:::
