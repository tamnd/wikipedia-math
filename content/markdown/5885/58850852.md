# Barbara Trader Faires

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Trader Faires"}

**Barbara Trader Faires** (born 1943) is an American mathematician who served as professor, department chair, and Vice President for Academic Affairs at [Westminster College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_College_%28Pennsylvania%29) in [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania) and served for 8 years as Secretary[^o2] of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA).[^o3] She received the Yueh-Gin Gung and Dr. Charles Y. Hu Award for Distinguished Service to Mathematics from the MAA in 2022.[^o4] She is now retired and living in [Pulaski Township, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulaski_Township,_Lawrence_County,_Pennsylvania).

:::

Faires earned her undergraduate degree at [East Carolina University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Carolina_University), double-majoring in mathematics and business.[^o3] She earned her doctorate at Kent State University in 1974; her dissertation title was "On [Grothendieck Spaces](wikipedia://en/17893372) and Vector Measures" and completed under the supervision of [Joseph Diestel](wikipedia://en/57504979). Faires extended her studies into computer science during a sabbatical leave at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) where she also had a visiting position. Another visiting position took her to [Westminster College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_College,_Oxford) in [Oxford, UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford).[^o3]

She was married to mathematician J. Douglas Faires (1941-2012)[^o6] who taught at nearby [Youngstown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown_State_University) in [Youngstown, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown,_Ohio). Together they authored several calculus textbooks.
