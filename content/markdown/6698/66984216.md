# I. Elaine Allen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="I. Elaine Allen"}

**Isabel Elaine Allen** is an American [survey statistician](wikipedia://en/272134) and [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) who works in [distance education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_education), the theory of [Likert scales](wikipedia://en/454402), antibacterial treatments, and [meta-analysis](wikipedia://en/62329) of medical trials. She is a professor of biostatistics and [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) at the [University of California, San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Francisco), and a professor emeritus of statistics at [Babson College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babson_College).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Allen majored in psychology at [Skidmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skidmore_College), graduating in 1970. She earned a master's degree in mathematics at the [University of Evansville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Evansville) in 1975, and completed her Ph.D. in statistics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1979.

She took a faculty position at the [Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania) even before completing her doctorate, and also held faculty positions at the [Medical College of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_College_of_Pennsylvania) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) prior to her position at [Babson College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babson_College). She also taught at [University of California, San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Francisco).

As well as her academic work, Allen has helped to found several companies, including the Quahog Research Group, [ARIAD Pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARIAD_Pharmaceuticals), Pondview Associates, StatSystems, and Bay View Analytics.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Allen was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2004. She was the main statistical expert for an [investigative journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_journalism) project concerning [fair housing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_housing), "Long Island Divided", which won the 2020 [Edward R. Murrow Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_R._Murrow_Award_%28Radio_Television_Digital_News_Association%29) of the [Radio Television Digital News Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Television_Digital_News_Association) for a news documentary by a large digital news organization.

Skidmore College gave her their Creative Thought Matters Award of Distinction award in 2015.
