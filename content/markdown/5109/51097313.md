# Rendiconti di Matematica e delle sue Applicazioni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rendiconti di Matematica e delle sue Applicazioni"}

The ***Rendiconti di Matematica e delle sue Applicazioni*** (*Reports on Mathematics and its applications*) is an open access [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal), jointly published by the "Guido Castelnuovo" Department of Mathematics of the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and by the [Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/34249626),[^o4] established in 1913.[^o5] The Journal started his publications a year after, in 1914, and his first director was [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra).[^o6]

:::

It publishes research articles in [pure](wikipedia://en/457210) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), without imposing restrictions on the length of the work: for this reason, the submission of surveys, articles of foundational nature and [doctoral dissertations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_dissertation) is also encouraged.[^o7] As in every [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_reviewed) journal, every article is refereed, and the journal adheres to the [EMS Code of Practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Mathematical_Society_Code_of_Practice).[^o8]

The journal is [abstracted and indexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstracting_and_indexing) by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*, *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)* and *[Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)*.

## Historical notes {#Historical_notes}

### Foundation and the first four series {#Foundation_and_the_first_four_series}

The journal was founded in 1913 as the press organ of the "Seminario Matematico della Facoltà di Scienze della Reale Uninversità di Roma":[^o5] its first director ([Editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief)) was Vito Volterra,[^o6] who held this position from its foundation to the year 1921. The direction passed to [Guido Castelnuovo](wikipedia://en/1558891) who held it from 1921 to 1922:[^o13] with the beginning of and during the publishing of the whole second and third series of the journal, from 1922 to 1935 [Federigo Enriques](wikipedia://en/1732661) was the director.[^o14] During fourth series of the journal, at the starting of which it underwent its first name change, the direction passed to [Gaetano Scorza](wikipedia://en/35074517), who held it until his death in 1939.[^o15]

### The INdAM and its influence {#The_INdAM_and_its_influence}

The founding of the [Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Nazionale_di_Alta_Matematica) in 1939, under the decisive influence of [Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/1565716),[^o17] had important consequences on the "Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico". On 23 November 1939, during its first meeting, the Scientific [Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council)[^o18] of the then newborn institute,[^o19] considering the need of a means to publish the results of the research done by the members and the students working at the institute,[^o20] decided to merge the needed means and the already existing journal in one single entity, giving birth to the "Rendiconti di Matematica e delle sue Applicazioni".[^o20] Entrusted with this double function the fifth series of the journal, from the first volume published in 1940[^o21] to the first [issue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issue_%28periodical%29) of the fourth volume published in 1943,[^o22] included a section listing the programs of the INdAM courses of the current [academic year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_year)[^o23] as well as section listing the research problems proposed by the lecturers currently working at the institute.[^o24]
