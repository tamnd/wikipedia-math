# Karl Stein (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Stein"}

**Karl Stein** (1 January 1913 in [Hamm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamm,_Westphalia), [Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westphalia) – 19 October 2000) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is well known for [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). [Stein manifolds](wikipedia://en/1053909) and [Stein factorization](wikipedia://en/1205940) are named after him.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Karl Stein received his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) with his dissertation on the topic *Zur Theorie der Funktionen mehrerer komplexer Veränderlichen; Die Regularitätshüllen niederdimensionaler Mannigfaltigkeiten* at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) under the supervision of [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678) in 1937. Karl Stein was conscripted into the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) sometime before 1942, and trained as a [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) to work at OKW/Chi, the [Cipher Department of the High Command of the Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cipher_Department_of_the_High_Command_of_the_Wehrmacht). He was assigned to manage the OKW/Chi IV, Subsection a, which was a unit responsible for security of own processes, cipher devices testing, and invention of new cipher devices. He managed a staff of 11[^o4] In 1955, he became professor at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) and [emeritated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in 1981. In 1990, he received the first [Cantor medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_medal).[^o5]

### Students {#Students}

Stein's doctoral students included Michael Schneider (mathematician), [Otto Forster](wikipedia://en/68051547), [Ivo Schneider](wikipedia://en/75882168), [Gunther Schmidt](wikipedia://en/44939247) and [Martin Schottenloher](wikipedia://en/52988610).
