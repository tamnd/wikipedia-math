# Semipermutable subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semipermutable subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), in the realm of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $H$ of a finite [group](wikipedia://en/19447) $G$ is said to be **semipermutable** if $H$ commutes with every subgroup $K$ whose order is relatively prime to that of $H$.

:::

Clearly, every [permutable subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutable_subgroup) of a finite group is semipermutable. The converse, however, is not necessarily true.
