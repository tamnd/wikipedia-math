# Alain A. Lewis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alain A. Lewis"}

**Alain A. Lewis** (born 1947) is an American mathematician. A student of the mathematical economist [Kenneth Arrow](wikipedia://en/49894), Lewis is credited by the historian of economics [Philip Mirowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Mirowski) with making Arrow aware of [computational limits](wikipedia://en/7543) to economic agency.[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Lewis gained his BA in philosophy, economics and statistics from [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) in 1969, and a PhD in applied mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1979. He was based at [Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Livermore_National_Laboratory) from 1978 to 1979, [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation) from 1979 to 1982, the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) from 1981 to 1983, [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) from 1983 to 1987 and [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) from 1987.[^o2]
