# International Society for Bayesian Analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Society for Bayesian Analysis"}

The **International Society for Bayesian Analysis** (**ISBA**) is a society with the goal of promoting [Bayesian analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_analysis) for solving problems in the sciences and government. It was formally incorporated as a not for profit corporation by economist [Arnold Zellner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Zellner) and statisticians [Gordon M. Kaufman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_M._Kaufman) and [Thomas H. Leonard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_H._Leonard) on 10 November 1992.[^o3] It publishes the electronic journal *[Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/39096238)* and organizes world meetings every other year.

:::

ISBA is an "official partner" of the [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662).[^o4]

## Fellows {#Fellows}

Every two years, ISBA selects Fellows, to recognize "outstanding contributions in some aspect of statistical work (publication, teaching, and service, including service to the society)".[^o7]
