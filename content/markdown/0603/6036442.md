# Theodorus Dekker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodorus Dekker"}

**Theodorus Jozef Dekker** (**Dirk Dekker**, 1 March 1927 - 25 November 2021)[^o3] was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Dekker completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 1958. His thesis was titled "Paradoxical Decompositions of Sets and Spaces".

Dekker invented an algorithm that allows two processes to share a single-use resource without conflict, using only [shared memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shared_memory_%28interprocess_communication%29) for communication, named [Dekker's algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dekker's_algorithm).
