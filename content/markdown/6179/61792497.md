# Wei Biao Wu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wei Biao Wu"}

**Wei Biao Wu** is a Chinese-born statistician. He is a professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the University of Chicago.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wu attended [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University), receiving his bachelor's degree in 1997. He went on to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) for graduate studies, receiving his PhD in 2001 under the supervision of [Michael Woodroofe](wikipedia://en/55829743) and [Sándor Csörgő](wikipedia://en/1988234). He was hired at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) shortly after completing his PhD, and has remained there since.

Wu is best-known for his work on [dependence](wikipedia://en/57691633), in which the main new idea is to interpret random processes as physical systems, and to examine coefficients that would have physical meaning.[^o5][^o6] According to [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar), this work has been cited over 350 times.[^o7] Wu has written over 100 papers.[^o8][^o9]

Wu has more than 20 students and their descendants working in academia.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Wu has practiced [Falun Gong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falun_Gong) since 1998. His religious practices and criticism of the [Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party) led to inaction on his 2005 request for renewal of his [Chinese passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_passport). Following the loss of his Chinese passport, Wu's [Chinese nationality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_nationality_law) was also revoked.[^o13]
