# Paul Epstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Epstein"}

*Image: Stolpersteine Paul Epstein mathematician.jpg.* *[Stolperstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stolperstein)* of Paul Epstein

**Paul Epstein** (July 24, 1871 – August 11, 1939) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was known for his contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), in particular the [Epstein zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epstein_zeta_function).

:::

Epstein was born and brought up in [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt), where his father was a professor. He received his PhD in 1895 from the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). From 1895 to 1918 he was a [Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent) at the University in Strasbourg, which at that time was part of the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire). At the end of [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I) the city of [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg) reverted to [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), and Epstein, being German, had to return to Frankfurt.

Epstein was appointed to a non-tenured post at the university and he lectured in Frankfurt from 1919. Later he was appointed professor at Frankfurt. However, after the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi) came to power in Germany he lost his university position. Because of his age (68) he was unable to find a new position abroad, and finally committed suicide by [barbital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbital) overdose at [Dornbusch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dornbusch_%28Frankfurt_am_Main%29), fearing [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo) torture because he was a Jew.
