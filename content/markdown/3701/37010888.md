# Charles C. Conley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles C. Conley"}

**Charles Cameron Conley** (26 September 1933 – 20 November 1984) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o2]

:::

The [Conley conjecture](wikipedia://en/74883647), [Conley index theory](wikipedia://en/22756507), [Conley's fundamental theorem of dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/74607085) and the [Conley–Zehnder theorem](wikipedia://en/34955091) are named after him.

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Conley was born in [Royal Oak, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Oak,_Michigan) and graduated from [Royal Oak High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Oak_High_School) in 1949. Starting in 1949, he attended [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University) in [Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit) for one year before he joined the [United States Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force). After four and a half years in the Air Force, mostly stationed in England, he returned to Wayne State. At Wayne State, he earned a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) degree in 1957, where he with the [Phi Beta Kappa Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa#Key), and an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.S.) degree in 1958.[^o2][^o4] He then moved to [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), where he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1962 under the supervision of [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316).

## Career {#Career}

After a postdoc at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)'s [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), he took up in 1963 an assistant professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), where he was promoted to full professor in 1968.[^o2]

## Family {#Family}

Charles Conley, who was also known by the nickname Charlie, met his wife, geneticist Catharine "Kit" Smith, in New York while he was a postdoc at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences). The couple married on December 28, 1963. Together, they had three children:[^o8]

1. Charles Henry Conley, born October 6, 1964
1. [Catharine Anastasia Conley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catharine_Anastasia_Conley), born October 9, 1966
1. John Alan Conley, born October 7, 1968
