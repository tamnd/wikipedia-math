# Koecher–Vinberg theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Koecher–Vinberg theorem"}

In [operator algebra](wikipedia://en/455987), the **Koecher–Vinberg theorem** is a reconstruction theorem for real [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371). It was proved independently by [Max Koecher](wikipedia://en/30232274) in 1957[^o2] and [Ernest Vinberg](wikipedia://en/19177826) in 1961.[^o3] It provides a [one-to-one correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-to-one_correspondence) between [formally real Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371#Formally_real_Jordan_algebras) and so-called domains of positivity. Thus it links [operator algebraic](wikipedia://en/455987) and [convex](wikipedia://en/245568) [order theoretic](wikipedia://en/361924) views on state spaces of physical systems.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

A [convex cone](wikipedia://en/3558266) $C$ is called *regular* if $a=0$ whenever both $a$ and $-a$ are in the closure $\overline{C}$.

A convex cone $C$ in a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) $A$ with an [inner product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_product) has a *dual cone* $C^* = \{ a \in A : \forall b \in C \langle a,b\rangle > 0 \}$. The cone is called *self-dual* when $C=C^*$. It is called *homogeneous* when to any two points $a,b \in C$ there is a real [linear transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_transformation) $T \colon A \to A$ that restricts to a bijection $C \to C$ and satisfies $T(a)=b$.

The Koecher–Vinberg theorem now states that these properties precisely characterize the positive cones of Jordan algebras.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem**: There is a one-to-one correspondence between [formally real Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371#Formally_real_Jordan_algebras) and convex cones that are:

- open;
- regular;
- homogeneous;
- self-dual.

Convex cones satisfying these four properties are called *domains of positivity* or *[symmetric cones](wikipedia://en/39156141)*. The domain of positivity associated with a real Jordan algebra $A$ is the interior of the 'positive' cone $A_+ = \{ a^2 \colon a \in A \}$.

:::
