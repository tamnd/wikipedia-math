# Nilcurve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nilcurve"}

In mathematics, a **nilcurve** is a pointed [stable curve](wikipedia://en/18948788) over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with an [indigenous bundle](wikipedia://en/36974140) whose [*p*-curvature](wikipedia://en/50961713) is square nilpotent. Nilcurves were introduced by (txt) as a central concept in his theory of [p-adic Teichmüller theory](wikipedia://en/36974047).

:::

The nilcurves form a stack over the [moduli stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_stack) of stable genus *g* curves with *r* marked points in characteristic *p*, of degree *p*<sup>3*g*–3+*r*</sup>.
