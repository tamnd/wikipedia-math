# Linear Algebra and Its Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linear Algebra and Its Applications"}

***Linear Algebra and Its Applications*** is a biweekly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) and covering [matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_theory) and finite-dimensional [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was established in January 1968 with [A.J. Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_J._Hoffman), [A.S. Householder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alston_Scott_Householder), [A.M. Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091), [H. Schneider](wikipedia://en/33844957), and [O. Taussky-Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Taussky-Todd)

as founding [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief).[^o4] The current editors-in-chief are [Richard A. Brualdi](wikipedia://en/22929637) ([University of Wisconsin at Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin_at_Madison)), [Volker Mehrmann](wikipedia://en/61754880) ([Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)), and Peter Semrl ([University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana)).
