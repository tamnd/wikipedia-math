# Fifth-order Korteweg–De Vries equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fifth-order Korteweg–De Vries equation"}

A **fifth-order Korteweg–De Vries (KdV) equation** is a [nonlinear partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/16678376) in 1+1 dimensions related to the [Korteweg–De Vries equation](wikipedia://en/344116).[^o2] Fifth order KdV equations may be used to model dispersive phenomena such as [plasma waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_waves) when the third-order contributions are small. The term may refer to equations of the form

:::

$$u_{t}+\alpha u_{xxx}+\beta u_{xxxxx} = \frac {\partial} {\partial x} f(u, u_{x}, u_{xx})$$

where $f$ is a [smooth function](wikipedia://en/1408000) and $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are real with $\beta \neq 0$. Unlike the KdV system, it is not integrable. It admits a great variety of [soliton](wikipedia://en/51654) solutions.[^o3][^o4]
