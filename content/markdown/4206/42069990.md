# Estevez–Mansfield–Clarkson equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Estevez–Mansfield–Clarkson equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Estevez–Mansfield–Clarkson equation** is the following [nonlinear partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/16678376):

:::

$$U_{tyyy}+\beta U_y U_{yt}+\beta U_{yy} U_t+U_{tt}=0,$$

where $U=U(x,y,t).$ It was introduced by Pilar Estevez, [Elizabeth Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Mansfield_%28mathematician%29), and Peter Clarkson.
