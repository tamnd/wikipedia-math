# Chafee–Infante equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chafee–Infante equation"}

The **Chafee–Infante equation** is a [nonlinear partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/16678376) introduced by [Nathaniel Chafee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathaniel_Chafee) and [Ettore Infante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ettore_Infante).[^o2]

:::

$$u_t-u_{xx}+\lambda(u^3-u)=0$$
