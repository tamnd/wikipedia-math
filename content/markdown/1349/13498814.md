# Chris Hall (cryptographer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chris Hall"}

**Christopher Hall** is an American [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He is one of the creators of the cryptosystem [Twofish](wikipedia://en/26508356).[^o2] He obtained a [BS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from the [University of Colorado-Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado-Boulder) Department of Computer Science and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2003, under [Nick Katz](wikipedia://en/1451427).[^o3]

:::

He is an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario).[^o4]
