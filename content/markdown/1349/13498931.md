# Laurent C. Siebenmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Laurent C. Siebenmann"}

**Laurent Carl Siebenmann** (the first name is sometimes spelled Laurence or Larry) (born 1939) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) based at the [Université de Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) at [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France).

:::

After working for several years as a Professor at Orsay he became a Directeur de Recherches at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) in 1976. He is a [topologist](wikipedia://en/29954) who works on manifolds and who co-discovered the [Kirby–Siebenmann class](wikipedia://en/2180593).

## Education {#Education}

Siebenmann's undergraduate studies were at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto). He received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [John Milnor](wikipedia://en/61632) in 1965 with the dissertation *The obstruction to finding a boundary for an open manifold of dimension greater than five*. His doctoral students at Orsay included [Francis Bonahon](wikipedia://en/56780028) and [Albert Fathi](wikipedia://en/50063959).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1985 he was awarded the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o5] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
