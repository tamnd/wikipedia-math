# Nachman Aronszajn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nachman Aronszajn"}

**Nachman Aronszajn** (26 July 1907 – 5 February 1980[^o3]) was a [Polish American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Aronszajn's main field of study was [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), where he systematically developed the concept of [reproducing kernel Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/651196). He also contributed to [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636).

:::

## Life {#Life}

An [Ashkenazi Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jew), Aronszajn received his Ph.D. from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), in 1930, in [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland). [Stefan Mazurkiewicz](wikipedia://en/629203) was his thesis advisor. He also received a Ph.D. from [Paris University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_University), in 1935; this time [Maurice Fréchet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Fréchet) was his thesis advisor. He joined the [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University–Stillwater) faculty, but moved to the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) in 1951 with his colleague [Ainsley Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ainsley_Diamond) after Diamond, a [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaker), was fired for refusing to sign a newly instituted loyalty oath.[^o5] Aronszajn retired in 1977. He was a Summerfield Distinguished Scholar from 1964 to his death.

## Work {#Work}

He introduced, together with [Prom Panitchpakdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prom_Panitchpakdi), [injective metric spaces](wikipedia://en/7840768) under the name of "hyperconvex metric spaces". Together with Kennan T. Smith, Aronszajn offered proof of the [Aronszajn–Smith theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aronszajn–Smith_theorem). Also, the existence of [Aronszajn trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aronszajn_trees) was proven by Aronszajn; [Aronszajn lines](wikipedia://en/9211715), also named after him, are the [lexicographic orderings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexicographic_ordering) of Aronszajn trees.

He also made a contribution to the theory of [reproducing kernel Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/651196). The [Moore–Aronszajn theorem](wikipedia://en/651196#Moore-Aronszajn_Theorem) is named after him.[^o7]
