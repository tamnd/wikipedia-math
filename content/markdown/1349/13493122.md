# Rifkat Bogdanov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rifkat Bogdanov"}

**Rifkat Ibragimovich Bogdanov** (Рифка́т Ибраги́мович Богда́нов; June 13, 1950 – November 3, 2013) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for contributions to [nonlinear dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamical_systems), [bifurcation theory](wikipedia://en/2326042), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He was an ethnic [Tatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars). In his work on bifurcations of [limit cycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_cycles) and [versal deformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versal_deformations) of [singular points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_singularity) of [vector fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_fields) (1975–76), he described a bifurcation of [co-dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-dimension) 2, which has become known in the literature as the [Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation](wikipedia://en/8564483).

:::

Born in the village of [Mamykovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamykovo) in [Tatarstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatarstan), Bogdanov had a doctorate under the supervision of [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490)[^o2] in physico-mathematical sciences and was a professor in two Russian research organizations: the [Skobeltsyn Institute of Nuclear Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skobeltsyn_Institute_of_Nuclear_Physics) at [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Moscow_State_University) and the Department of Higher Mathematics at MATI — the [Tsiolkovskii State Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsiolkovskii_State_Technological_University).

- R. I. Bogdanov (2003). *Nonlinear Dynamical Systems on a Plane and Their Applications*, Vuzovskaya Kniga Publishing House, Moscow \[in Russian\].
