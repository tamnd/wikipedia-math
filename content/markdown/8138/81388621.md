# Michael Cohen (statistician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Cohen"}

**Michael Paul Cohen** (born July 1947)[^o3][^o4] is an American mathematical statistician known for his contributions to survey methodology, education statistics, and the design of large-scale federal surveys. Cohen is a Principal Statistician at the [American Institutes for Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institutes_for_Research) (AIR). He has held research and leadership positions at the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](wikipedia://en/72528), the [National Center for Education Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Education_Statistics), the [Bureau of Transportation Statistics](wikipedia://en/1704636), Statistical Consulting LLC, and AIR.[^o5] His research has addressed topics including the [Consumer Price Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_Price_Index), multilevel survey design, Bayesian methods for unequal probability sampling, and applications of statistical methodology in education and transportation. Cohen is a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), the [American Educational Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Educational_Research_Association), the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), and the Washington Academy of Sciences, and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and [Sigma Xi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Xi).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Cohen earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in mathematics with honors from the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).[^o3][^o4] He went on to complete his Ph.D. in mathematics with specialty in mathematical statistics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), where his doctoral advisor was [Charles Joel Stone](wikipedia://en/68311766). His dissertation was titled *Fisher Information and Estimators of Location-Scale Parameters*.[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

After completing his doctorate, Cohen joined the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](wikipedia://en/72528) (1979–1987), where he contributed to survey methodology and research on the [Consumer Price Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_Price_Index) (CPI) and the [Consumer Expenditure Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_Expenditure_Survey). With John P. Sommers, he developed methods for estimating cost weights in the CPI that were implemented in the 1987 CPI Revision.[^o9]

At the [National Center for Education Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Education_Statistics), Cohen did foundational work on the newly established [Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_Postsecondary_Education_Data_System) (IPEDS) and later joined the Statistical Methodology Group that was responsible ensuring the soundness of NCES publications and data products.[^o10][^o11]

At the [Bureau of Transportation Statistics](wikipedia://en/1704636) (BTS), he was lead mathematical statistician for the U.S. Commodity Flow Survey (2000–2002) and later served as the BTS Assistant Director for Survey Programs (2002–2006) overseeing BTS statistical surveys.[^o12]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Cohen is a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science),[^o14] the [American Educational Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Educational_Research_Association),[^o15] the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA),[^o16] the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), and the Washington Academy of Sciences.[^o17] His ASA Fellow award was for "technical contributions to a wide range of topics in survey research; for technical leadership within government organizations; and for outstanding service to the profession."

In addition, Cohen was honored with the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) Outstanding Service Award in 2010[^o18] and the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607) (WSS) President's Award in 1999 for "many WSS contributions."[^o19]

## Editorial work {#Editorial_work}

Cohen served as an associate editor of the *Journal of the American Statistical Association* from 2004 to 2006.[^o21][^o22][^o23] He has been an associate editor of the *Journal of Official Statistics* since 2003,[^o24] and a consulting editor of the *Journal of Experimental Education* since 2010.[^o25]

## Professional activities {#Professional_activities}

Cohen was President of the Washington Academy of Sciences from 2003 to 2004.[^o17] He was President of the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607) from 2007 to 2008.[^o27] He served as a member of the Congress of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) from 2018 to 2021.[^o3][^o4] He also serves on the Board of Directors of the National Association of Academies of Science.[^o28]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- With [Lynn Kuo](wikipedia://en/55579792), Cohen investigated the decision‑theoretic properties of the empirical distribution function.[^o30][^o31]
- He studied how to design statistical surveys when the data are to be analyzed by multilevel models.[^o32][^o33]
- With coauthors, he researched the behavior of rural high school graduates after graduation, in one of the earliest applications of logistic multilevel models.[^o34]
- With Douglas Wright, he investigated possible bias in the [National Assessment of Educational Progress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assessment_of_Educational_Progress) (NAEP) caused by non‑participation of schools.[^o35]
- He developed the Bayesian bootstrap for unequal probability sampling.[^o36]
- He explored statistical hypothesis testing when the null hypothesis is an interval.[^o37][^o38]
- In philosophy of science, he conducted research in formal epistemology.[^o39][^o40]
