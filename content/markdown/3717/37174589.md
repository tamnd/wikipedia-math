# History of the Theory of Numbers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="History of the Theory of Numbers"}

*Image: History of the Theory of Numbers, 3 volumes.jpg.* By Chelsa publishing

***History of the Theory of Numbers*** is a three-volume work by [Leonard Eugene Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247) summarizing work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) up to about 1920. The style is unusual in that Dickson mostly just lists results by various authors, with little further discussion. The central topic of [quadratic reciprocity](wikipedia://en/25272) and higher [reciprocity laws](wikipedia://en/247622) is barely mentioned; this was apparently going to be the topic of a fourth volume that was never written (Fenster 1999).

:::

## Volumes {#Volumes}

- Volume 1 - [Divisibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisibility) and [Primality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primality) - 486 pages
- Volume 2 - [Diophantine Analysis](wikipedia://en/9109) - 803 pages
- Volume 3 - [Quadratic](wikipedia://en/251478) and Higher Forms - 313 pages
