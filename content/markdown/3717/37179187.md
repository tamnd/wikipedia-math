# Sue Whitesides

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sue Whitesides"}

**Sue Hays Whitesides** is a Canadian mathematician and computer scientist, a professor emeritus of computer science and the chair of the computer science department at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria) in [British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Columbia), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).[^o3][^o4] Her research specializations include [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) and [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Whitesides received her Ph.D. in mathematics in 1975 from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), under the supervision of [Richard Bruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Bruck). Before joining the University of Victoria faculty, she taught at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) and [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University);[^o6] at McGill, she was director of the School of Computer Science from 2005 to 2008.[^o7][^o8]

## Service {#Service}

Whitesides was the program chair for the 1998 [International Symposium on Graph Drawing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Symposium_on_Graph_Drawing)[^o10] and program co-chair for the 2012 [Symposium on Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Computational_Geometry).[^o11]
