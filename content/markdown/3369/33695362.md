# List of things named after Pythagoras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Pythagoras"}

This is a list of things named after [Pythagoras](wikipedia://en/23275), the ancient Greek philosopher, mystic, mathematician, and [music theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_theory).

:::

## Philosophy and mysticism {#Philosophy_and_mysticism}

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagoreanism"}

- [Pythagoreanism](wikipedia://en/291170) – the system of philosophy of Pythagoras and his followers

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Neopythagoreanism"}

- [Neopythagoreanism](wikipedia://en/1128528) – a later philosophical system

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean cup"}

- [Pythagorean cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagorean_cup) – a drinking cup that forces its user to imbibe only in moderation

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean letter"}

- [Pythagorean letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upsilon#Symbolism) – the Greek letter [upsilon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upsilon), used as a symbol by the Pythagoreans

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean diet"}

- [Pythagorean diet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagorean_diet) – vegetarianism

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean symbol"}

- [Pythagorean symbol](wikipedia://en/1570530#Pythagorean_symbol) – the [tetractys](wikipedia://en/1570530)

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean system"}

- [Pythagorean system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagorean_system) – the distinctive system of numerology used by the Pythagoreans

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean theorem"}

- [Pythagorean theorem](wikipedia://en/26513034) – the statement that the sum of the areas of the squares on the sides of a [right triangle](wikipedia://en/39420) is the area of the square on the hypotenuse

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean triple"}

- [Pythagorean triple](wikipedia://en/24172) – a set of three positive integers that can occur in the Pythagorean theorem

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean quadruple"}

- [Pythagorean quadruple](wikipedia://en/7672589) - a set of four positive integers that describes the space diagonal of a cuboid. Also known as a Pythagorean box.

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean expectation"}

- [Pythagorean expectation](wikipedia://en/315477) – a method of statistical analysis inspired by the Pythagorean theorem

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean field"}

- [Pythagorean field](wikipedia://en/31032774) – in algebra, a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) in which the sum of two squares is in every case itself a square

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean prime"}

- [Pythagorean prime](wikipedia://en/2669448) – a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) of the form 4*n* + 1

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean trigonometric identity"}

- [Pythagorean trigonometric identity](wikipedia://en/1050741) – any of several [trigonometric identities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometric_identity)

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean means"}

- [Pythagorean means](wikipedia://en/3314539) – the [arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/612), the [geometric mean](wikipedia://en/13046), and the [harmonic mean](wikipedia://en/14463)

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagorean addition"}

- [Pythagorean addition](wikipedia://en/14597289) – an arithmetic operation arising from the Pythagorean theorem

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagoras's constant"}

- [Pythagoras's constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoras's_constant) – the [square root of 2](wikipedia://en/341682)

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="Lute of Pythagoras"}

- [Lute of Pythagoras](wikipedia://en/5646030) – a geometric figure

:::

::: {#o22 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pythagoras tree"}

- [Pythagoras tree](wikipedia://en/2508505) – a [fractal](wikipedia://en/10913) geometric figure

:::

::: {#o23 .entry cue=list-entry name="Multiplication table"}

- Table of Pythagoras, another name for the [Multiplication table](wikipedia://en/57122)

:::

## Other {#Other}

- [Pythagoras' Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoras'_Cave), where Pythagoras is said to have hidden from the tyrant [Polycrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polycrates) on Mount [Kerkis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerkis) on [Samos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samos)
- [Pythagoreion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoreion), a town on [Samos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samos)
- [Pythagoras (crater)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoras_%28crater%29) – a lunar crater
- [PythagoraSwitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PythagoraSwitch) – a Japanese educational TV program, which also features sequences showing *Pythagorean Devices*. Pythagorean Devices are known in the US as "[Rube Goldberg machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rube_Goldberg_machine)", or in Great Britain as "[Heath Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Robinson)" contraptions.
- [Pythagoras Mechanical Workshop Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoras_Mechanical_Workshop_Museum) – a building—a former factory—in Sweden
- [School of Pythagoras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Pythagoras) – the oldest building in [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge)
- [Pythagoras ABM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagoras_ABM) – an agent-based software model
- [Knights of Pythagoras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_of_Pythagoras) – a Masonic body for boys
