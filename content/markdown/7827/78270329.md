# Bridget Tenner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bridget Tenner"}

**Bridget Eileen Tenner** is a professor of mathematics at [DePaul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DePaul_University) in Chicago. Her research focuses on [permutation patterns](wikipedia://en/24360911), and has also included work in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468), [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004), and [electoral geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_geography).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tenner majored in mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating [magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_cum_laude) in 2002. She completed a Ph.D. at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT) in 2006. Her doctoral dissertation, *The Combinatorics of Reduced Decompositions*, was supervised by [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397); as a doctoral student she also visited [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research), and the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) in Sweden.

She continued at MIT as a postdoctoral researcher until 2007, when she became an assistant professor at DePaul University. She was promoted to associate professor in 2011 and full professor in 2017.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Tenner was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.
