# Teena Gerhardt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Teena Gerhardt"}

**Teena Meredith Gerhardt** (born 1980) is a professor of mathematics at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University). Her research focuses on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), including [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) and [equivariant stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/42486328).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gerhardt studied mathematics as an undergraduate at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), graduating in 2002. She completed her Ph.D. in 2007 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Her dissertation, *The $R(S^1)$-graded equivariant homotopy of $\operatorname{THH}(\mathbb{F}_p)$*, was supervised by [Lars Hesselholt](wikipedia://en/45444071); it concerned the [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) of the [topological Hochschild homology ring](wikipedia://en/66192010) of a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615).

She was a Zorn Postdoctoral Fellow at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) from 2007 until 2010, when she joined Michigan State University as an assistant professor. She was promoted to associate professor in 2017 and full professor in 2023.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Gerhardt has won multiple teaching awards at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and Michigan State University. She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in the 2025 class of fellows. She was named as a Fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), in the 2027 class of fellows, "for her work ranging from local involvement with her AWM Student Chapter to her large-scale national service".
