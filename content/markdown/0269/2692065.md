# Antoine Deparcieux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antoine Deparcieux"}

*Image: Antoine Deparcieux (1703-1768).jpg.* Antoine Deparcieux

**Antoine Deparcieux** (28 October 1703 – 2 September 1768) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born at [Clessous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clessous) in the [Portes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portes,_Gard), province of [Languedoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languedoc). He attended the school of [Saint Florent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Florent-sur-Auzonnet) for 10 years while working on his family farm. In 1725, his desire for learning took him to [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon), where he studied at a [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus) school for five years. Then, in 1730, he went to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) to increase his knowledge of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). He made a living by manufacturing [sundials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial).

:::

In 1746, he became a member of the [Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences), and in about 1765 was named *Censeur Royal*. He was also librarian at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg), and member of the Academy of Sciences of Paris, Montpellier, Lyon, Amiens, Metz, Berlin, and Stockholm.

## Accomplishments {#Accomplishments}

*Image: Deparcieux - Essai sur les probabilités, 1746 - 145.tif.* *Essai sur les probabilités*, 1746.

Among his constructions were:

- A machine to raise water at [Crécy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crécy-en-Ponthieu) castle
- A pump for castle of [Arnouville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnouville)
- A press for the production of tobacco

He also published many works, including:

- *Traité de trigonométrie rectiligne et sphérique* (1738), approved by the Academy of Sciences
- *Nouveau traité de trigonométrie, (avec table des sinus et logarithmes)* (1740)
- *Traité complet de Gnomonique* (1741)
- *Essai sur les probabilités de la durée de la vie humaine* (1746) ("Essay on the probabilities of the human lifespan"), which is the work for which he is best known[^o3]
- *Mémoire sur la courbure des ondes* (1747)

In 1758, Deparcieux was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences).
