# Large gauge transformation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Large gauge transformation"}

Given a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *M*, a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) *G* and a [principal G-bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) over *M*, a [global section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_section) of that principal bundle is a [gauge fixing](wikipedia://en/2000736) and the process of replacing one section by another is a [gauge transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_transformation). If a gauge transformation isn't [homotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopic) to the identity, it is called a **large gauge transformation**. In [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028), *M* often is a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) and *G* is a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o2]

:::
