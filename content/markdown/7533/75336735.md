# Annihilating polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annihilating polynomial"}

A [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) *P* is *annihilating* or called an **annihilating polynomial** in [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) if the polynomial considered as a function of the [linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) or a matrix *A* evaluates to zero, i.e., is such that *P(A) = 0*.

:::

Note that all [characteristic polynomials](wikipedia://en/218268) and [minimal polynomials](wikipedia://en/9667107) of *A* are annihilating polynomials. In fact, every annihilating polynomial is the multiple of the minimal polynomial of an operator *A*.[^o2][^o3]
