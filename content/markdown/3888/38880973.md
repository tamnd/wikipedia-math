# Klein graphs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klein graphs"}

*Image: Triple torus illustration.png.* [Surface of genus 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_torus)

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Klein graphs** are two different but related [regular graphs](wikipedia://en/85821), each with 84 edges. Each can be embedded in the orientable [surface](wikipedia://en/27865) of [genus](wikipedia://en/27865#Classification_of_closed_surfaces) 3, in which they form [dual graphs](wikipedia://en/2536864).

:::

## The cubic Klein graph {#The_cubic_Klein_graph}

This is a 3-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) ([cubic](wikipedia://en/980508)) graph with 56 vertices and 84 edges, named after [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887).

It is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph), has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 6, diameter 6 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 7. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) graph. It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o4]

It can be embedded in the [genus](wikipedia://en/2073470#Genus_and_the_Euler_characteristic)-3 orientable [surface](wikipedia://en/2073470) (which can be represented as the [Klein quartic](wikipedia://en/390976)), where it forms the Klein map with 24 heptagonal faces, [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {7,3}<sub>8</sub>.

According to the *Foster census*, the Klein graph, referenced as F056B, is the only cubic symmetric graph on 56 vertices which is not [bipartite](wikipedia://en/244431).[^o5]

It can be derived from the 28-vertex [Coxeter graph](wikipedia://en/5358570).[^o6]

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

### Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Klein graph is the group PGL<sub>2</sub>(7) of order 336, which has [PSL<sub>2</sub>(7)](wikipedia://en/12659803) as a normal subgroup. This group acts transitively on its half-edges, so the Klein graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896).

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of this 56-vertex Klein graph is equal to $x^7 \,(x-3)\,(x+2)^6 \left(x^2-2\right)^6 \left(x^2+x-4\right)^7 \left(x^2-2 x-1\right)^8$

:::

## The 7-regular Klein graph {#The_7-regular_Klein_graph}

This is a 7-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) graph with 24 vertices and 84 edges, named after [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887).

It is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph), has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 4, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 7, radius 3, diameter 3 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3.

It can be embedded in the genus-3 orientable surface, where it forms the dual of the Klein map, with 56 triangular faces, [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,7}<sub>8</sub>.[^o11]

It is the unique [distance-regular graph](wikipedia://en/7768943) with intersection array $\{7,4,1; 1,2,7\}$; however, it is not a [distance-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/7768222).[^o12]

::: {#o14 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

### Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the 7-valent Klein graph is the same group of order 336 as for the cubic Klein map, likewise acting transitively on its half-edges.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of this 24-vertices Klein graph is equal to $(x-7) (x+1)^7 (x^2-7)^8$.[^o15]
