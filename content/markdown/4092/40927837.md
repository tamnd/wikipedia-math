# Gerrit Bol

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gerrit Bol"}

**Gerrit Bol** (May 29, 1906 – February 21, 1989) was a Dutch mathematician who specialized in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He is known for introducing [Bol loops](wikipedia://en/4741821) in 1937,[^o3] and [Bol's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bol's_conjecture) on [sextactic points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextactic_point).[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Bol earned his PhD in 1928 at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) under [Willem van der Woude](wikipedia://en/47912777). In the 1930s, he worked at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) on the geometry of [webs](wikipedia://en/34816085) under [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998) and later [projective differential geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_differential_geometry). In 1931 he earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation).

In 1933 Bol signed the *[Loyalty Oath of German Professors to Adolf Hitler and the National Socialist State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vow_of_allegiance_of_the_Professors_of_the_German_Universities_and_High-Schools_to_Adolf_Hitler_and_the_National_Socialistic_State)*.

In 1942–1945 during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Bol fought on the Dutch side, and was taken prisoner. On the authority of Blaschke, he was released. After the war, Bol became professor at the Albert-Ludwigs-[University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg), until retirement there in 1971.
