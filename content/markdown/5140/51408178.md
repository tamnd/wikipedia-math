# Tiberiu Popoviciu Institute of Numerical Analysis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tiberiu Popoviciu Institute of Numerical Analysis"}

The **Tiberiu Popoviciu Institute of Numerical Analysis** Institutul de Calcul "Tiberiu Popoviciu" (ICTP) is a mathematics research institute of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy), based in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania).

:::

ICTP is coordinated by the Mathematical Section and belongs to the Cluj-Napoca Branch of the Romanian Academy. The Institute performs fundamental research mainly in the field of [Numerical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Analysis).

ICTP was founded in 1951, as the Mathematical Section of the Cluj-Napoca Branch, with residence at 37 Republicii Street.[^o3]
