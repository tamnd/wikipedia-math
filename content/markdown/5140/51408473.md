# Laurent Clozel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Laurent Clozel"}

**Laurent Clozel** (born 23 October 1953 in [Gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gap,_Hautes-Alpes), France) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University). His mathematical work is in the area of [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), including the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Career and distinctions {#Career_and_distinctions}

Clozel was a student at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) and later obtained a Ph.D. under [Michel Duflo](wikipedia://en/36324838) and [Paul Gérardin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Gérardin).[^o4]

He received the [Prix Élie Cartan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elie_Cartan_Prize) of the French Academy for his work on [base change](wikipedia://en/32338538) for automorphic forms. He was an invited speaker at the 1986 [International congress of mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_congress_of_mathematicians) in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), talking about "Base change for GL(*n*)".

Together with [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931), [Nicholas Shepherd-Barron](wikipedia://en/7681535), and [Michael Harris](wikipedia://en/31954452) he proved the [Sato–Tate conjecture](wikipedia://en/597837).[^o5]
