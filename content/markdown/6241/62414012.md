# Sun Binyong

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sun Binyong"}

[Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_%28surname%29)

**Sun Binyong** (; born November 1976) is a Chinese mathematician. He is an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (CAS).[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sun was born in [Putuo District, Zhoushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putuo_District,_Zhoushan), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang) in November 1976, the second of three sons. His mother Liu Yadi () is a housewife. His father Sun Kaizhu () was a carpenter.[^o6] He attended Shuangtang Middle School, Putuo Middle School and the High School attached to [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University).[^o7] After high school, he entered [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University), where he graduated in 1999. In December 2004, he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology) under the supervision of [Li Jianshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Jianshu).

## Career {#Career}

From January to September 2005 he was a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in Switzerland. In September 2005 he became research associate at the Institute of Mathematics and Systems Sciences, [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (CAS), and was promoted to researcher in 2011.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Sun is married and has two daughters.[^o11]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 2014 Chen Jiageng Youth Science Award [^o13]
- June 2, 2016 Young Scientist Award of Chinese Academy of Sciences[^o14]
- August 14, 2018 [State Natural Science Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Natural_Science_Award) (Second Class)[^o15]
- November 22, 2019 Academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (CAS)
- 2025 [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100)
