# Vladas Sidoravicius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladas Sidoravicius"}

**Vladas Sidoravicius** (1963, [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius), [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) – 23 May 2019, [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai)) was a Lithuanian-Brazilian mathematician, specializing in probability theory.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

At [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University), Sidoravicius graduated in mathematics with [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in 1985 and [Magister degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magister_degree) in 1986.[^o3] At [Lomonosov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_State_University) he matriculated in 1986 and received his doctoral degree in 1990 with thesis advisor Vadim Aleksandrovich Malyshev. At [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) and at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University), Sidoravicius was a postdoc from 1991 to 1993.[^o3] In the early 1990s he gained an international reputation for his research in probability theory.[^o4] In 1993 he moved to Brazil.[^o2] He became a naturalized Brazilian citizen and was a full professor at the [Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708) ([IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708)) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) from 1999 to 2015, when he moved to China.[^o3] At [New York University Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Shanghai) (NYU Shanghai), he was a professor of mathematics and also served as the deputy director of NYU Shanghai's NYU-ECNU (East China Normal University) Institute of Mathematical Sciences from 2015 until his death in 2019 at age 55.[^o4]

Sidoravicius was the author or co-author of over 100 articles in refereed journals. He was a frequent collaborator of [Harry Kesten](wikipedia://en/19816546). Their 2008 article *A Shape Theory for the Spread of an Infection* is particularly noteworthy.[^o4][^o7]

In 2014 Sidoravicius was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.[^o8] In 2019 the XXIII *Escola Brasileira de Probabilidade* (XXIII Brazilian School of Probability) was dedicated to his memory.[^o9]
