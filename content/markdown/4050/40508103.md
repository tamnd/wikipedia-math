# Eric Zaslow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eric Zaslow"}

**Eric Zaslow** is an American [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Zaslow attended [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), earning his Ph.D. in physics in 1995,[^o4] with thesis "Kinks, twists, and folds : exploring the geometric musculature of quantum field theory" written under the direction of [Cumrun Vafa](wikipedia://en/644848). His research focuses on mathematical questions arising from [duality symmetries](wikipedia://en/644671) in [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) such as [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671). With [Andrew Strominger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Strominger) and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137), he formulated the [SYZ conjecture](wikipedia://en/17511256).[^o6]

He was named to the 2021 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to mathematical physics and mirror symmetry".[^o7]

Zaslow is also known for being internationally ranked in [ultimate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_%28sport%29), with seven world or national championships[^o4] and has written limericks about physics competitively.[^o8]
