# Jerome H. Friedman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Jerome H. Friedman"}

**Jerome Harold Friedman** (born December 29, 1939) is an American statistician, consultant and Professor of Statistics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), known for his contributions in the field of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Friedman studied at [Chico State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico_State_College) for two years before transferring to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1959, where he received his AB in Physics in 1962, and his PhD in High Energy Particle Physics in 1967.[^o7][^o8]

In 1968 he started his academic career as research physicist at the [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_National_Laboratory). In 1972 he started at Stanford University as leader of the Computation Research Group at the [Stanford Linear Accelerator Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Linear_Accelerator_Center), where he would participate until 2003. In the year 1976–77 he was a visiting scientist at [CERN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERN) in Geneva. From 1981 to 1984 he was visiting professor at the University of California, Berkeley. In 1982 he was appointed Professor of Statistics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o7]

In 1984 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o9] In 2002 he was awarded the SIGKDD Innovation Award by the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) (ACM).[^o10] In 2010 he was elected as a member of the [National Academy of Sciences (Applied mathematical sciences)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_National_Academy_of_Sciences_%28Applied_mathematical_sciences%29).

## Publications {#Publications}

Friedman has authored and co-authored many publications in the field of data-mining including "nearest neighbor classification, logistical regressions, and high dimensional data analysis. His primary research interest is in the area of machine learning."[^o4] A selection:

- Friedman, Jerome H.; Tukey, John W. (1974). "A projection pursuit algorithm for exploratory data analysis". *IEEE Transactions on Computers*. 23 (9). p. 881–890. doi:10.1109/T-C.1974.224051
- Friedman, Jerome H.; Stuetzle, Werner. (1981). "Projection pursuit regression". *Journal of the American Statistical Association*. 76 (376). p. 817–823. doi:10.1080/01621459.1981.10477729
- Friedman, Jerome H. (1991). "Multivariate adaptive regression splines". *Annals of Statistics*. 19 (1). p. 1–67. doi:10.1214/aos/1176347963. jstor:2241837
- Friedman, Jerome H. (2001). "Greedy function approximation: a gradient boosting machine". *Annals of Statistics*. 29 (5). p. 1189–1232. doi:10.1214/aos/1013203451. jstor:2699986
