# Henry Landau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Landau"}

**Henry Jacob Landau** was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773), including the theory of [bandlimited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandlimited) functions and on [moment problems](wikipedia://en/1244992).

:::

Landau was born to a Jewish family in the Soviet Union and immigrated to the United States in 1945.[^o2] He attended the [Bronx High School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx_High_School_of_Science) and later received an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) (1953), [A.M.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) (1955) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (1957) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). His thesis *On Canonical Conformal Maps of Multiply Connected Regions* was advised by [Lars Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636) and [Joseph Leonard Walsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Leonard_Walsh).

Landau later became a Distinguished Member of Technical Staff at [Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Laboratories) and was twice a visiting member at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He also served as an adjunct professor at [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong), and [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o4]

His son, Zeph Landau, became a research scientist at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley).[^o5]
