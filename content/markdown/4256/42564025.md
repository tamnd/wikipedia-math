# Chazy equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chazy equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Chazy equation** is the [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309)

:::

$$\frac{d^3y}{dx^3} = 2y\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} - 3 \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2.$$

It was introduced by (txt) as an example of a third-order differential equation with a movable singularity that is a natural boundary for its solutions.

One solution is given by the [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458)

$$E_2(\tau) =1-24\sum \sigma_1(n)q^n= 1-24q-72q^2-\cdots.$$

Acting on this solution by the group SL<sub>2</sub> gives a 3-parameter family of solutions.
