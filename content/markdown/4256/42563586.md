# Dioscorides (Stoic)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dioscorides"}

**Dioscorides** (Διοσκορίδης, fl. 225 BC), sometimes known as **Dioscurides**, was a [Stoic](wikipedia://en/19224834) philosopher, the father of [Zeno of Tarsus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeno_of_Tarsus) and a pupil of [Chrysippus](wikipedia://en/37143). All other information has been lost.[^o2]

:::

## Dedication {#Dedication}

Chrysippus dedicated the following works to Dioscorides:

- Four books on Probable Conjunctive Reasons
- Five books on the Art of Reasoning and of Modes
- A solution, according to the principles of the ancients, of the [law of non-contradiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_non-contradiction)
- Five volumes of Dialectic Arguments, with no solution
- Two books on Probable Arguments bearing on Definitions
- An essay on [Rhetoric](wikipedia://en/25447), spanning four books
