# Knot operation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Knot operation"}

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), a **knot move** or **operation** is a change or changes which preserve [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298598). Operations are used to investigate whether knots are equivalent, [prime](wikipedia://en/315030) or [reduced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nugatory_crossing).

:::

Knot moves or operations include the [flype](wikipedia://en/16891247), [Habiro move](wikipedia://en/23336882#Cn-equivalence), **Markov moves** (I. conjugation and II. stabilization), [pass move](wikipedia://en/6992479#Definition_by_pass_equivalence), [Perko move](wikipedia://en/16618607), and [Reidemeister moves](wikipedia://en/1152374) (I. twist move, II. poke move, and III. slide move).[^o2]
