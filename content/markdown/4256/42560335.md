# National Vital Statistics System

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Vital Statistics System"}

The **National Vital Statistics System** (**NVSS**) is an inter-governmental system of sharing data on the [vital statistics](wikipedia://en/999909) of the [population of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_of_the_United_States). It involves coordination between the different state health departments of the US states and the [National Center for Health Statistics](wikipedia://en/1042734), a division of the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention).[^o2]

:::

## Data and publications {#Data_and_publications}

The National Vital Statistics System includes the following data sets and publications:[^o2]

- Vital Statistics of the United States:[^o4] The data set goes back to 1890.
- National Vital Statistics Report:[^o5] This is a monthly report that goes back to January 1998. The earlier version of this report, called the Monthly Vital Statistics Report, goes back to July 1964.[^o6]
- Other reports[^o7]

All data is accessible online on the NVSS website.

## Related programs {#Related_programs}

The following programs are related to the National Vital Statistics System:[^o2]

- Linked Birth and Infant Death Data Set
- [National Survey of Family Growth](wikipedia://en/42558734)
- Matched Multiple Birth Data Set
- National Death Index
- National Maternal and Infant Health Survey
- National Mortality Followback Survey

## Reception and impact {#Reception_and_impact}

### Academic research {#Academic_research}

Data from the National Vital Statistics System has been cited in academic research on many topics, including births[^o11][^o12] and homicides.[^o13]

A 1995 paper by Weed for [Population Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_Index) described the history of NVSS and how to prepare it for the next century.[^o14]

### Use as a standard source {#Use_as_a_standard_source}

Libraries and other link collections have pointed to the NVSS as an [official data](wikipedia://en/18646178) source for the [vital statistics](wikipedia://en/999909) of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).[^o16]
