# Alexander Gammerman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Gammerman"}

**Alexander Gammerman** (born 2 November 1944) is a British computer scientist, and professor at [Royal Holloway University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway_University_of_London). He is the co-inventor of [conformal prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_prediction). He is the founding director of the Centre for Machine Learning at Royal Holloway, University of London, and a Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Gammerman's academic career has been pursued in the Soviet Union and the United Kingdom. He started working as a Research Fellow in the [Agrophysical Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrophysical_Research_Institute), St. Petersburg. In 1983, he emigrated to the United Kingdom and was appointed as a lecturer in the Computer Science Department at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University), [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh).[^o2] Together with [Roger Thatcher](wikipedia://en/48411421), Gammerman published several articles on [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571).[^o5] In 1993, he was appointed to the established chair in Computer Science at [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) tenable at [Royal Holloway and Bedford New College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway_and_Bedford_New_College), where he served as the Head of Computer Science department from 1995 to 2005.[^o6] In 1998, the Centre for Reliable Machine Learning was established, and Gammerman became the first director of the centre.

Gammerman has written 7 books.

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 1996, Gammerman received the P.W. Allen Award from the [Forensic Science Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_Science_Society).[^o8] In 2006, he became an Honorary Professor, at University College London. In 2009, he became a Distinguished Professor at [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid), Spain. In 2019, he received a research grant funded by the energy company Centrica about predicting the time to the next failure of equipment.[^o9] In 2020, he received the Amazon Research Award for the project titled Conformal Martingales for Change-Point Detection[^o10][^o11][^o12]
