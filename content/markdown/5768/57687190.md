# Nobushige Kurokawa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nobushige Kurokawa"}

**Nobushige Kurokawa** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), especially [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), multiple trigonometric function theory, [zeta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeta_functions) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms). He is currently a professor emeritus at [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Institute_of_Technology).[^o2]

:::
