# Sergey Solovyov (mathematician)

Yurievich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sergey Solovyov"}

**Sergey Yurievich Solovyov** (Серге́й Ю́рьевич Соловьёв; 3 February 1955 – 22 September 2023) was a Russian mathematician who was a professor at the [Faculty of Computer Science at the Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC).[^n1]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Solovyov graduated from the faculty [MSU CMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC) (1977).

Solovyov defended the thesis "Mathematical methods and principles of building automated knowledge engineering systems" for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1996).[^o5]

Solovyov was awarded the title of [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) (2003).

His area of scientific interests was information systems. He was a Project Manager for [Glossary](http://www.glossary.ru).[^n2] He wrote more than 70 scientific works on formal grammars, expert systems, experimental data processing systems and network technologies.[^o7][^o8]

Sergey Solovyov died on 22 September 2023, at the age of 68.[^o9]
