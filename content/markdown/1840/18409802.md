# Shayle R. Searle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shayle R. Searle"}

**Shayle Robert Searle** PhD (26 April 1928 – 18 February 2013) was a New Zealand mathematician who was [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of [biological statistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o2] He was a prominent figure in the fields of [linear](wikipedia://en/17904) and [mixed models](wikipedia://en/4521038) in statistics. He also published widely on the topics of linear models, mixed models, and [variance component estimation](wikipedia://en/6709643#Variance_components).[^o3]

:::

Searle was one of the first statisticians to use [matrix algebra](wikipedia://en/20556859) in statistical methodology, and was an early proponent of the use of applied statistical techniques in [animal breeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_breeding).

He died at his home in [Ithaca, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca,_New_York).

## Education {#Education}

- BA – [Victoria University of Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Wellington) – 1949
- MSc – Victoria University of Wellington – 1950
- PhD – [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) – 1958
- DSc ([h.c.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree)) – Victoria University of Wellington – 2005

## Employment {#Employment}

- Research statistician – [New Zealand Dairy Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Dairy_Board) – 1953 to 1955, 1959 to 1962
- Statistician – University Computing Center, Cornell University – 1962 to 1965
- Professor of biological statistics – Cornell University – 1965 to 1996

## Honours {#Honours}

- Winner, [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Humboldt_Prize) of the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation)
- Fellow, [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)
- Fellow, [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)
- Honorary Fellow, [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand)[^o7]
