# Friedrich Bachmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Bachmann"}

**Friedrich Bachmann** (11 February 1909 – 1 October 1982[^o3]) was a German mathematician who specialised in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Bachmann was the son of a [Lutheran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheranism) minister Hans Bachmann. Bachmann came from an intellectual family, his paternal grandfather was the [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) [Paul Gustav Heinrich Bachmann](wikipedia://en/641951).[^o6] Bachmann took his early education at the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster). After attending the Gymnasium, he attended the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) and the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) and graduated in 1927. While there he was a member of the [Münster Wingolfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wingolf).[^o3]

In 1933, Bachmann was promoted to [D.Phil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) with a thesis titled, *Studies on the foundation of arithmetic with special reference to [Dedekind](wikipedia://en/23475106), [Frege](wikipedia://en/48416), and [Russell](wikipedia://en/4163)* (Untersuchungen zur Grundlegung der Arithmetik mit besonderer Beziehung auf Dedekind, Frege, und Russell). His advisor was [Heinrich Scholz](wikipedia://en/41316662).[^o3]

His doctoral students include [Andreas Dress](wikipedia://en/57220260) and [Rolf Lingenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Lingenberg).[^o3]

Bachmann was married to a great-granddaughter of [Otto von Bismarck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_von_Bismarck), Alexandra von Bredow. They had a son together, Sebastian[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

In 1935, Bachmann moved to the [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg) where assisted [Kurt Reidemeister](wikipedia://en/520188). Bachman [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at Marburg before becoming a [Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent) in 1939.[^o3]

From 1941 Bachmann became a private lecturer at [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) and from 1943 at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin).

On 1 March 1949, he was promoted to full professor ([Ordentlicher Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordentlicher_Professor)) at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel). He became known for his work on geometry, especially for his axiomatic justification of elementary geometry with mirroring operations, previously begun by [Johannes Hjelmslev](wikipedia://en/30961758) and others.[^o3]

After the war, Bachmann worked with the mathematician [Karl-Heinrich Weise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Heinrich_Weise) to rebuild the mathematics department at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel). From 1960 Bachman was editor of the series titled, *Grundzüge der Mathematik für Lehrer an Gymnasien sowie für Mathematiker in Industrie und Wirtschaft* (Basic features of mathematics for teachers at grammar schools as well as for mathematicians in industry and business).[^o3]

In 1962–1963, Bachmann was elected [Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the mathematics department of the Faculty of Philosophy of Kiel University.[^o3]

Bachmann retired on 31 March 1977.
