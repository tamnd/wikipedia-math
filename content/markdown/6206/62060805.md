# Ascher Wagner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ascher Wagner"}

**Ascher Otto Wagner** (12 October 1930, in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) – 27 May 2000, in London) was an Austrian and British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in the theory of [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and [finite projective planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_projective_plane). He is known for the Dembowski–Wagner theorem.[^o2]

:::

Ascher Wagner received his Ph.D. in 1958 with the dissertation *Some Problems on Projective Planes and Related Topics in the Theory of Algebraic Operations* supervised by [Kurt Hirsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Hirsch). Wagner was a faculty member at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) and then at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham).

In 1958 he married Gillian Mary Jaidka (1929–1993) in [Hampstead, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead,_London).
