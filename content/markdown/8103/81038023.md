# René Carmona

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="René Carmona"}

**René Andre Carmona** (born August 6, 1947) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) who is known for developing a [probabilistic approach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_model) for [mean field games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_field_games). He is a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376), the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Carmona earned his undergraduate degree from the [Aix-Marseille University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University) in 1968 and would receive his Ph.D. from the same university in 1977. His thesis, *Contribution à l'étude des mesures gaussiennes dans les espaces de Banach*, was supervised by [Leonard Gross](wikipedia://en/64592988).

## Career {#Career}

Carmona's first academic job was at the [Aix-Marseille University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University) after he completed his Ph.D. He moved from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) before taking a job as an assistant professor at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) (UCI) in 1981. Carmona became a full professor by 1984 and was named a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) the following year.[^o6] He stayed at UCI until 1995 when he accepted a professorship at Princeton.[^o7]

In 2020, Carmona and François Delarue were awarded the [Joseph L. Doob Prize](wikipedia://en/70444084) by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for their two volume series *Probabilistic Theory of Mean Field Games with Applications*.[^o8] The books were published in 2018 by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) as part of the *Probability Theory and Stochastic Modeling* series.[^o9] The books contain the motivation and background for understanding the probabilistic model for [mean field games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_field_games) that Carmona and Delarue spent the last decade researching.[^o10]
