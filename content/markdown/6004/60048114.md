# Maria Heep-Altiner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Heep-Altiner"}

**Maria Heep-Altiner** (born 29 December 1959 in [Niederzeuzheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niederzeuzheim)) is a German mathematician, actuary and university lecturer.[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

After graduating from the Prince Johann Ludwig School in [Hadamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamar) in 1978, Heep-Altiner studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). In 1989 she earned her doctorate in mathematics on the [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) topic "Period relations for $GL_2(f)$" under [Günter Harder](wikipedia://en/50792386) and [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548).[^o5]

She then worked as an [actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) for [Gerling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atradius), before she moved companies in 1994 to Allgemeine Versicherungs-AG. There she became the actuarial manager for property insurance. In 2006, she moved to [Talanx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talanx), where she was responsible for setting up an internal holding model.[^o3]

In 2008, Heep-Altiner returned to academia as a professor at the Institute of Insurance at [Cologne university of applied sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_university_of_applied_sciences).[^o6] There she is responsible for the area of finance in the insurance company.

She is a member of the [German Actuarial Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Actuarial_Society) executive board.

In addition, she has co-published various publications on various actuarial topics, in particular on the [Solvency II Directive 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solvency_II_Directive_2009).

## Publications {#Publications}

- Altiner, Maria. (2001). "Versicherungsmathematische Anwendungen in der Praxis mit Schwerpunkt Kraftfahrt und Allgemeine Haftpflicht". VVW. isbn:978-3-88487-902-3
- Altiner, Maria. (2011). "Internes Holdingmodell nach Solvency II : - Schritt für Schritt zu einem internen Holdingmodell". Verlag Versicherungswirtschaft. isbn:978-3-89952-632-5
