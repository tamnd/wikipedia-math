# Divsha Amirà

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Divsha Amirà"}

**Divsha Amirà** (דיבשה אמירה; 1899 – 9 April 1966) was an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Amirà was born in [Brańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brańsk), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) to Rivka (Garbuz) and Aharon Itin. She [immigrated to Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) with her family in 1906. Her father was one of the founders of [Ahuzat Bayit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv) (today Tel Aviv), a founder of the Tel Aviv [Great Synagogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Synagogue_%28Tel_Aviv%29), and the owner of the first [publishing house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publishing_house) in [Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffa). She graduated in the second class of the [Herzliya Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzliya_Hebrew_Gymnasium) in 1914.[^o4]

Amirà studied at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and obtained her doctorate from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in 1924 under the guidance of [Herman Müntz](wikipedia://en/3204487).[^o5] Her [doctoral thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_thesis), published in 1925, provided a [projective](wikipedia://en/243849) synthesis of [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417).[^o6]

## Pedagogic career {#Pedagogic_career}

After leaving Geneva, Amirà worked at [Gymnasia Rehavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasia_Rehavia) in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem), and taught several courses on [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) at the [Einstein Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/59217494).[^o8] She later taught at the Levinsky College of Education and [Beit-Hakerem High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_Secondary_School), where her students included such future mathematicians as [Ernst G. Straus](wikipedia://en/6920898).

## Published works {#Published_works}

Amirà published an introductory [school textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on geometry in 1938, following the [axiomatic approach](wikipedia://en/838151) of Hilbert's *[Grundlagen der Geometrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grundlagen_der_Geometrie)*.[^o10] She published a more advanced textbook on the same topic in 1963.[^o11]
