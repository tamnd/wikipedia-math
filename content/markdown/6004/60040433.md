# Gideon Schechtman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gideon Schechtman"}

**Gideon Schechtman** (גדעון שכטמן; born 14 February 1947) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Schechtman received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1976 and was a [postdoctoral fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).

Since 1980 he has been affiliated with the [Weizmann Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), where he became [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in 2017.[^o5] His research focuses predominantly on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and the geometry of [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces).[^o6] Schechtman is an editor of the [Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics).[^o7]
