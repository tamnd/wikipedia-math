# Della Dumbaugh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Della Dumbaugh"}

*Image: DumbaughforJMM2020.jpg.* At the University of Richmond in 2020.

**Della Jeanne Dumbaugh** (also published as **Della Dumbaugh Fenster**) is an American mathematician and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), focusing on the history of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Richmond).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dumbaugh is originally from [Louisville, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky). She joined the University of Richmond faculty after completing her Ph.D. in 1994 at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia). Her dissertation, *Leonard Eugene Dickson and His Work in the Theory of Algebra*, concerned the work of [Leonard Eugene Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247) (1874–1954), one of the first American mathematicians to work in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). It was supervised by [Karen Parshall](wikipedia://en/36594635).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

With [Joachim Schwermer](wikipedia://en/56118470), Dumbaugh is a coauthor of the book *Emil Artin and Beyond – Class Field Theory and L-Functions* (Heritage of European Mathematics, European Mathematical Society, 2015), concerning the work of [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) and others on [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695) and [*L*-functions](wikipedia://en/245283). She is the co-editor of *A Century of Advancing Mathematics* celebrating the centennial of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (Mathematical Association of America, 2015) and, with [Deanna Haunsperger](wikipedia://en/53725340), of the book *Count Me In: Community and Belonging in Mathematics* on [diversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_%28politics%29) in mathematics (American Mathematical Society and Mathematical Association of America, 2022).

Dumbaugh was named editor-in-chief of *[The American Mathematical Monthly](wikipedia://en/1179483)*, beginning in January 2022. In 2025, Vadim Ponomarenko was announced as editor-elect, succeeding her.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dumbaugh was the 2019 winner of the John M. Smith Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).
