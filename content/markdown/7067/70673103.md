# Sven Leyffer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sven Leyffer"}

**Sven Leyffer** is an American computational mathematician specializing in [nonlinear optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_optimization). He is a Senior Computational Mathematician in the Laboratory for Applied Mathematics, Numerical Software, and Statistics at [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Leyffer received a Vordiplom in Pure and Applied Mathematics from the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) in 1989. Leyffer obtained his Ph.D. in 1994 from the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) under doctoral advisor [Roger Fletcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Fletcher_%28mathematician%29). His dissertation was *Deterministic Methods in Mixed Integer Nonlinear Programming*.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2006, Leyffer was awarded, alongside Roger Fletcher and Philippe L. Toint, the Lagrange Prize from the Mathematical Programming Society (MPS) and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).

In 2009, Leyffer was named a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) for *contributions to large-scale nonlinear optimization.*

## Service {#Service}

From 2017 to 2021, Leyffer was Editor-in-Chief of the journal [Mathematical Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Programming) B.

Leyffer is president (2023-2024) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).
