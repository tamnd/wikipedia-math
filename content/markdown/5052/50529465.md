# Behrend function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Behrend function"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Behrend function** of a scheme *X*, introduced by [Kai Behrend](wikipedia://en/31872403), is a [constructible function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructible_function_%28mathematics%29)

$$\nu_X: X \to \mathbb{Z}$$

such that if *X* is a quasi-projective proper moduli scheme carrying a [symmetric obstruction theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_obstruction_theory), then the **weighted Euler characteristic**

$$\chi(X, \nu_X) = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} n \, \chi(\{\nu_X = n\})$$

is the degree of the [virtual fundamental class](wikipedia://en/50529391)

$$[X]^{\text{vir}}$$

of *X*, which is an element of the zeroth [Chow group](wikipedia://en/4282051) of *X*. Modulo some solvable technical difficulties (e.g., what is the [Chow group of a stack](wikipedia://en/50534018)?), the definition extends to moduli stacks such as the moduli stack of stable sheaves (the [Donaldson–Thomas theory](wikipedia://en/32335603)) or that of [stable maps](wikipedia://en/2300947) (the [Gromov–Witten theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov–Witten_theory)).

:::
