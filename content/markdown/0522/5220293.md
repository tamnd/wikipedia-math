# Tristan Needham

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tristan Needham"}

**Tristan Needham** is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [University of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Francisco), best known to the public for his books *Visual Complex Analysis*, and *Visual Differential Geometry and Forms*.

:::

## Education, career and publications {#Education,_career_and_publications}

Tristan is the son of [social anthropologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_anthropologist) [Rodney Needham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodney_Needham) of [Oxford, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_England). He attended the [Dragon School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_School). Later Needham attended the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and studied [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at [Merton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College), and then transferred to the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850) where he studied under [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193). He obtained his D.Phil. in 1987 and in 1989 took up his post at University of San Francisco.[^o3][^o4]

In 1993 he published *A Visual Explanation of [Jensen's inequality](wikipedia://en/297811)*.[^o5] The following year he published *The Geometry of Harmonic Functions*, which won the [Carl B. Allendoerfer Award](wikipedia://en/40343255) for 1995.[^o6][^o7]

Needham wrote the book *Visual Complex Analysis*, which has received positive reviews.[^o8] Though it is described as a "radical first course in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) aimed at undergraduates", writing in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* D.H. Armitage said that "the book will be appreciated most by those who already know some complex analysis."[^o9] In fact [Douglas Hofstadter](wikipedia://en/8758) wrote "Needham's work of art with its hundreds and hundreds of beautiful figures á la Latta, brings complex analysis alive in an unprecedented manner".[^o10] Hofstadter had studied complex analysis at Stanford with [Gordon Latta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Latta), and he recalled "Latta's amazingly precise and elegant blackboard diagrams". In 2001 a German language version, translated by Norbert Herrmann and Ina Paschen, was published by R. Oldenbourg Verlag, Munich.

In 2021, Needham published *Visual Differential Geometry and Forms: A Mathematical Drama in Five Acts ([Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press))*[^o11]*.* (The original title was *Visual Differential Geometry*.) Much of this material was already developed in the writing of *Visual Complex Analysis*.
