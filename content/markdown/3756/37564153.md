# Ole Michael Ludvigsen Selberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ole Michael Ludvigsen Selberg"}

**Ole Michael Ludvigsen Selberg** (7 October 1877 – 11 December 1950) was a Norwegian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and educator. He was born in the [Eikefjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eikefjord_%28village%29) area of the old [Kinn Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinn_Municipality_%281838–1964%29).[^o3] He was married to Anna Kristina Brigtsdatter Skeie, and the father of [Sigmund](wikipedia://en/37555546), [Arne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_Selberg), [Henrik](wikipedia://en/37563752) and [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021). His thesis from 1925 treated the theory of [algebraic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_equations). Three of his sons became professors of mathematics, and one was professor of engineering. During the [occupation of Norway by Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Norway_by_Nazi_Germany) Selberg was a member of the Nazi party [Nasjonal Samling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasjonal_Samling). He is also known for his large collection of mathematics literature, which has later been donated to the [Norwegian University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_and_Technology).[^o3][^o4]
