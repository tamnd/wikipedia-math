# Henrik Selberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henrik Selberg"}

**Henrik Ludvig Selberg** (17 February 1906 – 3 September 1993) was a Norwegian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen) as the son of [Ole Michael Ludvigsen Selberg](wikipedia://en/37564153) and Anna Kristina Brigtsdatter Skeie. He was a brother of [Sigmund](wikipedia://en/37555546), [Arne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_Selberg) and [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021). He was appointed professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) from 1962 to 1973. He is best known for his works on [complex functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_functions) and [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342).[^o3][^o4]
