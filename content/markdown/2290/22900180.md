# Shri K. Singh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shri K. Singh"}

**S. K. Singh** was a professor of mathematics from [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) - [Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri). He received his Ph.D. on the Entire and [Meromorphic functions](wikipedia://en/80857) from [Aligarh Muslim University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aligarh_Muslim_University) in 1953. His advisor was [S. M. Shah](wikipedia://en/22900376). Singh was one of the founder fathers and Head of the Department of Mathematics, [Karnataka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka_University), [Dharwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharwar).[^o2]
