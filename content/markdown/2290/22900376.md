# S. M. Shah

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="S. M. Shah"}

*Image: S M Shah.png.* S. M. Shah at retirement, U of Ky, Lexington, KY, May, 1976

**Swarupchand Mohanlal Shah** (30 December 1905 – 21 April 1996)[^o2][^o3] was a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky).[^o3][^o4] He received his Ph.D. from [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) in 1942, advised by [Edward Titchmarsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Titchmarsh) who was a Ph.D. student of [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599). He was a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o2]

:::
