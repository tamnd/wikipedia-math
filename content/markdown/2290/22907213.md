# K. S. Chandrasekharan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="K. S. Chandrasekharan"}

**Komaravolu Chandrasekharan** (21 November 1920 – 13 April 2017)[^o3] was a professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich)[^o4] and a founding faculty member of School of Mathematics, [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR). He is known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [summability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summability). He received the [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri), the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Award), and the [Ramanujan Medal](wikipedia://en/32501943), and he was an honorary fellow of TIFR. He was president of [the International Mathematical Union (IMU)](wikipedia://en/14868) from 1971 to 1974.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chandrasekharan was born on 21 November 1920 in Machilipatnam, [Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh). Chandrasekharan completed his high school from Bapatla village in [Guntur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur) from [Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh). He completed M.A. in mathematics from the [Presidency College, Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Chennai) and a PhD from the Department of Mathematics, [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras) in 1942, under the supervision of [K. Ananda Rau](wikipedia://en/35226529).[^o3]

When Chandrasekharan was with the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Princeton, US, [Homi Bhabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homi_J._Bhabha) invited Chandrashekharan to join the School of Mathematics of the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR). Chandrashekharan persuaded mathematicians [L. Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._Schwarz), [C. L. Siegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._L._Siegel) and others from all over the world to visit TIFR and deliver lectures. In 1965, Chandrasekharan left the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research to join the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich),[^o3] where he retired in 1988.[^o6][^o7]

He was a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
