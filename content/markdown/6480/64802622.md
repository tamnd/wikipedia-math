# Sergei Ivanov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Ivanov"}

**Sergei Vladimirovich Ivanov** (Сергей Владимирович Иванов; born 31 May 1972) is a leading Russian mathematician working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and mathematical physics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

For each of the three years, 1987, 1988, and 1989, Ivanov won a gold medal in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o4] He studied at the [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University), where he received his Ph.D. ([Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)) with advisor [Yuri Burago](wikipedia://en/8688682).[^o5] Ivanov has worked for many years at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). There in 2009 he [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitated) ([Doktor nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_nauk)).

In 2014, he received, jointly with Yuri Burago and [Dmitri Burago](wikipedia://en/57995304), the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for their book *A course in metric geometry* published by the American Mathematical Society in 2001.

In addition to his research on differential geometry, Ivanov also works on [informatics](wikipedia://en/23997153).

In 2010, in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad) he was an invited speaker with talk *Volume comparison via boundary distances* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o6] In December 2011, he was elected a corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).
