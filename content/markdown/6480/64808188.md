# Japan Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Japan Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics"}

**Japan Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics** (JSIAM) is a Japanese [non-profit organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit_organization) for the field of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969). JSIAM is not a branch but a Japanese counterpart of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) based in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).

:::

## Activities {#Activities}

As same as SIAM, JSIAM publishes [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) in Japanese and English, hold academic conferences,[^o4] and give awards to applied mathematicians with JSIAM membership.

### English Journals and Publications from JSIAM {#English_Journals_and_Publications_from_JSIAM}

- SIAM Online Magazine
- JJIAM (Japan Journal of Industrial and Applied Mathematics)[^o6]
- JSIAM Letters

### Finance {#Finance}

The finance of JSIAM is based on membership fee and support from their corporate sponsors. Their sponsors include [Canon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_Inc.), [Nissan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nissan), [NEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEC), [NTT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Telegraph_and_Telephone), [Hitachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi), [Fujitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fujitsu) and [Ricoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricoh).[^o9]

### Contributions to EASIAM {#Contributions_to_EASIAM}

EASIAM (East Asia Section of SIAM) aims to advance the studies of applied mathematics in eastern Asia. As part of the Eastern Asian community, JSIAM is partially supporting EASIAM. Within their support, EASIAM is publishing the East Asian Journal of Applied Mathematics from the Global Science Press, and hold the EASIAM conference every year.
