# University of Chicago School Mathematics Project

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="University of Chicago School Mathematics Project"}

The **University of Chicago School Mathematics Project** (**UCSMP**) is a multi-faceted project of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), intended to improve competency in [mathematics in the United States](wikipedia://en/24836125) by elevating educational standards for children in [elementary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_school) and [secondary schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school).[^o3]

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

The UCSMP supports educators by supplying training materials to them and offering a comprehensive mathematics curriculum at all levels of [primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_education) and [secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education). It seeks to bring international strengths into the United States, translating non-English math textbooks for English students and sponsoring international conferences on the subject of math education. Launched in 1983 with the aid of a six-year grant from [Amoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amoco),[^o3] the UCSMP is used throughout the United States.

UCSMP developed *[Everyday Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyday_Mathematics)*, a [pre-K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-K) and [elementary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_school) [mathematics curriculum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_curriculum).
