# Polyhedral complex

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polyhedral complex"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **polyhedral complex** is a set of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470) in a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) that fit together in a specific way.[^o2] Polyhedral complexes generalize [simplicial complexes](wikipedia://en/252686) and arise in various areas of polyhedral geometry, such as [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041), [splines](wikipedia://en/457680) and [hyperplane arrangements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperplane_arrangements).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A **polyhedral complex** $\mathcal{K}$ is a set of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470) that satisfies the following conditions:

\1. Every [face](wikipedia://en/149926) of a polyhedron from $\mathcal{K}$ is also in $\mathcal{K}$.

\2. The [intersection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_intersection) of any two polyhedra $\sigma_1, \sigma_2 \in \mathcal{K}$ is a face of both $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$.

Note that the empty set is a face of every polyhedron, and so the intersection of two polyhedra in $\mathcal{K}$ may be empty.

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Tropical varieties](wikipedia://en/867041) are polyhedral complexes satisfying a certain *balancing condition*.[^o7]
- [Simplicial complexes](wikipedia://en/252686) are polyhedral complexes in which every polyhedron is a [simplex](wikipedia://en/39781).
- [Voronoi diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_diagrams).
- [Splines](wikipedia://en/457680).

:::

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Polyhedral fan"}

## Fans {#Fans}

A **(polyhedral) fan** is a polyhedral complex in which every polyhedron is a [cone](wikipedia://en/782427) from the origin. Examples of fans include:

- The [normal fan](wikipedia://en/60958781) of a [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471).
- The fan associated to a toric variety (see Toric variety#Fundamental theorem for toric geometry).
- The [Gröbner fan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_fan) of an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of a [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065).[^o10][^o11]
- A tropical variety obtained by tropicalizing an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) over a [valued field](wikipedia://en/347049) with trivial valuation.
- The *recession fan* of a tropical variety.
