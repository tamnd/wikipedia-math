# Sherman–Takeda theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sherman–Takeda theorem"}

In mathematics, specifically in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the **Sherman–Takeda theorem** states that if *A* is a [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) then its double dual is a [W\*-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W*-algebra), and is isomorphic to the [weak closure](wikipedia://en/458698) of *A* in the [universal representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_representation_%28C*-algebra%29) of *A*.

:::

The theorem was announced by (Sherman 1950) and proved by (Takeda 1954). The double dual of *A* is called the [universal enveloping W\*-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_enveloping_W*-algebra) of *A*.
