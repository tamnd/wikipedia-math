# Administrative error

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Administrative error"}

**Administrative error** is an error resulting from improper administration or execution of a survey. Such errors can be caused by carelessness, confusion, neglect, omission or another blunder.[^o2]

:::

## Types {#Types}

There are 4 types of administrative errors:

1. Data-processing error: A category of administrative error that occurs in [data processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_processing) because of incorrect data entry, incorrect [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming) or other error during data analysis.
1. Sample selection error: [Selection bias](wikipedia://en/394392): an administrative error caused by improper selection of a [sample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_%28statistics%29) during a [survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_survey), resulting in accidental [bias](wikipedia://en/40786) in the results.
1. Interviewer error: This type of administrative error is caused by failure of an [interviewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interview) to correctly pose questions or record responses. Interviewer error generally leads to biased results, and perhaps to an increase in variability.[^o4]
1. Interviewer cheating: The practice of filling in fake answers or falsifying [questionnaires](wikipedia://en/956483) while working as an interviewer.
