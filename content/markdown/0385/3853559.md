# Kit Fine

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Kit Fine"}

**Kit Fine** (born 26 March 1946) is a British philosopher who is the Silver Professor of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). Prior to joining the [philosophy department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Department_of_Philosophy) of NYU in 1997, he taught at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA). The author of multiple books and over 100 articles in international academic journals, he has made notable contributions to the fields of [philosophical logic](wikipedia://en/304939), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), and the [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language) and also has written on [ancient philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_philosophy), in particular on [Aristotle](wikipedia://en/308)'s account of logic and [modality](wikipedia://en/333365).

:::

He is also a distinguished research professor in the Department of Philosophy, [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham), England.[^o4] Since 2018, Fine is visiting professor at the [University of Italian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Italian_Switzerland).[^o5]

## Education, family and career {#Education,_family_and_career}

After graduating from [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford) (B.A. in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics, 1967), Fine received his Ph.D. from the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) in 1969, under the supervision of [A. N. Prior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._N._Prior). He then taught at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), and [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA), before moving to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) as the Silver Professor and University Professor of Philosophy and Mathematics.

He was elected a Corresponding Fellow of the [British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy) in 2005[^o7] and a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_&_Sciences) in 2006.[^o8] He has held fellowships from the [John Simon Guggenheim Memorial Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Simon_Guggenheim_Memorial_Foundation) and the [American Council of Learned Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Council_of_Learned_Societies) and is a former editor of the *[Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic)*.

Fine has two daughters from his former marriage to [Anne Fine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Fine). Anne Fine is an author of children's books; [Cordelia Fine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordelia_Fine) is a professor of philosophy at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne); [Ione Fine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ione_Fine) is a professor at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).[^o9][^o10]

## Philosophical work {#Philosophical_work}

In addition to his primary areas of research, he has written papers in [ancient philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_philosophy), [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and [economic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_theory).[^o12]

Fine has described his general approach to philosophy as follows: "I’m firmly of the opinion that real progress in philosophy can only come from taking common sense seriously. A departure from common sense is usually an indication that a mistake has been made."[^o13]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2013, Fine held the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431), titled *Truthmaker semantics.*[^o15]
