# Geir Ellingsrud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geir Ellingsrud"}

**Geir Ellingsrud** (born 29 November 1948) is a Norwegian professor [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), where he specialises in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

He took the [cand.real.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.real.) degree at the University of Oslo in 1973, and the doctorate at [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) in 1982. He was a lecturer at Stockholm University from 1982 to 1984, associate professor at the University of Oslo from 1984 to 1989, professor at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) from 1989 to 1993 and at the University of Oslo since 1993. He has been a visiting scholar in Nice, Paris, Bonn and Chicago. He has edited the journals *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)* and *Normat*.[^o3]

In 2005 Ellingsrud was elected to be [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_rectors_for_the_University_of_Oslo) of the University of Oslo for the period 2006-2009. His team also consisted of [Inga Bostad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inga_Bostad) and [Haakon Breien Benestad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haakon_Breien_Benestad). He did not seek reelection to a second term, and was succeeded by [Ole Petter Ottersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ole_Petter_Ottersen).
