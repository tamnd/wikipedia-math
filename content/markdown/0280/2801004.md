# Handle decompositions of 3-manifolds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Handle decompositions of 3-manifolds"}

In mathematics, a **handle decomposition of a 3-manifold** allows simplification of the original [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) into pieces which are easier to study.

:::

## Heegaard splittings {#Heegaard_splittings}

An important method used to decompose into [handlebodies](wikipedia://en/864472) is the [Heegaard splitting](wikipedia://en/864418), which gives a decomposition in two handlebodies of equal genus.[^o3]

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

As an example: [lens spaces](wikipedia://en/885725) are orientable 3-spaces and allow decomposition into two [solid tori](wikipedia://en/1251473), which are genus-one-handlebodies. The genus one non-orientable space is a space which is the union of two [solid Klein bottles](wikipedia://en/4721423) and corresponds to the twisted product of the 2-sphere and the 1-sphere: $\scriptstyle S^2\tilde{\times}S^1$.

:::

## Orientability {#Orientability}

Each orientable 3-manifold is the union of exactly two orientable handlebodies; meanwhile, each non-orientable one needs three orientable handlebodies.

## Heegaard genus {#Heegaard_genus}

The minimal genus of the glueing boundary determines what is known as the [Heegaard genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heegaard_genus). For non-orientable spaces an interesting invariant is the [tri-genus](wikipedia://en/3789546).
