# Yves Laszlo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yves Laszlo"}

**Yves Laszlo** ( born 4 April 1964) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud). He specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Laszlo obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1988 from the University of Paris-Sud under the supervision of [Arnaud Beauville](wikipedia://en/28767637). He started the Fondation Mathématique Jacques Hadamard in 2011, and directed it until 2012.[^o4]

The [Beauville–Laszlo theorem](wikipedia://en/16829511) on gluing [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) together is named after Laszlo and Beauville, who published it in 1995.[^o5]
