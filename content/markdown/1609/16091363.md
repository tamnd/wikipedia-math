# John Pagus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Pagus"}

**John Pagus**[^o2] (; fl. first half of the 13th century) was a [scholastic philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholastic_philosopher) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), generally considered the first [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) writing at the Arts faculty at Paris.[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He is thought to have been a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in the 1220s and to have taught [Peter of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_of_Spain_%28author%29).[^o5] At that time he was writing on [syncategorematic terms](wikipedia://en/20522132).[^o6][^o7]

## Works {#Works}

- *Appellationes*
- Commentary on the *[Sentences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentences)*
- *Rationes super Predicamenta Aristotelis*
- *Syncategoremata*
