# Stafford Tavares

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stafford Tavares"}

**Stafford Emanuel Tavares** is a Canadian [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer), professor [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston). His notable work includes the design (with [Carlisle Adams](wikipedia://en/9212118)) of the [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594) [CAST-128](wikipedia://en/240998) and [CAST-256](wikipedia://en/612143). He also helped organize the first [Selected Areas in Cryptography](wikipedia://en/9545951) (SAC) workshop in 1994. Since 2003, SAC has included an invited lecture in his honor, the Stafford Tavares Lecture.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Tavares received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1968 from [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).

In 2018 he was elected as a fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043), "for significant contributions to the design and analysis of block ciphers, for founding the SAC conference, and for service to the IACR".[^o6]
