# Guillaume Le Blond

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guillaume Le Blond"}

*Image: Guillaume le Blond, PA07732.jpg.* Guillaume Le Blond

**Guillaume Le Blond** (1705 – May 24, 1781) was a French mathematician. He was born in Paris.

:::

*Image: Le Blond, Guillaume – Eléments de fortification, 1752 – BEIC 1446424.jpg.* *Eléments de fortification*, 1752

He was a professor of mathematics at the grand stable of the King (1736) and then the [Enfants de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fils_de_France) (1756). Leblond kept this job until 1778, when he became secretary of the cabinet of [Madame Victoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Victoire).

Le Blond wrote the following works, some of which have been translated into [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language). His work "*Éléments de fortification"* was translated to *[Ottoman Turkish language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Turkish_language)* in 1833 as "*Usûl-i İstihkâmât*"(Method of Fortifications) by [Ishak Efendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishak_Efendi).[^o2]

- *Essai sur la castramétation*, 1748, in-8°);
- *Éléments de tactique*, 1758, in-4°;
- *Artillerie raisonnée contenant l’usage des différentes bouches à feu*, 1761, in-8°;
- *l’Arithmétique et la géométrie de l’officier*, 1768, 2 vol. in-8°;
- *Traité de l’attaque des places*, 1780, in-8°;
- *Éléments de fortification*, 1739, in-8°.

He also produced editions of *Mémoires d’artillerie* by [Pierre Surirey de Saint-Remy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Surirey_de_Saint-Remy) and the *Géométrie* by [Joseph Sauveur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Sauveur) and collaborated on the *[Encyclopédie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopédie)*.
