# Walter Neumann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Neumann"}

**Walter David Neumann** (1 January 1946 – 24 September 2024) was a British-American mathematician who worked in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), and [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). He was a professor at [Barnard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnard_College), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). Neumann obtained his Ph.D. under the joint supervision of [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495) and Klaus Jänich at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1969.[^o4]

:::

He was a son of the mathematicians [Bernhard Neumann](wikipedia://en/5032588) and [Hanna Neumann](wikipedia://en/7043308).[^o4] His brother [Peter M. Neumann](wikipedia://en/7151472) was also a mathematician.

Walter Neumann was elected a member of the [European Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2002.[^o5] He was in the Inaugural Class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), starting from 2013.[^o6]

Neumann died on 24 September 2024, at the age of 78.[^o7][^o8]
