# Irene Mulvey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Irene Mulvey"}

**Irene Mulvey** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and progressive activist.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Mulvey completed her doctoral dissertation, titled *Periodic, Recurrent and Non-Wandering Points for Continuous Maps of the Circle* at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University) in 1982, where she was advised by Ethan Coven. She was appointed to a professorship at [Fairfield University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfield_University) in 1985.[^o5]

### President of the American Association of University Professors {#President_of_the_American_Association_of_University_Professors}

In July 2020, Mulvey was elected president of the [American Association of University Professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_of_University_Professors), succeeding [Rudy Fichtenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudy_Fichtenbaum), serving in that role until June 2024.[^o7] During her tenure as AAUP President, she claimed that state bills targeted at [diversity, equity and inclusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity,_equity_and_inclusion) programs damage not only higher education but also democracy.[^o8]
