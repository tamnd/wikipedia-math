# Frankel conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frankel conjecture"}

In the mathematical fields of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Frankel conjecture** was a problem posed by [Theodore Frankel](wikipedia://en/34938025) in 1961. It was resolved in 1979 by [Shigefumi Mori](wikipedia://en/794811), and by [Yum-Tong Siu](wikipedia://en/6085925) and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137).

:::

In its differential-geometric formulation, as proved by both Mori and by Siu and Yau, the result states that if a [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538) has positive bisectional curvature, then it must be [biholomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biholomorphic) to [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977). In this way, it can be viewed as an analogue of the [sphere theorem](wikipedia://en/4685719) in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), which (in a weak form) states that if a closed and [simply-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply-connected) [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) has positive curvature operator, then it must be diffeomorphic to a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859). This formulation was extended by [Ngaiming Mok](wikipedia://en/52300670) to the following statement: Let $(M, g)$ be a closed Kähler manifold of nonnegative holomorphic bisectional curvature. Then the [universal cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_cover) of $M$, with its natural metric, is biholomorphically isometric to the metric product of complex Euclidean space, with some number of irreducible closed [hermitian symmetric spaces](wikipedia://en/4127357) with rank larger than one, with the product of some number of complex projective spaces, each of which has a Kähler metric of nonnegative holomorphic bisectional curvature.

In its algebro-geometric formulation, as proved by Mori but not by Siu and Yau, the result states that if $M$ is an irreducible and nonsingular [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), defined over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) $k$, which has [ample](wikipedia://en/1186896) tangent bundle, then $M$ must be isomorphic to the [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) defined over $k$. This version is known as the **Hartshorne conjecture**, after [Robin Hartshorne](wikipedia://en/5693379).
