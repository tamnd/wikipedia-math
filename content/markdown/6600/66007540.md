# Eva Vedel Jensen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eva Vedel Jensen"}

**Eva Bjørn Vedel Jensen** (born 14 June 1951) is a Danish mathematician and statistician known for her work in [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349), [stereology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereology), [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813), and [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging). She is a professor emeritus in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After earning a master's degree at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) in 1976, she became a faculty member at the university in 1979. She completed a doctorate at Aarhus in 1987, and became full professor there in 2003.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Vedel Jensen has been an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) since 1992, and is also a member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).

She won the Villum Kann Rasmussen Annual Award for Technical and Scientific Research of the [Villum Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villum_Foundation) in 2009. She was named a knight of the [Order of the Dannebrog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Dannebrog) in 2010. The [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) gave her an honorary doctorate in 2013.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Vedel Jensen is the author of books including:

- *Local Stereology* (World Scientific, 1998)
- *Stereology for Statisticians* (with [Adrian Baddeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Baddeley), Chapman & Hall/CRC, 2005)

She has also written several highly cited papers with [Hans Jørgen G. Gundersen](wikipedia://en/16840182) including:

- Jensen, E. B.; Gundersen, H. J. G.; Østerby, R. (January 1979). "Determination of membrane thickness distribution from orthogonal intercepts". *Journal of Microscopy*. 115 (1). p. 19–33. doi:10.1111/j.1365-2818.1979.tb00149.x
- Gundersen, H. J. G.; Jensen, E. B. (May 1985). "Stereological estimation of the volume-weighted mean volume of arbitrary particles observed on random sections". *Journal of Microscopy*. 138 (2). p. 127–142. doi:10.1111/j.1365-2818.1985.tb02607.x
- Gundersen, H. J. G.; Jensen, E. B. (September 1987). "The efficiency of systematic sampling in stereology and its prediction". *Journal of Microscopy*. 147 (3). p. 229–263. doi:10.1111/j.1365-2818.1987.tb02837.x
- Vedel Jensen, E. B.; Gundersen, H. J. G. (April 1993). "The rotator". *Journal of Microscopy*. 170 (1). p. 35–44. doi:10.1111/j.1365-2818.1993.tb03321.x
- Gundersen, H. J. G.; Jensen, E. B. V.; Kieu, K.; Nielsen, J. (March 1999). "The efficiency of systematic sampling in stereology – reconsidered". *Journal of Microscopy*. 193 (3). p. 199–211. doi:10.1046/j.1365-2818.1999.00457.x
