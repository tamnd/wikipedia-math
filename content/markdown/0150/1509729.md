# Order of a kernel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order of a kernel"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **order of a kernel** is the degree of the first non-zero [moment](wikipedia://en/368684) of a [kernel](wikipedia://en/8771473).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $\ell \geq 1$ be an integer. Then, $K: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is a *kernel of order $\ell$* if the functions $u\mapsto u^{j}K(u), ~ j=0,1,...,\ell$ are *integrable* and satisfy $\int K(u)du=1, ~ \int u^{j}K(u)du=0,~ ~j=1,...,\ell.$[^o5]

:::
