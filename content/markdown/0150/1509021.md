# Georg Hamel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georg Hamel"}

**Georg Karl Wilhelm Hamel** (12 September 1877 – 4 October 1954) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) with interests in [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) and [function theory](wikipedia://en/26478).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hamel was born in [Düren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Düren), [Rhenish Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhenish_Prussia). He studied at [Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen), [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and [Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe). His doctoral adviser was [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302). He taught at [Brünn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Technische_Hochschule_Brünn) in 1905, Aachen in 1912, and at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) in 1919. In 1927, Hamel studied the size of the [key space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_space_%28cryptography%29) for the [Kryha](wikipedia://en/1281315) [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) device. He was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1932 at Zurich and in 1936 at Oslo.[^o6] He was the author of several important treatises on mechanics. He became a member of the [Prussian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1938[^o7] and the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1953.[^o8] He died in [Landshut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landshut), [Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria).[^o3]
