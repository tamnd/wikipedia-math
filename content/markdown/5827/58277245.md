# Peter Chadwick (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Chadwick"}

**Peter Chadwick** (23 March 1931 – 12 August 2018) was a British applied mathematician and physicist.

:::

A [Huddersfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield) native born on 23 March 1931, Chadwick attended the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) (BSc, 1952) and completed his PhD at [Pembroke College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembroke_College,_Cambridge) in 1957. He was Professor of Mathematics at the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia) from 1965 to 1991, and was made a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] in 1977. He died on 12 August 2018, aged 87.[^o4]
