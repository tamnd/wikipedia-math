# G. Peter Scott

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="G. Peter Scott"}

**Godfrey Peter Scott**, known as **Peter Scott**, (1944 – 19 September 2023) was a British-American mathematician, known for the [Scott core theorem](wikipedia://en/1258371).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He was born in England to Bernard Scott (a mathematician) and Barbara Scott (a poet and sculptor). After attending [St Paul's School, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Paul's_School,_London)[^o4] and completing his BA at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford),[^o10] Peter Scott received his PhD in 1969 from the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) under Brian Joseph Sanderson, with thesis *Some Problems in Topology*. Scott held appointments at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) from 1968 to 1987, at which time he moved to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), where he was a professor until his retirement in 2018.[^o10]

His research dealt with low-dimensional geometric topology, differential geometry, and geometric group theory. He has done research on the geometric topology of 3-dimensional manifolds, 3-dimensional hyperbolic geometry, [minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734) theory, [hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/3172817), and [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) with their associated geometry, topology, and group theory.

In 1973, he proved what is now known as the *Scott core theorem* or the *Scott compact core theorem*. This states that every [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) $M$ with [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) fundamental group has a compact core $N$, *i.e.*, $N$ is a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) such that inclusion induces a [homotopy equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_equivalence) between $N$ and $M$; the submanifold $N$ is called a *Scott compact core* of the manifold $M$.[^o6] He had previously proved that, given a fundamental group $G$ of a 3-manifold, if $G$ is [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) then $G$ must be [finitely presented](wikipedia://en/99494).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 1986, he was awarded the [Senior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Berwick_Prize) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o10] In 2013, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

## Death {#Death}

Scott died of cancer on 19 September 2023.[^o10]
