# David Clark Dobson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Clark Dobson"}

**David Clark Dobson** (born 17 November 1962 in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City)) is an American mathematician.

:::

Dobson received in 1986 his bachelor's degree from [Utah State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_University) and his PhD in 1990 from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University). He was at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) from 1990 to 1993. He was from 1993 to 1996 an assistant professor, from 1996 to 2000 an assistant Professor and from 1996 to 2000 an associate professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). Since 2002 he is a full professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah).[^o3]

Dobson does research on [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), specifically, "computational methods and optimal design for [plasmonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmonic) structures and [metamaterials](wikipedia://en/906878), optimal design of photonic structures and devices, data analysis with physical constraints, and [inverse problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problems) in acoustics."[^o4]

In 2000 he received the first [Felix Klein Prize](wikipedia://en/969470). From 1997 to 1999 he was a Sloan Fellow.[^o3]
