# Baliński

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baliński"}

Baliński **Baliński** (feminine: **Balińska**) or **Balinski** is a Polish-language surname. It is a [toponymic surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toponymic_surname) for someone associated with any of various places named [Balin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balin_%28disambiguation%29).[^o2] Russian form: [Balinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinsky), Lithuanian: [Balinskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinskis).

:::

Notable people with the surname include:

- [Damian Baliński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damian_Baliński) (born 1977), Polish motorcycle speedway rider
- [Damian Baliński Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damian_Baliński_Jr.) (born 1989), Polish speedway rider
- [Ella Balinska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Balinska) (born 1996), British actress
- Hanna Balińska (born 1943), Polish actress
- [Karol Baliński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_Baliński) (1817–1864), Polish poet
- Jan Baliński (1827–1902), Polish/Russian psychiatrist
- [Maria Balinska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Balinska) (born 1965), American journalist
- Michał Baliński (1794–1864), Polish-Lithuanian historian and publicist
- [Michel Balinski](wikipedia://en/41213698) (1933–2019), Swiss-born Polish American and French mathematician
- [Stanisław Baliński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanisław_Baliński) (1889–1984), Polish poet, writer and diplomat
