# Sophie Germain Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sophie Germain Prize"}

The **Sophie Germain Prize** (in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Prix Sophie Germain*) is an annual mathematics prize awarded by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) to researchers who have carried out fundamental research in mathematics. The award has been conferred every year since 2003 and comes with a €8000 cash prize. It is named after the French mathematician [Sophie Germain](wikipedia://en/27791).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
