# Optimal discriminant analysis and classification tree analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Optimal discriminant analysis and classification tree analysis"}

**Optimal Discriminant Analysis** (**ODA**)[^o2] and the related **classification tree analysis** (**CTA**) are exact statistical methods that maximize predictive accuracy. For any specific sample and exploratory or confirmatory hypothesis, optimal discriminant analysis (ODA) identifies the statistical model that yields maximum predictive accuracy, assesses the exact [Type I error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_error) rate, and evaluates potential cross-generalizability. Optimal discriminant analysis may be applied to > 0 dimensions, with the one-dimensional case being referred to as UniODA and the multidimensional case being referred to as MultiODA. Optimal discriminant analysis is an alternative to [ANOVA](wikipedia://en/634) (analysis of variance) and [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997).

:::
