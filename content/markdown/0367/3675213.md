# Chua

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chua"}

**Chua** is a [Hokkien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Min) or [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_dialect) romanisation of the surname [Cai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cai_%28surname%29). People so named include:

:::

- [Alfrancis Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfrancis_Chua) (born 1966), Filipino sports executive
- [Amy Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Chua) (born 1962), American corporate lawyer, legal academic and author
- [Chua Beng Huat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Beng_Huat), Singaporean sociologist
- [Dino Carlo Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dino_Carlo_Chua) (born 1980), Filipino businessman and politician
- [Brent Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brent_Chua) (born 1985), Chinese-Filipino model and fashion photographer
- [Chua Ek Kay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Ek_Kay) (1947–2008), Singaporean artist
- [Chua Enlai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Enlai) (born 1979), Singaporean comedian and actor
- [Jean Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Chua) (born 1987), Malaysian professional golfer
- [Chua Jim Neo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Jim_Neo) (1905–1980), Singaporean chef and cookbook writer
- [Joel Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Chua) (born 1972), Filipino lawyer and politician
- [Joi Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joi_Chua) (born 1978), Singaporean singer
- [Jonathan Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Chua) (born 1990), Singaporean actor and singer
- [Chua Jui Meng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Jui_Meng) (1943–2023), Malaysian politician
- [Kat Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Chua) (born 1992), Filipino politician
- [Chua Lam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Lam) (born 1941), Singaporean columnist and food critic
- [Leon O. Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_O._Chua) (born 1936), American electrical engineer and computer scientist
- [Manuel Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Chua) (c. 1986), Filipino model and actor
- [Michael Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chua), Singaporean film producer, director and poet
- [Chua Phung Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Phung_Kim) (1939–1990), Singaporean weightlifter
- [Robert Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Chua) (born 1946), Singaporean broadcaster and businessman
- [Chua Ser Lien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Ser_Lien) (1961–2020), Singaporean kidnapper
- [Simon Chua Ling Fung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Chua_Ling_Fung), Singaporean bodybuilder
- [Chua Sock Koong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Sock_Koong) (born 1957), Singaporean businesswoman
- [Chua Soi Lek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Soi_Lek) (born 1947), Chinese-Malaysian politician
- [Chua Soon Bui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Soon_Bui) (born 1955), Malaysian politician
- [Tanya Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Chua) (born 1975), Singaporean singer-songwriter
- [Chua Tee Yong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Tee_Yong) (born 1977), Malaysian politician
- [Chua Tian Chang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Tian_Chang) (born 1963), Malaysian politician
- [Tony Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Chua) (1965–2009), Filipino Chinese businessman
