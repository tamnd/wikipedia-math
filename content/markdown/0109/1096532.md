# De Morgan Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Morgan Medal"}

*Image: De\_Morgan\_Augustus.jpg.* Augustus De Morgan

The **De Morgan Medal** is a prize for outstanding contribution to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), awarded by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). The Society's most prestigious award, it is given in memory of [Augustus De Morgan](wikipedia://en/18949289), who was the first President of the society. It is awarded every three years, usually to a mathematician living and working in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).[^o4]

:::

In 1968, [Mary Cartwright](wikipedia://en/1529094) became the first woman to receive the award.[^o2]
