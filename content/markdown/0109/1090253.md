# Biorthogonal system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Biorthogonal system"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **biorthogonal system** is a pair of [indexed families](wikipedia://en/23624339) of vectors

$$\tilde v_i \text{ in } E \text{ and } \tilde u_i \text{ in } F$$

such that

$$\left\langle\tilde v_i , \tilde u_j\right\rangle = \delta_{i,j},$$

where $E$ and $F$ form a pair of [topological vector spaces](wikipedia://en/45752) that are in [duality](wikipedia://en/63735167), $\langle \,\cdot, \cdot\, \rangle$ is a [bilinear mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilinear_mapping) and $\delta_{i,j}$ is the [Kronecker delta](wikipedia://en/182890).

:::

An example is the pair of sets of respectively left and right [eigenvectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvector) of a matrix, indexed by [eigenvalue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue), if the eigenvalues are distinct.[^o2]

A biorthogonal system in which $E = F$ and $\tilde v_i = \tilde u_i$ is an [orthonormal system](wikipedia://en/755400).

## Projection {#Projection}

Related to a biorthogonal system is the projection

$$P := \sum_{i \in I} \tilde u_i \otimes \tilde v_i,$$

where $(u \otimes v) (x) := u \langle v, x \rangle;$ its image is the [linear span](wikipedia://en/56353) of $\left\{\tilde u_i: i \in I\right\},$ and the [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) is $\left\{\left\langle \tilde v_i, \cdot \right\rangle = 0 : i \in I\right\}.$

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

Given a possibly non-orthogonal set of vectors $\mathbf{u} = \left(u_i\right)$ and $\mathbf{v} = \left(v_i\right)$ the projection related is

$$P = \sum_{i,j} u_i \left(\langle\mathbf{v}, \mathbf{u}\rangle^{-1}\right)_{j,i} \otimes v_j,$$

where $\langle\mathbf{v},\mathbf{u}\rangle$ is the matrix with entries $\left(\langle\mathbf{v}, \mathbf{u}\rangle\right)_{i,j} = \left\langle v_i, u_j\right\rangle.$

- $\tilde u_i := (I - P) u_i,$ and $\tilde v_i := (I - P)^* v_i$ then is a biorthogonal system.

:::
