# Albrecht Fröhlich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albrecht Fröhlich"}

**Albrecht Fröhlich** [^o5] (22 May 1916 – 8 November 2001) was a German-born British mathematician, famous for his major results and [conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjectures) on [Galois module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module) theory in the Galois structure of rings of integers.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He was born in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich) to a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) family. He fled from the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism) to [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), and then to [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine). He went to [Bristol University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_University) in 1945, gaining a B.Sc. in 1948 and a Ph.D. in 1951 with a dissertation entitled *On Some Topics in the Theory of Representation of Groups and Individual Class Field Theory* under the supervision of [Hans Heilbronn](wikipedia://en/4388848). He was a lecturer at the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester) and then at the [Keele University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Keele), then in 1962 moved as reader to [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) where he worked until his retirement in 1981 when he moved to [Robinson College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_College,_Cambridge).

## Awards {#Awards}

He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1976.[^o5] He was awarded the [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prizes) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 1976 and its [De Morgan Medal](wikipedia://en/1096532) in 1992. The Society's [Fröhlich Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fröhlich_Prize) is named in his honour.

## Personal {#Personal}

He is the brother of [Herbert Fröhlich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Fröhlich).
