# Edward Brandt Jr.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Brandt Jr."}

**Edward Newman Brandt Jr.** MD (July 3, 1933 – August 26, 2007) was an American [physician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physician), mathematician, and [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) administrator. He was appointed acting [surgeon general of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surgeon_General_of_the_United_States) from 1981 to 1982 and served as the [United States assistant secretary for health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Assistant_Secretary_for_Health) from 1981 to 1984.

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Born in [Oklahoma City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City), Brandt graduated with an MD and PhD in biostatistics from the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma) School of Medicine.[^o4] He was a faculty member from his alma mater from 1961 to 1970 before moving to the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas) at [Galveston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galveston,_Texas) (1970–1981), [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) at [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore) (1981–1989), and [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma) School of Medicine (1989–2007).[^o4]

## Death {#Death}

Brandt died of lung cancer on August 26, 2007; he was 74 years old.[^o6][^o7] His papers were donated to the [National Library of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Medicine). [^o8]
