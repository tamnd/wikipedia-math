# Thomas Wolff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Wolff"}

*Image: Thomas Wolff.jpg.* Wolff in 1992

**Thomas Hartwig Wolff** (July 14, 1954, [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) – July 31, 2000, [Kern County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_County)) was an American mathematician, working primarily in the fields of [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). As an undergraduate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), he regularly played poker with his classmate [Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates). While a graduate student at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1976 to 1979, under the direction of [Donald Sarason](wikipedia://en/53777531), he obtained a new proof of the [corona theorem](wikipedia://en/4499209), a famously difficult theorem in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He was made Professor of Mathematics at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) in 1986, and was there from 1988–1992 and from 1995 to his death in a car accident in 2000. He also held positions at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), and [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o2][^o3]

:::

He received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1985 and the [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) in 1999, for his contributions to analysis and particularly to the [Kakeya conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakeya_conjecture).[^o4][^o5] He was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1986 in Berkeley[^o6] and in 1998 in Berlin.[^o7]
