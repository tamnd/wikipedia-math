# L. T. F. Gamut

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="L. T. F. Gamut"}

**L. T. F. Gamut** was a collective [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) for the [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) [logicians](wikipedia://en/46426065) [Johan van Benthem](wikipedia://en/1498176), [Jeroen Groenendijk](wikipedia://en/15752422), [Dick de Jongh](wikipedia://en/3888928), [Martin Stokhof](wikipedia://en/3888996) and [Henk Verkuyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henk_Verkuyl).[^o2]

:::

Gamut stands for the Dutch universities of [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) (**G**), [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) (**am**), and [Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) (**ut**), then the affiliations of the authors.[^o3] The initials L. T. F. stand for the discussed topics, respectively, Logic (Dutch: ***L****ogica*), Language (Dutch: ***T****aal*) and Philosophy (Dutch: ***F****ilosofie*).

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Logica, taal en betekenis I: inleiding in de logica*, [Het Spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Het_Spectrum), 1982.
- *Logica, taal en betekenis II: intensionele logica en logische grammatica*, Het Spectrum, 1982.
- *Logic, Language and Meaning, Volume I: Introduction to Logic*, [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) Press, 1991. Translation and revision of *Logica, taal en betekenis I*.
- *Logic, Language and Meaning, Volume II: Intensional Logic and Logical Grammar*, University of Chicago Press, 1991. Translation and revision of *Logica, taal en betekenis II*.
