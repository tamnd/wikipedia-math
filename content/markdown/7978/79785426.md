# Limits and colimits in an ∞-category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Limits and colimits in an ∞-category"}

In mathematics, especially [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), **limits** and **colimits** in an [∞-category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-category) generalize [limits](wikipedia://en/39248) and [colimits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colimit_%28category_theory%29) in a category. Like the counterparts in ordinary category theory, they play fundamental roles in constructions (e.g., [Kan extensions](wikipedia://en/2502518)) as well as characterizations (e.g., sheaf conditions) in [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $I$ be a simplicial set and $C$ an ∞-category (a weak Kan complex). Fix a [Grothendieck universe](wikipedia://en/1072144). Then, roughly, a limit of a functor $f : I \to C$ amounts to the following isomorphism:

$$\operatorname{Hom}(a_{\textrm{const}}, f) \overset{\sim}\to \operatorname{Hom}(a, \varprojlim f)$$

functorially in $a$,[^o4] where $a_{\textrm{const}} : I \to C$ denotes the constant functor with value $a$.

A typical case is when $I = \Delta$ is the simplex category or rather its opposite; in the latter case, the functor $f$ is commonly called a [simplicial diagram](wikipedia://en/79777766).

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Facts"}

## Facts {#Facts}

The ordinary category of sets has small limits and colimits. Similarly,

- The ∞-category of ∞-categories and the ∞-category of Kan complexes both have all small limits and colimits.[^o7]
- The presheaf category $\mathcal{P}(C) = \textbf{Fct}(C, \textbf{Kan})$ on an ∞-category *C* has colimits, as a consequence of the above.[^o8]

Also, many of standard facts about limits and colimits in a category continue to hold for those in an ∞-category.

- An ∞-category has all small limits if and only if it has coequalizers and small coproducts.[^o9]
- If a functor admits a left adjoint, then it commutes with all limits.[^o10]

:::
