# Age of the captain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Age of the captain"}

The **age of the captain** is a [mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical) [word problem](wikipedia://en/297013) which cannot be answered even though there seems to be plenty of information supplied. It was given for the first time by [Gustave Flaubert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Flaubert) in [a letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaubert's_letters) to his sister Caroline in 1841:[^o2][^o3]

Puisque tu fais de la géométrie et de la trigonométrie, je vais te donner un problème : Un navire est en mer, il est parti de Boston chargé de coton, il jauge 200 tonneaux. Il fait voile vers le Havre, le grand mât est cassé, il y a un mousse sur le gaillard d’avant, les passagers sont au nombre de douze, le vent souffle N.-E.-E., l’horloge marque 3 heures un quart d’après-midi, on est au mois de mai…. On demande l’âge du capitaine ?[^o2][^o3]

:::

More recently, a simpler version has been used to study how students react to word problems:

> A captain owns 26 sheep and 10 goats. How old is the captain?[^o5]

Many children in elementary school, from different parts of the world, attempt to "solve" this nonsensical problem by giving the answer 36, obtained by adding the numbers 26 and 10.[^o5][^o6] It has been suggested that this indicates schooling and education fail to instill critical thinking in children, and do not teach them that a question may be unsolvable.[^o5][^o6] However, others have countered that in education students are taught that all questions have a solution and that giving any answer is better than leaving it blank, hence the attempt to "solve" it.[^o5][^o6]

This problem also appears in [Richard Rusczyk](wikipedia://en/2233847)'s *Introduction to Geometry* at the end of chapter 18 in the "extra" box, as well as in [Evan Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Chen)'s *Euclidean Geometry in Mathematical Olympiads* at the beginning of chapter 5.

## Variations {#Variations}

In 2018, the question "If a ship had 26 sheep and 10 goats onboard, how old is the ship's captain?" appeared in a fifth-grade examination for 11-year-old students of a [Shunqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shunqing) primary school. A [Weibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weibo) commenter noted that the total weight of 26 average sheep and 10 average goats is 7700 kg. In China, one needs to have possessed a boat license for five years to drive a ship with over 5000 kg of cargo. As the minimum age to get a boat's license is 23, the captain is at least 28.[^o9] The statutory retirement age in China is 60 for men and up to 55 for women, thus the captain's age is between 28 and 60.[^o10]

Some versions begin with a statement like "Imagine you're a ship's captain", before distracting the answerer with much irrelevant information. The answer is simply the age of the answerer.[^o11]
