# Marie Litzinger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marie Litzinger"}

**Marie Litzinger** (May 14, 1899 – April 7, 1952) was an American mathematician known for her research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [homogeneous polynomials](wikipedia://en/1698977), and [modular arithmetic](wikipedia://en/20087).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Marie Litzinger was born in [Bedford, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford,_Pennsylvania), the daughter of Rush Litzinger and Katherine O'Connell Litzinger.[^o11][^o4] Her father owned a marble works, and was an accountant for the [Pennsylvania Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_Railroad).[^o5]

Litzinger earned her [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) and [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) at [Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College) in 1920[^o6] and 1922, respectively. She won the college's European Fellowship in 1920.[^o7] She completed her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1934.[^o9] Her doctoral advisor was [Leonard Eugene Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247), one of the first American researchers in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384).[^o8] Her dissertation was titled "A Basis for Residual Polynomials in *n* Variables."[^o11]

## Career {#Career}

While studying, Litzinger taught mathematics at secondary institutions, before becoming an instructor at [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College) in 1925, where she remained for the rest of her career and rose to the rank of full professor in 1942.[^o11] She also served as department chair. She was a member of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), among other professional societies.[^o11][^o12][^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Litzinger died in her hometown, Bedford, Pennsylvania, in 1952, aged 52 years.[^o15]

## Papers {#Papers}

The Marie P. Litzinger Papers, which consist of Litzinger's college correspondence and photographs, are held at Bryn Mawr College with the Collection Identifier BMC-M124.[^o17]
