# Petru Mocanu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Petru Mocanu"}

**Petru T. Mocanu** (1 June 1931 – 28 March 2016) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was elected in 2009 as a titular member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy).

:::

Mocanu was born in [Brăila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brăila).[^o2][^o3] He studied at the Nicolae Bălcescu High School in Brăila, graduating in 1950.[^o4] He then went to study mathematics at [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University) in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca), completing his [B.Sc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc) in 1953, and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1959. His dissertation, written under the supervision of [Gheorghe Călugăreanu](wikipedia://en/71535582), was titled *Variational methods in the theory of univalent functions*. He continued as faculty at Babeș-Bolyai University, rising to the rank of Professor in 1970.

Mocanu was an invited professor at the [University of Conakry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamal_Abdel_Nasser_University_of_Conakry) in 1966–1967, and the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1992.[^o4]
