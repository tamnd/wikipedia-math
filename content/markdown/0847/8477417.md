# Dispersive partial differential equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dispersive partial differential equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **dispersive partial differential equation** or **dispersive PDE** is a [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) that is [dispersive](wikipedia://en/853778). In this context, dispersion means that waves of different [wavelength](wikipedia://en/33125) propagate at different [phase velocities](wikipedia://en/25098).

:::

## Examples {#Examples}

### Linear equations {#Linear_equations}

- [Euler–Bernoulli beam equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler–Bernoulli_beam_equation) with time-dependent loading
- Airy equation
- [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874)
- [Klein–Gordon equation](wikipedia://en/209627)

### Nonlinear equations {#Nonlinear_equations}

- [nonlinear Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/1422748)
- [Korteweg–de Vries equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korteweg–de_Vries_equation) (or KdV equation)
- [Boussinesq equation (water waves)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boussinesq_equation_%28water_waves%29)
- [sine–Gordon equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine–Gordon_equation)
