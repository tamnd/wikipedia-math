# Harish-Chandra character

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harish-Chandra character"}

In mathematics, the **Harish-Chandra character**, named after [Harish-Chandra](wikipedia://en/373190), of a representation of a [semisimple Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group) *G* on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* is a [distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29) on the group *G* that is analogous to the character of a finite-dimensional representation of a [compact group](wikipedia://en/750326).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that π is an irreducible [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of *G* on a Hilbert space *H*. If *f* is a [compactly supported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactly_supported) [smooth function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) on the group *G*, then the operator on *H*

$$\pi(f) = \int_Gf(x)\pi(x)\,dx$$

is of [trace class](wikipedia://en/192266), and the distribution

$$\Theta_\pi:f\mapsto \operatorname{Tr}(\pi(f))$$

is called the **character** (or **global character** or **Harish-Chandra character**) of the representation.

The character Θ<sub>π</sub> is a distribution on *G* that is invariant under conjugation, and is an eigendistribution of the center of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of *G*, in other words an invariant eigendistribution, with eigenvalue the [infinitesimal character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitesimal_character) of the representation π.

[Harish-Chandra's regularity theorem](wikipedia://en/11277644) states that any invariant eigendistribution, and in particular any character of an irreducible unitary representation on a Hilbert space, is given by a [locally integrable function](wikipedia://en/4258398).
