# Jeff Paris (mathematician)

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Jeff Paris"}

**Jeffrey Bruce Paris** (born 15 November 1944) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor of Logic in the [School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics,_University_of_Manchester) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester).[^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Paris gained his doctorate supervised by [Robin Gandy](wikipedia://en/3155895) at Manchester in 1969 with a dissertation on *[Large Cardinals](wikipedia://en/383644) and the [Generalized Continuum Hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Continuum_Hypothesis)*.[^o3]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Paris is known for his work on [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), in particular [provability](wikipedia://en/618119) in arithmetic, uncertain reasoning and [inductive logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_logic) with an emphasis on rationality and common sense principles.

The [Paris–Harrington theorem](wikipedia://en/3875355)[^o11] is a natural [Ramsey-theoretic](wikipedia://en/157175) statement that is expressible in [Peano arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_arithmetic), but is not provable within this system, an illustration of [Gödel's first incompleteness theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel's_first_incompleteness_theorem).

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Paris was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 1983 and elected a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy) (FBA) in 1999.[^o2][^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Paris was married to Malvyn Loraine Blackburn until 1983 when he married Alena Vencovská. He has three sons and three daughters including runner [Jasmin Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasmin_Paris).[^o2]
