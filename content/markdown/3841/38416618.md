# Ib Madsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ib Madsen"}

*Image: Ib Madsen.jpeg.* Ib Madsen in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2008

**Ib Henning Madsen** (born 12 April 1942, in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen))[^o2] is a Danish mathematician, a professor of mathematics at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). He is known for (with [Michael Weiss](wikipedia://en/46796669)) proving the Mumford conjecture on the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of the stable [mapping class group](wikipedia://en/793295), and for developing topological [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172) theory.[^o10]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Madsen earned a candidate degree from the University of Copenhagen in 1965, and a Ph.D. from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1970 under the supervision of [J. Peter May](wikipedia://en/14644098).[^o2][^o10] In 1971 he took a faculty position at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University), and he remained there until 2008, when he moved to Copenhagen.[^o2][^o10]

His doctoral students have included [Søren Galatius](wikipedia://en/45122752) and [Lars Hesselholt](wikipedia://en/45444071).[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Madsen was elected as a member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 1978,[^o6] as a foreign member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1998,[^o2][^o7] and as a foreign member of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) in 2000.[^o2][^o8] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

In 1992, he was awarded the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o2] In 2011, he won the [Ostrowski Prize](wikipedia://en/3159411) for outstanding achievement in pure mathematics, shared with [Kannan Soundararajan](wikipedia://en/8053624) and [David Preiss](wikipedia://en/19372849).[^o10]
