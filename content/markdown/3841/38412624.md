# Willi Rinow

*Image: Greifswald Friedrich-Ludwig-Jahn-Straße 15 Tafel Rinow.JPG.* Plaque for Willi Rinow on Friedrich-Ludwig-Jahn Street in Greifswald

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Willi Rinow"}

**Willi Ludwig August Rinow** (February 28th, 1907 in Berlin – March 29th, 1979 in [Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greifswald)) was a German mathematician who specialized in differential geometry and topology. Rinow was the son of a schoolteacher. In 1926, he attended the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), studying mathematics and physics under professors such as [Max Planck](wikipedia://en/19848), [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186), and [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082). There, he received his doctorate in 1931 (*Über Zusammenhänge zwischen der Differentialgeometrie im Großen und im Kleinen*, Math. Zeitschrift volume 35, 1932, page 512). In 1933, he worked at the Jahrbuch über die Fortschritte der Mathematik in Berlin. In 1937, he joined the [Nazi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party).[^o3] During 1937—1940, he was an editor of the journal *[Deutsche Mathematik](wikipedia://en/31018614)*. In 1937, he became a professor in Berlin and lectured there until 1950. His lecturing was interrupted because of his work as a mathematician at the [Oberspreewerk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberspreewerk) in Berlin (a producer of radio and telecommunications technology) from 1946 to 1949.

:::

In 1950, he became a professor at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald). He retired in 1972.

The [Hopf–Rinow theorem](wikipedia://en/681265) is named after Hopf and Rinow.

In 1959, he became the director of the Institute for Pure Mathematics at the [German Academy of Sciences at Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_at_Berlin) and president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).

## War work {#War_work}

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Rinow worked as a [cryptanalyst](wikipedia://en/5715) in Subsection *F* of Referat I of Group IV of the Inspectorate 7/VI, that was later called the [General der Nachrichtenaufklärung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_der_Nachrichtenaufklärung) (GdNA), achieving the rank of Colonel. Rinow worked on researching methods to solve foreign ciphers. Rinow was subordinated to [Herbert von Denffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_von_Denffer) who was Director of the section and subordinated to [Hans Pietsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Pietsch_%28Mathematican%29) who was Director of the section. Major Rudolf Hentze was head of the group. [Otto Buggisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Buggisch) who was a cryptanalyst who working in the GdNA and earlier the [OKW/Chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cipher_Department_of_the_High_Command_of_the_Wehrmacht) and was interrogated by [TICOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TICOM) agents after the war, stated that Rinow was one of the most capable people in the unit.[^o5]
