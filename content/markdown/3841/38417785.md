# Superelement

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Superelement"}

In [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing) and [computational engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_engineering), a **superelement** is a [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581) technique which consists of defining a new type of finite element by grouping and processing a set of finite elements. A superelement describes a part of a [problem](wikipedia://en/1467948), and can be locally solved, before being implemented in the global problem. Substructuring a problem by means of superelements may facilitate the [division of labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_labor) and overcome [computer memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_memory) limitations.

:::

## History {#History}

Superelements were invented in the [aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace) industry, where complexity and the size of problems exceeded the solving capabilities of the computational hardware. The development of superelements made solving of larger problems possible, by breakdown of [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system) such as complete airplanes.
