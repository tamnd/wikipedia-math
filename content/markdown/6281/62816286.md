# Pietro Corvaja

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pietro Corvaja"}

**Pietro Corvaja** (born 19 July 1967 in [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy))[^o3] is an Italian mathematician working in [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205). He is a professor of geometry at the [University of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Udine).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Corvaja was born in Padua, Italy on 19 July 1967.[^o3] He graduated with a scientific high school diploma from a [liceo scientifico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liceo_scientifico) in 1985,[^o3] before enrolling in the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) as a student of the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa).[^o3] He graduated from the Scuola Normale with an undergraduate thesis on the theory of [transcendental numbers](wikipedia://en/30325) under the direction of Roberto Dvornicich in 1989.[^o3][^o7]

After a one year scholarship at [INdAM](wikipedia://en/34249626) from 1989 to 1990, Corvaja completed his PhD under [Michel Waldschmidt](wikipedia://en/35216482) and Michel Laurent at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1995.[^o3] From 1994 to 1995, he was also a research assistant at the [Università Iuav di Venezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_Iuav_di_Venezia) as a collaborator of [Umberto Zannier](wikipedia://en/58056511).[^o3] In 2001, Corvaja obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) qualification at Pierre and Marie Curie University.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

In 1995, Corvaja became a researcher at the University of Udine.[^o3] From 1997 to 1998, he was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) under the direction of [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475).[^o10][^o3] In 2002, Corvaja became an associate professor of algebra at the University of Udine.[^o3] Since 2005, he has been a professor of geometry at the University of Udine.[^o3][^o7]

Corvaja is the coordinator of the mathematics program and the vice director of the [Scuola Superiore (School of Excellence)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_Graduate_Schools_in_Italy) of the University of Udine.[^o11][^o3][^o12]

## Research {#Research}

Corvaja and Zannier gave a new proof of [Siegel's theorem on integral points](wikipedia://en/3989092) in 2002 by using a new method based on the [subspace theorem](wikipedia://en/8830237).[^o14]

## Awards {#Awards}

Corvaja was inducted into the [Istituto Veneto di Scienze, Lettere ed Arti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Veneto_di_Scienze,_Lettere_ed_Arti) on 26 May 2016.[^o3]
