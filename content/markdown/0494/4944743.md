# Neil Tennant (philosopher)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neil Tennant"}

**Neil Tennant** (born 1 March 1950) is an American philosopher. He is Arts & Humanities Distinguished Professor of Philosophy at the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University); and, before taking up his appointment at the Ohio State University he held positions at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), the [University of Stirling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stirling), and the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University).

:::

Along with [Michael Dummett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dummett), [Crispin Wright](wikipedia://en/302883), Tennant is one of the most notable figures who have attempted to extend the project of providing [anti-realist semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_anti-realism_%28epistemology%29) for [empirical language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_language).[^o3][^o4] He has also written extensively on [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262) and other [non-classical logics](wikipedia://en/3192556).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tennant was the editor of the journal *[American Philosophical Quarterly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Quarterly)* from 2004 to 2007.

He was elected to the [Australian Academy of the Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_the_Humanities), as a [Corresponding Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academician#Corresponding_Member), in 1990.[^o6][^o7]

- *Philosophy, Evolution and Human Nature* (with F. von Schilcher), Routledge and Kegan Paul, 1984, viii+283 pp.
- *Anti-Realism and Logic: Truth as Eternal*, Clarendon Library of Logic and Philosophy, Oxford University Press, 1987, xii+325 pp.
- *Autologic*, Edinburgh University Press, 1992, xiii+239 pp.
- *The Taming of the True*, [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press), 1997, xvii+465 pp. Paperback edition 2002.

- "New Foundations for a Relational Theory of Theory-Revision", *Journal of Philosophical Logic*, vol. 35, no. 5, pp. 489–52, October 2006.
- *Introducing Philosophy: God, Mind, World, and Logic*, Routledge, 2015.
- *Core Logic*, Oxford University Press, 2017.
