# Totally disconnected group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Totally disconnected group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **totally disconnected group** is a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) that is [totally disconnected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_disconnected). Such topological groups are necessarily [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637).

:::

Interest centres on [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact) totally disconnected groups (variously referred to as groups of **td-type**,[^o2] [locally profinite groups](wikipedia://en/32286640),[^o3] or **t.d. groups**[^o4]). The [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) case has been heavily studied – these are the [profinite groups](wikipedia://en/24266) – but for a long time not much was known about the general case. A theorem of [van Dantzig](wikipedia://en/528853)[^o5] from the 1930s, stating that every such group contains a compact [open](wikipedia://en/39358) [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011), was all that was known. Then groundbreaking work by [George Willis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_A._Willis) in 1994,[^o6] opened up the field by showing that every locally compact totally disconnected group contains a so-called *tidy* subgroup and a special function on its [automorphisms](wikipedia://en/1160), the *scale function*, giving a quantifiable parameter for the local structure. Advances on the *global structure* of totally disconnected groups were obtained in 2011 by Caprace and [Monod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Monod), with notably a classification of [characteristically simple groups](wikipedia://en/4966729) and of [Noetherian groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_group).[^o7]

## Locally compact case {#Locally_compact_case}

In a locally compact, totally disconnected group, every [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) of the identity contains a compact open subgroup. Conversely, if a group is such that the identity has a [neighbourhood basis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_basis) consisting of compact open subgroups, then it is locally compact and totally disconnected.[^o3]

### Tidy subgroups {#Tidy_subgroups}

Let *G* be a locally compact, totally disconnected group, *U* a compact open subgroup of *G* and $\alpha$ a continuous automorphism of *G*.

Define:

$$U_{+}=\bigcap_{n\ge 0}\alpha^n(U)$$

$$U_{-}=\bigcap_{n\ge 0}\alpha^{-n}(U)$$

$$U_{++}=\bigcup_{n\ge 0}\alpha^n(U_{+})$$

$$U_{--}=\bigcup_{n\ge 0}\alpha^{-n}(U_{-})$$

*U* is said to be **tidy** for $\alpha$ if and only if $U=U_{+}U_{-}=U_{-}U_{+}$ and $U_{++}$ and $U_{--}$ are closed.

### The scale function {#The_scale_function}

The index of $U_{+}$ in $\alpha(U_{+})$ is shown to be finite and independent of the compact open subgroup *U* which is tidy for $\alpha$. Defining the scale $s(\alpha)$ of the continuous automorphism $\alpha$ to be this index, we obtain the scale function $s:\mathrm{Aut}(G)\to\mathbb{N}$. Restricting $s$ to [inner automorphisms](wikipedia://en/61839) by setting $s(x):=s(\alpha_{x})$ for a given element $x\in G$ with associated inner automorphism $\alpha_{x}$ results in a function $s:G\to\mathbb{N}$ with the following interesting properties.

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

#### Properties {#Properties}

- $s$ is continuous for the discrete topology on $\mathbb{N}$.
- $s(x)=1$, whenever x in *G* is a compact element.
- $s(x^n)=s(x)^n$ for every non-negative integer $n$.
- The modular function on *G* is given by $\Delta(x)=s(x)s(x^{-1})^{-1}$.

:::

### Calculations and applications {#Calculations_and_applications}

The scale function was used to prove a conjecture by Hofmann and Mukherja and has been explicitly calculated for [p-adic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and linear groups over local skew fields by Helge Glöckner.
