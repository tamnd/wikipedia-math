# Pamela G. Coxson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pamela G. Coxson"}

**Pamela Gail Coxson** is an American applied mathematician specialized in [disease modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease_models). She is retired as a specialist in the division of general and internal medicine at the [University of California, San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Francisco) Center for Vulnerable Populations.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Coxson completed a Ph.D. in mathematics from [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California). Her 1979 dissertation was titled *On the equivalence between open loop and closed loop control laws for linear systems*. Alan Schumitzky was her doctoral advisor.

In 1985, while working as a fellow at the [Mary Ingraham Bunting Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Ingraham_Bunting_Institute), Coxson initiated the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698)' Sonia Kovalevsky Math Day program for female high schoolers and their teachers.[^o5] In 1986, Coxson was a visiting assistant professor of mathematics at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). One of her mentors was [Violet B. Haas](wikipedia://en/70573868), whom she met in 1983.[^o6]

Coxson has worked in several areas of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) including mathematics in [pharmacokinetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacokinetics), [catalytic cracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalytic_cracking) of oil, satellite image processing, and [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging).[^o7] Formerly working as a specialist in the division of general and internal medicine at the [University of California, San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Francisco) Center for Vulnerable Populations,[^o8] she retired in 2018.[^o9]
