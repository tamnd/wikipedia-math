# Pseudo-abelian category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo-abelian category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **pseudo-abelian category** is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) that is [preadditive](wikipedia://en/59521) and is such that every [idempotent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent) has a [kernel](wikipedia://en/142616).[^o2] Recall that an idempotent [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) $p$ is an [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) of an object with the property that $p\circ p = p$. Elementary considerations show that every idempotent then has a [cokernel](wikipedia://en/279715).[^o3] The pseudo-abelian condition is stronger than preadditivity, but it is weaker than the requirement that every morphism have a kernel and cokernel, as is true for [abelian categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_categories).

:::

Synonyms in the literature for pseudo-abelian include **pseudoabelian** and **Karoubian**.

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Any [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063), in particular the category **Ab** of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974), is pseudo-abelian. Indeed, in an abelian category, *every* morphism has a kernel.

The category of [rngs](wikipedia://en/3405004) (not [rings](wikipedia://en/48404)!) together with multiplicative morphisms is pseudo-abelian.

A more complicated example is the category of [Chow motives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chow_motive). The construction of Chow motives uses the pseudo-abelian completion described below.

:::

## Pseudo-abelian completion {#Pseudo-abelian_completion}

The [Karoubi envelope](wikipedia://en/933541) construction associates to an arbitrary category $C$ a category $\operatorname{Kar}C$ together with a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987)

$$s:C \to \operatorname{Kar}C$$

such that the image $s(p)$ of every idempotent $p$ in $C$ splits in $\operatorname{Kar}C$. When applied to a preadditive category $C$, the Karoubi envelope construction yields a pseudo-abelian category $\operatorname{Kar}C$ called the **pseudo-abelian completion** or **pseudo-abelian envelope** of $C$. Moreover, the functor

$$C \to \operatorname{Kar}C$$

is in fact an additive morphism.

To be precise, given a preadditive category $C$ we construct a pseudo-abelian category $\operatorname{Kar}C$ in the following way. The objects of $\operatorname{Kar}C$ are pairs $(X,p)$ where $X$ is an object of $C$ and $p$ is an idempotent of $X$. The morphisms

$$f:(X,p) \to (Y,q)$$

in $\operatorname{Kar}C$ are those morphisms

$$f:X \to Y$$

such that $f = q \circ f = f \circ p$ in $C$. The functor

$$C \to \operatorname{Kar}C$$

is given by taking $X$ to $(X, \mathrm{id}_X)$.
