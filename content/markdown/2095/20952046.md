# Roland Glowinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Glowinski"}

**Roland Glowinski** (9 March 1937 – 26 January 2022) was a French-American mathematician. He obtained his PhD in 1970 from [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500) and was known for his work in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), in particular numerical solution and applications of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and [variational inequalities](wikipedia://en/1468817). He was a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) and held an [endowed chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowed_chair) at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) from 1985. Glowinski wrote many books on the subject of mathematics. In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

:::
