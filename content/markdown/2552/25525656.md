# Quantum KZ equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum KZ equations"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **quantum KZ equations** or **quantum Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov equations** or **qKZ equations** are the analogue for [quantum affine algebras](wikipedia://en/25666636) of the [Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov equations](wikipedia://en/16119106) for [affine Kac–Moody algebras](wikipedia://en/1146292). They are a consistent system of [difference equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation) satisfied by the *N*-point functions, the vacuum expectations of products of primary fields. In the limit as the deformation parameter *q* approaches 1, the *N*-point functions of the quantum affine algebra tend to those of the affine Kac–Moody algebra and the difference equations become [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). The quantum KZ equations have been used to study [exactly solved models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exactly_solved_model) in [quantum statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/712450).

:::
