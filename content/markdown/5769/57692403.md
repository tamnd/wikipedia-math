# Ferran Hurtado

Hurtado

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ferran Hurtado"}

**Ferran Hurtado Díaz** (8 May 1951 – 2 October 2014) was a Spanish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) known for his research in [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hurtado was born on 8 May 1951 in [Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia), Spain. He earned his Ph.D. degree from the [Polytechnic University of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Catalonia) in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona) in 1993 under the supervision of Oriol Serra Albó; his dissertation was *Problemas geométricos de visibilidad* \[*Geometric problems of visibility*\]. It won the *Premio Extraordinario de Doctorado UPC* in 1995.

He became a professor at the Polytechnic University of Catalonia, and died on 2 October 2014 in Barcelona.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Hurtado was a pioneer of Spanish [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), and of connections between computational geometry and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He is known, not only for his own research contributions to those subjects, but also for the questions he posed for others to solve. The topics of his research included [flip graphs](wikipedia://en/47526601) of [polygon triangulations](wikipedia://en/679384), [Voronoi diagrams](wikipedia://en/177668), [visibility](wikipedia://en/9331665), [simple polygons](wikipedia://en/1059530) with given points as vertices, [empty convex polygons](wikipedia://en/2576185) in point sets, [geometric graph theory](wikipedia://en/3313527), and problems on colored sets of points.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018, the journal *[Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_Geometry_%28journal%29)* published a special issue in Hurtado's memory.
