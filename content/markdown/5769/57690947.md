# Sumio Watanabe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sumio Watanabe"}

**Sumio Watanabe** is a Japanese mathematician and engineer working in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), applied [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412). He is currently a professor at [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Institute_of_Technology) in the Department of Computational Intelligence and Systems Science.[^o2] He is the author of the text, Algebraic Geometry and Statistical Learning Theory, which proposes a generalization of Fisher's [regular statistical theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_parametric_model) to [singular statistical models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_statistical_model).[^o3]

:::
