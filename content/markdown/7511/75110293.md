# Shavkat Ayupov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shavkat Ayupov"}

**Shavkat Abdullayevich Ayupov** (Шавкат Абдуллаевич Аюпов; born September 14, 1952, in [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent)) is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [Uzbek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbeks) scientist in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).[^o3][^o4] He is an Academician of the Uzbekistan Academy of Sciences (1995).[^o5] He is also a Senator in the Senate of the [Oliy Majlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliy_Majlis) of the [Republic of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan) (2020).[^o6] He was awarded the title of [Hero of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Uzbekistan) in 2021, and he holds the title of Distinguished Scientist of the Republic of [Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan) (2011). President of [Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Uzbekistan) from December 16, 2024.

:::

He was elected a Fellow of [the World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [International Core Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Core_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o7]

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born into an intellectual family. His father, Abdulla Talipovich Ayupov, was a participant in the Great Patriotic War and headed the Department of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent) University.[^o6][^o9] His mother, Marguba Khamidova, was a doctor who worked as a therapist at the 4th City Clinical Hospital.[^o6]

He graduated from [Tashkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent) University in 1974 and was a student of Academician T.A. Sarymsakov. He earned a Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree (1977) and a Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree (1983).[^o3][^o6][^o10][^o11]

He was a laureate of the [Lenin Komsomol Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Komsomol_Prize) as part of the authorial team consisting of Berdikulov, Musulmonkul Abdullaevich, Usmanov, Shukhrat Muttalibovich, and a research fellow at the Institute of Mathematics named after V.I. Romanovsky at the Academy of Sciences of the [Uzbek SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic); Abdullaev, Rustambai Zairovich, an assistant at the [Tashkent State Pedagogical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent_State_Pedagogic_University_named_after_Nizami) named after V.I. Lenin; Tikhonov, Oleg Yevgenyevich, an assistant, and Trunov, Nikolai Vasilievich, an associate professor at the V.I. Ulyanov [Kazan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_State_University), for their work on "Research on Operator Algebras and Non-Commutative Integration" (1989).

He became an Academician of the [World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2003.[^o6] From 2008 to 2013, he was an associated member of the Abdus Salam [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) (ICTP) in Trieste, Italy.[^o6] He became a member of the Senate of the Oliy Majlis of the Republic of Uzbekistan in 2020.[^o6]

In 1992, he became the head of the Institute of Mathematics named after V.I. Romanovsky at the Academy of Sciences of the [Republic of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Uzbekistan).[^o3][^o12]

In 1994, he embarked on a lengthy assignment at the University of [Louis Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur) in Strasbourg, France, where he conducted joint research with Professor J.-L. Lode.[^o6] He teaches at the [National University of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Uzbekistan) and is a professor of the Department of [Algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [Functional Analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o13]
