# Sylvie Paycha

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvie Paycha"}

**Sylvie Paycha** (born 27 March 1960 in [Neuilly-sur-Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuilly-sur-Seine)) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) working in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) as a professor at the [University of Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Potsdam).[^o3] She has chaired both European Women in Mathematics and [L'association femmes et mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'association_femmes_et_mathématiques).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

She completed her PhD thesis at the [University of Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bochum), Germany in 1988.[^o6] Her doctoral advisor was [Sergio Albeverio](wikipedia://en/28869024).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Albeverio, Sergio; Jost, Jürgen; Paycha, Sylvie; Scarlatti, Sergio. (1997). "A mathematical introduction to string theory". 225. Cambridge University Press. doi:10.1017/CBO9780511600791. isbn:0-521-55610-4[^o10]
- Paycha, Sylvie. (2012). "Regularised integrals, sums and traces". 59. American Mathematical Society. doi:10.1090/ulect/059. isbn:978-0-8218-5367-2[^o12]
