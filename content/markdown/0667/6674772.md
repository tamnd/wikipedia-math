# John H. Hubbard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John H. Hubbard"}

**John Hamal Hubbard** (born October 6 or 7, 1945) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and the [Université de Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Provence). He is known for the mathematical contributions he made with [Adrien Douady](wikipedia://en/1604604) in the field of [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518), including a study of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562). One of their most important results is that the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562) is [connected](wikipedia://en/6233).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Hubbard graduated with a [Doctorat d'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'État) from [Université de Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universite_de_Paris) in 1973 under the direction of [Adrien Douady](wikipedia://en/1604604); his thesis was entitled *Sur Les Sections Analytiques de La Courbe Universelle de Teichmüller* and was published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Writing {#Writing}

Hubbard and his wife [Barbara Burke Hubbard](wikipedia://en/69194056) wrote the book *Vector Calculus, Linear Algebra, and Differential Forms: A Unified Approach*.

He has also published three volumes of a book on [Teichmüller theory](wikipedia://en/1835661) and its applications to four revolutionary theorems of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Hubbard is married to [Barbara Burke Hubbard](wikipedia://en/69194056), a science writer. Together they have a son and three younger daughters.[^o6]
