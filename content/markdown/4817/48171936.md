# David Dolidze

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Dolidze"}

**David Egorovich Dolidze** (დავით დოლიძე, Давид Егорович Долидзе) (2 February – 1960) was a [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_%28country%29) and [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902),[^o3] known from his work in the mathematical theory of [fluid motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_motion). In particular he rediscovered an important uniqueness theorem for the [classical solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_solution) to the Navier–Stokes equations for an [incompressible fluid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incompressible_fluid), previously proved by [Emanuele Foà](wikipedia://en/48259346).[^o4]

:::

## Life and academic career {#Life_and_academic_career}

Born on 2 February 1908 in the [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) of Buknari,[^o6] He graduated from Tbilisi State University in 1928.[^o3] From 1934 he started working in his [Alma Mater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alma_Mater), and one year later, in 1935, he also started doing research at the Mathematics Institute of the [Georgian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Academy_of_Science).[^o3] In 1945 he earned both the [Doktor Nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_Nauk) degree and the title of [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).[^o3]
