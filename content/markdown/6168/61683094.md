# George Marinescu (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Marinescu"}

*Image: George Marinescu Oberwolfach 2022.jpg.* Marinescu at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2022

**George Marinescu** (born 22 June 1965, [Brașov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brașov)) is a Romanian mathematician, specializing in [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498), and [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713).

:::

Marinescu received from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 1988 his baccalaureate degree and in 1989 his master's degree.[^o2] He graduated in 1994 with Ph.D. from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (University of Paris 7) with thesis under the supervision of [Louis Boutet de Monvel](wikipedia://en/34055924). Marinescu was a postdoc from 1997 to 1998 at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), from 1998 to 1999 at the [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_mathématiques_de_Jussieu), and from 1999 to 2000 at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), completing there his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) qualification in 2005. He was at the Humboldt University of Berlin an assistant researcher from 2000 to 2005 and is since 2006 a professor at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne).[^o2]

He was awarded, jointly with [Xiaonan Ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaonan_Ma), the [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer](wikipedia://en/36136840) Prize in 2006 for their book "Holomorphic Morse inequalities and Bergman kernels".[^o4][^o5][^o6] Marinescu was awarded the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy)'s 2012 [Simion Stoilow Prize](wikipedia://en/20268944)[^o7] (with the formal ceremony in 2014).

His son is the scholar of ancient philosophy, Rareș I. Marinescu.[^o8]
