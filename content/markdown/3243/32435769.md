# Josef Teichmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Josef Teichmann"}

*Image: ETH-BIB-Teichmann, Josef (1972-)-Portr 17413.jpg.* Josef Teichmann, 2019

**Josef Teichmann** (\* 27 August 1972 in [Lienz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lienz)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) mathematician and professor at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) working on [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), stochastic analysis and machine learning.

:::

After studying mathematics at the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz), he pursued his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). The title of his dissertation in 1999 under the supervision of [Peter W. Michor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_W._Michor) was "The Theory of Infinite-Dimensional [Lie Groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_Group) from the Point of View of [Functional Analysis](wikipedia://en/11180)".

After working at the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology), he obtained the [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 2002. Since June 2009 he has been a professor at the Department of Mathematics at ETH Zürich. Since August 2023 he has been chair of the Department of Mathematics at ETH Zürich.

In 2005 he was awarded the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384) and in 2006 the [Start-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-Preis) of the [FWF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund).

In 2014 he was awarded the [Louis Bachelier Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bachelier_Prize) by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).
