# Pavel Gevorgyan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pavel Gevorgyan"}

**Pavel Georgyan** (born 8 April 1963 in [Azokh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azokh), [Hadrut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrut_Region) region, [Nagorno-Karabakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR)) is a professor, doctor of science in physics and mathematics, corresponding member of Russian Academy of Natural Sciences, Head of Department of Mathematical Analysis\][^o3][^o4] of [Moscow State Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_Pedagogical_University).

:::

Gevorgyan was awarded with the Russian Federation Government Prize in Education (2014).[^o5]

He is an Honorary Worker of higher professional education of [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation).

## Family {#Family}

Gevorgyan is married and has two children.

## Education {#Education}

- In 1980 entered [Yerevan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_State_University), Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics
- In 1984 became the winner of Mathematical Olympiad for high school students ([Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia))
- In 1984 he transferred to MSU [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), Department of higher geometry and topology
- 1985–1989: Postgraduate in [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), Department of higher geometry and topology
- 1989: Candidate of Science (equivalent of [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.)) in Physics and Mathematics.

Dissertation: “Equivariant movability” (under the supervision of professor [Yu.M.Smirnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smirnov_Yu.M.)).

- 2001: Doctor of Science in Physics and Mathematics

Dissertation: “Generalized [shape theory](wikipedia://en/18767352) and movability of continuous [transformation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_groups)”.

## Research area {#Research_area}

Topological transformation groups. Equivariant topology. Shape theory.

## Career and present positions {#Career_and_present_positions}

- 1993–1996: Dean of Faculty of Natural Sciences, 1994–1996: Head of Department of Higher Mathematics, 1996–2000: Rector of [Artsakh State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artsakh_State_University) (in [Nagorno-Karabakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh))
- 2000-2015: Professor of Department of Higher Mathematics of [Moscow Power Engineering Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Power_Engineering_Institute)
- 2008-2016: Head of Department of Higher and Applied Mathematics of [Academy of Labour and Social Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Labour_and_Social_Relations)
- 2015-2016: Vice-rector of [Moscow State Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_Pedagogical_University)
- Since 2015: Head of [Department of Mathematical Analysis](http://matanaliz.mpgu.edu/index.php/2012-04-07-22-24-42) of [Moscow State Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_Pedagogical_University)
- Since 2005: Member of Scientific-Methodological Council on mathematics of [Ministry of Education and Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_and_Science_%28Russia%29) of [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation)
- 2008: Corresponding member of Russian Academy of Natural Sciences
- 2012: Honorary Worker of Higher Professional Education of [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation)
