# Steffen Lauritzen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Steffen Lauritzen"}

**Steffen Lilholt Lauritzen** FRS (born 22 April 1947)[^o3] is former Head of the Department of Statistics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and Fellow of [Jesus College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Oxford), and currently emeritus Professor of Statistics at the University of Copenhagen. He is a leading proponent of [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) and [graphical models](wikipedia://en/447298).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lauritzen studied statistics at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), Denmark, completing the degree of Candidatus statisticae (M.Sc. level) in 1972 and Licentiatus statisticae (PhD level) in 1975. He was appointed there as lecturer of Statistics and remained until 1981. He continued as Professor of Mathematics and Statistics at [Aalborg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_University), Denmark, from 1981 to 2004. From 2004 to 2014 he was Professor of Statistics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford),[^o6] from 2014 to 2021, he was Professor of Statistics at the University of Copenhagen, and since 2021, he is emeritus Professor of Statistics there.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Lauritzen was elected a member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1984, and a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2011.[^o8]

Lauritzen was awarded the 1996 [Guy Medal in Silver](wikipedia://en/6752269) by the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664). He served, among others, as Editor-in-Chief of the *Scandinavian Journal of Statistics* from 1998 to 2000.

Lauritzen's book *Probabilistic Networks and Expert Systems* (1999, [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag)), written jointly with Robert G. Cowell, [Philip Dawid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Dawid), and [David Spiegelhalter](wikipedia://en/2803355), received the 2001 DeGroot Prize from the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312).[^o9]
