# Gideon J. Mellenbergh

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Gideon J. Mellenbergh"}

**Gideon Jan (Don) Mellenbergh** (9 August 1938 – 27 March 2021) was a Dutch [psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist), who was Professor of [Psychological methods](wikipedia://en/37384857) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), known for his contribution in the field of [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982),[^o4][^o5] and [Social Research Methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_research#Methodology).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born and raised in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), Mellenbergh received his MA in psychology from the University of Amsterdam in 1965, and his PhD in psychology in 1971 under the supervision of Professor [Adriaan de Groot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriaan_de_Groot) with a dissertation about [educational tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_%28assessment%29), entitled "Studies in studietoetsen" (Studies on educational tests).[^o7]

At the University of Amsterdam, Mellenbergh began his academic career as an assistant professor of psychological methods in 1965. Late 1969 he was visiting research assistant at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), studying educational methods. From 1975 to 1978 he was Associate professor [Psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982) at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht), and then returned to the University of Amsterdam where he was Professor, Psychological Methods from 1982 to 2003. Among his PhD students were [Henk Koppelaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henk_Koppelaar) and [Denny Borsboom](wikipedia://en/55870994). In 1991 and 1993 he had been Visiting professor at the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago_de_Compostela), Spain.[^o7]

Mellenbergh performed many administrative functions in Dutch research institutes, is member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (KNAW) since 1993,[^o8] the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500), the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association) (APA), the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (ISI), and the Interuniversity Graduate School of Psychometrics and Sociometrics (IOPS). And Mellenbergh was editor at [Psychometrika](wikipedia://en/22991646), on the editorial Board of *Psychological Methods* and member of the editorial council of *[Psicothema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psicothema)*.[^o7]

He died on 27 March 2021.[^o9]
