# Journal of Mathematics and the Arts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Mathematics and the Arts"}

The ***Journal of Mathematics and the Arts*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that deals with relationship between [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [the arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_arts).[^o3]

:::

The journal was established in 2007 and is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Mara Alagic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mara_Alagic) ([Wichita State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wichita_State_University), Kansas).
