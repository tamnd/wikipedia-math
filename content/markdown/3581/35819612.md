# Osculant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Osculant"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), the **osculant** or **tacinvariant** or **tact invariant** is an invariant of a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) that vanishes if the hypersurface touches itself, or an invariant of several hypersurfaces that [osculate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osculation), meaning that they have a common point where they meet to unusually high order.

:::
