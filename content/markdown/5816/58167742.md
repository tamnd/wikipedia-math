# Jeanne N. Clelland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeanne N. Clelland"}

**Jeanne A. Nielsen Clelland** (born 1970)[^o2] is an American mathematician specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and its applications to [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder), and the author of a textbook on [moving frames](wikipedia://en/439082), *From Frenet to Cartan: The Method of Moving Frames* (Graduate Studies in Mathematics 178, American Mathematical Society, 2017).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Clelland graduated from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 1991, and stayed at Duke for her graduate studies, completing her doctorate there in 1996. Her dissertation, *Geometry of Conservation Laws for a Class of Parabolic Partial Differential Equations*, was supervised by [Robert Bryant](wikipedia://en/24204031).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Clelland was awarded the [Alice T. Schafer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181252) from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in 1991. She is also the 2018 winner of the Burton W. Jones Distinguished Teaching Award, from the Rocky Mountain Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).
