# Diane Maclagan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diane Maclagan"}

**Diane Margaret Maclagan** (born 1974)[^o3] is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o4] She is a researcher in combinatorial and computational [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), with an emphasis on [toric varieties](wikipedia://en/737087), [Hilbert schemes](wikipedia://en/6612581), and [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

As a student at [Burnside High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnside_High_School) in [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch), New Zealand, Maclagan competed in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1990 and 1991, earning a bronze medal in 1991.[^o6][^o7] As an undergraduate, she studied at the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury), graduating in 1995.[^o8] She did her PhD at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), graduating in 2000. Her dissertation, *Structures on Sets of Monomial Ideals*, was supervised by [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels).[^o8]

After postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Maclagan was a Szegő Assistant Professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 2001 to 2004, an assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) from 2004 to 2007, then an associate professor there from 2007 to 2009. She moved to her present position at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) in 2007.[^o8]

## Books {#Books}

With Bernd Sturmfels, Maclagan is the author of the book *[Introduction to Tropical Geometry](wikipedia://en/63270243)*.[^o11] With [Rekha R. Thomas](wikipedia://en/44907501), Sara Faridi, Leah Gold, A. V. Jayanthan, Amit Khetan, and Tony Puthenpurakal, she is the author of *Computational Algebra and Combinatorics of Toric Ideals*.[^o12]
