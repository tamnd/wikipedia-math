# Isomorphism problem of Coxeter groups

::: {#o1 .problem cue=unsolved}

Given two Coxeter groups $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$, decide whether $W(\Gamma_1)\simeq{W(\Gamma_2)}$.

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Isomorphism problem of Coxeter groups"}

It is an unresolved problem in the mathematical field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) to determine whether or not two [Coxeter groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_groups) (specified by their [Coxeter diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram)) are [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) as [abstract groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_group). Equivalently, the problem asks to determine, for a given Coxeter group $W$, the possible subsets $S$ of $W$ that are Coxeter generating sets for $W$ (that is, for which $(W , S)$ is a Coxeter system).

:::

A slight generalization of the problem can be made by asking to find to all isomorphisms from one group onto the other.[^o3] In 2022, Yuri Santos Rego and Petra Schwer introduced a new framework to deal with the problem (a finite dimensional, locally finite, ranked [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686) to capture isomorphisms between finite rank Coxeter systems) and asked more related open questions motivated by it.[^o4]
