# Henry Cabourn Pocklington

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry Cabourn Pocklington"}

**Henry Cabourn Pocklington** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (28 January 1870, [Exeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter) – 15 May 1952, [Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds)) was an [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His primary profession was as a [schoolmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoolmaster), but he made important contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) with the discovery of [Pocklington's primality test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocklington_primality_test) in 1914[^o2] and the invention of [Pocklington's algorithm](wikipedia://en/26174604). He also derived the first equation for the current in a wire [antenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antenna_%28radio%29), Pocklington's integral equation.[^o3][^o4]
