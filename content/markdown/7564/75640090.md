# List of largest video game companies by revenue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of largest video game companies by revenue"}

This is a listing of top 50 largest video game [publishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_publisher) and [developers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_developer) ranked by reported revenue. [Tencent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent#Video_games) is the world's largest video game company, followed by [Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Interactive_Entertainment), [Xbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_%28division%29), [Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo), and [NetEase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NetEase).[^o2]

:::

Of the 50 largest video game companies, 9 are based in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), 9 in [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), 9 in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China), 7 in [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea), and 14 in other countries: 3 from [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), 2 from [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel), 2 from [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland), 2 from [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore), and 1 each from the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia), and [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland).

This list primarily includes [public companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_company) or those whose revenues are known. Certain major privately held companies not included because they do not publicly disclose detailed financial statements or revenue reports.
