# Tilak Raj Prabhakar

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tilak Raj Prabhakar"}

**Tilak Raj Prabhakar** was an Indian mathematician specializing in [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) and [integral equations](wikipedia://en/474234). In a paper published in 1971, he introduced a three-parameter extension of the [Mittag-Leffler function](wikipedia://en/1603459).[^o3] Subsequently, this function was found to have applications in various areas of mathematics like [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939) and also in certain areas of physics. The function has since been named "[Prabhakar function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prabhakar_function)" in his honor.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Prabhakar received his M.A. and B.A. degrees from the [Benaras Hindu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benaras_Hindu_University) (India) and obtained his PhD. from the [University of Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delhi) in 1970 with his thesis on "Integral Equations and Special Functions".

## Career {#Career}

Initially, he taught at the S.D. College in [Muzzaffarnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muzaffargarh). Then he joined the [Multani Mal Modi College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multani_Mal_Modi_College) in [Modinagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modinagar) where he became Head of the Department of Mathematics and later he moved to the Ramjas College of the University of Delhi. He was professor of mathematics at the National Institute of Electricity and Electronics in [Boomerdès, Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boumerdès) when he died in 1982.[^o8][^o9]
