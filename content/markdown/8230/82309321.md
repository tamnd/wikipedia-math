# Philip Bond (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Bond"}

**Philip Bond** is a British researcher recognized for leading an independent review on knowledge exchange in the mathematical sciences for UKRI – [UK Research and Innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Research_and_Innovation). His 2018 report, 'The era of mathematics'[^o2] highlights the critical, increasing role of mathematical modelling and data analysis across various UK industrial sectors and led to the founding of a new [national academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_academy) for [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/82295923).[^o3]

:::

Bond is a professor of creativity and innovation at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), a visiting professor in the Departments of Engineering Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Bristol, and a visiting fellow in the Oxford Industrial and Applied Mathematics Centre.[^o4]

In 2020, Bond was elected a fellow of the [Royal Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Engineering).[^o5] In 2012 he led a UK government foresight report on financial trading[^o6] and in 2014 Bond was appointed to the UK [Council for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_for_Science_and_Technology) which advises the prime minister.[^o7]
