# Frederick J. Almgren Jr.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frederick J. Almgren Jr."}

**Frederick Justin Almgren Jr.** (July 3, 1933 – February 5, 1997) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694). He was born in Birmingham, Alabama.

:::

Almgren received a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1974. Between 1963 and 1992 he was a frequent visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton.[^o3]

Almgren wrote one of the longest papers in mathematics,[^o4] proving what is now called the [Almgren regularity theorem](wikipedia://en/33077295): the singular set of an *m*-dimensional mass-minimizing surface has dimension at most *m−2*. He also developed the concept of [varifold](wikipedia://en/6706053),[^o5] first defined by [L. C. Young](wikipedia://en/9620408) in (Young 1951),[^o7] and proposed them as [generalized solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_solution) to [Plateau's problem](wikipedia://en/338129) in order to deal with the problem even when a concept of [orientation](wikipedia://en/2544098) is missing. He played also an important role in the founding of *[The Geometry Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Geometry_Center)*.

Almgren was a student of [Herbert Federer](wikipedia://en/12185345), one of the founders of [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), and was the advisor and husband (as his second wife) of [Jean Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Taylor). His daughter, [Ann S. Almgren](wikipedia://en/48140876), is an applied mathematician who works on computational simulations in astrophysics. His son, [Robert F. Almgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Almgren), is an applied mathematician working on market microstructure and trade execution.

Almgren died in Princeton, New Jersey on February 5, 1997, aged 63.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Almgren, Frederick J. Jr. (1964). "The theory of varifolds: A variational calculus in the large for the k-dimensional area integrand". Institute for Advanced Study. A set of [mimeographed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimeograph) notes in which Frederick J. Almgren Jr. introduces the term "*varifold*" for the first time.
- Almgren, Frederick J. Jr. (1966). "Plateau's Problem: An Invitation to Varifold Geometry". W. A. Benjamin, Inc. p. XII+74. mr:0190856. zbl:0165.13201. The first widely circulated book describing the concept of a varifold and its applications to the [Plateau's problem](wikipedia://en/338129).

- Almgren, Frederick J. Jr. (2000). "Almgren's big regularity paper. Q-valued functions minimizing Dirichlet's integral and the regularity of area-minimizing rectifiable currents up to codimension 2". 1. World Scientific Publishing Co. Inc. isbn:978-981-02-4108-7. mr:1777737. zbl:0985.49001. <https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9810241089>.
- Almgren, Frederick J. Jr. (2001). "Plateau's Problem: An Invitation to Varifold Geometry". 13. American Mathematical Society. p. xvi, 78. isbn:978-0-8218-2747-5. mr:1853442. zbl:0995.49001. <https://books.google.com/books?id=Tk8zHPGVmBsC>. The second edition of the book (Almgren 1966).
