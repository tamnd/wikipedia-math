# Sarah-marie belcastro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sarah-marie belcastro"}

**Sarah-marie belcastro**[^o5] (born 1970), stylized sarah-marie belcastro, is an American mathematician and book author. She is an instructor at the Art of Problem Solving Online School[^o9] and is the director of **MathILy**,[^o10] a residential math summer program hosted at [Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College). Although her doctoral research was in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), she has also worked extensively in [topological graph theory](wikipedia://en/1631772).[^o2] She is known for and has written extensively about [mathematical knitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_knitting), and has co-edited three books on fiber mathematics.[^o3] She exclusively uses the form "sarah-marie belcastro".[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Belcastro was born in [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego), California, in 1970, and grew up mostly in [Andover, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andover,_Massachusetts), and in [Dubuque, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubuque,_Iowa).[^o7] She earned a B.S. (1991) in Mathematics and Astronomy from [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College), an M.S. (1993) from The [University of Michigan, Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan,_Ann_Arbor), and a Ph.D. (1997) there for a thesis on “Picard Lattices of Families of K3 Surfaces” done with [Igor Dolgachev](wikipedia://en/22777439).[^o8]

Since 2012, she has also been an instructor at the Art of Problem Solving Online School.[^o9] Since 2013, she has been the director of [Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College)'s residential summer program MathILy (serious **Math**ematics **I**nfused with **L**evit**y**).[^o10] She is also a guest faculty member at [Sarah Lawrence College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lawrence_College).

She was Associate Editor for *[The College Mathematics Journal](wikipedia://en/21340776)* (2003–2019). She has also lectured frequently at the [University of Massachusetts, Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts,_Amherst) since 2012.[^o11][^o12]
