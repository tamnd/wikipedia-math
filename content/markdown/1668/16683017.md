# Size homotopy group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Size homotopy group"}

The concept of **size homotopy group** is analogous in [size theory](wikipedia://en/22016239) of the classical concept of [homotopy group](wikipedia://en/297506). In order to give its definition, let us assume that a [size pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Size_pair) $(M,\varphi)$ is given, where $M$ is a [closed manifold](wikipedia://en/669475) of class $C^0\$ and $\varphi:M\to \mathbb{R}^k$ is a [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122). Consider the [lexicographical order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexicographical_order) $\preceq$ on $\mathbb{R}^k$ defined by setting $(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\preceq(y_1,\ldots,y_k)\$ if and only if $x_1 \le y_1,\ldots, x_k \le y_k$. For every $Y\in\mathbb{R}^k$ set $M_{Y}=\{Z\in\mathbb{R}^k:Z\preceq Y\}$.

:::

Assume that $P\in M_X\$ and $X\preceq Y\$. If $\alpha\$, $\beta\$ are two paths from $P\$ to $P\$ and a [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) from $\alpha\$ to $\beta\$, based at $P\$, exists in the [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $M_{Y}\$, then we write $\alpha \approx_{Y}\beta\$. The **first size homotopy group** of the [size pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Size_pair) $(M,\varphi)\$ computed at $(X,Y)\$ is defined to be the [quotient set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_set) of the set of all [paths](wikipedia://en/1189553) from $P\$ to $P\$ in $M_X\$ with respect to the [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259) $\approx_{Y}\$, endowed with the operation induced by the usual composition of based [loops](wikipedia://en/1189560).[^o2]

In other words, the **first size homotopy group** of the [size pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Size_pair) $(M,\varphi)\$ computed at $(X,Y)\$ and $P\$ is the image $h_{XY}(\pi_1(M_X,P))\$ of the first [homotopy group](wikipedia://en/297506) $\pi_1(M_X,P)\$ with base point $P\$ of the [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $M_X\$, when $h_{XY}\$ is the [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) induced by the inclusion of $M_X\$ in $M_Y\$.

The $n$-th size homotopy group is obtained by substituting the loops based at $P\$ with the [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) $\alpha:S^n\to M\$ taking a fixed point of $S^n\$ to $P\$, as happens when higher [homotopy groups](wikipedia://en/297506) are defined.
