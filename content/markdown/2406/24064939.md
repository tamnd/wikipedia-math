# Watkins snark

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Watkins snark"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Watkins snark** is a [snark](wikipedia://en/727811) with 50 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 75 [edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_%28graph_theory%29).[^o4] It was discovered by John J. Watkins in 1989.[^o5]

:::

As a snark, the Watkins graph is a connected, [bridgeless](wikipedia://en/1305071) [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) with [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) equal to 4. The Watkins snark is also [non-planar](wikipedia://en/24314), [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632),[^o6] and [non-hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph). It has book thickness 3 and queue number 2.[^o7]

Another well known snark on 50 vertices is the [Szekeres snark](wikipedia://en/4776914), the fifth known snark, discovered by [George Szekeres](wikipedia://en/2563194) in 1973.[^o8]
