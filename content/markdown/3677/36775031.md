# John Leigh Smeathman Hatton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Leigh Smeathman Hatton"}

**Professor John Leigh Smeathman** (27 May 1865 – 13 January 1933) was a mathematician and Principal of East London College, England, one of the founding colleges of what is now [Queen Mary College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_College), part of [London University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_University). He was also [Vice Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Chancellor) of London University in the 1930s.[^o2]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Smeathman was born in Street Aston, near [Rugby, Warwickshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby,_Warwickshire).[^o4] on 27 May 1865, the eldest son of Revd J L S Hatton, Rector of [West Barkwith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Barkwith), [Lincolnshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincolnshire).

He was educated at [Hertford College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertford_College,_Oxford) where he obtained an [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) graduating with first class honours in 1889 and second class honours in physics a year later.[^o4] He also later qualified as a [barrister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrister) at [Lincoln's Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln's_Inn).[^o2] He also studied geometry at Oxford as an undergraduate under [Savilian Professor of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry) [James Joseph Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861).

## Career {#Career}

He was Director of Evening Classes (1892–1896), later Director of Studies 1896–1908 and Principal, East London College (University of London), now Queen Mary College, 1896–1933. He was Deputy Vice-Chancellor of the University of London from 1930 to 1931 and Vice-Chancellor from 1932 to 1933. He was Dean of the Faculty of Science at the university from 1922 to 1926. He also served as a member of the [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex) and the [West Ham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham) Education Committees during the 1920s.[^o2]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1897 he married Pauline Carlyle, daughter of R J Henderson of [Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombo) and they had two sons.
