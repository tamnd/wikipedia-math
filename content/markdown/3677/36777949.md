# Sigma Zeta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sigma Zeta"}

**Sigma Zeta** (**ΣΖ**) is a North American [honor society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_society) founded in 1925 to recognize undergraduate excellence in the [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). The society's purpose is to encourage and foster the attainment of knowledge in the natural and computer sciences and mathematics.[^o3] It has chartered more than eighty chapters in the United States and Canada.

:::

## History {#History}

Sigma Zeta was founded in the fall of 1925 at the now defunct [Shurtleff College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shurtleff_College) in [Alton, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alton,_Illinois) as a local coeducational organization to provide recognition for Shurtleff science and mathematics students. Its founders were Professor Elmer E. List of the biology department, J. Ellis Powell of the math department; and Ralph K. Calteton, of the chemistry department.[^o5] They selected the name Sigma Zeta and drafted a constitution and initiation ceremony on October 1, 1925.[^o5]

A second chapter, *Beta*, was chartered at McKendree College in 1926.[^o5] The society held its first conclave in June 1926.[^o5]

Soon after that other local campuses took an interest in the group, and following the approval of the petition by [McKendree College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKendree_University) to start a chapter in June 1926 Sigma Zeta began its growth into a national collegiate honor society.[^o6] In a letter that appeared in the correspondence section of the [American Chemical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Chemical_Society) *[Journal of Chemical Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Chemical_Education)* Sigma Zeta was offered as an alternative for small colleges to the existing [Sigma Xi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Xi) honor society which often passed over small colleges for membership as they focused on larger Universities.[^o7]

Sigma Zeta's annual convention has been held every year since 1926 except for 1943, 1944, and 1945 when it was canceled due to [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), and in 2020 when it was canceled due to the COVID-19 Pandemic.[^o8] The first three gatherings were held at the Shurtleff College campus which is now the site of the [Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Illinois_University_School_of_Dental_Medicine).

Sigma Zeta became an international organization in 2024 with the chartering of a *Gamma Phi chapter* at [St. Mary’s University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Mary's_University_%28Calgary%29) in [Calgary, Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary,_Alberta), Canada.[^o9]

## Symbols {#Symbols}

Sigma Zeta's badge is a gold key, shaped like an open book, with the Greek letters **ΣΖ** above various scientific symbols.[^o5] The associate member's pin is blue and white enamel, with the Greek letters **ΣΖ**.[^o5]

The colors of Sigma Zeta are blue and white.[^o5] Its publication is *The Sigma Zetan*.[^o11][^o5]

## Activities {#Activities}

The annual convention is Sigma Zeta's primary meeting where student members present papers and individual and chapter awards are presented.[^o13][^o14][^o15] Individual chapters often undertake activities including the hosting of speakers and service projects to benefit their local communities.[^o16] These activities can include programs for younger students at local schools helping to promote science education at early ages.[^o17][^o18]

In 1947, the three Sigma Zeta members presented the Founders Cup which is awarded to a chapter at each annual convention for papers presented.[^o5]

Sigma Zeta's interdisciplinary nature has been described as a benefit for smaller colleges and universities because, "It brings together students from all areas of science and mathematics, including computer science, so they can all work together on projects."[^o19]

## Membership {#Membership}

Chapters select students for membership who have met the following eligibility criteria:

- Major studies in at least one of the Natural Sciences, Computer Science, or Mathematics
- Completion of 25 semester hours of coursework with 15 hours in the Natural Sciences, Computer Science, or Mathematics
- A [Grade Point Average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grading_in_education) of 3.0 out of 4.0 both in Science and Mathematics as well as cumulatively among all classes taken[^o21]

## Chapters {#Chapters}

As of 2026, Sigma Zeta has chartered more than eighty chapters in the United States and Canada.[^o23]
