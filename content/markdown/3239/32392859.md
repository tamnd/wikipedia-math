# Murray R. Spiegel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Murray R. Spiegel"}

**Murray Ralph Spiegel** (October 20, 1923 – April 28, 1991) was an author of [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), including titles in a collection of [Schaum's Outlines](wikipedia://en/9597816).[^o3]

:::

Spiegel was a native of [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn) and a graduate of [New Utrecht High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Utrecht_High_School). He received his bachelor's degree in mathematics and physics from [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College) in 1943. He earned a master's degree in 1947 and doctorate in 1949, both in mathematics and both at Cornell University. His PhD dissertation, supervised by [Marc Kac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Kac),[^o4] was titled *On the Random Vibrations of Harmonically Bound Particles in a Viscous Medium.*

He was a teaching fellow at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1943–1945, a consultant with [Monsanto Chemical Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsanto_Chemical_Company) in the summer of 1946, and a teaching fellow at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) from 1946 to 1949. He was a consultant in geophysics for Beers & Heroy in 1950, and a consultant in aerodynamics for [Wright Air Development Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright_Air_Development_Center) from 1950 to 1954.

Spiegel joined the faculty of [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) in 1949 as an assistant professor. He became an associate professor in 1954 and a full professor in 1957. He was assigned to the faculty [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute of Hartford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_at_Hartford), CT, when that branch was organized in 1955, where he served as chair of the mathematics department.[^o6]
