# Cornelis Willem Opzoomer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cornelis Willem Opzoomer"}

*Image: Portret van Cornelis Willem Opzoomer, RP-P-OB-59.557.jpg.* Portrait of Cornelis Willem Opzoomer by [Dirk Jurriaan Sluyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Jurriaan_Sluyter)

**Cornelis Willem Opzoomer** (20 September 1821, [Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam) – 23 August 1892, [Oosterbeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oosterbeek)) was a Dutch jurist, [positivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivism) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [theologian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology). He was professor of philosophy at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) from 1846 to 1889.

:::

In 1856 he became member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o2]
