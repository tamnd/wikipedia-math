# Walther von Dyck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walther von Dyck"}

**Walther Franz Anton von Dyck** (6 December 1856 – 5 November 1934), born **Dyck** ([^o4]) and later [ennobled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von), was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is credited with being the first to define a mathematical [group](wikipedia://en/19447), in the modern sense in (Dyck 1882). He laid the foundations of [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834),[^o6] being the first to systematically study a group by [generators and relations](wikipedia://en/99494).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

*Image: Grabstaette Walther von Dyck.jpg.* Bust of Walther von Dyck at his grave in Munich

Von Dyck was a student of [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887)[^o6] and served as chairman of the commission publishing [Klein's encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klein's_encyclopedia). Von Dyck was also the editor of [Kepler's](wikipedia://en/15736) works. He promoted technological education as rector of the [Technische Hochschule of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule_of_Munich).[^o8] He was a Plenary Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1908 at Rome.[^o9]

Von Dyck is the son of the Bavarian painter [Hermann Dyck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Dyck).

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The [Dyck language](wikipedia://en/1852078) in formal language theory is named after him,[^o11] as are [Dyck's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyck's_theorem) and [Dyck's surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyck's_surface) in the theory of [surfaces](wikipedia://en/4342970), together with the [von Dyck groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Dyck_group), the [Dyck tessellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyck_tessellation), [Dyck paths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyck_path), and the [Dyck graph](wikipedia://en/24041180).
