# John Stillwell

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Stillwell"}

**John Colin Stillwell** (born 1942) is an Australian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) on the faculties of the [University of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Francisco) and [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Melbourne, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne,_Australia) and lived there until he went to the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) for his doctorate.[^o3] He received his PhD from MIT in 1970, working under [Hartley Rogers, Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartley_Rogers,_Jr),[^o5] who had himself worked under [Alonzo Church](wikipedia://en/49731).[^o6] From 1970 until 2001, he taught at Monash University back in Australia and in 2002 began teaching in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco).[^o3]

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2005, Stillwell was the recipient of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s prestigious [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) for his article "The Story of the [120-Cell](wikipedia://en/717358),"[^o8] [Notices of the AMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notices_of_the_AMS), January 2001, pp. 17–24.[^o9] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
