# Communications Machine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Communications Machine"}

The *Communications Machine*, or "CM", is a mechanical device used by the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) for most of the 1920s. It used a sliding alphabet system. It was developed by [Agnes Meyer Driscoll](wikipedia://en/477556) and William Gresham. In 1937 the United States Congress recognized the achievement of these two by awarding them both \$15,000.[^o2]
