# Hans-Joachim Nastold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hans-Joachim Nastold"}

**Hans-Joachim Nastold** (13 July 1929 – 26 January 2004) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who made notable contributions to [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

Born in [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart), Nastold earned his [Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur) in Göppingen.[^o3] He attended the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg), earning his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1957, under supervision of [Friedrich Karl Schmidt](wikipedia://en/36084772).
