# Nigel Boston

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nigel Boston"}

**Nigel Boston** (July 20, 1961 – March 31, 2024) was a British-American mathematician, who made notable contributions to [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Boston attended [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), earning his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1987, under supervision of [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679).[^o5] He was a Professor [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

Boston died on March 31, 2024, at the age of 62.[^o7]
