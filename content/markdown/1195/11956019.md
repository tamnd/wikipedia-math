# Blaschke selection theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Blaschke selection theorem"}

The **Blaschke selection theorem** is a result in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) about [sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) of [convex sets](wikipedia://en/6292). Specifically, given a sequence $\{K_n\}$ of convex sets contained in a [bounded set](wikipedia://en/48258), the theorem guarantees the existence of a subsequence $\{K_{n_m}\}$ and a convex set $K$ such that $K_{n_m}$ converges to $K$ in the [Hausdorff metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausdorff_metric). The theorem is named for [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998).

:::

## Alternate statements {#Alternate_statements}

- A succinct statement of the theorem is that the [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018) of convex bodies is [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact).
- Using the [Hausdorff metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausdorff_metric) on sets, every infinite collection of compact subsets of the unit ball has a limit point (and that limit point is itself a [compact set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_set)).

::: {#o4 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

As an example of its use, the [isoperimetric problem](wikipedia://en/326182) can be shown to have a solution.[^o5] That is, there exists a curve of fixed length that encloses the maximum area possible. Other problems likewise can be shown to have a solution:

- [Lebesgue's universal covering problem](wikipedia://en/52153555) for a convex universal cover of minimal size for the collection of all sets in the plane of unit diameter,[^o5]
- the maximum inclusion problem,[^o5]
- and the [Moser's worm problem](wikipedia://en/30774748) for a convex universal cover of minimal size for the collection of planar curves of unit length.[^o6]
