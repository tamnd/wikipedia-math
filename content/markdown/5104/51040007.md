# Robert Pollock Gillespie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Pollock Gillespie"}

**Robert Pollock Gillespie** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (1903–1977) was a Scottish mathematician. He was twice President of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society (1946–7 and 1968–9). He published several important books on mathematics.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 21 November 1903 in [Johnstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnstone), [Renfrewshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renfrewshire) the son of Thomas Gillespie a butcher and his wife, Jane Pollock. He was raised at Ashcot on Kilbarchan Road in Johnstone. He was educated locally then at [Paisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paisley,_Renfrewshire) Grammar School where he was [dux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dux). He then won a bursary to study Mathematics and Natural Philosophy (Physics) at [Glasgow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_University) graduating MA BSc in 1924. He did further postgraduate studies under [E. W. Hobson](wikipedia://en/1077078) at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) from 1924 to 1927 under a William Bryce Scholarship, gaining his doctorate (PhD) in 1932 due to a delay in submitting his thesis.[^o3]

He began lecturing in Mathematics at [Glasgow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_University) in 1929 under Prof [Thomas Murray MacRobert](wikipedia://en/27128722) and alongside Dr T S Graham.[^o4] In 1933 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) due to his numerous publications on mathematics. His proposers were [Thomas Murray MacRobert](wikipedia://en/27128722), [Neil M'Arthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_M'Arthur), [Richard Alexander Robb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Alexander_Robb) and [William Arthur](wikipedia://en/34359559).[^o5]

In the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) he served first in the [Clyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Clyde) River Patrol and in 1941 moved to the Air Traffic Control division of the [RAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF) based at [Prestwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_Prestwick_Airport). He served on the University Air Squadron after the war.[^o6]

In 1948 he was promoted to Senior Lecturer at Glasgow and remained there until retiral in 1969. On retiral he moved to [Edzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edzell) and indulged his love as an amateur artist.

He died in [Edzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edzell) on 1 January 1977.

## Family {#Family}

He was married to Maisie Bowman, daughter of Prof A. A. Bowman. Their son Alistair Gillespie was also a mathematician.

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Integration* (1939)
- *Partial Differentiation* (1951)
- *Solving Problems in Advanced Calculus* (1972)
