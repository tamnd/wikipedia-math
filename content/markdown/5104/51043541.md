# Grace Y. Yi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Grace Y. Yi"}

**Grace Yun Yi** is a professor of the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario) where she currently holds a Tier I [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in [Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_Science).[^o3][^o4] She was a professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), Canada, where she holds a University Research Chair in Statistical and Actuarial Science. Her research concerns [event history analysis](wikipedia://en/419259) with [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676) and its applications in medicine, engineering, and social science.[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Yi earned bachelor's and master's degrees from [Sichuan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_University), China, in 1986 and 1989. She moved to Canada and continued her studies with another master's degree from [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University) in 1996, and a Ph.D. from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 2000 under the supervision of [Donald A. S. Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_A._S._Fraser).[^o5] She came to Waterloo as a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher), and then continued there as an assistant professor in 2001.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Yi won the CRM–SSC Prize of the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) in 2010.[^o9] Her work with Xianming Tan and Runze Li won the Canadian Journal of Statistics Award for 2016.[^o10]

In 2015 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for research excellence in developing statistical theory and methods, particularly in missing and mis-measured data; for important contributions to [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878); and for excellence in statistical education and mentoring students."[^o11][^o12] She is also a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o13] She was elected chair of the ASA Lifetime Data Science Section in 2021.[^o14]
