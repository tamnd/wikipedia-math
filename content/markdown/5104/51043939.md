# Richard Eliot Chamberlin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Eliot Chamberlin"}

**Richard Eliot Chamberlin** (March 20, 1923 – March 14, 1994)[^o2] was an American mathematician, specializing in [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chamberlin was born on March 20, 1923, in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts). His father was [Ralph Vary Chamberlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Vary_Chamberlin). Eliot Chamberlin attended [East High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_High_School_%28Salt_Lake_City%29) in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City).[^o2] He received his bachelor's degree from the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah). In the early 1940s, he was a teaching fellow in physics at the University of Utah and then the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). After serving as an instructor of physics at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University), he served two years in the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II). After discharge from the Navy, he entered graduate school in mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and received his Ph.D. in 1950 with thesis supervisor [Hassler Whitney](wikipedia://en/391794).

Chamberlin joined the faculty of the mathematics department at the University of Utah in 1949 and retired there as professor emeritus on July 1, 1988.[^o5] Chamberlin gave an [invited address](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1950 in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
