# James Beckett (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Beckett"}

**James Beckett III** (born May 10, 1949) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), [author](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Author), [editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editing), and [publisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publisher). His publications are well known in the hobby of [sports card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_card) collecting. Beckett earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University) in 1975 and then joined the faculty of [Bowling Green State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green_State_University) as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor).

:::

## Sports card industry {#Sports_card_industry}

While at Bowling Green, Beckett began preparing [baseball card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_card) price guides, which he offered free upon request.

Beckett price guides, first published in 1979, rely upon information from sellers throughout the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), who supply information on customer interest and sales of products. Price guides typically carry two value labels, one based upon a high value, the other denoting low values. As the condition of collectibles is important in ascertaining their value, Beckett price guides also typically include a series of definitions for estimating condition.

In November 1984, Beckett began publishing *[Beckett Baseball Card Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_Media#Magazines)*. (Rival publication *[Tuff Stuff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuff_Stuff)* was also founded that year.) *Beckett Baseball Card Monthly* grew in popularity and became the basis for the success of [Beckett Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_Media), now based in [Dallas, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas,_Texas). Beckett Publications produces price guides for a variety of sports collectibles (Beckett's [Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_football), [Basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball), and [Hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey) guides would start in the early 1990s, with Beckett's monthly Racing Guide following in 1996). Market values for non-sports card collectibles such as [Pokémon Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokémon_Trading_Card_Game) and related products are also tracked. Beckett retains a position as Senior Advisor for Beckett Media, but is no longer Editor/Publisher as he was during the 1980s and 1990s.[^o3]

The publishing company became the leading publisher of sports and entertainment market collectible guides and was acquired by [Apprise Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apprise_Media) in 2005.[^o3][^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Beckett has three children from his first marriage. He is married to the former Diane Burgdorf, daughter of a Dallas car dealer and ex-wife of Sir [Mark Thatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Thatcher).[^o6] From time to time, Beckett attends some of the most prominent sports card and collectibles conventions held in various U.S. cities and is considered a celebrity within the industry.
