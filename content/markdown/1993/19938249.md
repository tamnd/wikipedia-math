# List of fields of application of statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of fields of application of statistics"}

**[Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)** is the mathematical science involving the collection, analysis and interpretation of data. A number of specialties have evolved to apply statistical and methods to various disciplines. Certain topics have "statistical" in their name but relate to manipulations of probability distributions rather than to statistical analysis.

:::

::: {#o2 .entry cue=list-entry name="Actuarial science"}

- **[Actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373)** is the discipline that applies mathematical and statistical methods to assess risk in the [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance) and [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) industries.

:::

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Astrostatistics"}

- **[Astrostatistics](wikipedia://en/37815827)** is the discipline that applies statistical analysis to the understanding of astronomical data.

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Biostatistics"}

- **[Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878)** is a branch of [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) that studies biological phenomena and observations by means of statistical analysis, and includes [medical statistics](wikipedia://en/11082896).

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Business analytics"}

- **[Business analytics](wikipedia://en/10147369)** is a rapidly developing business process that applies statistical methods to data sets (often very large) to develop new insights and understanding of business performance & opportunities

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Chemometrics"}

- **[Chemometrics](wikipedia://en/314204)** is the science of relating measurements made on a [chemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) system or process to the state of the system via application of mathematical or statistical methods.

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Demography"}

- **[Demography](wikipedia://en/45642)** is the statistical study of all [populations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_%28human_biology%29). It can be a very general science that can be applied to any kind of dynamic population, that is, one that changes over time or space.

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Econometrics"}

- **[Econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390)** is a branch of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) that applies statistical methods to the empirical study of economic theories and relationships.

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Environmental statistics"}

- **[Environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339)** is the application of statistical methods to [environmental science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_science). Weather, climate, air and water quality are included, as are studies of plant and animal populations.

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Epidemiology"}

- **[Epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997)** is the study of factors affecting the health and illness of populations, and serves as the foundation and logic of interventions made in the interest of public health and preventive medicine.

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Forensic statistics"}

- **[Forensic statistics](wikipedia://en/40081829)** is the application of probability models and statistical techniques to scientific evidence, such as DNA evidence, and the law. In contrast to "everyday" statistics, to not engender bias or unduly draw conclusions, forensic statisticians report likelihoods as likelihood ratios (LR).

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Spatial statistics"}

- **[Spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349)** is a branch of applied statistics that deals with the analysis of spatial data

:::

  - **[Geostatistics](wikipedia://en/51531)** is a branch of [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) that deals with the analysis of data from disciplines such as [petroleum geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_geology), [hydrogeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogeology), [hydrology](wikipedia://en/13435), [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904), [oceanography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanography), [geochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geochemistry), [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography).

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Jurimetrics"}

- **[Jurimetrics](wikipedia://en/45327439)** is the application of probability and statistics to [law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law).

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Machine learning"}

- **[Machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488)** is the subfield of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) that formulates algorithms in order to make predictions from data.

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Operations research"}

- **[Operations research](wikipedia://en/43476)** (or operational research) is an interdisciplinary branch of applied mathematics and formal science that uses methods such as mathematical modeling, statistics, and algorithms to arrive at optimal or near optimal solutions to complex problems; [Management science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_science) focuses on problems in the business world.

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Population ecology"}

- **[Population ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_ecology)** is a sub-field of [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) that deals with the dynamics of species populations and how these populations interact with the [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_%28biophysical%29).

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Psychometrics"}

- **[Psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982)** is the theory and technique of educational and psychological measurement of knowledge, abilities, attitudes, and personality traits.

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Quality control"}

- **[Quality control](wikipedia://en/41613)** reviews the factors involved in manufacturing and production; it can make use of [statistical sampling](wikipedia://en/160361) of product items to aid decisions in [process control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_control) or in accepting deliveries.

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Quantitative psychology"}

- **[Quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624)** is the science of statistically explaining and changing mental processes and behaviors in humans.

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Reliability engineering"}

- **[Reliability engineering](wikipedia://en/1724836)** is the study of the ability of a system or component to perform its required functions under stated conditions for a specified period of time.

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="Social statistics"}

- **[Social statistics](wikipedia://en/285943)** is the use of statistical measurement systems to study human behavior in a social environment.

:::

::: {#o22 .entry cue=list-entry name="Statistical finance"}

- **[Statistical finance](wikipedia://en/7458786)**, an area of [econophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econophysics), is an empirical attempt to shift finance from its [normative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normative_economics) roots to a [positivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivist) framework using exemplars from statistical physics with an emphasis on emergent or collective properties of financial markets.

:::

::: {#o23 .entry cue=list-entry name="Statistical mechanics"}

- **[Statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481)** is the application of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), which includes mathematical tools for dealing with large populations, to the field of [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), which is concerned with the motion of particles or objects when subjected to a force.

:::

::: {#o24 .entry cue=list-entry name="Statistical physics"}

- **[Statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics)** is one of the fundamental theories of [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and uses methods of probability theory in solving physical problems.

:::

::: {#o25 .entry cue=list-entry name="Statistical signal processing"}

- **[Statistical signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_signal_processing)** utilizes the statistical properties of [signals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal) to perform signal processing tasks.

:::

::: {#o26 .entry cue=list-entry name="Statistical thermodynamics"}

- **[Statistical thermodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_thermodynamics)** is the study of the microscopic behaviors of [thermodynamic systems](wikipedia://en/425850) using probability theory and provides a molecular level interpretation of thermodynamic quantities such as [work](wikipedia://en/3616613), [heat](wikipedia://en/19593167), [free energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic_free_energy), and [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891).

:::
