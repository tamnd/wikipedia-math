# Heinrich Burkhardt

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Burkhardt"}

**Heinrich Friedrich Karl Ludwig Burkhardt** (15 October 1861 – 2 November 1914) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He famously was one of the two examiners of [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s [PhD thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD_thesis) *Eine neue Bestimmung der Moleküldimensionen*.[^o3] Of Einstein's thesis he stated: "The mode of treatment demonstrates fundamental mastery of the relevant mathematical methods" and "What I checked, I found to be correct without exception."[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Burkhardt was born in [Schweinfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schweinfurt). Starting from 1879 he studied under [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459), [Alexander von Brill](wikipedia://en/28565454), and [Hermann Amandus Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Amandus_Schwarz) at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München), at the [Technische Hochschule München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule_München), at the [Friedrich Wilhelm University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wilhelm_University_of_Berlin), and at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He attained a doctorate in 1886 at the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München under [Gustav Conrad Bauer](wikipedia://en/53243976) with a thesis entitled: *Beziehungen zwischen der Invariantentheorie und der Theorie algebraischer Integrale und ihrer Umkehrungen* (Relations between the invariant theory and the theory of algebraic integrals and their inverses).

In 1887, he was an assistant at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 1889. Later, he was a professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) from 1897 to 1908 and the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München from October 1908. He worked on the theory of the [elliptical functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptical_function), [series expansions](wikipedia://en/1575813), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the [Burkhardt quartic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkhardt_quartic), and history of mathematics.

He died in Neuwittelsbach/[München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/München), of a disease of the stomach, diagnosed around Easter 1914.
