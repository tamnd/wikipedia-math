# Amber L. Puha

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amber L. Puha"}

**Amber Lynn Puha** is an American mathematician and educator at [California State University San Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University_San_Marcos). Her research concerns probability theory and stochastic processes.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

She earned a B.A. in mathematics at [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) (UCSD) in 1993 and a Ph.D. in mathematics at [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA) under the supervision of [Thomas M. Liggett](wikipedia://en/23204656) in 1998.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

She joined [California State University San Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University_San_Marcos) (CSUSM) in 1999. She was appointed an associate professor in 2005 and a full professor in 2010. She has been the chair of the Department of Mathematics at CSUSM since 2021.[^o7]

Her research has been focused on the modeling and analysis of stochastic networks.[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19] Jointly with H. Christian Gromoll and [Ruth J. Williams](wikipedia://en/44832164), she developed fluid ([law of large numbers](wikipedia://en/157055)) and diffusion ([central limit theorem](wikipedia://en/39406)) approximations for processor sharing queues with renewal arrivals and iid service requirements.[^o20][^o21] By removing the Poisson arrival and the exponential service assumptions in previous literatures, this work advanced the state of art of applied probability and has board applications, "from many-server parallel queues that model call centers to bandwidth sharing communications networks that model the Internet."[^o22]

### Professional services {#Professional_services}

From 2009 to 2011, she served as an associate director at the [Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/12023558), a federally funded mathematics research institute on the UCLA campus. From 2013 to 2015, she served a three-year elected term on the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Council, the main governing body of the society.[^o24] From 2016 to 2019, she served on the [INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) Applied Probability Society Prize Committee.[^o25] From 2017 to 2020, she served on the Committee On Travel Awards of the Institute for Mathematical Statistics.[^o26] Since 2013, she has been the coordinator of The Southern California Probability Symposium, an annual gathering of probabilists supported by [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), [UCSB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), [UCSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), and [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).[^o27]

Since 2019, she has been an associate editor of *[Mathematics of Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Operations_Research)* in the area of stochastic models.[^o28]

From 2013-2015, Puha served as a Council Member at Large for the American Mathematical Society.[^o29] Since 2016, she has been a life member at American Mathematical Society.

### Other services {#Other_services}

In addition to her academic work, she served as the faculty advisor for the CSUSM Surf Team, which competes in the [National Scholastic Surfing Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Scholastic_Surfing_Association) (NSSA). She led the team to its first and second National Championship in 2009[^o31] and 2019,[^o32] respectively. She won the NSSA Coach of the Year award in 2019.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) Applied Probability Society Best Publication Award (2007), jointly with H. Christian Gromoll and [Ruth J. Williams](wikipedia://en/44832164)[^o34]
- Greater San Diego Area Mathematics Council Outstanding Post Secondary Mathematics Teacher (2009)[^o3]
- CSUSM Presidents Outstanding Faculty Award for Scholarship and Creative Activity (2015/2016)[^o35]
- National Scholastic Surfing Association Coach of the Year (2019)[^o3]
