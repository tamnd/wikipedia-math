# Anna Skripka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Skripka"}

*Image: Anna skripka public math.jpg.*

**Anna Skripka** is a Ukrainian-American mathematician whose research topics include noncommutative analysis and probability. She is a professor at the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Skripka did her undergraduate studies at the [V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._N._Karazin_Kharkiv_National_University) in Ukraine. She completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri). After working as a visiting assistant professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) and as an assistant professor at the [University of Central Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Florida), she joined the University of New Mexico Department of Mathematics and Statistics in 2012, where she is currently a full professor.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Skripka is the 2019 winner of the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).
