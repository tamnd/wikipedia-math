# Laura Matusevich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Laura Matusevich"}

**Laura Felicia Matusevich** is an [Argentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

## Birth and Education {#Birth_and_Education}

Matusevich was born in [Córdoba, Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Córdoba,_Argentina), and earned her undergraduate degree from the [Universidad Nacional de Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Córdoba).[^o2] She earned her PhD from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2002.[^o4][^o5]

## Career {#Career}

From 2003 until 2004 Matusevich was a [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). From 2004 until 2006 she was a tenure-track assistant professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). In 2005, she began as a tenure-track assistant professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), where she became a tenure-track associate professor in 2009. She became a full professor there in 2017.[^o7][^o4]

## Publications {#Publications}

Matusevich has published 34 research articles as of the 16th of March 2022.[^o5][^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Matusevich is the recipient of multiple awards and honors, having been an [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship), a [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Postdoctoral Fellow, and an Antorchas Fellow (one of 25 people awarded this nationwide in Argentina), among others.[^o5]
