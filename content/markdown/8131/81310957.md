# Lan Wang (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lan Wang"}

**Lan Wang** is a Chinese and American statistician whose research focuses on [high-dimensional statistics](wikipedia://en/19330658), [quantile regression](wikipedia://en/14692673), and the application of statistics to [personalized medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personalized_medicine) and the statistical study of [health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care) costs. She is a professor and department chair of management science in the [University of Miami Patti and Allan Herbert Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herbert_Business_School), where she holds a Centennial Endowed Chair.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wang has a 1998 bachelor's degree from [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University). She completed her Ph.D. in 2003 at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University). Her doctoral dissertation, *Testing in heteroscedastic ANOVA with large number of levels and in nonparametric ANCOVA*, was supervised by [Michael Akritas](wikipedia://en/61443541).

She became a professor of statistics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) before moving to her present position at the University of Miami. She was co-editor-in-chief for the journal *[Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)* for the 2022–2024 term.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wang was elected as a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2017, and as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2018. In 2021, she was named as an Elected Member of the International Statistical Institute.
