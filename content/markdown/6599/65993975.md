# Mehmet Burak Erdoğan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mehmet Burak Erdoğan"}

**Mehmet Burak Erdoğan** (born 1972) is a Turkish mathematician, scientist, and professor of mathematics. He is a member of the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) (UIUC) Mathematics Department.[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Burak Erdoğan was born in 1972. He attended the high school Kayseri Fen Lisesi in [Kayseri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayseri), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), for two years before moving to [İzmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/İzmir), Turkey where he finished the high school Atatürk Lisesi in 1989. He ranked third in the country-wide university entrance exams. He graduated from [Bilkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_University) Electrical and Electronics Engineering in [Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara) in 1994. He finished his MSc degree in Mathematics department of the same university under the supervision of [Iossif Vladimirovich Ostrovskii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iossif_Ostrovskii) in 1996. He received his PhD in Mathematics under the supervision of [Thomas Wolff](wikipedia://en/1099744) at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) in 2001.[^o4] He was the last PhD student of Wolff, who died in the last year of Erdoğan's PhD work.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

He was a postdoc at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) between 2001-2002 and a visiting assistant professor at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) between 2002-2004. Since then he is a faculty member in UIUC Mathematics Department.

He has been an active researcher, having published more than 50 scientific manuscripts with more than 800 citations. He raised 6 PhD students.[^o2][^o7]

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

His research interests include [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [dispersive partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/8477417).
