# Boole polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boole polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Boole polynomials** *s*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are polynomials given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

:::

$$\displaystyle \sum s_n(x)t^n/n! = \frac{(1+t)^x}{1+(1+t)^\lambda}$$
