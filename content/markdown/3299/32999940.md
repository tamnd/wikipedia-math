# Peters polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peters polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Peters polynomials** *s*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are polynomials studied by George Peters[^o2][^o3] given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

:::

$$\displaystyle \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} s_n(x)\frac{t^n}{n!} = \frac{(1+t)^x}{(1+(1+t)^\lambda)^{\mu}}$$

(Roman 1984, 4.4.6), (Boas Buck 1958, p.37). They are a generalization of the [Boole polynomials](wikipedia://en/32995388).
