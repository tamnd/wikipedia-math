# Narumi polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Narumi polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Narumi polynomials** are polynomials introduced by (Narumi 1929)[^o3] given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

:::

$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{s_n(x)}{n!}t^n = \left(\frac{t}{\log(1+t)}\right)^a(1+t)^x.$$

They form the [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596) for[^o4][^o5]

$$g(t)=\Big(\frac{e^t-1}{t}\Big)^{-a}, \quad f(t)=e^t-1.$$
