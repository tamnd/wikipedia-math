# Niccolò Aggiunti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Niccolò Aggiunti"}

*Image: Aggiunti - Oratio de mathematicae laudibus, 1627 - 1227946.jpg.*

**Niccolò Aggiunti** (1600 in [Sansepolcro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sansepolcro) – 1635) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He studied in Pisa under [Benedetto Castelli](wikipedia://en/855653). After receiving his degree in 1621, he became a teacher of [Ferdinando II de 'Medici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinando_II_de'_Medici,_Grand_Duke_of_Tuscany). During this period he probably met [Galileo Galilei](wikipedia://en/29688374), later becoming one of his favorite students. In 1626 he was awarded the chair of mathematics in Pisa, as Castelli's successor. In Sansepolcro, his city, one of the main streets of the historic center is named after his name.
