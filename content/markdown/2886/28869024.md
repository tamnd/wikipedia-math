# Sergio Albeverio

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sergio Albeverio"}

**Sergio A. Albeverio** (born 17 January 1939)[^o3] is a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and mathematical [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) working in numerous fields of mathematics and its applications. In particular he is known for his work in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134) (including [infinite-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite-dimensional_analysis), [non-standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis), and [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis)), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and in the areas [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), as well as in applications, from [natural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science) to [social](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science)-[economic sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_Sciences).

:::

He initiated (with [Raphael Høegh-Krohn](wikipedia://en/28711790)) a systematic mathematical theory of [Feynman path integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feynman_path_integrals) and of infinite-dimensional [Dirichlet forms](wikipedia://en/25002118) and associated [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) (with applications particularly in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267)). He also gave essential contributions to the development of areas such as [*p*-adic](wikipedia://en/291327) functional and stochastic analysis as well as to the [singular perturbation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_perturbation_theory) for [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886). Other important contributions concern [constructive quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/1231204) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of infinite-dimensional [groups](wikipedia://en/19447). He also initiated a new approach to the study of galaxy and planet formation inspired by [stochastic mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_mechanics).
