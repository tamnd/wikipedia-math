# Per Lindström

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Per Lindström"}

**Per "Pelle" Lindström** (9 April 1936 – 21 August 2009, [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg))[^o2] was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_people) [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), after whom [Lindström's theorem](wikipedia://en/7510462) and the [Lindström quantifier](wikipedia://en/8030626) are named.[^o3] (He also independently discovered [Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé games](wikipedia://en/1040475).[^o2]) He was one of the key followers of [Lars Svenonius](wikipedia://en/28868294).[^o4]

:::

Lindström was awarded a PhD from the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg) in 1966. His thesis was titled *Some Results in the Theory of Models of First Order Languages*. A [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) for Lindström was published in 1986.[^o6]
