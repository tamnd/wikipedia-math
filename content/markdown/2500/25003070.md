# Endre Süli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Endre Süli"}

**Endre Süli** (also, Endre Suli or Endre Šili) is a mathematician. He is Emeritus Professor at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and Emeritus Fellow of [Worcester College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_College,_Oxford), formerly Professor of [Numerical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Analysis) in the [Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mathematical_Institute,_University_of_Oxford), [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) and [Tutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutor) in Mathematics at [Worcester College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_College,_Oxford) and [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of [Linacre College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linacre_College,_Oxford). He was educated at the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) and, as a [British Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Council) Visiting Student, at the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading) and [St Catherine's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catherine's_College,_Oxford). His research is concerned with the mathematical analysis of numerical algorithms for nonlinear partial differential equations.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Süli was elected a Foreign Member of the [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) (2009), Fellow of the European Academy of Sciences (FEurASc, 2010), Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (FSIAM, 2016),[^o4] member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (MAE, 2020),[^o5] and a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) (FRS, 2021).[^o6]

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Madrid in 2006[^o7] and was Chair of the [Society for the Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) (2002–2005).[^o8] Other honours include: [Fellow of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Institute_of_Mathematics_and_its_Applications) (FIMA, 2007), Charlemagne Distinguished Lecture (2011), IMA Service Award (2011), Professor Hospitus Universitatis Carolinae Pragensis, [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) (2012–), Distinguished Visiting Chair Professor [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiao_Tong_University) (2013), President, SIAM United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland Section (2013–2015), [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)/[New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society) [Forder Lectureship](wikipedia://en/59046614) (2015),[^o9] Aziz Lecture (2015), BIMOS Distinguished Lecture (2016), John von Neumann Lecture (2016),[^o10] Sibe Mardešić Lecture (2018),[^o11] [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) [Naylor Prize and Lectureship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naylor_Prize_and_Lectureship) (2021).[^o12]

Since 2005 Süli has been co-Editor-in-Chief of the IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis[^o13] published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press). He is a member of the Board of the Doctoral School for Mathematical and Physical Sciences for Advanced Materials and Technologies of the Scuola Superiore Meridionale at the University of Naples,[^o14] and was a member of the Scientific Steering Committee of the [Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton_Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (2011–2014), the Scientific Advisory Board of the [Berlin Mathematical School](wikipedia://en/14614258) (2016–2018), the Scientific Council of [Société de Mathématiques Appliquées et Industrielles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_Mathématiques_Appliquées_et_Industrielles) (SMAI) (2014–2020), the Scientific Committee of the Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach ([Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)) (2013–2021), the Scientific Advisory Board of the Berlin Mathematics Research Center MATH+ (2019–2025)[^o15] and the Scientific Advisory Board of the Archimedes Center for Modeling, Analysis and Computation at the University of Crete (2010–2014).[^o16] Between 2014 and 2022 he served as Delegate for Mathematics to the Board of Delegates of [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press).

He grew up in [Subotica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subotica) and is a recipient of the Pro Urbe Prize of the City of Subotica (2021). He is the father of Sterija Award-winning Serbian playwright and dramatist Fedor Süli (also, Fedor Šili) and social scientist Timea Süli.[^o17]
