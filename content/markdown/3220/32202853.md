# Binomial ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Binomial ring"}

In mathematics, a **binomial ring** is a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) whose additive [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is [torsion-free](wikipedia://en/3039330) and contains all [binomial coefficients](wikipedia://en/4668)

:::

$$\binom{x}{n} = \frac{x(x-1)\cdots(x-n+1)}{n!}$$

for *x* in the ring and *n* a positive integer. Binomial rings were introduced by (Hall 1969).

(Elliott 2006) showed that binomial rings are essentially the same as [λ-rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambda_ring) for which all [Adams operations](wikipedia://en/1226666) are the identity.
