# Marcelo Viana

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Marcelo Viana"}

**Marcelo Miranda Viana da Silva** (born 4 March 1962) is a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632).[^o4][^o5] He proved the [Zorich–Kontsevich conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zorich–Kontsevich_conjecture) together with [Artur Avila](wikipedia://en/23579022).

:::

He was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1993.[^o6] He received the [TWAS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS_Prize) in 1998[^o7] and in 2005 he was awarded the inaugural [ICTP Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525) for his research achievements.[^o5]

Viana was vice-president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) in 2011–2014, and president of the [Brazilian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociedade_Brasileira_de_Matemática) (2013–2015).[^o8][^o9]

In 1998, he was a [plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065), in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o10][^o11]

Viana is director elected of the [IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708) (for the period 2016–2019).

Viana is a [columnist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columnist) for *[Folha de S.Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folha_de_S.Paulo)*.[^o12]

He was the chair of the executive committee for the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), Rio de Janeiro.[^o13]

## Biography {#Biography}

Viana was born in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), Brazil, his parents being Portuguese. He grew up in Portugal, and his undergraduate studies were at the [University of Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Porto). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree from the IMPA in Rio de Janeiro, with [Jacob Palis](wikipedia://en/1540070) as advisor. He is now director at IMPA.[^o15]

## Work {#Work}

Viana's work concerns [chaotic dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotic_dynamical_system) and [strange attractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strange_attractor).[^o17]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Books:

- with José Espinar: *Differential Equations: A Dynamical Systems Approach to Theory and Practice* (2021)[^o19]
- with Krerley Oliveira: *Foundations of Ergodic Theory*, [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press)

Research papers:

- jointly with [AVILA, A.](wikipedia://en/23579022), "Simplicity of Lyapunov spectra: proof of the Zorich–Kontsevich conjecture". *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)*. vol. 198 (2007), no. 1, pp. 1–56.
- jointly with [PALIS, J.](wikipedia://en/1540070), "High dimension diffeomorphisms displaying infinitely many periodic attractors". *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*. vol. 140 (1994), no. 1, pp. 207–250.
- jointly with MORA, L., "Abundance of strange attractors". *Acta Mathematica*. vol. 171 (1993), no. 1, pp. 1–71.
