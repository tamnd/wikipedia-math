# Geometric and Functional Analysis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Geometric and Functional Analysis"}

***Geometric and Functional Analysis* (*GAFA*)** is a [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) journal published by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser), an independent division of [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag). The journal is published bi-monthly.

:::

The journal publishes major results on a broad range of mathematical topics related to geometry and analysis. [^o3] *GAFA* is both an [acronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym) and a part of the official full name of the journal.

## History {#History}

*GAFA* was founded in 1991 by [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29) and [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman). The idea for the journal was inspired by the long-running Israeli seminar series "Geometric Aspects of Functional Analysis" of which Vitali Milman had been one of the main organizers in the previous years. The journal retained the same acronym as the series to stress the connection between the two.[^o5]

## Journal information {#Journal_information}

The journal is reviewed cover-to-cover in [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646) and [zbMATH Open](wikipedia://en/1701961) and is indexed cover-to-cover in the [Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.2.[^o7]

The journal has seven editors: [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman) (editor-in-chief), [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305), [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29), [Larry Guth](wikipedia://en/31875947), [Boáz Klartag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boáz_Klartag), [Leonid Polterovich](wikipedia://en/32556386), and [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak).[^o8]
