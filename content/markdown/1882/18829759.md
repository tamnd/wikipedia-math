# Valuative criterion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valuative criterion"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **valuative criteria** are a collection of results that make it possible to decide whether a [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) of [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808), or more generally [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754), is [universally closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universally_closed), [separated](wikipedia://en/35457783#separated), or [proper](wikipedia://en/603782).

:::

## Statement of the valuative criteria {#Statement_of_the_valuative_criteria}

Recall that a [valuation ring](wikipedia://en/966856) *A* is a domain, so if *K* is the [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) of *A*, then Spec *K* is the [generic point](wikipedia://en/3043978) of Spec *A*.

Let *X* and *Y* be schemes, and let *f* : *X* → *Y* be a morphism of schemes. Then the following are equivalent:[^o3][^o4]

1. *f* is separated (resp. universally closed, resp. proper)
1. *f* is [quasi-separated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-separated) (resp. [quasi-compact](wikipedia://en/12410777), resp. of finite type and quasi-separated) and for every valuation ring *A*, if *Y'* = Spec *A* and *X'* denotes the generic point of *Y'*, then for every morphism *Y'* → *Y* and every morphism *X'* → *X* which lifts the generic point, then there exists at most one (resp. at least one, resp. exactly one) lift *Y'* → *X*.

The lifting condition is equivalent to specifying that the natural morphism

$$\text{Hom}_Y(Y', X) \to \text{Hom}_Y(\operatorname{Spec} K, X)$$

is injective (resp. surjective, resp. bijective).

Furthermore, in the special case when *Y* is (locally) [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/2783891), it suffices to check the case that *A* is a [discrete valuation ring](wikipedia://en/848633).
