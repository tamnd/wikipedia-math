# Z\* theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Z* theorem"}

In mathematics, [George Glauberman](wikipedia://en/9744285)'s **Z\* theorem** is stated as follows:

:::

> **Z\* theorem:** Let *G* be a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707), with *O*(*G*) being its maximal [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [order](wikipedia://en/493527). If *T* is a [Sylow 2-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) of *G* containing an [involution](wikipedia://en/293450#Group_theory) not [conjugate](wikipedia://en/49176) in *G* to any other element of *T*, then the involution lies in *Z\**(*G*), which is the inverse image in *G* of the [center](wikipedia://en/7125) of *G*/*O*(*G*).

This generalizes the [Brauer–Suzuki theorem](wikipedia://en/8239308) (and the proof uses the Brauer–Suzuki theorem to deal with some small cases).

## Details {#Details}

The original paper (Glauberman 1966) gave several criteria for an element to lie outside *Z\**(*G*). Its theorem 4 states:

> For an element *t* in *T*, it is necessary and sufficient for *t* to lie outside *Z\**(*G*) that there is some *g* in *G* and [abelian subgroup](wikipedia://en/2974) *U* of *T* satisfying the following properties:
>
> *g* normalizes both *U* and the [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) *C<sub>T</sub>*(*U*), that is *g* is contained in *N* = *N<sub>G</sub>*(*U*) ∩ *N<sub>G</sub>*(*C<sub>T</sub>*(*U*))
>
> *t* is contained in *U* and *tg* ≠ *gt*
>
> *U* is generated by the *N*-conjugates of *t*
>
> the [exponent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponent_%28group_theory%29) of *U* is equal to the [order](wikipedia://en/493527) of *t*
>
> Moreover *g* may be chosen to have [prime power](wikipedia://en/1630483) order if *t* is in the center of *T*, and *g* may be chosen in *T* otherwise.

A simple corollary is that an element *t* in *T* is not in *Z\**(*G*) if and only if there is some *s* ≠ *t* such that *s* and *t* commute and *s* and *t* are *G*-conjugate.

A generalization to odd [primes](wikipedia://en/23666) was recorded in (Guralnick Robinson 1993): if *t* is an element of prime order *p* and the [commutator](wikipedia://en/7193) \[*t*, *g*\] has order [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to *p* for all *g*, then *t* is central modulo the [*p*′-core](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-core). This was also generalized to odd primes and to [compact Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group) in (Mislin Thévenaz 1991), which also contains several useful results in the finite case.

(Henke Semeraro 2015) have also studied an extension of the Z\* theorem to pairs of groups (*G*, *H*) with *H* a normal subgroup of *G*.
