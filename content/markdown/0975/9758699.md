# ZJ theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ZJ theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [George Glauberman](wikipedia://en/9744285)'s **ZJ theorem** states that if a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) *G* is *p*-constrained and [*p*-stable](wikipedia://en/35850610) and has a [normal](wikipedia://en/21918) [*p*-subgroup](wikipedia://en/24664) for some [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*, then *O*<sub>*p*</sub>(*G*)*Z*(*J*(*S*)) is a normal [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of *G*, for any [Sylow *p*-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) *S*.

:::

## Notation and definitions {#Notation_and_definitions}

- *J*(*S*) is the **[Thompson subgroup](wikipedia://en/5662011)** of a *p*-group *S*: the subgroup generated by the [abelian subgroups](wikipedia://en/2974) of maximal [order](wikipedia://en/493527).
- *Z*(*H*) means the [center](wikipedia://en/7125) of a group *H*.
- *O*<sub>*p*</sub> is the maximal normal subgroup of *G* of order [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to *p*, the [*p*-core](wikipedia://en/893516#The_p-core)
- *O*<sub>*p*</sub> is the maximal normal *p*-subgroup of *G*, the [*p*-core](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-core).
- *O*<sub>*p*,*p*</sub>(*G*) is the maximal normal [*p*-nilpotent subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-nilpotent_group) of *G*, the [*p*,*p*-core](wikipedia://en/893516#The_p-core), part of the [upper *p*-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_p-series).
- For an odd prime *p*, a group *G* with *O*<sub>*p*</sub>(*G*) ≠ 1 is said to be **[*p*-stable](wikipedia://en/35850610)** if whenever *P* is a *p*-subgroup of *G* such that *PO<sub>p</sub>*(*G*) is normal in *G*, and \[*P*,*x*,*x*\] = 1, then the image of *x* in N<sub>*G*</sub>(*P*)/C<sub>*G*</sub>(*P*) is contained in a normal *p*-subgroup of N<sub>*G*</sub>(*P*)/C<sub>*G*</sub>(*P*).
- For an odd prime *p*, a group *G* with *O*<sub>*p*</sub>(*G*) ≠ 1 is said to be **[*p*-constrained](wikipedia://en/21837511)** if the [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) C<sub>*G*</sub>(*P*) is contained in *O*<sub>*p*,*p*</sub>(*G*) whenever *P* is a Sylow *p*-subgroup of *O*<sub>*p*,*p*</sub>(*G*).
