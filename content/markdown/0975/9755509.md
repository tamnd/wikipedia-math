# Mosco convergence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mosco convergence"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), **Mosco convergence** is a notion of convergence for [functionals](wikipedia://en/1374948) that is used in [nonlinear analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_analysis) and [set-valued analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set-valued_analysis). Named after the Italian mathematician Umberto Mosco, it is a particular case of [Γ-convergence](wikipedia://en/2991580). Mosco convergence is sometimes phrased as “weak Γ-liminf and strong Γ-limsup” convergence since it uses both the [weak and strong topologies](wikipedia://en/33662#The_strong_and_weak_topologies) on a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) *X*. In finite dimensional spaces, Mosco convergence coincides with [epi-convergence](wikipedia://en/50531340), while in infinite-dimensional spaces, Mosco convergence is a strictly stronger property.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *X* be a topological vector space and let *X*<sup>∗</sup> denote the [dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) of [continuous linear functionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_linear_functional) on *X*. Let *F*<sub>*n*</sub> : *X* → \[0, +∞\] be functionals on *X* for each *n* = 1, 2, ... The sequence (or, more generally, [net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_%28topology%29)) (*F*<sub>*n*</sub>) is said to **Mosco converge** to another functional *F* : *X* → \[0, +∞\] if the following two conditions hold:

- lower bound inequality: for each sequence of elements *x*<sub>*n*</sub> ∈ *X* [converging weakly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_convergence_of_measures) to *x* ∈ *X*,

$$\liminf_{n \to \infty} F_{n} (x_{n}) \geq F(x);$$

- upper bound inequality: for every *x* ∈ *X* there exists an approximating sequence of elements *x*<sub>*n*</sub> ∈ *X*, converging strongly to *x*, such that

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} F_{n} (x_{n}) \leq F(x).$$

Since lower and upper bound inequalities of this type are used in the definition of Γ-convergence, Mosco convergence is sometimes phrased as “weak Γ-liminf and strong Γ-limsup” convergence. Mosco convergence is sometimes abbreviated to **M-convergence** and denoted by

$$\mathop{\text{M-lim}}_{n \to \infty} F_{n} = F \text{ or } F_{n} \xrightarrow[n \to \infty]{\mathrm{M}} F.$$
