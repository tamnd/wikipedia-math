# Forum of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Forum of Mathematics"}

***Forum of Mathematics, Pi*** and ***Forum of Mathematics, Sigma*** are [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access) [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) journals for [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published under a [creative commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_commons) license by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o4][^o5] The founding [managing editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) was [Rob Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424).[^o6] He was succeeded by [Robert Guralnick](wikipedia://en/57893712), who is currently the managing editor of both journals.[^o7][^o8]

:::

*Forum of Mathematics, Pi* publishes articles of interest to a wide audience of mathematicians, while *Forum of Mathematics, Sigma* is intended for more specialized articles, with clusters of editors in different [areas of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areas_of_mathematics).[^o9][^o10]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

Both journals are abstracted and indexed in [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded),[^o12][^o13] [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683),[^o14][^o15] and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus).[^o16][^o17]
