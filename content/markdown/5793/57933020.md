# Ribenboim Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ribenboim Prize"}

The **Ribenboim Prize**, named in honour of [Paulo Ribenboim](wikipedia://en/9129242), is awarded by the Canadian Number Theory Association for distinguished research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) by a mathematician who is Canadian or has close connections to Canadian mathematics.[^o3] Normally, the winner will have received their Ph.D. in the last 12 years. The winner is expected to give a plenary talk at the award ceremony.[^o4]

:::

Due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), the 2020, 2022, and 2024 Ribenboim Prizes were all awarded in 2024.[^o5]
