# Ten Computational Canons

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ten Computational Canons"}

The ***Ten Computational Canons*** () was a collection of ten Chinese mathematical works dating from pre-[Han dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_dynasty) to early [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty), compiled by the early Tang mathematician [Li Chunfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Chunfeng) (602–670) in the 650s, as the official mathematical texts for [imperial examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_examination) in mathematics.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Included works {#Included_works}

The *Ten Computation Canons* consist of ten superlative works that conveyed mathematical knowledge from the Zhou to the early Tang dynasty, a time span of over a thousand years.[^o2]

The original *Ten Computational Canons* includes:

1. *[Zhoubi Suanjing](wikipedia://en/6131715)* (*Zhou Shadow Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Jiuzhang Suanshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiuzhang_Suanshu)* (*The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art*)
1. *[Haidao Suanjing](wikipedia://en/15419368)* (*The Sea Island Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Sunzi Suanjing](wikipedia://en/30291690)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Sun Zi*)
1. *[Zhang Qiujian Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52428193)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Zhang Qiujian*)
1. *[Wucao Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52462899)* (*Computational Canon of the Five Administrative Sections*)
1. *[Xiahou Yang Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52488778)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Xiahou Yang*)
1. *[Wujing Suanshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wujing_Suanshu)* (*Computational Prescriptions of the Five Classics*)
1. *[Jigu Suanjing](wikipedia://en/30257071)* (*Continuation of Ancient Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Zhui Shu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhui_Shu)* (*Method of Interpolation*)

In 1084 during the [Northern Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Song_dynasty), the text *[Shushu Jiyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shushu_Jiyi)* (*Notes on Traditions of Arithmetic Methods*) was selected to be part of this collection, replacing zh. Thus, zh has appeared in the subsequent issuing of the catalogue.

Since the Northern Song dynasty, the catalogue consist of:

1. *[Zhoubi Suanjing](wikipedia://en/6131715)* (*Zhou Shadow Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Jiuzhang Suanshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiuzhang_Suanshu)* (*The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art*)
1. *[Haidao Suanjing](wikipedia://en/15419368)* (*The Sea Island Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Sunzi Suanjing](wikipedia://en/30291690)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Sun Zi*)
1. *[Zhang Qiujian Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52428193)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Zhang Qiujian*)
1. *[Wucao Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52462899)* (*Computational Canon of the Five Administrative Sections*)
1. *[Xiahou Yang Suanjing](wikipedia://en/52488778)* (*The Mathematical Classic of Xiahou Yang*)
1. *[Wujing Suanshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wujing_Suanshu)* (*Computational Prescriptions of the Five Classics*)
1. *[Jigu Suanjing](wikipedia://en/30257071)* (*Continuation of Ancient Mathematical Classic*)
1. *[Shushu Jiyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shushu_Jiyi)* (*Notes on Traditions of Arithmetic Methods*)

## Impact {#Impact}

It was specified in [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty) laws on examination that zh and the zh together required one year of study; zh plus zh three years; zh three years; zh four years; and zh and zh one year each.

The government of the [Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty) actively promoted the study of mathematics. There were two government [xylograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xylograph) editions of *The Ten Computational Canons* in the years 1084 and 1213. The wide availability of these mathematical texts contributed to the flourishing of mathematics in the Song and [Yuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_dynasty) dynasties, inspiring mathematicians such as [Jia Xian](wikipedia://en/15141098), [Qin Jiushao](wikipedia://en/612837), [Yang Hui](wikipedia://en/1716898), [Li Zhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Zhi_%28mathematician%29) and [Zhu Shijie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhu_Shijie).

In the [Ming dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty) during the reign of the [Yongle Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongle_Emperor), the *Ten Computational Canons* were copied into the *[Yongle Encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongle_Encyclopedia)*. During the reign of the [Qianlong Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qianlong_Emperor) in the [Qing dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qing_dynasty), scholar [Dai Zhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dai_Zhen) made copies of the *Ten Computational Canons* including the zh from the *Yongle Encyclopedia* and transferred them into another encyclopedia, the *[Complete Library of the Four Treasuries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_Library_of_the_Four_Treasuries)*.
