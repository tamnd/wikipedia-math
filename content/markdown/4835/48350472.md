# Georgios Remoundos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georgios Remoundos"}

*Image: Georgios Remoundos.jpg.* Georgios Remoundos

**Georgios Remoundos** (Γεώργιος Ρεμούνδος, 1878 in Athens – 27 April 1928) was a Greek mathematician and a founding member of the [Academy of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Athens_%28modern%29) in 1926.

:::

After graduating from the [Varvakeio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varvakeio), he studied at the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) and was given a government scholarship to study in France. He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) and then at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), where he received in 1905 his Ph.D. (Thèse de doctorat) with thesis *Sur les zéros d'une classe de fonctions transcendantes*.[^o2] A post-doctoral student of [Émile Picard](wikipedia://en/42000), Remoundos published in French under the name "Georges J. Rémoundos". He was a professor of mathematics at the University of Athens.[^o3] He was a co-editor and co-founder, along with [P. Zervos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panagiotis_Zervos), N. Sakellarios, and K. Lambiris, of the journal *Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de Gréce*, first published in May 1919 written about one-third in French and two-thirds in Greek.[^o4] Remoundos was three times an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians): in 1908 at Rome, in 1912 at Cambridge (England), and in 1920 at Strasbourg.[^o5]
