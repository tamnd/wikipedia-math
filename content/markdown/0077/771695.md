# Hurwitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hurwitz"}

**Hurwitz** is one of the variants of a surname of [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi) Jewish origin (for historical background see the [Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horowitz) page). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Adolf Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/771690) (1859–1919), German mathematician
- [Andrew D. Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_D._Hurwitz) (born 1947), American judge
- [David Hurwitz (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hurwitz_%28disambiguation%29), multiple people
- [Edward Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Hurwitz) (born 1931), American diplomat
- [Emanuel Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emanuel_Hurwitz) (1919–2006), British violinist
- [Gregg Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregg_Hurwitz), American novelist
- [Henry Hurwitz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hurwitz_Jr.) (1918–1992), nuclear physicist
- [Hyman Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyman_Hurwitz) (1770–1844), professor of Hebrew in England
- [Jake Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Hurwitz) (born 1985), American comedian, writer and actor
- [Jerard Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerard_Hurwitz) (1928–2019), American biochemist
- [Johanna Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johanna_Hurwitz) (born 1937), American children's author
- [Jon Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Hurwitz) (born 1977), American screenwriter
- [Jonty Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonty_Hurwitz) (born 1969), electrical engineer and sculptor
- [Justin Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Hurwitz) (born 1985), American composer
- [Lazar Lipman Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazar_Lipman_Hurwitz) (1815–1852), editor and writer
- [Leo Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Hurwitz) (1909–1991), American documentary film maker
- [Moses ha-Levi Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_ha-Levi_Hurwitz) (d. 1820), Lithuanian rabbi
- [Mitchell Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitchell_Hurwitz), American television writer and producer
- [Shelley Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/55795939), American biostatistician
- [Shmuel Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shmuel_Hurwitz), Israeli agronomist
- [Shmuel Hurvits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shmuel_Hurvits), Yiddish poet
- [T. Alan Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._Alan_Hurwitz) (born 1942), 10th president of Gallaudet University
- [William Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hurwitz), American pain management physician
- [Yosef Yozel Horwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yosef_Yozel_Horwitz) (1849–1919), the *Alter of Navordok*
- [Zvi Harry Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvi_Harry_Hurwitz), (1924–2008) Israeli diplomat, public servant and biographer
