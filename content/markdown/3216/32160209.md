# Good cover

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Good cover"}

*Image: Good and "bad" covers.png.* The cover on the left is not a good cover, since while all open sets in the cover are contractible, their intersection is disconnected. The cover on the right is a good cover, since the intersection of the two sets is contractible.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an [open cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_cover) of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$ is a family of open subsets such that $X$ is the union of all of the open sets. A **good cover** is an open cover in which all sets and all non-empty intersections of finitely-many sets are [contractible](wikipedia://en/1752414) (Petersen 2006).

:::

The concept was introduced by [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) in 1952 for [differentiable manifolds](wikipedia://en/2119219), demanding the $U_{\alpha_1 \ldots \alpha_n}$ to be differentiably contractible. A modern version of this definition appears in (Bott Tu 1982).

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

A major reason for the notion of a good cover is that the [Leray spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/5174983) of a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) degenerates for a good cover, and so the [Čech cohomology](wikipedia://en/856614) associated with a good cover is the same as the Čech cohomology of the space. (Such a cover is known as a [Leray cover](wikipedia://en/2854390).) However, for the purposes of computing the Čech cohomology it suffices to have a more relaxed definition of a good cover in which all intersections of finitely many open sets have contractible [connected components](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_component_%28topology%29). This follows from the fact that higher [derived functors](wikipedia://en/433875) can be computed using [acyclic resolutions](wikipedia://en/19745659#Acyclic_resolution).

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

The two-dimensional surface of a sphere $S^2$ has an open cover by two contractible sets, open neighborhoods of opposite hemispheres. However these two sets have an intersection that forms a non-contractible equatorial band. To form a good cover for this surface, one needs at least four open sets. A good cover can be formed by projecting the faces of a [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) onto a sphere in which it is inscribed, and taking an open neighborhood of each face. The more relaxed definition of a good cover allows us to do this using only three open sets. A cover can be formed by choosing two diametrically opposite points on the sphere, drawing three non-intersecting segments lying on the sphere connecting them and taking open neighborhoods of the resulting faces.

:::
