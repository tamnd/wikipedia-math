# Horn angle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horn angle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **horn angle**, also called a cornicular angle, is a type of [curvilinear angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curvilinear_angle) defined as the angle formed between a circle and a straight line tangent to it, or, more generally, the angle formed between two curves at a point where they are tangent to each other.

:::
