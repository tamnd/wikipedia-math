# Jesper Møller (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jesper Møller"}

**Jesper Møller** (also written *Moller* or *Moeller*; born December 6, 1957)[^o3][^o4][^o6] is a Danish mathematician.

:::

He is a professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, [Aalborg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_University), where he conducts research and teaching in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). In 2012, he created the Bachelor and Master's Programmes in Mathematics and Technology at [Aalborg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_University).[^o5]

He was born in [Nakskov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakskov), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark).[^o6]

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

Jesper Møller is [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) ([cand.scient.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.scient.)) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) ([Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University)), 1984; [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) ([Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University)), 1988; and [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) ([dr.scient.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science); title of dissertation: \`Aspects of Spatial Statistics, Stochastic Geometry and Markov Chain Monte Carlo´; [Aalborg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_University)), 2000.[^o3][^o8]

He was Assistant Professor and Associate Professor at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University), 1988–1995, Associate Professor at Aalborg University, 1996–2000, and Professor at Aalborg University since 2001.

## Research {#Research}

Jesper Møller's research interests[^o3][^o4] include

- [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349) (in particular [statistical models](wikipedia://en/27576) and methods for [spatial point processes](wikipedia://en/2147685)),
- [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813) (in particular random t[essellations](wikipedia://en/321671)),
- [stochastic simulation](wikipedia://en/7210212) ([Markov-chain Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/236801) and [perfect simulation](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/statistics/staff/academic-research/thonnes/personal/perfect/) (2017-06-13). <https://web.archive.org/web/20170613100720/http://www2.warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/statistics/staff/academic-research/thonnes/personal/perfect>).

His research has been supported by project grants from [Danish National Research Foundation](https://dg.dk/en/), [Independent Research Fund Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Council_for_Independent_Research), [EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union), [Velux Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velux_Foundation), and [Spar Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spar_Nord) Foundation. He has been Associated Editor of [Annals of Applied Probability](wikipedia://en/31018340), [Advances in Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Applied_Probability), [Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability), and Associate Editor and National Editor of [the Scandinavian Journal of Statistics.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavian_Journal_of_Statistics)

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

- Elected Fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics, 1998.
- [Spar Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spar_Nord) Foundation Research Price (DKK 250,000), 2000.[^o11]
- Presentation of a read-paper at a [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) Meeting in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), 2005.
- Plenary Talk of 3 hours at the 21st Nordic Conference on [Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), presenting an invited paper for [the Scandinavian Journal of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavian_Journal_of_Statistics).
- Rising Star in the field of Mathematics ([Thomson Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomson_Reuters)), 2008.[^o12]
- [Knight of the Order of Dannebrog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_of_the_order_of_Dannebrog), 2015.[^o13]
- Professorem Hospitem, [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University), [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague), since 2020.
