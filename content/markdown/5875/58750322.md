# Fritz Bützberger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz Bützberger"}

**Fritz Bützberger** (26 March 1862 – 1 November 1922) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Bützberger was educated in the schools of [Langenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langenthal) and [Burgdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgdorf,_Switzerland) before enrolling at [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) in 1880. He graduated in 1884 and began his teaching in his former secondary school in Langenthal. Simultaneously he studied for his doctorate that was awarded him by the [university of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) in 1888 under the advice of [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408).[^n1]

From 1896 he was mathematics professor at the *Kantonsschule* of [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich). He also delivered some lectures on geometry to future teachers in the [university of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich). From 1903 he had a teaching post in the technical school of Burgdorf.

He was the author of several text books for secondary level very valued and reedited several times.[^n2] The matters included [stereometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereometry), [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118).[^n3]

He was also the editor of the oldest papers and manuscripts of [Jakob Steiner](wikipedia://en/567445).[^n4]

In 1896–97, he was on the organizing committee of the first [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zürich.
