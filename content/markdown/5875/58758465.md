# Dmitry Khovratovich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitry Khovratovich"}

**Dmitry Khovratovich** is a Russian [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer), currently a Lead Cryptographer for the [Dusk Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dusk_Network), researcher for the [Ethereum Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum_Foundation), and member of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Khovratovich, together with [Alex Biryukov](wikipedia://en/9189491), developed the [Equihash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equihash) [proof-of-work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof-of-work) algorithm which is currently being used as consensus mechanism for the [Zcash](wikipedia://en/51849458) [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188), and the [Argon2](wikipedia://en/48963187) key derivation function, which won the [Password Hashing Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Password_Hashing_Competition) in July 2015.[^o5] He is the publisher of several [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) papers for a number of mainstream cyphers, such as the first cryptanalytic attack on full-round [AES-192](wikipedia://en/1260) and AES-256 which is faster than a [brute-force attack](wikipedia://en/53784),[^o6] an attack on the [RadioGatún](wikipedia://en/8625699) cryptographic primitive,[^o7] and also the current best cryptanalysis on Skein,[^o8] a candidate for the [SHA-3 competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHA-3_competition).

In 2014, he published a research about the deanonymisation of clients in the [Bitcoin P2P network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcoin_network)[^o9]
