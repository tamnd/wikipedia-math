# Aleksandra Slavković

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksandra Slavković"}

**Aleksandra B.** (**Seša**) **Slavković** is an American statistician, a professor of statistics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), and Associate Dean for Graduate Education in the [Eberly College of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eberly_College_of_Science) at Pennsylvania State. She also chairs the Committee on Privacy and Confidentiality in Statistics of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). Her research interests include [statistical disclosure control](wikipedia://en/49680984), [algebraic statistics](wikipedia://en/17314682), and the applications of statistics in the social sciences.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Slavković majored in [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) at [Duquesne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duquesne_University), with a minor in biology, graduating in 1996. She went to [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) for her graduate studies, where she earned a master's degree in [human–computer interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human–computer_interaction) in 1999, a second master's degree in statistics in 2001, and a Ph.D. in statistics in 2004. Her dissertation, *Statistical Disclosure Limitation beyond the Margins: Characterization of Joint Distributions for Contingency Tables*, was supervised by [Stephen Fienberg](wikipedia://en/2660488).

She has been on the Pennsylvania State University faculty since 2004, with visiting positions at the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088), [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University), and [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). She is editor for the scholarly journal Statistics and Public Policy (SPP).[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Slavković became an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2012. She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2018. In 2021, she was named a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), "for novel contributions to the development of statistical disclosure techniques and algebraic methods, for contributions to graduate research, and for contributions to editorial and other publication activities of the IMS and other statistical organizations."
