# Weil–Châtelet group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weil–Châtelet group"}

In [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), the **Weil–Châtelet group** or **WC-group** of an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) such as an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) *A* defined over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *K* is the [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of [principal homogeneous spaces](wikipedia://en/363441) for *A*, defined over *K*. (txt) named it for (txt) who introduced it for [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), and (txt), who introduced it for more general groups. It plays a basic role in the [arithmetic of abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/384577), in particular for elliptic curves, because of its connection with [infinite descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_descent).

:::

It can be defined directly from [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315), as $H^1(G_K,A)$, where $G_K$ is the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of *K*. It is of particular interest for [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375) and [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358), such as [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822). For *K* a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615), (txt) proved that the Weil–Châtelet group is trivial for elliptic curves, and (txt) proved that it is trivial for any connected algebraic group.

## See also {#See_also}

The [Tate–Shafarevich group](wikipedia://en/25711736) of an abelian variety *A* defined over a number field *K* consists of the elements of the Weil–Châtelet group that become trivial in all of the completions of *K*.

The [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862), named after [Ernst S. Selmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_S._Selmer), of *A* with respect to an [isogeny](wikipedia://en/1085176) $f\colon A\to B$ of abelian varieties is a related group which can be defined in terms of Galois cohomology as

$$\mathrm{Sel}^{(f)}(A/K)=\bigcap_v\mathrm{ker}(H^1(G_K,\mathrm{ker}(f))\rightarrow H^1(G_{K_v},A_v[f])/\mathrm{im}(\kappa_v))$$

where *A*<sub>v</sub>\[*f*\] denotes the *f*-[torsion](wikipedia://en/3039330) of *A*<sub>v</sub> and $\kappa_v$ is the local Kummer map

$$B_v(K_v)/f(A_v(K_v))\rightarrow H^1(G_{K_v},A_v[f]).$$
