# Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences"}

*Image: Kyoto University RIMS.JPG.* Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences, [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University)

The **Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences** is a research institute attached to [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), hosting researchers in the [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) from all over Japan. RIMS was founded in April 1963.

:::
