# Complex line

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Complex line"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **complex line** is a [one-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-dimensional) [affine subspace](wikipedia://en/298834) of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over the [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers).[^o2][^o3] A common point of confusion is that while a complex line has [complex dimension](wikipedia://en/2783513) one over **C** (hence the term "[line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_%28mathematics%29)"), it has ordinary [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) two over the [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers) **R**, and is topologically equivalent to a [real plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_plane), not a real line.[^o4]

:::

The "[complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628)" commonly refers to the graphical representation of the complex line on the real plane, and is thus generally synonymous with the complex line, not the [complex coordinate plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_coordinate_plane).
