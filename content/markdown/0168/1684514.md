# John Wilson (English judge)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Wilson"}

**Sir John Wilson** (6 August 1741, [Applethwaite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applethwaite), [Westmorland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westmorland) – 18 October 1793, [Kendal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendal), Westmorland)[^o3] was an [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) mathematician and judge. [Wilson's theorem](wikipedia://en/227323) is named after him.

:::

Wilson attended school in [Staveley, Cumbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staveley,_Cumbria) before going up to [Peterhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhouse,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1757, where he was a student of [Edward Waring](wikipedia://en/9723). He was [Senior Wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) in 1761.[^o4] He was later knighted, and became a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1782. He was [Judge of Common Pleas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Common_Pleas_%28England%29) from 1786 until his death in 1793.
