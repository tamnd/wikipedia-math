# Infinity plus one

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinity plus one"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **infinity plus one** is a concept which has a well-defined formal meaning in some number systems, and may refer to:

:::

- [Transfinite numbers](wikipedia://en/189734), numbers that are larger than all the finite numbers.
  - [Cardinal numbers](wikipedia://en/6173), representations of sizes (cardinalities) of abstract sets, which may be infinite.
  - [Ordinal numbers](wikipedia://en/26547932), representations of order types of well-ordered sets, which may also be infinite.
- [Hyperreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51429), an extension of the real number system that contains infinite and infinitesimal numbers.
- [Surreal numbers](wikipedia://en/51432), another extension of the real numbers, contain the hyperreal and all the transfinite ordinal numbers.
