# Samuel Merrill III

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel Merrill III"}

**Samuel Merrill III** (born 1939) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [social choice theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_choice_theorist), and [political scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_scientist) best known for his work on alternative [voting systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_systems), voter behavior, party competition, and [arbitration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitration).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Merrill was raised in [Bogalusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogalusa), [Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana). He received his bachelor's degree from [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) and his Ph.D. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1965 from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) under [C. E. Rickart](wikipedia://en/45114564) with thesis *Banach Spaces of Analytic Functions*. Merrill was a professor of mathematics and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Wilkes University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilkes_University) until he retired in 2004.

Samuel Merrill is the author of three books on political science:

- *Making Multicandidate Elections More Democratic* (1988, Princeton University Press)
- *A Unified Theory of Voting* with [Bernard Grofman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Grofman) (1999, Cambridge University Press)
- *A Unified Theory of Party Competition* with James Adams and Bernard Grofman (2005, Cambridge University Press)
