# Monster Lie algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monster Lie algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **monster Lie algebra** is an [infinite-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite-dimensional) [generalized Kac–Moody algebra](wikipedia://en/1646167) acted on by the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422), which was used to prove the [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) conjectures.

:::

## Structure {#Structure}

The monster Lie algebra is a *Z<sup>2</sup>*-[graded Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/5475402). The piece of degree (*m*, *n*) has dimension *c*<sub>*mn*</sub> if (*m*, *n*) ≠ (0, 0) and dimension 2 if (*m*, *n*) = (0, 0). The [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) *c<sub>n</sub>* are the coefficients of *q*<sup>*n*</sup> of the [*j*-invariant](wikipedia://en/451988) as [elliptic modular function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_modular_function)

$$j(q) -744 = {1 \over q} + 196884 q + 21493760 q^2 + \cdots.$$

The [Cartan subalgebra](wikipedia://en/1336000) is the 2-dimensional subspace of degree (0, 0), so the monster Lie algebra has rank 2.

The monster Lie algebra has just one real [simple root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_root_%28root_system%29), given by the vector (1, −1), and the [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332) has order 2, and acts by mapping (*m*, *n*) to (*n*, *m*). The imaginary simple roots are the vectors (1, *n*) for *n* = 1, 2, 3, ..., and they have multiplicities *c*<sub>*n*</sub>.

The [denominator formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_denominator_formula) for the monster Lie algebra is the product formula for the *j*-invariant:

$$j(p) - j(q) = \left({1 \over p} - {1 \over q}\right) \prod_{n,m=1}^{\infty}(1-p^n q^m)^{c_{nm}}.$$

The denominator formula (sometimes called the Koike-Norton-Zagier infinite product identity) was discovered in the 1980s. Several mathematicians, including Masao Koike, [Simon P. Norton](wikipedia://en/1641166), and [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557), independently made the discovery.[^o3]

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

There are two ways to construct the monster Lie algebra. As it is a generalized Kac–Moody algebra whose simple roots are known, it can be defined by explicit generators and relations; however, this presentation does not give an action of the monster group on it.

It can also be constructed from the [monster vertex algebra](wikipedia://en/5032859) by using the [Goddard–Thorn theorem](wikipedia://en/730378) of [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305). This construction is much harder, but also proves that the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) acts naturally on it.[^o3]
