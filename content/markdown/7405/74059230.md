# Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem** (or just *optional decomposition theorem*) is a mathematical theorem on the decomposition of a positive [supermartingale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermartingale) $V$ with respect to a family of [equivalent martingale measures](wikipedia://en/441911) into the form

:::

$$V_t=V_0+(H\cdot X)_t-C_t,\quad t\geq 0,$$

where $C$ is an [adapted](wikipedia://en/6583420) (or [optional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optional_process)) process.

The theorem is of particular interest for [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), where the interpretation is: $V$ is the wealth process of a [trader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trader_%28finance%29), $(H\cdot X)$ is the gain/loss and $C$ the consumption process.

The theorem was proven in 1994 by Russian mathematician [Dmitry Kramkov](wikipedia://en/32350610).[^o2] The theorem is named after the [Doob-Meyer decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doob-Meyer_decomposition) but unlike there, the process $C$ is no longer [predictable](wikipedia://en/33414015) but only adapted (which, under the condition of the statement, is the same as dealing with an [optional process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optional_process)).

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem"}

## Kramkov's optional decomposition theorem {#Kramkov's_optional_decomposition_theorem}

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{A},\{\mathcal{F}_t\},P)$ be a filtered probability space with the filtration satisfying the [usual conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usual_conditions).

A $d$-dimensional process $X=(X^1,\dots,X^d)$ is *locally bounded* if there exist a sequence of [stopping times](wikipedia://en/1202193) $(\tau_n)_{n\geq 1}$ such that $\tau_n\to \infty$ [almost surely](wikipedia://en/351908) if $n\to \infty$ and $|X_t^i|\leq n$ for $1\leq i\leq d$ and $t \leq \tau_n$.

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

### Statement {#Statement}

Let $X=(X^1,\dots,X^d)$ be $d$-dimensional [càdlàg](wikipedia://en/30862839) (or RCLL) process that is locally bounded. Let $M(X)\neq \emptyset$ be the space of [equivalent local martingale measures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivalent_local_martingale_measure) for $X$ and [without loss of generality](wikipedia://en/296000) let us assume $P\in M(X)$.

Let $V$ be a positive stochastic process then $V$ is a $Q$-[supermartingale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermartingale) for each $Q\in M(X)$ if and only if there exist an $X$-integrable and predictable process $H$ and an adapted increasing process $C$ such that

$V_t=V_0 + (H\cdot X)_t-C_t,\quad t\geq 0.$[^o7][^o8]

#### Commentary {#Commentary}

The statement is still true under change of measure to an equivalent measure.
