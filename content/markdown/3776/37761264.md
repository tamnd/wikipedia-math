# Grothendieck–Teichmüller group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grothendieck–Teichmüller group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Grothendieck–Teichmüller group** *GT* is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) closely related to (and possibly equal to) the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024). It was introduced by (txt) and named after [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) and [Oswald Teichmüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Teichmüller), based on Grothendieck's suggestion in his 1984 essay *[Esquisse d'un Programme](wikipedia://en/19575137)* to study the absolute Galois group of the rationals by relating it to its action on the Teichmüller tower of Teichmüller groupoids *T*<sub>*g*,*n*</sub>, the [fundamental groupoids](wikipedia://en/55264190) of [moduli stacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_stacks) of genus *g* curves with *n* points removed.

:::

There are several variations of the group:

- a pro-*l* version, which is motivic [^o3]
- a *k*-pro-unipotent version, and
- a [profinite](wikipedia://en/24266) version, which is anabelian [^o4],[^o5];

These versions were jointly defined by [V. Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747) and [Y.Ihara](wikipedia://en/37592148).
