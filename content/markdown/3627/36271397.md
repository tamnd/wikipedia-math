# Conrad Henfling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conrad Henfling"}

**Conrad Henfling** (1648–1716 [Ansbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansbach), Germany),[^o2] [musicologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musicologist), musician, mathematician and lawyer[^o3] was an official and [privy councilor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privy_council) (Hofrat) at the court of the [Margrave of Ansbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Ansbach), Germany. He also invented a new type of keyboard for organ and [harpsichord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpsichord),[^o4] the design of which was extended by [Paul von Janko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_von_Janko) in his 1882 patent for a keyboard layout.[^o5]

:::

In a letter dated 30 August 1706, he wrote to [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281) dealing with many issues of [music theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_theory) he sets out detailed calculations for a method of [musical temperament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_temperament), using [Euclid's algorithm](wikipedia://en/10377) in his reasoning.[^o6] Leibniz had Henfling's work published as "Epistola de novo suo systemate musico" in [*Miscellanea berolinensia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miscellanea_Berolinensia#Publications), in 1710.[^o7]
