# Convex subgraph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Convex subgraph"}

*Image: 6n-graf.svg.* In this graph, triangle 1-2-5 is convex, but path 2-3-4 is not, because it does not include one of the two shortest paths from 2 to 4.

In metric [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **convex subgraph** of an undirected graph *G* is a subgraph that includes every [shortest path](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortest_path) in *G* between two of its vertices. Thus, it is analogous to the definition of a [convex set](wikipedia://en/6292) in geometry, a set that contains the line segment between every pair of its points.[^o2]

:::

Convex subgraphs play an important role in the theory of [partial cubes](wikipedia://en/30697444) and [median graphs](wikipedia://en/16970848). In particular, in median graphs, the convex subgraphs have the [Helly property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helly_property): if a family of convex subgraphs has the property that all pairwise intersections are nonempty, then the whole family has a nonempty intersection.[^o3]
