# Zdeněk Hedrlín

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdeněk Hedrlín"}

*Image: Zdenek Hedrlin.png.* Image of Zdenek Hedrlin

**Zdeněk Hedrlín** (1933 – April 22, 2018) was a Czech mathematician, specializing in [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200) and combinatorial theory, both in pure and applied mathematics.

:::

Zdeněk Hedrlín received his PhD from Prague's [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in 1963. His thesis on commutative semigroups was supervised by [Miroslav Katětov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miroslav_Katětov). Hedrlín held the title of [*Docent* (associated professor)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_the_Czech_Republic_and_in_Slovakia) at Charles University, working at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics for over 60 years until he died at age 85. He was among the first Czech mathematicians to do research on [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).[^o3]

Already in the mid-1960s, the Prague school of Zdeněk Hedrlín, Aleš Pultr and [Věra Trnková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Věra_Trnková) had devised a particularly nice notion of concrete categories over [***Set***, the so-called *functor-structured categories*](wikipedia://en/407039) ...[^o4]

In 1970 Hedrlín was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).[^o5] In the later part of his career, he focused on applications of relational structures and led very successful special and interdisciplinary seminars. Applications to biological cell behavior earned him and his students a European grant.[^o3] (He and his students worked on computational cell models of cancer.)

Hedrlín was a member of the editorial board of the *[Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Pure_and_Applied_Algebra)*.[^o6] His [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) is 1.[^o7] His doctoral students include [Vojtěch Rödl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vojtěch_Rödl) and [Svatopluk Poljak](wikipedia://en/80272750).[^o2]
