# Franklin & Marshall College Poll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franklin & Marshall College Poll"}

The **Franklin & Marshall College Poll** (formerly the **Keystone Poll**) is a Pennsylvania-based [opinion poll](wikipedia://en/277315).[^o2][^o3][^o4] The poll tracks citizen opinion on politics, public affairs, public policy, and elections in Pennsylvania.

:::

## History {#History}

The [Franklin & Marshall College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_&_Marshall_College) Poll first developed from a poll taken during the [United States Senate special election in Pennsylvania, 1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Pennsylvania,_1991), pitting incumbent Democrat [Harris Wofford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_Wofford) against former Pennsylvania governor [Dick Thornburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Thornburgh).[^o4] In 1992, the poll was formally inaugurated as the Keystone Poll at [Millersville University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millersville_University_of_Pennsylvania), with [Terry Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Madonna) working as the director of the poll and Berwood Yost, as the head methodologist.[^o4] Yost and Madonna took positions at [Franklin & Marshall College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_&_Marshall_College) in [Lancaster, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster,_Pennsylvania), in 2003, bringing the poll with them.[^o4] The poll underwent two significant changes in 2008: it was renamed the Franklin & Marshall College Poll and it was expanded beyond its Pennsylvania roots to study national opinion for 29 [Hearst-Argyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hearst-Argyle) televisions stations and other media outlets.[^o4]

During its existence, the poll has been published through a variety of media outlets.[^o6][^o4] In its early years, it was carried by the *[Philadelphia Daily News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Daily_News)* and [KYW-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KYW-TV).[^o4] Later, the [Fox 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTXF-TV) replaced KYW and the *[Harrisburg Patriot News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrisburg_Patriot_News)* and the *[Pittsburgh Tribune-Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_Tribune-Review)* began carrying the poll.[^o4] In 2002, the [Comcast Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comcast_Network) replaced [Fox 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTXF-TV). The statewide distribution now includes [WPVI-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WPVI-TV), [WGAL-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WGAL-TV),[^o7] [WTAE-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTAE-TV), and the [Times-Shamrock Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times-Shamrock_Communications).[^o4]

The Center for Opinion Research at Franklin & Marshall College conducts the F&M Poll. As a department of the college, the Center is both non-profit and non-partisan. The Center is directed by Berwood Yost, who also now directs the F&M Poll, since Terry Madonna has retired. Mr. Yost also works closely with Stephen Medvic, the Honorable and Mrs. John C. Kunkel Professor of Government at F&M, who is the director of F&M’s Center for Politics and Public Affairs.
