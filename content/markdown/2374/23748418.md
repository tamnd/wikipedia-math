# Guido Zappa

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guido Zappa"}

**Guido Zappa** (7 December 1915 – 17 March 2015) was an Italian mathematician and a noted [group theorist](wikipedia://en/41890): his other main research interests were [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and also the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). Zappa was particularly known for some examples of [algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curves) that strongly influenced the ideas of [Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/1565716).[^o3]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

### Honors {#Honors}

He was elected ordinary non-resident member of the [Accademia Pontaniana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Pontaniana) on June 16, 1949.[^o6] On June 3, 1951, he was elected the corresponding member to the class of mathematical sciences of the [Società Nazionale di Scienze Lettere e Arti in Napoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Società_Nazionale_di_Scienze_Lettere_e_Arti_in_Napoli): subsequently, he became an ordinary member (2 June 1951) and ordinary non-resident member (15 December 1953).[^o7] On 14 October 1960 he was elected corresponding member of the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_dei_Lincei): he became national member of the same [academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy) on March 21, 1977.[^o8]

- Zappa, Guido. (1970). "Fondamenti di teoria dei gruppi. Volume secondo". 18. Edizioni Cremonese. p. XXI+411. mr:258926. zbl:0201.03001. This book is the second part of a monograph in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) dealing extensively with many of its aspects.
- Zappa, Guido. (1984). "La scuola matematica di Francesco Severi intorno al 1940". *Rivista di Matematica della Università di Parma*. 10\*. p. 11–14. mr:0777309. zbl:0562.01015. <http://rivista.math.unipr.it/fulltext/1984-10s/1984-10s-011.pdf>. This work describes the research activity at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and at the (at that time newly created) "[Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/34249626)" from the end of the 1930s to the early 1940s.
