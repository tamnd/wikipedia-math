# Elisha Netanyahu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elisha Netanyahu"}

[Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netanyahu)

**Elisha Netanyahu** ((**Mileikowsky**);אֱלִישָׁע נְתַנְיָהוּ; December 21, 1912 – April 3, 1986) was a Polish-born Israeli mathematician specializing in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). Over the course of his work at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion), he was the Dean of the Faculty of Sciences and established the separate Department of Mathematics. He was the brother of historian [Benzion Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzion_Netanyahu) and the uncle of current Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Elisha Mileikowsky (later Netanyahu), was born in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland), to Sarah (Lurie) and the Russian Jewish writer and [Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionist) activist [Nathan Mileikowsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Mileikowsky). He was the third of nine children. In 1920, the family immigrated to [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_%28region%29). The family eventually settled in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem) and adopted [Hebrew name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_name) Netanyahu.

Elisha Netanyahu went to the Reali School in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), from which he graduated in 1930. He later returned to Reali in 1935 to teach mathematics there. He studied at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), from which he received his BS, MA and PhD (1942). His advisors were [Michael Fekete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fekete) and [Binyamin Amirà](wikipedia://en/60040792). After the graduation he joined the [British Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army) as a volunteer, serving in [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt) and then in Italy as an officer in a unit of the [Royal Engineering Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Engineers). He specialized in preparation of maps, which he continued to do during the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab–Israeli_War).

After he was demobilized in 1946, he became a lecturer at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion). He rose to a professor in 1958, and later became the head of the Mathematics Section, then as Dean of the Faculty of Sciences. His administrative efforts also played an important role towards establishment of the [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev).

He had long term visits at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (1953–54), [NYU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) (1961), the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico) (1969), the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (1973), and [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) (1979). In 1980, Netanyahu retired from the Technion and moved to Jerusalem, where he died of cancer in 1986.

Throughout his long career, Netanyahu collaborated with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), [Charles Loewner](wikipedia://en/9337483) and other leading mathematicians, continuing and expanding the analytical traditions at the Technion.

### Personal life {#Personal_life}

He was the brother of [Benzion Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzion_Netanyahu), a professor of [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History), and the uncle of the [Prime Minister of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Israel), [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu). In 1949 Netanyahu married [Shoshana Shenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoshana_Netanyahu), his former student at the Reali, who later became the second female justice at the [Israel Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Supreme_Court). They had two children: [Nathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Netanyahu) (b. 1951), a professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University), and Dan (b. 1954), an [information systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_systems) auditor.

## Elisha Netanyahu Memorial Lectures {#Elisha_Netanyahu_Memorial_Lectures}

The *Elisha Netanyahu Memorial Lecture Series* was established by his family: His brother Amos Milo (Mileikowsky), his wife, his children and the Technion to honor the memory in 1987 with the first lecture by Paul Erdős. In other years, the speakers included [Lars Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636), [Robert Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Aumann), [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972), [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475), [Charles Fefferman](wikipedia://en/502831), [Samuel Karlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Karlin), [David Kazhdan](wikipedia://en/3052833), [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725), [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498), [Wendelin Werner](wikipedia://en/6607797), and [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557).
