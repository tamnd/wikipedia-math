# SIMD (hash function)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SIMD"}

**SIMD** is a [cryptographic hash function](wikipedia://en/439526) based on the [Merkle–Damgård construction](wikipedia://en/4076217) submitted to the [NIST hash function competition](wikipedia://en/20051952) by Gaëtan Leurent. It is one of fourteen entries to be accepted into round two of the competition,[^o2] but was not shortlisted for the third and final round.[^o3]

:::

The designer states that the "most important component of SIMD is its message expansion, which is designed to give a high minimal distance". The algorithm's speed is claimed to be 11–13 [cycles per byte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cycles_per_byte).[^o4]
