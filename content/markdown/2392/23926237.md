# Zsolt Baranyai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zsolt Baranyai"}

**Zsolt Baranyai** (June 23, 1948 in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest) – April 6, 1978) was a Hungarian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

He graduated from [Fazekas High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazekas_Mihály_Gimnázium_%28Budapest%29) where he was a classmate of [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938), [Miklós Laczkovich](wikipedia://en/6021299), and [Lajos Pósa](wikipedia://en/22874054). He studied mathematics at [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) and went on to become a lecturer in the Analysis Department. He earned his Ph.D. in 1975 and was posthumously awarded the [Candidate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1978.

Baranyai is best known for his [theorem](wikipedia://en/24011805) on the decompositions of complete [hypergraphs](wikipedia://en/232535), which solved a long-standing open problem.[^o2] In addition to his mathematical pursuits, Baranyai was also a professional musician who played the [recorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recorder_%28musical_instrument%29). He died while touring Hungary with the Bakfark Consort in a car accident after a concert.[^o3]
