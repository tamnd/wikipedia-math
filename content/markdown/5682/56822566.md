# Gabriele Kaiser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriele Kaiser"}

**Gabriele Kaiser** is a German [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471). She is a professor of mathematics education at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).

:::

Kaiser completed a doctorate in 1986 and a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1997 at the [University of Kassel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kassel). Her doctoral dissertation, *Anwendungen im Mathematikunterricht - Konzeptionen und Untersuchungen zur unterrichtlichen Realisierung*, was supervised by Werner Blum. She became a professor at Hamburg in 1998, and served as vice dean of education from 2010 to 2016. Since 2005 she has been editor-in-chief of the journal *ZDM Mathematics Education*, and she is the editor or co-editor of 28 books on mathematics education.

Kaiser was a speaker at the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). In 2012, a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) was published in her honor.
