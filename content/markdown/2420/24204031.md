# Robert Bryant (mathematician)

*Image: BoysSurfaceTopView.PNG.* Robert Bryant, working with R. Kusner, found this [parametrization](wikipedia://en/453198) of [Boy's surface](wikipedia://en/61361) which minimizes the [Willmore energy](wikipedia://en/2072431)[^o2]

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Bryant"}

**Robert Leamon Bryant** (born August 30, 1953) is an American mathematician. He works at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) and specializes in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bryant grew up in a farming family in [Harnett County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnett_County,_North_Carolina) and was a [first-generation college student](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-generation_college_students_in_the_United_States).[^o17] He obtained a bachelor's degree at [North Carolina State University at Raleigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University_at_Raleigh) in 1974 and a PhD at [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 1979. His thesis was entitled "*Some Aspects of the Local and Global Theory of Pfaffian Systems*" and was written under the supervision of [Robert Gardner](wikipedia://en/47107406).[^o6]

He worked at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) for seven years, as assistant professor (1979–1981), associate professor (1981–1982) and full professor (1982–1986). He then moved to [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), where he worked for twenty years as J. M. Kreps Professor.

Between 2007 and 2013 he worked as full professor at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he served as the director of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI).[^o7] In 2013 he returned to Duke University as Phillip Griffiths Professor of Mathematics.

Bryant was awarded in 1982 a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o8] In 1986 he was [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#1986,_Berkeley) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berkeley.[^o9]

He was elected in 2002 a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences),[^o10] in 2007 a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o11] in 2013 a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o12] and in 2022 a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o13][^o14] He is also a member of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), the [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356) and the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o15]

He served as the [president of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822#Presidents) for the 2-years term 2015–2016,[^o16][^o17] for which he was the first openly gay president.[^o17][^o18]

Bryant is on the board of directors of [EDGE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EDGE_Program), a transition program for women entering graduate studies in the mathematical sciences.[^o19] He is also a board member of [Spectra](wikipedia://en/61935783), an association for LGBT mathematicians that he helped to create.[^o20][^o21]

## Research {#Research}

Bryant's research has been influenced by [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274), [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905), and [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414).[^o17] His research interests cover many areas in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), [geometry of PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564), [Finsler geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_geometry) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o23]

In 1987 he proved several properties of surfaces of unit constant [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210) in [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742), which are now called [Bryant surfaces](wikipedia://en/13622958) in his honour.[^o24] In 2001 he contributed many advancements to the theory of Bochner-Kähler metrics, the class of [Kähler metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kahler_metrics) whose Bochner curvature vanishes.[^o25]

In 1987 he produced the first examples of [Riemannian metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) with exceptional holonomy (i.e. whose [holonomy groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holonomy_groups) are [G<sub>2</sub>](wikipedia://en/292864) or [Spin(7)](wikipedia://en/411231)); this showed that every group in [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363)'s classification can arise as a holonomy group.[^o26] Later, he also contributed to the classification of exotic holonomy groups of arbitrary (i.e. non-Riemannian) [torsion](wikipedia://en/4342484)-free [affine connections](wikipedia://en/607690).[^o27][^o28]

Together with [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414) and others co-authors, Bryant developed the modern theory of [Exterior Differential Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_differential_system), writing two influential monographs, which have become the standard reference in the topic.[^o29][^o30] He also worked on their [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060)[^o31][^o32] and applications to PDEs.[^o33][^o34]

He is author of more than 60 papers,[^o35][^o36] and he has supervised 26 PhD students.[^o6]

- *Exterior Differential Systems*, MSRI Publ. 18, Springer Verlag 1991, (with [Robert Brown Gardner](wikipedia://en/47107406), [S. S. Chern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Chern), H. L. Goldschmidt and [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414))
- *Exterior Differential Systems and Euler-Lagrange Partial Differential Equations*, Chicago Lectures in Mathematics, University of Chicago Press 2003, (with Phillip Griffiths and Dan Grossman)[^o38]
- *Integral Geometry*, Contemporary Mathematics 63, AMS 1987 (editor with [Victor Guillemin](wikipedia://en/13236636), [Sigurdur Helgason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigurdur_Helgason_%28mathematician%29), R. O. Wells)
- *An introduction to [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and symplectic geometry*, in *Geometry and quantum field theory*, IAS/Park City Math. Series 1, American Mathematical Society 1995, pp. 5–181
- *Toward a Geometry of Differential Equations*, in: *Geometry, Topology & Physics*, Conf. Proc. Lecture Notes Geom. Topology, VI, International Press, Cambridge, MA, 1995, pp. 1–76 (with Lucas Hsu and Phillip Griffiths)

Bryant and [David Morrison](wikipedia://en/40896470) are the editors of vol. 4 of the *Selected Works of Phillip Griffiths*.
