# Nature Reviews Methods Primers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nature Reviews Methods Primers"}

***Nature Reviews Methods Primers*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) and online-only [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Nature Portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Portfolio). Established in 2021,[^o3] it covers [review articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_article) on analytical, applied, [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685), theoretical and [computational](wikipedia://en/1181008) methods used in the [life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_life_sciences) and [physical sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_of_physical_science).[^o4] , its [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Joseph Willson.[^o5]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Chemical Abstracts Core](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Abstracts_Core)[^o8]
- [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences[^o7]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o7]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o8]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2024 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 56.[^o9]
