# Bierlein's measure extension theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bierlein's measure extension theorem"}

**Bierlein's measure extension theorem** is a result from [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) on extensions of [probability measures](wikipedia://en/45437). The theorem makes a statement about when one can extend a probability measure to a larger [σ-algebra](wikipedia://en/29586). It is of particular interest for infinite dimensional spaces.

:::

The theorem is named after the German mathematician [Dietrich Bierlein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietrich_Bierlein), who proved the statement for [countable](wikipedia://en/6026) families in 1962.[^o2] The general case was shown by [Albert Ascherl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Ascherl) and [Jürgen Lehn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jürgen_Lehn) in 1977.[^o3]

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="A measure extension theorem of Bierlein"}

## A measure extension theorem of Bierlein {#A_measure_extension_theorem_of_Bierlein}

Let $(X,\mathcal{A},\mu)$ be a probability space and $\mathcal{S}\subset \mathcal{P}(X)$ be a σ-algebra, then in general $\mu$ can not be extended to $\sigma(\mathcal{A}\cup \mathcal{S})$. For instance when $\mathcal{S}$ is countably infinite, this is not always possible. Bierlein's extension theorem says, that it is always possible for disjoint families.

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

### Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Bierlein's measure extension theorem is

*Let $(X,\mathcal{A},\mu)$ be a probability space, $I$ an arbitrary index set and $(A_i)_{i\in I}$ a family of disjoint sets from $X$. Then there exists a extension $\nu$ of $\mu$ on $\sigma(\mathcal{A}\cup\{A_i\colon i\in I\} )$.*

:::

### Related results and generalizations {#Related_results_and_generalizations}

Bierlein gave a result which stated an implication for uniqueness of the extension.[^o2] Ascherl and Lehn gave a condition for equivalence.[^o3]

[Zbigniew Lipecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbigniew_Lipecki) proved in 1979 a variant of the statement for [group](wikipedia://en/19447)-valued measures (i.e. for "topological hausdorff group"-valued measures).[^o9]

:::
