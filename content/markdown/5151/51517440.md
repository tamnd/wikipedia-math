# Pomeranchuk's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pomeranchuk's theorem"}

**Pomeranchuk's theorem**, named after [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) physicist [Isaak Pomeranchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaak_Pomeranchuk), states that difference of cross sections of interactions of [elementary particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_particles) $\kappa_1+\kappa_2$ and $\kappa_1+\bar{\kappa_2}$ (i. e. particle ${\kappa_1}$ with particle $\kappa_2$, and with its [antiparticle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiparticle) $\bar{\kappa_2}$) approach 0 when $s \to \infty$, where $s$ is the energy in center of mass system.

:::
