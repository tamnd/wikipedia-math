# Volume conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Volume conjecture"}

**Volume conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Volume conjecture"}

In the branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) called [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **volume conjecture** is an [open problem](wikipedia://en/1333335) that relates [quantum invariants](wikipedia://en/22624170) of knots to the [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) of their [complements](wikipedia://en/1350120).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *O* denote the [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075). For any knot $K$, let $\langle K \rangle_N$ be the **Kashaev invariant** of $K$, which may be defined as

$$\langle K \rangle_N=\lim_{q\to e^{2\pi i/N}}\frac{J_{K,N}(q)}{J_{O,N}(q)},$$

where $J_{K,N}(q)$ is the $N$-[Colored Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642#Colored_Jones_polynomial) of $K$. The volume conjecture states that[^n1]

$$\lim_{N\to\infty} \frac{2\pi\log |\langle K \rangle_N|}{N} = \operatorname{vol}(S^3 \backslash K),$$

where $\operatorname{vol}(S^3 \backslash K)$ is the [simplicial volume](wikipedia://en/11197892) of the complement of $K$ in the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792), defined as follows. By the [JSJ decomposition](wikipedia://en/228599), the complement $S^3 \backslash K$ may be uniquely decomposed into a system of tori

$$S^3 \backslash K = \left( \bigsqcup_i H_i \right) \sqcup \left( \bigsqcup_j E_j \right)$$

with $H_i$ [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/1237700) and $E_j$ [Seifert-fibered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seifert-fibered). The **simplicial volume** $\operatorname{vol}(S^3 \backslash K)$ is then defined as the sum

$$\operatorname{vol}(S^3 \backslash K) = \sum_i \operatorname{vol}(H_i),$$

where $\operatorname{vol}(H_i)$ is the [hyperbolic volume](wikipedia://en/8158628) of the hyperbolic manifold $H_i$.[^n1]

As a special case, if $K$ is a [hyperbolic knot](wikipedia://en/1248299), then the JSJ decomposition simply reads $S^3 \backslash K = H_1$, and by definition the simplicial volume $\operatorname{vol}(S^3 \backslash K)$ agrees with the hyperbolic volume $\operatorname{vol}(H_1)$.

:::

## History {#History}

The Kashaev invariant was first introduced by Rinat M. Kashaev in 1994 and 1995 for hyperbolic links as a state sum using the theory of [quantum dilogarithms](wikipedia://en/31601344).[^o8][^o9] Kashaev stated the formula of the volume conjecture in the case of hyperbolic knots in 1997.[^o10]

(Murakami Murakami 2001) pointed out that the Kashaev invariant is related to the [colored Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642#Colored_Jones_polynomial) by replacing the variable $q$ with the root of unity $e^{i\pi/N}$. They used an [R-matrix](wikipedia://en/8498996) as the [discrete Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/8811) for the equivalence of these two descriptions. This paper was the first to state the volume conjecture in its modern form using the simplicial volume. They also prove that the volume conjecture implies the following conjecture of [Victor Vasiliev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Vasiliev):

If all [Vassiliev invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassiliev_invariant) of a knot agree with those of the unknot, then the knot is the unknot.

The key observation in their proof is that if every Vassiliev invariant of a knot $K$ is trivial, then $J_{K,N}(q) = 1$ for any $N$.

## Status {#Status}

The volume conjecture is open for general knots, and it is known to be false for arbitrary links. The volume conjecture has been verified in many special cases, including:

- The [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565) (Tobias Ekholm),[^n2]
- The [three-twist knot](wikipedia://en/26643955) (Rinat Kashaev and Yoshiyuki Yokota),[^n2]
- The [Borromean rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borromean_ring) ([Stavros Garoufalidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavros_Garoufalidis) and [Thang Le](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thang_Le)),[^n2]
- [Torus knots](wikipedia://en/1165182) (Rinat Kashaev and Olav Tirkkonen),[^n2]
- All knots and links with volume zero ([Roland van der Veen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_van_der_Veen)),[^n2]
- Twisted Whitehead links ([Hao Zheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hao_Zheng)),[^o18]
- [Whitehead doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehead_double) of nontrivial torus knots $T(p,q)$ with $q=2$ (Hao Zheng).[^o18]

## Relation to Chern-Simons theory {#Relation_to_Chern-Simons_theory}

Using complexification, (Murakami Murakami Okamoto Takata 2002) conjectured that for a hyperbolic knot $K$,

$$\lim_{N\to\infty} \frac{2\pi\log \langle K\rangle_N}{N} = \operatorname{vol}(S^3\backslash K) + CS(S^3\backslash K),$$

where $CS$ is the [Chern–Simons invariant](wikipedia://en/391908) of the frame field of the hyperbolic structure of $K$. This suggests a relationship between the colored Jones polynomial and complexified Chern–Simons theory.
