# Katherine Halvorsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Katherine Halvorsen"}

**Katherine Taylor Halvorsen** is an American statistician and [statistics educator](wikipedia://en/24985094) whose research topics have included [statistical significance](wikipedia://en/160995) for [contingency tables](wikipedia://en/935515), and the [conditional logistic regression](wikipedia://en/45447625) method for analysis of multiple risk factors in [case–control studies](wikipedia://en/788661). She was co-author of four editions of *Mathematics Education in the United States*, a quadrennial review publication of the [National Council of Teachers of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1561691), and serves on the Mathematical Sciences Academic Advisory Committee of the [College Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Board).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Halvorsen is a graduate of the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). After earning master's degrees from [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) and in 1978, the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), she completed her Ph.D. in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in 1984 at the [Harvard School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_School_of_Public_Health), with the dissertation *Estimating Population Parameters Using Information from Several Independent Sources* supervised by [Frederick Mosteller](wikipedia://en/6125615). She is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) of mathematics and statistics at [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College), where she was founding director of the Five College Statistics Program in 2011.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2008.
