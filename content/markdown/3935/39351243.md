# Camillo De Lellis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Camillo De Lellis"}

**Camillo De Lellis** (born 11 June 1976) is an Italian mathematician who is active in the fields of [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [hyperbolic systems of conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_law_%28physics%29), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694) and [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034). He is a permanent faculty member in the School of Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o3] He's a faculty member of the Mathematics area at the [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Sasso_Science_Institute).[^o4] He was also one of the two managing editors of [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Prior joining the faculty of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), De Lellis was a professor of mathematics at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) from 2004 to 2018.[^o6][^o7] Before this, he was a postdoctoral researcher at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506).[^o8] He received his PhD in mathematics from the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) at [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), under the guidance of [Luigi Ambrosio](wikipedia://en/25337146) in 2002.

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

De Lellis has given a number of remarkable contributions in different fields related to [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). In [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694) he has been interested in the study of regularity and singularities of minimising hypersurfaces, pursuing a program aimed at disclosing new aspects of the theory started by [Almgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_J._Almgren,_Jr.) in his ["Big regularity paper"](wikipedia://en/33077295).[^o10][^o11] There Almgren proved his famous [regularity theorem](wikipedia://en/33077295) asserting that the singular set of an *m*-dimensional mass-minimizing surface has dimension at most *m* − 2. De Lellis has also worked on various aspects of the theory of hyperbolic systems of conservation laws and of incompressible fluid dynamics. In particular, together with [László Székelyhidi Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/László_Székelyhidi_Jr.), he has introduced the use of convex integration[^o12] methods and [differential inclusions](wikipedia://en/13725281) to analyse non-uniqueness issues for weak solutions to the [Euler equation](wikipedia://en/396022).[^o13]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

De Lellis has been awarded the [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal) in 2009, the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) in 2013 and the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) in 2014.[^o15] He has been [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010 and plenary speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in 2012.[^o16] In 2012 he has also been awarded a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) grant.[^o17] In 2020 he has been awarded the [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872).[^o18][^o19] In 2021 he became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o20] He has also been included in the list of invited plenary speakers of the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg).[^o21] In 2022, he was awarded the [Maryam Mirzakhani Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/28306582) from the [NAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o22] In 2026, he was awarded the [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in mathematical sciences.[^o23]
