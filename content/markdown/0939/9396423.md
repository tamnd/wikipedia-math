# Regina Tyshkevich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regina Tyshkevich"}

**Regina Iosifovna Tyshkevich** (Рэгіна Іосіфаўна Тышкевіч; 20 October 1929 – 17 November 2019[^o3]) was a [Belarusian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), an expert in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences), [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of the [Belarusian State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_State_University).[^o4][^o5]

:::

Her main scientific interests included [Intersection graphs](wikipedia://en/7726759), [degree sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_sequence), and the [reconstruction conjecture](wikipedia://en/255594). She was also known for an independent introduction and investigation of the class of [split graphs](wikipedia://en/9928681) and for her contributions to [line graphs of hypergraphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_graphs_of_hypergraphs).

In 1998, she was awarded the Belarus State Prize for her book *Lectures in Graph Theory*.[^o4] Of note is her textbook *An Introduction into Mathematics*, written together with her two colleagues.

In October 2009 an international conference "Discrete Mathematics, Algebra, and their Applications", sponsored by the [Central European Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Initiative), was held in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk), Belarus in honor of her 80th anniversary.[^o6]

Regina Tyshkevich was a direct descendant of the [Tyszkiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyszkiewicz_family) [magnate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnate) family, therefore her colleagues sometimes called her "the countess of graph theory", which is a [pun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pun) in the [Russian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): the Russian word "граф" (graf) is a [homonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homonym) for two words meaning "[count](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Count)" and "[graph](wikipedia://en/325806)".[^o4]
