# Stefan Bergman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan Bergman"}

**Stefan Bergman** (5 May 1895 – 6 June 1977) was a [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Poland)-born American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose primary work was in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). He is known for the [kernel function](wikipedia://en/10273855) he discovered in 1922 at [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). This function is now known as the [Bergman kernel](wikipedia://en/3205923). Bergman taught for many years at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Częstochowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Częstochowa), [Congress Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_Poland), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), to a German Jewish family, Bergman received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at University of Berlin in 1921, for a [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) on [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659). His advisor, [Richard von Mises](wikipedia://en/821517), had a strong influence on him, lasting for the rest of his career.[^o5] In 1933, Bergman was forced to leave his post at the Berlin University because he was a [Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews). He fled first to [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia), where he stayed until 1939, and then to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). In 1939, he emigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), where he would remain for the rest of life.[^o5] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1951.[^o6] He was a professor at Stanford University from 1952 until his retirement in 1972.[^o7] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 1950 in Cambridge, Massachusetts[^o8] and in 1962 in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm) (*On meromorphic functions of several complex variables*).[^o9] He died in [Palo Alto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California), aged 82.

### Bergman Prize {#Bergman_Prize}

The [Stefan Bergman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bergman_Prize) in mathematics was initiated by Bergman's wife in her will, in memory of her husband's work. The [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) supports the prize and selects the committee of judges.[^o11] The prize is awarded for:[^o11]

1. the theory of the [kernel function](wikipedia://en/10273855) and its applications in real and complex analysis; or
1. function-theoretic methods in the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) of [elliptic type](wikipedia://en/550137) with a special attention to Bergman's and related operator methods.
