# James Cogdell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Cogdell"}

**James Wesley Cogdell** (born 22 September 1953) is an American mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He graduated from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1977 with a bachelor's degree and in 1981 with a Ph.D. His doctoral dissertation *Arithmetic Quotients of the Complex 2-Ball and Modular Forms of Nebentypus* was supervised by [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738). Cogdell was a postdoc at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) and the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). He was from 1982 to 1988 an assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). At [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University) he was from 1987 to 1988 assistant professor, from 1988 to 1994 an associate professor, and from 1994 to 2004 a full professor (from 1999 as Southwestern Bell Professor, from 2000 as Regents Professor, and from 2003 as Vaughan Foundation Professor). In 2004 he became a professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).[^o4]

In autumn 1983 and for the academic year 1999–2000 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He has held visiting positions at [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa), at [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), and at the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematical_Physics) (where he gave the 2009 Erwin Schrödinger Lecture).[^o4]

Cogdell works on [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283), [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) (within the context of the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905)), and analytic number theory. In collaboration with Piatetski-Shapiro, he proved [converse theorems](wikipedia://en/29554685) for L-functions for the [general linear groups](wikipedia://en/113564) $GL_n$. The goal is to characterize the L-functions that originate from automorphic forms. For $GL_2$ this was solved by [Hervé Jacquet](wikipedia://en/25600925) and [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986) and for $GL_3$ by Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and [Joseph Shalika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Shalika). The problem goes back to [Erich Hecke](wikipedia://en/1233990)'s characterization of the [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258) that come from [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000).

In 2002 Cogdell was, with Piatetski-Shapiro, an Invited Speaker with talk *Converse theorems, functoriality and applications to number theory* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing).[^o5] He was an editor, with [Simon Gindikin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Gindikin) and [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak), for *Selected Works of Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro* (2000, [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822)).[^o6]

Cogdell was elected in 2012 a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o7] and in 2016 a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o8]
