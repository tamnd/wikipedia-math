# Kristina Reiss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kristina Reiss"}

*Image: Kristina Reiss.jpg.* Kristina Reiss

**Kristina Reiss** (born 1952) is a German [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471). She is professor of mathematics education and Emerita of Excellence at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich), where she held the Heinz Nixdorf Chair of Mathematics Education from 2009 until 2021.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Reiss studied mathematics beginning in 1971 at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), completing her doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) there in 1980. Her dissertation, *Eine allgemeinere Kennzeichnung der sporadischen einfachen Gruppe von Rudvalis*, concerned [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and was supervised by [Zvonimir Janko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvonimir_Janko).

She worked as a researcher at the [Karlsruhe University of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_University_of_Education) from 1980 until 1991, when she became a professor of mathematics at the [Stuttgart Technology University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart_Technology_University_of_Applied_Sciences). She moved in 1992 to the [University of Flensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Flensburg), in 1997 to the [University of Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oldenburg), in 2002 to the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg), and in 2005 to [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) as a professor of mathematics and computer science education. In 2009, she became Heinz Nixdorf Professor of Mathematics Education at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (TUM). Between 2014 and 2021, she has been dean of the TUM School of Education. Kristina Reiss was director of the PISA study in Germany between 2014 and 2021.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2011 Reiss joined [Acatech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acatech), the German Academy of Science and Engineering.
