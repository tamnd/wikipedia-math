# Bradley Thiessen (statistician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Bradley Thiessen"}

**Bradley Adam Thiessen** is an American statistician and academic administrator. He served as the interim president of the [New College of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College_of_Florida) from January 2023 to March 2023.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Thiessen earned a bachelor's degree in secondary mathematics education at the [St. Ambrose University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Ambrose_University).[^o5] He earned a master's degree and Ph.D. in educational measurement and statistics from the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa).[^o5] His 2008 dissertation was titled, *Relationship between test security policies and test score manipulations*.[^o6] Timothy Neri Ansley was his doctoral advisor.[^o6]

Thiessen worked at the St. Ambrose University for 14 years as the university assessment coordinator and a faculty member in the department of mathematics and statistics.[^o7] He joined the [New College of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College_of_Florida) in July 2016 as the president's chief of staff, and a professor of statistics.[^o5][^o8] In August 2016, he became its first director of institutional performance assessment.[^o5] On January 2, 2020, he became the director of institutional research at the [University of Hawaiʻi at Hilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaiʻi_at_Hilo).[^o8] On January 31, 2023, Thiessen was appointed as the interim president of the [New College of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College_of_Florida) by the board of trustees, succeeding [Patricia Okker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Okker).[^o9] He served until March 2023 when he was replaced by [Richard Corcoran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Corcoran).[^o9]

In July 2024, Thiessen joined Florida Polytechnic University as interim provost.[^o10] On May 8, 2025, he was named Provost and Vice President for Academic Affairs at Florida Polytechnic University.[^o11]
