# Module of covariants

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Module of covariants"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), given an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) *G*, a [*G*-module](wikipedia://en/12695) *M* and a *G*-algebra *A*, all over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *k*, the **module of covariants** of type *M* is the $A^G$-module

:::

$$(M \otimes_k A)^G.$$

where $-^G$ refers to taking the elements fixed by the action of *G*; thus, $A^G$ is the [ring of invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_invariants) of *A*.
