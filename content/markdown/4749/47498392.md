# 110-vertex Iofinova–Ivanov graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="110-vertex Iofinova–Ivanov graph"}

The **110-vertex Iofinova–Ivanov graph** is, in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a [semi-symmetric](wikipedia://en/2554240) [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) with 110 vertices and 165 edges. The graph is named after [Marina Evgenievna Iofinova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Evgenievna_Iofinova) and [Alexander A. Ivanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_A._Ivanov), who constructed this graph in 1985 alongside four other graphs, with 126, 182, 506, and 990 vertices, with special symmetries.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

[Marina Evgenievna Iofinova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Evgenievna_Iofinova) and [Alexander A. Ivanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_A._Ivanov) proved in 1985 the existence of five and only five semi-symmetric cubic bipartite graphs whose automorphism groups act [primitively](wikipedia://en/2739252) on each partition.[^o5] The smallest has 110 vertices. The others have 126, 182, 506 and 990.[^o6] The 126-vertex Iofinova–Ivanov graph is also known as the [Tutte 12-cage](wikipedia://en/24384368). According to Ivanov, during their initial work on the graph, Iofinova believed that "This work will not make us famous." By 2025, their paper would go on to be Ivanov's second most cited work, followed by their paper with [Cheryl E. Praeger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl_E._Praeger) on [Affine 2-transitive graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_2-transitive_graphs), although Iofinova had died in 1999.[^o7]

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetries"}

### Symmetries {#Symmetries}

The Iofinova-Ivanov graph is significant as it was specifically constructed to be [acted on](wikipedia://en/12781) by a [symmetry group](wikipedia://en/28186) $G$ satisfying four properties. First, the graph $\Omega$ can [partitioned](wikipedia://en/340240) into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) $\Omega_1$ and $\Omega_2$ where the [stabilizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizer_%28group_theory%29) of an element in one partition has an orbit length of 3 on the elements in the other partition, and vice versa. Second, no non-trival [equivalence relation](wikipedia://en/9259) on the partitions are preserved by the group. Third, any [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027) on the graph that preserves the aforementioned length 3 relation will preserve the partions. Finally, the group must be [self-normalizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-normalizing) on the [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) on $\Omega$; any permutation that normalizes the group $G$ is necessarily in the group.[^o7]

A group $G$ that acts on a set $\Omega$ satisfying the mentioned properties must be one of five groups, up to [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828): $PGL_2(11)$, $G_2(2)$, $PGL_2(13)$, $PSL_2(23)$, and $\text{Aut}(M_{12})$. Here, $PGL_2(p)$ denotes the [Projective general linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_general_linear_group) with entries in the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of order $p$, $PSL_2(p)$ is the [Projective special linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) again with entries in the corresponding [field](wikipedia://en/10603), while $G_2(q)$ is the [Dickson group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dickson_group), and $\text{Aut}(M_{12})$ is the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [Mathieu group $M_{12}$](wikipedia://en/15200695). The corresponding sets that these groups act on are graphs with 110, 126, 182, 506, and 990 vertices respectively, and the Iofinova-Ivanov graph corresponds to the smallest of these graphs.[^o7]

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

### Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the 110-vertex Iofina-Ivanov graph is $(x-3) x^{20} (x+3) (x^4-8 x^2+11)^{12} (x^4-6 x^2+6)^{10}$. The symmetry group of the 110-vertex Iofina-Ivanov is the [projective linear group](wikipedia://en/382667) PGL<sub>2</sub>(11), with 1320 elements.[^o12]

## Semi-symmetry {#Semi-symmetry}

Few graphs show semi-symmetry: most edge-transitive graphs are also vertex-transitive. The smallest semi-symmetric graph is the [Folkman graph](wikipedia://en/23761072), with 20 vertices, which is 4-regular. The three smallest cubic semi-symmetric graphs are the [Gray graph](wikipedia://en/2554738), with 54 vertices, this the smallest of the Iofina-Ivanov graphs with 110, and the [Ljubljana graph](wikipedia://en/24131381) with 112.[^o14][^o15] It is only for the five Iofina-Ivanov graphs that the symmetry group acts primitively on each partition of the vertices.
