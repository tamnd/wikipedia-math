# Svetlozar Rachev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Svetlozar Rachev"}

*Image: ZRachev-photo.jpg.* Rachev in 2008

**Svetlozar** "**Zari**" **Todorov Rachev** is a professor at Texas Tech University who works in the field of [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680), probability theory, and statistics. He is known for his work in probability metrics, [derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_%28finance%29) pricing, [financial risk modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_risk_modeling), and [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390). In the practice of risk management, he is the originator of the methodology behind the flagship product of FinAnalytica.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Rachev earned a MSc degree from the Faculty of Mathematics at [Sofia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_University) in 1974, a PhD degree from [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of Vladimir Zolotarev in 1979, and a Dr Sci degree from [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) in 1986 under the supervision of [Leonid Kantorovich](wikipedia://en/194660), a Nobel Prize winner in economic sciences, [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161) and [Yuri Prokhorov](wikipedia://en/6787407).[^o3] Currently, he is Professor of Financial Mathematics at [Texas Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Tech_University).[^o4]

In mathematical finance, Rachev is known for his work on application of non-[Gaussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian) models for risk assessment, option pricing, and the applications of such models in [portfolio theory](wikipedia://en/734434).[^o5] He is also known for the introduction of a new risk-return ratio, the "[Rachev Ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachev_ratio)", designed to measure the reward potential relative to [tail risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tail_risk) in a non-Gaussian setting.[^o6][^o7][^o8]

In probability theory, his books on probability metrics and [mass-transportation](wikipedia://en/6631661) problems are widely cited.[^o9]

## FinAnalytica {#FinAnalytica}

Rachev's academic work on non-Gaussian models in mathematical finance was inspired by the difficulties of common classical Gaussian-based models to capture empirical properties of financial data.[^o5][^o6] Rachev and his daughter, Borjana Racheva-Iotova, established Bravo Group in 1999, a company with the goal to develop software based on Rachev's research on [fat-tailed models](wikipedia://en/4357461). The company was later acquired by FinAnalytica.
