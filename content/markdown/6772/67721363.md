# Teresa Melo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Teresa Melo"}

**Maria Teresa Rocha de Magalhães Melo** (born 1966) is a Portuguese mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476) who works as a professor in the business school of the Saarland University of Applied Sciences (htw saar), a [Fachhochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fachhochschule) in [Saarbrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarbrücken), Germany. Her research interests include [facility location](wikipedia://en/10469986), [supply chain management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_chain_management), and in-hospital [patient transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_transport).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Melo was born in 1966 in [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon), and studied applied mathematics at the [University of Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lisbon), graduating in 1989 and earning a master's degree there in statistics and operations research in 1991. Next, she went to the Econometric Institute of [Erasmus University Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_University_Rotterdam) for doctoral study, completing a PhD in operations research in 1996 with a dissertation concerning the [economic lot scheduling problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_lot_scheduling_problem).

She took postdoctoral research positions at the [Forschungszentrum Jülich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forschungszentrum_Jülich) and Fraunhofer Institute for Industrial Mathematics ITWM in 1997 and 1999 respectively, and became an invited lecturer at the [Technical University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Kaiserslautern) in 2000. She moved to htw saar as an associate professor in 2007, and became a full professor in 2011. At htw saar, she is also founding co-director of the Institute for Supply Chain and Operations Management.
