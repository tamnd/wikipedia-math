# Anna Marciniak-Czochra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Marciniak-Czochra"}

**Anna Marciniak-Czochra** (born 11 March 1974 in [Lublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lublin), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)) is a Polish [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [mathematical biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_biology). Since 2011 she has been a professor of applied mathematics in the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Sciences at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University).[^o3]

:::

She earned her [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in mathematics 1998 at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (with honors) in Mathematics 2004 at the Heidelberg University.[^o4][^o5]

Since 2016 she has been leader of applied analysis and modelling in biosciences at the Heidelberg University.[^o6]
