# William Pitt Durfee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Pitt Durfee"}

**William Pitt Durfee** (5 February 1855 – 17 December 1941) was an American mathematician who introduced [Durfee squares](wikipedia://en/27753472). He was a student of [James Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861), and after obtaining his degree in 1883 he became a professor at [Hobart college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobart_and_William_Smith_Colleges) in 1884 and became dean in 1888. [Durfee House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durfee_House) and Durfee Hall are named in his honor.

:::
