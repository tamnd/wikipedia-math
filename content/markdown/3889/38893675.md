# Rhombihexaoctagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombihexaoctagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombihexaoctagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of rr{8,6}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual tiling, called a **deltoidal hexaoctagonal tiling** represent the fundamental domains of \*4232 symmetry, a half symmetry of [\[8,6\], (\*862)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/862_symmetry) as \[8,1<sup>\+</sup>,6\].

*Image: 862\_symmetry\_0a0.png.*

:::

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are fourteen hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular order-6 octagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 7 forms with full \[8,6\] symmetry, and 7 with subsymmetry.
