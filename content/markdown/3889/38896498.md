# Snub hexaoctagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub hexaoctagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub hexaoctagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are three [triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_%28geometry%29), one [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733), and one [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *sr{8,6}*.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are fourteen hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular order-6 octagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 7 forms with full \[8,6\] symmetry, and 7 with subsymmetry.
