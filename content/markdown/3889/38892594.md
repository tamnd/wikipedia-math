# Truncated order-6 octagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-6 octagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-6 octagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{8,6}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Truncated\_order-6\_octagonal\_tiling\_with\_mirrors.png.* Truncated order-6 octagonal tiling with mirror lines,

The dual to this tiling represent the fundamental domains of \[(8,8,3)\] (\*883) symmetry. There are 3 small index subgroup symmetries constructed from \[(8,8,3)\] by mirror removal and alternation. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors.

The symmetry can be doubled as [862 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/862_symmetry) by adding a mirror bisecting the fundamental domain.
