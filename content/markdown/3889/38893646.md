# Hexaoctagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hexaoctagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **hexaoctagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Constructions"}

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are four uniform constructions of this tiling, three of them as constructed by mirror removal from the \[8,6\] [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope). Removing the mirror between the order 2 and 4 points, \[8,6,1<sup>\+</sup>\], gives \[(8,8,3)\], (\*883). Removing the mirror between the order 2 and 8 points, \[1<sup>\+</sup>,8,6\], gives \[(4,6,6)\], (\*664). Removing two mirrors as \[8,1<sup>\+</sup>,6,1<sup>\+</sup>\], leaves remaining mirrors (\*4343).

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual tiling has [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V6.8.6.8, and represents the fundamental domains of a quadrilateral kaleidoscope, [orbifold](wikipedia://en/2915678) (\*4343), shown here. Adding a 2-fold gyration point at the center of each rhombi defines a (2\*43) orbifold. These are subsymmetries of [\[8,6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/862_symmetry)\].
