# National Association of Mathematicians

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="National Association of Mathematicians"}

The **National Association of Mathematicians** is a professional association for mathematicians in the US, especially [African Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Americans) and other minorities.[^o3] It was founded in 1969.[^o4]

:::

## Lectures {#Lectures}

NAM hosts five lecture series that honor African American mathematicians.[^o6]

Two of these take place at the annual [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143) of the American Mathematical Society. The Cox-Talbot Lecture, named after [Elbert Frank Cox](wikipedia://en/2814552) and [Walter Richard Talbot](wikipedia://en/66228834), is an hour-long lecture that takes place during the NAM Banquet. The Claytor-Woodard Lecture at the JMM was inaugurated in 1980 and is named after [Dudley Weldon Woodard](wikipedia://en/39765925) and [William Waldron Schieffelin Claytor](wikipedia://en/58667460).[^o9] Each year a speaker is chosen on the basis of their work to improve opportunities in mathematical research for underrepresented American minorities. Past speakers include Ryan Hynd, [Monica Jackson](wikipedia://en/79276996), [Chelsea Walton](wikipedia://en/58828548), [Suzanne Weekes](wikipedia://en/53726284), and Henok Mawi.

## History {#History}

At the 1969 Joint Mathematics Meetings in New Orleans, seventeen mathematicians met on Sunday January 26 to begin a new organization:[^o16]

1. [James Ashley Donaldson](wikipedia://en/66475288), faculty at University Illinois at Chicago
1. Samuel Horace Douglas, faculty at Grambling College
1. Henry Madison Eldridge, faculty at Fayetteville State College
1. [Thyrsa Anne Frazier-Svager](wikipedia://en/53810333), faculty at Central State University
1. Richard Griego, faculty at the University of New Mexico at Albuquerque
1. Johnny Lee Houston, faculty at Stillman College and graduate student at Purdue University
1. Curtis Jefferson, faculty at Cuyahoga Community College
1. [Vivienne Malone-Mayes](wikipedia://en/40351523), faculty at Baylor University
1. Theodore Portis, faculty at Alabama State University
1. Charles R. Smith, faculty at Paine College
1. Robert S. Smith, graduate student at Pennsylvania State University
1. Beauregard Stubblefield, faculty at Texas Southern University
1. Henry Thaggert, faculty at Jarvis Christian College
1. [Walter Richard Talbot](wikipedia://en/66228834), faculty at Morgan State College
1. Argelia Valez-Rodriquez, faculty at Bishop College
1. Harriet Rose Junior Walton, faculty at & Morehouse College
1. [Scott W. Williams](wikipedia://en/10709770), graduate student at Lehigh University
