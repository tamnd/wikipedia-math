# Rudy Horne

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rudy Horne"}

**Rudy Lee Horne** (1968 – 2017) was an American mathematician and professor of mathematics at [Morehouse College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morehouse_College). He worked on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), including [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) waves. He was the mathematics consultant for the film *[Hidden Figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Figures)*.[^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Horne grew up in the [south side of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_side_of_Chicago).[^o9] His father worked at [Sherwin-Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherwin-Williams).[^o5] He graduated from [Crete-Monee High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crete-Monee_High_School).[^o6][^o7] He completed a [double degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_degree) in mathematics and physics at the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma) in 1991.[^o8][^o9] He joined the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) for his postgraduate studies, earning a master's in physics in 1994 and in mathematics in 1996. He completed his doctorate, *Collision induced timing jitter and four-wave mixing in wavelength division multiplexing soliton systems*, in 2001 which was supervised by [Mark J. Ablowitz](wikipedia://en/18578839).[^o2][^o10] He was the first African American to graduate from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) Department of Applied Mathematics.[^o11]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After completing his PhD, Horne had a position at the [California State University, East Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_East_Bay).[^o6] before working as [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), with Chris Jones.[^o13] Horne joined [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) in 2005.[^o11][^o14] Horne joined [Morehouse College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morehouse_College) in 2010 and was promoted to associate professor of mathematics in 2015.[^o6] He continued to study [four-wave mixing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-wave_mixing).[^o15] His work considered nonlinear optical phenomena.[^o13][^o16][^o17] He uncovered effects in [parity-time symmetric systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parity-time_symmetry).[^o18]

Horne was recommended to serve as a mathematics consultant for [Hidden Figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Figures) by [Morehouse College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morehouse_College).[^o19][^o20] He worked closely with [Theodore Melfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Melfi) ensured the actors knew how to pronounce "Euler's".[^o6][^o21][^o22][^o23][^o24][^o25] He spent four months working with [20th Century Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Century_Fox).[^o11] In particular, Horne worked with [Taraji P. Henson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraji_P._Henson) on the mathematics she required for her role as [Katherine Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Johnson).[^o26][^o27] He taught the cast how to get excited by mathematics.[^o28] His handwriting is on screen during a scene at the beginning of the film where [Katherine Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Johnson) solves a quadratic equation.[^o9] He appeared on the interview series *In the Know.*[^o29] Horne completed a [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) *Maths Fest* tour where he discussed the mathematics in [Hidden Figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Figures), focusing on the calculations that concerned Glenn's orbit around the Earth in 1962.[^o30][^o31] He appeared on [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR)'s Closer Look.[^o32]

He died on December 11, 2017, after surgery for a torn [aorta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aorta).[^o33][^o34][^o6] The [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) established a Rudy Lee Horne Memorial Fellowship in his honour.[^o11][^o35] He was described as a "rock star", inspiring generations of black students.[^o36][^o26] He was awarded the [National Association of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/58449356) (NAM) lifetime achievement award posthumously in 2018,[^o37] and was recognized by [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) as a Black History Month 2018 Honoree.[^o5]
