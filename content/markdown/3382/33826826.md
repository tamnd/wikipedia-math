# John Trevor Stuart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Trevor Stuart"}

**(John) Trevor Stuart** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (29 January 1929 – 17 December 2023)[^o3] was a mathematician and senior research investigator at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London)[^o5] working in theoretical [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), [hydrodynamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamic) stability of [fluid flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_flow) and nonlinear [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Stuart was educated [Gateway Grammar School, Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gateway_College)[^o3] and [Imperial College of Science and Technology, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) where he was awarded a Bachelor of Science degree in 1949.[^o3] He continued his study at Imperial College and in 1953 was awarded a Ph.D. based on *Stability of Viscous Motion for Finite Disturbances*.

## Career {#Career}

Stuart joined the Aeronautics Division of the National Research Laboratory, returning to join the staff of Imperial College after a few years. He was appointed professor of theoretical fluid mechanics in 1966 and was head of the Department of Mathematics from 1974 to 1979 and from 1983 to 1986. He was Dean of the [Royal College of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Science) from 1990 to 1993. He was an emeritus professor at Imperial until he died in 2023.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Stuart is known for his work on [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) [waves](wikipedia://en/33516) in the onset of [turbulence](wikipedia://en/154664) in [fluids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid). He also extended the work of [Lord Rayleigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Rayleigh) with research into steady streaming in unsteady [viscous flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscous_flow) at high [Reynolds numbers](wikipedia://en/23868856).[^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Stuart was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1974 and awarded the [Otto Laporte Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Laporte_Award) in 1985 and the [Senior Whitehead Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Whitehead_Prize) in 1984. He also holds honorary [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degrees from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) and the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia). He was the editor of the [Biographical Memoirs of Fellows of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biographical_Memoirs_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society) from 2012 to 2016.[^o12][^o13]
