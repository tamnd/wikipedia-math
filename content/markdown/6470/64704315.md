# Bohuslav Diviš

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bohuslav Diviš"}

**Bohuslav Diviš** (December 20, 1942 – July 26, 1976) was a Czech mathematician, who worked in the field of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o2][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bohuslav Diviš was born on December 20, 1942, in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague). He won the Czechoslovak and [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1959 and then studied mathematics at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in Prague (as a student of [Vojtěch Jarník](wikipedia://en/2175894)). He wrote his thesis in 1966 and his doctorate in 1969 with a thesis on "superlattice points in multidimensional ellipsoids" at the [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) under [Peter Roquette](wikipedia://en/41199245).[^o2]

In 1970 Diviš became assistant professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) (USA), and after 1973 an associate professor. He authored about 20 scientific articles.

On July 26, 1976, during a conference visit to [Illinois State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_State_University) in [Normal, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal,_Illinois), he died of heart failure at the age of 33.
