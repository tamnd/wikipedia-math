# FrontierMath

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FrontierMath"}

**FrontierMath** is a [test bed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_bed) to [benchmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benchmark_%28computing%29)[^o2] various [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) systems in their attempts to solve 14 bespoke[^o3] heretofore unexamined mathematical problems[^o4] (none of which are on the scale of the [Millennium Problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Problems)). It was established by the [non-profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonprofit_organization) research organization Epoch AI in November 2024.[^o5] The first such open problem—of the "moderately interesting" rank—to be solved was in [hypergraph](wikipedia://en/232535) theory: "A Constant-Factor Lower Bound For *H* (*n)*" by [GPT-5.4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.4).[^o6]

:::
