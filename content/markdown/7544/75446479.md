# Collar neighbourhood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Collar neighbourhood"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **collar neighbourhood** of a [manifold with boundary](wikipedia://en/2073470) $M$ is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood) of its boundary $\partial M$ that has the same structure as $\partial M \times [0, 1)$.

:::

Formally, if $M$ is a [differentiable manifold with boundary](wikipedia://en/2119219), $U \subset M$ is a *collar neighbourhood* of $M$ whenever there is a [diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564) $f : \partial M \times [0, 1) \to U$ such that for every $x \in \partial M$, $f (x, 0) = x$.[^o2] Since $[0, 1)$ is diffeomorphic to $[0, \infty)$, it is equivalent to take a diffeomorphism $f : \partial M \times [0, \infty) \to U$.[^o3]

Every differentiable manifold has a collar neighbourhood.[^o2][^o3]
