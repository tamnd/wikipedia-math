# Journal of Business & Economic Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Business & Economic Statistics"}

The ***Journal of Business & Economic Statistics*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o3] The journal covers a broad range of applied problems in [business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_statistics) and [economic statistics](wikipedia://en/340363), including [forecasting](wikipedia://en/246074), [seasonal adjustment](wikipedia://en/7579959), applied demand and cost analysis, applied [econometric modeling](wikipedia://en/1397536), empirical finance, analysis of [survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_survey) and [longitudinal data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_data) related to business and economic problems, the impact of discrimination on wages and productivity, the returns to education and training, the effects of [unionization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unionization), and applications of stochastic [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) to business and economic problems.[^o4][^o5]
