# Stylianos Pichorides

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stylianos Pichorides"}

**Stylianos Konstantinos Pichorides** (Στυλιανός Κωνσταντίνος Πιχωρίδης, 18 October 1940, [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens) – 18 June 1992, [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid)) was a Greek mathematician, specializing in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147).

:::

After graduating from secondary school in Athens, Pichorides matriculated at the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens), where he graduated in 1963 with a degree in electrical engineering. He then worked as an electrical engineer in Athens, but also studied mathematics and received in 1968 a scholarship to study at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o2] There in 1971 he received his Ph.D. with thesis *On the best values of the constants in the theorems of M. Riesz, Zygmund and Kolmogorov*[^o3] written under the supervision of [Antoni Zygmund](wikipedia://en/362477). In 1972 Pichorides returned to Athens and worked at the [National Centre of Scientific Research "Demokritos"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Centre_of_Scientific_Research_"Demokritos"), where he was employed until 1983, with interruptions by leave of absence. He was from 1974 to 1979 an *attaché de recherché* of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay), a visiting professor from 1979 to 1980 at the [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in Orsay, and from 1980 to 1981 a visiting professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). He organized, with Nicholas Petridis and [Nicholas Varopoulos](wikipedia://en/37551761), a successful conference on harmonic analysis in [Iraklion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraklion) in 1978. From 1983 until his death in 1992, Pichorides was a professor at the [University of Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Crete)'s mathematics department, which he co-founded. He held visiting professorships at Paris-Sud University in Orsay, Caltech, and the University of Chicago. For the academic year 1991–1992 he was a visiting professor at the [University of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cyprus). He had short stays at the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452), the University of Cambridge, Brown University, and the University of Chicago. He died unexpectedly while attending a conference in Spain in 1992.[^o2][^o5]

Pichorides is known for results on inequalities in the theory of Fourier series. In 1980 he received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) for his research on Littlewood's conjecture on a lower bound for averaged exponential sums.[^o2] Research by Pichorides and others provided the basis for the 1981 proof by [Sergei Vladimirovich Konyagin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Vladimirovich_Konyagin) of Littlewood's conjecture on the lower bound.[^o6][^o7]

The [Foundation for Research & Technology – Hellas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_for_Research_&_Technology_–_Hellas) (FORTH) has funded the Pichorides Postgraduate Scholarship[^o8] and the Pichorides Distinguished Lectureship.[^o9]
