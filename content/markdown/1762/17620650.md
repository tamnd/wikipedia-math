# Martin Kneser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Kneser"}

**Martin Kneser** (21 January 1928 – 16 February 2004) was a German mathematician. His father [Hellmuth Kneser](wikipedia://en/1368525) and grandfather [Adolf Kneser](wikipedia://en/1368530) were also mathematicians.

:::

He obtained his PhD in 1950 from the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) with the dissertation *"Über den Rand von Parallelkörpern*". His advisor was [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191).

His name has been given to [Kneser graphs](wikipedia://en/2724154) which he studied in 1955. He also gave a simplified proof of the [Fundamental theorem of algebra](wikipedia://en/51414).

Kneser was an invited speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 1962 in Stockholm.[^o3] His main publications were on [quadratic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_forms) and [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups).
