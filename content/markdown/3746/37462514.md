# Luna's slice theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luna's slice theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Luna's slice theorem**, introduced by (Luna 1973), describes the local behavior of an action of a [reductive algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) on an [affine variety](wikipedia://en/320468). It is an analogue in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) of the theorem that a [compact Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group) [acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) *X* has a slice at each point *x*, in other words a subvariety *W* such that *X* looks locally like *G*×<sub>*G*<sub>*x*</sub></sub> *W*. (see [slice theorem (differential geometry)](wikipedia://en/39906398).)

:::
