# Olaf Storaasli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Olaf Storaasli"}

**Olaf O. Storaasli** is a scientist & engineer who worked at [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_Langley_Research_Center)<https://larcalumni.org/about-the-laa/>), [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Ridge_National_Laboratory)<https://www.ornl.gov/group/ft>), [Centrus Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrus_Energy), & [Synective Labs](http://www.synective.se). At NASA, he led a hardware, software & applications teams to successfully develop one of NASA's first parallel computers, the [finite element machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element_machine), & developed rapid matrix equation algorithms tailored for high-performance computers to harness [FPGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FPGA) & GPU accelerators to solve science & engineering applications. He was a graduate advisor & instructor at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee), [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) & [Christopher Newport University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Newport_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Storaasli received a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in physics, mathematics, & French at [Concordia College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_College_%28Moorhead%29) (1964). He went on to earn an [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [University of South Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Dakota) (1966) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [engineering mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_mechanics) at [NCSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University) (1970). He was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Norwegian University of Science & Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_&_Technology) (<https://web.archive.org/web/20180117131542/http://73.121.39.212:8080/~olaf/OS/www/nasa/Thompson.jpg> (1984–85\]) & the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh)([2008](https://olaftn.com/NASA/Awards/2008epcc.pdf)).

## Research {#Research}

He developed, tested and documented [parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Computing) analysis software to speed [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) equation solution to simulate physical & biological behavior on advanced-computer architectures (e.g. NASA's [GPS](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20040086655/downloads/20040086655.pdf) solver based on prior [Finite element machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element_machine) and rapid parallel analysis of [Space Shuttle SRB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Solid_Rocket_Booster) redesign earned [Cray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray)'s 1st GigaFLOP Performance [Award](https://web.archive.org/web/20090226230512/http://www.geocities.com/olafva/SRB.jpg) at Supercomputing '89).
