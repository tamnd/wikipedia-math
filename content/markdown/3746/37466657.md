# Igusa variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igusa variety"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **Igusa curve** is (roughly) a coarse [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) in characteristic *p* with a level *p* **Igusa structure**, where an Igusa structure on an elliptic curve *E* is roughly a point of order *p* of *E*<sup>(*p*)</sup> generating the kernel of *V* : *E*<sup>(*p*)</sup> → *E*. An **Igusa variety** is a higher-dimensional analogue of an Igusa curve. Igusa curves were studied by [Igusa](wikipedia://en/33588064)[^n1] and Igusa varieties were introduced by [Harris](wikipedia://en/31954452) and [Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931)[^n2] for studying the [bad reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_reduction) of some [Shimura varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimura_varieties): the [$\ell$-adic cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ℓ-adic_cohomology) of Igusa varieties sheds some light on that of Shimura varieties.

:::
