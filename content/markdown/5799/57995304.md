# Dmitri Burago

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitri Burago"}

**Dmitri Yurievich Burago** (Дмитрий Юрьевич Бураго, born May 17, 1964) is a leading Russian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [differential](wikipedia://en/8625), [Riemannian](wikipedia://en/195243), [Finsler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler) geometry, [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), and applications to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

He is the son of the celebrated [Geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer) and Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Yuri Burago](wikipedia://en/8688682), with whom he also published well known book on metric geometry. Burago studied at [45th Physics-Mathematics School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Physics-Mathematics_School). Burago received his doctorate in 1994 at [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) under the supervision of [Anatoly Vershik](wikipedia://en/9036441). He was at the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) in Saint Petersburg and is now a professor at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University)'s Center for Dynamical Systems and Geometry.

In 1992, he was awarded the prize of the [Saint Petersburg Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9103238). In 1998, he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o3] In 2014, he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) with Yuri Burago and [Sergei Vladimirovich Ivanov](wikipedia://en/64802622) for their book *A course in metric geometry*.[^o4]
