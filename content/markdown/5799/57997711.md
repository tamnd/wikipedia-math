# Sergei B. Kuksin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei B. Kuksin"}

*Image: Kuksin sergei.jpg.* Sergei Kuksin, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2008

**Sergei Borisovich Kuksin** (Сергей Борисович Куксин, born 2 March 1955) is a French and Russian mathematician, specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) (PDEs).

:::

Kuksin received his doctorate under the supervision of [Mark Vishik](wikipedia://en/33293921) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1981. He was at the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) in Moscow and at the [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) and is a *directeur de recherché* (senior researcher) at the Institut Mathématiques de Jussieu of the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (Paris VII).

His research deals with [KAM theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser_Theorem) in partial differential equations (*i.e.* infinite dimensional Hamiltonian systems);[^o3] partial differential equations involved with random perturbations, turbulence and statistical hydrodynamics; and elliptic PDEs for functions between compact manifolds.

In 1992 he was an invited speaker with talk *KAM theory for partial differential equations* at the European Congress of European Mathematicians in Paris. In 1998 he was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o4] In 2016 he received the Lyapunov Prize from the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).
