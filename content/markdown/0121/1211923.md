# Bryant Tuckerman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bryant Tuckerman"}

**Louis Bryant Tuckerman III** (November 28, 1915 – May 19, 2002) was an American mathematician. He was a member of the team that developed the [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978) (DES).[^o3]

:::

He studied [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), where he invented the [Tuckerman traverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuckerman_traverse) method for revealing all the faces of a [flexagon](wikipedia://en/98316).[^o3]

On March 4, 1971, he discovered the 24th [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908),[^o4][^o5] a [titanic prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanic_prime), with a value of

$$2^{19937}-1.$$
