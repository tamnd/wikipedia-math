# Centered heptagonal number

*Image: Centered heptagonal number.svg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Centered heptagonal number"}

A **centered heptagonal number** is a [centered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centered_number) [figurate number](wikipedia://en/325260) that represents a [heptagon](wikipedia://en/365349) with a dot in the center and all other dots surrounding the center dot in successive heptagonal layers. The centered heptagonal number for *n* is given by the formula

:::

$${7n^2 - 7n + 2}\over2.$$

The first few centered heptagonal numbers are

[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), [8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_%28number%29), [22](wikipedia://en/362193), [43](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43_%28number%29), [71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71_%28number%29), [106](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/106_%28number%29), [148](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/148_%28number%29), [197](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/197_%28number%29), 253, 316, 386, 463, 547, 638, 736, 841, 953

## Centered heptagonal prime {#Centered_heptagonal_prime}

A **centered heptagonal prime** is a centered heptagonal number that is [prime](wikipedia://en/23666). The first few centered heptagonal primes are

43, 71, 197, 463, 547, 953, 1471, 1933, 2647, 2843, 3697, ...

The centered heptagonal [twin prime](wikipedia://en/41997) numbers are

43, 71, 197, 463, 1933, 5741, 8233, 9283, 11173, 14561, 34651, ...
