# 1990 Indonesian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1990 Indonesian census"}

The **1990 Indonesian census** was held on 31 October 1990, and recorded a population of 179 million people within the country. However, a revised figure of 179,378,946 people was the official result. Its population density during the 1990 census was 179378946 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>. In some provinces, estimates were made, like the notably then-secessionist [Aceh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh), and the census was criticized internationally for significant underreporting.

:::

The census also recorded ethnicity. A total of 101 ethnic groups were identified officially, but the real number was estimated to be in the thousands.
