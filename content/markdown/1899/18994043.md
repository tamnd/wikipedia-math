# Walter Benz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Benz"}

*Image: Walter Benz.jpeg.* Walter Benz in 1975(photo from the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669))

**Walter Benz** (May 2, 1931 [Lahnstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahnstein) – January 13, 2017 [Ratzeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratzeburg)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), an expert in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).[^o2]

:::

Benz studied at the [Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg_University_of_Mainz) and received his [doctoral degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_degree) in 1954, with Robert Furch as his advisor. After a position at the [Johann Wolfgang Goethe University Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Wolfgang_Goethe_University_Frankfurt_am_Main), he served as a professor at [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum), [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), and [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). Benz was honoured with the degree of a [Dr. h.c.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree)

Based on his book *Vorlesungen über Geometrie der Algebren* ([Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) 1973), certain geometric objects are called [Benz planes](wikipedia://en/17750374).

[Inner product spaces](wikipedia://en/14856) over the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) provide the basis of a 2007 book by Benz: *Classical Geometries in Modern Contexts*.[^o4]
