# Max Kelly

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Kelly"}

**Gregory Maxwell "Max" Kelly** (5 June 1930 – 26 January 2007) was an Australian mathematician who worked on [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kelly was born in [Bondi, New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondi,_New_South_Wales), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia), on 5 June 1930. He obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) in 1957, publishing his first paper in that area in 1959, *Single-space axioms for homology theory*.[^o4] He taught in the Pure Mathematics department at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney) from 1957 to 1966, rising from lecturer to reader.[^o4] During 1963–1965 he was a visiting fellow at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) and the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign),[^o4] where with [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501) he formalized and developed the notion of an [enriched category](wikipedia://en/142622) based on intuitions then in the air about making the [homsets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hom_set) of a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) just as abstract as the objects themselves.

He subsequently developed the notion in considerably more detail in his 1982 monograph *Basic Concepts of Enriched Category Theory*.[^o6] Let $\cal V$ be a [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604), and denote by $\cal V$-Cat the category of $\cal V$-enriched categories. Among other things, Kelly showed that $\cal V$-Cat has all weighted limits and colimits even when $\cal V$ does not have all ordinary [limits and colimits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limits_and_colimits). He also developed the enriched counterparts of [Kan extensions](wikipedia://en/2502518), density of the [Yoneda embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoneda_embedding), and essentially algebraic theories.

In 1967 Kelly was appointed Professor of Pure Mathematics at the [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales).[^o4] In 1972 he was elected a [Fellow of the Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science). He returned to the University of Sydney in 1973, serving as Professor of Mathematics until his retirement in 1992.[^o4] In 2001 he was awarded the Australian government's [Centenary Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenary_Medal). He continued to participate in the department as professorial fellow and professor emeritus until his death at age 76 on 26 January 2007.[^o4]

Kelly worked on many other aspects of category theory besides enriched categories, both individually and in a number of collaborations. His PhD students include [Ross Street](wikipedia://en/41038878).[^o5]
