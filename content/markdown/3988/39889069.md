# Marie-Françoise Roy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marie-Françoise Roy"}

**Marie-Françoise Roy** (born 28 April 1950 in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989)) is a French [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians) noted for her work in [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198). She has been Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes) since 1985 and in 2009 was made a *Chevalier* of the French [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour).[^o3] In 2004, she received an [Irène Joliot-Curie Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Roy works in [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198) in particular real spectra and, most recently, in complexity of [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms) in real algebraic geometry and also the applications.[^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Marie-Françoise Roy got her education at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) [de jeunes filles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_jeunes_filles) and was an assistant professor at [Université Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_13_University) during 1973.[^o7] She received her PhD at [Université Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_13_University) in 1980, supervised by [Jean Bénabou](wikipedia://en/70081438).

From 1981 she spent two years at [Abdou Moumouni University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdou_Moumouni_University) in [Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger). In 1985 she became a professor at [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes) in [Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennes), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France).

## Service {#Service}

Roy was president of [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France) from 2004 to 2007.[^o10]

In 1986, Roy was one of the founders[^o11] of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) (EWM), and was the convenor (president) of EWM 2009–2013.[^o12] In 1987 she co-founded the French organization for women in mathematics, [Femmes et Mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'association_femmes_et_mathématiques), and became the organization's first president.

Roy is scientific officer for Sub-Saharan Africa in Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées, CIMPA.[^o13] Roy is president of Association d'Echanges Culturels Cesson Dankassari (Tarbiyya-Tatali) an organization working for joint activities in a commune [Dan-Cassari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan-Kassari) in [Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger) and the French commune [Cesson-Sévigné](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cesson-Sévigné).

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In December 2022, Roy was awarded an [Honorary Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Doctorate) of Science (DSc) by the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath). The award recognises Marie-Françoise's work both in algebraic geometry, but also 'as a leading activist for the cause of women in mathematics, and \[as\] one of the most prominent and effective advocates for African mathematics.'[^o15]
