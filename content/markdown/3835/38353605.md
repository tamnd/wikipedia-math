# Truncated order-8 triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-8 triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-8 triangular tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are two [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and one [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{3,8}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual of this tiling represents the fundamental domains of \*443 symmetry. It only has one subgroup 443, replacing mirrors with gyration points.

This symmetry can be doubled to [832 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/832_symmetry) by adding a bisecting mirror to the fundamental domain.
