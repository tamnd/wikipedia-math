# Truncated octagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated octagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated octagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There is one [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) and two [hexakaidecagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexakaidecagon) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *t*{8,3}.

:::

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling has face configuration V3.16.16.

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

This hyperbolic tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (3.2n.2n), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are ten hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular octagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.
