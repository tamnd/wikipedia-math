# Order-5 pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 pentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-5 pentagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {5,5}, constructed from five pentagons around every vertex. As such, it is [self-dual](wikipedia://en/8815#Self-dual_polytopes_and_tessellations).

:::

## Related tilings {#Related_tilings}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra and tilings with vertex figure (5<sup>n</sup>).
