# Broken space diagonal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Broken space diagonal"}

In a [magic cube](wikipedia://en/372356), a **broken space diagonal** is a sequence of cells of the cube that follows a line parallel to a [space diagonal](wikipedia://en/1337505) of the cube, and continues on the corresponding point of an opposite face whenever it reaches a face of the cube.[^o2][^o3] The corresponding concept in two-dimensional magic squares is a [broken diagonal](wikipedia://en/3231123).

:::
