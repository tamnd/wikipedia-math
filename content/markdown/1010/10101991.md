# Newton's inequalities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Newton's inequalities"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Newton inequalities** refer to a set of [mathematical inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489) related to [mathematical series](wikipedia://en/15287). These inequalities are named after [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627) who proved the theorem in 1707.[^o2] Suppose *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *a*<sub>*n*</sub> are non-negative [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) and let $e_k$ denote the *k*th [elementary symmetric polynomial](wikipedia://en/1440695) in *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *a*<sub>*n*</sub>. Then the **elementary symmetric means**, given by

:::

$$S_k = \frac{e_k}{\binom{n}{k}},$$

satisfy the [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489)

$$S_{k-1}S_{k+1} \le S_k^2.$$

Equality holds [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) all the numbers *a*<sub>*i*</sub> are equal.

It can be seen that *S*<sub>1</sub> is the [arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/612), and *S*<sub>*n*</sub> is the *n*-th power of the [geometric mean](wikipedia://en/13046).
