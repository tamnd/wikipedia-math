# McLaughlin graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="McLaughlin graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **McLaughlin graph** is a [strongly regular graph](wikipedia://en/1399856) with parameters (275, 112, 30, 56) and is the only such graph.

:::

The [group theorist](wikipedia://en/41890) Jack McLaughlin discovered that the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of this graph had a subgroup of index 2 which was a previously undiscovered [finite simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group), now called the [McLaughlin sporadic group](wikipedia://en/18365236).

The automorphism group has [rank 3](wikipedia://en/29763000), meaning that its [point stabilizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_stabilizer) subgroup divides the remaining 274 vertices into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29). Those orbits contain 112 and 162 vertices. The former is the [colinearity graph of the generalized quadrangle](wikipedia://en/3947006#Graphs) GQ(3,9). The latter is a strongly regular graph called the [local McLaughlin graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_McLaughlin_graph).
