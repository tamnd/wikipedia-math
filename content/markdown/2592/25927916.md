# Octagrammic antiprism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Octagrammic antiprism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **octagrammic antiprism** is one in an infinite set of nonconvex [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms) formed by triangle sides and two regular [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) caps, in this case two [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613).

:::
