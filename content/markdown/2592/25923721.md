# Great complex icosidodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great complex icosidodecahedron"}

Uniform polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great complex icosidodecahedron** is a degenerate [uniform star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8464397). It has 12 vertices, and 60 (doubled) edges, and 32 faces, 12 [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544) and 20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654). All edges are doubled (making it degenerate), sharing 4 faces, but are considered as two overlapping edges as topological polyhedron.

:::

It can be [constructed](wikipedia://en/2950777) from a number of different [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719).

## As a compound {#As_a_compound}

The great complex icosidodecahedron can be considered a [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_polyhedron) of the [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843), {5/2,5}, and [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898), {3,5/2}, sharing the same vertices and edges, while the second is hidden, being completely contained inside the first.
