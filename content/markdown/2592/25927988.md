# Decagrammic antiprism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decagrammic antiprism"}

*Image: Decagrammic antiprism.stl.* 3D model of a (uniform) decagrammic antiprism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **decagrammic antiprism** is one in an infinite set of nonconvex [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms) formed by triangle sides and two regular [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) caps, in this case two [decagrams](wikipedia://en/19546742).

:::
