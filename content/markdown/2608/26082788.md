# Charalambos Aliprantis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charalambos Aliprantis"}

**Charalambos Dionisios Aliprantis** (Χαράλαμπος Διονύσιος Αλιπράντης; May 12, 1946 – February 27, 2009) was a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks)-American [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and mathematician who introduced [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) and [Riesz space](wikipedia://en/8099018) methods in economic theory. He was born in Cefalonia, Greece in 1946 and immigrated to the US in 1969, where he obtained his PhD in mathematics from [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) in June 1973.

:::

He was a distinguished professor of Economics and Mathematics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). He was the founding editor of the journals *[Economic Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_Theory_%28journal%29)* and *Annals of Finance*, an Editor of *Positivity* and a founding member of the [Society for the Advancement of Economic Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_the_Advancement_of_Economic_Theory).
