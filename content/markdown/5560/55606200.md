# Rudolf Kochendörffer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Kochendörffer"}

**Rudolf Paul Joachim Kochendörffer** (21 November 1911 in [Pankow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pankow) – 23 August 1980 in [Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dortmund)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics in the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) specialising in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and theory of [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and their representation.[^o4] During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Kochendörffer worked as a mathematical [cryptanalyst](wikipedia://en/5715) in the mathematical referat of Inspectorate 7/IV, that would later become part of Referat I of Group IV of the [General der Nachrichtenaufklärung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_der_Nachrichtenaufklärung) (abbr. GDNA), the [signals intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) agency of the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) and was known as a cryptographic tester of the [Enigma](wikipedia://en/9256) cipher machine.[^o5] Kochendörffer was a Member of the Scientific Advisory Council for Mathematics at the State Secretariat for the Higher and Specialist Schools of the GDR, a staff member of [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646) and collaborated with the [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rudolf Kochendörffer's father, Albert Kochendörffer(1877-1958) was a lending bookseller (Leihbuchhändler). His mother was Bertha Kochendörffer.

## Education {#Education}

In 1930, Kochendörffer completed his [Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur). Kochendörffer subsequently studied at the [Technische Hochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule) in Charlottenburg (now [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)) from 1930 to 1936 on the study of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

In 1936, he was promoted to [Dr Phil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy), with a doctoral thesis titled: *Investigations on a presumption of [W. Burnside](wikipedia://en/251883)* (German:Untersuchungen über eine Vermutung von W. Burnside) ([Burnside's theorem](wikipedia://en/1912650)). His doctoral advisors where [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186), [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522) and [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191). He was forced to resign as a Jew in 1935. During 1938-1939 he worked as an assistant at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

During the war Kochendörffer worked as a cryptanalyst, from 1939 to 1942 as an assistant in the cryptanalysis department [Pers Z S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pers_Z_S) of the [Foreign Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Foreign_Office) and from 1942-1945 in the [OKW/Chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cipher_Department_of_the_High_Command_of_the_Wehrmacht) and the GDNA. He documented the breaking of the Swiss [Enigma K](wikipedia://en/9256#Enigma_K) machine and was part of the team, with [Hans-Peter Luzius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans-Peter_Luzius) and [Willi Rinow](wikipedia://en/38412624), that broke the [M-209](wikipedia://en/743513) code.[^o10]

From 1946 to 1948 Kochendörffer worked as a Senior assistant at the Mathematical Institute of the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). In 1948, Kochendörffer [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in the subjects of mathematics in Berlin, and was promoted to full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor). He then appointed to position as professor of mathematics at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald), for a year.

In 1950, Kochendörffer became a professor of Mathematics at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock), a position he held until 1966.[^o4]Kochendörffer specialized in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He was also published algebra textbooks. Between 1967-1970 Kochendörffer was visiting professor of Mathematics, Faculty of Natural Sciences at the [Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg_University_of_Mainz),[^o11] and at the [University of Tasmania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tasmania). Between 1970-1977, Kochendörffer finished his career as professor of Mathematics at [TU Dortmund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Dortmund_University).[^o4]

He was a member of the Mathematische Gesellschaft der DDR, [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) and the member of [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik).[^o4]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 1960, he was awarded the [Patriotic Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriotic_Order_of_Merit) in Bronze, which was recognized in 1967. In 1963 he received the [National Prize of the German Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Prize_of_the_German_Democratic_Republic), National Prize III. for science and technology.

## Publications {#Publications}

- Einführung in die Algebra. Berlin 1955, 4. Aufl. 1974. (English Edition: Introduction to algebra. Groningen 1972).
- Determinants and matrices (German:Determinanten und Matrizen). Leipzig 1957, 5. Aufl. 1967. (License Issue Stuttgart 1970).
- Lehrbuch der Gruppentheorie. Unter besonderer Berücksichtigung der endlichen Gruppen. Leipzig 1966. (English Edition: Textbook of the group theory with special consideration of the finite groups. Leipzig 1966, English Group Theory, McGraw Hill 1970).
- On supplements of finite groups. Groningen 1963.
