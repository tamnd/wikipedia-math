# Read's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Read's conjecture"}

**Read's conjecture** is a conjecture, first made by [Ronald Read](wikipedia://en/18154982), about the [unimodality](wikipedia://en/1588509) of the coefficients of [chromatic polynomials](wikipedia://en/1460126) in the context of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o2][^o3] In 1974, [S. G. Hoggar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._G._Hoggar) tightened this to the conjecture that the coefficients must be strongly [log-concave](wikipedia://en/31178109). Hoggar's version of the conjecture is called the **Read–Hoggar conjecture**.[^o4][^o5]

:::

The Read–Hoggar conjecture had been unresolved for more than 40 years before [June Huh](wikipedia://en/54434626) proved it in 2009, during his PhD studies, using methods from [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2][^o6][^o7][^o8]
