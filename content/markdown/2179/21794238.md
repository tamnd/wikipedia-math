# Geoffrey K. Martin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey K. Martin"}

**Geoffrey K. Martin**is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) currently advising in the field of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). Martin is also the Associate Professor and Chair of the mathematics department at the [University of Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toledo). His fields of study include [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [relativity](wikipedia://en/30001), and the foundations of [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). Martin earned his Ph.D. at the [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in 1983. Geoffrey is the son of horticulturists Joy Lee Martin and Ernest Martin who owned Logee's Greenhouses.
