# List of Banach spaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Banach spaces"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) are among the most important objects of study. In other areas of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), most spaces which arise in practice turn out to be Banach spaces as well.

:::

## Classical Banach spaces {#Classical_Banach_spaces}

According to (Diestel 1984, Chapter VII), the **classical Banach spaces** are those defined by (Dunford Schwartz 1958), which is the source for the following table.

Glossary of symbols for the table below:

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="field"}

- $\mathbb{F}$ denotes the [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers) $\mathbb{R}$ or [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers) $\mathbb{C}.$

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Compact Hausdorff space"}

- $K$ is a [compact Hausdorff space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Hausdorff_space).

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Real number"}

- $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ are [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) with $1 < p, q < \infty$ that are [Hölder conjugates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hölder_conjugate), meaning that they satisfy $\frac{1}{q} + \frac{1}{p} = 1$ and thus also $q = \frac{p}{p-1}.$

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="\\sigma-algebra"}

- $\Sigma$ is a [$\sigma$-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_algebra) of sets.

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="algebra"}

- $\Xi$ is an [algebra](wikipedia://en/1018676) of sets (for spaces only requiring finite additivity, such as the [ba space](wikipedia://en/629005)).

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="measure"}

- $\mu$ is a [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) with [variation](wikipedia://en/683561#Total_variation_in_measure_theory) $|\mu|.$ A positive measure is a real-valued positive set function defined on a $\sigma$-algebra which is countably additive.

:::

## Banach spaces serving as counterexamples {#Banach_spaces_serving_as_counterexamples}

::: {#o47 .entry cue=list-entry name="James' space"}

- [James' space](wikipedia://en/44496861), a Banach space that has a [Schauder basis](wikipedia://en/3460662), but has no [unconditional Schauder Basis](wikipedia://en/3460662#Unconditionality). Also, James' space is isometrically isomorphic to its double dual, but fails to be reflexive.

:::

::: {#o48 .entry cue=list-entry name="Tsirelson space"}

- [Tsirelson space](wikipedia://en/628352), a reflexive Banach space in which neither [$\ell^p$](wikipedia://en/45194) nor [$c_0$](wikipedia://en/1701075#c_and_c0) can be embedded.

:::

::: {#o49 .entry cue=list-entry name="W.T. Gowers"}

- [W.T. Gowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.T._Gowers) construction of a space $X$ that is isomorphic to $X \oplus X \oplus X$ but not $X \oplus X$ serves as a counterexample for weakening the premises of the [Schroeder–Bernstein theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor–Bernstein–Schroeder_theorem)[^o50]

:::
