# C space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C space"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the space denoted by ***c*** is the [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) of all [convergent sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_sequence) $\left(x_n\right)$ of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) or [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826). When equipped with the [uniform norm](wikipedia://en/349755):

$$\|x\|_\infty = \sup_n |x_n|$$

the space $c$ becomes a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989). It is a [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) [linear subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of the [space of bounded sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_bounded_sequences), [$\ell^\infty$](wikipedia://en/45194), and contains as a closed subspace the Banach space $c_0$ of sequences converging to zero. The [dual](wikipedia://en/7988) of $c$ is isometrically isomorphic to $\ell^1,$ as is that of $c_0.$ In particular, neither $c$ nor $c_0$ is [reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091).

:::

In the first case, the isomorphism of $\ell^1$ with $c^*$ is given as follows. If $\left(x_0, x_1, \ldots\right) \in \ell^1,$ then the pairing with an element $\left(y_0, y_1, \ldots\right)$ in $c$ is given by

$$x_0\lim_{n\to\infty} y_n + \sum_{i=0}^\infty x_{i+1} y_i.$$

This is the [Riesz representation theorem](wikipedia://en/26452) on the [ordinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_%28mathematics%29) $\omega$.

For $c_0,$ the pairing between $\left(x_i\right)$ in $\ell^1$ and $\left(y_i\right)$ in $c_0$ is given by

$$\sum_{i=0}^\infty x_iy_i.$$
