# Xiao-Li Meng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xiao-Li Meng"}

Meng Xiaoli

**Xiao-Li Meng** (; born 1963) is a [Chinese American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_American) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and the Whipple V. N. Jones Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He has written numerous research papers about [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) algorithms and other statistical methodology.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Meng received his B.Sc. from [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in 1982 and his Ph.D. in statistics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1990.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

From 2004 to 2012, Meng was the Chair of Harvard's Department of Statistics, where he helped create innovative new statistics courses designed to give students a more positive impression of the subject.[^o7] He edited the journals *Bayesian Analysis* from 2003 to 2005 and *Statistica Sinica* from 2005 to 2008.[^o8] On August 14, 2012, Xiao-Li Meng was appointed dean of [Harvard Graduate School of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Graduate_School_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5] Since its inception in 2019, he has been the editor of the *[Harvard Data Science Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Data_Science_Review)*.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He received the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) in 2001.[^o12]

He was elected a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1997[^o13] and of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2004.[^o14] He was elected fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2020.[^o15] In 2013 he was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o16]
