# Jun S. Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jun S. Liu"}

**Jun S. Liu** (; born 1965) is a [Chinese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese-American) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) focusing on Bayesian statistical inference, statistical machine learning, and [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology). He was assistant professor of statistics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 1991 to 1994. From 1994 to 2004, he was Assistant, Associate, and full Professor of Statistics (promoted while being on leave) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). In 2000, Liu returned to Harvard as Professor of Statistics in the Department of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and also held a courtesy appointment at [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health). In September 2025, Liu left the United States and moved to [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) for a full-time appointment.[^o6]

:::

Liu has written [many research papers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-bHzVq8AAAAJ&hl=en) and a book[^o7] about [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) algorithms, including their applications in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology). He is also co-author of several early software on biological [sequence motif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence_motif) discovery.: MACAW, Gibbs Motif Sampler, BioProspector, Motif regressor, MDScan, Tmod; on genetic data analysis: BLADE, HAPLOTYPER, PL-EM, BEAM; and more recently on, genome structure, gene expression and cell type analysis: HiCNorm, BACH, CLIME, RABIT, CLIC, TIMER, and PhyloAcc.

## Education {#Education}

Liu received his [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 1985. He was a Ph.D. candidate of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) from 1986 to 1988, and obtained his Ph.D. in statistics under the supervision of [Wing Hung Wong](wikipedia://en/43406511) and [Augustine Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Kong) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1991.[^o3][^o10]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Liu was the recipient of the 2002 [COPSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COPSS) Presidents' Award,[^o12] which is arguably the most prestigious award in the field of statistics. He also won the 2010 Morningside Gold Medal[^o13] in Applied Mathematics; and awarded the 2016 [Pao-Lu Hsu award](https://www.icsa.org/awards/pao-lu-hsu-award/) by the [International Chinese Statistical Association](https://www.icsa.org/) (given every three years to an individual under age 50).[^o14]

Liu was an [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS) Medallion Lecturer in 2002 and a [Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) Lecturer in 2004. He was elected a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2004,[^o15] fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2005,[^o16] and fellow of the [International Society for Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Computational_Biology) in 2022.[^o17]
