# Cyclic category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclic category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **cyclic category** or **cycle category** or **category of cycles** is a [category](wikipedia://en/5869) of finite [cyclically ordered sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclically_ordered_set) and degree-1 maps between them. It was introduced by (Connes 1983).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The cyclic category Λ has one object Λ<sub>*n*</sub> for each natural number *n* = 0, 1, 2, ...

The morphisms from Λ<sub>*m*</sub> to Λ<sub>*n*</sub> are represented by increasing functions *f* from the integers to the integers, such that *f*(*x*+*m*+*1*) = *f*(*x*)+*n*+*1*, where two functions *f* and *g* represent the same morphism when their difference is divisible by *n*+*1*.

Informally, the morphisms from Λ<sub>*m*</sub> to Λ<sub>*n*</sub> can be thought of as maps of (oriented) necklaces with *m*+1 and *n*+1 beads. More precisely, the morphisms can be identified with homotopy classes of degree 1 increasing maps from *S*<sup>1</sup> to itself that map the subgroup **Z**/(*m*+1)**Z** to **Z**/(*n*+1)**Z**.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The number of morphisms from Λ<sub>*m*</sub> to Λ<sub>*n*</sub> is (*m*+*n*+1)!/*m*!*n*!.

The cyclic category is self dual.

The classifying space *B*Λ of the cyclic category is a classifying space *BS*<sup>1</sup>of the circle group *S*<sup>1</sup>.

:::

## Cyclic sets {#Cyclic_sets}

A cyclic set is a contravariant functor from the cyclic category to sets. More generally a **cyclic object** in a category *C* is a contravariant functor from the cyclic category to *C*.
