# Simone Warzel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simone Warzel"}

*Image: Simone Warzel MFO 2007.jpg.* Warzel at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2007

**Simone Warzel** (born 1973) is a German [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich). Her research involves [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and the [many-body problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-body_problem) in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202). She is a co-author of the book *Random Operators: Disorder Effects on Quantum Spectra and Dynamics*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Warzel was born on 2 February 1973 in [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlangen), where she grew up. She studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg), beginning in 1992, with a year at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) for [Part III of the Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos). She earned her doctorate (Dr. rer. nat.) from the University of Erlangen-Nuremberg in 2001. Her dissertation, *On Lifshits Tails in Magnetic Fields*, was supervised by [Hajo Leschke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajo_Leschke).

Before joining the Technical University of Munich, she was an assistant professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).

## Research {#Research}

Her research interests involve [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and the [many-body problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-body_problem) in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202). She is the author with [Michael Aizenman](wikipedia://en/22411175) of the book *Random Operators: Disorder Effects on Quantum Spectra and Dynamics*.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2009, the [International Union of Pure and Applied Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Pure_and_Applied_Physics) gave Warzel their Young Scientist Prize in Mathematical Physics. Warzel is a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship), and a former von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).

She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2011, a plenary speaker at the 2012 [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905), and a speaker in the mathematical physics section of the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Brazil.
