# Penny J. Davies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Penny J. Davies"}

**Penelope Jane Davies** OBE is a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who in 2009 became the second female president of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940), after [Elizabeth McHarg](wikipedia://en/58740509). Her research involves numerical simulation of the [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) describing [wave scattering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory) and elastic stability and biomechanical modelling. She is a senior lecturer in mathematics and statistics at the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Davies earned her Ph.D. in 1987 from [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) under the supervision of [John MacLeod Ball](wikipedia://en/3578468); her dissertation was *Stability problems in nonlinear elasticity*. She was on the staff of the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) before moving to the University of Strathclyde.

She was the President of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society from 2009 to 2011.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Davies was made an Officer of the Order of the British Empire "for services to mathematics" in the [2014 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Birthday_Honours).
