# Algebra: Chapter 0

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebra: Chapter 0"}

***Algebra: Chapter 0*** is a graduate [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) textbook written by [Paolo Aluffi](wikipedia://en/80625761). The book was first published in 2009 by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and is now a common choice in introductory courses in abstract algebra.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## History {#History}

*Algebra: Chapter 0* was published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)'s as part of their *Graduate Studies in Mathematics* series.[^o7] The book was well-received and an updated second printing was published in 2016.[^o8]

The book began as a transcript of Aluffi's lectures at [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University).[^o9]

Aluffi maintains an extensive list of errata on his Florida State University webpage.[^o10]

## Content {#Content}

The book aims to be a fully self-contained introduction to [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) for advanced undergraduates and beginning graduates. The main distinguishing factor of the book from other algebra texts is its early introduction to [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) which remain relevant throughout the book. It also features a greater emphasis on [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410).[^o7]
