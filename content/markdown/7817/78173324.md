# Hans Beck (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Beck"}

**Hans Heinrich Rudolf Beck** was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Hans Beck was born 16 August 1876 in Altzarrendorf, Kreis Grimmon, [Province of Pomerania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Pomerania_%281815%E2%80%931945%29), Prussia. He studied science at [University of Griefswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Griefswald). In 1902 he taught in Ostern, in 1903 in Hannover, and from 1909 to 1917 in [Charlottenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottenburg).[^o2]

He began research with [Eduard Study](wikipedia://en/663997) at [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and produced a dissertation on [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291). He translated [Maxime Bôcher](wikipedia://en/3180285)'s *Introduction to Higher Algebra* in 1910. In 1917 he took a position at the university. He produced a textbook on [coordinate geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_geometry) in 1919, and was promoted to professor in 1920. His *Introduction to the Axiomatics of Algebra* appeared in 1926. He died in [Bonn, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn,_Germany) on 24 October 1942.

He was a member of mathematical societies: [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973), Berliner Mathmatischer Gesellschaft, and [Circolo Matematico di Palermo](wikipedia://en/32146789).[^o3]

## Works {#Works}

- 1910: (as translator) *Einführung in die höhere Algebra* by [Maxime Bôcher](wikipedia://en/3180285)
- 1910: [Ein Seitenstück zur Mobius'schen Geometrie der Kreisverwandschaften](http://www.ams.org/journals/tran/1910-011-04/S0002-9947-1910-1500872-0/S0002-9947-1910-1500872-0.pdf), [Transactions of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/1388937) 11
- 1919: *Koordinatengeometrie*, [Julius Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Springer)
- 1926: *Einfuhrung in die Axiomatik der Algebra*[^o5]
