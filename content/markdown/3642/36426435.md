# Adrian Ioana

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adrian Ioana"}

**Adrian Ioana** (born 18 January 1981, [Târgu Jiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Târgu_Jiu)) is a Romanian mathematician.[^o3] He is currently a professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

Ioana earned a BS in Mathematics from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 2003, and completed his PhD at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2007, under the supervision of [Sorin Popa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorin_Popa). He then was a postdoc at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and a Research Fellow supported by the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655), after which he joined UC San Diego in 2011.[^o3]

For his contributions to [von Neumann algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebras) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), he was awarded a 2012 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize). In 2018 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Rio de Janeiro (on "Rigidity for von Neumann algebras").
