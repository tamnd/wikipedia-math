# Deviation of a local ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deviation of a local ring"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), the **deviations of a local ring** *R* are certain [invariants](wikipedia://en/1126638) ε<sub>*i*</sub>(*R*) that measure how far the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) is from being [regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_ring).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The deviations ε<sub>*n*</sub> of a [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) *R* with [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) *k* are non-negative integers defined in terms of its [Poincaré series](wikipedia://en/17006296) *P*(*t*) by

$$P(t)=\sum_{n\ge 0}t^n \operatorname{Tor}^R_n(k,k) = \prod_{n\ge 0} \frac{(1+t^{2n+1})^{\varepsilon_{2n}}}{(1-t^{2n+2})^{\varepsilon_{2n+1}}}.$$

The zeroth deviation ε<sub>0</sub> is the [embedding dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embedding_dimension) of *R* (the dimension of its tangent space). The first deviation ε<sub>1</sub> vanishes exactly when the ring *R* is a [regular local ring](wikipedia://en/506959), in which case all the higher deviations also vanish. The second deviation ε<sub>2</sub> vanishes exactly when the ring *R* is a [complete intersection ring](wikipedia://en/3116851), in which case all the higher deviations vanish.

:::
