# Kantor double

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kantor double"}

In mathematics, the **Kantor double** is a [Jordan superalgebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_superalgebra) structure on the sum of two copies of a [Poisson algebra](wikipedia://en/345704). It is named after [Isaiah Kantor](wikipedia://en/30235234), who introduced it in 1990.[^o2]

:::
