# Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction"}

In algebra, the **Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction** is a method of constructing a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) from a [Jordan algebra](wikipedia://en/936371), introduced by (txt), (txt), and (txt).

:::

If *J* is a Jordan algebra, the Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction puts a Lie algebra structure on *J* + *J* + Inner(*J*), the sum of 2 copies of *J* and the Lie algebra of inner derivations of *J*.

When applied to a [27-dimensional exceptional Jordan algebra](wikipedia://en/9153180) it gives a Lie algebra of type [E<sub>7</sub>](wikipedia://en/649115) of dimension 133.

The Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction was used by (Kac 1977) to classify the finite-dimensional simple [Jordan superalgebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_superalgebra).
