# Max Koecher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Koecher"}

*Image: Max Koecher 2.jpeg.* Max Koecher in Munich, 1967

**Max Koecher** (; 20 January 1924 in [Weimar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weimar) – 7 February 1990, [Lengerich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lengerich_%28Westfalen%29)) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Koecher studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). In 1951, he received his doctorate under [Max Deuring](wikipedia://en/5638258) with his work (Koecher 1953) on Dirichlet series with functional equation where he introduced [Koecher–Maass series](wikipedia://en/30235068). He qualified in 1954 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). From 1962 to 1970, Koecher was department chair at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich). He retired in 1989.

His main research area was the theory of Jordan algebras, where he introduced the [Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction](wikipedia://en/30230650) and the [Koecher–Vinberg theorem](wikipedia://en/37016909), and introduced the concept of an [admissible algebra](wikipedia://en/39299720). He discovered the [Koecher boundedness principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koecher_boundedness_principle) in the theory of [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927)
