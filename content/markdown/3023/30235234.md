# Isaiah Kantor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isaiah Kantor"}

**Isaiah Kantor** (or **Issai Kantor**, or **Isai Lʹvovich Kantor**) (1936–2006) was a mathematician who introduced the [Kantor–Koecher–Tits construction](wikipedia://en/30230650), and the [Kantor double](wikipedia://en/30235384), a Jordan superalgebra constructed from a [Poisson algebra](wikipedia://en/345704).

:::
