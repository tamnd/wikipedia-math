# Koecher–Maass series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Koecher–Maass series"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Koecher–Maass series** is a type of [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258) that can be expressed as a [Mellin transform](wikipedia://en/387297) of a [Siegel modular form](wikipedia://en/7721927), generalizing Hecke's method of associating a Dirichlet series to a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000) using Mellin transforms.
