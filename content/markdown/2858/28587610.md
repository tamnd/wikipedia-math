# Sylvia Serfaty

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvia Serfaty"}

**Sylvia Serfaty** (born 1975)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States). She won the 2004 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) for her contributions to the [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286), the [Henri Poincaré Prize](wikipedia://en/4890712) in 2012, and the Mergier–Bourdeix Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2013.[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Serfaty was born and raised in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989).[^n1] She was interested in mathematics since high school.

Serfaty earned her [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) in 1999, under supervision of [Fabrice Bethuel](wikipedia://en/28586899). She then held a teaching position ([agrégé préparateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégé_préparateur)) at the [École Normale Supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Cachan). Since 2007 she has held a professorship at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [NYU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

## Research {#Research}

Serfaty's research is part of the field of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). Her work particularly focuses on the Ginzburg-Landau model of superconductivity and [quantum vortices](wikipedia://en/7100728) in the [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286). She has also worked on the statistical mechanics of Coulomb-type systems.

In 2007 she published a book on the Ginzburg-Landau theory with Étienne Sandier, *Vortices in the Magnetic Ginzburg-Landau Model*.[^o8] She was an invited plenary speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o9]

She was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2019.[^o10]

She is one of the editors-in-chief of the scientific journal *Probability and Mathematical Physics*.[^o11]
