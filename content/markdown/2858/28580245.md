# Pierre Colmez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Colmez"}

**Pierre Colmez** (born 1962) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and *[directeur de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directeur_de_recherche)* at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) ([IMJ-PRG](wikipedia://en/57642956)) known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [*p*-adic analysis](wikipedia://en/291327).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Colmez studied at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) and obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble_Alpes_University).

## Research {#Research}

He works on special values of L-functions and $p$-adic representations of $p$-adic groups at the meeting point of Fontaine's and Langlands' programs. His contributions include:

- A proof of a $p$-adic analog of Dirichlet's analytic [class number formula](wikipedia://en/3021101).[^o5]
- A conjecture: the **Colmez conjecture** relating Artin L-functions at $s=0$ and periods of abelian varieties with complex multiplication, a far-reaching generalization of the [Chowla-Selberg formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chowla-Selberg_formula).[^o6]
- A proof of [Perrin-Riou's](wikipedia://en/42289771) conjectural explicit reciprocity law related to the functional equation of $p$-adic L-functions.[^o7]
- Several contributions to [Fontaine's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Marc_Fontaine) program of classification of $p$-adic representations of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, including proofs of conjectures of Fontaine such as "weakly admissible implies admissible"[^o8] and the "$p$-adic monodromy conjecture" which describe representations coming from geometry, or the overconvergence of all representations,[^o9][^o10] and the addition of new concepts such as "trianguline representations"[^o11] or "Banach-Colmez spaces".[^o12][^o13]
- A construction of the $p$-adic local [Langlands correspondence](wikipedia://en/171905) for $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$, via the construction of a functor (known as "Colmez's functor" or "Colmez's Montreal functor"[^o14]) from representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ to representations of the absolute Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}_p$.[^o15]
- Comparison theorems for $p$-adic algebraic and analytic varieties with applications to a geometrization of the $p$-adic local Langlands correspondence.[^o16][^o17][^o18]

With [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483), he co-edited the *Correspondance Grothendieck-Serre* (2001)[^o19][^o20] and the *Correspondance Serre-Tate*(2015).[^o21]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Colmez won the 2005 [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) for his contributions to the study of [L-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-functions) and p-adic [Galois representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_representations).

In 1998, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Berlin.[^o23]

Colmez has won the French [Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_%28game%29) championship four times.[^o24]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Pierre Colmez and [Leila Schneps](wikipedia://en/42154655) are the parents of [Coralie Colmez](wikipedia://en/42154683).[^o26][^o27] Violinist [David Grimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Grimal) is Colmez's first cousin.
