# Fabrice Bethuel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fabrice Bethuel"}

**Fabrice Bethuel** (born 7 June 1963) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He holds a chair at [Paris VI University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_VI_University).

:::

Bethuel earned his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) at [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) in 1989, under supervision of [Jean-Michel Coron](wikipedia://en/25110499). In 1998 Bethuel was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o3] He won the 1999 [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195), jointly with [Frédéric Hélein](wikipedia://en/28599398), for several important contributions to the theory of [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus). He also won the 2003 Mergier–Bourdeix Prize for his fundamental discoveries at the interface between analysis, topology, geometry, and physics.[^o4]
