# Burniat surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Burniat surface"}

In mathematics, a **Burniat surface** is one of the [surfaces of general type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_general_type) introduced by (txt).

:::

## Invariants {#Invariants}

The [geometric genus](wikipedia://en/3115807) and [irregularity](wikipedia://en/22857258) are both equal to 0. The [Chern number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_number) $c_1^2$ is either 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6.
