# Catanese surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Catanese surface"}

In mathematics, a **Catanese surface** is one of the [surfaces of general type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_general_type) introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The construction starts with a quintic *V* with 20 [double points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_point). Let *W* be the surface obtained by [blowing up](wikipedia://en/1817790) the 20 double points. Suppose that *W* has a double cover *X* branched over the 20 exceptional −2-curves. Let *Y* be obtained from *X* by blowing down the 20 −1-curves in *X*. If there is a group of order 5 [acting freely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_group_action) on all these surfaces, then the quotient *Z* of *Y* by this group of order 5 is a Catanese surface. Catanese found a 4-dimensional family of curves constructed like this.

:::

## Invariants {#Invariants}

The Catanese surface is a numerical [Campedelli surface](wikipedia://en/22843324) and hence has [Hodge diamond](wikipedia://en/2164886#Hodge_diamond)

1

and canonical degree $K^2 = 2$. The fundamental group of the Catanese surface is $\mathbf{Z}/5\mathbf{Z}$, as can be seen from its quotient construction.
