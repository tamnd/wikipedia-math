# Campedelli surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Campedelli surface"}

In mathematics, a **Campedelli surface** is one of the [surfaces of general type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfaces_of_general_type) introduced by Campedelli. Surfaces with the same [Hodge numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodge_number) are called **numerical Campedelli surfaces**.
