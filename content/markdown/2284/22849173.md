# James R. Norris

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James R. Norris"}

**James Ritchie Norris** (born 29 August 1960) is a mathematician working in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis).[^o3] He is the Professor of Stochastic Analysis in the [Statistical Laboratory, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge).

:::

He has made contributions to areas of mathematics connected to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), including [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875), [heat kernel estimates](wikipedia://en/3904336), and mathematical models for [coagulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coagulation) and [fragmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fragmentation_%28cell_biology%29). He was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1997.

Norris was an undergraduate at [Hertford College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertford_College,_Oxford) where he graduated in 1981. He completed his [D.Phil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1985 at [Wolfson College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfson_College,_Oxford) under the supervision of David Edwards. He was a research assistant from 1984 to 1985 at the [University College of Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_Swansea) before moving in 1985 to a lectureship at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) and a Fellowship of [Churchill College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_College,_Cambridge).[^o5] He was appointed Professor of Stochastic Analysis in 2005.[^o6] He is the director of the [Statistical Laboratory](wikipedia://en/3254271), a trustee of the [Rollo Davidson Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize)[^o7] and co-director of the Cambridge Centre for Analysis.[^o8]
