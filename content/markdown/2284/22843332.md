# Castelnuovo surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Castelnuovo surface"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Castelnuovo surface** is a [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) of [general type](wikipedia://en/2368356) such that the [canonical bundle](wikipedia://en/734893) is [very ample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_ample) and such that *c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup> = 3*p<sub>g</sub>* − 7. [Guido Castelnuovo](wikipedia://en/1558891) proved that if the canonical bundle is very ample for a surface of general type then *c*<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup> ≥ 3*p<sub>g</sub>* − 7.[^o2]

:::
