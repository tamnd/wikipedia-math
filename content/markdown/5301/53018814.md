# May Beenken

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="May Beenken"}

**May Margaret Beenken** (22 October 1901, [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania) – 21 December 1988, [St. Vincent Medical Center (Los Angeles)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Vincent_Medical_Center_%28Los_Angeles%29)) was an American mathematician.[^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Beenken was born in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania). Her parents were Sophie Kirn (1862 – 1941) and Henry Beenken (b. 1852). and she had at least three older siblings.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Beenken completed her PhD at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1928. Her doctoral advisor was Ernest Preston Lane and her thesis was titled *Surfaces in Five-Dimensional Space*.[^o6] She later became an instructor at [Oshkosh Teacher's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Oshkosh).[^o4] She also served as a lecturer at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1945, as well as an associate professor (later professor) at [Immaculate Heart College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Heart_College) from 1947 to 1969.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

After retiring, "she decided to turn to painting" and "applied herself here with the same single-mindedness as in teaching", according to a former colleague and student of hers.[^o4]
