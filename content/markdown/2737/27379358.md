# Wolfgang Hackbusch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Hackbusch"}

**Wolfgang Hackbusch** (born 24 October 1948 in [Westerstede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerstede), [Lower Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Saxony)) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his pioneering research in [multigrid methods](wikipedia://en/1747763) and later [hierarchical matrices](wikipedia://en/33198000), a concept generalizing the [fast multipole method](wikipedia://en/8280771). He was a professor at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) and is currently one of the directors of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig).

:::

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 1994 [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize)
- 1996 [Brouwer Medal](wikipedia://en/42578624)
- 1998 Plenary Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842)[^o4]
- 2020 [Gumin Prize for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/78691910)[^o5]
