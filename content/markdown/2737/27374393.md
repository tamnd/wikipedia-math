# Olof B. Widlund

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olof B. Widlund"}

**Olof B. Widlund** (born 1938), is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) mathematician. He is best known for his work in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), in particular his leading role in and fundamental contributions to [domain decomposition methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_decomposition_method). He received his Ph.D. at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in 1966[^o3] and is professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

:::

## Recognition {#Recognition}

- He was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to numerical analysis of domain decompositions within computational mathematics and for incubation through his writing and mentorship of a broad international, creative community of practice applied to highly resolved systems simulations".[^o7]
- The [Olof B. Widlund Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olof_B._Widlund_Prize) is named in his honor.

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Olof B. Widlund Prize"}

### Olof B. Widlund Prize {#Olof_B._Widlund_Prize}

The **Olof B. Widlund Prize** (formally, the **Olof B. Widlund Prize for Excellence in Domain Decomposition Methods**) is an award in numerical analysis given in the *Domain Decomposition Methods* (DDM) community. According to ddm.org, it is a prize for an established scientist recognizing contributions to domain decomposition methods (including theory, algorithms, scalable implementation, applications, and community service). The awardee gives a plenary lecture at the next ddm.org conference and receives a certificate and a cash award of €2500.[^o10]

Recipients have been:

- **2022**: Maksymilian Dryja (University of Warsaw).[^o11][^o12]
- **2024**: [Charbel Farhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charbel_Farhat) (Stanford University).[^o10]
- **2025**: [Jan Mandel](wikipedia://en/22308791) (University of Colorado).[^o10]

:::

## History {#History}

According to ddm.org, the prize was established by the Scientific Committee of ddm.org at DD26 (2020) and awarded for the first time at DD27 (Prague, 2022).[^o10]
