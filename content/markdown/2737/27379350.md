# Oregonator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oregonator"}

*Image: Limit\_cycle\_oscillation\_of\_Oregonator.png.* Limit cycle oscillation of Oregonator

The **Oregonator** is a theoretical model for a type of [autocatalytic reaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocatalytic_reaction). The Oregonator is the simplest realistic model of the chemical dynamics of the oscillatory [Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction](wikipedia://en/186262).[^o2] It was created by Richard Field and [Richard M. Noyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_M._Noyes) at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon).[^o3] It is a portmanteau of [Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon) and [oscillator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscillator).

:::

Earlier, the [Brusselator](wikipedia://en/27379198) model was proposed by [Ilya Prigogine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Prigogine) and his collaborators at the [Free University of Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles)[^o4] as a portmanteau of [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels) and [oscillator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscillator).

The Oregonator is a reduced model of the FKN mechanism (developed by Richard Field, [Endre Kőrös](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endre_Kőrös), and [Richard M. Noyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_M._Noyes)) which still involved 11 reactions and 12 species (21 intermediate species and 18 elementary steps). The Oregonator is characterized by the reactions

$$\begin{aligned} \text{I} \quad & A+Y \longrightarrow X+P \\[5pt] \text{II} \quad & X+Y \longrightarrow 2P \\[5pt] \text{III} \quad & A+X \longrightarrow 2X+2Z \\[5pt] \text{IV} \quad & 2X \longrightarrow A+P \\[5pt] \text{V} \quad & B+Z \longrightarrow \frac 1 2 f Y \\[5pt] & \frac{d [X]}{dt}= k_I [A] [Y] - k_{II} [X] [Y] + k_{III} [A] [X] - 2k_{IV} [X]^2 \\[5pt] & \frac{d [Y]}{dt}= -k_I [A] [Y] - k_{II} [X] [Y] + \frac{1}{2}f k_V [B] [Z] \\[5pt] & \frac{d [Z]}{dt}= 2k_{III} [A] [X] - k_V [B] [Z] \end{aligned}$$
