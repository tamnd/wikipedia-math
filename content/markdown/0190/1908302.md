# Slewing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Slewing"}

**Slewing** is the [rotation](wikipedia://en/39789) of an object around an axis, usually the [z axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z_axis).[^o2] An example is a [radar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar) scanning 360 degrees by slewing around the z axis. This is also common terminology in astronomy. The process of rotating a telescope to observe a different region of the sky is referred to as slewing.[^o3]

:::

The term slewing is also found in motion control applications. Often the slew axis is combined with another axis to form a motion profile.

In [crane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crane_%28machine%29) terminology, slewing is the angular movement of a crane boom or crane jib in a horizontal plane.

The term is also used in the [computer game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_game) [Microsoft Flight Simulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Flight_Simulator) wherein the user presses a key and they can rotate and move the virtual aircraft along all [three spatial planes](wikipedia://en/3054853).

In the modern day use of CNC programs, *slewing* is a vital part of the process.
