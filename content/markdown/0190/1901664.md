# Electrostatic ion cyclotron wave

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Electrostatic ion cyclotron wave"}

In [plasma physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_physics), an **electrostatic ion cyclotron wave** is a [longitudinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_wave) oscillation of the [ions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion) (and [electrons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron)) in a magnetized [plasma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_%28physics%29), propagating nearly (but not exactly) perpendicular to the [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563). The angle (in [radians](wikipedia://en/26003)) between the direction of propagation and the direction perpendicular to the magnetic field must be greater than about the square root of the mass ratio,

:::

$$\sqrt{\frac{m_e}{m_i}},$$

in order that the electrons can move along the field lines from crest to trough to satisfy the [Boltzmann relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_relation). The [dispersion relation](wikipedia://en/853778) is

$\omega^2=\Omega_c^2+k^2v_s^2$,[^o2]

where Ω<sub>c</sub> is the ion [cyclotron frequency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclotron_frequency) and *v*<sub>s</sub> is the [ion sound speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_acoustic_wave). This relation is the result of restoring forces due to the [Lorentz force](wikipedia://en/18631) (see [Upper hybrid oscillation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_hybrid_oscillation) for more details), the [electrostatic force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatic_force) (the *T*<sub>e</sub> term in *v*<sub>s</sub>), and the ion pressure (the *T*<sub>i</sub> term in *v*<sub>s</sub>).
