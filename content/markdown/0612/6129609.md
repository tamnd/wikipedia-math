# David Gabai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Gabai"}

**David Gabai** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and the [Hughes-Rogers Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Department_of_Mathematics) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o4] His research focuses on [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

David Gabai received his B.S. in mathematics from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1976 and his Ph.D. in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1980.[^o6] Gabai completed his doctoral dissertation, titled "Foliations and genera of links", under the supervision of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190).

After positions at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard) and [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), Gabai spent most of the period of 1986–2001 at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), and has been at Princeton since 2001. Gabai was the Chair of the Department of Mathematics at Princeton University from 2012 to 2019.[^o8]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 2004, David Gabai was awarded the [Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry](wikipedia://en/7085075),[^o10] given every three years by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

He was an invited speaker in the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 2010, [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India) on the topic of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).[^o11]

In 2011, he was elected to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o12] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]

Gabai was elected as a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2014.[^o14]
