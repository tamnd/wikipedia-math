# Extensive category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Extensive category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **extensive category** is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) **C** with finite [coproducts](wikipedia://en/310889) that are disjoint and well-behaved with respect to [pullbacks](wikipedia://en/841689). Equivalently, **C** is extensive if the coproduct [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) from the [product](wikipedia://en/4587226) of the [slice categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slice_category) **C**/*X* × **C**/*Y* to the slice category **C**/(*X* + *Y*) is an [equivalence of categories](wikipedia://en/379051) for all objects *X* and *Y* of **C**.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The categories **Set** and **Top** of [sets](wikipedia://en/26691) and [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450), respectively, are extensive categories.[^o5] More generally, the category of presheaves on any [small category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category) is extensive.[^o5]

The category **CRing**<sup>op</sup> of [affine schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_scheme) is extensive.
