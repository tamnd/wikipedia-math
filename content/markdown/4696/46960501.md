# Howard Masur

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Masur"}

**Howard Alan Masur** is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Masur was an invited speaker at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich).[^o6] and is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]

Along with [Yair Minsky](wikipedia://en/46905726), Masur is one of the pioneers of the study of [curve complex](wikipedia://en/51024643) geometry.[^o8] He also contributed to the understanding of the convergence of geodesic rays in [Teichmüller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_theory).[^o9]

Masur was a Ph.D. student of [Albert Marden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Marden) at the [University of Minnesota-Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota-Minneapolis).[^o10]

## Awards and recognitions {#Awards_and_recognitions}

The [Hubbard–Masur theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubbard–Masur_theorem) is named after Masur and [John H. Hubbard](wikipedia://en/6674772).[^o12] In 2009, a conference of mathematicians honored Masur's 60th birthday in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France).[^o13]
