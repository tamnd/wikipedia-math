# Grégory Miermont

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grégory Miermont"}

**Grégory Miermont** (born 16 July 1979) is a French mathematician working on [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [random trees](wikipedia://en/22192834) and [random maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_map).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After high school, Miermont trained for two years at [Classe préparatoire aux grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classe_préparatoire_aux_grandes_écoles) at the end of which he was admitted at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris. He studied there from 1998 to 2002, spending the 2001–2002 year as a visiting student in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). He received his doctorate at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 2003, under the supervision of Jean Bertoin. Then, he became a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) researcher in 2004 at [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) and [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), and was promoted to the rank of professor in 2009. Since 2012 he is a professor at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Lyon).

## Work {#Work}

Miermont worked on the theory of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), more precisely on the geometry and scaling limits of random planar maps, and on fragmentation related to random trees.
