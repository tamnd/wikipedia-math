# Jorge Mateu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jorge Mateu"}

**Jorge Mateu** is a Spanish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), author, and academic. He is a professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) within the Department of Mathematics at [University Jaume I of Castellon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_I_University)[^o4] and director of the Unit Eurocop for Data Science in criminal activities in the same department.[^o22]

:::

Mateu's research is centered on [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904), [geostatistics](wikipedia://en/51531), and [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), with a particular emphasis on [spatio-temporal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatiotemporal_pattern) [point processes](wikipedia://en/2147685).[^o5] He led the 'Mathematical-statistical modelling of [space-time](wikipedia://en/28758) data and [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253)' group at Universitat Jaume I to develop spatio-temporal statistical techniques used for [modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modeling_and_simulation) across fields of [public safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_security), [environmental management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_resource_management), and [criminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminology).[^o6] He is co-editor of books, including *Spatial Statistics Through Applications* (2002), *Case Studies in Spatial Point Process Modeling* (2005), *Spatio-temporal Design*. *Advances in Efficient Data Acquisition* (2012), *Spatial and Spatio-Temporal Geostatistical Modeling and Kriging* (2015), or *Geostatistical Functional Data Analysis* (2021). He has also received the Social Council Award from UJI and has been noted as a World Class Professor by an [Indonesian ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_and_Culture_%28Indonesia%29).[^o7]

Mateu is a Fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) and [Wessex Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wessex_Institute_of_Technology) in [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain) and a member of The [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute)[^o8] and the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability). He served as a guest editor for special issues in the *[Journal of Geophysical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Geophysical_Research)*, and *Environmetrics*, as the editor-in-chief for the *[Journal of Agricultural, Biological, and Environmental Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Agricultural,_Biological,_and_Environmental_Statistics)*[^o9] as well as an associate editor for *Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment*,[^o10] *[Spatial Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_Statistics)*,[^o11] *Environmetrics*,[^o12] and *International Statistical Review*.[^o13]

## Education {#Education}

Mateu earned his undergraduate degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) from the [University of Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valencia) in 1987, followed by a master's degree in 1995. He graduated with a Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematics at University of Valencia (UV) in 1998.[^o15]

## Career {#Career}

Mateu began his academic career as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) of statistics in the Department of Mathematics at Jaume I University in 1992[^o17] where he served as an associate professor from 2000 to 2007. In 2007, he assumed the position of Full Professor of Statistics at UJI.[^o18]

In 2011, he held the position of secretary for the International Environmetrics Society's board of directors[^o19] and became a co-director of the [Erasmus Mundus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Mundus) Master in Geospatial Technologies.[^o20] Additionally, he served as President of the [Board of Editors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) for METMA Workshops[^o21] Since 2014, he has been serving as the director of the Unit Eurocop: Statistical Modeling of Crime Data at Jaume I University.[^o22]

## Research {#Research}

Mateu focuses his research on the [intersection](wikipedia://en/41780044) of geostatistics, [spatial data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_database), stochastic processes, [computational sciences](wikipedia://en/1181008), and [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science), with a particular emphasis on [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904). He has analysed [crime data](wikipedia://en/177487) and [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) projects by employing a combination of statistical and [machine-learning](wikipedia://en/233488) methods.[^o24] He served as a joint [principal investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_investigator) for GEO-C.[^o25] He was worked on the projects (a) Statistical analysis of complex dependencies in space-time stochastic processes. Networks, functional marks and SPDE-based intensities. Ministry of Science and bInnovation (PID2022-141555OB-I00), 2023-2026, and (b) Spatio-temporal stochastic processes over networks and trajectories. Parametric models and functional marks. Generalitat Valenciana (CIAICO/2022/191), 2023-2025.

### Data science and stochastic processes {#Data_science_and_stochastic_processes}

Mateu's research on data science has included a range of topics such as filament delineation, [model selection](wikipedia://en/3664073), and stochastic processes. In his research on the automatic delineation of filaments obtained from redshift catalogs, he applied a marked point process, to gain insights into the cosmic filament structure.[^o27] Together with a number of coauthors, he extended Gneiting's work to develop new spatio-temporal covariance models, resulting in novel classes of stationary nonseparable functions.[^o28] In addition, his research of space-time [covariance function](wikipedia://en/9793263) estimation introduced two methods based on the concept of composite likelihood which were designed to strike a balance between computational complexity and statistical efficiency.[^o29] Furthermore, while addressing the challenge of model selection, he discussed the limitations of traditional models like [Bayesian Information Criterion](wikipedia://en/2473272) and proposed a practical extension aimed at handling model selection issues effectively.[^o30] In 2018, during his research on the use of administrative data, he identified challenges related to statistical analyses and discussed the need for a critical approach to ensure the validity and accuracy of results.[^o31]

### Spatial data and environmental management {#Spatial_data_and_environmental_management}

Mateu has conducted studies on the spatial and spatio-temporal point processes. He conducted research to analyse spatial point patterns across different experimental groups, summarising his findings using the K-function in a non-parametric approach to emphasise the strengths and limitations of spatial data.[^o33] His work on Functional Data Analysis demonstrated its connection with three traditional types of spatial data structures and provided examples to illustrate the integration of geostatistical data, and areal data.[^o34] He also introduced a methodological framework based on geostatistics that applied to agricultural planning and environmental restoration.[^o35] In collaboration with other colleagues, he analysed real-world soil penetration and presented an approach for predicting spatial patterns in functional data which enabled the estimation of values at unobserved locations.[^o36]

### Crime data and public health analysis {#Crime_data_and_public_health_analysis}

Mateu's research on functional environmental data, particularly in modelling air pollutant concentrations, emphasised the importance of cross-validation for parameter selection and provided insights into adapting kriging techniques.[^o38] In 2003, he introduced a spatiotemporal Hawkes-type point process model for analysing violence by incorporating daily and weekly periodic patterns in crime occurrences to shed light on the interplay of temporal trends in crime.[^o39] Expanding on this research, he introduced a deep learning approach in temporal correlations of historical data resulting in the enhancement of police resources, surveillance, crime event predictions, and prevention strategies.[^o18]
