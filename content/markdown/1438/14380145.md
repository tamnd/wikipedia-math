# Arthur J. Lohwater

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur J. Lohwater"}

**Arthur John "Jack" Lohwater** (October 20, 1922 - June 10, 1982) was an American mathematician.[^o3]

:::

He obtained a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) (1951), on the dissertation *The Boundary Values of a Class of Analytic Functions*, advised by [Wladimir Seidel](wikipedia://en/14432157). Later he joined the faculty at [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University). He was editor of [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646) (1962–65).[^o5] With [Norman Steenrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Steenrod) and [Sydney Gould](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Gould) he established important ties with Russian mathematicians, beginning with conferences in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) (1956, 58) and resulting in a dictionary. Lohwater died of lung cancer in 1982, after a lengthy illness.[^o6] He was married to the mathematician Marjorie White Lohwater (1925–2007).[^o7]
