# Ordinary Differential Equations (textbook)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ordinary Differential Equations"}

***Ordinary Differential Equations***, published by [MIT Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Press) in 1973, is a textbook by mathematician [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490). It was translated from the Russian by Richard A. Silverman. It's an introductory course for graduate students or strong undergraduate students.[^o3] The focus is on applications to [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics). Some opinions of reviewers: Chicone praised the book, while Sacker mentioned that many results are given without [proof](wikipedia://en/82285).[^o4][^o5][^o6] As put by the magazine *[American Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Scientist)*: "\[...\] textbook treats the subject of [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) in an entirely new way \[...\]. One can expect this book to bring new life into this old subject."[^o7]

:::
