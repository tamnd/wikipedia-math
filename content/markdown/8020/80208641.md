# Clara Torres Latorre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clara Torres Latorre"}

**Clara Torres Latorre** (born 1997)[^o3] is a Spanish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o4] She holds 16 international titles, ten of which are gold medals. This achievement has made her the Valencian student with the best results in history in university-level math competitions.[^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Torres studied the Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics and Physical Engineering at the CFIS of the [Polytechnic University of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Catalonia) as part of the Estalmat project. In 2014, she achieved gold medals in three high school science olympiads ([mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry)). That same year, she was honored at the [Palace of the Generalitat Valenciana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_of_the_Generalitat_Valenciana).[^o7] [Sir Harold Walter Kroto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Kroto), [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Chemistry), went specifically to the Palace to meet Torres and her teammates.

For two consecutive years, in 2013[^o8] and 2014, Torres won gold medals in the Mediterranean Mathematics Competition.[^o9] She also won gold in 2013, in [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia), at the Ibero-American Chemistry Olympiad (the greatest achievement in this discipline by any Spanish student).[^o10] She later added four more golds in mathematics: in 2014, in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), she earned gold at the International Mathematical Arhimede Contest; in 2015, in [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico), she won absolute gold at the Ibero-American Mathematical Olympiad,[^o11] leading the Spanish team that finished in third place. That same year, in [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria) at the International Mathematics Competition for University Students, she won another gold. In 2016, she repeated the achievement and also won gold in a speed programming competition in [Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto).[^o12]

In 2014, after placing in the top two positions for four years in the Kangaroo Math Contest, Torres Latorre received the Silver Pin, the highest award from the [Catalan Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/77808703) for competitors who earn recognition all four years.[^o13] [^o14]In 2016, at the 30th Ibero-American Mathematics Olympiad, she also won another gold medal.[^o15]

Years later, having fully embraced her [gender identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity) as Clara, in June 2024 Torres Latorre obtained her PhD in Mathematics on the theory of regularity in obstacle problems of the [Harnack inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnack_inequality), from the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona).[^o3]
