# Italian Statistical Society

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Italian Statistical Society"}

The **Italian Statistical Society** (Società italiana di statistica; **SIS**) is a scientific society established on 15 January 1939 as a non-profit [juridical person](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juridical_person) by 42 founding members who approved the first statute with the fundamental aim of promoting the development of [statistical sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_sciences) and their applications in economic, social, health, demographic, technological, productive, and many other fields of research. The Society carries out this task, assigned to it by the statute, through the organization of scientific meetings and conferences, [publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_publication), and collaborations with similar bodies at the national and international level.

:::

## Description {#Description}

Since its establishment, the Society has grown in step with the rapid spread of quantitative methods of [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954) in all areas of [scientific research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_research) and social life.

The Italian Statistical Society maintains scientific collaborations with numerous foreign statistical societies, is affiliated with the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (ISI), is a member of the [International Association of Classification Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Classification_Societies) (IFCS) through the SIS Section for Classification and Data Analysis (CLADAG), and supports the European Courses in Advanced Statistics (ECAS) program.

The Italian Statistical Society publishes a monthly information sheet, *SIS - Informazioni*, and the online journal *Statistica & Società*, as well as supporting the publication of the journal *Induzioni*, which is dedicated to teaching.

*[Statistical Methods & Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Methods_&_Applications)* is the scientific journal of the Society, printed in English[^o2] by the publishing house [Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag), created primarily with the aim of disseminating the main scientific works of the Italian school of statistics to other countries.

Over a thousand members belong to SIS, the majority (about ninety percent) of whom are [statisticians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statisticians) (Members, many of whom are [university professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_professor)), and the remainder are entities and societies such as the [Italian National Institute of Statistics](wikipedia://en/405440) (Istat), the [Bank of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Italy), the [Guglielmo Tagliacarne Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guglielmo_Tagliacarne_Institute), some [chambers of commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Commerce), some large [Italian municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune_%28Italy%29), as well as [Italian ministries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Republic_of_Italy).

The presidents of the SIS have been:

- [Gaetano Pietra](wikipedia://en/32009280) (1939–1941)
- [Corrado Gini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrado_Gini) (1941–1945, 1949–1965)
- Luigi Galvani (1945–1949)
- [Paolo Fortunati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Fortunati) (1967–1980)
- Giuseppe Leti (1980–1988)
- [Alberto Zuliani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Zuliani) (1988–1992)
- Alfredo Rizzi (1992–1996)
- [Luigi Biggeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Biggeri) (1996–2000)
- Benito V. Frosini (2000–2004)
- Daniela Cocchi (2004–2008)
- Maurizio Vichi (2008–2012)
- Nicola Torelli (2012–2016)
- Monica Pratesi (2016–2020)
- Corrado Crocetta (2020–2024)
- Marcello Chiodi (2024 to present)[^o5]
