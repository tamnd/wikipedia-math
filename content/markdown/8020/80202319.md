# George C. Tiao

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George C. Tiao"}

**George Ching-Hwuan Tiao** (; November 8, 1933 – April 25, 2026) was a Taiwanese-American [econometrician](wikipedia://en/10390) and statistician. He was a professor emeritus of economics and statistics at the [University of Chicago Booth School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Booth_School_of_Business). He was the founding chair editor of *[Statistica Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistica_Sinica)*. He contributed greatly to the field of [Bayesian econometrics](wikipedia://en/20484367).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Tiao was born in London while both his parents were studying at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics)[^o4] and raised in China. In 1950, he moved to [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) with his parents and studied at Nanjing Jinling High School. He earned a bachelor's degree in economics from the [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University). He moved to the United States in 1956 and earned an MBA in banking and finance from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1958. He earned his PhD in economics from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1962.[^o5] His doctoral advisors were Roger Frederick Miller and [George E. P. Box](wikipedia://en/2741338).

After graduating, he worked as a faculty member for twenty years at the University of Wisconsin, serving as chair of the statistics department from 1973 to 1975.[^o4] Since 1982, he worked at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.

He was the founding chair editor of *[Statistica Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistica_Sinica)* from 1991 to 1993.

Tiao died on April 25, 2026, at the age of 92.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Tiao became a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1973, a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1974, a member of [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) in 1976, and a member of the International Statistical Institute in 1980.[^o5] He was awarded the [Wilks Memorial Award](wikipedia://en/15966180) in statistics in 2001. [Charles III University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_III_University_of_Madrid) awarded him an honorary doctorate in 2003.[^o4]
