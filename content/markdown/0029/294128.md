# Pike (cipher)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pike"}

The **Pike** [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) was invented by [Ross Anderson](wikipedia://en/61571) to be a "leaner and meaner" version of [FISH](wikipedia://en/1290005) after he broke FISH in 1994. Its name is supposed to be a humorous allusion to the [pike fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pike_%28fish%29).

:::

The cipher combines ideas from [A5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A5_%28cryptography%29) with the [lagged Fibonacci generators](wikipedia://en/45619) used in FISH. It is about 10% faster than FISH, yet believed to be much stronger. It potentially has a huge [key length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_length), and no attacks have been published .

## Description {#Description}

Pike consists of three [lagged Fibonacci generators](wikipedia://en/45619) with relations

$$\begin{aligned} a_i = a_{i-55} + a_{i-24} \ (mod \ 2^{32}) \\[] b_i = b_{i-57} + b_{i-7} \ (mod \ 2^{32}) \\[] c_i = c_{i-58} + c_{i-19} \ (mod \ 2^{32}) \end{aligned}$$

The clock control is based on the carry bits. If all carry bits agree we step all three LFG's, otherwise we step the two who do agree. This control will be delayed 8 cycles.

The final output is the [XOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusive_Or) of the least significant words of all three generators.
