# List of things named after Carl Friedrich Gauss

*Image: Carl Friedrich Gauss 1840 by Jensen.jpg.* Carl Friedrich Gauss (1777–1855)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Carl Friedrich Gauss"}

[Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) (1777–1855) is the [eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym) of all of the topics listed below. There are over 100 topics all named after this German mathematician and scientist, all in the fields of mathematics, physics, and astronomy. The English eponymous adjective *Gaussian* is pronounced .[^o2]

:::

## Monuments, busts, and memorial plaques {#Monuments,_busts,_and_memorial_plaques}

Gauss Monuments were erected in Brunswick and Göttingen (the last together with Weber). [Busts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bust_%28sculpture%29) of Gauss were placed in the [Walhalla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walhalla_%28memorial%29) hall of fame near [Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regensburg) and in the [German Research Centre for Geosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GFZ_German_Research_Centre_for_Geosciences) in [Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potsdam). Several places where Gauss has stayed in Germany are marked with plaques.

## Other commemorations {#Other_commemorations}

Germany issued three postage stamps honoring Gauss, one in 1955 on the hundredth anniversary of his death and two others in 1977, the 200th anniversary of his birth.
