# David A. Wagner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David A. Wagner"}

**David A. Wagner** (born 1974) is an American professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and a well-known researcher in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security). He is a member of the [Election Assistance Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_Assistance_Commission)'s [Technical Guidelines Development Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_Guidelines_Development_Committee), tasked with assisting the EAC in drafting the Voluntary Voting System Guidelines. He was also a member of the [ACCURATE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACCURATE) project.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wagner received an A.B. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1995, an M.S. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from Berkeley in 1999, and a Ph.D. in computer science from Berkeley in 2000. He joined the faculty of Berkeley after graduation, became a Full Professor in 2010, and was chair of the Computer Science Department from 2020 to 2022.[^o4] He has received awards for his teaching.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Wagner has published two books and over 200 peer-reviewed scientific papers.[^o7] His notable achievements include:

- 2017 Development of the [Carlini-Wagner](wikipedia://en/45049676#Carlini_&_Wagner_%28C&W%29) attack on machine learning models (with [Nicholas Carlini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Carlini)); used it to break 20 adversarial machine learning defenses.
- 2007 Served as principal investigator for the source code review and also the documentation review of the historic [California state Top-to-Bottom review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_voting#California_top_to_bottom_review) of electronic voting systems certified for use. Flaws found with vendor-supplied [voting machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_machine) resulted in decertification and provisional recertification by the Secretary of State.
- 2001 Cryptanalysis of [WEP](wikipedia://en/422014), the security protocol used in [802.11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/802.11) "[WiFi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WiFi)" networks (with [Nikita Borisov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Borisov) and [Ian Goldberg](wikipedia://en/829135)).
- 2000 Cryptanalysis of the [A5/1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A5_%28cryptography%29) [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) used in [GSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSM) [cellphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellphone) (with [Alex Biryukov](wikipedia://en/9189491) and [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314)).
- 1999 Cryptanalysis of [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft)'s [PPTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-to-point_tunneling_protocol) tunnelling protocol (with [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732) and "[Mudge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peiter_Zatko)").
- 1999 Invention of the [slide attack](wikipedia://en/1821327), a new form of [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) (with [Alex Biryukov](wikipedia://en/9189491)); also the [boomerang attack](wikipedia://en/2137332) and [mod n cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/709367) (the latter with Bruce Schneier and John Kelsey).
- 1998 Development of [Twofish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twofish_encryption_algorithm) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594), which was a finalist for [NIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIST)'s [Advanced Encryption Standard competition](wikipedia://en/99431) (with [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732), [John Kelsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Kelsey_%28cryptanalyst%29), [Doug Whiting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Whiting), [Chris Hall](wikipedia://en/13498814), and [Niels Ferguson](wikipedia://en/204933)).
- 1997 Cryptanalyzed the [CMEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMEA_%28cipher%29) algorithm used in many [U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [cellphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellphone) (with [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732)).
- 1995 Discovered a flaw in the implementation of [SSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer) in [Netscape Navigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape_Navigator) (with [Ian Goldberg](wikipedia://en/829135)).[^o8]
