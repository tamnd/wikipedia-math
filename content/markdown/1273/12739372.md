# N-category number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="N-category number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **category number** of a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) is a humorous construct invented by [Dan Freed](wikipedia://en/40895609),[^o2][^o3] intended to measure the capacity of that mathematician to stomach the use of [higher categories](wikipedia://en/5274013). It is defined as the largest number *n* such that they can think about *n*-categories for a half hour without getting a splitting headache.

:::
