# Bachmann–Howard ordinal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bachmann–Howard ordinal"}

In mathematics, the **Bachmann–Howard ordinal** (also known as the **Howard ordinal**, or **Howard–Bachmann ordinal**[^o2]) is a [large countable ordinal](wikipedia://en/4376814). It is the [proof-theoretic ordinal](wikipedia://en/16084282) of several mathematical [theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_%28logic%29), such as [Kripke–Platek set theory](wikipedia://en/1124695) (with the [axiom of infinity](wikipedia://en/246449)) and the system CZF of [constructive set theory](wikipedia://en/5042360). It was introduced by (txt) and (txt).

:::

## Definition {#Definition}

The Bachmann–Howard ordinal is defined using an [ordinal collapsing function](wikipedia://en/16968563):

- ε<sub>*α*</sub> enumerates the [epsilon numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epsilon_numbers_%28mathematics%29), the ordinals *ε* such that ω<sup>*ε*</sup> = *ε*.
- Ω = ω<sub>1</sub> is the [first uncountable ordinal](wikipedia://en/20860540).
- ε<sub>Ω+1</sub> is the first epsilon number after Ω = ε<sub>Ω</sub>.
- ψ(*α*) is defined to be the smallest ordinal that cannot be constructed by starting with 0, 1, ω and Ω, and repeatedly applying [ordinal addition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_addition), multiplication and exponentiation, and ψ to previously constructed ordinals (except that ψ can only be applied to arguments less than *α*, to ensure that it is well defined).
- The **Bachmann–Howard ordinal** is ψ(ε<sub>Ω+1</sub>).

The Bachmann–Howard ordinal can also be defined as φ<sub>ε<sub>Ω+1</sub></sub>(0) for an extension of the [Veblen functions](wikipedia://en/16124925) φ<sub>*α*</sub> to certain functions *α* of ordinals; this extension was carried out by Heinz Bachmann and is not completely straightforward.[^o7][^o8]
