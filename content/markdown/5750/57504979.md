# Joseph Diestel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Diestel"}

**Joseph Diestel** (January 27, 1943 – August 17, 2017) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor of Mathematics at [Kent State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University). In addition to his contribution to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), particularly [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) theory and the theory of [vector measures](wikipedia://en/9399072), Diestel was known for a number of highly influential textbooks: in 1975 he published "Lecture Notes Geometry of Banach Spaces—Selected Topics";[^o3][^o4] in 1977, he published "Vector Measures"[^o5] with J. Jerry Uhl; in 1984, published "Sequences and series in Banach spaces"[^o6] and in 1995 he published "Absolutely summing operators"[^o7] with H. Jarchow and A. Tonge; as well as a number of other books.[^o8][^o9]

:::

Diestel received his Ph.D. degree in 1969 from [Catholic University of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_America) under [Victor Michael Bogdan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Michael_Bogdan). He had 29 graduate students and over 46 mathematical descendants.[^o10]
