# Sergei Aseev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Aseev"}

**Sergei Mironovich Aseev** (Сергéй Миро́нович Асéев; born 4 December 1957) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Dr. Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), and a [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

:::

He graduated from the faculty of [MSU CMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC) in 1980.

He defended the thesis «Extremal problems for differential inclusions with phase constraints» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1998).

Was awarded the title of [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (2008).[^o4]

Author of 1 book and more than 30 scientific articles.[^o5][^o6]

**Area of scientific interests**: The theory of multivalued mappings, optimal control, and mathematical models in economics.[^o7]
