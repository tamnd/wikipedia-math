# Algorithms and Combinatorics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algorithms and Combinatorics"}

**Algorithms and Combinatorics** (0937-5511) is a [book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_series) in mathematics, and particularly in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and the design and analysis of [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775). It is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), and was founded in 1987.

:::
