# Wirtinger sextic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wirtinger sextic"}

In mathematics, the **Wirtinger plane sextic curve**, studied by [Wirtinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirtinger) in 1892, is a degree 6 genus 4 plane curve with double points at the 6 vertices of a [complete quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_quadrilateral).

:::
