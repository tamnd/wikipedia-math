# Philip Treisman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Treisman"}

**Philip Uri Treisman** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and mathematics educator. He is the Director of the Charles A. Dana Center,[^o2] and is a Professor of Mathematics at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o3] He developed the Emerging Scholars Program (ESP), aimed at helping students from groups that are underrepresented in mathematics, science and engineering excel in calculus and other courses in science. The program was first implemented at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and has now disseminated throughout college campuses across the United States.[^o4] His efforts to improve American education have been recognized by [Newsweek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsweek), the Harvard Foundation and the [MacArthur Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Foundation), among other publications and societies.[^o5][^o6][^o7] He is a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o8]

:::

He graduated *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)* with a B.S. in Mathematics from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), and from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) with a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1985.[^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

- 1987 Charles A. Dana Award for Pioneering Achievement in American Higher Education[^o4]
- 1992 [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program)
- 2006 The Harvard Foundation's Scientist of the Year Award[^o11]
- 2016 Mathematics Leadership Excellence Award, [American Mathematical Association of Two-Year Colleges](wikipedia://en/3087414)[^o12]
- 2019 Yueh-Gin Gung and Dr. Charles Y. Hu Award for Distinguished Service to Mathematics, [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o13]
- 2020 [James Bryant Conant Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bryant_Conant_Award), [Education Commission of the States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Commission_of_the_States)[^o14]
