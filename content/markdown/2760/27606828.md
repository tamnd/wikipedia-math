# 4-5 kisrhombille

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="4-5 kisrhombille"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **4-5 kisrhombille** or **order-4 bisected pentagonal tiling** is a semiregular dual [tiling of the hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/18640974). It is constructed by congruent [right triangles](wikipedia://en/39420) with 4, 8, and 10 triangles meeting at each vertex.

:::

The name **4-5 kisrhombille** is by [Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), seeing it as a 4-5 rhombic tiling, divided by a [kis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator) operator, adding a center point to each rhombus, and dividing into four triangles.

The image shows a [Poincaré disk model](wikipedia://en/30871836) projection of the hyperbolic plane.

It is labeled V4.8.10 because each right triangle face has three types of vertices: one with 4 triangles, one with 8 triangles, and one with 10 triangles.

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

It is the dual tessellation of the [truncated tetrapentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38266312) which has one [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) and one [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575) and one [decagon](wikipedia://en/327393) at each vertex.
