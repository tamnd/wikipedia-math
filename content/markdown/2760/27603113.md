# 3-7 kisrhombille

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="3-7 kisrhombille"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **3-7 kisrhombille tiling** is a semiregular dual [tiling of the hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/18640974). It is constructed by congruent [right triangles](wikipedia://en/39420) with 4, 6, and 14 triangles meeting at each vertex.

:::

The image shows a [Poincaré disk model](wikipedia://en/30871836) projection of the hyperbolic plane.

It is labeled V4.6.14 because each right triangle face has three types of vertices: one with 4 triangles, one with 6 triangles, and one with 14 triangles. It is the dual tessellation of the [truncated triheptagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/8834676) which has one square and one heptagon and one tetrakaidecagon at each vertex.

## Naming {#Naming}

The name **3-7 kisrhombille** is given by [Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), seeing it as a 3-7 rhombic tiling, divided by a [*kis*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator) operator, adding a center point to each rhombus, and dividing into four triangles.

It is topologically related to a polyhedra sequence; see [discussion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisected_hexagonal_tiling#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings). This group is special for having all even number of edges per vertex and form bisecting planes through the polyhedra and infinite lines in the plane, and are the reflection domains for the (2,3,*n*) [triangle groups](wikipedia://en/2069342) – for the heptagonal tiling, the important [(2,3,7) triangle group](wikipedia://en/12659803).

See also the [uniform tilings of the hyperbolic plane with (2,3,7) symmetry](wikipedia://en/18640974#The_.5B7.2C3.5D_.287_3_2.29_group_family).

The kisrhombille tilings can be seen as from the sequence of rhombille tilings, starting with the cube, with faces [divided or kissed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator) at the corners by a face central point.

*Image: Morphing of modular tiling to 2 3 7 triangle tiling.gif.* Visualization of the map (2,3,∞) → (2,3,7) by morphing the associated tilings.[^o12]

Just as the (2,3,7) triangle group is a quotient of the [modular group](wikipedia://en/325019) (2,3,∞), the associated tiling is the quotient of the modular tiling, as depicted in the video at right.
