# Kisrhombille

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kisrhombille"}

*Image: Tiling Dual Semiregular V4-6-12 Bisected Hexagonal.svg.* Euclidean kisrhombille tiling

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **kisrhombille** is a [uniform tiling](wikipedia://en/12126635) of [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) faces, divided by central points into four triangles.

:::

Examples:

- [3-6 kisrhombille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-6_kisrhombille) – Euclidean plane
- [3-7 kisrhombille](wikipedia://en/27603113) – hyperbolic plane
- [3-8 kisrhombille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_trioctagonal_tiling#Order_3-8_kisrhombille) – hyperbolic plane
- [4-5 kisrhombille](wikipedia://en/27606828) – hyperbolic plane
