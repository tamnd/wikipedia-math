# Hartshorne ellipse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hartshorne ellipse"}

In mathematics, a **Hartshorne ellipse** is an [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277) in the [unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_ball) bounded by the [4-sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-sphere) **S**<sup>4</sup> such that the ellipse and the circle given by intersection of its plane with **S**<sup>4</sup> satisfy the [Poncelet condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poncelet_condition) that there is a triangle with vertices on the circle and edges tangent to the ellipse. They were introduced by (txt), who showed that they correspond to *k* = 2 [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons) on **S**<sup>4</sup>.

:::
