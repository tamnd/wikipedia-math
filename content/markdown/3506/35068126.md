# Lebrun manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lebrun manifold"}

In mathematics, a **LeBrun manifold** is a [connected sum](wikipedia://en/490134) of copies of the [complex projective plane](wikipedia://en/737155), equipped with an explicit self-dual metric. Here, self-dual means that the [Weyl tensor](wikipedia://en/1026848) is its own [Hodge star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodge_star). The metric is determined by the choice of a finite collection of points in [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742). These metrics were discovered by (txt), and named after LeBrun by (txt).

:::
