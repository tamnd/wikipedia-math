# Endrass surface

*Image: Endrass surface.png.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Endrass surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), an **Endrass surface** is a [nodal surface](wikipedia://en/46573101) of degree 8 with 168 real nodes, found by (txt).[^o3] This is the most real nodes known for its degree;[^o4] however, the best proven upper bound, 174, does not match the lower bound given by this surface.[^o4][^o5]

:::
