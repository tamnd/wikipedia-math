# Cathy O'Neil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cathy O'Neil"}

**Catherine Helen O'Neil** (born 1972) is an American mathematician, data scientist, and author. She is the author of the *New York Times* best-seller *[Weapons of Math Destruction](wikipedia://en/54256085)*, and opinion columns in [Bloomberg View](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_View). O'Neil was active in the [Occupy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_movement) movement.[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

O'Neil attended [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley) as an undergraduate,[^o4] received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1999,[^o5][^o16] and afterward held positions in the mathematics departments of [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) and [Barnard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnard_College).[^o6] She left academia in 2007, and worked for four years in the finance industry.[^o7] She worked as an analyst at [D. E. Shaw & Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._E._Shaw_&_Co.)[^o8] After becoming disenchanted with the world of finance, O'Neil became involved with the [Occupy Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street) movement,[^o9][^o4] participating in its Alternative Banking Group.[^o10]

O'Neil operates the blog mathbabe.org and is a contributor to Bloomberg View.[^o11][^o12][^o13]

Her first book, *Doing Data Science*, was written with Rachel Schutt and published in 2013.[^o12] In 2016, her second book, *Weapons of Math Destruction*, was published, long-listed for the [National Book Award for Nonfiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Book_Award_for_Nonfiction)[^o14][^o15] and became a *New York Times* best-seller.[^o6] A third book, *The Shame Machine: Who Profits in the New Age of Humiliation*, was published in March 2022.

She is the founder of O'Neil Risk Consulting & Algorithmic Auditing (ORCAA), an algorithmic auditing company[^o16][^o17] and is on the board of the nonprofit ORCAA Collaborative Expert Assistance Network (OCEAN).

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1993 O'Neil was awarded the [Alice T. Schafer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181252) from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) and in 2019 she won the [MAA](wikipedia://en/198772)'s [Euler Book Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Book_Prize) for her book *Weapons of Math Destruction*.[^o19]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

O'Neil lives in Massachusetts and has three sons.[^o21][^o22]
