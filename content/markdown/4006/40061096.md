# Local uniformization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Local uniformization"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **local uniformization** is a weak form of [resolution of singularities](wikipedia://en/9914115), stating that a variety can be desingularized near any [valuation](wikipedia://en/347049), or in other words that the [Zariski–Riemann space](wikipedia://en/27902093) of the array is in some sense non-singular. Local uniformization was introduced by (txt), who separated the problem of resolving the singularities of a variety into the problem of local uniformization and the problem of combining the local uniformizations into a global desingularization.

:::

Local uniformization of a [variety](wikipedia://en/248808) at a valuation of its [function field](wikipedia://en/2783843) means finding a projective model of the variety such that the [center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_%28valuation_ring%29) of the valuation is non-singular. It is weaker than resolution of singularities: if there is a resolution of singularities then this is a model such that the center of every valuation is non-singular. (Zariski 1944b) proved that if one can show local uniformization of a variety then one can find a finite number of models such that every valuation has a non-singular center on at least one of these models. To complete a proof of resolution of singularities, it is then sufficient to show that one can combine these finite models into a single model, but this seems rather hard. (Local uniformization at a valuation does not directly imply resolution at the center of the valuation: roughly speaking; it only implies resolution in a sort of "wedge" near this point, and it seems hard to combine the resolutions of different wedges into a resolution at a point.)

(Zariski 1940) proved local uniformization of varieties in any dimension over fields of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 0, and used this to prove resolution of singularities for varieties in characteristic 0 of dimension at most 3. Local uniformization in positive characteristic seems to be much harder. (txt) proved local uniformization in all characteristics for surfaces and in characteristics at least 7 for 3-folds, and was able to deduce global resolution of singularities in these cases from this. (Cutkosky 2009) simplified Abhyankar's long proof. (txt) extended Abhyankar's proof of local uniformization of 3-folds to the remaining characteristics 2, 3, and 5. (Temkin 2013) showed that it is possible to find a local uniformization of any valuation after taking a [purely inseparable extension](wikipedia://en/4770099) of the function field.

Local uniformization in positive characteristic for varieties of dimension at least 4 is (as of 2019) an open problem.
