# Siegel modular variety

*Image: CalabiYau5.jpg.* A 2D slice of a [Calabi–Yau](wikipedia://en/188932) quintic. One such quintic is birationally equivalent to the compactification of the Siegel modular variety *A*<sub>1,3</sub>(2).[^o7]

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel modular variety"}

In mathematics, a **Siegel modular variety** or **Siegel moduli space** is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) that parametrizes certain types of [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) of a fixed [dimension](wikipedia://en/253251). More precisely, Siegel modular varieties are the [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) of [principally polarized abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152#Polarisations) of a fixed dimension. They are named after [Carl Ludwig Siegel](wikipedia://en/716602), the 20th-century German [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) who introduced the varieties in 1943.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Siegel modular varieties are the most basic examples of [Shimura varieties](wikipedia://en/10389861).[^o4] Siegel modular varieties generalize [moduli spaces of elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/3010057) to higher dimensions and play a central role in the theory of [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927), which generalize classical [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) to higher dimensions.[^o7] They also have applications to [black hole entropy](wikipedia://en/339350) and [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774).[^o18]

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The Siegel modular variety *A*<sub>*g*</sub>, which parametrize principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension *g*, can be constructed as the [complex analytic spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_space) constructed as the [quotient](wikipedia://en/39544418) of the [Siegel upper half-space](wikipedia://en/1141106) of degree *g* by the action of a [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977). Complex analytic spaces have naturally associated algebraic varieties by [Serre](wikipedia://en/205483)'s [GAGA](wikipedia://en/630505#GAGA).[^o7]

The Siegel modular variety *A*<sub>*g*</sub>(*n*), which parametrize principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension *g* with a [level *n*-structure](wikipedia://en/47016783), arises as the quotient of the Siegel upper half-space by the action of the [principal congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694#Congruence_subgroups) of level *n* of a symplectic group.[^o7]

A Siegel modular variety may also be constructed as a Shimura variety defined by the Shimura datum associated to a [symplectic vector space](wikipedia://en/292852).[^o4]

:::

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Siegel modular variety *A*<sub>*g*</sub> has dimension *g*(*g* + 1)/2.[^o7][^o10] Furthermore, it was shown by Yung-Sheng Tai, [Eberhard Freitag](wikipedia://en/50890271), and [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) that *A<sub>g</sub>* is of [general type](wikipedia://en/1866873#General_type) when *g* ≥ 7.[^o7][^o11][^o12][^o13]

Siegel modular varieties can be compactified to obtain [projective varieties](wikipedia://en/320469).[^o7] In particular, a compactification of *A*<sub>2</sub>(2) is [birationally equivalent](wikipedia://en/382733#Birational_maps) to the [Segre cubic](wikipedia://en/23421399) which is in fact [rational](wikipedia://en/865939).[^o7] Similarly, a compactification of *A*<sub>2</sub>(3) is birationally equivalent to the [Burkhardt quartic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkhardt_quartic) which is also rational.[^o7] Another Siegel modular variety, denoted *A*<sub>1,3</sub>(2), has a compactification that is birationally equivalent to the [Barth–Nieto quintic](wikipedia://en/23421758) which is birationally equivalent to a modular [Calabi–Yau manifold](wikipedia://en/188932) with [Kodaira dimension](wikipedia://en/1866873) zero.[^o7]

Siegel modular varieties cannot be [anabelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anabelian).[^o14]

:::

::: {#o16 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Siegel modular forms arise as [vector-valued differential forms](wikipedia://en/3772116) on Siegel modular varieties.[^o7] Siegel modular varieties have been used in conformal field theory via the theory of Siegel modular forms.[^o17] In [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), the function that naturally captures the microstates of black hole entropy in the D1D5P system of [supersymmetric black holes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremal_black_hole) is a Siegel modular form.[^o18]

In 1968, [Aleksei Parshin](wikipedia://en/50782329) showed that the [Mordell conjecture](wikipedia://en/172199) (now known as Faltings' theorem) would hold if the [Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815) finiteness conjecture was true by introducing Parshin's trick.[^o19][^o22] In 1983 and 1984, [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226) completed the proof of the Mordell conjecture by proving the Shafarevich finiteness conjecture.[^o20][^o21][^o22] The main idea of Faltings' proof is the comparison of [Faltings heights](wikipedia://en/3141855#Faltings_height) and [naive heights](wikipedia://en/3141855#Naive_height) via Siegel modular varieties.[^o23]
