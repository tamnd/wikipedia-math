# Connection (fibered manifold)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Connection"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a [fibered manifold](wikipedia://en/3247635) is [surjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surjective) [submersion](wikipedia://en/356413) of [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) $Y \to X$. Locally trivial fibered manifolds are [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526). Therefore, a notion of **connection** on fibered manifolds provides a general framework of a [connection](wikipedia://en/308815) on fiber bundles.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Let $\pi : Y \to X$ be a fibered manifold. A generalized *connection* on $Y$ is a [section](wikipedia://en/367621) $\Gamma : Y \to J^{1}Y$, where $J^{1}Y$ is the [jet manifold](wikipedia://en/928060) of $Y$.[^o4]

### Connection as a horizontal splitting {#Connection_as_a_horizontal_splitting}

With the above manifold $\pi$ there is the following canonical [short exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215) of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over $Y$:

::: {#o6 .equation cue=numblk name="1"}

$$0\to \mathrm{V}Y\to \mathrm{T}Y\to Y\times_X \mathrm{T}X\to 0\,, \tag{1}$$

:::

where $TY$ and $TX$ are the [tangent bundles](wikipedia://en/211794) of $Y$, respectively, $VY$ is the [vertical tangent bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_bundle) of $Y$, and $Y \times_{X} TX$ is the [pullback bundle](wikipedia://en/1665392) of $TX$ onto $Y$.

A **connection** on a fibered manifold $Y \to X$ is defined as a linear bundle morphism

::: {#o7 .equation cue=numblk name="2"}

$$\Gamma: Y\times_X \mathrm{T}X \to \mathrm{T}Y \tag{2}$$

:::

over $Y$ which [splits](wikipedia://en/138215) the exact sequence (1). A connection always exists.

Sometimes, this connection $\Gamma$ is called the [Ehresmann connection](wikipedia://en/5731754) because it yields the [horizontal distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_bundle)

$$\mathrm{H}Y=\Gamma\left(Y\times_X \mathrm{T}X \right) \subset \mathrm{T}Y$$

of $TY$ and its *horizontal decomposition* $TY = VY \oplus HY$.

At the same time, by an Ehresmann connection also is meant the following construction. Any connection $\Gamma$ on a fibered manifold $Y \to X$ yields a horizontal lift $\Gamma \circ \tau$ of a [vector field](wikipedia://en/62641) $\tau$ on $X$ onto $Y$, but need not defines the similar lift of a path in $X$ into $Y$. Let

$$\begin{aligned}\mathbb R\supset[,]\ni t&\to x(t)\in X \\ \mathbb R\ni t&\to y(t)\in Y\end{aligned}$$

be two smooth paths in $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Then $t \to y(t)$ is called the horizontal lift of $x(t)$ if

$$\pi(y(t))= x(t)\,, \qquad \dot y(t)\in \mathrm{H}Y \,, \qquad t\in\mathbb R\,.$$

A connection $\Gamma$ is said to be the *Ehresmann connection* if, for each path $x([0,1])$ in $X$, there exists its horizontal lift through any point $y \in \pi^{-1}(x([0,1]))$. A fibered manifold is a fiber bundle if and only if it admits such an Ehresmann connection.

### Connection as a tangent-valued form {#Connection_as_a_tangent-valued_form}

Given a fibered manifold $Y \to X$, let it be endowed with an atlas of fibered coordinates $(x^{\mu}, y^{i})$, and let $\Gamma$ be a connection on $Y \to X$. It yields uniquely the [horizontal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_form) [tangent-valued one-form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector-valued_form)

::: {#o9 .equation cue=numblk name="3"}

$$\Gamma = dx^\mu\otimes \left(\partial_\mu + \Gamma_\mu^i\left(x^\nu, y^j\right)\partial_i\right) \tag{3}$$

:::

on $Y$ which projects onto the canonical tangent-valued form ([tautological one-form](wikipedia://en/1855974) or [solder form](wikipedia://en/9792297))

$$\theta_X=dx^\mu\otimes\partial_\mu$$

on $X$, and *vice versa*. With this form, the horizontal splitting (2) reads

$$\Gamma:\partial_\mu\to \partial_\mu\rfloor\Gamma=\partial_\mu +\Gamma^i_\mu\partial_i\,.$$

In particular, the connection $\Gamma$ in (3) yields the horizontal lift of any vector field $\tau = \tau^{\mu} \partial_{\mu}$ on $X$ to a projectable vector field

$$\Gamma \tau=\tau\rfloor\Gamma=\tau^\mu\left(\partial_\mu +\Gamma^i_\mu\partial_i\right)\subset \mathrm{H}Y$$

on $Y$.

### Connection as a vertical-valued form {#Connection_as_a_vertical-valued_form}

The horizontal splitting (2) of the exact sequence (1) defines the corresponding splitting of the dual exact sequence

$$0\to Y\times_X \mathrm{T}^*X \to \mathrm{T}^*Y\to \mathrm{V}^*Y\to 0\,,$$

where $T*Y$ and $T*X$ are the [cotangent bundles](wikipedia://en/221600) of $Y$, respectively, and $V*Y \to Y$ is the [dual bundle](wikipedia://en/7824691) to $VY \to Y$, called the vertical cotangent bundle. This splitting is given by the vertical-valued form

$$\Gamma= \left(dy^i -\Gamma^i_\lambda dx^\lambda\right)\otimes\partial_i\,,$$

which also represents a connection on a fibered manifold.

Treating a connection as a vertical-valued form, one comes to the following important construction. Given a fibered manifold $Y \to X$, let $f : X' \to X$ be a morphism and $f \ast Y \to X'$ the [pullback bundle](wikipedia://en/1665392) of $Y$ by $f$. Then any connection $\Gamma$ (3) on $Y \to X$ induces the *pullback connection*

$$f*\Gamma=\left(dy^i-\left(\Gamma\circ \tilde f\right)^i_\lambda\frac{\partial f^\lambda}{\partial x'^\mu}dx'^\mu\right)\otimes\partial_i$$

on $f \ast Y \to X'$.

### Connection as a jet bundle section {#Connection_as_a_jet_bundle_section}

Let $J^{1}Y$ be the [jet manifold](wikipedia://en/928060) of sections of a fibered manifold $Y \to X$, with coordinates $(x^{\mu}, y^{i}, y^{i}_{\mu})$. Due to the canonical imbedding

$$\mathrm{J}^1Y\to_Y \left(Y\times_X \mathrm{T}^*X \right)\otimes_Y \mathrm{T}Y\,, \qquad \left(y^i_\mu\right)\to dx^\mu\otimes \left(\partial_\mu + y^i_\mu\partial_i\right)\,,$$

any connection $\Gamma$ (3) on a fibered manifold $Y \to X$ is represented by a global section

$$\Gamma :Y\to \mathrm{J}^1Y\,, \qquad y_\lambda^i\circ\Gamma=\Gamma_\lambda^i\,,$$

of the jet bundle $J^{1}Y \to Y$, and *vice versa*. It is an [affine bundle](wikipedia://en/39358329) modelled on a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524)

::: {#o12 .equation cue=numblk name="4"}

$$\left(Y\times_X T^*X \right)\otimes_Y \mathrm{V}Y\to Y\,. \tag{4}$$

:::

There are the following corollaries of this fact.

::: {#o13 .equation cue=numblk name="5"}

Connections on a fibered manifold $Y \to X$ make up an [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834) modelled on the vector space of [soldering forms](wikipedia://en/9792297) $\sigma=\sigma^i_\mu dx^\mu\otimes\partial_i \tag{5}$ on $Y \to X$, i.e., sections of the vector bundle (4).; Connection coefficients possess the coordinate transformation law ${\Gamma'}^i_\lambda = \frac{\partial x^\mu}{\partial {x'}^\lambda}\left(\partial_\mu {y'}^i+\Gamma^j_\mu\partial_j{y'}^i\right)\,.$; Every connection $\Gamma$ on a fibred manifold $Y \to X$ yields the first order [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886) $D_\Gamma:\mathrm{J}^1Y\to_Y \mathrm{T}^*X\otimes_Y \mathrm{V}Y\,, \qquad D_\Gamma = \left(y^i_\lambda -\Gamma^i_\lambda\right)dx^\lambda\otimes\partial_i\,,$ on $Y$ called the *covariant differential* relative to the connection $\Gamma$. If $s : X \to Y$ is a section, its covariant differential $\nabla^\Gamma s = \left(\partial_\lambda s^i - \Gamma_\lambda^i\circ s\right) dx^\lambda\otimes \partial_i\,,$ and the [covariant derivative](wikipedia://en/431848) $\nabla_\tau^\Gamma s=\tau\rfloor\nabla^\Gamma s$ along a vector field $\tau$ on $X$ are defined.

:::

:::

## Curvature and torsion {#Curvature_and_torsion}

Given the connection $\Gamma$ (3) on a fibered manifold $Y \to X$, its *curvature* is defined as the [Nijenhuis differential](wikipedia://en/3291722)

$$\begin{aligned} R&=\tfrac12 d_\Gamma\Gamma\\&=\tfrac12 [\Gamma,\Gamma]_\mathrm{FN} \\&= \tfrac12 R_{\lambda\mu}^i \, dx^\lambda\wedge dx^\mu\otimes\partial_i\,, \\ R_{\lambda\mu}^i &= \partial_\lambda\Gamma_\mu^i - \partial_\mu\Gamma_\lambda^i + \Gamma_\lambda^j\partial_j \Gamma_\mu^i - \Gamma_\mu^j\partial_j \Gamma_\lambda^i\,. \end{aligned}$$

This is a vertical-valued horizontal two-form on $Y$.

Given the connection $\Gamma$ (3) and the soldering form $\sigma$ (5), a *torsion* of $\Gamma$ with respect to $\sigma$ is defined as

$$T = d_\Gamma \sigma = \left(\partial_\lambda\sigma_\mu^i + \Gamma_\lambda^j\partial_j\sigma_\mu^i -\partial_j\Gamma_\lambda^i\sigma_\mu^j\right) \, dx^\lambda\wedge dx^\mu\otimes \partial_i\,.$$

## Bundle of principal connections {#Bundle_of_principal_connections}

Let $\pi : P \to M$ be a [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) with a structure [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) $G$. A [principal connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_connection) on $P$ usually is described by a Lie algebra-valued connection one-form on $P$. At the same time, a principal connection on $P$ is a global [section](wikipedia://en/367621) of the jet bundle $J^{1}P \to P$ which is [equivariant](wikipedia://en/422711) with respect to the canonical right action of $G$ in $P$. Therefore, it is represented by a global section of the quotient bundle $C = J^{1}P/G \to M$, called the *bundle of principal connections*. It is an [affine bundle](wikipedia://en/39358329) modelled on the vector bundle $VP/G \to M$ whose typical fiber is the [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) $\mathbf{g}$ of structure group $G$, and where $G$ acts on by the [adjoint representation](wikipedia://en/302188). There is the canonical imbedding of $C$ to the quotient bundle $TP/G$ which also is called the [bundle of principal connections](wikipedia://en/310950).

Given a basis $\{e_{m}$} for a Lie algebra of $G$, the fiber bundle $C$ is endowed with bundle coordinates $(x^{\mu}, a^{m}_{\mu})$, and its sections are represented by [vector-valued one-forms](wikipedia://en/3772116)

$$A=dx^\lambda\otimes \left(\partial_\lambda + a^m_\lambda {\mathrm e}_m\right)\,,$$

where

$$a^m_\lambda \, dx^\lambda\otimes {\mathrm e}_m$$

are the familiar local [connection forms](wikipedia://en/310950) on $M$.

Let us note that the jet bundle $J^{1}C$ of $C$ is a [configuration space](wikipedia://en/24585634) of [Yang–Mills gauge theory](wikipedia://en/672202). It admits the canonical decomposition

$$\begin{aligned} a_{\lambda\mu}^r &= \tfrac12\left(F_{\lambda\mu}^r + S_{\lambda\mu}^r\right) \\ &= \tfrac12\left(a_{\lambda\mu}^r + a_{\mu\lambda}^r - c_{pq}^r a_\lambda^p a_\mu^q\right) + \tfrac12\left(a_{\lambda\mu}^r - a_{\mu\lambda}^r + c_{pq}^r a_\lambda^p a_\mu^q\right)\,, \end{aligned}$$

where

$$F=\tfrac{1}{2} F_{\lambda\mu}^m \, dx^\lambda\wedge dx^\mu\otimes {\mathrm e}_m$$

is called the *strength form* of a principal connection.
