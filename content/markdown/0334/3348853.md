# Zdzisław Krygowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdzisław Krygowski"}

**Zdzisław Jan Ewangelii Antoni Krygowski** ([Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów), 1872 — 1955, [Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań)) was a Polish mathematician, [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [Lwów Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_Polytechnic) (1917–18), and professor at [Poznań University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań_University) (1919–38, 1946–55).

:::

## Enigma {#Enigma}

Krygowski has become famous in the history of [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology) for having assisted the [Polish General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_the_Polish_Army) in setting up its cryptology course for [Poznań University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań_University) mathematics students that began on 15 January 1929. This led eventually to the General Staff's [Cipher Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biuro_Szyfrów)'s recruitment of [Marian Rejewski](wikipedia://en/4349420), [Henryk Zygalski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henryk_Zygalski) and [Jerzy Różycki](wikipedia://en/197851), who would jointly break and [decrypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decryption) the [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II)-era [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [Enigma-machine ciphers](wikipedia://en/9256), beginning at the end of December 1932.[^o3]

## Family {#Family}

Zdzisław Krygowski had three brothers - Tadeusz, Kazimierz and Stanisław. He married Rose New from England. They had one daughter Eileen Krygowska-Korczyńska, who after World War II worked for [Jan Nowak-Jeziorański](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Nowak-Jeziorański) at [RFE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Free_Europe/Radio_Liberty).
