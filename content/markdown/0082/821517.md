# Richard von Mises

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard von Mises"}

**Richard Martin von Mises** (; 19 April 1883 – 14 July 1953) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) scientist and mathematician who worked on [solid mechanics](wikipedia://en/458866), [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), [aerodynamics](wikipedia://en/2819), [aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautics), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He held the position of [Gordon McKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_McKay) Professor of Aerodynamics and Applied Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He described his work in his own words shortly before his death as, "practical [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [integral](wikipedia://en/474234) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), mechanics, [hydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and aerodynamics, [constructive geometry](wikipedia://en/61229), probability calculus, statistics and philosophy".[^o3]

:::

Although best known for his mathematical work, von Mises also contributed to the philosophy of science as a [neo-positivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_positivism) and [empiricist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empiricism), following the line of [Ernst Mach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Mach). Historians of the [Vienna Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Circle) of [logical empiricism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_empiricism) recognize a "first phase" from 1907 through 1914 with [Philipp Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Frank), [Hans Hahn](wikipedia://en/1075212), and [Otto Neurath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Neurath).[^o4] His older brother, [Ludwig von Mises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_von_Mises), held an opposite point of view with respect to positivism and epistemology. His brother developed *[praxeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praxeology)*, an *a priori* view.[^o5]

During his time in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul), Mises maintained close contact with [Philipp Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Frank),[^o6] a logical positivist and Professor of Physics in Prague until 1938. His literary interests included the Austrian novelist [Robert Musil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Musil) and the poet [Rainer Maria Rilke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainer_Maria_Rilke), on whom he became a recognized expert.[^o7]

## Life {#Life}

Richard Martin Edler von Mises[^o9] was born in [Lemberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) (today [Lviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv), Ukraine) into a [Jewish family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_family), eighteen months after his brother [Ludwig von Mises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_von_Mises), who became a prominent [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) of the [Austrian School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_School), a [heterodox school of economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterodox_economics). His parents were Arthur Edler von Mises, a doctor of technical sciences who worked for the [Austrian State Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Royal_Austrian_State_Railways), and Adele Landau. Richard and Ludwig had a younger brother, Karl von Mises, who died as an infant from [Scarlet Fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlet_fever).[^o10] Richard attended the [Akademisches Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademisches_Gymnasium_%28Vienna%29) in Vienna, from which he graduated with honors in [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) and mathematics in autumn 1901. After graduating in mathematics, physics and engineering from the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology), he was appointed as [Georg Hamel](wikipedia://en/1509021)'s assistant in Brünn ([Brno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brno)). In 1905, still a student, he published an article on the geometry of curves called "Zur konstruktiven Infinitesimalgeometrie der ebenen Kurven," in the prestigious *Zeitschrift für Mathematik und Physik*.

*Image: Mises family crest with transparent background.png.* The von Mises's family [crest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crest_%28heraldry%29)

In 1908, von Mises was awarded a doctorate from Vienna (his dissertation was on "the determination of flywheel masses in crank drives") and he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from Brünn (Brno) (on "Theory of the [Waterwheels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterwheel)") to lecture on engineering. In 1909, at 26, he was appointed professor of applied mathematics in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg) (then part of the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire)) and received [Prussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia) [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship). His application for a teaching position at the [Brno University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brno_University_of_Technology) was interrupted by the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I).

Before the war he had already become a pilot and lectured on aircraft design, and in 1913 at Strasbourg he gave the first university course on powered flight. At the outbreak of war it was natural for him to join the Austro-Hungarian army as a [test pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_pilot) and a flying instructor. In 1915, he supervised the construction of a 600-[horsepower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsepower) (450 [kW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KW)) aircraft – the "Mises-Flugzeug" (Mises aircraft) for the Austrian army. It was completed in 1916 but never saw active service.

After the war, von Mises held the new chair of [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics) and [aerodynamics](wikipedia://en/2819) at the [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden) *Technische Hochschule*. In 1919 he was appointed director and full professor at the new Institute of Applied Mathematics created at the behest of [Erhard Schmidt](wikipedia://en/672191) at the University of Berlin. In 1921 he founded the journal *Zeitschrift für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik* and became its editor.[^o11]

With the rise of the [National Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialist_German_Workers_Party) to power in 1933, Mises felt his position threatened[^o12], despite his [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War) military service. He moved to [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), where he assumed the new chair of pure and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Istanbul). In 1939 he accepted a position in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), where in 1944 he was appointed as Gordon McKay Professor of Aerodynamics and Applied Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). In 1943 he married [Hilda Geiringer](wikipedia://en/7553047), a mathematician who had been his assistant at the Institute and moved with her to Turkey and then to the U.S.

In 1950, von Mises declined honorary membership from the [Communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist)-dominated [East German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Democratic_Republic) Academy of Science.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In aerodynamics, von Mises made advances in [boundary-layer flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_layer_flow) theory and [airfoil](wikipedia://en/290053) design. He developed the *[distortion energy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Mises_stress)* of [stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_%28physics%29), an important factor in material strength calculations.

His ideas were not universally well received, although [Alexander Ostrowski](wikipedia://en/5827091) had said of him: "Only with the appointment of Richard von Mises to the University of Berlin did the first serious German school of applied mathematics with a broad sphere of influence come into existence. Von Mises was an incredibly dynamic person and at the same time amazingly versatile like [Runge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_David_Tolmé_Runge). He was especially well versed in the realm of technology." and also wrote "Because of his dynamic personality his occasional major blunders were somehow tolerated. One has even forgiven him his theory of probability." Yet [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161), whose rival [axiomatisation](wikipedia://en/188401) was better received, was less severe: "The basis for the applicability of the results of the mathematical theory of probability to real 'random phenomena' must depend on some form of the frequency concept of probability, the unavoidable nature of which has been established by von Mises in a spirited manner."

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), he proposed the famous "[birthday problem](wikipedia://en/73242)".[^o14] He also defined the [impossibility of a gambling system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impossibility_of_a_gambling_system).[^o15][^o16]

In [solid mechanics](wikipedia://en/458866), von Mises contributed to the theory of [plasticity](wikipedia://en/240123) by formulating the [von Mises yield criterion](wikipedia://en/1091136), independently of [Tytus Maksymilian Huber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tytus_Maksymilian_Huber).

He is often credited for the [Principle of Maximum Plastic Dissipation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_Maximum_Plastic_Dissipation).

The [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik)[^o17] (Society of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics) awards a [Richard von Mises Prize](wikipedia://en/56348140)[^o18] since 1989.
