# Ian Goldberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ian Goldberg"}

**Ian Avrum Goldberg** (born March 31, 1973) is a [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [cypherpunk](wikipedia://en/37314). He is best known for breaking [Netscape's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape_Communications_Corporation) implementation of [SSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer) (with [David Wagner](wikipedia://en/298253)),[^o3] and for his role as chief scientist of Radialpoint (formerly [Zero Knowledge Systems](wikipedia://en/41832496)), a Canadian software company. Goldberg is currently a professor at the Faculty of Mathematics of the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) within the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), and the Canada Research Chair in Privacy Enhancing Technologies.[^o4] He was formerly [Tor Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tor_%28anonymity_network%29) board of directors chairman,[^o5] and is one of the designers of [off the record messaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off_the_record_messaging).[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Goldberg attended high school at the [University of Toronto Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Schools), graduating in 1991. In 1995, he received a [BMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Mathematics) from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) and computer science. He obtained a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in December 2000. His thesis was entitled *A Pseudonymous Communications Infrastructure for the Internet*.[^o8] His advisor was [Eric Brewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Brewer_%28scientist%29).

## Accomplishments {#Accomplishments}

As a high school student, Goldberg was a member of Canada's team to the [International Math Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) from 1989 to 1991, where he received a bronze, silver, and gold medal respectively.[^o10] He was also a member of [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) team that won the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_International_Collegiate_Programming_Contest) in 1994.[^o11] In 1998, *Wired* Magazine chose him as a member of the "Wired 25".[^o12] In 2011 he won the [EFF Pioneer Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFF_Pioneer_Award).[^o13] In 2019, he won the [USENIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USENIX) Security Test of Time Award along with his colleagues David Wagner and Randi Thomas and former PhD supervisor Eric Brewer.[^o14] In 2023, he was named an [ACM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow).[^o15]

## Work in cryptography {#Work_in_cryptography}

In 1995, Goldberg with [David Wagner](wikipedia://en/298253) discovered a flaw in the [random number generator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_number_generator) used for temporary key generation in the SSL implementation of [Netscape Navigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape_Navigator).[^o3][^o17]

One of the first cryptanalyses on the [WEP](wikipedia://en/422014) wireless encryption protocol was conducted by Goldberg with [Nikita Borisov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Borisov) and David Wagner, revealing serious flaws in its design.[^o18][^o19]

Goldberg was a co-author of the [Off-the-Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-the-Record_Messaging) [instant messaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_messaging) encryption protocol. He is also the author of the [Perl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perl) script included in the novel *[Cryptonomicon](wikipedia://en/21861)* by [Neal Stephenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neal_Stephenson).[^o20]

In 2009 Goldberg was co-author of the Sphinx Mix Format,[^o21] which is nowadays implemented with the extension of a per-hop payload to increase the privacy of both payer and payee while routing [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265) payments through the [Lightning Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightning_Network).[^o22]

[Vitalik Buterin](wikipedia://en/41729424), co-founder of [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum), was a research assistant of Goldberg while a student at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o23][^o24]

Goldberg is a member of the Cryptography, Security and Privacy group as well as the Cybersecurity and Privacy Institute (CPI). He has been collaborating with the CPI works on the development of a new interdisciplinary research and education program.[^o4]
