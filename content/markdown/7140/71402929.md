# Tame topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tame topology"}

Tame manifold In mathematics, a **tame topology** is a hypothetical [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) proposed by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) in his research program *[Esquisse d’un programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquisse_d’un_programme)*[^o2] under the French name *topologie modérée* (moderate topology). It is a topology in which the theory of [dévissage](wikipedia://en/26377503) can be applied to [stratified structures](wikipedia://en/25886609) such as [semialgebraic](wikipedia://en/13329722) or [semianalytic sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semianalytic_set),[^o3] and which excludes some [pathological](wikipedia://en/372742) spaces that do not correspond to intuitive notions of spaces.

:::

Some authors consider an [o-minimal structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O-minimal_structure) to be a candidate for realizing tame topology in the [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) case.[^o4][^o5] There are also some other suggestions.[^o6]
