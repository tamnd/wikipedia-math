# Liu Sifeng

[Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Liu Sifeng"}

**Liu Sifeng** (; born 15 July 1955) is a Chinese systems engineer. He was the director of the Institute for Grey Systems Studies at [Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_University_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics), Nanjing, China. He is best known for his work on [grey system theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_system_theory).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Liu obtained his [BE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Engineering) in Mathematics from [Henan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_University) in 1981, then his MS in Economics (1986) and PhD in Systems Engineering (1998) from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huazhong_University_of_Science_and_Technology), Wuhan, China.[^o4] He was the doctoral student of [Julong Deng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julong_Deng), the founder of grey system theory.[^o5]

Liu was appointed as a lecturer at Henan University in 1985. He was promoted through the ranks, reaching full professor in 1994. In 2000, he moved as a distinguished professor to [Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_University_of_Aeronautics_and_Astronautics), where he also serves as director of the Institute for Grey Systems Studies. In 2014, he worked as a research professor at [De Montfort University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Montfort_University) in [Leicester, UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester,_UK).[^o4][^o5]

Liu is the editor-in-chief of *Grey Systems: Theory and Application*,[^o6] and of the *Journal of Grey System*.[^o7]

In 2013, he became the Fellow of [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Skłodowska-Curie_Actions).[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Liu is an honorary fellow of the World Organisation of Systems and Cybernetics,[^o10] and an honorary editor of "International Journal of Grey Systems" (USA).[^o11] German Chancellor [Angela Merkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Merkel) mentioned Liu's contributions to grey system theory in a 2019 speech at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.[^o12] In 2023, he received the Global Excellence Award from the Grey Systems Society of Pakistan.[^o13]
