# Paolo Piccione

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paolo Piccione"}

**Paolo Piccione** (born 24 April 1964) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is professor at the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) since 1996 and president of the [Brazilian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociedade_Brasileira_de_Matemática) since 2017.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Piccione was born in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) on 24 April 1964, in a genuinely Italian family – his paternal grandfather was from Sicily; and the maternal one of central Italy – of a social class "without any particular prominence", although he enjoyed the important advances of Italian society in the postwar period, Piccione achieved an education in high-level public schools. Like his parents, three decades before him, he graduated from the same [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), in Rome, where he obtained Laurea in Matematica and Master degree in 1987 supervisor by [Alessandro Figà Talamanca](wikipedia://en/36628813). He received his a PhD in Mathematics degree from the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1994 advisor by [Adrian Ocneanu](wikipedia://en/11870817).[^o4]

He is currently a full professor at the [Institute of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics,_University_of_São_Paulo), [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo), where he teaches since 1996 and completed his *[livre docência](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Law) (Doctor of Law's USP)*.

## Work {#Work}

His research has emphasis on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), he works mainly on the following topics: [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and variational geometric problems, Riemannian and global Lorentzian geometry, [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560), [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [Hamiltonian systems](wikipedia://en/1197531). Piccione develops research that deals with topics with possible applications to physics. The main results achieved in the area of Lorentzian geometry have an interpretation within [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024).[^o6]
