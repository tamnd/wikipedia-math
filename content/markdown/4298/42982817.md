# Matsusaka's big theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matsusaka's big theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), given an [ample line bundle](wikipedia://en/1186896) *L* on a compact complex manifold *X*, **Matsusaka's big theorem** gives an integer *m*, depending only on the [Hilbert polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_polynomial) of *L*, such that the tensor power *L*<sup>*n*</sup> is very ample for *n* ≥ *m*.

:::

The theorem was proved by [Teruhisa Matsusaka](wikipedia://en/47886466) in 1972 and named by Lieberman and [Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) in 1975.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

The theorem has an application to the theory of [Hilbert schemes](wikipedia://en/6612581).
