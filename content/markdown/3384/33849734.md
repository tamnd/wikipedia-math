# Identity channel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Identity channel"}

In [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory), the **identity channel** is a noise-free [quantum channel](wikipedia://en/1013550). That is, the channel outputs exactly what was put in.[^o2]

:::

The identity channel is commonly denoted as $I$, $\mathsf{id}$ or $\mathbb{I}$.
