# Hans Schneider (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Schneider"}

**Hans Schneider** (24 January 1927 – 28 October 2014) was a British-American mathematician, and James Joseph Sylvester Emeritus Professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). He was the first president of the International Matrix Group (1987–1990) and its successor, the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149) (1990 – 1996), which established the triennial [Hans Schneider Prize](wikipedia://en/58527033) in 1993.[^o2] Schneider was a founding editor (1968–1972) and then editor-in-chief of *[Linear Algebra and Its Applications](wikipedia://en/42068102)* (1972 – 2012)[^o3] and an Advisory Editor of the *[Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra](wikipedia://en/67679696)*.[^o4][^o5]

:::

He received his Ph.D. from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 1952; his advisor was [Alexander Craig Aitken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Craig_Aitken). Following his doctorate, he taught at the [Queen's University of Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_of_Belfast) until 1959, when he moved to the University of Wisconsin. He retired in 1993.[^o5] He was the author of over 160 research papers. His research covered many topics in linear algebra, such as Perron Frobenius theory and related topics, inertia theory. and lately [max algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-plus_algebra). For his experiences in the years 1938 – 1940 see the Kindertransport site.[^o6]

Schneider died of cancer at the age of 87.[^o7] He was the grandfather of [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) musician / producer [Kurt Hugo Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Hugo_Schneider).[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11]
