# Ueli Maurer (cryptographer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ueli Maurer"}

**Ueli Maurer** (born 26 May 1960[^o4]) is a professor of [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) at the [Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) (ETH Zurich).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Maurer studied electrical engineering at ETH Zurich and obtained his PhD in 1990,[^o4] advised by [James Massey](wikipedia://en/9543907).[^o6] He joined [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) as a postdoc from 1990 to 1991.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

In a seminal work, he showed that the [Diffie-Hellman problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffie-Hellman_problem) is (under certain conditions) [equivalent](wikipedia://en/9259) to solving the [discrete log](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_log) problem.[^o8]

From 2002 until 2008, Maurer also served on the board of [Tamedia AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamedia).[^o9]

Maurer was appointed editor-in-chief of the [Journal of Cryptology](wikipedia://en/87045) in 2002[^o10] for a three-year term.[^o11] He was reappointed to a second three-year term as editor-in-chief of the same journal from 2005.[^o12]

In 2008, Maurer was named a Fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043) "for fundamental contributions to information-theoretic cryptography, service to the IACR, and sustained educational leadership in cryptology."[^o13] In 2015, he was named a [Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery) "for contributions to cryptography and [information security](wikipedia://en/15036)."[^o14] In 2016, he was awarded the [RSA Award for Excellence in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_Award_for_Excellence_in_Mathematics).[^o15]

## Accolades {#Accolades}

- 2016: [RSA Security](wikipedia://en/301240) (RSA) Award for Excellence in Mathematics
- 2015: Fellow of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) (ACM)
- 2008: Fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043) (IACR)
- 2007: Member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina)
- 2003: Fellow of the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE)
