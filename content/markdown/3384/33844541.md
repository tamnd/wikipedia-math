# Myers–Steenrod theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Myers–Steenrod theorem"}

Two [theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theorems) in the [mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical) field of [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) bear the name **Myers–Steenrod theorem**, both from a 1939 paper by [Myers](wikipedia://en/3327747) and [Steenrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Steenrod). The first states that every distance-preserving surjective map (that is, an [isometry](wikipedia://en/254777) of [metric spaces](wikipedia://en/20018)) between two [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [Riemannian manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_manifolds) is a [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) [isometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometry_%28Riemannian_geometry%29) of Riemannian manifolds. A simpler proof was subsequently given by [Richard Palais](wikipedia://en/34779708) in 1957. The main difficulty lies in showing that a distance-preserving map, which is a priori only [continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_%28topology%29), is actually [differentiable](wikipedia://en/330206).

:::

The second theorem, which is harder to prove, states that the [isometry group](wikipedia://en/1101694) $\mathrm{Isom}(M)$ of a connected $\mathcal{C}^2$ Riemannian manifold $M$ is a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) in a way that is compatible with the [compact-open topology](wikipedia://en/726335) and such that the action $\mathrm{Isom}(M)\times M \longrightarrow M$ is $\mathcal{C}^1$differentiable (in both variables). This is a generalization of the easier, similar statement when $M$ is a [Riemannian symmetric space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_symmetric_space): for instance, the group of isometries of the two-dimensional unit [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) is the [orthogonal group](wikipedia://en/173954) $O(3)$. A harder generalization is given by the Bochner-Montgomery theorem, where $\mathrm{Isom}(M)$ is replaced by a locally compact transformation group of diffeomorphisms of $M$.[^o2]
