# Gaetano Scorza

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaetano Scorza"}

**Bernardino Gaetano Scorza** (29 September 1876, in [Morano Calabro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morano_Calabro) – 6 August 1939, in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome)) was an Italian mathematician working in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), whose work inspired the theory of [Scorza varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorza_varieties).

:::
