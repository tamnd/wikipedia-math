# Paul Baum (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Baum"}

*Image: Baum and Connes.jpg.* Paul Baum (left) and Alain Connes (2004)

**Paul Frank Baum** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), the Evan Pugh Professor of Mathematics at Pennsylvania State University. He is known for formulating the [Baum–Connes conjecture](wikipedia://en/11774498) with [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340) in the early 1980s.[^o2]

:::

Baum studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), earning a bachelor's degree *summa cum laude* in 1958. He went on to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) for his graduate studies, completing his Ph.D. in 1963 under the supervision of [John Coleman Moore](wikipedia://en/13433862) and [Norman Steenrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Steenrod). He was several times a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1964–65, 1976–77, 2004)[^o3] After several visiting positions and an assistant professorship at Princeton, he moved to [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1967, and remained there until 1987 when he moved to Penn State. He became a distinguished professor in 1991 and was given his named chair in 1996.[^o4]

In 2007, a meeting in honor of his 70th birthday was held in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) by the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4][^o6] In 2011, the [University of Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado) gave him an honorary doctorate.[^o4] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
