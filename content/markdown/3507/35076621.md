# Novikov–Shubin invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Novikov–Shubin invariant"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Novikov–Shubin invariant**, introduced by (txt), is an invariant of a compact [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) related to the [spectrum](wikipedia://en/206137) of the [Laplace operator](wikipedia://en/174706) acting on square-integrable [differential forms](wikipedia://en/221519) on its universal cover.

:::

The Novikov–Shubin invariant gives a measure of the density of eigenvalues around zero. It can be computed from a [triangulation](wikipedia://en/1440976) of the manifold, and it is a [homotopy invariant](wikipedia://en/212250). In particular, it does not depend on the chosen Riemannian metric on the manifold.[^n1]
