# NUTS statistical regions of Turkey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Turkey"}

*Image: NUTS Map of Turkey.png.* NUTS of Turkey on first level

*Image: Turkey NUTS 2nd Level.png.* NUTS of Turkey on second level

As a [candidate country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_enlargement_of_the_European_Union#Recognised_candidates) of the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) (TR) is included in the [Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) (NUTS). Defined in 2002 in agreement between [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat) and the Turkish authorities, Turkey's NUTS classifications are officially termed *statistical regions*, as Turkey is not a member of the EU and Eurostat only defines NUTS for member states.[^o2] The three NUTS levels are:

:::

- NUTS-1: 12 [Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-level_NUTS_of_the_European_Union)
- NUTS-2: 26 [Subregions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics#Levels)
- NUTS-3: 81 [Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Turkey)

Below the NUTS levels, there are two [LAU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_administrative_unit) levels:

- LAU-1: 923 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey)
- LAU-2: 37,675 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_municipalities_in_Turkey)[^o3]
