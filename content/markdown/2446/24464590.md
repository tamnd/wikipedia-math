::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 5-simplexes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 5-simplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex).

:::

There are unique 2 degrees of truncation. Vertices of the truncation 5-simplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 5-simplex. Vertices of the bitruncation 5-simplex are located on the triangular faces of the 5-simplex.

The **truncated 5-simplex** has 30 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 75 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 80 [triangular](wikipedia://en/30654) [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 45 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) (15 [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606), and 30 [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651)), and 12 [4-faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-face) (6 [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) and 6 [truncated 5-cells](wikipedia://en/4046891)).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated hexateron (Acronym: tix) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *truncated 5-simplex* can be most simply constructed on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 6-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,1,2) *or* of (0,1,2,2,2,2). These coordinates come from facets of the [truncated 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_6-orthoplex) and [bitruncated 6-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_6-cube) respectively.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Bitruncated hexateron (Acronym: bittix) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *bitruncated 5-simplex* can be most simply constructed on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 6-space as permutations of (0,0,0,1,2,2) *or* of (0,0,1,2,2,2). These represent positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) facets of the [bitruncated 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_6-orthoplex), and the [tritruncated 6-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tritruncated_6-cube) respectively.
