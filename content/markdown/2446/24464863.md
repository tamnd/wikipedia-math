# Michael Somos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Somos"}

**Michael Somos** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who was a visiting scholar in the [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University) Mathematics and Statistics department for four years and is a visiting scholar at The [Catholic University of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_America). In the late eighties he proposed a conjecture about certain [polynomial recurrences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_recurrence), now called [Somos sequences](wikipedia://en/24612539),[^o2] that surprisingly in some cases contain only integers. [Somos' quadratic recurrence constant](wikipedia://en/2223645) is also named after him.

:::
