# IDEA NXT

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="IDEA NXT"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), the **IDEA NXT** algorithm (previously known as **FOX**) is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed by Pascal Junod and [Serge Vaudenay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_Vaudenay) of [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) ([Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland)). It was conceived between 2001 and 2003. The project was originally named FOX and was published in 2003. In May 2005, it was announced by MediaCrypt under the name **IDEA NXT**. IDEA NXT is the successor to the [International Data Encryption Algorithm](wikipedia://en/15076) (IDEA) and also uses the [Lai–Massey scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai–Massey_scheme).[^o4] MediaCrypt AG holds [patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent) on elements of IDEA and IDEA NXT. The cipher is specified in two configurations: NXT64 (with block of 64 bits, key of 128 bits, 16 rounds) and NXT128 (with block of 128 bits, key of 256 bits, 16 rounds).
