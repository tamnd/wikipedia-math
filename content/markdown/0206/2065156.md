# Claude Mydorge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claude Mydorge"}

*Image: Mydorge, Claude – Prodromi catoptricorum et dioptricorum sive Conicorum operis ad abdita radii reflexi et refracti mysteria praevij et facem praeferentis, 1639 – BEIC 182660.jpg.* *Prodromi catoptricorum et dioptricorum sive Conicorum operis ad abdita radii reflexi et refracti mysteria praevij et facem praeferentis*, 1639

**Claude Mydorge** (1585 – July 1647) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His primary contributions were in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939).

:::

Mydorge served on a scientific committee (whose members included [Pierre Hérigone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Hérigone) and [Étienne Pascal](wikipedia://en/2121127)) set up to determine whether [Jean-Baptiste Morin's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste_Morin_%28mathematician%29) scheme for determining [longitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitude) from the Moon's motion was practical.
