# List of chaotic maps

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of chaotic maps"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a [chaotic map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotic_map) is a [map](wikipedia://en/516931) (an [evolution function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete-time_dynamical_system)) that exhibits some sort of [chaotic behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotic_behavior). Maps may be parameterized by a discrete-time or a continuous-time parameter. Discrete maps usually take the form of [iterated functions](wikipedia://en/1448702). Chaotic maps often occur in the study of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

Chaotic maps and [iterated functions](wikipedia://en/1448702) often generate [fractals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractals). Some fractals are studied as objects themselves, as [sets](wikipedia://en/26691) rather than in terms of the maps that generate them. This is often because there are several different iterative procedures that generate the same fractal. See also [Universality (dynamical systems)](wikipedia://en/1752072).
