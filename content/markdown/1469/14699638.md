# Karl Hessenberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Hessenberg"}

**Karl Adolf Hessenberg** (September 8, 1904 – February 22, 1959) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer). The [Hessenberg matrix](wikipedia://en/519204) form is named after him.

:::

## Education {#Education}

From 1925 to 1930 he studied [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) at the Technische Hochschule Darmstadt (today [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt)) and graduated with a [diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma). From 1931 to 1932 he was an assistant to [Alwin Walther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alwin_Walther) at the Technische Hochschule Darmstadt, afterwards he worked at the power station in [Worms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worms,_Germany), Germany. From 1936 he worked as an engineer at [AEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEG_%28German_company%29), first in Berlin and later in Frankfurt. In 1940 he received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from Alwin Walther at the Technische Hochschule in Darmstadt.[^o3]

## Family {#Family}

Hessenberg was also the brother of composer [Kurt Hessenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Hessenberg), and the great-grandson of doctor and author [Heinrich Hoffmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Hoffmann_%28author%29).

The [Hessenberg sum and product](wikipedia://en/2139226#Natural_operations) of ordinals are named after [Gerhard Hessenberg](wikipedia://en/3759880), another mathematician and near relative of Karl Hessenberg.

His father was Eduard Hessenberg, and his mother was Emma Kugler Hessenberg.[^o5]
