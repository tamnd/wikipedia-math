# Carleson–Jacobs theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carleson–Jacobs theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Carleson–Jacobs theorem**, introduced by (txt), describes the best approximation to a [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122) on the [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071) by a function in a [Hardy space](wikipedia://en/314780).[^n1]

:::
