# Jessen–Wintner theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jessen–Wintner theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Jessen–Wintner theorem**, introduced by (txt), asserts that a [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685) of **Jessen–Wintner type**, meaning the sum of an almost surely convergent series of independent discrete random variables, is of pure type.

:::
