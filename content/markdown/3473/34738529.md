# Lange's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lange's conjecture"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Lange's conjecture** is a theorem about stability of [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over curves, introduced by Herbert\_Lange\_(mathematician)[^o2] and proved by [Montserrat Teixidor i Bigas](wikipedia://en/38849883) and [Barbara Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Russo) in 1999.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *C* be a smooth projective [curve](wikipedia://en/253260) of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891#Algebraic_geometry) greater or equal to 2. For [generic](wikipedia://en/12886758) vector bundles $E_1$ and $E_2$ on *C* of ranks and degrees $(r_1, d_1)$ and $(r_2, d_2)$, respectively, a generic extension

$$0 \to E_1 \to E \to E_2 \to 0$$

has *E* [stable](wikipedia://en/18909129) provided that $\mu(E_1) < \mu(E_2)$, where $\mu(E_i) = d_i/r_i$ is the [slope](wikipedia://en/18909129#Stable_vector_bundles_over_curves) of the respective bundle. The notion of a generic vector bundle here is a generic point in the [moduli space of semistable vector bundles](wikipedia://en/361609#Moduli_of_vector_bundles) on *C*, and a generic extension is one that corresponds to a generic point in the vector space [$\operatorname{Ext}^1$](wikipedia://en/840758)$(E_2,E_1)$.

An original formulation by Lange is that for a pair of integers $(r_1, d_1)$ and $(r_2, d_2)$ such that $d_1/ r_1 < d_2/r_2$, there exists a [short exact sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_exact_sequence) as above with *E* stable. This formulation is equivalent because the existence of a short exact sequence like that is an open condition on *E* in the moduli space of semistable vector bundles on *C*.

:::
