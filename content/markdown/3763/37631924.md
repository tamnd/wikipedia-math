# Algebrator

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Algebrator"}

**Algebrator** (also called **Softmath**) is a [computer algebra system](wikipedia://en/54427) (CAS), originally known as Edusym and developed beginning in 1988. This is a CAS specifically geared towards algebra education. Beside the computation results, it shows step by step the solution process and context sensitive explanations.

:::
