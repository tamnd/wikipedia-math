# Symmetric variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symmetric variety"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **symmetric variety** is an algebraic analogue of a [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877) in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), given by a quotient *G*/*H* of a [reductive algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) *G* by the [subgroup *H* fixed](wikipedia://en/43166060) by some [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) of *G*.

:::
