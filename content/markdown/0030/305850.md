# Alexey Chervonenkis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexey Chervonenkis"}

**Alexey Yakovlevich Chervonenkis** (Алексей Яковлевич Червоненкис; 7 September 1938 – 22 September 2014) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician. Along with [Vladimir Vapnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Vapnik), he was one of the main developers of the [Vapnik–Chervonenkis theory](wikipedia://en/305843), also known as the "fundamental theory of learning", an important part of [computational learning theory](wikipedia://en/387537). Chervonenkis held joint appointments with the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) and [Royal Holloway, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway,_University_of_London).[^o2]

:::

Alexey Chervonenkis got lost in [Losiny Ostrov National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Losiny_Ostrov_National_Park) on 22 September 2014, and later during a search operation was found dead near [Mytishchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mytishchi), a suburb of Moscow.[^o3] He had died of [hypothermia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypothermia).[^o2]
