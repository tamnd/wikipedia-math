# 50 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="50"}

**50** (**fifty**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [49](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/49_%28number%29) and preceding [51](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/51_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

*Image: square-sum-50.png.* 50 as the sum of two non-zero squares

Fifty is the smallest number that is the sum of two non-zero [square numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_numbers) in two distinct ways.[^o4] 50 is an unsigned [Stirling number of the first kind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stirling_number_of_the_first_kind) and a [Narayana number](wikipedia://en/12267066).

## In science {#In_science}

Fifty is the fifth [magic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_%28physics%29) in nuclear physics, corresponding to the element [tin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin).[^o8]

## In religion {#In_religion}

In Hindu [tantric tradition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantra), the number 50 holds significance as the 50 [Rudras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudras) in the [Malinīvijayottara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_Shaivism) correlate with the 50 [phonemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoneme) of [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit), as well as with the 50 severed heads worn around goddess [Kali's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kali) head. The [mantra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantra) *Aham* ("I am"), as laid out in the [Vijñāna Bhairava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijñāna_Bhairava_Tantra) represents the first अ(a) and last ह(ha) phonemes of the Sanskrit alphabet and is believed to represent [ultimate reality](wikipedia://en/9249581), in accordance with its [non-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonduality_%28spirituality%29) philosophy.

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

The [percentage](wikipedia://en/64493) (50%) equivalent to one half, so that the phrase "fifty-fifty" commonly expresses something divided equally in two; in business this is often denoted as being the ultimate in equal partnership

Fifty years of marriage is marked by the gold or "golden" [wedding anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_anniversary).

50 is the number of states the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) contains.[^o11]
