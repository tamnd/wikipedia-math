# Drinfeld reciprocity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Drinfeld reciprocity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Drinfeld reciprocity**, introduced by (txt), is a correspondence between eigenforms of the moduli space of [Drinfeld modules](wikipedia://en/5578871) and factors of the corresponding [Jacobian variety](wikipedia://en/489734), such that all twisted L-functions are the same.

:::
