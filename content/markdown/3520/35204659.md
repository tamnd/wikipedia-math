# Genus character

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Genus character"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **genus character** of a [quadratic number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_number_field) *K* is a character of the [genus group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_group) of *K*. In other words, it is a real character of the [narrow class group](wikipedia://en/2567750) of *K*. Reinterpreting this using the [Artin map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artin_map), the collection of genus characters can also be thought of as the unramified real characters of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of *K* (i.e. the characters that factor through the Galois group of the [genus field](wikipedia://en/18189943) of *K*).

:::
