# Minimal K-type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minimal K-type"}

In mathematics, a **minimal K-type** is a representation of a maximal compact subgroup *K* of a [semisimple Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group) *G* that is in some sense the smallest representation of *K* occurring in a [Harish-Chandra module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harish-Chandra_module) of *G*. Minimal K-types were introduced by Vogan[^o2] as part of an algebraic description of the [Langlands classification](wikipedia://en/10985992).

:::
