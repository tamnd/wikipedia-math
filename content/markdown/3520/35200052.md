# Mestre bound

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mestre bound"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Mestre bound** is a bound on the [analytic rank](wikipedia://en/244541) of an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) in terms of [its conductor](wikipedia://en/43604164), introduced by (txt).

:::
