# Jean-Michel Salanskis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Michel Salanskis"}

**Jean-Michel Emmanuel Salanskis** (born 5 April 1951 in Paris) is a French [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor of science and philosophy at the [University of Paris X Nanterre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_West_University_Nanterre_La_Défense).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Originally gaining a [Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies) in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210), he went on to study philosophy with [Luis Puig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Puig) and [Jean-Francois Lyotard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Francois_Lyotard) from 1974 to 1983. In 1986 he completed a doctoral dissertation on *Le continu et le discret* (the continuous and the discrete).

## Work {#Work}

He is an important interpreter of [continental philosophers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_philosophy) such as [Jacques Derrida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Derrida), [Emmanuel Levinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Levinas), [Edmund Husserl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Husserl), [Martin Heidegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Heidegger),[^o4] and [Gilles Deleuze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilles_Deleuze), and he has published widely in English and French. He has also written about Judaism and the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).[^o5][^o6]

In his book *La gauche et l'égalité* he argues that [the left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing_politics) is structured by a “critique of power taking the form of a critique of man’s humiliation at the hands of transcendence" (p. 22), and that it is therefore necessary "to eliminate entirely the communist episode from the left,” for this episode partakes of the crushing of the people by one man who can “become the keystone of the world, restoring the attributes and the aura of royalty" (p. 37).[^o7]

In his 2010 book *Derrida*, he presents the philosophy of Jacques Derrida in an accessible manner for the lay reader, showing how Derrida's work related to the fields of psychoanalysis, radical politics, and literature.[^o8]

In *Les temps du sens* he embarks on a project to devise a mathematical hermeneutics that can be applied to fields such as [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science), [cognitive sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science) and [philosophy of religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_religion).[^o9]
