# Néron differential

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Néron differential"}

In mathematics, a **Néron differential**, named after [André Néron](wikipedia://en/9175241), is an almost canonical choice of [1-form](wikipedia://en/314692) on an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) or [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) defined over a [local field](wikipedia://en/145375) or [global field](wikipedia://en/288358). The Néron differential behaves well on the [Néron minimal models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néron_minimal_model).

:::

For an elliptic curve of the form

$$y^2+a_1xy+a_3y=x^3+a_2x^2+a_4x+a_6$$

the Néron differential is

$$\frac{dx}{2y+a_1x+a_3}$$
