# Lê Thị Thanh Nhàn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lê Thị Thanh Nhàn"}

**Lê Thị Thanh Nhàn** (born March 23, 1970)[^o2] is a [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is a professor of mathematics and vice rector for the College of Science at [Thái Nguyên University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thái_Nguyên_University).[^o3] Her research concerns [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o8]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nhàn's father was a soldier, who died when he was young, and her mother was a teacher. She was born in [Thừa Thiên–Huế](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thừa_Thiên–Huế_Province), and grew up in [Thái Nguyên](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thái_Nguyên_province) as the middle of five children in a poor family.[^o5]

Planning to become a teacher herself,[^o5] she studied mathematics at the Thái Nguyên College of Education from 1986 to 1990, earning a bachelor's degree, and on graduating became a lecturer in mathematics at the same institution. She continued her education at the Hanoi University of Education, earning a master's degree in 1995 and then at the Institute of Mathematics, Vietnam Academy of Sciences and Technology, earning her Ph.D. in 2001,[^o2] under the joint supervision of Prof. Nguyen Tu Cuong and Marcel Morales of [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University).

She moved from the College of Education to the College of Science in 2002,[^o2] and was promoted to associate professor in 2005, becoming the youngest mathematician in Vietnam with that rank.[^o7] She has also been associated with the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) in Italy as a junior associate member from 2002 to 2007 and as a regular associate member from 2009 to 2014.[^o2][^o8] In 2009 she became vice rector.[^o2]

Nhàn was promoted to mathematics professor in 2015, becoming the second female mathematics professor in Vietnam (The first Vietnamese female mathematics professor in Vietnam is professor Hoàng Xuân Sính)

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2011, she was one of two winners of the Kovalevskaya Prize, an annual award to promote women in the sciences in Vietnam.[^o3][^o8][^o5][^o7] The award is named after Russian mathematician [Sofia Kovalevskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kovalevskaya) and was established in 1985 by mathematician [Neal Koblitz](wikipedia://en/1587628) and his wife [Ann Hibner Koblitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Hibner_Koblitz), based on the profits from Ann Koblitz' biography of Kovalevskaya.[^o10]
