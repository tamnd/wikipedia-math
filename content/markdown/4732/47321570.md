# Valeria de Paiva

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Valeria de Paiva"}

**Valeria Correa Vaz de Paiva** is a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). Her work includes research on logical approaches to [computation](wikipedia://en/30402), especially using [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [knowledge representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_representation) and [natural language](wikipedia://en/21173) [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107), and [functional programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_programming) with a focus on foundations and [type theories](wikipedia://en/40282).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

De Paiva earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1982,[^o3] a master's degree in 1984 (on pure [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384)) and completed a doctorate at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1988, under the supervision of [Martin Hyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Hyland).[^o7] Her thesis introduced [Dialectica spaces](wikipedia://en/12288146), a [categorical](wikipedia://en/5869) way of constructing models of [linear logic](wikipedia://en/579675), based on [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736)'s [Dialectica interpretation](wikipedia://en/16974776).

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

She worked for nine years at [PARC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PARC_%28company%29) in [Palo Alto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California), and also worked at Rearden Commerce and [Cuil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuil) before joining Nuance.[^o4][^o11] She is an honorary [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) in computer science at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham).[^o11] She is currently on the Council of the [Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science and Technology of the International Union of History and Philosophy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DLMPST) (2020–2023).[^o12]

### Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Applied Category Theory in Chemistry, Computing, and Social Networks*. (with Baez, Cho, Ciccala and Otter). Notices of the American Mathematical Society, vol. 69, number 2, February 2022.
- *Term Assignment for Intuitionistic Linear Logic*. (with Benton, Bierman and Hyland). Technical Report 262, [University of Cambridge Computer Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Computer_Science_and_Technology,_University_of_Cambridge). August 1992.
- *Lineales*. (with J.M.E. Hyland) In "O que nos faz pensar" Special number in Logic of "Cadernos do Dept. de Filosofia da PUC", Pontificial Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro, April 1991.
- *A Dialectica-like Model of Linear Logic*. In Proceedings of Category Theory and Computer Science, Manchester, UK, September 1989. Springer-Verlag LNCS 389 (eds. D. Pitt, D. Rydeheard, P. Dybjer, A. Pitts and A. Poigne).
- *The Dialectica Categories*. In Proc of Categories in Computer Science and Logic, Boulder, CO, 1987. Contemporary Mathematics, vol 92, American Mathematical Society, 1989 (eds. J. Gray and A. Scedrov)
