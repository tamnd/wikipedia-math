# Jean-Yves Chemin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Yves Chemin"}

**Jean-Yves Chemin** (; born 23 April 1959 in [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen)) is a French mathematician, specializing in nonlinear partial differential equations.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chemin studied from 1979 at the [École normale supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Cachan) (now named the École normale supérieure Paris-Saclay) with [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in 1980 and *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* in 1982. At [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) (Paris XI) he graduated in 1983 with [Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) (DEA) and in 1986 with doctorate in mathematics.[^o3] His doctoral dissertation *Analyse microlocale précisée de solutions d’équations aux dérivées partielles non linéaires* was supervised by [Jean-Michel Bony](wikipedia://en/50769397).[^o4] Chemin became in 1986 *[Attaché de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France)* at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and in 1988 *[Chargé de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chargé_de_recherche)* at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). In 1989 he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) with a thesis on singularities of nonlinear hyperbolic partial differential equations. From 1991 to 1995 he was *[Maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* at the École Polytechnique. At [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris VI) in the Jacques-Louis Lions Laboratory, he was from 1993 to 2001 a professor and is since 2004 a professor. From 2001 to 2004 he was *Professeur à temp plein* (full-time non-tenured professor) at the École Polytechnique.[^o3]

His research deals with nonlinear partial differential equations describing physical systems that evolve over time. He focuses particularly on the [Navier-Stokes equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equations) and also works on the semi-linear Schrödinger equation and analysis on the [Heisenberg group](wikipedia://en/448518).[^o5]

In 2012 he was awarded the *Grand Prix Servant* of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for his work on the Navier-Stokes equations.[^o6]

From 1995 to 2001 he was a member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France). In 1995 he was awarded the *Prix Langevin* of the Académie des Sciences.[^o3]

Chemin was an invited speaker in 1994 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Zürich[^o7] and an invited speaker with [Hajer Bahouri](wikipedia://en/56443690) in 2002 at the ICM in Beijing.[^o8]

Chemin's doctoral students include [Isabelle Gallagher](wikipedia://en/48035795).
