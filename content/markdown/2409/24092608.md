::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of three tetrahedra"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **[compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound) of three [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra)** can be constructed by three [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra) rotated by 60 degree turns along an axis of the middle of an edge. It has [dihedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_symmetry), D<sub>3d</sub>, order 12. It is a uniform [prismatic compound of antiprisms](wikipedia://en/14855183), UC23.

:::

It is similar to the [compound of two tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_two_tetrahedra) with 90 degree turns. It has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the convex [hexagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860125).

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

A subset of edges of this compound polyhedron can generate a compound [regular skew polygon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_skew_polygon), with 3 skew squares. Each tetrahedron contains one skew square. This regular compound polygon containing the same symmetry as the uniform polyhedral compound.
