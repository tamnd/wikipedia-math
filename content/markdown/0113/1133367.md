# Trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

*Image: J75 trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{75}$). It contains 20 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 30 [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) and 12 [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014). It is also a [canonical polyhedron](wikipedia://en/4709281#Canonical_polyhedron).

:::

It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with three [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) rotated through 36 degrees. Related Johnson solids are:

- The [gyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131111) ($J_{72}$) where one cupola is rotated;
- The [parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177997) ($J_{73}$) where two opposing cupolae are rotated;
- And the [metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1178006) ($J_{74}$) where two non-opposing cupolae are rotated.
