# Pentagonal cupola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal cupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal cupola** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{5}$). It can be obtained as a slice of the [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486). The pentagonal [cupola](wikipedia://en/689743) consists of 5 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 5 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), 1 [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014), and 1 [decagon](wikipedia://en/327393).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The pentagonal [cupola](wikipedia://en/689743)'s faces are five [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), five [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), one [regular pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_pentagon), and one [regular decagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_decagon). It has the property of [convexity](wikipedia://en/6292) and [regular polygonal](wikipedia://en/333306) faces, from which it is classified as the fifth [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034). This cupola cannot be sliced by a plane without cutting within a face, so it is an [elementary polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_polyhedron).

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [circumradius](wikipedia://en/991784) $R$, and height $h$, [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) $A$, and [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) $V$ may be applied if all [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) with edge length $a$:

$$\begin{aligned} h &= \sqrt{\frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{10}}a &\approx 0.526a, \\ R &= \frac{\sqrt{11+4\sqrt{5}}}{2}a &\approx 2.233a, \\ A &= \frac{20+5\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5\left(145+62\sqrt{5}\right)}}{4}a^2 &\approx 16.580a^2, \\ V &= \frac{5+4\sqrt{5}}{6}a^3 &\approx 2.324a^3. \end{aligned}$$

*Image: Cupula pentagonal 3D.stl.* 3D model of a pentagonal cupola

It has an [axis of symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_of_symmetry) passing through the center of both top and base, which is symmetrical by rotating around it at one-, two-, three-, and four-fifth of a full-turn angle. It is also mirror-symmetric relative to any perpendicular plane passing through a bisector of the hexagonal base. Therefore, it has [pyramidal symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramidal_symmetry), the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) $C_{5\mathrm{v}}$ of order ten.

:::

## Related polyhedron {#Related_polyhedron}

The pentagonal cupola can be applied to construct a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). A construction that involves the attachment of its base to another polyhedron is known as [augmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29); attaching it to [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) or [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851) is known as [elongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29) or [gyroelongation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongation). Some of the Johnson solids with such constructions are:

- [elongated pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1177955) $J_{20}$
- [gyroelongated pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1177960) $J_{24}$
- [pentagonal orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177964) $J_{30}$
- [pentagonal gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177965) $J_{31}$
- [pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196275) $J_{32}$
- [pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196281) $J_{33}$
- [elongated pentagonal orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177967) $J_{38}$
- [elongated pentagonal gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177969) $J_{39}$
- [elongated pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200006) $J_{40}$
- [gyroelongated pentagonal bicupola](wikipedia://en/1178048) $J_{46}$
- [gyroelongated pentagonal cupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200024) $J_{47}$
- [augmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233344) $J_{68}$
- [parabiaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233345) $J_{69}$
- [metabiaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233346) $J_{70}$
- [triaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233347) $J_{71}$
- [gyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131111) $J_{72}$
- [parabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177997) $J_{73}$
- [metabigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1178006) $J_{74}$
- [trigyrate rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133367) $J_{75}$

Relatedly, a construction from polyhedra by removing one or more pentagonal cupolas is known as [diminishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishment_%28geometry%29):
