# Tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{83}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with three [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) removed.

*Image: J83 tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

Related Johnson solids are:

- $J_{76}$: [diminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131101) with one cupola removed,
- $J_{80}$: [parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133303) with two opposing cupolae removed,
- $J_{81}$: [metabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133317) with two non-opposing cupolae removed, and
- $J_{82}$: [gyrate bidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1178005) with two non-opposing cupolae removed and one cupola rotated 36 degrees.
