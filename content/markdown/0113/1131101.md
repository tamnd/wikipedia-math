# Diminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Diminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **diminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{76}$). It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with one [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) removed.

*Image: J76 diminished rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a diminished rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

Related Johnson solids are:

- $J_{80}$: [parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133303) with two opposing cupolae removed, and
- $J_{81}$: [metabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133317) with two non-opposing cupolae removed, and
- $J_{83}$: [tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133338) with three cupola removed.
