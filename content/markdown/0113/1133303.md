# Parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{80}$). It is also a [canonical polyhedron](wikipedia://en/4709281#Canonical_polyhedron).

*Image: J80 parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a parabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron

:::

It can be constructed as a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) with two opposing [pentagonal cupolae](wikipedia://en/1131073) removed. Related Johnson solids are the [diminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1131101) ($J_{76}$) where one cupola is removed, the [metabidiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133317) ($J_{81}$) where two non-opposing cupolae are removed, and the [tridiminished rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1133338) ($J_{83}$) where three cupolae are removed.
