# 1,000,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1,000,000,000"}

**1,000,000,000** ("one [billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billion)" on the [short scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_and_short_scales); "one milliard" on the [long scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_and_short_scales); one thousand million) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [999,999,999](wikipedia://en/2847945#900,000,000_to_999,999,999) and preceding 1,000,000,001. With a number, "billion" can be abbreviated as **b**, **bil**[^o3] or **bn**.[^o4][^o5]

:::

In standard form, it is written as **1 × 10<sup>9</sup>**. The [metric prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_prefix) [giga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giga) indicates 1,000,000,000 times the base unit. Its symbol is **[G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giga-)**.

One billion years may be called an *[eon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeon)* in astronomy or geology.

Previously in [British English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_English) (but not in [American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_American_and_British_English#Numbers)), the word "billion" referred exclusively to a [million millions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trillion) (1,000,000,000,000). However, this is not common anymore, and the word has been used to mean one thousand million (1,000,000,000) for several decades.[^o6]

The term ***milliard*** could also be used to refer to 1,000,000,000; whereas "milliard" is rarely used in English,[^o7] variations on this name often appear in other languages.

In the [Indian numbering system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system), it is known as 100 [crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore) or 1 [arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system).

1,000,000,000 is also the [cube](wikipedia://en/451445) of [1000](wikipedia://en/442472).

It is a common metric used in [macroeconomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macroeconomics) when describing national economies.

## Sense of scale {#Sense_of_scale}

*Image: Visualisation 1 billion.svg.* Visualization of powers of ten from one to 1 billion

The facts below give a sense of how large 1,000,000,000 (10<sup>9</sup>) is, according to current scientific evidence:

### Time {#Time}

- 10<sup>9</sup> seconds (1 gigasecond) equal 11,574 days, 1 hour, 46 minutes and 40 seconds (approximately 31.7 years, or 31 years, 8 months, 8 days).
- About 10<sup>9</sup> minutes ago, the [Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire) was flourishing and Christianity was emerging. (10<sup>9</sup> minutes is roughly 1,901 years.)
- About 10<sup>9</sup> hours ago, modern human beings and their ancestors were living in the [Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Age) (more precisely, the [Middle Paleolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Paleolithic)). (10<sup>9</sup> hours is roughly 114,080 years.)
- About 10<sup>9</sup> days ago, *[Australopithecus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australopithecus)*, an ape-like creature related to an ancestor of modern humans, roamed the African [savannas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna). (10<sup>9</sup> days is roughly 2.738 million years.)
- About 10<sup>9</sup> months ago, [dinosaurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur) walked the Earth during the late [Cretaceous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cretaceous). (10<sup>9</sup> months is roughly 83.3 million years.)
- About 10<sup>9</sup> years—a [gigaannus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gigaannus)—ago, the first [multicellular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multicellular_organism) [eukaryotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eukaryote) appeared on Earth.
- About 10<sup>9</sup> decades ago, the [thin disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin_disk) of the [Milky Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milky_Way) started to form. (10<sup>9</sup> decades is exactly 10 billion years.)
- The [universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe) is thought to be about [\13.8 × 10<sup>9</sup> years old.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_the_universe)[^o10]

### Distance {#Distance}

- 10<sup>9</sup> inches is 15783 mi, more than halfway around the world and thus sufficient to reach any point on the globe from any other point.
- 10<sup>9</sup> [metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metre) (called a [gigametre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gigametre)) is almost three times the distance from the [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228) to the [Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon).
- 10<sup>9</sup> [kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre) (called a [terametre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terametre)) is over six times the distance from the [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228) to the [Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun).

### Area {#Area}

- A billion square inches could make a square about one half mile on a side.
- A bolt of finely woven [1000-TC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thread_count) bed sheet linen with a billion thread crossings would have an area of 40 m2, comparable to the floor area of a motel unit.

### Volume {#Volume}

- There are one billion cubic [millimetres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millimetres) in a [cubic metre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_metre), and a billion cubic metres in a [cubic kilometre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_kilometre).
- A billion grains of table salt or granulated sugar would occupy a volume of about \2.5 cuft.
- A billion cubic inches would be a volume comparable to a large commercial building slightly larger than a typical supermarket.

### Weight {#Weight}

- Any object that weighs 1000000000 kg would weigh about as much as 5,525 empty [Boeing 747-400s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747-400).
- A cube of iron that weighs 1000000000 lbs would be \38.62 m on each side.

### Products {#Products}

- As of July 2016, [Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc.) has sold one billion [iPhones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone).[^o16] This makes the iPhone one of the most successful product lines in history, surpassing the [PlayStation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation) and the [Rubik's Cube](wikipedia://en/25971).
- As of January 2025, [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook) has 3.065 billion users.[^o17]

### Nature {#Nature}

- A small mountain, slightly larger than [Stone Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Mountain) in Georgia, United States, would weigh (have a mass of) a billion tons.
- There are billions of worker ants in the largest ant colony in the world,[^o19] which covers almost 4,000 mi of the Mediterranean coast.
- In 1804, the [world population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_population) was one billion.

### Count {#Count}

**A** is a cube; **B** consists of 1000 cubes the size of cube *A*, **C** consists of 1000 cubes the size of cube *B*; and **D** consists of 1000 cubes the size of cube *C*. Thus there are 1 million *A*-sized cubes in *C*; and 1,000,000,000 *A*-sized cubes in *D*.
