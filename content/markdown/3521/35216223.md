# Abel–Goncharov interpolation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abel–Goncharov interpolation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Abel–Goncharov interpolation** determines a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) such that various higher derivatives are the same as those of a given [function](wikipedia://en/185427) at given points. It was introduced by (txt) and rediscovered by (txt).

:::
