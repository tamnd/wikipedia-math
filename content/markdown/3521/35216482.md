# Michel Waldschmidt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Waldschmidt"}

*Image: Michel Waldschmidt.jpg.* Michel Waldschmidt in 2007 at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach)

**Michel Waldschmidt** (born June 17, 1946 at [Nancy, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France)) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), especially [transcendental numbers](wikipedia://en/30325).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Waldschmidt was educated at Lycée Henri Poincaré and the [University of Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nancy) until 1968. In 1972 he defended his thesis, titled *Indépendance algébrique de nombres transcendants* (Algebraic independence of transcendental numbers) and directed by Jean Fresnel, the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux).

He was a research associate of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the University of Bordeaux in 1971–1972. He was then a lecturer at [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) in 1972–1973. He became a lecturer at the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI) (Pierre et Marie Curie) and then became a professor there in 1973. He is a member of the Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu.

Waldschmidt was also a visiting professor at places including the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure).

From 2001 to 2004 he was president of the [Mathematical Society of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Society_of_France). He is a member of several mathematical societies, including the [EMS](wikipedia://en/969470), the [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) and [Ramanujan Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_Mathematical_Society).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Waldschmidt was awarded the [Albert Châtelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Châtelet) Prize in 1974, the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 1978, the Marquet Prize[^o4] of [Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_%28France%29) in 1980 and the Special Award of the [Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Harold_Hardy)–[Ramanujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan) Society in 1986. In 2021, he was awarded the Bertrand Russell Prize by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
