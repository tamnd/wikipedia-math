# Nullform

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nullform"}

In mathematics, a **nullform** of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) [acted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on [linearly](wikipedia://en/18102) by a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is a vector on which all [invariants](wikipedia://en/363762) of the group vanish. Nullforms were introduced by (txt).[^o3][^o4]

:::
