# Kazuhiko Aomoto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kazuhiko Aomoto"}

**Kazuhiko Aomoto** is a Japanese mathematician who introduced the [Aomoto-Gel'fand hypergeometric function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aomoto-Gel'fand_hypergeometric_function) and the [Aomoto integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aomoto_integral).

:::

He was a professor at [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University). In 1996 he received the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) autumn prize for his research on complex integration.
