# Wilhelm Frahm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Frahm"}

**Wilhelm Frahm** (14 May 1849 – 17 August 1875) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2] He was born in [Rendsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendsburg), [Schleswig-Holstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein), and studied mathematics in [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tübingen).[^o3] He died in Munich of [typhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhus).

:::
