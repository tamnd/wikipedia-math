# Young–Deruyts development

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Young–Deruyts development"}

In mathematics, the **Young–Deruyts development** is a method of writing [invariants](wikipedia://en/1126638) of an [action of a group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on an *n*-dimensional [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) *V* in terms of invariants depending on at most *n*–1 vectors ().

:::
