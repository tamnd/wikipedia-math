# Charles F. Dunkl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles F. Dunkl"}

**Charles F. Dunkl** (born 1941 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866)) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) who introduced [Dunkl operators](wikipedia://en/947234).

:::
