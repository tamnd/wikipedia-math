# Corrado de Concini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Corrado de Concini"}

*Image: Corrado De Concini.jpg.* De Concini in 2003

**Corrado de Concini** (born 28 July 1949, in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome)) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome). He studies [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678), [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

He was born in Rome in 1949, the son of [Ennio De Concini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennio_De_Concini), a noted screenwriter and film director.[^o3]

Corrado de Concini received in 1971 the mathematics degree from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and in 1975 a Ph.D. from the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) under the supervision of [George Lusztig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lusztig) (*The mod-2 cohomology of the orthogonal groups over a finite field*).

In 1975 he was a lecturer (Professore Incaricato) at the [University of Salerno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salerno), and in 1976 was associate professor at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). In 1981 he went to the University of Rome, where in 1983 he was a professor of higher algebra. From 1988 to 1996 he was professor at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), and from 1996 to 2019 professor at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome). Since 2020 he is emeritus professor at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome)[^o5]

De Concini was also a visiting scientist at the [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University), the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) (1981), the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (1982), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1987), the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (1989), the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (1992, 1996), the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) (2004, Lagrange Michelet Chair), and the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (2000, 2002).

From 2003 to 2007 he was president of [Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/34249626).

In 1986 he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) (*Equivariant embeddings of homogeneous spaces*). In 1992, he held a plenary lecture on the first [European Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Congress_of_Mathematicians)[^o6] in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) (*Representations of quantum groups at roots of 1*). In 1986 he was awarded the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352). In 1990 he was awarded the gold medal of [Accademia nazionale delle scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze).[^o7] Since 1993 he is a corresponding member and since 2009 a full member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei) and since 2005 a corresponding member of the [Istituto Lombardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo).

From 2021 to 2025 Corrado de Concini has been the president of the Accademia delle Scienze detta dei XL.
