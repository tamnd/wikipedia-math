# Otto Dersch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Dersch"}

prof

*Image: Das Fotoalbum für Weierstraß 015 (Otto Dersch).jpg.* Otto Georg Dersch

**Otto Georg Dersch** (born March 17, 1848, in [Ortenberg, Hesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortenberg,_Hesse))[^o2] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in algebraic geometry. Dersch got his Ph.D. 1873 in [Gießen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gießen). He was teacher in [Groß-Umstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groß-Umstadt) and [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt) and then director of a secondary school in [Offenbach am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offenbach_am_Main), and then became director of a secondary school (*Oberrealschule*) in [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt) until at least 1915.[^o3]

:::
