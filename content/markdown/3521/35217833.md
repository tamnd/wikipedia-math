# Isaak Bacharach

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Isaak Bacharach"}

**Isaak Bacharach** (2 December 1854 – 22 September 1942) was a German [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) professor in [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlangen) who proved the [Cayley–Bacharach theorem](wikipedia://en/3064553) on intersections of [cubic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_curve).[^o3][^o4]

:::

He was murdered at the [Theresienstadt concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theresienstadt_Ghetto) during [The Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust).[^o5]
