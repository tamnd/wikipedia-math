# Chevalley–Iwahori–Nagata theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chevalley–Iwahori–Nagata theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Chevalley–Iwahori–Nagata theorem** states that if a [linear algebraic group](wikipedia://en/395885) *G* is [acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) linearly on a finite-dimensional [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) *V*, then the map from *V*/*G* to the [spectrum](wikipedia://en/37291) of the ring of [invariant polynomials](wikipedia://en/363762) is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/12397) if this ring is finitely generated and all [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of *G* on *V* are [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) (). It is named after [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040), [Nagayoshi Iwahori](wikipedia://en/32092783), and [Masayoshi Nagata](wikipedia://en/3086117).
