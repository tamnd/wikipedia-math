# Claudio Procesi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claudio Procesi"}

**Claudio Procesi** (born 31 March 1941 in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome)) is an Italian mathematician, known for works in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Procesi studied at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), where he received his degree (Laurea) in 1963. In 1966 he graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) advised by [Israel Herstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Herstein), with a thesis titled "On rings with polynomial identities". From 1966 he was assistant professor at the University of Rome, 1970 associate professor at the [University of Lecce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lecce), and 1971 at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). From 1973 he was full professor in Pisa and in 1975 ordinary Professor at the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome). He was a visiting scientist at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) (1969–1970), the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (1973/74), at the [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708), at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (1991), at the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University), at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) (1981/2), at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) (1984), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1994), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (1992, etc.), at the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) in Trieste, and at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure).

Procesi studies noncommutative algebra, algebraic groups, invariant theory, enumerative geometry, infinite dimensional algebras and quantum groups, polytopes, braid groups, cyclic homology, geometry of orbits of compact groups, arrangements of subspaces and tori.

Procesi[^n1] proved that the polynomial invariants of $n \times n$ matrices over a field $K$ all come from the [Hamilton-Cayley theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton-Cayley_theorem), which says that a square matrix satisfies its own [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268).[^o4]

In 1981 he was awarded the Medal of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei), of which he is a member since 1987. In 1986 he received the [Feltrinelli Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feltrinelli_Prize) in mathematics. In 1978 he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki).[^o5] From 2007 to 2010 he is a vice-president of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868). He was an editor of the [Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal), the [Journal of Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Algebra), [Communications in Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications_in_Algebra), and [Advances in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Mathematics). Furthermore, he was on the committee of the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) and the algebra committee for the ICM 1986–1994.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Procesi is the father of [Michela Procesi](wikipedia://en/77615848), also a mathematician.[^o7]
