# Georgian Mathematical Union

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Georgian Mathematical Union"}

The **Georgian Mathematical Union** or **GMU** (Georgian: საქართველოს მათემატიკოსთა კავშირი, **სმკ**) is a [mathematical society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_society) founded in [Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_%28country%29) in 1923. The GMU has its headquarters at the Andrea Razmadze Mathematical Institute in [Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi). It is a founding member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) and is recognised by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868).

:::

The Georgian Mathematical Union describes its mission as "to promote mathematical sciences, especially among the young generation, and to establish and strengthen contacts with colleagues from abroad and with international professional organisations." Since 2010 the GMU has held an annual conference in [Batumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batumi); the 13th conference was held as a [hybrid event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_event) in September 2023.

## History {#History}

The predecessor of the Georgian Mathematical Union was the Georgian Mathematical Society, which was founded on 21 February 1923. Among the founders were Georgian mathematicians Nikolz Muskhelishvili, [Giorgi Nikoladze](wikipedia://en/80166286), Archil Kharadze, and [Andrei Razmadze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Razmadze), who served as the society's first president. At the 1924 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto) the society became a member of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868).

Razmadze died in 1929, and the society went dormant until being reformed in 1962 by [Viktor Kupradze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Kupradze), Boris Khvedelidze, Levan Gokieli and Archil Kharadze. The reformed society still dates its formation to 1923.

The Georgian Mathematical Society was a founding member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) in 1990, and once more joined the IMU in 1991. In 1994 its name was changed to the Georgian Mathematical Union.

## Logo {#Logo}

The logo of the Georgian Mathematical Union contains the outline of Georgia, with its [national flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Georgia_%28country%29) superimposed, and surrounded by four equations.

- $\Delta u + k^2u=0$ is the [Helmholtz equation](wikipedia://en/1156215).

## Presidents {#Presidents}

The past presidents of the Georgian Mathematical Union are:

- [Andrei Razmadze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Razmadze) (1923–1929)
- [Viktor Kupradze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Kupradze) (1962–1966)
- Levan Gokieli (1966–1970)
- Archil Kharadze (1970–1974)
- Levan Magnaradze (1974–1994)
- David Natroshvili (1994–1997)
- Roland Duduchava (1997–2001)
- Teimuraz Vepkhvadze (2001–2005)
- Jondo Sharikadze (2005–2009)
- Roland Duduchava (2009–2017)
- David Natroshvili (2017–2022)
- Alexander Meskhi (2022–present)
