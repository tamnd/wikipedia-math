# List of Chinese provincial-level divisions by GDP

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Chinese provincial-level divisions by GDP"}

The article lists [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)'s [first-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province-level_divisions_of_China) [administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_China) by [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP). Each province's GDP is listed in both the national currency [renminbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renminbi) (CNY), and at nominal [U.S. dollar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_dollar) values according to annual average exchange rates. [Purchasing power parity](wikipedia://en/64489) (PPP) indicators, measured in [international dollars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_dollar) fluctuate frequently, and its relevant data is not included in the main table, only included at the end of the entry. PPP reference comes from the [World Economic Outlook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Economic_Outlook) published by the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund) (IMF). The [average CNY exchange rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_renminbi_exchange_rates) used here is from the [China NBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Statistics_of_China), and [CNY PPP exchange rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_renminbi_exchange_rates#List_of_purchasing_power_parity_exchange_rates) are estimated according to the IMF.
