# Wlodek Rabinowicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wlodek Rabinowicz"}

**Wlodek Rabinowicz** (Włodzimierz Rabinowicz, born on 14 January 1947) is a Polish-Swedish philosopher and Professor Emeritus at [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University). Rabinowicz's areas of expertise include [ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics), [normativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_%28philosophy%29), [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216) and [utilitarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utilitarianism). From 1995 to 1998, he was [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of *[Theoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoria_%28philosophy_journal%29)*.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He began his undergraduate studies at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw),[^o5] but after the [1968 Polish political crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Polish_political_crisis) he moved to the [University of Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Uppsala), where he graduated in 1970. He received his doctorate in 1979.

Rabinowicz is a former director (1994–1995) of the [Swedish Collegium for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Collegium_for_Advanced_Study) in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), as well as a multiple Residential Fellow at the same institute (Spring 1994, Spring 2004, Fall 2008, September 2009, Fall 2011, Fall 2014).[^o6] He was appointed Professor of practical philosophy at [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University) in 1995. ).[^o7]
