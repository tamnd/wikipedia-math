# Peter H. Haynes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter H. Haynes"}

**Peter Howard Haynes** (born 23 July 1958) is a British [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) in the [Faculty of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3254271) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). He is a Fellow of [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge)[^o3] and served as head of the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (DAMTP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DAMTP) from 2005 to 2015.[^o4]

:::

He was educated at the [Royal Grammar School, Guildford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Grammar_School,_Guildford), and [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge) (B.A. 1979, M.A. 1983, Ph.D. 1984).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

His research includes [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [atmospheric dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_dynamics).[^o7]
