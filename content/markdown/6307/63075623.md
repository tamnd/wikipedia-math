# Harry Harman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Harry Harman"}

**Harry Horace Harman** (June 5, 1913 – June 8, 1976) was a Polish-born American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) known for his work on [factor analysis](wikipedia://en/253492).[^o3] A fellow of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association), he served as president of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500) from 1968 to 1969 and of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) from 1974 to 1975.[^o4] He died of a heart attack in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), on June 8, 1976.[^o5]

:::
