# Information integration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Information integration"}

**Information integration** (**II**) is the merging of information from heterogeneous sources with differing conceptual, contextual and typographical representations. It is used in [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253) and consolidation of data from unstructured or semi-structured resources. Typically, *information integration* refers to textual representations of knowledge but is sometimes applied to [rich media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_media) content. Information fusion, a related term, involves the combination of information into a new set of information in order to reduce redundancy and uncertainty.[^o2]

:::

Examples of [technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology) available to integrate information include [deduplication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_deduplication) and [string metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_metric), which allow the detection of similar text in different data sources by [fuzzy matching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzzy_string_searching). Other methods rely on causal estimates of the outcomes based on a model of the sources.[^o3]
