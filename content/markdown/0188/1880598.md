# August Meitzen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="August Meitzen"}

**August Meitzen** (; born 16 December 1822, in [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau); died 19 January 1910, in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) was a German [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in Breslau (now [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)) and educated at [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) and [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen). He was a prominent member of the Statistical Bureau, and in 1875 was made an associate professor of political sciences (*Staatswissenschaften*) at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) (honorary professor from 1892).[^o4] Between 1889 and 1891, he advised [Max Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Weber) in his completion of his post-doctoral [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation#Germany), a thesis that Weber titled *[Roman Agrarian History and Its Significance for Public and Private Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Agrarian_History_and_Its_Significance_for_Public_and_Private_Law)*.

## Works {#Works}

His contributions to the science of statistics include:

- *Die internationale land- und forstwirtschaftliche Statistik* ("International statistics on land and forest management"; 1873).
- *Geschichte, Theorie und Technik der Statistik* ("History, theory and technique of statistics"; 1886).

He also wrote:

- *Der Boden und die landwirthschaftlichen Verhältnisse des Preussischen Staates* ("The soil and the agricultural conditions of the Prussian state"; 8 volumes and 2 atlases, 1868–1908).
- *Die Mitverantwortlichkeit der Gebildeten für das Wohl der arbeitenden Klassen* (The obligation of the educated to the well-being of the working classes; 1876).
- *Siedelung und Agrarwesen der Westgermanen und Ostgermanen, der Kelten, Römer, Finnen und Slawen* ("Settlement and agriculture of the west and east Germans, the Celts, Romans, Finns and Slavs"; 1895).[^o6][^o7]

He was the author of several biographies in the *[Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allgemeine_Deutsche_Biographie)*.[^o8]
