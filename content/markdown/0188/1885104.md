# Semiregular space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semiregular space"}

A **semiregular space** is a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) whose [regular open sets](wikipedia://en/47306) (sets that equal the interiors of their closures) form a [base](wikipedia://en/59220) for the topology.[^o3]

:::

## Properties and examples {#Properties_and_examples}

Every [regular space](wikipedia://en/53058) is semiregular.[^o3] The converse is not true. For example, the space $X = \mathbb{R}^2 \cup \{0^*\}$ with the [double origin topology](wikipedia://en/29904885)[^o4] and the [Arens square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arens_square)[^o5] are [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) semiregular spaces that are not regular.

Open subspaces of a semiregular space are semiregular.[^n1] But arbitrary subspaces, even closed subspaces, need not be semiregular.[^n1]

The [product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_%28topology%29) of an arbitrary family of semiregular spaces is semiregular.[^n1]

Every topological space may be embedded into a semiregular space.[^o3]
