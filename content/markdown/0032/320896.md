# Sylvester

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvester"}

**Sylvester** or **Silvester** is a name derived from the [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) [adjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective) *silvestris* meaning "wooded" or "wild", which derives from the [noun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noun) *silva* meaning "woodland". [Classical Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_Latin) spells this with *i*. In Classical Latin, *y* represented a separate sound distinct from *i*, not a native Latin sound but one used in transcriptions of foreign words. After the Classical period *y* was pronounced as *i*. Spellings with *Sylv-* in place of *Silv-* date from after the Classical period.

:::
