# Center of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Center of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University"}

org **Center of Mathematical Sciences** (**CMS**, ), is a renowned mathematical research center based in China. It belongs to the [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang).

:::

## Introduction {#Introduction}

The center was mainly founded by the [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) in August 2002. The first directors and advisors were [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) [Su Buqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su_Buqing) and [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905), both of whom are [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_Province) natives.[^o3] The first academic director of the center is Shing-Tung Yau.[^o4]

In China, Zhejiang University has one of the best traditions of mathematical research, which is known as the *Zhe School* (*of Mathematics*) and the *Chen-Su School* (*of Differential Geometry*; in memorial of mathematicians [Su Buqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su_Buqing) and [Chen Jiangong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Jiangong)).[^o5] For continuing keeping and developing such excellent tradition, the center was founded, and coupled with the Department of Mathematics of Zhejiang University.

Every year, many mathematicians and scientists from all over the world visit or do research at the center.
