# Bombieri–Lang conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bombieri–Lang conjecture"}

In [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), the **Bombieri–Lang conjecture** is an unsolved problem [conjectured](wikipedia://en/6138) by [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475) and [Serge Lang](wikipedia://en/872991) about the [Zariski density](wikipedia://en/241744) of the set of [rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200) of an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) of [general type](wikipedia://en/1866873#General_type).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The weak Bombieri–Lang conjecture for surfaces states that if $X$ is a smooth [surface of general type](wikipedia://en/2368356) defined over a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) $k$, then the $k$-rational points of $X$ do not form a [dense set](wikipedia://en/23523594) in the [Zariski topology](wikipedia://en/241744) on $X$.[^o5]

The general form of the Bombieri–Lang conjecture states that if $X$ is a positive-dimensional algebraic variety of general type defined over a number field $k$, then the $k$-rational points of $X$ do not form a dense set in the Zariski topology.[^o13][^o14][^o9]

The refined form of the Bombieri–Lang conjecture states that if $X$ is an algebraic variety of general type defined over a number field $k$, then there is a dense [open subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_subset) $U$ of $X$ such that for all number field extensions $k'$ over $k$, the set of $k'$-rational points in $U$ is finite.[^o9]

:::

## History {#History}

The Bombieri–Lang conjecture was independently posed by Enrico Bombieri and Serge Lang. In a 1980 lecture at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), Enrico Bombieri posed a problem about the degeneracy of rational points for surfaces of general type.[^o5] Independently in a series of papers starting in 1971, Serge Lang conjectured a more general relation between the distribution of rational points and [algebraic hyperbolicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_hyperbolicity),[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8] formulated in the "refined form" of the Bombieri–Lang conjecture.[^o9]

## Generalizations and implications {#Generalizations_and_implications}

The Bombieri–Lang conjecture is an analogue for surfaces of [Faltings' theorem](wikipedia://en/172199), which states that algebraic curves of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) greater than one only have finitely many rational points.[^o11]

If true, the Bombieri–Lang conjecture would resolve the [Erdős–Ulam problem](wikipedia://en/52483865), as it would imply that there do not exist dense subsets of the Euclidean plane all of whose pairwise distances are rational.[^o11][^o12]

In 1997, [Lucia Caporaso](wikipedia://en/56679890), [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679), [Joe Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251), and Patricia Pacelli showed that the Bombieri–Lang conjecture implies a [uniform boundedness conjecture for rational points](wikipedia://en/66851658): there is a constant $B_{g,d}$ depending only on $g$ and $d$ such that the number of rational points of any genus $g$ curve $X$ over any [degree](wikipedia://en/4382697) $d$ number field is at most $B_{g,d}$.[^o13][^o14]
