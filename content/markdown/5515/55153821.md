# Lobachevsky integral formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lobachevsky integral formula"}

In mathematics, [Dirichlet integrals](wikipedia://en/1235977) play an important role in [distribution theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29). We can see the Dirichlet integral in terms of distributions.

:::

One of those is the improper integral of the [sinc function](wikipedia://en/610583) over the positive real line,

$$\int_0^\infty \frac {\sin x} x \, dx =\int_0^\infty \frac {\sin^2 x}{x^2} \, dx = \frac \pi 2.$$

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Lobachevsky's Dirichlet integral formula"}

## Lobachevsky's Dirichlet integral formula {#Lobachevsky's_Dirichlet_integral_formula}

Let $f(x)$ be a [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122) satisfying the $\pi$-periodic assumption $f(x+\pi)=f(x)$, and $f(\pi-x)=f(x)$, for $0\leq x<\infty$. If the [integral](wikipedia://en/15532) $\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin x} x f(x) \, dx$ is taken to be an [improper Riemann integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improper_Riemann_integral), we have [Lobachevsky](wikipedia://en/648527)'s [Dirichlet integral](wikipedia://en/1235977) formula

$$\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^2 x}{x^2} f(x) \, dx = \int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x} x f(x) \, dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} f(x) \, dx$$

Moreover, we have the following identity as an extension of the [Lobachevsky](wikipedia://en/648527) Dirichlet integral formula[^o4]

$$\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^4x}{x^4} f(x) \, dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} f(t) \, dt-\frac 2 3 \int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^2tf(t) \, dt.$$

As an application, take $f(x)=1$. Then

$$\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^4x}{x^4} \, dx = \frac \pi 3 .$$

:::
