# Goro Azumaya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goro Azumaya"}

**Gorō Azumaya** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced the notion of [Azumaya algebra](wikipedia://en/3125089) in 1951.[^o3][^o4] His advisor was [Shokichi Iyanaga](wikipedia://en/18357287). At the time of his death he was an emeritus professor at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_%28Bloomington%29).

:::
