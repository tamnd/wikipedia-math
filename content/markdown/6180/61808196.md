# Mai Gehrke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mai Gehrke"}

**Mai Gehrke** (born 10 May 1964) is a Danish mathematician who studies the theory of [lattices](wikipedia://en/469304) and their applications to [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392). She is a director of research for the French [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS), affiliated with the Laboratoire J. A. Dieudonné (LJAD) at the [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis).

:::

## Education {#Education}

As a child, Gehrke was educated at a French school in [Algiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algiers), which used a [Bourbakist](wikipedia://en/167394) and very abstract mathematics curriculum. As a high school student in Denmark, she spent a year as an exchange student in [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston) studying painting, but was brought back to mathematics by a Polish mathematics teacher who taught her [point-set topology](wikipedia://en/178649) according to the [Moore method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore_method).

She earned her Ph.D. from the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston) in 1987. Her dissertation, *Order Structure of Stone Spaces and the TD-axiom*, was supervised by Klaus Hermann Kaiser.

## Career {#Career}

After postdoctoral study at [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University), Gehrke joined the faculty of [New Mexico State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_University) in 1990. She moved to [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) in 2007, and to CNRS in 2011. From 2011 to 2017 her work for CNRS was associated with the Laboratoire d'Informatique Algorithmique: Fondements et Applications (LIAFA) at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University); in 2017 she moved to LJAD in Sophia Antipolis.
