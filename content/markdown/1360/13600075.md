# Eduard Heinrich Graeffe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eduard Heinrich Graeffe"}

**Eduard Heinrich Graeffe** or **Gräffe** (27 December 1833, [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich) – 23 April 1916 [Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana)) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_people) [zoologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoologist) and [naturalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naturalist). As an [entomologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entomologist), he specialised in [Hymenoptera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymenoptera), [Diptera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diptera) and [Hemiptera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemiptera).

:::

From around 1860, Graeffe was in the employ of [Johann Cesar VI. Godeffroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Cesar_VI._Godeffroy), a wealthy shipping magnate from [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg). Graeffe was hired in order to organize Godeffroy's natural history collection as a scientific museum, the "[Museum Godeffroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Godeffroy)".[^o2][^o3][^o4] From 1862 to 1873, he was based in [Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoa),[^o3] conducting scientific research and collecting specimens from throughout the South Pacific. Among his writings of the expedition was: *Reisen im Innern dei Insel Viti Levu* (in: Neujahrsblatt der Naturforschende Gesellschaft in Zürich auf das Jahr 1868, Nr. 70).[^o5]

From 1875 to 1898, he worked as an inspector at the Zoological Station in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste).[^o6] The [Fiji whistler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiji_whistler) (*Pachycephala graeffii*) is named in honor,[^o7][^o8] as is *Arthrocormus graeffii*, a [botanical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanical) species described by German [bryologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryologist) [Karl Müller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Müller_%28bryologist%29).[^o9]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Beobachtungen über Radiaten und Würmer in Nizza*, 1858 - Observations of [Radiata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiata) and worms in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).
- *Das Susswasser-Aquarium*. (1861) - The [freshwater aquarium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freshwater_aquarium).
- *Reisen nach verschiedenen Inseln der Südsee* 1867-68 - Journeys to different islands in the South Seas.
- *Reise im Innern dei Insel Viti-Levu*. Neuejahrblatt der Naturforschende Gesellschaft in Zurich 70: 1-48. (1868) - Journey in the interior of [Viti Levu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viti_Levu). Translated into English by Stefanie Vuikabe Waine and published as "Travels in the interior of Vitilevu" (1986).
- *Ueber eine Sendung Vögel aus Huahine*, 1873 - About an avian shipment from [Huahine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huahine).
- *Topographie von Samoa*, 1873 - The topography of Samoa.
- *Samoa oder die Schifferinseln*. 1873 - Samoa or the Schiffer Islands.
- *Algen der Fidschi-, Tonga- und Samoa-Inseln, gesammelt von Eduard Graeffe*, 1874 (with [Albert Grunow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Grunow)) - [Algae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algae) from [Fiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiji), [Tonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonga) and Samoa.
- *Coelenteraten*, 1882 - [Coelenterata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coelenterata).
- *Ueber die Sprache, Sitten und Gebräuche der Samoaner*, 1888 - On the language, customs and other particulars of Samoans.
- *Contributo alla Fauna dei Ditteri dei dintorni di Trieste*. S.1-25. Trieste (1895), (with A. Funk).[^o11]
- *Übersicht der Grabwespen (Fossores) des Küstenlandes*, (1912).[^o12]
- *Die Carolineninsel Yap oder Guap nach den Mittheilungen von Alf. Tetens und Johann Kubary*, (Microform Reprint New Haven, Conn.) - The [Carolines island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Islands) of [Yap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yap_Island) or Guap according to the reports of [Alfred Tetens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Tetens) and [Johann Kubary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stanislaw_Kubary).[^o13][^o14]

## Collections {#Collections}

Initially Graeffe's insect collection was in Das [Museum Godeffroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Godeffroy) in Hamburg. This collection was sold and Graeffe's specimens (and the material he had worked on) were divided between [Museo Civico di Storia Naturale di Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Civico_di_Storia_Naturale_di_Trieste) (Hymenoptera, Diptera, [Orthoptera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoptera) and Hemiptera) and the [Naturhistorisches Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naturhistorisches_Museum) (Hymenoptera, [Orthoptera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoptera) Diptera and Hemiptera). At least some material also ended up in the [Museum für Naturkunde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_für_Naturkunde) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).
