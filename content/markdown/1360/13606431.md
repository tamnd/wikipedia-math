# International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics"}

The **International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics (ICIAM)** is an international congress in the field of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) held every four years under the auspices of the [International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606227). The initial proposal for this conference series was made by [Gene Golub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Golub).[^o2]

:::
