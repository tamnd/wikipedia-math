# List of things named after Joseph-Louis Lagrange

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Joseph-Louis Lagrange"}

Several concepts from [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) are named after the mathematician and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) [Joseph-Louis Lagrange](wikipedia://en/87793), as are a [crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_crater) on the Moon and Rue Lagrange.

:::
