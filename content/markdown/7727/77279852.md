# Stacey Smith?

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stacey Smith?"}

**Stacey R. Smith?** (formerly **Robert Joseph Smith?**,[^o5][^o3] born 28 October 1972) is an Australian-Canadian mathematician known for scholarly research work on the modeling of infectious outbreaks, particularly including the analysis of outbreaks of [zombies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombies) and [Bieber Fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieber_Fever) (a phenomenon characterized by extreme fandom for the Canadian singer [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber)). She has also published scholarly books on [science fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Smith? received a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Macquarie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_University) in Sydney, Australia, in 1994. After graduation, she went to [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University), where she received a master's degree in 1996 and a Ph.D. in 2001. Her doctoral thesis investigated the technique of self-cycling fermentation; her Ph.D. advisor was [Gail Wolkowicz](wikipedia://en/54344146).

After finishing her doctorate, Smith? worked as a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario), where she became involved with the mathematical study of disease. Smith? did further postdoctoral research at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), and at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign).

In 2009, Smith? published the first academic article mathematically modeling a [zombie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie) outbreak, which brought Smith? to the attention of international media,[^o6][^o7] including a [Guinness World Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Record) for being the first mathematician to create such a model.[^o8] Smith? received further attention in media in 2012 for publishing a mathematical model of "[Bieber Fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieber_Fever)", together with her student, Valerie Tweedle.[^o9][^o10] In 2019, she was awarded the [Society for Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Biology)'s Distinguished Service Award, and then elected a fellow of the Society in 2025.[^o11][^o12]

As of December 2025, Smith? is a professor of mathematics and statistics at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa).[^o13]

She has also written a *[Doctor Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Who)* short story published in the "Short Trips: History of Christmas" collection, as well as several *Doctor Who* reference books.[^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Smith? is a [trans woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_woman) and is [polyamorous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyamory).[^o16]
