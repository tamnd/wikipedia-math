# Degeneration (algebraic geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Degeneration"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **degeneration** (or **specialization**) is the act of taking a limit of a family of [varieties](wikipedia://en/248808). Precisely, given a morphism

:::

$$\pi: \mathcal{X} \to C,$$

of a variety (or a [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754)) to a curve *C* with origin 0 (e.g., affine or projective line), the fibers

$$\pi^{-1}(t)$$

form a family of varieties over *C*. Then the fiber $\pi^{-1}(0)$ may be thought of as the limit of $\pi^{-1}(t)$ as $t \to 0$. One then says the family $\pi^{-1}(t), t \ne 0$ *degenerates* to the *special* fiber $\pi^{-1}(0)$. The limiting process behaves nicely when $\pi$ is a [flat morphism](wikipedia://en/542520) and, in that case, the degeneration is called a **flat degeneration**. Many authors assume degenerations to be flat.

When the family $\pi^{-1}(t)$ is trivial away from a special fiber; i.e., $\pi^{-1}(t)$ is independent of $t \ne 0$ up to (coherent) isomorphisms, $\pi^{-1}(t), t \ne 0$ is called a general fiber.

## Infinitesimal deformations {#Infinitesimal_deformations}

Let *D* = *k*\[*ε*\] be the [ring of dual numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_dual_numbers) over a field *k* and *Y* a scheme of finite type over *k*. Given a closed subscheme *X* of *Y*, by definition, an **embedded first-order infinitesimal deformation** of *X* is a closed subscheme *X'* of *Y* ×<sub>Spec(*k*)</sub> Spec(*D*) such that the projection $X \to \operatorname{Spec} D$ is flat and has *X* as the special fiber.

If *Y* = Spec *A* and $X = \operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ are affine, then an embedded infinitesimal deformation amounts to an ideal $I$ of *A*\[*ε*\] such that $A[\varepsilon]/ I$ is flat over *D* and the image of $I$ in *A* = *A*\[*ε*\]/*ε* is $I$.

In general, given a pointed scheme (*S*, 0) and a scheme *X*, a morphism of schemes $\pi$: *X'* → *S* is called the [deformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_%28algebraic_geometry%29) of a scheme *X* if it is flat and the fiber of it over the distinguished point 0 of *S* is *X*. Thus, the above notion is a special case when *S* = Spec *D* and there is some choice of embedding.
