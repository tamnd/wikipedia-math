# Fritz Scheuren

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz Scheuren"}

**Fritz J. Scheuren** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) known for his work on human rights, record linkage, and administrative records. He was president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2011.[^o3]

:::

Scheuren is a vice president of the [National Opinion Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Opinion_Research_Center).

## Career {#Career}

Scheuren was a coauthor, with Thomas Herzog and [William E. Winkler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_E._Winkler), of the 1997 book, *Data Quality and Record Linkage Techniques* (Springer, 1997).[^o5] He co-edited the book *Statistical Methods for Human Rights* with [David L. Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_L._Banks) and [Jana Asher](wikipedia://en/59427766).[^o6]

In 1998 Scheuren was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o7]
