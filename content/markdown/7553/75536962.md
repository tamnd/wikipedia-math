# Chung Soon-yeong

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chung Soon-yeong"}

Chung

**Chung Soon-yeong** (; born February 21, 1959) is a mathematics professor in the Department of Mathematics and Convergence Biomedical Engineering at [Sogang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sogang_University). He was the fifth president of the [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326)[^o4][^o5] and a vice president of the [Korean Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Mathematical_Society).[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Chung went to [Yushin High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yushin_High_School), a private high school in [Suwon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon), entering in 1974. He focused on [natural science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science). Entering [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) in 1978, he majored in mathematics with an emphasis on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) for his PhD.[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

After graduation, he was an assistant professor at [Duksung Women's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duksung_Women's_University) followed by an associate professor and then full professor at Sogang University.[^o10] From 2000, he held other positions at Sogang, including head of the Department of Mathematics, director of the Research Institute for Basic Science,[^o11] dean of academic affairs, director of the Teaching and Learning Center,[^o12][^o13] dean of the College of Natural Science.[^o14]

His service to the Korean Mathematical Society included director of the 42nd Math Olympiad,[^o15] organizer,[^o16] editing executive director,[^o17] and vice president.[^o18] For a two-year term starting in 2007, he headed the Mathematical Sciences Division at the [National Research Foundation of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Foundation_of_Korea).

He became the fifth president of the [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326) from January 30, 2018, to January 29, 2021.[^o19][^o20][^o21]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- 2019: Outstanding Researcher in Science and Engineering, [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_%28South_Korea%29)[^o23]
- 2013: Best Lecturer of the School of Natural Sciences, Sogang University
- 2010: Fellow, [Korean Academy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Academy_of_Science_and_Technology)[^o24]
- 2010: Achievement Award, Korean Mathematical Society[^o25]
- 2009: Alumni Award, Yushin High School
- 2008: Public Relations Achievement Award, [National Research Foundation of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Foundation_of_Korea)
- 2005: Outstanding Instructor Award, Graduate School of Education, Sogang University
- 2002: LG Yonam Overseas Professor
- 2001–2017: Professor with Outstanding Research Achievement, Sogang University
