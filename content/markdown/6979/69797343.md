# Dimitris Koukoulopoulos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dimitris Koukoulopoulos"}

**Dimitris Koukoulopoulos** (born 1984) is a Greek [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) as a professor at the [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal).[^o3][^o4]

:::

In 2019, in joint work with [James Maynard](wikipedia://en/42512102), he proved the [Duffin-Schaeffer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duffin-Schaeffer_conjecture).[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

He was an invited speaker at the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o9]

He was the 2022 recipient of the [Ribenboim Prize](wikipedia://en/57933020) of the Canadian Number Theory Association.[^o10]
