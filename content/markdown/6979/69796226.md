# Mohammad Sal Moslehian

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mohammad Sal Moslehian"}

**Mohammad Sal Moslehian** (محمد صال مصلحیان)[^o3] (born on 21 March 1966 in [Mashhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashhad), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran)) is an Iranian mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [Ferdowsi University of Mashhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdowsi_University_of_Mashhad), Iran. He is the President of the [Iranian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/46332302) for the period of 2021-2024 and an invited member of the [Iranian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Academy_of_Sciences). His [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) is 3.[^o4] He is known for his contribution to the operator and norm [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489). He has developed the orthogonality in Hilbert C\*-modules [^o5] and has significant contributions to operator means.[^o6][^o7] He established noncommutative versions of martingale and maximum inequalities that play an essential role in noncommutative probability spaces.[^o8] In addition, he has written several expository papers discussing research[^o10] and education,[^o11] as well as promoting mathematics.[^o12]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Moslehian obtained his Ph.D. from the [Ferdowsi University of Mashhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdowsi_University_of_Mashhad) under the supervision of Professor [Assadollah Niknam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assadollah_Niknam) in 1999. His research areas include [Functional Analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [Operator Theory](wikipedia://en/712675), specifically [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987)[^o14] and [Hilbert C<sup>\*</sup>-modules](wikipedia://en/16059132).[^o15] He spent his sabbatical leave at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) (UK) and [Karlstad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlstad_University) (Sweden) in 2006 and 2015 respectively and was a Senior Associate Researcher at the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP, Trieste)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) for the period of 2012-2017 and a non-resident researcher of the [Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Research_in_Fundamental_Sciences) during 2006–2007. He supervised more than 20 PhD students.[^o16]

His most significant scientific-executive responsibilities are as follows:

- The President of the Iranian Mathematical Society (2021-2024).
- A member of the executive committee of the Iranian Mathematical Society (2004-2012) & (2021-2024).[^o17]
- An invited member of the Academy of Sciences (2015-).
- Vice Dean of the Faculty of Mathematical Science at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (2003-2004).
- Director of Center of Excellence in Analysis on Algebraic Structures (2007-).[^o20]
- General Director of Graduate Studies at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (2008-2012).
- Vice-Dean (Research Affairs) of the Faculty of Mathematical Science at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (2016-2018).[^o19]
- General Director of Research at the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (2018-).[^o22]
- The founder and director of the "Tusi Mathematical Research Group", Iran.

## Editorial board {#Editorial_board}

He is the founder and the editor-in-chief of three mathematics journals: [Banach Journal of Mathematical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_Journal_of_Mathematical_Analysis),[^o25] [Annals of Functional Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annals_of_Functional_Analysis),[^o26] and [Advances in Operator Theory](wikipedia://en/69784352)[^o27] that are published by [Springer Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Nature)/[Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser) as well as being the editor-in-chief of the Bulletin of the Iranian Mathematical Society (2010-2011) and the Newsletter of the Iranian Mathematical Society (2004-2007).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Some of Moslehian's most important awards/honors for his contributions to mathematics include:

- A gold medal (third place) at the Annual Iranian Student Competition in Mathematics (**مسابفه ریاضی دانشجویی کشوری**) organized by the [Iranian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/46332302) (1989).
- A gold medal and award from Ferdowsi Academic Foundation (**بنیاد دانشگاهی فردوسی**) as the distinguished academic member in Basic Sciences (2011).[^o32]
- The Razavi Scientific award (**نشان علمی رضوی**) as an elected researcher in Basic Sciences by the governor-general of [Razavi Khorasan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razavi_Khorasan_Province) (2016).[^o33]
- An award as a distinguished researcher elected by [Ministry of Science, Research and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Science,_Research_and_Technology_%28Iran%29) and a certificate from the first vice president of Iran (2018).[^o34]
- Recipient of the national [Allame Tabatabaei Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allame_Tabatabaei_Award) (**جايزه علامه طباطبايى**) and certificate from a vice president of Iran (2019).[^o35]
