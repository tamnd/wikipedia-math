# Małgorzata Klimek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Małgorzata Klimek"}

**Małgorzata Klimek** (born 1957) is a Polish [mathematical analyst](wikipedia://en/48396) and [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist) known for her research on the [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939) and fractional [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). She is a professor in the Institute of Mathematics and Dean of the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science at [Częstochowa University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Częstochowa_University_of_Technology) in Poland.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Klimek was born on 16 July 1957, in [Bielsko-Biała](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielsko-Biała). She earned a master's degree in mathematics at the [Wrocław University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in 1981, and earned a Ph.D. in 1993 through the Institute of Theoretical Physics of the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław), supervised by Jerzy Lukierski. She completed her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) through the Institute of Theoretical Physics in 2003.

Klimek joined the staff of the Częstochowa University of Technology in 1985. She was named a full professor in 2015, and is Dean of the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science. She was president of the Częstochowa Branch of the [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791) for 2011–2013.

As well as many journal publications, she is the author of a research monograph on fractional differential equations, and two textbooks.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Klimek has been given multiple awards by the rector of the Częstochowa University of Technology for her research and organizational accomplishments. In 2010 she was given the medal of the [Commission of National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_of_National_Education).
