# Eberhard Zeidler (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eberhard Zeidler"}

*Image: Eberhard Zeidler.jpg.* Eberhard Zeidler, Bonn, 2006

**Eberhard Hermann Erich Zeidler**[^o13] (6 October 1940 in Leipzig, Germany – 18 November 2016 ibid) was a German mathematician, who worked primarily in the field of non-linear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

After attending the Leipzig Humboldt-Schule (Leipzig)|Humboldtschule[^o7] Eberhard Zeidler began studying mathematics at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) in 1959. In 1961, he was [exmatriculated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exmatriculation) because of critical statements, and was forced to work as a transport worker and absolve his military service in the East-German's [NVA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_People's_Army).[^o9] In 1964, he was allowed to continue his studies.[^o9] In 1967, he received his Dr. rer. nat. (PhD) with his work "Über eine Klasse nichtlinearer singulärer Randwertaufgaben der Funktionentheorie mit Symmetrieverhalten" under Herbert Beckert.

In 1970, he was appointed to [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) and became a lecturer for [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134) at the University of Leipzig.[^o9] From 1974 to 1996 he was [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professorship) for analysis.

In the winter semester of 1979–1980, Eberhard Zeidler was a visiting professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) (USA).

From 1992 to 1996, he was head of the [DFG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) research group on "Nichtlineare Funktionalanalysis und ihre Anwendungen" (Nonlinear functional analysis and its applications), and from 1992 to 2000 he was a member of the scientific advisory board of the [Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach).[^o7]

In 1996, Eberhard Zeidler became the first managing director of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) when it was founded in Leipzig. In 2003, he gave up the management, but remained director. He retired in October 2007.

Eberhard Zeidler has been a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) since 1994. Also, he was one of the founding members of the Foundation Board and the Foundation Advisory Board of the [Stiftung Benedictus Gotthelf Teubner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stiftung_Benedictus_Gotthelf_Teubner).[^o8]
