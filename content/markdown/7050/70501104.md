# Cami Sawyer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cami Sawyer"}

**Cameron Cunningham** (**Cami**) **Sawyer** is an American mathematician who has worked in New Zealand at [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University) and the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_%28New_Zealand%29). Trained in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), her work in New Zealand has focused on [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), [educational technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_technology), [distance learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_learning), and the needs of [Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Māori_people) students in mathematics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sawyer has a postgraduate diploma in teaching from [Texas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_State_University), and completed a PhD in 1999 at the [University of North Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas). Her dissertation, *On the Cohomology of the Complement of a Toral Arrangement*, was supervised by J. Matthew Douglass.

She had already visited New Zealand under a [Rotary Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_Scholarships) in the 1980s, and in the early 2000s emigrated there with her husband and children. She became a secondary school teacher before joining the Massey University staff as a senior tutor in mathematics in the Institute of Fundamental Sciences. Since 2015, she has also been associated with the Pūhoro STEM Academy, a program hosted by Massey for encouraging secondary-school Māori to continue their science and technology education.

In 2021 she moved from Massey to the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_%28New_Zealand%29), as Learning Area Lead of Mathematics and Statistics.

## Recognition and service {#Recognition_and_service}

Sawyer is a Fellow of the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society) (NZMS), and has chaired the NZMS Education Group. In 2019 she won a Sustained Excellence in Tertiary Teaching Award in the Te Whatu Kairangi Awards of the Ako Aotearoa National Centre for Tertiary Teaching Excellence, a government-funded organisation for the support and promotion of tertiary-level education.
