# Cartan–Kuranishi prolongation theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartan–Kuranishi prolongation theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), given an [exterior differential system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_differential_system) defined on a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) *M*, the **Cartan–Kuranishi prolongation theorem** says that after a finite number of *prolongations* the system is either *in involution* (admits at least one 'large' integral manifold), or is impossible.

:::

## History {#History}

The theorem is named after [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274) and [Masatake Kuranishi](wikipedia://en/48550243). Cartan made several attempts in 1946 to prove the result, but it was in 1957 that Kuranishi provided a proof of Cartan's conjecture.[^o3]

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

This theorem is used in infinite-dimensional [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681).

:::
