# Daniel B. Szyld

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel B. Szyld"}

**Daniel B. Szyld** (born 1955 in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires)) is an [Argentinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentines) and [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is a professor at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) in Philadelphia. He has made contributions to numerical and applied linear algebra as well as matrix theory.[^o8][^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He was admitted without an undergraduate degree to the graduate school at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University),[^o5] where he defended his PhD in 1983.[^o2] While there, he worked as a research assistant for [Wassily Leontief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wassily_Leontief).[^o5]

## International awards and appointments {#International_awards_and_appointments}

He was named as a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow)[^o7] and as a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o8] in 2017. In 2020, he was elected president of the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149).[^o9] He was [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) for the [Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/53219445) from 2005 to 2013[^o10] and [SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Matrix_Analysis_and_Applications) from 2015 to 2020[^o11] and is on the editorial boards of several journals, including the [Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra](wikipedia://en/67679696) (ELA),[^o12] the [Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/53219445) (ETNA),[^o10] [Linear Algebra and its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Algebra_and_its_Applications),[^o13] [Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation),[^o14] Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications,[^o15] and Journal of Numerical Analysis and Approximation Theory.[^o16] A conference in honor of his 65th birthday was held in 2022[^o17]
