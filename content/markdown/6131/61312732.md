# Paul Muhly

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Muhly"}

**Paul Muhly** (born September 7, 1944)[^o3] is an American mathematician. In 2015 he was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his contributions to [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) as well as mentoring and service to the community.[^o4] He has supervised over 20 Ph.D. students.
