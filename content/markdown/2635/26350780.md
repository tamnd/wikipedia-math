# Clyde E. Love

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clyde E. Love"}

**Clyde Elton Love** (December 12, 1882 – January 31, 1960) was an American [contract bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_bridge) author and mathematics professor at the [University of Michigan, Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o2] He was a native of [Bancroft, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bancroft,_Michigan) and graduated from the University of Michigan in 1905.[^o3]

:::

Love is well known in bridge circles for his 1959 book *[Bridge Squeezes Complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_Squeezes_Complete)*, one of the earliest efforts to codify then-existing squeeze play. Love established rules for recognizing bridge squeezes, and for executing them when they occurred. His system of classifying squeezes has been used by most bridge writers since.[^o4] He was also the author of many magazine articles.[^o2]

He died at his home in [Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor,_Michigan), aged 77. He was survived by his wife and daughter.[^o3]
