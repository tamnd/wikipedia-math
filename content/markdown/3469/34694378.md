# Virasoro conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Virasoro conjecture"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Virasoro conjecture** states that a certain [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993) encoding [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291) of a [smooth](wikipedia://en/981915) [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469) is fixed by an action of half of the [Virasoro algebra](wikipedia://en/348029). The Virasoro conjecture is named after [theoretical physicist](wikipedia://en/19594028) [Miguel Ángel Virasoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Ángel_Virasoro_%28physicist%29). (txt, 71–80) proposed the Virasoro conjecture as a generalization of [Witten's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witten's_conjecture). (txt) gave a survey of the Virasoro conjecture.

:::

The proof of the genus 0 Virasoro conjecture for all smooth projective varieties (or more generally, compact symplectic manifolds) was first given by [Xiaobo Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaobo_Liu_%28mathematician%29) and [Gang Tian](wikipedia://en/6716707) (1998).[^o4]
