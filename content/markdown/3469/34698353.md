# Everett C. Dade

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Everett C. Dade"}

**Everett Clarence Dade** was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) working on [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), who introduced the [Dade isometry](wikipedia://en/29775880) and [Dade's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34698237). While an undergraduate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), he became a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) twice, in 1955 and 1957.[^o3]

:::

## Work {#Work}

The [Dade isometry](wikipedia://en/29775880) is an [isometry](wikipedia://en/254777) from [class functions](wikipedia://en/1001329) on a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) *H* with support on a subset *K* of *H* to class functions on a group *G* (Collins 1990, 6.1). It was introduced by (txt) as a generalization and simplification of an isometry used by (Feit Thompson 1963) in their [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of the [odd order theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odd_order_theorem), and was used by (Peterfalvi 2000) in his revision of the [character theory](wikipedia://en/691741) of the odd order theorem.

[Dade's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34698237) is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) relating the numbers of characters of blocks of a finite group to the numbers of characters of blocks of local subgroups.
