# Dade's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dade's conjecture"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), **Dade's conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) relating the numbers of [characters](wikipedia://en/691741) of [blocks](wikipedia://en/1110685#Blocks_and_the_structure_of_the_group_algebra) of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) to the numbers of characters of blocks of local [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011), introduced by [Everett C. Dade](wikipedia://en/34698353).

:::
