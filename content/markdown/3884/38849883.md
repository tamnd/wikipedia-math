# Montserrat Teixidor i Bigas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Montserrat Teixidor i Bigas"}

**Montserrat Teixidor i Bigas** (born February 25, 1958) is a Spanish-American academic who is a professor of mathematics at [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University) in [Medford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medford,_Massachusetts), [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts).[^o3] She specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), especially [Moduli of Vector Bundles](wikipedia://en/361609#Moduli_of_vector_bundles) on curves.[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Teixidor i Bigas was born in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona) in 1958. She earned a bachelor's degree and PhD from the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona), where she wrote her dissertation, "[Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [linear systems](wikipedia://en/722503) on [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260)", under the supervision of Gerard Eryk Welters.[^o6][^o7]

## Career {#Career}

She worked in the department of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool), where she wrote "The divisor of curves with a vanishing theta-null",[^o9] for *[Compositio Mathematica](wikipedia://en/31882322)* in 1988.

In 1997, she proved [Lange's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34738529) for the generic curve, with [Barbara Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Russo_%28mathematician%29), which states that "If $0<s\le n'(n-n')(g-1)$, then there exist stable vector bundles with $s_{n'}(E)=s$." They also clarified what happens in the interval $n'(n-n')(g-1)<s\le n'(n-n')g$ using a degeneration argument to a reducible curve.[^o10]

She took up an appointment as an Associate Professor of Mathematics at Tufts University, and has been on the faculty of Tufts since 1989. She has been a reviewer for several journals, including the [Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2668304), the [Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal), and the [journal of algebraic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_algebraic_geometry). She has held visiting positions at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) and the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o11] She was also a co-organizer of the [Clay Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Institute)'s workgroup on Vector Bundles on Curves.[^o12]

In 2004, she spent a year at [Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College) as a [Vera M. Schuyler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_Mikol) Fellow, devoting her time to study of "the interplay between the geometry of curves and the equations defining them."[^o13]
