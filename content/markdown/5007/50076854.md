# Barry Arthur Cipra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barry Arthur Cipra"}

**Barry Arthur Cipra**, an American mathematician and freelance writer, regularly contributes to [*Science* magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_%28journal%29) and *SIAM New*s, a monthly publication of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376). Along with Dana Mackenzie and Paul Zorn he is the author of several of the volumes in the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) series *What's Happening in the Mathematical Sciences*, a collection of articles about recent results in pure and applied mathematics oriented towards the undergraduate mathematics major.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Cipra got his Ph.D. from [University of Maryland College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland_College_Park) in 1980. He was an instructor at [The Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). He was an assistant professor of mathematics at [St. Olaf College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Olaf_College) in [Northfield, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northfield,_Minnesota). Cipra received the 1991 Merten M. Hasse Prize from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for his work on the [Ising model](wikipedia://en/292744).[^o4] In 2005 he received the [JPBM Communications Award](wikipedia://en/4675178#JPBM_Communications_Award).[^o5]
