# Robert Arnott Wilson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Arnott Wilson"}

**Robert Arnott Wilson** (born 1958) is a retired mathematician in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), England, who is best known for his work on classifying the maximal subgroups of finite [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600) and for the work in the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422). He is also an accomplished violin, viola and piano player, having played as the principal viola in the Sinfonia of Birmingham. Due to a damaged finger, he now principally plays the [kora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kora_%28instrument%29).[^o3]

:::
