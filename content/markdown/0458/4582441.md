# Richard A. Parker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard A. Parker"}

*Image: Richard Parker, Mathematician, 2021.jpg.* Richard A. Parker

**Richard A. Parker** (29 January 1953 – 26 January 2024[^o2]) was a mathematician and freelance computer programmer born in [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey) who lived for much of his life in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England). He invented many of the algorithms for computing the modular character tables of finite [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600). He discovered the relation between [Niemeier lattices](wikipedia://en/1652072) and deep holes of the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919), and constructed Parker's [Moufang loop](wikipedia://en/755329) of order 2<sup>13</sup>[^o3] (which was used by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) in his construction of the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422)).

:::
