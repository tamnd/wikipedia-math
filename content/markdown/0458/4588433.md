# Monodromy matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monodromy matrix"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and particularly [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations) (ODEs), a **monodromy matrix** is the [fundamental matrix](wikipedia://en/10125619) of a system of ODEs evaluated at the period of the coefficients of the system. It is used for the analysis of periodic solutions of ODEs in [Floquet theory](wikipedia://en/2987843).

:::
