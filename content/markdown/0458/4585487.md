# Monoidal natural transformation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monoidal natural transformation"}

Suppose that $(\mathcal C,\otimes,I)$ and $(\mathcal D,\bullet, J)$ are two [monoidal categories](wikipedia://en/330604) and

:::

$(F,m):(\mathcal C,\otimes,I)\to(\mathcal D,\bullet, J)$ and $(G,n):(\mathcal C,\otimes,I)\to(\mathcal D,\bullet, J)$

are two [lax monoidal functors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lax_monoidal_functor) between those categories.

A **monoidal natural transformation**

$$\theta:(F,m) \to (G,n)$$

between those functors is a [natural transformation](wikipedia://en/45022) $\theta:F \to G$ between the underlying functors such that the diagrams

A **symmetric monoidal natural transformation** is a monoidal natural transformation between [symmetric monoidal functors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_monoidal_functor).
