# Johann Gottlieb Friedrich von Bohnenberger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Gottlieb Friedrich von Bohnenberger"}

*Image: Johann Gottlob Friedrich Bohnenberger-3.jpg.* Johann Gottlieb Friedrich von Bohnenberger.

**Johann Gottlieb Friedrich von Bohnenberger** (5 June 1765 – 19 April 1831) was a German astronomer born at [Simmozheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simmozheim), [Württemberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Württemberg). He studied at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen). In 1798, he was appointed professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) at the university.

:::

He published:

- *Anleitung zur geographischen Ortsbestimmung* (Guide to geographic locations), 1795
- *Astronomie* (Astronomy), 1811
- *Anfangsgründe der höhern Analysis* (Initial reasons of higher analysis), 1812.

In 1817, he systematically explained the design and use of a [gyroscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroscope) apparatus which he called simply a “Machine.” Several examples of the 'Machine' were constructed by [Johann Wilhelm Gottlob Buzengeiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Wilhelm_Gottlob_Buzengeiger) of Tübingen. Johann Friedrich Benzenberg had already mentioned Bohnenberger's invention (describing it at length) in several letters beginning in 1810.[^o2][^o3]

Bohnenberger died at [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tübingen).

The [lunar crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_crater) [Bohnenberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohnenberger_%28crater%29) is named after him.
