# Johannes Runnenburg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Runnenburg"}

**Johannes Theodorus Runnenburg** (19 February 1932 – 16 April 2008) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of probability theory and analysis at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) from 1962 to 1997.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in Amsterdam he received his MA in Mathematics in 1956 at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), and his PhD cum laude in Mathematics and Physics in 1960 with a thesis entitled "On the Use of Markov Processes in One-server Waiting-time Problems and Renewal Theory" advised by [Nicolaas Govert de Bruijn](wikipedia://en/3166399).[^o3]

Runnenburg was appointed Lector in [Probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and analysis at the University of Amsterdam in 1961. In 1962, he was promoted to Professor of Probability theory and analysis, and from 1966 to his retirement in 1997 was Professor of Pure and Applied Mathematics.[^o4] Among his doctorate students were [Gijsbert de Leve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gijsbert_de_Leve) (1964), [Laurens de Haan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurens_de_Haan) (1970), Fred Steutel (1971), Wim Vervaat (1972), [August Balkema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Balkema) (1973), [Frits Göbel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frits_Göbel) (1974), [Arie Hordijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arie_Hordijk) (1974), Aegle Hoekstra (1983), Peter de Jong (1988) and Leo Klein Haneveld (1996).[^o3]
