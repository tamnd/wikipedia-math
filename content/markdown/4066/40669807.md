# Jacqueline Meulman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacqueline Meulman"}

*Image: Jacqueline Meulman.jpg.* Jacqueline Meulman, 2009

**Jacqueline Meulman** (born 7 July 1954) is a Dutch [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and professor emerita of Applied Statistics at the Mathematical Institute of [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague), Meulman received her master's degree in mathematical psychology and data theory at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) in 1981, and obtained her PhD in data theory in 1986 with the thesis entitled "A distance approach to nonlinear multivariate analysis" advised by [Jan de Leeuw](wikipedia://en/30963930) and [John P. van de Geer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_P._van_de_Geer).[^o3] She was a consultant for [Bell Telephone Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Telephone_Laboratories) in Murray Hill, NJ, from 1982 to 1983.

In addition to being an associate professor in the Department of Data Theory in Leiden, she was an adjunct professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) from 1993 to 1999. In 1998, she was appointed Professor of Applied Data Theory at Leiden University. Since 2009 she is Professor of Applied Statistics at the Mathematical Institute in Leiden. She is currently also an adjunct professor at the Department of Statistics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).

Meulman has received several awards, including a five-year "fellowship" of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1987, de J.C. Ruigrok prijs from the [Royal Holland Society of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holland_Society_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) (KHMW) in 1991, a [Fulbright Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Award) in 1992, and a PIONEER grant from the Netherlands Organization of Scientific Research (NWO) in 1994. In 2001 she was elected President of the International [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500), and since 2002 she is a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (KNAW).[^o4] From 2011 to 2017 she was president of the [Netherlands Society for Statistics and Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Society_for_Statistics_and_Operations_Research). She is an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) since 1996, and she was elected to the [Royal Holland Society of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holland_Society_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) in 2015.

## Work {#Work}

Meulman's research interest are in the field of statistics and data science, and her work includes the development of new statistical methods with applications in the life and behavioral sciences.

Since the 1990s, she manages the development of software in the package CATEGORIES of IBM SPSS Statistics that includes programs for optimal scaling in regularized multiple regression analysis, principal components analysis, correspondence analysis, [multidimensional scaling](wikipedia://en/398786) and unfolding.[^o6][^o7] At the Mathematical Institute in Leiden, she was Program Director of the first Dutch Master in Applied Statistics and Data Science in a Faculty of Science.[^o8] She is also one of the Founding Fathers and co-director of the Leiden Centre of Data Science (LCDS). [^o9]
