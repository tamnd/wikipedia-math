# Ordinary singularity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ordinary singularity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **ordinary singularity** of an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) is a [singular point](wikipedia://en/4230456) of [multiplicity](wikipedia://en/19211790) *r* where the *r* tangents at the point are distinct (Walker 1950, p. 54). In higher dimensions the literature on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) contains many inequivalent definitions of ordinary singular points.

:::
