# Fricke involution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fricke involution"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Fricke involution** is the [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) of the [modular curve](wikipedia://en/669552) *X*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) given by τ → –1/*N*τ. It is named after [Robert Fricke](wikipedia://en/10291191). The Fricke involution also acts on other objects associated with the modular curve, such as spaces of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) and the [Jacobian](wikipedia://en/489734) *J*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) of the modular curve. The quotient of *X*<sub>0</sub>(*N*) by the Fricke involution is a curve called *X*<sub>0</sub><sup>\+</sup>(*N*), and for *N* prime this has genus zero only for a finite list of primes, called [supersingular primes](wikipedia://en/22965333), which are the primes that divide the order of the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422).

:::
