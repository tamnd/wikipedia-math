# Crista Arangala

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crista Arangala"}

**Crista Arangala** (née **Crista Lynn Coles**) is an American mathematician and textbook author, specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). She is a professor of mathematics and chair of the department of mathematics and statistics at [Elon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_University), and a [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Arangala graduated from [Allegheny College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegheny_College) in 1993. She earned her Ph.D. in 2000 from the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati), with a dissertation on *Numerical Identification of Parameters in Inverse Heat Conduction and Inverse Euler Bernoulli Beam Theory* supervised by Diego Antonio Murio.

## International work {#International_work}

Arangala has regularly taken her Elon mathematics classes to India and Sri Lanka, where they have participated in elementary-school mathematics education. In 2013–2014 she was a [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) in Sri Lanka.
