# Jean Favard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Favard"}

**Jean Favard** (28 August 1902 – 21 January 1965) was a French mathematician who worked on analysis.

:::

Favard was born in [Peyrat-la-Nonière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peyrat-la-Nonière). During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he was a prisoner of war in Germany.

He also was a President of the [French Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Mathematical_Society) in 1946. He died in [La Tronche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Tronche), aged 62.
