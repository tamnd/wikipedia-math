# Bernard de Neumann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard de Neumann"}

**Frederick Bernard de Neumann** (known in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) and [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) as *Bernhard von Neumann*; (15 December 1943 – 18 April 2018[^o2]) was a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), [inventor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventor), and [naval historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_history).

:::

He was educated at the [Royal Hospital School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Hospital_School) and [Birmingham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_University), and was Professor of Mathematics at [The City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_St_George's,_University_of_London).

He was a descendant of Johann Andreas von Neumann, nobleman of the [Holy Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire), [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), 29 March 1797, and of Johann Heinrich von Neumann, nobleman of the [Kingdom of Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bavaria), [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), 20 January 1824.
