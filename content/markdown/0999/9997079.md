# Pseudo-scholarship

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo-scholarship"}

**Pseudo-scholarship** (from *[pseudo-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudo-)* and *[scholarship](wikipedia://en/772062)*) is a term used to describe work (e.g., a publication or lecture) or a body of work that is presented as, but is not, the product of rigorous and objective study or research; the act of producing such work; or the pretended learning upon which it is based.[^o2]

:::

Examples of pseudo-scholarship include:

- [Pseudoarchaeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoarchaeology)
- [Pseudohistory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudohistory)[^o3]
- [Pseudolaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudolaw)
- [Pseudomathematics](wikipedia://en/929709)
- [Pseudophilosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudophilosophy)
- [Pseudoscience](wikipedia://en/23047)[^o4]
