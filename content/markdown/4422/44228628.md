# Emilio Baiada

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emilio Baiada"}

**Emilio Baiada** (also known as **Emilio Bajada**);[^o8] (12 January 1914 – 14 May 1984) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in mathematical analysis and the calculus of variation.[^o9]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Baiada was born on 12 January 1914 in [Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis). He studied at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), where he graduated with highest honors in June 1937 along with [Leonida Tonelli](wikipedia://en/10018991),[^o11] with whom he worked as an assistant from 1938 to 1941,[^o12] when he left for the war.[^o13] In 1945 he began to teach analysis, theory of functions, [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) and [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) at the Scuola Normale. In 1948 he obtained a degree in Analysis; his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) thesis was written under the direction of Tonelli and [Marston Morse](wikipedia://en/856523).

In 1949 he went on leave from the University of Pisa and moved first to the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati),[^o15][^o16] where he worked with scientists like [Otto Szász](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Szász) and [Charles Napoleon Moore](wikipedia://en/35228519), and then to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University),[^o17] where he worked with Morse.[^o18] In 1952 he obtained the chair of analysis of the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo), where he taught until 1961 before transferring to the [University of Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Modena).[^o19] where he re-launched the Institute of Mathematics and developed its Library and Mathematical Seminar.[^o20]

## Work {#Work}

### Institutional work {#Institutional_work}

Baiada was one of the leading forces behind the reprise in mathematical studies in Modena in the postwar period.[^o23] Teaching mathematical analysis in Modena from 1961,[^o19] he was director of the Institute of Mathematics of the university from the academic year 1962–63 to the 1966—67:[^o24] he promoted its 1966 dedication to [Giuseppe Vitali](wikipedia://en/634990),[^o25] and was also behind the construction of the then new building for the department, which opened officially during the 1974–75 academic year.[^o26] Moreover, he held the direction of the direction of the Atti del Seminario Matematico e Fisico dell'Università di Modena from 1977 up to 1983.[^o27]

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

He published more than 60 papers on differential equations, [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038), series expansion of orthonormal functions, topology of varieties, real analysis, calculus of variations and on the theory of functions.[^o29]

### Teaching activity {#Teaching_activity}

In 1961 he went from Palermo to Modena, teaching several mathematical analysis courses up to the year of his death.[^o31] (Vinti 2007) gives a complete list of Emilio Baiada's doctoral students.

## Honors {#Honors}

Baiada won the Michel prize for the best thesis in Pisa,[^o4] and the 1940 Merlani prize of the [Accademia delle Scienze dell'Istituto di Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_delle_Scienze_dell'Istituto_di_Bologna) for "contributions on subjects of calculus of variations".[^o5] During his stay in Palermo, from 1952 to 1961, he was elected corresponding member of the Accademia Nazionale di Scienze, Lettere e Arti di Palermo.[^o34] In 1967 he was elected [corresponding member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_member) of the Accademia di Scienze, Lettere e Arti di Modena.[^o35] On 9 June 1976, he was awarded the Golden medal "[Benemeriti della Scuola, della Cultura, dell'Arte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benemeriti_della_Scuola,_della_Cultura,_dell'Arte)" by the [President of the Italian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Italian_Republic).[^o6][^o7]
