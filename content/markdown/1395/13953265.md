# Bicone

the geometric shape

*Image: Bicone.svg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bicone"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **bicone** or **dicone** (from bi-, and [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): *di-*, both meaning "two") is the three-dimensional [surface of revolution](wikipedia://en/329549) of a [rhombus](wikipedia://en/183884) around one of its [axes of symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axes_of_symmetry). Equivalently, a bicone is the surface created by joining two [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) [right circular cones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_circular_cone) at their bases. A bicone has [circular symmetry](wikipedia://en/2217599) and orthogonal [bilateral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_symmetry).

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

For a bicone with radius $r$ and half-height $h$, the volume is

$$V = \frac{2}{3} \pi r^2 h$$

and the surface area is

$$A =2\pi r \ell\,$$

where

$$\ell = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$$

is the [slant height](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slant_height).
