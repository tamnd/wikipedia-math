# Current Index to Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Current Index to Statistics"}

The **Current Index to Statistics** was an online database published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) and the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) that contained bibliographic data of articles in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), and related fields. It was shut down at the end of 2019.[^o3]

:::
