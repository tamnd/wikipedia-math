# David Webb (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Webb"}

**David Webb** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work on [hearing the shape of a drum](wikipedia://en/1072325).

:::

Webb attended [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), where he received his PhD in 1983 under the supervision of [Kenneth Stephen Brown](wikipedia://en/41104429). He is currently a professor of mathematics at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) in [Hanover, New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover,_New_Hampshire).[^o3]

In 2001 Webb and co-author [Carolyn S. Gordon](wikipedia://en/25624170) were awarded the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) for their 1996 *[American Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Scientist)* paper, "You can't hear the shape of a drum".[^o4][^o5]
