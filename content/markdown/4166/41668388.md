# Wojciech Kucharz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wojciech Kucharz"}

Prof. [dr. hab.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._hab.) **Wojciech Janusz Kucharz** (born January 2, 1952) is a Polish mathematician, full professor at the Department of Real Functions of the Institute of Mathematics, Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science of the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

- 2022: Knight's Cross of the [Order of Polonia Restituta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Polonia_Restituta)[^o6]
- 2020: Nicolaus Copernicus Scientific Award[^o7]
- 2020: Jagiellonian Laurel Award[^o8]
- 2018: Stefan Banach Award[^o9]
