# Gabon Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabon Mathematical Society"}

The **Gabon Mathematical Society** (in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Language): *Société Mathématique du Gabon*, **SMG**) is a [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) of the mathematicians from [Gabon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabon), recognized by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) as the national mathematical organization for its country.[^o2] It was founded in 2013 and its current president is [Philibert Nang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philibert_Nang), from the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure,_Gabon), [Libreville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libreville).[^o3]
