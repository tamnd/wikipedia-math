# Clement W. H. Lam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clement W. H. Lam"}

**Clement Wing Hong Lam** () is a Canadian mathematician, specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He is famous for the computer proof, with Larry Thiel and S. Swiercz, of the nonexistence of a [finite projective plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_projective_plane) of order 10.[^o2]

:::

Lam earned his PhD in 1974 under [Herbert Ryser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Ryser) at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) with thesis *Rational G-Circulants Satisfying the Matrix Equation $A^2=d I+\lambda J$*. He is a professor at [Concordia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_University) in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal).

In 2006 he received the [Euler medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_medal). In 1992 he received the [Lester Randolph Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Randolph_Ford_Award) for the article *The search for a finite projective plane of order 10*.[^o4] The eponymous [Lam's problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lam's_problem) is equivalent to finding a finite projective plane of order 10 or finding 9 orthogonal [Latin squares](wikipedia://en/37447) of order 10.[^o5]
