# Latvian Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Latvian Mathematical Society"}

The **Latvian Mathematical Society** (in [Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_Language): *Latvijas Matemātikas Biedrība*, **LMB**) is a [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) of mathematicians from [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia), recognized by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) as the national mathematical organization for its country.[^o2] Its goals are stimulating mathematical activity in Latvia while consolidating the former achievements, and it has the responsibility of representing the Latvian mathematicians at the international level.[^o3] It was founded in 1993.[^o4][^o5]

:::

The current president is [Andrejs Reinfelds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrejs_Reinfelds), from the [University of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Latvia), [Riga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga).
