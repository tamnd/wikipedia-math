# Philippe Michel (number theorist)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Philippe Michel"}

**Philippe Gabriel Michel** (born 23 January 1969) is a French mathematician who holds the chair in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in Switzerland.[^o3]

:::

## Early life, education and career {#Early_life,_education_and_career}

Michel was born in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon). He studied from 1989 to 1993 at the [École normale supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Cachan), and then moved to the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), where he earned a doctorate in 1995 under the supervision of [Étienne Fouvry](wikipedia://en/46799655) and then a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1998.[^o3] He was a professor at the [University of Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montpellier) from 1998 to 2008, when he moved to EPFL.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1999, Michel was awarded the Peccot-Vimont Prize[^o7] and gave the [Peccot Lecture](wikipedia://en/60897406) at the Collège de France.[^o8] In 2006, he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o9][^o10] In 2011, he was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o11] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]
