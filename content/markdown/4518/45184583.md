# Joseph A. Thas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph A. Thas"}

*Image: Blokhuis Hirschfeld Jungnickel Thas.jpg.* *From left:* Aart Blokhuis, [James William Peter Hirschfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_William_Peter_Hirschfeld), [Dieter Jungnickel](wikipedia://en/59538916), and Joseph A. Thas, at the [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2001

**Joseph Adolphe François Thas** (born 13 October 1944, [Dilbeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilbeek), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium)) is a Belgian mathematician, who works on [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [incidence geometry](wikipedia://en/1576323) and [finite geometries](wikipedia://en/267061).

:::

Thas received in 1969 his PhD from [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) under Julien Bilo with thesis *Een studie betreffende de projective rechte over de totale matrix algebra $M_3 (K)$ der 3x3-matrices met elementen in een algebraïsch afgesloten veld K*.[^o2] Thas showed how to extend [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) and [cross-ratios](wikipedia://en/401767) with the concept of a [projective line over a ring](wikipedia://en/1271901).[^o3]

Thas is an [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) at Ghent University.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1994 Thas received the [Euler medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_medal). In 1998 he gave an [invited address at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_address_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) with lecture *Finite geometries, varieties and codes*.[^o5] He received in 1969 the prize of the [Royal Academy of Sciences, Letters and Fine Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Science,_Letters_and_Fine_Arts_of_Belgium), in 1970 the Scientific Louis Empain Award and in the same year the François Deruyts prize of the [Royal Academy of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Belgium).

In 1988 he became a member of the [Royal Flemish Academy of Belgium for Science and the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Flemish_Academy_of_Belgium_for_Science_and_the_Arts); he was vice-director of the Class of Sciences in 1998 and director in 1999. In 1999 he was awarded an [Erskine Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Angus_Erskine#Erskine_Fellowship) of the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury), [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand), in 2008 he was Platinum Jubilee Lecturer at the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), and in 2012 he became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

Recipient, [Grand Officer of the Order of Leopold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Officer_of_the_Order_of_Leopold) (Belgium)(*Grootofficier in de Leopoldsorde*) (2012)

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- 1981: (with J. Bilo) *Enkele aspecten van de theorie der axiomatische projectieve vlakken*, [Simon Stevin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Stevin_%28journal%29) Supplement, Vol. 55
- 1984: (with Stanley E. Payne) [*Finite generalized quadrangles*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Finite_Generalized_Quadrangles.html?id=TvtBx19Zx7MC), Pitman, 2nd edition 2009, [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470)
- 1991: (with [J. W. P. Hirschfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._W._P._Hirschfeld)) *General Galois Geometries*, [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press)
- 1995: "Projective geometry over a finite field" and "Generalized Polygons" in F. Buekenhout, *Handbook of Incidence Geometry*, [North-Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Holland_%28imprint%29)
- 2006: (with Koen Thas & H. Van Maldeghem) [*Translation Generalized Quadrangles*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Translation_Generalized_Quadrangles.html?id=ZCuYos5PfkUC), [World Scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Scientific)
