# 100,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="100,000"}

**100,000** (**one hundred thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [99,999](wikipedia://en/13280234#Selected_numbers_in_the_range_90,000–99,999) and preceding 100,001. In scientific notation, it is written as 10<sup>5</sup>.

:::

## Terms for 100,000 {#Terms_for_100,000}

In [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) and [South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asia), one hundred thousand is called a [lakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakh), and is written as **1,00,000**.[^o4] The [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language), [Lao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_language), [Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language) and [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_language) languages also have separate words for this number: แสน, ແສນ, សែន (all *saen*), and ức respectively. The [Malagasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_language) word is hetsy.[^o5]

In [the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), a 'ton' is a colloquialism for a denomination of 100.000 monetary units. In the [guilders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_guilder) period a ton would denote 100.000 guilders. With the introduction of the euro, a ton would come to mean 100.000 euros. Usage is mostly limited to the financial sphere and to the buying and selling of houses. It is not used in official settings because of the ambiguity with the commonly used [metric tonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonne). While usage is common in the Netherlands, it sees almost no use in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium).

In [Cyrillic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic_numerals), it is known as the legion (Cyrl):

## Values of 100,000 {#Values_of_100,000}

In [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), **100,000 metres**, **100 kilometres**, or **100 km** (62 miles) is the [altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude) at which the [Fédération Aéronautique Internationale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fédération_Aéronautique_Internationale) (FAI) defines [spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaceflight) to begin.

In [paleoclimatology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoclimatology), the [100,000-year problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100,000-year_problem) is a mismatch between the temperature record and the modeled [incoming solar radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_irradiation).

In the [Irish language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language), **ga** () is a popular greeting meaning "a hundred thousand welcomes".

### Prime numbers {#Prime_numbers}

There are **9,592** primes less than 10<sup>5</sup>, where 99,991 is the largest prime number smaller than 100,000.

Increments of 10<sup>5</sup> from 100,000 through a [one million](wikipedia://en/1045999) have the following prime counts:

- **8,392** primes between 100,000 and 200,000. This is a difference of [1,200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1200_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - 104,729 is the 10,000th prime, which is in this range.
  - 199,999 is prime.
- **8,013** primes between 200,000 and 300,000. A difference of [379](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/379_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - 224,737 is the 20,000th prime.
- **7,863** primes between 300,000 and 400,000. A difference of [150](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/150_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - 350,377 is the 30,000th prime.
- **7,678** primes between 400,000 and 500,000. A difference of [185](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/185_%28number%29) primes from the previous range. Here, the difference increases by a count of [35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/35_%28number%29).
  - 479,909 is the 40,000th prime.
- **7,560** primes between 500,000 and 600,000. A difference of [118](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/118_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - **[7,560](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7560_%28number%29)** is the twentieth highly composite number.[^o19]
  - 599,999 is prime.
- **7,445** primes between 600,000 and 700,000. A difference of [115](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/115_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - 611,953 is the 50,000th prime.
- **7,408** primes between 700,000 and 800,000. A difference of [37](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/37_%28number%29) primes from the previous range.
  - 700,001 and 799,999 are both prime.
  - 746,773 is the 60,000th prime.
- **7,323** primes between 800,000 and 900,000. A difference of [85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/85_%28number%29) primes from the previous range. Here, the difference increases by a count of [48](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/48_%28number%29).
  - 882,377 is the 70,000th prime.
- **7,224** primes between 900,000 and [1,000,000](wikipedia://en/1045999). A difference of [99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99_%28number%29) primes from the previous range. The difference increases again, by a count of [14](wikipedia://en/19223527).
  - 900,001 is prime.

In total, there are **68,906** prime numbers between 100,000 and 1,000,000.[^o88]
