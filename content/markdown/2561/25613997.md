# John Watrous (computer scientist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Watrous"}

**John Harrison Watrous** is an American computer scientist who served as the Technical Director of IBM Quantum Education at [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) and was a professor of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), a member of the [Institute for Quantum Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Quantum_Computing), an affiliate member of the [Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics) and a Fellow of the [Canadian Institute for Advanced Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Institute_for_Advanced_Research).[^o3][^o4] He was a faculty member in the Department of Computer Science at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) from 2002 to 2006 where he held a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220).[^o3]

:::

He is an editor of the journal *[Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Computing_%28journal%29)*[^o5] and former editor for the journal *[Quantum Information & Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Information_&_Computation)*.[^o6] His research interests include [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) and [quantum computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_computation). He is well known for his work on [quantum interactive proofs](wikipedia://en/25608560), and the quantum analogue of the celebrated result [IP](wikipedia://en/2197070) = [PSPACE](wikipedia://en/39351): [QIP](wikipedia://en/25608560) = PSPACE.[^o7][^o8][^o9] This was preceded by a series of results, showing QIP can be constrained to 3 messages,[^o10] QIP is contained in [EXP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EXP),[^o11] and the 2-message version of QIP is in PSPACE.[^o12] He has also published important papers on [quantum finite automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_finite_automata)[^o13] and [quantum cellular automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_cellular_automata).[^o14] With [Scott Aaronson](wikipedia://en/20420243), he showed that certain forms of [time travel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_travel) can make [quantum and classical computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Computation) equivalent: together, the authors showed that quantum effects do not offer advantages for computation if computers can send information to the past through a type of [closed timelike curve](wikipedia://en/287091) proposed by the physicist [David Deutsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Deutsch).[^o15]

He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1998 at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) under the supervision of [Eric Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Bach).[^o16][^o17]
