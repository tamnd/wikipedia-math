# Eberhard Melchior

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eberhard Melchior"}

**Eberhard Melchior** (born 12 November 1912 – July 11, 1956)[^o2] was a German mathematician who provided the first proof of [Sylvester's line problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester&ndash;Gallai_theorem) in 1940.[^o3] He also discovered the [related inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melchior's_inequality) now named after him.

:::

Melchior was born in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden) on 12 November 1912. He studied at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and at the [Dresden Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_Technical_University) (TH Dresden). He obtained his Ph.D. at [Berlin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_University) on 2 June 1937 with a thesis on a problem on the theory of configurations[^o4] advised by [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186).[^o5]
