# Term (argumentation)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Term"}

In [argumentation theory](wikipedia://en/1317383), a **term** (or **notion**) is that part of a statement in an argument which refers to a specific thing. A term is usually, but not always expressed as a [noun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noun). According to *Essentials of Logic*, the word is derived from the Latin "terminus."[^o2]

:::

One of the requirements to informally prove a conclusion with a [deductive argument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deductive_argument) is for all its terms to be used [unambiguously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unambiguous). The ambiguous use of a term in a deductive argument may be an instance of the [fallacy of four terms](wikipedia://en/698149).[^o2]
