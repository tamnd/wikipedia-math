# Sergei Viktorovich Bochkarev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Viktorovich Bochkarev"}

**Sergei** (or **Sergey**) **Viktorovich Bochkarev** (or **Bočkarev**) (Сергей Викторович Бочкарёв, July 24, 1941, in Kuybyshev now renamed [Samara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samara) – July 8, 2021[^o2]) is a Soviet and Russian mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received in 1964 his undergraduate degree from [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) and in 1969 his Russian [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree (PhD) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). His dissertation о рядах Фурье по системе Хаара[^o4] (On Fourier series in the Haar system) was supervised by [Pyotr Lavrentyevich Ulyanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyotr_Lavrentyevich_Ulyanov).[^o6] From Moscow State University, Bochkarev received in 1974 his Russian [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degree (habilitation).[^o7] Since 1971 he has worked at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494), where he holds the title of leading scientific researcher in the Department of Function Theory.[^o8]

His research deals with harmonic analysis, [BMO spaces](wikipedia://en/4694026), [Hardy spaces](wikipedia://en/314780), functional analysis, construction of orthogonal bases in various [function spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Function_spaces), and exponential sums.

In 1977 he was awarded the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize). In 1978 he was an Invited Speaker with talk Метод усреднения в теории ортогональных рядов (The averaging method in the theory of orthogonal bases) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Helsinki.[^o9][^o10]
