::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of six tetrahedra with rotational freedom"}

The **compound of six tetrahedra with rotational freedom** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) made of a symmetric arrangement of 6 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), considered as [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms). It can be constructed by superimposing six tetrahedra within a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), and then rotating them in pairs about the three axes that pass through the centres of two opposite cubic faces. Each tetrahedron is rotated by an equal (and opposite, within a pair) angle *θ*. Equivalently, a tetrahedron may be inscribed within each [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) in the [compound of six cubes with rotational freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_six_cubes_with_rotational_freedom), in such a way as to preserve [tetrahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2904472).

:::

When *θ* = 0, all six tetrahedra coincide. When *θ* is 45 degrees, the more symmetric [compound of six tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/14810194) (without rotational freedom) arises. Otherwise, the compound of six tetrahedra with rotational freedom shares the same vertex arrangement as a nonuniform [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654) in a way related to a beveled tetrahedron.
