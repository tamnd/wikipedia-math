::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of twenty octahedra with rotational freedom"}

The **compound of twenty octahedra with rotational freedom** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It's composed of a symmetric arrangement of 20 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), considered as triangular [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms). It can be constructed by superimposing two copies of the [compound of 10 octahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compounds_of_ten_octahedra) UC<sub>16</sub>, and for each resulting pair of octahedra, rotating each octahedron in the pair by an equal and opposite angle *θ*.

:::

When *θ* is zero or 60 degrees, the octahedra coincide in pairs yielding (two superimposed copies of) the [compounds of ten octahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compounds_of_ten_octahedra) UC<sub>16</sub> and UC<sub>15</sub> respectively. When

$$\theta=2\tan^{-1}\left(\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}\left(13-4\sqrt{10}\right)}\right)\approx 37.76124^\circ,$$

octahedra (from distinct rotational axes) coincide in sets four, yielding the [compound of five octahedra](wikipedia://en/8010546). When

$$\theta=2\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-4\sqrt{3}-2\sqrt{15}+\sqrt{132+60\sqrt{5}}}{4+\sqrt{2}+2\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{10}}\right)\approx14.33033^\circ,$$

the vertices coincide in pairs, yielding the [compound of twenty octahedra](wikipedia://en/14737027) (without rotational freedom).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

$$\begin{aligned} & \scriptstyle \Big( \pm2\sqrt3\sin\theta,\, \pm(\tau^{-1}\sqrt2+2\tau\cos\theta),\, \pm(\tau\sqrt2-2\tau^{-1}\cos\theta) \Big) \\ & \scriptstyle \Big( \pm(\sqrt2 -\tau^2\cos\theta + \tau^{-1}\sqrt3\sin\theta),\, \pm(\sqrt2 + (2\tau-1)\cos\theta + \sqrt3\sin\theta),\, \pm(\sqrt2 + \tau^{-2}\cos\theta - \tau\sqrt3\sin\theta) \Big) \\ & \scriptstyle \Big(\pm(\tau^{-1}\sqrt2-\tau\cos\theta-\tau\sqrt3\sin\theta),\, \pm(\tau\sqrt2 + \tau^{-1}\cos\theta+\tau^{-1}\sqrt3\sin\theta),\, \pm(3\cos\theta-\sqrt3\sin\theta) \Big) \\ & \scriptstyle \Big(\pm(-\tau^{-1}\sqrt2 + \tau\cos\theta - \tau\sqrt 3\sin\theta),\, \pm(\tau\sqrt2 + \tau^{-1}\cos\theta-\tau^{-1}\sqrt3\sin\theta),\, \pm(3\cos\theta+\sqrt3\sin\theta) \Big) \\ & \scriptstyle \Big(\pm(-\sqrt 2 + \tau^2\cos\theta+\tau^{-1}\sqrt 3 \sin\theta), \, \pm(\sqrt 2 + (2\tau-1)\cos\theta - \sqrt 3 \sin\theta), \, \pm(\sqrt 2 + \tau^{-2}\cos\theta + \tau\sqrt 3 \sin\theta) \Big) \end{aligned}$$

where *τ* = (1 + $\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written *φ*).
