::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of twelve pentagonal antiprisms with rotational freedom"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 12 [pentagonal antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860091). It can be constructed by inscribing one pair of pentagonal antiprisms within an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), in each of the six possible ways, and then rotating each by an equal and opposite angle θ.

:::

When θ is 36 degrees, the antiprisms coincide in pairs to yield (two superimposed copies of) the compound of six pentagonal antiprisms (without rotational freedom).

This compound shares its vertices with the compound of twelve pentagrammic crossed antiprisms with rotational freedom.

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(±(2τ−1−(2τ+4)cos θ), ±2(√(5τ+10))sin θ, ±(τ+2+(4τ−2)cos θ))

(±(2τ−1−(2τ−1)cos θ−τ(√(5τ+10))sin θ), ±(−5τcos θ+τ<sup>−1</sup>(√(5τ+10))sin θ),

±(τ+2+(3−τ)cos θ+(√(5τ+10))sin θ))

(±(2τ−1+(1+3τ)cos θ−(√(5τ+10))sin θ), ±(−5cos θ−τ(√(5τ+10))sin θ),

±(τ+2−(τ+2)cos θ+τ<sup>−1</sup>(√(5τ+10))sin θ))

(±(2τ−1+(1+3τ)cos θ+(√(5τ+10))sin θ), ±(5cos θ−τ(√(5τ+10))sin θ),

±(τ+2−(τ+2)cos θ−τ<sup>−1</sup>(√(5τ+10))sin θ))

(±(2τ−1−(2τ−1)cos θ+τ(√(5τ+10))sin θ), ±(5τcos θ+τ<sup>−1</sup>(√(5τ+10))sin θ),

±(τ+2+(3−τ)cos θ−(√(5τ+10))sin θ))

where τ = (1+√5)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).
