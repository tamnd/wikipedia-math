::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of twelve tetrahedra with rotational freedom"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 12 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), considered as [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms). It can be constructed by superimposing six identical copies of the [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula), and then rotating them in pairs about the three axes that pass through the centres of two opposite cubic faces. Each [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) is rotated by an equal (and opposite, within a pair) angle θ. Equivalently, a [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) may be inscribed within each [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) in the [compound of six cubes with rotational freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_six_cubes_with_rotational_freedom), which has the same vertices as this compound.

:::

When *θ* = 0, all six [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) coincide. When *θ* is 45 degrees, the [stella octangula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_octangula) coincide in pairs yielding (two superimposed copies of) the [compound of six tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/14810194).

Except if θ = 0 or θ = 45, the compound of twelve tetrahedra with rotational freedom will have a [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of a nonuniform [truncated cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879)
