# Vladimir Kanovei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Kanovei"}

**Vladimir G. Kanovei** (born 1951) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working at the Institute for Information Transmission Problems in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), Russia. His interests include [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [foundations](wikipedia://en/169358), as well as [mathematical history](wikipedia://en/14220).

:::
