# Amplitude death

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amplitude death"}

In the theory of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), **amplitude death** refers to a complete stop of [oscillations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscillation) as a result of coupling interactions. The phenomenon can arise from systems either in [periodic motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periodic_motion) or [chaotic motion](wikipedia://en/6295) before it goes to amplitude death.[^o2] Understanding the complex behavior of oscillators is important for applications in pendulums, fluid, ecological, and population dynamics.

:::

A dynamical system can go to amplitude death because of change in intrinsic parameters of the system or its interaction with other systems or its environment.[^o3][^o4] Amplitude death can appear also because of the delay in the coupling between the systems[^o5][^o6]
