# Good filtration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Good filtration"}

In mathematical [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **good filtration** is a [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) of a representation of a [reductive algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_algebraic_group) *G* such that the [subquotients](wikipedia://en/1639294) are isomorphic to the spaces of [sections](wikipedia://en/367621) *F*(λ) of [line bundles](wikipedia://en/361366) λ over *G*/*B* for a [Borel subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) *B*. In [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 0 this is automatically true as the [irreducible modules](wikipedia://en/29430) are all of the form *F*(λ), but this is not usually true in positive characteristic. (Mathieu 1990) showed that the [tensor product of two modules](wikipedia://en/43079145) *F*(λ)⊗*F*(μ) has a good filtration, completing the results of (Donkin 1985) who proved it in most cases and (Wang 1982) who proved it in large characteristic. (Littelmann 1992) showed that the existence of good filtrations for these tensor products also follows from [standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718).

:::
