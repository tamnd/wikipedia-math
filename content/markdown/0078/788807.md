# Harold Stark

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harold Stark"}

**Harold Mead Stark** (born August 6, 1939)[^o3] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He is best known for his solution[^o4] of the [Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) [class number 1 problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_1_problem), in effect [correcting and completing](wikipedia://en/391251) the earlier work of [Kurt Heegner](wikipedia://en/621799), and for [Stark's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stark's_conjecture). More recently, he collaborated with [Audrey Terras](wikipedia://en/23003425) to study [zeta functions](wikipedia://en/1342362) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is currently on the faculty of the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

Stark received his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1961 and his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1964. He was on the faculty at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) from 1964 to 1968, at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1968 to 1980, and at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) from 1980 to the present.[^o5]

Stark was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1983 and to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2007.[^o3][^o5] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
