# Instituto Nacional de Estatística (Cape Verde)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Instituto Nacional de Estatística"}

The **Instituto Nacional de Estatística** (Portuguese for the *National Statistics Institute*, abbreviated as **INE**) is the [public statistics institute](wikipedia://en/18646178) of [Cape Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verde).[^o2] Its current president is Osvaldo Rui Monteiro dos Reis Borges.[^o3] The population [censuses](wikipedia://en/6889) are held every first year of a decade; the most recent was in 2010. Its main office is on 18 Rua da Caixa Económica in the capital city of [Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praia).[^o3]

:::

## History {#History}

Until 1975, under Portuguese rule, statistics were done by the Provincial Statistics Bureau (*Repartição Provincial de Estatística*), part of the [Instituto Nacional de Estatística](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Estatística_%28Portugal%29) of Portugal. When Cape Verde became independent, the *Serviço Nacional de Estatística* (SNE, Portuguese for the National Statistics Service) was established, part of the Ministry of Economy. Its first president was Edgard Chrysostome Pinto. At the end of 1985, the Directorate-General of Statistics was created. In 1996, the new law of the National Statistics System was approved, and the *Istituto Nacional de Estatística* (INE) was created under the direction of Francisco Fernandes Tavares.[^o5]
