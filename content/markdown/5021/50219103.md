# John Strain (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Strain"}

**John Andrew Strain** is a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). His areas of interest are [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics), [Algorithms](wikipedia://en/775), [Numerical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Analysis), and [Materials Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_Science).[^o2] John Strain received his PhD in Mathematics from the University of California, Berkeley in 1988 working with his advisor [Alexandre Joel Chorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Joel_Chorin).[^o3] His [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) paper was on the numerical study of dendritic solidification. Notable publications include *Piecewise-polynomial discretization and Krylov-accelerated multigrid for elliptic interface problems*,[^o4] *Locally corrected semi-Lagrangian methods for Stokes flow with moving elastic interfaces*,[^o5] *Locally-corrected spectral methods and overdetermined elliptic systems*,[^o6] *Fractional step methods for index-1 differential-algebraic equations*,[^o7] and *Growth of the zeta function for a quadratic map and the dimension of the Julia set*.[^o8]

:::
