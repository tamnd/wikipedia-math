# Gianfranco Cimmino

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gianfranco Cimmino"}

**Gianfranco Cimmino** (12 March 1908 – 30 May 1989) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and [theory of elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787): he is known for being the first mathematician generalizing in a [weak sense](wikipedia://en/3368981) the notion of boundary value in a [boundary value problem](wikipedia://en/436779),[^o3][^o4] and for doing an influential work in numerical analysis.[^o5]

:::
