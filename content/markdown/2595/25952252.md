::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Pentellated 6-simplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentellated 6-simplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728) with 5th order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex).

:::

There are unique 10 degrees of pentellations of the 6-simplex with permutations of truncations, cantellations, runcinations, and sterications. The simple **pentellated 6-simplex** is also called an **expanded 6-simplex**, constructed by an [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) operation applied to the regular [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex). The highest form, the *pentisteriruncicantitruncated 6-simplex*, is called an *omnitruncated 6-simplex* with all of the nodes ringed.

### Cross-sections {#Cross-sections}

The maximal cross-section of the pentellated 6-simplex with a 5-dimensional hyperplane is a [stericated hexateron](wikipedia://en/22946968#Stericated_5-simplex). This cross-section divides the pentellated 6-simplex into two [hexateral hypercupolas](wikipedia://en/689743#Hypercupolas) consisting of 7 [5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), 21 [5-cell prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-cell_prism) and 35 [Tetrahedral-Triangular duoprisms](wikipedia://en/2114155) each.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *pentellated 6-simplex* can be positioned in 7-space as permutations of (0,1,1,1,1,1,2). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [pentellated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentellated_7-orthoplex).

A second construction in 7-space, from the center of a [rectified 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-orthoplex) is given by coordinate permutations of:

(1,-1,0,0,0,0,0)

### Root vectors {#Root_vectors}

Its 42 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) A<sub>6</sub>. It is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [6-simplex honeycomb](wikipedia://en/31747782).

The **omnitruncated 6-simplex** has 5040 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 15120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 16800 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) (4200 [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and 1260 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29)), 8400 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29), 1806 4-faces, and 126 5-faces. ### Permutohedron and related tessellation {#Permutohedron_and_related_tessellation}

The omnitruncated 6-simplex is the [permutohedron](wikipedia://en/12745947) of order 7. The omnitruncated 6-simplex is a [zonotope](wikipedia://en/669406), the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of seven line segments parallel to the seven lines through the origin and the seven vertices of the 6-simplex.

Like all uniform omnitruncated n-simplices, the **omnitruncated 6-simplex** can [tessellate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessellate) space by itself, in this case 6-dimensional space with three facets around each hypercell. ### Full snub 6-simplex {#Full_snub_6-simplex}

The **full snub 6-simplex** or **omnisnub 6-simplex**, defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 6-simplex is not uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502) 3,3,3,3,3<sup>\+</sup>, and constructed from 14 [snub 5-simplexes](wikipedia://en/22946968#Full_snub_5-simplex), 42 snub 5-cell antiprisms, 70 3-s{3,4} duoantiprisms, and 2520 irregular [5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices.

## Related uniform 6-polytopes {#Related_uniform_6-polytopes}

The pentellated 6-simplexes are in a set of 35 [uniform 6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821728) based on the \[3,3,3,3,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004), all shown here in A<sub>6</sub> [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052).
