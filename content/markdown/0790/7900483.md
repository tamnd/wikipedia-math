# Siavash Shahshahani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Siavash Shahshahani"}

**Siavash Mirshams Shahshahani** (سیاوش میرشمس شهشهانی; born May 31, 1942) is an Iranian mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics and head of Mathematical Sciences Department at [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_University_of_Technology). He headed up the IRNIC registry for the [.ir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.ir) ccTLD until his retirement from that position in late 2008. He has also served as a director of [APTLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APTLD) (the Asia Pacific Top Level Domain Association) between 2007 and his retirement from that position in February 2009.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Shahshahani completed his PhD under the supervision of [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294) at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) in 1969. He has since devoted a substantial part of his career to mathematical education.
