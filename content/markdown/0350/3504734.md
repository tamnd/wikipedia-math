# Harnack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harnack"}

**Harnack** is the surname of a German family of intellectuals, artists, mathematicians, scientists, theologians and those in other fields. Several family members were executed by the Nazis during the last years of the [Third Reich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Reich).

:::

- [Theodosius Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodosius_Harnack) (1817–1889), German theologian
  - Anna Harnack (1849–?)
  - [Adolf von Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_von_Harnack) (1851–1930), German liberal theologian and historian of religion
    - Agnes von Zahn-Harnack (1884–1950), German writer and women's rights activist
    - [Ernst von Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_von_Harnack) (1888–1945), German anti-Nazi resistance fighter
      - Gustav-Adolf von Harnack (1917–2010), German pediatrician
    - Elisabet von Harnack (1892–1976), German social worker
    - [Axel von Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axel_von_Harnack) (1895–1974), German historian and philologist
  - [Carl Gustav Axel Harnack](wikipedia://en/1925276) (1851–1888), German mathematician
  - Erich Harnack, professor of pharmacology
  - Otto Harnack, literature historian
    - Clara Harnack, painter and wife of Otto
      - [Arvid Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Harnack) (1901–1942), German anti-Nazi resistance fighter
        - [Mildred Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mildred_Harnack) (1902–1943), American anti-Nazi resistance fighter, wife of Arvid
      - [Falk Harnack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falk_Harnack) (1913–1991), German anti-Nazi resistance fighter, film and stage director, brother of Arvid Harnack
