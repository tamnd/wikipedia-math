::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Trigonal trapezohedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **trigonal trapezohedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with six [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) quadrilateral faces, which may be scalene or rhomboid.[^o9][^o10] The variety with [rhombus](wikipedia://en/183884)-shaped [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) is a [rhombohedron](wikipedia://en/2706406).[^o11][^o12] An alternative name for the same shape is the *trigonal deltohedron*.[^o13]

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

Six identical rhombic faces can construct two configurations of trigonal trapezohedra. The *acute* or *prolate* form has three acute angle corners of the rhombic faces meeting at the two polar axis vertices. The *obtuse* or *oblate* or *flat* form has three obtuse angle corners of the rhombic faces meeting at the two polar axis vertices.

More strongly than having all faces congruent, the trigonal trapezohedra are [isohedral figures](wikipedia://en/6286120), meaning that they have symmetries that take any face to any other face.[^o12]

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

A [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) is a special case of a trigonal trapezohedron, since a square is a special case of a rhombus.[^o17]

A [gyroelongated triangular bipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroelongated_triangular_bipyramid) constructed with equilateral triangles can also be seen as a trigonal trapezohedron when its coplanar triangles are merged into rhombi.

The two **golden rhombohedra** are the acute and obtuse form of the trigonal trapezohedron with [golden rhombus](wikipedia://en/20825292) faces. Copies of these can be assembled to form other convex polyhedra with golden rhombus faces, including the [Bilinski dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/50652823) and [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025).[^o18]

Four oblate rhombohedra whose ratio of face diagonal lengths are the [square root of two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_root_of_two) can be assembled to form a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631). The same rhombohedra also tile space in the [trigonal trapezohedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41775061).[^o19]

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The trigonal trapezohedra are special cases of [trapezohedra](wikipedia://en/719845), polyhedra with an even number of congruent [kite](wikipedia://en/84840)-shaped faces. When this number of faces is six, the kites [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952) to rhombi, and the result is a trigonal trapezohedron. As with the rhombohedra more generally, the trigonal trapezohedra are also special cases of [parallelepipeds](wikipedia://en/23975), and are the only parallelepipeds with six congruent faces. Parallelepipeds are [zonohedra](wikipedia://en/669402), and [Evgraf Fedorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgraf_Fedorov) proved that the trigonal trapezohedra are the only infinite family of zonohedra whose faces are all congruent rhombi.[^o12]

[Dürer's solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dürer's_solid) is generally presumed to be a [truncated triangular trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/41031846), a trigonal trapezohedron with two opposite vertices [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429), although its precise shape is still a matter for debate.[^o13]
