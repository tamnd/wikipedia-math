# Hexagonal trapezohedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hexagonal trapezohedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hexagonal trapezohedron** or **deltohedron** is the fourth in an infinite series of [trapezohedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trapezohedra) which are [dual polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polyhedra) to the [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851). It has twelve faces which are [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) [kites](wikipedia://en/84840). It can be described by the [Conway notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) [$dA6$](http://levskaya.github.io/polyhedronisme/?recipe=C100dA6).

:::

It is an [isohedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isohedral) (face-transitive) figure, meaning that all its [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) are the same. More specifically, all faces are not merely congruent but also *transitive*, i.e. lie within the same *[symmetry orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_orbit)*. [Convex](wikipedia://en/1817885) isohedral polyhedra are the shapes that will make [fair dice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_dice).[^o3]

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Hexagonal trapezohedron.stl.* 3D model of a hexagonal trapezohedron

The [symmetry](wikipedia://en/28186) a hexagonal trapezohedron is D<sub>6d</sub> of order 24. The [rotation group](wikipedia://en/2694525#Rotation_groups) is D<sub>6</sub> of order 12.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Variations"}

## Variations {#Variations}

One degree of freedom within D<sub>6</sub> symmetry changes the kites into congruent quadrilaterals with 3 edges lengths. In the limit, one edge of each quadrilateral goes to zero length, and these become [bipyramids](wikipedia://en/4153).

[Crystal arrangements](wikipedia://en/456410) of atoms can repeat in space with a hexagonal trapezohedral configuration around one atom, which is always [enantiomorphous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomorphous),[^o8] and comprises [space groups](wikipedia://en/463721) 177–182.[^o9] [Beta-quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta-quartz) is the only common mineral with this crystal system.[^o10]

If the kites surrounding the two peaks are of different shapes, it can only have C<sub>6v</sub> symmetry, order 12. These can be called *unequal trapezohedra*. The dual is an *unequal [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851)*, with the top and bottom polygons of different radii. If it twisted and unequal its symmetry is reduced to cyclic symmetry, C<sub>6</sub> symmetry, order 6.

## Spherical tiling {#Spherical_tiling}

The hexagonal trapezohedron also exists as a [spherical tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_tiling), with 2 vertices on the poles, and alternating vertices equally spaced above and below the equator.
