# Jean Jacod

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Jacod"}

**Jean Jacod** (born 13 November 1944)[^o3] is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in stochastic processes and probability theory. He has been a professor at the [Université Pierre et Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Pierre_et_Marie_Curie).[^o4] He has made fundamental contributions to a wide range of topics in probability theory including [stochastic calculus](wikipedia://en/228001), [limit theorems](wikipedia://en/39406), martingale problems, [Malliavin calculus](wikipedia://en/837875) and statistics of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Jean Jacod graduated from [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique) in 1965 and obtained his [Doctorat d'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'État) in Mathematics from the Université Paris-VI. His advisor was [Jacques Neveu](wikipedia://en/56512426).
