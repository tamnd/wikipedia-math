# Roger Horn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roger Horn"}

**Roger Alan Horn** (born January 19, 1942) is an American mathematician specializing in [matrix analysis](wikipedia://en/40956141). He was research professor of mathematics at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah). He is known for formulating the [Bateman–Horn conjecture](wikipedia://en/1085053) with [Paul T. Bateman](wikipedia://en/26642296) on the density of [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) values generated by systems of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000).[^o4] His books *Matrix Analysis* and *Topics in Matrix Analysis*, co-written with [Charles R. Johnson](wikipedia://en/29828058), are standard texts in advanced linear algebra.[^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Roger Horn graduated from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) with high honors in mathematics in 1963,[^o9] after which he completed his PhD at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1967. Horn was the founder and chair of the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) from 1972 to 1979.[^o10] As chair, he held a series of short courses for a monograph series published by the [Johns Hopkins Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Press). He invited [Gene Golub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Golub) and [Charles Van Loan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Van_Loan) to write a monograph, which later became the seminal *Matrix Computations* text book.[^o11] He later joined the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) as research professor. In 2007, the journal [Linear Algebra and its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Algebra_and_its_Applications) published a special issue in honor of Roger Horn.[^o12] He was Editor of [The American Mathematical Monthly](wikipedia://en/1179483) during 1997–2001.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1987, Horn submitted testimony to the US Senate Subcommittee on Transportation regarding the [1987 Maryland train collision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Maryland_train_collision) which killed his 16-year-old daughter [Ceres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Maryland_train_collision#Memorials) who was returning to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from the family home in Baltimore for her freshman year fall term final exams.[^o14]
