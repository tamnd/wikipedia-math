# Viorel P. Barbu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Viorel P. Barbu"}

**Viorel P. Barbu** (born 14 June 1941) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), and [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Deleni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deleni,_Vaslui), [Vaslui County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaslui_County), Romania.[^o3] He attended the Mihail Kogălniceanu High School in [Vaslui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaslui) and then the [Costache Negruzzi National College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costache_Negruzzi_National_College) in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași). Barbu completed his undergraduate degree at the [Alexandru Ioan Cuza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University) of Iași in 1964,[^o3] and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the same university in 1969. His doctoral advisor was Adolf Haimovici; his dissertation thesis was titled *Regularity Theory of Pseudodifferential Operators*.[^o4] He became a professor at the University of Iași in 1980.[^o3]

In 1993, he was elected a titular member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy).[^o5] In 2011 he was awarded the [Order of the Star of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Star_of_Romania), Knight rank by [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Romania) [Traian Băsescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traian_Băsescu).[^o6]
