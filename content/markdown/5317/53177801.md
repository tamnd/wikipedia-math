# Michael Vogelius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Vogelius"}

**Michael Steenstrup Vogelius** (born 1953) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Vogelius completed his [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy). at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) College Park in 1980. His doctoral advisor was [Ivo Babuška](wikipedia://en/13503456). His dissertation thesis was titled *A Dimensional Reduction Approach to the Solution of Partial Differential Equations*.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Vogelius has been a member of the faculty of the Mathematics Department at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) since 1989.[^o5] Since 1997, he has supervised the doctoral dissertations of at least six students at the [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o3] He has also worked as a Division Director at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation).[^o6][^o7]
