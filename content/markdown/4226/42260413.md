# Erdős–Nicolas number

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Erdős–Nicolas number"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), an **Erdős–Nicolas number** is a number that is not [perfect](wikipedia://en/23670), but that equals one of the [partial sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_sum) of its [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492). That is, a number $n$ is an Erdős–Nicolas number when there exists another number $m$ such that

:::

$\sum_{d\mid n,\ d\leq m}d=n.$[^o3]

The first ten Erdős–Nicolas numbers are

[24](wikipedia://en/362201), 2016, 8190, 42336, 45864, 392448, 714240, 1571328, 61900800 and 91963648. ()

They are named after [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Jean-Louis Nicolas](wikipedia://en/45046166), who wrote about them in 1975.[^o4]
