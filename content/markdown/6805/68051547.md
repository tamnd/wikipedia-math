# Otto Forster

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Forster"}

*Image: OttoForster.jpg.* Otto Forster in [Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) 1987

**Otto Forster** (born 8 July 1937 in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich)) is a German mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Forster received his *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)* in 1960 from [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) (LMU). There he received his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) in 1961. His thesis *Banachalgebren stetiger Funktionen auf kompakten Räumen* (Banach algebras of continuous functions on compact spaces) was supervised by [Karl Stein](wikipedia://en/7218529). In 1965 Forster also completed his habilitation in Munich. After spending the academic year 1966–1967 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179)[^o3] and the academic year 1967–1968 as a substitute professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), he became a full professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) in 1968. In 1968–1969 he was a visiting professor at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva). In 1975, he moved to the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). Since 1982 he has been a professor at the Mathematical Institute of the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München. Even after his retirement in summer 2005, he still regularly offers lectures for advanced students.

In 1970, he was an invited speaker with talk *Topologische Methoden in der Theorie Steinscher Räume* (Topological methods in the theory of [Stein spaces](wikipedia://en/1053909)) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).[^o4] In 1984 he became a member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).

Forster's research deals mainly with [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), but also with questions of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), and [algorithmic number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_number_theory). His program ARIBAS, an interpreter with a [Pascal-like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_%28programming_language%29) syntax, offers powerful [arbitrary-precision arithmetic](wikipedia://en/600892) and various library functions based on such computational arithmetic. ARIBAS, available under the [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License), also serves as the basis for the algorithms discussed in Forster's book *Algorithmische Zahlentheorie* (Algorithmic number theory).[^o5] He wrote two appendices for the 2nd edition of [Dale Husemöller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Husemoller)'s book *Elliptic Curves*.[^o6]

He discovered the [Forster–Swan theorem](wikipedia://en/78537564).
