# Zinovy Reichstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zinovy Reichstein"}

**Zinovy B. Reichstein** (born 1961) is a Russian-born American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a professor at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver). He studies mainly [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups). He introduced (with [Joe P. Buhler](wikipedia://en/58419310)) the concept of [essential dimension](wikipedia://en/16950168).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

In high school, Reichstein participated in the national mathematics olympiad in Russia and was the third highest scorer in 1977 and second highest scorer in 1978.

Because of the [Antisemitism in the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism_in_the_Soviet_Union) at the time, Reichstein was not accepted to Moscow University, even though he had passed the special math entrance exams. He attended a semester of college at [Russian University of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University_of_Railway_Engineering) instead.

His family then decided to emigrate, arriving in Vienna, Austria, in August 1979 and New York, United States in the fall of 1980. Reichstein worked as a delivery boy for a short period of time in New York. He was then accepted to and attended [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) for his undergraduate studies.[^o5]

Reichstein received his [PhD degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD_degree) in 1988 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under the supervision of [Michael Artin](wikipedia://en/1519821). Parts of his thesis entitled "The Behavior of Stability under Equivariant Maps" were published in the journal *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)*.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

As of 2011, Reichstein is on the editorial board of the mathematics journal [*Transformation groups*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_groups_%28journal%29).[^o8]

After graduating from Harvard, Reichstein joined the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). In 1992, he began working at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). The following year, he moved to [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University). In 2001, Reichstein accepted a position at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia).[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Winner of the 2013 [Jeffery-Williams Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffery-Williams_Prize) awarded by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055)[^o11]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012[^o12]
- Invited Speaker to the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) ([Hyderabad, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India) 2010)[^o13]
- The Algebraic Groups and Algebraic Geometry conference was held in 2021 in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o14]
- [Putnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition#Winners) Individual, Honorable Mention 1980[^o15]
- Putnam Team (Honorable Mention) 1981[^o15]
- Putnam Team (3rd Place) 1982[^o15]
