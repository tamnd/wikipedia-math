# Jean-Loup Waldspurger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Loup Waldspurger"}

**Jean-Loup Waldspurger** (born 2 July 1953) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905) and related areas. He proved [Waldspurger's theorem](wikipedia://en/35154167), the [Waldspurger formula](wikipedia://en/55941264), and the local [Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture](wikipedia://en/58884863) for orthogonal groups. He played a role in the proof of the [fundamental lemma](wikipedia://en/25431009), reducing the conjecture to a version for [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras). This formulation was ultimately proven by [Ngô Bảo Châu](wikipedia://en/11133659).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Waldspurger attained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) at [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in 1980, under supervision of [Marie-France Vignéras](wikipedia://en/31869453).

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

J.-L. Waldspurger's work concerns the theory of automorphic forms. He highlighted the links between Fourier coefficients of modular shapes of half full weight and function values L or periods of modular shapes of full weight. With C. Moeglin, he demonstrated Jacquet's conjecture describing the discrete spectrum of the GL(n) groups.[^o5] Other works are devoted to orbital integrals on p-adic groups: unipotent orbital integrals, proof of the conjecture of Langlands-Shelstad transfer conditional on the "fundamental lemma" (which was later proved by Ngo-Bao-Chau[^o6]). J.-L. Waldspurger proved the Gross-Prasad conjecture for SO(N) groups on a p-adic field. With C. Moeglin, he wrote two large volumes establishing the stable trace formula for twisted spaces.[^o7]

Some recent publications are available on its website.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

He won the Mergier–Bourdeix Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1996. He was awarded the 2009 [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) for his results in [*p*-adic harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/291327). He was elected as a member of [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2017.[^o10]
