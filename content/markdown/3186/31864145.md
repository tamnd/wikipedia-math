# Principal subalgebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Principal subalgebra"}

In mathematics, a **principal subalgebra** of a [complex simple Lie algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_simple_Lie_algebra) is a 3-dimensional simple subalgebra whose non-zero elements are [regular](wikipedia://en/31861096).

:::

A finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra has a unique [conjugacy class](wikipedia://en/49176) of principal subalgebras, each of which is the span of an [sl<sub>2</sub>-triple](wikipedia://en/14994282).
