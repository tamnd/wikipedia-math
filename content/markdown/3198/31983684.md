::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 24-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **truncated 24-cell honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can be seen as a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422), containing [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) and [truncated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_24-cell) cells.

:::

It has a uniform [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117), called the [snub 24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/31806890). It is a snub from the ${\tilde{D}}_4$ construction. This truncated 24-cell has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t{3<sup>1,1,1,1</sup>}, and its [snub](wikipedia://en/9428917) is represented as s{3<sup>1,1,1,1</sup>}.

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated icositetrachoric tetracomb
- Truncated icositetrachoric honeycomb
- Cantitruncated 16-cell honeycomb
- Bicantitruncated tesseractic honeycomb

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are five different symmetry constructions of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by different arrangements of colored [truncated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_24-cell) facets. In all cases, four truncated 24-cells, and one [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) meet at each vertex, but the vertex figures have different symmetry generators.
