# Eutactic lattice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eutactic lattice"}

In mathematics, a **eutactic lattice** (or **eutactic form**) is a [lattice](wikipedia://en/469246) in [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) whose minimal vectors form a [eutactic star](wikipedia://en/24141211). This means they have a set of positive **eutactic coefficients** *c*<sub>*i*</sub> such that (*v*, *v*) = Σ*c*<sub>*i*</sub>(*v*, *m*<sub>*i*</sub>)<sup>2</sup> where the sum is over the minimal vectors *m*<sub>*i*</sub>. "Eutactic" is derived from the Greek language, and means "well-situated" or "well-arranged".

:::

(Voronoi 1908) proved that a lattice is extreme if and only if it is both [perfect](wikipedia://en/31985822) and eutactic.

(Conway Sloane 1988) summarize the properties of eutactic lattices of dimension up to 7.
