# Wrapped exponential distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wrapped exponential distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [directional statistics](wikipedia://en/1444970), a **wrapped exponential distribution** is a [wrapped probability distribution](wikipedia://en/25329528) that results from the "wrapping" of the [exponential distribution](wikipedia://en/45906) around the [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) of the wrapped exponential distribution is[^o4]

$$f_\text{WE}(\theta;\lambda)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty \lambda e^{-\lambda (\theta+2 \pi k)}=\frac{\lambda e^{-\lambda \theta}}{1-e^{-2\pi \lambda}} ,$$

for $0 \le \theta < 2\pi$ where $\lambda > 0$ is the rate parameter of the unwrapped distribution. This is identical to the [truncated distribution](wikipedia://en/7179738) obtained by restricting observed values *X* from the [exponential distribution](wikipedia://en/45906) with rate parameter *λ* to the range $0\le X < 2\pi$. Note that this distribution is not periodic.

:::

## Characteristic function {#Characteristic_function}

The [characteristic function](wikipedia://en/3229401) of the wrapped exponential is just the characteristic function of the exponential function evaluated at integer arguments:

$$\varphi_n(\lambda)=\frac{1}{1-in/\lambda}$$

which yields an alternate expression for the wrapped exponential PDF in terms of the circular variable *z* = *e**i*(*θ*-*m*) valid for all real *θ* and *m*:

$$\begin{aligned} f_\text{WE}(z;\lambda) & =\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{n=-\infty}^\infty \frac{z^{-n}}{1-in/\lambda}\\[10pt] & = \begin{cases} \frac{\lambda}{\pi}\,\textrm{Im}(\Phi(z,1,-i\lambda))-\frac{1}{2\pi} & \text{if }z \neq 1 \\[12pt] \frac{\lambda}{1-e^{-2\pi\lambda}} & \text{if }z=1 \end{cases} \end{aligned}$$

where $\Phi()$ is the [Lerch transcendent](wikipedia://en/1342978) function.

## Circular moments {#Circular_moments}

In terms of the circular variable $z=e^{i\theta}$ the circular moments of the wrapped exponential distribution are the characteristic function of the exponential distribution evaluated at integer arguments:

$$\langle z^n\rangle=\int_\Gamma e^{in\theta}\,f_\text{WE}(\theta;\lambda)\,d\theta = \frac{1}{1-in/\lambda} ,$$

where $\Gamma\,$ is some interval of length $2\pi$. The first moment is then the average value of *z*, also known as the mean resultant, or mean resultant vector:

$$\langle z \rangle=\frac{1}{1-i/\lambda} .$$

The mean angle is

$$\langle \theta \rangle=\mathrm{Arg}\langle z \rangle = \arctan(1/\lambda) ,$$

and the length of the mean resultant is

$$R=|\langle z \rangle| = \frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{1+\lambda^2}} .$$

and the variance is then 1 − *R*.

## Characterisation {#Characterisation}

The wrapped exponential distribution is the [maximum entropy probability distribution](wikipedia://en/1813193) for distributions restricted to the range $0\le \theta < 2\pi$ for a fixed value of the expectation $\operatorname{E}(\theta)$.[^o4]
