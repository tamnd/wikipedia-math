# Neil O'Connell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neil O'Connell"}

**Neil Michael O'Connell** is an Irish mathematician from [Shannon, County Clare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shannon,_County_Clare). He attended [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin), and was elected to scholarship in 1987.[^o3] He earned his bachelor's degree in mathematics and a gold medal in 1989 and completed an M.Sc. in 1990.[^o5] He obtained his PhD in 1993 at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley) under the supervision of [Steven Neil Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Neil_Evans).[^o4][^o5] He subsequently worked at the [Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Institute_for_Advanced_Studies),[^o6] and the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o5]

:::

He works in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), in particular [random matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_matrices). He was awarded the inaugural Itô prize in 2002 (together with Ben Hambly and James Martin),[^o7] and the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 2005.[^o8] In 2013 he was Doob Lecturer at the 36th Conference on Stochastic Processes and Their Applications, in Boulder, Colorado.[^o9] He is currently Professor at [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin).[^o5]
