# Peter J. Olver

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter J. Olver"}

**Peter John Olver** (11 January 1952, [Twickenham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twickenham)) is a British-American mathematician working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After moving to the USA in 1961, Olver obtained a bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1973 and a PhD in Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1976. His PhD thesis was entitled "*Symmetry Groups of Partial Differential Equations"* and was written under the supervision of [Garrett Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/1180916).

He worked as a [L.E. Dickson](wikipedia://en/1790247) Instructor in Mathematics at [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (1976-1978) and as a research fellow at [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (1978-1980). He then moved to [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) as assistant professor, and he became full professor in the same university in 1985. Between 1992 and 1993 he was professor at [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park).[^o6]

Olver was member of the board of directors of [Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) from 2002 to 2014.[^o7][^o8][^o9] He was elected fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013[^o10] and of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2014, for "*developing new geometric methods for differential equations leading to applications in fluid mechanics, elasticity, quantum mechanics, and image processing*."[^o11] Olver is also member of [International Society for the Interaction of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_the_Interaction_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics)[^o12] and an elected fellow of the [Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Physics).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Olver's primary research fields are [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). His main interests involve the application of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and [symmetries](wikipedia://en/28186) to the geometry of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309),[^o14][^o15][^o16] as well as well the theories of [moving frames](wikipedia://en/439082) and [Cartan's equivalence method](wikipedia://en/1841654),[^o17][^o18] [differential invariants](wikipedia://en/25412108)[^o19] and [pseudogroups](wikipedia://en/1255458).[^o20][^o21]

He has also contributed to various topics in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), including [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing) and [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision),[^o22][^o23][^o24] [wave](wikipedia://en/33691) and [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988),[^o25][^o26], [quasi-exact solvability](wikipedia://en/48720870)[^o27] and [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29).[^o28][^o29]

He has written five books and over 150 research papers in peer-reviewed journals.[^o30][^o31] In 2003, Olver was one of the top 234 most cited mathematicians in the world.[^o3][^o32] He has supervised 23 PhD students.[^o5][^o6]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Olver married Iranian mathematician [Chehrzad Shakiban](wikipedia://en/78770053) in 1976.[^o34]
