# Lawrence G. Brown

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence G. Brown"}

**Lawrence G. Brown** (February 6, 1943 – April 26, 2026) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who studies [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987).

:::

Brown studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating in 1968 with [George Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mackey) as his advisor and his thesis *On the Structure of Locally Compact Groups*. He was a professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) until his retirement.

With [Peter A. Fillmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_A._Fillmore) and [Ronald G. Douglas](wikipedia://en/14044625), he developed the [Brown-Douglas-Fillmore theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown-Douglas-Fillmore_theory) in the theory of operator algebras based on techniques of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).
