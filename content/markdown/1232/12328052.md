# Journal of Semantics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Semantics"}

The ***Journal of Semantics*** is a leading international [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) journal of [semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_semantics) of natural languages published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press). Its Managing Editors are Emmanuel Chemla ([CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS)) and Yasutada Sudo ([University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London)). The journal is available online with subscription via [Oxford Journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press#Scholarly_journals).

:::

It is one of four top journals in formal semantics, alongside [Natural Language Semantics](wikipedia://en/12327811), [Linguistics and Philosophy](wikipedia://en/22832531), and [Semantics and Pragmatics](wikipedia://en/49204907).[^o3]
