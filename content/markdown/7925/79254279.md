# Yosef Hochberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yosef Hochberg"}

**Yosef Hochberg** (יוסף הוכברג; 1945 – December 3, 2013)[^o3] was an Israeli statistician and professor of statistics at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). He is best known for the development (with [Yoav Benjamini](wikipedia://en/36596077)) of the [false discovery rate](wikipedia://en/4574271) (FDR) criterion and the [Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) procedure](wikipedia://en/4574271#Benjamini–Hochberg_procedure) for controlling the FDR rate, as well as [Hochberg's step-up procedure](wikipedia://en/4621448#Hochberg's_step-up_procedure) for controlling the [family-wise error rate](wikipedia://en/4621448).

:::

Hochberg earned his PhD at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 1974.[^o4][^o5] [Pranab K. Sen](wikipedia://en/32302045) was his doctoral advisor.

While on leave from Tel Aviv University, he visited the Statistics and Operations Research Department at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o6]

He became a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1994.[^o7] He was the seventh president of the Israeli Statistical Association.[^o5]

He died on December 3, 2013.[^o5] His daughter is Israeli-American scholar [Gil Z. Hochberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Z._Hochberg).[^o8]
