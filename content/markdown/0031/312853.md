# Totally real number field

*Image: TotallyReal.svg.* The number field **Q**(√2) sits inside **R**, and the two embeddings of the field into **C** send every element in the field to another element of **R**, hence the field is totally real.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Totally real number field"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) *F* is called **totally real** if for each [embedding](wikipedia://en/52119) of *F* into the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) the [image](wikipedia://en/579311) lies inside the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438). Equivalent conditions are that *F* is generated over **Q** by one [root](wikipedia://en/264210) of an [integer polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_polynomial) *P*, all of the roots of *P* being real; or that the [tensor product algebra](wikipedia://en/372240) of *F* with the real field, over **Q**, is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to a tensor power of **R**.

:::

For example, [quadratic fields](wikipedia://en/454781) *F* of degree 2 over **Q** are either real (and then totally real), or complex, depending on whether the [square root](wikipedia://en/29208) of a positive or negative number is adjoined to **Q**. In the case of [cubic fields](wikipedia://en/22076179), a cubic integer polynomial *P* [irreducible](wikipedia://en/188725) over **Q** will have at least one real root. If it has one real and two complex roots the corresponding cubic extension of **Q** defined by adjoining the real root will *not* be totally real, although it is a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of real numbers.

Totally real number fields play a significant special role in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705). An [abelian extension](wikipedia://en/174448) of **Q** is either totally real, or contains a totally real [subfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subfield_%28mathematics%29) over which it has degree two.

Any number field that is [Galois](wikipedia://en/457064) over the [rationals](wikipedia://en/19727024) must be either totally real or [totally imaginary](wikipedia://en/34962418).
