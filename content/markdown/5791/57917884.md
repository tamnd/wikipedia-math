# Bashar ibn Shu'aib

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bashar ibn Shu'aib"}

**Bashar** (or **Bishr**) **ben Phinehas ibn Shu'aib** was a tenth century [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

According to Hottinger (*Promptuarium*, p. 96), the [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) works of Ibn Shu'aib are often quoted by [Arabic writers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_literature). In 997, Abu 'Ali 'Isa ibn Zara'ah addressed to Ibn Shu'aib a [pamphlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamphlet) against [Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism) which seemed to be an answer to a pro-Jewish work by Ibn Shu'aib (see Ibn Abi Uṣaibi'a, *Uyun al-Anba*, ii. 236).
