# Amnestic functor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amnestic functor"}

In the mathematical field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), an **amnestic functor** *F* : *A* → *B* is a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) for which an *A*-[isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) *ƒ* is an [identity](wikipedia://en/298428) whenever *Fƒ* is an identity.

:::

An example of a functor which is *not* amnestic is the forgetful functor **Met**<sub>c</sub>→**Top** from the category of [metric spaces](wikipedia://en/20018) with [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) for morphisms to the [category of topological spaces](wikipedia://en/435051). If $d_1$ and $d_2$ are [equivalent metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivalent_metrics) on a space $X$ then $\operatorname{id}\colon(X, d_1)\to(X, d_2)$ is an isomorphism that covers the identity, but is not an identity morphism (its domain and codomain are not equal).
