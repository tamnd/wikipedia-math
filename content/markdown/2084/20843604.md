# Mean integrated squared error

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mean integrated squared error"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **mean integrated squared error (MISE)** is used in [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671). The MISE of an [estimate](wikipedia://en/10043) of an unknown [probability density](wikipedia://en/43487) is given by[^o2]

:::

$$\operatorname{E}\|f_n-f\|_2^2=\operatorname{E}\int (f_n(x)-f(x))^2 \, dx$$

where *ƒ* is the unknown density, *ƒ*<sub>*n*</sub> is its estimate based on a [sample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_%28statistics%29) of *n* [independent and identically distributed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_and_identically_distributed) random variables. Here, E denotes the [expected value](wikipedia://en/9653) with respect to that sample.

The MISE is also known as *L*<sup>2</sup> [risk function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_function).
