# Algebraic analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebraic analysis"}

**Algebraic analysis** is an area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) that deals with systems of [linear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) by using [sheaf theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheaf_theory) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) to study properties and generalizations of [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) such as [hyperfunctions](wikipedia://en/1678626) and microfunctions. Semantically, algebraic analysis is the application of algebraic operations on analytic quantities. As a research programme, it was started by the Japanese mathematician [Mikio Sato](wikipedia://en/373336) in 1959.[^n1] This can be seen as an algebraic geometrization of [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). According to [Schapira](wikipedia://en/41082949), parts of Sato's work can be regarded as a manifestation of [Grothendieck's](wikipedia://en/2042) style of mathematics within the realm of classical analysis. It derives its meaning from the fact that the differential operator is right-invertible in several function spaces.

:::

It helps in the simplification of the proofs due to an algebraic description of the problem considered.

::: {#o4 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Microfunction"}

## Microfunction {#Microfunction}

Let *M* be a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438)-[analytic manifold](wikipedia://en/3183798) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/2073470#Mathematical_definition) *n*, and let *X* be its complexification. The [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of **microlocal functions** on *M* is given as[^n2]

$$\mathcal{H}^n(\mu_M(\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes \mathcal{or}_{M/X})$$

where

- $\mu_M$ denotes the [microlocalization functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microlocalization_functor),
- $\mathcal{or}_{M/X}$ is the [relative orientation sheaf](wikipedia://en/49112440).

A microfunction can be used to define a Sato's [hyperfunction](wikipedia://en/1678626). By definition, the sheaf of [Sato's hyperfunctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sato's_hyperfunction) on *M* is the restriction of the sheaf of microfunctions to *M*, in parallel to the fact the sheaf of [real-analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478) on *M* is the restriction of the sheaf of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) on *X* to *M.*

:::
