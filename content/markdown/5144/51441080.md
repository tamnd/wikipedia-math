# Neena Gupta (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neena Gupta"}

**Neena Gupta** (born 24 November 1984[^o3]) is an [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the Statistics and Mathematics Unit of the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) (ISI), [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata).[^o4] She is known for solving [Zariski Cancellation Problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zariski_Cancellation_Problem), originally posed by [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352) in 1949. Her primary fields of interest are [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [affine algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/320468).[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Gupta graduated with honours in mathematics from [Bethune College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethune_College) in 2006. She earned her post graduation in mathematics from the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) in 2008[^o7] and subsequently, her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in 2011 with [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) as her specialization under the guidance of Amartya Kumar Dutta. The title of her dissertation was "Some results on [Laurent polynomial](wikipedia://en/61351) [fibrations](wikipedia://en/316826) and Quasi A\*-algebras".

## Career {#Career}

Gupta was a [Shyama Prasad Mukherjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shyamaprasad_Mukherjee) Research Fellow at ISI Kolkata from 2008 to 2012, and, after a visiting position at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR) Mumbai, became INSPIRE Faculty at ISI Kolkata in 2012.[^o10] She has been a professor in the Statistical and Mathematics Unit of ISI Kolkata since 2014.[^o11]

She has won the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) (2019) in the category of mathematical sciences, the highest honor in India in the field of science and technology.[^o12] In 2022 she was awarded the [ICTP Ramanujan award](wikipedia://en/13595525). She is the third woman from India who got this award (after the duo [Raman Parimala](wikipedia://en/11853200) (1987 – [Sujatha Ramdorai](wikipedia://en/31145744) (2004)).

Neena Gupta received the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) Young Scientist award in 2014.[^o13] She solved the Zariski Cancellation Problem.[^o14][^o15] Her work has also earned her the inaugural Saraswathi Cowsik Medal in 2013, awarded by the TIFR Alumni Association.[^o16]
