# Guido De Philippis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guido De Philippis"}

**Guido De Philippis** (born [August 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_16), [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985) at [Fiesole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiesole)) is an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He works on [the calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694).

:::

In 2016, he was awarded the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize), "for his outstanding contributions to the regularity of solutions of [Monge–Ampère equation](wikipedia://en/4914066) and [optimal maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_map) and for his deep work on quantitative stability inequalities for the first eigenvalue of the Laplacian and rigidity in some [isoperimetric type inequalities](wikipedia://en/326182).".[^o3] In 2018 he was awarded the [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal). In 2021, he received the ISAAC award.

De Philippis was a PhD student of [Luigi Ambrosio](wikipedia://en/25337146) and [Luis Caffarelli](wikipedia://en/2070173).[^o4]
