# Adolfo Rumbos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolfo Rumbos"}

**Adolfo J. Rumbos** is an American mathematician whose research interests include [nonlinear analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_analysis) and [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779).[^o2] He is the Joseph N. Fiske Professor of Mathematics at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College) in [Claremont, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California).[^o2]

:::

Rumbos is a graduate of [Humboldt State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_State_University).[^o2] He completed his Ph.D. in 1989 from the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) with the dissertation *Applications of the Leray-Schauder topological Degree to Boundary Value Problems for Semilinear Differential Equations* supervised by Edward M. Landesman.
