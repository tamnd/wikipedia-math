# Weddle surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weddle surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Weddle surface**, introduced by (txt, footnote on page 69), is a [quartic surface](wikipedia://en/20133775) in 3-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135), given by the locus of vertices of the family of cones passing through 6 points in [general position](wikipedia://en/383736).

:::

Weddle surfaces have 6 nodes and are [birational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birational) to [Kummer surfaces](wikipedia://en/647758).
