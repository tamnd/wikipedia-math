# Kummer variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kummer variety"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Kummer variety** of an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) is its quotient by the map taking any element to its inverse. The Kummer variety of a 2-dimensional abelian variety is called a [Kummer surface](wikipedia://en/647758).

:::
