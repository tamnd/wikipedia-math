# Elizabeth A. Stuart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elizabeth A. Stuart"}

**Elizabeth A. Stuart** is a professor of mental health, [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878), and health policy and management in the [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health). Her research involves [causal inference](wikipedia://en/37103476) and [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676) in the statistics of mental health. She was a co-author on a study showing that post-suicide-attempt counseling can significantly reduce the risk of future suicide.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Stuart graduated from [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College) in 1997, with a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) major and [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) minor.[^o5] She completed her Ph.D. at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2004, in statistics, under the supervision of [Donald Rubin](wikipedia://en/7790659).[^o5] After working for two years as a researcher at [Mathematica Policy Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica_Policy_Research), she joined the Johns Hopkins faculty in 2006.[^o5] In 2023, she was promoted to The Frank Hurley and Catharine Dorrier Professor and Chair of the Hopkins Biostatistics department.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2014 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for outstanding contributions to research in the estimation of causal effects; for extensive efforts in the dissemination of statistical methods in education and mental health, including the development of widely used software; and for extensive service to the ASA and the profession."[^o8] In 2020, she was elected Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science), in the Section on Statistics.[^o9] In 2020, she became a Bloomberg Professor of American Health.[^o10]
