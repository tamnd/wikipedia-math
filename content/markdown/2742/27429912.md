# Cartan's lemma (potential theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartan's lemma"}

In [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Cartan's lemma**, named after [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536), is a bound on the measure and complexity of the set on which a logarithmic [Newtonian potential](wikipedia://en/814816) is small.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

The following statement can be found in Levin's book.[^o4]

Let *μ* be a finite positive [Borel measure](wikipedia://en/4322) on the complex plane **C** with *μ*(**C**) = *n*. Let *u*(*z*) be the logarithmic potential of *μ*:

$$u(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_\mathbf{C} \log|z-\zeta|\,d\mu(\zeta)$$

Given *H* ∈ (0, 1), there exist discs of radii *r*<sub>*i*</sub> such that

$$\sum_i r_i < 5H$$

and

$$u(z) \ge \frac{n}{2\pi}\log \frac{H}{e}$$

for all *z* outside the union of these discs.

:::
