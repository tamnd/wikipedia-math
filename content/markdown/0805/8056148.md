# Differential ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Differential ideal"}

In the theory of [differential forms](wikipedia://en/221519), a **differential ideal** *I* is an *algebraic ideal* in the ring of smooth differential forms on a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), in other words a [graded ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graded_algebra) in the sense of [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424), that is further closed under [exterior differentiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_differentiation) *d*, meaning that for any form α in *I*, the exterior derivative *d*α is also in *I*.

:::

In the theory of [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587), a **differential ideal** *I* in a differential ring *R* is an ideal which is mapped to itself by each differential operator.

## Exterior differential systems and partial differential equations {#Exterior_differential_systems_and_partial_differential_equations}

An **exterior differential system** consists of a smooth manifold $M$ and a differential ideal

$$I\subset \Omega^*(M).$$

An **integral manifold** of an exterior differential system $(M,I)$ consists of a [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) $N\subset M$ having the property that the pullback to $N$ of all differential forms contained in $I$ vanishes identically.

One can express any [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) system as an exterior differential system with independence condition. Suppose that we have a *k*th order partial differential equation system for maps $u: \mathbb{R}^m \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$, given by

$$F^r\left(x, u, \frac{\partial^{|I|}u }{\partial x^I}\right)=0, \quad 1\le |I|\le k.$$

The graph of the $k$-jet $(u^a,p^a_i,\dots,p^a_I)=(u^a(x),\frac{\partial u^a}{\partial x^i},\dots,\frac{\partial^{|I|}u }{\partial x^I})_{1\le |I|\le k}$ of any solution of this partial differential equation system is a submanifold $N$ of the [jet space](wikipedia://en/1521924), and is an integral manifold of the [contact system](wikipedia://en/928060) $du^a-p^a_i dx^i,\dots,dp^a_I-p^p_{Ij} dx^j{}_{1\le |I|\le k-1}$on the $k$-jet bundle.

This idea allows one to analyze the properties of partial differential equations with methods of differential geometry. For instance, we can apply the [Cartan–Kähler theorem](wikipedia://en/1968544) to a system of partial differential equations by writing down the associated exterior differential system. We can frequently apply [Cartan's equivalence method](wikipedia://en/1841654) to exterior differential systems to study their symmetries and their diffeomorphism invariants.

## Perfect differential ideals {#Perfect_differential_ideals}

A differential ideal $I \,$ is perfect if it has the property that if it contains an element $a \in I$ then it contains any element $b \in I$ such that $b^n = a$ for some $n > 0 \,$. In other words, perfect differential ideals are [radical](wikipedia://en/252009) differential ideals.
