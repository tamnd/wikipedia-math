# Monothetic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monothetic group"}

In mathematics, a **monothetic group** is a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) with a [dense](wikipedia://en/23523594) [cyclic subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_subgroup). They were introduced by (txt). An example is the additive group of [p-adic integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_integers), in which the integers are dense.

:::

A monothetic group is necessarily [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974).
