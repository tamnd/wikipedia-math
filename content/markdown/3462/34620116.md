# Syamadas Mukhopadhyaya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Syamadas Mukhopadhyaya"}

**Syamadas Mukhopadhyaya** (22 June 1866 – 8 May 1937) was an [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced the [four-vertex theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-vertex_theorem) and [Mukhopadhyaya's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukhopadhyaya's_theorem) in [plane geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_geometry).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Syamadas Mukhopadhyaya was born at [Haripal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haripal_%28community_development_block%29), [Hooghly district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooghly_district), in [Bengal Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency), [British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_India). He graduated from [Hooghly College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooghly_Mohsin_College), received his M.A. degree from [Presidency College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_University,_Kolkata) in [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta), and his Ph.D. degree from [Calcutta University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_University) in 1910. He also took classes from the [Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Association_for_the_Cultivation_of_Science).[^o4]

Mukhopadhyaya was appointed by [Asutosh Mookerjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashutosh_Mukherjee) as professor of mathematics in the [Rajabazar Science College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajabazar_Science_College), [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta).[^o5] [Jacques Hadamard](wikipedia://en/312675) communicated with Mukhopadyaya about the latter's work on the geometry of a plane arc[^o6] and [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998)'s book on geometry had a reference to Mukhopadhyaya.[^o7]

He worked at [Bangabasi College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangabasi_College) and then at [Bethune College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethune_College) in Calcutta, where he lectured in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [English Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Literature), and [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy). In 1932, he was elected president of the [Calcutta Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_Mathematical_Society). He served in this capacity until his death from [heart failure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_failure) in 1937. Mukhopadhyaya went to Europe on a Ghose Travelling Fellowship in 1933 to study methods of education. He gave lectures in Paris University.[^o8]
