# Amel Ben Abda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amel Ben Abda"}

**Amel Ben Abda** is a professor of mathematics at the [National Engineering School of Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Engineering_School_of_Tunis). She was the first to earn a PhD in applied mathematics in Tunisia. She is the Tunisian representative of the steering committee of the *International Laboratory for Computer Sciences and Applied Mathematics* on the advisory board of the *Tunisian Woman Mathematician Association.*

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ben Abda studied [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [National Engineering School of Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Engineering_School_of_Tunis), graduating in 1988.[^o4] After her degree, she worked in the [Preparatory Institute for Engineering Studies of Nabeul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preparatory_Institute_for_Engineering_Studies_of_Nabeul).[^o5] She went on to earn her Master's Degree in Applied Mechanics in 1988 where she also had got her PhD in Applied Mathematics in 1993.[^o6][^o7][^o8]. With that she also had an impact on the inception of the country's applied mathematics doctoral program.[^o9] After going on to defend her habilitation in applied mathematics at the National Engineering School of Tunis in 1998. [^o6]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

In the field of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), Ben Abda has worked on a "reciprocity gap" method that can be used as an indication of defects in materials.[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14] She also works on the problem of reconstructing [boundary conditions](wikipedia://en/436779) from incomplete data.[^o15]

In 1993, Ben Abda joined the Preparatory Institute for Scientific and Technical Studies, where she was promoted to assistant professor that same year.[^o5] In 1999 she joined the [National Engineering School of Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Engineering_School_of_Tunis).[^o5] When she defended Tunisia's first [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) it was in 1998.[^o4] She is responsible for the inverse theorems team at the Laboratory of Mathematical Modelling and Numeric in Engineering Sciences (LAMSIN).[^o16]

She is the Tunisian representative of the steering committee of the *International Laboratory for Computer Sciences and Applied Mathematics.*[^o7] She is on the advisory board of the *Tunisian Woman Mathematician Association* (TWMA).[^o17][^o18] The TWMA give an annual award for the best PhD thesis in mathematics.[^o19]

In 1999, she published the article "Reciprocity principle and crack identification"[^o20] with Stéphane Andrieux and Huy Duong Bui, later referring its work as here most influential and impactful research she has done on real world problems.[^o21]

In 2018 she was selected as one of [OkayAfrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OkayAfrica)'s Top 100 Women.[^o7]

In 2016 she received a trophy at their annual meeting, and the trophy was for her impact on Tunisian Women that are also in the field of mathematics.[^o8]

She is a prominent advocate to the Tunisian Women in Mathematics Association, she was actively a part of broader to helping with empowering other women who are also in STEM all across the continent.[^o22]
