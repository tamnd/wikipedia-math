# Tarski Lectures

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tarski Lectures"}

The **Alfred Tarski Lectures** are an annual distinction in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and series of lectures held at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). Established in tribute to [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920) on the fifth anniversary of his death, the award has been given every year since 1989.[^o2][^o3] Following a 2-year hiatus after the 2020 lecture was not given due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), the lectures resumed in 2023.[^o4]

:::
