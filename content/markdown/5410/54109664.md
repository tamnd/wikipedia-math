# Liouville space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Liouville space"}

In the [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), **Liouville space**, also known as **line space**, is the space of operators on [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932). Liouville space is itself a Hilbert space under the [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert-Schmidt_inner_product).[^o3][^o2]

:::

Abstractly, Liouville space is equivalent ([isometrically isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometric_isomorphism)) to the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of a Hilbert space with its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual).[^o3][^o4] A common computational technique to organize computations in Liouville space is [vectorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vectorization_%28math%29).[^o2]

Liouville space underlies the [density operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density_operator) formalism and is a common computation technique in the study of [open quantum systems](wikipedia://en/1079106).[^o2][^o4]
