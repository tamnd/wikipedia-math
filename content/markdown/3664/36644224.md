# Cohen ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohen ring"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **Cohen ring** is a field or a complete [discrete valuation ring](wikipedia://en/848633) of [mixed characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_characteristic) $(0, p)$ whose [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164) is generated by *p*. Cohen rings are used in the [Cohen structure theorem](wikipedia://en/27443678) for [complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_ring) [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391).

:::
