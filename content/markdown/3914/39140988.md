# Minkowski space (number field)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minkowski space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically the field of [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), a **Minkowski space** is a [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) associated with an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822).[^o2]

:::

If *K* is a number field of [degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_of_a_number_field) *d* then there are *d* distinct embeddings of *K* into **C**. We let *K*<sub>**C**</sub> be the image of *K* in the product **C**<sup>*d*</sup>, considered as equipped with the usual [Hermitian inner product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_inner_product). If *c* denotes [complex conjugation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_conjugation), let *K*<sub>**R**</sub> denote the subspace of *K*<sub>**C**</sub> fixed by *c*, equipped with a [scalar product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_product). This is the Minkowski space of *K*.
