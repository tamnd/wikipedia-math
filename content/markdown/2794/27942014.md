# Theodor Reye

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodor Reye"}

*Image: Theodor Reye.jpeg.* Theodor Reye

**Karl Theodor Reye** (born 20 June 1838 in [Ritzebüttel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuxhaven), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and died 2 July 1919 in [Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Würzburg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He contributed to geometry, particularly [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) and [synthetic geometry](wikipedia://en/267484). He is best known for his introduction of [configurations](wikipedia://en/7200539) in the second edition of his book, *Geometrie der Lage* (Geometry of Position, 1876).[^o2] The [Reye configuration](wikipedia://en/35301443) of 12 points, 12 planes, and 16 lines is named after him.

:::

Reye also developed a novel solution to the following three-dimensional extension of the [problem of Apollonius](wikipedia://en/2642185): Construct all possible spheres that are simultaneously tangent to four given spheres.[^o3]

## Life {#Life}

Reye obtained his Ph.D. from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1861. His dissertation was entitled "Die mechanische Wärme-Theorie und das Spannungsgesetz der Gase" (The mechanical theory of heat and the potential law of gases).

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

Reye worked on [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673), [quadrics](wikipedia://en/145570) and [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849).

Reye's work on linear manifolds of projective plane pencils and of bundles on spheres influenced later work by [Corrado Segre](wikipedia://en/2422061) on manifolds. He introduced [Reye congruences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reye_congruence), the earliest examples of [Enriques surfaces](wikipedia://en/3094760).
