# Eells–Kuiper manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eells–Kuiper manifold"}

In mathematics, an **Eells–Kuiper manifold** is a [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) of $\mathbb{R}^n$ by a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) of dimension $n/2$, where $n=2,4,8$, or $16$. It is named after [James Eells](wikipedia://en/37329912) and [Nicolaas Kuiper](wikipedia://en/3344929).

:::

If $n=2$, the Eells–Kuiper manifold is [diffeomorphic](wikipedia://en/8564) to the [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325) $\mathbb{RP}^2$. For $n\ge 4$ it is [simply-connected](wikipedia://en/523879) and has the integral cohomology structure of the [complex projective plane](wikipedia://en/737155) $\mathbb{CP}^2$ ($n = 4$), of the [quaternionic projective plane](wikipedia://en/2808931) $\mathbb{HP}^2$ ($n = 8$) or of the [Cayley projective plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayley_projective_plane) ($n = 16$).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

These manifolds are important in both [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560) and [foliation theory](wikipedia://en/371227):

Theorem:[^o4] *Let $M$ be a [connected](wikipedia://en/308815) [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) (not necessarily [orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientable)) of dimension $n$. Suppose $M$ admits a [Morse function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function) $f\colon M\to \mathbb{R}$ of class $C^3$ with exactly three [singular points](wikipedia://en/1951424). Then $M$ is a Eells–Kuiper manifold.*

Theorem:[^o5] *Let $M^n$ be a compact connected manifold and $F$ a [Morse foliation](wikipedia://en/26558892#Morse_foliation) on $M$. Suppose the number of [centers](wikipedia://en/26558892#Morse_foliation) $c$ of the foliation $F$ is more than the number of [saddles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle) $s$. Then there are two possibilities:*

- *$c=s+2$, and $M^n$ is homeomorphic to the sphere $S^n$*,
- *$c=s+1$, and $M^n$ is an Eells–Kuiper manifold, $n=2,4,8$ or $16$.*

:::
