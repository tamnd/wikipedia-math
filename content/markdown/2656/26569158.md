# Hurwitz determinant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hurwitz determinant"}

In mathematics, **Hurwitz determinants** were introduced by (txt), who used them to give a criterion for all roots of a polynomial to have negative real part.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Consider a [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) *P* in the variable *λ* of the form:

$$P(\lambda)= a_0 \lambda^n + a_1 \lambda^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{n-1} \lambda + a_n$$

where $a_i$, $i=0,1,\ldots,n$, are real.

The square [Hurwitz matrix](wikipedia://en/78292145) associated to *P* is given below:

$$H= \begin{pmatrix} a_1 & a_3 & a_5 & \dots & \dots & \dots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ a_0 & a_2 & a_4 & & & & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & a_1 & a_3 & & & & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ \vdots & a_0 & a_2 & \ddots & & & 0 & \vdots & \vdots \\ \vdots & 0 & a_1 & & \ddots & & a_n & \vdots & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & a_0 & & & \ddots & a_{n-1} & 0 & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & 0 & & & & a_{n-2} & a_n & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & & & a_{n-3} & a_{n-1} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & \dots & \dots & a_{n-4} & a_{n-2} & a_n \end{pmatrix}.$$

The *i-*th *Hurwitz determinant* is the *i-*th [leading principal minor](wikipedia://en/262107#Other_applications) (minor is a determinant) of the above Hurwitz matrix *H*. There are *n* Hurwitz determinants for a characteristic polynomial of degree *n*.

:::
