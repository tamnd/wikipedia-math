# David Dickey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Dickey"}

**David Alan Dickey** (born c. 1945) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who has specialised in [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis). He is a William Neal Reynolds Professor[^o3] in the Department of Statistics at [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University). The [Dickey–Fuller test](wikipedia://en/2473516) is named for him and [Wayne Arthur Fuller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Arthur_Fuller). David Dickey is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher) by the ISI Highly Cited Database of the [ISI Web of Knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_Web_of_Knowledge).[^o4] He is an elected Fellow (2000) of the American Statistical Association.[^o5] He is from [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio).

:::
