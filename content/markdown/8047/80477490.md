# Luen-Fai Tam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luen-Fai Tam"}

**Luen-Fai Tam** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 譚聯輝, 1947 – 20 November 2025) was a mathematician working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), and [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101). He was a [research professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_professor) at the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Tam obtained his PhD in 1984 at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), advised by [Robert S. Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_S._Finn) with a thesis on the behavior of [capillary surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capillary_surface) in [low gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_gravity).[^o3] After completing his doctoral studies, Tam was appointed as a research [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) from 1984 to 1986, where his research shifted towards geometric aspects of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). In 1986, he joined the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) as a [tenure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenure)-track assistant professor. From 1987 to 1990, he was a lecturer at the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong) (CUHK). He then moved to the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in 1990 and was promoted to a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) there from 1992 to 1997. Tam returned to CUHK in 1997 as a senior lecturer until his retirement in 2007. Following his retirement, Tam was appointed as a [research professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_professor) at CUHK and advised five doctoral students.[^o6]

Tam had published 96 mathematical research articles with over 1,300 citations by 546 mathematicians. His research included [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), [harmonic functions](wikipedia://en/55762), [harmonic maps](wikipedia://en/4577484) on [complete](wikipedia://en/6875557) [noncompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_manifold) [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652), [mathematical relativity](wikipedia://en/1789646), and complex [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538). According to [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683), he had collaborated with 29 mathematicians, including [Peter Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Li_%28mathematician%29) at UC Irvine (Tam's postdoctoral mentor while at Purdue) and [Yuguang Shi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuguang_Shi) at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o6]

In honor of Tam's birthday and mathematical contributions, a conference on geometric analysis and mathematical relativity was held at [Fujian Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fujian_Normal_University) in December 2018.[^o6]
