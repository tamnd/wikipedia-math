# Ludmila Bourchtein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ludmila Bourchtein"}

**Ludmila Bourchtein** (also transliterated as Lioudmila Bourchtein) is a Soviet and Brazilian mathematician and textbook author, a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) at the [Federal University of Pelotas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Pelotas) in Brazil. Her research concerns [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [conformal mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_mapping).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bourchtein received a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [Far Eastern Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_Eastern_Federal_University) in [Vladivostok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladivostok) in 1960, and continued to work at Far Eastern Federal University for 34 years as an assistant, associate, and full professor. Meanwhile, she earned a doctorate through [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) in 1968, with a dissertation on the solution of [functional equations](wikipedia://en/364377) for [complex analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478) on the [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071).

She joined the Federal University of Pelotas in Brazil in 1997, as an associate professor and later as a full professor, retired in 2010 and was named a professor emerita in 2019. At the Federal University of Pelotas, one of her students was [Adriana Neumann de Oliveira](wikipedia://en/59712907), who credits Bourchtein as being her first female role model in mathematics.

## Books {#Books}

With Andrei Bourchtein (her son), Ludmila Bourchtein is the coauthor of books, including:

- *CounterExamples: From Elementary Calculus to the Beginnings of Analysis* (CRC Press, 2015)
- *Counterexamples on Uniform Convergence: Sequences, Series, Functions, and Integrals* (Wiley, 2017)
- *Complex Analysis* (Springer, 2021)
- *Theory of Infinite Sequences and Series* (Springer, 2022)
- *Elementary Functions* (Birkhäuser, 2023)
- *Ordinary Differential Equations: Exercises and Problems* (College Publications, 2024)
