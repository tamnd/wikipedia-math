# David Preiss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Preiss"}

**David Preiss** FRS (born 21 January 1947) is a Czech and British mathematician, specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick)[^o2]

:::

Preiss is a recipient of the [Ostrowski Prize](wikipedia://en/3159411) (2011) and the winner of the 2008 [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28LMS%29)[^o3] for his 1987 result on *Geometry of Measures*, where he solved the remaining problem in the geometric theoretic structure of sets and [measures](wikipedia://en/19873) in [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697).[^o4] He was an invited speaker at the ICM 1990 in Kyoto.[^o5] He is a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (2004)[^o6] and a Foreign Fellow of the [Learned Society of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_the_Czech_Republic) (2003).[^o7]

He is associate editor of the mathematical journal *[Real Analysis Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Analysis_Exchange)*.[^o8]
