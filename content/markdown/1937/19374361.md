# Timeline of calculus and mathematical analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of calculus and mathematical analysis"}

A timeline of **[calculus](wikipedia://en/5176)** and **[mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396)**.

:::

## 500BC to 1600 {#500BC_to_1600}

- 5th century BC - The [Zeno's paradoxes](wikipedia://en/34535),
- 5th century BC - [Antiphon](wikipedia://en/507012) attempts to [square the circle](wikipedia://en/201359),
- 5th century BC - [Democritus](wikipedia://en/8211) finds the [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of [cone](wikipedia://en/782427) is 1/3 of volume of [cylinder](wikipedia://en/859283),
- 4th century BC - [Eudoxus of Cnidus](wikipedia://en/48388) develops the [method of exhaustion](wikipedia://en/1721284),
- 3rd century BC - [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844) displays [geometric series](wikipedia://en/12630) in *[The Quadrature of the Parabola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Quadrature_of_the_Parabola).* He further develops the method of exhaustion.[^o3]
- 3rd century BC - [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844) develops a concept of the indivisibles—a precursor to [infinitesimals](wikipedia://en/160990)—allowing him to solve several problems using methods now termed as integral [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176). Archimedes also derives several [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for determining the [area](wikipedia://en/1209) and volume of various solids including [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859), [cone](wikipedia://en/782427), [paraboloid](wikipedia://en/145845) and [hyperboloid](wikipedia://en/145844).[^o4]
- Before 50 BC - Babylonian cuneiform tablets show use of the [Trapezoid rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trapezoid_rule) to calculate of the position of Jupiter.[^o5]
- 3rd century - [Liu Hui](wikipedia://en/202719) rediscovers the method of exhaustion in order to find the [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220).
- 4th century - The [Pappus's centroid theorem](wikipedia://en/509331),
- 5th century - [Zu Chongzhi](wikipedia://en/151762) established a method that would later be called [Cavalieri's principle](wikipedia://en/21697672) to find the volume of a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859).
- 600 - Liu Zhuo is the first person to use second-order interpolation for computing the positions of the sun and the moon.[^o6]
- 665 - [Brahmagupta](wikipedia://en/297206) discovers a second order Newton-Stirling interpolation for $\sin (x+\epsilon)$,
- 862 - The [Banu Musa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banu_Musa) brothers write the *"Book on the Measurement of Plane and Spherical Figures"*,
- 9th century - [Thābit ibn Qurra](wikipedia://en/207547) discusses the quadrature of the [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231) and the volume of different types of [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673).[^o7]
- 12th century - [Bhāskara II](wikipedia://en/480509) discovers a rule equivalent to [Rolle's theorem](wikipedia://en/200305) for $\sin x$,
- 14th century - [Nicole Oresme](wikipedia://en/593659) proves of the [divergence](wikipedia://en/876428) of the [harmonic series](wikipedia://en/142488),
- 14th century - [Madhava](wikipedia://en/2793007) discovers the [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) expansion for $\sin x$, $\cos x$, $\arctan x$ and $\pi/4$ [^o8][^o9] This theory is now well known in the Western world as the [Taylor series](wikipedia://en/30448) or infinite series.[^o10]
- 14th century - [Parameshvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parameshvara) discovers a third order Taylor interpolation for $\sin (x+\epsilon)$,
- 1445 - [Nicholas of Cusa](wikipedia://en/282522) attempts to square the circle,
- 1501 - [Nilakantha Somayaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilakantha_Somayaji) writes the *[Tantrasamgraha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantrasamgraha)*, which contains the Madhava's discoveries,
- 1548 - [Francesco Maurolico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Maurolico) attempted to calculate the [barycenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycenter) of various bodies (pyramid, paraboloid, etc.),
- 1550 - [Jyeshtadeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyeshtadeva) writes the *[Yuktibhāṣā](wikipedia://en/5864214)*, a commentary to Nilakantha's *Tantrasamgraha*,
- 1560 - [Sankara Variar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankara_Variar) writes the *[Kriyakramakari](wikipedia://en/31085465)*,
- 1565 - [Federico Commandino](wikipedia://en/2828269) publishes *De centro Gravitati*,
- 1588 - [Commandino](wikipedia://en/2828269)'s translation of [Pappus](wikipedia://en/509300)' *Collectio* gets published,
- 1593 - [François Viète](wikipedia://en/310238) discovers the [first infinite product](wikipedia://en/1609504) in the history of mathematics,

## 17th century {#17th_century}

- 1606 - [Luca Valerio](wikipedia://en/22538720) applies methods of Archimedes to find volumes and [centres of gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_of_gravity) of solid bodies,
- 1609 - [Johannes Kepler](wikipedia://en/15736) computes the integral $\int_0^\theta \sin x\ dx = 1-\cos \theta$,
- 1611 - [Thomas Harriot](wikipedia://en/780445) discovers an interpolation formula similar to [Newton's interpolation formula](wikipedia://en/216333),
- 1615 - Johannes Kepler publishes *Nova stereometria doliorum*,
- 1620 - [Grégoire de Saint-Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grégoire_de_Saint-Vincent) discovers that the area under a [hyperbola](wikipedia://en/14052) represented a [logarithm](wikipedia://en/17860),
- 1624 - [Henry Briggs](wikipedia://en/210399) publishes *Arithmetica Logarithmica*,
- 1629 - [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966) discovers his [method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_theorem_%28stationary_points%29) of maxima and minima, precursor of the [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) concept,
- 1634 - [Gilles de Roberval](wikipedia://en/196880) shows that the area under a [cycloid](wikipedia://en/41638) is three times the area of its generating circle,
- 1635 - [Bonaventura Cavalieri](wikipedia://en/412561) publishes *Geometria Indivisibilibus*,
- 1637 - [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525) publishes *[La Géométrie](wikipedia://en/239981)*,
- 1638 - [Galileo Galilei](wikipedia://en/29688374) publishes *[Two New Sciences](wikipedia://en/268475)*,
- 1644 - [Evangelista Torricelli](wikipedia://en/210413) publishes *Opera geometrica*,
- 1644 - [Fermat's methods of maxima and minima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_theorem_%28stationary_points%29) published by [Pierre Hérigone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Hérigone),
- 1647 - Cavalieri computes the [integral](wikipedia://en/30839740) $\int_0^a x^{n}\ dx=\frac{1}{n+1}a^{n+1}$,
- 1647 - [Grégoire de Saint-Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grégoire_de_Saint-Vincent) publishes *Opus Geometricum*,
- 1650 - [Pietro Mengoli](wikipedia://en/11335853) proves of the divergence of the harmonic series,
- 1654 - [Johannes Hudde](wikipedia://en/1365598) discovers the [power series](wikipedia://en/3995885) expansion for $\ln(1+x)$,
- 1656 - [John Wallis](wikipedia://en/239290) publishes *Arithmetica Infinitorum*,
- 1658 - [Christopher Wren](wikipedia://en/52946) shows that the length of a [cycloid](wikipedia://en/41638) is four times the diameter of its generating circle,
- 1659 - Second edition of [Van Schooten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Schooten)'s Latin translation of Descartes' Geometry with appendices by [Hudde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudde) and [Heuraet](wikipedia://en/6022646),
- 1665 - [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627) discovers the generalized [binomial theorem](wikipedia://en/4677) and develops his version of [infinitesimal calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitesimal_calculus),
- 1667 - James Gregory publishes *Vera circuli et hyperbolae quadratura*,
- 1668 - [Nicholas Mercator](wikipedia://en/1486120) publishes *Logarithmotechnia*,
- 1668 - [James Gregory](wikipedia://en/220425) computes the [integral of the secant function](wikipedia://en/31981806),
- 1669 - Newton invents a [Newton's method](wikipedia://en/22145) for the computation of [roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_function) of a function,
- 1670 - Newton rediscovers the power series expansion for $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ (originally discovered by [Madhava](wikipedia://en/2793007)),
- 1670 - [Isaac Barrow](wikipedia://en/97805) publishes *Lectiones Geometricae*,
- 1671 - James Gregory rediscovers the power series expansion for $\arctan x$ and $\pi/4$ (originally discovered by [Madhava](wikipedia://en/2793007)),
- 1672 - [René-François de Sluse](wikipedia://en/2680203) publishes *A Method of Drawing Tangents to All Geometrical Curves*,
- 1673 - [Gottfried Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Leibniz) also develops his version of [infinitesimal calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitesimal_calculus),
- 1675 - Leibniz uses the modern notation for an integral for the first time,
- 1677 - Leibniz discovers the rules for differentiating [products](wikipedia://en/248988), [quotients](wikipedia://en/209128), and the [function of a function](wikipedia://en/6113).
- 1683 - [Jacob Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64740) discovers the number $e$,
- 1684 - Leibniz publishes his first paper on calculus,
- 1685 - Newon formulates and solves [Newton's minimal resistance problem](wikipedia://en/54492474), giving birth to the field of [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882),
- 1686 - The first appearance in print of the $\int$ notation for integrals,
- 1687 - Isaac Newton publishes *[Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/48781)*,
- 1691 - The first proof of [Rolle's theorem](wikipedia://en/200305) is given by [Michel Rolle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Rolle),
- 1691 - Leibniz discovers the technique of [separation of variables](wikipedia://en/387750) for [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753),
- 1694 - [Johann Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/268886) discovers the [L'Hôpital's rule](wikipedia://en/18531),
- 1696 - [Guillaume de L'Hôpital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillaume_François_Antoine,_Marquis_de_l'Hôpital) publishes *[Analyse des Infiniment Petits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analyse_des_Infiniment_Petits_pour_l'Intelligence_des_Lignes_Courbes)*, the first calculus textbook,
- 1696 - Jakob Bernoulli and Johann Bernoulli solve the [brachistochrone problem](wikipedia://en/171879).

## 18th century {#18th_century}

- 1711 - Isaac Newton publishes *[De analysi per aequationes numero terminorum infinitas](wikipedia://en/34514568)*,
- 1712 - [Brook Taylor](wikipedia://en/210429) develops [Taylor series](wikipedia://en/30448),
- 1722 - [Roger Cotes](wikipedia://en/236798) computes the [derivative of sine function](wikipedia://en/13146531) in his *Harmonia Mensurarum*,
- 1730 - [James Stirling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Stirling_%28mathematician%29) publishes *The Differential Method*,
- 1734 - [George Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Berkeley) publishes *[The Analyst](wikipedia://en/341394)*,
- 1734 - [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) introduces the [integrating factor](wikipedia://en/1912874) technique for solving first-order ordinary differential equations,
- 1735 - Leonhard Euler solves the [Basel problem](wikipedia://en/490303), relating an [infinite series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_series) to π,
- 1736 - Newton's [Method of Fluxions](wikipedia://en/252953) posthumously published,
- 1737 - [Thomas Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Simpson) publishes *Treatise of Fluxions*,
- 1739 - Leonhard Euler solves the general homogeneous [linear ordinary differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_ordinary_differential_equation) with [constant coefficients](wikipedia://en/379868#Basic_terminology),
- 1742 - Modern definion of logarithm by [William Gardiner](wikipedia://en/49461780),
- 1742 - [Colin Maclaurin](wikipedia://en/7824) publishes *Treatise on Fluxions*,
- 1748 - Euler publishes *[Introductio in analysin infinitorum](wikipedia://en/19777721)*,
- 1748 - [Maria Gaetana Agnesi](wikipedia://en/42842830) discusses analysis in *Instituzioni Analitiche ad Uso della Gioventu Italiana*,
- 1762 - [Joseph Louis Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Louis_Lagrange) discovers the [divergence theorem](wikipedia://en/151864),
- 1797 - Lagrange publishes *Théorie des fonctions analytiques*,

## 19th century {#19th_century}

- 1807 - [Joseph Fourier](wikipedia://en/25767401) announces his discoveries about the [trigonometric decomposition of functions](wikipedia://en/59038),
- 1811 - Carl Friedrich Gauss discusses the meaning of [integrals with complex limits](wikipedia://en/704359) and briefly examines the dependence of such integrals on the chosen path of integration,
- 1815 - [Siméon Denis Poisson](wikipedia://en/44670) carries out integrations along paths in the complex plane,
- 1817 - [Bernard Bolzano](wikipedia://en/302185) presents the [intermediate value theorem](wikipedia://en/14884) — a [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122) which is negative at one point and positive at another point must be zero for at least one point in between,
- 1822 - [Augustin-Louis Cauchy](wikipedia://en/1842) presents the [Cauchy integral theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_integral_theorem) for integration around the boundary of a rectangle in the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628),
- 1825 - Augustin-Louis Cauchy presents the [Cauchy integral theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_integral_theorem) for general integration paths—he assumes the function being integrated has a [continuous derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuously_differentiable), and he introduces the theory of [residues](wikipedia://en/73102) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759),
- 1825 - [André-Marie Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André-Marie_Ampère) discovers [Stokes' theorem](wikipedia://en/25441497),
- 1828 - [George Green](wikipedia://en/243343) introduces [Green's theorem](wikipedia://en/243334),
- 1831 - [Mikhail Vasilievich Ostrogradsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Vasilievich_Ostrogradsky) rediscovers and gives the first proof of the divergence theorem earlier described by Lagrange, Gauss and Green,
- 1841 - [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) discovers but does not publish the [Laurent expansion theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_expansion_theorem),
- 1843 - [Pierre-Alphonse Laurent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-Alphonse_Laurent) discovers and presents the Laurent expansion theorem,
- 1850 - [Victor Alexandre Puiseux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Alexandre_Puiseux) distinguishes between [poles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poles_and_zeros) and branch points and introduces the concept of [essential singular points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_singularity),
- 1850 - [George Gabriel Stokes](wikipedia://en/212838) rediscovers and proves [Stokes' theorem](wikipedia://en/25441497),
- 1861 - Karl Weierstrass starts to use the language of epsilons and deltas,
- 1873 - [Georg Frobenius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Frobenius) presents his method for finding series solutions to linear differential equations with [regular singular points](wikipedia://en/3166061),

## 20th century {#20th_century}

- 1908 - [Josip Plemelj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josip_Plemelj) solves the Riemann problem about the existence of a differential equation with a given [monodromic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monodromic_group) and uses Sokhotsky - Plemelj formulae,
- 1966 - [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024) presents [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis).
- 1985 - [Louis de Branges de Bourcia](wikipedia://en/79126) proves the [Bieberbach conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieberbach_conjecture),
