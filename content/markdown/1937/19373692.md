# Timeline of probability and statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of probability and statistics"}

The following is a timeline of **probability** and **statistics**.

:::

## Before 1600 {#Before_1600}

- 8th century – [Al-Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Khalil_ibn_Ahmad_al-Farahidi), an [Arab mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_in_medieval_Islam) studying [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology), wrote the *Book of Cryptographic Messages*. The work has been lost, but based on the reports of later authors, it contained the first use of permutations and combinations to list all possible [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) words with and without vowels.[^o3]
- 9th century - [Al-Kindi](wikipedia://en/272065) was the first to use [frequency analysis](wikipedia://en/157934) to decipher [encrypted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encrypted) messages and developed the first [code breaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_breaking) algorithm. He wrote a book entitled *Manuscript on Deciphering Cryptographic Messages*, containing detailed discussions on statistics and [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715).[^o4][^o5][^o6] Al-Kindi also made the earliest known use of [statistical inference](wikipedia://en/27577).[^o3]
- 13th century – An important contribution of [Ibn Adlan](wikipedia://en/35570756) was on [sample size](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_size) for use of frequency analysis.[^o3]
- 13th century – the first known calculation of the probability for throwing 3 dices is published in the Latin poem *[De vetula](wikipedia://en/20792933)*.
- 1560s (published 1663) – [Cardano](wikipedia://en/13145)'s *Liber de ludo aleae* attempts to calculate probabilities of dice throws. He demonstrates the efficacy of defining [odds](wikipedia://en/172069) as the ratio of favourable to unfavourable outcomes (which implies that the probability of an event is given by the ratio of favourable outcomes to the total number of possible outcomes[^o7]).
- 1577 – [Bartolomé de Medina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomé_de_Medina_%28theologian%29) defends [probabilism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_probabilism), the view that in ethics one may follow a probable opinion even if the opposite is more probable

## 17th century {#17th_century}

- 1654 – [Blaise Pascal](wikipedia://en/4068) and [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966) create the mathematical theory of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934),
- 1657 – [Chistiaan Huygens](wikipedia://en/42127)'s *De ratiociniis in ludo aleae* is the first book on mathematical probability,
- 1662 – [John Graunt](wikipedia://en/30875381)'s *Natural and Political Observations Made upon the Bills of Mortality* makes inferences from statistical data on deaths in London,
- 1666 – In Le Journal des Sçavans xxxi, 2 August 1666 (359–370(=364)) appears a review of the third edition (1665) of John Graunt's Observations on the Bills of Mortality. This review gives a summary of 'plusieurs reflexions curieuses', of which the second are Graunt's data on life expectancy. This review is used by Nicolaus Bernoulli in his De Usu Artis Conjectandi in Jure (1709).
- 1669 – Christiaan Huygens and his brother [Lodewijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodewijck_Huygens) discuss between August and December that year Graunts mortality table (Graunt 1662, p. 62) in letters #1755
- 1693 – [Edmond Halley](wikipedia://en/36858805) prepares the first [mortality tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortality_table) statistically relating death rate to age,

## 18th century {#18th_century}

- 1710 – [John Arbuthnot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Arbuthnot) argues that the constancy of the [ratio of male to female births](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sex_ratio) is a sign of divine providence,
- 1713 – Posthumous publication of [Jacob Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64740)'s *[Ars Conjectandi](wikipedia://en/17554277)*, containing the first derivation of a [law of large numbers](wikipedia://en/157055),
- 1724 – [Abraham de Moivre](wikipedia://en/57327) studies mortality statistics and the foundation of the theory of [annuities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annuities) in *Annuities upon Lives*,
- 1733 – de Moivre introduces the [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462) to approximate the [binomial distribution](wikipedia://en/3876) in probability,
- 1739 – [David Hume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hume)'s *[Treatise of Human Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treatise_of_Human_Nature)* argues that [inductive reasoning](wikipedia://en/393736) is unjustified,
- 1761 – [Thomas Bayes](wikipedia://en/149117) proves [Bayes' theorem](wikipedia://en/49569),
- 1786 – [William Playfair](wikipedia://en/1411831)'s *Commercial and Political Atlas* introduces [graphs](wikipedia://en/6879051) and [bar charts](wikipedia://en/393311) of data,

## 19th century {#19th_century}

- 1801 – [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) predicts the orbit of [Ceres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceres_%28dwarf_planet%29) using a line of best fit
- 1805 – [Adrien-Marie Legendre](wikipedia://en/49695) introduces the [method of least squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_of_least_squares) for fitting a curve to a given set of observations,
- 1814 – [Pierre-Simon Laplace](wikipedia://en/344783)'s *Essai philosophique sur les probabilités* defends a definition of probabilities in terms of equally possible cases, introduces [generating functions](wikipedia://en/160993) and [Laplace transforms](wikipedia://en/18610), uses [conjugate priors](wikipedia://en/846412) for [exponential families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_families), proves an early version of the [Bernstein–von Mises theorem](wikipedia://en/32422777) on the asymptotic irrelevance of prior distributions on the limiting posterior distribution and the role of the [Fisher information](wikipedia://en/598971) on asymptotically normal posterior modes.
- 1835 – [Adolphe Quetelet](wikipedia://en/184215)'s *Treatise on Man* introduces social science statistics and the concept of the "average man",
- 1866 – [John Venn](wikipedia://en/251482)'s *Logic of Chance* defends the [frequency interpretation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequentist) of probability.
- 1877–1883 – [Charles Sanders Peirce](wikipedia://en/6117) outlines [frequentist statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequentist_statistics), emphasizing the use of objective [randomization](wikipedia://en/65462) in [experiments](wikipedia://en/6033300) and in [sampling](wikipedia://en/160361). Peirce also invented an [optimally designed experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_design) for [regression](wikipedia://en/4481194).
- 1880 – [Thorvald N. Thiele](wikipedia://en/701256) gives a mathematical analysis of [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), introduces the [likelihood function](wikipedia://en/44968), and invents [cumulants](wikipedia://en/359684).
- 1888 – [Francis Galton](wikipedia://en/49072) introduces the concept of [correlation](wikipedia://en/157057),
- 1900 – [Louis Bachelier](wikipedia://en/1062753) analyzes [stock price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_price) movements as a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895),

## 20th century {#20th_century}

- 1908 – [Student's t-distribution](wikipedia://en/105375) for the mean of small samples published in English (following earlier derivations in German).
- 1913 – [Michel Plancherel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Plancherel) states fundamental results in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).
- 1920 – The [central limit theorem](wikipedia://en/39406) in its modern form was formally stated.
- 1921 – [John Maynard Keynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Maynard_Keynes)' *Treatise on Probability* defends a logical interpretation of probability. [Sewall Wright](wikipedia://en/1700593) develops [path analysis](wikipedia://en/590893).[^o12]
- 1928 – [L. H. C. Tippett](wikipedia://en/9168194) and [Ronald Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824) introduce [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732),
- 1933 – [Andrey Nikolaevich Kolmogorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Nikolaevich_Kolmogorov) publishes his book *Basic notions of the calculus of probability* (*Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung*) which contains an [axiomatization of probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_axiom) based on [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory),
- 1935 – Fisher's *Design of Experiments* (1st ed),
- 1937 – [Jerzy Neyman](wikipedia://en/2386106) introduces the concept of [confidence interval](wikipedia://en/280911) in statistical testing,
- 1941 – Due to the [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), research on detection theory started, leading to the [receiver operating characteristic](wikipedia://en/922505)
- 1946 – [Cox's theorem](wikipedia://en/49089) derives the axioms of probability from simple logical assumptions,
- 1948 – [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693)'s *[Mathematical Theory of Communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Theory_of_Communication)* defines capacity of communication channels in terms of probabilities,
- 1953 – [Nicholas Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Metropolis) introduces the idea of thermodynamic [simulated annealing](wikipedia://en/172244) methods
