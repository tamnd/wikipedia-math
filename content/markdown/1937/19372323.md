# Gail Carpenter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gail Carpenter"}

**Gail Alexandra Carpenter** is an American [cognitive scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science), [neuroscientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscientist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for her work with [Stephen Grossberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Grossberg) developing [adaptive resonance theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_resonance_theory), a theory of how the human brain [processes information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_processing_theory), and for her work on the [Hodgkin–Huxley model](wikipedia://en/2991090) of how [neurons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuron) operate.[^o11]

:::

She is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) of mathematics and statistics at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University),[^o4] where she was also a professor of cognitive and neural systems.[^o11]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Carpenter attended the [International School of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_of_Geneva)[^o4] then went to the [University of Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado).[^o11] She completed her Ph.D. in mathematics in 1975 at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), with the dissertation *Traveling Wave Solutions of Nerve Impulse Equations* supervised by [Charles C. Conley](wikipedia://en/37010888).

Carpenter was a faculty member at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University) before joining Boston University.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Carpenter was the first woman to receive the [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) (IEEE) Neural Networks Pioneer Award in 2008.[^o7][^o8] She received the International Neural Network Society (INNS) Gabor Award in 1999.[^o9]

She is a Fellow of the INNS,[^o10] and was named as an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) in 2013.[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Carpenter married [Stephen Grossberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Grossberg) in 1979.[^o13]
