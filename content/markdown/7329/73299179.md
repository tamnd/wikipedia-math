# Cristina Toninelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cristina Toninelli"}

**Cristina Toninelli** is an Italian mathematician who works in France as a director of research for the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS), at the Centre de recherche en mathématiques de la décision (Ceremade) of [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University). Her research concerns the [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) of [phase transitions](wikipedia://en/54423) in [interacting particle systems](wikipedia://en/37809826), including [bootstrap percolation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bootstrap_percolation), [glass transitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_transition), and [jamming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamming_%28physics%29). She has also studied [cellular automata](wikipedia://en/54342) and [group testing](wikipedia://en/24958527).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Toninelli studied physics at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), earning a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in 2000 and completing a Ph.D. in 2004. Her doctoral dissertation, *Kinetically constrained models for glassy dynamics*, was supervised by [Giovanni Jona-Lasinio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Jona-Lasinio).

She became a postdoctoral researcher in the laboratory for theoretical physics (LPT) at the [École normale supérieure (Paris)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 2003 to 2005 and then for shorter periods at Service de Physique Théorique in [CEA Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CEA_Paris-Saclay) and at the Laboratory of Theoretical Physics and Statistical Models in [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University), also visiting the [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708) in Brazil. In 2006 she began working as a researcher for the CNRS. She has been a director of research since 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

The [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205) gave Toninelli the 2021 Marc Yor Prize, citing her work on the relaxation towards equilibrium of [interacting particle systems](wikipedia://en/37809826).

She was a 2026 recipient of the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal).
