# Eleanor Mollie Horadam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eleanor Mollie Horadam"}

**Eleanor Mollie Horadam** (29 June 1921 – 5 May 2002) was an English-Australian mathematician specialising in the [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) of generalised integers.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Horadam was born in [Dewsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewsbury), [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire). She read mathematics at [Girton College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girton_College,_Cambridge). Then, while doing wartime service by day for [Rolls-Royce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolls-Royce_Limited) performing [stress–strain analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress–strain_analysis) of [jet engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_engine), she took night classes in engineering at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), earning first-class honours there.

She moved to Australia by herself in 1949, becoming a lecturer at the [University of New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29). There, she married mathematician Alwyn Horadam and raised three children, persuading the university to update their maternity policies so that (unusually for the time) she could keep her position as a lecturer. She completed a doctorate and became a senior lecturer in 1965, retired in 1983, and was named a fellow of the university in 1995.

Her daughter [Kathy Horadam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Horadam), also became a mathematician.

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Horadam's research concerned generalised integers, formed from a sequence of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) greater than one (called generalised [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666)) as the products of finite multisets of generalised primes.

She was also the author of a textbook published by the University of New England, *Principles of mathematics for economists* (1982).
