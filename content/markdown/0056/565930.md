# Taher Elgamal

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Taher Elgamal"}

**Taher Elgamal** (طاهر الجمل) (born 18 August 1955) is an [Egyptian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Egyptian_Americans) [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and tech executive.[^o4] Since January 2023, he has been a partner at venture capital firm [Evolution Equity Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution_Equity_Partners).[^o5] Prior to that, he was the founder and CEO of Securify and the director of engineering at RSA Security. From 1995 to 1998, he was the chief scientist at [Netscape Communications.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape) From 2013 to 2023, he served as the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of Security at [Salesforce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesforce).[^o6][^o7]

:::

Elgamal's 1985 paper entitled "A Public Key Cryptosystem and A Signature Scheme Based on Discrete Logarithms" proposed the design of the [ElGamal discrete log cryptosystem](wikipedia://en/59458) and of the [ElGamal signature scheme](wikipedia://en/2033473).[^o8] The latter scheme became the basis for [Digital Signature Algorithm](wikipedia://en/59470) (DSA) adopted by [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) (NIST) as the [Digital Signature Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Signature_Standard) (DSS). His development of the [Secure Sockets Layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer) (SSL) [cryptographic protocol](wikipedia://en/591994) at Netscape in the 1990s was also the basis for the [Transport Layer Security](wikipedia://en/187813) (TLS) and [HTTPS](wikipedia://en/13586) [Internet protocols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_protocols).[^o18][^o9][^o10]

## Biography {#Biography}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Elgamal was born in Egypt.[^o13] He earned a BSc in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from [Cairo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_University) in 1977, and an MS and PhD in the same field from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1981 and 1984, respectively.[^o14][^o13] [Martin Hellman](wikipedia://en/422387) was his dissertation advisor.[^o14]

### Career {#Career}

Elgamal joined the technical staff at [HP Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_Labs) in 1984. He served as chief scientist at Netscape Communications from 1995 to 1998,[^o16] where he was a driving force behind [Secure Sockets Layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer).[^o17] *Network World* described him as the "father of [SSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer)."[^o18] SSL was the basis for the [Transport Layer Security](wikipedia://en/187813) (TLS)[^o9] and [HTTPS](wikipedia://en/13586) [Internet protocols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_protocols).[^o10][^o19]

He also was the director of engineering at [RSA Security](wikipedia://en/301240) Inc.[^o35] before founding [Securify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securify) in 1998 and becoming its [chief executive officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_executive_officer). According to an interview with Elgamal,[^o20] when Securify was acquired by [Kroll-O'Gara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroll-O'Gara),[^o21] he became the president of its [information security](wikipedia://en/15036) group. After helping Securify spin out from Kroll-O'Gara,[^o22] Taher served as the company's chief technology officer (CTO) from 2001 to 2004.[^o23] In late 2008, Securify was acquired by Secure Computing[^o24] and is now part of [McAfee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McAfee).[^o25] In October 2006, he joined [Tumbleweed Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumbleweed_Communications) as a CTO.[^o26] Tumbleweed was acquired in 2008 by Axway Inc. Until 2023, Elgamal was CTO for security at Salesforce.com.[^o7][^o13][^o27] He now works as a partner at Evolution Equity Partners.[^o5]

#### Entrepreneurial ventures {#Entrepreneurial_ventures}

Elgamal is a co-founder of NokNok Labs[^o29] and InfoSec Global. He serves as a director of Vindicia, Inc.,[^o30] which provides online payment services, [Zix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zix) Corporation, which provides email encryption services, and Bay Dynamics.[^o31] He has served as an adviser to Cyphort, Bitglass, Onset Ventures, Glenbrook Partners, PGP corporation, Arcot Systems, Finjan, Actiance, Symplified, and Zetta. He served as Chief Security Officer of [Axway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axway_Software), Inc. He is vice chairman of SecureMisr.

#### Executive roles {#Executive_roles}

Elgamal has also held executive roles at technology and security companies, including

- CTO of Security at Salesforce.com from 2013 to 2023,[^o33]
- CSO at Axway, Inc. from 2008 to 2011,
- CTO at Tumbleweed Communications from 2006 to 2008,
- CTO at Securify, Inc. from 2001 to 2004,
- CEO and President of Securify, Inc. from 1998 to 2001 and,
- Chief Scientist of Netscape Communications from 1995 to 1998.[^o35]

### Recognition {#Recognition}

- Elgamal is a recipient of the RSA Conference 2009 Lifetime Achievement Award,[^o35] and he is recognized as the "father of SSL,"[^o18] the Internet security standard Secure Sockets Layer.
- Elgamal and [Paul Kocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Carl_Kocher) were jointly awarded the 2019 [Marconi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marconi_Prize) for "their development of [SSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Sockets_Layer)/[TLS](wikipedia://en/187813) and other contributions to the security of communications".[^o36]
- Election to the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering), 2022, for contributions to cryptography, e-commerce, and protocols for secure internet transactions.[^o37]

## Publications {#Publications}

As a scholar, Elgamal published 4 articles:

- T. ElGamal, "A subexponential-time algorithm for computing discrete logarithms over *GF*(*p*<sup>2</sup>)", IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory, vol. 31, no. 4, pp. 473–481, 1985.[^o39]
- T. Elgamal, "A public key cryptosystem and a signature scheme based on discrete logarithms", IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory, vol. 31, no. 4, pp. 469–472, Jul. 1985.[^o8]
- T. ElGamal, "On Computing Logarithms Over Finite Fields", in Advances in Cryptology – CRYPTO ’85 Proceedings, 1986, pp. 396–402.[^o40]
- T. Elgamal, "The new predicaments of security practitioners", Computer Fraud & Security, vol. 2009, no. 11, pp. 12–14, Nov. 2009.[^o41]
