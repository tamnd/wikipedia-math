# 3000 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="3000"}

**3000** (**three thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [2999](wikipedia://en/490395#2900_to_2999) and preceding 3001. It is the smallest number requiring thirteen letters in English (when "and" is required from 101 forward).

:::
