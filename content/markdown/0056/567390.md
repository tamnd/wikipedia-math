# Jakow Trachtenberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jakow Trachtenberg"}

Jakow Trachtenberg

**Jakow Trachtenberg** (17 June 1888 – 26 October 1951) was a Ukrainian-Jewish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who developed the [mental calculation](wikipedia://en/567292) techniques called the [Trachtenberg system](wikipedia://en/567391).[^o3]

:::

*Image: L'ingénieur et écrivain russe Jakow Trachtenberg, né à Odessa a été enregistré le 23 avril 1940 par la Gestapo Vienne base de données Gestapo résistance individuelle.jpg.* Trachtenberg in a [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo) mugshot, 1940

He was born in [Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa), in the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) (modern [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)) to Jewish parents. He graduated with highest honors from the [Mining Engineering Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Mineral_Resources_University) in [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) and later worked as an engineer in the [Obukhov arms factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obukhov_State_Plant).[^o4] While still in his early twenties, he became Chief Engineer with 11,000 men under his supervision.[^o3] The Tsarist government gave him the responsibility of supervising the formation of a well-developed [navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navy).

Trachtenberg was a dedicated [pacifist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacifism). When war broke out in 1914, he was instrumental in organising a society known as the Society of Good Samaritans. The idea was to train Russian students to take care of the wounded. It also had a special recognition from Tsar [Nicholas II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II_of_Russia). He was against violence of any sort despite having a leading position in tsarist arms production.[^o3]

After the [Russian Revolutions of 1917](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Revolutions_of_1917), Trachtenberg fled to the [Weimar Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weimar_Republic) where he became critical of [Nazi policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_policies). He was imprisoned in a Nazi [concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_camp) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II). He developed his system of [mental arithmetic](wikipedia://en/567292) during his imprisonment. He later fled to Switzerland. He died in 1951.[^o4]
