# 5000 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="5000"}

**5000** (**five thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 4999 and preceding 5001. Five thousand is, at the same time, the largest [isogrammic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterogram_%28literature%29) numeral, and the smallest number that contains every one of the five [vowels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vowel) (a, e, i, o, u) unrepeated in certain dialects of the [English language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) (i.e. those that do not include the word “and” when writing out 230, 250, 260, 602, and 640).

:::
