# Bredon cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bredon cohomology"}

The **Bredon cohomology**, introduced by [Glen E. Bredon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_E._Bredon), is a type of [equivariant cohomology](wikipedia://en/2976342) that is a [contravariant functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contravariant_functor) from the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of $G$-complexes with equivariant [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) maps to the category of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974) together with the connecting [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) satisfying some conditions.

:::
