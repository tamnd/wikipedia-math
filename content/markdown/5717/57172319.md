# Busemann G-space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Busemann G-space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Busemann *G*-space** is a type of [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018) first described by [Herbert Busemann](wikipedia://en/25491802) in 1942.

:::

If $(X,d)$ is a metric space such that

1. for every two distinct $x, y \in X$ there exists $z \in X\setminus\{x,y\}$ such that $d(x,z)+d(y,z)=d(x,y)$ ([Menger convexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menger_convexity))
1. every $d$-bounded set of infinite cardinality possesses [accumulation points](wikipedia://en/226505)
1. for every $w \in X$ there exists $\rho_w$ such that for any distinct points $x,y \in B(w,\rho_w)$ there exists $z \in ( B(w,\rho_w)\setminus\{ x,y \} )^\circ$ such that $d(x,y)+d(y,z)=d(x,z)$ ([geodesics](wikipedia://en/91096) are locally extendable)
1. for any distinct points $x,y \in X$, if $u,v \in X$ such that $d(x,y)+d(y,u)=d(x,u)$, $d(x,y)+d(y,v)=d(x,v)$ and $d(y,u)=d(y,v)$, then $u=v$ (geodesic extensions are unique).

then *X* is said to be a *Busemann* *G*-*space*. Every Busemann *G*-space is a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325).

The [Busemann conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busemann_conjecture) states that every Busemann *G*-space is a [topological manifold](wikipedia://en/2119193). It is a special case of the [Bing–Borsuk conjecture](wikipedia://en/57169339). The Busemann conjecture is known to be true for dimensions 1 to 4.[^o2][^o3]
