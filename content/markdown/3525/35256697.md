# Richard Lyons (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Lyons"}

**Richard Neil Lyons** (born January 22, 1945, in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29))[^o2] is an American mathematician specializing in finite group theory.

:::

Lyons received his PhD in 1970 at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) under [John Griggs Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Griggs_Thompson) with the thesis *Characterizations of Some Finite Simple Groups with Small 2-Rank*. From 1972 to 2017, he was a professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

With [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478) and [Ronald Solomon](wikipedia://en/35255960) he wrote, and is continuing to write, now with [Inna Capdeboscq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inna_Capdeboscq), a series on the second-generation proof of the [classification program for finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445). Ten volumes of this series have been published so far. He discovered a [sporadic group](wikipedia://en/324806)[^o4] which [Charles Sims](wikipedia://en/20022166) constructed and called the [Lyons group](wikipedia://en/1110773) *Ly*.

In 2012, he shared the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition, awarded by the American Mathematical Society, with [Michael Aschbacher](wikipedia://en/1652651), Stephen D. Smith, and Ronald Solomon. In 2013, he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to the classification of the finite simple groups, including the discovery of one of the 26 sporadic finite simple groups.".[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

- with Gorenstein: *The local structure of finite groups of characteristic 2 type*, American Mathematical Society, 1983
- with Daniel Gorenstein, Ronald Solomon: *The classification of the finite simple groups*, American Mathematical Society, 10 Vols., 1994–2023
- with Michael Aschbacher, Stephen D. Smith, Ronald Solomon: *The classification of finite simple groups: Groups of characteristic 2 type*, American Mathematical Society, 2011
