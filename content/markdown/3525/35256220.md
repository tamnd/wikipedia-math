# Robert Fortet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Fortet"}

**Robert Fortet** (1 May 1912, [Boulazac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulazac), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) – 3 July 1998, [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989)) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28math%29). His doctoral advisor was [Maurice René Fréchet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_René_Fréchet).

:::

Fortet was a professor at the [University of Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen) and at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). Some of his Ph.D. students in Paris were [Egon Balas](wikipedia://en/35872667), [Jyotiprasad Medhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyotiprasad_Medhi) and [Jacques Neveu](wikipedia://en/56512426).
