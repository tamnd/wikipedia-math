# Ronald Solomon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald Solomon"}

**Ronald "Ron" Mark Solomon** (b. 15 December 1948[^o2]) is an American mathematician specializing in the theory of [finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_groups).

:::

Solomon studied as an undergraduate at [Queens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens_College,_City_University_of_New_York) and received a PhD in 1971 at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) under [Walter Feit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Feit) with the thesis *Finite Groups with Sylow 2-Subgroups of the Type of the Alternating Group on Twelve Letters*. In 1972, he began his participation in the [classification program for finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445), after hearing a lecture by [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478). He was for two years an instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and the academic year 1974–1975 at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), before he became a professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), where he has remained. In 2006, he received the [Levi L. Conant Prize](wikipedia://en/4535938) and in 2012 the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550). In 2012, he was elected a Fellow of the American Mathematical Society.

With [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478) and [Richard Lyons](wikipedia://en/35256697) he wrote, and is continuing to write now with [Inna Capdeboscq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inna_Capdeboscq), a series on the second-generation proof of the classification program for finite simple groups. Ten volumes of this series have been published so far.
