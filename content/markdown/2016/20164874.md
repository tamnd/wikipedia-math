# Mayagüez metropolitan area

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mayagüez metropolitan area"}

The **Mayagüez metropolitan area** ([Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language): *área metropolitana de Mayagüez*), is the fourth largest [metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area) (MSA) in [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico), comprising the municipalities of [Mayagüez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayagüez,_Puerto_Rico), [Cabo Rojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabo_Rojo,_Puerto_Rico), [San Germán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Germán,_Puerto_Rico), [Lajas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajas,_Puerto_Rico), [Sabana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabana_Grande,_Puerto_Rico), and [Hormigueros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormigueros,_Puerto_Rico) between the [coastal plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_plain) and the [Cordillera Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Central_%28Puerto_Rico%29) mountain subrange in the southwestern region of the [main island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Puerto_Rico). One of [6 metropolitan statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_statistical_areas#Metropolitan_statistical_areas) in Puerto Rico, it lies within the Mayagüez–[Aguadilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguadilla_metropolitan_area) [combined statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_statistical_area) (CSA), which is one of [3 primary statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_statistical_areas#Combined_and_primary_statistical_areas) in the main island as defined by the [United States Census Bureau](wikipedia://en/57070). As of 2023, census estimates place the population of the Mayagüez metropolitan area at 207,877.[^o3][^o4][^o6]

:::

## Mayagüez metropolitan area {#Mayagüez_metropolitan_area}

With an estimated population of 207,877 as of 2023, the Mayagüez metropolitan area (MSA) is the fourth populous in [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico), covering 6 of 78 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Puerto_Rico) in the southwestern region of the [main island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Puerto_Rico). Mayagüez is considered to be its [principal city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_city).[^o6] The Mayagüez metropolitan area is the [223rd most populated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) between the [Lexington Park, MD MSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California-Lexington_Park,_MD_MSA) and the [Chico, CA MSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico,_CA_MSA).

### Topography {#Topography}

*Image: USA Puerto Rico topographic location map.png.*

*Image: Puerto\_Rico\_geography.gif.* General [physiographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_geography) map of Puerto Rico, with [mountainous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain) terrain in green, [karst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karst) in orange, and [plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_plain) in yellow

Comprising Mayagüez, Cabo Rojo, San Germán, Lajas, Sabana Grande, and Hormigueros, the Mayagüez metropolitan area is situated between the western and southern [coastal plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_plain), the [Añasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Río_Grande_de_Añasco) Valley, the [Lajas Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajas_Valley), the [Guanajibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanajibo_River) Valley, and the [Cordillera Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Central_%28Puerto_Rico%29) mountain subrange in the southwestern region of the [main island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Puerto_Rico) of Puerto Rico.

All municipalities have an urbanized, densely populated downtown area and administrative center, categorized as a [barrio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrios_of_Puerto_Rico) and known as a *pueblo,* and concentrated urbanized residential areas with large populations, especially along the coastlines of [Mayagüez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayagüez,_Puerto_Rico), [Cabo Rojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabo_Rojo,_Puerto_Rico), and [Lajas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajas,_Puerto_Rico). All municipales have rural areas, particularly those that lie on mountainous terrain like [San Germán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Germán,_Puerto_Rico), [Sabana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabana_Grande,_Puerto_Rico), and [Hormigueros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormigueros,_Puerto_Rico).

## Mayagüez–Aguadilla combined area {#Mayagüez–Aguadilla_combined_area}

The Mayagüez–Aguadilla [combined statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_statistical_area) (CSA) is divided into two [metropolitan statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area) (MSA), the Mayagüez MSA and Aguadilla MSA, covering 13 of 78 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Puerto_Rico) in [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico).

In 2009, the Mayagüez–Aguadilla combined statistical area comprised 6.5% of total population in Puerto Rico. The [2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census) placed the population at 519,331, a 51.61% increase over the [2000 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census,_2000) figure of 251,260.[^o12][^o13] The [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census) placed the population at 467,599, a 9.96% decrease over the [2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census) figure of 519,331.

With an estimated population of 458,312 as of 2023, the Mayagüez–Aguadilla combined statistical area is the [2nd most populous CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_statistical_areas#Combined_and_primary_statistical_areas) in Puerto Rico and the [93rd most populous CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_statistical_area) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) between the [Kalamazoo–Battle Creek–Portage, MI CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalamazoo-Battle_Creek-Portage,_MI_Combined_Statistical_Area) and the [Cedar Rapids–Iowa City, IA CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedar_Rapids-Iowa_City,_IA_Combined_Statistical_Area).
