# Non-Euclidean crystallographic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Non-Euclidean crystallographic group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **non-Euclidean crystallographic group**, **NEC group** or **N.E.C. group** is a [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536) of [isometries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometries) of the [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/241291) plane. These [symmetry groups](wikipedia://en/28186) correspond to the [wallpaper groups](wikipedia://en/386214) in [euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417). A NEC group which contains only [orientation-preserving](wikipedia://en/187446) elements is called a [Fuchsian group](wikipedia://en/471365), and any non-Fuchsian NEC group has an index 2 Fuchsian subgroup of orientation-preserving elements.

:::

The hyperbolic [triangle groups](wikipedia://en/2069342) are notable NEC groups. Others are listed in [Orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678#Hyperbolic_plane).
