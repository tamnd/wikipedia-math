# Stanisław Gołąb

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Gołąb"}

**Stanisław Gołąb** (July 26, 1902 – April 30, 1980) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), working in particular on the field of [affine geometry](wikipedia://en/243890).

:::

In 1932, he proved that the [perimeter](wikipedia://en/23636) of the [unit disc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_disc) respect to a given [metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_%28mathematics%29) can take any value in between 6 and 8, and that these extremal values are obtained if and only if the unit disc is an [affine regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_regular_polygon) [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) resp. a [parallelogram](wikipedia://en/133496).[^o3]

He worked at the [State Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/67042302), which was incorporated into the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1952.[^o4]
