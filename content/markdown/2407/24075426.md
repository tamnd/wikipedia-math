# James Wiegold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="James Wiegold"}

**James Wiegold** (15 April 1934 – 4 August 2009) was a [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Born in [Trecenydd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trecenydd), [Caerphilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caerphilly), he earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) in 1958, studying under [Bernhard Neumann](wikipedia://en/5032588), and is most notable for his contributions to [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

Wiegold died from [leukaemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leukemia) on 4 August 2009, in [Penarth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penarth), [Vale of Glamorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vale_of_Glamorgan).[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

- Assistant Lecturer, University College of North Staffordshire (now [Keele University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keele_University)), 1957–1960
- Lecturer, [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), 1960–1963
- Lecturer, University College of South Wales and Monmouthshire (now [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)), 1963–1966
- Senior Lecturer, University College of South Wales and Monmouthshire (now [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)), 1966–1969
- Visiting Senior Lecturer and Visiting Reader, [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), 1968–1970
- Reader, University College of South Wales and Monmouthshire (now [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)), 1969–1974
- Professor, University College, Cardiff (now [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)), 1974–his death
- Dean of Science, University College, Cardiff (now [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)), 1982–1985
