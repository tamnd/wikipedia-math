# Ivar Stakgold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivar Stakgold"}

**Ivar Stakgold** (December 13, 1925 – May 29, 2018)[^o2] was a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegians)-born American academic mathematician and [bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_bridge) player from [Newark, Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_Delaware).[^o3] As the sole author of two books he specialized in [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) ([LCSH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LCSH)).[^o8][^o11]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Stakgold was born in [Oslo, Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo,_Norway) to parents with Russian-Jewish heritage.[^o5][^o6] He studied applied mathematics at Harvard University and earned the Ph.D. in 1949 with a dissertation, *The Cauchy Relations In A Molecular Theory of Elasticity*, under [Léon Nicolas Brillouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Léon_Brillouin).[^o24] He was professor emeritus of mathematical sciences at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) and a researcher at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).[^o7] He was a former president of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).[^o8]
