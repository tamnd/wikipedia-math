# Katalin Vesztergombi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Katalin Vesztergombi"}

Vesztergombi Katalin

**Katalin Vesztergombi** (born July 17, 1948) is a Hungarian mathematician known for her contributions to [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468). A student of [Vera T. Sós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_T._Sós) and a co-author of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), she is an emeritus associate professor at [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

As a high-school student in the 1960s, Vesztergombi became part of a special class for gifted mathematics students at [Fazekas Mihály Gimnázium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazekas_Mihály_Gimnázium_%28Budapest%29) with her future collaborators [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938), József Pelikán, and others. She completed her Ph.D. in 1987 at [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University). Her dissertation, *Distribution of Distances in Finite Point Sets*, is connected to the [Erdős distinct distances problem](wikipedia://en/30193394) and was supervised by Vera Sós.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Vesztergombi's research contributions include works on [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027),PR [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) and [graph products](wikipedia://en/11492935),XN combinatorial [discrepancy theory](wikipedia://en/1116842),SS distance problems in [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468),LD [geometric graph theory](wikipedia://en/3313527),GR the [rectilinear crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298594) of the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816),CQ and [graphons](wikipedia://en/26987628).D1D2

With [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938) and József Pelikán, she is the author of the textbook *Discrete Mathematics: Elementary and Beyond*.DM

## Personal {#Personal}

Vesztergombi is married to [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938), with whom she is also a frequent research collaborator.
