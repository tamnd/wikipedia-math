# Dmitry Fuchs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitry Fuchs"}

Borisovich

*Image: Dmitry Fuchs (cropped).jpg.* Fuchs at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2006

**Dmitry Borisovich Fuchs** (Дмитрий Борисович Фукс,[^o2] born 30 September 1939) is a Soviet and American mathematician, specializing in the representation theory of infinite-dimensional [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and in topology.[^o3] He was born in [Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan), [Soviet Tatarstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Tatarstan).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fuchs received in 1964 his Russian candidate degree (Ph.D.) under [Albert S. Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_S._Schwarz) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he taught thereafter. Schwarz conducted a seminar on algebraic topology with [Mikhail Postnikov](wikipedia://en/19633564) and [Vladimir Boltyansky](wikipedia://en/20464140). Fuchs participated in the seminar and, as a student, published papers with Schwarz, as did [Askold Ivanovich Vinogradov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askold_Ivanovich_Vinogradov) a few years earlier. Fuchs received his Russian doctorate (higher doctoral degree) in 1987 at [Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University). Since 1991 he has been a professor at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).

With [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) he introduced in 1970 the Gelfand-Fuchs cohomology of Lie algebras.[^o7] Gelfand-Fuchs cohomology has applications in the proof of the [Macdonald identities](wikipedia://en/5027268) in combinatorics and in the calculation of characteristic classes of [foliations](wikipedia://en/371227). With [Boris Feigin](wikipedia://en/53823921) he determined the structure of [Verma modules](wikipedia://en/2975234) in the [Virasoro algebra](wikipedia://en/348029) representation theory, which has applications in [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) and [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774).[^o8]

His students include Boris Feigin (with whom he has collaborated extensively), Fedor Malikov, [Sergei Tabachnikov](wikipedia://en/45658812), and [Vladimir Rokhlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Rokhlin_Jr.), as well as [Edward Frenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Frenkel) for whom Fuchs was a second advisor.[^o9] Frenkel, among many others, was affected by the Soviet antisemitism which flourished from 1954 to 1970 (described by Fuchs) and on into the 1980s.[^o10][^o11]

In 1978 Fuchs was an Invited Speaker with talk *New results on the characteristic classes of foliations* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Fuchs's father was also a mathematician, and a student of [Stefan Bergman](wikipedia://en/9395687).[^o13] His daughter Elena Fuchs is also a professor in the mathematics department at UC Davis, and his daughter Ekaterina Fuchs is a professor in the mathematics department at the [City College of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_San_Francisco).[^o14][^o15][^o16]
