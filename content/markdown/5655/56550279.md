# Valérie Berthé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valérie Berthé"}

*Image: Valérie Berthé.jpg.* Valérie Berthé

**Valérie Berthé** (born 16 December 1968) is a French mathematician who works as a director of research for the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), a joint project between CNRS and [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). Her research involves [symbolic dynamics](wikipedia://en/1986263), [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490), [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468), [numeral systems](wikipedia://en/21170), [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671), and [fractals](wikipedia://en/10913).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Berthé completed her baccalauréat at age 16, and studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 1988 to 1993. She earned a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) and master's degree in pure mathematics from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1989, a [Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) from [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 1991, completed her [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1992, and was recruited by CNRS in 1993. Continuing her graduate studies, she defended a doctoral thesis in 1994 at the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1). Her dissertation, *Fonctions de Carlitz et automates: Entropies conditionnelles* was supervised by Jean-Paul Allouche. She completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1999, again under the supervision of Allouche, at the [University of the Mediterranean Aix-Marseille II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Mediterranean); her habilitation thesis was *Étude arithmétique et dynamique de suites algorithmiques*.

## Research {#Research}

Berthé's research spans the area of symbolic dynamics, combinatorics on words, numeration systems and discrete geometry. She has recently made significant process in the study of S-adic dynamical systems, and also of continued fractions in higher dimensions.[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

## Associations {#Associations}

Berthé is a vice-president of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205) (SMF), and director of publications for the SMF. She has played an active role in [L'association femmes et mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'association_femmes_et_mathématiques). Berthé has also been associated with the [M. Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474) pseudonymous mathematical collaboration on [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490) and the Pythias Fogg pseudonymous collaboration on [substitution systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_system).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2013, Berthé was elevated to the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour).
