# Eduard Zehnder

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eduard Zehnder"}

**Eduard J. Zehnder** (10 November 1940 – 22 November 2024) was a Swiss mathematician, considered one of the founders of [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Zehnder studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eidgenössische_Technische_Hochschule_Zürich) from 1960 to 1965, where he also did his Ph.D. in theoretical physics, defending his thesis on the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420) in 1971 under the direction of [Res Jost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Res_Jost). He was a visiting professor at [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) (invited by [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316)), visiting member of [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_%28New_Jersey%29) from 1972 to 1974. He passed his habilitation in mathematics in 1974 at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg). He had appointments at the [University of Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bochum) from 1976 to 1986; at the [University of Aix-la-Chapelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aix-la-Chapelle) during the academic year 1987–88, where he was director of the Mathematical Institute. From 1988, he had a chair at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eidgenössische_Technische_Hochschule_Zürich), where he became [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) in 2006. He was [plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) (ICM) in 1986 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5] In 2021 he was elected a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o6]

Zehnder made fundamental contributions to the field of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). In particular, in one of his groundbreaking works with [Charles C. Conley](wikipedia://en/37010888), he established the celebrated [Arnold conjecture](wikipedia://en/72203783) for fixed points of Hamiltonian [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564), and paved the way for the development of the new field of [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology).

He directed the thesis of several mathematicians. His first student was [Andreas Floer](wikipedia://en/2280157), who defended his thesis in 1984.

Zehnder died in [Greifensee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greifensee,_Zürich) on 22 November 2024, at the age of 84.[^o7][^o8]

- Eduard Zehnder. Lectures on dynamical systems. Hamiltonian vector fields and symplectic capacities. EMS Textbooks in Mathematics. European Mathematical Society, Zürich, 2010. x+353 pp.
- [Helmut Hofer](wikipedia://en/34920327) and Eduard Zehnder. Symplectic invariants and Hamiltonian dynamics. Reprint of the 1994 edition. Modern Birkhäuser Classics. Birkhäuser Verlag, Basel, 2011. xiv+341 pp.
