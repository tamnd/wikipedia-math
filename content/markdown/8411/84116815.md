# Michał Wojtylak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michał Wojtylak"}

**Michał Wojtylak** is a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) professor of [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675). He is the head of the Department of [Functional Analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science of [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University)[^o3].

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wojtylak earned a master's degree in mathematics from Jagiellonian University in 2003[^o5]. In 2007, he completed his Ph.D. in mathematics under the supervision of [Jan Stochel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Stochel)[^o6]. Between 2012 and 2014, he was a postdoctoral researcher at [Berlin University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)[^o7], where in 2015 he obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) for his thesis *Analysis of spectra of operators and matrices in indefinite inner product spaces*[^o8]. In 2016, he obtained his habilitation from his alma mater, Jagiellonian University. In 2026, he was awarded the academic title of professor by the [President of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Poland)[^o9]. He is currently a professor at Jagiellonian University and the head of the Department of Functional Analysis[^o10].

He has published articles in *SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications*, *Journal of Functional Analysis*, *Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications*, *Electronic Communications in Probability*, *Linear Algebra and Its Applications*, and *Integral Equations and Operator Theory*.[^o11][^o12]
