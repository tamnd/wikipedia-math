# Ligon–Schaaf regularization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ligon–Schaaf regularization"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **Ligon–Schaaf regularization** is a method to describe the [singularities](wikipedia://en/54240) of collisions in the [Kepler problem](wikipedia://en/2410571) using the **Ligon–Schaaf (regularization) map**. Both are named after Thomas Ligon and Manfred Schaaf, who introduced them in 1976. (Thomas Ligon wrote his [Diploma Thesis,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma_thesis) now Master Thesis, about them under Manfred Schaaf's supervision.) Compared to other methods of regularization, the Ligon–Schaaf regularization possesses special properties like not transforming time or being able to transform all negative energy levels together. (The related [Moser regularization](wikipedia://en/84111360) requires a fixed energy.)

:::

## Ligon–Schaaf map {#Ligon–Schaaf_map}

Let $E\colon T^*(\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\})\rightarrow\mathbb{R}, (q,p)\mapsto\frac{1}{2}|p|^2-\frac{1}{|q|}$ the [Hamilton function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_function) ([energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy)) of the [Kepler problem](wikipedia://en/2410571). Let furthermore:[^o3]

$$u(q,p) :=\left(\sqrt{-2E(q,p)}|q|p,|p|^2|q|-1\right) \in\mathbb{R}^3;$$

$$v(q,p) :=\left(|q|^{-1}q-\langle q,p\rangle,\sqrt{-2E(q,p)}\langle q,p\rangle\right) \in\mathbb{R}^3;$$

$$\phi(q,p) :=\sqrt{-2E(q,p)}\langle q,p\rangle.$$

The Ligon–Schaaf map $\Phi\colon T^*(\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\})\supset E^{-1}((-\infty,0])\rightarrow T^*S^2\setminus S^2$ is then given by:[^o3]

$$\Phi(q,p) :=\left(\cos(\phi)u+\sin(\phi)v,\sin(\phi)u-\cos(\phi)v\right) \in T^*S^2\subset T^*\mathbb{R}^3\cong \mathbb{R}^3\times\mathbb{R}^3.$$

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- $u$ and $v$ are [orthonormal](wikipedia://en/194743), hence $\langle u,v\rangle=0$, $|u|=1$ und $|v|=1$.[^o6][^o7] Hence the Ligon–Schaaf map indeed maps to the subset $T^*S^2\subset T^*\mathbb{R}^3\cong \mathbb{R}^3\times\mathbb{R}^3$ and is therefore [well-defined](wikipedia://en/253456).
- The Ligon–Schaaf map $\Phi$ is a [symplectomorphism](wikipedia://en/312304).[^o8]
- With the Delaunay Hamiltonian $D\colon T^*S^2\setminus S^2\rightarrow\mathbb{R}, y\mapsto -\frac{1}{2y^2}$, one has:[^o9][^o10]

$$E=D\circ\Phi.$$

- With the [cross product](wikipedia://en/157092) $\mu\colon T^*S^2\setminus S^2\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^3, (x,y)\mapsto x\times y$, the [Runge–Lenz vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge–Lenz_vector) $A$ and [angular momentum](wikipedia://en/2839) $L$:[^o11][^o12]

$$\left(\frac{(A_2,-A_1))}{\sqrt{-2E}},L\right)=\mu\circ\Phi.$$

:::
