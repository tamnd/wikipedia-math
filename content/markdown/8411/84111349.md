# Kustaanheimo–Stiefel regularization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kustaanheimo–Stiefel regularization"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **Kustaanheimo–Stiefel regularization** is a method to transform the spatial [Kepler problem](wikipedia://en/2410571) into a spatial harmonic oscillator using the **Kustaanheimo–Stiefel (regularization) map**. Both are named after [Paul Kustaanheimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Kustaanheimo) and [Eduard Stiefel](wikipedia://en/12223876), who introduced them in 1965. Simply put, the idea is to increase the dimension to untangle [singularities](wikipedia://en/54240) arising from collisions.

:::

## Kustaanheimo–Stiefel map {#Kustaanheimo–Stiefel_map}

The Kustaanheimo–Stiefel map is an extension of the complex [Hopf fibration](wikipedia://en/580384) $h_\mathbb{C}\colon S^3\rightarrow S^2$, using complex and [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) given by $h_\mathbb{C}\colon\mathbb{C}^2\supset S^3\rightarrow S^2\cong\mathbb{C}P^1, (z,w)\mapsto[z:w]$, by a squared radial component. It is a map $\Phi\colon\mathbb{R}^4\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^3$ given by filling with $0\mapsto 0$ of the map:

$$h_\mathbb{C}\times-^2\colon \mathbb{R}^4 \cong S^3\times\mathbb{R}^+\rightarrow S^2\times\mathbb{R}^+ \cong\mathbb{R}^3.$$

In [cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates), the Kustaanheimo–Stiefel map $\mathbb{R}^4\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^3, (x,y,z,w)\mapsto(a,b,c)$ is given by:[^o3]

$$a=x^2-y^2-z^2+w^2;$$

$$b=2(xy-zw);$$

$$c=2(xz+yw).$$

The above squaring of the distance, hence a$a^2+b^2+c^2=(x^2+y^2+z^2+w^2)^2$, follows from the [binomial formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_formula).
