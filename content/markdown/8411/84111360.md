# Moser regularization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moser regularization"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the **Moser regularization** is a method to transform the flow of the [Kepler problem](wikipedia://en/2410571), meaning the motion of [celestial bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_bodies), into a [geodesic flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_flow) on the [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) using the **Moser (regularization) map**. Both are named after [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316), who introduced them in 1970.

:::

## Moser map {#Moser_map}

Similar to the [stereographic projection](wikipedia://en/143431), the Moser map $\Phi\colon T^*\mathbb{R}^n\rightarrow T^*(S^n\setminus\{0\})$ is given by:[^o3]

$$\Phi_1(\mathbf{q},\mathbf{p}) :=\left(\frac{2\mathbf{p}}{p^2+1},\frac{2p^2}{p^2+1}-1\right);$$

$$\Phi_2(\mathbf{q},\mathbf{p}) :=\left(-\frac{1}{2}(p^2+1)\mathbf{q}+(\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{p})\mathbf{p},-\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{p}\right).$$

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The Moser map $\Phi$ is a [symplectomorphism](wikipedia://en/312304).[^o6]

:::
