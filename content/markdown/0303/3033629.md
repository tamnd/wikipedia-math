# Olga Oleinik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olga Oleinik"}

**Olga Arsenievna Oleinik** (also as *Oleĭnik*) [HFRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HFRSE) (О́льга Арсе́ньевна Оле́йник) (2 July 1925 – 13 October 2001) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who conducted pioneering work on the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), the theory of strongly inhomogeneous elastic media, and the mathematical theory of [boundary layers](wikipedia://en/224301). She was a student of [Ivan Petrovsky](wikipedia://en/4598657). She studied and worked at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).

:::

She received many prizes for her remarkable contributions: the [Chebotarev Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebotarev_Prize) in 1952; the State Prize 1988; the [Petrowsky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrowsky_Prize) in 1995; and the Prize of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1995. Also she was member of several foreign academies of sciences, and earned several honorary degrees.

## Life {#Life}

On 2 May 1985 Olga Oleinik was awarded the [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) [honoris causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_causa) by the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), jointly with [Fritz John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_John).[^o4]

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

She authored more than 370 mathematical publications and 8 monographs, as the sole author or in collaboration with others: her work covers [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) where her work enlightened various aspects, [elasticity theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_theory) and [boundary layers](wikipedia://en/224301) theory.

### Teaching activity {#Teaching_activity}

She was a very active teacher, advising the [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) of 57 "[candidates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)".
