# Aumann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aumann"}

**Aumann** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Franz Joseph Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Joseph_Aumann), (1728–1797), Austrian composer
- [Georg Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Aumann) (1906–1980), German mathematician
- [Raimond Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raimond_Aumann) (born 1963), German footballer
- [Robert Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Aumann) (born 1930), Israeli mathematician
  - [Aumann's agreement theorem](wikipedia://en/20515591)
- [Susan L. M. Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_L._M._Aumann) (born 1960), American politician from Maryland
