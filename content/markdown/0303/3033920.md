# Platon Poretsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Platon Poretsky"}

**Platon Sergeevich Poretsky** (Платон Серге́евич Порецкий; 3 October 1846 in [Elisavetgrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirovohrad), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) – 9 August 1907 in [Gorodnyansky Uyezd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorodnyansky_Uyezd), [Chernigov Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernigov_Governorate), Russian Empire) was a noted [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) Imperial [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).

:::

Graduated from [Kharkov University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkov_University), he worked in [Astrakhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrakhan) and [Pulkovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulkovo_Observatory) in [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg).

Later, as an astronomer at [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_University), following the advice of his older colleague Professor of Mathematics A. V. Vasiliev at [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_University) (father of [Nicolai A. Vasiliev](wikipedia://en/4820490)) to learn the works of [George Boole](wikipedia://en/38298), Poretsky developed "logical calculus" and through specific "logical equations" applied it to the [theory of probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_probability). Thus, he extended and augmented the works of logicians and mathematicians George Boole, [William Stanley Jevons](wikipedia://en/207021) and [Ernst Schröder](wikipedia://en/448015) (known as Boole–Jevons–Schröder–Poretsky method).[^o7] He discovered [Poretsky's law of forms](wikipedia://en/40152955)[^o4] and gave the first general treatment of antecedent and consequent Boolean reasoning,[^o5][^o6] laying the groundwork for [Archie Blake](wikipedia://en/69419362)'s work on the [Blake canonical form](wikipedia://en/39986184).
