# Nikolai Chebotaryov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Chebotaryov"}

Grigorievich

**Nikolai Grigorievich Chebotaryov** (often spelled **Chebotarov** or **Chebotarev**; Никола́й Григо́рьевич Чеботарёв; Мико́ла Григо́рович Чеботарьо́в; 15 June 1894 – 2 July 1947) was a Soviet [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He is best known for the [Chebotaryov density theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebotaryov_density_theorem).[^o4]

:::

He was a student of [Dmitry Grave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Grave). Chebotaryov worked on the algebra of polynomials, in particular examining the distribution of the zeros. He also studied [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316) and wrote a textbook on the subject titled *Basic Galois Theory*. His ideas were used by [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351) to prove the [Artin reciprocity law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artin_reciprocity_law).[^o6] He worked with his student Anatoly Dorodnov on a generalization of the [quadrature of the lune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrature_of_the_lune),[^o7] and proved the conjecture now known as the [Chebotarev theorem on roots of unity](wikipedia://en/38937850).

## Early life {#Early_life}

Nikolai Chebotaryov was born on 15 June 1894 in [Kamianets-Podilskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamianets-Podilskyi), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) (now in [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)). He entered the department of physics and mathematics at [Kiev University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv) in 1912. In 1928, he became a professor at [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_Federal_University), remaining there for the rest of his life. He died on 2 July 1947. He was an atheist.[^o9] On 14 May 2010, a memorial plaque for Nikolai Chebotaryov was unveiled on the main administration building of [I.I. Mechnikov Odessa National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I.I._Mechnikov_Odessa_National_University).[^o10]
