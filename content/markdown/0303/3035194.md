# Novikov conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Novikov conjecture"}

The **Novikov conjecture** is one of the most important [unsolved problems](wikipedia://en/1333335) in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). It is named for [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515) who originally posed the [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) in 1965.

:::

The Novikov conjecture concerns the [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) invariance of certain [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) in the [Pontryagin classes](wikipedia://en/385944) of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470), arising from the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004). According to the Novikov conjecture, the *higher signatures*, which are certain numerical invariants of [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), are homotopy invariants.

The conjecture has been [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) for [finitely generated abelian groups](wikipedia://en/58527). It is not yet known whether the Novikov conjecture holds true for all [groups](wikipedia://en/19447). There are no known [counterexamples](wikipedia://en/143151) to the conjecture.

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Precise formulation of the conjecture"}

## Precise formulation of the conjecture {#Precise_formulation_of_the_conjecture}

Let $G$ be a [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536) and $BG$ its [classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556), which is an [Eilenberg–MacLane space](wikipedia://en/1073230) of type $K(G,1)$, and therefore unique up to [homotopy equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_equivalence) as a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022). Let

$$f\colon M\rightarrow BG$$

be a continuous map from a closed oriented $n$-dimensional manifold $M$ to $BG$, and

$$x \in H^{n-4i} (BG;\mathbb{Q} ).$$

Novikov considered the numerical expression, found by evaluating the cohomology class in top dimension against the [fundamental class](wikipedia://en/669440) $[M]$, and known as a **higher signature**:

$$\left\langle f^*(x) \cup L_i(M),[M] \right\rangle \in \mathbb{Q}$$

where $L_i$ is the $i^{\rm th}$ [Hirzebruch polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirzebruch_polynomial), or sometimes (less descriptively) as the $i^{\rm th}$ $L$-polynomial. For each $i$, this polynomial can be expressed in the Pontryagin classes of the manifold's [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794). The **Novikov conjecture** states that the higher signature is an invariant of the oriented homotopy type of $M$ for every such map $f$ and every such class $x$, in other words, if $h\colon M' \rightarrow M$ is an orientation preserving homotopy equivalence, the higher signature associated to $f \circ h$ is equal to that associated to $f$.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Connection with the Borel conjecture"}

## Connection with the Borel conjecture {#Connection_with_the_Borel_conjecture}

The Novikov conjecture is equivalent to the rational injectivity of the [assembly map](wikipedia://en/21311179) in [L-theory](wikipedia://en/696568). The [Borel conjecture](wikipedia://en/7181855) on the rigidity of aspherical manifolds is equivalent to the assembly map being an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828).

:::
