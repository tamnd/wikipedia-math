# Volodymyr Marchenko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volodymyr Marchenko"}

Olexandrovych

**Volodymyr Olexandrovych Marchenko** (Володимир Олександрович Марченко; 7 July 1922 – 1 January 2026) was a Ukrainian mathematician who specialised in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o2]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Marchenko was born in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv) on 7 July 1922. He defended his PhD thesis in 1948 under the supervision of [Naum Landkof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naum_Landkof), and in 1951, he defended his DSc thesis. He worked in [Kharkiv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Kharkiv) until 1961. For four decades, he headed the Mathematical Physics Department at the [Verkin Institute for Low Temperature Physics and Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkin_Institute_for_Low_Temperature_Physics_and_Engineering) of the [National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Ukraine).

He was awarded the [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) in 1962, the N.N. Krylov Prize in 1980, the [State Prize of Ukraine in Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Prize_of_Ukraine_in_Science_and_Technology) in 1989, and the N. N. Bogolyubov prize in 1996. Since 1969 he was a member of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, since 1987 of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) and since 2001 of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o2]

Marchenko [turned 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian) on 7 July 2022,[^o4] and died in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto) on 1 January 2026, at the age of 103.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

### Differential operators {#Differential_operators}

Marchenko made fundamental contributions to the analysis of the [Sturm–Liouville operators](wikipedia://en/490990). He introduced one of the approaches to the inverse scattering problem for Sturm–Liouville operators, and derived what is now called the [Marchenko equation](wikipedia://en/5644561).

### Random matrices {#Random_matrices}

Together with [Leonid Pastur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Pastur), Volodymyr Marchenko discovered the [Marchenko–Pastur law](wikipedia://en/25115911) in [random matrix theory](wikipedia://en/1648765).

### Homogenization {#Homogenization}

Together with E. Ya. Khruslov, Marchenko authored one of the first mathematical books on [homogenization](wikipedia://en/23852047).[^o10]
