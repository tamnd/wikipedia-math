# List of eponyms of special functions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of eponyms of special functions"}

This is a **list of special function eponyms** in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), to cover the theory of [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), the [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) they satisfy, named [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886) of the theory (but not intended to include every mathematical [eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym)). Named [symmetric functions](wikipedia://en/22510359), and other special polynomials, are included.

:::
