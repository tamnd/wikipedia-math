# Vafa–Witten theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vafa–Witten theorem"}

In [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028), the **Vafa–Witten theorem**, named after [Cumrun Vafa](wikipedia://en/644848) and [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894), is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) that shows that vector-like [global symmetries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_symmetry) (those that transform as expected under reflections) such as [isospin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isospin) and [baryon number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryon_number) in vector-like gauge theories like [quantum chromodynamics](wikipedia://en/25264) cannot be [spontaneously broken](wikipedia://en/297466) as long as the [theta angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_vacuum) is zero. This theorem can be proved by showing the exponential fall off of the propagator of fermions.
