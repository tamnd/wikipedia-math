# Georgy Voronoy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georgy Voronoy"}

**Georgy Feodosevich Voronoy** (Георгий Феодосьевич Вороной; Георгій Феодосійович Вороний; 28 April 1868 – 20 November 1908) was an Imperial Russian mathematician of [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) descent noted for defining the [Voronoi diagram](wikipedia://en/177668).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Voronoy was born in the village of Zhuravka, [Pyriatyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyriatyn), in the [Poltava Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poltava_Governorate), which was a part of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) at that time and is in Varva Raion, [Chernihiv Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernihiv_Oblast), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine).

Beginning in 1889, Voronoy studied at [Saint Petersburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University), where he was a student of [Andrey Markov](wikipedia://en/915). In 1894 he defended his master's thesis *On algebraic integers depending on the roots of an equation of third degree*. In the same year, Voronoy became a professor at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), where he worked on [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199). In 1897 he defended his doctoral thesis *On a generalisation of a continuous fraction*. He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1904 at [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University).[^o6]

## Works {#Works}

Voronoy introduced the concept of what we today call [Voronoi diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_diagrams) or tessellations. They are used in many areas of science, such as the analysis of spatially distributed data, having become an important topic in [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics), [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904), [condensed matter physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_matter_physics), and [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups).

These tessellations are widely used in many areas of computer graphics, from architecture to film making and video games. [Blender 3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blender_3D) includes a Voronoi texture generator as one of its main sources of randomly generated images, that can be applied as textures for many different uses.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Among his students was [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122) (Ph.D. at [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) in 1906). Although he was not formally the doctoral advisor of [Boris Delaunay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Delaunay) ([Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at [Kyiv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv)), his influence on the latter earns him the right to be considered so.[^o10]

In 2008 Ukraine released two-[hryvnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_hryvnia) coins commemorating the centenary of Voronoy's death.[^o11]

## Family {#Family}

In 1895 he had a son with his wife Olga Mytrofanivna, named [Yurii Voronyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yurii_Voronyi),[^o13] who became a prominent transplant surgeon who performed the world's first human-to-human kidney transplant in 1933,[^o14] volunteered for the army of [Central Council of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Council_of_Ukraine), and fought in the [Battle of Kruty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kruty).[^o15] His daughter Mariia Vorona-Vasylenko became a teacher of [Ukrainian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language).[^o16]
