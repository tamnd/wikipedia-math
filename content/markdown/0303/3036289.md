# Weil's conjecture on Tamagawa numbers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weil's conjecture on Tamagawa numbers"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Weil conjecture on Tamagawa numbers** is the statement that the [Tamagawa number](wikipedia://en/1168653) $\tau(G)$ of a simply connected simple [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) defined over a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) is 1. In this case, *simply connected* means "not having a proper *algebraic* covering" in the algebraic [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) sense, which is not always [the topologists' meaning](wikipedia://en/523879).

:::

## History {#History}

(txt) calculated the Tamagawa number in many cases of [classical groups](wikipedia://en/10172878) and observed that it is an [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) in all considered cases and that it was equal to 1 in the cases when the group is simply connected. The first observation does not hold for all groups: (Ono 1963) found examples where the Tamagawa numbers are not integers. The second observation, that the Tamagawa numbers of simply connected semisimple groups seem to be 1, became known as the Weil conjecture.

[Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986) (1966) introduced [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) methods to show it for [Chevalley groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group). K. F. Lai (1980) extended the class of known cases to [quasisplit reductive groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasisplit_reductive_group). (Kottwitz 1988) [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) it for all groups satisfying the [Hasse principle](wikipedia://en/489707), which at the time was known for all groups without [*E*<sub>8</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E8_%28group%29) factors. V. I. Chernousov (1989) removed this restriction, by proving the Hasse principle for the resistant *E*<sub>8</sub> case (see [strong approximation in algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_approximation_in_algebraic_groups)), thus completing the proof of Weil's conjecture. In 2011, [Jacob Lurie](wikipedia://en/26663863) and [Dennis Gaitsgory](wikipedia://en/6571030) announced a proof of the conjecture for algebraic groups over function fields over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615),[^n1] with part of the argument published in (Gaitsgory Lurie 2019), and planned to be completed in a second volume using the [Grothendieck-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck-Lefschetz_trace_formula)[Lefschetz trace formula](wikipedia://en/582530#Frobenius) and the Ran space.

::: {#o9 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

(Ono 1965) used the Weil conjecture to calculate the Tamagawa numbers of all semisimple algebraic groups.

For [spin groups](wikipedia://en/411231), the conjecture implies the known [Smith–Minkowski–Siegel mass formula](wikipedia://en/13362584).[^n1]
