# Harvey Friedman (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Harvey Friedman"}

**Harvey Friedman** (born 23 September 1948)[^o3] is an American [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636) at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in [Columbus, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio). He has worked on [reverse mathematics](wikipedia://en/326365), a project intended to derive the axioms of mathematics from the theorems considered to be necessary. In recent years, this has advanced to a study of Boolean relation theory, which attempts to justify [large cardinal](wikipedia://en/383644) [axioms](wikipedia://en/928) by demonstrating their necessity for deriving certain propositions considered "concrete".

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Friedman is the brother of mathematician [Sy Friedman](wikipedia://en/17145259). Friedman earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1967, at age 19, with a [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) on *Subsystems of Analysis*. His advisor was [Gerald Sacks](wikipedia://en/3050504). Friedman received the [Alan T. Waterman Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_T._Waterman_Award) in 1984. He also assumed the title of Visiting Scientist at [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM).[^o6] He delivered the [Tarski Lectures](wikipedia://en/54104355) in 2007.

In 1967, Friedman was listed in the *[Guinness Book of World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_Book_of_World_Records)* for being the world's youngest professor when he taught at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) at age 18 as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy).[^o3][^o7][^o8] He has also been a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and a professor of music.[^o9] He officially retired in July 2012. In September 2013, he received an honorary doctorate from [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University).[^o10]

## Career {#Career}

Friedman invented and proved important theorems regarding the finite promise games and greedy clique sequences, and [Friedman's grand conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedman's_grand_conjecture) bears his name. Friedman was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#1974,_Vancouver) at the 1974 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), with a talk titled "Some systems of second order arithmetic and their use",[^o12] which established the field of [reverse mathematics](wikipedia://en/326365) by establishing various equivalences between a number of classical theorems in [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29) in the setting of a weak background [axiomatic system](wikipedia://en/188401). According to [zbMATH Open](wikipedia://en/1701961), Friedman has published approximately 100 [peer-reviewed research articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed_article) and [conference papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_papers) during the course of his academic career to early 2025.[^o13]

Jordana Cepelewicz (2017) profiled Friedman in *[Nautilus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nautilus_%28science_magazine%29)*, describing him as "about to bring incompleteness and infinity out of quarantine.".[^o14]

Friedman also made headlines in the Italian newspaper *[La Repubblica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Repubblica)* for his manuscript *A Divine Consistency Proof for Mathematics*,[^o15] which was later published in the book *Ontology of Divinity*.[^o16] In this paper, Friedman showed that mathematics, as formalized by the usual [ZFC axioms](wikipedia://en/152214), is consistent, if one makes the strong assumption that there is an [ultrafilter](wikipedia://en/31911) with a certain property on the [universe of all sets](wikipedia://en/445980). Following the ideas in [Gödel's ontological proof](wikipedia://en/12420), such an ultrafilter is considered in Friedman's paper as a mathematical abstraction of the concept of God's existence.[^o17] Similarly to Gödel's proof, the argument is purely mathematical, and the theological interpretation of the mathematical assumption is not essential for Friedman's work.
