# Fuzzy differential inclusion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fuzzy differential inclusion"}

**Fuzzy differential inclusion** is the extension of [differential inclusion](wikipedia://en/13725281) to [fuzzy sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzzy_sets) introduced by [Lotfi A. Zadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotfi_A._Zadeh).[^o2][^o3]

:::

$x'(t) \in [ f(t , x(t))]^\alpha$ with $x(0) \in [x_0]^\alpha$

Suppose $f(t,x(t))$ is a fuzzy valued continuous function on Euclidean space. Then it is the collection of all normal, [upper semi-continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_semi-continuous), [convex](wikipedia://en/6292), [compactly](wikipedia://en/6042) supported fuzzy subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

## Second order differential {#Second_order_differential}

The second order differential is

$x''(t) \in [kx]^ \alpha$ where $k \in [K]^ \alpha$, $K$ is trapezoidal fuzzy number $(-1,-1/2,0,1/2)$, and $x_0$ is a trianglular fuzzy number (-1,0,1).

::: {#o6 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Fuzzy differential inclusion (FDI) has applications in

- [Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetics)[^o7]
- [Artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), [Neural network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network),[^o8][^o9]
- [Medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging)
- [Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotics)
- [Atmospheric dispersion modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_dispersion_modeling)
- [Weather forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting)
- [Cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone)
- [Pattern recognition](wikipedia://en/126706)
- [Population biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_biology)[^o10]
