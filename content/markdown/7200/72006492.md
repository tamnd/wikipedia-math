# Sfold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sfold"}

**Sfold** is a software program developed to predict probable [RNA secondary structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_secondary_structure) through structure-ensemble sampling and centroid predictions[^o3][^o4] with a focus on assessment of [RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA) target accessibility,[^o5] for major applications to the rational design of [siRNAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SiRNA)[^o6] in the suppression of gene expressions, and to the identification of targets for regulatory RNAs, particularly [microRNAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MicroRNA).[^o7][^o8]

:::

## Development {#Development}

The core RNA-secondary-structure-prediction algorithm is based on rigorous statistical ([stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222)) sampling of [Boltzmann ensemble](wikipedia://en/4107) of RNA secondary structures, enabling statistical characterization of any local structural features of potential interest to experimental investigators. In a review on nucleic-acid structure and prediction,[^o10] the potential of structure sampling described in a prototype algorithm[^o11] was highlighted. With the publication of the mature algorithms for Sfold,[^o3][^o4] the sampling approach became the focus of a comprehensive review,[^o12] with both the sampling approach and the centroid predictions being discussed.[^o13]

As an application module of the Sfold package, the STarMir program[^o14] has been widely used for its capability in modeling target accessibility.[^o8] STarMir was described in an independent study on microRNA target prediction,[^o15] and STarMir predictions have been used in an attempt to derive improved predictions.[^o16] Predictions by Sfold have led to new biological insights.[^o17] The novel ideas of ensemble sampling and centroids have been adopted by others not only for RNA problems, but also for other fundamental problems in [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) and [genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics).[^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22]

An implementation of stochastic sampling has been included in two widely used RNA software packages: RNA Structure[^o23] and the [ViennaRNA Package](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ViennaRNA_Package),[^o24] which are also based on the Turner RNA thermodynamic parameters.[^o25] Sfold was featured on a *Nucleic Acids Research* cover,[^o26] and was highlighted in *Science* NetWatch.[^o27] The underlying novel model for STarMir[^o14] was featured in the Cell Biology section of *Nature Research Highlights*.[^o28]

## Distribution {#Distribution}

Sfold runs under [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux), is freely available to the scientific community for non-commercial applications, and is available under license for commercial applications. Both the source code and the executables are available at [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub).
