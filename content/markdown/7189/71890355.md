# Klaus Schmitt

*Image: KlausSchmittPortrait.png.* Portrait of Professor Klaus Schmitt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klaus Schmitt"}

**Klaus Schmitt** (May 1940, [Rimbach/Odenwald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimbach,_Hesse) – April 2025 [Mesquite, Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesquite,_Nevada))[^o2] was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) doing research in [nonlinear differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear_differential_equations), and [nonlinear analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_analysis).

:::

Schmitt completed the [Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur) at Rimbach's Martin-Luther-Schule in 1960. He received a BA in mathematics and physics from [St. Olaf College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Olaf_College) in 1962, an MA (1964) and PhD in mathematics from the [University of Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) in 1967. He began his 43-year career at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1967, first as assistant, then associate, then as full professor of mathematics. He also served as chairman of the department of mathematics from 1989 to 1992.

Schmitt served short-term appointments as visiting professor at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe), [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart), [University Catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain), [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen), [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg), [University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kaiserslautern), [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), [Universidad de Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chile), [Universidad Catolica de Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Catolica_de_Chile), [National Chengchi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chengchi_University) in Taiwan, [National Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tsing_Hua_University) in Taiwan and the [Chern Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/79274968) in Tianjin, China.

He retired from the University of Utah as professor emeritus of mathematics in 2010.[^o2]

Schmitt was awarded the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in mathematics in 1978 and was honored as a University of Nebraska Distinguished Alumnus in 2000.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- "Boundary value problems for quasilinear elliptic partial differential equations", *Nonlinear Analysis*, **2** (1978), 263–309.[^o4]
- "Nonlinear elliptic boundary value problems versus their finite difference approximations: Numerically irrelevant solutions" (with [H.O. Peitgen](wikipedia://en/1562451) and D. Saupe), *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (*Crelle's Journal*), **322** (1981), 74–117.[^o5]
- "Global analysis of elliptic two parameter eigenvalue problems" (with H.O. Peitgen), *[Transactions of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/1388937)*, 283(1984), 57–95.[^o6]
- "Global aspects of the discrete and continuous Newton method: A case study" (with H.O. Peitgen and M. Prüfer), *[Acta Applicandae Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Applicandae_Mathematicae)*, **13** (1988), 123–202.[^o7]
- "On positive solutions of semilinear elliptic equations" (with [E.N. Dancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norman_Dancer)), *[Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society)*, **101** (1987), 445–452.[^o8]
- "Permanence and dynamics in biological systems" (with V. Hutson), *[Mathematical Biosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences)*, **111** (1992), 1-71.[^o9]
- "Landesman-Lazer type problems at an eigenvalue of odd multiplicity" (with [J. Mawhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Mawhin)), *[Results in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43695804)*, **14** (1988), 138–146.[^o10]
- "Positive solutions and conjugate points for systems of differential equations" (with H. Smith), *Nonlinear Analysis: Theory, Methods & Applications*, **2** (1978), 93–105.[^o11]
- "Asymptotic behavior of positive solution branches of semilinear elliptic problems with linear part at resonance" (with R. Schaaf), *[Zeitschrift für Angewandte Mathematik und Physik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeitschrift_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Physik)*, **43** (1992), 645–676.[^o12]
- "Minimization problems for noncoercive functionals subject to constraints" (with V. K. Le), *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*, **347** (1995), 4485–4513.[^o13]
- *Global Bifurcation in Variational Inequalities: Applications to Obstacle and Unilateral Problems* (with L. K. Vy), vol 123, Applied Mathematical Sciences, Springer Verlag, New York, 1997.[^o14]
- "Mountain pass type solutions of quasilinear elliptic equations" (with P. Clément, M. García-Huidobro, and R. Manásevich), *Calculus of Variations and Partial Differential Equations*, **11** (2000), 33–62.[^o15]
- "On the existence of soliton solutions to quasilinear Schrödinger equations" (with M. Poppenberg and Z. Wang), *Calculus of Variations and Partial Differential Equations*, **14** (2002), 329–344.[^o16]
- "The Liouville-Bratu-Gelfand problem for radial operators" (with J. Jacobsen), J. Differential Equations, 184(2002), 283–298.[^o17]
- "On principal eigenvalues for quasilinear elliptic differential operators: an Orlicz-Sobolev space setting" (with M. García-Huidobro, V. Le, R. Manásevich), *Nonlinear Differential Equations and Applications*, **6** (1999), 207–225.[^o18]
- "Radial solutions of quasilinear elliptic equations" (with J. Jacobsen), pp. 359–435 in *Handbook on Differential Equations*, Canada, Drabek, Fonda, editors. Elsevier, Amsterdam, 2004.[^o19]
