::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="5-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **4-simplex honeycomb**, **5-cell honeycomb** or **pentachoric-dispentachoric honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It is composed of [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) and [rectified 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2720430) facets in a ratio of 1:1.

:::

## Structure {#Structure}

Cells of the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) are ten [tetrahedrons](wikipedia://en/30606) and 20 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), corresponding to the ten [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) and 20 [rectified 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2720430) that meet at each vertex. All the vertices lie in parallel realms in which they form [alternated cubic honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternated_cubic_honeycomb), the tetrahedra being either tops of the rectified 5-cell or the bases of the 5-cell, and the octahedra being the bottoms of the rectified 5-cell.[^o7]

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cyclopentachoric tetracomb
- Pentachoric-dispentachoric tetracomb

## A4 lattice {#A4_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the *5-cell honeycomb* is called the **A4 lattice**, or **4-simplex lattice**. The 20 vertices of its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719), the [runcinated 5-cell](wikipedia://en/2386577) represent the 20 roots of the ${\tilde{A}}_4$ Coxeter group.[^o14][^o15] It is the 4-dimensional case of a [simplectic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplectic_honeycomb).

The A\* lattice[^o16] is the union of five A<sub>4</sub> lattices, and is the dual to the [omnitruncated 5-simplex honeycomb](wikipedia://en/31737675), and therefore the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of this lattice is an [omnitruncated 5-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_5-cell)

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The *tops* of the 5-cells in this honeycomb adjoin the *bases* of the 5-cells, and vice versa, in adjacent [laminae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamination_%28geology%29) (or layers); but alternating laminae may be inverted so that the tops of the rectified 5-cells adjoin the tops of the rectified 5-cells and the bases of the 5-cells adjoin the bases of other 5-cells. This inversion results in another non-Wythoffian uniform convex honeycomb. [Octahedral prisms](wikipedia://en/8813885) and [tetrahedral prisms](wikipedia://en/5977290) may be inserted in between alternated laminae as well, resulting in two more non-Wythoffian elongated uniform honeycombs.[^o18]

The **cyclotruncated 4-simplex honeycomb** or **cyclotruncated 5-cell honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can also be seen as a **birectified 5-cell honeycomb**.

It is composed of [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821), [truncated 5-cells](wikipedia://en/4046891), and [bitruncated 5-cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-cell) facets in a ratio of 2:2:1. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [tetrahedral antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedral_antiprism), with 2 [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521), 8 [triangular pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_pyramid), and 6 [tetragonal disphenoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetragonal_disphenoid) cells, defining 2 [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821), 8 [truncated 5-cell](wikipedia://en/4046891), and 6 [bitruncated 5-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-cell) facets around a vertex.

It can be constructed as five sets of parallel [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862) that divide space into two half-spaces. The 3-space hyperplanes contain [quarter cubic honeycombs](wikipedia://en/3757507) as a collection facets.[^o30]

#### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cyclotruncated pentachoric tetracomb
- Small truncated-pentachoric tetracomb

The **omnitruncated 4-simplex honeycomb** or **omnitruncated 5-cell honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654). It can also be seen as a **cyclosteriruncicantitruncated 5-cell honeycomb**.

It is composed entirely of [omnitruncated 5-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_5-cell) (omnitruncated 4-simplex) facets.

[Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) calls this **Hinton's honeycomb** after [C. H. Hinton](wikipedia://en/562977), who described it in his book *The Fourth Dimension* in 1906.[^o55]

The facets of all [omnitruncated simplectic honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_simplectic_honeycomb) are called [permutohedra](wikipedia://en/12745947) and can be positioned in *n+1* space with integral coordinates, permutations of the whole numbers (0,1,...,n).

#### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Omnitruncated cyclopentachoric tetracomb
- Great-prismatodecachoric tetracomb

#### A<sub>4</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice {#A4*_lattice}

The A\* lattice is the union of five A<sub>4</sub> lattices, and is the dual to the omnitruncated 5-cell honeycomb, and therefore the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of this lattice is an [omnitruncated 5-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_5-cell).[^o58]

## Alternated form {#Alternated_form}

This honeycomb can be [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117), creating [omnisnub 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2386577#Full_snub_5-cell) with irregular [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) created at the deleted vertices. Although it is not uniform, the 5-cells have a symmetry of order 10.
