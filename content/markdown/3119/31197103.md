# Ahlfors measure conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ahlfors measure conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Ahlfors conjecture**, now a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977), states that the [limit set](wikipedia://en/5492198) of a [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) [Kleinian group](wikipedia://en/1362652) is either the whole [Riemann sphere](wikipedia://en/30876799), or has [measure zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_zero).

:::

The [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) was introduced by [Lars Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636),[^o2] who [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) it in the case that the Kleinian group has a [fundamental domain](wikipedia://en/469365) with a finite number of sides. [Richard Canary](wikipedia://en/46914909) proved the Ahlfors conjecture for [topologically tame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologically_tame) groups,[^o3] by showing that a topologically tame Kleinian group is geometrically tame, so the Ahlfors conjecture follows from Marden's [tameness conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tameness_conjecture) that [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700) with finitely generated fundamental groups are topologically tame ([homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to the interior of compact 3-manifolds). This latter conjecture was proved, independently, by [Ian Agol](wikipedia://en/32084718)[^o4] and by [Danny Calegari](wikipedia://en/21907908) and [David Gabai](wikipedia://en/6129609).[^o5]

Canary also showed that in the case when the limit set is the whole sphere, the action of the Kleinian group on the limit set is ergodic.[^o3]
