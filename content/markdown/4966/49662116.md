# Trudy Govier

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Trudy Govier"}

**Trudy Rose Govier** (born August 3, 1944) is a Canadian philosopher known for her work in [informal logic](wikipedia://en/14122398) and [argumentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argumentation). She is the author of the influential text *A Practical Study of Argument*. She has also been a frequent commentator in Canadian media on issues related to violence and conflict resolution.[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Govier received her PhD in philosophy from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 1971. She has taught philosophy at [Trent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trent_University), [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University), the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary), the [University of Lethbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lethbridge), and the [University of Winnipeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Winnipeg).[^o4] Much of Govier's work has surrounded practical argument, focusing on issues of trust, forgiveness, and reconciliation. She has been a distinguished visiting researcher with the [Centre for Research in Reasoning, Argumentation, and Rhetoric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Research_in_Reasoning,_Argumentation,_and_Rhetoric) at the [University of Windsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Windsor).[^o6] She retired from teaching in 2012.[^o7]

The nature of Govier's approach to argumentation has prompted her to engage practically with social issues outside of academia. She is a founding member of the Calgary chapter of the peace activist organization [Project Ploughshares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Ploughshares)[^o4] and has been involved in labour advocacy.[^o8]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Taking Wrongs Seriously: Acknowledgement, Reconciliation, and the Politics of Sustainable Peace* (Amherst, NY: Humanity Books, 2006)
- *A Delicate Balance: What Philosophy Can Tell Us About Terrorism* (Boulder, CO: Westview Press, 2002)
- *Forgiveness and Revenge* (London: Routledge, 2002)
- *The Philosophy of Argument* (Newport News, VA: Vale Press, 1999)
- *Dilemmas of Trust* (Montreal & Kingston: McGill-Queen's University Press, 1998)
- *Social Trust and Human Communities* Montreal & Kingston: McGill-Queen's University Press, 1997)
- *Socrates' Children* (Peterborough, ON: Broadview Press, 1997)
- *God, the Devil, and the Perfect Pizza.* (Peterborough, ON: Broadview Press, 1989)
- *Problems in Argument Evaluation and Analysis* (Dordrecht, NL and Berlin: Foris/deGruyter, 1987)
