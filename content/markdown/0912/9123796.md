# Journal of Physics A

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Physics A"}

The ***Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by IOP Publishing, the publishing branch of the [Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Physics). It is part of the *[Journal of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Physics_%28disambiguation%29)* series and covers [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) focusing on sophisticated mathematical and computational techniques.

:::

The journal is divided into six sections covering: [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics); [chaotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_%28physics%29) and [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_systems); [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416); [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) and [quantum information theory](wikipedia://en/25274); [classical](wikipedia://en/1293340) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267); [fluid](wikipedia://en/2684988) and [plasma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_%28physics%29) theory.

The [editor in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor_in_chief) is Joseph A Minahan ([Uppsala Universitet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), Sweden). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2025 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.9.[^o4]

## History {#History}

*Journal of Physics A* was established in 1968 as one of the subdivisions of the earlier title, *[Proceedings of the Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_Physical_Society)*, established in 1874, the flagship journal of the [Physical Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Society_of_London). The Physical Society later became the Institute of Physics, the current publisher of the journal. Its papers began being made available electronically in 1991; by 2002, its entire back archive had been digitised, as the first step in a larger project to digitise all of the Institute's publishing archives.[^o6]
