# Method of supplementary variables

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Method of supplementary variables"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), the **method of supplementary variables** is a technique to solve for the stationary distribution of an [M/G/1 queue](wikipedia://en/33378064). It was introduced by [David Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771)[^o2] and [David George Kendall](wikipedia://en/4631370).[^o3]

:::
