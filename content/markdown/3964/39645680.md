# Decomposition method (queueing theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decomposition method"}

In [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578), a discipline within the mathematical [theory of probability](wikipedia://en/23542), the **decomposition method** is an approximate method for the analysis of queueing networks where the network is broken into subsystems which are independently analyzed.[^o2][^o3]

:::

The individual queueing nodes are considered to be independent [G/G/1 queues](wikipedia://en/38677129) where arrivals are governed by a [renewal process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewal_process) and both service time and arrival distributions are parametrised to match the first two moments of data.
