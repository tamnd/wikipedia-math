# Barsotti–Tate group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barsotti–Tate group"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Barsotti–Tate groups** or ***p*-divisible groups** are similar to the points of order a power of *p* on an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) in characteristic *p*. They were introduced by (txt) under the name equidimensional hyperdomain and by (txt) under the name p-divisible groups, and named Barsotti–Tate groups by (Grothendieck 1971).

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

(Tate 1967) defined a *p*-divisible group of height *h* (over a scheme *S*) to be an inductive system of groups *G*<sub>*n*</sub> for *n*≥0, such that *G*<sub>*n*</sub> is a finite group scheme over *S* of order *p*<sup>*hn*</sup> and such that *G*<sub>*n*</sub> is (identified with) the group of elements of order divisible by *p*<sup>*n*</sup> in *G*<sub>*n*+1</sub>.

More generally, (Grothendieck 1971) defined a Barsotti–Tate group *G* over a scheme *S* to be an [fppf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fppf_topology) sheaf of commutative groups over *S* that is *p*-divisible, *p*-torsion, such that the points *G*(1) of order *p* of *G* are (represented by) a finite locally free scheme. The group *G*(1) has rank *p*<sup>*h*</sup> for some locally constant function *h* on *S*, called the **rank** or **height** of the group *G*. The subgroup *G*(*n*) of points of order *p*<sup>*n*</sup> is a scheme of rank *p*<sup>*nh*</sup>, and *G* is the direct limit of these subgroups.

:::

::: {#o10 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

- Take *G*<sub>*n*</sub> to be the cyclic group of order *p*<sup>*n*</sup> (or rather the group scheme corresponding to it). This is a *p*-divisible group of height 1.
- Take *G*<sub>*n*</sub> to be the group scheme of *p*<sup>*n*</sup>th roots of 1. This is a *p*-divisible group of height 1.
- Take *G*<sub>*n*</sub> to be the subgroup scheme of elements of order *p*<sup>*n*</sup> of an abelian variety. This is a *p*-divisible group of height 2*d* where *d* is the dimension of the Abelian variety.

:::
