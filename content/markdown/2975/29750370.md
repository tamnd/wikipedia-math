# Quadratic pair

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quadratic pair"}

In mathematical finite group theory, a **quadratic pair** for the odd [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*, introduced by (Thompson 1971), is a finite [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* together with a **quadratic module**, a [faithful representation](wikipedia://en/2617872) *M* on a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with *p* elements such that *G* is generated by elements with [minimal polynomial](wikipedia://en/9667107) (*x* − 1)<sup>2</sup>. Thompson classified the quadratic pairs for *p* ≥ 5. (Chermak 2004) classified the quadratic pairs for *p* = 3. With a few exceptions, especially for *p* = 3, groups with a quadratic pair for the prime *p* tend to be more or less [groups of Lie type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_Lie_type) in characteristic *p*.

:::
