# Michelle L. Wachs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michelle L. Wachs"}

*Image: Michelle L Wachs Berkeley 2013.jpg.* [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), 2013

**Michelle Lynn Wachs** is an American mathematician who specializes in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami).[^o2]

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Wachs and her advisor [Adriano Garsia](wikipedia://en/18857510) are the namesakes of the [Garsia–Wachs algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garsia–Wachs_algorithm) for [optimal binary search trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_binary_search_tree), which they published in 1977.A She is also known for her research on [shellings](wikipedia://en/32457161) for [simplicial complexes](wikipedia://en/252686),F [partially ordered sets](wikipedia://en/23572),C and [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004),B and on [random permutation statistics](wikipedia://en/4915182)E and [set partition](wikipedia://en/340240) statistics.D

## Education {#Education}

Wachs earned her doctorate in 1977 from the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), under the supervision of [Adriano Garsia](wikipedia://en/18857510). Her dissertation was *Discrete Variational Techniques in Finite Mathematics*.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012 Wachs became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). In 2013 she and her husband, mathematician Gregory Galloway (the chair of the mathematics department at Miami) were recognized as [Simons Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation). A conference in her honor was held in January 2015 at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami).
