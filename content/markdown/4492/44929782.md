# Carol Wood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carol Wood"}

**Carol Saunders Wood** (born February 9, 1945)[^o3] is a retired American mathematician, the [Edward Burr Van Vleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Burr_Van_Vleck) Professor of Mathematics, *Emerita*, at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University).[^o4] Her research concerns [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [model-theoretic](wikipedia://en/19858) [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384),[^o5] and in particular the theory of [differentially closed fields](wikipedia://en/10470883).[^o6]

:::

Wood graduated in 1966 from [Randolph-Macon Woman's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_College), a small [United Methodist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Methodist_Church) college in [Lynchburg, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynchburg,_Virginia).[^o5] She earned her doctorate in 1971 from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) with a dissertation on [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205) supervised by [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024). At Wesleyan, she served three times as department chair.[^o3] She was an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Council member at large from 1987 to 1989.[^o8] She was president of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from 1991 to 1993,[^o5] and served on the board of trustees of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) from 2002 to 2007.[^o3] She has served on the AMS Committee on Women in Mathematics since it was formed in 2012 and was chair from 2012 to 2015.[^o9] She supervised 4 doctoral students at Wesleyan.[^o7]

Wood was the 1998 commencement speaker for mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o10] In 2012, she became one of the inaugural fellows of the American Mathematical Society.[^o11] In 2017, she was selected as a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the inaugural class.[^o12]
