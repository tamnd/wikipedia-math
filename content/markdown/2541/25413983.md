# Chen Chung Chang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chen Chung Chang"}

**Chen Chung Chang** () was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). Typically known by his initials "C.C." he obtained his PhD from [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley) in 1955 on "Cardinal and Ordinal Factorization of Relation Types" under [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920). He then became a professor at the mathematics department of the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), where he remained for the rest of his career.[^o3]

:::

Chang wrote the standard text (Chang Keisler 1990) on model theory. [Chang's conjecture](wikipedia://en/25403351) and [Chang's model](wikipedia://en/43565537) are named after him. He also proved the ordinal partition theorem (expressed in the [arrow notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrow_notation_%28Ramsey_theory%29) for [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175)) ω<sup>ω</sup>→(ω<sup>ω</sup>,3)<sup>2</sup>, originally a problem of [Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Hajnal](wikipedia://en/21765779). He also introduced [MV-algebras](wikipedia://en/2483815) as models for [Łukasiewicz logic](wikipedia://en/11001950).
