# Eldon Hansen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eldon Hansen"}

**Eldon Robert Hansen** (born 1927) is an American mathematician and author who has published in global optimization theory and [interval arithmetic](wikipedia://en/2176160).

:::

Hansen was born on July 16, 1927 in [Rochester, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_Washington). After receiving bachelor's and master's degrees at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1950 and 1952, and working at the [University of California Radiation Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_National_Laboratory) from 1952 until 1956,[^o2] he received his Ph.D. in mathematics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1960 with a dissertation on [Jacobi methods](wikipedia://en/4047104) and Block-Jacobi methods for computing [matrix eigenvalues](wikipedia://en/516560), supervised by [George Forsythe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Forsythe). He worked for the [Lockheed Missiles and Space Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Missiles_and_Space_Company) beginning in 1956.[^o2] He later held a position at [Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_University),[^o4] and supervised a doctoral student there.[^o3]

His books include:

- *Global Optimization Using Interval Analysis* (Marcel Dekker, 1992; 2nd ed. with William G. Walster, 2004)[^o5]
- *A Table of Series and Products* (Prentice-Hall, 1975)[^o6]
- *Topics in Interval Analysis* (edited, Clarendon Press, 1969)[^o7]
