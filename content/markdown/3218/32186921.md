# Rod group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rod group"}

In mathematics, a **rod group** is a three-dimensional [line group](wikipedia://en/32186226) whose [point group](wikipedia://en/1138942) is one of the axial [crystallographic point groups](wikipedia://en/663786). This constraint means that the point group must be the symmetry of some three-dimensional lattice.

:::
