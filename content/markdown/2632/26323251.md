# Shmuel Gal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shmuel Gal"}

**Shmuel Gal** (שמואל גל; born 1940) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) in Israel.

:::

He devised the [Gal's accurate tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gal's_accurate_tables) method for the computer evaluation of elementary functions.[^o3][^o4] With Zvi Yehudai he developed in 1993 a new algorithm for [sorting](wikipedia://en/28442) which is used by IBM.[^o5]

Gal has solved the [Princess and monster game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_and_monster_game)[^o6] and made several significant contributions to the area of [search games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_games).[^o7][^o8][^o9]

He has been working on [rendezvous problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendezvous_problem) with his collaborative colleagues [Steve Alpern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Alpern), Vic Baston, and John Howard.[^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13]

Gal received a Ph.D. in mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). His thesis advisor was [Aryeh Dvoretzky](wikipedia://en/19901447).
