# Gerald Teschl

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gerald Teschl"}

**Gerald Teschl** (born 12 May 1970 in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz)) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He works in the area of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416); in particular direct and inverse [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) with application to completely integrable [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) ([soliton](wikipedia://en/51654) equations).

:::

## Career {#Career}

After studying [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology) (diploma thesis 1993), he continued with a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri). The title of his thesis supervised by [Fritz Gesztesy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Gesztesy) was *Spectral Theory for Jacobi Operators* (1995). After a [postdoctoral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral) position at the [Rheinisch-Westfälischen Technische Hochschule Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen) (1996/97), he moved to Vienna, where he received his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Wien) in May 1998. Since then he has been a professor of mathematics there.

In 1997 he received the [Ludwig Boltzmann Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Boltzmann_Prize) from the [Austrian Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Physical_Society), 1999 the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384).[^o4] In 2006 he was awarded with the prestigious [START-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-Preis) by the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund) (FWF). In 2011 he became a member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences) (ÖAW).

His most important contributions are to the fields of [Sturm–Liouville theory](wikipedia://en/490990), [Jacobi operators](wikipedia://en/28868152) and the [Toda lattice](wikipedia://en/3240033). He also works in [biomathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomathematics), in particular in the novel area of [breath gas analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breath_gas_analysis), and has written a successful undergraduate textbook (Mathematics for Computer Science, in [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language)) with his wife [Susanne Teschl](wikipedia://en/68623746).
