# David Lovelock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Lovelock"}

**David Lovelock** (born 1938) is a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) theoretical [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for the [Lovelock theory of gravity](wikipedia://en/30031074) and [Lovelock's theorem](wikipedia://en/52823070).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
