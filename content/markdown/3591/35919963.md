# Exeter point

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Exeter point"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Exeter point** is a special point associated with a plane [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) and is designated as *X*(22)[^o2] in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593). This was discovered in a computers-in-mathematics workshop at [Phillips Exeter Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillips_Exeter_Academy) in 1986.[^o3] This is one of the recent triangle centers, unlike the classical triangle centers like [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926), [incenter](wikipedia://en/316837), and [Steiner point](wikipedia://en/35857329).[^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: Exeter point.svg.* solid black dashed black 2px solid cyan solid #2fc437 solid magenta solid red

The Exeter point is defined as follows.[^o3][^o7]

- Let $\triangle ABC$ be any given triangle.
- Let the [medians](wikipedia://en/1072006) through the vertices $A$, $B$, $C$ meet the [circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432) of $\triangle ABC$ at $A'$, $B'$, $C'$ respectively.
- Let $\triangle DEF$ be [the triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangential_triangle) formed by the [tangents](wikipedia://en/31482) at $A$, $B$, $C$ to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$. (Let $D$ be the vertex opposite to the side formed by the tangent at the vertex $A$, $E$ be the vertex opposite to the side formed by the tangent at the vertex $B$, and $F$ be the vertex opposite to the side formed by the tangent at the vertex $C$.)
- The lines through $DA'$, $EB'$, $FC'$ are [concurrent](wikipedia://en/2206157). The point of concurrence is the *Exeter point* of $\triangle ABC$.

Put succinctly, the Exeter point is the [perspector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspector) of the **circummedial triangle** and the [tangential triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangential_triangle).

:::

## Trilinear coordinates {#Trilinear_coordinates}

The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the Exeter point are

$$a(b^4 + c^4 - a^4) : b(c^4 + a^4 - b^4) : c(a^4 + b^4 - c^4)$$

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The Exeter point of triangle *ABC* lies on the [Euler line](wikipedia://en/203622) (the line passing through the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926), the [orthocenter](wikipedia://en/161244), the [de Longchamps point](wikipedia://en/14593776), the [Euler centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_centre) and the [circumcenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumcenter)) of triangle *ABC*. So there are 6 points collinear on a single line.

:::
