# Derek J. S. Robinson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Derek J. S. Robinson"}

**Derek John Scott Robinson** (born 25 September 1938 in [Montrose, Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montrose,_Scotland))[^o2] is a British mathematician, specialising in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Robinson graduated in 1960 with a bachelor's degree from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and in 1963 with a Ph.D. from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o3] His Ph.D. thesis *Theory of Subnormal Subgroups* was supervised by [Philip Hall](wikipedia://en/1655033). As a postdoc, Robinson was from 1963 to 1965 an instructor at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign). From 1965 to 1968 he was a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at [Queen Mary College (now named Queen Mary University of London)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_University_of_London). At the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign he was an assistant professor from 1968 to 1969, an associate professor from 1969 to 1974, and a full professor from 1974 to 2007, when he retired as professor emeritus. He held visiting appointments in Switzerland, Italy, Germany, and Singapore.[^o3]

Robinson's 1964 paper on [T-groups](wikipedia://en/10545261)[^o6] has over 250 citations. He was awarded in 1970 the [Sir Edmund Whittaker Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/4579429) and received in 1979 a [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o3]
