# International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics"}

The **International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics** (**FPSAC**) is an annual [academic conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) in the areas of [algebraic](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533) and their applications and relations with other areas of mathematics, physics, biology and computer science.[^o3]

:::

## History {#History}

FPSAC was first held in 1988 and has been held annually since 1990, typically in June or July.[^o5]

The most recent conference in the series, FPSAC 2026, was held in July 2026 at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle), Washington, USA.[^o6] The next conference is slated to take place July 5-9, 2027 at [University of Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Galway) in [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway), Ireland.[^o7]

The proceedings of conferences in the series have appeared variously as books published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o8] and [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media),[^o9] and as issues in the journals [Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21988115),[^o10] [Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Computer_Science),[^o11] and [Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Séminaire_Lotharingien_de_Combinatoire).[^o12]

Invited speakers at previous FPSAC conferences include [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [June Huh](wikipedia://en/54434626), [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) and [Wolf Prize](wikipedia://en/714278) recipient [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651), [Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) recipient [Richard Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397), and [Wolf Prize](wikipedia://en/714278) and [Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) recipient [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643).[^o13]
