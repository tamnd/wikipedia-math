# Krishnaswami Alladi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Krishnaswami Alladi"}

**Krishnaswami Alladi** (born October 5, 1955) is an Indian-American mathematician who specializes in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida), and was chair of the mathematics department there from 1998 to 2008.[^o3] He is also the editor-in-chief of *[The Ramanujan Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ramanujan_Journal)* (published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media)), which he founded in 1997.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Alladi was born in [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram), and did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras), graduating in 1975.[^o3] While still an undergraduate, he wrote to [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) concerning his research on the function that maps each integer to the sum of its [prime factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_factor) (with repetition); Erdős came to Madras to meet him, and their collaboration on this subject became Alladi's first paper;[^o5][^o6] the two mathematicians coauthored 5 papers in total.[^o7] He earned his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1978 under the supervision of [Ernst G. Straus](wikipedia://en/6920898).[^o3] After visiting the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), and the [University of Hawaiʻi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaii), he became an associate professor in 1981 at the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai](wikipedia://en/2718996), which had been founded by his father [Alladi Ramakrishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alladi_Ramakrishnan). He moved to the University of Florida in 1986.[^o3]

In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
