# Luchezar Avramov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Luchezar Avramov"}

**Luchezar L. Avramov** (Лъчезар Л. Аврамов) is a Bulgarian-American mathematician who works in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He held the Dale M. Jensen Chair in Mathematics at the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln), and is now an Emeritus.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Avramov was educated at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), earning a master's degree in 1970, a Ph.D. in 1975 (under the supervision of [Evgeny Golod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeny_Golod)), and a D.Sc. in 1986. He worked for the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences in 1970–1981 and 1989–1990, and [Sofia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_University) in 1981–1989, before moving to the United States in 1991 to become a professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). He moved again to the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in 2002.[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, he became a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in its inaugural class.[^o8]
