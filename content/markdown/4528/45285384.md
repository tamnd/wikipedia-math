# Mónica Clapp

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mónica Clapp"}

**Mónica Alicia Clapp Jiménez Labora** is a mathematician at the [Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM) known for her work in [nonlinear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/16678376) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Clapp was born in [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City).[^o4] She graduated from UNAM in 1974. Clapp then graduated with her [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D) from [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) in 1979, and has been a faculty member at UNAM since that time.

Clapp has been an editor of the journals [Boletín de la Sociedad Matemática Mexicana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boletín_de_la_Sociedad_Matemática_Mexicana) and [Aportaciones Matemáticas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aportaciones_Matemáticas).[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Clapp became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] She is also a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o8]

In 2018, she received the [National Prize for Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Prize_for_Arts_and_Sciences) in the *Physics, Mathematics, and Natural Sciences* category, "for his outstanding contribution in the fields of nonlinear partial differential equations, variational and topological methods in nonlinear analysis, as well as algebraic topology."[^o9][^o10]
