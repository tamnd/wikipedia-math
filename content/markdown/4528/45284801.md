# Maria-Carme Calderer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Maria-Carme Calderer"}

**Maria-Carme T. Calderer** ([Berga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berga), 1951)[^o3] is a professor of mathematics at [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Her research concerns [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Calderer received her Ph.D. from [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in 1980. She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088) from 1984 to 1987, first as a postdoctoral researcher, and then as a visiting professor.[^o6] She worked at [Penn State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State) from 1989 until 2001, when she joined the faculty of [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2000 Calderer received the Teresa Cohen Service Award from [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University).[^o8]

In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

In 2022 she will become a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), "For being a role model nationally and internationally due to her outstanding research contributions in the mathematics of materials; for her long record of mentoring, advising, and supervising women in applied mathematics; and for her leadership role in the mathematics community by organizing conferences, workshops, and thematic years."[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Calderer was raised in [Berga, Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berga,_Spain).[^o12] She is married to [Douglas Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Arnold), one of her fellow professors of mathematics at [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).
