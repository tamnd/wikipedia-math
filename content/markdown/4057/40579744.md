# Jesús María Sanz-Serna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jesús María Sanz-Serna"}

**Jesús María Sanz-Serna** (born 12 June 1953 in [Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valladolid), Spain) is a mathematician who specializes in applied mathematics. Sanz-Serna pioneered the field of [geometric integration](wikipedia://en/2208188) and wrote the first book on this subject.[^o2] From 1998 to 2006, he was rector of the [University of Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valladolid).[^o3]

:::

He received the inaugural Dahlquist Prize from the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 1995[^o4] and became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o5] His 60th birthday was celebrated at the 2013 International Conference on Scientific Computation and Differential Equations (SciCADE) in Valladolid.[^o6]
