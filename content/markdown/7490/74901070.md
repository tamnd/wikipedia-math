# Robert L. Causey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert L. Causey"}

**Robert L. Causey** is an American philosopher and professor emeritus of Philosophy at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). Causey is known for his works on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Books {#Books}

- *Unity of Science* (Synthese Library Volume 109). D. Reidel Publishing Co., Dordrecht and Boston, 1977. Now distributed by Springer Verlag
- *Logic, Sets, and Recursion*, Jones and Bartlett Pub., Boston, 1994, 2nd Edition 2006
