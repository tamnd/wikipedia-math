# Edwin A. Maxwell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edwin A. Maxwell"}

**Edwin Arthur Maxwell** (12 January 1907 – 27 August 1987) was a Scottish mathematician, who worked at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) for most of his career. Although his contributions to original research were limited, his main contribution was in the area of mathematical education,[^o2] including his 1959 work *[Fallacies in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallacies_in_Mathematics)*. His doctoral supervisor was [H.F. Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.F._Baker). He was president of the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403).

:::

He is buried in the [Parish of the Ascension Burial Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascension_Parish_Burial_Ground) in Cambridge.
