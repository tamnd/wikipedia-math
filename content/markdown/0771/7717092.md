# Steve Hofmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Hofmann"}

**Steve Hofmann** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who helped solve the famous [Kato's conjecture](wikipedia://en/3543381). Said Hofmann, “It's a problem that has interested me since I was a graduate student... It was one of the biggest open problems in my field and everybody thought it was too hard and wouldn't be solved. I had toyed with it for years and then put in three years of very serious work before hitting the key breakthrough.”<https://web.archive.org/web/20070224133836/http://www.missouri.edu/academics/accomplishments/hofmann.php>

:::

Hofmann, Curators' professor at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri), worked alongside other prominenent mathematicians ([Pascal Auscher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Auscher), [Michael Lacey](wikipedia://en/6508239), John Lewis, [Alan McIntosh](wikipedia://en/57297191) and Philippe Tchamitchian) to solve this problem, one that was put into place in the early 1950s by [Tosio Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosio_Kato), a Mathematician at The [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley). Hofmann received his PhD from the [University of Minnesota, Twin Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).

He delivered an invited address at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid.[^o2] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
