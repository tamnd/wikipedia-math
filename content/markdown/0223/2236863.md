# List of properties of sets of reals

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of properties of sets of reals"}

This article lists some properties of [sets](wikipedia://en/26691) of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438). The general study of these concepts forms [descriptive set theory](wikipedia://en/396619), which has a rather different emphasis from [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649).

:::
