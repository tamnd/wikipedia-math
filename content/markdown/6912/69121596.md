# Emilio Gagliardo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emilio Gagliardo"}

**Emilio Gagliardo** (5 November 1930, [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa) – 15 August 2008, [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the field of [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He did his PhD in [Algebraic Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Geometry) at the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa) with Eugenio Togliatti and graduated in 1953.He then became an assistant of [Guido Stampacchia](wikipedia://en/4872667) and started to study [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). In 1959 he got his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) and spend some time abroad with [Nachman Aronszajn](wikipedia://en/13496987) at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) and with [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500) in Nancy. In 1961 he became Professor in Genoa. From 1968 to 1975 he was at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) and since 1975 at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia).

His main contributions are to the field of [parabolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/7466971), [interpolation](wikipedia://en/18587912) in [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces), and [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964).

1964 was awarded the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352).
