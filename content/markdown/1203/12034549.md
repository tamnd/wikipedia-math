# Morita conjectures

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morita conjectures"}

The **Morita conjectures** in [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649) are certain problems about [normal spaces](wikipedia://en/48629), now solved in the affirmative. The [conjectures](wikipedia://en/6138), formulated by [Kiiti Morita](wikipedia://en/12034472) in 1976, ask

:::

1. If $X \times Y$ is normal for every normal space *Y*, is *X* a [discrete space](wikipedia://en/56061)?
1. If $X \times Y$ is normal for every normal [P-space](wikipedia://en/41775284#P-spaces_in_the_sense_of_Morita) *Y*, is *X* [metrizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable)?[^o2]
1. If $X \times Y$ is normal for every normal countably [paracompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact) space *Y*, is *X* metrizable and [sigma-locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma-locally_compact)?

The answers were believed to be affirmative. Here a **normal P-space** *Y* is characterised by the property that the product with every metrizable *X* is normal; thus the conjecture was that the [converse](wikipedia://en/155941) holds.

Keiko Chiba, Teodor C. Przymusiński, and [Mary Ellen Rudin](wikipedia://en/2976742)[^o3] [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) conjecture (1) and showed that conjectures (2) and (3) cannot be proven false under the standard [ZFC](wikipedia://en/152214) axioms for mathematics (specifically, that the conjectures hold under the [axiom of constructibility](wikipedia://en/696472) *V=L*).

Fifteen years later, [Zoltán Tibor Balogh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoltán_Tibor_Balogh) succeeded in showing that conjectures (2) and (3) are true.[^o4]
