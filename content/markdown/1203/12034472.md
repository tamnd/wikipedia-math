# Kiiti Morita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kiiti Morita"}

**Kiiti Morita** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

Morita was born in 1915 in [Hamamatsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamamatsu), [Shizuoka Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shizuoka_Prefecture) and graduated from the [Tokyo Higher Normal School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tsukuba) in 1936. Three years later he was appointed assistant at the [Tokyo University of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University_of_Science).[^o3] He received his Ph.D. from [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Prefecture_University) in 1950, with a thesis in topology.[^o3] After teaching at the Tokyo Higher Normal School, he became professor at the [University of Tsukuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tsukuba) in 1951. He held this position until 1978, after which he taught at [Sophia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_University).[^o3][^o5] Morita died of heart failure in 1995 at the Sakakibara Heart Institute in [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo);[^o5] he was survived by his wife, Tomiko, his son, Yasuhiro, and a grandson.[^o5][^o6]

He introduced the concepts now known as [Morita equivalence](wikipedia://en/3296134) and [Morita duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morita_duality) which were given wide circulation in the 1960s by [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910) in a series of lectures. The [Morita conjectures](wikipedia://en/12034549) on [normal topological spaces](wikipedia://en/48629) are also named after him.
