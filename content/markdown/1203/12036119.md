# Gromov's inequality for complex projective space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gromov's inequality for complex projective space"}

In [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), [Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29)'s optimal stable 2-[systolic](wikipedia://en/2545455) inequality is the inequality

:::

$$\mathrm{stsys}_2{}^n \leq n! \;\mathrm{vol}_{2n}(\mathbb{CP}^n),$$

valid for an arbitrary Riemannian metric on the [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977), where the optimal bound is attained by the symmetric [Fubini–Study metric](wikipedia://en/2574486), providing a natural geometrisation of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202). Here $\operatorname{stsys_2}$ is the stable 2-systole, which in this case can be defined as the infimum of the areas of rational 2-cycles representing the class of the complex projective line $\mathbb{CP}^1 \subset \mathbb{CP}^n$ in 2-dimensional homology.

The inequality first appeared in (Gromov 1981) as Theorem 4.36.

The proof of Gromov's inequality relies on the [Wirtinger inequality for exterior 2-forms](wikipedia://en/12096417).

## Projective planes over division algebras $\mathbb{R,C,H}$ {#Projective_planes_over_division_algebras_\mathbb{R,C,H}}

In the special case n=2, Gromov's inequality becomes $\mathrm{stsys}_2{}^2 \leq 2 \mathrm{vol}_4(\mathbb{CP}^2)$. This inequality can be thought of as an analog of [Pu's inequality for the real projective plane](wikipedia://en/12004717) $\mathbb{RP}^2$. In both cases, the boundary case of equality is attained by the symmetric metric of the projective plane. Meanwhile, in the quaternionic case, the symmetric metric on $\mathbb{HP}^2$ is not its systolically optimal metric. In other words, the manifold $\mathbb{HP}^2$ admits Riemannian metrics with higher systolic ratio $\mathrm{stsys}_4{}^2/\mathrm{vol}_8$ than for its symmetric metric (Bangert Katz Shnider Weinberger 2009).
