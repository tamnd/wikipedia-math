# Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter"}

**Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter** (born 21 May 1959) is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrians) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and professor of applied statistics and econometrics at the [Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Economics_and_Business). She is known for her research in [Bayesian analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_analysis). In 2020 she was the President of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter was born in 1959 in the [Brigittenau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigittenau) district of Vienna. After attaining her doctorate in [engineering mathematics](wikipedia://en/5381408) from the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) she held numerous academic positions, including professor of statistics at the [Johannes Kepler University Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kepler_University_Linz). Since 2011, she is full professor of statistics at the [Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Economics_and_Business).[^o5] Since 2014 she is Full Member of the Division of Humanities and the Social Sciences of the Austrian Academy of Sciences. Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter is married and mother of three sons.

## Research {#Research}

In her research, Sylvia Frühwirth-Schnatter inter alia explores ideas relating to [Bayesian econometrics](wikipedia://en/20484367), such as efficient [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) methods and [Bayesian analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_analysis) of finite [mixture models](wikipedia://en/871681). In 2014, she co-developed a Bayesian approach to [exploratory factor analysis](wikipedia://en/24688832) with [James Heckman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Heckman).[^o7] She is a quadruple winner of the [WU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Economics_and_Business) Best Paper Award[^o8] and recipient of the DeGroot Prize[^o9] bestowed by the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) for her monograph on [Markov switching models](wikipedia://en/236801).
