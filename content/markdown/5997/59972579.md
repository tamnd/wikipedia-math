# Gudrun Kalmbach

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gudrun Kalmbach"}

**Gudrun Kalmbach** (27 May 1937 – 25 July 2025) was a German mathematician and educator known for her contributions in the field of [quantum logic](wikipedia://en/663426) and for the educational programmes she developed.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

After teacher training in mathematics and chemistry, Kalmbach obtained a PhD at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) under the supervision of [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031) on the topic of [low-dimensional](wikipedia://en/621774) [CW complexes](wikipedia://en/353022) in non-[compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470).[^o4]

From 1967 to 1969, Kalmbach taught at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign). After this she held positions as assistant professor at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst) (1970–1971) and [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) (1969–1975). In 1975, she qualified as professor at the [University of Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ulm), becoming the first female professor in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) (at the chair of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542)).[^o5] She worked there until her retirement in 2002.

Kalmbach's research focused on [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), [lattice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_theory) and quantum structures.[^o6]

In addition to her research career, Kalmbach invented in 1985 the talent program in [STEM](wikipedia://en/3437663) subjects (called MINT in Germany).[^o7][^o8] Part of this is the yearly mathematics competition at the University of Ulm.[^o9] From this, pupils were selected to attend a mathematics summer school with teaching in computer science, chemistry, physics and where they were introduced to university mathematics.[^o10]

She was a founder of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372),[^o11] and a member and chair of the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Verein which supports female mathematicians.[^o12]

Kalmbach last lived in Bad Wörishofen,[^o13] where she died on 25 July 2025, at the age of 88.[^o14]
