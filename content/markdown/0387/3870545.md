::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 dodecahedral honeycomb"}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), the **order-5 dodecahedral honeycomb** is one of four compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{5,3,5\},$ it has five [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/7149361) cells around each [edge](wikipedia://en/12158034), and each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417) is surrounded by twenty dodecahedra. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968).

:::

## Description {#Description}

The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) of a Euclidean [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) is ~116.6°, so no more than three of them can fit around an edge in Euclidean 3-space. In hyperbolic space, however, the dihedral angle is smaller than it is in Euclidean space, and depends on the size of the figure; the smallest possible dihedral angle is 60°, for an ideal hyperbolic regular dodecahedron with infinitely long edges. The [dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/7149361) in this dodecahedral honeycomb are sized so that all of their dihedral angles are exactly 72°.

There is another honeycomb in hyperbolic 3-space called the [order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3754285), {5,3,4}, which has only four dodecahedra per edge. These honeycombs are also related to the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) which can be considered as a honeycomb in positively curved space (the surface of a 4-dimensional sphere), with three dodecahedra on each edge, {5,3,3}. Lastly the dodecahedral [ditope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditope), {5,3,2} exists on a [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792), with 2 hemispherical cells.

There are [nine uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_uniform_honeycombs_in_hyperbolic_space#.5B5.2C3.2C5.5D_family) in the \[5,3,5\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form. Also the [bitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncation_%28geometry%29) form, t<sub>1,2</sub>{5,3,5}, , of this honeycomb has all [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282) cells.

The [Seifert–Weber space](wikipedia://en/1233773) is a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) that can be formed as a [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) of the order-5 dodecahedral honeycomb.
