::: {#o10 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 cubic honeycomb"}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), the **order-5 cubic honeycomb** is one of four compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{4,3,5\},$ it has five [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) $\{4,3\}$ around each [edge](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 20 cubes around each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) with the [order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3754285).

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

It has a radical subgroup symmetry construction with [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/7149361) fundamental domains: [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502#Extended_symmetry): \[4,(3,5)<sup>\*</sup>\], index 120.

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The order-5 cubic honeycomb has a related [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) honeycomb, ↔ , with [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) and [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) cells.

The honeycomb is also one of four regular compact honeycombs in 3D hyperbolic space:

There are [fifteen uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_uniform_honeycombs_in_hyperbolic_space#.5B5.2C3.2C4.5D_family) in the \[5,3,4\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including the order-5 cubic honeycomb as the regular form:

The order-5 cubic honeycomb is in a sequence of [regular polychora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_polychora) and honeycombs with [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/14968) [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719).

It is also in a sequence of [regular polychora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_polychora) and honeycombs with [cubic](wikipedia://en/6285) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29). The first polytope in the sequence is the [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112), and the second is the Euclidean [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318).

The **runcicantic order-5 cubic honeycomb** is a uniform compact space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) h<sub>2,3</sub>{4,3,5}. It has [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538), [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534), and [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651) cells, with an irregular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).
