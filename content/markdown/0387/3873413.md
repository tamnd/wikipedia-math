# Pro rata

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pro rata"}

***Pro rata*** is an adverb or adjective meaning in equal portions or in proportion.[^o2] The term is used in many legal and economic contexts. The hyphenated spelling ***pro-rata*** for the adjective form is common, as [recommended for adjectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_compound#Hyphenated_compound_modifiers) by some English-language style guides. In [American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_English), this term has been [vernacularized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernacular) as ***prorated*** or ***pro-rated***.

:::

## Meanings {#Meanings}

More specifically, *pro rata* means:

- In [proportionality](wikipedia://en/81863) to some factor that can be exactly [calculated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculate)[^o4][^o5]
- To count based on an amount of [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) that has passed out of the total time
- Proportional [ratio](wikipedia://en/87837)[^o6]

Pro rata has a [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) [etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etymology), from *pro*, according to, for, or by, and *rata*, [feminine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_gender) [ablative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ablative) of calculated (rate or change).[^o7]

::: {#o9 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

When liability for a [toxic tort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toxic_tort) or a [defective product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_liability) concerns many manufacturers, the [liability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_liability) under [tort law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tort_law) is allocated pro rata.[^o10] Examples in [law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law) and [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) include the following, noted below:

### Partnership liability {#Partnership_liability}

Each of several [partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partnership) "is liable for \[her or his\] own share or proportion only, they are said to be bound *pro rata*. An example ... may be found in the liability of partners; each is liable ... only *pro rata* in relation to between themselves."[^o12]

::: {#o14 .statement cue=named-heading name="Bankruptcy law"}

### Bankruptcy law {#Bankruptcy_law}

When a [debtor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debtor) files for [bankruptcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankruptcy), and "the debtor is insolvent, [creditors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creditor) generally agree to accept a pro rata share of what is owed to them. If the debtor has any remaining funds, the money is divided proportionately among the creditors, according to the amount of the individual debts."[^o15] "A creditor of an insolvent estate is to be paid pro rata with creditors of the same class."[^o16]

:::

### Worker's pay and benefits {#Worker's_pay_and_benefits}

A worker's part-time work, [overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overtime) pay, and [Annual leave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annual_leave) time are typically calculated on a pro rata basis.[^o18][^o19]

Under [US Federal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_government_of_the_United_States) [regulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulations), a [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government) worker has the right such that: "When an employee's service is interrupted by a non-leave earning period, leave is earned on a pro rata basis for each fractional pay period that occurs within the continuity of employment."[^o20]

The [American Federation of Teachers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Federation_of_Teachers) (AFT), a [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [labor union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_union), argues that *all* part-time or [adjunct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunct_professor) instructors should get pro-rata pay for teaching [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) courses.[^o21] This is an important issue, as of 2010, for part-time [faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher).[^o22]

[Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland) [secondary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school) teachers are entitled to pro rata pay for part-time work.[^o23]

Under [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) [employment law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment_law), "Regulations state that, where appropriate, the pro rata principle should be applied to any comparison ... to be given ... holiday."[^o24]

Likewise, in [Tasmania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tasmania), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia), the law clearly grants workers the privilege of part-time benefits for [leave of absence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leave_of_absence).[^o25][^o26] This is granted under the *Long Service Leave Act 1976*.[^o27]

::: {#o29 .statement cue=named-heading name="Investment laws"}

### Investment laws {#Investment_laws}

In [corporate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate) practice, "a pro-rata dividend means that every shareholder gets an equal proportion for each share he or she owns."[^o30]

In [banking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banking), "Pro-rating also refers to the practice of applying interest rates to different time frames. If the interest rate was 12% per annum, you could pro-rate this number to be 1% a month (12%/12 months)."[^o30]

:::

### Venture capital {#Venture_capital}

In [venture capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital), it can refer to the Pro-Rata Participation right and mean "the right to continue to participate in future rounds so that you can maintain your ownership."[^o32]

### Insurance {#Insurance}

In [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance), *pro rata* is used to determine [risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk) based on the time the [insurance policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance_policy) is in effect.[^o34] It may also be used to describe *proportional liability* when more than one person is responsible for a loss or [accident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accident).

### Insurance cancellation method {#Insurance_cancellation_method}

Calculation of [return premium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_premium) of a cancelled [insurance policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance_policy) is often done using a [cancellation method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancellation_method) called *[pro rata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_rata_cancellation)*. First, a [return premium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_premium) factor is calculated by taking the number of days remaining in the policy period divided by the number of total days of the policy. This factor is then multiplied by the policy premium to arrive at the [return premium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_premium). This calculation is referred to as a "wheel calculation".[^o36]

### College tuition {#College_tuition}

When a [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) [student](wikipedia://en/155526) withdraws, colleges may refund tuition payments on a pro rata basis.

### Aviation {#Aviation}

In the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), a private pilot may pay no less than the pro rata share of the total price of the flight when carrying passengers.[^o39]

### Automobile {#Automobile}

Pro-Ration (pro rata) means if the battery fails anytime within the first two years of service, it is replaced for free. After those first two years, a pro-rated fee is assessed for the replacement. However, the pro-ration (pro rata) is typically calculated from the date the battery was sold.

:::
