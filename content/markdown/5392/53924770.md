# Joseph Brain (academic)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Brain"}

**Joseph David Brain** (January 20, 1940 – January 29, 2024) was an American [physiologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physiology) and [environmental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_health) researcher, who served as the Cecil K. and Philip Drinker Professor of Environmental Physiology at [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health),[^o3][^o4] where he taught and researched for over fifty years.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Born in [Paterson, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paterson,_New_Jersey) and raised in [Wayne, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne,_New_Jersey), Brain attended [Eastern Christian High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Christian_High_School) in [North Haledon, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Haledon,_New_Jersey),[^o6] after which he went in 1957 to [Taylor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_University) in [Upland, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upland,_Indiana), where he majored in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). After his graduation from (then) Taylor College, Brain was offered the [Danforth Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danforth_Foundation) Graduate Fellowship (1961–1966) at Harvard.[^o7]

- B.A. (Physics, Summa cum laude), 1961, [Taylor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_University), [Upland, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upland,_Indiana)[^o10][^o11][^o9]
- S.M., 1962, [Harvard Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_John_A._Paulson_School_of_Engineering_and_Applied_Sciences) - on [Danforth Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danforth_Foundation) Graduate Fellowship (1961–1966)

## Career {#Career}

Brain served as the Cecil K. and [Philip Drinker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Drinker) Professor of Environmental Physiology[^o13] at [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health), and participated in the leadership of [The Lown Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Lown#The_Lown_Institute_and_the_Right_Care_Alliance).[^o14][^o15] After graduation from Taylor, Brain was offered the Danforth Foundation Graduate Fellowship (1961–1966) at Harvard.[^o7]

Brain took part in a five-year collaboration signed between the [University of Malaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malaya) (UM) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in the search for more effective treatment of lung diseases through [nanomedicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanomedicine). In 1998, he was named the Director of the Kresge Center for Environmental Health at Harvard.

Brain was director of Respiratory Biology Program (1981–1993) and Physiology Program (1993–1997). He was also chair of the Department of Environmental Health at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health (1990–2005).

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Brain was married and lived in [Essex, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex,_Massachusetts). He served on the Taylor University Board of Trustees from 1988 to 2009.

Joseph Brain died on January 29, 2024, at the age of 84.[^o17]
