# Susan Paddock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Paddock"}

**Susan Mary Paddock** is an American statistician whose publications have included work on [nonparametric Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/223369), [substance abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substance_abuse), and the safety of [autonomous vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_vehicle).

:::

Paddock is a graduate of the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) and has a Ph.D. from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University). Her 1999 doctoral dissertation, *Randomized Polya Trees: Bayesian Nonparametrics for Multivariate Data Analysis*, was supervised by Mike West. Formerly head of the RAND Statistics Group at the [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation), she moved to [NORC at the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORC_at_the_University_of_Chicago) in 2019 as chief statistician and executive vice president.

She was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2013, and in the same year won the Mid-Career Achievement Award of the American Statistical Association's Health Policy Statistics Section. She was the 2019 chair of the association's Section on Bayesian Statistical Science.
