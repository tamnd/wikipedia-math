# Toeplitz algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toeplitz algebra"}

In [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras), the **Toeplitz algebra** is the [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) generated by the [unilateral shift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unilateral_shift) on the [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) [*l*<sup>2</sup>(**N**)](wikipedia://en/1701075).[^o2] Identifying *l*<sup>2</sup>(**N**) with the [Hardy space](wikipedia://en/314780) [*H*<sup>2</sup>](wikipedia://en/20041584), the Toeplitz algebra consists of elements of the form

:::

$$T_f + K\;$$

where *$T_f$* is a [Toeplitz operator](wikipedia://en/14598730) with continuous symbol $f$ and *K* is a [compact operator](wikipedia://en/6690773).

Toeplitz operators with continuous symbols commute modulo the compact operators. So the Toeplitz algebra can be viewed as the C\*-algebra extension of continuous functions on the circle by the compact operators. This extension is called the **Toeplitz extension**.

By [Atkinson's theorem](wikipedia://en/10533603), an element of the Toeplitz algebra *$T_f + K\;$*is a [Fredholm operator](wikipedia://en/279722) if and only if the symbol $f$ of *$T_f$* is invertible. In that case, the Fredholm index of *$T_f + K\;$*is precisely the [winding number](wikipedia://en/86113) of *f*, the equivalence class of *f* in the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) of the circle. This is a special case of the [Atiyah-Singer index theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atiyah-Singer_index_theorem).

[Wold decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wold_decomposition) characterizes [proper isometries](wikipedia://en/254777) acting on a Hilbert space. From this, together with properties of Toeplitz operators, one can conclude that the Toeplitz algebra is the [universal C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/668934) generated by a proper isometry; this is *Coburn's theorem*[^o3].
