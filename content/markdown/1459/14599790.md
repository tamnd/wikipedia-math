# Quantaloid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantaloid"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quantaloid** is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) over the category **Sup** of [complete lattices with supremum-preserving maps](wikipedia://en/44777#Morphisms_of_complete_lattices).[^o2] In other words, for any [objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object_%28category_theory%29) *a* and *b* the [Hom](wikipedia://en/27716891#Hom-set) object between them is not just a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) but a complete [lattice](wikipedia://en/469304), in such a way that composition of morphisms preserves all joins:

:::

$$(\bigvee_i f_i) \circ (\bigvee_j g_j) = \bigvee_{i,j} (f_i \circ g_j)$$

The [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) lattice $\mathrm{Hom}(X,X)$ of any object $X$ in a quantaloid is a [quantale](wikipedia://en/716803), whence the name.
