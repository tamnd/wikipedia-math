# Gelfand ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gelfand ring"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Gelfand ring** is a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* with identity such that if *I* and *J* are distinct right [ideals](wikipedia://en/25977) then there are elements *i* and *j* such that *iRj* = 0, *i* is not in *I*, and *j* is not in *J*. (Mulvey 1979) introduced them as rings for which one could [prove](wikipedia://en/82285) a generalization of [Gelfand duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelfand_duality), and named them after [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643).[^o3]

:::

In the [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346) case, Gelfand rings can also be characterized as the rings such that, for every $a$ and $b$ summing to $1$, there exists $r$ and $s$ such that

$$(1+ra)(1+sb) = 0.$$

Moreover, their [prime spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_spectrum) [deformation retracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_retract) onto the [maximal spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximal_spectrum).[^o4][^o5]
