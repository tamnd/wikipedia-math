# Chia-Kun Chu

[Chu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhu_%28surname%29)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Chia-Kun Chu"}

**Chia-Kun (John) Chu** (; August 14, 1927 – January 2, 2023) was a Chinese-American applied mathematician who was the Fu Foundation Professor Emeritus of Applied Mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). He had been on Columbia faculty since 1965 and served as the department chairman of applied physics and nuclear engineering three times (1982–1983, 1985–1988, 1995–1997).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chu received a bachelor's in mechanical engineering from [Chiao-Tung University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiao_Tong_University) in 1948, a master's from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1950, and a Ph.D. from [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1959.[^o3]

Chu was an internationally recognized applied mathematician and one of the pioneers of computational mathematics in fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, and shock waves. He developed approximations to the differential equations of fluid dynamics and coined the term "computational fluid dynamics".[^o5]

Chu received numerous honors. He was a recipient of [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) and was elected fellow of [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society) and fellow of [Japan Society for the Promotion of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Society_for_the_Promotion_of_Science). He was awarded an honorary Doctor of Science degree from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2006.[^o3][^o5]

Chia-Kun Chu was the son of bank chairman Ju Tang Chu.[^o6] Chia-Kun Chu was also the brother-in-law of [Fu Zaiyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fu_Zaiyuan), a Columbia donor who gave his name for the [Fu Foundation School of Engineering and Applied Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fu_Foundation_School_of_Engineering_and_Applied_Science).[^o7]

Chia-Kun Chu died on January 2, 2023, at the age of 95.[^o8][^o9]
