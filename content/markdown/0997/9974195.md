# Moscow Institute of Electronics and Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moscow Institute of Electronics and Mathematics"}

**Moscow Institute of Electronics and Mathematics** (**MIEM**; Московский институт электроники и математики НИУ ВШЭ, МИЭМ; also occasionally referred to as *Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering*[^o3]) — a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [higher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education) [educational institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_institution) in the field of [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics), [computer engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_engineering), and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

:::

## History {#History}

The institute was founded by the joint decree of the [Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Soviet_Union) [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_the_Soviet_Union) and the [USSR government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Soviet_Union) of 21 April 1962 as the *Moscow Institute of Electronic Machine Building* (Московский институт электронного машиностроения, МИЭМ) from the *Moscow Evening Machine Building Institute* (Московский вечерний машиностроительный институт (founded in 1929).[^o5][^o6] It was designed to educate personnel for the technologically advanced enterprises of the USSR's military industry.[^o6] The institute changed over to the current name in 1993, retaining the same abbreviation.

In 2011, the institute was incorporated into the [National Research University Higher School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_University_Higher_School_of_Economics).[^o7] In December 2014, the institute moved to a new building located in the northwestern suburb of Moscow, [Strogino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strogino_District), from its previous location at 3 Tryokhsvyatitelskiy lane in central [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow).[^o8]

## Faculties {#Faculties}

As of 2015, the institute has 3 departments:

- Faculty of Electronic Engineering;
- Faculty of Computer Engineering;
- Faculty of Applied Mathematics.
