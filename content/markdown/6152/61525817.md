# Chris Soteros

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chris Soteros"}

**Christine Elaine Soteros** is a Canadian [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969). She is a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saskatchewan) and was the University's Site Director for the [Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/34978739) from 2015 to 2024. Her research involves the folding and packing behavior of DNA, proteins, and other string-like biomolecules, and the [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) of random [space curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_curve).

:::

Soteros graduated from the [University of Windsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Windsor) in 1980. She completed her Ph.D. in chemical engineering at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1988. Her dissertation, *Studies of Metal Hydride Phase Transitions Using the Cluster Variation Method*, was supervised by [Carol K. Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_K._Hall). After postdoctoral research at the University of Toronto, working with Stuart Whittington and [De Witt Sumners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Witt_Sumners), she became a faculty member at the University of Saskatchewan in 1989.
