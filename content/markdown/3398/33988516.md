# Helmut Grunsky

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Helmut Grunsky"}

**Helmut Grunsky** (11 July 1904 – 5 June 1986) was a German mathematician who worked in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [geometric function theory](wikipedia://en/5458932). He introduced [Grunsky's theorem](wikipedia://en/33983889) and the [Grunsky inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grunsky_inequalities).[^o3]

:::

In 1936, he was appointed editor of *[Jahrbuch über die Fortschritte der Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahrbuch_über_die_Fortschritte_der_Mathematik)*. In 1939 he was forced to leave this position after [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186) accused him of employing Jewish referees in a notorious letter.[^o4] He joined the [Nazi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party) on 1 April 1940, though he seems to have had little sympathy with its philosophy.[^o5] He published in the journal [Deutsche Mathematik](wikipedia://en/31018614). From 1949 he was Privatdozent at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen); later, he was professor at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) and at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg).
