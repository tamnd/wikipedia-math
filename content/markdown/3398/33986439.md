# August Friedrich Müller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="August Friedrich Müller"}

*Image: August-Friedrich-Mueller.jpg.* August Friedrich Müller

**August Friedrich Müller** (15 December 1684 – 1 May 1761) was a German legal scholar and logician.

:::

August Friedrich was born in [Penig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penig), [Electorate of Saxony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electorate_of_Saxony), the son of Johann Adam Müller and his wife Johanne Susanne, daughter of a pharmacist in [Rochlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochlitz), Johann Fromhold. Prefigured by his father, he attended school in 1697 and studied at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) from 1703. Here he completed a degree in early philosophical sciences; [Andreas Rüdiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Rüdiger) (1673–1731) was his most important teacher. On the side he studied law under [Gottlieb Gerhard Titius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottlieb_Gerhard_Titius) (1661–1714).

In 1707 he received his degree of [Magister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magister_%28degree%29) in Leipzig, where he set up a school of philosophy. After a stay at the [University of Erfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erfurt), where on 8 October 1714 he received his doctorate in law, he returned to Leipzig, where he also lectured on law. A position was offered to him at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle) but he became an associate professor of philosophy[^o2] at the University of Leipzig in 1732, succeeding [Christian Thomasius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Thomasius) and his pupil, Andreas Rüdiger. He was Dean of Philosophy several times, first in 1736. He died in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig), aged 76.

Bach composed the cantata *[Zerreißet, zersprenget, zertrümmert die Gruft, BWV 205](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zerreißet,_zersprenget,_zertrümmert_die_Gruft,_BWV_205)* in 1725 for the [name day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Name_day) of Müller and performed the piece on the evening of 3 August 1725 in front of the professor's house at 2 [Katharinenstraße](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharinenstrasse) in Leipzig.[^o2]
