# Semyon Kutateladze

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Semyon Kutateladze"}

**Semyon** **Samsonovich Kutateladze** (Семён Самсонович Кутателадзе;[^o3] October 2, 1945 – January 15, 2025) was a Russian mathematician. He was known for his contributions to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and its applications to [vector lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_lattice) and [optimization](wikipedia://en/52033).[^o4][^o5] In particular, he made contributions to the [calculus](wikipedia://en/50416) of [subdifferentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdifferential) for vector-lattice valued functions, to whose study he introduced methods of [Boolean-valued models](wikipedia://en/2732301) and [infinitesimals](wikipedia://en/160990).

:::

He was professor of mathematics at [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University),[^o6] where he continued and enriched the scientific tradition of [Leonid Kantorovich](wikipedia://en/194660).[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10] His father was the Soviet heat physicist [Samson Kutateladze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samson_Kutateladze). Kutateladze died on January 15, 2025, aged 79.[^o11]
