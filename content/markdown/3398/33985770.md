# Ed Corrigan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ed Corrigan"}

**Francis Edward Corrigan** FRS (born 10 August 1946, in [Birkenhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkenhead)) is a British mathematician and theoretical physicist. He is professor at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Corrigan attended [St Bede's College, Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Bede's_College,_Manchester) and earned a BA (1968) and PhD (1972) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

## Career {#Career}

He was Addison Wheeler Fellow in the Department of Mathematical Sciences [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) 1972–74, CERN Fellow 1974–75, and returned to work at Durham from 1976 to 1999 (including as Head of Department 1996–98). He then moved to the University of York where he was Head of Department of Mathematics 1999–2004, 2005–2007 and 2011–2015. He served as Principal of [Collingwood College, Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collingwood_College,_Durham), from 2008 to 2011.
