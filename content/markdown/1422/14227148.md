# Martin Charles Golumbic

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Charles Golumbic"}

**Martin Charles Golumbic** (born 1948) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) known for his research on [perfect graphs](wikipedia://en/670531), [graph sandwich problems](wikipedia://en/26387294), [tolerance graphs](wikipedia://en/61928385), [compiler optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compiler_optimization), and [spatial-temporal reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial-temporal_reasoning). He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of computer science at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa), and was the founder of the journal *Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Golumbic majored in mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), graduating in 1970 with bachelor's and master's degrees. He completed his Ph.D. at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1975, with the dissertation *Comparability Graphs and a New Matroid* supervised by [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501).

He became an assistant professor in the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) from 1975 until 1980, when he moved to [Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Laboratories). From 1983 to 1992 he worked for [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research) in Israel, and from 1992 to 2000 he was a professor of mathematics and computer science at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University). He moved to the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) in 2000, where he founded the Caesarea Edmond Benjamin de Rothschild Institute for Interdisciplinary Applications of Computer Science.

In 1989, Golumbic founded the Bar-Ilan Symposium in Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, a leading artificial intelligence conference in Israel. In 1990 Golumbic became the founding editor-in-chief of the journal *Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence*, published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Golumbic is a fellow of the [European Association for Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_for_Artificial_Intelligence) (2005). He was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2013.

At the 2019 Bar-Ilan Symposium in Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Golumbic was given the Lifetime Achievement and Service Award of the Israeli Association for Artificial Intelligence.
