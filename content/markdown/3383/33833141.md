# Marek Kuczma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marek Kuczma"}

**Marek Kuczma** (10 October 1935 in [Katowice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katowice) – 13 June 1991 in Katowice) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) mathematician working mostly in the area of [functional equations](wikipedia://en/364377). He wrote several influential monographs in this field.

:::
