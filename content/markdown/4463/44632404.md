# Integrated Household Survey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Integrated Household Survey"}

The **Integrated Household Survey** (IHS) is a survey made up of multiple other surveys in many countries including the [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK) . It includes about 340,000 respondents, making it the largest collection of social data in the UK after the census.[^o2] The surveys it is made up of are the [Annual Population Survey](wikipedia://en/24456749) and the [Living Cost and Food Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_Cost_and_Food_Survey).

:::
