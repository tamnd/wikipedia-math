# European Union Statistics on Income and Living Conditions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="European Union Statistics on Income and Living Conditions"}

The **European Union Statistics on Income and Living Conditions**, (also known as *European Union Survey on Income and Living Conditions*) abbreviated **EU-SILC**, is a survey department of the [EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU). In 2004, it replaced the *European Community Household Panel* (ECHP), which covered the years 1994–2003. The EU-SILC includes only minor changes relative to its predecessor the ECHP; most importantly, it significantly expands the number of countries included in the sample.

:::
