# Symposium on Discrete Algorithms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symposium on Discrete Algorithms"}

The **Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms** (**SODA**) is an [academic conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) in the fields of [algorithm design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithm_design) and [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492). It is considered to be one of the top conferences for research in algorithms.[^o2] SODA has been organized annually since 1990, typically in January.[^o3] SODA is jointly sponsored by the [ACM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) Special Interest Group on Algorithms and Computation Theory ([SIGACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGACT)) and the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Discrete Mathematics, and in format is more similar to a [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) conference than to a mathematics conference.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

The first Symposium on Discrete Algorithms was held in 1990 at [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco), organized by [David Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_S._Johnson). In 2012, the ACM Special Interest Group on Algorithms and Computation Theory (ACM SIGACT) and SIAM Activity Group on Discrete Mathematics (SIAG/DM) jointly established SODA Steering Committee to work with SIAM and ACM on organizing SODA.
