# Vyacheslav Rychkov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vyacheslav Rychkov"}

**Vyacheslav Rychkov** (called **Slava Rychkov**, Russian Вячеслав Рычков, transcription Vyacheslav Rychkov; born 27 May 1975 in Samara, Russia [^o3]) is a Russian-Italian-French[^o3] theoretical physicist and mathematician.

:::

## Career {#Career}

In 1996, Rychkov obtained his diploma ([B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science), [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science)) from the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology).[^o3] From 1996 to 1998 he studied at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena).[^o5] He received his doctorate in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Department_of_Mathematics), under the supervision of [Elias Stein](wikipedia://en/418073), in 2002[^o6] with a thesis titled "Estimates for Oscillatory Integral Operators".[^o7] [Alexander Polyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Markovich_Polyakov) was his unofficial supervisor.[^o8] He was a post-doctoral fellow at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) (2002-2005) and at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in Pisa, where he became assistant professor in 2007.[^o8] In 2009 he became a professor of physics at the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI) and a member of the Laboratory of Theoretical Physics at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris.[^o3] Since 2012 to 2017 he was a staff member of the Department of Theoretical Physics at [CERN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERN).[^o8] He has been a [Mitsubishi Heavy Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Heavy_Industries) Professor of High Energy Physics at École Normale Supérieure in Paris since 2016 [^o9] and in 2017 he became a permanent professor at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques).[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Slava Rychkov’s research interests mostly concern problems of theoretical physics to which the methods of [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774) are applicable. He is the deputy director of the international collaboration on the nonperturbative bootstrap[^o12] financed by the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).

## Awards {#Awards}

Between 2012 and 2017 he was a Junior Member of l’[Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).[^o14]

In 2014 he received the [New Horizons in Physics Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Horizons_in_Physics_Prize) “For developing new techniques in conformal field theory, reviving the conformal bootstrap program for constraining the spectrum of operators and the structure constants in 3D and 4D CFT’s.”[^o15]

He was the 2019 laureate of the Grand Prix Mergier-Bourdeix of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for his work on the [conformal bootstrap](wikipedia://en/47273717).[^o16]
