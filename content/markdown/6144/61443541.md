# Michael Akritas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Akritas"}

**Michael G. Akritas** (born 1950) is a Greek American statistician and professor emeritus of Statistics at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o3]

:::

His research has focused on [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), factorial designs, censored data, high-dimensional data modeling, [astrostatistics](wikipedia://en/37815827), and [social statistics](wikipedia://en/285943).[^o4]

Akritas was elected Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2001,[^o5] and Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2001.[^o6]

## Education {#Education}

Akritas received a B.Sc. in mathematics from the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) in 1972, an M.Sc. in Operations Research from the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton), studied at the [University of Patras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Patras) under George G. Roussas,[^o8] and in 1978 he received a Ph.D. in statistics from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) under Richard A. Johnson.[^o9]

## Career {#Career}

Akritas taught at MIT from 1978 to 1979, and again from 1980 to 1981 after serving in the Greek military. He then moved to the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), and in 1985 he joined the Department of Statistics at The Pennsylvania State. He held a position at [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens), and visiting positions at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), [University of Goettingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Goettingen), and [University of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cyprus). He co-founded, and served as Director of, the Statistical Consulting Center for Astronomy;[^o11] he also co-founded, and served as Treasurer of, the International Society for Nonparametric Statistics.[^o12] He has published 104 articles which have been cited over 4700 times.[^o4]
