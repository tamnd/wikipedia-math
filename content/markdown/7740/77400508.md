# Hecke eigensheaf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hecke eigensheaf"}

In mathematics, a **Hecke eigensheaf** is any [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) whose [value](wikipedia://en/4095147) is based on an [eigenfunction](wikipedia://en/228601). It is an object that is a tensor-multiple of itself when formed under the integral transform of a [Hecke correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hecke_correspondence). Hecke eigensheaves are part of the geometric [Langlands correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands_correspondence).[^o2][^o3]

:::
