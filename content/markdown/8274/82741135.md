# Nodal line conjecture

*Image: 2D Wavefunction (2,1) Surface Plot.png.* A second eigenfunction in a rectangular domain; the nodal line is visible at the bottom.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nodal line conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **nodal line conjecture** is a statement posed in 1967 by [Lawrence E. Payne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_E._Payne) about the [Laplacian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplacian) [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564). The original conjecture predicts that for the [Dirichlet](wikipedia://en/423504) problem on a bounded two-dimensional domain, the second eigenfunction has a nodal line that meets the boundary of the domain. The general conjecture was proved false in 1997 by carving a large number of microscopic holes out of a disk,[^o2] a technique that simulates a [Schrödinger](wikipedia://en/59874) potential.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Other positive and negative results are known for various special cases of domains; in general, it remains an open problem to describe how simple the domain must be for the conjecture to hold.[^o5]
