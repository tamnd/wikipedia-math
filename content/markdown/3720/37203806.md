# Jörg Bewersdorff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jörg Bewersdorff"}

Jörg Bewersdorff

*Image: Jörg Bewersdorff.jpg.* Bewersdorff in 2006

**Jörg Bewersdorff** (born 1 February 1958 in [Neuwied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuwied))[^o2] is a German mathematician who is working as mathematics writer and [game designer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_designer).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

After obtaining his *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* from the Werner-Heisenberg-Gymnasium in Neuwied[^o6] Bewersdorff studied mathematics from 1975 to 1982 at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o7] In 1982 he submitted his diploma in mathematics in Bonn[^o7] and in 1985 he received his doctorate there under the supervision of [Günter Harder](wikipedia://en/50792386) (*A [Lefschetz fixed point formula](wikipedia://en/582530) for [Hecke operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hecke_operators)*).[^o9]

In 1985 Bewersdorff started a career as game designer.[^o2] Since 1998[^o7] he is general manager of subsidiaries of the [Gauselmann AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauselmann).[^o2][^o10][^o11] Bewersdorff is author of four textbooks dealing with [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [mathematics of gambling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_gambling), [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [combinatorial game theory](wikipedia://en/292231), [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316), [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), [JavaScript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JavaScript) and [object-oriented programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object-oriented_programming). Two of them were translated into English. One book was translated also to Korean.[^o12] Bewersdorff's books are undergraduate level books. Their goal is to explain applications and what is going on behind the formalism.[^o13][^o14]
