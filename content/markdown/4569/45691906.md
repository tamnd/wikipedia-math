# Lieb conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lieb conjecture"}

In [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) theory, the **Lieb conjecture** is a theorem concerning the [Wehrl entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrl_entropy) of quantum systems for which the classical [phase space](wikipedia://en/191101) is a sphere. It states that no state of such a system has a lower Wehrl entropy than the [SU(2) coherent states](wikipedia://en/32776711).

:::

The analogous property for quantum systems for which the classical [phase space](wikipedia://en/191101) is a *plane* was conjectured by Alfred Wehrl in 1978 and proven soon afterwards by [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb),[^o2] who at the same time extended it to the SU(2) case. The conjecture was proven in 2012, by Lieb and [Jan Philip Solovej](wikipedia://en/67069196).[^o3] The uniqueness of the minimizers was only proved in 2022 by Rupert L. Frank[^o4] and Aleksei Kulikov, Fabio Nicola, Joaquim Ortega-Cerda' and Paolo Tilli.[^o5]
