# Sheaf of planes

*Image: Sheaf of Planes.gif.* Sheaf of planes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sheaf of planes"}

In mathematics, a **sheaf of planes** is the set of all [planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) that have the same common [line](wikipedia://en/946975).[^o2][^o3] It may also be known as a **fan of planes** or a *[pencil of planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_of_planes)*.

:::

When extending the concept of line to the *[line at infinity](wikipedia://en/403142)*, a set of *[parallel planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_planes)* can be seen as a [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of planes intersecting in a line at infinity. To distinguish it from the more general definition, the adjective *parallel* can be added to it, resulting in the expression **parallel sheaf of planes**.[^o4]
