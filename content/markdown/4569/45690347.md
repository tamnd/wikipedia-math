::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="5-orthoplex honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 5-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **5-orthoplex honeycomb** is one of five paracompact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is paracompact because it has infinite [vertex figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_figures), with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,3,4,3}, it has three [5-orthoplexes](wikipedia://en/7594615) around each cell. It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [24-cell honeycomb honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24-cell_honeycomb_honeycomb).

:::
