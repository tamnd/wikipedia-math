In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 5-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **16-cell honeycomb honeycomb** is one of five paracompact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is called *paracompact* because it has infinite [vertex figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_figures), with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,4,3,3}, it has three [16-cell honeycombs](wikipedia://en/9409435) around each cell. It is self-[dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope).

:::
