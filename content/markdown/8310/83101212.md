# Supercell composite parameter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Supercell composite parameter"}

*Image: Supercell Composite April 27, 2011.png.* The supercell composite index on [April 27, 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Super_Outbreak), overlaid with tracks of [EF5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enhanced_Fujita_scale)-rated tornadoes (in black)

The **Supercell Composite Parameter**, often abbreviated as **SCP**, is a multi-parameter index used by the [Storm Prediction Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Prediction_Center) that outlines areas of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) where the development of [supercell thunderstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercell_thunderstorm) is favorable.[^o2] The parameter uses [convective available potential energy](wikipedia://en/1200324), [storm relative helicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_relative_helicity) and [vertical wind shear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_wind_shear) to determine specific areas.[^o3] A value of 1 indicates a normal atmosphere while values above two are conducive for supercells.[^o4]
