# Leopoldt's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leopoldt's conjecture"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), **Leopoldt's conjecture**, introduced by [Heinrich-Wolfgang Leopoldt](wikipedia://en/27700368),[^n1][^n2] states that the [*p*-adic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_numbers) regulator of a [number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) does not vanish. The *p*-adic regulator is an analogue of the usual [regulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulator_%28mathematics%29) defined using [*p*-adic logarithms](wikipedia://en/28760028#p-adic_logarithm_function) instead of the usual logarithms.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Formulation"}

## Formulation {#Formulation}

Let *K* be a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) and for each [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) *P* of *K* above some fixed rational prime *p*, let *U*<sub>*P*</sub> denote the local [units](wikipedia://en/289666) at *P* and let *U*<sub>1,*P*</sub> denote the subgroup of principal units in *U*<sub>*P*</sub>. Set

$$U_1 = \prod_{P\,\mid\, p} U_{1,P}.$$

Then let *E*<sub>1</sub> denote the set of global units *ε* that map to *U*<sub>1</sub> via the [diagonal embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagonal_embedding) of the global units in *E*.

Since *E*<sub>1</sub> is a finite-[index](wikipedia://en/230581) subgroup of the global units, it is an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of rank *r*<sub>1</sub> + *r*<sub>2</sub> – 1, where *r*<sub>1</sub> is the number of real embeddings of *K* and *r*<sub>2</sub> the number of pairs of complex embeddings. **Leopoldt's conjecture** states that the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module rank of the closure of $E_1$ embedded diagonally in *U*<sub>1</sub> is also *r*<sub>1</sub> + *r*<sub>2</sub> – 1.

Leopoldt's conjecture is known in the special case where *K* is an [abelian extension](wikipedia://en/174448) of $\mathbb{Q}$ or an abelian extension of an imaginary [quadratic number field](wikipedia://en/454781): [Ax](wikipedia://en/6165715) reduced the abelian case to a *p*-adic version of [Baker's theorem](wikipedia://en/28751817),[^n3] which was proved shortly afterwards by Brumer.[^n4] [Mihăilescu](wikipedia://en/639556) has announced a proof of Leopoldt's conjecture for all CM-extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$.[^n5][^n6]

[Colmez](wikipedia://en/28580245) expressed the residue of the *p*-adic [Dedekind zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243) of a [totally real field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_real_field) at *s* = 1 in terms of the *p*-adic regulator.[^n7] As a consequence, Leopoldt's conjecture for those fields is equivalent to their *p*-adic Dedekind zeta functions having a simple pole at *s* = 1.

:::
