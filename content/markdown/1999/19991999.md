# Hemi-octahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hemi-octahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hemi-octahedron** is an [abstract regular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/1211056), containing half the faces of a [regular octahedron](wikipedia://en/22458).

:::

It has 4 triangular faces, 6 edges, and 3 vertices. Its [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) is the [hemicube](wikipedia://en/10431815).

It can be realized as a [projective polyhedron](wikipedia://en/26952055) (a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of the [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325) by 4 triangles), which can be visualized by constructing the projective plane as a hemisphere where opposite points along the boundary are connected and dividing the hemisphere into four equal parts. It can be seen as a [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455) without its base.

It can be represented symmetrically as a hexagonal or square [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595):

*Image: Hemioctahedron.png.*

It has an unexpected property that there are two distinct edges between every pair of vertices – any two vertices define a [digon](wikipedia://en/5442728).
