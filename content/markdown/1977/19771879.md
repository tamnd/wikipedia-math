# Splitting lemma (functions)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Splitting lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), the **splitting lemma** is a useful result due to [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633) which provides a way of simplifying the local expression of a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) usually applied in a [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) of a degenerate [critical point](wikipedia://en/1951424).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Formal statement"}

## Formal statement {#Formal_statement}

Let $f:(\mathbb{R}^n, 0) \to (\mathbb{R}, 0)$ be a smooth function germ, with a critical point at 0 (so $(\partial f/\partial x_i)(0) = 0$ for $i = 1, \dots, n$). Let *V* be a [subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of $\mathbb{R}^n$ such that the [restriction](wikipedia://en/1589032) *f* |<sub>*V*</sub> is [non-degenerate](wikipedia://en/1951424), and write *B* for the [Hessian matrix](wikipedia://en/412108) of this restriction. Let *W* be any complementary subspace to *V*. Then there is a change of coordinates $\Phi(x, y)$ of the form $\Phi(x, y) = (\phi(x, y), y)$ with $x \in V, y \in W$, and a smooth function *h* on *W* such that

$$f\circ\Phi(x,y) = \frac{1}{2} x^TBx + h(y).$$

This result is often referred to as the parametrized [Morse lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_lemma), which can be seen by viewing *y* as the parameter. It is the *gradient version* of the [implicit function theorem](wikipedia://en/581005).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Extensions"}

## Extensions {#Extensions}

There are extensions to infinite dimensions, to [complex analytic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_function), to functions invariant under the [action](wikipedia://en/12781) of a [compact group](wikipedia://en/750326), ...

:::
