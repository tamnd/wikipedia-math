# Cantor Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cantor Medal"}

The **Cantor Medal** of the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung) is named in honor of [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216), the first president of the society. It is awarded at most every second year during the yearly meetings of the society.[^o2] The prize winners are [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians) who are associated with the German language. The prize money is €4.000.[^o3]

:::

## Prize winners {#Prize_winners}

- 1990 [Karl Stein](wikipedia://en/7218529)[^o5]
- 1992 [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316)[^o5][^o6]
- 1994 [Erhard Heinz](wikipedia://en/31696540)[^o5][^o6]
- 1996 [Jacques Tits](wikipedia://en/219570)[^o5][^o6]
- 1999 [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960)[^o5][^o6]
- 2002 [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086)[^o5][^o6]
- 2004 [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495)[^o6]
- 2006 [Hans Föllmer](wikipedia://en/29997344)[^o6]
- 2008 [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031)[^o6]
- 2010 [Matthias Kreck](wikipedia://en/33582824)[^o7]
- 2012 [Michael Struwe](wikipedia://en/41336151)[^o8]
- 2014 [Herbert Spohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Spohn)[^o9]
- 2017 [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226)[^o10]
- 2019 [Hélène Esnault](wikipedia://en/5358788)[^o11]
- 2021 [Martin Grötschel](wikipedia://en/55085835)[^o12]
- 2024 [Felix Otto](wikipedia://en/4248548)[^o13]
