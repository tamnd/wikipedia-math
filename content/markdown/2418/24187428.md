# Bidiakis cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bidiakis cube"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **bidiakis cube** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 12 vertices and 18 edges.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The bidiakis cube is a [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and can be defined by the [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720) \[−6,4,−4\]<sup>4</sup>.

The bidiakis cube can also be constructed from a cube by adding edges across the top and bottom faces which connect the centres of opposite sides of the faces. The two additional edges need to be perpendicular to each other. With this construction, the bidiakis cube is a [polyhedral graph](wikipedia://en/24656270), and can be realized as a [convex polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron). Therefore, by [Steinitz's theorem](wikipedia://en/19762817), it is a [3-vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) simple [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314).[^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The bidiakis cube is not a [vertex-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/581175) and its full automorphism group is isomorphic to the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) of order 8, the group of symmetries of a [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), including both rotations and reflections.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the bidiakis cube is $(x-3)(x-2)(x^4)(x+1)(x+2)(x^2+x-4)^2$.
