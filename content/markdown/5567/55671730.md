# Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel"}

**Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel** (born 1982)[^o11] is a [Turkish-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish-American) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and professor of the practice at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), and a professional educator at [RStudio](wikipedia://en/36691501). She is the author of several open source statistics textbooks[^o23][^o24][^o25][^o26] and is an instructor for [Coursera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coursera).[^o5][^o6] She is the chair-elect of the Statistical Education Section of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o7] Previously, she was a senior lecturer at [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Çetinkaya-Rundel grew up in [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) and graduated from [Robert College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_College),[^o10] before coming to the United States for her undergraduate studies to study actuarial science at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). After working as an [actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) at [Buck Consultants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buck_Consultants),[^o11] Çetinkaya-Rundel enrolled in graduate school at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). She earned her master's degree and PhD in statistics at UCLA where her dissertation on estimating the impact of [air pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution)[^o11] was supervised of [Jan de Leeuw](wikipedia://en/30963930).

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Çetinkaya-Rundel coauthored three open-access textbooks, *[OpenIntro Statistics](wikipedia://en/39102305)*,[^o14] *Introductory Statistics with Randomization and Simulation*, and *OpenIntro: Advanced High School Statistics*.[^o15] She is an author on the associated [R package](wikipedia://en/376707), openintro.[^o16] Çetinkaya-Rundel is also a proponent of reproducible analysis[^o17] in the context of statistics education.[^o18]

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Çetinkaya-Rundel was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) in 2020.[^o20] She was elected chair of the ASA Statistical Computing program in 2021.[^o21]

### Selected publications {#Selected_publications}
