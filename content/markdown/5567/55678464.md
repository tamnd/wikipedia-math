# Inverse Dirichlet distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Inverse Dirichlet distribution"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **inverse Dirichlet distribution** is a derivation of the [matrix variate Dirichlet distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_variate_Dirichlet_distribution). It is related to the [inverse Wishart distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_Wishart_distribution).

:::

Suppose $U_1,\ldots,U_r$ are $p\times p$ [positive definite matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_definite_matrix) with a [matrix variate Dirichlet distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_variate_Dirichlet_distribution), $\left(U_1,\ldots,U_r\right)\sim D_p\left(a_1,\ldots,a_r;a_{r+1}\right)$. Then $X_i={U_i}^{-1},i=1,\ldots,r$ have an inverse Dirichlet distribution, written $\left(X_1,\ldots,X_r\right)\sim \operatorname{ID}\left(a_1,\ldots,a_r;a_{r+1}\right)$. Their joint [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) is given by

$$\left\{\beta_p\left(a_1,\ldots,a_r;a_{r+1}\right)\right\}^{-1} \prod_{i=1}^r \det\left(X_i\right)^{-a_i-(p+1)/2}\det\left(I_p-\sum_{i=1}^r{X_i}^{-1}\right)^{a_{r+1}-(p+1)/2}$$
