# Stephen S. Kudla

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen S. Kudla"}

**Stephen S. Kudla** (born 1950 Caracas, Venezuela[^o3]) is an American mathematician working in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146). He is a professor in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Life {#Life}

After receiving his doctorate, Kudla spent a year at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, following which he joined the faculty at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park).[^o7] Since 2006, he has been a professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) where he held a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) for the period 2006–2020.

In 1997, he discovered relationships between the Fourier coefficients of derivatives of [Siegel Eisenstein series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_Eisenstein_series) and arithmetic invariants of [Shimura varieties](wikipedia://en/10389861) (heights pairings of arithmetic cycles).[^o8]

He was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellow) in 1981, received the Max-Planck Research Award in 2000, and the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) in 2009. He was an Invited Speaker at the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing, where he gave a lecture on "Derivatives of Eisenstein series and arithmetic geometry". He was on the Scientific Review Panel of the [Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/34978739) (PIMS) for the period 2009–2013. For 2004 - 2010, he was a co-editor of the [Canadian Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Journal_of_Mathematics), and has been co-organizer of several conferences at the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach).
