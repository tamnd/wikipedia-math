# Karl H. Hofmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl H. Hofmann"}

**Karl Heinrich Hofmann** (born 3 October 1932[^o3]), known professionally as **Karl H. Hofmann** is a German mathematician and illustrator who is professor emeritus at the [Technical University of Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Darmstadt) (TU Darmstadt). His research is primarily focused on [topological algebra](wikipedia://en/2688895) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Hofmann began his first mathematics degree at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) but transferred to the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) and completed it there. He remained in Tübingen for his master's degree and Ph.D. which was completed in 1958 under supervisor [Hellmuth Kneser](wikipedia://en/1368525).

## Career {#Career}

Most of Hofmann's work is in [lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and [topological analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_analysis). His book with [Sidney Morris](wikipedia://en/80500214), *The Structure of Compact Groups*, has been printed in four editions and is a standard reference for the subject.[^o7][^o8]

The [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen) hired Hofmann as an assistant professor following the completion of his Ph.D. in 1958. He remained at Tübingen until 1963 where he became an associate professor at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University). He became a full professor two years later an in 1979 the university appointed him as W. R. Irby Professor of Mathematics.[^o3] Hofmann supervised 37 doctoral students throughout his fifty-year career.[^o5] In 2013, Hofmann was inducted as a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

Alongside his academic career, Hofmann worked as an illustrator. He illustrated multiple books, including *[Proofs from THE BOOK](wikipedia://en/4500115)*, a [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) winner.[^o10] He has also created posters for [TU Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Darmstadt)'s colloquiums since 1983.[^o11][^o12]
