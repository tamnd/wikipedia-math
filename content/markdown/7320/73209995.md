# Margaret Smagorinsky

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Smagorinsky"}

**Margaret Smagorinsky** (23 December 1915 – 14 November 2011) was an American statistician, computer programmer, and weather technologist.[^o3] She was the first female statistician hired by the [United States Weather Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Weather_Service).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Smagorinsky was born in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), New York, as the second of four daughters of Anne and George Knoepfel. She attended [Bay Ridge High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_Ridge_High_School) in Brooklyn.[^o5]

Smagorinsky was the first member of her family to attend college when she attended [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College). She graduated at age 19 with a degree in mathematics, and taught school in a one-room schoolhouse in Ashland, New York, for four years.[^o6]

## Statistics career {#Statistics_career}

Smagorinsky took the [civil service exam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Civil_Service_Commission) for a statistical clerk in the US. She was offered a job at the [Railroad Retirement Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railroad_Retirement_Board) in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), where she worked as a statistician processing paperwork for employees looking to enter the civil service.

At age 26, Smagorinsky joined the Weather Bureau, where she was the first woman statistician.[^o6][^o8] After being sent to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) for additional coursework,[^o9] she met [Joseph Smagorinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Smagorinsky) in a graduate statistics course. He was 8 years her junior, and they married on May 29, 1948.[^o5]

Smagorinsky had five children: Anne, [Peter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Smagorinsky), Teresa, Julia, and Frederick. She left full-time government employment after her first child was born in 1951.

Smagorinsky, in supporting her husband's work, processed data and programmed the [ENIAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENIAC) (Electronic Numerical Integrator And Computer).[^o10] In April 1950, a group of meteorologists at New Jersey's [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) successfully produced the first weather forecast using the ENIAC and numerical prediction techniques.[^o11][^o12] Smagorinsky is cited as a programmer of computers for 5-day weather forecast models created by the Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory at the [National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Oceanic_and_Atmospheric_Administration).[^o10]
