# Marie A. Vitulli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marie A. Vitulli"}

**Marie A. Vitulli** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor emerita at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Vitulli's research is in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and applications to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). More specific topics in her research include [deformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory) of monomial curves, [seminormal rings](wikipedia://en/35984591), the weak normality of commutative rings and algebraic varieties, weak subintegrality, and the theory of [valuations](wikipedia://en/347049) for commutative rings.[^o15] Along with her colleague [David K. Harrison](wikipedia://en/58145902), she developed a unified valuation theory for rings with zero divisors that generalized both Krull and Archimedean valuations.

She was an undergraduate at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester)[^o15] and obtained her PhD in 1976 at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) under the supervision of Dock-Sang Rim. Her dissertation was *Weierstrass Points and Monomial Curves*. The title of her 2014 Falconer lecture was "From Algebraic to Weak Subintegral Extensions in Algebra and Geometry."[^o5]

## Activism {#Activism}

Vitulli and political scientist [Gordon Lafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Lafer) led an effort to unionize faculty at the University of Oregon beginning in the spring of 2007.[^o7] This effort eventually led to the formation of the United Academics at the University of Oregon.[^o8]

Vitulli heads the Women in Math Project at the University of Oregon.[^o15][^o9] With [Mary Flahive](wikipedia://en/56636978), Vitulli has also studied patterns in hiring among women mathematicians.[^o10] Vitulli has also written about the difficulties involved with documenting the lives of female mathematicians on Wikipedia.[^o11]

Vitulli participated in the panel discussion held as the first official activity of [Spectra](wikipedia://en/61935783) at the 2015 [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143).[^o12] She endowed the [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Marie A. Vitulli Graduate Fellowship to "support one advanced graduate student in mathematics, per academic year, to attend an MSRI program with their advisor."[^o13]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Vitulli was recognized as an [AWM/MAA Falconer Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM/MAA_Falconer_Lecturer) in 2014.[^o15] Vitulli received a Service Award from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in 2017.[^o16]

She is part of the 2019 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o17] She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to commutative algebra, and for service to the mathematical community particularly in support of women in mathematics".[^o18]
