# István Juhász (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="István Juhász"}

**István Juhász** (born 2 July 1943, Budapest) is a Hungarian mathematician, working in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He works at the [Rényi Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Juhász graduated from [Eötvös Universit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University)y, Budapest in 1966 and worked there until 1974 when he moved to the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences), where he is currently a professor emeritus.

Juhász obtained a DSc degree in 1977 from the Academy and he was elected a corresponding member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences (2007). He was the president of the European Set Theory Society for the period 2015–2018.[^o3] He is a member of the editorial board of the journals *Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum* and *[Topology and its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology_and_its_Applications)*.
