# Wilson polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilson polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Wilson polynomials** are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by James Wilson[^o2] that generalize [Jacobi polynomials](wikipedia://en/30863587), [Hahn polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585658), and [Charlier polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585960).

:::

They are defined in terms of the [generalized hypergeometric function](wikipedia://en/361184) and the [Pochhammer symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pochhammer_symbol) by

$$p_n(t^2)=(a+b)_n(a+c)_n(a+d)_n {}_4F_3\left( \begin{matrix} -n&a+b+c+d+n-1&a-t&a+t \\ a+b&a+c&a+d \end{matrix} ;1\right).$$

## See also {#See_also}

- [Askey–Wilson polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askey–Wilson_polynomial) are a q-analogue of Wilson polynomials.
