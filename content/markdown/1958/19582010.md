# Askey–Wilson polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Askey–Wilson polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Askey–Wilson polynomials** (or ***q*-Wilson polynomials**) are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by [Richard Askey](wikipedia://en/2836040) and [James A. Wilson](wikipedia://en/32810784) as [q-analogs](wikipedia://en/1619396) of the [Wilson polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585496).[^n1] They include many of the other orthogonal polynomials in 1 variable as [special](wikipedia://en/31308010) or [limiting cases](wikipedia://en/40400729), described in the [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). Askey–Wilson polynomials are the special case of [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285) (or [Koornwinder polynomials](wikipedia://en/19599247)) for the non-reduced [affine root system](wikipedia://en/32702474) of type ($C^{\lor}_{1}, C_{1}$), and their 4 parameters $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ correspond to the 4 orbits of roots of this root system.

:::

They are defined by

$$p_n(x) = p_n(x;a,b,c,d\mid q) := a^{-n}(ab,ac,ad;q)_n\;_{4}\phi_3 \left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n}&abcdq^{n-1}&ae^{i\theta}&ae^{-i\theta} \\ ab&ac&ad \end{matrix} ; q,q \right]$$

where $\varphi$ is a [basic hypergeometric function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function), $x = \cos \theta$, and $(,,,)_{n}$ is the [*q*-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717). **Askey–Wilson functions** are a generalization to non-integral values of $n$.

::: {#o4 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

This result can be proven since it is known that

$$p_n(\cos{\theta}) = p_n(\cos{\theta};a,b,c,d\mid q)$$

and using the definition of the *q*-Pochhammer symbol

$$p_n(\cos{\theta})= a^{-n}\sum_{\ell=0}^{n}q^{\ell}\left(abq^{\ell},acq^{\ell},adq^{\ell};q\right)_{n-\ell}\times\frac{\left(q^{-n},abcdq^{n-1};q\right)_{\ell}}{(q;q)_{\ell}}\prod_{j=0}^{\ell-1}\left(1-2aq^j\cos{\theta}+a^2q^{2j}\right)$$

which leads to the conclusion that it equals

$$a^{-n}(ab,ac,ad;q)_n\;_{4}\phi_3 \left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n}&abcdq^{n-1}&ae^{i\theta}&ae^{-i\theta} \\ ab&ac&ad \end{matrix} ; q,q \right]$$

:::
