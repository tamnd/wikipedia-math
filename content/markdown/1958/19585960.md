# Charlier polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charlier polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Charlier polynomials**, also called **Poisson–Charlier polynomials**, are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by [Carl Charlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Charlier) in 1905.[^n1] They are given in terms of the [generalized hypergeometric function](wikipedia://en/361184) by

:::

$$C_n(x; \mu)= {}_2F_0(-n,-x;-;-1/\mu)=(-1)^n n! L_n^{(-1-x)}(-1/\mu),$$

where $L$ are [generalized Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917). They satisfy the following [orthogonality relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonality_relation) in the [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) of square summable sequences associated with the [Poisson distribution](wikipedia://en/23009144) with parameter $\mu$

$$e^\mu\langle C_n(\cdot,\mu),C_m(\cdot,\mu)\rangle=\sum_{x=0}^\infty \frac{\mu^x}{x!} C_n(x; \mu)C_m(x; \mu)=e^\mu\mu^{-n} n! \delta_{nm}, \quad \mu>0,$$

where $\delta_{nm}$ is the [Kronecker delta](wikipedia://en/182890). They form a [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596) related to the [Poisson process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_process), similar to how [Hermite polynomials](wikipedia://en/195984) relate to [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436).

Charlier polynomials have a [*q*-analog](wikipedia://en/1619396), the ***q*-Charlier polynomials**, which are given in terms of the [basic hypergeometric function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$C_n(q^{-x};a;q) = {}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},q^{-x};0;q,-q^{n+1}/a).$$
