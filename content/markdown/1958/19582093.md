::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="2 22 honeycomb"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **2<sub>22</sub> honeycomb** is a [uniform tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb) of the six-dimensional Euclidean space. It can be represented by the [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,3<sup>2,2</sup>}. It is constructed from **[2<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19232473)** [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) and has a **[1<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070548)** [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719), with 54 **2<sub>21</sub>** polytopes around every vertex.

:::

Its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is the *E<sub>6</sub> lattice*, and the [root system](wikipedia://en/277087) of the [E<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725) [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) so it can also be called the **E<sub>6</sub> honeycomb**.

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Kissing number {#Kissing_number}

Each vertex of this tessellation is the center of a 5-sphere in the densest known [packing](wikipedia://en/368621) in 6 dimensions, with [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 72, represented by the vertices of its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) [1<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070548).

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The 2<sub>22</sub> honeycomb is one of 127 uniform honeycombs (39 unique) with ${\tilde{E}}_6$ symmetry. 24 of them have doubled symmetry \[\[3,3,3<sup>2,2</sup>\]\] with 2 equally ringed branches, and 7 have sextupled (3[!](wikipedia://en/10606)) symmetry \[\[3,3<sup>2,2,2</sup>\]\] with identical rings on all 3 branches. There are no regular honeycombs in the family since its Coxeter diagram a nonlinear graph, but the 2<sub>22</sub> and birectified 2<sub>22</sub> are [isotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive#Related_terms), with only one type of [facet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet): [2<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19232473), and [rectified 1<sub>22</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_1_22_polytope) polytopes respectively.

#### Construction {#Construction}

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter&ndash;Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter&ndash;Dynkin_diagram), .

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes a triangular proprism {3}×{3}×{3} (or triangular triprism), .

Removing a node on the end of one of the 3-node branches leaves the [rectified 1<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070548), its only [facet](wikipedia://en/7186647) type, .

Removing a second end node defines 2 types of 5-faces: [birectified 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_5-simplex), 0<sub>22</sub> and [birectified 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_5-orthoplex), 0<sub>211</sub>.

Removing a third end node defines 2 types of 4-faces: [rectified 5-cell](wikipedia://en/2720430), 0<sub>21</sub>, and [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), 0<sub>111</sub>.

Removing a fourth end node defines 2 types of cells: [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), 0<sub>11</sub>, and [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), 0<sub>20</sub>.

:::

### k<sub>22</sub> polytopes {#k22_polytopes}

The 2<sub>22</sub> honeycomb, is fourth in a dimensional series of uniform polytopes, expressed by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) as k<sub>22</sub> series. The final is a paracompact hyperbolic honeycomb, [3<sub>22</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3_22_honeycomb). Each progressive [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) is constructed from the previous as its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

The 2<sub>22</sub> honeycomb is third in another dimensional series 2<sub>2k</sub>.
