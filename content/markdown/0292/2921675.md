# Michael D. Morley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael D. Morley"}

**Michael Darwin Morley** (September 29, 1930 – October 11, 2020) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). At his death in 2020, Morley was professor emeritus at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). His research was in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), and he is best known for [Morley's categoricity theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morley's_categoricity_theorem), which he proved in his PhD thesis *Categoricity in Power* in 1962.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Morley was born in [Youngstown, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown,_Ohio), on September 29, 1930.[^o4][^o5] He obtained his BS in mathematics from [Case Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Institute_of_Technology) in 1951 and his PhD in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1962. Morley's formal PhD advisor at the University of Chicago was [Saunders Mac Lane](wikipedia://en/336574), but he completed his thesis under the guidance of [Robert Vaught](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Vaught) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o4] His dissertation was titled *Categoricity in Power*.

## Career {#Career}

Morley was an assistant professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) from 1963 to 1967. He joined the faculty at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1967 as an associate professor, was promoted to professor in 1970, and became a professor emeritus in 2003.[^o4] He served as president of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438) from 1986 to 1989.[^o4]

Morley received the 2003 [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Seminal Contribution to Research from the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his 1965 paper "Categoricity in Power". This paper, his doctoral dissertation, introduced [Morley rank](wikipedia://en/1401941) and proved [Morley's categoricity theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morley's_categoricity_theorem).[^o4][^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Morley died on October 11, 2020, in [Sayre, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayre,_Pennsylvania).[^o4]
