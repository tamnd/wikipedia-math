# Yvon Villarceau

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yvon Villarceau"}

**Antoine-Joseph Yvon Villarceau** (15 January 1813 – 23 December 1883) was a French [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), mathematician, and engineer.

:::

He constructed an equatorial [meridian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meridian_%28astronomy%29)-instrument and an isochronometric regulator for the [Paris Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Observatory).

He wrote *Mécanique Céleste. Expose des Méthodes de Wronski et Composantes des Forces Perturbatrices suivant les Axes Mobiles* (Paris: Gauthier-Villars, 1881) and *Sur l'établissement des arches de pont, envisagé au point de vue de la plus grande stabilité* (Paris: Imprimerie Impériale, 1853).

He is the eponym of [Villarceau circles](wikipedia://en/1547360), which are two circular sections of a [torus](wikipedia://en/74800) other than the two trivial ones.

A short street in the [16th arrondissement of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_arrondissement_of_Paris) is named after Villarceau.
