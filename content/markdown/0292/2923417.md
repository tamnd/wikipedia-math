# Interprime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Interprime"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **interprime** is the [average](wikipedia://en/612) of two consecutive [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [primes](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o2] For example, 9 is an interprime because it is the average of 7 and 11. The first interprimes are:

:::

4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 26, 30, 34, 39, 42, 45, 50, 56, 60, 64, 69, 72, 76, 81, 86, 93, 99, ...

Interprimes cannot be prime themselves (otherwise the primes would not have been consecutive).[^o2]

Since there are infinitely many primes, there are also infinitely many interprimes.
