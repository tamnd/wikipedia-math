# Institute of Statistical Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Statistical Mathematics"}

*Image: Institute of Statistical Mathematics.jpg.* The facilities in Tachikawa.

*Image: NIPR and ISM Library in Jan 2016.JPG.* ISM Library

The **Institute of Statistical Mathematics** is Japan's national research institute for [statistical science](wikipedia://en/26685). In October 2009, it relocated from the [Azabu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azabu) district of Tokyo to [Tachikawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachikawa). Founded in 1944, since 2004 it has been part of the Research Organization of Information and Systems (情報・システム研究機構).

:::

The Institute is represented on the national [Coordinating Committee for Earthquake Prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinating_Committee_for_Earthquake_Prediction).[^o2]

## Publications {#Publications}

The Institute publishes the scientific journal *[Annals of the Institute of Statistical Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annals_of_the_Institute_of_Statistical_Mathematics)* and the [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis) software package TIMSAC.
