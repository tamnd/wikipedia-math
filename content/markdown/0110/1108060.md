# Felix Iversen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Iversen"}

*Image: Felix-Iversen-1967.jpg.* Iversen in 1951

**Felix Christian Herbert Iversen** (22 October 1887 – 31 July 1973) was a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)[^o2] and a [pacifist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacifist).[^o3] He was a student of [Ernst Lindelöf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Lindelöf), and later an associate professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University) of [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki).[^o5] Although he stopped performing serious research in mathematics around 1922, he continued working as a professor until his retirement in 1954 and published a textbook on mathematics in 1950.[^o2] The [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) awarded Felix Iversen the [Stalin Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalin_Peace_Prize) in 1954.[^o5]
