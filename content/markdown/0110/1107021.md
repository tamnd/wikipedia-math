# Accumulation function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Accumulation function"}

In actuarial mathematics, the **accumulation function** *a*(*t*) is a function of time *t* expressing the ratio of the value at time *t* ([future value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_value)) and the initial investment ([present value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Present_value)).[^o2][^o3] It is used in [interest theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interest_theory).

:::

Thus *a*(0) = 1 and the value at time *t* is given by:

$$A(t) = A(0) \cdot a(t).$$

where the initial investment is $A(0).$

For various interest-accumulation protocols, the accumulation function is as follows (with *i* denoting the [interest rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interest_rate) and *d* denoting the [discount rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annual_effective_discount_rate)):

- [simple interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_interest): $a(t)=1+t \cdot i$
- [compound interest](wikipedia://en/434428): $a(t)=(1+i)^t$
- [simple discount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_discount): $a(t) = 1+\frac{td}{1-d}$
- [compound discount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_discount): $a(t) = (1-d)^{-t}$

In the case of a positive [rate of return](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_of_return), as in the case of interest, the accumulation function is an [increasing function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Increasing_function).

## Variable rate of return {#Variable_rate_of_return}

The [logarithmic or continuously compounded return](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_of_return#Logarithmic_or_continuously_compounded_return), sometimes called [force of interest](wikipedia://en/434428#Force_of_interest), is a function of time defined as follows:

$$\delta_{t}=\frac{a'(t)}{a(t)}\,$$

which is the rate of change with time of the natural logarithm of the accumulation function.

Conversely:

$$a(t)= \exp \left( \int_0^t \delta_u\, du \right),$$

reducing to

$$a(t)=e^{t \delta}$$

for constant $\delta$.

The effective [annual percentage rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annual_percentage_rate) at any time is:

$$r(t) = e^{\delta_t} - 1$$
