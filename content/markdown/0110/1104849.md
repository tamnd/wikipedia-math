# Efim Zelmanov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Efim Zelmanov"}

**Efim Isaakovich Zelmanov** (Ефи́м Исаа́кович Зе́льманов; born 7 September 1955) is a Russian-American[^o3] mathematician, known for his work on combinatorial problems in [nonassociative algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonassociative_algebra) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), including his solution of the [restricted Burnside problem](wikipedia://en/151452). He was awarded a [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) in 1994.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Zelmanov was born on 7 September 1955 into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) family in [Khabarovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khabarovsk). He entered [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1972, when he was 17 years old.[^o5] He obtained a doctoral degree at [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1980, and a higher degree at [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University) in 1985. He had a position in Novosibirsk until 1987, when he left the Soviet Union.

*Image: Zelmanov and Hunter in Lincoln.jpg.* Efim Zelmanov (right) with the [University of Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lincoln) (UK) Deputy Vice-Chancellor Professor Andrew Hunter (left) after receiving Honorary DSc degree. 5th September 2016, Lincoln, UK.

In 1990, he moved to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), becoming a professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). He was at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1994/5, then at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). In 1996, he became a Distinguished Professor at the [Korea Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/16984) and in 2002, he became a professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).[^o6] In 2011 he got hon DSc from QUB (Belfast)[^o7]

In 2022, he moved to the [People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Republic_of_China) and joined the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in Shenzhen, China.[^o8][^o9] He served as a chair professor and the scientific director of the SUSTech International Center for Mathematics.

Zelmanov was elected a member of the [U.S. National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2001,[^o10] becoming, at the age of 47, the youngest member of the mathematics section of the academy.[^o11] He is also an elected member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (1996)[^o12] and a foreign member of the [Korean Academy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Academy_of_Science_and_Technology) and of the [Spanish Royal Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Royal_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o13] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]

Zelmanov gave invited talks at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Warsaw (1983), Kyoto (1990) and Zurich (1994).[^o15] He delivered the 2004 Turán Memorial Lectures.[^o16] He was awarded Honorary Doctor degrees from the [University of Hagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hagen), Germany (1997),[^o17] the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta), Canada (2011),[^o18] [Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv), Ukraine (2012),[^o19] the [Universidad Internacional Menéndez Pelayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Internacional_Menéndez_Pelayo) in Santander, Spain (2015),[^o20] the [University of Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lincoln), UK (2016),[^o21][^o22] and the [Vrije Universiteit Brussel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit_Brussel), Belgium (2023).[^o23]

Zelmanov's early work was on [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371) in the case of infinite dimensions. He was able to show that [Glennie's identity](wikipedia://en/36151261) in a certain sense generates all identities that hold. He then showed that the [Engel identity](wikipedia://en/36147939) for [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) implies [nilpotence](wikipedia://en/3358867), in the case of infinite dimensions.
