# Affine action

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Affine action"}

Let $W$ be the [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332) of a [semisimple Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1337587) $\mathfrak{g}$ (associate to fixed choice of a [Cartan subalgebra](wikipedia://en/1336000) $\mathfrak{h}$). Assume that a set of [simple roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_root_%28root_system%29) in $\mathfrak{h}^*$ is chosen.

:::

The **affine action** (also called the *dot action*) of the Weyl group on the space $\mathfrak{h}^*$ is

$$w\cdot \lambda:=w(\lambda+\delta)-\delta$$

where $\delta$ is the sum of all [fundamental weights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_weight), or, equivalently, the half of the sum of all [positive roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_root).
