# List of small polyhedra by vertex count

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of small polyhedra by vertex count"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) is a solid in [three dimensions](wikipedia://en/3054853) with flat faces and straight edges. Every edge has exactly two faces, and every vertex is surrounded by alternating faces and edges. The smallest polyhedron is the [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) with 4 triangular faces, 6 edges, and 4 vertices. Named polyhedra primarily come from the families of [platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905), [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847), [Catalan solids](wikipedia://en/564393), and [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034), as well as dihedral symmetry families including the [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045), [bipyramids](wikipedia://en/4153), [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007), [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851), and [trapezohedrons](wikipedia://en/719845).

:::
