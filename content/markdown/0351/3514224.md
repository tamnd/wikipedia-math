# Bicupola

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bicupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **bicupola** is a solid formed by connecting two [cupolae](wikipedia://en/689743) on their bases. Here, two classes of bicupola are included because each cupola (bicupola half) is bordered by alternating triangles and squares. If similar faces are attached together the result is an *orthobicupola*; if squares are attached to triangles it is a *gyrobicupola*.

:::

### Set of gyrobicupolae {#Set_of_gyrobicupolae}

An $n$-gonal gyrobicupola has the same topology as an $n$-gonal rectified antiprism, [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705): $\operatorname{aA}n$.

::: {#o7 .table cue=table}

| [Symmetry](wikipedia://en/2871265) | Picture | Description |
|---|---|---|
| $D_{2d} [2^{+},4] 2*2$ | *Image: Gyrobifastigium.png.* | [Gyrobifastigium](wikipedia://en/1177963) ($J_{26}$) or *digonal gyrobicupola*: 4 triangles, 4 squares. |
| $D_{3d} [2^{+},6] 2*3$ | *Image: Cuboctahedron.png.* | *Triangular gyrobicupola* or [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280): 8 triangles, 6 squares. Its dual is the [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631). |
| $D_{4d} [2^{+},8] 2*4$ | *Image: Square gyrobicupola.png.* | [Square gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128615) ($J_{29}$): 8 triangles, 10 squares. Its dual is the elongated [tetragonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3504410) |
| $D_{5d} [2^{+},10] 2*5$ | *Image: Pentagonal gyrobicupola.png.* | [Pentagonal gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1177965) ($J_{31}$): 10 triangles, 10 squares, 2 pentagons. Its dual is the elongated [pentagonal trapezohedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_trapezohedron) |
| $D_{nd} [2^{+},2n] 2*n$ |  | $n$-gonal gyrobicupola: $2n$ triangles, $2n$ rectangles, 2 $n$-gons. |
