# Elongated dodecahedron

*Image: Elongated Dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of an elongated dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated dodecahedron**, **elongated rhombic dodecahedron**, **extended rhombic dodecahedron**, **rhombo-hexagonal dodecahedron** or **hexarhombic dodecahedron** is a convex [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) with eight [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) and four [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Given that $a$ is the edge length of an elongated dodecahedron. One can calculate its surface area $A$ and volume $V$ in the formulas below:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= 2\left(3\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{15}\right)a^2 \approx 18.14a^2\\ V &= 6a^3\\ \end{aligned}$$

:::

::: {#o6 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Elongated dodecahedral honeycomb"}

## Parallelohedron {#Parallelohedron}

The elongated dodecahedron can be constructed by [elongating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_%28geometry%29) a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631) – i.e., slicing it into two congruent concave polyhedra and covering the bases of a [square prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_prism). As a result, it has eighteen vertices, twenty-eight edges, and twelve faces (which include eight rhombi and four hexagons).

Both the rhombic dodecahedron and the elongated dodecahedron are two of the five types of [parallelohedron](wikipedia://en/25207334) identified by [Evgraf Fedorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgraf_Fedorov). In other words, it is a [space-filling polyhedron](wikipedia://en/58607583), meaning the elongated dodecahedron and its copy can [tile space](wikipedia://en/6008654) face-to-face by translations periodically. For the elongated dodecahedron, it has five sets of parallel edges called zones or belts. This produces an elongated dodecahedral honeycomb. It is the [Wigner–Seitz cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner–Seitz_cell) for certain [body-centered tetragonal lattices](wikipedia://en/661808).

*Image: Rhombo-hexagonal dodecahedron tessellation.png.* Elongated dodecahedral honeycomb

This is related to the [rhombic dodecahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_dodecahedral_honeycomb) with an elongation of zero. Projected normal to the elongation direction, the honeycomb looks like a [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) with the [rhombi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombi) projected into [squares](wikipedia://en/659939).

## Variations {#Variations}

The expanded dodecahedra can be distorted into cubic volumes, with the honeycomb as a half-offset stacking of cubes. It can also be made concave by adjusting the 8 corners downward by the same amount as the centers are moved up.

The elongated dodecahedron can be constructed as a contraction of a uniform [truncated octahedron](wikipedia://en/279654), where square faces are reduced to single edges and regular hexagonal faces are reduced to 60-degree rhombic faces (or pairs of equilateral triangles). This construction alternates square and rhombi on the 4-valence vertices, and has half the symmetry, D<sub>2h</sub> symmetry, order 8.
