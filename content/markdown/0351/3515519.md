# Lambert quadrilateral

*Image: Lambert quadrilateral.svg.* A Lambert quadrilateral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lambert quadrilateral"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **Lambert quadrilateral** (also known as **Ibn al-Haytham–Lambert quadrilateral**),[^o2][^o3] is a [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278) in which three of its angles are right angles. Historically, the fourth angle of a Lambert quadrilateral was of considerable interest since if it could be shown to be a right angle, then the Euclidean [parallel postulate](wikipedia://en/33731493) could be proved as a theorem. It is now known that the type of the fourth angle depends upon the geometry in which the quadrilateral exists. In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) the fourth angle is [acute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acute_angle), in [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417) it is a [right angle](wikipedia://en/76956) and in [elliptic geometry](wikipedia://en/236020) it is an [obtuse angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obtuse_angle).

:::

A Lambert quadrilateral can be constructed from a [Saccheri quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/3818958) by joining the midpoints of the base and summit of the Saccheri quadrilateral. This line segment is perpendicular to both the base and summit and so either half of the Saccheri quadrilateral is a Lambert quadrilateral.

## Lambert quadrilateral in hyperbolic geometry {#Lambert_quadrilateral_in_hyperbolic_geometry}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) a Lambert quadrilateral *AOBF* where the [angles](wikipedia://en/1196) $\angle FAO , \angle AOB , \angle OBF$ are [right](wikipedia://en/76956), and *F* is opposite *O* ,$\angle AFB$ is an [acute angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acute_angle), and the [curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) = -1 the following relations hold:[^o5]

$$\sinh AF = \sinh OB \cosh BF$$

$$\tanh AF = \cosh OA \tanh OB$$

$$\sinh BF = \sinh OA \cosh AF$$

$$\tanh BF = \cosh OB \tanh OA$$

$$\cosh OF = \cosh OA \cosh AF$$

$$\cosh OF = \cosh OB \cosh BF$$

$$\sin \angle AFB = \frac {\cosh OB}{ \cosh AF} = \frac {\cosh OA}{ \cosh BF }$$

$$\cos \angle AFB = \sinh OA \sinh OB = \tanh AF \tanh BF$$

$$\cot \angle AFB = \tanh OA \sinh AF = \tanh OB \sinh BF$$

$$\sin \angle AOF = \frac {\sinh AF}{ \sinh OF}$$

$$\cos \angle AOF = \frac {\tanh OA}{ \tanh OF}$$

$$\tan \angle AOF = \frac {\tanh AF}{ \sinh OA}$$

Where $\tanh , \cosh , \sinh$ are [hyperbolic functions](wikipedia://en/56567)
