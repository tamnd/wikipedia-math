# Hiroaki Terao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hiroaki Terao"}

**Hiroaki Terao** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known as, with [Peter Orlik](wikipedia://en/46865643) and [Louis Solomon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Solomon), a pioneer of the theory of [arrangements of hyperplanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrangements_of_hyperplanes). He was awarded a [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) Algebra Prize in 2010.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Terao started his studies at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo), where he earned in 1974 his bachelor's degree and in 1976 his master's degree. For his graduate studies he went to [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University), where he earned in 1981 his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree, with a thesis written under the supervision of [Kyoji Saito](wikipedia://en/56830460).

## Career {#Career}

He held teaching positions at [International Christian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Christian_University) (1977–1991), [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) (1990–1999), [Tokyo Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_University) (1998–2006), and [Hokkaido University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokkaido_University) (1996–1998, 2006–2015). He was [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the school of science of Hokkaido University (2013–2015), after which he became [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_president) of Hokkaido University (2015–2017). He has been a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at Hokkaido University since 2017. He is currently a guest professor at [Tokyo Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_University).

## Research {#Research}

In 1983, Terao asked whether the freeness of an arrangement is determined from its [intersection lattice](wikipedia://en/864438#The_intersection_semilattice_and_the_matroid).[^o6] This problem is now known as the **Terao conjecture**, and is still open.
