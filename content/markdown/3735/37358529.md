# British Mathematical Olympiad Subtrust

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="British Mathematical Olympiad Subtrust"}

The **British Mathematical Olympiad Subtrust** (**BMOS**) is a section of the [United Kingdom Mathematics Trust](wikipedia://en/552624) which currently runs the [British Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/3264277) as well as the UK Mathematical Olympiad for Girls,[^o2] several training camps throughout the year such as a winter camp in [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary), an Easter camp at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), and other training and selection of the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) team. Since 1999, it also organizes the [UK National Mathematics Summer Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_National_Mathematics_Summer_Schools).[^o3] It was established alongside the British Mathematical Olympiad Committee (BMOC) in 1991 with the support of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940), [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139), the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), and the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403), each nominated two members. The BMOS replaced some of the Mathematical Association's activities.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

In 1996, the United Kingdom Mathematics Trust (UKMT) was set up to manage competitions of this nature, though the BMOC remained in charge of the senior olympiads.[^o6][^o7]

## Problems group {#Problems_group}

The 'problems group' is a subsection of the BMOS which is responsible for supplying new and interesting problems for use domestic competitions and for submission to the IMO each year.[^o7]
