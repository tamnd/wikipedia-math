# Frank Nielsen (computer scientist)

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Nielsen"}

**Frank Nielsen** is a [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) working on computational and [information geometry](wikipedia://en/487312). He became an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_fellows_of_the_IEEE) in 2026 for his "contributions to geometric information theory and information measures".[^o5][^o6] He received his PhD from [Université Nice-Sophia-Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Nice-Sophia-Antipolis) under the supervision of [Jean-Daniel Boissonnat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Daniel_Boissonnat).[^o3]

:::

He is known for his work on [statistical region merging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_region_merging),[^o7] as well as his contributions to information geometry including several introductory writings about the topic.[^o8]

## Books {#Books}

- *Computational Information Geometry*, 2017, Springer International Publishing AG.
- *A Concise and Practical Introduction to Programming Algorithms in Java*, 2009, Springer London.
- *Introduction to HPC with MPI for Data Science*, 2016, Springer International Publishing Switzerland.
- *Matrix Information Geometry*, 2012, Springer Berlin, Heidelberg.
- *Visual Computing: Geometry, Graphics, And Vision (Graphics Series)*, 2005, Charles River Media, Inc.
