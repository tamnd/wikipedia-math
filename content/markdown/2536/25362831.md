# Rees algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rees algebra"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), the **Rees algebra** or **Rees ring** of an [ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_of_a_ring) *I* in a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) *R* is defined to be

> $$R[It]=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty} I^n t^{n}\subseteq R[t].$$

The **extended Rees algebra** of *I* (which some authors[^o2] refer to as the Rees algebra of *I*) is defined as

> $$R[It,t^{-1}]=\bigoplus_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}I^nt^{n}\subseteq R[t,t^{-1}].$$

This construction has special interest in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) since the [projective scheme](wikipedia://en/2285000) defined by the Rees algebra of an ideal in a ring is the [blowing-up](wikipedia://en/1817790) of the [spectrum](wikipedia://en/37291) of the ring along the [subscheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subscheme) defined by the ideal (see Ideal sheaf).[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Rees algebra is an algebra over $\mathbb{Z}[t^{-1}]$, and it is defined so that, quotienting by $t^{-1}=0$ or *t=λ* for *λ* any invertible element in *R*, we get

> $$\text{gr}_I R \ \leftarrow\ R[It]\ \to\ R.$$

Thus it interpolates between *R* and its associated graded ring *gr<sub>I</sub>R*.

- Assume *R* is [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129); then *R\[It\]* is also Noetherian. The [Krull dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) of the Rees algebra is $\dim R[It]=\dim R+1$ if *I* is not contained in any [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) *P* with $\dim(R/P)=\dim R$; otherwise $\dim R[It]=\dim R$. The Krull dimension of the extended Rees algebra is $\dim R[It, t^{-1}]=\dim R+1$.[^o6]
- If $J\subseteq I$ are ideals in a Noetherian ring *R*, then the ring extension $R[Jt]\subseteq R[It]$ is [integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_extension) if and only if *J* is a reduction of *I*.[^o6]
- If *I* is an ideal in a Noetherian ring *R*, then the Rees algebra of *I* is the [quotient](wikipedia://en/184635) of the [symmetric algebra](wikipedia://en/654098) of *I* by its [torsion](wikipedia://en/3039330) submodule.

:::

## Relationship with other blow-up algebras {#Relationship_with_other_blow-up_algebras}

The [associated graded ring](wikipedia://en/38279783) of *I* may be defined as

> $$\operatorname{gr}_I(R)=R[It]/IR[It].$$

If *R* is a Noetherian [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, then the [special fiber ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_fiber_ring) of *I* is given by

> $$\mathcal{F}_I(R)=R[It]/\mathfrak{m}R[It].$$

The Krull dimension of the special fiber ring is called the [analytic spread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_spread) of *I*.
