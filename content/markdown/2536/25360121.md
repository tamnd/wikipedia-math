# Hilbert–Samuel function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hilbert–Samuel function"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) the **Hilbert–Samuel function**, named after [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) and [Pierre Samuel](wikipedia://en/2604194),[^o2] of a nonzero [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/326430) [module](wikipedia://en/276410) $M$ over a commutative [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) $A$ and a [primary ideal](wikipedia://en/3021435) $I$ of $A$ is the map $\chi_{M}^{I}:\mathbb{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{N}$ such that, for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$,

:::

$$\chi_{M}^{I}(n)=\ell(M/I^{n}M)$$

where $\ell$ denotes the [length](wikipedia://en/816070) over $A$. It is related to the [Hilbert function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_function) of the [associated graded module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_graded_module) $\operatorname{gr}_I(M)$ by the identity

$$\chi_M^I (n)=\sum_{i=0}^n H(\operatorname{gr}_I(M),i).$$

For sufficiently large $n$, it coincides with a polynomial function of degree equal to $\dim(\operatorname{gr}_I(M))$, often called the **Hilbert-Samuel polynomial** (or [Hilbert polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_polynomial)).[^o3]

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

For the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) of [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012) in two variables $k[[x,y]]$ taken as a module over itself and the ideal $I$ generated by the monomials *x*<sup>2</sup> and *y*<sup>3</sup> we have

$\chi(1)=6,\quad \chi(2)=18,\quad \chi(3)=36,\quad \chi(4)=60,\text{ and in general } \chi(n)=3n(n+1)\text{ for }n \geq 0.$[^o3]

:::

## Degree bounds {#Degree_bounds}

Unlike the Hilbert function, the Hilbert–Samuel function is not additive on an [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215). However, it is still reasonably close to being additive, as a consequence of the [Artin–Rees lemma](wikipedia://en/9553854). We denote by $P_{I, M}$ the Hilbert-Samuel polynomial; i.e., it coincides with the Hilbert–Samuel function for large integers.

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Theorem"}

**Theorem.**

Let $(R, m)$ be a Noetherian local ring and *I* an m-[primary ideal](wikipedia://en/3021435). If

$0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ is an exact sequence of finitely generated *R*-modules and if $M/I M$ has finite length,[^o8] then we have:[^o9]

$P_{I, M} = P_{I, M'} + P_{I, M''} - F$ where *F* is a polynomial of degree strictly less than that of $P_{I, M'}$ and having positive leading coefficient. In particular, if $M' \simeq M$, then the degree of $P_{I, M''}$ is strictly less than that of $P_{I, M} = P_{I, M'}$.

:::

Proof: Tensoring the given exact sequence with $R/I^n$ and computing the kernel we get the exact sequence:

$$0 \to (I^n M \cap M')/I^n M' \to M'/I^n M' \to M/I^n M \to M''/I^n M'' \to 0,$$

which gives us:

$$\chi_M^I(n-1) = \chi_{M'}^I(n-1) + \chi_{M''}^I(n-1) - \ell((I^n M \cap M')/I^n M').$$

The third term on the right can be estimated by Artin-Rees. Indeed, by the lemma, for large *n* and some *k*,

$$I^n M \cap M' = I^{n-k} ((I^k M) \cap M') \subset I^{n-k} M'.$$

Thus,

$$\ell((I^n M \cap M') / I^n M') \le \chi^I_{M'}(n-1) - \chi^I_{M'}(n-k-1).$$

This gives the desired degree bound.

## Multiplicity {#Multiplicity}

If $A$ is a local ring of [Krull dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) $d$, with $m$-primary ideal $I$, its Hilbert polynomial has leading term of the form $\frac{e}{d!}\cdot n^d$ for some [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) $e$. This integer $e$ is called the **multiplicity** of the ideal $I$. When $I=m$ is the [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164) of $A$, one also says $e$ is the multiplicity of the local ring $A$.

The multiplicity of a point $x$ of a scheme $X$ is defined to be the multiplicity of the corresponding local ring $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$.
