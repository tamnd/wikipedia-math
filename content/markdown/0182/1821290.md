# Stanisław Ruziewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Ruziewicz"}

**Stanisław Ruziewicz** (29 August 1889 – 12 July 1941) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and one of the founders of the [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328).

:::

He was a former student of [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122), earning his doctorate in 1913 from the [University of Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_University); his thesis concerned [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) that are not [differentiable](wikipedia://en/330206). He became a professor at the same university (then named Jan Kazimierz University) and [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [Academy of Foreign Trade in Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Foreign_Trade_in_Lwów).[^o4] During the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War), Ruziewicz's home city of [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów) was annexed by the [Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic), but then taken over by the [General Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Government) of [German-occupied Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland_%281939–1945%29) in July 1941; Ruziewicz was arrested and murdered by the [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo) on 12 July 1941 in Lwów, during the [Massacre of Lwów professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massacre_of_Lviv_professors).[^o5]

The [Ruziewicz problem](wikipedia://en/3100245), asking whether the [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) on the sphere may be characterized by certain of its properties, is named after him.[^o6]
