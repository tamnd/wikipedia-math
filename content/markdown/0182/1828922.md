# Prismatic surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prismatic surface"}

*Image: Hexagonal\_Prism\_BC.svg.*

In [solid geometry](wikipedia://en/507960), a **prismatic surface** is a [polyhedral surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_surface) generated by all the [lines](wikipedia://en/946975) that are [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to a given line and that intersect a [polygonal chain](wikipedia://en/12056032) in a plane that is not parallel to the given line.[^o2] The polygonal chain is the *directrix* of the surface; the parallel lines are its *generators* (or *elements*). If the directrix is a [convex polygon](wikipedia://en/660019), then the surface is a *closed prismatic surface*. The part of a closed prismatic surface between two parallel copies of the directrix is a [prism](wikipedia://en/283007).[^o3]

:::
