# Giovanni Paolo Galdi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Paolo Galdi"}

**Giovanni Paolo Galdi** (born January 3, 1947) is an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who works primarily on the mathematical analysis of the [Navier-Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395); in particular, on the topics of fluid-structure interactions and hydrodynamic stability. He is a Distinguished Professor of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science, the Leighton E. and Mary N. Orr Professor of Engineering, and Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), as well as adjunct professor at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai). He serves on the editorial board of the journal *Nonlinear Analysis* and is co-founder and editor-in-chief of the *[Journal of Mathematical Fluid Mechanics](wikipedia://en/84060965)* as well as the book series *Advances in Mathematical Fluid Mechanics* and *Lecture Notes in Mathematical Fluid Mechanics*.[^o7][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Galdi earned his doctorate in Physics from the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II) in 1971.[^o7] He would later serve as professor there, in the Department of Mathematics, after completing his post-doctoral mathematical training in 1976, under the mentorship of [Salvatore Rionero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvatore_Rionero) and [James Serrin](wikipedia://en/22296309).[^o8][^o9] In 1985, he joined the [University of Ferrara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ferrara), where he founded the School of Engineering, serving as engineering Dean from 1989 to 1995.[^o4] Since 1999, he has been a professor at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), first in the Department of Mathematics, and then later in the [Swanson School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swanson_School_of_Engineering), where he currently holds his tenure.[^o7]

## Mathematical contributions {#Mathematical_contributions}

Galdi works in mathematical [hydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), having written on the [Navier-Stokes Equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_Equations) and [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310).[^o7] Among his purely mathematical contributions is a generalized theory of homogeneous [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964) for [unbounded domains](wikipedia://en/16796643), which has become a cornerstone in the study of [external flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/External_flow).[^o11] He has also done foundational work in the mathematical theory of [fluid-structure interactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid-structure_interaction),[^o12] a field of research in which he is still currently active. His book, *An Introduction to the Mathematical Theory of the Navier-Stokes Equations: Steady-State Problems* (1994)[^o11] and article *On the Motion of a Rigid Body in a Viscous Liquid: A Mathematical Analysis with Applications* (2002)[^o12] are widely used.

In 2010, a dedicated [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) volume was published in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o4]
