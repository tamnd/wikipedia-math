# Kuen surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kuen surface"}

The **Kuen surface** is a [mathematical surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_surface) of constant negative unit [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623), making it an example of a [pseudospherical surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudospherical_surface).[^o2][^o3] It can be described as a [parametric surface](wikipedia://en/3400953)[^o3] in terms of the [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108)

:::

$$x = 2 \cosh v \, (\cos u + u \sin u) / w$$

$$y = 2 \cosh v \, ( \sin u - u \cos u ) / w$$

$$z = v - (2 \sinh v \cosh v ) / w$$

where

$$w = (\cosh v)^2 + u^2$$

It is named after, and was first described by, the German mathematician [Theodor Kuen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Kuen) in 1884.[^o4][^o5] The surface is a special case of the class of [Enneper surfaces](wikipedia://en/4688830), first described by [Alfred Enneper](wikipedia://en/2996246).

The Kuen surface was of interest to surrealist artists, including [Max Ernst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Ernst) and [Man Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_Ray).[^o6] The surface has also inspired work by the Japanese sculptor [Toshimasa Kikuchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toshimasa_Kikuchi).[^o7]
