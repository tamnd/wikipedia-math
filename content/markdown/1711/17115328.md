# Menachem Magidor

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Menachem Magidor"}

**Menachem Magidor** (מנחם מגידור; born January 24, 1946) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), in particular [set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatic_Set_Theory). He served as president of the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), was president of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438) from 1996 to 1998 and as president of the [Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science and Technology of the International Union for History and Philosophy of Science](wikipedia://en/33072433#DLMPST) (DLMPST/IUHPS) from 2016 to 2019. In 2016 he was elected an honorary foreign member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. In 2018 he received the [Solomon Bublick Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Bublick_Award).

:::

## Early and personal life {#Early_and_personal_life}

Menachem Magidor was born in [Petah Tikva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petah_Tikva), Israel. He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1973 from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). His thesis, *On Super Compact Cardinals*, was written under the supervision of [Azriel Lévy](wikipedia://en/18329294). The Oxford philosopher [Ofra Magidor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofra_Magidor) is his daughter.

## Career {#Career}

Magidor obtained several important consistency results on powers of [singular cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cardinal), substantially developing the method of [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205). He [generalized](wikipedia://en/15550368#Magidor_forcing) the [Prikry forcing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prikry_forcing) in order to change the [cofinality](wikipedia://en/6693) of a [large cardinal](wikipedia://en/383644) to a predetermined [regular cardinal](wikipedia://en/374128). He proved that the least [strongly compact cardinal](wikipedia://en/1957057) can be equal to the least [measurable cardinal](wikipedia://en/248102) or the least [supercompact cardinal](wikipedia://en/248116), but not to both.

Assuming the consistency of [huge cardinals](wikipedia://en/248117), he constructed models (1977) of set theory with the first examples of nonregular [ultrafilters](wikipedia://en/31911) over very small cardinals (related to the famous Guilmann–[Keisler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Jerome_Keisler) problem concerning existence of nonregular ultrafilters), even with the example of jumping cardinality of [ultrapowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrapower). He proved that it is consistent with $2^{\aleph_\omega}=\aleph_{\omega+2}$ for $\aleph_\omega$ to be strong limit, and strengthened the condition that $\aleph_\omega$ is strong limit to the condition that the [generalised continuum hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalised_continuum_hypothesis) holds below $\aleph_\omega$. This constituted a negative solution to the [singular cardinals hypothesis](wikipedia://en/4623947). Both proofs used the consistency of very large cardinals.

Magidor, [Matthew Foreman](wikipedia://en/3332703), and [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211) formulated and proved the consistency of [Martin's maximum](wikipedia://en/15978374), a provably maximal form of [Martin's axiom](wikipedia://en/1550783). Magidor also gave a simple proof of the Jensen and the Dodd–Jensen [covering lemmas](wikipedia://en/326356). He proved that if [0<sup>\#</sup>](wikipedia://en/248085) does not exist, then every [primitive recursive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_recursive) closed set of ordinals is the union of countably many sets in $L$.

He served as president of the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) from 1997 to 2009, following [Hanoch Gutfreund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanoch_Gutfreund) and succeeded by [Menachem Ben-Sasson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menachem_Ben-Sasson).[^o7]
