# Monthly Labor Review

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Monthly Labor Review"}

The ***Monthly Labor Review*** (*MLR*) is published by the U.S. [Bureau of Labor Statistics](wikipedia://en/72528) (BLS). Issues often focus on a particular topic. Most articles are by BLS staff.

:::

Annually since 1969, the Lawrence R. Klein Award has been awarded to authors of articles appearing in the *Monthly Labor Review*, generally one to BLS authors and one to non-BLS authors.[^o3]

## History {#History}

In 1915, under commissioner [Royal Meeker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Meeker), BLS began publishing the *Monthly Review*, with a circulation of 8,000. The name became *Monthly Labor Review* in 1918, and circulation rose to 20,000 in June 1920.[^o5] The journal has published its articles on the web for a decade. In 2008 the journal ceased to publish a bound paper edition, and now publishes only online.
