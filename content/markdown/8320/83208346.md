# Gaetano Marzagaglia

*Image: Marzagaglia, Gaetano – Del calcolo balistico, 1748 – BEIC 11380801.jpg.* *Del calcolo balistico* (1748)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaetano Marzagaglia"}

**Gaetano Marzagaglia**, or **Marzagalia** or **Marcegaglia** ([Chiampo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiampo), 7 August 1716 – [Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verona), 1 July 1787), was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [Catholic priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_priest).[^o3]

:::

Marzagaglia was an advocate of the applications of mathematics and its importance in society. He directed the episcopal seminary of Verona from 1760 to 1772.[^o2]

He refuted the theses of [Giuseppe Suzzi](wikipedia://en/54245052) in *Lettera di un matematico italiano* ("Letter of an Italian Mathematician"). In *Dimostrazioni algebriche* ("Algebraic Demonstrations"), he replied to a pamphlet by [Francesco Ventretti](wikipedia://en/54299522).[^o2]
