# Ana Justel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ana Justel"}

**Ana María Justel Eusebio** is a Spanish statistician and Antarctic scientist specializing in [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), including work on [multivariate](wikipedia://en/19384) versions of the [Kolmogorov–Smirnov test](wikipedia://en/16714) and on [mixture models](wikipedia://en/871681), and applications to the [limnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limnology) and [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904) of [Antarctica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctica). She is a professor of statistics at the [Autonomous University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Madrid).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Justel earned a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in mathematics from the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid) in 1990, and earned a doctorate in statistics and econometrics from [Charles III University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_III_University_of_Madrid) in 1995, with the dissertation *Algoritmos adaptativos de Gibbs Sampling para la identificación de heterogeneidad en regresión y series temporales*.

After postdoctoral research at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain), she joined the staff at the Autonomous University of Madrid in 1996; her position there was made permanent in 2000.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Justel won the inaugural Margarita Salas Prize of the Talent Woman initiative, in 2019.
