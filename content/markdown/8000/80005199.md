# Tore Dalenius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tore Dalenius"}

**Tore Dalenius** (1917-2002) was an employee of [Statistics Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Sweden). Much of his early work was published in the Swedish language journal *[Statistisk Tidskrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistisk_Tidskrift)*, the predecessor to today’s [Journal of Official Statistics](wikipedia://en/19899707). In 1977, he proposed a [formal methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_method) for [statistical disclosure control](wikipedia://en/49680984),[^o2] which later influenced the development of the concepts such as [k-anonymity](wikipedia://en/41698769) and [Differential Privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Privacy).

:::

Dalenius earned his PhD from [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in 1957.[^o3] His advisor was [Herman Wold](wikipedia://en/454834).
