# Domenico Chelucci

*Image: Chelucci - Institutiones arithmeticae cum appendice de natura, atque usu logarithmorum, 1743 - 1287354.jpg.* *Institutiones arithmeticæ* (1743)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Domenico Chelucci"}

**Domenico Chelucci** (in [religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_name) **Paolino di S. Giuseppe**; [Lucca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucca), 25 April 1681 – [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome), 17 January 1754) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priesthood_in_the_Catholic_Church) of the Order of the [Piarists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarists), professor and later rector of the Collegio Nazareno ("Nazarene College") in Rome.[^o3]

:::
