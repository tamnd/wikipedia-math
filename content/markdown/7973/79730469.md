# Valerio Lucarini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valerio Lucarini"}

**Valerio Lucarini** (born 11 August 1976 in Ancona, Italy[^o3]) is an Italian and British mathematician, physicist, and climate scientist. He has given key contributions in the area of mathematics and physics of [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate).[^o4] In particular, he has shown how the use of [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) can advance the understanding of the link between forced and free variability of the climate system across multiple scales of motion, define a robust framework for understanding climatic [tipping points](wikipedia://en/16817594), and improve our ability to interpret and model [extreme events](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_event_attribution).[^o5][^o6]

:::

He is currently a Professor of Applied Mathematics at the School of Computing and Mathematical Sciences of the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester), where he coordinates the research group in computational modelling for sciences and engineering.[^o7]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 2025 [Fellow of the American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Physical_Society)[^o9]
- 2024 Keilis-Borok Medal, International Union of Geodesy and Geophysics[^o6]
- 2022 Member, [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o3]
- 2022 Mathematics of Planet Earth Career Prize, Society of Industrial and Applied Mathematics[^o10]
- 2021 Lorenz Lecture, [American Geophysical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Geophysical_Union)[^o11]
- 2020 [L.F. Richardson Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Fry_Richardson#Legacy), European Geophysical Union[^o5]
- 2018 [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913), [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)[^o12][^o13]
- 2010 Outstanding Young Scientist (now Arne Richter) Award, European Geophysical Union[^o14]
