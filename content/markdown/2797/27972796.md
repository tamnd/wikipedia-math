# Paul Erdős Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Erdős Prize"}

The **Paul Erdős Prize** (formerly Mathematical Prize) is given to Hungarian mathematicians not older than 40 by the Mathematics Department of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). It was established and originally funded by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).

:::
