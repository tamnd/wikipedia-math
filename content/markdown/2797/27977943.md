# Icosian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Icosian"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **icosians** are a specific set of Hamiltonian [quaternions](wikipedia://en/51440) with the same symmetry as the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447). The term can be used to refer to two related, but distinct, concepts:

:::

- The **icosian [group](wikipedia://en/19447)**: a [multiplicative group](wikipedia://en/1110742) of 120 quaternions, positioned at the vertices of a 600-cell of unit radius. This group is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) to the [binary icosahedral group](wikipedia://en/3195145) of [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_group) 120.
- The **icosian [ring](wikipedia://en/48404)**: all finite sums of the 120 unit icosians.

## Unit icosians {#Unit_icosians}

The icosian group, consisting of the 120 unit icosians, comprises the distinct [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of

- ½(±2, 0, 0, 0) (resulting in 8 icosians),
- ½(±1, ±1, ±1, ±1) (resulting in 16 icosians),
- ½(0, ±1, ±1*/φ*, ±*φ*) (resulting in 96 icosians).

In this case, the vector (*a*, *b*, *c*, *d*) refers to the quaternion *a* + *b***i** + *c***j** + *d***k**, and φ represents the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) ($\sqrt{5}$ + 1)/2. These 120 vectors form the vertices of a 600-cell, whose symmetry group is the Coxeter group [H4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H4_%28mathematics%29) of order 14400. In addition, the 600 icosians of norm 2 form the vertices of a [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358). Other [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) of icosians correspond to the [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112), [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) and [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).

## Icosian ring {#Icosian_ring}

The icosians are a subset of quaternions of the form, (*a* + *b*$\sqrt{5}$) + (*c* + *d*$\sqrt{5}$)**i** + (*e* + *f*$\sqrt{5}$)**j** + (*g* + *h*$\sqrt{5}$)**k**, where the eight variables are [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024).. This quaternion is only an icosian if the vector (*a*, *b*, *c*, *d*, *e*, *f*, *g*, *h*) is a point on a lattice *L*, which is isomorphic to an [E8 lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359).

More precisely, the quaternion norm of the above element is (*a* + *b*$\sqrt{5}$)<sup>2</sup> + (*c* + *d*$\sqrt{5}$)<sup>2</sup> + (*e* + *f*$\sqrt{5}$)<sup>2</sup> + (*g* + *h*$\sqrt{5}$)<sup>2</sup>. Its Euclidean norm is defined as *u* + *v* if the quaternion norm is *u* + *v*$\sqrt{5}$. This Euclidean norm defines a [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) on *L*, under which the lattice is isomorphic to the [E8 lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359).

This construction shows that the [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) $H_4$ embeds as a subgroup of $E_8$. Indeed, a linear isomorphism that preserves the quaternion norm also preserves the Euclidean norm.
