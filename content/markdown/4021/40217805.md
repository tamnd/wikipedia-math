# Giovanni Bordiga

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Bordiga"}

*Image: Pinacoteca Querini Stampalia - Ritratto di Giovanni Bordiga 1899 - Lino Selvatico.jpg.* Giovanni Bordiga by [Lino Selvatico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lino_Selvatico)

**Giovanni Bordiga** (2 April 1854 in [Novara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novara) – 16 June 1933 in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice)) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [algebraic](wikipedia://en/1997) and [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) at the [university of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua). He introduced the [Bordiga surface](wikipedia://en/23333699).

:::

Giovanni was the son of Carlo and Amalia Adami. He matriculated at a young age to [Turin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin_University), graduating in 1874 in [civil engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineering).

From 22 December 1929 until his death, he was president of the [Ateneo Veneto of Science, Letters and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_Veneto).

He was the paternal uncle of Italian [Left Communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_communism) theorist [Amadeo Bordiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amadeo_Bordiga).[^o2]

## Biography {#Biography}

He was a professor of projective geometry at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua). His fame is mainly linked to his role as president of the [Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_di_Belle_Arti_di_Venezia). During his tenure, in December 1926, he founded the [Università Iuav di Venezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_Iuav_di_Venezia),[^o4][^o5] the second high school of architecture to be established in [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).

An [Art criticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_criticism) and promoter of activities aimed at disseminating Venetian art, from 1920 to 1926 he was the first president of the [Venice Biennale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Biennale) not to also hold the office of mayor of the city,[^o6] while, as president of the [Fondazione Querini Stampalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondazione_Querini_Stampalia), in 1925 he oversaw the reorganization and refurbishment of several rooms in the museum ,[^o7] with particular attention to the art gallery, for which he edited the catalog.

Politically, he was a militant supporter of late Risorgimento radicalism, while his nephew [Amadeo Bordiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amadeo_Bordiga) was one of the founders of the [Italian Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Communist_Party).

The city of [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua) has named a street after him.
