# Hugh Segar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hugh Segar"}

*Image: Segar-hugh-william-ap-1.jpg.* Segar (year unknown)

**Hugh William Segar** (31 January 1868 – 18 September 1954) was a New Zealand mathematician and university professor. He was born in [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool), [Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancashire), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) in 1868. He was educated at [Liverpool College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_College) and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he was 2nd Wrangler and a Yeats Prizeman.[^o3] He was Professor of Mathematics at [University College, Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Auckland).[^o4]
