# Federico Ardila

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Federico Ardila"}

**Federico Ardila** (born 1977) is a [Colombian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is also active as a [DJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ). His research is in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), with a focus on [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory). Ardila is currently a professor at [Queen Mary University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_University_of_London), and has also held roles at [San Francisco State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_State_University) and the [University of the Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Andes_%28Colombia%29).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ardila was born in [Bogotá, Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogotá).[^o6] While at [Colegio San Carlos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colegio_San_Carlos), Ardila represented [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia) twice in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), winning bronze in 1993 and silver in 1994.[^o7]

Ardila earned a degree in mathematics from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1998 and completed his PhD in 2003 under [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397).[^o8]

Beyond mathematics, Ardila is a co-founder of the DJ collective, La Pelanga.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Ardila has written about increasing representation in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and proposed principles for inclusive learning environments, which he calls 'The Axioms'.[^o3][^o10]

He established the SFSU-Colombia Combinatorics Initiative, a collaborative program between [San Francisco State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_State_University) and the [Universidad de los Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Andes_%28Colombia%29), and "Todos Cuentan," a community-based project aimed at supporting mathematicians from underrepresented backgrounds.[^o11] He has published over 200 hours of free lectures and mathematical resources online.[^o12][^o13][^o14] He has also made several appearances in the mathematical [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) video series *[Numberphile](wikipedia://en/61716514)*.[^o15]
