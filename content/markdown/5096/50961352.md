# Nicolas Fizes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolas Fizes"}

**Nicolas Fizes** (27 October 1648 in [Frontignan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontignan) – 1718) was a French professor of mathematics and hydrography, who lived during the reign of [Louis XIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIV). He is especially known as the [librettist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libretto) who wrote [*L'Opéra de Frontignan*](http://occitanica.eu/omeka/items/show/223) (1678), a play in [Occitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occitan_language), dealing with a slight love intrigue, and an idyllic poem on the fountain of Frontignan.

*Image: Fizes Nicolas signature 1688.jpg.* Signature in 1688 of Nicolas Fizes

:::

## Career {#Career}

Nicolas Fizes's parents were carpenters in the French Navy. He studied with the [Jesuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus), and became engineer to armies and a doctor of law. In 1682 he held the first professorship of mathematics and hydrography in [Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montpellier).

From 1689, he headed a school of hydrography in Frontignan. In the hall of the Town Hall, he taught a few young sailors the concepts of mathematics and astrology. But this school was previously free, and Fizes asked for a salary of 150 pounds a year, which led to a conflict with the consuls of Frontignan. The school only survived 7 years, and closed its doors in 1696.
