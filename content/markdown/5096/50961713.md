# P-curvature

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="P-curvature"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **$p$-curvature** is an invariant of a [connection](wikipedia://en/308815) on a [coherent sheaf](wikipedia://en/603780) for [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754) of characteristic $p > 0$. It is a construction similar to a usual [curvature](wikipedia://en/130526), but only exists in finite characteristic.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose *X/S* is a [smooth morphism](wikipedia://en/7837041) of schemes of finite [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) $p > 0$, *E* a vector bundle on *X*, and $\nabla$ a connection on *E*. The **$p$-curvature** of $\nabla$ is a map $\psi: E \to E\otimes \Omega^1_{X/S}$ defined by

$$\psi(e)(D) = \nabla^p_D(e) - \nabla_{D^p}(e)$$

for any derivation *D* of $\mathcal{O}_X$ over *S*. Here we use that the *p*th power of a derivation is [still a derivation](wikipedia://en/17373539) over schemes of characteristic $p$. A useful property is that the expression is $\mathcal{O}_X$-linear in *e*, in contrast to the Leibniz rule for connections. Moreover, the expression is *p*-linear in *D*.

By the definition $p$-curvature measures the failure of the map $\operatorname{Der}_{X/S} \to \operatorname{End}(E)$ to be a homomorphism of [restricted Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/21329707), just like the usual [curvature](wikipedia://en/130526) in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) measures how far this map is from being a homomorphism of [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944).

:::
