# Barbara Niethammer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Niethammer"}

**Barbara Niethammer** (born 1967) is a German mathematician and [materials scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science) who works as a professor at the [Hausdorff Center for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o2] Her research concerns [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) for physical materials, and in particular the phenomenon of [Ostwald ripening](wikipedia://en/3508610) by which particles in liquids grow over time.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Niethammer completed her PhD in 1996 at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), under the supervision of [Hans Wilhelm Alt](wikipedia://en/56765498). Her dissertation was *Approximation of Coarsening Madels by Homogenization of a Stefan Problem*.

After postdoctoral research at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute), she returned to Bonn for her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2002, after which she became in 2003 a professor at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). She moved to the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 2007, where she was a fellow of [St Edmund Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Edmund_Hall,_Oxford). In 2012 she returned as a professor to the University of Bonn.[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Niethammer won the [Richard von Mises Prize](wikipedia://en/56348140) of the [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik) in 2003 for her work on Ostwald ripening,[^o6] and the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in 2011 "for her deep and rigorous contributions to material science, especially on the Lifshitz–Slyozov–Wagner and Becker–Doering equations".[^o7][^o8]

She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014.[^o9]
