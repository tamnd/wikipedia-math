::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Icositetrahedron"}

In geometry, an **icositetrahedron**[^o5] refers to a polyhedron with 24 faces, none of which are [regular polyhedra](wikipedia://en/333299). However, many are composed of regular polygons, such as the [triaugmented dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1177995) and the [disphenocingulum](wikipedia://en/1170258). Some icositetrahedra are near-[spherical](wikipedia://en/27859), but are not composed of regular polygons. A minimum of 14 vertices is required to form a icositetahedron.[^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

There are many symmetric forms, and the ones with highest symmetry have chiral [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844):

Four [Catalan solids](wikipedia://en/564393), convex:

- [Triakis octahedron](wikipedia://en/722549) - isosceles triangles
- [Tetrakis hexahedron](wikipedia://en/722554) - isosceles triangles
- [Deltoidal icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/723339) - kites
- [Pentagonal icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/725126) - pentagons

27 [uniform star-polyhedral duals](wikipedia://en/2850448): (self-intersecting)

- [Small rhombihexacron](wikipedia://en/24081874), [Great rhombihexacron](wikipedia://en/24083321)
- [Small hexacronic icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/24081904), [Great hexacronic icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/20826087)
- [Great deltoidal icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/20830203)
- [Great triakis octahedron](wikipedia://en/24083295)

Examples with lower symmetry include certain [dual polyhedra](wikipedia://en/8815) of [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034), such as the [gyroelongated square bicupola](wikipedia://en/1128650) and the [elongated square gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128551).

:::

## Common examples {#Common_examples}

Common examples include [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) and [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045), and include certain Johnson solids and [Catalan solids](wikipedia://en/564393).

### Icositrigonal pyramids {#Icositrigonal_pyramids}

**Icositrigonal pyramids** are a type of cone with an [icositrigon](wikipedia://en/54460134) as a [base](wikipedia://en/9579379), with 24 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 46 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 24 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).[^o11] Regular icositrigonal pyramids have a regular icositrigon as a base, and its [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) is {}∨{23}. The surface area $S$ and volume $V$ with side length $s$ and height $h$ can be calculated as follows:[^o11]

$$V=\frac{23 h s^2 \cot{\frac{\pi}{23}}}{12}\approx 13.9448 h s^2$$

$$S=\frac{23s\left(\sqrt{4h^2+s^2\cot^{2}{\frac{\pi}{23}}}+s\cot{\frac{\pi}{23}}\right)}{4}\approx 5.75s\left(\sqrt{4h^2+52.9335s^2}+7.27554s\right)$$

### Icosidigonal prism {#Icosidigonal_prism}

**Icosidigonal prisms** are a type of cylinder with an icosidigon as a base, with 24 faces, 66 edges, and 44 vertices.[^o13] Regular icosidigonal prisms have a regular icosidigon as a base, with each face a [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048). Every vertex borders 2 squares and an icosidigon base. Its [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) is $4{.}4{.}22$, its Schläfli symbol is {22}×{} or t{2,22}, its [Coxeter diagram](wikipedia://en/5358554) is , and its [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) is P22. The surface area $S$ and volume $V$ with side length $s$ and height $h$ can be calculated as follows:

$$V=\frac{11 h s^2 \cot{\frac{\pi}{22}}}{2}\approx 38.2533 h s^2$$

$$S=11s\left( 2h+s\cot{\frac{\pi}{22}}\right)\approx 11s\left(2h+6.95515s\right)$$

### Hendecagonal antiprism {#Hendecagonal_antiprism}

*Image: Hendecagonal\_antiprism.png.* A hendecagonal antiprism

**Hendecagonal antiprisms** are [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851) with a [hendecagon](wikipedia://en/614840) as a base, with 24 faces, 44 edges, and 22 vertices. Regular hendecagonal antiprisms have a regular hendecagon as a base, with each face an equilateral triangle. Every vertex borders 2 triangles and a hendecagon base. Its vertex configuration is $11{.}3{.}3{.}3$.

### Dodecagonal trapezohedron {#Dodecagonal_trapezohedron}

**Dodecagonal trapezohedra** are the tenth member of the [trapezohedra](wikipedia://en/719845) family, made of 24 [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330) kites arranged radially. Every dodecagonal trapezohedron has 24 faces, 28 edges, and 26 vertices. There are two types of vertices, ones bordering 12 kits and ones bordering 3. Its dual polyhedron is the Hendecagonal antiprism.[^o16] Its Schläfli symbol is { }⨁{12}, its Coxeter diagram is or , and its Conway polyhedron notation is dA12.

Dodecagonal trapezohedra are [isohedral figures](wikipedia://en/6286120).
