# Jinichi Nagumo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jinichi Nagumo"}

**Jinichi Nagumo** (南雲仁一, 1926 -- March 10, 1999) was a professor at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo), known for his contributions to [nonlinear dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamics), mathematical modeling of neural systems, [nonlinear oscillation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_oscillation), and engineering. The [FitzHugh–Nagumo model](wikipedia://en/9105867) for neuron dynamics is named after him and [Richard FitzHugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_FitzHugh).[^o2]

:::
