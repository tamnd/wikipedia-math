# Helge Tverberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Helge Tverberg"}

*Image: Helge Tverberg.jpg.* Helge Tverberg at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 1981

**Helge Arnulf Tverberg** (March 6, 1935 – December 28, 2020) was a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a professor in the Mathematics Department at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen), his speciality being [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170); he retired at the mandatory age of seventy.

:::

He was born in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen).[^o2] He took the [cand.real.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.real.) degree at the University of Bergen in 1958, and the [dr.philos.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr.philos.) degree in 1968. He was a lecturer from 1958 to 1971 and professor from 1971 to his retirement in 2005. He was a visiting scholar at the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading) in 1966 and at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), in Canberra, from 1980 to 1981, 1987 to 1988 and in 2004.[^o3] He was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o4]

Tverberg, in 1965, proved a result on intersection patterns of partitions of [point configurations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_configuration) that has come to be known as [Tverberg's partition theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tverberg's_partition_theorem). It inaugurated a new branch of [combinatorial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry), with many variations and applications. An account by [Günter M. Ziegler](wikipedia://en/14628647) of Tverberg's work in this direction appeared in the issue of the Notices of the American Mathematical Society for April, 2011.[^o5]
