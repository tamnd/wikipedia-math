# De Rham–Weil theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Rham–Weil theorem"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **De Rham–Weil theorem** allows computation of [sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357) using an [acyclic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acyclic_sheaf) [resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_resolution) of the sheaf in question.

:::

Let $\mathcal F$ be a [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) on a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$ and $\mathcal F^\bullet$ a resolution of $\mathcal F$ by acyclic sheaves. Then

$$H^q(X,\mathcal F) \cong H^q(\mathcal F^\bullet(X)),$$

where $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ denotes the $q$-th [sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357) group of $X$ with coefficients in $\mathcal F.$

The De Rham–Weil theorem follows from the more general fact that derived functors may be computed using acyclic resolutions instead of simply injective resolutions.
