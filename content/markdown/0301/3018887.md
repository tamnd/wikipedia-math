# Mautner's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mautner's lemma"}

**Mautner's lemma** in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), named after Austrian-American mathematician [Friederich Mautner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friederich_Mautner), states that if *G* is a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) and π a [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of *G* on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H*, then for any *x* in *G*, which has [conjugates](wikipedia://en/490861#C)

:::

*yxy*<sup>−1</sup>

converging to the [identity element](wikipedia://en/14962) *e*, for a [net](wikipedia://en/22170) of elements *y*, then any vector *v* of *H* invariant under all the π(*y*) is also invariant under π(*x*).
