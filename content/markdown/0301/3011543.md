# Anton Felkel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anton Felkel"}

Anton Felkel

*Image: Anton Felkel between books - engraving.jpg.* Engraving showing Anton Felkel

**Anton Felkel** (26 April 1740, [Kamenz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamieniec_Ząbkowicki), [Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchies_of_Silesia) – c. 1800, possibly in [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon), [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal)) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on the determination of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666).

:::

## Work {#Work}

In 1776 and 1777, Felkel published a table giving complete prime factorizations of all integers not divisible by 2, 3, and 5, from 1 to 408,000. Felkel had planned to extend his table to 10 million.[^o3]
