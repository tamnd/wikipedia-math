# LITS

*Image: LITS.png.* Moderately difficult *LITS* puzzle (solution)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="LITS"}

**LITS**, formerly known as **Nuruomino** (ヌルオミノ), is a binary determination puzzle published by [Nikoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikoli_%28publisher%29).[^o2]

:::

## Rules {#Rules}

*LITS* is played on a [rectangular grid](wikipedia://en/3771917), typically 10×10; the grid is divided into [polyominoes](wikipedia://en/72634), none of which have fewer than four cells. The goal is to shade in a [tetromino](wikipedia://en/30411) within each pre-printed polyomino in such a way that no two matching tetrominoes are orthogonally adjacent (with rotations and reflections counting as matching), and that the shaded cells form a valid [nurikabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurikabe_%28puzzle%29): they are all orthogonally contiguous (form a single polyomino) and contain no 2×2 square tetrominoes as subsets.

## History {#History}

The puzzle was first printed in *[Puzzle Communication Nikoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle_Communication_Nikoli) #106*; the original title is a combination of 'nuru' ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): "to paint") and 'omino' ([polyomino](wikipedia://en/72634)). In issue #112, the title was changed to the present one, which represents the four (of five) tetrominoes used in the puzzle: the L-shape, the straight, the T-shape, and the skew (square tetrominoes may never appear in the puzzle as they are a direct violation of the rule).

## Complexity {#Complexity}

Testing whether a given LITS puzzle has a solution is [NP-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP-complete).[^o6]
