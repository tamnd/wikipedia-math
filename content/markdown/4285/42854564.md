# Petersson algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Petersson algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Petersson algebra** is a [composition algebra](wikipedia://en/3861635) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) constructed from an order-3 [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) of a [Hurwitz algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurwitz_algebra). They were first constructed by (Petersson 1969).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

Suppose that *C* is a Hurwitz algebra and *φ* is an automorphism of order 3. Define the new product of *x* and *y* to be *φ*($\overline{x}$)*φ*<sup>2</sup>($\overline{y}$). With this new product the algebra is called a Petersson algebra.

:::
