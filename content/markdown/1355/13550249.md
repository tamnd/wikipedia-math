# Scripta Mathematica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Scripta Mathematica"}

***Scripta Mathematica*** was a quarterly journal published by [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University) devoted to the [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), history, and expository treatment of mathematics.[^o3] It was said to be, at its time, "the only mathematical magazine in the world edited by specialists for laymen."[^o4]

:::

The journal was established in 1932 under the editorship of [Jekuthiel Ginsburg](wikipedia://en/21074637), a professor of mathematics at Yeshiva University,[^o5] and its first issue appeared in 1933[^o4] at a subscription price of three dollars per year.[^o6] It ceased publication in 1973. Notable papers published in *Scripta Mathematica* included work by [Nobelist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize) [Percy Williams Bridgman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Williams_Bridgman) concerning the implications for [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) of [set-theoretic](wikipedia://en/27553) [paradoxes](wikipedia://en/24390),[^o7] and [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544)'s obituary of [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896).[^o8][^o9]

Some sources describe *Scripta Mathematica* as having been assigned 0036-9713[^o10] but it ceased publication prior to the establishment of the ISSN system.
