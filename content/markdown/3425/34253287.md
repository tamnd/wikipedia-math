# Isidor Natanson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isidor Natanson"}

**Isidor Pavlovich Natanson** (Исидор Павлович Натансон; February 8, 1906 in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich) – July 3, 1964 in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad)) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland)-born [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for contributions to [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478) and [constructive function theory](wikipedia://en/32135416), in particular, for his textbooks on these subjects.[^o2] His son, Garal'd Natanson (1930–2003), was also a known mathematician.[^o3]

:::
