# David Gruen (economist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Gruen"}

**David William Gruen** (born 31 August 1954) is an Australian statistician and mathematician. He is the current Australian Statistician at the [Australian Bureau of Statistics](wikipedia://en/4732815) serving since 11 December 2019. He previously served as Deputy Secretary, Economic and Australia's G20 Sherpa at the [Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_the_Prime_Minister_and_Cabinet_%28Australia%29). He previously held office as the executive director of the Macroeconomic group of [Australian Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasury_%28Australia%29) and was the head of the Economic Research Department of the [Reserve Bank of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserve_Bank_of_Australia) from 1998 to 2002.[^o5][^o6]

:::

Gruen was appointed an [Officer of the Order of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Australia) in the [2022 Australia Day Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australia_Day_Honours).[^o7] He was elected a Fellow of the [Academy of the Social Sciences in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_the_Social_Sciences_in_Australia) in 2023.[^o8]
