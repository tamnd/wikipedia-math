# Angelo Felice Capelli

*Image: Capelli - Breve compendio d'operazioni geometriche da farsi colla sola riga, e compasso, 1765 - 74088.jpg.* *Breve compendio d'operazioni geometriche*, 1765

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Angelo Felice Capelli"}

**Angelo Felice Capelli** ([Parma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parma), [Duchy of Parma and Piacenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Parma_and_Piacenza) 2 November 1681 – [Ceneda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceneda,_Italy), 16 November 1749) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).[^o3]

:::
