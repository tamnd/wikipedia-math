# Valentin Poénaru

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valentin Poénaru"}

**Valentin Alexandre Poénaru** (born 1932 in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest)) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania)–[French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a Professor of Mathematics at [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), specializing in [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Born in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest),[^o4] [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), he did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). In 1962, he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden). While at the congress, Poénaru [defected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defection), subsequently leaving for [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). He arrived in mid-September 1962 at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette); the IHÉS decided to support him, and he has remained associated with the institute ever since then.[^o5]

Poénaru defended his Thèse d'État at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) on March 23, 1963. His dissertation topic was *Sur les variétés tridimensionnelles ayant le type d'homotopie de la sphère S<sub>3</sub>*, and was written under the supervision of [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108). After that, he went to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), spending four years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). In 1967, he returned to France.

Poénaru has worked for several decades on a proof of the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798), making a number of related breakthroughs. His first attempt at proving the conjecture dates from 1957. He has described his general approach over the years in different papers and conferences. On December 19, 2006, he posted a preprint to the [arXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv), claiming to have finally completed the details of his approach and proven the conjecture.

His doctoral students include [Jean Lannes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Lannes_%28mathematician%29).

## Iconography {#Iconography}

His friend the Peruvian painter [Herman Braun-Vega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Braun-Vega) made of him a family portrait with his wife the painter [Rigmor Poenaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigmor_Poenaru), where figures and mathematical symbols in the form of graffiti evoke his research works.[^o9]
