# Susan Hilsenbeck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Hilsenbeck"}

**Susan Galloway Hilsenbeck** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) whose research interests include [biomarkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomarker) and the applications of biostatistics in [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer) research. She is a professor in the [Baylor College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baylor_College_of_Medicine), where she directs the Quantitative Sciences Share Resource in the Dan L. Duncan Comprehensive Cancer Center.

:::

Hilsenbeck earned her Ph.D. in 1990, at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami). Her dissertation, *Acquisition and use of information on data quality in large population-based tumor registries*, was supervised by Charles Kurucz. She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2011.

Her hobbies include [scuba diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuba_diving) and [quilting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quilting). Her quilts have been featured in the books *Amish Quilts, The Adventure Continues* (2013) and *Quarantine Quilts: Creativity in the Midst of Chaos* (2021).
