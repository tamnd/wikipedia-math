# Jennie Golding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jennie Golding"}

**Jenefer Golding** is a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics policy and practice at the [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) [Institute of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCL_Institute_of_Education).

:::

Golding graduated from [St Hugh's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh's_College,_Oxford) in 1971 with a degree in mathematics. After spending several years teaching schoolchildren, she moved to [UCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London), where she focuses on development of mathematics education policy.[^o2]

In 2016, she served as president of the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403), a [professional association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_association) which is involved in issues of [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) in the UK.[^o3]

In January 2024, she submitted written testimony to the [Education Select Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Select_Committee) of the [UK Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_the_United_Kingdom) which was published as part of the report called *Delivering effective financial education* in May of 2024.[^o4]
