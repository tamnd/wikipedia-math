# Barber–Johnson diagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barber–Johnson diagram"}

A **Barber–Johnson diagram** is a method of presenting hospital statistics combining four different variables in a unique [graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_graphics), introduced in 1973.[^o2] The method constructs a [scattergram](wikipedia://en/412544) where [length of stay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_of_stay), turnover interval, discharges, and deaths per available bed are combined. These four [variables](wikipedia://en/437701) have a common relationship between them and their combination in the diagram permitted a new improved way for analyzing [efficiency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_efficiency) and performance of the hospital sector. The most complete reference about how to construct the diagram could be found in Yates.[^o3] In this book, the appendix explains in detail the way for elaborating this kind of diagram.

:::
