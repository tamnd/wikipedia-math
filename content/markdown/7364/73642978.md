# Alena Šolcová

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alena Šolcová"}

**Alena Šolcová** (born 26 March 1950) is a Czech mathematician and [science historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science_and_technology). She is the founder of the Kepler Museum, an astronomy museum in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Between 1968 and 1973, Šolcová studied mathematics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics and Philosophy at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University). Between 2002 and 2005, she completed her doctoral studies in mathematics in Civil Engineering with the doctoral thesis titled *[Fermat's](wikipedia://en/7576966) Ideas Revived in Mathematics Applied in Engineering,* and in 2009 she completed her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [Czech Technical University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Technical_University_in_Prague), and was appointed associate professor in the field of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o4]

Šolcová works at the [Faculty of Information Technologies of the Czech Technical University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_of_Information_Technology,_Czech_Technical_University_in_Prague), where she teaches [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and computer science. She also deals with logic, [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), some numerical methods and the history of mathematics, computer science and astronomy. Since 1992, she has led the *SEDMA* seminar (Seminar for the History of Mathematics, Informatics and Astronomy) and the working group for the history of exact sciences *HEXA*. She was the initiator and founder of an astronomy museum, the [Kepler Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler_Museum) in Prague (after its establishment, the museum was managed by the Czech Astronomical Society, in operation from 2009 to 2017).[^o4]

She initiated the establishment of commemorative plaques to [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736) on Prague's Old Town Square and another to Austrian mathematician [Johann Radon](wikipedia://en/339331) in [Děčín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Děčín), Czech Republic.

Šolcová is an honorary member of *the [Union of Czech Mathematicians and Physicists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Czech_mathematicians_and_physicists)* and has served as its chairman beginning in 2018. She is also a member of the editorial board of the *Czechoslovak Journal for Physics*. She remains an active member of the Czech Mathematical Society and the Czech Society for Cybernetics and Informatics, which provides expertise concerning issues in logic, probability and reasoning.[^o5]

### Honors {#Honors}

- *The magic of numbers - From great discoveries to applications*, Academia Praha, 2009, 2011, 2018 with co-authors Michal Křížek and Lawrence Somer, was awarded the Hlávká prize for scientific literature in 2010 (First prize).
- In 1998, the [International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union) named planet No. [58682 Alenašolcová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_minor_planets:_58001–59000) in her honor.[^o4][^o7]
