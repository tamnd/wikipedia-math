# Jozef Teugels

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jozef Teugels"}

Academics

*Image: Jef Teugels 1987.jpg.* Image of Jef Teugels

**Jozef Lodewijk Maria Teugels**, or **Jozef L. Teugels**, **Jef Teugels** (born February 20, 1939) is a Belgian mathematical statistician and actuary. His main contributions are in [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732), [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and [reinsurance theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinsurance).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Teugels was born in [Londerzeel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Londerzeel), Belgium and studied at the [Catholic University of Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Louvain), where he received his licentiate in mathematics in 1963. He moved to the US to pursue further study and obtained his MSc in 1966 and PhD in 1967, both at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), where he was supervised by [Marcel F. Neuts](wikipedia://en/73888681).[^o4] He returned to Belgium in the same year and took up a faculty position at [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven), where he was promoted to full professorship in 1973. He remained in Louvain until his retirement in 2004. During this period, Teugels was the chair of the Department of Mathematics from 1970 to 1977 and from 1982 to 1989.[^o2][^o5]

Teugels was named a distinguished alumnus of Purdue University in 2004.[^o6] He was president of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) between 2009 and 2011.[^o7]
