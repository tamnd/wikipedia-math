# Tiling with rectangles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tiling with rectangles"}

A **tiling with rectangles** is a [tiling](wikipedia://en/321671) which uses [rectangles](wikipedia://en/70048) as its parts. The [domino tilings](wikipedia://en/10565476) are tilings with rectangles of $1 \times 2$ side ratio. The tilings with straight [polyominoes](wikipedia://en/72634) of shapes such as $1 \times 3$, $1 \times 4$ and tilings with polyominoes of shapes such as $2 \times 3$ fall also into this category.

:::

## Tilings with non-congruent rectangles {#Tilings_with_non-congruent_rectangles}

The smallest square that can be cut into (m × n) rectangles, such that all m and n are different integers, is the 11 × 11 square, and the tiling uses five rectangles.[^o5]

The smallest rectangle that can be cut into (m × n) rectangles, such that all m and n are different integers, is the 9 × 13 rectangle, and the tiling uses five rectangles.[^o5][^o6]
