# Jeff Kahn (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeff Kahn"}

*Image: Jeff Kahn.jpg.* Jeff Kahn at [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) in 2008

**Jeffry Ned Kahn** is a professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) notable for his work in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kahn received his Ph.D. from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1979 after completing his dissertation under his advisor [Dijen K. Ray-Chaudhuri](wikipedia://en/18064578).

## Research {#Research}

In 1980, he showed the importance of the [bundle theorem](wikipedia://en/41702992) for ovoidal [Möbius planes](wikipedia://en/38245519).[^o5] In 1993, together with [Gil Kalai](wikipedia://en/19709056), he disproved [Borsuk's conjecture](wikipedia://en/7765817).[^o6] In 1996, he was awarded the [Pólya Prize (SIAM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28SIAM%29).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He was an invited speaker at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.

In 2012, he was awarded the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) (jointly with Anders Johansson and [Van H. Vu](wikipedia://en/21419416)) for determining the threshold of edge density above which a [random graph](wikipedia://en/358477) can be covered by disjoint copies of a given smaller graph.[^o8][^o9] Also in 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
