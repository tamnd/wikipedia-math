# Casio fx-3900Pv

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Casio fx-3900Pv"}

The [Casio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casio) **fx-3900Pv** is a [programmable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_program) [scientific calculator](wikipedia://en/439711) with 300 steps. Introduced in 1992, its production has since stopped with the introduction of [fx-3650P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casio_fx-3650P) and [fx-3950P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casio_fx-3950P).

:::
