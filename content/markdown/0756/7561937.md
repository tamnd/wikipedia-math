# Étienne Ghys

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Étienne Ghys"}

*Image: Étienne Ghys.jpeg.* Étienne Ghys, 2007

**Étienne Ghys** (born 29 December 1954) is a French mathematician. His research focuses mainly on [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), though his mathematical interests are broad. He also expresses much interest in the historical development of mathematical ideas, especially the contributions of [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740).

:::

Alumnus of the [École normale supérieure de Saint-Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Saint-Cloud), Ghys obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1979 from the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille) with thesis *Sur les actions localement libres du groupe affine* ([English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language): On the locally free actions of the [affine](wikipedia://en/298828) [group](wikipedia://en/41890)) written under the [direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) of Gilbert Hector.

He is currently a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) [directeur de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directeur_de_recherche) at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Lyon) in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon). He is also editor-in-chief of the *[Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publications_Mathématiques_de_l'IHÉS)* and a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).

Ghys was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto) in 1990, and a plenary speaker at the ICM in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) in 2006.[^o3] In 2015, he was awarded the inaugural [Clay Award for Dissemination of Mathematical Knowledge](wikipedia://en/7655).[^o4]

He co-authored the [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics) mathematical movie *[Dimensions: A walk through mathematics!](wikipedia://en/898778)*. His doctoral students include [Serge Cantat](wikipedia://en/58936850).[^o2]
