# George D. Edwards

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George D. Edwards"}

**George DeForest Edwards** (1890 – 1974) was a 20th-century [quality control](wikipedia://en/41613) expert most notable for having served as the first president of [American Society for Quality Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_for_Quality).

:::

Edwards' reputation in quality control had been established by his work as head of the inspection engineering department of [Bell Telephone Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Telephone_Laboratories) and as Bell’s director of [quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_assurance), a term he coined. During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), he served as a consultant to the [US Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Army) [Ordnance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon) Department, and later to the [War Production Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_Production_Board).

Edwards retired from Bell in 1955 but he remained active in ASQ, serving as chair of the Committee on [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) and [Bylaws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bylaw), and later as deputy executive secretary for dues abatement.
