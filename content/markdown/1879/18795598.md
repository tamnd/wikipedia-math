# Ramaiyengar Sridharan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramaiyengar Sridharan"}

**Ramaiyengar Sridharan** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675), formerly at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

He was born in [Cuddalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuddalore) in 1935.[^o4] He obtained his Ph.D. from Columbia under the guidance of [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501) with his thesis on [filtered algebras](wikipedia://en/2954244) and representations of [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) in 1960.

## Awards {#Awards}

Sridharan was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) (SSB prize) in Mathematical Science in 1980.[^o4]
