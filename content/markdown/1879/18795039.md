# Thiruvenkatachari Parthasarathy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thiruvenkatachari Parthasarathy"}

**Thiruvenkatachari Parthasarathy** (29 February 1940 - 22 September 2023), known and published as **T. Parthasarathy**, was an Indian [game theorist](wikipedia://en/11924) and mathematician.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He co-authored a book on [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) with [T.&nbsp;E.&nbsp;S.&nbsp;Raghavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T.&nbsp;E.&nbsp;S.&nbsp;Raghavan),[^o4][^o5] and two research monographs, one on [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29)[^o6] and one on univalence theory,[^o7] published by [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag). He was a former president of the [Indian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematical_Society).

He received his BSc and MSc degrees from [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras). He worked on the topic of "Minimax Theorems and Product solutions for simple games" under the guidance of [C. R. Rao](wikipedia://en/1418009) and received his PhD in 1967 from the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Kolkata. He delivered many lectures and seminars at University of Madras, [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), and [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675). On the list of his scientific publication since 1965 there are more that 90 positions.[^o8][^o9] He promoted PhDs students.[^o10]

Parthasarathy received [Shanti Swaroop Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swaroop_Bhatnagar_Award) for Mathematical Sciences (1986). He was elected as Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) (1988) and [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (1995).

He died on September 22, 2023, at the age of 83 at his [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai) residence.
