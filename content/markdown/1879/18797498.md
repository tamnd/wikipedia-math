# Experimental Mathematics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Experimental Mathematics"}

***Experimental Mathematics*** is a quarterly [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by [A K Peters, Ltd.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_K_Peters,_Ltd.) until 2010, now by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis). The journal publishes papers in [experimental mathematics](wikipedia://en/256363), broadly construed. The journal's mission statement describes its scope as follows: "Experimental Mathematics publishes original papers featuring formal results inspired by experimentation, conjectures suggested by experiments, and data supporting significant hypotheses."[^o3] Its [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Alexander Kasprzyk ([University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham)).

:::

## History {#History}

*Experimental Mathematics* was established in 1992 by [David Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Epstein_%28mathematician%29), Silvio Levy, and Klaus Peters.[^o5] *Experimental Mathematics* was the first mathematical research journal to concentrate on [experimental mathematics](wikipedia://en/256363) and to explicitly acknowledge its importance for [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) as a general research field. The journal's launching was described as "something of a watershed".[^o6] Indeed, the launching of the journal in 1992 was surrounded by some controversy in the mathematical community about the value and validity of experimentation in mathematical research.[^o6][^o7] Some critics of the new journal suggested that it be renamed as the "Journal of Unproved Theorems".[^o8][^o9] In a 1995 article in the *Notices of the American Mathematical Society*, in part responding to such criticism, Epstein and Levy described the journal's aims as follows:[^o10] But the main difference reflects the philosophy above: we are interested not only in theorems and proofs but also in the way in which they have been or can be reached. Note that we do value proofs: experimentally inspired results that can be proved are more desirable than conjectural ones. However, we do publish significant conjectures or explorations in the hope of inspiring other, perhaps better-equipped researchers to carry on the investigation. The objective of Experimental Mathematics is to play a role in the discovery of formal proofs, not to displace them.

Despite the initial controversy, *Experimental Mathematics* quickly established a solid reputation and is now a highly respected mathematical publication. The journal is reviewed cover-to-cover in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)* and is indexed in the [Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science).
