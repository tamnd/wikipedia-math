# Brailey Sims

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brailey Sims"}

**Brailey Sims** (born 26 October 1947) is an Australian mathematician born and educated in [Newcastle, New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle,_New_South_Wales). He received his BSc from the [University of Newcastle (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle_%28Australia%29) in 1969 and, under the supervision of [J. R. Giles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._R._Giles), a PhD from the same university in 1972. He was on the faculty of the [University of New England (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29) from 1972 to 1989. In 1990 he took up an appointment at the [University of Newcastle (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Newcastle_%28Australia%29). where he was Head of Mathematics from 1997 to 2000.

:::

He is best known for his work in [nonlinear analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_analysis) and especially metric fixed point theory and its connections with Banach and metric space geometry, and for his efforts to promote and enhance mathematics at the secondary and tertiary level.
