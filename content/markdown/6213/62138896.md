# Halperin conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Halperin conjecture"}

In [rational homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/13998981), the **Halperin conjecture** concerns the [Serre spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/3085825) of certain fibrations. It is named after the [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian) mathematician [Stephen Halperin](wikipedia://en/58919732).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that $F \to E \to B$ is a fibration of [simply connected spaces](wikipedia://en/523879) such that $F$ is [rationally elliptic](wikipedia://en/13998981#Elliptic_and_hyperbolic_spaces) and $\chi(F) \neq 0$ (i.e., $F$ has non-zero [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420)), then the [Serre spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/3085825) associated to the fibration collapses at the $E_2$ page.[^o4]

:::

## Status {#Status}

As of 2019, Halperin's conjecture is still open. Gregory Lupton has reformulated the conjecture in terms of formality relations.[^o6]
