# L-statistic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="L-statistic"}

Mathematics In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), an **L-statistic** is a [statistic](wikipedia://en/26703) (function of a data set) that is a [linear combination](wikipedia://en/55632) of [order statistics](wikipedia://en/160986); the "L" is for "linear". These are more often referred to by narrower terms according to use, namely:

:::

- [L-estimator](wikipedia://en/17398955), using L-statistics as estimators for parameters
- [L-moment](wikipedia://en/24350828), L-statistic analogs of the conventional moments
