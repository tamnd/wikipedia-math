# OpenIntro Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="OpenIntro Statistics"}

***OpenIntro Statistics*** is an open-source textbook for introductory [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), written by David Diez, Christopher Barr, and [Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel](wikipedia://en/55671730).[^o3]

:::

The textbook is available online as a free PDF, as [LaTeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaTeX) source and as a royalty-free paperback.[^o4]
