# Aparna V. Huzurbazar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aparna V. Huzurbazar"}

**Aparna V. Huzurbazar** is an American statistician known for her work using [graphical models](wikipedia://en/447298) to understand [time-to-event data](wikipedia://en/419259). She is the author of a book on this subject, *Flowgraph Models for Multistate Time-to-Event Data* (Wiley, 2004).

:::

Huzurbazar is a research scientist at the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory). She graduated in 1988 with two bachelor's degrees from two different universities: one in mathematics from [Claremont McKenna College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont_McKenna_College), and another in aerospace engineering from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder). She completed a Ph.D. in statistics in 1994 at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University). Her dissertation, supervised by Ronald W. Butler, was *Prediction in Stochastic Networks*. She took a faculty position at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida), but then moved to the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico) in 1996, and moved again to Los Alamos in 2007.

Huzurbazar is the daughter of noted Indian statistician [V. S. Huzurbazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._S._Huzurbazar) and the sister of noted statistician [Snehalata V. Huzurbazar](wikipedia://en/55918193); her husband, Brian J. Williams of Los Alamos, is also a statistician. All four are [Fellows of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association); Aparna was elected as a Fellow in 2008, her father in 1983, Williams in 2015, and her sister in 2017. Huzurbazar was also elected as a member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2006.
