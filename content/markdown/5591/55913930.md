# Charles Niven

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Niven"}

**Charles Niven** (1845–1923) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and physicist who spent most of his career at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen). He published on mechanics, electricity, and heat.[^o3]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Charles Niven studied mathematics at Aberdeen and was awarded a BA in 1863, and then studied at [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). Charles and his older brother [William D.](wikipedia://en/40756335) were tutored by [Edward Routh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Routh) for the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos). Charles became [senior wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_wrangler) in 1867.[^n1]

In 1867, Niven was appointed Professor of Mathematics at [Queen's College Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College_Cork), in Ireland[^n2] a position that [George Boole](wikipedia://en/38298) had previously occupied.

From 1880, Niven was professor of Natural Philosophy in the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen), and he was responsible for establishing the Physics Department in [Marischal College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marischal_College) in 1906.[^o7] He retired at the end of 1922.[^n3]

Charles Niven was a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) from 1880 and honorary member of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) from 1883.[^o9]
