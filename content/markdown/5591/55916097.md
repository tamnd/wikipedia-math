# Robert E. Johnson (scientist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert E. Johnson"}

**Robert E. Johnson** is an American mathematician, engineer and physicist, currently the John Lloyd Newcomb Professor Engineering Physics and Materials Science at [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), with interests on biomolecular, atmospheric and condensed-gas solids.[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He earned his B.S. at [Colorado College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_College) in 1961, his M.S. at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University) in 1963 and his Ph.D. at [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) and was also awarded an honorary degree at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University).[^o4][^o5]
