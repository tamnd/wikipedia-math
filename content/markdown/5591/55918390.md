# Polynomial functor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polynomial functor"}

In algebra, a **polynomial functor** is an [endofunctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endofunctor) on the [category $\mathcal{V}$ of finite-dimensional vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_of_vector_spaces) that depends polynomially on vector spaces. For example, the [symmetric powers](wikipedia://en/57047243) $V \mapsto \operatorname{Sym}^n(V)$ and the [exterior powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_power) $V \mapsto \wedge^n(V)$ are polynomial functors from $\mathcal{V}$ to $\mathcal{V}$; these two are also [Schur functors](wikipedia://en/11597641).

:::

The notion appears in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) as well as [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) (the [calculus of functors](wikipedia://en/23694557)). In particular, the category of homogeneous polynomial functors of degree *n* is equivalent to the [category of finite-dimensional representations](wikipedia://en/55872661) of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) $S_n$ over a field of characteristic zero.[^o2]

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *k* be a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) zero and $\mathcal{V}$ the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of finite-dimensional *k*-[vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) and *k*-[linear maps](wikipedia://en/18102). Then an [endofunctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endofunctor) $F\colon \mathcal{V} \to \mathcal{V}$ is a *polynomial functor* if the following equivalent conditions hold:

- For every pair of vector spaces *X*, *Y* in $\mathcal{V}$, the map $F\colon \operatorname{Hom}(X, Y) \to \operatorname{Hom}(F(X), F(Y))$ is a [polynomial mapping](wikipedia://en/56059449) (i.e., a vector-valued polynomial in linear forms).
- Given linear maps $f_i: X \to Y, \, 1 \le i \le r$ in $\mathcal{V}$, the function $(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_r) \mapsto F(\lambda_1 f_1 + \cdots + \lambda_r f_r)$ defined on $k^r$ is a polynomial function with [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) in $\operatorname{Hom}(F(X), F(Y))$.

A polynomial functor is said to be *[homogeneous](wikipedia://en/622844) of degree*n if for any linear maps $f_1, \dots, f_r$ in $\mathcal{V}$ with common domain and codomain, the vector-valued polynomial $F(\lambda_1 f_1 + \cdots + \lambda_r f_r)$ is homogeneous of degree *n*.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Variants"}

## Variants {#Variants}

If “finite vector spaces” is replaced by “finite sets”, one gets the notion of [combinatorial species](wikipedia://en/160076) (to be precise, those of polynomial nature).

:::
