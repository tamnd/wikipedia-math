# Snehalata V. Huzurbazar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snehalata V. Huzurbazar"}

**Snehalata V. Huzurbazar** is an American statistician, known for her work in [statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736), and also interested in applications of statistics to [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology). She is a professor of biostatistics, and chair of the biostatistics department, at the [West Virginia University School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_University_School_of_Public_Health).

:::

Huzurbazar was born in [Ames, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ames,_Iowa). In 1984, she graduated from [Grinnell College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grinnell_College) with an independently designed major that combined [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History), [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology), and [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language), with a year off in [Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagreb) learning [Croatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_language). She earned a master's degree in economics from [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) in 1988, with V. Kerry Smith as her advisor, and completed her Ph.D. in statistics in 1992 at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University). Her dissertation, supervised by Ronald W. Butler, was *Saddlepoint Approximations in Multivariate Analysis*. She joined the department of statistics of the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) in 1992, moved to the [University of Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wyoming) in 1995, was on leave there from 2012 to 2014 while working as deputy director of the [Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/41998171) in [Research Triangle Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Triangle_Park), [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina), and moved to West Virginia University as chair in 2017.

Huzurbazar is the daughter of noted Indian statistician [V. S. Huzurbazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._S._Huzurbazar) and the sister of noted statistician [Aparna V. Huzurbazar](wikipedia://en/55918192), whose husband, Brian J. Williams, is also a statistician. All four are [Fellows of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association); Snehalata was elected as a Fellow in 2017, her father in 1983, her sister in 2008, and Williams in 2015.
