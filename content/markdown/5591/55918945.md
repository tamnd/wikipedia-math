# Charmaine Dean

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charmaine Dean"}

**Charmaine B. Dean** (born 1958) is a statistician from [Trinidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad). She is the vice president for research at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), a professor of statistical and actuarial sciences at both Waterloo and [Western University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario), the former president of the Western North American Region of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society), and the former [President of the Statistical Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Statistical_Society_of_Canada). Her research interests include [longitudinal studies](wikipedia://en/1557623), [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259), [spatiotemporal data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatiotemporal_database), [heart surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_surgery), and [wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfire).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dean was born in [San Fernando, Trinidad and Tobago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando,_Trinidad_and_Tobago) in 1958 and moved to Canada when she was 19. She graduated in 1980 with honours in mathematics from the [University of Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saskatchewan). She did her graduate studies at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), earning a master's degree in 1984 and a Ph.D. in 1988, supervised by Jerry Lawless. Her dissertation was *Mixed Poisson Models and Regression Methods for Count Data*.

After a year at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary), she joined the faculty at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) in 1989, and became the founding chair of the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science there at its establishment in 2001. She served as president of the [Western North American Region](https://www.wnar.org) of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) in 2002[^o4] and of the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) in 2007. She became Burnaby Mountain Research Chair at Simon Fraser before moving to the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario) as Dean of Science in 2011. She stepped down as Dean at Western and became vice president for research at Waterloo in 2017.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dean was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2007, and in 2010 she was elected as a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science). She is also an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

In 2003, she won the CRM-SSC Prize in Statistics of the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) and the Statistical Society of Canada. In 2012 she won the Trinidad & Tobago Canadian High Commission Award. She was awarded the Gold Medal of the Statistical Society of Canada in 2023.
