# Journal of Modern Applied Statistical Methods

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Modern Applied Statistical Methods"}

The ***Journal of Modern Applied Statistical Methods*** is a biannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open access journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_journal).[^o3] It was established in 2002 by [Shlomo Sawilowsky](wikipedia://en/11453957), and is currently published by the [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University) Library System in [Detroit, MI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit,_MI). The *Current Index to Statistics* classifies it as one of over 160 core statistics journals.[^o4] The journal originally appeared as a print and electronic journal through volume 8(1) in 2009, and subsequently as an electronic journal only. It publishes peer-reviewed work pertaining to new [statistical tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_hypothesis_testing) and the comparison of existing statistical tests; [bootstrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bootstrapping), [Jackknife](wikipedia://en/3763850#Jackknife), and [resampling](wikipedia://en/3763850) methods; [nonparametric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-parametric_statistics), robust, [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027), exact, and approximate [randomization](wikipedia://en/65462) methods; and statistical [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775), [pseudorandom number generators](wikipedia://en/45524), and simulation techniques. The journal is indexed in the Elsevier Bibliographic Database, [EMBASE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMBASE), [Compendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compendex), Geobase, [PsycINFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PsycINFO), [ScienceDirect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScienceDirect), and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus). It is also listed in the *Encyclopedia of Measurement and Statistics*[^o5] and Cabells.[^o6]
