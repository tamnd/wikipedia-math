# François Labourie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Labourie"}

**François Labourie** (born 15 December 1960) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has made various contributions to [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), including [pseudoholomorphic curves](wikipedia://en/2294542), [Anosov diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/2320078), and [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882). In a series of papers with [Yves Benoist](wikipedia://en/48465632) and [Patrick Foulon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Foulon), he solved a conjecture on Anosov flows in compact [contact manifolds](wikipedia://en/476398).

:::

He was educated at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) and [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University), where he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1987 under supervision of [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29) and [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363). In 1992 he was awarded one of the inaugural prizes of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470). In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o4]
