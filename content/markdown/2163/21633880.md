# Alfred William Flux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred William Flux"}

**Sir Alfred William Flux**, (8 April 1867 – 16 July 1942) was a British [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Flux was born in the [Landport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landport) district of [Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth) in 1867, the son of a cement maker.[^o7] He attended [Portsmouth Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth_Grammar_School) then studied mathematics at [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge), where he was a [Senior Wrangler](wikipedia://en/19355609) in 1887 (sharing the honour in a tie with three others). While at Cambridge he became friends with [Alfred Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Marshall), who interested him in economics. He was a foundation member of the Economic Society (1890). In 1893 he was appointed as Cobden Lecturer in Political Economy at [Owens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester), Manchester[^o5] and from then until 1908 taught economics, at Manchester and then at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University), Montreal.[^o6] In 1897, while in Manchester he married Harriet Emily Hansen, a Danish woman.[^o7] He served as secretary of the [Manchester Literary and Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Literary_and_Philosophical_Society) in 1900–1901.

Flux returned to London in 1908 to take up a post as advisor to the Commercial, Labour and Statistics Department. In 1918, he was appointed Head of the Statistics Department of the [Board of Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Trade).[^o7] The [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) awarded him the [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in Silver in 1921 and in Gold in 1930. He also served as President of the Society between 1928 and 1930.[^o8]

Flux retired to Denmark in 1932 and was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Bachelor) in 1934. He died of pneumonia in 1942, aged 75.[^o7][^o4]
