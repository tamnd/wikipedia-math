# Mieczysław Omyła

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mieczysław Omyła"}

**Mieczysław Władysław Omyła** (born 1941[^o3]) is a Polish professor of humanities, logician, philosopher. He is a lecturer in logic and philosophy at the [Cardinal Stefan Wyszyński University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_Stefan_Wyszyński_University_in_Warsaw) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

### Biography {#Biography}

He comes from [Soblówka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soblówka).[^o7] He was a student of [Roman Suszko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Suszko) and an associate of [Bogusław Wolniewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogusław_Wolniewicz).

He continued Suszko's logical and ontological works, often referring to Wolniewicz's [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology) of the situation. In 1991 he became the Head of the Logic Department at the Institute of Philosophy of the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). In 1997 he obtained the academic title of professor of humanities.[^o8] He was a member of the Polish Semiotic Society (Polskie Towarzystwo Semiotyczne) and the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) (Polska Akademia Nauk).[^o8]

Janusz Wesserling obtained a PhD degree under his supervision, .[^o9]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- Paradygmat fregowski a teorie sytuacji (The frigate paradigm and theories of the situation) (2009)[^o8]
- Remarks on Non-Fregean Logic (2007)[^o8]
- Homomorfizm semantyczny a reifikacja sytuacji (Semantic homomorphism and reification of the situation) (2006)[^o8]
- Aksjomat Fregego a ekstensjonalność (Frege's axiom and extensionality) (2005)[^o8]
