# 1899 Puerto Rico census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1899 Puerto Rico census"}

The **1899 Puerto Rico census** (**Porto Rico** was also used) was a [national population census held in Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census) and first under [U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) control by the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau) for the U.S. War Department.[^o4] It was the tenth census combined with the nine previous censuses taken by [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain). The day used for the census was Friday November 10, 1899 with a total population counted as 953,243 - an increase of 154,678 or 16% over the previous [1887 census](wikipedia://en/78707423) taken by the [Spanish government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_government).[^o5]

:::

## Population schedule {#Population_schedule}

*Image: Enumerators of Arecibo.jpg.* Enumerators of [Arecibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arecibo).

The population schedule used in the census, translated into English and reduced in size is as follows:[^o7] **Situation**

- Street.
- Number of house.
- Number of house or building in the order of visitation.
- Number of family in the order of visitation.
- Name of every person residing with this family or in this house

**Relationship**

- Relationship of each person to the head of the family.

**Personal description**

- [Color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_terminology_for_race).
- [Sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity).
- [Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_ageing) at last birthday.
- Status.

**Nativity**

- Native country of this person.

**Citizenship**

- [Cuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubans), [Spaniard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaniards), or in suspense.

**Occupation**

- Occupation, trade or profession of every person 10 years of age and over.

**Instruction**

- Months of attendance at school during the last school year.
- Can read. (primary)
- Can write. (primary)
- Higher.

**Sanitary conditions**

- Source of water used.
- Disposition of garbage.
- [Latrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latrine) system.

## Method of tabulation {#Method_of_tabulation}

It was taken that to save time, the tabulation should be done by machine and not by the old hand-tally system. As the machines invented by Mr [Herman Hollerith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Hollerith) were successfully used in the earlier eleventh and were to be used for the twelfth U.S. census, it was adopted.[^o9]

## Nativity {#Nativity}

### Birthplace {#Birthplace}

The number of foreign-born returned by the census is 13,872 or about 1.5% of the total population. There were few regions in the [Western Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Hemisphere) in which the proportion of natives is so high and that of the foreign-born so low. Of the total number of foreigners 5,935 or 43 per cent were found in the three cities of [San Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan,_Puerto_Rico), [Ponce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponce,_Puerto_Rico) and [Mayagüez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayagüez,_Puerto_Rico). ### Race {#Race}

With reference to [race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_%28human_categorization%29), the population of Porto Rico is divided by the census into two main classes - those who are and those who are not [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Puerto_Ricans). The number belonging to each of these classes is as follows:[^o75]

::: {#o76 .table cue=table}

| Race | Population | % |
|---|---|---|
| [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Puerto_Ricans) | 589,426 | \61.8 |
| Colored\* | 363,817 | \38.2 |
| *(Persons of [mixed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial) blood)* | *304,352* | *\83.6* |
| Puerto Rico | 953,243 | \100.0 |

:::

The colored in the table includes very few (75) [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_immigration_to_Puerto_Rico) and many persons of mixed white and Black African blood as well as pure Black. Somewhat more than three-fifths of the population of Porto Rico are white and nearly two-fifths are partly or entirely African or Black. By the 1899 census five-sixths (83.6 per cent) of the total colored were returned as of mixed blood.[^o79]
