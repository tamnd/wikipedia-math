# Apala Majumdar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Apala Majumdar"}

**Apala Majumdar** is a British applied mathematician specialising in the mathematics of [liquid crystals](wikipedia://en/17973). She is a professor of Applied Mathematics at the [Department of Mathematics, University of Manchester](wikipedia://en/10387568).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Majumdar did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol). As a graduate student at Bristol, she also worked with [Hewlett Packard Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_Labs). She was awarded a PhD in applied mathematics at the University of Bristol in 2006; her dissertation, *Liquid crystals and tangent unit-vector fields in polyhedral geometries*, was jointly supervised by Jonathan Robbins and Maxim Zyskin.

After working as a Royal Commission of the Exhibition of 1851 Research Fellow at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), she moved to the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath) in 2012, having been awarded a 5-year EPSRC Career Acceleration Fellowship in 2011. At Bath she became a Reader and the Director of the Centre for Nonlinear Mechanics (2018-2019). In 2019 she was appointed as a professor of Applied Mathematics at the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde) and moved to [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 2025.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

The [British Liquid Crystal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Liquid_Crystal_Society) gave Majumdar their Young Scientist Award in 2012. The [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) gave her their [Anne Bennett Prize](wikipedia://en/61347778) in 2015. In 2019 she was the winner of the academic category of the [FDM Everywoman in Technology Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everywoman_%28organisation%29). In 2024, she was elected as a fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o5]
