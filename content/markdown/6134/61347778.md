# Anne Bennett Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anne Bennett Prize"}

The **Anne Bennett Prize** and **Senior Anne Bennett Prize** are awards given by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3][^o28]

:::

In every third year, the society offers the Senior Anne Bennett prize to a mathematician normally based in the United Kingdom for work in, influence on or service to mathematics, particularly in relation to advancing the careers of women in mathematics.[^o3]

In the two years out of three in which the Senior Anne Bennett Prize is not awarded, the society offers the Anne Bennett Prize to a mathematician within ten years of their doctorate for work in and influence on mathematics, particularly acting as an inspiration for women mathematicians.[^o3]

Both prizes are awarded in memory of Anne Bennett, an administrator for the London Mathematical Society who died in 2012.[^o4] The prize was established in 2013 and first given in 2014.

The Anne Bennett Prizes should be distinguished from the Anne Bennett Memorial Award for Distinguished Service of the [Royal Society of Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Chemistry),[^o5] for which Anne Bennett also worked.[^o4]

## Anne Bennett Prize Winners {#Anne_Bennett_Prize_Winners}

The winners of the Anne Bennett Prize have been:

- 2015 [Apala Majumdar](wikipedia://en/61348467), in recognition of her outstanding contributions to the mathematics of liquid crystals and to the liquid crystal community.[^o7][^o24]
- 2016 [Julia Wolf](wikipedia://en/58446958), in recognition of her outstanding contributions to additive number theory, combinatorics and harmonic analysis and to the mathematical community.[^o8][^o25]
- 2018 [Lotte Hollands](wikipedia://en/59049883), in recognition of her outstanding research at the interface between quantum theory and geometry and of her leadership in mathematical outreach activities.[^o9][^o27]
- 2019 [Eva-Maria Graefe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva-Maria_Graefe), in recognition of her outstanding research in quantum theory and the inspirational role she has played among female students and early career researchers in mathematics and physics.[^o10]
- 2021 [Viveka Erlandsson](wikipedia://en/69989833), "for her outstanding achievements in geometry and topology and her inspirational active role in promoting women mathematicians".[^o11]
- 2022 [Asma Hassannezhad](wikipedia://en/71610910), "for her outstanding work in spectral geometry and her substantial contributions toward the advancement of women in mathematics."[^o12]
- 2024 [Ana Ros Camacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Ros_Camacho), "for her ground-breaking work on categorical proofs of the Landau–Ginzburg/Conformal Field Theory correspondence and her tireless dedication to the advancement of women in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)"[^o13]
- 2025 [Henna Koivusalo](wikipedia://en/80387862), "for her work on cut-and-project sets, dynamical systems and fractals and her dedication to the advancement of women in mathematics."[^o14][^o15]

## Senior Anne Bennett Prize Winners {#Senior_Anne_Bennett_Prize_Winners}

The winners of the Senior Anne Bennett Prize have been:

- 2014 [Caroline Series](wikipedia://en/43764434), in recognition of her leading contributions to hyperbolic geometry and [symbolic dynamics](wikipedia://en/1986263), and of the major impact of her numerous initiatives towards the advancement of women in mathematics.[^o17][^o23]
- 2017 [Alison Etheridge](wikipedia://en/46715790), in recognition of her outstanding research on measure-valued stochastic processes and applications to population biology; and for her impressive leadership and service to the profession.[^o18][^o26]
- 2020 [Peter Clarkson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Clarkson), "in recognition of his tireless work to support gender equality in UK mathematics, and particularly for his leadership in developing good practice among departments of mathematical sciences".[^o19]
- 2023 [Eugénie Hunsicker](wikipedia://en/61820104), "for her outstanding work to improve equality and diversity in the mathematical community and for the depth of her mathematical achievements across an impressive range of areas, from L2 [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590) to data science."[^o20]
