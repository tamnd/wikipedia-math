# Sigmund Selberg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sigmund Selberg"}

**Sigmund Selberg** (11 August [1910](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910_in_Norway) – 20 April [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_in_Norway)) was a Norwegian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Langesund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langesund) as the son of [Ole Michael Ludvigsen Selberg](wikipedia://en/37564153) and Anna Kristina Brigtsdatter Skeie. He was twin brother of [Arne Selberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_Selberg) and brother of [Henrik Selberg](wikipedia://en/37563752) and [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021). He was appointed professor of mathematics at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) in [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim) from 1947 to 1977. His works mainly focused on the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o3][^o4]
