# Rolph Ludwig Edward Schwarzenberger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rolph Ludwig Edward Schwarzenberger"}

**Rolph Ludwig Edward Schwarzenberger** (7 February 1936 – 29 February 1992) was a British mathematician at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) who worked on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) (where he introduced [jumping lines](wikipedia://en/37551700)), [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography), and mathematics education.

:::

He was President of the [Mathematical Association](wikipedia://en/2742403) in 1983–1984.
