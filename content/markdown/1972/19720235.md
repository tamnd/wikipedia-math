# Michel Mandjes

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Mandjes"}

**Michael Robertus Hendrikus "Michel" Mandjes** (born 14 February 1970 in [Zaandam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaandam)) is a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for several contributions to [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578) and applied [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).[^o4] His research interests include queueing models for telecommunications, [traffic management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_management) and analysis, and [network economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_effect).

:::

He holds a full-professorship (Applied Probability and Queueing Theory) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) ([Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/66341573)).[^o5] From September 2004 he is advisor of the "Queueing and Performance Analysis" theme at [EURANDOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURANDOM), Eindhoven.[^o6]

He is author of the book "Large deviations for Gaussian queues", and is associate editor of the journals Stochastic Models and Queuing Systems.[^o7]

He contributed to the book *Queues and Lévy fluctuation theory*, published in 2015.[^o8]
