# John Lennox

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Lennox"}

**John Carson Lennox** (born 7 November 1943) is a Northern Irish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [bioethicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioethicist), and [lay theologian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lay_theologian). He has written many books on religion, ethics, the relationship between science and God (such as *Has Science Buried God* and *Can Science Explain Everything*); he has also participated in public debates with atheists, including [Richard Dawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins) and [Christopher Hitchens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Hitchens).

:::

Lennox earned a doctorate in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), followed by second and third doctorates from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University), respectively. As a professor, Lennox specialised in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He is emeritus professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)[^o3] at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), where he is also Emeritus Fellow in Mathematics and [Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_Science) at [Green Templeton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Templeton_College), and has worked as [adjunct lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunct_lecturer) at [Wycliffe Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wycliffe_Hall) and at the Oxford Centre for Christian Apologetics. He is also an Associate Fellow of the [Saïd Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saïd_Business_School) and a Senior Fellow at the [Trinity Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Forum).

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

John Carson Lennox was born on 7 November 1943 in [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast) and brought up in [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh), where his father ran a store.[^o5] He attended [The Royal School, Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Royal_School,_Armagh), and went on to become Exhibitioner and Senior Scholar at [Emmanuel College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_College,_Cambridge), where in 1962, he also attended the last lectures of [C.&nbsp;S. Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.&nbsp;S._Lewis) on the poet [John Donne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Donne). Lennox obtained a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts_%28Oxbridge_and_Dublin%29), a [Master of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2108291) and a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) with the dissertation *Centrality and Permutability in Soluble Groups* (1970).[^o6]

Lennox was awarded a [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) by [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University) in 1993 for his research. Lennox also holds a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (by incorporation)[^o7] and an [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) degree in [bioethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioethics) at the [University of Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Surrey). In addition, he was a [Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Fellow) at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg) and the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

*Image: John Lennox.jpg.* Lennox in 2008

Upon completing his doctorate, Lennox moved to [Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff), [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales), becoming a [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29) in Mathematics at the [University of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales), Cardiff. Lennox is currently the President of OCCA (The Oxford Centre for Christian Apologetics). He also teaches [science and religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_religion) in the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). During his 29 years in Cardiff he spent a year at each of the universities of [Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Würzburg), [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg) (as an [Alexander von Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Fellow)), and [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), and has lectured extensively in both Eastern and Western Europe, [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia), and [North America](wikipedia://en/21139) on mathematics, [apologetics](wikipedia://en/246903), and the exposition of [scripture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripture).

Lennox is the author of a number of books on the relations between science, religion, and ethics. He has published over 70 peer-reviewed articles on mathematics, co-authored two Oxford Mathematical Monographs, and worked as a translator of Russian mathematics.

He has spoken in many different countries, in conferences and as an academic fellow, including numerous trips to the former [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^o10][^o11]

On 14 March 2012, he presented an edition of the [Lent Talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lent_Talks) for [BBC Radio Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_Four). Lennox has also given lectures at the [Veritas Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veritas_Forum) on topics such as the relationship between science and religion,[^o12][^o13] the [existence of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existence_of_god),[^o14] [doubt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubt), and the [problems of evil and suffering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problem_of_evil).[^o15] Additionally, he is a Senior Fellow of the Trinity Forum, a Christian nonprofit organisation that develops leaders to make contributions to cultural renewal.

### Debates {#Debates}

*Image: John Lennox and Christopher Hitchens debating.jpg.* Lennox (left) debating religion with [Christopher Hitchens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Hitchens) in Alabama, March 2009

Lennox has been part of numerous public debates defending the Christian faith, including debates with [Christopher Hitchens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Hitchens), [Michael Shermer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shermer), [Richard Dawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins), [Lawrence Krauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Krauss), [Peter Atkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Atkins), [Victor Stenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Stenger), [Michael Tooley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Tooley), [Stephen Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Law), and [Peter Singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Singer).

- On 3 October 2007, Lennox debated [Richard Dawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins) at the [Samford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samford_University) in [Birmingham, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham,_Alabama), on Dawkins's views expressed in his book *[The God Delusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_God_Delusion)*.[^o17][^o18][^o19]
- Lennox and Dawkins had a discussion in April 2008 at [Trinity College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Oxford), to expand upon topics left undeveloped during The God Delusion Debate.[^o20][^o21]
- On 9 August 2008, Lennox debated [Christopher Hitchens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Hitchens) at the [Edinburgh International Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_International_Festival) in [Edinburgh, Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh,_Scotland), on the question of whether Europe should jettison its religious past and welcome the "[New Atheism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Atheism)".[^o22][^o23]
- On 23 August 2008, Lennox debated [Michael Shermer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shermer) at the Wesley Conference Centre in [Sydney, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney,_Australia), on the existence of God.[^o24][^o25]
- On 21 October 2008, Lennox again debated Dawkins at the [Oxford University Museum of Natural History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Museum_of_Natural_History), the site of the [1860 Oxford evolution debate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1860_Oxford_evolution_debate) between [Thomas Henry Huxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Henry_Huxley) and [Samuel Wilberforce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Wilberforce). The debate was titled "Has Science Buried God?".[^o26][^o27] *[The Spectator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Spectator)* called the event "[Huxley-Wilberforce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1860_Oxford_evolution_debate), Round Two."[^o28]
- On 3 March 2009, Lennox debated Hitchens for the second time at [Samford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samford_University) in [Birmingham, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham,_Alabama), on the question "Is God Great?" The debate addressed the validity of some of Hitchens' claims in his book *[God is Not Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_is_Not_Great)*.[^o29][^o30]
- On 20 July 2011, Lennox debated [Peter Singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Singer) at the [Melbourne Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Town_Hall) in [Melbourne, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne,_Australia), on the topic "Is There a God?"[^o31][^o32][^o33]
- In August 2021, Lennox was the chief guest for Malhar Fest, hosted by [St. Xavier's College, Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Xavier's_College,_Mumbai).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Lennox speaks English, Russian, French, Spanish, and German. He is married to Sally and has three children and 10 grandchildren.[^o17] He has a brother named Gilbert Lennox, previously an elder in Glenabbey Church, [Glengormley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glengormley).[^o35] The hymn writer and recording artist [Kristyn Getty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristyn_Getty), Gilbert's daughter, is Lennox's niece.
