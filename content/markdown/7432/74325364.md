# Kato's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kato's inequality"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a subfield of mathematics, **Kato's inequality** is a [distributional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29) inequality for the [Laplace operator](wikipedia://en/174706) or certain [elliptic operators](wikipedia://en/550137). It was proven in 1972 by the Japanese mathematician [Tosio Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosio_Kato).[^o2]

:::

The original inequality is for some degenerate elliptic operators.[^o3] This article treats the special (but important) case for the Laplace operator.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=named-heading name="Inequality for the Laplace operator"}

## Inequality for the Laplace operator {#Inequality_for_the_Laplace_operator}

Let $\Omega\subset \mathbb{R}^d$ be a bounded and [open set](wikipedia://en/39358), and $f\in L^1_{\operatorname{loc}}(\Omega)$ such that $\Delta f\in L^1_{\operatorname{loc}}(\Omega)$. Then the following holds[^o7][^o4]

$\Delta |f| \geq \operatorname{Re}\left((\operatorname{sgn}\overline f) \Delta f\right)\quad$ in $\;\mathcal{D}'(\Omega)$,

where

$\operatorname{sgn}\overline f=\begin{cases}\frac{\overline{f(x)}}{|f(x)|} & \text{if }f\neq 0\\ 0 & \text{if }f=0. \end{cases}$[^o8]

$L^1_{\operatorname{loc}}$ is the space of [locally integrable functions](wikipedia://en/4258398) – i.e., functions that are [integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_function) on every [*compact* subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_subset) of their [domains of definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_of_definition).

### Remarks {#Remarks}

- Sometimes the inequality is stated in the form

$\Delta f^+ \geq \operatorname{Re}\left(1_{[f\geq 0]} \Delta f\right)\quad$ in $\;\mathcal{D}'(\Omega)$

where $f^+=\operatorname{max}(f,0)$ and $1_{[f\geq 0]}$ is the [indicator function](wikipedia://en/240790).

- If $f$ is continuous in $\Omega$ then

$\Delta |f| \geq \operatorname{Re}\left((\operatorname{sgn}\overline f) \Delta f\right)\quad$ in $\;\mathcal{D}'(\{f\neq 0\})$.[^o10]

:::
