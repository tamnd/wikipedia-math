# Congruent isoscelizers point

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Congruent isoscelizers point"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **congruent isoscelizers point** is a special point associated with a plane [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) and it is listed as *X*(173) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593). This point was introduced to the study of triangle geometry by Peter Yff in 1989.[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: Congruent isoscelizers point.svg.* $\overline{P_1Q_1} = \overline{P_2Q_2} = \overline{P_3Q_3}$

An [isoscelizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoscelizer) of an angle $A$ in a triangle $\triangle ABC$ is a line through points $P_{1}$ and $Q_{1}$, where $P_{1}$ lies on $AB$ and $Q_{1}$ on $AC$, such that the triangle $\triangle AP_{1}Q_{1}$ is an isosceles triangle. An isoscelizer of angle $A$ is a line perpendicular to the bisector of angle $A$.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be any triangle. Let $P_{1}Q_{1}, P_{2}Q_{2}, P_{3}Q_{3}$ be the isoscelizers of the angles $A, B, C$ respectively such that they all have the same length. Then, for a unique configuration, the three isoscelizers $P_{1}Q_{1}, P_{2}Q_{2}, P_{3}Q_{3}$ are concurrent. The point of concurrence is the *congruent isoscelizers point* of triangle $\triangle ABC$.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

*Image: Construction for congruent isoscelizers point.svg.* Construction for congruent isoscelizers point. solid black solid red solid cyan solid green solid blue dashed black 2px

- The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the congruent isoscelizers point of triangle $\triangle ABC$ are[^o2]

$$\begin{array}{ccccc} \cos\frac{B}{2} + \cos\frac{C}{2} - \cos\frac{A}{2} &:& \cos\frac{C}{2} + \cos\frac{A}{2} - \cos\frac{B}{2} &:& \cos\frac{A}{2} + \cos\frac{B}{2} - \cos\frac{C}{2} \\[4pt] = \quad \tan\frac{A}{2} + \sec\frac{A}{2} \quad \ \ &:& \tan\frac{B}{2} + \sec\frac{B}{2} &:& \tan\frac{C}{2} + \sec\frac{C}{2} \end{array}$$

- The [intouch triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intouch_triangle) of the intouch triangle of triangle $\triangle ABC$ is [perspective](wikipedia://en/14909067) to $\triangle ABC$, and the congruent isoscelizers point is the [perspector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspector). This fact can be used to locate by geometrical constructions the congruent isoscelizers point of any given $\triangle ABC$.[^o2]

:::
