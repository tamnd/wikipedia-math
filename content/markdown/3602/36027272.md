# Lester Dubins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lester Dubins"}

*Image: Lester Dubins.jpg.* Lester Dubins

**Lester Dubins** (April 27, 1920 – February 11, 2010) was an American mathematician noted primarily for his research in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He was a faculty member at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) from 1962 through 2004, and in retirement was Professor [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) of Mathematics and Statistics.

:::

It has been thought that, since classic red-and-black casino [roulette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roulette) is a game in which the house on average wins more than the gambler, that "bold play", i.e. betting one's whole purse on a single trial, is a uniquely optimal strategy. While a graduate student at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), Dubins surprised his teacher [Leonard Jimmie Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Jimmie_Savage) with a mathematical demonstration that this is not true. Dubins and Savage wrote a book that appeared in 1965 titled *How to Gamble if You Must (Inequalities for [Stochastic Processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes))* which presented a mathematical theory of [gambling](wikipedia://en/11921) processes and optimal behavior in gambling situations, pointing out their relevance to traditional approaches to probability. Under the influence of the work of [Bruno de Finetti](wikipedia://en/180825), Dubins and Savage worked in the context of [finitely additive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_additivity) rather than [countably additive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable_additivity) probability theory, thereby bypassing some technical difficulties.[^o2]

Dubins was the author of nearly a hundred scholarly publications. Besides probability, some of these were on curves of minimal length under constraints on curvature and initial and final tangents (see [Dubins path](wikipedia://en/35938288)), [Tarski's circle squaring problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarski's_circle_squaring_problem), [convex analysis](wikipedia://en/3237201), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).

His doctoral students include [Theodore Hill](wikipedia://en/815323). Together with [Gideon E. Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gideon_E._Schwarz) he proved the [Dubins–Schwarz theorem](wikipedia://en/74144071).
