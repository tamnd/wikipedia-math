# Abel equation of the first kind

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abel equation of the first kind"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **Abel equation of the first kind**, named after [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573), is any [ordinary differential equation](wikipedia://en/32742753) that is [cubic](wikipedia://en/243709) in the unknown function. In other words, it is an equation of the form

:::

$$y'=f_3(x)y^3+f_2(x)y^2+f_1(x)y+f_0(x) \,$$

where $f_3(x)\neq 0$.

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

If $f_3(x)=0$ and $f_0(x)=0$, or $f_2(x)=0$ and $f_0(x)=0$, the equation reduces to a [Bernoulli equation](wikipedia://en/1911217), while if $f_3(x) = 0$ the equation reduces to a [Riccati equation](wikipedia://en/304999).

:::

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Abel equation of the second kind"}

## Solution {#Solution}

The substitution $y=\dfrac{1}{u}$ brings the Abel equation of the first kind to the **Abel equation of the second kind**, of the form

$$uu'=-f_0(x)u^3-f_1(x)u^2-f_2(x)u-f_3(x). \,$$

The substitution

$$\begin{aligned} \xi & = \int f_3(x)E^2~dx, \\[6pt] u & = \left(y+\dfrac{f_2(x)}{3f_3(x)}\right)E^{-1}, \\[6pt] E & = \exp\left(\int\left(f_1(x)-\frac{f_2^2(x)}{3f_3(x)}\right)~dx\right) \end{aligned}$$

brings the Abel equation of the first kind to the [canonical form](wikipedia://en/515096)

$$u'=u^3+\phi(\xi). \,$$

[Dimitrios E. Panayotounakos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimitrios_E._Panayotounakos) and [Theodoros I. Zarmpoutis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodoros_I._Zarmpoutis) discovered an analytic method to solve the above equation in an implicit form.[^o6]

:::
