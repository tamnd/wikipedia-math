*Image: Political map of the world, April 1989. LOC 2010585495.jpg.* Map of countries in 1989

This is a **list of countries by population in 1989**, providing an overview of the world population before the [fall of the Iron Curtain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutions_of_1989).

:::

While the population data[^o8] is almost exclusively dated 1989, political developments before the summer of 1990 are taken into account, including [Yemeni unification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_unification) and [Namibian independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namibian_War_of_Independence) but not [German reunification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_reunification) which was finalised only in October, the [breakup of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakup_of_Yugoslavia) and [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) took place two years later, and the [dissolution of Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_Czechoslovakia) three years later.

The numbers given in *Aktuell '91*[^o8] are fully compatible with the data given by the U.S. Census Bureau,[^o9] where they can be compared, as the US Census Data refers to modern national borders instead of 1989 borders. Similar remarks apply to 1990 estimates in the [List of countries by past and future population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_past_and_future_population) which also only apply to modern-day national borders. See also [Soviet Census (1989)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Census_%281989%29) and [1990 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_United_States_census) for comparison.
