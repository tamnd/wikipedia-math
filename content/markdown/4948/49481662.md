# Andreas Brandstädt

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Brandstädt"}

**Andreas Brandstädt** (born 17 January 1949 in [Arnstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnstadt), [East Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany)) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

He graduated from the Friedrich Schiller [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena), Germany, with a Ph.D. (Dr. rer. nat.) in stochastics in 1976 and a habilitation (Dr. sc. nat.) in complexity theory in 1983. Since 1974 he worked there in the group of his academic teacher [Gerd Wechsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerd_Wechsung).

From 1991 to 1994, he was the professor for Computer Science in the Department of Mathematics, [^o4] at the [University of Duisburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg), Germany, and from 1994 to 2014 he was the professor for Theoretical Computer Science[^o5] at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock), Germany.

He was a visiting professor at the universities of [Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Metz), [Amiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amiens), and [Clermont-Ferrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Clermont-Ferrand) (France) and at the [University of Primorska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Primorska) in [Koper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koper) ([Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia)). He was Invited Speaker at various conferences in Argentina, Austria, [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus), Brasil, Canada, China, France, Greece, India, Israel, Norway, Poland, [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia), and Switzerland.

Brandstädt is an active researcher in [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithms), [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555), and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). A frequently used tool in his papers is tree structure of graphs and hypergraphs such as for [hypertrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypertree), [strongly chordal graphs](wikipedia://en/24809733) and [chordal graphs](wikipedia://en/744165).

He frequently took part in program committees such as Workshop on Graph-Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science (and four times was a co-organizer of this conference) and is member of the [Editorial Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_Board) of [Discrete Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47656173).[^o6]
