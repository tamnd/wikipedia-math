# Bruno Courcelle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bruno Courcelle"}

**Bruno Courcelle** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), best known for [Courcelle's theorem](wikipedia://en/35810608) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Courcelle earned his Ph.D. in 1976 from the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413), then called IRIA, under the supervision of [Maurice Nivat](wikipedia://en/26259455). He then joined the Laboratoire Bordelais de Recherche en Informatique (LaBRI) at the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1), where he remained for the rest of his career.[^o5] He has been a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) since 2007.[^o4]

A workshop in honor of Courcelle's retirement was held in Bordeaux in 2012.[^o5][^o6] Courcelle was the first recipient of the [S. Barry Cooper Prize](wikipedia://en/22521356#S._Barry_Cooper_Prize) of the [Association Computability in Europe](wikipedia://en/22521356) in 2020.[^o7] In 2022, Courcelle was awarded the [EATCS-IPEC Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EATCS-IPEC_Nerode_Prize).[^o8]

During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), Courcelle protested against vaccination mandates in France.[^o9]

## Work {#Work}

He is known for [Courcelle's theorem](wikipedia://en/35810608), which combines [second-order logic](wikipedia://en/341428), the theory of [formal languages](wikipedia://en/10939), and [tree decompositions of graphs](wikipedia://en/159023) to show that a wide class of algorithmic problems in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) have efficient solutions.

Notable publications also include:

- Bruno Courcelle. (1983). "Fundamental Properties of Infinite Trees". *Theoretical Computer Science*. 25 (2). p. 95–169. doi:10.1016/0304-3975(83)90059-2
- Bruno Courcelle. (1990). "Formal Models and Semantics". B. Elsevier. p. 459–492. isbn:0-444-88074-7
- Courcelle, Bruno. (1999). "Rewriting Techniques and Applications". 1631. Springer. p. 90–91. doi:10.1007/3-540-48685-2\_7. isbn:978-3-540-66201-3
