# Stephen Whisson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Whisson"}

**Stephen Whisson** (1710[^o3] – 3 November 1783) was a tutor at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), United Kingdom, and coached 72 students in the 1744–1754 period.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wisson was from [St Neots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Neots), [Huntingdonshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntingdonshire) and was the son of a [publican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publican). In 1735, he matriculated from [Wakefield School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_Grammar_School,_Wakefield), [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire).

On 29 November 1734, he was admitted as a [sizar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sizar) at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), becoming a scholar in 1738.

- 1741 priest
- 1746 – c. 1766 Vicar of [Babraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babraham), [Cambridgeshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridgeshire).

- 1771–83 Rector of [Orwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orwell,_Cambridgeshire), [Cambridgeshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridgeshire).
- 1783 Buried in [Trinity Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Chapel).
