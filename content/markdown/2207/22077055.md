# Walter Taylor (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Taylor"}

**Walter Taylor** (1700 – 23 February 1743/44) was a [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) mathematics tutor who coached 83 students in the 1724–1743 period. He later was appointed as the [Regius Professor of Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Greek_%28Cambridge%29).

:::

He was the son of John Taylor, Vicar of [Tuxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuxford), [Nottinghamshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottinghamshire). He matriculated in 1716 from [Wakefield School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_Grammar_School,_Wakefield), [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire). Taylor was admitted as a [pensioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pensioner) at Trinity on 7 April 1716.

[Robert Smith](wikipedia://en/447816) was Taylor's Cambridge tutor.
