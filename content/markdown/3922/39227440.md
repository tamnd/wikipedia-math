# Linked field

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linked field"}

In mathematics, a **linked field** is a [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_%28algebra%29) for which the [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478) attached to [quaternion algebras](wikipedia://en/2776890) have a common property.

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Linked quaternion algebras"}

## Linked quaternion algebras {#Linked_quaternion_algebras}

Let *F* be a field of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) not equal to 2. Let *A* = (*a*<sub>1</sub>,*a*<sub>2</sub>) and *B* = (*b*<sub>1</sub>,*b*<sub>2</sub>) be quaternion algebras over *F*. The algebras *A* and *B* are **linked quaternion algebras** over *F* if there is *x* in *F* such that *A* is equivalent to (*x*,*y*) and *B* is equivalent to (*x*,*z*).[^o4]

The **Albert form** for *A*, *B* is

$$q = \left\langle{ -a_1, -a_2, a_1a_2, b_1, b_2, -b_1b_2 }\right\rangle \ .$$

It can be regarded as the difference in the [Witt ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witt_ring_%28forms%29) of the ternary forms attached to the imaginary subspaces of *A* and *B*.[^o5] The quaternion algebras are linked if and only if the Albert form is [isotropic](wikipedia://en/10917170).[^o4]

:::

## Linked fields {#Linked_fields}

The field *F* is *linked* if any two quaternion algebras over *F* are linked.[^o4] Every [global](wikipedia://en/288358) and [local field](wikipedia://en/145375) is linked since all quadratic forms of degree 6 over such fields are isotropic.

The following properties of *F* are equivalent:[^o4]

- *F* is linked.
- Any two quaternion algebras over *F* are linked.
- Every *Albert form* (dimension six form of discriminant −1) is isotropic.
- The quaternion algebras form a subgroup of the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078) of *F*.
- Every dimension five form over *F* is a [Pfister neighbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfister_neighbour).
- No [biquaternion algebra](wikipedia://en/42600802) over *F* is a [division algebra](wikipedia://en/26549625).

A nonreal linked field has [u-invariant](wikipedia://en/36821900) equal to 1,2,4 or 8.[^o4]
