# Quaternionic structure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quaternionic structure"}

In mathematics, a **quaternionic structure** or $\boldsymbol{Q}$**\-structure** is an axiomatic system that abstracts the concept of a [quaternion algebra](wikipedia://en/2776890) over a [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_%28algebra%29).

:::

A *quaternionic structure* is a triple $(G, Q, q)$ where $G$ is an [elementary abelian group](wikipedia://en/4843202) of [exponent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponent_%28group_theory%29) $2$ with a distinguished element $-1$, $Q$ is a [pointed set](wikipedia://en/1242444) with distinguished element $1$, and $q$ is a symmetric [surjection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surjection) $G\times G \to Q$ satisfying axioms

$$\begin{aligned}\text{1.} \quad &q(a,(-1)a) = 1,\\ \text{2.} \quad &q(a,b) = q(a,c) \Leftrightarrow q(a,bc) = 1,\\ \text{3.} \quad &q(a,b) = q(c,d) \Rightarrow \exists x\mid q(a,b) = q(a,x), q(c,d) = q(c,x)\end{aligned}.$$

Every field $F$ gives rise to a $Q$-structure by taking $G$ to be $F^{\ast}/F^{\ast2}$, $Q$ the set of [Brauer classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brauer_class) of quaternion algebras in the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078) of $F$ with the [split quaternion algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_quaternion_algebra) as distinguished element and $q(a,b)$ the quaternion algebra $(a,b)_{F}$.
