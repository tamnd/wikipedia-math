# Paul Mahlo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Mahlo"}

**Friedrich Paul Mahlo** (born 28 July 1883 in [Coswig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coswig,_Saxony-Anhalt), [Duchy of Anhalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Anhalt); died 20 August 1971 in [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_%28Saale%29), [Bezirk Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezirk_Halle)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

Mahlo introduced [Mahlo cardinals](wikipedia://en/248081) in 1911. He also showed that the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705) implies the existence of a [Luzin set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luzin_set).
