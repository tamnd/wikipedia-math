# Hořava–Lifshitz gravity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hořava–Lifshitz gravity"}

**Hořava–Lifshitz gravity** (or **Hořava gravity**) is a theory of [quantum gravity](wikipedia://en/25312) proposed by [Petr Hořava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petr_Hořava_%28theorist%29) in 2009.[^o2] It solves the problem of different concepts of time in [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024) by treating the quantum concept as the more fundamental so that space and time are not equivalent ([anisotropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anisotropy)) at high energy level. The relativistic concept of time with its [Lorentz invariance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_invariance) emerges at large distances. The theory relies on the theory of [foliations](wikipedia://en/371227) to produce its [causal structure](wikipedia://en/14246049). It is related to topologically massive gravity and the [Cotton tensor](wikipedia://en/1074140). It is a possible [UV completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UV_completion) of general relativity. Also, the speed of light goes to infinity at high energies. The novelty of this approach, compared to previous approaches to quantum gravity such as [loop quantum gravity](wikipedia://en/152664), is that it uses concepts from [condensed matter physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_matter_physics) such as [quantum critical phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_critical_point). Hořava's initial formulation was found to have side-effects such as predicting very different results for a spherical [Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun) compared to a slightly non-spherical Sun, so others have modified the theory. Inconsistencies remain,[^o3][^o4][^o5] though progress was made on the theory.[^o6] Nevertheless, observations of [gravitational waves](wikipedia://en/8111079) emitted by the neutron-star merger [GW170817](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GW170817) contravene predictions made by this model of gravity.[^o7][^o8][^o9] Some have revised the theory to account for this.[^o10]

:::

## Detailed balance condition {#Detailed_balance_condition}

Hořava originally imposed the theory to satisfy the detailed balance condition which considerably reduces the number of coupling constants in the action.
