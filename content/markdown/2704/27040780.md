# Adrian Krzyżanowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adrian Krzyżanowski"}

*Image: Adryan Krzyżanowski (58073).jpg.*

**Adrian Krzyżanowski** (born 8 September 1788 in [Dębowo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dębowo,_Augustów_County) - died 21 August 1852 in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw)) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and translator of [German literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_literature).

:::

## Life {#Life}

From 1805 to 1810 he taught in a school in Warsaw, then was a professor of mathematics in Radzyń and [Płock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Płock) before studying from 1817 to 1820 in Paris. He was also a professor at the [Warsaw Lyceum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Lyceum), which had been founded by Prussia, and at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw).

Krzyżanowski was also involved in the [November Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_Uprising) after which the university was closed by the Russians. He made a living by translating literature into Polish, for example the German novels of [Alexander von Oppeln-Bronikowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Oppeln-Bronikowski).

He also published several articles on [Nicolaus Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus).

Krzyżanowski died of cholera in 1852 and was buried at the [Powązki Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powązki_Cemetery) (163, VI).[^o3]
