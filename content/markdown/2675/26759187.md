# Summation equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Summation equation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **summation equation** or **discrete integral equation** is an equation in which an unknown [function](wikipedia://en/185427) appears under a [summation](wikipedia://en/246160) sign. The theories of summation equations and [integral equations](wikipedia://en/474234) can be unified as *integral equations on time scales*[^o2] using [time scale calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_scale_calculus). A summation equation compares to a [difference equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation) as an [integral equation](wikipedia://en/474234) compares to a [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309).

:::

The Volterra summation equation is:

$$x(t) = f(t) + \sum_{s=m}^n k \bigl( t, s, x(s) \bigr)$$

where $x$ is the unknown function, $s, t$ are [integers](wikipedia://en/14563), and $f, k$ are known functions.
