# Beppo-Levi space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beppo-Levi space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a branch of mathematics, a **Beppo Levi space**, named after [Beppo Levi](wikipedia://en/8718312), is a certain space of [generalized functions](wikipedia://en/1580246).

:::

In the following, $D'$ is the space of [distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29), $S'$ is the space of [tempered distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempered_distributions) in $\mathbf{R}^{n}$, $D^{\alpha}$ the differentiation operator with $\alpha$ a multi-index, and $\widehat{v}$ is the [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) of $v$.

The Beppo Levi space is

$$\dot{W}^{r,p} = \left \{v \in D' \ : \ D^\alpha v \in L^p \text{ for all }|\alpha|=r\right \}.$$

An alternative definition is as follows: let $m \in \mathbf{N}, s \in \mathbf{R}$ such that

$$-m + \tfrac{n}{2} < s < \tfrac{n}{2}$$

and define:

$$\begin{aligned} H^s &= \left \{ v \in S' \ : \ \widehat{v} \in L^1_\text{loc}(\mathbf{R}^n), \int_{\mathbf{R}^n} |\xi|^{2s}| \widehat{v} (\xi)|^2 \, d\xi < \infty \right \} \\ [6pt] X^{m,s} &= \left \{ v \in D' \ : \ \forall \alpha \in \mathbf{N}^n, |\alpha| = m, D^{\alpha} v \in H^s \right \} \\ \end{aligned}$$

Then $X^{m,s}$ is the Beppo-Levi space.
