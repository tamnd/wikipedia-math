# Chris Godsil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chris Godsil"}

**Christopher David Godsil** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and the former Chair at the Department of [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [Optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) in the [faculty of mathematics](wikipedia://en/4320685) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). He wrote the popular textbook on [algebraic graph&nbsp;theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_graph&nbsp;theory), entitled *Algebraic Graph Theory*, with [Gordon Royle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Royle),[^o3] His earlier textbook on [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) discussed [distance-regular graphs](wikipedia://en/7768943) and [association&nbsp;schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association&nbsp;scheme).[^o4]

:::

## Background {#Background}

He started the [Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Algebraic_Combinatorics), and was an Editor-in-Chief of the [Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics) from 2004 to 2008. He is also on the editorial board of the [Journal of Combinatorial Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory) Series B and [Combinatorica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorica).[^o6]

He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1979 at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) under the supervision of Derek Alan Holton.[^o7] He wrote a paper with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), so making his [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) equal to 1.[^o8]
