# Shoshana Kamin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shoshana Kamin"}

**Shoshana Kamin** (Шошана Камин, שושנה קמין) (born December 24, 1930),[^o3] born **Susanna L'vovna Kamenomostskaya** (Сусанна Львовна Каменомостская),[^o3][^o4] is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)-born [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working on the theory of [parabolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/7466971) and related [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) problems.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Shoshana Kamin graduated from [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University) in 1953 and earned her "[candidate of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_science)" degree from the same university in 1959,[^o3] under the supervision of [Olga Oleinik](wikipedia://en/3033629).[^o6] She and her two sons left the Soviet Union in the early 1971. After that she became a professor in [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University),[^o7] where she is now [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o8]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In the late 1950s, she gave the first proof of the existence and uniqueness of the [generalized solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_solution) of the three-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [Stefan problem](wikipedia://en/679186).[^o10] Her proof was generalised by Oleinik.[^o11]

Later, she made important contributions to the study of the [porous medium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous_medium) equation,[^o12]

$$\partial_t u = \Delta_x u^m, \,\, m > 1,$$

and to non-linear elliptic equations.[^o13]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Kamenomostskaya, S. L. (1958). "On Stefan Problem". *Nauchnye Doklady Vysshey Shkoly, Fiziko-Matematicheskie Nauki*. 1 (1). p. 60–62. zbl:0143.13901. The earlier account of the research of Shoshana Kamin on the [Stefan problem](wikipedia://en/679186).
- Kamenomostskaya, S. L. (1961). "On Stefan's problem". *Matematicheskii Sbornik*. 53(95) (4). p. 489–514. mr:0141895. zbl:0102.09301. <http://mi.mathnet.ru/eng/msb/v95/i4/p489>. In this paper and in the paper (Oleinik 1960), the first existence and uniqueness proofs for the [generalized solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_solution) of the [three-dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) Stefan problem are given.
- Kamenomostskaya, S. L. (March 1973). "The asymptotic behaviour of the solution of the filtration equation". *Israel Journal of Mathematics*. 14 (1). p. 76–87. doi:10.1007/BF02761536. mr:0315292. zbl:0254.35054.
- Kamin, S. (June 1976). "Similar solutions and the asymptotics of filtration equations". *Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis*. 60 (2). p. 171–183. doi:10.1007/BF00250678. mr:0397202. zbl:0336.76036. According to (Vázquez 2007, 15) this is one of the most important papers in the [asymptotic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_theory) of the [porous medium equation](wikipedia://en/73078000). Also, perhaps for the last time ever, she signed this work with both her present and former surnames, precisely writing "*S. Kamin (Kamenomostskaya)*".
