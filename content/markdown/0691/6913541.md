# Zoltán Pál Dienes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zoltán Pál Dienes"}

**Zoltán Pál Dienes** (; September 11, 1916 – January 11, 2014), anglicized as **Zoltan Paul Dienes**, was a [Hungarian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_mathematician) whose ideas on [education](wikipedia://en/326471) (especially of small children) have been popular in some countries.[^o2] He was a world-famous theorist and tireless practitioner of the "[new mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_mathematics)": an approach to mathematics learning that uses games, songs, and dance to make it more appealing to children.[^o3] He is credited with the creation of [base ten blocks](wikipedia://en/24279751), popularly referred to as *Dienes blocks*.[^o4]

:::

Dienes's life and ideas are described in his [autobiography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autobiography), *Memoirs of a Maverick Mathematician* (), and his book of [mathematical games](wikipedia://en/20434), *I Will Tell You Algebra Stories You've Never Heard Before* ().[^o5] He has also published a book of poetry, *Calls from the Past* ().

His later life contributions have been chronicled by [Bharath Sriraman](wikipedia://en/28051541) in the second monograph of *[The Montana Mathematics Enthusiast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Montana_Mathematics_Enthusiast)*.[^o6]
