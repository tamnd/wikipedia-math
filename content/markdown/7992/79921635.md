# Liner shipping network design and scheduling problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Liner shipping network design and scheduling problem"}

The **liner shipping network design and problem** (**LSNDP**) is a [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) problem in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) that models [maritime transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_transport) logistic problems.[^o2][^o3] It is of practical interest in the shipping industry, as improvements in mathematical techniques can be directly applied to real-world problems.

:::

The problem consist of two subproblems; 1) designing feasible services, and 2) flowing the container flows through the network.[^o3]

An industry-standard benchmark dataset, LINERLIB, is used to measure the effectiveness of mathematical approaches to the LSNDSP.[^o4]
