# Schoch circles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schoch circles"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Schoch circles** are twelve [Archimedean circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_circle) constructed by Thomas Schoch.[^o2]

:::

## History {#History}

In 1979, Thomas Schoch discovered a dozen new Archimedean circles; he sent his discoveries to *[Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American)**s "Mathematical Games" editor [Martin Gardner](wikipedia://en/20435). The manuscript was forwarded to [Leon Bankoff](wikipedia://en/7733725). Bankoff gave a copy of the manuscript to Professor Clayton Dodge of the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine) in 1996. The two were planning to write an article about the Arbelos, in which the Schoch circles would be included; however, Bankoff died the year after.***

In 1998, Peter Y. Woo of [Biola University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biola_University) published Schoch's findings on his website. By generalizing two of Schoch's circles, Woo discovered an infinite family of Archimedean circles named the [Woo circles](wikipedia://en/17794292) in 1999.[^o4]
