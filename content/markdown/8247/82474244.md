# Adequate subcategory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adequate subcategory"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, an **adequate subcategory** of a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) *X* is an analog of a [dense subspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dense_subspace) in topology for [presheaves](wikipedia://en/4840944): namely, a subcategory $i : A \hookrightarrow X$ such that the restriction of the [Yoneda embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoneda_embedding) $X \hookrightarrow \mathbf{P}(X)$ along $i$ is still [fully faithful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fully_faithful).[^o2] The notion was introduced by [Isbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Isbell) in 1960. Note some authors[^o3] use the term **dense subcategory** for this notion, although it can mean a different thing in other contexts.

:::
