# Jonathan Lubin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Lubin"}

**Jonathan Darby Lubin** (born August 10, 1936, in [Staten Island, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staten_Island,_New_York)) is a professor emeritus of mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). He received an A.B. from [Columbia College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_College,_Columbia_University) in 1957 and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1963 under the direction of [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933). He taught at [Bowdoin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowdoin_College) from 1962–1967 and at Brown University from 1967–2000.

:::

He and Tate introduced [Lubin–Tate formal group laws](wikipedia://en/31457610) and used them to construct explicit local [class field theory](wikipedia://en/242695).
