# National Institute of Statistics (Romania)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Institute of Statistics"}

*Image: Institutul Național de Statistică.jpg.* The National Institute of Statistics in Bucharest in 2009

The **National Institute of Statistics** (Institutul Național de Statistică, **INS**) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [government agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Romania) which is responsible for collecting national [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), in fields such as [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography), the [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy), [demographics](wikipedia://en/45642) and society. The institute is also responsible for conducting Romania's [census](wikipedia://en/6889) every ten years, with the latest census being organised in 2022.

:::

## Leadership {#Leadership}

The head of the NIS is currently Tudorel Andrei, while the three vice-presidents are:[^o3]

- Ioan-Silviu Vîrva, in charge of economic and social statistics
- Marian Chivu, in charge of national accounts and the dissemination of statistical information
- Beatrix Gered, in charge of IT activities and statistical infrastructure

## History {#History}

Romania's first official statistics body was the Central Office for Administrative Statistics (*Oficiul Central de Statistică Administrativă*), established on July 12, 1859, under the reign of [Alexandru Ioan Cuza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza). The organisation, one of the first national statistics organisations in Europe, conducted its first public census between 1859 and 1860.[^o5]

The Romanian national statistics organisation was known under various names throughout the country's history, as can be seen in the table below:

::: {#o6 .table cue=table}

| English name | Romanian name | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Central Office for Administrative Statistics | Oficiul Central de Statistică Administrativă | 1859–1892 |
| State Directorate for General Statistics | Direcția de Statistică Generală a Statului | 1892–1925 |
| State Institute for General Statistics | Institutul de Statistica Generală a Statului | 1925–1936 |
| Central Institute of Statistics | Institutul Central de Statistică | 1936–1951 |
| Central Directorate of Statistics | Direcția Centrală de Statistică | 1951–1989 |
| National Commission for Statistics | Comisia Națională pentru Statistică | 1989–1998 |
| National Institute of Statistics and Economic Studies | Institutul Național de Statistică și Studii Economice | 1998–2001 |
| National Institute of Statistics | Institutul Național de Statistică | 2001–present |

:::
