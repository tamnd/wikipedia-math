# Marie-Odette Dubois-Violette

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marie-Odette Dubois-Violette"}

**Marie-Odette Dubois-Violette** (1918-2004) was the first female [Inspector General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_General) of National Education in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

She was born in 1918 in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France),[^o5] and her father was Léon Blaise.[^o6] She married the [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) Pierre-Louis Dubois-Violette.[^o7]

## Career and honors {#Career_and_honors}

From 1942 to 1945, she received a research grant from the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research). From 1946 to 1950, she was a researcher at the CNRS while working on her [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis); she obtained her [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1951.[^o7][^o3]

She was a member of the jury for the mathematics [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in the late 1960s.[^o7]

From 1971 until 1976 she was the first female [Inspector General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_General) of National Education in France.[^o3][^o2]

She retired in 1976.[^o7]

She was named a Chevalier of the [Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honor) in 1977.[^o9]
