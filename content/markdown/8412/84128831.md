# Michele Caputo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michele Caputo"}

**Michele Caputo** (5 May 1927-) is an Italian [geophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysicist), [geodesist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesist), and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

He is known for his formulation of the [Caputo fractional derivative](wikipedia://en/76975056).

## Education {#Education}

Caputo obtained degrees in mathematics and physics from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In his research on [seismological Q-models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_models_%28seismology%29), Caputo introduced a notion of [fractional derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_derivative) which now bears his name. For a nonnegative [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) $m$ and [fractional part](wikipedia://en/501532) $0 < z < 1$, the Caputo fractional derivative of order $m + z$ is defined by

$$\frac{\operatorname{d}^{m+z}}{\operatorname{d}x^{m + z}}\left[ f\left( x \right) \right] = \frac{1}{\operatorname{\Gamma} \left( 1 - z \right)} \int_0^x \frac{f^{\left( m + 1 \right)}\left( t \right)}{\left( x - t \right)^{z}} \, \operatorname{d}t$$

provided that $f$ is [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) on $(0, \infty)$.[^o7]

Caputo conducted [geomagnetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomagnetic), [gravimetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravimetric), and [glaciological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaciological) research during the [1954 Italian Karakoram expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_Italian_Karakoram_expedition).[^o8]
