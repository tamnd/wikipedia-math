# Isabel Fernández (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isabel Fernández"}

[Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernández) **Isabel Fernández Delgado** (born 1979) is a Spanish mathematician specializing in [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474). She is university professor of applied mathematics at the [University of Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Seville), and an editor-in-chief of the *Revista Matemática Iberoamericana*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fernández was born in [Linares, Jaén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linares,_Jaén), in 1979. She received a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in mathematics from the [University of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Granada) in 2002. She completed her Ph.D. there in 2006 with the dissertation *Superficies maximales con singularidades aisladas* supervised by Francisco López Fernández.

After short-term positions at the [University of Murcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Murcia) and the [University of Extremadura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Extremadura), she obtained an assistant professor position at the University of Seville in 2007. In 2010 she was promoted to *profesora titular*, the equivalent of an associate professor. Her current title is *profesora titular de universidad* (university professor).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Fernández was an invited speaker at the 2010 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). The Royal Seville Academy of Sciences and Royal Cavalry Master of Seville gave Fernández their 2012 Young Researcher Prize.
