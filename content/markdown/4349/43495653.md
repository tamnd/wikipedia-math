# Andrew Pullan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew Pullan"}

**Andrew John Pullan** (1963 – 7 March 2012) was a New Zealand mathematician specialising in bio-electrical modelling. He was a fellow of the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After attending [Aorere College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aorere_College) in [Papatoetoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papatoetoe), Pullan received a scholarship to the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland) where he studied mathematics before moving to the engineering school to work on [biomedical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedical_engineering) finite-element models of the heart and models of electrical activity in the gastrointestinal tract. His 1988 doctoral thesis was titled *Quasilinearised infiltration and the boundary element method* with Professor Ian Collins as his supervisor.[^o5] He was appointed head of department from 2008 to 2010. He died in March 2012 of [metastatic melanoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metastatic_melanoma).[^o6]
