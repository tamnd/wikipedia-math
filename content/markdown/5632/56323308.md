# Gabriele Vezzosi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriele Vezzosi"}

*Image: Vezzosi toen.jpg.* Gabriele Vezzosi and Bertrand Toen, Oberwolfach 2002

**Gabriele Vezzosi** is an Italian mathematician, born in Florence, Italy. His main interest is [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Vezzosi earned an MS degree in Physics at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence), under the supervision of [Alexandre M. Vinogradov](wikipedia://en/62909762), and a PhD in Mathematics at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), under the supervision of [Angelo Vistoli](wikipedia://en/74721751). His first papers dealt with [differential calculus](wikipedia://en/50416) over [commutative rings](wikipedia://en/61346), [intersection theory](wikipedia://en/3148602), ([equivariant](wikipedia://en/18887838)) algebraic [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), [motivic homotopy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motivic_homotopy_theory), and existence of [vector bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_bundles) on singular [algebraic surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311).

Around 2001–2002 he started his collaboration with [Bertrand Toën](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Toën). Together, they created [homotopical algebraic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopical_algebraic_geometry) (HAG),[^o2][^o3][^o4] whose more relevant part is [derived algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/40778281) (DAG),[^o5] which is by now a powerful and widespread theory.[^o6][^o7] Slightly later, this theory was reconsidered, and highly expanded by [Jacob Lurie](wikipedia://en/26663863).

More recently, Vezzosi together with [Tony Pantev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Pantev), [Bertrand Toën](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Toën) and [Michel Vaquié](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Vaquié) defined a derived version of symplectic structures[^o8] and studied important properties and examples (an important instance being [Kai Behrend](wikipedia://en/31872403)'s [symmetric obstruction theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_obstruction_theory)); further together with [Damien Calaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Calaque) these authors introduced and studied a derived version of [Poisson](wikipedia://en/882902) and coisotropic structures[^o9] with applications to [deformation quantization](wikipedia://en/4278245).[^o10]

Lately Toën and Vezzosi (partly in collaboration with Anthony Blanc and Marco Robalo) moved to applications of derived and non-commutative geometry to arithmetic geometry, especially to [Spencer Bloch](wikipedia://en/25794633)'s [conductor conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conductor_conjecture).[^o11][^o12][^o13]

Vezzosi also defined a derived version of quadratic forms, and in collaboration with Benjamin Hennion and Mauro Porta, proved a very general formal gluing result along non-linear flags[^o14] with hints of application to a yet conjectural [Geometric Langlands program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_Langlands_program) for varieties of dimension bigger than 1. Together with Benjamin Antieau, Vezzosi proved a [Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg_theorem) (HKR) for varieties of dimension *p* in [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) p.[^o15]

In 2015 he organised the Oberwolfach Seminar on Derived Geometry[^o16] at the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in Germany, and is an organiser of the one-semester thematic program at [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California) in 2019 on [Derived algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/40778281).[^o7]

Vezzosi spent his career so far in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence), [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna) and [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), has had three PhD students (Schürg, Porta and Melani) and is full professor at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) (Italy).
