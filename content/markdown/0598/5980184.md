# Paul Sutcliffe

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Sutcliffe"}

**Paul Michael Sutcliffe**[^o4] is British [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) and mathematician, currently Professor of Theoretical Physics at the [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Durham).[^o5] He specialises in the study of [topological solitons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_soliton).

:::

He serves as the Project Director of the SPOCK (*Scientific Properties of Complex Knots*) research programme dedicated to the study of [knotted](wikipedia://en/366808) structures.[^o6] Related subjects of research include [skyrmions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyrmion).

Sutcliffe was awarded the [LMS](wikipedia://en/591097) [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2006 for contributions to the study of [topological solitons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_soliton) and their dynamics.[^o7]

## Education {#Education}

Sutcliffe graduated from [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) in 1989.[^o4]
