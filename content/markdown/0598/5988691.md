# Overconvergent modular form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Overconvergent modular form"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **overconvergent modular forms** are special [p-adic modular forms](wikipedia://en/37776049) that are elements of certain *p*-adic Banach spaces (usually infinite dimensional) containing classical spaces of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) as subspaces. They were introduced by [Nicholas M. Katz](wikipedia://en/1451427) in 1972.

:::
