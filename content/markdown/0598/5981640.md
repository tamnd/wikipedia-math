# Dodecahedral prism

*Image: Dodecahedral prism net.png.* [Net](wikipedia://en/1148129)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dodecahedral prism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **dodecahedral prism** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). This [4-polytope](wikipedia://en/24979) has 14 [polyhedral](wikipedia://en/23470) cells: 2 [dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/8407) connected by 12 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659). It has 54 faces: 30 squares and 24 pentagons. It has 80 edges and 40 vertices.

:::

It can be constructed by creating two coinciding dodecahedra in 3-space, and translating each copy in opposite perpendicular directions in 4-space until their separation equals their edge length.

It is one of 18 convex [uniform polyhedral prisms](wikipedia://en/1645899#Polyhedral_hyperprisms) created by using uniform [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) to connect pairs of parallel [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847).

## Structure {#Structure}

The dodecahedral prism consists of two dodecahedra connected to each other via 12 [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) prisms. The pentagonal prisms are joined to each other via their square faces.

## Projections {#Projections}

The pentagonal-prism-first orthographic projection of the dodecahedral prism into 3D space has a [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) envelope (see diagram). Two of the pentagonal prisms project to the center of this volume, each surrounded by 5 other pentagonal prisms. They form two sets (each consisting of a central pentagonal prism surrounded by 5 other non-uniform pentagonal prisms) that cover the volume of the decagonal prism twice. The two dodecahedra project onto the decagonal faces of the envelope.

The dodecahedron-first orthographic projection of the dodecahedral prism into 3D space has a dodecahedral envelope. The two dodecahedral cells project onto the entire volume of this envelope, while the 12 pentagonal prism cells project onto its 12 pentagonal faces.
