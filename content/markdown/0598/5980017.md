# Richard S. Ward

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Richard S. Ward"}

**Richard Samuel Ward** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (born 6 September 1951) is a British [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416). He is a Professor of Mathematical & Theoretical Particle Physics at the [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Durham).[^o6]

:::

## Work {#Work}

Ward earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1977, under the supervision of [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193). He is most famous for his extension of Penrose's [twistor theory](wikipedia://en/260836) to nonlinear cases, which he with [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698) used to describe [instantons](wikipedia://en/296077) by vector bundles on the three-dimensional complex [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). He has related interests in the theory of [monopoles](wikipedia://en/2415128), [topological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological) [solitons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solitons) and [skyrmions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyrmions).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Ward was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 1989 for his work in mathematical physics.[^o9] He was elected as a fellow of the Royal Society of London in 2005.[^o10] His certificate of election reads: Richard Ward is distinguished for pioneering and elegant research in mathematical physics. He adapted the [twistor transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twistor_transform) to the [self-dual](wikipedia://en/609737) [Yang-Mills](wikipedia://en/672202) (SDYM) equation, and with Atiyah constructed general multi-instanton solutions. His discovery of the toroidal [BPS two-monopole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BPS_monopole) was a breakthrough in [soliton](wikipedia://en/51654) theory. He showed that virtually all known [integrable equations](wikipedia://en/2573213) arise from SDYM by dimensional and algebraic reductions, allowing a unified solution method. Ward's twistor transform of SDYM, applied to [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), is leading to striking progress in quantum Yang-Mills theory.[^o11]
