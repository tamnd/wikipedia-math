# Ehresmann's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ehresmann's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), or specifically, in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), **Ehresmann's lemma** or **Ehresmann's fibration theorem** states that if a [smooth mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_mapping) $f\colon M \rightarrow N$, where $M$ and $N$ are [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), is

:::

1. a surjective [submersion](wikipedia://en/356413), and
1. a [proper map](wikipedia://en/1190262) (in particular, this condition is always satisfied if *M* is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042)),

then it is a [locally trivial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_trivial) [fibration](wikipedia://en/316826). This is a foundational result in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562) due to [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108), and has many variants.
