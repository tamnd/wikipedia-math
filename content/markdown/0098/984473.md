# Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics"}

The **Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics** (Институт прикладной математики им. М.В.Келдыша, ИПМ) is a research institute specializing in [computational](wikipedia://en/5926) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was established to solve computational tasks related to government programs of [nuclear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power) and [fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_power) energy, [space research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_research) and [missile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile) technology. The Institute is a part of the Department of Mathematical Sciences of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). The main direction of activity of the institute is the use of [computer technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_technology) to solve complex scientific and technical issues of practical importance. Since 2016, the development of mathematical and computational methods for [biological research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_research), as well as a direct solution to the problems of [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) with the use of such methods, has also been included in the circle of scientific activities of the institute.

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

### [Nuclear physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics) {#Nuclear_physics}

Theoretical physicist [Yakov Zeldovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakov_Zeldovich) headed one of the departments placed in charge of the theoretical aspects of the work on the creation of [nuclear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_weapon) and [thermonuclear weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermonuclear_weapon). [Alexander Samarskii](wikipedia://en/56476667) performed the first realistic calculations of macrokinetics of [chain reaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_reaction) of a [nuclear explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_explosion), which led to the practical importance estimated power of nuclear weapons. In relation to nuclear energy, the institute was also involved in modelling of processes of [neutron transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_transport) and [nuclear reactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reaction). In particular, E. Kuznetsov is known for his work on the theory of [nuclear reactors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reactor).

Currently, such work in **KIAM** is continuing in the field of [plasma physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_%28physics%29) and controlled [thermonuclear fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermonuclear_fusion), which began under the leadership of [Sergei Kurdyumov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Kurdyumov), A. A. Samarskii, Yu. P. Popov.

### [Cosmonautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmonautics) {#Cosmonautics}

[Dmitry Okhotsimsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Okhotsimsky) directed the works in the department created for research of the dynamics of [space flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaceflight). In 1966, the department was reorganized into the Ballistic Centre.[^o6] The Ballistic Centre was involved in the calculations of optimal orbit trajectory and actual corrections for all space flights, from uncrewed interplanetary and lunar vehicles to the crewed "[Soyuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_%28spacecraft%29)" and orbital station "[Salyut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salyut_programme)" and "[Mir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mir)". The institute took an active part in the creation of the Soviet space shuttle "[Buran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buran_programme)".

**KIAM** continues to engage in Russian space projects. Current research is connected with:

- development of systems for management and navigation of space vehicles in real time with the use of [global satellite navigation systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_navigation) [GPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System) and [GLONASS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GLONASS);
- exploration of prospects for further interplanetary missions using electric rocket engines;
- participation in such projects as "[RadioAstron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spektr-R)" and [Fobos-Grunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fobos-Grunt).

### [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Mathematical Modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modelling) {#Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Modelling}

One of the greatest mathematicians of the twentieth century [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) was at the head of the Department of heat transmission before his departure for the United States in 1989. He was carrying out the fundamental works on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). [Andrey Nikolayevich Tikhonov](wikipedia://en/616048) worked initially also in these areas of mathematics. However, Tikhonov is better known for his works of more applied orientation, such as methods for solving [ill-posed problems](wikipedia://en/176673) ([Tikhonov regularization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikhonov_regularization)). Tikhonov also created the theory of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) with a small parameter at the highest derivative. The general theory of stability of [difference schemes](wikipedia://en/6054681) was developed by A. A. Samarskii. Samarskii considered mathematical modelling as an independent scientific discipline. Sergei Kurdyumov created a whole scientific school in [nonlinear dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamics) and [synergetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synergetics_%28Haken%29) in Russia.

Currently, the existing arsenal of numerical methods is updated and improved in response to the growing complexity of the models and the possibilities of modern [supercomputers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercomputer). Scientists of KIAM elaborate [grid methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_computing) for solving computational problems, which has led, in particular, to the creation of a declarative language "Norma".[^o8]

### [Computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer) and [Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming) {#Computers_and_Programming}

The first studies were carried out still on [mechanical calculators](wikipedia://en/432897) "Mercedes" by a large staff of estimators. Currently, work is underway to create a distributed computing system based on combining several [supercomputers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercomputer), which are used for grid – technology and developed a specialized [operating system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system) (DVM).

### [Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotics) {#Robotics}

Currently, work on robotics is carried out in:

- the Group of V. E. Pavlovsky of the Sector under the guidance of A. K. Platonov (virtual football[^o11]),
- the Group of S. M. Sokolov ([vision systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision)),
- the Group of V. E. Pryanishnikova (autonomous tracked vehicles).

### [Computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) {#Computational_biology}

Since 2016, the sphere of interest of KIAM includes also computational biology problems, which are solved in [IMPB RAS – Branch of KIAM RAS](wikipedia://en/51149390) in [Pushchino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushchino).

### Teaching and social activities {#Teaching_and_social_activities}

Most of the leading employees of **KIAM** worked part-time as professors of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology). A. N. Tikhonov was the organizer and first Dean of the [Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Computational_Mathematics_and_Cybernetics).

I. M. Gelfand was engaged in the mathematical education of secondary school pupils.

## History {#History}

### Milestones {#Milestones}

The institute is located in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia). In 1978, it is named after [Mstislav Keldysh](wikipedia://en/2769461). The institute was created in 1966 when it split from [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). Already as the Department of Applied Mathematics of Steklov Institute it had conducted some outstanding research in the field of space exploration: in 1953 it developed the method of ballistic [spacecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacecraft) descent, that was used on 12 April 1961 for [Yuri Gagarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Gagarin)'s return to the Earth, and in 1957 [Sputnik 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sputnik_1) orbit was calculated there using the [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer) processing of [optical observation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation) data.[^o17]

As a result of the reorganization in [RAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) of 2015–2016 years[^o18] [Institute of Mathematical Problems of Biology](wikipedia://en/51149390) became a branch of **KIAM**.

- 1978–1989 [Tikhonov, Andrey Nikolayevich](wikipedia://en/616048), Academician of RAS, mathematician.
- 1989–1999 [Kurdyumov, Sergei Pavlovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Kurdyumov), Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences, mathematician.
- 1999–2008 Popov, Yuriy Petrovich, Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences, mathematician.
- 2008–2015 Chetverushkin, Boris Nikolaevich, Academician of RAS, mathematician.
- 2016-2024 Aptekarev, Alexander Ivanovich, Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences, mathematician.
- since 2025 Yakobovskiy, Mikhail Vladimirovich, Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences, mathematician.
