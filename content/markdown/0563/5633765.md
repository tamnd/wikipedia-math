# C. S. Seshadri

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="C. S. Seshadri"}

**Conjeevaram Srirangachari Seshadri** [^o3][FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (29 February 1932 – 17 July 2020), was an Indian mathematician.[^o4] He was the founder and director-emeritus of the [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675), and is known for his work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o5] The [Seshadri constant](wikipedia://en/3086195) is named after him. He was also known for his collaboration with mathematician [M. S. Narasimhan](wikipedia://en/6980576), for their proof of the [Narasimhan–Seshadri theorem](wikipedia://en/35098864) which proved the necessary conditions for [stable vector bundles](wikipedia://en/18909129) on a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181).He also introduced and named the concept called [Standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718).

:::

He was a recipient of the [Padma Bhushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Bhushan) in 2009, the third highest civilian honor in the country.[^o6]

## Degrees and posts {#Degrees_and_posts}

Seshadri was born into a [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu) [Brahmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmin) family in present-day [Kanchipuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanchipuram), [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu).[^o8] He received his B.A. (Hons) degree in mathematics from [Madras University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_University) in 1953, and was mentored by the [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit) priest Fr. Charles Racine and S. Naryanan there.[^o9][^o10] He completed his PhD from [Bombay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_University) in 1958 under the supervision of [K. S. Chandrasekharan](wikipedia://en/22907213). He was elected Fellow of the Indian Academy in 1971.[^o12]

Seshadri worked in the School of Mathematics at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in Bombay from 1953 to 1984, starting as a Research Scholar and rising to a senior professor. From 1984 to 1989, he worked at the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai](wikipedia://en/2718996). From 1989 to 2010, he worked as the founding director of the [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675). After stepping down, he continued to be the institute's Director-Emeritus till his death in 2020. He also served on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) in 2010 and 2011.

## Research work {#Research_work}

Seshadri's main work was in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). His work with [M S Narasimhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M_S_Narasimhan) on unitary vector bundles and the [Narasimhan–Seshadri theorem](wikipedia://en/35098864) has influenced the field. His work on Geometric Invariant Theory and on Schubert varieties, in particular his introduction of [standard monomial theory](wikipedia://en/37358718), is widely recognized.
