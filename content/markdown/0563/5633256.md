# Stability group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stability group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **stability group**[^o2] of a [subnormal series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_series) is the [group of automorphisms](wikipedia://en/56661772) that act as the identity on each [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526).

:::
