# Normal automorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normal automorphism"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the realm of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **normal automorphism** of a group is an [automorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_automorphism) that takes every [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) bijectively to itself.[^o2] As a result, it gives a corresponding automorphism for every [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526).

:::

All [family automorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_automorphism) are normal, and particularly, all [class automorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_automorphism) and [power automorphisms](wikipedia://en/5632274) are. As well, all [inner automorphisms](wikipedia://en/61839) are normal (but not vice versa),[^o2] and more generally, any automorphism defined by an *[algebraic formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_formula)* is normal.
