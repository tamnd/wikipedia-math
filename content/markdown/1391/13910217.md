# Direct image with compact support

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Direct image with compact support"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **direct image with compact (or proper) support** is an [image functor](wikipedia://en/15769518) for [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) that extends the [compactly supported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactly_supported) [global sections functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_sections_functor) to the relative setting. It is one of [Grothendieck's](wikipedia://en/2042) [six operations](wikipedia://en/39560800).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a [continuous mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_mapping) of [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact) [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450), and let $\mathrm{Sh}(-)$ denote the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of sheaves of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974) on a topological space. The **direct image with compact (or proper) [support](wikipedia://en/381013)** is the [functor](wikipedia://en/10987)

$$f_{!}:\mathrm{Sh}(X)\to \mathrm{Sh}(Y)$$

that sends a sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$ to the sheaf $f_{!}(\mathcal{F})$ given by the formula

$$f_{!}(\mathcal{F})(U):=\{s\in\mathcal{F}(f^{-1}(U)) \mid {f\vert}_{\operatorname{supp}(s)}:\operatorname{supp}(s)\to U \text{ is proper}\}$$

for every open subset $U$ of $Y$. Here, the notion of a [proper map](wikipedia://en/1190262) of spaces is unambiguous since the spaces in question are locally compact Hausdorff.[^o4] This defines $f_{!}(\mathcal{F})$ as a subsheaf of the [direct image](wikipedia://en/3022744) sheaf $f_*(\mathcal{F})$ and the functoriality of this construction then follows from basic properties of the support and the definition of sheaves.

The assumption that the spaces be locally compact Hausdorff is imposed in most sources (e.g., Iversen or Kashiwara–Schapira). In slightly greater generality, Olaf Schnürer and [Wolfgang Soergel](wikipedia://en/68408304) have introduced the notion of a "locally proper" map of spaces and shown that the functor of direct image with compact support remains well-behaved when defined for separated and locally proper continuous maps between arbitrary spaces.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- If $f$ is proper, then $f_!$ equals $f_*$.
- If $f$ is an open [embedding](wikipedia://en/52119), then $f_!$ identifies with the extension by zero functor.[^o8]

:::
