# Cutting sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cutting sequence"}

*Image: Fibonacci word cutting sequence.png.* The [Fibonacci word](wikipedia://en/1702139) is an example of a [Sturmian word](wikipedia://en/1701956). The start of the cutting sequence shown here illustrates the start of the word 0100101001.

In [digital geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_geometry), a **cutting sequence** is a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of symbols whose elements correspond to the individual grid lines crossed ("cut") as a [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) crosses a [square grid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_grid).[^o2]

:::

[Sturmian words](wikipedia://en/1701956) are a special case of cutting sequences where the curves are [straight lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_line) of [irrational](wikipedia://en/20647689) slope.[^o3]
