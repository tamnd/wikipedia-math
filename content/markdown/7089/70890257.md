# Zhi-Ming Ma

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zhi-Ming Ma"}

**Zhi-Ming Ma**. () is a Chinese mathematics professor of [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3] Ma is a former Vice Chairman of the Executive Committee for [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868),[^o4] a two times president of [Chinese Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Mathematical_Society), an elected member of [World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) and the Chairman of Graduate Degree Committee of Academy of Math and Systems Science, [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5][^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ma was born in January, 1948 at [Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu), [Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan) while Jiaocheng County in Shanxi Province was his native origin. He obtained his first degree in Mathematics from [Chongqing Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqing_Normal_University) in 1978.[^o3] In 1981, he received his Master's degree from [China Science and Technology University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China), Graduate School of Science [Beijing Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). In 1984 he received his doctorate degree in [Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Ma's contribution in the theory of Dirichlet forms and Markov processes brought an end to a twenty years puzzle in the field. Ma and his team discovered a new framework of quasi-regular Dirichlet forms which correspond to right processes in one-to-one manner. His book, written in collaboration with Michael Rockner, An Introduction to the Theory of (Non-symmetric) Dirichlet Forms, has become a notable text in this field. His work on the proof of the Feynman-Kac probabilistic representation of mixed boundary problems of Schrodinger operators with measure-valued potentials is an important contribution to the theory of the Schrodinger equation.[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

Ma's contributions to science have led to his recognition with various awards including; the First Class Prize for Natural Sciences by the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences), the [Max-Planck Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Research_Group_Optics,_Information_and_Photonics) by the Max-Planck Society and Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, the Chinese National Natural Sciences Prize, the Shiing-Shen Chern Mathematics Prize, the Qiu-Shi Outstanding Young Scholars Prize, the He-Liang-He-Li Sciences and Technology Progress Prize, and the Hua Loo-Keng Mathematics Prize.[^o3]

## Memberships {#Memberships}

Ma is a member of different recognised scientific organisations. He was elected as an Academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1995 and he became a fellow of the [Third World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third-World_Academy_of_sciences) in 1999.[^o15] He was the Chairman of the organizing committee for the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) that was held in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) (2002). He was elected as a member of the Executive Committee for [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) in 2003 and he became the Vice president in 2007. He was elected as a member of [Chinese Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Mathematical_Society) and he became the president in 2003.[^o3][^o15]
