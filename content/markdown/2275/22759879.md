# Nowhere commutative semigroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nowhere commutative semigroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **nowhere commutative semigroup** is a [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) *S* such that, for all *a* and *b* in *S*, if *ab* = *ba* then *a* = *b*.[^o2] A semigroup *S* is nowhere commutative [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) any two elements of *S* are [inverses](wikipedia://en/1757814) of each other.[^o2]

:::

## Characterization of nowhere commutative semigroups {#Characterization_of_nowhere_commutative_semigroups}

Nowhere commutative semigroups can be [characterized](wikipedia://en/780886) in several different ways. If *S* is a semigroup then the following statements are [equivalent](wikipedia://en/169324):[^o4]

- *S* is nowhere commutative.
- *S* is a [rectangular band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangular_band) (in the sense in which the term is used by [John Howie](wikipedia://en/2916430)[^o5]).
- For all *a* and *b* in *S*, *aba* = *a*.
- For all *a*, *b* and *c* in *S*, *a*<sup>2</sup> = *a* and *abc* = *ac*.

Even though, by definition, the rectangular bands are concrete semigroups, they have the defect that their definition is formulated not in terms of the basic [binary operation](wikipedia://en/3948) in the semigroup. The approach via the definition of nowhere commutative semigroups rectifies this defect.[^o4]

To see that a nowhere commutative semigroup is a rectangular band, let *S* be a nowhere commutative semigroup. Using the defining properties of a nowhere commutative semigroup, one can see that for every *a* in *S* the [intersection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_%28mathematics%29) of the [Green classes](wikipedia://en/1192012) *R*<sub>*a*</sub> and *L*<sub>*a*</sub> contains the unique element *a*. Let *S*hair/hair*L* be the family of *L*-classes in *S* and *S*hair/hair*R* be the family of *R*-classes in *S*. The mapping

ψ : *S* → (*S*hair/hair*R*) × (*S*hair/hair*L*)

defined by

*a*ψ = (*R*<sub>*a*</sub>hair, *L*<sub>*a*</sub>)

is a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942). If the [Cartesian product](wikipedia://en/24104095) (*S*hair/hair*R*) × (*S*hair/hair*L*) is made into a semigroup by furnishing it with the rectangular band multiplication, the map ψ becomes an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828). So *S* is isomorphic to a rectangular band.

Other claims of equivalences follow directly from the relevant definitions.
