# List of NP-complete problems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of NP-complete problems"}

This is a list of some of the more commonly known problems that are [NP-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP-complete) when expressed as [decision problems](wikipedia://en/8336). As there are thousands of such problems known, this list is in no way comprehensive. Many problems of this type can be found in (Garey Johnson 1979).

:::

## Graphs and hypergraphs {#Graphs_and_hypergraphs}

[Graphs](wikipedia://en/12401) occur frequently in everyday applications. Examples include biological or social networks, which contain hundreds, thousands and even billions of nodes in some cases (e.g. [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook) or [LinkedIn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn)).

## Other {#Other}

- [Berth allocation problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berth_allocation_problem)[^o146]
- [Betweenness](wikipedia://en/1460717)
- Assembling an optimal [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265) block.[^o147]
- [Boolean satisfiability problem](wikipedia://en/4715) (SAT).[^o59][^o7] There are many variations that are also NP-complete. An important variant is where each clause has exactly three literals (3SAT), since it is used in the proof of many other NP-completeness results.[^o7]
- [Circuit satisfiability problem](wikipedia://en/34599223)
- [Conjunctive Boolean query](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjunctive_Boolean_query)[^o7]
- [Cyclic ordering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_ordering)[^o148]
- [Exact cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_cover) problem. Remains NP-complete for 3-sets. Solvable in polynomial time for 2-sets (this is a [matching](wikipedia://en/581797)).[^o59][^o7]
- Finding the global minimum solution of a [Hartree-Fock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartree-Fock) problem[^o149]
- [Upward planarity](wikipedia://en/338199#Upward_planarity) testing[^o51]
- [Hospitals-and-residents problem with couples](wikipedia://en/22306120#Couples)
- [Knot genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_genus)[^o150]
- [Latin square completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_square_completion) (the problem of determining if a partially filled square can be completed)
- [Maximum 2-satisfiability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_2-satisfiability)[^o7]
- Maximum volume [submatrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submatrix) – Problem of selecting the best conditioned subset of a larger $m\times n$ matrix. This class of problem is associated with Rank revealing [QR factorizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_factorization) and D optimal experimental design.[^o151]
- Minimal [addition chains](wikipedia://en/578656) for sequences.[^o152] The complexity of minimal addition chains for individual numbers is unknown.[^o153]
- [Modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365) [S5](wikipedia://en/12708106)-Satisfiability
- [Pancake sorting](wikipedia://en/478065) distance problem for strings[^o154]
- Solubility of two-variable quadratic polynomials over the integers.[^o155] Given positive integers $\textstyle A,B,C$, decide existence of positive integers $x,y$ such that $Ax^2+By-C=0$
- By the same article[^o155] existence of bounded [modular square roots](wikipedia://en/3667375) with arbitrarily composite modulus. Given positive integers $\textstyle A,B,C \geq 0$, decide existence of an integer $x\in[0,C]$ such that $x^2\equiv A\bmod B$. The problem remains NP-complete even if a [prime factorization](wikipedia://en/23666#Unique_factorization) of $B$ is provided.
- [Serializability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serializability) of database histories[^o7]
- [Set cover](wikipedia://en/870399) (also called "minimum cover" problem). This is equivalent, by transposing the incidence matrix, to the hitting set problem.[^o59][^o7]
- [Set packing](wikipedia://en/2017636)[^o59][^o7]
- [Set splitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_splitting) problem[^o7]
- Scheduling to minimize weighted completion time
- Block Sorting[^o156] (Sorting by Block Moves)
- [Sparse approximation](wikipedia://en/15951862)
- Variations of the [Steiner tree problem](wikipedia://en/498304). Specifically, with the discretized Euclidean metric, rectilinear metric. The problem is known to be NP-hard with the (non-discretized) Euclidean metric.[^o7]
- Three-dimensional [Ising model](wikipedia://en/292744)[^o157]
