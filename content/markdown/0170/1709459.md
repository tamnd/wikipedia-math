# Edward Charles Titchmarsh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Charles Titchmarsh"}

**Edward Charles** "**Ted**" **Titchmarsh** (June 1, 1899 – January 18, 1963) was a leading British mathematician.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Titchmarsh was educated at [King Edward VII School (Sheffield)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Edward_VII_School_%28Sheffield%29) and [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford), where he began his studies in October 1917.

## Career {#Career}

Titchmarsh was known for work in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659) and other parts of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He wrote several classic books in these areas; his book on the [Riemann zeta-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_zeta-function) was reissued in a 1986 edition edited by [Roger Heath-Brown](wikipedia://en/2984550).

Titchmarsh was [Savilian Professor of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) from 1932 to 1963. He was a Plenary Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1954 in Amsterdam.

He was on the governing body of [Abingdon School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abingdon_School) from 1935-1947.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society), 1931[^o3]
- [De Morgan Medal](wikipedia://en/1096532), 1953
- [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal), 1955
- [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prize) winner, 1956

## Publications {#Publications}

- *The Zeta-Function of Riemann* (1930);
- *Introduction to the Theory of Fourier Integrals* (1937)[^o11] 2nd. edition(1939) 2nd. edition (1948);
- *The Theory of Functions* (1932);[^o12]
- *Mathematics for the General Reader* (1948);
- *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function* (1951);[^o13] [2nd edition](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Theory_of_the_Riemann_Zeta_function.html?id=1CyfApMt8JYC), revised by [D. R. Heath-Brown](wikipedia://en/2984550) (1986)
- *Eigenfunction Expansions Associated with Second-order Differential Equations. Part I* (1946)[^o14] 2nd. edition (1962);
- *Eigenfunction Expansions Associated with Second-order Differential Equations. Part II* (1958);[^o15]
