# Rhombic enneacontahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombic enneacontahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rhombic enneacontahedron** (plural: **rhombic enneacontahedra**) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) composed of 90 [rhombic](wikipedia://en/183884) faces; with three, five, or six rhombi meeting at each vertex. It has 60 broad rhombi and 30 slim. The rhombic enneacontahedron is a [zonohedron](wikipedia://en/669402) with a superficial resemblance to the [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025).

:::

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It can also be seen as a nonuniform [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282) with pyramids augmented to the pentagonal and hexagonal faces with heights adjusted until the [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046) are 180 degrees (so the faces become coplanar), and the two pyramid type side edges are equal length. This construction is expressed in the [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) *jtI* with join operator *j*. Without the equal edge constraint, the wide rhombi are [kites](wikipedia://en/84840) if limited only by the [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844).

The sixty broad rhombic faces in the rhombic enneacontahedron are identical to those in the [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631), with diagonals in a ratio of 1 to the [square root of 2](wikipedia://en/341682). The face angles of these rhombi are approximately 70.528° and 109.471°. The thirty slim rhombic faces have face vertex angles of 41.810° and 138.189°; the diagonals are in ratio of 1 to [φ<sup>2</sup>](wikipedia://en/12386).

It is also called a **rhombic enenicontahedron** in [Lloyd Kahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Kahn)'s *Domebook 2*.

:::

## Close-packing density {#Close-packing_density}

The optimal [packing fraction](wikipedia://en/43518103) of rhombic enneacontahedra is given by

$$\eta = 16 - \frac{34}{\sqrt{5}} \approx 0.7947377530014315.$$

It was noticed that this optimal value is obtained in a [Bravais lattice](wikipedia://en/661808) by (txt). Since the rhombic enneacontahedron is contained in a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631) whose [inscribed sphere](wikipedia://en/991786) is identical to its own inscribed sphere, the value of the optimal packing fraction is a corollary of the [Kepler conjecture](wikipedia://en/368311): it can be achieved by putting a rhombicuboctahedron in each cell of the [rhombic dodecahedral honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_dodecahedral_honeycomb), and it cannot be surpassed, since otherwise the optimal packing density of spheres could be surpassed by putting a sphere in each rhombicuboctahedron of the hypothetical packing which surpasses it.
