# Theodor Inama von Sternegg

Theodor Inama von Sternegg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodor Inama von Sternegg"}

**Karl Theodor Ferdinand Michael Inama von Sternegg** (20 January 1843, in [Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg) – 28 November 1908, in [Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innsbruck)) was a German-born Austrian political scientist, statistician, and economist, who served as the second president of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) from 1899 to 1908, and was a member of the [Austrian Imperial Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Council_%28Austria%29), the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences), the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei), the [Prussian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_Academy_of_Sciences), the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities), and the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina). He was the father of painter [Fanny Inama von Sternegg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanny_Inama_von_Sternegg).[^o2][^o3][^o4]
