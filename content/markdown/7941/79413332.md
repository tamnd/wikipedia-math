# Statistics of SailGP

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Statistics of SailGP"}

This article gives the summarized standings of each season of **SailGP**, an international [sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_%28sport%29) competition that features high-performance [F50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F50_%28catamaran%29) [foiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_hydrofoil) [catamarans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catamaran).[^o2]
