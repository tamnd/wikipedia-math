# ∞-Chern–Simons theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="∞-Chern–Simons theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **∞-Chern–Simons theory** (not to be confused with [infinite-dimensional Chern–Simons theory](wikipedia://en/79416300)) is a generalized formulation of [Chern–Simons theory](wikipedia://en/391908) from [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) using the formalism of [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013), which in particular studies [∞-categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-categories). It is obtained by taking [general abstract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_abstract_nonsense) analogs of all involved concepts defined in any cohesive [∞-topos](wikipedia://en/44533341), for example that of smooth [∞-groupoids](wikipedia://en/40965675). [Principal bundles](wikipedia://en/310950) on which [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) act are for example replaced by ∞-principal bundles on with [group objects](wikipedia://en/53507) in ∞-topoi act.[^o2] The theory is named after Shiing-Shen Chern and [James Simons](wikipedia://en/2430844), who first described [Chern–Simons forms](wikipedia://en/294383) in 1974,[^o3] although the generalization was not developed by them.
