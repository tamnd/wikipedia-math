# Toric ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toric ideal"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **toric ideal** is an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) generated by differences of two [monomials](wikipedia://en/357416). An affine or projective [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) defined by a toric [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) or a [homogeneous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_ideal) toric ideal is an affine or projective [toric variety](wikipedia://en/737087).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
