# Simon Sidon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simon Sidon"}

**Simon Sidon** or **Simon Szidon** (1892 in [Versec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vršac), [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary) – 27 April 1941, [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary)) was a reclusive Hungarian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on trigonometric series and orthogonal systems and who introduced [Sidon sequences](wikipedia://en/20823677) and [Sidon sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidon_set).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Death {#Death}

On 27 April 1941, Sidon died from [pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia) in the hospital after a ladder fell on him and broke his leg.[^o5]
