# Trigonometric Series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trigonometric Series"}

[Antoni Zygmund](wikipedia://en/362477) wrote a classic two-volume set of books entitled ***Trigonometric Series***, which discusses many different aspects of [trigonometric series](wikipedia://en/4099125). The first edition was a single volume, published in 1935 (under the slightly different title *Trigonometrical Series*). The second edition of 1959 was greatly expanded, taking up two volumes, though it was later reprinted as a single volume paperback. The third edition of 2002 is similar to the second edition, with the addition of a preface by Robert A. Fefferman on more recent developments, in particular [Carleson's theorem](wikipedia://en/24105681) about [almost everywhere pointwise convergence](wikipedia://en/340198#Almost_everywhere_convergence) for [square-integrable functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square-integrable_functions).

:::
