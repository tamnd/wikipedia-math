# Waraszkiewicz spiral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Waraszkiewicz spiral"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Waraszkiewicz spirals** are subsets of the [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) introduced by (txt). Waraszkiewicz spirals give an example of an uncountable family of pairwise incomparable continua, meaning that there is no [continuous map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_map_%28topology%29) from one onto another.

:::
