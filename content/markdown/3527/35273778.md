# James Alexander Shohat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Alexander Shohat"}

**James Alexander Shohat** ( Jacques Chokhate (or Chokhatte) Яков Александрович Шохат, 18 November 1886, [Brest-Litovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest,_Belarus) – 8 October 1944, [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia)) was a Russian and American mathematician at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) who worked on the [moment problem](wikipedia://en/1244992).[^o2] He studied at the [University of Petrograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Petrograd) and married the physicist [Nadiascha W. Galli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadiashda_Galli-Shohat), the couple emigrating from Russia to the United States in 1923.[^o2]

:::

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 at Toronto.[^o3]
