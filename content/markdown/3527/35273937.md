# Gen'ichirō Sunouchi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gen'ichirō Sunouchi"}

**Gen'ichirō Sunouchi**[^o2] was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038) and analysis.[^o3] He authored over 90 articles written in collaboration with 17 coauthors. The [Sunouchi operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunouchi_operators) are named after him.[^o4]

:::

- Sunouchi, Gen'ichirō, [Strong summability of Walsh-Fourier series](http://projecteuclid.org/euclid.tmj/1178243709). Tōhoku Math. J. (2) 16 1964 228–237.
- Sunouchi, Gen'ichirō, [Notes on Fourier analysis. XVIII. Absolute summability of series with constant terms](http://projecteuclid.org/euclid.tmj/1178245769). Tōhoku Math. J. (2) 1, (1949). 57–65.
- Kaneko, Makoto; Sunouchi, Gen'ichirō, [On the Littlewood-Paley and Marcinkiewicz functions in higher dimensions](http://projecteuclid.org/euclid.tmj/1178228647). Tōhoku Math. J. (2) 37 (1985), no. 3, 343–365.
