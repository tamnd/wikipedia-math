# Karin Reich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karin Reich"}

**Karin Anna Reich** is a German historian of mathematics.

:::

## Career {#Career}

From 1967 to 1973 Reich was a scientific assistant at the Research Institute of the [Deutsches Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsches_Museum) in Munich and the Institute for the History of Mathematics and Natural Sciences at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich), where in 1973, she graduated under supervision of [Helmuth Gericke](wikipedia://en/35272621).[^o4][^o5] In 1980, she completed her time in Munich, publishing *The development of [tensor calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_calculus)*, in 1994 in a revised form as a book.[^o6]

In 1980, she became Professor of the History of Natural Science and Engineering at the [Stuttgart College of Librarianship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart_College_of_Librarianship).[^o6] In 1980/81 and 1981/82 she had a teaching assignment for the History of Mathematics at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). In 1981, she represented the Department of History of Science at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).[^o7] In 1982, she became associate professor and in 1988 Professor for History of Mathematics at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart).[^o4] From 1994 until her retirement, she was a professor at the Institute for the History of Natural Science, Mathematics and Engineering at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), where she succeeded [Christoph J. Scriba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_J._Scriba) as director.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Reich is a corresponding member of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o7]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Reich's publications include biographies of [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125), [Michael Stifel](wikipedia://en/26485293) and [François Viète](wikipedia://en/310238).[^o6] With Gericke, Reich produced an annotated translation of Viète's *Analyticam In artem Isagoge* from 1591.[^o10] She wrote a history of vector-and tensor and differential geometry. With [Kurt Vogel](wikipedia://en/56552055), Gericke and Reich reissued [Johannes Tropfke](wikipedia://en/59764977)'s history of elementary mathematics.[^o11]

Reich's books include:

- *Maß, Zahl und Gewicht: Mathematik als Schlüssel zu Weltverständnis und Weltbeherrschung* \[*Measure, number and weight: Mathematics as key to understanding and mastering the world*\] (with [Menso Folkerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menso_Folkerts) and [Eberhard Knobloch](wikipedia://en/35228357), VCH, Acta Humaniora, Weinheim, 1989)[^o12]
- *Die Entwicklung des Tensorkalküls: Vom absoluten Differentialkalkül zur Relativitätstheorie* \[*The development of tensor calculus: From the absolute differential calculus to relativity theory*\] (Birkhäuser, 1994)[^o13]
- *Im Umfeld der "Theoria motus": Gauß' Briefwechsel mit Perthes, Laplace, Delambre und Legendre* \[*On matters having to do with the "Theoria motus": Gauss' correspondence with Perthes, Laplace, Delambre and Legendre*\] (Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 2001)[^o14]
- *Carl Friedrich Gauß und Russland: Sein Briefwechsel mit in Russland wirkenden Wissenschaftlern* \[*Carl Friedrich Gauss and Russia: His correspondence with scientists working in Russia*\] (with Elena Roussanova, De Gruyter, 2012)[^o15]
- *Carl Friedrich Gauß und Christopher Hansteen: Der Briefwechsel beider Gelehrten im historischen Kontext* \[*Carl Friedrich Gauss and Christopher Hansteen:A correspondence between two scholars in historical context*\] (with Elena Roussanova, De Gruyter, 2015)[^o16]
