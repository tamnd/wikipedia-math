# Zenon Waraszkiewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zenon Waraszkiewicz"}

**Zenon Waraszkiewicz** (1909–1946) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Waraszkiewicz spirals](wikipedia://en/35274769).

:::
