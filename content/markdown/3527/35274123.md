# Zygmunt Zalcwasser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zygmunt Zalcwasser"}

**Zygmunt Zalcwasser** (1898 – 1943) was a Polish mathematician from the [Warsaw School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_School_of_Mathematics) in the period [between the World Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic) collaborating especially in the fields of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649) and [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). Zalcwasser, who worked on the [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038), introduced the [Zalcwasser rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalcwasser_rank) \[Za\] measuring the uniform convergence of sequences of [continuous functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_functions) on the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324).[^o2] Zalcwasser received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University) in 1928. He served as professor at the *[Wolna Wszechnica Polska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolna_Wszechnica_Polska)* in 1933–34 and after the [invasion of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Poland) in 1939 lived in the [Warsaw Ghetto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Ghetto). He was murdered in the gas chambers of the [Treblinka extermination camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treblinka_extermination_camp) in 1943 during [the Holocaust in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust_in_Poland).[^o3]
