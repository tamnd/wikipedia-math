# Marc Zamansky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marc Zamansky"}

**Marc Zamansky** (Genève 14 February 1916 - 6 November 1996) was a French mathematician and a member of the [French Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Resistance).
