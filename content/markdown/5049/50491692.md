# Mitio Nagumo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mitio Nagumo"}

**Mitio (Michio) Nagumo** (南雲 道夫; May 7, 1905 – February 6, 1995) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who specialized in the theory of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). He gave the first [necessary and sufficient condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necessary_and_sufficient_condition) for [positive invariance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_invariant_set) of [closed sets](wikipedia://en/47279) under the flow induced by ordinary differential equations ([Nagumo/Bony-Brezis theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bony-Brezis_theorem)).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Mitio Nagumo graduated from the Department of Mathematics at the [Imperial University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Tokyo) in March 1928. In March 1931 he was appointed Lecturer in the Faculty of Technology at the [Imperial University of Kyushu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyushu_University). In February 1932 he left [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan) for an academic visit to [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where he remained for two years. Upon his return from [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) in March 1934, he was appointed Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at the [Imperial University of Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University),[^o5] and was promoted to Associate Professor in September that year, becoming Professor in the Faculty of Science in March 1936. In March 1937 Nagumo received a [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degree from the [Imperial University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Tokyo). In the 1960s he made a number of academic visits abroad, spending time at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) (where he visited [Kurt Friedrichs](wikipedia://en/3626171) and other old acquaintances from his time at [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen)) and the [Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul](wikipedia://en/889448) in 1960, followed by a visit to the [National Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tsing_Hua_University) in 1963-64. He retired from [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University) in December 1966, after which he was granted the title of Honorary Professor. He was subsequently appointed Professor at [Sophia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_University), from which he retired in March 1976, having reached [mandatory retirement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_retirement) age.[^o6]

Much of Nagumo's original work was published in [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language) and [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language),[^o6] however his research and teaching have influenced numerous mathematicians (especially in [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan)) who have since made many of his results available in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).[^o7]
