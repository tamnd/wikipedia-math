# Annalisa Buffa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annalisa Buffa"}

**Annalisa Buffa** (born 14 February 1973)[^o3] is an Italian mathematician, specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) (PDE). She is a professor of mathematics at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) and holds the Chair of Numerical Modeling and Simulation.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Buffa received her master's degree in computer engineering in 1996 and received her PhD in 2000, with supervisor [Franco Brezzi](wikipedia://en/50489556), from the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan) with thesis *Some numerical and theoretical problems in computational electromagnetism*. From 2001 to 2004 she was a Researcher, from 2004 to 2013 a Research Director (rank equivalent to Professor), and from 2013[^o3] to 2016 she was the Director at the Istituto di matematica applicata e tecnologie informatiche "E. Magenes" (IMATI) of the [CNR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consiglio_Nazionale_delle_Ricerche) in [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia).[^o8]

In 2016 she was promoted to Full Professor of Mathematics. She holds the Chair of Numerical Modeling and Simulation at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPFL).[^o4][^o5]

She has been a visiting scholar at many institutions, including the Laboratoire Jacques-Louis Lions at the University of Paris VI, the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), the [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), and the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) (Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences, ICES).[^o3]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Buffa's research deals with a wide range of topics in PDEs and numerical analysis:[^o10] "isogeometric analysis, fully compatible discretization of PDEs, linear and non linear elasticity, contact mechanics, integral equations on non-smooth manifolds, functional theory for Maxwell equations in non-smooth domains, [finite element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element) techniques for Maxwell equations, non-conforming domain decomposition methods, asymptotic analysis, stabilization techniques for finite element discretizations."[^o11]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2007 Buffa was awarded the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize). In 2015 she was awarded the Collatz Prize "for her spectacular use of deep and sophisticated mathematical concepts to obtain outstanding contributions to the development of computer simulations in science and industry" (Laudatio).[^o13] In 2014, she was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul) with talk *Spline differential forms*. In 2008, she received an ERC Starting Grant and in 2016 an ERC Advanced Grant. She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2016.[^o14]
