# Tim Austin (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tim Austin"}

**Timothy Derek Austin** is a British mathematician known for his work in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), probability, and related areas of [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is currently the [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), a position to which he was appointed in 2023.[^o2]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Austin received his B.A. in mathematics from [Trinity College, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) in 2005. He then completed the Certificate of Advanced Study in Mathematics (Part III of the Mathematical Tripos) at Trinity College in 2006, with distinction. He earned his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2010 under the supervision of [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498). His dissertation was entitled *Multiple recurrence and the structure of probability-preserving systems*.

## Career {#Career}

From 2010 to 2012, Austin was a visiting academic at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) while holding a Clay Research Fellowship (2010–2015). He then joined the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), first as an assistant professor (2012–2014) and later as an associate professor (2015–2017). He moved to [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) as an associate professor in 2017, becoming a full professor in 2019. In the same year he published his proof of a weak [Pinsker conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinsker_conjecture), which had remained unsolved since the 1970s.[^o7] In 2023, he was appointed [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).

He has also held visiting research positions at [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) (Redmond and New England).

## Research {#Research}

Austin works in ergodic theory, harmonic analysis, additive combinatorics, metric geometry, high-dimensional probability, and rigorous statistical mechanics.
