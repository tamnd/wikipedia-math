# Omri Sarig

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Omri Sarig"}

**Omri Moshe Sarig** (Hebrew: עמרי משה שריג) is an Israeli mathematician specializing in ergodic theory and dynamical systems. He is a professor at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), where he holds the Theodore R. and Edlyn Racoosin Professorial Chair in Mathematics.[^o2] Sarig is known for his contributions to thermodynamic formalism, symbolic dynamics, and the use of transfer operators in non-uniformly hyperbolic dynamics.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Sarig earned his Ph.D. in mathematics from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 2001.[^o2] His doctoral dissertation, *Thermodynamic Formalism for Countable Markov Shifts*, was supervised by [Jon Aaronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Aaronson).[^o4]

From 2000 to 2003, he was the Warwick Zeeman Lecturer at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o2] He then joined [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), serving first as an assistant professor (2003–2006) and later as an associate professor (2006–2011).[^o2]

In 2006, Sarig moved to the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), where he became associate professor in 2009 and full professor in 2014.[^o2] He served as Head of the Department of Mathematics from 2014 to 2018.[^o2]

## Research {#Research}

Sarig has developed methods using transfer operators to study countable Markov shifts and symbolic dynamics in smooth ergodic theory. His research also includes results on horocycle flows and multifractal analysis.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601) (2013), "for his work on the thermodynamics of countable Markov shifts and his Markov partition for surface diffeomorphisms with positive topological entropy".[^o7]
- [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) (2013), awarded by the Israel Mathematical Union.[^o8]
- [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) (2006).[^o9]
- Invited Speaker, [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (Hyderabad, 2010) in the section "Dynamical Systems and Ordinary Differential Equations".[^o10][^o11]
