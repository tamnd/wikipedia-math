# Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics"}

***Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published quarterly by the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470). It was established in 2007 and covers all aspects of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), [group actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). The journal is indexed by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH). Its 2009 [MCQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Citation_Quotient) was 0.65, and its 2012 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) is 0.867.
