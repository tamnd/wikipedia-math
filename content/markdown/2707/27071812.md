# Triangle graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Triangle graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **triangle graph** is a [planar](wikipedia://en/24314) [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 3 vertices and 3 edges, in the form of a triangle.

:::

The triangle graph is also known as the [cycle graph](wikipedia://en/878962) $C_3$ and the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) $K_3$.

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The triangle graph has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 1, diameter 1 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3. It is also a 2-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 2-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

Its chromatic polynomial is $(x-2)(x-1)x.$

:::
