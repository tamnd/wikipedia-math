# Mental model theory of reasoning

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mental model theory of reasoning"}

The **mental model theory of reasoning** was developed by [Philip Johnson-Laird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Johnson-Laird) and [Ruth M.J. Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_M.J._Byrne).[^o2] It has been applied to major domains of [deductive inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deductive_inference), including [relational inferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relational_structure) (spatial and temporal reasoning), [propositional inferences](wikipedia://en/18154) (conditional, disjunctive and negation reasoning), quantified inferences ([syllogisms](wikipedia://en/48046)), and meta-deductive inferences.

:::

Subsequent research on [mental models and reasoning](wikipedia://en/994704#Mental_models_and_reasoning) has extended the theory to account for [probabilistic inference](wikipedia://en/49571)[^o3] and [counterfactual thinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterfactual_thinking).[^o4]
