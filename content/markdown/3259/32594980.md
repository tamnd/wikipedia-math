# Takahiro Shiota

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takahiro Shiota"}

**Takahiro Shiota** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University). In 1986, he proved [Novikov's](wikipedia://en/628515) conjecture about the [Riemann–Schottky problem](wikipedia://en/4125909) by characterization of [Jacobian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_varieties).[^o3]

:::

Shiota obtained his doctorate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1984.[^o4]
