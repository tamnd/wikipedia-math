# Semyon Alesker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semyon Alesker"}

**Semyon Alesker** (סמיון אלסקר; born 1972) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). For his contributions in [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [integral geometry](wikipedia://en/349336), in particular his work on [valuations](wikipedia://en/63011965), he won the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 2000,[^o3] and the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) in 2004.

:::
