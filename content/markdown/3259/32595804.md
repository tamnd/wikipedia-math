# Raphaël Cerf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raphaël Cerf"}

**Raphaël Cerf** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University) (previously [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University)). For his contributions to [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), he won the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1999, and the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 2000.[^o4][^o5] He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006 in Madrid.[^o6]

:::
