# Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications"}

The ***Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications*** is an [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) in mathematics, specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and related topics in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969). It was founded in 1960 by [Richard Bellman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Bellman),[^o3] as part of a series of new journals on areas of mathematics published by [Academic Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Press),[^o4] and is now published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier). For most years since 2003 it has been ranked by [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank) as among the top 25% of journals in its topic areas.[^o5]
