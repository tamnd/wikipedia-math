# Separation test

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Separation test"}

A **separation test** is a [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685) procedure for early-phase research, to decide whether to pursue further research. It is designed to avoid the prevalent situation in early-phase research, when a statistically [underpowered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_power) test gives a negative result.

:::
