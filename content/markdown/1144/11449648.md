# Gilbert Baumslag

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gilbert Baumslag"}

**Gilbert Baumslag** (April 30, 1933[^o2] – October 20, 2014[^o3]) was a [Distinguished Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Professor) at the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), with joint appointments in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering). He was director of the [Center for Algorithms and Interactive Scientific Software](http://www.ccny.cuny.edu/caiss/index.cfm), which grew out of the MAGNUS [computational group theory](wikipedia://en/618584) project he also headed. Baumslag was also the organizer of the New York Group Theory Seminar.

:::

Baumslag graduated from the [University of the Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Witwatersrand) in [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa) with a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) Honours (Masters) and [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science)[^o4] He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 1958; his thesis, written under the direction of [Bernhard Neumann](wikipedia://en/5032588), was titled *Some aspects of groups with unique roots*. His contributions include the [Baumslag–Solitar groups](wikipedia://en/11485892) and [parafree groups](wikipedia://en/5780961).

Baumslag was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1968–69.[^o6] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
