# Opinion polling for the 2024 Indian general election

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opinion polling for the 2024 Indian general election"}

Media outlets known for their [sycophanchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politico-media_complex) [of the BJP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godi_media) were accused of exaggerating the scale of BJP's victory in their exit polls in an [attempt to demoralise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_warfare) the Opposition campaign.[^o940][^o941][^o942] Talking in a show of [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC) Hindi, political strategist [Yogendra Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogendra_Yadav) described it as [crowd manipulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowd_manipulation) orchestrated [by the media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_manipulation) and stated that had these media outlets been honest about the situation on ground, the BJP wouldn't have won even 200 seats (read [Tinkerbell effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinkerbell_effect)).[^o943]
