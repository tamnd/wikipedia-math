# Joan Leitzel

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Joan Leitzel"}

**Joan Ruth Leitzel** (née **Phillips**; July 2, 1936 – March 5, 2026) was an American mathematician and university administrator. She served as interim [Chancellor of the University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_of_the_University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) for six months before becoming the president of the [University of New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Hampshire). She served in that role from 1996 to 2002[^o4] and received the Charles Holmes Pettee Medal in 2002 for her contributions to the University of New Hampshire.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Leitzel graduated from [Valparaiso High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valparaiso_High_School). She then attended [Hanover College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_College) (BA 1958), [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) (AM 1961), and [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) (PhD 1965) in algebra under the supervision of George William Whaples.[^o6]

She was the commencement speaker at [Hanover College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_College) and received an honorary doctorate degree from her undergraduate alma mater in May 2024.[^o8]

## Death {#Death}

Joan Leitzel died on March 5, 2026, at the age of 89. A widow, she was survived by two sons, two siblings, four grandchildren, one great-granddaughter, and extended family.[^o10]
