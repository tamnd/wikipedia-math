# Ștefan Emilian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ștefan Emilian"}

*Image: Misu Popp - Stefan Emilian.jpg.* Ștefan Emilian (portrait by [Mișu Popp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mișu_Popp))

**Ștefan Emilian** (August 8, 1819 – November 1899) was an [Imperial Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Austria)-born [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician and architect.

:::

Born in Bonchida, [Kolozs County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolozs_County) (now [Bonțida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonțida), [Cluj County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj_County)), in the [Principality of Transylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Transylvania_%281711–1867%29), he was given the surname *Kertész* as a child, although his birth name was *Emilian*. He attended high school in [Sibiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibiu). Then, from 1841 to 1845, he studied at the [Academy of Fine Arts Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Fine_Arts_Vienna), graduating with an architect's degree. Additionally, from 1841 to 1843, he took courses at the [Vienna Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien). Emilian returned home shortly before 1848, in time for the [Transylvanian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transylvanian_Revolution_of_1848). Pursued by the authorities, he sought refuge in [Wallachia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallachia). By 1850, he was back in Transylvania, where he taught mathematics at [Brașov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brașov)'s [Greek Orthodox High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Șaguna_National_College_%28Brașov%29). He remained there until 1858, a period during which he designed the new school building. Additionally, he was the architect for the first paper factory in [Zărnești](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zărnești).[^o2]

In 1858, he was invited to [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), the capital of [Moldavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldavia), in order to teach drawing and geometry to the upper classes of *[Academia Mihăileană](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Mihăileană)*. Emilian remained there for two years, until the founding of the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University). Additionally, he taught at the military officers' school and the technical school of arts and professions. At the new university, he was named full professor of [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) and linear perspective, remaining from October 1860 to October 1892, when he had to retire. Meanwhile, he designed the Iași anatomy institute, the [Lipovan Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipovan_Church,_Iași), and the church in [Bosia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ungheni,_Iași). A single published book of his is known: the 1886 *Curs practic de perspectivă liniară*. Emilian's funeral eulogy was delivered by [Alexandru Dimitrie Xenopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Dimitrie_Xenopol).[^o2]

He married [Cornelia Ederlly de Medve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornelia_Emilian).[^o3]
