# M. J. Bayarri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. J. Bayarri"}

**María Jesús (Susie) Bayarri García** (September 16, 1956 – August 19, 2014) was a Spanish [Bayesian statistician](wikipedia://en/404412) who served as president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) (1998) and of the Sociedad Española de Biometría (2001–2003).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After earning a bachelor's degree in 1976, a master's degree in 1979, and a doctorate in 1984 (in mathematics) from the [University of Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valencia), Bayarri remained at the university as a faculty member for the rest of her career. After the death of her husband in 1984, she became a Fulbright scholar, and frequently visited the US, becoming an adjunct professor at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

She was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 1997, of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2008, and in the inaugural class of fellows of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2014. Her publications won the 2005 [Frank Wilcoxon Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Wilcoxon_Prize), and the Jack Youden Prize in 2008.
