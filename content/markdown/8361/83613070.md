# Princeton University Mathematics Competition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Princeton University Mathematics Competition"}

The **Princeton University Mathematics Competition**, also known as **PUMaC**, is an annual [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school) [mathematics competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_olympiad) started in 2006.[^o2][^o3][^o4] The competition typically takes place in [January](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January) each year, organized entirely by the students of the [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) Math Club with hundreds of students participating from across the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).[^o5]

:::

For 19 years, the competition was only held at Princeton University, attracting high school students and high school math teams from all across the country who prepared for PUMaC and other similar competitions throughout the year.[^o6][^o7] In 2026, PUMaC organizers partnered with Beijing's ASEEDER to hold the competition in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) for the first time.[^o8][^o9] [Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya)'s Learning Sprouts organization will also partner with Princeton to hold the first [African](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa) PUMaC in [Nairobi, Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairobi) in 2027.[^o4][^o10][^o11]

## Tournament format {#Tournament_format}

The competition has two divisions: Division A and Division B. Division A is intended for teams with more competition experience while Division B is designed for teams that are more new to competition.[^o13][^o14] Both divisions are the same structurally.

Teams and individuals both are able to apply to participate within the competition. A full team consists of 8 individuals.[^o14]

The table below shows the layout for the tournament.[^o15][^o6]

::: {#o16 .table cue=table}

| Round | Format |
|---|---|
| Individual Round | Each individual round participant will take two Individual Tests out of a selection of 4 categories: [Algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and [Number Theory](wikipedia://en/21527). Each individual test is 50 minutes. |
| Individual Final | The top 10 students in each division qualify for the Individual Final, a series of 3 difficult [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) questions. The contestants are given 90 minutes for the 3 problems. |
| Team Round | Teams work together to solve 15 problems with 1 bonus question. Teams are given a varying amount of time yearly, typically ranging between 30 and 60 minutes. |
| Power Round | Teams work together on a variety of proof-style theorems and conjectures. Problems are usually released a week beforehand and teams are allotted that time to work. Proofs are due the date of the competition. |

:::

## Expansion {#Expansion}

### Beijing {#Beijing}

PUMaC partnered with China's ASEEDER competition organization to hold China's first PUMaC on January 10-11, 2026.[^o9] The scores for the Chinese competition were held separate from the scores within the US.[^o8]

### Kenya {#Kenya}

Through a partnership with Kenya's Learning Sprouts, an African education-focused organization, PUMaC is set to organize its first competition in Nairobi for 2027, accessible to students across the entire continent.[^o4][^o10][^o11]
