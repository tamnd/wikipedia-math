# MSN (association football)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MSN"}

*Image: Barcelona fans - Champions league 2015 Berlin.JPG.* Barcelona fans holding placards of the *MSN* trio ahead of the [2015 UEFA Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_UEFA_Champions_League_final) in Berlin, Germany. In order: Suarez, Neymar, Messi.

***MSN*** (an [acronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym) for *Messi*, *Suárez* and *Neymar*) was the attacking trio of [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) players [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi), [Luis Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Suárez_%28Uruguayan_footballer%29), and [Neymar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neymar), who played together for the Spanish club [FC Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona) for three seasons during 2014–2017[^o2] following Suárez's arrival from [Liverpool F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) in the summer of 2014.[^o3] During their three seasons together, they scored 364 goals and provided 174 assists[^o4] while helping Barcelona win nine trophies,[^o5] including the [continental treble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_treble) in [2014–15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014–15_FC_Barcelona_season).[^o5]

:::

With Messi in the right wing position, Suárez in the middle, and Neymar in the left wing position, the trio were able to create a "symbiotic" relationship[^o6] in which Suárez was a relentless runner, Messi was at the centre of orchestration and team play,[^o7] and Neymar was an explosive and creative threat.[^o8] In 2015, French sports newspaper *[L'Équipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Équipe)* ranked Messi, Neymar and Suárez as the three best players in the world in a ranking of 100 players, placing them first, second and third, respectively.[^o9]

The success and popularity of *MSN* also popularised the use of acronyms to describe other attacking trios in Europe. They were frequently compared with their [rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Clásico) Spanish club [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF)'s "***BBC***" ([Gareth Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Bale), [Karim Benzema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Benzema) and [Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)).[^o10]

## Background {#Background}

*Image: Messi with Neymar Junior the Future of Brazil.jpg.* [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi) and [Neymar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neymar) after the [2011 FIFA Club World Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_FIFA_Club_World_Cup_final), before becoming future teammates at [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona).

Prior to the formation of *MSN*, Messi had achieved unprecedented feats in football, which included winning four [Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d'Or) awards consecutively from 2009 to 2012.[^o13] Neymar joined Barcelona from his boyhood Brazilian club [Santos FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santos_FC) in 2013 after emerging as one of the most sought-after young players in the world.[^o14] During his debut season at Barcelona, he failed to replicate the form he had at Santos.[^o15] Suárez signed from Liverpool in July 2014 for a reported fee of around €81 million[^o16] after scoring 31 goals in the [2013–14 Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013–14_Premier_League) season.[^o17] He was regarded as the best striker in the [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League).[^o18]

The trio first started together on 25 October 2014 in the first *[El Clásico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Clásico)* of the [2014–15 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014–15_FC_Barcelona_season) against Real Madrid at the [Bernabéu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernabéu_%28stadium%29) stadium, which was the debut of Suárez (he was ineligible to make his debut earlier owing to a [FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA) suspension),[^o19] though Barcelona lost 3–1.[^o20]

By February 2015, the media had noted a shift in Barcelona's style of play under new manager [Luis Enrique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Enrique), particularly through his use of the attacking trio of Messi, Suárez and Neymar.[^o21] While Barcelona previously played under the traditional [tiki-taka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiki-taka) philosophy, the team had now adopted a more direct approach, relying mostly on the trio's individual quality in attack.[^o21] Suárez's dynamic movement would create space for Messi and Neymar "to cut inside from the wings and wreak havoc."[^o21]

## Impact {#Impact}

After a disappointing [2013–14 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013–14_FC_Barcelona_season) for Barcelona,[^o23] considered to be "their weakest since 2008",[^o24] the emergence of *MSN* restored Barcelona into one of the most feared teams in the world again.[^o25][^o26][^o27] By the end of 2014–15, *MSN* had proved to be pivotal to Barcelona completing the continental treble,[^o28] scoring a combined 122 goals, which was the highest ever by an attacking trio in a single season at the time, a record they surpassed the following year with 131 goals.[^o29] Between 2014 and 2017, they scored 364 goals and provided 174 assists,[^o30] helping the club win nine trophies, including two [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) titles,[^o31] three [Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_del_Rey) titles[^o32] and the [2014–15 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014–15_UEFA_Champions_League) as part of the treble.[^o33]

*MSN* became synonymous with Barcelona's identity during their tenure together and were instrumental in many Barcelona victories,[^o31][^o34][^o35] including the [6–1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona_6–1_Paris_Saint-Germain_FC) second leg victory against [Paris Saint-Germain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Saint-Germain_FC) (PSG) in the [2016–17 Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016–17_UEFA_Champions_League) round of 16,[^o36] considered one of the greatest comebacks in football history.[^o37][^o38][^o39] Prior to the match, Barcelona's defender [Lucas Digne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Digne) backed *MSN* to help the team overcome the first leg loss,[^o40] whilst PSG's [Julian Draxler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Draxler) highlighted the threat of *MSN*, saying "they can do everything".[^o41] In 2017, a writer for *[Bleacher Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleacher_Report)* said, "Barcelona's front three of Lionel Messi, Luis Suárez and Neymar strike fear into opposition defences every time they take to the pitch, and everybody knows just how pivotal they are to the team's success."[^o42] Following the Champions League fixture between Barcelona and English club [Arsenal F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C.) in 2016, their manager [Arsène Wenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsène_Wenger) said: "We played against a team with the three best strikers I've ever seen \[...\] They can create a chance out of nothing."[^o43]

The chemistry and unselfish play of *MSN* led some to consider them as the greatest attacking trio football has ever seen.[^o8][^o44][^o45] In 2016, Barcelona captain [Andrés Iniesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrés_Iniesta), who had previously played alongside the formidable attacking trio of Lionel Messi, [Samuel Eto'o](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Eto'o) and [Thierry Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thierry_Henry) that helped Barcelona win the continental treble in [2008–09](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008–09_FC_Barcelona_season), described *MSN* as the superior partnership, calling Messi, Suárez and Neymar "the three best players I've ever seen".[^o46] *MSN*'s willingness to share goalscoring opportunities drew praise.[^o47] [Gerard Piqué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Piqué), who played with *MSN* at Barcelona, drew attention to their selflessness and lack of ego considering that Messi was previously the "main star" at Barcelona, a position that Suárez was also used to when he was at Liverpool.[^o48]

Following Neymar's departure to PSG in 2017,[^o49] Barcelona struggled to emulate the level of attacking threat that *MSN* produced.[^o50][^o51][^o52]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

*MSN* continues to be celebrated and remembered highly by media outlets.[^o54][^o55][^o56] In 2025, [*Goal*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_%28website%29) ranked *MSN* as the greatest attacking trio of the 21st century.[^o57] Besides the on-field quality of *MSN*, the personal long-lasting friendship between the three players has also been publicised.[^o58][^o59][^o60] The trio have frequently been the subject of speculation over a reunion at professional level, particularly at American club [Inter Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Miami_CF), where Messi and Suárez currently play.[^o61]

The acronym itself, *MSN*, inspired similar labels for other attacking trios, most notably Real Madrid's *BBC* ([Gareth Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Bale), [Karim Benzema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Benzema) and [Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)) and PSG's *MNM* (Messi, Neymar and [Kylian Mbappé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylian_Mbappé)).[^o62][^o63][^o64] *MSN* often remains a benchmark in discussions about attacking trios in football.[^o65] In 2024–25, Barcelona's attacking threat of [Raphinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphinha), [Robert Lewandowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lewandowski) and [Lamine Yamal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamine_Yamal) drew comparisons to *MSN*.[^o66][^o67][^o68] In 2026, German club [FC Bayern Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bayern_Munich)'s attacking trio of [Luis Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Díaz_%28footballer,_born_1997%29), [Harry Kane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Kane) and [Michael Olise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Olise) was also compared to *MSN*.[^o69]
