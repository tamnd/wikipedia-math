# Jing (Maggie) Chen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jing (Maggie) Chen"}

**Jing** (**Maggie**) **Chen** is a Chinese mathematician specialising in [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), including the mathematical modelling of [liquidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquidity) and its relation to financial policy, [Hawkes processes](wikipedia://en/61323423), and the applications of [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) to [financial technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_technology). She is responsible for bringing the financial applications of Hawkes processes to the attention of Alan Hawkes, and together they published foundational work on the subject. She works in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), where she holds a personal chair as a professor of mathematics at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chen studied computer science at [Lanzhou Jiaotong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanzhou_Jiaotong_University) in China, and began working in the computer software industry. She continued her studies in the UK at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen), receiving a master's degree in finance and investment management and then a doctorate in finance in 2011.

She became a lecturer in the [Swansea University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University) School of Management, and was promoted to senior lecturer there before moving to Cardiff University in 2015 as a senior lecturer.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Chen was elected as a [Fellow of the Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Learned_Society_of_Wales) in 2025.
