# François Loeser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Loeser"}

**François Loeser** (born August 25, 1958) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is Professor of Mathematics at the [Pierre-and-Marie-Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-and-Marie-Curie_University) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). From 2000 to 2010 he was Professor at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). Since 2015, he is a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).

:::

He was awarded the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 2011 and the Charles-Louis de Saulces de Freycinet Prize of the French Academy of Sciences in 2007. He was awarded an ERC Advanced Investigator Grant in 2010 and has been a Plenary Speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Amsterdam in 2008. In 2014 Loeser was an [Invited Speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul). In 2015 he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to algebraic and arithmetic geometry and to model theory".[^o3] He was elected member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2019. In 2025-26, he was awarded the Jacques Tits Chair founded by the Belgian Mathematical Society.

He is a specialist of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and is best known for his work on [motivic integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motivic_Integration), part of it in collaboration with [Jan Denef](wikipedia://en/6837784).
