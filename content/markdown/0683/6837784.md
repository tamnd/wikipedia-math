# Jan Denef

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Denef"}

*Image: Jan Denef.jpg.* Jan Denef

**Jan Denef** (born 4 September 1951) is a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is an Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at the [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven) (KU Leuven).[^o2]

:::

Denef obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from KU Leuven in 1975 with a thesis on [Hilbert's tenth problem](wikipedia://en/101851); his advisors were Louis Philippe Bouckaert and Willem Kuijk.

He is a specialist of [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is well known for his early work on Hilbert's tenth problem and for developing the theory of [motivic integration](wikipedia://en/6838270) in a series of papers with [François Loeser](wikipedia://en/6838345). He has also worked on [computational number theory](wikipedia://en/511466).

Recently he proved a conjecture of [Jean-Louis Colliot-Thélène](wikipedia://en/15256996) which generalizes the [Ax–Kochen theorem](wikipedia://en/5911859).

In 2002 Denef was an [Invited Speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). His [Hirsch-index](wikipedia://en/3604693) is 24.
