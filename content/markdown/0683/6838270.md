# Motivic integration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Motivic integration"}

**Motivic integration** is a notion in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) that was introduced by [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509) in 1995 and was developed by [Jan Denef](wikipedia://en/6837784) and [François Loeser](wikipedia://en/6838345). Since its introduction it has proved to be very useful in various branches of algebraic geometry, most notably [birational geometry](wikipedia://en/382733) and [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). Roughly speaking, motivic integration assigns to subsets of the arc space of an algebraic variety, a volume living in the [Grothendieck ring](wikipedia://en/1634790) of [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808). The naming 'motivic' mirrors the fact that unlike ordinary [integration](wikipedia://en/15532), for which the values are [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers), in motivic integration the values are geometric in nature.

:::
