# Veera Baladandayuthapani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Veera Baladandayuthapani"}

**Veera Baladandayuthapani** is an Indian and American statistician, currently serving as Jeremy M.G. Taylor Collegiate Professor and Chair of the Department of Biostatistics at the [University of Michigan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan_School_of_Public_Health). He is widely recognized for his pioneering research in biostatistics, cancer data science, and the development of Bayesian methods for high-dimensional biomedical data analysis.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Baladandayuthapani was born in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India). He studied at the [Indian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology) (IIT), Kharagpur, earning a BSc in Mathematics and Computing. He received a MA in Statistics from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 2000 and a PhD in Statistics from [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) in 2005.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

## Career {#Career}

After his PhD, Baladandayuthapani joined the [University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_MD_Anderson_Cancer_Center) as a Professor and Institute Faculty Scholar, working there for 13 years. He held adjunct appointments at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), and [UT School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UT_School_of_Public_Health), contributing to oncology and genomics modeling.[^o3][^o5][^o6]

In 2018, he joined the [University of Michigan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan_School_of_Public_Health), and has served as Professor, Associate Director of Quantitative Data Sciences, and Director of the Cancer Data Science Shared Resource at the [Rogel Cancer Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogel_Cancer_Center). As of July 1, 2024, he was named Chair of the Department of Biostatistics.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Baladandayuthapani is a leader in the application of Bayesian probabilistic models and machine learning to high-dimensional biological data such as genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics. His work also extends to neuro- and cancer-imaging.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

He has co-authored over 150 papers[^o10] in top statistical, machine learning, bioinformatics, biomedical, and oncology journals, and a book on Bayesian analysis of gene expression data.[^o6] His group has developed multiple software tools for integrative genomics and spatial biology.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Baladandayuthapani lives in [Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan), with his wife Upali Nanda, Professor of Practice of Architecture at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and EVP, Global Sector Director of Innovation at HKS, Inc. He has two sons, Aarith and Aayush.[^o4]
