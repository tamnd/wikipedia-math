# Alexander Scott (British academic)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Scott"}

**Alex Scott** (born 1967) is a British mathematician, currently Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and Fellow and Dominic Welsh Tutor in Mathematics at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford).[^o4]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

As a sixteen year old schoolboy, Scott wrote the [role-playing game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role-playing_game) [Maelstrom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maelstrom_%28role-playing_game%29) for [Puffin Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puffin_Books).[^o6] He took his first degree at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), gaining [first class honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_undergraduate_degree_classification#First-class_honours).

## Career {#Career}

From 1993 to 1997, Scott was a Junior Research Fellow at Trinity College, Cambridge, where he graduated [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_philosophy) in 1995.[^o9] His dissertation, titled "Unavoidable Induced Subgraphs", was supervised by [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956).[^o8] From 1996 to 2001 he was lecturer in mathematics at [University College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_London), then Reader there from 2001 to 2005. In 1997 he was a [Visiting Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_Professor) at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis). In 2005 he was elected as a fellow and appointed as Dominic Welsh Tutor in Mathematics at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford), then in 2006 became Professor of Mathematics in the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). [^o9][^o4]
