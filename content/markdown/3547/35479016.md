# Wiman's sextic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wiman's sextic"}

In mathematics, **Wiman's sextic** is a degree 6 [plane curve](wikipedia://en/971691) with four [nodes](wikipedia://en/981915) studied by (txt). It is given by the equation (in [homogeneous coordinates](wikipedia://en/243316))

:::

$$x^6+y^6+z^6 + (x^2+y^2+z^2)(x^4+y^4+z^4)=12 x^2y^2z^2$$

Its [normalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalization_of_an_algebraic_variety) is a [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_of_an_algebraic_curve) 6 curve with [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) isomorphic to the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) *S*<sub>5</sub>.
