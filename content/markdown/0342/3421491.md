# Anton Davidoglu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anton Davidoglu"}

*Image: Davidoglu.jpg.* [Postage stamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postage_stamp) featuring Davidoglu

**Anton Davidoglu** (June 30, 1876 – May 27, 1958) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialized in [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).

:::

He was born in 1876 in [Bârlad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bârlad), [Vaslui County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaslui_County), the son of Profira Moțoc and Doctor Cleante Davidoglu. His older brother was General [Cleante Davidoglu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleante_Davidoglu).

He studied under [Jacques Hadamard](wikipedia://en/312675) at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), defending his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) dissertation in 1900. His thesis — the first mathematical investigation of deformable solids — applied [Émile Picard](wikipedia://en/42000)'s [method of successive approximations](wikipedia://en/666177) to the study of fourth order differential equations that model traverse vibrations of non-homogeneous elastic bars.[^o2]

After returning to Romania, Davidoglu became a professor at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). In 1913, he was founding [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [Academy of High Commercial and Industrial Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest_University_of_Economic_Studies) in Bucharest. He also continued to teach at the University of Bucharest, until his retirement in 1941.[^o3]

Davidoglu was a founding member of the [Romanian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o4] and was featured on a 1976 Romanian [postage stamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postage_stamp).[^o5] He died in 1958 in Bucharest.
