# Charles Pisot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Pisot"}

**Charles Pisot** (2 March 1910 – 7 March 1984) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is chiefly recognized as one of the primary investigators of the numerical set associated with his name, the [Pisot–Vijayaraghavan numbers](wikipedia://en/519303).

:::

He followed the classical path of great French mathematicians by studying at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) on Ulm street, where he was received first at the agrégation in 1932. He then began his academic career at the [Bordeaux University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordeaux_University) before being offered a chair at the Science Faculty of Paris and at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). He was a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o3]

Also of interest is the recently solved Pisot conjecture on [rational functions](wikipedia://en/361210). (For a technical account and bibliography see [Umberto Zannier](wikipedia://en/58056511)'s paper in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*.)[^o4] He was also the coauthor (with Marc Zamansky) of a textbook in general mathematics (titled *Mathématiques générales*) which was very popular in the 1960s and 1970s.
