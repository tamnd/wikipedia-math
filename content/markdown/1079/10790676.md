# Modulated complex lapped transform

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modulated complex lapped transform"}

The **modulated complex lapped transform (MCLT)** is a [lapped transform](wikipedia://en/39747050), similar to the [modified discrete cosine transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_discrete_cosine_transform), that explicitly represents the phase (complex values) of the signal.

:::
