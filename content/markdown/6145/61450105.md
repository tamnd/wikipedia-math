# María del Pilar Díaz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="María del Pilar Díaz"}

**María del Pilar Díaz** is an Argentine statistician. She is a professor of statistics in the Faculty of Economics at the [National University of Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Córdoba) (UNC).[^o3] Pilar Díaz was an instructor in the Department of Statistics and Biostatistics in the School of Nutrition in the Facultad de Ciencias Médicas (Universidad Nacional de Córdoba) at UNC in 1983.[^o4][^o5]

:::

Pilar Díaz completed a bachelor's degree in mathematics at UNC. She earned a master's degree (in 1992) and doctorate in statistics (in 1996) at the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo).[^o3][^o4]
