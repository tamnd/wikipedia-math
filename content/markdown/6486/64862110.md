# Ivan Losev (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Losev"}

**Ivan Vadzimavich Loseu** (publishing under the name *Ivan Losev*; [Belarusian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_language) Іван Вадзімавіч Лосеў, [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) Иван Вадимович Лосев, born 18 October 1981 in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk), [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus)) is a Belarusian-American mathematician, specializing in representation theory, symplectic geometry, algebraic geometry, and combinatorial algebra.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Losev matriculated in 1999 at [Belarusian State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_State_University), where he graduated in 2004 with an M.Sc. from the Department of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science. From 2004 to 2007 he was a graduate student in the Department of Mechanics and Mathematics, Moscow State University.[^o3] There in 2007 he received his Ph.D. ([Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)) with thesis *Classification of some coisotropic actions of algebraic groups* and advisor [E. B. Vinberg](wikipedia://en/19177826). As a postdoc Losev was from 2007 to 2008 an engineer-programmer at Belarusian State University and from 2008 to 2011 a [C.L.E. Moore Instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_Instructor) at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). At [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University) he was from 2011 to 2015 an associate professor and from 2015 to 2018 a full professor. He was a full professor from 2018 to 2019 at the University of Toronto and is since 2019 a full professor at Yale University.

Losev has given talks at many conferences and workshops, has served as a referee for several academic journals, and is a member of the editorial boards of the journals *Transformation Groups*, *Selecta Mathematica*, and the *Journal of Combinatorial Algebra*.[^o3] In 2017 he was elected a fellow (of the class of 2018) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) with a citation for "contributions to geometric representation theory, noncommutative algebra, and the theory of [categorification](wikipedia://en/1596969)."[^o6] In 2010 in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad) he was an invited speaker with talk, *Finite W-algebras* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o7]
