# John Mackintosh Howie

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Mackintosh Howie"}

**John Mackintosh Howie** (23 May 1936 – 26 December 2011) was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and prominent [semigroup theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semigroup_theory).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Howie was educated at [Robert Gordon's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gordon's_College), [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen), the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) and [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford), where he wrote a Ph.D. thesis under the direction of [Graham Higman](wikipedia://en/2600152).

In 1966 the [University of Stirling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stirling) was established with [Walter D. Munn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_D._Munn) (fr) at head of the department of mathematics. Munn recruited Howie to teach there.[^o5]

According to Christopher Hollings,

...a 'British school' of semigroup theory cannot be said to have taken off properly until the mid-1960s when John M. Howie completed an Oxford DPhil in semigroup theory (partly under [Preston's](wikipedia://en/22694802) influence) and Munn began to supervise research students in semigroups (most notably, Norman R. Reilly).[^o5]

He won the [Keith Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Prize) of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh), 1979–81. He was [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics) at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) from 1970 to 1997. No successor to this chair was named until 2015 when [Igor Rivin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Rivin) was appointed.

Howie was charged with reviewing universal, comprehensive [secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education) in Scotland, which was viewed as failing its students. Impressed with [education in Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Denmark), his committee proposed a [tracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracking_%28education%29) scheme to improve academic outcomes, and communicated recommendations in *Upper Secondary Education in Scotland* (1992).[^o6][^o7]

## Public appointments {#Public_appointments}

- Mathematics Panel, [Scottish Examination Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Examination_Board) 1967–1973; Convener from 1970
- Chairman, [Scottish Central Committee for Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Central_Committee_for_Mathematics) 1975–1981
- President, [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) 1973–74
- [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097):
  - Council 1982–1988, 1989–1992
  - Vice-president 1986–1988, 1990–1992
  - Chairman of Education Committee 1985–1989
  - Chairman of Public Affairs Committee 1990–1992
- Member of [Dunning Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunning_Committee) 1975–1977
- Chairman of Governors, [Dundee College of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dundee_College_of_Education) 1983–1987
- Governor, [Northern College of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_College_of_Education) 1987–2001
- Chairman, [Scottish Mathematical Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Mathematical_Council) 1987–1993
- Chairman, Committee to review fifth and sixth years ([Howie Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howie_Committee)) 1990–1992
- Council, [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) 1992–1995
- Chairman, Steering Committee, [International Centre for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/8298904) 1991–1997

## Books {#Books}

- 1976: *An Introduction to Semigroup Theory*, [Academic Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Press)
- 1991: *Automata and Languages*, [Clarendon Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarendon_Press)
- 1992: *Upper Secondary Education in Scotland* (Howie Report)
- 1995: *Fundamentals of Semigroup Theory*, Clarendon Press
- 2001: *Real Analysis*, [Springer books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_books)
- 2003: *Complex Analysis*, Springer books
- 2006: *Fields and Galois Theory*, Springer books
