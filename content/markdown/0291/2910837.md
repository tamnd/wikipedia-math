# Computational finance

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Computational finance"}

*Image: Random Walk example.svg.* Simulation of [Brownian Motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brownian_Motion) sample paths is an important tool in calculating the price of financial instruments under the [risk-neutral measure](wikipedia://en/441911).

**Computational finance** is a branch of applied [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) that deals with problems of practical interest in [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance).[^o2] Some slightly different definitions are the study of [data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data) and [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) currently used in finance[^o3] and the [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) of [computer programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_program) that realize financial [models](wikipedia://en/20590) or [systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System).[^o4]

:::

Computational finance emphasizes practical [numerical methods](wikipedia://en/21506) rather than [mathematical proofs](wikipedia://en/82285) and focuses on techniques that apply directly to [economic analyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics).[^o5] It is an interdisciplinary field between [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680) and [numerical methods](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o6] Two major areas are efficient and accurate computation of [fair values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_value) of [financial securities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_%28finance%29) and the modeling of [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) [time series](wikipedia://en/406624).[^o7]

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="History of computational finance"}

## History {#History}

The birth of computational finance as a discipline can be traced to [Harry Markowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Markowitz) in the early 1950s. Markowitz conceived of the portfolio selection problem as an exercise in mean-variance optimization. This required more computer power than was available at the time, so he worked on useful algorithms for approximate solutions.[^o10] Mathematical finance began with the same insight, but diverged by making simplifying assumptions to express relations in simple [closed forms](wikipedia://en/585143) that did not require sophisticated computer science to evaluate.[^o11]

In the 1960s, hedge fund managers such as [Ed Thorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_O._Thorp)[^o12] and [Michael Goodkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Goodkin) (working with Harry Markowitz, [Paul Samuelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Samuelson) and [Robert C. Merton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_C._Merton))[^o13] pioneered the use of computers in [arbitrage](wikipedia://en/3162) trading. In academics, sophisticated computer processing was needed by researchers such as [Eugene Fama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Fama) in order to analyze large amounts of financial data in support of the [efficient-market hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Efficient-market_hypothesis).[^o11]

During the 1970s, the main focus of computational finance shifted to [options pricing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_of_options) and analyzing [mortgage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgage_loan) [securitizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securitization).[^o14] In the late 1970s and early 1980s, a group of young [quantitative practitioners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_analyst) who became known as "rocket scientists" arrived on [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street) and brought along [personal computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer). This led to an explosion of both the amount and variety of computational finance applications.[^o15] Many of the new techniques came from [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324) and [speech recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing) rather than traditional fields of [computational economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_economics) like [optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) and [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) analysis.[^o15]

By the end of the 1980s, the winding down of the [Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War) brought a large group of displaced [physicists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and [applied mathematicians](wikipedia://en/24295969), many from behind the [Iron Curtain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Curtain), into finance. These people became known as "[financial engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_engineering)" ("quant" is a term that includes both rocket scientists and financial engineers, as well as quantitative portfolio managers).[^o16] This led to a second major extension of the range of computational methods used in finance, also a move away from personal computers to [mainframes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainframe_computer) and [supercomputers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercomputer).[^o14] Around this time computational finance became recognized as a distinct academic subfield. The first degree program in computational finance was offered by [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) in 1994.[^o17]

Over the last 20 years, the field of computational finance has expanded into virtually every area of finance, and the demand for practitioners has grown dramatically.[^o2] Moreover, many specialized companies have grown up to supply computational finance software and services.[^o13]
