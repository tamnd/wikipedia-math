# Denjoy–Koksma inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denjoy–Koksma inequality"}

In mathematics, the **Denjoy–Koksma inequality**, introduced by (Herman 1979, p.73) as a combination of work of [Arnaud Denjoy](wikipedia://en/1485520) and the [Koksma–Hlawka inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koksma–Hlawka_inequality) of [Jurjen Ferdinand Koksma](wikipedia://en/13283214), is a bound for [Weyl sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_sum) $\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}f(x+k\omega)$ of functions *f* of [bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that a map *f* from the circle *T* to itself has irrational rotation number *α*, and *p*/*q* is a rational approximation to *α* with *p* and *q* [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime), |*α* – *p*/*q*| \< 1/*q*<sup>2</sup>. Suppose that *φ* is a function of bounded variation, and *μ* a [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437) on the circle invariant under *f*. Then

$$\left|\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} \phi \circ f^i(x) - q\int_T \phi \, d\mu \right| \leqslant \operatorname{Var}(\phi)$$

(Herman 1979, p.73)

:::
