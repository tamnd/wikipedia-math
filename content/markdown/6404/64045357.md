# Positive linear operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Positive linear operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **positive linear operator** from an [preordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221) $(X, \leq)$ into a preordered vector space $(Y, \leq)$ is a [linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) $f$ on $X$ into $Y$ such that for all [positive elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_element_%28ordered_group%29) $x$ of $X,$ that is $x \geq 0,$ it holds that $f(x) \geq 0.$ In other words, a positive linear operator maps the positive cone of the [domain](wikipedia://en/50263) into the positive cone of the [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264).

:::

Every [positive linear functional](wikipedia://en/454930) is a type of positive linear operator. The significance of positive linear operators lies in results such as [Riesz–Markov–Kakutani representation theorem](wikipedia://en/38948504).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A [linear function](wikipedia://en/152111) $f$ on a [preordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221) is called **positive** if it satisfies either of the following equivalent conditions:

1. $x \geq 0$ implies $f(x) \geq 0.$
1. if $x \leq y$ then $f(x) \leq f(y).$[^n1]

The set of all positive linear forms on a vector space with positive cone $C,$ called the **[dual cone](wikipedia://en/10063937)** and denoted by $C^*,$ is a cone equal to the [polar](wikipedia://en/1764022) of $-C.$ The preorder induced by the dual cone on the space of linear functionals on $X$ is called the **dual preorder**.[^n1]

The **[order dual](wikipedia://en/12259232)** of an ordered vector space $X$ is the set, denoted by $X^+,$ defined by $X^+ := C^* - C^*.$

:::

## Canonical ordering {#Canonical_ordering}

Let $(X, \leq)$ and $(Y, \leq)$ be preordered vector spaces and let $\mathcal{L}(X; Y)$ be the space of all linear maps from $X$ into $Y.$ The set $H$ of all positive linear operators in $\mathcal{L}(X; Y)$ is a cone in $\mathcal{L}(X; Y)$ that defines a preorder on $\mathcal{L}(X; Y)$. If $M$ is a vector subspace of $\mathcal{L}(X; Y)$ and if $H \cap M$ is a proper cone then this proper cone defines a **canonical partial order** on $M$ making $M$ into a partially ordered vector space.[^n2]

If $(X, \leq)$ and $(Y, \leq)$ are [ordered topological vector spaces](wikipedia://en/63988132) and if $\mathcal{G}$ is a family of bounded subsets of $X$ whose union covers $X$ then the [positive cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_cone_of_an_ordered_vector_space) $\mathcal{H}$ in $L(X; Y)$, which is the space of all continuous linear maps from $X$ into $Y,$ is closed in $L(X; Y)$ when $L(X; Y)$ is endowed with the [$\mathcal{G}$-topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology_of_uniform_convergence).[^n2] For $\mathcal{H}$ to be a proper cone in $L(X; Y)$ it is sufficient that the positive cone of $X$ be total in $X$ (that is, the span of the positive cone of $X$ be dense in $X$). If $Y$ is a locally convex space of dimension greater than 0 then this condition is also necessary.[^n2] Thus, if the positive cone of $X$ is total in $X$ and if $Y$ is a locally convex space, then the canonical ordering of $L(X; Y)$ defined by $\mathcal{H}$ is a regular order.[^n2]

## Properties {#Properties}

::: {#o13 .statement kind=statement/proposition cue=inline-head name="Proposition"}

**Proposition**: Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are ordered [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) topological vector spaces with $X$ being a [Mackey space](wikipedia://en/1804899) on which every [positive linear functional](wikipedia://en/454930) is continuous. If the positive cone of $Y$ is a [weakly normal cone](wikipedia://en/63990912) in $Y$ then every positive linear operator from $X$ into $Y$ is continuous.[^n2]

:::

::: {#o15 .statement kind=statement/proposition cue=inline-head name="Proposition"}

**Proposition**: Suppose $X$ is a [barreled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barreled_space) [ordered topological vector space](wikipedia://en/63988132) (TVS) with positive cone $C$ that satisfies $X = C - C$ and $Y$ is a [semi-reflexive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-reflexive) ordered TVS with a positive cone $D$ that is a [normal cone](wikipedia://en/63990912). Give $L(X; Y)$ its canonical order and let $\mathcal{U}$ be a subset of $L(X; Y)$ that is directed upward and either majorized (that is, bounded above by some element of $L(X; Y)$) or simply bounded. Then $u = \sup \mathcal{U}$ exists and the section filter $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{U})$ converges to $u$ uniformly on every precompact subset of $X.$[^n2]

:::
