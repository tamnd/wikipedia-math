# Rom Varshamov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rom Varshamov"}

**Rom Rubenovich Varshamov** (Russian: Ром Рубенович Варшамов; Born April 9, 1927, in Tbilisi; Died August 24, 1999, in Moscow) was a Soviet Armenian mathematician who worked in [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869), especially on [error-correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error-correcting_codes) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

Varshamov studied in [Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi) with [Arnold Walfisz](wikipedia://en/4956669) (where he was Georgian students’ champion in the 100 metres), as well as in [Tomsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomsk). After that, he was a researcher in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) with [Ivan Matveyevich Vinogradov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Matveyevich_Vinogradov), especially on Number theory and Coding theory, and the Ministry of Radio Engineering (working in [Cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432)). In 1957 he proved the [Gilbert-Varshamov bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert-Varshamov_bound) for [linear codes](wikipedia://en/1866743) (independently of [Edgar Gilbert](wikipedia://en/30980291) who proved the non-linear part). From 1968 he worked in [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan) and was director of the Computer Centre (now [Institute for Informatics and Automation Problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Informatics_and_Automation_Problems)[^o3]) of the Academy of Sciences of the Armenian SSR. He was author and co-author of more than 25 scientific articles[^o4] and also a member of the [Armenian National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5]
