# Viviane Baladi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Viviane Baladi"}

**Viviane Baladi** (born 23 May 1963) is a mathematician who works as a director of research at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) in France. Originally Swiss, she has become a naturalized citizen of France.[^o3] Her research concerns [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Baladi earned masters degrees in mathematics and computer science in 1986 from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva).[^o3] She stayed in Geneva for her doctoral studies, finishing a Ph.D. in 1989 under the supervision of [Jean-Pierre Eckmann](wikipedia://en/31446069), with a dissertation concerning the [zeta functions of dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/1342156).[^o5]

She worked at CNRS beginning in 1990, with a leave of absence from 1993 to 1999 when she taught at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and the University of Geneva. She also spent a year as a professor at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) in 2012–2013.[^o3]

## Books {#Books}

She is the author of the book *Positive Transfer Operators and Decay of Correlation* (Advanced Series in Nonlinear Dynamics 16, World Scientific, 2000)[^o7] and of *Dynamical Zeta Functions and Dynamical Determinants for Hyperbolic Maps: A Functional Approach* ([Ergebnisse der Mathematik und ihrer Grenzgebiete](wikipedia://en/15767827) 68, Springer, 2018).[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2014, speaking in the section on "Dynamical Systems and Ordinary Differential Equations".[^o10] She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2018.[^o11] Baladi was awarded the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 2019.[^o12]
