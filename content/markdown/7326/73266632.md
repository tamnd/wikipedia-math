# Alexandra Carpentier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandra Carpentier"}

**Alexandra Carpentier** is a French mathematical statistician and machine learning researcher known for her work in [stochastic optimization](wikipedia://en/7325543), [compressed sensing](wikipedia://en/11403316), and [multi-armed bandit](wikipedia://en/2854828) problems. She works in Germany as a professor at [University of Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Potsdam) and head of the Mathematical Statistics & Machine Learning research group.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After studying [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), statistics, and economics at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) and [ENSAE Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENSAE_Paris), Carpentier earned a doctorate in 2012 through research at the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413) (Inria) in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille).

She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) from 2012 through 2015, and then in 2015 became a professor at the [University of Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Potsdam), funded through the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) program of the German Research Foundation. In 2017 she moved to Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg. Currently, she is a professor at the University of Potsdam.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Carpentier received the 2020 von Kaven Award of the German Research Foundation.
