# Marcel F. Neuts

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marcel F. Neuts"}

**Marcel Fernand Neuts** (21 February 1935 – 9 March 2014) was a Belgian-American mathematician and probability theorist. He is known for contributions in [algorithmic probability](wikipedia://en/402688), [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and [queuing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Neuts was born in [Ostend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostend), Belgium and studied at the [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven). He moved to the US in 1956 along with his wife Olga. He was admitted to graduate programs at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and received his MSc in 1959 and PhD at the same place in 1961, just two years after the MSc degree, under the supervision of [Samuel Karlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Karlin). He held positions at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) from 1962 to 1976, the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) from 1976 to 1985, and at the Department of Systems and Industrial Engineering at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) from 1985 until his retirement in 1997.[^o8][^o9]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Neuts received the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) in 1969.[^o11] Neuts served as the chairman of the Applied Probability Society of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences) between 1977 and 1978.[^o9] He received the Alexander von Humboldt Fellowship in 1983 to conduct research at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart). He was the founding editor of the journal [Stochastic Models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_Models) and a contributing editor for [Journal of Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Applied_Probability) and [Advances in Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Applied_Probability).[^o12]

The journal Stochastic Models has established a prize after Neuts for best papers published in the journal.[^o13]
