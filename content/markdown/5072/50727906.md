# C. V. Mourey

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="C. V. Mourey"}

**C. V. Mourey** (1791? – 1830?) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who wrote a work of 100 pages titled La vraie théorie des quantités négatives et des quantités prétendues imaginaires *(The true theory of negative quantities and of alleged imaginary quantities)*, published in Paris in 1828 and reedited in 1861, in which he gave a systematic presentation of [vector theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_theory). He seems to be the first mathematician to state the necessity of specifying the conditions of equality between vectors.[^o4]

:::

Mourey also stated that there exists a more general [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) but no other writings by him have survived.[^o5]

Nothing is known about Mourey's life.[^o6] The [St. Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Andrews_University)'s researcher Elizabeth Lewis, supposes Mourey was a technician in Paris, but says she cannot positively identify him.[^o7]
