# William Caspar Graustein

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="William Caspar Graustein"}

**William Caspar Graustein** (15 November 1888 – 22 January 1941) was an American mathematician. He graduated magna cum laude from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1910[^o3] and later became an instructor at Harvard University. In 1921, he married [Mary Curtis Graustein](wikipedia://en/64018758) (1884—1972), who was the first American woman to earn a mathematics Ph.D. (1917) from [Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College).[^o4]

:::

He died in an automobile accident, at the age of 52. At the time, Graustein was professor of mathematics and assistant dean at Harvard.[^o4]

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

Some of his books and papers are:[^o6]

- *The scientific work of Joseph Lipka*
- *Applicability with preservation of both curvatures*
- *Extensions of the [four-vertex theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-vertex_theorem)*
- *Introduction to higher geometry*
- *Differential Geometry* MacMillan Company 1935. Republished Dover 1966 2006.
