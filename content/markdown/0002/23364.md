# Demographics of Pakistan

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Pakistan"}

[Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) had a population of 247,541,947 according to the final results of the [2023 Pakistani census](wikipedia://en/69619764).[^o5][^o6][^o7] Pakistan is the world's [fifth–most populous country](wikipedia://en/69058) and has the [second-largest Muslim population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_by_country#Countries) as of 2023.[^o8][^o9][^o10] Between 1951 and 2023, Pakistan's population expanded over sevenfold, going from 33.7 million to 247.5 million. The country has a relatively high, although declining, growth rate supported by high birth rates and low death rates. Between 1998 and 2017, the average annual population growth rate stood at +2.40%.[^o11]

:::

Dramatic social changes have led to [urbanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanisation_in_Pakistan) and the emergence of two [megacities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megacity): [Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi) and [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore). The country's urban population more than tripled between 1981 and 2017 (from 23.8 million to 75.7 million), as Pakistan's [urbanisation rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization) rose from 28.2% to 36.4%. Even with this, the nation's urbanisation rate remains one of [the lowest in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization_by_sovereign_state), and in 2017, over 130 million Pakistanis (making up nearly 65% of the population) lived in rural areas.

Due to a high [fertility rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_fertility_rate), which was estimated at 3.5 in 2022, Pakistan has one of the world's [youngest populations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_median_age). The 2017 census recorded that 40.3% of the country's population was under the age of 15, while only 3.7% of Pakistanis were aged 65 or more.[^o12] The [median age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_age) of the country was 19,[^o12] while its [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) was recorded to be 105 males per 100 females.[^o13] Pakistan ranked last (148th of 148 countries) on the [Global Gender Gap Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Gender_Gap_Report), with a gender parity score of 56.7%, reflecting one of the lowest levels of gender equality globally.[^o14]

The [demographic history of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographic_history_of_Pakistan) from the ancient [Indus Valley civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Valley_Civilisation) to the modern era includes the arrival and settlement of many cultures and ethnic groups in the modern region of [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) from [Eurasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasia) and the nearby [Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East). Because of this, Pakistan has a multicultural, multilinguistic, and multiethnic society. Pakistan is also thought to have the world's fourth-largest [refugee population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states_by_refugee_population), estimated at 1.4 million in mid-2021 by the [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_High_Commissioner_for_Refugees).[^o15]

### Population distribution {#Population_distribution}

Pakistan's population is distributed unevenly, with over half of the country's people living in the [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab,_Pakistan) province. On the other hand, Balochistan, which is geographically Pakistan's largest province, is the least-populated. The population is mainly clustered around the most agriculturally fertile areas (which is the reason Punjab has the highest population as it is agriculturally fertile), particularly the [Indus River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River) and its tributaries. Most of the country's people live in [rural areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_area), but two large and growing [megacities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megacity) exist: the coastal [Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi) and [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore) in eastern Punjab. Numerous smaller cities (such as [Faisalabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisalabad), [Rawalpindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi), [Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar), [Gujranwala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujranwala) and the capital [Islamabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad)) dot the rest of the country.

#### Largest cities {#Largest_cities}

As urbanisation has progressed and owing to the country's large population, Pakistan today has many very large urban centers which act as hubs for commerce and culture. The nation has two megacities, [Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi) and [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore). With populations of 18.9 million and 13 million respectively (as of 2023), they are among the world's largest metropolises. The country also has eight more cities with more than 1 million residents each: [Faisalabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisalabad), [Rawalpindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi), [Gujranwala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujranwala), [Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan), [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_Pakistan), [Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar), [Quetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta), and [Islamabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad). All of them play a significant role in the country, housing nearly 19 million people altogether.

## Vital statistics {#Vital_statistics}

As Pakistan lacks a national [vital statistics](wikipedia://en/999909) system that publicly disseminates data, all of the following information is made from estimates, which are constantly being revised. The [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations) estimated that in February 2021, only 42% of births in Pakistan were [officially registered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_registration), making it the world's most populous country where more than half of births remained unregistered.[^o55] The United Nations was unable to estimate how many deaths were officially registered.[^o55]

## Nationality, ethnicity, and language {#Nationality,_ethnicity,_and_language}

### Ethnic groups {#Ethnic_groups}

The major [ethnolinguistic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnolinguistic_group) of [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) include [Punjabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabis), [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), [Sindhis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhis), [Saraikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_people), [Muhajirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhajir_%28Pakistan%29), [Balochs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people), [Hindkowans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindkowans)/[Hazarewals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazarewal), [Meos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meo_%28ethnic_group%29), and [Kohistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Kohistani_people)[^o179] with significant numbers of [Shina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shina_people), [Baltis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balti_people), [Kashmiris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiri_people), [Paharis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahari_people_%28Kashmir%29), [Chitralis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kho_people), [Hazaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaras), [Burusho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burusho_people) and other various minorities.[^o133][^o134]

Pakistan's census does not include the 1.7 million [Afghans in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghans_in_Pakistan),[^o135] who have been ordered to leave the country or face deportation.[^o138] Majority of them were born in Pakistan within the last four decades and are ethnically [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), [Tajiks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajiks), [Uzbeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbeks) and others.[^o136]

### Foreign-born population in Pakistan {#Foreign-born_population_in_Pakistan}

After the [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India) of [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) in 1947, millions of Muslims from India migrated to Pakistan and they are the largest group of foreign-born residents. This group is dwindling because of its age. The second-largest group of foreign-born residents consists of [Afghans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghans_in_Pakistan) who have been ordered to leave Pakistan or face deportation.[^o138] There are also smaller groups of Muslim immigrants from countries such as [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh), [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq), [Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalia), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran), [Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajikistan), and [Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan), among others.

### Languages {#Languages}

[Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) is a [multilingual country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_multilingual_countries_and_regions) with dozens of regional languages spoken as [first languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language) by various ethnolinguistic groups.[^o141][^o142] The majority Pakistan's languages belong to the [Indo-Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Iranian_languages) group of the [Indo-European language family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-European_languages).[^o143][^o144]

[Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) is the [national language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_language) and the [lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca) of Pakistan, and while sharing official status with [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), it is the preferred and dominant language used for communication between different ethnic groups.[^o141][^o142] Despite Urdu being the country's lingua franca, estimates on how many languages are spoken in the country range from 75 to 85,[^o145][^o146] and in 2023, the country's three largest languages were [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) (being spoken by up to 36.98% of the total population), [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) (18.15%), and [Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language) (14.31%).[^o180] Languages with more than a million speakers each include [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language), [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto), [Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language), [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language), [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language), [Hindko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindko), [Brahui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahui_language), [Mewati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewati_language) and [Kohistani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Kohistani).[^o147][^o148]

[Ethnologue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnologue) lists 74 languages in Pakistan. Of these, 66 are indigenous and 8 are non-indigenous. In terms of their vitality, 7 are classified as 'institutional', 17 are 'developing', 37 are 'vigorous', 10 are 'in trouble', and 3 are 'dying'.[^o149]

### Recent changes and detailed demographic data {#Recent_changes_and_detailed_demographic_data}

[Pakistan Bureau of Statistics](wikipedia://en/4766769) released religious data of Pakistan Census 2017 on 19 May 2021.[^o167] 96.47% are Muslims, followed by 2.174% Hindus, 1.27% Christians, 0.09% Ahmadis and 0.02% others.

The 2017 census showed marginal increase in the share of [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindus).The census also recorded Pakistan's first Hindu-majority district, called [Umerkot District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umerkot_District).

On the other hand, [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity) in Pakistan, while increasing in raw numbers, has fallen significantly in percentage terms since the last census. Christians are concentrated in the most developed parts of Pakistan, [Lahore District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_District) (over 5% Christian), [Islamabad Capital Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Capital_Territory) (over 4% Christian), and Northern Punjab.

The [Ahmadiyya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadiyya) movement shrunk in size (both raw numbers and percentage) between 1998 and 2017, while remaining concentrated in [Lalian Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalian_Tehsil), [Chiniot District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiniot_District), where approximately 13% of the population is Ahmadiyya.
