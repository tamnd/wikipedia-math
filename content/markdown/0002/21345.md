## Ethnic groups {#Ethnic_groups}

Ethnic [Melanesians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanesians) known as [Kanak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanak_people) constituted 41.2% of the population in 2019, followed by [Europeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europeans) ([Caldoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldoche) and [Zoreille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoreille)) with 24.1%. The Europeans are the largest ethnic group in the [South Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Province,_New_Caledonia), where they make up a plurality, while Kanak are the majority in the two other provinces. The remainder of the population are [Wallis and Futunan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallis_and_Futuna) (8.3%), [Tahitian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahitians) (2.0%), [Indonesians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Indonesians) (1.4%), [Ni-Vanuatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ni-Vanuatu) (0.9%), [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Vietnamese), other [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia) (0.4%), [Mixed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_people) (11.3%), and belong to other ethnic groups (9.5%). An estimated 15,000 Caledonians were of [Algerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algeria) descent.[^o11]
