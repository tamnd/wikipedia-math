# Pumping lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pumping lemma"}

In the theory of [formal languages](wikipedia://en/10939), the **pumping lemma** may refer to:

:::

- [Pumping lemma for regular languages](wikipedia://en/2070351), the fact that all sufficiently long strings in such a language have a substring that can be repeated arbitrarily many times, usually used to prove that certain languages are not regular
- [Pumping lemma for context-free languages](wikipedia://en/3193758), the fact that all sufficiently long strings in such a language have a pair of substrings that can be repeated arbitrarily many times, usually used to prove that certain languages are not context-free
- Pumping lemma for [indexed languages](wikipedia://en/7324284#Properties)
- [Pumping lemma for regular tree languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumping_lemma_for_regular_tree_languages)

## See also {#See_also}

- [Ogden's lemma](wikipedia://en/4105321), a stronger version of the pumping lemma for context-free languages
