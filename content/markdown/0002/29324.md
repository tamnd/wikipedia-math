# Signal processing

*Image: Signal processing system.png.* Signal transmission using electronic signal processing. [Transducers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transducer) convert signals from other physical [waveforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waveform) to electric [current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_current) or [voltage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage) waveforms, which are then processed, transmitted as [electromagnetic waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_wave), received and converted by another transducer to their final form.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Signal processing"}

**Signal processing** is an [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) subfield that focuses on analyzing, modifying and synthesizing *[signals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal)*, such as [sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_signal_processing), [images](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing), [potential fields](wikipedia://en/461654), [seismic signals](wikipedia://en/377931), [altimetry processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altimeter), and [scientific measurements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_measurements).[^o2] Signal processing techniques are used to optimize transmissions, [digital storage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_storage) efficiency, correcting distorted signals, improve [subjective video quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subjective_video_quality), and to detect or pinpoint components of interest in a measured signal.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="History of signal processing"}

## History {#History}

According to [Alan V. Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_V._Oppenheim) and [Ronald W. Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_W._Schafer), the principles of signal processing can be found in the classical [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) techniques of the 17th century. They further state that the digital refinement of these techniques can be found in the digital [control systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_system) of the 1940s and 1950s.[^o6]

In 1948, [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693) wrote the influential paper "[A Mathematical Theory of Communication](wikipedia://en/828061)" which was published in the *[Bell System Technical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_System_Technical_Journal)*.[^o7] The paper laid the groundwork for later development of information communication systems and the processing of signals for transmission.[^o8]

Signal processing matured and flourished in the 1960s and 1970s, and [digital signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signal_processing) became widely used with specialized [digital signal processor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signal_processor) chips in the 1980s.[^o8]

:::

## Definition of a signal {#Definition_of_a_signal}

In signal processing, a signal is represented as a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) of time: $x(t)$, where this function is either[^o10]

- deterministic (then one speaks of a deterministic signal) or
- a path $(x_t)_{t \in T}$, a realization of a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) $(X_t)_{t \in T}$

## Categories {#Categories}

### Analog {#Analog}

Analog signal processing is for signals that have not been digitized, as in most 20th-century [radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio), telephone, and television systems. This involves linear electronic circuits as well as nonlinear ones. The former are, for instance, [passive filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passive_filter), [active filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_filter), [additive mixers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_mixer), [integrators](wikipedia://en/1047173), and [delay lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_delay_line). Nonlinear circuits include [compandors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compandor), multipliers ([frequency mixers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequency_mixer), [voltage-controlled amplifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage-controlled_amplifier)), [voltage-controlled filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage-controlled_filter), [voltage-controlled oscillators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage-controlled_oscillator), and [phase-locked loops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase-locked_loop).

### Continuous time {#Continuous_time}

[Continuous-time signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous-time_signal) processing is for signals that vary continuously in time and are not broken into individual interrupted points, i.e., [samples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_%28signal_processing%29).

The methods of signal processing include [time domain](wikipedia://en/173419), [frequency domain](wikipedia://en/370346), and [complex frequency domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_frequency). This technology mainly discusses the modeling of a [linear time-invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_time-invariant) continuous system, integral of the system's zero-state response, setting up system function and the continuous-time filtering of deterministic signals. For example, in time domain, a continuous-time signal $x(t)$ passing through a [linear time-invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_time-invariant) filter/system denoted as $h(t)$, can be expressed at the output as

$$y(t) = \int_{-\infty}^\infty h(\tau) x(t - \tau) \, d\tau$$

In some contexts, $h(t)$ is referred to as the impulse response of the system. The above [convolution](wikipedia://en/7519) operation is conducted between the input and the system.

### Discrete time {#Discrete_time}

[Discrete-time signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete-time_signal) processing is for sampled signals, defined only at discrete points in time, and as such are quantized in time, but not in magnitude.

*Analog discrete-time signal processing* is a technology based on electronic devices such as [sample and hold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_and_hold) circuits, analog time-division [multiplexers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplexer), [analog delay lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_delay_line) and [analog feedback shift registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_feedback_shift_register). This technology was a predecessor of digital signal processing (see below), and is still used in advanced processing of gigahertz signals.[^o15]

The concept of discrete-time signal processing also refers to a theoretical discipline that establishes a mathematical basis for digital signal processing, without taking [quantization error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantization_error) into consideration.

### Digital {#Digital}

Digital signal processing is the processing of digitized discrete-time sampled signals. Processing is done by general-purpose [computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer) or by digital circuits such as [ASICs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASIC), [field-programmable gate arrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field-programmable_gate_array) or specialized [digital signal processors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signal_processor). Typical arithmetical operations include [fixed-point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-point_arithmetic) and [floating-point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floating-point), real-valued and complex-valued, multiplication and addition. Other typical operations supported by the hardware are [circular buffers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_buffer) and [lookup tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lookup_table). Examples of algorithms are the [fast Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/11512) (FFT), [finite impulse response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_impulse_response) (FIR) filter, [Infinite impulse response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_impulse_response) (IIR) filter, and [adaptive filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_filter) such as the [Wiener](wikipedia://en/1216721) and [Kalman filters](wikipedia://en/180855).

### Nonlinear {#Nonlinear}

Nonlinear signal processing involves the analysis and processing of signals produced from [nonlinear systems](wikipedia://en/146103) and can be in the time, [frequency](wikipedia://en/10779), or spatiotemporal domains.[^o18][^o19] Nonlinear systems can produce highly complex behaviors including [bifurcations](wikipedia://en/2326042), [chaos](wikipedia://en/6295), [harmonics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonics), and [subharmonics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subharmonics) which cannot be produced or analyzed using linear methods.

Polynomial signal processing is a type of non-linear signal processing, where [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) systems may be interpreted as conceptually straightforward extensions of linear systems to the nonlinear case.[^o20]

::: {#o22 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Statistical Signal Processing"}

### Statistical {#Statistical}

**Statistical signal processing** is an approach which treats signals as [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), utilizing their [statistical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical) properties to perform signal processing tasks.[^o23] Statistical techniques are widely used in signal processing applications. For example, one can model the [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) of noise incurred when photographing an image, and construct techniques based on this model to [reduce the noise](wikipedia://en/476836) in the resulting image.

:::

### Graph {#Graph}

**Graph signal processing** generalizes signal processing tasks to signals living on non-Euclidean domains whose structure can be captured by a weighted graph.[^o25] Graph signal processing presents several key points such as sampling signal techniques,[^o26] recovery techniques[^o27] and time-varying techiques.[^o28] Graph signal processing has been applied with success in the field of image processing, computer vision[^o29][^o30][^o31] and sound anomaly detection.[^o32]

## Application fields {#Application_fields}

*Image: Seismic Data Processing.jpg.* Seismic signal processing

- [Audio signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_signal_processing) – for electrical signals representing sound, such as [speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_signal_processing) or music[^o35]
- [Image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing) – in digital cameras, computers and various imaging systems
- [Video processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_processing) – for interpreting moving pictures
- [Wireless communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wireless_communication) – waveform generations, demodulation, filtering, equalization
- [Control systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_systems)
- [Array processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Array_processing) – for processing signals from arrays of sensors
- [Process control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_control) – a variety of signals are used, including the industry standard [4-20&nbsp;mA current loop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-20&nbsp;mA_current_loop)
- [Seismology](wikipedia://en/28935)
- [Feature extraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feature_extraction), such as [image understanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_understanding), [semantic audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_audio) and [speech recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_recognition).
- Quality improvement, such as [noise reduction](wikipedia://en/476836), [image enhancement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_enhancement), and [echo cancellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_cancellation).
- Source coding including [audio compression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_compression_%28data%29), [image compression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_compression), and [video compression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_compression).
- [Genomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomic) signal processing[^o36]
- In [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics), signal processing is used to amplify the signal vs the noise within [time-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-series) measurements of geophysical data. Processing is conducted within the [time domain](wikipedia://en/173419) or [frequency domain](wikipedia://en/370346), or both.[^o37][^o38]

In communication systems, signal processing may occur at:

- OSI layer 1 in the seven-layer [OSI model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OSI_model), the [physical layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_layer) ([modulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulation), [equalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equalization_%28communications%29), [multiplexing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplexing), etc.);
- OSI layer 2, the [data link layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_link_layer) ([forward error correction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_error_correction));
- OSI layer 6, the [presentation layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presentation_layer) (source coding, including [analog-to-digital conversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog-to-digital_conversion) and [data compression](wikipedia://en/8013)).

:::

## Typical devices {#Typical_devices}

- [Filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_%28signal_processing%29) – for example analog (passive or active) or digital ([FIR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIR_filter), [IIR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIR_filter), frequency domain or [stochastic filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_filter), etc.)
- [Samplers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_%28signal_processing%29) and [analog-to-digital converters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog-to-digital_converter) for [signal acquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_acquisition) and reconstruction, which involves measuring a physical signal, storing or transferring it as a digital signal, and possibly later rebuilding the original signal or an approximation thereof.
- [Digital signal processors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signal_processor) (DSPs)

## Mathematical methods applied {#Mathematical_methods_applied}

- [Differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations)[^o41] – for modeling system behavior, connecting input and output relations in linear time-invariant systems. For instance, a low-pass filter such as an [RC circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RC_circuit) can be modeled as a differential equation in signal processing, which allows one to compute the continuous output signal as a function of the input or initial conditions.
- [Recurrence relations](wikipedia://en/146806)[^o42]
- [Transform theory](wikipedia://en/16704202)
- [Time-frequency analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-frequency_analysis) – for processing non-stationary signals[^o43]
- [Linear canonical transformation](wikipedia://en/4415205)
- [Spectral estimation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_estimation) – for determining the spectral content (i.e., the distribution of power over frequency) of a set of [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) data points[^o44]
- [Statistical signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_signal_processing) – analyzing and extracting information from signals and noise based on their stochastic properties
- [Linear time-invariant system](wikipedia://en/1383899) theory, and [transform theory](wikipedia://en/16704202)
- [Polynomial signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_signal_processing) – analysis of systems which relate input and output using polynomials
- [System identification](wikipedia://en/552466)[^o18] and classification
- [Calculus](wikipedia://en/5176)
- [Coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869)
- [Complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759)[^o45]
- [Vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_spaces) and [Linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422)[^o46]
- [Functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180)[^o47]
- [Probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes)[^o23]
- [Detection theory](wikipedia://en/1156527)
- [Estimation theory](wikipedia://en/1565926)
- [Optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization)[^o48]
- [Numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods)
- [Data mining](wikipedia://en/42253) – for statistical analysis of relations between large quantities of variables (in this context representing many physical signals), to extract previously unknown interesting patterns
