# George Pólya Award

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George Pólya Award"}

The **George Pólya Award** is presented annually by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) for articles of expository excellence that have been published in the [College Mathematics Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Mathematics_Journal). The award was established in 1976, and up to two awards of \$1,000 each are given each year.[^o3][^o4] The award is named after Hungarian mathematician [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya).

:::
