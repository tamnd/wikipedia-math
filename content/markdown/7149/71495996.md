# Pál Révész

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pál Révész"}

**Pál Révész** (6 June 1934 – 14 November 2022),[^o2] [anglicized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglicisation) as **Pal Revesz**, was a Hungarian mathematician known for his research in probability and mathematical statistics, including the mathematical foundations of the [law of large numbers](wikipedia://en/157055), theory of [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671), and [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451).[^o7][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Révész was born in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest). He studied in the applied mathematics program at the Faculty of Science, [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) and graduated there in 1957. Afterwards, he got a job at the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University)'s probability department, where he worked as an assistant professor. In 1964, he transferred to the Mathematical Research Institute of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, where he started working as a scientific associate. In 1963, he defended his candidate's thesis in mathematics, and in 1969 he defended his academic doctoral thesis. He became a member of the Mathematical Committee of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). He did a significant part of his scientific work here. He was elected a corresponding member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1982, and a full member in 1987. In 1985, he received a second position as a university professor at the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology).[^o6] He left the research institute in 1987, when he was appointed professor at the Institute of Mathematics of the [Budapest University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_University_of_Technology_and_Economics). In Vienna, he also headed the Department of Statistics and Probability, from where he retired in 1998.[^o7] Between 1999 and 2005, he was the deputy chairman of the Mathematics Department. Meanwhile, he also worked in the Committee on International Relations.

Révész became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) in 1991.[^o7] In addition to his academic duties, he also contributed to the management of several scientific societies: from 1981 to 1983 he was the president of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). Between 1995 and 1997, he held the position of acting president of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615).

## Academic research {#Academic_research}

Révész's main research areas were probability and mathematical statistics. His results related to the so-called strong approximation of stochastic processes and the estimation of the [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) are significant. He also dealt with statistical applications of the [stochastic approximation](wikipedia://en/8979437) method. He was the first to provide a method for estimating the regression function that is suitable for simultaneous estimation of all points of the function. He was a close collaborator of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).[^o9]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Révész received the [State Award of the People's Republic of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Award_of_the_People's_Republic_of_Hungary) in 1978 for his achievements in probability, especially in the theory of stochastic processes and their practical application.[^o7] He was an honorary professor at [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) and the [University of Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged).[^o11]
