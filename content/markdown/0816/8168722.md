# HP-27

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="HP-27"}

*Image: 27new.jpg.* A HP 27

The **HP-27** was a hand-held scientific and financial, but not programmable, calculator made by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard) between 1976 and 1978.

:::

Unlike all previous HP's pocket calculators, the HP-27 could do mathematic, statistic and business operations. It used [Reverse Polish Notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_Polish_Notation) (RPN) for calculations, working on a four-level [stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_%28abstract_data_type%29) (x,y,z,t). Nearly all keys had two alternate functions, accessed by a yellow and black prefix key. So despite the 30 keys only keyboard it could access about 68 functions. The HP-27 also had a 10-digit red [LED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LED) display and 10 registers to store numbers.
