# Tal Malkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tal Malkin"}

**Tal Geula Malkin** (טל גאולה מלכין; born 1970) is an Israeli-American cryptographer who works as a professor of computer science at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), where she heads the Cryptography Lab and the Data Science Institute Cybersecurity Center.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Malkin graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) in 1993, with a bachelor's degree in mathematics and computer science. She earned a master's degree in computer science from [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) in 1995, with the master's thesis *Deductive Tableaux for Temporal Logic* supervised by [Amir Pnueli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amir_Pnueli), and completed a Ph.D. in 2000 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) with the dissertation *A Study of Secure Database Access and General Two-Party Computation* supervised by [Shafi Goldwasser](wikipedia://en/842178).

As a doctoral student, she also worked as an intern for [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research) at the [Thomas J. Watson Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Watson_Research_Center), and as a research scientist for [AT&T Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Labs), continuing there through 2002. In 2003 she joined Columbia University as an assistant professor of computer science, earning tenure there in 2009.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Malkin was named as a 2020 Fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043), "for foundational contributions, including black-box separations, multiparty computation, and tamper resilience, and for service to the IACR".
