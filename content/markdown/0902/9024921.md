# Leonard James Rogers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonard James Rogers"}

*Image: Leonard James Rogers.jpg.* Leonard James Rogers

**Leonard James Rogers** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society)[^o2] (30 March 1862 – 12 September 1933) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was the first to discover the [Rogers–Ramanujan identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogers–Ramanujan_identity) and [Hölder's inequality](wikipedia://en/191538), and who introduced [Rogers polynomials](wikipedia://en/32744201). The [Rogers–Szegő polynomials](wikipedia://en/32745764) are named after him.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Rogers was born in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford), the second son of [James Edwin Thorold Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Edwin_Thorold_Rogers) and his second wife Anne Reynolds, and brother of [Annie Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Rogers).[^o5] He matriculated at [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford), graduating [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) and [BMus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Music) in 1884 and [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts_%28Oxford,_Cambridge,_and_Dublin%29) in 1887.

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Rogers became [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) in mathematics at [Wadham College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College,_Oxford) in 1885.

In 1888 Rogers was appointed [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Yorkshire College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire_College), by then a constituent college of the [Victoria University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_%28United_Kingdom%29). The Yorkshire College became the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 1904. In 1919 he retired because of poor health.[^o5]

Rogers worked initially on reciprocants in the theory of [differential invariants](wikipedia://en/25412108), and then moved into the area of [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), where he anticipated results of Ramanujan. In the late 1920s, he published in the *[Mathematical Gazette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Gazette)* four notes on geometrical problems, including on [Malfatti's Problem](wikipedia://en/1475894).

## Honours {#Honours}

He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (FRS) in 1924.[^o5]

## Death {#Death}

Rogers died in Oxford on 12 September 1933, aged 71.[^o5]
