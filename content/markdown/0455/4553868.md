# David Corfield

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Corfield"}

**David Neil Corfield** is a British [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) specializing in [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) and [philosophy of psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_psychology). He was [Senior Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Lecturer) in Philosophy at the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent) until 2024.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Corfield studied mathematics at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and later earned his MSc and PhD in the philosophy of science and mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London).[^o6][^o7] His doctoral advisor was [Donald A. Gillies](wikipedia://en/24874263).[^o8]

## Work {#Work}

Corfield is the author of *Towards a Philosophy of Real Mathematics* (2003), in which he argues that the philosophical implications of mathematics did not stop with [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736)'s [incompleteness theorems](wikipedia://en/58863). He has also co-authored a book with [Darian Leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darian_Leader) about [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and [psychosomatic medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychosomatic_medicine), *Why Do People Get Ill?* (2007).

He joined the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent) in September 2007 where he was [Senior Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Lecturer) in Philosophy until 2024.

He is a member of the informal steering committee of [*n*Lab](wikipedia://en/27226892), a wiki-lab for collaborative work on mathematics, physics, and philosophy.
