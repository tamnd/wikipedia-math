# Centered tree

*Image: Centered tree.gif.* On the left a centered tree, on the right a bicentered one. The numbers show each node's eccentricity.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Centered tree"}

In the [mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical) subfield of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **centered tree** is a [tree](wikipedia://en/48560) with only one [center](wikipedia://en/10447275), and a **bicentered tree** is a tree with two centers.

:::

Given a graph, the eccentricity of a [vertex](wikipedia://en/638899) $v$ is defined as the greatest [distance](wikipedia://en/1020021) from $v$ to any other vertex. A *center* of a graph is a vertex with minimal [eccentricity](wikipedia://en/1020021#Related_concepts). A graph can have an arbitrary number of centers. However, (Jordan 1869) has proved that for trees, there are only two possibilities:

1. The tree has precisely one center (centered trees).
1. The tree has precisely two centers (bicentered trees). In this case, the two centers are adjacent.

A proof of this fact is given, for example, by Harary.[^o3]
