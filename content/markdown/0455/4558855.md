# Selman Akbulut

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Selman Akbulut"}

**Selman Akbulut** (born 1949) is a [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in research in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He was a professor at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) until February 2020.

:::

## Career {#Career}

In 1975, he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) as a student of [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424). In topology, he has worked on [handlebody](wikipedia://en/864472) theory, low-dimensional [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470),[^o4] [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), [G2 manifolds](wikipedia://en/648062). In the topology of [real-algebraic sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_algebraic_set), he and Henry C. King proved that every [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [piecewise-linear manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise-linear_manifold) is a real-algebraic set; they discovered new topological invariants of real-algebraic sets.[^o5]

He was a visiting scholar several times at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (in 1975-76, 1980–81, 2002, and 2005).[^o6]

On February 14, 2020, Akbulut was removed from his tenured position at MSU by the Board of Trustees, after disputes over his teaching allotments and communications with colleagues.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

He has developed 4-dimensional handlebody techniques, settling conjectures and solving problems about 4-manifolds, such as a conjecture of [Christopher Zeeman](wikipedia://en/871415),[^o11] the [Harer–Kas–Kirby conjecture](wikipedia://en/22729603), a problem of [Martin Scharlemann](wikipedia://en/49932035),[^o12] and problems of [Sylvain Cappell](wikipedia://en/17173914) and [Julius Shaneson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Shaneson).[^o13][^o14][^o15] He constructed an exotic compact 4-manifold (with boundary) from which he discovered "[Akbulut corks](wikipedia://en/17974424)".[^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19]

His most recent results concern the 4-dimensional [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798).[^o20] He has supervised 14 Ph.D. students as of 2019. He has more than 100 papers and three books published, and several books edited.
