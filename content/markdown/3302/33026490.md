# Georg Faber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georg Faber"}

**Georg Faber** (5 April 1877 – 7 March 1966) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Faber polynomials](wikipedia://en/33026245), [Faber series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faber_series), the [Lévy C curve](wikipedia://en/1057085), and [Faber–Schauder systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faber–Schauder_system).

:::
