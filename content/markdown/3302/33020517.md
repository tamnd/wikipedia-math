# Pentagonal polytope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal polytope"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentagonal polytope** is a [regular polytope](wikipedia://en/518693) in *n* dimensions constructed from the [H<sub>*n*</sub> Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004). The family was named by [H. S. M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328), because the two-dimensional pentagonal polytope is a [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014). It can be named by its [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) as {5, 3<sup>*n* − 2</sup>} (dodecahedral) or {3<sup>*n* − 2</sup>, 5} (icosahedral).

:::

## Family members {#Family_members}

The family starts as [1-polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) and ends with *n* = 5 as infinite tessellations of 4-dimensional hyperbolic space.

There are two types of pentagonal polytopes; they may be termed the *dodecahedral* and *icosahedral* types, by their three-dimensional members. The two types are duals of each other.

### Dodecahedral {#Dodecahedral}

The complete family of dodecahedral pentagonal polytopes are:

1. [Line segment](wikipedia://en/22634860), { }
1. [Pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014), {5}
1. [Dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), {5, 3} (12 [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) faces)
1. [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358), {5, 3, 3} (120 [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/8407) cells)
1. [Order-3 120-cell honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order-3_120-cell_honeycomb), {5, 3, 3, 3} (tessellates hyperbolic 4-space (∞ [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) facets)

The facets of each dodecahedral pentagonal polytope are the dodecahedral pentagonal polytopes of one less dimension. Their vertex figures are the [simplices](wikipedia://en/39781) of one less dimension.

### Icosahedral {#Icosahedral}

The complete family of icosahedral pentagonal polytopes are:

1. [Line segment](wikipedia://en/22634860), { }
1. [Pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014), {5}
1. [Icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), {3, 5} (20 [triangular](wikipedia://en/173285) faces)
1. [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), {3, 3, 5} (600 [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) cells)
1. [Order-5 5-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976509), {3, 3, 3, 5} (tessellates hyperbolic 4-space (∞ [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) facets)

The facets of each icosahedral pentagonal polytope are the [simplices](wikipedia://en/39781) of one less dimension. Their vertex figures are icosahedral pentagonal polytopes of one less dimension.

## Related star polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_star_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The pentagonal polytopes can be [stellated](wikipedia://en/180253) to form new [star regular polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_polytope):

- In two dimensions, we obtain the [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544) {5/2},
- In three dimensions, this forms the four [Kepler–Poinsot polyhedra](wikipedia://en/16814), {[3,5/2](wikipedia://en/3029898)}, {[5/2,3](wikipedia://en/3029878)}, {[5,5/2](wikipedia://en/2860441)}, and {[5/2,5](wikipedia://en/3029843)}.
- In four dimensions, this forms the ten [Schläfli–Hess polychora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli–Hess_polychoron): {[3,5,5/2](wikipedia://en/8604585)}, {[5/2,5,3](wikipedia://en/8604577)}, {[5,5/2,5](wikipedia://en/8604660)}, {[5,3,5/2](wikipedia://en/8604599)}, {[5/2,3,5](wikipedia://en/8604610)}, {[5/2,5,5/2](wikipedia://en/8604676)}, {[5,5/2,3](wikipedia://en/8604627)}, {[3,5/2,5](wikipedia://en/8604644)}, {[3,3,5/2](wikipedia://en/8604538)}, and {[5/2,3,3](wikipedia://en/8604513)}.
- In four-dimensional hyperbolic space there are [four regular star-honeycombs](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_4-space): [{5/2,5,3,3}](wikipedia://en/41964064), [{3,3,5,5/2}](wikipedia://en/41964116), [{3,5,5/2,5}](wikipedia://en/41964194), and [{5,5/2,5,3}](wikipedia://en/41964210).

In some cases, the star pentagonal polytopes are themselves counted among the pentagonal polytopes.[^o8]

Like other polytopes, regular stars can be combined with their duals to form compounds;

- In two dimensions, a [decagrammic star figure](wikipedia://en/19546742) {10/2} is formed,
- In three dimensions, we obtain the [compound of dodecahedron and icosahedron](wikipedia://en/8010901),
- In four dimensions, we obtain the [compound of 120-cell and 600-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_120-cell_and_600-cell).

Star polytopes can also be combined.
