# Cornelia Druțu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cornelia Druțu"}

**Cornelia Druțu** is a Romanian mathematician notable for her contributions in the area of [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).[^o3] She is Professor of mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford)[^o3] and Fellow[^o4] of [Exeter College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Druțu was born in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), Romania. She attended the [Emil Racoviță](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Racoviță) High School (now the National College Emil Racoviță[^o6]) in Iași. She earned a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in Mathematics from the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University), where besides attending the core courses she received extra curricular teaching in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) from Professor Liliana Răileanu.[^o4]

In 1996 Druțu earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics from [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), with a thesis entitled *Réseaux non uniformes des groupes de Lie semi-simple de rang supérieur et invariants de quasiisométrie*, written under the supervision of [Pierre Pansu](wikipedia://en/19383362). She then joined the [University of Lille 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology) as Maître de conférences (MCF). In 2004 she earned her [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) degree from the University of Lille 1.[^o8]

In 2009 she became Professor of mathematics at the [Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850).[^o3]

She held visiting positions at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California). She visited the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge) as holder of a Simons Fellowship.[^o9] From 2013 to 2020 she chaired the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470)/European Women in Mathematics scientific panel of women mathematicians.[^o10][^o11]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2009, Druțu was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for her work in geometric group theory.[^o13]

In 2017, Druțu was awarded a Simons Visiting Fellowship.[^o9]

## Publications {#Publications}

### Selected contributions {#Selected_contributions}

- The [quasi-isometry](wikipedia://en/12747259) invariance of relative hyperbolicity; a characterization of [relatively hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/21993809) using geodesic triangles, similar to the one of [hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/3172817).
- A classification of relatively hyperbolic groups up to quasi-isometry; the fact that a group with a quasi-isometric embedding in a relatively hyperbolic metric space, with image at infinite distance from any peripheral set, must be relatively hyperbolic.
- The non-distortion of [horospheres](wikipedia://en/13487128) in [symmetric spaces](wikipedia://en/1642877) of non-compact type and in Euclidean [buildings](wikipedia://en/275015), with constants depending only on the [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332).
- The quadratic filling for certain [linear](wikipedia://en/3983656) [solvable groups](wikipedia://en/105012) (with uniform constants for large classes of such groups).
- A construction of a 2-generated recursively presented group with continuum many non-[homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) [asymptotic cones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_cone). Under the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705), a [finitely generated group](wikipedia://en/963042) may have at most continuum many non-homeomorphic asymptotic cones, hence the result is sharp.
- A characterization of [Kazhdan's property (T)](wikipedia://en/1362724) and of the [Haagerup property](wikipedia://en/15970956) using affine isometric actions on [median spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_space).
- A study of generalizations of Kazhdan's property (T) for [uniformly convex](wikipedia://en/9133418) [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989).
- A proof that [random groups](wikipedia://en/31868831) satisfy strengthened versions of Kazhdan's property (T) for high enough density; a proof that for random groups the [conformal dimension](wikipedia://en/14889378) of the boundary is connected to the maximal value of *p* for which the groups have fixed point properties for isometric affine actions on [$L^p$ spaces](wikipedia://en/45194).
