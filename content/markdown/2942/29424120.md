# Tamás Hausel

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Tamás Hausel"}

**Tamás Hausel**[^o5] (born 1972) is a Hungarian mathematician working in the areas of [combinatorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry), [differential](wikipedia://en/8625) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).[^o2][^o6] More specifically the [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498), geometry, topology and arithmetic of [hyperkähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/646120), [Yang–Mills instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang–Mills_instanton), non-Abelian [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590), [Geometric Langlands program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_Langlands_program), and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [quivers](wikipedia://en/355100) and [Kac–Moody algebras](wikipedia://en/1146292).[^o7]

:::

Hausel is currently associated with the [Institute of Science and Technology Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Science_and_Technology_Austria) (IST) where he has been a full professor since 2016.[^o8] Prior to joining IST he was a professor at [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL). He was previously at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), both a [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) University [Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Fellow) at the university's mathematical institute, and a Tutorial Fellow in Mathematics at [Wadham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College,_Oxford). Previous to that, Hausel was an assistant and then associate professor at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2008, Hausel was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for his investigations into hyperkähler geometry which have led him to prove deep results in fields as diverse as the representation theory of quivers, mirror symmetry and Yang–Mills instantons.[^o10]
