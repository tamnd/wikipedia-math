# Francesco Gerbaldi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Gerbaldi"}

*Image: Francesco Gerbaldi - Superficie di Steiner.jpg.*

**Francesco Gerbaldi** (29 July 1858, La Spezia, [Kingdom of Sardinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia) – 29 June 1934, Pavia, Italy) was an Italian [geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer), who proved [Gerbaldi's theorem](wikipedia://en/31737114).[^o2]

:::

Gerbaldi studied at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) with *laurea* in 1879 and then became there an assistant to [Enrico D'Ovidio](wikipedia://en/55588432). After further study in Germany, he was in Pavia and Rome before being appointed a professor at the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo). He was a colleague of [Giovanni Guccia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Guccia), the founder of the Circolo Matematico di Palermo. Guccia and Gerbaldi enhanced the reputation of the Faculty of Mathematics of the University of Palermo by recruiting [Giuseppe Bagnera](wikipedia://en/55832077), [Michele De Franchis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_De_Franchis), [Pasquale Calapso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasquale_Calapso) and [Michele Cipolla](wikipedia://en/25816120). In 1908 Gerbaldi moved to the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia), where he remained until his retirement in 1931 due to ill health.

He is known for what is now called Gerbaldi's theorem, the construction of six pairwise apolar linearly independent nondegenerate ternary quadratic forms (the lowest dimensional [quadrics](wikipedia://en/145570)).[^o3] Later he studied the symmetry group of these six quadrics and found that it is isomorphic to the [Valentiner group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentiner_group).[^o4]

In 1897 in Zürich, Gerbaldi was an Invited Speaker at the first [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o5]
