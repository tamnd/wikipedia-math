# Connecticut Death Index

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Connecticut Death Index"}

The **Connecticut Death Index** is maintained by the [Connecticut Department of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Department_of_Public_Health) and list all people who died in [Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut) starting in 1949. In 2011 the state switched to an online system for recording deaths to replace the hand written [death certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_certificate).[^o2]

:::
