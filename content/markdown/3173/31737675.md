::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Omnitruncated 5-simplex honeycomb"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **omnitruncated 5-simplex honeycomb** or **omnitruncated hexateric honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is composed entirely of [omnitruncated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_5-simplex) facets.

:::

The facets of all [omnitruncated simplectic honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_simplectic_honeycomb) are called [permutahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutahedron) and can be positioned in *n+1* space with integral coordinates, permutations of the whole numbers (0,1,..,n).

## A<sub>5</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice {#A5*_lattice}

The A\* lattice (also called A6) is the union of six [A<sub>5</sub> lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A5_lattice), and is the dual [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) to the *omnitruncated 5-simplex honeycomb*, and therefore the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of this lattice is an [omnitruncated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_5-simplex).
