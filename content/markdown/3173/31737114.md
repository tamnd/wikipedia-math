# Gerbaldi's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerbaldi's theorem"}

In [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849), **Gerbaldi's theorem**, proved by (txt), states that one can find six pairwise apolar [linearly independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linearly_independent) nondegenerate ternary [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478). These are permuted by the [Valentiner group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentiner_group).

:::
