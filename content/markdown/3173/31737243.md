In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **cyclotruncated 5-simplex honeycomb** or **cyclotruncated hexateric honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is composed of [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), [truncated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_5-simplex), and [bitruncated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-simplex) facets in a ratio of 1:1:1.

:::

## Structure {#Structure}

Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an elongated 5-cell antiprism, two parallel [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) in dual configurations, connected by 10 tetrahedral pyramids (elongated 5-cells) from the cell of one side to a point on the other. The vertex figure has 8 vertices and 12 5-cells.

It can be constructed as six sets of parallel [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862) that divide space. The hyperplane intersections generate [cyclotruncated 5-cell honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclotruncated_5-cell_honeycomb) divisions on each hyperplane.
