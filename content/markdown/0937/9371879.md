# Burt Kaliski

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Burt Kaliski"}

**Burton S. "Burt" Kaliski, Jr.** is a cryptographer, who is currently the [chief technology officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_technology_officer) (CTO) and senior vice president at [Verisign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verisign).[^o3] Before joining Verisign in 2011, he was the founding director of the EMC Innovation Network at [EMC Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMC_Corporation) since its 2006 acquisition of [RSA Security](wikipedia://en/301240) where he was Chief Scientist for RSA Laboratories. His notable work includes the development of such [public key cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_key_cryptography) standards as [PKCS](wikipedia://en/616418)[^o4] and [IEEE P1363](wikipedia://en/1644154),[^o5] the extension of [linear cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/59469) to use multiple approximations, and the design of the [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) [Crab](wikipedia://en/7866313).

:::

Kaliski received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in computer science from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), where his research was on cryptography. He was a visiting assistant professor of computer engineering at [Rochester Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester_Institute_of_Technology) before joining RSA Security.[^o7]

He was also a guest professor and member of the international advisory board of [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), School of Software and Microelectronics.[^o7]
