# Victor Bangert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Bangert"}

**Victor Bangert** (born 28 November 1950) is Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the Mathematisches Institut in [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). His main interests are [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632). He specialises in the theory of [closed geodesics](wikipedia://en/19903176), wherein one of his significant results, combined with another one due to [John Franks](http://www.math.northwestern.edu/people/faculty/john-franks.html), implies that every [Riemannian](wikipedia://en/144652) 2-sphere possesses infinitely many closed geodesics. He also made important contributions to [Aubry–Mather theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aubry–Mather_theory).

:::

He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Universität Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Dortmund) in 1977 under the supervision of [Rolf Wilhelm Walter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Wilhelm_Walter), with the thesis *Konvexität in riemannschen Mannigfaltigkeiten*.

He served in the editorial board of "[manuscripta mathematica](https://link.springer.com/journal/229)" from 1996 to 2017.

Bangert was an [invited speaker at the 1994 International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich).[^o4]
