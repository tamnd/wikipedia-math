# List of letters used in mathematics, science, and engineering

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of letters used in mathematics, science, and engineering"}

[Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_letters) and [Greek letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_letters) are used in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), and other areas where mathematical notation is used as symbols for [constants](wikipedia://en/48779551), [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), and also conventionally for [variables](wikipedia://en/3728109) representing certain quantities.

:::
