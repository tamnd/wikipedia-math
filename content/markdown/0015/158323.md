# Landen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Landen"}

**Landen** () is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium) and [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Belgium)[^o3] located in the [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) province of [Flemish Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Brabant). The municipality comprises the city of Landen proper and the villages of Attenhoven, [Eliksem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliksem), Ezemaal, Laar, Neerlanden, [Neerwinden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neerwinden), Overwinden, Rumsdorp, Waasmont, Walsbets, Walshoutem, Wange and [Wezeren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wezeren). On January 1, 2006, Landen had a total population of 14,682. The total area is 54.05 km² which gives a [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) of 272 inhabitants per km².

:::

## History {#History}

Landen is the birthplace of [Pippin of Landen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pippin_of_Landen), and his daughter, [Saint Gertrude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gertrude_of_Nivelles), is the city's patron Saint.

On the territory of the municipality two great battles were fought. The [first was fought in 1693](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Landen), during the [Nine Years' War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Years'_War) between the Anglo-Allied army under [William III of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_III_of_England) and the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) under the [Duke of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/François-Henri_de_Montmorency,_duc_de_Luxembourg), ending in a French victory. The second [Battle of Neerwinden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Neerwinden_%281793%29) took place during the [French Revolutionary Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_First_Coalition) between the [Austrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) under Prince [Josias of Coburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josias_of_Coburg) and the French under General [Dumouriez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_François_Dumouriez) and ended in an Austrian victory (these battles are commonly referred to as the Battles of Neerwinden). Within the municipal boundaries, one can find multiple Roman tombs.
