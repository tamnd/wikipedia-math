# Noetherian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noetherian"}

In mathematics, the [adjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective) **Noetherian** is used to describe [objects](wikipedia://en/5869#Categories.2C_objects.2C_and_morphisms) that satisfy an [ascending or descending chain condition](wikipedia://en/3189) on certain kinds of subobjects, meaning that certain ascending or descending sequences of subobjects must have finite length. Noetherian objects are named after [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896), who was the first to study the ascending and descending chain conditions for rings. Specifically:

:::

- [Noetherian group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_group), a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) that satisfies the ascending chain condition on [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011).
- [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129), a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) that satisfies the ascending chain condition on [ideals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_of_a_ring).
- [Noetherian module](wikipedia://en/542431), a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) that satisfies the ascending chain condition on submodules.
- More generally, an object in a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) is said to be Noetherian if there is no infinitely increasing filtration of it by subobjects. A category is Noetherian if every object in it is Noetherian.
- [Noetherian relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_relation), a [binary relation](wikipedia://en/3931) that satisfies the ascending chain condition on its elements.
- [Noetherian topological space](wikipedia://en/2936080), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) that satisfies the descending chain condition on [closed sets](wikipedia://en/47279).
- [Noetherian induction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_induction), also called well-founded induction, a proof method for binary relations that satisfy the descending chain condition.
- Noetherian rewriting system, an [abstract rewriting system](wikipedia://en/23947591) that has no infinite chains.
- [Noetherian scheme](wikipedia://en/2783891), a [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) that admits a finite covering by open [spectra](wikipedia://en/37291) of Noetherian rings.
