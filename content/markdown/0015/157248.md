# Elmer Rees

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elmer Rees"}

**Elmer Gethin Rees** (19 November 1941 – 4 October 2019[^o3][^o4]) was a [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_people) mathematician with publications in areas ranging from [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560) to [robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotics). He held the post of Director of the [Heilbronn Institute for Mathematical Research](wikipedia://en/61922775), a partnership between the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) and the British [signals intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) [agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_agency) [GCHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters), from its creation in 2005 until 2009.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rees was born in [Llandybie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llandybie) and grew up in [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales). He studied at [St Catharine's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catharine's_College,_Cambridge) gaining a [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) before moving on to the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), where he completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1967. His thesis on *Projective Spaces and Associated Maps*, was written under the supervision of [David B. A. Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_B._A._Epstein).

Rees's career had taken him to [University of Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), [Swansea University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swansea_University) and [St Catherine's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catherine's_College,_Oxford), before becoming a professor at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 1979, where he remained until retiring from the post in 2005.

He was elected as a fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1982. One of his most notable legacies was the establishment of the [International Centre for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/8298904).

Rees was appointed [Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (CBE) in the [2009 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Birthday_Honours).

While at the universities of Oxford and Edinburgh, he supervised at least 15 PhD students,[^o8] including Anthony Bahri, John D. S. Jones, Gregory Lupton, Jacob Mostovoy, Simon Willerton and Richard Hepworth.
