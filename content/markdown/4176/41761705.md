# Squeaky Dolphin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Squeaky Dolphin"}

**Squeaky Dolphin** is a program developed by the [Government Communications Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters) (GCHQ), a British [intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_agency) and [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_agency) organization, to collect and analyze data from [social media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media) networks. The program was first revealed to the general public on [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) on 27 January 2014 based on documents previously leaked by [Edward Snowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Snowden).[^o2]

:::

## Scope of surveillance {#Scope_of_surveillance}

According to a document of the GCHQ dated August 2012, the program enables broad, [real-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real-time_data) surveillance of the following items:

- [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) video views[^o2][^o4]
- The [Like button](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Like_button) on [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook).[^o4] Facebook has since then encrypted the data.[^o2]
- [Blogspot/Blogger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blogger_%28service%29) visits[^o2][^o4]
- [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter),[^o5] which has however encrypted its communications since this presentation was made[^o6]

The program can be supplemented with commercially available analytic software to determine which videos are popular among residents of specific cities.[^o2] The dashboard software chosen was made by [Splunk](wikipedia://en/19135734).[^o7]

The presentation, which was originally shown to an [NSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSA) audience and was made public by the NBC, contains a note saying the program was "Not interested in individuals just broad trends!". However, "according to other Snowden documents" obtained by NBC, in 2010, "GCHQ exploited unencrypted data from Twitter to identify specific users around the world and target them with propaganda."[^o2]
