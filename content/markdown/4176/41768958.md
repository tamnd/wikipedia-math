# George E. Collins

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George E. Collins"}

**George E. Collins** (born on January 10, 1928 in [Stuart, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart,_Iowa) – and died on November 21, 2017 in [Madison, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison,_Wisconsin))[^o3] was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). He is the inventor of [garbage collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garbage_collection_%28computer_science%29) by [reference counting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_counting)G60 and of the method of [quantifier elimination](wikipedia://en/2150441) by [cylindrical algebraic decomposition](wikipedia://en/12218154).G75

:::

He received his PhD from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1955.[^o4] He worked at [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) (1966–1986) [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), [RISC-Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Institute_for_Symbolic_Computation), [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware), and [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University).[^o3]
