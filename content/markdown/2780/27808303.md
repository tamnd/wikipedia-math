::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 7-simplexes"}

In seven-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 7-simplex** is a convex [uniform 7-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821719), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex).

:::

There are four unique degrees of rectifications, including the zeroth, the 7-simplex itself. Vertices of the *rectified 7-simplex* are located at the edge-centers of the *7-simplex*. Vertices of the *birectified 7-simplex* are located in the triangular face centers of the *7-simplex*. Vertices of the *trirectified 7-simplex* are located in the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cell centers of the *7-simplex*.

The rectified 7-simplex is the [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) of the [2<sub>51</sub> honeycomb](wikipedia://en/19550444). It is called **0<sub>5,1</sub>** for its branching Coxeter-Dynkin diagram, shown as .

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as S1.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Rectified octaexon (Acronym: roc) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *rectified 7-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 8-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,0,0,1,1). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [rectified 8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_8-orthoplex).

The *trirectified 7-simplex* is the [intersection](wikipedia://en/23476429) of two regular [7-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex) in [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) configuration.

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as S3.

This polytope is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [1<sub>33</sub> honeycomb](wikipedia://en/19593765). It is called **0<sub>3,3</sub>** for its branching Coxeter-Dynkin diagram, shown as .

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Hexadecaexon (Acronym: he) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *trirectified 7-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 8-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,1,1,1,1). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [trirectified 8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_8-orthoplex).

The *trirectified 7-simplex* is the [intersection](wikipedia://en/23476429) of two regular 7-simplices in [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) configuration. This characterization yields simple coordinates for the vertices of a trirectified 7-simplex in 8-space: the 70 distinct permutations of (1,1,1,1,−1,−1,−1,−1).
