# Truncated hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated hexagonal tiling"}

Uniform tiling stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated hexagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). There are 2 [dodecagons](wikipedia://en/508486) (12-sides) and one [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417).

:::

As the name implies this tiling is constructed by a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) operation applied to a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719), leaving dodecagons in place of the original [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733), and new triangles at the original vertex locations. It is given an extended [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *t*{6,3}.

[Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls it a **truncated hextille**, constructed as a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) operation applied to a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) (hextille).

There are 3 [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Euclidean_tilings) and 8 [semiregular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_uniform_tilings) in the plane.

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

*Image: Contracted truncated hexagonal tilings.png.* A truncated hexagonal tiling can be contracted in one dimension, reducing dodecagons into decagons. Contracting in second direction reduces decagons into octagons. Contracting a third time make the [trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864397).

### Wythoff constructions from hexagonal and triangular tilings {#Wythoff_constructions_from_hexagonal_and_triangular_tilings}

Like the [uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2537223) there are eight [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/12126635) that can be based from the regular hexagonal tiling (or the dual [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858)).

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms, 7 which are topologically distinct. (The *truncated triangular tiling* is topologically identical to the hexagonal tiling.)

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (3.2n.2n), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.

### Circle packing {#Circle_packing}

The truncated hexagonal tiling can be used as a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665), placing equal diameter circles at the center of every point.[^o13] Every circle is in contact with 3 other circles in the packing ([kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555)). This is the lowest density packing that can be created from a uniform tiling.

### Triakis triangular tiling {#Triakis_triangular_tiling}

*Image: Wallpaper group-p6m-6.jpg.* On painted [porcelain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porcelain), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)

The **triakis triangular tiling** is a tiling of the Euclidean plane. It is an equilateral [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) with each triangle divided into three obtuse triangles (angles 30-30-120) from the center point. It is labeled by [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V3.12.12 because each isosceles triangle face has two types of vertices: one with 3 triangles, and two with 12 triangles.

[Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls it a **kisdeltille**,[^o17] constructed as a [kis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator) operation applied to a [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) (deltille).

In Japan the pattern is called **asanoha** for *hemp leaf*, although the name also applies to other triakis shapes like the [triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/722873) and [triakis octahedron](wikipedia://en/722549).[^o18]

It is the dual tessellation of the truncated hexagonal tiling which has one triangle and two dodecagons at each vertex.

*Image: P4 dual.png.*

It is one of eight [edge tessellations](wikipedia://en/52044791), tessellations generated by reflections across each edge of a prototile.[^o20]

#### Related duals to uniform tilings {#Related_duals_to_uniform_tilings}

It is one of 7 dual uniform tilings in hexagonal symmetry, including the regular duals.
