# Lorentz Eichstadt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lorentz Eichstadt"}

*Image: Lorenz Eichstaedt.jpg.* Lorentz Eichstadt.

**Lorentz Eichstadt** (10 August 1596 – 8 June 1660) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer). He was a doctor of medicine in [Szczecin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szczecin) in [Pomerania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomerania) and taught medicine and mathematics in [Danzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danzig).[^o2]

:::

The lunar crater [Eichstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eichstadt_%28crater%29) is named after him.[^o3]
