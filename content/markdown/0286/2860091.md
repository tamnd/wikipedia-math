# Pentagonal antiprism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal antiprism"}

Prism polyhedron stat table

*Image: Pentagonal antiprism.stl.* 3D model of a uniform pentagonal antiprism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal antiprism** is the third in an infinite set of [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms) formed by an even-numbered sequence of triangle sides closed by two polygon caps. It consists of two [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) joined to each other by a ring of ten [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) for a total of twelve faces. Hence, it is a non-regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Parabidiminished icosahedron"}

## Geometry {#Geometry}

If the faces of the pentagonal antiprism are all regular, it is a [semiregular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/1548894). It can also be considered as a **parabidiminished [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084)**, a shape formed by removing two [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461) from a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) leaving two nonadjacent pentagonal faces; a related shape, the [metabidiminished icosahedron](wikipedia://en/1123562) (one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034)), is likewise form from the icosahedron by removing two pyramids, but its pentagonal faces are adjacent to each other. The two pentagonal faces of either shape can be augmented with pyramids to form the icosahedron.

The semiregular pentagonal antiprism is inscribed in a cylinder whose bases are the disks in which the pentagonal faces are inscribed. If this polygon is projected radially onto a sphere and spherical trigonometry used to solve for the angular measures of the edges, the result is the arctangent of 2, matching the regular icosahedron; this implies that the radius of the cylinder equals its height.

### Relation to polytopes {#Relation_to_polytopes}

The pentagonal antiprism occurs as a constituent element in some higher-dimensional [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471). Two rings of ten pentagonal antiprisms each bound the hypersurface of the four-dimensional [grand antiprism](wikipedia://en/4058047). If these antiprisms are augmented with pentagonal prism pyramids and linked with rings of five tetrahedra each, the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) is obtained.

## Crossed antiprism {#Crossed_antiprism}

A crossed pentagonal antiprism is topologically identical to the *pentagonal antiprism*, although it can't be made uniform. The sides are [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428). It has D<sub>5h</sub> symmetry group of order 20. Its [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) is 3.3/2.3.5, with one triangle retrograde and its [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is the same as a [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659).
