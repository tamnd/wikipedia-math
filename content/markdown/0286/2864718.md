# Snub trihexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub trihexagonal tiling"}

Uniform tiling stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub hexagonal tiling** (or *snub trihexagonal tiling*) is a [semiregular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_tiling) of the Euclidean plane. There are four triangles and one hexagon on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) *sr{3,6}*. The [snub tetrahexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38257752) is a related hyperbolic tiling with Schläfli symbol *sr{4,6}*.

:::

[Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls it a **snub hextille**, constructed as a [snub](wikipedia://en/9428917) operation applied to a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) (hextille).

There are three [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Euclidean_tilings) and eight [semiregular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_uniform_tilings) in the plane. This is the only one which does not have a reflection as a symmetry.

There is only one [uniform coloring](wikipedia://en/18624814) of a snub trihexagonal tiling. (Labeling the colors by numbers, "3.3.3.3.6" gives "11213".)

## Circle packing {#Circle_packing}

The snub trihexagonal tiling leads to a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665), each vertex becoming the center of a circle of fixed diameter. Every circle is in contact with 5 other circles in the packing ([kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555)).[^o3] The lattice domain (red rhombus) repeats 6 distinct circles. The hexagonal gaps can be filled by exactly one circle, leading to the densest packing from the [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858#circle_packing).

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

*Image: 2-Uniform Tiling 20 Colored by Regular Polygon Orbits.svg.* There is one related [2-uniform tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling), which mixes the [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) 3.3.3.3.6 of the snub trihexagonal tiling and 3.3.3.3.3.3 of the [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858).

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This semiregular tiling is a member of a sequence of [snubbed](wikipedia://en/9428917) polyhedra and tilings with vertex figure (3.3.3.3.*n*) and [Coxeter–Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/5358554) . These figures and their duals have (n32) rotational [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678), being in the Euclidean plane for n=6, and hyperbolic plane for any higher n. The series can be considered to begin with n=2, with one set of faces degenerated into [digons](wikipedia://en/5442728).

## 6-fold pentille tiling {#6-fold_pentille_tiling}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **6-fold pentille** or **floret pentagonal tiling** is a dual semiregular tiling of the Euclidean plane.[^o8] It is one of the 15 known [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [pentagon tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_tiling). Its six pentagonal tiles radiate out from a central point, like petals on a [flower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flower).[^o9] Each of its pentagonal [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) has four 120° and one 60° angle.

It is the dual of the uniform snub trihexagonal tiling, and has [rotational symmetries of orders 6-3-2](wikipedia://en/2868583#Wallpaper_groups) symmetry.

### Variations {#Variations}

The floret pentagonal tiling has geometric variations with unequal edge lengths and rotational symmetry, which is given as monohedral [pentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/4850275#Monohedral_convex_pentagonal_tilings) type 5. In one limit, an edge-length goes to zero and it becomes a [deltoidal trihexagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deltoidal_trihexagonal_tiling).

### Fractalization {#Fractalization}

Replacing every V3<sup>6</sup> hexagon by a [rhombitrihexagon](wikipedia://en/2864311) furnishes a 6-uniform tiling, two vertices of 4.6.12 and two vertices of 3.4.6.4.

Replacing every V3<sup>6</sup> hexagon by a [truncated hexagon](wikipedia://en/2864030) furnishes a 8-uniform tiling, five vertices of 3<sup>2</sup>.12, two vertices of 3.4.3.12, and one vertex of 3.4.6.4.

Replacing every V3<sup>6</sup> hexagon by a [truncated trihexagon](wikipedia://en/2864170) furnishes a 15-uniform tiling, twelve vertices of 4.6.12, two vertices of 3.4<sup>2</sup>.6, and one vertex of 3.4.6.4.

In each fractal tiling, every vertex in a floret pentagonal domain is in a different orbit since there is no chiral symmetry (the domains have 3:2 side lengths of $1+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}:2+\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$ in the rhombitrihexagonal; $1+\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}:2+\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}}$ in the truncated hexagonal; and $1+\sqrt{3}:2+2\sqrt{3}$ in the truncated trihexagonal).
