# Snub square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub square tiling"}

Uniform tiling stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub square tiling** is a [semiregular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_tiling) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). There are three triangles and two squares on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). Its [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) is *s{4,4}*.

:::

[Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls it a **snub quadrille**, constructed by a [snub](wikipedia://en/9428917) operation applied to a [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) (quadrille).

There are 3 [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Euclidean_tilings) and 8 [semiregular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_uniform_tilings) in the plane.

## Circle packing {#Circle_packing}

The snub square tiling can be used as a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665), placing equal diameter circles at the center of every point. Every circle is in contact with 5 other circles in the packing ([kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555)).[^o9]

## Wythoff construction {#Wythoff_construction}

The **snub square tiling** can be [constructed](wikipedia://en/2950777) as a [snub](wikipedia://en/9428917) operation from the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809), or as an [alternate truncation](wikipedia://en/4349117) from the [truncated square tiling](wikipedia://en/2864244).

An alternate truncation deletes every other vertex, creating a new triangular faces at the removed vertices, and reduces the original faces to half as many sides. In this case starting with a *truncated square tiling* with 2 [octagons](wikipedia://en/314575) and 1 [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) per vertex, the octagon faces into squares, and the square faces degenerate into edges and 2 new triangles appear at the truncated vertices around the original square.

If the original tiling is made of regular faces the new triangles will be isosceles. Starting with octagons which alternate long and short edge lengths, derived from a regular [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486), will produce a snub tiling with perfect equilateral triangle faces.
