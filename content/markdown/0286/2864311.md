# Rhombitrihexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombitrihexagonal tiling"}

Uniform tiling stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombitrihexagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). There are one [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654), two [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), and one [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of rr{3,6}.

:::

[John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) calls it a **rhombihexadeltille**.[^o2] It can be considered a [cantellated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) by [Norman Johnson's](wikipedia://en/715527) terminology or an [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) by [Alicia Boole Stott](wikipedia://en/3325459)'s operational language.

There are three [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Euclidean_tilings) and eight [semiregular tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_uniform_tilings) in the plane.

## Uniform colorings {#Uniform_colorings}

There is only one [uniform coloring](wikipedia://en/18624814) in a rhombitrihexagonal tiling. (Naming the colors by indices around a vertex (3.4.6.4): 1232.)

With edge-colorings there is a half symmetry form (3\*3) [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678). The hexagons can be considered as truncated triangles, t{3} with two types of edges. It has [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) s<sub>2</sub>{3,6}. The bicolored square can be distorted into [isosceles trapezoids](wikipedia://en/511691). In the limit, where the rectangles degenerate into edges, a [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) results, constructed as a snub triangular tiling, .

## Related tilings {#Related_tilings}

*Image: Circular\_rhombitrihexagonal\_tilng.svg.* The tiling can be replaced by circular edges, centered on the hexagons as an [overlapping circles grid](wikipedia://en/48441511). In [quilting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quilting) it is called *Jacks chain*.

There is one related [2-uniform tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-uniform_tiling), having hexagons dissected into six triangles.[^o12][^o13] The *rhombitrihexagonal tiling* is also related to the [truncated trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864170) by replacing some of the hexagons and surrounding squares and triangles with dodecagons:

### Circle packing {#Circle_packing}

The rhombitrihexagonal tiling can be used as a [circle packing](wikipedia://en/16879665), placing equal diameter circles at the center of every point. Every circle is in contact with four other circles in the packing ([kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555)).[^o16] The translational lattice domain (red rhombus) contains six distinct circles.

*Image: 1-uniform-6-circlepack.svg.*

### Wythoff construction {#Wythoff_construction}

There are eight [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/12126635) that can be based from the regular hexagonal tiling (or the dual [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858)).

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are eight forms, seven topologically distinct. (The *truncated triangular tiling* is topologically identical to the hexagonal tiling.)

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of [cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) polyhedra with vertex figure (3.4.n.4), and continues as tilings of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742). These [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) figures have (\*n32) reflectional [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678).

### Deltoidal trihexagonal tiling {#Deltoidal_trihexagonal_tiling}

*Image: Aperiodic monotile smith 2023.svg.* A 2023 discovered aperiodic monotile, solving the [Einstein problem](wikipedia://en/43542926), is composed by a collection of 8 kites from the deltoidal trihexagonal tiling

The **deltoidal trihexagonal tiling** is a dual of the semiregular tiling known as the rhombitrihexagonal tiling. [Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) called it a **tetrille**.[^o2] The edges of this tiling can be formed by the intersection overlay of the regular [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) and a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719). Each [kite](wikipedia://en/84840) face of this tiling has angles 120°, 90°, 60° and 90°. It is one of only eight tilings of the plane in which every edge lies on a line of symmetry of the tiling.[^o22]

The **deltoidal trihexagonal tiling** is a dual of the semiregular tiling rhombitrihexagonal tiling.[^o23] Its faces are deltoids or [kites](wikipedia://en/84840).

*Image: P5 dual.svg.*

#### Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

It is one of seven dual uniform tilings in hexagonal symmetry, including the regular duals.

This tiling has [face transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_transitive) variations, that can distort the kites into bilateral trapezoids or more general quadrilaterals. Ignoring the face colors below, the fully symmetry is p6m, and the lower symmetry is p31m with three mirrors meeting at a point, and threefold rotation points.[^o25]

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of tilings with [face configurations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V3.4.n.4, and continues as tilings of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742). These [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) figures have (\*n32) reflectional [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678).

#### Other deltoidal (kite) tiling {#Other_deltoidal_(kite)_tiling}

Other deltoidal tilings are possible.

Point symmetry allows the plane to be filled by growing kites, with the topology as a [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809), V4.4.4.4, and can be created by crossing string of a [dream catcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_catcher). Below is an example with dihedral hexagonal symmetry.

Another [face transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_transitive) tiling with kite faces, also a topological variation of a square tiling and with [face configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_configuration) V4.4.4.4. It is also [vertex transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_transitive), with every vertex containing all orientations of the kite face.
