# Slutsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Slutsky"}

**Slutsky** is a [Belarusian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_language), [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language), and [Ashkenazi Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jewish_surnames) [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) that derived from [Slutsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slutsk) in [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus). The [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_ethnicity) family name 'Slutsky' is an Ashkenazized form of (סלוצקי; Слу́цкий; Слуцький) It is shared by the following people:

:::

- [Abram Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abram_Slutsky) (1898–1938), Jewish Ukrainian Soviet head of the foreign intelligence service (GUGB)
- [Allan Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Slutsky), known as "Dr. Licks" (born 1953, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania), a Jewish American arranger
- [Boris Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Slutsky) (1919, Slovyansk, Ukraine - 1986), Jewish Ukrainian-Soviet poet
- [Erik Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Slutsky) (born 1953), Jewish Canadian contemporary, figurative painter
- [Eugen Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugen_Slutsky) (also *Yevgeny Evgenievich Slutsky*, 1880–1948), Ukrainian-Russian/Soviet mathematical statistician, economist and political economist
  - [Slutsky equation](wikipedia://en/1838280)
  - [Slutsky's theorem](wikipedia://en/5213747)
- [Irina Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Slutsky), American internet video personality
- [Irina Slutskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Slutskaya), Russian figure skater
- [Leonid Slutsky (football manager)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Slutsky_%28football_manager%29) (born 1971), Russian association football manager
- [Leonid Slutsky (politician)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Slutsky_%28politician%29) (born 1968), member of the State Duma of Russia
- [Marc Slutsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Slutsky), drummer for [Splender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splender)
- Rachel Slutsky, A Social Worker and B'nai Mitzvah tutor at Anshe Sholom Bnai Israel
