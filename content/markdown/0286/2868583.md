# List of planar symmetry groups

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of planar symmetry groups"}

This article summarizes the classes of [discrete](wikipedia://en/56061) [symmetry groups](wikipedia://en/28186) of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). The symmetry groups are named here by three naming schemes: [International notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann–Mauguin_notation), [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678), and [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502). There are three kinds of symmetry groups of the plane:

:::

::: {#o2 .entry cue=list-entry name="Rosette groups"}

- 2 families of [rosette groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosette_groups) – 2D [point groups](wikipedia://en/1138942#Two_dimensions)

:::

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Frieze group"}

- 7 [frieze groups](wikipedia://en/375503) – 2D [line groups](wikipedia://en/32186226#Two-dimensional)

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Wallpaper group"}

- 17 [wallpaper groups](wikipedia://en/386214) – 2D [space groups](wikipedia://en/463721).

:::

## Wallpaper groups {#Wallpaper_groups}

The 17 [wallpaper groups](wikipedia://en/386214), with finite fundamental domains, are given by [International notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann–Mauguin_notation), [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678), and [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502), classified by the 5 [Bravais lattices](wikipedia://en/661808#Bravais_lattices_in_at_most_2_dimensions) in the plane: [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), oblique (parallelogrammatic), hexagonal (equilateral triangular), rectangular (centered rhombic), and rhombic (centered rectangular).

The *p1* and *p2* groups, with no reflectional symmetry, are repeated in all classes. The related pure reflectional [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) are given with all classes except oblique.
