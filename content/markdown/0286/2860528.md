# Pentagrammic prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagrammic prism"}

Prism star polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagrammic prism** is one of an infinite set of nonconvex [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) formed by square sides and two regular [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) caps, in this case two [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544).

:::

It is a special case of a right prism with a pentagram as base, which in general has rectangular non-base faces. Topologically it is the same as a convex [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659).

It is the 78th model in the [list of uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2850448), as the first representative of [uniform star prisms](wikipedia://en/11064788), along with the [pentagrammic antiprism](wikipedia://en/4157315), which is the 79th model.

## Geometry {#Geometry}

It has 7 faces, 15 edges and 10 vertices. This polyhedron is identified with the indexed name U<sub>78</sub> as a [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223).[^o3]

The pentagram face has an ambiguous interior because it is self-intersecting. The central pentagon region can be considered interior or exterior, depending on how the interior is defined. One definition of the interior is the set of points from which a [ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_%28geometry%29) crosses the boundary an odd number of times; this makes the central pentagon exterior, as every ray beginning within it crosses two edges.

## Pentagrammic dipyramid {#Pentagrammic_dipyramid}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagrammic [dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipyramid)** (or **bipyramid**) is first of the infinite set of [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) star dipyramids containing [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) arrangement of edges. It has 10 intersecting [isosceles triangle](wikipedia://en/315428) faces. It is topologically identical to the [pentagonal dipyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagonal_dipyramid).

Each star dipyramid is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of a star polygon based [uniform prism](wikipedia://en/283007).
