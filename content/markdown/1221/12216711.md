# Abel's binomial theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abel's binomial theorem"}

**Abel's binomial theorem**, named after [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573), is a [mathematical identity](wikipedia://en/298428) involving [sums](wikipedia://en/246160) of [binomial coefficients](wikipedia://en/4668). It states the following:

:::

$$\sum_{k=0}^m \binom{m}{k} (w+m-k)^{m-k-1}(z+k)^k=w^{-1}(z+w+m)^m.$$

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

### The case *m* = 2 {#The_case_m_=_2}

$$\begin{aligned} & {} \quad \binom{2}{0}(w+2)^1(z+0)^0+\binom{2}{1}(w+1)^0(z+1)^1+\binom{2}{2}(w+0)^{-1}(z+2)^2 \\ & = (w+2)+2(z+1)+\frac{(z+2)^2}{w} \\ & = \frac{(z+w+2)^2}{w}. \end{aligned}$$

:::
