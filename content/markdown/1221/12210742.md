# Uniform k 21 polytope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform k 21 polytope"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **uniform *k*<sub>21</sub> polytope** is a [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471) in *k* + 4 dimensions constructed from the [*E*<sub>*n*</sub>](wikipedia://en/5351858) [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004), and having only [regular polytope](wikipedia://en/518693) facets. The family was named by their [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) ***k*<sub>21</sub>** by its bifurcating [Coxeter–Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/5358554), with a single ring on the end of the *k*-node sequence.

:::

[Thorold Gosset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorold_Gosset) discovered this family as a part of his 1900 enumeration of the [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) and [semiregular polytopes](wikipedia://en/11684875), and so they are sometimes called **Gosset's semiregular figures**. Gosset named them by their dimension from 5 to 9, for example the *5-ic semiregular figure*.

## Family members {#Family_members}

The sequence as identified by Gosset ends as an infinite tessellation (space-filling honeycomb) in 8-space, called the [E8 lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359). (A final form was not discovered by Gosset and is called the **E9 lattice**: 6<sub>21</sub>. It is a tessellation of hyperbolic 9-space constructed of ∞ 9-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and ∞ 9-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets with all vertices at infinity.)

The family starts uniquely as [6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/26021427). The *triangular prism* and *rectified 5-cell* are included at the beginning for completeness. The *demipenteract* also exists in the [demihypercube](wikipedia://en/6833695) family.

They are also sometimes named by their symmetry group, like **E6 polytope**, although there are many [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) within the [*E*<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725) symmetry.

The complete family of Gosset semiregular polytopes are:

1. [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584): −1<sub>21</sub> (2 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 3 [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) faces)
1. [rectified 5-cell](wikipedia://en/2720430): 0<sub>21</sub>, *Tetroctahedric* (5 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) and 5 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) cells)
1. [demipenteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demipenteract): 1<sub>21</sub>, *5-ic semiregular figure* (16 [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) and 10 [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) facets)
1. [2 21 polytope](wikipedia://en/19232473): 2<sub>21</sub>, *6-ic semiregular figure* (72 5-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 27 5-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets)
1. [3 21 polytope](wikipedia://en/19236353): 3<sub>21</sub>, *7-ic semiregular figure* (576 6-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 126 6-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets)
1. [4 21 polytope](wikipedia://en/19236362): 4<sub>21</sub>, *8-ic semiregular figure* (17280 7-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and 2160 7-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets)
1. [5 21 honeycomb](wikipedia://en/26842937): 5<sub>21</sub>, *9-ic semiregular check* tessellates Euclidean 8-space (∞ 8-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and ∞ 8-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets)
1. [6 21 honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_21_honeycomb): 6<sub>21</sub>, tessellates hyperbolic 9-space (∞ 9-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and ∞ 9-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets)

Each polytope is constructed from (*n* − 1)-[simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) and (*n* − 1)-[orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) facets.

The orthoplex faces are constructed from the [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) *D*<sub>*n*−1</sub> and have a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {3<sup>1,*n*−1,1</sup>} rather than the regular {3<sup>*n*−2</sup>,4}. This construction is an implication of two "facet types". Half the facets around each orthoplex [ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridge_%28geometry%29) are attached to another orthoplex, and the others are attached to a simplex. In contrast, every simplex ridge is attached to an orthoplex.

Each has a [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) as the previous form. For example, the *rectified 5-cell* has a vertex figure as a *triangular prism*.
