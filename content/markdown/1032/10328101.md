# Hexacode

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hexacode"}

In [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869), the **hexacode** is a length 6 [linear code](wikipedia://en/1866743) of dimension 3 over the [Galois field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_field) $GF(4)=\{0,1,\omega,\omega^2\}$ of 4 elements defined by

:::

$$H=\{(a,b,c,f(1),f(\omega),f(\omega^2)) : f(x):=ax^2+bx+c; a,b,c\in GF(4)\}.$$

It is a 3-dimensional subspace of the [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) of dimension 6 over $GF(4)$. Then $H$ contains 45 codewords of [weight](wikipedia://en/1127884) 4, 18 codewords of weight 6 and the zero word. The full [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the hexacode is $3.A_6$.[^o2] The hexacode can be used to describe the [Miracle Octad Generator](wikipedia://en/27105153) of R. T. Curtis.
