# Institute for Mathematics and its Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute for Mathematics and its Applications"}

The **Institute for Mathematics and its Applications** located at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) is an organization established in 1982 by the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (NSF) of the United States.

:::

## Objectives {#Objectives}

The primary mission of the IMA is to increase the impact of mathematics by fostering interdisciplinary research and linking mathematics and scientific and technological problems from other disciplines and industry.

## Activities {#Activities}

The IMA hosts long-term visitors, funds [postdoctoral research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_research) positions, and holds several conferences annually. The NSF has granted the IMA \$19.5 million over the period 2005–2010, the largest single mathematics grant the NSF has ever awarded. <http://www1.umn.edu/umnnews/Feature_Stories/U_math_institute_recognized_for_international_excellence.html> <https://www.ams.org/mathmedia/mathdigest/md-200508-toc.html#200510-society> (2008-09-05). <https://web.archive.org/web/20080905204629/http://www.ams.org/mathmedia/mathdigest/md-200508-toc.html#200510-society>

## Prize in Mathematics and its Applications {#Prize_in_Mathematics_and_its_Applications}

The IMA annually awards a prize to a mathematician who has received their PhD within the last 10 years. This award recognizes an individual who has made a transformative impact on the mathematical sciences and their applications.[^o5]

- 2019: [Jacob Bedrossian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Bedrossian)
- 2018: [Anders C. Hansen](wikipedia://en/70583703)
- 2017: [Jianfeng Lu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jianfeng_Lu)
- 2016: [Rachel Ward](wikipedia://en/53724765) and [Deanna Needell](wikipedia://en/53725102)
- 2015: [Jonathan Weare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Weare)
- 2014: [David F. Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_F._Anderson)

## Organization with similar names {#Organization_with_similar_names}

Sharing a very similar name and the acronym IMA, it should not be confused with the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139), a professional body for mathematicians in the UK.
