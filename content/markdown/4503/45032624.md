# Chiu-Chu Melissa Liu

[Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_%28surname%29)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Chiu-Chu Melissa Liu"}

**Chiu-Chu Melissa Liu** (; born 15 December 1974) is a Taiwanese mathematician. She is a professor of mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). Her research interests include [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Liu was born on December 16, 1974, in Taiwan. She graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with her [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.S.) in mathematics in 1996 and earned her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2002 under the supervision of [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

After continuing at Harvard as a [Junior Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Society_of_Fellows), she took a faculty position at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), and moved to Columbia in 2006.[^o4]

Liu won the [Morningside Silver Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) in 2007.[^o4] She was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010.[^o4] In 2012, she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
