# Ricardo Baeza Rodríguez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ricardo Baeza Rodríguez"}

Baeza **Ricardo Baeza Rodríguez** is a [Chilean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works as a professor at the [University of Talca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Talca).[^o6][^o3] He earned his Ph.D. in 1970 from [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University), under the joint supervision of Robert W. Berger and Manfred Knebusch.[^o3][^o4] His research interest is in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o7]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Baeza became a member of the Chilean Academy of Sciences in 1983.[^o6] He was the 2009 winner of the Chilean [National Prize for Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Prize_for_Exact_Sciences_%28Chile%29).[^o3][^o7] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), the only Chilean to be so honored.[^o3][^o8]

## Research {#Research}

In 1990, Baeza proved the [norm theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_theorem) over [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) two; it had been previously proved in other characteristics.[^o10] The theorem states that if *q* is a nonsingular [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *F*, and $\pi(t)\in F[t]$ be a monic [irreducible polynomial](wikipedia://en/188725) (with $F(\pi) := F[t]/\pi(t)$ the corresponding [field extension](wikipedia://en/11634)), then $(\pi(t))\otimes q\cong q$ if and only if $q\otimes F(\pi)$ is hyperbolic.[^o10]

In 1992, Baeza and [Roberto Aravire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Aravire) introduced a modification of Milnor's [k-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) for [quadratic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_forms) over a field of characteristic two.[^o11] In particular, if $W_q(F)$ denotes the [Witt group](wikipedia://en/5578523) of quadratic forms over a field *F*, then one can construct a group $k_n(F)$ and an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) $s_n : h_n(F) \to I^{n - 1}W_q(F)/I^nW_q(F)$ for every value of *n*.[^o11]

In 2003, Baeza and Aravire studied quadratic forms and [differential forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_forms) over certain [function fields of an algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/2783843) of characteristic two.[^o12] Using this result, they deduced the characteristic two analogue of [Knebusch's degree conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knebusch's_degree_conjecture).[^o12]

In 2007, Baeza and Arason found a [group presentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_presentation) of the groups $I^n(K)\subset W(K)$, generated by *n*-fold bilinear [Pfister forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfister_forms), and of the groups $I^nW_q(K)\subset W_q(K)$, generated by quadratic Pfister forms.[^o13]
