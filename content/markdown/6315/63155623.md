# Janis Johnston

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Janis Johnston"}

**Janis E. Johnston** (born 1957) is an American statistician, sociologist, and book author known for her work on [permutation tests](wikipedia://en/2468117) in statistics. Johnston earned a Ph.D. in 2006 from [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University), and works as a social science analyst for the [Food and Nutrition Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Nutrition_Service) of the [United States Department of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Agriculture).

:::

Her books include:

- *A Chronicle of Permutation Statistical Methods: 1920–2000, and Beyond* (with Kenneth J. Berry and Paul W. Mielke Jr., Springer, 2014)
- *Inequality: Social Class and Its Consequences* (edited with D. Stanley Eitzen, Paradigm Publishers, 2007, and Routledge, 2015)
- *Permutation Statistical Methods: An Integrated Approach* (with Kenneth J. Berry and Paul W. Mielke Jr., Springer, 2016)
- *The Measurement of Association: A Permutation Statistical Approach* (with Kenneth J. Berry and Paul W. Mielke Jr., Springer, 2018)
- *A Primer of Permutation Statistical Methods* (with Kenneth J. Berry and Paul W. Mielke Jr., Springer, 2019)
