# Andrew M. Bruckner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew M. Bruckner"}

**Andrew Michael Bruckner** (born December 17, 1932) is an American retired [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his contributions to [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478).[^o3]

:::

He got his PhD in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (1959) on the dissertation *Minimal Superadditive Extensions of Superadditive Functions* advised by [John Green (mathematician)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Green_%28mathematician%29).[^o4] He joined the faculty at [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara). The "Andy Award" has been given annually in his name since 1978, to significant contributors to real analysis.[^o5][^o6]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]

## Books {#Books}

- *Differentiation of real functions* ([American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 1994)
- *Real analysis* (1997). With [Judith B. Bruckner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_B._Bruckner) and [Brian S. Thomson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_S._Thomson).[^o9]
- *Elementary real analysis* (2001). With B. Thomson and J. Bruckner.[^o9]
