# Kraków School of Mathematics and Astrology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kraków School of Mathematics and Astrology"}

The **Kraków School of Mathematics and Astrology** (krakowska szkoła matematyczna i astrologiczna) was an influential mid-to-late-15th-century group of mathematicians and astrologers at the [University of Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) (later *Jagiellonian University*).

:::

## Notable members {#Notable_members}

- [Jan of Głogów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_of_Głogów) (1445–1507), author of widely recognized mathematical and astrological tracts
- Marcin Biem (1470–1540), contributor to the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar)
- [Marcin Bylica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Bylica) of [Olkusz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olkusz) (1433–93), later court astrologer to King [Matthias Corvinus of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Corvinus_of_Hungary)
- [Albert Brudzewski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Brudzewski) (1446–1495), teacher to notable scholars active at European universities
- Marcin Król of [Żurawica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Żurawica) (1422–1460)
- [Nicolaus Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus) (1473–1543), student at Kraków in 1491–95
