# Mechthild Stoer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mechthild Stoer"}

**Mechthild Maria Stoer** is a German applied mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476) known for her work on the [minimum cut](wikipedia://en/3562453) problem and in [network design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_design). She is one of the namesakes of the [Stoer–Wagner algorithm](wikipedia://en/48751362) for minimum cuts, which she published with Frank Wagner in 1994.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Stoer was a master's student of [Martin Grötschel](wikipedia://en/55085835) at the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg) in Germany, receiving a diploma in 1987 with the thesis *Dekompositionstechniken beim Travelling Salesman Problem*. She continued working with Grötschel in Augsburg for a Ph.D.; her 1992 dissertation, *Design of Survivable Networks*, was also published by [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag) in the series [Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235) (vol. 1531, 1992). After completing her doctorate she worked for [Telenor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telenor) in Norway, focusing on the applications of [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555) in [telecommunication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunication).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Stoer's work on minimum cuts received the inaugural [European Symposium on Algorithms Test-of-Time Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Symposium_on_Algorithms#ESA_Test-of-Time_Award) in 2015. The award citation states that "The algorithm continues to be taught because of its elegance and used because of its efficiency and ease of implementation."
