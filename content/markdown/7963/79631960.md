# Guido Kings

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guido Kings"}

**Guido Kings** (1965)[^o3] is a German mathematician working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kings studied mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) between 1984 and 1989. He received his doctorate in 1994 and [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2000 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster). In 2001, he held a research professorship at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn. He has been a full professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) since 2001.[^o3]

Kings has done research in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), the theory of [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), [polylogarithms](wikipedia://en/482471), and [special values of *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882).[^o5]

He was the speaker of the research group *Algebraic Cycles and L-Functions* funded by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) (DFG).[^o6][^o7] Since 2012, he has been the speaker of the [collaborative research centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_Research_Centres) (SFB) *Höhere Invarianten – Wechselwirkungen zwischen Arithmetischer Geometrie und Globaler Analysis*, also funded by the DFG.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

Together with his coauthor [Annette Huber-Klawitter](wikipedia://en/32350426), Kings was the recipient of the award of the Universitätsgesellschaft Münster for young scientists in 1999.[^o10] Kings and Huber-Klawitter were also invited speakers at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing in 2002.[^o11][^o12]

Since 2012, he has been a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society).[^o13]

In 2025, Kings and his former student Johannes Sprang received the Frontiers of Science Award for their joint paper *Eisenstein-Kronecker classes, integrality of critical values of Hecke L-functions and p-adic interpolation*, which appeared in [Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)).[^o13][^o14][^o15]
