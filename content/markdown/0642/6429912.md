# Gisbert Wüstholz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gisbert Wüstholz"}

*Image: Gisbert Wuestholz.jpg.* Wüstholz at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2005

**Gisbert Wüstholz** (born 4 June 1948, in [Tuttlingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuttlingen), Germany) is a German mathematician internationally known for his fundamental contributions to number theory (in the field of [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698), [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558)) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gisbert Wüstholz was born in 1948 in [Tuttlingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuttlingen) and studied from 1967 to 1973 at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) where he finished his PhD under the supervision of [Theodor Schneider](wikipedia://en/10312621) in 1978.

## Career {#Career}

On the invitation of [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495), Wüstholz stayed for a year as a Postdoc at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), and then he got a Postdoc position at the [University of Wuppertal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wuppertal), where he worked with [Walter Borho](wikipedia://en/37392549) from 1979 till 1984, and then moved to Bonn to become associate professor at the newly founded [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960). From 1985 to 1987 he was full Professor for Mathematics at Wuppertal, and in 1987 elected for a chair in Mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). He founded the Zurich Graduate School in Mathematics in 2003, and served as the director since then until 2008. Since 2013 he is a professor emeritus at ETH Zurich.

He is Member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) (since 2000),[^o4] of the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) (since 2003),[^o5] of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (since 2008)[^o6] where he was chairman of the Mathematics Section from 2011 to 2013, and of the [European Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts) (since 2016).[^o7] From 1999 he was an Honorary Advisory Professor at the [Tongji University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongji_University), Shanghai. From 2011 he was Senator for Mathematics at the Leopoldina. He is an Honorary Professor at [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology), Austria (since 2017).

Wüstholz stayed for extended periods at a number of universities and research institutes, such as the [University of Michigan at Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) (1984,1988) and the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in Bures-sur-Yvette (1987). He was member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton (1986, 1990, 1994/95, 2011), in 1992 he was Visiting Fellow Commoner at [Trinity College in Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) for research projects with [Alan Baker](wikipedia://en/18950800), and visited in the following year the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in Berkeley (1993). He was frequently a guest at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) at Bonn, and the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematics and Physics (ESI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematical_Physics) at Vienna. Since 2015 he is staying as a guest at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich). In the academic year 2017/18 he was Senior Research Fellow at the [Freiburg Institute for Advanced Studies (FRIAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_Institute_for_Advanced_Studies).

Since 1980 Wüstholz has close connections to a number of universities in Asia: he stayed for a couple of months each at [Kyushu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyushu_University) at Fukuoka (1992), the Morningside Center of Mathematics of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) at Beijing, at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology) (1996, 1997, 2006, 2010) and at the [University of Hong Kong (HKU)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hong_Kong) (1999, 2011, 2012). Several visits took him to the Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM) (2010, 2017), to the [Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS)](wikipedia://en/16984) and to the [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) at Taipei (2009, 2013, 2016).

In 1986 Wüstholz delivered an [invited address](wikipedia://en/47738065#Speakers#1986,_Berkeley) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians (ICM)](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berkeley, in 1992 the Mordell Lecture in Cambridge, in 2001 the 13th Kuwait foundation lecture, an invited lecture at the Leonhard Euler Festival in St. Petersburg in 2007 on the occasion of the celebration of [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902)’s 300th birthday, and the Academy Lecture at [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) in 2008.

## Research {#Research}

Wüstholz's research interests are [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558), [Transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698), and [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590). Highlights of his scientific work are his [Analytic subgroup theorem](wikipedia://en/35708725),[^o9] his proof of the abelian analogue[^o10] of [Lindemann's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindemann's_theorem), his joint work with [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226)[^n1] on the [Schmidt subspace theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_subspace_theorem), his joint work with [David Masser](wikipedia://en/2172777) on isogeny estimates[^o12] for [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties), and his joint work[^n2] with [Alan Baker](wikipedia://en/18950800) on [linear forms in logarithms](wikipedia://en/28751817).
