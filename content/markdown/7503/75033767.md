# Lester Mackey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lester Mackey"}

**Lester Mackey** is an American computer scientist and statistician. He is a principal researcher at [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) and an adjunct professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). Mackey develops [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) methods, models, and theory for large-scale learning tasks driven by applications from climate forecasting, healthcare, and the social good. He was named a 2023 [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Mackey grew up on [Long Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island).[^o5] He has said that, as a teenager, the [Ross Mathematics Program](wikipedia://en/2410144#Ross_Mathematics_Program) in number theory introduced him to proof-based mathematics, where he learned about induction and rigorous proof.[^o5] He got his first taste of academic research at the [Research Science Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Science_Institute).[^o5] He joined [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) as an undergraduate student, where he earned his BSE in Computer Science. There he conducted research with [Maria Klawe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Klawe) and David Walker.[^o6] Mackey was a graduate student at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he earned a PhD in Computer Science (2012) and an MA in Statistics (2011).[^o3][^o7] At Berkeley, his dissertation, advised by [Michael I. Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_I._Jordan), included work on sparse principal components analysis (PCA) for gene expression modeling, low-rank matrix completion for recommender systems, robust matrix factorization for video surveillance, and concentration inequalities for matrices.[^o8] After Berkeley, he joined [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), first as a postdoctoral fellow working with [Emmanuel Candès](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Candès) and then as an assistant professor of statistics and, by courtesy, computer science. At Stanford, he created the Statistics for Social Good working group.[^o3]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

In 2016, Mackey joined [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research) as a researcher and was appointed as an adjunct professor at Stanford University. He was made a principal researcher in 2019.[^o3]

Mackey's early work developed a method to predict progression rates of people with [ALS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALS). He used the PRO-ACT database of clinical trial data and [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571) to predict disease prognosis.[^o3] He has also developed machine learning models for subseasonal climate and weather forecasting, to more accurately predict temperature and precipitation 2-6 weeks in advance.[^o3] His models outperform the operational, physics-based dynamical models used by the [United States Bureau of Reclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Bureau_of_Reclamation).[^o3]

- 2006 [Barry M. Goldwater Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_M._Goldwater_Scholarship)[^o13]
- 2007 [Computing Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing_Research_Association) Outstanding Undergraduate Award Winner[^o14]
- 2007 Moses Taylor Pyne Honor Prize[^o15]
- 2009 Second Place in the \$1 million [Netflix Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix_Prize) competition for collaborative filtering[^o16][^o17]
- 2010 Best Student Paper Award, [International Conference on Machine Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Conference_on_Machine_Learning)[^o18]
- 2012 First Place in the ALS Prediction Prize4Life Challenge for predicting Lou Gehrig's disease progression[^o19]
- 2019 Winner of [U.S. Bureau of Reclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Bureau_of_Reclamation)'s Subseasonal Climate Forecast Rodeo[^o20]
- 2022 Elected to the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344) Leadership Academy[^o21]
- 2022 Outstanding Paper Award, [NeurIPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NeurIPS)[^o22]
