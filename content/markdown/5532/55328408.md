# Order-6 apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 apeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-6 apeirogonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {∞,6}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual to this tiling represents the fundamental domains of \[∞,6\*\] symmetry, [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) \*∞∞∞∞∞∞ symmetry, a hexagonal domain with five ideal vertices.

*Image: H2chess 26ib.png.*

The order-6 apeirogonal tiling can be uniformly colored with 6 colored apeirogons around each vertex, and coxeter diagram: , except ultraparallel branches on the diagonals.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is also topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra and tilings with six faces per vertex, starting with the [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,6}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , with *n* progressing to infinity.
