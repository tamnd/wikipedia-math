# Franciszek Pokorny

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franciszek Pokorny"}

*Image: Franciszek Pokorny.jpg.* Franciszek Pokorny

Major **Franciszek Pokorny** (15 June 1891 in [Mosty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosty_u_Jablunkova), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) – 22 November 1966 in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh), [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland)[^o2]) was a [Polish Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Army) officer who, after [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), headed the Polish [General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff)'s [Cipher Bureau](wikipedia://en/625326) before Major (eventually, Lt. Col.) [Gwido Langer](wikipedia://en/2919669).

:::

## Life {#Life}

When the first [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) military [Enigma](wikipedia://en/9256)-[enciphered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encipher) messages were broadcast by [radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio) on 15 July 1928, the Cipher Bureau's German section attempted unsuccessfully to [decrypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decryption) them. Likely due to the successes of leading Polish [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) in breaking [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Russia) ciphers during the [Polish-Soviet War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish-Soviet_War) (1919–21), a secret [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology) course was conducted in 1929 at [Poznań University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań_University) for selected mathematics students with a knowledge of the [German language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language). Three participants in the course — [Marian Rejewski](wikipedia://en/4349420), [Jerzy Różycki](wikipedia://en/197851) and [Henryk Zygalski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henryk_Zygalski) — were, three years later, hired by the [Cipher Bureau](wikipedia://en/625326).[^o4]

Pokorny was the third man to lecture in the course, after engineer [Antoni Palluth](wikipedia://en/2294303) and then-Capt. [Maksymilian Ciężki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maksymilian_Ciężki).[^o4]

Franciszek Pokorny was a cousin of the outstanding [Austro-Hungarian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_Army) cryptologist during [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), [Hermann Pokorny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Pokorny).[^o4]
