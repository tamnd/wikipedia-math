# Circle-valued Morse theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Circle-valued Morse theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **circle-valued Morse theory** studies the topology of a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) by analyzing the [critical points](wikipedia://en/1951424) of smooth maps from the manifold to the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), in the framework of [Morse homology](wikipedia://en/2868120).[^o2] It is an important special case of [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515)'s [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560) of closed [one-forms](wikipedia://en/314692).[^o3]

:::

[Michael Hutchings](wikipedia://en/38294757) and Yi-Jen Lee have connected it to [Reidemeister torsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reidemeister_torsion) and [Seiberg–Witten theory](wikipedia://en/3526032).[^o4]
