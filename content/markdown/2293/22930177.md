# John Adrian Bondy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Adrian Bondy"}

**John Adrian Bondy** (born 1944) is a retired English mathematician, known for his work in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Bondy received his Ph.D. in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1969. His advisor was [Dominic Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Welsh). Between 1969 and 1994, Bondy was *Professor of Graph Theory* at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in Canada, and then, until his retirement, at [Université Lyon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Lyon_1) in France.[^o5] From 1976, he was managing editor, and, between 1979 and 2004, co-editor-in-chief (together with [U. S. R. Murty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U._S._R._Murty)) of [Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory,_Series_B).[^o6] Throughout his career, Bondy has (co-)authored over 100 publications[^o5][^o7] with 51 co-authors,[^o8] including the widely influential textbook *Graph Theory with Applications* (with [U. S. R. Murty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U._S._R._Murty)), and supervised 12 Ph.D. students.[^o4] His [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) is 1.[^o9]

Bondy was dismissed from his tenured position at the University of Waterloo in 1995, after 25 years in which he had been a major contributor to the renown of the University's Department of Combinatorics and Optimization.[^o10] The reasons for his dismissal centered on *"Bondy's acceptance of a teaching post in France, and the acceptability of someone who is on UW's faculty payroll holding a full-time job elsewhere."*[^o11] Protesting the decision, [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) returned his honorary doctorate to the University of Waterloo,[^o12] and [Vašek Chvátal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vašek_Chvátal) resigned from his position of *Adjunct Professor* at the Department of Combinatorics and Optimization.[^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Bondy has a passion for [photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photography), having taken pictures since his childhood. In the 1980s, he regularly exhibited his work, most notably at the *Kitchener-Waterloo Art Gallery* in [Kitchener, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchener,_Ontario). In 2010, Bondy founded the non-profit association *Mind's Eye*[^o15] with the aim *"to reflect upon the conceptual links between mathematics and photography."*[^o16] In 2012, he opened a photo gallery with the same name on the [Rue Saint-Jacques, Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rue_Saint-Jacques,_Paris). Between 2012 and 2022, *Mind's Eye / Galerie Adrian Bondy* has hosted 42 exhibitions with works of 27 artists, including [John Claridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Claridge) and [Jean Dréville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Dréville).[^o17]
