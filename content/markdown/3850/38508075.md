# Marc Voorhoeve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marc Voorhoeve"}

**Marc Voorhoeve** (5 April 1950, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam) – 7 October 2011, [Eindhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven)) was a Dutch mathematician who introduced the [Voorhoeve index](wikipedia://en/32394679) of a [complex function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_function) in 1976.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Marc studied at the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leiden) where he wrote a thesis on [exponential polynomials](wikipedia://en/9023027).[^o4] The Voorhoeve index is a result from this work.

He then worked at [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) and at *Philips Data Systems*, a division of [Philips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philips) that manufactured minicomputers.

From 1985 to 2011, he was assistant professor in [Kees van Hee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kees_van_Hee)'s group at [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology), which specialized in [business process modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_process_modeling) techniques based on sound mathematical principles, in particular [Petri nets](wikipedia://en/225192) and [process algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_algebra).[^o5]
