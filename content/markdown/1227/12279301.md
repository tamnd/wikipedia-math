# Erwin Engeler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erwin Engeler"}

*Image: ETH-BIB-Engeler,\_Erwin\_(1930-)-Portr\_08852.tif.* Erwin Engeler (ca. 1975)

**Erwin Engeler** (13 February 1930 – 28 July 2026) was a Swiss mathematician who did pioneering work on the interrelations between [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [scientific computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computation) in the 20th century. He was one of [Paul Bernays](wikipedia://en/2556580)' students at the [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

After completing his doctorate in 1958, Engeler spent fourteen years in the United States, teaching at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) and at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In 1959 he contributed an independent proof of several equivalent conditions to [omega-categoricity](wikipedia://en/26628083), an important concept in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). He returned to Switzerland in 1972, where he served as a professor of logic and computer science at the ETH until his retirement in 1997.

Engeler was named a [Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Association_for_Computing_Machinery) in 1995.

Engeler died on 28 July 2026, at the age of 96.[^o3]
