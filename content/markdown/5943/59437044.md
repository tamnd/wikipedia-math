# Paula Roberson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paula Roberson"}

**Paula Karen Roberson** is a [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878) at the [University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas_for_Medical_Sciences), where she chairs the department of biostatistics. Her research interests include the design of [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717), [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369), and [feature selection](wikipedia://en/1179950). She was president of the [Caucus for Women in Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucus_for_Women_in_Statistics) in 2015.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Roberson graduated from [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University) in 1974 with a bachelor's degree in mathematics and statistics. She completed her Ph.D. in [biomathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomathematics) at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1979. Her dissertation, *Distributional and Robustness Problems in Time-Space Disease Clustering*, was supervised by Lloyd Fisher. She joined the University of Arkansas faculty in 1993, and became the founding chair of the biostatistics department there in 2004.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Roberson became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2000. In 2014, she was elected as a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science). In 2022 Roberson was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).
