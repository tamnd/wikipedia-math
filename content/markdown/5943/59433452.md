# Thomas Ward (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Ward"}

**Thomas Boulton Ward** (born 3 October 1963) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and its relations to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ward was the fourth child of the physicist [Alan Howard Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Howard_Ward) and Elizabeth Honor Ward, a physics teacher. He attended Woodlands Primary School in [Lusaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusaka), [Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambia), [Waterford Kamhlaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterford_Kamhlaba) ([United World College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_World_Colleges)) in [Swaziland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eswatini), and (briefly) the [Thomas Hardye School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thomas_Hardye_School) in [Dorchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorchester,_Dorset), England. He studied mathematics at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) from 1982, gaining an MSc with dissertation entitled "Automorphisms of solenoids and *p*-adic entropy" in 1986 and a PhD with dissertation entitled "[Topological entropy and periodic points for ***Z****<sup>d</sup>* actions on compact abelian groups with the Descending Chain Condition](http://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/73322/)" in 1989, both under the supervision of [Klaus Schmidt](wikipedia://en/39443367).

## Career {#Career}

Ward worked at the [University of Maryland in College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), and the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia). In 2012 he moved to [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) as Pro-Vice-Chancellor for Education,[^o5] in 2016 to the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) as Deputy Vice-Chancellor for Student Education,[^o6] and to Newcastle University as Pro-Vice-Chancellor for Education from 2021 to 2023.[^o7] He served in editorial roles for the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) from 2002 to 2012 and was a managing editor of [Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergodic_Theory_and_Dynamical_Systems) from 2012 to 2014. He served on the [HEFCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Funding_Council_for_England) advisory committees for Widening Participation and Student Opportunity (2013–15) and Teaching Excellence and Student Opportunity (2015–17).[^o8]

## Works {#Works}

In 2012 Ward, along with [Graham Everest](wikipedia://en/42017535) (posthumously) was awarded the [Paul R. Halmos - Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_R._Halmos_-_Lester_R._Ford_Award) for [*A Repulsion Motif in Diophantine Equations*](https://www.maa.org/sites/default/files/pdf/upload_library/22/Ford/Everest-2012.pdf) printed in the [American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly).[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Ward met Tania Barnett, third child of Dr. Bernard Barnett and [Ruth Barnett (née Michaelis)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Barnett_%28Holocaust_survivor%29) at the University of Warwick in 1986. They were married in London in 1990 and have two children, Adele Louise Barward-Symmons (1992) and Raphael Leslie Barnett-Ward (1995).
