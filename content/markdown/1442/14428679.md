# David Widder

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Widder"}

**David Vernon Widder** (25 March 1898 – 8 July 1990) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1924 under [George Birkhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Birkhoff) and went on to join the faculty there.

:::

He was a co-founder of the *[Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal)* and the author of the textbook *Advanced Calculus* ([Prentice-Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prentice-Hall), 1947).[^o3] He wrote also *The Laplace transform*[^o4] (in which he gave a first solution to [Landau's problem on the Dirichlet eta function](wikipedia://en/447035#Landau's_problem)),[^o5] *An introduction to transform theory*,[^o6] and *The convolution transform* (co-author with I. I. Hirschman).
