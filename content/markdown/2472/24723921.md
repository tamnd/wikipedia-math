# Gareth Roberts (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gareth Roberts"}

**Gareth Owen Roberts** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) [FLSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales) (born 1964) is a statistician and applied probabilist. He is [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in the Department of Statistics and Director of the Centre for Research in Statistical Methodology (CRiSM) at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o4] He is an established authority on the stability of [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876), especially applied to [Markov chain Monte Carlo](wikipedia://en/236801) (MCMC) theory methodology for a wide range of latent statistical models with applications in [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349), [infectious disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infectious_disease) [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) and [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance).[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Roberts was educated at [Liverpool Blue Coat School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_Blue_Coat_School) and [Jesus College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Oxford), graduating in 1985 in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)[^o8] and subsequently went on to complete in 1988 a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) thesis on *Some boundary hitting problems for diffusion processes*[^o9] under the supervision of Saul Jacka at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).

## Career {#Career}

Following his PhD, Roberts held various academic positions at the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham), the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and [Lancaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_University) before returning to the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).[^o4] He was a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_%28Oxbridge%29) of [St Catharine's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catharine's_College,_Cambridge) from 1992 to 1998.

Roberts is a talented [tournament bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_bridge) player, whose achievements include winning the Great Northern Swiss Pairs in 1997,[^o11] and the Garden Cities Trophy in 2008 and 2013.[^o12][^o13]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 1995 – Raybould Fellowship
- 1997 – [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)'s [Guy medal in Bronze](wikipedia://en/6752269)
- 1999 – [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge)
- 2004 – [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher)[^o8] (Mathematics: Ranked 16th [^o15])
- 2008 – [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664)'s [Guy medal in Silver](wikipedia://en/6752269)
- 2009 – [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) Medallion
- 2010 – Editor of [Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143) (Series B)
- 2013 – Elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society)
- 2019 - Elected a [Fellow of the Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales)[^o16]

His nomination to become a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_2013) reads:

- 2015 – [Wolfson Research Merit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfson_Research_Merit_Award) from the Royal Society [^o18]
