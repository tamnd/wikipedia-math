# Jeremy Gray (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeremy Gray"}

**Jeremy John Gray** (born 25 April 1947) is an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) primarily interested in the history of mathematics.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gray studied mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) from 1966 to 1969, and then at [Warwick University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick_University), obtaining his PhD in 1980 under the supervision of [Ian Stewart](wikipedia://en/332183) and [David Fowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fowler_%28mathematician%29). He has worked at the [Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_University) since 1974, and became a lecturer there in 1978. He also lectured at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) from 2002 to 2017, teaching a course on the history of mathematics.

Gray was a consultant on the [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television) series, *[The Story of Maths](wikipedia://en/20029977)*,[^o4] a co-production between the [Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_University) and the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC).[^o5] He edits [Archive for History of Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive_for_History_of_Exact_Sciences).

In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]

## Books {#Books}

Gray has been awarded prizes for his contributions to mathematics, including the [Albert Leon Whiteman Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/26015197) from the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2009,[^o9][^o10] the Otto Neugebauer Prize of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) in 2016,[^o11] and the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)'s [Hirst Prize and Lectureship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirst_Prize_and_Lectureship) in 2018.

He has authored the following:

- *Ideas of space: Euclidean, non-Euclidean, and relativistic*. Oxford University Press 1979, 2nd edition 1989, Spanish edition, Ideas de Espacio, Mondadori Espana, 1992. Romanian edition, Idei Spatiu, Editura All Educational, 1998.
- *Linear differential equations and group theory from Riemann to Poincaré* Birkhäuser, 1986, 2nd edition with three new appendices and other additional material, 2000, Japanese edition 2002.
- *The Geometrical Work of Girard Desargues* (with [J. V. Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_V._Field)). Springer 1986. Field, Judith V.; Gray, Jeremy. (6 December 2012). "2012 pbk edition". Springer. isbn:978-1-4613-8694-0. <https://books.google.com/books?id=zSvSBwAAQBAJ>
- *Geometry* (with D.A. Brannan and M. Esplen). Cambridge University Press 1999, 2nd edition 2012, Arabic edition 2001.
- [*The Hilbert Challenge*](https://books.google.com/books?id=z-mh6YwRIWIC). Oxford University Press 2000,[^o13][^o14] French translation Le défi de Hilbert Dunod, 2003, Taiwanese translation 2003, Spanish translation El reto de Hilbert Critica, 2004.
- *János Bolyai, non-Euclidean Geometry and the Nature of Space*. Burndy Library, MIT, 2004.[^o15]
- *Worlds out of Nothing; A Course on the History of Geometry in the 19th Century*. Springer 2006, 2nd revised edition 2010.
- *Plato’s Ghost: The Modernist Transformation of Mathematics*. Princeton University Press 2008.[^o16][^o17]
- [*Henri Poincaré: a scientific biography*](https://archive.org/details/henripoincaresci0000gray). Princeton University Press 2012.[^o18]
- [*Hidden Harmony – Geometric Fantasies. The Rise of Complex Function Theory*](https://books.google.com/books?id=X08dgn3YcLwC) (with [Umberto Bottazzini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umberto_Bottazzini)). Springer 2013.[^o19]
- *The Real and the Complex: A History of Analysis in the 19th Century*. Springer 2015.
- *A History of Abstract Algebra: From Algebraic Equations to Modern Algebra*. Springer 2018.
- [*Change and Variations: A History of Differential Equations to 1900*](https://books.google.com/books?id=oKkxEAAAQBAJ). Springer 2021.

Books edited or co-edited:

- *The History of Mathematics; a Reader* (with [John Fauvel](wikipedia://en/68235162)). Macmillan 1987.
- *L'Europe mathématique, Mathematical Europe* (with [C. Goldstein](wikipedia://en/54390735) and J. Ritter). Edited with an introductory essay, Éditions de la maison des sciences de l'homme, Paris 1996.
- *Henri Poincaré: Three Supplementary Essays on the Discovery of Fuchsian Functions* (with Scott Walter) Edited with an introductory essay, Akademie Verlag and Blanchard 1997.
- [*The symbolic universe: geometry and physics, 1890-1930*](https://books.google.com/books?id=VbSATwMDFg8C). Edited with an introduction and an essay, Oxford University Press 1999.
- *Jacques Hadamard: Non-Euclidean Geometry in the Theory of Automorphic Functions* (with Abe Shenitzer), translated Abe Shenitzer with an introductory essay by J.J. Gray, American and London Mathematical Societies Series in the History of Mathematics, HMath17, 1999.
- [*Mathematical Conversations; Selections from the Mathematical Intelligencer*](https://books.google.com/books?id=e0TTBwAAQBAJ) (with [Robin Wilson](wikipedia://en/2715277)) Springer 2000. There is also a Japanese edition.
- [*Gauss − Titan of Science*](https://books.google.com/books?id=4mwSrfxBSzkC). A re-edition of [G. Waldo Dunnington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._Waldo_Dunnington)'s book of 1956, with a new introduction and appendices by J.J. Gray, Mathematical Association of America, 2003.
- The Architecture of Modern Mathematics. Edited volume, with an essay by J.J. Gray and one with the co-editor, José Ferreirós, Oxford University Press 2006.
- *Episodes in the History of Modern Algebra 1800—1950*. Edited volume, with an introduction by J.J. Gray and the co-editor, [Karen Parshall](wikipedia://en/36594635), American and London Mathematical Societies Series in the History of Mathematics, HMath32, 2007.

He has also contributed to other books:

- *[The Princeton Companion to Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Princeton_Companion_to_Mathematics)*
- *The Oxford Handbook of the History of Mathematics*
- *[Revolutions in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24860178)*
