# Preventable fraction among the unexposed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Preventable fraction among the unexposed"}

In [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), **preventable fraction among the unexposed** (PFu) is the proportion of incidents in the unexposed group that could be prevented by exposure. It is calculated as $PF_u = (I_u - I_e)/I_u = 1 - RR$, where $I_e$ is the [incidence](wikipedia://en/167008) in the exposed group, $I_u$ is the incidence in the unexposed group, and $RR$ is the [relative risk](wikipedia://en/2622159).[^o2][^o3] It is a synonym of the [relative risk reduction](wikipedia://en/9795530).

:::

It is used when an exposure reduces the risk, as opposed to increasing it, in which case its symmetrical notion is [attributable fraction among the exposed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attributable_fraction_among_the_exposed).
