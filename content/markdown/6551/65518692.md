# Carlo Ignazio Giulio

Carlo Ignazio Giulio

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Carlo Ignazio Giulio"}

**Carlo Ignazio Giulio** (11 August 1803 – 29 June 1859) was an Italian mathematician, mechanical engineer and politician.[^o3][^o4]

:::
