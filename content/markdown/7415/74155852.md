# Wan Zhexian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wan Zhexian"}

[Wan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wan_%28surname%29)

**Wan Zhexian** (; 7 November 1927 – 30 May 2023) was a Chinese mathematician, an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wan was born in Zichuan (now [Zichuan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zichuan_District) of [Zibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zibo)), [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong), on 7 November 1927, while his [ancestral home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestral_home_%28Chinese%29) is in [Xiantao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiantao), [Hubei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubei).[^o5] He attended Zhangdian Primary School ().[^o6]

Wan was admitted to the Mathematics Department of [National South-West Associated University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_South-West_Associated_University) in 1944.[^o5] He graduated from [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) in 1948 and taught there after graduation.[^o5] In 1950, he was transferred to the Institute of Systems science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where he successively worked as an assistant, assistant researcher, associate researcher, and researcher.[^o5] In the same year, he studied [classical group](wikipedia://en/10172878) under the guidance of Professor [Hua Luogeng](wikipedia://en/2337582), and co-wrote "Classical Group" with Hua Luogeng in 1963.[^o7]

Wan joined the [China Association for Promoting Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Association_for_Promoting_Democracy) in 1953 and the [Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party) in 1985.[^o5]

On 30 May 2023, he died of an illness in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing), aged 95.[^o5]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 1987 State Natural Science Award (Third Class) for the isomorphism theory of Classical Group
- 1991 Member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (CAS)
- 1995 2nd Hua Luogeng Mathematics Prize
