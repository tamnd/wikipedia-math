# Samit Dasgupta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Samit Dasgupta"}

**Samit Dasgupta** is a professor of mathematics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) working in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dasgupta graduated from [Montgomery Blair High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery_Blair_High_School) in 1995 and placed fourth in the 1995 [Westinghouse Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westinghouse_Science_Talent_Search) with a project on [Schinzel's hypothesis H](wikipedia://en/604052).[^o4] He then attended [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he received a bachelor's degree in 1999.[^o4][^o5] In 2004, Dasgupta received a PhD in mathematics from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the supervision of [Ken Ribet](wikipedia://en/30876850) and [Henri Darmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Darmon).

Dasgupta was previously a faculty member at [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz).[^o4] As of 2020, he is a professor of mathematics at Duke University.[^o5][^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Dasgupta's research is focused on [special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882), algebraic points on [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152), and [units](wikipedia://en/289666) in [number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field).[^o13] In particular, Dasgupta's research has focused on the [Stark conjectures](wikipedia://en/5685631) and [Heegner points](wikipedia://en/5828654).[^o6][^o9][^o10][^o11]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2009, Dasgupta received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o13] He was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to number theory, in particular the theory of special values of classical and p-adic L-functions".[^o14]
