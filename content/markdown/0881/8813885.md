::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Octahedral prism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **octahedral prism** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). This 4-polytope has 10 [polyhedral](wikipedia://en/23470) cells: 2 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) connected by 8 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584).

:::

## Alternative names {#Alternative_names}

- Octahedral dyadic prism ([Norman W. Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527))
- Ope (Jonathan Bowers, for octahedral prism)
- Triangular antiprismatic prism
- Triangular antiprismatic hyperprism

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

It is a [Hanner polytope](wikipedia://en/39408400) with vertex coordinates, permuting first 3 coordinates:

(\[±1,0,0\]; ±1)

## Structure {#Structure}

The octahedral prism consists of two octahedra connected to each other via 8 triangular prisms. The triangular prisms are joined to each other via their square faces.

## Projections {#Projections}

*Image: Octahedral hyperprism Schlegel.png.* Transparent [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595)

The octahedron-first [orthographic projection](wikipedia://en/203052) of the octahedral prism into 3D space has an octahedral [envelope](wikipedia://en/880235). The two octahedral cells project onto the entire volume of this envelope, while the 8 triangular prismic cells project onto its 8 triangular faces.

The triangular-prism-first orthographic projection of the octahedral prism into 3D space has a [hexagonal prismic](wikipedia://en/2859668) envelope. The two octahedral cells project onto the two hexagonal faces. One triangular prismic cell projects onto a triangular prism at the center of the envelope, surrounded by the images of 3 other triangular prismic cells to cover the entire volume of the envelope. The remaining four triangular prismic cells are projected onto the entire volume of the envelope as well, in the same arrangement, except with opposite orientation.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It is the second in an infinite series of [uniform antiprismatic prisms](wikipedia://en/8813490).

It is one of 18 uniform polyhedral prisms created by using uniform [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) to connect pairs of parallel [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905) and [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847).

It is one of four four-dimensional [Hanner polytopes](wikipedia://en/39408400); the other three are the [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112), the [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), and the dual of the octahedral prism (a [cubical bipyramid](wikipedia://en/72270279)).[^o14]
