::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Uniform antiprismatic prism"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **uniform antiprismatic prism** or **antiduoprism** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) with two [uniform antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851) cells in two parallel 3-space [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862), connected by [uniform prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) cells between pairs of faces. The symmetry of a *p*-gonal antiprismatic prism is \[2*p*,2<sup>\+</sup>,2\], order 8*p*.

:::

A **p-gonal antiprismatic prism** or **p-gonal antiduoprism** has 2 *p*-gonal [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851), 2 *p*-gonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007), and *2p* [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) cells. It has 4*p* [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285), 4*p* [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) and 4 [regular *p*-gon](wikipedia://en/333306) faces. It has 10*p* edges, and 4*p* vertices.

## Convex uniform antiprismatic prisms {#Convex_uniform_antiprismatic_prisms}

There is an infinite series of convex uniform antiprismatic prisms, starting with the *digonal antiprismatic prism* is a [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290), with two of the tetrahedral cells degenerated into squares. The *triangular antiprismatic prism* is the first nondegenerate form, which is also an [octahedral prism](wikipedia://en/8813885). The remainder are unique uniform 4-polytopes.

A **square antiprismatic prism** or **square antiduoprism** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It is formed as two parallel [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045) connected by cubes and triangular prisms. The symmetry of a square antiprismatic prism is \[8,2<sup>\+</sup>,2\], order 32. It has 16 [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654), 16 [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) and 4 [square](wikipedia://en/659939) faces. It has 40 edges, and 16 vertices.
