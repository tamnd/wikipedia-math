# Dmitry Ioffe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitry Ioffe"}

**Dmitry** (**Dima**) **Ioffe** (דמיטרי יופה, Дмитрий Иоффе; 5 April 1963 – 1 October 2020) was an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

**Dmitry Ioffe** obtained his diploma from the [Moscow Mining Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Mining_Institute) in 1985 and his PhD in mathematics in 1991 from the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion), under the supervision of [Ross Pinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Pinsky). He then spent a post-doc at the University of California, Davis and the Courant Institute. He was an assistant professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) (1993-1995) and a researcher at the Weierstrass Institute of Analysis and Stochastics (WIAS) in Berlin (1995-1997), before returning to the Technion, where he spent the rest of his life as professor. From 2014, he was the incumbent of the Alexander Goldberg chair in management sciences.[^o5]

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

Ioffe made fundamental contributions to several areas of statistical mechanics, including random interface models, [interacting particle systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interacting_particle_systems), polymers in random environments, random perturbations of dynamical systems, metastability and homogenization. In particular, he extended the [Dobrushin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobrushin)-Kotecky-Shlosman two dimensional [Wulff construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulff_construction) to the full range of subcritical temperatures and developed with Bodineau and Velenik a robust analytic alternative that worked also in higher dimension. With collaborators, he developed the [Ornstein-Zernike theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ornstein-Zernike_equation) (at temperatures above criticality) and introduced a diamond representation for a range of models including [self-avoiding walks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-avoiding_walks), Bernoulli [percolation](wikipedia://en/299184), Ising ferromagnets and [polymers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymers). He also made important contributions to the analysis of quantum spin systems and [metastability](wikipedia://en/19833).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Ioffe's family applied for permission to leave the USSR for Israel in 1976 but was refused and hence he became [refusenik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refusenik). It was only in 1987, following a hunger strike by his father, mathematician [Alexander Ioffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Ioffe), that Dmitry and his family were allowed to emigrate.[^o8]

## Prizes and honors {#Prizes_and_honors}

Ioffe got the Prix de l’Institut Henri Poincaré (2005) [^o10] and a [Humboldt research award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) (2011).
