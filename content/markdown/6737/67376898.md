# Sheldon M. Ross

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sheldon M. Ross"}

**Sheldon Mark Ross**[^o2] (born April 30, 1943) is the Daniel J. Epstein Chair and Professor at the [USC Viterbi School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USC_Viterbi_School_of_Engineering). He is the author of several books in the field of probability.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ross was born in Brooklyn.[^o3] He received his B. S. degree in mathematics from [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College) in 1963, his M.S. degrees in mathematics from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 1964 and his Ph.D. degree in Statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1968, studying under Gerald Lieberman and [Cyrus Derman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_Derman). He served as a Professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1976 until joining the [USC Viterbi School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USC_Viterbi_School_of_Engineering) in 2004. He serves as the Editor for several journals, among which [Probability in the Engineering and Informational Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_in_the_Engineering_and_Informational_Sciences). In 2013 he became a fellow of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).

In 1978, he formulated what became known as [Ross's conjecture](wikipedia://en/37881898) in queuing theory,[^o5] which was solved three years later by Tomasz Rolski at Poland's [Wroclaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wrocław).[^o6]
