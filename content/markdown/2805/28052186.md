# Dwork family

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dwork family"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Dwork family** is a one-parameter family of [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101) depending on an integer *n*, studied by [Bernard Dwork](wikipedia://en/1678313). Originally considered by Dwork in the context of [local zeta-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_zeta-function), such families have been shown to have relationships with [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671) and extensions of the [modularity theorem](wikipedia://en/174475).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Dwork family is given by the equations

$$x_1^n + x_2^n +\cdots +x_n^n = -n\lambda x_1x_2\cdots x_n \, ,$$

for all $n\ge 1$.

:::

## History {#History}

The Dwork family was originally used by B. Dwork to develop the [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory) of [zeta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeta_functions) of nonsingular hypersurfaces in [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135).[^o6]
