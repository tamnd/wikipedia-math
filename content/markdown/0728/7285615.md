# Lucien Godeaux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lucien Godeaux"}

**Lucien Godeaux** (1887–1975) was a prolific [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgians) mathematician. His total of more than 1000 papers and books, 644 of which are found in [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), made him one of the most published mathematicians.[^o2] He was the sole author of all but one of his papers.[^o3]

:::

He is best remembered for work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). From [Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liège), he was attracted to the work of the [Italian school of algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/383424) by the work of one of its masters, [Federigo Enriques](wikipedia://en/1732661). Godeaux went to [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna) to study with him. The [Godeaux surface](wikipedia://en/3102463) is a construction of a special type, which has subsequently been much studied.

Since 2007, the Belgian Mathematical Society (BMS) is organising a "Godeaux lecture" in his memory.

- Les involutions cycliques appartenant à une surface algébrique (1935)[^o9]
- Les géométries (1937)

- Introduction à la géométrie supérieure (1946)

- Géométrie algébrique I. Transformations birationnelles et géométrie hyperespatielle
