# National Mathematical Society of Pakistan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="National Mathematical Society of Pakistan"}

The **National Mathematics Society of Pakistan** (NMSP), is an academic, non-profit, and scientific society of applied mathematicians and engineers dedicated to the development and promotion of applied mathematics at all levels.[^o4] The society is aim to serve as the national community by organizing seminars, workshops, competitions, meetings and publications.[^o4]

:::

Established in 2010, the society was initially headquartered in [Abdus Salam School of Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/31742070) (AS-SMS), at the [Government College University, Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_College_University,_Lahore).[^o4] It is working abroad to come and be a part of the international mathematical society and exchange ideas and skills with the other members of the society in Pakistan.[^o4] It is currently the adhering organization of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) on Pakistan.[^o3]

On 2015, the [Abdus Salam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdus_Salam) Shield Of Honor in Mathematics was initiated by NMSP to promote and recognize quality research in Mathematics. The first Shield was given to Prof. Hassan Azad from [KFUPM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFUPM) in February 2016.[^o5]

On 2017, NSMP initiated the Abdus Salam Medal for Mathematics to recognise the young and senior mathematicians at national and international levels for their excellent performance. The first recipients were Dr. Ayesha Asloob Qureshi from [Sabancı University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabancı_University) and Dr. Sahibzada Waleed Noor from [University of Campinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Campinas).[^o6]
