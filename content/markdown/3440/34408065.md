# Leif Arkeryd

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leif Arkeryd"}

**Leif O. Arkeryd** (born 24 August 1940) is professor emeritus of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology).[^o2] He is a specialist on the theory of the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522).

:::

Arkeryd earned his doctorate from [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University) in 1966, under the supervision of Jaak Peetre.
