# Tsirelson's stochastic differential equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tsirelson's stochastic differential equation"}

**Tsirelson's stochastic differential equation** (also **Tsirelson's drift** or **Tsirelson's equation**) is a [stochastic differential equation](wikipedia://en/1361454) which has a [weak solution](wikipedia://en/3555416) but no [strong solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_solution). It is therefore a counter-example and named after its discoverer [Boris Tsirelson](wikipedia://en/1661441).[^o2] Tsirelson's equation is of the form

:::

$$dX_t = a[t,(X_s, s\leq t)]dt + dW_t, \quad X_0=0,$$

where $W_t$ is the one-dimensional [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436). Tsirelson chose the drift $a$ to be a bounded [measurable function](wikipedia://en/44055) that depends on the past times of $X$ but is independent of the [natural filtration](wikipedia://en/5864648) $\mathcal{F}^W$ of the Brownian motion. This gives a weak solution, but since the process $X$ is not $\mathcal{F}_{\infty}^W$-measurable, not a strong solution.

## Tsirelson's Drift {#Tsirelson's_Drift}

Let

- $\mathcal{F}_t^{W}=\sigma(W_s : 0 \leq s \leq t)$ and $\{\mathcal{F}_t^{W}\} _{t\in \mathbb{R}_+}$ be the natural Brownian filtration that satisfies the usual conditions,
- $t_0=1$ and $(t_n)_{n\in-\mathbb{N}}$ be a descending sequence $t_0>t_{-1}>t_{-2} >\dots,$ such that $\lim_{n\to -\infty } t_n=0$,
- $\Delta X_{t_n}=X_{t_n}-X_{t_{n-1}}$ and $\Delta t_n=t_n-t_{n-1}$,
- $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x \rfloor$ be the [decimal part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_place).

Tsirelson now defined the following drift

$$a[t,(X_s, s\leq t)]=\sum\limits_{n\in -\mathbb{N}}\bigg\{\frac{\Delta X_{t_n}}{\Delta t_n}\bigg\}1_{(t_n,t_{n+1}]}(t).$$

Let the expression

$$\eta_n=\xi_n+\{\eta_{n-1}\}$$

be the abbreviation for

$$\frac{\Delta X_{t_{n+1}}}{\Delta t_{n+1}}=\frac{\Delta W_{t_{n+1}}}{\Delta t_{ n+1}}+\bigg\{\frac{\Delta X_{t_n}}{\Delta t_n}\bigg\}.$$

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Theorem"}

### Theorem {#Theorem}

According to a theorem by Tsirelson and [Yor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Yor):

\1) The natural filtration of $X$ has the following decomposition

$$\mathcal{F}_t^{X}=\mathcal{F}_t^{W} \vee \sigma\big(\{\eta_{n-1}\}\big),\quad \forall t\geq 0, \quad \forall t_n\leq t$$

\2) For each $n\in -\mathbb{N}$ the $\{\eta_n\}$ are [uniformly distributed](wikipedia://en/1699223) on $[0,1)$ and independent of $(W_t)_{t\geq 0}$ resp. $\mathcal{F}_{\infty}^{W}$.

\3) $\mathcal{F}_{0+}^{X}$ is the $P$-[trivial σ-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivial_σ-algebra), i.e. all events have probability $0$ or $1$.[^o6][^o7]
