# Seppo Linnainmaa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Seppo Linnainmaa"}

**Seppo Ilmari Linnainmaa** (born 28 September 1945) is a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finns) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) known for creating the modern version of [backpropagation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpropagation).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Pori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pori).[^o4] He received his MSc in 1970 and introduced a reverse mode of [automatic differentiation](wikipedia://en/734787) in his MSc thesis.[^o9][^o5] In 1974 he obtained the first doctorate ever awarded in computer science at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki).[^o8] In 1976, he became Assistant Professor. From 1984 to 1985 he was Visiting Professor at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland), USA. From 1986 to 1989 he was Chairman of the Finnish Artificial Intelligence Society. From 1989 to 2007, he was Research Professor at the [VTT Technical Research Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VTT_Technical_Research_Centre_of_Finland) of [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland). He retired in 2007.

## Backpropagation {#Backpropagation}

Explicit, efficient error [backpropagation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpropagation) in arbitrary, discrete, possibly sparsely connected, [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_networks)-like networks was first described in Linnainmaa's 1970 master's thesis,[^o9][^o10] albeit without reference to NNs,[^o7] when he introduced the reverse mode of [automatic differentiation](wikipedia://en/734787) (AD), in order to efficiently compute the [derivative](wikipedia://en/7921) of a [differentiable](wikipedia://en/330206) [composite function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composite_function) that can be represented as a [graph](wikipedia://en/87352), by recursively applying the [chain rule](wikipedia://en/6113) to the building blocks of the function.[^o8][^o9][^o10] Linnainmaa published it first, following Gerardi Ostrowski who had used it in the context of certain process models in chemical engineering some five years earlier, but didn't publish.
