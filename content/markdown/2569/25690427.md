# James McKernan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James McKernan"}

**James McKernan** (born 1964) is a mathematician, and a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). He was a professor at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 2007 until 2013.

:::

## Education {#Education}

McKernan was educated at [The Campion School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Campion_School) and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), before going on to earn his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1991.[^o4] His dissertation, *On the Hyperplane Sections of a Variety in Projective Space*, was supervised by [Joe Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

McKernan was the joint winner of the [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in 2009,[^o7][^o8] and joint recipient of the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) in 2007.[^o9] Both honors were received jointly with his colleague [Christopher Hacon](wikipedia://en/29023273). He gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010, on the topic of "Algebraic Geometry".[^o10] He was the joint winner (with [Christopher Hacon](wikipedia://en/29023273)) of the 2018 [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).

He was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to algebraic geometry, in particular his proof of the finite generation of the canonical ring, the existence of flips and the boundedness of varieties of log general type".[^o11]
