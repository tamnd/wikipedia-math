# 2015 Transnistrian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2015 Transnistrian census"}

The **2015 Transnistrian census** was organized in the unrecognized state of [Transnistria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnistria) in 2015. It was held after [Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldova), internationally recognized as the owner of the state's territory, made [a census](wikipedia://en/41781986) in 2014.

:::

## Census results {#Census_results}

According to the census results, [Transnistria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnistria)'s population was of 475,373 people in 2015. This represented a population decline of 14.47%[^o4] (or nearly 80,000 people) since the [2004 Transnistrian census](wikipedia://en/4837542), which is an amount similar to the population of the second largest Transnistrian-controlled city, [Bender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bender,_Moldova) (Tighina).[^o5]

The ethnic composition results were the following:[^o4]

- [Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians): 29.1%
- [Moldovans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovans): 28.6%
- [Ukrainians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians): 22.9%
- [Bulgarians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarians): 2.4%
- [Gagauz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gagauz_people): 1.1%
- [Belarusians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusians): 0.5%
- Transnistrians: 0.2%
- Others: 1.4%

This census was the first one in Transnistria in which the population was given the option to identify as "Transnistrian", which more than 1,000 people did. Furthermore, 14% of the population did not specify its ethnic affiliation.[^o4]
