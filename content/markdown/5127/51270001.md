# Louise Hay Award

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Louise Hay Award"}

The **Louise Hay Award** is a mathematics award planned in 1990 and first issued in 1991 by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in recognition of contributions as a math educator. The award was created in honor of [Louise Hay](wikipedia://en/31132826).[^o3]

:::
