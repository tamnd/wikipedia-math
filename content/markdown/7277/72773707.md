# Alexander Richardson (Puritan intellectual)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Richardson"}

**Alexander Richardson** (c1565-1613?) was an English [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Richardson was a Master of Arts at [Queens' College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens'_College,_Cambridge).[^o4] He also tutored at [George Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Walker_%28Puritan%29)'s house in Barking, Essex.[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

Richardson wrote a logic textbook in the [Ramist](wikipedia://en/1691461) tradition, *The logicians school-master: or, A comment upon Ramus logick.: By Mr. Alexander Richardson, sometime of Queenes Colledge in Cambridge. Whereunto are added, his prelections on Ramus his grammer; Taleus his rhetorick; also his notes on physicks, ethicks, astronomy, medicine, and opticks.* The book was first published in London in 1629 and enlarged in 1657.[^o7]
