# Yadollah Ordokhani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yadollah Ordokhani"}

**Yadollah Ordokhani** is an Iranian mathematician and Professor of Mathematics at [Alzahra University](wikipedia://en/1162910). He is among the most-cited Iranian researchers and is known for his works on [fractional wavelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_wavelet), [optimal control problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_control_problem), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equations) and [time-delay systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-delay_systems).[^o3] He is a former head of Iran's [National Elites Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Elites_Foundation)-Tehran Branch and a former Deputy Head of Research at Alzahra University.[^o4][^o5]

:::
