# Kenneth O. May Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth O. May Prize"}

*Image: Medal Kenneth O May Prize.jpg.*

**Kenneth O. May Prize** and **Medal** in [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) is an award of the [International Commission on the History of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/35011217) (ICHM) "for the encouragement and promotion of the history of mathematics internationally". It was established in 1989 and is named in honor of [Kenneth O. May](wikipedia://en/719577), the founder of ICHM. Since then, the award is given every four years, at the ICHM congress.

:::
