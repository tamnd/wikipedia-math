# Mathematical sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical sciences"}

The **mathematical sciences** are a group of areas of study that includes, in addition to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), those [academic disciplines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_disciplines) that are primarily mathematical in nature but may not be universally considered subfields of mathematics proper.

:::

[Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), for example, is mathematical in its methods but grew out of [bureaucratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureaucracy) and [scientific observations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation#Science),[^o2] which merged with [inverse probability](wikipedia://en/908703) and then grew through applications in some areas of [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [biometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometrics), and the [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_sciences) to become its own separate, though closely allied, field. [Theoretical astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_astronomy), [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028), [theoretical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) and [applied mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mechanics), [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918), [mathematical chemistry](wikipedia://en/1570072), [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), [computational science](wikipedia://en/1181008), [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904), [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476), [quantitative biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_biology), [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390), [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics) and [mathematical geosciences](wikipedia://en/27290438) are likewise other fields often considered part of the mathematical sciences.

Some institutions offer degrees in mathematical sciences (e.g. the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and [University of Khartoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Khartoum)) or applied mathematical sciences (for example, the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island)).
