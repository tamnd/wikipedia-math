# John Lane Bell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Lane Bell"}

**John Lane Bell** (born March 25, 1945) is a Canadian philosopher, mathematician, and logician specializing in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and the foundations of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He served as Professor of Philosophy at the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario) from 1989 until his retirement in 2019, and previously held positions at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) from 1968 to 1989[^o3]. Elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 2009, Bell's research encompasses [constructive mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_mathematics), [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [topos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topos_theory), and the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840), among other topics.[^o3]

:::

Bell has authored influential books including *Intuitionistic Set Theory* (2014), which explores set-theoretic foundations intuitionistic principles, and *Oppositions and Paradoxes* (2016), addressing logical and philosophical [paradoxes](wikipedia://en/24390). His work bridges technical developments in logic with philosophical inquiries into continuity, [infinitesimals](wikipedia://en/160990), and the nature of [mathematical structures](wikipedia://en/1809181), contributing to the ongoing debates in the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).[^o3]

## Biography {#Biography}

John Bell was awarded a scholarship to [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) at the age of 15, and graduated with a D.Phil. in Mathematics: his dissertation supervisor was [John Crossley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Crossley_%28mathematician%29). During 1968–89 he was Lecturer in Mathematics and Reader in Mathematical Logic at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).[^o5]

Bell's students include [Graham Priest](wikipedia://en/3659382) (Ph.D. Mathematics LSE, 1972), [Michael Hallett](wikipedia://en/78475974) (Ph.D. Philosophy LSE, 1979), [David DeVidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_DeVidi) (Ph.D. Philosophy UWO, 1994), [Elaine Landry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaine_Landry) (Ph.D. Philosophy UWO, 1997) and [Richard A. Feist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_A._Feist) (Ph.D. Philosophy UWO, 1999).
