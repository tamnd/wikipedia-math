# Colette Guillopé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Colette Guillopé"}

*Image: Colette Guillopé à la Fête de l'Humanité 2023.jpg.* Colette Guillopé

**Colette Guillopé** (born 1951[^o2]) is a French mathematician specializing in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988). She is a professor [emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus)[^o3][^o4] at [Paris 12 Val de Marne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_12_Val_de_Marne_University), where she is also the gender officer for the university.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Guillopé's parents were both professors. She studied at the École normale supérieure de Fontenay-aux-Roses.

## Education {#Education}

Guillopé earned a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies). In 1977, Guillopé earned her doctorate at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique). She completed a [thèse d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thèse_d'état) in 1983 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) under the supervision of [Roger Temam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Temam).

## Career {#Career}

Guillopé was a founding member of [L'association femmes et mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'association_femmes_et_mathématiques) in 1987, and was its president from 1996 to 1998. She also led the association [Femmes et Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Femmes_et_Sciences) from 2004 to 2008. In 2016 she became an officer in the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour), being promoted from the rank of a knight in the Legion.

She was president of French women mathematicians.
