# Dan Archdeacon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Archdeacon"}

**Dan Steven Archdeacon** (1954–2015) was an American [graph theorist](wikipedia://en/12401) specializing in [topological graph theory](wikipedia://en/1631772), who served for many years as a professor of mathematics and statistics at the [University of Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vermont).

:::

Archdeacon was born on May 11, 1954, in [Dayton, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio), and grew up in [Centerville, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centerville,_Ohio). He did his undergraduate studies at [Earlham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earlham_College), graduating in 1975. He completed his Ph.D. in 1980 from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), under the supervision of Henry Hatfield Glover, with a dissertation proving an analogue of [Kuratowski's theorem](wikipedia://en/54493) for the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350). He took a position at the University of Vermont in 1982, joining fellow graph theorist and Ohio State graduate [Jeff Dinitz](wikipedia://en/7403486), after previously working as an instructor at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas). He died of cancer on February 18, 2015, in [Burlington, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington,_Vermont).

In 2003–2004, the University of Vermont named him as University Scholar. A special issue of the *[Australasian Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasian_Journal_of_Combinatorics)* was published in his honor in 2017.
