# Brian H. Murdoch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brian H. Murdoch"}

**Brian Hughes Murdoch** (3 April 1930 – 9 December 2020) was an Irish mathematician who served for 23 years as [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (TCD). He was an analyst with expertise in potential functions and [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451).[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Born in [Worcestershire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcestershire) in England in 1930, Brian Hughes Murdoch grew up in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin). He attended Trinity College Dublin, where he won a scholarship in mathematics in 1948.[^o4] He obtained his BA in mathematics in 1951. In 1954, he got his PhD at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton) under [William Feller](wikipedia://en/206644), for a thesis on “Preharmonic Functions”.[^o5] After a couple of years teaching at [Hull University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_University) in [Kingston upon Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_upon_Hull) and [Queen's University Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast), he returned to TCD, where he taught for another 35 years. He was elected Fellow of TCD in 1965, and the following year he was appointed [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) at TCD, a position he held until he retired in 1989. In 1988 he became a Senior Fellow.

A [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaker), he was a second cousin of the author [Iris Murdoch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_Murdoch).[^o6]

He died on 9 December 2020.[^o7][^o8]
