# Wang Dongming (academic)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wang Dongming"}

**Wang Dongming** (; born July 1961 in [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)) is Research Director (Directeur de Recherche) at the French National Center for Scientific Research ([Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_National_de_la_Recherche_Scientifique), CNRS).[^o2] He was awarded Wen-tsün Wu Chair Professor at the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China) in 2001,[^o3] Changjiang Scholar of the [Chinese Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Ministry_of_Education) in 2005,[^o4] and Bagui Scholar of [Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangxi_Zhuang_Autonomous_Region), China in 2014.[^o5] He was elected Member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2017.[^o6]

:::

Wang worked on algorithmic [elimination theory](wikipedia://en/245978), geometric reasoning and [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management), and applications of [symbolic computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_computation) to qualitative analysis of differential equations. In 1993 he proposed an elimination method for [triangular decomposition](wikipedia://en/29321051) of polynomial systems,[^o7] which has been referred to as Wang's method and compared with other three methods.[^o8] Later on he introduced the concepts of regular systems and simple systems[^o9] and devised algorithms for regular and simple triangular decompositions.[^o10][^o11] He also developed a package, called Epsilon,[^o12] which implements his methods.[^o13][^o14]

Wang popularized the use of methods and tools of [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805) for symbolic analysis of stability and bifurcation of differential and biological systems. He constructed a class of cubic differential systems with six small-amplitude limit cycles[^o15] and rediscovered the incompleteness of Kukles' center conditions of 1944,[^o16] which stimulated the study of Kukles' system in hundred papers.[^o17] Since 2004 he has been involved in research projects on geometric knowledge management and discovery. With co-workers he developed an algorithmic approach for automated discovery of geometric theorems from images of diagrams.[^o18]

Wang served as General Chair of [ISSAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISSAC) 2007 and is founding Editor-in-Chief and Managing Editor of Mathematics in Computer Science[^o19] and Executive Associate Editor-in-Chief of SCIENCE CHINA Information Sciences.[^o20]

Currently he works as Professor at [Beihang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beihang_University) and [Guangxi University for Nationalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangxi_University_for_Nationalities), China on leave (détaché) from CNRS.
