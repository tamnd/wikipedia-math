# The Tiger That Isn't

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The Tiger That Isn't"}

***The Tiger That Isn't: Seeing Through a World of Numbers*** is a [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) book written by [Michael Blastland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Blastland) and [Andrew Dilnot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Dilnot), the creator and presenter of [BBC Radio 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_4)'s *[More or Less](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_or_Less_%28radio_programme%29)*. Like the radio show, it addresses the misuse of statistics in politics and the media.

:::

The book has received favourable reviews for the simple presentation of complicated ideas.[^o3][^o4][^o5]
