# Françoise Tisseur

::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Françoise Tisseur"}

**Françoise Tisseur** is a [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) and Professor of Numerical Analysis[^o2][^o9] at the [Department of Mathematics, University of Manchester](wikipedia://en/10387568), UK. She works in [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and in particular on [nonlinear eigenvalue problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_eigenproblem) and structured [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) problems, including the development of algorithms and software.[^o2][^o10]

:::

She is a graduate of the University of St-Etienne, France, from where she gained her Maitrise (Mathematical Engineering) in 1993, Diplome d'Etude Approfondie in 1994, and PhD (Numerical Analysis) in 1997.

She has contributed software to [LAPACK](wikipedia://en/1013768), [ScaLAPACK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScaLAPACK), and the [MATLAB](wikipedia://en/20412) distribution.

Tisseur is a member of the editorial boards of the SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications, the IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis and the Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Tisseur was awarded the 2010 [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for her research achievements in numerical linear algebra, including polynomial eigenvalue and structured matrix problems.[^o4] She was awarded the 2011–2012 [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110) of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) for her work on [polynomial eigenvalue problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_eigenvalue_problem)[^o5] and holds a Royal Society Wolfson Research Merit Award in 2014–2019.[^o6] She delivered the Olga Taussky-Tood Lecture at the International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics in Valencia, Spain, in 2019.[^o12] She is the 2020 winner of the [Fröhlich Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fröhlich_Prize) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) "for her important and highly innovative contributions to the analysis, perturbation theory, and numerical solution of nonlinear eigenvalue problems".[^o13]

Tisseur became a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2016 "for contributions to numerical linear algebra, especially numerical methods for eigenvalue problems".[^o14] She held an EPSRC Leadership Fellowship in 2011–2016,[^o15] and is a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications.
