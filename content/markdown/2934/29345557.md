# Jens Marklof

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jens Marklof"}

**Jens Marklof** is a German mathematician working in the areas of [quantum chaos](wikipedia://en/313779), [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), [equidistribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equidistribution), [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o3][^o4] He will be president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) in the period 2023-2024.

:::

Marklof is currently professor of mathematical physics at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol), UK.[^o3][^o4]

## Education {#Education}

After studying physics at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), Marklof was awarded a doctorate in 1997 at the [University of Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ulm).[^o6]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In June 2010, Marklof was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for his work on quantum chaos, [random matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_matrices) and number theory.[^o8] Marklof was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_2015).[^o9]
