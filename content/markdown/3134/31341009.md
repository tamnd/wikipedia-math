# Steenrod homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steenrod homology"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), **Steenrod homology** is a [homology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) for [compact metric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_metric_space) introduced by (txt), based on regular cycles. It is similar to the homology theory introduced rather sketchily by [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161) in 1936.

:::
