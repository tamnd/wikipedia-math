# Fariba Fahroo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fariba Fahroo"}

**Fariba Fahroo** is an American Persian mathematician, a program manager at the [Air Force Office of Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Office_of_Scientific_Research), and a former program manager at the [Defense Sciences Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_Sciences_Office). Along with [I. M. Ross](wikipedia://en/36457556), she has published papers in [pseudospectral optimal control](wikipedia://en/15983837) theory.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] [^o7] [^o8] [^o9] [^o10] The [Ross–Fahroo lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross–Fahroo_lemma) and the [Ross–Fahroo pseudospectral method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross–Fahroo_pseudospectral_method) are named after her. In 2010, she received (jointly with [Ross](wikipedia://en/36457556)), the [AIAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIAA) Mechanics and Control of Flight Award for fundamental contributions to flight mechanics.[^o11]

:::

In 2019, she was named a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for outstanding scientific leadership while managing AFOSR and DARPA programs in dynamics and control and computational mathematics and fundamental research accomplishments in computational optimal control".[^o12] She was named to the 2021 class of Fellows of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o13]
