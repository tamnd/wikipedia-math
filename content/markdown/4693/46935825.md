# Kanta Gupta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kanta Gupta"}

**Chander Kanta Gupta** FRSC (8 October 1938 – 27 March 2016)[^o3][^o4] was a Canadian distinguished professor of mathematics at the [University of Manitoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manitoba), known for her research in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).[^o5] Much of her research concerns the [automorphisms](wikipedia://en/1160) in different [varieties of groups](wikipedia://en/1021753).[^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Gupta earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Jammu and Kashmir, a master's degree from the [Aligarh Muslim University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aligarh_Muslim_University), another master's degree from the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), and a Ph.D. in 1967 from ANU under the supervision of Michael Frederick Newman.[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was elected to the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1991, and awarded the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) in 2000.[^o7]

## Personal {#Personal}

Her husband, Narain Gupta (1936–2008) was also an ANU alumnus, and a distinguished professor of mathematics at the University of Manitoba.[^o11]
