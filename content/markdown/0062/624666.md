# List of partition topics

Generally, a **partition** is a division of a whole into non-overlapping parts. Among the kinds of partitions considered in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) are

- [partition of a set](wikipedia://en/340240) or an [ordered partition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordered_partition) of a set,

- [partition of a graph](wikipedia://en/11973947),

- [partition of an integer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_an_integer),

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Partition of unity"}

- [partition of unity](wikipedia://en/48636),

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Block matrix"}

- partition of a matrix; see [block matrix](wikipedia://en/457464), and

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Partition of the sum of squares"}

- [partition of the sum of squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_the_sum_of_squares) in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) problems, especially in the [analysis of variance](wikipedia://en/634),
