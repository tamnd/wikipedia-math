# Giacomo Albanese

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giacomo Albanese"}

**Giacomo Albanese** (11 July 1890 – 8 June 1947[^o2]) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He took a permanent position in the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), in 1936.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Albanese attended the school in [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo), [Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicily). He graduated from there in 1909. Then he entered the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) as a student of mathematics, and received his doctorate in 1913. He was awarded the *[Ulisse Dini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulisse_Dini)* prize for his doctoral essay on the topic *Continuous systems of curves on an [algebraic](wikipedia://en/974169) surface*, written under the direction of [Eugenio Bertini](wikipedia://en/33285389).

He taught at Scuola Normale Superiore from 1913 to 1919, with an interruption in 1917–1918, when he was conscripted into the Italian Army to fight in [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I). After spending a year at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) to work with [Francesco Severi](wikipedia://en/1565716), he took up in 1920 a professorship in Analysis and Algebra at the [Italian Naval Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Naval_Academy) in [Livorno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livorno). Five years later Albanese moved to the [University of Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Catania), and then went in 1927 to the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo), where he spent the next two years. From 1929 until 1936 he held the chair of Geometry at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa).
