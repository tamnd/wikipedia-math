# Niels Nielsen (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Niels Nielsen"}

**Niels Nielsen** (2 December 1865, in [Ørslev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ørslev_%28Vordingborg_Municipality%29) – 16 September 1931, in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen)) was a Danish mathematician who specialised in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Nielsen was the son of humble peasants and grew up in the western part of the island of [Funen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funen). In 1891 he graduated in mathematics from the University of Copenhagen and in 1895 obtained his doctorate. In 1909 he succeeded [Julius Petersen](wikipedia://en/76861) as Professor of Mathematics at the University of Copenhagen.

His most original works were on [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), with an important contribution to the theory of the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316).

In 1917 he suffered from an illness from which he never fully recovered. From this date onward he became interested in the [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [Bernoulli numbers](wikipedia://en/4964), [Stirling numbers](wikipedia://en/95465), and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), writing two books on Danish mathematicians of the time period 1528-1908, and two other books on French mathematicians.
