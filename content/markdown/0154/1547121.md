# Twisted cubic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Twisted cubic"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **twisted cubic** is a smooth, [rational curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_curve) *C* of [degree](wikipedia://en/2776558) three in [projective 3-space](wikipedia://en/242135) **P**<sup>3</sup>. It is a fundamental example of a [skew curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skew_curve). It is essentially unique, up to [projective transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_transformation) (*the* twisted cubic, therefore). In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the twisted cubic is a simple example of a [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469) that is not linear or a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101), in fact not a [complete intersection](wikipedia://en/3116835). It is the three-dimensional case of the [rational normal curve](wikipedia://en/2380117), and is the [image](wikipedia://en/579311) of a [Veronese map](wikipedia://en/1569217#Veronese_map) of degree three on the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578).

:::

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: Twisted cubic curve.png.*

The twisted cubic is most easily given [parametrically](wikipedia://en/453198) as the image of the map

$$\nu:\mathbf{P}^1\to\mathbf{P}^3$$

which assigns to the [homogeneous coordinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_coordinate) $[S:T]$ the value

$$\nu:[S:T] \mapsto [S^3:S^2T:ST^2:T^3].$$

In one [coordinate patch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_patch) of projective space, the map is simply the [moment curve](wikipedia://en/29536414)

$$\nu:x \mapsto (x,x^2,x^3)$$

That is, it is the closure by a single [point at infinity](wikipedia://en/403139) of the [affine curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_curve) $(x,x^2,x^3)$.

The twisted cubic is a [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469), defined as the intersection of three [quadrics](wikipedia://en/64926134). In homogeneous coordinates $[X:Y:Z:W]$ on **P**<sup>3</sup>, the twisted cubic is the closed [subscheme](wikipedia://en/364754) defined by the vanishing of the three [homogeneous polynomials](wikipedia://en/1698977)

$$F_0 = XZ - Y^2$$

$$F_1 = YW - Z^2$$

$$F_2 = XW - YZ.$$

It may be checked that these three [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478) vanish identically when using the explicit parameterization above; that is, substitute *x*<sup>3</sup> for *X*, and so on.

More strongly, the [homogeneous ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_ideal) of the twisted cubic *C* is generated by these three homogeneous polynomials of degree 2.

## Properties {#Properties}

The twisted cubic has the following properties:

- It is the set-theoretic complete intersection of $XZ - Y^2$ and $Z(YW-Z^2)-W(XW-YZ)$, but not a scheme-theoretic or ideal-theoretic complete intersection; meaning to say that the ideal of the variety cannot be generated by only 2 polynomials; a minimum of 3 are needed. (An attempt to use only two polynomials make the resulting ideal not [radical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_ideal), since $(YW-Z^2)^2$ is in it, but $YW-Z^2$ is not).
- Any four points on *C* span **P**<sup>3</sup>.
- Given six points in **P**<sup>3</sup> with no four coplanar, there is a unique twisted cubic passing through them.
- The [union](wikipedia://en/47949) of the [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) and [secant lines](wikipedia://en/241886) (the [secant variety](wikipedia://en/17761472)) of a twisted cubic *C* fill up **P**<sup>3</sup> and the lines are pairwise disjoint, except at points of the curve itself. In fact, the union of the [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) and [secant](wikipedia://en/241886) lines of any non-planar smooth [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) is three-dimensional. Further, any smooth [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) with the property that every length four subscheme spans **P**<sup>3</sup> has the property that the tangent and secant lines are pairwise disjoint, except at points of the variety itself.
- The projection of *C* onto a plane from a point on a tangent line of *C* yields a [cuspidal cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuspidal_cubic).
- The projection from a point on a secant line of *C* yields a [nodal](wikipedia://en/46573101) cubic.
- The projection from a point on *C* yields a [conic section](wikipedia://en/19008673).
