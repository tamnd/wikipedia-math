# John Robert Kline

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Robert Kline"}

**John Robert Kline** (December 7, 1891 – May 2, 1955)[^o3] was an American mathematician and educator.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

One of three children born to Henry K. Kline and Emma M. Kline, he was Professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) from 1920 to 1955. A Ph.D. student of [Robert Lee Moore](wikipedia://en/1077133), he was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) in 1925, later Chairman of the Department of Mathematics from 1933 to 1954, and [Thomas A. Scott Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Scott_Professorship_of_Mathematics) from 1941 to 1955.[^o5]

His doctoral students include [Lida Barrett](wikipedia://en/42860487), Donald Flanders, Joseph Harrison Kusner, [Arthur Milgram](wikipedia://en/21029542), [Athanasios Papoulis](wikipedia://en/617543), [Dudley Weldon Woodard](wikipedia://en/39765925), [Leo Zippin](wikipedia://en/31837971),[^o6] and [William Waldron Schieffelin Claytor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Waldron_Schieffelin_Claytor).[^o7]

Kline was elected to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society) in 1941.[^o8]
