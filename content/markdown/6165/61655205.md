# Victoria Powers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victoria Powers"}

*Image: Vicki Powers.jpg.* Vicki Powers

**Vicki Powers** (July 28, 1958 – February 2, 2025), born Victoria Ann Powers,[^o2] was an American mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and known for her work on [positive polynomials](wikipedia://en/28416554) and on the mathematics of [electoral systems](wikipedia://en/29066482). She was a professor in the department of mathematics at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University), where she worked starting in 1987.[^o10]

:::

Powers was the author of the book *Certificates of Positivity for Real Polynomials—Theory, Practice, and Applications* (Springer, 2021).[^o3] A review on [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683) said that "In the reviewer's opinion this is a very nice and concise presentation of the most important pillars of real algebra up to the present time".

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Powers graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1980, with a bachelor's degree in mathematics. She completed her Ph.D. in 1985 at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). Her dissertation, *Finite Constructable Spaces of Signatures*, was supervised by [Alex F. T. W. Rosenberg](wikipedia://en/47675768).

After completing her doctorate, she joined the faculty at the [University of Hawaiʻi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaiʻi), but moved to Emory University only two years later, in 1987.

She was on leave from Emory as a [Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Fellow) and Alexander von Humboldt research professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) in 1991–1992, as a visiting professor at the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid) in 2002–2003, and as a program officer at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) in 2013–2015. From 2012 to 2014, Powers served as a Council Member at Large for the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

Powers' work moved from abstract [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198) to more concrete questions related to positive polynomials in one and several variables and [voting theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_theory). Her collaborators included [Bruce Reznick](wikipedia://en/61232693), [Eberhard Becker](wikipedia://en/24629435), Mari Castle, Claus Scheiderer and Thorsten Wormann.

Powers was married to [Colm Mulcahy](wikipedia://en/51053993), an Irish mathematician who had the same doctoral advisor. On February 2, 2025, she died at home from complications of [ALS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALS).[^o10]

## Selected papers {#Selected_papers}

- 1988 "Higher level reduced [Witt rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witt_ring_%28forms%29) of [skew fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skew_field)", Math Z., vol. 198, no.4, 545–554.
- 1991 "Holomorphy rings and higher level orders on skew fields", J. Algebra, vol. 136, no.1, 51-59.
- 1996 (with [Eberhard Becker](wikipedia://en/24629435)) "Sums of powers in rings and the real holomorphy ring", J. Reine Angew. Math., vol 480, 71-103.
- 1996 "[Hilbert's 17th problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_17th_problem) and the champagne problem", Amer. Math. Monthly, vol. 103, no.10, 879-887.
- 1998 (with Thorsten Wormann) "An algorithm for [sums of squares](wikipedia://en/31005435) of [real polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_polynomial)", J. Pure Appl. Algebra, vol. 127, no.1, 99-104.
- 2000 (with [Bruce Reznick](wikipedia://en/61232693)) "Polynomials that are positive on an interval", Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., vol. 352, no. 10, 4677-4692.
- 2000 (with Bruce Reznick) "Notes towards a constructive proof of Hilbert's theorem on [ternary quartics](wikipedia://en/35768238)", [Quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478) and their applications (Dublin 1999), Contemp. Math. vol. 272, 209-227.
- 2001 (with Claus Scheiderer) "The moment problem for non-compact semialgebraic sets.", Adv. Geom, vol.1, 71-88
- 2001 (with Bruce Reznick) "A new bound for Polya's theorem with applications to polynomials positive on polyhedra", J. Pure Appl. Algebra, vol.164, no. 1-2, 221-229.
- 2004 (with Bruce Reznick, Claus Scheiderer, and Frank Sottile) "A new approach to Hilbert's theorem on ternary quartics", C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris, vol. 339, no.9, 617-620.
- 2007 (with [James Demmel](wikipedia://en/31677002) and Jiawang Nie) "Representations of [positive polynomials](wikipedia://en/28416554) on [noncompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncompact) [semialgebraic sets](wikipedia://en/13329722) via KKT ideals." J. Pure Appl. Algebra, vol. 209, no.1, 189-200.
- 2017 "Positive polynomials and sums of squares: theory and practice" [Real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198), Panor. Syntheses, vol. 51, 155-180.
- 2019 "Power index rankings in bicameral legislatures and the US legislative system., Soc. Choice, Welf. vol. 53, no. 2, 179-196.
- 2024 Chapter "Who's Got the Power? Measuring Power in the US Legislative System" in book *Teaching Mathematics through Cross-Curricular Projects*, MAA Press, 2024

## Awards {#Awards}

In May 2024, Powers was the recipient of Emory University's George P. Cuttino Award for mentoring.[^o8]
