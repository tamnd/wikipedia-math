# Meredith graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Meredith graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Meredith graph** is a 4-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 70 vertices and 140 edges discovered by Guy H. J. Meredith in 1973.

:::

The Meredith graph is 4-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 4-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph), has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 5, radius 7, diameter 8, girth 4 and is [non-Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph).[^o4] It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o5]

Published in 1973, it provides a counterexample to the [Crispin Nash-Williams](wikipedia://en/16550517) conjecture that every 4-regular 4-vertex-connected graph is Hamiltonian.[^o6][^o7] However, [W. T. Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021) showed that all 4-connected [planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314) are hamiltonian.[^o8]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Meredith graph is $(x-4) (x-1)^{10} x^{21} (x+1)^{11} (x+3) (x^2-13) (x^6-26 x^4+3 x^3+169 x^2-39 x-45)^4$.
