# Cocompact group action

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cocompact group action"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* on a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X* is **cocompact** if the [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) *X*/*G* is a [compact space](wikipedia://en/6042). If *X* is [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact), then an equivalent condition is that there is a compact subset *K* of *X* such that the image of *K* under the action of *G* covers *X*.[^o2] It is sometimes referred to as *mpact*, a tongue-in-cheek reference to [dual](wikipedia://en/609737) notions where prefixing with "co-" twice would "cancel out".

:::
