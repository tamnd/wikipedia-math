# Strictly convex space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Strictly convex space"}

*Image: Vector norms.svg.* The unit ball in the middle figure is strictly convex, while the other two balls are not (they contain a line segment as part of their boundary).

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **strictly convex space** is a [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (*X*, || ||) for which the closed unit [ball](wikipedia://en/160556) is a strictly [convex set](wikipedia://en/6292). Put another way, a strictly convex space is one for which, given any two distinct points *x* and *y* on the [unit sphere](wikipedia://en/27033090) ∂*B* (i.e. the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) of the unit ball *B* of *X*), the segment joining *x* and *y* meets ∂*B* *only* at *x* and *y*. Strict convexity is somewhere between an [inner product space](wikipedia://en/14856) (all inner product spaces being strictly convex) and a general [normed space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) in terms of structure. It also guarantees the uniqueness of a best approximation to an element in *X* (strictly convex) out of a convex subspace *Y*, provided that such an approximation exists.

:::

If the normed space *X* is [complete](wikipedia://en/3989) and satisfies the slightly stronger property of being [uniformly convex](wikipedia://en/9133418) (which implies strict convexity), then it is also reflexive by the [Milman–Pettis theorem](wikipedia://en/9144207).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The following properties are equivalent to strict convexity.

- A [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (*X*, || ||) is strictly convex if and only if *x* ≠ *y* and || *x* || = || *y* || = 1 together imply that || *x* + *y* || \< 2.
- A [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (*X*, || ||) is strictly convex if and only if *x* ≠ *y* and || *x* || = || *y* || = 1 together imply that || *αx* + (1 − *α*)*y* || \< 1 for all 0 \< *α* \< 1.
- A [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (*X*, || ||) is strictly convex if and only if *x* ≠ *0* and *y* ≠ *0* and || *x* + *y* || = || *x* || + || *y* || together imply that *x* = *cy* for some constant *c > 0*;
- A [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (*X*, || ||) is strictly convex [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) the [modulus of convexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulus_of_convexity) *δ* for (*X*, || ||) satisfies *δ*(2) = 1.

:::
