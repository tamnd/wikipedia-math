# Stallings–Zeeman theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stallings–Zeeman theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Stallings–Zeeman theorem** is a result in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), used in the proof of the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) for [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) greater than or equal to five. It is named after the [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) [John R. Stallings](wikipedia://en/18729795) and [Christopher Zeeman](wikipedia://en/871415).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let *M* be a finite [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686) of dimension dim(*M*) = *m* ≥ 5. Suppose that *M* has the [homotopy type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_type) of the *m*-dimensional [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) **S**<sup>*m*</sup> and that *M* is locally [piecewise linearly](wikipedia://en/444224) [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to *m*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) **R**<sup>*m*</sup>. Then *M* is homeomorphic to **S**<sup>*m*</sup> under a map that is piecewise linear except possibly at a single point *x*. That is, *M* \\ {*x*} is piecewise linearly homeomorphic to **R**<sup>*m*</sup>.

:::
