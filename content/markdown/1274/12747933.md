# Quasivariety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasivariety"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quasivariety** is a class of [algebraic structures](wikipedia://en/106364) generalizing the notion of [variety](wikipedia://en/1021753) by allowing equational conditions on the axioms defining the class.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A *trivial algebra* contains just one element. A **quasivariety** is a class *K* of algebras with a specified [signature](wikipedia://en/7788156) satisfying any of the following equivalent conditions:[^o4]

1. *K* is a [pseudoelementary class](wikipedia://en/12747956) closed under [subalgebras](wikipedia://en/45239#Subalgebras_in_universal_algebra) and [direct products](wikipedia://en/8887).
1. *K* is the class of all [models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28logic%29) of a set of [quasi-identities](wikipedia://en/12748025), that is, implications of the form $s_1 \approx t_1 \land \ldots \land s_n \approx t_n \rightarrow s \approx t$, where $s, s_1, \ldots, s_n,t, t_1, \ldots, t_n$ are [terms](wikipedia://en/24885593) built up from variables using the operation symbols of the specified signature.
1. *K* contains a trivial algebra and is closed under [isomorphisms](wikipedia://en/14828), subalgebras, and [reduced products](wikipedia://en/2174269).
1. *K* contains a trivial algebra and is closed under isomorphisms, subalgebras, direct products, and [ultraproducts](wikipedia://en/273642).

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every [variety](wikipedia://en/1021753) is a quasivariety by virtue of an equation being a quasi-identity for which *n* = 0.

The [cancellative semigroups](wikipedia://en/22772051) form a quasivariety.

Let *K* be a quasivariety. Then the class of [orderable algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orderable_algebra) from *K* forms a quasivariety, since the preservation-of-order axioms are [Horn clauses](wikipedia://en/333824).[^o7]

:::
