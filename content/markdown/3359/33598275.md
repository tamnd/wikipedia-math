# Graham Brightwell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graham Brightwell"}

**Graham Brightwell** is a British mathematician working in the field of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492).[^o4]

:::

A professor at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics), he has published nearly 100 papers in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210), including over a dozen with [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956). His research interests include [random combinatorial structures](wikipedia://en/5170); partially ordered sets; [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms); [random graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_graphs); [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o6] (Bollobás supervised his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) on "Linear Extensions of Partially Ordered Sets" at [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), awarded 1988.)

## Othello {#Othello}

Brightwell started playing [Othello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reversi) in 1985, after finding himself sharing an apartment with fellow mathematician and Othello player [Imre Leader](wikipedia://en/3756629).[^o9] He has finished three times as runner-up in the World Othello Championship and is a 5-time British Champion, and has served as chairman of the British Othello Federation and as editor of the British Othello Newsletter.[^o10][^o11] He created the Brightwell Quotient, often used in Othello tournaments, to resolve ties.
