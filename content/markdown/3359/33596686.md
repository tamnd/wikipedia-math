# Rupert Lodge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rupert Lodge"}

**Rupert Clendon Lodge** (1886–1961) was an Anglo-Canadian philosopher, "the most widely read of all philosophers in Canada".[^o2]

:::

Lodge was born in [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England), but spent most of his academic career at the [University of Manitoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manitoba), where he taught from 1920 to 1947. [Marshall McLuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_McLuhan) was a student of Lodge in the early 1930s.[^o3] Lodge's works on Plato remain influential, and were reissued by Routledge in the 2000s and 2010s.

## Works {#Works}

- (tr.) *The great problems* by [Bernardino Varisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernardino_Varisco). London: G. Allen & Co., 1914.
- *The meaning and function of simple modes in the philosophy of John Locke*, 1918.
- *An Introduction to Logic*, 1920.
- *Plato's theory of ethics: the moral criterion and the highest good*, 1928. In the series [The International Library of Psychology, Philosophy and Scientific Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_International_Library_of_Psychology,_Philosophy_and_Scientific_Method).
- *Philosophy of education*, 1937.
- *The questioning mind; a survey of philosophical tendencies*, 1937
- *Philosophy of business*, Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1945.
- *Plato's theory of education*, 1947.
- *The great thinkers*, 1949.
- *Applied philosophy*, 1951.
- *Plato's theory of art*, 1953.
- *The philosophy of Plato*, 1956.
