# Peter Topping

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Topping"}

*Image: Peter Topping.jpg.* Peter Topping

**Peter Topping** (born 1971) is a British mathematician working in [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474).

:::

He obtained his PhD in 1997 at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) under the supervision of Mario Joseph Micallef. He is currently Professor at the University of Warwick.[^o3]

In 2005 he was awarded the LMS [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) and in 2006 he was awarded the [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize).[^o4]

Topping is the author of the 2006 book *Lectures on the Ricci Flow*. He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 2014 at Seoul.
