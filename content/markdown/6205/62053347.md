# Greta Panova

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Greta Panova"}

**Greta Cvetanova Panova**[^o3] (Грета Цветанова Панова, born 1983 in [Sofia, Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia)) is a [Bulgarian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She is a professor of mathematics and Gabilan Distinguished Professor in Science and Engineering at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles). Her research interests include [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Panova received her B.S. in 2005 from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT). She received M.A. in 2006 from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and Ph.D. in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 2011, under the supervision of [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397). She was then a postdoc at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) (2011–2014), Assistant and Associate Professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) (2014–2018), and is currently a tenured Professor at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).[^o5] In 2023, she was named Gabilan Distinguished Professor.[^o6]

Panova research is in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) with applications to [geometric complexity theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_complexity_theory), probability and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481).[^o17] , she is a co-Editor-in-Chief of the *[Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*, and a member of the editorial board of [*Algebraic Combinatorics*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Combinatorics_%28journal%29) and the *[Arnold Mathematical Journal](wikipedia://en/59868645)*.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Selected awards {#Selected_awards}

Panova was a three time medalist at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (1999–2001, one gold and two silver medals) and a third prize winner at the [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) (2001).[^o11][^o12] She is a recipient of Katz Fellowship (UC Berkeley), Putnam Fellowship (Harvard), James Mills Peirce Fellowship (Harvard), [Simons Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) (UCLA), and von Neumann Fellowship ([IAS](wikipedia://en/184179)).[^o13] Panova was an invited plenary speaker at the 2017 [International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/75145047) and at the 2023 [Current Developments in Mathematics Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Developments_in_Mathematics_Conference).[^o14][^o15] Panova is the 2020 recipient of the IMI Award, awarded once every three years by the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics at the [Bulgarian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Academy_of_Science) to a Bulgarian citizen under the age of 40 for high achievements in the field of mathematics,[^o16] and the 2024–5 Birman Fellow of the [American Mathematics Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematics_Society).[^o17]
