# Jennifer Mueller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jennifer Mueller"}

**Jennifer L. Mueller** is an [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [biomedical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedical_engineering) whose research concerns [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956) and their applications, particularly to problems in medical imaging related to [electrical impedance tomography](wikipedia://en/904056). She is a professor of mathematics at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University), where she also holds a joint appointment in the school of biomedical engineering and the department of electrical and computer engineering.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mueller completed a Ph.D. in 1997 at the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln). Her dissertation, *Inverse Problems in Singular Differential Equations*, was supervised by Thomas S. Shores. After postdoctoral research at the [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) she joined the Colorado State Mathematics Department in 2000, and became a founding member of the School of Biomedical Engineering in 2007. She was promoted to full professor in 2011.

## Book {#Book}

With Samuli Siltanen, Mueller is the author of the book *Linear and Nonlinear Inverse Problems with Practical Applications* (SIAM, 2012).
