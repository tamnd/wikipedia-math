# Woo circles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Woo circles"}

*Image: Woo circles.svg.* Two of infinitely many Woo circles (green) all have the center on the Schoch line (cyan)

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Woo circles**, introduced by Peter Y. Woo, are a set of [infinitely many](wikipedia://en/20110824) [Archimedean circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_circle).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

Form an [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197) with the two inner [semicircles](wikipedia://en/617831) [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) at point *C*. Let *m* denote any [nonnegative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonnegative) [real number](wikipedia://en/20646438). Draw two circles, with [radii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radii) *m* times the radii of the smaller two arbelos semicircles, centered on the arbelos ground line, also tangent to each other at point *C* and with radius *m* times the radius of the corresponding small arbelos arc. Any circle centered on the [Schoch line](wikipedia://en/16875708) and externally [tangent to the circles](wikipedia://en/19491492) is a Woo circle.[^o4]

:::
