# Evectant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Evectant"}

In [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), an **evectant** is a contravariant constructed from an invariant by acting on it with a [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886) called an **evector**. Evectants and evectors were introduced by James Sylvester.[^o2]

:::
