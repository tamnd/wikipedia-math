# Theodor Spieker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodor Spieker"}

**Theodor Spieker** (8 August 1823 – 9 April 1913) was a German mathematician, a teacher at a [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) in [Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potsdam).[^o2]

:::

Spieker's geometry textbook Lehrbuch der ebenen Geometrie mit Übungs-Aufgaben für höhere Lehranstalten (Verlag von August Stein, Potsdam, 1862)[^o3][^o4] was republished in many editions. A copy of this textbook was given to [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736) by his tutor when Einstein was twelve, and quickly led Einstein to become interested in higher mathematics.[^o5]

Spieker is the namesake of the [Spieker circle](wikipedia://en/9502005) of a triangle (the circle inscribed in its [medial triangle](wikipedia://en/3339743)) and the [Spieker center](wikipedia://en/35728942) (the center of the Spieker circle).
