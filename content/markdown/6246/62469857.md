# Kac–Bernstein theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kac–Bernstein theorem"}

The **Kac–Bernstein theorem** is one of the earliest and most well-known characterization theorems in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711). It states that the independence of the sum and difference of two independent random variables characterizes the [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462) (Gaussian distribution). The theorem was proved independently by the Polish-American mathematician [Mark Kac](wikipedia://en/1062425)[^o2] and the Soviet mathematician [Sergei Bernstein](wikipedia://en/976516)[^o3].

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $\xi$ and $\eta$ be independent random variables. If $\xi+\eta$ and $\xi-\eta$ are independent, then $\xi$ and $\eta$ are normally distributed (Gaussian).

A generalization of the Kac–Bernstein theorem is the [Darmois–Skitovich theorem](wikipedia://en/62469606), in which the normal distribution is characterized by the independence of two linear forms of $n$ independent random variables.

:::

## Generalizations to locally compact Abelian groups {#Generalizations_to_locally_compact_Abelian_groups}

The first works devoted to generalizing the Kac–Bernstein theorem to locally compact Abelian groups are due to A. L. Rukhin[^o7] and also to H. Heyer and Ch. Rall[^o8]. These works studied the following problem. Let $X$ be a [locally compact Abelian group](wikipedia://en/454325), and let $\xi$ and $\eta$ be independent random variables taking values in $X$ with distributions $\mu$ and $\nu$. For which groups $X$ does the independence of $\xi+\eta$ and $\xi-\eta$ imply that $\mu$ and $\nu$ are convolutions of [Gaussian](wikipedia://en/66573106) and idempotent distributions? Some sufficient conditions were established in [^o7] and [^o8]. The final result was obtained by [G. M. Feldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gennadiy_Feldman), who proved the following theorem.

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem**[^o10]; see also [^o11]. Let $X$ be a second countable locally compact Abelian group. Let $\xi$ and $\eta$ be independent random variables taking values in $X$ with distributions $\mu$ and $\nu$. Then the independence of $\xi+\eta$ and $\xi-\eta$ implies that $\mu$ and $\nu$ are convolutions of Gaussian and idempotent distributions if and only if the connected component of the zero of the group $X$ contains no elements of order 2.

:::
