# Shahar Mozes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shahar Mozes"}

**Shahar Mozes** (שחר מוזס) is an Israeli mathematician.[^o3]

:::

In 1991, Mozes received his doctorate from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) with thesis *Actions of Cartan subgroups* under the supervision of [Hillel Fürstenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillel_Fürstenberg).[^o5] At the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Mozes became in 1993 a senior lecturer, in 1996 associate professor, and in 2002 a full professor.

Moses does research on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and discrete subgroups of Lie groups, [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), and [aperiodic tilings](wikipedia://en/868145). His collaborators include [Jean Bourgain](wikipedia://en/1024131), [Alex Eskin](wikipedia://en/31883666), [Elon Lindenstrauss](wikipedia://en/28371257), [Gregory Margulis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Margulis), and [Hee Oh](wikipedia://en/40932180).

In 2000, Mozes received the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize). In 1998 he was an invited speaker with talk *Products of trees, lattices and simple groups* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Berlin.[^o6] He was a plenary speaker at the ICM Satellite Conference on "Geometry Topology and Dynamics in Negative Curvature" held at the [Raman Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raman_Research_Institute) of the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS) from August 2 to August 7, 2010.[^o7][^o8]
