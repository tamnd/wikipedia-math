# Wiesława Nizioł

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wiesława Nizioł"}

**Wiesława Krystyna Nizioł** (pronounced 'v) is a Polish mathematician, director of research at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), based at Institut mathématique de Jussieu. Her research concerns [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and in particular [*p*-adic Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/26086853), [Galois representations](wikipedia://en/486929), and [*p*-adic cohomology](wikipedia://en/46927724).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nizioł earned an M.S. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1984. She was employed as an assistant professor at the University of Warsaw from 1984 to 1988.

After beginning doctoral studies in computer science at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) under the supervision of [Andrew Yao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Yao), she followed him to Princeton University, but she switched to mathematics,[^o4] and received her Ph.D. in 1991 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226).

Thereafter she held temporary positions at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) before joining the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1996. More recently, she has spent time at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179)[^o6] in 2010 as a visitor and in 2017 and 2024 as a member as well as at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute)[^o7] in 2014, 2018 and 2023 as part of programs on [perfectoid spaces](wikipedia://en/39197973), the [homological conjectures](wikipedia://en/490135) and [Euler systems](wikipedia://en/1052176), respectively.

She moved to France in 2012 as a directrice de recherches at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), first in [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) and, since 2020 at Institut mathématique de Jussieu in Paris.

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

She studies the cohomology of $p$-adic varieties. Her contributions include:

- Comparison theorems, via motivic methods, between [de Rham](wikipedia://en/221530) and $p$-adic [étale](wikipedia://en/396231) cohomologies of algebraic varieties over $p$-adic fields (proofs[^o9][^o10] of the conjectures $C_{\mathrm{cris}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{st}}$ of [Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Marc_Fontaine)).
- A definition[^o11][^o12] for $p$-adic algebraic varieties, of a $p$-adic analog (the syntomic cohomology) of the classical [Deligne cohomology](wikipedia://en/31615715) for algebraic varieties over the real numbers.
- A comparison theorem,[^o13] via [syntomic methods](wikipedia://en/31870479), for $p$-adic analytic varieties, and the computation[^o14][^o15] of the $p$-adic étale cohomology of various $p$-adic symmetric spaces with applications to the $p$-adic local [Langlands correspondence](wikipedia://en/171905).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was an [Invited Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at the 2006 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), with a talk entitled "*p*-adic motivic cohomology in arithmetic".[^o17] She was elected a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2021,[^o18] and as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.[^o19]. She is recipient of the Sierpiński Medal of the University of Warsaw and the Polish Mathematical Society, 2025 [^o20]
