# Hermann Vermeil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Vermeil"}

**Hermann Vermeil** (1889–1959) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who produced the first published proof that the [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) is the only absolute invariant among those of prescribed type suitable for [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)’s theory.[^o3] The theorem was proved by him in 1917[^o4] when he was [Hermann Weyl](wikipedia://en/187544)'s assistant.

:::
