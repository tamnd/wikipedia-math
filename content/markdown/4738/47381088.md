# Jun O'Hara

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jun O'Hara"}

**Jun O'Hara**, legally named **Jun Imai**, is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works on the fields of [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). He is a professor at [Chiba University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiba_University).

:::

He is known for his discovery of [Möbius energy](wikipedia://en/17716960), a type of knot energy.[^o2][^o3]

He was born on 29 March 1963 in [Hiroshima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiroshima), Japan.

He was a PhD student of Takashi Tsuboi at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo).
