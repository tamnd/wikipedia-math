In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-7 dodecahedral honeycomb** is a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)).

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,7}, it has seven [dodecahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodecahedra) {5,3} around each edge. All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with infinitely many dodecahedra existing around each vertex in an [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365) [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644).
