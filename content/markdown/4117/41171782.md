# Colin J. Bushnell

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Colin J. Bushnell"}

**Colin John Bushnell** (1947 – 1 January 2021) was a British mathematician specialising in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He spent most of his career at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), including a stint as the head of the School of Physical Sciences and Engineering, and made several contributions to the representation theory of [reductive](wikipedia://en/1088386) [*p*-adic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_group) and the [local Langlands correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Langlands_correspondence).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Bushnell was born in 1947.[^o4] He studied mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London), where he received his first class honors undergraduate degree and then a Ph.D. in 1972 under the supervision of [Albrecht Fröhlich](wikipedia://en/1096590).[^o5][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

From 1972 to 1975, Bushnell was a lecturer at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign).[^o5] He returned to King's College London in 1975 as Lecturer, before being promoted to Reader in 1985 and Professor in 1990.[^o5] From 1988 to 1989, he was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o7] From 1996 to 1997, he was a chairman of the mathematics department and from 1997 to 2004 he was the head of the School of Physical Sciences and Engineering.[^o5] He retired in 2014.[^o5] He died on 1 January 2021 at the age of 73.[^o4]

Bushnell has advised doctoral students including [Graham Everest](wikipedia://en/42017535).

## Research {#Research}

Bushnell's research included "major contributions to the representation theory of [reductive *p*-adic groups](wikipedia://en/1088386) and the study of the [local Langlands correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Langlands_correspondence)."[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1994, Bushnell was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Zurich (*Smooth representations of p-adic groups: the role of compact open subgroups*).[^o5]

In 1995, Bushnell was awarded the [Senior Whitehead Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Whitehead_Prize).[^o5] In 2002, he became a Fellow of King's College London.[^o5] He was inaugurated in the 2013 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5][^o11]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- With [Albrecht Fröhlich](wikipedia://en/1096590), *Gauss sums and p-adic division algebras,* lecture notes in mathematics, vol. 987, Springer Verlag 1983
- With [Guy Henniart](wikipedia://en/31961003), *The local Langlands conjecture for GL(2),* Springer-Verlag, 2006, (Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften 335)
- With [Philip Kutzko](wikipedia://en/33587856), *The admissible dual of GL(N) via compact open subgroups,* [Annals of Mathematics Studies](wikipedia://en/83104341) 129, Princeton University Press 1993
