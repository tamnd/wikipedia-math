# Roy Adler

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Roy Adler"}

**Roy Lee Adler** (February 22, 1931 – July 26, 2016[^o4]) was an American mathematician.

:::

Adler earned his Ph.D. in 1961 from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) under the supervision of [Shizuo Kakutani](wikipedia://en/2572542) (*On some algebraic aspects of measure preserving transformations*).[^o5] He then worked as a mathematician for IBM at the [Thomas J. Watson Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Watson_Research_Center).

Adler studies [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [symbolic](wikipedia://en/1986263) and [topological dynamics](wikipedia://en/16379713) and [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869). The [road coloring problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_coloring_problem) that was solved by [Avraham Trakhtman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avraham_Trakhtman) in 2007 came from him, along with [L. W. Goodwyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._W._Goodwyn) and [Benjamin Weiss](wikipedia://en/40423267).

He was a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] A paper was written on his work and the impact of his work by Bruce Kitchens and others.[^o7]

## Writings {#Writings}

- With [Brian Marcus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Marcus): *Topological entropy and equivalence of dynamical systems*. Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society. **20** (1979), no 219.
- With [Benjamin Weiss](wikipedia://en/40423267): *Similarity of automorphisms of the torus*, Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society (1970), no 98.[^o9]
- [*Symbolic dynamics and Markov partitions*](https://www.ams.org/journals/bull/1998-35-01/S0273-0979-98-00737-X/), Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society. **35** (1998), no 1, 1–57.
- With L. Wayne Goodwyn and Benjamin Weiss: [*Equivalence of topological Markov shifts*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02761605), [Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics). **27** (1977), 49–63.
- With [Alan Konheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Konheim) and M. H. McAndrew: *Topological Entropy*, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society. **114** (1965), 309–319.
- With Tomasz Downarowicz and [Michał Misiurewicz](wikipedia://en/29006092): [Topological Entropy](http://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Topological_entropy). Scholarpedia. **3** (2008), no 2, 2200.
- With Charles Tresser and Patrick A. Worfolk: *Topological conjugacy of linear endomorphisms of the 2-torus*, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society. **349** (1997), 1633–1652.
- With Benjamin Weiss: *Entropy, a complete metric invariant for automorphisms of the torus*, Proceeding of the National Academy of Sciences. **57** (1967), 1573–1576.
