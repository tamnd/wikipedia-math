# Joseph Lipman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Lipman"}

**Joseph Lipman** (born June 15, 1938)[^o3] is a Canadian-American mathematician, working in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Lipman graduated from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) with a bachelor's degree in 1960 and then went to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), receiving his master's degree in 1961. He then earned a Ph.D. there in 1965 under the supervision of [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352). In 1965 he was an assistant professor at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in Kingston and in 1966 was an assistant professor at the [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), where he became professor in 1971. From 1987 to 1992, there, he was head of the mathematics department. He was a member of the MSRI and visiting scholar at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice) and a visiting professor at the [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and Harvard University.

He specializes in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763) in algebraic geometry.[^o4]

In 1958, while studying at the University of Toronto, he became a Putnam Fellow both in the spring and fall [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition).[^o5] In 1982 he received the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
