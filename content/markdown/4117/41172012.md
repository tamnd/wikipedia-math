# Hugo Rossi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hugo Rossi"}

**Hugo E. Rossi** (born 1935)[^o3] is an American mathematician working in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

Rossi graduated from the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York) with bachelor's degree in 1956, and graduated from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) with the master's degree in 1957, and received a Ph.D. under the supervision of [Isadore Singer](wikipedia://en/324564) in 1960 (*Maximality of algebras of holomorphic functions*).[^o4] In 1960 he became an assistant professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), and in the same year at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). In 1963 he became an associate professor and a professor at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1966. After 11 years at Brandeis and two years as the department chair, he moved to the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1975, and he served as dean of the [College of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah_College_of_Science) from 1987 to 1993.[^o5] In 1989 Rossi went on temporary leave from his post as dean to serve as director of the [National Cold Fusion Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cold_Fusion_Institute).[^o6] Amid increasing concerns about the lack of conclusive results regarding [cold fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_fusion), Rossi resigned and returned to his post as dean of the College.[^o7]

From 1983 to 1984, he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey. From 1980 to 1985, he was the editor of the *Pacific Journal of Mathematics* and from 1973 to 1978 a co-editor of transactions of the American Mathematical Society. He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] He served as chairman of the board of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) from 1985 to 1989 and twice as deputy directory, from 1997 to 1995 and 2003 to 2005.[^o9]
