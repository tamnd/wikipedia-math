# Jürgen Jost

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jürgen Jost"}

**Jürgen Jost** (born 9 June 1956) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in geometry. He has been a director of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) since 1996.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

In 1975, he began studying mathematics, physics, economics and philosophy. In 1980 he received a [Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.) from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) under the supervision of Stefan Hildebrandt. In 1984 he was at the University of Bonn for the habilitation. After his habilitation, he was at the [Ruhr University Bochum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr_University_Bochum), the chair of Mathematics X, Analysis. During this time he was the coordinator of the project "Stochastic Analysis and systems with infinitely many degrees of freedom" from July 1987 to December 1996. For this work he received the 1993 [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize), awarded by the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft).

Since 1996, he has been director and scientific member at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences in Leipzig, having left Bochum after more than 10 years of work there, to "tackle new research problems in the border area between mathematics and the natural sciences and simultaneously encourage mathematical research in Germany, particularly in the fields of geometry and analysis."[^o5]

In 1998 he was an honorary professor at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig). In 2002, there, he initiated with two other scientists from the Max Planck Institute, the Interdisciplinary Center for Bioinformatics (IZBI).

In 1986 he was [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in Berkeley (*Two dimensional geometric variational problems*). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

His research focuses are:

- [Complex dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_dynamical_system)
- [Neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network)
- [Cognitive structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_structure) and [theoretical neurobiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_neurobiology), [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science), theoretical and [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology)
- [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243); analysis and geometry
- [Calculus of variations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_variation) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564); [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)
