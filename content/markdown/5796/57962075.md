# Taivo Arak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taivo Arak"}

**Taivo Arak** (2 November 1946, [Tallinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn) – 17 October 2007, [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm)) was an Estonian mathematician, specializing in probability theory.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1969 he graduated from [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg_State_University).[^o3] There he received in 1972 his Russian candidate degree (Ph.D.) under [I. A. Ibragimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ildar_Abdulovich_Ibragimov).

In 1983 Arak defended his dissertation for his [Russian doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_nauk) (higher doctoral degree similar to [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)).

From 1972 to 1981 he worked at the [Tallinn University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn_University_of_Technology). From 1981 he worked at the Institute of Cybernetics of the Academy of Sciences of the Estonian SSR.[^o5] In 1986 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o6]

Most of his research dealt with the theory of probability.

## Awards {#Awards}

- Markov Prize (1983) - for the series of papers "Равномерные предельные теоремы для сумм независимых случайных величин" (Uniform limit theorems for sums of independent random variables).
