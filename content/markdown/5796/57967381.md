# William Hamilton Meeks, III

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Hamilton Meeks, III"}

**William Hamilton Meeks, III** (August 8, 1947 - May 24, 2026) was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Meeks studied at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), with a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) in 1971, a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in 1974, and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1975 with supervisor [H. Blaine Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Blaine_Lawson) and thesis *The Conformal Structure and Geometry of Triply Periodic Minimal Surfaces in $\mathbb R ^ 3$*.[^o5] He was an assistant professor in 1975–1977 at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA), in 1977–1978 at the [Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_de_Matemática_Pura_e_Aplicada) (IMPA), and in 1978–1979 at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). From 1979 to 1983 he was a professor at IMPA. He was from 1983 to 1984 a visiting member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and from 1984 to 1986 a professor at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) with the academic year 1985–1986 spent as a visiting professor at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara). From 1986 to 2018 he was the George David Birkhoff Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Massachusetts, Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts,_Amherst).[^o6] Later, he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) after assuming professor emeritus status at UMass Amherst.[^o7]

He was known as an expert on minimal surfaces and their computer graphics visualization; on the latter subject he collaborated with [David Allen Hoffman](wikipedia://en/63668906). For the academic year 2006/07 Meeks was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow).[^o6]

In 1986 at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), he was Invited Speaker with talk *Recent progress on the geometry of surfaces in $R ^ 3$ and on the use of computer graphics as a research tool*.[^o6]
