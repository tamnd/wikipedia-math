# Panos Papasoglu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Panos Papasoglu"}

**Panos Papasoglu** (Παναγιώτης Παπάζογλου; original name is also [transliterated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transliteration) in English as Panagiotis Papazoglou) is a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), Lecturer of Mathematics at the Mathematics Department of the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o2] His main research interests are [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).

:::

He got his doctorate under [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910) in [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1993. Papasoglou taught at [University of Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XI) until he returned to Greece in the early 2000s. He taught at the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) until the late 2000s.[^o3]
