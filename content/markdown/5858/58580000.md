# Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind"}

The **Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind**[^o6][^o2] $\psi_{n}(x)$, also known as the **Fontana–Bessel polynomials**,[^o3] are the polynomials defined by the following generating function:

$$\frac{z(1+z)^x}{\ln(1+z)}= \sum_{n=0}^\infty z^n \psi_n(x) ,\qquad |z|<1.$$

:::

The first five polynomials are:

$$\begin{aligned} \psi_0(x) &= 1 \\[2mm] \psi_1(x) &= x + \frac{1}{2} \\[2mm] \psi_2(x) &= \frac{1}{2} x^2 - \frac{1}{12} \\[2mm] \psi_3(x) &= \frac{1}{6} x^3 - \frac{1}{4} x^2 + \frac{1}{24} \\[2mm] \psi_4(x) &= \frac{1}{24} x^4 - \frac{1}{6} x^3 + \frac{1}{6} x^2 - \frac{19}{720} \end{aligned}$$

Some authors define these polynomials slightly differently[^o4][^o5]

$$\frac{z \left(1+z\right)^x}{\ln(1+z)} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{z^n}{n!} \psi^*_n(x) ,\qquad |z|<1,$$

so that $\psi^*_n(x) = \psi_n(x) \, n!$ and may also use a different notation for them (the most used alternative notation is $b_{n}(x)$). Under this convention, the polynomials form a [Sheffer sequence](wikipedia://en/218596).

The Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind were largely studied by the Hungarian mathematician Charles Jordan,[^o6][^o2] but their history may also be traced back to much earlier works.[^o3]

## Integral representations {#Integral_representations}

The Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind may be represented via these integrals[^o6][^o2]

$$\psi_{n}(x) = \int_x^{x+1}\! \binom{u}{n} \, du = \int_0^1 \binom{x+u}{n} \, du$$

as well as[^o3]

$$\begin{aligned} \psi_{n}(x) &= \frac{\left(-1\right)^{n+1}}{\pi} \int_0^\infty \frac{\pi \cos\pi x - \sin\pi x \ln z}{(1+z)^n} \cdot\frac{z^x dz}{\ln^2 z +\pi^2} ,\qquad -1\leq x\leq n-1\, \\[3mm] \psi_{n}(x) &= \frac{\left(-1\right)^{n+1}}{\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{\pi \cos\pi x - v\sin\pi x }{\left(1+e^v\right)^n} \cdot \frac{e^{v(x+1)} }{v^2 +\pi^2}\, dv ,\qquad -1\leq x\leq n-1\, \end{aligned}$$

These polynomials are, therefore, up to a constant, the [antiderivative](wikipedia://en/2823) of the [binomial coefficient](wikipedia://en/4668) and also that of the [falling factorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falling_factorial).[^o6][^o2][^o3]

::: {#o9 .statement cue=named-heading name="Explicit formula"}

## Explicit formula {#Explicit_formula}

For an arbitrary $n$, these polynomials may be computed explicitly via the following summation formula[^o6][^o2][^o3]

$$\psi_{n}(x) = \frac{1}{(n-1)!}\sum_{l=0}^{n-1} \frac{s(n-1,l)}{l+1} x^{l+1} + G_{n}, \qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots$$

where $s(n,l)$ are the signed [Stirling numbers of the first kind](wikipedia://en/2229296) and $G_{n}$ are the [Gregory coefficients](wikipedia://en/50593506).

The expansion of the Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind into a Newton series reads[^o6][^o2]

$$\psi_{n}(x) = G_0 \binom{x}{n} + G_1 \binom{x}{n-1} + G_2 \binom{x}{n-2} + \ldots + G_n$$

It can be shown using the second integral representation and [Vandermonde's identity](wikipedia://en/916157).

:::

::: {#o11 .statement cue=named-heading name="Recurrence formula"}

## Recurrence formula {#Recurrence_formula}

The Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind satisfy the recurrence relation[^o6][^o2]

$$\psi_{n}(x+1) - \psi_{n}(x) = \psi_{n-1}(x)$$

or equivalently

$$\Delta\psi_{n}(x) = \psi_{n-1}(x)$$

The repeated difference produces[^o6][^o2]

$$\Delta^m\psi_{n}(x) = \psi_{n-m}(x)$$

:::

## Symmetry property {#Symmetry_property}

The main property of the symmetry reads[^o2][^o4]

$$\psi_{n}{\left(\tfrac{1}{2}n-1+x\right)} = \left(-1\right)^n \psi_{n}{\left(\tfrac{1}{2}n-1-x\right)}$$

## Some further properties and particular values {#Some_further_properties_and_particular_values}

Some properties and particular values of these polynomials include

$$\begin{aligned} &\psi_n(0) = G_n \\[2mm] &\psi_n(1) = G_{n-1} + G_{n} \\[2mm] &\psi_n(-1) = \left(-1\right)^{n+1} \sum_{m=0}^n \left|G_m\right| = \left(-1\right)^n C_n\\[2mm] &\psi_n(n-2) = - \left|G_n\right| \\[2mm] &\psi_n(n-1) = \left(-1\right)^n \psi_n(-1) = 1 - \sum_{m=1}^n \left|G_m\right| \\[2mm] &\psi_{2n}(n-1) = M_{2n} \\[2mm] &\psi_{2n}(n-1+y) = \psi_{2n}(n-1-y) \\[2mm] &\psi_{2n+1}(n-\tfrac{1}{2}+y) = -\psi_{2n+1}(n-\tfrac{1}{2}-y) \\[2mm] &\psi_{2n+1}(n-\tfrac{1}{2}) = 0 \end{aligned}$$

where $C_{n}$ are the *Cauchy numbers of the second kind* and $M_{n}$ are the *central difference coefficients*.[^o6][^o2][^o3]

## Some series involving the Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind {#Some_series_involving_the_Bernoulli_polynomials_of_the_second_kind}

The [digamma function](wikipedia://en/406935) $\Psi(x)$ may be expanded into a series with the Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind in the following way[^o3]

$$\Psi(v) = \ln(v+a) + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^n\psi_{n}(a)\,(n-1)!}{(v)_{n}},\qquad \Re(v) > -a,$$

and hence[^o3]

$$\gamma= -\ln(a+1) - \sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n \psi_{n}(a)}{n},\qquad \Re(a)>-1$$

and

$$\gamma = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{2n}\left\{\psi_{n}(a)+ \psi_{n}\left(-\frac{a}{1+a}\right)\right\}, \quad a>-1$$

where $\gamma$ is [Euler's constant](wikipedia://en/148594). Furthermore, we also have[^o3]

$$\Psi(v) = \frac{1}{v + a - \frac{1}{2}} \left\{\ln\Gamma(v+a) + v - \frac{1}{2}\ln(2\pi) - \frac{1}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\left(-1\right)^n \psi_{n+1}(a)}{(v)_{n}} \left(n-1\right)!\right\}, \quad \Re(v)>-a,$$

where $\Gamma(x)$ is the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316). The [Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/406952) and [Riemann zeta functions](wikipedia://en/25809) may be expanded into these polynomials as follows[^o3]

$$\zeta(s,v) = \frac{(v+a)^{1-s} }{s-1} + \sum_{n=0}^\infty (-1)^n \psi_{n+1}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(-1\right)^k \binom{n}{k} (k+v)^{-s}$$

and

$$\zeta(s)= \frac{(a+1)^{1-s} }{s-1} + \sum_{n=0}^\infty (-1)^n \psi_{n+1}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(-1\right)^k \binom{n}{k} (k+1)^{-s}$$

and also

$$\zeta(s) = 1 + \frac{(a+2)^{1-s}}{s-1} + \sum_{n=0}^\infty (-1)^n \psi_{n+1}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(-1\right)^k \binom{n}{k} (k+2)^{-s}$$

The Bernoulli polynomials of the second kind are also involved in the following relationship[^o3]

$$\big(v+a-\tfrac{1}{2}\big)\zeta(s,v) = -\frac{\zeta(s-1,v+a)}{s-1} + \zeta(s-1,v) + \sum_{n=0}^\infty \left(-1\right)^n \psi_{n+2}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(-1\right)^k \binom{n}{k} (k+v)^{-s}$$

between the zeta functions, as well as in various formulas for the [Stieltjes constants](wikipedia://en/1479085), e.g.[^o3]

$$\gamma_m(v) = -\frac{\ln^{m+1}(v+a)}{m+1} + \sum_{n=0}^\infty (-1)^n \psi_{n+1}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(-1\right)^k \binom{n}{k}\frac{\ln^m (k+v)}{k+v}$$

and

$$\gamma_m(v)=\frac{1}{\tfrac{1}{2}-v-a} \left\{\frac{(-1)^m}{m+1}\,\zeta^{(m+1)}(0,v+a)- (-1)^m \zeta^{(m)}(0,v) - \sum_{n=0}^\infty (-1)^n \psi_{n+2}(a) \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k \binom{n}{k}\frac{\ln^m (k+v)}{k+v}\right\}$$

which are both valid for $\Re(a) > -1$ and $v \in \mathbb{C}\setminus\!\{0,-1,-2,\ldots\}$.
