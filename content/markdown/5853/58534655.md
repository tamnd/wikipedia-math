# Herbert Ellsworth Slaught

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Herbert Ellsworth Slaught"}

**Herbert Ellsworth Slaught** (1861–1937) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was president of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) and editor of the journal *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)*.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Slaught, born in the [Finger Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_Lakes) area, left his place of birth when he was 13 years old, due to the bankruptcy of the family's farm. The family moved to [Hamilton, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_New_York), where he studied at [Colgate University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colgate_University),[^n1] graduating in 1883. After teaching some years at the [Peddie School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peddie_School) ([Hightstown, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hightstown,_New_Jersey)), he received in 1892 a fellowship from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago),[^n2] where he was awarded a PhD in 1898. Slaught remained as professor at Chicago for the rest of his academic life, till his retirement in 1931.

In 1907, he became editor of the *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)*, a journal addressed to secondary teachers of mathematics. During his years as editor he worked heavily to broad the basis of the journal.[^n3] He was founding member of the [National Council of Teachers of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1561691) and an early member of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^n4] He did not do research in mathematics, but his inspired teaching encouraged a lot of young people to follow a career in this matter.

Slaught was the main initiator of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA), which publishes the *American Mathematical Monthly*. His idea was to form a non-profit organization to publish a mathematical journal supported by the dues paid by the organization's members. He stated that the purpose of the *American Mathematical Monthly* is "to *stimulate activity* on the part of college teachers ... that may lead to *production*".[^n5]
