# List of global sustainability statistics

List

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of global sustainability statistics"}

**Global sustainability statistics** are benchmarks for measuring the status of sustainability parameters. The following agencies provide baseline data for sustainability governance. They are just one form of data used for [sustainability accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainability_accounting) and are valuable for assessing trends and measuring progress.

:::

**This list provides sources of statistics at the *global* level of governance only.**

- **General lists**
  - Meadows, D.H., Randers, J. & Meadows, D.L. 2004. *[Limits to growth: the 30-year update](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Limits_to_Growth).* Chelsea Green Publishing Company, White River Junction, USA.
  - *The [CIAs World Fact Book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Factbook)*[^o2]
  - *[World Data Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Data_Center)*[^o3]
  - *[United Nations Environmental Indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Environmental_Indicators)*[^o4] Also publications on [environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339) and statistical methods.
    - Water (water resources, water supply industry, waste water)
    - Air pollution (SO<sub>2</sub> & NO<sub>x</sub>),
    - Climate change (greenhouse gas emissions; by sector(absolute & percentage); CO<sub>2</sub> emissions; CH<sub>4</sub> & N<sub>2</sub>O emissions)
    - Waste (municipal waste collection, treatment, hazardous waste)
    - Land use (total land area by country, forest area by country, agricultural area by country).
  - *European Commission ([Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat))*[^o5]
- **Biodiversity**
  - Groombridge, B & Jenkins, M.D. 2002. *World Atlas of Biodiversity.* UNEP World Conservation Monitoring Centre[^o6]
- **Energy**

  - *The [International Energy Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Energy_Agency)*. Key World Energy Statistics[^o8]
  - *[UN Energy Statistics Database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Energy_Statistics_Database)*[^o9]

  - *UN [Food and Agriculture Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Agriculture_Organization)*[^o10]
- **Forests**
  - *UN Food and Agriculture Organization*[^o11]
- **Fertilizer**
  - *[International Fertilizer Industry Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Fertilizer_Industry_Association)*[^o12]
- **Food and agriculture**
  - *UN Food and Agriculture Organization. [FAOSTAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Agriculture_Organization_Corporate_Statistical_Database)*[^o13]
- **Population**
  - *[United Nations Population Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Population_Division)*[^o14]
  - *[United Nations Database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Database)*[^o15]
