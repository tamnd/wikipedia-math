# Gabriella Pinzari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriella Pinzari"}

**Gabriella Pinzari** is an Italian mathematician known for her research on the [$n$-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Pinzari's research on the [$n$-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945) has been described as "the most natural way to apply" the [Kolmogorov–Arnold–Moser theorem](wikipedia://en/16972) to the problem. The original work of [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490) on this theorem attempted to use it to show the stability of the [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System) or similar systems of planetary orbits, but this worked only for the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420) because of a [degeneracy](wikipedia://en/232952) in Arnold's mathematical framework. Pinzari showed how to eliminate this problem, and extended the solution to larger numbers of bodies, by developing "a rotation-invariant version of the KAM theory".

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pinzari earned master's degrees in both physics and mathematics from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), in 1990 and 1996 respectively. She completed her doctorate in 2009 at [Roma Tre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Tre_University) under the supervision of [Luigi Chierchia](wikipedia://en/62545142). She joined the faculty of the [University of Naples Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II) since 2013, and later moved to the [University of Padova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padova).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was an Invited Speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), speaking on her work in the session on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753).
