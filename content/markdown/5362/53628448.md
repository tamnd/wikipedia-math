# Julia Chuzhoy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Chuzhoy"}

**Julia Chuzhoy** (ג'וליה צ'וז'וי) is an Israeli mathematician and computer scientist at the [Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Technological_Institute_at_Chicago), known for her research on [approximation algorithms](wikipedia://en/563105) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chuzhoy earned bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees from the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) in 1998, 2000, and 2004 respectively. Her dissertation, on approximation algorithms, was supervised by [Seffi Naor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Seffi_Naor). She has been at the Toyota Technological Institute since 2007, and also holds a position in the Computer Science Department of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).

## Contributions and recognition {#Contributions_and_recognition}

Chuzhoy won the best paper award at the 2012 [Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Foundations_of_Computer_Science) for her paper with Shi Li on approximating the problem of connecting many given pairs of vertices in a graph by edge-disjoint paths.CL12 She is also known for her work showing a polynomial relation between the size of a [grid graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_graph) [minor](wikipedia://en/353042) of a graph and its [treewidth](wikipedia://en/3987086).CC16 This connection between these two graph properties is a key component of the [Robertson–Seymour theorem](wikipedia://en/351769), is closely related to [Halin's grid theorem](wikipedia://en/38580904) for infinite graphs, and underlies the theory of [bidimensionality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bidimensionality) for graph approximation algorithms.

She was an Invited Speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).
