# Linguistics and Philosophy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Linguistics and Philosophy"}

***Linguistics and Philosophy*** is a peer-reviewed journal which publishes work addressing [meaning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meaning_%28linguistics%29) and structure in [natural language](wikipedia://en/21173).[^o3] It is one of four top journals in formal semantics, alongside [Natural Language Semantics](wikipedia://en/12327811), the [Journal of Semantics](wikipedia://en/12328052), and [Semantics and Pragmatics](wikipedia://en/49204907).[^o4] Papers in the journal tend to emphasize concerns shared by linguists and philosophers, and are intended to be accessible to readers from both fields.[^o5]

:::

The journal is a continuation of the earlier ***Foundations of Language*** which had been founded by [Frits Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frits_Staal) in order to encourage interaction between linguists, philosophers, and logicians.[^o6] The current Editors-in-Chief are philosopher [Michael Glanzberg](wikipedia://en/65305591) ([Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University)) and linguist [Yael Sharvit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yael_Sharvit) ([UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA)).[^o3]
