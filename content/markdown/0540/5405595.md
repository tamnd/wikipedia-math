# Heath-Brown–Moroz constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heath-Brown–Moroz constant"}

The **Heath-Brown–Moroz constant** *C*, named for [Roger Heath-Brown](wikipedia://en/2984550) and [Boris Moroz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Moroz), is defined as

:::

$$C=\prod_p\left(1-\frac{1}{p}\right)^7\left(1+\frac{7p+1}{p^2}\right) = 0.001317641...$$

where *p* runs over the [primes](wikipedia://en/23666).[^o2][^o3]

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

This constant is part of an [asymptotic estimate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_estimate) for the distribution of [rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200) of bounded [height](wikipedia://en/3141855) on the [cubic surface](wikipedia://en/526126) *X*<sub>0</sub><sup>3</sup>=*X*<sub>1</sub>*X*<sub>2</sub>*X*<sub>3</sub>. Let *H* be a positive real number and *N*(*H*) the number of solutions to the equation *X*<sub>0</sub><sup>3</sup>=*X*<sub>1</sub>*X*<sub>2</sub>*X*<sub>3</sub> with all the *X*<sub>*i*</sub> non-negative integers less than or equal to *H* and their [greatest common divisor](wikipedia://en/12354) equal to 1. Then

$$N(H)= C \cdot \frac{H(\log H)^6} {4\times 6!} + O(H(\log H)^5).$$

:::
