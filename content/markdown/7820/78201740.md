# Gabriel Navarro Ortega

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel Navarro Ortega"}

**Gabriel Navarro Ortega** (born in [Sueca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sueca,_Spain), [Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencian_Community)) is a Spanish mathematician specializing in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of finite groups. Currently he is a full professor at the [Universitat de València](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valencia).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Navarro received his PhD at the Universitat de València in 1989. He held a [Fulbright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) post doctoral position at [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) and at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) under the supervision of [I. M. Isaacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Isaacs). He is fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o4] and Distinguished Speaker of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).[^o5]

In 2024 together with [G. Malle](wikipedia://en/58001049), [A. Schaeffer-Fry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Schaeffer-Fry) and [P. H. Tiep](wikipedia://en/58794546), he completed the proof of [Brauer's Height Zero Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brauer's_Height_Zero_Conjecture).[^o6] He also extended the [McKay Conjecture](wikipedia://en/51116107) (with congruences of degrees modulo p with I. M. Isaacs,[^o7] and with [Galois automorphisms](wikipedia://en/12673): the [Galois-McKay conjecture](wikipedia://en/51116107)[^o8]). Together with I. M. Isaacs and G. Malle, he reduced the McKay conjecture to a question of finite simple groups[^o9] establishing the path for its final solution by [M. Cabanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Cabanes) and [B. Späth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._Späth) in 2024. This reduction inspired several other reductions, such as the [Alperin Weight Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alperin_Weight_Conjecture)[^o10] (with P. H. Tiep) or the [Alperin-McKay conjecture](wikipedia://en/51116107)[^o11] (by B. Späth).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- with G. Malle, A. Schaeffer-Fry, P. H. Tiep: *Brauer's Height Zero Conjecture*, Ann. of Math. 200 (2024), 557–608.
- with P. H. Tiep: *The fields of values of characters of degree not divisible by p*. Forum Math. Pi 9 (2021), vol 9, 1-28.
- *Character theory and the McKay conjecture*. Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics, 175. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 2018.
- with Britta Spath: *On Brauer's Height Zero Conjecture*, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 16, 695-747 (2014).
- with P. H. Tiep: *Characters of relative p'-degree with respect to a normal subgroup*, Ann. of Math.178 (3) (2013), 1135–1171.
- with P. H. Tiep: *A reduction theorem for the Alperin weight conjecture*. Invent. Math. 184 (2011), no. 3, 529–565.
- with I. M. Isaacs and G. Malle: *A reduction theorem for the McKay conjecture*. Invent. Math. 170 (2007), no. 1, 33–101.
- *The McKay conjecture and Galois automorphisms*. Ann. of Math. (2) 160 (2004), no. 3, 1129–1140.
- with I. M. Isaacs: *New refinements of the McKay conjecture for arbitrary finite groups*. Ann. of Math. (2) 156 (2002), no. 1, 333–344.
- *Characters and blocks of finite groups*. London Mathematical Society Lecture Note Series, 250. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 1998.
