# Giovanni Battista Mazzini

*Image: Mazzini, Giovanni Battista – Congetture fisico-meccaniche intorno le figure delle particelle componenti il ferro, 1714 – BEIC 12735158.jpg.* *Congetture fisico-meccaniche* (1714)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Battista Mazzini"}

**Giovanni Battista Mazzini** or **Mazini** (1677 – 24 May 1743) was an Italian [physician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physician), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and one of the most fervent advocates of the [iatromechanical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iatrophysics) school.[^o3]

:::
