# Richard Earl Block

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Earl Block"}

**Richard Earl Block** (born 1931) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor) at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) who works on [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras) over [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) of prime [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621).

:::

Block earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1956 under the supervision of [Abraham Adrian Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Adrian_Albert). He was the first to discover the [central extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_extension_%28mathematics%29) of the [Witt algebra](wikipedia://en/1510857) that gives the [Virasoro algebra](wikipedia://en/348029), though his discovery went unnoticed for many years. With Robert Lee Wilson he classified the simple Lie algebras over "well behaved" fields of finite characteristic.

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
