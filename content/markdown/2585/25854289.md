# On Indivisible Lines

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="On Indivisible Lines"}

***On Indivisible Lines*** (Greek Περὶ ἀτόμων γραμμῶν; Latin *De Lineis Insecabilibus*) is a short treatise attributed to [Aristotle](wikipedia://en/308),[^o2] but likely written by a member of the [Peripatetic school](wikipedia://en/529396) some time before the 2nd century BC.

:::

*On Indivisible Lines* seeks to refute [Xenocrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenocrates)' views on lines and minimal parts.
