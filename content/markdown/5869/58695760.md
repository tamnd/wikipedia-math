# Élisabeth Bouscaren

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Élisabeth Bouscaren"}

**Élisabeth Bouscaren** (born 1956)[^o3] is a French mathematician who works on algebraic geometry, algebra and mathematical logic (model theory).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bouscaren received her doctorate in 1979 from the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII) and her habilitation in 1985. From 1981 she worked at the [French National Center for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Center_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS) until 2005, when she moved to the [University of Paris XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_XI). Since 2007, she has held the position of Research Director at CNRS.

She has been a visiting scholar at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) and [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and has published a book on [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981)'s proof of the [Mordell-Lang](wikipedia://en/3141761) conjecture. She was an invited speaker in the logic session of the 2002 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o6]

In 2020, Bouscaren gave the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431), titled *The ubiquity of configurations in Model Theory.*
