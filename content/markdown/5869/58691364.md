# Fang Fuquan

[Fang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fang_%28surname%29)

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Fang Fuquan"}

**Fang Fuquan** (; born October 1964) is a Chinese mathematician. He is one of the leading mathematicians in the fields of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) in China.[^o5] He is a delegate to the [13th National People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_National_People's_Congress).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fang was born in [Tongcheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongcheng,_Anhui), [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui) in October 1964. His father was a small official in his hometown during the [Republic of China (1912-1949)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_%281912-1949%29). Because of his record, he was denounced as a "counterrevolutionary" and suffered [political persecution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_persecution) during the [Cultural Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Revolution). He entered [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huazhong_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in September 1983, majoring in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the Department of Mathematics, where he graduated in 1986.[^o7] After a short period of teaching at his alma mater, he earned his doctor's degree from [Jilin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilin_University) in 1991. Fang pursued advanced studies in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), he did post-doctoral research at [Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg_University_Mainz) from 1993 to 1994.

He returned to China in May 1994 and that year became associate professor at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University). He was a visiting scholar of [Max Planck Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society) from October 1995 to June 1996 and then [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) from July 1996 to June 1997.

He joined the faculty of [Capital Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Normal_University) in November 2004 and was promoted to dean of the Department of Mathematics in July 2015. In July 2016 he was appointed vice-president of Capital Normal University, a position at vice-ministerial level.[^o8]

He was elected as a member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) on November 28, 2017.[^o9]
