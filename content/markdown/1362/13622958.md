# Bryant surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bryant surface"}

In [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), a **Bryant surface** is a 2-dimensional surface embedded in 3-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742) with constant [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210) equal to 1.[^o2][^o3] These surfaces take their name from the geometer [Robert Bryant](wikipedia://en/24204031), who proved that every [simply-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply-connected) [minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734) in 3-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) is [isometric](wikipedia://en/254777) to a Bryant surface by a [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/14110) parameterization analogous to the (Euclidean) [Weierstrass–Enneper parameterization](wikipedia://en/2996193).[^o4]

:::
