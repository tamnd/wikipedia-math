# Gérard Vergnaud

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gérard Vergnaud"}

**Gérard Vergnaud** (8 February 1933 – 6 June 2021) was a French mathematician, philosopher, educator, and psychologist. He earned his doctorate from the International Center for Genetic [Epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology) in Geneva under the [supervision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) of [Jean Piaget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Piaget). Vergnaud was a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) in Paris, where he was a researcher in mathematics. Among his most significant work has been the development of the [Theory of Conceptual Fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Conceptual_Fields), which describes how children develop an understanding of mathematics.[^o3]

:::

Gérard Vergnaud graduated from [HEC Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HEC_Paris) in 1956 and from the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) in 1968.[^o4]
