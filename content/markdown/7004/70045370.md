# Ronen Eldan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronen Eldan"}

**Ronen Eldan** (רונן אלדן) is an Israeli mathematician, working at [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI).[^o3] Previously, Eldan was a professor at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) working on [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) and the theory of [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488).[^o4] He received the 2018 [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize), the 2022 [Blavatnik Award for Young Scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blavatnik_Award_for_Young_Scientists)[^o5] and the 2023 [New Horizons Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933).[^o18][^o6] He was a speaker at the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o7][^o8]

:::
