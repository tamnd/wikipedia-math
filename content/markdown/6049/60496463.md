# Joel Smoller

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joel Smoller"}

**Joel Alan Smoller** (2 January 1936 – 27 September 2017) was an American mathematician.

:::

Joel Smoller was born in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn) on 2 January 1936 to parents Benjamin, a taxicab driver, and Olga, who died when he was young.[^o2][^o3] Smoller attended [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), and completed his doctoral work at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 1963, after which he taught at the University of Michigan. In 1970, he was appointed a full professor, and assumed the [Lamberto Cesari](wikipedia://en/17581349) Collegiate Professorship of Mathematics in 1998.[^o2][^o3] He was editor of *[Transactions of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/1388937)* from 1981 to 1985, and later the *Journal for Applicable Analysis*.[^o4] Smoller was award a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1979,[^o5] the [George David Birkhoff Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_David_Birkhoff_Prize) in 2009,[^o2] and elected to fellowship of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013, a member of its inaugural class of fellows.[^o6] Smoller retired in June 2017, and died, aged 81, on 27 September 2017.[^o2][^o3] Following his death, the Joel Smoller Graduate Fellowship was established.[^o7]
