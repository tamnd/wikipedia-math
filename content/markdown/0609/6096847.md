# Aleksandr Nekrasov

Ivanovich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksandr Nekrasov"}

**Aleksandr Ivanovich Nekrasov** (Алекса́ндр Ива́нович Некра́сов; 9 December 1883 – 21 May 1957) was a Soviet and Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his mathematical contributions to [hydromechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988) and [aeromechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeromechanics). The [Nekrasov integral equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nekrasov_integral_equation) describing surface waves is named for him.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nekrasov was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) where he would remain for the rest of his life. He went to school and graduated from the [University of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1906. Nekrasov graduated with a first class diploma.

Nekrasov earned a gold medal for work of his essay, *Theory of the Satellites of Jupiter*.
