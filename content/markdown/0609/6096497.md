# Nikolay Govorun

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolay Govorun"}

**Nikolay Nikolayevich Govorun** (1930–1989) was a Soviet [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known best for his contributions to [computational mathematics](wikipedia://en/10433833).[^o2]

:::
