# František Wolf

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="František Wolf"}

**František Wolf** (1904–1989) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), specifically the study of the perturbation of [linear operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator).

:::

Wolf was born 1904 in [Prostějov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostějov), then part of the [Austro-Hungarian empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_empire) and now part of the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic), the elder of two children of a furniture maker. He studied physics at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague), and then mathematics at [Masaryk University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masaryk_University) in [Brno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brno) under the supervision of [Otakar Borůvka](wikipedia://en/2923087); he was awarded a doctorate in 1928 (degree *Rerum Naturum Doctor*). He then taught mathematics at the high school level until 1937, when he obtained a faculty position at Charles University. When the German army invaded [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) in 1938, Wolf obtained an invitation to visit the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) in Sweden; he remained in Sweden as part of the underground resistance to the Germans until 1941 before emigrating to the United States. He taught at [Macalester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macalester_College) for a year, and then joined the faculty of the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1942. At Berkeley, he was one of the co-founders of the *[Pacific Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Journal_of_Mathematics)* in 1951. He retired in 1972, but then moved to [Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guatemala) where he helped to set up a graduate program in mathematics at the [Universidad del Valle de Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Valle_de_Guatemala). He died on August 12, 1989, in Berkeley.[^o3][^o4][^o5]
