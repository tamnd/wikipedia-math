# Leonty Magnitsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonty Magnitsky"}

*Image: Monument in honor of Leonty Magnitsky in the city Ostashkov.JPG.* Monument in honor of Magnitsky in Ostashkov

**Leonty Filippovich Magnitsky** (Леонтий Филиппович Магницкий), born **Telyatin** (Телятин; June 9, 1669, in [Ostashkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostashkov) – October 19, 1739, in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Magnitsky was born into a peasant family. According to some accounts, he graduated from the [Slavic Greek Latin Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavic_Greek_Latin_Academy) in Moscow.[^o2] From 1701 and until his death, he taught [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261) at the [Moscow School of Mathematics and Navigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_School_of_Mathematics_and_Navigation), becoming its director in 1716.

In 1703, Magnitsky wrote his famous *[Arithmetic](wikipedia://en/70124962)* (Арифметика; 2,400 copies), which was used as the principal textbook on mathematics in Russia until the middle of the 18th century.[^o2] [Mikhail Lomonosov](wikipedia://en/63375) was himself taught by this book,[^o4] which he called the "gates to his own erudition".[^o5] In 1703, Magnitsky also produced a Russian edition of [Adriaan Vlacq](wikipedia://en/4292863)'s log tables called *Таблицы логарифмов и синусов, тангенсов и секансов* (Tables of [logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860), [sines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine), [tangents](wikipedia://en/31482), and [secants](wikipedia://en/30367#Reciprocal_functions)).

Legend has it that Leonty Magnitsky was nicknamed *Magnitsky* by [Peter the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_the_Great), who considered him a "people's [magnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnet)" (магнит, or "magnit" in Russian). For his educatorial achievements he was ennobled in 1704, and was given numerous awards and gifts by the Tsar.
