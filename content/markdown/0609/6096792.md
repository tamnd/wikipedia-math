# Timofei Osipovsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timofei Osipovsky"}

*Image: Timofei Osipovsky.jpg.* Timofey Fedorovic Osipovsky

**Timofei Fyodorovich Osipovsky** (Тимофей Федорович Осиповский; February 2, 1766, [Osipovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osipovo) – June 24, 1832, Moscow) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) Imperial mathematician, physicist, [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), and philosopher.

:::

Timofei Osipovsky graduated from the St Petersburg Teachers Seminary. He became a teacher at [Imperial Kharkov University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_University), in 1805, the year it was founded. The city of [Kharkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkov), thanks to its educational establishments, became one of the most important cultural and educational centres of [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). In 1813 he became rector of the university. However, in 1820, Osipovsky was suspended from his post on religious grounds.

Osipovsky's most famous work was the three volume book *A Course of Mathematics* (1801–1823), which soon became a standard university text and was used in universities for many years.
