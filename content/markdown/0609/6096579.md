# Dmitrii Menshov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitrii Menshov"}

**Dmitrii Yevgenyevich Menshov** (also spelled *Men'shov*, *Menchoff*, *Menšov*, *Menchov*; Дми́трий Евгéньевич Меньшóв; 18 April 1892 – 25 November 1988) was a Soviet and Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to the theory of [trigonometric series](wikipedia://en/4099125).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dmitrii Menshov studied languages as a schoolboy, but from the age of 13 he began to show great interest in mathematics and physics. In 1911, he completed high school with a gold medal. After a semester at the Moscow Engineering School, he enrolled at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1912 and became a student of [Nikolai Luzin](wikipedia://en/576228).

In 1916, Menshov completed his dissertation on the topic of trigonometric series. He became a docent of Moscow State University in 1918. Soon after, he moved to [Nizhny Novgorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod) where he was appointed a professor of the Ivanovsky Pedagogical Institute. After a few years, he returned to Moscow in 1922 and began to teach at Moscow State University.

In 1935, Menshov became a full professor of Moscow State University and was awarded the title of Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences. He gave lectures at Moscow State University and also [Moscow State Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_Pedagogical_University) in numerical analysis, complex functions, and differential equations to undergraduate and graduate students. In this position, he taught and influenced an entire generation of young up-and-coming Russian mathematicians and physicists, including such renowned scientists as his student [Sergey Stechkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Stechkin). He received the [Stalin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) in 1951 and was elected to the position of corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1953.

His construction of a [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038) with non-zero coefficients which converges to zero [almost everywhere](wikipedia://en/44688) gave rise to the theory of [Menshov sets](wikipedia://en/799649).

He proved the [Rademacher–Menchov theorem](wikipedia://en/34957160), the [Looman–Menchoff theorem](wikipedia://en/17523721), and the [Lusin–Menchoff theorem](wikipedia://en/2257262).

Menshov was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 in Bologna and in 1958 in Edinburgh.[^o4]
