# E8 manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="E8 manifold"}

In [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the ***E*<sub>8</sub> manifold** is the unique [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) topological [4-manifold](wikipedia://en/1362795) with [intersection form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_form_%284-manifold%29) the [*E*<sub>8</sub> lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359).

:::

## History {#History}

The $E_8$ manifold was discovered by [Michael Freedman](wikipedia://en/658970) in 1982. [Rokhlin's theorem](wikipedia://en/7031816) shows that it has no [smooth structure](wikipedia://en/1542238) (as does [Donaldson's theorem](wikipedia://en/2180754)), and in fact, combined with the work of [Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson) on the [Casson invariant](wikipedia://en/7066452), this shows that the $E_8$ manifold is not even [triangulable](wikipedia://en/1440976) as a [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686).

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The manifold can be constructed by first plumbing together disc bundles of [Euler number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_number_%28topology%29) 2 over the [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859), according to the [Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/277109) for $E_8$. This results in $P_{E_8}$, a 4-manifold whose boundary is homeomorphic to the [Poincaré homology sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_homology_sphere). Freedman's theorem on [fake 4-balls](wikipedia://en/18669800) then says we can cap off this homology sphere with a fake 4-ball to obtain the $E_8$ manifold.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- $M_{E_8}\times S^n$ and $M_{E_8}\times T^n$ have no smooth structure (and not even a PL structure) for any $n\geq 0$.[^o7]

:::

## Connected sums {#Connected_sums}

[Freedman's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedman_classification)[^o9] yields the following three orientation-preserving homeomorphisms between [connected sums](wikipedia://en/490134) of the **$E_8$**-manifold $M_{E_8}$, the orientation-reversed **$E_8$**-manifold $\overline{M_{E_8}}$, fake second complex projective space $*\mathbb{C}P^2$ and the K3 surface $\mathrm{K3}$ (solutions of $x^4+y^4+z^4+w^4=0$ in [twistor space](wikipedia://en/2688710) $\mathbb{C}P^3$):[^o9]

$$\overline{M_{E_8}}\#*\mathbb{C}P^2 \cong\mathbb{C}P^2\#8\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2};$$

$$M_{E_8}\#\overline{M_{E_8}} \cong\#8(S^2\times S^2);$$

$$2\overline{M_{E_8}}\#3(S^2\times S^2) \cong\mathrm{K3}.$$

Required are the isomorphisms of their intersection forms: $-E_8\oplus[+1]\cong[+1]\oplus8[-1]$ follows from [Serre's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_classification),[^o10] furthermore $E_8\oplus-E_8\cong 8\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ and $Q_\mathrm{K3}\cong -2E_8\oplus 3\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$. In the above homeomorphisms, $\mathbb{C}P^2\#8\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2}$ is the [complex surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_surface) underlying the [Barlow surface](wikipedia://en/3102370), an exotic smooth structure. Now the above connected sums show, that even though the left sides have no canonical smooth structure (hence cannot give any [diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564)), these are smoothable just like the right sides and potentially even in multiple ways.
