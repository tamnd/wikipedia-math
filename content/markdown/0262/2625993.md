# Erdős–Bacon number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erdős–Bacon number"}

A person's **Erdős–Bacon number** is the sum of their [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742)—which measures the "collaborative distance" in authoring academic papers between that person and Hungarian mathematician [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284)—and their [Bacon number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacon_number)—which represents the number of links, through roles in films, by which the person is separated from American actor [Kevin Bacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Bacon).[^o2][^o3] The lower the number, the closer a person is to Erdős and Bacon, which reflects a [small world phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_world_phenomenon) in academia and entertainment.[^o4]

:::

To have a defined Erdős–Bacon number, it is necessary to have both appeared in a film and co-authored an academic paper, although this in and of itself is not sufficient as one's co-authors must have a known chain leading to Paul Erdős, and one's film must have actors eventually leading to Kevin Bacon.

## Academic scientists {#Academic_scientists}

### 3 {#3}

Mathematician [Daniel Kleitman](wikipedia://en/5299894) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 3. He co-authored papers with Erdős and has a Bacon number of 2 via his appearance as an extra in *[Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting)*, with [Minnie Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnie_Driver); Driver and Bacon appeared together in *[Sleepers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleepers_%28film%29)*.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

### 4 {#4}

Physicist [Nicholas Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Metropolis) has an Erdős number of 2,[^o11] and also a Bacon number of 2 via the [Woody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen) film *[Husbands and Wives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husbands_and_Wives)*,[^o12] giving him an Erdős–Bacon number of 4.

Paul Erdős himself has an Erdős number of 0, and a Bacon number of 4, since he appeared in a documentary about his life, *[N Is a Number: A Portrait of Paul Erdős](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N_Is_a_Number:_A_Portrait_of_Paul_Erdős)*, in which Tomasz Luczak appeared, who has a Bacon number of 3, giving Erdős an Erdős-Bacon number of 4.[^o13]

Mathematician [Dave Bayer](wikipedia://en/1442686) has an Erdős number of 2, and also a Bacon number of 2 via the movie *[A Beautiful Mind](wikipedia://en/3569822)*, in which Rance Howard appeared, who has a Bacon number of 1 via the movie *[Apollo 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_13_%28film%29)*.

### 5 {#5}

Mathematician [Jordan Ellenberg](wikipedia://en/19765796) appeared in the film *[Gifted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gifted_%282017_film%29)* with [Octavia Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octavia_Spencer), who was in *[Beauty Shop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beauty_Shop)* with Bacon[^o15]. Ellenberg also has an Erdős number of 3 via [Christopher Skinner](wikipedia://en/59264582) and [Andrew Odlyzko](wikipedia://en/1986042).[^o16]

Mathematician [Dror Bar-Natan](wikipedia://en/10363723) appeared in an episode of the show *[Horizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_%28British_TV_series%29)* with Dilly Barlow, giving him a Bacon number of 3. He also collaborated with [Brendan McKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_McKay_%28mathematician%29), giving him an Erdős number of 2, and thus an Erdős-Bacon number of 5.[^o17]

Electrical and Computer Engineer [Robert J. Marks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_J._Marks) appeared in the movie [Expelled: No Intelligence Allowed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expelled:_No_Intelligence_Allowed)[^o18] starring [Ben Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stein) who appeared in the movie [Planes, Trains and Automobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planes,_Trains_and_Automobiles)[^o19] with Kevin Bacon. His Bacon number is 2. Marks coauthored with [Donald Wunsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Wunsch).[^o20] Wunsch published with [Frank Harary](wikipedia://en/1731731) [^o21] who published with Erdős [^o22] giving Marks an Erdös number of 3 and an Erdös-Bacon number of 5.

### 6 {#6}

Metropolis and [Richard Feynman](wikipedia://en/25523) both worked on the [Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project) at [Los Alamos Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_Laboratory). Via Metropolis, Feynman has an Erdős number of 3 and, from having appeared in the film *[Anti-Clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Clock)* alongside [Tony Tang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Tang_%28actor%29), Feynman also has a Bacon number of 3. Richard Feynman thus has an Erdős–Bacon number of 6.[^o11]

Theoretical physicist [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 6: his Bacon number of 2 (via his appearance alongside [John Cleese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cleese) in *[Monty Python Live (Mostly)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monty_Python_Live_%28Mostly%29)*, who acted alongside Kevin Bacon in *[The Big Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Picture_%281989_film%29)*) is lower than his Erdős number of 4.[^o24]

Linguist [Noam Chomsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky) has an Erdős number of 4,[^o25] and he has a Bacon number of 2, having co-starred with [Danny Glover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Glover) in the 2005 documentary *The Peace!*,[^o26] giving him a combined Erdős–Bacon number of 6.

Mathematician [Ray Solomonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Solomonoff), the inventor of [algorithmic probability](wikipedia://en/402688), has an Erdős number of 3[^o27] and also appeared in the [Steven Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Wright) film [One Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Soldier);[^o28] Wright's Bacon number of 2[^o29] gives Solomonoff a Bacon number of 3 and an Erdős–Bacon number of 6.

### 7 {#7}

Scientist [Carl Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 7. He has an Erdős number of 4, and a Bacon number of 3, through appearing in [The Earth Day Special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Earth_Day_Special) with [Candice Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Bergen).[^o31]

### 8 {#8}

Mathematician [Hannah Fry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Fry) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 8. Fry appeared in [Finding Father Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finding_Father_Christmas_%282025_film%29) with [Lenny Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenny_Rush) who has a Bacon number of 2. Fry has an Erdős number of 5 through her mathematics research [^o33][^o34]

## Actors {#Actors}

### 4 {#4}

Canadian actor [Albert M. Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_M._Chan) has Erdős–Bacon number of 4. He co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on [orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_frequency-division_multiplexing), giving him an Erdős number of 3.[^o37][^o38][^o39] Chan appeared alongside Kevin Bacon in *[Patriots Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriots_Day_%28film%29)*, giving him a Bacon number of 1.[^o40]

### 6 {#6}

American actress [Danica McKellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danica_McKellar), who played [Winnie Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnie_Cooper) in *[The Wonder Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wonder_Years)*, has an Erdős–Bacon number of 6. While an undergraduate at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), McKellar coauthored a [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) paper[^o42] with Lincoln Chayes, who via his wife [Jennifer Tour Chayes](wikipedia://en/23107812)[^o43] has an Erdős number of 3, giving McKellar one of 4. Having worked with [Margaret Easley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Easley), McKellar has a Bacon number of 2.[^o3]

British actor [Colin Firth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Firth) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 6. Firth is credited as co-author of a neuroscience paper, "Political Orientations Are Correlated with Brain Structure in Young Adults",[^o44] after he suggested on [BBC Radio 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_4) that such a study could be done.[^o45] Another author of that paper, [Geraint Rees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geraint_Rees), has an Erdős number of 4,[^o46] which gives Firth an Erdős number of 5. Firth's Bacon number of 1 is due to his appearance in *[Where the Truth Lies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_the_Truth_Lies)*.[^o48]

### 7 {#7}

Israeli-American actress [Natalie Portman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Portman) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 7.[^o50] She collaborated (using her birth name, Natalie Hershlag) with Abigail A. Baird,[^o51] who has a collaboration path[^o52][^o53][^o54] leading to [Joseph Gillis](wikipedia://en/44935884), who has an Erdős number of 1, giving Portman an Erdős number of 5.[^o55] Portman appeared in *A Powerful Noise Live* (2009) with [Sarah Michelle Gellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar), who appeared in *[The Air I Breathe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Air_I_Breathe)* (2007) with Bacon, giving Portman a Bacon number of 2.[^o56]

American actress [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart) has an Erdős–Bacon number of 7; she is credited as a co-author on an [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) paper that was written after a technique was used for her short film *[Come Swim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Swim)*, giving her an Erdős number of 5,[^o57][^o58] and she co-starred with [Michael Sheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Sheen) in *[Twilight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_%282008_film%29)*, who co-starred with Bacon in *[Frost/Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost/Nixon_%28film%29)*, giving her a Bacon number of 2.[^o59]

## Others {#Others}

### 5 {#5}

[Sergey Brin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin) has an Erdős number of 3 through papers with [Jeffrey Ullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Ullman) and [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694),[^o62] and he has two cameos in the 2013 comedy *[The Internship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Internship)*,[^o63] leading to a Bacon number of 2 via [Rose Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Byrne)[^o64] and consequently an Erdős–Bacon number of 5.

### 6 {#6}

[Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates) has an Erdős number of 4[^o66] and in 1987 he participated in a short [mockumentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mockumentary) titled *Citizen Steve* about [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg), where he co-starred with [Whoopi Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whoopi_Goldberg), giving him a Bacon number of 2[^o67] and consequently an Erdős–Bacon number of 6.
