# Bhatnagar–Gross–Krook operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bhatnagar–Gross–Krook operator"}

The **Bhatnagar–Gross–Krook** (**BGK**) **operator** is a collision [operator](wikipedia://en/22213) used in the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522) and in the [lattice Boltzmann method](wikipedia://en/5074413), a [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924) technique. It is given by the formula

:::

$$\Omega_i = -\tau^{-1}(n_i - n_i^\text{eq}),$$

where $n_i^\text{eq}$ is a local equilibrium value for the population of particles in the direction of link $\mathbf{e}_i$. The term $\tau$ is a relaxation time and related to the viscosity.

The operator is named after [Prabhu L. Bhatnagar](wikipedia://en/7431793), [Eugene P.&nbsp;Gross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_P.&nbsp;Gross), and [Max Krook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Krook), the three scientists who introduced it in an article in *[Physical Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Review)* in 1954.[^o2]
