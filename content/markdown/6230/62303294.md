# Geometrically (algebraic geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geometrically"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), especially in [scheme theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_theory), a property is said to hold **geometrically** over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) if it also holds over the [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of the field. In other words, a property holds geometrically if it holds after a base change to a [geometric point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_point). For example, a [smooth variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) is a variety that is geometrically [regular](wikipedia://en/43016199).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Geometric irreducibility"}

## Geometrically irreducible and geometrically reduced {#Geometrically_irreducible_and_geometrically_reduced}

Given a scheme *X* that is of finite type over a field *k*, the following are equivalent:[^o4]

- *X* is geometrically irreducible; i.e., $X \times_k \overline{k} = X \times_{\operatorname{Spec} k} {\operatorname{Spec} \overline{k}}$ is [irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreducible_scheme), where $\overline{k}$ denotes an [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of *k*.
- $X \times_k k_s$ is irreducible for a [separable closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_closure) $k_s$ of *k*.
- $X \times_k F$ is irreducible for each [field extension](wikipedia://en/11634) *F* of *k*.

The same statement also holds if "irreducible" is replaced with "[reduced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_scheme)" and the separable closure is replaced by the [perfect closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_closure).[^o5]
