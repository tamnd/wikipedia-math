# Bass–Quillen conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bass–Quillen conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Bass–Quillen conjecture** relates [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) over a [regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_ring) [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) *A* and over the [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065) $A[t_1, \dots, t_n]$. The [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) is named for [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910) and [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519), who formulated the conjecture.[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Statement of the conjecture"}

## Statement of the conjecture {#Statement_of_the_conjecture}

The conjecture is a statement about [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/326430) [projective modules](wikipedia://en/364488). Such [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) are also referred to as vector bundles. For a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *A*, the set of [isomorphism classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphism_class) of vector bundles over *A* of rank *r* is denoted by $\operatorname{Vect}_r A$.

The conjecture asserts that for a regular Noetherian ring *A* the assignment

$$M \mapsto M \otimes_A A [t_1, \dots, t_n]$$

yields a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942)

$$\operatorname{Vect}_r A \,\stackrel \sim \to \operatorname{Vect}_r(A[t_1, \dots, t_n]).$$

:::

## Known cases {#Known_cases}

If *A* = *k* is a [field](wikipedia://en/10603), the Bass–Quillen conjecture asserts that any projective module over $k[t_1, \dots, t_n]$ is [free](wikipedia://en/326454). This question was raised by [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) and was later [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by Quillen and Suslin; see [Quillen–Suslin theorem](wikipedia://en/539562). More generally, the conjecture was shown by (Lindel 1981) in the case that *A* is a smooth [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) over a field *k*. Further known cases are reviewed in (Lam 2006).

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Extensions"}

## Extensions {#Extensions}

The set of isomorphism classes of vector bundles of rank *r* over *A* can also be identified with the [nonabelian cohomology](wikipedia://en/40814966) group

$$H^1_{Nis}(Spec (A), GL_r).$$

Positive results about the homotopy invariance of

$$H^1_{Nis}(U, G)$$

of [isotropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotropic_group) [reductive groups](wikipedia://en/1088386) *G* have been obtained by (Asok Hoyois Wendt 2018) by means of [**A**<sup>1</sup> homotopy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_homotopy_theory).

:::
