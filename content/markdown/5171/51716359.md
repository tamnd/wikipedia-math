# Haar space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Haar space"}

In [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), a **Haar space** or **Chebyshev space** is a [finite-dimensional](wikipedia://en/38267) [subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) $V$ of $\mathcal C(X, \mathbb K)$, where $X$ is a [compact space](wikipedia://en/6042) and $\mathbb K$ either the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) or the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), such that for any given $f \in \mathcal C(X, \mathbb K)$ there is exactly one element of $V$ that approximates $f$ "best", i.e. with minimum distance to $f$ in [supremum norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum_norm).[^o3]

:::
