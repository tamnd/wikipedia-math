# L-matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="L-matrix"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the class of L-matrices are those [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859) whose off-diagonal entries are less than or equal to zero and whose diagonal entries are positive; that is, an L-matrix *L* satisfies[^o2]

:::

$$L=(\ell_{ij});\quad \ell_{ii} > 0; \quad \ell_{ij}\leq 0, \quad i\neq j.$$

## See also {#See_also}

- [Z-matrix](wikipedia://en/7838302)—every L-matrix is a Z-matrix
- [Metzler matrix](wikipedia://en/7886457)—the negation of any L-matrix is a Metzler matrix
