# Geoffrey Horrocks (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Horrocks"}

**Geoffrey Horrocks** (1932/33 [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester) – 12 September 2012) was a British mathematician working on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524), who introduced the [Horrocks construction](wikipedia://en/37542163) used in the [ADHM construction](wikipedia://en/5165589), and the [Horrocks–Mumford bundle](wikipedia://en/3102767) and [monads](wikipedia://en/37569292).

:::

He was a professor at [Newcastle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_University) until his retirement in 1998.
