# ADHM construction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ADHM construction"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) and [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/64324611), the **ADHM construction** or **monad construction** is the construction of all [instantons](wikipedia://en/296077) using methods of linear algebra by [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698), [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747), [Nigel Hitchin](wikipedia://en/1034164), [Yuri I. Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_I._Manin) in their paper "Construction of Instantons."

:::

## ADHM data {#ADHM_data}

The ADHM construction uses the following data:

- complex [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) *V* and *W* of dimension *k* and *N*,
- *k* × *k* complex matrices *B*<sub>1</sub>, *B*<sub>2</sub>, a *k* × *N* complex matrix *I* and a *N* × *k* complex matrix *J*,
- a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) [moment map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) $\mu_r = [B_1,B_1^\dagger]+[B_2,B_2^\dagger]+II^\dagger-J^\dagger J,$
- a [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) moment map $\displaystyle\mu_c = [B_1,B_2]+IJ.$

Then the ADHM construction claims that, given certain regularity conditions,

- Given *B*<sub>1</sub>, *B*<sub>2</sub>, *I*, *J* such that $\mu_r=\mu_c=0$, an anti-self-dual [instanton](wikipedia://en/296077) in a [SU(*N*)](wikipedia://en/173997) [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155) with instanton number *k* can be constructed,
- All anti-self-dual [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons) can be obtained in this way and are in one-to-one correspondence with solutions up to a U(*k*) rotation which acts on each *B* in the [adjoint representation](wikipedia://en/302188) and on *I* and *J* via the [fundamental](wikipedia://en/649743) and antifundamental representations,
- The [metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_%28mathematics%29) on the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of instantons is that inherited from the flat metric on *B*, *I* and *J*.

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalizations"}

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

### Noncommutative instantons {#Noncommutative_instantons}

In a [noncommutative](wikipedia://en/295917) gauge theory, the ADHM construction is identical but the moment map $\vec\mu$ is set equal to the self-dual projection of the noncommutativity matrix of the spacetime times the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718). In this case instantons exist even when the gauge group is U(1). The noncommutative instantons were discovered by [Nikita Nekrasov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Nekrasov) and [Albert Schwarz](wikipedia://en/28995317) in 1998.

### Vortices {#Vortices}

Setting *B*<sub>2</sub> and *J* to zero, one obtains the classical moduli space of nonabelian vortices in a [supersymmetric](wikipedia://en/224636) gauge theory with an equal number of colors and flavors, as was demonstrated in Vortices, instantons and branes. The generalization to greater numbers of flavors appeared in Solitons in the Higgs phase: The Moduli matrix approach. In both cases the [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fayet–Iliopoulos_D-term), which determines a [squark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squark) [condensate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_quark_condensate), plays the role of the noncommutativity parameter in the real moment map.

:::

## The construction formula {#The_construction_formula}

Let *x* be the 4-dimensional [Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9697) [spacetime](wikipedia://en/28758) coordinates written in [quaternionic](wikipedia://en/51440) notation $x_{ij}=\begin{pmatrix}z_2&z_1\\-\bar{z_1}&\bar{z_2}\end{pmatrix}.$

Consider the 2*k* × (*N* + 2*k*) matrix

$$\Delta= \begin{pmatrix}I&B_2+z_2&B_1+z_1\\J^\dagger&-B_1^\dagger-\bar{z_1}&B_2^\dagger+\bar{z_2}\end{pmatrix}.$$

Then the conditions $\displaystyle\mu_r=\mu_c=0$ are equivalent to the factorization condition

$\Delta\Delta^\dagger=\begin{pmatrix}f^{-1}&0\\0&f^{-1}\end{pmatrix}$ where *f*(*x*) is a *k* × *k* [Hermitian matrix](wikipedia://en/189682).

Then a hermitian [projection](wikipedia://en/519182) operator *P* can be constructed as

$$P=\Delta^\dagger\begin{pmatrix}f&0\\0&f\end{pmatrix}\Delta.$$

The [nullspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullspace) of Δ(*x*) is of dimension *N* for generic *x*. The basis vectors for this null-space can be assembled into an (*N* + 2*k*) × *N* matrix *U*(*x*) with orthonormalization condition *U*<sup>†</sup>*U* = 1.

A regularity condition on the rank of Δ guarantees the completeness condition

$$P+UU^\dagger=1. \,$$

The anti-selfdual [connection](wikipedia://en/308815) is then constructed from *U* by the formula

$$A_m=U^\dagger \partial_m U.$$
