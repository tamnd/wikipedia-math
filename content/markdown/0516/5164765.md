# Bifurcation locus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bifurcation locus"}

In [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518), the **[bifurcation](wikipedia://en/2326042) [locus](wikipedia://en/542465)** of a parameterized family of one-variable [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) informally is a [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) of those parameterized points for which the dynamical behavior changes drastically under a small perturbation of the parameter. Thus the bifurcation locus can be thought of as an analog of the [Julia set](wikipedia://en/56434) in parameter space. Without doubt, the most famous example of a [bifurcation](wikipedia://en/2326042) [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) is the boundary of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562).

:::

Parameters in the complement of the bifurcation locus are called **[J-stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J-stable)**.
