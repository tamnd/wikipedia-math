# AQH share

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="AQH share"}

**AQH share** is a statistic that measures [broadcast radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_broadcasting) listenership.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

::: {#o4 .definition cue=dfni name="AQH"}

::: {#o5 .definition cue=dfni name="Share"}

::: {#o6 .definition cue=dfni name="AQH Share"}

**AQH** is an abbreviation for *average quarter-hour persons*, defined by [Arbitron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitron) (now referred to as Nielsen Audio) as the average number of persons listening to a particular station for at least five minutes during a 15-minute period. **Share** is the percentage of those listening to radio in an Arbitron "market" (typically a [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area)) who are listening to a particular radio station. Thus, **AQH Share** for a given station is mathematically expressed as:

$$\frac{\text{AQH persons listening to station}}{\text{AQH persons listening to all market radio stations}} \times 100\%$$

:::

:::

:::

:::

## Usage {#Usage}

AQH Share is most often used in conjunction with [TSL](wikipedia://en/276939) (Time Spent Listening) to measure listenership in a market. While AQH measures the average number of listeners to the station, TSL tracks the length of time listeners are tuned continuously to the station.
