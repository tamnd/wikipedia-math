# Richard Lashof

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Lashof"}

**Richard K. Lashof** (November 9, 1922 – February 4, 2010) was an American mathematician. He contributed to the field of [geometric](wikipedia://en/621732) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), working with [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905), [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294), among others. Lashof was regarded as "the key figure in sustaining the Chicago Mathematics Department as an international center for research and the training of topologists" by Melvin Rothenberg.[^o3] He received the [Quantrell Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantrell_Award).[^o4]

:::

Born in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania), Lashof earned a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in chemical engineering from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1943 and was a [communications officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications_officer) in the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) from 1943 to 1946. He earned his [doctoral degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_degree) in mathematics from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1954 under the supervision of [Richard Vincent Kadison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Vincent_Kadison). In 1971 he was an invited to speak at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Nice.[^o5]

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Lashof was married to [Joyce Lashof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_C._Lashof), a physician, and had three children, Judith, who died in 2018, [Carol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Lashof), and Dan.[^o7]

Lashof died of heart failure on February 4, 2010, after a long illness. He was 87.[^o7]
