# T. M. F. Smith

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="T. M. F. Smith"}

**Terence Michael Frederick Smith** (18 January 1934 – 7 December 2019)[^o2][^o3] was a British [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) known for his research in [survey sampling](wikipedia://en/27578).

:::

Fred Smith gained his first degree in 1959.

He succeeded Prof [Maurice Quenouille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Quenouille)[^o4] as Professor of Statistics at the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton) in 1975.[^o5] He received the [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in bronze from the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1979. In 1983 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o6]

He was [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Royal_Statistical_Society) of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1991–1993.[^o7]
