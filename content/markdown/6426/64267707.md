# Countably quasi-barrelled space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Countably quasi-barrelled space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) is said to be **countably quasi-barrelled** if every strongly bounded countable union of [equicontinuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equicontinuous) subsets of its [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) is again equicontinuous. This property is a generalization of [quasibarrelled spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasibarrelled_space).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A TVS *X* with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **countably quasi-barrelled** if $B^{\prime} \subseteq X^{\prime}$ is a [strongly bounded](wikipedia://en/63951372) subset of $X^{\prime}$ that is equal to a countable union of [equicontinuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equicontinuous) subsets of $X^{\prime}$, then $B^{\prime}$ is itself equicontinuous.[^n1] A [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) TVS is countably quasi-barrelled if and only if each [bornivorous](wikipedia://en/64267682) [barrel](wikipedia://en/1733629) in *X* that is equal to the countable intersection of closed [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) neighborhoods of 0 is itself a neighborhood of 0.[^n1]

### σ-quasi-barrelled space {#σ-quasi-barrelled_space}

A TVS with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **σ-quasi-barrelled** if every [strongly bounded](wikipedia://en/63951372) (countable) sequence in $X^{\prime}$ is equicontinuous.[^n1]

### Sequentially quasi-barrelled space {#Sequentially_quasi-barrelled_space}

A TVS with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **sequentially quasi-barrelled** if every [strongly](wikipedia://en/63951372) convergent sequence in $X^{\prime}$ is equicontinuous.

:::

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every countably quasi-barrelled space is a σ-quasi-barrelled space.

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

Every [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592), every [countably barrelled space](wikipedia://en/64267369), and every [quasi-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-barrelled_space) is countably quasi-barrelled and thus also σ-quasi-barrelled space.[^n1] The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of a [distinguished space](wikipedia://en/64266856) and of a metrizable locally convex space is countably quasi-barrelled.[^n1]

Every [σ-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space) is a σ-quasi-barrelled space.[^n1] Every [DF-space](wikipedia://en/63703274) is countably quasi-barrelled.[^n1] A σ-quasi-barrelled space that is [sequentially complete](wikipedia://en/64126151) is a [σ-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space).[^n1]

There exist [σ-barrelled spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space) that are not [Mackey spaces](wikipedia://en/1804899).[^n1] There exist σ-barrelled spaces (which are consequently σ-quasi-barrelled spaces) that are not countably quasi-barrelled spaces.[^n1] There exist [sequentially complete](wikipedia://en/64126151) [Mackey spaces](wikipedia://en/1804899) that are not σ-quasi-barrelled.[^n1] There exist sequentially barrelled spaces that are not σ-quasi-barrelled.[^n1] There exist [quasi-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-complete) locally convex TVSs that are not sequentially barrelled.[^n1]
