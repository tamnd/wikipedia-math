# Distinguished space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Distinguished space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **distinguished spaces** are [topological vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_vector_spaces) (TVSs) having the property that [weak-\*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology) bounded subsets of their biduals (that is, the [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) of their strong dual space) are contained in the weak-\* [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of some bounded subset of the bidual.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that $X$ is a [locally convex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space) and let $X^{\prime}$ and $X^{\prime}_b$ denote the [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of $X$ (that is, the [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) of $X$ endowed with the [strong dual topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual)). Let $X^{\prime \prime}$ denote the continuous dual space of $X^{\prime}_b$ and let $X^{\prime \prime}_b$ denote the strong dual of $X^{\prime}_b.$ Let $X^{\prime \prime}_{\sigma}$ denote $X^{\prime \prime}$ endowed with the [weak-\* topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology) induced by $X^{\prime},$ where this topology is denoted by $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$ (that is, the topology of pointwise convergence on $X^{\prime}$). We say that a subset $W$ of $X^{\prime \prime}$ is $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$-bounded if it is a bounded subset of $X^{\prime \prime}_{\sigma}$ and we call the closure of $W$ in the TVS $X^{\prime \prime}_{\sigma}$ the $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$-closure of $W$. If $B$ is a subset of $X$ then the [polar](wikipedia://en/1764022) of $B$ is $B^{\circ} := \left\{ x^{\prime} \in X^{\prime} : \sup_{b \in B} \left\langle b, x^{\prime} \right\rangle \leq 1 \right\}.$

A [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex space](wikipedia://en/518844) $X$ is called a **distinguished space** if it satisfies any of the following equivalent conditions:

If $W \subseteq X^{\prime \prime}$ is a $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$-bounded subset of $X^{\prime \prime}$ then there exists a bounded subset $B$ of $X^{\prime \prime}_b$ whose $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$-closure contains $W$.[^n1] If $W \subseteq X^{\prime \prime}$ is a $\sigma\left(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime}\right)$-bounded subset of $X^{\prime \prime}$ then there exists a bounded subset $B$ of $X$ such that $W$ is contained in $B^{\circ\circ} := \left\{ x^{\prime\prime} \in X^{\prime\prime} : \sup_{x^{\prime} \in B^{\circ}} \left\langle x^{\prime}, x^{\prime\prime} \right\rangle \leq 1 \right\},$ which is the [polar](wikipedia://en/1764022) (relative to the [duality](wikipedia://en/63735167) $\left\langle X^{\prime}, X^{\prime\prime} \right\rangle$) of $B^{\circ}.$[^n1] The [strong dual](wikipedia://en/63951372) of $X$ is a [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592).[^n1]

If in addition $X$ is a [metrizable](wikipedia://en/64278078) [locally convex topological vector space](wikipedia://en/518844) then this list may be extended to include:

([Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042)) The strong dual of $X$ is a [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457).[^n1]

:::

## Sufficient conditions {#Sufficient_conditions}

All [normed spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) and [semi-reflexive spaces](wikipedia://en/22124711) are distinguished spaces.[^n2] [LF spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LF_space) are distinguished spaces.

The [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) $X_b^{\prime}$ of a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) $X$ is distinguished if and only if $X$ is [quasibarrelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasibarrelled_space).[^o10]

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every locally convex distinguished space is an [H-space](wikipedia://en/600570).[^n2]

:::

::: {#o15 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

There exist distinguished [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) spaces that are not [semi-reflexive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-reflexive).[^n1] The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of a distinguished Banach space is not necessarily [separable](wikipedia://en/27855); [$l^{1}$](wikipedia://en/45194) is such a space.[^n3] The [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) of a distinguished [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) is not necessarily [metrizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable).[^n1] There exists a distinguished [semi-reflexive](wikipedia://en/22124711) non-[reflexive](wikipedia://en/210091) non-[quasibarrelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasibarrelled_space) [Mackey space](wikipedia://en/1804899) $X$ whose strong dual is a non-reflexive Banach space.[^n1] There exist [H-spaces](wikipedia://en/600570) that are not distinguished spaces.[^n1]

Fréchet [Montel spaces](wikipedia://en/1796319) are distinguished spaces.

:::
