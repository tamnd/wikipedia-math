# Countably barrelled space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Countably barrelled space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) is said to be **countably barrelled** if every weakly bounded countable union of [equicontinuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equicontinuous) subsets of its [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) is again equicontinuous. This property is a generalization of [barrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/1733592).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A TVS *X* with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **countably barrelled** if $B^{\prime} \subseteq X^{\prime}$ is a [weak-\*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology) bounded subset of $X^{\prime}$ that is equal to a countable union of [equicontinuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equicontinuous) subsets of $X^{\prime}$, then $B^{\prime}$ is itself equicontinuous.[^n1] A [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) TVS is countably barrelled if and only if each [barrel](wikipedia://en/1733629) in *X* that is equal to the countable intersection of closed [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) neighborhoods of 0 is itself a neighborhood of 0.[^n1]

### σ-barrelled space {#σ-barrelled_space}

A TVS with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **σ-barrelled** if every [weak-\*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology) bounded (countable) sequence in $X^{\prime}$ is equicontinuous.[^n1]

### Sequentially barrelled space {#Sequentially_barrelled_space}

A TVS with continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ is said to be **sequentially barrelled** if every [weak-\*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology) convergent sequence in $X^{\prime}$ is equicontinuous.[^n1]

:::

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every countably barrelled space is a [countably quasibarrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countably_quasibarrelled_space), a [σ-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-barrelled_space), a [σ-quasi-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-quasi-barrelled_space), and a [sequentially barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequentially_barrelled_space).[^n1] An [H-space](wikipedia://en/600570) is a TVS whose [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) is countably barrelled.[^n1]

Every countably barrelled space is a σ-barrelled space and every σ-barrelled space is sequentially barrelled.[^n1] Every σ-barrelled space is a [σ-quasi-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Σ-quasi-barrelled_space).[^n1]

A locally convex [quasi-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-barrelled_space) that is also a 𝜎-barrelled space is a [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592).[^n1]

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

Every [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592) is countably barrelled.[^n1] However, there exist [semi-reflexive](wikipedia://en/22124711) countably barrelled spaces that are not barrelled.[^n1] The [strong dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_dual) of a [distinguished space](wikipedia://en/64266856) and of a metrizable locally convex space is countably barrelled.[^n1]

### Counter-examples {#Counter-examples}

There exist σ-barrelled spaces that are not countably barrelled.[^n1] There exist [normed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) [DF-spaces](wikipedia://en/63703274) that are not countably barrelled.[^n1] There exists a [quasi-barrelled space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-barrelled_space) that is not a 𝜎-barrelled space.[^n1] There exist σ-barrelled spaces that are not [Mackey spaces](wikipedia://en/1804899).[^n1] There exist σ-barrelled spaces that are not [countably quasi-barrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/64267707) and thus not countably barrelled.[^n1] There exist sequentially barrelled spaces that are not σ-quasi-barrelled.[^n1] There exist [quasi-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-complete) locally convex TVSs that are not sequentially barrelled.[^n1]
