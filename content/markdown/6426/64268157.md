# Ultrabarrelled space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ultrabarrelled space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **ultrabarrelled space** is a [topological vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_vector_spaces) (TVS) for which every ultrabarrel is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A subset $B_0$ of a TVS $X$ is called an **ultrabarrel** if it is a closed and [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) subset of $X$ and if there exists a sequence $\left(B_i\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ of closed balanced and [absorbing](wikipedia://en/1728179) subsets of $X$ such that $B_{i+1} + B_{i+1} \subseteq B_i$ for all $i = 0, 1, \ldots.$ In this case, $\left(B_i\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ is called a **defining sequence** for $B_0.$ A TVS $X$ is called **ultrabarrelled** if every ultrabarrel in $X$ is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin.[^n1]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

A [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) ultrabarrelled space is a [barrelled space](wikipedia://en/1733592).[^n1] Every ultrabarrelled space is a [quasi-ultrabarrelled space](wikipedia://en/64274563).[^n1]

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

Complete and metrizable TVSs are ultrabarrelled.[^n1] If $X$ is a complete [locally bounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_bounded) non-locally convex TVS and if $B_0$ is a closed [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) and bounded neighborhood of the origin, then $B_0$ is an ultrabarrel that is not [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) and has a defining sequence consisting of non-convex sets.[^n1]

### Counter-examples {#Counter-examples}

There exist [barrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/1733592) that are not ultrabarrelled.[^n1] There exist TVSs that are complete and metrizable (and thus ultrabarrelled) but not barrelled.[^n1]
