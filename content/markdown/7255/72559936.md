# Isabel Dotti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isabel Dotti"}

**Isabel Graciela Dotti de Miatello** (born 1947) is an Argentine mathematician specializing in the connections between [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), including the theory of complex [nilmanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilmanifolds), [nilpotent Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilpotent_Lie_group), [hypercomplex manifolds](wikipedia://en/6990840), and [hyperkähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/646120). She is a professor in the Faculty of Mathematics, Astronomy and Physics of the [National University of Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Córdoba).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dotti was born on 21 June 1947 in Freyre (Argentina), a town in [San Justo Department, Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Justo_Department,_Córdoba). She earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1970 at the National University of Córdoba, and completed a doctorate at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in the United States in 1976. Her dissertation, *Extension of Actions on Stiefel Manifolds*, was supervised by [Glen Bredon](wikipedia://en/41359008).

After temporary positions at the [Federal University of Pernambuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Pernambuco) in Brazil, at Rutgers, and at the National University of Córdoba, she obtained a permanent faculty position at the National University of Córdoba in 1983.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dotti is a numbered member of the [National Academy of Sciences of Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Argentina), elected in 2007.
