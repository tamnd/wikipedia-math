# Structural Equations with Latent Variables

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Structural Equations with Latent Variables"}

***Structural Equations with Latent Variables*** is a [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) textbook on [structural equation modeling](wikipedia://en/2007748)[^o3] by [social scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_scientist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) [Kenneth Bollen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Bollen). Published in 1989, it covers topics in the statistics like measurement [validity](wikipedia://en/239140), [reliability](wikipedia://en/239138), overall fit indices, [model identification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_identification), causality, and the statistical software package [LISREL](wikipedia://en/17249082).[^o4][^o5] Examples from [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), and [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) are used in the textbook to illustrate the practical applications of these methods. The book examines covariances rather than individual cases.[^o4] It is used in graduate-level courses that focus on structural equation modeling within the social sciences.

:::
