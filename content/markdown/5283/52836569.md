# Paul de Clermont

*Image: Clermont\_-\_Geometrie\_pratique\_de\_l'ingenieur,\_ou\_L'art\_de\_mesurer,\_1693\_-\_1264551.jpg.* *La geometrie pratique de l'ingenieur, ou L'art de mesurer* (1693)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul de Clermont"}

**Paul de Clermont** was a 17th-century French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [military engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_engineer).[^o2]

:::

His book about practical [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), *La geometrie pratique de l'ingenieur, ou L'art de mesurer* (1693), was a reference work reprinted for 60 years.[^o3][^o4]

Clermont served in the French Army as [artillery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artillery) [commissary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissary) in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg).[^o2]
