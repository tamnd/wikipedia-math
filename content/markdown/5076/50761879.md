# Link distance

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Link distance"}

In [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927), the **link distance** between two points in a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) is the minimum number of line segments of any [polygonal chain](wikipedia://en/12056032) within the polygon that has the two points as its endpoints. The **link diameter** of the polygon is the maximum link distance of any two of its points.

:::

A polygon is a [convex polygon](wikipedia://en/660019) if and only if its link diameter is one. Every [star-shaped polygon](wikipedia://en/12002655) has link diameter at most two: every two points may be connected by a polygonal chain that bends once, inside the kernel of the polygon. However, this property does not characterize star-shaped polygons, as there also exist [polygons with holes](wikipedia://en/67194923) in which the link diameter is two.
