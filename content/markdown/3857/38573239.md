# Acceptable ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Acceptable ring"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **acceptable ring** is a generalization of an [excellent ring](wikipedia://en/10016360), with the conditions about [regular rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_ring) in the definition of an excellent ring replaced by conditions about [Gorenstein rings](wikipedia://en/515613). Acceptable rings were introduced by (Sharp 1977).

:::

All finite-dimensional Gorenstein rings are acceptable, as are all [finitely generated algebras](wikipedia://en/11985661) over acceptable rings and all [localizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localization_%28algebra%29) of acceptable rings.
