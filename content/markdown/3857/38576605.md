# Thomas Bond Sprague Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Bond Sprague Prize"}

The **Thomas Bond Sprague Prize** is a prize awarded annually to the student or students showing the greatest distinction in [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance), [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance), [mathematics of operational research](wikipedia://en/43476), [probability](wikipedia://en/23542), [risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in the [Master of Mathematics/Master of Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos) examinations of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), also known as Part III of the Mathematical Tripos.[^o2] The prize is named after [Thomas Bond Sprague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Bond_Sprague), the only person to have been president of both the [Institute of Actuaries](wikipedia://en/852871) in London and the [Faculty of Actuaries](wikipedia://en/2380461) in Edinburgh. It is awarded by the [Rollo Davidson Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize)[^o3] of [Churchill College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_College,_Cambridge), following a donation by D. O. Forfar, MA, FFA, FRSE (alumnus of Trinity College, Cambridge), former Appointed [Actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) of [Scottish Widows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Widows).[^o4]

:::
