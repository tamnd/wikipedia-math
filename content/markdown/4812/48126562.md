# List of Indian states and union territories by GDP per capita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Indian states and union territories by GDP per capita"}

This is a list of Indian states and union territories by their per capita net state domestic product (NSDP). NSDP is the state counterpart to a country's [net domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_domestic_product) (NDP), which equals the [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP) minus depreciation on capital goods.[^o2][^o3]

:::
