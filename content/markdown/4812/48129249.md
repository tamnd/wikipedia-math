# Arakelyan's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arakelyan's theorem"}

In mathematics, **Arakelyan's theorem** is a generalization of [Mergelyan's theorem](wikipedia://en/3277014) from compact subsets of an open subset of the complex plane to relatively closed subsets of an open subset.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Theorem"}

## Theorem {#Theorem}

Let Ω be an open subset of $\mathbb{C}$ and *E* a relatively closed subset of Ω. By Ω<sup>\*</sup> is denoted the [Alexandroff compactification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandroff_compactification) of Ω.

Arakelyan's theorem states that for every *f* continuous in *E* and holomorphic in the interior of *E* and for every *ε* > 0 there exists *g* holomorphic in Ω such that |*g* − *f*| \< *ε* on *E* if and only if Ω<sup>\*</sup> \\ *E* is connected and locally connected.[^o4]

:::
