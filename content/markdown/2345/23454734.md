# Second-order propositional logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Second-order propositional logic"}

A **second-order propositional logic** is a [propositional logic](wikipedia://en/18154) extended with [quantification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantification_%28logic%29) over propositions. A special case are the logics that allow **second-order Boolean propositions**, where quantifiers may range either just over the Boolean [truth values](wikipedia://en/161711), or over the Boolean-valued [truth functions](wikipedia://en/604707).

:::

The most widely known formalism is the [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262) with impredicative quantification, [System F](wikipedia://en/767637). (Parigot 1997) showed how this calculus can be extended to admit [classical logic](wikipedia://en/55868).
