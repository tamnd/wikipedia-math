# Siberian Mathematical Journal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siberian Mathematical Journal"}

The ***Siberian Mathematical Journal*** (abbreviated as **Sib. Math. J.**) is a cover-to-cover English translation of the [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) *Sibirskii Matematicheskii Zhurnal*, a publication of the Sobolev Institute of Mathematics of the Siberian Division of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) ([Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk)). *Sibirskii Matematicheskii Zhurnal* was established in 1960 and the *Siberian Mathematical Journal* was launched in 1966. It is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).

:::

The journal publishes research papers in all branches of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), including [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), ill-posed problems of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), computational methods of [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), etc.

The journal is indexed in [Russian Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Science_Citation_Index).[^o2]
