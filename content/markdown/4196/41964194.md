In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 icosahedral 120-cell honeycomb** is one of four [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) star-[honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,5,5/2,5}, it has five [icosahedral 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604585) around each face. Acronym: tifipte[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [great 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964210).

It can be constructed by replacing the [great dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/2860441) cells of the great 120-cell honeycomb with their [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/14968) convex hulls, thus replacing the [great 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604660) with [icosahedral 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604585). It is thus analogous to the four-dimensional [icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604585). It has [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) 10.
