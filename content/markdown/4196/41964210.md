::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Great 120-cell honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **great 120-cell honeycomb** is one of four [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) star-[honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,5/2,5,3}, it has three [great 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604660) around each face. Acronym: gohitte[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [order-5 icosahedral 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964194).

It can be seen as a [greatening](wikipedia://en/180253#Naming_stellations) of the [120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976507), and is thus analogous to the three-dimensional [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441) {5,5/2} and four-dimensional [great 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604660) {5,5/2,5}. It has [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) 10.
