::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagrammic-order 600-cell honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **pentagrammic-order 600-cell honeycomb** is one of four [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) star-[honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,5,5/2}, it has five [600-cells](wikipedia://en/501447) around each face in a [pentagrammic](wikipedia://en/197544) arrangement. Acronym: fipte[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [small stellated 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964064). It can be considered the higher-dimensional analogue of the 4-dimensional [icosahedral 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604585) and the 3-dimensional [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441). It is related to the [order-5 icosahedral 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964194) and [great 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964210): the [icosahedral 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604585) and [great 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604660) in each honeycomb are replaced by the [600-cells](wikipedia://en/501447) that are their convex hulls, thus forming the pentagrammic-order 600-cell honeycomb.

This honeycomb can also be constructed by taking the [order-5 5-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976509) and replacing clusters of 600 [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) meeting at a vertex with 600-cells. Each 5-cell belongs to five such clusters, and thus the pentagrammic-order 600-cell honeycomb has [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) 5.
