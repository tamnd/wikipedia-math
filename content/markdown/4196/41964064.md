In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **small stellated 120-cell honeycomb** is one of four [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) star-[honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5/2,5,3,3}, it has three [small stellated 120-cells](wikipedia://en/8604577) around each face. Acronym: sishitte[^n1]

:::

It is [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) to the [pentagrammic-order 600-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41964116).

It can be seen as a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976507), and is thus analogous to the three-dimensional [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843) {5/2,5} and four-dimensional [small stellated 120-cell](wikipedia://en/8604577) {5/2,5,3}. It has [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) 5.
