# Jayanta Kumar Ghosh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jayanta Kumar Ghosh"}

**Jayanta Kumar Ghosh** (Bengali: জয়ন্ত কুমার ঘোষ, 23 May 1937 – 30 September 2017) was an Indian [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), an [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) at [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) and a professor of statistics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He obtained a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from [Presidency College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Kolkata), then affiliated with the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta), and subsequently a [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) and a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) under the supervision of [H. K. Nandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._K._Nandi). He started his research career in the early 1960s, studying [sequential analysis](wikipedia://en/3509530) as a graduate student in the department of statistics at the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Among his best-known discoveries are the [Bahadur–Ghosh–Kiefer representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahadur–Ghosh–Kiefer_representation) (with [R. R. Bahadur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._R._Bahadur) and [Jack Kiefer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Kiefer_%28statistician%29))[^o7] and the [Ghosh–Pratt identity](wikipedia://en/82285549) along with [John W. Pratt](wikipedia://en/33863253).[^o8]

His research contributions fall within the fields of:

- [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571)
- [Asymptotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotics)
- [Modeling](wikipedia://en/27576) and [model selection](wikipedia://en/3664073)
- [High dimensional data analysis](wikipedia://en/22562715)
- [Nonparametric regression](wikipedia://en/4536068) and [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671)
- [Survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259)
- [Statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736)

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Elected member of the International Statistical Institute
- Advisory editor, *Journal of Statistical Planning and Inference*
- Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)
- Fellow of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy)
- Life member and director of the [Calcutta Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_Statistical_Association)
- Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences)
- Japanese Society for Promotion of Sciences Fellowship, 1978
- [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), 1981
- President, Statistics Section of the [Indian Science Congress Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Science_Congress_Association), 1991
- President, [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), 1993
- Mahalanobis Gold Medal of Indian Science Congress Association, 1998
- Invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), 1998[^o10]
- [P. V. Sukhatme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandurang_Vasudeo_Sukhatme) Prize for Statistics, 2000
- Mahalanobis Memorial Lecture, State Science and Technology Congress, W. Bengal, 2003
- D.Sc. (h.c.), B.C. Roy Agricultural University, W. Bengal, India, 2006
- International Indian Statistical Association (IISA) Lifetime Achievement Award, 2010 [^o11]
- [Padma Shree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shree) (2014) by the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India)

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

He has published over 50 research papers. He has also published four books, which are:

- *Invariance in Testing and Estimation* (Lecture Notes), 1967, published by [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta).
- *Higher Order Asymptotics* (based on CBMS-NSF lecture), published jointly by [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) and [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), 1994.
- (with R.V. Ramamoorthi) *Bayesian Nonparametrics* (Springer 2003).
- (with Mohan Delampady and Tapas Samanta) *An Introduction to Bayesian Analysis - Theory and Methods*, Springer 2006.
