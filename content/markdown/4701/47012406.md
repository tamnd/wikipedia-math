# George Murray Burnett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George Murray Burnett"}

**George Murray Burnett** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) FRSA FRIC LLD (1921–1980) was a Scottish mathematician and chemist. He served as both Principal and Vice-Chancellor of [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) from 1974 until 1980. He is largely remembered for his work on polymer reactions.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in Messina in South Africa on 12 July 1921.

His family moved to London in his childhood and he was educated at Mile-End School before again relocating to Aberdeen in Scotland. Here he attended [Robert Gordon's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gordon's_College) before being accepted by [Aberdeen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_University) studying chemistry and mathematics. He graduated in 1943.[^o4] On graduation he immediately moved to a teaching role within the university. In 1943/4 being an Assistant Lecturer in Natural Philosophy and 1944/5 a lecturer in chemistry. In 1945 he moved to [Birmingham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_University) serving as a lecturer in chemistry there for ten years. In 1955 he received a chair in Aberdeen University and was a professor there until 1974. He then received a post as Principal of Heriot-Watt University. He served as this until his death aged only 59.

Over and above his role as Principal he served as Vice Chancellor under Chancellor [Lord Thomson of Monifieth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Thomson_of_Monifieth).[^o5] On his death he was replaced by Prof [Thomas Diery Patten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Diery_Patten).[^o6]

In 1956 he was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).

He held three separate doctorates. [Aberdeen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_University) awarded him a PhD in 1947. [Birmingham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_University) awarded him a DSc in 1954. [Strathclyde University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strathclyde_University) awarded him an honorary Doctor of Letters (LLD) in 1979.

Burnett married Nan Bow in 1946. He died in Edinburgh on 4 September 1980.

## Publications {#Publications}

- ’'Mechanism of Polymer Reactions'’ (1954)
- ’'Energy v. 2'’ (1969)
- ’'Transfer and Storage of Energy by Molecules'’ (1969)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Burnett was painted by [Peter Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Collins_%28artist%29) in 1982. The painting is held by [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University).[^o9]

A building on the Heriot-Watt campus is named George Burnett Hall in his honour.[^o10]
