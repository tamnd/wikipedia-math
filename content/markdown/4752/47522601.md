# Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems"}

The **Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems**, abbreviated as the **Brin Prize**, is awarded to mathematicians who have made outstanding advances in the field of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and are within 14 years of their PhD.[^o2] The prize is endowed by and named after Michael Brin,[^o2] whose son [Sergey Brin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin)[^o3] is a co-founder of [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google). Michael Brin is a retired mathematician at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) and a specialist in dynamical systems.[^o4]

:::

The first prize was awarded in 2008, between 2009 and 2017 it has been awarded bi-annually, and since 2017 annually. [Artur Avila](wikipedia://en/23579022), the 2011 awardee, went on to win the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 2014.[^o5]

From 2016, the Brin prize for young mathematicians is awarded as well, which is given to mathematicians within 4 years of their PhD.

## Past winners {#Past_winners}

- 2008 : [Giovanni Forni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Forni_%28mathematician%29) for his work on area-preserving flows.[^o7][^o8][^o9]
- 2009 : [Dmitry Dolgopyat](wikipedia://en/47601930) for his work on rapid mixing of flows.[^o10][^o11][^o12][^o13]
- 2011 : [Artur Avila](wikipedia://en/23579022) for his work on Teichmüller dynamics and interval-exchange transformations.[^o14][^o15][^o16]
- 2013 : [Omri Sarig](wikipedia://en/81078246) for his work on the thermodynamics of countable Markov shifts and his Markov partition for surface diffeomorphisms.[^o17][^o18][^o19]
- 2015 : [Federico Rodriguez Hertz](wikipedia://en/61753917) for his work on geometric and measure rigidity and on stable ergodicity of partially hyperbolic systems.[^o20][^o21][^o22]
- 2017 : [Lewis Bowen](wikipedia://en/81081580) for creation of entropy theory for a broad class of non-amenable groups and solution of the long-standing isomorphism problem for Bernoulli actions of such groups.[^o23][^o24]
- 2018 : [Mike Hochman](wikipedia://en/71922376) for his work in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and fractal geometry.[^o25][^o26][^o27]
- 2019 : [Sébastien Gouëzel](wikipedia://en/81081712) for his work on the [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) of transfer operators and statistical properties of hyperbolic dynamical systems and random walks on hyperbolic groups.[^o28][^o29][^o30]
- 2020 : [Corinna Ulcigrai](wikipedia://en/46712569) for her work on the ergodic theory of locally Hamiltonian flows on surfaces and translation flows on periodic surfaces.[^o31][^o32][^o33]
- 2021 : [Tim Austin](wikipedia://en/81078882) for his proof the weak Pinsker conjecture, for his groundbreaking approach to non-conventional multiple ergodic theorems, and his contributions to [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).[^o34][^o35]
- 2022 : [Zhiren Wang](wikipedia://en/81087562) for his fundamental contributions to the study of topological and measure rigidity of higher rank actions, and his proof of Moebius disjointness for several classes of dynamical systems.[^o36][^o37]
- 2023 : [Jacopo De Simoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacopo_De_Simoi) for his fundamental contributions to the study of Fermi acceleration, of marked length spectrum rigidity for integrable and dispersing billiards, and entropy rigidity for conservative Anosov flows in dimension 3.
- 2024 : [Amir Mohammadi](wikipedia://en/81276118) for his fundamental contributions to effective counting and equidistribution in Teichmüller and homogeneous dynamics.
- 2025 : [Dan Cristofaro-Gardiner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Cristofaro-Gardiner) for his for his groundbreaking contributions to symplectic and [contact geometry](wikipedia://en/476398) and dynamics.
- 2026 : [Dzmitry Dudko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzmitry_Dudko) for his important contributions to renormalization techniques in complex dynamics with applications to the conjecture of [local connectivity of the Mandelbrot set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandelbrot_Set#Local_connectivity) (MLC) and his work on Thurston maps.
