# Virginia Halmos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Virginia Halmos"}

**Virginia Halmos** (1915–2015), born Virginia Pritchett, was an American philanthropist who supported mathematics research. She and her husband [Paul Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598) endowed the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)'s [Joseph L. Doob Prize](wikipedia://en/70444084) for research exposition.[^o2] They also made substantial gifts to the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o3] In particular, they endowed the Mathematical Association of America's [Euler Book Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_Book_Prize), which was first given in 2007.[^o4] After her husband's death, Virginia Halmos donated funds to the Mathematical Association of America to support digitization of her husband's extensive archives.[^o5]

:::

Virginia Halmos (then Pritchett) earned an undergraduate degree at [Vassar College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassar_College). She went to graduate school at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where she studied logic. She left the graduate program in 1945 when she married Paul Halmos, but she maintained a strong interest in mathematics and the lives of mathematicians.[^o6]
