# Anne-Laure Dalibard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anne-Laure Dalibard"}

**Anne-Laure Dalibard** is a French mathematician working on [asymptotic behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_behavior) of fluid equations occurring in [oceanographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanography) models. She works as a staff scientist at the Jacques-Louis Lions Laboratory, a [joint research unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unité_mixte_de_recherche) between [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) and the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (UMR 7598.)

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dalibard earned her PhD from [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University),[^o4] working on the homogenization of scalar conservation laws and transport equations[^o5] under the supervision of [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) laureate [Pierre-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/1270068).

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 2023 Mathematics medal of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o7]
- 2020 [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France) Maurice Audin Prize [^o8]
- 2018 [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) bronze medal[^o9]
- 2015 [European Research Council starting grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ERC_Starting_Grant)
- 2010 [College de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_de_France) Peccot Prize[^o10]
