# Megaprime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Megaprime"}

A **megaprime** is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) with at least one million decimal digits.[^o2]

:::

Other terms for large primes include "titanic prime", coined by [Samuel Yates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Yates) in the 1980s for a prime with at least 1000 digits[^o3] (of which the smallest is 10<sup>999</sup>+7),[^o4] and "gigantic prime" for a prime with at least 10,000 digits[^o5] (of which the smallest is 10<sup>9999</sup>+33603).[^o6]

*Image: Megaprimes found by year.png.* Number of megaprimes found by year through 2025

, there are 3,797 known megaprimes[^o7] which have more than 1,000,000 digits.[^o8] The first to be found was the [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908) 2<sup>6972593</sup>−1 with 2,098,960 digits, discovered in 1999 by [Nayan Hajratwala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nayan_Hajratwala), a participant in the [distributed computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_computing) project [GIMPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIMPS).[^o9][^o10] Nayan was awarded a [Cooperative Computing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperative_Computing_Award) from the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Frontier_Foundation) for this achievement.

[Almost all](wikipedia://en/1309) primes are megaprimes, as the number of primes with fewer than one million digits is finite. However, the vast majority of known primes are not megaprimes.

All numbers from 10<sup>999999</sup> through 10<sup>999999</sup> + 593498 are known to be [composite](wikipedia://en/82289), and there is a very high probability that 10<sup>999999</sup> + 593499, a [strong probable prime](wikipedia://en/160560#Variants) for each of 8 different bases, is the smallest megaprime.[^o11] , the smallest number known to be a megaprime is 10<sup>999999</sup> + 308267×10<sup>292000</sup> + 1.

The last prime that is not a megaprime is currently unknown. , the largest prime number known to not be a megaprime is 10<sup>999999</sup> − 1022306×10<sup>287000</sup> − 1. There is a very high probability that 10<sup>999999</sup> − 172473 is the biggest non-mega prime.[^o12][^o13]
