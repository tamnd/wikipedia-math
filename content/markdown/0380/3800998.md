# Relative contact homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Relative contact homology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the area of [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology), **relative contact homology** is an invariant of spaces together with a chosen subspace. Namely, it is associated to a [contact manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_manifold) and one of its [Legendrian submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legendrian_submanifold). It is a part of a more general invariant known as [symplectic field theory](wikipedia://en/2672856#Symplectic_field_theory), and is defined using [pseudoholomorphic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoholomorphic_curves).

:::

## Legendrian knots {#Legendrian_knots}

The simplest case yields invariants of [Legendrian knots](wikipedia://en/476398#Legendrian_submanifolds_and_knots) inside [contact three-manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_three-manifold). The relative contact homology has been shown to be a strictly more powerful invariant than the "classical invariants", namely [Thurston-Bennequin number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston-Bennequin_number) and [rotation number](wikipedia://en/3928569) (within a class of smooth knots).

[Yuri Chekanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Chekanov) developed a purely combinatorial version of relative contact homology for Legendrian knots, i.e. a combinatorially defined invariant that reproduces the results of relative contact homology.

Tamas Kalman developed a combinatorial invariant for loops of Legendrian knots, with which he detected differences between the [fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/11004) of the space of smooth knots and of the space of Legendrian knots.

## Higher-dimensional legendrian submanifolds {#Higher-dimensional_legendrian_submanifolds}

In the work of [Lenhard Ng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenhard_Ng), relative SFT is used to obtain invariants of smooth knots: a knot or link inside a topological three-manifold gives rise to a [Legendrian torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legendrian_torus) inside a contact [five-manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-manifold), consisisting of the unit conormal bundle to the knot inside the unit cotangent bundle of the ambient three-manifold. The relative SFT of this pair is a differential graded algebra; Ng derives a powerful knot invariant from a combinatorial version of the zero-th degree part of the homology. It has the form of a finitely presented [tensor algebra](wikipedia://en/363628) over a certain ring of multivariable [Laurent polynomials](wikipedia://en/61351) with [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) coefficients. This invariant assigns distinct invariants to (at least) knots of at most ten crossings, and dominates the [Alexander polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647) and the [A-polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-polynomial) (and thus distinguishes the [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075)).
