# Daniel Tătaru

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Tătaru"}

**Daniel Ioan Tătaru** (born 6 May 1967) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).

:::

He was born in [Piatra Neamț](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piatra_Neamț). As a student at the [Petru Rareș High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petru_Rareș_National_College_%28Piatra_Neamț%29), he won the National Mathematical Olympiad three times and the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) twice. He then studied mathematics at the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University). His 1990 diploma thesis, supervised by [Viorel P. Barbu](wikipedia://en/53177930), was on [Hamilton–Jacobi equations](wikipedia://en/897539) in [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989) and nonlinear semigroups; it won the Gheorghe Țițeica Prize from the Romanian Academy of Sciences.[^o8] Tătaru earned his doctorate from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) in 1992, under supervision of [Irena Lasiecka](wikipedia://en/44759993). The next year he joined the faculty at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) and in 2001 he moved to Berkeley.[^o8]

He won the 2002 [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) for his research[^o4] on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). In 2003, he was awarded the [Romanian Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Merit_%28Romania%29), Commander rank.[^o5] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] In 2013 he was selected as a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator)[^o7] in mathematics. In 2023, he was elected honorary member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy).[^o8]
