# John McCarthy (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John McCarthy"}

**John Edward McCarthy** (born 20 January 1964) is a mathematician. He is currently the [Spencer T. Olin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Truman_Olin) Professor of Arts and Sciences, and former chair of the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis).[^o2] He works in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675)[^o4] and [several complex variables](wikipedia://en/5759), and applications of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) to other areas.

:::

## Early life, education, and career {#Early_life,_education,_and_career}

McCarthy is a native of Ireland, and received a B.A. from [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin), in 1983.[^o4] He completed a Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1989. His Ph.D. Advisor was [Donald Sarason](wikipedia://en/53777531).

He originally intended to return to Ireland after his doctorate, but the Irish universities were subject to a hiring freeze at the time. Instead, after postdoctoral research at [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington),[^o6] he joined Washington University in St. Louis in 1991, and became a US citizen in 1996.[^o4] He has been a full professor since 1999, and Spencer T. Olin Professor of [Arts and Sciences at Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_and_Sciences_at_Washington_University_in_St._Louis) since 2011. He chaired the Department of Mathematics and Statistics from 2016 to 2021.[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

McCarthy was one of two 2016 recipients of the [Gilbert de Beauregard Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_de_Beauregard_Robinson) Award of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055)[^o8] He was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2018.[^o9]

## Books {#Books}

McCarthy's books include:

- *Holomorphic Spaces* (edited with [Sheldon Axler](wikipedia://en/30456092) and [Donald Sarason](wikipedia://en/53777531)), [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), 1998.
- *Pick Interpolation and Hilbert function spaces* (with [Jim Agler](wikipedia://en/50605185)), [Graduate Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871519), vol. 44, [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2002.[^o11]
- *Transition to Higher Mathematics: Structure and Proof* (with Bob A. Dumas), 1st ed., [McGraw Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGraw_Hill), 2006; 2nd. ed., Washington University Open Scholarship, 2015.
- *Operator Analysis: Hilbert space methods in complex analysis* (with Jim Agler and [Nicholas Young](wikipedia://en/58269490)), Cambridge Tracts in Mathematics, vol. 219, [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), 2020.[^o12]
