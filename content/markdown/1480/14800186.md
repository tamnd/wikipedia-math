::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of five cuboctahedra"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), this [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 5 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280). It has [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/2877844) symmetry *I<sub>h</sub>*. It could also be called the anticosicosahedron.

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(±2, 0, ±2)

(±τ, ±τ<sup>−1</sup>, ±(2τ−1))

(±1, ±τ<sup>−2</sup>, ±τ<sup>2</sup>)

where τ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).

::: {#o7 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The compound of 5 cuboctahedra could be made by the [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the [compound of five cubes](wikipedia://en/8010502) or [compound of five octahedra](wikipedia://en/8010546). It could also be formed by the [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) of the compound of [five](wikipedia://en/3645795) or [ten](wikipedia://en/8010465) [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606).

:::
