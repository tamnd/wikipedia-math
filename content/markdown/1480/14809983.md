In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **compound of three cubes** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) formed from three [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) arranged with [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805). It has been depicted in works by [Max Brückner](wikipedia://en/48703370) and [M.C. Escher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.C._Escher).

:::

## History {#History}

This compound appears in [Max Brückner](wikipedia://en/48703370)'s book *Vielecke und Vielflache* (1900), and in the [lithograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithograph) print *[Waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfall_%28M._C._Escher%29)* (1961) by [M.C. Escher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.C._Escher), who learned of it from Brückner's book. Its [dual](wikipedia://en/8815), the [compound of three octahedra](wikipedia://en/33808818), forms the central image in an earlier Escher [woodcut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodcut), *[Stars](wikipedia://en/1170784)*.

In the 15th-century manuscript *[De quinque corporibus regularibus](wikipedia://en/65240274)*, [Piero della Francesca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piero_della_Francesca) includes a drawing of an octahedron circumscribed around a cube, with eight of the cube edges lying in the octahedron's eight faces. Three cubes inscribed in this way within a single octahedron would form the compound of three cubes, but della Francesca does not depict the compound.

## Construction and coordinates {#Construction_and_coordinates}

This compound can be constructed by superimposing three identical cubes, and then rotating each by 45 degrees about a separate axis (that passes through the centres of two opposite faces).

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound can be chosen as all the [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of $(0,\pm 1,\pm\sqrt{2})$.
