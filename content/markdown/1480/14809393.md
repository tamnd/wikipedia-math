This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 5 [stellated truncated hexahedra](wikipedia://en/4157363), formed by star-truncating each of the cubes in the [compound of 5 cubes](wikipedia://en/8010502).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(±(2−$\sqrt{2}$), ±$\sqrt{2}$, ±(2−$\sqrt{2}$))

(±φ, ±(φ<sup>−1</sup>−φ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{2}$), ±(2φ−1−φ$\sqrt{2}$))

(±1, ±(φ<sup>−2</sup>+φ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{2}$), ±(φ<sup>2</sup>−φ$\sqrt{2}$))

(±(1−$\sqrt{2}$), ±(−φ<sup>−2</sup>+$\sqrt{2}$), ±(φ<sup>2</sup>−$\sqrt{2}$))

(±(φ−φ$\sqrt{2}$), ±(−φ<sup>−1</sup>), ±(2φ−1−φ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{2}$))

where φ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).
