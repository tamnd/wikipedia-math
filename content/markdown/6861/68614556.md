# Agnès Sulem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Agnès Sulem"}

**Agnès Sulem** (born 1959) is a French applied mathematician whose research topics include [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216), [jump diffusion](wikipedia://en/13760259), and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Sulem earned a Ph.D. in 1983 at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University), with the dissertation *Résolution explicite d'Inéquations Quasi-Variationnelles associées à des problèmes de gestion de stock* supervised by [Alain Bensoussan](wikipedia://en/61749817).

## Career {#Career}

She is a director of research at the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413) (INRIA) in Paris, where she heads the MATHRISK project on [mathematical risk handling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_risk_modeling). She is currently a professor at the [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg) in the Mathematics department. She is a coauthor of the book *Applied Stochastic Control of Jump Diffusions* (with [Bernt Øksendal](wikipedia://en/6782528), Springer, 2005; 2nd ed., 2007; 3rd ed., 2019).[^o4] Sulem is also an associate editor at the *[Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications](wikipedia://en/54943204)* and at the *[SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Financial_Mathematics)*.
