# Warazan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Warazan"}

*Image: Warazan, straw calculator from Ryukyu Islands - Ridai Museum of Modern Science, Tokyo - DSC07450.JPG.* Example of *warazan* at the Museum of Science, [Tokyo University of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University_of_Science)

*Image: "Warazan" 「わら算」Yaeyamajima Kuramoto Kujichō (University of the Ryukyus Library).jpg.* Instruction to use *warazan* to record the level of tax assessed, in the *Yaeyama-jima Kuramoto Kujichō* (1873 copy of the 1857 original); the fourth to sixth characters in the fifth line from the right read「わら算」([University of the Ryukyus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Ryukyus) Library)[^o2][^o3]

***Warazan*** was a system of record-keeping using knotted straw at the time of the [Ryūkyū Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryukyu_Kingdom).[^o4][^o5] In the [Southern Ryukyuan languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ryukyuan_languages) of the [Sakishima Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakishima_Islands) it was known as *barazan* and on [Okinawa Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Island) as *warazani* or *warazai*.[^o6] Formerly used in particular in relation to the "head tax", it is still to be found in connection with the annual Itoman Giant Tug-of-War, to record the amount of *miki* or sacred *[sake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sake)* dedicated.[^o2]
