# Hannah Markwig

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hannah Markwig"}

*Image: Markwig bertram cavalieri (cropped).jpg.* Markwig at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2012

**Hannah Markwig** (born 19 November 1980) is a German mathematician specializing in [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041). In 2010 she won both the [Heinz Maier-Leibnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Maier-Leibnitz) Prize of the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) and the [Helene Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helene_Lange) Prize for her research.

:::

Markwig studied mathematics at the [University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kaiserslautern) beginning in 1999, and completed her PhD there in 2006. Her dissertation, supervised by Andreas Gathmann and reviewed by [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels), was *The Enumeration of Plane Tropical Curves*. After postdoctoral study at the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088) and the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), she became an assistant professor in the Courant Research Center at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). She moved in 2011 to [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University), and then in 2016 to the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen), where she is a professor of geometry in the department of mathematics.
