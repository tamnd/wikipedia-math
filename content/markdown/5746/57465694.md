# Sergei Abramov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Abramov"}

Абрамов, Сергей Михайлович (учёный)

**Sergei Mikhailovich Abramov** (Сергей Михайлович Абрамов; born 25 March 1957) is a [Russian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematician), [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29), [Dr.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), Director of the Institute of Program Systems of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), Rector of the [University of Pereslavl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pereslavl) (2003–2017). Specialist in the field of system programming and information technologies (supercomputer systems, telecommunication technologies, theory of constructive metasystems and meta-calculations).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated from the faculty [MSU CMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_CMC) (1980).

He presented his thesis «Meta-calculations and their application» for the degree of [Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (1995).[^o6][^o7]

Was awarded the title of [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_%28highest_academic_rank%29) (1996), [Corresponding Member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_Member) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (2006).
