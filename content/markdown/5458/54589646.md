# Aequationes Mathematicae

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aequationes Mathematicae"}

***Aequationes Mathematicae*** is a [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal). It is primarily devoted to [functional equations](wikipedia://en/364377), but also publishes papers in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).[^o6] As well as publishing regular journal submissions on these topics, it also regularly reports on international symposia on functional equations and produces bibliographies on the subject.[^o3]

:::

[János Aczél](wikipedia://en/22717925) founded the journal in 1968 at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), in part because of the long publication delays of up to four years in other journals at the time of its founding.[^o3][^o4] It is currently published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), with [Zsolt Páles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zsolt_Páles) of the [University of Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen) as its editor in chief. [János Aczél](wikipedia://en/22717925) remains its honorary editor in chief.[^o5]

It is frequently listed as a second-quartile mathematics journal by [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank).[^o6]
