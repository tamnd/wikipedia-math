# Mina Teicher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mina Teicher"}

**Mina Teicher** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מינה טייכר) is an Israeli mathematician at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University), specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Teicher earned bachelor's, masters, and doctoral degrees from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1974, 1976, and 1981 respectively. Her dissertation, *Birational Transformation Between 4-folds*, was supervised by [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738). Since 1999, she has directed the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Research Institute for Mathematics at Bar-Ilan University. In 2001–2002 she was the inaugural Emmy Noether Visiting professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where she lectured about [braid groups](wikipedia://en/550138).

She has held leadership roles in academia and science, including serving from 2005 to 2007 as chief scientist at Israel's [Ministry of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Science,_Technology_and_Space), and chairing the board of governors of the [United States – Israel Binational Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_–_Israel_Binational_Science_Foundation) from 2012 to 2013.
