# HAIFA construction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="HAIFA construction"}

The **HAIFA construction** (*hash iterative framework*) is a cryptographic structure used in the design of [hash functions](wikipedia://en/439526). It is one of the modern alternatives to the [Merkle–Damgård construction](wikipedia://en/4076217),[^o2] avoiding its weaknesses like [length extension attacks](wikipedia://en/36667216). The construction was designed by [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920) and [Orr Dunkelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orr_Dunkelman) in 2007.

:::

Three of the 14 second round candidates in the [NIST hash function competition](wikipedia://en/20051952) were based on HAIFA constructions ([BLAKE](wikipedia://en/30054589), SHAvite-3, ECHO). Other hash functions based on it are LAKE, Sarmal, [SWIFFTX](wikipedia://en/25702256) and HNF-256. The construction of [Skein](wikipedia://en/20007592) ([Unique Block Iteration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_Block_Iteration)) is similar to HAIFA.[^o3] Another alternative construction is the [sponge construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sponge_construction).
