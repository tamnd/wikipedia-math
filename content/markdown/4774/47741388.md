# Guoliang Yu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Guoliang Yu"}

**Guoliang Yu** is a [Chinese American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). After receiving his Ph.D. from [SUNY at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in 1991 under the direction of [Ronald G. Douglas](wikipedia://en/14044625), Yu spent time at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (1991–1992), the [University of Colorado at Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_at_Boulder) (1992–2000), [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) (2000–2012), and a variety of visiting positions. He currently holds the Powell Chair in Mathematics [^o3] and was appointed University Distinguished Professor in 2018[^o4] at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3][^o5] He is also a [Simons Fellow in Mathematics](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/grant/simons-fellows-in-mathematics/?tab=awardees).[^o6]

:::

Yu's research interests include noncommutative geometry, higher index theory of elliptic operators, K-theory, and geometric group theory. He is best known for his fundamental contributions to the [Novikov conjecture](wikipedia://en/3035194) on homotopy invariance of higher signatures,[^o7][^o8] the [Baum–Connes conjecture](wikipedia://en/11774498) on K-theory of group C\*-algebras,[^o9] and the stable Borel conjecture on rigidity of manifolds.[^o10] In his work on the [Novikov conjecture](wikipedia://en/3035194), he developed controlled operator K-theory.[^o7] In the mathematical literature, several concepts are named after him, including Yu's property A[^o11] and Yu's localization algebra.[^o12]

Yu has delivered invited addresses at the American Mathematical Society meeting in 1999, and at the [International Congress of Mathematicians in Madrid in 2006](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o13] He was a plenary speaker at the Topology Festival [^o14] in 2002 and the [Geometry Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_Festival) in 2007. He is an editor of the Journal of Noncommutative Geometry, the Annals of K-Theory, the Journal of Topology and Analysis, and the Kyoto Journal of Mathematics.
