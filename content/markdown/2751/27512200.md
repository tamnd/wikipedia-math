# Reeb stability theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reeb stability theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Reeb stability theorem**, named after [Georges Reeb](wikipedia://en/20126484), asserts that if one leaf of a [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149)-one [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) is [closed](wikipedia://en/669475) and has finite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004), then all the leaves are closed and have finite fundamental group.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Reeb local stability theorem"}

## Reeb local stability theorem {#Reeb_local_stability_theorem}

Theorem:[^o4] *Let $F$ be a $C^1$, codimension $k$ [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) $M$ and $L$ a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) leaf with finite [holonomy group](wikipedia://en/644921). There exists a [neighborhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) $U$ of $L$, saturated in $F$ (also called invariant), in which all the leaves are compact with finite holonomy groups. Further, we can define a [retraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_retract) $\pi: U\to L$ such that, for every leaf $L'\subset U$, $\pi|_{L'}:L'\to L$ is a [covering map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_map) with a finite number of sheets and, for each $y\in L$, $\pi^{-1}(y)$ is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to a [disk](wikipedia://en/329542) of [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) k and is [transverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transversality_%28mathematics%29) to $F$. The neighborhood $U$ can be taken to be arbitrarily small.*

The last statement means in particular that, in a neighborhood of the point corresponding to a compact leaf with finite holonomy, the space of leaves is [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637). Under certain conditions the Reeb local stability theorem may replace the [Poincaré–Bendixson theorem](wikipedia://en/2784317) in higher dimensions.[^o5] This is the case of codimension one, singular foliations $(M^n,F)$, with $n\ge 3$, and some center-type singularity in $Sing(F)$.

The Reeb local stability theorem also has a version for a noncompact codimension-1 leaf.[^o6][^o7]

:::

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Reeb global stability theorem"}

## Reeb global stability theorem {#Reeb_global_stability_theorem}

An important problem in foliation theory is the study of the influence exerted by a compact leaf upon the global structure of a [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227). For certain classes of foliations, this influence is considerable.

Theorem:[^o4] *Let $F$ be a $C^1$, codimension one foliation of a closed manifold $M$. If $F$ contains a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) leaf $L$ with finite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004), then all the leaves of $F$ are compact, with finite fundamental group. If $F$ is transversely [orientable](wikipedia://en/187446), then every leaf of $F$ is [diffeomorphic](wikipedia://en/8564) to $L$; $M$ is the [total space](wikipedia://en/276526) of a [fibration](wikipedia://en/316826) $f:M\to S^1$ over $S^1$, with [fibre](wikipedia://en/6603892) $L$, and $F$ is the fibre foliation, $\{f^{-1}(\theta)|\theta\in S^1\}$.*

This theorem holds true even when $F$ is a foliation of a [manifold with boundary](wikipedia://en/2073470#Manifold_with_boundary), which is, a priori, [tangent](wikipedia://en/30807) on certain components of the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) and [transverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transversality_%28mathematics%29) on other components.[^o10] In this case it implies [Reeb sphere theorem](wikipedia://en/26558892).

Reeb Global Stability Theorem is false for foliations of codimension greater than one.[^o11] However, for some special kinds of foliations one has the following global stability results:

- In the presence of a certain transverse geometric structure:

Theorem:[^o12] *Let $F$ be a [complete](wikipedia://en/7832) [conformal](wikipedia://en/295907) foliation of codimension $k\ge 3$ of a [connected](wikipedia://en/87141) manifold $M$. If $F$ has a compact leaf with finite [holonomy group](wikipedia://en/644921), then all the leaves of $F$ are compact with finite holonomy group.*

- For [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/509742) foliations in complex [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538):

Theorem:[^o13] *Let $F$ be a holomorphic foliation of codimension $k$ in a compact complex [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538). If $F$ has a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) leaf with finite [holonomy group](wikipedia://en/644921) then every leaf of $F$ is compact with finite holonomy group.*
