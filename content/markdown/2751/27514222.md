# Edmond Pourchot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edmond Pourchot"}

*Image: Edme Pourchot.jpg.* Edmond Pourchot.

**Edmond Pourchot** (1651, [Poilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poilly) – 1734, [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989)) was a university professor noted for his controversial advocacy of [Cartesianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesianism) (and the [Cartesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_physics) theory of [mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586)) in place of [Aristotelianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotelianism).[^o2][^o3] The change within the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) from Aristotelianism to Cartesianism during the 1690s was important in the history of the development of [natural philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_philosophy) in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) and continental [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe).[^o4][^o5]

:::

Pourchot was named Professor of Philosophy in 1677, and he was a long-standing vice-chancellor/rector of the University of Paris, where he taught for 26 years. He authored a popular multi-volume [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) text entitled *Institutiones philosophicae ad faciliorem veterum, ac recentiorum philosophorum lectionem comparatae* (Paris, 1695; Paris, 1700; [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon), 1711; [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice), 1715; Lyon, 1716–1717; Venice, 1730 \[standard edition\]; Paris & Lyon & [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua), 1733; Padua, 1751; Venice, 1755).[^o6][^o7] This text was well regarded among other French intellectuals, and gained followers for Cartesianism in many other countries including [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) and [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland).[^o8][^o9] He was also a scholar of the [Hebrew language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language). The Latin form of his name was **Edmundus Purchotius** (**Edmundi Purchotii**).

*Image: Pourchot-001.jpg.* *Institutiones Philosophicae* (1717).

- Volume 1[^o10][^o11] - [Logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics)
- Volume 2[^o12] - [Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and general [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) (including [optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optics), [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics), [simple machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_machine), [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952), and [dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamics_%28physics%29) featuring [projectiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projectile), [pendulums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendulum), etc.)
- Volume 3[^o13][^o14] - [Cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology) ([heliocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heliocentric) and [geocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geocentric)), [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), [zoology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoology), [human anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_anatomy), [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904), [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), [magnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetism), [metallurgy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy), and [geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography)
  1. including a world map showing [Terra Australis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terra_Australis), the [Prime Meridian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Meridian) passing through [El Hierro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Hierro), and the [Island of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_of_California) (Table 24)
  1. including a presentation of heliocentric Cartesian [ethereal vortices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_explanations_of_gravitation) in/around the [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System) (Table 20)...[^o15] this theory was supported by many notable scientists (for example [Christiaan Huygens](wikipedia://en/42127) and [Johann Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/268886)) prior to being supplanted by [Newtonian](wikipedia://en/14627) mechanics (published 1686)[^o16][^o17]
  1. including an [armillary sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armillary_sphere) showing the plane of the [ecliptic](wikipedia://en/9264) on the [celestial sphere](wikipedia://en/48239) (Table 16)
  1. including an illustration of magnetic [field lines](wikipedia://en/5000404) which were not fully understood for another 150 years until [Faraday](wikipedia://en/19727) and [Maxwell](wikipedia://en/28989696) (Table 26)
- Volume 4[^o18] - [Ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics)
- Volume 5[^o19] - [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) (including [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) and [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology))
