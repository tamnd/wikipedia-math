# Sima Marković

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sima Marković"}

**Sima Marković** (8 November 1888 – 19 April 1939) was a Serbian mathematician, communist and socialist politician and philosopher, known as one of the founders and first leaders of the [Communist Party of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Yugoslavia).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Marković was a doctor of mathematical sciences and a university professor. He has written many works in mathematics, philosophy, physics and politics.[^o4]

He was an early activist and member of the [Serbian Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Social_Democratic_Party_%28Kingdom_of_Serbia%29) in the Kingdom of Serbia, and since the unification of the Yugoslav communists in 1919 a member of the Communist Party of Yugoslavia.[^o5]

Sima Marković advocated the preservation and peaceful reform of [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia) into the republic, as opposed to the then position of the [Comintern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comintern). He was killed in [Stalinist purges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalinist_purges) in 1939 along with many other leading Yugoslav communists. He was rehabilitated on 10 June 1958 by a decision of the Supreme Court of the Soviet Union.[^o6]
