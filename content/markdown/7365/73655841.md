# Tatiana Roque

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tatiana Roque"}

**Tatiana Marins Roque** (born April 24, 1970) is a Brazilian [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and politician.

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

*Image: Tatiana\_Roque\_ICM\_2018\_4.jpg.* Tatiana Roque at the International Congress of Mathematicians in 2018

Roque is a professor at the [Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/61253701) (IM) of the [Federal University of Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro) (UFRJ),[^o4] with a Ph.D. in [Production Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering) from the [Alberto Luiz Coimbra Institute of Graduate Studies and Research in Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COPPE) (Coppe), also at UFRJ.[^o5] Her research area covers the historiography of mathematics, the relationship between history and mathematics education, and the history of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and celestial mechanics theories at the turn of the 19th to the 20th century. Her book *História da matemática: uma visão crítica, desfazendo mitos e lendas* (2012) was one of the winners of the 2013 [Jabuti Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prêmio_Jabuti).[^o6][^o7][^o8]

She was a [guest speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_speaking) at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), which took place in Rio de Janeiro, and served as the coordinator of Forum of Science and Culture of the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro between 2019 and 2022.[^o9][^o10]

## Political career {#Political_career}

In the 2018 elections, Roque ran as a candidate for Federal Deputy for the [Socialism and Liberty Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism_and_Liberty_Party) (PSOL) in Rio de Janeiro. She received 15,789 votes but was not elected.[^o12] In the 2022 elections, she ran for the same position under the [Brazilian Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Socialist_Party) (PSB).[^o13] She received 30,764 votes but was not elected, becoming the first alternate to Eduardo Bandeira de Mello.[^o14][^o15]

In February 2023, she assumed the position of Secretary of Science and Technology of the city of Rio de Janeiro, being appointed by Mayor [Eduardo Paes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Paes).[^o5][^o16][^o17]
