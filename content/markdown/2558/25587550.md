# Suresh Venapally

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Suresh Venapally"}

**Suresh Venepally** (సురేశ్ వేనెపల్లి; born 1966) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his research work in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). He is a professor at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University).

:::

## Background {#Background}

Suresh was born in Vangoor, Telangana, India and studied in ZPHS at Vangoor up to 9th standard. He did his M.Sc at [University of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hyderabad). He joined [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (TIFR) in 1989 and got his PhD in under the guidance of [Raman Parimala](wikipedia://en/11853200) (1994). He later joined the faculty at [University of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hyderabad).

## Honors {#Honors}

- 2026 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o5]
- [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Award) for Mathematical Sciences in 2009[^o6]
- [Invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) held at Hyderabad, India in 2010[^o7]
- Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences)
- Andhra Pradesh Scientist Award, 2008
- B. M. Birla Science prize, 2004
- INSA Medal for Young Scientists, 1997[^o8]
