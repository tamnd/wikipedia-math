# Morphic word

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morphic word"}

In mathematics and computer science, a **morphic word** or **substitutive word** is an infinite sequence of symbols which is constructed from a particular class of [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569) of a [free monoid](wikipedia://en/523166).

:::

Every [automatic sequence](wikipedia://en/7740302) is morphic.[^o10]

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *f* be an endomorphism of the free monoid *A*<sup>∗</sup> on an alphabet *A* with the property that there is a letter *a* such that *f*(*a*) = *as* for a non-empty string *s*: we say that *f* is **prolongable** at *a*. The word

$$a s f(s) f(f(s)) \cdots f^{(n)}(s) \cdots \$$

is a **pure morphic** or **pure substitutive word**. Note that it is the limit of the sequence *a*, *f*(*a*), *f*(*f*(*a*)), *f*(*f*(*f*(*a*))), ... It is clearly a fixed point of the endomorphism *f*: the unique such sequence beginning with the letter *a*.[^o4][^o5] In general, a morphic word is the image of a pure morphic word under a coding, that is, a morphism that maps letter to letter.[^o10]

If a morphic word is constructed as the fixed point of a prolongable *k*-[uniform morphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_morphism) on *A*<sup>∗</sup> then the word is *k*-[automatic](wikipedia://en/7740302). The *n*-th term in such a sequence can be produced by a [finite-state automaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite-state_automaton) reading the digits of *n* in base *k*.[^o10]

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The [Thue–Morse sequence](wikipedia://en/491903) is generated over {0,1} by the 2-uniform endomorphism 0 → 01, 1 → 10.[^o8][^o9]
- The [Fibonacci word](wikipedia://en/1702139) is generated over {*a*,*b*} by the endomorphism *a* → *ab*, *b* → *a*.[^o10][^o8]
- The [tribonacci word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribonacci_word) is generated over {*a*,*b*,*c*} by the endomorphism *a* → *ab*, *b* → *ac*, *c* → *a*.[^o9]
- The [Rudin–Shapiro sequence](wikipedia://en/20256156) is obtained from the fixed point of the 2-uniform morphism *a* → *ab*, *b* → *ac*, *c* → *db*, *d* → *dc* followed by the coding *a*,*b* → 0, *c*,*d* → 1.[^o9]
- The [regular paperfolding sequence](wikipedia://en/20313560) is obtained from the fixed point of the 2-uniform morphism *a* → *ab*, *b* → *cb*, *c* → *ad*, *d* → *cd* followed by the coding *a*,*b* → 0, *c*,*d* → 1.[^o11]

:::

::: {#o13 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="D0L system"}

## D0L system {#D0L_system}

A **D0L system** (deterministic context-free [Lindenmayer system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindenmayer_system)) is given by a word *w* of the free monoid *A*<sup>∗</sup> on an alphabet *A* together with a morphism σ prolongable at *w*. The system generates the infinite D0L word ω = lim<sub>*n*→∞</sub> σ<sup>*n*</sup>(*w*). Purely morphic words are D0L words but not conversely. However, if ω = *u*ν is an infinite D0L word with an initial segment *u* of length |*u*| ≥ |*w*|, then *z*ν is a purely morphic word, where *z* is a letter not in *A*.[^o14]

:::
