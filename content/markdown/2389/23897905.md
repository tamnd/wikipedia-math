# International Congress on Mathematical Physics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Congress on Mathematical Physics"}

The **International Congress on Mathematical Physics** (**ICMP**) is the largest research congress in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). It is held every three years, on behalf of the [International Association of Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897340) (IAMP).[^o2]

:::

## Prizes {#Prizes}

The [Henri Poincaré Prize](wikipedia://en/4890712) and the IAMP early career award are both delivered at the ICMP.
