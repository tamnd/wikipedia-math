# Mathieu group M23

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathieu group M23"}

In the area of [modern algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_algebra) known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Mathieu group** *M*<sub>23</sub> is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

10,200,960 = 2<sup>7</sup>3<sup>2</sup>571123

≈ 1 × 10<sup>7</sup>.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*M*<sub>23</sub> is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was introduced by (txt). It is a 4-fold transitive [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) on 23 objects. The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) are both [trivial](wikipedia://en/193059).

(Milgram 2000) calculated the integral cohomology, and showed in particular that M<sub>23</sub> has the unusual property that the first 4 integral homology groups all vanish.

In 2026, Xiaoyu Huang, Blake Jackson, Kyu-Hwan Lee, Bjorn Poonen, Rachel Pries, and Shaowu Zhang proved that M<sub>23</sub> occurs as a [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) over the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024), completing the realization of all 26 sporadic simple groups. They constructed a regular Galois extension of $\mathbf{Q}(t)$ with Galois group M<sub>23</sub> and an explicit degree-23 [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) with rational coefficients whose splitting field has Galois group M<sub>23</sub> over $\mathbf{Q}$:[^o5]

f(x) = x<sup>23</sup> – 184x<sup>21</sup> – 1150x<sup>20</sup> + 26151x<sup>19</sup> + 18400x<sup>18</sup> – 1808490x<sup>17</sup> + 1545462x<sup>16</sup> + 67672923x<sup>15</sup> – 42732528x<sup>14</sup> – 1333395744x<sup>13</sup> + 290615166x<sup>12</sup> + 10550424369x<sup>11</sup> + 3700476348x<sup>10</sup> + 35123826654x<sup>9</sup> – 194398310718x<sup>8</sup> – 1023887308293x<sup>7</sup> + 3961650395556x<sup>6</sup> + 1949980486716x<sup>5</sup> – 28142323927002x<sup>4</sup> + 53599151839311x<sup>3</sup> – 46185312415788x<sup>2</sup> + 19169943578802x – 3150159884154

Also in 2026, Spencer Bromberg and Alexander Bromberg presented a complementary positive-dimensional [Hurwitz-space](wikipedia://en/73636093) approach to the [inverse Galois problem](wikipedia://en/739166) for M<sub>23</sub> over $\mathbf{Q}$, beginning with the Nielsen class (2A, 2A, 3A, 5A). Their preprint reconstructs a 980-element pure-[braid](wikipedia://en/550138) orbit and the associated genus-119 Hurwitz curve, passes to a degree-2,940, genus-43 reduced *j*-line cover, and develops connected five- and higher-branch refinements relating this geometry to the Huang–Jackson–Lee–Poonen–Pries–Zhang realization.[^o6]

### Construction using finite fields {#Construction_using_finite_fields}

Let $\mathbf{F}_{2^{11}}$ be the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with 2<sup>11</sup> elements. Its [group of units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_units) has order $2^{11}$ − 1 = 2047 = 23 · 89, so it has a [cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327) [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $C$ of order 23.

The Mathieu group M<sub>23</sub> can be identified with the group of $\mathbf{F}_{2}$-[linear automorphisms](wikipedia://en/18102#Endomorphisms_and_automorphisms) of $\mathbf{F}_{2^{11}}$ that stabilize $C$. More precisely, the [action](wikipedia://en/12781) of this [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) on $C$ can be identified with the 4-fold transitive action of M<sub>23</sub> on 23 objects.

## Representations {#Representations}

M<sub>23</sub> is the point stabilizer of the action of the [Mathieu group M24](wikipedia://en/15200706) on 24 points, giving it a 4-transitive [permutation representation](wikipedia://en/59487764) on 23 points with point stabilizer the [Mathieu group M22](wikipedia://en/15200699).

M<sub>23</sub> has 2 different [rank 3 actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_3_action) on 253 points. One is the action on unordered pairs with orbit sizes 1+42+210 and point stabilizer M<sub>21</sub>.2, and the other is the action on heptads with orbit sizes 1+112+140 and point stabilizer 2<sup>4</sup>.A<sub>7</sub>.

The integral representation corresponding to the permutation action on 23 points decomposes into the trivial representation and a 22-dimensional representation. The 22-dimensional representation is irreducible over any [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) not 2 or 23.

Over the field of order 2, it has two 11-dimensional representations, the restrictions of the corresponding representations of the [Mathieu group M24](wikipedia://en/15200706).

:::
