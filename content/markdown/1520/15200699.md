# Mathieu group M22

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathieu group M22"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Mathieu group** *M<sub>22</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

443,520 = 2<sup>7</sup>3<sup>2</sup>5711

≈ 4.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*M<sub>22</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was introduced by (txt). It is a 3-fold transitive [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) on 22 objects. The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) of M<sub>22</sub> is cyclic of order 12, and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2.

There are several incorrect statements about the 2-part of the Schur multiplier in the mathematical literature. (Burgoyne Fong 1966) incorrectly claimed that the Schur multiplier of M<sub>22</sub> has order 3, and in a correction (Burgoyne Fong 1968) incorrectly claimed that it has order 6. This caused an error in the title of the paper (Janko 1976) announcing the discovery of the [Janko group J4](wikipedia://en/10796995). (Mazet 1979) showed that the Schur multiplier is in fact cyclic of order 12.

(Adem Milgram 1995) calculated the 2-part of all the cohomology of M<sub>22</sub>.

::: {#o10 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

M<sub>22</sub> has a 3-transitive permutation representation on 22 points, with point stabilizer the group PSL<sub>3</sub>(4), sometimes called M<sub>21</sub>. This action fixes a [Steiner system](wikipedia://en/28016) S(3,6,22) with 77 hexads, whose full automorphism group is the automorphism group M<sub>22</sub>.2 of M<sub>22</sub>.

M<sub>22</sub> has three [rank 3 permutation representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_3_permutation_representation): one on the 77 hexads with point stabilizer 2<sup>4</sup>:A<sub>6</sub>, and two rank 3 actions on 176 heptads that are conjugate under an outer automorphism and have point stabilizer A<sub>7</sub>.

M<sub>22</sub> is the point stabilizer of the action of [M<sub>23</sub>](wikipedia://en/15200701) on 23 points, and also the point stabilizer of the [rank 3 action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_3_action) of the [Higman–Sims group](wikipedia://en/403178) on 100 = 1+22+77 points.

The triple cover 3.M<sub>22</sub> has a 6-dimensional faithful representation over the field with 4 elements.

The 6-fold cover of M<sub>22</sub> appears in the centralizer 2<sup>1+12</sup>.3.(M<sub>22</sub>:2) of an involution of the [Janko group J4](wikipedia://en/10796995).

:::
