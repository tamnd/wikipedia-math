# Mathieu group M12

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathieu group M12"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Mathieu group** *M<sub>12</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

95,040 = 12111098 = 2<sup>6</sup>3<sup>3</sup>511.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*M<sub>12</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was introduced by (txt). It is a [sharply 5-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29#Transitivity_properties) [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634) on 12 objects. (Burgoyne Fong 1968) showed that the [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) of M<sub>12</sub> has order 2 (correcting a mistake in (Burgoyne Fong 1966) where they incorrectly claimed it has order 1).

The double cover had been implicitly found earlier by (Coxeter 1958), who showed that M<sub>12</sub> is a subgroup of the [projective linear group](wikipedia://en/382667) of dimension 6 over the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) with 3 elements.

The [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2, and the full automorphism group M<sub>12</sub>.2 is contained in M<sub>24</sub> as the stabilizer of a pair of complementary dodecads of 24 points, with outer automorphisms of M<sub>12</sub> swapping the two dodecads.

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

(Frobenius 1904) calculated the complex character table of M<sub>12</sub>.

M<sub>12</sub> has a strictly 5-transitive permutation representation on 12 points, whose point stabilizer is the [Mathieu group M<sub>11</sub>](wikipedia://en/15200683). Identifying the 12 points with the projective line over the field of 11 elements, M<sub>12</sub> is generated by the permutations of PSL<sub>2</sub>(11) together with the permutation (2,10)(3,4)(5,9)(6,7). This permutation representation preserves a [Steiner system](wikipedia://en/28016) S(5,6,12) of 132 special hexads, such that each pentad is contained in exactly 1 special hexad, and the hexads are the supports of the weight 6 codewords of the extended [ternary Golay code](wikipedia://en/3070794). In fact M<sub>12</sub> has two inequivalent actions on 12 points, exchanged by an outer automorphism; these are analogous to the two inequivalent actions of the symmetric group *S*<sub>6</sub> on 6 points.

The double cover 2.M<sub>12</sub> is the automorphism group of the extended [ternary Golay code](wikipedia://en/3070794), a dimension 6 length 12 code over the field of order 3 of minimum weight 6. In particular the double cover has an irreducible 6-dimensional representation over the field of 3 elements.

The double cover 2.M<sub>12</sub> is the automorphism group of any 12×12 [Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/507553).

M<sub>12</sub> centralizes an element of order 11 in the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422), as a result of which it acts naturally on a [vertex algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_algebra) over the field with 11 elements, given as the [Tate cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_cohomology) of the [monster vertex algebra](wikipedia://en/5032859).

:::
