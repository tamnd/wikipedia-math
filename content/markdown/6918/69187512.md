# Anand Pillay

*Image: Anand Pillay.jpg.* Anand Pillay, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2010

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anand Pillay"}

**Anand Pillay** (born 7 May 1951) is a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) working in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) and its applications in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pillay studied as an undergraduate at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), obtaining a Bachelor in Mathematics and Philosophy in 1973 at [Balliol College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College). At the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), he received his master's degree in mathematics in 1974 and his PhD in 1978 with [Wilfrid Hodges](wikipedia://en/1789771) at [Bedford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_College,_London), titled *Gaifman Operations, Minimal Models, and the Number of Countable Models*. In 1978, he was a [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) Fellow and visiting scientist at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). After teaching at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) starting in 1981 and at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) in Canada, he joined the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) as an assistant professor in 1983, where he became an associate professor in 1986 and a full professor in 1988. From 1996 to 2006, he was Swanlund Professor at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), where he is now Professor Emeritus. Since 2005, he has been the Chair of Mathematical Logic at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds). He also held positions as a visiting scholar at the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) in Toronto, at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in Berkeley, and at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in Cambridge.

## Career {#Career}

Pillay's dissertation work concerned the number of [countable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable) [models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28logic%29) of countable [theories](wikipedia://en/3512103); under the influence of the Paris school of model theory, he also worked on [stability theory](wikipedia://en/15828771). Later, he dealt with applications of model theory in other areas of mathematics, including [Nash manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_manifold) and [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), algebraic theory of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587), classification of [compact complex manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_complex_manifold), and [diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205).

Pillay was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) in 1994. In 2009 he was invited to present the [Tarski Lectures](wikipedia://en/54104355), titled *Compact Spaces, Definability, and Measures, in Model Theory.* His three lectures were titled "The Logic Topology", "Lie Groups from Nonstandard Models", and "Measures and Domination".[^o5] In 2001, he received the [Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation)'s research award, and was also a Humboldt Fellow at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel) in 1988 and at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) in 1992. In 2011, he gave the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431). He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
