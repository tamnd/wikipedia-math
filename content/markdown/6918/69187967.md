# Eleonora Di Nezza

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eleonora Di Nezza"}

**Eleonora Di Nezza** is an Italian mathematician, a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) researcher at the [Centre de mathématiques Laurent-Schwartz](wikipedia://en/62392394) and a professor of mathematics at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique),[^o3] in [Palaiseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaiseau), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). Her research is at the intersection of various branches of mathematics[^o4] including complex and differential geometry,[^o5] and focuses on [Kähler geometry](wikipedia://en/390538).[^o6]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Di Nezza earned her master's degree in mathematics from the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome),[^o8] and did her doctoral research between the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) and [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse_III_-_Paul_Sabatier_University) in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), during which reunified results on fractional [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964) in collaboration with Giampiero Palatucci and Enrico Valdinoci.[^o9][^o10] Her dissertation was on the Geometry of complex Monge-Ampère equations on compact Kähler manifolds.[^o11]

After receiving her PhD, she became a postdoctoral fellow at [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College), in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK) under a [Marie Curie Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Skłodowska-Curie_Actions), during which she joined the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), United States. In 2017 she moved to France to join the [Institute of Advanced Scientific Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) before becoming a lecturer at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) and a professor of mathematics at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was awarded the [CNRS Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Bronze_Medal) in 2021.[^o13][^o14] She was the 2023 recipient of the Prix Reine-Elizabeth from the Pierre Le Conte Foundation of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o15]

In 2023 she was named as a junior member to the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).[^o15]
