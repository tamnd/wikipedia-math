# John Purser (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Purser"}

**John Purser** (1835–1903) was an Irish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who was professor at [Queen's College, Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Belfast).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Son of John Tertius Purser (1809–1893), the general manager of the well known brewery [Guinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness),[^n1] Purser was educated in a wealthy family, which included artists, as his cousin [Sarah Purser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Purser), or engineers, as his brother-in-law [John Purser Griffith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Purser_Griffith). He was the brother of mathematician [Frederick Purser](wikipedia://en/60145273). He studied in [Trinity College, Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Dublin), graduating BA in mathematics in 1856.

The following years Purser was tutor to the children of [Lord Rosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Parsons,_3rd_Earl_of_Rosse), [Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Parsons,_4th_Earl_of_Rosse) and [Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Algernon_Parsons). In 1863, he was appointed professor of mathematics at [Queen's College, Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Belfast), he was in place until his retirement in 1901.[^n2]

Purser is much better known as a teacher than as a researcher,[^o6] and he had a good number of notable students, including Sir [Joseph Larmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Larmor), theoretical physicist who served as [Lucasian Professor of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/147921) at the University of Cambridge; [Charles Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Algernon_Parsons), the inventor of the turbine; Sir [John Henry MacFarland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Henry_MacFarland), who became Chancellor of Melbourne University; and [William McFadden Orr](wikipedia://en/14036131).[^n3]
