# William Zame

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Zame"}

**William R. Zame** is an American economist and mathematician, and distinguished professor of economics. Dr Zame earned his PhD degree at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University). Before joining the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1991, he held appointments in the Mathematics Departments of [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) and [the State University of New York at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Buffalo), and in the Economics and Mathematics Departments at The [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University). His research areas are [Experimental Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_economics), [Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance), [Game Theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [Microeconomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microeconomics). Research topics include the impact of culture on economic outcomes in diverse societies, [informational asymmetries](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/asymmetricinformation.asp) in macroeconomics, [experimental financial markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_finance), and a number of topics in [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488). He is currently Co-Editor of Economic Theory and Associate Editor of Theoretical Economics.[^o2][^o3][^o4]
