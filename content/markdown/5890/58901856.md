# Steinmetz curve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steinmetz curve"}

*Image: Steinmetz-kurve.svg.* Steinmetz curves for various cases

*Image: Steinmetz-cc.svg.* Steinmetz solid (intersection of two cylinders) involving Steinmetz curves (purple)

A **Steinmetz curve** is the curve of intersection of two right circular cylinders of [radii](wikipedia://en/1780815) $a$ and $b,$ whose axes intersect perpendicularly. In case of $a=b$ the Steinmetz curves are the edges of a [Steinmetz solid](wikipedia://en/7564265). If the cylinder axes are the x- and y-axes and $a\le b$, then the Steinmetz curves are given by the [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108):

:::

$$\begin{aligned} x (t) & = a \cos t \\ y (t) & = \pm \sqrt{b^2 - a^2 \sin^2 t} \\ z (t) & = a \sin t \end{aligned}$$

It is named after mathematician [Charles Proteus Steinmetz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Proteus_Steinmetz), along with [Steinmetz's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinmetz's_equation), [Steinmetz solids](wikipedia://en/7564265), and [Steinmetz equivalent circuit theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Induction_motor#Steinmetz_equivalent_circuit).

In the case when the two cylinders have equal radii the curve degenerates to two intersecting [ellipses](wikipedia://en/9277).
