# Zorya Shapiro

Academics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zorya Shapiro"}

**Zorya Yakovlevna Shapiro** (Зоря Яковлевна Шапиро; 7 December 1914 – 4 July 2013) was a Soviet mathematician, educator and translator. She is known for her contributions to representation theory and functional analysis in her collaboration with [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643), and the Shapiro-Lobatinski condition in elliptical boundary value problems.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Zorya Shapiro attended the [Moscow State University Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) from where she received her undergraduate and doctoral degrees by 1938.[^o4] She was active in the military department of the university, especially in aviation, learning to fly and land aeroplanes.[^o5]

She started her teaching career at the Faculty, shortly after Zoya Kishkina (1917–1989) and Natalya Eisenstadt (1912–1985), and very quickly became recognized for her courses in analysis.[^o4]

Shapiro married [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) in 1942. They had 3 sons, one of whom died in childhood.[^o6] Shapiro and Gelfand later divorced.[^o7]

In the 1980s, Shapiro lived in the same house as [Akiva Yaglom](wikipedia://en/28210480).[^o8] In 1991 Shapiro moved to [River Forest, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Forest,_Illinois) to live with her younger son. She died there on 4 July 2013.

## Career {#Career}

Shapiro published several works on [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). A contribution (with Gelfand) in integral geometry was to find inversion formulae for the reconstruction of the value of a function on a manifold in terms of integrals over a family of submanifolds, a result with applicability in [non-linear differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear_differential_equations), [tomography](wikipedia://en/385523), multi-dimensional [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and other domains.[^o10] Another work was on the representations of rotation groups of 3-dimensional spaces.[^o11]

Shapiro is best known for her elucidation of the conditions for well-defined solutions to the elliptical boundary value problem on [Sobolev spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobolev_spaces).[^o12]
