# Linda Gilbert Saucier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linda Gilbert Saucier"}

**Linda Gilbert Saucier** ( Phillips; born 1948)[^o2] is an American mathematician and textbook author, a distinguished professor emerita of mathematics and computer science at the [University of South Carolina Upstate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina_Upstate).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Linda Phillips was the daughter of Rudd George Phillips, an education specialist for the [United States Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force). She grew up in [Gulfport, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulfport,_Mississippi), and earned B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees from [Louisiana Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Tech_University) in 1970, 1972, and 1977 respectively. Her dissertation applied [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) to [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997); it was titled *An application of the Jordan canonical form to the epidemic problem*. She also became a faculty member at Louisiana Tech with her husband and co-author, Jimmie Gilbert, who died in 2005. She retired from the [University of South Carolina Upstate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina_Upstate) and was given the distinguished professor emerita title in 2011.

## Books {#Books}

Under the name Linda Gilbert, she became the author of "more than 37 mathematics textbooks" including *Elements of Modern Algebra*, *College Algebra*, *College Trigonometry*, *Precalculus*, and *Matrix Theory*.
