# Erling Størmer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Erling Størmer"}

**Erling Størmer** (born 2 November 1937) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who has mostly worked with [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras).

:::

He was born in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo) as a son of [Leif Størmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leif_Størmer).[^o3] He was a grandson of [Carl Størmer](wikipedia://en/1906523) and nephew of [Per Størmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_Størmer).[^o4] He took his doctorate at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1963 with thesis advisor [Richard Kadison](wikipedia://en/25798650),[^o5] and was a professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) from 1974 to his retirement in 2007.[^o3]

He is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
