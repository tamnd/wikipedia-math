# Stål Aanderaa

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stål Aanderaa"}

**Stål Aanderaa** (1 February 1931 – 24 January 2026) was a Norwegian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Aanderaa was born in [Beitstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beitstad). He completed the mag.scient. degree in 1959 and his doctorate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1966. He was a professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) from 1978 to his retirement in 2001.[^o5]

Aanderaa was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o6]

## Work {#Work}

Aanderaa is one of the namesakes of the [Aanderaa–Karp–Rosenberg conjecture](wikipedia://en/21681084).
