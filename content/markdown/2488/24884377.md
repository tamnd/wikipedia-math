# Ragni Piene

*Image: Piene ragni.jpg.* Ragni Piene

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ragni Piene"}

**Ragni Piene** (born 18 January 1947, [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo))[^o2] is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), with particular interest in enumerative results and intersection theory.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After a bachelor's degree from the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1969 and a DEA from [Université de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris) in 1970 [^o5] Piene received a doctorate in mathematics from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1976, advised by [Steven Kleiman](wikipedia://en/16169105). Her dissertation was titled *Plücker Formulas*.[^o6]

She was appointed professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1987.[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was elected a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 1994,[^o3][^o9] and in 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o10] and a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o11] She is also one of the protagonists of the Women of mathematics exhibition. [^o12]

## Service {#Service}

Since 2003 she has been a member of the executive committee of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868), and was the chair of the [Abel Committee](wikipedia://en/159285) from 2010–2011 to 2013–2014.[^o14][^o15]
