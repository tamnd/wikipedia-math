# John Grue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Grue"}

**John Grue** (born 1957) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) noted for his contributions to marine [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics) and [internal waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_waves).

:::

He took the [cand.real.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.real.) degree in 1982 and the [dr.philos.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr.philos.) degree in 1987, both at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo). He stayed at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1987 to 1988, and was appointed associate professor at the University of Oslo in 1990. In 1994 he was promoted to professor.[^o2] He has later served as guest professor at [Harbin Engineering University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbin_Engineering_University) in China.[^o3] He was Chair of the Abel Board 2018-2022 of the Abel Prize in mathematics.

He is a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters)[^o4] and the [Norwegian Academy of Technological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Technological_Sciences).[^o5]
