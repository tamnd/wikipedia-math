# Helge Holden

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Helge Holden"}

**Helge Holden** (born 28 September 1956) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the field of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He was Praeses of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) from 2014 to 2016.

:::

He earned the [dr.philos.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr.philos.) degree at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1985. The title of his dissertation with [Raphael Høegh-Krohn](wikipedia://en/28711790) was *Point Interactions and the Short-Range Expansion. A Solvable Model in Quantum Mechanics and Its Approximation*. He was appointed professor at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) (now: the [Norwegian University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_University_of_Science_and_Technology) ) in 1991.[^o3] His research interests are Differential equations, mathematical physics (in particular [hyperbolic conservation laws](wikipedia://en/1575825#Hyperbolic_system_and_conservation_laws) and [completely integrable systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completely_integrable_system)), [Stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis), and flow in porous media.

In 2014 he became Chairman of the board of the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) fund.[^o4]

He was elected Secretary General of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU) for the period 2019–2022.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He is a member of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters), the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters)[^o7] and of the [Norwegian Academy of Technological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Technological_Sciences).[^o8]

In 2013 he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to partial differential equations".[^o9] He was elected as a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2017, "for contributions to nonlinear partial differential equations and related fields, to research administration, and to the dissemination of mathematics".[^o10]

In 2022 he was awarded the [Gunnerus Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunnerus_Medal) for his academic achievement. The Gunnerus Medal is awarded by [The Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o11]
