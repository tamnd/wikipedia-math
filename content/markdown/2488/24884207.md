# Kristian B. Dysthe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kristian B. Dysthe"}

**Kristian Barstad Dysthe** (16 September 1937 – 30 July 2023) was a Norwegian mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dysthe took the [cand.real.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.real.) degree at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) in 1962, and the [dr.philos.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr.philos.) degree in 1972. He became professor in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Tromsø](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tromsø) in 1972, and at the University of Bergen from 1992 to retirement in 2007. He has been a visiting scholar at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), the [Scripps Institution of Oceanography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_Institution_of_Oceanography) and the [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o3] He was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o4] Dysthe died on 30 July 2023, at the age of 85.[^o5]
