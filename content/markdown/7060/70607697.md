# Opinion polling for the 2025 Dutch general election

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opinion polling for the 2025 Dutch general election"}

In the run-up to the [2025 Dutch general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Dutch_general_election), various organisations are carrying out [opinion polling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling) to gauge voting intentions in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands). Results of such polls are displayed in this list.

:::

The date range for these opinion polls are from the [2023 Dutch general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dutch_general_election), held on 22 November, to the present. Four main pollsters have performed opinion polls on voting intentions in the Netherlands during this period, these being: I&O Research, Ipsos, Peil.nl and Verian. I&O Research and Ipsos merged in January 2024, polling separately until July when they decided to poll together under the name Ipsos I&O.[^o2] Peil.nl does not give percentages, only showing seats while the other pollsters do include percentages.

## Political polling in the Netherlands {#Political_polling_in_the_Netherlands}

The political polling industry in the Netherlands is generally considered reliable. Absolute Total Raw Error in selected past elections has ranged from 11.8% to 16.0%, comparable to the European average. Documented conflicts of interest are rare, although peil.nl has been criticized for underrepresenting certain demographic groups in its samples.[^o4][^o5]
