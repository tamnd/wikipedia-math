# Abhyankar's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abhyankar's lemma"}

Abhyankar's lemma is not directly related to [Abhyankar's conjecture](wikipedia://en/3832555).

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Abhyankar's lemma** (named after [Shreeram Shankar Abhyankar](wikipedia://en/30876162)) allows one to kill [tame ramification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_ramification) by taking an extension of a base field.

:::

More precisely, Abhyankar's lemma states that if *A*, *B*, *C* are [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375) such that *A* and *B* are [finite extensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_extension) of *C*, with [ramification indices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramification_index) *a* and *b*, and *B* is tamely ramified over *C* and *b* divides *a*, then the [compositum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compositum) *AB* is an unramified extension of *A*.
