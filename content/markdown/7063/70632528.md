# Howard Elton Lacey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Elton Lacey"}

**Howard Elton Lacey** (February 9, 1937 in [Leakey, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leakey,_Texas) – June 21, 2013) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who studied [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134).

:::

After beginning his undergraduate studies at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), Lacey graduated from [Abilene Christian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abilene_Christian_University) with a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1959 and a master's degree in 1960.[^o2] He completed his Ph.D. in 1963 [New Mexico State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_University); his dissertation, *Generalized Compact Operators in Locally Convex Spaces*, was supervised by [Edward Thorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Thorp). He returned to Abilene Christian University as an assistant professor, then in 1964 moved to the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). In 1980 he returned to Texas A&M University as a professor and chair of the mathematics department, a position he held for eleven years. He was Associate Dean of Science for one year and retired in 2002.[^o2]

His specialty was [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces). He also worked a lot with Polish scientists, including 1972/73 as a *Fulbright Fellow* at the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw). He also worked in applied research at the [White Sands Missile Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Sands_Missile_Range) and at the *Man Space Craft Center* in [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston).[^o2]

He had been married to Bonnie Brown since 1958, whom he met while he was on a summer job with the [Army Corps of Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Corps_of_Engineers) in [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi) in 1957.[^o2]
