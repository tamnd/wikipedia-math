# Journal of Geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Geometry"}

The ***Journal of Geometry*** is a triannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), broadly considered. In particular this includes "foundations of geometry, geometric algebra, finite geometries, combinatorial geometry, and special geometries". It was established in 1971 by [Walter Benz](wikipedia://en/18994043) and is published by [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Hans Havlicek ([Technische Universität Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Wien)) and Alexander Kreuzer ([Universität Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Hamburg)).

:::
