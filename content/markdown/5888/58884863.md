# Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

**Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture** is a [restriction](wikipedia://en/880754) problem in [the representation theory](wikipedia://en/292786) of [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) or [*p*-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) posed by [Gan Wee Teck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gan_Wee_Teck), [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863), and [Dipendra Prasad](wikipedia://en/38106303).[^o3] The problem originated from a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) of Gross and Prasad for [special orthogonal groups](wikipedia://en/173954) but was later generalized to include all four [classical groups](wikipedia://en/10172878). In the cases considered, it is known that the [multiplicity of the restrictions is at most one](wikipedia://en/25976893)[^o4][^o5][^o6] and the conjecture describes when the multiplicity is precisely one.

:::

## Motivation {#Motivation}

A motivating example is the following classical branching problem in the theory of [compact Lie groups](wikipedia://en/750326#Compact_Lie_groups). Let $\pi$ be an [irreducible](wikipedia://en/385982) finite-dimensional representation of the compact [unitary group](wikipedia://en/173993) $U(n)$, and consider its restriction to the naturally embedded [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $U(n-1)$. It is known that this restriction is multiplicity-free, but one may ask precisely which irreducible representations of $U(n-1)$ occur in the restriction.

By the [Cartan–Weyl theory of highest weights](wikipedia://en/58092933), there is a classification of the irreducible representations of $U(n)$ via their [highest weights](wikipedia://en/277179#Highest_weight) which are in natural [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) with sequences of [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) $\underline{a} = (a_1 \geq a_2 \geq \cdots \geq a_n)$. Now suppose that $\pi$ has highest weight $\underline{a}$. Then an irreducible representation $\tau$ of $U(n-1)$ with highest weight $\underline{b}$ occurs in the restriction of $\pi$ to $U(n-1)$ (viewed as a subgroup of $U(n)$) if and only if $\underline{a}$ and $\underline{b}$ are interlacing, i.e. $a_1 \geq b_1 \geq a_2 \geq b_2 \geq \cdots \geq b_{n-1} \geq a_n$.[^o8]

The Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture then considers the analogous restriction problem for other classical groups.[^o9]

## Statement {#Statement}

The conjecture has slightly different forms for the different classical groups. The formulation for [unitary groups](wikipedia://en/173993#Indefinite_forms) is as follows.

### Setup {#Setup}

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) $k$ not of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) $2$ equipped with a non-degenerate [sesquilinear form](wikipedia://en/860138) that is $\varepsilon$-Hermitian (i.e. $\varepsilon = 1$ if the form is Hermitian and $\varepsilon = -1$ if the form is skew-Hermitian). Let $W$ be a non-degenerate [subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of $V$ such that $V = W \oplus W^\perp$ and $W^\perp$ is of dimension $(\varepsilon + 1)/2$. Then let $G = G(V) \times G(W)$, where $G(V)$ is the unitary group preserving the form on $V$, and let $H = \Delta G(W)$ be the [diagonal subgroup](wikipedia://en/1314854) of $G$.

Let $\pi = \pi_1 \boxtimes \pi_2$ be an irreducible smooth representation of $G$ and let $\nu$ be either the [trivial representation](wikipedia://en/450573) (the "Bessel case") or the [Weil representation](wikipedia://en/2981795) (the "Fourier–Jacobi case"). Let $\varphi = \varphi_1 \times \varphi_2$ be a generic [L-parameter](wikipedia://en/7676971#Local_Langlands_conjectures_for_other_groups) for $G = G(V) \times G(W)$, and let $\Pi_\varphi$ be the associated Vogan L-packet.

::: {#o14 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

### Local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture {#Local_Gan–Gross–Prasad_conjecture}

If $\varphi$ is a local L-parameter for $G$, then

$$\sum_{\text{relevant } \pi \in \Pi_\varphi} \dim \operatorname{Hom}_H (\pi \otimes \overline{\nu}, \mathbb{C}) = 1.$$

Letting $\eta_{\mathrm{GP}}$ be the "distinguished character" defined in terms of the [Langlands–Deligne local constant](wikipedia://en/32103592), then furthermore

$$\operatorname{Hom}_H (\pi(\varphi, \eta) \otimes \overline{\nu}, \mathbb{C}) \neq 0 \text{ if and only if } \eta = \eta_{\mathrm{GP}}.$$

:::

::: {#o16 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

### Global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture {#Global_Gan–Gross–Prasad_conjecture}

For a quadratic field extension $E/F$, let $L_E(s, \pi_1 \times \pi_2) := L_E(s, \pi_1 \boxtimes \pi_2, \mathrm{std}_n \boxtimes \mathrm{std}_{n-1})$ where $L_E$ is the global [L-function](wikipedia://en/245283) obtained as the product of local L-factors given by the [local Langlands conjectures](wikipedia://en/7676971). The conjecture states that the following are equivalent:

1. The period interval $P_H$ is nonzero when restricted to $\pi$.
1. For all places $v$, the local Hom space $\operatorname{Hom}_{H(F_v)}(\pi_v, \nu_v) \neq 0$ and $L_E(1/2, \pi_1 \times \pi_2) \neq 0$.

:::

## Current status {#Current_status}

::: {#o19 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

### Local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture {#Local_Gan–Gross–Prasad_conjecture}

In a series of four papers between 2010 and 2012, [Jean-Loup Waldspurger](wikipedia://en/31862139) [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) the local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for [tempered representations](wikipedia://en/10995628) of special orthogonal groups over [*p*-adic fields](wikipedia://en/51423).[^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23] In 2012, [Colette Moeglin](wikipedia://en/51408467) and Waldspurger then proved the local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for generic non-tempered representations of special orthogonal groups over *p*-adic fields.[^o24]

In his 2013 thesis, Raphaël Beuzart-Plessis proved the local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for the tempered representations of unitary groups in the *p*-adic Hermitian case under the same hypotheses needed to establish the [local Langlands conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Langlands_conjecture).[^o25]

Hongyu He proved the Gan–Gross–Prasad conjectures for discrete series representations of the real unitary group U(p,q).[^o26]

:::

::: {#o28 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture"}

### Global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture {#Global_Gan–Gross–Prasad_conjecture}

In a series of papers between 2004 and 2009, [David Ginzburg](wikipedia://en/58203934), [Dihua Jiang](wikipedia://en/63243143), and [Stephen Rallis](wikipedia://en/52065873) showed the (1) implies (2) direction of the global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for all quasisplit classical groups.[^o29][^o30][^o31]

In the Bessel case of the global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for unitary groups, [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911) used the theory of the [relative trace formula](wikipedia://en/20419621) by [Hervé Jacquet](wikipedia://en/25600925) and the work on the fundamental lemma by [Zhiwei Yun](wikipedia://en/51494005) to prove that the conjecture is true subject to certain local conditions in 2014.[^o32]

In the Fourier–Jacobi case of the global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for unitary groups, [Yifeng Liu](wikipedia://en/59846401) and Hang Xue showed that the conjecture holds in the skew-Hermitian case, subject to certain local conditions.[^o33][^o34]

In the Bessel case of the global Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for special orthogonal groups and unitary groups, [Dihua Jiang](wikipedia://en/63243143) and Lei Zhang used the theory of twisted automorphic descents to prove that (1) implies (2) in its full generality, i.e. for any irreducible cuspidal [automorphic representation](wikipedia://en/782146) with a generic global Arthur parameter, and that (2) implies (1) subject to a certain global assumption.[^o35]

:::
