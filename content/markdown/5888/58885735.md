# The Indispensability of Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="The Indispensability of Mathematics"}

***The Indispensability of Mathematics***[^o3] is a 2001 book by [Mark Colyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Colyvan) in which he examines the [Quine–Putnam indispensability argument](wikipedia://en/58885745) in the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439). This thesis is based on the premise that mathematical entities are placed on the same ontological foundation as other theoretical entities indispensable to our best [scientific theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_theories).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::
