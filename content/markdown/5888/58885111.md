# Onorato Candiota

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Onorato Candiota"}

**Onorato Candiota** (... – after 1808) was an Italian professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [math](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Math) at the Real Convitto di Bari, in [Bari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bari), Italy.[^o3] He lived between the 18th and 19th centuries. The exact dates and places of birth and death are currently unknown, even though it is known that he was from [Altamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altamura), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).[^o4] He's best known for his participation in the so-called [Altamuran Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altamuran_Revolution) (1799). He died short after 1808.[^o5]

:::

In 1796 he was appointed as member of [Accademia dei Georgofili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Georgofili) in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence). Moreover, following the founding of *Istituto nazionale della Repubblica Napoletana* in 1799, he was also appointed as member of that academy in the class of physics, natura history and chemistry. He was also member of the [Royal Society of Encouragement to Natural Sciences of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Encouragement_to_Natural_Sciences_of_Naples).[^o6]

A street in [Altamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altamura), Italy has been named after him (*via Onorato Candiota*).

## Altamuran Revolution {#Altamuran_Revolution}

Onorato Candiota, together with his brother Gian Giacomo Candiota,[^o8] was from a wealthy family. During the [Altamuran Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altamuran_Revolution) (1799), he defended the city of Altamura and, because of this, he was jailed together with his brother in the prison Forte di Brindisi; he was released together with the other people imprisoned, thanks to the [Treaty of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Florence) (1801). When they came back, they looked very different and they weren't recognized by their relatives.[^o9][^o10][^o11]
