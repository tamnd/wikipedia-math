# Melvin R. Novick

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Melvin R. Novick"}

**Melvin R. Novick** (September 21, 1932 – May 20, 1986) was an American statistician. He was a professor of Statistics at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa), and a consultant for the [Educational Testing Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service) (ETS).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
