# Dan Kalman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Kalman"}

**Daniel "Dan" Simon Kalman** (born March 21, 1952, in [Oakland, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California)) is an American mathematician and winner of nine awards for expository writing in mathematics.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After graduating from [Oakland High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_High_School_%28Oakland,_California%29) in 1970, Kalman matriculated at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College), where he graduated in 1974. From 1974 to 1980 he was a graduate student at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison),[^o4] where he received his PhD in 1980. He was from 1978 to 1979 an instructor at [Lawrence University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_University) in [Appleton, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appleton,_Wisconsin), and from 1979 to 1983 an assistant professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Green Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Green_Bay). After teaching as a visiting lecturer from 1983 to 1985 at [Augustana College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustana_University) in [Sioux Falls, South Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sioux_Falls,_South_Dakota), Kalman worked from 1985 to 1993 as a member of the technical staff of [The Aerospace Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Aerospace_Corporation) in Los Angeles. At Washington, D.C.'s [American University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University) he was from 1993 to 1998 an assistant professor, from 1998 to 2003 an associate professor, and from 2003 to 2018 a full professor, retiring in 2018 as professor emeritus. From 1996 to 1997 he was Associate Executive Director of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) in Washington, D.C.[^o4]

The MAA has given Kalman nine awards for outstanding expository articles or books. He won the [George Pólya Award](wikipedia://en/36409098) in 1994 and in 2003, the [Trevor Evans Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Evans_Award) in 1997 and (with coauthor Nathan Carter) in 2012, the [Carl B. Allendoerfer Award](wikipedia://en/40343255) (with coauthors Robert Mena and Shariar Shariari) in 1998 and (alone) in 2003, the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) in 2009 and (with coauthor Mark McKinzie) in 2013, and the [Beckenbach Book Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckenbach_Book_Prize) in 2012.[^o2]
