# Sakura Schafer-Nameki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sakura Schafer-Nameki"}

**Sakura Schafer-Nameki** is a German [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) working in [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) and [supersymmetric gauge theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetric_gauge_theory). She works at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) as a Professor of Mathematical Physics in the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850) and as a senior research fellow of [Wadham College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College,_Oxford).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Although partly of Japanese descent, Schafer-Nameki is originally from [Swabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swabia) in Germany. She studied both physics and mathematics at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) from 1995 to 1998. After coming to the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) for the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos), which she passed with distinction in 1999, she remained at Cambridge for doctoral studies. She completed her [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy). in 2003; her dissertation, *D-Branes in Boundary Field Theory*, was supervised by [Peter Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Goddard_%28physicist%29).

## Career {#Career}

After completing her doctorate, Schafer-Nameki became a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), a Postdoctoral Prize Fellow at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), and a senior postdoctoral fellow at the [Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavli_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics). She took a position as a lecturer at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) in 2010, and was promoted to reader in 2014. In 2016 she moved to Oxford as Professor of Mathematical Physics and Tutorial Fellow of Wadham College, becoming a senior research fellow at the college in 2020.

Her research combines [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) and geometry.[^o5] She was the principal investigator for the five-year [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) project "Higgs bundles: Supersymmetric Gauge Theories and Geometry" which began in 2016.[^o6][^o7] In 2020 she joined the Simons Collaboration on Special Holonomy in Geometry, Analysis, and Physics as one of its Principal Investigators.[^o5]
