# Geoffrey Timms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Timms"}

**Geoffrey Timms** OBE [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (1903–1982) was a 20th century British mathematician and cryptoanalyst. In the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) he was one of the several mathematicians working alongside [Alan Turing](wikipedia://en/1208) at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) breaking the Enigma code.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [Bradford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford) on 16 February 1903, son of Frederick Timms (1865-1947), Managing Director of a rope company, and Clara Louisa Barraclough (1867-1946). Both his parents were from [Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds).[^o5]

He studied Mathematics at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) graduating MA in 1925, then did postgraduate studies at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University), gaining his doctorate (PhD) in 1928. In 1929 he began lecturing in mathematics at [St Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews_University).

In 1933 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were fellow physicians [Herbert Westren Turnbull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Westren_Turnbull), [Edward Thomas Copson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Thomas_Copson), [Alexander Craig Aitken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Craig_Aitken) and Sir [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Taylor_Whittaker).[^o6]

A recognised mathematical genius he was seconded to work on the cracking of the Enigma code during the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War). He officially resigned from St Andrews in September 1945 to continue working with the Foreign Office, and got a position at GCHQ.[^o7]

In retired in 1968.

He died on 2 December 1982 in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland), [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand).
