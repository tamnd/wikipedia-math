# Lawrence Hubert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence Hubert"}

**Lawrence J. Hubert** (born 23 May 1944) is an American psychologist and statistician.

:::

Hubert earned a doctorate from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) under [Lee Cronbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Cronbach) and [Patrick Suppes](wikipedia://en/4824664), and held professorships in psychology (later as [Lyle H. Lanier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyle_H._Lanier) Professor) and statistics at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign).[^o2][^o3] Hubert was elected a foreign member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2007.[^o4] The [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500) gave him the Career Award for Lifetime Achievement in 2015.[^o5]
