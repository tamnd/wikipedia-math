In 8-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **1<sub>42</sub>** is a [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), constructed within the symmetry of the [E<sub>8</sub>](wikipedia://en/647994) group.

:::

Its [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) is **1<sub>42</sub>**, describing its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 1-node sequences.

The **rectified 1<sub>42</sub>** is constructed by points at the mid-edges of the **1<sub>42</sub>** and is the same as the birectified 2<sub>41</sub>, and the quadrirectified 4<sub>21</sub>.

These polytopes are part of a family of 255 (2<sup>8</sup> − 1) convex [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) in 8 dimensions, made of [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) facets and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), defined by all non-empty combinations of rings in this [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

The **1<sub>42</sub>** is composed of 2400 facets: 240 **[1<sub>32</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_1_32_polytope)** polytopes, and 2160 [7-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube) (**1<sub>41</sub>**). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [birectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_7-simplex).

This polytope, along with the [demiocteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiocteract), can [tessellate](wikipedia://en/6008654) 8-dimensional space, represented by the symbol **1<sub>52</sub>**, and Coxeter-Dynkin diagram: .

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- [E. L. Elte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._Elte) (1912) excluded this polytope from his listing of semiregular polytopes, because it has more than two types of 6-faces, but under his naming scheme it would be called V<sub>17280</sub> for its 17280 vertices.[^o15]
- [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) named it **1<sub>42</sub>** for its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 1-node branch.
- **Diacositetraconta-dischiliahectohexaconta-zetton** (acronym: bif) – 240-2160 facetted polyzetton (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The 17280 vertices can be defined as sign and location permutations of:

All sign combinations (32): (280 × 32 = 8960 vertices)

(4, 2, 2, 2, 2, 0, 0, 0)

Half of the sign combinations (128): ((1 + 8 + 56) × 128 = 8320 vertices)

(2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2)

(5, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1)

(3, 3, 3, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1)

The edge length is 2$\sqrt{2}$ in this coordinate set, and the polytope radius is 4$\sqrt{2}$.
