::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="2 41 polytope"}

In 8-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **2<sub>41</sub>** is a [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), constructed within the symmetry of the [E<sub>8</sub>](wikipedia://en/647994) group.

:::

Its [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) is **2<sub>41</sub>**, describing its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 2-node sequences.

The **rectified 2<sub>41</sub>** is constructed by points at the mid-edges of the **2<sub>41</sub>**. The **birectified 2<sub>41</sub>** is constructed by points at the triangle face centers of the **2<sub>41</sub>**, and is the same as the [rectified 1<sub>42</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_1_42_polytope).

These polytopes are part of a family of 255 (2<sup>8</sup> − 1) convex [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) in 8-dimensions, made of [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) facets, defined by all permutations of rings in this [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

The **2<sub>41</sub>** is composed of 17,520 [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) (240 [2<sub>31</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_2_31_polytope) polytopes and 17,280 [7-simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex)), 144,960 *6-faces* (6,720 [2<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19239704) polytopes and 138,240 [6-simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex)), 544,320 5-faces (60,480 [2<sub>11</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentacross) and 483,840 [5-simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex)), 1,209,600 *4-faces* ([4-simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentachoron)), 1,209,600 cells ([tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606)), 483,840 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) ([triangles](wikipedia://en/30654)), 69,120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 2160 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [7-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube).

This polytope is a facet in the [uniform tessellation, 2<sub>51</sub>](wikipedia://en/19550444) with [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram):

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- [E. L. Elte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._Elte) named it V<sub>2160</sub> (for its 2160 vertices) in his 1912 listing of semiregular polytopes.[^o15]
- It is named **2<sub>41</sub>** by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) for its bifurcating Coxeter-Dynkin diagram, with a single ring on the end of the 2-node sequence.
- **Diacositetraconta-myriaheptachiliadiacosioctaconta-zetton** for 240-17280 facetted polyzetton; Acronym: bay (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 8 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 8-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

Removing the node on the short branch leaves the [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex): . There are 17280 of these facets

Removing the node on the end of the 4-length branch leaves the [2<sub>31</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_2_31_polytope), . There are 240 of these facets. They are centered at the positions of the 240 vertices in the [4<sub>21</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_4_21_polytope) polytope.

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [7-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube), 1<sub>41</sub>, .

Seen in a [configuration matrix](wikipedia://en/56419724), the element counts can be derived by mirror removal and ratios of [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004#Properties) orders.[^o20]
