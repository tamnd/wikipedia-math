# Founders of statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Founders of statistics"}

[Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) is the theory and application of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) to the [scientific method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_method) including hypothesis generation, [experimental design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_design), [sampling](wikipedia://en/160361), [data collection](wikipedia://en/11501746), [data summarization](wikipedia://en/27587), [estimation](wikipedia://en/10043), [prediction](wikipedia://en/246066) and [inference](wikipedia://en/27577) from those results to the [population](wikipedia://en/27585) from which the experimental sample was drawn. [Statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661) are skilled people who thus apply statistical methods. [Hundreds of statisticians are notable](wikipedia://en/140829). This article lists statisticians who have been especially instrumental in the development of [theoretical](wikipedia://en/888711) and [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics).
