# Goldie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goldie"}

**Clifford Joseph Price** (born 19 September 1965), known professionally as **Goldie**, is an English music producer, [DJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ), graffiti artist and actor.

:::

Initially gaining exposure for his work as a [graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) artist, Goldie became well known for his pioneering role as a musician in the 1990s UK [jungle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_music), [drum and bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_and_bass) and [breakbeat hardcore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakbeat_hardcore) scenes. He released a variety of singles as **Rufige Kru** and **Metalheads** and co-founded the label [Metalheadz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalheadz). He later released several albums under his own name, including the 1995 album *[Timeless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeless_%28Goldie_album%29)*, which entered the UK charts at number 7.

Goldie's acting credits include the [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film *[The World Is Not Enough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Is_Not_Enough)*, [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie)'s *[Snatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snatch_%28film%29)*, and the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC) soap opera *[EastEnders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EastEnders)*.

## Early life {#Early_life}

Born in [Walsall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsall), England, but raised in [Wolverhampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton),[^o5] Price is of Jamaican and Scottish heritage.[^o5][^o6] He was put up for [adoption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adoption) at the age of three, and raised in childcare homes and by several [foster parents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_parents).[^o6] According to his 2002 autobiography, he was physically and sexually abused during this time.[^o7] Price was a member of the [breakdance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakdance) crew Westside, based in the [Whitmore Reans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitmore_Reans) and [Heath Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Town) areas of Wolverhampton, in the 1980s. He later joined a breakdance crew called the Birmingham [Bboys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bboy), and made his name as a [graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) artist in the [West Midlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Midlands_%28county%29).

His artwork around [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham) and Wolverhampton was featured heavily in [Dick Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Fontaine)'s documentary *Bombin'*. He is mentioned for his graffiti in the book *[Spraycan Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spraycan_Art)* by [Henry Chalfant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Chalfant) and [James Prigoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Prigoff), which contains several examples of his art.

He moved to the United States owing to graffiti projects, and also started selling [grills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grill_%28jewelry%29) (gold teeth jewellery) in New York and Miami; he continued this business after his return to the UK in 1988.[^o6] His nickname stems from "Goldielocks", an earlier nickname given to him during his Bboys days and subsequently shortened when he no longer wore [dreadlocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreadlocks).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

### Music {#Music}

By 1991, Price had discovered the British [breakbeat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakbeat) music scene when his girlfriend, DJ [Kemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemistry), introduced him to the pioneering [jungle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldskool_jungle) and [drum and bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_and_bass) [producers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_producer) Dennis "Dego" McFarlane and Mark "[Marc Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Mac)" Clair, known as [4hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4hero).[^o6] He went on to execute some design and [A&R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A&R) work for 4hero's [Reinforced Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforced_Records) label.

In 1992, Price made his first record appearance by contributing to an EP by Icelandic group Ajax Project that included the track *Ruffige*.[^o10][^o11]

His releases *Killa Muffin* b/w *Krisp Biscuit* and the *Dark Rider* [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play) were released under the "Rufige Kru" name. The track "Terminator", released under the name "Metalheads" in 1992,[^o12] was a pivotal [darkcore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkcore) release and is noted for its use of [pitch shifting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_shifting). In late 1993, this was followed up with *Angel/You and Me*, and the release of *Internal Affairs*, a collaboration between Price and 4hero with vocals from [Diane Charlemagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Charlemagne).[^o13]

1994 saw him setting up his own record label, [Metalheadz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalheadz).[^n1]

*Image: Goldie timeless tattoo.jpg.* Goldie next to a "Metalheadz" tattoo, 2001

His first studio album, *[Timeless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeless_%28Goldie_album%29)*, followed in 1995. *Timeless* entered the [UK Albums Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Albums_Chart) at number seven.[^o14] The album fused the [breakbeats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakbeat) and basslines common in jungle with orchestral textures and [soul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soul_music) vocals by [Diane Charlemagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Charlemagne). The album's title track was a 21-minute [symphonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphonic) piece. "[Inner City Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_City_Life)", a track from the album, reached number 39 in the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart).[^o14] *Timeless* helped to popularise drum and bass as a form of [musical expression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_expression).[^o15] The music critic [Simon Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Reynolds) noted that Price's credentials as a musical innovator—and particularly as one of the key driving forces of innovation in the jungle/breakbeat scene—were exceptional. "Goldie revolutionised jungle not once but thrice", he noted in *[The Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire_%28magazine%29)* magazine, continuing, "First there was 'Terminator' (pioneering the use of time stretching), then 'Angel' (fusing [Diane Charlemagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Charlemagne)'s live vocal with [David Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Byrne)/[Brian Eno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Eno) samples to prove that hardcore could be more 'conventionally' musical), now there's 'Timeless', a 22-minute hardcore symphony."[^o16]

In 1996, he released the *Toasted Both Sides Please* remix of the [Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bush_%28British_band%29) song "[Swallowed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swallowed_%28song%29)", which topped charts in the US and Canada.[^o17][^o18][^o19]

Price released his second album, *[Saturnz Return](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturnz_Return)*, in 1998. The album's opening track, "Mother", is an hour-long orchestral drum and bass piece. The album featured appearances by [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie), [Noel Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noel_Gallagher) and [KRS-One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KRS-One). The album met with mixed reviews. David Brown of *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly)* called the album "ambitious but monotonous and overlong—[Pink Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Floyd) with a gold tooth".[^o20]

In 2002, Price said that he had been working for three years on a film called *Sine Tempus*,[^o21] described as a coming-of-age story of a young paintbrush artist. In 2006, he announced the soundtrack as his new album.[^o22] The album was released via the Metalheadz website in 2008, but the film has not been released.

His Rufige Kru project has enlisted a number of producers and engineers to work in collaboration with over time. These have included [Mark Rutherford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Rutherford_%28composer%29), [Rob Playford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Playford), Technical Itch, Heist, Cujo, Danny J, [Doc Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Scott) and Agzilla da Ice.[^o23][^o24][^o25]

March 2013 saw the release of *The Alchemist: The Best of Goldie 1992–2012*, featuring prominent tracks from throughout Price's musical career.[^o26] A subsequent compilation, the three-CD Masterpiece set released by Ministry of Sound in 2014, brought together tracks that influenced him ([Soul II Soul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soul_II_Soul)'s "Back To Life", [Roy Ayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Ayers)' "Everybody Loves The Sunshine") with cuts that soundtracked his entry into the rave scene and key moments from the drum'n'bass scene.[^o27]

In 2017, the Goldie album *[The Journey Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Journey_Man)* was released, which Price described as his "[magnum opus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum_opus)" and "the most important thing that I've ever made."[^o7]

In 2020, Goldie launched his new record label, Fallen Tree 1Hundred.[^o28]

A 25th anniversary edition of Timeless was released by London Records the following year. The new release included a triple gold vinyl version and a compact disc version.[^o29][^o30]

### Television and film {#Television_and_film}

Price has appeared in numerous television shows and programmes, including *[Celebrity Big Brother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebrity_Big_Brother_%28British_TV_series%29)*, [Bravo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bravo_%28British_TV_channel%29) TV's *The World's Deadliest Gangs*, *[Passengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passengers_%28British_TV_series%29)*, and as gangster [Angel Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Hudson) in the British soap opera *[EastEnders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EastEnders)* between 2001 and 2002.

In film, he has appeared in *[Everybody Loves Sunshine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everybody_Loves_Sunshine)* and the [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film, *[The World Is Not Enough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Is_Not_Enough)* in 1999, and [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie)'s *[Snatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snatch_%28film%29)* in 2000.

His earliest television appearances were in 1987, appearing in [BBC1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC1)'s *Daytime Live* in October 1987 to discuss the rise of urban art across the United Kingdom,[^o32] *Central Weekend*, a Friday evening topical debate show on [Central TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITV_Central), promoting [graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) as an art form, and Central TV's *Here and Now* programme featuring [Pogus Caesar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogus_Caesar)'s photographs of New York.

In June 2008, Price participated in the German/French TV channel [ARTEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arte) format '[Into the Night with ...](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durch_die_Nacht_mit_...)' episode 052, where he met artist [Skream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skream). The episode not only shows an adventurous encounter between two hard-drinking stars of the club scene, but also a close-up look at the operation of one of Europe's most important electro festivals, the [Melt Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melt_Festival).[^o33]

During August and September 2008, the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC) broadcast *[Maestro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maestro_%28British_TV_series%29)*, a reality television show in which eight celebrities learned to conduct a concert orchestra, Price placed second.[^o7]

As a result of *Maestro*, he appeared in the programme *[Classic Goldie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_Goldie)* in July 2009, learning how to write a score for a large orchestra and choir. The resulting composition, commissioned by the BBC and entitled *Sine Tempore* (*Timeless*), was performed at two children's [Promenade concerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Proms) in the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall) on 1 and 2 August 2009, which featured music connected with [Charles Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin) and the creation and evolution of the world.[^o34]

On 26 March 2011, he appeared in a three-part reality television series, *[Goldie's Band: By Royal Appointment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldie's_Band:_By_Royal_Appointment)* in which he led a group of music experts as they conducted a nationwide search for young talented musicians and then selected and coached 12 of them, who collaborated to create some musical pieces for a performance at [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace).[^o35]

In December 2020, Price presented the [Sky Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Arts) programme *Goldie: The Art That Made Me* covering influential [graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) and [street art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_art).[^o36]

### Art {#Art}

In 2007, Price returned to the art world with an art exhibition, "Love Over Gold", which was held at the Leonard Street Gallery, London.[^o38] In 2008, he teamed up with [Pete Tong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Tong) to provide much of the artwork for Tong's new [Wonderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonderland_%28event%29) club night at Eden nightclub in [San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Antoni_de_Portmany), [Ibiza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibiza).[^o39]

There was an exhibition of Price's art in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) from 13 to 26 June 2008.[^o40] In April 2009, a retrospective exhibition titled "Kids Are All Riot" took place in [Shoreditch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoreditch), London, coinciding with the release of his screenprint "Apocalypse Angel".

Around 2008, Price's art work was displayed on the [London Underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Underground) by the arts company [Art Below](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Below).[^o41]

In 2021, he modelled for [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton) Menswear Spring / Summer collection and contributed a song.

### Books {#Books}

The innovative nature of Goldie's art (both sonic and visual) was a consistent theme in [Kodwo Eshun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodwo_Eshun)'s acclaimed book 'More Brilliant Than The Sun' (published in 1998) which has long been seen as a landmark text on Afro-futurism. Among the works that Eshun referenced were Goldie's 1986 graffiti piece Future World Machine and his 1992 single 'Terminator'. Noting the links between the multi-dimensionality in both painting and sound, Eshun noted: "There's a big interface between graffiti and the break."[^o43]

In 2002, Goldie's autobiography 'Nine Lives' was published by [Hodder & Stoughton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodder_&_Stoughton). Written with London-based writer [Paul Gorman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Gorman), the book traced his life story from childhood in care and foster homes through to the first flush of his success as a groundbreaking musical artist.

A second autobiography 'All Things Remembered' was published in November 2017 by Faber & Faber. Written when Goldie was 52, it covers a more comprehensive span than the earlier book but is more philosophic in tone. While incidents from his extraordinary childhood are recounted, it also includes his ruminations on musical heroes such as Pat Metheny and a conversation with "my left-hand man" [Doc Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Scott) as well as memories of his work in TV and film. The book includes pictures of Goldie on set with [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie). Co-written with Ben Thompson, 'All Things Remembered' was chosen by [Nicola Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Barker) as one of The Guardian's Books Of The Year. She described it as "a fabulous, whirling kaleidoscope of music, memory and trauma . . . magical and cautionary."[^o44]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In the early 1990s, Price had a relationship with [drum and bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_and_bass) artist [Kemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemistry), who died in a car accident in 1999,[^o46] and was romantically involved with Icelandic singer [Björk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Björk) in the mid-1990s.[^n1]

In 1998, Price bought a country house in [Bovingdon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bovingdon), Hertfordshire.[^o22][^o47]

Price married model Sonjia Ashby in 2002.[^o48] They divorced in 2005.[^o22]

In 2007, Goldie began a relationship with Mika Wassenaar, a Canadian. The couple appeared in the 20 June 2009 episode of ITV's *[All Star Mr & Mrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Star_Mr_&_Mrs)* with [Phillip Schofield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillip_Schofield) and [Fern Britton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fern_Britton). Mika and Price married in 2010.[^o49]

On 29 April 2010, Price opened the William Tyndale Primary School Sports Pitch in [Islington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islington), London.[^o50]

In July 2010, Price received an honorary doctorate in Social Sciences from [Brunel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University), Uxbridge.[^o51] On 3 September 2010, he received an honorary degree of Doctor of Design from the [University of Wolverhampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wolverhampton).[^o52]

Price and his wife Mika moved to Thailand in 2015.[^o7]

Price was appointed a [Member of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (MBE) in the [2016 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_New_Year_Honours) awards, for services to music and young people.

Price was accused of assaulting a security guard at the 2017 Glastonbury festival after the guard had refused to let Price's daughter backstage; Price later emailed a response which read, "Yep, guilty as charged". District judge Lynn Matthews at Bristol Magistrates' Court rejected Price's unorthodox emailed response and in a video call in March 2018, Price admitted to assault,[^o54] resulting being ordered to pay almost £2,500 in fines.[^o55]

Price has five children, including Danny[^o47] and Jamie, who was sentenced to [life imprisonment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_imprisonment) for murder in 2010.[^o56]
