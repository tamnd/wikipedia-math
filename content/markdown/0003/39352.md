This is a **list of telecommunications encryption terms**. This list is derived in part from the *Glossary of Telecommunication Terms* published as [Federal Standard 1037C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Standard_1037C).

- [A5/1](wikipedia://en/294149) – a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) used to provide over-the-air communication [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy) in the [GSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_System_for_Mobile_Communications) [cellular telephone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_phone) standard.
