# Jana Rodriguez Hertz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jana Rodriguez Hertz"}

Rodriguez Hertz **María Alejandra** (**Jana**) **Rodriguez Hertz Frugoni** (born 1970) is an Argentine and Uruguayan mathematician, professor, and researcher.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rodriguez Hertz was born on February 11, 1970,[^o5] in [Rosario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosario,_Santa_Fe), Argentina.[^o4] She studied mathematics at the Facultad de Ciencias Exactas, Ingeniería y Agrimensura of the [National University of Rosario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Rosario), Rosario, Argentina, earning a bachelor's degree there in 1994. She earned a doctorate from the [University of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Republic_%28Uruguay%29) in [Montevideo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montevideo), Uruguay, in 1999.[^o5]

Formerly a professor at the Faculty of Engineering at the University of the Republic,[^o6] she became the first and only woman with a Grade 5 in mathematics in Uruguay.[^o7] Since 2016 she has been a professor at the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_University_of_Science_and_Technology) in China.[^o5]

In 2016, Rodriguez Hertz was elected a Regional Vice President, Latin America & the Caribbean, of the [Organization for Women in Science for the Developing World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_for_Women_in_Science_for_the_Developing_World) for the period of 2016–20.[^o8]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Rodriguez Hertz works on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).

Since 2011, Rodriguez Hertz has been involved in the Uruguayan educational debate. She has participated in numerous interviews, public events and conferences on the subject. She has developed a critical point of view on the educational budget, [PISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programme_for_International_Student_Assessment) tests, teaching career development, and other topics. Her particular point of view made her a benchmark of public opinion on the issues.[^o10][^o11] She has been a columnist in different media, highlighting her participation in 2014 in the "La Tertulia" episode of the program *En Perspectiva* by [Emiliano Cotelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emiliano_Cotelo) on [Radio El Espectador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Espectador_810), as well as the "Jana y sus hermanas" and the "Suena Tremendo" episodes on the same radio station.[^o12] She is also the author of the blog, "¿Y por qué no?".[^o13]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- 1992, Premio Jóvenes Notables, Fundación Bolsa de Comercio de [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires).
- 1999, Investigadora Grado 3, PEDECIBA Matemática.
- 2004, Premio Fondo Nacional de Investigadores, CONICYT.
- 2009, Investigadora Nivel II, Sistema Nacional de Investigadores, SNI, ANII.
- 2013, Investigadora de Primer Nivel, Grado 5, Programa de Desarrollo de las Ciencias Básicas (PEDECIBA).
