# Milman's reverse Brunn–Minkowski inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milman's reverse Brunn–Minkowski inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly, in asymptotic [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882), **Milman's reverse Brunn–Minkowski inequality** is a result due to [Vitali Milman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Milman)[^o2] that provides a reverse inequality to the famous [Brunn&ndash;Minkowski inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunn&ndash;Minkowski_inequality) for [convex bodies](wikipedia://en/8652020) in *n*-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. Namely, it bounds the volume of the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of two bodies from above in terms of the volumes of the bodies.

:::

## Introduction {#Introduction}

Let *K* and *L* be convex bodies in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. The Brunn–Minkowski inequality states that

$$\mathrm{vol}(K+L)^{1/n} \geq \mathrm{vol}(K)^{1/n} + \mathrm{vol}(L)^{1/n}~,$$

where vol denotes *n*-dimensional [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) and the + on the left-hand side denotes Minkowski addition.

In general, no reverse bound is possible, since one can find convex bodies *K* and *L* of unit volume so that the volume of their Minkowski sum is arbitrarily large. Milman's theorem states that one can replace one of the bodies by its image under a properly chosen volume-preserving [linear map](wikipedia://en/18102) so that the left-hand side of the Brunn–Minkowski inequality is bounded by a constant multiple of the right-hand side.

The result is one of the main structural theorems in the local theory of [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces).[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=named-heading name="Statement of the inequality"}

## Statement of the inequality {#Statement_of_the_inequality}

There is a constant *C*, independent of *n*, such that for any two centrally symmetric convex bodies *K* and *L* in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>, there are volume-preserving linear maps *φ* and *ψ* from **R**<sup>*n*</sup> to itself such that for any real numbers *s*, *t* > 0

$$\mathrm{vol} ( s \, \varphi K + t \, \psi L )^{1/n} \leq C \left( s\, \mathrm{vol} ( \varphi K )^{1/n} + t\, \mathrm{vol} ( \psi L )^{1/n} \right)~.$$

One of the maps may be chosen to be the identity.[^o7]

:::
