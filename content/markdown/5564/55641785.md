# Thérèse Stukel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thérèse Stukel"}

**Thérèse A. Stukel** is a Canadian statistician who works as a senior core scientist at the [Institute for Clinical Evaluative Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Clinical_Evaluative_Sciences), as a professor in the Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation of the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), and as an adjunct professor of epidemiology and of [The Dartmouth Institute for Health Policy and Clinical Practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dartmouth_Institute_for_Health_Policy_and_Clinical_Practice) at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College). Topics in her research include [surgical mortality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perioperative_mortality), the effects of regional variations in healthcare spending, and [heart-related health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiology).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Stukel studied mathematics at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa), graduating in 1973. and earned a maîtrise (one-year master's degree) in applied mathematics from the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes) in 1974. After completing a [diploma in advanced studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies) in statistics jointly from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) and [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University) in 1975, she returned to Canada for a Ph.D. in statistics at the University of Toronto in 1983. Her dissertation, supervised by David F. Andrews, was *Generalized Logistic Models*.

She became an assistant professor of statistics at [Old Dominion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Dominion_University) in 1983, and in 1984 moved to Dartmouth College as an assistant professor of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878). In 2002 she moved to the Institute for Clinical Evaluative Sciences, as vice president for research, and at the same time took a position as a professor at the University of Toronto.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2007.
