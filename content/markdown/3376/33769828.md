# Quantum revival

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum revival"}

*Image: Fullrevival.gif.* Full and exact revival of the semi-Gaussian wave function in an infinite two-dimensional [potential well](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_potential_well) during its time evolution. Fractional revivals occur when the scaled shape of the wave function replicates itself an integer number of times over the well area.

In [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), **quantum revival** is a periodic recurrence of the quantum [wave function](wikipedia://en/145343) during its time-evolution.[^o2] This can be either many times in space as multiple scaled copies of the initial wave function (**fractional revival**), or approximately or exactly to its original form (**full revival**). A quantum wave function that is periodic in time therefore exhibits a full revival every [period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Period_%28physics%29). The phenomenon of revival is most readily observable in wave functions that are [well-localized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_wave_packet) [wave packets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_packet) at the beginnings of their time-evolutions, such as in the [hydrogen atom](wikipedia://en/14225). For hydrogen, fractional revivals show up as multiple angular [Gaussian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_distribution) bumps around the circle drawn by the radial maximum of the leading circular-state component (that with the highest amplitude in the [eigenstate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenstate) expansion) of the original localized state, and the full revival as the original Gaussian.[^o3] Full revivals are exact for the [infinite quantum well](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_quantum_well), [harmonic oscillator](wikipedia://en/50719), or hydrogen atom, while for shorter times are approximate for the hydrogen atom and many other quantum systems.[^o4]

## Example – arbitrary truncated wave function of the quantum system with rational energies {#Example_–_arbitrary_truncated_wave_function_of_the_quantum_system_with_rational_energies}

Consider a quantum system with the energies $E_i$ and the eigenstates $\psi_i$

$$H \psi_i = E_i \psi_i,$$

and let the energies be the [rational](wikipedia://en/19727024) fractions of some constant $C$:

$$E_i= C {M_i \over N_i}$$

(for example, for the [hydrogen atom](wikipedia://en/14225), $M_i=1$, $N_i=i^2$, and $C=-13.6 eV$).

Then the truncated (till $\mathbb{N}_{max}$ of states) solution of the time-dependent [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) is

$$\Psi(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{\mathbb{N}_{max}}a_i e^{-i {{E_i} \over \hbar} t} \psi_i.$$

Let $L_{cm}$ be the [least common multiple](wikipedia://en/17961) of all $N_i$, and $L_{cd}$ be the [greatest common divisor](wikipedia://en/12354) of all $M_i$. Then for each $N_i$, the quantity ${L_{cm}}/ N_i$ is an integer, for each $M_i$ the quantity ${M_{i}}/ L_{cd}$ is an integer, $2 \pi M_i {L_{cm}}/(N_i L_{cd})$ is the full multiple of $2 \pi$ angle, and

$$\Psi(t)=\Psi(t+T)$$

after the full revival time

$$T={2 \pi \hbar \over {L_{cd} C}} L_{cm}.$$

For a quantum system as small as hydrogen and $\mathbb{N}_{max}$ as small as 100, a full revival may take quadrillions of years. For example, a [Trojan wave packet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_wave_packet) in a hydrogen atom repeats itself after sweeping almost the whole hypercube of quantum phases exactly every full revival time.

In a system with rational energies—that is, where exact full revival exists—its existence immediately proves the quantum [Poincaré recurrence theorem](wikipedia://en/2404544), and the time of the full quantum revival equals the Poincaré recurrence time. While the rational numbers are [dense](wikipedia://en/23523594) in real numbers, and the arbitrary function of the quantum number can be approximated arbitrarily closely with [Padé approximants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padé_approximants), for the arbitrarily long times, each quantum system therefore revives almost exactly. It also means that Poincaré recurrence and full revival are mathematically the same thing,[^o7] and it is commonly accepted that the recurrence is called the full revival if it occurs after a reasonable and physically measurable time that is detectable by realistic apparatus. This happens due to a very special energy spectrum having a large basic energy spacing gap of which the energies are arbitrary (not necessarily harmonic) multiples.
