# Archives of American Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archives of American Mathematics"}

The **Archives of American Mathematics,** located at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), aims to collect, preserve, and provide access to the papers principally of American mathematicians and the records of American mathematical organizations.

:::

## History {#History}

The Archives began in 1975 at the University of Texas at Austin with the preservation of the papers of Texas mathematicians [R.L. Moore](wikipedia://en/1077133) and [H.S. Vandiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Vandiver).[^o3]

In 1978, the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) established the university as the official repository for its archival records and the name "Archives of American Mathematics" was adopted to encompass all of the mathematical archival collections at the university.[^o4] Originally a part of the [Harry Ransom Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Ransom_Center), in 1984, the Archives was added to the special collections of the [Briscoe Center for American History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolph_Briscoe_Center_for_American_History) at the University of Texas at Austin.[^o5]

## Collections {#Collections}

The AAM includes approximately 120 collections.

### Notable examples {#Notable_examples}

- [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) Records.
- [Thomas F. Banchoff](wikipedia://en/4708571) Papers document a career of teaching, writing, and making mathematical films.
- [Marion Walter](wikipedia://en/42350645) Photograph Collection includes photographs of A.A. Albert, H.S.M. Coxeter, Paul Erdős, Fritz John, D.H. Lehmer, Alexander Ostrowski, George Polya, Mina Rees, and Olga Taussky-Todd.[^o8]
- [School Mathematics Study Group](wikipedia://en/1700591) Records document the history of the "New Math" movement of the 1960s, and includes the files of the director, Edward G. Begle.
- [Dorothy L. Bernstein](wikipedia://en/7097637) Papers reflect both her professional and personal life.
- [Paul R. Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598) Photograph Collection consists of 14,000 photographs Halmos and others took from the 1930s to 2006.
- [Ivor Grattan-Guinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivor_Grattan-Guinness) Papers reflect the career of a mathematics historian.
- [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159) Correspondence Collection consists of 435 letters between Erdős and Pomerance.
