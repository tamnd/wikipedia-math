# Fred Sommers

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fred Sommers"}

**Frederic Tamler Sommers** (January 1, 1923 – October 2, 2014), better known as **Fred Sommers**, was an American philosopher who, after an initial focus on [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology) generally, turned his attention specifically to a revival of [classical logic](wikipedia://en/55868). He is the father of the philosopher [Tamler Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamler_Sommers).

:::

## Birth and education {#Birth_and_education}

Sommers was born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) on January 1, 1923.[^o4] His family was Jewish and he studied under Rabbi Joseph Soloveitchik in the 1950s.[^o5] He received his BA and PhD in philosophy at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), his dissertation being entitled *An Empiricist Ontology: A Study in the Metaphysics of [Alfred North Whitehead](wikipedia://en/43395).*[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Sommers began his academic career at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University),[^o7] where he was assistant professor of philosophy from 1955 to 1963. He was invited to [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) in 1964 as an associate professor of philosophy and was promoted to full professor in 1966.[^o4] From 1965 until his retirement, he held the [Harry Austryn Wolfson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Austryn_Wolfson) Chair of Philosophy; from 1993 until his death he was professor emeritus of Philosophy at Brandeis. He died aged 91 in 2014.[^o8]
