# Noether Lecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noether Lecture"}

The **Noether Lecture** is a distinguished lecture series that honors women "who have made fundamental and sustained contributions to the mathematical sciences". The [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) established the annual lectures in 1980 as the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lectures, in honor of one of the leading mathematicians of her time. In 2013 it was renamed the AWM-AMS Noether Lecture and since 2015 is sponsored jointly with the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS). The recipient delivers the lecture at the yearly American [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143) held in January.[^o2]

:::

The **ICM Emmy Noether Lecture** is an additional lecture series, sponsored by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868). Beginning in 1994 this lecture was delivered at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), held every four years. In 2010 the lecture series was made permanent.[^o3]

The 2021 Noether Lecture was supposed to have been given by [Andrea Bertozzi](wikipedia://en/37498758) of [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA), but it was cancelled. The cancellation was made during the [George Floyd protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Floyd_protests): "This decision comes as many of this nation rise up in protest over racial discrimination and brutality by police".[^o4] Although she intended to speak on other topics, Bertozzi is known for research on the mathematics of policing,[^o5] and in a letter to the AMS, [Sol Garfunkel](wikipedia://en/5233980) concluded that "the reason for her exclusion was one of her areas of research".[^o6] In an official blog of the AMS, a group calling themselves The Just Mathematics Collective called for a boycott of mathematical collaborations with police, dismissing Garfunkel's letter as "intended to further dismiss the boycott" and celebrating the cancellation of Bertozzi's lecture.[^o7]
