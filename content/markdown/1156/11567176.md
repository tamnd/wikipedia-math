# Linda Keen

*Image: Linda Keen.jpg.* Linda Keen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linda Keen"}

**Linda Jo Goldway Keen** (born August 9, 1940, in New York City, New York) is an American mathematician and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). Since 1965, she has been a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) and a Professor of Mathematics at [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York).

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

As a high school student she attended the [Bronx High School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx_High_School_of_Science). She received her Bachelor of Science degree from the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), then studied at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), earning her Doctor of Philosophy in mathematics in 1964. She wrote her thesis on [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) under the direction of [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

Keen has worked at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College), [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), and the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), as well as at various mathematical institutes in Europe and South America. After her initial appointment in 1965, in 1974 Keen was promoted to Full Professor at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) and the CUNY Graduate Center. She served as Executive Officer of the Mathematics Program at the Graduate Center before retiring in 2017.

Keen served as president of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) during 1985-1986 and as vice-president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) during 1992-1995. She served on the board of trustees of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) from 1999-2009 and as Associate Treasurer from 2009-2011. Keen worked with the mathematicians Paul Blanchard, [Robert L. Devaney](wikipedia://en/47958305), Jane Gilman, Lisa Goldberg, Yunping Jiang, Nikola Lakic and [Caroline Series](wikipedia://en/43764434) among many others.

In 1975, Keen presented an AMS invited address and in 1989 she presented an MAA joint invited address. In 1993 she was selected as a [Noether Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_Lecturer) by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o5]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In addition to studying [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181), Keen has worked in [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) and [Fuchsian groups](wikipedia://en/471365), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and [hyperbolic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_dynamics). In the field of hyperbolic geometry, she is known for the [Collar lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collar_lemma).

## Personal {#Personal}

She is married to Jonathan Brezin and resides in New York.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

She has been honored with:

- AAUW Postdoctoral Fellowship Award, 1964–65
- National Science Foundation Postdoctoral Fellow, 1964–65
- Edwin S. Webster-Abby Rockefeller Mauze Award, Massachusetts Institute of Technology 1990
- Finnish Mathematical Society Invited Foreign Speaker, January 1991
- [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Emmy [Noether Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_Lecturer), 1993
- Joint Irish and London Mathematical Societies Invited Speaker, 1998
- Lehman College Foundation Faculty Award, 1998
- MAA Invited hour Address, Boulder, Colorado, 1989
- Swedish Royal Academy of Sciences Kovalevsky Days Programme Main Speaker, 2006

In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

In 2017, she was selected as a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in the inaugural class.[^o10]
