# Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche"}

The **Mathematics Institute of Jussieu–Paris Rive Gauche** (Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu–Paris Rive Gauche, , **IMJ-PRG**) is a French research institute in [fundamental mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210). It is a "mixed research unit", with three parent organizations: the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University), and the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_%282019%29). It is located in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), split between two campuses: [Jussieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jussieu_Campus) and [Paris Rive Gauche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Rive_Gauche).

:::

It is the largest research center for fundamental mathematics in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France).[^o3] More than 200 permanent researchers work at the institute, around 100 PhD students, as well as [emeritus professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus), postdocs, invited researchers, and ATERs, and support staff.

The IMJ-PRG is the largest research unit linked to the [doctoral school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_school) of mathematical sciences of Paris center (*École doctorale de sciences mathématiques de Paris-Centre*). It has its own [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal), the *Journal de l'institut de mathématiques de Jussieu*, published by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press) and covering all areas of fundamental mathematics.[^o4]

Each year since 2001, the institute organizes an international [summer school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_school) dedicated to a hot topic in current mathematical research.

## History {#History}

The institute was created on January 1, 1994, under the name *Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu*. It moved in 1999 to the Chevaleret location in Paris. In 2010, half of the institute moved back to Jussieu; in 2013, the other half moved to Paris Rive Gauche and the institute changed its name to the current one.

The institute is one of the founding members of the [Research Federation in Mathematics of Paris Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Federation_in_Mathematics_of_Paris_Center) (*Fédération de recherche en mathématiques de Paris centre*). Since 2007, it has been affiliated with the [Mathematical Sciences Foundation of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Foundation_of_Paris) (*Fondation sciences mathématiques de Paris*).

The directors of the Institute were successively: Christian Peskine (1994-2000), Harold Rosenberg (2000-04), Gilles Godefroy (2004-08), [Håkan Eliasson](wikipedia://en/50020105) (2008-12), Patrice Le Calvez (2012-16), [Loïc Merel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loïc_Merel) (2016-21), Olivier Biquard (2021-25), David Hernandez (2026-).

### Distinctions {#Distinctions}

Several members of the IMJ-PRG received national and international awards for their research.[^o7] Most prominently, [Artur Avila](wikipedia://en/23579022) received the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 2014,[^o8], [Claire Voisin](wikipedia://en/5980475) received the [CNRS Gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_medal) in 2016,[^o9], and [Michel Talagrand](wikipedia://en/11091583) received the [Abel prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel_prize) in 2024[^o10].

## Themes {#Themes}

The research at the IMJ-PRG covers most of fundamental mathematics. It is subdivided in twelve team-projects: [algebraic analysis](wikipedia://en/20846707); [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/186101); [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180); [operator algebra](wikipedia://en/455987); [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization); [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146); [history of mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/14220); geometry and [dynamics](wikipedia://en/9087); [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), [representations](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/955164); [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636); [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527); and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [(algebraic) geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).
