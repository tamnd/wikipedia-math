# Kasia Rejzner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kasia Rejzner"}

*Image: Schenkel Benini Rejzner Schweigert 2016 MFO21430.jpg.* Alexander Schenkel, Marco Benini, Kasia Rejzner, and Christoph Schweigert at [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) for the 2016 mini-workshop *New Interactions between Homotopical Algebra and Quantum Field Theory*

**Katarzyna (Kasia) Anna Rejzner** (born 1985) is a Polish [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) specializing in [algebraic quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/346489) and the theory of [renormalization](wikipedia://en/291453), including the [Batalin–Vilkovisky formalism](wikipedia://en/672259). She works as a professor in mathematics at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rejzner was born in 1985 in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), the daughter of two architects. She earned a master's degree in physics in 2009 from [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University), and completed her Ph.D. in 2011 at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) under the supervision of [Klaus Fredenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Fredenhagen), with a dissertation on the [Batalin–Vilkovisky formalism](wikipedia://en/672259). After postdoctoral studies at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) she joined the University of York in 2013, and was promoted to senior lecturer there in 2017. In 2016 and 2017, she visited the [Perimeter Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute) as an [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Visiting Fellow. She was promoted to the position of full professor in Mathematics, University of York in 2024. During the term 2024-2026, Rejzner serves as the President of the [International Association of Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897340) (IAMP), being the first female in this position since the foundation of the IAMP.

## Book {#Book}

Rejzner is the author of the book *Perturbative Algebraic Quantum Field Theory: An Introduction for Mathematicians* (Mathematical Physics Studies, Springer, 2016).
