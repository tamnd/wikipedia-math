# Willard L. Miranker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Willard L. Miranker"}

**Willard L. Miranker** (March 8, 1932 – April 28, 2011) was an American mathematician and computer scientist known for his contributions to [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [numerical mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_mathematics).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Miranker was raised in Brooklyn, New York. He received a bachelor of arts in 1952, master of science in 1953 and Ph.D. in 1957 all in mathematics from the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). His doctoral thesis *The Asymptotic Theory of Solutions of U + (K<sup>2</sup>)U = 0* was advised by [Joseph Keller](wikipedia://en/4454126).

## Career {#Career}

### Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Upon graduating from New York University, he worked for the mathematics department at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs) (1956–1958) before joining [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research) (1961). Upon retirement from IBM, he joined the computer science faculty at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (1989) as research faculty.

He also held professor affiliations at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (1963), [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) (1966–1967), [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) (1968), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) (1970–1973), [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (1973) and [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) (1974).

Miranker's work[^o5] includes articles and books on [stiff differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stiff_differential_equation),[^o6] [interval arithmetic](wikipedia://en/2176160),[^o7] [analog computing](wikipedia://en/2428), and [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network) and the modeling of [consciousness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consciousness).

### Painting {#Painting}

Miranker was also an accomplished and prolific painter. Over the course of his life, Willard Miranker painted ~4000 watercolors/aquarelles and ~200 oil paintings. He exhibited internationally in New York City, Paris and [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn).[^o9]
