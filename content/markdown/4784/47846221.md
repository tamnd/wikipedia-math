# Zdeněk Dvořák

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zdeněk Dvořák"}

**Zdeněk Dvořák** (born April 26, 1981) is a Czech mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

Dvořák was born in [Nové Město na Moravě](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nové_Město_na_Moravě).[^o4] He competed on the Czech national team in the 1999 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208),[^o2] and in the same year in the [International Olympiad in Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Olympiad_in_Informatics), where he won a gold medal.[^o3] He earned his Ph.D. in 2007 from [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague), under the supervision of [Jaroslav Nešetřil](wikipedia://en/6948446). He remained as a research fellow at Charles University until 2010, and then did postdoctoral studies at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology) and [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University). He then returned to the Computer Science Institute (IUUK) of Charles University, obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2012, and has been a full professor there since 2022.[^o4]

He was one of three winners of the 2015 [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics), "for his fundamental contributions to graph theory, in particular for his work on structural aspects of graph theory, including solutions to Havel's 1969 problem and the Heckman–Thomas 14/5 problem on fractional colourings of cubic triangle-free graphs.[^o5] This refers to two different results of Dvořák:

- Havel's conjecture is a strengthening of [Grötzsch's theorem](wikipedia://en/24917736). It states that there exists a constant *d* such that, if a [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314) has no two triangles within distance *d* of each other, then it can be [colored](wikipedia://en/426743) with three colors. A proof of this conjecture of Havel was announced by Dvořák and his co-authors in 2009.[^o6]
- C. C. Heckman and [Robin Thomas](wikipedia://en/24641679) conjectured in 2001 that [triangle-free graphs](wikipedia://en/7667996) of maximum degree three have [fractional chromatic number](wikipedia://en/692369) at most 14/5.[^o7] A proof was announced by Dvořák and his co-authors in 2013 and published by them in 2014.[^o8]
