# Hungarian Actuarial Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hungarian Actuarial Society"}

The **Hungarian Actuarial Society** was founded in 1991 to represent [actuaries](wikipedia://en/43405) in [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary). , it has about 140 members, 80 of which are qualified (full) members. It is a full member of the [International Actuarial Association](wikipedia://en/228150) and the [Groupe Consultatif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groupe_Consultatif).
