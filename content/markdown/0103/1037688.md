# Proper linear model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Proper linear model"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **proper linear model** is a [linear regression](wikipedia://en/48758386) model in which the weights given to the predictor [variables](wikipedia://en/3728109) are chosen in such a way as to optimize the relationship between the prediction and the criterion. Simple [regression analysis](wikipedia://en/826997) is the most common example of a proper linear model. [Unit-weighted regression](wikipedia://en/3620926) is the most common example of an **improper linear model**.

:::
