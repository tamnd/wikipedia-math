# Peter Turner (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Turner"}

**Peter Turner** (1586–1652) was an [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He matriculated at [St Mary Hall, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mary_Hall,_Oxford) in 1600, and graduated M.A. at [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford) in 1605. He became a Fellow of [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford) in 1607.

He gave public lectures as professor of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) at [Gresham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gresham_College), London from 1620 to 1630. He was an ally of [William Laud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Laud) in his time as Chancellor of the University of Oxford, working with [Brian Twyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Twyne) on a revision of the statutes.[^o4]

He held the [Savilian Chair of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) from 1631 to 1649. In 1641 he enlisted in the royalist forces of [John Byron, 1st Baron Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Byron,_1st_Baron_Byron). Taken prisoner in an early skirmish of the [First English Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_English_Civil_War), near [Stow-on-the-Wold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stow-on-the-Wold), he was eventually released in 1643 in a prisoner exchange. He was ejected from his chair after the 1648 [Parliamentary visitation of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_visitation_of_Oxford), and lived in poverty with his sister in [Southwark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwark).[^o4]
