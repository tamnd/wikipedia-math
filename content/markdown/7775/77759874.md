# Valentino Tosatti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valentino Tosatti"}

**Valentino Tosatti** (born c. 1981) is an Italian mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born c. 1981, in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), Tosatti studied from 2000 at the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) and at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), graduating with a *[laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea)* in 2004. He then studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he graduated with an M.A. in 2005 and a Ph.D. in 2009.[^o3] His Ph.D. thesis *Geometry of complex Monge-Ampère equations* was supervised by [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137).[^o5] Tosatti was from 2009 to 2012 a Joseph Fels Ritt Assistant Professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). At [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), he was an associate professor from 2012 to 2015 and a full professor from 2015 to 2020. From 2020 to 2022, he taught as a professor at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University). In 2022 he became a professor at [NYU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)'s [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences).[^o3]

Tosatti does research on complex and differential geometry; geometric analysis on complex, Hermitian, and symplectic manifolds; and partial differential equations. He is also interested in the connections of his main research topics with algebraic geometry and dynamical systems.[^o3]

In 2017, with [Gábor Székelyhidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gábor_Székelyhidi) and Ben Weinkove, Valentino Tosatti proved a conjecture published in 1984 by [Paul Gauduchon](wikipedia://en/65568796).[^o6][^o7] *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)* published their proof that on n-dimensional [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) there is always a Gauduchon metric with prescribed [volume form](wikipedia://en/1855811). For this purpose, the behavior of a large class of [elliptic nonlinear partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_differential_equation) of second order on [Hermitian manifolds](wikipedia://en/2640459) had to be investigated.[^o6]

In 2011, Tosatti received a [Blavatnik Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blavatnik_Awards_for_Young_Scientists).[^o8] In 2012, he was awarded a two-year Sloan Research Fellowship.[^o3] In 2018, he received the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352). In 2019, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for "contributions in geometric analysis and complex geometry".[^o9]
