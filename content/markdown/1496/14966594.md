# Burke

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Burke"}

**Burke** ([de Búrca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Búrca); de Burgo) is a [Norman-Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normans_in_Ireland) surname, deriving from the ancient Anglo-Norman and Hiberno-Norman noble dynasty, the [House of Burgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Burgh). In Ireland, the descendants of [William de Burgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_de_Burgh) (*circa* 1160–1206) had the surname*[de Burgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Burgh)*, which was [gaelicised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelicisation) in [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language) as *[de Búrca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Búrca)* and over the centuries became *Búrc*, then Burke, and [Bourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourke_%28surname%29).[^o3]
