# Iwasawa manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Iwasawa manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), an **Iwasawa manifold** is a compact quotient of a 3-dimensional complex [Heisenberg group](wikipedia://en/448518) by a [cocompact](wikipedia://en/5358554), [discrete](wikipedia://en/468536) subgroup. An Iwasawa manifold is a [nilmanifold](wikipedia://en/3668693), of real dimension 6.

:::

Iwasawa manifolds give examples where the first two terms *E*<sub>1</sub> and *E*<sub>2</sub> of the [Frölicher spectral sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frölicher_spectral_sequence) are not isomorphic.

As a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742), such an Iwasawa manifold is an important example of a compact complex manifold which does not admit any [Kähler metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kähler_metric).
