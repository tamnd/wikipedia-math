# Walter Noll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Noll"}

**Walter Noll** (January 7, 1925 – June 6, 2017) was a German mathematician, and Professor Emeritus at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University). He is best known for developing mathematical tools of [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586), [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952), and [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Weimar Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weimar_Germany), Noll had his school education in a suburb of Berlin. In 1954, Noll earned a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 1954 under [Clifford Truesdell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Truesdell).[^o5]

His thesis "On the Continuity of the Solid and Fluid States" was published both in *[Journal of Rational Mechanics and Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Rational_Mechanics_and_Analysis)* and in one of Truesdell's books.[^o6] Noll thanks [Jerald Ericksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerald_Ericksen) for his critical input to the thesis.

Noll has served as a visiting professor at the [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), the [University of Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Karlsruhe), the [Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology), the [Institut National Polytechnique de Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_National_Polytechnique_de_Lorraine) in [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France), the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia), and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o5]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] Noll died on June 6, 2017, at the age of 92.[^o8]

::: {#o10 .statement cue=named-heading name="Principle of material objectivity"}

## Principle of material objectivity {#Principle_of_material_objectivity}

In [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918), Noll introduced the so-called **principle of material objectivity**,[^o11] which states that the constitutive laws governing the internal conditions of a physical system and the interactions between its parts should not depend on whatever external [frame of reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frame_of_reference) is used to describe them.

"Principle of material objectivity" is now an obsolete term that has been replaced by **principle of material frame-indifference**.[^o12]

:::
