# Spectral network

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spectral network"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [supersymmetric gauge theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetric_gauge_theory), **spectral networks** are "networks of [trajectories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trajectory) on [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) obeying certain local rules. Spectral networks arise naturally in [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) *N* = 2 theories coupled to surface defects, particularly the theories of class S."[^o2][^o3]

:::
