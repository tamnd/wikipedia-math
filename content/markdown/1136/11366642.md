# Vikraman Balaji

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vikraman Balaji"}

**Vikraman Balaji** is an [Indian mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_mathematician) and is currently a professor at [Chennai Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/3124675). He completed his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) under the supervision of [C. S. Seshadri](wikipedia://en/5633765). His primary area of research is in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). Balaji was awarded the 2006 [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Award) in Mathematical Sciences along with [Indranil Biswas](wikipedia://en/37647470) "for his outstanding contributions to moduli problems of [principal bundles](wikipedia://en/310950) over [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808), in particular on the Uhlenbeck-Yau compactification of the Moduli Spaces of μ-semistable bundles." He was elected Fellow of the Indian Academy of Sciences in 2007, Fellow of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) in 2015[^o2] and was awarded the J.C. Bose National Fellowship in 2009.[^o3]

:::
