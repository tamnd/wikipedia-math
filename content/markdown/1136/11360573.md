# The Fibonacci Association

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Fibonacci Association"}

The **Fibonacci Association** is a mathematical organization that specializes in the [Fibonacci number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_number) sequence and related topics in mathematics.[^o2]

:::

## History {#History}

The organization was founded in 1963 by Brother [Alfred Brousseau](wikipedia://en/11633991) FSC of [Saint Mary's College of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Mary's_College_of_California) and [Verner E. Hoggatt Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verner_Emil_Hoggatt._Jr) of San Jose State College (now San Jose State University), together with Stanley L. Basin, Terrance A. Brennan, Paul F. Byrd, and I. Dale Ruggles.[^o4]

## Publications {#Publications}

Since the year of its founding, the Fibonacci Association has published an international [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal), The *[Fibonacci Quarterly](wikipedia://en/12227896)*.[^o2][^o4]

The Fibonacci Association also publishes proceedings for [its international conferences](wikipedia://en/78753906), held every two years since 1984.[^o2]
