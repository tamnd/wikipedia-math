# Karl Heun

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Heun"}

*Image: Karl Heun.jpg.* thumd

**Karl Heun** (; 3 April 1859, [Wiesbaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiesbaden) – 10 January 1929, [Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Heun's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heun's_equation), [Heun functions](wikipedia://en/2070045), and [Heun's method](wikipedia://en/11754125).

:::

Karl Heun studied mathematics and philosophy at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and briefly at the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle)). In 1881, with the dissertation *Die Kugelfunktionen und Laméschen Funktionen als Determinanten* he received his doctorate under [Schering](wikipedia://en/23621209) at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He then worked as a teacher at an agricultural college in [Wehlau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehlau), until in 1883 he immigrated to England where he taught until 1885 in [Uppingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppingham).

He completed his studies in London and received his Habilitierung qualification in June 1886 at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) with the thesis *Über lineare Differentialgleichungen zweiter Ordnung, deren Lösungen durch den Kettenbruchalgorithmus verknüpft sind*. From 1886 to 1889, he taught at the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, but due to financial circumstances from 1890 to 1902, he had to work as a teacher in Berlin.

In 1900, Karl Heun received the title of Professor and then in 1902 he obtained the professorial chair of [theoretical mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_mechanics) at the [Technische Hochschule Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule_Karlsruhe), where he worked until he retired with a pension in 1922.
