# Gert Sabidussi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gert Sabidussi"}

**Gert Sabidussi** (28 October 1929 – 1 April 2022) was an Austrian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sabidussi was born in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), Austria, on 28 October 1929. His family later moved to [Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innsbruck), where his father was a Protestant [deacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deacon). He graduated from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), where he attended lectures by [Felix Ehrenhaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Ehrenhaft), [Nikolaus Hofreiter](wikipedia://en/22209837), [Johann Radon](wikipedia://en/339331) and [Hans Thirring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Thirring). In 1953, he defended his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) on [0–1 matrices](wikipedia://en/4109196) under the supervision of [Edmund Hlawka](wikipedia://en/5442007) and received a two-year fellowship at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He was then an instructor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis), but because of the heavy teaching load moved a year later, in 1956, to [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans). He moved to [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University) in [Hamilton, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_Ontario), in 1960 and afterwards to the [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal) in 1969. He was instrumental in bringing to Canada a number of combinatorialists and graph theorists, including [Anton Kotzig](wikipedia://en/25648858) and [Jaroslav Nešetřil](wikipedia://en/6948446), who wrote a thesis under Sabidussi. Over the years, he had 13 graduate students. His 60th, 70th and 80th birthdays were celebrated with large graph theory birthday conferences. Sabidussi died on 1 April 2022, at the age of 92.[^o3]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

Sabidussi wrote foundational work on [Cayley graphs](wikipedia://en/358277), [graph products](wikipedia://en/11492935) and [Frucht's theorem](wikipedia://en/26259599).
