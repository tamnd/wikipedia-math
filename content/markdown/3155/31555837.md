# Dichromatic reflectance model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dichromatic reflectance model"}

In Shafer's **dichromatic reflection model**,[^o2] scene [radiance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiance) has two components:

:::

$$L(\lambda) = m_\mathrm{b} c_\mathrm{b}(\lambda) + m_\mathrm{s}c_\mathrm{s}(\lambda)$$

*λ* is the [wavelength](wikipedia://en/33125),

*c*<sub>b</sub> is the body ([diffuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffuse_reflection)) reflected component,

*c*<sub>s</sub> is the surface (interface) ([specular](wikipedia://en/679297)) reflected component,

*m*<sub>b</sub> and *m*<sub>s</sub> are [scale factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scale_factor) depending on [illumination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illumination_%28lighting%29), view directions and surface orientation.

## Separation algorithm {#Separation_algorithm}

### BREN (body reflection essence-neuter) model based {#BREN_(body_reflection_essence-neuter)_model_based}

Body essence is an entity invariant to interface reflection, and has two degrees of freedom. The Gaussian coefficient generalizes a conventional simple thresholding scheme, and it provides detailed use of body color similarity.[^o5]
