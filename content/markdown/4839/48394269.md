# Henri Skoda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Skoda"}

**Henri Skoda** (born 1945) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in the analysis of [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables).[^o2]

:::

Skoda studied from 1964 at l'[École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) and received there in 1967 his [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics. He received in 1972 his Ph.D. from the [University of Nice Sophia Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nice_Sophia_Antipolis) under [André Martineau](wikipedia://en/50811617) (primary advisor) and [Pierre Lelong](wikipedia://en/34789789) (secondary advisor) with thesis *Étude quantitative des sous-ensembles analytiques de C<sup>n</sup> et des idéaux de functions holomorphes*.[^o3] Skoda became a professor in Toulon and since 1976 has been a professor at the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI).

For many years he ran an analysis seminar with Pierre Lelong and [Pierre Dolbeault](wikipedia://en/31593159).

In 1978 Skoda received the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828) and as an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) gave a talk *Integral methods and zeros of holomorphic functions*.[^o4]

His doctoral students include [Jean-Pierre Demailly](wikipedia://en/53350424).
