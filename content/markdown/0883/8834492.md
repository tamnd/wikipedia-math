# Triheptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Triheptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **triheptagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane, representing a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [Order-3 heptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order-3_heptagonal_tiling). There are two [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and two [heptagons](wikipedia://en/365349) alternating on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of r{7,3}.

:::

Compare to [trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864397) with [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) *\3.6.3.6*.

## 7-3 Rhombille {#7-3_Rhombille}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **7-3 rhombille tiling** is a [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of identical [rhombi](wikipedia://en/183884) on the [hyperbolic plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_plane). Sets of three and seven rhombi meet two classes of vertices.
