# Heptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **heptagonal tiling** is a [regular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hyperbolic_tiling) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {7,3}, having three regular [heptagons](wikipedia://en/365349) around each vertex.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,3}.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.

## Hurwitz surfaces {#Hurwitz_surfaces}

*Image: 3-7 kisrhombille.svg.* The symmetry group of the heptagonal tiling has fundamental domain the (2,3,7) [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325), which yields this tiling.

The symmetry group of the tiling is the [(2,3,7) triangle group](wikipedia://en/12659803), and a [fundamental domain](wikipedia://en/469365) for this action is the (2,3,7) [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325). This is the smallest hyperbolic Schwarz triangle, and thus, by the proof of [Hurwitz's automorphisms theorem](wikipedia://en/1113637), the tiling is the universal tiling that covers all [Hurwitz surfaces](wikipedia://en/12807841) (the [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) with maximal symmetry group), giving them a tiling by heptagons whose symmetry group equals their automorphism group as Riemann surfaces. The smallest Hurwitz surface is the [Klein quartic](wikipedia://en/390976) (genus 3, automorphism group of order 168), and the induced tiling has 24 heptagons, meeting at 56 vertices.

The dual [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365) has the same symmetry group, and thus yields [triangulations](wikipedia://en/679384) of Hurwitz surfaces.
