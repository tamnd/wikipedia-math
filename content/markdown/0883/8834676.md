# Truncated triheptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated triheptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated triheptagonal tiling** is a semiregular [tiling](wikipedia://en/321671) of the [hyperbolic plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_plane). There is one [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), one [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733), and one [tetradecagon](wikipedia://en/6058229) (14-sides) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of $\operatorname{tr}\{7,3\}.$

:::

## Uniform colorings {#Uniform_colorings}

There is only one [uniform coloring](wikipedia://en/18624814) of a truncated triheptagonal tiling. (Naming the colors by indices around a vertex: 123.)

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

Each [triangle in this dual tiling, order 3-7 kisrhombille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_3-7_kisrhombille#Symmetry), represent a fundamental domain of the [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) for the symmetry group \[7,3\].

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

This tiling can be considered a member of a sequence of uniform patterns with vertex figure (4.6.2p) and [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) . For *p* \< 6, the members of the sequence are [omnitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncation_%28geometry%29) polyhedra ([zonohedrons](wikipedia://en/669402)), shown below as spherical tilings. For *p* > 6, they are tilings of the hyperbolic plane, starting with the truncated triheptagonal tiling.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.
