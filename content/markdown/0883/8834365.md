# Order-7 triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-7 triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-7 triangular tiling** is a [regular tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hyperbolic_tiling) of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) with a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {3,7}.

*Image: H3\_337\_UHS\_plane\_at\_infinity.png.* The [{3,3,7}](wikipedia://en/41477358) honeycomb has {3,7} vertex figures.

:::

## Hurwitz surfaces {#Hurwitz_surfaces}

The symmetry group of the tiling is the [(2,3,7) triangle group](wikipedia://en/12659803), and a fundamental domain for this action is the (2,3,7) [Schwarz triangle](wikipedia://en/2069325). This is the smallest hyperbolic Schwarz triangle, and thus, by the proof of [Hurwitz's automorphisms theorem](wikipedia://en/1113637), the tiling is the universal tiling that covers all [Hurwitz surfaces](wikipedia://en/12807841) (the [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) with maximal symmetry group), giving them a triangulation whose symmetry group equals their automorphism group as Riemann surfaces.

The smallest of these is the [Klein quartic](wikipedia://en/390976), the most symmetric genus 3 surface, together with a tiling by 56 triangles, meeting at 24 vertices, with symmetry group the simple group of order 168, known as [PSL(2,7)](wikipedia://en/390426). The resulting surface can in turn be polyhedrally [immersed](wikipedia://en/6993953) into Euclidean 3-space, yielding the [small cubicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/4157031).[^o6]

The dual [order-3 heptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order-3_heptagonal_tiling) has the same symmetry group, and thus yields heptagonal tilings of Hurwitz surfaces.

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

It is related to two star-tilings by the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644): the [order-7 heptagrammic tiling](wikipedia://en/42012723), {7/2,7}, and [heptagrammic-order heptagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/42013674), {7,7/2}.

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,p}.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.
