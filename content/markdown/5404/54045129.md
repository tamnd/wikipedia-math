# Ratnasingham Shivaji

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ratnasingham Shivaji"}

**Ratnasingham Shivaji** is an American mathematician, focusing on mathematics and mathematical ecology, currently the H. Barton Excellence Professor[^o2][^o3] at [University of North Carolina at Greensboro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Greensboro) and formerly a W. L. Giles Distinguished Professor at [Mississippi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_State_University).[^o4]

:::

Shivaji completed his undergraduate studies, earning a Special Degree in Mathematics from the [University of Peradeniya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Peradeniya), Sri Lanka, in 1977 with a first-class honors. He obtained his PhD in Mathematics from Heriot- Watt University in Edinburgh, Scotland, in 1981.

He was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to the theory of semipositone elliptic questions applied to reaction-diffusion systems, for mentoring, and for providing leadership for the inception of a doctoral program in mathematics".[^o5]

He received the 2024 Conference of Southern Graduate Schools Outstanding Mentor Award[^o6] and the 2020 Mathematical Association of America Southeastern Section Distinguished Teaching Award[^o7].
