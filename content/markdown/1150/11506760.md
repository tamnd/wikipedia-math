# Journal of Philosophical Logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Philosophical Logic"}

The ***Journal of Philosophical Logic*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) covering all aspects of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065). It was established in 1972 and is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Rosalie Iemhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalie_Iemhoff) ([Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University)), [Reinhard Muskens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinhard_Muskens) ([University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam)), and [Kai Wehmeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Wehmeier) ([University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine)).

:::
