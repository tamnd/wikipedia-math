# Combinatorial commutative algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Combinatorial commutative algebra"}

**Combinatorial commutative algebra** is a relatively new, rapidly developing [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) discipline. As the name implies, it lies at the intersection of two more established fields, [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and frequently uses methods of one to address problems arising in the other. Less obviously, [polyhedral geometry](wikipedia://en/1817885) plays a significant role.

:::

One of the milestones in the development of the subject was [Richard Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397)'s 1975 [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of the **[Upper Bound Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Bound_Conjecture)** for [simplicial spheres](wikipedia://en/22359636), which was based on earlier work of [Melvin Hochster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Hochster) and Gerald Reisner. While the problem can be formulated purely in geometric terms, the methods of the proof drew on commutative algebra techniques.

A signature [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) in combinatorial commutative algebra is the characterization of [*h*-vectors](wikipedia://en/11503563) of [simplicial polytopes](wikipedia://en/8853373) [conjectured](wikipedia://en/6138) in 1970 by [Peter McMullen](wikipedia://en/40964822). Known as the [*g*-theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-theorem), it was proved in 1979 by Stanley ([necessity](wikipedia://en/169319) of the conditions, algebraic argument) and by [Louis Billera](wikipedia://en/22444348) and Carl W. Lee ([sufficiency](wikipedia://en/169319), combinatorial and geometric construction). A major [open question](wikipedia://en/1333335) was the extension of this characterization from simplicial polytopes to simplicial spheres, the [*g*-conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-conjecture), which was resolved in 2018 by [Karim Adiprasito](wikipedia://en/47874333).

## Important notions of combinatorial commutative algebra {#Important_notions_of_combinatorial_commutative_algebra}

- Square-free **[monomial ideal](wikipedia://en/43023424)** in a [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065) and [Stanley–Reisner ring](wikipedia://en/24394535) of a [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686).
- [Cohen–Macaulay rings](wikipedia://en/497407).
- **[Monomial ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monomial_ring)**, closely related to an **affine semigroup ring** and to the [coordinate ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_ring) of an [affine](wikipedia://en/320468) [toric variety](wikipedia://en/737087).
- **Algebra with a straightening law**. There are several versions of those, including **[Hodge algebras](wikipedia://en/38348416)** of [Corrado de Concini](wikipedia://en/35219521), [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845), and [Claudio Procesi](wikipedia://en/35219117).
