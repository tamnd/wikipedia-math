# International rankings of Uruguay

The following are [international rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_rankings) of Uruguay.

## Demographics {#Demographics}

- [Population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_population_%28United_Nations%29) ranked 134 out 233 nations

## Economy {#Economy}

- [Nominal GDP](wikipedia://en/380845) ranked 80 out of 216 economies
- [GDP per capita](wikipedia://en/695403) – 60th highest, at [I\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_dollar)11,969
- [Income Equality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_income_equality), 0.449 ([Gini Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gini_Index))
- [Unemployment rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_unemployment_rate) – 112th, at 8.70%

## Education {#Education}

- [Literacy Rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_literacy_rate) – 51st, at 97.7%

## Energy {#Energy}

- [Total electricity consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_electricity_consumption) ranked 88th highest consumption

## Environment {#Environment}

- [Carbon dioxide emissions per capita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_carbon_dioxide_emissions_per_capita) ranked 125th highest emissions

## Geography {#Geography}

## Health {#Health}

- [Fertility rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_territories_by_fertility_rate)- 140th most fertile, at 1.85 per woman
- [Birth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_birth_rate) – 157th most births, at 13.91 per 1000 people
- [Infant mortality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_infant_mortality_rate_%282005%29) – 128th most deaths, at 1 per 1000 live births
- [Death rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_death_rate) – 84th highest death rate, at 9.16 per 1000 people
- [Life Expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_life_expectancy) – 47th highest, at 76.4 years
- [Suicide Rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_suicide_rate) – 24th highest suicide rate, at 15.1 for males and 6.4 for females per 100,000 people
- [HIV/AIDS rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_HIV/AIDS_adult_prevalence_rate) – 108th most cases, at 0.30%
