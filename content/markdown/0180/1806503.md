# Canadian Society for History and Philosophy of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Canadian Society for History and Philosophy of Mathematics"}

The **Canadian Society for History and Philosophy of Mathematics** (**CSHPM**) is dedicated to the study of the [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).[^o2] It was proposed by [Kenneth O. May](wikipedia://en/719577), in conjunction with the journal *[Historia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/10841658)*,[^o3] and was founded in 1974.[^o4]

:::
