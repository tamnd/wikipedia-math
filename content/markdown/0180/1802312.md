# Paul Vojta

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Vojta"}

**Paul Alan Vojta** (born September 30, 1957) is an American mathematician, known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) on [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205) and [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In formulating [Vojta's conjecture](wikipedia://en/34682455), he pointed out the possible existence of parallels between the [Nevanlinna theory](wikipedia://en/393135) of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), and [diophantine analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantine_analysis) in the circle of ideas around the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture) and [abc conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108). This suggested the importance of the *integer solutions* ([affine space](wikipedia://en/298834)) aspect of [diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109).

Vojta wrote the [.dvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVI_%28file_format%29)-previewer [xdvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xdvi). He also wrote a [vi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vi_%28text_editor%29) clone.[^o4][^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He was an undergraduate student at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), where he became a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) in 1977,[^o7] and a doctoral student at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1983). He currently is a professor in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
