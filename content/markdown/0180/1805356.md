# Sierpiński's constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sierpiński's constant"}

**Sierpiński's constant** is a [mathematical constant](wikipedia://en/48779551) usually denoted as ***K***. One way of defining it is as the following limit:

:::

$$K=\lim_{n \to \infty}\left[\sum_{k=1}^{n}{r_2(k)\over k} - \pi\ln n\right]$$

where *r*<sub>2</sub>(*k*) is a [number of representations](wikipedia://en/49824012) of *k* as a sum of the form *a*<sup>2</sup> + *b*<sup>2</sup> for [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) *a* and *b*.

It can be given in closed form as:

$$\begin{aligned} K &= \pi \left(2 \ln 2+3 \ln \pi + 2 \gamma - 4 \ln \Gamma \left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)\right)\\ &=\pi \ln\left(\frac{4\pi^3 e^{2\gamma}}{\Gamma \left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)^4}\right)\\ &=\pi \ln\left(\frac{\pi^2e^{2\gamma}}{2\varpi^2}\right)\\ &= 2.58498 17595 79253 21706 58935 87383\dots \end{aligned}$$

where $\varpi$ is the [lemniscate constant](wikipedia://en/2079065) and $\gamma$ is the [Euler-Mascheroni constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler-Mascheroni_constant).

Another way to define/understand Sierpiński's constant is,

*Image: Sierpinski constant.gif.* Graph of the given equation where the straight line represents Sierpiński's constant

Let r(n)[^o2] denote the number of representations of $n$ by $k$ squares, then the Summatory Function[^o3] of $r_2(k)/k$ has the Asymptotic[^o4] expansion

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n}{r_2(k)\over k}=K+\pi\ln n+o\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt n}\right),$$

where $K=2.5849817596$ is the Sierpiński constant. The above plot shows

$$\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n}{r_2(k)\over k}\right)-\pi\ln n,$$

with the value of $K$ indicated as the solid horizontal line.
