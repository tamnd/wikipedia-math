# Karine Beauchard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karine Beauchard"}

**Karine Beauchard** (born 27 November 1978) is a French mathematician known for her research in [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039). She is a University Professor at the [École normale supérieure de Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Rennes), and was the [Peccot Lecturer](wikipedia://en/60897406) of the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France) for 2007–2008.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

From 1999 to 2003, Beauchard studied at the [École normale supérieure Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_Paris-Saclay). She earned her *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* in 2002 and a [Master of Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Advanced_Studies) in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) in 2003 through [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University). She completed a doctorate in 2005 at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud); her dissertation, *Contribution à l'étude de la contrôlabilité et la stabilisation de l'équation de Schrödinger*, was directed by [Jean-Michel Coron](wikipedia://en/25110499). She earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2010 at Cachan, with a habilitation thesis on *Analyse et contrôle de quelques équations aux dérivées partielles*.

She worked at Cachan from 2005 to 2006, and as a chargée de recherche at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) from 2006 until 2014, when she took her present position as a professor at Rennes.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Beauchard was the [Peccot Lecturer](wikipedia://en/60897406) of the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France) for 2007–2008, giving a course on the control of [Schrödinger equations](wikipedia://en/59874). In 2017 she won the Michel-Monpetit Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences). She became a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) in 2018.
