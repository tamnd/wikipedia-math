# Lamberto Cesari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lamberto Cesari"}

**Lamberto Cesari** (23 September 1910 – 12 March 1990) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [naturalized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naturalized) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), known for his work on the [theory of surface area](wikipedia://en/1209), the [theory of functions of bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673), the [theory of optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) and on the [stability theory of dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/5493795): in particular, by extending the concept of [Tonelli plane variation](wikipedia://en/683561#Tonelli_plane_variation), he succeeded in introducing the class of functions of bounded variation of [several variables](wikipedia://en/3728109) in its full generality.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1933, he was awarded his *[laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea)* degree at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa) under the direction of [Leonida Tonelli](wikipedia://en/10018991). After a period of study from 1934 to 1935 in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) at [Monaco di Baviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco_di_Baviera) under the direction of [Constantin Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625), he went back to [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa) at the Scuola Normale Superiore for a year, and then to [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) at the [Istituto Nazionale per le Applicazioni del Calcolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Nazionale_per_le_Applicazioni_del_Calcolo), at the time directed by [Mauro Picone](wikipedia://en/10162009).

From 1938 to 1946 he went back as a *professore incaricato* at [Pisa University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa_University): in 1947 he was at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

In 1948 he went to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) as a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_professor) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in [Lafayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Indiana), at the [University of California - Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_-_Berkeley) and at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).

In 1960 he was appointed as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) at [Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor) where he remained until his retirement in 1981. In 1976 he became a [citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizen) of the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), while keeping close scientific contacts with the Italian mathematical community.

### The Lamberto Cesari chair {#The_Lamberto_Cesari_chair}

The department of Mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) honoured the memory of Lamberto Cesari with the creation of a professorship chair.

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

He is remembered for his achievements on the [Plateau's problem](wikipedia://en/338129), on the theory of parametric [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734), on [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) of [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) and related other [variational problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_variation): he also worked in the field of [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) and studied periodic solutions of systems of nonlinear [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) by using methods of nonlinear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). In the paper (Cesari 1936) he introduced a generalization of functions of [bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673) to the multi-dimensional setting, now acknowledged as the most versatile of such generalizations. He wrote about 250 [scientific works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_work) on topics such as non linear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565): his published works include the fundamental [monographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph) (Cesari 1956), (Cesari 1971) and (Cesari 1983).
