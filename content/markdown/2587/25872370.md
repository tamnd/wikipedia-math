# Export of cryptography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Export of cryptography"}

article The **export of cryptography** is the transfer from one country to another of devices and technology related to [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).

:::

In the early days of the [Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War), the United States and its allies developed an elaborate series of export control regulations designed to prevent a wide range of Western technology from falling into the hands of others, particularly the [Eastern bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_bloc). All export of technology classed as 'critical' required a license. [CoCom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoCom) was organized to coordinate Western export controls.

Many countries, notably those participating in the [Wassenaar Arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wassenaar_Arrangement), introduced restrictions. The Wassenaar restrictions were largely loosened in the late 2010s.[^o2][^o3]
