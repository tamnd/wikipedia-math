# Håkan Eliasson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Håkan Eliasson"}

*Image: Eliasson\_hakan.jpg.* Eliasson at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2011

**Lars Håkan Eliasson** (born 13 July 1952)[^o2] is a Swedish mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Eliasson received in 1984 his PhD from the [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) under [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316) with thesis *Hamiltonian systems with Poisson commuting integrals*. He was a professor at the [Royal Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Technology) in Stockholm and then became a professor at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII) (Denis Diderot) and the Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu of the Universities Paris VI and VII and the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS).

His research deals with dynamical systems, quasiperiodic motion, the problem of small denominators in perturbation theory, the [KAM Theory](wikipedia://en/16972#KAM_Theory) and multiscale analysis in perturbation theory, Hamiltonian partial differential equations, and localization and diffusion in quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators.

In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o5] In 2005 and in 2012 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o6]

In 1990 he received the Wallenberg Prize from the [Swedish Mathematical Society](https://www-history.mcs.st-andrews.ac.uk/Honours/Wallenberg_Prize.html), 1995 he received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize), 2007 he received the Eva & Lars Gårding Prize in Mathematics and 2008 he received the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384).

He was at the editorial committee of [Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708) 2012–2017.
