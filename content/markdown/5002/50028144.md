# Oleg Besov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oleg Besov"}

**Oleg Vladimirovich Besov** (Олег Владимирович Бесов; born 1933)[^o11] is a Russian mathematician. He heads the Department of Function Theory at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494), where he defended his PhD in 1960 and [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1966.[^o11] He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1970 in Nice.[^o3] He is professor at the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) and a member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (since 1990) and European Academy of Sciences (since 2002).[^o9][^o10] A festschrift was published in honor of Besov's 70th birthday.[^o4]

:::
