# Tannenbaum (surname)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tannenbaum"}

**Tannenbaum**, **Tanenbaum**, **Tenenbaum**, or **Tennenbaum** (טננבאום, טננבוים) is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Emmanuel David Tannenbaum](wikipedia://en/38009207) (1978–2012), Israeli/American biophysicist
- [Frank Tannenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Tannenbaum) (1893–1969), American sociologist
- [Judith Tannenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_Tannenbaum) (born 1947), American teaching artist and writer
