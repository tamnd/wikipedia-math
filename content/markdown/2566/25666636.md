# Quantum affine algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum affine algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quantum affine algebra** (or **affine quantum group**) is a [Hopf algebra](wikipedia://en/325714) that is a *q*-deformation of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of an [affine Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1350865). They were introduced independently by (Drinfeld 1985) and (Jimbo 1985) as a special case of their general construction of a [quantum group](wikipedia://en/340678) from a [Cartan matrix](wikipedia://en/1146433). One of their principal applications has been to the theory of [solvable lattice models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exactly_solvable_model#Exactly_solvable_models) in [quantum statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/712450), where the [Yang–Baxter equation](wikipedia://en/3720810) occurs with a [spectral parameter](wikipedia://en/506713). Combinatorial aspects of the representation theory of quantum affine algebras can be described simply using [crystal bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_basis), which correspond to the degenerate case when the deformation parameter *q* vanishes and the [Hamiltonian](wikipedia://en/14381) of the associated lattice model can be explicitly diagonalized.

:::
