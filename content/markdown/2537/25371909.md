# Ben Andrews (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ben Andrews"}

**Ben Andrews** is an Australian mathematician at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University).[^o2] He is known for contributions to [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), with a majority of his work being in the field of [extrinsic geometric flows](wikipedia://en/14617515). He received his Ph.D. from Australian National University in 1993, under the supervision of [Gerhard Huisken](wikipedia://en/39508202).[^o3] As of 2020, he has had nine Ph.D. students.

:::

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2002, he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). In 2003, he received the [Australian Mathematical Society Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Mathematical_Society_Medal), along with Andrew Hassell, for distinguished research in the mathematical sciences.[^o5] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
