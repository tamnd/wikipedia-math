# Quantale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantale"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **quantales** are certain [partially ordered](wikipedia://en/23572) [algebraic structures](wikipedia://en/106364) that generalize [locales](wikipedia://en/650751#Frames_and_locales) ([point free topologies](wikipedia://en/24764)) as well as various multiplicative [lattices](wikipedia://en/469304) of [ideals](wikipedia://en/25977) from [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) ([C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-star_algebra), [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185)).[^o2] Quantales are sometimes referred to as *complete [residuated semigroups](wikipedia://en/2483542#residuated_semilattice)*.

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

A **quantale** is a [complete lattice](wikipedia://en/44777) $Q$ with an [associative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associative) [binary operation](wikipedia://en/3948) $\ast\colon Q \times Q \to Q$, called its **multiplication**, satisfying a distributive property such that

$$x*\left(\bigvee_{i\in I}{y_i}\right) = \bigvee_{i\in I}(x*y_i)$$

and

$$\left(\bigvee_{i\in I}{y_i}\right)*{x}=\bigvee_{i\in I}(y_i*x)$$

for all $x, y_i \in Q$ and $i \in I$ (here $I$ is any [index set](wikipedia://en/393055)). The quantale is **unital** if it has an [identity element](wikipedia://en/14962) $e$ for its multiplication:

$$x*e = x = e*x$$

for all $x \in Q$. In this case, the quantale is naturally a [monoid](wikipedia://en/19652) with respect to its multiplication $\ast$.

A unital quantale may be defined equivalently as a [monoid](wikipedia://en/3955446) in the category **[Sup](wikipedia://en/44777#Morphisms_of_complete_lattices)** of complete [join-semilattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join-semilattice).

A unital quantale is an idempotent [semiring](wikipedia://en/371468) under join and multiplication.

A unital quantale in which the identity is the [top element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greatest_element) of the underlying lattice is said to be **strictly two-sided** (or simply *integral*).

A **commutative quantale** is a quantale whose multiplication is [commutative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative). A [frame](wikipedia://en/650751), with its multiplication given by the [meet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meet_%28mathematics%29) operation, is a typical example of a strictly two-sided commutative quantale. Another simple example is provided by the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324) together with its usual [multiplication](wikipedia://en/20845).

An **idempotent quantale** is a quantale whose multiplication is [idempotent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent). A [frame](wikipedia://en/650751) is the same as an idempotent strictly two-sided quantale.

An **involutive quantale** is a quantale with an involution

$$(xy)^\circ = y^\circ x^\circ$$

that preserves joins:

$$\biggl(\bigvee_{i\in I}{x_i}\biggr)^\circ =\bigvee_{i\in I}(x_i^\circ).$$

A **quantale [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564)** is a [map](wikipedia://en/516931) $f\colon Q_1 \to Q_2$ that preserves joins and multiplication for all $x, y, x_i \in Q_1$ and $i \in I$:

$$f(xy) = f(x) f(y),$$

$$f\left(\bigvee_{i \in I}{x_i}\right) = \bigvee_{i \in I} f(x_i).$$
