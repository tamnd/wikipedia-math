# Wolf Prize in Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolf Prize in Mathematics"}

The **Wolf Prize in Mathematics** is awarded almost annually by the [Wolf Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Foundation) in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel). It is one of the six [Wolf Prizes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize) established by the foundation and awarded since 1978; the others are in [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Agriculture), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Chemistry), [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Medicine), [physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Physics) and [arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Arts). The Wolf Prize includes a monetary award of \$100,000.[^o2]

:::

According to a reputation survey conducted in 2013 and 2014, the Wolf Prize in Mathematics is the third most prestigious international academic award in mathematics, after the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) and the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859).[^o3][^o4]
