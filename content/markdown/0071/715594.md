# Samuel L. Greitzer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel L. Greitzer"}

*Image: Greitzer-Sam.jpg.* Samuel Greitzer

**Samuel L. Greitzer** (August 10, 1905 – February 22, 1988) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), the founding chairman of the [United States of America Mathematical Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America_Mathematical_Olympiad), and the publisher of the precollege mathematics journal *Arbelos*.[^o5] Together with [H.S.M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328) in 1967, Greitzer coauthored the well-received textbook *Geometry Revisited*, which has remained in print for more than 40 years.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), Greitzer moved to the United States in 1906, graduated from [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School), received his bachelor's degree in 1927 from [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), and later earned a Ph.D. from [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University). He held academic positions at [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University), [Brooklyn Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Polytechnic_Institute), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o5] In the 1960s and 1970s, he directed a [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) summer program at Rutgers for high-ability high-school math students.

Samuel Greitzer and his wife Ethel had one son.[^o4] Samuel died on February 22, 1988, in [Metuchen, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metuchen,_New_Jersey).[^o5][^o6]
