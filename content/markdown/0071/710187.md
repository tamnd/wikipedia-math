# Ronald L. Iman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald L. Iman"}

**Ronald L. Iman** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He was one of the developers of the statistical technique known as [Latin hypercube sampling](wikipedia://en/710164).

:::

In 1991 Iman was awarded the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).[^o2] He was [president of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/14833025) in 1994.

## Books {#Books}

- *A Data-Based Approach to Statistics* (1994)
- *A Data-Based Approach to Statistics: Concise Version* (1995)
- *Modern Business Statistics* with W. J. Conover, 2e (1989)
- *Modern Business Statistics* with W. J. Conover, (1983)
- *Introduction to Modern Business Statistics* with W. J. Conover, (1983)
- *A Modern Approach to Statistics* with W. J. Conover, (1983)
