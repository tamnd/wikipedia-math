# Marie-Claude Arnaud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marie-Claude Arnaud"}

**Marie-Claude Arnaud-Delabrière** (born 24 February 1963) is a French mathematician, specializing in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). She is University Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Avignon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Avignon) and a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Arnaud was a mathematics student at the [École normale supérieure (Paris)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 1983 to 1987; she earned a bachelor's degree in 1984, an [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1985, and a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in 1986. She earned her doctorate in 1990 from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) under the supervision of [Michael Herman](wikipedia://en/28451358), and completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1999 at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University).

After working as an assistant at [Louis Pasteur University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur_University) from 1987 to 1989, and then as a [temporary researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attaché_Temporaire_d'Enseignement_et_de_Recherche) at Paris Diderot University from 1989 to 1991, she became an assistant professor at Paris Diderot University in 1991. In 2001 she moved to Avignon as a full professor.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2010, Arnaud was a speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). In 2011 she won the Gabrielle Sand and M. Guido Triossi Prize of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for her work on [Hamiltonian dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/1197531), and in particular on the regularity of invariant curves in the [dynamics of billiards](wikipedia://en/2160429). She was named to the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) as a senior member in 2013. She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2020.
