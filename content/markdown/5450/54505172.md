# Polynomial differential form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polynomial differential form"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the ring of **polynomial differential forms** on the standard [*n*-simplex](wikipedia://en/39781) is the [differential graded algebra](wikipedia://en/2415863):[^o2]

:::

$$\Omega^*_{\text{poly}}([n])= \mathbb{Q}[t_0, ..., t_n, dt_0, ..., dt_n]/(\sum t_i - 1, \sum dt_i).$$

Varying *n*, it determines the [simplicial commutative dg algebra](wikipedia://en/40782000):

$$\Omega^*_{\text{poly}}$$

(each $u: [n] \to [m]$ induces the map $\Omega^*_{\text{poly}}([m]) \to \Omega^*_{\text{poly}}([n]), t_i \mapsto \sum_{u(j)=i} t_j$).
