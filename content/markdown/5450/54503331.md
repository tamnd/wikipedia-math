# Weyl's tube formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weyl's tube formula"}

**Weyl's tube formula** gives the [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of an object defined as the set of all points within a small distance of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470).

:::

Let $\Sigma$ be an [oriented](wikipedia://en/187446), closed, two-dimensional surface, and let $N_\varepsilon(\Sigma)$ denote the set of all points within a distance $\varepsilon$ of the surface $\Sigma$. Then, for $\varepsilon$ sufficiently small, the volume of $N_\varepsilon(\Sigma)$ is

$$V = 2A(\Sigma)\varepsilon + \frac{4\pi}{3} \chi(\Sigma)\varepsilon^3,$$

where $A(\Sigma)$ is the [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of the surface and $\chi(\Sigma)$ is its [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420). This expression can be generalized to the case where $\Sigma$ is a $q$-dimensional [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) of $n$-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
