# Factorization homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Factorization homology"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), **factorization homology** is a variant of [topological chiral homology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_chiral_homology), motivated by an application to [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757) and [cobordism hypothesis](wikipedia://en/41419738) in particular. It was introduced by David Ayala, John Francis, and Nick Rozenblyum.[^o2]

:::
