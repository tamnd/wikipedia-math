# T. E. S. Raghavan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="T. E. S. Raghavan"}

**Tirukkannamangai Echambadi Srinivasa Raghavan** (born on September 01, 1940) is an Indian-American mathematician and game theorist.[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He holds the degree of M.Sc. in [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) (Presidency College, [Madras University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras)) and received his Ph.D. in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) from [ISI Calcutta](wikipedia://en/2238418) in 1968 under the guidance of statistician [Calyampudi Radhakrishna Rao](wikipedia://en/1418009) based on his thesis: *Extensions on the Theory of Positive Operators and Their Relationship to Minimax Games*.

Raghavan’s interests are [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear) and [non-linear programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear_programming), [matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_theory), [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics), [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476).[^o5] He made contributions to [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924),[^o6][^o7][^o8] [decision science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_science), and special [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859)[^o9] are well known. He co-authored books on [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) with [T. Parthasarathy](wikipedia://en/18795039),[^o10][^o11] and the nonnegative matrices and their applications with R.B. Bapat.[^o9] He contributed to the understanding of stochastic games[^o12] and cooperative games.[^o8][^o7] T.E.S. Raghavan organized 4 international conferences at [UIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Chicago), the first on Stochastic games in honor of [L.Shapley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Shapley) (1987), special Game Theory International Conferences at UIC in honor of [Shapley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Shapley)(1991), [Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Aumann) (1994), [Maschler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Maschler) (1996).

He published more than 60 research papers, 4 monographs,[^o13][^o14][^o5] and guided 14 PhD students.[^o15]

## Cultural engagement and educational initiatives {#Cultural_engagement_and_educational_initiatives}

Being an ardent fan of Carnatic music, Raghavan has been actively involved in promoting the art form through the annual **Chicago Tyagaraja Utsavam**, which he helps organize in Chicago.[^o17]

In line with the ancient Indian educational system, Raghavan also runs a *[Gurukulam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurukula)* (Sanskrit: गुरुकुलम्) in Game Theory in his native village of Pulavanur, Tamil Nadu. This Gurukulam was organized in collaboration with CARAMS, MAHE, Manipal, furthering its academic outreach and fostering interdisciplinary dialogue.[^o3]
