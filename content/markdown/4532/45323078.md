# Jason Behrstock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jason Behrstock"}

**Jason Alan Behrstock** is a mathematician at the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) known for his work in [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Behrstock was born in California and was educated in California's public school system.[^o5] He received his Ph.D. from [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook) in 2004. He went to work at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) before his time at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College), [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York).[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 2009, Behrstock was award the [Feliks Gross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feliks_Gross) Endowment Award by the [Graduate Center of the City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center_of_the_City_University_of_New_York), a research award for young faculty.[^o8]
- In 2010, Behrstock was awarded the [Alfred P. Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Fellowship).[^o9]
- In 2012, Behrstock became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]
- Behrstock became a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) in 2014.[^o11]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Behrstock, Jason A. "Asymptotic geometry of the mapping class group and Teichmüller space". *Geom. Topol.* 10 (2006), pages 1523–1578.
- Behrstock, Jason; Druţu, Cornelia; Mosher, Lee. "Thick metric spaces, relative hyperbolicity, and quasi-isometric rigidity". *Math. Ann.* 344 (2009), number 3, pages 543–595.
- Behrstock, Jason A.; Minsky, Yair N. "Dimension and rank for mapping class groups". *Ann. of Math.* (2) 167 (2008), number 3, pages 1055–1077.
