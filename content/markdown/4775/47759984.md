# Combinatorics, Probability and Computing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Combinatorics, Probability and Computing"}

***Combinatorics, Probability and Computing*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) in mathematics published by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press). Its [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956) ([DPMMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPMMS) and [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis)).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was established by Bollobás in 1992.[^o5] [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [Timothy Gowers](wikipedia://en/365715) calls it "a personal favourite" among combinatorics journals and writes that it "maintains a high standard".[^o6]

## Content {#Content}

The journal covers [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392). Currently, it publishes six issues annually. As with other journals from the same publisher, it follows a hybrid green/gold [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) policy, in which authors may either place copies of their papers in an [institutional repository](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institutional_repository) after a six-month embargo period, or pay an open access charge to make their papers free to read on the journal's website.
