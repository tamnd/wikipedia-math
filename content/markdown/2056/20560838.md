# Equivariant L-function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Equivariant L-function"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), an **equivariant Artin L-function** is a function associated to a finite [Galois extension](wikipedia://en/457064) of [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358) created by packaging together the various [Artin L-functions](wikipedia://en/2774171) associated with the extension. Each extension has many traditional Artin L-functions associated with it, corresponding to the [characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_character) of [representations](wikipedia://en/6355948) of the Galois group. By contrast, each extension has a unique corresponding equivariant L-function.

:::

Equivariant L-functions have become increasingly important as a wide range of conjectures and theorems in number theory have been developed around them. Among these are the [Brumer&ndash;Stark conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brumer&ndash;Stark_conjecture), the [Coates-Sinnott conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coates-Sinnott_conjecture), and a recently developed [equivariant version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_Iwasawa_conjecture) of the [main conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwasawa_main_conjecture) in [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150).
