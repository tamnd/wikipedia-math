# Gaston Albert Gohierre de Longchamps

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaston Albert Gohierre de Longchamps"}

**Gaston Albert Gohierre de Longchamps** (1 March 1842 – 9 July 1906) was a French mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Gohierre de Longchamps was born on 1 March 1842 in [Alençon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alençon). He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) beginning in 1863, and began a career as a teacher beginning in 1866. He retired from the [Lycée Saint-Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Saint-Louis), his final teaching position, in 1897, and died in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) on 9 July 1906.[^o2][^o3]

De Longchamps was a member of many international scientific societies, and in 1892 became a chevalier of the [Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honor).[^o2] He was the editor of the *Journal de mathématiques élémentaires* and an associated journal, the *Journal de mathématiques spéciales*, taking both journals over from their founder J. Bourget.[^o2][^o3][^o4] The [de Longchamps point](wikipedia://en/14593776) of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) is named after him, for his 1886 study that identified this point.[^o3][^o5]
