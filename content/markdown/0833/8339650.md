# List of mathematic operators

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of mathematic operators"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an [operator](wikipedia://en/22213) or [transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_%28mathematics%29) is a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) from one [space of functions](wikipedia://en/340630) to another. Operators occur commonly in [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and mathematics. Many are [integral operators](wikipedia://en/2649421) and [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886).

:::

In the following *L* is an operator

$$L:\mathcal{F}\to\mathcal{G}$$

which takes a function $y\in\mathcal{F}$ to another function $L[y]\in\mathcal{G}$. Here, $\mathcal{F}$ and $\mathcal{G}$ are some unspecified [function spaces](wikipedia://en/340630), such as [Hardy space](wikipedia://en/314780), [*L*<sup>p</sup> space](wikipedia://en/45194), [Sobolev space](wikipedia://en/611964), or, more vaguely, the space of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110).
