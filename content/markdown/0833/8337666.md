# Wilhelm Magnus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Magnus"}

**Hans Heinrich Wilhelm Magnus**, known as **Wilhelm Magnus** (5 February 1907 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire) – 15 October 1990 in [New Rochelle, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Rochelle,_New_York)), was a German-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made important contributions in [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834), [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [elliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_functions), and the study of [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1931, Magnus received his PhD from the [University of Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt) in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). His thesis, written under the direction of [Max Dehn](wikipedia://en/570602), was entitled *Über unendlich diskontinuierliche Gruppen von einer definierenden Relation (der [Freiheitssatz](wikipedia://en/12756944))*.

Magnus was a faculty member in Frankfurt from 1933 until 1938. He refused to join the [Nazi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party) and, as a consequence, was not allowed to hold an academic post during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II). In 1947, he became a professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

In 1948, he emigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) to collaborate on the [Bateman Manuscript Project](wikipedia://en/3053039) as a co-editor while a visiting professor at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). In 1950, he was appointed professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) in [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He stayed there until 1973, when he moved to the [Polytechnic Institute of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_New_York), before retiring in 1978. His doctoral students include [Joan Birman](wikipedia://en/8625257), Martin Greendlinger, [Edna Grossman](wikipedia://en/6643135), [Herbert Keller](wikipedia://en/24424009), [Seymour Lipschutz](wikipedia://en/35712077), and [Kathryn F. Kuiken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_F._Kuiken).[^o4]
