# Peter Wynn (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Wynn"}

**Peter Wynn** (1931—2017) was an English mathematician. His main achievements concern [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713) – in particular the theory of [Padé approximants](wikipedia://en/3090820) – and its application in [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_mathematics) for improving the [rate of convergence](wikipedia://en/999701) of [sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438).

:::
