# Barry Pennington

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barry Pennington"}

**William Barry Pennington** (9 July 1923 – 5 March 1968) was a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for his work on [Ramanujan's tau function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan's_tau_function).[^o2]

:::

Pennington was born in [Bawtry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bawtry), [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire). He was educated at [Loughborough Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_Grammar_School), and [Jesus College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University). During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he served as a Radar Officer in the [RNVR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNVR), before returning to Cambridge to complete his doctorate under [Albert Edward Ingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Edward_Ingham). After completing his doctorate, he spent two years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), returning to a Research Fellowship at Jesus in 1952. He was appointed Reader in mathematics at [Westfield College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield_College), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) in 1953. In 1961 he was appointed Professor of Pure Mathematics at [University College of Wales, Aberystwyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_Wales,_Aberystwyth), in succession to [V.C. Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V.C._Morton),[^o3] and where he remained until his death, aside from the 1959–60 [academic year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_year) which he spent at [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University) in [Hamilton, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_Ontario).
