# Jacobi form

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacobi form"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Jacobi form** is an [automorphic form](wikipedia://en/782146) on the [Jacobi group](wikipedia://en/32004414), which is the [semidirect product](wikipedia://en/48246) of the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) Sp(n;R) and the [Heisenberg group](wikipedia://en/448518) $H^{(n,h)}_R$. The theory was first systematically studied by (Eichler Zagier 1985).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Jacobi form of level 1, weight *k* and index *m* is a function $\phi(\tau,z)$ of two complex variables (with τ in the upper half plane) such that

- $$\phi\left(\frac{a\tau+b}{c\tau+d},\frac{z}{c\tau+d}\right) = (c\tau+d)^ke^{\frac{2\pi i mcz^2}{c\tau+d}}\phi(\tau,z)\text{ for }{a\ b\choose c\ d}\in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$$
- $\phi(\tau,z+\lambda\tau+\mu) = e^{-2\pi i m(\lambda^2\tau+2\lambda z)}\phi(\tau,z)$ for all integers λ, μ.
- $\phi$ has a Fourier expansion

$$\phi(\tau,z) = \sum_{n\ge 0} \sum_{r^2\le 4mn} C(n,r)e^{2\pi i (n\tau+rz)}.$$

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Examples in two variables include [Jacobi theta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_theta_functions), the [Weierstrass ℘ function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_P_function), and Fourier–Jacobi coefficients of [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927) of genus 2. Examples with more than two variables include characters of some irreducible highest-weight representations of affine [Kac–Moody algebras](wikipedia://en/1146292). Meromorphic Jacobi forms appear in the theory of [Mock modular forms](wikipedia://en/5954264).

:::
