# List of derivatives and integrals in alternative calculi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of derivatives and integrals in alternative calculi"}

There are many alternatives to the [classical calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) of [Newton](wikipedia://en/14627) and [Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281); for example, each of the infinitely many non-Newtonian calculi.[^o2] Occasionally an alternative calculus is more suited than the classical calculus for expressing a given scientific or mathematical idea.[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

The table below is intended to assist people working with the alternative calculus called the "geometric calculus" (or its discrete analog).

## Table {#Table}

In the following table;

$\psi(x)=\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)}$ is the [digamma function](wikipedia://en/406935),

$\operatorname{K}(x)=e^{\zeta^\prime(-1,x)-\zeta^\prime(-1)}=e^{\frac{z-z^2}{2}+\frac z2 \ln (2\pi)-\psi^{(-2)}(z)}$ is the [K-function](wikipedia://en/2060130),

$(!x)=\frac{\Gamma(x+1,-1)}{e}$ is [subfactorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subfactorial),

$B_a(x)=-a\zeta(-a+1,x)$ are the generalized to real numbers [Bernoulli polynomials](wikipedia://en/228161).
