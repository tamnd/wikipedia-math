# David Klein (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Klein"}

**David Klein** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Mathematics at [California State University in Northridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University_Northridge).[^n1] He is an advocate of increasingly rigorous treatment of mathematics in school curricula and a frequently cited opponent of reforms based on the [NCTM standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCTM_standards). One of the participants in the founding of [Mathematically Correct](wikipedia://en/6876796), Klein appears regularly in the [Math Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Math_Wars).

:::

Klein, who is a member of the U.S. Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of Israel, supports the [BDS movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BDS_movement) which seeks to impose comprehensive boycotts against Israel until it meets its obligations under [international law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_law).[^n2] Klein hosts a webpage supportive of the BDS movement on his university website and, starting in 2011, it became the target of numerous complaints from the pro-Israel groups [AMCHA Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMCHA_Initiative), [Shurat HaDin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shurat_HaDin), and the Global Frontier Justice Center who claimed that it constituted a misuse of state resources.[^o4] The complaints were dismissed both by the university's staff and by legal authorities as baseless.[^o5][^o6]

Consistent with his support for the BDS movement, Klein defended [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) associate professor [John Cheney-Lippold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cheney-Lippold)'s decision to decline to write a letter of recommendation to a student who planned to study in Israel.[^o7]

Klein is the director of CSUNs Climate Science Program.
