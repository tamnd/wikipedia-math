# Joan L. Richards

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joan L. Richards"}

**Joan Livingston Richards** (born 1948) is an American [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and a professor of history at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where she directs the Program of Science and Technology Studies.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Richards graduated *[magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors)* from [Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College) in 1971. She completed a Ph.D. in the history of science at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1981. Her dissertation, *Non-Euclidean Geometry In Nineteenth-century England: A Study of Changing Perceptions of Mathematical Truth*, was supervised by [I. Bernard Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._Bernard_Cohen).

After postdoctoral research at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), she joined the Brown University faculty in 1982, and was promoted to full professor in 2001.

## Books {#Books}

Richards is the author of the monograph *Mathematical Visions: The Pursuit of Geometry in Victorian England* (Academic Press, 1988) and of a memoir on her struggle to balance her academic work with caring for a son with a brain tumor, *Angles of Reflection: Logic and a Mother's Love* (W. H. Freeman, 2000).

She is the co-editor of *The Invention of Physical Science: Intersections of Mathematics, Theology and Natural Philosophy since the Seventeenth Century, Essays in Honor of Erwin N. Hiebert* (with [Mary Jo Nye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Jo_Nye) and [Roger H. Stuewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_H._Stuewer), Kluwer, 1992).
