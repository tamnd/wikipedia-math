# Edith Rita Lowenstern

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edith Rita Lowenstern"}

**Edith Rita Lowenstern** (26 December 1904 – 1970) was an Australian mathematician.

:::

She was born in [Dandenong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandenong) and educated at [Melbourne Girls Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Girls_Grammar_School) and the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), where she captained the [women's hockey team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_University_Hockey_Club) in 1924–1925.[^o2] She then worked as a tutor at Melbourne before being appointed as lecturer at the [University of Tasmania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tasmania), becoming the first full-time female lecturer at that institution, as well as the second female mathematics lecturer in Australia.[^o3]

Lowenstern resigned in 1936, married, and moved to [Bicheno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicheno).[^o4]
