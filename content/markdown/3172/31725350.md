# Julius Bergmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julius Bergmann"}

**Julius Bergmann** (; 1 April 1839, [Opherdike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holzwickede), [Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Westphalia) – 24 August 1904, [Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg)) was a German [neo-Kantian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Kantian) philosopher.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

At the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), Bergmann devoted himself to [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and philosophy. At Göttingen, he studied logic under [Hermann Lotze](wikipedia://en/191379).[^o3]

He was appointed to the chair of philosophy at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) in 1872, and three years later to a similar chair at the [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg).

## Works {#Works}

- *Grundlinien einer Theorie des Bewusstseins* (*Outlines of a theory of consciousness*, 1870)
- *Zur Beurteilung des Kriticismus* (*On judging criticism*, 1875)
- *Reine Logik* (*Pure logic*, 1879)
- *Sein und Erkennen* (*To be and to recognize*, 1880)
- *Die Grundprobleme der Logik* (*Fundamental problems in logic*, 1882)
- *Geschichte der Philosophie* (*History of philosophy*, 1892–94)
- *Untersuchungen über Hauptpunkte der Philosophie* (*Investigations on the main points of philosophy*, 1900)
- *System des objektiven Idealismus* (*A system of objective idealism*, 1903)
