# Waring's prime number conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Waring's prime number conjecture"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), **Waring's prime number conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) related to [Vinogradov's theorem](wikipedia://en/4633030), named after the English mathematician [Edward Waring](wikipedia://en/9723). It states that every [odd number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odd_number) exceeding 3 is either a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) or the sum of three prime numbers. It follows from the [generalized Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/152567),[^o2] and ([trivially](wikipedia://en/193053)) from [Goldbach's weak conjecture](wikipedia://en/147164).

:::
