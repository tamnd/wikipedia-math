# Minhyong Kim

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Minhyong Kim"}

**Minhyong Kim** () is a South Korean [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialises in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kim received his PhD at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1990 under the supervision of [Serge Lang](wikipedia://en/872991) and [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679), going on to work in a number of universities, including [M.I.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona), [Purdue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), the [Korea Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/16984), UCL ([University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London)), the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), where he was Head of the Number Theory Research Group, and the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), where he was the Christopher Zeeman Professor of Algebra, Geometry, and the Public Understanding of Mathematics. He is currently the Director of the International Centre for Mathematical Sciences and Sir Edmund Whittaker Professor of Mathematical Sciences at the Maxwell Institute, the joint graduate school of the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University).

He is married to Larissa McGoldrick, a former dancer for [Merce Cunningham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merce_Cunningham). They have two sons, Oisin and Niall. Kim's father is the literature scholar [Kim Uchang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Uchang) and his brother is the biologist [Junhyong Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junhyong_Kim).

## Research {#Research}

Kim has made contributions to the application of arithmetic [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) theory to the study of [Diophantine problems](wikipedia://en/9109), especially to finiteness theorems of the [Faltings](wikipedia://en/172199)–[Siegel type](wikipedia://en/3989092).

His work was featured in 2017 in the [Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976), where he described his work as being inspired by physics.[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012, Minhyong Kim received the [Ho-Am Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho-Am_Prize) for Science,[^o8] with the Ho-Am committee citing him as "one of the leading researchers in the area of arithmetic algebraic geometry". He was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in their 2024 class of fellows.[^o9], and was elected as a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in their 2025 class of fellows.[^o10] In 2026, he was elected as an inaugural fellow of the UK Academy for the Mathematical Sciences [^o11].
