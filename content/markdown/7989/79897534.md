# Sabine Bögli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sabine Bögli"}

**Sabine Bögli** (also published as Boegli) is a Swiss mathematician specialising in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), including the [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) of [non-self-adjoint](wikipedia://en/187408) [Schrödinger operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schrödinger_operator) and their applications in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). Her research has resolved a decades-old dispute over the location of [autoionizing resonances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoionization) in atoms and molecules, answered a longstanding open question on the accumulation of [eigenvalues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of Schrödinger operators, and disproved a conjecture of Laptev and Safronov relating the magnitude of these eigenvalues to the norm of the potential. She works in England as an associate professor at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bögli is Swiss, and speaks [Swiss German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_German) natively. After secondary education at the Gymnasium Biel-Seeland in [Biel/Bienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biel/Bienne), she studied mathematics at the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern), receiving a bachelor's degree in 2010, a master's degree in 2012, and a Ph.D. in 2014. Her doctoral dissertation, *Spectral approximation for linear operators and applications*, was supervised by [Christiane Tretter](wikipedia://en/63227596).

After postdoctoral research at the University of Bern, [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University), [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), and [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich), and a Chapman Fellowship at Imperial College London, she joined [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) as an assistant professor in 2019. She was promoted to associate professor in 2023.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bögli was a 2024 recipient of the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), given for her research on Schrödinger operators.
