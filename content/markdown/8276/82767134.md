# Deligne's completeness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deligne's completeness theorem"}

In mathematics, **Deligne's completeness theorem** says a [coherent topos](wikipedia://en/64383764) has enough points. It was first introduced by [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544) in [*SGA* 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SGA_4).

:::

In 1970s, the category theorist [William Lawvere](wikipedia://en/872760) observed that Deligne's theorem implies the [Gödel completeness theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_completeness_theorem).[^o2]
