# List of United States regional mathematics competitions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of United States regional mathematics competitions"}

Many [math competitions](wikipedia://en/19710) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) have regional restrictions. Of these, most are statewide.

:::

For a more complete list, please visit [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110119132429/http://homeschoolmathcontests.com/regional.aspx) .

The contests include:
