# Institute of Mathematics, Physics, and Mechanics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Mathematics, Physics, and Mechanics"}

**Institute of Mathematics, Physics, and Mechanics** (Inštitut za matematiko, fiziko in mehaniko; **IMFM**) is the leading [research institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute) in the areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) in [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia). It includes researchers from [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana), [University of Maribor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maribor) and [University of Primorska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Primorska). It was founded in 1960.[^o2]

:::

The IMFM is composed of the following departments:

- Department of Mathematrics
- Department of Physics
- Department of Theoretical Computer Science

The director is [Peter Šemrl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Šemrl).
