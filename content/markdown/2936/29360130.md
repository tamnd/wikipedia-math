# Bethany Rose Marsh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bethany Rose Marsh"}

**Bethany Rose Marsh** is a mathematician working in the areas of [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of finite-dimensional algebras, [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), [tilting theory](wikipedia://en/30365107), [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678), [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) and [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004).[^o3] Marsh currently works at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) as a Professor of pure mathematics. She was a [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPSRC) Leadership Fellow from 2008 to 2014.[^o3] In addition to her duties at the University of Leeds, Marsh was an editor of the [Glasgow Mathematical Journal](wikipedia://en/21523412) from 2008 to 2013 and served on the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) editorial board from 2014 to 2018.[^o4]

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

In July 2009, Marsh was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for her work on representation theory, and especially for her research on cluster categories and cluster algebras.[^o6]

She was one of two 2024 Mary Cartwright Lecturers of the London Mathematical Society.[^o7]
