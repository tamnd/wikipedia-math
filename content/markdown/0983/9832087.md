# Bloch space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bloch space"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), the **Bloch space**, named after French mathematician [André Bloch](wikipedia://en/11772883) and denoted $\mathcal{B}$ or ℬ, is the space of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) *f* defined on the [open](wikipedia://en/39358) [unit disc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_disc) **D** in the complex plane, such that the function

:::

$$(1-|z|^2)|f^\prime(z)|$$

is bounded. $\mathcal{B}$ is a type of [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989), with the norm defined by

$$\|f\|_\mathcal{B} = |f(0)| + \sup_{z \in \mathbf{D}} (1-|z|^2) |f'(z)|.$$

This is referred to as the **Bloch norm** and the elements of the Bloch space are called **Bloch functions**.
