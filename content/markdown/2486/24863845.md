# Stefan Müller (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan Müller"}

*Image: Mueller stefan.jpg.* Stefan Müller

**Stefan Müller** (born 15 March 1962 in [Wuppertal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuppertal))[^o2] is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and currently a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). He has been one of the founding directors of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in 1996 and was acting there until 2008.

:::

He is well known for his research in [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134) and the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). He is interested in the application of mathematical methods in the theory [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918), especially [material science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_science) and problems involving microstructures.
