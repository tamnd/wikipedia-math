# Dato Barbakadze

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dato Barbakadze"}

**Dato Barbakadze** (დათო ბარბაქაძე); born 7 February 1966) is a [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_%28country%29) writer, essayist and translator.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dato Barbakadze was born in [Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi). He studied [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) in *[Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University)* between 1984 and 1992 and holds a [Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) degree in Philosophy. In 1992-1994 he pursued his post-graduate studies in the Department of Sociology at Tbilisi State University, but left a standard academic career to pursue independent projects in Georgian and German literature: he has followed a non-traditional academic path for the past twenty years (2010). In 1991 Dato Barbakadze founded a literary video-magazine *Dato Barbakadze’s Magazine*, which for two years was regularly performed at Tbilisi State University. He also founded the literary magazines *Polilogue* (1994, four issues) and *± Literature* (1996, four issues). These publications featured many of the innovations of Georgian literature in the 1990s. From 1991 to 2001 Dato Barbakadze taught courses in [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), the history of philosophy, [esthetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esthetics) and introductory philosophy courses in several universities in Tbilisi. In 2002-2005 he lived in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) where he pursued his literary interests and studied philosophy, ancient history and [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology) at [Westphalian Wilhelms-University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westphalian_Wilhelms-University_of_Münster). After returning to Tbilisi in 2005 Dato Barbakadze started the project *XXth century Austrian Poetry*, serving both as an editor and contributor. Dato Barbakadze has been a member of [Die Kogge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Kogge), an association of European writers, since 2007. His works have been translated into English, German, French and Russian. He lives in Tbilisi, Georgia.
