# MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference"}

The **MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference** (SSAC) is an annual event that provides a forum for industry professionals (executives and leading researchers) and students to discuss the increasing role of [analytics](wikipedia://en/487132) in the [sports industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_industry). The conference is held in the [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston) area, and while its location has moved from the [MIT campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campus_of_the_Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) to higher capacity convention centers such as the [Hynes Convention Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hynes_Convention_Center) and the [Boston Convention and Exhibition Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Convention_and_Exhibition_Center),[^o3] it has always occurred during February or March.

:::

Founded in 2006, the conference is co-chaired by [Daryl Morey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Morey), who was most recently president of basketball operations for the [Philadelphia 76ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_76ers), and Jessica Gelman, CEO of [Kraft Analytics Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraft_Analytics_Group),[^o4] who oversee [MIT Sloan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Sloan_School_of_Management) students (from the EMS Club) in the planning and operating of the yearly conference. It is the largest student-run conference in the world, attracting students from over 170 different schools and representatives from over 80 professional sports teams in [Major League Baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball), the [National Basketball Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_Association), [National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League), [National Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hockey_League), [Major League Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Soccer), and [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League).[^o5] The conference has been sold out every year and has become the premier venue for [sports analytics](wikipedia://en/51589225) discussion. [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN) has been the presenting [sponsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sponsor_%28commercial%29) since 2010, and the conference has garnered national attention through media outlets such as *[Sports Illustrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated)*, *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal)*, *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times)*, *[The Boston Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boston_Globe)*, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_%28magazine%29)*, *[BusinessWeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BusinessWeek)*, [NBC Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_Sports), [Fox Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports_%28United_States%29), *[Pardon the Interruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardon_the_Interruption)*, and *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)*. The conference was ranked by *[Fast Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_Company_%28magazine%29)* as the third-most innovative company in sports in 2012.[^o6]
