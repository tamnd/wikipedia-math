# Masao Haji

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Masao Haji"}

*Image: Hajisan2 recuture@setagaya.JPG.* Haji-san's lecture at setagaya

*Image: 土師さん世田谷2.JPG.* Haji's Home

**Masao Haji** was a Japanese political activist, mathematics lecturer and critic. He also wrote books under the name Ryūsuke Nomi. He was chair of mathematics at the correspondence-course "Z-kai", and taught at the three top exam preparation schools ([juku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juku)): Yoyogi Seminar, Sundai Preparatory School and Kawai Juku Groupwork.

:::

## Career {#Career}

For many years, he was responsible for the "basic mathematics seminar" at Yoyogi exam preparation school. He was a lecturer in Oubun-Sha company's "University entrance exam radio". Oubun-Sha was the author of the "fundamental issues lecture" and "standard issue lecture" series and the author of "training" series of the Zoushin-kai Publishing House, such as Aleph's "Calculus Lab".

He gave counseling to young people, including homeless youth.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He graduated from the Department of Engineering of the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo).

Haji graduated from the kyu-sei-ichi-koukou (currently, University of Tokyo liberal arts).

*Image: Hajisan1sinobu.JPG.* Haji-san Memorial event

In his youth he suffered from [tuberculosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuberculosis) and recovered but sometimes had hoarseness or cough.

He is buried in Aoyama cemetery in Minato-ku, Tokyo.[^o4][^o5]

Haji was involved in the "Peace to Vietnam" committee.
