# Fanghua Lin

Lin Fanghua

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fanghua Lin"}

**Fanghua Lin** (; born March 11, 1959), also written as **Fang-Hua Lin**, is a Chinese-born American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is currently the Silver Professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). He applies rigorous analysis to nonlinear systems and is a leader in this field.[^o3] He was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2025.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Lin was born in 1959 in [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang), China, and graduated from the Department of Mathematics of [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in 1981. He went to the United States to obtain his PhD from the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1985. From 1985 to 1988, he was an instructor at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University. Then he went to the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and was a full professor there from 1988 until 1989. In 1989, he started his professorship at New York University. He was awarded the Silver Professorship at the Courant Institute.[^o6][^o7]

Lin made substantial contributions in [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286).[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 1989–1991, [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship)
- 1989–1994, [Presidential Young Investigator Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Young_Investigator_Award)
- 1990, invited speaker, [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), Kyoto[^o9]
- 1999, Ordway Chair Visiting Professor, University of Minnesota
- 2002, [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872), by [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for fundamental contributions to our understanding of the Ginzburg-Landau equations[^o10]
- 2004, [Shiing-shen Chern Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiing-shen_Chern)
- 2004, fellow, [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences)[^o11]
- 2014, fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o12]
- 2022, [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow), "for significant contributions to our understanding of the properties of solutions throughout nonlinear partial differential equations"[^o13]
