# Loredana Lanzani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Loredana Lanzani"}

**Loredana Lanzani** (born 1965) is an Italian-American mathematician specializing in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). She is a professor of mathematics at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Lanzani earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) in 1989, and completed a Ph.D. at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 1997. Her dissertation, *A New Perspective On The Cauchy Transform For Non-Smooth Domains In The Plane And Applications*, was supervised by Steven R. Bell.

She became an assistant professor at the [University of Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas) in 1997, and moved up the academic ranks there until becoming a full professor in 2008, also being given the Robert C. & Sandra Connor Endowed Faculty Fellowship in the same year. From 2011 to 2013 she was a program director for the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation), and in 2014 she took her present position as a professor of mathematics at Syracuse University.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Lanzani was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to function theory in one and several complex variables". She became the first Syracuse University mathematician to win this honor.
