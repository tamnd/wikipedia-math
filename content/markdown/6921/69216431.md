# Gödel Lecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gödel Lecture"}

The **Gödel Lecture** is an honor in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) given by the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438), associated with an annual lecture at the association's general meeting. The award is named after [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736) and has been given annually since 1990.[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Award winners {#Award_winners}

The list of award winners and lecture titles is maintained online by the Association for Symbolic Logic.[^o5]

- 1990 [Ronald Jensen](wikipedia://en/3785996), *Inner Models and Large Cardinals.*
- 1991 [Dana Scott](wikipedia://en/161875), *Will Logicians be Replaced by Machines?*
- 1992 [Joseph R. Shoenfield](wikipedia://en/48884267), *The Priority Method.*
- 1993 [Angus Macintyre](wikipedia://en/11551260), *Logic of Real and p-adic Analysis: Achievements and Challenges.*
- 1994 [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), *L(R): A Survey.*
- 1995 [Leo Harrington](wikipedia://en/5693354), *Gödel, Heidegger, and Direct Perception (or, Why I am a Recursion Theorist).*
- 1996 [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211), *Categoricity without compactness.*
- 1997 [Solomon Feferman](wikipedia://en/355401), *Occupations and Preoccupations with Gödel: His \*Works\* and the Work.*
- 1998 [Alexander S. Kechris](wikipedia://en/2291355), *Current Trends in Descriptive Set Theory.*
- 1999 [Stephen Cook](wikipedia://en/39432), *Logic and computational complexity.*
- 2000 [Jon Barwise](wikipedia://en/912858) — cancelled due to the death of the speaker.[^o6]
- 2001 [Theodore Slaman](wikipedia://en/5693320), *Recursion Theory.*
- 2002 [Harvey Friedman](wikipedia://en/3038108), *Issues in the foundations of mathematics.*
- 2003 [Boris Zilber](wikipedia://en/47992829), *Categoricity.*
- 2004 [Michael O. Rabin](wikipedia://en/298404), *Proofs persuasions and randomness in mathematics.*
- 2005 [Menachem Magidor](wikipedia://en/17115328), *Skolem-Lowenheim theorems for generalized logics.*
- 2006 [Per Martin-Löf](wikipedia://en/1925764), *The two layers of logic.*
- 2007 [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981) — a lecture on his work titled *Algebraic Model Theory* was given by [Thomas W. Scanlon](wikipedia://en/80080715) in his absence.[^o7]
- 2008 [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071), *The Continuum Hypothesis, the $\Omega$ Conjecture, and the inner model problem of one supercompact cardinal.*
- 2009 [Richard Shore](wikipedia://en/18435038), *Reverse Mathematics: the Playground of Logic.*
- 2010 [Alexander Razborov](wikipedia://en/3533454), *Complexity of Propositional Proofs.*
- 2011 [Anand Pillay](wikipedia://en/69187512), *First order theories.*
- 2012 [John R. Steel](wikipedia://en/2290967), *The hereditarily ordinal definable sets in models of determinacy.*
- 2013 [Kit Fine](wikipedia://en/3853559), *Truthmaker semantics.*
- 2014 [Julia F. Knight](wikipedia://en/40816534), *Computable structure theory and formulas of special forms.*
- 2015 [Alex Wilkie](wikipedia://en/10343119), *Complex continuations of functions definable in $\mathbb{R}_{an, exp}$ with a diophantine application.*
- 2016 [Stevo Todorčević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevo_Todorčević), *Basis problems in set theory.*
- 2017 [Charles Parsons](wikipedia://en/1821365), *Gödel and the universe of sets.*
- 2018 [Rod Downey](wikipedia://en/34805600), *Algorithmic randomness.*
- 2019 [Samuel Buss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Buss), *Totality, provability and feasibility.*
- 2020 [Élisabeth Bouscaren](wikipedia://en/58695760), *The ubiquity of configurations in Model Theory.*
- 2021 [Matthew Foreman](wikipedia://en/3332703), *Gödel Diffeomorphisms.*
- 2022 [Patricia Blanchette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Blanchette), *Formalism in Logic.*
- 2023 [Carl Jockusch](wikipedia://en/9859617), *From algorithms which succeed on a large set of inputs to the Turing degrees as a metric space.*
- 2024 [Thomas W. Scanlon](wikipedia://en/80080715), *(Un)decidability in fields.*
- 2025 [Joan Bagaria](wikipedia://en/55464505), "New challenges to our conception of the Higher Infinite."
- 2026 [John P. Burgess](wikipedia://en/24554560), "Logical and mathematical conventionalism."
