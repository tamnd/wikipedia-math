# Pierre Dolbeault

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Dolbeault"}

**Pierre Dolbeault** (October 10, 1924 – June 12, 2015) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Dolbeault studied with [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) and graduated in 1944 from the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1955 under the supervision of Cartan, with a dissertation titled *Formes différentielles et cohomologie sur une variété analytique complexe*.

He taught in the 1950s at the [University of Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montpellier) and the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux), and later at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) ([Jussieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jussieu_Campus)). Together with [Pierre Lelong](wikipedia://en/34789789) and [Henri Skoda](wikipedia://en/48394269) he held an Analysis seminar in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989).

[Dolbeault cohomology](wikipedia://en/3189601) is named after him, and so is the [Dolbeault theorem](wikipedia://en/3189601#Dolbeault's_theorem).
