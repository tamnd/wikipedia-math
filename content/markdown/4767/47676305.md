# David Sumner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Sumner"}

**David P. Sumner** is an American mathematician known for his research in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He formulated [Sumner's conjecture](wikipedia://en/28866227) that [tournaments](wikipedia://en/1427502) are [universal graphs](wikipedia://en/1452141) for [polytrees](wikipedia://en/1731488) in 1971,[^o2] and showed in 1974 that all [claw-free graphs](wikipedia://en/12415907) with an even number of vertices have [perfect matchings](wikipedia://en/44031).[^o3] He and [András Gyárfás](wikipedia://en/614969) independently formulated the [Gyárfás–Sumner conjecture](wikipedia://en/56030165) according to which, for every [tree](wikipedia://en/48560) *T*, the *T*-free graphs are [χ-bounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Χ-bounded).

:::

Sumner earned his doctorate from the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst) in 1970, under the supervision of [David J. Foulis](wikipedia://en/47668601). He is a distinguished professor emeritus at the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina).[^o5]
