# Landauer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Landauer"}

**Landauer** is a surname, originally referring to somebody from [Landau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau). It may refer to:

:::

- Berthold Landauer
- [Gustav Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Landauer) (1870–1919), German anarchist, writer, and critic
- [Karl Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Landauer) (12 October 1887 – 27 January 1945) was a German psychoanalyst
- [Kurt Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Landauer) (1884–1961), German football official
- [M. H. Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._H._Landauer) (1808–1841), German rabbi and writer on Jewish mysticism
- [Robert S. Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_S._Landauer) (1924–2004), American scientist specializing in radiation measurement
- [Rolf Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Landauer) (1927–1999), German-American physicist
- [Samuel Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Landauer) (1846–1937), German orientalist and librarian
- [Thomas Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Landauer) (1932–2014), American professor of psychology
- Walter Landauer (1910–1983), Austrian pianist best known as half of the [Rawicz and Landauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawicz_and_Landauer) piano duo
