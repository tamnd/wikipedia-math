# Ann S. Almgren

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ann S. Almgren"}

**Ann S. Almgren** is an American applied mathematician who works as a senior scientist and group leader of the Center for Computational Sciences and Engineering at the [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_National_Laboratory). Her primary research interests are in computational algorithms for solving [PDE](wikipedia://en/52564)'s for fluid dynamics in a variety of application areas. Her current projects include the development and implementation of new multiphysics algorithms in high-resolution adaptive mesh codes that are designed for the latest multicore architectures.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Almgren is the daughter of mathematician [Frederick J. Almgren, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_J._Almgren,_Jr.) and his first wife, Beverly Stewart.[^o5] She earned a bachelor's degree in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1984 and a master's degree in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1987. Her doctoral degree in mechanical engineering was completed in 1991 at UC Berkeley under the direction of [Phillip Colella](wikipedia://en/24924923) with a dissertation titled *A Fast Adaptive Vortex Method in Three Dimensions*.[^o6] After visiting the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), she joined the applied mathematics group of the [Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Livermore_National_Laboratory) in 1992, and moved to the [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Berkeley_National_Laboratory) in 1996.[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2015 she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for contributions to the development of numerical methods for fluid dynamics and applying them to large-scale scientific and engineering problems."[^o9] She also serves on the editorial boards of SIREV[^o10] and CAMCoS[^o11]
