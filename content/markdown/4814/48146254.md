# Kunizo Yoneyama

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kunizo Yoneyama"}

**Kunizō Yoneyama** was a Japanese mathematician at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) working in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). In 1917, he published the construction of the [Lakes of Wada](wikipedia://en/5774599), which he named after his teacher [Takeo Wada](wikipedia://en/31487590), to whom he credited the discovery.[^o2]

:::
