# Jessica Fintzen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jessica Fintzen"}

*Image: Jessica Fintzen MFO 2016.jpg.* Fintzen at the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669), 2016

**Jessica Fintzen** is a German mathematician whose research concerns the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) over the [$p$-adic numbers](wikipedia://en/51423), with connections to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905). She is a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fintzen competed for Germany in the 2008 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), earning a bronze medal, and earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Jacobs University Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobs_University_Bremen) in 2011. She went to [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) for graduate study in mathematics, completing her Ph.D. in 2016. Her dissertation concerned the [Moy–Prasad filtration](wikipedia://en/63638453) and was supervised by [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863).

After postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), she became an assistant professor of mathematics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) and was promoted to full professor there in 2022. She has also been Royal Society University Research Fellow and Lecturer at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) since 2020. In 2022 she became a full professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Fintzen won the 2018 Friedrich Hirzebruch Dissertation prize of the [German Academic Scholarship Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studienstiftung) and Theodor Pfizer Foundation, and the 2018 [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Dissertation Prize. She was named as a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow) in 2021. In 2022, Fintzen won the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913), "for her groundbreaking work in representation theory, in particular as it relates to number theory via the (local) Langlands program". In 2024, she was awarded the [Cole Prize in Algebra](wikipedia://en/1372177),[^o4] and EMS Prize "for her transformative work on the representation theory of p-adic groups, in particular for her spectacular proof that Yu’s construction of supercuspidal representations is exhaustive*".*[^o5]
