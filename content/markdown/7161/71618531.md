# Institute of Statistics and Economic Studies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Statistics and Economic Studies"}

Institut de la statistique et des études économiques The **Institute of Statistics and Economic Studies - New Caledonia** (ISEE-NC) (Institut de la Statistique et des Études Économiques-Nouvelle-Calédonie) is a public institution in [New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Caledonia) that collects, produces and analyzes [statistical information](wikipedia://en/26685).
