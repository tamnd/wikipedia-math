# HP-17B

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="HP-17B"}

**HP-17B** is an [algebraic entry](wikipedia://en/199014) [financial and business calculator](wikipedia://en/28598560) manufactured by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard), introduced on 4 January 1988 along with the [HP-19B](wikipedia://en/30220535), [HP-27S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP-27S) and the [HP-28S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP-28S). It was a simplified business model, like the 19B. There were two versions, the US one working in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) only, and the international one with a choice of six languages (English, [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language), and [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language)).

:::

## HP-17B {#HP-17B}

HP-17B code name was *Trader* and it belonged to the Pioneer series of Hewlett-Packard calculators.[^o5] It had a 131×16 [LCD dot matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dot_matrix_display), 22×2 characters, menu-driven display,[^o6] used a [Saturn processor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_%28microprocessor%29) and had a memory of 8000 [bytes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bytes), of which 6750 bytes were available to the user for variable and equation storage. The HP-17B had a clock with alarm that allowed for basic agenda capabilities, as well an [infrared port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_port) for printing to some Hewlett-Packard infrared [printers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Printer_%28computing%29).

## HP 17BII {#HP_17BII}

The 17B was replaced by the **HP 17BII** (F1638A) (code name *Trader II*) in January 1990, which added [RPN](wikipedia://en/26513) entry.

## HP 17bII+ {#HP_17bII+}

The 17BII was replaced by the **HP 17bII+** in 2003. Two significantly different case variants of the 17bII+ exist. The newer 17bII+ (F2234A), introduced in 2007, with [Sunplus Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunplus) [SPLB31A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunplus_SPLB31A) CPU was developed and is manufactured by [Kinpo Electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinpo_Electronics).[^o9]
