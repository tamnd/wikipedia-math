# Sample matrix inversion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sample matrix inversion"}

**Sample matrix inversion** (or **direct matrix inversion**) is an [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) that estimates weights of an array ([adaptive filter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_filter)) by replacing the [correlation matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correlation_matrix) $R$ with its estimate. Using $K$ $N$-dimensional samples $X_1, X_2,\dots,X_K$, an unbiased estimate of $R_{X}$, the $N \times N$ correlation matrix of the array signals, may be obtained by means of a simple [averaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Averaging) scheme:

:::

$$\hat{R}_{X} = \frac{1}{K} \sum\limits_{k=1}^K X_k X^H_k,$$

where $H$ is the [conjugate transpose](wikipedia://en/217548). The expression of the theoretically optimal weights requires the [inverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_matrix) of $R_{X}$, and the inverse of the estimates matrix is then used for finding estimated optimal weights.
