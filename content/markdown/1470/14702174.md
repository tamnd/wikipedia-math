# OxMetrics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="OxMetrics"}

**OxMetrics** is an econometric software including the [Ox programming language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ox_programming_language) for [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), developed by Jurgen Doornik and [David Hendry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hendry). OxMetrics originates from PcGive, one of the first econometric software for personal computers, initiated by David Hendry in the 1980s at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).

:::

OxMetrics builds on the [Ox programming language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ox_programming_language) of Jurgen Doornik developed at [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). (Renfro 2004) describes the history of econometric software packages.

OxMetrics is a family of software packages for the [econometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Econometric) and [financial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial) analysis of [time series](wikipedia://en/406624), [forecasting](wikipedia://en/246074), econometric [model selection](wikipedia://en/3664073) and for the statistical analysis of [cross-sectional data](wikipedia://en/3297700) and [panel data](wikipedia://en/2499813).

The main modules apart from *PcGive* for dynamic econometric models (ARDL, VAR, GARCH, Switching, Autometrics), panel data models (DPD), limited dependent models, are *STAMP* for structural [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) modelling, "SsfPack" for [State space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_space) methods and "G@RCH" for financial [volatility](wikipedia://en/11930108) modelling. (Hendry Nielsen 2007) present many empirical examples in PcGive for OxMetrics in their [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) textbook. (Durbin Koopman 2012) give modern examples in their [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis) textbook.
