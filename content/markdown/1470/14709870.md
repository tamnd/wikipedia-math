# Nicoteles of Cyrene

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicoteles of Cyrene"}

**Nicoteles of Cyrene** (Νικοτέλης ὁ Κυρηναῖος) (c. 250 BC) was a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from [Cyrene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrene,_Libya).

:::

He is mentioned in the preface to Book 4 of the *Conics* of [Apollonius](wikipedia://en/257242), as criticising [Conon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conon_of_Samos) concerning the maximum number of points with which a [conic section](wikipedia://en/19008673) can meet another conic section. Apollonius states that Nicoteles claimed that the case in which a conic section meets opposite sections could be solved, but had not demonstrated how.[^o2]

It is possible that Nicoteles could be a misspelling of [Nicomedes](wikipedia://en/12434756).
