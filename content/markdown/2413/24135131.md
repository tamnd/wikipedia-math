# Aizik Volpert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aizik Volpert"}

**Aizik Isaakovich Vol'pert** (Айзик Исаакович Вольперт; 5 June 1923[^o3][^o4] – January 2006) (the family name is also transliterated as **[Volpert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volpert)**[^o6] or **Wolpert**[^o7]) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [chemical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineer)[^o8] working in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [functions of bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673) and [chemical kinetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_kinetics).

:::

## Life and academic career {#Life_and_academic_career}

Vol'pert graduated from [Lviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv_University) in 1951, earning the [candidate of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_science) degree and the [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent#Central_and_Eastern_Europe) title respectively in 1954 and 1956 from the same university:[^o3] from 1951 on he worked at the [Lviv Industrial Forestry Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_National_Forestry_University).[^o3] In 1961 he became [senior research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow#Russian_Federation)[^o10] while 1962 he earned the "[doktor nauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_nauk)"[^o4] degree from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). In the 1970s–1980s A. I. Volpert became one of the leaders of the Russian Mathematical Chemistry scientific community.[^o11] He finally joined [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion)’s Faculty of Mathematics in 1993,[^o5] doing his [Aliyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) in 1994.[^o12]

## Work {#Work}

### Index theory and elliptic boundary problems {#Index_theory_and_elliptic_boundary_problems}

Vol'pert developed an effective [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) for calculating the index of an elliptic problem before the [Atiyah-Singer index theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atiyah-Singer_index_theorem) appeared:[^o15] He was also the first to show that the index of a singular matrix operator can be different from zero.[^o16]

### Functions of bounded variation {#Functions_of_bounded_variation}

He was one of the leading contributors to the theory of [*BV*-functions](wikipedia://en/330673): he introduced the concept of [functional superposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_superposition), which enabled him to construct a calculus for such functions and applying it in the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o18] Precisely, given a [continuously differentiable function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuously_differentiable_function) $f:\mathbb{R}^p \to \mathbb{R}$ and a function of [bounded variation](wikipedia://en/330673) $\boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol x)= (u_1(\boldsymbol{x}), \ldots , u_p(\boldsymbol{x}))$ with $\boldsymbol{x}\in \mathbb R^n$ and $n\ge 1$, he proves that $f\circ \boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol{x}) = f(\boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol{x}))$ is again a function of bounded variation and the following [chain rule](wikipedia://en/6113) [formula](wikipedia://en/164040) holds:[^o19]

$$\frac{\partial f(\boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol{x}))}{\partial x_i}=\sum_{k=1}^p\frac{\partial\bar{f}(\boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol{x}))}{\partial u_k}\frac{\partial{u_k(\boldsymbol{x})}}{\partial x_i} \qquad\forall i=1,\ldots,n$$

where ${\bar f}(\boldsymbol{u}(\boldsymbol{x}))$ is the already cited functional superposition of $f$ and $\boldsymbol u$. By using his results, it is easy to prove that functions of bounded variation form an [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_%28ring_theory%29) of [discontinuous functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discontinuous_function): in particular, using his calculus for $n=1$, it is possible to define the product $H\cdot \delta$ of the [Heaviside step function](wikipedia://en/87299) $H(x)$ and the [Dirac distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_distribution) $\delta(x)$ in one [variable](wikipedia://en/3728109).[^o20]

### Chemical kinetics {#Chemical_kinetics}

His work on chemical kinetics and [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering) led him to define and study [differential equations on graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equation_on_a_graph).[^o22]
