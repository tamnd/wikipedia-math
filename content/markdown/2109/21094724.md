# Top (algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Top"}

In the context of a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) *M* over a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R*, the **top** of *M* is the largest [semisimple](wikipedia://en/1001361) [quotient module](wikipedia://en/364820) of *M* if it exists.

:::

For finite-dimensional *k*-algebras (*k* a field) R, if rad(*M*) denotes the intersection of all proper [maximal submodules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximal_submodule) of *M* (the [radical of the module](wikipedia://en/9379691)), then the top of *M* is *M*/rad(*M*). In the case of local rings with maximal ideal *P*, the top of *M* is *M*/*PM*. In general if *R* is a [semilocal ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semilocal_ring) (=semi-artinian ring), that is, if *R*/Rad(*R*) is an [Artinian ring](wikipedia://en/489273), where Rad(*R*) is the [Jacobson radical](wikipedia://en/57656) of *R*, then *M*/rad(*M*) is a semisimple module and is the top of *M*. This includes the cases of local rings and finite dimensional algebras over fields.
