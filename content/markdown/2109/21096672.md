# Raphael Levi Hannover

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raphael Levi Hannover"}

*Image: RLHannover.jpg.* Raphael Levi Hannover

**Raphael Levi Hannover** (1685 – May 17, 1779) was a German [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer). The son of Jacob Joseph, Hannover was born at [Weikersheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weikersheim), [Franconia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franconia) in 1685. He was educated at the [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) school of Hanover and at the yeshivah of [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main), and became bookkeeper in the firm of [Simon Wolf Oppenheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Wolf_Oppenheimer) in [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover).[^o2] Here he attracted the attention of [Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Leibniz), and for a number of years was one of his most distinguished pupils and lived by him for three years (including last secretary),[^o3][^o4] and afterward teacher of mathematics, astronomy, and natural philosophy. He also corresponded with [Moses Mendelssohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Mendelssohn).[^o3]

:::

Raphael Levi Hannover wrote: "Luḥot ha-'Ibbur," astronomical tables for the [Jewish calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_calendar);[^o5] "Tekunat ha-Shamayim," on astronomy and calendar-making, especially commenting on the [Talmudical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talmudical) passages on these topics, with glosses of Moses Tiktin.[^o6] An enlarged revision of the latter work, with two other astronomical works of his, is in manuscript. The "Luḥot ha-'Ibbur" has been published with [M. E. Fürth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meyer_Fürth)'s "Yir'at Shamayim," on Maimonides' "Yad," Ḳiddush ha-Ḥodesh.[^o7] He died in [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover) in 1779.
