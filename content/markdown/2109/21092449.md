# Glen E. Baxter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glen E. Baxter"}

**Glen Earl Baxter** (March 19, 1930 – March 30, 1983) was an American mathematician.

:::

Baxter's fields of research include [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [combinatorial analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_analysis), [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). He is known for the [Baxter strong limit theorem.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baxter's_strong_limit_theorem.) Lately, his 1960 work[^o3] on the derivation of a specific operator identity that later bore his name, the [Rota–Baxter identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota&ndash;Baxter_algebra), and emanated from some of the fundamental results of the famous probabilist [Frank Spitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Spitzer) in [random walk theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_walk_theory) has received attention in fields as remote as [renormalization](wikipedia://en/291453) theory in perturbative [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).[^o4][^o5]

In 1983 the Glen E. Baxter Memorial Fund was established by family and friends at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o6]
