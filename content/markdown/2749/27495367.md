# Sven Erlander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sven Erlander"}

**Sven Bertil Erlander** (25 May 1934 – 13 June 2021)[^o3][^o4] was a Swedish mathematician and academic.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Erlander was the son of [Tage Erlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tage_Erlander), who was the Prime Minister of Sweden from 1946 to 1969. He published several of his father's diaries.[^o6]

He received his PhD in mathematics from [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) in 1968. In 1971 he became a professor in [Optimisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29) at [Linköping University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linköping_University). In Linköping he was [department head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_head) of the Mathematics department from 1973 to 1976, and [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the [Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linköping_Institute_of_Technology) between 1978 and 1983.

In 1983–1995 he was the [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of Linköping University.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

- Member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Engineering_Sciences), 1983
- Honorary Doctorate, [Gdańsk University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gdańsk_University), Poland

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Cost-Minimizing Choice Behavior in Transportation Planning*, monograph, [Springer Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag), 2010.
