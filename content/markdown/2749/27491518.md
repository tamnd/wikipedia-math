# Miscellanea Logica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miscellanea Logica"}

***Miscellanea Logica*** is an [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) based at the [Charles University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University_in_Prague). It is part of [Acta Universitatis Carolinae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Universitatis_Carolinae).[^o2][^o3]

:::
