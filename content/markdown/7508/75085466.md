# John David Maitland Wright

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="John David Maitland Wright"}

**John David Maitland Wright** FRSE (20 May 1942 – 7 August 2023) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specialising mainly in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Wright was born on 20 May 1942, the son of Phyllis (née Harris) and mathematician [Sir Edward Maitland Wright](wikipedia://en/529941). He went to [Aberdeen Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_Grammar_School) and subsequently studied mathematics at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen). [^o6]

Wright graduated with first class honours from University of Aberdeen in 1964. For his postgraduate studies he went to [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford) where he obtained his doctorate in 1967. [^o2] For his thesis on [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), Wright was awarded the Oxford Senior Mathematical Prize and Johnson Prize. [^o9]

## Career {#Career}

In 1965 Wright was appointed to a junior college lectureship at Christ Church and in 1968 became the Leathersellers’ Tutorial Fellow in Mathematics at [St Catherine’s College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Catherine’s_College,_Oxford).[^o9]

In 1971, at the age of 28, he was appointed professor of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading) and later became head of department.[^o9]

Wright was treasurer of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) from 1986 to 1994 and deputy director of the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in Cambridge from 1994 to 1996.[^o6]

In 2004 he was appointed to a Sixth Century Chair at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) which he held until his retirement in 2015. Subsequently he became senior associate [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) of [Christ Church, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford) until 2021.[^o6]

Wright worked mainly in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and related areas, including applications to [quantum theory](wikipedia://en/25202). He published over 130 papers in mathematical journals. [^o9][^o7] A significant part of Wright's research work was on [AW\*-Algebras](wikipedia://en/39435930) and monotone complete C\*-algebras culminating in a book coauthored with Kazuyuki Saito. [^o9] Other notable work include results on [Jordan C\*-Algebras](wikipedia://en/39304879) in 1977 and a solution of the Mackey-Gleason Problem in 1994 (with L.J. Bunce).[^o9]

## Honours {#Honours}

Wright was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1978 [^o6][^o9][^o10] and Foreign (Honorary) Academician of the Società Nazionale di Scienze, Lettere e Arti in Napoli in 1994. [^o9]
