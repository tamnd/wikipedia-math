# Dominic Bezzina

*Image: Dominic Bezzina - Institutum Philosophicum.jpg.* *Institutum Philosophicum* (1823) of Dominic Bezzina

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dominic Bezzina"}

**Dominic Bezzina** (18th/19th centuries) was a minor Maltese philosopher who mainly specialised in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). He also dealt with [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

It seems that Bezzina was born around the mid-18th century. After becoming a priest, he taught [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science) at the Cathedral School at [Mdina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mdina), [Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malta). He was also a Canon at the Bishop's Cathedral Chapter. Bezzina taught at [Mdina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mdina) at least since 1819. One of the courses he delivered concerned [physics](wikipedia://en/22939).

## Extant work {#Extant_work}

Bezzina's only extant work is called ***Institutum Philosophicum*** (Philosophical Teaching).[^o6] It is still in manuscript form,[^o7] and contains 171 back to back folios. Unfortunately, the document is certainly incomplete. The extant manuscript is just the first part of a larger work. It is not known how many further part the whole work contained.

The extant part of the *Institutum* concerns logic. The division is typically that of [Scholasticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholasticism), organised in books, chapters, and articles. Throughout the work Bezzina discusses the nature and conditions of Aristotelian logic, consciousness, and the genesis of ideas.
