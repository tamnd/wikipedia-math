# Pekka Tukia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pekka Tukia"}

**Pekka Pertti Tukia** (born 3 November 1945 in [Pihtipudas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pihtipudas)[^o2]) is a Finnish mathematician[^o3] who does research on [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) and their geometric properties (such as [limit sets](wikipedia://en/5492198)).

:::

Tukia received his PhD in 1972 with thesis advisor Kaarlo Virtanen in Helsinki. Tukia is a professor at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki).

He made substantial contributions to the collective work of about a dozen mathematicians who proved the Seifert fiber space conjecture.[^o5] In 1992 he was an invited speaker with talk *Generalizations of Fuchsian and Kleinian groups* at the European Congress of European Mathematicians in Paris. In 1994 he was an invited speaker with talk *A survey of Möbius groups* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich).
