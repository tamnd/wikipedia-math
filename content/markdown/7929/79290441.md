# Choi Yun Sung

Choi

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Choi Yun Sung"}

**Choi Yun Sung** (; born 1958) is an emeritus professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Pohang University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohang_University_of_Science_and_Technology).[^o5] He became president of the [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326) in 2025.[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Choi graduated from [Kyungnam High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyungnam_High_School) in 1976. Majoring in mathematics, he completed his undergrad at Seoul National University. With [Leopoldo Nachbin](wikipedia://en/19050267) as his academic advisor, Choi focused on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), receiving a M.A. and Ph.D. from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in 1984 and 1986, respectively.[^o8][^o9]

## Career {#Career}

After completing his doctorate, he taught at [Pusan National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pusan_National_University) for two years before going to [Pohang University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohang_University_of_Science_and_Technology) (POSTECH)[^o11] where he taught for at least 35 years.[^o12][^o13] In additional to his role as professor, he was also head of the Department of Mathematics and director of the POSTECH Mathematics Institute.[^o14] He has organized and held the ILJU School of Mathematics-Banach Spaces and Related Topics annually since 2009.[^o15][^o16]

Choi has been a chief editor of the *[Journal of Korean Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Mathematical_Society)*[^o17][^o18] and an editor of *Communications of the Korean Mathematical Society*.

In 2025, Choi became president of [National Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/70473326).[^o19][^o20]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 2023: Education Award, [Korean Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Mathematical_Society)[^o22][^o23]
- 2015: Academic Award, Korean Mathematical Society[^o24][^o25]
