# Daniel Yekutieli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Yekutieli"}

**Daniel Yekutieli** is an Israeli statistician and professor at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). He is one of the leading researchers in the field of applied statistics in Israel. He is known for the creation of the [false coverage rate](wikipedia://en/36643952) and for his work on the [false discovery rate](wikipedia://en/4574271) with [Yoav Benjamini](wikipedia://en/36596077), including the [Benjamini–Yekutieli (BY) procedure](wikipedia://en/4574271#Benjamini–Yekutieli_procedure) for controlling it.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Yekutieli was born in [Rehovot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehovot), Israel. His father, Gideon Yekutieli, was the first nuclear physicist in Israel and a professor at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science). His paternal grandfather was the founder of the [Maccabiah Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabiah_Games), [Yosef Yekutieli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yosef_Yekutieli). His great-grandfather was [Akiva Aryeh Weiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiva_Aryeh_Weiss), one of the founders of *[Ahuzat Bayit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv)* (modern-day Tel Aviv).

Yekutieli received his BA in Mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1992, and went on to earn his MA and PhD in Applied Statistics from Tel Aviv University. His PhD thesis, supervised by [Yoav Benjamini](wikipedia://en/36596077) and completed in 2002, first introduced the concept of the [false coverage rate](wikipedia://en/36643952).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Together with Yoav Benjamini and [Ruth Heller](wikipedia://en/79294753), he won the [Rousseeuw Prize for Statistics](wikipedia://en/72172766) in 2024 "for the pioneering work on the [false discovery rate](wikipedia://en/4574271) and methods to control it".[^o6][^o7]

He was elected fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2020.[^o8]
