# Zico Kolter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zico Kolter"}

**Jeremy Zico Kolter** is a professor at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) and director of its machine learning department.[^o3][^o4][^o5] He focuses primarily on [AI safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety) research.[^o4] He is a co-founder and senior advisor of Gray Swan AI, an AI safety and security company.[^o6] In 2024, he was appointed to the board of directors for [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI), and became chair of its safety and security committee.[^o4][^o6] In 2025, he was named as a recipient of funding from [Schmidt Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_Sciences) AI safety science program.[^o7]

:::

Kolter earned his PhD in computer science at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o8][^o5] He joined the CMU faculty in 2012.[^o5] His other corporate positions have included chief data scientist at [C3.ai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C3.ai) and chief expert at Bosch Center for AI.[^o5] At CMU, he has worked on projects such as finding ways to automate assessment of large language model safety.[^o9]
