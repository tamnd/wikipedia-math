# List of Russian mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Russian mathematicians"}

*Image: The Soviet Union 1961 CPA 2627 stamp (Publicizing Communist labor teams in their efforts for labor. education and relaxation. Workers studying mathematics).jpg.* 1961 Soviet stamp depicting "Workers studying mathematics"

This **list of [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902)** includes the famous mathematicians from the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation).

:::

## Alphabetical list {#Alphabetical_list}

### A {#A}

*Image: Vladimir Arnold-1.jpg.* **[Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490)**

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Georgy Adelson-Velsky"}

- **[Georgy Adelson-Velsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgy_Adelson-Velsky)**, inventor of [AVL tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AVL_tree) algorithm, developer of [Kaissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaissa), the first world computer chess champion

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sergei Adian"}

- **[Sergei Adian](wikipedia://en/5028401)**, known for his work in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), especially on the [Burnside problem](wikipedia://en/151452)

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Aleksandr Aleksandrov"}

- **[Aleksandr Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov)**, developer of [CAT(k) space](wikipedia://en/12941912) and [Alexandrov's uniqueness theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandrov's_uniqueness_theorem) in geometry

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pavel Alexandrov"}

- **[Pavel Alexandrov](wikipedia://en/576245)**, author of the [Alexandroff compactification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandroff_compactification) and the [Alexandrov topology](wikipedia://en/542198)

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Dmitri Anosov"}

- **[Dmitri Anosov](wikipedia://en/2320194)**, developed [Anosov diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/2320078)

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Vladimir Arnold"}

- **[Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490)**, an author of the [Kolmogorov–Arnold–Moser theorem](wikipedia://en/16972) in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), solved [Hilbert's 13th problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_13th_problem), raised the [ADE classification](wikipedia://en/648042) and [Arnold's rouble problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold's_rouble_problem)

:::

### B {#B}

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Alexander Beilinson"}

- **[Alexander Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613)**, influential mathematician in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sergey Bernstein"}

- **[Sergey Bernstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Bernstein)**, developed the [Bernstein polynomial](wikipedia://en/340136), [Bernstein's theorem](wikipedia://en/3526626) and [Bernstein inequalities in probability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernstein_inequalities_in_probability_theory)

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nikolay Bogolyubov"}

- **[Nikolay Bogolyubov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Bogolyubov)**, mathematician and theoretical physicist, author of the [edge-of-the-wedge theorem](wikipedia://en/1657665), [Krylov–Bogolyubov theorem](wikipedia://en/7635266), [describing function](wikipedia://en/3877767) and multiple important contributions to [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202)

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Vladimir Berkovich"}

- **[Vladimir Berkovich](wikipedia://en/34421039)**, developed [Berkovich spaces](wikipedia://en/34420832)

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Viktor Bunyakovsky"}

- **[Viktor Bunyakovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Bunyakovsky)**, noted for his work in theoretical mechanics and number theory, and is credited with an early discovery of the [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality](wikipedia://en/38128)

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Leonid Berlyand"}

- **[Leonid Berlyand](wikipedia://en/55895288)**, [PDE](wikipedia://en/52564) theorist, worked on [asymptotic homogenization](wikipedia://en/23852047) methods, [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) winner

:::

### C {#C}

*Image: Chebyshev.jpg.* **[Chebyshev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev)**

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Georg Cantor"}

- **[Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216)**, inventor of [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). Cantor was born into the Russian Empire, moving to Saxony with his family at age 11.

:::

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sergey Chaplygin"}

- **[Sergey Chaplygin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Alexeyevich_Chaplygin)**, author of [Chaplygin's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplygin's_equation) important in [aerodynamics](wikipedia://en/2819) and notion of [Chaplygin gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplygin_gas).

:::

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Nikolai Chebotaryov"}

- **[Nikolai Chebotaryov](wikipedia://en/3033696)**, author of [Chebotarev's density theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebotarev's_density_theorem)

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="Pafnuti Chebyshev"}

- **[Pafnuti Chebyshev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pafnuti_Chebyshev)**, prominent tutor and founding father of Russian mathematics, contributed to [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), author of the [Chebyshev's inequality](wikipedia://en/156533), [Chebyshev distance](wikipedia://en/1503963), [Chebyshev function](wikipedia://en/5793598), [Chebyshev equation](wikipedia://en/3760497) etc.

:::

::: {#o22 .entry cue=list-entry name="Sergei Chernikov"}

- **[Sergei Chernikov](wikipedia://en/51015113)**, significant contributor to both infinite group theory (developer of Chernikov groups), and linear programming.

:::

::: {#o26 .entry cue=list-entry name="Eugene Dynkin"}

- **[Eugene Dynkin](wikipedia://en/1351540)**, developed [Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/277109), [Doob–Dynkin lemma](wikipedia://en/27057803) and [Dynkin system](wikipedia://en/2549191) in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934)

:::

::: {#o28 .entry cue=list-entry name="Dmitri Egorov"}
