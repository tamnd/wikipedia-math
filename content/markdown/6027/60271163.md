# Peter Braam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Braam"}

**Peter J. Braam** is a Dutch-American computer scientist, mathematician and entrepreneur known for his contributions to large-scale computing systems. He has held academic positions at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah), the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University). Braam is recognized for creating the Lustre parallel file system, used in high-performance computing (HPC) environments.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Braam was born in [Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht), Netherlands. He completed his undergraduate studies at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) in 1984. He pursued doctoral research under the supervision of [Sir Michael Atiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Michael_Atiyah) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), earning his DPhil in 1987 with a thesis titled *Magnetic Monopoles and Hyperbolic Three-manifolds*.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Following his doctorate, Braam became a Junior Research Fellow at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford), and a C&C Huygens Fellow of the Netherlands Science Foundation.[^o7]

From 2013 to 2018, Braam has collaborated with the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) on the [Square Kilometre Array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_Kilometre_Array) radio telescope project, focusing on data-intensive computing challenges. He has also served as a consultant to partners in the founding committee of Horizon 2020[^o8] and the [European Processor Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Processor_Initiative).

At the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) he has been a Visiting Professor in the Department of Physics since 2019, and a visiting research fellow in mathematics since 2025, and a visiting professor of computer science at [Waseda University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waseda_University) since 2022.

## Philanthrophy {#Philanthrophy}

Braam has endowed the Peter J. Braam Junior Research Fellowship and Graduate Scholarship in Human Wellbeing at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford). These initiatives support early-career researchers and contribute to studies aimed at improving human wellbeing.[^o7]
