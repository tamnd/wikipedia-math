# Richard Elman (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Elman"}

**Richard Steven Elman** (born 21 March 1945) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), known for his work in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923). He received his Ph.D. at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1972, under the supervision of [Tsit Yuen Lam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsit_Yuen_Lam).[^o2]

:::

He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3] Among his collaborators are Nikita Karpenko and [Alexander Merkurjev](wikipedia://en/31902178).
