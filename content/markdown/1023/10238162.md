# Mark Goresky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mark Goresky"}

**Robert Mark Goresky** is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who invented [intersection homology](wikipedia://en/705749) with his advisor and life partner [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Goresky received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1976. His thesis, titled *Geometric Cohomology and Homology of Stratified Objects*, was written under the direction of MacPherson. Many of the results in his thesis were published in 1981 by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He has taught at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in Vancouver, and [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University).

## Awards {#Awards}

Goresky received a Sloan Research Fellowship in 1981.[^o5] He received the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) in 1984[^o6] and the [Jeffery-Williams Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffery-Williams_Prize) in 1996.[^o7][^o8] In 2002, Goresky and MacPherson were jointly awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Seminal Contribution to Research by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9][^o10] In 2012 Goresky became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]

## Personal {#Personal}

Goresky's PhD advisor, [Robert D. MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121), later became his life partner. Their discovery of intersection homology made "both of them famous."[^o13] After the collapse of the Soviet Union, they were instrumental in channeling aid to Russian mathematicians, especially many who had to hide their sexuality.[^o13]
