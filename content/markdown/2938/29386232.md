# Studies in Logic, Grammar and Rhetoric

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Studies in Logic, Grammar and Rhetoric"}

***Studies in Logic, Grammar and Rhetoric*** is a journal of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), publishing articles of diverse streams in English.[^o3]

:::

The journal is abstracted and indexed by [DOAJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DOAJ),[^o4] [ERIH PLUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ERIH_PLUS), [SCOPUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCOPUS), CEJSH (The Central European Journal of Social Sciences and Humanities) e [Index Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_Copernicus).[^o5]

As of 2024, it has registered a [H-index](wikipedia://en/3604693) of 17.[^o6]
