# Mojżesz David Kirszbraun

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mojżesz David Kirszbraun"}

**Mojżesz David Kirszbraun** (1903–1942)[^o3] was a Polish mathematician, mostly known for the [Kirszbraun theorem](wikipedia://en/472932) on extensions of Lipschitz maps. This theorem appears in his master's thesis, defended in Warsaw in 1930.

:::

Kirszbraun finished school in 1922. Together with his classmate [Adolf Lindenbaum](wikipedia://en/1029330), he continued his studies in [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University). Later, he worked as an actuary in an insurance company. He died in a ghetto in 1942.
