# Bull graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bull graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **bull graph** is a [planar](wikipedia://en/24314) [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 5 vertices and 5 edges, in the form of a triangle with two disjoint pendant edges.

:::

It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, radius 2, diameter 3 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3. It is also a [self-complementary graph](wikipedia://en/15093884), a [block graph](wikipedia://en/29106883), a [split graph](wikipedia://en/9928681), an [interval graph](wikipedia://en/363423), a [claw-free graph](wikipedia://en/12415907), a 1-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 1-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

## Bull-free graphs {#Bull-free_graphs}

A graph is bull-free if it has no bull as an [induced subgraph](wikipedia://en/1655188). The [triangle-free graphs](wikipedia://en/7667996) are bull-free graphs, since every bull contains a triangle. The [strong perfect graph theorem](wikipedia://en/744171) was proven for bull-free graphs long before its proof for general graphs,[^o5] and a [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time) recognition algorithm for Bull-free perfect graphs is known.[^o6]

[Maria Chudnovsky](wikipedia://en/19731900) and [Shmuel Safra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shmuel_Safra) have studied bull-free graphs more generally, showing that any such graph must have either a large [clique](wikipedia://en/524466) or a large [independent set](wikipedia://en/524501) (that is, the [Erdős–Hajnal conjecture](wikipedia://en/42585591) holds for the bull graph),[^o7] and developing a general structure theory for these graphs.[^o8][^o9][^o10]

## Chromatic and characteristic polynomial {#Chromatic_and_characteristic_polynomial}

*Image: Chromatically equivalent graphs.svg.* The three graphs with a [chromatic polynomial](wikipedia://en/1460126) equal to $(x-2)(x-1)^3x$.

The [chromatic polynomial](wikipedia://en/1460126) of the bull graph is $(x-2)(x-1)^3x$. Two other graphs are chromatically equivalent to the bull graph.

Its [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) is $-x(x^2-x-3)(x^2+x-1)$.

Its [Tutte polynomial](wikipedia://en/3892303) is $x^4+x^3+x^2y$.
