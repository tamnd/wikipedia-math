# Agustín Rayo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Agustín Rayo"}

Rayo

*Image: Agustin Rayo MIT.jpg.* Agustín Rayo

**Agustín Rayo Fierro** (; born 1973) is a Mexican philosopher of [logic](wikipedia://en/21368888), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), and [language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language). He is the dean of the [MIT School of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_School_of_Humanities,_Arts,_and_Social_Sciences) and a professor of philosophy. He was the first to define [Rayo's number](wikipedia://en/42297252), which was named after him as a result.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rayo was raised in [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City). He completed a bachelor's degree at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) in 1996. Rayo earned a Ph.D. at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2001. He was a [postdoctoral researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).[^o3]

Rayo was an assistant professor of philosophy at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). In 2005, he joined the [MIT School of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_School_of_Humanities,_Arts,_and_Social_Sciences). Rayo was the associate dean from 2016 to 2019. He succeeded [Melissa Nobles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Nobles) in 2021 as interim dean.[^o3]

Rayo was elected to the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 2018. His book, *On the Brink of Paradox* (2019) won the 2020 [PROSE Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PROSE_Awards) for best textbook in the humanities, mathematics and sciences.[^o3]
