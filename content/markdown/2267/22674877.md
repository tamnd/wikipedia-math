# Paul Chester Kainen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Chester Kainen"}

**Paul Chester Kainen** is an American mathematician, an adjunct associate professor of mathematics, and the director of the Lab for Visual Mathematics at [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University).[^o2] Kainen is the coauthor of a popular book on the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949)[^n1] and is also known for his work on [book embeddings](wikipedia://en/3987703) of [graphs](wikipedia://en/12401).[^n2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kainen received his Bachelor of Arts degree from George Washington University in 1966 and was awarded the [Ruggles Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruggles_Prize) for Excellence in Mathematics. He went on to get his Ph.D. from Cornell University in 1970 with [Peter Hilton](wikipedia://en/8951286) as his thesis advisor. Kainen's father was the American artist [Jacob Kainen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Kainen).
