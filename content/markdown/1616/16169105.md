# Steven Kleiman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Steven Kleiman"}

**Steven Lawrence Kleiman** (born March 31, 1942) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Kleiman is a professor emeritus of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Born in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), he did his undergraduate studies at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT). He received his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1965, after studying there with [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352) and [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594), and joined the MIT faculty in 1969.[^o4] Kleiman held a prestigious NATO Postdoctoral Fellowship (1966–1967), [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) (1968), and [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) (1979).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Kleiman is known for his work in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He has made seminal contributions in [motivic cohomology](wikipedia://en/809314), [moduli theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_theory), [intersection theory](wikipedia://en/3148602) and [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1989 the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) awarded him an honorary doctorate[^o7] and in May 2002 the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) hosted a conference in honor of his 60th birthday and elected him as a foreign member.[^o8] In 1992 Kleiman was elected foreign member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] He was an invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematics at Nice in 1970.[^o10]
