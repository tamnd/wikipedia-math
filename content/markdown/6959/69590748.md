# Pál Medgyessy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pál Medgyessy"}

Medgyessy Pál **Pál Medgyessy** (October 19, 1919, in [Egercsehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egercsehi) – October 8, 1977, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) was a mathematician, [Doctor of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) (1973).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated at the [University of Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) as a student of Eötvös József Collegium. He started his career as a trainee at the Institute of Medical Physics at the [University of Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen). Due to his illness, his scientific work was slow to develop.

He read the [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin)-language classics in the original, spoke [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language) well, and during his captivity he also mastered [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language), so his skills were utilised as an interpreter during the war. He published some of his books in English.[^o3]

He was an aspirant to the candidate's degree at the Mathematical Research Institute of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences), a student of [Alfréd Rényi](wikipedia://en/614988)’s. From 1955 on he was a research associate and scientific advisor at the Institute. He received his doctorate in mathematics from the HAS in 1973.

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

His main research interests were [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), and their applications; his results on numerical methods are also remarkable. Many of his papers are in the fields of [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), and [spectroscopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectroscopy). His results on various [non-electronic calculators and equipment](wikipedia://en/432897) are noteworthy.

He was an outstanding expert in [bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliography), credited with the modernization of the mathematics section of the [decimal classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewey_Decimal_Classification) and the preparation of several bibliographies, including those of the works of [Frigyes Riesz](wikipedia://en/1073126) and [Alfréd Rényi](wikipedia://en/614988).

His works on the history of science deal with ancient [Babylonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylonia) and [Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egypt) science and the history of [Chinese mathematics](wikipedia://en/1524543) and [astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_astrology).
