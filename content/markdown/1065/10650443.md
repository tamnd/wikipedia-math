# Preimage theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Preimage theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly in the field of [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), the **preimage theorem** is a variation of the [implicit function theorem](wikipedia://en/581005) concerning the [preimage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_image) of particular points in a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) under the action of a [smooth map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_map).[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Statement of Theorem"}

## Statement of Theorem {#Statement_of_Theorem}

*Definition.* Let $f : X \to Y$ be a smooth map between manifolds. We say that a point $y \in Y$ is a *regular value of* $f$ if for all $x \in f^{-1}(y)$ the map $d f_x: T_x X \to T_y Y$ is [surjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surjective_map). Here, $T_x X$ and $T_y Y$ are the [tangent spaces](wikipedia://en/30807) of $X$ and $Y$ at the points $x$ and $y.$

*Theorem.* Let $f: X \to Y$ be a smooth map, and let $y \in Y$ be a regular value of $f.$ Then $f^{-1}(y)$ is a submanifold of $X.$ If $y \in \text{im}(f),$ then the [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) of $f^{-1}(y)$ is equal to the dimension of $Y.$ Also, the [tangent space](wikipedia://en/30807) of $f^{-1}(y)$ at $x$ is equal to $\ker(df_x).$

There is also a complex version of this theorem:[^o6]

*Theorem.* Let $X^n$ and $Y^m$ be two [complex manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_manifolds) of complex dimensions $n > m.$ Let $g : X \to Y$ be a [holomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic) map and let $y \in \text{im}(g)$ be such that $\text{rank}(dg_x) = m$ for all $x \in g^{-1}(y).$ Then $g^{-1}(y)$ is a complex submanifold of $X$ of complex dimension $n - m.$

:::
