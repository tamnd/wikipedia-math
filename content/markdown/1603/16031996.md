# St. Petersburg Department of Steklov Mathematical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="St. Petersburg Department of Steklov Mathematical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences"}

*Image: Lomi.jpg.* POMI office building at 27, [Fontanka River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fontanka) embankment, [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg)

The **St. Petersburg Department of Steklov Institute of Mathematics of the Russian Academy of Sciences** (Санкт-Петербургское отделение Математического института им. В. А. Стеклова РАН, abbreviated ПОМИ (**PDMI**) for "Петербургское отделение Математического института", Petersburg Department of the Mathematical Institute; PDMI) is a mathematical research institute in [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg), part of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). Until 1992 it was known as **Leningrad Department of Steklov Institute of Mathematics of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences)** (ЛОМИ, LOMI).

:::

The name of the institution is a historical tradition and since 1995 it has no subordination to the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494).[^o2]

The institute was established in 1940 as a department of the Steklov Institute and is named after [Vladimir Andreevich Steklov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Andreevich_Steklov), a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)/[Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [mechanician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist).[^o2]
