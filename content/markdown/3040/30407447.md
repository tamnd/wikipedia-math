# Alfred Loewy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Loewy"}

*Image: Alfred Loewy.jpg.* Alfred Loewy

**Alfred Loewy** (20 June 1873 – 25 January 1935) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). [Loewy rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loewy_ring), [Loewy length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loewy_length), [Loewy decomposition](wikipedia://en/41390065) and [Loewy series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loewy_series) are named after him.

:::

His graduate students included [Wolfgang Krull](wikipedia://en/964328) and [Friedrich Karl Schmidt](wikipedia://en/36084772).
