# Pham Huu Tiep

*Image: Pham Tiep Oberwolfach 2020.jpg.* Pham Tiep at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2020

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pham Huu Tiep"}

**Pham Huu Tiep** (Phạm Hữu Tiệp) is a [Vietnamese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He is currently a Joshua Barlaz Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

:::

Pham Tiep graduated from [Chu Văn An High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chu_Văn_An_High_School_%28Hanoi%29), and received a silver medal at the [IMO](wikipedia://en/82208) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) in 1979.[^o2] He received his Ph.D. at [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University) in 1988 under supervision of [Alexei Kostrikin](wikipedia://en/31810848).[^o3] He gave an invited talk at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) in [2018](wikipedia://en/47738065#2018,_Rio_de_Janeiro). He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), a [Clay Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Institute) Senior Scholar, and a [Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) Fellow.

Pham was the fifth Vietnamese mathematician invited to speak at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065), following [Frédéric Pham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frédéric_Pham) [(1970)](wikipedia://en/47738065#1970,_Nice), [Duong Hong Phong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duong_Hong_Phong) [(1994)](wikipedia://en/47738065#1994,_Zürich), [Ngô Bảo Châu](wikipedia://en/11133659) [(2006,2010)](wikipedia://en/47738065#2006,_Madrid) and [Van H. Vu](wikipedia://en/21419416)[(2014)](wikipedia://en/47738065#2014,_Seoul).

Pham was a member of the 2010 collaboration which completed the proof of [Ore's conjecture](wikipedia://en/585271#Ore's_conjecture).

In a September 2024 paper, Pham, along with Gunter Malle, Gabriel Navarro and Amanda Schaeffer Fry, proved[^o4] [Brauer's height zero conjecture](wikipedia://en/78200945) on the modular representation theory of [Brauer blocks](wikipedia://en/1110685) and their [defect groups](wikipedia://en/1110685#Defect_groups).

Also in 2024,[^o5] Pham, along with Michael Larsen and others, derived uniform character bounds for various finite classical groups, leading to substantial progress on Thompson's conjecture[^o6] that each finite non-abelian simple group $G = C^2$ for some [conjugacy class](wikipedia://en/49176) $C \subseteq G$.

- 2016: Huu Tiep, Pham. (2016). "The \\alpha-invariant and Thompson's conjecture". *Forum of Mathematics, Pi*. 4. doi:10.1017/fmp.2016.3 e5, 28 pages
- 2013: "Characters of relative p'-degree over normal subgroups", [Ann. of Math.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann._of_Math.) 178 (2013), 1135 – 1171 (with [Gabriel Navarro](wikipedia://en/78201740))
- 2011: "Waring problem for finite simple groups", [Ann. of Math.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann._of_Math.) 174 (2011), 1885 – 1950 (with [Michael Larsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Larsen) and [Aner Shalev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aner_Shalev))
- 2011: "A reduction theorem for the Alperin weight conjecture", [Invent. Math.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invent._Math.) 184 (2011), 529 – 565 (with [Gabriel Navarro](wikipedia://en/78201740))
- 2010: "The Ore conjecture", [Journal of the European Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_European_Mathematical_Society) (with [Martin Liebeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Liebeck), EA O'Brien, [Aner Shalev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aner_Shalev))
- 2008: "Symmetric powers and a problem of Kollar and Larsen", [Invent. Math.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invent._Math.) 174 (2008), 505 – 554 (with [Robert M. Guralnick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Guralnick))
