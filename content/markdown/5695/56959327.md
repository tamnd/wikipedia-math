# Elsasser number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elsasser number"}

The **Elsasser number**, Λ, is a dimensionless number in [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310) that represents the ratio of magnetic forces to the Coriolis force.[^n1]

:::

$$\Lambda = \frac{\sigma B^2}{\rho \Omega}$$

where σ is the [conductivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_conductivity) of the fluid, *B* is the [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563), ρ is the density of the fluid, and Ω is the rate of rotation of the body.

::: {#o4 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

This number is useful when studying the [linear stability](wikipedia://en/3652079) of an [accretion disc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accretion_disc) to the [magnetorotational instability](wikipedia://en/12010787).[^o5]

:::
