# Johann Heinrich Graf

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Heinrich Graf"}

**Johann Heinrich Graf** (16 August 1852 – 17 June 1918) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was rector of the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) and promoter of the [Swiss National Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Library).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Graf, son of a policeman, studied at [Polytechnicum of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) where he graduated in science in 1874. Once graduated, he became a secondary school teacher in [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern), while He did the doctorate studies supervised by [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408). He received his doctorate in 1877 and the following year he was named assistant professor at the [Berne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berne) in which he remained until his death. In 1892, when Schläfli retired, he held his chair.

Aside from his research and teaching duties at the university, Graf exercised numerous administrative responsibilities: he was dean and rector of the university, he was councilor of the city of Bern, he was editor of the magazine of the society of natural sciences (*Naturforschende Gesellschaft*), secretary of the Central Commission of National Geography and, from 1895 to 1918, he chaired the Swiss Library Commission, a post from which he promoted the creation of the [National Library of Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Switzerland) (*Schweizerische Landesbibliothek*).[^n1]

Graf's mathematical work focuses on the [Bessel functions](wikipedia://en/4700), a topic about he published a book with [Salomon Eduard Gubler](wikipedia://en/56000397), published in two volumes appeared in 1898 and 1900. He also wrote other works about [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) or [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608).
