# List of things named after Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet"}

The [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet](wikipedia://en/240002) (1805–1859) is the [eponym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponym) of many things.

:::

- Theorems named *Dirichlet's theorem*:
  - [Dirichlet's approximation theorem](wikipedia://en/2770230) ([diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558))
  - [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/101453) ([number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), specifically [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666))
  - [Dirichlet's unit theorem](wikipedia://en/460227) ([algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and [rings](wikipedia://en/48404))
