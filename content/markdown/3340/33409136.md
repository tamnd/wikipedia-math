# Howard Hawks Mitchell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Hawks Mitchell"}

**Howard Hawks Mitchell** (January 13, 1885, [Marietta, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marietta,_Ohio) – 1943) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and who introduced [Mitchell's group](wikipedia://en/14810325).

:::

In 1910 he received a PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) as [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen)'s first doctoral student. During the academic year 1910/1911 Mitchell was an instructor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). At the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) he was an instructor from 1911 to 1914 and then a professor until his death in 1943 at age 58 from coronary thrombosis.

Mitchell was elected to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society) in 1925.[^o2] His doctoral students include [Leonard Carlitz](wikipedia://en/10356778).
