# Zarhin trick

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zarhin trick"}

In mathematics, the **Zarhin trick** is a method for eliminating the polarization of [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152) *A* by observing that the abelian variety *A*<sup>4</sup> × *Â*<sup>4</sup> is principally polarized. The method was introduced by (txt) in his proof of the [Tate conjecture](wikipedia://en/3124804) over [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358) of positive [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621).
