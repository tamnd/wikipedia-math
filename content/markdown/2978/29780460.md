# Exceptional character

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Exceptional character"}

In mathematical [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) theory, an **exceptional character** of a group is a [character](wikipedia://en/293418) related in a certain way to a character of a subgroup. They were introduced by (Suzuki 1955, p. 663), based on ideas due to Brauer in (Brauer Nesbitt 1941).

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *H* is a subgroup of a finite group *G*, and *C*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *C*<sub>*r*</sub> are some conjugacy classes of *H*, and φ<sub>1</sub>, ..., φ<sub>*s*</sub> are some irreducible characters of *H*. Suppose also that they satisfy the following conditions:

1. *s* ≥ 2
1. φ<sub>*i*</sub> = φ<sub>*j*</sub> outside the classes *C*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *C*<sub>*r*</sub>
1. φ<sub>*i*</sub> vanishes on any element of *H* that is conjugate in *G* but not in *H* to an element of one of the classes *C*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *C*<sub>*r*</sub>
1. If elements of two classes are conjugate in *G* then they are conjugate in *H*
1. The centralizer in *G* of any element of one of the classes *C*<sub>1</sub>,...,*C*<sub>*r*</sub> is contained in *H*

Then *G* has *s* irreducible characters *s*<sub>1</sub>,...,*s*<sub>*s*</sub>, called **exceptional characters**, such that the induced characters φ<sub>*i*</sub>\* are given by

φ<sub>*i*</sub>\* = ε*s*<sub>*i*</sub> + *a*(*s*<sub>1</sub> + ... + *s*<sub>*s*</sub>) + Δ

where ε is 1 or −1, *a* is an integer with *a* ≥ 0, *a* + ε ≥ 0, and Δ is a character of *G* not containing any character *s*<sub>*i*</sub>.

:::

::: {#o7 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The conditions on *H* and *C*<sub>1</sub>,...,*C*<sub>*r*</sub> imply that induction is an isometry from generalized characters of *H* with support on *C*<sub>1</sub>,...,*C*<sub>*r*</sub> to generalized characters of *G*. In particular if *i*≠*j* then (φ<sub>*i*</sub> − φ<sub>*j*</sub>)\* has norm 2, so is the difference of two characters of *G*, which are the exceptional characters corresponding to φ<sub>*i*</sub> and φ<sub>*j*</sub>.

:::
