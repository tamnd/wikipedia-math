# Liu Yingming

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Liu Yingming"}

[Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_%28surname%29)

**Liu Yingming** (; 8 October 1940 – 15 July 2016) was a Chinese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) (CAS).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Liu graduated from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 1963, majoring in mathematics. He was assigned to [Sichuan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_University) after his graduation. His research field was [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [fuzzy mathematics](wikipedia://en/14075975), mainly in the algebra problem of unclear topology, embedded theory and nonclear convex sets.[^o4] Liu was the deputy president of Sichuan University between 1989 and 2005.[^o5] He was elected an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1995. Liu joined [Jiusan Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiusan_Society) in 1995. He was the vice chairman of the central committee of Jiusan Society from 1997 to 2007.[^o6]

Liu was diagnosed as [leukemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leukemia) in November 2015. He died on 15 July 2016 at the age of 75 in [Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu).[^o4]
