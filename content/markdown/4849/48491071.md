# Julio Cesar Firrufino

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julio Cesar Firrufino"}

*Image: Julio Cesar Firrufino.png.* Front page of *Platica* (1626)

**Julio Cesar Firrufino** or **Ferrufino** (16th century) was an [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who had a chair in mathematics in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) from 1604 to 1650.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

He was the son of Julian Firrufino (ca 1535–1604), born in [Alessandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandria) ([Duchy of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Milan)) who had also a chair in Geometry and Artillery granted by the king of Spain.[^o4] However, we know few biographical data of Julio Cesar, his son.[^o5] Only from a document dated 1644, we know he was 66 years old, so we can suppose he was born in 1578, when his father had a chair of Naval Artillery in the *[Casa de Contratación](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_Contratación)* and, probably, he was born in [Sevilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevilla).[^o6]

In 1600 he was assistant of the Spanish minister of Artillery[^o7] and from 1604, after the death of his father, he was appointed to a chair on Mathematics and Fortification,[^o8] after the approval of Andrés García de Céspedes and João Baptista Lavanha who examined him.[^o9] He was in charge of the chair until 1650, one year before his death; in this date he was replaced by his student [Luis Carduchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Carduchi), member of a family of old friends of Firrufino.[^o10]

In 1626 he published *Plática Manual y Breve Compendio de Artillería*, which was an abstract of a treatise of artillery that he was not allowed to publish by political reasons.[^o11]

In 1638 he was accused by fraud in the production of 66 artillery guns in Sevilla; for this reason he was imprisoned during some time in 1644.[^o12]

In 1648 was published his most important work: *El perfecto artillero*, which is an encyclopedic treatise, but not much original, based in [Tartaglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niccolò_Fontana_Tartaglia) ideas.[^o13]
