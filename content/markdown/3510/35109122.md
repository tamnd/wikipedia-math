# Quillen determinant line bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quillen determinant line bundle"}

In mathematics, the **Quillen determinant line bundle** is a [line bundle](wikipedia://en/361366) over the space of [Cauchy–Riemann operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy–Riemann_operator) of a vector bundle over a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181), introduced by (txt). Quillen proved the existence of the [Quillen metric](wikipedia://en/49946643) on the determinant line bundle, a Hermitian metric defined using the [analytic torsion](wikipedia://en/14388042) of a family of differential operators.

:::
