# Werner Gysin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Werner Gysin"}

**Werner Gysin** (15 July 1915 – 1998) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) mathematician who introduced the [Gysin sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gysin_sequence) and [Gysin homomorphism](wikipedia://en/7095671) in his only published paper (Gysin 1942).

:::

Gysin obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 1941 with a thesis written under the direction of [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082) and [Eduard Stiefel](wikipedia://en/12223876).
