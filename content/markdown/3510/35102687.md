# Metasymplectic space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Metasymplectic space"}

In mathematics, a **metasymplectic space**, introduced by (txt) and (Tits 1974, 10.13), is a [Tits building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tits_building) of type F4 (a specific generalized [incidence structure](wikipedia://en/723125)). The four types of vertices are called points, lines, planes, and symplecta.

:::
