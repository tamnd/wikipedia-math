# Brieskorn–Grothendieck resolution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brieskorn–Grothendieck resolution"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Brieskorn–Grothendieck resolution** is a resolution conjectured by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042), that in particular gives a resolution of the universal deformation of a [Kleinian singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleinian_singularity). (txt) announced the construction of this resolution, and (txt) published the details of Brieskorn's construction.

:::
