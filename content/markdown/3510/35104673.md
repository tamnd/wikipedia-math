# Peter Slodowy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Slodowy"}

*Image: Peter Slodowy (1981).jpg.* Peter Slodowy

**Peter Slodowy** (12 October 1948, in [Leverkusen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverkusen) – 19 November 2002, in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) thesis at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) in 1978 under the direction of Theodor Bröcker and [Egbert Brieskorn](wikipedia://en/34748690). The [Slodowy correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slodowy_correspondence) is named after him.
