# Weil algebra

*The term "Weil algebra" is also sometimes used to mean a finite-dimensional real local [Artinian ring](wikipedia://en/489273)*.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weil algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Weil algebra** of a [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) *g*, introduced by (txt) based on unpublished work of [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019), is a [differential graded algebra](wikipedia://en/2415863) given by the [Koszul algebra](wikipedia://en/7987653) Λ(*g*\*)⊗*S*(*g*\*) of its dual *g*\*.

:::
