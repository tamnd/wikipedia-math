# Annette Werner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annette Werner"}

*Image: Annette Werner Oberwolfach 2022.jpg.* Werner at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2022

**Annette Werner** (born 1966) is a German mathematician. Her research interests include [diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205) and the [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) of [non-Archimedean ordered fields](wikipedia://en/31046646), including the study of [buildings](wikipedia://en/275015), [Berkovich spaces](wikipedia://en/34420832), and [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041). She is a professor of mathematics at [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Werner earned a diploma in mathematics from the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) in 1991. She earned her Ph.D. at the same university in 1995, jointly supervised by [Christopher Deninger](wikipedia://en/32349981) and [Siegfried Bosch](wikipedia://en/41232820); her dissertation was *Local Heights on Uniformized Abelian Varieties and on Mumford Curves*. She also completed her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at Münster in 2000.

She worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn in 1997–1998, and as an assistant at Münster from 1998 to 2003. She became a professor at the [University of Siegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Siegen) in 2004, but in the same year moved to the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart). She has been at the University of Frankfurt since 2007.

## Book {#Book}

Werner is the author of a German-language book on [elliptic curve cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_curve_cryptography), *Elliptische Kurven in der Kryptographie* (Springer, 2002).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Werner was [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in Munich in 2010.
