# Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics"}

The ***Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [computational](wikipedia://en/10433833) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969). It was established in 1975 and is published biweekly by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Yalchin Efendiev ([Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University)), Taketomo Mitsui ([Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University)), Michael Kwok-Po Ng ([Hong Kong Baptist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Baptist_University)) and Fatih Tank ([Ankara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_University)). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.6.[^o3]
