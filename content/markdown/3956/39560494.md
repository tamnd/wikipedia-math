# Keel–Mori theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Keel–Mori theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Keel–Mori theorem** gives conditions for the existence of the quotient of an [algebraic space](wikipedia://en/2394027) by a [group](wikipedia://en/19447). The theorem was proved by (txt).

:::

A consequence of the Keel–Mori theorem is the existence of a coarse [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of a separated [algebraic stack](wikipedia://en/717377), which is roughly a "best possible" approximation to the stack by a separated algebraic space.

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

All algebraic spaces are assumed of finite type over a locally Noetherian base. Suppose that *j*:*R*→*X*×*X* is a flat groupoid whose stabilizer *j*<sup>−1</sup>Δ is finite over *X* (where Δ is the diagonal of *X*×*X*). The Keel–Mori theorem states that there is an algebraic space that is a geometric and uniform categorical quotient of *X* by *j*, which is separated if *j* is finite.

A corollary is that for any flat group scheme *G* acting properly on an algebraic space *X* with finite stabilizers there is a uniform geometric and uniform categorical quotient *X*/*G* which is a separated algebraic space. (txt) proved a slightly weaker version of this and described several applications.

:::
