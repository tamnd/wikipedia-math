# Jouko Väänänen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jouko Väänänen"}

**Jouko Antero Väänänen** (born September 3, 1950 in [Rovaniemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rovaniemi), [Lapland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapland_%28Finland%29))[^o3] is a Finnish [mathematical logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_logician) known for his contributions to [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553),[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7] [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858),[^o8][^o9][^o24] logic[^o23][^o10][^o11] and [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358).[^o7][^o12] He served as the vice-rector at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki),[^o13] and a professor of mathematics at the University of Helsinki, as well as a professor of mathematical logic and foundations of mathematics at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam).[^o14][^o15] He completed his PhD at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) under the supervision of [Peter Aczel](wikipedia://en/24089125) in 1977 with the PhD thesis *Applications of Set Theory to Generalized Quantifiers*.[^o16] He was elected to the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 2002.[^o17] He served as a member of the Senate of the University of Helsinki from 2004 to 2006 and the Treasurer of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) from 2007 to 2014,[^o18] as well as the Treasurer of the European Set Theory Society[^o19] since 2012. Jouko Väänänen received the Magnus Ehrnrooth Foundation Prize in mathematics on April 29, 2024.[^o20]

:::
