# Robert Schatten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Schatten"}

**Robert Schatten** (January 28, 1911 – August 26, 1977) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Robert Schatten was born to a Jewish family in [Lviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lviv). His intellectual origins were at [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328), particularly well known for fundamental contributions to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). His entire family was murdered during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), he himself emigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).

In 1933 he got *[magister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magister_%28degree%29)* degree at [Jan Kazimierz University of Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów_University), and in 1939 he got master's degree at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). Supervised by [Francis Joseph Murray](wikipedia://en/1732264), he got doctorate degree in 1942 for the thesis "*On the Direct Product of Banach Spaces*". Shortly after being appointed to a junior [professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), he joined the [United States army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_army) where during training he suffered a back injury which affected him for the remainder of his life. In 1943 he was appointed to an [assistant professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [University of Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vermont). At [National Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Research_Council), by two years he worked with [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) and [Nelson Dunford](wikipedia://en/21022291). In 1946, he went to the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas), first as [extraordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_professor) until 1952 and then as [ordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_professor) until 1961. He stayed at [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1950 and 1952–1953, at [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) in 1960–1961, and at [State University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York) in 1961–1962. In 1962 he became professor at [Hunter College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_College), where he stayed until his death.

Schatten widely studied [tensor products](wikipedia://en/30844) of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989). In functional analysis, he is the namesake of the [Schatten norm](wikipedia://en/10687767) and the [Schatten class operators](wikipedia://en/10698304). His doctoral students included [Elliott Ward Cheney, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Ward_Cheney,_Jr.) at University of Kansas, and Peter Falley and Charles Masiello at City University of New York.

Schatten died in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) in 1977.
