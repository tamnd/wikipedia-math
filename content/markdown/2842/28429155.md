# Alexander Shapiro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Shapiro"}

**Alexander Shapiro** is an A. Russell Chandler III Chair and Professor in H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech). He was [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the journal *[Mathematical Programming, Series A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Programming)* and was an area editor of the journal *[Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research_%28journal%29)*. Shapiro graduated with M.Sc. degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1971 and ten years later got his Ph.D. in applied mathematics and statistics from [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev).[^o2]

:::
