# Adriana Neumann de Oliveira

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adriana Neumann de Oliveira"}

**Adriana Neumann de Oliveira** (born 25 January 1980)[^o6] is a Brazilian mathematician specializing in [interacting particle systems](wikipedia://en/37809826), awarded by the L'Oréal-UNESCO Prizes for women in science in 2016. She is a professor in the Department of Pure and Applied Mathematics of the [Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul](wikipedia://en/889448).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Neumann earned her Ph.D. in 2011 at the [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708). Her dissertation, *Hydrodynamical Limit and Large Deviations Principle for the Exclusion Process with Slow Bonds*, was supervised by Cláudio Landim.

She is an affiliate member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences), elected in 2020.[^o6]
