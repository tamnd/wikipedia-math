# Achille Sannia

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Achille Sannia"}

**Achille Sannia** (14 April 1822 – 2 August 1892) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Achille Sannia was a senator of the [Kingdom of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Italy).[^o4] He was born in [Campobasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campobasso) and later moved from [Molise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molise) to [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples) to continue his studies together with [Enrico D'Ovidio](wikipedia://en/55588432). He first taught in a private school before moving to a University in 1865 as a professor of geometry.[^o5]

In 1871, he created a school of [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering). He wrote two important treatises, one concerning [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) and the other [elementary geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_geometry). He was a member of the [Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_sciences).[^o5]

He had a son, [Gustavo Sannia](wikipedia://en/35228504), who was also a mathematician.[^o6]

## Works {#Works}
