# Leon Ehrenpreis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leon Ehrenpreis"}

**Eliezer 'Leon' Ehrenpreis** (May 22, 1930 – August 16, 2010, [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn)) was a mathematician at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) who proved the [Malgrange–Ehrenpreis theorem](wikipedia://en/14160015), the fundamental theorem about [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886) with constant coefficients. He previously held tenured positions at [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University) and at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Leon was born in New York City to a family of Jewish immigrants from Eastern Europe. He graduated from [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School) and studied Mathematics as an undergraduate at [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York). Afterward, he enrolled as a doctoral student at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) where he studied under mathematician [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040), obtaining his PhD in 1953 at the age of 23. His [doctoral thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_thesis) was entitled "Theory of Distributions in Locally Compact Spaces".[^o3]

## Religion {#Religion}

Ehrenpreis was also a [Rabbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi), having received his ordination from the renowned [Rabbi Moshe Feinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi_Moshe_Feinstein).[^o5] He was the author of a work on the [Chumash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chumash_%28Judaism%29) and other religious topics, currently in manuscript.

## Miscellaneous {#Miscellaneous}

Ehrenpreis ran the [New York City Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Marathon) every year from its inception until 2007.
