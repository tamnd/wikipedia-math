# List of things named after Jacques Hadamard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of things named after Jacques Hadamard"}

These are **things named after [Jacques Hadamard](wikipedia://en/312675)** (1865–1963), a French mathematician. (For references, see the respective articles.)

:::

## Complex analysis {#Complex_analysis}

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cauchy–Hadamard theorem"}

- [Cauchy–Hadamard theorem](wikipedia://en/13620523) – a statement in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) describing the [radius of convergence](wikipedia://en/61476) of a [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard factorization theorem"}

- [Hadamard factorization theorem](wikipedia://en/2369798) – concerning the factorization of [entire functions](wikipedia://en/10372) of finite order

:::

  - The [Hadamard product](wikipedia://en/25809#Hadamard_product) formula for the Riemann zeta function

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard gap theorem"}

- [Hadamard gap theorem](wikipedia://en/13021753) – concerning the [analytic continuation](wikipedia://en/80842) of complex [power series](wikipedia://en/59958)

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard three-circle theorem"}

- [Hadamard three-circle theorem](wikipedia://en/1499388) – concerning the maxima of holomorphic functions within concentric circles in the complex plane

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard three-lines theorem"}

- [Hadamard three-lines theorem](wikipedia://en/30418364) – concerning the maxima of holomorphic functions defined in vertical strips of the complex plane

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard's lemma"}

- [Hadamard's lemma](wikipedia://en/15967005) – a result closely related to the first-order term in [Taylor's theorem](wikipedia://en/51714)

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard regularization"}

- [Hadamard regularization](wikipedia://en/15866439)

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard's gamma function"}

- [Hadamard's gamma function](wikipedia://en/49425654)

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard multiplication theorem"}

- [Hadamard multiplication theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard_multiplication_theorem) – for the singularities of coefficient-wise product of power series

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard's theorem on the radius of meromorphy"}

- [Hadamard's theorem on the radius of meromorphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard's_radius_of_meromorphy_theorem) – for the formula for the *m-*th radius of meromorphy

:::

## Matrix theory {#Matrix_theory}

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard matrix"}

- [Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/507553) – a square matrix whose entries are either +1 or −1 and whose rows are mutually orthogonal

:::

  - [Regular Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/11338044)
  - [Hadamard conjecture](wikipedia://en/6899907#The_Hadamard_conjecture)
  - [Complex Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/5157854)
  - [Butson-type Hadamard matrix](wikipedia://en/5171634)

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard code"}

- [Hadamard code](wikipedia://en/7428961) – a system used for signal error detection and correction based on Hadamard matrices

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard's inequality"}

- [Hadamard's inequality](wikipedia://en/612000) – a bound on the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of a [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) whose entries are [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers) in terms of the lengths of its column vectors

:::

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard's maximal determinant problem"}

- [Hadamard's maximal determinant problem](wikipedia://en/30556829)

:::

::: {#o18 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard product"}

- [Hadamard product](wikipedia://en/34260496) – for entry-wise matrix multiplication

:::

## Differential geometry {#Differential_geometry}

::: {#o20 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard space"}

- [Hadamard space](wikipedia://en/2850665) – a geodesically complete metric space of non-positive curvature

:::

::: {#o21 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cartan–Hadamard theorem"}

- [Cartan–Hadamard theorem](wikipedia://en/11939373) – a statement in [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243) concerning the structure of complete [Riemannian manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_manifolds) of non-positive [sectional curvature](wikipedia://en/285618).

:::

::: {#o22 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cartan–Hadamard conjecture"}

- [Cartan–Hadamard conjecture](wikipedia://en/62358280) – an open problem in Riemannian geometry and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694) concerning the generalization of the classical [isoperimetric inequality](wikipedia://en/326182) to spaces of nonpositive sectional curvature

:::

::: {#o23 .entry cue=list-entry name="Hadamard manifold"}

- [Hadamard manifold](wikipedia://en/12471936)

:::

- Hadamard's embedding theorem
