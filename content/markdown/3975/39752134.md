# Phoolan Prasad

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Phoolan Prasad"}

**Phoolan Prasad** (born 1 January 1944) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialised in [Partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988). He was awarded in 1983 the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), in the mathematical sciences category. He is Fellow of all Indian Science Academies: [The National Academy of Sciences, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India) (NASI), [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) (IAS) and [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) (INSA).[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Phoolan Prasad was born in a village Khejuri, District Ballia, UP and educated up to class 8 there.

Then he studied in Nalhati HP High School, Krishnath College Berhampore, received his B.Sc. from the [Presidency College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Kolkata), Calcutta University and M.Sc. from [Rajabazar Science College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajabazar_Science_College) campus of [Calcutta University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_University) followed by a Ph.D. from [Indian Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Science) (IISc), Bangalore in 1968.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Prasad started his career as a lecturer in the Department of Applied Mathematics in 1967 at Indian Institute of Science, thereafter he became a professor in 1977. Meanwhile, he also remained a Postdoctoral Fellow at [Leeds University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_University) (1970–72) and [Alexander von Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Fellow) (1980–81).[^o3] He was at the [St John's College, Cambridge with privileges of a Fellow of the College](https://www.joh.cam.ac.uk/). At IISc, he held Distinguished chair of MSIL Professorship during 1993–96, was Chairman of the Department of Mathematics during 1996–2000, was Professor in super-time-scale before his retirement on 31 July 2006, was Honorary Professor during 2006–2011, was DAE Raja Ramanna Fellow also during 2006–2011, was INSA Senior Scientist in 2011, was NASI-Senior Scientist Platinum Jubilee Fellow during 2012 -2016

Prasad has done some significant work in the area on non-linear hyperbolic equations. He succeeded in assessing the basic properties of the equations of various physical phenomena, generalized these mathematical properties and then used his theory to explain new results in the field of non-linear waves. He gave a proof of the existence of a new type of wave on the interface of a clear liquid and a mixture in a sedimentation process.[^o6]
