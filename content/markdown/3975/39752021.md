# Inder Bir Singh Passi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Inder Bir Singh Passi"}

**Inder Bir Singh Passi** (20 August 1939 – 2 October 2021) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialised in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923).

:::

IBS Passi was the former dean of university instructions (DUI) and professor emeritus of the department of mathematics at Panjab University.[^o3] He was awarded in 1983 the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, in the mathematical sciences category. Passi was a noted [group-theorist](wikipedia://en/41890) in India, had made significant contribution to certain aspects of [theory of groups](wikipedia://en/41890) specially to the study of [group rings](wikipedia://en/349223). His results on the dimension subgroups, augmentation powers in group rings, and related problems have received wide recognition. His 1979 monograph[^o4] summarizing the state of the subject is a basic reference source.[^o5]
