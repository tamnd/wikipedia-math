# P. Kanagasabapathy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="P. Kanagasabapathy"}

**Perampalam Kanagasabapathy** (1923–1977) was a [Ceylon Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceylon_Tamil) mathematician, academic and dean of the Faculty of Science at the Jaffna Campus of the [University of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sri_Lanka).

:::

## Early life and family {#Early_life_and_family}

Kanagasabapathy was born in 1923.[^o4] He was the son of Iyampillai Perampalam from [Erlalai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlalai) in northern [Ceylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Ceylon).[^o4] He was educated at [Jaffna Hindu College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffna_Hindu_College).[^o4] After school he joined the [University of Ceylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ceylon).[^o4] He then went to the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), graduating with a master's degree in mathematics.[^o4]

Kanagasabapathy married Meenambikai in 1949.[^o4] They had three children (Pathmini, Mythili and Nanada Kumaran).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Kanagasabapathy worked at the [University of Ceylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ceylon), Peradeniya from 1950 to 1974 as a lecturer, senior lecturer and professor of mathematics.[^o4] He joined the Jaffna Campus of the [University of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sri_Lanka) in October 1974 as a professor of mathematics.[^o4] He was head of the Department of Mathematics and Statistics and dean of the Faculty of Science at the Jaffna campus from October 1974 to January 1977.[^o6][^o7] He died in January 1977.[^o4] He published number of papers in Mathematical Journals.[^o8][^o9]
