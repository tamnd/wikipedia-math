# Gaoyong Zhang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gaoyong Zhang"}

**Gaoyong Zhang** is an American mathematician. He is professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in New York City. His main research interests are [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and its connections with [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gaoyong Zhang graduated from [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) in Philadelphia with a PhD in 1995. His advisor was Eric Grinberg.[^o4] Before he became a professor at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [NYU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYU), he was a member of the [Institute of Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o5]

Zhang became an Inaugural Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o6] in 2012. He is a member of the editorial board at Advanced Nonlinear Studies (De Gruyter)[^o7] and at the [Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society).[^o8]

## Work {#Work}

Gaoyong Zhang is known for his inverse of the Petty projection inequality,[^o10] one of the few inequalities in convex geometry where simplices were proved to be extremals. He obtained a positive solution for the [Busemann–Petty problem](wikipedia://en/33294058) in $R^4$.[^o11] He is known for his contributions (in collaboration with [Erwin Lutwak](wikipedia://en/55537162) and Deane Yang) to the L<sub>p</sub> Brunn Minkowski Theory and, in particular, his solution to the logarithmic Minkowski problem.[^o12]
