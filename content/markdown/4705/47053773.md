# 2022 Japan Football League

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2022 Japan Football League"}

The **2022 [Japan Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Football_League)** was the ninth season having a fourth-tier status in [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan) [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football), and the 24th season since the establishment of Japan Football League. The slogan for this season was "Enchant with a different play!"[^o3] The matches were mostly broadcast/streamed live at Japan Football League's channel.[^o4] The league's awards, not held since 2020 due to special measures regarding [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19), was held on 6 December 2022.[^o5]

:::

## League structure and organisation {#League_structure_and_organisation}

Iwaki FC won the league for the first time in their history, after joining the league only two seasons ago. So, they were promoted and joined J3 for the [2022 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_J3_League). Then, sixteen clubs featured in this season of Japan Football League. [FC Kariya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Kariya) were relegated after losing the playoff against [Criacao Shinjuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criacao_Shinjuku), who were duly promoted to the JFL after qualifying in the top two spots of the 45th Regional Play-off Series.[^o7] Honda Lock kept their stay at JFL, as despite having finished 16th last season, they won their playoff match against FC Ise-Shima to avoid relegation to the Regional Leagues.

As of November 2022, there were six teams eligible for promotion to the J3 League, as the only way teams from JFL can be promoted to the J3, is by having a license (the same applies for teams being promoted from J3 to J2, and from J2 to J1). Up from none to 2 teams could be promoted from the [Japan Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Football_League), or relegated to the [Regional Leagues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Regional_Leagues), depending on the circumstances.

The league is played under a [round-robin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Round-robin_tournament) format, with the 16 teams playing home-and-away matches against each other, playing in total 30 matches across the competition, starting from 13 March and ending on 20 November.[^o8] It features the usual point-earning format, with 3 points for winning, 1 for drawing, and no points for losing.

Regarding the breaks during the match, because of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), the JFL continued to follow FIFA's recommendation of a maximum of 5 available substitutions, and 3 substitution windows per team in each match (excluding the half-time).[^o9] The teams are allowed a water break at each half of the matches regardless of the [WBGT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet-bulb_globe_temperature) on the matchday. The decision to have or not to have a water break is up to whose teams are playing the match.[^o10]
