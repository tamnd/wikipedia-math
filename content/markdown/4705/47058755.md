# Nguyễn Đình Ngọc

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nguyễn Đình Ngọc"}

**Nguyễn Đình Ngọc** (13 August 1932 – 2 May 2006) was a Vietnamese [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Army_of_Vietnam) intelligence officer, later a general, and a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nguyễn Đình Ngọc was born in Phượng Dực commune, [Phú Xuyên District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phú_Xuyên_District), [Hanoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanoi), [Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam). In 1953, he joined the Intelligence Service and served in the [Vietminh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietminh) Army in the field of public security.[^o4] He was then assigned to [Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saigon) to work as a spy for [North Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam) (officially known as the Democratic Republic of Vietnam). In 1955, he moved to [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) to complete his studies after being awarded a scholarship. He attended the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), from which he graduated with three engineering degrees: Hydrology and Meteorology, Shipbuilding and Telecommunications. Additionally, he earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [Geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) and in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), under the supervision of [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108), with a thesis entitled "Sur les espaces fibres et les prolongements" (On [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526) and prolongations) at the Paris-Sorbonne University in 1963.

## Activity {#Activity}

In 1966, Ngọc returned to [Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam) where he was assigned the task of managing and developing the public telecommunications in the field of [information security](wikipedia://en/15036) and technology for [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam) (officially known as Republic of Vietnam), while teaching mathematics at the Saigon University of Science and at various colleges in South Vietnam, as told by himself in an autobiographical article.[^o7] At the same time he was a communist spy who sent important information to the North.[^o8] After the [Fall of Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Saigon) and reunification of Vietnam on 30 April 1975, he helped developing the computer and telecommunication for the [Socialist Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Vietnam)'s government. He also studied and accessed the computers provided by [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and other communist countries.

In 1989 Ngọc joined a newly established [Thang Long University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thang_Long_University) - a private university in [Hanoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanoi). He is a founder of the [Radio and Electronics Association of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_and_Electronics_Association_of_Vietnam) and the [Informatics Association of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informatics_Association_of_Vietnam). In 1994 he was promoted to [Major general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_general) of [Vietnam People's Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_People's_Public_Security) (police and security forces).[^o9]

## Death {#Death}

He died of cancer on May 2, 2006 at the hospital 198 (Ministry of Public Security).
