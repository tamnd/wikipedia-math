# Kevin McConway

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kevin McConway"}

**Kevin McConway** (born 12 October 1950) is [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) of [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics) at the [Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_University),[^o2][^o3] where he spent most of his career. He was the first Vice President (Academic Affairs) of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), from 2012-2016.

:::

After growing up in Northumberland, McConway studied mathematics at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) before taking a PhD at [UCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London), under the supervision of [Philip Dawid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Dawid).

He was academic adviser to the [BBC Radio Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_Four) programme *[More or Less](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_or_Less_%28radio_programme%29)*[^o4] and has written about that experience.[^o5] He is currently a trustee and advisory board member of the [Science Media Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Media_Centre) and has written about experience communicating statistics with the media,[^o6][^o7] and this is developed as general guidance, and to statisticians specifically - "remember to sound human".[^o8]

During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), from November 2020 he was one of two [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) Fellows nominated to an advisory group to support the UK government prepare visualisations for broadcast press conferences.[^o9][^o10]
