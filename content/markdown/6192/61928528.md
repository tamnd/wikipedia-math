# Karen L. Collins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karen L. Collins"}

**Karen Linda Collins** is an American mathematician at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University), where she is the Edward Burr Van Vleck Professor of Mathematics, Chair of Mathematics and Computer Science, and Professor of Integrative Sciences. The main topics in her research are [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

Collins graduated from [Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College) in 1981, and completed her Ph.D. in 1986 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Her dissertation, *Distance Matrices of Graphs*, was supervised by [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397). In the same year, she joined the Wesleyan faculty.

She was given the Edward Burr Van Vleck Professorship in 2017.
