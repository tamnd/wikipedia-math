# Contact type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Contact type"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more precisely in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), a [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) $\Sigma$ of a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) $(M,\omega)$ is said to be of **contact type** if there is 1-form $\alpha$ such that $j^{*}(\omega)=d\alpha$ and $(\Sigma,\alpha)$ is a [contact manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_manifold), where $j: \Sigma \to M$ is the natural inclusion. The terminology was coined by [Alan Weinstein](wikipedia://en/7344764).

:::
