# Felipe Cucker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felipe Cucker"}

**Juan Felipe Cucker Farkas** (born 1958) is an [Uruguayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguayans) mathematician and [theoretical computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_computer_scientist) who has done research into the [complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543) of the [Blum–Shub–Smale computational model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blum–Shub–Smale_machine) and the [complexity](wikipedia://en/6511) of [numerical algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_algorithm) in [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730) and [numerical algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/52802478).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Cucker was born in [Montevideo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montevideo) in 1958.[^o4][^o5][^o6] Due to the situation in Uruguay in the 70s, he emigrated to Spain to study the [bachelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) of mathematics at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona),[^o5][^o6] which he completed in 1983. He obtained his PhD degree at the [University of Cantabria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cantabria) and [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1) in 1986 under the supervision Tomás Recio and Michel Coste. His thesis was about [Nash functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_functions) on real [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808).[^o4]

From 1987 to 1992 Cucker was professor at the [Polytechnic University of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Catalonia).[^o4][^o6]

In 1992 Cucker became professor at the recently created [Pompeu Fabra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompeu_Fabra_University).[^o6] In 1995 he was promoted to chair professor at this university.[^o6]

In 1993 he organized the workshop *Continuous Algorithms and Complexity* at the [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525) in Barcelona[^o8][^o9] where he started his long-term collaboration with [Lenore Blum](wikipedia://en/1884319), [Michael Shub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shub), and [Steve Smale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Smale).[^o6] In 1996 he moved with them to the [City University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_Hong_Kong) to write the book [Complexity and Real Computation](wikipedia://en/63053280).[^o5][^o6] Although this was intended as a temporal position,[^o6] Cucker would stay in Hong Kong and became an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) of this university in 1998, [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 2003, and chair professor in 2006.[^o4]

Cucker was one of the founders of the nonprofit association [Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics), of which he has been a member of the board of directors since its creation in 1995 until 2017. Additionally, Cucker was Chair of this society from 2008 to 2011 and Editor-in-Chief of its journal from 2011 to 2017.[^o4][^o10][^o11]

In 2006 Cucker was named foreign member of the [Royal Academy of Sciences and Arts of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts_of_Barcelona).[^o4][^o12]

From 2017 to 2019 Cucker was Einstein Visiting Fellow of the [Berlin Mathematical School](wikipedia://en/14614258) and [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin).[^o6][^o13]

In 2019, the conference *Complexity of numerical computation: A conference in honor of Felipe Cucker* was organised at the [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) in Berlin celebrating his work.[^o14]

Since 2018 he is Head of the Mathematics Department at [City University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_Hong_Kong).[^o6][^o15]

## Work {#Work}

During the 90s, he worked in the complexity classes that appear when dealing with the [Blum–Shub–Smale machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blum–Shub–Smale_machine). In 1998, he co-authored one of the fundamental books in this topic, *[Complexity and Real Computation](wikipedia://en/63053280)*, together with [Lenore Blum](wikipedia://en/1884319), [Michael Shub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shub), and [Steve Smale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Smale).[^o4]

In the 2000s he focused on the analysis of numerical algorithms in linear programming. Together with Dennis Cheung he introduced the notions of the GCC condition number in linear programming.[^o17]

In 2007 together with [Steve Smale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Smale) he proposed the so-called Cucker-Smale flocking model. This model, which has received extensive attention in mathematics and other fields,[^o18] plays an important role in the mathematical study of [flocking dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flocking_%28behavior%29).[^o4][^o19]

In 2011 together with [Peter Bürgisser](wikipedia://en/61977106) he contributed to the solution of [Smale's 17th problem](wikipedia://en/8294746).[^o4]

In 2013 Cucker won an Honorable Mention in the Category of Mathematics of the [PROSE Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PROSE_Awards) for his book *Manifold Mirrors: The Crossing Paths of the Arts and Mathematics*, which dealt with [mathematics and art](wikipedia://en/22468661).[^o20]
