# KCipher-2

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="KCipher-2"}

**KCipher-2** is a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) jointly developed by [Kyushu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyushu_University) and Japanese telecommunications company [KDDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KDDI).[^n1] It is standardized as [ISO/IEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC) 18033–4,[^n2] and is on the list of recommended ciphers published by the Japanese [Cryptography Research and Evaluation Committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptography_Research_and_Evaluation_Committees) (CRYPTREC).[^n3] It has a key length of 128 bits, and can encrypt and decrypt around seven to ten times faster than the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260) (AES) algorithm.[^n1]

:::
