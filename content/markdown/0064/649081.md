# Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics"}

The **Rouse Ball Professorship of Mathematics** is one of the senior [chairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_%28academic%29) in the Mathematics Departments at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). The two positions were founded in 1927 by a bequest from the mathematician [W. W. Rouse Ball](wikipedia://en/308290). At Cambridge, this bequest was made with the "hope (but not making it in any way a condition) that it might be found practicable for such Professor or Reader to include in his or her lectures and treatment historical and philosophical aspects of the subject."[^o2]

:::

## List of Rouse Ball Professors at Cambridge {#List_of_Rouse_Ball_Professors_at_Cambridge}

- 1928–1950 [John Edensor Littlewood](wikipedia://en/158371)
- 1950–1958 [Abram Samoilovitch Besicovitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abram_Samoilovitch_Besicovitch)
- 1958–1969 [Harold Davenport](wikipedia://en/991111)
- 1971–1993 [John G. Thompson](wikipedia://en/862189)
- 1994–1997 [Nigel Hitchin](wikipedia://en/1034164)
- 1998–2020 [William Timothy Gowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Timothy_Gowers)
- 2023– [Wendelin Werner](wikipedia://en/6607797)[^o4]

## List of Rouse Ball Professors at Oxford {#List_of_Rouse_Ball_Professors_at_Oxford}

The chair at Oxford was established with a £25,000 bequest and was initially advertised by the University as a Chair in Mathematical Physics.[^o6] The Rouse Ball Professor is now hosted at the university's [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850),[^o8] and holds a Fellowship at [Wadham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College).

- 1928–1950 [E. A. Milne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Milne)
- 1952–1972 [Charles Coulson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Coulson)
- 1973–1999 [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193), [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics
- 1999–2020 [Philip Candelas](wikipedia://en/38529128), [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics[^o7]
- 2020– [Luis Fernando Alday](wikipedia://en/70059871), currently Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics[^o8]
