# Cumrun Vafa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cumrun Vafa"}

**Cumrun** or **Kamran Vafa** (کامران وفا, ; born 1 August 1960) is an [Iranian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian-American) theoretical physicist and the [Hollis Professor of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy](wikipedia://en/31306720) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). In October 2025, Vafa was appointed the Timken University Professor at Harvard, the highest distinction a faculty member can receive at the university.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Cumrun Vafa was born in [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran) on 1 August 1960.[^o5] He became interested in physics as a young child (with such questions as why the moon did not fall from the sky), and developed his interest in math in high school, fascinated by how mathematics could predict the movement of objects.[^o6]

He graduated from [Alborz High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz_High_School) in Tehran and moved to the United States in 1976 to study at university.[^o5] He received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT) in 1986. He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in physics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1988 after completing a doctoral dissertation, titled "Symmetries, inequalities and index theorems", under the supervision of [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894).[^o5][^o7]

## Academia {#Academia}

After his PhD degree, Vafa became a junior fellow via the [Harvard Society of Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Society_of_Fellows) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he later got a junior faculty position. In 1989 he was offered a senior faculty position, and he has been there ever since.

Vafa worked at Princeton University within the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), within the School of Natural Sciences and the School of Mathematics in 1994.[^o9][^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Vafa's research is primarily in [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), and is focused on the nature of [quantum gravity](wikipedia://en/25312) and the relation between geometry and quantum field theories.[^o12] He is known in the string theory community for his co-discovery with [Strominger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Strominger) that the [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](wikipedia://en/339350) of a black hole can be accounted for by [solitonic](wikipedia://en/51654) states of superstring theory,[^o13][^o14] and for expounding the relation between geometry and field theories that arise through string dualities (culminating in the [Gopakumar–Vafa conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopakumar–Vafa_invariant)). This topic has been known as "geometric engineering of quantum field theories".

In 1997, he developed [F-theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-theory), a 12-dimensional theory that compactifies to 10-D Type IIB superstring theory.

He is also interested in understanding the underlying meaning of string dualities, as well as trying to apply superstring theory to some unsolved questions of elementary particle physics such as the hierarchy problem and the [cosmological constant](wikipedia://en/38992) problem.

He has made contributions to [topological string theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_string_theory) and to the understanding of [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671).

He is a trustee of Network of Iranians for Knowledge and Innovation (NIKI).[^o15]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2017, Vafa, alongside [Andrew Strominger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Strominger) and [Joseph Polchinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Polchinski), jointly won the [Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_Prize_in_Fundamental_Physics) for their advancement of string theory[^o17] and jointly won the 2014 Breakthrough Prize in Physics Frontiers Prize.[^o18]

He is the recipient of the [Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) (ICTP)'s, 2008 [Dirac Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_Medal_%28ICTP%29), which was won alongside [Juan Maldacena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Martín_Maldacena), and [Joseph Polchinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Polchinski) for their advancement of string theory.[^o10]

In 1998 he was a Plenary Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o19]

In 2016, Vafa was awarded the [Dannie Heineman Prize for Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/4854281).[^o20]

Vafa was elected as a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2005 and as a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2009.[^o21][^o22]

In 2021, Vafa was awarded the [Mustafa Prize](https://apnews.com/article/technology-science-bangladesh-united-states-physics-8de1b236b2ad52325b7827f19e9ef4a3).[^o20]

### Timken University Professor appointment {#Timken_University_Professor_appointment}

In October 2025, Vafa was appointed the Timken University Professor at Harvard, the highest distinction a faculty member can receive at the university. The Timken professorship specifically honors distinguished scholars in any field of science.[^o3]

## Publications {#Publications}

### Research articles {#Research_articles}

Vafa has co-authored and published more than 300 research articles in the fields of string theory, mathematics, and physics, with many other researchers, including [Robbert Dijkgraaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbert_Dijkgraaf), [Hirosi Ooguri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirosi_Ooguri), [Mina Aganagic](wikipedia://en/47538197), [Sergei Gukov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Gukov), [Rajesh Gopakumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajesh_Gopakumar), [Lotte Hollands](wikipedia://en/59049883), and many others. This is a select list of these works:

- Dijkgraaf, Robbert; Vafa, Cumrun. (September 2009). "Toda Theories, Matrix Models, Topological Strings, and N=2 Gauge Systems". p. 41
- Dijkgraaf, Robbert; Hollands, Lotte; Sulkowski, Piotr; Vafa, Cumrun. (2008). "Supersymmetric Gauge Theories, Intersecting Branes and Free Fermions". *Journal of High Energy Physics*. 2008 (2). p. 106. doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2008/02/106. arxiv:0709.4446
- Dijkgraaf, Robbert; Gopakumar, Rajesh; Ooguri, Hirosi; Vafa, Cumrun. (September 2006). "Baby universes and string theory". *International Journal of Modern Physics D*. 15 (10). p. 1581–1586. doi:10.1142/S0218271806008978
- Dixon, L; Harvey, JA; Vafa, C; Witten, E. ["Strings on orbifolds"](http://theory.uchicago.edu/~harvey/pdf_files/orbiI.pdf) (2017-08-12). <https://web.archive.org/web/20170812121732/http://theory.uchicago.edu/~harvey/pdf_files/orbiI.pdf>. *Nuclear Physics*.
