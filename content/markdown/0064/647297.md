# Half-space (geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Half-space"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **half-space** is either of the two parts into which a [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) divides the three-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697). If the space is [two-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-dimensional), then a half-space is called a *[half-plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-plane)* (open or closed).[^o3][^o4] A half-space in a [one-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-dimensional) space is called a *half-line* or [ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_%28mathematics%29)*.*

:::

More generally, a **half-space** is either of the two parts into which a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) divides an *n*-dimensional [space](wikipedia://en/27667). That is, the points that are not incident to the hyperplane are [partitioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_%28set_theory%29) into two [convex sets](wikipedia://en/6292) (i.e., half-spaces), such that any subspace connecting a point in one set to a point in the other must intersect the hyperplane.

A half-space can be either *open* or *closed*. An **open half-space** is either of the two [open sets](wikipedia://en/39358) produced by the subtraction of a hyperplane from the affine space. A **closed half-space** is the union of an open half-space and the hyperplane that defines it.

The open (closed) *upper half-space* is the half-space of all $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ such that $x_n \ge 0$ ($x_n > 0$). The open (closed) *lower half-space* is defined similarly, by requiring that $x_n$ be negative (non-positive).

A half-space may be specified by a linear inequality, derived from the [linear equation](wikipedia://en/17570) that specifies the defining hyperplane. A strict linear [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489) specifies an open half-space:

$$a_1x_1+a_2x_2+\cdots+a_nx_n > b.$$

A non-strict one specifies a closed half-space:

$$a_1x_1+a_2x_2+\cdots+a_nx_n \geq b.$$

Here, one assumes that not all of the real numbers *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>, ..., *a*<sub>*n*</sub> are zero.

A half-space is a [convex set](wikipedia://en/6292).
