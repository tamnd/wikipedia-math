# Interactive computation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Interactive computation"}

In [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), **interactive computation** is a [mathematical model](wikipedia://en/20590) for [computation](wikipedia://en/5926) that involves [input/output](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Input/output) communication with the external world during computation.

:::

::: {#o3 .application cue=heading name="Uses"}

## Uses {#Uses}

Among the currently studied mathematical models of computation that attempt to capture interaction are [Giorgi Japaridze](wikipedia://en/1866981)'s hard- and easy-play machines elaborated within the framework of [computability logic](wikipedia://en/616985), [Dina Q. Goldin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dina_Q._Goldin)'s Persistent Turing Machines (PTMs), and [Yuri Gurevich](wikipedia://en/16488014)'s [abstract state machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_state_machine). [Peter Wegner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Wegner_%28computer_scientist%29) has additionally done a great deal of work on this area of computer science .

:::
