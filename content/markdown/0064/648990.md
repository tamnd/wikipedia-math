# Lowndean Professor of Astronomy and Geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lowndean Professor of Astronomy and Geometry"}

The **Lowndean chair of Astronomy and Geometry** is one of the two major Professorships in [Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) (alongside the [Plumian Professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumian_Professor_of_Astronomy_and_Experimental_Philosophy)) and a major Professorship in Mathematics at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). It was founded in 1749 by [Thomas Lowndes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Lowndes_%28astronomer%29), an [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) from [Overton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallwood,_Cheshire) in [Cheshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheshire).

:::

The original bequest stated that the holder must give two courses of twenty lectures each year, one in astronomy, and the other in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and spend at least six weeks making astronomical observations.

Originally the holder was elected by a committee consisting of the [Lord Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Chancellor), the [Lord President of the Privy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_President_of_the_Council), the [Lord Privy Seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Privy_Seal), the [Lord Steward of the Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Steward_of_the_Household), and the Lord High Treasurer of the [First Lord of the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lord_of_the_Treasury).

By the 20th century, the electors had changed to comprise the most senior scientists in the United Kingdom: the [President of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents_of_the_Royal_Society), the President of the [Royal Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Astronomical_Society), the [Astronomer Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer_Royal), the [Vice Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Chancellor) of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and the [Lucasian](wikipedia://en/147921), [Sadleirian](wikipedia://en/291556), and [Plumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumian_Professor_of_Astronomy_and_Experimental_Philosophy) Professors.

Notwithstanding the title, a professor can be chosen who specializes solely or chiefly in only one, rather than both, of the subjects of astronomy and geometry.[^o6]

## Lowndean Professors {#Lowndean_Professors}

- 1750–1771 [Roger Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Long)
- 1771–1795 [John Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Smith_%28astronomer%29)
- 1795–1837 [William Lax](wikipedia://en/31076367)
- 1837–1859 [George Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Peacock_%28mathematician%29)
- 1859–1892 [John Couch Adams](wikipedia://en/176360)
- 1892–1913 [Robert Stawell Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Stawell_Ball)
- 1914–1936 [H. F. Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._F._Baker)
- 1936–1970 [W. V. D. Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430)
- 1970–1989 [J. Frank Adams](wikipedia://en/1223959)
- 1990–1999 [Graeme Segal](wikipedia://en/6713316)
- 2000–2014 [Burt Totaro](wikipedia://en/11626155)[^o3]
- 2015-present [Mihalis Dafermos](wikipedia://en/23535913)[^o4]
