# Paul Gustav Heinrich Bachmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Gustav Heinrich Bachmann"}

**Paul Gustav Heinrich Bachmann** (22 June 1837 – 31 March 1920) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Bachmann studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the university of his native city of [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and received his doctorate in 1862 for his thesis on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He then went to [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau) to study for his habilitation, which he received in 1864 for his thesis on [Complex Units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_Unit). He was a professor at [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau) and later at [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster).

## Works {#Works}

- *Zahlentheorie*, Bachmann's work on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) in five volumes (1872-1923):
  - Vol. I: *[Die Elemente der Zahlentheorie](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k99474n)* (1892)[^o6]
  - Vol. II: *[Analytische Zahlentheorie](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k994750)* (1894), a work on [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) in which [Big O notation](wikipedia://en/44578) was first introduced
  - Vol. III: *[Die Lehre von der Kreistheilung und ihre Beziehungen zur Zahlentheorie](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k99483m)* (first published in 1872)
  - Vol. IV (Part 1): *[Die Arithmetik der quadratischen Formen](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k994828)* (1898)
  - Vol. IV (Part 2): *[Die Arithmetik der quadratischen Formen](https://archive.org/details/arithdquadrachen02bachrich)* (posthumously published in 1923)
  - Vol. V: *[Allgemeine Arithmetik der Zahlenkörper](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k99483m)* (1905)
- *Niedere Zahlentheorie*: [First part](https://archive.org/details/nederezahlenthe01bachrich) (1902), [Second part](https://archive.org/details/nederezahlenthe02bachrich) (1910),[^o7][^o8] a two-volume work on elementary [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)
- *Das Fermat-Problem in seiner bisherigen Entwicklung*, a work about [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953)

Bachmann introduced the [Big O notation](wikipedia://en/44578), which was later popularized by [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230).
