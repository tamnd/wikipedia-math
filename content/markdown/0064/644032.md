# Adjoint

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adjoint"}

In mathematics, the term ***adjoint*** applies in several situations. Several of these share a similar formalism: if *A* is adjoint to *B*, then there is typically some formula of the type

:::

(*Ax*, *y*) = (*x*, *By*).

Specifically, **adjoint** or **adjunction** may mean:

- [Adjoint of a linear map](wikipedia://en/1442470), also called its transpose in case of matrices
- [Hermitian adjoint](wikipedia://en/667175) (adjoint of a linear operator) in functional analysis
- [Adjoint endomorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_endomorphism) of a Lie algebra
- [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_representation_of_a_Lie_group)
- [Adjoint functors](wikipedia://en/53991) in category theory
- [Adjunction (field theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunction_%28field_theory%29)
- [Adjunction formula (algebraic geometry)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunction_formula_%28algebraic_geometry%29)
- [Adjunction space](wikipedia://en/1240842) in topology
- [Conjugate transpose](wikipedia://en/217548) of a matrix in linear algebra
- [Adjugate matrix](wikipedia://en/202840), related to its inverse
- [Adjoint equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_equation)
- The upper and lower adjoints of a [Galois connection](wikipedia://en/156411) in order theory
- The adjoint of a [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886) with general polynomial coefficients
- [Kleisli adjunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleisli_adjunction)
- [Monoidal adjunction](wikipedia://en/4623563)
- [Quillen adjunction](wikipedia://en/11344467)
- [Axiom of adjunction](wikipedia://en/43548952) in set theory
- [Adjunction (rule of inference)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunction_%28rule_of_inference%29)
