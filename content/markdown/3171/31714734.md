# Kummer's congruence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kummer's congruence"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Kummer's congruences** are some [congruences](wikipedia://en/48167) involving [Bernoulli numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_numbers), found by Ernst Eduard Kummer.[^o2]

:::

(Kubota Leopoldt 1964) used Kummer's congruences to define the [p-adic zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_zeta_function).[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The simplest form of Kummer's congruence states that

$$\frac{B_h}{h}\equiv \frac{B_k}{k} \pmod p \text{ whenever } h\equiv k \pmod {p-1}$$

where *p* is a prime, *h* and *k* are positive even integers not divisible by *p*−1 and the numbers *B*<sub>*h*</sub> are [Bernoulli numbers](wikipedia://en/4964).

More generally if *h* and *k* are positive even integers not divisible by *p* − 1, then

$$(1-p^{h-1})\frac{B_h}{h}\equiv (1-p^{k-1})\frac{B_k}{k} \pmod {p^{a+1}}$$

whenever

$$h\equiv k\pmod {\varphi(p^{a+1})}$$

where φ(*p*<sup>*a*+1</sup>) is the [Euler totient function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_totient_function), evaluated at *p*<sup>*a*+1</sup> and *a* is a non negative integer. At *a* = 0, the expression takes the simpler form, as seen above. The two sides of the Kummer congruence are essentially values of the [p-adic zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_zeta_function), and the Kummer congruences imply that the *p*-adic zeta function for negative integers is continuous, so can be extended by continuity to all *p*-adic integers.

:::
