# Aldous' spectral gap conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aldous' spectral gap conjecture"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Aldous' spectral gap conjecture** is the statement that two [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876) associated to a graph, namely the [random walk](wikipedia://en/235451) and the interchange process, have the same [spectral gap](wikipedia://en/7999250). The conjecture was proven in 2009 by Caputo, Liggett, and Richthammer.[^o2]

:::

## History {#History}

The statement was proven for the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) by [Persi Diaconis](wikipedia://en/65467) and Mehrdad Shahshahani in 1981,[^o4] and for [star graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_graph) by Leopold Flatto, [Andrew Odlyzko](wikipedia://en/1986042), and David Wales in 1985.[^o5] It was first formally stated as an open problem in a book in preparation by [David Aldous](wikipedia://en/14785295) and James Fill.[^o6]

For weighted trees, the statement was proven in 1996 by Shirin Handjani and Douglas Jungreis.[^o7] For boxes in $\mathbb{Z}^{d}$, the statement was independently proven by Morris and by Conomos and Starr in 2008.[^o8][^o9] The full connector was proven in 2009.[^o2]
