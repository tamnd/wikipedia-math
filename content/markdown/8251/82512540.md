# Continuous binomial distribution

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous binomial distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **continuous binomial distribution** (also called the **cobin distribution**) is a family of continuous probability distributions on the unit interval that belongs to an [exponential dispersion family](wikipedia://en/15670753). It was introduced as a response distribution for [generalized linear models](wikipedia://en/747122) for continuous proportional data, proposed as an alternative to [beta regression](wikipedia://en/73327646).[^o3] The special case $\lambda=1$ coincides with the [continuous Bernoulli distribution](wikipedia://en/64250099)[^o4].

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A random variable $X$ is said to follow a continuous binomial (cobin) distribution with natural parameter $\theta$ and inverse dispersion $\lambda\in\{1,2,\dots\}$, written $X\sim\mathrm{cobin}(\theta,\lambda^{-1})$, if it has density on $[0,1]$ given by

$$f(x;\theta,\lambda)=h(x;\lambda)\exp\!\big(\lambda\theta x-\lambda B(\theta)\big),\qquad 0\le x\le 1,$$

where the log-partition function is

$$B(\theta)= \begin{cases} \log\!\left(\frac{e^\theta-1}{\theta}\right), & \theta\ne 0,\\ 0, & \theta=0, \end{cases}$$

and the base measure $h(x;\lambda)$ is

$$h(x;\lambda)=\frac{\lambda}{(\lambda-1)!}\sum_{k=0}^{\lambda}(-1)^k{\lambda\choose k}\,\max(0,\lambda x-k)^{\lambda-1},$$

with $h(x;1)=1$. The function $h(x;\lambda)/\lambda$ coincides with the probability density function of the [Irwin–Hall distribution](wikipedia://en/15329594) with parameter $n=\lambda$, evaluated at $\lambda x$.

When $\lambda$ is fixed, the cobin distribution belongs to a one-parameter [natural exponential family](wikipedia://en/9413032) in $\theta$.

:::

## Related distributions {#Related_distributions}

- **Continuous Bernoulli distribution**: when $\lambda=1$, the cobin distribution reduces to the [continuous Bernoulli distribution](wikipedia://en/64250099).
- **Bates distribution**: when $\theta=0$, the density reduces to $h(x;\lambda)$, corresponding to the distribution of the mean of $\lambda$ independent $\mathrm{Uniform}(0,1)$ random variables (equivalently, a scaled [Irwin–Hall distribution](wikipedia://en/15329594) or [Bates distribution](wikipedia://en/31100511)).
- **Uniform distribution**: when $\lambda=1$ and $\theta=0$, the distribution reduces to the [continuous uniform distribution](wikipedia://en/1699223) on $[0,1]$.
- If $X_1,\dots,X_\lambda$ are independent and identically distributed [continuous Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64250099) random variables with common natural parameter $\theta$, then

$$\bar{X}=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda} X_i \sim \mathrm{cobin}(\theta,\lambda^{-1}).$$

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

### Mean and variance {#Mean_and_variance}

The mean and variance of $X\sim\mathrm{cobin}(\theta,\lambda^{-1})$ can be expressed in terms of derivatives of $B(\theta)$:

- $\operatorname{E}(X)=B'(\theta)=\frac{e^\theta}{e^\theta-1}-\frac{1}{\theta}$, for $\theta\ne 0$.
- $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda}B''(\theta)=\frac{1}{\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\theta^2}-\frac{e^\theta}{(e^\theta-1)^2}\right)$, for $\theta\ne 0$.

If $\theta=0$, then $\operatorname{E}(X)=\frac{1}{2}$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{12\lambda}$.

### Sufficient statistic for the mean {#Sufficient_statistic_for_the_mean}

If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are independent and identically distributed continuous binomial random variables with common natural parameter $\theta$ and fixed inverse dispersion parameter $\lambda$, then the sample mean

$$\bar{X}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i$$

is a sufficient statistic for $\theta$.

This is in contrast with the [beta distribution](wikipedia://en/207074): under a mean–precision parameterisation $X_i\sim\mathrm{Beta}(\mu\phi,(1-\mu)\phi)$ with fixed $\phi$, a sufficient statistic for the mean $\mu$ is

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log\!\left(\frac{X_i}{1-X_i}\right),$$

not the sample mean $\bar{X}$.

:::

::: {#o13 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The cobin distribution has been proposed as a response distribution for [generalized linear models](wikipedia://en/747122) of continuous proportional data, as an alternative to beta regression, including extensions with random effects.
