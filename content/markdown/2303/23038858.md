# Arithmetic variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arithmetic variety"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **arithmetic variety** is the [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) of a [Hermitian symmetric space](wikipedia://en/4127357) by an [arithmetic subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_subgroup) of the associated algebraic [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Kazhdan's theorem"}

## Kazhdan's theorem {#Kazhdan's_theorem}

Kazhdan's theorem says the following:

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Kazhdan's theorem"}

**Kazhdan's theorem.**

If X is an arithmetic variety, then, for all automorphisms σ of the complex numbers, σX is also an arithmetic variety.[^o5]

:::
