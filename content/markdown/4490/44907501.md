# Rekha R. Thomas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rekha R. Thomas"}

**Rekha Rachel Thomas** is a mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476). She works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), and was the Robert R. and Elaine F. Phelps Professor there from 2008 until 2012. Her research interests include [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033) and [computational algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805).[^o2]

:::

Thomas earned a PhD in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1994, supervised by [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels); her dissertation concerned [Gröbner bases](wikipedia://en/357328) and [integer programming](wikipedia://en/411215).[^o2] Prior to joining the University of Washington in 2000, she did postdoctoral studies at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and the [Zuse Institute Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuse_Institute_Berlin), and held a faculty position at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) beginning in 1995.[^o2][^o5]

Thomas is the author of the textbook *[Lectures in Geometric Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/64951967)* (Student Mathematical Library, 33, [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2006).[^o4] She was a plenary speaker at the 21st International Symposium on Mathematical Programming in 2012.[^o5]

In 2013 she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
