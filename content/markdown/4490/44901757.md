# Cotangent sheaf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cotangent sheaf"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), given a [morphism](wikipedia://en/7482797) *f*: *X* → *S* of [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754), the **cotangent sheaf** on *X* is the [sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules](wikipedia://en/44887605) $\Omega_{X/S}$ that [represents](wikipedia://en/844461) (or classifies) *S*-[derivations](wikipedia://en/897658)[^o2] in the sense: for any $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules *F*, there is an isomorphism

:::

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{O}_X}(\Omega_{X/S}, F) = \operatorname{Der}_S(\mathcal{O}_X, F)$$

that depends naturally on *F*. In other words, the cotangent sheaf is characterized by the [universal property](wikipedia://en/32245): there is the differential $d: \mathcal{O}_X \to \Omega_{X/S}$ such that any *S*-derivation $D: \mathcal{O}_X \to F$ factors as $D = \alpha \circ d$ with some $\alpha: \Omega_{X/S} \to F$.

In the case *X* and *S* are affine schemes, the above definition means that $\Omega_{X/S}$ is the module of [Kähler differentials](wikipedia://en/603916). The standard way to construct a cotangent sheaf (e.g., Hartshorne, Ch II. § 8) is through a diagonal morphism (which amounts to gluing modules of Kähler differentials on affine charts to get the globally defined cotangent sheaf). The [dual module](wikipedia://en/44902322) of the cotangent sheaf on a scheme *X* is called the **tangent sheaf** on *X* and is sometimes denoted by $\Theta_X$.[^o3]

There are two important [exact sequences](wikipedia://en/138215):

1. If *S* →*T* is a morphism of schemes, then

$$f^* \Omega_{S/T} \to \Omega_{X/T} \to \Omega_{X/S} \to 0.$$

1. If *Z* is a closed subscheme of *X* with [ideal sheaf](wikipedia://en/9508138) *I*, then

$I/I^2 \to \Omega_{X/S} \otimes_{O_X} \mathcal{O}_Z \to \Omega_{Z/S} \to 0.$[^o4][^o5]

The cotangent sheaf is closely related to [smoothness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) of a variety or scheme. For example, an algebraic variety is [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) of dimension *n* if and only if Ω<sub>*X*</sub> is a [locally free sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_free_sheaf) of rank *n*.[^o6]

## Construction through a diagonal morphism {#Construction_through_a_diagonal_morphism}

Let $f: X \to S$ be a morphism of schemes as in the introduction and Δ: *X* → *X* ×<sub>*S*</sub> *X* the diagonal morphism. Then the image of Δ is [locally closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_closed); i.e., closed in some open subset *W* of *X* ×<sub>*S*</sub> *X* (the image is closed if and only if *f* is [separated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separated_morphism)). Let *I* be the ideal sheaf of Δ(*X*) in *W*. One then puts:

$$\Omega_{X/S} = \Delta^* (I/I^2)$$

and checks this sheaf of modules satisfies the required universal property of a cotangent sheaf (Hartshorne, Ch II. Remark 8.9.2). The construction shows in particular that the cotangent sheaf is [quasi-coherent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-coherent_sheaf). It is coherent if *S* is [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/2783891) and *f* is of finite type.

The above definition means that the cotangent sheaf on *X* is the restriction to *X* of the [conormal sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conormal_sheaf) to the diagonal embedding of *X* over *S*.

## Relation to a tautological line bundle {#Relation_to_a_tautological_line_bundle}

The cotangent sheaf on a projective space is related to the [tautological line bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tautological_line_bundle) *O*(-1) by the following exact sequence: writing $\mathbf{P}^n_R$ for the [projective space over a ring](wikipedia://en/2285000#Projective_n-space) *R*,

$$0 \to \Omega_{\mathbf{P}^n_R/R} \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{P}^n_R}(-1)^{\oplus(n+1)} \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{P}^n_R} \to 0.$$

(See also [Chern class#Complex projective space](wikipedia://en/294349#Complex_projective_space).)

## Cotangent stack {#Cotangent_stack}

For this notion, see § 1 of

A. Beilinson and V. Drinfeld, Quantization of Hitchin’s integrable system and Hecke eigensheaves <http://www.math.uchicago.edu/~mitya/langlands/hitchin/BD-hitchin.pdf> (2015-01-05). <https://web.archive.org/web/20150105083234/http://www.math.uchicago.edu/~mitya/langlands/hitchin/BD-hitchin.pdf>[^o11]

There, the cotangent stack on an [algebraic stack](wikipedia://en/717377) *X* is defined as the [relative Spec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_Spec) of the [symmetric algebra](wikipedia://en/654098) of the tangent sheaf on *X*. (Note: in general, if *E* is a [locally free sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_free_sheaf) of finite rank, $\mathbf{Spec}(\operatorname{Sym}(\check{E}))$ is the [algebraic vector bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_vector_bundle) corresponding to *E*.)

See also: [Hitchin fibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitchin_fibration) (the cotangent stack of $\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)$ is the total space of the Hitchin fibration.)

:::
