# Uhlenbeck's compactness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uhlenbeck's compactness theorem"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and in particular [Yang–Mills theory](wikipedia://en/672202), **Uhlenbeck's compactness theorem** is a result about [sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) of (weak Yang–Mills) connections with uniformly bounded [curvature](wikipedia://en/489440) having weakly or [uniformly convergent](wikipedia://en/50652) subsequences up to [gauge](wikipedia://en/23912155). It is an important theorem used in the [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) of the anti self-dual Yang–Mills moduli space (ASDYM moduli space), which is central to the construction of [Donaldson invariants](wikipedia://en/11256383) on four-dimensional [manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) (short [4-manifold](wikipedia://en/1362795)) or [monopole Floer homology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopole_Floer_homology) on three-dimensional manifolds (short [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257)). The theorem is named after [Karen Uhlenbeck](wikipedia://en/4384796), who first described it in 1982. In 2019, Uhlenbeck became the first woman to be awarded the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285), in part for her contributions to [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155).[^o2] Uhlenbeck's compactness theorem was generalized to [Yang–Mills flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang–Mills_flow) by Alex Waldron in 2018.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Uhlenbeck's weak compactness theorem"}

## Uhlenbeck's weak compactness theorem {#Uhlenbeck's_weak_compactness_theorem}

Let $X$ be a $n$-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) and $P\twoheadrightarrow X$ be a [principal $G$-bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) with a compact [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) $G$. Let $1<p<\infty$ with $p>n/2$ and let $(A_m)_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\in\mathcal{A}^{1,p}(P) :=W^{1,p}(X,\operatorname{Ad}(P))\subset L^p(X,\operatorname{Ad}(P))$ be a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of [Sobolev](wikipedia://en/611964) [connections](wikipedia://en/470632) with uniform bound for $\|F_{A_m}\|_p$, the [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) of their [curvatures](wikipedia://en/489440). Then there exists a sequence $u_m\in\mathcal{G}^{2,p}(P)$ of [gauge transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_transformations), so that $u_m^*A_m$ [converges weakly](wikipedia://en/2119266). In other words, any $L^p$-[bounded](wikipedia://en/48258) subset of $\mathcal{A}^{1,p}(P)/\mathcal{G}^{2,p}(P)$ is [weakly compact](wikipedia://en/33662).[^o5][^o6]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Uhlenbeck's strong compactness theorem"}

## Uhlenbeck's strong compactness theorem {#Uhlenbeck's_strong_compactness_theorem}

Let $X$ be a $n$-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) and $P\twoheadrightarrow X$ be a [principal $G$-bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) with a compact [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) $G$. Let $1<p<\infty$ with $p>n/2$ and $p>4/3$ if $n=2$. Let $(A_m)_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\in\mathcal{A}^{1,p}(P) :=W^{1,p}(X,\operatorname{Ad}(P))\subset L^p(X,\operatorname{Ad}(P))$ be a sequence of [weak](wikipedia://en/3555416) [Yang–Mills connections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang–Mills_connections), hence so that:

$$\int_X\langle F_{A_m},\mathrm{d}_{A_m}\beta\rangle\mathrm{d}\operatorname{vol}_g=0$$

for all $m\in\mathbb{N}$ and $\beta\in\mathcal{A}^{1,p}(P)$, with uniform bound for $\|F_{A_m}\|_p$. Then there exists a [subsequence](wikipedia://en/236098), also denoted $(A_m)_{m\in\mathbb{N}}$, and a sequence $u_m\in\mathcal{G}^{2,p}(P)$ of gauge transformations, so that $u_m^*A_m$ [converges uniformly](wikipedia://en/50652) to a smooth connection $A\in\mathcal{A}(P)$.[^o9] (Uhlenbeck's strong compactness theorem is not stated explicitly in Uhlenbeck's 1982 paper, but follows from the results within.)

:::
