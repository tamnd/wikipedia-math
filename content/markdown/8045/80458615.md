# Carolina Benedetti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carolina Benedetti"}

[Benedetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedetti)

**Carolina Benedetti Velasquez** is a Colombian mathematician and educator who worked as an assistant professor at the [University of the Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Andes_%28Colombia%29). She specialized in [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/5170) objects, [algebraic](wikipedia://en/1997) structures, [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [Ehrhart polynomial](wikipedia://en/316904), and [supercharacters theory](wikipedia://en/691741). She also a founder of [Mathematical Circles](wikipedia://en/5163815) Colombia, an initiative designed to teach mathematical thinking to communities outside the university. She has received several fellowships, which are from the [Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées](wikipedia://en/50319899) (CIMPA), the Institute of the Mathematical Sciences of the Americas (IMSA) and Lluís Santaló Fellowship from [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525).

:::

## Education and organization {#Education_and_organization}

Benedetti studied her bachelor's degree at the [Universidad Nacional de Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Colombia) in [Bogotá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogotá), Colombia. She studied her MSc in mathematics at the [Universidad de los Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Andes_%28Colombia%29), Colombia. She was awarded a PhD by the [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University) in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), Canada in 2013.[^o3] Her thesis was titled "Combinatorial Hopf algebras and supercharacters."[^o4] Benedetti researches [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/5170) objects, [algebraic](wikipedia://en/1997) structures, [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [Ehrhart theory](wikipedia://en/316904).[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8] She has also contributed to the study of the theory of supercharacters.[^o3]

Benedetti has worked as a visiting associate professor at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) in the United States and has been employed as an assistant professor at the Universidad de los Andes since 2023.[^o9][^o10] She is a member of Comunidad Colombiana de Combinatoria and the Comisión de Equidad y Género (Gender and Equity Commission),[^o3] She is co-executive director of Mathematical Circles Colombia[^o11] and founder of this initiative, which teaches mathematical thinking to students and the communities outside the university.[^o12]

## Fellowship {#Fellowship}

She was a 2022 laureate of the Research in Pairs programme, awarded by the [Centre International de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées](wikipedia://en/50319899).[^o14] In 2024, She later received a 2024 fellowship from the Institute of the Mathematical Sciences of the Americas[^o15] and in 2026, she was named a Lluís Santaló Fellow, honoring the mathematician [Luis Santaló](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Santaló) that awarded by [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525).[^o16]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Benedetti is learning to play Pacific music.[^o9]
