# Thomas Luby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Luby"}

**Thomas Luby** (1800 – 12 June 1870) was an Irish mathematician.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Thomas Luby was born at [Clonmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clonmel), [County Tipperary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Tipperary), in 1800. He was descended from a [Huguenot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot) family which fled from France in 1685 and settled in [Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canterbury). His father, John Luby, married Eleanor Fogarty, of the old Irish family of Castle Fogarty. He entered [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) as a [sizar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sizar) in 1817, obtaining a [scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scholars_of_Trinity_College_Dublin) in 1819. He graduated B.A. in 1821, and was awarded the degrees of M.A. in 1825 and D.D. in 1840.

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Elected to a junior fellowship in 1831, he was co-opted senior fellow in 1847. Among the various college offices filled by him were those of university preacher, censor, junior dean, bursar, senior dean, and senior lecturer, and mathematical examiner in the school of civil engineering. He served as [Donegall Lecturer in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/60213290) between 1832 and 1847, and as [Regius Professor of Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Greek_%28Dublin%29) between 1852 and 1855.[^o4][^o5]

His popularity as a college tutor was unexampled. He was a member of the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy), to which he presented the autograph of [Charles Wolfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Wolfe)'s "Burial of [Sir John Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Moore_%28British_Army_officer%29) after [Corunna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Corunna)", and he wrote for college use *An Introductory Treatise on Physical Astronomy* (London, 1828), and *The Elements of Plane Trigonometry* (1825; third edition 1852). He also edited [John Brinkley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Brinkley_%28astronomer%29)'s *Astronomy* (Dublin, 1836), and was associated with [W. R. Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._R._Hamilton) in many of his publications.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He married first Mary Anne Wetherall, niece of General Sir [Frederick Wetherall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Augustus_Wetherall), K.C.B., and secondly Jane Rathborne of Dunsina, and had six sons and four daughters. He was the uncle of the Irish nationalist revolutionary [Thomas Clarke Luby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Clarke_Luby).[^o5]

He died in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin) on 12 June 1870, and was buried at [Aberystwyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberystwyth).
