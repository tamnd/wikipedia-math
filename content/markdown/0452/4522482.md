# International Association for Computing and Philosophy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Association for Computing and Philosophy"}

The **International Association for Computing and Philosophy** (**IACAP**) is a professional, philosophical [association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_association) emerging from a history of conferences on computing and philosophy that began in 1986 (the CAP conferences). After the American A-CAP and the European E-CAP, the IACAP was founded in 2004.[^o2] It adopts its mission from these conferences in order to promote scholarly dialogue on all aspects of the computational turn and the use of computers in the service of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy).

:::

The role of the IACAP is to help facilitate a global communications network for those interested in computing and philosophy as defined by these themes. To this end, it sponsors a series of international conferences and this developing website.

The founding members of IACAP include Ron Barnette, [Selmer Bringsjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selmer_Bringsjord), [Terry Bynum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Bynum), [Robert Cavalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Cavalier), [Preston Covey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_Covey), Marvin Croy, Charles Ess, [James H. Moor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Moor), Nelson Pole, and Saul Traiger.[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

IACAP has two annual awards: The Covey Award and The Herbert A. Simon Award. Both are decided by an independent committee and awarded at a ceremony at the annual conference.

- [Artificial life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_life) / [computer modeling in biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_modeling_in_biology)
- [Computer ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_ethics) / [information ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_ethics)
- [Computer-mediated communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-mediated_communication)
- Culture and society
- [Digital physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_physics)
- [Distance education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_education) and electronic pedagogy
- [Electronic publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_publishing)
