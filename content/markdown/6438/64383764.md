# Coherent topos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coherent topos"}

coherent category In mathematics, a **coherent topos** is a [topos](wikipedia://en/21391464) generated by a collection of quasi-compact quasi-separated objects closed under finite products.[^o2]

:::

[Deligne's completeness theorem](wikipedia://en/82767134) says a coherent topos has [enough points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enough_points).[^o3][^o4][^o5]
