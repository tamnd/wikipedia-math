# Anne Boutet de Monvel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anne Boutet de Monvel"}

**Anne-Marie Boutet de Monvel** (née Berthier, born 1948, also published as **Anne-Marie Berthier** and **Anne-Marie Boutet de Monvel-Berthier**) is a French [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416), and a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) in the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_%282019%29), affiliated with the [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche](wikipedia://en/57642956).

:::

## Books {#Books}

Boutet de Monvel is the author of *Spectral theory and wave operators for the Schrödinger equation* (Pitman, 1982). With Werner Amrein and Vladimir Georgescu she is the co-author of *Hardy type inequalities for abstract differential operators* (American Mathematical Society, 1987) and *$C_0$-groups, commutator methods and spectral theory of $N$-body Hamiltonians* ([Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser), 1996).

For many years she was co-editor-in-chief of the book series *Progress in Mathematical Physics*, following its relaunch by Birkhäuser in 1999.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to mathematical physics, particularly Schroedinger [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), and to the theory of integrable systems".

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Boutet de Monvel was married to [Louis Boutet de Monvel](wikipedia://en/34055924) (1941–2014), also a mathematician.
