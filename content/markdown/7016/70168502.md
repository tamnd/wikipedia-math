# Hart circle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hart circle"}

*Image: Hart Circle Picture created with Autocad.png.* The circle H touches the incircles I, $I_A$,$I_B$,$I_C$ of a circular triangle ABC and its associated triangles.

In geometry, the **Hart circle** is derived from three given circles that cross pairwise to form eight [circular triangles](wikipedia://en/48574319). For any one of these eight triangles, and its three neighboring triangles, there exists a **Hart circle**, tangent to the [inscribed circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inscribed_circle) of these four circular triangles. Thus, the three given circles have eight Hart circles associated with them. The Hart circles are named after their discover, [Andrew Searle Hart](wikipedia://en/13956502). They can be seen as analogous to the [nine-point circle](wikipedia://en/161243) of straight-sided triangles.[^o2]
