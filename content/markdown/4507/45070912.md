# Peter Paule

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Paule"}

*Image: Paule Peter.jpg.* Peter Paule, 2010

**Peter Paule** is an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrians) mathematician who works in [symbolic computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_computation) and its connections to [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), and [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110). Since 1990 he has held a faculty position at the [Research Institute for Symbolic Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Institute_for_Symbolic_Computation) of the [Johannes Kepler University of Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kepler_University_of_Linz), and since 2009 he has directed the Institute.[^o2]

:::

Paule earned his doctorate from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1982 under the supervision of Johann Cigler, and earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from Johannes Kepler University in 1996.[^o2] He is a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea),[^o4] and in 2013 he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
