# Kosovo Agency of Statistics

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Kosovo Agency of Statistics"}

The **Kosovo Agency of Statistics** (Agjencia e Statistikave të Kosovës*, ASK;* Agencija za statistiku Kosova) is the national statistics bureau of [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo).[^o4]

:::

It was officially established in 1948,[^o5] but restarted working as an independent agency in 1999.[^o6] After [Kosovo's declaration of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kosovo_declaration_of_independence) in 2008, the *ACK* organized Kosovo's first population census in 2011. It is supported by international organizations and statistic agencies, like the [OSCE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OSCE), [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations), etc. As of 2011, the bureau works within the Kosovo Prime Ministry framework.[^o7]

The last census organized by Kosovo Agency of Statistics took place during April and May of 2024.[^o8]
