# A Passage to Infinity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="A Passage to Infinity"}

***A Passage to Infinity: Medieval Indian Mathematics from Kerala and Its Impact***[^o3][^o4][^o5] is a 2009 book by [George Gheverghese Joseph](wikipedia://en/62174276) chronicling the social and mathematical origins of the [Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics](wikipedia://en/3099132). The book discusses the highlights of the achievements of [Kerala school](wikipedia://en/3099132) and also analyses the hypotheses and conjectures on the possible transmission of Kerala mathematics to [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe).

:::

## An outline of the contents {#An_outline_of_the_contents}

1. Introduction
1. The Social Origins of the [Kerala School](wikipedia://en/3099132)
1. The Mathematical Origins of the [Kerala School](wikipedia://en/3099132)
1. The Highlights of [Kerala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala) [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy)
1. [Indian Trigonometry](wikipedia://en/6785051#Indian_mathematics): From Ancient Beginnings to [Nilakantha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilakantha_Somayaji)
1. [Squaring the Circle](wikipedia://en/201359): The Kerala Answer
1. Reaching for the Stars: The [Power Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Series) for [Sines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine) and [Cosines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosine)
1. Changing Perspectives on [Indian Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Mathematics)
1. Exploring Transmissions: A Case Study of Kerala Mathematics
1. A Final Assessment
