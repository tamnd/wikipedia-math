# André Néron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="André Néron"}

**André Néron** (; November 30, 1922, [La Clayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Clayette), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) – April 6, 1985, [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), France) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [Université de Poitiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Poitiers) who worked on [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) and [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152). He discovered the [Néron minimal model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néron_minimal_model) of an elliptic curve or abelian variety, the [Néron differential](wikipedia://en/35204535), the [Néron–Severi group](wikipedia://en/1873706), the [Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion](wikipedia://en/35128259), the [local height](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_height) and [Néron–Tate height](wikipedia://en/9236652) of [rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200) on an abelian variety over a [discrete valuation ring](wikipedia://en/848633) or [Dedekind domain](wikipedia://en/145132), and classified the possible fibers of an [elliptic fibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_fibration).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He was a student of [Albert Châtelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Châtelet), and his PhD students were [Jean-Louis Colliot-Thélène](wikipedia://en/15256996), Gérard Ligozat, and Dimitrios Poulakis.

He gave invited talks at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1954 and 1966 (). In 1983 the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences) awarded him the [Émile Picard Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émile_Picard_Medal).

He died of cancer in 1985.
