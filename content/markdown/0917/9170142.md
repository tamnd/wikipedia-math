# Bernard Malgrange

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Malgrange"}

**Bernard Malgrange** (6 July 1928 – 5 January 2024) was a French mathematician who worked on [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). He proved the [Ehrenpreis–Malgrange theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrenpreis–Malgrange_theorem) and the [Malgrange preparation theorem](wikipedia://en/14188480), essential for the [classification theorem](wikipedia://en/4576710) of the elementary [catastrophes](wikipedia://en/434288) of [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Université Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Henri_Poincaré) (Nancy 1) in 1955. His advisor was [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485). He was elected to the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences) in 1988. In 2012 he gave the [Łojasiewicz Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łojasiewicz_Lecture) (on "Differential algebraic groups") at the [Jagiellonian University in Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University).[^o3] Malgrange died on 5 January 2024, at the age of 95.[^o4]

:::
