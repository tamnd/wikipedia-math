# Oswald Leroy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oswald Leroy"}

**Oswald Jozef Leroy** (16 May 1936 – 7 September 2022) was a Belgian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to theoretical [acousto-optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acousto-optics).

:::

Leroy's biggest achievement was a theoretical study of the interaction of light with two adjacent ultrasonic beams under different conditions in terms of beam shape, frequency content and intensity.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] Understanding of this phenomenon was very important in the 1970s when new acousto-optic devices were developed (mainly due to novel developments in laser technology) that utilized adjacent ultrasonic beams. Such devices were being used in [optical modulators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acousto-optics#Acousto-optic_modulator),[^o7] optical scanners,[^o8] information processing[^o9][^o10][^o11] , optical filtering[^o12] and frequency-spectrum analysis.[^o13][^o14][^o15] Before his contribution only the interaction of light with one ultrasonic beam was understood. Since then, acousto-optic devices have been used in telecommunication and military applications.

Leroy died at [Ostend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostend), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) in 2022.[^o16]

## Career {#Career}

Leroy received his PhD from [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) in the group of Robert A. Mertens[^o18] for his thesis entitled *Diffraction of light by ultrasound*. He worked as an assistant [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) from 1966 to 1972 and has been a tenured professor at the [Catholic University of Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven) since 1972. New developments in [laser physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_physics) formed the ground for collaborations between the team of Leroy and different other laboratories. He was a guest professor at the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) and [Université de Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Bordeaux), the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee) and the [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Institute_of_Technology). Furthermore, he has collaborated with the [University of Gdansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gdansk), [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University), and the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston). He retired in 2001 and received the title of [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor).

## Awards {#Awards}

*Image: Oswald Leroy Doctor Honoris Causa University Gdansk 1991.jpg.* Oswald Leroy Doctor Honoris Causa University Gdansk 1991. From middle to right: Oswald Leroy, Antoni Sliwiski (University of Gdansk)

In 1991 Leroy was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from the University of Gdansk, for his contributions to theoretical [acousto-optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acousto-optics) and to celebrate a collaboration with the team of Antoni Sliwinski at the Institute of Physics of the [University of Gdansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gdansk).[^o20] In 2001 he has received the ‘Médaille étrangère’ of the [French Acoustical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Acoustical_Society).
