# Zeev Nehari

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zeev Nehari"}

**Zeev Nehari** (born **Willi Weissbach**; 2 February 1915 – 1978) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on Complex Analysis, Univalent Functions Theory, and Differential and Integral Equations. He was a student of [Michael (Mihály) Fekete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fekete). The [Nehari manifold](wikipedia://en/25235416) is named after him.

:::
