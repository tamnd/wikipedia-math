# Prokhorov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prokhorov"}

**Prokhorov** (masculine, Прохоров) or **Prokhorova** (feminine, Прохорова) is a Russian [patronymic surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic_surname) derived from the given name [Prokhor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prokhor). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Alexander Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Prokhorov) (1916–2002), Russian physicist
- [Aleksandr Prokhorov (footballer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Prokhorov_%28footballer%29) (1946–2005), Soviet soccer player and coach
- [Alexandra Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Prokhorova), Russian-Spanish actress, screenwriter, director and poet
- [Alexey Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Prokhorov) (1923–2002), Soviet aircraft pilot and twice [Hero of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Soviet_Union)
- [Anton Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Prokhorov) (born 1992), Russian Paralympic athlete
- [Irina Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Prokhorova) (born 1956), Russian philologist, literary critic and cultural historian
- [Maria Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Prokhorova) (1903–1993), Russian scientist and biologist
- [Mikhail Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Prokhorov) (born 1965), Russian–Israeli oligarch and politician
- [Militsa Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsa_Prokhorova), Soviet architect
- [Nikita Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Prokhorov) (born 1991), Russian Paralympic athlete
- [Olga Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Prokhorova) (born 1979), Russian swimmer
- [Varvara Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varvara_Prokhorova) (born 1991), Russian skier
- [Vitali Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Prokhorov) (born 1966), Soviet–Russian ice hockey player
- [Vladimir Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Prokhorov) (born 1984), Russian luger
- [Yekaterina Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterina_Prokhorova), Russian entrepreneur
- [Yelena Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelena_Prokhorova) (born 1978), Russian heptathlete
- [Yevgeniya Prokhorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeniya_Prokhorova) (1912–1942), Soviet aviator
- [Yuri Vasilevich Prokhorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Vasilevich_Prokhorov) (1929–2013), Russian mathematician
