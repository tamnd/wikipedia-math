# ARIA (cipher)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ARIA"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **ARIA** is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594)[^o3] designed in 2003 by a large group of [South Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea) researchers.[^o4] In 2004, the [Korean Agency for Technology and Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Agency_for_Technology_and_Standards) selected it as a standard cryptographic technique.

:::

The algorithm uses a [substitution–permutation network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution–permutation_network) structure based on [AES](wikipedia://en/1260). The interface is the same as AES: 128-bit [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) with [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) of 128, 192, or 256 bits. The number of rounds is 12, 14, or 16, depending on the key size. ARIA uses two 8×8-bit [S-boxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_box) and their inverses in alternate rounds; one of these is the [Rijndael S-box](wikipedia://en/1903857).

The [key schedule](wikipedia://en/609603) processes the [key](wikipedia://en/53039) using a 3-round 256-bit [Feistel cipher](wikipedia://en/352709), with the binary expansion of 1/[$\pi$](wikipedia://en/23601) as a source of "[nothing up my sleeve numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_up_my_sleeve_number)".
