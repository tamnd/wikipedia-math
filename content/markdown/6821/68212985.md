# Eduardo Casas-Alvero

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eduardo Casas-Alvero"}

**Eduardo Casas-Alvero** (born 1948) is a Spanish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona). His work lies in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), especially [curve theory](wikipedia://en/253260).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Casas-Alvero received his PhD at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) under the direction of Josep Teixidor i Batlle in 1975.

Casas-Alvero introduced the [Casas-Alvero conjecture](wikipedia://en/37236964) characterizing certain [polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomials) whose factors match their [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921) as powers of a linear polynomial.[^o4]
