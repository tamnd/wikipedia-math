# Herbert Edward Soper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herbert Edward Soper"}

**Herbert Edward Soper** (1865 – 1930) was a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), who worked with [Karl Pearson](wikipedia://en/164695). He was awarded the [**Guy Medal in Silver**](wikipedia://en/6752269) of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) in 1930.[^o2] He had an obituary in the *[Journal of the Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/13327143)*.[^o3]

:::
