# Ovidiu Savin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ovidiu Savin"}

**Ovidiu Vasile Savin** (born January 1, 1977) is a Romanian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is active in the field of the [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

Savin received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in 2003 having [Luis Caffarelli](wikipedia://en/2070173) as advisor; he is professor of mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). Savin is mostly known for his important work on [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401)'s conjecture about global solutions to certain semilinear equations, that he proved up to dimension 8.[^o4] It is to be noticed that the conjecture turns out to be false in higher dimensions, as eventually proved by Manuel del Pino, Michał Kowalczyk, and [Juncheng Wei](wikipedia://en/17265646).[^o5] Savin has also worked on various regularity questions proving the gradient continuity of solutions to the infinity-Laplacian equation in two dimensions and obtaining results on the boundary regularity of solutions to the [Monge–Ampère equation](wikipedia://en/4914066).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Savin won a gold medal with a perfect score in the 1995 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208).[^o7] As an undergraduate at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) in 1997, Savin was a [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) fellow.[^o8] Savin was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2006.[^o9] He was awarded the [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal) in 2012.
