# Andrea Malchiodi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrea Malchiodi"}

**Andrea Malchiodi** (born September 30, 1972) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is active in the fields of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), with several contributions to [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474).

:::

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

Malchiodi received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in 2000 under the supervision of [Antonio Ambrosetti](wikipedia://en/39561229). He is a professor of mathematics at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) at Pisa. He was previously professor of mathematics at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) and at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick). Malchiodi has developed topological and analytical methods allowing to deal with a number of questions in geometric analysis, such as the [Yamabe problem](wikipedia://en/11757994), the [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) problem, problems coming from fourth order [conformal geometry](wikipedia://en/295907) and concentration for singular perturbation problems.[^o4][^o5][^o6] In particular, he proved some new intricate forms of improved Moser-[Trudinger](wikipedia://en/15173259) inequalities allowing to prove existence results for singular [Liouville](wikipedia://en/341810) equations.[^o7] Similar types of inequalities allow to prove existence results for Toda systems on surfaces.[^o8] Malchiodi has been visiting professor in many universities and institutions, amongst which are [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [The Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at Princeton, and [ETH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) at Zurich. He belongs to the editorial board of several mathematical journals and is one of the managing editors of the journal Calculus of Variations & PDE.[^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Malchiodi was awarded the [Caccioppoli prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) in 2006. In 2005 he was awarded, jointly with Antonio Ambrosetti, the [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer](wikipedia://en/36136840) prize.[^o11] Malchiodi was included in the list of invited speakers at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.[^o12]
