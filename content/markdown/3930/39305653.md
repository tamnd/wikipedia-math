# Homogeneous variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Homogeneous variety"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **homogeneous variety** is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) on which an [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) acts transitively.[^o2][^o3] Homogeneous varieties over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) are [quotient varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_variety) $G/H$ where $G$ is an algebraic group and $H$ a [subgroup scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) (for instance, an algebraic subgroup).[^o4]

:::

Such varieties are always [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) [quasi-projective varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-projective_varieties).

Classical examples are [flag varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_varieties) (when $G$ is semisimple and $H$ a [parabolic subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabolic_subgroups_of_algebraic_groups)), or more generally homogeneous [spherical varieties](wikipedia://en/39305846). [Severi-Brauer varieties](wikipedia://en/1669368) are examples of homogeneous varieties over a field without any rational points.
