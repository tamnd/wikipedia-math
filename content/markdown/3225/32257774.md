# Algebraic cobordism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebraic cobordism"}

In mathematics, **algebraic cobordism** is an analogue of [complex cobordism](wikipedia://en/1033666) for smooth [quasi-projective schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-projective_scheme) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603). It was introduced by (txt).

:::

An oriented [cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_theory) on the category of smooth quasi-projective schemes **Sm** over a field *k* consists of a [contravariant functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contravariant_functor) *A*\* from **Sm** to commutative [graded rings](wikipedia://en/103094), together with [push-forward maps](wikipedia://en/16649410) *f*<sub>\*</sub> whenever *f*:*Y*→*X* has relative dimension *d* for some *d*. These maps have to satisfy various conditions similar to those satisfied by complex cobordism. In particular they are "oriented", which means roughly that they behave well on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524); this is closely related to the condition that a [generalized cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_cohomology_theory) has a [complex orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_orientation).

Over a field of characteristic 0, algebraic cobordism is the universal oriented cohomology theory for smooth varieties. In other words there is a unique morphism of oriented cohomology theories from algebraic cobordism to any other oriented cohomology theory.

(Levine 2002) and (Levine Morel 2007) give surveys of algebraic cobordism.

The algebraic cobordism ring of [generalized flag varieties](wikipedia://en/385339) has been computed by (Hornbostel Kiritchenko 2011).
