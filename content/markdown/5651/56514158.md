# Andrew Barclay (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew Barclay"}

**Andrew Jeffrey Gunion Barclay** (19 April 1849 – 15 September 1943) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for being one of the founders of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Barclay studied at [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) where he graduated in mathematics in 1880.[^o4] Then he was professor of mathematics at [George Watson's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson's_College) (Edinburgh) and at [High School of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_School_of_Glasgow).[^n1] He retired in 1914 and went to reside in London with a son.

Barclay, with [Alexander Yule Fraser](wikipedia://en/57797407) and [Cargill Gilston Knott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargill_Gilston_Knott), issued in January 1883 a circular calling for a Mathematical Society.[^n2] That year the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) was founded and Barclay became its president in 1884.
