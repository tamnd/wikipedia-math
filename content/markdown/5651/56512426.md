# Jacques Neveu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Neveu"}

**Jacques Jean-Pierre Neveu** (14 November 1932 – 17 May 2016)[^o3] was a Belgian (and then French) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He is one of the founders of the French school (post WW II) of probability and statistics.[^o8]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jacques Neveu received in 1955 from the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) his doctorate in mathematics under [Robert Fortet](wikipedia://en/35256220) with dissertation *Étude des semi-groupes de Markov*.

In 1960, Neveu was, with Robert Fortet, one of the first two members of the Laboratoire de Probabilités et Modèles Aléatoires (LPMA). He was the LPMA's director from 1980[^o6] until 1989 when Jean Jacod became the director.

In 1962, Neveu was a *chargé de cours* (university lecturer) at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France). He taught at the Sorbonne and, after the reorganization of the University of Paris, at the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI) at the Laboratory for Probability of the Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu. He was a professor at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). In 1976, he gave a course at l'école d'été de Saint-Flour (a summer school in probability theory sponsored by the [University of Clermont Auvergne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Clermont_Auvergne)).[^o7] He was a visiting professor in Brussels, [São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Paulo), and [Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leuven).

From 1969 to 1987, Neveu was the thesis advisor for 19 doctoral students.[^o5] In 1977, he was the president of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205). In 1991, he founded the group Modélisation Aléatoire et Statistique (MAS) of the [Société de Mathématiques Appliquées et Industrielles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_Mathématiques_Appliquées_et_Industrielles) (SMAI).[^o8] In 2012, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Research {#Research}

Neveu is one of the founders of the modern theory of probability. His research deals with [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876), [Gaussian processes](wikipedia://en/302944), [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [random trees](wikipedia://en/22192834) (especially [Galton-Watson processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galton-Watson_process) and Galton-Watson trees), and [Dirac measures](wikipedia://en/1019002), as well as applications of probability theory to statistics, computer science, combinatorics, and statistical physics. In 1986 he introduced the concept of *arbre de Galton-Watson* (Galton-Watson tree) within the framework of discrete random trees;[^o10][^o11] within the mathematical formalism of Galton-Watson trees, the *notation de Neveu* is named in his honor.

## Commemoration {#Commemoration}

Several mathematicians have paid tribute to Neveu for his influence on the modern theory of probability.[^o13]

He was outstanding in teaching as well as research. In honor of Neveu, a prize is awarded by the MAS group of the SMAI to the year's best of the new French holders of doctorates in mathematicians or statistics on the basis of the judged quality of the dissertation.[^o14]
