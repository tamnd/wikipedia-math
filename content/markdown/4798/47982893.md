# Mathias Schacht

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathias Schacht"}

**Mathias Schacht** (born 1977) is a German mathematician who specializes in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o2]

:::

Schacht earned a diploma in business mathematics in 1999 from [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin).[^o2] He did his graduate studies at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University), completing a PhD in 2004 under the supervision of [Vojtěch Rödl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vojtěch_Rödl). His dissertation, on [hypergraph](wikipedia://en/232535) generalizations of the [Szemerédi regularity lemma](wikipedia://en/1962417), won the 2006 Richard Rado Prize of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).[^o2] He worked at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) as a postdoctoral researcher and acting professor from 2004 until 2009, when he moved to the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg). He received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from Humboldt University in 2010, and has been Heisenberg Professor at the University of Hamburg from 2010 to 2015.[^o2]

In 2012, Schacht and Rödl won the [George Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) for their research on hypergraph regularity.[^o4]
