# Alberto Tognoli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Tognoli"}

*Image: Tognoli a.jpg.* Alberto Tognoli (1967)

**Alberto Tognoli** (born 26 July 1937, [Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brescia), died 3 March 2008 in [Rapallo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapallo)) was an Italian mathematician, who worked on algebraic geometry.

:::

Tognoli received his Ph.D. (Laurea) in 1960 from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). From 1970 he became full professor at the same university, and he also taught in Cosenza, Ferrara, Paris and Tours. He was also a professor of geometry at the [University of Trento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trento) from 1986 until his retirement as professor emeritus in 2005.[^o2]

He is known for his research on [Nash functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_function) and Nash manifolds (named after [John Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forbes_Nash,_Jr.)). He proved Nash's conjecture that smooth compact manifolds are diffeomorphic to non-singular real algebraic manifolds (known as the Theorem of Nash and Tognoli).[^o3]

He received in 1974 the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) and in 1988 the gold medal in Mathematics of the [Accademia dei XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_XL).
