# Jinqiao Duan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jinqiao Duan"}

**Jinqiao** **Duan** (; born in December 1962 in the [lunar calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_calendar) and in January 1963 in the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar)) is a professor of mathematics at the [Illinois Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3]

:::

He is known for scientific contributions to [stochastic](wikipedia://en/47895) and [nonlinear dynamics](wikipedia://en/146103), [stochastic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/12473239), non-equilibrium statistical physics, and applications to biophysical and geophysical sciences. His current research also includes data science and stochastic dynamics, stochastic Hamilton/Contact dynamics and geometric mechanics, and open quantum dynamics and stochastic dynamics. His particular contributions include [a random invariant manifold framework](wikipedia://en/29763944), effective reduction and approximation, quantifying non-Gaussian stochastic dynamics by nonlocal partial differential equations, a nonlocal Kramers-Moyal formula, non-Gaussian data assimilation, [Onsager-Machlup action functional theory](wikipedia://en/38771161), and transitions between metastable states for [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) dynamical systems (especially with non-Gaussian Levy fluctuations).

He served as Associate Director of the [Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/12023558) in Los Angeles from 2011-2013. He is the director of the Center for Stochastic Dynamics at the [Illinois Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Institute_of_Technology).[^o4]

He earned a BS degree in Computational Mathematics from Wuhan University; MS degree in Mathematical Physics from the Chinese Academy of Sciences; MS degree in Mathematics from the University of Massachusetts Amherst; and PhD degree in Applied Mathematics from Cornell University. He was a postdoc with [Stephen Wiggins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Wiggins) and an instructor at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech).

## Scientific contributions {#Scientific_contributions}

\1. A framework for random invariant manifolds

\2. Dynamical features of stochastic systems under non-Gaussian Levy fluctuations

\3. Effective dynamics of stochastic partial differential equations

\4. Transition pathways and transition time & an Onsager-Machlup action functional theory for stochastic systems under non-Gaussian Levy fluctuations

\5. Data assimilation for non-Gaussian stochastic dynamical systems

\6. Stochastic Dynamics & Data Science

Duan's research fields include the theory, calculation and simulation of stochastic dynamical systems and nonlinear dynamical systems, as well as the trans-disciplinary researches of mathematics and other fields (random and complex phenomena related to the earth and environmental science, life sciences, etc).

Jinqiao Duan has made important contributions to the research of non-Gaussian stochastic dynamical systems, homogenization of stochastic partial differential equations and related application research fields, and was supported by a number of scientific research funds and programs.

He is the Managing Editor for Stochastics and Dynamics. https://www.editorialmanager.com/sd/default2.aspx[^o6]

and Editor-in-Chief for Interdisciplinary Mathematical Sciences. https://www.worldscientific.com/series/ims[^o7]

and Editor for Nonlinear Processes in Geophysics. https://www.nonlinear-processes-in-geophysics.net/[^o8]
