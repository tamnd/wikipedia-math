# Wolfgang Krieger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Krieger"}

*Image: Wolfgang krieger 1970.jpg.* Wolfgang Krieger, Columbus 1970

**Wolfgang Krieger** (born 3 June 3, 1940, [Garmisch-Partenkirchen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garmisch-Partenkirchen)) is a German mathematician, specializing in analysis.

:::

Krieger studied mathematics and physics at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) from 1959, where he obtained his doctorate in 1968 under Elmar Thoma with the thesis *Über Maßklassen*. Krieger studied at Harvard University from 1962 to 1965, earning a master's degree in 1964. From 1966 to 1968 he was a research assistant in Munich; and from 1968 assistant professor, from 1970 associate professor and from 1972 full professor at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University). For the academic year 1973–1974 he was a visiting professor at the University of Göttingen. In 1974, until his retirement in 2006, he was a professor at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). From 1985 to 1987 he was Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics.[^o3]

His research deals with [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), and [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987). Cuntz-Krieger algebras, introduced in 1980, are named after him and [Joachim Cuntz](wikipedia://en/38543091).[^o4]

Krieger was a visiting scholar at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES) and [Paris VI University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_VI_University) in 1977–1978, at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) in 1982–1983, at the [Almaden Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaden_Research_Center) of [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) in 1988–1989, and at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 2005–2006. In 1997 he was a Fellow of the [Japan Society for the Promotion of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Society_for_the_Promotion_of_Science). He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012. He was an invited speaker with the talk *On Generators in Ergodic Theory* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Vancouver in 1974.
