# Bernard Maurey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Maurey"}

**Bernard Maurey** (born 1948)[^o2] is a French mathematician who deals with [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and especially the theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989).

:::

He received in 1973 his Ph.D. from the [University Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Paris_VII) (Denis Diderot) under [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) with thesis *Théorèmes de factorisation pour les opérateurs linéaires à valeurs dans les espaces L<sup>p</sup>*.[^o3] Maurey is a professor at the University of Paris VII and a member of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS)'s *Laboratoire d'Analyse et de Mathématiques Appliquées* of the [University of Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne-la-Vallée). He was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1974 in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver).

He introduced stable Banach spaces in 1981 with [Jean-Louis Krivine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Louis_Krivine).[^o5][^o6] In 1992, with [Timothy Gowers](wikipedia://en/365715), Maurey resolved the "unconditional basic sequence problem" in the theory of Banach spaces, by showing that not every infinite-dimensional Banach space has an infinite-dimensional subspace that admits an [unconditional Schauder basis](wikipedia://en/3460662).[^o7]
