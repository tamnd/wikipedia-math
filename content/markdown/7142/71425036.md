# Mihyun Kang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mihyun Kang"}

**Mihyun Kang** (강미현) is a South Korean mathematician specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), including [graph enumeration](wikipedia://en/9982439) and the [topological properties](wikipedia://en/37439184) of [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477). She is a professor in the Institute of Discrete Mathematics at the [Graz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kang completed a PhD at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST), the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, in 2001. Her dissertation, *Random Walks on a Union of Finite Groups*, was supervised by Geon Ho Choe.

She became a postdoctoral researcher at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) from 2001 to 2008, and completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 2007. From 2008 to 2011, she was funded by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) as a Heisenberg Fellow. After taking an acting professorship at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) in 2011, she became a full professor at the Graz University of Technology in 2012. At the same time, she became head of the Institute of Discrete Mathematics at Graz.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kang was a 2019 winner of the Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel Research Prize of the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation).
