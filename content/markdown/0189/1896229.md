# List of stochastic processes topics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of stochastic processes topics"}

In the [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) is a random [function](wikipedia://en/185427). In practical applications, the domain over which the function is defined is a time interval (*[time series](wikipedia://en/406624)*) or a region of space (*[random field](wikipedia://en/540838)*).

:::

Familiar examples of **time series** include [stock market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market) and exchange rate fluctuations, signals such as speech, audio and video; medical data such as a patient's [EKG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EKG), [EEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroencephalography), blood pressure or temperature; and random movement such as [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) or [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451).

Examples of **random fields** include static images, random topographies (landscapes), or composition variations of an inhomogeneous material.

::: {#o34 .entry cue=list-entry name="Point process"}

- [Point processes](wikipedia://en/2147685): random arrangements of points in a space $S$. They can be modelled as stochastic processes where the domain is a sufficiently large family of subsets of *S*, ordered by inclusion; the range is the set of natural numbers; and, if *A* is a subset of *B*, *ƒ*(*A*) ≤ *ƒ*(*B*) with probability 1.
