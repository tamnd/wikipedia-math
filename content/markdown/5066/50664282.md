# Hesse's principle of transfer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hesse's principle of transfer"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **Hesse's principle of transfer** (Übertragungsprinzip) states that if the points of the [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578) **P**<sup>1</sup> are depicted by a [rational normal curve](wikipedia://en/2380117) in **P**<sup>*n*</sup>, then the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of the [projective transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_transformation) of **P**<sup>*n*</sup> that preserve the curve is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the group of the projective transformations of **P**<sup>1</sup> (this is a generalization of the original Hesse's principle, in a form suggested by [Wilhelm Franz Meyer](wikipedia://en/57755859)).[^o2][^o3] It was originally introduced by [Otto Hesse](wikipedia://en/740995) in 1866, in a more restricted form. It influenced [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) in the development of the [Erlangen program](wikipedia://en/243382).[^o4][^o5][^o6] Since its original conception, it was generalized by many mathematicians, including [Klein](wikipedia://en/41887), [Fano](wikipedia://en/2452888), and [Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274).[^o7]

:::
