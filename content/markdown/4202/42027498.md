# Global analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Global analysis"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **global analysis**, also called **analysis on manifolds**, is the study of the global and topological properties of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) on [manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_%28mathematics%29) and [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524).[^o2][^o3] Global analysis uses techniques in infinite-dimensional manifold theory and topological spaces of mappings to classify behaviors of differential equations, particularly nonlinear differential equations.[^o4] These spaces can include [singularities](wikipedia://en/54240) and hence [catastrophe theory](wikipedia://en/434288) is a part of global analysis. [Optimization problems](wikipedia://en/1126536), such as finding [geodesics](wikipedia://en/91096) on [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652), can be solved using differential equations, so that the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) overlaps with global analysis. Global analysis finds application in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) in the study of [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems)[^o5] and [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757).

:::
