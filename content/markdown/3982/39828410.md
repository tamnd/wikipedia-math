# Siva Athreya

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Siva Athreya"}

**Siva Ramachandran Athreya** (born 1971) is an Indian [probability theorist](wikipedia://en/23542) specialising in [statistical physics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [population biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_biology).[^o3]

:::

He graduated in mathematics from [St Stephen's College, Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Stephen's_College,_Delhi), went to the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) in Kolkata and Bangalore where he completed his master's, and obtained his PhD degree from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1998 under the supervision of Krzysztof Burdzy.[^o4] He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in 2012, the highest science award in India, in the mathematical sciences category.[^o5]

He has co-written the book, "Probability and Statistics with Examples using R" (along with Deepayan Sarkar and Steve Tanner).
