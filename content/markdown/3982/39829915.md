# Debashish Goswami

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Debashish Goswami"}

**Debashish Goswami** is an Indian mathematician. He obtained his PhD degree from [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) under the supervision of [Kalyan Bidhan Sinha](wikipedia://en/39771218). He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in 2012, the highest science award in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), in the mathematical sciences category.[^o3]

:::

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- INSA medal for Young Scientists (2004)[^o5]
- B. M. Birla Science Prize in Mathematics for the year 2006[^o5]
- Swarnajayanti Fellowship from the Dept. of Science and Technology of Govt. of India in 2009
