# Kohji Matsumoto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kohji Matsumoto"}

**Kohji Matsumoto** is a Japanese mathematician. He is professor of mathematics at [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University) in [Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya), Japan.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Matsumoto graduated from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1981.[^o4] He got a doctoral degree from [Rikkyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rikkyo_University) in 1986,[^o4] advised by Akio Fujii.[^o5] His thesis was titled *Discrepancy estimates for the value-distribution of the Riemann zeta-function*.[^o5] He became a lecturer at [Iwate University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwate_University) in 1987 and an associate professor there in 1990.[^o4] He joined Nagoya University in 1995, becoming a full professor there in 2001.[^o4]

### Research {#Research}

Matsumoto's specializations include [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), zeta theory, and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is mostly recognized for the [Matsumoto zeta function](wikipedia://en/26702096), a zeta function named after him. He co-edited *Analytic Number Theory* (2002), a book about [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers), divisor problems, [Diophantine equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantine_equations), and other topics related to [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), including Diophantine approximations, and the theory of zeta and L-functions.[^o7] His other book, *Algebraic And Analytic Aspects Of Zeta Functions And L-Functions*, a compilation of lectures at the French-Japanese Winter School, was published in 2010.[^o8]
