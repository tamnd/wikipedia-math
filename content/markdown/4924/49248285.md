# Alexandru Dimca

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandru Dimca"}

**Alexandru Dimca** (born July 21, 1953) is a French-Romanian mathematician, who works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) at [Université Nice-Sophia-Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Nice-Sophia-Antipolis).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dimca was born in [Cernavodă](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cernavodă) on 21 July 1953.[^o3] He competed in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1970, 1971, and 1972, earning one bronze medal and two silver medals. He obtained his PhD in 1981 from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest); his thesis "Stable mappings and singularities", was written under the direction of Gheorghe Galbură.

Among his influential professors we mention [Dan Burghelea](wikipedia://en/66504202), Martin Jurchescu, Constantin Bănica, and Lucian Bădescu. Dimca's [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar) [h-index](wikipedia://en/3604693) is 38.

Dimca is a distinguished mathematician working in various aspects of algebra, geometry, and topology.[^o6] He has written four important books in this field: *Hyperplane Arrangements*, *Sheaves in Topology*, *Singularities and Topology of Hypersurfaces*, and *Topics on real and complex singularities* as well as more than 180 journal papers.

Dimca started his research career in Romania, where he worked at the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230) from 1979 to 1988. He then was a visiting member at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn) and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey). After working as Senior Lecturer at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney) from 1991 to 1994, he became a professor at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux), from where he moved in 2004 to [Côte d'Azur University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Côte_d'Azur_University) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).[^o3]

## Honors {#Honors}

- 2005 – Honorary membership, IMAR, Romania[^o8]
- [Doctor Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Honoris_Causa) from [Ovidius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovidius_University) in 2007.[^o9]
- Senior Member of Institut Universitaire de France from 2011 to 2016.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Alexandru Dimca is married to Gabriela and has three children, Jean, Georges and Maria.
