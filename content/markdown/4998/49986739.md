# Alice Guionnet

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alice Guionnet"}

**Alice Guionnet** (born 24 May 1969)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for her work in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), in particular on large [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Guionnet entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) (Paris) in 1989. She earned her PhD in 1995 under the supervision of [Gérard Ben Arous](wikipedia://en/48269909) at [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud). Focuses of her academic research can be viewed in her thesis, *Dynamique de Langevin d'un verre de spins* ([Langevin Dynamics](wikipedia://en/5168545) of spin glass).

She has held positions at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), and [ENS (Paris)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). She is currently a Director of Research at [ENS de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon).[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

Alice Guionnet is known for her work on large random matrices.[^o7] In this context, she established principles of large deviations for the empirical measurements of the eigenvalues of large random matrices with [Gérard Ben Arous](wikipedia://en/48269909)[^o8] and [Ofer Zeitouni](wikipedia://en/56965332),[^o9] applied the theory of concentration of measure, initiated the rigorous study of matrices with a heavy tail, and obtained the convergence of spectral measurement of non-normal matrices.[^o10] She developed the analysis of Dyson-Schwinger equations to obtain topological asymptotic expansions,[^o11][^o12] and studied changes in beta-models[^o13] and random tilings.[^o14] In collaboration with [Alessio Figalli](wikipedia://en/38609892),[^o15][^o16] she introduced the concept of approximate transport to demonstrate the universality of local fluctuations.

Alice Guionnet also demonstrated significant results in free probabilities by comparing Voiculescu entropies,[^o17] building with [Vaughan Jones](wikipedia://en/1227628) and [Dimitri Shlyakhtenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimitri_Shlyakhtenko) a round of subfactors from planar algebras of any index,[^o18] and establishing isomorphisms between the algebras of von Neumann generated by q-Gaussian variables by constructing free transport.[^o19]

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

The [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) awarded her the [Oberwolfach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach#Oberwolfach_Prize) in 1998.

In 2003 she was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize#List_of_recipients_of_the_Rollo_Davidson_Prize) for her work in probability.[^o21]

In 2006, the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) awarded her the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602).[^o22]

For her contributions, she won the 2009 [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize).[^o23]

In 2012 she became a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator).[^o24] She was elected to the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2017. She is also a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o25]

Guinnet is the 2018 winner of the Blaise Pascal Medal in Mathematics of the European Academy of Sciences.[^o26][^o27] She became a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2017.[^o28]

She has been a Knight of the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour) since 2012.

In 2022 she was elected as an international member to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) (NAS)[^o29] and International Honorary Member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (AAAS).[^o30]
