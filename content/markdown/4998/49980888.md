# Richard Kenyon

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Kenyon"}

**Richard W. Kenyon** (born 1964) is an American mathematician known for his contributions in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He is the Erastus L. DeForest Professor of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).

:::

Kenyon graduated from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) and then earned his PhD under supervision of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o3] He won the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 2001 and the [Loève Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loève_Prize) in 2007. In 2014 Kenyon was chosen as a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator) and inducted into the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences). In 2018, he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#2018,_Rio_de_Janeiro) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.
