# Applied Psychological Measurement

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Applied Psychological Measurement"}

***Applied Psychological Measurement*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [SAGE Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAGE_Publications). The journal covers research on methodologies and research on the application of psychological measurement in [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and related disciplines, as well as reviews of [books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_review) and computer programs. The journal's [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is John R. Donoghue ([Educational Testing Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service)). *Applied Psychological Measurement* was featured in *[ScienceWatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information)* in May 2011, as the number of citations to the journal placed it among highly cited journals in the fields of [psychiatry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatry) and psychology.[^o3]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*Applied Psychological Measurement* is abstracted and indexed in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) and the [Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, its 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) is 1.522, ranking it 9th out of 13 journals in the category "Psychology, Mathematical"[^o5] 38th out of 53 in the category "Social Sciences, Mathematical Models".[^o6]
