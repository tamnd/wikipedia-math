# Mireille Martin-Deschamps

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mireille Martin-Deschamps"}

*Image: 2020 Mireille Martin-Deschamps.jpg.* Mireille Martin-Deschamps in 2020

**Mireille Martin-Deschamps** is a French mathematician who studies the [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) of [space curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_curve). She was president of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Martin-Deschamps studied at the [École normale supérieure de jeunes filles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_jeunes_filles) from 1965 to 1969. She defended her doctoral thesis in mathematics in 1976, supervised by [Pierre Samuel](wikipedia://en/2604194) and completed her doctorate in 1976 at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University). In 1985, she obtained the accreditation to supervise research at the University of Paris 7.

She was a researcher for the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS) from 1969 until 2003, when she became a professor at [Versailles Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles_Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines_University). In 2006, she became a member of the Executive Committee of the *European Mathematical Society* and was vice president of the society from 2011 to 2013.

From 2003 to 2007, she was a government commissioner *(Chargée de mission)* for mathematics and information sciences in the research department of the French Ministry of National Education, Research and Technology (*Ministère de l'Education Nationale, de la Recherche et de la Technologie,* MENRT). From 2006 to 2009, she was also a member of the scientific council of the French Committee for the Evaluation of University and Scientific Cooperation with Brazil (*Comité Français d'Évaluation de la Coopération Universitaire et Scientifique avec le Brésil,* COFECUB), and since 2002, she was a member of the scientific council of the City of Paris. In 2008 and 2009, she was president of the jury for the *Prix La Recherche.*[^o9]

She retired in 2010, and the university held a colloquium in honor of her retirement.

### Research {#Research}

Martin-Deschamps's doctoral work was in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) in the style of [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042). Her later work involved [Hilbert schemes](wikipedia://en/6612581) of [space curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_curve) in [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135).

### Memberships {#Memberships}

- President of the [Société Mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205) from 1998 to 2001.
- Member of the executive committee of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) beginning in 2006.

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2004, she was made a Knight of the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour)[^o6]
