# Paolo Aluffi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paolo Aluffi"}

**Paolo Aluffi** is an Italian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) who is known for his book *[Algebra: Chapter 0](wikipedia://en/80260138)*. His research primarily focuses on [characteristic classes](wikipedia://en/363530). Throughout his career Aluffi has received funding from the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation), the [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939), and the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation). He is currently a distinguished research professor at [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) and serves as one of the managing editors for the [Journal of Singularities](wikipedia://en/61073107).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1983, Aluffi received his [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics from the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) in Italy, mentored by [Alberto Collino](wikipedia://en/65302108). Four years later he completed his Ph.D. at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o3] His dissertation, *On Some Characteristic Numbers for Smooth Plane Curves*, was written under the supervision of [William Fulton](wikipedia://en/9642149).

## Career {#Career}

With the completion of his Ph.D. in 1987, Aluffi spent two years as a Dickson Instructor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) followed by another two years as a visiting assistant professor at [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University). He moved to [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) as an assistant professor in 1991 where he became a full professor by 2000.[^o7] Since 2009 he has also held a visiting position at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech).[^o8][^o9] In 2024 he was named a Distinguished Research Professor at Florida State University.[^o10]

At Florida State University Aluffi wrote *[Algebra: Chapter 0](wikipedia://en/80260138)*, a graduate-level [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) textbook published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11] The book is an edited compilation of transcripts from his [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) lectures.[^o12] It received positive reviews and is the textbook of choice for a number of universities.[^o13] In 2021, Aluffi published another abstract algebra book aimed at undergraduate students, titled *Algebra - Notes from the Underground*. What separates Aluffi's books from most abstract algebra texts is the ordering of the material. In *Chapter 0*, Aluffi introduces [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) and [universal properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_properties) at the outset. In *Notes from the Underground*, he takes a modern "[ring](wikipedia://en/48404)-first" approach as opposed to the standard "[group](wikipedia://en/19447)-first" approach.[^o14] The idea is that someone with no knowledge of [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) will find it easier to learn about rings as they are already comfortable with the ring of integers.[^o15]

## Research {#Research}

Aluffi’s research is in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). Most of it deals with [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092) and with [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), specifically [characteristic classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_classes) for singular varieties. He has also worked on questions in algebraic geometry motivated by connections with [high energy physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_energy_physics). His collaborators include [Carel Faber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carel_Faber), [Matilde Marcolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matilde_Marcolli), and [Jonathan Mboyo Esole](wikipedia://en/59013241).[^o17]
