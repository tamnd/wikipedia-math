# Quasitopological space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasitopological space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quasi-topology** on a set *X* is a function that associates to every [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) *C* a collection of mappings from *C* to *X* satisfying certain natural conditions. A set with a quasi-topology is called a **quasitopological space**.

:::

They were introduced by Spanier, who showed that there is a natural quasi-topology on the space of continuous maps from one space to another.
