# Conformal connection

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conformal connection"}

In [conformal](wikipedia://en/295907) [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a **conformal connection** is a [Cartan connection](wikipedia://en/412272) on an *n*-dimensional manifold *M* arising as a deformation of the [Klein geometry](wikipedia://en/2908224) given by the [celestial *n*-sphere](wikipedia://en/48239), viewed as the [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325)

:::

O<sup>\+</sup>(n+1,1)/*P*

where *P* is the [stabilizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29#Orbits_and_stabilizers) of a fixed null line through the origin in **R**<sup>*n*+2</sup>, in the [orthochronous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthochronous) [Lorentz group](wikipedia://en/230489) O<sup>\+</sup>(n+1,1) in *n*+2 dimensions.

## Normal Cartan connection {#Normal_Cartan_connection}

Any manifold equipped with a [conformal structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_structure) has a canonical conformal connection called the **normal Cartan connection**.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

A conformal connection on an *n*-manifold *M* is a [Cartan geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartan_geometry) modelled on the conformal sphere, where the latter is viewed as a homogeneous space for O<sup>\+</sup>(n+1,1). In other words, it is an O<sup>\+</sup>(n+1,1)-bundle equipped with

- a O<sup>\+</sup>(n+1,1)-connection (the Cartan connection)
- a [reduction of structure group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduction_of_structure_group) to the stabilizer of a point in the conformal sphere (a null line in **R**<sup>*n*+1,1</sup>)

such that the [solder form](wikipedia://en/9792297) induced by these data is an isomorphism.

:::
