# H.234

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="H.234"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **H.234** is an [international standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_standard) that defines popular [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) systems used to secure communications, specifically [Diffie-Hellman key exchange](wikipedia://en/7903) and [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28cryptosystem%29).[^o3] It also defines ISO 8732 key management.

:::

## History {#History}

**H.234** was first defined by the [International Telecommunications Union's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Telecommunication_Union) Standardization sector ([ITU-T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITU-T)) in 1994 by Study Group 15.

Subsequently, the standard was revised by Study Group 16 in November 2002, which remains in force to date.

At the time of first publication of **H.234**, RSA was covered by a [patent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent) in the United States (but not elsewhere), the US patent expired in 2000.

## Specification {#Specification}

The standard describes three methods of encryption key management:[^o6]

- [Diffie-Hellman](wikipedia://en/7903)
- [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28cryptosystem%29)
- ISO 8732
