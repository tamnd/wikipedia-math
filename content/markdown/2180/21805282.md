# Valery Goppa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valery Goppa"}

**Valery Denisovich Goppa** (Вале́рий Дени́сович Го́ппа; born 1939[^o2]) is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) mathematician.

:::

He discovered a relation between [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and codes, utilizing the [Riemann-Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/247261). Today these codes are called [algebraic geometry codes](wikipedia://en/1882683).[^o3] In 1981 he presented his discovery at the algebra seminar of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).

He also constructed other classes of codes in his career, and in 1972 he won the best paper award of the [IEEE Information Theory Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Information_Theory_Society) for his paper "A new class of linear correcting codes".[^o4] It is this class of codes that bear the name of “[Goppa code](wikipedia://en/37834267)”.
