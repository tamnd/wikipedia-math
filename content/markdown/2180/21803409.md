# Geoffrey Hunter (logician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Hunter"}

**Geoffrey Basil Bailey Hunter** (14 December 1925 – 8 June 2000) was a British [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), and [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065). Hunter was Professor Emeritus of the [University College of Wales, Bangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor_University), where he was professor from 1978 until he retired in 1992. He also taught at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario) (1950–1952) and was a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) in Philosophy at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) (1952–1965), and reader in Logic at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) (1965–1978). Geoffrey was probably most known for his work titled *Metalogic: An Introduction to the Metatheory of Standard First-Order Logic*, published in 1971.

:::
