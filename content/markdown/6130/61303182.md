# Marjorie Devaney

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marjorie Devaney"}

**Marjorie Ann** "**Marge**" **Jones Devaney** (March 3, 1931 – September 20, 2007) was an American mathematician, electrical engineer, and computer scientist who assisted in the development of the [MANIAC I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MANIAC_I) (Mathematical Analyzer Numerical Integrator And Computer) computer in 1951 as a member of the Theoretical Division at the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory), making her one of world's earliest [computer programmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programmer).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Life and education {#Life_and_education}

Marjorie Jones was the child of farmers from Wisconsin who lost their money and possessions in the [Wall Street Crash of 1929](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_Crash_of_1929). Soon after, her family relocated to [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California) where her father had a job arranged as a gravestone manufacturer. She was born in [Bell, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell,_California), in March 1931, making her the youngest of three children (one brother and one sister).

At age four, her family moved to [Pomona, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona,_California) where Jones spent her childhood years and graduated high school. She started her college education at the [University of Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Denver) in September 1948, and graduated in August 1951 with a bachelor's degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In the early 1970s, she began post-graduate studies at the [University of New Mexico–Los Alamos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico–Los_Alamos) (UNMLA). She completed graduate school with a master's degree in [computer science and electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science_and_Engineering).[^o3] Jones married Joseph James Devaney, and together they had one daughter.[^o3] Devaney died in Los Alamos, New Mexico on September 20, 2007.

## Career {#Career}

Marjorie Devaney began her career on October 8, 1951, at the Los Alamos National Laboratory in [Los Alamos, New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos,_New_Mexico). She joined the MANIAC I program as a member of the Theoretical Division under the leadership of [Nicholas Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Metropolis) and Jack Johnson. The Theoretical Division eventually became the Computing Division, where she worked for forty years. She retired in 1991 having made a number of contributions to the [MANIAC I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MANIAC_I) and [MANIAC II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MANIAC_II) programs. Her most notable work includes the co-creation of a central file system (CFS) used at the laboratory in Los Alamos, a task which took more than a decade to complete. Devaney wrote and co-wrote several publications primarily related to mathematics and programming.[^o3][^o4][^o7] Additionally, Devaney's work is referenced in numerous, independent publications.[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11]
