# Jane Piore Gilman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jane Piore Gilman"}

**Jane Piore Gilman** (born 1945)[^o2] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a distinguished professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o3] Her research concerns [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gilman is one of three children of physicist [Emanuel R. Piore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emanuel_R._Piore).[^o5] She did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), graduating in 1965,[^o3] and received her Ph.D. from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1971. Her thesis, supervised by [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972), was entitled *Relative Modular Groups in Teichmüller Spaces*. She worked for a year as an instructor at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) before joining Rutgers in 1972.[^o3]

## Books {#Books}

Gilman is the author of a monograph on the problem of testing whether pairs of elements of [PSL(2,**R**)](wikipedia://en/382667) (the group of orientation-preserving [isometries](wikipedia://en/254777) of the [hyperbolic plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_plane)) generate a [Fuchsian group](wikipedia://en/471365) (a [discrete subgroup](wikipedia://en/468536) of PSL(2,**R**)). It is *Two-generator Discrete Subgroups of*PSL(2, **R**) (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society 117, 1995).[^o8] With [Irwin Kra](wikipedia://en/35432060) and [Rubí E. Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubí_Rodríguez) she is the co-author of a graduate-level textbook on [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), *Complex Analysis: In the Spirit of Lipman Bers* ([Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980) 245, Springer, 2007; 2nd ed., 2013).[^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2014, she was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to topology and group theory, and for service to her department and the larger community."[^o11]
