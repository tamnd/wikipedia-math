# Irena Peeva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Irena Peeva"}

*Image: Irena Peeva 1995 (portion B).jpg.* Peeva in 1995

**Irena Vassileva Peeva** is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), specializing in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990).[^o2] She disproved the [Eisenbud–Goto regularity conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenbud–Goto_regularity_conjecture) jointly with [Jason McCullough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_McCullough).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Peeva did her graduate studies at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University), earning a Ph.D. in 1995 under the supervision of [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845) with a thesis entitled *Free Resolutions*. She was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) before joining the Cornell Department of Mathematics faculty in 1998.[^o6]

Peeva is an editor of the Transactions of AMS.[^o7]

## Books {#Books}

Peeva is the author of:

- *Graded Syzygies* (Springer, 2011).[^o9]
- *Minimal Free Resolutions over Complete Intersections* (with [David Eisenbud](wikipedia://en/3649845), Springer, 2016).[^o10]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2014 Peeva was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to commutative algebra and its applications."[^o12]

In 2019/2020 and in 2012/2013 Peeva was a Simons Foundation fellow.[^o13] During 1999-2001 she was a Sloan Foundation fellow[^o14] and was a Sloan doctoral dissertation fellow in 1994/1995.
