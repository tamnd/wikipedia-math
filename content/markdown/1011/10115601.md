# Atlas of Lie groups and representations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Atlas of Lie groups and representations"}

Organization The **Atlas of Lie Groups and Representations** is a [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) project to solve the [problem of the unitary dual](wikipedia://en/292788) for real [reductive](wikipedia://en/1088386) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o2][^o3]

:::
