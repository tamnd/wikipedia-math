# Darkside communication group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Darkside communication group"}

**Darkside Communication Group** is a publishing group of Japanese [Dōjinshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dōjinshi) in [Kashiwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashiwa) city. The group is known in Japan for its scientific and [Otaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otaku) activities. It was established in the 1990s. Their best known work is *Pi one million digits* in 1996. Their monthly magazine *Monthly Pi* won "Best titled book in Japan (日本タイトルだけ大賞)" in 2012.

:::

## History {#History}

- 1993: They started by Kana Hoshino.
- 1996: They started to join the [Comiket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comiket).
- 1999: Their works are introduced in ""ミニコミ魂"" by Tsutomu Kushima.
- 2012: Talking event produced by Junkudo book store in [Ikebukuro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikebukuro) town.
- 2014: *Getting difficultly(入手困難)* won the *Strange book award(ヘンな本大賞)* in Japan.
- 2015: *Rocket news* (English version) wrote about them.
- 2016: *Rocket news* (Japanese version) wrote about them.

## Books {#Books}

More than 200 titles are published. Their books are able to classify of three types; number lists, avant-garde books and scientific descriptions.

## Members {#Members}

- *Kana Hoshino* acts for the group.
- *Shinkiroh Shiroma* was a writer in past.
- *Shi* is a main writer in the group.
- *Takaki Makino* wrote the *円周率1000000桁表* in 1996.
- *Shigeo Mitsunari* wrote *パターン認識と機械学習の学習* which gave explanations of *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning* by prof. Bishop.

Over ten other members write books and sell their books in the [Comiket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comiket).

## Performances {#Performances}

- They sell their books using a weight indicator in the [Comiket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comiket).
- They discount their books by amount of Pi memorized in the [Comiket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comiket).
- Coupon tickets are often issued in the [Comiket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comiket).
- *Shi* cantillated the Pi in the Comiket Special 4.
