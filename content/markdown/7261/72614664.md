# Chen Mufa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chen Mufa"}

**Chen Mufa** is a Chinese professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Beijing Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Normal_University). He is a member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences). In addition to his work on [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), as a mathematician, Chen contributed to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Chen is a faculty member at [Beijing Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Normal_University) and a member of the advisory committee for the Beijing International Center for Mathematics.[^o6]

He is [Jiangsu Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangsu_Province)'s first academician of [mathematical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_science).[^o7]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chen obtained a doctorate degree in 1983 from Beijing Normal University. He joined Beijing Normal University in 1980 and became a full professor in 1985. In 2003, he was elected a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and in 2009, he was elected a member of the World Academy of Sciences. He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
