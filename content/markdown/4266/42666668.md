# Sergey Bobkov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergey Bobkov"}

**Sergey Bobkov** (Russian: Сергей Германович Бобков; born 15 March 1961) is a Russian mathematician. Currently Bobkov is a professor at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities.

:::

He was born in [Vorkuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorkuta) ([Komi Republic, Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komi_Republic)) and graduated from the Department of Mathematics and Mechanics in [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University). In 1988 he earned PhD in Mathematics and Physics (under direction of Vladimir N. Sudakov, [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494)) and in 1997 earned his Doctor of Science. During 1998–2000 Bobkov held positions at [Syktyvkar State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syktyvkar_State_University), Russia.[^o3] From 1995 to 1996 he was an [Alexander von Humboldt Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Fellow) at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), Germany. He spent the summers of 2001 and 2002 as an EPSRC Fellow at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), UK.[^o4] Bobkov was awarded a Simons Fellowship (2012) and [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) (2014).[^o5][^o6]

Bobkov is known for research in mathematics on the border of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29), [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773). He has achieved important results about [isoperimetric problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoperimetric_problems), [concentration of measure](wikipedia://en/788497) and other high-dimensional phenomena.

[Bobkov's inequality](wikipedia://en/73966770) is named after him.
