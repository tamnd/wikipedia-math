# Tom Hutchcroft

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tom Hutchcroft"}

**Tom Hutchcroft** is a British mathematician whose research is in probability theory. His work on [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805) won him the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 2019 and the 2024 EMS Prize given by the European Mathematical Society. He is a professor at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3] In 2026 he will become a professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hutchcroft grew up in [Glastonbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury).[^o6] He received a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 2013. Next he completed a Ph.D. from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in 2017. During his graduate studies he had internships with the Microsoft Research theory group. After his graduate studies, Hutchcroft was a [Senior Research Associate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Research_Associate) and Herchel Smith postdoctoral research fellow at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), from 2017 to 2021. He then joined the Caltech faculty in 2021.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

Hutchcroft is known for his work on percolation theory in [non-Euclidean geometries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Euclidean_geometries) and for his proof of [Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580)'s locality conjecture.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}
