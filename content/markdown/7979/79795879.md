# Jörn Dunkel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jörn Dunkel"}

**Jörn Dunkel** is a German-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist). He is the [MathWorks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathWorks) Professor of Mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT). His research combines applied mathematics, physics, and biology to understand [collective behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_behavior) in [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dunkel earned diplomas in physics and mathematics from [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), and a Ph.D. in statistical physics from the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg) in 2008. He conducted postdoctoral work at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). He joined MIT in 2013 and became a full professor in 2022.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Dunkel studies [active fluids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_fluids), [soft matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_matter), and the mechanics of living systems. His work has addressed [bacterial turbulence](https://fyfluiddynamics.com/2020/09/bacterial-turbulence/), [pattern formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_formation) in cell monolayers, and [morphogenesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphogenesis).

In 2023, Dunkel was named a [Schmidt Science Polymath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_Science_Polymath) for his transdisciplinary research spanning mathematics and biology.[^o6]
