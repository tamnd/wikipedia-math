# Statistical Methods in Medical Research

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Statistical Methods in Medical Research"}

***Statistical Methods in Medical Research*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that publishes papers in the fields of [Health Care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Care) and [Medical Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Informatics). The journal's [editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Brian Everitt ([King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London)). It has been in publication since 1992 and is currently published by [SAGE Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAGE_Publications).

:::

## Scope {#Scope}

*Statistical Methods in Medical Research* is a scholarly journal which publishes articles in the main areas of medical statistics and it is also used as a reference for medical statisticians. The journal focuses solely on statistics and medicine and aims to keep professionals up to date with all statistical techniques.

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*Statistical Methods in Medical Research* is abstracted and indexed in, among other databases: [SCOPUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCOPUS), and the [Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, its 2013 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) is 2.957, ranking it 14 out of 85 journals in the category ‘Health Care Sciences & Services’.,[^o5] 5 out of 119 journals in the category ‘Statistics & Probability’.,[^o6] 6 out of 52 journals in the category ‘Mathematical & Computational Biology’.[^o7] and 3 out of 25 journals in the category ‘Medical Informatics’.[^o8]
