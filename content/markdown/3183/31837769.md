::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Steric 6-cubes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **steric 6-cube** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728). There are unique 4 steric forms of the 6-cube.

:::
