In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcic 6-cube** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728). There are 2 unique runcic for the 6-cube.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cantellated 6-demicube
- Cantellated demihexeract
- Small rhombated hemihexeract (Acronym: sirhax) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a runcic 6-cube centered at the origin are coordinate permutations:

(±1,±1,±1,±3,±3,±3)

with an odd number of plus signs.
