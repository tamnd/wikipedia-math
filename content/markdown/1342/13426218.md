# Attrition (research)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Attrition"}

In science, **attrition** are [ratios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratios) regarding the loss of participants during an [experiment](wikipedia://en/59861). Attrition rates are values that indicate the participant drop out. Higher attrition rates are found in [longitudinal studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitudinal_studies).

:::
