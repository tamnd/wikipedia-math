# Roland Carl Backhouse

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Carl Backhouse"}

**Roland Carl Backhouse** (born 18 August 1948) is a British [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of computing science at the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Backhouse was born and raised in the [Thorntree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorntree) district of Middlesbrough, an industrial town in the north-east of England. In 1959, he won a place at the then all-male Acklam Hall Grammar School before going on to [Churchill College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_College,_Cambridge), in 1966. His [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (Ph.D.) was completed under the supervision of Jim Cunningham at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London).

## Career {#Career}

Backhouse's career has included [Royal Aircraft Establishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Aircraft_Establishment) (1969–1970), [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) (1973–1982), [University of Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essex) (1982–1986). He was formerly Professor of Computer Science at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) (1986–1990) and [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) (1990–1999) in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), before his position at the University of Nottingham.

He was a member of the [International Federation for Information Processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_for_Information_Processing) (IFIP) [IFIP Working Group 2.1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFIP_Working_Group_2.1) on Algorithmic Languages and Calculi,[^o7] which [specified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specification_%28technical_standard%29), maintains, and supports the [programming languages](wikipedia://en/23015) [ALGOL 60](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALGOL_60) and [ALGOL 68](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALGOL_68).[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Backhouse is married to Hilary, née Mitchell. They have three sons, Kevin, Andrew, and [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Backhouse).

## Academic interests {#Academic_interests}

His research interests lie in the mathematics of program construction and [algorithmic](wikipedia://en/775) problem solving. Together with [Jan L. A. van de Snepscheut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_L._A._van_de_Snepscheut) (1953—1994), he began the biennial series of conferences on the Mathematics of Program Construction, the first of which was held in 1989.
