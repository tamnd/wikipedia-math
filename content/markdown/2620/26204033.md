# Dialectica

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dialectica"}

***Dialectica*** is a quarterly philosophy journal established in 1947 by [Gaston Bachelard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaston_Bachelard), [Paul Bernays](wikipedia://en/2556580), and [Ferdinand Gonseth](wikipedia://en/54012910).

:::

It was published by [Blackwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley-Blackwell) between 2004 and 2019, then between 2020 and 2024 it was published by Philosophie.ch in full [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access). It focuses on [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211) and is the official journal of the [European Society for Analytic Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Society_for_Analytic_Philosophy).

The journal is considered among the most respected general journals in the discipline.[^o3][^o4]
