# Amber E. Boydstun

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amber E. Boydstun"}

**Amber Ellen Boydstun** is an American [political scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_scientist) and [data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist). She is a professor and director of graduate studies at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Boydstun was born to Faye Ellen Ashley.[^o6] Her paternal grandmother Janie Trevarton and her mother Janie Hughes helped maintain a farm and family.[^o6] Boydstun's maternal grandmother, Marion Ashley (née DeWolf) was a military wife and homemaker.[^o6] She completed a B.A. in philosophy and mathematics from [St. John's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College_%28Annapolis/Santa_Fe%29) in 1999.[^o4] Following graduation, she tutored mathematics at the [Native American Preparatory School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_Preparatory_School) for a semester.[^o5] She earned a M.A. (2004) and Ph.D. (2008) in political science from the [Penn State Graduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_Graduate_School).[^o4][^o6] Her dissertation was titled, *How Policy Issues Become Front-Page News*.[^o6] [Frank Baumgartner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Baumgartner) was Boydstun's doctoral advisor.[^o6]

Boydstun researches the interaction between media and politics.[^o8] She joined [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) in 2008 as an assistant professor.[^o4] She codeveloped a smartphone [app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software) for students to react to the [2012 United States presidential debates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_United_States_presidential_debates) in real time.[^o7] Boydstun was promoted to associate professor in 2015 and professor of political science in 2020.[^o4] She is the director of [graduate studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_studies).[^o8] She is the chief data scientist of the diversity lab.[^o9]
