# Eleonora Catsigeras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eleonora Catsigeras"}

**Eleonora Dolores Catsigeras García** (born 1956) is a Uruguayan mathematician who specializes in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and is a winner of the [L'Oréal-UNESCO Award for Women in Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards) in 2014, with her project *Neurodynamics*.[^o2][^o3] She completed the Doctorate in Sciences at the National Institute of Pure and Applied Mathematics in Rio de Janeiro since 1991, obtaining the title in 1995.[^o4] She later began working as a teacher at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics of the [University of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Republic_%28Uruguay%29), where she became professor.[^o5]

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

- Special Mention at the Pedeciba-UNDP Caldeyro Barcia Awards (1999)[^o5]
- [L'Oréal-UNESCO Award for Women in Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards) (2014)[^o2]
