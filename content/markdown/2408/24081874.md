# Small rhombihexacron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small rhombihexacron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small rhombihexacron.stl.* 3D model of a small rhombihexacron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small rhombihexacron** (or **small dipteral disdodecahedron**) is the dual of the [small rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157005). It is visually identical to the [small hexacronic icositetrahedron](wikipedia://en/24081904). Its faces are [antiparallelograms](wikipedia://en/5817627) formed by pairs of [coplanar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coplanar) [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654).

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each antiparallelogram has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2})\approx 16.842\,116\,236\,30^{\circ}$ and two angles of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{2})\approx 98.421\,058\,118\,15^{\circ}$. The diagonals of each antiparallelogram intersect at an angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{2})\approx 64.736\,825\,645\,55^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-7-4\sqrt{2}}{17})\approx 138.117\,959\,055\,51^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long edges and the short ones equals $\sqrt{2}$.
