# Noncommutative unique factorization domain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncommutative unique factorization domain"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **noncommutative unique factorization domain** is a [noncommutative ring](wikipedia://en/24062516) with the [unique factorization property](wikipedia://en/60546).

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) of [Hurwitz quaternions](wikipedia://en/772241), also known as integral [quaternions](wikipedia://en/51440). A quaternion *a* = *a*<sub>0</sub> + *a*<sub>1</sub>**i** + *a*<sub>2</sub>**j** + *a*<sub>3</sub>**k** is integral if either all the coefficients *a*<sub>i</sub> are [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) or all of them are [half-integers](wikipedia://en/1568608).
- All [free associative algebras](wikipedia://en/494669).[^o4]

:::
