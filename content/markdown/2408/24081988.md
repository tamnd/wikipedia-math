# Small dodecicosacron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small dodecicosacron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small dodecicosacron.stl.* 3D model of a small dodecicosacron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small dodecicosacron** (or **small dipteral trisicosahedron**) is the dual of the [small dodecicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157154) (U50). It is visually identical to the [Small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24369027). It has 60 intersecting [bow-tie](wikipedia://en/5817627)-shaped faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each face has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{5}{12}+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{5})\approx 12.661\,078\,804\,43^{\circ}$ and two angles of $\arccos(-\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{20}\sqrt{5})\approx 129.657\,475\,656\,13^{\circ}$. The diagonals of each antiparallelogram intersect at an angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{12}+\frac{19}{60}\sqrt{5})\approx 37.681\,445\,539\,45^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-44-3\sqrt{5}}{61})\approx 146.230\,659\,755\,53^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long edges and the short ones equals $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{5}$, which is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). Part of each face lies inside the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.
