# Peter Aczel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Aczel"}

**Peter Henry George Aczel** (; 31 October 1941 – 1 August 2023) was a British mathematician, logician and [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) joint [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in the [Department of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Computer_Science,_University_of_Manchester) and the [School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics,_University_of_Manchester) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester). He is known for his work in [non-well-founded set theory](wikipedia://en/1091767),[^o4] [constructive set theory](wikipedia://en/5042360),[^o5][^o6] and [Frege](wikipedia://en/48416) structures.[^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Aczel completed his [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1963[^o10] followed by a [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1966 under the supervision of [John Crossley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Crossley_%28mathematician%29).[^o3][^o11]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After two years of visiting positions at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), Aczel took a position at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester). He has also held visiting positions at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington).[^o10] In the Spring of 2009, Aczel was a Residential Fellow at the [Swedish Collegium for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Collegium_for_Advanced_Study) in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden).[^o13] He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2012.[^o14]

Aczel was on the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of the *[Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic](wikipedia://en/40764984)*[^o15] and the Cambridge Tracts in Theoretical Computer Science, having previously served on the editorial boards of the *[Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic)* and the *[Annals of Pure and Applied Logic](wikipedia://en/71697901)*.[^o10][^o16]

He died on 1 August 2023.[^o17]
