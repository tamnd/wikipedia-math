# Great triakis octahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great triakis octahedron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Great triakis octahedron.stl.* 3D model of a great triakis octahedron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great triakis octahedron** is the dual of the [stellated truncated hexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157363) (U<sub>19</sub>). It has 24 intersecting [isosceles triangle](wikipedia://en/30654#Types_of_triangle) faces. Part of each triangle lies within the solid, hence is invisible in solid models.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

The triangles have one angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2})\approx 16.842\,116\,236\,30^{\circ}$ and two of $\arccos(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{2})\approx 81.578\,941\,881\,85^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-3+8\sqrt{2}}{17})\approx 60.722\,386\,809\,64^{\circ}$.
