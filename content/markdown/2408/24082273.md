# Icositetragon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Icositetragon"}

Regular polygon stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **icositetragon** (or **icosikaitetragon**) or 24-gon is a twenty-four-sided [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621). The sum of any icositetragon's interior angles is 3960 degrees.

:::

## Regular icositetragon {#Regular_icositetragon}

The *[regular](wikipedia://en/333306) icositetragon* is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {24} and can also be constructed as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486), t{12}, or a twice-truncated [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733), tt{6}, or thrice-truncated triangle, ttt{3}.

One interior angle in a [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) icositetragon is 165°, meaning that one exterior angle would be 15°.

The [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of a regular icositetragon is: (with *t* = edge length)

$$A = 6t^2 \cot \frac{\pi}{24} = {6}t^2(2+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{6}).$$

The icositetragon appeared in Archimedes' polygon approximation of [pi](wikipedia://en/23601), along with the [hexagon](wikipedia://en/59733) (6-gon), [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486) (12-gon), tetracontaoctagon (48-gon), and enneacontahexagon (96-gon).

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

As 24 = 2<sup>3</sup> × 3, a regular icositetragon is [constructible](wikipedia://en/523430) using a [compass and straightedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_and_straightedge).[^o5] As a truncated [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486), it can be constructed by an edge-[bisection](wikipedia://en/152547) of a regular dodecagon.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

*Image: Symmetries of icositetragon.png.* Symmetries of a regular icositetragon. Vertices are colored by their symmetry positions. Blue mirrors are drawn through vertices, and purple mirrors are drawn through edge. Gyration orders are given in the center.

The *regular icositetragon* has [Dih<sub>24</sub> symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_symmetry), order 48. There are 7 subgroup dihedral symmetries: (Dih<sub>12</sub>, Dih<sub>6</sub>, Dih<sub>3</sub>), and (Dih<sub>8</sub>, Dih<sub>4</sub>, Dih<sub>2</sub> Dih<sub>1</sub>), and 8 [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) symmetries: (Z<sub>24</sub>, Z<sub>12</sub>, Z<sub>6</sub>, Z<sub>3</sub>), and (Z<sub>8</sub>, Z<sub>4</sub>, Z<sub>2</sub>, Z<sub>1</sub>).

These 16 symmetries can be seen in 22 distinct symmetries on the icositetragon. [John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) labels these by a letter and group order.[^o8] The full symmetry of the regular form is **r48** and no symmetry is labeled **a1**. The dihedral symmetries are divided depending on whether they pass through vertices (**d** for diagonal) or edges (**p** for perpendiculars), and **i** when reflection lines path through both edges and vertices. Cyclic symmetries in the middle column are labeled as **g** for their central gyration orders.

Each subgroup symmetry allows one or more degrees of freedom for irregular forms. Only the **g24** subgroup has no degrees of freedom but can be seen as [directed edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directed_edge).

:::

## Dissection {#Dissection}

*24-gon with 264 rhombs*

[Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) states that every [zonogon](wikipedia://en/55312103) (a 2*m*-gon whose opposite sides are parallel and of equal length) can be dissected into *m*(*m*-1)/2 parallelograms.[^o11] In particular this is true for regular polygons with evenly many sides, in which case the parallelograms are all rhombi. For the *regular icositetragon*, *m*=12, and it can be divided into 66: 6 squares and 5 sets of 12 rhombs. This decomposition is based on a [Petrie polygon](wikipedia://en/18571869) projection of a [12-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12-cube).

## Skew icositetragon {#Skew_icositetragon}

*3 regular skew zig-zag icositetragons*

:::

A **skew icositetragon** is a [skew polygon](wikipedia://en/6545461) with 24 vertices and edges but not existing on the same plane. The interior of such an icositetragon is not generally defined. A *skew zig-zag icositetragon* has vertices alternating between two parallel planes.

A **regular skew icositetragon** is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) with equal edge lengths. In 3-dimensions it will be a zig-zag skew icositetragon and can be seen in the vertices and side edges of a dodecagonal antiprism with the same D<sub>12d</sub>, \[2<sup>\+</sup>,24\] symmetry, order 48. The dodecagrammic antiprism, s{2,24/5} and dodecagrammic crossed-antiprism, s{2,24/7} also have regular skew dodecagons.
