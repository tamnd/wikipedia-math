# Rhombicosacron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombicosacron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombicosacron** (or **midly dipteral ditriacontahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonconvex_uniform_polyhedron) [rhombicosahedron](wikipedia://en/4157189), U56. It has 50 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 60 [crossed-quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed-quadrilateral) faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each face has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{3}{4})\approx 41.409\,622\,109\,27^{\circ}$ and two angles of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{6})\approx 99.594\,068\,226\,86^{\circ}$. The diagonals of each antiparallelogram intersect at an angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{8}+\frac{7\sqrt{5}}{24})\approx 38.996\,309\,663\,87^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(-\frac{5}{7})\approx 135.584\,691\,402\,81^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long edges and the short ones equals $\frac{3}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{5}$, which is the square of the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).
