# Great rhombihexacron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Great rhombihexacron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **great rhombihexacron** (or **great dipteral disdodecahedron**) is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [uniform](wikipedia://en/8464397) [great rhombihexahedron](wikipedia://en/4157645) (U<sub>21</sub>). It has 24 identical [bow-tie](wikipedia://en/5817627)-shaped [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 18 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), and 48 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034).[^o3]

:::

It has 12 outer vertices which have the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), and 6 inner vertices with the vertex arrangement of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635).

As a surface geometry, it can be seen as visually similar to a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), the [disdyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/724752), with much taller rhombus-based pyramids joined to each face of a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631).

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each bow-tie has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{2})\approx 31.399\,714\,809\,92^{\circ}$ and two angles of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2})\approx 62.799\,429\,619\,84^{\circ}$. The diagonals of each bow-tie intersect at an angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{2})\approx 85.800\,855\,570\,24^{\circ}$. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-7+4\sqrt{2}}{17})\approx 94.531\,580\,798\,20^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long edges and the short ones equals $\sqrt{2}$.
