# Small rhombidodecacron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Small rhombidodecacron"}

Uniform dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Small rhombidodecacron.stl.* 3D model of a small rhombidodecacron

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **small rhombidodecacron** is a nonconvex [isohedral](wikipedia://en/6286120) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470). It is the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of the [small rhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157204). It is visually identical to the [Small dodecacronic hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_dodecacronic_hexecontahedron). It has 60 intersecting [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627) faces.

:::

## Proportions {#Proportions}

Each face has two angles of $\arccos(\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{8}\sqrt{5})\approx 25.242\,832\,961\,52^{\circ}$ and two angles of $\arccos(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{5}\sqrt{5})\approx 93.025\,844\,508\,96^{\circ}$. The diagonals of each antiparallelogram intersect at an angle of $\arccos(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{10}\sqrt{5})\approx 61.731\,322\,529\,52^{\circ}$. The ratio between the lengths of the long edges and the short ones equals $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{5}$, which is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) equals $\arccos(\frac{-19-8\sqrt{5}}{41})\approx 154.121\,363\,125\,78^{\circ}$.
