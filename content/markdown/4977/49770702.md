# Iskander Taimanov

*Image: Taymanov Isk.jpg.* Iskander Taimanov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Iskander Taimanov"}

**Iskander Asanovich Taimanov** (born 20 December 1961, *Искандер Асанович Тайманов*) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerns [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), and [soliton theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soliton_theory). He is the chair of the department of geometry and topology of [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University).

:::

He is a member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o2]

He was a Ph.D. student of [Sergey Petrovich Novikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Petrovich_Novikov).

Some topics of his work are [Morse–Novikov theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse–Novikov_theory) and [Willmore surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willmore_surface).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

He is the author of the textbook *Lectures on Differential Geometry*.[^o7]
