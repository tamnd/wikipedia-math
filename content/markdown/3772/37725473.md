# William Payne (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Payne"}

**William Payne** (unknown – c. 1779) was an English mathematician[^o2] and the author of books about mathematics, [draughts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draughts), and [whist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whist). Payne was the brother of prominent London bookseller [Thomas Payne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Payne_%28publisher%29), who sold his works and published some of them.[^o2][^o4][^o3]

:::

Payne's first book, *An Introduction to the Game of Draughts*, was published in 1756.[^o2][^o4] The dedication and preface were written by [Samuel Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Johnson).[^o4][^o3]

Payne's second book, *An Introduction to Geometry: Containing the Most Useful Propositions in Euclid, & Other Authors*, was published in 1767.[^o5]

The book *Maxims for Playing the Game of Whist; With All Necessary Calculations, and Laws of the Game* was published anonymously in 1773; published by his brother Thomas, it is believed to have been written by William Payne.[^o6]
