# Norman J. Pullman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Norman J. Pullman"}

**Norman J. Pullman** (1931-3-31 – 1999) was a mathematician, professor of mathematics, and Doctor of Mathematics, who specialized in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_theory), [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), and [theory of tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tournament_theory).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

He earned an M.A. degree in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and in 1962, he was awarded the Doctorate degree of Mathematics from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University). [^o4]

From 1962 to 1965, he was professor of Mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University). And in 1965 he was awarded a postdoctoral fellowship at [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).[^o4]

In 1965 he started to work at the faculty of [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), and held a professorship position since 1971.[^o4]

He lectured in professional meetings for the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582).

He was a visiting scholar for [Curtin University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtin_University) in a great many occasions, and had a professional association with the institution.

During his career, he supervised mathematicians like [Dominique de Caen](wikipedia://en/37587115), [Rolf S. Rees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_S._Rees), and [Bill Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Jackson_%28mathematician%29), among others.[^o4]

His research included contributions in [matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_theory), [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), and [theory of tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tournament_theory).[^o4]
