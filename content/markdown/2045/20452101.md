# Frances Yao

*[Chu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chu_%28Chinese_surname%29)*(maiden name) and *[Yao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yao_%28surname%29)*(married name)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frances Yao"}

**Frances Foong Chu Yao** () is a Chinese mathematician and theoretical computer scientist. She is currently a Chair Professor at the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Interdisciplinary_Information_Sciences) (IIIS) of [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University). She was Chair Professor and Head of the Department of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [City University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_Hong_Kong), where she is now an [honorary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_professor).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

After receiving a B.S. in mathematics from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) in 1969, Yao did her Ph.D. studies under the supervision of [Michael J. Fischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fischer) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), receiving her Ph.D. in 1973. Her doctoral dissertation was titled, "On Lower Bounds for Selection Problems."[^o5] She then held positions at the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign), [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), and [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), before joining the staff at the [Xerox Palo Alto Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerox_Palo_Alto_Research_Center) in 1979 where she stayed until her retirement in 1999.

In 2003, she came out of retirement to become the Head and a Chair Professor of the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, which she held until June 2011. She is a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science); in 1991, she and [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694) won the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._R._Ford) of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for their expository article, *A Whirlwind Tour of Computational Geometry*.[^n1]

Yao's husband, [Andrew Yao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Yao), is also a well-known theoretical computer scientist and [Turing Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_Award) winner.[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] In 2015, Frances Yao renounced her United States citizenship along with her husband.[^o12]

Much of Yao's research has been in the subject of [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) and combinatorial algorithms; she is known for her work with [Mike Paterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Paterson) on [binary space partitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_space_partitioning),[^n2] her work with Dan Greene on finite-resolution computational geometry,[^n3] and her work with Alan Demers and [Scott Shenker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Shenker) on [scheduling algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheduling_algorithm) for energy-efficient [power management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_management).[^n4]

More recently she has been working in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). Along with her husband Andrew Yao and [Wang Xiaoyun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xiaoyun), they found new attacks on the [SHA-1](wikipedia://en/26672) cryptographic hash function.[^o16][^o17]
