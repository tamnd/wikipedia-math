# Hung Cheng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hung Cheng"}

**Hung Cheng** (鄭洪; born March 2, 1937), also known as **Hong Zheng**, is an American mathematician, novelist, and physicist teaching at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Cheng earned his [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.S.) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), in 1959 and 1961, respectively. He had post-doctorate research appointments at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) before joining the MIT faculty in applied mathematics in 1965. His doctoral advisor was [Leverett Davis, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverett_Davis,_Jr.), and his thesis was on spin absorption lines of solids.

## Career {#Career}

In 1978, he was elected academician of Taiwan's [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica). He has also served as the chairman of the applied mathematics committee at the MIT Department of Mathematics, and is on the editorial board of the journal *[Studies in Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studies_in_Applied_Mathematics)*. His recent research interests have been directed to the mathematical physics of [dark matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_matter) and [dark energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_energy). In 2017, Cheng received the Distinguished Achievement Award in Technology and Humanity/Humanities from the [Chinese Institute of Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Institute_of_Engineers_USA).

In 2016, Cheng published his first novel, *[Nanjing Never Cries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Never_Cries)*.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Cheng lives in [Brookline, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookline,_Massachusetts).
