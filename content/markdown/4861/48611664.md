# Ju-Lee Kim

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ju-Lee Kim"}

**Ju-Lee Kim** (김주리, born 1969) is a South Korean mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT). Her research involves the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [*p*-adic groups](wikipedia://en/1842075).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kim completed her undergraduate studies at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) in 1991,[^o3] and earned a Ph.D. from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1997 supervised by [Roger Howe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Evans_Howe);[^o3] at Yale, she was also mentored by [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738).

After postdoctoral study at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), she joined the faculty as an assistant professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1998.[^o6] Kim joined the faculty at [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) in 2002, and then moved to MIT in 2007.[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2015 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to the representation theory of semisimple groups over nonarchimedean local fields and for service to the profession."[^o9]

## Personal {#Personal}

Her husband, [Paul Seidel](wikipedia://en/27678785), is also a mathematician at MIT.[^o7]
