# Equal parallelians point

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Equal parallelians point"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **equal parallelians point**[^o2][^o3] (also called **congruent parallelians point**) is a special point associated with a [plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) and it is denoted by *X*(192) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593).[^o4] There is a reference to this point in one of Peter Yff's notebooks, written in 1961.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: EqualParalleliansPoint.svg.* solid black solid red

The **equal parallelians point** of triangle $\triangle ABC$ is a point $P$ in the plane of $\triangle ABC$ such that the three [line segments](wikipedia://en/22634860) through $P$ parallel to the [sidelines](wikipedia://en/43958459) of $\triangle ABC$ and having endpoints on these sidelines have equal lengths.[^o2]

:::

## Trilinear coordinates {#Trilinear_coordinates}

The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the equal parallelians point of triangle $\triangle ABC$ are

$$bc(ca+ab-bc) \ : \ ca(ab+bc-ca) \ : \ ab(bc+ca-ab)$$

::: {#o9 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction for the equal parallelians point"}

## Construction for the equal parallelians point {#Construction_for_the_equal_parallelians_point}

*Image: ConstructionOfEqualParalleliansPoint.svg.* Construction of the equal parallelians point. solid black dashed green 2px solid cyan dashed blue 2px

Let $\triangle A'B'C'$ be the [anticomplementary triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticomplementary_triangle) of triangle $\triangle ABC$. Let the [internal bisectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_bisector) of the angles at the vertices $A, B, C$ of $\triangle ABC$ meet the opposite sidelines at $A'', B'', C''$ respectively. Then the lines $A'A'', B'B'', C'C''$ concur at the equal parallelians point of $\triangle ABC$.[^o3]

As a result, the equal parallelians point is the [isotomic conjugate](wikipedia://en/9492657) of the [incenter](wikipedia://en/316837) of and with respect to the anticomplementary triangle.

:::
