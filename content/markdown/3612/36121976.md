# Stefano Bianchini

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stefano Bianchini"}

**Stefano Bianchini** (born 1970) is an Italian mathematician known for his research on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). He won the 2004 [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) for his contributions to the theory of discontinuous solutions of one-dimensional hyperbolic conservation laws.[^o3]

:::

Bianchini earned his PhD from the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in 2000, under supervision of [Alberto Bressan](wikipedia://en/23804321). Along with Bressan, he co-authored a paper that led to the solution of the long-standing problem of stability and convergence of vanishing viscosity approximations.[^o4]
