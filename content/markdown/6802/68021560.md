# Aleksei Chernavskii

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksei Chernavskii"}

**Aleksei Viktorovich Chernavskii** (or **Chernavsky** or **Černavskii**) (Алексей Викторович Чернавский; 17 January 1938 – 22 December 2023) was a Russian mathematician, specializing in differential geometry and topology.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Chernavskii was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) and completed undergraduate study at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1959. He enrolled in graduate school at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). In 1964 he defended his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) (PhD) thesis, written under the guidance of [Lyudmila Keldysh](wikipedia://en/48580898), on the topic Конечнократные отображения многообразий (Finite-fold mappings of manifolds). In 1970 he defended his Russian [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) (habilitation) thesis Гомеоморфизмы и топологические вложения многообразий (Homeomorphisms and topological embeddings of manifolds). In 1970 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Nice.[^o4]

Chernavskii worked as a senior researcher at the Steklov Institute until 1973 and from 1973 to 1980 at [Yaroslavl State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslavl_State_University). From 1980 to 1985 he was a senior researcher at the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology). From 1985 he was employed the [Kharkevich Institute for Information Transmission Problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkevich_Institute_for_Information_Transmission_Problems) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5] From 1993 he was working part-time as a professor at the Department of Higher Geometry and Topology, Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Moscow State University. He wrote a textbook on differential geometry for advanced students.[^o6]

Chernavskii died on 22 December 2023, at the age of 85.[^o7]

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Chernavskii's theorem"}

## Chernavskii's theorem {#Chernavskii's_theorem}

**Chernavskii's theorem** (1964): If $M$ and $N$ are *n*-manifolds and $f$ is a discrete, open, continuous mapping of $M$ into $N$ then the branch set $B$<sub>$f$</sub> = { x: x is an element of $M$ and $f$ fails to be a local homeomorphism at x} satisfies dimension ($B$<sub>$f$</sub>) ≤ *n* – 2.[^o10][^o11][^o12]

:::
