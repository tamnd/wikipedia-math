# Matteo Mainardi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matteo Mainardi"}

*Image: Mainardi, Matteo – Opere, 1646 – BEIC IE7970797.jpg.* *Opere mercantili et economiche*, 1646

**Matteo Mainardi** ([Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna), late 16th century – after 1646) was an Italian [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and [arithmetician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetics).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Born in [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna), he is known for having done the history of the [bookkeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bookkeeping) in Italy in the 17th century, along with [Antonio Maria Villavecchia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Maria_Villavecchia), [Ambrogio Lerici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambrogio_Lerici), [Giovanni Domenico Peri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Domenico_Peri), [Simon Grisogono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Grisogono), [Giovanni Antonio Moschetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Antonio_Moschetti), [Lodovico Flori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodovico_Flori), and others. He wrote many books about bookkeeping, and a history of the origin of every church in Bologna. All of his works were reprinted in 1700 in Bologna by Longhi.[^o3] His 1632 work *La scrittura mercantile formatamente regolata* is considered particularly relevant.[^o4]
