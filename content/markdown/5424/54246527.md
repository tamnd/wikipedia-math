# Domenico Cocoli

*Image: Cocoli\_-\_Proposizioni\_fisico-matematiche,\_1775\_-\_1831.jpg.* *Proposizioni fisico-matematiche*, 1775

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Domenico Cocoli"}

**Domenico Cocoli** ([Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brescia), 12 August 1747 – 27 November 1812) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)[^o2][^o3] and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), especially active on [hydraulics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulics).[^o4] He was a top physicist of the late [Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice), then of the [Napoleonic Italian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Republic_%28Napoleonic%29).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brescia) in 1747, he began studying [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture) and later was awarded a pension by a wealthy patron to continue his studies.[^o4] In 1774 he had the chair of physics and mathematics. In 1777 he published his *Elements of Geometry and Trigonometry*.[^o4] Thanks to the fame of the book, he became a consultant of the [Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice) being among the five physicists who took care of the problem of the floodings of the [Brenta river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brenta_river).[^o4] Until 1797 he carried out various assignments.[^o4] In 1802 he was appointed member of the *Collegio dei Dotti* of the [Napoleonic Italian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Republic_%28Napoleonic%29).[^o4]In 1806 he became a general inspector inside the "*Corpo di Acque e Strade*", always inside the Napoleonic Italian Republic.[^o6]
