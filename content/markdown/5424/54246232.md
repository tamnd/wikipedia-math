# Lanfranco Mignoti

*Image: Ultima parte della geometria, nella quale s'insegna il vero modo di livellar l'acqua et sue misure, livellar molini, gli arzeni del Pò, con la sua proporzionata forma, et divisione , 1620 - 193354.jpg.* *Ultima parte della geometria*, 1620

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lanfranco Mignoti"}

**Lanfranco Mignoti** ([Piode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piode), 16th century – 17th century) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Born in [Piode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piode) in [Valsesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsesia),[^o2] he made a relevant contribution to [hydraulic engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic_engineering).
