# Johann Lantz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Lantz"}

*Image: Lantz\_-\_Institutionum\_arithmeticarum\_libri\_quatuor,\_1619\_-\_193839.jpg.* *Institutionum arithmeticarum libri quatuor*, 1619

**Johann Lantz** or **Lanz** (1564 – 20 September 1638) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Tettnang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tettnang) on [Lake Constance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Constance) in 1564, he was admitted as [novice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novice) in [Landsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landsberg_am_Lech) in 1589.[^o3] He became a professor of [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) at the [University of Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ingolstadt). After 1609-1610 he left his place to his pupil [Christoph Scheiner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Scheiner)[^o4] and moved to [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), where he died in 1638.[^o3]

He wrote several works on mathematics. He analyzed the four genres of numbers, then the astronomic fractions.[^o3] He is remembered also by [Mario Bettini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Bettini) in his *Aerarium philosophiae mathematicae* (1648).[^o3][^o5][^o6]
