# Camille-Christophe Gerono

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Camille-Christophe Gerono"}

**Camille-Christophe Gerono** (1799 in [Paris, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris,_France) – 1891 in Paris) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He concerned himself above all with [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). The [Lemniscate of Gerono](wikipedia://en/5211007) or *figure-eight curve* was named after him.[^o3] With [Olry Terquem](wikipedia://en/161565), he was founding co-editor in 1842 of the [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) *[Nouvelles Annales de Mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nouvelles_Annales_de_Mathématiques)*.[^o4]
