# Murray S. Klamkin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Murray S. Klamkin"}

**Murray Seymour Klamkin** (March 5, 1921 – August 6, 2004) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known as prolific proposer and editor of professionally-challenging mathematical problems.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Klamkin was born on March 5, 1921, in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). He received a bachelor's degree from the [Cooper Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Union) in 1942 and, after four years of service in the United States Army, earned a master's degree from the [Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_New_York) in 1947, where he taught from 1948 until 1957.

After this, Klamkin worked at [AVCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AVCO), taught at [SUNY Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo,_The_State_University_of_New_York) (1962–1964), and served as the principal research scientist at [Ford Motor Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Motor_Company) (1965–1976). During this period, he was also a visiting professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). After leaving Ford, he became a professor at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o4] From 1976 to 1981 Klamkin was the chairman of the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta).[^o5]

After 1981 he became an [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) professor at Alberta. Klamkin died August 6, 2004.[^o6]

## Mathematical problems {#Mathematical_problems}

Klamkin was known worldwide as a prolific proposer and editor of professionally challenging mathematical problems. He served as problem editor for *[SIAM Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Review)*, the *[American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly)*, *[Math Horizons](wikipedia://en/21340642)*, and other journals. He was also known for his work in high-level [mathematics competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_competitions), such as the [USA Mathematical Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America_Mathematical_Olympiad), the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), and the [Putnam Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition). In 1988 the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) gave him its Award for Distinguished Service to Mathematics, its highest service award.[^o8] In 1992, the [World Federation of National Mathematics Competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federation_of_National_Mathematics_Competitions) awarded Klamkin the [David Hilbert Award](wikipedia://en/39435849) for his contributions to mathematics competitions.[^o9]
