# Change detection (GIS)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Change detection"}

In [geographic information system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_information_system) (GIS), **change detection** is a process that measures how the [attributes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attribute_%28computing%29) of a particular area have changed between two or more time periods. Change detection often involves comparing aerial photographs or satellite imagery of the area taken at different times. Change detection has been widely used to assess shifting cultivation, [deforestation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deforestation), urban growth, impact of natural disasters like tsunamis, earthquakes, and use/land cover changes etc.[^o2]

:::
