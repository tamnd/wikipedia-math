# Hessian group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hessian group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Hessian group** is a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) of [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_group) 216, introduced by (txt) who named it for [Otto Hesse](wikipedia://en/740995). It may be represented as the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of [affine transformations](wikipedia://en/38449) with [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) 1 of the affine plane over the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of 3 elements.[^o3] It has a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) that is an [elementary abelian group](wikipedia://en/4843202) of order 3<sup>2</sup>, and the [quotient](wikipedia://en/11526) by this [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the group SL<sub>2</sub>(3) of order 24. It also acts on the [Hesse pencil](wikipedia://en/35283082) of [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), and forms the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the [Hesse configuration](wikipedia://en/35282956) of the 9 inflection points of these curves and the 12 lines through triples of these points.

:::

The triple cover of this group is a [complex reflection group](wikipedia://en/5381096), <sub>3</sub>\[3\]<sub>3</sub>\[3\]<sub>3</sub> or of order 648, and the [product](wikipedia://en/3706015) of this with a group of order 2 is another complex reflection group, <sub>3</sub>\[3\]<sub>3</sub>\[4\]<sub>2</sub> or of order 1296.
