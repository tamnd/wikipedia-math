# Charles Clayton Grove

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Clayton Grove"}

**Charles Clayton Grove** (1875–?) was an American mathematician who studied the [Hesse group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesse_group) and the [Hesse pencil](wikipedia://en/35283082).

:::
