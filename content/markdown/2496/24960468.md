# Robert Sorgenfrey

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Sorgenfrey"}

**Robert Henry Sorgenfrey** (August 14, 1915 – January 7, 1996) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor of Mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) at UCLA until his retirement in 1979.[^o6] The [Sorgenfrey line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorgenfrey_line) and the [Sorgenfrey plane](wikipedia://en/682853) are named after him; the Sorgenfrey line was the first example of a [normal](wikipedia://en/48629) [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) whose product with itself is not normal.

:::
