# The Code Book

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Code Book"}

***The Code Book: The Science of Secrecy from Ancient Egypt to Quantum Cryptography*** is a book by [Simon Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Singh), published in 1999 by [Fourth Estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Estate_%28imprint%29) and [Doubleday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubleday_%28publisher%29).

:::

*The Code Book* describes some illustrative highlights in the history of [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), drawn from both of its principal branches, [codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code) and [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244). Thus the book's title should not be misconstrued as suggesting that the book deals only with codes, and not with ciphers; or that the book is in fact a [codebook](wikipedia://en/530786).[^o3]

## Contents {#Contents}

*The Code Book* covers diverse historical topics including the [Man in the Iron Mask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_in_the_Iron_Mask), [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) cryptography, [Charles Babbage](wikipedia://en/5698), the mechanisation of cryptography, the [Enigma machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enigma_Machine), and the decryption of [Linear B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_B) and other ancient writing systems.[^o5][^o6]

Later sections cover the development of [public-key cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_key_cryptography). Some of this material is based on interviews with participants, including persons who worked in secret at [GCHQ](wikipedia://en/12884).

The book concludes with a discussion of "[Pretty Good Privacy](wikipedia://en/23080)" (PGP), [quantum computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_computer), and [quantum cryptography](wikipedia://en/28676005).

The book announced a "cipher challenge" of a series of ten progressively harder ciphers, with a cash prize of £10,000, which has since been won.[^o7]

The book is not footnoted but has a "Further Reading" section at the end, organized by chapter.
