# Alexa Beiser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexa Beiser"}

**Alexa S. Beiser** is an American professor of biostatistics and public health researcher.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Beiser did her PhD in mathematics at Boston University, following her M.A. at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) in Applied Mathematics and B.A. in Biology and Psychology from the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz).[^o4][^o5]

She has worked at the [Boston University School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University_School_of_Public_Health) since 1985, currently in the [Framingham Heart Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framingham_Heart_Study) (FHS) neurology group. Beiser co-developed the biostatistics doctoral program at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

She has worked on areas such as risk factors for [dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dementia),[^o8] how stress affects memory,[^o9] and how physical activity can improve health for people with diabetes.[^o10] She currently leads the FHS neurology group data management team, with a focus of analysing data relating to dementia.
