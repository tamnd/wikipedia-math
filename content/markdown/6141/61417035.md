# Oscar Randal-Williams

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Oscar Randal-Williams"}

**Oscar Randal-Williams** is a British mathematician and professor at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge),[^o3] working in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Randal-Williams studied mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (MMath 2006, DPhil 2009), where he wrote his doctoral thesis *Stable moduli spaces of manifolds* under the supervision of [Ulrike Tillmann](wikipedia://en/18517738). Since 2012 he has been at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), since 2017 as [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29), since 2020 as professor,[^o6] and since 2024 as the [Sadleirian Professor of Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/291556).[^o7]

In joint work with [Søren Galatius](wikipedia://en/45122752), he studied moduli spaces of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), leading to a sequence of papers about which his coauthor talked at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 2014.[^o8]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 2017 Randal-Williams received[^o10] a [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) from the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and a [Philip Leverhulme Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Leverhulme_Prize).[^o11][^o12] In 2018 he was awarded an ERC Starting Grant,[^o13] and in 2019 the [Dannie Heineman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dannie_Heineman_Prize_%28Göttingen%29) of the [Göttingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) and the [Oberwolfach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach_Prize). In 2022 he was awarded the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) jointly with Søren Galatius,[^o14] and spoke at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o15] In 2024 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society).[^o16]
