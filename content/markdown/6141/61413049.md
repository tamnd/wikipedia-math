# Marc van Kreveld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marc van Kreveld"}

van Kreveld **Marc Johan van Kreveld** is a Dutch [computational geometer](wikipedia://en/176927), known as one of the authors of the textbook *Computational Geometry: Algorithms and Applications* (with [Mark de Berg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_de_Berg), [Otfried Cheong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otfried_Cheong), and [Mark Overmars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Overmars), Springer, 1997; 3rd ed., 2008).

:::

Van Kreveld completed his Ph.D. in 1992 at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University). His dissertation, *New Results on Data Structures in Computational Geometry*, was supervised by [Mark Overmars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Overmars). He is a professor of computer science at Utrecht University.

With [Ferran Hurtado](wikipedia://en/57692403), van Kreveld was co-chair of the 2011 [Symposium on Computational Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symposium_on_Computational_Geometry). He has also worked in [geographic information systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_information_system), and (with Jürg Nievergelt, Thomas Roos, and Peter Widmayer) is the author of the textbook *Algorithmic Foundations of Geographic Information Systems* (Springer, 1997).
