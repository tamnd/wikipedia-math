# Peter L. Antonelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter L. Antonelli"}

*Image: Peter Antonelli, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (August, 2010).jpg.* Photo of Peter L. Antonelli (American mathematician) in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (August. 2010)

**Peter Louis Antonelli** (March 5, 1941 – February 15, 2020) was an American mathematician known for his work on [mathematical biology](wikipedia://en/662088), [Finsler geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_geometry), and their connections.

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

Antonelli was born on March 5, 1941, in [Syracuse, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse,_New_York), and became a student at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University), graduating in 1963.[^o3] He completed a PhD at Syracuse University, with the 1966 dissertation *Structure Theory for Montgomery-Samelson Fiberings Between Manifolds* supervised by Erik Hemmingsen.[^o4]

After a short period as assistant professor at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee), Knoxville from 1967 to 1968, and an NSF post-doctoral fellowship at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), from 1968 to 1970, he took a faculty position at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta), Canada, where he stayed for the remainder of his career.[^o3][^o6] In 2006, he moved to Brazil with his wife and colleague S.F. Rutz, where he was a visiting professor at [Federal University of Pernambuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Pernambuco), Recife.[^o7]

He died in 2020.[^o8]

## Contributions to mathematics {#Contributions_to_mathematics}

In his early years, Peter L. Antonelli's interests were focused on physics, especially [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024). As a Ph.D. student, he studied mathematical objects such as special groups of [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) and [exotic spheres](wikipedia://en/1114931). After 1970, his interests shifted towards applied mathematics, especially applications of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) to [developmental biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_biology), [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology), and [genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetics). As a visiting professor in the biology department at the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex) in the early 1970s, he pursued interests that had developed from his work in the early 1960s as a United States Public Health Service Fellow in mathematical biology at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o3]

During the course of his career, Peter L. Antonelli published over 120 research papers in a variety of domains including non-linear mechanics, [Hamiltonian systems](wikipedia://en/1197531), [diffusion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion_theory), [stochastic calculus](wikipedia://en/228001) and [stochastic geometry](wikipedia://en/22037813), [geometric probability](wikipedia://en/733613), differential [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924), [bifurcation theory](wikipedia://en/2326042), geometry of paths, and [Riemannian](wikipedia://en/195243), [Finslerian](wikipedia://en/385510) and Lagrangian geometries. The geometry of certain non-Riemannian metrics now bear his name.[^o3] Along with his extensive work on the mathematical ecology of the [Great Barrier Reef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Barrier_Reef),[^o10][^o11] he also showed that all living plants and animals are likely derived from two primitive species of bacteria, through the process of [endosymbiosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endosymbiosis).[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1987, Antonelli was awarded a McCalla Professorship at the University of Alberta for research excellence. In 2001, he was awarded the degree of Honorary Professor from [Alexandru Ioan Cuza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University) in Romania. Papers from a conference held there in honor of his 60th birthday were later published as a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift).[^o3]
