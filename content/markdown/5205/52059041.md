# John Brian Helliwell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Brian Helliwell"}

**John Brian Helliwell** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) FIMA (1924–1992) was a British mathematician and astrophysicist. He was Professor of Engineering Mathematics at [Bradford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_University) 1968 to 1985. He is remembered for his work on the behaviour of gases at transonic speeds and upon the action of conductive gases within magnetic fields.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in [York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York) on 2 January 1924. He was educated at [Goole Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goole_High_School) He then studied at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds), graduating with a BSc in 1945. His studies were interrupted by the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) during which he served first at the [Royal Aircraft Establishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Aircraft_Establishment) in [Farnborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farnborough,_Hampshire) then in the gas department of [Metropolitan-Vickers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan-Vickers) in [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester).[^o3] He gained his PhD from Leeds in 1949 on the subject of [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).[^o4]

After his PhD he remained in academia, first at Birmingham and Manchester, then as a Lecturer at the Royal College of Science and Technology in [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), now known as [Strathclyde University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strathclyde_University). In 1963 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [Donald Pack](wikipedia://en/57698032), [Benjamin Noble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Noble), [Reginald Lord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reginald_Lord), and [Patrick Dunbar Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Dunbar_Ritchie).[^o5]

In 1967 he moved to [Bradford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford) as Professor of Engineering Mathematics at [Bradford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_University), until his retirement due to ill-health in 1985. He returned to [North Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Yorkshire) where he died on 14 July 1992.[^o3]

## Family {#Family}

In 1951 he married Joyce Hutchinson. They had three daughters.[^o3]
