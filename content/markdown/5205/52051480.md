# Unikrn

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Unikrn"}

**Unikrn Inc.** (styled as "**Unikrn**") is an esports betting and entertainment media company based in [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami), Florida.

:::

In August 2021, international betting, gambling and interactive entertainment company [Entain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entain) acquired Unikrn.[^o3][^o4]

## Overview {#Overview}

Founded in November 2014, Unikrn primarily offers real money[^o6][^o7] and token-based wagering on competitive video game tournaments, otherwise known as [esports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esports).[^o8] Unikrn creates betting markets on [esports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esports) (organized video game competitions) matches akin to traditional betting.[^o9]

## History {#History}

The company was founded by [Rahul Sood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahul_Sood) and Karl Flores in November 2014.

In 2016, Unikrn became an investor in BIG (Berlin International Gaming),[^o11] a professional Counter-Strike: Global Offensive team. Unikrn also has an investment in [MAINGEAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAINGEAR),[^o12] a high-end PC manufacturer.

In the second quarter of 2017, Unikrn acquired LEET,[^o13] an esports startup based in Las Vegas, Nevada. Unikrn also owns an Indian-based esports company, DotaProHub.[^o14]

In November 2017, the company released UnikoinGold, an [ERC-20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ERC-20) [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188), running on the [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum) blockchain.[^o15][^o16][^o17]

In September 2020, Unikrn agreed to a settlement with the [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Securities_and_Exchange_Commission) after raising \$31 million via a digital asset called UnikoinGold in an unregistered 2019 [token sale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Token_sale) of digital.[^o18] Unikrn volunteered to settle without a determination of guilt by paying a \$6.1 million penalty to be paid via a [Fair Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_Fund) to its token holders. Unikrn also undertook to disable UnikoinGold [^o19] Unikrn settled despite lack of consensus within the SEC that Unikoingold constituted a security, with Commissioner Hester M. Peirce saying, "I do not concur in my colleagues’ opinion that Unikrn’s token offering constituted a securities offering\[...\] By failing to challenge ourselves to experiment with new approaches to regulation, we, and those whose interests we are pledged to serve, risk surrendering the fruits of innovation. I respectfully dissent from the Commission’s actions today relating to Unikrn."[^o20] She further clarified, "\[Unikrn\] is not alleged to have engaged in any fraud."
