# Wilson Sutherland

*Image: Wilson Sutherland plaque.jpg.* Plaque in [New College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wilson Sutherland"}

**Wilson Alexander Sutherland** (26 September 1935 – 7 October 2019) was a British mathematician at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

:::

Sutherland earned a doctorate at the University of Oxford in 1963 under the joint supervision of [J. H. C. Whitehead](wikipedia://en/385179) and [Ioan James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioan_James), with a dissertation in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).[^o8] He was, for many years, a lecturer in mathematics at Oxford, and a mathematics tutor at [New College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford). He also taught at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester), and, as a visiting professor, at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen).[^o9]

Sutherland died at his home in [Heversham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heversham), [Cumbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbria) on 7 October 2019 at the age of 84.[^o10]
