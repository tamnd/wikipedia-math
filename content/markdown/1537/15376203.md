# TI-35

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="TI-35"}

*Image: Texas Instruments TI-35 plus.jpg.* TI-35 plus

**Texas Instruments TI-35** is a series of [scientific calculators](wikipedia://en/439711) by [Texas Instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Instruments). The original TI-35 was notable for being one of Texas Instruments' first use of [CMOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMOS) controller chips in their designs, and was at the time distinguished from the lower-end [TI-30](wikipedia://en/811200) line by the addition of some [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) functions.

:::

## TI-35 (1979) {#TI-35_(1979)}

It was built with the same slimline design as the 1978 [TI-30](wikipedia://en/811200), but with different processor and slightly different set of features.

It used TP0324-4N processor (CMOS variant of [TMS1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMS1000) family).

The display can handle eight digits (five-digit [mantissa](wikipedia://en/419250) with a two-digit exponent) with 11-digit internal precision.

It was manufactured in the USA.

**1980**

It was a version with TP0324-4NL processor, which increased accuracy.

**1982**

It was a version with CD4557, which increased accuracy over the TP processor.

Cosmetic updates, include silver shell and new keyboard styling.

It was manufactured in the USA.

## TI-35 (1982) {#TI-35_(1982)}

It retained the 1982 styling, but used CD4557 processor.

## TI-35 GALAXY (1984) {#TI-35_GALAXY_(1984)}

It is a horizontal variant, but the functionality was identical to the European 1984 TI-30 GALAXY.

Solar version was called TI-35 GALAXY SOLAR.[^o5]

## TI-35 II (1984) {#TI-35_II_(1984)}

It is a replacement for the 1979 TI-35.

It used TP0456A or CD4557 processor.

It was originally built in Taiwan, but later in Italy and USA.

## TI-35 PLUS (1986) {#TI-35_PLUS_(1986)}

*Image: TI35-Plus.JPG.* TI-35 Plus

It added [hexadecimal](wikipedia://en/13263) and [octal](wikipedia://en/22330) calculations and a 10+2 display (i.e. ten-digit [mantissa](wikipedia://en/419250) with a two-digit exponent) with 12-digit internal precision.

## TI-35X (1991) {#TI-35X_(1991)}

The design was based on contemporary [TI-68](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TI-68). The cosmetics were updated in 1993.

## End of life {#End_of_life}

Following the update of TI-36X Solar in 1996, the TI-35 designation was discontinued after 10 years of coexisting with the TI-36 line.
