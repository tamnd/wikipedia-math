# Vyacheslav Lebedev (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vyacheslav Lebedev"}

**Vyacheslav Ivanovich Lebedev** (Вячеслав Иванович Лебедев) (January 27, 1930 – March 22, 2010) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work on numerical analysis.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Lebedev was a Ph.D. student of [Sobolev](wikipedia://en/380798). He worked at the [Kurchatov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurchatov_Institute) and Soviet/[Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), and taught students at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology). He authored over a hundred papers and several books, most noticeably "Numerical methods in the theory of neutron transport" jointly with [Gury Marchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gury_Marchuk) and "Functional Analysis in Computational Mathematics", based on his lectures. He graduated over 15 Ph.D. students. [Lebedev quadrature](wikipedia://en/4265440) has become one of the popular methods of integration on a sphere.

### Areas of expertise {#Areas_of_expertise}

He worked in many areas of computational and applied mathematics, ranging from software development for nuclear reactors modeling to approximation by polynomials, from [quadrature on a sphere](wikipedia://en/4265440) to numerical solution of [stiff equations](wikipedia://en/2091823), for which he developed explicit Chebyshev methods called DUMKA, [systems](wikipedia://en/249617) of [PDEs](wikipedia://en/52564), from [domain decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_decomposition) and [Poincaré–Steklov operators](wikipedia://en/25561272) to [finite difference methods](wikipedia://en/6054681), from iterative solvers to [parallel computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing). He even contributed to finding the roots of a cubic equation.

## Awards {#Awards}

He was a recipient of the [Soviet State Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_State_Award) for developing mathematical methods of particle transport theory in 1987 and [Chebyshev Gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev_Gold_medal) (ru:Золотая медаль имени П. Л. Чебышева) in 2002–03.
