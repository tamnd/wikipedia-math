# Steve Awodey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Awodey"}

**Steven M. Awodey**[^o4] (; born 1959) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician). He is a Professor of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Awodey studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). He earned his Ph.D. from Chicago under [Saunders Mac Lane](wikipedia://en/336574) in 1997. He is an active researcher in the areas of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), and has also written on the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439). He is one of the originators of the field of [homotopy type theory](wikipedia://en/33974223).[^o6] He was a member of the School of Mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2012–13.[^o7]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Awodey is the brother of late artist [Marc Awodey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Awodey).[^o9][^o10]
