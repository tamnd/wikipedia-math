# Andrei Knyazev (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrei Knyazev"}

**Andrew Knyazev** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He graduated from the [Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Computational_Mathematics_and_Cybernetics) of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of [Evgenii Georgievich D'yakonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgenii_Georgievich_D'yakonov) (Евгений Георгиевич Дьяконов) in 1981 and obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat_nauk) in [Numerical Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21506) at the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) under the supervision of [Vyacheslav Ivanovich Lebedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyacheslav_Ivanovich_Lebedev) (Вячеслав Иванович Лебедев) in 1985. He worked at the [Kurchatov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurchatov_Institute) between 1981–1983, and then to 1992 at the [Marchuk Institute of Numerical Mathematics](https://www.inm.ras.ru/en/) (ru:Институт вычислительной математики имени Г. И. Марчука РАН) of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences), headed by [Gury Marchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gury_Marchuk) (Гурий Иванович Марчук).

:::

From 1993–1994, Knyazev held a visiting position at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), collaborating with [Olof B. Widlund](wikipedia://en/27374393).[^o3] From 1994 until retirement in 2014, he was a Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver), supported by the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)[^o4] and [United States Department of Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Energy) grants. He was a recipient of the 2008 Excellence in Research Award,[^o5] the 2000 college Teaching Excellence Award, and a finalist of the CU President's Faculty Excellence Award for Advancing Teaching and Learning through Technology in 1999.[^o6] He was awarded the title of Professor Emeritus at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver)[^o7] and named the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) [Fellow](wikipedia://en/1883376#SIAM_Fellows) Class of 2016[^o8] and [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) [Fellow](wikipedia://en/198822#Fellows) Class of 2019.[^o9]

From 2012–2018, Knyazev worked at the [Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Electric_Research_Laboratories)[^o10] on algorithms for image and video processing, data sciences, optimal control, and material sciences, resulting in dozens of publications and 13 patent applications.[^o11] Since 2018, he contributed to numerical techniques in [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220) at [Zapata Computing](https://www.zapatacomputing.com), real-time embedded [anomaly detection](wikipedia://en/8190902) in [automotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automotive) data, and algorithms for [silicon photonics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_photonics)-based hardware.

Knyazev is mostly known for his work in numerical solution of large [sparse](wikipedia://en/341015) [eigenvalue problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue), particularly [preconditioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preconditioning)[^o12] and the iterative method [LOBPCG](wikipedia://en/26951185).[^o13] Knyazev's implementation of [LOBPCG](wikipedia://en/26951185) is available in many open source software packages, e.g., [BLOPEX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLOPEX), [SciPy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SciPy), and [ABINIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABINIT).[^o14]

Knyazev collaborated with [John Osborn](wikipedia://en/32072535) [^o15] on the theory of the [Ritz method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritz_method) in the [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581) context and with [Nikolai Sergeevich Bakhvalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Sergeevich_Bakhvalov) (Николай Серге́евич Бахвалов) ([Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) 3 via [Leonid Kantorovich](wikipedia://en/194660)) on numerical solution of [elliptic partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_partial_differential_equations) with large jumps in the main coefficients.[^o16] Jointly with his Ph.D. students, Knyazev pioneered using [majorization](wikipedia://en/2864495) for bounds in the [Rayleigh–Ritz method](wikipedia://en/2111994) (see[^o17] and references there) and contributed to the theory of [angles between flats](wikipedia://en/46955123).[^o18] [^o19]
