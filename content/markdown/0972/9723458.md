# Kalmanson combinatorial conditions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kalmanson combinatorial conditions"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Kalmanson combinatorial conditions** are a set of conditions on the [distance matrix](wikipedia://en/831350) used in determining the solvability of the [traveling salesman problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveling_salesman_problem). These conditions apply to a special kind of [cost matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost_matrix), the **Kalmanson matrix**, and are named after Kenneth Kalmanson.

:::
