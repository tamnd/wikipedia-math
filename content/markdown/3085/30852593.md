# Probability Theory and Related Fields

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Probability Theory and Related Fields"}

***Probability Theory and Related Fields*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). Established in 1962,[^o3] it was originally named ***Zeitschrift für Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie und verwandte Gebiete***, with the English replacing the German starting from volume 71 (1986).[^o4] The journal publishes articles on [probability](wikipedia://en/22934). The journal is indexed by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)* and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH). Its 2019 [MCQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Citation_Quotient) was 2.29, and its 2019 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) was 2.125.

:::

The current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are [Fabio Toninelli](wikipedia://en/66360967) (Technical University of Vienna) and [Bálint Tóth](wikipedia://en/16296902) (University of Bristol and Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics).[^o5]

The journal [CiteScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CiteScore) is 3.8 and its [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank) is 3.198, both from 2020.[^o6] It is currently ranked 11th in the field of Probability & Statistics with Applications according to [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar).[^o7]
