# Uniformly disconnected space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniformly disconnected space"}

In mathematics, a **uniformly disconnected space** is a [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018) $(X,d)$ for which there exists $\lambda > 0$ such that no pair of distinct points $x,y \in X$ can be connected by a $\lambda$-chain. A $\lambda$-chain between $x$ and $y$ is a sequence of points $x= x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_n = y$ in $X$ such that $d(x_i,x_{i+1}) \leq \lambda d(x,y), \forall i \in \{0,\ldots,n\}$.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Uniform disconnectedness is invariant under [quasi-Möbius maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-Möbius_map).[^o5]

:::
