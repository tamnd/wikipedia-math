# Moti Gitik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moti Gitik"}

**Moti Gitik** (מוטי גיטיק) is a mathematician, working in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), who is professor at the [Tel-Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel-Aviv_University). He was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the 2002 [International Congresses of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congresses_of_Mathematicians), and became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o3]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Gitik proved the [consistency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_consistency) of "all uncountable cardinals are [singular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cardinal)" (a strong negation of the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840)) from the consistency of "there is a proper class of [strongly compact cardinals](wikipedia://en/1957057)". He further proved the [equiconsistency](wikipedia://en/3435716) of the following statements:

- There is a cardinal *κ* with [Mitchell order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitchell_order) *κ*<sup>\++</sup>.
- There is a [measurable cardinal](wikipedia://en/248102) *κ* with 2<sup>*κ*</sup> > *κ*<sup>\+</sup>.
- There is a [strong limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_limit_cardinal) [singular cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_cardinal) *λ* with 2<sup>*λ*</sup> > *λ*<sup>\+</sup>.
- The [GCH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuum_Hypothesis) holds below ℵ<sub>ω</sub>, and 2<sup>ℵ<sub>ω</sub></sup>=ℵ<sub>ω+2</sub>.

Gitik discovered several methods for building models of ZFC with complicated Cardinal Arithmetic structure. His main results deal with consistency and equi-consistency of non-trivial patterns of the Power Function over singular cardinals.
