# Cyclocycloid

*Image: EpitrochoidIn3.gif.* The cyclocycloid (in this case an [epicycloid](wikipedia://en/713354)) with *R* = 3, *r* = 1 and *d* = 1/2

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclocycloid"}

A **cyclocycloid** is a [roulette](wikipedia://en/846500) traced by a point attached to a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) of [radius](wikipedia://en/1780815) *r* rolling around, a fixed circle of radius *R*, where the point is at a distance *d* from the center of the exterior circle.

:::

*Image: HypotrochoidOutThreeFifths.gif.* The red curve is a cyclocycloid (in this case an [hypocycloid](wikipedia://en/592735)) drawn as the smaller black circle rolls around inside the larger blue circle (parameters are *R* = 5, *r* = -3, *d* = 5).

The [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108) for a cyclocycloid are

$$x (\theta) = (R + r)\cos\theta - d\cos\left({R + r \over r}\theta\right),\,$$

$$y (\theta) = (R + r)\sin\theta - d\sin\left({R + r \over r}\theta\right).\,$$

where $\theta$ is a parameter (not the polar angle). And r can be positive (represented by a ball rolling outside of a circle) or negative (represented by a ball rolling inside of a circle) depending on whether it is of an [epicycloid](wikipedia://en/713354) or [hypocycloid](wikipedia://en/592735) variety.

The classic [Spirograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirograph) toy traces out these curves.
