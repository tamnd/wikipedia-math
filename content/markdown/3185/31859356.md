# Stephen Semmes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Semmes"}

**Stephen William Semmes** (born 26 May 1962) is the [Noah Harding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Harding) Professor of Mathematics at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University).[^o2] He is known for contributions to analysis on [metric spaces](wikipedia://en/20018), as well as [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_variable), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He received his B.S. at the age of 18, a Ph.D. at 21 from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) and became a full professor at Rice at 25.

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

Semmes was awarded a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_P._Sloan_Foundation) in 1987.[^o4] In 1994, he gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o5]
