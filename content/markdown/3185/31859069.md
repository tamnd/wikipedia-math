# Metric Structures for Riemannian and Non-Riemannian Spaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Metric Structures for Riemannian and Non-Riemannian Spaces"}

***Metric Structures for Riemannian and Non-Riemannian Spaces*** is a book in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) by [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov). It was originally published in French in 1981 under the title ***Structures métriques pour les variétés riemanniennes***, by CEDIC (Paris).

:::

## History {#History}

The 1981 edition was edited by [Jacques Lafontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Lafontaine) and [Pierre Pansu](wikipedia://en/19383362). The English version, considerably expanded, was published in 1999 by [Birkhäuser Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser_Verlag), with appendices by Pierre Pansu, [Stephen Semmes](wikipedia://en/31859356), and [Mikhail Katz](wikipedia://en/25830095). The book was well received[^o4][^o5] and has been reprinted several times.
