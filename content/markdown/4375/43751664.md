In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hexecontahedron** (or **hexacontahedron**[^o6]) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with 60 faces. There are many symmetric forms, and the ones with highest symmetry have [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844):

:::

Four [Catalan solids](wikipedia://en/564393), convex:

- [Pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196) - isosceles triangles
- [Deltoidal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/724753) - kites
- [Pentagonal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/727271) - pentagons
- [Triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/722873) - isosceles triangles

Concave

- [Rhombic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/43733185) - rhombi

27 [uniform star-polyhedral duals](wikipedia://en/2850448): (self-intersecting)

- [Small dodecicosacron](wikipedia://en/24081988), [Great dodecicosacron](wikipedia://en/18681460)
- [Small rhombidodecacron](wikipedia://en/24083182), [Great rhombidodecacron](wikipedia://en/24368340)
- [Small dodecacronic hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_dodecacronic_hexecontahedron), [Great dodecacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24369129)
- [Rhombicosacron](wikipedia://en/24083203)
- [Small icosacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24368929), [Medial icosacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24369037), [Great icosacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24083222)
- [Small stellapentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/24161157), [Great stellapentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/24161236)
- [Great pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/24369063)
- [Great triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/24368872)
- [Small ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24369027), [Great ditrigonal dodecacronic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24368960)
- [Medial deltoidal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24161179), [Great deltoidal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24161250)
- [Medial pentagonal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24161196), [Great pentagonal hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_pentagonal_hexecontahedron)
- [Medial inverted pentagonal hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medial_inverted_pentagonal_hexecontahedron), [Great inverted pentagonal hexecontahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_inverted_pentagonal_hexecontahedron)
- [Great pentagrammic hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/24368316)
