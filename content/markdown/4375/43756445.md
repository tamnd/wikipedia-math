# Al-Isfizari

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Al-Isfizari"}

**Abū Ḥātim al-Muẓaffar al-Isfazārī** (ابوحاتم مظفر اسفزاری; [fl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floruit). late 11th or early 12th century) was an Islamic [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) and engineer from [Khurasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khurasan). According to the historian and geographer [Ibn al-Athir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_al-Athir) and the [polymath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath) [Qutb al-Din al-Shirazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qutb_al-Din_al-Shirazi), he worked in the [Seljuq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seljuq_Empire) observatory of [Isfahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isfahan). The Persian writer [Nezami Aruzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nezami_Aruzi) met him in [Balkh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkh) in (in present-day Afghanistan) in 1112 or 1113.

:::

Al-Isfazārī was a contemporary of the Persian [polymath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath) [Umar al-Khayyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_al-Khayyam) and the Persian astronomer [Al-Khazini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Khazini). Al-Isfazārī's main surviving work, ar (*Guiding the Possessors of Learning in the Art of the Steelyard*), sets out the theory of the [steelyard balance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steelyard_balance) with unequal arms. His other surviving works include a summary of [Euclid's *Elements*](wikipedia://en/244107), a text on [geometrical measurements](wikipedia://en/18973446), and a treatise in [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) on [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904).

Al-Isfazārī's corpus of [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) is composed of two sets of texts, which have been published as ar(Text of Al-Muzaffar Al-Isfazar in the words Al-Taql and Al-Hail) by the [Al-Furqan Islamic Heritage Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Furqan_Islamic_Heritage_Foundation).
