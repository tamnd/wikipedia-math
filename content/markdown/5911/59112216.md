# ENO methods

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ENO methods"}

**ENO (essentially non-oscillatory) methods** are classes of [high-resolution schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-resolution_scheme) in numerical solution of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations).

:::

## History {#History}

The first ENO scheme was developed by [Harten](wikipedia://en/20340671), [Engquist](wikipedia://en/2070192), [Osher](wikipedia://en/3008100) and Chakravarthy in 1987. In 1994, the first [weighted version](wikipedia://en/59111950) of ENO was developed.[^o3]
