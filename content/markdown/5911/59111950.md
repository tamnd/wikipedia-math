# WENO methods

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="WENO methods"}

In numerical solution of differential equations, **WENO (weighted essentially non-oscillatory) methods** are classes of [high-resolution schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-resolution_scheme). WENO are used in the numerical solution of hyperbolic partial differential equations. These methods were developed from [ENO methods](wikipedia://en/59112216) (essentially non-oscillatory). The first WENO scheme was developed by Liu, [Osher](wikipedia://en/3008100) and Chan in 1994.[^o2] In 1996, Guang-Shan Jiang and [Chi-Wang Shu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi-Wang_Shu) developed a new WENO scheme[^o3] called WENO-JS.[^o4] Nowadays, there are many WENO methods.[^o5]

:::
