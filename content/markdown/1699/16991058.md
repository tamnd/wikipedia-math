# Grandi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grandi"}

**Grandi** is an Italian surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Achille Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achille_Grandi) (1883-1946), Italian catholic syndicalist
- [Alberto Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Grandi) (born 1967), Italian historian
- [Alessandro Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Grandi) (1586–1630), Italian composer
- [Allegro Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegro_Grandi) (1910–1973), Italian cyclist
- [Bruno Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Grandi) (1934–2019), Italian gymnast
- [Daniele Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniele_Grandi) (born 1993), Italian footballer
- [Dino Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dino_Grandi) (1895–1988), Italian politician
- [Ercole Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ercole_Grandi) (1491–1531), Italian painter
- [Filippo Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filippo_Grandi) (born 1957), Italian diplomat
- [Giuseppe Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Grandi) (1843–1894), Italian sculptor
- [Guido Grandi (priest)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_Grandi_%28priest%29) (1671–1742), Italian priest and mathematician
- [Guido Grandi (entomologist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_Grandi_%28entomologist%29) (1886–1970), Italian entomologist
- [Irene Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Grandi) (1969), Italian singer
- [Luigi Guido Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Guido_Grandi) (1671–1742), Italian monk, priest, philosopher, theologian, mathematician, and engineer
- [Margherita Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margherita_Grandi) (Margaret Gard) (1892–1971), Australian soprano
- [Marta Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_Grandi) (1915–2005), Italian entomologist
- [Matteo Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Grandi) (born 1992), Italian footballer
- [Serena Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Grandi) (1958), Italian actress
- [Stefano Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefano_Grandi) (born 1962), Italian swimmer
- [Thomas Grandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Grandi) (1972), Canadian alpine skier
