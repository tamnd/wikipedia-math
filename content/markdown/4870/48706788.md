# Erica Klarreich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Erica Klarreich"}

**Erica Gail Klarreich** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalism) and [science popularizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_science).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Klarreich's father was a professor of mathematics, and her mother was a mathematics teacher.[^o4]

Klarreich obtained her Ph.D. in mathematics under the guidance of [Yair Nathan Minsky](wikipedia://en/46905726) at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in 1997.[^o5]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

As a mathematician, Klarreich proved that the boundary of the [curve complex](wikipedia://en/51024643) is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the space of ending [laminations](wikipedia://en/23280456).[^o7]

## Popular science writing {#Popular_science_writing}

As a popular science writer, Klarreich's work has appeared in publications such as *[Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_%28journal%29)*, *[Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American)*, *[New Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Scientist)*, and *[Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976)*.[^o9][^o10] She is one of the winners of the 2021 [Joint Policy Board for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/4675178) Communications Award for her popular science writing.[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

As of 2025, Klarreich lives in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o13]
