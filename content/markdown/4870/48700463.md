# Pooling equilibrium

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pooling equilibrium"}

A **pooling equilibrium** in [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) is an [equilibrium](wikipedia://en/45337) outcome of a [signaling game](wikipedia://en/1395967).[^o2][^o3]

:::

In a [signaling game](wikipedia://en/1395967), players send actions called "signals" to other players. These signals are based on [privately held information](wikipedia://en/1603001), which is not known to others in the game.[^o4] These actions do not reveal a player's "type" to other players, who then choose their [strategies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy) accordingly. In a pooling equilibrium, all types of a given sender send the same signal. Some senders represent their true type, while others correctly mimic the type of others, having no [incentive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incentive) to differentiate themselves. As a result, the receiver acts as if they have received no information, maximizing their [utility](wikipedia://en/45479) according to their [prior](wikipedia://en/472877) beliefs.[^o5]
