# Bernard Epstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Epstein"}

*Image: Portrait Photo Bernard Epstein 1989.jpg.* Bernard Epstein 1989

**Bernard Epstein** (10 August 1920, [Harrison, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison,_New_Jersey) – 30 March 2005, [Montgomery County, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery_County,_Maryland)) was an American mathematician and physicist who wrote several widely used textbooks on mathematics.[^o2]

:::

Epstein was the son of Jewish immigrants from Lithuania and Romania, Yitzkhak Aharon Epstein and Sophie-Sarah née Goldenberg,[^o3] and was the first person in his family to go to college. He received bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics and physics from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) and then in 1947 a Ph.D. in applied mathematics, with thesis advisor [Maurice Heins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Heins), from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) with thesis *Method for the Solution of the [Dirichlet Problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_Problem) for Certain Types of Domains*.

In the early 1940s, he worked as a physicist at what is now the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology). During World War II, he was selected to join the [Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project), which produced the first atomic bombs. After the war, he worked for two years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a research associate, taught mathematics as an associate professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania),[^o5] [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and NYU and as a professor at [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University)[^o6] and then spent 21 years on the faculty of the [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico) as a professor of mathematics until his retirement in 1984.[^o2]

Sabbaticals included [Office of Naval Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Naval_Research), London;[^o7] The [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) in Haifa, Israel; [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland); and [Air Force Office of Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Office_of_Scientific_Research). After retirement, he taught at [George Mason University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mason_University).[^o6]

Epstein was dissertation advisor for the following Ph.D. students:

- Anne Scheerer, University of Pennsylvania, 1953 [^o4]
- William Trench, University of Pennsylvania, 1958 [^o4]
- [Jack Minker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Minker), University of Pennsylvania, 1959 [^o4]
- Edwin Sherry, Yeshiva University, 1964 [^o4]
- Darrell L. Hicks, University of New Mexico, 1969 [^o8]
- Harvey Z. Senter, Yeshiva University [^o9][^o10]

Upon his death at age 84, he was survived by his wife, five children, and 16 grandchildren.[^o2] His sixth child, a daughter, predeceased him.
