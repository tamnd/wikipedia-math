# Data for Black Lives

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Data for Black Lives"}

**Data for Black Lives** (**D4BL**) is an American [non-profit organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonprofit_organization) with the mission of using data science to create concrete and measurable change in the lives of black people.[^o3][^o4] Headquartered in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts), Data for Black Lives was founded by [Yeshimabeit Milner](wikipedia://en/66405338) and [Lucas Mason-Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Mason-Brown).[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o3] Milner attended [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University); having encountered discrimination towards the black community, she organized a group of scientists to combat the mistreatment of black people within data algorithms.[^o3][^o8]

:::

## History {#History}

D4BL began in November 2017 as statistical research project, and expanded into working with a team of people on data analysis. The formation of D4BL was initiated by Yeshimabeit Milner, who witnessed racial discrimination, watching her peers suffer from [police brutality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_brutality_in_the_United_States).[^o10] Milner discovered through research that black children were getting suspended at a much higher rate than white children.[^o11] After graduating from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), Milner incorporated her passion for [data science](wikipedia://en/35458904) into social activism.[^o11]

D4BL has regional organization chapters, including a group in [Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh).[^o12] Additionally there is a community of other organizations working towards the same goals, including Data and Society, [Algorithmic Justice League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_Justice_League), and the [Distributed Artificial Intelligence Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_Artificial_Intelligence_Research_Institute) (DAIR).[^o13]

Between 2019 and 2021, D4BL was awarded a grant by the [MacArthur Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Foundation) for broad operating support.[^o14][^o15]
