::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Snub rhombicuboctahedron"}

The **snub rhombicuboctahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213). It has 74 faces: 18 squares, and 56 triangles. It can also be called the [Conway](wikipedia://en/5021705) **snub cuboctahedron** in but will be confused with the Coxeter **snub cuboctahedron**, the [snub cube](wikipedia://en/180249). See [snub cuboctahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snub_cuboctahedron).

:::
