# Gabriele Nebe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriele Nebe"}

**Gabriele Nebe** (born 1967)[^o3] is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) with contributions in the theory of [lattices](wikipedia://en/469246), [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000), [spherical designs](wikipedia://en/9730285), and [error-correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error-correcting_codes). With [Neil Sloane](wikipedia://en/400062), she maintains the Online Catalogue of Lattices.[^o4] She is a professor in the department of mathematics at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Nebe earned a doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) in 1995 from [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University). Her dissertation, *Endliche Rationale Matrixgruppen vom Grad 24* concerned the theory of finite [matrix groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_group) and was supervised by Wilhelm Plesken.

## Research {#Research}

Nebe is known for using integral representations of finite groups to construct explicit examples of discrete mathematical structures using [computer algebra systems](wikipedia://en/54427). Her constructions include extremal even [unimodular lattices](wikipedia://en/1648605) in 48, 56, and 72 dimensions and an extremal 3-modular lattice in 64 dimensions.[^o4] These lattices represent the [densest](wikipedia://en/43518103) known [sphere packings](wikipedia://en/368621) and the highest known [kissing numbers](wikipedia://en/408555) in these dimensions. Her discovery of an extremal unimodular lattice in 72 dimensions settled a long open problem.[^o8]
