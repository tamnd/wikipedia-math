# Walter Carnielli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Carnielli"}

*Image: Walter Carnielli.jpg.* Walter Carnielli in Berkeley in 1984

**Walter Alexandre Carnielli** (born 11 January 1952 in [Campinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campinas), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil)) is a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), who works as a full professor of Logic at the [State University of Campinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_Campinas). After obtaining his Bachelor and M.Sc. degrees in mathematics at the State University of Campinas, he also obtained his Ph.D. in 1984 there under the supervision of [Newton da Costa](wikipedia://en/1537143); subsequently, he worked as a [post-doc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), following an invitation by [Leon Henkin](wikipedia://en/356165).

:::

## Areas of interest {#Areas_of_interest}

### Many-valued logic and paraconsistent logic {#Many-valued_logic_and_paraconsistent_logic}

Carnielli contributed to the [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478) and [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107) of [many-valued logics](wikipedia://en/38024) and [paraconsistent logics](wikipedia://en/421085). His [tableau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_tableau) method for many-valued logics generalized all previous treatments of the subject.[^o4] His proposal of the possible-translations semantics (a new semantical interpretation for paraconsistent logics) contributed to a revival in the philosophical interpretation of paraconsistent logics.[^o5][^o6]

The logics of formal inconsistency, which systematize a large class of paraconsistent logics, opened the way to the application of paraconsistency to computer science and to new philosophical investigations on paraconsistency.

### Combinatorics, modulated logics, and combinations of logics {#Combinatorics,_modulated_logics,_and_combinations_of_logics}

He also published on finite and infinite [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and developed (with his collaborators A. M. Sette and P. A. Veloso) the modulated logics, a new kind of logics that allows the formalization of qualitative reasoning by means of special generalized quantifiers. His research also includes [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), [non-classical logics](wikipedia://en/3192556), foundations of [quantum computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_computer), and combinations of logics.

## Positions and awards {#Positions_and_awards}

Carnielli served as a Director for the Centre for Logic, Epistemology and the History of Science at UNICAMP for three terms, and served as President of the [Brazilian Logic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Logic_Society). He was distinguished with an [Alexander von Humboldt grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation) for long-term research stays in Germany, and served as en editor and/or a member of editorial boards of major journals, such as *[Studia Logica](wikipedia://en/27777526)*, *Logic and Logical Philosophy*, *[Journal of Applied Logic](http://www.elsevier.com/wps/find/journaldescription.cws_home/672712/description#description)*, *CLE e-Prints*, *Reports on Mathematical Logic* and *[Journal of Applied Non Classical Logics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Applied_Non_Classical_Logics)*. He received the Telesio-Galilei Gold Medal Award in Philosophy and Mathematics in 2012.
