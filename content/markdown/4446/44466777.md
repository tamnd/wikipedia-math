# Theta constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theta constant"}

In mathematics, a **theta constant** or **Thetanullwert** (German for theta zero value; plural **Thetanullwerte**) is the restriction *θ*<sub>*m*</sub>(*τ*) = *θ*<sub>*m*</sub>(*τ*,0) of a [theta function](wikipedia://en/449745) *θ*<sub>*m*</sub>(*τ*,*z*) with rational characteristic *m* to *z* = 0. The variable *τ* may be a [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) in the [upper half-plane](wikipedia://en/287826) in which case the theta constants are modular forms, or more generally may be an element of a Siegel upper half plane in which case the theta constants are [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927). The [theta function of a lattice](wikipedia://en/24016906) is essentially a special case of a theta constant.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The theta function *θ*<sub>*m*</sub>(*τ*,*z*) = *θ*<sub>*a*,*b*</sub>(*τ*,*z*) is defined by

$$\theta_{a,b}(\tau,z) = \sum_{\xi\in Z^n} \exp\left[\pi {\rm{i}}(\xi+a)\tau(\xi+a)^t + 2\pi i(\xi+a)(z+b)^t\right]$$

where

- *n* is a positive [integer](wikipedia://en/14563), called the genus or rank.
- *m* = (*a*,*b*) is called the characteristic
- *a*,*b* are in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>
- *τ* is a complex *n* by *n* matrix with positive definite imaginary part
- *z* is in **C**<sup>*n*</sup>
- *t* means the transpose of a row vector.

If *a*,*b* are in **Q**<sup>*n*</sup> then *θ*<sub>*a*,*b*</sub>(*τ*,0) is called a **theta constant**.

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

If *n* = 1 and *a* and *b* are both 0 or 1/2, then the functions *θ*<sub>*a*,*b*</sub>(*τ*,*z*) are the four [Jacobi theta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_theta_functions), and the functions θ<sub>*a*,*b*</sub>(*τ*,0) are the classical Jacobi theta constants. The theta constant *θ*<sub>1/2,1/2</sub>(*τ*,0) is identically zero, but the other three can be nonzero.

:::
