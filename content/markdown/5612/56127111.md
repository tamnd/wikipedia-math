# Po-Shen Loh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Po-Shen Loh"}

**Po-Shen Loh** (; born June 18, 1982) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). Loh teaches at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), and from 2014 to 2023 served as the national coach of the United States' [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) team. He is the founder of educational websites Expii and Live, and lead developer of [contact-tracing app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_tracing_app) NOVID.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Loh was born on June 18, 1982, in California[^o4] and raised in [Madison, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison,_Wisconsin), to Singaporean immigrants Wei-Yin and Theresa Loh. As a middle school student, Loh twice represented Wisconsin in the national [Mathcounts](wikipedia://en/530021) competition. He attended [James Madison Memorial High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vel_Phillips_Memorial_High_School), and in 1999 won a silver medal representing the US in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO).

Loh studied mathematics as an undergraduate student at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). In 2003, he won a [Goldwater Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_M._Goldwater_Scholarship). In 2004, he graduated with honors, ranked first in his graduating class, and his undergraduate thesis received an honorable mention for the 2004 [Morgan Prize](wikipedia://en/4180157). Loh completed a one-year master's degree at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) on a [Churchill Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_Scholarship).[^o6]

Loh pursued graduate studies in mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) with the support of a [Hertz Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertz_Foundation), and, under the supervision of [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803), received a Ph.D. in 2010 with his dissertation *Results in extremal and probabilistic combinatorics*.

## Career {#Career}

### Teaching and coaching {#Teaching_and_coaching}

Loh's math coaching career started in 2002 when he first served as an assistant coach at the US national IMO training camp, [Mathematical Olympiad Summer Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Olympiad_Summer_Program). In 2010, Loh was appointed deputy leader Team USA for the IMO, and in 2014 he was appointed leader and was the national coach for 9 years, until 2023.[^o9] Under his coaching, the team won the competition in 2015, 2016, 2018, and 2019—their first victories since 1994.

Loh has been a professor at Carnegie Mellon University since 2010, where he teaches courses on [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [extremal combinatorics](wikipedia://en/2675917). Loh runs the training seminar for the [Putnam competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) for Carnegie Mellon undergraduates.

### Research {#Research}

Loh works at the intersections of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), and as of 2025 he has written 41 publications.[^o11]

In 2019, Loh developed an alternative method and exposition for the solution of [quadratic equations](wikipedia://en/25175), based on the symmetry of [parabolas](wikipedia://en/23231).

### Other projects {#Other_projects}

Loh is a creator of expository math videos on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) under the channel name *Daily Challenge with Po-Shen Loh*. He has also made many appearances on other math-related channels, which have collectively been viewed millions of times.[^o13] Loh's videos have been praised for their attractive diagrams and high quality.[^o14]

Loh is the founder of Expii, a [crowdsourced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowdsourcing) math lesson and problem solving website with tens of thousands of users.[^o15] Loh also founded Live, a math education site in which student teachers teach small courses surrounding middle and high school competition math concepts via [livestreaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestreaming) and [video chat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videotelephony), as a way to improve interactivity of remote learning during the COVID-19 pandemic.[^o16]

In March 2020, Loh and other Hertz fellows were asked to assist in helping combat the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic). They developed NOVID, a [contact tracing app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_tracing_app) with the unique feature of notifying users before exposure, whenever someone in their social network is affected, rather than after. NOVID tracks infections anonymously using location information gathered from inter-communicating cell phones,[^o17] and was tested in pilot studies on college campuses.

## Family {#Family}

Loh is the son of [Wei-Yin Loh](wikipedia://en/81119994), a Singaporean statistician at the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin). His sister [Po-Ling Loh](wikipedia://en/81227908) is a professor of statistics at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and his brother Po-Ru Loh is an associate professor of medicine at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). His uncle, Wei-Liem Loh, is a statistician at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880).
