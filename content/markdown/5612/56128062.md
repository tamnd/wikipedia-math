# Gaspare Mignosi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaspare Mignosi"}

**Gaspare Mignósi** (1875, Palermo – 1951, Palermo) was an Italian mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Mignosi became in 1930 a professor of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari) and in 1932 a professor at the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo). He continued the studies of the school of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), which flourished in Sicily around 1920 with [Gaetano Scorza](wikipedia://en/35074517) and [Michele Cipolla](wikipedia://en/25816120). Particularly noteworthy is the contribution made by Mignosi to the so-called apiristic solution of binomial congruences.[^o2]

Mignosi determined the fundamental subgroups of the linear projective group of dimension two over a field.[^o3]

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 in Bologna.[^o4]
