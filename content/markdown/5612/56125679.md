# Journal of Commutative Algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Commutative Algebra"}

The ***Journal of Commutative Algebra*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of [mathematical research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_research) that specializes in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and closely related fields. It has been published by the [Rocky Mountain Mathematics Consortium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountain_Mathematics_Consortium) (RMMC) since its establishment in 2009. It is currently published four times per year.[^o3]

:::

Historically, the *Journal of Commutative Algebra* filled a niche for the Rocky Mountain Mathematics Consortium when the *[Canadian Applied Mathematics Quarterly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Applied_Mathematics_Quarterly)*, formerly published by the RMMC, was acquired by the [Applied Mathematics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics_Institute) of the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta). Founding editors [Jim Coykendall](wikipedia://en/70468783) (currently at [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University)) and [Hal Schenck](wikipedia://en/67786208) (currently at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University)) began the journal with the goal of creating a top-tier journal in commutative algebra.
