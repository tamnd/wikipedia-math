# Bifundamental representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bifundamental representation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028), a **bifundamental** representation is a [representation](wikipedia://en/19378200) obtained as a [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of two [fundamental](wikipedia://en/649743) or [antifundamental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antifundamental_representation) representations.

:::

For example, the *MN*-dimensional representation (*M*,*N*) of the group

$$SU(M) \times SU(N)$$

is a bifundamental representation.[^o2]

These representations occur in [quiver diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quiver_diagram).
