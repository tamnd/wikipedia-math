# R. M. Wilson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="R. M. Wilson"}

**Richard Michael Wilson** (23 November 1945) is a mathematician and a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o5] Wilson and his PhD supervisor [Dijen K. Ray-Chaudhuri](wikipedia://en/18064578), solved [Kirkman's schoolgirl problem](wikipedia://en/8876082) in 1968.[^o3] Wilson is known for his work in [combinatorial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_mathematics), as well as on historical [flutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flute).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Wilson was educated at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) where he was awarded a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree in 1966.[^o5] followed by a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1968. His [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD), also from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) was awarded in 1969 for research supervised by [Dijen K. Ray-Chaudhuri](wikipedia://en/18064578).

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

His breakthrough in [pairwise balanced designs](wikipedia://en/5170#pairwise_balanced_designs), and [orthogonal Latin squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graeco-Latin_square#Mutually_orthogonal_Latin_squares) built upon the groundwork set before him, by [R. C. Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._C._Bose), [E. T. Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._T._Parker), [S. S. Shrikhande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Shrikhande), and [Haim Hanani](wikipedia://en/37650460) are widely referenced in [combinatorial design theory](wikipedia://en/2761968) and [coding theory](wikipedia://en/5170#Coding_theory).[^o8]

## Musical career {#Musical_career}

Wilson is also one of the world's top experts on historical flutes.[^o10]
