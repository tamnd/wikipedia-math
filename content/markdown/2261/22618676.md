# Paul Garabedian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Garabedian"}

**Paul Roesel Garabedian** (August 2, 1927 – May 13, 2010) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506).[^o3] Garabedian was the Director-Division of Computational Fluid Dynamics at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o4] He is known for his contributions to the fields of [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924) and [plasma physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_physics), which ranged from elegant [existence](wikipedia://en/251177) [proofs](wikipedia://en/82285) for [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342) and [conformal mappings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_mapping)[^o5] to the design and optimization of [stellarators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellarator).[^o6] Garabedian was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1975.[^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati), [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio), Garabedian received a bachelor's degree from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1946 and a master's degree from the [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1947, both in mathematics. He received his Ph.D., also from Harvard University, in 1948 under the direction of [Lars Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636).[^o9] It was at Brown University that he met his longtime colleague and collaborator, [Frances Bauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Bauer).[^o10]

In 1949 Garabedian joined the faculty at the [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California) as an Assistant Professor and became associate professor in 1952. In 1956, he moved to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) as a professor of mathematics. In 1959 he moved to the Institute of Mathematical Sciences \[later renamed the Courant Institute\] at New York University. In 1978 he was appointed the Director-Division of [Computational Fluid Dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_Fluid_Dynamics) at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), New York University. In a long and fruitful academic career, Garabedian supervised 27 Ph.D. theses. The first was in 1953 (Edward McLeod) and the last came in 1997 (Connie Chen).

- Fairchild Distinguished Scholar [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), 1975
- NASA Public Service Group Achievement Award by [NASA Langley Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley_Research_Center), 1976

- [Theodore von Kármán Prize](wikipedia://en/24419775), [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376), 1989
