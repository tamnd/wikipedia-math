# Herz–Schur multiplier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herz–Schur multiplier"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), a **Herz–Schur multiplier** (named after [Carl S. Herz](wikipedia://en/42020168) and [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522)) is a special kind of mapping from a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) to the [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let Ψ be a mapping of a group *G* to the complex numbers. It is a Herz–Schur multiplier if the induced map Ψ: *N*(*G*) → *N*(*G*) is a [completely positive map](wikipedia://en/6083511), where *N*(*G*) is the closure of the span *M* of the image of λ in *B*(*ℓ*<sup>2</sup>(*G*)) with respect to the [weak topology](wikipedia://en/33662), λ is the left [regular representation](wikipedia://en/360011) of *G* and Ψ is on *M* defined as

$$\Psi:~\sum\limits_{g\in G}\mu_g\lambda_g\mapsto\sum\limits_{g\in G}\psi(g)\mu_g\lambda_g.$$

:::
