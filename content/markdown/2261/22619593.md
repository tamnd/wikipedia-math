# Carl Kostka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Kostka"}

**Carl Gottfried Franz Albert Kostka** (3 December 1846 in [Lyck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ełk) – 28 December 1921 in [Insterburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insterburg)) was a mathematician who introduced [Kostka numbers](wikipedia://en/22606041) in 1882. He lived and worked in [Insterburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insterburg).

:::
