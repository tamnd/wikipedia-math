# Semiperfect magic cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semiperfect magic cube"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **semiperfect magic cube** is a [magic cube](wikipedia://en/372356) that is not a [perfect magic cube](wikipedia://en/372373), i.e., a magic cube for which the [cross section](wikipedia://en/1337370) diagonals do not necessarily sum up to the cube's [magic constant](wikipedia://en/546969).

:::
