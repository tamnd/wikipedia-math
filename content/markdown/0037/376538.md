# Truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated dodecahedron** is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847). It has 12 regular [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) faces, 20 regular [triangular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular) faces, 60 vertices and 90 edges.

*Image: Truncated dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a truncated dodecahedron

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The truncated dodecahedron is constructed from a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) by cutting all of its vertices off, a process known as [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429). Alternatively, the truncated dodecahedron can be constructed by [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070): pushing away the edges of a regular dodecahedron, forming the [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) faces into [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) faces, as well as the vertices into [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654). Therefore, it has 32 faces, 90 edges, and 60 vertices.

The truncated dodecahedron may also be constructed by using [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate). With an edge length $2\varphi - 2$ centered at the origin, they are all even permutations of

$$\left(0, \pm \frac{1}{\varphi}, \pm (2 + \varphi) \right), \qquad \left(\pm \frac{1}{\varphi}, \pm \varphi, \pm 2 \varphi \right), \qquad \left(\pm \varphi, \pm 2, \pm (\varphi + 1) \right),$$

where $\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

The surface area $A$ and the volume $V$ of a truncated dodecahedron of edge length $a$ are:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= 5 \left(\sqrt{3}+6\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right) a^2 &&\approx 100.991a^2 \\ V &= \frac{5}{12} \left(99+47\sqrt{5}\right) a^3 &&\approx 85.040a^3 \end{aligned}$$

The dihedral angle of a truncated dodecahedron between two regular dodecahedral faces is 116.57°, and that between triangle-to-dodecahedron is 142.62°.

The truncated dodecahedron is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847), meaning it is a highly symmetric and semi-regular polyhedron, and two or more different regular polygonal faces meet in a vertex.[^o14] It has the same symmetry as the regular icosahedron, the [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844).[^o17] The polygonal faces that meet for every vertex are one equilateral triangle and two regular decagon, and the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of a truncated dodecahedron is $3 \cdot 10^2$. The dual of a truncated dodecahedron is [triakis icosahedron](wikipedia://en/722873), a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), which shares the same symmetry as the truncated dodecahedron.

The truncated dodecahedron is non-[chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160), meaning it is congruent to its mirror image.

## Truncated dodecahedral graph {#Truncated_dodecahedral_graph}

*Image: Truncated dodecahedral graph.png.* The graph of a truncated dodecahedron

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a *truncated dodecahedral graph* is the [graph of vertices and edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-skeleton) of the *truncated dodecahedron*, one of the [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847). It has 60 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 90 edges, and is a [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [Archimedean graph](wikipedia://en/44969442).

:::

## Related polyhedron {#Related_polyhedron}

The truncated dodecahedron can be applied in the polyhedron's construction known as the [augmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_%28geometry%29). Examples of polyhedrons are the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034), whose constructions are involved by attaching [pentagonal cupolas](wikipedia://en/1131073) onto the truncated dodecahedron: [augmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233344), [parabiaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233345), [metabiaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233346), and [triaugmented truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/1233347).
