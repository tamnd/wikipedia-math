# 69 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="69"}

**69** (**sixty-nine**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [68](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/68_%28number%29) and preceding [70](wikipedia://en/343334). An [odd number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odd_number) and a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), 69 is [divisible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisible) by [1](wikipedia://en/77591639), [3](wikipedia://en/208157), [23](wikipedia://en/362203), and 69.

:::

The number and its pictograph give the name to the [sexual position](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_position) of [the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69ing). The association of the number with this particular sex position has resulted in it being associated in [meme culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_meme) also with sex.

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

69 is a [semiprime](wikipedia://en/339542), or a natural number that is the [product](wikipedia://en/24977) of exactly two prime numbers (3 and 23). It is an [interprime](wikipedia://en/2923417), sitting between [67](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/67_%28number%29) and [71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71_%28number%29).[^o4][^o5] 69 is not divisible by any [square number](wikipedia://en/213919) other than 1, making it a [square-free integer](wikipedia://en/29525).[^o6] 69 is a [Blum integer](wikipedia://en/2218352) since the two factors of 69 are both [Gaussian primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_prime), and an [Ulam number](wikipedia://en/8711785), an integer that is the sum of two distinct previously occurring Ulam numbers in a sequence.[^n1][^o7] 69 is a [deficient number](wikipedia://en/321843) because the [sum of its proper divisors](wikipedia://en/12374274) (which excludes itself) is less than itself.[^o8] As an integer for which the [arithmetic mean](wikipedia://en/612) average of its [positive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_number) [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492) is also an integer, 69 is an [arithmetic number](wikipedia://en/36551969).[^o9] 69 is a [congruent number](wikipedia://en/2334769)—a positive integer that is the area of a [right triangle](wikipedia://en/39420) with three [rational number](wikipedia://en/19727024) sides—and an [amenable number](wikipedia://en/1372167).[^o10][^o11] 69 can be expressed as the sum of consecutive positive integers in multiple ways, making it a [polite number](wikipedia://en/4047259).[^o12] 69 is a [lucky number](wikipedia://en/208183) because it is a natural number that remains after repeatedly removing every *n*th number in a sequence of natural numbers, starting from 1.[^o13][^o14]

In [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214), 69 is the only natural number whose [square](wikipedia://en/659942) (4,761) and [cube](wikipedia://en/451445) (328,509) use every digit from 0–9 exactly once.[^o15][^o16] It is also the largest number whose [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) is less than a [googol](wikipedia://en/18994679). On many handheld scientific and graphing [calculators](wikipedia://en/7593), 69! (1.711224524) is the highest factorial that can be calculated due to memory limitations.[^o17] In its [binary expansion](wikipedia://en/238686) of 1000101,[^o18] 69 is equal to [105](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/105_%28number%29) [octal](wikipedia://en/22330), while 105 is equal to 69 [hexadecimal](wikipedia://en/13263) (this same property can be applied to all numbers from [64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64_%28number%29) to 69).[^o19][^o20] In [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing), 69 equates to 2120 in [ternary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_3) (base-3); 153 in [senary](wikipedia://en/29652) (base-6); and 59 in [duodecimal](wikipedia://en/8400) (base-12).[^o21][^o22][^o23]

Visually, in [Arabic numerals](wikipedia://en/1786), 69 is a [strobogrammatic number](wikipedia://en/2906596) because it looks the same when viewed both right-side and upside down.[^o24] 69 is a [centered tetrahedral number](wikipedia://en/39528968), a [figurate number](wikipedia://en/325260) that represents a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) with a triangular base and all other points arranged in layers above the base, forming a [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) shape.[^o25] 69 is also a [pernicious number](wikipedia://en/36585685) because there is a prime number of 1s when it is written as a [binary number](wikipedia://en/238686), and an [odious number](wikipedia://en/2924960), as it is a positive integer that has an odd number of 1s in its binary expansion.[^o26][^o27]

## In culture {#In_culture}

[69ing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69ing) is a [sex position](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_position) wherein each partner aligns themselves to simultaneously achieve [oral sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_sex) with each other.[^o29] In reference to this sex act, the number 69 itself has become an [Internet meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_meme) as an inherently funny number in which users will respond to any occurrence of the number with the word "nice" to draw specific attention to it.[^o30] This means to humorously imply that the reference to the sex position was intentional. Because of its association with the sex position and resulting meme, 69 has been named "the sex number".[^o30]

On American highways, "69 mile" marker signs are frequently [stolen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_sign_theft). As a result, the [Colorado DOT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Department_of_Transportation) began replacing them with "68.5 mile" ones.[^o31][^o32]
