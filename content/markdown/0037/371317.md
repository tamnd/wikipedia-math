# Quantitative psychological research

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantitative psychological research"}

**Quantitative psychological research** is psychological research that employs [quantitative research methods](wikipedia://en/389564).[^o2]

:::

Quantitative research falls under the category of [empirical research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_studies).[^o3]
