# Quaternionic discrete series representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quaternionic discrete series representation"}

In mathematics, a **quaternionic discrete series representation** is a [discrete series representation](wikipedia://en/7205312) of a [semisimple Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group) *G* associated with a quaternionic structure on the [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877) of *G*. They were introduced by (txt).

:::

Quaternionic discrete series representations exist when the [maximal compact subgroup](wikipedia://en/1496726) of the group *G* has a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) isomorphic to [SU(2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SU%282%29). Every complex simple [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) has a real form with quaternionic discrete series representations. In particular the classical groups SU(2,*n*), SO(4,*n*), and Sp(1,*n*) have quaternionic discrete series representations.

Quaternionic representations are analogous to [holomorphic discrete series representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_discrete_series_representation), which exist when the symmetric space of the group has a complex structure. The groups SU(2,*n*) have both holomorphic and quaternionic discrete series representations.
