::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="16-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **16-cell honeycomb** is one of the three regular space-filling [tessellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessellations) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)), represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,4,3}, and constructed by a 4-dimensional packing of [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29), three around every (triangular) face.

:::

Its dual is the [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422). Its vertex figure is a [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485). The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the B<sub>4</sub>, D<sub>4</sub>, or [F<sub>4</sub> lattice](wikipedia://en/292877#F4_lattice).[^o9][^o10]

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Hexadecachoric tetracomb/honeycomb
- Demitesseractic tetracomb/honeycomb

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

Vertices can be placed at all integer coordinates (i,j,k,l), such that the sum of the coordinates is even.

## D<sub>4</sub> lattice {#D4_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the 16-cell honeycomb is called the [D<sub>4</sub> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D4_lattice) or F<sub>4</sub> lattice.[^o10] The vertices of this lattice are the centers of the [3-spheres](wikipedia://en/39792) in the densest known [packing](wikipedia://en/368621) of equal spheres in 4-space;[^n1] its [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) is 24, which is also the same as the kissing number in **R**<sup>4</sup>, as proved by Oleg Musin in 2003.[^n2][^o17]

The kissing number for D+ is 2<sup>3</sup> = 8, (2<sup>*n* − 1</sup> for *n* \< 8, 240 for *n* = 8, and 2*n*(*n* − 1) for *n* > 8).[^n3]

The related D\* lattice (also called D4 and C2) can be constructed by the union of all four D<sub>4</sub> lattices, but it is identical to the *D<sub>4</sub> lattice*: It is also the 4-dimensional [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic), the union of two [4-cube honeycombs](wikipedia://en/9400139#Sphere_packing) in dual positions.[^n4]

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the regular hyperbolic 5-space [5-orthoplex honeycomb](wikipedia://en/45690347), {3,3,3,4,3}, with [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615) facets, the regular 4-polytope [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), {3,4,3} with octahedral (3-orthoplex) cell, and cube {4,3}, with (2-orthoplex) square faces.

It has a 2-dimensional analogue, [{3,6}](wikipedia://en/2863858), and as an [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) form (the **demitesseractic honeycomb**, h{4,3,3,4}) it is related to the [alternated cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternated_cubic_honeycomb).
