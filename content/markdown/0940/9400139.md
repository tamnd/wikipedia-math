::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Tesseractic honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **tesseractic honeycomb** is one of the three [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_Euclidean_4-space) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)), represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,3,4}, and consisting of a packing of [tesseracts](wikipedia://en/31112) (4-[hypercubes](wikipedia://en/39783)).

:::

Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422). Two tesseracts meet at each cubic [cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29), four meet at each [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) [face](wikipedia://en/149926), eight meet on each [edge](wikipedia://en/12158034), and sixteen meet at each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417).

It is an analog of the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809), {4,4}, of the plane and the [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318), {4,3,4}, of 3-space. These are all part of the [hypercubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12237982) family of tessellations of the form {4,3,...,3,4}. Tessellations in this family are [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual_polytope).

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

Vertices of this honeycomb can be positioned in 4-space in all integer coordinates (i,j,k,l).

## Sphere packing {#Sphere_packing}

Like all regular [hypercubic honeycombs](wikipedia://en/12237982), the tesseractic honeycomb corresponds to a [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621) of edge-length-diameter spheres centered on each vertex, or (dually) inscribed in each cell instead. In the hypercubic honeycomb of 4 dimensions, vertex-centered 3-spheres and cell-inscribed 3-spheres will both fit at once, forming the unique regular [body-centered cubic](wikipedia://en/666697) lattice of equal-sized spheres (in any number of dimensions). Since the tesseract is [radially equilateral](wikipedia://en/31112#Radial_equilateral_symmetry), there is exactly enough space in the hole between the 16 vertex-centered 3-spheres for another edge-length-diameter 3-sphere. (This [4-dimensional body centered cubic lattice](wikipedia://en/9409435#D4_lattice) is actually the union of two tesseractic honeycombs, in dual positions.)

This is the same densest known regular 3-sphere packing, with [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 24, that is also seen in the other two regular tessellations of 4-space, the [16-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9409435#D4_lattice) and the [24-cell-honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422#Kissing_number). Each tesseract-inscribed 3-sphere kisses a surrounding shell of 24 3-spheres, 16 at the vertices of the tesseract and 8 inscribed in the adjacent tesseracts. These 24 kissing points are [the vertices of a 24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485#Radially_equilateral_honeycomb) of radius (and edge length) 1/2.

## Constructions {#Constructions}

There are many different [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) of this honeycomb. The most symmetric form is [regular](wikipedia://en/518693), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,3,4}. Another form has two alternating [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) facets (like a checkerboard) with Schläfli symbol {4,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>}. The lowest symmetry Wythoff construction has 16 types of facets around each vertex and a prismatic product Schläfli symbol {∞}<sup>4</sup>. One can be made by [stericating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication) another.

:::

## Related polytopes and tessellations {#Related_polytopes_and_tessellations}

The [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422) is similar, but in addition to the vertices at integers (i,j,k,l), it has vertices at half integers (i+1/2,j+1/2,k+1/2,l+1/2) of odd integers only. It is a half-filled [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic) (a checkerboard in which the red 4-cubes have a central vertex but the black 4-cubes do not).

The [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) can make a regular tessellation of the [4-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782), with three tesseracts per face, with Schläfli symbol {4,3,3,3}, called an *order-3 tesseractic honeycomb*. It is topologically equivalent to the regular polytope [penteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penteract) in 5-space.

The tesseract can make a regular tessellation of 4-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742), with 5 tesseracts around each face, with Schläfli symbol {4,3,3,5}, called an [order-5 tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926110).

The [Ammann–Beenker tiling](wikipedia://en/19292825) is an [aperiodic tiling](wikipedia://en/868145) in 2 dimensions obtained by [cut-and-project](wikipedia://en/868145#Cut-and-project_method) on the tesseractic honeycomb along an eightfold rotational axis of symmetry.[^o12][^o13]

### Birectified tesseractic honeycomb {#Birectified_tesseractic_honeycomb}

A [birectified tesseractic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_tesseractic_honeycomb), , contains all rectified 16-cell ([24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485)) facets and is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [D<sub>4</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice](wikipedia://en/9409435#D4_lattice). Facets can be identically colored from a doubled ${\tilde{C}}_4$×2, \[\[4,3,3,4\]\] symmetry, alternately colored from ${\tilde{C}}_4$, \[4,3,3,4\] symmetry, three colors from ${\tilde{B}}_4$, \[4,3,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry, and 4 colors from ${\tilde{D}}_4$, \[3<sup>1,1,1,1</sup>\] symmetry.
