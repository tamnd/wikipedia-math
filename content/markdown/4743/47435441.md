# John C. Oxtoby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John C. Oxtoby"}

**John C. Oxtoby** (1910–1991) was an American mathematician. In 1936, he graduated with a Master of Science in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o2] He was a professor of mathematics at [Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College) in [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania) from 1939 until his retirement in 1979.

:::
