# Martin Bland

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Bland"}

**John Martin Bland** (born 6 March 1947), known as **Martin Bland**, is a British [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He has been professor of [health statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_statistics) at the [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York) since 2003.[^o3] Bland is known for his work on medical measurement, particularly methodology for method comparison studies such as the [Bland–Altman plot](wikipedia://en/3146632).

:::

Bland was born in [Stockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport) and obtained a BSc, MSc and diploma from [Imperial College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College,_London), followed by a PhD in epidemiology from the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London). Between 1976 and 2003 he worked at [St. George's Hospital Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George's_Hospital_Medical_School), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London).[^o3]
