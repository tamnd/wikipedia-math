# Alfred H. Clifford

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred H. Clifford"}

**Alfred Hoblitzelle Clifford** (July 11, 1908 – December 27, 1992) was an American mathematician born in St. Louis, Missouri who is known for [Clifford theory](wikipedia://en/4777875) and for his work on [semigroups](wikipedia://en/27799). He did his undergraduate studies at [Yale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and his PhD at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), and worked at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [Johns Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) (as Associate Professor),[^o3] and later, in 1955, [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) (as Professor of Mathematics and Head of Sophie Newcomb College).[^o4]

:::

The Alfred H. Clifford Mathematics Research Library at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) is named after him.[^o5]
