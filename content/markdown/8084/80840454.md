# Leonardo polyhedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonardo polyhedron"}

*Image: De divina proportione - Vigintisex Basium Planum Vacuum.jpg.* [Rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213), one of the [Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci)'s geometrical shapes illustration in 1509 *[Divina proportione](wikipedia://en/16175342)*

A **Leonardo polyhedron** is a polyhedron with a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905)'s rotational symmetry and [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) $g \ge 2$. Here, a polyhedron is the unbounded [2-manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-manifold) embedded in [three-dimensional Euclidean space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-dimensional_Euclidean_space). The polyhedron is named after [Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci), who illustrated geometrical shapes in [Luca Pacioli](wikipedia://en/18473)'s *[De divina proportione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_divina_proportione)* in three phases: drawing Platonic solids and [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847); replacing the edges of those solids by struts, forming a [convex polygon](wikipedia://en/660019), and this results in the first polyhedron with many genera; and placing each hole with the [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_%28topology%29) of a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045).[^n1]

:::

[Alicia Boole Stott](wikipedia://en/3325459) discovered the first [regular](wikipedia://en/333299) Leonardo polyhedron (its property has transitivity by [the set consisting of vertex, edge, and face of a polyhedron](wikipedia://en/9352497)). Similar to Leonardo's work, she began the construction with a [four-dimensional polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-dimensional_polytope), projecting to a [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595), and replacing its edges with quadrilateral-shaped struts. [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Scott_MacDonald_Coxeter) later discovered the [regular skew polyhedron](wikipedia://en/19193037).[^n2] [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) discovered the three genera. Together with [Robert Fricke](wikipedia://en/10291191), they found the five genera of Leonardo polyhedra.[^n3] Some colleagues further discovered the locally regular and the genus up to 14.
