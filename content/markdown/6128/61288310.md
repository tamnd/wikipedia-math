# Fatma Moalla

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fatma Moalla"}

**Fatma Moalla** (born January 14, 1939) is a Tunisian mathematician who has published research on [Finsler spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_space) and geometry and worked as an assistant at [Faculté des Sciences Mathématique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculté_des_Sciences_Mathématique), Physiques et Naturelles. The International Fatma Moalla Award for the Popularization of Mathematics is now given in her honor.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fatma Moalla was born in [Tunis, Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunis) on January 14, 1939. Her father's name was Mohamed Moalla and he worked selling books. She attended secondary school at [Lycée de la Rue du Pacha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_de_la_Rue_du_Pacha). In 1956, Moalla switched schools and began attending Lycée Carnot of Tunis where she chose to specialize in mathematics. Moalla then attended university at "Institut des Hautes Études de Tunis...\[and\] She graduated with her mathematics degree in June 1960."[^o3] Moalla is the first Tunisian to have been awarded the [Agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in Mathematics in France in 1961 and the first Tunisian woman to be awarded a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in Mathematics in France in 1965.[^o5] Later she was placed into the National Union of Tunisian Women.

## Awards and achievements {#Awards_and_achievements}

The International Fatma Moalla Award for the Popularization of Mathematics is now given each year in honor of Fatma Moalla.[^o7]
