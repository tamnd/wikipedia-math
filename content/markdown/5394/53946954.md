# Mario Pulvirenti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mario Pulvirenti"}

**Mario Pulvirenti** is an Italian mathematician, Professor emeritus of [Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Physics) at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Mario Pulvirenti received a master's degree in physics from the [Sapienza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University) in 1970, where he is Professor emeritus of [Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Physics).[^o5] He also worked at [University of L'Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_L'Aquila) and [University of Camerino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Camerino). He spent research periods at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). He is currently member of [Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica](wikipedia://en/34249626) and of [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei), one of the highest Italian academic institutions.[^o6]

In 2006 has been invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid). In the same year he won the Tartufari prize form [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).

He is one of the major experts in mathematical aspects of [kinetic theory](wikipedia://en/64204),[^o7] and among his research topics are also [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481). In particular, he obtained (together with Reinhard Illner) the only rigorous global derivation in time of the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522) from particle dynamics known up to now.[^o7] He is also interested in clarifying some particular aspects of [history of mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_mechanics).
