# Shiba Inu (cryptocurrency)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Shiba Inu"}

**Shiba Inu** token (ticker: **SHIB**) is a [decentralized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_exchange) [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188) created in August 2020 by an anonymous person or group using the pseudonym "Ryoshi".[^o2] It is inspired by the [Shiba Inu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiba_Inu) (柴犬), a Japanese dog breed, which also serves as the mascot for [Dogecoin](wikipedia://en/41360413), another cryptocurrency with [meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme) origins. Some categorize Shiba Inu as a "[meme coin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme_coin)."[^o4][^o5] Concerns have been expressed about the concentration of the coin with a single "whale" wallet controlling billions of dollars' worth of the token, as frenzied buying by retail investors took place around November 2021, motivated by [fear of missing out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fear_of_missing_out) (FOMO).[^o6] Shiba Inu was introduced with a branding that positioned it as a potential 'Dogecoin competitor'.[^o2]

:::

On 13 May 2021, [Vitalik Buterin](wikipedia://en/41729424) donated more than 50 trillion SHIB (worth over \$1 billion at the time) to the India [COVID-Crypto Relief Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-Crypto_Relief_Fund).[^o7] Also in May 2021, Buterin donated SHIB worth \$665 million to the [Future of Life Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_of_Life_Institute), which focuses on regulating [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) to protect from [existential risk from advanced artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existential_risk_from_artificial_general_intelligence).[^o8]

In October 2021, the market price of the cryptocurrency experienced a significant rise, reportedly increasing by 240% within a week.[^o9] However, at the beginning of November, it faced a decline, reportedly losing approximately 55% of its value by the end of the month.[^o10][^o11][^o12]
