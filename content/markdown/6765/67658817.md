# Susan Schechter Bortner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Schechter Bortner"}

**Susan Schechter Bortner** (published as **Susan Schechter**) is an American [survey statistician](wikipedia://en/272134), formerly in US Government service and now a researcher at [NORC at the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORC_at_the_University_of_Chicago), a private nonprofit social research organization.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Schechter graduated from [Montgomery Blair High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery_Blair_High_School) in [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland) in 1972. She earned a bachelor's degree in [criminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminology) from the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), and a master's degree in human development from [Antioch University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioch_University).

Her positions in government service included working as a survey researcher in the [National Center for Health Statistics](wikipedia://en/1042734), as a senior statistician in the [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget), focusing on the classification of race and ethnicity, and in the [United States Census Bureau](wikipedia://en/57070), where she headed the [American Community Survey](wikipedia://en/592043) from 2006 to 2010. After more than 25 years of government work, she moved to [NORC at the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORC_at_the_University_of_Chicago) in 2010, where she became Project Director for the Medicare Current Beneficiary Survey.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Schechter was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2005. She chaired the Social Statistics Section of the American Statistical Association in 2006. In 2017, NORC gave her their Norman Bradburn Career Achievement Award.
