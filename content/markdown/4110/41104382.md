# Frobenius category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frobenius category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Frobenius category** is an [exact category](wikipedia://en/10987985) with enough projectives and enough injectives, where the classes of projectives and injectives coincide. It is an analog of a [Frobenius algebra](wikipedia://en/1814652).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The [stable category](wikipedia://en/21102203) of a Frobenius category is canonically a [triangulated category](wikipedia://en/1679700).[^o4]
