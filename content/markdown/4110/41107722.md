# Royal Concierge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Royal Concierge"}

**Royal Concierge** is the codename of a secret [GCHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters) programme that watches the bookings made at select hotels worldwide.[^o2] The programme was introduced in 2010 and monitored 350 hotels around the world. The system alerted GCHQ to a diplomat's travel arrangements and allowed GCHQ to bug rooms in preparation for the diplomat's arrival.[^o3]

:::
