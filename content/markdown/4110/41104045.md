# Urs Schreiber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Urs Schreiber"}

**Urs Schreiber** (born 1974) is a mathematician specializing in the connection between [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) (especially [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305)) and currently working as a researcher at [New York University Abu Dhabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Abu_Dhabi).[^o2] He was previously a researcher at the [Czech Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Academy_of_Sciences), Institute of Mathematics, Department for Algebra, Geometry and Mathematical Physics.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Schreiber obtained his doctorate from the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen) in 2005 with a thesis supervised by [Robert Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Graham_%28physicist%29) and titled *From Loop Space Mechanics to Nonabelian Strings*.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Schreiber's research fields include the [mathematical foundation of quantum field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_foundation_of_quantum_field_theory).

Schreiber is a co-creator of the [*n*Lab](wikipedia://en/27226892), a [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki) for research mathematicians and physicists working in [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013).
