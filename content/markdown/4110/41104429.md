# Kenneth Brown (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth Brown"}

**Kenneth Stephen Brown** (born 1945) is a professor of mathematics emeritus who spent his career at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), working in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_theory) as well as in [buildings](wikipedia://en/275015). Among other things, he is known for [Ken Brown's lemma](wikipedia://en/79382796) in the theory of [model categories](wikipedia://en/2895304).[^o3] He is also the author of the book *Cohomology of Groups* ([Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980) 87, Springer, 1982).[^o4][^o5]

:::

Brown grew up in [University City, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_City,_Missouri), outside [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis,_Missouri), and graduated from [University City High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_City_High_School_%28Missouri%29) in 1963.[^o6] Using a [National Merit Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Merit_Scholarship), he attended [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University),[^o6] graduating from there in 1967 with an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.B.) degree.[^o9] Brown earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1971 from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), under the supervision of [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519), with thesis *Abstract Homotopy Theory and Generalized Sheaf Cohomology*.

Hired by [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1971, Brown started as an assistant professor.[^o9] Courses that Brown taught in his early years there included "Calculus \[IV\]", "Linear Algebra", and "Algebra and Number Theory".[^o8] Brown became an associate professor in 1976 and a full professor in 1981.[^o9] He served as chair of the mathematics department at Cornell beginning in 2002,[^o10] and concluding in 2006.[^o11] He also directed the Summer Math Institute in 2009.[^o14]

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1978 in Helsinki.[^o12] In 2012 he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]

Known for his teaching, at Cornell Brown received the college-wide Clark Teaching Award and also the Mathematics Department Senior Faculty Award.[^o14] An October 2010 conference entitled "Approaches to Group Theory" was held in his honor at Cornell.[^o15] It was organized by his colleagues and former students, and led by the mathematicians [Susan Hermiller](wikipedia://en/59003656), John Meier, [Karen Vogtmann](wikipedia://en/20455422), and [David Webb](wikipedia://en/41665960).[^o15] Brown became a professor emeritus at Cornell in 2014.[^o9]

- *Buildings*, Springer-Verlag, New York, 1989 \[reprinted 1998\]
