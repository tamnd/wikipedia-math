# Ron Donagi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ron Donagi"}

*Image: Donagi ron 1990.jpg.* Ron Donagi, Berkeley 1990

**Ron Yehuda Donagi** (born March 9, 1956) is an American mathematician, working in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Donagi received a Ph.D. in 1977 under the supervision of [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (*On the geometry of Grassmannians*). Currently, he is a professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

From 1981 to 1982, from 1996 to 1997 and in 2013 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), where he worked with [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894).[^o4] In the 1980s Donagi applied algebraic geometry to string theory and related theories such as supersymmetric Yang-Mills theories in order to develop models for [heterotic string theory](wikipedia://en/644870) from suitable compactifications. Among his achievements in classical algebraic geometry are his work on the [Schottky problem](wikipedia://en/4125909) and generalizing the [Torelli theorem](wikipedia://en/3002552).

He is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Writings {#Writings}

- With Tony Pantev: *Torus fibrations, Gerbes and duality,* Memoirs AMS 2008
- Editor with Mauro Francaviglia: *Integrable systems and quantum groups* (CIME Lectures, Montcatini Terme, June 1993), Springer Verlag 1996
- Publisher: *Curves, Jacobians, and Abelian varieties,* Proceedings of an AMS-IMS-SIAM Joint Summer Research Conference on the Schottky problem, AMS 1992
- With [Katrin Wendland](wikipedia://en/44832360) (Eds.): *From Hodge theory to integrability and TQFT: tt \*-geometry* (Workshop University of Augsburg 2007), AMS 2008
- With Witten: *Supersymmetric Yang-Mills Systems and Integrable Systems,* Nucl. Phys. B, 460, 1996, 299-344, Arxiv
- With Josh Guffin, [Sheldon Katz](wikipedia://en/48435071), Eric Sharpe, *A mathematical theory of quantum sheaf cohomology,* Preprint 2011
- With Guffin, Katz, Sharpe: *Physical aspects of quantum sheaf cohomology for deformation of tangent bundles of toric varieties,* Preprint 2011
- With Vincent Bouchard, *on SU (5) Heterotic standard model,* Phys. Lett. B, 633, 2006, 783-791
