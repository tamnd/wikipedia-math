# Vladimir Popov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Popov"}

**Vladimir Leonidovich Popov** (Влади́мир Леони́дович Попо́в; born 3 September 1946) is a Russian mathematician working in the [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762) and the theory of [transformation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_group).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

In 1969 he graduated from the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). In 1972 he received his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree (PhD) with thesis Стабильность действия алгебраических групп и арифметика квазиоднородных многообразий (Stability of the action of algebraic groups and the arithmetic of quasi-homogeneous varieties). In 1984 he received his Russian [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) degree (habilitation) with thesis Группы, образующие, сизигии и орбиты в теории инвариантов (Groups, generators, syzygies and orbits in the theory of invariants).[^o6]

He is a member of the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) and a professor of the [National Research University – Higher School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_University_–_Higher_School_of_Economics).[^o3] In 1986, he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (Berkeley, USA),[^o7] and in 2008–2010 he was a core member of the panel for Section 2, "Algebra" of the Program Committee for the 2010 International Congress of Mathematicians ([Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), India).[^o8]

In 1987 he published a proof of a conjecture of [Claudio Procesi](wikipedia://en/35219117) and Hanspeter Kraft.[^o9] In 2006, with Nicole Lemire and [Zinovy Reichstein](wikipedia://en/31869776), Popov published a solution to a problem posed by Domingo Luna in 1973.[^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012, he was elected a member of the inaugural class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o12] which recognizes mathematicians who have made significant contributions to the field.

In 2016, he was elected a corresponding member of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
