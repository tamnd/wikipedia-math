# Jonathan Rosenberg (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Rosenberg"}

*Image: Rosenberg jonathan.jpg.* Jonathan Rosenberg, [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach) 2005

**Jonathan Micah Rosenberg** (born December 30, 1951, in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois))[^o2] is an American mathematician, working in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987), [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), with applications to [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) (especially dualities) in physics.

:::

Rosenberg received his Ph.D. in 1976, under the supervision of [Marc Rieffel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Rieffel), from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (*Group C\*-algebras and square integrable representations*).[^o3] From 1977 to 1981 he was an assistant professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). Since 1981, he has been at the [University of Maryland at College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland_at_College_Park) where he is the [Ruth M. Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_M._Davis) Professor of Mathematics. He is also a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS).[^o4]

He studies operator algebras and their relations with topology, geometry, with the unitary representation theory of Lie groups, K-theory and index theory. Along with [H. Blaine Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Blaine_Lawson) and [Mikhail Leonidovich Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov), he is known for the [Gromov–Lawson–Rosenberg conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov–Lawson–Rosenberg_conjecture).

Since 2015 he has been a managing editor of the *Annals of K-Theory*. During 2007–2015 he was an editor of the *Journal of K-Theory*. Before that, he was an associate editor of the *Journal of the AMS* (2000–2003), and of the *Proceedings of the AMS* (1988–1992). He was a Sloan Fellow from 1981 to 1984.
