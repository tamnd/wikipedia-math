::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Cubical bipyramid"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **cubical bipyramid** is the [direct sum](wikipedia://en/2084687) of a cube and a segment, {4,3} + { }. Each face of a central [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) is attached with two [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455), creating 12 square pyramidal cells, 30 triangular faces, 28 edges, and 10 vertices. A cubical bipyramid can be seen as two [cubic pyramids](wikipedia://en/36537625) augmented together at their base.[^o6]

:::

It is the dual of a [octahedral prism](wikipedia://en/8813885).

Being convex and regular-faced, it is a [CRF polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRF_polytope).

## Coordinates {#Coordinates}

It is a [Hanner polytope](wikipedia://en/39408400) with coordinates:[^o8]

- \[2\] (0, 0, 0; ±1)
- \[8\] (±1, ±1, ±1; 0)
