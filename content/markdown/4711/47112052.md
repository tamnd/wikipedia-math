# Max Simon (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Simon"}

*Image: Dr. Professor.jpg.* Gravestone of Max Simon

**Maximilian Simon** (born 8 June 1844 in [Kołobrzeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kołobrzeg); died 15 January 1918 in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg)) was a German historian of mathematics and mathematics teacher. He was concerned mostly with mathematics in antiquity.

:::

Born into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) family,[^o3] he studied from 1862 to 1866 at the [Friedrich Wilhelm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University) of [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), obtaining his Ph.D. from [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) und [Ernst Eduard Kummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Eduard_Kummer)[^o4] He was a mathematics teacher in Berlin from 1868 to 1871, and in Strasbourg from 1871 to 1912, where he became an honorary professor of the [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg).

## Works {#Works}

- *Euclid und die sechs planimetrischen Bücher*, Teubner 1901
- [*Über die Entwicklung der Elementargeometrie im 19 Jahrhundert*](https://archive.org/details/berdieentwickl00simouoft), Bericht der [Deutschen Mathematikervereinigung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Mathematiker-Vereinigung), Teubner 1906
- *Geschichte der Mathematik im Altertum in Verbindung mit antiker Kulturgeschichte*, Berlin: B. Cassirer 1909
- *Nichteuklidische Geometrie in elementarer Behandlung* (Kuno Fladt ed.), Teubner 1925
- *Analytische Geometrie der Ebene*, 3rd edition, Sammlung Göschen 1900
- *Analytische Geometrie des Raumes*, 2 volumes, Sammlung Göschen 1900, 1901
