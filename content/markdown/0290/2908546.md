# Serre's modularity conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Serre's modularity conjecture"}

**Serre's modularity conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Serre's modularity conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Serre's modularity conjecture**, introduced by (txt), states that an odd, [irreducible](wikipedia://en/385982), two-dimensional [Galois representation](wikipedia://en/486929) over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) arises from a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000). A stronger version of this conjecture specifies the weight and level of the modular form. The conjecture in the level 1 case was proved by [Chandrashekhar Khare](wikipedia://en/11350853) in 2005,[^o4] and a proof of the full conjecture was completed jointly by Khare and [Jean-Pierre Wintenberger](wikipedia://en/30759845) in 2008.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .definition cue=heading name="Formulation"}

## Formulation {#Formulation}

The conjecture concerns the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) $G_\mathbb{Q}$ of the [rational number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_number_field) $\mathbb{Q}$.

Let $\rho$ be an [absolutely irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolutely_irreducible), [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122), two-dimensional [representation](wikipedia://en/874400) of $G_\mathbb{Q}$ over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) $F = \mathbb{F}_{\ell^r}$.

$$\rho \colon G_\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_2(F).$$

Additionally, assume $\rho$ is odd, meaning the [image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_%28matrix%29) of [complex conjugation](wikipedia://en/173918) has determinant -1.

To any normalized [modular eigenform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_eigenform)

$$f = q+a_2q^2+a_3q^3+\cdots$$

of [level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_of_a_modular_form) $N=N(\rho)$, [weight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_of_a_modular_form) $k=k(\rho)$, and some [Nebentype character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebentype_character)

$$\chi \colon \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow F^*,$$

a theorem due to [Shimura](wikipedia://en/174724), [Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544), and Serre-Deligne attaches to $f$ a representation

$$\rho_f\colon G_\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathcal{O}),$$

where $\mathcal{O}$ is the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) of integers in a [finite extension](wikipedia://en/4382697) of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. This representation is characterized by the condition that for all prime numbers $p$, [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to $N\ell$ we have

$$\operatorname{Trace}(\rho_f(\operatorname{Frob}_p))=a_p$$

and

$$\det(\rho_f(\operatorname{Frob}_p))=p^{k-1} \chi(p).$$

Reducing this representation [modulo](wikipedia://en/1352428), the [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164) of $\mathcal{O}$ gives a mod $\ell$ representation $\overline{\rho_f}$ of $G_\mathbb{Q}$.

Serre's conjecture asserts that for any representation $\rho$ as above, there is a modular eigenform $f$ such that

$$\overline{\rho_f} \cong \rho.$$

The level and weight of the conjectural form $f$ are explicitly conjectured in Serre's article. In addition, he derives a number of results from this conjecture, among them [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953) and the now-proven Taniyama–Weil (or Taniyama–Shimura) conjecture, now known as the [modularity theorem](wikipedia://en/174475) (although this implies Fermat's Last Theorem, Serre proves it directly from his conjecture).

:::

## Optimal level and weight {#Optimal_level_and_weight}

The strong form of Serre's conjecture describes the level and weight of the modular form.

The optimal level is the [Artin conductor](wikipedia://en/34170016) of the representation, with the power of $l$ removed.

::: {#o10 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

A proof of the level 1 and small weight cases of the conjecture was obtained in 2004 by [Chandrashekhar Khare](wikipedia://en/11350853) and [Jean-Pierre Wintenberger](wikipedia://en/30759845), using [P-adic L-functions](wikipedia://en/22059815), [supersingular elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/3028216), and a theorem from [Mark Kisin](wikipedia://en/30016901).[^o11] Another proof was independently published by [Luis Dieulefait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Dieulefait) in 2007.[^o12]

In 2005, Chandrashekhar Khare obtained a proof of the level 1 case of Serre conjecture,[^o13] and in 2008 a proof of the full conjecture in collaboration with Jean-Pierre Wintenberger.[^o5]

:::
