# Yasuo Akizuki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yasuo Akizuki"}

Biographies

**Yasuo Akizuki** (23 August 1902 – 11 July 1984) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He was a professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).[^o4] Alongside [Wolfgang Krull](wikipedia://en/964328), [Oscar Zariski](wikipedia://en/359352), and [Masayoshi Nagata](wikipedia://en/3086117), he is famous for his early work in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). In particular, he is most well known in helping to demonstrate [Akizuki–Hopkins–Levitzki theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akizuki–Hopkins–Levitzki_theorem).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Yasuo Akizuki was born on 23 August 1902 in [Wakayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakayama_Prefecture). In 1926, Akizuki graduated Faculty of Mathematics, Department of Science, [Kyoto Imperial University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_Imperial_University).

He was inaugurated as a professor of [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1948.
