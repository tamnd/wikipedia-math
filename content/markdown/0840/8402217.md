# Synge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Synge"}

**Synge** is an Irish surname. Notable people with the name include:

:::

- [Cathleen Synge Morawetz](wikipedia://en/1254108) (1923–2017), Canadian mathematician
- [Edward Synge (archbishop of Tuam)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Synge_%28archbishop_of_Tuam%29) (1659–1741), Anglican clergyman who served in the Church of Ireland
- [Edward Synge (bishop of Elphin)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Synge_%28bishop_of_Elphin%29) (1691–1762), Anglican bishop in the Church of Ireland
- [Edward Hutchinson Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Hutchinson_Synge) (1890–1957), inventor of the near-field optical microscope
- [John Lighton Synge](wikipedia://en/1252509) (1897–1995), Irish mathematician and physicist
- [John Millington Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Millington_Synge) (1871–1909), Irish playwright, poet, and prose writer
- [Mary Helena Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Helena_Synge) (1840-1917) Irish composer
- [Nicholas Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Synge) (died 1771), 18th-century Irish Anglican priest
- [Patrick Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Synge) (1910–1982), British botanist, writer and plant hunter
- [Richard Laurence Millington Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Laurence_Millington_Synge) (1914–1994), British biochemist
- [Violet Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violet_Synge) Girl Guide Chief Commissioner for England
- [William Synge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Synge) (1826-1891), British diplomat and writer
