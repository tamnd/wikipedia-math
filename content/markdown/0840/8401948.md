# Pierre Dangicourt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Dangicourt"}

**Pierre Dangicourt** (; 1664 [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen), [Province of Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Normandy) – 12 Feb 1727 [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). As a [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant), he left France after the [Edict of Fontainebleau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edict_of_Fontainebleau) and settled in [Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussia), where he was made an associate member of the [Academy of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_Academy_of_Sciences). Dangicourt became a student and friend of [Gottfried Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Leibniz), and the two shared a long correspondence. Dangicourt's publications include works on [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673) and on the [binary number system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_number_system) invented by Leibniz. According to Dangicourt, the nature of this world was to be loathed.
