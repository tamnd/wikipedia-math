# Morgan Crofton

*Image: Morgan Crofton.jpeg.* Morgan Crofton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morgan Crofton"}

**Morgan William Crofton** (1826, [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin), Ireland – 1915, [Brighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton), England) was an Irish mathematician who contributed to the field of [geometric probability theory](wikipedia://en/349336). He also worked with [James Joseph Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861) and contributed an article on probability to the 9th edition of the *[Encyclopædia Britannica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopædia_Britannica)*. [Crofton's formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crofton's_formula) is named in his honour.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Morgan Crofton was born into a wealthy [Anglo-Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Irish) family. His father, the [Reverend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverend) William Crofton, [Rector of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28ecclesiastical%29) [Skreen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skreen), [County Sligo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Sligo), was the younger brother of [Sir Malby Crofton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crofton_baronets), [2nd Baronet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baronet) of Longford House.[^o3] He was also the cousin of [Lord Edward Crofton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Crofton,_2nd_Baron_Crofton), [Baron Crofton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron_Crofton) of the Mote. Despite being born into an [aristocratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristocratic), [Anglican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican) family, Crofton joined to the [Roman Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church) in the 1850s in part due to an interest in [Cardinal John Henry Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_Newman). This led to his resignation at [Queen's College, Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Ireland,_Galway) and transference to various Catholic colleges.

He married twice: firstly on 31 August 1857 Julia Agnes Cecilia, daughter of J B Kernan (died 1902) and secondly Katherine, daughter of Holland Taylor of Manchester.

## Career {#Career}

He was Professor of Mathematics at the [Royal Military Academy, Woolwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Military_Academy,_Woolwich) and Professor of Natural Philosophy at [Queen's University of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_of_Ireland). He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in June, 1868.[^o5]
