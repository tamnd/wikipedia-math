# Leonid Berlyand

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leonid Berlyand"}

**Leonid Berlyand** is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and American mathematician, a professor of [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University). He is known for his works on [homogenization](wikipedia://en/23852047), [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286), mathematical modeling of [active matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_matter) and mathematical foundations of [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Leonid Berlyand was born in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv), Ukraine. His father, Viktor Berlyand, was a mechanical engineer, and his mother, Mayya Genkina, an electronics engineer. He graduated from the department of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) at the [V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VN_Karazin_Kharkiv_National_University). Then he obtained his Ph. D. degree from the same university. In his Ph. D. thesis, he applied the [homogenization theory](wikipedia://en/23852047) to study of elastic composite materials. After his defence, he worked at the [Semenov Institute of Chemical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semenov_Institute_of_Chemical_Physics) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Russia). In 1991 he moved to the United States and started working at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), where he has served as a full professor since 2003. He has held long-term visiting positions at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506), [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University), [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), [Argonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory) and [Los Alamos National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory). His research has drawn support from the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)(NSF),[^o4] [NIH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIH)/[NIGMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIGMS),[^o5] the [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) Program of the [DOE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Energy) Office of Sciences,[^o6] BSF (the Bi-National Science Foundation USA-Israel)[^o7] and the [NATO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO) Science for Peace and Security Section. Berlyand has authored over 100 works on [homogenization theory](wikipedia://en/23852047) and [PDE](wikipedia://en/52564)/[variational problems](wikipedia://en/171882) in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) and [material science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_science). He has organized a number of professional conferences and serves as one of two founding co-directors of the Center for Mathematics of Living and Mimetic Matter and the Center for Interdisciplinary Mathematics at [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University). He has supervised 16 Ph. D. students and 8 postdoctoral fellows.[^o8][^o9]

## Research {#Research}

Drawing upon fundamental works in classical [homogenization theory](wikipedia://en/23852047), Berlyand advanced the methods of homogenization in many versatile applications. He obtained mathematical results applicable to diverse scientific areas including biology, [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), [superconductivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconductivity), [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29), and [material science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_science). His [mathematical modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_modeling) explains striking experimental result in the collective swimming of [bacteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacteria).[^o11] His homogenization approach to multi-scale problems was transformed into a practical computational tool by introducing a concept of polyharmonic homogenization which led to a new type of multiscale [finite elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_elements).[^o12] Together with H. Owhadi, he introduced a "transfer-of-approximation" modeling concept, based on the similarity of the asymptotic behavior of the errors of [Galerkin solutions](wikipedia://en/2664839) for two [elliptic PDEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_PDE).[^o13][^o14] He also contributed to mathematical aspects of the [Ginzburg–Landau theory](wikipedia://en/396286) of [superconductivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconductivity)/[superfluidity](wikipedia://en/35607283) by introducing a new class of semi-stiff boundary problems.[^o15]

In 2020s Berlyand got interested in the rapidly developing area of [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning). His focus has been on mathematical foundations of deep learning, specifically he addressed the mathematical issues of convergence and stability of training algorithms for [deep neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning) (DNNs)[^o16] and formation of [fixed points](wikipedia://en/449738) of DNNs.[^o17] Subsequently he worked on application of the [Marchenko-Pastur distribution](wikipedia://en/25115911) of [Random Matrix Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_Matrix_Theory) to [pruning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pruning_%28artificial_neural_network%29) of DNNs that drastically improves training efficiency.[^o18] Berlyand also studied existence and stability of fixed points of [autoencoder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoencoder) neural networks. This work was done in collaboration with his Ukrainian colleagues.[^o17] In 2023 he published a textbook on introduction to mathematics of deep learning together with Pierre-Emmanuel Jabin.[^o19]
