# Mary Cleophas Garvin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Cleophas Garvin"}

Sister **Mary Cleophas**, born Linetta Anna Garvin, (11 December 1899 – 16 January 1990) was an American mathematician.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Linetta Garvin was born in [Vickery, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vickery,_Ohio), one of six children of Odelia Margaret and automobile and meat salesman Austin Edward Garwin.[^o3]

After graduating from the Notre Dame Academy in [Toledo, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio) in 1917, she joined the [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland,_Ohio) congregation of the [Sisters of Notre Dame de Namur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisters_of_Notre_Dame_de_Namur) in 1918, and took holy vows in 1920 and 1923, with the name Mary Cleophas recalling her brother Cleophus, who died in childhood.[^o3]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

With the Sisters of Notre Dame, Mary Cleophas became a high school teacher in Cleveland. During this time, she studied part-time at [Fordham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordham_University), earning a bachelor's degree there in 1927. She became a full-time student in 1929,[^o3] and earned her master’s in physics in 1931 and a Ph.D. in mathematics in 1934 from [St. Louis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis_University_%28United_States%29).[^o5]

On completing her doctorate, she became professor of mathematics and physics and head of the mathematics department at [Notre Dame College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre_Dame_College). She remained there for 50 years, as a professor until retiring in 1975 and as an archivist until 1984.[^o5]
