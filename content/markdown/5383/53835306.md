# Natalia Berloff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Natalia Berloff"}

**Natalia G. Berloff** is a Russian professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). She is interested in fluid dynamics, particularly [superfluidity](wikipedia://en/35607283) and the dynamics of quantum fluids. Her research includes the use of [polaritons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polariton) to simulate structures such as the [classical XY model](wikipedia://en/954006).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Berloff grew up in Russia.[^o9] She earned her undergraduate degrees of BS (1991) and MS (1992) from [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).[^o4][^o5][^o6] She earned her Ph.D. from [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) in 1996, under the supervision of [Louis Norberg Howard](wikipedia://en/48284809).

## Career {#Career}

Berloff was a UC President's Research Fellow in the Department of Mathematics of the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) from 1997 to 1999, and continued there as PIC Assistant Professor from 1999 to 2002, when she became a Fellow of [Jesus College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Cambridge), and a faculty member in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics at Cambridge, where she is now Professor in Applied Mathematics.[^o9] Berloff is the principal investigator of the Pi-computing team.[^o10] Berloff describes herself as a fluid dynamicist, and she is interested in describing the dynamics of physical systems, such as quantum fluids, [superfluidity](wikipedia://en/35607283), and [Bose-Einstein condensate](wikipedia://en/4474).[^o9] Her research includes the use of [polaritons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polariton) to simulate structures such as the [classical XY model](wikipedia://en/954006).[^o11]

From 2013 to 2016 Berloff took a leave of absence from Cambridge to serve as a Professor and Director of Photonics and Quantum Materials Program at the [Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolkovo_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) in Russia.[^o11]

In 2005 Berloff was awarded the Pilkington Prize by the Cambridge Centre of Teaching and Learning.[^o6]
