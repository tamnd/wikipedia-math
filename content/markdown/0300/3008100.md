# Stanley Osher

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stanley Osher"}

**Stanley Osher** (born April 24, 1942) is an American mathematician, known for his many contributions in [shock capturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_capturing), [level-set methods](wikipedia://en/1203063), and [PDE](wikipedia://en/52564)-based methods in [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision) and [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing). Osher is a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA), Director of Special Projects in the [Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/12023558) (IPAM) and member of the California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI) at UCLA.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Osher received a bachelor's degree from [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College) in 1962. He continued his studies at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), where he received a master's degree in 1964[^o4] and completed his Ph.D. in 1966.

After two years working at the [Brookhaven National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookhaven_National_Laboratories), he joined the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) as an assistant professor of mathematics in 1968. He moved to [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) as an associate professor in 1970, and there was promoted to full professor in 1975. He moved again to the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) Department of Mathematics in 1977.[^o4]

He co-founded a spinoff company, Cognitech, in 1988, and remained affiliated with Cognitech until 1995.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Osher was a [Fulbright Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) in 1971,[^o7] an [Alfred P. Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship), 1972–1974[^o8] an SERC Fellow in England in 1982, and a US-Israel BSF Fellow in 1986.[^o4]

He was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2005,[^o9] to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2009,[^o10] and to the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 2018.[^o11] He was named as a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2009,[^o12] and of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o13]

He is a 1992 recipient of the [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) Public Service Group Achievement Award,[^o4] the 2002 recipient of the Computational Mechanics Award of the Japan Society of Mechanical Engineering,[^o4] the 2003 recipient of the [ICIAM](wikipedia://en/13606431) Pioneer Prize,[^o21] the 2005 recipient of the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Kleinman Prize,[^o14] the 2007 recipient of the Computational and Applied Sciences Award of the United States Association for Computational Mechanics,[^o17] and the 2007 recipient of the International Cooperation Award, given at the [International Congress of Chinese Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/45275469).[^o15] He received the 2013 John von Neumann Lecture prize from [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376),[^o16] the 2014 [Carl Friedrich Gauss Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gauss_Prize),[^o17] and the 2016 William Benter Prize in Applied Mathematics.[^o18]

He has honorary doctorates from [ENS Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Cachan) in France in 2006[^o19] and from [Hong Kong Baptist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Baptist_University) in 2009.[^o20] He was a plenary speaker at the 2010 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o21]
