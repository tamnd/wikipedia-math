# Polish School of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polish School of Mathematics"}

*Image: Lwowscy\_matematycy1930.jpg.* Lviv mathematicians, 1930.

The **Polish School of Mathematics** was the [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) community that flourished in [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) in the 20th century, particularly during the [Interbellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interbellum) between [World Wars I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I) and [II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

The Polish School of Mathematics subsumed:

- the [Lwów School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1024328) - mostly focused on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180);
- the [Warsaw School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_School_of_Mathematics) - mostly focused on [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954); and
- the [Kraków School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1028214) - mostly focused on [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), [analytic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_functions), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

### Nomenclature {#Nomenclature}

Poland's mathematicians provided a name to [Polish notation](wikipedia://en/25056) and [Polish space](wikipedia://en/396622).

### Background {#Background}

It has been debated what stimulated the exceptional efflorescence of mathematics in Poland after [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I). Important preparatory work had been done by the [Polish "Positivists"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positivism_in_Poland) following the disastrous [January 1863 Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_Uprising). The Positivists extolled [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science) and [technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology), and popularized slogans of "organic work" and "building from the foundations." In the 20th century, mathematics was a field of endeavor that could be successfully pursued even with the limited resources that Poland commanded in the [interbellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interbellum) period.

### Historical Influences {#Historical_Influences}

Over the centuries, Polish mathematicians have influenced the course of history. [Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copernicus) used mathematics to buttress his revolutionary [heliocentric theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heliocentric_theory). Four hundred years later, [Marian Rejewski](wikipedia://en/4349420) — subsequently assisted by fellow [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902)-[cryptologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptologist) [Jerzy Różycki](wikipedia://en/197851) and [Henryk Zygalski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henryk_Zygalski) — in December 1932 first broke the German [Enigma machine](wikipedia://en/9256) [cipher](wikipedia://en/5244), thus laying the foundations for [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain) [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) reading of Enigma ciphers ("[Ultra](wikipedia://en/31748)"). After the war, [Stanisław Ulam](wikipedia://en/41531) showed [Edward Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Teller) how to construct a practicable [hydrogen bomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_bomb).
