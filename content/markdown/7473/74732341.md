# Arie Bialostocki

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Arie Bialostocki"}

**Arie Bialostocki** (אריה ביאלוסטוקי) is an Israeli American mathematician with expertise and contributions in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_groups).[^o7][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Arie received his BSc, MSc, and PhD (1984) from [Tel-Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel-Aviv_University) in Israel.[^o5] His dissertation was done under the supervision of Marcel Herzog.[^o6] After a year of postdoc at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary) in Canada, he took a faculty position at the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho), becoming a full professor in 1992.[^o5] Arie retired from his position at the University of Idaho in 2011.[^o7] He maintained strong international connections, corresponding with mathematicians from India, Kashmir, Iran, Jordan, and China.[^o7] His [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) is 1.[^o8] He has supervised seven PhD students and numerous undergraduate students who enjoyed his colorful anecdotes and advice.[^o7] He organized a summer [Research Experience for Undergraduates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Experience_for_Undergraduates) (REU) program at the University of Idaho from 1999 to 2003 and maintained contact with many of the students who participated, some of whom went on to pursue their own careers in research.[^o7]

## Mathematics research {#Mathematics_research}

Arie has published more than 50 publications.[^o10][^o11] Some of Bialostocki's contributions include:

- Bialostocki[^o12] redefined[^o13] a $B$-injector in a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) G to be any maximal nilpotent subgroup $B$ of $G$ satisfying $d_2(B)=d_2(G)$, where $d_2(X)$ is the largest cardinality of a subgroup of $G$ which is nilpotent of class at most $2$. Using his definition, it was proved by several authors[^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17] that in many non-solvable groups the nilpotent injectors form a unique conjugacy class.
- Bialostocki contributed to the generalization of the [Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv_theorem) (also known as the [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem)).[^o18][^o19] He conjectured: if $A=(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n)$ is a sequence of elements of ${\mathbb Z}_m$, then $A$ contains at least ${\lfloor{n/2}\rfloor \choose {m}}+{\lceil{n/2}\rceil \choose{m}}$ zero sums of length $m$. The [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem) is a special case where $n=2m-1$. The conjecture was partially confirmed by [Kisin](wikipedia://en/30016901),[^o20] [Füredi](wikipedia://en/22730000) and [Kleitman](wikipedia://en/5299894),[^o21] and Grynkiewicz.[^o22]
- Bialostocki introduced the EGZ polynomials and contributed to generalize the [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem) for higher degree polynomials.[^o23][^o24] The [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem) is associated with the first degree elementary polynomial.
- Bialostocki and Dierker[^o25][^o26] introduced[^o27] the relationship of [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem) to [Ramsey Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_Theory) on graphs.
- Bialostocki, [Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), and Lefmann[^o8] introduced[^o28] the relationship of [EGZ theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGZ_theorem) to [Ramsey Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_Theory) on the positive integers.
- In Jakobs and [Jungnickel](wikipedia://en/59538916)'s book "Einführung in die Kombinatorik",[^o29] Bialostocki and Dierker are attributed for introducing [Zero-sum Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/73857687). In Landman and [Robertson](wikipedia://en/69365391)'s book "Ramsey Theory on the Integers",[^o30] the number $b(m, k; r)$ is defined in honor of Bialostocki's contributions to the [Zero-sum Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/73857687).
- Bialostocki, Dierker, and Voxman[^o31] suggested[^o32] a conjecture offering a modular strengthening of the [Erdős–Szekeres theorem](wikipedia://en/3115543) proving that the number of points in the interior of the polygon is divisible by $k$, provided that total number of points $n\geqslant k+2$. Károlyi, [Pach](wikipedia://en/19900560) and [Tóth](wikipedia://en/2388442)[^o33] made further progress toward the proof of the conjecture.
- In [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446), Arie's paper[^o5] on application of [elementary group theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_group_theory) to [Peg Solitaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peg_Solitaire) is a suggested reading in [Joseph Gallian](wikipedia://en/18351855)'s book[^o34] on [Abstract Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_Algebra).
