# Carré du champ operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carré du champ operator"}

The **carré du champ operator** (French for *square of a field* operator) is a [bilinear](wikipedia://en/4365), [symmetric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_map) operator from [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). The carré du champ operator measures how far an [infinitesimal generator](wikipedia://en/1644938#Infinitesimal_generator) is from being a [derivation](wikipedia://en/897658).[^o2]

:::

The operator was introduced in 1969[^o3] by Hiroshi Kunita and independently discovered in 1976[^o4] by [Jean-Pierre Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Pierre_Roth_%28mathematician%29) in his doctoral thesis.

The name *"carré du champ"* comes from [electrostatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatics).

## Carré du champ operator for a Markov semigroup {#Carré_du_champ_operator_for_a_Markov_semigroup}

Let $(X,\mathcal{E},\mu)$ be a [σ-finite](wikipedia://en/4316174) [measure space](wikipedia://en/45270), $\{P_t\}_{t\geq 0}$ a [Markov semigroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_semigroup) of non-negative operators on $L^2(X,\mu)$, $A$ the [infinitesimal generator](wikipedia://en/74035291#Infinitesimal_generator_of_the_semigroup) of $\{P_t\}_{t\geq 0}$ and $\mathcal{A}$ the algebra of functions in $\mathcal{D}(A)$, i.e. a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) such that for all $f,g\in \mathcal{A}$ also $fg\in \mathcal{A}$.

### Carré du champ operator {#Carré_du_champ_operator}

The **carré du champ operator** of a Markovian semigroup $\{P_t\}_{t\geq 0}$ is the operator $\Gamma:\mathcal{A}\times \mathcal{A}\to\mathbb{R}$ defined (following [P. A. Meyer](wikipedia://en/3653083)) as

$$\Gamma(f,g)=\frac{1}{2}\left(A(fg)-fA(g)-gA(f)\right)$$

for all $f,g \in \mathcal{A}$.[^o7][^o8]

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

### Properties {#Properties}

From the definition, it follows that[^o2]

$$\Gamma(f,g)=\lim\limits_{t\to 0}\frac{1}{2t}\left(P_t(fg)-P_tfP_tg\right).$$

For $f\in\mathcal{A}$ we have $P_t(f^2)\geq (P_tf)^2$ and thus $A(f^2)\geq 2 fAf$ and

$$\Gamma(f):=\Gamma(f,f)\geq 0,\quad \forall f\in\mathcal{A}$$

therefore the carré du champ operator is positive.

The domain is

$$\mathcal{D}(A):=\left\{f \in L^2(X,\mu) ;\;\lim\limits_{t\downarrow 0}\frac{P_t f-f}{t}\text{ exists and is in } L^2(X,\mu)\right\}.$$

:::

### Remarks {#Remarks}

- The definition in Roth's thesis is slightly different.[^o4]
