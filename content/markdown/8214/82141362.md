# Dimitrie D. Stancu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dimitrie D. Stancu"}

**Dimitrie D. Stancu** (11 February 1927 – 17 April 2014) was a Romanian mathematician and professor. His work primarily focused on [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and approximation theories.[^o3] During his career, he served as the Member of the Scientific Council of the Institute from 1990 until 2014.[^o4] He then published his works at the [Babes-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University) in 1977.[^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Dimitrie D. Stancu was born on 11 February 1927 in [Călacea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orțișoara), Timiș County. His father had died prior, leaving his mother to take care of him. He began to work as a [shepherd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepherd). His brother, a painter who worked in [Arad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arad,_Romania), took him to an orphanage in Arad in 1937 when he was 10 years old. He was then brought up by a family some time later.[^o3][^o7]

Stancu moved to [Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj) where he studied at [Victor Babeș University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Babeș_University) in 1947 under his supervisor [Tiberiu Popoviciu](wikipedia://en/39854514), and graduated in 1951.[^o5] After his graduation, he was appointed to the Department of Mathematical Analysis of Babeș University. He obtained a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1956.[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Between 1961 and 1962, he was tasked by the [Romanian Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Ministry_of_Education) to undertake research in the Department of Numerical Analysis at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), conducted by [Preston C Hammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_C_Hammer). When he returned to Romania, he was named full professor of the Numerical and Statistical Calculus Chair from the Faculty of Mathematics at the Babeș-Bolyai University.[^o3] In 1968, he studied and introduced a new linear equation known as the "fundamental polynomials of Stancu”.[^o9]

During his career, he wrote over 120 papers of research dedicated to mathematics.[^o10] In 1999, he was elected an honorary member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy), with the [University of Sibiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sibiu) and the [North University of Baia Mare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_University_of_Baia_Mare) titling him as [Doctor Honoris Causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Honoris_Causa) alongside.[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Stancu married mathematician Felicia. They later had two daughters, Angela Stancu (born 1957) and Mirela (born 1958), both of whom became teachers. Stancu died on 17 April 2014 in [Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca). He was 87.[^o3]
