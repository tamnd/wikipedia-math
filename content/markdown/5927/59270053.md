# Susanne Ditlevsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susanne Ditlevsen"}

**Susanne Ditlevsen** (born 1965)[^o3] is a Danish mathematician and statistician, interested in [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology), [perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception), [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), and [statistical modeling of biological systems](wikipedia://en/3878).[^o4] She is a professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences of the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), where she heads the section of statistics and probability theory.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Ditlevsen was born in Denmark. She graduated from Det frie Gymnasium in 1983. She was an actress before she became a researcher.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Ditlevsen earned a master's degree in mathematics from the [National University of Distance Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Distance_Education) in Spain in 1999. The following year, she earned a master's degree in statistics from the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). In 2004, she received a Ph.D. from the University of Copenhagen with the dissertation *Modeling of physiological processes by stochastic differential equations*.[^o3]

After receiving her Ph.D., Ditlevsen became a professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Copenhagen in 2011.[^o3] In 2012, she became an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), and in 2016, she was elected to the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o7][^o8]

In 2023, she and her brother Peter, a climate scientist, published an article predicting that the [Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Meridional_Overturning_Circulation) has a chance of collapsing between 2025 and 2095 (95% confidence interval), with the statistical average of the predictions being 2057 (updated in August 2025 to between 2037 and 2109, with average of 2065).[^o9] When this tipping point is reached, it will have severe consequences to the world's climate, especially of northern Europe (see [Effects of AMOC slowdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Meridional_Overturning_Circulation#Effects_of_AMOC_slowdown)).[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Her niece is actress [Sara Hjort Ditlevsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Hjort_Ditlevsen).[^o11]
