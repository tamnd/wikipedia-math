# Bernd Wegner (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernd Wegner"}

**Bernd Wegner** (11 February 1942 – 18 January 2024) was a German mathematician. He was the editor-in-chief of [Zentralblatt für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_für_Mathematik) from 1974 until 2011.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wegner was born on 11 February 1942, in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o3] He attended high school at the Humboldt Gymnasium in Berlin.[^o3] He then attended the [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), receiving his diploma in 1966.[^o3] He stayed at Technische Universität Berlin for his PhD, which he completed in 1970 under the direction of Kurt Leichtweiß, with a dissertation titled *Beiträge zur Differentialgeometrie transnormaler Mannigfaltigeiten*. He spent the remainder of his career at Technische Universität Berlin, retiring in 2007.[^o3]

Wegner became editor-in-chief of [Zentralblatt für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_für_Mathematik) (later zbMATH Open) in 1974, taking over from [Ulrich Güntzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulrich_Güntzer).[^o5][^o6] His tenure as editor encompassed the separation of [East Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany) from zbMath, the reunification of East and West Germany, and the transition of zbMath from a paper resource to a digital resource.[^o5][^o6][^o7] While editor-in-chief, Wegner also helped to set up procedures for maintenance of the [Mathematics Subject Classification](wikipedia://en/3008091).[^o8] He stepped down in 2011, and was succeeded by [Gert-Martin Greuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gert-Martin_Greuel).[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Wegner died on 18 January 2024, following a fall.[^o3][^o5]
