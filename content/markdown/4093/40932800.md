# Helmut Röhrl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Helmut Röhrl"}

**Helmut Röhrl** or **Rohrl**[^o2] (born 22 March 1927 in [Straubing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straubing), died 30 January 2014) was a German mathematician.

:::

Besides [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) (including among other subjects the Riemann–Hilbert problem), he worked on algebra and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [totally convex spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_convex_space). In 1964, he edited the new edition of the classic textbook on complex analysis by [Adolf Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/771690) and [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238).

Röhrl received his doctorate in 1949 at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) (now LMU Munich) under [Robert König](wikipedia://en/37041244) (and [Oskar Perron](wikipedia://en/1485469)) with doctoral thesis *Über Differentialsysteme, welche aus multiplikativen Klassen mit exponentiellen Singularitäten entspringen*[^o4] and his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1953 with habilitation thesis *Abelsche Integrale auf Riemannschen Flächen endlichen Geschlechts*. He was a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) in mathematics from 1949 to 1951 at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), from 1951 to 1953 at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, from 1953 to 1955 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) under [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678) and from 1955 to 1958 again at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München. In the academic year from 1958 to 1959, he was at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), became in 1959 an associate professor and subsequently professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) and was from 1964 a professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

In the academic year from 1962 to 1963, Röhrl was a visiting professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), from 1967 to 1968 at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), from 1972 to 1973 at LMU Munich, and at the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg), the [FernUniversität Hagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FernUniversität_Hagen), the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich), and in 1976 at the [University of Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nagoya).

## Works {#Works}

- Das Riemann-Hilbert’sche Problem der linearen Differentialgleichungen, Mathematische Annalen, vol. 133, 1957, pp. 1–25.
- as annotator and editor: [Adolf Hurwitz](wikipedia://en/771690), [Richard Courant](wikipedia://en/715238) *Lehrbuch der Funktionentheorie*, Springer Verlag, 4th edn. 1964
  - annotations (pp. 551–696) forming two new chapters: *Weitere Abbildungstheoreme der Funktionentheorie* (including quasiconformal mappings) and *Holomorphe und meromorphe Funktionen auf Riemannschen Flächen* (including the Riemann–Roch theorem, Runge approximation theorem, topology and vector bundles on [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181), and automorphe functions)
- Röhrl, Helmut. (1962). "Holomorphic fiber bundles over Riemann surfaces". *Bulletin of the AMS*. 68 (3). p. 125–160. doi:10.1090/S0002-9904-1962-10715-0. mr:146854
- with Dieter Pumplün: *Banach spaces and totally convex spaces*, parts 1, 2, Comm. in Algebra, vol. 12, 1984, pp. 935–1019; vol. 13, 1985, pp. 1047–1113
