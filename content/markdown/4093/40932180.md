# Hee Oh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hee Oh"}

**Hee Oh** (오희, born 27 October 1969) is a Korean American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and the Abraham Robinson Professor of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o5] She has made contributions to [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), discrete subgroups of [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups), and their connections to [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Career {#Career}

She graduated with a bachelor's degree from [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) in 1992 and obtained her Ph.D. from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1997 under the guidance of [Gregory Margulis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Margulis).[^o7] She held several faculty positions, including ones at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), before joining the Department of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 2013 as the first female tenured professor in Mathematics there.[^o8] She served as Vice President of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), February 1, 2021 – January 31, 2024.[^o9]

## Work {#Work}

She has worked extensively on counting and [equidistribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equidistribution) for [Apollonian circle packings](wikipedia://en/1049256), [Sierpinski carpets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierpinski_carpet) and [Schottky dances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_dance).[^o11] Her recent work continues to be centered around the exploration and use of [lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). More specifically, working with [representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representations_of_Lie_groups) by using discrete sub groups, like [Anosov Groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anosov_Groups). Her research explores how these sub groups are useful in measuring characteristics of dynamical systems like maximal entropy.[^o12]

## Honours {#Honours}

Hee Oh was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad) in 2010, and gave a joint invited address at the 2012 AMS-MAA Joint Mathematics Meeting.[^o14] In 2012 she became an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15] She is the 2015 recipient of the [Ruth Lyttle Satter Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/37554140).[^o16] In 2017, she was named a [Guggenheim fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_fellow). In 2018, she received the [Ho-Am Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho-Am_Prize) for Science. In 2024, she was inducted into the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o17]

She served on the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) committee at the 2018 ICM in Rio. She is on the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) committee for 2024-2026.
