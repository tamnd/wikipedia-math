# Emily Stone (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emily Stone"}

**Emily Foster Stone** is an American mathematician whose research includes work in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montana), where she chairs the Department of Mathematical Sciences. She previously served as the chair of the Activity Group on Dynamical Systems of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Stone majored in physics at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz), graduating in 1984. She completed her Ph.D. in theoretical and applied mechanics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1989; her dissertation, *A Study of Low-Dimensional Models for the Wall Region of a Turbulent Boundary Layer*, was supervised by [Philip Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Holmes).

## Career {#Career}

Stone taught at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University) from 1992 to 1993, and at [Utah State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_University) from 1993 to 2004, before joining the University of Montana faculty in 2004.

## Service {#Service}

Stone was elected as chair of the Activity Group on Dynamical Systems (SIAG-DS) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) in 2020. She was elected Vice Chair of the same SIAG in 2022.[^o5]
