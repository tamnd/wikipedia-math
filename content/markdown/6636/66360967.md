# Fabio Toninelli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fabio Toninelli"}

**Fabio Toninelli** (born 1975) is an Italian mathematician who works in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) and probabilistic aspects of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

## Education {#Education}

He obtained his PhD in physics, in 2003, from [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa).

## Career {#Career}

Between 2004 and 2020 he was senior researcher at [Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (CNRS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) in Lyon. Since 2020 he has been Professor of Mathematics at [Technische Universität Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien).[^o5]

In 2021–2024 he was co-editor-in-chief (jointly with [Bálint Tóth](wikipedia://en/16296902)) of the journal *[Probability Theory and Related Fields](wikipedia://en/30852593)*.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Toninelli has contributed substantially to the mathematical theory of disordered [statistical mechanical systems](wikipedia://en/28481#:~:text=Statistical_mechanics_is_one_of,large_assemblies_of_microscopic_entities.), mixing of [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876), dimer models. His most significant contributions concern the theory of mean-field [spin glasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_glasses), of polymers in random environments and of stochastic interface dynamics.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Toninelli was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) ICM-2018 (Rio de Janeiro),[^o9] an invited plenary speaker of the 9th European Congress of Mathematics (Sevilla, 2024, https://www.ecm2024sevilla.com/), an invited plenary speaker of the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905) ICMP-2018 (Montreal),[^o10] and an invited plenary speaker of the Conference on Stochastic Processes and their Applications SPA-2014 (Buenos Aires).[^o11]
