# Fischer group Fi23

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fischer group Fi23"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Fischer group** *Fi<sub>23</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

4,089,470,473,293,004,800

\= 2<sup>18</sup>3<sup>13</sup>5<sup>2</sup>711131723

≈ 4.

## History {#History}

*Fi<sub>23</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and is one of the three [Fischer groups](wikipedia://en/327206) introduced by (txt) while investigating [3-transposition groups](wikipedia://en/29399459).

The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) are both [trivial](wikipedia://en/193059).

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

The Fischer group Fi<sub>23</sub> has a rank 3 action on a graph of 31671 vertices corresponding to 3-transpositions, with point stabilizer the double cover of the [Fischer group Fi22](wikipedia://en/36210170). It has a second rank-3 action on 137632 points

Fi<sub>23</sub> is the centralizer of a transposition in the [Fischer group Fi24](wikipedia://en/36207356). When realizing Fi<sub>24</sub> as a subgroup of the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422), the full centralizer of a transposition is the double cover of the [Baby monster group](wikipedia://en/324799). As a result, Fi<sub>23</sub> is a subgroup of the Baby monster, and is the normalizer of a certain S<sub>3</sub> group in the Monster.

The smallest faithful complex representation has dimension $782$. The group has an irreducible representation of dimension 253 over the field with 3 elements.

:::

## Generalized Monstrous Moonshine {#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) is not limited to the monster, but that similar phenomena may be found for other groups. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. For *Fi*<sub>23</sub>, the relevant McKay-Thompson series is $T_{3A}(\tau)$ where one can set the constant term a(0) = 42 (),

$$\begin{aligned}j_{3A}(\tau) &=T_{3A}(\tau)+42\\ &=\left(\left(\tfrac{\eta(\tau)}{\eta(3\tau)}\right)^{6}+3^3 \left(\tfrac{\eta(2\tau)}{\eta(\tau)}\right)^{6}\right)^2\\ &=\frac{1}{q} + 42 + 783q + 8672q^2 +65367q^3+371520q^4+\dots \end{aligned}$$

and *η*(*τ*) is the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020).
