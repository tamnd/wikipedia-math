# Fischer group Fi24

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fischer group Fi24"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Fischer group** *Fi<sub>24</sub>* or *F<sub>24</sub>* or *F<sub>3+</sub>* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

1,255,205,709,190,661,721,292,800

\= 2<sup>21</sup>3<sup>16</sup>5<sup>2</sup>7<sup>3</sup>1113172329

≈ 1.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*Fi<sub>24</sub>* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and is the largest of the three Fischer groups introduced by (txt) while investigating [3-transposition groups](wikipedia://en/29399459). It is the 3rd largest of the sporadic groups (after the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) and [Baby Monster group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_Monster_group)).

The [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2, and the [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 3. The automorphism group is a 3-transposition group Fi<sub>24</sub>, containing the simple group with index 2.

The centralizer of an element of order 3 in the monster group is a triple cover of the sporadic simple group *Fi<sub>24</sub>*, as a result of which the prime 3 plays a special role in its theory.

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

The centralizer of an element of order 3 in the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) is a triple cover of the Fischer group, as a result of which the prime 3 plays a special role in its theory. In particular it acts on a vertex operator algebra over the field with 3 elements.

The simple Fischer group has a rank 3 action on a graph of 306936 (=2<sup>3</sup>.3<sup>3</sup>.7<sup>2</sup>.29) vertices corresponding to the 3-transpositions of *Fi<sub>24</sub>*, with point stabilizer the [Fischer group *Fi<sub>23</sub>*](wikipedia://en/36209836).

The triple cover has a complex representation of dimension 783. When reduced modulo 3 this has 1-dimensional invariant subspaces and quotient spaces, giving an irreducible representation of dimension 781 over the field with 3 elements.

:::

## Generalized Monstrous Moonshine {#Generalized_Monstrous_Moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) is not limited to the monster, but that similar phenomena may be found for other groups. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. For *Fi*<sub>24</sub> (as well as *Fi*<sub>23</sub>), the relevant McKay-Thompson series is $T_{3A}(\tau)$ where one can set the constant term a(0) = 42 (),

$$\begin{aligned}j_{3A}(\tau) &=T_{3A}(\tau)+42\\ &=\left(\left(\tfrac{\eta(\tau)}{\eta(3\tau)}\right)^{6}+3^3 \left(\tfrac{\eta(2\tau)}{\eta(\tau)}\right)^{6}\right)^2\\ &=\frac{1}{q} + 42 + 783q + 8672q^2 +65367q^3+371520q^4+1741655q^5+\dots \end{aligned}$$
