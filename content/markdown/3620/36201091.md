# Ehrenpreis conjecture

*Image: Jeremy Kahn and Vladimir Markovic.jpg.* [Jeremy Kahn](wikipedia://en/48625747) and [Vladimir Markovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Markovic), who first proved the conjecture, at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) in 2012.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ehrenpreis conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Ehrenpreis conjecture** of [Leon Ehrenpreis](wikipedia://en/14162704) states that for any *K* greater than 1, any two closed [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) at least 2 have finite-degree [covers](wikipedia://en/250323) which are *K*-[quasiconformal](wikipedia://en/2749226): that is, the covers are arbitrarily close in the [Teichmüller metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_metric).

:::

A proof was announced by [Jeremy Kahn](wikipedia://en/48625747) and [Vladimir Markovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Markovic) in January 2011, using their proof of the [Surface subgroup conjecture](wikipedia://en/4565128) and a newly developed "good [pants](wikipedia://en/2812977) homology" theory. In June 2012, Kahn and Markovic were given the [Clay Research Awards](wikipedia://en/664167) for their work on these two problems by the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) at a ceremony at [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University).[^o2]
