::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated pentakis dodecahedron"}

The **truncated pentakis dodecahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) constructed as a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [pentakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/723196). It is [Goldberg polyhedron](wikipedia://en/39781140) G<sub>V</sub>(3,0), with pentagonal faces separated by an edge-direct distance of 3 steps.

:::
