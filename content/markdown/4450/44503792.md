# Siegel operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegel operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Siegel operator** is a [linear map](wikipedia://en/18102) from (level 1) [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927) of degree *d* to Siegel modular forms of degree *d* − 1, generalizing taking the constant term of a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000). The [kernel](wikipedia://en/1072915) is the space of [Siegel cusp forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_cusp_form) of degree *d*.

:::
