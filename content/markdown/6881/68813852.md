# Ulrich Görtz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrich Görtz"}

**Ulrich Görtz** (1973)[^o5] is a German mathematician specialising in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

From 1993 to 1997, Görtz studied mathematics at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).[^o5] He completed his PhD at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) in 2000;[^o5] his advisor was [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548). In 2006, Görtz [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o5] From 2008 to 2009 he was the recipient of a Heisenberg-Stipendium of the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) (DFG). He received the Von-Kaven-Ehrenpreis of the DFG.[^o4] Since 2009, Görtz has been a professor at the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen).[^o5]

## Books {#Books}

Together with Torsten Wedhorn, Görtz authored the textbook *Algebraic Geometry (Part I: Schemes)* in 2010.[^o7] Since August 2015,[^o8] Görtz has been a member of the editorial board of the journal *[Results in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43695804)*.[^o9]

- Görtz, Ulrich; Wedhorn, Torsten. (2020). "Algebraic geometry. I, Schemes : with examples and exercises". isbn:978-3-658-30733-2

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Görtz is an [Esperantist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperantist), and had been active in the youth organisation of the German Esperanto Association.[^o12]
