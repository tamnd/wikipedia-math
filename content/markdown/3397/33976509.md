In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 5-cell honeycomb** is one of five compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,3,5}, it has five [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) around each face. Acronym: pente[^n1]

:::

Its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) is the [120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976507), {5,3,3,3}.

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the [order-5 tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926110), {4,3,3,5}, and [order-5 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926439), {5,3,3,5}.

It is topologically similar to the finite [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615), {3,3,3,4}, and [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), {3,3,3,3}.

It is analogous to the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), {3,3,5}, and [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), {3,5}.
