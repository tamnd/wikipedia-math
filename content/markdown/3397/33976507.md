::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="120-cell honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 4-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **120-cell honeycomb** is one of five compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,3,3}, it has three [120-cells](wikipedia://en/717358) around each face. Acronym: hitte[^n1]

:::

Its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) is the [order-5 5-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/33976509), {3,3,3,5}.

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is related to the [order-4 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926407), {5,3,3,4}, and [order-5 120-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926439), {5,3,3,5}.

It is topologically similar to the finite [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215), {4,3,3,3}, and [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), {3,3,3,3}.

It is analogous to the [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358), {5,3,3}, and [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), {5,3}.
