# Israel Michael Sigal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Israel Michael Sigal"}

**Israel Michael Sigal** (born 31 August 1945 in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev), [Ukrainian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic)) is an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis), [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o3] He is a professor at the [University of Toronto Department of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics).

:::

He was an invited speaker at [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), Kyoto—1990[^o4] and in [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905), Lausanne—1979, W. Berlin—1981, Marseille—1986.

## Education {#Education}

Born in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev), [Ukrainian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic),[^o3] Sigal obtained his bachelor's degree at Gorky University and his Ph.D. at [Tel-Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel-Aviv_University)[^o6]

## Research interests {#Research_interests}

Partial differential equation of quantum physics, Quantum mechanics and [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) theory, Quantum field theory, Statistical mechanics, Non-linear equations, Mathematical biology, Pattern recognition[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

- The Jeffrey-Williams Lectureship, CMS Summer Meeting, 1992.[^o10]
- [John L. Synge Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_L._Synge_Award), 1993.
- [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada), 1993.
- [University Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Professor), 1997.
- Norman Stuart Robertson Chair in Applied Mathematics, 1998.
- [CRM-Fields-PIMS prize](wikipedia://en/19284033),[^o11] 2000.
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012.[^o12]
