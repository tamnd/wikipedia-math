# László Filep

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="László Filep"}

**László Filep** (6 December 1941 [Császló](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Császló) – 19 November 2004 [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) was a Hungarian mathematician who specialized in [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). His Ph.D. advisors at the [University of Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen) were [Barna Szénássy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barna_Szénássy) and [Lajos Tamássy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajos_Tamássy).

:::
