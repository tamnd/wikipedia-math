# David Kazhdan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Kazhdan"}

**David Kazhdan** (דוד קשדן), born **Dmitry Aleksandrovich Kazhdan** (Дмитрий Александрович Каждан; born 20 June 1946), is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for work in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). Kazhdan is a [1990 MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program). In 2012, he received the [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize) for Mathematics and Computer sciences.[^o8]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kazhdan was born on 20 June 1946 in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR).[^o4] His father is [Alexander Kazhdan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Kazhdan). He earned a doctorate under [Alexandre Kirillov](wikipedia://en/3772515) in 1969 and was a member of [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643)'s school of mathematics. He is [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish), and emigrated from the Soviet Union to take a position at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1975. He changed his name from Dmitri Aleksandrovich to David and became an [Orthodox Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthodox_Jew) around that time.

In 2002, he immigrated to Israel and is now a professor at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) as well as a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professors_in_the_United_States#Professor_emeritus_and_emerita) at Harvard.

On October 6, 2013, Kazhdan was critically injured in a car accident while riding a bicycle in Jerusalem.[^o5]

Kazhdan has four children. His son, [Eli Kazhdan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Kazhdan), was general director of [Natan Sharansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natan_Sharansky)'s [Yisrael BaAliyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yisrael_BaAliyah) political party (now merged with [Likud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Likud)), chief of staff in the [Ministry of Industry and Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_%28Israel%29) and the [Ministry of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_%28Israel%29), and a foreign policy advisor to Sharansky.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

He is known for collaboration with [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643), [Victor Kac](wikipedia://en/4697697), [George Lusztig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lusztig) (on the [Kazhdan–Lusztig conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhdan–Lusztig_conjecture) on [Verma modules](wikipedia://en/2975234)), with [Grigory Margulis](wikipedia://en/1104948) ([Kazhdan–Margulis theorem](wikipedia://en/51147390)), with [Yuval Flicker](wikipedia://en/4592495) and [S. J. Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._J._Patterson) on the representations of [metaplectic groups](wikipedia://en/2981795). [Kazhdan's property (T)](wikipedia://en/1362724) is widely used in representation theory.

Kazhdan held a [MacArthur Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellowship) from 1990 to 1995. He was the doctoral advisor of [Vladimir Voevodsky](wikipedia://en/104482), a recipient of the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), one of the highest awards in mathematics. Kazhdan has been a member of [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1990, of the [Israel Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) since 2006, and of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) since 2008. In 2012, he was awarded the [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize), the country's highest academic honor, for mathematics and computer science.[^o8] In 2020 he received the [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in Mathematics.[^o9]
