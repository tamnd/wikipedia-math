# Zeev Rudnick

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zeev Rudnick"}

**Zeev Rudnick** or **Ze'ev Rudnick** (זאב רודניק; born 1961 in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa), Israel) is a mathematician, specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), notably [quantum chaos](wikipedia://en/313779). Rudnick is a professor at the School of Mathematical Sciences and the Cissie and Aaron Beare Chair in Number Theory at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Rudnick received his PhD from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1990 under the supervision of [Ilya Piatetski-Shapiro](wikipedia://en/802738) and [Roger Evans Howe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Evans_Howe).

## Career {#Career}

Rudnick joined Tel Aviv University in 1995, after working as an assistant professor at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and [Stanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). In 2003–04 Rudnick was a [Leverhulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverhulme_Trust) visiting professor at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) and in 2008–2010 and 2015–2016 he was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at Princeton.

In 2012, Rudnick was inducted as a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Rudnick has been studying different aspects of quantum chaos and number theory. He has contributed to one of the discoveries concerning the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809), namely, that the Riemann zeros appear to display the same statistics as those which are believed to be present in energy levels of quantum chaotic systems and described by [random matrix theory](wikipedia://en/1648765).[^o8] Together with [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak), he has formulated the Quantum Unique Ergodicity conjectures[^o9] for eigenfunctions on negatively curved manifolds,[^o10] and has investigated the question arising from Quantum Chaos in other arithmetic models such as the Quantum Cat map (with Par Kurlberg) and the flat torus (with CP Hughes and with [Jean Bourgain](wikipedia://en/1024131)). Another interest is the interface between function field arithmetic and corresponding problems in number fields.
