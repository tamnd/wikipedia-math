# Central Organization for Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Central Organization for Statistics"}

The **Commission Statistics and GIS** (**COSIT**) or the **Commission Statistics and GIS** (**COS**, هيأة الاحصاء ونظم المعلومات الجغرافية) is the [Government of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Iraq)'s statistics agency. It has its headquarters in Hay Al-Elwiya, [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad).[^o3]
