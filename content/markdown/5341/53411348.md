# David Thissen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Thissen"}

**David Michael Thissen** (born c. 1950) is an [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) professor of [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) and former President of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500).[^o3] He is a fellow at the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and the [American Psychological Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Society).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

The eldest of five children, Thissen graduated from [St. Edmond High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Edmond_High_School_%28Fort_Dodge,_Iowa%29) in [Fort Dodge, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Dodge,_Iowa) and was a national semifinalist in the 1967 [Westinghouse Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regeneron_Science_Talent_Search).[^o5] He earned a bachelor's degree from [Saint Louis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis_University_%28United_States%29) and a PhD in quantitative psychology from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he was awarded an [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSF-GRF).

## Career {#Career}

Upon receiving his PhD in 1976, Thissen joined the psychology faculty at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) and was appointed an associate professor (with tenure) five years later. He moved to the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 1990 as a full professor of psychology and served as the chair of the L. L. Thurstone Psychometric Laboratory until 2002. He continues to work at UNC as a full professor in the Department of Psychology and Neuroscience. He published *Test Scoring* with [Howard Wainer](wikipedia://en/30741761) in 2001.
