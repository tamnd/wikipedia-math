# Bengt O. Muthén

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bengt O. Muthén"}

**Bengt Olov Muthén** is a [psychometrician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychometrician) and Professor Emeritus at the Graduate School of Education & Information Studies, University of California, Los Angeles. He is a former president of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500).[^o3]
