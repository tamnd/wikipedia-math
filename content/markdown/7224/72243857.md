# FinVect

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FinVect"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), **FinVect** (or **FdVect**) is the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) whose [objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object_%28category_theory%29) are all [finite-dimensional](wikipedia://en/38267) [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) and whose [morphisms](wikipedia://en/27716891) are all [linear maps](wikipedia://en/18102) between them.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

**FinVect** has two [monoidal](wikipedia://en/330604) products:

- the [direct sum of vector spaces](wikipedia://en/58899#Construction_for_two_vector_spaces), which is both a [categorical product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_product) and a [coproduct](wikipedia://en/310889),
- the [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844), which makes **FinVect** a [compact closed category](wikipedia://en/5557623).

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

[Tensor networks](wikipedia://en/66567874) are [string diagrams](wikipedia://en/3967296) interpreted in **FinVect**.[^o7]

[Group representations](wikipedia://en/12695) are [functors](wikipedia://en/10987) from [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), seen as one-object categories, into **FinVect**.[^o8]

[DisCoCat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DisCoCat) models are [monoidal functors](wikipedia://en/4278182) from a [pregroup grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pregroup_grammar) to **FinVect.**[^o9]
