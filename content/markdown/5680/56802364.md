# Nikolai Kapitonovich Nikolski

*Image: Nikolski1.jpg.* Nikolai Nikolski, 2006, near Sintra, Portugal, during the WOAT conference (from the personal archive of Galina Spitkovskaya).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Kapitonovich Nikolski"}

**Nikolai Kapitonovich Nikolski** (Николай Капитонович Никольский, sometimes transliterated as **Nikolskii**, born 16 November 1940)[^o2] is a Russian mathematician, specializing in real and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and functional analysis.

:::

In 1966, Nikolski earned his Candidate of Sciences degree (PhD) from the [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) under Viktor Khavin with thesis *Invariant subspaces of certain compact operators* (title translated from Russian).[^o3] In 1973 he received his Doctor of Sciences degree (habilitation) published as monograph (VI) below. He was a Laboratory director (of Math Analysis) at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) in Leningrad and Professor of Department of Mathematics at [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_State_University). In 1991 he became a professor at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux).

Nikolski's research deals with operator theory, harmonic analysis, and complex analysis. He has published more than 100 papers and six research monographs.

He was an Invited Speaker with talk *What problems do spectral theory and functional analysis solve for each other?* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1978 in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki). In 2010, he was awarded the [Ampère Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampère_Prize) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o4] and in 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), as well as for several temporary positions as Fellow of the Advanced Study Institute at Indiana University (Bloomington, 1988), Distinguished [Visiting Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar), [Ben-Gurion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) (Israel, 1993), [MSRI](wikipedia://en/401367) Research grant (Berkeley, 1995), [Marie Curie Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie_Actions) Senior Fellow, TODEQ Project, 2008, Taussky-Todd Distinguished Professor, [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) (Pasadena, 2015).

His doctoral students (total 26[^o3]) include Alexander Borichev, [Nikolai Makarov](wikipedia://en/56789183), [Sergei Treil](wikipedia://en/76888592), Vasily Vasyunin, [Alexander Volberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Volberg).

Among his notable contributions, Nikolski was one of the Leningrad mathematicians who in 1984 verified the correctness of the proof of the [Bieberbach conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieberbach_conjecture) by [Louis de Branges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Branges).[^o5]
