# Maria E. Schonbek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maria E. Schonbek"}

**Maria Elena Schonbek** is an Argentine-American mathematician at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz). Her research concerns [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034) and associated [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) such as the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395).[^o2][^o9]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Schonbek received a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires).[^o2] She completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1976. Her dissertation, *Boundary Value Problems for the Fitzhugh–Nagumo Equations*, was supervised by [Jeffrey Rauch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Rauch).

Schonbek joined the University of California, Santa Cruz faculty in 1986.[^o9] Formerly a professor of mathematics there,[^o2] she has retired to become a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o5] At Santa Cruz, Schonbek advocated replacing the campus's system of narrative evaluations of student performance in each course with the more standard system of [letter grades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_grade) used at most other US universities.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012, Schonbek became a member of the inaugural class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8][^o9]
