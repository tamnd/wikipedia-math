# Barbara L. Osofsky

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara L. Osofsky"}

**Barbara L. Osofsky** (born August 4, 1937)[^o3] is a retired professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o4] Her research concerns [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). Osofsky's contributions to mathematics include her characterization of [semisimple rings](wikipedia://en/1001361#Semisimple_rings) in terms of properties of [cyclic modules](wikipedia://en/4128720).[^o5][^o6] Osofsky also established a [logical equivalence](wikipedia://en/169324) between the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705) and statements about the [global dimension](wikipedia://en/2291739) of [associative rings](wikipedia://en/48404).[^o7][^o8]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Osofsky received her Ph.D. from Rutgers University in 1964. She then worked at Rutgers until 2004, when she retired.[^o14] She served as acting chair of the Rutgers mathematics department in 1978.[^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1973, Osofsky addressed a national meeting of the AMS. She was the first woman in 50 years to do so. She became the first female editor of an AMS journal in 1974 when she became the editor of [Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society).[^o13]

From 2000 to 2002, Osofsky served as First Vice-President of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o14] In 2005, she was awarded the MAA meritorious service award.

In 2012, Osofsky became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Osofsky, B. L. Rings all of whose finitely generated modules are injective. *Pacific J. Math.* 14 1964 645–650.
