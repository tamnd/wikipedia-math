# Ellen Kirkman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ellen Kirkman"}

**Ellen Elizabeth Kirkman** is a professor of mathematics at [Wake Forest University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wake_Forest_University). Her research interests include [noncommutative algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_algebra), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

She received her Ph.D. in Mathematics and M.A. in Statistics from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) in 1975. Her doctoral dissertation, *On the Characterization of Inertial Coefficient Rings*, was supervised by Edward C. Ingraham.

## Professional activities {#Professional_activities}

Kirkman is on the board of directors for [Enhancing Diversity in Graduate Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enhancing_Diversity_in_Graduate_Education) (EDGE), a program dedicated to helping women pursuing studies in mathematical sciences. From 2012 to 2020, she served as treasurer of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM).[^o7]

Kirkman's professional activities include serving on the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) Nominating Committee from 2009 to 11,[^o8] as a [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) Governor from 2006 to 2008, on the Joint Data Committee of AMS-ASA-MAA-IMS-SIAM (2000– 2007 and 2009–present)[^o9] and directing the CBMS 2010 survey of undergraduate mathematical sciences programs.[^o10] She is also an associate editor of Communications in Algebra.[^o11]

Kirkman served as treasurer and was on the Executive Committee of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) from 2012 to 2020.[^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012, Kirkman became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14] is also a fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) inducted in 2019.[^o15] Additionally, she received service awards from Wake Forest University and the Southeastern Section of the MAA. In 2022, she received the [AWM Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM_Service_Award) "for her eight years of service (2012 – 2020) as AWM Treasurer and Chair of the Financial Oversight and Investment Committee, for her service on the Membership Portfolio Committee, and for her role as an organizer and a research leader in the WINART (Women in Noncommutative Algebra and Representation Theory) Research Network."[^o16]
