# Zhao Youqin's π algorithm

*Image: Zhao Youqin circle dissection algorithm.svg.* Zhao Youqin's $\pi$ algorithm

*Image: 赵友钦割圆术书影.JPG.* A page from Zhao Youqin's book *Ge Xiang Xin Shu* vol 5

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zhao Youqin's π algorithm"}

**Zhao Youqin's $\pi$ algorithm** is an algorithm devised by [Yuan dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_dynasty) Chinese astronomer and mathematician [Zhao Youqin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhao_Youqin) (赵友钦, ? – 1330) to calculate the value of [$\pi$](wikipedia://en/23601) in his book *Ge Xiang Xin Shu* (革象新书).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Algorithm"}

## Algorithm {#Algorithm}

Zhao Youqin started with an inscribed square in a circle with [radius](wikipedia://en/1780815) r.[^o4]

If $\ell$ denotes the length of a side of the square, draw a [perpendicular](wikipedia://en/76944) line d from the center of the circle to side l. Let e denotes r − d. Then from the diagram:

$$d=\sqrt{r^2-\left(\frac{\ell}{2}\right)^2}$$

$$e=r-d=r-\sqrt{r^2-\left(\frac{\ell}{2}\right)^2}.$$

Extend the perpendicular line d to dissect the circle into an [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575); $\ell_2$ denotes the length of one side of octagon.

$$\ell_2=\sqrt{\left(\frac{\ell}{2}\right)^2+e^2}$$

$$\ell_2=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ \ell^2 +4\left(r-\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{4r^2-\ell^2}\right)^2}$$

Let $l_3$ denotes the length of a side of [hexadecagon](wikipedia://en/6440317)

$$\ell_3=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ \ell_2^2 +4\left(r-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4r^2-\ell_2^2}\right)^2 }$$

similarly

$$\ell_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ \ell_n^2 +4\left(r-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4r^2-\ell_n^2}\right)^2}$$

Proceeding in this way, he at last calculated the side of a 16384-gon, multiplying it by 16384 to obtain 3141.592 for a circle with diameter = 1000 units, or

$$\pi =3.141592. \,$$

He multiplied this number by 113 and obtained From this he deduced that of the traditional values of $\pi$, that is 3, 3.14, $\tfrac{22}{7}$ and $\tfrac{355}{113}$, the last is the most exact.[^o5]
