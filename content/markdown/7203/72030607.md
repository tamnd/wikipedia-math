# Sandra Collins (librarian)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sandra Collins"}

**Sandra Collins** (born 16 July 1970) is an Irish mathematician and leading academic librarian, who is the university librarian of [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin), Ireland's largest university, since 2022. She was director of the [National Library of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Ireland) from 2015.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Collins attended [University College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Dublin), graduating with a [BSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Mathematics and Mathematical Physics in 1991, and receiving a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Nonlinear Fluid Dynamics in 1996.[^o4]

She worked as a lecturer in mathematics at [Dublin City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_City_University), with [Ericsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ericsson), and as director of the Digital Repository of Ireland at the [Royal Irish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Academy).

She was appointed director of the National Library of Ireland in 2015, for a five-year term,[^o4] and agreed a second term from 2020.[^o5] She has been the university librarian at University College Dublin since 2022.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Collins received three Ireland eGovernment awards for collaborative digital cultural projects: 'Promoting Ireland Overseas', 'Open Source', and 'Overall Winner'.[^o8] She received the UCD Alumni Award in Research, Innovation and Impact in 2020.[^o9]
