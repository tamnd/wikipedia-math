# Athanase Dupré

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Athanase Dupré"}

**Louis Victoire Athanase Dupré** (28 December 1808 – 10 August 1869) was a French mathematician and physicist noted for his 1860s publications on the [mechanical theory of heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_theory_of_heat) ([thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952)); work that was said to have inspired the publications of engineer [François Massieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/François_Massieu) and his [Massieu functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massieu_function); which in turn inspired the work of American engineer [Willard Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Gibbs) and his [fundamental equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_equation).

:::
