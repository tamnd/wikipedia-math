# Sylvester's determinant identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvester's determinant identity"}

In [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) theory, **Sylvester's determinant identity** is an [identity](wikipedia://en/298428) useful for evaluating certain types of [determinants](wikipedia://en/8468). It is named after [James Joseph Sylvester](wikipedia://en/234861), who stated this identity without proof in 1851.[^o2]

:::

Given an *n*-by-*n* matrix $A$, let $\det(A)$ denote its determinant. Choose a pair

$$u =(u_1, \dots, u_m), v =(v_1, \dots, v_m) \subset (1, \dots, n)$$

of *m*-element ordered [subsets](wikipedia://en/27631) of $(1, \dots, n)$, where *m* ≤ *n*. Let $A^u_v$ denote the (*n*−*m*)-by-(*n*−*m*) submatrix of $A$ obtained by deleting the rows in $u$ and the columns in $v$. Define the auxiliary *m*-by-*m* matrix $\tilde{A}^u_v$ whose elements are equal to the following determinants

$$(\tilde{A}^u_v)_{ij} := \det(A^{u[\hat{u}_i]}_{v[\hat{v}_j]}),$$

where $u[\hat{u_i}]$, $v[\hat{v_j}]$ denote the *m*−1 element subsets of $u$ and $v$ obtained by deleting the elements $u_i$ and $v_j$, respectively. Then the following is **Sylvester's determinantal identity** (Sylvester, 1851):

$$\det(A)(\det(A^u_v))^{m-1}=\det(\tilde{A}^u_v).$$

When *m* = 2, this is the [Desnanot–Jacobi identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desnanot–Jacobi_identity) (Jacobi, 1851).
