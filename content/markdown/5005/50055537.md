# Crossed polygon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Crossed polygon"}

*Image: Equilateral pentagon-decatile3.svg.* A crossed [equilateral pentagon](wikipedia://en/19922843)

*Image: Snub\_icosidodecadodecahedron\_vertfig.png.* The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of a [snub icosidodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157687) is a crossed hexagon.

*Image: Crossed-decagon.png.* A symmetric crossed decagon

A **crossed polygon** is a polygon in the plane with a [turning number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_number) or [density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density_%28polygon%29) of zero, with the appearance of a figure 8, [infinity symbol](wikipedia://en/1605389), or [lemniscate](wikipedia://en/5210590) curve.

:::

*Crossed polygons* are related to [star polygons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_polygons) which have [turning numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_number) greater than 1.

The vertices with [clockwise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clockwise) turning angles equal the vertices with counterclockwise turning angles. A *crossed polygon* will always have at least 2 edges or vertices intersecting or coinciding.

Any [convex polygon](wikipedia://en/660019) with 4 or more sides can be remade into a crossed polygon by swapping the positions of two adjacent vertices.

*Crossed polygons* are common as [vertex figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_figures) of [uniform star polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2850448#Uniform_star_polyhedra).[^o2]

## Crossed quadrilateral {#Crossed_quadrilateral}

[Crossed quadrilaterals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed_quadrilateral) are most common, including:

- *crossed parallelogram* or [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627), a crossed quadrilateral with alternate edges of equal length.
- *crossed trapezoid* has two opposite parallel edges.
- *[crossed rectangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed_rectangle)*, an antiparallelogram whose edges are two opposite sides and the two diagonals of a [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048).
- *crossed square*, a crossed rectangle with two equal opposite sides and two diagonals of a square.
