# List of self-intersecting polygons

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of self-intersecting polygons"}

**Self-intersecting polygons**, **crossed polygons**, or **self-crossing polygons** are [polygons](wikipedia://en/23621) some of whose [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034) cross each other. They contrast with [simple polygons](wikipedia://en/1059530), whose edges never cross.

:::

Some types of self-intersecting polygons are:

::: {#o2 .entry cue=list-entry name="Crossed quadrilateral"}

- the [crossed quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed_quadrilateral), with four edges

:::

  - the [antiparallelogram](wikipedia://en/5817627), a crossed quadrilateral with alternate edges of equal length
    - the [crossed rectangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossed_rectangle), an antiparallelogram whose edges are two opposite sides and the two diagonals of a [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048), hence having two edges parallel

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Star polygon"}

- [Star polygons](wikipedia://en/313303)

:::

  - [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544), with five edges
  - [hexagram](wikipedia://en/302441), with six edges
  - [heptagram](wikipedia://en/180673), with seven edges
  - [octagram](wikipedia://en/2667613), with eight edges
  - [enneagram](wikipedia://en/34963576) or nonagram, with nine edges
  - [decagram](wikipedia://en/19546742), with ten edges
  - [hendecagram](wikipedia://en/1173083), with eleven edges
  - [dodecagram](wikipedia://en/26173953), with twelve edges
  - [icositetragram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icositetragram), with twenty four edges
  - [257-gram](wikipedia://en/5024265#257-gram), with two hundred and fifty seven edges
