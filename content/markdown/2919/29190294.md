# Simplicially enriched category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simplicially enriched category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **simplicially enriched category**, is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) over the category of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784). Simplicially enriched categories are often also called, more ambiguously, **simplicial categories**; the latter term however also applies to simplicial objects in **Cat** (the category of [small](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category) categories). Simplicially enriched categories can, however, be identified with simplicial objects in **Cat** whose object part is constant, or more precisely, all face and degeneracy maps are [bijective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijective) on objects. Simplicially enriched categories can model [(∞, 1)-categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%28∞,_1%29-category), but the dictionary has to be carefully built. Namely, many notions (limits, for example) are different from the limits in the sense of enriched category theory.

:::

The [homotopy coherent nerve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_coherent_nerve) of a simplicially enriched category is a simplicial set that generalizes the nerve of a category.
