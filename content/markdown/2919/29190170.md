# Topological category (enriched category theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Topological category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a discipline in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **topological category** is a category that is [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) over the category of [compactly generated](wikipedia://en/899382) [Hausdorff spaces](wikipedia://en/13637). They can be used as a foundation for [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013), where they can play the role of ($\infty$,1)-categories. An important example of a topological category in this sense is given by the category of [CW complexes](wikipedia://en/353022), where each set Hom(*X*,*Y*) of continuous maps from *X* to *Y* is equipped with the [compact-open topology](wikipedia://en/726335). (Lurie 2009)

:::
