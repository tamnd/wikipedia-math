# Kathleen Gates

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kathleen Gates"}

**Kathleen Marie "Katie" Gates** is an American [neuroscientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscientist), [quantitative psychologist](wikipedia://en/3067624), and faculty member in the L. L. Thurstone Psychometric Laboratory at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). She is known for her contributions to [network analysis](wikipedia://en/325726), [time series analysis](wikipedia://en/406624), and [structural equation modeling](wikipedia://en/2007748) toward the development and dissemination of methods for quantifying [intra-individual change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_psychology) and person-specific processes as they unfold across time.

:::

She and [Peter Molenaar](wikipedia://en/57976933) are co-inventors of GIMME, an [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) for finding [mathematical models](wikipedia://en/20590) of [psychophysiological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychophysiology) processes across time.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

A native of [Troy, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy,_Michigan), Gates earned a bachelor's degree from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), a master's degree in [forensic psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_psychology) from the [John Jay College of Criminal Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Jay_College_of_Criminal_Justice) at the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), and a PhD in human development and family studies with a focus in [quantitative methods](wikipedia://en/389564) from [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University). She joined the Psychology and Neuroscience faculty at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 2013.[^o5]

She is an elected member of the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Gates publishes statistical methods for the analysis of [intensive longitudinal data](wikipedia://en/2499813). Her primary source of funding is the [National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Biomedical_Imaging_and_Bioengineering).[^o8]
