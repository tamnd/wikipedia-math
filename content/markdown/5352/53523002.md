# Forrest W. Young

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Forrest W. Young"}

**Forrest Wesley Young** (April 10, 1940 – 2006) was a professor emeritus of [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) and President of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500).[^o3] He is the developer of [ViSta](wikipedia://en/25377077) a software for data visualization.[^o4]

:::

## Books {#Books}

- Schiffman, S.S., Reynolds, M.L. & Young, F.W. (1981). *Introduction to Multidimensional Scaling*. New York: Academic Press.
- Young, F.W. & Sarle, W.S. (1982). *Exploratory Multivariate Data Analysis*. Cary, NC: SAS Institute, Inc.
- Young, F.W. & Hamer, R.M. (1987). *Multidimensional Scaling: History, Theory and Applications*. New York: Erlbaum Associates. (reprinted, 1994)
