# Joséphine Guidy Wandja

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joséphine Guidy Wandja"}

**Joséphine Guidy Wandja** (born 1945, also **Guidy-Wandja**) is an [Ivorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_Coast) mathematician. She is the first African woman with a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics.

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

She moved to France aged 14.[^o5] She attended the [Lycée Jules-Ferry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Jules-Ferry_%28Paris%29) in Paris, and later the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).[^o5] Her [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) thesis was entitled *Sous les courbes fermées convexes du plan et le théorème des quatre sommets* (*Under closed convex curves in the plane and the theorem of four peaks*).[^o6] Whilst working in Paris in the late 1960s she was advised by [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633), [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) and [Paulette Liberman](wikipedia://en/46726637).[^o7] She studied for a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at the [University of Abidjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Abidjan), becoming the first African woman to get a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics.[^o8][^o9]

## Career {#Career}

In 1969, she worked at the Lycée [Jacques Amyot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Amyot) in [Melun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melun), before working for a year at the [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University).[^o5] In 1971, she joined the University of Abidjan.[^o5] There she became the first African female university mathematics professor.[^o9] In 1983, she was appointed the president of the International Committee on Mathematics in Developing Countries (ICOMIDC). The organisation was set up during the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU) [conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), Poland, but without the IMU's knowledge.[^o11] In 1986, she wrote a humorous 24 page mathematical comic book *Yao crack en maths*.[^o8][^o12] In 1985, she organised an ICOMIDC conference in [Yamoussoukro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamoussoukro), Ivory Coast.[^o11]

She is an officer of the Ivorian Order of Merit of National Education, and the French [Ordre des Palmes Académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_Académiques).[^o5][^o9]

## Publications {#Publications}

- Guidy Wandja, Joséphine, Yao crack en maths (in French), Nouvelles Éditions africaines, 1985.
