# Nature Computational Science

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nature Computational Science"}

***Nature Computational Science*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published monthly by [Nature Portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Portfolio). Established in 2021,[^o3] it covers research on the development and use of [computational techniques](wikipedia://en/1181008) and mathematical models.[^o4] Its current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Fernando Chirigati.[^o5]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Engineering, Computing & Technology[^o8]
- Current Contents/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences[^o8]
- [Ei Compendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ei_Compendex)[^o7]
- [MEDLINE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MEDLINE)[^o7]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o8]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o7]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2024 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 18.3.[^o9]
