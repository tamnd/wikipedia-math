# Instituto Argentino de Matemática

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Instituto Argentino de Matemática"}

Based in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires), the **Instituto Argentino de Matemática** (I. A. M.) [^o2] is a research center of the National Research Council of Argentina ([CONICET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONICET)). It is named after [Alberto Pedro Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Pedro_Calderón) in honor of one of its former directors, the Argentinian mathematician widely recognized as a towering figure of 20th-century mathematics. The overarching goal of the institute is to promote the development of pure and applied mathematics research in [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina).

:::
