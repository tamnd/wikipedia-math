# Martin Kutta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Kutta"}

**Martin Wilhelm Kutta** (; 3 November 1867 – 25 December 1944) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). In 1901, he co-developed the [Runge–Kutta method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge–Kutta_method), used to solve ordinary [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) numerically. He is also remembered for the [Zhukovsky–Kutta aerofoil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joukowski_airfoil), the [Kutta–Zhukovsky theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kutta–Zhukovsky_theorem) and the [Kutta condition](wikipedia://en/4265190) in [aerodynamics](wikipedia://en/2819).

:::

Kutta was born in [Pitschen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitschen), [Upper Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Silesia), [Kingdom of Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia) (today [Byczyna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byczyna), Poland). He attended the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau) from 1885 to 1890, and continued his studies at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) until 1894, where he became the assistant of [Walther Franz Anton von Dyck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walther_Franz_Anton_von_Dyck). From 1898, he spent half a year at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). From 1899 to 1909, he worked again as an assistant of von Dyck at the Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München; from 1909 to 1910, he was an adjunct professor at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena). He was professor at the [RWTH Aachen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen) from 1910 to 1912. Kutta became professor at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) in 1912, where he stayed until his retirement in 1935. Kutta died in [Fürstenfeldbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fürstenfeldbruck), Germany in 1944.
