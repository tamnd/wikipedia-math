# Cavalieri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cavalieri"}

**Cavalieri** is an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language) [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Bonaventura Cavalieri](wikipedia://en/412561) (1598–1647), Italian mathematician
- [Caterina Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caterina_Cavalieri) (1755–1801), Austrian opera soprano
- [Diego Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Cavalieri) (born 1982), Brazilian footballer; goalkeeper for Crystal Palace F.C.
- [Emilio de' Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_de'_Cavalieri) (1550–1602), Italian composer of the late Renaissance
- [Grace Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Cavalieri) (born 1932), American poet and playwright
- [Joey Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Cavalieri), American comic book writer and editor
- [Lina Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lina_Cavalieri) (1874–1944), Italian opera soprano
- [Paola Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paola_Cavalieri) (born 1950), Italian philosopher
- [Tommaso dei Cavalieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommaso_dei_Cavalieri) (c. 1508–1587), Italian friend of Michelangelo
