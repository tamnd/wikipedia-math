# Rafael Artzy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rafael Artzy"}

**Rafael Artzy** (רפאל ארצי; 23 July 1912 – 22 August 2006) was an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).

:::

## Education and emigration {#Education_and_emigration}

Artzy was born July 23, 1912, in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). His father was Edward I. Deutschlander and his mother Ida Freudenheim. Rafael studied at [Königsberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg_University) from 1930 to 1933. He transferred to [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) and obtained a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in 1934. He married Elly Iwiansky on October 12, 1934. Rafael continued his studies at Hebrew University under [Theodore Motzkin](wikipedia://en/13554055), obtaining a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1945. Elly and Rafael raised three children: Ehud, Michal, and Barak. Ehud and Barak died before their father. Michal Artzy is [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) in Marine Civilization[^o4] at the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa).

Rafael served as both teacher and principal of Israel High School from 1934 to 1951. He was an instructor and assistant professor at the [Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Institute_of_Technology) from 1951 to 1956.

## American tour {#American_tour}

Rafael Artzy took up a position as research associate and lecturer at [University of Wisconsin, Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin,_Madison) in 1956. That year he also made his first of many contributions to [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646). Artzy became [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) at [University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina,_Chapel_Hill) in 1960. The following year [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) made him a full professor. In 1964 he was a visitor at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o6] He wrote *Linear Geometry* (1965) which was favorably reviewed by [H. S. M. Coxeter](wikipedia://en/216328)[^o7] In 1965 Artzy was at [State University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York) in [Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York). In 1967 he joined [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) where he was for five years.

## Return {#Return}

In 1972 Rafael Artzy returned to Israel and participated in mathematics at Technion in Haifa. He helped organize a quadrennial conference on geometry at Haifa. For instance, in March 1979 such a conference was held and the proceedings *Geometry and Differential Geometry* was edited by Artzy and I. Vaisman and published as [Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235) #792. In 1992 he published *Geometry. An Algebraic Approach*[^o9] Artzy had made 224 contributions to *Mathematical Reviews* by his last submission in 1995.
