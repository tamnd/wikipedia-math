# Jonathan Mock Beck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Mock Beck"}

**Jonathan Mock "Jon" Beck** (November 11, 1935 – March 11, 2006, [Somerville, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerville,_Massachusetts)) was an American mathematician, who worked on [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Beck received his PhD in 1967 under [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University). Beck was a faculty member of the mathematics department of [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and of the University of Puerto Rico. He is known for the eponymous [Beck's tripleableness (monadicity) theorem](wikipedia://en/2082726) and the Beck–Chevalley condition.
