# Andreotti–Grauert theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreotti–Grauert theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Andreotti–Grauert theorem**, introduced by (txt), gives conditions for [cohomology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) of [coherent sheaves](wikipedia://en/603780) over [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) to vanish or to be finite-dimensional.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $X$ be a (not necessarily [reduced](wikipedia://en/539749)) [complex analytic space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_space), and $\mathcal{F}$ a coherent analytic sheaf over X. Then,

- $\rm{dim}_{\mathbb{C}} \; H^i (X, \mathcal{F}) < \infty$ for $i \geq q$ (resp. $i < \rm{codh} \; (\mathcal{F}) - q$), if $X$ is q-pseudoconvex (resp. q-pseudoconcave). (finiteness)[^o5][^o6]
- $H^i (X, \mathcal{F}) = 0$ for $i \geq q$, if $X$ is q-complete. (vanish)[^o7]

:::
