# Andreotti–Vesentini theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreotti–Vesentini theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Andreotti–Vesentini separation theorem**, introduced by (txt) states that certain [cohomology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) of [coherent sheaves](wikipedia://en/603780) are separated.

:::
