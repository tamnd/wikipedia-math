# Adolf Krazer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Krazer"}

German article title

*Image: Adolf Krazer.jpg.* Adolf Krazer

**Adolf Carl Josef Krazer** (15 April 1858 in [Zusmarshausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zusmarshausen) – 7 August 1926 in [Karlsruhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe)) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) mathematician.
