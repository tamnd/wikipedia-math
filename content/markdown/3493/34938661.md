# Edoardo Vesentini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edoardo Vesentini"}

Edoardo Vesentini

*Image: Edoardo Vesentini.jpg.* Edoardo Vesentini in 1967

**Edoardo Vesentini** (31 May 1928 – 28 March 2020) was an Italian mathematician and politician who introduced the [Andreotti–Vesentini theorem](wikipedia://en/34938611). He was awarded the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352) in 1962.

:::

He was elected a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 1989.[^o2]

Vasentini was born in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome), and died on 28 March 2020, aged 91.[^o3]
