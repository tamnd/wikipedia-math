# Aldo Andreotti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aldo Andreotti"}

the Italian sport shooter

**Aldo Andreotti** (15 March 1924 – 21 February 1980) was an Italian mathematician who worked on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), on the [theory of functions of several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables) and on [partial differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_operator). Notably he proved the [Andreotti–Frankel theorem](wikipedia://en/3300363), the [Andreotti–Grauert theorem](wikipedia://en/34938110), the [Andreotti–Vesentini theorem](wikipedia://en/34938611) and introduced, jointly with [François Norguet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/François_Norguet), the [Andreotti–Norguet integral representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreotti–Norguet_integral_representation) for functions of several complex variables.

:::

Andreotti was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1951 and again from 1957 through 1959.[^o3]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Aldo Andreotti published 100 scientific works, including papers, books and lecture notes: many of them, except all his books but (Andreotti Nacinovich 1980), are collected in his "*Selecta*" (). In his "Selecta" are also included three unpublished sets of lecture notes,[^o7] the first one prepared by Philippe Artzner from a course on the theory of analytic functions of several complex variables held by Andreotti during winter 1961 at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg),[^o8] the second and third ones taken from two lectures held by Francesco Gherardelli at the "Seminario di Geometria"[^o9] of the Scuola Normale Superiore during the years 1971–1972 and 1971–1972 respectively, on topics concerning his joint work with Andreotti:[^o10] despite their nature of unpublished works, (Vesentini 1992, 5) states that they have brought significant contributions to research.[^o12]
