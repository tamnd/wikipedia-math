# Joachim Nitsche

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joachim Nitsche"}

**Joachim A. Nitsche** (September 2, 1926, [Nossen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nossen) – January 12, 1996) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg), known for his important contributions to the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/48396) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). The duality argument for estimating the error of the [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581) and a scheme for the weak enforcement of [Dirichlet boundary conditions](wikipedia://en/423504) for [Poisson's equation](wikipedia://en/193735) bear his name.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

### Education {#Education}

Nitsche graduated from school at [Bischofswerda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bischofswerda) in 1946. Starting in summer 1947, he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where he received his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) (under supervision of [Franz Rellich](wikipedia://en/4172897)) after only six semesters. In 1951, he received his degree (Dr. rer. nat.) at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). After only two years, he received his [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin).

### Marriage and children {#Marriage_and_children}

In 1952, Nitsche married Gisela Lange, with whom he had three children.

### Professional career {#Professional_career}

From 1955 to 1957, Nitsche held a teaching position at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin), which he left for a position at [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) in [Böblingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Böblingen). He became professor at the [Albert Ludwigs University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Ludwigs_University_of_Freiburg) in 1958 and received the chair for [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) there in 1962. He remained in this position until he became [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in 1991.
