# Thin group (algebraic group theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thin group"}

In [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) theory, a **thin group** is a discrete [Zariski-dense](wikipedia://en/241744) subgroup of *G*(**R**) that has infinite covolume, where *G* is a [semisimple algebraic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_algebraic_group) over the [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438). This is in contrast to a [lattice](wikipedia://en/469246), which is a discrete subgroup of finite covolume.

:::

The theory of "group expansion" ([expander graph](wikipedia://en/9313) properties of related [Cayley graphs](wikipedia://en/358277)) for particular thin groups has been applied to arithmetic properties of [Apollonian circles](wikipedia://en/5023947) and in [Zaremba's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaremba's_conjecture).[^o2]
