# John Hudson (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Hudson"}

**John Hudson** (1773 – 31 October 1843) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [clergyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clergyman). He was notable for being a [senior wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_wrangler) as well as the tutor of [George Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Peacock_%28mathematician%29).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

John Hudson was the son of John Hudson, a farmer at [Haverbrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverbrack) in the parish of [Beetham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beetham). He attended [Heversham School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heversham_Grammar_School) and entered [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) in 1793. He became [senior wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_wrangler) in 1797, also winning the [Smith's prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_prize) in that year, and obtained his MA in 1800.

## Career {#Career}

He became a Fellow, in 1798, and tutor, in 1807, of [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he notably tutored [George Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Peacock_%28mathematician%29): he also tutored [John Martin Frederick Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Martin_Frederick_Wright). In 1815, he became the vicar of [Kendal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendal), [Westmoreland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westmorland). In 1815, he married the daughter of an army officer by the name of Culliford.

At Cambridge, Hudson also tutored [Charles James Blomfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_James_Blomfield) who became a prominent bishop. As a bishop, Blomfield visited Hudson's parish and at a dinner party declared "I remember well, Mr. Hudson, how much I stood in awe of you at College." To which Hudson retorted, "Perhaps so, but your Lordship has turned the tables on me now."

Hudson died at [Haverbrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverbrack), Tuesday, 31 October 1843 at the age of 71 and was buried in the interior of the parish church at [Kendal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendal).
