# Subgroup analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Subgroup analysis"}

**Subgroup analysis** refers to repeating the [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134) of a study within [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) of subjects defined by a subgrouping variable. For example: [smoking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoking) status defining two subgroups: smokers and non-smokers.[^o2]

:::
