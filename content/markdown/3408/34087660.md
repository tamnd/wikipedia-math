# Danish pile-driving formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Danish pile-driving formula"}

**The Danish pile-driving formula** is a formula which enables one to have a good gauge of the bearing capacity of a driven pile.

:::

## History {#History}

The formula was constructed by the Danish civil engineer Andreas Knudsen in 1955. It was made as a part of his final project at [The Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Technical_University) and was published for the Geotechnic Congress in London in 1956. It later became part of the Danish Code of Practice for Foundation Engineering and was named.

::: {#o4 .statement cue=named-heading name="The formula"}

## The formula {#The_formula}

$$Q_{dy} = \frac{\alpha W_H H}{S + 0.5S_e}$$

in which

$$S_e = \sqrt{\frac{2 \alpha W_H HL}{AE}}$$

where

- Q<sub>dy</sub> = ultimate dynamic bearing capacity of driven pile
- α = pile driving hammer efficiency
- W<sub>H</sub> = weight of hammer
- H = hammer drop
- S = inelastic set of piles, in distance pr. hammer blow
- S<sub>e</sub> = elastic set of piles, in distance pr. hammer blow
- L = pile length
- A = pile end area
- E = modulus of elasticity of pile material

:::
